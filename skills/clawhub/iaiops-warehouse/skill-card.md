## Description: <br>
iaiops-warehouse helps agents inspect and analyze warehouse automation systems across EtherNet/IP, Profinet, Modbus, OPC-UA, and MQTT-Sparkplug for predictive maintenance, downtime triage, throughput/OEE, alarm analysis, and asset health. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, warehouse automation engineers, and operations teams use this skill to guide diagnostics, telemetry reads, predictive maintenance, bottleneck analysis, OEE/throughput review, and alarm triage for distribution-center material-handling equipment. Use in real warehouse or OT environments should be reviewed before installation and gated by change-control where write or publish capabilities are available. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

Risk: Server security evidence says the skill is presented as read-only while documenting high-impact industrial write and publish capabilities. <br>
