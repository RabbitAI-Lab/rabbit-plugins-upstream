## Description:

Professional tier of Seedream 5.0, stronger on fine detail, typography and complex composition. Suited to commercial key visuals and demanding brand assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creative teams can generate commercial images from text prompts, with optional reference images, for key visuals and brand assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial and reported a mismatch between the image-generation purpose and the authentication and CLI behavior it reviewed; package details were not verified.

Mitigation: Inspect the flagged instructions and confirm the external CLI and package before use.

Risk: Prompts and supplied local images are sent to dLazy-hosted services, and use requires a dLazy API key.

Mitigation: Review the authentication flow and service before use; provide only approved prompts and images, and rotate or revoke keys when needed.
