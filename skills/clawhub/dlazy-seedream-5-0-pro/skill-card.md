## Description:

Professional tier of Seedream 5.0, stronger on fine detail, typography and complex composition. Suited to commercial key visuals and demanding brand assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can generate commercial key visuals and other brand assets with Seedream 5.0 Pro by supplying a prompt and optional reference media through the dLazy CLI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security verdict is benign with medium confidence, and the partial scan did not fully verify several references. Prompts and supplied media are sent to dLazy services, and CLI login is required.

Mitigation: Review the CLI and service data handling before use, send only prompts and media you are comfortable processing through dLazy, and use --dry-run to inspect the payload and estimated cost.
