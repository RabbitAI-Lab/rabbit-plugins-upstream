## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Overly broad activation metadata may cause the skill to activate outside explicit SkillScan or ClawHub security-workflow requests. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow or disable implicit invocation before installation so activation is limited to the intended security workflow contexts. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance, checklists, code, or configuration suggestions may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific deployment. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against the stated success criteria and scan or test any resulting skill changes before deployment. <br>
