## Description:

Use right after User Intuition is installed or connected, or when the user asks how to get started, whether their User Intuition account is connected, or what User Intuition can do. Checks the connection, asks what they want to learn and from whom, and routes to the right workflow. Do not use for a specific research request that another User Intuition skill already covers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users of User Intuition use this setup skill to check their account connection, clarify what they want to learn and from whom, and find the appropriate research workflow. It also explains how to connect when sign-in is missing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The user could connect the wrong account or organization or grant connection permissions they did not intend.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended account and organization during browser sign-in, and review the requested connection permissions.

Risk: A later research workflow could create a study, recruit participants, or incur costs without the user's intended approval.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit approval before study creation, participant recruitment, or spending; approval for one step does not approve the others.
