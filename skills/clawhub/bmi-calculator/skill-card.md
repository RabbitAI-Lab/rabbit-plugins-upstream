## Description:

Calculates adult BMI (IMC) from metric or imperial height and weight, explains the WHO adult category and healthy-weight range in English or Spanish, and notes the limits for children, teens, and pregnancy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nbkdevs](https://clawhub.ai/user/nbkdevs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can provide their age, height, and weight to calculate an adult BMI, see its WHO category, and get the healthy-weight range for their height in English, Spanish, or both.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Height and weight are personal health details that may be shared in chat.

Mitigation: Share only health details you are comfortable including in chat.

Risk: BMI may be mistaken for a diagnosis or a complete measure of health.

Mitigation: Present BMI as a screening estimate, not a diagnosis, and explain its limits.

Risk: The optional linked calculator is a separate website with its own privacy practices.

Mitigation: Treat the linked calculator as a separate service and review its privacy practices before using it.
