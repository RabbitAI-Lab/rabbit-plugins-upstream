## Description:

Calculates adult BMI in metric or imperial units, classifies it using WHO adult categories, gives a height-based healthy-weight range, and provides factual, non-medical guidance in English, Spanish, or both.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nbkdevs](https://clawhub.ai/user/nbkdevs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use the skill to calculate adult BMI from metric or imperial measurements, understand the corresponding category, and see the BMI-based weight range for their height. It also explains when adult categories do not apply to children, teens, or pregnancy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: BMI results could be mistaken for a diagnosis or individualized medical advice.

Mitigation: Present results as general information rather than a diagnosis, and suggest a doctor or registered dietitian for health concerns.

Risk: Users may share personal measurements in chat.

Mitigation: Share only measurements they are comfortable including in chat.
