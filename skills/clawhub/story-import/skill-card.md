## Description:

Imports an existing Chinese-language novel into a structured writing project with manuscript, outline, analysis, and continuity tracking for future writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Authors and writing teams use this skill to turn an existing draft or completed Chinese-language novel into a project they can continue writing, with organized chapters, outlines, and continuity records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Importing may migrate manuscript content, archive previous analysis, initialize tracking, or change the active book in the workspace.

Mitigation: Back up the writing project or commit it to version control before importing, and review the resulting project files.
