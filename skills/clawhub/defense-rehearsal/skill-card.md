## Description: <br>
Defense Rehearsal helps academic defense candidates prepare, rehearse, and debrief by generating mock committee questions, scanning cited-literature weak spots, and reviewing recorded delivery through Tencent Meeting transcripts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, researchers, and academic presenters use this skill to rehearse proposal, mid-term, and graduation defenses. It produces mock committee questions, cited-literature gap checks, delivery review from authorized meeting recordings, and post-rehearsal improvement guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can use meeting recordings or transcripts that may contain sensitive or unpublished research content. <br>
Mitigation: Get participant consent and avoid uploading sensitive defense materials or recordings to Tencent Meeting unless that handling is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Local method-level usage tags are collected by default after use. <br>
Mitigation: Disable the feature by saying "\u522b\u8bb0\u4e86" if local usage tagging is not wanted. <br>
Risk: The simulated committee and delivery feedback are rehearsal aids, not substitutes for advisor or peer review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the outputs to focus practice and follow up with human reviewers for academic judgment. <br>
