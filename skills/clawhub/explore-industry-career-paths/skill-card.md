## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to research industries, career transitions, skill learning paths, freelancing, first-client acquisition, shop opening, and low-cost startup validation. It is oriented toward beginner-actionable guidance, current resource verification, risk boundaries, and concrete next steps.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Current prices, policies, licenses, broker rules, trading hours, local resources, and training options can vary by date and location.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's country, city, budget, timeline, and risk constraints, then verify current official or primary sources and record verification dates.

Risk: Career, startup, regulated-service, and finance guidance can be mistaken for guaranteed income, employment, licensing, legal, tax, medical, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as research and coaching guidance, avoid outcome guarantees, identify jurisdictional assumptions, and direct users to qualified professionals or regulators for binding decisions.

Risk: Finance-education paths and account or platform comparisons could lead users toward real-money trading or account opening before they understand the risks.

Mitigation: Use educational examples, simulated practice, official license checks, and risk-capacity prompts; do not place trades, open accounts, provide personalized buy or sell recommendations, or use referral incentives.
