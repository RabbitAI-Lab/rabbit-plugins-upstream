## Description:

为中文零基础用户判断陌生行业、转行求职、学习技能、接单或验证创业方向，并把模糊目标转化为行业研究、能力训练、7 天低风险实验和持续复盘。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-language users use this skill to evaluate career changes, skill learning paths, freelancing, local services, and small-business ideas. It emphasizes routed guidance, source checking for current resources, low-risk validation, progress cards, and follow-up review instead of guaranteed outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career, business, course, pricing, platform, and local-resource guidance can become outdated or vary by region.

Mitigation: Require current-source verification for time-sensitive or local claims and clearly label unverifiable information as an assumption or search method.

Risk: Users could treat personal investing content as buy, sell, or return advice.

Mitigation: Keep investing guidance educational, use simulated research workflows, and avoid personalized trade instructions or promised returns.

Risk: Users may act on regulated, licensed, contractual, health, safety, or significant-spending decisions without proper confirmation.

Mitigation: Direct users to region-specific professional, regulatory, or qualified supervision before real-money, licensing, contract, or safety-sensitive action.

Risk: Career, freelancing, or business plans may be misread as guaranteed employment, customers, revenue, traffic, or commercial success.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as experiments with evidence requirements, stop lines, and review checkpoints instead of outcome guarantees.
