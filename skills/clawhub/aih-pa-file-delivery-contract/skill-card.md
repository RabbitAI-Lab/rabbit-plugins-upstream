## Description:

将用户提供的资料和交付要求转化为可核验的成果文件、来源映射和交付检查表，并可按需使用 AI-HIVE。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

个人或团队使用此技能，把已有资料整理成可审阅的文档等成果，并核对来源和验收标准。需要生成、渲染或使用 AI-HIVE 时，应先确认可用工具和授权。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or unapproved material could be sent to an external service.

Mitigation: Confirm exactly which files may be uploaded and redact or minimize sensitive content before using AI-HIVE.

Risk: Paid tasks or external actions could exceed the user's intended scope.

Mitigation: Confirm current pricing, currency and budget before paid work; get separate explicit authorization for sending, payment, deletion or publishing.

Risk: A plan or unverified draft could be mistaken for a completed deliverable.

Mitigation: Check that the output file exists, opens correctly and matches its sources before marking the task complete.
