## Description:

Sets handling guidance for a content item and prepares a content review plan from supplied information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content reviewers use supplied content profiles to establish handling guidance, then use that guidance to prepare a review plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A supplied content profile could include information not intended for review.

Mitigation: Provide only the content profile or handling guidance intended for review.
