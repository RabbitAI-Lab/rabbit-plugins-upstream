## Description: <br>
Tech Bid Writer helps bidders create technical proposal packages from tender documents, including Word-ready technical text, technical deviation tables, compliance prechecks, and scoring-point coverage checks, while keeping commercial pricing and qualification materials out of scope. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External bidding teams use this skill to plan, draft, check, and package the technical portion of tender responses from tender files and optional company knowledge-base materials. It supports page planning, scoring-item mapping, chapter drafting, technical compliance checks, deviation tables, and final Word-oriented delivery with required human review gates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tender files and company knowledge-base materials may contain sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Run conversions and generated outputs in a private project directory and restrict access to generated files. <br>
Risk: Generated technical parameters, standards, certificates, or bid commitments may be incorrect if not verified against source materials. <br>
Mitigation: Manually verify all AI-generated technical claims, standards, certificates, and bid commitments before submission. <br>
Risk: Document conversion and Word export scripts generate local output files. <br>
Mitigation: Use the script output options intentionally and review generated paths before sharing or submitting deliverables. <br>
