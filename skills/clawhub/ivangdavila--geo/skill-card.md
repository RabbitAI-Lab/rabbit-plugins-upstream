## Use Case:

External marketers, founders, and growth teams use this skill to audit how AI assistants recommend brands in a category and to plan content, community, and PR actions that improve AI recommendation visibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate during broad AI visibility or recommendation discussions.

Mitigation: Use it when marketing or generative-engine-optimization guidance is intended.

Risk: Public-platform tactics could be misused for spam, astroturfing, or deceptive promotion.

Mitigation: Follow platform rules and keep participation authentic, transparent, and non-spammy.

Risk: AI recommendation audits can vary by model, prompt phrasing, date, and account tier.

Mitigation: Record exact prompts, models, dates, tiers, and repeat the same audit matrix over time before acting on trends.
