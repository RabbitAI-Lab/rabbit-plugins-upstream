## Description:

Consulting Delivery helps consultants produce executive briefings, consulting reports, solution frameworks, benchmarking analyses, desk research, interview notes, and project status reports from structured templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ebandao777-oss](https://clawhub.ai/user/ebandao777-oss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Consultants and business teams use this skill to turn project inputs into structured consulting deliverables, including executive summaries, full reports, MECE solution frames, benchmarking outputs, research briefs, interview notes, and weekly project updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential consulting materials may be processed through broad local tools or sent to Notion when that connector is available.

Mitigation: Limit tool and file access to the relevant engagement materials, disable Notion publishing for sensitive work, or require explicit review of the exact content and destination before publishing.

Risk: Consulting outputs can become misleading when the user provides incomplete inputs, unverifiable data, or unsupported benchmark assumptions.

Mitigation: Require source traceability, preserve missing-data markers, and review generated recommendations before using them in client or executive deliverables.
