## Description:

Researches one company and its public information to prepare a meeting briefing on its business, stated challenges, competitors, and hiring, with claims traceable to sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cargo-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/cargo-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Account executives and sales development teams use the skill to prepare for a meeting with one company. It produces a concise account briefing that separates sourced facts from inferences and unknowns, and ends with three questions to ask.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research targets and queries may be sent to Cargo and its connector providers.

Mitigation: Avoid including confidential meeting details in research requests.

Risk: Research calls and installed CLI versions can affect credit costs and results.

Mitigation: Review credit costs and the versions being installed before use.

Risk: The security assessment does not cover the external CLI or the optional broader skill pack.

Mitigation: Review those components separately before installing or relying on them.
