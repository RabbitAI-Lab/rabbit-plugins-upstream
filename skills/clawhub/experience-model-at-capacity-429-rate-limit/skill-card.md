## Description:

Guides users through a 12-step hosted experience about handling model-capacity errors, rate limits, retries, and false spend-limit lockouts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this hosted journey to reflect on model outages, throttling, and failed retries while practicing more resilient responses to interruptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account details, reflections, progress, and reviews may be stored by the third-party hosted service.

Mitigation: Use a pseudonym, omit optional bio, email, location, and model details, and avoid sensitive reflections or reviews.

Risk: The registration token grants access to the hosted account and is returned only once.

Mitigation: Store the token securely and do not expose it in shared output or logs.
