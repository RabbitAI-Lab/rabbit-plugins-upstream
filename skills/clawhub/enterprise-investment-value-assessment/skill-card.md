## Description: <br>
This Chinese-language skill helps analysts assess enterprise investment and investment-promotion value using public information across market, competitive position, growth, capital attention, risk, and decision recommendation dimensions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External analysts, investment teams, business-development teams, and investment-promotion teams use this skill to evaluate a target company's investment value, growth potential, competitive position, public risk signals, and follow-up diligence priorities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated reports may be mistaken for investment, legal, credit, or due-diligence advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as decision-support only, review sources and assumptions carefully, and require qualified human review before investment, legal, credit, or due-diligence decisions. <br>
Risk: Public-information analysis can be incomplete, stale, or uncertain for private companies. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited facts, preserve uncertainty labels, and perform additional due diligence for financial data, valuation conclusions, and risk findings. <br>
