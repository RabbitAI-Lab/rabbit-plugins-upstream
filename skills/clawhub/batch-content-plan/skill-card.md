## Description: <br>
Plans a week or month of strategically balanced, on-brand social media post briefs, maps them to a calendar, and prepares approved batches for scheduling. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Social media managers, creators, and business owners use this skill to turn brand profile, voice, and content pillars into a realistic weekly or monthly calendar of actionable post briefs. It helps plan balanced content across platforms, review the batch, and hand approved items to scheduling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad content-planning phrases when the user only wants a narrower answer. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when the user specifically wants batch social media content planned, and confirm the intended period, platforms, goals, and capacity before building the calendar. <br>
Risk: Generated calendars could include incorrect dates, unsuitable post volume, or content that does not fit the user's actual capacity. <br>
Mitigation: Present the calendar for user review and adjustment before any drafting or scheduling handoff. <br>
Risk: Suggested posting times could be mistaken for personalized analytics guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Label timing recommendations as general guidance unless the user provides analytics data. <br>
Risk: Scheduling a batch without review could publish unwanted or unapproved content. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before handing the batch to scheduling-and-queue. <br>
