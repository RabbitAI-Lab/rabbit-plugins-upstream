## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language evaluation skill that helps creators assess whether a knowledge-product niche is viable for overseas monetization across seven market and commercialization dimensions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[e211e](https://clawhub.ai/user/e211e) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, freelancers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate overseas knowledge-product ideas and receive a structured feasibility report with scoring, elimination signals, conclusions, and recommended niche entry points. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Benchmark numbers, platform metrics, pricing, or market examples may be stale or inaccurate. <br>
Mitigation: Verify traffic, sales, pricing, and community activity against current public sources before making commercial decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill can produce opinionated recommendations for business direction selection. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as research guidance and validate them with independent market tests before committing budget or product work. <br>
Risk: Some evaluated niches may involve regulated or policy-sensitive topics such as medical, financial, legal, health, fitness, or weight-loss content. <br>
Mitigation: Apply domain-specific compliance review and avoid outcome guarantees before publishing or selling related content. <br>
