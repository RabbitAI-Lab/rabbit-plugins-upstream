## Use Case: <br>
Customer success, support, operations, and account teams use this skill to compare feasible remediation options after facts and responsibility are clear enough for a decision. It helps structure conditional plans, approvals, timelines, customer communication points, and follow-up checks without making unauthorized commitments. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat generated remediation options as authorization to promise refunds, replacements, discounts, deadlines, or policy exceptions. <br>
Mitigation: Require approval and policy checks before communicating final commitments, and preserve the skill's distinction between standard, exception, and approval-required plans. <br>
Risk: Incomplete facts, unclear responsibility, or uncertain resources can lead to overconfident recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Use conditional plans when required inputs are missing or disputed, and confirm facts, resources, timelines, and approval authority before execution. <br>
