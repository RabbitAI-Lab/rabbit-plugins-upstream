## Description:

Creates and edits images, video, voice-overs, transcripts and subtitles with OceanToken models and saves assets into a project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oceantoken](https://clawhub.ai/user/oceantoken)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to generate or edit project images, video, narration and subtitles, estimate generation costs, and save the resulting assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media generation spends the user's OceanToken credits.

Mitigation: Estimate larger jobs and confirm the budget before approving costly or repeated generations.

Risk: Local reference images may be uploaded to OceanToken for editing or animation.

Mitigation: Review the images and obtain consent before sharing sensitive project assets.

Risk: Saving generated assets could overwrite project files.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination and ask before replacing an existing file.
