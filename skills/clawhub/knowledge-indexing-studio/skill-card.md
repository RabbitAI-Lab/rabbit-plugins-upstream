## Description:

Segment knowledge notes and update the knowledge-base index.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, knowledge managers, and agents use this skill to segment supplied knowledge notes into structured sections and update a section and backlink index from prepared segmented notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The top-level invocation wording is broad and could be selected for ambiguous knowledge-operation requests.

Mitigation: Review ambiguous requests and invoke only the requested stage: segment a supplied knowledge note or update the index from a prepared segmented note.
