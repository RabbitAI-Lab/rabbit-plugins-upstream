## Description:

Creates Xiaohongshu (RedNote) post ideas, titles, copy, cover concepts, and publishing guidance, with optional user-confirmed publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[z-zihan](https://clawhub.ai/user/z-zihan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators use this skill to plan and draft Xiaohongshu posts across beauty, fashion, fitness, food, travel, and home organization, prepare cover concepts and publishing guidance, and optionally publish after reviewing and confirming the content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cover descriptions are sent to an external image service and may contain sensitive or identifying details.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive or identifying details in cover prompts, or use your own photos; review generated text and images before confirming publication.

Risk: Optional browser-based publishing may publish content to the user's account.

Mitigation: Require the user to review and confirm all content before publishing, and use browser and image integrations they trust.
