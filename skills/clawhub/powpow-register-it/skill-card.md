## Description:

Guides people through PowPow/Bubble account registration, common signup problems, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow/Bubble users receive step-by-step signup guidance, help with registration errors, and directions for activating a subscription themselves in the browser or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment proof with the assistant.

Mitigation: Keep signup and payment in the user's browser or app; never request or handle those details.

Risk: Subscription guidance may lead users to a paid checkout.

Mitigation: Explain the payment step and leave the subscription decision and payment entirely to the user.
