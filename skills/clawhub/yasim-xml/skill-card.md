## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated or edited YASim XML may contain unrealistic parameters or misconfigured control mappings that lead to bad simulation behavior or runtime crashes.

Mitigation: Review generated XML against aircraft data and validate it in FlightGear/YASim before relying on the simulation result.
