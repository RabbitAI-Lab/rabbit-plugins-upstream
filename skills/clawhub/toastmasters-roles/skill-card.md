## Description: <br>
Splits complex tasks through a Toastmasters-style role model with officers such as President, VPE, VPM, and VPPR. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[nathanontheearth-spec](https://clawhub.ai/user/nathanontheearth-spec) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and teams use this skill to break complex work into Toastmasters officer responsibilities, assign accountable roles, and consolidate the result into an action plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad role-assignment or task-splitting requests. <br>
Mitigation: Install and invoke it as a planning helper when Toastmasters-style officer decomposition is desired. <br>
Risk: Generated role assignments or action plans may be incomplete or mismatched to the user's actual organization. <br>
Mitigation: Review the proposed assignments, responsibilities, dependencies, and timelines before acting on the plan. <br>
Risk: Artifact README includes ClawHub CLI publishing commands that affect a user's account if run intentionally. <br>
Mitigation: Run publishing or package-management commands only when you intend to publish or manage this skill from your own ClawHub account. <br>
