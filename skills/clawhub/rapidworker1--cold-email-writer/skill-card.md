## Description:

Drafts US B2B cold emails from a prospect brief and checks key message elements before a representative sends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External US B2B sales representatives and demand-generation teams use the skill to draft cold outreach and identify content or compliance issues for human review before sending.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checker can miss obvious problems and create false reassurance about compliance.

Mitigation: Independently verify sender details, truthful subjects, postal address, working opt-out, and claims before sending.

Risk: The optional Windows program is unnecessary for the documented local workflow and needs separate review.

Mitigation: Use the local workflow without the optional program unless it is needed and has been reviewed separately.
