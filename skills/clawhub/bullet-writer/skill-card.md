## Description:

Analyzes Amazon product details and review evidence to identify strengths, buyer questions, and gaps in five-point bullet copy, then suggests improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External Amazon sellers and marketplace operators use the skill to review product details and customer review evidence, then improve the product's five bullet points. It does not cover title writing, ad targeting, or publishing page changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release includes paid analysis and recurring monitoring, and conflicting safeguards may allow spending without fresh approval.

Mitigation: Use a corrected package with explicit approval for paid analysis and recurring monitoring; obtain clear approval before enabling either.
