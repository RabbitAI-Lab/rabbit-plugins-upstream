## Description: <br>
Daily English learning with spaced repetition, a built-in A1-B2 word bank, new words daily, quiz mode (MCQ/fill-in/spelling), streak tracking, and level progression. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External learners use this skill to practice English vocabulary through daily word study, spaced repetition review, quizzes, streak tracking, and level progression from A1 through B2. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: English learning progress, streaks, points, and SRS state are stored in a MEMORY.md profile block managed by the local agent. <br>
Mitigation: Keep each english-daily profile block scoped to one user, avoid manually editing the embedded SRS JSON, and check future updates replace only the intended english-daily profile block. <br>
