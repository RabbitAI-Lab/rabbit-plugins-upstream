## Description:

Use saved brand context, generate content and images, review and publish social posts, and read available Insights through Influence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[influence-so](https://clawhub.ai/user/influence-so)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Influence workspace users draft and review social posts, publish to authorized connected accounts, generate content and images, and read available account and post insights.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing to the wrong account, with incorrect content, images, or timing.

Mitigation: Review the exact destinations, text, images, settings, and schedule before approving publication.

Risk: AI generation can spend workspace credits.

Mitigation: Review quoted costs and confirm the spend before starting generation.
