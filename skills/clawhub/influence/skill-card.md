## Description:

Uses saved brand context to plan campaigns, draft and review content, schedule approved posts across connected social accounts, send approved WhatsApp Business messages, and read delivery status and account insights through Influence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[influence-so](https://clawhub.ai/user/influence-so)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Influence customers use this skill to prepare and review social campaigns, schedule approved posts, message selected WhatsApp Business recipients, and check delivery and account insights from their connected accounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts or messages could reach unintended accounts or recipients.

Mitigation: Review the exact destinations, recipients, content, and scheduled time before approving each action.

Risk: AI planning and image generation can spend IQ credits.

Mitigation: Check the stated credit cost or quote before confirming a billable action.

Risk: Connected-account campaign, contact, and insight data may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Use only the intended Influence account and scoped access; do not share credentials in chat.
