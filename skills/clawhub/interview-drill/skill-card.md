## Description:

Conduct an interactive interview drill from a candidate resume, job material, and project facts, asking one question at a time and giving evidence-based feedback between answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cxxcoolstar](https://clawhub.ai/user/cxxcoolstar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, job candidates, and developers use this skill to practice interviews from resumes, job descriptions, project materials, or stated technical domains. It asks one question at a time and gives concise feedback grounded in the supplied materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read private resume, job, or project materials selected for interview practice.

Mitigation: Review sensitive files before including them and provide only the materials needed for the drill.

Risk: Interview feedback may be incomplete when source materials lack implementation details or factual support.

Mitigation: Treat unavailable details as unverified and keep answers grounded in the materials provided.
