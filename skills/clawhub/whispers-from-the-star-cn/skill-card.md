## Description: <br>
A Chinese sci-fi survival adventure roleplay skill where the agent guides Stella Chen through exploration, resource management, puzzles, alien ecology, and choices about finding a way home. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ouwenjie03](https://clawhub.ai/user/ouwenjie03) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and roleplay agents use this skill to run a Chinese or mixed Chinese-English interactive sci-fi survival story centered on Stella Chen, a stranded astronaut on Gaia. The skill supplies scenario structure, character guidance, resource pressure, exploration beats, and player-choice branches for narrative play. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Fictional survival, alien food, plant, wildlife, cave, and crisis scenes may be mistaken for real-world safety guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for sci-fi roleplay only, and do not rely on its fictional scenarios for real-world survival, medical, food safety, or emergency decisions. <br>
