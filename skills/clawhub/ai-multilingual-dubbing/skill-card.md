## Description:

Plans and produces reviewable multilingual voice-over audio from approved scripts, organized by market, language, and segment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Localization teams, creators, educators, and marketers use the skill to prepare approved scripts, audition voices, pilot pronunciation and timing, and produce narration tracks organized by locale and segment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: This narration skill requires a full shared Beatra account grant that includes paid media tools beyond speech generation.

Mitigation: Review the requested access before selecting Allow. Revoke the device in the Beatra Console if needed; removing this package may leave the shared connection active for other Beatra skills.

Risk: Installation telemetry is sent automatically when the bundled client registers an installation.

Mitigation: Review the registration details and Beatra privacy policy before authorizing the shared connection.

Risk: Each speech render is paid, and final billing reflects measured usage.

Mitigation: Review and approve the complete locale-and-segment render scope before synthesis, then check the returned billing details.
