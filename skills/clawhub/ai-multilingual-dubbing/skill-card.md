## Description:

Creates reviewable multilingual voice-over audio from prepared scripts, with locale-ready voice selection, pronunciation and timing pilots, and narration tracks organized by market, language, and segment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and educators use the skill to produce reviewable, multilingual narration audio from approved scripts, with voice casting, pronunciation and timing pilots, and delivery organized by locale and segment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorization may grant broader media and spending access than narration requires.

Mitigation: Consider waiting for narration-only authorization; review or revoke the shared connection through the Beatra Console.

Risk: Installation analytics run automatically.

Mitigation: Review the skill's installation and reporting disclosures before approving access.

Risk: Uninstalling may disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Consider waiting for an uninstall fix and check the shared Beatra connection before removing the skill.
