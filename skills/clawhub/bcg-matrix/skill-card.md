## Description: <br>
Guides agents through BCG growth-share portfolio analysis to classify business units, map cash generation and absorption, and recommend invest, harvest, or exit decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, consultants, investors, and strategy teams use this skill to review portfolios with multiple business units competing for shared capital. It produces a portfolio map with quadrant assignments, trend arrows, cash-generator and cash-absorber views, and resource-allocation recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Strategic recommendations could be incorrect or misleading if market boundaries, competitor shares, or growth data are weak. <br>
Mitigation: Review the portfolio map against external market data and the skill's verification checklist before acting on investment, divestiture, or capital-reallocation recommendations. <br>
Risk: The matrix may oversimplify highly interdependent units or technology-transition markets where growth and share signals are unreliable. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for portfolios with distinct business units and named competitors, and supplement it with adjacent strategy analysis when unit synergies or market transitions are material. <br>
