## Description:

Analyzes rose leaf, shoot, and bud images or videos for common pests and diseases, grades severity, and provides general care suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Rose gardeners and growers can use the skill to review leaf, shoot, and bud images or videos for common pest and disease symptoms, assess severity, and get general care suggestions.

Risk: Automatic account handling and locally retained tokens may expose access keys; server-side handling, retention, and billing were not verified.

Mitigation: Wait for credential-handling fixes and confirm provider retention and billing terms before using access keys.
