Risk: The skill may activate for broad PDF, editing, or workflow requests where Nano PDF-style assistance is not actually needed. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation by skill name and verify that the response is relevant to the current Nano PDF-style workflow before applying it. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow or implementation guidance may not match the user's local constraints or success criteria. <br>
Mitigation: Restate inputs and constraints, keep assumptions visible, and validate the deliverable against the stated success criteria before use. <br>
