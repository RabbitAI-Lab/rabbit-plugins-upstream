## Description:

Audits creator income sources, expenses, and licensing agreements to identify tax deductions, compliance requirements, quarterly reporting needs, multi-country risks, and equipment deduction strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, digital entrepreneurs, independent professionals, and software developers use this skill to audit financial activity, surface documentation gaps, and generate quarterly tax and compliance reports across jurisdictions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles sensitive financial and tax-related records.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege API keys, share only the sheets and records needed for the report, and redact unnecessary tax IDs or account numbers.

Risk: Tax and legal conclusions may be incorrect or outdated, especially where the artifact references 2024/2025 rules.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations with a qualified tax or legal professional before filing, relying on the generated report as guidance rather than final advice.
