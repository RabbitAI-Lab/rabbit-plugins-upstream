## Description: <br>
Create, edit, replicate, and iteratively refine editable research and technical diagrams in diagrams.net/draw.io (.drawio XML) from prompts, papers, repositories, screenshots, or existing diagrams. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[holdyounger](https://clawhub.ai/user/holdyounger) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, and technical writers use this skill to create editable draw.io diagrams for research figures, system architectures, workflows, and high-fidelity reference-image reproductions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Previewing diagrams through diagrams.net may expose diagram contents to that service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid previewing sensitive content through the hosted embed unless the environment and data handling are approved. <br>
Risk: Large or high-fidelity diagram work can expand through repeated refinement cycles. <br>
Mitigation: Set an iteration or time budget before starting complex diagram requests. <br>
