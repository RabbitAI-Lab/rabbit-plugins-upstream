## Description:

Turn a novel into a fully playable game on the selected target platform. Orchestrates the whole adaptation pipeline — requirements intake, gameable deconstruction, concept selection, world and visual design, target-runtime build, and evidence-based QA — for a novel in any language.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Game designers and developers use this agent to turn a novel in any language into a playable game, coordinating source analysis, concept and world design, implementation, and evidence-based quality checks. It supports quick, director, and resume workflows for different levels of decision-making.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
