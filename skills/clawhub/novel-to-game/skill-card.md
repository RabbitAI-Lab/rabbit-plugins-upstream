## Description:

Orchestrates the adaptation of novels in any language into playable games through requirements intake, story deconstruction, concept selection, design, implementation, and evidence-based QA.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game creators and developers use this agent to turn a novel or story-writing project into a playable game, with staged creative decisions and evidence-based quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow relies on six separately installed companion skills, which are not included in this release.

Mitigation: Review each companion skill before installing it, and set a production budget with checkpoints for major creative decisions.

Risk: Adapting a source novel or using visual assets may raise rights and authorization questions.

Mitigation: Confirm source adaptation rights and asset permissions before committing to production or distribution.
