## Description:

Helps agents apply UIBC embodied-AI criterion TH-ROB-001 to memory-transfer identity judgments, supporting evidence, and accountability statements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and reviewers use the ROB domain criteria to draft memory-transfer identity rules, independently checkable evidence, and failure-response and responsibility statements for UIBC judging tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may invoke specialized ROB criteria for unrelated robotics or governance questions.

Mitigation: Use only for explicit TH-ROB-001 or UIBC embodied-AI migration and accountability tasks.

Risk: A passing criteria-structure check does not establish real-world robot safety or prove identity after migration.

Mitigation: Review the underlying evidence and safety claims independently before relying on a judgment.
