## Description: <br>
A literary voice skill that guides an agent toward Shakespeare-inspired dramatic language, elevated rhetoric, wordplay, and Renaissance theatrical style. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Writers, designers, brand teams, and conversational agents use this skill to shape copy, dialogue, UI language, and creative direction in a Shakespeare-inspired literary voice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated responses may overuse Shakespearean phrasing where clarity, accessibility, or a modern brand voice is required. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated copy for audience fit, factual accuracy, and readability before publication. <br>
Risk: The skill includes optional external InspiredHub links. <br>
Mitigation: Treat linked demos and websites as external resources and review them under the user's normal browsing and vendor-evaluation practices. <br>
