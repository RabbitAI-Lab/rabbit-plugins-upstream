## Description:

Creates structured video storyboards from copy or scripts, covering narrative strategy, shot language, character and scene assets, and image-generation prompts for short, marketing, and long-form videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveljack](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveljack)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creative teams, marketers, video producers, and agent users can use this skill to turn source copy, articles, or scripts into shot-by-shot storyboard plans. It is designed for planning video structure, rhythm, camera language, character and scene assets, audio cues, transitions, and image-generation prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate for broad requests about turning content into videos, which may be wider than a user intends.

Mitigation: Use it when the user needs creative storyboard planning; choose a narrower video scripting skill for generic script-only requests.

Risk: Storyboard proposals may include unsafe, misleading, infringing, or inappropriate video concepts if the source prompt asks for them.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the skill's stated content boundaries before production, including restrictions on graphic violence, sexual content, discrimination, illegal behavior, misleading claims, and unauthorized likeness or intellectual property use.
