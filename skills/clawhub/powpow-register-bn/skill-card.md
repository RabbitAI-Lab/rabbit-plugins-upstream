## Use Case:

External Bengali-speaking PowPow users use this skill to understand the product, register an account on the official PowPow site, troubleshoot registration issues, and activate a subscription without sharing credentials or payment proof with the agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment proof while asking for registration help.

Mitigation: Keep registration and payment on official PowPow pages and do not ask the user to share passwords, verification codes, or payment proof with the agent.

Risk: Users may expect the agent to complete payment or subscription activation inside the chat.

Mitigation: Direct users to the official PowPow subscription page and supported payment channels; provide guidance only and do not process payments.

Risk: Platform registration or subscription behavior may change after the skill's reference material was written.

Mitigation: Use the official PowPow website and subscription page as the current source of truth when platform behavior differs from the reference material.
