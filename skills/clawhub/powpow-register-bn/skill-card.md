## Use Case:

PowPow users can learn about the service, register their own account, resolve common sign-up errors, and find the appropriate subscription activation steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing passwords, verification codes, or payment proof with the assistant can expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Complete registration, verification, and payment only on official PowPow pages or in the app; do not share these details with the assistant.

Risk: Registration and subscription instructions may become outdated when PowPow changes its process.

Mitigation: Check the current instructions on the official PowPow registration and subscription pages before proceeding.
