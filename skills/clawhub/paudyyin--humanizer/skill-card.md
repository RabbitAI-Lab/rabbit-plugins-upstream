## Description: <br>
Remove AI-sounding patterns from generated text. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, writers, and editors use this skill to rewrite user-provided text so it sounds more natural while preserving meaning and tone. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Rewriting can unintentionally alter meaning, tone, or authorship-sensitive wording in academic, legal, compliance, or archival documents. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only on explicit requests and review the rewritten text against the original before applying changes. <br>
Risk: Language fallback behavior is poorly specified and may produce unsuitable edits for mixed-language or non-English text. <br>
Mitigation: Specify the intended language or locale when needed and have the user correct language-specific output before use. <br>
