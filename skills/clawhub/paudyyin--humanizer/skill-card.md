## Description:

Remove AI-sounding patterns from generated text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise AI-generated or formulaic text into more natural prose while preserving meaning and intended tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to change tone and wording, which may alter nuance in legal, policy, security, academic, or compliance-sensitive writing.

Mitigation: Review suggested edits carefully before relying on the revised text in sensitive contexts.

Risk: The skill may add personality or remove style markers in ways that do not match the author's intended voice.

Mitigation: Compare the rewrite with the original and preserve domain-specific phrasing or intentional stylistic choices.
