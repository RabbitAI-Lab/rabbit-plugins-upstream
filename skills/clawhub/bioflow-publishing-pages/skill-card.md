## Description:

Publishes or schedules a BioFlow page draft after showing what will change and obtaining the user's explicit confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

BioFlow page owners use this skill to review draft changes and publish their page immediately or schedule it to go live after explicit confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confirming the second publish call can make draft changes public or schedule them to become public.

Mitigation: Review the preview and scheduled time with the user, then proceed only after explicit confirmation of that preview.
