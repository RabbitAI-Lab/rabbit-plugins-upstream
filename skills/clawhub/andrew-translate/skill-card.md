## Description:

A three-stage translation workflow that drafts, critiques, and refines translations across languages and regional styles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linkxzhou](https://clawhub.ai/user/linkxzhou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People translating articles, technical documents, emails, subtitles, or other passages use this skill to produce polished translations, preserve formatting and terminology, and adapt wording to a requested region. It can also polish an existing translation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long translations can leave source text and draft translations in local .translate-work files, including sensitive content.

Mitigation: Review before translating confidential material; use a disposable workspace or delete .translate-work after use.
