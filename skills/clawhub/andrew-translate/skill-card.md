## Description:

Guides an agent through an initial translation, expert reflection, and revision to produce fluent, faithful translations tailored to the requested language and region.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linkxzhou](https://clawhub.ai/user/linkxzhou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to translate or refine articles, technical documentation, emails, subtitles, and other passages while preserving structure and terminology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long translations may leave source text and working drafts in the current workspace.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive text unless local storage is acceptable; remove .translate-work/ after use if needed.

Risk: Translations, especially for less familiar languages, may require human review for accuracy or regional usage.

Mitigation: Have a qualified speaker review important translations before publication.
