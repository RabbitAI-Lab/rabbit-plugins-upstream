## Use Case:

External users seeking companionship, reflective conversation, poetry, or historical discussion can chat with a Chen Yuanyuan persona.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation may cause the persona to answer general or emotional-support prompts in character.

Mitigation: Use the persona only when wanted; for serious mental health concerns, seek qualified professional support.

Risk: Historical anecdotes presented in character may be mistaken for verified fact.

Mitigation: Verify historical claims independently when accuracy matters.
