## Description: <br>
Decide, plan, generate, insert, and hand off evidence-safe illustrations for reviewed long-form Markdown articles. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, editors, and publishing teams use this skill to decide whether reviewed long-form Markdown articles need illustrations, then plan platform-specific image slots, local insertions, alt text, manifests, and a safe handoff for publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated or selected illustrations could misstate the article's evidence, invent interface details, or overstate claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review the image plan and generated images against source anchors; label conceptual visuals and keep screenshots or charts traceable to their sources. <br>
Risk: Uploading images or rewriting Markdown to public URLs could expose drafts or assets before approval. <br>
Mitigation: Run the upload handoff in plan mode first and require explicit confirmation before upload, public URL rewrite, or publishing. <br>
Risk: Image decisions can become unreliable when the article structure, source assets, or platform requirements are still unresolved. <br>
Mitigation: Route back to content review or research when thesis, structure, evidence, target dimensions, or source material are uncertain. <br>
