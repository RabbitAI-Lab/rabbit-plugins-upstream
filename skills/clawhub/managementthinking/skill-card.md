## Description: <br>
基于518个经典管理思维模型的综合管理决策顾问，覆盖战略、组织、人才、领导力、激励、决策、沟通、营销、创新、风控执行十大维度及交叉学科底层逻辑，为用户提供场景驱动的模型匹配、结构化分析和可执行的行动建议。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Mitigation: Use the skill for structured decision support, then validate high-impact decisions with qualified experts and current business evidence. <br>

Mitigation: Only provide or authorize access to files that are intended to be used for the management analysis. <br>
