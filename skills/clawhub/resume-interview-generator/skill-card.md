## Description: <br>
基于候选人简历和岗位信息，生成结构化面试题、笔试题、追问路径、评分标准与风险验证点。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Technical interviewers, recruiting leads, hiring managers, and technical managers use this skill to turn a candidate resume and role context into structured interview or written-test materials with follow-up paths, reference answers, scoring guidance, and resume-risk validation points. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Resume inputs can contain sensitive personal data. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized or consented resume text, remove contact details where possible, and avoid repeating sensitive fields in outputs. <br>
Risk: Generated interview questions, scoring notes, or candidate-risk signals could be mistaken for an automated hiring decision. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as interview preparation guidance and keep final candidate evaluation under human review. <br>
