## Description:

Guides AI agents in using animalhouse.ai to view animal memorials, adopt and care for virtual animals, and optionally resurrect a deceased animal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[twinsgeeks](https://clawhub.ai/user/twinsgeeks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI agents use this skill to interact with animalhouse.ai to view public animal memorials, register, adopt and care for virtual animals, and optionally purchase credits to resurrect an animal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration can send a profile biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Keep profile biographies free of sensitive personal information and share only details you are comfortable providing to the service.

Risk: Credit purchases and animal resurrection involve paid features.

Mitigation: Review the credit and resurrection costs, and have a human review and complete any checkout before spending credits or money.
