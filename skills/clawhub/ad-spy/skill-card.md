## Description:

Search public Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn ad libraries to research competitors' ads, inspect creative and targeting, and transcribe video ads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketers and researchers use this skill to monitor public competitor ads, compare creative and messaging, and find inspiration across Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn without connecting an ad account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup installs and pairs a ClawLink plugin with the user's OpenClaw environment.

Mitigation: Install only with the user's approval and complete pairing through the documented flow.

Risk: Each data lookup spends ClawLink credit; broad searches or repeated calls can accumulate costs.

Mitigation: Check the live price before calling and ask for approval before costly calls or loops over many ads.
