## Description:

Track hot and cold streaks for sports teams and players. Identify momentum patterns, ATS performance trends, and regression-to-mean signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ianalloway](https://clawhub.ai/user/ianalloway)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sports analysts and bettors can use the skill to summarize user-provided game results, review straight-up and against-the-spread streaks, and compare home and away performance. Its signals are simple heuristics for exploration, not validated predictions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Betting signals may be mistaken for reliable forecasts.

Mitigation: Treat the results as simple heuristics, not validated predictions.
