## Description: <br>
Track expenses via natural language, get spending summaries, and set budgets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jhillin8](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhillin8) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to log everyday expenses in natural language, categorize spending, track category budgets, and request summaries, reports, or CSV exports while keeping data local. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Expense and budget details may remain in local Clawd memory. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive financial details unless local storage is acceptable, and manage retained local memory according to your environment's controls. <br>
Risk: CSV exports may create local files containing spending records. <br>
Mitigation: Review export location and file contents before sharing, syncing, or retaining exported CSV files. <br>
