## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn validated demand for ontology-style productivity workflows into practical plans, checklists, artifacts, analysis, code changes, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify ontology-style productivity work, create concrete local-hardware-friendly deliverables, and verify outputs against stated success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms such as knowledge, graph, creating, and bug fix may activate the skill more often than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow trigger terms or disable implicit invocation where precise routing is important. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance or proposed code changes could be incorrect for a user's specific environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed artifacts, run applicable checks, and verify outputs against the user's success criteria before deployment. <br>
