## Description:

Turn any industry into a daily intelligence briefing by searching, filtering, writing, and delivering structured reports to nine channels with machine-checked formatting and a business review gate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Industry analysts and business teams use the skill to produce recurring, source-linked daily briefings for a chosen industry. The workflow includes a business review gate before delivery to configured channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports deployed through GitHub Pages may be publicly visible.

Mitigation: Review report contents and intended visibility before enabling GitHub Pages deployment.

Risk: The security evidence reports an SMTP certificate-verification weakness for email delivery.

Mitigation: Review certificate verification before enabling email delivery; use another channel for sensitive reports until it is addressed.
