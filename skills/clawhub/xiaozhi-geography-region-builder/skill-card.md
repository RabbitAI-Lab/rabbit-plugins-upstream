## Description:

Guides students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions instead of memorizing place names.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking secondary-school students use this skill to learn a four-step method for describing and comparing world and Chinese regions, with guidance adapted to their grade level.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, minor-consent expectations, and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, consent rules, and crisis referral channels before use in another jurisdiction.

Risk: Student location or learning records could expose personal information.

Mitigation: Ask only for county-level hometown information; keep records session-only by default and obtain explicit consent before saving or sharing.

Risk: Incorrect geographic boundaries or generated exercises could mislead students.

Mitigation: Use the relevant textbook and standard maps for boundaries, avoid drawing or altering borders, and check generated questions before presenting them.
