## Description:

Guides students through locating, characterizing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions, from continents and countries to regions of China and their hometowns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this skill to learn a repeatable method for describing and comparing regions, practice geography questions, and connect regional features. It also supports educators preparing region-focused learning activities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students discussing their hometown could reveal more precise location information than needed.

Mitigation: Keep hometown discussion to county or district level and do not request street, neighborhood, or home address details.

Risk: Geography assignments may involve outdoor observations or community interviews.

Mitigation: Follow school activity rules, go with an adult and a group, and conduct interviews in public places rather than entering homes.
