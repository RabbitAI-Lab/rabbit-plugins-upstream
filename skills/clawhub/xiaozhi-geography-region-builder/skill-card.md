## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved learning records or sharing integrations may expose more student learning information than intended.

Mitigation: Keep use session-only by default; enable saved records, sharing, or reminders separately with consent, and review the related profile and reminder skills before enabling those integrations.

Risk: A hometown discussion could prompt a student to disclose an unnecessarily precise location.

Mitigation: Keep hometown examples at county or district level and do not ask for a street address or more precise location.
