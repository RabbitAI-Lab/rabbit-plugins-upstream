## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to practice describing a region's location and characteristics, comparing regions, and explaining their connections within Chinese geography curricula.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize language, curriculum, consent rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may not fit students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize the language, curriculum, consent rules, and local referral contacts before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Learning-progress records and reminders could reveal a student's information without permission.

Mitigation: Review profile and reminder permissions; require explicit consent before saving progress or scheduling reminders.
