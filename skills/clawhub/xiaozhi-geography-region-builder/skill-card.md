## Description:

Guides students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions using curriculum-aligned examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators in mainland China's Chinese-language K–12 setting use this skill to practice describing and comparing regions, from world geography to their home county. Elementary students require adult supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, crisis contacts, and protections for minors may not fit use outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, crisis resources, language, and minor-consent rules before use elsewhere.

Risk: Optional profile memory, cross-skill or parent sharing, and reminders may expose student information without permission.

Mitigation: Enable each feature only after explicit student or guardian consent; otherwise keep the session self-contained.
