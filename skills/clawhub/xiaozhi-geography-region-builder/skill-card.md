## Description:

Guides students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions using maps and curriculum-aligned examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students and their educators use this Chinese-language tutoring skill to learn a repeatable four-step method for understanding geographic regions and answering regional-comparison questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, minor-consent requirements, and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student location and learning records may expose personal information when retained or shared.

Mitigation: Keep tutoring session-only by default; request explicit consent before profile memory, cross-skill sharing, or reminders, and ask about hometown only at county or district level.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize learning materials, minor-consent rules, and emergency contacts before wider deployment; confirm the student's location before giving local crisis numbers.
