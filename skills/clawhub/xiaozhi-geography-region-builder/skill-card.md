## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking geography students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting regions using a four-step learning method.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students and educators use this skill to learn how to characterize geographic regions, compare them consistently, and explain connections between them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K–12 geography curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum or territorial descriptions may be inaccurate or unsuitable outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Check explanations against the applicable curriculum, textbook, and standard maps; review local requirements before use elsewhere.

Risk: A minor's learning profile or location details may be shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep learning records off by default, obtain explicit consent before storing or sharing them, and avoid collecting a precise home address.

Risk: Crisis referrals may use the wrong emergency contacts outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and review local emergency contact guidance before deployment.
