## Description:

帮助初高中学生通过定位、归纳特征、比较和联系四步认识地理区域，并用同一方法分析新区域。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中地理学习者，指导他们描述区域位置和特征、比较不同区域并说明区域间联系；教师或家长也可陪同学生使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; curriculum, minor-consent requirements and crisis contacts require localization elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and emergency contacts may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize grade levels, consent rules and crisis referral contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: Minor students could disclose precise home locations or have learning profiles shared without consent.

Mitigation: Ask for no address more precise than county or district; keep records session-only unless the student opts in and confirms each update.

Risk: Regional boundaries or territorial descriptions may be inaccurate if generated without reference material.

Mitigation: Use the applicable curriculum and standard maps; do not draw or alter national borders or administrative divisions.
