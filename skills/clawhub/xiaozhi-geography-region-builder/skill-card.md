## Description:

Guides Chinese secondary-school students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions using maps and curriculum-aligned examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use the skill to practice describing geographic locations and features, comparing regions, and explaining regional connections in Chinese middle- and high-school geography.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused curriculum; localize curriculum, consent practices, and crisis referrals before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A child's learning profile or reminders may retain or share sensitive progress information.

Mitigation: Keep memory and reminders off by default; enable them only with informed consent and confirm profile updates individually.

Risk: Chinese curriculum and crisis-referral guidance may be inappropriate in other locations.

Mitigation: Adapt curriculum, consent handling, and local referral contacts before deploying outside the intended Chinese K12 tutoring context.
