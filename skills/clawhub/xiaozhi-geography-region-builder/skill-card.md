## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through locating, describing, comparing, and connecting geographic regions, with curriculum-aligned support for advanced high-school topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill for Chinese-language geography tutoring: building regional understanding, comparing places, and practicing mainland Chinese middle- and high-school geography topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K–12 curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records, cross-skill sharing, or reminders may expose student information.

Mitigation: Enable these features only with student or guardian consent and use the named platform controls to review or stop them.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis referrals may not fit users outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the language, curriculum, consent rules, and emergency contacts before use in another locale.
