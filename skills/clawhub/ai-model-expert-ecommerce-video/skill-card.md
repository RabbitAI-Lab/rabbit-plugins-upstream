## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uploads user-selected media to AI-HIVE for ecommerce video generation, which can expose confidential, regulated, or third-party content.

Mitigation: Review all files before upload, avoid confidential or regulated media, and confirm permission and AI-HIVE retention terms for third-party assets.

Risk: Video generation can incur cost, and retrying after local timeouts may duplicate submissions.

Mitigation: Confirm prompt, budget, routing mode, and batch size before generation; preserve task IDs and query existing tasks instead of resubmitting after a timeout.

Risk: Generated ecommerce videos may contain inaccurate product claims, brand misuse, or rights issues if prompts or references are not checked.

Mitigation: Verify product facts, brand elements, claims, calls to action, and usage rights before publishing generated outputs.
