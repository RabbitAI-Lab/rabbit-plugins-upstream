# Cinematic Camera Script Generator / cinematic-camera-script

## Overview

Generate professional cinematic camera movement scripts and AI video generation prompts in one click. Upload a reference image or describe your scene in words, and receive second-by-second storyboard breakdowns, camera movement parameters, and BGM beat-sync rhythm—ready for live-action filming or AI video generation.

**Core Value**

- **Smart image analysis**: Upload an image to automatically identify characters, scene, and lighting, generating a perfectly matched camera script
- **Second-by-second storyboard**: Complete timeline breakdown with subject, camera movement, lighting, and beat-sync for every shot
- **One-click video prompt**: The output prompt can be pasted directly into AI video tools—no manual assembly needed
- **Multi-style support**: Suits formal wear, street fashion, ancient Chinese style, sportswear, and more

**Intended Users**

- 🎬 **Short video creators** — Quickly produce cinematic scripts to elevate video quality and completion rates
- 📸 **Photographers / Directors** — Get professional camera movement parameters and composition guidance for live shoots
- 🤖 **AI video enthusiasts** — Generate complete prompts in one click for AI video generation tools
- 🎵 **Beat-sync video makers** — Camera rhythm precisely aligned with BGM, eliminating manual syncing

## Usage Guide

Simply describe your needs in natural language—no commands to memorize.

### Quick Reference

| Intent | Example Phrase | Result |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Image to script | "Generate a cinematic camera script based on this image" | Auto-analyze the image, output second-by-second storyboard and full prompt |
| Description to script | "4 people in suits on a rooftop, cool cinematic tone, 10s vertical" | Generate a style-matched camera script from description |
| Specify style | "Street fashion style, 6 people, 15 seconds" | Choose a built-in template and customize by parameters |
| Generate video | "Generate the video directly from this script" | One-click AI video generation from the script |

### Output Example

After generation, you will receive a complete script with the following modules:

1. **Script overview**: Style, duration, BGM, quality standard
2. **Detailed storyboard**:

```
[Shot 1 | 0-2s]
Frame: Extreme close-up of character ①, bokeh background
Camera: Static, shallow depth of field
Lighting: Rim light contours face, rich shadow layers
Beat-sync: Entry syncs with first beat of BGM

[Shot 2 | 2-4s]
Frame: Camera orbits behind character ①, revealing full scene
Camera: Slow counter-clockwise orbit, 15-30 degrees
...

[Shot N | Closing freeze]
...
```

3. **Core camera movement rules**
4. **Live-action filming tips**
5. **★ Complete video generation prompt** (ready to copy and use)
