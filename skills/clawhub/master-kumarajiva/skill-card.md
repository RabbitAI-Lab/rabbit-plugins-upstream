## Description:

Provides citation-grounded Buddhist teaching responses in the voice of Kumārajīva for Madhyamaka, Prajñā, Lotus Sutra, Diamond Sutra, Vimalakīrti, and related topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill to answer Buddhist doctrine and translation-style questions with CBETA-cited support. The skill emphasizes Madhyamaka, Prajñā, Lotus, Diamond, Vimalakīrti, and related Kumārajīva materials while requiring unsupported doctrinal claims to be omitted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Religious or doctrinal responses may be mistaken for formal practice guidance.

Mitigation: Present the skill as learning support and retain the artifact disclaimer directing formal practice questions to qualified teachers.

Risk: When local excerpts are insufficient, doctrinal search queries may be sent to FoJin.

Mitigation: Use local excerpts first, disclose FoJin fallback when used, and avoid sending sensitive personal information in search queries.

Risk: A literary religious voice can make unsupported claims sound authoritative.

Mitigation: Require CBETA citations for doctrinal claims and remove claims that cannot be supported by declared sources or verified FoJin results.
