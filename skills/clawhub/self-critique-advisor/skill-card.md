## Description: <br>
Guides users or agents through structured self-review of beliefs, judgments, and decisions using the Paul-Elder critical thinking framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chinaonlineniu](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinaonlineniu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and agents use this skill to review decisions, detect cognitive bias, evaluate reasoning quality, and form calibrated conclusions with confidence and update conditions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat structured self-reflection as mental-health treatment. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for decision review and reasoning calibration only; seek professional help for persistent distress or mental-health needs. <br>
Risk: Repeated self-critique may intensify negative self-judgment or emotional volatility. <br>
Mitigation: Pause use when emotional swings increase and prioritize emotional support before continuing the workflow. <br>
Risk: The skill can surface plausible but incomplete reasoning if the user supplies limited context. <br>
Mitigation: Review the output, add missing evidence, and treat confidence scores and update conditions as prompts for further checking. <br>
