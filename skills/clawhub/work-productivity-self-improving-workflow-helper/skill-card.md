## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users and skill maintainers create practical self-improving and proactive-agent workflows for bug fixing, setup hardening, reliability improvement, and adjacent skill design. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn self-improving or proactive-agent requests into concrete workflows, checklists, analyses, code changes, or decision support that can be checked against stated success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms and implicit invocation can cause the skill to appear for ordinary productivity or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the requested outcome and review generated workflows, code, or skill changes before applying them. <br>
Risk: Workflow or skill-change proposals may be incomplete or misleading when important constraints are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Validate outputs against explicit success criteria and ask for missing information when it materially changes the result. <br>
