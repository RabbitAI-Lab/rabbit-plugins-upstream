## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn self-improving and proactive-agent workflow needs into practical plans, checklists, analysis, artifacts, or implementation support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to convert self-improving or proactive-agent work into actionable workflows, checklists, decision aids, analysis, code changes, or verification notes that can be executed on ordinary local hardware. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as self, improving, proactive, and bug fix may cause the skill to activate for generic productivity or self-improvement requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review invocation behavior after installation and narrow trigger phrases if precise routing matters. <br>
