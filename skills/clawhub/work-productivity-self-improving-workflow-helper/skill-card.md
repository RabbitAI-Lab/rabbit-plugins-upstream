## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt self-improving and proactive agent patterns into practical local workflows for bug fixing, setup hardening, reliability improvement, and adjacent skill creation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad implicit invocation can make the helper appear in unrelated productivity or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or narrower trigger terms, and review generated plans or changes before applying them. <br>
