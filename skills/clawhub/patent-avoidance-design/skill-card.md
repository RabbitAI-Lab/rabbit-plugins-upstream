## Description:

Provides a TRIZ-based patent avoidance design workflow for analyzing patents, restoring functional models, generating non-infringing alternatives, and assessing infringement criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent analysts, product engineers, and legal-adjacent teams use this skill to convert patent claims into functional models, identify TRIZ trimming directions, search for cross-domain solutions, and draft patent-avoidance design alternatives. It is intended to support analysis and design work, not to replace qualified legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent-avoidance or non-infringement conclusions may be incomplete, incorrect, or unsuitable for business decisions without legal review.

Mitigation: Have a qualified patent professional review all freedom-to-operate, non-infringement, and design-around conclusions before acting on them.

Risk: Patent files, product specifications, or business requirements may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Use only properly authorized patent data sources and avoid uploading confidential materials unless the configured services and access controls are appropriate.
