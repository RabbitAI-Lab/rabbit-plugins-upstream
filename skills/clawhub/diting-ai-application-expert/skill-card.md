## Description: <br>
Diting Ai Application Expert helps agents advise on enterprise AI use-case design, tool selection, change management, data privacy, AI policy, and ROI analysis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tuobadaidai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuobadaidai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business leaders, HR or operations teams, and implementation advisors use this skill to plan practical AI adoption, compare AI tools, manage organizational change, evaluate data privacy and governance needs, and estimate AI investment returns. It is not intended for pure algorithm development or model training work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: AI compliance, privacy, and legal recommendations may affect high-impact business decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill output as planning guidance and review decisions with qualified legal, security, or compliance reviewers before implementation. <br>
Risk: ROI estimates and tool comparisons may rely on incomplete business, cost, or vendor information. <br>
Mitigation: Validate assumptions with current pricing, internal cost data, pilot results, and finance review before committing budget. <br>
Risk: AI-generated adoption plans can miss operational edge cases or create overreliance on automated decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Require human fallback, confidence labels, and evidence checks for sensitive workflows and high-impact AI use cases. <br>
