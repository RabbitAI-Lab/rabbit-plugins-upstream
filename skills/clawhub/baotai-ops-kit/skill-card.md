## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated medical marketing copy may contain claims, success-rate language, or platform-specific wording that requires review before publication. <br>
Mitigation: Have the hospital or a qualified reviewer confirm medical claims, statistics, advertising compliance, and publication readiness before use. <br>
Risk: Patient-case content may expose sensitive health details or imply identifiable personal information if reused without review. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized cases, remove identifying details, and confirm consent and privacy requirements before publishing patient stories. <br>
Risk: Generated educational or customer-service content may be mistaken for individualized medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Route medical guidance through qualified clinical review, avoid prescription details or dosage instructions, and add appropriate disclaimers where the publishing channel requires them. <br>
