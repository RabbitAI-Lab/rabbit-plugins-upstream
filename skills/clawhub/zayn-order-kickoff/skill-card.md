## Description: <br>
Coordinates confirmed hardware, equipment, and spare-parts order kickoff work across purchasing, engineering, QC, packaging, logistics, finance, and sales so teams can verify supply, compatibility, firmware, testing, serial labels, packaging, customs requirements, ownership, deadlines, and risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Operations, sales, procurement, engineering, quality, logistics, and finance teams use this skill after an order is confirmed to prepare kickoff inputs, identify missing or conflicting facts, define department checklists, assign owners, and track follow-up decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users could treat the coordination output as authority to update order records, change payment or logistics facts, or promise delivery dates. <br>
Mitigation: Verify payment, logistics, deadlines, responsibilities, and customer commitments in source systems before acting on the plan. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or conflicting kickoff inputs could lead to premature analysis or unclear ownership. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's parameter status table and minimum-run conditions to mark missing, conflicting, or unverified facts before producing a formal kickoff plan. <br>
