# Hook ideas for faceless reels

The hook is the first spoken line plus the first picture. It has about one second. Eight words or fewer. Start on the surprise. The picture shows the same thing as the line.

Fill the brackets with true, sourced facts. Never invent a number.

## Hook types

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Impossible comparison | "[Big thing] would [vanish or fit] inside [other thing]." | "Everest vanishes down here." |
| Number with a thing | "[Number] [things]. [One consequence.]" | "Eleven kilometres of water above you." (check the number) |
| Broken rule | "You were taught [rule]. It is wrong." | "You were taught [common belief]. The evidence says otherwise." (only if it holds up) |
| Stage clock | "One minute after [event]." | "One minute after the Sun goes out." |
| Either-or | "Would you rather [A] or [B]?" | "Would you rather lose your phone or your keys?" |
| Myth bust | "[Common belief]. Not true." | "Goldfish memory. Not true." (check the fact) |
| Ending first | "[Result]. Here is how." | "He built a castle in one night. Here is how." |
| Scale shock | "[Small thing] is [bigger or older] than [big thing]." | "[Tree] is older than [country]." |
| Direct warning | "Never [do thing] to [object]." | "Never [safe, true instruction]." (no unsafe or untrue claims) |
| Unfinished question | "What happens when [odd condition]?" | "What happens when a train never stops?" |
| Specific moment | "At [time] on [date], [event]." | "At noon on 8 December 1941, the sky went dark." (use true events) |
| Before and after | "This was [state]. Now it is [state]." | "This was a river. Now it is a road." |

## How to write five and pick one

1. Write the surprise as one plain sentence.
2. Write it five ways using five different types above.
3. Cut every word before the first picture-making word.
4. Keep the one you can see. The best hook is the one where you can name the picture at once.

## Hooks by format

- **Diorama build:** "This whole [place] fits on a desk." Show the finished piece for one second.
- **Claymation:** "This clay [character] has one day to [goal]." Show the character at the problem.
- **Animal facts:** "[Animal] can [fact]." Show the animal doing it.
- **Would you rather:** "Would you rather [A] or [B]?" Show both in one frame.
- **How deep is the ocean:** "[Big thing] vanishes down here." Show the big thing going under.
- **What-if stages:** "What if [event]?" Show the world just before it happens.
- **Stickman:** "This stick man [does odd thing]." Show the figure doing it.
- **Block-world:** "He has one night to [goal]." Show the world at dusk.
- **2.5D history cartoon:** "In [year], [people] did [impossible thing]." Show the place and the date caption.

## What to avoid

- Greetings and channel names: "Hey guys", "Welcome back".
- Announcements: "In this video", "Today we", "Let me show you".
- Vague words: "amazing", "incredible", "insane", "you won't believe".
- A hook the video does not pay off.
- A hook with two ideas.
- A hook that needs the viewer to know the topic first.

## The check for a hook

1. Under 9 words.
2. Starts on the surprise.
3. One picture matches it.
4. The caption word for the first spoken word is on screen from frame one.
5. A stranger can say what the reel is about after hearing it.
