## Description:

Helps create faceless short-form video hooks, scripts, and shot lists, then guides production in an AI reels maker.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0xnirlin](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xnirlin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and social media teams use this skill to turn a topic into hooks, a structured short-form video script, a shot list, and posting guidance. They can then take the material to an AI video maker such as Ziztu Reel Factory.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Topics entered into Ziztu Reel Factory may be sent to Ziztu.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting personal or confidential information, and review the topic before sending it to the service.
