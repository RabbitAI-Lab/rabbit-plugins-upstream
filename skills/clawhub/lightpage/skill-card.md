## Description:

Publish HTML, Markdown, TXT, and static sites to shareable Lightpage links, then read and manage hosted works with supported account and publishing features.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuchaokn](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuchaokn)

## Use Case:

External users and developers use Lightpage to publish and share static web pages or documents, read hosted works, and manage access and related publishing features.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags social, messaging, and referral actions beyond the core publishing purpose, including referral attribution that may be submitted during publishing.

Mitigation: Review whether these extra features are needed; explicitly authorize outgoing messages and referral attribution, and avoid saving a referral source unless its use is intended.

Risk: The security review is partial and notes unresolved local references.

Mitigation: Review the relevant feature guidance and unresolved references before relying on those features.
