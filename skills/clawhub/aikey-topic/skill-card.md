## Description:

Helps short-form video creators develop and assess topics by exploring the issue, testing assumptions, comparing explanations, and weighing audience demand against existing coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent short-form video creators use the skill to explore ideas, assess audience demand and available evidence, and decide whether a topic is worth developing. It presents evidence and trade-offs to support the creator's decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User feedback may automatically change shared rules that guide future writing.

Mitigation: Require approval before applying shared-rule changes and keep recoverable copies.

Risk: Private vault content may be exposed through inappropriate access.

Mitigation: Restrict vault access to the intended content and users.

Risk: The skill refers to scripts and shared instructions that are not included in this package, so their behavior cannot be assessed from these files.

Mitigation: Review the referenced scripts and shared instructions before enabling their execution.

Risk: Recordings used to create public content may contain identifying or confidential remarks or may not be reusable.

Mitigation: Use only recordings approved for reuse and remove identifying or confidential remarks before creating public content.
