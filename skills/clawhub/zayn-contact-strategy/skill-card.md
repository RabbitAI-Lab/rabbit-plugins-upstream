## Description:

Plans prioritized departments, roles, contact discovery paths, first outreach angles, preparation items, and confidence labels for manually confirmed target companies without bulk email guessing or automated outreach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business development and sales users use this skill to plan contact strategy for already screened companies, including which departments and roles to prioritize, where to look for contacts, and how to frame a first approach. It keeps uncertain emails and contacts labeled by confidence and leaves actual outreach to the user or another reviewed workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested contact paths or email patterns may be uncertain or inappropriate for a specific business context.

Mitigation: Review suggested contacts manually, verify uncertain email patterns, and preserve the skill's confidence labels before outreach.

Risk: Using contact discovery guidance for outreach can create privacy, anti-spam, or platform terms issues.

Mitigation: Follow applicable privacy laws, anti-spam rules, and platform terms before contacting any person or organization.
