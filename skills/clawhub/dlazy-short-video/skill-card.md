## Description:

Creates 15–25-second vertical social videos with voiceover and burned-in subtitles through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to generate finished vertical short-form videos for TikTok, Shorts, Reels, and similar platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only prompts and files approved for upload to the service.

Risk: The CLI may store an API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local key and rotate or revoke it if compromised.
