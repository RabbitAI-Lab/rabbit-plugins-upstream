## Description:

Helps create finished 15–25-second vertical social videos with a hook-led storyboard, generated shots, voiceover, and burned-in subtitles through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to produce short vertical videos for social platforms rather than scripts or conversion-focused product ads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, project context, and attached files may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only content authorized for upload; omit secrets, private personal data, and regulated material unless approved.

Risk: A saved dLazy API key may remain usable if exposed.

Mitigation: Protect the key and rotate or revoke it if exposure is suspected.
