## Description:

Creates finished 15–25-second vertical social videos from a hook-first storyboard, generated shots, voiceover, and burned-in subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and social media teams use this skill to generate finished vertical short videos for platforms such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels, and Douyin. It supports story-led social clips rather than conversion-focused product ads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected attachments are sent to dLazy’s hosted service; attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Use the intended organization and project, and attach only files you are comfortable sharing with the service.

Risk: Using the hosted video service may incur usage charges.

Mitigation: Check the organization’s available credits and usage before generating videos.
