## Description:

Generates finished 15–25 second 9:16 social videos from a hook-first storyboard, with shot clips, TTS voiceover, and burned-in subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to plan and generate short vertical videos for social platforms such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Douyin.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy-hosted services, and the scan guidance notes that unresolved references remain.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive material unless the service is approved for it; review the CLI package and unresolved references before installation.

Risk: Using the CLI requires a dLazy API key and project access.

Mitigation: Consider whether the CLI login and project-listing behavior fits your needs, and keep the organization key scoped and revocable.
