## Description:

Creates finished 15–25 second vertical social videos with a hook-led storyboard, shot visuals, voiceover, and burned-in subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and marketers can use this skill to produce short, vertical videos for social platforms, including TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected attachments are sent to dLazy, and video generation can consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Choose the intended project, upload only approved media, and confirm expected credit use before generation.

Risk: Logging in stores an organization API key in the local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Use an account-scoped key and revoke it when it is no longer needed.
