## Description:

Creates finished 15–25 second vertical social videos from a hook-first storyboard, generated shot visuals and voiceover, and burned-in subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Social media creators and marketing teams use the skill to turn a brief into a finished 9:16 short for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or Douyin.
