## Description:

Turn one product photo into a vertical product video that speaks. This AI product video generator and product video maker builds ecommerce product videos, product ads, and commerce short videos from a single photo — composing a 9:16 opening frame, writing a short script from what the photo shows and the details you supply, voicing it with a selected narrator, and directing one finished clip ready to post. Use it for product launches, listing videos, shoppable social posts, storefront promos, and turning a phone snap of merchandise into a video that sells, with no shoot, no crew, and no editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External merchants and ecommerce teams use this skill to turn a product photo and merchant-supplied product details into a short, narrated vertical video for product listings, launches, and social commerce. It prepares the image and narration, then creates one finished clip after the user confirms paid stages.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting uses shared account permissions broader than product-video creation, including music-generation and voice-writing permissions.

Mitigation: Review the shared authorization and its requested permissions before connecting.

Risk: Product photos and scripts are sent to Beatra, and installation metadata is reported automatically.

Mitigation: Submit only photos and copy appropriate to share, and review Beatra's data-handling information.

Risk: Removing this skill may leave the shared connection active for other installed Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Revoke device access in the Beatra Console when access is no longer wanted.
