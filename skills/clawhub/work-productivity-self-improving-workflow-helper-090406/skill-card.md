## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn self-improving workflow needs into practical plans, checklists, templates, implementation aids, and validation notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify a desired workflow outcome, plan practical local-hardware-friendly steps, produce an actionable artifact, and check the result against visible success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may select this skill for ordinary requests that mention terms such as self, improving, errors, or bug fix. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation by skill name or narrow implicit activation rules before enabling it in shared or sensitive agent environments. <br>
