## Description:

Know when a scheduled job stops or a website goes down. Set up free monitoring that continues after the agent exits, then expand coverage when needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use HookPulse to monitor scheduled jobs and website availability, review failures and recovery events, and configure alerts that continue after an agent session ends.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Monitoring continues after the agent session and can contact configured destinations.

Mitigation: Use authorized routines, URLs, and alert destinations, then verify monitor state and recovery.

Risk: Monitor credentials may be returned, and expanded coverage can incur charges.

Mitigation: Keep tokens private and confirm spending limits and current pricing before paid expansion.

Risk: The live service and linked contracts have not been independently verified.

Mitigation: Review current terms and confirm check-in and notification behavior before relying on monitoring.
