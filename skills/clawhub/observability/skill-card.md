## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Observability recommendations may affect production telemetry cost, retention, alerting, or sampling behavior if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations against the production environment before applying changes, especially sampling, redaction, logging volume, and skill installation prompts. <br>
Risk: Logging guidance can influence whether sensitive data is captured in telemetry. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm redaction and data-handling controls before adopting logging or tracing recommendations in systems that process secrets or personal data. <br>
