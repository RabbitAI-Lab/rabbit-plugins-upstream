## Description:

Localizes video ads for target markets by adapting scripts, generating matching voiceovers, syncing talking faces, rebuilding captions and on-screen text, and preparing cuts for native review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketing teams use the skill to adapt short video ads for new TikTok Shop and Meta markets, including localized scripts, voice, captions, on-screen text, and edits. A native speaker reviews local wording, prices, claims, and calls to action before launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media and voice are processed by external generation services, with privacy, retention, and cost implications.

Mitigation: Use media and voices you have permission to process externally, and check provider privacy, retention, and generation costs.

Risk: Localization can change the meaning of product claims, pricing, or calls to action across markets.

Mitigation: Have a native speaker check the localized hook, listing price and currency, approved claims, platform wording, and AI label before launch.

Risk: The workflow relies on companion editing scripts and the revisions installed alongside the skill.

Mitigation: Review companion editing scripts and installed revisions separately.
