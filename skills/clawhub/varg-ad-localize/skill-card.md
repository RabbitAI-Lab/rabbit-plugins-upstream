## Description:

Localizes short video ads for social commerce markets by adapting the script, voice, captions, on-screen text, and edit for each market, then preparing the result for native-speaker review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and ad teams use this skill to adapt a winning short video ad for another market, including localized wording, voice, captions, prices, and on-screen text, with a native-review checklist before launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
