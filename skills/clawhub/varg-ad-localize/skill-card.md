## Description:

Localizes video ads for new markets by adapting the script, voice, timing, captions, and on-screen text, then guiding native review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External sellers and ad-production teams use this skill to adapt short video ads for TikTok Shop and Meta markets, including local scripts, voice, captions, on-screen text, and review guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source scripts or footage may contain confidential material that is sent to Varg or its processing providers.

Mitigation: Share only material authorized for processing and review the providers' retention terms before uploading confidential content.

Risk: Voice cloning can reproduce a speaker's voice without their consent.

Mitigation: Obtain the speaker's permission before creating or using a voice clone.

Risk: Localized ads may contain inaccurate local prices, unsupported claims, or a mismatched call to action.

Mitigation: Check the local offer and approved claims, then have a native speaker review the ad and its platform compliance before launch.
