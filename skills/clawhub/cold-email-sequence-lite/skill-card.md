## Description:

Drafts a two-touch cold outreach sequence from a prospect brief, with subject options, email skeletons, and basic copy checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nenadzivotic](https://clawhub.ai/user/nenadzivotic)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and outreach teams use this skill to draft a short opener and polite follow-up for their own prospects from an ideal-customer profile and pain points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach could become unwanted mass solicitation.

Mitigation: Use only for legitimate prospects, review each draft, and retain the polite opt-out follow-up.

Risk: An unreviewed or changed release could introduce unexpected behavior.

Mitigation: Prefer a reviewed or pinned release before installation.
