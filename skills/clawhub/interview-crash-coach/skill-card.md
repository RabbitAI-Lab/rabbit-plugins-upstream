## Description:

Prepares a company-and-role interview pack from public research and offers optional question-by-question practice with feedback, without requiring a resume.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to prepare for a specific company and role with a researched study pack, practice questions, answer frameworks, and optional interactive feedback. It requires JishuDB for the preparation pack; retaining personal practice requires separate approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Preparing a pack may require installing JishuDB Desktop and configuring an agent connection.

Mitigation: Review the proposed installation and connection changes, and approve them only if JishuDB is acceptable; declining setup blocks the workflow.

Risk: Personal interview answers and feedback could be retained in files or JishuDB.

Mitigation: Do not approve personal-practice storage unless the specific material and destination are acceptable; generic preparation can proceed without retaining personal practice.
