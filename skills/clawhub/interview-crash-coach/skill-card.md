## Description:

Prepares company and role research, interview questions, answer frameworks, and optional one-question-at-a-time mock practice without requiring a resume.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents assisting job candidates use this skill to create a focused interview preparation pack for a known company and role, then optionally run consent-gated mock practice with feedback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request write access to a selected JishuDB knowledge base for research and preparation records.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended knowledge base and write scope before use, and keep saved records limited to the approved company and role preparation materials.

Risk: Personal answers, feedback, scores, resume excerpts, or inferred weaknesses could be retained if the user grants separate consent.

Mitigation: Require explicit consent naming the material and destination before saving personal practice content; declining personal retention should still allow generic preparation and conversation-only practice.

Risk: Saved knowledge base content may be visible to other authorized readers of that knowledge base.

Mitigation: Use an appropriate knowledge base for the sensitivity of the preparation material and avoid saving personal content unless its visibility is acceptable.
