## Description:

Prepares a company-and-role interview pack from public research and offers optional one-question-at-a-time practice with feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to research a company and role, build a sourced preparation pack with practice questions and hypothetical answer frameworks, and optionally rehearse with feedback. A resume is not required; saving personal practice needs separate consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup may download, install, and launch desktop software or configure a client.

Mitigation: Review the approval scope and authorize only the expected per-user download, installation, launch, and non-secret client entry.

Risk: Saved practice answers and feedback may contain personal information.

Mitigation: Require separate explicit consent before retaining personal practice; keep it in the conversation otherwise.
