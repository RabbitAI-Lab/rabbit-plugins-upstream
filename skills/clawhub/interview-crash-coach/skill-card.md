## Description:

Prepares a company-and-role interview pack from public research, role knowledge, practice questions, and hypothetical answer frameworks, with optional interactive mock feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Interview candidates use this skill to prepare for a specific company and role using a sourced company brief, role checklist, practice questions, answer frameworks, and optional one-question-at-a-time feedback. A resume is not required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Required JishuDB setup may install software or change client configuration.

Mitigation: Review the displayed installation plan, release source, destination paths, and client configuration target before approving; heed the Windows unsigned-publisher warning.

Risk: Personal practice answers and feedback may be retained if saving is approved.

Mitigation: Keep practice in the conversation by default; approve saving only after checking the material, destination, and access controls.
