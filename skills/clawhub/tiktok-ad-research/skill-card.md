## Description:

Research TikTok Creative Center Top Ads by brand, market, industry, and objective through Crawlora. Use for competitor-ad comparisons, creative briefs, messaging and format analysis, and inspection of public ad-performance charts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, growth teams, and developers use this skill to research public TikTok Creative Center Top Ads and produce sourced competitor-ad comparisons, creative briefs, messaging analysis, and creative-test hypotheses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok ad research queries, material IDs, and Crawlora API-authenticated usage are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Keep the API key in the environment, avoid sensitive internal terms in queries, and review Crawlora privacy and retention terms before confidential use.

Risk: Public Top Ads results can be mistaken for complete advertiser activity or causal proof of performance.

Mitigation: Preserve filters, sort order, lookback, retrieval time, material IDs, and metric labels; verify advertiser identity where available and present creative tests as hypotheses.
