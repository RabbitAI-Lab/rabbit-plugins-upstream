## Description:

Research TikTok Creative Center Top Ads by brand, market, industry, and objective through Crawlora to support competitor comparisons, creative briefs, and analysis of public ad-performance signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, creative strategists, and analysts compare public TikTok Top Ads across brands and markets to identify messaging patterns, prepare creative briefs, and propose tests grounded in observed ads and available performance signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and the Crawlora API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only queries and credentials approved for the service; keep the key in the environment and use the bundled helper.

Risk: Returned signed media URLs may expire.

Mitigation: Treat media URLs as temporary external data and retain material IDs and source-page links when available.
