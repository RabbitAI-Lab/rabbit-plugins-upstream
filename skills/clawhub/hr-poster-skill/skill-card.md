## Description: <br>
生成简历方向、HR方向的招聘海报内容与设计指引，适用于招聘海报、校招海报、岗位招募视觉稿和HR传播素材。 <br>

## Use Case: <br>
HR, recruiting, and employer-branding users can use this skill to turn hiring goals and partial role information into a structured Chinese recruitment poster plan, including copy, visual direction, image-generation prompt, and a quality checklist. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recruitment posters can mislead candidates if missing job, salary, benefit, location, or application details are invented. <br>
Mitigation: The skill directs the agent to ask follow-up questions for missing information, mark assumptions and pending details, and avoid fabricating company or role claims. <br>
