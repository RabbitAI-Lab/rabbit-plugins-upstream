## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automation plans may affect payments, account creation, customer communications, production records, public posting, or recurring jobs without active approval.

Mitigation: Require explicit human approval before execution for sensitive workflows, especially where the workflow can spend money, alter accounts, contact customers, publish content, modify production data, or run on a schedule.

Risk: Automations may run with excessive access or insufficient validation.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege credentials, dry runs, staging data, audit logs, duplicate checks, timeouts, retries, and timeout-to-escalation behavior.
