## Description:

Get a local business recommended when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Siri or Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode for a service "near me". Use for "why does ChatGPT recommend the place down the road", "AI says we're closed" or local AEO, LLM SEO and GEO work. Runs a located prompt set, maps the listings AI reads and fixes them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Local business owners and SEO or marketing practitioners use this skill to assess how AI assistants answer location-specific service queries, identify the listings and pages those answers rely on, and prioritize improvements to local business information.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional DataForSEO workflow may share prompts and precise location coordinates with DataForSEO.

Mitigation: Explain this data sharing before using the optional workflow, obtain the user's consent, and use only the location precision needed for the analysis.

Risk: The security review is incomplete because three local path-like references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved referenced materials and rerun the security scan before relying on a complete security assessment.

Risk: Unconfirmed business facts or limited prompt runs can lead to misleading conclusions about AI answers or recommended fixes.

Mitigation: Confirm business facts with the owner and record the location, prompt, engine, and repeated runs before reporting errors or proposing live listing changes.
