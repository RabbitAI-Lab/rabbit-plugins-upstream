## Description: <br>
Management Review guides ISO 9001:2015 clause 9.3 management reviews through preparation, meeting, and follow-up phases, collecting inputs step by step and producing review plans, minutes, reports, and improvement tracking records. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Quality managers, management representatives, and internal audit teams use this skill to organize ISO 9001 management reviews, collect required review inputs, draft stage deliverables, and track improvement actions through closure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated management review documents may contain sensitive company information supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing sensitive company data unless it is appropriate for generated local files, and review generated documents before sharing. <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to create several business documents in the working folder. <br>
Mitigation: Run it from the intended project folder and inspect generated files before using them as official records. <br>
Risk: Draft conclusions or improvement actions could be mistaken for management decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting and organization support; responsible company personnel should review, approve, and sign final decisions. <br>
