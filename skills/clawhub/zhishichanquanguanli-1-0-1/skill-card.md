## Description:

A Chinese-language intellectual-property knowledge pack for creating IP knowledge bases, workflows, templates, checklists, and risk-aware guidance across trademarks, patents, copyright, trade secrets, and enforcement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[urselect](https://clawhub.ai/user/urselect)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, legal operations teams, IP agencies, and enterprise legal/IPR staff use this skill to draft Chinese-language IP knowledge-base outlines, document templates, process checklists, and practical guidance while preserving legal-risk boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with legal content oriented toward mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat China-mainland-oriented IP material as legal advice or apply it directly to other jurisdictions.

Mitigation: Use it as general reference, verify current rules with official sources, and consult qualified local counsel for legal decisions.

Risk: Templates, review periods, fees, and procedural details may become outdated as IP office rules change.

Mitigation: Check official CNIPA, WIPO, and relevant IP office sources before filing or relying on dates, fees, or procedures.
