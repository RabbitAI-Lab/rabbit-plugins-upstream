## Description:

Provides Gujarati-language guidance for registering and activating a PowPow account, including troubleshooting registration and subscription issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

New PowPow users follow Gujarati-language, step-by-step guidance to register, resolve common sign-up problems, and activate a subscription themselves on the official site or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive account or payment information could be disclosed in chat.

Mitigation: Use official PowPow pages for registration and payment; do not share passwords, verification codes, or payment proof in chat.
