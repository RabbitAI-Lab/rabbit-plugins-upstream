## Description:

Offers reflective guidance based on Su Shi’s poetry and life philosophy to help people process emotional difficulties, find meaning, and consider manageable next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People facing emotional difficulties or questions of meaning use this skill for literary reflection, perspective-taking, and small practical steps. It is intended for personal reflection, not clinical care or crisis response.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake literary reflection for mental-health treatment or rely on it during a crisis.

Mitigation: Keep responses within reflective support; for self-harm risk, crisis situations, clinical concerns, or persistent symptoms, direct users to emergency or qualified professional support as the skill specifies.

Risk: Users may disclose sensitive personal or health information while seeking emotional guidance.

Mitigation: Request only details needed for the current reflection, encourage redaction or generalization of identifying information, and use external services only with explicit user authorization.
