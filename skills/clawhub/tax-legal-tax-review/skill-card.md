This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Tax, legal, finance, audit, and compliance practitioners use this skill to structure tax due diligence, review tax-sensitive transaction terms, draft tax-related legal materials, run self-check workflows, and prepare reference-level compliance reports. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Confidential tax, legal, M&A, audit, or client-advisory content may be sent to cloud services or public-search fallback paths. <br>
Mitigation: Do not enter client secrets, transaction documents, identities, or privileged legal analysis unless the publisher provides acceptable data-handling, retention, deletion, and consent controls. <br>
Risk: Auto-created API credentials and local plaintext logs may expose sensitive prompts or service access details on the user's machine. <br>
Mitigation: Review local credential and log storage before use, restrict access to the local data directory, and remove or rotate credentials when the skill is no longer needed. <br>
Risk: Tax calculations, risk scoring, and legal-tax conclusions can be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific matter. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference material only and confirm important positions with official sources, tax authorities, licensed tax advisers, auditors, or counsel. <br>
