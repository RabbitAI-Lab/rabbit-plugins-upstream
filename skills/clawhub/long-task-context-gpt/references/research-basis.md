# Research basis and limits

Reviewed 2026-09-26. These are primary research and engineering sources, not installed components. The skill adapts small workflow principles; it implements none of the papers' learned models, runtime compressors, graph stores or benchmark harnesses. Do not transfer their numerical gains or guarantees to this skill.

| Primary source | Relevant finding or approach | Limited adaptation here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wang et al., [MemForest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08273v1), 2026-09-08; method §§3.2–3.4 | Organizes memories by event and temporal neighborhood, then merges related nodes and retrieves around anchors. | Group only compatible events/entities; retain source anchors and retrieve neighboring evidence for a defined gap. No EventTree or graph algorithm is claimed. |
| Zerhoudi et al., [The Compaction Cliff in Long-Running AI Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22752v1), 2026-08-24; §§3, 5 | Different knowledge types need different retention policies; repeated uniform compression can drop constraints. Guarantees depend on classification recall and budget feasibility. | Protect applicable requirements and exact clauses; shorten background separately; compare results against source requirements. A manual check is not their classifier or proof. |
| Wang et al., [Lost in Compaction / COMPINT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11242v1), 2026; §§3–4 | Session-wide side constraints can disappear even when the main goal remains. Separately evaluates retention and subsequent compliance. | Preserve project-wide restrictions and preference strength; test subsequent actions as well as the summary text. |
| Min et al., [Toward Reliable Context Compression for Long-Horizon Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06503v1), 2026-08-06; §4 | Preliminary TRACE experiments compare closed-loop continuations around a compression boundary and examine blocked/repeated actions. | Test the next action and repeated-operation avoidance. Functional scenarios alone do not reproduce TRACE or establish a causal gain. |
| Chao et al., [STALE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06527v1), 2026-05-07 | Evaluates recognizing obsolete memories, resisting stale premises and applying updates to later behavior. | Reconcile revisions and effective times; invalidate dependent current conclusions without erasing historical checks. |
| Anthropic, [Effective context engineering for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents), 2025-09-29 | Describes focused context, on-demand retrieval, compaction and durable notes for long tasks. | Load evidence for the next decision, keep a concise persistent record and separate it from originals. No claim of native compaction control. |

The 2026 papers are evolving research; assess their particular datasets and implementations before extrapolating. This workflow requires actual storage and retrieval tools. It neither enlarges the model window nor guarantees that a later session will automatically discover a checkpoint.
