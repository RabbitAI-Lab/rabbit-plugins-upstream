## Description:

Video Script Gen helps agents draft YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram Reels scripts with hooks, platform-specific structure, retention techniques, and calls to action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and marketing teams use this skill to ask an agent for short-form and long-form video scripts, including opening hooks, content beats, platform timing, and CTA wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated promotional scripts may include unverified claims or omit required disclosures for regulated topics such as health, supplements, finance, or legal services.

Mitigation: Require subject-matter review and independent verification of claims, disclosures, and compliance requirements before publishing scripts.

Risk: The artifact includes external PayPal, Telegram, and crypto payment references that are commercial upsell links.

Mitigation: Treat payment references as optional external commerce links and do not grant additional runtime permissions or credentials because of them.
