## Description:

Prepares customer record fields and assembles reporting export rows from user-supplied information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer reporting staff use this skill to turn supplied record notes into named customer fields and assemble those fields into reporting export rows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer record contents may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Supply only the customer data intended for conversion into report fields or export rows.
