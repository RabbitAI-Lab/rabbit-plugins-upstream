## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad Google, Workspace, and CLI-related trigger wording may invoke the skill for tasks that do not need this workflow helper. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation for relevant workflow-helper tasks, or disable implicit invocation when the hosting platform supports it. <br>
Risk: Generated setup, safety, reliability, code, or configuration guidance could be misapplied to a live workspace or skill project. <br>
Mitigation: Review the proposed workflow or change, test it in a non-production context when practical, and check the included assumptions and validation notes before applying it. <br>
