## Description:

Creates platform-shaped thumbnail and cover images with concise, readable text after the user reviews the image-generation credit quote.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators use this skill to develop a single cover image for a video or social post, choosing its frame, model, text, and any photo references before approving image generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation spends credits.

Mitigation: Review the quoted credit cost and approve each generation before it runs.

Risk: Reference photos are provided to the image service.

Mitigation: Use only photos you are comfortable providing to that service.

Risk: The security review covers the skill instructions, not Phantra's external tool implementations.

Mitigation: Consider the external service's terms and handling of images before sharing sensitive material.
