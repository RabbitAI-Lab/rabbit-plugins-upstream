## Description: <br>
Helps founders, builders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and practical decision-support artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns product-validation requests into repeatable workflows, checklists, templates, analyses, code changes, or decision-support outputs with visible assumptions and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad automatic invocation wording may activate the skill for general business, startup, prototype, or validation prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated output for fit before acting on it, especially when the prompt was not specifically about product validation. <br>
