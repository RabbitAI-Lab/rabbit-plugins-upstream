## Description:

Helps assess public-sector AI systems against LGD registration, evidence, and approval-gate criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Public-sector AI teams use this skill to self-check system registration, supporting evidence, and approval gates for decision support, public responses, and data governance. Its results are an internal checklist, not legal or regulatory advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A checklist result may be mistaken for legal or regulatory approval.

Mitigation: Use it for internal self-checks only; verify current official requirements and obtain qualified human review for consequential decisions.

Risk: Documentation and theory may be reused under the mistaken assumption that the MIT code license covers them.

Mitigation: Apply MIT only to the Python code; review the separate all-rights-reserved terms before redistributing documentation or theory text.
