## Description:

Militia Training (EN) provides educational prompt templates, method patterns, and safety checklists for militia-style anti-air defense, NBC protection, marksmanship, combat tactics, combat service support, and field survival topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may generate actionable firearm training and combat-planning guidance.

Mitigation: Install only for lawful, authorized educational or simulation use, and restrict or remove patrol, raid, ambush, live-operations, and firearm-training content before broad exposure.

Risk: NBC defense and battlefield first-aid guidance can be harmful if treated as current operational or medical instruction.

Mitigation: Limit NBC content to defense and protection, require qualified review against current safety and medical guidance, and include professional referral language for medical scenarios.

Risk: The artifact index references module files that are not present in this release artifact.

Mitigation: Treat the included method-patterns, prompt-template, and robustness-checklist files as the review scope, and verify coverage before relying on the skill for training workflows.
