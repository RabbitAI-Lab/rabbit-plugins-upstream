## Use Case: <br>
Food production teams, quality managers, and HACCP reviewers use this skill to draft, review, and maintain HACCP plans under GB 14881-2025, including hazard analysis, CCP determination, monitoring, corrective actions, verification, and microbiological monitoring. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: HACCP plans and controls may be incomplete or unsuitable if applied without facility-specific review. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified food safety personnel verify product hazards, critical limits, monitoring frequencies, corrective actions, and regulatory obligations before implementation. <br>
Risk: Food safety guidance may not reflect every applicable local requirement or authority interpretation. <br>
Mitigation: Validate outputs against applicable regulations, customer requirements, and competent authority guidance for the specific product and production site. <br>
