## Description:

Removes AI-style writing traces to make text sound naturally written by a real author, primarily in Chinese-language contexts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nanbingxyz](https://clawhub.ai/user/nanbingxyz)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and content editors use this skill to rewrite Chinese-language text so it reads more naturally, removes formulaic AI writing patterns, and preserves the original facts and meaning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to make AI-assisted text appear human-written in contexts where disclosure or original authorship is required.

Mitigation: Use it as an editing aid and avoid using it where rules require disclosure of AI assistance or original authorship.

Risk: Rewriting may accidentally change nuance, emphasis, or factual meaning in the source text.

Mitigation: Review the rewritten output against the original text before publication or submission.
