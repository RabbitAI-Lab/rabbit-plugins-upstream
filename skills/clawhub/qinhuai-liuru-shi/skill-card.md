## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers can invoke the persona during casual or emotional-support conversations when it is not wanted.

Mitigation: Use clearer activation wording or opt in to the persona when appropriate.

Risk: Roleplayed support may not meet the needs of someone facing serious mental health concerns.

Mitigation: Encourage professional help for serious mental health concerns, as directed by the skill.
