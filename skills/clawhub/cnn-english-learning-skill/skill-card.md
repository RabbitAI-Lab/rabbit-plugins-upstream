## Description:

Helps learners study English using CNN news links or excerpts through Chinese summaries, translations, grammar and vocabulary explanations, and quizzes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[anshanzhaoqian](https://clawhub.ai/user/anshanzhaoqian)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English learners use CNN news links or pasted passages to practice reading comprehension with Simplified Chinese explanations, vocabulary review, and interactive exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inaccessible articles or partial page content can leave the lesson incomplete.

Mitigation: Identify which text was accessible and ask the learner to paste the relevant passage rather than guessing missing content.

Risk: A brief '继续' ('continue') may resume a prior lesson when this skill is active.

Mitigation: Specify the article and whether to resume or begin a new lesson when continuity is unclear.
