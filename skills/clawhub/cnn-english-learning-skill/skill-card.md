## Description:

Helps learners study CNN English news in Simplified Chinese through summaries, sentence and vocabulary explanations, grammar guidance, and quizzes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

English learners use this skill to study CNN articles or pasted excerpts with Chinese explanations, vocabulary review, and practice questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Short requests such as 'continue' or 'make it simpler' may trigger the skill outside an article-study session.

Mitigation: Route these requests to the skill only when the user is already studying an article.

Risk: An inaccessible article or a link-only copyrighted article can lead to unsupported explanations or excessive reproduction.

Mitigation: Explain access limits, request an excerpt when needed, and use summaries and necessary short quotations rather than reproducing the full article.
