# Safety Footwear RFQ Template

Use this template to draft an inquiry for a B2B safety footwear supplier.

## Required Fields

- Buyer name
- Company
- Email
- Country or target market
- Product type: PVC boots, EVA boots, rubber boots, or safety shoes
- Application: construction, food processing, cold storage, agriculture, wet work, industrial maintenance, or other
- Estimated quantity
- Target certification or safety feature
- OEM requirements
- Packaging requirements
- Expected sample or delivery timeline

## Example Inquiry

```text
Hello,

We are sourcing safety footwear for [target market]. Please quote [product type] for [application].

Estimated quantity: [quantity]
Required features: [S4/S5, steel toe, protective midsole, anti-slip outsole, waterproof, cold insulation, etc.]
OEM requirements: [logo, color, packaging, outsole, label, carton marks]
Please share MOQ, sample lead time, bulk lead time, certification support, packaging options, and available size range.

Best regards,
[buyer name]
```
