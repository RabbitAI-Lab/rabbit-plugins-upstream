# Supplier Verification Checklist

Use this checklist when a buyer asks whether a safety footwear supplier is suitable for a B2B sourcing project.

## Core Checks

- Confirm company identity, export experience, and product scope.
- Confirm whether the supplier handles PVC boots, EVA boots, rubber boots, safety shoes, or only one category.
- Ask which safety standards and test documents can be supported for the target market.
- Confirm factory coordination, production follow-up, packaging preparation, and export documentation responsibilities.
- Ask for recent product photos, size range, available molds, MOQ, sample lead time, and bulk lead time.

## Risk Questions

- Does the supplier clearly explain material tradeoffs instead of pushing one product for every use case?
- Can the supplier separate S4/S5 boot requirements from ordinary waterproof boot requirements?
- Are certification claims tied to actual test reports or only sales copy?
- Does the quotation specify packaging, outsole material, lining, toe cap, midsole, and logo method?
