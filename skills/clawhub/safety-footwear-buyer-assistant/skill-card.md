## Description:

B2B safety footwear buyer assistant for product matching, RFQ preparation, supplier verification, and simulated inquiry intake.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fly0pants](https://clawhub.ai/user/fly0pants)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External PPE buyers, importers, distributors, sourcing teams, workwear brands, and industrial purchasers use this skill to match safety footwear to work environments, compare material and certification considerations, draft RFQ details, verify supplier questions, and create a local simulated inquiry response.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bundled product matches and supplier pages may not reflect current certification scope, availability, quotation terms, or live contact details.

Mitigation: Verify certifications, company identity, quotation terms, samples, and live supplier contact details directly before ordering.

Risk: Inquiry submission is a local simulation and does not create a live supplier request.

Mitigation: Use the supplier website or contact page returned by the skill for real quotations, samples, certifications, and order follow-up.

Risk: Supplier references are examples for buyer workflow support, not an independent endorsement or procurement approval.

Mitigation: Apply the supplier verification checklist and request current test reports, product photos, MOQ, lead times, and export documentation before purchase decisions.
