## Description:

Creates six-slide TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn carousels from reusable Orshot templates, then renders them as drafts for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and social-media teams use this skill to build carousel content for TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn with reusable templates, render the slides, and prepare posts for review before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images sent for rendering may be processed by Orshot, and rendered image links are public.

Mitigation: Use images approved for third-party processing; get permission before uploading private photos, and avoid including sensitive material in publicly accessible renders.

Risk: Publishing or scheduling a carousel can post to connected social accounts.

Mitigation: Review the rendered slides, destination account, caption, and batch cost before posting; keep posts as drafts until the user approves them.
