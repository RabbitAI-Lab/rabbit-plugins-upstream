## Description:

Creates template-based TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn carousels from source content, with options to prepare drafts through Orshot or Postiz.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use the skill to turn blog posts, spreadsheet rows, or hook ideas into branded TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn carousels. It can prepare posts as drafts and help review performance across posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Content sent to Orshot or exposed through public media URLs may be visible beyond the intended audience; publishing can affect connected accounts and incur costs.

Mitigation: Use content approved for sharing, review the slides, destination accounts, captions, and costs before publication, and verify recurring schedules and how to disable them.
