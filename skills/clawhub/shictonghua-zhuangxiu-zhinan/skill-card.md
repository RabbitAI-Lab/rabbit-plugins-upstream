## Description: <br>
适童化装修指南 is a Chinese-language child-room renovation advisor that helps families plan safer, lower-pollution materials, furniture, layouts, and ventilation choices for children’s rooms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and families with children or pregnancy plans use this skill to ask Chinese-language questions about child-room renovation materials, furniture, layouts, safety details, and pollution prevention before making purchasing or design choices. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat material-safety and health-related renovation claims as professional environmental, medical, or legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present responses as consumer guidance and encourage independent verification for important renovation, safety, health, or compliance decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill is intentionally strongly opinionated, so its recommendations may not fit every home, jurisdiction, product standard, or budget. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations against local building requirements, current product certifications, household constraints, and qualified professional advice before acting. <br>
