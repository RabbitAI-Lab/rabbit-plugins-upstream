## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language guidance skill for child-friendly home renovation that answers questions about children's rooms, safer material choices, furniture, layout, formaldehyde prevention, and common renovation pitfalls. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External Chinese-speaking homeowners and assistants use this skill to answer child-friendly renovation questions and produce practical guidance on materials, furniture, room layouts, ventilation, and safety details for homes with children or planned children. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill makes strong claims about formaldehyde testing, brands, and renovation materials that users may over-apply to health or construction decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review the claims before relying on them for safety-critical choices, ask for sources where needed, and consult qualified health, construction, or code professionals for high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: Opinionated renovation guidance may not fit every home, region, budget, product, or regulatory context. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as consumer guidance and compare recommendations against local codes, product documentation, and site-specific constraints. <br>
