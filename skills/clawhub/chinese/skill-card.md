## Description: <br>
Writes and edits Mandarin Chinese that reads as if a native wrote it, covering casual and formal registers, mainland and Taiwan usage, mechanics such as 的/地/得 and measure words, punctuation, slang, politeness, naming, and AI-sounding text. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and writers use this skill to draft, revise, and proofread Mandarin Chinese for messages, business writing, social posts, documents, speeches, product names, and audience-specific register choices. It is intended for original Chinese composition and repair rather than source-bound translation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can create long-lived local records about contacts, channels, documents, templates, outcomes, and writing mistakes. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when that persistence is acceptable, and periodically review and prune the files under ~/Clawic/data/. <br>
Risk: The skill may write local Chinese-writing memory without asking for explicit consent before each write. <br>
Mitigation: Monitor the skill's announced write and deletion lines, and remove entries that should not persist. <br>
Risk: Chinese writing artifacts can contain sensitive HR, medical, legal, financial, client-confidential, or personal relationship details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid using the skill for those contexts unless the local data files can be reviewed; keep reusable templates and register decisions while stripping personal details from stored full texts. <br>
