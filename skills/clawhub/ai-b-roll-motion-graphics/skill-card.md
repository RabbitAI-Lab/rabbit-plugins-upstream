## Description:

Insert contextual B-roll cutaways, animated data charts, lower thirds, and callout overlays on video timelines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video editors and creators use this skill to add contextual B-roll, animated data visualizations, lower thirds, and split-screen or picture-in-picture overlays to video timelines using plain-language edit requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edit instructions and linked media are processed by Levea.

Mitigation: Review Levea's privacy policy and share only material you are authorized to send.

Risk: Timeline changes may proceed without a review of the proposed plan.

Mitigation: Enable requirePlanApproval and confirm the planned edits before execution.

Risk: The Levea API key could be exposed or misused.

Mitigation: Keep the API key in tool configuration.
