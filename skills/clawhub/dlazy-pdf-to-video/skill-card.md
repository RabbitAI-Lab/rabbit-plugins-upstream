## Description:

Turns a PDF into an explainer, report presentation, courseware, or training video through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, video creation, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who want to turn a PDF into an explainer, report presentation, courseware, or training video use this skill to guide document parsing, outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, video creation, and validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy for processing, and video generation may consume account credits.

Mitigation: Check that documents are suitable to upload before sharing them, and review credit use for the task.

Risk: The locally saved organization API key could be exposed or remain active after it is no longer needed.

Mitigation: Protect the local CLI configuration and rotate or revoke the API key from the dLazy dashboard when needed.
