## Description:

Helps turn PDFs and other documents into narrated explainer, presentation, or training videos through dLazy's video workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn PDFs and other documents into narrated explainers, report summaries, courseware, and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to an external service, which may be inappropriate for private or regulated content.

Mitigation: Attach sensitive documents only if uploading them to dLazy is intended and permitted; review dLazy's terms before use.

Risk: Using the skill requires a dLazy API key and installation or execution of an external CLI.

Mitigation: Review the pinned npm package and listed source before installing, and safeguard the API key.
