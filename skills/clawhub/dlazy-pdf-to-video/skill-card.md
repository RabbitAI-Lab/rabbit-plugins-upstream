## Description:

Helps turn PDFs and other documents into explainer, presentation, courseware, or training videos using dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business teams use this skill to turn PDFs and other documents into narrated explainer, report, courseware, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only documents approved for the service and review its terms before uploading private material.

Risk: Using the service requires a dLazy API key and the dLazy CLI.

Mitigation: Protect or revoke the key as needed; review the CLI source and use the on-demand installation option if a persistent global install is unwanted.
