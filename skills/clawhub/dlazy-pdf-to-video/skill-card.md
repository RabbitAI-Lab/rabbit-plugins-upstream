## Description:

PDF to explainer, report, courseware, and training video creation through document parsing, outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, video building, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who need an explainer, report broadcast, courseware, or training video can use the skill to turn a PDF into a video through a dLazy project session.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags organization project listing and a broad activation trigger; the scan was partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the behavior and unresolved references before installation, limit use to explicit video-generation requests, and confirm project listing is needed.
