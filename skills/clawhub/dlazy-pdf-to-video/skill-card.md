## Description:

Helps turn PDFs and other documents into explainer, report, courseware, and training videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, and assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and developers use this skill to turn PDFs and other documents into narrated explainer, report, courseware, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are shared with the third-party dLazy service.

Mitigation: Use only documents you are comfortable sending to dLazy; review its service terms before uploading sensitive material.

Risk: Installing the CLI and saving an account API key requires trusting a third-party tool.

Mitigation: Prefer the pinned on-demand npx option if avoiding a persistent global install, and rotate or revoke the key in the dLazy dashboard when needed.
