## Description:

Helps users turn PDFs and other documents into explainer, presentation, courseware, or training videos through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating educational, training, or report videos use this skill to guide document-to-video work with dLazy and continue existing video projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, options, and attached documents are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only files and content you are comfortable sending to dLazy.

Risk: The dLazy CLI uses an organization API key and can remain installed globally.

Mitigation: Use the pinned on-demand npx option if preferred, and rotate or revoke the key when you stop using the service.
