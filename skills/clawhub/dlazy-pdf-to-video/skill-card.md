## Description:

Helps turn PDFs and other documents into explainer, presentation, or training videos through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users can request explainer, report, courseware, or training videos from PDFs and other documents using the dLazy service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading confidential documents unless your account and data-handling expectations permit it.

Risk: The CLI can store an API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local credential and rotate or revoke it if compromised.
