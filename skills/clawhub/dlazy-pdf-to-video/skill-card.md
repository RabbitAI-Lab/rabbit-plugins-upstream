## Description:

Helps turn PDFs into explainer, presentation, courseware, or training videos by guiding outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, generation, and validation through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users can provide a PDF and request a narrated explainer, report summary, courseware, or training video using dLazy's hosted service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Send only documents and prompts approved for use with dLazy's service.

Risk: The service requires a dLazy API key.

Mitigation: Rotate or revoke the API key when it is no longer needed.
