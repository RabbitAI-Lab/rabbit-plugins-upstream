## Use Case:

Employees and external collaborators use this skill to turn meeting notes, chat logs, or discussion summaries into concise summaries, decisions, and follow-up action items with owners, deadlines, and priorities where available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting notes can contain sensitive business or personal information.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive meeting details unless the user is comfortable having the agent process that content.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-first, which may reduce quality for non-Chinese meeting notes.

Mitigation: Review outputs carefully when using non-Chinese source material or translate inputs before processing.
