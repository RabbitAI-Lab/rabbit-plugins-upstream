## Description: <br>
Enterprise AI Advisor is a Chinese-language diagnostic assistant that interviews small and midsize businesses, identifies practical AI adoption opportunities, and generates an implementation roadmap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[g620710](https://clawhub.ai/user/g620710) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External business users and consultants use this skill to assess a company's AI readiness, prioritize three to five high-ROI AI use cases, and produce a practical 30-day adoption plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for business details and budget information to estimate ROI. <br>
Mitigation: Use approximate or non-sensitive values unless the deployment environment is approved for confidential business information. <br>
Risk: The generated ROI estimates, tool choices, and implementation steps may be inaccurate or incomplete for a specific company. <br>
Mitigation: Review the report with business and technical stakeholders before making purchasing, staffing, or deployment decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill may suggest follow-up paid services after producing the diagnostic report. <br>
Mitigation: Treat service recommendations as optional commercial guidance and evaluate them independently before engagement. <br>
