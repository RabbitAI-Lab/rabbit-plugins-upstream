## Description:

AI legal documents and contract drafting powered by CellCog. Contracts, NDAs, terms of service, privacy policies, freelance agreements, employment contracts, compliance reviews, legal research. Precision legal drafting from natural language prompts. Not legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive legal material may be shared with CellCog.

Mitigation: Send only information authorized for sharing and check CellCog's confidentiality and retention terms before including sensitive material.

Risk: AI-generated legal documents may need correction for accuracy or jurisdiction-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Have a qualified attorney review important documents before execution.

Risk: The separate core skill, SDK, dependency code, and external service behavior were not verified in the supplied assessment.

Mitigation: Review the core skill and SDK before installation and prefer reviewed versions.
