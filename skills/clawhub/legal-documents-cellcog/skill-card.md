## Description:

AI legal documents and contract drafting powered by CellCog. Contracts, NDAs, terms of service, privacy policies, freelance agreements, employment contracts, compliance reviews, legal research. Precision legal drafting from natural language prompts. Not legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users can draft contracts, policies, compliance materials, and legal research documents from natural-language prompts. Have a qualified attorney review generated documents before execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts may contain confidential information sent to CellCog.

Mitigation: Check CellCog’s privacy and retention terms before submitting confidential information.

Risk: Generated legal documents may contain errors or omit jurisdiction-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Have a qualified attorney review documents before execution, especially for high-value contracts, regulated industries, or jurisdiction-specific matters.

Risk: The supporting CellCog software is a separate dependency outside this artifact review, and Max-tier use can incur costs.

Mitigation: Review supporting software before installation and account for Max-tier usage costs.
