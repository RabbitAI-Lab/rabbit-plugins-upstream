## Description:

AI legal documents and contract drafting powered by CellCog, including contracts, NDAs, terms of service, privacy policies, freelance agreements, employment contracts, compliance reviews, and legal research; not legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and teams use this skill to ask CellCog for legal document drafting, policy drafting, compliance checklists, legal memos, and legal research support. Generated legal material should be treated as a starting point and reviewed by a qualified attorney before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and generated legal documents may contain sensitive external-service data.

Mitigation: Do not send confidential client material, regulated health data, trade secrets, or real personal details unless authorized and comfortable with CellCog's data handling terms.

Risk: AI-generated legal documents may contain legal errors, omissions, or jurisdiction-specific issues.

Mitigation: Have generated legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before execution or other high-impact use.
