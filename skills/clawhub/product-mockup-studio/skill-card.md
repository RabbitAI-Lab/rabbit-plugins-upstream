## Description:

Guides agents to create and refine branded product mockup images and animate approved stills into short videos using AgentPMT.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agentpmt](https://clawhub.ai/user/agentpmt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants, marketers, and creative teams use an agent to turn product photos into listing images, lifestyle mockups, and short product videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos are sent to a remote service.

Mitigation: Review sensitive photos before uploading and provide only the files needed for the mockup.

Risk: Generated files are retained in File Manager for seven days.

Mitigation: Download needed results before retention expires.

Risk: Generated images or videos may misrepresent labels or product details.

Mitigation: Visually review brand text, colors, dimensions, and product details before publishing.

Risk: Related setup skills may be installed from unpinned sources.

Mitigation: Prefer pinned or trusted installation routes for setup skills.
