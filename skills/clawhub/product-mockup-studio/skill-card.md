## Description:

Guides agents in creating branded product mockup images, refining approved stills, and animating them into short videos through AgentPMT.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agentpmt](https://clawhub.ai/user/agentpmt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce brands, marketers, and agencies use this skill to turn product photos into listing images, lifestyle mockups, and short promotional clips, then review and refine the results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos, prompts, and brand details are sent to AgentPMT and its generation providers.

Mitigation: Upload only assets approved for third-party processing; avoid confidential unreleased material.

Risk: Generation calls consume credits, and generated files are retained in File Manager for seven days.

Mitigation: Confirm budget before generating and download needed files before retention expires.

Risk: Generated visuals can misrepresent packaging, labels, colors, or product details.

Mitigation: Review each image or video against the actual product before publication.

Risk: Untrusted setup commands may expose an environment to unintended changes.

Mitigation: Use pinned, trusted setup commands where possible.
