## Description: <br>
Maoyan helps agents guide movie-ticketing choices and explain Maoyan Pro box-office data, including theater formats, refund caveats, market indicators, and citation norms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to compare movie formats and seats, explain ticketing/refund caveats, interpret Maoyan Pro box-office indicators, and write movie-market analysis with clear source and timestamp notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Ticket prices, discounts, refund rules, and box-office figures can change quickly. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current values in the official Maoyan app or cited source before purchase, publication, or decision-making. <br>
Risk: Movie-market analysis can be misleading when box-office data is quoted without source, timestamp, or metric definition. <br>
Mitigation: Include the Maoyan Pro source, timestamp, and metric context when using the skill's citation guidance. <br>
