## Description:

Helps English learners analyze complex CNN news sentences with step-by-step grammar explanations and Chinese translations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[anshanzhaoqian](https://clawhub.ai/user/anshanzhaoqian)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English learners use the skill to understand CNN news sentences through Chinese-language sentence breakdowns, grammar explanations, vocabulary notes, and translations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A supplied CNN page may be inaccessible, leaving the article text unavailable for analysis.

Mitigation: Ask the user to paste the passage instead of claiming to have read the inaccessible page.
