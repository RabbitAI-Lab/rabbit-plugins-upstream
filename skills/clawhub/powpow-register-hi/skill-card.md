## Description:

Guides Hindi-speaking users through PowPow account registration, email verification, subscription activation, and common registration or payment issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand PowPow, open the registration and subscription pages, resolve account setup issues, and complete activation themselves in the PowPow web or app interface.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment proof in chat while trying to register or activate an account.

Mitigation: The skill instructs the assistant not to request or handle those items and to keep registration, verification, subscription, and payment actions on PowPow pages.

Risk: Registration or subscription behavior may differ if the PowPow platform changes after release.

Mitigation: The skill frames platform rules as guidance from current evidence and directs users to rely on the live PowPow interface when behavior differs.
