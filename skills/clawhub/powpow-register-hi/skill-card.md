## Description:

Guides Hindi-speaking users through PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Hindi-speaking PowPow users receive step-by-step help registering an account, resolving sign-up problems, and activating a subscription. Users complete registration and payment themselves on the relevant websites or apps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment details in chat while seeking registration help.

Mitigation: Direct users to enter sensitive details only on the official PowPow site or payment provider pages, never in chat.
