## Description:

Comprehensive multi-domain color design system covering digital product UI with semantic CSS color tokens, dark/light mode, and WCAG AA contrast, plus graphic and print design with CMYK/Pantone specs, print paper absorption, spot colors, 60-30-10 harmony rules, and poster or packaging palettes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and brand teams use this skill to generate UI color token systems, dark/light mode palettes, WCAG-aware CSS, and print-ready CMYK/Pantone color specifications for digital products, posters, packaging, and brand collateral.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CMYK and Pantone values may vary by printer, substrate, paper absorption, and production workflow.

Mitigation: Treat print values as design guidance and verify final specifications with the printer or production workflow before use.

Risk: Generated UI palettes may not satisfy accessibility or brand requirements in every final layout.

Mitigation: Review generated tokens in context and validate WCAG contrast thresholds before deployment.
