## Description:

Helps agents guide browser-based publishing to WeChat Official Accounts, Baijiahao, and Xiaohongshu.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lsmonet](https://clawhub.ai/user/lsmonet)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prepare and publish social media posts through web publishing consoles, including title, body, and cover-image workflows. The skill is intended for assisted browser operation where the user reviews the target platform and content before final confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases could start an unintended publishing workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected platform and requested action before proceeding.

Risk: Social posts may be published with incorrect platform, title, body, or cover image.

Mitigation: Have the user review the selected platform and content before confirming any publish action.
