## Description: <br>
Provides outdoor photography timing guidance for golden hour, blue hour, and other times of day based on natural light characteristics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and photography assistants use this skill to plan outdoor portraits, landscapes, architecture, city scenes, and travel shoots around the light qualities of each time of day. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Golden-hour and blue-hour timing varies by location, season, and weather. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning guidance and verify exact local sunrise, sunset, weather, and site conditions before scheduling a shoot. <br>
Risk: The skill is not intended for indoor lighting, light-direction selection, or cloudy and rainy conditions where time-of-day effects are reduced. <br>
Mitigation: Route those cases to more specific lighting guidance or state the limitation before giving timing advice. <br>
