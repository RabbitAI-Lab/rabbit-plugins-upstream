## Description:

A Thai-language assistant that guides users through PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without submitting forms or payments for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users receive Thai-language, step-by-step guidance to create an account, resolve registration or login issues, and complete subscription activation on PowPow's own pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment proof in chat while seeking help.

Mitigation: Do not request or share these details in chat; enter registration and payment information only on official PowPow pages.

Risk: Registration or subscription steps may change, and payment activation may not be immediate.

Mitigation: Follow the current official PowPow pages and confirm account status there; allow for delayed payment confirmation before troubleshooting.
