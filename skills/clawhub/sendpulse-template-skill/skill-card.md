## Description:

Generates responsive HTML email templates for SendPulse campaigns and guides optional uploads and mailing management through the SendPulse MCP server or API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Help marketers and developers create responsive HTML emails for welcome, promotional, newsletter, transactional, and other campaign needs, with optional SendPulse upload and mailing guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional SendPulse integration requires account credentials or API keys.

Mitigation: Enable only needed tools, protect keys, and revoke exposed or unused credentials.

Risk: Campaigns can reach unintended recipients or be sent with unapproved content or timing.

Mitigation: Review and approve recipients, content, and send timing before any immediate or scheduled send.

Risk: Open- and click-tracking may not suit every audience or privacy expectation.

Mitigation: Decide whether tracking is appropriate for the recipients before enabling it.

Risk: Example links and images may remain in a campaign template.

Mitigation: Replace and verify example links and images before sending.
