## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill persists knowledge entries and index files on the local filesystem. <br>
Mitigation: Keep usage scoped to a chosen KNOWLEDGE_DIR and review generated Markdown entries and INDEX.md before relying on them. <br>
Risk: The release includes SEO-oriented wording that may overstate the knowledge-base behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for local Markdown knowledge management and do not rely on SEO claims unless the publisher adds matching SEO-specific functionality. <br>
