## Description: <br>
ADHD Sisterhood is a Chinese-language education and peer-support assistant for late-diagnosed women with ADHD, focused on symptom understanding, emotional adjustment, practical coping strategies, and clinician-facing questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[celia998](https://clawhub.ai/user/celia998) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Late-diagnosed women with ADHD use this skill for education, emotional support, practical executive-function strategies, and preparation for discussions with qualified clinicians. It is also useful for exploring women-specific ADHD topics such as masking, hormones, midlife diagnosis, relationships, treatment options, and emerging neuroscience without treating the skill as medical care. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat ADHD education or peer-support guidance as diagnosis or medical care. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for learning, coping strategies, and preparing questions for clinicians; do not use it to self-diagnose or replace a qualified medical professional. <br>
Risk: Medication, biomarker, genetic-risk, fNIRS, or AI-diagnosis topics may be mistaken for individualized clinical interpretation. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those topics educational and direct users to qualified clinicians for medication decisions, diagnostic interpretation, and treatment changes. <br>
