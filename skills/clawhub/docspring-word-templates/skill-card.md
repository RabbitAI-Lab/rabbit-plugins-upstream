## Description:

Creates and fixes DocSpring Word templates that use Liquid tags to generate Word documents or PDFs from submission data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docspring](https://clawhub.ai/user/docspring)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to create, troubleshoot, and update DocSpring Word templates. It guides them through Liquid tags, field setup, previews, and publishing so submissions produce the intended Word documents or PDFs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports medium confidence and incomplete reference checks, leaving some uncertainty about the guidance.

Mitigation: Review unresolved references and confirm relevant steps against DocSpring documentation before relying on them.

Risk: Incorrect or unpreviewed template edits can cause failed submissions or malformed documents.

Mitigation: Use made-up sample data and preview every page after each change before publishing.
