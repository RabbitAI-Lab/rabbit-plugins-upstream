## Description:

Use whenever you write, edit or review anything that cites papers (literature reviews, manuscripts, grant text, essays, answers with references). Finds real sources with the Phở Research Tools MCP server, verifies every reference against Crossref, PubMed and OpenAlex before showing it, formats the bibliography from the database record, and flags anything that is fabricated, wrong or retracted.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, writers, and reviewers use this skill to find and assess literature, verify and format references, check retractions, and support systematic-review screening. It can also help locate research datasets and software.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference lists, search claims, and submitted screening decisions may be sent to the external Phở service; its retention practices are not explained in the skill.

Mitigation: Prefer public bibliographic identifiers and confirm permission before submitting confidential research materials.

Risk: English-language searches may miss relevant literature in other languages.

Mitigation: Search in relevant languages as well and review local or regional sources when coverage matters.
