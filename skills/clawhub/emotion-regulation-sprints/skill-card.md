## Description:

Provides 5-15 minute emotion regulation micro-exercises based on CBT, DBT, and mindfulness for anxiety, emotional surges, stress, and calming down quickly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for short, text-only self-help exercises that support emotional grounding, breathing, cognitive reframing, and daily emotional maintenance. It is not medical care, crisis support, or a substitute for qualified human support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat acute emotion-regulation guidance as medical care or crisis support.

Mitigation: Present the skill as self-help exercises only, and direct users who are in danger, at risk of self-harm, or in severe distress to local emergency services, crisis hotlines, or qualified human support.

Risk: Broad emotional trigger phrases may route users in serious distress to the skill.

Mitigation: Review trigger scope before deployment and keep crisis-boundary language visible in the user-facing guidance.
