## Description:

Helps create code-driven motion graphics, kinetic typography, and animated infographics with Remotion rather than AI-generated footage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan and produce animated text, graphics, and explainer videos through the dLazy motion-graphics service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and options are sent to the external dLazy service.

Mitigation: Share only information you intend to send to dLazy.

Risk: Files attached to a project are uploaded to dLazy storage.

Mitigation: Attach only files you intend to share with dLazy.

Risk: Authentication saves a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local key and rotate or revoke it in the dLazy dashboard if needed.
