## Description:

Creates motion graphics such as kinetic typography, animated text, infographics, and explainers in Remotion code, then polishes and exports them for code-driven animation rather than AI-generated footage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to build and refine code-driven motion graphics, including kinetic typography, animated infographics, and explainers, through dLazy's motion-graphics workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the CLI involves dLazy login and organization project listing, which the security scan noted do not clearly fit the stated animation purpose.

Mitigation: Before installing, decide whether login and organization project listing are needed for your workflow, and review whether you trust the CLI and its data handling.

Risk: Prompts and options are sent to dLazy, and local files attached with --files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid attaching sensitive files or including sensitive information in prompts unless dLazy is approved to receive it.
