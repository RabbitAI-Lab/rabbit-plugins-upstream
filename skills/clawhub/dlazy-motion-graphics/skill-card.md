## Description:

Creates motion graphics, kinetic typography, animated text videos, animated infographics, and explainers as Remotion code, then polishes and exports them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and motion designers use this skill to create code-driven motion graphics, kinetic typography, animated infographics, and explainer videos with Remotion, then refine and export them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy services, and the workflow includes authentication and organization project listing.

Mitigation: Review dLazy data handling and access scope before use, avoid sending sensitive files, and confirm the intended organization and project.
