Combine transactional game analysis with Baker influence strategy for leadership conversations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Leaders, managers, and advisors use this skill to diagnose repeated leadership conversation loops, choose an ethical influence mode, and produce action-ready language for meetings or messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat coaching suggestions as professional HR, legal, or mental-health advice in sensitive workplace situations.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as coaching suggestions and seek HR, legal, safety, or professional support when abuse, retaliation, threats, harassment, or serious mental-health risk may be involved.

Risk: Conversation pattern labels could be overread as diagnosis or used to justify manipulation.

Mitigation: Treat labels as hypotheses, preserve agency, and use the skill's ethical boundaries against deception, coercion, shame, threats, or exploiting insecurity.
