## Description:

Describes Agiexa’s planned HubSpot integration for Shopify, its future CRM tools and triggers, and an optional launch waitlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants and their agents can use this skill to review the planned Agiexa HubSpot integration, understand future CRM workflows, and optionally join the launch waitlist with explicit consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may mistake the planned HubSpot tools and triggers for capabilities available today.

Mitigation: Describe the integration as a preview and state that HubSpot actions are not available until launch.

Risk: Joining the waitlist shares the user’s confirmed email, role, and any optional details with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit details only after explicit permission and confirmation; explain what will be shared before sending.

Risk: Connecting the future integration involves account permissions and security claims outside this package’s verification.

Mitigation: Have the merchant review requested permissions and verify Agiexa’s security information separately before connecting.
