## Description:

Combines preparation of an agreement obligation schedule with adding entries to a renewal schedule.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal and account teams use this skill to read agreement sections, derive dated renewal obligations, and append those entries to a renewal schedule for handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated obligation entries may be inaccurate or incomplete for legal and account renewal workflows.

Mitigation: Review generated obligation entries for accuracy before applying them to a renewal schedule.
