## Description:

Company firmographics, tech stack, headcount, and intelligence from a domain — agent-ready account research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and market research teams can use this skill to enrich a company or domain with firmographics, headcount, funding, competitors, technology signals, and domain email results, or to search and discover companies for target lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends authenticated requests to Superagnt's external API and requires a Superagnt API key.

Mitigation: Use only an authorized key, and confirm that the external service and account credit lookup fit your needs before running requests.
