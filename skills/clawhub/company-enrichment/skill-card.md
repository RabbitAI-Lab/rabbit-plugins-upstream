## Description:

Company firmographics, tech stack, headcount, and intelligence from a domain — agent-ready account research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, account research, and market-mapping teams can use this skill to enrich a domain or company name with firmographics, headcount, funding, competitors, technology data, and domain email information. It also supports company discovery, search, and bulk enrichment for account lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company information submitted for enrichment is shared with Superagnt and its upstream providers.

Mitigation: Submit only company information you intend to share with those providers.

Risk: Bulk enrichment can incur costs.

Mitigation: Review expected costs before sending bulk requests.

Risk: An MCP connection may expose tools or permissions beyond what the user expects, and the remote service has not been audited.

Mitigation: Inspect enabled MCP tools and consent permissions before use; assess the remote service for your needs.

Risk: The documented example quoting and request schemas may be incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Correct the example quoting and obtain complete request schemas before use.
