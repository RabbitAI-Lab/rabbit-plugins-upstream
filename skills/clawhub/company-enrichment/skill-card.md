## Description:

Company firmographics, tech stack, headcount, and intelligence from a domain — agent-ready account research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and account research teams can enrich company domains or names with firmographics, headcount, funding, competitors, technographics, and domain email data. They can also search or discover companies and enrich account lists for outreach preparation, ICP filtering, and market mapping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company queries send company data to Superagnt and may consume credits.

Mitigation: Send only company lists you are authorized to share, and check credit usage before bulk requests.

Risk: A compromised API key or an overly broad MCP connection could expose account access.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and verify the enabled tools and access scope before approving an optional MCP connection.

Risk: Incomplete command examples may fail or behave differently than intended.

Mitigation: Correct and review the command examples before use.
