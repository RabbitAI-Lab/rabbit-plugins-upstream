This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales, growth, and market research agents can enrich a company domain or search for companies to support account research, ICP filtering, target-list building, and market mapping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requests send a bearer API key and company or domain information to Superagnt's API.

Mitigation: Use an authorized API key, keep it confidential, and submit only company or domain information you are permitted to share.
