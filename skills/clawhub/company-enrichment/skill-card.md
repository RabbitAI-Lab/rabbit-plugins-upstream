## Use Case:

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company names, domains, search criteria, and account-list data are sent to Superagnt's service.

Mitigation: Use only data your organization has approved for sharing with Superagnt, and review Superagnt's terms and privacy practices before using confidential, regulated, or internal-only prospect lists.
