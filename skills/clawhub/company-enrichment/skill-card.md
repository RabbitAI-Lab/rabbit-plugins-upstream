This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales, business development, and market research teams use the skill to enrich company domains, find organizations matching target criteria, and prepare account research for outreach, ICP filtering, and market mapping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company lookups, especially bulk requests, may consume credits, and the API key must be protected.

Mitigation: Keep SUPERAGNT_API_KEY private, check expected credit costs, and review the size of bulk lists before submitting them.

Risk: Some setup examples have documentation problems or omit complete request parameters.

Mitigation: Check the complete endpoint parameter documentation and correct shell quoting before running example commands.
