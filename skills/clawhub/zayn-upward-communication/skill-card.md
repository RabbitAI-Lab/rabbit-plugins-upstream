## Description: <br>
Helps users in different roles prepare upward communications for supervisors, managers, department leads, or executives, including status updates, requests, approvals, decisions, escalations, resource asks, policy exceptions, scheduling, leave, dissenting views, performance, and career-development conversations across chat, email, verbal briefings, meetings, and approval systems. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and managers use this skill to turn facts, impacts, recommendations, and requests into concise upward communication that a manager can quickly understand, judge, and answer. It supports reporting, approval requests, decision requests, escalations, resource requests, policy exceptions, personal work matters, feedback, and follow-up messages. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafted workplace messages could be mistaken for approved business, policy, legal, HR, financial, or technical decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a drafting aid and verify all material claims, approvals, budgets, policies, risks, and recommended actions before sending. <br>
Risk: Inputs for upward communication can contain sensitive personal or business details. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the minimum necessary sensitive information and remove details that are not needed for the manager to make the requested decision. <br>
Risk: Incomplete facts can change responsibility, approval scope, cost, or risk conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: When key facts are missing, use the skill's framework and follow-up questions instead of treating a draft as a final request. <br>
