## Description:

This skill analyzes US-listed technology giants such as Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, or Tesla through structured investment and competitive research covering business model, market share, growth prospects, technology moat, financial reports, and stock price trend.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ttggjj](https://clawhub.ai/user/ttggjj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, students, and agent operators use this skill to turn broad requests about major US technology companies into sourced, six-part Markdown analysis. It supports company profiles and comparisons while framing stock information as context rather than investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake financial and stock-market context for personalized investment advice or trading direction.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as public-data research, avoid personalized recommendations, and do not place trades.

Risk: Current financial, market-share, and stock data can become stale or may be unavailable from public sources.

Mitigation: Cite each quantitative claim with source and data date, and state data gaps instead of guessing.
