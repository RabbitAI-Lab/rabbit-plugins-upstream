## Description:

This skill produces deep, data-rich investment and competitive analyses of US-listed technology giants, covering business model, market share, growth prospects, moat, financials, stock trends, valuation, risks, and sourced charts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External analysts, investors, strategy teams, and students use this skill to turn broad questions about US-listed technology giants into structured, sourced company analysis with financials, competitive context, valuation tables, risks, and charts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial figures, market data, analyst targets, and stock-price context can become stale or vary by source.

Mitigation: Require dated sources for every figure, prefer primary filings and investor-relations materials, and review outputs for freshness and source quality before use.

Risk: Users may mistake generated stock trend, valuation, or risk discussion for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as informational analysis and include the skill's required disclaimer that reports are not investment advice.

Risk: Valuation comparisons, market-share estimates, and risk matrices may be misleading if source methodology or missing data is not visible.

Mitigation: State source methodology caveats, explicitly mark unavailable data instead of guessing, and review comparison tables and risk assessments before relying on them.
