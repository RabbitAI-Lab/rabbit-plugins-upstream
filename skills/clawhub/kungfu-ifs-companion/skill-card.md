## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake companion-style emotional support for professional mental health care or emergency support.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's non-clinical boundary visible and direct users toward licensed, crisis, or trusted real-world support for medical, clinical, or immediate safety needs.

Risk: IFS-inspired parts language could be overused or treated as literal diagnosis during high distress.

Mitigation: Use parts language lightly, preserve user agency, and switch to short stabilization and real-world support guidance for crisis, delusion, harm, medication, or clinical claims.

Risk: Companion responses can create dependency if they imply exclusive understanding or replacement of human support.

Mitigation: Avoid exclusivity or secrecy claims and reinforce trusted people, professional support, and the user's own agency when support needs are significant.
