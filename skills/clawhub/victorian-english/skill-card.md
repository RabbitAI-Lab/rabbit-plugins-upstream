## Description: <br>
Rewrites user-supplied text as ornate Victorian English prose while preserving the intended meaning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[neefactory](https://clawhub.ai/user/neefactory) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to transform phrases, sentences, and short passages into elaborate Victorian-style English while preserving facts, intent, and emotional tone. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Requests that use the word "translate" may activate Victorian restyling when the user intended ordinary language translation. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit phrasing such as "rewrite in Victorian English" for this skill, and use a dedicated translation skill when a target language is requested. <br>
