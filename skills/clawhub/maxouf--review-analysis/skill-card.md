## Description:

Analyzes review datasets in bulk, summarizing sentiment, themes, complaint severity, unanswered complaints, and 90-day trends per business.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[maxouf](https://clawhub.ai/user/maxouf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business operators and analysts use the skill to summarize review data across businesses, identify recurring praise and complaints, and find unanswered or severe issues.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reported workflow may send review data to a third-party Actor without a privacy warning.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive or confidential review data unless external processing is authorized.
