## Use Case:

Writers and designers can use this skill to create artist biographies and brand narratives in an anecdote-led Vasarian voice. Clearly label literary imitation and verify factual claims about real people.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages invented biographical details to be presented with false certainty.

Mitigation: Use it only for clearly labeled literary imitation; verify claims about real people, and remove fabrication and uncertainty-suppression instructions for factual biographies or branding.
