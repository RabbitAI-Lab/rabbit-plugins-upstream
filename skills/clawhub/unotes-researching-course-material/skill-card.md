## Description:

Searches the uNotes study library for course materials and answers questions with each document source named.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and other learners use this skill to find public course notes, exams, assignments, and lab reports in uNotes and get answers grounded in named documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting uNotes grants access to searchable public documents and document text visible to the account.

Mitigation: Review and approve the uNotes connection and its requested access before using the skill.

Risk: Long documents may be cut off, leaving an answer incomplete.

Mitigation: Disclose when retrieved text is truncated and limit conclusions to the available excerpt.
