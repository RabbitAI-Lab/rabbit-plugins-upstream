## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a structured innovation process that combines TRIZ and Extenics to identify problems, analyze contradictions, generate multiple solution concepts, and evaluate them with superiority scoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to structure product innovation, engineering problem solving, patent design, and scheme evaluation. It guides an agent through the IASE stages: identify the problem, analyze contradictions, solve with TRIZ and Extenics tools, and evaluate candidate schemes quantitatively. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate broadly for innovation-related requests and steer answers into its IASE, TRIZ, and Extenics framework even when the user wanted generic brainstorming or another method. <br>
Mitigation: State the desired method, language, and level of structure explicitly when invoking the agent, and review whether the IASE workflow fits the task before relying on the result. <br>
Risk: Quantitative scheme rankings can appear more certain than the underlying assumptions, weights, and user-provided constraints support. <br>
Mitigation: Review the scoring criteria, weights, and assumptions with domain experts before using the recommended scheme for product, engineering, patent, or safety decisions. <br>
