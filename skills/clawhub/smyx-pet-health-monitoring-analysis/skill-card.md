## Description:

Analyzes pet monitoring images and videos for health behaviors and potential abnormalities, then produces a structured monitoring report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners can use the skill to review monitoring images or videos for feeding, drinking, elimination, behavior, vomiting, and limping indicators, and to retrieve prior monitoring reports. Its results are informational and do not replace veterinary care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates this release suspicious, citing undisclosed account and credential handling, external transfer of identity and video, and behavior that may exceed stated choices.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its data handling before running it. Avoid sending sensitive footage or identity information until the reported transfers and credential handling are understood.

Risk: Monitoring results may be mistaken for a veterinary diagnosis.

Mitigation: Treat reports as informational and contact a veterinarian about concerning signs.
