## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and marketing teams can use this skill to search YouTube and gather channel, video, subtitle, comment, recommendation, and trend data for content research and creator intelligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the service sends requests authenticated with an API key to an external API; channel email lookup can return creators’ business contact information.

Mitigation: Review the service and its data handling, avoid sending sensitive information unless you trust the destination, and use contact information only for the stated purpose rather than bulk unsolicited outreach.
