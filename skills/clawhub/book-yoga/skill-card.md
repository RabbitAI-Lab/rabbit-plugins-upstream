## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to search for yoga services, check availability, and create bookings through Lokuli's MCP service. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking creation may share personal contact details with Lokuli's external service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, date, time, name, email, and phone number before creating a booking, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information. <br>
Risk: An agent may create an unintended booking if booking details are not reviewed. <br>
Mitigation: Require user confirmation of the booking details before calling the create_booking tool. <br>
