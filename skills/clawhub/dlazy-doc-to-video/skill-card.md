## Description:

Helps turn documents into explainer, report, and training videos through dLazy's hosted workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users use this skill to turn supplied documents into narrated explainers, report summaries, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using a third-party CLI and keeping a dLazy API key on the local device.

Mitigation: Review the CLI before installation and protect or rotate the API key as needed.

Risk: Selected documents and prompts are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Only attach files and submit content you intend to share with dLazy.
