## Description:

Turns user-provided documents into narrated explainer, report, or training video projects through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn documents into explainer, report, or training video projects with outlines, storyboards, voiceovers, and validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review documents before attaching them and avoid sensitive content unless sharing it is intended.

Risk: The CLI can store a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key if it is exposed.

Risk: Using the skill installs or runs a third-party CLI package.

Mitigation: Review the pinned package and its linked source before installation.
