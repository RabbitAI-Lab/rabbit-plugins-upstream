## Description:

Helps turn documents into explainer, report, or training videos through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn Word, Markdown, PDF, or presentation documents into narrated explainers, report briefings, and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents may be uploaded to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Use only documents approved for sharing with dLazy; review attachments before submission.

Risk: Login may save an API key in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key in the dLazy dashboard if needed.

Risk: Installing the third-party CLI globally persists a local executable.

Mitigation: Prefer the skill's pinned npx invocation when a global install is not desired.
