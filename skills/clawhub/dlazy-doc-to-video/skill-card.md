## Description:

Turns documents into explainer, report, or training videos by parsing the source, outlining, storyboarding, adding voiceover, building, and validating the result.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can turn Word, Markdown, and other documents into explainer, report, courseware, or training videos through dLazy's hosted agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected documents or attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Attach only intended materials and review dLazy's service terms and data practices before use.

Risk: The external CLI executable and its credential permissions were not independently verified.

Mitigation: Keep API keys out of chat and review the pinned @dlazy/cli@1.2.3 package before installation.
