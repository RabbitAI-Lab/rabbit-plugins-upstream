## Description:

Guides Yoruba-speaking users through PowPow/Bubble account registration, registration troubleshooting, and subscription activation while keeping account creation and payment actions on the official PowPow site or app.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand PowPow/Bubble, follow the registration flow, resolve common sign-up issues, and complete subscription activation on the official PowPow web or app experience. Agents use it to provide concise Yoruba guidance and clear next steps without collecting passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, email verification codes, or payment credentials with the agent during registration or activation.

Mitigation: Keep all credential entry and payment activity on the official PowPow site or app, and instruct the agent not to ask for passwords, verification codes, or payment details.

Risk: Users may expect the skill to create accounts, submit forms, or pay on their behalf.

Mitigation: Present the skill as guidance-only and direct users to perform registration, verification, and payment actions themselves on the official PowPow flow.

Risk: Subscription activation status may be delayed, especially for manually confirmed WeChat QR payments.

Mitigation: Explain that PayPal activation may depend on webhook timing, WeChat QR activation requires manual confirmation, and users should wait or contact the site team when activation remains pending.
