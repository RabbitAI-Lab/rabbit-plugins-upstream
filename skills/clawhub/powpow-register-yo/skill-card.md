## Description:

Provides Yoruba-language guidance for registering a PowPow Bubble account, troubleshooting sign-up, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People signing up for PowPow Bubble use this skill for step-by-step Yoruba guidance on registration, email verification, common sign-up errors, and subscription activation. Users complete registration and payment themselves on the relevant website or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive account or payment information could be exposed in chat.

Mitigation: Enter passwords, email verification codes, and payment details only on the official PowPow or payment pages, never in chat.

Risk: An unverified external link could lead users to an unintended registration or payment page.

Mitigation: Verify registration and payment links before opening them or entering information.
