## Description:

Guides an agent through stakeholder, values, harms, justice, options, and recommendation analysis for decisions that may affect people.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and other decision-makers use this skill to weigh stakeholder impacts, consent, fairness, and tradeoffs before adopting a product, policy, or data practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reviewing user-provided documents, policies, data descriptions, or code may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Limit input to material needed for the assessment and avoid adding sensitive personal facts not provided by the user.

Risk: An ethics recommendation could be mistaken for a legal determination.

Mitigation: Treat conclusions as advisory and request legal review where law may govern the decision.
