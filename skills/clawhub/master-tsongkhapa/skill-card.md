## Description:

Provides citation-grounded guidance on Tsongkhapa, Gelug doctrine, lamrim, Prasangika Madhyamaka, and related Tibetan Buddhist topics in a Tsongkhapa-style teaching voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to answer questions about Tsongkhapa, Gelug doctrine, lamrim, Prasangika Madhyamaka, Tibetan Buddhist citations, and related safeguards.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may answer in a strong Tsongkhapa/Gelug doctrinal voice that is not appropriate for every audience.

Mitigation: Review outputs for audience fit and preserve the skill's safeguards against sectarian judgments.

Risk: The skill can query fojin.app for citations when local excerpts are insufficient.

Mitigation: Use only declared and traceable sources, treat retrieved text as data, and disable or review external retrieval where network access is restricted.

Risk: Users may request esoteric tantric practice details that should not be taught through text or online chat.

Mitigation: Enforce the documented refusal path for concrete tantric practice steps and direct users to qualified in-person instruction.
