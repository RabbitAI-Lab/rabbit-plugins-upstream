## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outreach recommendations involving automated DMs, follower targeting, or tracking can create spam, privacy, or platform-compliance issues. <br>
Mitigation: Review each outreach plan against platform terms, anti-spam rules, privacy obligations, rate limits, opt-out expectations, and the project's community standards before acting. <br>
Risk: Paid submissions, agency promotion, or community posting can mislead audiences or violate community guidelines if handled as undisclosed promotion. <br>
Mitigation: Use genuine, relevant, transparent submissions and verify third-party promotion practices against the target community's rules before spending budget. <br>
Risk: Growth benchmarks and region or language sequencing advice can be misapplied as guaranteed results or metric manipulation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat benchmarks as planning inputs, validate tactics with project analytics and community feedback, and avoid buying stars or manipulating engagement metrics. <br>
