## Use Case:

Developers and coding agents use this skill to structure non-trivial software tasks around intent, alignment, causal diagnosis, validation, decision-making, and completion evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit invocation may shape planning and completion behavior often.

Mitigation: Use it for non-trivial software tasks where quality-control guidance is appropriate, and keep final responses focused on observed outcomes and material uncertainty.

Risk: Guidance could be applied mechanically and create proxy completion claims.

Mitigation: Require direct acceptance evidence for the primary user-visible path before claiming work is done.

Risk: Proposals influenced by the skill could still introduce incorrect or misleading guidance into a workflow.

Mitigation: Review and scan the skill before deployment, especially after edits.
