## Description: <br>
Extract and risk-rate hidden assumptions in a product brief or PRD, producing a prioritised assumption map with confidence and impact scores, recommended validation methods, and critical assumption flags. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, founders, and product teams use this skill to review product briefs, PRDs, or concept notes for hidden desirability, feasibility, viability, and usability assumptions before development decisions are made. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product briefs and PRDs may contain confidential product plans or strategy details. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in an agent environment approved for the material being analyzed, and avoid pasting confidential plans unless that processing is acceptable. <br>
Risk: The assumption map may miss or mis-rank assumptions if the source brief is incomplete or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated assumptions, require coverage across desirability, feasibility, viability, and usability, and validate high-impact, low-confidence items before acting on the plan. <br>
