## Description:

Prepare salary negotiations with a structured toolkit: market-rate anchoring math, walk-away floor calculation, total-compensation evaluation (equity, bonuses, benefits), negotiation scripts for offers, raises, and counter-offers, and BATNA analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and job candidates use this skill to prepare compensation conversations for job offers, raise requests, competing offers, and salary-market comparisons. It helps agents gather market data, compute walk-away and total-compensation values, and draft negotiation scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary guidance may rely on stale or incomplete market data, or may be mistaken for legal or financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support, verify compensation data from current sources, and seek legal or financial advice for contracts, non-competes, tax-heavy equity questions, or unusual executive compensation terms.
