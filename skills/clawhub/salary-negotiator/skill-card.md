## Description:

Prepare salary negotiations with a structured toolkit: market-rate anchoring math, walk-away floor calculation, total-compensation evaluation (equity, bonuses, benefits), negotiation scripts for offers, raises, and counter-offers, and BATNA analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees and job seekers use this skill to prepare salary negotiations, evaluate offers and raises, compare compensation packages, calculate a walk-away floor, and generate negotiation scripts. It is intended for salaried employment compensation planning, not legal review or freelance rate setting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary, offer, and household budget details are sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Ask only for details needed for the calculation and avoid retaining or sharing them beyond the local session.

Risk: Negotiation outputs may be mistaken for legal or financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the generated strategy as preparation guidance and seek qualified review for non-competes, equity agreements, tax questions, or other legal terms.
