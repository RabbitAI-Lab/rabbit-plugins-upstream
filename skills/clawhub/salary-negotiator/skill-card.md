## Description:

Prepare salary negotiations with a structured toolkit: market-rate anchoring math, walk-away floor calculation, total-compensation evaluation, negotiation scripts for offers, raises, and counter-offers, and BATNA analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees and job candidates use this skill to evaluate salary offers, compare total compensation packages, calculate a walk-away floor, and prepare negotiation or raise scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generated negotiation numbers or scripts as legal, tax, financial, or employment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational negotiation support and consult qualified professionals for legal, tax, equity agreement, or employment-specific decisions.

Risk: Recommendations depend on the quality of user-supplied market, role, level, location, and compensation data.

Mitigation: Use matched market data from multiple sources and review the tool's assumptions before acting on a negotiation plan.

Risk: The skill may run a local Python calculator with sensitive compensation figures provided by the user.

Mitigation: Run the bundled script locally, avoid sharing unnecessary personal details, and review generated outputs before sending them to an employer or recruiter.
