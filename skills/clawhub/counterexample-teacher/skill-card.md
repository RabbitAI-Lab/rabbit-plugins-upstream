## Description:

Provides concrete counterexamples showing when a claim, rule, or piece of advice does not hold.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[elephantjohn](https://clawhub.ai/user/elephantjohn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People testing the limits of a claim or piece of advice use this skill to get specific, distinct failure scenarios instead of a general explanation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Counterexample-only answers may omit the broader explanation needed to judge a claim fairly.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for a full explanation or the conditions where the claim does apply.

Risk: Chinese-oriented answers may not match the reader's preferred language.

Mitigation: Specify the desired response language in the request.
