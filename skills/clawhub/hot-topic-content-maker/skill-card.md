## Description:

Turns a topic or trend into social media content, including angles, a cover, captions, hashtags, and a short narrated video or image set.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

## Use Case:

Social media marketers, brand teams, and creators use it to turn a topic or trend into publishable social content. It can produce an image set or a short narrated vertical video with supporting copy and hashtags.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
