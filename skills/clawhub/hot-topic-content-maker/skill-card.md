## Description:

Turns current social trends into platform-ready image posts or narrated short videos, with captions, hashtags, and cover assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media creators and marketing teams can use it to find a timely topic or angle and prepare a publishable post or short video for channels such as Douyin, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release requires broader account permissions than its workflow needs, and malformed installation records can disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Request narrower authorization and preserve shared credentials if installation records are malformed; revoke device access in Beatra Console when needed.

Risk: Shared authorization covers Beatra skills and allows spending approved through agent instructions.

Mitigation: Review and explicitly approve any credit-spending actions before they are carried out.

Risk: Providing a personal photo may involve processing and retention by Beatra.

Mitigation: Review Beatra's processing and retention terms first, or use a virtual presenter instead of uploading a real person's photo.
