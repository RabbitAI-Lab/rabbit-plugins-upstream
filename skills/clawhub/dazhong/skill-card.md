## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill provides heuristic guidance and cannot guarantee restaurant quality, current rankings, prices, hours, or reservation availability. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current details in Dianping or another current source before acting on a recommendation. <br>
Risk: Ratings, platform list rules, and review patterns can change over time, so stale or incomplete listing data may lead to misleading choices. <br>
Mitigation: Use the latest available listing information, recent reviews, and a backup option when making time-sensitive decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill discusses review manipulation signals and could be misapplied as operational advice for gaming ratings. <br>
Mitigation: Keep use limited to reliability assessment and merchant communication; do not provide rating manipulation, fake-review, or paid-ranking instructions. <br>
