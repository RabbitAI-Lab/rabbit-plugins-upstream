This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External operators, founders, and team members use this skill to convert Loom videos, meeting transcripts, voice notes, rough notes, or verbal walkthroughs into actionable SOPs with structure, edge cases, metrics, and maintenance guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source transcripts, recordings, notes, or process descriptions can contain secrets, customer data, regulated information, or private internal details.

Mitigation: Review inputs for sensitive information before use and handle generated SOP drafts only in approved tools with appropriate sharing and retention settings.

Risk: Automation ideas involving third-party tools can create unintended data sharing or retention exposure.

Mitigation: Use approved automation tools and confirm their access, sharing, and retention settings before connecting recordings, transcripts, or SOP libraries.
