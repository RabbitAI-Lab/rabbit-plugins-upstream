## Description: <br>
This skill helps Chinese-speaking homeowners screen renovation companies by surfacing selection criteria, warning signs, quote and contract checks, and public-information lookup steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and renovation customers use this skill to identify common renovation-company risks, prepare screening questions, review quote and contract warning signs, and gather public information about a named company before making their own decision. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Named-company lookups are intentionally negative-focused and may over-weight complaints or omit balanced context. <br>
Mitigation: Require clearly cited public sources, separate factual findings from interpretation, and tell users to verify important claims independently before acting. <br>
Risk: The artifact contains a whitelist that suppresses public web searches for specific organizations. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose the whitelist behavior to reviewers and users, and review whether exceptions are appropriate before publication or installation. <br>
Risk: Renovation guidance may be mistaken for legal, financial, or professional construction advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the disclaimer visible, avoid definitive recommendations, and direct users to local professionals for contracts, safety, and final vendor decisions. <br>
