# Comfort Routing Reference

## Day Design

- Start from the fixed hotel sequence and draw the simplest driving line between nights.
- Put the day’s one real objective first. Everything else is a bonus.
- Cap “hard stops” at 2-3 per day for low-stamina travelers.
- Avoid long scenic detours after a long attraction, timed tour, or late lunch.
- Treat sunset and fatigue as constraints, not details.

## Low-Stamina / Pregnancy-Friendly Heuristics

Prefer:
- Indoor attractions with seating, bathrooms, cafes, and controlled temperature.
- Viewpoints that are car-adjacent or under 20 minutes.
- Town strolls with benches, food, and easy parking.
- Scenic drives where the view is the experience.

Avoid or demote:
- Sand, steep stairs, loose trails, exposed heat, long hikes, remote roads after dark.
- Attractions with strict tour times when the day already has a long drive.
- Restaurants that require long waits when the route has sparse food options.

## Priority Labels

Use priority labels to make the plan runnable on the road:

- `A 优先保留` or `A PCH 必停`: high reward, low or acceptable cost, strongly worth keeping.
- `B 看状态`: good but optional; do if timing/body battery is good.
- `C 可跳过` or `C 体力好再停`: nice-to-have, poor cost/reward, parking-heavy, or redundant.

Each priority needs:
- Why it earns that rank.
- Typical stop time.
- What to do instead if skipped.

## Drive-Time Difficulty

Report:
- Total drive time range.
- Approximate distance.
- Difficulty: `低`, `中`, `中高`, or `高`.
- The real source of fatigue: heat, city traffic, mountain roads, parking, low-speed coast road, remote services, or late arrival.

Use ranges, not false precision. Scenic coastal routes often have similar mileage to highways but feel harder because they add slow driving, parking, and mental load.

## Meal and Rest Strategy

- Put lunch before remote scenic stretches.
- Mark formal restaurants as optional when waiting would harm the route.
- Add grocery/water/snack reminders before low-service areas.
- If a venue has a cafe, treat it as a fatigue valve, not necessarily the best meal.
- Add bathroom/rest stops every 75-90 minutes when practical.

## Safety and Medical Backup

When relevant, add nearby hospitals or urgent-care backups by region, not only near hotels. Keep this concise and map-linked.

Do not provide medical advice. Phrase as logistics: “backup hospital,” “nearest major hospital,” “keep insurance/medical documents available.”
