## Description:

Build a 90-day SEO plan for one specific business that diagnoses the site's stage, puts money pages first, sets content pillars from real customer problems, lists what to skip, and fits the hours you have.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, founders, and SEO or marketing leads use this skill to turn business goals, customer problems, site evidence, and available capacity into a prioritized 90-day SEO action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may send customer-derived questions to external AI services without a privacy warning.

Mitigation: Use generic, non-identifying questions and only services approved for the data involved.
