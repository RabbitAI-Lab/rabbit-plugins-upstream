## Description:

Helps Chinese-language creators draft platform-specific titles and cover text that fit their content and avoid misleading promises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent creators use this skill to draft and compare Chinese-language titles and cover text for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and other content platforms. It checks that each title fits the script or finished footage and explains its chosen structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback and performance notes may be saved to local skill memory for later sessions.

Mitigation: Review or disable memory write-back before use if persistent notes are unwanted.

Risk: Broad title and cover-text triggers may route unrelated requests to this skill.

Mitigation: Narrow the activation triggers if generic title requests should be handled elsewhere.
