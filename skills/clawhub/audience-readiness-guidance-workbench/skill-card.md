## Description:

Build a client-facing brief from approved sections while recording omissions for internal follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Communications teams use this skill to compose a client-facing briefing preview from audience guidance and supplied request sections. It includes approved sections, excludes disallowed or internal material, and records omission reasons for follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect audience guidance or section tags could cause the generated brief to include or omit the wrong sections.

Mitigation: Confirm the audience guidance and section tags before circulation, and review the excluded_sections list for unexpected omissions.
