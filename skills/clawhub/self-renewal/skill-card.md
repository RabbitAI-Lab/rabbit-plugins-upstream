## Description:

Guides individuals and teams through testing and updating assumptions that may no longer fit current conditions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Leaders, teams, and individuals use this skill to identify assumptions behind stale strategies or persistent patterns, examine credible evidence against them, and define considered behavior changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Repeated challenges may encourage changing beliefs that remain valid or are not affected by changed conditions.

Mitigation: Keep beliefs that withstand credible evidence, check that conditions have changed, and set a limit on repeated challenges.

Risk: Suggested behavior changes may be acted on before their basis and effects are reviewed.

Mitigation: Review suggested behavior changes before acting.
