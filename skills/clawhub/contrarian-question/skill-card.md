## Description:

Guides an agent through a two-part test of whether a business or investment claim is genuinely non-consensus and supported by evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, investors, and interview candidates use the skill to stress-test one business or investment claim, separate real disagreement from consensus phrased provocatively, and assess whether the claim has an evidence-based defense.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may rely on the assessment as sufficient support for a business or investment decision.

Mitigation: Use the framework as a thinking aid and independently verify the evidence before relying on its conclusions for business or investment decisions.
