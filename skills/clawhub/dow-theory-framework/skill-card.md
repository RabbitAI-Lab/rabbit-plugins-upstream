## Description: <br>
Activates when an agent needs to classify market trend scale and stage using Dow Theory principles such as confirmation, volume validation, and decisive reversal signals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to guide technical market analysis by separating primary, secondary, and minor trends, checking cross-market confirmation, and avoiding premature reversal calls. It is educational analysis support, not personalized financial advice or authorization to trade. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake educational market-analysis guidance for personalized financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as educational technical-analysis support and require users to make independent decisions or consult qualified financial professionals. <br>
Risk: Trend classification can be late or wrong in choppy markets, leading to overconfidence. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit confidence limits, cross-market confirmation checks, and clear escalation to adjacent tools for support lines or reversal-pattern analysis. <br>
