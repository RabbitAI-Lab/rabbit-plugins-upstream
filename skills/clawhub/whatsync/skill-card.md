## Description:

Guides an agent in reading, searching, summarizing, and replying to a user's synced WhatsApp chats and contacts through WhatSync.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[itsanishjain](https://clawhub.ai/user/itsanishjain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with a connected WhatSync account can ask their agent to find and summarize synced WhatsApp chats, identify conversations needing replies, locate contacts and attachments, and send messages after confirming the recipient and exact text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may access sensitive synced messages, contacts, and attachments.

Mitigation: Connect only chats the user is comfortable sharing and request attachment links only when needed.

Risk: Sending a message reaches a real recipient and cannot be undone.

Mitigation: Show the exact recipient and message, obtain clear approval, send once, and check status instead of retrying when uncertain.
