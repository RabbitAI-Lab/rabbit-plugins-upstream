## Description:

Turns buyer objections into concise response cards with an answer, a proof point, and a next question for U.S. B2B reps and sales engineers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

U.S. B2B reps and sales engineers use the skill to structure buyer objections into concise responses, supporting proof points, and follow-up questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The supplied YAML template is incompatible with the script's heading-based parser, and the script does not generate answers.

Mitigation: Use input organized under the expected headings, prepare answers separately, and review the generated digest in a dedicated output folder.
