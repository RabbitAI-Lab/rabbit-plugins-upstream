## Description: <br>
Build, customize, review, debug, migrate, and optimize Ghost CMS Handlebars themes. Use for Ghost theme templates and partials, package.json custom settings, routes.yaml, memberships, Portal, comments, search, GScan, or converting a design into a Ghost theme. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated changes to routes.yaml, membership or account templates, search integrations, analytics settings, npm commands, or GScan commands could affect a production Ghost site. <br>
Mitigation: Review those changes before applying them to production, and run GScan plus any available theme build or lint command before release. <br>
Risk: Theme output may be technically valid but still create accessibility, SEO, or membership-state confusion. <br>
Mitigation: Review rendered templates for semantic landmarks, heading order, focus states, contrast, Ghost metadata helpers, and member-aware fallbacks. <br>
