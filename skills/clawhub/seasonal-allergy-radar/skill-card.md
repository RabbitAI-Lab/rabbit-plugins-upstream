## Description:

Helps people track seasonal allergy symptoms, compare them with climate-zone pollen calendars, and plan for upcoming pollen seasons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People with seasonal allergies can record daily symptoms, explore possible tree, grass, or weed pollen patterns, and review a 14-day planning forecast and suggested medication start dates. It is not a diagnostic tool or a substitute for clinical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested medication timing may be inappropriate for an individual's health circumstances.

Mitigation: Use the plan for information only; confirm medication choices and timing with a pharmacist or clinician, particularly for children, pregnancy, other conditions, or breathing symptoms.

Risk: Locally stored symptom logs may contain sensitive health information.

Mitigation: Store the JSON log in a location with appropriate access controls and share it only when intended.

Risk: Static climate-zone calendars may not reflect local weather or current pollen conditions.

Mitigation: Treat forecasts as planning estimates rather than diagnoses; check current conditions and seek clinical advice for concerning symptoms.
