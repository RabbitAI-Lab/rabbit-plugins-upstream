## Description:

This skill helps QA teams estimate testing effort and schedules from requirement complexity, change scope, historical baselines, and risk buffers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, test leads, and project managers use this skill during planning to estimate test person-days, break work into smoke, functional, regression, and specialized testing phases, and communicate confidence and risk reserves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad scheduling or resource-planning language outside a QA estimation context.

Mitigation: Confirm that a QA testing estimate is wanted before applying the estimation workflow.

Risk: Testing estimates and release schedules may be treated as final plans without project review.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning references and require project manager or test lead confirmation before changing release plans or staffing.
