## Description:

Provides traditional Chinese medicine reference guidance in the Ni Haixia Jingfang style, including six-channel and organ-pattern differentiation, classic and Han Tang formula lookup, herb information, dosage logic, and lifestyle cautions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ahfai1](https://clawhub.ai/user/ahfai1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to ask about Ni Haixia-style Jingfang and Han Tang formula references, herb properties, pattern differentiation, and educational TCM reasoning. It should be used only as historical or educational reference material, not as medical diagnosis, prescription, or treatment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is broad enough to generate symptom-based herbal prescription and dosage guidance for serious health conditions.

Mitigation: Review before installing and restrict use to historical or educational TCM reference material, not diagnosis, prescriptions, medication changes, or treatment decisions.

Risk: Users may apply generated guidance to urgent conditions or higher-risk populations.

Mitigation: Direct users to consult a licensed clinician for real health decisions, and avoid use for serious symptoms, pregnancy, children, elder care, or urgent conditions.
