## Description: <br>
Find, book, and change flights for a human via email through BonBook. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aszelem](https://clawhub.ai/user/aszelem) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and their agents use this skill to search, book, change, or cancel flights through BonBook by email, with explicit human approval for account setup, traveler information, checkout, payment, calendar sync, and booking changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill depends on agent email access and may involve travel profile details, payment setup, and optional calendar sync. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when the user accepts BonBook handling booking data, and require personal approval for account creation, traveler information, calendar connection, purchases, changes, cancellations, fees, and payment steps. <br>
Risk: Flight booking, changes, and cancellations can create financial charges or travel disruption. <br>
Mitigation: Review itinerary details, refund eligibility, fees, and checkout pages with the human before selecting offers or confirming transactions. <br>
Risk: Email exchanges may include booking references, flight details, first names, and receipts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with intended email permissions and avoid sending full legal names, DOB, passport details, credentials, or card data through email. <br>
