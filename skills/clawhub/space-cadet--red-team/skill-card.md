## Description: <br>
Red Team is a protocol for validating numerical claims through five written gates before treating tentative observations as reportable results. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[space-cadet](https://clawhub.ai/user/space-cadet) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, and analysts use this skill to decide whether numerical results are ready to report by requiring citations, method parameters, uncertainty estimates, falsification notes, and peer-review-style checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The protocol may delay or downgrade numerical conclusions when required evidence is missing. <br>
Mitigation: Use tentative language until citations, parameters, uncertainty estimates, falsification notes, and a written review log are complete. <br>
Risk: A checklist can organize review but does not independently prove that a calculation or cited source is correct. <br>
Mitigation: Review the underlying methods, parameters, data, uncertainty estimates, and sources before relying on promoted claims. <br>
