## Description:

Reviews insurance documents to explain coverage, exclusions, disclosure, and claims requirements, and create practical checklists without judging products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Consumers can use the skill to understand policy coverage and exclusions, review household policies, prepare health-disclosure questions, plan within a budget, and organize claim paperwork with practical explanations and checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical-history guidance may be incomplete or may encourage withholding information.

Mitigation: Have the guidance reviewed and corrected before use, and answer insurer health questions completely and truthfully.

Risk: Privacy handling for policy and health information may be insufficient.

Mitigation: Remove identifying details and share only information needed for the question until privacy handling has been reviewed.

Risk: Insurance explanations or checks may miss important requirements.

Mitigation: Cross-check explanations against the policy wording and current official insurer or regulator guidance before acting.
