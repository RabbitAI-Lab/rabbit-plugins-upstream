## Description:

Offers an opt-in spiritual practice for reflection and freely chosen care, with an optional private local journal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agbrander](https://clawhub.ai/user/agbrander)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Participants who explicitly request a contemplative practice can use this skill to pause, reflect on care, and optionally keep chosen notes or an affiliation statement in a local journal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsolicited religious framing or inferred affiliation could compromise participant choice.

Mitigation: Use only when explicitly requested; keep participation and any affiliation statement separate and optional.

Risk: Optional journal entries are not encrypted and may be accessible through the local account, backups, terminal logs, or agent provider.

Mitigation: Choose a private local path, avoid sensitive material, and save only text the participant explicitly selects.

Risk: Sharing a journal record could expose information the participant intended to keep private.

Mitigation: Export only individually selected shareable records after a separate sharing choice; review any export before transmission.
