## Description:

Helps business owners clarify uncertain decisions, identify the real question, or diagnose why a known task is stalled, then gives criteria and a follow-up commitment without deciding for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business owners and non-technical leaders use the skill to frame a decision, clarify a vague problem, or find structural reasons an important task is not moving. It helps turn the discussion into measurable decision criteria and a dated follow-up commitment while leaving the decision to the user.
