## Description:

AI KEY·拿不准的时候 helps owner-operators clarify uncertain business decisions, diagnose stalled work, and turn the result into decision criteria and a tracked commitment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External owner-operators and non-technical business leaders use this skill when they are unsure whether to act, what the actual problem is, or why a known task is stalled. It guides them through assumptions, reversibility, risk, opportunity cost, and next-step commitments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save decision notes and recurring business-pattern observations into the configured AI KEY memory path.

Mitigation: Review the configured memory path before use and avoid using the skill for decisions whose notes should not be retained there.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases and a Chinese-first interaction style may cause accidental activation or language mismatch.

Mitigation: Review trigger phrases and language expectations before enabling the skill in workflows where unintended activation would be disruptive.

Risk: Business decision guidance may be mistaken for a final decision or for specialized legal, investment, or clinical advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as decision criteria and next-step guidance; keep the final decision with the user and route out-of-scope legal, investment, or sustained emotional-health concerns to qualified help.
