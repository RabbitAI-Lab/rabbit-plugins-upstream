## Description:

Helps business owner-operators clarify difficult decisions, identify the real question, and diagnose structural causes of stalled work using explicit criteria and follow-up commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business owners and operators use the skill when deciding whether to act, clarifying what problem they are facing, or diagnosing why a known task is stalled. It guides them through assumptions, trade-offs, criteria, and a dated follow-up commitment without making the decision for them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal and behavioral notes may be retained without clear user consent or controls for deletion.

Mitigation: Before storing confidential business or personal information, obtain approval for memory entries and ensure users can inspect, correct, and delete them. Verify the storage location and review the safeguards for interaction and tracking components.
