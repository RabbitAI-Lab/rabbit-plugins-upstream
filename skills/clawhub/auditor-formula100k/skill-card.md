## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Client-confidential metrics or account data may pass through Composio, Apify, or Telegram during audits.

Mitigation: Confirm the approved data path before use and avoid sensitive audits unless third-party transit is acceptable for the client.

Risk: Business audit details may be persisted in deliverable folders and local memory files.

Mitigation: Use explicit retention expectations, restrict workspace access, and remove client data when the audit no longer requires it.
