# The Sub-Crafts — What Changes by Domain

The arc, the questions, and the commitment mechanics are the same everywhere. What changes is what counts as progress, which trap is standard, and where the boundary sits. Load the section that matches `niche`, or the one the client's goal implies.

**Before working in a niche**, check `niche` in `~/Clawic/data/coach/config.yaml` and `## Focus` in `memory.md`. A client whose declared niche is executive and whose actual goal is an exit is a career engagement wearing a corporate badge, and the metrics differ.

## Executive

- **What they actually want**: usually not the presented goal. Presented: "communicate better with the board." Actual: a decision they have been avoiding, a relationship that is failing, or the question of whether they still want the job.
- **Progress metric**: behavior other people notice. 360 feedback at baseline and at the end, or three named colleagues asked the same question twice (`progress.md`).
- **Standard trap**: the sponsor's goal and the client's diverge and nobody says it. Three-way contracting at intake, in one meeting (`intake.md`).
- **Time is the binding constraint.** Commitments must survive a week where the calendar is not theirs — the 20-minute worst-day test is doing real work here.
- **Isolation is the recurring theme.** Often you are the only person who can be told the truth, which is valuable and is also the thing that quietly becomes dependency (SKILL.md Rule 9).
- Frequent real topics: delegation they say is a bandwidth problem and is actually control; a peer relationship they are managing by avoidance; succession; a founder who cannot let a function go; whether to leave.

## Career

- **Progress metric**: leading indicators only — conversations had, applications sent, drafts finished. Offers are lagging and mostly not controlled (`goal-setting.md`).
- **Standard trap**: coaching the résumé when the constraint is that they have not told anyone they are looking. Ask for the count of people told; it is often zero.
- **Decision paralysis** between two acceptable options is a values question, not an analysis question: what would each choice cost them in five years, and which cost do they refuse.
- **Non-linear moves** (industry change, IC to manager and back, going independent) need a bridge plan and a runway number, or the goal is a wish.
- Boundary: offer evaluation, equity, negotiation mechanics and layoff response are analysis, not coaching — that is `career`. Coach the decision-making and the follow-through around it.
- Redundancy and firing carry grief. Give it room before any planning; a plan built in week one of a layoff is usually abandoned in week three.

## Business and Founder

- **Progress metric**: one number the client agrees is the business's number this quarter, plus the weekly process that moves it.
- **Standard trap**: the founder brings strategy questions because they are more comfortable than the people question underneath. Ask what conversation they are not having.
- **Working in vs on the business** is the classic frame and it stays useful: count the hours in each for one week rather than discussing the ratio.
- **Co-founder conflict** is the most common engagement-ending topic and it is a two-sided system — coach one side's contribution to it or contract with both (`teams.md`).
- Cash runway is a hard constraint on every commitment; a goal that assumes six months when there are three is not a goal.
- Boundary: business model, pricing strategy and fundraising mechanics are expertise. Switch to advice explicitly or refer (`referral.md`).

## Health and Fitness

- **Progress metric**: process adherence first (sessions completed, meals prepared), body metrics second and slowly. Coaching to the scale weekly produces demoralization on normal variance.
- **Standard trap**: intensity over consistency. Three sessions a week that happen beat five that do not — and the client always proposes five in January.
- **Identity is the lever**: "someone who trains on Tuesdays" outlasts any target weight (`goal-setting.md`).
- **Hard boundary**: no programming, no nutrition prescription, no interpretation of symptoms or bloodwork. That is a physician, a dietitian, or a qualified trainer. Coach adherence, environment, and identity.
- Disordered eating signals, rapid unexplained loss, or exercise that continues through injury are Red Flags rows — refer (`referral.md`).
- Body metrics belong in the shared health box, never duplicated into coaching files (`memory-template.md`).

## Life

- **The vaguest brief, so measurement matters most.** Wheel of life at baseline gives eight 0-10 ratings; re-read the same eight at each review — the delta is the engagement's evidence (`progress.md`).
- **Standard trap**: coaching all eight domains at once. Three concurrent goals is the ceiling, and life coaching invites eight (`goal-setting.md`).
- **Values work earns its place here** more than in any other niche, because there is no external scoreboard to substitute for it.
- **Energy audit** is the highest-yield single exercise: two weeks of logging what gives and takes energy, then one thing removed permanently.
- Boundary: "I'm unhappy and I don't know why" with no goal that survives two sessions is closer to therapy than to coaching (`referral.md`).

## Creative

- **Progress metric**: volume shipped, not quality. Words, pages, drafts, uploads, submissions — quality is unmeasurable in the short run and improves through volume anyway.
- **Standard trap**: coaching the work instead of the process. You are not their editor; if they want an editor, that is a different service.
- **Resistance is scheduled, not resolved.** The reliable structure is a fixed daily block with a floor version ("50 words on a collapsed day"), not motivation.
- **Comparison and public reception** wreck output. Separate the making commitment from the sharing commitment; they need different structures and different weeks.
- Rejection cadence: for submission-based work, commit to a rejection count rather than an acceptance count — it is the only version they control.
- Perfectionism as avoidance has a specific tell: version seven, unshipped. Define "good enough" as an observable and ship the current version this week (`stuck.md`).

## ADHD and Executive Function

Standard accountability fails here, and pushing harder adds shame to a constraint that is not about wanting it enough.

- **Externalize everything.** Timers, body doubling, a visible list of one item, alarms tied to the cue rather than the intention (`accountability.md`).
- **Same-day starts.** A commitment due in nine days does not exist. Cycles of 7-14 days, not 90.
- **Initiation, not planning, is the bottleneck.** Coach the first 60 seconds: the file open, the shoes on, the message drafted. What follows is rarely the problem.
- **Time blindness** means "how long will that take?" gets a genuinely wrong answer. Measure one real instance and use that number thereafter.
- **Interest-based activation** is real: pair the boring task with novelty, urgency, a person, or music, and stop treating the need for it as a flaw.
- **Shame is the load-bearing obstacle.** Every missed commitment sits on a long history of them. Neutral verdicts, no disappointment, resize immediately (SKILL.md Rule 5).
- Boundary: assessment, diagnosis and medication are clinical. Coaching adapts to the constraint; it does not treat it.

## Sports and Performance

- **The technical coach and the performance coach are different roles.** Yours is preparation, focus, confidence, routine, and the response to a bad result — not technique.
- **Pre-performance routine** is the deliverable: a fixed, rehearsed sequence that is the same in training and in competition. Its value is that it is unchanged under pressure.
- **Process goals only on the day.** Outcome goals at the start line raise arousal and cost execution; the client's attention has one slot.
- **Post-performance debrief within 48 hours**, in a fixed shape: what worked, what to change, one thing to practice. Later than that is memory, not review.
- Injury, weight-making pressure, and overtraining are health boundaries and route to clinicians (`referral.md`).

## Financial

- **Coaching, not advice.** Behavior with money: spending, avoidance, negotiation, the conversation with a partner. Never products, allocation, or tax — that is regulated in most jurisdictions.
- **The avoidance is the pattern**: unopened statements, unknown balances, a business whose numbers the owner has not looked at in a quarter. First commitment is usually "look at the number", nothing more.
- **Money conversations with a partner** are a rehearsal exercise: the opening sentence, out loud, in the session.
- **Progress metric**: behaviors (statements opened, one transfer automated, one conversation had), not net worth.
- Debt distress, insolvency, and anything with legal exposure route to licensed advisers and specialist debt services (`referral.md`).

## Academic

- **Progress metric**: study sessions completed and problems attempted, not hours "spent studying", which is unfalsifiable.
- **Standard trap**: re-reading and highlighting feel productive and are the weakest study behaviors. Coach toward retrieval — closed-book attempts, past papers, explaining it out loud.
- **The dissertation pattern** is avoidance with a virtuous surface: more reading instead of writing. Daily minimum word count with a floor, written before reading (`stuck.md`).
- Exam anxiety that produces panic symptoms is a Red Flags row, not a preparation problem.
- Parents as sponsors of a teenage client is three-way contracting, with the same rule: the student sees whatever the parent sees (`intake.md`).

## Relationship

- **One person in the room, one system.** You coach their contribution, their requests, and their boundaries — never the absent partner's behavior.
- **"How do I make them…" has no coaching answer.** Convert to: what will you ask for, how, and by when.
- **Rehearsal is the intervention**: the actual opening sentence, spoken, with the coach reacting as the other person might.
- **Both parties present** is couples work and requires that training. Refer.
- Any sign of abuse, coercion, or fear of the partner: stop and route to specialist support (SKILL.md Red Flags).

## Manager as Coach

The most common coaching context in the world, and the one with a structural conflict most people ignore.

- **You hold power. "What do you want?" is not a neutral question** from the person who sets their salary, and the answer will be adjusted for you.
- **Separate the conversations by name.** "This is a coaching conversation" vs "this is a decision I am making." Never blend them; a decision delivered in coaching form is manipulation with a friendly voice.
- **Coach the how, decide the what.** Direction, priorities and standards are management. Approach, obstacles and development are coachable.
- **Never use coaching material in a performance decision.** Once, and nobody in that team is honest with you again.
- **Skills transfer**: the GROW arc works in fifteen minutes at a desk, and one good question in a 1:1 beats a monthly coaching ritual nobody wants.
- Refer out anything personal, clinical, or about their future beyond your team — you cannot be neutral about whether they leave.

## Choosing a Niche

For the coach deciding where to practice (`practice.md`):

- **A niche is a buyer, not a topic.** "Founders raising a seed round" has a buyer, a budget, and a place they gather; "life coaching" has none of the three.
- **Credibility comes from having been the buyer** or from a body of visible work with that buyer. Pick where you have one of the two, or accept a long unpaid runway building it.
- **Test with 5 conversations before rebranding anything.** If nobody can name their problem in the same words you used, the niche is yours and not theirs.
- Deep niches command higher rates and shorter sales cycles; the cost is exposure to that population's cycle — a niche tied to one industry's hiring or funding market is a business with one customer.

**When a niche, a metric, or a domain-specific adaptation is agreed**, record it: the niche as a declaration in `config.yaml`, the baseline markers and their scale in `clients/<name>.md` or `## Focus`, and any exercise the client will re-read (wheel of life, values list, energy audit, pre-performance routine) as its own file in `artifacts/<kebab-name>.md` with its `## Boxes` line, in the same turn (`memory-template.md`). Body metrics go to the shared health box, never here.
