## Description: <br>
Moneyprinter helps users, with explicit permission, turn recent accessible AI work into one cash-first route and continue private preparation up to the next real evidence event. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bilbop1](https://clawhub.ai/user/bilbop1) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and builders use this skill to inspect permitted recent AI session history, summarize monetizable work, choose one cash-first route, and prepare private artifacts for a bounded payable test. It requires explicit approval before any contact, posting, purchase, contract, account change, payment collection, or other external commitment. <br>
