## Description:

Track your sports bets in a local CSV journal. Calculate ROI, CLV, win rate by sport/bet-type, and identify where you're actually making money.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ianalloway](https://clawhub.ai/user/ianalloway)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who place sports bets can use this skill to record wagers in a local CSV journal and review results by sport, bet type, ROI, and closing-line value.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The local journal contains personal betting history.

Mitigation: Limit access to the CSV and keep secure backups.

Risk: Imported records may be incorrect if source columns do not match the example mapping.

Mitigation: Review the import mapping and sample records before appending them to the journal.
