## Description: <br>
Deslop, humanize, edit, or audit AI-sounding, robotic, generic, corporate, or ChatGPT-like copy while preserving the writer's voice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[skyzer](https://clawhub.ai/user/skyzer) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, marketers, and developers use this skill to clean up AI-sounding drafts or audit text for named copy patterns while preserving the original voice and meaning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad editing triggers and strict punctuation rules may change ordinary copy-editing requests more than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit prompts when a lighter edit, source fidelity, or different punctuation is required, and review edited text before publishing. <br>
Risk: Detection findings identify writing patterns but do not prove that text was written by AI. <br>
Mitigation: Treat findings as editorial evidence only and avoid numeric scores or authorship claims. <br>
