## Use Case:

Biopharma strategy, IP, and product-development users use this skill to map biotech pipelines, compare competitors, evaluate CAR-T and gene-therapy assets, and draft evidence-tagged FTO and landscape reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can submit proprietary biological sequences for patent FTO work.

Mitigation: Use it only with sequences the user is authorized to share with the configured sequence-search provider.

Risk: FTO and design-around outputs can influence patent or product-development decisions.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as preliminary research and require review by qualified patent counsel before action.

Risk: Pipeline, clinical, regulatory, and patent evidence may be incomplete or time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Preserve source markers, label missing or unverified facts explicitly, and confirm current regulatory or patent status from authoritative sources.
