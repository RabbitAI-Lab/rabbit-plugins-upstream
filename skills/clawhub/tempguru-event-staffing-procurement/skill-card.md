## Description:

Answers event staffing procurement and vendor-onboarding questions from TempGuru's published policies, then helps buyers plan an event and prepare a buyer-operated quote-form handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kissmyabs32](https://clawhub.ai/user/kissmyabs32)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Procurement teams, buyers, and venues use this skill to answer event staffing vendor-onboarding questions from TempGuru's published policies and identify which terms require coordinator confirmation. For a real event, it helps gather details for a staffing plan and, after buyer confirmation, provides a buyer-submitted quote-form handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States and Canada

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event details may be sent to TempGuru or saved, and buyers may not be told about this sharing.

Mitigation: Tell buyers that event details may be sent to TempGuru and saved, and let them decide what information they are comfortable sharing.
