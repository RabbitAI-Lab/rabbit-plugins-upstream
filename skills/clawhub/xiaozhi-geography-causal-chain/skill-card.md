## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking geography students to explain physical and human geography phenomena by building cause-and-effect chains one step at a time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to work through middle- and high-school geography cause-and-effect questions, identify missing steps, and practice explaining the result in their own words.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and curriculum guidance may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts and curriculum references before use in another region or language.

Risk: A minor's learning profile or reminders could be used without appropriate permission.

Mitigation: Verify that profile memory and reminder integrations honor the disclosed consent controls and local minor-consent requirements.
