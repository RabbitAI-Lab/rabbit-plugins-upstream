## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students to explain geographic phenomena by building a cause-and-effect chain one step at a time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this geography tutor to work through why a phenomenon occurs, fill missing causal steps, and practice explaining natural and human-environment processes without being given a complete answer upfront.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (simplified-Chinese K12); use elsewhere requires localization of curriculum, privacy and guardian-consent rules, and crisis contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning-profile data may be stored or shared without meaningful consent.

Mitigation: Keep tutoring session-only by default; require explicit consent for profile access and individual confirmation for updates or sharing.

Risk: Crisis referrals or curriculum assumptions may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and privacy rules before deployment elsewhere; confirm the student's location before suggesting local crisis contacts and refer urgent concerns to a trusted adult and local emergency services.
