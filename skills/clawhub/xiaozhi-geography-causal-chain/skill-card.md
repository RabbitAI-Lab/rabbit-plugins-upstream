## Description:

Guides students through geography cause-and-effect chains by identifying an outcome, finding a starting cause, filling in intermediate steps, and checking the explanation against curriculum materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use this skill to work through questions about the causes, processes, and effects of geographic phenomena. It prompts them to connect each step in a causal explanation and review incomplete answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese middle- and high-school geography curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records, sharing, and reminders may involve student data.

Mitigation: Keep memory disabled unless the learner wants records retained; require consent before enabling records or sharing, and review companion skills and platform privacy controls before enabling sharing or reminders.
