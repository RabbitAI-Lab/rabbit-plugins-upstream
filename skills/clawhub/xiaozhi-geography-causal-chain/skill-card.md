## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary students to build step-by-step causal explanations for geography phenomena and exam questions rather than supplying a finished answer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to practice explaining the causes, processes, and effects of middle- and high-school geography topics through guided questions. Optional learning-profile features require consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China curriculum context; localize before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and emergency contacts may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language, consent rules, and crisis contacts before serving other regions.

Risk: Student learning records, parent sharing, or reminders could disclose a minor's information without consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory off by default and enable each feature only with student and required guardian consent; confirm individual writebacks.

Risk: A student may disclose self-harm, abuse, or immediate danger during tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring, refer the student to a trusted adult, and offer locally appropriate emergency resources.
