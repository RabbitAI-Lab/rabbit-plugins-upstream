## Description:

A Chinese-language geography tutor that helps middle and high school students connect the causes, intermediate steps, and effects behind natural and human geography phenomena.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school students use the tutor to build complete causal explanations for geography questions, one step at a time. It adapts explanations to the stated grade band and points students to related study support when a question needs a different skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language instruction; exam-specific guidance should follow local requirements)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records, sharing, and reminders may involve student information.

Mitigation: Keep memory off for current-conversation tutoring; enable these features only when wanted, review companion integrations, and follow the skill's consent and sharing controls.
