## Description:

Guides students to connect the causes of geography phenomena step by step and practice answering causal questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use this skill to understand natural and human geography processes, build complete cause-and-effect chains, and improve answers to causal questions. It guides learners with stepwise prompts so they can form and check the chain themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); the skill is designed for Chinese K–12 geography curricula and asks for local context where guidance depends on region.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A causal explanation may be incomplete or differ from the learner’s textbook or local exam guidance.

Mitigation: Use the prompts as learning support, check subject depth against the learner’s course and local exam guidance, and have the student build the chain step by step.

Risk: Optional learner profiles and cross-skill sharing involve personal learning information.

Mitigation: Keep the default session-only approach; read or share profile information only with the required explicit consent, confirm each profile write, and honor pause and deletion controls.

Risk: A learner may disclose an urgent safety or wellbeing concern during tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop the lesson flow, state the AI’s limits, encourage contact with a trusted adult, and provide region-appropriate emergency or professional support without recording sensitive incident details.
