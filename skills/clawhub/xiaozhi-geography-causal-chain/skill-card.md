## Description:

Coaches Chinese middle- and high-school students to explain geographic phenomena by connecting causes, intermediate processes, and outcomes step by step.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to practice explaining geographic causes and processes across middle- and high-school topics without receiving a ready-made answer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese K12 geography curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student progress, wrong-answer categories, or reminders could be retained or shared without the student's or guardian's comfort.

Mitigation: Keep profile memory and cross-skill sharing off for session-only tutoring; confirm consent before enabling storage, sharing, or reminders.
