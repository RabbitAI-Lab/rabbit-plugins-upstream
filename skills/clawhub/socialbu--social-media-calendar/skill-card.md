## Description:

Build a social media content calendar from a brief, website, campaign, or existing content. Use for weekly or monthly editorial plans, platform-specific drafts, content repurposing, and posting schedules. Works without an account; optionally schedule approved posts through SocialBu.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[socialbu](https://clawhub.ai/user/socialbu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media managers and business teams use this skill to turn briefs, campaigns, websites, or existing content into dated, platform-specific post drafts. They can optionally schedule approved posts through SocialBu after authorizing an account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scheduling sends requested content and media to SocialBu and may lead to public posts; OAuth permissions and service costs also apply.

Mitigation: Review SocialBu permissions and costs before connecting, and confirm the intended accounts, copy, media, and times before scheduling.
