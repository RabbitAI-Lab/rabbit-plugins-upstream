## Description:

Plans a four-week LinkedIn calendar using account settings and recent content, then prepares posts for review before scheduling approved drafts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

LinkedIn creators and professionals use this skill to plan a month of posts around their content pillars, cadence, audience, and milestones. It creates complete drafts after the calendar is confirmed and schedules each post only after the user reviews and approves it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting Taplio gives the assistant access to account profile information and unpublished drafts.

Mitigation: Run the skill only for an explicit LinkedIn planning request and review the proposed calendar before draft creation or revision.

Risk: Scheduling an approved draft leads to automatic publication at the selected time.

Mitigation: Review each complete post and its scheduled time before approving it for scheduling.
