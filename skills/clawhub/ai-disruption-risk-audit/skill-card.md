## Description:

Assesses how AI may disrupt or reinforce business activities by examining substitutability, cost changes, customer bypass, and where competitive advantage may move.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders, founders, and strategy teams use the skill to identify which revenue-generating activities face AI substitution or customer bypass, where advantage may migrate, and whether to hold, attack, or retreat. It produces an activity-level impact assessment and action table grounded in task tests and cost evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsupported or outdated scores could lead to inaccurate business recommendations.

Mitigation: Test AI on real tasks, support cost-collapse scores with current price quotes, mark unsupported scores unverified, and validate recommendations against current task tests before making business changes.
