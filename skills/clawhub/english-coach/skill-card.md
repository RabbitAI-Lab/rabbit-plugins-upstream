## Description:

English Coach helps users practice conversational English and review English materials through chunk-based coaching, ZPD-focused feedback, and structured session summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kennethg128](https://clawhub.ai/user/kennethg128)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners use this skill to practice English conversation, submit English materials for chunk-based review, and receive post-session summaries with corrections and next learning targets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Practice text, corrections, progress, and profile notes may be retained in local state files.

Mitigation: Avoid highly sensitive personal chats or documents, and periodically review or clear the state files when a fresh start is needed.
