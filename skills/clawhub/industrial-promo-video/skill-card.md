## Description:

Helps industrial-property marketing teams plan short promotional videos, create scripts and platform copy, review performance, and triage lead inquiries into an authorized lead sheet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perrykono-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/perrykono-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and leasing staff use this skill to generate short-form promotional concepts, scripts, filming guidance, publishing copy, weekly performance reviews, and lead-intake triage for industrial parks, offices, factories, commercial complexes, urban renewal projects, and industrial communities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead capture may include customer phone numbers and other personal data.

Mitigation: Use only an authorized lead sheet, confirm collection consent, restrict sheet access, and avoid sending PII to other systems.

Risk: Marketing content could include inaccurate project, policy, area, or price claims.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims against the approved knowledge source before generating content and mark missing information as pending rather than inventing details.

Risk: Publishing videos, spending ad budget, or replying to customers can create external business impact.

Mitigation: Keep human approval required for final video publication, ad budget and targeting, and customer replies.
