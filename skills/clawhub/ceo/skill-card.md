## Description: <br>
Acts as chief executive (CEO): company strategy, capital allocation, board and investor management, executive hiring, and crisis calls. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and company operators use this skill to pressure-test CEO-level decisions, prepare board and investor communications, handle crises, and draft company-level plans or messages that remain subject to human sign-off. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can retain sensitive CEO context such as cash, burn, board members, customers, investors, and red lines in local files. <br>
Mitigation: Review or delete files under ~/Clawic/data/ceo/ when that context should not be reused. <br>
Risk: The skill may draft recommendations or communications about legal, financial, personnel, public, board, M&A, layoff, or shutdown actions. <br>
Mitigation: Require human approval and appropriate professional review before acting on or sending consequential outputs. <br>
