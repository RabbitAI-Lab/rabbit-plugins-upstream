## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language 408 postgraduate entrance exam tutoring skill that combines Feynman-style questioning, Ebbinghaus review scheduling, knowledge-base search, exam analysis, structured summaries, and personalized review reminders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External learners preparing for China's 408 computer science postgraduate entrance exam use this skill for guided Q&A, knowledge-point lookup, past-exam practice, weak-point diagnosis, and spaced review planning. The skill is intended for ongoing tutoring workflows that maintain study summaries and review reminders. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill maintains ongoing study summaries, weak-point tracking, and review reminders, which can include personal study progress. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive personal information during tutoring and disable or avoid the skill when only one-off, non-persistent Q&A is needed. <br>
Risk: Tutoring answers and exam-frequency guidance depend on the available 408 study knowledge base and stated source citations. <br>
Mitigation: Review cited courseware, textbook, or past-exam sources before relying on an answer, and treat uncertain frequency statistics as estimates. <br>
