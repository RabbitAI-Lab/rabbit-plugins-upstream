## Description:

Turns product descriptions, manuals, catalogs, and ecommerce listings into shopping-video concepts with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants, marketers, and their agents use this skill to create product ads and cross-border shopping videos from product details or listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product details, prompts, and attached files may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only materials permitted by your data-handling requirements; avoid confidential product documents unless approved.

Risk: The CLI may store an API key locally, and global installation leaves the CLI installed.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and use the pinned npx invocation if you do not want a persistent global install.
