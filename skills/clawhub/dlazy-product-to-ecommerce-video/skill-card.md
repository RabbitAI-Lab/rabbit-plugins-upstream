## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or ecommerce listings into shopping videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and marketing teams use this skill to create product ads and cross-border ecommerce videos from product materials or listing links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached product files may be sent to the dLazy hosted service.

Mitigation: Send only product materials approved for upload and review the service terms before use.

Risk: Login may store a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and revoke or rotate the key if it is exposed.
