## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or ecommerce listings into conversion-focused shopping videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn product information or marketplace listings into shopping videos for product advertising and cross-border sales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and options are sent to dLazy services, attached files are uploaded, and the skill can list projects in the organization.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive information, attach only approved files, and use an account with access limited to the projects needed.

Risk: The workflow depends on the third-party @dlazy/cli npm package.

Mitigation: Review the package and install specification before use; the documented install pins version 1.2.3.
