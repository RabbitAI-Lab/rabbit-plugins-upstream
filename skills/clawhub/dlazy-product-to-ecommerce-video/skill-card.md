## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or Amazon, Shopify, eBay, and Temu listings into conversion-focused ecommerce videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use the skill to create product advertising videos from product materials or marketplace listings, with optional multilingual voiceover and a virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product materials and attached files are sent to dLazy for processing.

Mitigation: Share only product materials you are comfortable sharing with dLazy.

Risk: Continuing in the wrong project may use an unintended saved session.

Mitigation: Check the project ID before resuming work.

Risk: Video generation may consume service credits.

Mitigation: Consider credit costs before starting generation.

Risk: Authentication can save an organization API key in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and rotate or revoke the key if it may be exposed.

Risk: The skill relies on a pinned CLI package release.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI dependency before installation.
