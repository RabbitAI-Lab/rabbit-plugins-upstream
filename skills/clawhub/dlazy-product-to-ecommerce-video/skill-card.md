## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or marketplace listings into ecommerce videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and ecommerce marketers can turn product information or marketplace listings into shopping videos for product advertising and cross-border sales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
