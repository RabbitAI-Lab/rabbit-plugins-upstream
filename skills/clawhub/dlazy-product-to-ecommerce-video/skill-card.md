## Description:

Turns product documents or online listings into ecommerce video projects with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketing teams use this skill to create product ads and cross-border shopping videos from specifications, catalogs, or marketplace listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to the third-party dLazy service.

Mitigation: Only submit prompts and files approved for upload to dLazy.

Risk: Authentication stores an API key locally, and using the external CLI introduces third-party software.

Mitigation: Protect and rotate the key as needed; review the CLI before installing, or use the on-demand npx option instead of a global install.
