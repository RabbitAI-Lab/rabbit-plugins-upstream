## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or marketplace listings into conversion-focused ecommerce videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and ecommerce teams use this skill to create product advertising videos from product information or marketplace listings, including cross-border and social commerce campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected attachments are sent to dLazy services to create videos.

Mitigation: Use only with prompts and attachments you are comfortable sharing with dLazy, and review files before attaching them.

Risk: The CLI stores an organization API key and video generation may use credits.

Mitigation: Protect the stored API key, use the intended organization and project, and monitor credit usage.
