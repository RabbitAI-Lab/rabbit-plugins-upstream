## Description:

Helps sellers turn product specifications, catalogs, or marketplace listings into shopping-video projects with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketers use this skill to plan and generate ecommerce product videos from product materials or marketplace listings for ads and cross-border sales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to dLazy's cloud service.

Mitigation: Share only materials you are comfortable sending to dLazy; review attachments before uploading.

Risk: The CLI may save an API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key from the dLazy dashboard when needed.

Risk: A global CLI installation persists on the device.

Mitigation: Use the pinned npx invocation instead if you prefer not to install the CLI globally.
