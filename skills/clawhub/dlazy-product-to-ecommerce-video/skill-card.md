## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or marketplace listings into conversion-focused ecommerce videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and ecommerce marketers use this skill to create product advertising and cross-border shopping videos from product materials or marketplace listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Files attached for video generation are uploaded to dLazy storage.

Mitigation: Only attach files you are comfortable sending to the service.

Risk: The organization API key is stored in the local CLI configuration, and conversations retain project context.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration, confirm the intended organization and project before use, and rotate or revoke the key when needed.

Risk: Video generation may incur service costs.

Mitigation: Check the organization's available credits and expected costs before starting generation.
