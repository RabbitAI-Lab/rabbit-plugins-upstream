## Description:

Turn a product spec, manual, catalog, or Amazon, Shopify, eBay, or Temu listing into a conversion-focused shopping video with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and ecommerce teams use this skill to turn product information or marketplace listings into shopping videos for product ads and cross-border sales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports organization-project listing outside the stated video-generation purpose.

Mitigation: Confirm that listing organization projects is expected and within the intended account scope before use.

Risk: The review could not verify some install and local references; prompts and attached files use hosted API and media-storage flows.

Mitigation: Review the CLI version and API and file-upload flows before sending sensitive product material.
