## Description:

Answers Chinese pharmaceutical research questions by searching and summarizing patents, literature, database records, clinical trials, and licensing deal information for drugs, targets, and diseases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External life-science researchers, analysts, and developers use this skill to investigate drugs, targets, indications, competition, safety signals, clinical evidence, and business-development deals through configured PatSnap Life Science MCP services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses external PatSnap MCP services and may send API keys and pharmaceutical research queries to those services.

Mitigation: Install only if you trust PatSnap with the API key and query content; use approved keys and avoid sending sensitive research queries unless authorized.

Risk: Pharmaceutical research outputs could be mistaken for medical, dosing, safety, or patient-care advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research intelligence and review safety, dosing, and patient-care decisions with qualified medical or regulatory sources before use.
