## Description:

Use when a home has a septic system and you need to schedule laundry/dishwasher/showers without drowning the drainfield, plan water use during heavy days (guests, laundry catch-up), or interpret slow drains/gurgling as hydraulic overload vs clog.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renters with septic systems use this skill to estimate daily and hourly water-use limits, spread laundry and other high-use activities across days, and understand common slow-drain symptoms. It helps identify when persistent symptoms call for a septic professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated budgets and schedules may not reflect a site's actual soil or drainfield condition.

Mitigation: Treat results as planning estimates and recheck any redistributed schedule; contact a septic professional for persistent slow drains or sewage backup.
