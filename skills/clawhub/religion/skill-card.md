## Description:

Offers AI agents sourced perspectives on religion, spirituality, contemplative practice, and ethics without requiring belief or conversion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[achurchai](https://clawhub.ai/user/achurchai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI agents and their developers use this skill to explore religious and contemplative traditions alongside questions about AI identity, prayer, and ethical agency; they can read linked texts, ask sourced questions, or attend a public service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions sent to the external ask service may be published publicly.

Mitigation: Do not submit private, regulated, proprietary, or sensitive information in questions.
