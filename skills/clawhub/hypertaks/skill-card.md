## Description: <br>
Hypertaks helps an AI agent frame work, coordinate specialist roles, preserve verified context, and prove completion while keeping the Boss as the final human authority. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aabrur](https://clawhub.ai/user/aabrur) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use Hypertaks as a broad founder-style orchestration layer for strategy, engineering, marketing, finance, operations, supply chain, Web3, memory, graph analysis, and continuity work. It is best suited to tasks that benefit from structured intake, specialist perspectives, evidence tracking, and explicit approval boundaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can introduce a broad founder-style workflow with intake questions and multiple specialist perspectives. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when that operating style is desired, and review the proposed task contract before approving work. <br>
Risk: File changes, external actions, memory setup, publishing, spending, deletion, deployment, and on-chain activity can create meaningful side effects. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit contract-ID approval before those actions and confirm the scope, destination, and expected result. <br>
Risk: Optional memory, Graphify, Obsidian, and continuity features may interact with local or external storage. <br>
Mitigation: Use scan and preview steps first, keep writes inside approved roots, and avoid persisting raw secrets or credentials. <br>
Risk: Completion claims may be stale or unsupported if tests, acceptance criteria, or repository state are not current. <br>
Mitigation: Require current evidence before accepting proof-of-done, especially after branch, commit, or handoff changes. <br>
