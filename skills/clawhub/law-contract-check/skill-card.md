## Description:

Reviews supplied contract terms for common risks and suggests plain-language explanations, possible revisions, missing clauses, and pre-signing checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals, small-business operators, and legal or compliance practitioners can review supplied lease, service, purchase, employment, or partnership terms before signing. They receive a prioritized risk checklist and suggested wording to discuss with the other party.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contract text may contain sensitive personal or business information.

Mitigation: Share only necessary excerpts and redact sensitive details before providing them.

Risk: Suggested contract changes may be incomplete or mistaken and are not formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a review checklist; seek a qualified lawyer for high-value, disputed, or regulated matters.
