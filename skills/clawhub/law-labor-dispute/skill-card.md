## Description:

Helps users organize labor-dispute facts, evidence, deadlines, resolution options, and employer communications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Workers and legal or compliance practitioners use this skill to prepare an initial self-check for dismissal, unpaid wages, overtime, social insurance, workplace injury, or missing employment contracts. It organizes the timeline and available evidence and suggests questions and resolution routes to verify locally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A mistaken deadline or unsuitable dispute route could delay action.

Mitigation: Confirm deadlines and procedural requirements with official local sources or a qualified professional before acting.

Risk: Employment records may contain sensitive personal details.

Mitigation: Share only necessary facts and redact personal identifiers from documents and generated outputs.

Risk: Complex disputes may not be adequately addressed by a general self-check.

Mitigation: Seek advice from a labor arbitration body or qualified lawyer for workplace injury, non-compete, equity, or other complex matters.
