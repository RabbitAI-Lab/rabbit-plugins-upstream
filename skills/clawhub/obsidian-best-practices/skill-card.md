### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recommendations may include noisy copied web content or links that have changed since the references were collected. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important links and current Obsidian documentation before relying on time-sensitive guidance. <br>
Risk: The reference material includes Obsidian Headless sync commands and modes that can revert local vault changes. <br>
Mitigation: Review commands before running them, back up vaults, and avoid destructive sync modes unless explicitly intended. <br>
