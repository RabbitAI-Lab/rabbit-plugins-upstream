## Description:

Guides the alignment of offline knowledge bases with online verification through gap reviews, source checks, updates, and offline question-answer testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

All rights reserved

## Use Case:

Knowledge-base maintainers and agents use this methodology to identify gaps in local reference collections, verify changing facts against online sources, record updates, and test offline answers before relying on them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad knowledge-related prompts may activate the skill unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Confirm that offline knowledge-base alignment is the intended task before following its workflow.

Risk: Unreviewed online findings or local edits may introduce incorrect regulatory or medical references.

Mitigation: Check cited primary sources and have a qualified reviewer approve proposed knowledge-base changes before use.
