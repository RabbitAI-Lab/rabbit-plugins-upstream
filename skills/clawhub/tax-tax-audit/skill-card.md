## Description: <br>
Tax audit and fiscal compliance assistant for financial statement tax procedures, internal control testing, tax fraud indicators, tax risk disclosure, and self-check reporting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zxj2devs](https://clawhub.ai/user/zxj2devs) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, auditors, and finance or tax compliance teams use this skill to structure Chinese tax audit procedures, identify tax compliance control gaps and fraud red flags, and draft tax risk disclosure or self-check guidance. It provides reference guidance only and does not replace licensed tax, audit, or legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, scenarios, metrics, and registration identifiers may be sent to external cloud services. <br>
Mitigation: Use only after the organization approves the endpoint and data handling; avoid confidential client tax records unless that approval is in place. <br>
Risk: API credentials and logs may be stored locally. <br>
Mitigation: Review local storage locations, file permissions, and credential handling before installing or enabling the skill. <br>
Risk: MCP configuration entries may be added when setup is enabled. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed configuration changes and keep setup in dry-run/default mode unless explicit installation changes are intended. <br>
Risk: Tax audit guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific matter. <br>
Mitigation: Verify policy conclusions against official sources and consult qualified tax, audit, or legal professionals for material decisions. <br>
