## Description:

Calculate solar shadow azimuth and latitude geometry, query regional driving sides, road markings, calling codes and electrical grid standards, and inspect EXIF GPS metadata tag definitions via LocatePic MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[locatepic](https://clawhub.ai/user/locatepic)

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and people working with visual geolocation clues can use LocatePic MCP to calculate solar-shadow geometry and look up regional driving, road-marking, phone-code, and electrical-grid information. They can also consult EXIF GPS tag definitions and related reference pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
