## Description: <br>
Acts as a chief technology officer: architecture calls, build vs buy, hiring and team scaling, tech debt, engineering metrics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders, CTOs, engineering leaders, and advisors use this skill to make or review technical strategy decisions, including architecture choices, build-vs-buy calls, hiring plans, team scaling, debt prioritization, operations, and stakeholder translation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can influence important business and engineering decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as decision support and keep final approval with an accountable human owner, especially for one-way technical decisions, senior people decisions, security incidents, and vendor commitments. <br>
Risk: The skill may store CTO-related preferences and stack notes locally under ~/Clawic/data/cto/. <br>
Mitigation: Review local configuration contents for sensitive business or stack information before sharing machines, backups, or logs. <br>
