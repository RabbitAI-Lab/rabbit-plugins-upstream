## Description:

Dueback: outcome-based bill negotiation. Detect negotiable bills, fees, and purchases; Dueback's backend negotiates and verifies savings against bank data. Free to try — pay only on verified savings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to identify potentially negotiable bills, fees, subscriptions, and purchases, review Dueback's outcome-based pricing, and—after explicit consent—request negotiation and track verified results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing bill details and linking a bank account exposes sensitive financial information to Dueback.

Mitigation: Confirm the provider's identity, current fees, and bank-data permissions before sharing bill details or connecting a bank account; proceed only with explicit consent.
