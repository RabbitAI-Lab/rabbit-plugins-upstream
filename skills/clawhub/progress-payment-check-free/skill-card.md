## Description:

A deterministic local checker for progress payment worksheets that recalculates payment, retention, paid amount, totals, duplicates, and blank fields, with findings tied to source lines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenqg618](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenqg618)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Construction and engineering teams use this skill to check monthly progress payment worksheets against stated arithmetic rules for progress payments, retention, paid amounts, column totals, duplicates, and blanks. It is intended for worksheet consistency review, not legal, valuation, or site-completion judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill directs users toward an unpinned external installer and purchase flow outside the reviewed release.

Mitigation: Use the free local checker without following upgrade instructions unless the external package, onboarding skill, merchant, and payment flow are separately trusted and verified.

Risk: Worksheet checks can be mistaken for broader construction, contract, legal, or valuation assurance.

Mitigation: Treat results as arithmetic and completeness checks only, and review contract terms, site completion, taxes, deductions, and legal issues separately.
