## Description: <br>
赛博销售 helps sales users analyze customer chats, screenshots, voice messages, and call transcripts to decode intent, identify buyer profiles, and draft follow-up or objection-handling responses. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales teams and individual sellers use this skill to interpret customer intent from conversations, classify buyer posture, detect vague commitments, and generate copy-ready Chinese sales replies and follow-up strategies. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Customer chats, screenshots, call transcripts, and voice-message content may contain private customer data, account details, pricing, contracts, or internal strategy. <br>
Mitigation: Redact sensitive personal, customer, commercial, and third-party information before use, and only process recordings or messages when you have the right to share them. <br>
