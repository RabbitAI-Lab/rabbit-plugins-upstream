## Description: <br>
Designs early-access launch programs with a waitlist-to-GA stage ladder, per-stage graduation criteria, cohort gating, tester recruitment, feedback-loop structure, and referral mechanics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Launch teams, founders, and product marketers use this skill to structure an early-access program from waitlist through GA. It helps define stage purpose, opt-in semantics, graduation criteria, invite throttling, feedback handling, and referral mechanics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Proposed registry events can encode incorrect stage dates, graduation criteria, or GA opt-out handling. <br>
Mitigation: Review each proposed launch registry event before accepting it, with particular attention to stage dates, graduation criteria, and opt-out handling for GA rollout. <br>
Risk: Launch-planning memory could preserve premature or inaccurate program decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Save launch-planning memory only after explicit user confirmation and after reviewing the reusable summary. <br>
