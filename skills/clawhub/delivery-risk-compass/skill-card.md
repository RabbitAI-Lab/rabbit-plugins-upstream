## Description: <br>
Project delivery risk compass that uses industry, delivery phase, project shape, and risk signals to produce delivery health, industry risks, blockers, communication strategy, and recovery actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[william202404](https://clawhub.ai/user/william202404) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Project managers, delivery leads, and implementation engineers use this skill to assess enterprise project delivery risk before kickoff, during requirements and implementation, before go-live, or when acceptance and delay issues need recovery actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill produces advisory project delivery guidance that may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific client situation. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations with qualified delivery, technical, and business stakeholders before acting on them. <br>
Risk: Project descriptions may include confidential client, vendor, personal, or sensitive delivery details. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized project aliases and avoid entering sensitive details unless they are appropriate for the agent context. <br>
