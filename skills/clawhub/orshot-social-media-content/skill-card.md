## Description:

Creates reusable, on-brand social media post templates and renders them with fresh copy for major platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Brands and social media teams use the skill to design repeatable post templates and produce on-brand graphics for announcements, events, quotes, menus, and other recurring posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Briefs, copy, and images are processed by Orshot.

Mitigation: Share only authorized material and connect the intended workspace.

Risk: Rendering consumes Orshot credits.

Mitigation: Check expected credit use before producing a large batch.
