## Description:

Social media content generator and social media design for AI agents. Create on-brand Instagram post, LinkedIn post, TikTok, Reels, story, ad, banner, thumbnail and carousel templates with Orshot, then render them with new copy on every post. Use for content creation for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube and Pinterest, for a content creator or marketing team that wants designed templates instead of one-off AI images, as a Canva alternative an agent can drive, and when refining a template that looks generic. Applies the craft rules (clear hierarchy, 2-font typography, 60-30-10 color, restrained motion), consults the brand kit first, varies layouts so designs never repeat, and runs a render-and-critique loop over the hosted Orshot MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Text and design assets are processed by Orshot, and edits can change saved templates.

Mitigation: Use material authorized for sharing with Orshot, and review the workspace access requested during OAuth.
