## Description: <br>
Describe your concept and discover what makes it distinctive - structured analysis for patent consultation, not legal advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, inventors, and product teams use this skill to structure natural-language concept descriptions into distinctive technical patterns, scoring, claim-framing prompts, and next steps for attorney consultation. The skill supports patent-preparation discussion but does not provide legal advice or patentability determinations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat structured concept analysis as legal advice or a patentability determination. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the skill's disclaimer visible in outputs and require consultation with a registered patent attorney for intellectual-property decisions. <br>
Risk: Invention descriptions can contain commercially sensitive information that may be stored in chat history or logs. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering proprietary information in shared or logged environments and review outputs before sharing. <br>
Risk: Analysis may identify distinctive patterns without checking existing implementations. <br>
Mitigation: Use the recommended validation workflow and professional review before relying on findings. <br>
