## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat decorating guidance as professional construction, engineering, legal, financial, or code-compliance advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as advisory only and direct users to verify demolition, balcony, waterproofing, structural, code, and property-buying decisions with local qualified professionals.

Risk: Renovation recommendations can be wrong for a specific home because layouts, budgets, occupants, products, and local rules vary.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's layout, budget, household needs, and location before giving recommendations, and keep final decisions tied to local measurements and professional review.

Risk: Material, environmental, and product-quality claims may vary by supplier, region, and installation quality.

Mitigation: Encourage users to confirm product specifications, certifications, installation requirements, and warranty terms before purchase or construction.
