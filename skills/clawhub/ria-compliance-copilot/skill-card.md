## Description:

Provides compliance risk scoring and review assistance for RIA marketing materials, client communications, and Form ADV filings per SEC and FINRA rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

RIA compliance staff, chief compliance officers, marketing teams, and compliance consultants use this skill to review advertising, client communications, social posts, SEC exam preparation materials, and Form ADV language before human compliance and legal approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake compliance drafting and review support for legal advice or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.

Mitigation: Require every output to state that it is educational review assistance only and must be approved by the firm's CCO and retained compliance counsel before publication, filing, or reliance.

Risk: SEC, FINRA, and state-specific requirements may change or depend on facts not supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Instruct users to verify current requirements with qualified compliance and legal reviewers, including state regulator requirements for state-registered RIAs.

Risk: During an active examination, editing or deleting historical materials could create records-retention or spoliation concerns.

Mitigation: Advise users to preserve historical materials, document issues, and route remediation plans through the CCO and counsel.
