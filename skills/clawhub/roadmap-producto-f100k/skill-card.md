## Description:

Builds a Backward Design roadmap for a course, community, service, or physical product by mapping the customer journey from the current state to a measurable promised outcome.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, operators, and product teams use this skill to turn a product promise into a structured customer roadmap and creator production plan. It supports digital courses, Skool-style communities, consulting or coaching services, and physical products.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can produce sales-offer strategy, pricing, projections, and pitch materials beyond a basic roadmap.

Mitigation: Review business claims, pricing, projections, and pitch content before relying on or sharing generated outputs.

Risk: Generated files and chat summaries may expose client names, private metrics, revenue figures, or business plans when shared externally.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive business information before sending files through Telegram, posting in a community, or sharing pitch material.

Risk: Persistent copies in a second-brain folder may retain outdated or sensitive product strategy.

Mitigation: Limit installation to workflows that need persistent roadmap archives and periodically review stored copies for accuracy and confidentiality.
