## Description:

Design Deep Research helps graphic and visual designers collect public design references across platforms, cluster aesthetic trends, and produce visual HTML research reports with reusable exploratory prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fize](https://clawhub.ai/user/fize)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Graphic and visual designers use this skill to research design directions, analyze aesthetic trends, gather inspiration moodboards, and turn verified public references into clustered design guidance and exploratory prompt seeds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill searches third-party design sites and may expose confidential campaign details, private reference URLs, or unreleased brand information if those are included in the research brief.

Mitigation: Use public or non-sensitive briefs and reference URLs unless the user is comfortable using the information for web research.

Risk: Research outputs can include incorrect, broken, or fabricated source links if reference collection is not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review harvested references and enforce the skill's requirement that every image URL and project source link be authentic and accessible.
