# Design Research Platform Guide (设计调研平台指南)

A curated catalog of core platforms for harvesting graphic design assets, creative trends, mockups, and typography references.

---

## 1. Global Creative Inspiration & Trend Portals

### Behance (`behance.net`)
- **Strengths**: Flagship global design network by Adobe. World-class source for comprehensive brand identity systems, packaging suites, and cutting-edge editorial layouts.
- **Search Strategy**: English queries; filter by "Case Studies" or "Curated Galleries". Formula: `[Design Type] + [Style/Aesthetic] + [Sector]`.
- **Asset Type**: Complete end-to-end case studies with design rationale and contextual applications.

### Pinterest (`pinterest.com`)
- **Strengths**: Industry benchmark recommendation algorithm. Clicking a single visual anchor cascades into hyper-targeted boards of color palettes, Swiss layouts, and typographic styles. Essential for Moodboards.
- **Search Strategy**: English keywords. Find a high-signal pin, then explore "More like this".
- **Asset Type**: Single image inspiration, curated user moodboards.

### Dribbble (`dribbble.com`)
- **Strengths**: Product, UI/UX, 3D motion, and vector illustrations. Exceptional for experimental color palettes and micro-interactions.
- **Search Strategy**: English queries; filter by "Popular" or "Goods".
- **Asset Type**: Conceptual shots, typographic vignettes, vector graphics.

---

## 2. High-Fidelity Mockups & 3D Assets

### Mockup World (`mockupworld.co`)
- **Strengths**: Global hub for free photorealistic PSD mockups (packaging boxes, stationery, billboard OOH, paper bags, editorial books).
- **Search Strategy**: Filter by product packaging category.
- **Asset Type**: Layered PSD files with smart objects.

### Graphic Burger (`graphicburger.com`)
- **Strengths**: Hand-curated textures and mockups with tactile paper grain, foil stamping, and debossing depth.
- **Asset Type**: Mockups, surface textures, display fonts.

### Yellow Images (`yellowimages.com`)
- **Strengths**: Industry gold standard for 3D packaging mockups (bottles, tubes, pouches, shrink wraps). Rotatable angles with realistic material specularity.
- **Asset Type**: 3D Smart Object mockups (commercial/paid).

---

## 3. Vectors, Illustrations & Graphic Elements

### Freepik (`freepik.com`)
- **Strengths**: Massive repository of vector graphics (AI/EPS), ornamental frames, geometric badges, and poster layout templates.
- **Search Strategy**: Filter by Vector / Free with commercial license filters.

### Vecteezy (`vecteezy.com`)
- **Strengths**: Specialized vector graphics, decorative borders, icons, and geometric patterns.

---

## 4. Royalty-Free Photographic Imagery

### Unsplash (`unsplash.com`)
- **Strengths**: High aesthetic caliber editorial photography. Authentic cinematic lighting, natural human emotion, and documentary realism.
- **Search Strategy**: Mood-based English keywords (`moody cinema`, `analog film grain`, `warm editorial`).
- **License**: Unsplash Free License / Commercial safe.

### Pexels (`pexels.com`)
- **Strengths**: Broad multi-language search support, 4K b-roll video footage, and diverse lifestyle imagery.

---

## 5. Regional & Asian Design Platforms (亚太与国内特色平台)

### 站酷 · Zcool (`zcool.com.cn`)
- **特点**: 国内顶尖设计师综合交流社区，覆盖品牌全案、C4D三维渲染、插画、电商设计全品类。
- **检索策略**: 中文关键词（如“新中式包装”、“国潮海报”、“品牌全案”）。
- **素材类型**: 商业落地全案、高水准视觉概念。

### 花瓣网 · Huaban (`huaban.com`)
- **特点**: 国内最大的瀑布流灵感采集库，国内设计师做情绪板（Moodboard）的首选工具。
- **检索策略**: 中文搜索，顺着优秀画板探索推荐流。

### 古田路9号 (`gtn9.com`)
- **特点**: 专注品牌设计、包装结构、商业VI视觉的垂直行业门户。研究国内消费品与快消落地商业案例的最佳平台。
- **检索策略**: 聚焦商业落地案例与包装延展。

### 大作网 (`bigbigwork.com`)
- **特点**: 聚合全球几十家设计社区与图库的元搜索引擎，支持跨平台一键灵感检索。

### 优设网 · UISDC (`uisdc.com`)
- **特点**: 平面与UI设计资源站，提供设计趋势报告、中文排版教程及免费商用字体索引。

## Platform Recommendation Matrix

|---|---|
| **Brand Identity / VI** | Behance + Pinterest + 古田路9号 |
| **Packaging & 3D** | Yellow Images + Mockup World + Behance |
| **Poster & Typography** | Pinterest + Dribbble + Unsplash |

---

## Excluded Sources (Quality Guardrails)

- **Do NOT harvest from generic search engine image scrapers** (Baidu Images, Google generic search thumbnails) due to low resolution, watermarks, and unclear copyright.
- **Do NOT scrape walled-garden social feeds** (WeChat moments, non-indexed feeds) where source attribution cannot be verified.
