## Description: <br>
Elite PPT Pro guides agents to create consulting-style, data-driven presentations using structured research requirements, curated slide layouts, restrained visual themes, source citations, and dual PPTX plus HTML preview outputs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[forrestneo](https://clawhub.ai/user/forrestneo) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and presentation-building agents use this skill to produce consulting decks, strategy reports, investor presentations, market analysis, annual reviews, and business framework slides. It is best suited for workflows that need research-first content, cited data points, and polished PPTX or HTML deliverables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated presentations may include incorrect, stale, or weakly sourced data from external research. <br>
Mitigation: Review source citations, dates, and calculations before using the deck for business decisions. <br>
Risk: The workflow may create local PPTX and HTML deliverables containing confidential business information. <br>
Mitigation: Inspect generated files and avoid sharing decks or previews until confidential content has been approved for release. <br>
