Good questions

Before you send your first card.

The five things people ask before they download. The help center has the rest, and a real person if you get stuck.

Visit the help center
Is Wishwell free?

Yes. Sending cards, scheduling and group gifts are free forever. Wishwell Plus adds video cards, unlimited photo pages and reminders for $3 a month.

Does the person I send to need the app?

No. Cards open in any browser from a link or a text. If they do have the app, the card lands in their inbox with a little confetti.

Where can Wishwell Cash be spent?

At every shop in the gift catalogue, in 48 countries, or withdrawn to a bank account. It never expires and there are no fees on either side.

What if I get the date wrong?

Edit or cancel a scheduled card any time before it goes out, from your inbox. Sent cards can be followed with a second one; we do not judge.

Can I get a refund on a gift card?

Unclaimed gift cards can be refunded in full for 30 days. Once claimed, the value belongs to the recipient, who can swap it for a different shop.

Common questions about the flow

Does the recipient need Wishwell installed?
No. Every card opens in a browser from the link we send. Recipients only need the app if they want to keep a wallet, swap gifts or send cards back.
Can I send to someone in another country?
Cards, yes, anywhere. Gift cards depend on the shop; each card page lists where it can be redeemed. Wishwell Cash works in every country where the shop is available.
What happens if I schedule a card and change my mind?
Open it from your inbox and edit, reschedule or cancel it, right up until the minute it sends. Attached gifts are only charged when the card actually goes out.

Are there fees on gift cards?
No. You pay the face value of the card. The shops pay us a small commission, which is how sending stays free.
Can I cancel Plus any time?
Yes. Cancel from your account page and you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. Your cards, wallet and reminders stay.
Is there a student or nonprofit rate?
Plus is half price for students with a school email, and free for registered nonprofits sending thank-you cards to donors. Write to us from the help page.

Sending cards

How do I schedule a card for a future date?
On the final screen, tap the calendar next to Send and choose a date and hour. The card is delivered at that hour in the recipient's time zone. You can edit or cancel it from your inbox until it goes out.
Can several people sign one card?
Yes. Turn on Group card while editing and share the link. Friends sign and chip in from a browser without an account. Free accounts allow five signers; Plus and Family allow forty.
What formats can I add to a card?
Photos, text, drawings, stickers, a voice note up to twelve seconds, a song from the built-in library, and video on paid plans.

Gifts and wallet

How does Wishwell Cash work?
Cash is a balance in the recipient's wallet. They can spend it on any gift card in the shop, combine it with other gifts, or keep it. It never expires and there are no fees on the receiving side.
Can a recipient swap a gift card?
Yes. Any unredeemed gift card can be swapped for Wishwell Cash at full value from the wallet, then spent on something else.
Do you refund a gift I sent by mistake?
If the card has not been opened, cancel it from your inbox and the gift is refunded to your payment method within three business days. Opened gifts belong to the recipient.

Account and privacy

Do recipients see my phone number?
No. Cards are delivered from Wishwell with your display name. Your number and email are never shown unless you type them into the card.
How do I delete my account?
Open Settings, tap Account, then Delete account. Any wallet balance must be spent or refunded first. Cards you sent stay with the people who received them.
Where can I read the terms and privacy policy?
Both are on the legal page, written in plain language with a short summary at the top of each section.

Legal

Terms of use and privacy policy

Written in plain language. Each section starts with a short summary of what it means for you.

Terms of use

Summary: use Wishwell to send cards and gifts to people who want them; do not use it to spam, harass or resell gift cards.

1. Your account

You need to be 16 or older to create an account and 18 or older to hold a wallet balance. Keep your sign-in private; you are responsible for what is sent from your account.

2. Cards and gifts

You pay the face value of gift cards and any optional plan fee. Wishwell Cash is a stored balance operated by our licensed payments partner. Unopened cards can be cancelled and refunded; opened gifts belong to the recipient.

3. Content

You own what you put in your cards. You give us permission to store and deliver it. Do not upload anything you do not have the right to share, and do not send content that is unlawful or abusive. We remove such cards and may close the account.

4. Plans

Plus and Family renew monthly or yearly until cancelled. Cancel any time from your account page; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Privacy policy

Summary: we collect what is needed to deliver cards and nothing more. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

What we collect

  • Your name, email and phone number, to run your account and deliver cards.
  • Card content, stored so recipients can reopen cards later.
  • Dates you add for reminders. Contact import is optional and stays on your device unless you choose to sync it.
  • Payment details, handled by our payments partner; we never see full card numbers.

What we do not do

  • Sell or rent personal data.
  • Show advertising inside the app.
  • Share your contact list with recipients or shops.

Your choices

Turn off open receipts in Settings. Export every card you sent or received as a PDF. Delete your account and its content from Settings; wallet balances must be spent or refunded first.

Contact

Questions about these terms go to legal@wishwell.example or through the help center. Last updated 1 September 2026.
