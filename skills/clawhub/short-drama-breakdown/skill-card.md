## Description:

拆解对标短剧或漫剧成片，测量节奏与对白等特征，并将观察与项目计划或成片对照，形成差距与复核建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

外部创作者和短剧制作团队可用它分析经授权提供的对标视频、字幕和项目材料，比较集长、镜长、对白密度、开场及集尾，并把未预期的差距交给对应创作技能处理。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Measurement records can retain transcript text, and contact sheets contain video frames; exposing these files may disclose reference material.

Mitigation: Keep source media, transcripts, measurement records, and contact sheets in private folders, and do not publish them.

Risk: Generated files with matching names may overwrite existing files.

Mitigation: Choose and check output folders carefully before generating files.
