## Description:

测量并拆解对标短剧的节奏、对白、镜头、开场与集尾，并与项目计划或成片对照差距，按需还原剧本与分镜。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and story development teams use this skill to measure reference dramas, compare their pacing and storytelling with a project plan or finished episodes, and prepare evidence-based handoff notes. It can also reconstruct scripts and storyboards for analysis, subject to the source skill’s authorization boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Processing long or unusually shaped videos may use excessive memory.

Mitigation: Limit processing to shorter, standard-shaped clips and review memory use before processing long or unusually shaped videos.
