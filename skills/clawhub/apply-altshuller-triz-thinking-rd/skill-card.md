## Description:

Applies an Altshuller/TRIZ-informed framework to engineering and invention problems, including contradiction analysis, ideal final result framing, inventive-principle prompts, substance-field analysis, technology-evolution reasoning, invention-level reflection, and systematic alternatives to brainstorming.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and invention teams use this skill to structure technical problem solving with TRIZ concepts, formulate contradictions and ideal final results, generate mechanism-based alternatives, and plan validation steps.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TRIZ-guided outputs may be mistaken for validated engineering conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as structured prompts and require domain testing, engineering review, and safety review before implementation.

Risk: Generated concepts may overlook patent, manufacturability, reliability, or integration constraints.

Mitigation: Use the skill's validation and patent-search prompts before relying on any recommended concept.

Risk: Historical claims, quotations, and TRIZ source details may vary by source or edition.

Mitigation: Verify historical or bibliographic statements against credible primary or archival sources before publication.
