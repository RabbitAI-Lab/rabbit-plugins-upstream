This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and product teams use this skill to structure technical problem solving with TRIZ concepts, including contradiction analysis, ideal final result framing, resource analysis, inventive-principle prompts, and validation planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TRIZ-style outputs could be mistaken for authoritative engineering, safety, patent, or historical conclusions.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an analytical framework and validate proposed mechanisms, claims, and decisions with domain evidence, experiments, safety review, and patent analysis.
