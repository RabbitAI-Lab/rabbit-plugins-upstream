## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Gas appliances and lines, structural alterations, and concealed plumbing or waterproofing can create serious safety, structural, or leak hazards if handled as DIY work.

Mitigation: Use this guide to understand options and communicate with tradespeople only. Have qualified professionals assess and perform the work; obtain required gas-company, property, and structural approvals, preserve ventilation and service access, and document concealed work.
