External users use this skill to get practical renovation hardware buying guidance across floor drains, sinks, faucets, shower fixtures, cabinet hardware, door locks, hangers, budgeting, and common installation pitfalls. The skill is intended to help users compare options and ask better questions before purchasing or renovating, while deferring regulated or high-risk work to local professionals.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill bundles broad home-renovation advice beyond hardware purchasing, including structural demolition, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical, and second-hand property topics.

Mitigation: Use the guidance for planning and product-selection support only; require local code checks, building-management approval, and qualified structural, electrical, plumbing, or waterproofing professionals for high-risk work.

Risk: Under-qualified renovation advice could lead users to rely on the skill for safety-critical repairs or irreversible construction decisions.

Mitigation: Treat safety-critical recommendations as prompts for professional review, and verify site-specific conditions before demolition, utility changes, waterproofing work, or property-purchase decisions.
