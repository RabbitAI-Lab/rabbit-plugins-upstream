## Description: <br>
Provides tendering and bidding compliance guidance for the People's Republic of China tendering and bidding law framework, including legal Q&A, tender document review, bid compliance checks, rejection-risk identification, collusion-signal analysis, and objection or complaint guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External procurement, legal, compliance, and bid teams use this skill to analyze tendering and bidding questions, review tender or bid documents, identify rejection and violation risks, and prepare procedural guidance within the stated China tendering and bidding law scope. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat legal-adjacent compliance guidance as a lawyer's opinion or regulator decision. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as compliance analysis only and verify important conclusions against current law or professional counsel. <br>
Risk: Tender or bid documents may contain confidential procurement, commercial, or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only documents the user is authorized to share with the agent. <br>
Risk: The skill is scoped to tendering and bidding law and explicitly excludes government procurement goods and services scenarios. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the artifact's scope check first, refuse out-of-scope government procurement requests, and ask for project type, funding source, and purchasing entity when scope is unclear. <br>
