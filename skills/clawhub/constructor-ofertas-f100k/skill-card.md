## Description:

Builds Spanish-language marketing offers, lead magnets, and low-ticket infoproduct outlines using value-equation, M-A-G-I-C naming, bonus-stacking, and guarantee frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and business operators use this skill to structure compelling Spanish-language offers, lead magnets, low-ticket products, bonus stacks, guarantees, pricing rationale, and handoff-ready Markdown offer documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated offers may include incorrect claims, pricing logic, or business context if the user has not supplied complete and accurate inputs.

Mitigation: Require the skill's brief before generation and review all claims, prices, guarantees, proof points, and destination paths before publishing or saving outputs.

Risk: The skill may handle sensitive business context while preparing marketing offers.

Mitigation: Share only business information that is appropriate for the agent environment and confirm any copied summaries before adding them to persistent knowledge files.
