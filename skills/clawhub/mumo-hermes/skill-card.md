## Description:

Runs multi-model deliberations and returns model responses with typed cross-model reactions to support architecture, product, design, and plan decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ericatmumo](https://clawhub.ai/user/ericatmumo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use the skill to gather independent model perspectives, review plans and designs, and pressure-test high-impact decisions before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deliberation may send user-provided content to external model providers.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting secrets or confidential material unless you have reviewed the applicable data policies and are comfortable sharing it.
