## Description: <br>
Builds a joint customer success plan for a specific account with business goals, milestones, success metrics, ownership, risks, communications, and a 90-180 day roadmap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Customer success managers and account teams use this skill to draft a co-authorable success plan for onboarding, kickoff, QBR, renewal, or at-risk account conversations. The plan aligns customer outcomes, measurable success criteria, owners, milestones, mutual commitments, risks, communications, and escalation paths. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The plan may include sensitive customer names, stakeholder names, contract dates, renewal details, adoption risks, and account health information. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that users are allowed to provide this customer information to the agent, and minimize or redact sensitive details when a full account record is not required. <br>
Risk: Unconfirmed milestone dates, owners, commitments, or success metrics could misrepresent what the customer has agreed to. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated plan with the customer and account team before using it for kickoff, QBR, renewal, or escalation decisions. <br>
