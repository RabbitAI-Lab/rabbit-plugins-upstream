This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated articles may overstate expertise, fabricate or misrepresent citations, or include unsupported claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review and verify all claims, citations, credentials, statistics, and source attributions before publishing. <br>
Risk: Platform engagement tactics may conflict with a publisher's editorial standards or comfort level. <br>
Mitigation: Review calls to action, engagement language, and hot-list optimization choices before use. <br>
