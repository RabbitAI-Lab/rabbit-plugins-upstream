## Description: <br>
A solution architecture and presales workbench that helps solution architects, presales consultants, and enterprise architects draft discovery notes, proposals, architecture diagrams, ADRs, SOW annexes, RFP responses, bid materials, and related client-facing documents. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, solution architects, presales consultants, and enterprise architects use this skill to structure customer discovery, solution planning, architecture documentation, proposal responses, bid packages, and editable draw.io diagram source. It is intended for drafting and reference workflows where humans review the business, technical, and legal content before reuse. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated SOW, legal, commercial, or advisory language may be unsuitable for direct reuse. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated legal and commercial material with qualified counsel and responsible business owners before relying on it. <br>
Risk: The skill is used for presales and project documents that may involve confidential business content. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill intentionally, share only necessary context, and apply the user's confidentiality and data-handling policies. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording can make the skill relevant to many architecture or presales drafting tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the requested task and review the generated output for relevance, accuracy, and client-specific assumptions. <br>
