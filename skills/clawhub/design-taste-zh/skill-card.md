## Description:

Guides agents in designing, refining, and reviewing visual work, including interfaces, websites, images, and game art.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linkxzhou](https://clawhub.ai/user/linkxzhou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and developers use this skill to create and improve visual interfaces, establish design tokens, and critique websites, images, and game assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Website reviews open the supplied URL, load its resources, and may click common cookie-consent buttons.

Mitigation: Only submit URLs you intend the browser to visit, and review the target site before running a review.
