## Description:

Helps designers and developers review, create, and refine UI, websites, images, and game art with intentional visual direction and measurable design checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linkxzhou](https://clawhub.ai/user/linkxzhou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and developers use this skill to critique or build visible interfaces and visual assets, establish design tokens, and review user-provided websites and images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reviewing private or internal URLs can load pages and assets and save screenshots and metrics locally.

Mitigation: Only review URLs you are authorized to access; inspect and protect saved screenshots and metrics before sharing them.

Risk: UI and image reviews may inspect sensitive local source files or images.

Mitigation: Provide only files needed for the task and review reports and generated assets before distributing them.
