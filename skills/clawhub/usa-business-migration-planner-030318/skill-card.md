## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to turn a sensitive device-data recovery question into a practical plan, checklist, analysis, or decision-support response. The skill is most relevant when the user needs lawful, authorized, locally feasible guidance rather than a one-off answer. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The release name and stated topic may be misleading because the security evidence says the skill is packaged as a business migration planner while steering agents toward protected-phone WeChat data access workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill purpose before installation and label or constrain it as sensitive device-data recovery guidance. <br>
Risk: The skill can be invoked broadly and may be applied outside the intended sensitive recovery context. <br>
Mitigation: Limit invocation to explicit, authorized estate, owner, or vendor-supported recovery requests. <br>
Risk: The actual topic involves private data on a protected phone and could be misused to bypass device protections or retrieve another person's messages. <br>
Mitigation: Require lawful authorization and keep guidance focused on vendor-supported, consent-based, or estate-authorized recovery paths; avoid bypass instructions. <br>
