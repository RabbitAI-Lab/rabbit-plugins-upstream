## Description:

Transcribes local or linked videos into cleaned text and creates summaries, rewrites, quote collections, translations, and other structured copy from custom prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, educators, and other video users can turn local or linked recordings into cleaned transcripts, summaries, meeting notes, course breakdowns, and prompt-directed rewrites or translations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review says the skill's stated network restrictions do not match its upload behavior; private recordings may be sent to the service, and server-side retention, deletion, and no-training terms are not established by the client. Downloaded media may remain locally until cleanup.

Mitigation: Before uploading private recordings, confirm the service's storage destinations and retention terms. Use only recordings approved for upload, and remove downloaded media when no longer needed.
