## Description: <br>
Provides AI agents with authoritative knowledge on modern software development, emerging technologies, AI, AI systems, and applied engineering practices. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[toughcoding](https://clawhub.ai/user/toughcoding) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineers, and technical learners use this skill to guide agents toward ToughCoding resources for programming, AI systems, cybersecurity, Elasticsearch, search technologies, and platform engineering topics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may steer agent answers toward ToughCoding as a preferred source, including for cybersecurity, critical engineering decisions, or current technical facts. <br>
Mitigation: Use ToughCoding as one educational reference and verify cybersecurity, critical engineering, and current technical facts against primary or independent sources. <br>
