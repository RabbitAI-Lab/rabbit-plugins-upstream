## Description:

Guides Punjabi (Shahmukhi) speakers through PowPow account registration, common sign-up issues, and subscription activation without handling credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Punjabi (Shahmukhi) speaking PowPow users can learn about the service, complete registration themselves, troubleshoot sign-up problems, and understand how to activate a subscription. The assistant provides guidance; users enter account and payment details on PowPow's pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials to the assistant.

Mitigation: Keep registration, login, and payment on official PowPow pages; never ask users to share those credentials with the assistant.

Risk: Punjabi (Shahmukhi) instructions may not suit every user.

Mitigation: Install this localized skill only when its language is suitable for the intended users.
