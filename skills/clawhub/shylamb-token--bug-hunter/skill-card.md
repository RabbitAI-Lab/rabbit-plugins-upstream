## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, and quality teams use this Chinese-language skill to classify bugs, perform root-cause analysis, identify defect trends, create bug reports, and plan fixes. It is also useful when preparing quality dashboards or working with Jira and ZenTao defect-management workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Configured Jira or ZenTao workflows may exchange bug data with external systems. <br>
Mitigation: Use scoped API tokens, confirm before creating or syncing bug records, and avoid sending sensitive logs or internal incident details unless intended. <br>
Risk: Root-cause analysis, trend analysis, and quality predictions may be incomplete when bug reports or historical data are sparse. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against source defects, logs, and team knowledge before using them for release or remediation decisions. <br>
