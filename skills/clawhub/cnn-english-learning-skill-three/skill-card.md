## Description:

Explains user-provided CNN English passages sentence by sentence and word by word, with Chinese translations and contextual vocabulary and grammar guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[anshanzhaoqian](https://clawhub.ai/user/anshanzhaoqian)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English learners use this skill to understand user-supplied CNN news excerpts through Chinese translations, word meanings in context, sentence structure, collocations, and grammar explanations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Default Chinese explanations may not meet an English-only or bilingual learner's needs.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for English-only or bilingual explanations when needed.
