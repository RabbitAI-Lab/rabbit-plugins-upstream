## Description:

Research high-performing posts across major social platforms with okdata, then summarize effective hooks, formats, and angles with linked examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketers can research which posts perform well for a topic, compare examples across platforms, and use the resulting patterns to plan content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries are sent to okdata and may contain sensitive details.

Mitigation: Keep confidential or identifying information out of search queries.

Risk: Returned sources may not match the documented platform scope; the scan noted a Weibo trends request.

Mitigation: Check that returned platforms match the request and label any unexpected sources in the report.

Risk: The security scan had incomplete evidence and its benign verdict has medium confidence.

Mitigation: Review the connected data sources and results before relying on them.
