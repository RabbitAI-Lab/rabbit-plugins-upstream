## Description:

Research what content works for a topic on Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RedNote), WeChat Official Accounts, Bilibili, TikTok, X (Twitter) and YouTube with the okdata API. Searches each platform, ranks posts by engagement, opens the best ones in full, and reports the hooks, titles, formats and angles that perform, with examples and links. Use when the user wants content ideas, top-performing posts, viral examples, what to write or film about a topic, or a swipe file of reference posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries may be sent to okdata and could expose confidential or identifying topics.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive topics and avoid including confidential or identifying information in queries.

Risk: The security review had partial coverage and noted a mismatch between the described research workflow and a Weibo trends request.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its unresolved references before installation; the available scan does not fully establish behavior.

Risk: API calls are paid and costs can accumulate across searches and detail requests.

Mitigation: Estimate the call plan and budget first; the skill instructs users to ask before exceeding $1 unless they set a budget.
