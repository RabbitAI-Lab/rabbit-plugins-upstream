# rule-table.md (Korea VAT)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Items marked **[verify]** are periodically adjusted; confirm with NTS / Korea Customs before relying.

## Rate
- Flat 10% VAT on domestic supplies and imports.
- Exports out of Korea: zero-rated (not relevant to inbound cross-border sellers).

## De Minimis & Personal Clearance
- Personal-use imports ≤ US$150 (≤ US$200 for US-origin goods): duty and VAT exempt.
- Requires the buyer's PCCC (Personal Customs Clearance Code).
- Splitting bulk commercial shipments into PCCC personal entries = customs evasion — seizure + back taxes.

## Local Stock & Registration
- Inventory in Coupang Rocket or local 3PL warehouses = domestic sales → Korean business registration (사업자등록) and VAT filing required.
- Foreign sellers typically register via a local entity/branch or tax manager **[verify structure with 세무사]**.

## Platform Boundary
- NO marketplace deemed-supplier regime for goods: Coupang / Naver / Gmarket do NOT remit VAT for sellers.
- The foreign e-service VAT regime (since 2015) covers digital services only — out of scope for goods sellers.

## Receipts & Invoices
- Consumer prices must be VAT-inclusive.
- Registered sellers: cash receipts (현금영수증) for B2C on request; tax invoices (세금계산서) for B2B.
