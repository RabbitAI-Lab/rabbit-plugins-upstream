# deadlines.md (Korea VAT)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; verify with NTS or a 세무사.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- Individual business owners: quarterly VAT returns, due the 25th after quarter end (Jan 25 / Apr 25 / Jul 25 / Oct 25).
- Corporations: semiannual returns, due the 25th after the half-year end (Apr 25 / Oct 25), plus preliminary return periods (Jan / Jul) for interim payments.
- Customs-side collections on direct cross-border shipments: per-shipment at import, not periodic filings.

## Usage Notes
- Deadlines falling on weekends/holidays shift to the next business day.
- Nil returns are required once registered.
- If the user's entity type or registration status is unknown, mark "cannot determine."
