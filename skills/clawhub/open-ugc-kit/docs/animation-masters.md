# Seedance 2.5 animation masters

Image-to-video. Attach the still as the **first frame**. Paste the matching master. Fill brackets.

These follow the Seedance 2.5 five-layer brief: shot → subject continuity → timed action → light → audio. See [seedance-2.5.md](seedance-2.5.md).

**Settings every time**

```
model: bytedance:seedance@2.5
duration: 8
width: 720
height: 1280
audio: true
first frame: the still
optional: product photo as @Image1
draft: 480 × 854, then re-run at 720 × 1280
```

Do not re-describe the still. Do not ask for cuts.

## 1. Unboxing

```
An 8-second vertical UGC clip, 9:16.

Shot: handheld phone, chest height over the package, organic micro-shake, no dolly, no orbit, no cinematic grade.

Subject: keep the person, room, wardrobe, [PACKAGING], and [PRODUCT] identical to the first frame. Do not redesign the label. @Image1 is the product if attached.

Action:
[0-2s]: hands settle on [PACKAGING], eyes flick to the lens, mouth already moving.
[2-6s]: open the package the real way — peel tape, lift a flap, or pop a lid. Do not skip to a clean hero. [PRODUCT] stays inside until this window.
[6-8s]: lift [PRODUCT] into the lower third, turn it once in the same light, look back at the lens and say: "[HOOK_LINE]".

Light: keep the existing lighting and composition.

Audio: cardboard, tape or plastic, a short breath, room tone of this room. No BGM, only ambient and action sounds.

No subtitles. No on-screen text. No watermark. One continuous take, no cuts. [PRODUCT] is rigid. Five fingers on each hand.
```

## 2. Street interview

```
An 8-second vertical UGC clip, 9:16.

Shot: handheld interviewer phone, arm's length, slight wide-angle distortion, organic micro-shake, no gimbal smoothness, no cinematic grade.

Subject: keep both people, the street, wardrobe, and [PRODUCT] identical to the first frame. Do not add crew, boom mics, or new bystanders.

Action:
[0-2s]: interviewee finishes hearing the question, eyes go from interviewer to [PRODUCT].
[2-6s]: they turn [PRODUCT], feel the weight, or bring it closer to read. Mouth moves. Background pedestrians drift, they do not teleport. They say: "[HOOK_LINE]".
[6-8s]: a real laugh, grimace, or shrug, then they lift [PRODUCT] a little toward the lens.

Light: keep the existing lighting and composition.

Audio: street ambience, faint traffic or voices, product tap in the hands, their short reply. No BGM, only ambient and action sounds.

No subtitles. No on-screen text. No watermark. One continuous take, no cuts. Two distinct people, same as frame 1. [PRODUCT] is rigid.
```

## 3. Face-cam review

```
An 8-second vertical UGC clip, 9:16, front camera.

Shot: handheld front camera, selfie-arm fatigue (tiny drift), no switch to a rear camera, no gimbal, no cinematic grade.

Subject: keep the same face, hair, wardrobe, room, and [PRODUCT] as the first frame. Do not apply a beauty filter over time.

Action:
[0-2s]: talking to the lens, natural blinks, small head movement. The hand with [PRODUCT] gestures once.
[2-6s]: bring [PRODUCT] closer or do one real use (pump, shake, uncap, unfold). Face stays in frame. They say: "[HOOK_LINE]".
[6-8s]: settle back, finish the thought, same energy as the still.

Light: keep the existing lighting and composition.

Audio: close-mic voice, room tone of this room, one product handling sound. No BGM, only ambient and action sounds.

No subtitles. No on-screen text. No watermark. One continuous take, no cuts. [PRODUCT] is rigid. Five fingers on each hand.
```

## 4. Q&A / who sells more

```
An 8-second vertical UGC clip, 9:16.

Shot: handheld phone capturing both people, slight wide, organic micro-shake, no split screen, no vs graphic, no cinematic grade.

Subject: keep both people, the room, wardrobe, and the single [PRODUCT] identical to the first frame. Do not clone the product. Do not add a third person.

Action:
[0-2s]: the asker is already finishing the question. The other person starts to answer.
[2-6s]: they talk over each other a little. [PRODUCT] gets pointed at, passed, or tapped. One head-shake, one lean-in. One of them says: "[HOOK_LINE]".
[6-8s]: both glance at the lens as if they remembered the camera, then a laugh or an argument face.

Light: keep the existing lighting and composition.

Audio: two overlapping voices, room tone, a product tap. No BGM, only ambient and action sounds.

No subtitles. No on-screen text. No watermark. No lower-thirds. One continuous take, no cuts. [PRODUCT] is rigid.
```
