## Description:

Build realistic AI UGC video prompts for Seedance 2.5 (unboxing, street interview, face-cam review, Q&A). Use when the user wants UGC prompts, Seedance 2.5 openings, to clone a viral ad from EzUGC Ad Spy, or a still-then-I2V workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use the skill to turn product details into photorealistic UGC still prompts and timed Seedance 2.5 image-to-video prompts for short vertical ads. It supports unboxing, street interview, face-cam review, Q&A, and adapting an ad opening to a new product.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
