## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Deployment, rollback, domain, database, secret, package-install, and git-push commands can affect production systems or credentials. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the active provider account, target project, branch, and environment, and require explicit approval before executing those commands. <br>
Risk: Incorrect deployment steps or missed pre-deploy checks can cause failed releases, misconfiguration, or service downtime. <br>
Mitigation: Review the proposed steps, run local builds and tests, confirm environment variables are configured, and use the pre-deploy checklist before production deployment. <br>
