## Description:

Turns a music track into a beat-synced lyric video, slideshow, or kinetic promo using optional user-supplied media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to plan and produce music-led videos from a track, with optional images or footage synchronized to its pacing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: HyperFrames commands may run mutable package code and update globally installed skills.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or locally reviewed CLI, use a constrained workspace, and confirm updates before running them.

Risk: Rendered videos may contain flashing visual effects.

Mitigation: Review the rendered video for flashing effects before publishing.
