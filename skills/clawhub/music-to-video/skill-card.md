## Description:

Turns a music track or mood brief into a beat-synced lyric video, slideshow, or kinetic promo using optional supplied media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video teams use this skill to plan and produce music-led videos with beat-aligned typography and optional images or clips.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup may run changeable HyperFrames packages and update skills outside the project.

Mitigation: Use an isolated workspace and review update or initialization prompts before running them.

Risk: Media-provider credentials may be exposed if stored in the project.

Mitigation: Keep provider keys out of the repository and use the supported authentication flow.

Risk: Flash-heavy video effects may be unsuitable for public audiences.

Mitigation: Soften or avoid flashing templates and review the video before publication.
