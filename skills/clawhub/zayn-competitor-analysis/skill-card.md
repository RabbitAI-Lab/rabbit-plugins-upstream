## Description: <br>
基于公开企业、产品、渠道、价格带、交付、品牌、社媒、客户评价和售后信息，分析直接竞争对手、间接竞争对手或替代方案与我方的重叠、优势、弱点、威胁和差异化机会，并严格区分公开事实、外部信号和分析推断。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and business teams use this skill to compare competitors, alternatives, and their own offering across market, product, channel, delivery, pricing, brand, social, review, and after-sales evidence. It is intended for evidence-bound competitor analysis, not unsupported SWOT scoring or hostile claims. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide confidential business data while asking for competitor analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Use public sources or intentionally supplied materials only, and avoid providing confidential data unless it is intended for the analysis. <br>
Risk: Public competitor information may be incomplete, outdated, promotional, or conflicting. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve source, timing, and condition details; label unresolved assumptions and do not invent prices, market share, or capabilities. <br>
