## Description:

Generates Mo-style Chinese educational HTML card decks from learning content, with themed card layouts, image prompts, navigation, progress indicators, and completion feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[erich1566](https://clawhub.ai/user/erich1566)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn knowledge points, course material, articles, or science content into shareable Chinese HTML card decks styled after Mo cards. It is suited for educational summaries that need mobile and desktop viewing, card navigation, chapter markers, illustrations, and a compact one-card-one-point structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated card text is rendered as HTML, so untrusted pasted HTML or hostile source content could affect the resulting deck.

Mitigation: Use trusted or sanitized source content, review card text and image URLs, and inspect the generated HTML before sharing.
