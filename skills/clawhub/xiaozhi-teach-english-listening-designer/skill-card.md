## Description:

为中国大陆中小学英语教师设计分层听力材料、课堂活动和微技能训练方案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆中小学英语教师，辅助选择合法且难度适宜的听力材料，编排听前、听中、听后活动，并制定听力微技能训练与待教师确认的班级记录。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；在其他地区使用前须本地化课程、隐私和危机求助信息。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student listening records may contain identifying information.

Mitigation: Use de-identified student data and obtain teacher approval before saving or sharing class records.

Risk: Generated lessons, resources, or exam items may be inaccurate or unsuitable.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review and confirm each generated entry before it is saved or used.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy rules, and crisis contacts may not fit settings outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before deployment elsewhere.
