## Description:

英语听力材料设计 helps teachers turn English listening classes from replay-and-check-answer sessions into goal-driven lessons with material selection, prediction, layered listening tasks, post-listening work, and micro-skill training.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers and education teams use this skill to design Chinese K12 English listening lessons for upper-primary and junior-middle students. It supports listening goals, legally sourced material selection, pre-listening prediction, layered listening tasks, post-listening activities, micro-skill practice, rubrics, and class-workspace updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland by default; localize curriculum assumptions, privacy rules, consent requirements, and emergency resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student recordings or listening profiles may involve minor student data and school privacy obligations.

Mitigation: Confirm school policy, guardian or student consent where required, approved storage, retention limits, and sharing controls before use.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy, consent, and emergency-resource assumptions are designed for mainland China and may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum alignment, privacy rules, consent requirements, and emergency resources before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Listening materials can create copyright risk if unauthorized audio or paid content is copied into shared resources.

Mitigation: Require copyrightStatus for every material and keep unauthorized or uncertain sources as indexes only rather than copied content.

Risk: AI-generated listening materials or questions may contain factual, answer-key, difficulty, or distractor errors.

Mitigation: Label AI-generated items and require teacher verification before adding them to a resource bank, lesson, or exam.

Risk: Student text may surface self-harm, bullying, family safety, or severe distress signals outside the learning-support scope.

Mitigation: Stop the teaching workflow, avoid diagnosis or minimization, and route to trusted adults or local emergency/crisis resources according to the crisis exception protocol.
