## Description:

帮助中小学英语教师选编听力材料，设计听前预测、分层听中任务和听后微技能训练。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

中小学英语教师用它规划听力课，选择有适当难度和使用权限的材料，设计分阶段练习和听力微技能训练；生成内容须由教师审定后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; designed around Chinese K–12 English curricula and local examination requirements

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student listening records may expose personal or sensitive classroom data.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, share only necessary data, and disable memory or writeback without consent; teachers confirm entries before saving.

Risk: Listening materials may have unclear copyright permissions.

Mitigation: Check usage rights and label each material's copyright status; do not reproduce unauthorized audio.

Risk: Generated exercises or assessments may be inaccurate or unsuitable for students.

Mitigation: Have teachers review answers, difficulty, and suitability before classroom use or inclusion in a question bank.
