## Description:

Helps teachers design level-appropriate English listening lessons, materials, activities, and micro-skill practice for Chinese K–12 learners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English teachers use this skill to select appropriately licensed listening materials, plan before/during/after-listening activities, and draft practice questions and instructional feedback for upper-primary through high-school classes. Teachers review generated materials and approve any classroom-record updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize curriculum, legal guidance, and crisis resources outside Mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class assessment and listening records may contain student information.

Mitigation: Limit access to authorized teachers and require confirmation and consent before writing items or student-profile updates.

Risk: Generated listening questions or lesson guidance may be inaccurate or unsuitable for the class.

Mitigation: Have teachers review and verify generated items, difficulty, and answer keys before use or storage.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, legal guidance, and crisis resources are oriented toward Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before student-facing use elsewhere and confirm location before providing emergency contacts.

Risk: Unlicensed listening materials or audio could be copied into shared teaching resources.

Mitigation: Check usage rights, label source status, and keep restricted materials as source references rather than copying them.
