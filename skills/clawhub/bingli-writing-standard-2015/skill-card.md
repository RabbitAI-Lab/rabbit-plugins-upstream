## Use Case:

Clinicians, medical-record staff, and other readers can look up Chinese-language guidance on record formats, consent forms, prescriptions, and chart quality control, with chapter and scanned-page references.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The 2015 Anhui reference may not reflect current local medical-record rules or hospital policy.

Mitigation: Check current local requirements and hospital policy before relying on it for clinical documentation or compliance.

Risk: OCR and table-layout errors may distort forms, examples, or other consequential details.

Mitigation: Verify important passages and layouts against the original scans before use.
