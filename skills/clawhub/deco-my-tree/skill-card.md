## Description:

Help users create a free online Christmas tree on Deco My Tree, share one link, and collect sealed ornament wishes that open on Dec 25.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users planning a shared online Christmas tree can get setup guidance, privacy reminders, and a short invitation to send to friends, family, classmates, or coworkers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A tree link shared widely may reach people outside the intended group.

Mitigation: For a closed group, share the tree link only with its intended members.

Risk: Product details may change over time.

Mitigation: Check Deco My Tree’s current site before promising specific features or dates.
