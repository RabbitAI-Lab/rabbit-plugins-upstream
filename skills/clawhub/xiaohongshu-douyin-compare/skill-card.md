## Description:

Compares 2–10 Xiaohongshu, Douyin, TikTok, YouTube, or Bilibili posts in one report, highlighting shared themes, hooks, formats, selling points, audience concerns, and content gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to compare competitor or high-performing posts and find patterns that can inform content planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted post links and the LINKDIGEST_API_KEY are sent to LinkDigest.

Mitigation: Only submit links you are comfortable sharing with LinkDigest, protect the API key, and review credit use before running a batch.

Risk: The security review notes a possible gap between the advertised batch comparison workflow and the described single-link behavior.

Mitigation: Check results from a small batch and verify cited examples against their source posts before relying on the comparison.
