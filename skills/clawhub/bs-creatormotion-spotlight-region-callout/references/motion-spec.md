## Timing and media contract

The composition lasts 9 seconds at 30 fps in each of the four aspect ratios. The module contains the exact deterministic GSAP beat positions; these are authored directing choices, not recovered source keyframes. Keep the source scene, the change of focus and the final reading hold as separate phases. Use short transitions for changes of attention and reserve stillness for reading. Do not apply one generic entrance to every item.

Keep the documented input schema from `SKILL.md`. Image keys and presenter video remain replaceable. Where a transparent presenter matte is used, keep it aligned to the same original take and timing. Do not loop a short take to fill a longer shot; end its visible interval before the supplied media ends. No third-party creator footage, identities or proprietary interface screenshots belong in the generated example or package.
