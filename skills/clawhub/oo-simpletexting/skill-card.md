## Description:

Helps agents manage SimpleTexting contacts and lists through a connected OOMOL account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

People with a connected SimpleTexting account use this skill to look up and manage contacts, contact lists, memberships, custom fields, and segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the optional oo CLI without reviewing its installation method.

Mitigation: Check the installation guide and verify the installer before running it.

Risk: Contact or list changes, including deletions, may alter SimpleTexting data.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact target and effect with the user; require explicit approval before destructive actions.
