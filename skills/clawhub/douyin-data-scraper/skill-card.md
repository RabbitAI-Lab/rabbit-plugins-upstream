## Description:

Retrieves public Douyin videos, creator posts, comments, and trending topics as structured data for content research and analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content researchers, creators, and marketing teams can retrieve public Douyin search results, hot lists, creator posts, and comments for trend monitoring, competitor comparisons, and content planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and Douyin links are shared with the third-party API provider, and usage may incur charges.

Mitigation: Review the provider's terms, privacy information, and pricing before use; submit only queries and links you are comfortable sharing.

Risk: Retrieved comments, user identifiers, and request details remain in local logs until removed.

Mitigation: Limit access to the logs and delete them when they are no longer needed.
