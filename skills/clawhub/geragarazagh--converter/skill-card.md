## Description:

Converts a file into another format, size or form and checks the result: video and audio (play on a phone or TV, compress to a size, cut, join, GIF, extract sound), photos (HEIC to JPEG), PDF (merge, split, compress, OCR to searchable), DjVu, Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF, e-books, archives, subtitles, garbled letters and wrong text encodings, JSON, YAML, CSV. Use whenever a file has to become another file or be fixed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
