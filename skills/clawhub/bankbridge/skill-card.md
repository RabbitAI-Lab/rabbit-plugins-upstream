## Description:

Answer questions about a user's finances using live, read-only data from their connected US bank, credit card, and brokerage accounts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jakemarsh](https://clawhub.ai/user/jakemarsh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can review their own connected US financial accounts to understand balances, spending, recurring charges, cash flow, transactions, and investments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected financial information may appear in the conversation, and provider privacy terms or subscription charges may apply.

Mitigation: Connect only accounts intended for the agent, review BankBridge's privacy terms and charges, and disconnect the bank or revoke the connector when access is no longer needed.
