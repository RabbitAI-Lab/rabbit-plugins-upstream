## Description:

Helps infant-care practitioners plan age-appropriate complementary foods, sample meals, and daily feeding and sleep routines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Infant-care practitioners use this skill to draft age-based food introduction plans, weekly meal examples, and daily feeding, nap, and activity schedules from a baby's age, feeding history, and household circumstances.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General feeding or allergy guidance may be mistaken for medical advice in higher-risk cases.

Mitigation: Use a qualified clinician for significant allergies, premature infants, abnormal growth, medication or supplement dosing, and other medical-risk situations.

Risk: Emergency symptoms or choking events require immediate care rather than a routine plan.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the skill for emergencies or choking events; seek urgent professional help.
