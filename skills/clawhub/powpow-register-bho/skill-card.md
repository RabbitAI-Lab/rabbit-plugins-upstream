## Description:

Guides users in Bhojpuri through PowPow account registration, subscription activation, and common registration or payment troubleshooting without handling credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External PowPow users use this Bhojpuri-language agent skill to understand the product, register accounts, resolve common registration errors, and complete subscription activation on PowPow's own website or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while seeking registration help.

Mitigation: The skill directs users to complete registration, verification, login, and payment on PowPow's own pages and not to share sensitive values in chat.

Risk: Subscription activation can be delayed or require manual confirmation, which may confuse users after payment.

Mitigation: The skill explains the PayPal, WeChat, and Huawei activation paths and guides users to refresh, wait for manual confirmation, or contact platform support when appropriate.

Risk: Platform registration rules or payment behavior may change after the skill's release.

Mitigation: The skill treats PowPow's live website or app behavior as authoritative and avoids submitting forms or making payment actions on the user's behalf.
