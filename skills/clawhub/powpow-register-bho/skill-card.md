## Description:

Provides Bhojpuri-language guidance for registering a PowPow account, troubleshooting signup, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users get Bhojpuri-language instructions for account registration, signup troubleshooting, and subscription activation. Users complete registration and payment themselves on the provider's pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users might share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Mitigation: Do not request these details; direct users to complete registration and payments on PowPow, PayPal, WeChat, or Huawei pages.

Risk: Subscription activation may be delayed, especially when manual confirmation is required.

Mitigation: Ask users to check their status on PowPow and contact platform support if payment remains unconfirmed.
