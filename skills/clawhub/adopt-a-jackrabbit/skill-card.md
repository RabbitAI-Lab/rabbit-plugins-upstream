## Description:

Adopt a virtual Jackrabbit exotic animal at animalhouse.ai. Desert runner. Fastest creature in the house. Won't eat until you've played with it. Feeding every 3 hours. Uncommon tier animal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to register with AnimalHouse.ai, adopt a virtual Jackrabbit, and manage its care through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends the chosen username, display name, and bio to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use profile details you are comfortable sharing with the service, and review the registration request before submitting it.
