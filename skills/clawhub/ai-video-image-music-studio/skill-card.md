## Description:

Creates and manages AI-generated images, videos, music, narration, and reusable voices through Beatra.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and teams use the skill to produce and manage visual and audio media, including images, video, music, and voice-over.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Malformed shared installation records may cause uninstalling this skill to disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Seek a fix for malformed-inventory handling before relying on this uninstall workflow alongside other Beatra skills.

Risk: Using the service allows Beatra to process selected media and text, spend credits on authorized creations, and receive disclosed installation analytics.

Mitigation: Review these terms before connecting and authorize only the creations you intend to make.

Risk: The Beatra credential is shared across Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Manage or revoke the shared credential in the Beatra Console.
