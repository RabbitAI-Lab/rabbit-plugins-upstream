## Description:

Generate AI video, images, music, and voice-over in one connected creative flow, edit visual results, and keep everything you make easy to find.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and creative teams use it to turn ideas, scripts, and source media into images, videos, music, and voice-over, then edit and manage the finished assets.

Risk: The security evidence reports a suspicious verdict with medium confidence and incomplete scanner coverage.

Mitigation: Review the package and its security notes before connecting an account, and decide whether the reported installation telemetry is acceptable.

Risk: Prompts and uploaded media are sent to an external service for processing.

Mitigation: Share only material you are authorized to send, and avoid sensitive content unless Beatra's privacy practices meet your needs.

Risk: The security guidance reports that the update package is unpinned.

Mitigation: Use a trusted release channel and review the package source and version before updating.

Risk: Uninstall may affect shared local state if inventory data is malformed.

Mitigation: Review the uninstall notes and shared inventory before removing the skill or its local data.
