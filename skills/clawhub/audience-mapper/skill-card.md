## Description: <br>
Audience Mapper helps agents produce audience profiles and niche-community dossiers for influencer planning, including platform priorities, personas, cultural norms, key-voice tiers, Brand Fit Score, entry strategy, and handoff criteria. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and influencer-program teams use this skill to understand a target audience or niche community before selecting creators. It turns brand, product, customer, or community inputs into research-backed selection criteria, cultural risks, and next-step handoffs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Audience profiles may include sensitive customer demographics, inferred psychographics, community sensitivities, or named creator assessments. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs before saving or reusing them, and avoid approving memory saves that expose sensitive or unsupported audience claims. <br>
Risk: Named creator or key-voice recommendations can be mistaken for final partnership decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as preliminary research and run separate discovery, vetting, and outreach review before contracting creators. <br>
Risk: Community-entry guidance can miss local norms or cultural taboos when based on incomplete inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Validate inferred attributes with current first-party or social-listening data and preserve the skill's confidence labels in downstream briefs. <br>
