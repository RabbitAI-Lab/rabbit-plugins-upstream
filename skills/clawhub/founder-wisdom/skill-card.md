## Description:

Surfaces opinionated, pattern-matched startup operating axioms for founders, operators, investors, advisors, coaches, and mentors working through company-building decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chris-graffagnino](https://clawhub.ai/user/chris-graffagnino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External founders, startup operators, investors, advisors, coaches, mentors, and aspiring founders use this skill to retrieve relevant startup axioms, ask Socratic decision questions, and frame tradeoffs across hiring, fundraising, product, sales, finance, governance, strategy, crisis, and company mechanics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on startup guidance for legal, tax, financial, employment, or regulated-industry matters.

Mitigation: Treat the guidance as contextual decision support and consult qualified professionals for specialized or regulated decisions.

Risk: Broad startup-related activation may provide opinionated founder advice when the user expected neutral or general-purpose help.

Mitigation: Signal that the corpus is opinionated and confirm the user's context before applying strong axioms to ambiguous situations.

Risk: The corpus is biased toward largely U.S., venture-backed, software startup patterns and survivor-sampled source material.

Mitigation: Name geographic, sector, structural, and outcome-sampling caveats when they materially affect the advice.
