## Description:

Researches products, prices, sellers, and reviews across online marketplaces and retailers using Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shoppers, researchers, and commerce teams use this skill to find products, compare retailer prices and sellers, and review public listing feedback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product queries, product identifiers, store lookups, and supplied location parameters are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for the lookup and avoid sensitive personal data.

Risk: Observed prices and shipping details may not reflect the final checkout total.

Mitigation: Check currency, destination, shipping charges, and current listing terms before relying on a comparison.
