## Description: <br>
Writes session-based exploratory testing charters with risk overview, focused missions, tactics, oracles, timeboxes, setup needs, and reporting guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
QA engineers, developers, and product teams use this skill to plan purposeful exploratory testing for a feature or area. It is especially useful when a brief is incomplete because it labels assumptions and still produces risk-prioritized charters. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated charters may rely on inferred assumptions when the testing brief is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review and correct assumptions before using the charters for real test planning. <br>
