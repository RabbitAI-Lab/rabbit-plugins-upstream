## Description: <br>
Translate rough visual ideas into structured prompts for image, video, NFT, sprite, and campaign work. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, designers, and developers use this skill to turn rough visual ideas into production-ready prompts and creative briefs for generated images, video shots, NFT assets, sprites, thumbnails, ads, posters, and campaign visuals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated campaign or public-posting prompts may include claims, logos, trademarks, or living-person likenesses that require review or approval. <br>
Mitigation: Review public-facing prompts and outputs for factual claims, rights, trademarks, and likeness permissions before publishing. <br>
Risk: Generated text inside images may render inaccurately. <br>
Mitigation: Add exact typography or important copy later in a design tool when precision is required. <br>
