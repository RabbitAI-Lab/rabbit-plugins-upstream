## Description: <br>
A simple paper tracking system for research that supports daily scanning and deep research modes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, analysts, and developer teams use this skill to track new papers, run periodic scans, and perform deeper literature surveys around a configured research focus. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Future daily scan or deep research configuration may add network access, scheduled runs, or storage of research results. <br>
Mitigation: Review configured sources, scheduling behavior, and result storage before enabling recurring or expanded workflows. <br>
Risk: Paper tracking results may be incomplete or stale if the configured research focus or source list is too narrow. <br>
Mitigation: Validate important findings against primary papers and adjust sources or queries for high-stakes research decisions. <br>
