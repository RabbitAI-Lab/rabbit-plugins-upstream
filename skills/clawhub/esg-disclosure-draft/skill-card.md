## Description: <br>
Draft an honest, audit-ready ESG disclosure section in a CSRD/ESRS-flavored structure, adaptable to other frameworks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, sustainability teams, and disclosure writers use this skill to draft ESG report sections from provided sustainability topics, materiality decisions, metrics, targets, known gaps, and audience requirements. The draft is structured for review by compliance, legal, or assurance stakeholders before publication. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A disclosure draft may be mistaken for legal, compliance, or assurance approval. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as drafting assistance and have final text reviewed against the applicable framework and regulation by qualified compliance and legal reviewers. <br>
Risk: Sensitive ESG metrics or business data may be shared with the agent environment. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information that is appropriate for the operating environment and redact confidential data when it is not needed for drafting. <br>
