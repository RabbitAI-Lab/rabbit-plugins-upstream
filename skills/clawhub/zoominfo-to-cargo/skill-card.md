## Description:

Rebuild a ZoomInfo, Lusha, or Cognism list on Cargo and compare verified coverage and enrichment cost before renewal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cargo-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/cargo-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Revenue operations and sales leaders use this skill to assess whether Cargo can replace a seat-priced contact database by comparing verified coverage and credit cost on a small export sample.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contact data in an export is shared with Cargo and its enrichment providers during comparison.

Mitigation: Use a sample your organization permits sharing and check Cargo's and its enrichment providers' data-handling terms.

Risk: Enriching a full list can incur substantial credit costs.

Mitigation: Start with 10–20 records, report the observed cost and hit rate, then get approval for a full run using the record count and estimated credits.
