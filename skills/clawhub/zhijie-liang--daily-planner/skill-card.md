## Description: <br>
Daily Planner generates structured daily plans from a user's task list, including priority ordering, time-block schedules, Pomodoro blocks, and Markdown checklists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhijie-liang](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhijie-liang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to turn daily tasks, deadlines, available work windows, and rest preferences into a prioritized Markdown schedule with time blocks and Pomodoro breaks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated Markdown plans may contain personal task, deadline, or schedule details and may be saved in the current working directory. <br>
Mitigation: Review the plan before sharing it and confirm the output path before saving a daily plan file. <br>
