## Description:

Provides household hazardous-waste disposal routes, cleanout manifests, interim storage guidance, and US, EU, and UK take-back options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Households and their assistants use this skill to sort common hazardous items into disposal or take-back channels, organize cleanout trips, and plan safe storage until drop-off.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

US, EU, and UK guidance; confirm local rules and facility acceptance before use

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local disposal rules, accepted materials, and facility hours can differ from general guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm accepted items, local requirements, and operating hours with the municipality or facility before transporting materials.

Risk: General household disposal guidance does not address active spills, reacting chemicals, airborne asbestos, or commercial quantities.

Mitigation: Do not use this skill for these situations; seek appropriate emergency, professional, or regulatory guidance.
