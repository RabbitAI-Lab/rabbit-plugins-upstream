## Description: <br>
Maps an organisation's risks against its insurance policy portfolio to identify uncovered, underinsured, or double-covered exposures and produce a coverage matrix, deductible review, and ranked recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Insurance, risk, and finance teams use this skill to compare a risk register with policy details, identify coverage gaps or overlaps, review deductible exposure, and prioritize follow-up questions for management or a licensed broker or adviser. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive internal risk, policy, and insurance portfolio details. <br>
Mitigation: Use only appropriate business data handling practices and limit inputs to information the agent is authorized to analyze. <br>
Risk: Coverage conclusions may be mistaken for binding insurance, legal, or purchase advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as analytical support and have coverage decisions reviewed by a licensed broker, adviser, or other qualified professional. <br>
Risk: Incomplete policy wording or missing limits can produce incorrect gap or underinsurance findings. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm missing policy terms, exclusions, limits, deductibles, and management risk-retention decisions before acting on recommendations. <br>
