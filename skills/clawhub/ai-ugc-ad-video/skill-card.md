## Description:

Creates prompts and production guidance for AI-generated UGC product ads with a consistent creator, voice, and product, and labeling for the resulting video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and developers can use the skill to plan and generate vertical AI UGC product ads through varg, including scripts, shot prompts, and labeling guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented install commands are unpinned, so an install may not select a fixed release.

Mitigation: Pin the release or version when installing and verify the selected package before use.

Risk: Product images, prompts, scripts, or voice material may be sent to external generation services.

Mitigation: Review which service receives each input and share only content you are authorized to provide.

Risk: The security scan reports incomplete coverage because a local reference was unresolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and the skill contents before installing or using it.
