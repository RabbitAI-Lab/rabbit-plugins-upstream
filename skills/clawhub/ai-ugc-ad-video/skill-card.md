## Description:

Creates labeled AI UGC-style product ads with a synthetic presenter, using product imagery, scripts, and selected image, video, and speech services.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Marketers and product teams use the skill to create and edit labeled vertical ad videos with a fictional AI presenter. It guides scripts, product demonstrations, generation prompts, review, and editing while avoiding fabricated first-person testimonials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected images, scripts, and voice references may be processed by Varg and selected generation services.

Mitigation: Upload only media you are authorized to share and review the providers’ privacy terms.

Risk: Generating a full batch of ads can spend service credits.

Mitigation: Estimate and approve the full batch cost before generation.
