## Description:

Creates labeled, vertical AI UGC video ads featuring a generated creator presenting a real product.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and creators use this skill to plan, generate, review, edit, and label AI UGC ads featuring a product. It supports formats such as unboxing, direct-to-camera, street-interview, and in-car videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The installer is not pinned to an exact version, so a later install may retrieve changed content.

Mitigation: Review the release and pin or otherwise verify the intended version before installing.

Risk: Product images, prompts, voice recordings, or other supplied media may be sent to external generation services.

Mitigation: Review the services' data handling and only submit media and prompts approved for external processing.
