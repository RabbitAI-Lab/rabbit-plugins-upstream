## Description:

Creates labeled vertical AI UGC ads with a creator presenting a product, using prompts that keep the creator, voice, and product consistent across shots.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External marketers and developers use this skill to plan and generate short-form product ads with AI creators. It guides casting, scripting, video generation, review, editing, and disclosure while avoiding fabricated first-person testimonials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted photos and voice samples may include material the user is not authorized to share.

Mitigation: Use only photos and voices you have permission to submit.

Risk: Confidential material may be processed by Varg and the selected generation providers.

Mitigation: Review their privacy terms before submitting confidential material.

Risk: Generating a batch, including retakes, can spend credits.

Mitigation: Estimate and approve the batch cost before generation.

Risk: Viewers may mistake synthetic ad content for real footage or a real testimonial.

Mitigation: Keep AI disclosures in finished ads and do not script first-person testimonials or results for the AI creator.
