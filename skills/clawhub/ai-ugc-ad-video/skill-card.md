## Description:

Creates vertical AI UGC ad videos featuring a creator speaking about a product, with a consistent face, voice, and product label across shots.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and developers can use the skill to create short UGC-style product ads for social campaigns, using an AI creator, a product photo, and a timed script. It is intended for general social ads rather than TikTok Shop affiliate videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
