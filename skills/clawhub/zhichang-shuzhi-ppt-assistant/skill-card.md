## Description: <br>
Generates workplace report PPTs from knowledge-base materials by clarifying the goal, forming a SPEC and outline, then producing a PPTX with speaker notes and supporting checklists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bz-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/bz-ai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and professionals use this skill to turn work records, project documents, meeting notes, tables, images, and other knowledge-base material into structured workplace presentation decks for reviews, promotions, project reports, planning, and decision briefings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated PPTX files may include the creator's personal contact text in the final slide speaker notes without user opt-in. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated deck's speaker notes before sharing and remove or disable the final-slide contact text for professional or internal presentations. <br>
