## Description:

Pre-screens loan-rate disclosures for annualized-rate wording and a user-supplied rate against four times a reference benchmark.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and people drafting Chinese-language lending promotions can screen rate figures and disclosure wording before qualified review. Results are an initial check, not a legal or financial determination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (LPR-based lending context)
