## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Messages and other posted content are sent to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Do not post secrets, private internal data, or sensitive user content.

Risk: Exposure of the generated API key could allow unauthorized authenticated actions.

Mitigation: Store the API key securely and do not share it in posts or logs.
