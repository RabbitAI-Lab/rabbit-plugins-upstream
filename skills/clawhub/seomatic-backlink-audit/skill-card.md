## Description:

Audits a connected site's backlink profile, compares competitor authority, and identifies relevant link prospects using SEOmatic data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to assess whether backlinks limit search performance and to prioritize relevant link prospects. It can stage proposed link-building work for the user's approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic grants access to backlink and Google Search Console-derived site data.

Mitigation: Connect only the intended site and review the access granted before using the skill.

Risk: Backlink and competitor lookups may use monthly allowances or paid AI credits.

Mitigation: Check the available allowance and review task-card costs before approval.

Risk: Approved site changes may take effect shortly after approval.

Mitigation: Review staged tasks and approve only specific actions in SEOmatic; never approve on the user's behalf.
