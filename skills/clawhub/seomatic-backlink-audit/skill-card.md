## Description:

Analyze a connected site's backlink profile, compare it with competitors, and identify relevant link prospects using SEOmatic data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to assess backlink quality and velocity, compare their site's authority with competitors, and identify relevant link prospects. It summarizes connected SEOmatic data into a report that can guide link-building decisions.
