## Description:

Generate Chinese and English weekly reports for Lazada LazFlash ELP module across 6 SEA countries from dashboard xlsx data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ruotingw](https://clawhub.ai/user/ruotingw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business analysts and Lazada ELP teams use this skill to turn weekly dashboard workbooks into bilingual performance reports and data-driven insights for six Southeast Asian markets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Southeast Asia (Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, and Vietnam).

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using a workbook with a different layout or metric mapping may produce unreliable report figures.

Mitigation: Confirm the workbook tabs, documented cells, and metric definitions match before relying on the reports.

Risk: Business data may be sensitive or unauthorized for processing.

Mitigation: Process only business files you are authorized to use in your agent environment.
