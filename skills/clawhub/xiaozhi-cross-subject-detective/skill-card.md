## Description:

用一个真实主题在一周内串联多门学科，找出学科之间的联结。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Upper-primary, middle-school, and high-school students use the skill to explore a topic through several subject areas, ask deeper questions, and build connections in a project record. The skill requires an adult to be present for younger students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records may contain student information, and storage or sharing controls depend on the hosting platform.

Mitigation: Review platform storage, consent, and sharing controls; use 「这次不要记忆」 to disable record access and saving for the session.

Risk: Younger students need adult supervision, and disclosures about harm or severe distress require support beyond tutoring.

Mitigation: Have a parent or teacher present for younger students and follow the skill's crisis referral guidance when safety concerns arise.
