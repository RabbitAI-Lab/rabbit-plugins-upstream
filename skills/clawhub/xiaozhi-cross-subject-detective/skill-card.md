## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking K–12 students through a week-long investigation of a real-world topic across subjects, helping them identify connections and organize a project record.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Upper-primary through high-school students use this skill to explore one topic from several subject perspectives, discuss connections, and prepare a project record and concept-map updates. Younger students need an accompanying parent or teacher; the skill does not complete research reports for students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K–12 context); localize curriculum, consent practices, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project and concept-map records contain student learning information.

Mitigation: Confirm updates before saving and honor requests to pause, view, correct, export, or delete records.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, consent rules, and crisis contacts may not apply in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before deployment elsewhere and use region-appropriate crisis referrals.
