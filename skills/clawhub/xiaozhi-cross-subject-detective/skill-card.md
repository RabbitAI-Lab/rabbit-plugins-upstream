## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking K12 students explore one real-world theme across multiple subjects over a week, identify interdisciplinary connections, and record the resulting project work in a concept graph.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking K12 learners use this skill to run a structured interdisciplinary inquiry project, moving from theme selection to subject perspectives, guided deep dives, connection-building, and project records. It is intended for upper-primary, middle-school, and high-school contexts, with adult presence or consent where the supporting materials require it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with localization required for curriculum assumptions, minor-consent requirements, and crisis-support contacts outside Mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-speaking K12 learning contexts and includes Mainland China curriculum and crisis-support assumptions.

Mitigation: Before use in another region, localize curriculum expectations, minor-consent requirements, and emergency or youth-support contacts.

Risk: Project records and concept-graph updates may rely on profile memory or cross-skill sharing.

Mitigation: Review consent settings before enabling memory or sharing, and preserve the provided controls for viewing, correcting, deleting, pausing, exporting, and limiting shared records.
