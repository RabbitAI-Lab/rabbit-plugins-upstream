## Description:

引导中小学学生围绕一个真实主题开展一周的跨学科探究，发现知识之间的联系并整理项目记录。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students in upper primary, middle, and high school use this skill to explore a real-world theme across subjects, develop their own connections, and compile a project record. Upper-primary students require a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; adapt curriculum, consent practices, and crisis resources outside mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student project and concept records, including minors' information, may be retained or shared across learning skills.

Mitigation: Confirm consent and who may enable, view, or share records; honor pause, export, correction, and deletion requests before deployment.

Risk: Mainland-China-focused crisis resources and curriculum assumptions may not fit other locations.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and localize emergency referrals, curriculum, and consent rules before use outside mainland China.
