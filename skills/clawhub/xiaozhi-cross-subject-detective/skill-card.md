## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through a weeklong investigation of a real-world topic, connecting ideas across subjects and organizing their findings into a project record.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary through high school use this skill to explore a topic from several subject perspectives, identify connections, and assemble a weekly project record. Younger primary students need adult guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (default audience and safety guidance; localize crisis resources elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project records and cross-skill or parent sharing may expose a minor's learning history.

Mitigation: Confirm memory and sharing preferences before use; use the provided pause, deletion, and sharing controls when appropriate.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contact details may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency and support resources to the student's location before relying on them.
