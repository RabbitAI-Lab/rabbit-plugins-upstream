## Description:

Guide inventors and R&D teams through a seven-step English invention-disclosure workflow, organize rough technical notes, identify missing technical support, and generate a structured draft for inventor and qualified patent-professional review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External inventors, R&D teams, in-house IP teams, and patent professionals use the skill to turn rough ideas, product notes, paper abstracts, experiment records, design discussions, or incomplete disclosures into technically structured invention-disclosure drafts for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished invention material may be exposed through an unapproved browser, extension, clipboard history, downloads folder, backup system, or storage location.

Mitigation: Use only approved devices, browsers, storage, and document or case-management systems; clear clipboard and downloaded draft artifacts according to organizational policy.

Risk: A generated disclosure draft could be mistaken for a patent application, legal opinion, filing instruction, or conclusion about patentability, infringement, validity, or freedom to operate.

Mitigation: Treat the draft as an internal review aid and require inventor plus qualified patent-professional review before legal or filing use.

Risk: Optional PatSnap research results may be overinterpreted as complete prior-art coverage or a legal conclusion.

Mitigation: Record search coverage and returned URLs exactly, and avoid claims about novelty, inventive step, validity, infringement, or freedom to operate.
