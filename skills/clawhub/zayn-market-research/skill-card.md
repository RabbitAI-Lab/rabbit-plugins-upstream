## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and analysts use this skill to conduct structured market and industry research when the industry or product, geography, customer segment, purpose, time range, depth, and key questions are clear. It helps define research boundaries, compare evidence, separate global from regional data, and produce opportunity, risk, and source-backed conclusions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Market research output may be misleading if the industry, product boundary, geography, customer segment, purpose, or time range is unclear. <br>
Mitigation: Require clarification before formal research and include the parameter status and research boundaries in the output. <br>
Risk: Market size, growth, share, pricing, or forecast claims can be overstated if sources are old, regional and global figures are mixed, or predictions are treated as facts. <br>
Mitigation: Separate global and regional data, label historical and forecast periods, show source conflicts by methodology, and avoid unsupported figures. <br>
Risk: The skill may need current public data or user-provided materials to support reliable conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for materials or access boundaries when needed, cite sources, and state data gaps instead of inventing missing values. <br>
