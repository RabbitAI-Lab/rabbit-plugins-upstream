## Description:

Design and build a responsive frontend website or accessible landing page for a small business or creator from the first brief. Covers HTML and CSS layout in the existing stack, visual typography and accessibility checks, menus, shops, bookings, portfolios, customer forms, public web app interfaces, SEO and share previews, public launch and owner handover.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A prototype enquiry or booking flow could be mistaken for a live service.

Mitigation: Make connection status clear, use a draft or copy fallback when needed, and report delivery only after the service confirms it.

Risk: An optional domain purchase or DNS change could cause an unwanted charge or disrupt the site.

Mitigation: Keep purchases optional and owner-controlled; review current host records and preserve unrelated DNS records before changes.
