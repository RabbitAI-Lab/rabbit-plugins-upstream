## Description:

个人信息保护合规护栏。在隐私政策、用户协议、App权限申请、数据收集告知、营销推送文案发布前，实时检测是否符合《个人信息保护法》《数据安全法》《网络安全法》及GB/T 35273等规范要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

## Use Case:

Privacy, legal, product, and compliance teams use this skill to review privacy policies, user agreements, app permission prompts, data collection notices, SDK sharing disclosures, marketing copy, deletion flows, cross-border transfer notices, and child privacy statements against Chinese personal information protection requirements. It produces a structured compliance review report with compliant items, warnings, non-compliant findings, cited rule areas, and revision suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance review output may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as compliance guidance and have qualified legal or data compliance professionals review high-stakes publication or regulatory decisions.

Risk: The skill can block or flag content based on checklist rules that may not cover every factual, jurisdictional, or business-specific nuance.

Mitigation: Use the report as a structured review aid, verify cited issues against current requirements, and perform human review before publication.
