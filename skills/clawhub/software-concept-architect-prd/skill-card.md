## Description:

Transcribes a confirmed concept model into a traceable PRD and colocated concept and synchronization specifications when explicitly invoked.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to document an already confirmed concept model as a PRD, concept contracts, synchronization rules, and acceptance criteria with traceable sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Updating existing PRD and specification files may change human-maintained documentation or links.

Mitigation: Review generated edits and cross-document links before accepting them.

Risk: Transcribed requirements or acceptance criteria may misrepresent an incomplete or unconfirmed model.

Mitigation: Compare output against the confirmed model and requirement sources; leave gaps as open decisions rather than inventing behavior.
