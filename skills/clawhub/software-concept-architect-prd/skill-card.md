## Description:

Transcribes a confirmed software concept model into a traceable PRD and colocated concept and synchronization specifications without redesigning the model.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to turn a confirmed concept model into an overall PRD, concept specifications, synchronization rules, and acceptance criteria with traceable sources and open decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated specifications may misstate the confirmed model or acceptance criteria.

Mitigation: Review documentation changes against the confirmed model and requirement sources before relying on them.
