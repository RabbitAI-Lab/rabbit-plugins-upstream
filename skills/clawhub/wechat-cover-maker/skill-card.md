## Description:

Turn an article title, topic, summary, or reference image into a WeChat Official Account cover, WeChat article cover, article hero image, post cover, headline image, or supporting article visual. This AI cover generator and article cover maker distills one clear visual hook, then creates either a rendered headline or a text-free headline-safe area. Use logos, portraits, products, and brand references to shape a brand cover image, and refine composition, focal point, color, thumbnail clarity, and crop resilience for a publish-ready WeChat cover design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

WeChat Official Account publishers and content creators can use this skill to turn an article idea, supplied visual references, or an accepted draft into a focused article cover, then review its headline treatment, composition, and crop resilience.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared Beatra authorization covers other media generation and voice management in addition to this cover workflow.

Mitigation: Connect only if you accept that broader account access; revoke the authorization in the Beatra Console when access is no longer wanted.

Risk: Reference images selected for a cover are uploaded to Beatra.

Mitigation: Upload only images you are comfortable sending to the service.

Risk: Image generation is paid.

Mitigation: Review the final prompt and request details, then confirm before the paid image call.
