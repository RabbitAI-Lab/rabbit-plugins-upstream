## Description:

Helps data analysts plan Excel cleanup and provides ordered steps, copyable formulas, validation checks, and common pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Data analysts and spreadsheet users describe messy Excel tables and their analysis goals to receive a practical cleaning plan with formulas and checks. Users decide business-specific rules such as what counts as a duplicate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive spreadsheet contents may be included in a cleaning request.

Mitigation: Share only the necessary column descriptions and anonymized examples; omit unnecessary personal or confidential data.

Risk: Broad table-organizing or duplicate-data requests may activate the skill unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm that Excel cleaning guidance is relevant before applying the proposed steps.

Risk: Suggested duplicate and anomaly rules may not match business definitions.

Mitigation: Confirm business rules and check row counts and totals before using cleaned data.
