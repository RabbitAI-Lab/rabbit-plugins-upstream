## Description:

Helps QA, development, and product teams shift testing earlier into requirements, design, and development by producing testability review prompts, early quality gates, checklists, and intervention records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, developers, product managers, and engineering leads use this skill during requirements review, technical design, and active development to identify ambiguous requirements, testability gaps, interface risks, and missing early quality gates before defects become more expensive to fix.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on brief Chinese shift-left testing phrases when the user did not intend a full QA planning workflow.

Mitigation: Use it when requirements, design, or development review needs QA guidance, and confirm the requested scope before applying its checklists.

Risk: The skill declares Bash access, so any proposed shell command could affect the local workspace if run without review.

Mitigation: Review proposed Bash commands before execution and allow only commands that are necessary for the active QA review task.
