## Description: <br>
Reconciles a positioning canvas against shippable-stage records and claims-ledger evidence to produce a differentiation truth set with defensible claims and [needs source] gaps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and narrative teams use this skill after a positioning canvas exists to test differentiators against what the product can actually ship and what the claims ledger substantiates. It helps prepare a defensible truth set before message-house work or TALE T1 review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Pasted canvases, ledger excerpts, and scraped competitor pages can contain untrusted or misleading content. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those sources as untrusted, keep competitor messaging proxy-labeled, and require review before relying on any claim. <br>
Risk: Proposed memory or registry updates could introduce unsupported positioning or claims. <br>
Mitigation: Ask before writing memory, route unverifiable claims as proposals, and leave claim adjudication to the claims registry. <br>
