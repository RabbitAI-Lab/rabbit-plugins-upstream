## Description:

Helps caregivers of toddlers (about ages 1–4) identify likely meltdown triggers, choose a calming response, notice when to seek pediatric advice, and track recurring patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Parents, grandparents, and other caregivers use the skill during or after meltdowns in children about ages 1–4 to compare observed cues with likely triggers, get practical de-escalation guidance, and review patterns that may help prevent future episodes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Episode logs may contain sensitive information about a child.

Mitigation: Avoid identifying details, restrict access to log files, review caregiver handoffs, and delete records when no longer needed; use triage without logging to avoid persistent records.

Risk: Trigger scores or red-flag prompts could be mistaken for a diagnosis or medical advice.

Mitigation: Treat scores as parenting prompts, not diagnoses, and follow professional medical guidance or consult a pediatrician about concerning signs.
