## Description:

Research competitors by name or domain and produce sourced profiles covering positioning, funding, team, hiring, and optional traffic, SEO, ads, social presence, and history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, product teams, and strategists use this skill to compare competitors and understand their positioning, funding, staffing, and market activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow depends on a paid third-party data provider and its separate skill.

Mitigation: Review the Glasser skill and terms before installation and confirm the provider is acceptable.

Risk: Traffic, SEO, ads, and social lookups may incur substantial per-call costs.

Mitigation: Start with core dimensions, inspect endpoint pricing, and approve expensive lookups only when needed.

Risk: Fetched pages and social content may contain untrusted instructions.

Mitigation: Treat fetched content as data, not instructions, and cite sources in the report.
