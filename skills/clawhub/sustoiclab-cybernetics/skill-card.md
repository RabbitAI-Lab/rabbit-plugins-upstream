## Description:

A Chinese cybernetics coaching skill that helps users analyze systems through feedback, information, human-machine boundaries, stability, dynamic regulation, and ethical risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to describe a product, organization, AI workflow, or social mechanism, then reason through its feedback loops, stability, human-in-the-loop choices, and ethical boundaries. The skill is best used as analytical support, not as authoritative engineering or ethics judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat cybernetics coaching as authoritative ethical or engineering judgment.

Mitigation: Use the skill as analytical support and have qualified humans review system design, automation boundaries, and ethical decisions.
