## Description:

Researches public LinkedIn company, product, and showcase pages through Crawlora, including product-category discovery and directory search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, sales teams, and developers use this skill to look up public LinkedIn company, product, and showcase information or discover products by keyword and category.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and lookup IDs are sent to Crawlora and may reveal sensitive business interests.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive queries unless sharing them with Crawlora is appropriate.

Risk: The Crawlora API key is a credential, and successful requests can consume credits.

Mitigation: Keep CRAWLORA_API_KEY private and run requests only when the lookup is needed.
