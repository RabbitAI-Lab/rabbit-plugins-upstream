## Description: <br>
Guides hardware, equipment, spare-part, and secondhand-product aftersales responsibility checks by separating confirmed facts, customer claims, missing evidence, warranty or contract boundaries, and current responsibility status before any final service conclusion. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Aftersales, support, and operations teams use this skill to structure hardware responsibility reviews when evidence is incomplete. It helps keep confirmed facts, customer statements, internal assumptions, open verification items, and externally usable wording separate before escalation or solution selection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the assessment as legal advice or as authorization to deny service, approve compensation, refund, exchange, or otherwise close an aftersales case. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only as an internal responsibility-boundary aid; final legal, warranty, management, engineering, or remedy decisions must remain with authorized reviewers. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or conflicting evidence may lead to premature attribution of fault or externally risky wording. <br>
Mitigation: Require the parameter status table, preserve missing and conflicting items, separate confirmed facts from claims and assumptions, and limit output to preliminary status plus next verification steps when minimum conditions are not met. <br>
Risk: The workflow is scoped to hardware aftersales responsibility and may be misapplied to general products, services, or project responsibility questions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the appropriate general responsibility workflow for non-hardware or non-aftersales cases. <br>
