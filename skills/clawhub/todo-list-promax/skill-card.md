## Description:

Helps users capture, organize, and retrieve personal todos from chat messages and attachments, with optional daily reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[z-zihan](https://clawhub.ai/user/z-zihan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to keep a local personal todo list, query and update tasks, and optionally receive reminders for unfinished tasks with due dates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved todos may retain the full source message and attachments, including sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review saved items when messages contain sensitive information; ask to remove or clear records you do not want retained.

Risk: Daily reminders may send background notifications.

Mitigation: Enable the daily reminder only after confirming you want it, and disable it when no longer needed.
