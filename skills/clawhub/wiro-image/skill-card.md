## Description:

Generate images with a Wiro AI model picked from the catalog, with the price shown first. Use when the user wants new images made with Wiro AI or through the Wiro MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wiroai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wiroai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using an AI agent can create images through Wiro by selecting a model, reviewing its price, and providing a prompt and image settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image prompts may be sent to Wiro and could contain private information.

Mitigation: Review prompts before generation and omit sensitive or identifying information.
