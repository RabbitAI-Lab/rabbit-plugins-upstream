## Description:

Applies a product minimalism framework inspired by Zhang Xiaolong to help assess product direction, user experience, and feature priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and app developers can use the skill to evaluate product direction, user experience, and feature priorities through structured questions about user needs, simplicity, product values, and iteration. It provides decision support and self-checks rather than a launch-ready product plan or real-time market research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill primarily responds in Chinese, which may not suit every audience.

Mitigation: Confirm Chinese output fits the intended users; request bilingual output or translate it before sharing when needed.

Risk: Product discussions may contain sensitive user or business information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum needed, using anonymized or aggregated details; do not include credentials or account information.

Risk: The skill includes dated industry and regulatory observations and does not provide real-time research.

Mitigation: Check time-sensitive claims against current official sources before using them to make decisions.
