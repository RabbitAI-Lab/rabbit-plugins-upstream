## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad implicit invocation and generic trigger terms may route general writing, editing, reviewing, or bug-fix requests to this skill. <br>
Mitigation: Install it only when a general Humanizer-style productivity helper is desired, or narrow its triggers and invocation policy before deployment. <br>
Risk: The skill produces plans, checklists, code changes, shell commands, configuration guidance, and decision support that may need local review before use. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated artifacts against the user's success criteria and validate code, commands, or configuration in a controlled environment before applying them. <br>
