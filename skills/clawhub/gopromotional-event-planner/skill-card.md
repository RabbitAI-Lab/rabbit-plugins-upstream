## Description:

Plan an event backwards from its date: countdown, a 12-step planning timeline, giveaway quantities for a stand, and the last safe date to order printed or branded merchandise. Use when someone asks how long until an event, what to do when, how many giveaways to order, or when to place a merch order.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
