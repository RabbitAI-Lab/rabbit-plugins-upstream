## Description:

Uses Amazon product details and review evidence to develop evidence-based product positioning directions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product operators use this skill to derive product positioning from product details and customer reviews through the fixed product positioning workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Amazon marketplaces in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, Canada, France, Spain, and Italy.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broader account controls and inconsistent approval rules could cause unexpected credit spending or recurring charges.

Mitigation: Review spending and monitoring controls before use; prefer positioning-only operations with prior approval, disable automatic spending, use manual collection, and approve recurring plans separately.
