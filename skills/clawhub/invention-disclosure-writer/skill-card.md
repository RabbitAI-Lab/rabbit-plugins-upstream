## Description:

Turns R&D materials or an idea into a Word invention disclosure, with patent-point comparisons and a separate review-notes document.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External inventors, developers, and engineering teams use the skill to organize technical materials into an invention disclosure or explore possible invention directions from an idea. It produces a draft disclosure and review notes for checking before sharing with a patent professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: API-key collection, storage, and network handling may expose credentials.

Mitigation: Do not paste permanent API keys into chat; use a limited-spend key through the environment, and request HTTPS enforcement and protection against forwarding credentials across redirects.

Risk: Uploading confidential invention materials may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Upload confidential materials only after approving PatentMax's privacy and retention terms.
