## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Copied release workflows may grant excessive token or release permissions or download unreviewed tooling.

Mitigation: Review GitHub Actions and winget steps, token scopes, release permissions, and downloaded tooling before use.

Risk: The detailed reference material is in Chinese, which may limit review by readers who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Translate or have a Chinese-speaking reviewer check relevant sections before applying their guidance.
