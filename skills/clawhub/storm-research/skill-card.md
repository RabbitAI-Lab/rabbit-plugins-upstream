## Description: <br>
Builds a multi-perspective research map and evidence plan before content drafting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External content creators and researchers use this skill to structure a topic before writing by comparing perspectives, mapping contradictions, reviewing confidence, and planning source-backed evidence collection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can organize simulated perspectives that may be mistaken for verified facts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a research plan and verify claims with primary sources before writing or relying on them. <br>
Risk: The skill is not intended for medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Pause on high-stakes topics and use qualified review and source-backed fact checking before taking action. <br>
Risk: Unsupported social screenshots or unverifiable claims may weaken downstream research. <br>
Mitigation: Treat unsupported social screenshots as non-primary evidence and route verification questions into the evidence plan. <br>
