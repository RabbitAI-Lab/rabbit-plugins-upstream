## Description:

Drafts a founder's periodic update to investors and LPs for review, then hands it back to the founder to send.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[skillsandagentsco](https://clawhub.ai/user/skillsandagentsco)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders use this skill to draft monthly or quarterly investor and LP updates from provided or connected period materials, including asks, highlights, metrics, team updates, product updates, and review notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investor updates can contain sensitive revenue, burn, runway, customer, hiring, and fundraising details.

Mitigation: Review the generated draft carefully before sending and decide which sensitive figures are appropriate for the intended audience.

Risk: Connected or pasted Slack, email, analytics, and billing material can include untrusted text or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Use only sources the founder explicitly provides or connects, treat source material as evidence rather than instructions, and leave unsupported numbers or asks blank for founder review.
