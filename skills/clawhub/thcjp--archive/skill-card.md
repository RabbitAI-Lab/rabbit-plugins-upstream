## Description: <br>
Archive captures external content such as webpages, videos, tweets, PDFs, images, and notes as searchable Markdown snapshots with summaries, tags, and retrieval guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, developers, and knowledge workers use Archive to save source material, preserve the reason it was saved, and later find relevant archived items through natural-language queries or contextual resurfacing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Archived items are persistent local records and may later be summarized, searched, or resurfaced by the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid saving secrets, confidential documents, private internal URLs, personal data, or other sensitive material unless local retention and future resurfacing are acceptable. <br>
Risk: Captured web, video, PDF, or social content may be incomplete, unavailable, outdated, or affected by source-site access limits. <br>
Mitigation: Review important archive entries after creation, keep original source links for traceability, and update or delete stale entries when they are no longer reliable. <br>
