## Description: <br>
A full-stack workbench for B2B product managers that provides methodology guidance, product lifecycle workflows, document templates, diagram and prototype guidance, and AI product design support. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, product managers, and product teams use this skill to plan B2B products, conduct product discovery, draft PRDs/BRDs/MRDs/FRDs, design permissions and workflows, prepare go-to-market materials, and structure AI product requirements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter confidential customer data, contracts, credentials, screen contents, or regulated information into external AI tools while applying the skill's workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use only organization-approved tools, remove or mask sensitive information, and follow internal data-handling policies before sharing enterprise data. <br>
Risk: Interview and workstation-observation guidance can expose private client or employee information. <br>
Mitigation: Get consent before observing or recording screens, avoid collecting unnecessary personal data, and follow applicable company and client policies. <br>
Risk: Product, technical, legal, and commercial recommendations may be incomplete or outdated for a specific organization. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference material and validate important decisions with qualified product, engineering, legal, security, and business stakeholders. <br>
