## Description:

根据覆盖结局的完整故事概要、逐章摘要和投稿长度要求，整理300、800、1500字三档投稿梗概与人物关系摘要，保留关键因果、角色选择和结局，并分别标注实际字数；缺少章节或结局时先指出需补充的资料。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中文小说作者及投稿者，将完整故事概要和逐章摘要整理成编辑可阅读的投稿材料，呈现不同长度的完整梗概与人物关系摘要。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated plot details or character counts may not match the supplied material.

Mitigation: Review the generated facts and character counts against the source story before submitting the materials.
