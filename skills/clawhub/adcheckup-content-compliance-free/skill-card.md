## Description:

A local Chinese-language advertising-copy pre-check that flags absolute-claim wording and applies disclosed exemption handling for advertising absolute-terms guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenqg618](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenqg618)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, ecommerce, and document-preparation teams use this skill before publication to check Chinese copy for absolute advertising claims and see disclosed exemption handling. It is a pre-check only and does not replace legal review or regulator determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat a narrow absolute-claim pre-check as full advertising-law or legal clearance.

Mitigation: Use the output as a local pre-check only; route final publication decisions and areas outside the free-version scope to qualified review.

Risk: The skill does not cover medical, finance, education, real estate, broad sensitive-word, or general advertising-law compliance checks.

Mitigation: Confirm whether the copy falls into those categories before relying on the result, and use additional review for any withheld or unsupported check area.

Risk: Rule-based findings and exemptions may miss context or require human judgment.

Mitigation: Review findings, exemptions, and cited basis against the full advertisement context before publication.
