## Description:

用于敏感词检测、违禁词检测、文案发布前检查、内容安全检查、文案合规审核、能不能发判断、平台风险提示和改写建议。支持小红书、抖音、快手和通用文本场景，来自 SocialDataX 社媒数据助手。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and agents preparing social-platform copy use this skill to check draft text for sensitive or prohibited wording before publication. It reports violation status, risk level, matched categories, highlights, and rewrite suggestions for generic, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and Kuaishou contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided text is sent to SocialDataX and may be stored server-side for history, billing, and troubleshooting.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting secrets or highly sensitive private content unless that handling is acceptable for the intended use case.
