## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SocialDataX retains submitted drafts and structured results, and API use may incur charges.

Mitigation: Submit only drafts approved for external processing and review applicable account charges before use.

Risk: The downloaded helper is not version-pinned, and the external helper was not included in the inspected artifact.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, fixed helper version and restrict its access to the credentials and files it needs.
