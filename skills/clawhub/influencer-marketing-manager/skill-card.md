## Description:

Provides expert management for influencer, creator, KOL, UGC, and ambassador partnerships, from goal framing and evidence-led qualification through outreach, negotiation, fulfillment, measurement, and iteration under approved rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing and business users use this skill to manage influencer partnership workflows, including strategy, creator discovery and qualification, outreach drafting, reply handling, negotiation preparation, fulfillment tracking, measurement, and learning. It is intended for work that needs business judgment, relationship progress, or autonomous execution under approved rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help draft and potentially send creator outreach under approved rules, which can affect recipients, sender reputation, and campaign commitments.

Mitigation: Review project rules before use and verify recipient, sender, message version, links, attachments, consent, suppression state, and scope before any external send.

Risk: Influencer negotiations can create material commitments around price, deliverables, rights, exclusivity, payment, budget, schedule, or contract language.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation before externalizing any material commitment or changed commercial package.

Risk: Creator data, contact signals, and performance metrics can be incomplete, stale, or misleading if used without source context.

Mitigation: Keep decisions evidence-led, preserve source and observation time, separate creator fit from contact readiness, and treat missing or default-looking values as unknown until the source defines them.
