## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to structure complex choices, compare alternatives under uncertainty, estimate expected value, and present decision trees with qualitative risk context. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Expected-value results can be mistaken for financial, legal, medical, or business advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a structured thinking aid and keep final decisions subject to domain expertise, risk tolerance, and user judgment. <br>
Risk: The calculator can read JSON files and save output to user-provided paths. <br>
Mitigation: Run it only on files and save paths that the user intentionally chooses. <br>
Risk: Subjective probabilities and values can make results unstable or misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Check that probabilities sum to 100%, make worst cases explicit, and present qualitative context alongside the expected-value ranking. <br>
