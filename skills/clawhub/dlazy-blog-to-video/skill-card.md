## Description:

Converts a blog post into a narrated video with a storyboard, voiceover, and build.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams can turn a blog post supplied as text or a link into a narrated video for social media or YouTube.
