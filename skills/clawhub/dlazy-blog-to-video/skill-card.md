## Description:

Convert a blog post provided as text or a link into a narrated video with a storyboard and voiceover.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who want to adapt a blog post for social media or YouTube can use this skill to create a narrated video. They can provide the post as text or a link and continue work in a project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Messages and selected attachments are sent to dLazy, and video generation can consume account credits.

Mitigation: Send only content you intend to share with the service, choose the destination project carefully, and consider credit use before generating.
