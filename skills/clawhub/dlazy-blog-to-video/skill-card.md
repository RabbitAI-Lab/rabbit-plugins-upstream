## Description:

Turns blog posts or articles into narrated videos with storyboards and voiceovers for social media or YouTube.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a blog post, article, or document into a narrated video with a storyboard and voiceover.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and options are sent to an external hosted service, and attached files may be uploaded there.

Mitigation: Obtain approval before sending confidential or regulated documents; review what is shared.

Risk: Login may store a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local key and rotate it if exposure is suspected.

Risk: A global CLI installation persists on the system.

Mitigation: Use the documented npx option if a global installation is undesirable.
