## Description:

Helps creators turn blog posts and other documents into narrated videos with storyboards and voiceover through dLazy's hosted workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to request narrated videos from blog posts, articles, or documents for social media, explainers, and training.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The blog-to-video label understates the broader file/document-to-video workflow.

Mitigation: Review the actual requested workflow and attached materials before starting a project.

Risk: Attached files are uploaded to dLazy's service.

Mitigation: Attach only files you are comfortable uploading to dLazy.

Risk: Installing the CLI globally or saving an API key creates persistent local state.

Mitigation: Verify the npm package before installation; consider per-run npx and DLAZY_API_KEY instead.
