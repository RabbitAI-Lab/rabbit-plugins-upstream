## Description:

Consolidates software concept design discussions into a proposal for confirmation, covering goals, scope, concept specifications, synchronization rules, tradeoffs, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and unresolved issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use the skill to consolidate software design discussions into a reviewable concept proposal. It organizes decisions, scenarios, specifications, tradeoffs, assumptions, and open questions for confirmation before further work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposed design may include incorrect details or unresolved assumptions.

Mitigation: Review the proposal and its assumptions before confirming it; authorize implementation and publication separately.
