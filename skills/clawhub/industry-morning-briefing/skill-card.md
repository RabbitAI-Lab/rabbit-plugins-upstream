## Description: <br>
Generate a structured morning briefing for a given industry using public web search and page fetch, focused on current highlights, policy and regulation, supply chain and company moves, implications, and watch items. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, developers, and analysts use this skill to ask an agent for a concise daily industry news briefing with source-backed highlights and follow-up watch items. It is intended for current public information summaries, not historical research, investment advice, or vendor-specific promotion. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public web search results can be incomplete, stale, or misleading for fast-moving industry news. <br>
Mitigation: Cite source URLs for factual claims, prefer today or yesterday's sources, and state dates explicitly when current results are limited. <br>
Risk: Industry briefings may be mistaken for financial or investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep summaries factual, avoid price predictions, stock picks, or financial forecasts, and remind users to verify sources for decisions. <br>
