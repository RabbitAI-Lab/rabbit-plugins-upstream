## Description: <br>
Reviews lost or interrupted customer opportunities from project evidence, separating direct causes, deeper causes, controllable factors, uncontrollable factors, and items that still need verification. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, customer management, and account teams use this skill to review stalled or lost opportunities from confirmed project facts, customer feedback, and related evidence. It helps distinguish facts from assumptions before turning a case into future adjustment rules. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may produce incorrect or misleading lost-deal conclusions if customer records, project facts, or feedback are incomplete or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Provide approved, preferably anonymized project evidence and verify conclusions before using them for sales policy or operational decisions. <br>
Risk: A single case could be overgeneralized into an absolute rule, or a lack of response could be treated as a confirmed lost deal. <br>
Mitigation: Keep uncertain items marked for verification, preserve source conflicts, and require human confirmation before forming repeatable rules. <br>
