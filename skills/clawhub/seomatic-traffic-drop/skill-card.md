## Description:

Diagnoses organic traffic drops using connected Google Search Console data, checks indexing and competition, and prioritizes evidence-backed fixes for approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to investigate traffic or ranking losses, distinguish seasonal changes from actual declines, and prioritize fixes supported by connected site data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting a site gives the skill access to its Search Console and related SEO data.

Mitigation: Install only for a site whose SEO data you intend to connect to SEOmatic.

Risk: Approved SEOmatic tasks may execute automatically on paid plans.

Mitigation: Review each staged task before approving it, and never approve a task without the user's specific consent.

Risk: Recent Search Console figures may be provisional, and unsupported conclusions can lead to incorrect fixes.

Mitigation: Quote the dates and source numbers, check seasonality and indexing, and state what could not be verified.
