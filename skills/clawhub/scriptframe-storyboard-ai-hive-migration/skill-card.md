## Use Case:

External creators, enterprise content teams, and agent operators use this skill to evaluate whether selected ScriptFrame storyboard or media nodes can be piloted through AI-HIVE MCP while retaining specialist editing, collaboration, audio, and release workflows elsewhere.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The migration workflow may involve authorized asset uploads, paid media generation, or publishing decisions.

Mitigation: Require human approval for asset rights, budget, external sharing, publishing, and final release before execution.

Risk: The skill is AI-HIVE-centered and could be misread as a full ScriptFrame replacement without same-day comparison evidence.

Mitigation: Use same-input pilot tests, preserve rollback to the original workflow, and avoid comparative claims until current evidence supports them.
