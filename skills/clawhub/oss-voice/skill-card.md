## Description:

Guides the writing and maintenance of open-source repository documentation, including multilingual READMEs, changelogs, contribution guides, and installation docs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qikairo7](https://clawhub.ai/user/qikairo7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and maintainers use this skill to draft or revise repository documentation while preserving the project's language conventions, document structure, and consistency across translations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic synchronization may alter multilingual files beyond the intended edit.

Mitigation: Specify target files and languages, and confirm whether counterpart documents should be updated before applying changes.

Risk: Default language and naming choices for new repositories may not fit the intended audience.

Mitigation: Confirm the repository's audience and preferred naming convention before creating new language variants.
