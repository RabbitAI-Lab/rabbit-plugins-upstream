## Description:

Map candidate skills to an interview plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters and hiring teams use supplied candidate and role profiles to identify required skill matches, gaps, and interview discussion topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Candidate and role profiles may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Provide only the details needed for planning to the local agent workflow.

Risk: A skills match based on supplied profile data does not verify a candidate's ability.

Mitigation: Review the plan and assess skills through the interview rather than treating matches as verified qualifications.
