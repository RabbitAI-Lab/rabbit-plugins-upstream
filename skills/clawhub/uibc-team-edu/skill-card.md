## Use Case:

UIBC education-domain reviewers and agents use this skill to document identity judgments after memory transfer, assemble reproducible evidence, and assign responsibility for failed judgments under TH-EDU-001.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad education and accountability terms may trigger the skill outside its intended judging context.

Mitigation: Confirm the request concerns UIBC education-domain judging under TH-EDU-001 before relying on its output.

Risk: A passing criteria check confirms document structure, not the identity of a migrated agent.

Mitigation: Review the migration evidence independently before accepting an identity or accountability judgment.
