External users and home-renovation planners use this skill to get Chinese-language guidance on tile, wood-look tile, flooring, skirting, threshold stone, kitchen and bathroom tile, installation details, and budget-aware purchasing choices. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes broad, opinionated home-renovation advice that may touch structural, electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, balcony, or demolition decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and require qualified local professionals, code checks, and property-management approvals before acting on safety-critical renovation work.

Risk: The bundled corpus may contain unsafe DIY guidance or advice that is too general for a specific property.

Mitigation: Ask for project context, surface uncertainty, and avoid presenting recommendations as final technical instructions for high-risk work.

Risk: The corpus may contain leftover promotional contact details or purchase prompts.

Mitigation: Do not surface promotional contact details, and advise users to verify vendors, materials, prices, and warranties independently.
