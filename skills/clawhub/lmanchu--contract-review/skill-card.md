## Description: <br>
Legal contract analysis using CUAD dataset (41 risk categories). Supports NDA, SaaS, M&A, employment, payment/merchant, and finder/broker agreements. Identifies red flags, suggests redlines, compares to market standards. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[lmanchu](https://clawhub.ai/user/lmanchu) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, legal operations teams, and business reviewers use this skill to review contracts, extract key terms, flag red flags, and draft negotiation-focused redline suggestions. It is informational only and is not a substitute for qualified legal counsel. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Legal agreements may contain sensitive or privileged information. <br>
Mitigation: Use copies of important agreements when possible and avoid exposing unnecessary sensitive content. <br>
Risk: Generated redlines or legal-risk interpretations may be incorrect, incomplete, or jurisdiction-dependent. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated edits before accepting them and involve qualified counsel for material terms. <br>
Risk: File edit permissions support redline drafting but can modify agreement files. <br>
Mitigation: Work from copies or version-controlled documents and inspect all proposed edits before applying them. <br>
