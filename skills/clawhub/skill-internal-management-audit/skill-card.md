## Description: <br>
Guides users through a four-stage internal management audit workflow for ISO 9001/IATF 16949 contexts and produces printable TXT and Markdown audit documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality managers, internal auditors, and audit coordinators use this skill to plan internal audits, build implementation plans and department checklists, and draft internal audit reports from user-provided audit information and optional uploaded manuals or records. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Uploaded audit manuals, procedures, and prior audit records may contain sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Use an appropriate working directory and review generated documents before sharing them outside the intended audit audience. <br>
Risk: Generated local files may conflict with existing filenames because overwrite safeguards are not documented. <br>
Mitigation: Check the working directory for existing audit files and keep backups before generating replacement documents. <br>
Risk: The skill drafts audit documents but does not replace authorized audit judgment, conclusions, or signatures. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified enterprise personnel review findings, classifications, conclusions, approvals, and any customer-specific requirements before use. <br>
