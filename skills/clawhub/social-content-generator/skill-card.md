## Description: <br>
Helps agents create, schedule, optimize, and repurpose social media content across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and related platforms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and business teams use this skill to draft platform-specific posts, threads, captions, content calendars, and engagement strategies. It supports planning and optimization for social campaigns before any publishing action is approved. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can guide public posting, scheduling, comments, or direct messages without clearly scoping user approval. <br>
Mitigation: Require an explicit preview and approval before any post, schedule, comment, or direct message is sent through connected tools. <br>
Risk: The skill discusses collecting social-platform data at scale, which may violate platform rules or privacy expectations if done without authorization. <br>
Mitigation: Collect social-platform data only through authorized, compliant methods and review platform terms before using scraping or automation tools. <br>
