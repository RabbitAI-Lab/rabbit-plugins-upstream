## Description: <br>
Compares an acceptance checklist and test cases item by item, then produces pass, conditional pass, or fail conclusions with defect details. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
QA reviewers, developers, and release owners use this skill to evaluate functional acceptance criteria against test cases, classify defects by severity, and summarize readiness decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated acceptance conclusions may be incorrect or may overstate release readiness. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as review guidance only; the responsible owner should confirm conclusions and inspect defect details before making acceptance decisions. <br>
