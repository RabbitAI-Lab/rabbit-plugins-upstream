## Description:

Plan and inspect protected content areas for 9:16 Douyin video or image-post layouts, or create a transparent reference overlay. Treat included measurements as adjustable references and verify against the current publishing preview.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan text and graphic placement for Douyin video, image-post, and profile-cover layouts, and to create a transparent guide for checking composition.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference margins may not match current app controls, captions, devices, or playback crops.

Mitigation: Adjust the margins for the target content and verify the layout in the current publishing preview.

Risk: The transparent guide could be included in exported content by mistake.

Mitigation: Hide or remove the guide before export, then inspect the final asset at its intended display size.
