## Description:

Generate structured user personas from interview, survey, or observation data for UX and human factors coursework. Produces goals, pain points, behaviors, scenarios, and design opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zxw-wwwww](https://clawhub.ai/user/zxw-wwwww)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, UX practitioners, product designers, and researchers use this skill to turn interview notes, survey results, observations, or user feedback into 1-3 structured personas for user modeling, needs analysis, and design decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persona generation may involve interview, survey, or observation data that includes personal identifiers.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing unnecessary personal identifiers; use pseudonyms and omit real names, contact details, and other sensitive information.

Risk: Personas can blur user-provided evidence with assumptions when source data is incomplete.

Mitigation: Clearly label assumptions and distinguish them from real research evidence in the generated personas.
