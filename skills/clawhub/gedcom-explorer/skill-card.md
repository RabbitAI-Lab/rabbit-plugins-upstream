## Description: <br>
Generate an interactive family tree dashboard from any GEDCOM (.ged) file as a single-file HTML app with dashboard, tree, people, timeline, daily alerts, search, and person detail views. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[justinhartbiz](https://clawhub.ai/user/justinhartbiz) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, and family-history users use this skill to convert local GEDCOM files into an interactive HTML dashboard for exploring genealogy data, family relationships, timelines, and anniversaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated dashboards can embed private family-history data, including living-person details. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated HTML as private and review or redact sensitive details before sharing, emailing, syncing, or hosting it. <br>
Risk: Opening the generated dashboard can contact Google through the included Google Fonts link. <br>
Mitigation: For stricter privacy, remove or block the Google Fonts link before distributing or opening the dashboard. <br>
