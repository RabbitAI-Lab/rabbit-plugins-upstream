## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill for Kazakhstan tax, accounting, customs, and compliance questions, including VAT, corporate income tax, personal tax, social contributions, transfer pricing, permanent establishment, import/export procedures, and official-source lookup. It helps turn official laws and regulator materials into Chinese-language guidance with Russian terminology and source notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax, accounting, customs, and payment guidance may be time-sensitive or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Verify important conclusions against current official Kazakhstan sources and consult a licensed local accountant or tax adviser before major transactions or filings.

Risk: The skill may direct users to government, banking, tax, customs, or payment portals where sensitive personal, business, or payment information could be entered.

Mitigation: Independently verify portal URLs and recipient details before submitting credentials, taxpayer information, or payment instructions.
