## Description:

Helps freelancers track overdue invoices, schedule payment reminders, estimate late fees, assess small-claims readiness, and draft demand and stop-work notices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Freelancers and small studios use this skill to organize unpaid invoices, prepare measured collection communications, and evaluate possible next steps before seeking local advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, United Kingdom, Germany, and Ontario (Canada) reference jurisdictions; verify local rules before use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Jurisdiction caps, fees, deadlines, and late-charge estimates may be outdated or inapplicable.

Mitigation: Check current local law and the contract, and seek qualified local advice before charging fees or filing a claim.

Risk: Demand or stop-work notices may not fit the parties' agreement or circumstances.

Mitigation: Review each draft against the contract and applicable local requirements before sending it.
