## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through weekly interest exploration, with consent-based tracking of how their curiosity holds up when they face difficulties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students in middle primary school through high school use this skill to explore interests through weekly activities and reflections; younger students need the stated guardian consent and supervision. It can offer a single unrecorded conversation when tracking consent is absent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language K–12 contexts)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent interest records about minors may be collected or shared without informed consent.

Mitigation: Explain retention and sharing in Chinese before tracking, obtain the student's and required guardian's consent, and support pause, review, export, correction, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Short observation periods can lead to premature judgments about a student's interests.

Mitigation: Treat early observations as tentative; do not make definitive interest judgments in the primary-school eight-week format.
