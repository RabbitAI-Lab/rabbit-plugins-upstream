## Description: <br>
X Design helps agents turn visual-design requests into polished HTML-based deliverables, including slide decks, prototypes, animations, design systems, and review passes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, and content teams use this skill to create visual artifacts from rough prompts, source material, brand references, or structured data. It supports presentation decks, UI prototypes, animated HTML, social graphics, design-system extraction, and visual quality review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may perform web lookups and fetch brand, product, or public-entity assets for visual-design work. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing private brand materials, proprietary repositories, sensitive screenshots, or confidential product details unless they are appropriate for the design task. <br>
Risk: Generated visual assets, layouts, exports, and HTML files may contain inaccurate or unsuitable content if the prompt or references are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated deliverables before publication, especially branded pages, presentations, product claims, and public-facing exports. <br>
Risk: The skill can create or modify HTML, design files, and supporting assets in the workspace. <br>
Mitigation: Run normal source review and scanning before deployment or sharing generated files externally. <br>
