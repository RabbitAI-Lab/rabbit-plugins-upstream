## Description:

SN Data by Shikshan Nivesh: Form 4 insider trades and Form 144 planned insider sales for US-listed companies, each linked to its SEC filing. Use when a question asks who bought or sold a company's shares inside the company.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shikshannivesh](https://clawhub.ai/user/shikshannivesh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, researchers, and developers can look up reported purchases, sales, and planned sales by insiders at US-listed companies, then cite the related SEC filings. Present the information with its fiscal period and source credit; it is not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insider-trade records could be mistaken for a recommendation or presented without enough filing context.

Mitigation: Quote figures as returned with the fiscal period, link the SEC filing, credit Data by Shikshan Nivesh, and state that the information is not investment advice.
