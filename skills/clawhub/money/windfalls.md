# Windfalls — Bonus, Inheritance, Vest, Sale

**Before answering**, read `## Debt Plan`, `## Goals` and `## Money Shape` in `~/Clawic/data/money/memory.md`. A windfall is allocated by the ladder against the household's actual position; advice given without the debt rates is guessing at step 3.

A windfall is the rare moment when a household can move several ladder steps at once. It is also when the largest single mistakes get made, because the money arrives without a plan attached and the decision gets made under social pressure and a short clock.

## The Sequence

1. **Withhold the tax before anything else.** Move the estimated liability into a separate account the day the money lands, and treat it as not-yours (`self-employed.md` account structure). Equity vests and asset sales are the usual traps — withholding at a flat supplemental rate often falls short of the recipient's marginal rate, and the shortfall surfaces at filing when the money is gone (`taxes.md`).
2. **Park the rest for 30-90 days** in instant-access cash. Nothing is decided in the first week. The parking period is the single highest-value step here, and it costs nothing but interest.
3. **Say nothing to anyone who does not need to know.** Disclosure changes relationships and generates requests that arrive faster than plans do.
4. **Then walk the ladder in order** (SKILL.md). A windfall does not get its own rules; it accelerates the plan that already exists.
5. **Allow one deliberate, capped celebration** — a stated percentage, decided before the allocation. A plan with zero enjoyment gets abandoned, and an uncapped one absorbs everything.
6. **Write the allocation down before executing it**, with the reason and the rejected alternatives.

## Allocation Against The Ladder

| Position | Where the windfall goes |
|---|---|
| Any priority arrears (tier 1 in `debt.md`) | There first, in full, whatever the rate |
| Debt above `high_interest_rate_pct` | Clear it entirely; a 22% balance retired is a guaranteed 22% (Rule 1) |
| Buffer below `emergency_fund_months` | Fill it to target |
| A goal with a date inside 5 years | Fund it in cash or short bonds, never in equities (Rule 5) |
| Everything funded | Long-term investing per the written policy; a lump sum invested at once beats averaging in about two thirds of the time (`investing.md`) |
| Mid-rate debt below the line | Prepayment competes here with investing, on after-tax real rates (`housing.md`) |

**A windfall does not raise the standard of living.** Converting a one-off into a recurring commitment — a bigger flat, a car on finance, a school — spends it many times over. If the household wants a permanent change, fund it from the income the windfall generates, not from the windfall.

## By Source

| Source | The specific trap |
|---|---|
| Annual bonus | Treated as salary and pre-spent. Budget from the floor month; the bonus is a windfall each time it arrives (`income.md`) |
| Equity vest | Under-withholding, plus the concentration that comes from not selling. Vested public shares are cash that chose one company — the test is whether you would buy that stock today at that price |
| Inheritance | Grief and timing. Nothing is decided during probate or in the first months; there is no deadline, and the pressure to "do something with Mum's money" is emotional, not financial. Also check whether the estate carries debts and whether any tax falls on the estate or the recipient in `country` |
| Property sale | Large, taxable in some cases, and immediately targeted. Know the tax position before completion, not after |
| Business sale | The largest and most professional-advice-dependent of these. Red Flags: structure, timing and reliefs are decided before the sale, and afterwards nothing can be changed |
| Legal settlement | Often intended to fund a specific future cost — care, lost earnings, adaptation. Ring-fence that portion before any allocation |
| Redundancy payment | It is a buffer, not a bonus: it funds the gap to the next income (`shocks.md`). Treat it as the emergency fund until employment resumes |
| Lottery, crypto gain, gambling win | Highest failure rate of any source, because the money arrives with no accompanying habits. Park it longer — 90 days minimum — and use a professional for anything life-changing |
| Gift from family | Check whether it is a gift or an expectation, and whether `country` runs a clock on gifts before death (`taxes.md`). Undocumented family money is the most common source of later conflict (`household.md`) |

## The Requests

Any windfall large enough to be known about generates requests. Decide the policy before the first one, because deciding case-by-case under pressure is what damages relationships.

- Set a **total** annual figure for family help and give from that pot, rather than judging each request on merit. The pot is the answer, and it is not a negotiation about whether someone deserves it.
- A loan to family is a gift with paperwork in most outcomes. If it is genuinely a loan, write the amount, schedule and consequence down and expect to enforce none of it.
- Guaranteeing or co-signing someone's borrowing is not a favour, it is taking on the debt with none of the control. Treat a guarantee request as a request for the full amount in cash, and answer it that way.
- "No" is a complete answer, and giving it once is cheaper than giving it repeatedly (`scams.md` — affinity pressure).

## Scale Changes The Advice

Above roughly two years of household income, a windfall stops being an allocation problem and becomes a structuring problem: tax across years, estate consequences, whether entities are involved, and how the money is held. That is the Red Flags line — route to a qualified adviser in `country`, on a **flat fee or hourly basis**, and go with a written list of questions. Percentage-of-assets pricing applied to a new lump sum is the most expensive form of advice available at exactly the moment the household has the least experience (`scams.md`).

What an agent can still do at that scale: keep the parking period, hold the ladder order, write down what was decided and why, and make sure the tax reserve was moved on day one.

**Write it down.** The allocation, the amounts, the reason and the rejected alternatives go as a row in `~/Clawic/data/money/decisions/<year>.md`; anything that changed a balance or cleared a debt updates `~/Clawic/data/finances/accounts.md` and `## Debt Plan`, and the new position updates `## Net Worth` in `~/Clawic/data/money/memory.md`. The family-help policy, once set, is an artifact at `~/Clawic/data/money/artifacts/family-help-policy.md` with its `## Boxes` line in the same turn — it is written once and read every time a request arrives.
