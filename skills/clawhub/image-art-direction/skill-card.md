## Description:

Turns single-image requests into coherent visual specifications and prompts, and supports image generation, editing, critique, and targeted revision when requested.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mc0571](https://clawhub.ai/user/mc0571)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creative teams use this skill to direct a single static image, compile a usable image prompt, or review and revise an image while preserving specified subjects and reference details. It does not cover UI, charts, logos, video, or multi-image product campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An ambiguous request may result in a prompt instead of a generated or edited image, or in an unexpected response language.

Mitigation: Specify whether you want a prompt, an image, an edit, or a critique, and state your preferred language.
