## Description:

Helps assess privacy-computing AI systems against LGD identity, evidence, and approval-gate checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and compliance teams use the skill to self-assess AI systems handling encrypted or minimized data, identify missing governance evidence, and review a suggested release gate. It does not replace professional compliance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Checklist scores or keyword-based assessments may be mistaken for proof of legal compliance or cryptographic security.

Mitigation: Verify the underlying evidence and obtain qualified professional review before making compliance or release decisions.

Risk: Future versions may request broad agent tools such as Write or WebFetch.

Mitigation: Review each new version and grant only the tools needed for the intended task.
