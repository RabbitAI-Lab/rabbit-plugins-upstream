## Description:

Helps adults organize advance care preferences, medical authorization, decision-maker roles, family-conflict steps, and questions for professional review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adults can use this skill to prepare a structured advance care planning draft covering treatment preferences, medical decision-maker authorization, links to voluntary guardianship, emergency decision steps, family disagreements, and questions for local legal and clinical professionals. Its outputs are planning aids and require qualified review before reliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China; formal requirements vary by locality and should be reviewed under applicable local rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on planning guidance as legal or medical advice, including on local authorization, witnessing, or treatment requirements.

Mitigation: Have a qualified local lawyer and clinician review authorization, witnessing, conflict-resolution, and treatment-preference wording before relying on any output.

Risk: Advance care planning may involve sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Share only the personal information needed for planning.
