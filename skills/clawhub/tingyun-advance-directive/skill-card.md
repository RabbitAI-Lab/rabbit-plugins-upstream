## Description:

Provides adults with structured advance care planning checklists for treatment preferences, medical decision authorization, emergency decision workflows, and questions for legal and clinical review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adults use this skill to organize advance care preferences and prepare draft checklists and questions for discussion with local legal and clinical professionals. It provides planning support only; outputs require professional review before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (local rules vary)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal and health information may be included in planning conversations.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the planning task.

Risk: A planning draft may be mistaken for legally or medically reliable advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and obtain local legal and clinical review before relying on them.
