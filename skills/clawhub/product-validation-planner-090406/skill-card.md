## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning before investing heavily. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to structure product idea validation, produce actionable plans or prototypes, and prepare user-facing positioning. It is intended for lightweight advisory planning that remains feasible on ordinary local hardware. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to influence product-validation or startup-planning conversations when the user did not explicitly request it. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow trigger keywords or require explicit invocation in environments where accidental activation would be disruptive. <br>
Risk: Advisory product-validation output may still contain assumptions or incomplete market guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, constraints, and validation criteria before using the output to make product or investment decisions. <br>
