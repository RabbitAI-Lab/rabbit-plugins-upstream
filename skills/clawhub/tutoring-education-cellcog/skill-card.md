## Description: <br>
AI tutoring and education powered by CellCog for study guides, exam prep, coding tutorials, language learning, math help, science explanations, practice problems, and interactive lessons using diagrams, analogies, and worked examples. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External learners, educators, and developers use this skill to ask CellCog for tutoring outputs such as concept explanations, homework walkthroughs, study guides, practice problems, coding tutorials, and language practice across subjects. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts and study materials sent through the skill may include private documents, code, school records, or account data. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user trusts CellCog for the materials being sent and avoid submitting sensitive content unless appropriate approvals and data handling expectations are in place. <br>
Risk: Live use requires a CellCog API key and dependency installation. <br>
Mitigation: Install dependencies from trusted sources, protect CELLCOG_API_KEY, and rotate or revoke the key if it may have been exposed. <br>
