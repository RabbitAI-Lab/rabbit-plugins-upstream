# Practical Feeding Guide

## Reading a Food Label

Find the **metabolizable energy** statement:

> "Metabolizable Energy: 3,850 kcal/kg (412 kcal/cup), 370 kcal/can"

- Use the **kcal/kg** figure with this calculator (cups vary by kibble shape/density).
- "Crude protein/fat" minimums tell you little about calories; fat drives energy density (fat ≈ 8.5 kcal/g vs protein/carb ≈ 3.5 kcal/g).
- Diet/light foods: look for kcal/kg AND check protein stays adequate for weight loss (dogs >25% DM, cats >35% DM — cats are obligate carnivores).

## Meal Frequency

| Animal | Meals/day |
|---|---|
| Puppy <6 months | 3-4 |
| Puppy 6-12 months | 2-3 |
| Adult dog | 2 |
| Adult cat | 2-4 (cats prefer multiple small meals; puzzle feeders help) |
| Senior | 2 (smaller, if appetite flags) |

Split the calculator's grams/day across meals evenly.

## Food Transition Schedule (7-day)

| Days | New food | Old food |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 25% | 75% |
| 3-4 | 50% | 50% |
| 5-6 | 75% | 25% |
| 7+ | 100% | — |

Sensitive-stomach pets: double the schedule (14 days). Add a tablespoon of
plain canned pumpkin (not pie filling) if stools soften — fiber, not medicine.

## Switching Between Food Types

- **Dry → wet:** wet is ~70-80% water; calories come per can, not per kg of
  dry matter. Convert: daily grams wet = MER ÷ (kcal per can ÷ grams per can).
- **Wet → dry:** watch total calories, cats especially — free-feeding dry
  after wet portions is the classic obesity path.
- **Mixed feeding:** compute dry portion for the remaining calories after
  cans are accounted for.

## Treat Budget (hard rule: ≤10% of MER)

The report reserves 10% of calories for treats; subtract from kibble:

```
kibble_kcal = MER − treats_kcal
```

Use pieces of the daily kibble ration as training treats where possible.
Break commercial treats into pea-sized pieces — dogs count events, not grams.

## Cost Comparison

Given food price per bag and bag size:

```
monthly_cost = (grams/day × 30.4 ÷ 1000) × (price ÷ bag_kg)
```

Reality checks: premium dry for a 30kg dog ≈ €45-70/month; wet-only feeding
a 5kg cat often costs 2-3× dry. Prescription diet premiums (renal, GI,
hydrolyzed) run 1.5-3× standard premium — compare against home-prepared only
with a boarded nutritionist formulation.

## Weight-Loss Plan (how to run it)

1. Vet check first — rule out hypothyroidism (dogs), medication effects.
2. Set target weight (BCS math or vet's number).
3. Feed at the calculator's weight-loss calories **measured by scale**.
4. Everyone in the household signs the no-extra-treats pact (the failure mode #1).
5. Weigh every 2 weeks. Dog losing >2%/week → +10% food; <0.5%/week → −10%.
6. Cats: unlimited-plan B — some do better on multiple puzzle feeders with
   the same total calories.
7. Recompute portions every kg lost (calories fall with weight).
8. Expected duration: a 40kg→30kg dog at ~1.5%/week ≈ 16-20 weeks.

## Red Flags — Call the Vet

- Cat refusing food >24-48h (lipidosis risk)
- Dog losing >3%/week on the plan
- Vomiting/diarrhea >2 days during food transition
- Sudden appetite change in seniors (dental, renal, hyperthyroid signals)
- Drinking/urinating much more than usual (diabetes/kidney signals)

## Water

Always-fresh water, especially on dry diets. Rough needs: dogs ~50ml/kg/day,
cats ~45ml/kg/day — cats on wet food drink visibly less (normal).
