Developers and agent operators use this skill to create and improve boss-skills-style workflows from workplace source material while adding verification, reflection, and learning hooks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process real chats, email, meeting notes, or company documents.

Mitigation: Use approved or redacted material and review generated profiles before relying on or sharing them.

Risk: The skill may create local skill files and persistent learner records without clear retention limits.

Mitigation: Run it only in workspaces where generated boss-related files are acceptable, and inspect or remove learner notes when they are no longer needed.
