## Description:

Lingala-focused guidance-only skill that helps users register for a PowPow Bubble account, troubleshoot sign-up and activation issues, and understand subscription activation without collecting credentials or performing actions for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External PowPow users and support agents use this skill to guide Lingala-speaking users through product introduction, account registration, sign-up troubleshooting, and subscription activation. The skill provides guidance only; users complete forms and payments themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The assistant could ask for or receive sensitive registration, verification, or payment information while guiding the user.

Mitigation: Keep all form entry and payment steps on the official PowPow pages, and do not request passwords, email verification codes, or payment credentials.

Risk: The assistant could imply that subscription payment or activation was completed when manual or external confirmation is still pending.

Mitigation: Describe subscription steps as guidance only, distinguish automatic and manual activation paths, and direct users to wait or contact platform support when activation remains pending.
