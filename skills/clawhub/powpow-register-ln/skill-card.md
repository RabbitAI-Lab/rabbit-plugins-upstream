## Description:

Guides Lingala-speaking users through PowPow registration, signup troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users can learn about the service, follow registration steps, resolve common signup problems, and understand how to activate a subscription. Users complete registration and payment themselves on the PowPow site or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose passwords, email verification codes, or payment details during signup.

Mitigation: Never request those details; direct users to enter them themselves on the linked PowPow pages.

Risk: Signup or payment links outside the disclosed PowPow pages could misdirect users.

Mitigation: Use only the linked PowPow pages for registration and payment.
