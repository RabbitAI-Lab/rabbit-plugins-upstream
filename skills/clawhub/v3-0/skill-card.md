## Description: <br>
九维雷达V3.0终极选股系统 is a China A-share stock-screening skill that applies a nine-dimensional scoring framework to rank stock pools, analyze single stocks, construct portfolios, and discuss position sizing and risk controls. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to evaluate China A-share candidates with a nine-factor scoring method, produce ranked stock-screening reports or single-stock analyses, and structure portfolio and risk-control guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill gives specific stock-screening and trading guidance with confident return or doubling-potential claims. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational analysis, verify all market data independently, consider personal risk tolerance, and do not rely on the skill alone for real trading decisions. <br>
Risk: Optional automated scans depend on external market data access and may use delayed, incomplete, or rate-limited data. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check results with independent data sources and manually review top-ranked candidates before acting. <br>
