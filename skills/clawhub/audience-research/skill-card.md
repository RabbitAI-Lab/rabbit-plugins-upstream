## Description: <br>
Develops a deep, usable audience profile for a brand using jobs-to-be-done, pains, objections, and voice-of-customer language from available evidence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External content teams, marketers, and brand operators use this skill to turn a brand profile and available audience evidence into an operational audience profile for downstream content planning and writing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Audience research may capture private customer details, sensitive support records, DMs, or sales-call notes in the language bank. <br>
Mitigation: Use public or authorized sources, redact names and identifiers, and avoid including sensitive customer details unless organizational policy permits that use. <br>
Risk: Thin audience evidence can lead to unsupported audience claims or invented customer language. <br>
Mitigation: Mark assumptions as hypotheses to validate and do not present guessed language or pains as confirmed findings. <br>
