# Product campaign image quality checklist

## Product fidelity

- The silhouette, proportions, material, color, cap, controls, and label geometry match the approved reference.
- No extra product parts, duplicate products, invented packaging text, or warped details are present.
- Reflections, shadows, contact points, and scale are physically credible.

## Campaign communication

- The frame communicates one message and one intended action.
- The product is recognizable at likely mobile display size.
- Props and setting support the benefit instead of competing with the product.
- The copy-safe and CTA-safe regions remain visually calm.

## Copy and claims

- Every visible word matches the approved copy character for character.
- Claims, prices, discounts, certifications, and review language are supplied and approved.
- Required label information and warnings are not altered or hidden.

## Format adaptation

- Critical content remains inside the crop-safe core for every requested ratio.
- Each export uses the requested pixel dimensions and color profile.
- Photographic and mixed-media finals use a high-quality WebP unless the placement requires another format.
- Filenames identify the campaign, placement, and aspect ratio.

## Rights and safety

- No unapproved logo, slogan, watermark, protected character, recognizable person, or source campaign asset appears.
- The frame does not imply an endorsement, certification, partnership, or product capability that was not provided.
- The final image was inspected at full size and thumbnail size.
