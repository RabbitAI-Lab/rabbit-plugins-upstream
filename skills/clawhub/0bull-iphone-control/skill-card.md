## Description:

Drive a real rented iPhone through the 0bull MCP server: see the screen, tap, swipe, type, run a macro, or hand a narrow task to the on-phone agent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents assisting users with a rented 0bull iPhone can inspect the screen, perform requested device interactions, and report the results of phone tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Remote phone actions may affect accounts, purchases, or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review sensitive actions and keep delegated phone-agent tasks narrow.

Risk: Rebooting interrupts phone activity, and clearing photos deletes the camera roll.

Mitigation: Ask before rebooting or deleting photos.

Risk: Passwords, codes, or clipboard contents may be exposed through phone interactions.

Mitigation: Use passwords or codes only when provided for that purpose, and review clipboard operations.

Risk: Starting or requesting a rental may spend the user's money.

Mitigation: Do not start or request phone rentals as part of this skill.
