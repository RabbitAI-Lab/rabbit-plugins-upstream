## Description:

Reviews short-video, livestream, social, and e-commerce copy for potential compliance issues and produces suggested edits and publishing checklists for human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to review short-video, livestream, social, and e-commerce copy before publication, identify potentially risky claims or calls to action, and prepare suggested edits and a review checklist. It is a planning aid that requires human review and does not replace legal, tax, or medical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some instructions encourage concealing misleading claims, and suggested replacements or platform-policy statements may be unsupported.

Mitigation: Remove concealment advice, require factual evidence for replacement claims, and have a human check current official platform rules before publishing.

Risk: The advertised scanner and templates are absent, so automated checking is unavailable; any later-supplied code has not been reviewed.

Mitigation: Treat results as advisory, manually review the copy, and inspect any added code before running it.
