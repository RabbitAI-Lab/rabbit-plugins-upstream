## Description:

Helps creators review short-video, livestream, social media, and e-commerce copy for potential compliance issues and prepare rewrite suggestions and publishing checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and livestream sellers can use the skill to review draft copy, identify possible platform or advertising concerns, compare safer wording, and prepare a pre-publication checklist. Its guidance requires human review and is not legal, tax, or medical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Livestream incident-response advice may leave viewers misled while helping a streamer avoid scrutiny.

Mitigation: Review or replace that section before relying on the skill, and require clear, prompt corrections backed by evidence for medical, financial, and promotional claims.

Risk: The partial scan and missing referenced files leave automated scan claims unverified.

Mitigation: Manually review the supplied skill and reference files before relying on its compliance guidance.
