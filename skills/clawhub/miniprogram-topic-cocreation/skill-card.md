## Description: <br>
Guides Chinese-speaking users through a three-step co-creation workflow for turning WeChat mini-program ideas into topic pools, feasibility judgments, and actionable product or technical specifications. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, product planners, and developers use this skill to explore WeChat mini-program ideas, compare topic options, identify feasibility and compliance risks, and produce a concise SOP-style product or technical specification before implementation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow may rely on web searches for market, competitor, and compliance signals that can be incomplete or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Treat search results as decision inputs, verify important claims with current sources, and keep the user as the final decision-maker at confirmation gates. <br>
Risk: Generated mini-program specifications may involve location, photos, account identifiers, or AI-processed user input. <br>
Mitigation: Add clear privacy notices, consent flows, retention rules, and delete or clear-all controls before implementing those specifications. <br>
Risk: Naming, category, and content-safety suggestions for WeChat mini-programs may affect review eligibility. <br>
Mitigation: Review app category, name, privacy, and content-safety choices against current WeChat platform requirements before release. <br>
