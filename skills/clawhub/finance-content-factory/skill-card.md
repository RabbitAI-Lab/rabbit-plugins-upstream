## Description:

AI-powered financial content factory for planning, drafting, reviewing, scoring, and adapting financial marketing content across articles, social posts, video scripts, live scripts, and content calendars.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Financial marketing teams, content operators, and brand communication teams use this skill to create content plans, draft multi-format marketing materials, run compliance self-checks, score quality, and prepare review-ready content frameworks. Public-facing financial content still requires qualified compliance and business approval before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial marketing output could be mistaken for legal, compliance, investment, product, or return advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as drafting and planning support, and require qualified compliance and business review before publication.

Risk: Public-facing content may include unsupported claims, prohibited return language, weak risk disclosure, missing generated-content labeling, or unsourced data.

Mitigation: Apply the included compliance checklist, data-source requirements, generated-content labeling checks, and approval workflow before release.

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill for general writing requests outside the intended financial marketing workflow.

Mitigation: Installers can narrow triggers to financial marketing, content calendar, compliance review, and quality scoring use cases.
