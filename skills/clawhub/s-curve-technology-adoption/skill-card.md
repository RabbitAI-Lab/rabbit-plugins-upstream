## Description:

Guides analysis of technology adoption curves, adopter stages, market saturation, and strategy shifts for reaching the next adopter group.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, growth, and strategy teams use this skill to diagnose an innovation's adoption stage, estimate its saturation ceiling and trajectory, and adjust product and go-to-market choices for the next adopter group.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market projections may be used to make business decisions without reviewing their assumptions.

Mitigation: Review projections and assumptions before making business decisions.

Risk: Reusable failure notes may include confidential details.

Mitigation: Omit confidential details from reusable failure notes.
