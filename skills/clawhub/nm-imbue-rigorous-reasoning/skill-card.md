## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and agents use this skill to apply checklist-driven reasoning, avoid agreement bias, and evaluate contested claims with explicit standards for evidence, harm, rights, correction, and retraction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can push an agent toward firm conclusions from limited evidence, especially in sensitive interpersonal, ethical, medical, legal, or safety-related contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when a checklist-driven argumentative style is desired, require explicit evidence limits in the answer, and rely on qualified review for sensitive or high-impact decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill changes conversational posture toward direct critique and anti-sycophancy, which may be inappropriate for support, care, or consensus-building interactions. <br>
Mitigation: Deploy it for analysis and contested-claim review workflows, and disable or override it where empathetic support or professional advice is the primary need. <br>
