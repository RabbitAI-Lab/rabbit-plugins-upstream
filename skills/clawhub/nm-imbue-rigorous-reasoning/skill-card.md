## Description:

Applies an anti-sycophancy checklist to help agents prioritize validity, evidence, and clear conclusions over agreeable responses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users apply this skill when they want an assistant to evaluate contested claims, debates, conflict scenarios, and proposed conclusions without reflexive agreement or unsupported hedging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad triggers may make an agent adopt a stricter reasoning posture during ordinary analysis conversations.

Mitigation: Enable the skill when direct, less agreeable evaluation is desired, and review outputs for tone and fit in routine conversations.
