## Description:

Helps diners build restaurant meals that fit calorie, protein, sodium, and allergen goals using published nutrition and allergen figures, with totals and swap suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nbkdevs](https://clawhub.ai/user/nbkdevs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External diners use this skill to compare restaurant meals with calorie, protein, sodium, and allergen goals, and find suitable swaps based on published figures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published nutrition and allergen figures may be outdated or vary by location, portion, or recipe.

Mitigation: Use the restaurant's current official nutrition and allergen information, and identify missing figures instead of estimating.

Risk: Restaurant menus may not show cross-contact that matters for a serious allergy.

Mitigation: Confirm allergy safety directly with restaurant staff before ordering.

Risk: Meal calculations could be mistaken for medical or dietetic advice.

Mitigation: For a prescribed diet, follow the targets set by the user's clinician.
