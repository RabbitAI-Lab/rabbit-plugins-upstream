## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persona-framed investment or business advice may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as general perspective and verify consequential decisions with qualified professionals.

Risk: Historical analogies may be inaccurate or inappropriate for a modern decision.

Mitigation: Check historical claims and assess the current situation on its own merits before acting.
