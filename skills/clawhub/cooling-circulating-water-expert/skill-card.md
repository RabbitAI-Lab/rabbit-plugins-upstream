## Description:

Expert in cooling circulating water treatment for industrial plants, providing design, optimization, and troubleshooting guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External industrial water-treatment specialists and facility teams use this agent to plan, optimize, and troubleshoot cooling circulating water systems. It provides treatment strategy guidance, chemical dosing recommendations, and report-ready operating suggestions based on project requirements and optional water-quality data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Water-treatment recommendations may be unsuitable if site-specific water-quality data, operating conditions, or local environmental rules are missing.

Mitigation: Require qualified review and validate formulas, dosing, and operating changes against current site measurements and applicable regulations before implementation.

Risk: The package includes legacy .doc reference files.

Mitigation: Open reference documents with standard document-safety controls and keep them subject to normal endpoint scanning and file-handling policies.
