## Description:

Researches public local-business profiles, reviews, complaints, scam-report patterns, menus, and photos across Yelp, BBB, Trustpilot, and OpenTable through Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users can compare local businesses, conduct vendor due diligence, and prepare source-bounded reputation or regional scam-report briefs from public records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business names, locations, source URLs, and the API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use the skill only if that disclosure is acceptable; keep private personal details out of prompts and reports.

Risk: Public reviews and scam reports may be incomplete, platform-specific, or unverified allegations.

Mitigation: Keep platforms and report samples distinct, check business identity, and do not present allegations as confirmed wrongdoing.
