## Description:

Finds and qualifies creators for a product and market, producing a scored shortlist with audience evidence, available quoted rates, and contact paths using paid Glasser data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and creators' partners use this skill to identify and compare influencers for campaigns across relevant social platforms and markets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Glasser data-provider queries incur charges.

Mitigation: Confirm the paid provider and expected usage before running searches.

Risk: Audience, engagement, rates, and contact details can be incomplete or inferred.

Mitigation: Check sources and dates, label inferences, and verify contacts and rates before outreach.

Risk: Outreach using creator contact details can implicate privacy and platform rules.

Mitigation: Use attributable business contacts and follow applicable privacy rules and platform terms.
