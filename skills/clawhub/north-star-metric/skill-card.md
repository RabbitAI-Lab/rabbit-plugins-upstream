## Description: <br>
Guides agents through selecting and stress-testing a single North Star Metric that captures customer value and acts as a leading indicator for revenue. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product leaders, founders, growth teams, and strategy agents use this skill to audit candidate product metrics, choose one North Star Metric, distinguish supporting metrics, and plan validation against customer value, strategy fit, and revenue-leading behavior. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill in unrelated optimization discussions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants North Star Metric guidance before running the audit when the request is generic. <br>
Risk: Metric recommendations can mislead if adopted without product-specific evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Validate candidate metrics against customer value, strategy fit, revenue-leading correlation, and perverse-incentive guardrails before adoption. <br>
