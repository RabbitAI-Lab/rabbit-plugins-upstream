## Description:

Guides teams through selecting and validating a customer-value metric that can lead revenue.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product leaders and cross-functional teams use the skill to align around one measurable customer outcome, evaluate candidate metrics against strategy and revenue, and identify vanity metrics and gaming risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Teams may optimize a chosen metric in ways that inflate activity without improving customer value.

Mitigation: Define completion in customer-value terms, stress-test perverse incentives, monitor quality guardrails, and validate leading-indicator relationships against cohort data.
