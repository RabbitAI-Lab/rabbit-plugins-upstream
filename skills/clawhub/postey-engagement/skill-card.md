## Description:

Read and reply to the comments your posts actually got, and see which automations are running, without sounding like a bot. Covers triage, tone, escalation and when not to reply at all.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[posteyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/posteyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Postey account owners use this skill to review audience comments, draft context-aware replies for approval, and inspect active automations. It helps them triage comments and decide when to respond or hand a matter back to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Replies are public and attributed to the selected account.

Mitigation: Check the account and post, then review and approve each reply draft or batch before sending.

Risk: The separate Postey dependency, server behavior, and shared tone guide were not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review those components before relying on their behavior or tone guidance.
