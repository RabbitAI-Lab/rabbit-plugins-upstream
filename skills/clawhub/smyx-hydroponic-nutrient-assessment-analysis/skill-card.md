## Description:

Assesses hydroponic nutrient concentration from images or video of plant roots and leaves, then provides a qualitative status and adjustment guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Hydroponic growers use this skill to review root and leaf images or video for visible signs associated with nutrient concentration and receive qualitative adjustment suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
