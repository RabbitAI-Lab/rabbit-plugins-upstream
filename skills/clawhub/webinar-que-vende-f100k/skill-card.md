## Description:

Helps Spanish-speaking creators structure, audit, and script sales webinars using a nine-agreement method, with a minute-by-minute outline and slide deck.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this Spanish-language skill to plan or audit sales webinars and live classes, then prepare a presenter script and reusable slides.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business briefs, client cases, or screenshots may appear in generated slides and scripts.

Mitigation: Review source materials and generated files for sensitive client or business details before sharing.

Risk: Unverified sales claims or artificial urgency could mislead viewers.

Mitigation: Use real evidence, prices, guarantees, and deadlines; leave unsupported claims marked for completion.

Risk: The slide template requests Google Fonts when opened online.

Mitigation: Remove the font links or use local fonts if external requests are unsuitable.
