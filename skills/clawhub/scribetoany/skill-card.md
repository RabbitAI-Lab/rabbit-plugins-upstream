## Description:

Transcribes local audio or video and supported public links into text or subtitle files with timestamps and optional speaker labels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hypercube67-formal](https://clawhub.ai/user/hypercube67-formal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to transcribe recordings, meetings, interviews, podcasts, lectures, and videos or create subtitles and captions. It guides the user through authentication, transcription options, and saving or retrieving results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected recordings are uploaded to the user's ScribeToAny account and may contain confidential material.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is comfortable uploading the recording; ask before uploading confidential material.

Risk: The optional package-download fallback is not version-pinned.

Mitigation: Prefer a verified, version-pinned CLI installation.
