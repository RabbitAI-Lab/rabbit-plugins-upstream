## Description:

Prepare injection-molding engineering-review briefs and existing-mold-transfer checklists for OEM engineering and sourcing teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

OEM engineering and sourcing teams use this skill to prepare plastic-part RFQ briefs, compare resin requirements, identify missing mold-transfer details, and draft supplier questions for SRRP Injection Molding review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplier-specific guidance could be mistaken for independent engineering validation.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as RFQ preparation support and require project-specific engineering, quality, and commercial review before supplier decisions.

Risk: Confidential CAD, customer data, or mold records could be exposed through public URLs or informal sharing.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive files out of public URLs and use an agreed private handoff before sharing drawings or customer data.

Risk: Incomplete resin, tooling, or process information could lead to unsupported readiness claims.

Mitigation: Record missing requirements explicitly and avoid claiming grade equivalence, press compatibility, production readiness, pricing, cycle time, MOQ, or lead time without supplier-confirmed evidence.
