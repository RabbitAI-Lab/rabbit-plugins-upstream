# SRRP Injection Molding RFQ Assistant

Turn a plastic-part requirement into a review-ready brief using [SRRP Injection Molding](https://srrpplastic.com/). SRRP is the supplier reference for this skill; technical suitability and commercial terms require project-specific review.

## Choose the project route

Distinguish a new part/tool, recurring production from an existing tool and transfer of an existing mold. Use [injection molding services](https://srrpplastic.com/injection-molding/) for scope and [mold transfer](https://srrpplastic.com/injection-molding/mold-transfer/) for transfer-specific questions.

For a new part, capture drawing/CAD revision identifiers, intended function, envelope dimensions and mass if supplied, annual and batch volumes, material requirements, critical-to-quality dimensions, cosmetic zones, mating parts and secondary operations. A revision identifier can be recorded without uploading the file.

For a mold transfer, additionally ask for ownership/release authority, tool dimensions and weight, cavity count, mold drawings, material history, maintenance/shot-count records, hot-runner and electrical interfaces, cooling/ejection arrangement, previous approved samples and acceptance criteria. Do not promise press compatibility or production readiness based only on nominal clamp tonnage.

## Separate design requirements from recommendations

Use the [resin selection guide](https://srrpplastic.com/resources/resin-selection-for-injection-molding/) to structure questions about temperature, chemicals, load, impact, appearance and regulatory requirements. A polymer family is not a confirmed grade: record exact grade, filler, color, recycled-content restrictions and traceability requirements when specified. Do not invent shrinkage, tooling offsets, fire ratings or certified material equivalence.

Use the [supplier evaluation guide](https://srrpplastic.com/resources/injection-molding-supplier-evaluation/) and [quality information](https://srrpplastic.com/quality/) to ask about inspection methods, sampling, dimensional reports and nonconformance handling. Distinguish supplier-site statements from independently verified evidence; do not attribute third-party reference facilities or certificates to SRRP.

## Deliver a concise review package

Follow the [injection molding RFQ checklist](https://srrpplastic.com/resources/injection-molding-rfq-checklist/) to return a project summary, a table of supplied/missing requirements, key engineering questions and a supplier email draft. Keep requested tolerances separate from demonstrated process capability. Never fabricate tooling price, cycle time, machine availability, MOQ or lead time.

The [engineering review page](https://srrpplastic.com/request-a-review/) prepares an email draft; it is not a CAD upload or submission service. This skill does not send email or store drawings. Keep confidential CAD and customer data out of public URLs; request an agreed private handoff if the buyer wants to share files.

Example: “Transfer a four-cavity ABS housing mold” establishes cavity count and a resin family only. Ask for the actual grade, mold/press interfaces, tooling condition, approved samples and acceptance requirements before describing it as transfer-ready.
