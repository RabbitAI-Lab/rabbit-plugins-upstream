## Use Case: <br>
Consumers, buyers, and support agents use this skill to check Chinese domestic product packaging, licenses, enterprise status, and regulated-category claims before purchase, complaint, or continued use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save product photos, screenshots, and purchase evidence locally. <br>
Mitigation: Review the planned evidence package before use, avoid unnecessary personal data, and delete local evidence files when the investigation is complete. <br>
Risk: Receipts or purchase records can contain personal or payment-adjacent information. <br>
Mitigation: Redact names, phone numbers, addresses, order numbers, and payment details unless they are required for a complaint. <br>
Risk: Product compliance conclusions can be wrong when packaging details, license numbers, or public registry data are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Base conclusions only on cited sources, preserve confidence levels, and treat unclear results as requiring follow-up verification. <br>
