## Description: <br>
Use to grow a TikTok account by diagnosing where growth is stuck, prioritizing completion rate, rewatches, search, and sustainable posting, then routing to related skills for scripts, hooks, profile optimization, calendars, hashtags, trend work, and reverse engineering. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and social media operators use this skill to diagnose TikTok growth stalls, interpret native TikTok analytics, and choose the next strategy or related skill to improve reach, search discovery, follower conversion, and retention. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for marketing context and TikTok analytics that include sensitive business or account information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the analytics and context needed for diagnosis, and remove confidential or personal data before sharing. <br>
Risk: The skill routes to publishing and scheduling skills that may affect a TikTok account. <br>
Mitigation: Review those related skills before installation and require explicit approval before posting, scheduling, or account-changing actions. <br>
Risk: TikTok algorithm benchmarks and platform behavior can change. <br>
Mitigation: Re-check native TikTok analytics and current platform benchmarks before relying on specific targets or cadence recommendations. <br>
