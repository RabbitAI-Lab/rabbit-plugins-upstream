## Description: <br>
Write a conference talk proposal / CFP submission for a tech or developer conference. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, speakers, and conference applicants use this skill to turn a technical talk idea into a CFP-ready proposal with title options, an abstract, audience takeaways, an outline, organizer notes, and a speaker bio. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Conference proposals may include sensitive company, project, or personal background details if users provide them. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including private company details, unreleased project information, or personal speaker background that should not appear in a proposal. <br>
Risk: Generated CFP content may overstate speaker credentials, project results, or talk scope. <br>
Mitigation: Review the title, abstract, takeaways, outline, organizer notes, and speaker bio for factual accuracy and realistic scope before submission. <br>
