## Description:

Yuan Dian helps agents search and retrieve Chinese legal, case, regulation, and enterprise due-diligence information through an OOMOL-connected Yuan Dian account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, legal researchers, and developers use this skill to search and retrieve Yuan Dian legal materials, check legal text against statutes and cases, and gather enterprise due-diligence records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: First-time setup recommends running remote installer scripts directly.

Mitigation: Review and verify the installer source and expected system changes before running the one-line installer commands.

Risk: The skill uses an OOMOL-connected Yuan Dian account to access legal and enterprise records.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with a trusted OOMOL account and an authorized Yuan Dian connection.
