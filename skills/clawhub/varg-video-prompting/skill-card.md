## Description:

Guides agents in choosing Varg video, image, voice, and music models and writing, iterating, and quality-checking prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and production teams use this skill to select models, prepare prompts and references, and check generated video and audio against approved visuals, dialogue, and budgets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes advice to retry prompts rejected by content filters.

Mitigation: Revise this advice to respect refusals and make substantive, compliant changes before another attempt.

Risk: Uploaded identifiable faces or voices may be sensitive, and hosted-file access, retention, and deletion controls are unclear.

Mitigation: Get authorization for the specific media and confirm access, retention, and deletion controls before uploading it.

Risk: Paid media generations can incur significant credit costs.

Mitigation: Keep the documented spending approvals, estimate costs, and run an approved pilot before larger batches.
