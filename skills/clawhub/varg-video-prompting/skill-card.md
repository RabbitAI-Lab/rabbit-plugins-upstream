## Description:

Guides agents in choosing varg video, image, voice, and music models and writing prompts that keep shots visually and audibly consistent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Video creators and creative teams use the skill to select varg models, build structured prompts and reference sets, plan generation stages, and review generated video and audio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media used for generation, transcription, or hosted quality checks is sent off-device.

Mitigation: Confirm sharing permission and URL privacy before sending footage or recordings, especially confidential material.

Risk: Upstream downloads may change after review.

Mitigation: Review or pin upstream downloads and install only the skill needed.

Risk: Generation and retry workflows can consume credits.

Mitigation: Keep spending approvals documented and ensure retries follow provider restrictions.

Risk: Separate skills, live-service behavior, and data-retention controls were not verified in this review.

Mitigation: Review companion skills and the service's current behavior and retention controls before using sensitive media.
