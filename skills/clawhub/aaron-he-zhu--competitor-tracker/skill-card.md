## Description: <br>
Tracks competitor influencer marketing by producing competitor partner rosters, campaign and content-strategy breakdowns, performance estimates, and gap or opportunity lists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Apache-2.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, analysts, and agents use this skill to benchmark a brand against named competitors, track influencer partnerships and campaign patterns, and turn observed gaps into ranked competitive-intelligence opportunities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may retain competitive intelligence reports and named creator observations in memory. <br>
Mitigation: Use public or authorized data, review saved memory entries, and avoid storing sensitive or unauthorized creator information. <br>
Risk: Performance, reach, spend, and opportunity rankings are estimates that could influence marketing decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Label estimates clearly and verify material numbers against trusted data sources before acting on recommendations. <br>
Risk: The security evidence notes scope ambiguity around creator prospecting. <br>
Mitigation: Treat creator-opportunity sections as competitive intelligence unless the publisher clarifies that creator prospecting is fully in scope. <br>
