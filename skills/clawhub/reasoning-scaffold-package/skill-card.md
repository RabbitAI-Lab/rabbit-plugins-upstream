## Description: <br>
reasoning-scaffold helps agents structure decision analysis with a six-step reasoning framework and a checklist of common cognitive and behavioral traps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[guoranyt](https://clawhub.ai/user/guoranyt) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to make judgment, forecasting, comparison, planning, and risk-assessment responses more structured, traceable, and explicit about uncertainty. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate broadly on ordinary analysis or decision questions. <br>
Mitigation: Review whether the six-step structure is appropriate for the user's request and skip or simplify it when it adds unnecessary friction. <br>
Risk: The artifact references helper script paths that are not present in the submitted files. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a reasoning template unless a later release supplies and validates the referenced script files. <br>
