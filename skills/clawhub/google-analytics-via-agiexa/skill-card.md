## Description:

Explains Agiexa's planned Google Analytics integration for Shopify and offers an optional waitlist signup with the user's permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants and their agents use this skill to understand Agiexa's planned Google Analytics integration, explore supported analytics use cases, and optionally request a waitlist signup for launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A waitlist signup shares the user's email and role with Agiexa, and may include an optional link or message.

Mitigation: Review the submitted details with the user and obtain explicit permission before sending the signup request.

Risk: Installing this skill does not connect Google Analytics or enable the advertised tools.

Mitigation: Explain that the integration is planned for November 2026 and that access requires a separate app connection and user-approved permissions.
