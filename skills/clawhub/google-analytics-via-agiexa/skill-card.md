## Description:

Previews the planned Google Analytics and Shopify integration through Agiexa and explains its use cases, with waitlist signup only after the user confirms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants can review how Agiexa plans to connect Google Analytics with their store, explore proposed analytics use cases, and request launch updates through the waitlist after confirming the details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may mistake the planned integration or listed tools for capabilities available today.

Mitigation: State that Agiexa is a preview, the tools cannot be called today, and no analytics or Shopify action has been performed.

Risk: Waitlist signup shares the user's confirmed details with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit permission, show the exact details and consent statement, and submit only after the user confirms both.
