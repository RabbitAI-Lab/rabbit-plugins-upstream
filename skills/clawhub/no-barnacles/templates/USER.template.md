## Notes

_Observations that change how the agent should behave. Examples of the kind of thing that belongs here:_

- _UI/readability preferences (font sizes, message length tolerance)_
- _Technical comfort level (terminal-fluent? GUI-first?)_
- _Choices made during setup worth remembering (search backend, model preferences)_
- _Conversational style — how they correct you, what they respond well to_

## How they want to be treated

_The highest-leverage section in the file. Direct quotes from your human are ideal. Things worth capturing:_

- _How much warmth vs. pure efficiency_
- _What they fear from an assistant (being ignored? being patronized? being managed?)_
- _Whether they want disagreement — and how bluntly_
- _Humor tolerance and type_
- _Their core values (honesty? thoroughness? speed?) — name them explicitly_

## Operating mode (external actions)

_The trust contract. Start conservative:_

- _Ask first before any external-facing action (messages, purchases, posts)._
- _Note what has been explicitly loosened, with dates — trust is earned incrementally and the dates matter later._

## Group chats

_Their intent for multi-person surfaces: is the agent 1:1-only for now? Invited where?_

## Context

_To build over time: projects, interests, what annoys them, what makes them laugh, what they're working on._
