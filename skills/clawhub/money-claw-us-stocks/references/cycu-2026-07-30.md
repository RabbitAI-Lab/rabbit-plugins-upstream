# CYCU 5-Minute Case Study — 2026-07-30

CYCU is a forward, out-of-sample study of the `CPHI_SUBTYPE`: the official gap
was below 20%, while abnormal premarket activity, regular-session turnover,
VWAP control, and successive 5-minute expansions produced the extreme move.
The case teaches execution sequencing; it does not claim that every such setup
was tradable at the displayed prices.

## Catalyst and supply conflict

The company announced its largest contract, described as a `$54.60 million`
10-year award through an unnamed top-five global consulting firm, with work
scheduled to begin in November 2026. The headline was large relative to the
company's micro-cap valuation and was capable of rapid social amplification.
Its verification quality was medium rather than high because the counterparty
was unnamed, the economics spanned ten years, and the initial evidence was a
company press release rather than a filed contract exhibit.

Primary source:

- [Company press release](https://investors.cycurion.com/pr/cycurion-lands-largest-contract-in-company-history-546-million-10year-award-with-top5-global-consulting-firm)

Two contemporaneous hard risks prevent the skill from treating technical
strength as permission to buy:

- A preliminary S-1 covered resale of up to `25,888,888` common shares,
  including `25,000,000` shares tied to an equity purchase agreement and
  `888,888` shares from preferred conversion.
- A July 10 Form 8-K disclosed a Nasdaq staff delisting determination after the
  stock remained below `$1.00`; a requested hearing stayed suspension pending
  the process, with no assurance of a favorable outcome.

Primary sources:

- [SEC S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1868419/000149315226031976/forms-1.htm)
- [SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1868419/000162828026048496/cycu-20260710.htm)

Under the production rules, confirmed resale/dilution overhang blocks
`EXECUTE`. CYCU can therefore be studied as a price-state example while a live
new-long decision remains `NO_NEW_ENTRY`.

## What was and was not executable

The cleanest theoretical long window was after an early completed 5-minute
retest during `TREND_EXPANSION`, before the move became more than 40% extended
above VWAP. A valid entry still required:

- upstream `EXECUTE`;
- no confirmed or unresolved dilution overhang;
- spread no more than 2.50%;
- retest bar holding VWAP or the breakout level;
- next bar breaking the retest high on renewed volume.

CYCU failed the live supply gate because of the resale registration. The case
therefore supports the phase classifier and exit logic, but it is not recorded
as an authorized production trade.

## Bias controls

- Use only data available at each timestamp.
- Exclude post-market data from regular-session labels.
- Model fills after confirmation, never at the signal bar's low.
- Record halts, spread, and slippage; screenshots alone cannot reconstruct them
  reliably.
- Do not treat the final 581.00% high as evidence that the earlier setup was
  certain to continue.
