## Description:

Creates HTML storyboard and reference boards, consistent stills for look approval, and device or app UI mockups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use the skill to prepare reviewable still boards and app or device UI mockups before committing to video production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference files may be shared externally.

Mitigation: Review their contents and intended recipients before sending or publishing a board.

Risk: The release scan was partial and flagged installation supply-chain concerns.

Mitigation: Review the skill and board template, and verify or pin installation sources before installing.
