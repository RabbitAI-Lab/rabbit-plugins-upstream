## Description:

Creates HTML review boards and consistent still or device mockups so teams can approve a video concept before animation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and reviewers use this skill to plan storyboards, moodboards, look tests, and UI mockups, then review stills and approve shots before animation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference images and prompts may be processed by varg and its generation providers.

Mitigation: Use only material you are authorized to share and review provider terms before submitting sensitive content.

Risk: Image generation uses paid credits.

Mitigation: Check the estimate and approve the total before generating.

Risk: Optional external templates may contain code that has not been reviewed.

Mitigation: Inspect a template separately before running it.

Risk: Review notes remain in the browser until cleared.

Mitigation: Clear board notes when finished, especially on a shared device.
