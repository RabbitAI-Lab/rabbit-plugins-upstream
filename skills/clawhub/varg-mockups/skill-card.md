## Description:

Creates self-contained HTML review boards for storyboard stills, visual references, and device or app mockups before video production.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video makers and product developers use the skill to review stills, visual direction, and screen concepts before paying for video generation or building an interface.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment flags possible unreviewed sharing of prompts and reference images with hosted services.

Mitigation: Confirm that sharing is acceptable for each prompt and reference image; avoid confidential or sensitive material unless that sharing has been approved.

Risk: The assessment reports filter-evasion guidance and says its analysis of part of the board template was incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the flagged guidance and template before use; the assessment says the available evidence does not establish that the skill is safe.
