## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a local digital wellness aid to start social media detox periods, track screen-free streaks, log urges, review time reclaimed, and configure personal app or notification boundaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Urge logs can reveal private emotional patterns and social media habits. <br>
Mitigation: Keep detox logs local, avoid sharing them unintentionally, and review stored entries before exporting or showing agent context. <br>
Risk: App-blocking or notification changes may affect expected access to social platforms. <br>
Mitigation: Review each proposed boundary change before applying it and keep a clear reversal path for blockers, limits, and notification settings. <br>
