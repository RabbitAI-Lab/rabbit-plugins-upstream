## Description: <br>
Guides agents in building academic-style .docx papers with citation styles, numbered equations, cross-references, footnotes or endnotes, bibliographies, and multi-column journal layouts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[iceyliu](https://clawhub.ai/user/iceyliu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, developers, and researchers use this skill to produce OfficeCLI command guidance for scholarly Word documents such as journal papers, conference manuscripts, thesis chapters, and citation-heavy research reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The setup instructions ask users to run remote OfficeCLI installer scripts directly in a shell. <br>
Mitigation: Review the installer source first, prefer signed releases or package-manager installation where available, and run setup only when the OfficeCLI publisher and domain are trusted. <br>
