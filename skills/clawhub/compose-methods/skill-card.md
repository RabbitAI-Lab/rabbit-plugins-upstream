## Description:

Compose Methods is a content-structuring skill that guides agents through checklist-based or sample-based workflows for producing structured deliverables such as documents, plans, reports, presentations, business plans, code, and templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and content authors use this skill to choose a composition method and guide an agent through assembling structured outputs from components or by analyzing and adapting a sample. It is best suited for general content organization and should be paired with specialized expertise for regulated, technical, legal, medical, or other high-stakes domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can appear for many structured writing tasks and may be too generic for regulated, technical, legal, medical, or other high-stakes work.

Mitigation: Use it for general composition workflows, and prefer specialized skills or expert review when domain-specific accuracy, compliance, or safety matters.

Risk: Sample-based composition can over-rely on source examples if the agent copies content instead of adapting structure and style.

Mitigation: Use samples as structural references only, replace source content with the user's actual content, and review the final output for originality and fitness for purpose.
