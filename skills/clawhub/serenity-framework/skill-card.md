## Description: <br>
Analyzes stocks, sectors, or industries using Serenity's five-layer supply-chain bottleneck investment framework with online data verification. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kooui](https://clawhub.ai/user/kooui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and market researchers use this skill to generate structured stock, sector, or industry research reports that map macro demand, supply-chain bottlenecks, company exposure, position sizing, execution considerations, and risks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may produce concrete trading, leverage, position-sizing, stop-loss, or day-trading guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational research only and do not rely on them as personalized investment advice. <br>
Risk: Market data, filings, supply-chain signals, and OSINT inputs can be stale, incomplete, or wrong. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify current filings, market data, news, and supply-chain evidence before acting. <br>
Risk: The framework discusses high-volatility strategies, margin use, and drawdowns that can exceed normal risk tolerance. <br>
Mitigation: Check personal risk tolerance and consult a qualified financial professional before using any allocation, leverage, or execution suggestion. <br>
