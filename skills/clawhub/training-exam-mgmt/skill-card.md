## Description: <br>
Manages the full exam lifecycle, including question banks, test paper generation, scheduling, online or offline execution, score entry, analysis, certificates, and issuance tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Training administrators and program managers use this skill to plan exams, manage question banks and schedules, record and analyze grades, and prepare certificate issuance workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Exam administration data may include candidate personal information and grades. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the records needed for the task and follow the organization's privacy and data-handling rules. <br>
Risk: Generated exam plans, score analyses, or certificate criteria may affect learner outcomes if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified administrator review exam configuration, scoring rules, analysis, and certificate eligibility before use. <br>
