## Description:

Find and verify work emails from a name and company — waterfall lookup across providers, one call for agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can find a person's work email from their name and company, verify an address before sending, or list known email addresses for a domain.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email lookup sends contact information to Superagnt and its providers.

Mitigation: Use only contact information you intend to share with them.

Risk: The API key is broadly usable, and lookups can consume workspace credits.

Mitigation: Protect the key and review expected lookup costs before use.

Risk: The documented request schemas and shell examples may need correction or completion.

Mitigation: Obtain complete request schemas and corrected examples before running requests.
