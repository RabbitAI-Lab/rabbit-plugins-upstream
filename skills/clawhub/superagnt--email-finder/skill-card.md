## Description:

Find and verify work emails for prospects from a name and company — one call per lookup, and it never reads your inbox.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

## Use Case:

External sales and prospecting agents use this skill to find and verify professional email addresses or list known addresses for a company domain. It supports lead enrichment and list cleaning before outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
