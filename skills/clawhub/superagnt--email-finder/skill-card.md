## Description:

Find and verify work emails for prospects from a name and company — one call per lookup, and it never reads your inbox.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Outbound sales and lead enrichment agents can use Email Finder to retrieve a prospect’s work email, verify an address before a campaign, or list known emails for a company domain. It supports contact enrichment and list cleaning without reading the user’s inbox.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill contacts SuperAGNT and uses an API key for its email lookup and verification calls.

Mitigation: Use it only if you trust SuperAGNT and are comfortable with those API calls; keep SUPERAGNT_API_KEY private.
