## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, workflows, checklists, analysis, or implementation support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether product ideas are worth building before spending heavily. It converts a live product or startup question into practical validation plans, prototype workflows, positioning drafts, checklists, analysis, code changes, or decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad startup, SaaS, prototype, validation, or product wording may activate the skill in conversations where a full planning workflow is not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrower routing phrases in environments that require precise skill activation. <br>
Risk: Product validation plans can overstate confidence when the user provides limited market, customer, or prototype evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Keep assumptions visible, state validation limits, and ask only for missing inputs that materially change the recommendation. <br>
