## Description:

Define and calculate dataset quality metrics, sampling plans and uncertainty, including completeness, verified correctness, coverage, precision and recall. Use for measurement questions rather than the whole delivery-acceptance or vendor-investment decision.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Data analysts, data stewards, and developers use this skill to define and calculate dataset quality measures, design samples, quantify uncertainty, and interpret results for a specific customer decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A biased or poorly defined sample can produce misleading quality estimates.

Mitigation: Define the eligible population, denominators, and verification policy; document sample selection and assumptions, and report uncertainty before relying on the estimate.
