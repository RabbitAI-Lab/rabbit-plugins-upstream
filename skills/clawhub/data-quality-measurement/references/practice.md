# Practice: define the metric

Synthetic scenario. Of 40 inspected records, 38 have the required address populated. You independently check 30 addresses: 27 are correct, 3 are wrong. The remaining 10 records have no verified truth label.

The customer wants to reach clinicians in a particular state. The total relevant clinician population is unknown.

Explain what rates you can report, their denominators, what you cannot conclude, and the next check you would prioritize. Do not count an unverified address as correct merely because it is filled in.
