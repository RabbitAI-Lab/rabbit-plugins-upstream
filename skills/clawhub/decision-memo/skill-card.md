## Description: <br>
Write a crisp decision memo that drives a clear decision, not a discussion. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, leaders, operators, and product teams use this skill to turn a pending choice into a recommendation-first memo with options, trade-offs, assumptions, risks, and a clear ask by a deadline. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A generated memo may overstate a recommendation or omit important context when the user's inputs are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the memo before sharing, verify the options, trade-offs, assumptions, risks, decider, and deadline, and adjust the recommendation where the evidence does not support it. <br>
