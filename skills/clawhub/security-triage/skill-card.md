## Description:

Triage OpenClaw security advisories, drafts, and GHSA reports with shipped-tag and trust-model proof.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

OpenClaw maintainers use this skill to assess security advisories and GHSA reports against the project's trust model and released versions, then prepare a focused decision and draft response.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment notes clipboard write behavior that may not fit a triage-only workflow.

Mitigation: Review the clipboard step and verify the copied text before pasting or posting it.
