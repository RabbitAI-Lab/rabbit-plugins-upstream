## Description: <br>
Write a weekly engineering status report for a team, service, or initiative. Use when asked to write a team update, weekly engineering report, sprint status email, or standing team communication to stakeholders. Produces a concise, scannable weekly report covering shipping progress, metrics, decisions, blockers, and next-week priorities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Engineering managers, team leads, and engineers use this skill to draft concise weekly status reports for stakeholders. It organizes shipped work, active progress, blockers, decisions, metrics, risks, escalations, and next-week priorities into a repeatable report format. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Weekly reports can include sensitive internal team details such as names, PTO, morale notes, customer-impacting incidents, or escalation details. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended audience and omit or sanitize sensitive details before broad distribution. <br>
Risk: Missing report inputs or unavailable metrics can lead to misleading status updates if values are invented. <br>
Mitigation: Use [data needed] markers for unavailable metrics and request concrete owners, blockers, decisions, and dates before sharing the report. <br>
