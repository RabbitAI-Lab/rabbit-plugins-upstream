## Description: <br>
Helps agents find, qualify, score, and present prospect lists across B2B SaaS, general B2B, local small businesses, and early-stage demand-signal discovery. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, marketing, founder, and business development users use this skill to turn an ICP or market signal into a verified outreach-ready lead sheet. It guides branch selection, candidate discovery, evidence-based qualification, scoring, contact verification, and compliant handoff to downstream outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prospecting can involve personal data, enrichment tools, visitor-identification tools, and email-finding workflows that may trigger privacy, consent, notice, retention, and opt-out obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm lawful basis, provider terms, retention limits, notice obligations, and opt-out handling before using enrichment or person-level contact data, especially for EU, UK, or Canada prospects. <br>
Risk: Platform data collection for LinkedIn, Google Maps, paywalled sites, or rate-limited sources can violate platform terms when performed as bulk scraping or automated extraction. <br>
Mitigation: Use browser-assisted research, public business sources, licensed providers within their terms, request spacing, and source URL/date capture rather than bulk scraping or bypassing access controls. <br>
Risk: Lead quality can degrade when firmographic, technographic, funding, or contact data is stale or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check candidates across independent sources, verify email deliverability before adding contacts to final lists, and label confidence and unknowns in the output. <br>
