## Description:

Screens Chinese-language food and health-product advertising copy for specified treatment claims and missing product warnings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and their agents can pre-screen Chinese-language food and health-product copy for specified claims and missing warnings before human compliance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A limited phrase check may miss misleading claims or flag acceptable wording.

Mitigation: Treat results as preliminary screening and have a qualified reviewer assess claims in context.

Risk: Advertising and health claims may depend on current rules and professional judgment.

Mitigation: Check current official regulations and obtain legal or medical review before relying on a decision.
