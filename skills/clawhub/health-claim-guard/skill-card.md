## Description:

Screens Chinese-language food and supplement advertising copy for selected prohibited health claims and missing warning language.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content teams and agents can preliminarily screen Chinese-language food and supplement promotions for specified treatment claims and missing warning text before qualified review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A preliminary keyword check may miss misleading claims or disagree with current advertising requirements.

Mitigation: Have qualified professionals review consequential claims against current official rules; do not treat a PASS as legal or medical clearance.
