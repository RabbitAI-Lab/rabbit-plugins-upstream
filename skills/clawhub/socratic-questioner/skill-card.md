## Description:

Guides users through decisions with one question at a time, helping them examine assumptions, evidence, and consequences rather than supplying a conclusion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[elephantjohn](https://clawhub.ai/user/elephantjohn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People seeking to think through a decision themselves use the skill for a sequence of clarifying questions rather than immediate advice. It exits questioning mode when the user requests a direct answer or needs factual or professional guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests to think something through may activate questioning when the user wants a direct answer.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's preference and switch to a direct answer if requested.

Risk: Question-only responses can delay useful help for factual, urgent, medical, legal, or crisis requests.

Mitigation: Leave questioning mode and provide direct help or professional-guidance-oriented support as appropriate.
