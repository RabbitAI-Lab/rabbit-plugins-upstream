## Description:

Provides an academic workplace bullying framework based on Ståle Einarsen's work, covering definitions, NAQ measurement, distinctions from harassment, incivility, and conflict, role models, and organizational antecedents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, HR practitioners, and organizational diagnostics teams use this skill to frame workplace bullying assessments, distinguish bullying from adjacent workplace behaviors, and plan measurement using NAQ plus interviews and policy review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat academic framing or measurement guidance as legal, medical, or HR-adjudication advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an informational academic reference and route actual workplace cases through qualified HR, legal, medical, and local policy processes.

Risk: Users may overclassify bullying from NAQ behavior frequency alone.

Mitigation: Separate negative-behavior frequency from a bullying determination, and combine NAQ results with interviews, policy audit, persistence, power-imbalance, and harm criteria.
