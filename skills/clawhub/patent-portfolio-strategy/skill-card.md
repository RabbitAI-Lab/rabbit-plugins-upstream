## Description:

Provides technology-route analysis and patent portfolio planning, including route selection, design-around options, filing schedules, and budget allocation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, inventors, and patent-strategy teams use the skill to compare technology routes and plan patent filings, jurisdictions, schedules, and budgets. Its recommendations are technical and commercial input, not legal opinions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Potentially confidential invention details may be sent to PatentMax without clear disclosure boundaries.

Mitigation: Start with public or redacted information, approve exactly what will be uploaded, and review PatentMax's privacy policy (https://www.ip930.com/legal/privacy) before sharing unpublished inventions.

Risk: Paid API keys may be stored for reuse.

Mitigation: Keep paid API keys out of chat and protect cached keys with owner-only permissions.
