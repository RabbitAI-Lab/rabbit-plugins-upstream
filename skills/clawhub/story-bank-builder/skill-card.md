## Description: <br>
Builds a reusable story bank from customer and interview material, tagging origin, founder, customer, transformation, and proof stories to message-house pillars and claim IDs with proof status labels. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and narrative teams use this skill to turn user-provided interviews, case notes, testimonials, and win-loss material into reusable story units. It is intended for projects that already have message-house pillars, brand voice, and claims-ledger context available. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Customer interviews, testimonials, and case material may include content the user is not authorized to reuse. <br>
Mitigation: Review user-provided material for usage rights before turning it into story units or reusable proof assets. <br>
Risk: Unverified proof points could be mistaken for substantiated claims. <br>
Mitigation: Keep every proof labeled Measured, User-provided, or [needs source], and route unsupported proof wording as a proposal instead of treating it as approved. <br>
Risk: Story outputs may be persisted into future-session memory without clear consent. <br>
Mitigation: Save story-bank results only after explicit user confirmation, as described by the artifact behavior and server security guidance. <br>
