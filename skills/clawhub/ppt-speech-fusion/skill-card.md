## Description: <br>
Generates a presentation recovery PDF from a meeting recording and PPTX by transcribing the audio, aligning the narrative to slide pages, placing the recovered speech above each original slide image, and producing a self-check report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and external users use this skill to convert meeting, training, roadshow, or product presentation recordings plus PPTX files into a page-aligned presentation recovery PDF. The workflow is intended for users who need a readable speech narrative paired with the corresponding original slide on each page. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process confidential recordings and slide decks. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with files you are authorized to process, and confirm whether transcription and PDF generation run locally or through approved third-party services before handling confidential meetings. <br>
Risk: Poor audio quality, unsupported audio formats, unsupported PPT files, or very large slide decks can reduce output quality or prevent completion. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear m4a, mp3, or wav audio with pptx input, confirm before processing slide decks over 50 pages, and review the generated self-check table before sharing the PDF. <br>
