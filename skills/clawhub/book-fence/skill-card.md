## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to find fence-service providers, check availability, and create bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bookings are created through an external Lokuli service using customer contact details. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, time slot, and that the customer's name, email, and phone number will be sent to the external booking service. <br>
Risk: Search and availability results depend on the external MCP endpoint. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when comfortable relying on Lokuli for fence-service search and booking. <br>
