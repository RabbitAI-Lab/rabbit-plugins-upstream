## Description:

Sales Rescue Coach diagnoses stalled customer conversations, identifies the sales funnel stage or deadlock, and suggests the next follow-up action and talking points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[meimei-shoulu](https://clawhub.ai/user/meimei-shoulu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Salespeople, business owners, shop assistants, private-domain sellers, and similar external users use this skill to analyze stalled sales chats, locate the sticking point, and choose one practical follow-up action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste personal details, phone numbers, company names, or confidential business terms from customer conversations.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting unnecessary personal or confidential details, and redact identifiers before using the skill.

Risk: The skill includes an off-platform WeChat payment upsell for a paid version.

Mitigation: Review the upsell before deployment and ensure users understand that payment happens outside the platform.

Risk: Sales coaching can become misleading if it promises guaranteed outcomes or encourages manipulative tactics.

Mitigation: Keep guidance framed as judgment and next-step advice, preserve the skill's no-guarantee rule, and avoid deceptive, bribery-related, or sensitive gray-area tactics.
