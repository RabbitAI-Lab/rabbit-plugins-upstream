## Description:

Compares recorded displayed-price snapshots for a primary Amazon product and authorized competitors, clarifying snapshot timing and evidence gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product operators use this skill to compare displayed prices for a primary product and authorized competitor pages while keeping the snapshot time and evidence gaps clear.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags conflicting guidance around paid analysis and recurring collection, which may permit charges without fresh approval.

Mitigation: Require approval before each paid operation and review existing collection schedules and product watches; prefer a corrected package that enforces the stated comparison scope and approval requirements.
