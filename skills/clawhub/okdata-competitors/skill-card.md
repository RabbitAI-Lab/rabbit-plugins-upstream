## Description:

Retrieves recent posts and engagement metrics for named competitor accounts across 11 social platforms and summarizes them in a comparison table.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[conn-ho](https://clawhub.ai/user/conn-ho)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and competitive intelligence teams use this skill to review recent competitor posts and compare their engagement across supported social platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill makes paid requests to an external data service, and the security scan was partial, so the available scan evidence does not cover every reference.

Mitigation: Review okdata's data handling terms, share only account identifiers you are comfortable sending to the service, and confirm the per-call cost before running queries.
