## Description: <br>
This skill helps agents produce structured investment attraction project assessment reports by evaluating project fit, enterprise reliability, industrial impact, investment feasibility, landing conditions, economic contribution, risks, and recommended next steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users such as local government investment teams, industrial park operators, and business development analysts use this skill to screen investment leads, compare projects, prepare decision materials, and identify risks before advancing project negotiations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill produces decision-support recommendations based on public and business research that may be incomplete or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify source data independently before using the report for investment, legal, policy, or approval decisions. <br>
Risk: Users may provide confidential project or company materials during assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential materials unless they are approved for use in the agent workflow. <br>
Risk: Assessment output could be mistaken for legal or financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the report as supporting analysis and obtain qualified legal, financial, and policy review before acting on recommendations. <br>
