## Description:

Summarizes user-provided text into concise Chinese key points and a short conclusion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hxo-resistor](https://clawhub.ai/user/hxo-resistor)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users who provide longer passages can use this skill to extract three to five main points and a brief conclusion in Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests Chinese output even when the user or surrounding conversation expects another language.

Mitigation: Confirm that Chinese is appropriate for the audience; otherwise use a summarizer configured for the requested language.

## Reference(s):

- [Smart Summarizer on ClawHub](https://clawhub.ai/hxo-resistor/skills/hxo-resistor-smart-summarizer)

## Skill Output:

**Output Type(s):** [Text, Markdown]

**Output Format:** [Chinese Markdown bullet list followed by a short conclusion]

**Output Parameters:** [1D]

**Other Properties Related to Output:** [Three to five key points; summary at most 200 Chinese characters and conclusion at most 50 characters; declines to summarize inputs under 50 characters.]
