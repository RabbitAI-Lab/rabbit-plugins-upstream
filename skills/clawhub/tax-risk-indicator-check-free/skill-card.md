## Use Case:

External finance and accounting users use this skill during closing, reconciliation, or tax-table review to check Chinese-language tax risk indicator self-check tables for arithmetic mismatches, duplicate periods, blank placeholders, and negative amounts. It reports missing input when the table is insufficient instead of inventing conclusions.
