# Recipe Notes

Detailed cooking notes for featured recipes in the Leftover Chef database.

## Quick Reference by Cuisine

### Italian
- **Pasta Aglio e Olio** — The classic 5-ingredient pasta. Use plenty of garlic and good olive oil. Reserve pasta water to emulsify.
- **Margherita Pizza** — Use "00" flour if available. Get your oven as hot as possible. Fresh mozzarella makes a huge difference.
- **Risotto** — Use Arborio or Carnaroli rice. Stir constantly and add stock gradually. The rice is done when creamy but still has a slight bite (al dente).
- **Caprese Salad** — Use the ripest tomatoes you can find. Salt the tomatoes 10 minutes before assembling.

### Asian
- **Fried Rice** — Day-old cold rice is essential. Break up clumps before frying. High heat, fast cooking.
- **Chicken Stir-Fry** — Prep all ingredients first (mise en place). Cook in batches to avoid steaming. Sear the chicken before adding vegetables.
- **Pad Thai** — Soak rice noodles in warm (not hot) water until pliable. Don't over-soak or they'll turn mushy.

### Mexican
- **Guacamole** — Use Haas avocados. Keep the pit in the bowl to slow browning. Add lime juice immediately.
- **Beef Tacos** — Warm tortillas on a dry comal or pan. Don't overfill.
- **Chili Con Carne** — Better the next day. A square of dark chocolate adds depth.

### Middle Eastern
- **Shakshuka** — Don't overcook the eggs. The yolks should still be runny. Serve with crusty bread.
- **Hummus** — Peel the chickpeas for the smoothest texture. Ice water while blending makes it fluffier.

### Indian
- **Chicken Curry** — Toast the curry powder briefly in oil to bloom the spices. Don't rush the onion base.

### American
- **Pancakes** — Don't overmix; lumps are fine. Let the batter rest 5 minutes. Flip only once when bubbles pop.
- **Mac and Cheese** — Make a proper roux (butter + flour) before adding milk. Gruyère mixed with cheddar is excellent.
- **Banana Bread** — The riper the bananas, the better. Dark, spotty skins are ideal.

### French
- **Crepes** — Let the batter rest 30 minutes. Use a non-stick pan. The first crepe is always a test.

## Cooking Techniques

### Sautéing vs. Frying
- **Sauté**: medium-high heat, small amount of fat, food moved constantly.
- **Pan-fry**: medium heat, more fat (¼ inch), food turned once or twice.
- **Deep-fry**: 175-190°C, food fully submerged.

## Seasoning Tips
- Salt pasta water "like the sea" — it's the only chance to season the pasta itself.
- Acid (lemon, lime, vinegar) brightens dishes the way salt deepens them.
- Toast whole spices in a dry pan for 30-60 seconds before grinding or using.

## Storage Quick Guide
- Cooked rice: refrigerate within 1 hour, use within 3 days.
- Cooked chicken: 3-4 days refrigerated.
- Tomatoes: keep at room temperature; refrigerating kills flavour.
- Avocados: ripen on counter, then refrigerate up to 3 days.
