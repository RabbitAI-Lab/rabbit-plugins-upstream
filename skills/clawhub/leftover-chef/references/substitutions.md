# Ingredient Substitutions

Common substitutions the agent can suggest when ingredients are missing.

## Dairy
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| Butter | Margarine, ghee, olive oil (for cooking), coconut oil |
| Milk | Oat milk, soy milk, almond milk, water + extra butter |
| Heavy cream | ¾ cup milk + ¼ cup melted butter |
| Buttermilk | 1 cup milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar (let sit 5 min) |
| Sour cream | Plain yogurt, crème fraîche |
| Cream cheese | Blended cottage cheese, mascarpone |
| Parmesan | Pecorino Romano, Grana Padano, nutritional yeast (vegan) |

## Eggs
| Missing | Substitute (per egg) |
|---------|----------------------|
| Egg (baking) | 1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water; ½ mashed banana; ¼ cup applesauce |
| Egg (binding) | 2 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water |
| Egg (coating) | Milk or water dip before breading |

## Flours & Starches
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| All-purpose flour | 1:1 gluten-free blend, spelt flour |
| Breadcrumbs | Crushed crackers, oats, panko |
| Cornstarch | Arrowroot powder, potato starch (2 tsp per 1 tbsp cornstarch) |
| Baking powder | ¼ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp cream of tartar |

## Oils & Fats
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| Olive oil | Canola oil, vegetable oil, grapeseed oil |
| Sesame oil | Peanut oil + a dash of soy sauce |
| Vegetable oil | Any neutral oil (canola, sunflower) |

## Sugars
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| White sugar | Brown sugar, honey (use ¾ amount), maple syrup |
| Brown sugar | 1 cup white sugar + 1 tbsp molasses |
| Powdered sugar | Blend 1 cup white sugar + 1 tbsp cornstarch |

## Herbs & Spices
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| Fresh basil | Dried basil (use ⅓ amount), fresh spinach + oregano |
| Fresh parsley | Dried parsley (⅓), fresh cilantro |
| Fresh ginger | ¼ tsp ground ginger per tbsp fresh; ginger paste |
| Fresh garlic | ¼ tsp garlic powder per clove; 1 tsp pre-minced jar garlic |
| Curry powder | Garam masala + turmeric |
| Chili powder | Cayenne (use less!), paprika + cumin |

## Proteins
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| Ground beef | Ground turkey, lentils, mushrooms + beans (vegetarian) |
| Chicken | Turkey, firm tofu, chickpeas |
| Fish | Another firm white fish; chicken breast |
| Bacon | Smoked paprika + oil, turkey bacon, pancetta |

## Vegetables
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| Bell pepper | Any colour pepper, celery + carrot |
| Onion | Shallot, leek, scallion whites |
| Tomato (fresh) | Canned diced tomato (drained), tomato puree |
| Potato | Sweet potato, cauliflower (low-carb) |
| Broccoli | Cauliflower, broccolini |

## Pantry
| Missing | Substitute |
|---------|-----------|
| White wine | Chicken/veg broth + 1 tsp lemon juice or vinegar |
| Soy sauce | Tamari, coconut aminos, Worcestershire + salt |
| Rice wine vinegar | Apple cider vinegar + pinch of sugar |
| Chicken broth | Vegetable broth, water + bouillon cube |
| Coconut milk | Heavy cream, yogurt thinned with milk |
| Tahini | Peanut butter (smooth), sunflower seed butter |
