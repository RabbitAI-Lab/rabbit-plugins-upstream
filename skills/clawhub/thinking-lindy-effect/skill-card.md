## Description:

Use when longevity of a non-perishable option matters. Treat survival duration as a remaining-life prior, then check domain drift before favoring the proven.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, architects, and technical decision makers use this skill to evaluate long-lived technology, dependency, pattern, and skill bets where survival history should influence the decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Lindy heuristic can be over-applied to perishable vendors, products, hardware, or throwaway work where longevity is not the main decision factor.

Mitigation: Apply the skill only to non-perishable concepts, technologies, and practices; for perishable targets, score fit, risk, and exit cost separately.

Risk: A newer option can be rejected solely because it is young even when it closes a necessary present gap.

Mitigation: Use the documented burden-of-proof and domain-drift checks, and accept newer options when they meet a necessary advantage the older option cannot meet at acceptable cost.
