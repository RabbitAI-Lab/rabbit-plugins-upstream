## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, checklists, lightweight analyses, and user-facing positioning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns rough product concepts into repeatable workflows, checklists, templates, analyses, code-oriented support, and validation notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad startup, SaaS, prototype, and validation trigger wording may activate the skill for loosely related business requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review the user's request before relying on the skill output, and tighten trigger wording if routing precision is important. <br>
Risk: Product validation plans may be misleading when user inputs, constraints, or success criteria are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions visible, ask only for missing information that materially changes the output, and verify the final plan against stated success criteria. <br>
