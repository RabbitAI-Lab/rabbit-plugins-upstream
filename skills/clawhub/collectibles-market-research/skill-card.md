## Description:

Helps collectors research trading cards and sports collectibles using PSA reference data, marketplace listings, auction results, sold items, and market trends through Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Collectors and researchers use this skill to assemble sourced buying and market-research briefs, comparing card identity, grades, live asking prices, bids, and sold prices without treating them as authentication or guaranteed appraisals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, certification numbers, and route parameters are sent to Crawlora using a Crawlora API key.

Mitigation: Use only a key and query details you are comfortable sharing with Crawlora.

Risk: Listings, auction results, and PSA guide values can be mistaken for proof of authenticity or a guaranteed appraisal.

Mitigation: Cite sources, distinguish asking prices from completed sales, and present findings as research evidence rather than authentication, investment advice, or a purchase recommendation.
