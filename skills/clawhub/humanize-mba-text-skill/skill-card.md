## Description: <br>
Detects AI writing patterns in Chinese MBA thesis drafts and provides guidance for making the text read more naturally while preserving academic style. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[stephenlzc](https://clawhub.ai/user/stephenlzc) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, editors, and academic-support users use this skill to detect AI-like patterns in Chinese MBA thesis drafts and receive concrete rewrite guidance. It should be used for transparent editing, formatting, and clarity work that complies with school or publisher rules. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can be used to hide AI authorship or bypass AI detectors for submitted academic work. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for transparent editing, formatting, and clarity work that complies with school or publisher rules, and disclose AI assistance where required. <br>
Risk: Similarity-reduction and plagiarism-check guidance can support academic misconduct if used to disguise copied work. <br>
Mitigation: Use similarity checks to identify passages that need proper quotation, citation, or original analysis, not to lower scores for submission. <br>
Risk: Rewriting may change citations, data, claims, or intended meaning. <br>
Mitigation: Preview changes before applying them and verify all citations, data, factual claims, and meaning against source materials. <br>
