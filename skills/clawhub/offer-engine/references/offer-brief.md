# Offer brief guidance

Build one primary offer from opportunity evidence carried with Run
Authorization. The authorization permits private offer drafting without
another approval and never permits external execution. Separate facts,
evidence-backed observations, assumptions, and open questions. A price,
outcome, buyer preference, or demand statement remains an assumption unless its
claim ledger gives a URL, source class, and review date.

## Required brief shape

Use the twelve headings in `offer-engine/SKILL.md` exactly. Define one buyer,
one promised deliverable, a bounded scope, an honest price hypothesis, proof
requirements, exclusions, delivery method and time, a safe risk reversal, and
the smallest payable version. Link material claims to the opportunity evidence
ledger and show unit economics from `unit-economics.md`.

## Illustrative examples

These are offer shapes, not claims that these buyers have demand or will pay.
Validate them against current opportunity evidence before use.

### Roofer

**Example offer:** a permissioned missed-call and estimate-follow-up audit with
a call-log taxonomy, approved callback script drafts, and a two-week measurement
sheet. Exclude answering calls, sending messages, changing the CRM, or claiming
revenue recovery. The smallest payable version is a fixed-price audit of one
week of authorized, redacted data. A business owner must approve data access,
scripts, channel, timing, and every customer communication.

### Lawyer

**Example offer:** a licensed firm's internal intake-workflow map and public
FAQ inventory for qualified attorney review. Exclude personalized legal advice,
legal conclusions, client-data processing outside authorized controls, and any
public legal claim without qualified review. The smallest payable version is a
fixed-scope internal workflow review using sanitized, authorized materials.

### Creator

**Example offer:** an approved content-to-offer measurement pack: a content
inventory, disclosed call-to-action variants, and a tracking worksheet.
Exclude undisclosed endorsements, fake scarcity, fabricated testimonials,
platform-policy violations, guaranteed conversion, and posting without
approval. The smallest payable version is one approved content batch analysis
and draft package, with results labeled as Pipeline signal or Simulation as
appropriate.

### Technical operator

**Example offer:** an authorized workflow bottleneck audit with a reproducible
handoff, measured baseline, and one reviewed implementation recommendation.
Exclude production deployment, access outside authorization, security claims
without evidence, and uptime or savings guarantees. The smallest payable version
is a fixed-scope audit of a non-production or authorized environment.

### Beginner service

**Example offer:** a fixed-scope spreadsheet cleanup for a reachable,
permissioned local business: deduplicate approved records, normalize selected
columns, and return a change log. Exclude unsanctioned data collection,
financial advice, guarantees of revenue impact, and bulk outreach. The smallest
payable version is one approved sample file with an agreed turnaround and
acceptance check.

For every example, test delivery authority, privacy, regulations, platform
rules, and unit economics before proposing a payable version. If a condition is
unknown, pause and use a smaller manual discovery step.

## Run handoff and external action

Return the completed Offer Brief to `moneyprinter` so it can update Money
Machine Run and continue to payable-test; do not wait for a separate offer
approval. Keep any buyer-facing use staged before its exact gate. After an
immediately preceding exact approval, the current host may perform only that
approved action, record an `Action receipt`, and return it to `moneyprinter`.
Broad, batch, stale, or materially changed permission is invalid.
