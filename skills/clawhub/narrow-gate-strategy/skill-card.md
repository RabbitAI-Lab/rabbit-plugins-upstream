## Description: <br>
Narrow Gate Strategy helps agents coach users through choosing and evaluating hard, durable, compounding strategic paths with a structured Narrow Gate Map. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and strategy-focused agents use this skill to decide whether to persist, pivot, or invest in a difficult long-horizon capability, market, technology, or moat-building path. It is intended for cases where the hard path must be tested for rightness, compounding value, phase transitions, and survivability. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill links to external reference material and may introduce bilingual phase terminology. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when that strategy-coaching framework and its external references are appropriate for the agent's use case. <br>
Risk: A user may frame a merely hard path as a narrow gate and rationalize sunk cost. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's stop rule: require an explicit compounding mechanism, phase-transition criteria, and survivability through Phase 2 before recommending persistence. <br>
