## Description:

Coaches users to evaluate long-horizon strategies by testing whether a difficult path builds durable value, compounds over phases, and remains survivable.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders, strategists, and teams use this skill to assess whether to persist with or pivot from a difficult long-term initiative. It guides them through alternatives, durable value, compounding mechanisms, phase milestones, and resource survivability, producing a Narrow Gate Map.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategic guidance may rely on assumptions that do not fit the organization's circumstances.

Mitigation: Share only business information intended for the agent to use, and review the framework's assumptions before making consequential decisions.
