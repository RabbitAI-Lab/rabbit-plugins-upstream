## Description: <br>
Writes a professional staged payment-reminder email sequence for overdue invoices, escalating from a friendly nudge to a final notice while preserving the client relationship and flagging legal or fee language for confirmation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business owners, finance teams, and operators use this skill to draft staged overdue-invoice reminder emails that include invoice details, payment instructions, and appropriate escalation notes without presenting legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafting reminders may involve invoice amounts, client names, payment terms, and payment links. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the business details needed for the message and review all client, invoice, and payment information before sending. <br>
Risk: Late fees, interest, and escalation language may vary by jurisdiction or contract. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm fee, interest, and escalation language with an accountant or lawyer before using it. <br>
