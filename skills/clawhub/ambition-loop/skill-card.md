## Description:

Helps agents deepen substantial work tasks with measurable targets, appropriate methods, outside verification, and an honest gap report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linkxzhou](https://clawhub.ai/user/linkxzhou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other professionals use this skill to turn substantial coding, analysis, research, and writing requests into measurable work products, with independent checks and clear reporting of what remains unproven. It excludes personal or career goals and explicitly minimal or urgent tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may expand a task beyond the quick or narrow answer the user wanted.

Mitigation: Apply the fit check and honor explicitly minimal scope before expanding the work.

Risk: An expanded plan may require spending, new access, destructive changes, public actions, or a materially broader scope.

Mitigation: Present the action, cost, risk, and smaller options; obtain explicit approval before proceeding.
