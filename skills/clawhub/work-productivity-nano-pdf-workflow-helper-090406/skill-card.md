## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be selected for broad PDF, editing, or similar generic requests because implicit invocation is enabled and trigger terms are broad. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation by skill name, and narrow or disable implicit invocation in managed configurations when precise routing is required. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow or implementation guidance could be incomplete for a user's specific PDF, reliability, or setup context. <br>
Mitigation: Require the agent output to state assumptions, validate against the user's success criteria, and list remaining risks or follow-up work. <br>
