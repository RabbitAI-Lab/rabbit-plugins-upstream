Helps agents plan and produce video content using AI video generation tools, AI avatars, editing workflows, and programmatic video frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to choose video production approaches, write prompts and scripts, generate or assemble assets, and produce demos, explainers, social clips, ads, and repeatable video templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Examples may ask agents to run mutable third-party npm commands or packages.

Mitigation: Review and pin package versions before execution, and run examples in a clean sandbox without unrelated secrets.

Risk: Avatar and video workflows may involve likeness, voice, scripts, customer data, or proprietary assets shared with third-party vendors.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, rights, data handling approval, and vendor suitability before uploading sensitive media or generating avatar content.
