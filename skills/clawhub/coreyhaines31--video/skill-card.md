This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and developers use this skill to choose video production approaches, draft prompts and scripts, create beat-sheet edit specs, and assemble agent-native workflows for demos, explainers, social clips, ads, avatars, and templated videos. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read named product-marketing context files that could contain sensitive strategy or customer details. <br>
Mitigation: Review product-marketing context files before use and keep secrets or unrelated confidential information out of those files. <br>
Risk: Video guidance may rely on external paid tools, APIs, or model services with changing pricing, availability, and quality. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm current tool availability, pricing, account limits, and output quality before committing to a production workflow. <br>
Risk: Reverse-engineering a reference edit can create originality risk if the reference's footage, script, music, or exact creative expression is copied. <br>
Mitigation: Use the beat sheet to copy reusable editing grammar only, and apply it to the user's own footage, message, licensed audio, and brand assets. <br>
