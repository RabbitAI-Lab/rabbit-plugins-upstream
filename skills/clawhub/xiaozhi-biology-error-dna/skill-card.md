## Description:

Analyzes Chinese middle- and high-school biology mistakes across six dimensions to identify root causes and suggest targeted practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to identify recurring biology misconception patterns, plan focused practice, and request weakness reports. Learning-profile updates require student consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language middle- and high-school biology learning)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student weakness records may be retained or shared beyond the learner's expectations.

Mitigation: Confirm profile, cross-skill sharing, reminder, and parent-sharing consent settings before recording or sharing student data.
