## Description:

Analyzes Chinese middle- and high-school biology mistakes across six dimensions to identify underlying weaknesses and suggest targeted practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese middle- and high-school students use this skill to understand recurring biology mistakes, receive targeted practice, and request a report of learning weaknesses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese K–12 learning context; localize before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students' learning profiles could be retained or shared without effective consent controls.

Mitigation: Confirm the host enforces consent, pause, deletion, export, and cross-skill sharing controls before enabling persistent profiles.

Risk: Curriculum guidance or crisis referrals may be inappropriate outside the intended Chinese K–12 setting.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language, consent rules, and crisis contacts before deployment in another setting.
