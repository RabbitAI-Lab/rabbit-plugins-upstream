## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school biology errors across six dimensions and identifies recurring weak areas with targeted practice guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to find whether recurring biology mistakes come from concepts, structure and function, graphs, experiments, terminology, or calculations, then receive focused practice guidance. It analyzes learning records only with the student's authorization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cross-skill profile sharing may expose a learner's error history beyond this skill; the security review reports medium confidence because the profile-writeback reference was unresolved.

Mitigation: Review sharing settings before enabling cross-skill writeback, require applicable learner consent, and keep sharing off when it is not needed.
