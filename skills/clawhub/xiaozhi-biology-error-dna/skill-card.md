## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school biology mistakes across six dimensions, identifies likely root causes, and summarizes learning weaknesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to understand recurring causes of biology errors and review weak areas. It can create or update learning records only with the student's consent and applicable sharing permissions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records may expose personal learning information if sharing preferences or record ownership are not respected.

Mitigation: Save or share records only with the required consent, honor pause and deletion controls, and verify record ownership before updates.

Risk: An integration-format mismatch could cause a handoff or record update to be interpreted incorrectly.

Mitigation: Validate handoffs against the shared protocol schemas and enforce authorization separately from schema validation.
