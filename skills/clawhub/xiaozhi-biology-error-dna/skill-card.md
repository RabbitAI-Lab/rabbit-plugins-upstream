## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school biology mistakes across six dimensions to identify underlying weak points and provide targeted study guidance and optional weakness reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking secondary-school students use the skill to understand recurring biology errors, focus practice on specific weak points, and request a progress report. With consent, it can maintain a biology learning profile and share limited analysis with related study skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent language, and crisis-help channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's biology weak-point history could be retained or shared beyond their wishes.

Mitigation: Confirm consent before persistent records or cross-skill sharing; honor pause, deletion, and parent-sharing controls.

Risk: Chinese curriculum assumptions and mainland crisis contacts may misdirect students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and help channels before deployment outside mainland China; use locally appropriate emergency contacts.
