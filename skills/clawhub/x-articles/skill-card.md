## Description: <br>
X Articles helps agents draft, format, and publish long-form X (Twitter) Articles using viral content patterns, X-specific formatting guidance, and local browser automation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[nextfrontierbuilds](https://clawhub.ai/user/nextfrontierbuilds) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, marketers, and agent users use this skill to prepare X-ready article drafts, convert Markdown into plain text compatible with X Articles, and load content into a logged-in browser for manual review and publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can act through a browser that is already logged into X. <br>
Mitigation: Review the account, article text, media, and final publish dialog before posting, and keep the CDP browser local and limited to the intended account. <br>
