## Description:

Helps students track study time, identify productive periods, use Pomodoro sessions, and reflect on distractions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 students use this skill to log study time, run focus sessions, and review patterns in their attention. Long-term records, reminders, and sharing require separate opt-in consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); localization required for other regions

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student focus records, reminders, or cross-skill sharing may expose personal study patterns.

Mitigation: Leave each feature off until separately authorized by the student and, where required, a guardian; review pause, delete, export, and sharing controls.

Risk: Chinese K–12 defaults and emergency resources may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended audience and localize consent requirements and support resources before deployment elsewhere.
