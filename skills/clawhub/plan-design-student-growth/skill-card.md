## Use Case: <br>
Design students and education-planning users use this skill to create practical growth roadmaps for design majors, software learning, course projects, competitions, portfolios, graduate entrance preparation, internships, and employment planning. The skill tailors plans to the user's current major, stage, abilities, time budget, goals, and project evidence. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: School admissions requirements, competition rules, software versions, and job requirements may change after the skill's knowledge snapshot. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current requirements against official sources before making enrollment, submission, software, or career decisions. <br>
Risk: Educational plans may be overinterpreted as outcome guarantees for admission, awards, employment, or course fit. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance, preserve stated assumptions, and avoid relying on the skill for guarantees or final eligibility decisions. <br>
Risk: Users may provide unnecessary sensitive personal information while discussing academic or career plans. <br>
Mitigation: Do not request IDs, phone numbers, school accounts, exam credentials, or other unnecessary personal data for routine planning. <br>
