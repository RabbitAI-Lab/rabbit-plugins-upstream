## Description: <br>
Helps agents diagnose brand trust breakdowns by auditing functional reliability, customer recovery behavior, and identity alignment before recommending layer-specific interventions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business strategists, marketers, founders, and customer experience teams use this skill to locate the lowest broken layer of brand trust and choose an operational, recovery, or identity mechanism grounded in behavioral evidence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may apply advisory brand-trust guidance to active legal, crisis-communication, or product-engineering failures that the skill excludes. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only after urgent legal or crisis response needs and core product failures are handled by qualified owners. <br>
Risk: Recommendations can be misleading when behavioral data is missing or perception surveys are treated as sufficient evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Require behavioral metrics, document data gaps, and verify the constraint layer before selecting a mechanism. <br>
