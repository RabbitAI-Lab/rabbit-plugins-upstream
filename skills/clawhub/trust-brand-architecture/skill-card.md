## Description:

Provides a three-layer framework for diagnosing brand trust gaps and planning behavioral, layer-specific improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand and marketing teams use this skill to assess customer trust through behavioral metrics, identify the weakest trust layer, and plan measurable operational improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations based on incomplete or unapproved customer metrics could lead to poor business changes.

Mitigation: Use approved customer metrics, document data gaps, and review recommendations before making business changes.

Risk: The framework does not address immediate legal or crisis-response needs.

Mitigation: Use appropriate legal and crisis-response support first when an urgent incident requires it.
