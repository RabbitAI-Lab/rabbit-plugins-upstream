## Description:

Create, edit, review, publish, manage, or analyze AI-Shifu (AI师傅) courses and their Teaching Prompts and Course Prompts in MarkdownFlow (MDF). Use for course materials, lesson design, interactions, course memory, images, deployment, platform administration, learner progress, learner profile distributions, orders, revenue, and credit-consumption questions.

## Use Case:

Course authors and educators use this skill to plan and write learner-facing course content, Teaching Prompts, and Course Prompts for AI-Shifu. They can also use it to review, deploy, manage, and analyze courses through the platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing can expose course content to a wider audience than intended.

Mitigation: Keep work local or draft-only when publication is not intended, and verify course access settings before publishing.

Risk: Re-importing an existing course may replace lessons and reset their permissions.

Mitigation: For restricted courses, prefer individual lesson updates; re-import only when replacing lessons and resetting permissions is intended.
