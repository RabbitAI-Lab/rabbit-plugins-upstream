## Description: <br>
Guides Chinese-language, multi-round screening of early-stage one-person-company projects across team fit, market validation, technical feasibility, business model, finance, and risk. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, incubator operators, and project reviewers use this skill to facilitate a five-round OPC startup screening conversation and produce a consensus-based evaluation summary with action items. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive founder background, financial details, risks, or business plans during the screening conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Use only information approved for the agent environment, redact confidential details where possible, and avoid sharing business secrets unless the deployment context permits it. <br>
