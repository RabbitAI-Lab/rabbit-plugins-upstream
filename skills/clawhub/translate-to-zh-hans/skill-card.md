## Description:

Translate one user-selected article from ai-thoughts/docs/ into Simplified Chinese and save it as a same-name -zh-hans.md file.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and developers use this skill to translate one explicitly selected article into Simplified Chinese while preserving its structure and technical terms. It requires approval before creating or overwriting the translated file.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A translation or source attribution could be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Review the translated article and verify its source attribution before publishing.

Risk: An existing translation could be overwritten unintentionally.

Mitigation: Check for an existing translation and obtain explicit overwrite approval before writing.
