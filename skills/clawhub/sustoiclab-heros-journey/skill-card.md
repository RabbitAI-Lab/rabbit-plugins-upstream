## Description:

坎贝尔《千面英雄》叙事结构教练。用"单一神话/英雄旅程三幕12阶段"帮你诊断文章或故事的叙事缺口、强化钩子与转折。触发词：千面英雄、英雄之旅、英雄旅程、叙事结构、故事结构、单一神话、三幕、写作结构、文章骨架、叙事诊断。

## Use Case:

External writers and content teams use this skill to self-diagnose story or article structure with the 12-stage Hero's Journey framework. It guides stage-by-stage reflection in Chinese and helps identify missing narrative turns, weak emotional anchors, and stronger endings without writing the content for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad writing-structure requests where the Hero's Journey framework is not desired.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for Hero's Journey narrative diagnosis and ignore it for unrelated editing, factual-writing, or non-structural writing tasks.

Risk: Narrative coaching can steer a user's draft toward a specific story framework even when another structure would fit better.

Mitigation: Treat the prompts as diagnostic guidance and keep final story structure decisions with the writer.
