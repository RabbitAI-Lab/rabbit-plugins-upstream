## Description: <br>
Career Coach Ai helps users with career planning, resume improvement, interview practice, salary negotiation, promotion planning, job changes, and workplace communication using structured coaching frameworks and action-oriented next steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this agent for career coaching tasks such as clarifying goals, improving resumes, practicing interviews, preparing salary or promotion conversations, and working through workplace communication decisions. The skill emphasizes structured diagnosis, templates or scripts, and one concrete next action for the current week. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may persist career-coaching intake details across sessions, including sensitive workplace context. <br>
Mitigation: Ask before saving details, avoid storing employer names, coworker names, compensation specifics, or sensitive workplace conflicts, and honor requests to remove stored notes. <br>
Risk: Career coaching output can influence employment decisions or touch emotional distress, legal, medical, or psychological topics. <br>
Mitigation: Present guidance as options and tradeoffs, avoid guarantees or fabricated data, use official or current sources for market claims, and direct legal, medical, psychological, or crisis concerns to qualified real-world support. <br>
