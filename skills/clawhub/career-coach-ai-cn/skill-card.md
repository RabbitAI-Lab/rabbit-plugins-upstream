## Description: <br>
Career Coach Ai is a Chinese-language career coaching skill that helps users with career planning, resume optimization, interview practice, salary negotiation, promotion planning, workplace communication, and job transition decisions using structured frameworks such as GROW, Ikigai, SWOT, SMART, and STAR. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, job seekers, and career changers use this skill for structured career reflection, resume revisions, interview preparation, workplace communication scripts, salary negotiation planning, and concrete next-step action plans. It is intended to support decisions with options and tradeoffs rather than make major life or career decisions for the user. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive career, salary, company, or personal information while seeking coaching. <br>
Mitigation: Ask only for relevant context, avoid identity documents, banking details, passwords, and unnecessarily specific names, and use redacted company and person names where possible. <br>
Risk: Career, salary, legal, medical, or mental-health-adjacent guidance may be mistaken for definitive professional advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present options, tradeoffs, and scripts without guarantees; tell users to verify current market data and consult licensed professionals for legal, medical, psychological, or crisis concerns. <br>
Risk: The skill can retain career context when persistence is available, which may preserve information the user did not intend to keep. <br>
Mitigation: Keep persisted context limited to useful career-coaching facts and avoid storing sensitive identifiers or private third-party details. <br>
