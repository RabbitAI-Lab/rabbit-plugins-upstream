## Description: <br>
T3go helps agents provide Chinese and English guidance for selecting and using T3 ride-hailing, including B2C platform positioning, compliance and safety considerations, enterprise ride use, and comparisons with Didi or Cao Cao. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to answer questions about when T3 ride-hailing may fit daily, business, peak-hour, night, or compliance-sensitive travel scenarios. The skill also helps structure comparisons with Didi, Cao Cao, and aggregator-platform options. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may provide stale information about prices, availability, coverage cities, or compliance rankings because it does not use live data. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current details in the relevant ride-hailing app or official/public sources before acting on the guidance. <br>
