## Description: <br>
Helps personal content creators plan AI-assisted music generation with platform selection, lyric drafting, style and BPM guidance, basic music theory support, and copyright reminders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External content creators and independent developers use this skill to choose AI music platforms, draft lyrics and prompts, match musical styles, and check basic usage limitations before generating music. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording and execution/write permissions could cause an agent to apply the skill to unrelated media or automation tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Limit use to music generation guidance, lyric drafting, BPM/style selection, and platform-selection advice; review generated actions before execution. <br>
Risk: Generated music may be subject to third-party platform limits or noncommercial terms. <br>
Mitigation: Check the selected music platform's current terms and required subscription level before publishing or monetizing generated output. <br>
