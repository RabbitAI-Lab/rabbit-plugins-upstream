## Description:

WeChat Article Pro helps an agent research current topics, draft 3,000-5,000 word WeChat public-account articles, generate a cover image through WeChat's editor, format the article, and save it as a draft.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agentforge-cyber](https://clawhub.ai/user/agentforge-cyber)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to have an agent research a topic, compose a long-form WeChat public-account article, prepare an AI-generated cover image, and save the result as a draft for human review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses a logged-in WeChat public-platform session to create and save article drafts.

Mitigation: Install only when this account access is intended, and keep final publication as a manual human action.

Risk: Generated article facts, writing style, and cover images may be inaccurate or unsuitable for the publisher's brand.

Mitigation: Review drafts, cited facts, generated cover images, and style before publication.
