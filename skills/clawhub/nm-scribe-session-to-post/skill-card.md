## Description: <br>
Converts a Claude Code session into a blog post, case study, or Reddit post. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering teams use this skill after a completed Claude Code session to turn real work into publishable blog posts, case studies, social threads, or Reddit posts grounded in session evidence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated posts may expose private implementation details, unreleased plans, repository links, or claims that have not been verified. <br>
Mitigation: Review the draft before publication and remove sensitive details, private links, and unsupported claims; use the skill's proof-of-work guidance for claims that need evidence. <br>
