## Description:

Write scroll-stopping opening beats for video and image ads using 17 named hook mechanics, with rules for which ones fit which product.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hermoso-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/hermoso-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, creators, and ad writers use this skill to choose a visual opening mechanic and draft the first seconds of video and image ads. It helps tailor hooks to the product and format, including UGC, product-only, software, and regulated categories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Advertising hooks may include factual claims that are inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Check factual advertising claims against reliable product evidence before use.

Risk: Publishing ads or running paid tests can trigger external spending or public claims.

Mitigation: Separately authorize publishing and paid testing.
