## Description:

Creates Bilibili upload copy and a matching landscape 16:9 thumbnail from a video's topic, title, outline, or finished script.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bilibili creators use this skill to prepare submission-ready titles, descriptions, tags, chapter markers, pinned-comment prompts, and a matching thumbnail for a video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared account authorization grants media-generation and voice-management access beyond the skill's stated copywriting and thumbnail tasks.

Mitigation: Review the requested access before connecting and proceed only if you accept those broader permissions.

Risk: Installation reporting occurs automatically during use.

Mitigation: Be aware of the automatic reporting and avoid use in environments where it is unacceptable.

Risk: Thumbnail generation may incur a displayed price.

Mitigation: Review and approve the displayed price for each thumbnail separately before generation.

Risk: The shared device connection may remain authorized after use.

Mitigation: Revoke the shared device connection in Beatra Console when it is no longer needed.
