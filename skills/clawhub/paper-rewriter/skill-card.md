## Description:

Helps scholars review and improve Chinese and English academic prose with local style checks, mechanical cleanup, guided revision, and integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars and academic authors use this skill to identify formulaic phrasing, revise their own Chinese or English manuscripts for clarity, and check that numbers, references, and terminology survive editing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Revisions may alter numbers, citations, or technical terms.

Mitigation: Run the integrity check and personally review the revised manuscript and reports before using them.

Risk: Style checks or cleanup could be mistaken for proof of authorship or a substitute for required AI-use disclosure.

Mitigation: Treat scores as writing feedback only and follow applicable journal or institutional disclosure rules.

Risk: Local drafts are read and reports or rewrites are saved to chosen output paths.

Mitigation: Run the scripts only on drafts you intend to process and review output paths before execution.
