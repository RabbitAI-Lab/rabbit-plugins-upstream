## Description:

Helps authors review and revise Chinese and English academic prose with local style checks, mechanical cleanup, and factual-integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars and other authors use this skill to self-check and improve the clarity and naturalness of Chinese or English manuscripts while preserving numbers, citations, and terminology. Authors must review revisions and comply with their institution's or journal's AI-use disclosure rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewrites may alter facts, citations, or specialized terminology.

Mitigation: Review each revision and use the integrity check with an author-confirmed term list before accepting it.

Risk: Style scores could be mistaken for evidence of authorship or compliance.

Mitigation: Treat scores as writing-pattern heuristics for self-review, not proof of authorship or regulatory compliance.

Risk: Editing AI-assisted work could obscure disclosure obligations.

Mitigation: Keep required AI-use disclosures and follow applicable institution and journal policies.

Risk: Chinese command-line messages may be hard to interpret when working in English.

Mitigation: Review the report carefully and consult the supplied examples and error reference before relying on a result.
