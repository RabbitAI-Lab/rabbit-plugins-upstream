## Description:

Helps scholars improve Chinese and English academic prose through local style checks, text cleanup, guided revision, and integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic authors use this skill to review stiff or templated drafts, clean up writing artifacts, and revise for clarity while checking that numbers, references, and terminology remain intact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewriting may change factual meaning or remove required academic details.

Mitigation: Review revised text for accuracy and check numbers, citations, and terminology against the original draft.

Risk: Style cleanup could be misused to conceal AI involvement or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Edit only drafts you are authorized to revise, and retain all disclosures required by your school, journal, or platform.

Risk: Learned terminology guards persist locally after a revision session.

Mitigation: Review and remove saved guards when they are no longer needed.
