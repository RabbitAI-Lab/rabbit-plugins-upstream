## Description:

Helps authors review and improve Chinese and English academic prose through local style reports, text cleanup, guided revision, and checks that preserve factual details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic authors use this skill to self-check bilingual drafts, clean mechanical writing artifacts, revise for clarity, and compare edits while preserving numbers, citations, and terminology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Revisions or PDF text extraction may introduce inaccuracies or omit important manuscript details.

Mitigation: Review generated edits and extracted PDF text manually, and check the integrity report against the original draft.

Risk: Style scores can be mistaken for authorship evidence or used in place of required AI-use disclosure.

Mitigation: Treat reports as writing self-checks and follow the applicable institution or publisher's AI-use disclosure rules.
