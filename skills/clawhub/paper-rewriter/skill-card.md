## Description:

Helps authors revise Chinese and English academic drafts for clearer, more natural prose while checking that key facts and terminology survive editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic authors use this skill to assess formulaic prose, clean up draft text, plan revisions, and check that numbers, citations, and terminology remain intact. Authors remain responsible for reviewing edits and disclosing AI assistance when required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow reads supplied manuscripts and can write revised drafts, reports, and local settings.

Mitigation: Use only intended input paths, review output locations, and check generated files before sharing.

Risk: Rewriting could obscure required AI disclosure or unintentionally alter academic claims.

Mitigation: Disclose assistance as required, review revisions against the original, and run integrity checks for facts and terminology.
