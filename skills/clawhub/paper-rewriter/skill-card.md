## Description:

Helps authors review and improve Chinese and English academic prose through style checks, guided rewriting, mechanical cleanup, and integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and other authors use this skill to self-check and revise Chinese or English academic drafts for clarity and natural style while preserving key figures, citations, and terminology. Authors remain responsible for reviewing edits and disclosing AI assistance when required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An edit could change a manuscript's meaning or inadvertently alter protected facts and terminology.

Mitigation: Review revisions before submission and run the provided whole-document integrity guard on rewrites.

Risk: Use of writing assistance could obscure required AI-use disclosure or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for legitimate writing-quality review and disclose AI assistance where required.

Risk: Some instructions and command output are Chinese-first and may be misunderstood by other-language users.

Mitigation: Check the English guidance and confirm command results before relying on them.
