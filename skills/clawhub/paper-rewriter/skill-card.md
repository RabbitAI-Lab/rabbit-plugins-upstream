## Description:

Helps scholars revise Chinese and English academic prose through local style checks, text cleanup, and integrity-aware editing guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic authors use this skill to review stiff or templated writing, clean drafts, and check that revisions preserve key facts and terminology. It supports legitimate editing and disclosure, not concealment of AI assistance or misrepresentation of authorship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edits could alter meaning or remove important manuscript details.

Mitigation: Review generated edits and reports, and run integrity checks before relying on revised text.

Risk: Style revision could be misused to conceal AI assistance or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Use for legitimate self-review and disclose AI assistance where required.

Risk: Chinese- and China-oriented messages or compliance guidance may not fit every reader or jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable institutional and publication requirements independently.
