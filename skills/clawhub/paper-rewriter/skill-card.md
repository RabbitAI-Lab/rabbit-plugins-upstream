## Description:

Helps scholars review and improve Chinese and English academic prose with local style checks, deterministic cleanup, rewrite guidance, and integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars and academic writers use the skill to self-check stiff or templated Chinese and English drafts, clean mechanical writing artifacts, and plan clearer revisions while checking that numbers, citations, and terminology remain intact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style scores may be mistaken for proof of AI authorship or absence of AI use.

Mitigation: Treat scores and reports as heuristic writing aids, never as authorship verdicts.

Risk: Rewriting could be used to conceal required AI disclosure or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Use the toolkit for legitimate self-review and follow institutional and journal disclosure rules.

Risk: Revisions or imperfect PDF extraction may change or obscure manuscript details.

Mitigation: Run the integrity checks, spot-check extracted PDF text, and review facts and terminology before submission.
