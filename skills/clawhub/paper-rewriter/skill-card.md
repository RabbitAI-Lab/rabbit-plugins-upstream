## Description:

Helps authors self-check and improve Chinese and English academic prose through local style reports, text cleanup, guided revisions, and factual-integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and researchers use this skill to review and revise their own Chinese or English drafts for clarity while checking that citations, numbers, and key terminology remain intact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stylistic revisions may change a factual claim, citation, number, or important term.

Mitigation: Review every edit manually and run the integrity check before submitting a revised draft.

Risk: Style scores could be mistaken for an official AI-authorship or compliance determination.

Mitigation: Use reports only for writing self-review; follow applicable institutional and journal disclosure requirements.
