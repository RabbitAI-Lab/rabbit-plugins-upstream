## Description:

This bilingual academic writing toolkit helps authors identify formulaic phrasing, clean mechanical artifacts, and revise scholarly prose while checking that key facts and terminology remain intact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External scholars and academic authors use the skill to assess style patterns, clean text artifacts, plan revisions, and improve their own academic prose while following applicable AI disclosure rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scanner evidence reports mismatches between some rewriting behavior and stated preservation guarantees; inspection was also partial.

Mitigation: Review each revised passage against its source, especially numbers, identifiers, terminology, and intended meaning.
