## Description:

Helps scholars improve Chinese and English academic prose with local style checks, text cleanup, guided revision, and factual-integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic authors use this skill to self-review and revise stiff or templated manuscripts while preserving citations, numbers, and terminology. Style reports are writing aids, not authorship determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Revisions could alter facts, citations, or technical terminology.

Mitigation: Check the revised text against the original with the integrity guard and review all edits before use.

Risk: Writing-quality assistance could be misused to conceal required AI disclosure or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for legitimate self-review and follow applicable institutional and journal disclosure requirements.

Risk: Running local scripts can read manuscripts and write outputs at selected paths.

Mitigation: Run only on files you intend to process, choose output paths deliberately, and inspect reports before relying on them.
