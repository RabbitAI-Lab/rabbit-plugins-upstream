## Description:

Helps scholars revise Chinese and English academic drafts for clearer, more natural prose using local style checks, mechanical cleanup, and integrity guards for numbers, citations, and terminology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars and academic authors use this skill to self-review and revise stiff or templated Chinese and English manuscripts while checking that key factual details remain intact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The tool reads manuscripts and batch directories supplied by the user and writes drafts or reports to selected paths.

Mitigation: Use only manuscript and output paths you intend to expose to the local tool; check generated files before sharing.

Risk: Automated rewriting and style checks can produce misleading edits or assessments.

Mitigation: Review rewrites manually and verify factual details, citations, and terminology against the source.

Risk: Revisions could be used to misrepresent authorship or omit required AI-use disclosures.

Mitigation: Keep required disclosures and use the skill for legitimate quality editing, not to evade academic integrity review.
