## Description:

Helps scholars self-check and improve Chinese and English academic prose while preserving factual details and required AI-use disclosures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic authors use this skill to identify formulaic phrasing, clean up drafts, and plan clearer revisions without changing statistics, citations, or terminology. Authors review all revisions and retain any required AI-use disclosures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A rewrite could change a factual detail or technical term.

Mitigation: Review revisions against the original draft and check numbers, identifiers, and terminology before use.

Risk: Style self-checks could be mistaken for proof of authorship or grounds to skip disclosure.

Mitigation: Treat reports as writing feedback only and keep any required school, journal, or platform AI-use disclosures.

Risk: An experimental PDF extraction may produce unreliable text.

Mitigation: Inspect the extracted text before revising it; use an editable text export if extraction is unclear.
