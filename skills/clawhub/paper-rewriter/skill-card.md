## Description:

Provides bilingual academic writing style self-checks, deterministic cleanup, and revision guidance with integrity checks for numbers, citations, and terminology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars, especially bilingual and non-native English authors, use the skill to review and improve academic prose, remove mechanical residue, and compare revisions with their source text. Authors remain responsible for factual review and any required AI-use disclosure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Terminology replacement can corrupt citations, and the integrity checker can approve altered scientific numbers.

Mitigation: Work on copies with explicit output filenames, review every terminology replacement, and independently compare scientific numbers and citation identifiers. Treat PASS as one signal rather than proof that all data survived unchanged.
