## Description:

Helps scholars improve the clarity and natural style of Chinese and English academic drafts through local cleanup, revision guidance, and integrity checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars and academic authors use this skill to self-check and revise bilingual manuscripts, remove mechanical writing artifacts, and compare drafts while checking that numbers, citations, and terminology remain intact. Authors remain responsible for factual review and required AI-use disclosures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A revised draft or heuristic style report may be mistaken for verified facts or a reliable determination of AI authorship.

Mitigation: Review edits and reports before relying on them; use the integrity checks and verify claims against the original draft.

Risk: Academic use could omit a required disclosure of AI assistance.

Mitigation: Follow applicable journal and institutional disclosure rules; do not use revisions to misrepresent authorship.

Risk: Requested modes write local drafts, reports, guard settings, or batch resume files.

Mitigation: Choose output locations deliberately and review generated local files.
