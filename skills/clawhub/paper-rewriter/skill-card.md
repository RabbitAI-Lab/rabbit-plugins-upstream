## Description:

Helps scholars revise Chinese and English academic manuscripts with local style checks, guided rewriting, and checks for changes to numbers, citations, and terminology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic editors use this skill to identify formulaic writing, plan revisions, clean up manuscript text, and check that important facts and terminology survive a rewrite. It supports self-review rather than determining AI authorship or replacing required AI-use disclosure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style scores may be mistaken for evidence of AI authorship or a substitute for academic review.

Mitigation: Read revisions and reports manually; verify claims against the original manuscript and disclose AI assistance when required.

Risk: Batch progress and learned guard rules can remain in the working directory after use.

Mitigation: Review output locations and remove saved progress and custom guard files when no longer needed.
