## Description:

Helps scholars improve Chinese and English academic prose with style self-checks, mechanical cleanup, guided revision, and checks that facts and terminology survive editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Scholars and academic writing assistants use this toolkit to identify templated or stiff phrasing, revise drafts for clarity, and check that edits preserve numbers, citations, and terminology. Style scores are self-review aids, not evidence of authorship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Running the toolkit reads manuscripts and may write revised text or reports to chosen paths.

Mitigation: Use only manuscripts and output locations you authorize; review resulting files before sharing.

Risk: Stylistic rewrites or heuristic scores could be mistaken for proof of authorship or alter factual and citation meaning.

Mitigation: Treat scores as writing guidance, run integrity checks, review every revision for factual and citation accuracy, and make required AI-use disclosures.

Risk: Some user-facing reports and guidance are Chinese-heavy.

Mitigation: Check language suitability when preparing English-only deliverables.
