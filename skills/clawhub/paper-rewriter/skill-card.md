## Description:

Bilingual Chinese/English academic-writing toolkit for style self-checks, deterministic cleanup, integrity verification, and guided revision while preserving key facts and citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External scholars and academic or medical writers use this skill to identify templated prose, apply deterministic cleanup, and prepare self-reviewed revisions while preserving facts, numbers, citations, and terminology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Revision suggestions or edits may still introduce incorrect or misleading content, even when verification passes.

Mitigation: Keep the original manuscript and write edits to a separate output file; review every warning and manually verify facts and citations.

Risk: Generated reports may contain manuscript excerpts and external navigation links.

Mitigation: Review reports for sensitive manuscript excerpts and inspect external links before sharing or opening them.
