## Description:

将商家已批准的运费、配送时效、售后与商品规格政策整理为中文分类 FAQ 知识库；输出可直接复用的标准问答、原始政策依据和待补充字段，适用于店铺、自媒体选品页与小说周边销售。资料缺失时只标注缺口，不推测政策，也不代替客服向用户发送消息。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

中文小店经营者、创作者和客服知识库维护者可用它把已批准的运费、配送、售后与商品规格政策整理成可复用 FAQ，并标出无法依据资料回答的字段。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsupported or outdated policy details could become misleading customer-facing answers.

Mitigation: Use approved, current store policies as input and review each answer against them before publishing.

Risk: Fictional bundled examples could be mistaken for a real store's approved policies.

Mitigation: Treat examples as format demonstrations only; use the store's own approved policy text.
