## Description:

Helps create pet jewelry collar concepts, hand-drawn design sheets, multiview sketches, and interactive 3D viewer links from prompts and reference images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ai-hub-admin](https://clawhub.ai/user/ai-hub-admin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners and jewelry designers use this skill to explore custom collars for cats, dogs, rabbits, and other pets through design drafts, sketches, and 3D previews. The workflow also supports other jewelry categories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Design prompts, selected options, and reference image URLs are sent to a third-party gateway.

Mitigation: Use only approved, non-sensitive inputs and review the service's data handling before submitting designs.

Risk: Generated results may be available through shareable links.

Mitigation: Check link access before sharing and avoid using the workflow for confidential designs.
