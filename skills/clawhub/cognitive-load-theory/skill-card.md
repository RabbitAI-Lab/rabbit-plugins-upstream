## Description: <br>
Helps agents diagnose cognitive overload in training, onboarding, documentation, tutorials, and interfaces, then redesign instruction using cognitive load theory. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, developers, and instructional designers use this skill to redesign learning material, onboarding, documentation, tutorials, and user workflows when learners are overwhelmed or not understanding. It guides diagnosis of intrinsic, extraneous, and germane load, then produces a CLT-informed design and test plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad phrases such as "this is overwhelming" even when cognitive load is not the main issue. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the fit before applying CLT guidance, especially when the audience is expert or the bottleneck is motivational rather than cognitive. <br>
Risk: Real user or company examples could be added back into the skill during use. <br>
Mitigation: Do not persist sensitive examples into the skill unless that retention is intentional and approved. <br>
