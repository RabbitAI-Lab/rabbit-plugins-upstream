## Use Case:

External users use this skill during routine invoice preparation to turn an invoice currency and calculation preference into concise rounding guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake lightweight invoice rounding guidance for accounting, tax, or legal advice for regulated billing decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for routine rounding guidance only, and route regulated billing, accounting, tax, or legal decisions to qualified review.
