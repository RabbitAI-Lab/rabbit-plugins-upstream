## Description:

Provides concise invoice rounding guidance from a supplied currency and calculation preference.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People preparing invoices use this skill to draft rounding guidance for a supplied currency, decimal scale, rounding mode, and calculation stage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested rounding wording may not match the user's accounting policy.

Mitigation: Review the guidance against the applicable policy before using it on an invoice; the skill does not enforce policy or update records.
