## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance on avoiding common pitfalls when updating finished apartments, renovating older homes, or planning partial remodels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners use this skill to compare renovation options, identify budget and contractor pitfalls, and prepare questions for qualified local professionals. Its advice is for planning and discussion, not construction instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Examples may normalize unsafe wall demolition or balcony and doorway changes.

Mitigation: Do not treat examples as approval to demolish; obtain a structural assessment, property-management approval, required permits, and qualified local oversight before any changes.

Risk: Categorical waterproofing omissions could lead to leaks and damage.

Mitigation: Have a qualified local professional assess waterproofing and verify the plan against applicable building requirements.

Risk: Electrical or gas advice could be mistaken for actionable installation guidance.

Mitigation: Use the advice for discussion only; leave electrical work to licensed electricians and gas work to authorized gas professionals under local rules.
