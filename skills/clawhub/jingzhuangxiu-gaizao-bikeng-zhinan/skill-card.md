## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Structural, gas, electrical, and waterproofing changes can create safety or compliance risks if treated as do-it-yourself construction instructions.

Mitigation: Use the guide for planning and questions for qualified professionals; have licensed professionals perform regulated work, and obtain property-management and structural-professional confirmation before wall changes.

Risk: Advice about gas-meter enclosures and storage may be unsafe if applied without site-specific review.

Mitigation: Have the gas provider review enclosure and storage plans, preserve ventilation and maintenance access, and follow the cited official gas-safety guidance.
