## Description:

精装修改造避坑指南专用 Skill，覆盖老房旧房改造、厨房卫生间改造、墙地面翻新、拆改项目与顺序、预算报价与维权、精装修微改等全流程，帮助用户获得更省心、少踩坑的装修建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer Chinese-language questions about finished-home renovation, old-home upgrades, kitchen and bathroom changes, wall and floor refreshes, demolition order, budgeting, and dispute avoidance. It is best used for planning, comparison, and risk-awareness guidance rather than as professional construction, gas, electrical, structural, or permitting advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation guidance may touch gas appliances, wall removal, balcony enclosure, waterproofing, electrical work, or structural changes where bad advice can cause safety, legal, or property damage.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance only and require licensed professionals, permits, property-management approval, and local code checks before acting.

Risk: The reference corpus may contain retained sales language and opinionated phrasing.

Mitigation: Review responses for neutrality and remove promotional or overly certain claims before relying on them.
