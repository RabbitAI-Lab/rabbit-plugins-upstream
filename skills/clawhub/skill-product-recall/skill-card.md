## Description: <br>
Helps quality and compliance teams structure product recall trigger assessment, severity classification, notification matrices, timelines, and exercise plans, producing plain-text and Markdown recall reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality directors, compliance teams, and managers use this skill to draft recall plans, notification responsibilities, milestone timelines, and simulation tables from product, batch, risk, and jurisdiction-specific inputs. It is a drafting and decision-support aid, not a substitute for legal, compliance, or regulator decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recall plans may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction, company procedure, or regulatory obligation. <br>
Mitigation: Verify generated plans against applicable law, internal procedures, and legal or compliance review before acting. <br>
Risk: The skill may provide decision-support language that users could mistake for final recall, reporting, or regulator-submission decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as structured drafts and require accountable business, compliance, and legal approval for final decisions and external submissions. <br>
