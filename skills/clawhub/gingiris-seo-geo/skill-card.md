## Description: <br>
Gingiris Seo Geo is an SEO and Generative Engine Optimization playbook that helps agents plan keyword strategy, structure content, create comparison pages, add JSON-LD schema, configure IndexNow, and monitor AI-search citations for developer tools, open-source projects, and B2B SaaS products. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, marketers, founders, and developers use this skill to guide an agent through SEO and GEO workflows: keyword research, content briefs, structured data, comparison-page planning, IndexNow submission guidance, and AI-citation monitoring. It is optimized for dev tools, open-source projects, and B2B SaaS, with adaptation guidance for consumer products. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: IndexNow, analytics, and site-management workflows may involve API keys or privileged website access. <br>
Mitigation: Keep API keys and analytics access scoped to the relevant site, avoid sharing secrets in prompts or generated content, and review commands before execution. <br>
Risk: Generated SEO, GEO, or comparison-page claims may be inaccurate, outdated, promotional, or unsupported. <br>
Mitigation: Review claims before publishing, require source-backed evidence for core assertions, and mark unverifiable claims for follow-up rather than inventing data. <br>
Risk: Programmatic SEO or aggressive optimization can create thin pages, doorway-page patterns, or misleading competitor comparisons. <br>
Mitigation: Require each page to provide unique user value, keep competitor comparisons factual and sourced, and avoid keyword stuffing or mass-produced low-value pages. <br>
