## Description:

Improves Russian-language writing through typography, proofreading, editorial style, UX copy, business correspondence, and AI-text cleanup.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[talkstream](https://clawhub.ai/user/talkstream)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Russian-language writers, editors, and product teams use this skill to polish prose and interface text, check grammar and punctuation, and assess text quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Implicit invocation can silently change Russian typography, including text that must retain exact formatting.

Mitigation: Request verbatim reproduction or specify a preferred style when exact wording or machine-readable formatting matters; review the resulting text.
