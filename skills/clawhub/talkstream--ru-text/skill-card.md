## Description:

Applies Russian typography to agent responses and offers requested proofreading, editing, writing guidance, and text-quality assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[talkstream](https://clawhub.ai/user/talkstream)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Russian-speaking writers, editors, and teams use this skill to improve the typography and clarity of Russian text, proofread drafts, write interface or business copy, and assess text quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Always-on typography may subtly alter Russian wording, punctuation, spacing, or formatting when exact text preservation matters.

Mitigation: Request exact preservation or another style explicitly, and review edits before using them in sensitive text.
