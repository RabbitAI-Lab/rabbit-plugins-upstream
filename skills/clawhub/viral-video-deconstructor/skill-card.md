## Description:

Analyzes short videos or ideas across six platforms and produces hook summaries, shot-by-shot breakdowns, original scripts, and optional AI video prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use the skill to analyze short videos, draft original platform-specific scripts and storyboards, and review copy before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Transcription can automatically download and run unverified native dependencies from third-party or changing sources.

Mitigation: Set up dependencies manually in an isolated virtual environment; verify FFmpeg from an official source against a pinned checksum and avoid the proxy unless explicitly trusted.

Risk: Generated scripts can contain copyright, platform-policy, medical, or financial claims that need review.

Mitigation: Review source permissions and factual claims, and check current official platform policies before publishing.
