## Description:

Explains technical errors in plain language, lists likely causes, and suggests troubleshooting steps in order of effort.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[elephantjohn](https://clawhub.ai/user/elephantjohn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can paste a technical error to get a plain-language explanation, likely causes, and a practical sequence of checks. This does not replace debugging with the relevant code and environment details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad debugging requests or incomplete error snippets can lead to speculative diagnoses.

Mitigation: Provide the actual error, traceback, and relevant environment details; distinguish likely causes from confirmed ones.
