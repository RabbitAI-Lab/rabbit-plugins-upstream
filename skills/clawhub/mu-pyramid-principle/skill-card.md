## Description:

A Chinese-first Pyramid Principle coach that diagnoses logic problems in user-provided text and restructures ideas with MECE checks, SCQA openings, pyramid outlines, and polished rewrites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and writers use this skill to diagnose weak logic in reports, proposals, emails, speeches, and loose ideas, then rebuild the content into conclusion-first pyramid structures. It is also used for guided logical-thinking practice around facts versus judgments, deduction versus induction, MECE checks, and pyramid reassembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger the skill during ordinary writing-help requests.

Mitigation: Use the skill when the user asks for Pyramid Principle coaching, logic diagnosis, structured thinking, MECE, SCQA, conclusion-first writing, or similar structure-focused help.

Risk: Generated diagnoses and rewrites may change emphasis or introduce misleading structure when the source material is ambiguous.

Mitigation: Confirm the audience, goal, constraints, and available evidence, then review whether the proposed conclusion, MECE grouping, and rewrite preserve the user's core information.

Risk: README badges and the included landing page can load third-party resources if opened.

Mitigation: Review external links before opening them and deploy the Markdown skill without relying on remote assets where external loading is not acceptable.
