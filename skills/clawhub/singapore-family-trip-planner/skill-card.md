## Use Case: <br>
External users and families use this skill to draft a one-week Singapore itinerary for a family with three children, including daily activities, meal suggestions, family travel tips, and rough budget estimates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Itinerary details, attraction availability, prices, transport options, and travel requirements may change after publication. <br>
Mitigation: Verify hours, ticket prices, transit, travel documents, insurance needs, and booking availability with current official sources before purchase or travel. <br>
Risk: The plan is generic family travel guidance and may not match a specific child's age, accessibility needs, health constraints, or stamina. <br>
Mitigation: Review the itinerary against the travelers' ages, mobility, medical needs, dietary needs, and rest requirements before using it. <br>
