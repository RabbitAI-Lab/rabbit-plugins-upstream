## Description:

Builds an editable, responsive local website from a product or service description, with researched copy and revisions retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other users create editable local landing pages, product demos, or small showcase sites from product or service descriptions. The skill requires JishuDB for research and revision records; deployment and production backends are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires installing and launching JishuDB Desktop before building a website, even for a simple local site.

Mitigation: Review the installation approval envelope, including release identity, publisher or signing status, destination paths, and write scope; decline if JishuDB persistence is not wanted.

Risk: Product source material and site revisions may be retained in JishuDB.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected knowledge base and obtain task-specific permission for source transfer and retention before storing material.
