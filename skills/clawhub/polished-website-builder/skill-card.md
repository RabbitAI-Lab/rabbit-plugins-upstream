## Description:

Builds editable, responsive local websites from product or service descriptions, with researched copy and JishuDB-backed sources and revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn product or service descriptions into editable local landing pages, demos, and small showcase sites. It prepares researched page copy and retains sources and revision records in JishuDB; deployment and production backends are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Required JishuDB use retains research, drafts, final copy, citations, and revision records.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended knowledge base and retention scope, and avoid putting private product plans into public searches.

Risk: Optional JishuDB Desktop setup installs software and changes the selected client configuration.

Mitigation: Review the approval envelope, including version, download source, install path, endpoint, configuration target, and any Windows unsigned-publisher warning; never share secrets in chat.
