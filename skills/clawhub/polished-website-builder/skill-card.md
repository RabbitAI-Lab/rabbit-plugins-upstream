## Description:

Build an editable responsive local website with researched copy and actual assets, retain sources and revisions in JishuDB, and exclude deployment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, makers, and product teams use this skill to create a responsive local landing page or showcase site from a product or service description, with editable HTML/CSS, supporting copy, sources, assets, and revision records retained in JishuDB.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB may retain approved product research, copy, sources, and revision records in the selected knowledge base.

Mitigation: Confirm the target KB, write authorization, source retention rights, and revision-record scope before using the skill.

Risk: Generated sites are local static artifacts and do not provide deployment, payment processing, or production form handling.

Mitigation: Keep any static form clearly demo-only and use a separate approved workflow for deployment, payments, or production data handling.

Risk: Public research or provided material can introduce unsupported claims, unsafe links, or copied third-party content.

Mitigation: Prefer official sources, maintain source attribution and claim mappings, render acquired copy as text, and reject unsafe link schemes.
