## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms or listing details could expose confidential client information.

Mitigation: Use generic search terms and omit confidential client details.

Risk: Paid checks can incur charges, and watches continue after the agent exits.

Mitigation: Enable paid checks and watches only within the intended scope and budget; remove watches when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Live service documentation and billing behavior were not independently verified.

Mitigation: Consult the linked service documentation and confirm current billing behavior before relying on paid features.
