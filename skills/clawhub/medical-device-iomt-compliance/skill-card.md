## Description:

Compliance guide for connected medical devices that combine sensors, a companion app and a cloud service: it lays out the SaMD and SiMD registration paths, cybersecurity expectations, UDI traceability and remote patient monitoring scope across NMPA, FDA and EU MDR. Produces a submission-document checklist and the key points reviewers will look for.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs teams and medical device developers use this skill to map connected-device features across SaMD and SiMD pathways, cybersecurity, UDI traceability and remote monitoring, and to prepare submission checklists for NMPA, FDA and EU MDR review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements can change, so generated checklists may become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify checklists against current official requirements with a qualified regulatory professional.

Risk: The bundled audit's outdated SKILL.md checksum means its integrity attestation cannot be relied on for this release.

Mitigation: Do not rely on that attestation as proof of file integrity; verify the current release through a trusted source.

Risk: Product details shared in prompts could contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use deidentified product information.
