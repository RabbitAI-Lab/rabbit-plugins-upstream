## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs, quality, product, and engineering teams use this skill to draft checklists and registration planning notes for connected medical devices. It focuses on China NMPA, US FDA, and EU MDR considerations across software classification, cybersecurity evidence, UDI traceability, and remote patient monitoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China, United States, and European Union

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory guidance can become outdated or vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify current NMPA, FDA, MDR, UDI, and cybersecurity requirements with official sources or qualified RA/legal professionals before relying on generated materials.

Risk: Generated checklists or document drafts may be mistaken for professional legal or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as drafting support and route final registration decisions and submission materials through qualified review.

Risk: Medical-device project information may include sensitive patient, customer, or nonpublic commercial details.

Mitigation: De-identify inputs and outputs; avoid including patient data, customer names, pricing, or unreleased project details.
