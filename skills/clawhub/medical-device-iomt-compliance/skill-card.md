## Description:

带传感器/APP/云的智能医疗器械（IoMT）合规助手，覆盖SaMD+SiMD注册路径、网络安全IEC 81001-5-1、UDI追溯和RPM远程监测，帮助理清联网器械的NMPA/FDA/MDR合规拼图并输出可提交文档清单与要点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External regulatory affairs, product, quality, and engineering teams use this skill to map connected medical-device products across SaMD/SiMD classification, NMPA/FDA/MDR registration paths, cybersecurity evidence, UDI traceability, and RPM monitoring documentation.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory guidance can change after release and may be incomplete for a specific target market or product claim.

Mitigation: Verify current regulator guidance before relying on generated registration, cybersecurity, UDI, or RPM submission materials.

Risk: Generated compliance checklists may be mistaken for professional regulatory or legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as drafting support and have major registration decisions reviewed by qualified regulatory affairs staff or counsel.
