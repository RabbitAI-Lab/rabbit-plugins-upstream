## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share unnecessary personal financial details while asking for credit guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum context needed and avoid collecting, storing, or repeating sensitive financial details. <br>
Risk: Zhima Credit, Alipay, visa, or central-bank credit-report procedures can change after the skill release. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current procedures through official channels before taking action. <br>
Risk: Users may seek score boosting, score buying, or credit repair shortcuts that are scams or policy violations. <br>
Mitigation: Decline those requests and provide legitimate guidance focused on timely fulfillment, accurate information, and realistic expectations. <br>
