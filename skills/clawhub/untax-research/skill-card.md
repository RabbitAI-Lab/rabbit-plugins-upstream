## Description:

Use Untax for source-backed property assessment research, relevant taxpayer questions, public VA office or form lookup, and explicitly requested private review PDFs, requirements or test checkout workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Property owners and taxpayers use the skill to research property assessments and potential tax relief, while people seeking veteran benefits can look up public VA resources or request a private review draft. The agent follows the user's direction for private workflows and distinguishes research and test outputs from official filings or confirmed eligibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private property or VA information may be shared during account workflows.

Mitigation: Review account permissions and privacy terms before sharing personal information; use only the permissions needed for the requested workflow, and keep VA sharing optional.

Risk: A review PDF or sandbox checkout could be mistaken for an official filing, confirmed eligibility, or a live payment.

Mitigation: Clearly identify review PDFs as unsigned and unfiled, and checkout as test-only; do not present either as proof of eligibility, county filing, or a live charge.
