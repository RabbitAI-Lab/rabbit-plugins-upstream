## Description:

Research property assessments with Untax, look up public VA offices and forms, and help users answer questions about a selected property. Use private review PDFs, VA sharing and test checkout only when requested. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can research property assessments and public veteran-benefit information, then review returned evidence and missing details. Private VA sharing, review PDFs, and sandbox checkout are used only when the user requests them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property or VA information may be shared without the user understanding the service permissions or privacy terms.

Mitigation: Review the service's permissions and privacy terms before sharing information, and use private VA sharing only with the user's explicit choice.

Risk: A sandbox checkout or review PDF could be mistaken for a real payment or official filing.

Mitigation: State clearly that sandbox activity is a test and that review PDFs are unsigned and not filed.
