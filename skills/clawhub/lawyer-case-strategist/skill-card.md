## Description:

This skill helps civil and commercial lawyers analyze cases, build litigation strategy, review pleadings, prepare for trial, and predict judicial outcomes through a six-phase lifecycle workbench based on the Nine-Step Method of Elements Trial.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiangongshilv](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiangongshilv)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External legal professionals use this skill to screen civil and commercial matters, structure claims and defenses, rehearse trial positions, audit litigation documents, and prepare judgment forecasts and fallback strategies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process confidential case material when users run the DOCX export helper or share generated DOCX/ZIP outputs.

Mitigation: Run the converter only on intended Markdown files or case directories, and review generated DOCX/ZIP outputs before sharing them.
