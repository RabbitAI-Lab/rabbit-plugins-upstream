## Description:

When explicitly invoked, design, specify, implement, review, refactor, or explain software using Daniel Jackson's concepts and synchronizations, check CONCEPT.md/SYNCS.md structure, and generate Mermaid relationship diagrams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
