## Description: <br>
Manju Script Generator helps creators turn story text into manju scripts, character and scene prompts, prop descriptions, storyboards, and image or video prompt packages for AI short-drama production. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and production teams use this skill to generate scripts, character sheets, scene prompts, prop prompts, three-view character prompts, and storyboard packages for AI-assisted manju and short-drama workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill includes face-restriction bypass and watermark-removal workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review those sections before installation and remove or ignore them unless the work uses consented likenesses and licensed or self-owned assets. <br>
Risk: Input story text may be sent to the configured local LLM gateway when generate.py runs. <br>
Mitigation: Use only approved local gateways and avoid sending confidential or unapproved story material. <br>
