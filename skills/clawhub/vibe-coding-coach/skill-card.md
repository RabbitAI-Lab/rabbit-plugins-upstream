## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Secrets or customer data may be exposed when pasted into AI coding prompts.

Mitigation: Use variable names and synthetic or redacted examples instead of real credentials or customer data.

Risk: AI-generated code may contain defects in authentication, payments, privacy, or deployment changes.

Mitigation: Independently review and verify generated code, with professional review for security-sensitive features, before production use.
