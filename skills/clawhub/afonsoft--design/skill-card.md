## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to plan, critique, and improve frontend interfaces with mobile-first responsive behavior, accessibility checks, design tokens, and framework-aware guidance for Angular, React, or Blazor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated design recommendations may conflict with an existing product brand, workflow, or codebase conventions.

Mitigation: Review proposed UI changes against the product brief, existing design system, and framework conventions before implementation.

Risk: Design guidance can be incomplete if rendered behavior, accessibility, responsive states, or edge-case content are not verified.

Mitigation: Validate changes with rendered evidence, keyboard navigation, contrast checks, reduced-motion handling, 200% zoom, and realistic long, empty, error, and permission-limited states.
