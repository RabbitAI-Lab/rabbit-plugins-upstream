## Description:

Helps agents write, rewrite, and improve persuasive marketing copy for website pages such as homepages, landing pages, pricing pages, feature pages, about pages, and product pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, founders, and product teams use this skill to draft conversion-oriented website page copy, including section-by-section copy, headlines, CTAs, alternatives, annotations, and SEO meta content when relevant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad copywriting phrases when a more specialized skill would fit better.

Mitigation: Review the task type before relying on the output, and route email, popup, editing, offer, or conversion-rate strategy work to the specialized skill named in the artifact when applicable.

Risk: The skill may read project-level product marketing context files when they exist.

Mitigation: Keep unrelated sensitive information out of product marketing context files and review those files before using the skill in shared workspaces.
