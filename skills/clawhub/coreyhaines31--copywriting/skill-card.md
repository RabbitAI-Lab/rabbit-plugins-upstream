## Description: <br>
Helps agents write, rewrite, and improve persuasive website marketing copy for pages such as homepages, landing pages, pricing pages, feature pages, about pages, and product pages. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and website builders use this skill to produce conversion-focused website copy, CTAs, page structure, annotations, alternatives, and optional SEO meta content. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read local product marketing context files that contain sensitive business details. <br>
Mitigation: Review local product marketing context files before using the skill in shared or external-output workflows. <br>
Risk: Generated copy can introduce unsupported claims, fabricated proof points, or misleading marketing language. <br>
Mitigation: Review claims, proof points, testimonials, and compliance-sensitive copy before publication. <br>
