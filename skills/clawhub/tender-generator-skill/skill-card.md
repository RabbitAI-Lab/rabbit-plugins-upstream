## Description: <br>
智能项目文档生成器，通过对话收集信息，自动填充模板生成全套项目文档。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mama1234421](https://clawhub.ai/user/mama1234421) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and business teams use this skill to collect tender or project information, select Word templates, generate a package of bid documents, and review required follow-up items before sharing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill handles sensitive company, banking, contact, quote, template, and generated bid-document data. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in a trusted workspace, prefer placeholder data for testing, keep outputs in a dedicated directory, and delete or secure generated Word and ZIP files after use. <br>
Risk: Generated tender documents may be incomplete or unsuitable for formal submission without domain review. <br>
Mitigation: Review every generated file, replace required stamped or signed scans manually, and confirm all project data, pricing, and technical parameters before sharing. <br>
