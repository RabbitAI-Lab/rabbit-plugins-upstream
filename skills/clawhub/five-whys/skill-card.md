## Description:

Guides evidence-backed Five Whys investigations to identify actionable root causes and countermeasures for recurring failures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and incident responders use this skill to investigate recurring outages, defects, and process failures, distinguish verified causes from hypotheses, and propose countermeasures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investigations may require access to sensitive logs, commits, tickets, timelines, or configurations.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where access to that project evidence is appropriate.

Risk: An unverified causal link could be mistaken for an established root cause.

Mitigation: Keep hypotheses labeled and state how to verify them before treating them as findings.
