## Description:

Analyzes China's Super Lotto draws, summarizes expert picks, generates entertainment-only number combinations, checks results, and explains lottery randomness and spending trade-offs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hmily741963](https://clawhub.ai/user/hmily741963)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Lottery players and their assistants use this skill to review public draw data and expert views, create entertainment-only picks, check draw results, and understand costs and the limits of prediction. It does not promise winnings or positive returns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local automation may inspect or synchronize WorkBuddy skill directories and Windows scheduled tasks.

Mitigation: Review these local actions before installation and prefer manual or offline runs unless scheduled maintenance is explicitly wanted.

Risk: Reports write files to the user's Desktop and online runs fetch public lottery data.

Mitigation: Confirm the output location and data access are acceptable; use the offline option when network fetching is unwanted.

Risk: Lottery picks and expert views can be mistaken for reliable predictions or a basis for spending.

Mitigation: Treat picks as entertainment, verify draw results independently, and do not rely on them for financial returns.
