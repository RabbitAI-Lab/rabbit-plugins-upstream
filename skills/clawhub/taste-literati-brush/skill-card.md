## Description:

Aesthetic guidance for AI agents applying Chinese literati painting traditions through calligraphic line, ink gradation, negative space, inscriptions, and scholarly restraint.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnnyzijianwu](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnnyzijianwu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Artists, designers, and AI agents use this skill to generate or evaluate visual-art outputs against Chinese literati brush-painting aesthetics. It is most useful when an agent needs concise style tokens, anti-tokens, application rules, and evaluation criteria for Song-to-Qing literati painting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally biases art-related outputs toward Chinese literati brush aesthetics.

Mitigation: Install or invoke it only when that stylistic direction is desired for the task.

Risk: Creative guidance may produce outputs that do not fully capture the cultural or historical nuance of the referenced tradition.

Mitigation: Review generated work for aesthetic coherence, scholarly restraint, and fit with the stated evaluation criteria before publication.
