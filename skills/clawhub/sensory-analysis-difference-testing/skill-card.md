## Description: <br>
Provides Chinese-language guidance for conducting sensory difference tests under GB/T 10220-2012/ISO 6658:2005, including paired comparison, triangle, duo-trio, two-out-of-five, and A-not-A methods. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cloudyxuq](https://clawhub.ai/user/cloudyxuq) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Food product developers, sensory analysts, and quality teams use this skill to select and run sensory difference tests, prepare panels and samples, analyze results, and report whether product samples differ perceptibly. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incorrect or outdated sensory-analysis guidance could lead to invalid test design, statistical interpretation, or reporting. <br>
Mitigation: Verify procedures, significance thresholds, and conclusions against the applicable official standards and qualified sensory-analysis practice before relying on results. <br>
