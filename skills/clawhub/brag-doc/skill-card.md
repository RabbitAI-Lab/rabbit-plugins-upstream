## Description: <br>
Keep a running brag document of accomplishments by turning wins, impact, scope, dates, and evidence into structured, impact-first entries for reviews or promotion packets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and managers use this skill to maintain a dated accomplishment log that captures outcomes, metrics, scope, evidence links, and competency mapping for self-reviews or promotion cases. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brag documents can contain sensitive workplace metrics, internal links, or performance-review material. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information appropriate for the agent workspace and intended audience, and omit confidential details or internal links unless sharing is permitted. <br>
Risk: Accomplishment entries may overstate individual contribution or omit evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Review entries for accurate scope, clearly distinguish individual and team contributions, and include verifiable evidence links where available. <br>
