## Description: <br>
Audits an existing social media account using user-provided native analytics and the AUDIT framework to produce a keep, kill, refresh triage, pillar and voice findings, profile fixes, and a quick-wins versus long-term roadmap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External social media operators, marketers, creators, and account owners use this skill to evaluate recent account performance, identify what content to keep, refresh, or stop producing, and turn findings into an action-oriented roadmap. It is for retrospective diagnosis and recommendations, not automated publishing, deletion, or metric collection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may analyze social account performance data through related analytics workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm user intent before sharing analytics and use only user-provided native analytics. <br>
Risk: Audit recommendations could lead to content changes or deletion decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations manually, prefer refresh before kill, and require human approval before deleting content. <br>
