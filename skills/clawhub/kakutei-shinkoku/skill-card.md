## Description:

フリーランス・個人事業主のための確定申告サポート。所得分類、経費整理、控除確認、消費税・インボイス制度の知識アシスタント。令和7年度税制改正（基礎控除95万円段階・160万円の壁）対応。API接続なし、マイナンバー取扱なし。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, especially freelancers, sole proprietors, and side-business earners in Japan, use this skill to organize income, expenses, deductions, consumption tax, invoice-system questions, and filing preparation before confirming conclusions with official sources or a tax professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Japan

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter sensitive financial details into the optional tax profile, expense log, or annual summary memory template.

Mitigation: Treat stored tax profile, income, expense, and deduction details as sensitive financial data and avoid entering My Number or unnecessary personal identifiers.

Risk: Tax guidance or calculations may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a user's filing situation.

Mitigation: Verify tax conclusions against official sources or a qualified tax professional before filing.
