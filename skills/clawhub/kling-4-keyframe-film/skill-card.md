## Description:

Guides an agent through planning and making a cinematic short film with Kling 4 by building a beat sheet, creating reference stills, generating video takes, and editing the usable footage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to plan cinematic short films, music videos, trailers, and narrative scenes with Kling 4, using reference stills to guide shots and editing guidance to assemble usable takes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
