## Description:

Guides creators through planning a cinematic short film with reference stills, Kling 4 video takes, and a curated edit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Filmmakers and creators use this skill to plan and produce short films, music videos, trailers, and narrative scenes with Kling 4. It provides shot planning, reference-image and video prompts, generation guidance, and editing examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference photos or URLs may contain sensitive material or reusable credentials and may be shared with VARG and its model providers.

Mitigation: Use only references you have permission to share, and avoid sensitive photos or URLs containing reusable credentials.

Risk: Video generation retries can increase total costs.

Mitigation: Estimate each take and agree on a total budget that includes retries before generating.
