## Description:

Guides an agent through planning a cinematic short film with reference stills and Kling 4 video takes, then editing the usable footage into a finished video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External creators use this skill to plan and produce short films, music videos, sketches, trailers, and narrative scenes with Kling 4. It provides a key-still workflow, prompt guidance, generation calls, and editing steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cast or other personal images may be sent to external generation providers.

Mitigation: Confirm you have permission to share depicted people’s images and review the providers’ data handling terms.

Risk: An unpinned installer can change between uses.

Mitigation: Review the installer and pin a version you trust before running it.

Risk: Multiple long video takes and retries can add up in cost.

Mitigation: Estimate each take before generation and draft at lower resolution before rerunning selected takes at higher resolution.
