## Description:

Meeting Note structures discussion and decision meeting notes into conclusions, consensus, disagreements, decision history, assumptions, risks, opportunities, action items, and Zettelkasten links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and agents use this skill to turn Chinese discussion or decision meeting content into structured notes with decisions, open disagreements, action items, and Zettelkasten-style knowledge links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The server security summary says the skill declares broad command, file, and API-related authority beyond the core meeting-note workflow.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and grant only the minimum read/write, shell, network, and API permissions needed for the intended note-generation task.

Risk: Meeting inputs may contain personal, confidential, or business-sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use approved workspaces, restrict access to generated notes, and redact sensitive details before sharing or storing outputs outside the intended environment.
