## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Detailed invasive or forceful treatment procedures may be used for unsafe self-treatment.

Mitigation: Frame procedural material for qualified clinicians; provide high-level summaries rather than step-by-step invasive instructions to non-professional users.

Risk: Reference information could be mistaken for a diagnosis or replacement for medical care.

Mitigation: Avoid diagnosing or prescribing treatment; direct users to clinical assessment and provide emergency or referral guidance when appropriate.
