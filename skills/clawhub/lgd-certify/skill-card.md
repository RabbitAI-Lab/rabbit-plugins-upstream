## Description:

Helps teams create a local AI-project passport, hash supporting evidence, and generate an LGD gate result and badge files.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (Python code only); documentation and theory all rights reserved

## Use Case:

Developers and project teams use this skill to organize AI-project identity and evidence, run a local evidence gate, and prepare reviewable certification and badge files. Its results are not legal, regulatory, or third-party approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Locally generated certification or badges may be mistaken for legal, regulatory, or third-party endorsement.

Mitigation: Review certification.json and BADGES.md before publication and identify them as local LGD workflow results, not external approval.

Risk: A gate result depends on the completeness and accuracy of user-provided evidence.

Mitigation: Verify the underlying evidence and resolve missing or inaccurate materials before relying on the result.
