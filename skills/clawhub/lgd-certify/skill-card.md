## Description:

Creates local AI artifact passports, hashed evidence chains, self-assessed gate results, and badge snippets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and governance teams use this skill to record an AI artifact's identity, hash six classes of supplied evidence, and produce a local self-certification result and badge embed code.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A local PASS or badge may be mistaken for independent validation of evidence or legal compliance.

Mitigation: Review the underlying evidence and seek appropriate professional or regulatory review before relying on the result.
