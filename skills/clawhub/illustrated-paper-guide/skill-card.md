## Description:

Create or improve a source-grounded study guide for an academic paper or PDF, including top-down reading plans, figure walkthroughs, coverage audits, limitations, explanatory diagrams, and durable follow-up notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[illustrated-paper-guide](https://clawhub.ai/user/illustrated-paper-guide)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, educators, and developers use this skill to turn academic papers or PDFs into reviewed study-guide files with source-grounded explanations, progressive reading checkpoints, figure guidance, and follow-up answers written back into durable notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on common paper-reading phrases and may update guide or notes files during follow-up answers.

Mitigation: Use a narrower instruction or opt out when you want a chat-only answer, and review file changes before keeping them.

Risk: Public outputs can create copyright or permission issues if they embed paper figures without clear reuse rights.

Mitigation: Confirm figure reuse permissions before embedding, otherwise link to the source or use original teaching diagrams with attribution.

Risk: A host without companion-skill support may not run the specialized diagram workflow automatically.

Mitigation: Follow the bundled companion skill instructions directly before creating diagrams when companion invocation is unavailable.
