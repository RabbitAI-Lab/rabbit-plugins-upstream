## Description:

Real Ai Fortune Clawhub is a traditional Chinese metaphysics entertainment skill that supports Bazi charting, Mei Hua Yi Shu divination, and Ba Zhai Feng Shui self-checks using bundled rule references and local Python calculators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for cultural-entertainment readings around Bazi, Mei Hua Yi Shu, and Feng Shui. The skill produces rule-based initial interpretations and guidance, not scientific predictions or licensed medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate broadly for fortune-telling topics and collect sensitive birth, location, or home-environment details.

Mitigation: Use it only when the user intentionally wants Chinese metaphysics entertainment content, minimize personal details, and avoid sharing exact birth data, home photos, or other sensitive information unless necessary.

Risk: Outputs or referred human consultation could be mistaken for professional advice in health, legal, financial, or mental-health contexts.

Mitigation: Keep outputs non-deterministic and advisory, and route high-impact decisions to licensed professionals rather than treating the skill or any external consultant as authoritative.

Risk: The release has unclear local-record handling and optional off-platform consultation framing.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, avoid unnecessary record retention, and disclose any off-platform consultation path before users provide sensitive information.
