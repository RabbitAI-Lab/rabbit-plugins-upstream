## Description:

Displays a one-shot scoreboard of publisher-reported agentic benchmark averages for code, tokens, cost, time, and checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can request a quick display of Ponytail's published benchmark averages, without claiming savings for their own repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Benchmark figures are publisher claims and may be mistaken for measured savings in the current repository.

Mitigation: Verify the referenced README and benchmark results independently before relying on the figures; do not present them as per-repository savings.
