## Description:

Displays a one-shot scoreboard of published agentic benchmark averages for code size, tokens, cost, time, and safety.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to display published benchmark results without estimating savings for their own repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published benchmark averages may be mistaken for savings measured in the user's repository.

Mitigation: Label the figures as benchmark averages and do not claim repository-specific savings.
