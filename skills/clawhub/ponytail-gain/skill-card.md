## Description:

Displays a one-shot scoreboard of published agentic benchmark averages for code size, token use, cost, and time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers can view published benchmark averages in a compact scoreboard without changing files or claiming savings for their current repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published benchmark savings could be mistaken for measured savings in the current repository.

Mitigation: Present the figures as benchmark averages only; do not claim repository-specific savings.
