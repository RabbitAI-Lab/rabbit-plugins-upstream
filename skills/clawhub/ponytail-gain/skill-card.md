## Description:

Displays Ponytail's published agentic benchmark results as a one-shot scoreboard for code, token, cost, and time comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users invoke the skill to view published Ponytail benchmark averages at a glance without modifying their repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published benchmark averages may be mistaken for savings measured in the user's repository.

Mitigation: Label the figures as benchmark results and do not claim repository-specific savings.
