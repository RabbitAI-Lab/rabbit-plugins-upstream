## Description:

Displays published Ponytail 5 benchmark results as a one-shot scoreboard without changing the current repository.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users can view the published Ponytail 5 benchmark comparison for code size, tokens, cost, time, and quality checks without altering their repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published benchmark figures could be mistaken for measured savings in the current repository.

Mitigation: Present the figures as published benchmark claims only; do not claim repository-specific savings without a measured baseline.
