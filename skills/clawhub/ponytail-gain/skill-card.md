## Description:

Show ponytail measured impact as a scoreboard: less code, less cost, more speed, from the agentic benchmark averages. One-shot display.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers can view the publisher-reported Ponytail benchmark averages in a one-shot scoreboard. The figures describe benchmark runs, not measurements of the user's repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers could mistake publisher-reported benchmark averages for measurements of their own repository.

Mitigation: Present the figures as benchmark averages and do not claim per-repository savings.
