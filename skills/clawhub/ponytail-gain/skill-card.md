## Description:

Displays a one-shot scoreboard of published Ponytail agentic benchmark averages for code, tokens, cost, speed, and checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to view published Ponytail benchmark comparisons without changing their project or claiming repo-specific savings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Benchmark numbers are publisher claims; the cited benchmark files are not included in this artifact.

Mitigation: Check the underlying benchmark material before relying on the figures, and do not present them as verified results.

Risk: Published averages could be mistaken for measured savings in the current repository.

Mitigation: Label the figures as benchmark averages and do not claim repo-specific savings without a measured baseline.
