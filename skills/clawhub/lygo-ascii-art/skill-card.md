## Description: <br>
LYGO ASCII Art Studio directs agents and users to a browser-local web tool for converting photos into ASCII, text, or type art. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deepseekoracle](https://clawhub.ai/user/deepseekoracle) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to route photo-to-ASCII requests to the live LYGO ASCII Art Studio and provide concise operating guidance for converting, tuning, copying, and exporting text art. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill points to an external web page that can change over time and may use third-party services. <br>
Mitigation: Review the live site before using sensitive photos, and avoid sensitive images unless the user trusts the current page behavior. <br>
Risk: Converted images or exported ASCII art may reveal sensitive visual content if shared. <br>
Mitigation: Do not publish or share generated art unless the user explicitly approves the destination and content. <br>
