## Description:

A Chinese-language home renovation reference skill for layouts, dimensions, utility rough-ins, construction workflow, inspection checkpoints, budgeting, contracts, material selection, environmental controls, and common pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer Chinese-language home renovation questions with practical dimensions, construction and inspection checklists, budget and contract guardrails, material guidance, and environmental safety references. It is reference guidance and should be verified for site-specific design, legal, price, code, and testing decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat renovation dimensions, construction steps, or acceptance thresholds as final site-specific professional decisions.

Mitigation: Present the skill as reference guidance and verify structural, electrical, waterproofing, code, and inspection decisions with qualified local professionals before work proceeds.

Risk: Budget and contract guidance can become inaccurate for a user's city, contractor, materials, or current market conditions.

Mitigation: Use the skill's numbers as planning ranges and confirm pricing, payment terms, change-order limits, warranties, and legal obligations against current local quotes and contracts.

Risk: Environmental and formaldehyde guidance may be mistaken for a certified indoor air quality result.

Mitigation: Require qualified testing services and current local standards before making move-in, remediation, or health-related decisions.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language renovation contexts and may not transfer cleanly to other regions or building codes.

Mitigation: Localize measurements, materials, safety rules, and legal references for the user's jurisdiction before relying on them.
