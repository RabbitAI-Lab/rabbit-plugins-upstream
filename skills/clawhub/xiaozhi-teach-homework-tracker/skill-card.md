## Description:

帮助独立教师登记作业、追踪提交与订正状态、归纳错因，并整理下节课的教学重点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking independent K–12 teachers in mainland China use this skill to track homework completion, organize error patterns and prepare teacher-reviewed follow-up for the next lesson.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student progress records may be stored or shared without appropriate consent or teacher review.

Mitigation: Confirm profile and sharing consent, use student aliases, and require teacher confirmation before storing or sharing records.

Risk: Crisis guidance, curriculum assumptions and minor-data practices may not fit use outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize crisis resources, curriculum assumptions and minor-data compliance rules before using the skill elsewhere.
