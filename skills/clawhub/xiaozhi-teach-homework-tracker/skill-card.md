## Description:

Helps independent teachers track homework submissions, identify recurring learning difficulties, and plan the next lesson from documented errors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent K–12 teachers use this Chinese-language skill to register homework, follow submission and correction status, summarize error patterns, and prepare next-lesson priorities. Teachers review proposed follow-ups and confirm changes to persistent student profiles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China defaults; other regions require localized crisis resources, curriculum terms, and consent practices.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent records or cross-skill sharing may expose student learning information.

Mitigation: Use aliases and limited learning data; check documented consent and teacher confirmation before storing or sharing records.

Risk: Mainland China crisis resources and curriculum defaults may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize emergency referrals, curriculum terms, and consent practices before deployment in another region.
