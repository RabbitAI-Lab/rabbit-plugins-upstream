## Description:

帮助独立教师登记并跟进课上练习和学员带来的学校作业、考试错题，归纳错因并准备下节课的教学重点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

独立教师用它记录课上练习的完成情况、分析学员自带的学校作业和考试错题，并据此准备下节课的讲解重点；弱项认定和对外使用的内容由教师确认。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize language, curriculum, emergency guidance, and legal references before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent student homework and learning-weakness records may be shared or retained inappropriately.

Mitigation: Review workspace permissions and require teacher confirmation for recording and sharing; honor pause, export, correction, and deletion requests.

Risk: Chinese-language emergency, curriculum, and legal guidance may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Use in a Mainland China K12 teaching context or localize the language, emergency resources, curriculum assumptions, and legal references first.
