## Description:

帮助独立教师记录课上练习和学员带来的学校作业、跟踪完成状态与错因，并整理下一节课的教学重点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

独立教师可用此技能登记课上练习及学员带来的学校作业或考试错题，追踪完成状态、整理错因并准备下节课的教学重点。技能以化名记录低敏学习信息，并要求教师确认后才更新持久弱项记录。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records could expose learner information if identifiable details are entered or sharing settings are too broad.

Mitigation: Use student aliases and confirm the platform's storage and sharing settings before entering records.

Risk: Tracking recommendations or proposed updates may be inaccurate if used without teacher review.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes against the recorded evidence and keep persistent weakness updates under teacher confirmation.
