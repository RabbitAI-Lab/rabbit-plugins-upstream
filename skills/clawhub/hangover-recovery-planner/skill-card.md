## Description:

Use when someone drank too much and needs an evidence-based recovery plan, or mid-session wants to know if the next drink is worth it — Widmark BAC tracking, fluid/electrolyte deficit math from actual drinks consumed, congener load scoring, staged rehydration/food/light/sleep protocol, symptom-to-mechanism router, medication warnings (acetaminophen, NSAIDs), and red flags that mean ER not electrolytes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can provide session and symptom details to receive a personalized hangover recovery plan, track estimated BAC and hydration needs, and review medication cautions and emergency warning signs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated BAC and pacing guidance can be mistaken for proof that it is safe to drink more or drive.

Mitigation: Do not use estimates or a ‘pace OK’ result to decide to drink or drive; arrange a sober ride and treat any impairment seriously.

Risk: Medication guidance may not account for a person's health conditions or other medicines.

Mitigation: Check medication advice with a clinician or pharmacist.

Risk: Serious symptoms may require urgent care rather than self-treatment.

Mitigation: Seek emergency help for serious symptoms or listed red flags.
