## Description:

Auto-cut beat-synced flash videos from your music and clips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yysws5566](https://clawhub.ai/user/yysws5566)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and video editors can use this skill to turn authorized music and video clips into short videos cut to the beat, with optional flash text and visual effects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment flags privacy and installation concerns, including uncertainty about cloud-service needs and local-project access.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive footage or text to external providers; verify dependency versions and antivirus alerts before installing.

Risk: The security assessment says the package includes advertising and narration features beyond the stated beat-synchronized video purpose.

Mitigation: Clarify the package scope and data handling before installation, and avoid using cloud features that are not needed.

Risk: Using music or footage without authorization can create rights issues.

Mitigation: Use only music and clips you created or are authorized to use, then review the video before publishing.
