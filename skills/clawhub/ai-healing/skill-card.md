心屿（Xinyu）AI 深度心理疗愈技能：当用户表达情绪困扰（焦虑、空虚、低落）、想聊梦与象征、感到意义缺失、面临职业/人生转折、或需要深度情感陪伴时触发。以荣格深度心理学与存在关怀为底色的共情对话方法，先承接情绪再探索，内建危机安全刹车，不做临床诊断。

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for reflective emotional support around distress, dreams, meaning, relationships, and life or career transitions. It provides empathetic, non-diagnostic conversation guidance with explicit crisis boundaries and does not replace professional mental health care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on reflective support during crisis, self-harm thoughts, violent intent, psychosis-like symptoms, or impaired daily functioning.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as non-clinical support only and route crisis or impaired-functioning situations to professional, emergency, or trusted-person support.

Risk: The skill may activate on broad everyday emotional or life-decision language.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants reflective support and stop or redirect when the user asks for factual, technical, or non-wellness help.
