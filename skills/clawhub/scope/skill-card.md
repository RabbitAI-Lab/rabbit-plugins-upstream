## Description:

Pauses an agent before actions outside the agreed task scope and asks the user how to proceed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aradmanamnaoon](https://clawhub.ai/user/aradmanamnaoon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users use this skill to catch scope drift during a task and decide whether to proceed, defer the work, or revert.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scope checks may pause legitimate work when the agreed boundaries are unclear.

Mitigation: Review the specific proposed action and clarify scope before choosing to proceed or defer.
