## Description:

Helps medical-device teams score risks with a reference 5×5 matrix, compare residual risks, review risk-control options, and check risk-management file completeness against ISO 14971:2019.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Medical-device quality and regulatory teams use this skill to prepare or review risk assessments and identify possible gaps in risk-management files before human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reference matrix may not match a manufacturer's risk-acceptability criteria.

Mitigation: Apply the manufacturer's risk-management plan when deciding acceptability; treat matrix results as aids only.

Risk: Keyword matches may be mistaken for evidence of compliance, and the file check only supports English and Chinese.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer examine the underlying records and manually review documents in other languages or with unusual headings.

Risk: Reviewing a file requires providing its contents to the skill.

Mitigation: Run the check only on files intentionally selected for review.
