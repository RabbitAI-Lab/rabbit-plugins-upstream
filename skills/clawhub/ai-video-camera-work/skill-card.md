## Description:

Design and prompt AI video using named camera work, with a 100-term bilingual camera index, a 60-second blueprint workflow, and ready-to-use prompts for specific camera moves, shot sizes, angles, lenses, focus, composition, and timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to turn video ideas into clearly specified shot lists and prompts, selecting camera movement, shot size, angle, lens, composition, subject action, and timing. It also provides a blueprint workflow for planning short AI-generated videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Remove confidential content before submitting prompts.

Mitigation: Enter API keys only through a trusted secret-input mechanism.

Risk: Claims about model versions, prices, and quotas may be unverified or outdated.

Mitigation: Confirm current model, pricing, and quota details with the provider before relying on them.
