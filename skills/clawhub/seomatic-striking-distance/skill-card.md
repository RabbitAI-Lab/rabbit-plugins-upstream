## Description:

Identifies page-two search queries from a connected site's Search Console data, estimates potential click gains, and recommends or stages specific SEO improvements for approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to prioritize near-page-one queries, estimate traffic opportunities from their own Search Console data, and stage targeted improvements for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Access to connected Search Console and page data may expose sensitive site performance information.

Mitigation: Connect only sites whose data you are authorized to share with SEOmatic.

Risk: Approved SEO tasks may change live site content.

Mitigation: Review each staged task and its proposed changes before approval; verify the resulting changes afterward.

Risk: Projected click gains may not materialize.

Mitigation: Present estimates as ranges based on the site's data and measure outcomes rather than promising ranking gains.
