## Description:

Provides Chinese-language strategic coaching for creators, entrepreneurs, side-business owners, and small teams through business diagnosis, growth roadmaps, milestones, and reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lichengyu1207](https://clawhub.ai/user/lichengyu1207)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, entrepreneurs, side-business owners, and small-business teams use this skill to assess their current position, plan growth against income targets, and track weekly actions and monthly progress. Income figures are planning targets, not guaranteed outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Full use requires an installation ID to be sent to the author for off-platform registration.

Mitigation: Review the registration requirement before installation and share the ID only if comfortable contacting the author.

Risk: Activation data and business-progress notes persist on the local device.

Mitigation: Avoid recording confidential client, financial, or identifying details; review or remove ~/.yihaiyoudao/activate.json and artifact/memory/learning-log.md when no longer needed.
