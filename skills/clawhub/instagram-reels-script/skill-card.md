## Description:

Writes a short Reels script with the spoken hook, on-screen text, caption and shot list, all inside the platform limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External social media managers and small brand owners use the skill to plan and write short Instagram Reels, including a hook, timed beats, on-screen text, caption, shot list, and disclosure reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The helper overwrites reels-script.md in its output folder.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated output folder.

Risk: The optional Windows download is separate from the bundle, and the publisher release currently describes ModelBox rather than the named toolkit; a matching hash alone does not establish safety.

Mitigation: Assess the optional download separately with the publisher before running it; do not treat a matching hash as proof that it is safe.
