## Description:

Turns one product image and its selling points into a coordinated set of product photos across multiple angles and scenes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and content creators use this skill to create coordinated product visuals for listings and detail pages from a single product photo and its selling points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and descriptions are sent to the selected external generation provider.

Mitigation: Use only images and descriptions approved for that provider, and review its data handling terms before sending them.

Risk: Security analysis is incomplete because only 5 of 9 components were fully scanned.

Mitigation: Review the unscanned components and reassess the skill before installation or use.
