## Description: <br>
Extracts YouTube video captions and helps an agent produce concise summaries with main points, key arguments, and conclusions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vovavvk](https://clawhub.ai/user/vovavvk) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
AGPL-3.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill when a user provides a YouTube URL and needs a fast, structured summary of captioned video content. It is most useful for educational, news, documentary, and reference videos where captions are available. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cookie files may expose authenticated YouTube sessions if stored, shared, synced, or committed. <br>
Mitigation: Use public videos when possible; when cookies are required, restrict file access, keep them out of shared or synced folders, never commit or share them, and delete them after use. <br>
Risk: The transcript extractor weakens HTTPS certificate checks through yt-dlp configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installation and prefer a version that removes nocheckcertificate unless a specific trusted environment requires it. <br>
Risk: Cached transcripts and video metadata may persist local copies of user-requested content. <br>
Mitigation: Store the cache in an appropriate local directory, review retention needs, and clear cached transcripts and metadata when no longer needed. <br>
