## Description:

When the Bitcoin market looks like it does now, what happened next? Finds past moments with similar funding, basis, positioning, volatility and order-book signals in AgentReeve's recorded history and reports BTC's move over the next 4, 24 and 72 hours against normal - with sample size and an honest quality grade. Free sample; live per request over x402.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users and market analysts use the skill to compare current Bitcoin market conditions with historical periods and review what followed over 4, 24 and 72 hours. It provides historical context rather than a forecast or financial advice.
