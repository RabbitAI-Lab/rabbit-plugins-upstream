## Description:

Restate the last message by cleaning it up only: make it more logical and more concise, and remove filler and empty talk, without polishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

## Use Case:

Users who want a concise restatement use this skill to remove filler and repetition from their latest message while keeping its claims and wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: One unresolved local reference leaves the scan evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference before installing; the scan cannot account for what it points to.
