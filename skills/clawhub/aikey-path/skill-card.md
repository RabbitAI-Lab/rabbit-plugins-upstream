## Description:

Turns a broad goal into a measurable, actionable path with one confirmed bottleneck, a first step, and a criterion for changing course.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and small business owners use this skill to turn a vague goal into a measurable plan, identify one bottleneck, compare a few possible paths, and define a first action with a review criterion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal or business observations may be saved for future use without clear user control.

Mitigation: Review the configuration and shared instructions first; require approval for each saved note, exclude confidential details, and establish how to inspect and delete saved memory and reference-library entries.
