## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary-school students through recognizing physical phenomena, selecting applicable models, and expressing them mathematically before attempting calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to practice choosing and transferring physics models across middle- and high-school problems, rather than receiving complete worked solutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localized crisis referrals and review of minors' data-sharing controls.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles and reminder handoffs may expose minors' learning data.

Mitigation: Obtain appropriate consent; review or disable memory, sharing, and reminders for minors.

Risk: Mainland-China crisis contacts may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize crisis referrals and emergency contacts before use outside Mainland China.
