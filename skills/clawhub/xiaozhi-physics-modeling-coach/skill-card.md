## Description:

面向初高中学生的中文物理建模教练，通过识别现象、选择模型和数学表达，帮助学生判断物理规律及其适用条件。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to practice identifying physics phenomena, selecting suitable models, checking their conditions, and transferring those models to new problems. It guides modeling rather than completing entire solutions or experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; localize curriculum, crisis contacts, and consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles or modeling progress may be shared across skills or retained without the student's intended consent.

Mitigation: Confirm consent before profile use or handoff; honor requests to pause memory, delete records, or stop sharing.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, safety contacts, or consent assumptions may mislead users elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and consent rules, confirm location before providing crisis contacts, and use local support channels.
