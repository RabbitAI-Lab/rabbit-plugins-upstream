## Description:

面向初中至高一学生，通过识别现象、选择模型和数学表达三步训练物理建模思维。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students in Mainland China's junior-secondary and first-year senior-secondary physics courses use this coach to identify physical phenomena, select applicable models, and express relationships mathematically rather than applying formulas by rote. Teachers and guardians can use its guided modeling exercises to support practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent requirements, and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, minor-consent rules, or emergency contacts may not suit students outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Use in a Chinese-language Mainland China K12 setting unless curriculum, consent rules, and emergency contacts have been localized.

Risk: A student's modeling profile or reminder requests may persist or be shared beyond the immediate conversation.

Mitigation: Review memory, sharing, and reminder permissions with the student or guardian; write profile updates and enqueue reminders only with the required consent.
