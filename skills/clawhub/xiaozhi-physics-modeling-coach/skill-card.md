## Description:

Guides Chinese middle and high school students through identifying physical phenomena, choosing a suitable model, and expressing it mathematically before applying formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school physics students use guided questions to identify phenomena, check a model's conditions, and translate it into equations. Educators can use the same approach to practice transferring models across different problems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions after localization

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning profiles, parent or cross-skill sharing, and reminders may expose a minor's learning data if consent or controls are misconfigured.

Mitigation: Confirm student or guardian consent as appropriate and test pause, deletion, and export controls before enabling those features.

Risk: Chinese-language instruction and region-specific crisis guidance may not suit students outside the intended learning context.

Mitigation: Use in a Chinese K12 physics-learning context, or localize the instruction and support resources before deployment elsewhere.
