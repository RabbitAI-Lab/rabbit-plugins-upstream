## Description:

Coaches Chinese-speaking middle and high school students to identify physics phenomena, choose an applicable model, and express it mathematically before solving problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill for guided physics-model selection and transfer practice across Chinese middle and high school topics; it is not a full worked-solution or experiment-design tutor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize curriculum, minor-data consent, and crisis referrals before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles and reminders may involve minors' educational records.

Mitigation: Keep consent-gated memory and sharing controls enabled; let users pause, inspect, correct, or delete their profile.

Risk: Curriculum and crisis-referral guidance is tailored to mainland China.

Mitigation: Before using elsewhere, localize the curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent requirements.
