## Description:

Coaches Chinese-speaking junior-high and first-year high-school students to identify physical phenomena, choose an applicable model, and express it mathematically before solving.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language coach to practice selecting physics models, checking their applicability, and transferring the same model across problem settings. It covers junior-high physics and the first compulsory volume of first-year high-school physics, not full worked solutions or experiment design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localized crisis resources, curriculum assumptions, and privacy and guardian-consent rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning profile or reminders could be used or shared without the appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm the platform enforces student and guardian consent for profile memory, cross-skill sharing, parent visibility, and reminders; honor pause, delete, and sharing controls.

Risk: Crisis guidance and curriculum assumptions may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency resources, curriculum scope, and privacy and guardian-consent rules before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Incorrect model selection or generated practice questions could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check model applicability and grade level, and review generated questions for adequate conditions and a valid solution before presenting them.
