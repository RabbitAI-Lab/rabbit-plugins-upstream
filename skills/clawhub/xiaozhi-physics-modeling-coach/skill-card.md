## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learner profiles, sharing, and reminders involve personal learning data.

Mitigation: Enable them only when wanted, use “这次不要记忆” to pause profile access and recording, and review host storage controls and companion skill behavior.

Risk: Some guidance may provide complete solutions even though the skill is focused on modeling practice.

Mitigation: Check whether the response stays within the requested modeling support and route full solution requests to the appropriate tutoring skill.
