## Description:

Track brand mentions and citations across AI answer engines through the DataForSEO API, with a cost estimate and spending cap before each run.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, SEO consultants, and brand strategists use the skill to track how often AI answers mention a brand, which sources they cite, and how visibility changes across repeated runs. It produces comparable reports from approved prompt sets and spending limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial because referenced setup material was unresolved.

Mitigation: Review the referenced setup materials and any scripts or MCP configuration before installation.

Risk: Runs can incur API charges, and prompts may be sent to DataForSEO and other AI providers.

Mitigation: Estimate costs and obtain approval before paid calls; remove personal or confidential details from prompts before sending them.
