Mitigation: Narrow trigger terms or disable implicit invocation to reduce accidental activation. <br>
Risk: Workflow advice for PDF-related tasks can be incorrect, incomplete, or mismatched to the user's actual files and constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans, code, commands, and checklists before applying them, and validate outputs against the stated success criteria. <br>
