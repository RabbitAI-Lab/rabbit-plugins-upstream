## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes dual-use discussion of governance attacks, MEV behavior, and adversarial protocol strategies.

Mitigation: Use the material for defensive threat modeling, protocol review, and incentive design; avoid applying attacker-framed examples to manipulate governance systems or harm users.

Risk: Game-theoretic analysis can be incomplete or misleading when assumptions about actors, payoffs, information, or market conditions are wrong.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations with protocol-specific data and qualified security, economic, and governance review before relying on them for deployment decisions.
