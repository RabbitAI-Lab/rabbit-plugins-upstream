## Description:

Helps bakers maintain sourdough starters through feeding schedules, hydration calculations, readiness checks, troubleshooting, storage guidance, and levain timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Home bakers and other sourdough enthusiasts use this skill to plan starter feedings, calculate hydration, assess readiness, troubleshoot symptoms, store starters, and time levain builds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General starter guidance could be mistaken for a food-safety guarantee when growth looks contaminated.

Mitigation: Discard starters with mold or unusual colored growth; do not try to salvage them.
