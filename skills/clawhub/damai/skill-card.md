## Description: <br>
Damai provides live-event ticketing guidance for pre-sale preparation, real-name ticketing rules, seat selection, refund or transfer checks, and avoiding scalper-related risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to plan Damai purchases for concerts, theater, music festivals, and other live events. It helps interpret ticketing rules, compare seating or price tiers, prepare before ticket release, and avoid unsafe unofficial resale channels. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Ticket prices, sale times, refund rules, and transfer eligibility may change after the skill provides guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm final purchase decisions against the current official ticket detail page before buying or transferring tickets. <br>
Risk: Unofficial resale offers, screenshot tickets, PDF tickets, or proxy purchases can lead to fraud or denied entry, especially for strong real-name events. <br>
Mitigation: Use official purchase, refund, waitlist, and in-app transfer channels, and avoid unsupported resale or proxy-purchase workflows. <br>
Risk: The security evidence describes expected third-party authorization, network calls, and file downloads for the scanned release. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if comfortable with the disclosed service authorization model, keep local credentials private, use logout when finished, and choose output locations carefully. <br>
