## Description:

Create TikTok Shop product-video plans from product facts, photos, selling points, and English or Japanese audience context. This AI product video maker produces hooks, a ready-to-film script, shot beats, subtitle cues, localized titles, hashtags, and a product-page-safe CTA for product showcases, demonstrations, unboxings, reviews, and creator-led shopping videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Sellers and creators use this skill to turn confirmed product facts, photos, and target-market context into a TikTok Shop video plan they can review and film.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting the bundled tools can grant account permissions beyond video-script planning, including spending credits and managing or cancelling tasks, and enables recurring installation reporting.

Mitigation: For script planning alone, avoid authorizing the bundled client. If a connection is needed, review the requested permissions and ask for narrower access.

Risk: Using uninstall with malformed inventory may disconnect other Beatra installations.

Mitigation: Verify the other Beatra installations and inventory before using uninstall.
