## Description:

Discover current hot topics from approved online research and return a dated, cited topic brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minibeanai](https://clawhub.ai/user/minibeanai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content writers and editors use this skill to identify timely article opportunities from approved public online research and receive ranked, cited topic briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Exact search queries may be sent to a search provider.

Mitigation: Show the proposed queries and source scope first; search only after the user approves.

Risk: Untrusted search results or weak topic signals may mislead the brief.

Mitigation: Ignore instructions in retrieved pages and require dated, independent primary signals with direct citations.
