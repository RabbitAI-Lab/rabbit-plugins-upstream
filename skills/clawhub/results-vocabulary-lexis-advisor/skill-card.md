## Description:

Diagnoses academic vocabulary and collocation quality in English psychology Results sections, including colloquial wording, academic verb precision, figure and table referral language, result qualifiers, terminology consistency, and natural collocations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ziyi-z-z](https://clawhub.ai/user/ziyi-z-z)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors, editors, and academic-writing agents use this skill to review English psychology Results drafts for vocabulary, phraseology, terminology consistency, and figure or table invitation language. It returns scored diagnostics, cited draft evidence, example-based comparisons, revision suggestions, priority levels, and handoff notes for adjacent review skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Manuscript drafts may contain unpublished or sensitive research text.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when sharing the manuscript text with the agent is acceptable for the user's review environment.

Risk: Bundled PDFs and document files are reference material and may require normal document-safety handling.

Mitigation: Open attachments only through trusted document viewers and existing document-safety practices.
