## Description:

Diagnoses academic vocabulary and collocation quality in English psychology Results sections, including colloquial phrasing, reporting verb strength, figure and table invitations, result qualifiers, terminology consistency, and natural academic collocations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ziyi-z-z](https://clawhub.ai/user/ziyi-z-z)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External authors, editors, and academic writing agents use this skill to review English psychology Results drafts for vocabulary precision, academic register, terminology consistency, figure/table wording, and collocation naturalness. It produces structured diagnostics and revision suggestions for the vocabulary stage of a broader Results-writing review workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
