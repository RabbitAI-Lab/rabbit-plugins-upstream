# Examples：图表邀请语（Figure / Table Invitations）

来源：8 篇心理学经典论文的 Results 及证据报告段落。Type 为 Positive Example 的可直接作为诊断基准；Problem Example 为基于正例构造的典型学生问题句，用于 Before / After 对照。

---

## Example ID：FT-01

Source：Rhee et al., 2023, *Scientific Reports*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Table 1 shows the mean PRS-11 scores (mean and standard deviation) and repeated-measures ANOVA results for the experimental sites (F and p-value)."

Why it is useful：
段首以 "Table X shows + 内容概要" 开门见山，并预告表格包含哪些统计量，读者无需跳转即知表格结构。

Reusable Rule：
每个结果小节首句用 "Table/Figure X shows + 内容概要"，并在句内注明统计方法或变量。

Possible Application：
诊断时发现 "You can see the results in Table 1" 或仅写 "(see Table 1)" 而无任何内容说明时，引用本条给出改写。

## Example ID：FT-02

Source：Song et al., 2022, *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Figure 2 summarizes the results of the risk of bias assessment for the included studies."
"Table 1 provides an overview of the fundamental aspects of the studies that were considered."

Why it is useful：
Figure 作主语 + summarizes / provides an overview of 是图表邀请的黄金搭配，动词选择区分了"汇总结果"与"总览特征"两种功能。

Reusable Rule：
汇总型图用 "Figure X summarizes the results of..."；总览型表用 "Table X provides an overview of..."。

Possible Application：
用户通篇只用 "shows" 时，建议按图表功能替换为 summarizes / provides an overview of / illustrates。

## Example ID：FT-03

Source：Johnson et al., 2021, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"As seen in Fig. 1, the improvement in posttest reaction times was higher after the nature intervention (MdiffNATURE = 7.4 ms, 95% CI [18.8, -4.0]) than the urban intervention... but the difference was not significant (1-tailed, paired t-test, t(30) = 0.35, p = .36)."

Why it is useful：
"As seen in Fig. X" 句首引导 + 描述性比较 + 括号统计量 + 显著性判断，一句话完成图—数据—推断闭环，是图证先行写法的完整模板。

Reusable Rule：
图证先行模板："As seen in Fig. X, [描述性比较], but/and the difference was (not) significant (统计量)"。

Possible Application：
当用户把图引用与统计结果拆成多个松散句子时，用本条示范如何整合。

## Example ID：FT-04

Source：Chen et al., 2020, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"As shown in Fig. 5, EEG theta-alpha power was higher in the medial-to-posterior side of the brain during the restorative experience than during the nonrestorative experience, especially in the occipital lobe (O1 and O2)."
"The results revealed a strong negative association ... (Fig. 6)."

Why it is useful：
"as shown in" 句首式与句末 "(Fig. X)" 括号式交替使用，避免同一段落内邀请句式单调重复。

Reusable Rule：
同一段内交替使用 "As shown in Fig. X, ..." 与句末 "(Fig. X)"；不要连续两句都用同一句式。

Possible Application：
检测用户段落内图表邀请句式的重复度，超过 2 次同句式时引用本条建议替换。

## Example ID：FT-05

Source：Toba et al., 2024, *Neuropsychology Review*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"This white matter bundle is subdivided into three major pathways (SLF I, II, and III, Fig. 3 and Table 1), based on their frontal projections (Thiebaut de Schotten et al., 2011)."

Why it is useful：
括号式嵌入 "(列表, Fig. X and Table Y)" 将图表引用与分类列举并置，信息密度高且不打断主句。

Reusable Rule：
次要指图信息放括号内嵌入主句；主句主干保持完整。

Possible Application：
用户用完整句子引用次要图表（如另起一句 "Figure 3 shows this."）时，建议改为括号嵌入。

## Example ID：FT-06

Source：Posner & Petersen, 1990, *Annual Review of Neuroscience*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"...an area of the left ventral occipital lobe (gray area in Figure 1) is active during processing of visual words but not for letter-like forms."

Why it is useful：
用 "(gray area in Figure 1)" 指明图中具体视觉元素，读者可直接定位，是"指向图内元素"的精确写法。

Reusable Rule：
指向图内特定元素时用括号说明："(元素描述 in Figure X)"。

Possible Application：
用户写 "as the figure shows" 但不指明图中哪个部分时，引用本条要求具体化。

---

## Example ID：FT-07

Source：Song et al., 2022, *IJERPH*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Finally, we included 24 studies ... in the review (Figure 1)."

Why it is useful：
结果句末尾 "(Figure 1)" 轻量指图，先给结论再给图证，适合流程类结果（PRISMA）。

Reusable Rule：
结论句末尾用 "(Figure X)" 附上图证，不必每个图都另起邀请句。

Possible Application：
与 FT-03/FT-04 配合，教学生区分"主图详细引"与"辅图轻量引"两个层级。

## Example ID：FT-08

Source：构造问题例（对照 FT-01/FT-02/FT-04）

Dimension：Vocabulary — 图表邀请语

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence：
"From Figure 2 we can see that the nature group got better scores. The picture above shows this clearly."

Why it is useful：
集中展示三类典型错误：① "we can see" 口语化第一人称视角；② "got better" 口语化动词；③ "The picture" 误称学术图件（应为 Figure），"above" 在期刊排版中位置不稳定。

Reusable Rule：
禁用 "we can see / the picture / obviously shows"；改为 "As shown in Figure X, ..." 或 "Figure X shows..."。

Possible Application：
命中口语化图表邀请语时的标准 Before / After 对照：
Before: "From Figure 2 we can see that the nature group got better scores."
After: "As shown in Figure 2, the nature group obtained higher scores than the urban group."
