# Examples：学术动词与结果报告句式（Academic Verbs）

按动词功能分组：报告直接结果、报告相关/关联、报告阴性结果、引述已有证据、结果→解释衔接。

---

## Example ID：VB-01

Source：Toba et al., 2024, *Neuropsychology Review*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Results revealed a limited number of independent frontal or control processes called 'branches' (i.e., energization, task setting, and monitoring) with distinct anatomical underpinnings that could explain performance in each task."

Why it is useful：
"Results revealed + 名词短语 + (i.e., 操作化定义)" 是 Results 核心发现报告的经典骨架句，reveal 适合"经分析浮现的结构"。

Reusable Rule：
报告核心发现用 "Results revealed + 名词短语"，首次出现的成分用 i.e. 给出操作性说明。

Possible Application：
用户用 "We can find that..." 引出主结果时，替换为 "Results revealed/showed that..."。

## Example ID：VB-02

Source：Johnson et al., 2021, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"The combined effect size estimate indicated no improvement in attention following the nature intervention compared to the control condition (Hedges' g = -0.04, SEg = 0.07, 95% CI [-0.20, 0.13])."

Why it is useful：
indicate 是中性报告动词，与零结果搭配自然；estimate 作主语避免 "we found nothing" 的口语化。

Reusable Rule：
元分析主结果句式："The combined effect size estimate indicated [方向] (g = ..., SE = ..., 95% CI [...])"；零结果用 "indicated no improvement/difference"。

Possible Application：
用户写 "The result proved that nature doesn't work" 时，用本条示范强度降级与中性措辞。

## Example ID：VB-03

Source：Song et al., 2022, *IJERPH*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"An overall random effects model test on the selected samples revealed that green space exposure had significant effects on alleviating fatigue, anger, tension, and confusion and enhancing vigor."
"The Egger regression test yielded p-values greater than 0.05 ..., indicating that there was no publication bias in the literature of this study."

Why it is useful：
以分析方法作主语（"An overall random effects model test ... revealed that..."）是元分析报告规范句式；yield 专门用于"检验产生数值结果"，后接 indicating 分词收束解释。

Reusable Rule：
检验类结果用 "[检验名] yielded [数值], indicating that [结论]"；模型检验用 "[模型] revealed that..."。

Possible Application：
用户写 "The test said/showed p > 0.05" 时，替换 said → yielded。

## Example ID：VB-04

Source：Chen et al., 2020, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"The restorative experience induced significantly more efficiency in the theta band ... than that induced by the nonrestorative experience, but this effect was not significant in the alpha band ..."
"No association was found between the connectivity index and the other three PRS scores (i.e., a sense of being away, fascination or compatibility)."

Why it is useful：
induce 准确表达实验操纵引起的效应（仅限实验设计）；阴性相关用规范被动句 "No association was found between X and Y"。

Reusable Rule：
实验操纵效应用 "X induced significantly more ... than Y, but this effect was not significant in ..."；阴性相关固定句 "No association was found between X and Y"。

Possible Application：
用户写 "There was no relationship" 时升级为规范被动句；非实验设计误用 induce 时降级为 was associated with。

## Example ID：VB-05

Source：Rhee et al., 2023, *Scientific Reports*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Bonferroni post hoc tests showed that the restorative quality of the Sites C and B was significantly higher in PRS-general, Fascination, Being away, and Scope than Site A."

Why it is useful：
"[检验名] post hoc tests showed that + 比较结果" 是事后检验报告的标准框架，检验方法与动词搭配固定。

Reusable Rule：
事后检验统一用 "X post hoc tests showed/indicated that A was significantly higher/lower in ... than B"。

Possible Application：
用户写 "After the Bonferroni test, we knew C was better" 时，替换为规范框架。

## Example ID：VB-06

Source：Harvey, 2019, *Dialogues in Clinical Neuroscience*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Performance on the UFOV has been shown to predict driving problems and interventions aimed at speed dual-task processing have been shown to reduce accidents in older drivers."
"Thus, other tasks such as working memory, where activation of the frontal lobe has been repeatedly demonstrated, are often designated as executive functioning tasks."

Why it is useful：
现在完成被动 "has been shown to" / "has been repeatedly demonstrated" 表达领域内证据累积，是引述确证证据的强度标记。

Reusable Rule：
引述已被反复验证的证据用 "X has been (repeatedly) shown/demonstrated to ..."；单研究直接结果不用完成体。

Possible Application：
区分用户文中"自己结果"（showed）与"领域共识"（has been shown）的动词体。

## Example ID：VB-07

Source：Wang, Lin & Lin, 2025, *Frontiers in Forests and Global Change*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Dadvand et al. (2018) studied 253 children aged 6–7 and found that increased time spent in green spaces was associated with greater gray matter volume in the bilateral prefrontal cortices and left premotor cortex."
"MRI data further revealed that individuals with greater exposure to green spaces exhibited lower prefrontal cortex activity."

Why it is useful：
横断面/相关证据用 was associated with（审慎），数据呈现用 exhibited；further revealed 衔接追加证据，动词层级清晰（studied → found → was associated with）。

Reusable Rule：
相关/横断面设计一律用 "X was associated with / was correlated with Y"，禁用 cause / improve / reduce；被试表现用 "exhibited + 指标变化"。

Possible Application：
动词强度分级的核心对照例：用户用 "green space improved brain structure" 报告相关研究时，引用本条降级。

## Example ID：VB-08

Source：Johnson et al., 2021, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"The results showed that even though participants rated simulated nature settings as more restorative than simulated urban settings, the change in executive attention performance after the nature intervention did not significantly differ from the change in performance after the urban intervention."

Why it is useful：
even though 让步从句先确认操纵检查成功，主句再报告核心零结果 "did not significantly differ from"，逻辑严密且措辞中性。

Reusable Rule：
报告零结果模板："even though [操纵有效], [核心指标] did not significantly differ from ..."。

Possible Application：
用户用 "failed to differ" / "had no effect at all" 报告零结果时，替换为 did not significantly differ from。

## Example ID：VB-09

Source：Toba et al., 2024, *Neuropsychology Review*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Results showed a reduction of attentional lapses, suggesting that temporarily increasing connectivity in the DAN via network-targeted cerebellar stimulation can enhance sustained attention, particularly in individuals with lower baseline performance."

Why it is useful：
"showed ..., suggesting that ..." 将结果与解释分层，particularly 限定适用范围，是从结果到推论的规范过渡。

Reusable Rule：
结果→推论句式："Results showed X, suggesting that Y, particularly in Z"；suggesting 保持推测语气，不用 proving。

Possible Application：
用户在 Results 内直接写解释性断言（"This proves that..."）时，示范用 suggesting 分层并转介成员 F。

## Example ID：VB-10

Source：构造问题例（对照 VB-01/03/07）

Dimension：Vocabulary — 学术动词 / 口语化

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence：
"We looked at the data and it turns out that the nature group did a lot better, which basically proves that nature helps attention."

Why it is useful：
集中展示：① looked at / turns out / did a lot better / basically 全是口语化表达；② proves 动词强度严重超标（单研究+未明设计）；③ helps 因果泛化。

Reusable Rule：
口语动词替换表：looked at → examined/analyzed；turns out → revealed/showed；did better → outperformed / obtained higher scores；a lot → significantly/substantially；proves → suggests/indicates。

Possible Application：
标准 Before / After：
Before: "We looked at the data and it turns out that the nature group did a lot better, which basically proves that nature helps attention."
After: "The analysis revealed that the nature group significantly outperformed the urban group, suggesting that nature exposure may benefit attentional performance."
