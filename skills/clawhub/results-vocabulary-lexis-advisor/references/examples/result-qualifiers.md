# Examples：结果描述词与限定语（Result Qualifiers）

主题：significant / robust / consistent / marginal 等描述词的精度；阴性结果与边缘结果的措辞；结果与理论一致性表达。

---

## Example ID：RQ-01

Source：Rhee et al., 2023, *Scientific Reports*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Although differences in the mean score were observed among the three sites, the ANOVA results did not show a statistically significant difference among them."

Why it is useful：
Although 让步结构区分"描述性差异存在"与"统计上不显著"，避免读者误读；statistically significant 全称限定，排除歧义。

Reusable Rule：
报告非显著结果模板："Although [描述性差异], the results did not show a statistically significant difference ..."；避免只写 "there was no difference"（抹平描述性差异）。

Possible Application：
用户写 "There was no difference between groups (p = .08)" 时，示范补充描述性信息。

## Example ID：RQ-02

Source：Johnson et al., 2021, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"While tiredness increased after the urban intervention, it was not significant after correction for multiple comparisons."
"Given the high degree of heterogeneity among effect sizes..., the combined effect size estimate should be interpreted cautiously."

Why it is useful：
如实报告"校正后不显著"是统计透明的规范措辞；"should be interpreted cautiously" 是给结论降温的标准限定语。

Reusable Rule：
校正后非显著："...was not significant after correction for multiple comparisons (ns)"；高异质性结论降温："Given ..., the estimate should be interpreted cautiously"。

Possible Application：
用户只报告校正前显著结果、或高异质性下仍强断言时，引用本条。

## Example ID：RQ-03

Source：Song et al., 2022, *IJERPH*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Depression obtained a moderate effect size of -0.50 (95% CI: -0.82 to -0.18), but the results were not significant (p = 0.096)."

Why it is useful：
效应量分级形容词（small/moderate/large）+ but 转折如实报告阴性结果，不回避、不夸大。

Reusable Rule：
阴性结果模板："[变量] obtained a [分级] effect size of X (95% CI: ...), but the results were not significant (p = ...)"；效应量分级词需与领域基准一致（Cohen's conventions）。

Possible Application：
用户隐瞒阴性结果或写 "almost significant" 时，用本条替换（p = .096 不得称 almost significant——直接报告数值）。

## Example ID：RQ-04

Source：Chen et al., 2020, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"An independent t-test revealed a difference, though not significant, in the occipital lobes in the alpha ... and theta ... bands (p = 0.079 / 0.090)."
"...revealed no significant difference between restorative and nonrestorative exposures ..., despite the potential trend implied in Fig. 4."

Why it is useful：
"though not significant" 插入语与 "despite the potential trend" 让步语是报告边缘结果的两种规范措辞，既承认趋势又不越界断言。

Reusable Rule：
边缘结果（.05 < p < .10）用 "a difference, though not significant, ..." 或 "no significant difference ..., despite the potential trend ..."；禁用 "marginally significant" 暗示接近达标（除非目标期刊明确允许，并直接报告 p 值）。

Possible Application：
用户写 "The result was almost/marginally significant (p = .07), showing that..." 并据此做强结论时，示范降温措辞并转介成员 F。

## Example ID：RQ-05

Source：Posner & Petersen, 1990, *Annual Review of Neuroscience*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"This result is compatible with the view that visual orienting involves systems separate but interconnected with those used for language processing."
"The parietal PET activation during visual orienting fits well with the lesion and single cell recording results discussed above."

Why it is useful：
be compatible with / fits well with 表达"结果与理论一致但不排他"，比 "This proves the theory" 精确得多。

Reusable Rule：
结果支持某理论但不排他时，用 "be compatible/consistent with the view that ..." 或 "X fits well with Y (discussed above)"。

Possible Application：
用户写 "The results prove our hypothesis" 时，替换为 "The results are consistent with our hypothesis" 并引用本条。

## Example ID：RQ-06

Source：Toba et al., 2024, *Neuropsychology Review*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"They also observed that patients whose performance was significantly impaired relative to controls had DLPFC lesions, whereas patients with inferior medial frontal areas lesions made the fewest errors."
"...whereas TMS over the left FEF had no significant effect on performance (Hung et al., 2011)."

Why it is useful：
"significantly impaired relative to controls" + whereas 对照是组间比较规范；"had no significant effect on" 是阴性结果的固定搭配（不说 "did not affect"）。

Reusable Rule：
组间对比用 "X was significantly impaired relative to controls, whereas Y ..."；无效应固定写 "X had no significant effect on Y"。

Possible Application：
用户写 "TMS didn't affect performance" 时升级为 "TMS had no significant effect on performance"。

## Example ID：RQ-07

Source：Harvey, 2019, *Dialogues in Clinical Neuroscience*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"...the rapid forgetting of adequately learned information... is a robust finding in populations who have medial temporal lobe brain dysfunction."
"...careful combination of tasks can reveal patterns of performance consistent with a variety of different neurological and neuropsychiatric conditions."

Why it is useful：
"robust finding" 仅用于跨样本/跨研究稳定的结果；"patterns ... consistent with" 表达模式层面的一致性。

Reusable Rule：
robust 只修饰可复现的发现（a robust finding in populations who ...），不用来修饰单个 p 值；一致性用 patterns/findings consistent with X。

Possible Application：
用户用 robust 修饰单次结果（"a robust p-value"）时纠正。

## Example ID：RQ-08

Source：Wang, Lin & Lin, 2025, *Frontiers in Forests and Global Change*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Sudimac and Kühn (2022) reported that the reduction in amygdala activity was significant only among female participants, suggesting potential gender differences in neural sensitivity to environmental stressors."

Why it is useful：
"significant only among ..." 精确限定显著性的适用子群体；"suggesting potential ..." 双重降调保持推测语气。

Reusable Rule：
显著性有条件时用 "significant only among/in [子群体]"；亚组推论用 "suggesting potential ... differences"。

Possible Application：
用户把亚组显著结果泛化为全样本结论（"The treatment reduced stress"）时，示范限定写法。

## Example ID：RQ-09

Source：Johnson et al., 2021, *Journal of Environmental Psychology*

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence：
"Our findings are, therefore, inconsistent with Berman et al.'s findings (2008, Experiment 2)."

Why it is useful：
be (in)consistent with 精确点名与何研究一致/冲突，且定位到实验编号，是复制研究与前人对比的标准措辞。

Reusable Rule：
与前人结果对比用 "Our findings are (in)consistent with X et al. (year, Exp. N)"，不写 "different from previous studies" 这类模糊表达。

Possible Application：
用户写 "This is different from what others found" 时，要求具体化并给出规范句式。

## Example ID：RQ-10

Source：构造问题例（对照 RQ-01/03/04）

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词 / 口语化

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence：
"The result was very significant and super clear. There was a huge difference between the two groups."

Why it is useful：
very / super / huge 是口语强化词，不能修饰统计显著性；significant 是二元的（达到阈值与否），不存在"很显著"；且缺少 p 值与统计量支撑。

Reusable Rule：
禁用 very/quite/super/huge 修饰 significant 或 difference；显著性直接报告统计量与 p 值，差异大小用效应量分级词（small/moderate/large effect）。

Possible Application：
标准 Before / After：
Before: "The result was very significant and super clear. There was a huge difference between the two groups."
After: "The difference between the two groups was significant, t(58) = 3.76, p = .029, representing a large effect (d = 0.85)."

## Example ID：RQ-11

Source：Harvey, 2019, *Dialogues in Clinical Neuroscience*（警示例）

Dimension：Vocabulary — 结果描述词（歧义警示）

Type：Problem Example（歧义用法警示）

Original Sentence：
"For instance, performance on various coding tasks is not only the most significant impairment in schizophrenia but is also the cognitive ability domain that is most strongly correlated with impairments in everyday functioning."

Why it is useful：
此句中 "the most significant impairment" 的 significant 意为"最突出的"而非"统计显著的"。在 Results 中这种双关极易误读，诊断时应注意区分。

Reusable Rule：
Results 中 significant 默认指统计显著；表达"突出的/重要的"用 pronounced / marked / prominent，避免歧义。

Possible Application：
用户写 "the most significant finding" 但无统计含义时，建议改为 "the most pronounced/notable finding"。
