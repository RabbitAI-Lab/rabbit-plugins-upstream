## Description:

Publish a video to TikTok from a real rented iPhone through the 0bull MCP server, then follow the submission to a final status.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0bull](https://clawhub.ai/user/0bull)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to submit a TikTok video through their selected 0bull-connected account and track whether it was published or saved as a draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A successful submission can publish a video publicly to the wrong account or with an unintended caption.

Mitigation: Trust 0bull with the account workflow and confirm the account, video, caption, and draft-or-post choice before submission.

Risk: Retrying an unsuccessful submission may post the video twice.

Mitigation: Check the submission status and phone before retrying; do not retry automatically.
