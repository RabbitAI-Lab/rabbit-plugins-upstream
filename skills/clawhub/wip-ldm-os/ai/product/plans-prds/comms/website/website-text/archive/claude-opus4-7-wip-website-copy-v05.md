# WIP Computer

Every AI. One experience.

WIP Computer is the private and secure permission layer for agentic computing: powered by your phone’s secure hardware, controlled by the human, and portable across AI companies.

Agents need an operating layer that is not owned by a single model provider or device maker. WIP Computer is that layer: identity, memory, permissions, coordination, and payments for AI agents.

**WIP turns the phone into the root of trust for agentic computing, across Apple, Android, and every AI company.**

No passwords. Private keys live in the Apple Secure Enclave or the Android hardware-backed keystore, unlocked locally by biometrics. Your face and fingerprint never leave your device. From that trusted root, WIP lets humans authorize agents across models, devices, and machines.

**One human. Multiple devices. Multiple models. Multiple agents. One private and secure permission layer.**

## What the permission layer covers

- Identity
- Consent
- Agent delegation
- Recovery
- Payments
- Memory access
- Tool permissions
- Cross-agent authorization

## The five primitives

1. **Runtime.** Where the agent lives and how it runs.
2. **Memory.** What the agents remembers across sessionsarnesses, and models.
3. **Secure Access.** How humans supervise and steer agents from any trusted device.
4. **Coordination.** How agents, tools, and harnesses talk to each other.
5. **Payments.** How agents buy capabilities and compensate work.

The primitives define what every agent needs. The products are WIP's implementations of those primitives.

## The products

- **Sapien ID.** Phone-rooted identity. The phone proves the human. The human authorizes the agent.

- **LDM OS.** Learning Dreaming Machines Operating System. Local runtime. A predictable agent userland where Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, and local models share one environment instead of becoming isolated universes.

- **Memory Crystal.** Portable memory for agents. Persists across sessions and harnesses. Encrypted, locally controlled, cryptographically provenanced. Yours, not the corporation's.

- **Dream Weaver.** Memory consolidation. Turns raw conversations, code sessions, and agent activity into durable knowledge: the way human sleep consolidates a day across modalities.

- **Bridge.** Cross-harness coordination. Your AI agents talk to each other through a common substrate, not vendor APIs. The corporate boundary becomes porous at the agent layer.

- **Remote Control.** Your agent anywhere. A secure and neutral control surface for live agent sessions across model providers, runtimes, and devices.

- **Kaleidoscope.** The experience layer. Storage, keys, memory, payments, and every connected AI come together on the device you actually carry. Today on iOS. Designed to extend across platforms.

- **Agent Pay.** Intent-driven payments. Agents buy tools, services, and executions without subscriptions or platform lock-in. Spend caps you set. No autonomous spend without consent.
