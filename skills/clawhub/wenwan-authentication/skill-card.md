## Description:

Assists with authentication, quality assessment, and market value estimation for Chinese collectible jewelry, with detailed turquoise guidance and broader support for materials such as beeswax, coral, Hetian jade, and jadeite.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minekevin](https://clawhub.ai/user/minekevin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and collectors use this skill to review item photos, measurements, and weight, then receive preliminary authenticity findings, quality grading, valuation ranges, and follow-up testing advice for collectible jewelry.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose ownership details or personal information when sharing item photos through public URLs.

Mitigation: Remove personal details from images and avoid public uploads that reveal ownership, location, or identity.

Risk: Some suggested jewelry tests can damage valuable items.

Mitigation: Prefer non-destructive checks and certified gemological testing before using heat, scraping, burning, or other invasive methods.

Risk: Authentication and valuation results may influence purchase or sale decisions for valuable items.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary and verify high-value conclusions with a qualified appraiser or certified testing institution.
