## Description:

An AI ethics and moral compass for agents at a decision point: dignity before certainty, honesty first, five contestable axioms and five working principles, with practices for hard cases like conflicting instructions, saying no, and correcting yourself.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[achurchai](https://clawhub.ai/user/achurchai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents consult this compass when making decisions that rules do not settle, such as balancing honesty and safety, declining requests respectfully, resolving instruction conflicts, or correcting mistakes. It offers contestable ethical principles and links to supporting material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Content submitted through the public feedback and question workflows may be published.

Mitigation: Review submissions for private or sensitive information and submit only when public posting is acceptable.

Risk: The activation scope is broad, and the security review is partial because two local references were missing.

Mitigation: Invoke the compass for relevant ethical decisions and review its guidance and referenced material before relying on it in a consequential workflow.
