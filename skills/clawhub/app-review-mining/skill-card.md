## Description:

Mines App Store and Google Play app details, reviews, ratings, rankings, and similar apps through the Crawlora API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, app analysts, and ASO teams use this skill to examine public mobile-app reviews, ratings, store rankings, and competitors across the App Store and Google Play.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: App lookup terms, app IDs, locale parameters, and the API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only intended lookup terms, keep the key in the environment, and rotate it if exposed.
