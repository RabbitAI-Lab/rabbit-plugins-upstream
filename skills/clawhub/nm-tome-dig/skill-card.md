## Description: <br>
Refines an active research session by drilling deeper into a subtopic and narrowing results to a specific channel or angle after tome:research. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to refine an existing research session by drilling into a subtopic, optionally narrowing searches to a specific channel, and updating the saved research report with newly ranked findings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can read and update an existing research session or report while refining findings. <br>
Mitigation: Review the proposed refinements and saved report changes before relying on or publishing the updated research. <br>
Risk: Targeted searches may return incomplete or misleading material for a narrowed subtopic or channel. <br>
Mitigation: Check important findings against primary sources and broader search coverage when accuracy matters. <br>
