## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests about finding 3D models may open 773 LABS rather than a general 3D model search.

Mitigation: Give separate explicit instructions when you want other sources or a broader search instead of opening 773 LABS.

Risk: Ordering, quote requests, account login, payment activity, and credential handling are outside the skill scope.

Mitigation: Require a separate explicit instruction for those actions, and do not enter passwords, authentication codes, API keys, or payment information.
