## Description:

Guides M&A deal teams in creating Box data rooms, managing folder-level access, checking permissions before sharing, and answering due-diligence questions with cited Box AI responses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iskysun96](https://clawhub.ai/user/iskysun96)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Deal teams and legal professionals use this skill to organize due-diligence documents in Box, manage internal and external access, and answer document questions with citations. Legal judgments remain with counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External access or shared links could expose sensitive deal-room documents, including files added later.

Mitigation: Confirm recipients, folder scope, permission level, and expiration; audit access before and after changes.

Risk: Unpinned prerequisite skill installs may introduce unreviewed changes.

Mitigation: Use pinned or otherwise vetted versions of the prerequisite Box skills.

Risk: Broad M&A and due-diligence triggers may invoke the workflow for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Confirm that the request concerns a Box deal room before taking action.
