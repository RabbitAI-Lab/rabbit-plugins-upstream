## Use Case:

Employees and workplace professionals use this skill to turn a report topic, audience, and goal into a concise five-part Chinese report outline with fill-in prompts. It is intended for upward reporting across roles and industries.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include sensitive patient, financial, or personnel details when asking for report outlines.

Mitigation: Use anonymized or minimized facts in prompts and avoid including sensitive details unless the deployment context permits them.

Risk: The generated outline could frame workplace facts inaccurately if the user's inputs are incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Review the outline against source data and adjust claims, numbers, owners, and decision requests before sharing.
