## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security evidence flags high-risk guidance around scaled social accounts, synthetic personas, ban avoidance, hidden promotion, and bought app ratings. <br>
Mitigation: Use only for legitimate creator-program planning, require platform Terms of Service and advertising-disclosure review, and prohibit fake reviews, undisclosed ads, synthetic identities presented as real people, sockpuppet account networks, ban evasion, and coordinated inauthentic promotion. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes tactics for AI-assisted and multi-account social publishing that can create compliance and reputation risk if executed without controls. <br>
Mitigation: Keep one primary account per real person or brand identity, disclose AI-generated content where required, post within platform limits, and use legitimate creator agencies or creators operating within platform policies. <br>
