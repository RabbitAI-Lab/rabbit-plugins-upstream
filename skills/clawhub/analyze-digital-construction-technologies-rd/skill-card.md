This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

R&D, engineering, IP, and strategy teams use this skill to prepare evidence-backed technology intelligence, competitive landscape, patent landscape, market-entry, and due-diligence reports for digital construction in transport infrastructure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may use authorized web or PatSnap research services and depends on external evidence coverage.

Mitigation: Use only approved services, record source URLs, cutoff dates, query details, returned counts, and coverage limits for material claims.

Risk: Generated reports may include confidential evidence or sensitive project context.

Mitigation: Review JSON inputs before report generation and exclude confidential evidence from outputs unless disclosure is intended and approved.

Risk: Patent, legal, engineering, safety, procurement, or investment conclusions may be misused as authoritative decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision preparation and require qualified specialist review before relying on material conclusions.
