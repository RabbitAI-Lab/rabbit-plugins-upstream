## Description:

Founder Agency Workbench helps agency owners and growth consultants package, price, onboard, deliver, and monitor Reddit/GEO managed-service operations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agency owners, freelance growth consultants, GEO specialists, and in-house growth teams use this skill to design service tiers, pricing models, onboarding SOPs, white-label reports, capacity plans, churn-risk workflows, and case-study drafts for Reddit and GEO managed services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing benchmarks, ROI projections, margin targets, and retained-revenue estimates may be treated as guaranteed business outcomes.

Mitigation: Review calculations against current agency economics, client contracts, staffing costs, and finance data before using them in pricing, sales, or planning decisions.

Risk: Generated public posts, replies, reports, and case studies could create client approval, reputation, or accuracy issues.

Mitigation: Keep human approval on all public posts, replies, reports, decks, testimonials, and case studies; label case studies as drafts until the client approves them.

Risk: Operational workflows may involve access to analytics, Slack, billing, brand assets, and Reddit-related materials.

Mitigation: Grant only the minimum required access, prefer read-only analytics access where possible, and remove access when client work ends.
