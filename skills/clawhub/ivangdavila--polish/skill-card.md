## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to make Polish writing sound more natural, casual, and human instead of overly formal or machine-like.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make Polish text too informal for business, legal, medical, customer-facing, or unfamiliar-recipient contexts.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for formal Polish or avoid this skill when a formal register is required.
