## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to find appliance-repair providers, check service availability, and create a booking through Lokuli's MCP server. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking creation sends customer contact details to the Lokuli MCP service. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, date, time, customer name, email, and phone number with the user. <br>
