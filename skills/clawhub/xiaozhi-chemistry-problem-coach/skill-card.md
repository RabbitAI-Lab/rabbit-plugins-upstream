## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary-school students through one chemistry problem at a time, using a four-step method to identify substances, connect observations with particles and symbols, set up equations, and check conservation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students studying Chinese middle-school chemistry or introductory high-school chemistry use the skill for guided help on a specific problem, including deductions, reaction equations, and solution calculations. Long-term records, wrong-answer handoff, and reminders are optional and require separate consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese-language K–12 settings; localize curriculum, consent rules, and emergency contacts for other regions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional student records, wrong-answer handoff, or reminders could expose learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep all three off by default and obtain separate student consent, plus guardian consent where required, before enabling them.

Risk: Chinese curriculum assumptions and emergency-help contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Use in the intended Chinese K–12 setting or localize language, curriculum, consent rules, and local emergency contacts before broader deployment.

Risk: Chemistry advice could be mistaken for permission to perform an unsafe experiment.

Mitigation: Keep tutoring focused on written problems and follow the skill's lab-safety boundary; do not provide at-home experiment procedures.
