## Description:

Guides middle- and high-school students through individual chemistry problems using a four-step method, with optional memory, error handoff, and reminders only after consent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students studying chemistry in Simplified Chinese use this skill to work through a specific middle- or high-school problem with graduated hints and checks of chemical reasoning and calculations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (K–12 curriculum alignment)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile memory, wrong-answer handoff, or reminders could expose student learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep all three features off by default; verify separate student consent, any required guardian consent, and field-level limits before enabling them.

Risk: Chemistry questions involving experiments could prompt unsafe at-home activity.

Mitigation: Use the skill's laboratory safety boundaries and redirect experimental design or operations rather than giving at-home procedure steps.
