## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking middle and high school students through individual chemistry problems using a four-step method, with memory, error logging, and reminders off by default.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this skill to work through a specific chemistry problem with guided prompts, equations, and conservation checks rather than receive an immediate answer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; localize emergency contacts, curriculum, and minor-consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records or reminders could be used without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, wrong-answer handoffs, sharing, and reminders off unless the student and, where required, a guardian explicitly enable each use.

Risk: Mainland China emergency contacts, curriculum, and minor-consent assumptions may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize these safeguards before offering the skill outside Mainland China.

Risk: Chemistry guidance could be mistaken for instructions to perform an unsafe experiment.

Mitigation: Keep laboratory discussion educational, follow the bundled safety guidance, and do not provide at-home experiment steps.
