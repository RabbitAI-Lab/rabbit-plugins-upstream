## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary-school students through individual chemistry problems using a four-step, hint-led approach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to work through a specific junior-high or introductory senior-high chemistry question, from identifying substances to checking equations and conservation. Optional learning-profile access, wrong-answer handoff, and reminders require separate consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; localize crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, and consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's learning data could be read or shared without informed consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, wrong-answer handoff, and reminders off by default; obtain separate student and, when needed, guardian consent for each action.

Risk: Mainland-China crisis contacts or curriculum assumptions may not fit students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize contacts and curriculum before deployment outside mainland China; confirm the student's location before giving crisis numbers.

Risk: Chemistry guidance could be mistaken for safe instructions to perform an experiment.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's lab-safety boundaries and route experimental design or operations to appropriate supervision rather than giving at-home procedures.
