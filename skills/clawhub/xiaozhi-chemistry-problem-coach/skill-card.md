## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through individual chemistry problems using a four-step approach: identify substances, connect observations with particles and symbols, set up equations, and check conservation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese middle- and high-school students use this skill to work through a specific chemistry question with graduated hints and checks, rather than receiving an immediate answer. Educators may use it to support curriculum-aligned practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese K–12 curriculum; localize before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profile information or study history could be shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile memory, cross-skill sharing, wrong-answer handoff, and reminders off unless the student and any required guardian explicitly enable each feature.

Risk: Chinese curriculum, safety referrals, and consent rules may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Use in the intended Chinese K–12 context; localize curriculum, safety referrals, and consent rules before deployment elsewhere.
