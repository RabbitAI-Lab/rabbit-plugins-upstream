## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through individual chemistry problems using a four-step method, with optional learning-profile features requiring consent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to work through a specific middle- or high-school chemistry question with stepwise hints and checks of substances, representations, equations, and conservation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved student learning-profile data may be read without a clearly enforced separate sharing permission.

Mitigation: Before sharing saved profile fields, require cross-skill sharing, profile access and current-session student consent, any required guardian consent, and working memory-pause controls; otherwise use session-only mode.
