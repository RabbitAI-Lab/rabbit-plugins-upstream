## Description:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records, wrong-answer handoff, parent sharing or reminders may disclose a student's learning information.

Mitigation: Keep these features off by default and obtain student or guardian understanding and explicit consent before enabling them.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may not fit students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and adapt curriculum guidance and emergency or support contacts before use outside Mainland China.
