## Description:

陪初中和高中学生按四步法分析化学题，从确定物质、连接宏观与微观表征，到列式计算并检查守恒。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生可用它分步梳理推断、方程式、溶液与计算题，在尝试后逐级获得提示，并检查方程式和结果是否守恒、合理。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland Chinese K–12 curricula)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records and reminders depend on companion skills and platform consent enforcement.

Mitigation: Use conversation-only mode by default; enable saved records or reminders only with explicit consent and verified platform enforcement.

Risk: Chemistry tutoring could be mistaken for instructions to conduct experiments at home.

Mitigation: Keep help focused on problem solving and theory; do not provide at-home experiment steps, and refer hands-on procedures to teacher-supervised lab guidance.
