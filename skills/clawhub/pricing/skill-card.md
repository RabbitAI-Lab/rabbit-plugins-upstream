## Description: <br>
Pricing helps agents set, test, present, and change prices across value metrics, tier packaging, discounts, price increases, willingness-to-pay research, enterprise quotes, marketplace take rates, and price-display compliance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators, founders, sales and revenue teams, and agents advising them use this skill to produce pricing recommendations, price books, discount policies, research plans, pricing tests, and price-change plans. The skill is advisory: a human executes live billing changes and customer communications. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may maintain local pricing records, deal history, contacts, and project notes. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when local storage of these business records is acceptable, and review configured paths before use. <br>
Risk: Pasted billing exports, contracts, screenshots, or discount lists may contain secrets. <br>
Mitigation: Do not paste live secrets unless the agent is expected to replace them with pointers before saving; review local notes for stripped secret values. <br>
Risk: Pricing advice can affect customer trust, revenue, renewals, or legal compliance if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the generated prices, plans, notices, and compliance checks as human-reviewed proposals; the skill does not change live billing systems or send customer messages. <br>
