## Description:

Guides users through PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People signing up for PowPow use this skill to learn about the service, complete registration on the official site, resolve common sign-up issues, and find the subscription steps needed to activate their account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account or payment details could be disclosed in chat or entered on an unofficial page.

Mitigation: Use official PowPow pages for registration, verification, and payment; never share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Risk: Subscription activation can lag behind payment, particularly when manual confirmation is required.

Mitigation: Check account status on the official site, allow time for confirmation, and contact PowPow support if activation remains pending.
