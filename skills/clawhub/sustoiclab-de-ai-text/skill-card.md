## Description:

去AI味文本润色助手 · Su's StoicLab helps agents polish Chinese text to reduce AI-generated style markers while preserving the original meaning, facts, and paragraph structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and editors use this skill to revise Chinese articles, drafts, and review text so it reads more naturally and less like AI-generated prose without adding information or changing facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used to make AI-assisted prose less obvious.

Mitigation: Install and use it only where that matches the organization's acceptable-use policy and any disclosure requirements.

Risk: Style editing can unintentionally change nuance or meaning despite the skill's instruction to preserve facts.

Mitigation: Review revised text against the source before publication, especially for factual, legal, medical, or policy-sensitive material.
