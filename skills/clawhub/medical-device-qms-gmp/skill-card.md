## Description:

Generates and reviews medical-device GMP and ISO 13485 quality-system document frameworks, validation plans, and audit-preparation checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Medical-device quality and regulatory teams use this skill to draft or review QMS manuals, procedure frameworks, process and reprocessing validation plans, sterilization qualification, and audit-readiness checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted quality-system or validation guidance may be outdated or unsuitable for a specific device or jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Check current official regulations and standards and obtain qualified professional review before using the drafts for compliance decisions.

Risk: Bundled manifest hashes do not match the released files and cannot establish package integrity.

Mitigation: Do not treat the manifest as an integrity attestation until its hashes are corrected and independently checked.
