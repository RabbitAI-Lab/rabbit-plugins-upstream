## Description:

A vocabulary of camera moves and framings for Phantra scene prompts, with guidance on when each works in a 2–10 second shot and the rule of one move per shot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and agents use the skill to choose and describe camera movement, framing, and shot timing for a scene beat. It helps write clear shot prompts and can guide a complete scene draft when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may save a complete scene when the user only wants camera suggestions.

Mitigation: Ask for advice-only camera guidance when scene saving is not wanted.

Risk: Rendering is paid and may incur a charge.

Mitigation: Review the quoted price and approve before rendering.

Risk: Separately supplied tools were outside the security review.

Mitigation: Review those tools separately before using them.
