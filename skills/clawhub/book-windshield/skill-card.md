## Use Case: <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The booking flow can send personal contact details and appointment details to a third-party service endpoint. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, time slot, customer name, email, phone number, and user consent before creating a booking. <br>
