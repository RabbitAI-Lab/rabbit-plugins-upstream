## Description: <br>
Helps agents assess cross-industry return, refund, rework, cancellation, and service-remedy intake requests by checking eligibility conditions, required evidence, timing, fees, handoff, and acceptance paths. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Customer support, operations, service, and commercial teams use this skill to triage whether a return, refund, cancellation, rework, or other remedy request should enter an intake process. It structures the facts, missing materials, deadlines, cost boundaries, handoff steps, acceptance criteria, risks, and customer communication points while routing uncertain or high-impact cases to manual approval. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill processes order, contract, policy, evidence, service, and customer-request details that may be sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Users should provide only information appropriate for the agent environment and follow their organization's handling rules for customer, contract, and evidence data. <br>
Risk: Return, refund, rework, cancellation, or compensation decisions can be affected by policy conflicts, legal obligations, liability, or high-value exceptions. <br>
Mitigation: The skill should be used for advisory intake only; uncertain, conflicting, legal, high-value, or exception cases should be escalated for manual approval before any remedy is authorized. <br>
