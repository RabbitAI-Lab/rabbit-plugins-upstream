## Description:

Recommends an appropriate AI media skill for a video, image, voice, or music task and presents choices before generation begins.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and agents use this skill to find existing workflows for AI media tasks and compare visual options before choosing a tool. It routes people toward relevant skills without performing the media generation itself.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries are sent to varg.ai and could contain sensitive details.

Mitigation: Keep confidential information and private links out of search wording.

Risk: Recommended skills and installation commands may involve third-party components that have not been reviewed by this skill.

Mitigation: Review the recommended skill and its installation command before running it.

Risk: Following a recommendation into media generation can require an account and credits.

Mitigation: Check the selected service's authorization and credit requirements before proceeding with generation.
