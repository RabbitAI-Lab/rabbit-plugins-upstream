## Description:

生成、改写和润色校园学生场景的规范中文邮件与书面公文，覆盖课程或活动请假、导师沟通与毕业设计进度汇报、实习岗位投递、奖学金申请与自述、调课申请、校园活动正式通知与群发邮件等。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, campus staff, and other Chinese-speaking users use this skill to draft, revise, and polish campus emails, applications, formal notices, and group messages from user-provided facts while preserving placeholders for missing details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain unresolved placeholders or user-provided facts that are incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable to send.

Mitigation: Review every draft before sending, replace all `【待填写】` placeholders, and verify dates, recipients, claims, attachments, approvals, grades, and permissions.

Risk: Campus messages can include sensitive personal details such as medical, family, contact, or academic information.

Mitigation: Keep only information needed for the message, avoid unnecessary sensitive details, and confirm any publicly shared contact information before use.
