## Description: <br>
Provides concise stock research for A-share, Hong Kong, and U.S. equities using market data, recent web search, and mandatory regulatory-event checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tanteng](https://clawhub.ai/user/tanteng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Investors, analysts, and agents use this skill to assemble a quick stock-research brief with rating, trading guidance, key price levels, core points, and risk notes. It is intended as research assistance and should not be treated as financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce investment ratings and trading suggestions based on market data and web search. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance, verify important facts independently, and do not rely on the output as financial advice. <br>
Risk: External market data and web search results may be incomplete, stale, or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check material claims against authoritative filings, exchange data, and current regulatory disclosures before acting. <br>
