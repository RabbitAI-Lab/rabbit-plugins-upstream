## Description:

Provides workplace civility guidance based on Robert I. Sutton's No Asshole Rule and Asshole Survival Guide frameworks for recognizing, responding to, and reducing bullying or demeaning workplace behavior.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[timo2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/timo2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and workplace-culture practitioners use this skill to identify patterns of bullying or demeaning conduct, evaluate the costs of tolerating high-performing toxic colleagues, and choose practical response or organization-level prevention steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill when the user did not intend to request workplace-civility guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user is asking about workplace behavior before applying the Sutton framework.

Risk: Users may mistake workplace behavior guidance for legal, compliance, or HR advice.

Mitigation: Keep guidance focused on observable behavior and direct discrimination, harassment, or formal complaint issues to appropriate legal, compliance, or HR channels.
