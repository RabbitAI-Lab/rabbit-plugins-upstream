## Description: <br>
Builds an engineering hiring rubric and technical interview scorecard for evaluating software engineers at a specific role and level. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Hiring managers, recruiters, and engineering interviewers use this skill to create calibrated role- and level-specific interview loops, rubrics, scorecards, behavioral questions, technical questions, system design criteria, and debrief agendas. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated hiring rubrics and scorecards may conflict with company hiring policies or applicable employment law. <br>
Mitigation: Have the rubric reviewed by the appropriate hiring, HR, or legal owner before using it in an interview process. <br>
