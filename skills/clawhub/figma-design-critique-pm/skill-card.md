## Description: <br>
Runs a PM-perspective design critique focused on product outcomes and user goals, not aesthetics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, founders, and product teams use this skill to review Figma design descriptions against user goals, business outcomes, requirements, and key metrics. It produces outcome-focused critique rather than visual design feedback. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Design descriptions, business goals, requirements, and key metrics supplied for critique may contain confidential product information. <br>
Mitigation: Share only context the user is comfortable disclosing and redact sensitive roadmap, customer, financial, or unreleased product details before use. <br>
