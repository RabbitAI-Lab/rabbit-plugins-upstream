## Description:

Helps students save, tag, retrieve, and review Chinese writing materials for essays and reading practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to collect and find tagged Chinese-language writing examples, quotations, and ideas, with optional usage summaries. It supports material retrieval rather than taking over the essay-writing process.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, student-data consent, and crisis-support guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing materials or usage information may be retained or shared contrary to student or parent preferences.

Mitigation: Confirm memory and profile settings, cross-skill sharing, and parent-sharing preferences before use; honor pause, deletion, and consent controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis contacts may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, consent requirements, and crisis-support contacts before deploying elsewhere.

Risk: Unverified quotations or attributions could mislead students.

Mitigation: Record sources where available and label uncertain sources for verification before use.
