## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language e-commerce operating expert-team skill for brand diagnostics, weekly or monthly reviews, platform growth issues, advertising ROI and profit optimization, content and livestream analysis, and action plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External e-commerce brand owners, operators, and growth teams use this skill to diagnose cross-channel store performance, review business reports, analyze platform-specific growth problems, and produce prioritized action plans. It is intended for experienced e-commerce users who need direct, evidence-based operating guidance rather than basic marketing education. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad Chinese business-planning requests that are not specifically about e-commerce. <br>
Mitigation: State the e-commerce platform, channel, metric, and business question explicitly when invoking it. <br>
Risk: Business diagnostics can be misleading when source data is incomplete, stale, or mixed across platforms. <br>
Mitigation: Provide official backend exports or clearly dated figures, and keep the skill's data gaps and assumptions visible in the final report. <br>
Risk: Store reports, advertising metrics, and margin data can contain sensitive commercial information. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the minimum data needed for the analysis and remove account credentials, customer personal data, and unnecessary identifiers. <br>
