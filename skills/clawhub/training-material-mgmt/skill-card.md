## Use Case: <br>
Training operations, learning teams, and administrators use this skill to organize courseware and training materials across versioning, archival, review, copyright confirmation, distribution, and change tracking workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Archived courseware, reviewer notes, distribution logs, and copyright authorization records may contain sensitive or controlled information. <br>
Mitigation: Define access controls and retention rules before using the workflow with real training files or records. <br>
Risk: Materials may be distributed without confirmed copyright scope, authorization period, or usage restrictions. <br>
Mitigation: Require copyright ownership, authorization scope, validity dates, and usage restrictions to be recorded before distribution. <br>
