## Description: <br>
Helps agents guide SaaS pricing, packaging, monetization, value metric, tiering, pricing research, and price increase decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and operators use this skill to structure SaaS pricing decisions, including pricing metrics, tiers, price points, pricing research, price increases, and pricing page guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read local product marketing notes to tailor pricing advice. <br>
Mitigation: Review local product-marketing context before use and avoid including confidential data that is not needed for pricing guidance. <br>
Risk: Pricing recommendations can affect business revenue, conversion, and churn decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as strategic guidance for human review and validate material changes with customer research, business metrics, or controlled experiments. <br>
