## Description: <br>
Xiaoke is an all-department AI assistant for Haike Electronics that helps employees clarify requests and produce role-specific drafts, reports, analyses, tables, emails, and task guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees at Guangzhou Haike Electronics use this skill as a Chinese workplace assistant for department-specific daily work such as drafting documents, preparing reports, summarizing data, managing shortcuts, and generating daily work summaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to retain employee profiles, work context, memories, screenshots, scheduled reports, and shared organizational knowledge. <br>
Mitigation: Deploy only after confirming company policy for data access, memory retention, screenshot handling, automation, record deletion, and cross-department knowledge sharing. <br>
Risk: Daily reports and task summaries may include sensitive workplace activity or business data. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated summaries before sharing and restrict report access to approved employees and teams. <br>
