## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for loosely related Google, Workspace, CLI, or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review implicit invocation settings and overlap with other productivity or Google Workspace skills before deployment. <br>
Risk: As a documentation-only workflow helper, outputs can be incomplete or misleading when the user's context is underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Require visible assumptions, success criteria, validation notes, and human review before applying generated workflow or code guidance. <br>
