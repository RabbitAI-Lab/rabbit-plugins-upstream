## Description: <br>
Scheduling review of material at expanding intervals to optimize long-term retention. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Learners, educators, and knowledge workers use this skill for practical guidance on spaced repetition schedules, card design, review load, and when spaced repetition is or is not the right tool. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The scan evidence is clean, but the skill provides learning guidance that may not fit every subject, deadline, or learner constraint. <br>
Mitigation: Review the SKILL.md before use and apply its recommendations against the user's actual goals, time budget, and domain requirements. <br>
Risk: Users may over-apply spaced repetition to material that is better handled through practice, lookup, or short-term task support. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's own wrong-tool guidance to distinguish recall-heavy material from procedures, motor skills, daily work, and rapidly changing information. <br>
