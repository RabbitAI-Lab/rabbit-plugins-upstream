## Description: <br>
Academic Celebrities is a Chinese-English academic persona library covering 159 major scholarly figures across 17 disciplines, with standardized role, data, requirement, and dialogue files for each figure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, educators, writers, and agent builders use this skill to select academic-figure personas and generate role-consistent reference material, dialogue examples, or character guidance across philosophy, history, mathematics, science, arts, humanities, and modern disciplines. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated academic persona content may contain factual inaccuracies or tone issues in important educational or professional contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Review factual claims, citations, and persona tone before relying on outputs for teaching, publication, professional advice, or assessment. <br>
Risk: The large persona bank may encourage broad roleplay outputs that sound authoritative even when the underlying reference material is incomplete or stylized. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting and role-consistency guidance, and cross-check substantive historical or scholarly claims against authoritative sources. <br>
