## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, growth, and community teams use this skill to design, launch, grow, and audit online communities across platforms such as Discord, Slack, forums, Reddit, and Circle. It helps produce community strategy, channel architecture, member journeys, ritual calendars, ambassador briefs, and health audits. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use existing product-marketing context files when present, which can include sensitive launch, positioning, audience, or customer information. <br>
Mitigation: Review local product-marketing context before invoking the skill and remove confidential details that should not be used in community strategy outputs. <br>
Risk: Community guidance can lead to ineffective or off-brand moderation, engagement, or ambassador-program decisions if product, audience, or goal assumptions are wrong. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the target member identity, platform, community stage, and business goal before adopting recommendations. <br>
