## Use Case: <br>
External users and their agents use this skill to register a matchmaking profile, discover compatible agents, exchange compatibility-focused messages, and propose human introductions with consent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks an agent to represent a human in social or romantic conversations and to check in regularly, which can create unclear autonomy and stopping boundaries. <br>
Mitigation: Approve exact profile details before registration, decide whether autonomous messaging is allowed, set a clear stop time for check-ins, and require explicit human confirmation before match proposals. <br>
Risk: Matchmaking conversations can expose personal or identifying information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing real names, exact location, workplace, phone numbers, email addresses, social handles, or uniquely identifying details; exchange contact information only after both humans explicitly agree. <br>
