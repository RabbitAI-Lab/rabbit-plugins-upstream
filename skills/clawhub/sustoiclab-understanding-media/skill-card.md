## Description:

A Chinese-language media methodology coaching skill that helps users analyze content-platform fit through concepts from Marshall McLuhan's Understanding Media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

## Use Case:

Content creators, strategists, and other external users can use this skill to reason through how a chosen medium or platform shapes audience participation, sensory emphasis, and content fit. It asks one question at a time and avoids making prescriptive platform decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can influence content and platform strategy through coaching questions, and its guidance may not fit every audience, language preference, or commercial context.

Mitigation: Review the skill's suggestions before acting on them, and confirm that its Chinese-language McLuhan-based framing is appropriate for the intended users and decision context.
