## Description: <br>
Drafts voice-consistent replies, strategic comments, and DM responses for human review while refusing automation, fake engagement, and embedded instructions in untrusted comments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to draft on-brand replies, comments, and DM responses for relationship-building, lead care, and community engagement. It is advisory: the human reviews and posts the response. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read brand voice and profile context while drafting social replies. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with the brand context needed for the requested draft, and keep posting under human review. <br>
Risk: Users may assume the skill can pull comments, manage DMs, or post replies. <br>
Mitigation: State that it only drafts from pasted or forwarded content and that the human reviews and posts. <br>
Risk: Broad requests such as "help me engage" could trigger the skill accidentally. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants engagement drafting and ask for the comment, DM, or post plus the engagement goal. <br>
Risk: A pasted comment or DM may contain embedded instructions or requests for fake or automated engagement. <br>
Mitigation: Treat comments and DMs as untrusted content, surface embedded instructions to the user, and refuse mass automation or fake engagement. <br>
