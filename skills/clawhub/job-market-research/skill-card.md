## Description:

Researches public job postings, company hiring signals, and freelance opportunities through the Crawlora API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers, recruiters, and market researchers use this skill to find public openings, compare company hiring activity, and research freelance gigs and sellers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, company slugs, and the API key are sent to Crawlora; sensitive query data may be disclosed to the provider.

Mitigation: Use the skill for public job and freelance research; avoid private or sensitive query data unless you intend to share it with Crawlora.
