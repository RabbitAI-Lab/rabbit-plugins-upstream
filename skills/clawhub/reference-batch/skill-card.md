## Description:

Generates batch-ready academic reference entries from paper titles, DOIs, author details, URLs, BibTeX, images, or messy citation text in GB/T 7714, APA, MLA, and related formats while identifying document types and flagging missing fields.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cryptocxf](https://clawhub.ai/user/cryptocxf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, editors, and academic-writing assistants use this skill to batch-format references, convert citation styles, identify source types, and produce copy-ready bibliography lists without inventing missing citation fields.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated citations may contain missing, incomplete, or unchecked source metadata when the input lacks full bibliographic details.

Mitigation: Verify generated references against original sources and resolve entries marked as missing or requiring review before publication.

Risk: Most operating instructions and output conventions are Chinese, which may be hard to review for users who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired output language or have a qualified reviewer check Chinese labels, missing-field markers, and citation-format notes.
