## Description: <br>
Zhihu Writer helps agents turn Zhihu trend data into topic recommendations, competitive analysis, polished Zhihu answers, Yanyan short-story drafts, publishing guidance, and archive-ready Markdown. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cooperiano](https://clawhub.ai/user/cooperiano) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers and agents use this skill to monitor Zhihu trends, select topics aligned with AI, technology, or story niches, draft high-engagement Zhihu answers, create Yanyan short fiction outlines and drafts, and produce posting and archiving guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Archive steps may save generated drafts to a local notes path or filename the user did not intend. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the destination path and filename before saving any draft. <br>
Risk: Persona details, account context, or publishing advice may be unsuitable for the user's real public identity or posting goals. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit persona details and publishing recommendations before posting under the user's name. <br>
