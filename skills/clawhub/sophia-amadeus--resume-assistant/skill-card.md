## Description: <br>
AI skill to create, polish, customize, score, and export resumes with multi-format support and ATS optimization. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sophia-amadeus](https://clawhub.ai/user/sophia-amadeus) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Job seekers and career-support agents use this skill to draft, polish, tailor, score, and export resumes or CVs for target roles. It supports resume review workflows, ATS-oriented improvements, job-description matching, and multi-format resume outputs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Resume and job-description inputs can contain sensitive personal data. <br>
Mitigation: Ask users to provide only necessary details and avoid pasting information that is not needed for the requested resume task. <br>
Risk: Broad natural-language routing can make export or job-tailoring actions ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit commands or confirm the intended action, target job, and export format before generating tailored or exported resume content. <br>
Risk: Hosted or API deployments may transmit resumes or job descriptions outside the local chat context. <br>
Mitigation: Provide a clear privacy notice and obtain user consent before sending resume or job-description content to external services. <br>
