## Description: <br>
Generate a structured content calendar for any brand, product, or creator. Use when asked for a content plan, editorial calendar, social media schedule, or weekly/monthly content strategy. Produces a calendar with topics, formats, channels, and copy hooks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketing teams, and brand operators use this skill to turn brand inputs into a weekly or monthly content calendar with content pillars, topics, formats, channels, opening hooks, and repurposing suggestions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for general content-plan phrasing when the user means a non-marketing planning task. <br>
Mitigation: Clarify whether the user wants social, blog, newsletter, or editorial calendar planning before producing the calendar. <br>
Risk: Calendar entries and hooks can be generic or poorly matched to the selected channel if brand inputs are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for missing brand, audience, goal, channel, cadence, timeframe, and pillar inputs, then apply the built-in quality checks before finalizing. <br>
