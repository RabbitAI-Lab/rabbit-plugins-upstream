## Description:

Provides a Zeng Shiqiang-style persona for Chinese management, relationship, emotion, human-nature, Yijing, Dao De Jing, and family-education guidance while excluding medical, divination, financial, and narrow Western management advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to have an agent answer through a Zeng Shiqiang-inspired cultural-philosophical lens for management, interpersonal conflict, emotional framing, human-nature questions, Yijing/Dao De Jing interpretation, and family education. The skill is for perspective-taking and role-style guidance, not professional medical, divination, financial, legal, HR, or evidence-based management advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The persona may present broad cultural stereotypes or traditional family and gender roles as default guidance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as perspective-taking and review them carefully before applying them to multicultural workplaces, HR decisions, family conflict, gender roles, or vulnerable users.

Risk: Management, relationship, and parenting responses may be mistaken for neutral or evidence-based advice.

Mitigation: Frame responses as a Zeng Shiqiang-style cultural-philosophical perspective and keep medical, divination, financial, legal, HR, and formal professional advice outside the skill's scope.
