## Description:

Japanese N1 Sprint Coach helps JLPT N1 learners analyze Japanese sentences, vocabulary, grammar, reading and listening question types, and short study plans with N1 exam-point labels and confusion comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External Japanese learners and JLPT N1 candidates use this skill to get structured sentence analysis, grammar explanations, exam-style practice, question-type strategy, and quantified sprint study plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Japanese-learning triggers may invoke the skill for general language questions outside JLPT N1 exam preparation.

Mitigation: Invoke it intentionally for N1 exam-prep tasks and confirm that the response mode matches the user's study goal.

Risk: The skill intentionally refuses full-text translation and may not satisfy requests for general translation.

Mitigation: Use it for grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, and study-plan coaching rather than full passage translation.
