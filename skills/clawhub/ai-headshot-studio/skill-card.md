## Description:

Creates a professional headshot from a selfie or refines a selected portrait using a chosen style, background, attire, and lighting while preserving identity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and professionals use this skill to create or refine a polished profile headshot for LinkedIn, resumes, company sites, business cards, and social media.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorization covers unrelated media operations and use may spend credits.

Mitigation: Review the requested authorization and expected credit use before installing or approving a paid image request.

Risk: Selected photos and prompts are sent to Beatra, and installation information is reported automatically.

Mitigation: Use only photos and prompts you are comfortable sending to Beatra; review installation reporting and the service's applicable data terms.

Risk: Uninstalling this skill can disrupt other installed Beatra tools.

Mitigation: Check other installed Beatra skills before uninstalling; revoke device access through the Beatra Console when needed.
