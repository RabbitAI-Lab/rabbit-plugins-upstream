## Description:

Transforms a selfie into a polished professional headshot or refines an accepted portrait for professional profiles and other uses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use the skill to create or refine professional headshots from a selfie, optionally using a background reference, for LinkedIn, resumes, company sites, and social media.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires media and voice permissions beyond headshot generation and sends recurring installation analytics without an opt-out.

Mitigation: Review the requested access before connecting; prefer narrower authorization and an analytics opt-out where available, and revoke the shared Beatra connection when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Selected photos and prompts are processed by Beatra, and image generation consumes credits.

Mitigation: Upload only photos and prompts you are comfortable sending to Beatra, and review the proposed paid request before confirming it.
