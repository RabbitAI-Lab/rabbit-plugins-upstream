# Sources by Industry

## new-energy (default)

Trusted sources to consult:

- Renewables / general energy
  - Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/news/archive/renewableenergy
  - Bloomberg: https://www.bloomberg.com/energy
  - Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/companies/energy/
  - PV Magazine: https://www.pv-magazine.com/news/
  - Energy Storage News: https://www.energy-storage.news/
  - IEA: https://www.iea.org/news

Search keywords (English):
- `new energy`, `renewable energy`, `solar`, `wind`, `battery storage`, `EV`, `grid storage`, `hydrogen`, `carbon neutral`, `net zero`, `capacity auction`, `subsidy`, `tariff`

## General search tips

- Prefer sites with `lastModified` or explicit publish dates.
- Drop anything older than ~72 hours for a morning brief unless still unfolding.
- Deduplicate the same story across multiple outlets.
