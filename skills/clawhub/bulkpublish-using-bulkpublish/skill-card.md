## Description:

Helps agents draft, schedule, publish, and analyze social media posts across 15 networks through BulkPublish.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People managing BulkPublish-connected social accounts use this skill to prepare network-specific posts, schedule approved content, handle failed deliveries, and review engagement metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts may reach a live audience and cannot be withdrawn by deleting the post in BulkPublish.

Mitigation: Show drafts, destinations, and scheduled times for review; publish only after explicit approval.

Risk: Replies, moderation, or deletion may affect real interactions and may not be reversible.

Mitigation: Confirm each recipient, exact reply, or deletion individually before acting.
