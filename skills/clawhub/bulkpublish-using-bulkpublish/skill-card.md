## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts may reach live social accounts immediately or on a schedule.

Mitigation: Review channel-specific drafts and destinations before approving scheduling or publishing.

Risk: Deletions, replies, and comment moderation can affect public content or real recipients.

Mitigation: Confirm each recipient, reply, deletion, or moderation action before execution.

Risk: Local media uploads or API-key setup may expose files or credentials.

Mitigation: Review local upload paths and safeguard the API key before connecting an account.
