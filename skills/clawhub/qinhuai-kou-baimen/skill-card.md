## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Users can chat with a historical-character persona for casual companionship, reflection on relationships and decisions, or discussion of poetry and history.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Companion responses may be mistaken for professional mental-health or other safety-critical advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as conversation, not professional advice; seek qualified help for serious concerns.

Risk: Historical roleplay may blur fictional dialogue with historical fact.

Mitigation: Verify historical claims against reliable sources before relying on them.
