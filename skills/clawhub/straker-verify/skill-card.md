Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Selected text, files, and project metadata may be sent to Straker Verify for translation-related services. <br>
Mitigation: Submit only content approved for Straker processing, and avoid secrets, regulated data, or confidential customer documents unless your organization has approved that use. <br>
Risk: The skill requires a Straker Verify API key. <br>
Mitigation: Keep STRAKER_VERIFY_API_KEY protected and avoid exposing it in prompts, logs, or shared files. <br>
