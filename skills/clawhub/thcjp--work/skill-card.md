## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests command and write access that is not clearly needed for workplace advice.

Mitigation: Remove exec/write permissions or limit them to explicit, user-approved file, API, or command actions before installation.

Risk: Workplace guidance may be applied in sensitive employment or interpersonal situations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory drafts, review them for company policy and local context, and seek qualified support for legal, HR, or high-impact employment matters.
