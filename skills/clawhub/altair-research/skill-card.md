## Description:

Fetches published AI research on covered US stocks and ETFs, including model ratings, price targets, confidence, horizons, summaries, and disagreements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[altair-world](https://clawhub.ai/user/altair-world)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can request dated, model-by-model research views for covered US stocks, ETFs, and broad market topics. The skill summarizes published ratings and supporting analysis for informational use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries are sent to Altair, and AI-generated reports may be outdated or incorrect.

Mitigation: Use the default endpoint unless an alternative deployment is trusted, check report dates, and treat results as informational research rather than investment advice.
