## Description: <br>
Bid Clarification Advisor helps bidders assess clarification requests, avoid impermissible substantive changes, and draft compliant response and recordkeeping packages. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External bidders, procurement teams, and bid-response advisors use this skill to analyze clarification letters, identify unlawful or risky requests, and prepare bounded response drafts with retention and challenge-path checklists. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: AI-assisted procurement and legal analysis can be incorrect or outdated when project documents, cited laws, deadlines, or local rules are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited laws, deadlines, and local rules against current official sources and project documents before relying on the output. <br>
Risk: Using a generated clarification response without review could introduce substantive changes, new commitments, or unsupported allegations that harm a bid. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer compare each draft against the submitted bid and remove any price change, new evidence, new commitment, or unverified legal conclusion. <br>
