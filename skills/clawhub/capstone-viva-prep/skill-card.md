## Description:

A project-specific practice skill for engineering undergraduates preparing for capstone vivas or defenses, with six question categories, follow-up chains, committee simulations, scoring, and honest answer frames.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Engineering students use this skill to rehearse defense questions based on their actual project and report, including technical fundamentals, evidence for reported results, and likely follow-ups. It provides question prompts, practice structure, and feedback while leaving factual answers to the student.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may disclose project material they are not permitted to share or overlook their course's rules for AI-assisted preparation.

Mitigation: Share only permitted project material and check the course's AI-use and disclosure rules before preparing.
