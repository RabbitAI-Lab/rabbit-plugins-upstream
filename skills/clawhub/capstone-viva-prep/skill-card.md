## Description:

Prepares engineering students for capstone vivas with project-specific questions, follow-up drills, committee simulations, scoring, and honest answer guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External engineering students use the skill to rehearse a capstone viva or defense using their own project topic, report, and measurements. It helps them practice follow-up questions and prepare concise, evidence-based answers without supplying findings on their behalf.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Practice prompts or answer frames could be mistaken for verified project results.

Mitigation: Base answers on the student's own work and measurements; identify untested areas honestly and do not invent data or findings.

Risk: Sharing project material with an AI agent could conflict with university rules or confidentiality limits.

Mitigation: Share only material the student is allowed to provide, follow university AI-use rules, and check supervisor guidance for restricted projects.
