## Description:

Creates original brand jingles, advertising songs, and audio-logo stings from a brand brief, with style sketches and derived cuts for campaign use.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and creators use the skill to turn a brand brief into original jingles, campaign songs, or short sonic logos, then review style sketches before requesting derived lengths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports broad shared permissions.

Mitigation: Review the requested permissions against the intended jingle task before authorizing.

Risk: The security review reports recurring installation-linked data reporting without an opt-out.

Mitigation: Review the reporting behavior in the updates and security reference before installing.

Risk: The security review reports a possible uninstall disruption to other skills.

Mitigation: Review the uninstall and disconnect instructions and consider other installed skills before removal.
