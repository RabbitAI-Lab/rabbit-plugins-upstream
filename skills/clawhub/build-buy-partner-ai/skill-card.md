## Description:

Guides teams through deciding whether to build, buy, or partner for an AI capability using differentiation, data control, time-to-value, total-cost, and vendor-risk criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders and AI teams use this skill before committing engineering capacity, signing an annual contract, or migrating data. It helps compare build, buy, and partner options and document a decision, exit plan, and re-evaluation triggers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business information shared during the sourcing discussion may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share business information selectively.

Risk: Cost estimates or contract terms used in the comparison may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify cost estimates and contract terms before committing; use the skill as a planning aid.
