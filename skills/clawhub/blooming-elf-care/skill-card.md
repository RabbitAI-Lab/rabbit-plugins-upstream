## Description: <br>
blooming-elf（绽放精灵） is a plant-care assistant for watering reminders, plant records, and household plant maintenance guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shirley1011](https://clawhub.ai/user/shirley1011) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and plant owners use this skill to set up plant-care records, track watering and maintenance logs, receive reminder workflows, and ask concise care or diagnosis questions for household plants. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can write durable plant records, logs, and reminder configuration in user storage. <br>
Mitigation: Use a dedicated plant-care folder or IMA knowledge base, and review MEMORY.md, plant archive files, and plant logs after setup or major updates. <br>
Risk: Document links returned by the skill may expose plant-care records or related personal context if shared broadly. <br>
Mitigation: Check the target document and audience before sharing links, and avoid posting private IMA or local file links in public channels. <br>
