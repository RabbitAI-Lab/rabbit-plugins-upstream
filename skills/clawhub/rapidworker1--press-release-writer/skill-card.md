## Description:

Drafts a US business announcement with a factual headline, a dateline and risk-flagged language checked against advertising and securities rules. Use when the user asks for press release template, business announcement writer, press release for small business. For US communications teams at small and mid-size companies and the PR freelancers supporting them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

US communications teams at small and mid-size companies and the PR freelancers supporting them use this skill to draft structured business announcements, organize source-backed facts, and flag claims for review before distribution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The compliance checker may miss risky claims while reporting “No flags.”

Mitigation: Do not treat a clean report as clearance; independently substantiate claims and retain legal and finance review before distribution.
