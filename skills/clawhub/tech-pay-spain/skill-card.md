## Description:

Answer what jobs pay in Spain (salario, sueldo, cuánto se cobra) by role, region, years of experience or company, from medians of real Spanish salaries. Use for salary questions, offer checks, negotiation prep, finding open positions and applying to jobs in Spain.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Candidates and job seekers can use the skill to compare reported technology salaries in Spain, assess offers, prepare for negotiation, find relevant openings, and get help with applications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Spain; some remote job listings are open to Spain or Europe.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill contacts an external salary and job service and may ask whether the user wants to star a GitHub repository.

Mitigation: Review the external service endpoint and decide whether to use it; treat the repository-star request as optional.
