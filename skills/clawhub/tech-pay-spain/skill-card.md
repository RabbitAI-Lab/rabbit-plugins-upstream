## Description:

Answer what jobs pay in Spain (salario, sueldo, cuánto se cobra) by role, region, years of experience or company, from medians of real Spanish salaries. Use for salary questions, offer checks, negotiation prep, finding open positions and applying to jobs in Spain.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Job seekers and people evaluating compensation in Spain use this skill to compare salary medians by role, region, experience, or company, check offers, prepare negotiations, and find relevant openings. It can also help prepare job applications.
