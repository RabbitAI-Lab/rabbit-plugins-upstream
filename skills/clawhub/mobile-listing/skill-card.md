## Description:

Uses product detail and review evidence to assess Amazon Listing readability, information order, and comprehension barriers on mobile.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and listing operators use this skill to review how clearly a product page communicates on mobile and to identify evidence-based changes to test. Its analysis draws on product details and review evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
