## Description:

Applies a Sima Guang-inspired historical perspective to questions about governance, people, decisions, business, creative work, and other cross-domain problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking a historically informed perspective can use this skill to examine decisions, governance, relationships, and strategy, or to draft plans and creative writing. Its analogies and recommendations are not substitutes for professional advice or independent verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A broad, persistent persona may steer ordinary conversations beyond the requested analysis.

Mitigation: Enable it only when this perspective is wanted; review or narrow broad triggers and identity rules.

Risk: Local learning writes may persist details from conversations across sessions.

Mitigation: Disable or review learning writes before discussing sensitive information, and inspect retained learning logs.

Risk: Automatic or background maintenance may run without clear user direction.

Mitigation: Disable automatic maintenance workflows and require explicit approval before running maintenance scripts.
