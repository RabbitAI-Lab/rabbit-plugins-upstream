## Use Case:

People and teams use this skill to examine decisions, historical parallels, strategy, creative projects, and competing explanations, with explicit uncertainty and human review for consequential conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation and local learning notes may capture sensitive conversations or update local knowledge files.

Mitigation: Review or disable learning and logging workflows before sharing private, workplace, legal, medical, financial, relationship, or image content.

Risk: Photo-based person analysis and lie-detection claims may be unreliable or unfair.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as speculative; do not use them to decide hiring, legal, credit, medical, or interpersonal trust outcomes.

Risk: Cross-domain advice may be mistaken for professional or verified guidance.

Mitigation: Check sources and seek qualified human review before consequential legal, medical, or financial decisions.
