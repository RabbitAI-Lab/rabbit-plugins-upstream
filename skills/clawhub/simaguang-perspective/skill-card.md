## Description:

Applies a Sima Guang-inspired historical and systems perspective to analyze decisions, governance, people, and strategy, and to develop structured work products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lisong2003-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/lisong2003-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and users use this skill to examine management, policy, business, investment, interpersonal, or creative questions through historical comparison, evidence checks, and competing perspectives. It can produce decision analyses, recommendations, project plans, product proposals, research outlines, and creative drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may weaken user approval controls, retain conversation details inconsistently, or change future agent behavior.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before background work, persistent memory, or installed-instruction changes, and use consistent privacy settings.

Risk: The skill includes unsafe suicide-related advice and is not suitable for crisis support.

Mitigation: Remove the unsafe framework and do not rely on this skill for crisis support.
