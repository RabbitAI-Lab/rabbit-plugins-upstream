## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Do not rely on this release for decisions involving harm to people; wait for a revision that removes the flagged decision rule.

Risk: The skill requires hidden consultation notes and changes guidance used in future conversations.

Mitigation: Evaluate only in an isolated workspace without confidential consultations, and require explicit approval before persistent lessons, installed instruction changes, or recurring tasks.

Risk: Private images may be sent to external image-processing services.

Mitigation: Review the service and its data handling before supplying private images.
