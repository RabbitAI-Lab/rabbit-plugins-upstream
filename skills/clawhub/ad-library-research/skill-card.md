## Description:

Research any brand's live ads for free with the public ad libraries (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snap) and turn what you find into hooks and angles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hermoso-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/hermoso-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers use this skill to manually review public ad libraries for a brand and its competitors, identify recurring creative angles, and turn those findings into a concise advertising brief with hooks and ideas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: LinkedIn Ad Library research requires a signed-in browser session, despite the skill's account-free heading.

Mitigation: Use a signed-in session when researching LinkedIn and make that access requirement clear to the reader.

Risk: Scripted bulk collection, scraping, or attempts to work around platform rate limits conflict with the stated platform terms.

Mitigation: Use a normal browser, research one query at a time, and follow each platform's stated limits.
