## Description:

Research any brand's live ads for free with the public ad libraries - Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snap - and turn what you find into hooks and angles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hermoso-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/hermoso-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, growth teams, and creative strategists use this skill to manually research public ad libraries, compare brand and competitor ads, and convert observed patterns into hooks, angles, and creative briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill as a basis for scripted scraping, bulk collection, or rate-limit workarounds may violate platform terms.

Mitigation: Use normal browser access one query at a time, and use a licensed research tool for automated collection.
