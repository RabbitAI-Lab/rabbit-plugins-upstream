## Description: <br>
公文润色 helps polish Chinese official documents, speeches, reports, cases, letters, and similar drafts by diagnosing intent, improving structure, refining headings, and raising formal expression quality. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Chinese-speaking staff, writers, and agents use this skill to improve existing official-document drafts for public-sector or enterprise contexts. It is best suited for intent-driven editing that restructures logic, strengthens formal wording, offers scenario-specific versions, and explains key revisions without inventing hard data, real names, or roles. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generic editing requests may receive a formal official-document style that is not appropriate for ordinary prose. <br>
Mitigation: Use this skill for Chinese official-document drafts; choose a general editing skill for ordinary prose. <br>
Risk: Generated official text may include unsuitable tone, unsupported policy-style phrasing, or factual implications if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review factual accuracy, tone, and added policy-style wording before use, and verify all hard data, names, and roles. <br>
Risk: Speech or address placeholders may be mistaken for real names or titles. <br>
Mitigation: Replace placeholders only with verified values or leave them clearly marked as placeholders. <br>
