## Description:

Analyzes video of a pet left alone, reports possible separation-anxiety behaviors, and suggests comfort strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners and boarding staff can submit a recording of a pet alone to receive a behavior summary and suggested comfort strategies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan flags credential-handling and privacy concerns around the skill.

Mitigation: Before installing, verify that its account and credential handling is necessary and trusted, and confirm where video and credentials are sent.

Risk: ClawScan reports that the visible functions do not clearly match the advertised pet-monitoring and comfort actions.

Mitigation: Confirm that the advertised monitoring and comfort behavior is actually provided before relying on it.
