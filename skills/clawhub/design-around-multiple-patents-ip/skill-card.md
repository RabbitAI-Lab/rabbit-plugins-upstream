## Description:

Develops and screens engineering design-around concepts against two or more potentially relevant patent rights using an application-driven eight-step freedom-to-operate workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, engineers, and IP review teams use this skill to organize multi-patent freedom-to-operate screening, claim mapping, candidate design-space analysis, repair iterations, and attorney-review packages. It supports evidence-backed engineering analysis but does not provide legal clearance or a legal opinion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live patent searches may disclose confidential technical or business information to configured patent research services.

Mitigation: Use authorized connectors only, apply appropriate confidentiality controls, and minimize or redact sensitive inputs before external searches.

Risk: Freedom-to-operate screening outputs could be mistaken for legal clearance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as evidence-backed analysis for review and require qualified counsel to confirm claim construction, status, enforceability, equivalents, and any formal FTO conclusion.
