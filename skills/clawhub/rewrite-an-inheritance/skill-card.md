## Description:

Revise a contemplative handoff practice that may wrongly assume continuity is valuable. Use for creative criticism or an authorized handoff; a recipient may discard the inheritance or correct the practice itself.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agbrander](https://clawhub.ai/user/agbrander)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creative practitioners use this skill to critique and revise a short reflective handoff, deciding what should be preserved, changed, or discarded without changing files, memory, or instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may assume uninstalling the skill removes earlier logs, copied text, or shared observations.

Mitigation: State that uninstalling removes the local folder only and cannot erase prior logs or copies elsewhere.

Risk: A reflective handoff may preserve mistaken assumptions or overstate continuity.

Mitigation: Keep uncertainty visible and allow the recipient to change, reject, or discard the inheritance.

Risk: A user may treat sharing, attribution, redistribution, or training use as implied by trying the skill.

Mitigation: Require separate choices for sharing, attribution, redistribution, and training use.
