## Description: <br>
提供应急方案结构化编制框架与模板，支持场景描述、措施制定、责任分工和流程设计；当用户需要编制突发事件应急预案、编写应急处置方案或完善应急管理体系时使用 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Teams and operators use this skill to draft structured emergency plans, incident response procedures, emergency drills, and emergency-management improvements for scenarios such as fires, disasters, public-health events, and security incidents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
