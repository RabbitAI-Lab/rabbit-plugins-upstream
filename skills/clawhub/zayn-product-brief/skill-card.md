## Description: <br>
基于型号、描述、截图和已有资料，快速整理产品用途、关键规格、版本差异、采购关注点和信息缺口。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, sourcing, and engineering teams use this skill to turn customer-provided product models, descriptions, screenshots, and available records into a concise product brief with confirmed facts, version differences, purchasing considerations, information gaps, and follow-up questions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process business or product records such as historical quotes or order records when the user includes them. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only relevant, approved materials and omit records that are not needed for the product brief. <br>
Risk: Unverified product details could be carried into downstream sales, sourcing, or engineering workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review uncertain fields, information gaps, and suggested follow-up questions before using the output downstream. <br>
