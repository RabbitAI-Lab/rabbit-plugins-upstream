## Description: <br>
面向服务器、存储、内存、硬盘、SSD、GPU、网络设备和笔记本配件等 IT 硬件，基于型号、PN、标签、截图、Datasheet 和已有资料整理用途、关键规格、版本差异、兼容与采购关注点；当用户需要通用产品或服务速览时改用 zayn-general-product-brief。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, procurement, and technical support users use this skill to turn customer-provided IT hardware identifiers, screenshots, datasheets, and existing records into cautious product briefs. It focuses on product identification, confirmed specifications, version differences, procurement concerns, information gaps, and follow-up questions before RFQ, quote, or compatibility workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat draft product identification or specifications as final purchasing guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Require human confirmation for compatibility, inventory, pricing, warranty, exact part number, condition, packaging, and purchasing decisions before acting. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or conflicting source materials can produce an unreliable product brief. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve conflicts, mark unverified information clearly, prefer original datasheets and official pages, and ask for the smallest missing confirmation needed to proceed. <br>
Risk: Unnecessary sensitive business records may be included in product-analysis prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the product identifiers, screenshots, datasheet excerpts, and operational context needed for the brief. <br>
