知识产权成果统计工作流程助手。结合智慧芽MCP工具，引导科技管理部门从数据采集、核验、分类、评级到汇总上报的全流程操作，输出台账、质量分级简报与上报材料。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Technology management leads, IP specialists, and R&D managers use this skill to collect, verify, classify, rate, summarize, report, and archive intellectual-property portfolio statistics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports and external submissions may include business-sensitive patent portfolio information.

Mitigation: Review reports before sharing externally and remove or mask confidential business details.

Risk: The skill depends on Zhihuiya MCP patent tools; without access and authorization it can only provide process guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm account authorization and tool availability before relying on database-backed results.

Risk: Patent database results and internal ledgers may disagree.

Mitigation: Use the skill's staged confirmation and source-labeling workflow to review differences before updating records.
