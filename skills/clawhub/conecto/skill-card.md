## Description:

Connect an OpenClaw agent to Conecto's paid MCP service for CRM, email, phone, invoices and bookkeeping, bookings, e-signatures, and help desk tools, with usage charged per tool call from a prepaid balance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[conecto](https://clawhub.ai/user/conecto)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Small business owners and their OpenClaw agents use Conecto tools to manage CRM records, email, phone, invoicing and bookkeeping, bookings, e-signatures, support tickets, and related business workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; phone number support is described for the US and Canada.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tool calls connect to Conecto's hosted service and can incur charges from the business's prepaid balance.

Mitigation: Review Conecto's data handling and tool prices, monitor the balance and budgets, and follow the skill's confirmation guidance for consequential or higher-cost actions.
