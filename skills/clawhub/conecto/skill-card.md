## Description:

Business tools for your OpenClaw agent through one MCP server: a CRM, email inboxes, phone numbers it answers, invoices and bookkeeping, a booking calendar, e-signatures and a help desk. Conecto is a paid service, charged per tool call from a prepaid balance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Small business owners and their agents use Conecto to access CRM, email, phone, invoicing, bookkeeping, scheduling, e-signature, help desk, and related business tools through one MCP connection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The benign scan noted expected external transmission to Conecto; connected tools can also perform paid or customer-facing actions.

Mitigation: Review each tool's scope and price. Ask before sending messages to people outside the business, starting a monthly rental, or making a call that costs more than $1 unless specifically requested.
