## Description:

为短剧人物、造型、地点、道具和状态编写或修改可直接复制的图片提示词 Markdown。用户提到角色设定图、三视图、参考图、场景板、道具图、风格帧、Look Development、状态变体或局部编辑提示词时使用；不生成图片，也不调用供应商。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

短剧创作者可用此技能为人物、造型、地点、道具和局部编辑编写、修订可复用的图片提示词，并将其整理到项目 Markdown 文档中。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompt wording or revisions may diverge from accepted visual facts and continuity constraints.

Mitigation: Review proposed wording and revisions against the project's accepted visual settings before use.

Risk: Prompt text could be mistaken for a generated or approved image.

Mitigation: Treat the output as text only; image generation requires the separate production skill and explicit confirmation.
