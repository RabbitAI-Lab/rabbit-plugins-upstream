## Description:

Finds and prioritizes industry reports, market studies, and white papers, delivering a sourced reading list with inspected key points, dates, links, and clear access labels retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and other report readers use this skill to find relevant industry publications, compare their usefulness, and distinguish inspected summaries from accessible full reports. It requires authorized browsing and JishuDB for retaining the reading list and source records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The required JishuDB setup may install or repair local software and change application or connector configuration.

Mitigation: Review the proposed setup and authorize material changes; pause for required operating-system, trust, or OAuth prompts. Decline setup if these changes are unacceptable.

Risk: Restricted or unread reports could be misrepresented as inspected full text or freely available PDFs.

Mitigation: Use authorized sources only and label each item by the material actually inspected and its access limits.
