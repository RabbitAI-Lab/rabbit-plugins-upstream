## Description: <br>
Helps agent users and skill authors create practical ontology-style typed workflows for bug fixes, setup hardening, reliability improvements, and adjacent workflow design. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn broad ontology-style workflow needs into actionable plans, checklists, code or configuration guidance, and verification notes. It is aimed at practical local-hardware-friendly support for workflow reliability, setup hardening, and related troubleshooting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger on general productivity, workflow, graph, knowledge, or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for ontology-style or structured workflow help before applying the template. <br>
