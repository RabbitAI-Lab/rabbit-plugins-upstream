## Description:

Analyzes submitted child-focused video for visible positive-emotion moments and returns structured findings and report links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and childcare providers use the skill to identify and preserve children’s visible happy moments from video and review the resulting event reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Children’s video may be uploaded even when the no-analysis option is selected.

Mitigation: Defer use with real children’s footage until informed approval is required and the no-analysis behavior is fixed.

Risk: The skill may create remote accounts without clear approval, and provider privacy guarantees could not be verified.

Mitigation: Require informed approval for account creation and confirm the provider’s retention, deletion, access, and billing terms before use.

Risk: Video may include children and other people who have not consented to recording.

Mitigation: Obtain consent from guardians and everyone recorded; send credential-bearing requests only to trusted HTTPS destinations.
