## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance and local calculators for non-performing loan valuation, due diligence, bid limits, recovery modeling, and disposal strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Investors and asset-recovery professionals use this Chinese-language reference to assess non-performing loan portfolios, compare disposal options, prepare due-diligence checklists, and estimate bid ceilings from recovery assumptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Illustrative calculator estimates may misstate value or bid ceilings when inputs or assumptions are inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify asset details and model parameters independently; review sensitivity before relying on an estimate.

Risk: Outdated or inapplicable legal and market assumptions could mislead real investment decisions.

Mitigation: Confirm current local legal and market facts and obtain professional investment, legal, and valuation review before placing bids.
