## Description: <br>
Applies prioritisation frameworks such as RICE, MoSCoW, Kano, ICE, and Opportunity Scoring to rank features and backlog items with rationale, recommended build order, deprioritised items, and assumptions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, founders, and delivery teams use this skill to choose a prioritisation framework, score backlog items against a stated goal or metric, and produce a defensible build order with documented cuts and assumptions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill references a scoring helper script that is not present in the artifact, so command examples may fail if used as written. <br>
Mitigation: Check available files before running commands; use the framework instructions directly or provide an equivalent reviewed script. <br>
Risk: Prioritisation results can be misleading when estimates, goals, or stakeholder overrides are undocumented. <br>
Mitigation: Anchor each scoring session to one metric and document assumptions, cut reasons, and overrides before acting on recommendations. <br>
