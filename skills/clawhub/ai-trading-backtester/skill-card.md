## Description:

An AI-powered assistant for designing, coding, and evaluating quantitative strategies across historical A-share, Hong Kong, and US equity market data, covering mean reversion, momentum, breakout, pairs trading, and machine learning approaches for quantitative analysts and retail traders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Quantitative analysts and retail traders use the skill to turn a strategy idea into Python backtest scaffolding and structured performance reviews for A-share, Hong Kong, and US equities. It emphasizes market-specific assumptions, costs, execution constraints, and validation against overfitting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Backtests can overstate performance when timing, costs, market trading limits, liquidity, or point-in-time data are modeled incorrectly.

Mitigation: State the market and data assumptions, model realistic execution constraints and costs, and check results out of sample.

Risk: Generated strategy code and historical results could be mistaken for trading advice or a prediction of future returns.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research scaffolding, review and run code in your own environment, and do not use it as a buy or sell recommendation.

Risk: Some local references could not be resolved, so security review coverage is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the referenced examples and source material before relying on the skill's outputs.

Risk: Understanding the explanations and conclusions may require Chinese.

Mitigation: Use a Chinese-capable reviewer when the strategy rationale or conclusions need to be relied on.
