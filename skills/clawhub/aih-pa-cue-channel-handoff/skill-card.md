## Description:

Helps users hand off tasks across channels and devices using scoped inputs, reviewable deliverables, and authorized tools when available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to turn requests from different channels into traceable task handoffs, device work packages, or a unified inbox. It distinguishes draft plans from completed actions and calls for separate authorization before external actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cross-channel handoffs or uploads may expose private messages or identity information.

Mitigation: Share only task-relevant, authorized details; redact sensitive material and never put passwords, tokens, codes, or full identity documents in task records.

Risk: External actions or AI-HIVE usage may incur charges or change data without approval.

Mitigation: Confirm current tools, upload scope, prices, and budget before use; obtain separate authorization for sending, paying, deleting, or publishing.

Risk: A draft or unverified receipt may be mistaken for completed work.

Mitigation: Check real outputs and receipts before marking tasks accepted; state clearly when tools, access, or verification are unavailable.
