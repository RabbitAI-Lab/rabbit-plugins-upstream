# Scorecard Metrics, Formulas, and Cost Checklist

## Full-cost checklist (the denominator)

Include every line before computing any efficiency metric:

- Creator fee (per deliverable or package)
- Product cost (COGS of gifted/seeded units, incl. multiples)
- Shipping / handling for seeding
- Usage / whitelisting / ads rights fees
- Agency or platform commission (often 15-25%)
- Discount cost of creator-specific codes (margin impact of the coupon itself)
- Internal handling estimate if material (briefing, review cycles)

## Core scorecard

| Metric | Formula | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Engaged-view CPM | Full cost ÷ (engaged views ÷ 1000) | Engaged view = platform's qualified view (e.g., 15s+ or 50%+ watch); state definition |
| ER vs own baseline | Campaign ER ÷ creator's 90-day median ER | <0.7 = brand content underperforms their organic; >1.1 = strong fit |
| CTR | Clicks ÷ impressions | Only meaningful with proper links/stickers |
| CVR | Orders ÷ clicks | Compare to site's paid-social CVR |
| Revenue per dollar (RPD) | Tracked revenue ÷ full cost | The headline number |
| Margin-adjusted RPD | (Tracked revenue × gross margin) ÷ full cost | >1.0 = directly profitable; 0.8-1.0 often acceptable with halo/library value |
| Creator CAC | Full cost ÷ new customers | Compare to blended and paid CAC |
| AVD% (video) | Avg view duration ÷ video length | Content quality signal, not a money signal |

## Attribution hierarchy (best → worst)

1. Dedicated discount codes + dedicated links (UTM) per creator per post
2. Platform-native attribution (TikTok Shop, IG Shopping) with stated window
3. Post-purchase survey ("How did you hear about us?") weighted
4. Geo/time-based lift analysis
5. "Sales rose that week" — do not use as evidence, only as a hypothesis

Always state which level the audit uses. Mixed levels across creators = flag comparability.

## Benchmark guidance

Use in this order — creator's own history > category norms > platform-wide averages:

- Engagement rate norms differ by follower size: nano (<10K) and micro (10-100K) accounts typically run meaningfully higher ER than mid/macro accounts on every platform. A mid-tier account at 4-5% ER on TikTok/Reels is generally healthy; ~1% on a 500K-follower account is a red flag on short-video platforms but can be normal on Instagram feed.
- Halo effects are real but must be bounded: if tracked revenue is X, total attributable revenue is rarely >2X. Never justify a creator purely on unmeasurable halo.
- Content library value: price usage rights at what an equivalent UGC/studio asset would cost you (typical $150-500 per usable asset) and add it to the value side explicitly.

## Sample-size rules

- Minimum for a confident verdict: 3 posts or 2 campaigns or 90 days
- 1-2 posts: verdict allowed but marked low-confidence, with a required follow-up test
- Exclude the single best and worst post when a creator has 6+ posts (trimmed view) and check whether the verdict changes
