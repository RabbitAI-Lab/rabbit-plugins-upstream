# Four-Axis Scoring, Authenticity Checks, and Verdict Thresholds

## The four axes

Score each 1-5. Weight by business goal (default weights in parentheses).

### 1. Reach quality (20%)

Not "how many" but "how many of the right people": audience geo/demo/interest match to buyer profile, engaged-view share, follower overlap with existing customers if measurable.

### 2. Engagement authenticity (20%)

Is the engagement real and from humans who could buy?

- Follower growth curve: organic growth is noisy but continuous; single-day spikes of >5% of total followers suggest purchases or giveaways
- ER-to-follower-size sanity: unusually high ER + generic comments = pods/bots
- Comment sampling: read 30-50 comments across 3 posts. Real: questions, objections, tags with context. Fake: emoji walls, repeated phrasing, brand-new accounts
- Like-to-comment ratio wildly off category norms

### 3. Conversion contribution (35%)

Tracked revenue, RPD, margin-adjusted RPD, creator CAC vs paid CAC, assisted conversions if measurable. The heaviest axis because it's the point.

### 4. Cost efficiency (25%)

Engaged-view CPM vs your paid media CPM, fee vs category rate cards, trend of the creator's pricing vs delivered performance over time.

## Verdict mapping

| Verdict | Typical profile | Threshold guidance |
|---|---|---|
| SCALE | Margin-adj RPD ≥ 1.2 AND authenticity pass AND sample ≥ 3 posts | Lock longer package before rates rise; add usage rights |
| KEEP | Margin-adj RPD 0.8-1.2, or strong axis mix with fixable weak spot | Renew same terms; fix the brief/format issue; re-audit next cycle |
| RENEGOTIATE | Content performs (ER, AVD, CVR healthy) but price kills the math | Counter with breakeven-derived rate or same fee + rights/coupon push |
| DROP | Margin-adj RPD < 0.5 with no fixable driver, OR authenticity fail | Non-renew; reallocate; document why for future vetting |

Overrides:

- Authenticity fail → DROP regardless of other scores (the data can't be trusted)
- Thin sample (≤2 posts) → cap at KEEP/RENEGOTIATE with a mandated test, never SCALE or DROP on one post unless authenticity failed
- Strategic value (exclusivity vs competitor, category authority) may bump one level — must be stated explicitly, not implied

## Diagnosing before dropping

When conversion is weak, check in order — many "bad creators" are bad briefs:

1. **Format:** did demo/tutorial posts convert while lifestyle posts didn't?
2. **Offer:** was there a working code/link with real incentive?
3. **Timing:** posted during a stockout, price change, or site issue?
4. **Audience:** right people but wrong product tier (price mismatch)?
5. **Frequency:** single mention vs sustained presence — creators often convert on the 2nd-3rd exposure.

If any of 1-5 explains the miss, the verdict is KEEP (new brief) or RENEGOTIATE, not DROP.

## Pre-commitment vetting (mini-check for new creators)

Before first deals, run axes 1-2 only: audience match, growth curve, comment sampling, plus one paid test post with tracked link. Never sign multi-post packages on media-kit numbers.
