## Description: <br>
Audits creator and influencer campaign performance for commerce teams, scoring reach quality, engagement authenticity, conversion contribution, and cost efficiency to produce evidence-backed keep, scale, renegotiate, or drop decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Commerce, growth, and creator marketing teams use this skill to audit creators with campaign history, normalize cross-platform performance, check authenticity and full cost, and decide whether to renew, scale, renegotiate, or drop each creator. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Creator campaign inputs can include sensitive commercial data or unnecessary personal data. <br>
Mitigation: Use aggregated campaign metrics where possible and avoid providing credentials, private customer records, or personal data that is not needed for the audit. <br>
Risk: Weak attribution, thin samples, or vanity metrics can lead to misleading creator renewal decisions. <br>
Mitigation: State the attribution method and sample window, use full-cost and margin-adjusted metrics, flag low-confidence cases, and require evidence for every verdict. <br>
