## Description: <br>
Helps founders and builders turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning through repeatable workflows, checklists, lightweight analysis, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns validation needs into practical plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analyses, code support, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill during general product, SaaS, startup, prototype, or validation conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for product validation work or adjust invocation settings when tighter routing is preferred. <br>
