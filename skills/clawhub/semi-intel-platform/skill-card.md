## Description:

SemiWatch Pro helps semiconductor equipment teams generate source-grounded intelligence dashboards and briefs using PatSnap/Zhihuiya patent data across patent competition, supply-chain risk, technology gaps, policy controls, and competitor analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External developers, analysts, and semiconductor business teams use this skill to turn user-provided materials, search conditions, and PatSnap/Zhihuiya patent data into reviewable semiconductor intelligence reports, dashboards, and next-step recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP data or uploaded business materials may be restricted by an organization's data-sharing policies.

Mitigation: Confirm permitted data use before installation or analysis, and avoid uploading materials that the organization has not approved for the connected tools.

Risk: Generated FTO, infringement, validity, or other patent-law discussion may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat legal-facing content as research support only and route it to qualified counsel before relying on it for decisions.

Risk: Patent, market, or technical conclusions can be misleading if source coverage, search scope, dates, or uncertainty are omitted.

Mitigation: Require reports to preserve sources, search ranges, time windows, key evidence, and uncertainty notes for human review.
