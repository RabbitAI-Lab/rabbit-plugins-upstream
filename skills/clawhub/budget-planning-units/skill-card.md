## Description:

Combines campaign budget forecasting with a scenario planning ledger for routine planning work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing operations and finance stakeholders use this skill to forecast lightweight campaign budget scenario amounts and record selected amounts in a scenario planning ledger.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Forecasted or recorded campaign budget amounts may be inaccurate or unsuitable for finance decisions.

Mitigation: Review scenario amounts and ledger entries for accuracy before relying on them for planning or finance decisions.
