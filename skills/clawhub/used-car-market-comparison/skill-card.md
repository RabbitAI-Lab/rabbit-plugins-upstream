## Description:

Compare used-car asking prices with matched local listings through Crawlora to build a vehicle shortlist or explain a listing's premium or discount.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Buyers and their agents compare a used-car listing with matched local advertisements, summarize asking-price ranges, and identify uncertainties and questions for the seller.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vehicle search details and ZIP codes are sent to Crawlora using an API key.

Mitigation: Use a protected API key and search only within the buyer's stated scope.

Risk: Advertised prices and listing descriptions may be mistaken for a final valuation or verified vehicle condition.

Mitigation: Present results as a dated asking-price sample, disclose coverage and fee or history gaps, and recommend independent vehicle checks.
