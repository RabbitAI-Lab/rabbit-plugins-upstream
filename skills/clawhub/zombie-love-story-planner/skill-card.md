## Description:

Plans original, gentle zombie-reunion stories as text story briefs, five-beat storyboards, prompts, and rights-and-consent checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gpt-img-2](https://clawhub.ai/user/gpt-img-2)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People planning an original, non-graphic zombie-reunion story for a partner, friend, family member, or pet use this skill to draft a story brief and storyboard while checking photo rights and consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference photos or personal details may be used without permission or shared unnecessarily.

Mitigation: Confirm photo rights and each person's consent; avoid sharing private photos or sensitive details unless comfortable doing so.

Risk: Enabling the optional MCP runs external code through npx.

Mitigation: Review and trust the GitHub-sourced package before enabling the optional MCP; the text-planning workflow works without it.
