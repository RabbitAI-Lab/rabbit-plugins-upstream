## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users and skill authors create practical self-improving and proactive workflow support, including bug-fixing plans, setup hardening checklists, reliability improvements, and adjacent skill ideas. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn demand for self-improving and proactive agent workflows into actionable plans, checklists, analyses, code changes, and validation notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad invocation terms may activate the skill for unrelated self-improvement or productivity requests. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation by name or apply it only to self-improving and proactive agent workflow tasks. <br>
Risk: Planning guidance could be incorrect or too general for a specific environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans, checklists, code changes, and validation notes against the user's stated constraints before applying them. <br>
