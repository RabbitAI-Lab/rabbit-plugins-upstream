## Use Case:

Students, researchers, editors, and writing agents use this skill to format Harvard-style reference lists and in-text citations for papers that require an author-date Harvard variant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The wrong institutional Harvard variant can produce citations that are internally inconsistent or unacceptable for the target paper.

Mitigation: Confirm the required guide, such as a module handbook, Cite Them Right, or an institutional style guide, before generating citations and keep that variant consistent throughout.

Risk: Incomplete or inaccurate source details can lead to misleading references even when the formatting pattern is correct.

Mitigation: Provide accurate bibliographic metadata and review every generated in-text citation and reference-list entry for completeness.
