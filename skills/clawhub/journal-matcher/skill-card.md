## Description:

Find and rank suitable academic journals for a manuscript using multi-source semantic matching, metrics, OA options, indexing, and predatory-risk checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[comeingwind](https://clawhub.ai/user/comeingwind)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External researchers, authors, and publication-support agents use this skill to turn a manuscript title, abstract, keywords, and submission preferences into a ranked journal shortlist with fit rationale, quality checks, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share unpublished or sensitive manuscript titles or abstracts with external journal finder and indexing sites.

Mitigation: Ask for the minimum needed manuscript information, process content ephemerally where possible, and warn users before querying third-party services.

Risk: Journal metrics, acceptance rates, timelines, and publisher finder results may be biased, incomplete, or outdated.

Mitigation: Cross-check multiple independent sources, show data gaps, and present recommendations as a reviewable shortlist rather than a guaranteed placement decision.
