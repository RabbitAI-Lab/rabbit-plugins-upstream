# Plant DLI Requirements & Symptom Cross-Reference

Ranges from commercial CEA (controlled-environment agriculture) literature;
"min" = survival floor, "opt" = where the plant actually thrives / blooms.

## Symptoms → likely light diagnosis

| Symptom | Typical cause | Confirm by |
|---|---|---|
| Long gaps between leaves, stems stretching toward glass | DLI far below optimal (etiolation) | Internode length vs. plant's normal form |
| Pale / yellowish new growth (all leaves, evenly) | Insufficient light (chlorosis of shade) | Improves within 3-4 weeks of more light |
| Variegated plant turning solid green | Low light — plant abandons white tissue | Reversion starts on newest leaves |
| Monstera/split-leaf: new leaves solid, no holes | DLI < 8 | Fenestration returns at 8+ |
| Plant grows fine April-September, stalls Oct-Feb | Seasonal DLI crash (normal) | Monthly table; consider winter grow light |
| Leaves near window fine, back of plant bare | Distance falloff — light at back is a fraction | Move plant or rotate weekly |
| Brown crispy patches on sun-side leaves | TOO MUCH light (direct sun on shade plant) | Sharp-edged burn on the glass side |
| Orchid grows leaves, never reblooms | DLI enough to live, not to flower (need 8+) | Phalaenopsis: nightly drop + more light |
| Succulent rosette growing as tower / leaning | Classic etiolation, DLI < 9 | Irreversible shape; behead & repropagate |
| Slow growth despite fertilizer | Light caps growth; nutrients can't compensate | Fertilizing a dark-window plant = salt burn |

## Quick placement cheat sheet (northern hemisphere)

| Window | Typical DLI band (mid-latitude, unobstructed, summer) | Sweet-spot plants |
|---|---|---|
| South | 10-25+ | Herbs, succulents, cacti, hoya, citrus (summer), peppers |
| East | 5-10 | African violets, phalaenopsis, pothos (thriving), most philodendron |
| West | 5-12 (hotter) | Herbs (mind summer heat), monstera, rubber plant, jade |
| North | 1.5-4 | ZZ, snake, pothos, aglaonema, heartleaf philodendron |
| North + 20W LED 12h | +5-6 → 6-10 | Unlocks herbs, lettuce, violets, orchids |

For southern hemisphere swap S↔N in the table. Near the equator (|lat| < 15°)
E/W windows dominate (sun passes overhead) and orientation matters less than
obstruction.
