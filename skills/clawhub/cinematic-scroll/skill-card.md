## Description:

Design and build cinematic websites, interactive portfolios, product stories, and real-time 3D experiences with subject-specific art direction and scroll choreography. Use for new builds, redesigns, motion improvements, scroll audits, or storyboards in standalone HTML and existing apps; includes responsive, reduced-motion, and static fallbacks. Not for ordinary dashboards or unrelated animation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and designers use this skill to design, build, repair, or audit cinematic websites and scroll-driven experiences in standalone HTML or an existing app. It also provides storyboards and focused motion guidance, with responsive, reduced-motion, and static fallback considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Motion or real-time 3D may make key content difficult to access when enhancement is unavailable or motion is reduced.

Mitigation: Keep the content and primary action available in the static state, and provide responsive and reduced-motion behavior as described in the skill.
