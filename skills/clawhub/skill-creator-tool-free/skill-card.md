Mitigation: Use a dedicated workspace and review generated or modified files before using, installing, or publishing them. <br>
Risk: The skill may propose command execution or package installation as part of setup or examples. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm commands and dependencies before execution, and avoid granting broader filesystem or network access than the task requires. <br>
