## Description:

Helps people prepare existing artwork for custom wall art, apparel, and gifts through AutoPrint.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with existing artwork or photos use this skill to get print-product recommendations and a studio preview link for wall art, apparel, or gifts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The public artwork URL and print idea are sent to AutoPrint's service.

Mitigation: Share only public image links you have rights to use and are comfortable sending to AutoPrint.

Risk: Product choices and ordering happen on the returned studio page.

Mitigation: Review the preview and product details before choosing a size or placing an order on the website.
