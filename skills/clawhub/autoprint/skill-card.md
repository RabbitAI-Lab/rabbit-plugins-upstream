## Description:

Helps people turn existing artwork or photos into previews for custom wall art, apparel, and gifts through AutoPrint.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with existing artwork or photos use this skill to request a print-product recommendation and preview link for wall art, apparel, or gifts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The selected public image link and print idea are sent to AutoPrint's external service.

Mitigation: Share only images and ideas you are comfortable sending to AutoPrint; avoid private or sensitive images.
