## Description:

Removes AI-generated writing tells from presentation and content drafts by rewriting tone before prose freezes into a data model or HTML, using an Audience-State-Transfer outline method, an always-on hygiene layer, and four register tiers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, presentation authors, and developers use this skill to rewrite outlines, slide drafts, or structured content blocks into a selected register while removing common AI-generated writing tells. It is intended to preserve facts, numbers, names, conclusions, and structure while changing how the content is phrased.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tone rewriting can unintentionally change emphasis or make a formal deliverable too casual if the register tier is chosen poorly.

Mitigation: Choose and confirm the register tier before rewriting, especially for formal or executive-facing materials.

Risk: Rewriting presentation or content drafts can accidentally alter factual meaning if outputs are accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review rewritten content for factual preservation, including numbers, names, conclusions, and structure.
