## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for pest-control providers, check availability, and create bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can create bookings that may involve provider terms, prices, cancellation rules, or user contact information. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, date, time, price or cancellation terms, and consent to send contact information to Lokuli and the service provider. <br>
