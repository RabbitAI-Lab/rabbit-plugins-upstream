## Description: <br>
Run a consulting client discovery session that uncovers the real problem, scope, decision process, fit qualifiers, success criteria, red flags, and proposal-oriented follow-up. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Consultants, freelancers, and client-facing teams use this skill to prepare discovery calls that qualify a lead, identify the underlying business problem, capture decision criteria, and set up a scoped proposal. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A discovery plan can reflect incomplete assumptions about the prospect, budget, authority, timeline, or success criteria. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated plan with business judgment before using it with a prospect. <br>
Risk: Prospect and client details used during preparation may be commercially sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive prospect information unless the agent environment is approved for that information. <br>
