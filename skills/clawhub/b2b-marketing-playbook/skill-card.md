## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Business outreach guidance could be applied to unlawful contact lists, weak consent practices, inappropriate recipient tracking, payment pressure, or escalation tactics. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with lawful contact lists, appropriate consent or legitimate-basis review, anti-spam compliance, and review of tracking, payment, and escalation tactics before use. <br>
Risk: Marketing benchmarks and playbook recommendations could be mistaken for guaranteed outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat benchmarks as planning guidance and validate channel performance against the user's own pipeline data before making budget or sales commitments. <br>
