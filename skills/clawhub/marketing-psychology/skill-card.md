## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated persuasion or urgency recommendations could be applied in a misleading or manipulative way. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations for truthful constraints, clear claims, and ethical application before using them in customer-facing marketing. <br>
Risk: The skill may use local product-marketing context files when present. <br>
Mitigation: Check those files for sensitive or outdated product information before relying on tailored recommendations. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for marketing-adjacent psychology questions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the task is actually marketing-focused before applying the skill's mental-model guidance. <br>
