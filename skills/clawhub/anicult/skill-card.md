## Description:

Helps agents search and discover anime, inspect titles and episodes, and find streaming links using AniList and Anikoto.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jrrqd](https://clawhub.ai/user/jrrqd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Anime fans and their agents use this skill to search titles, browse trending and popular anime, inspect episode details, and find watch links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anime searches and IDs are sent to AniList and Anikoto.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive searches or identifiers to these services.

Risk: Links to unofficial third-party streaming sites may be unsafe.

Mitigation: Prefer official or legal providers when available; avoid opening unfamiliar streaming domains in sensitive browser profiles.
