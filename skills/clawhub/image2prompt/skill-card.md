## Description: <br>
Analyze images and generate detailed prompts for image generation. Supports portrait, landscape, product, animal, illustration categories with structured or natural output. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and prompt engineers use this skill to analyze source images and produce detailed image-generation prompts or structured visual descriptions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Selected images may contain sensitive personal, confidential, or proprietary content that is sent to the configured vision model provider. <br>
Mitigation: Use only images you are permitted to process and avoid sensitive personal photos, confidential screenshots, or proprietary product images unless provider retention and privacy settings are acceptable. <br>
Risk: Generated prompts and structured descriptions may incorrectly describe visual details or include unwanted attributes from the source image. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated prompts before reuse, especially for portraits, product imagery, or any workflow where inaccurate attributes could create downstream risk. <br>
