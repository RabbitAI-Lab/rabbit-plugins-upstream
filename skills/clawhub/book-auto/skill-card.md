## Description: <br>
Book auto services through Lokuli MCP for smog checks, oil changes, car detailing, mechanics, tire services, and other automotive service requests. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[edwardrodriguez703-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/edwardrodriguez703-design) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for automotive service providers, compare pricing and availability, and create bookings through Lokuli after explicit confirmation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bookings may share customer contact details with Lokuli or automotive service providers. <br>
Mitigation: Before confirming a booking, review the provider, price, appointment time, and contact details that will be shared. <br>
