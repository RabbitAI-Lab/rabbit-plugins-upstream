## Description:

Help users draft and, when authorized, submit feedback to volcengine/volcengine-agent-toolkit: skill problems, missing capabilities, cloud scenarios, and successful Agent workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[volc-sdk-team](https://clawhub.ai/user/volc-sdk-team)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agent users with Volcengine cloud experience use this skill to draft concise feedback about skill issues, missing capabilities, cloud scenarios, or successful workflows. It presents a preview and requires explicit approval of the exact content and public destination before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback is published publicly and may expose secrets or private business or customer information.

Mitigation: Review the exact preview and destination before authorizing publication; remove secrets and private business or customer information. Keeping the feedback as a draft requires no GitHub access.
