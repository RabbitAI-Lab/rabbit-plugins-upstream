## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer personal or financial information may be included in requests or generated drafts.

Mitigation: Minimize and redact customer data before use, and follow the institution's approved data handling rules.

Risk: Draft analysis or allocation language could be mistaken for a final recommendation or customer-ready communication.

Mitigation: Have qualified staff review outputs against current customer information, product terms, suitability requirements, and institutional controls before acting or sharing.

Risk: The scanner reports partial analysis because two references to planning-logic.md could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review whether the missing planning material is relevant before relying on the corresponding workflows.
