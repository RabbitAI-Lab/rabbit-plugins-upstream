## Description:

Plans and writes a short explainer video in Phantra (what it is, how it works, why now) as timed shots held to the person's own product or place photos, then joins the rendered scenes and makes one copy per platform.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and product or service teams use the skill to plan a concise explainer video from their own product or place photos, then join the scenes and prepare platform-specific versions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal or business photos shared with Phantra may disclose information the user did not intend to upload.

Mitigation: Use only photos the person is comfortable uploading to Phantra.

Risk: Video rendering spends credits, and unsupported claims could make the explainer misleading.

Mitigation: Review the credit quote before approving a render and keep claims grounded in information the person supplied.
