## Description: <br>
基于原目标、实际结果和关键证据复盘有效动作、无效动作、可控因素、不可控因素和下次调整。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees can use this skill to structure a retrospective from stated goals, actual results, key evidence, controllable factors, uncontrollable factors, and next adjustments. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The review depends on user-provided goals, results, and evidence, so sensitive details may be included in the prompt. <br>
Mitigation: Share only details needed for the retrospective and omit or redact sensitive information when possible. <br>
Risk: Insufficient or conflicting evidence could lead to tentative conclusions being mistaken for facts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's parameter status table and require missing or conflicting information to be supplied or marked before formal analysis. <br>
