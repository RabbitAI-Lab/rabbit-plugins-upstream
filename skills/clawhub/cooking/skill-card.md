## Description: <br>
Cooks and rescues real dishes at the stove with guidance for heat control, seasoning, doneness, timing, substitutions, and food safety. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Home cooks and cooking assistants use this skill to troubleshoot dishes, adapt recipes, answer numeric doneness and safety questions, and sequence meal preparation so food lands correctly timed and safe. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may persist allergies, guest constraints, kitchen facts, and dish outcomes in local cooking, health, and contact records. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when persistent local cooking memory is desired, and review files under ~/Clawic/data/ before and after use. <br>
Risk: Food safety and allergy guidance can affect health if local records are stale or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Verify allergy, dietary, and contact records before relying on ingredient or safety recommendations. <br>
