## Description:

Combines plan a translation setting with build a translation job entry.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Localization operations staff and content teams use this skill to plan translation settings from localization notes and record selected language, glossary, and register settings for translation jobs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Localization notes may include confidential source text or business context.

Mitigation: Avoid placing confidential content in localization notes unless normal workspace and model settings allow that data to be processed.
