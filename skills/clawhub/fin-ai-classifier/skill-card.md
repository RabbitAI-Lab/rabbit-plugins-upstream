## Description:

Helps classify financial AI applications by autonomy and impact, returning JSON decision-support guidance based on its TH-FIN-002 framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Financial AI developers and compliance reviewers can use this skill to triage applications by autonomy and impact and review suggested obligations. Its classifications are decision support, not legal or regulatory determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A financial AI classification may be mistaken for authoritative compliance guidance or rely on outdated rules.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer check conclusions against current official requirements before acting on them.
