## Description:

Use when starting or restocking a fish tank: fishless nitrogen-cycle planning with dosing schedule, bioload-safe stocking plans (adult-size IOU rule), compatibility screening, and a nitrate-driven water-change scheduler. Prevents new-tank syndrome and overstocking deaths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Aquarium keepers use this skill to plan freshwater fishless cycling, evaluate adult-size stocking and species compatibility, and estimate nitrate-driven water changes. It also provides an emergency fish-in-cycle plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning estimates and care guidance may be incomplete or differ from script behavior; incorrect cycling, stocking, or treatment advice could harm fish.

Mitigation: Confirm tank measurements, water-test readings, species requirements, dosing, and treatment advice with reliable aquarium or veterinary sources before acting; use measured test results rather than the schedule alone to decide when stocking is safe.
