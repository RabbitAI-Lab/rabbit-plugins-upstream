## Description:

Guides users through TaskFlow-based review, classification, and metadata preparation before publishing and verifying digital resources with ClawHub.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Developers and content publishers use this guide to prepare and review a digital resource, publish it to ClawHub, and check its listing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing a resource directory may expose secrets, personal data, proprietary files, or unintended metadata.

Mitigation: Review the target directory and metadata before publication.

Risk: A live publish may release content before its final checks are complete.

Mitigation: Use the documented dry-run flow before a real registry publish.
