## Description:

AI image generation via Midjourney Discord bot: create images from prompts, upscale, create variations, blend images, describe images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to generate artwork, illustrations, concept art, image variations, upscales, blends, and prompt suggestions through the Midjourney Discord bot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and uploaded images may be shared with Discord and Midjourney.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive, private, regulated, or proprietary prompts or images unless the user has confirmed channel visibility and service terms are appropriate.

Risk: The skill does not answer commercial licensing questions and points users to Midjourney terms instead.

Mitigation: Review Midjourney terms directly before using generated images in commercial or regulated contexts.

Risk: The workflow depends on Discord and Midjourney bot access.

Mitigation: Confirm Discord is installed, the account can access Midjourney, and the target server or channel supports the required slash commands before relying on the workflow.
