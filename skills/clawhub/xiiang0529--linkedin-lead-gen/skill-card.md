## Description: <br>
Linkedin Lead Gen helps agents search for, research, and verify non-tech founders on LinkedIn to identify prospects for technology services and produce professional lead reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[xiiang0529](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiiang0529) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, business development, and technology service teams use this skill to identify non-tech founders, research their companies, analyze website gaps, and prepare targeted outreach reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill profiles LinkedIn prospects and may encourage collection or use of personal professional information without enough privacy guardrails. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for lawful, business-appropriate prospect research on public professional information, avoid sensitive personal data, and respect LinkedIn and target-site terms. <br>
Risk: The report generator inserts untrusted prospect data into HTML, which can produce unsafe reports if input is not reviewed. <br>
Mitigation: Review and sanitize prospect data, validate URLs, and avoid opening generated reports from untrusted input until HTML escaping is fixed. <br>
