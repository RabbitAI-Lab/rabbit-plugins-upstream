## Description:

Drafts first-touch cold-email outreach for each lead using concise Chinese copy, public-source hooks, subject lines, unsubscribe language, and a source summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and growth teams use this skill to turn a company list and basic offer details into personalized first-touch outreach. It is aimed at Chinese-market cold-email drafting where each message needs a verifiable public hook, concise value proof, a low-friction call to action, and review before sending.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach can be sent to recipients without proper consent or without a clear unsubscribe path.

Mitigation: Review recipient list provenance, consent status, unsubscribe language, and applicable legal requirements before sending.

Risk: Personalized hooks or proof points may be inaccurate if public-source signals are stale, weak, or missing.

Mitigation: Check the cited public signal for each lead and replace unsupported claims with industry-level language or remove them.

Risk: Optional email sending can create unwanted volume or cadence issues if delegated without approval.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval for recipients, message count, sending cadence, and initial small-batch limits before any agent-assisted sending.
