## Description:

Maps candidate profiles to role-focused interview topics and builds interview agendas for recruiter and panel coordination.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiting teams use this skill to turn candidate and role profiles into interview topics, then convert those topic plans into agendas for recruiter and panel coordination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Candidate and role profiles may contain sensitive personal data.

Mitigation: Share only the candidate information needed for interview planning and avoid pasting unnecessary sensitive data.

Risk: Generated interview topics or agendas may be incomplete, unfair, or misaligned with the role.

Mitigation: Recruiters and interview panels should review the generated materials for fairness, relevance, and role fit before use.
