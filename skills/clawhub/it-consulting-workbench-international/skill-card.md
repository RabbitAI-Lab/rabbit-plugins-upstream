## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can guide users into handling sensitive company, employee, cloud, billing, repository, security, or credential information. <br>
Mitigation: Use enterprise data-handling rules, anonymize or aggregate employee data where possible, require written authorization for sensitive access, and keep secrets in an approved vault. <br>
Risk: The skill may support high-impact operational actions such as cloud shutdowns, purchases, remediation, or access revocation. <br>
Mitigation: Require human approval, documented rollback plans, and appropriate change-management review before executing high-impact actions. <br>
Risk: Generated consulting guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific client context. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified professionals review outputs and independently verify facts, assumptions, benchmarks, and recommendations before use. <br>
