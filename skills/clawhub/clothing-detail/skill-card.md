## Description:

Generates macro close-up images of garment details from a clothing photo, highlighting visible fabric texture, stitching, and construction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce content creators use the skill to turn garment photos into close-up detail images for product pages, such as views of a collar, cuff, seam, or fabric texture.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source images may be submitted to external providers, and the security review could not fully inspect all components.

Mitigation: Before use, decide whether external processing is acceptable for confidential or personal images, and treat some behavior as unverified.
