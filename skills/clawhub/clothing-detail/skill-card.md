## Description:

服装工艺细节放大图。服装图 → 面料纹理、走线、织法的微距特写。当用户说「细节图」「特写」「面料放大」「工艺展示」「近景细节」时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce teams can use this skill to create close-up detail images from garment photos, highlighting areas such as seams, fabric texture, collars, cuffs, and trims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The image-generation workflow sends images and prompts to external providers, and the security scan had incomplete coverage.

Mitigation: Check which provider will receive the images and prompts; avoid sending confidential or personal material unless you are comfortable with that transfer.
