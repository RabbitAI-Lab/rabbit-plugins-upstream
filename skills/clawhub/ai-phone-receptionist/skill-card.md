## Description:

Give a business a phone number that an AI answers: a greeting that says it is an AI, opening hours, messages taken with a full transcript, and follow-up by ticket or email. Use when the person wants an AI receptionist, a virtual receptionist, a phone answering line or missed calls handled. Runs on Conecto, a paid service: numbers from $2.00 a month and $0.25 per started minute, on credit the owner bought. US and Canada numbers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Small businesses use the skill to configure an AI phone line for incoming calls, collect caller messages and transcripts, and route follow-up through tickets or email.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States and Canada

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The phone line uses Conecto to handle caller audio and transcripts, and paid numbers can renew monthly.

Mitigation: Confirm that Conecto is an approved service and review recording and transcription disclosures. Get explicit approval before renting a number, which renews monthly, and limit authorized account access to the tools needed.
