## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, briefs, scripts, media, and project content sent through the skill are shared with Cospark and providers needed for processing, which may include private or confidential information.

Mitigation: Explain this data sharing before sending content, follow the user's permissions, and send only material they have authorized.
