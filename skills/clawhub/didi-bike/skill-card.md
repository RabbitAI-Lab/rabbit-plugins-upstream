## Description: <br>
Qingju Bike assistant for DiDi bike-sharing users: bike versus e-bike choice, pricing structure, ride-pass analysis, parking rules, and over-zone fee avoidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to compare Qingju bike and e-bike options, estimate whether a ride pass is worthwhile, troubleshoot common fee issues, and plan short urban trips with current app data. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: City-specific prices, operating zones, parking penalties, and helmet requirements can change or vary by location. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current ride costs, allowed parking areas, operating zones, and helmet requirements in the official Didi or Qingju app before acting on the guidance. <br>
Risk: Ride-pass calculations can be misleading if based on stale usage assumptions or promotional pricing. <br>
Mitigation: Use current app pricing and the user's expected monthly ride count when comparing pass cost against pay-per-ride cost. <br>
