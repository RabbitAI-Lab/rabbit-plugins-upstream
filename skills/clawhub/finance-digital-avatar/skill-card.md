## Description:

Provides text-only design specifications, persona guidance, authorization ledgers, and compliance checklists for financial digital avatars.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Financial institution brand, product, and compliance teams use this skill to plan digital avatar appearance, voice settings, and personas, and to review authorization and content requirements before publication. It does not create media, clone voices, publish content, or provide investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial or synthetic-content compliance guidance may not match local requirements.

Mitigation: Have legal or compliance staff verify applicable rules and review content before publication.

Risk: Supplying biometric, customer, employee, or confidential material may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use written descriptions and fictional examples; do not upload source photos, voice samples, customer records, or confidential material.

Risk: Avatar images, voices, or other assets may lack permission for the intended use.

Mitigation: Obtain written authorization covering channels, geography, duration, and reuse before using any assets.
