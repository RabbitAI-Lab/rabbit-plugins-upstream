## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory or compliance guidance may become outdated or may not fit a specific jurisdiction or product.

Mitigation: Confirm current requirements with official sources or qualified counsel before publishing digital-avatar content.

Risk: Avatar images, cloned voices, fonts, music, or other media may be used without sufficient rights.

Mitigation: Use only legally authorized assets and keep records covering use scenario, channel, term, geography, derivative use, and proof of authorization.

Risk: Financial digital-avatar content may omit generated-content labels, risk warnings, suitability context, or human escalation paths.

Mitigation: Require compliance review before publication and include visible AI-content labels, product risk notices, and a human support path where appropriate.
