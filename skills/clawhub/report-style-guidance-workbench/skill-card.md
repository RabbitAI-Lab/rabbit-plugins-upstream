## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Employees and business users use this skill to turn supplied status information into a concise stakeholder-facing report format.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive status details supplied by the user may be processed in the current conversation.

Mitigation: Avoid including unnecessary confidential information in report requests and review the formatted report before sharing it with stakeholders.
