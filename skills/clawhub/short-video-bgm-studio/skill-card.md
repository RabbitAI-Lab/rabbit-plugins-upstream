## Description:

Creates original instrumental background music from a scene description, shaping mood and energy for short-form video and other visual content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use the skill to generate instrumental background music tailored to a described scene for short videos, vlogs, product clips, tutorials, livestreams, podcasts, and slideshow recaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared Beatra connection covers media and voice features beyond music and reports installation details.

Mitigation: Review the shared authorization scope and installation-reporting disclosures; install only if acceptable, and revoke access when finished.

Risk: Music generation spends credits, and reference recordings may be uploaded to Beatra.

Mitigation: Confirm the current generation price before paid work and upload only recordings you intend to share.
