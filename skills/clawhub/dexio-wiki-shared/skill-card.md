## Description:

Guides agents and people sharing a wiki to avoid conflicting edits, track changes, review important content, and keep private material in the right scope.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dexio](https://clawhub.ai/user/dexio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams and developers use this skill to coordinate wiki updates by multiple agents or people, preserve change history, review important pages, and separate private or client-specific content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applying shared-wiki guidance to unrelated repositories or sensitive notes could lead to inappropriate edits.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended wiki before use, and avoid applying these practices to unrelated repositories or sensitive notes by default.

Risk: Shared documentation can expose private or client-specific content across reader groups.

Mitigation: Keep private and client-specific scopes separate, and review access before sharing or merging pages.
