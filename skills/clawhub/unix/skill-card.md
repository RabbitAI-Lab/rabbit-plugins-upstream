## Description: <br>
Unix guides an agent through a strict six-stage Chinese-language engineering workflow for clarifying requirements, decomposing work, choosing build versus reuse, implementing scoped changes, reviewing adversarially, and delivering concise results. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
