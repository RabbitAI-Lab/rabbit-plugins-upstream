## Use Case:

Telegram-facing agent operators use this skill to keep group, channel, and direct-message responses in Persian while preserving code, commands, technical identifiers, and URLs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persian-only enforcement can conflict with workflows that must preserve a user's requested language, relay exact legal or safety wording, or serve multilingual audiences.

Mitigation: Install only for Telegram chats where Persian output is the intended default, and avoid or override it in workflows requiring exact wording or multilingual output.
