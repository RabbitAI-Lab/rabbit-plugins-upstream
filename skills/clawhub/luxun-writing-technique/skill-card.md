## Description: <br>
A Chinese writing guidance skill that helps agents revise or draft essays, criticism, speeches, elegies, and reflective prose in a colder Lu Xun-inspired style focused on critique, structural harm, spectatorship, self-examination, and anti-sentimentality. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bfchain2-hub](https://clawhub.ai/user/bfchain2-hub) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and writing agents use this skill to transform sentimental or generic Chinese drafts into Lu Xun-inspired critical prose, or to create serious essays, commentary, speeches, elegies, and reflective writing with a cold analytical posture. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may over-activate on broad Chinese writing prompts and apply a colder Lu Xun-inspired critique when the user did not intend that style. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for Lu Xun-style critique or rewrite tasks, and review outputs for tone before using them. <br>
Risk: The anti-sentimental style may be unsuitable for supportive, celebratory, marketing, or sensitive interpersonal writing. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for serious criticism, reflective prose, speeches, elegies, and essays where a cold analytical posture is appropriate. <br>
