## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, consultants, trainers, and other professionals use this skill to turn domain expertise into a structured personal knowledge-IP plan. It guides them through audience discovery, knowledge mapping, positioning, competitor analysis, content planning, course design, monetization planning, and next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Competitor analysis may depend on web search and can become incomplete or stale when search is unavailable or market data changes. <br>
Mitigation: Ask users to provide current competitor examples or verify search-derived competitor data before relying on positioning decisions. <br>
Risk: Revenue estimates and monetization paths are advisory and can be mistaken for guaranteed financial outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Present estimates with assumptions such as audience size, conversion rate, and price, and remind users that actual results depend on execution, traffic, and market conditions. <br>
Risk: The workflow is primarily written for Chinese-language knowledge-IP and course-planning contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's target language, market, and platform mix before applying templates outside Chinese-language creator ecosystems. <br>
