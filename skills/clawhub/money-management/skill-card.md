## Description: <br>
Guides agents to size trading positions, set stop losses, enforce capital limits, evaluate reward-to-risk ratios, and avoid adding margin or pyramiding losing trades. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent builders use this skill for educational trading risk-management support, including position sizing, stop-loss placement, reward-to-risk checks, market concentration limits, and disciplined pyramiding rules. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can influence trading risk decisions and may be mistaken for personalized financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as educational risk-management support, verify calculations independently, and consult qualified financial professionals before making trading decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill is written mostly in Chinese, which may lead to misunderstanding by users who prefer another language. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to restate the guidance in the user's preferred language before relying on the output. <br>
