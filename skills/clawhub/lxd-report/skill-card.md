## Description: <br>
Analyzes Chinese work report drafts to identify missing context, progress, evidence, next steps, and up to three prioritized improvements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ladeng6666](https://clawhub.ai/user/ladeng6666) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, and team contributors use this skill to review Chinese work reports, weekly updates, project progress notes, retrospectives, and upward-reporting drafts before sharing them with a team or manager. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste workplace-sensitive report drafts because the skill is designed to activate on obvious work-report content. <br>
Mitigation: Review and remove confidential, personal, or restricted business details before using the skill. <br>
Risk: The skill focuses its visible feedback on at most three prioritized issues, so lower-priority gaps may not appear in the first response. <br>
Mitigation: Iterate with revised drafts until the analysis no longer reports blocking errors or important warnings. <br>
Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language output and may not fit non-Chinese report workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for Chinese work-report drafts or adapt the output expectations before deployment. <br>
