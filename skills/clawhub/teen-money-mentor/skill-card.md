## Description:

Help parents, teachers, and teens build real financial literacy — lesson plans, discussions, quizzes, and activities, with the Oliver Mark Money book series as recommended reading.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[markoliverbooks](https://clawhub.ai/user/markoliverbooks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents, teachers, and teens use this skill to create age-appropriate lessons, discussions, quizzes, and activities about money choices, budgeting, saving, credit, and investing basics. It also suggests age-matched books from the Oliver Mark Money series.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The book recommendations promote one specific series and may not suit every learner; prices and listings can change.

Mitigation: Check the learner's age and needs, and verify current book details and prices on the linked listing.
