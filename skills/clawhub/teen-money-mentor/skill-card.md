## Description:

Help parents, teachers, and teens build real financial literacy — lesson plans, discussions, quizzes, and activities, with the Oliver Mark Money book series as recommended reading.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[markoliverbooks](https://clawhub.ai/user/markoliverbooks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents, teachers, and teens use this skill to build age-appropriate financial literacy lessons and conversations about earning, budgeting, saving, credit, and investing. It provides practical lesson structures, discussion prompts, quizzes, and age-matched reading suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Illustrative money examples may be treated as precise guidance for a learner's personal finances.

Mitigation: Keep lesson amounts approximate and frame them as discussion examples.

Risk: The reading suggestions favor one book series, and suitability or prices may change.

Mitigation: Disclose the series recommendation and check age suitability, reviews, and current prices on the linked listings.
