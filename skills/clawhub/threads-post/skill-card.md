## Description: <br>
Writes and prepares single Threads posts with a conversation-first post, topic tag, media direction, reply angles, and WoopSocial publishing guidance for one text/media post. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External social media teams and agent operators use this skill to draft, validate, schedule, and publish a single Threads-native post via WoopSocial while keeping reply work human-led. It is intended for post-level execution, not broader channel strategy or automated community management. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can help create public Threads content. <br>
Mitigation: Review the final post, schedule, media, and account before publishing. <br>
Risk: Users may expect native-only formats, reply handling, or multi-post chains that the artifact says are outside WoopSocial scope. <br>
Mitigation: Use WoopSocial only for one Threads post, and route replies, polls, voice notes, GIFs, and native chains to human or native platform handling. <br>
Risk: Drafts could contain engagement bait, press-release tone, fabricated metrics, or promised reach outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Require a genuine conversation starter, use native analytics only, and avoid promised reach multiples or fabricated performance claims. <br>
