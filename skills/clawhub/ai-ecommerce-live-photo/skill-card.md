## Description:

Helps ecommerce merchants and content teams turn authorized product photos into short Live Photo-style social assets with prompts, acceptance checks, rollback criteria, and platform-specific deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce merchants, content teams, agencies, and advertising teams use this skill to generate AI-HIVE Live Photo-style product asset samples from authorized SKU materials, then verify brand, product, cost, and platform requirements before scaling variants.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate on broad ecommerce or platform terms, which may be unintended outside Live Photo-style ecommerce asset generation.

Mitigation: Use it only when the user intends to generate ecommerce Live Photo-style assets, and confirm the target SKU, platform, and deliverables before proceeding.

Risk: Generated assets may process product photos, brand materials, or business context that the user is not authorized to provide.

Mitigation: Require owned or authorized source materials and keep human approval, pilot-first generation, and stop conditions for unclear rights, budget overrun, or failed platform requirements.
