## Description:

Before committing scarce time, people, or money, name the best forgone use of those resources and the value delta of the chosen path versus that alternative.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, developers, and planning teams use this skill to compare scarce resource commitments against the best realistic alternative. It helps structure decisions about time, headcount, budget, feature work, technical debt, and other tradeoffs by separating best forgone value from net value delta.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may shape resource-allocation reasoning using incomplete or incorrect estimates supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Treat the output as decision support and review the stated alternatives, assumptions, values, risks, and countercase before acting.

Risk: Users may provide sensitive business, budget, staffing, or planning details while applying the checklist.

Mitigation: Share only information approved for the agent environment and avoid unnecessary confidential details.
