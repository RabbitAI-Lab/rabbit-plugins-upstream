## Description:

This skill researches cross-border regulations using public legal sources and prepares Chinese reports with bilingual key provisions and source links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External legal, compliance, and law professionals use the skill to research foreign regulations for market entry, cross-border compliance, litigation research, and comparative law. It returns a sourced research report and states that it does not provide formal legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; coverage focuses on the United States, European Union, and Singapore.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An API key shared in chat may be exposed.

Mitigation: Configure the API key locally instead of pasting it into chat, and replace any key that has already been shared.

Risk: Complete legal questions are sent to Cue without a privacy check.

Mitigation: Remove confidential client, personal, and business details before submitting a question.

Risk: The downloaded runner may change before execution.

Mitigation: Review and pin the runner version before running it.
