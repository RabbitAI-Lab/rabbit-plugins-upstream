## Description:

Helps legal and compliance teams research foreign regulations and produce Chinese-language reports with links to source material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal, compliance, and cross-border business teams use this skill to investigate foreign laws, compare requirements, and prepare source-linked Chinese-language research reports. The reports are not a substitute for professional legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; research focuses mainly on the United States, European Union, and Singapore.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research questions may contain sensitive business or legal details that are sent to Cue.

Mitigation: Share only information approved for external processing and remove unnecessary confidential details.

Risk: The skill asks users to paste an API key into chat.

Mitigation: Do not paste API keys into chat; configure credentials as local secrets instead.

Risk: An external runner is installed or upgraded without a pinned revision.

Mitigation: Review the runner before execution and pin a trusted revision where feasible.

Risk: Research coverage can be incomplete and legal conclusions may be unsuitable for direct reliance.

Mitigation: Check cited primary sources and seek qualified legal review before acting.
