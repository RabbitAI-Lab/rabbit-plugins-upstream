External legal, compliance, and cross-border counsel teams use the skill to research foreign regulations and receive Chinese-language reports with linked official sources. The reports support research and due diligence and are not legal advice.

Global, with documented primary coverage of the United States, European Union, and Singapore.

Mitigation: Submit only public or redacted questions unless your organization permits Cue to process confidential information; check Cue's retention and report-sharing controls.

Mitigation: Review and pin the runner version before installing or updating it.
