## Description: <br>
Analyze competitors and create competitive landscape documentation with feature matrices, positioning maps, win/loss analysis, and strategic recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product, sales, and leadership teams use this skill to compare a product or company against competitors, structure market findings, and turn feature, pricing, positioning, and win/loss analysis into actionable recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Competitive-analysis outputs can become stale or misleading when pricing, feature, funding, or recent-activity claims are not current. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to cite current sources, label assumptions, and re-verify time-sensitive claims before using the analysis for product, sales, or leadership decisions. <br>
Risk: Feature-only comparisons can overstate parity and miss meaningful quality, positioning, go-to-market, or segment differences. <br>
Mitigation: Require quality notes, segment-aware win/loss analysis, pricing comparison, and strategic recommendations tied to specific findings. <br>
