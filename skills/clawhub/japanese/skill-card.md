## Description: <br>
Japanese writes and edits native-sounding Japanese across casual, business, formal, creative, and social contexts, with guidance on register, keigo, grammar, punctuation, names, numbers, slang, dialect, and AI-sounding phrasing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and external users use this skill to draft, revise, and review Japanese text for messages, business writing, documents, social posts, speeches, and creative dialogue. It is also used to choose appropriate politeness, honorifics, punctuation, script balance, name readings, counters, and register for a given audience or channel. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill keeps long-term local memory about Japanese writing, contacts, projects, pain points, and artifacts without per-write permission. <br>
Mitigation: Review or restrict the declared Clawic data folders before installation, avoid saving sensitive personal details, and require explicit confirmation before writes or deletions if this risk matters. <br>
Risk: Saved names, honorifics, relationship context, and project details may expose personal or business context on shared machines. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in a trusted local environment and periodically audit the Japanese, contacts, and projects data folders for sensitive entries. <br>
