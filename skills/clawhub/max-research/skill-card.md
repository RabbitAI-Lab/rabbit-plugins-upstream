## Description:

Max Research guides agents through exhaustive, multi-source deep-dive investigations for extremely complex topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiesou](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiesou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they need an agent to investigate complex questions through clarification, iterative why-chain research, multi-source verification, and a comprehensive source-backed report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can be broad and time-consuming.

Mitigation: Set scope, source budget, and stopping criteria before use when exhaustive research is not required.

Risk: Research may involve external searches, non-English sources, video, PDF, and local file review.

Mitigation: For confidential work, explicitly limit which local files and external searches the agent may use.

Risk: Complex research can surface contradictory or unreliable sources.

Mitigation: Require independent corroboration for key claims and label unresolved contradictions or uncertainty in the final report.
