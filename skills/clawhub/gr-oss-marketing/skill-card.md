## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill encourages targeted outreach, including competitor-follower direct messages, which may be unsolicited or violate platform expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outreach manual, opt-in where possible, rate-limited, and compliant with each platform's rules. <br>
Risk: The skill tells agents to fetch changing remote reference material before deeper use. <br>
Mitigation: Review fetched material before acting on it and do not treat remote content as automatically trusted. <br>
