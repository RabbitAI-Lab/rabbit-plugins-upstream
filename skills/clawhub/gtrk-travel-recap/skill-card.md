## Description:

Orchestrates gtrk to turn travel footage into a Chinese narrated video editing project, including scripting, voiceover, B-roll, title cards, music, and subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitruck](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitruck)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travel video creators and editors use it to turn original travel footage or a long travel video into a Chinese narrated recap project, with a review checkpoint before production steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
