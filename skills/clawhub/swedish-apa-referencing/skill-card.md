## Description:

Swedish APA 7 referencing with Sophiahemmet (SHH) deviations: reference lists, in-text citations, laws (SFS), ICN codes, secondary sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External students and academic writers use this skill to prepare Swedish APA 7 reference lists and in-text citations that follow Sophiahemmet Hogskola deviations for coursework.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Citation output may become incorrect if course requirements, source documents, or citation guidance differ from the bundled reference material.

Mitigation: Verify final references against current course instructions and the original source documents before submission.

Risk: Some courses may restrict AI assistance for student work.

Mitigation: Check the applicable course AI policy before using the skill beyond language checking or reference-format review.
