## Description:

Helps eBay, Etsy, Mercari, and Poshmark sellers compare estimated shipping options and listing profit using package dimensions, carrier tiers, and platform fees.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External online sellers can use this skill before listing or sourcing an item to compare dimensional-weight estimates, USPS flat-rate and cubic options, and estimated net proceeds after shipping, packaging, goods cost, and marketplace fees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Built-in carrier rate bands are approximate and may not match current or marketplace-negotiated label prices.

Mitigation: Confirm the leading service's live price in the carrier or marketplace label flow before purchase.
