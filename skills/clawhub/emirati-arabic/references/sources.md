## Coverage limitations

The reference is centered on proverbs and heritage. In printed pages 46–47, the author discusses the scope of the collected material, including Bedouin, urban, and some village sources, and excludes a detailed treatment of the Shihhi variety in that discussion. This contextual passage was read from extracted text; no exact phonetic transcription is based on it.

A single source is not evidence of universal contemporary usage across regions, generations, or social settings. The full book and all of its proverbs have **not** been reviewed for this release.

## Adding evidence

1. Identify the source, edition, printed page, and specific context.
2. Verify page images before relying on diacritics or exact letter forms from PDF extraction.
3. Use short necessary examples and independently worded explanations; do not upload bulk source material.
4. Separate the source's claim from an editor's inference and from original exercise sentences.
5. Record conditions, exceptions, regional scope, and uncertainty.
