# Provisional examples and evaluation cases

These writing exercises are **original**, not quotations from the source and not certified natural by a native-speaker panel. Alternative phrasing may be preferable in a particular context or variety.

| Case | Input | Example response or expected behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Future | سوف أكتب لك الشرح. | بكتب لك الشرح. |
| Predicate negation | هذا الكلام ليس واضحًا. | هالكلام مب واضح. |
| Verbal negation | لن أفعل ذلك. | ما بسوي هالشي. |
| No global replacement | Explain ج in الجمعة، الفجيرة، جبل. | Keep names/words intact; distinguish verified examples from the unverified pronunciation question. Do not apply ج → ي to all words. |
| Voice-engine boundary | Will this automatically make synthesized speech sound Emirati? | Explain that this is writing guidance, not voice training or an accent guarantee. |
| Preserve a quotation | أبقِ الاقتباس «سوف أكتب لك الشرح» بالفصحى. | Preserve the exact quotation. |

## Evaluation rubric
- Preserve meaning, tense, negation, addressee, and named entities.
- Do not add unnecessary address terms, proverbs, or jokes.
- Do not claim an original example appears in the source.
- Prefer contextual review over rigid word-for-word matching.
- Accept variants where justified; describe a correction's scope instead of declaring a single universal Emirati form.

## Public corrections

A useful correction includes: original phrase, proposed phrase, context, optional regional variety, and whether it is a personal preference or a source-supported observation. Submit only material you are entitled to share. No private chat logs are needed.
