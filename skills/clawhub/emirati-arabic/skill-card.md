## Use Case:

External users and language practitioners use this skill to draft or review Emirati Arabic conversation, explanations, dialogue, and voiceover scripts while preserving meaning, register, and named entities. It can also explain documented pronunciation distinctions within the source material's stated limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Limited source examples may not represent contemporary usage across Emirati regions or speakers, leading to inaccurate or overly generalized wording.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as provisional and seek review from an Emirati Arabic speaker for important wording; follow the user's register and corrections.
