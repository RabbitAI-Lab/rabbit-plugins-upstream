## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing to a public platform without confirming the content, destination account, platform, and current authorization could cause an unintended post.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact content, platform, account, and permission for this publication before submitting; complete login and verification yourself.

Risk: An ambiguous or delayed publication result could be mistaken for a failed submission and lead to duplicate posting.

Mitigation: Check the original task and platform record or management page before retrying, and report pending or unknown status accurately.
