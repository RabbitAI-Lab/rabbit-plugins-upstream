## Description:

Advise audience readiness and compose circulation-ready briefs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Communications owners and account teams use this skill to classify stakeholder briefing material by audience and compose circulation-ready sections while tracking excluded internal notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential draft material could be exposed if users paste it into an agent session that is not approved for that content.

Mitigation: Use only agent sessions approved for the sensitivity of the briefing material and avoid entering confidential drafts unless that processing is acceptable.

Risk: Incorrect audience classification could place unresolved estimates, drafting notes, or internal ownership details in a circulation-ready brief.

Mitigation: Review the selected audience and the included_sections and excluded_sections lists before circulating the brief.
