## Description: <br>
漫剧剪辑合成 helps agents produce Chinese comic-video post-production plans, including dubbing, editing steps, subtitles, BGM, sound effects, color grading, and export settings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, editors, and agent workflows use this skill to turn comic-storyboard materials into a practical post-production plan. It defines voice direction, cut timing, transitions, subtitles, BGM, sound effects, color treatment, export settings, and final checks for Chinese comic-video production. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
