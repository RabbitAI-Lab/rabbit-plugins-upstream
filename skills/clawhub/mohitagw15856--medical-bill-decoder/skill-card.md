## Description:

Decode itemized medical bills and EOBs into plain English, flag likely duplicate, unbundled, balance-billing, or implausible charges, and draft scripts for itemized-bill requests, financial assistance, and negotiation calls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand itemized medical bills or EOBs, identify charges worth disputing, and prepare call scripts and next steps for billing departments or insurers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste sensitive medical or insurance details into the agent.

Mitigation: Redact identifiers where possible before using the skill.

Risk: Billing protections and financial decisions can depend on jurisdiction, plan type, and individual circumstances.

Mitigation: Confirm jurisdiction-specific protections or major financial decisions with a qualified professional.

Risk: The skill may produce incorrect or misleading plain-language billing guidance if source bill or EOB details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the original bill and EOB, request a fully itemized bill when needed, and verify uncertain codes with the billing department or insurer.
