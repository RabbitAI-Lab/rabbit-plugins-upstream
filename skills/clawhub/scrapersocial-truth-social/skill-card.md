## Description:

Retrieves public Truth Social profiles and user posts as JSON through ScraperSocial.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nikhonit](https://clawhub.ai/user/nikhonit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and media analysts use this skill to retrieve public Truth Social profiles and posts by account handle for monitoring and analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: API requests use a ScraperSocial key and may consume credits.

Mitigation: Use a key you are comfortable sharing with ScraperSocial, keep it private, and check credit costs before requesting posts.
