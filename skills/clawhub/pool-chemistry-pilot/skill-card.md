## Description:

Helps residential pool and spa owners interpret water tests and calculate ordered chemical dosing, chlorine targets, and maintenance steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

MIT

## Use Case:

Residential pool and spa owners use the skill to interpret test results, calculate chemical doses, plan treatment in a safe order, and track water-chemistry trends. It is not intended for public-pool regulatory compliance or chemical-ingestion emergencies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect pool volume or test readings can lead to unsafe chemical doses.

Mitigation: Verify pool volume and test results; add chemicals incrementally and re-test between treatment steps.

Risk: Mishandling pool chemicals, especially mixing acid and chlorine, can cause serious injury.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's handling precautions: use eye protection, add acid to water, never mix acid with chlorine, and store chemicals securely.

Risk: Residential advice may not meet public-pool requirements.

Mitigation: Use applicable public-pool regulations and qualified professional guidance for regulated facilities.
