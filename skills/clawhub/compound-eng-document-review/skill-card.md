## Description: <br>
Structural review of documents for gaps, clarity, completeness, and organization before the next workflow step. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, product teams, and technical writers use this skill to review brainstorms, plans, specs, ADRs, and similar planning documents for clarity, completeness, scope control, feasibility, and security considerations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow can make small wording or formatting edits without a separate approval step. <br>
Mitigation: Review the saved document after the pass and require approval before accepting substantive restructuring, removals, or meaning changes. <br>
Risk: Document-review findings can miss context or over-prioritize a perceived gap when the source document depends on prior discussion. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's approval path for material changes and provide the relevant planning context when requesting review. <br>
