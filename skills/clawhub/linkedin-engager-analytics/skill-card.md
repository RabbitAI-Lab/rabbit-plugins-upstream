## Description: <br>
Pull the people who liked or commented on any LinkedIn post and segment them by ICP fit (peer / aspirational / prospect / other). <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to analyze LinkedIn post engagers, classify them by ICP fit, and prepare follow-up action lists for relationship-building or outreach workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can scrape and profile LinkedIn engagers for outreach. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only on posts you own or have clear permission to track, and provide explicit post URLs with a capped engager count. <br>
Risk: Competitor-audience scraping or high-volume profiling may create platform, privacy, or trust concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid competitor-audience scraping unless there is a lawful and platform-compliant basis, and review the collected roster before acting on it. <br>
Risk: Same-day outreach based on engagement can be intrusive or non-compliant. <br>
Mitigation: Wait 24-72 hours before outreach, send at most one opener per engager, and use only outreach with an appropriate lawful basis. <br>
