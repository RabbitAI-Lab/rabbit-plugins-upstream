## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contract safety, reward claims, and unstaking guarantees have not been independently verified.

Mitigation: Before signing, verify the contract addresses, selected action, and transaction amount; seek independent confirmation of contract behavior before relying on rewards or unstaking claims.

Risk: Burning a card permanently destroys the NFT, and transaction fees apply to the documented actions.

Mitigation: Confirm the token ID and action with the human before burning, and review the amount and fees before signing.
