## Description:

Helps plan a Six Flags or former Cedar Fair park visit using live ride waits, park hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Park visitors and trip planners use the skill to check operating hours, ride waits and showtimes, then choose what to ride next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad questions about Six Flags or former Cedar Fair parks may activate this skill when trip planning is not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's park and intent before offering park-specific planning guidance.

Risk: Ride waits and showtimes may be absent outside operating hours.

Mitigation: Check the park schedule and open status before interpreting missing waits or shows.
