## Description:

Helps plan a Six Flags or Cedar Fair park visit using live ride waits, park hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Park visitors and trip planners can check ride waits, operating hours, and showtimes, then choose what to ride next at Six Flags and former Cedar Fair parks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wait times and showtimes may be unavailable outside park operating hours.

Mitigation: Check park hours and open status before relying on live wait or show information.
