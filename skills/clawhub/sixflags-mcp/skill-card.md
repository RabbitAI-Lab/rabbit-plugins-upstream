## Description:

Helps plan visits to Six Flags and Cedar Fair parks using ride waits, operating hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Park visitors use this skill to check current waits and hours, find showtimes, and choose what to ride next at Six Flags and Cedar Fair parks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live waits, hours, or showtimes may be missing or inaccurate outside operating hours or during upstream outages.

Mitigation: Check park operating status and confirm time-sensitive plans with the park before relying on live results.
