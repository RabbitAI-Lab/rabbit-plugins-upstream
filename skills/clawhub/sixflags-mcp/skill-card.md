## Description:

Plan a day at a Six Flags or Cedar Fair theme park with live ride wait times, park hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions backed by the sixflags-mcp server over the public themeparks.wiki API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and travel planners use this skill to plan visits to Six Flags and Cedar Fair parks, check current waits and hours, and choose rides or shows during a park day.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live park data may be unavailable or stale outside operating hours.

Mitigation: Check park operating status and schedule before relying on wait times or show times, and present time-sensitive results as current public data rather than guaranteed availability.
