## Description:

Helps visitors plan days at Six Flags and former Cedar Fair parks using ride waits, park hours, showtimes, and next-ride suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Park visitors and trip planners use this skill to check hours and ride waits, choose what to ride next, and fit shows into a visit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live park data depends on an external service and may be unavailable or misleading outside operating hours.

Mitigation: Check park-open status and hours, and confirm important plans with the park.
