## Description:

Plan a day at a Six Flags or Cedar Fair theme park with live ride wait times, park hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions from public themeparks.wiki data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External guests and travel planners use this skill to compare live waits, park hours, show schedules, and ride options when planning or adjusting a Six Flags or Cedar Fair park visit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked broadly for park-name trip-planning questions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking about Six Flags or Cedar Fair planning and ask for a specific park when the request is ambiguous.

Risk: Live waits and show schedules may be absent or misleading outside operating hours.

Mitigation: Check park operating hours and open status before treating wait-time or show data as actionable.
