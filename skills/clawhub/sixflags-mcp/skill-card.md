## Description:

Helps plan visits to Six Flags and former Cedar Fair parks using public ride waits, park hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Park visitors and trip planners use this skill to check operating hours, ride waits, and showtimes and decide what to ride next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live waits and schedules may be unavailable or inaccurate outside park operating hours or when the upstream service is unavailable.

Mitigation: Check park hours and live status before planning; confirm time-sensitive details with the park.
