## Description:

Helps visitors plan a Six Flags park day using ride waits, operating hours, showtimes, and next-ride suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Park visitors use this skill to check current ride waits and schedules, then choose what to ride next at Six Flags and former Cedar Fair parks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live ride waits and showtimes may be unavailable or stale when the park is closed or the upstream service is unreachable.

Mitigation: Check park operating hours and live status before relying on the data; confirm time-sensitive plans with the park.
