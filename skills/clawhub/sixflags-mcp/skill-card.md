## Description: <br>
Plan a day at a Six Flags theme park with live ride wait times, park hours, show schedules, and next-ride suggestions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to plan visits to Six Flags and Cedar Fair parks, including checking live waits, operating hours, show schedules, attraction directories, and next-ride options. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Live wait times, show schedules, and park status can be unavailable or stale outside operating hours or when the external public API is unavailable. <br>
Mitigation: Check park schedules and current park-open status before relying on ride or show recommendations. <br>
Risk: Trip-planning recommendations may be incomplete when public park data omits rides, showtimes, or temporary closures. <br>
Mitigation: Use recommendations as planning guidance and verify important park details with current official park information before acting. <br>
