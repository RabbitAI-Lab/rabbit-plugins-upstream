## Description: <br>
Works as counsel for contract review, negotiation, legal-risk pricing, drafting, compliance, IP, privacy, entity, employment, dispute, and outside-counsel workflows while routing red-flag matters to licensed practitioners. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and business operators use this skill to review, redline, draft, compare, negotiate, and track legal documents, deadlines, obligations, and counsel work. It is aimed at commercial legal workflows where the agent should name the jurisdiction, side, deadline, and exposure, then produce human-reviewed text or records. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill automatically reads and updates sensitive legal, contact, project, and finance records without asking each time. <br>
Mitigation: Require confirmation before writes or deletions, segment records by client or matter, and limit what is saved into shared contacts, projects, and finances. <br>
Risk: Durable local memory can preserve privileged, confidential, or matter-sensitive legal context for later reuse. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use for privileged or highly confidential matters unless durable local memory is acceptable; keep strategy with licensed counsel and store only necessary business records. <br>
Risk: Legal guidance can exceed safe self-help boundaries for filed cases, regulator contact, criminal exposure, securities offerings, immigration, family, personal injury, insolvency, or urgent statutory deadlines. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's red-flag routing behavior to stop drafting and prepare a jurisdiction-specific handoff for a licensed practitioner. <br>
