## Description:

Provides Madrid System trademark registration guidance, including applications, refusals, renewals, fees, deadlines, and a dedicated China practice section.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Trademark agents, IP lawyers, in-house counsel, and brand owners use this skill to plan and review Madrid System filings, responses, renewals, fees, and deadlines, with optional guidance for procedures involving China.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated fees or deadlines may lead to incorrect filing decisions.

Mitigation: Confirm current fees and applicable deadlines against official WIPO and relevant office sources before acting.

Risk: Procedural guidance may be mistaken for formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as practice assistance and obtain qualified legal review for case-specific decisions.

Risk: Optional utilities may contact WIPO websites or write user-selected output files.

Mitigation: Review intended network access and output paths before running optional utilities.
