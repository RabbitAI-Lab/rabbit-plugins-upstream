## Description:

Assists trademark practitioners with Madrid System applications, subsequent designations, refusals, renewals, record changes, fee estimates, and China-specific procedures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huiningip](https://clawhub.ai/user/huiningip)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Trademark agents, IP lawyers, in-house counsel, and rights holders use this skill to plan and review Madrid System filings, responses, renewals, and fee estimates, including CNIPA-related procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated fees or deadlines may lead to incorrect filing decisions.

Mitigation: Check current fees, deadlines, and applicable notices against official sources before relying on an estimate.

Risk: Legal-practice assistance may be mistaken for case-specific legal advice.

Mitigation: Have a qualified practitioner review jurisdiction-specific rules and filing decisions.

Risk: Maintenance scripts can update local reference files.

Mitigation: Run fetch or write scripts only when an update is intended and review the resulting changes.
