## Description:

Supports Madrid System trademark practice with guidance on international applications, designations, refusals, renewals, recordals, fees, and status checks, including a dedicated China chapter.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huiningip](https://clawhub.ai/user/huiningip)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Trademark attorneys, IP lawyers, in-house counsel, and brand owners use this skill to prepare and check Madrid System filings, fee estimates, deadlines, and responses across contracting parties.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional official-site checks make outbound requests that could expose nonpublic matter details.

Mitigation: Avoid entering nonpublic matter details unless outbound sharing is acceptable in the practice environment.

Risk: Fee and deadline calculations are estimates and may differ from current official determinations.

Mitigation: Check results against current official notices, calculators, and invoices before relying on them.
