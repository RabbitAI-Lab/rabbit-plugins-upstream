## Description:

Helps trademark practitioners navigate Madrid System filings, designations, refusals, renewals, fees, and related international registration procedures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huiningip](https://clawhub.ai/user/huiningip)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Trademark attorneys, IP lawyers, and in-house counsel use this skill to plan Madrid System registrations, track deadlines, estimate fees, and prepare application checklists and refusal-response memos. Its China-specific guidance is optional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fees, declarations, and deadlines can become outdated or vary by contracting party.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive amounts and requirements against current official WIPO or local-office sources before relying on them.

Risk: Live WIPO fee checks transmit filing parameters to the WIPO calculator.

Mitigation: Use live checks only with non-confidential parameters and when sharing those details with WIPO is acceptable.

Risk: Practice guidance and calculated results could be mistaken for definitive legal advice or a final fee assessment.

Mitigation: Have a qualified practitioner review advice and confirm payable amounts and deadlines with authoritative sources.
