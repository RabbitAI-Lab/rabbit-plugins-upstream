Trademark agents, IP lawyers, in-house counsel, and rights holders use this skill to plan Madrid System applications, subsequent designations, renewals, refusals, and recordal tasks, including China/CNIPA-related steps.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Case-specific legal guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a designated jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Use the output as practice assistance, not formal legal advice; confirm strategy with current official sources or local counsel.

Risk: Fees, deadlines, and member declarations can change after reference data is captured.

Mitigation: Check current WIPO/CNIPA information and official notices before filing, paying, or relying on calculated dates.

Risk: Live fee and WIPO Lex refresh scripts use network access and may update local reference files.

Mitigation: Run them only when network access and local output are expected; review refreshed references before use.
