## Description:

Draw tarot cards from QiyueAstro - one card, daily card, or any of 13 spreads. Browse the full 78-card Rider-Waite deck and read card meanings in English or Chinese. No API key needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to draw tarot cards, browse Rider-Waite cards and spreads, and display QiyueAstro-provided card meanings in English or Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tarot questions entered by users may be sent to qiyueastro.com.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive personal, health, employment, financial, or relationship details in the question field unless sharing that text with QiyueAstro is acceptable.

Risk: Users may treat tarot output as decision-grade advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as entertainment and self-reflection content, and display the API-returned meanings without adding independent interpretation.
