## Description:

Research football fixtures, match reports, player form, transfers, standings, and broadcast listings through Crawlora FotMob tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Football researchers and fans use this skill to prepare source-attributed match briefs, compare teams or players, follow transfers and league leaders, and check broadcast listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Football research queries are sent to Crawlora using a Crawlora API key.

Mitigation: Use the skill only if sharing those queries and providing an API key to Crawlora is acceptable; keep the key confidential.

Risk: English/en-US news behavior and incomplete or estimated match data can mislead comparisons.

Mitigation: Check source links and collection times, compare like seasons and competitions, and label estimates, rumours, and missing data clearly.
