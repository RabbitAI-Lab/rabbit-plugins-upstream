## Description:

Helps educators draft blended-learning course designs and teaching calendars, with optional interactive HTML courseware and presentation slides.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[dever-lee](https://clawhub.ai/user/dever-lee)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 CN

## Use Case:

University and vocational educators use this Chinese-language skill to draft blended-learning plans and teaching calendars, optionally with interactive courseware and presentation slides. The packaged terms prohibit commercial use; teachers must review and adapt generated drafts before classroom or competition use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Normal use may install Python document-generation dependencies.

Mitigation: Review and approve dependencies before running the generation scripts.

Risk: Course-research searches may send queries to external search engines.

Mitigation: Keep student data, credentials, confidential institutional materials, and unpublished sensitive course details out of queries and generated inputs.

Risk: Generated course files may contain inaccurate material or overwrite existing files.

Mitigation: Review drafts for accuracy and check the output directory before generation.
