Employees, external consultants, and safety or compliance practitioners use this skill to plan ISO 45001 occupational health and safety management systems, identify risks and opportunities, prepare management documents, and support certification or OHSAS 18001 transition work. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Occupational health, legal, or certification guidance may be incomplete or outdated for a specific jurisdiction or certifying body. <br>
Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current local regulations and qualified occupational health, safety, legal, or certification professionals before use. <br>
Risk: Planning inputs may include sensitive employee health, incident, or workplace safety records. <br>
Mitigation: Share only necessary, de-identified information and avoid unnecessary personal medical details or confidential employee records. <br>
