## Description:

Identifies pages losing Search Console traffic, compares them with current search results, and prepares specific refresh briefs and approval-pending tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO teams use this skill to prioritize declining pages using Search Console data, prepare evidence-backed content refresh briefs, and stage proposed fixes for approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic grants access to Search Console and site-analysis data.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended site and permissions before connecting Search Console.

Risk: Approved tasks, especially content pruning or auto-executable changes, may quickly modify site content.

Mitigation: Review each staged task and its scope before approving it; require explicit sign-off for pruning.
