## Description:

Combines select a contact for a client brief with send a finance-review message.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Client communications and account teams use this skill to choose routing contacts for routine client briefs, account updates, and implementation handoffs, then prepare finance-review messages for selected recipients.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outbound finance-review messages could be sent to the wrong recipient or with incorrect account-review details.

Mitigation: Ask for user confirmation before calling the send tool, especially when recipient addresses or account-review details are involved.
