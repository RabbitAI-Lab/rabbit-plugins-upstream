## Description: <br>
Convert a business question into a complete dashboard specification. Use when asked to design a dashboard, create a dashboard spec or brief, plan a BI report, or define what charts and metrics a dashboard should include. Produces a structured spec with metrics, dimensions, chart types, filters, and layout guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
BI, data, product, and operations teams use this skill to turn a business question or monitoring need into an implementation-ready dashboard brief with metrics, visualizations, filters, layout guidance, and data requirements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Dashboard briefs may be incomplete or misleading if the user provides missing, inaccurate, or unavailable data sources. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the business question, available data sources, metric definitions, and required fields before using the brief for implementation. <br>
Risk: Business context or data-source details shared during use may be sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive business details unless they are appropriate for the agent conversation. <br>
