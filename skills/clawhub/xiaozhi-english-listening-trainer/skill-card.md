## Description:

根据学习者的词汇量和兴趣生成英语听力材料，并帮助定位理解困难；高中训练还覆盖说话者的意图与态度。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 English learners use this skill to practise listening with passages matched to their level and interests, then identify whether vocabulary, sentence structure, or speech rate caused difficulty. It supports primary, junior-high, and senior-high learners; primary-school use requires an adult present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles may be saved or reused across sessions and companion skills.

Mitigation: Enable profile access and cross-skill sharing only when wanted; say “这次不要记忆” to skip profile access and saving for a session.

Risk: Speech and storage privacy depends on the hosting platform and companion skills.

Mitigation: Review their privacy practices before enabling integrations.

Risk: The skill is designed for primary-school learners as well as older students.

Mitigation: Have a parent or teacher present when a primary-school learner uses it.
