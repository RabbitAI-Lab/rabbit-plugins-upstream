## Description:

Generates interest- and level-matched English listening passages and questions, guides learners through listening and comprehension, and identifies vocabulary, sentence-structure, or pace difficulties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 learners use this skill to practice level-appropriate English listening and get targeted comprehension feedback; elementary pupils should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K–12); adapt curriculum and safety referrals before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A learner's long-term profile, vocabulary, or parent-visible progress could be shared without intended consent.

Mitigation: Confirm profile, cross-skill, and parent-sharing consent before accessing or sharing records; honor ‘这次不要记忆’ for private sessions.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum or crisis referrals may be inappropriate for learners in other regions.

Mitigation: Use in the disclosed Chinese K–12 setting; localize learning standards and safety referrals before deploying elsewhere.
