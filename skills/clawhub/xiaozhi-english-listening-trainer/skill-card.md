## Description:

Creates interest- and level-matched English listening passages for Chinese-speaking students, then guides comprehension practice and identifies vocabulary, sentence-structure, or listening-speed difficulties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking upper-primary, middle-school, and high-school students use this skill to practice level-appropriate English listening and understand where comprehension breaks down. Younger students need a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; use elsewhere only after localizing crisis resources and minor-consent practices.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning profile or interests could be used or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Configure guardian consent, memory, and cross-skill sharing before personalization; honor requests to pause memory, delete records, or opt out of sharing.

Risk: Crisis guidance could direct a student to the wrong regional resource.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts before use outside mainland China; confirm the student's region before supplying regional numbers and direct urgent danger to local emergency services.

Risk: Reading-only practice or transcribed speech may be mistaken for assessed listening or pronunciation.

Mitigation: Clearly label the reading fallback and avoid pronunciation assessment unless the platform has suitable audio scoring.
