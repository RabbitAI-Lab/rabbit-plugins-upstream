# Payable test designs

Run Authorization permits private test selection, design, and draft creation
without another approval; it never permits external execution. Choose one
design only after checking the Offer Brief's buyer, authority,
economics, delivery boundary, evidence label, and cost ceiling. Every design
must make the price, payment condition, deliverable, delivery timing,
qualification, and cancellation or refund terms clear. Stage a draft first;
the approval gate controls any external use.

## Test type selection

- **Paid diagnostic**: a fixed-scope audit, assessment, or plan with a defined
  artifact and no implied outcome guarantee.
- **Paid pilot**: a small, time-bounded delivery for a qualified buyer with
  acceptance criteria and capacity reserved only after approval.
- **Preorder with clear terms**: payment or an authorized payment commitment
  for a defined future deliverable, delivery date/window, terms, and lawful
  cancellation/refund handling.
- **Deposit-backed booking**: a clear deposit that reserves a defined service
  slot, with the balance, schedule, cancellation, and refund/correction terms
  disclosed before payment.
- **Manual concierge service**: a manually delivered, bounded service that
  validates demand before automation or a larger build.
- **Paid workshop or office hour**: a paid, time-bounded education or advisory
  session within credentials and regulatory limits, with a stated agenda.
- **Existing-customer upsell**: a qualified, permissioned offer to an existing
  customer that adds a defined service or product without fabricated urgency.
- **Conversion or recovery experiment**: an authorized change to an existing
  buyer path that records payment behavior and preserves a rollback path.

## Route-to-test map

Each option below is a legitimate payable test only after the stated access,
consent, authority, delivery, and policy conditions are true. None authorizes
contact, a charge, publication, or an account change.

### 1. Existing-business optimization

- Sell a fixed-price paid diagnostic of one approved funnel or fulfillment
  bottleneck, returning a measurement sheet and prioritized recommendations.
- Run an owner-approved conversion or recovery experiment on one controlled
  existing path, with a documented baseline, spend ceiling, payment metric, and
  rollback.

### 2. Productized service

- Offer a paid pilot for one standardized outcome, with scope, acceptance
  criteria, delivery time, and price stated up front.
- Take a deposit-backed booking for one defined delivery slot, including the
  balance, schedule, cancellation, and refund terms.

### 3. Expert service

- Sell a paid diagnostic within verified credentials and scope, with qualified
  review for regulated work and no implied professional outcome.
- Offer a paid workshop or office hour with a disclosed agenda, audience,
  credentials, boundaries, and no individualized regulated advice where barred.

### 4. Local-business revenue recovery

- Sell a fixed-price paid diagnostic of permissioned missed-call, estimate, or
  follow-up records using authorized and appropriately protected data.
- Run an owner-approved conversion or recovery experiment on an existing
  inbound path, with consent, capacity, measurement, and rollback confirmed.

### 5. Creator or audience monetization

- Offer a preorder with clear terms for a disclosed, policy-compliant audience
  product, delivery window, and cancellation/refund handling.
- Offer an existing-customer upsell to a permissioned customer segment for a
  defined add-on, without fake scarcity, undisclosed endorsement, or pressure.

### 6. Ecommerce conversion improvement

- Sell a paid diagnostic of one authorized product, checkout, support, or
  retention path with a measurement plan and no guaranteed conversion claim.
- Run an authorized conversion or recovery experiment on a controlled existing
  page or lifecycle path, with a payment metric, spend cap, and rollback.

### 7. Digital product or paid knowledge

- Offer a preorder with clear terms for a bounded, non-regulated educational
  asset with delivery date/window and lawful cancellation/refund terms.
- Run a paid workshop or office hour that validates a defined topic, audience,
  agenda, capacity, and payment condition before recording or scaling it.

### 8. Technical implementation service

- Sell a paid diagnostic of an authorized workflow or environment, returning a
  bounded findings and handoff artifact rather than an unverified performance
  promise.
- Offer a paid pilot for one non-production or authorized implementation,
  with access limits, acceptance criteria, rollback, and human review.

### 9. Starting-from-zero skill-to-service bridge

- Offer a manual concierge service for one verified transferable skill and one
  reachable buyer, with a small fixed scope, turnaround, and acceptance check.
- Take a deposit-backed booking for a clearly defined manual service slot only
  after delivery capacity, lawful terms, and refund/correction handling exist.

## Evidence discipline

A settled payment supported by an inspected, privacy-safe settlement artifact
is `Cash collected`. A signed obligation supported by an inspected,
privacy-safe signed-obligation artifact is `Revenue contracted`; an invoice or
order is `Revenue booked`; interest without a payment condition is `Pipeline
signal`. Modeling a likely result is `Estimated economic value`; a hypothetical
example is `Simulation`; a report without the artifact required for a stronger
label is `Unverified anecdote`. Use the label that the observed evidence earns,
not the label that makes the test look strongest.

## Run handoff and execution

Return the selected design and staged test artifact to `moneyprinter` so it can
update Money Machine Run and continue to ethical-acquisition. Do not stop for a
separate design approval. Before exact approval, keep external use staged.
After an immediately preceding exact approval, the current host may perform
only that approved action, record an `Action receipt`, and return it to
`moneyprinter`. Broad, batch, stale, or materially changed permission is
invalid.
