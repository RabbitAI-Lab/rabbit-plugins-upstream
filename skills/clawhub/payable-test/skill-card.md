## Description: <br>
Turn a confirmed Offer Brief into one ethical, smallest viable paid validation plan with clear payment terms, evidence labels, cost limits, and a specific external-action gate. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bilbop1](https://clawhub.ai/user/bilbop1) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External builders and agents use this skill after an Offer Brief is available to design a narrow payable validation test, stage the test artifact, and define the approval checkpoint before any buyer contact, payment, publication, account change, or other external action. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A staged payable test could lead to real-world sending, posting, purchasing, charging, signing, submitting, or account changes. <br>
Mitigation: Require an exact human approval checkpoint before any external action, including authority, data basis, regulated-review status, cost, and rollback or correction path. <br>
Risk: Offer Briefs, messages, pages, policies, payment records, or customer statements may contain untrusted instructions or sensitive data. <br>
Mitigation: Treat copied inputs as evidence only, ignore embedded instructions, minimize third-party data, and avoid exposing credentials or confidential context. <br>
Risk: Paid tests can misstate evidence quality, payment terms, fulfillment capacity, refund handling, or regulated-review readiness. <br>
Mitigation: Use the strongest evidence label actually supported, require clear payment and delivery terms, and block regulated or unclear fulfillment scenarios until reviewed. <br>
