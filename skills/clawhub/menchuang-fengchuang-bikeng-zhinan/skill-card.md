## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for homeowners comparing windows, balcony enclosures, and entry doors while avoiding common renovation pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners use this skill to compare window and door options, identify potential enclosure pitfalls, and prepare questions for qualified installers. It is discussion material, not construction guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some renovation references contain broad or potentially unsafe construction advice.

Mitigation: Review relevant references before relying on them for real renovation decisions; use the skill for homeowner discussion only.

Risk: Changes to walls, doors or fire safety, balcony waterproofing, gas, or electrical systems may create safety or compliance hazards.

Mitigation: Check local requirements and obtain property-management approval; have appropriately licensed professionals assess and perform the work.
