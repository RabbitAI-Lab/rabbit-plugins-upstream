## Use Case:

External users and home renovation advisers use this skill to answer practical Chinese-language questions about window selection, balcony enclosure, entry and interior doors, curtains, seals, hardware, waterproofing, and related renovation tradeoffs. It is intended for informal planning guidance and should not replace licensed structural, code, property-management, health, or environmental review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide informal renovation advice involving structural changes, balcony enclosure, door removal, new openings, waterproofing, or gas and kitchen changes.

Mitigation: Verify those decisions with licensed professionals, property management, and local code authorities before acting.

Risk: The skill includes out-of-scope health or environmental topics such as formaldehyde and may contain overly absolute construction guidance.

Mitigation: Treat health and environmental claims as informal opinion and confirm them with qualified medical, environmental testing, or construction experts.
