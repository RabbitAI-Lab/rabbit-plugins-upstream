## Description: <br>
采购人/采购代理机构编制政府采购文件时，合法落实中小企业扶持、绿色采购、特殊群体视同等政策红利，输出可直接粘贴的条款模板与合规红线。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Government procurement buyers and procurement agencies use this skill to draft compliant Chinese government procurement policy-benefit clauses, including SME set-asides or price preferences, green procurement, special-group equivalents, and related red-line checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafted clauses may cite procurement rules or local policies that are outdated or mismatched to the project. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited rules, article numbers, and local policy currency against authoritative sources before using final procurement language. <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on generated procurement clauses without professional review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting assistance and have procurement or legal reviewers confirm final clauses before publication. <br>
Risk: Incomplete source documents or missing project details may lead to an incorrect policy-benefit path. <br>
Mitigation: Provide complete procurement documents and required project slots; do not treat missing policy sections as proof that no policy benefits apply. <br>
