## Description: <br>
Write a board pre-read that summarizes the business, metrics versus plan, recent changes, decisions and asks, and risks so board meetings focus on decisions rather than status. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Executives, founders, operators, and staff use this skill to turn company updates into a concise board pre-read for advance review. It helps surface metrics versus plan, wins, misses, explicit board decisions or asks, and risks in a standalone document. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Board pre-read inputs can contain sensitive board and company information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only appropriate company materials, limit sharing to intended reviewers, and review the generated pre-read before circulation. <br>
Risk: A generated pre-read may omit context or frame metrics and risks in a way that does not match the source materials. <br>
Mitigation: Check the TL;DR, metrics versus plan, decisions, asks, runway, and risk sections against the underlying company data before sending. <br>
