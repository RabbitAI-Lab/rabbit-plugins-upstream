## Use Case: <br>
External travelers and travel-support agents use this skill to answer China outbound visa questions, generate destination-specific application checklists, and summarize recent visa policy changes. It is intended for general planning guidance, not final legal or consular advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Policy-update queries may leave the platform for external search or proxy-backed lookup. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for general visa requirements and checklists, and avoid entering passport numbers or sensitive identity details. <br>
Risk: Visa policies can change and skill output may be incomplete or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify final requirements with official consulate or immigration sources before travel or application submission. <br>
