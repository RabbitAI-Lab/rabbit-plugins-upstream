## Description:

TRIZ Innovation Pro assists with TRIZ-based innovative solution analysis through system component analysis, contact relationship analysis, functional modeling, causal chain analysis, and innovation solution generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and product teams use this skill to structure technical innovation work with TRIZ methods, identify root causes, select intervention points, and generate concept and detailed solution proposals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Technical problem descriptions, generated analyses, and selected solution details may contain sensitive proprietary design information.

Mitigation: Do not provide trade secrets, regulated data, or sensitive proprietary design information unless the organization allows that processing by the TRIZ analysis tools.

Risk: Generated TRIZ analyses and solution proposals may be incomplete, incorrect, or unsuitable for a specific engineering context.

Mitigation: Review proposed analyses and solutions with qualified engineering and product stakeholders before relying on them for design, patent, or implementation decisions.
