## Description:

Automates integration tests of the ClawHub skill lifecycle by publishing, inspecting, updating, and deleting a test skill through the REST API.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[yinwuzhe](https://clawhub.ai/user/yinwuzhe)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this temporary test skill to check the ClawHub skill lifecycle by publishing, inspecting, updating, and deleting a disposable skill they control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lifecycle testing can update or delete a ClawHub skill if the wrong slug is targeted.

Mitigation: Use a disposable skill you control and verify its slug before updating or deleting it.
