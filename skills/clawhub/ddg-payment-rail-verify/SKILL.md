# DDG Payment Rail Verify

Payment code rots quietly: an SDK bump changes the signature shape, a wallet
rotation breaks the payTo, a seller's 402 envelope drifts off-spec. This workflow
proves the rail works and then keeps proving it on a schedule.

**Endpoints:** `POST /v1/tx-smoke-test` ($0.01), `POST /v1/402/preflight` ($0.01),
`POST /v1/x402/trust-probe` ($0.01), `POST /v1/receipt-verify` (**free**), all on
`https://agents.daedalusdevelopmentgroup.com`.

**External cost:** $0.01 per call in USDC on Base via x402; receipt
verification is free and the smoke test is free for the first 25
calls/day per identity. No account or API key is required; the paid path
needs an x402-capable client (wallet signer).

**Free tier:** `/v1/tx-smoke-test` is a funnel route — the first 25 calls/day per
`X-Agent-Id` are free, which makes it a zero-cost canary until you want the
receipt-only paid path. Send `X-DDG-Free-Tier: skip` when you specifically need
the paid 200 + `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` receipt.

## Procedure

1. **Paid smoke test** — prove your client can complete a real settlement and get
   a receipt header back:
   ```bash
   curl -s -D out/smoke.headers -X POST https://agents.daedalusdevelopmentgroup.com/v1/tx-smoke-test \
     -H 'content-type: application/json' -H "X-Agent-Id: $DDG_AGENT_ID" \
     -H "X-PAYMENT: $SIGNED_X402_PAYMENT" -d '{"request_label":"canary"}' > out/smoke.json
   grep -i '^payment-response\|^payment-receipt' out/smoke.headers
   ```
   A settled call returns `200`, `Payment-Receipt: x402-…` and a base64
   `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` naming the transaction. Missing `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` means a
   strict buyer SDK will treat the payment as failed — fix that before go-live.
2. **Verify the receipt** (free, no payment): POST the receipt hash to
   `/v1/receipt-verify` and confirm it resolves to your payment.
3. **Pre-flight any endpoint you are about to depend on** — the preflight probes
   a URL's 402 envelope and verdicts it against v1/v2 + MPP shape rules:
   ```bash
   curl -s -X POST https://agents.daedalusdevelopmentgroup.com/v1/402/preflight \
     -H 'content-type: application/json' -H "X-Agent-Id: $DDG_AGENT_ID" \
     -d '{"url":"https://seller.example.com/paid-route"}' > out/preflight.json
   ```
   Read the verdict list: `accepts[]` present, network format, atomic amounts,
   payTo. A seller that fails preflight will burn your settlement attempts.
4. **Trust-probe the seller** — challenge health, latency, and on-chain
   settlement history (distinct payers, recent settle tx):
   ```bash
   curl -s -X POST https://agents.daedalusdevelopmentgroup.com/v1/x402/trust-probe \
     -H 'content-type: application/json' -H "X-Agent-Id: $DDG_AGENT_ID" \
     -d '{"url":"https://seller.example.com/paid-route"}' > out/trust.json
   ```
   A single-payer, zero-recent-settle endpoint is a settlement risk, not a bargain.
5. **Schedule the canary** — weekly (or after every wallet/SDK change):
   smoke test + receipt verify are the minimum; add preflight/trust-probe for each
   seller you pay.
6. **On 402** — the smoke test is paid by design once the allowance is used: take
   `x402.accepts[0]` (network `eip155:8453`, asset USDC, atomic `amount`), sign,
   and retry the identical body. `upgrade_path` in the body repeats this.

## Failure handling

- `402` with `payment_rejected` → read `payment_rejected.reason`: `settlement_failed`
  means no funds moved, `settlement_pending` means a tx is in flight and the
  identical retry reconciles instead of re-charging. Never retry a changed body
  against a pending settlement.
- `409 payment_identity_mismatch` → the settled payment belongs to another
  identity; retry with the paying identity, or pay again from the new one.
- `409 spend_limit_exceeded` → your `X-DDG-Max-Spend-USD` header is below the
  route price; raise it deliberately rather than removing it.

## Not for

- Not a substitute for reconciling your own on-chain payments; it verifies the
  rail and the receipts, not your accounting.
- Not for buyers who cannot sign x402 payments at all — use the free receipt-verify
  and preflight routes first to establish what you need.

## Example

**Ask:** "Our settlement failed twice last week. Prove the rail works and set up
something that would have caught it."

**Before:** payments tested by hand before launch, then untested for months; the
first sign of breakage is a failed purchase in production.

**After:** a weekly canary that settles $0.01, verifies the receipt for free and
alerts on any change in the 402 envelope or receipt headers — plus preflight and
trust-probe verdicts for every seller the agent pays.
