# Synthetic Example: Condensed Decision Packet

## Decision

- Decision question: Whether to move two teams immediately, run a bounded operating intervention first, or gather more evidence.
- Accountable human owner: VP Product & Engineering.
- Required reviewer: People Partner.
- Status: Provisional.

## Evidence

Verified:
- Two releases were missed; documented external dependencies accounted for 18 of 27 delayed days. [Source A]
- Delivery predictability was 71% against an 85% target. [Source B]
- De-identified role clarity declined 16 points with a 74% response rate. [Source C]
- Reporting-line changes require People Partner review and executive approval. [Source F]

Statements requiring corroboration:
- The VP believes escalation and cross-functional influence are weak. [Source D]
- The manager reports repeated priority changes and unclear decision rights. [Source E]

Missing:
- Specific examples and timing of late escalation.
- Decision-rights map and priority-change log.
- Expected benefits, transition cost, and success measures for the proposed reporting change.
- Evidence about team-level performance beyond the two releases.

## Options

1. Reorganize now.
   - Benefit: immediate clarity if the diagnosis is correct.
   - Risk: disruption and unfairly attributing system delays to one manager.
   - Review: requires People Partner and executive approval.

2. Run a 30-day operating intervention before deciding.
   - Actions: clarify Product/Engineering decision rights, establish escalation thresholds, freeze or log priority changes, and review delivery weekly.
   - Benefit: tests competing explanations with reversible changes.
   - Risk: delays structural action if the leadership gap is already well established.

3. Gather evidence only.
   - Benefit: reduces premature attribution.
   - Risk: allows current delivery problems to continue without intervention.

## Bounded Recommendation

Provisional recommendation: choose Option 2 while collecting the missing evidence. The current packet supports a real delivery and role-clarity problem, but does not isolate the manager as the primary cause strongly enough to justify an immediate reorganization.

The VP and People Partner retain the decision.

## Next Action and Receipt

- Owner: VP Product & Engineering.
- Next action: approve a 30-day intervention charter with measures and review date.
- Prohibited agent actions: change reporting lines, communicate a decision, update systems, or assess the named manager.
- Sources used: A-F.
- Human decision status: pending.
