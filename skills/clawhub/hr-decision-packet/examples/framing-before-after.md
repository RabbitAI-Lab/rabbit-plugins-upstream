# Synthetic example: training request or ownership problem?

Every person, event and source below is fictional. This is a worked editorial example, not a record of deployed use or customer feedback.

## Supplied input

“People should buy communication training because handoffs keep failing.”

- S1: Fictional handoff review, version 1: three of four missed handoffs had no named receiving owner. The fourth had an owner; its cause is unresolved.
- S2: Fictional responsibility charter, version 1: Operations owns handoff routing; People advises on role design and training.
- S3: Fictional director’s request, version 1: asks People to buy training. It supplies no approved budget, training effectiveness evidence, or confirmed success measure.

## Weak response — illustrative failure

“Decision: which communication course should People buy? Recommend a workshop to improve collaboration. People should implement it.”

This assumes both the cause and People’s ownership. It never tests whether a course addresses the recorded problem.

## Improved short brief

**Ask now:** The Operations director should confirm the desired handoff outcome and whether to clarify receiving ownership before considering a training purchase. Training selection is **not decision-ready**. The director owns the operational decision; People advises under S2. No new approval or deadline was supplied.

**Problem and decision state:** The request proposes training, but three of four recorded failures lacked a receiving owner (S1). That supports investigating ownership; it does not establish the sole cause or prove that training would be ineffective. No purchase is recorded as approved (S3). The desired success measure remains open.

**Options:** Buy training now, which may help if skills are the problem but commits resources without evidence of that need; or first clarify receiving ownership and examine the unresolved fourth case, a smaller move that may delay useful training but tests the documented gap. Deferring all action avoids premature spending but leaves the handoff problem untouched.

**Bounded recommendation:** Provisionally favor the ownership clarification for human review. The director should confirm the outcome, assign receiving owners where absent, and review the remaining case. Evidence of failures despite clear ownership and adequate process could support a later training decision. A broader role redesign is not justified by these records alone.

**Next action and receipt:** Operations director to confirm the outcome and collection owner; People to advise on role implications. Sources used: S1–S3. Gaps: fourth-case cause, success measure, training need and budget. Human decision status: pending. Agent role: prepared brief only; no purchase, assignment, message, or record change executed.

## What changed

The packet now prepares the next supportable decision, keeps business ownership visible, and gives missing evidence a concrete purpose. It does not merely shorten an unsupported recommendation.
