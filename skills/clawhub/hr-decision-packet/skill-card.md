## Description:

HR decision briefs clarify the problem, ownership, and evidence for one accountable human decision rather than a weekly roundup.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mwclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/mwclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR business partners, People leaders, managers, and advisors use this skill to prepare source-backed briefs that clarify a people issue, compare options, and identify the accountable human decision-maker. It does not make or execute employment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive employee information may be exposed in the input or brief.

Mitigation: Use de-identified or minimum necessary HR information and omit unrelated sensitive details.

Risk: An unsupported brief could be mistaken for an authorized employment decision.

Mitigation: Keep evidence gaps and decision status visible; require a qualified HR, legal, or business owner to decide and communicate through authorized systems.
