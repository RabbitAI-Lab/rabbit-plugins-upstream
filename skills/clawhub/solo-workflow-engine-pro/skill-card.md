## Description: <br>
Solo Workflow Engine provides a seven-step no-code automation framework for independent developers, solo companies, and freelancers to identify automation opportunities, choose tools, design and test workflows, maintain automations, handle advanced scenarios, and calculate ROI. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and solo operators use this skill to plan and evaluate no-code automation workflows for recurring business tasks such as lead capture, onboarding, content distribution, invoicing, monitoring, troubleshooting, and ROI analysis. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill declares read, write, and exec tool use even though its primary behavior is automation-planning guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Install with the narrowest practical permissions, require previews and explicit approval before shell execution, and grant broad write or exec access only when a specific workflow requires it. <br>
Risk: Automation guidance can cause users to connect business tools or run examples without enough validation. <br>
Mitigation: Test workflows with non-production or sample data first, review field mappings and error handling, and monitor workflow logs after enabling automations. <br>
