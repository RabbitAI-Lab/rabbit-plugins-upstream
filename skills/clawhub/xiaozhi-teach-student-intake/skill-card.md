## Description:

Helps independent teachers plan diagnostic trial lessons, document observations, and create minimal student profiles with guardian consent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to interview families, assess a prospective student in a trial lesson, draft feedback for teacher review, and create a pseudonymous student profile and initial course-package record after consent. It does not send messages or handle scheduling, renewals, or post-lesson records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China Mainland; localize curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profiles and trial observations concern minors and may be stored or shared without valid consent.

Mitigation: Verify guardian consent and sharing permissions before creating or sharing a profile; retain only the minimum pseudonymous teaching information.

Risk: Trial records may outlive their intended retention period.

Mitigation: Check retention and deletion settings, including the 90-day deletion window for trials that do not convert.

Risk: China Mainland curriculum, consent assumptions, and crisis resources may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Review local requirements and replace region-specific teaching and crisis guidance before deploying outside China Mainland.
