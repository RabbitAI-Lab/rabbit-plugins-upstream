## Description:

帮助独立教师以诊断式试听了解新学员的学习需求，并在确认监护人同意后建立最小化的学员档案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to plan a diagnostic trial lesson, capture learning observations and needs, and create a consent-based student profile. Parent-facing follow-up drafts require teacher review before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize crisis resources, consent assumptions, curriculum references, and field values before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student onboarding records concern minors and could expose personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian consent, use aliases, omit names and contact details, and review records before saving.

Risk: Crisis guidance and consent assumptions may not fit regions outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize crisis resources, age-related consent assumptions, curriculum references, and field values before use elsewhere.

Risk: Course-package or follow-up drafts may be unsuitable for a particular family.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review drafts before saving or sharing them.
