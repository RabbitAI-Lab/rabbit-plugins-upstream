## Description:

帮助独立教师设计诊断式试听、记录学习观察，并在获得监护人同意后以化名建立最小化学员档案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to interview families, plan and assess a trial lesson, draft teacher-reviewed follow-up messages, and create consent-based student intake records and initial lesson packages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (designed for mainland China K–12 teachers)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student intake records concern minors and may contain sensitive learning observations.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian consent before saving, use pseudonyms, and keep real names and contact details outside the skill.

Risk: Incorrect initial package counts could affect business records.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review package totals before writing them.

Risk: A single trial lesson offers limited evidence for long-term conclusions.

Mitigation: Record observed facts, label provisional findings, and have the teacher review any assessment.
