## Description:

帮助独立教师通过需求访谈和诊断式试听了解新学员，并在确认监护人同意后建立最小化学员档案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers working with Chinese K–12 learners use this skill to plan and record diagnostic trial lessons, discuss learning needs, and create consent-based student profiles. It does not schedule lessons or contact families directly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China; localize and review applicable requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Minor student records may be saved without informed guardian consent or contain identifying details.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian consent before saving; use pseudonyms and exclude real names and contact details.

Risk: The Chinese K–12 intake workflow may not fit other locations or local privacy and education requirements.

Mitigation: Use for the intended Chinese independent-teacher context, or localize and review applicable requirements before use.

Risk: Initial course-package and availability fields could collect more learner information than necessary.

Mitigation: Review these fields against privacy requirements and record only necessary schedule windows and package details.
