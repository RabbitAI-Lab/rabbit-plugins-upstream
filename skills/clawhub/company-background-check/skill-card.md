## Description:

Produces source-backed company background reports from procurement records, including bidding history, customer relationships, competitors, and publicly reported risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhiliaobiaoxun](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhiliaobiaoxun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business users assess a prospective customer, supplier, partner, or competitor using a single-company report or a two-company comparison grounded in procurement records and public sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account setup uses an API key and may collect a device-derived identifier.

Mitigation: Use an existing key where possible; obtain consent before registration and protect the stored key.

Risk: Reports consume trial or paid credits.

Mitigation: Disclose expected credit use before starting and confirm before exceeding the planned budget.

Risk: Shareable HTML reports may expose signed source links and returned contact details.

Mitigation: Share reports only with the intended audience and review links and contact details before distribution.
