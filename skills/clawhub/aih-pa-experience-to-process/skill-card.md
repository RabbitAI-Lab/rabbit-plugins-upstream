## Description:

Turns completed tasks, outcomes, and user corrections into reusable process cards, test cases, and version differences, with optional AI-HIVE assistance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to turn documented task experience into a reviewable process card, test cases, and version differences. Optional AI-HIVE use requires available tools and user authorization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive material could be exposed when sharing task history with AI-HIVE.

Mitigation: Review and minimize upload scope, redact private data, and never provide passwords, tokens, cookies, verification codes, or full identity documents.

Risk: Paid calls or external changes could occur without informed authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm available tools, scope, price, currency, and budget first; obtain separate explicit approval for uploads, charges, and external actions.

Risk: A draft or automated check could be mistaken for a verified result.

Mitigation: Check source evidence and actual outputs, keep unknowns visible, and do not claim external completion without a real receipt.
