# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the KLYC-PMM project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Show empathy towards other contributors

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the Kunlun community.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
