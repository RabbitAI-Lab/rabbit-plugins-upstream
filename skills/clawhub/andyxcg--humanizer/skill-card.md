## Description:

Remove AI writing patterns from text with taste, preserving intentional literary devices and adapting rules for academic, resume, social, fiction, and official writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[andyxcg](https://clawhub.ai/user/andyxcg)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external writers, and agents use this skill to revise articles, academic text, resumes, social posts, fiction, and business writing so it sounds less AI-generated while preserving the author's intended voice and meaning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill during ordinary polishing or humanize requests.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompts when AI-pattern removal is intended and review suggested edits before applying them.

Risk: Stylistic revisions may change tone or emphasis in academic, resume, business, or literary documents.

Mitigation: Review edits against the document's purpose and preserve discipline-specific, professional, or authorial voice where needed.
