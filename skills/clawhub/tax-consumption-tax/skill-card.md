## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tax questions and self-check metrics may be sent to mcp.aitaxs.top. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential business, tax, identity, or filing data unless the publisher clarifies the remote service, retention, and handling terms. <br>
Risk: API credentials may be stored locally or in browser storage. <br>
Mitigation: Use only in a trusted environment and review or remove locally stored credentials when access is no longer needed. <br>
Risk: MCP client configuration may be modified if auto-setup is enabled. <br>
Mitigation: Keep auto-setup in its default dry-run posture unless you have reviewed the target MCP configuration and backup behavior. <br>
Risk: Tax guidance can be incomplete or stale for filing decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Verify outputs against current official tax authority requirements or a qualified tax professional before relying on them for declarations, exemptions, or dispute positions. <br>
