## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports that an unpinned latest CLI package could run with the user's permissions and access configured credentials.

Mitigation: Review the package and use a fixed, trusted version; keep credentials and execution permissions limited to what the task needs.

Risk: Search terms, URLs, or share text may be sent to SocialDataX.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive queries or links unless their disclosure to SocialDataX is acceptable.
