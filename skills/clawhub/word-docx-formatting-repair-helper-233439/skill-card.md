## Description: <br>
Helps users plan and produce document formatting, find-and-replace, style cleanup, automation, and verification support for Word or DOCX-like editing tasks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users such as office workers, legal and operations teams, students, and documentation maintainers use this skill to turn Word or DOCX formatting cleanup, style management, find-and-replace, and lightweight automation requests into actionable workflows, scripts, checklists, or verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger the skill on general productivity or editing prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for Word or DOCX formatting, style cleanup, find-and-replace, and related document workflows. <br>
Risk: Generated scripts, templates, or find-and-replace steps could unintentionally change document content or formatting if applied directly to important files. <br>
Mitigation: Apply suggested changes to a copy first, review the diff or final document, and verify the result against the stated success criteria before replacing the original. <br>
