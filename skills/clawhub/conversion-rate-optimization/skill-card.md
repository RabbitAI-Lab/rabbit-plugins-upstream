## Description: <br>
Audit a landing page or funnel step and produce a prioritised CRO test plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, product, growth, and optimization teams use this skill to audit landing pages, signup flows, checkout steps, and other funnel stages. It turns page goals, audience context, performance data, and known analytics signals into a prioritized CRO test plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow may involve page goals, audience details, performance data, analytics, session-recording observations, or survey signals that could contain sensitive customer or business information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide aggregated analytics and only the business context needed for CRO planning; omit unnecessary customer-level or personally identifiable data. <br>
Risk: CRO recommendations can be misleading if tests are run with insufficient traffic, no pre-set significance threshold, or no downstream guardrail metric. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's sample-size, duration, significance-threshold, and guardrail checks before launching tests, and use qualitative or sequential methods when traffic is too low for A/B significance. <br>
