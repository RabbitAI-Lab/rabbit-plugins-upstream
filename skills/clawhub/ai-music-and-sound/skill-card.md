## Description: <br>
Helps agents brief original or licensed music beds and sound design for social videos, choose safer licensed audio sources, and advise on licensing, Content ID, and AI disclosure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and social-media operators use this skill to score Reels, TikToks, Shorts, and other short-form videos with music and sound effects that fit the edit while reducing licensing, Content ID, and platform mute risk. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask an agent to review brand profiles and video assets to match audio to an edit. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the assets needed for the scoring task and review any generated brief before sending it to third-party audio services. <br>
Risk: Third-party music, stock-audio, platform, and AI-disclosure terms can change and may affect commercial use. <br>
Mitigation: Recheck the chosen service's current commercial terms for important work, use paid tiers when commercial rights are required, and keep license records. <br>
Risk: AI or licensed audio can still trigger Content ID claims or platform moderation. <br>
Mitigation: Save receipts and license evidence, document human contribution, apply required disclosures, and avoid copyrighted or trending music for brand content unless separately licensed. <br>
