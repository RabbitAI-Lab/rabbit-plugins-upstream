## Description: <br>
AI Music & Sound helps social creators brief original or licensed music beds and sound effects for short videos, choose safer licensed sources, and plan licensing, Content ID, and disclosure steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media creators and brand teams use this skill to plan background music and sound design for Reels, TikToks, Shorts, and other short videos. It guides the agent to match audio to the video's structure, prefer licensed commercial sources, and avoid unsupported legal-safety claims. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Licensing workflow guidance could be mistaken for legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current tool terms, use paid commercial licenses for brand work, keep receipts, and treat the skill's advice as workflow guidance rather than legal advice. <br>
Risk: Copyrighted or platform trending audio used without rights can create mute, claim, or legal exposure for brand videos. <br>
Mitigation: Use original or licensed audio sources, avoid copyrighted or trending platform music without explicit rights, and route native in-app audio decisions outside this skill. <br>
Risk: Content ID can still claim AI-generated or licensed audio. <br>
Mitigation: Keep proof of the tool license, document human contribution, and prepare to dispute claims with receipts. <br>
Risk: The agent could overstate that an AI music workflow is completely safe. <br>
Mitigation: State caveats clearly, including active music AI litigation, limited indemnification, and the instruction never to promise 100% legal safety. <br>
