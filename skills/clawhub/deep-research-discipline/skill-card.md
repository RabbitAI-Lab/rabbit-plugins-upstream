## Description:

Evidence-first workflow for multi-source deep research: requires cited sources, three-state confidence labels, cross-validation, and an independent review gate. Use when asked to research a topic in depth, verify claims across several sources, compare options, or produce a cited report. Tool-agnostic; needs only search, page fetch, and file read/write. Not for quick lookups, single-source summaries, or real-time data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Research assistants use this workflow to investigate multi-source questions and produce reports with traceable citations, confidence labels, explicit evidence gaps, and an independent review record.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cited, labeled research can still contain errors or coverage gaps.

Mitigation: State limitations explicitly, preserve unresolved claims, use the independent review gate, and seek domain expertise for consequential decisions.
