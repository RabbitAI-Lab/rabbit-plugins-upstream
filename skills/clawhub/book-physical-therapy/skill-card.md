## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to search for physical-therapy providers, check appointment availability, and create a booking through Lokuli's MCP server. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking submission sends appointment and contact details to the disclosed Lokuli endpoint. <br>
Mitigation: Before submitting a booking, confirm the provider, service, time slot, and exact contact details, and avoid sharing unnecessary medical details. <br>
