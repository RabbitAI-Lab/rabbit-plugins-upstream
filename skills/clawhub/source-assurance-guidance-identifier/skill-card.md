## Description:

Generates concise guidance on how many sources should agree before accepting a field and how to handle conflicts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People reconciling information from multiple sources use this skill to choose a source-agreement threshold and conflict-handling guidance for a field.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A short agreement rule may not capture the importance or reliability of individual sources.

Mitigation: Review the proposed threshold and conflict handling against the actual sources and applicable review policy before relying on it.
