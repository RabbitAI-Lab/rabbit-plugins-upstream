## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated or edited DOCX files may contain incorrect content or formatting issues. <br>
Mitigation: Render and review generated documents before sharing or relying on them. <br>
Risk: Package-manager commands such as pip, brew, or apt-get may change the local environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review dependency installation commands before execution and run them only in approved environments. <br>
Risk: The optional callback_url field could send workflow status outside the local machine if an implementation later supports it. <br>
Mitigation: Leave callback_url unset unless the destination is trusted and approved. <br>
