## Description:

Coordinates a 14-role software development team to route full-stack engineering work through requirements, design, implementation, quality review, delivery, and documentation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zengfr](https://clawhub.ai/user/zengfr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to coordinate full-stack projects, feature work, administration systems, refactoring, debugging, reviews, and documentation. It routes tasks across specialist roles and provides engineering guidance and handoff templates for supported technology stacks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
