## Description:

Use when planning lawn care timing and products: soil-temperature windows for pre-emergent crabgrass control, N-P-K fertilizer math from bag guarantees, seeding vs pre-emergent conflicts, and a region-adjusted annual calendar. Prevents the two classic mistakes — applying too late and over-fertilizing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and lawn-care practitioners can use the skill to plan seasonal lawn treatments, estimate pre-emergent timing, compare fertilizer quantities, and prepare a lawn-care calendar from grass type, location, lawn area, and product label information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags dangerous herbicide dose-unit errors and safeguard claims that the script does not enforce, which could lead to unsafe chemical advice.

Mitigation: Have the publisher correct the herbicide units and safeguards. Verify application quantities and seeding intervals against the exact product label before relying on the skill.
