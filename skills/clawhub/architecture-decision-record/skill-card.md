## Description: <br>
Create an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) for a technical decision, documenting context, options, the selected approach, consequences, risks, and tradeoffs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering teams use this skill to draft ADRs that capture why a technical decision was made, which alternatives were considered, and what consequences or revisit triggers should be tracked. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated ADRs may contain inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading reasoning if the provided decision context is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated ADRs for accuracy before treating them as authoritative team records. <br>
Risk: ADR drafting can involve sensitive internal architecture, operational, or business context. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive internal context unless it is appropriate for the workspace and intended audience. <br>
