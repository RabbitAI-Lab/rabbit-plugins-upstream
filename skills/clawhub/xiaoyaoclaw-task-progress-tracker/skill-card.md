## Description:

Tracks workspace tasks and projects through file-based progress cards, document indexes, and status summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dtsola](https://clawhub.ai/user/dtsola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users and developers use this skill to create and maintain task and project progress cards, attach document indexes, and review or close ongoing work in their workspace.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Attaching a document may move it from its original location, including when it contains sensitive information.

Mitigation: Preview source and destination paths and confirm the move before attaching documents.

Risk: Progress tracking creates persistent task, project, and memory notes in the workspace.

Mitigation: Review intended write locations and avoid recording sensitive details in progress or memory notes.
