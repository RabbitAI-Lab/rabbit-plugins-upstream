## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product Hunt ranking and platform rules can change after release. <br>
Mitigation: Review the advice against Product Hunt's current rules before using it in a launch plan. <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad launch or Product Hunt phrasing when a narrower advisory context is intended. <br>
Mitigation: Install or configure it for explicit Product Hunt launch requests when broad activation is not desired. <br>
Risk: Product Hunt may be a poor fit for consumer, education, app, game, or other non-maker audiences. <br>
Mitigation: Validate the target audience and channel fit before prioritizing Product Hunt over vertical communities, short-form video, or creator-led channels. <br>
