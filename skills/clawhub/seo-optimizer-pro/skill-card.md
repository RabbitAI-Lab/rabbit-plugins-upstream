## Use Case: <br>
Content marketers, SEO professionals, developers, and documentation teams use this skill to analyze existing HTML or plain-text content for readability, keyword density, technical SEO issues, and answer-engine optimization opportunities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Privacy documentation is contradictory about external processing, and analyzed content may be sent to the selected AI provider. <br>
Mitigation: Review the chosen provider's data retention and privacy terms before use, and test with non-sensitive sample content before analyzing confidential, regulated, proprietary, or customer content. <br>
Risk: Provider API keys are required and the selected provider may bill for usage. <br>
Mitigation: Use environment variables for provider keys, configure only the provider needed for the chosen model, and monitor provider-side usage and access controls. <br>
Risk: SEO and AEO recommendations may be inaccurate or may not improve rankings. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer validate recommendations before publishing content changes or using reports for business decisions. <br>
