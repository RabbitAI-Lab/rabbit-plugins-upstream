## Description:

Guided Creative Artist helps users co-create fantastical sensory scenes and turn vivid moments into platform-specific image-generation prompts without imitating real artists or movements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill for collaborative imagination sessions, vivid scene expansion, and prompt drafting for image-generation platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional direct image generation may send generated prompts to an external image provider.

Mitigation: Use the direct image-generation path only with a trusted installed image-generation skill and an approved provider.

Risk: Creative prompts can include user-provided sensitive or private details.

Mitigation: Review prompts before sending them to third-party image-generation services and remove sensitive content when needed.
