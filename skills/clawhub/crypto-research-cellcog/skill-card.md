## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to request crypto research, market analysis, DeFi due diligence, portfolio review, and strategy guidance through CellCog. It is intended as a research aid whose outputs should be checked against primary market, protocol, and on-chain sources before acting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may send precise or identifying portfolio information, wallet addresses, exchange account details, seed phrases, or private keys to CellCog while requesting crypto research. <br>
Mitigation: Use rounded percentages, synthetic examples, or coarse value ranges, and do not share seed phrases, private keys, wallet addresses, exchange account details, or other identifying information. <br>
Risk: Crypto research outputs can be stale, incomplete, or unsuitable for high-stakes financial decisions without independent verification. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill output as a research starting point and verify prices, on-chain data, protocol claims, tax assumptions, and smart contract risk with primary sources before acting. <br>
