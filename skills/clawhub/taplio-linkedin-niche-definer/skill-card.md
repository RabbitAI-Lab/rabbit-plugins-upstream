## Description:

Help the user define or sharpen their LinkedIn niche by clarifying their audience, problem, unique angle, and positioning, using Taplio profile and analytics data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

LinkedIn creators and professionals use this skill to narrow their audience, the problem they solve, and their unique point of view, then create a positioning statement and related content ideas. Taplio profile and analytics data ground the recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reads the user's Taplio profile and analytics.

Mitigation: Review connector permissions and use the skill only if comfortable sharing that Taplio data.

Risk: An optional positioning post draft may not reflect the user's intent.

Mitigation: Review and approve any draft, and keep publication under explicit user control.
