## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and lightweight analyses. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users such as solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building before major investment. It turns early product concepts into practical validation workflows, checklists, templates, analyses, prototypes, and positioning support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad triggers may invoke the skill for loosely related product, startup, validation, prototype, or SaaS requests. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing the plan or artifact. <br>
Risk: The demand score and linked evidence may be mistaken for independently verified market research. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the score and links as planning inputs, and ask users to verify market evidence directly before making investment decisions. <br>
