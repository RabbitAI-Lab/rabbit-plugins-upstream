## Description:

Generates Chinese HTML patent portfolio analysis reports for IPO diligence, M&A valuation, R&D strategy, litigation response, market entry, and competitive landscape work using Patsnap-sourced patent data and structured analysis modules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Patent analysts, IP strategy teams, and business stakeholders use this skill to produce Chinese patent portfolio diligence and strategy reports for companies, patent lists, technology domains, or competitor sets. The skill is intended for data-grounded reporting when authorized Patsnap MCP access is configured.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated patent, customer, competitor, and stakeholder information may be inappropriate or inaccurate if shared externally without review.

Mitigation: Review generated reports, verify Patsnap-sourced data, and confirm sensitive stakeholder or competitive claims before distribution.

Risk: Without authorized Patsnap MCP access, the skill cannot produce database-grounded patent conclusions.

Mitigation: Configure authorized Patsnap MCP access before relying on report findings; otherwise treat outputs as report structure and guidance only.
