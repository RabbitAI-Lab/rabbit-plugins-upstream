## Description:

Guides QA teams through selecting testing tools by comparing requirements, team constraints, costs, risks, and implementation plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, test leads, and engineering teams use this skill to evaluate and choose test automation frameworks, performance testing tools, and test management platforms. It produces comparison criteria, recommendations, POC validation plans, migration guidance, and risk notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce advisory vendor, pricing, or migration recommendations that affect budget or engineering commitments.

Mitigation: Independently validate vendor claims, current pricing, and migration impact before committing budget or engineering time.

Risk: The skill may read project documents while gathering requirements and constraints.

Mitigation: Use it only with project documents you are comfortable letting the agent read.
