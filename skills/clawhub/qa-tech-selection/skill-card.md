## Description:

Helps QA teams compare testing tools against project requirements, team capabilities, costs, and risks, then recommend a solution with a proof-of-concept and migration plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers and testing teams use this Chinese-language skill to evaluate automation frameworks, performance tools, or test-management platforms against project requirements and technical constraints. It produces a comparison, recommendation, risk assessment, and implementation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad tool-choice prompts may trigger this skill outside a testing-tool evaluation.

Mitigation: Confirm the request concerns QA or testing tools and clarify project requirements and constraints before comparing options.
