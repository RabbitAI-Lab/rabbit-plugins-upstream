## Description:

A Chinese-language agent skill that helps Web3 content creators, entrepreneurs, and community operators design a co-build community operations framework around contribution mapping, community incentives, and four-integration resource coordination.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, community operators, and Web3 entrepreneurs use this skill to turn users, fans, and ecosystem partners into long-term co-builders through a value proposition, contribution-to-rights mapping, sticky-tool design, and a four-integration operations matrix. It is educational community-operations guidance and should not be used as legal, financial, investment, token-issuance, fundraising, or smart-contract advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the community-operations framework as legal, financial, investment, token-issuance, fundraising, or smart-contract advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as educational guidance only and require current compliance review and specialist support for legal, financial, token, fundraising, or smart-contract topics.

Risk: Chinese Web3 community scenarios can involve fast-changing compliance expectations and jurisdiction-specific restrictions.

Mitigation: Keep deployment guidance non-financial, route compliance questions to specialist review, and avoid using the skill to approve token issuance or fundraising designs.
