## Description:

Guides agents through April Dunford’s positioning framework to identify a product’s best-fit customers, distinctive value, and a market frame where its strengths are clear.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams and marketers use this skill to clarify positioning when customers do not understand what a product does, messaging is not converting, or the market category needs reconsideration. It guides them through alternatives, unique attributes, customer value, best-fit segments, and a market frame.
