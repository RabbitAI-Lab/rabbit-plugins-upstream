## Description: <br>
Guides agents through April Dunford's Obviously Awesome positioning workflow for reframing products, clarifying competitive alternatives, mapping unique attributes to value, and aligning sales, website, and onboarding messaging. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Agents and go-to-market teams use this skill when a strong product is being misunderstood, conversion is weak, or the product needs a clearer category and competitive frame. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: External promotional or reference links may be optional context rather than required execution steps. <br>
Mitigation: Treat external links as supplemental and verify any referenced claims before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Positioning recommendations can mislead if applied without evidence about real customer alternatives and best-fit segments. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against customer research, competitive alternatives, and current sales or website messaging before adoption. <br>
