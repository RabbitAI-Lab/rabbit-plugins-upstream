## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and product teams use this skill to design and operate a Chinese-language legal-assistance product for contract review, clause explanation, legal RAG search, rights-protection guidance, prompt templates, disclaimers, and commercialization planning. The skill is a build guide and drafting aid, not a substitute for licensed legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Legal-assistance outputs may be incorrect, outdated, jurisdiction-specific, or mistaken for formal legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep jurisdiction, date of law, source citations, uncertainty language, mandatory disclaimers, and licensed-lawyer review in the workflow. <br>
Risk: High-stakes matters such as large contract value, criminal exposure, foreign parties, litigation, real estate, equity, or urgent harm require professional handling. <br>
Mitigation: Escalate these matters to a licensed lawyer or appropriate legal-aid channel before users rely on the output. <br>
Risk: A legal RAG knowledge base can become stale or cite unsupported authorities. <br>
Mitigation: Use source-traceable legal materials, show effective dates, avoid unsupported citations, and update the legal corpus on a defined cadence. <br>
