## Description: <br>
OPC Board uses five advisor perspectives to stress-test a solo project or one-person company idea across logic, deliverability, growth, viability, and risk, then produces a scored Go/No-Go feasibility report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, solo founders, indie developers, and project maintainers use this skill to evaluate whether a one-person company, side project, open-source project, or solo venture is feasible before investing more effort. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on the Go/No-Go result for legal, financial, medical, or other regulated ideas. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the report as advisory and obtain qualified review before making regulated, financial, legal, medical, or business-critical decisions. <br>
Risk: The intake flow asks for business idea details, goals, constraints, technical stack, and personal resources. <br>
Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the agent environment and redact confidential, personal, or sensitive business details before submission. <br>
