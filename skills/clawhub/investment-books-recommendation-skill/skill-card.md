## Description: <br>
为投资、商业和经济主题提供中文书籍推荐、阅读路径、单本介绍和中英文书目对照，并基于 aimunger 书籍资源梳理书目关联。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chenjing002](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenjing002) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Readers and agents use this skill to answer Chinese-language questions about investment reading, build themed reading paths, explain individual books, and compare original and Chinese titles. It is intended for investment, business, economics, management, and long-term thinking book-list workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad investing, business, or economics mentions. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for book-list and reading-path requests in those domains, and route unrelated investing or business tasks to a more specific skill. <br>
Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese even when another language would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the desired response language when the user asks in another language or needs bilingual output. <br>
Risk: The skill intentionally limits book facts and recommendations to the aimunger book resource. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose when a requested book or detail is outside that source instead of filling gaps from memory or other sources. <br>
