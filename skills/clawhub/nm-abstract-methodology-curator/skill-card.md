This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and skill authors use this skill to choose domain methodologies before creating or evaluating skills, hooks, or agents and to compare existing work against established frameworks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad design or evaluation language. <br>
Mitigation: Installers can narrow triggers or invoke it only when methodology selection or evaluation is explicitly needed. <br>
Risk: Curated methodology guidance may be incomplete or mismatched to a specific project context. <br>
Mitigation: Review selected frameworks against the current task, domain constraints, and source material before applying changes. <br>
