## Description:

Helps agents translate between Chinese and Uyghur, answer and write in Uyghur, translate documents, and recognize and translate text in images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[youteacher](https://clawhub.ai/user/youteacher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and agents working with Chinese and Uyghur use this skill for text and document translation, image OCR and translation, and Uyghur-language questions or writing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requested text, documents, or images are sent to the service or a configured alternative endpoint.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination and obtain consent before uploading files or sensitive content; check the service's data-handling terms first.

Risk: A shared platform API key may have broader access than a product-specific key.

Mitigation: Prefer the product-specific key for tighter isolation and do not share keys in conversations or logs.
