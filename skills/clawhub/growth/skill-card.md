## Description: <br>
Growth helps operators diagnose funnel constraints, choose and size growth loops and channels, compute CAC, payback, and LTV:CAC, and produce growth models, tracking plans, lifecycle maps, referral programs, and forecasts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Growth, product, and marketing operators use this skill to identify the constrained stage in a growth system, defend channel and budget decisions with unit economics, and turn durable growth work into reusable records. It is intended for funnel diagnosis, activation and retention work, channel portfolio decisions, lifecycle and referral design, marketplace, mobile, ecommerce, B2B, experimentation-program, and forecasting workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Persistent growth, budget, project, and contact records may be read or updated without clear per-action consent. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if this access is acceptable, run with review or confirmation for file changes, and keep backups of shared records. <br>
Risk: Durable records can carry sensitive business context across future sessions. <br>
Mitigation: Review the configured Clawic data paths before use and remove or redact records that should not persist. <br>
