## Description: <br>
The annual moment-planning skill maps seasonal, cultural, and commercial moments that fit a brand, gates them for fit and authenticity, tiers them by weight, and plans them in advance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External marketing teams and agents use this skill to choose the few seasonal or cultural moments that fit a brand, plan lead times, and feed a content calendar. It helps decide when to participate, when to route larger moments into campaign planning, and when to stay silent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Scheduled public posts can contain incorrect, outdated, or poorly fitting seasonal guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review planned posts before publishing and re-check time-sensitive dates for the target market. <br>
Risk: Brand and audience context may be used to make moment-selection recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only appropriate brand context and keep human review over authenticity, public messaging, and publishing decisions. <br>
