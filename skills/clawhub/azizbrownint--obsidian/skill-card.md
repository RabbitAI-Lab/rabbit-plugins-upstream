## Description:

Work with Obsidian vaults using the official obsidian CLI: read/search/create/edit notes, tasks, links, properties, plugins.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and knowledge workers use this skill to guide an agent in reading, searching, creating, editing, moving, and deleting notes in local Obsidian vaults through the official Obsidian CLI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide read, write, move, delete, plugin, and eval operations against local Obsidian vault content.

Mitigation: Give exact vault and path instructions for destructive or bulk actions, review proposed commands before execution, and keep backups or version control for important notes.
