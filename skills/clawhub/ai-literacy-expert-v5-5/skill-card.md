## Use Case:

Educators, students, and training teams use this skill to design AI literacy lessons, interactive p5.js courseware, immersive learning games, lesson-prep bundles, assessments, course recommendations, and collaborative planning assets. It is especially focused on BNBU/SAI programs and WorkBuddy-supported teaching workflows.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Teaching materials, assessment reports, and learning progress may be stored locally or synced to IMA/Tencent Docs.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before saving or syncing, and avoid student names, contact details, or other sensitive learner data.

Risk: Sharing, cloud saves, uploads, reminders, and calendar actions can affect real users or organizations.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit confirmation before sharing, saving to cloud, creating reminders or calendar items, or uploading results.

Risk: The security verdict recommends review before use in real classes or organizations.

Mitigation: Review the skill and generated teaching workflows before installation or operational classroom use.
