## Description:

Deep single-page SEO analysis covering on-page elements, content quality, technical meta tags, schema, images, and performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to audit a single public webpage and receive a scorecard, prioritized issues, concrete recommendations, and schema suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may fetch and analyze a URL supplied by the user, and optional DataForSEO tools may process SERP or backlink checks when available.

Mitigation: Use public pages or content the user is comfortable sharing with those tools.

Risk: Pages that are unreachable, authenticated, or heavily JavaScript-rendered can lead to incomplete analysis.

Mitigation: Report the limitation clearly and request a public URL or rendered HTML snapshot when needed.
