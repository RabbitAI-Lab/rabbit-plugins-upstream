## Description:

Finds live events and activities in New York City tailored to a person's plans, with times, prices, images, and booking links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liangstl](https://clawhub.ai/user/liangstl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and their assistants use SyncSo to find and plan New York City outings that fit their timing, location, interests, and budget.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

New York City, United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The account token and outing details, including location, preferences, accessibility needs, or budget, are sent to SyncSo.

Mitigation: Use only with the user's informed consent and include only details relevant to the outing; protect the account token.

Risk: Searches and detail requests can spend the user's SyncSo credits or allowance.

Mitigation: Explain the possible cost and limit calls or returned results to what the user needs.
