## Description:

Use when answering at a decision point - recommending an approach, reporting a verification result, finishing a task, proposing a fix, or assessing whether something is ready - so the judgment leads and the reader can evaluate it instead of only accepting it. Triggers on "brief mode", "bluf this", "give me the bottom line", "brief it", "stop burying the answer".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI agents use this skill when a user needs a recommendation, a verification report, a task handoff, or an assessment of readiness or risk. It structures the answer so the judgment and its supporting reasons are easy to evaluate.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Probability or confidence labels could overstate the evidence if used without a clear basis.

Mitigation: Follow the estimative-language reference: give a one-line basis for confidence and do not attach probability or confidence markings to observed facts.
