This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to triage CI Tools component support requests in GitLab, provide source-backed answers, and ask for the smallest safe detail needed when more context is required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill advises reopening locked support discussions without checking the moderation reason or maintainer authorization.

Mitigation: Before reopening a discussion, review why it was locked and confirm that the action fits the project's moderation process and is authorized by a maintainer.
