## Description:

Triage and answer CI Tools components catalog support issues and help requests in GitLab discussions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and users of the CI Tools components catalog use this skill to triage support issues and provide actionable, evidence-based replies with public references. It also helps identify requests that need a focused follow-up or maintainer review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may unlock an existing support issue without checking why it was locked or obtaining moderator approval.

Mitigation: Check the lock reason and obtain moderator approval before enabling an unlock; escalate when the reason or authorization is unclear.
