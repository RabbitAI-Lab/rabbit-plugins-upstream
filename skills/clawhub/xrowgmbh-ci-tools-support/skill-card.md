## Description:

Triage and answer CI Tools components catalog support issues and help requests in GitLab discussions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

CI Tools maintainers and support agents use the skill to triage support requests in GitLab discussions and provide actionable, source-linked answers while protecting private details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unlocking an existing discussion without checking its moderation decision or getting moderator approval may bypass moderation.

Mitigation: Confirm the moderation decision and obtain moderator approval before unlocking a discussion.

## Reference(s):

- [CI Tools component documentation](https://ci-tools.xrow.de/Components/<component>)

## Skill Output:

**Output Type(s):** [Markdown, Guidance]

**Output Format:** [Markdown support replies with source links, ordered checks, and a focused follow-up question when needed]

**Output Parameters:** [1D]

**Other Properties Related to Output:** [Actionable support guidance grounded in available public documentation and request details.]
