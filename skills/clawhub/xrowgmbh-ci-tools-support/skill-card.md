## Description:

Triage and answer support requests for the xrow-public/ci-tools GitLab components catalog.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Support teams and maintainers use this skill to triage eligible CI Tools GitLab support requests and answer with public, cited information or hand off requests outside scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Support replies or label changes could expose confidential customer details or affect public GitLab threads.

Mitigation: Scope GitLab permissions to the support workflow; review public replies and label changes, and keep private logs out of public responses.

Risk: Requests from unapproved domains could receive inappropriate support.

Mitigation: Configure SUPPORT_TRUSTED_DOMAINS and verify requester eligibility before responding.
