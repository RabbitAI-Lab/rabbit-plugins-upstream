## Description:

Check whether AI crawlers and search bots (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Googlebot, Google-Extended, bingbot, Applebot-Extended, CCBot) can reach a web page, and whether robots.txt or the server/firewall blocks each one. Use when someone asks why ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews cannot see, read or cite their site, asks to audit robots.txt for AI bots, or wants to know if their host or Cloudflare blocks AI crawlers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Site owners, SEO specialists, and developers can use this skill to check whether selected AI and search crawlers can reach a page and to distinguish robots.txt rules from server or firewall blocks. It helps diagnose why services such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews cannot see or cite a site.
