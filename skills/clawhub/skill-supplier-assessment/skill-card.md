## Description: <br>
Guides SQE and procurement quality engineers through supplier assessment in two modes: building a structured assessment plan or organizing an existing plan and supplier information into reference-only scoring dashboards. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
SQE and procurement quality engineers use this skill to standardize supplier assessment planning, structure supplier evidence, and create traceable reference scoring outputs for review discussions. The skill keeps final supplier judgments with the authorized evaluator. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Supplier scores and summaries may be mistaken for final approval or rejection decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Require review and confirmation by an authorized evaluator before using any assessment output. <br>
Risk: Sensitive supplier or business data may be provided during assessment preparation. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide data approved for assessment use and avoid unnecessary confidential supplier details. <br>
Risk: Report generation may depend on a referenced build script that is not included in the artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated report instructions as guidance and use manual output handling or a separately reviewed script when needed. <br>
