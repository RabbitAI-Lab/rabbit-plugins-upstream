## Description:

Searches public Douyin videos and images by keyword, retrieves trending topics and creator posts, and collects video comments for content analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[why20261](https://clawhub.ai/user/why20261)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and analysts use this skill to research public Douyin trends, compare creators, and analyze comments through structured search results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, Douyin links, and the API token are sent to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Use only when sharing those queries and credentials with www.guaikei.com is acceptable.

Risk: Local result logs may retain sensitive queries, comments, or account research.

Mitigation: Review and delete generated logs when researching sensitive topics.
