## Description:

Generates images from text prompts through Pollinations.ai and saves them locally.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[andrewkuster-bit](https://clawhub.ai/user/andrewkuster-bit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users generate images from text prompts for visual content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image prompts are sent to Pollinations.ai.

Mitigation: Do not include secrets, private personal data, or confidential business material in prompts.

Risk: Generated images are stored in the local OpenClaw media folder.

Mitigation: Use the skill only if local storage of generated images is acceptable.
