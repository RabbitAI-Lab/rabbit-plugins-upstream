## Description: <br>
Create a decision-ready Amazon product opportunity brief from a product idea, keyword, ASIN, or URL. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[techterrydev](https://clawhub.ai/user/techterrydev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to turn an Amazon product idea, keyword, ASIN, or URL into a structured opportunity brief that separates known facts, assumptions, unknowns, and validation steps. It supports early market investigation without claiming live market data or business certainty. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the brief as proof that a product will sell. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as planning support and independently verify current market data, costs, customer evidence, and launch assumptions before making business decisions. <br>
Risk: The brief may surface legal, IP, platform-policy, regulated-product, or claims questions that require qualified review. <br>
Mitigation: Escalate those questions to qualified human reviewers before taking legal, policy, sourcing, or marketplace action. <br>
