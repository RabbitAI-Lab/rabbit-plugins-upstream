## Description:

FitCheck guides users to EmoCity for on-device face and voice scans that summarize workout readiness, stress, mood, composure, and video or challenge results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to guide workout-readiness, recovery, stress, mood, and composure check-ins through the EmoCity browser app. The skill should frame outputs as self-insight or entertainment, not as medical, psychological, employment, relationship, or lie-detection evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage sensitive analysis of face, voice, stress, heart-rate, and deception-style signals for users or other people.

Mitigation: Use only with explicit consent from everyone recorded or analyzed, and frame results as self-insight or entertainment rather than evidence.

Risk: Challenge links and uploads may involve other people whose consent or context is unclear.

Mitigation: Avoid analyzing third-party media or sharing challenge links unless participants understand the purpose and limits of the scan.

Risk: Outputs could be mistaken for medical, psychological, employment, relationship, or lie-detection conclusions.

Mitigation: State that the app does not provide clinical, forensic, employment, relationship, or truthfulness determinations.
