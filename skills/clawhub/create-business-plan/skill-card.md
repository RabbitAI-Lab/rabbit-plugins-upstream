## Description: <br>
Create Business Plan helps agents produce investment-review-ready new-product business plans with data-integrity tagging, market and behavior validation, competitive analysis, market sizing, launch planning, unit-economics modeling, compliance checkpoints, and editable deliverables. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[swanwei](https://clawhub.ai/user/swanwei) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill to help founders, product teams, and business operators create, revise, or evaluate business plans for new product categories. It is especially suited to structured BP work that needs explicit assumptions, market validation, competitor matrices, TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, unit economics, compliance gates, and editable outputs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks agents to persistently modify the skill instructions after normal use. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before any SKILL.md edits and review proposed instruction changes before accepting them. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger language could cause the skill to activate for adjacent planning or document-editing tasks where it is not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow activation to explicit business-plan, product-launch, or investment-review requests. <br>
Risk: Feedback or project memory could become persistent instructions without sufficient review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat feedback and memory as evidence for review, not automatic instruction updates. <br>
