## Description: <br>
A free social media content planning assistant that helps individual creators draft content calendars, platform-adapted posts, content pillars, and hashtag strategies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Individual creators and small operators use this skill to plan social media publishing rhythms, generate weekly or monthly content calendars, adapt drafts for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, define content pillars, and build layered hashtag strategies. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated calendars, drafts, and hashtag strategies may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for a specific platform or audience. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit generated content before publishing, and verify platform limits, claims, links, and brand voice manually. <br>
Risk: The skill declares exec capability even though the release is primarily Markdown guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat filesystem actions, exports, and shell commands as proposals; review them before execution and avoid granting unnecessary write or network access. <br>
Risk: The free release does not include direct posting, analytics, team approval, or automated schedule export. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as copy-paste planning material and rely on separate reviewed tools for publishing, analytics, approvals, and calendar automation. <br>
