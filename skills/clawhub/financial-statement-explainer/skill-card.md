## Description: <br>
Explains P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statements in plain English for non-finance readers, highlighting the important line items, ratios, and overall story without providing financial advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, founders, managers, and operators use this skill to understand provided financial statements and the questions those statements answer. It turns supplied figures into an educational walkthrough, flags missing data, and points readers toward professional review for decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Readers may mistake an educational explanation for financial, investment, tax, or accounting advice. <br>
Mitigation: State that outputs are educational and direct decisions or compliance questions to a qualified accountant or advisor. <br>
Risk: Sensitive financial figures may be shared with the agent environment. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide financial information the user is comfortable processing in the agent environment. <br>
Risk: Missing or incomplete statement data can lead to misleading conclusions if values are invented or assumptions are hidden. <br>
Mitigation: Use only provided figures, mark missing values as unknown, and avoid computing ratios without the required inputs. <br>
