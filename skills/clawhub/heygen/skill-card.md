## Description: <br>
Heygen helps an agent plan AI avatar and talking-head videos by preparing lean scripts, setup briefs, avatar choices, localization plans, consent checks, disclosure plans, and human-review handoffs for HeyGen-rendered content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and social media operators use this skill to prepare scripted avatar videos, multilingual localizations, faceless explainers, and personalized sales clips. It guides the user toward consented avatars, AI disclosure, B-roll cutaways, and a render-review-publish workflow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Avatar and voice cloning can affect likeness rights and public trust. <br>
Mitigation: Use only consented people, stock avatars, or licensed talent; refuse non-consensual impersonation or implied endorsement requests. <br>
Risk: Undisclosed AI avatar content can mislead audiences or violate platform and regulatory expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Keep AI disclosure in the final video, using caption and/or on-screen labeling as appropriate. <br>
Risk: Rendered avatar content may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for publication. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review and editing before rendering final assets or scheduling publication. <br>
Risk: HeyGen product capabilities, API details, and pricing are volatile. <br>
Mitigation: Re-check HeyGen documentation and account settings before building integrations, quoting costs, or relying on a specific API schema. <br>
