## Description:

Optimizes websites for traditional SEO and AI search through keyword strategy, site architecture, on-page updates, structured data, internal linking, multilingual planning, and AI-citation-ready content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, content strategists, and developers use this skill to audit, plan, and rewrite website SEO/GEO assets so pages can rank for high-intent queries, become easier for AI systems to cite, and route qualified visitors toward conversion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read project positioning context such as market-profile.md when SEO or GEO work is requested.

Mitigation: Keep unrelated sensitive information out of market-profile.md and review generated SEO/GEO recommendations before publishing.

Risk: SEO, GEO, schema, and keyword recommendations can become inaccurate or misleading if applied without review.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against the target website, audience, current search requirements, and visible page content before deployment.
