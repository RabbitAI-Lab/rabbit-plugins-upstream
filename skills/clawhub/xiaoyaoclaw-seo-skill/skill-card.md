## Description:

Helps agents audit website search visibility, diagnose SEO issues, and propose or implement confirmed fixes for pages, content, structured data, and AI search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dtsola](https://clawhub.ai/user/dtsola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Website owners, SEO practitioners, and developers use this skill to audit a named public site or page, prioritize search visibility issues, and prepare fixes for review before changing website files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect SEO changes to robots.txt, sitemaps, canonical tags, structured data, or content may harm indexing and traffic.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation before edits and review and verify proposed production SEO changes before applying them.
