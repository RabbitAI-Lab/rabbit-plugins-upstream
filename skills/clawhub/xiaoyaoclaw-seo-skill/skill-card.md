## Description:

Audits website search visibility and recommends actionable improvements to technical SEO, page content, structured data, and AI search visibility.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dtsola](https://clawhub.ai/user/dtsola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Website owners, developers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to audit a named site or page, diagnose search visibility problems, and prepare prioritized fixes. It can suggest or, with approval, make SEO-related changes in the user's repository.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Changes to production SEO files or tags can disrupt crawling and indexing.

Mitigation: Review the proposed changes and their impact, approve edits before they are made, and verify them with a rollback path before production deployment.
