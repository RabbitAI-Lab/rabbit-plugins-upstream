## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Research outputs can include incorrect, outdated, or weakly sourced market and company claims. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important data with multiple sources, cite source dates, and label confidence and limitations in the final report. <br>
Risk: User research workflows can expose sensitive or unnecessary personal attributes. <br>
Mitigation: Process only data the user is allowed to use, avoid unnecessary sensitive attributes, and anonymize or aggregate interview and survey details. <br>
Risk: Optional company-data connectors and public page fetching can retrieve third-party business data with access or policy constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm connector authorization and data-use permissions before querying or including enterprise data in deliverables. <br>
Risk: Generated chart HTML can depend on CDN-hosted JavaScript libraries. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated HTML and external library references before sharing, publishing, or deploying chart files. <br>
