## Description:

Translate complete academic papers into searchable Chinese PDFs while preserving scholarly layout, equations, figures, tables, citations, footnotes, and appendices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vittta](https://clawhub.ai/user/vittta)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, translators, and developers use this skill to create complete Chinese editions of academic papers, with searchable text, preserved scholarly structure, and documented coverage and quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Translating a paper without permission may violate the source's rights.

Mitigation: Confirm the license or obtain authorization before transforming the full document.

Risk: Confidential manuscripts could be exposed if sent to an external translation service.

Mitigation: Keep processing local unless sharing with an external service is separately authorized.

Risk: Extraction, translation, or layout errors could omit or misrepresent scholarly content.

Mitigation: Review every source and output page, verify figures and numeric claims, and complete coverage and PDF audits before delivery.
