## Description:

Use when comparing student loan repayment paths (standard vs extended vs graduated vs income-driven), planning PSLF qualification, sizing extra payments, or projecting forgiveness tax bombs. Runs real amortization and discretionary-income math and ranks strategies by total cost and payoff date.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Student-loan borrowers and financial counselors use the skill to compare modeled repayment strategies, explore PSLF and forgiveness timelines, estimate tax exposure, and evaluate extra payments. Its figures are estimates, not tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial estimates may be inconsistent or outdated, affecting payment comparisons, eligibility, and forgiveness projections.

Mitigation: Independently verify calculations, eligibility, forgiveness dates, and current rules with authoritative sources before changing repayment plans.

Risk: Unnecessary disclosure of sensitive account information.

Mitigation: Provide only the financial figures needed for estimates; account credentials are unnecessary.
