## Description:

Uses Cue to research emerging industries, including market potential, competitive landscapes, business models, and common IPO and M&A compliance issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Investors, business analysts, and legal teams use the skill to explore unfamiliar emerging industries and identify potential IPO or M&A compliance questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill installs and runs externally maintained Cue code.

Mitigation: Review the runner before installing or updating it, especially in sensitive environments.

Risk: Research queries are sent to Cue and may contain confidential deal details.

Mitigation: Only submit confidential IPO or M&A information if Cue is approved for that data.

Risk: Cue API usage may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Confirm the expected cost and available credits before running research.
