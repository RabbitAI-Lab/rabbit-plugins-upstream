## Description: <br>
Wenchang Orchestrator coordinates multi-platform content workflows across research, review, platform adaptation, image generation, URL preparation, release checks, and reusable asset capture. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, operators, and agents use this skill to choose the right Wenchang workflow stage and coordinate assets for WeChat, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu Idea, blogs, or multi-platform publishing. It is most useful when a request needs research, review, image workflow planning, publish preflight, handoff state, and final human-gated publishing decisions kept aligned. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated workflow output could be mistaken for approval to publish, upload, rewrite URLs, or take account actions. <br>
Mitigation: Keep explicit human confirmation gates before uploads, URL rewrites, account actions, and external publication. <br>
Risk: Handoff packs, paid image generation, Computer Use prompt submission, or reference uploads may require judgment at action time. <br>
Mitigation: Review handoff packs and confirm manually before paid image generation, Computer Use prompt submission, or reference uploads. <br>
Risk: Unresolved claims, platform requirements, or hot tags may be inaccurate or stale. <br>
Mitigation: Pause when key claims lack evidence or when current platform requirements and hot tags need live verification. <br>
