## Description:

Plan evidence-led growth experiments for AI agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and growth teams use this skill to research public demand, map agent-product funnels, compare alternatives, and produce a prioritized experiment backlog for AI-agent distribution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public-source observations can be incomplete or time-bound.

Mitigation: Review citations, access dates, and assumptions before using the plan to make growth decisions.

Risk: Growth planning can become sensitive if private product data or credentials are provided.

Mitigation: Provide non-sensitive product context and avoid sharing API keys, tokens, cookies, private URLs, tenant identifiers, or personal contact lists.

Risk: A planning brief could be mistaken for authorization to execute campaigns or external changes.

Mitigation: Keep the skill read-only; require separate human approval and authorized tooling for posting, messaging, purchases, deployments, or record changes.
