## Description:

感通荣格（Gantong） is an AI emotional-support and depth psychology companion for reflective conversations about feelings, dreams, meaning, relationships, anxiety, emptiness, career crossroads, and life transitions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this agent for non-clinical emotional support, dream and symbol reflection, meaning exploration, relationship concerns, and life-transition conversations. It is not for emergencies, self-harm, suicidality, violence risk, psychosis, diagnosis, or replacing licensed mental-health care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake reflective emotional-support guidance for clinical mental-health care.

Mitigation: Keep the skill positioned as non-clinical support, preserve its no-diagnosis and no-treatment-substitution boundaries, and direct users toward licensed professionals for clinical needs.

Risk: Crisis, self-harm, suicidality, violence risk, or psychosis scenarios require support beyond this skill.

Mitigation: Use the documented crisis boundary: stop analysis, respond in clear reality-based language, and recommend emergency, local crisis, trusted-person, or professional support.

Risk: Depth-psychology interpretations can overstate certainty or encourage emotional dependence.

Mitigation: Use tentative language, offer reversible low-intensity exercises, avoid single explanations, and reinforce user agency and professional boundaries.
