## Description:

根据行程起终点、天数、驾驶时长、同行人员及偏好，拟定逐日自驾路线、休息补给安排和出行风险提示。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

汽车出行从业者和自驾旅行者用它拟定逐日行程、驾驶休息节奏、补给与住宿建议，并识别需要出发前核实的路况和天气风险。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated mileage, road conditions, weather, altitude, and safety guidance may be inaccurate or out of date.

Mitigation: Before travel, confirm the route and conditions with current navigation, official road and weather sources, and local guidance; adjust or postpone travel when conditions are unsafe.
