## Description: <br>
Livestream And Realtime helps agents plan livestream formats, run-of-show, pre-live promotion, human-run live engagement, and post-live repurposing for platforms such as Instagram Live, TikTok LIVE, YouTube Live, LinkedIn Live, and X. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, brands, and social media teams use this skill to plan a live event, schedule supporting promotion through WoopSocial, guide the human-run broadcast, and repurpose the recording into clips and replay content. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public promotional posts or repurposed clips could be scheduled with the wrong platform, timing, copy, or creative. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm platform, timing, copy, and clips before publishing through WoopSocial. <br>
Risk: Users may expect the agent or WoopSocial to broadcast, moderate comments, run live shopping, or report analytics directly. <br>
Mitigation: Keep broadcast, realtime engagement, live shopping, and analytics as human/native responsibilities. <br>
Risk: Livestream platform thresholds and feature availability can change. <br>
Mitigation: Re-check current platform requirements before relying on follower thresholds, scheduling windows, shopping features, or premium requirements. <br>
Risk: Viewer counts or engagement metrics could be overstated if invented or sourced from fake views. <br>
Mitigation: Use native platform analytics only and do not fabricate viewer numbers or buy fake live views. <br>
