## Description: <br>
Flux helps agents plan FLUX image generation and editing workflows by choosing an appropriate variant and license path, writing on-brand prompts and edit instructions, and routing human review before publishing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to choose the right FLUX path for brand image generation, write scene prompts or targeted edit instructions, and verify licensing, likeness, provenance, and rendered text before publication. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Connected FLUX provider APIs, WoopSocial publishing workflows, and brand or reference assets may involve external services or user-controlled resources. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm each connected provider, publishing workflow, and asset source is intended for the deployment before installing or using the skill. <br>
Risk: Commercial and self-hosted FLUX licensing terms can change or differ by variant. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill's licensing content as operational guidance and re-check current provider terms and variant rights before commercial or self-hosted use. <br>
Risk: Generated image workflows can create likeness, IP, provenance, disclosure, or rendered-text accuracy issues. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review of every render, avoid unpermitted likenesses or copied trade dress, preserve provenance metadata, apply required AI disclosures, and verify in-image text before publication. <br>
