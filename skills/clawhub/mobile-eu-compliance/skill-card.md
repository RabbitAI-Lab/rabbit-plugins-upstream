## Description:

Assesses Android, iOS, Flutter and connected-device companion apps against the EU Cyber Resilience Act, the Radio Equipment Directive delegated act with EN 18031-1, -2 and -3, and GDPR with ePrivacy: inventory, requirement mapping, evidence, gaps and a remediation plan in a gap report table. Engineering guidance, not legal advice. Use for CRA or EN 18031 readiness, SBOM and vulnerability handling, BLE or Wi-Fi companion apps, or GDPR reviews of mobile apps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and mobile product teams use this skill to assess an app or connected-device companion app against relevant EU cybersecurity and privacy requirements. It helps gather evidence, identify gaps, and prepare a remediation plan for review by the team's compliance or legal owner.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A review could overstate readiness if it uses an incorrect product scope or unverified legal requirements.

Mitigation: Limit the assessment to the intended app project, record unsettled scope questions, and have the compliance owner verify current legal sources.
