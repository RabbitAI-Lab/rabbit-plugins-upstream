## Description:

Scrapes public Instagram posts tagged at a specific location using apidojo's Instagram Location scraper on Apify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

## Use Case:

External teams, marketers, hospitality operators, event staff, and developers use this skill to collect public Instagram location-tagged posts, rank top content and creators, and prepare UGC reports or outreach lists for a venue, event, or city.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Location-based monitoring and creator profiling can affect privacy, consent, or safety, especially for sensitive venues.

Mitigation: Use only public Instagram location data for a legitimate business purpose, avoid sensitive venues and private-person tracking, and review creator lists before saving or sharing them.

Risk: Instagram location tags can be imprecise or refer to similarly named places.

Mitigation: Manually review top posts and location matches before using the results for outreach, reporting, or republication.

Risk: Repurposing user-generated content or contacting creators may create terms, permission, or policy issues.

Mitigation: Confirm Instagram terms, privacy law, and organizational policy, and obtain creator permission where required before reuse or outreach.
