## Description: <br>
Generates Chinese personal legal documents for labor arbitration, rental disputes, and consumer rights matters with structured templates, legal citations, claim calculations, evidence lists, and disclaimers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Individuals and agents use this skill to draft Chinese legal letters and applications for labor arbitration, rental disputes, and consumer complaints. It helps collect facts, calculate claims, cite relevant laws, prepare evidence lists, and include practical risk reminders. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill handles privacy-sensitive legal facts, identity details, account information, and evidence descriptions. <br>
Mitigation: Use placeholders or masked ID and account numbers until the final submission stage, and avoid sharing full identity documents, bank details, or evidence bundles in informal chat unless necessary. <br>
Risk: Generated legal documents and cited laws may affect important rights if submitted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Verify legal citations and calculations independently for important matters, and consult a qualified lawyer for high-stakes or complex disputes. <br>
Risk: Pro features use a paid third-party activation flow. <br>
Mitigation: Install and activate only if you are comfortable paying the third-party publisher and following the documented activation-code verification process. <br>
