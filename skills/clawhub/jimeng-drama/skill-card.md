## Description:

用即梦（Dreamina）官方 CLI 生成 9:16 竖屏 AI 短剧。当用户要做短剧、AI 短片、竖屏分镜视频、把剧本变成成片、或提到"即梦出片""dreamina 短剧"时使用。覆盖剧本拆解、角色定妆、逐镜生成、配音字幕、成片拼接全流程。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Creators and developers use the skill to turn a script into a vertical short drama, from shot planning and character setup through generated video, voice, subtitles, and final assembly.
