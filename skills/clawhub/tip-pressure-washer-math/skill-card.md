## Description:

Helps size pressure-washer nozzles, compare machine output, calculate chemical dilution, and plan washing for concrete, wood, vinyl, and cars.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and pressure-washing operators use this skill to compare washers, match nozzles to pump flow, calculate detergent mixtures, and choose surface-appropriate washing plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Excessive pressure or an unsuitable nozzle can injure people or damage surfaces and equipment.

Mitigation: Follow the washer manufacturer's instructions, wear appropriate PPE, test an inconspicuous area, and never spray people, animals, electrical fixtures, or fragile surfaces.

Risk: Incorrect chemical mixing or bleach routed through the pump can harm surfaces or damage equipment.

Mitigation: Follow chemical manufacturers' instructions, check dilution and injector settings, and keep bleach-based mixtures out of the pump.
