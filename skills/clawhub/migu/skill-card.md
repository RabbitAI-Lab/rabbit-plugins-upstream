## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to plan sports viewing on Migu Video, check China Mobile membership benefits before paying, and compare likely platform choices across Migu, Tencent, and iQIYI Sports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sports rights, membership prices, and China Mobile benefits can change frequently. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current platform availability, prices, and account benefits in official apps or current sources before purchasing. <br>
Risk: A user may treat the guidance as a static rights or membership list. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a decision process and confirm event rights and membership terms before making a viewing or payment decision. <br>
