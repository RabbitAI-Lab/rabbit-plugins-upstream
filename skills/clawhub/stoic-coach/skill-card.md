## Description:

Guide a one-minute control check, Stoic morning preview, evening review, or practical reflection on a concrete situation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who ask for Stoic reflection can use the skill to separate choices from outcomes and complete a brief daily practice or reflection on a specific situation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user could mistake philosophical reflection for mental-health treatment.

Mitigation: Describe the practice as reflection, not treatment, and encourage suitable professional or emergency support for serious concerns.

Risk: A practice log could expose personal emotional details.

Mitigation: Use only details the user chooses to share, and save a log only after explaining what will be saved and receiving confirmation.
