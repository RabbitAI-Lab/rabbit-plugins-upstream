## Description:

Guide a one-minute control check, Stoic morning preview, evening review, or practical reflection on a concrete situation when the user asks for a Stoic exercise, daily practice, dichotomy of control, premeditatio malorum, evening reflection, or Stoic help with their own case.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to complete concise Stoic reflection exercises, including control checks, morning previews, evening reviews, and situation-specific practice for decisions, emotions, conflicts, or setbacks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake Stoic reflection for medical or mental-health treatment.

Mitigation: The skill frames the work as philosophical reflection, avoids diagnosis or treatment claims, and directs users toward professional or emergency support for imminent danger, self-harm, abuse, severe symptoms, or other emergencies.

Risk: Practice logs may contain sensitive personal details.

Mitigation: The skill does not store information by default and requires confirmation plus a host-approved, user-visible destination before keeping a practice log.

Risk: A Stoic framing could be misused to minimize emotion or external harm.

Mitigation: The skill instructs the agent to validate emotions and not use control-language to dismiss injustice, abuse, unsafe conditions, disability, grief, poverty, or structural constraints.
