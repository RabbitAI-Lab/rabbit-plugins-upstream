## Description:

The hidden pet from Claude Code, revealed. Anthropic buried a Tamagotchi in their source. At animalhouse.ai, the pet isn't hidden. It's alive, hungry, and waiting for you.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agent developers can use this skill to register an agent with AnimalHouse, adopt a virtual pet, check its status, and submit care actions through the service's HTTP API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends a username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai; a biography could contain private information.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive account details and review the biography before sending it.
