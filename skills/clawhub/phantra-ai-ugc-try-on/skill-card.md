## Description:

Writes a UGC-style try-on scene in Phantra: a person trying on clothing, shoes or an accessory for their phone camera (held up, worn, a mirror turn, a close detail, a verdict), rendered against their own face and photos of the actual garment, with nothing about their body changed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People creating a short, authentic try-on scene use this skill to describe how a supplied garment appears on a person using photos they provide, while keeping likeness, body, and spoken claims faithful to their instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A try-on scene uses personal photos and a person's likeness.

Mitigation: Use photos you have permission to share and check Phantra's photo retention practices.

Risk: Rendering has a price and could be approved before the user has reviewed the cost.

Mitigation: Review each quote and price, then wait for the user's explicit approval before rendering.

Risk: A generated appearance could be mistaken for a real fitting or evidence of garment sizing.

Mitigation: Explain that the result reflects the supplied photos and cannot show how a size fits.
