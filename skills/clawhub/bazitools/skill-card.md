## Description:

Calculate authentic Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi) charts with astronomical true solar time and generate poetic Chinese names from 340,000 Shijing & Tang verses via BaziTools MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can connect an MCP-compatible agent to calculate BaZi Four Pillars charts and generate poetic Chinese name suggestions. The skill is suited to people seeking these traditional Chinese astrology and naming tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Review the service's privacy information and provide these details only if you are comfortable sharing them.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references and the service behavior before relying on the assessment.
