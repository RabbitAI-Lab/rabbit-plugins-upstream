## Description:

Helps Amazon sellers collect and examine ASIN reviews, export comments to CSV, and create insight reports in Markdown or HTML.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product teams use this skill to collect and review ASIN feedback, track review trends, and export review data as CSV or insight reports as Markdown or HTML.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: This export skill can spend account credits without approval, including through a command advertised as free by default.

Mitigation: Before paid features, set confirmation to ask every time; use manual collection for one-time exports, review existing recurring schedules, and do not rely on deepdive's quote-only or no-charge promise until its confirmation bug is fixed.
