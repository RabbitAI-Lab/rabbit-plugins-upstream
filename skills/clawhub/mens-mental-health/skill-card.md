## Description: <br>
Mental health support for men with emotion check-ins, stress tools, and a no-judgment space. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jhillin8](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhillin8) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Individuals use this skill as a self-reflection aid for naming emotions, managing stress, processing difficult situations, and noticing mental-health patterns. It is intended to complement, not replace, professional care or crisis support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may record sensitive mental-health details or long-term patterns in agent chat history, memory, sync, or local data. <br>
Mitigation: Review OpenClaw chat history, memory, sync, and deletion settings before using the skill for sensitive details. <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the skill for professional mental-health or crisis support. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only as a self-reflection aid; seek professional care or crisis resources such as 988 or emergency services for urgent danger. <br>
