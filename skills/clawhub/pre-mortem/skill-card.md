## Description:

Helps teams anticipate how a proposed plan, launch, or migration could fail, rank specific causes, and decide on mitigations and go/no-go conditions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams planning launches, migrations, and other consequential changes use this skill to identify plausible failure scenarios, prioritize warning signs and mitigations, and make a go/no-go decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An incomplete or outdated plan may lead to missed failure scenarios or misleading priorities.

Mitigation: Review current plan materials, dependencies, and past incidents before ranking causes; validate warning signs and ownership with the team before acting.
