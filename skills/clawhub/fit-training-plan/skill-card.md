## Description:

Creates tailored weekly fitness plans with exercise substitutions, progression rules, warm-ups, tracking tables, and recovery guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fitness practitioners and individuals use this skill to draft weekly training plans adapted to goals, available time, equipment, experience, and physical limitations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A general workout draft may be unsuitable for injuries, cardiovascular conditions, or post-surgery training.

Mitigation: Seek assessment from a qualified professional before training with these conditions; do not treat the plan as medical advice.

Risk: Tailoring a plan can involve sensitive health details.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum health information needed to adapt the workout.
