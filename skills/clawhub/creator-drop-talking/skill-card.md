## Description:

Turn a confirmed new-drop script and authorized stills into one drop talking clip per still. This product drop video studio writes a speakable launch talking clip and new-drop announcement for each photo, then animates a 2 to 15s talking teaser. Use it for creator drop videos and talking teasers that stay one photo, one clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, sellers, and listing teams use this skill to turn confirmed product-drop facts and authorized still images into one short talking teaser per still. It guides the agent through consent checks, speech generation, image-to-video animation, billing confirmation, and delivery of generated media artifacts.

Global
