## Description:

Use when the user needs to continuously discover business idea candidates from real external signal sources. Focus only on discovery and collection, without judging the value or feasibility of the ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dreamcoin1998](https://clawhub.ai/user/dreamcoin1998)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, product teams, and researchers use this skill to collect business idea candidates supported by real external signals. It organizes each candidate by problem, target user, current solution, supporting signal, and source without judging feasibility or market value.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Discovery candidates are not assessed for feasibility or competitive viability and may not be actionable opportunities.

Mitigation: Verify the supporting sources and assess feasibility and competition separately before pursuing a candidate.
