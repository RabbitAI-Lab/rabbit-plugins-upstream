## Description:

Helps agents write, polish, fact-check, and cite Chinese-language articles on Chinese history, literature, and culture while preserving the user's structure and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writing agents use this skill to turn user-provided Chinese history, literature, or culture outlines and drafts into polished Chinese articles with researched citations. It is also used to fact-check drafts and flag unverifiable or conflicting claims before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research or citation mistakes could introduce incorrect or misleading historical or literary claims.

Mitigation: Review the generated citations, factual corrections, and flagged unverifiable claims before publishing.

Risk: The skill may create corrections documents or update README indexes in a compatible writing repository.

Mitigation: Inspect file changes before accepting or publishing them.
