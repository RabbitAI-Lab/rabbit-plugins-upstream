## Description:

Chinese owned-channel customer operations toolkit for drafting WeChat Moments, group messages, welcome scripts, WeCom acquisition flows, diagnostics, market research, customer records, and follow-up navigation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External business operators and consultants use this skill to plan and produce Chinese private-domain customer operations work, including content, outreach, diagnostics, evidence-based vendor research, and customer follow-up records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with Chinese-language workflows focused on WeChat and WeCom private-domain operations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release evidence flags review-worthy risk from a self-update path that can change installed skill behavior.

Mitigation: Use the update command only when the publisher is trusted and global skill changes are acceptable.

Risk: The release evidence flags persistent plaintext customer records as a security risk.

Mitigation: Avoid saving passwords, API keys, cookies, or highly sensitive customer data, and keep local skill record folders out of synced or shared locations.
