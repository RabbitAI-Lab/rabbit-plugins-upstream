## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved client notes, archives, and diagnostic files may contain sensitive information and remain local plaintext.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected client before saving, prefer redacted notes, and restrict access to local archives and diagnostics.

Risk: An update runs an unpinned installer and changes globally installed project skills.

Mitigation: Request updates deliberately; verify the intended project and release, and protect local changes before proceeding.

Risk: Vendor, product, pricing, policy, and platform information can become outdated or be insufficiently supported.

Mitigation: Check current, dated sources for material claims and narrow or qualify conclusions when evidence is missing.

Risk: Knowledge approval labels may be imperfect and do not guarantee that a claim is reliable.

Mitigation: Check the underlying source and evidence before relying on a knowledge item.
