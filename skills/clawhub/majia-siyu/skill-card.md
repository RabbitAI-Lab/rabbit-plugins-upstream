## Description:

私域专家团 · 马甲实战版 is a Chinese private-domain operations assistant that routes requests for Moments posts, broadcast messages, welcome scripts, WeCom traffic, diagnostics, full-funnel planning, market research, customer archives, and follow-up reports, while requiring live verification for dynamic external facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External operators, consultants, and business owners use this skill to choose the next private-domain operation task and produce Chinese-language copy, diagnostics, research snapshots, customer records, and reports for private traffic workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create local plaintext customer archives and reports that contain sensitive client or customer information.

Mitigation: Review archive and report contents before saving or sharing sensitive client data.

Risk: Update commands can change the installed skill package.

Mitigation: Use update commands only when the operator intends to update this specific skill package.

Risk: Dynamic vendor, product, price, policy, platform-rule, or company-status claims can become stale or unsupported.

Mitigation: Require current public web verification with source links and dates before using those claims in recommendations.
