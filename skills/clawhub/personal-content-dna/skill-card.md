## Description:

Builds a traceable personal writing library from scoped conversations and project work, then uses approved materials and reference methods for writing practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0xcjl](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xcjl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and their agents use this skill to capture explicitly scoped decisions and feedback as local, reviewable materials, then prepare briefs and drafts grounded in approved personal input.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal or restricted material could be retained or reused beyond the intended scope.

Mitigation: Specify permitted conversations, files, repositories and library destination; keep private text and credentials out of ordinary cards and review cards before approving writing use.

Risk: Drafts could misattribute AI contributions or present unverified personal experiences as fact.

Mitigation: Keep source and contribution records, check claims with the user and use only approved, publicly allowed materials in routine writing.

Risk: Permission to use material in writing could be mistaken for permission to publish it.

Mitigation: Confirm public-use boundaries separately and obtain explicit authorization before publication or account operations.
