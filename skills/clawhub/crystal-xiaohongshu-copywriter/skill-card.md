Risk: Generated posts may imply effects on mood, sleep, energy, cleansing, or platform compliance. <br>
Mitigation: Review each post before publishing and add personal-experience or cultural-context disclaimers where needed. <br>
Risk: Metaphysical wording can be questionable or overstated even when no hidden execution or data-access behavior is present. <br>
Mitigation: Keep claims framed as cultural sharing, aesthetic guidance, or personal experience, and avoid medical, guaranteed-outcome, or supernatural-effect claims. <br>
