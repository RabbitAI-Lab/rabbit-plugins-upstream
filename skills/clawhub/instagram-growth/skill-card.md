## Description: <br>
Instagram Growth helps agents diagnose stalled Instagram growth, identify the broken stage in the growth loop, and route users to targeted content, profile, cadence, or discovery actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media operators, founders, and marketers use this skill to diagnose Instagram reach, follow-conversion, retention, sends, and cadence issues, then choose targeted next actions based on native Instagram Insights and user-provided brand context. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use sensitive brand, audience, social strategy, and Instagram Insights context supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the context needed for the diagnosis and review any downstream publishing, scheduling, or account-access skills separately before use. <br>
Risk: Instagram growth recommendations can be mistaken for guaranteed growth outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as strategy guidance, verify recommendations against current native Instagram Insights, and avoid fake engagement or guaranteed-growth claims. <br>
