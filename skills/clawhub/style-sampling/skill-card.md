## Description:

Make a new episode in a proven video style - Collage Explainer, Finance Explainer, Animated Science Explainer, 3D Brand Explainer, 3D True Crime - on the person's topic. Use for "an episode of", "in the style of", "sample this style", 「用这个风格做一集」. One video to copy is reverse-any-video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External video creators use this skill to plan and produce a new episode on their topic using one of the supported video series styles, with their own voice and, when needed, their own face.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video generation can incur paid usage, and the workflow does not volunteer cost and credit details unless requested.

Mitigation: Require a cost estimate, enforce a total budget that includes retries, and provide a final usage report.

Risk: The workflow may send a user's script, voice, or photo to HyperFrames or HeyGen for processing.

Mitigation: Confirm the user accepts that processing and configure telemetry preferences before proceeding.

Risk: External CLI tools and downloaded templates are not verified by the supplied static scan.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, pinned CLI and inspect downloaded template tools before execution.
