## Description:

Make a new episode in a proven video style - Collage Explainer, Finance Explainer, Animated Science Explainer, 3D Brand Explainer, 3D True Crime - on the person's topic. Use for "an episode of", "in the style of", "sample this style", 「用这个风格做一集」. One video to copy is reverse-any-video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators use this skill to turn a topic into a new explainer or reconstruction with one of five HyperFrames Pro style templates. They provide their own voice and, for presenter formats, their own face.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scanner reports repeated use of an unpinned command-line package.

Mitigation: Review the package source and version before installing.

Risk: The startup usage beacon may be account-linked when signed in, and the workflow handles personal voice and face media.

Mitigation: Review the telemetry opt-out and use only personal media you have rights and consent to use.

Risk: Generated media costs and retries need clear limits.

Mitigation: Review cost and retry behavior, and set a credit limit before generation.

Risk: The security scan was partial because a referenced local path could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the complete package and resolve referenced files before installation.

Risk: A broad activation phrase may invoke the skill for loosely related requests.

Mitigation: Invoke it for the documented style-template workflow and confirm the template choice before pulling it.
