## Description:

Make a new episode on the person's topic using a proven video style such as a collage, finance, animated science, 3D brand, or 3D true-crime explainer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators use this skill to plan and produce a new episode in one of its available template styles on a topic they choose. It guides them through scripting, storyboarding, media selection, production checks, and rendering while using their own voice and, where needed, their own face.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Risk: The skill reports telemetry and possible credit use or media transfer, with limited cost and privacy disclosures.

Mitigation: Check the service's current cost and data-sharing terms before use, and avoid sending media or other information you do not intend to share.

Risk: The security review is incomplete because a referenced local path was missing.

Mitigation: Resolve the missing path and review its contents before relying on the scan as a complete assessment.
