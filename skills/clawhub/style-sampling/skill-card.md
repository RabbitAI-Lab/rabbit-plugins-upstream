## Description:

Creates a new explainer episode on the user's topic using one of five established video styles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and editors use the skill to plan and produce a new explainer episode on a chosen topic in one of five visual styles, using their own voice and, for presenter-led formats, their own face.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid media generation can consume credits without a required spending cap, while spending information may be hidden.

Mitigation: Before paid generation, require visible estimates and usage reporting, enforce a cap that covers retries, verify the CLI and pulled template tools, and confirm how HeyGen will process any voice or photos.
