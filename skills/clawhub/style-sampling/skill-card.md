## Description:

Make a new episode in a proven video style - Collage Explainer, Finance Explainer, Animated Science Explainer, 3D Brand Explainer, 3D True Crime - on the person's topic. Use for "an episode of", "in the style of", "sample this style", 「用这个风格做一集」. One video to copy is reverse-any-video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators use this skill to plan and make a new explainer or narrative video episode on their topic using one of five established style templates. The episode uses the person's own voice and, for presenter formats, their own face.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video generation can consume paid credits without showing cost and usage information by default.

Mitigation: Set an approved total spending cap, obtain cost estimates before generation, and keep an actual-usage record.

Risk: The workflow may send a person's voice or photo through HyperFrames and HeyGen.

Mitigation: Confirm the person's permission before sending their voice or photo through either service.

Risk: Downloaded template tools may have behavior or credit-limit enforcement that has not been reviewed.

Mitigation: Use a verified, version-pinned CLI and review downloaded template tools before use.
