## Description:

48 philosopher personas (Socrates to Arendt, Confucius to Shankara). Use /philosophers <name> to speak with any of them, or /philosophers debate <name> <name> <question> to stage a debate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bhackerj](https://clawhub.ai/user/bhackerj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using an agent can select one of 48 philosopher personas for in-character discussion or stage a multi-persona debate around a question.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Roleplay may be mistaken for a historical figure's authentic statements or dependable consequential advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as historical simulations and verify quotations, factual claims, and consequential advice independently.

Risk: A user may share personal details during an in-character conversation.

Mitigation: Share personal details only when comfortable doing so.
