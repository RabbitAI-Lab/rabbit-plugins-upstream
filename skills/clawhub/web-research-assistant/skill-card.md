## Description: <br>
AI-powered web research assistant that leverages BrowserAct API to supplement restricted web access by searching the internet for additional information. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[phheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/phheng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and researchers use this skill to gather supplemental web research when direct access is restricted or insufficient. It can search multiple sources, extract source metadata, and produce a concise research report for authorized use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill promotes bypassing paywalls, CAPTCHA or verification checks, and geographic or IP restrictions. <br>
Mitigation: Use only for lawful, authorized research, and do not use it to bypass paywalls, CAPTCHA, anti-bot checks, or regional and licensing restrictions. <br>
Risk: Research queries may be sent to BrowserAct and could contain sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sending confidential, personal, regulated, or secret information unless BrowserAct data-handling terms have been reviewed and accepted. <br>
Risk: Search results and extracted snippets may be incomplete, stale, or misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-reference important findings against multiple reliable sources before using them in decisions or publications. <br>
