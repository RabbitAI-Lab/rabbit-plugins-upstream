## Description:

Grades and provides feedback on English written homework for Chinese elementary school students in grades 3-6, including sentence writing, fill-in-the-blank, translation, short writing, and mixed exercise sheets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to review English homework from Chinese elementary school students in grades 3-6 and receive age-appropriate scoring, corrections, praise, and concise improvement suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate for homework review requests outside its intended grade 3-6 English-writing scope.

Mitigation: Narrow activation to English written homework for grades 3-6 and confirm the student's grade when it is unclear.

Risk: Feedback language may not match the user's preferred language or the student's needs.

Mitigation: Confirm whether feedback should be in Chinese, English, or both before grading when the preference is ambiguous.
