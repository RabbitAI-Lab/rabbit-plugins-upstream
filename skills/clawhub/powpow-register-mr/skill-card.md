## Description:

Provides Marathi-language guidance for PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without completing forms or payments for the user.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

PowPow users use this skill for Marathi-language instructions on creating an account, resolving registration problems, and understanding how to activate a subscription. Users complete registration and payment themselves on the relevant website or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration and subscription can involve passwords, verification codes, and payment details.

Mitigation: Enter these details only on the official PowPow or payment-provider pages, never in chat; the skill provides guidance only.
