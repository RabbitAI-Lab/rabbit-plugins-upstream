## Description:

Creates distinct, shoot-ready short-video scripts for a product or topic, with hooks, shot lists, narration, captions, and platform-specific pacing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[petehuax](https://clawhub.ai/user/petehuax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketers use this skill to draft batches of differentiated short-video scripts from a product or topic and its selling points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may contain inaccurate or unsupported product claims, especially about food, beauty, or health.

Mitigation: Review claims for accuracy and applicable advertising requirements before publishing; avoid unsupported efficacy promises.
