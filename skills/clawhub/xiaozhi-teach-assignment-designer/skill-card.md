## Description:

Helps teachers design differentiated K–12 homework task cards with time estimates, scoring rubrics, and feedback templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft differentiated homework, per-question scoring criteria, and feedback for students and parents. It does not grade submissions or create exams or revision schedules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated questions or scoring guidance may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Teachers should check questions and rubrics before assigning or grading homework.

Risk: Student identifiers or confidential unreleased exam material could be exposed in inputs or feedback.

Mitigation: Avoid entering real student identifiers or unreleased exam material, and review feedback before sharing.

Risk: Stored homework completion records may be incorrect.

Mitigation: Teachers should confirm stored homework records before relying on them.
