## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 teachers design differentiated homework cards with time estimates, grading criteria, and feedback templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to draft A/B/C-level assignments, marking rubrics, and student or parent feedback for Chinese K–12 classes. Teachers review generated questions and approve any classroom record updates before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curricula, privacy practices, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions or marking guidance may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify generated questions and scoring criteria before assigning them.

Risk: Classroom records and parent feedback may expose student information.

Mitigation: Avoid real student names, check sharing consent, and require teacher confirmation before record writes.

Risk: Unreleased examination materials could be disclosed to an AI tool.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam questions, answer keys, or marking schemes.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts may be inappropriate in another region.

Mitigation: Localize crisis and emergency guidance before deployment outside Mainland China.
