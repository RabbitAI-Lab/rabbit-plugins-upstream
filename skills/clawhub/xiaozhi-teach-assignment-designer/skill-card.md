## Description:

帮助中国大陆中小学教师设计分层、限时的作业任务卡，并提供评分标准和反馈模板。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to turn lesson goals and class learning data into differentiated homework with per-question time estimates, grading criteria, and feedback drafts. It does not automatically grade work or replace teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated questions or scoring criteria may contain errors.

Mitigation: Teachers should check and verify generated questions and rubrics before assigning them.

Risk: Student records or unreleased exam materials could be exposed.

Mitigation: Keep student data within the class workspace, omit identifying details from feedback, and do not enter unreleased exam questions.

Risk: An unreviewed update could alter class assignment records.

Mitigation: Present proposed writebacks for teacher confirmation before saving them.
