## Description:

Helps teachers design differentiated homework task cards with time estimates, scoring criteria, and feedback templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use the skill to create differentiated homework with difficulty tiers and estimated completion times, along with scoring criteria and feedback templates. It can also prepare structured completion summaries for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese-language K–12 education context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions or timetable suggestions may be inaccurate; the security assessment notes a minor inconsistency in a sample revision timetable.

Mitigation: Review generated questions and timetable suggestions before use, and verify examples against the teacher's plan.

Risk: Student records or shared feedback may reveal sensitive information if identity and permissions are not handled carefully.

Mitigation: Keep student information pseudonymous, check sharing permissions, and confirm records before saving; use the skill's pause-memory, export, and deletion controls when appropriate.
