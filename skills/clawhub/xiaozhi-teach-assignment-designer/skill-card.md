## Description:

Creates differentiated, time-bounded homework task cards for teachers, with grading criteria, feedback templates, and completion summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to prepare differentiated homework for primary and secondary classes, estimate time per task, set transparent grading criteria, and draft feedback or completion summaries. It produces teacher-reviewed drafts; it does not automatically grade student work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions, feedback, or suggested dates may need correction, and classroom records may contain sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Review generated questions and dates before use; use student aliases and aggregate results; confirm record writes; and check the host platform's storage and sharing controls.

Risk: Companion skills used alongside this skill may have separate behavior and risks.

Mitigation: Review companion skills separately before use.
