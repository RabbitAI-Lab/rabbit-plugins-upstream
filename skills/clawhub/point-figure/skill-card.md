## Description: <br>
Guides agents in explaining point-and-figure charts, including X/O columns, three-box reversal logic, support/resistance interpretation, horizontal count price targets, and disciplined stop-loss framing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and market-analysis assistants use this skill to explain when point-and-figure charting is appropriate and to produce structured guidance on box size, reversal thresholds, support and resistance, price targets, and stop-loss discipline. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake trading-signal language for automatic financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as educational chart-interpretation support only, and require independent review of suitability, risk tolerance, position sizing, stop losses, and current market context before any trading decision. <br>
