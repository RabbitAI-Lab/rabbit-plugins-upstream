## Description:

Diagnoses whether a product has genuine network effects, estimates critical mass, and guides amplification or defense.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product strategists, and investors use this skill to test whether a product or competitor has a structural network-effect advantage. It classifies the mechanism, estimates the critical-mass threshold, distinguishes network effects from scale and virality, and suggests a strategy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:
