## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to search for fitness providers, check availability, and create bookings through Lokuli after confirming provider, service, time, and contact details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill sends search and booking requests to Lokuli's external fitness booking service. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if you are comfortable using Lokuli and treat searches as external requests. <br>
Risk: Creating a booking can require personal contact details such as name, email, and phone number. <br>
Mitigation: Provide contact details only after confirming the provider, service, time, and booking details to be sent. <br>
