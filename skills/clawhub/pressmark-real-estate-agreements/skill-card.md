## Description:

Typesets publication-grade real estate agreements, residential leases, commercial NNN leases, property management owner statements, multi-unit rent rolls, and closing settlement statements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Landlords, property managers, and real-estate professionals use this skill to prepare residential and commercial leases, rent rolls, owner statements, closing settlement statements, and tenant notices through Pressmark.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documents may contain personal, property, or banking details that are sent to the external Pressmark service.

Mitigation: Review Pressmark's privacy and retention terms before use, and omit unnecessary personal or banking details.

Risk: Real-estate statutory disclosures, notice periods, and financial requirements vary by jurisdiction, so generated documents may not meet local requirements.

Mitigation: Have a qualified local professional review jurisdiction-specific terms and disclosures before relying on or executing a document.
