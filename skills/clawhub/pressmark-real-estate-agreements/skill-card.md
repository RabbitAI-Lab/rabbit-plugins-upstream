## Description:

Typesets publication-grade real estate agreements across 29 leasing, property management, investor analysis, and deed archetypes with a 50-state + DC + 6 municipal 4-layer statutory lease engine.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External property owners, managers, brokers, and other users can prepare leases, tenant notices, property management records, investor analyses, purchase agreements, and deeds for U.S. properties. The skill gathers property, party, and financial details and uses Pressmark to preview and generate documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property and party details are sent to Pressmark for document compilation; the external service implementation was not verified.

Mitigation: Share only information you are comfortable sending to Pressmark, check its privacy, retention, and checkout terms, and review generated agreements before use.
