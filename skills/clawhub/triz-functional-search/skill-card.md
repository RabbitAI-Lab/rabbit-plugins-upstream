## Description:

Functional-search skill that helps R&D personnel transform a specific technical problem into generalized functions and identify cross-domain technical or patent solutions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

R&D teams and technical search specialists use this skill to convert concrete engineering problems into functional search strategies, identify cross-domain patent or technical options, and outline migration considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may guide patent and technical searches that users could mistake for legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as R&D search support only and require qualified legal review for FTO, infringement, validity, or other legal decisions.

Risk: Generated search reports may contain unsupported patents, market claims, or technical evidence if source grounding is weak.

Mitigation: Use user-provided materials and available PatSnap/Zhihuiya data sources, and require source, scope, date range, and uncertainty labels in the output.
