## Description:

Helps R&D teams turn a concrete technical problem into a generalized functional search for cross-domain patents and technical solutions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and product R&D teams use this skill to generalize specific technical problems, form patent and technology search strategies, screen cross-domain solutions, and identify migration paths for follow-up validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent and technology search outputs can be incomplete, stale, or based on unavailable external data.

Mitigation: Verify cited patent sources, search scope, and time range before using results for product or research decisions.

Risk: FTO, infringement, validity, or similar legal topics may be mistaken for legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat those discussions as research support only and obtain qualified legal review before making legal or commercial decisions.
