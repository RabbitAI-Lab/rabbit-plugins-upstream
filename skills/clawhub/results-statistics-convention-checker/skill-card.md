## Description:

Checks English psychology Results sections for APA-style statistical reporting issues across descriptive statistics, inferential tests, p values, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and consistency between statistics and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External authors, editors, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to review English psychology Results drafts for statistical reporting completeness and APA-style presentation. It produces a scored diagnostic with quoted draft evidence, example-based comparisons, and concrete revision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake reporting-format and completeness feedback for independent verification of the underlying statistical calculations.

Mitigation: Review the original data, analysis plan, and statistical software output separately; use this skill only for Results-section reporting conventions.

Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese by default when the user needs English-facing manuscript feedback.

Mitigation: State the required response language when invoking the skill, especially when preparing output for authors, reviewers, or journal submission.

Risk: APA-style guidance and bundled examples may not match a target journal's specific reporting requirements.

Mitigation: Compare suggested revisions against the target journal instructions and override the default APA framing where journal policy differs.
