## Description: <br>
Chinese-language habit coaching skill that uses the Atomic Habits four-laws framework to produce personalized plans for building good habits, breaking bad habits, handling relapse, and optionally tailoring advice with a brief Big Five personality check. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wangzhanchao883](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangzhanchao883) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to turn a habit goal or unwanted habit into a structured, personalized action plan with prompts, environment design, rewards, relapse handling, and optional personality-based adjustments. It is intended for habit coaching, not clinical diagnosis or treatment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat habit coaching as a substitute for professional help when addiction, withdrawal, severe impairment, debt, health damage, or mental-health concerns are present. <br>
Mitigation: The skill discloses clinical limits and directs users with severe addiction or mental-health concerns to qualified medical professionals instead of continuing with habit-plan coaching. <br>
Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language coaching, which may not fit users who cannot review the advice accurately in Chinese. <br>
Mitigation: Install and use it only when Chinese-language habit coaching is appropriate for the user and review context. <br>
