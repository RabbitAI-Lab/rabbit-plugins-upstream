## Description:

Creates a daily pet-care plan tailored to a pet's species, breed, age, weight, living environment, and existing routine.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners and pet-care workers use this skill to organize feeding, activity, grooming, and environmental care into practical schedules and checklists, with signs that warrant veterinary attention.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General pet-care guidance could be mistaken for veterinary advice on vaccines, deworming, medication, illness, or urgent symptoms.

Mitigation: Follow a veterinarian's plan for clinical care and seek veterinary attention for warning signs; do not use the skill for diagnosis or medication dosing.

Risk: Sharing pet and household details in a conversation may reveal information the user prefers to keep private.

Mitigation: Provide only pet details you are comfortable sharing and omit unnecessary personal information.
