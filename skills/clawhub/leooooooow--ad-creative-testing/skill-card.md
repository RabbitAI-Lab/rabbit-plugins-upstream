## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth practitioners, and ad operators use this skill to design structured A/B tests for ad creatives, hooks, destination pages, and audience segments with clear hypotheses, metrics, sample-size logic, and decision criteria.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could act on ad testing recommendations with incorrect sample-size, budget, or statistical assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify sample-size assumptions, budgets, and statistical calculations before launching or scaling tests.
