## Description:

Provides practical guidance and examples for writing Kling 4 video prompts using composed key frames, concise director's prompts, reference inputs, and timed shot lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to write Kling 4 prompts, plan key-frame references, and troubleshoot common video-generation results. It also provides examples for reference-to-video calls and shot timing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference images may be sent to an external video service, potentially disclosing sensitive or identifiable media.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive or identifiable media unless you have checked the service's data handling, and confirm you have permission to share reference media before submitting it.
