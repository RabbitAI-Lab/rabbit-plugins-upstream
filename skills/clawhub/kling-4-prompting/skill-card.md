## Description:

Guides agents in writing cinematic Kling 4 video prompts using composed key frames, concise director prompts, reference inputs, timed shots, dialogue, and troubleshooting patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to write or improve Kling 4 prompts for cinematic video, including reference-to-video, key-frame, multi-shot, and dialogue workflows. It also helps diagnose common motion, framing, and continuity problems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
