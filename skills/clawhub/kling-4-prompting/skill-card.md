## Description:

Guides creators in prompting Kling 4 for cinematic video with composed key frames, concise shot direction, reference inputs, and practical fixes for common generation issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers can use the skill to prepare image-led Kling 4 prompts and reference assets for cinematic videos, and to diagnose common framing, motion, and continuity problems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference media are sent to external video-generation services.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting private or sensitive media unless you are comfortable sharing it with those services.

Risk: The installation reference is unpinned, and the security scan was partial because three local path-like references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the installation source and inspect unresolved files before use.
