## Description:

Guides users in composing cinematic Kling 4 videos with prepared key frames and short prompts, including reference inputs, shot timing, dialogue, and common failure fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to write Kling 4 prompts and plan reference-to-video generations, including key frames, multi-shot timing, dialogue, and visual style. It also helps diagnose common generation problems and refine prompts or reference images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference media are sent to Varg and the selected model provider.

Mitigation: Use only media you have permission to share and avoid confidential material.

Risk: Generating multiple takes can incur costs.

Mitigation: Check estimated costs before generating multiple takes.
