## Description:

Guides agents in creating cinematic Kling 4 video prompts with designed key frames, concise direction, and practical advice on references, timing, and common generation issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and agent users can use this skill to draft Kling 4 prompts, prepare key-frame and reference inputs, and troubleshoot common video-generation results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media and prompts may be sent to varg and its generation providers, and may include sensitive details.

Mitigation: Share only content intended for those services; remove secrets and sensitive access-bearing URLs.

Risk: Repeated video takes can incur generation costs.

Mitigation: Set a budget and estimate costs before running repeated generations.

Risk: This review covers the bundled instructions, not external service implementations or their data-retention practices.

Mitigation: Review the relevant providers' terms and data practices before sending media or prompts.

Risk: Installing an unreviewed release or a larger bundle may add skills beyond what is needed.

Mitigation: Prefer reviewed installer versions and repository revisions, and install only this skill when it is all you need.
