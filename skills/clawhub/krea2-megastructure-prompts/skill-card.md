## Description:

Generates Krea 2 megastructure image prompts with English long-form prompt text, Chinese notes, style or viewpoint variants, and Krea 2 parameter suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangminrui2022](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangminrui2022)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Artists, designers, worldbuilders, and prompt engineers use this skill to turn brief megastructure concepts into Krea 2-ready prompts for giant architecture, creatures, natural formations, vehicles, and surreal hybrids. It is especially useful when outputs need visible scale cues, atmospheric depth, readable structure, and concrete Krea 2 settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger the skill for a wide range of giant-scale or epic image prompt requests.

Mitigation: Review whether the request is specifically about Krea 2 megastructure prompting before applying the workflow.

Risk: The skill defaults to English prompts with Chinese notes, which may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Ask for or honor the user's requested output language when language preference matters.
