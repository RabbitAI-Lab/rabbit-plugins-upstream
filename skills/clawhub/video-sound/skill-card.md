## Description:

Plans and produces video soundtracks with directed dialogue, voice, music, effects, timed cues, and measured mixes, with people selecting voices and reviewing the final sound.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, video editors, and creative teams use this skill to plan and build soundtracks for videos, including dialogue, music, effects, and mixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The price check shares dialogue with the provider before the approval gate, and edited project files can cause unintended file writes or processing.

Mitigation: Use nonconfidential audition text unless sharing is approved; review audition manifests and cue sheets; run in a restricted project environment; validate numeric cue fields, contain download paths, and pin dependencies.
