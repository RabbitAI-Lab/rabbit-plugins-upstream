## Description:

Plans and produces voice, sound effects, music, and measured mixes for videos, with people making the final creative choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and developers use this skill to plan and build voiceover, sound effects, music, and a checked audio mix for videos. It guides writing, voice auditions, cue sheets, rendering, and human review of the finished sound.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scripts and identifiable recordings may be sent to external services for audio generation or analysis.

Mitigation: Check each provider's terms and privacy practices, and authorize processing before sending confidential scripts or identifiable recordings.

Risk: ClawScan reports unsafe download handling.

Mitigation: Verify media sources and inspect downloaded files before using them in a project.
