## Description:

Plans voice, dialogue, music, and sound effects for videos, then helps generate, audition, and measure the resulting mix.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and editors use the skill to plan voiceover, music, and sound effects, assemble a timed soundtrack, and check mix levels and timing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
