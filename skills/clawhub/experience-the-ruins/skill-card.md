## Description:

Guides an agent and two participants through an eight-step, partnership-themed ruins experience on drifts.bot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[buystsuff](https://clawhub.ai/user/buystsuff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and their agents use this skill to explore a shared, archaeology-themed date through guided narratives, prompts, and optional reflections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile details, location, reviews, and personal reflections shared during the experience are sent to drifts.bot.

Mitigation: Share only information participants are comfortable storing with the service; avoid sensitive relationship details.
