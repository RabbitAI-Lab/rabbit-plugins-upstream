## Description: <br>
Provides outbound investment compliance diagnosis and planning for OPC entrepreneurs, including approval paths, risk markers, and differentiated guidance for state-owned enterprises, private enterprises, and individuals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
OPC entrepreneurs and advisors use this skill to assess outbound investment compliance, compare entity-specific approval paths, and prepare risk-marked planning materials for overseas investment decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with content focused on China-related outbound investment scenarios. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Investment-compliance, tax, foreign-exchange, and jurisdiction-specific requirements can change or vary by case. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and verify requirements with qualified professionals or official sources before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Sensitive countries, industries, or transaction sizes may require additional approvals or may be highly restricted. <br>
Mitigation: Escalate sensitive scenarios for professional legal and compliance review before taking action. <br>
