## Description:

Generates 15-25 cinematic B-roll clips for long-form VSL, webinar, or masterclass scripts using Higgsfield Seedance 2.0 and Cinema Studio 3.0, including scene prompts, insertion phrases, and video URLs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and video production teams use this skill to turn a long VSL, webinar, or masterclass script into a reviewed B-roll scene plan and a final catalog of generated clips with exact insertion phrases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may inspect and upload personal reference photos to Higgsfield and reuse the resulting media ID later.

Mitigation: Use one explicitly approved reference photo, avoid broad selfie folders, and instruct the agent not to save or publish media IDs unless reuse is explicitly desired.

Risk: Video generation can incur material Higgsfield credit costs across 15-25 clips.

Mitigation: Require the agent to calculate total credits and wait for explicit approval before launching generation jobs.

Risk: Generated B-roll and prompts may be unsuitable, rejected by content filters, or misleading if used without review.

Mitigation: Review the proposed scene table before generation and inspect final clips before publishing or attaching them to a campaign.
