## Use Case: <br>
Transit riders and travel assistants use this skill to look up Seoul subway arrivals, station details, routes, last trains, exits, accessibility information, restrooms, and service alerts. It is suitable for normal subway lookup workflows where users are comfortable sending station names and route search parameters to the disclosed proxy. <br>
