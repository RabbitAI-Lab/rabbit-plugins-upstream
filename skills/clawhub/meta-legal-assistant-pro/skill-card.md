## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for China-focused legal information, contract-risk triage, dispute-resolution pathways, evidence checklists, and procedural guidance. Outputs are informational and should be checked against current law and qualified counsel before use as formal legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes persistent learning and cross-session memory in a sensitive legal-use context.

Mitigation: Avoid recording confidential legal facts or personal details unless storage scope, deletion behavior, and user consent controls are clarified.

Risk: Legal guidance can become incomplete or outdated, and the artifact notes that current legal developments require online verification.

Mitigation: Verify statutes, judicial interpretations, and case references against current authoritative sources before relying on the output.

Risk: Users may treat informational legal guidance as formal legal advice or litigation representation.

Mitigation: Preserve the artifact's boundaries: do not issue formal legal opinions or represent litigation, and refer users to licensed counsel, police, or legal aid where appropriate.
