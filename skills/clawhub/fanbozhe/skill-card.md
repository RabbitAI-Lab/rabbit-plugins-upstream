## Description:

Fanbozhe Clean gives agents a Chinese-language contrarian decision-review persona that challenges business ideas, tests assumptions, and returns blunt red-team feedback when invoked.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to request a skeptical second opinion on projects, partnerships, and decisions. It helps surface weak assumptions, demand signals, execution risks, and reality-based counterexamples without making the final decision for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The contrarian persona can produce blunt feedback that may feel harsher than a normal assistant response.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when a direct skeptical review is desired, and treat the output as decision support rather than personal judgment.

Risk: Reality-based counterexamples may be unavailable or outside the model's reliable knowledge.

Mitigation: Require the agent to say when it does not know and verify important examples or business claims before acting on them.

Risk: High-certainty framing can make a weak critique seem more conclusive than the available facts support.

Mitigation: Check whether the critique is tied to user-provided facts, demand signals, or verifiable commercial evidence before changing plans.
