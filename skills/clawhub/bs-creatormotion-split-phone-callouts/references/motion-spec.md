## Timing and media contract

The composition lasts 6.8 seconds at 30 fps in each of the four aspect ratios. The module contains the exact deterministic GSAP beat positions; these are authored directing choices, not recovered source keyframes. Keep the source scene, the change of focus and the final reading hold as separate phases. Use short transitions for changes of attention and reserve stillness for reading. Do not apply one generic entrance to every item.

Keep the documented input schema from `SKILL.md`. Image keys and presenter video remain replaceable. Where a transparent presenter matte is used, keep it aligned to the same original take and timing. Do not loop a short take to fill a longer shot; end its visible interval before the supplied media ends. No third-party creator footage, identities or proprietary interface screenshots belong in the generated example or package.

## Composition and palette

Use the bundled Google Fonts. all three palettes retain the same content, timing and geometry. Black & White is neutral and dark backgrounds retain the fine stationary grid. Make labels and markers serve the current explanation; do not add arbitrary glow, particle effects, noise or spring movement. Default sample text and generated raster imagery are illustrative demonstrations, not verified source evidence.

## Visual acceptance

- Render with HyperFrames. Check the first visible state, each focus transition and the final reading interval in the MP4.
- Review all three palettes, including dark-on-light marker text and light-on-dark document text.
- Source photos and speaker video may be deliberately cropped inside their viewports. Exact clipping is acceptable only when the face or intended evidence stays intact.
- Mark intentional crop or occlusion precisely; do not suppress layout checks on essential explanatory text.
- Inspect actual transition frames for seams, premature labels and blank interstitial frames. Keep the final state composed rather than empty.
- A silent preview demonstrates visual direction only. Claim narration synchronisation only when the real audio/cues were supplied and checked.
