## Description:

A local Chinese-language finance self-check skill that verifies segmented finance tables for row arithmetic, totals, duplicates, and missing data, with source citations for findings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenqg618](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenqg618)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance and bookkeeping users can run local monthly close, reconciliation, and inventory-check materials through deterministic table checks. The skill reports missing inputs instead of drawing conclusions when the supplied segmented material is incomplete.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reviewed free package points users toward an unpinned remote install and purchase flow outside the reviewed package.

Mitigation: Use the local free checker unless the user separately trusts the payment tool, accepts remote package execution, and reviews newly installed instructions before authorizing payment or wallet actions.

Risk: Finance-table checks can be mistaken for assurance, tax, filing, or policy advice.

Mitigation: Treat output as arithmetic and consistency checking only; require qualified review for filings, attestations, tax positions, and business decisions.
