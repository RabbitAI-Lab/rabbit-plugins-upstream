## Description: <br>
Searches for, researches, and verifies non-tech founders on LinkedIn to identify high-value prospects for technology services and generate professional prospect reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[harshhmaniya](https://clawhub.ai/user/harshhmaniya) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External sales, business development, and agency operators use this skill to identify non-tech founders who may need web, CRM, ERP, app, SEO, or AI services, assess public profile and website gaps, and prepare a prospect report for reviewed outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill performs LinkedIn-style B2B prospect research and may collect or summarize personal or profile data outside the intended scope. <br>
Mitigation: Define target scope and source limits before use, avoid bulk scraping or sensitive personal data, and review generated reports before sharing. <br>
Risk: Generated prospect reports or pitch ideas may contain incorrect, outdated, or misleading business conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Verify candidate details, company websites, and outreach claims manually before using them for sales activity. <br>
