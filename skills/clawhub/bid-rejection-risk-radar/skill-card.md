## Description: <br>
Bid Rejection Risk Radar analyzes tender documents to extract bid-rejection clauses, hard qualification requirements, bid bond requirements, hidden risk points, and a structured pre-submission checklist. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Procurement, bid, and compliance reviewers use this skill to screen tender documents for clauses that could cause a bid to be rejected and to prepare a structured checklist for manual pre-submission review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tender documents can contain sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the skill is intentionally invoked before uploading files and follow the organization's document-handling requirements. <br>
Risk: The generated checklist may miss clauses, misread scanned text, or apply legal and knowledge-base anchors incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified personnel manually review the source tender documents, extracted risks, and any legal anchoring before relying on the checklist. <br>
