## Description:

Helps users publish recurring daily or weekly reports to LivePage for sharing in WeChat, keeping one link per series and reporting read receipts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[verkyyi](https://clawhub.ai/user/verkyyi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and teams use this skill to publish an approved daily or weekly report as a new version of an existing LivePage series, share its stable link in WeChat, and check factual engagement afterward.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports sent to LivePage or shared through WeChat may expose sensitive content to unintended viewers.

Mitigation: Confirm the report and recipients before publishing; keep visibility private or passcode-protected and do not make reports public on the user's behalf.

Risk: Read receipts can be mistaken for evidence of a recipient's intent or response.

Mitigation: Report observed device activity only, without inferring what the recipient thought or why they did not respond.
