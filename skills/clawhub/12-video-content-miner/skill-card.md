## Description:

Analyzes short-video accounts by extracting spoken transcripts from Douyin/Kuaishou videos and summarizing hooks, structure, conversion patterns, and repeatable content formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and analysts use this skill to study public short-video content, extract spoken scripts, and identify reusable structure and topic patterns for content analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used on videos the user is not allowed to analyze or in ways that violate Douyin/Kuaishou platform rules.

Mitigation: Use it only with videos the user has permission to analyze and require collection through public, authorized interfaces.

Risk: Extracted transcripts may include private, copyrighted, or sensitive content.

Mitigation: Keep transcripts for internal analysis only unless the user has permission to store, share, or redistribute them.
