## Description: <br>
Generates a structured project site-selection analysis report that evaluates the fit between a proposed project and target regions across land availability, industrial base, policy support, logistics, talent, costs, supporting services, and implementation risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use web research and user-provided project details such as investment scale, land requirements, and target regions. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business information unless appropriate for the deployment context. <br>
Risk: Policy, land, cost, market, and regional capacity claims may affect investment or site-selection decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Verify material claims against authoritative current sources before making business or investment decisions. <br>
