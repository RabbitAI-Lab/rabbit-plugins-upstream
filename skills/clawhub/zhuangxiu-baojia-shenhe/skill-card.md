## Description: <br>
This skill helps homeowners review renovation quotes, compare bids, and identify budget traps, hidden costs, unit-price issues, water and electrical estimates, and semi-package versus full-package tradeoffs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and renovation clients use this skill to understand contractor quotes, spot missing line items or unclear pricing, and ask practical questions before signing or comparing bids. It is oriented toward renovation budget review and consumer guidance, not legal, engineering, or contractor certification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad renovation-budget wording and provide guidance outside a specific quote-review context. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for renovation quote, budget, and contractor bid review questions, and verify important financial decisions against the actual contract and local market conditions. <br>
Risk: Quote documents may contain personal details such as full addresses, phone numbers, contract IDs, or payment information. <br>
Mitigation: Redact unnecessary personal or payment information before sharing quote documents for review. <br>
