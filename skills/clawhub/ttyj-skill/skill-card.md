## Description:

天台研究 helps an agent answer Tiantai Buddhism questions and produce structured research reports with source-checked CBETA citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gouchunlei2-png](https://clawhub.ai/user/gouchunlei2-png)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and practitioners use this skill for Tiantai doctrine Q&A, ancestor text interpretation, doctrinal comparison, practice-system research, and longer research reports grounded in configured knowledge bases and CBETA citations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live CBETA API searches or online document tools may send research terms to external services.

Mitigation: Avoid using private or sensitive prompts with live external searches or online document tools unless that disclosure is acceptable.

Risk: Doctrinal answers or quotations may be misleading if sources are missing or citation checks are skipped.

Mitigation: Require CBETA or knowledge-base source annotations and state when the available materials do not cover a claim.
