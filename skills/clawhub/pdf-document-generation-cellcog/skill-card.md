## Description: <br>
AI PDF document generation powered by CellCog - PDF and DOCX output for resumes, contracts, reports, proposals, invoices, certificates, NDAs, letters, brochures, legal documents, cover letters, and other professional documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to ask CellCog to draft and generate polished PDF or DOCX documents such as resumes, contracts, reports, proposals, invoices, certificates, policies, and marketing materials. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Document prompts and uploaded content may be sent to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when the user is comfortable sharing the relevant prompt and content with CellCog. <br>
Risk: Generated contracts, privacy policies, finance materials, and compliance text may be incomplete or unsuitable as final advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat these documents as drafts and route them through qualified legal, privacy, finance, or compliance review before use. <br>
Risk: The skill depends on a CellCog API key. <br>
Mitigation: Store CELLCOG_API_KEY in the agent environment or secret manager and avoid placing it in prompts or generated documents. <br>
