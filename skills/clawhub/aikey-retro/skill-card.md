## Description:

AI KEY·发布后复盘 helps short-video creators collect post-publish performance data, compare it with pre-publish predictions, and turn validated lessons into reusable content memory.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and content operators use this skill after publishing short videos to review real platform data, explain performance through observable funnel signals, and decide what to preserve, test, or change in the next content cycle.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposed memory or tracking-file updates can influence future content decisions.

Mitigation: Review and approve proposed updates before accepting them into the local content vault.

Risk: Post-publish attribution can be misleading when platform metrics are incomplete, delayed, or below the skill's own sample thresholds.

Mitigation: Keep uncertain findings as hypotheses, wait for delayed metrics where required, and avoid promoting n=1 results into reusable rules.
