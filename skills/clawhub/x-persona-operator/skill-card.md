## Description:

Provides guidance for designing an X account persona, creating on-brand content, growing an audience, and managing community interactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[skills-repo](https://clawhub.ai/user/skills-repo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individuals and small teams managing a single X account use this skill to define its voice and boundaries, plan and adapt posts, build a growth calendar, and handle replies and community interactions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested posts or interactions may affect the account's public voice, relationships, or reputation.

Mitigation: Set clear boundaries for posts, replies, DMs, deletions, and blocks before connecting account tools; have the account owner decide sensitive topics and disputes.

Risk: External installers or connected account tools may introduce risks beyond the reviewed guidance files.

Mitigation: Use a scoped installation and review the installer version and repository revision before running installation commands.
