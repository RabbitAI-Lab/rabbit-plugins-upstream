## Description:

30-day countdown protocol for third-party compliance audits, including EcoSure, health department, and corporate brand audits, with milestone-driven preparation for restaurant and franchise operators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Restaurant and franchise operators, general managers, assistant managers, and shift leads use this skill to prepare for audit windows through a 30-day sequence of baseline checks, team communication, daily compliance activation, mock audits, and final readiness sweeps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may retain audit findings, corrective actions, and respondent names or roles in agent memory during an audit cycle.

Mitigation: Use roles or initials instead of full names where possible, avoid entering confidential corporate records unless needed, and delete or summarize old milestone data after the audit cycle closes.

Risk: Generic audit-readiness guidance may not fully match a specific brand, franchise, health department, or third-party audit checklist.

Mitigation: Provide the current audit criteria when available and have the responsible manager verify corrective actions against the applicable standards.
