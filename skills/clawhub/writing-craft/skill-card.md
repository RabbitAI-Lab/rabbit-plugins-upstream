## Description: <br>
A Chinese writing-coaching skill based on Liu Junqiang's writing-method framework that helps users turn topics or drafts into structured outlines, full articles, writing reviews, quotable lines, angle explorations, and training exercises. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[xtoyun](https://clawhub.ai/user/xtoyun) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External writers, creators, and other users use this skill to clarify writing problems, choose a time scale and angle, build a tree-structured argument, draft or review Chinese prose, extract short quotes, and run writing exercises. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may engage from broad natural-language writing requests, not only explicit /写作手艺 invocations. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation when strict opt-in behavior is desired, and disable or avoid broad natural-language activation where the host allows. <br>
