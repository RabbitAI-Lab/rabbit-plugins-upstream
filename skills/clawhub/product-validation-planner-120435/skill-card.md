## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns early product concepts into practical validation workflows, checklists, prototypes, positioning, and success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill has broad activation wording for product, startup, SaaS, validation, and prototype requests, so it may be selected for adjacent business questions outside its intended planning scope. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for product validation planning; name another skill or clarify the scope when the user needs unrelated business advice or technical implementation work. <br>
