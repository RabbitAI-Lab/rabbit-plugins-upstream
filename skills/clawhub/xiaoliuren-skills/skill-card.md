## Description:

Provides Xiaoliu Ren divination charting and interpretation for requests involving number, time, or palace-based readings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianmingwangluo](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianmingwangluo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to let an agent answer Xiaoliu Ren divination requests, generate a chart, explain palace and spirit relationships, and provide reference-only guidance. It is scoped to Xiaoliu Ren and excludes unrelated divination systems or general destiny-analysis requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat health, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes readings as decision-grade advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as reference-only trend analysis and direct users to qualified professionals for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: The skill can be invoked for the wrong divination or destiny-analysis system.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or scope checks and decline unrelated systems such as Liuyao, Meihua Yishu, Qimen Dunjia, Bazi, or Zi Wei Dou Shu.
