## Description: <br>
Scan your codebase for distinctive technical patterns and produce structured scoring and evidence for patent consultation; this is not legal advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering teams use this skill to inspect a repository for distinctive implementation patterns, prioritize technically interesting findings, and prepare structured evidence for discussion with a registered patent attorney. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Analysis outputs may reveal sensitive repository details, proprietary code structure, secrets, or unreleased patent-sensitive ideas. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only on repositories appropriate for the chat environment and logs, avoid proprietary or client code unless approved, and review outputs before sharing. <br>
Risk: Users may overread technical distinctiveness scoring as a legal conclusion. <br>
Mitigation: Treat results as technical preparation only and consult a registered patent attorney for patentability, filing, or freedom-to-operate decisions. <br>
