## Description:

Turns a local video or public video link into a polished transcript and can generate summaries, rewrites, highlights, scripts, or translations from its content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and teams use this skill to turn recorded videos into readable transcripts or prompt-directed summaries and publication drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video content, video URLs, prompts, task IDs, and the API token are sent to the provider's cloud service.

Mitigation: Confirm before submitting sensitive media, avoid private or internal URLs, and use a dedicated token with appropriate access controls.

Risk: The package's data retention and deletion claims may not meet an organization's requirements.

Mitigation: Check the provider's terms and retention practices before uploading confidential material.

Risk: Transcripts may mishear names, figures, or specialist terminology, particularly when audio is unclear.

Mitigation: Review important outputs against the source video before publication or consequential use.
