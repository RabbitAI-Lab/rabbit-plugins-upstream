## Description:

Automatically selects a dLazy CLI audio or text-to-speech model based on the prompt.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and content creators use the skill to generate speech, music, dialogue, and sound effects through the dLazy hosted service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was partial and notes image or video capabilities beyond the stated audio/TTS purpose; the skill also documents uploading supplied media to dLazy hosted storage.

Mitigation: Review the skill instructions and install details, and only send prompts or media that are appropriate for processing by the dLazy hosted service.
