## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cooking temperature, allergy, nutrition, or food-safety guidance may be insufficient for health-sensitive situations. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as general cooking guidance and rely on specialized sources for medical diets, serious allergy risk, suspected food poisoning, or other health-sensitive decisions. <br>
Risk: Generated substitutions or technique advice may not fit a specific recipe, appliance, or ingredient constraint. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations before cooking and adjust them against the recipe, ingredient labels, equipment manuals, and local food-safety guidance. <br>
