## Description: <br>
Suno AI 音乐提示词专家 helps users turn rough music ideas into copy-ready English Suno prompts using genre, mood, instrumentation, tempo, structure, negative prompts, and iteration guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and creators use this skill to build, diagnose, and refine Suno music prompts. It provides copy-ready English prompts with Chinese explanations, variants, and adjustment guidance for better generated results. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Suno version claims and prompting advice may become outdated as the platform changes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the guidance as version-specific and review it against current Suno behavior before relying on it for important workflows. <br>
Risk: Music prompts could be used to closely copy protected songs, artists, or voices without proper rights. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for style and structure guidance, and avoid prompts intended to replicate protected works, artists, or voices. <br>
