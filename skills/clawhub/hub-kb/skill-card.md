## Description:

Provides Chinese-language knowledge-base guidance for configuring and troubleshooting proxy clients and cores, while declining provider recommendations, account support, and server administration questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ladderkit](https://clawhub.ai/user/ladderkit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users use the skill to understand, configure, and troubleshoot proxy clients and cores, including subscriptions, routing, updates, and connectivity. It also identifies provider, account, and server-side requests that are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions and screenshot error text may be sent to a remote service before sensitive information is removed.

Mitigation: Remove subscription links, passwords, tokens, node addresses, and account details from questions and screenshots before use. Review the remote MCP connection or use a verified local-only integration when troubleshooting information must remain private.
