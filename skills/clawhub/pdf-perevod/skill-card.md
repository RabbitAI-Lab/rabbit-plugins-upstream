## Description:

Translates English text in PDFs into Russian while preserving page layout, charts, and formatting, including offline OCR for scanned pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iirtegova](https://clawhub.ai/user/iirtegova)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People translating reports, presentations, or scans use this skill to produce a Russian PDF that retains the original page design and graphics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source documents, extracted text, OCR output, translations, and completed PDFs can remain in local job state or a user Deliverables folder.

Mitigation: Use only for documents permitted to persist in those locations, and manage retained files according to your data-handling policy.
