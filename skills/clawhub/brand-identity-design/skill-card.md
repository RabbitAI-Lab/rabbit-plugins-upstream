## Description:

End-to-end brand visual identity (VI) design methodology — a six-stage workflow from brand soul discovery to visual identity system building and multi-touchpoint brand manual delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Designers, brand teams, and agents use this skill to run a structured visual identity process from brand research through strategy, system design, touchpoint validation, and brand manual delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce visual identity guidance that is strategically incomplete or misleading if stages are skipped.

Mitigation: Follow the required six-stage workflow sequentially and keep rationale tied to brand strategy, user needs, competitive gaps, or technical constraints.

Risk: Color recommendations could be inaccessible if contrast is not checked.

Mitigation: Verify WCAG AA contrast before including colors in the visual identity system.
