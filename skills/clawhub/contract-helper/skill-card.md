## Description: <br>
Contract Helper helps agents draft or review influencer partnership agreements, producing agreement frameworks, clause-by-clause risk reviews, and negotiation notes for deliverables, compensation, usage rights, exclusivity, and FTC disclosure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creator managers, and agents use this skill after influencer terms are agreed in principle to draft agreement templates, review incoming agency terms, flag clause risks, and prepare negotiation points. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Contract drafts and review memos may be mistaken for legal advice or signed without appropriate review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting aids and have contracts reviewed by qualified legal counsel before execution. <br>
Risk: The skill can save influencer contract drafts, review memos, and signed deal terms in local memory. <br>
Mitigation: Review stored outputs for sensitive commercial or personal information and apply the host workspace's access controls and retention practices. <br>
