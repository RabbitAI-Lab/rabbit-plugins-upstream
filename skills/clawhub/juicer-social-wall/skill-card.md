## Description:

Run a live social media wall for an event, conference, wedding or campaign with Juicer. Collects posts with the event hashtag from Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube and more, moderates them so nothing embarrassing reaches the screen, and gives a link or embed for the big screen or website.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Event organizers use this skill to gather hashtag and account posts into a Juicer social wall, configure moderation, and display the wall on an event screen or website. During and after the event, they can review posts, moderate what appears, and request a recap.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An event hashtag may also be used for unrelated posts, or an unmoderated post may appear on a public screen.

Mitigation: Check the hashtag before setup and require approval before display when unwanted posts would be disruptive.

Risk: Periodic checks can continue after the event if left enabled.

Mitigation: Enable periodic checks only when wanted and stop them after the event.

Risk: The separate Juicer integration was not included in this package review.

Mitigation: Confirm the Juicer account and event sources before setup, and review the integration separately.
