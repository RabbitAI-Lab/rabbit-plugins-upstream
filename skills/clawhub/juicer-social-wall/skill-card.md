## Description:

Run a live social media wall for an event, conference, wedding or campaign with Juicer. Collects posts with the event hashtag from Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube and more, moderates them so nothing embarrassing reaches the screen, and gives a link or embed for the big screen or website. Moderate from chat during the event. Use for "social wall", "hashtag wall", "event wall", "live tweet wall", "show the event hashtag on screen".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Event organizers use this skill to collect social posts for a live event wall, control which posts appear through moderation, and share the wall on a screen or website.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts may appear on the event wall before an organizer reviews them.

Mitigation: Require approval for posts that need review and moderate the queue before display.

Risk: Adding social sources can incur credits or connect social accounts.

Mitigation: Check source costs first and connect only the intended social accounts.

Risk: Recurring checks may continue after the event.

Mitigation: Stop recurring checks when the event ends.
