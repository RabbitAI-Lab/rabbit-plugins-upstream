## Description:

Creates a live Juicer social wall for an event, collecting hashtag posts across supported social networks and letting organizers moderate what appears on screen.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[juicer](https://clawhub.ai/user/juicer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Event organizers use this skill to collect posts for an event hashtag and manage which posts appear on a live wall for an event screen or website.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts that organizers would not want shown may appear on a public event wall.

Mitigation: Use approval-before-display for public screens and review posts before approving them; enable available moderation controls.

Risk: A Juicer API key grants full account access.

Mitigation: Protect the integration credential and revoke it when it is no longer needed.
