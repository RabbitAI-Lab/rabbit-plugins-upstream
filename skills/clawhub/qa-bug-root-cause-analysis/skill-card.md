## Description:

Guides QA and engineering teams through symptom classification, hypothesis testing, and root-cause analysis of recurring or production bugs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers and developers use this skill to investigate defects using bug descriptions, logs, and environment details, then identify contributing causes and propose fixes and prevention measures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bug logs and order or payment examples may contain sensitive customer or financial data.

Mitigation: Use masked test data where possible; do not paste real production records, customer information, or financial credentials.

Risk: Suggested shell commands or log and monitoring access may affect production systems.

Mitigation: Review commands before running them and investigate in a controlled environment.
