## Description: <br>
Thesis Tracker records, checks, and reports on investment theses by storing assumptions, triggers, time windows, targets, and status updates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[dzy11650](https://clawhub.ai/user/dzy11650) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Investors, analysts, and research teams use this skill to turn investment ideas into structured thesis records, periodically compare market data against positive and negative triggers, and produce status reports for pending theses. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Investment theses, assumptions, targets, and checks are saved locally and may be copied into IMA notes. <br>
Mitigation: Review stored thesis content before use with sensitive portfolio strategy or proprietary research, and require explicit confirmation before each save or IMA sync. <br>
Risk: Automated thesis status updates can be mistaken for investment advice or complete validation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated reports as tracking summaries and independently verify financial data before making investment decisions. <br>
