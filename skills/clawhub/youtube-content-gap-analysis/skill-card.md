## Description:

Map observed YouTube topic and format coverage using Crawlora searches, channel samples, videos, and available captions. Use for an evidence-based content brief or sampled topic-gap map with search selection, language, transcript basis, identity, and demand limits preserved.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content strategists and creators can use this skill to map observed YouTube topic and format coverage for a defined audience, then prepare a source-linked content brief from sampled gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries may include confidential information that is sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Keep confidential information out of queries; use a scoped Crawlora API key and set request and credit budgets.
