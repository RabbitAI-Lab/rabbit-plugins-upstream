## Description:

Use SocialBu to schedule and publish social media posts and manage connected accounts, drafts, queues, analytics, automations, inbox conversations, and social listening.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Social media teams and account managers use this skill to manage connected SocialBu accounts, prepare and schedule approved posts, review performance, and manage queues, automations, inbox conversations, and listening items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorized posts, queues, or automations may publish content later.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended SocialBu workspace, accounts, content, timing, and action before authorizing changes.

Risk: Selected media is uploaded to SocialBu.

Mitigation: Review the media and confirm it is appropriate to share with the authorized SocialBu workspace.
