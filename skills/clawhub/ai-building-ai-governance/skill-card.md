## Description:

A governance and safety playbook for AI systems that build AI, covering coding agents, synthetic data and distillation, automated AI research, self-improvement risks, development gates, artifact verification, and organizational rollout.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI engineering, governance, security, and strategy teams use this skill to classify AI-building-AI scenarios, identify frontier risks, define human gates, and generate governance checklists for code, data, research, and model artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may treat the governance playbook as a security guarantee.

Mitigation: Use it as reference material and keep human review, security testing, and deployment approval gates in place.

Risk: Verification scripts can update files under the skill's own verify directory.

Mitigation: Run optional verification commands in a controlled workspace and review generated files before publishing or committing them.

Risk: The licensing and attestation text includes usage restrictions around copying, redistribution, model training, and commercial resale.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable license and accompanying attestation terms before redistribution or downstream commercial use.

Risk: AI governance, legal, and frontier-risk guidance can become stale as regulations and model capabilities change.

Mitigation: Re-check legal, policy, and security assumptions against current organizational requirements before relying on the guidance.
