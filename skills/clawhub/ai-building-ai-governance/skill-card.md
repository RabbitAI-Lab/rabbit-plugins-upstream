## Description:

A governance and safety playbook for AI systems that build, train, or improve other AI, covering coding agents, synthetic data, distillation, automated research, and self-improvement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI engineering, governance, security, and strategy teams use this skill to classify AI-building-AI workflows, assess risks, set development gates, govern synthetic data, and validate AI-generated code, research, and models.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Governance guidance may be mistaken for a security certification or an enforcement system.

Mitigation: Use it as an offline reference and have qualified reviewers verify substantive claims before applying them to production decisions.

Risk: Bundled self-audit scores and reports do not provide independent assurance, and the evidence notes an outdated manifest hash.

Mitigation: Check the current verification materials and independently validate governance claims before relying on the bundled audit results.
