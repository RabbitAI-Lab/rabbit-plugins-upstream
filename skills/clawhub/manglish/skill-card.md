## Description:

Write in Manglish, Malaysian English that sounds like how Malaysians talk, with natural Malaysian slang rather than textbook English.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People using an agent for casual Malaysian English writing use this skill to draft or rewrite text in natural Manglish. It supports casual texting, lighter semi-formal workplace phrasing, and standard English with Malaysian flavor for formal contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Informal slang and culturally specific phrasing may be unsuitable or misunderstood in formal, professional, or cross-cultural contexts.

Mitigation: Review outputs before use and choose lighter Manglish or standard English for formal communication.

Risk: Overusing particles, loanwords, or exaggerated dialect can reduce readability or sound unnatural.

Mitigation: Keep the intensity appropriate to the audience and context, using the skill's own guidance to balance casual and formal tone.
