## Description:

First-run guide for the Makernice video and image tools. Use when the user has just connected Makernice, asks how it works, wants to check their balance and spending limits, or wants to make a first low-cost test video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Makernice users can confirm their connection, understand available balances and spending limits, compare current prices, and make a low-cost image or video test with approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media generation can spend account funds, and completed outputs blocked by safety review are still charged.

Mitigation: Review the connected account's spending limits and get approval for the quoted price or an explicit budget before generating; explain that a blocked output is still charged.

Risk: Prompts are sent to Makernice, and jobs that have started cannot be canceled.

Mitigation: Tell the user that prompts go to Makernice and explain the cancellation limit before they approve a generation.
