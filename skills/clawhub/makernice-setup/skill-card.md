## Description:

First-run guide for the Makernice video and image tools. Use when the user has just connected Makernice, asks how it works, wants to check their balance and spending limits, or wants to make a first low-cost test video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Makernice users use this guide after connecting the service to check their balance and spending limits, compare current test prices, and run a low-cost image or video job after approving its price.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid media generation can spend account funds, including when a completed output is blocked by safety review.

Mitigation: Check the current estimate, available balance, and applicable account and connection limits before confirming a job.

Risk: A job cannot be canceled after it starts.

Mitigation: Review the exact model, settings, and price before authorizing generation.
