## Description: <br>
Standardized grading and feedback prompt for grading elementary school English written homework (grades 3-6). <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, parents, and teachers use this skill to check and grade written English homework for Chinese primary school students in grades 3-6. It provides structured scoring, corrections, praise, and one concrete next step in Chinese with English examples where useful. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad correction or grading requests may trigger this elementary English homework rubric when the user wants a different type of English review. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the task is grades 3-6 English homework, and ask for clarification or use a different review workflow when the request is outside that scope. <br>
Risk: Feedback may be less useful if the grade level, task type, or total points are missing or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Infer conservatively from the student's work, state the assumption, and ask for missing grading details when they materially affect scoring. <br>
