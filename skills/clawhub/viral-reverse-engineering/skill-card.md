## Description: <br>
Reverse-engineers why a social media post or video overperformed by analyzing observable inputs, identifying the repeatable mechanism, and translating it into guidance for the user's own niche. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media strategists, creators, and content teams use this skill to break down a single viral or overperforming post from visible signals, separate real drivers from incidental features, and apply the transferable principle without copying the original. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide private analytics, confidential strategy, or sensitive brand and audience materials during content intake. <br>
Mitigation: Use only content details the user is comfortable sharing, and avoid providing confidential or private analytics unless they are appropriate for the analysis context. <br>
Risk: A bare link or partial transcript can lead to unsupported conclusions if the model assumes it has seen unavailable frames, pacing, comments, or metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Analyze only supplied observable signals and explicitly name gaps in the available content evidence. <br>
Risk: Applying a viral example too literally can create derivative content or intellectual-property concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Extract the underlying mechanism and adapt it to the user's own subject, voice, and audience instead of copying the original work. <br>
