## Description: <br>
HS Code Lookup for Chinese Products. Query customs codes, tariff rates, declaration elements, and regulatory requirements via HSCIQ MCP API. Create classification consultation requests with image upload for expert review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[toucao](https://clawhub.ai/user/toucao) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, trade operations teams, and customs-compliance users use this skill to query HS codes, tariff rates, declaration elements, regulatory requirements, and historical classification examples for Chinese products. They can also create HSCIQ classification consultation requests with product images for expert review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product descriptions, product images, and QQ or WeChat contact fields may be sent to HSCIQ during consultation workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when that external sharing is approved, and do not submit confidential, regulated, or customer-sensitive material through create-guilei-form unless external human review is authorized. <br>
