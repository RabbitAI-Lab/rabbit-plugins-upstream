## Description: <br>
A Chinese tax compliance assistant for capital reduction and shareholder withdrawal scenarios, including unpaid subscribed capital reductions, individual income tax calculations, risk checks, compliance plans, and report templates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zxj2devs](https://clawhub.ai/user/zxj2devs) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, tax professionals, and developers use this skill to assess Chinese capital reduction and shareholder withdrawal tax scenarios, generate risk self-checks, individual income tax calculations, evidence checklists, and compliance report guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive tax questions, scenarios, and self-check metrics may be sent to mcp.aitaxs.top. <br>
Mitigation: Review the provider and data-handling terms before use, and avoid entering confidential company, shareholder, or tax exposure details unless approved. <br>
Risk: The skill may persist local API credentials and logs. <br>
Mitigation: Review local storage behavior before installation and remove stored credentials or logs according to the user's security policy. <br>
Risk: Autosetup can optionally alter MCP client configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Enable autosetup only after reviewing the proposed configuration changes and keeping a backup of existing MCP client settings. <br>
