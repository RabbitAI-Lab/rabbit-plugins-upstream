## Description: <br>
Tax Capital Reduction helps users assess Chinese personal income tax and compliance risks for company capital reductions, shareholder withdrawals, unpaid subscribed-capital reductions, directed reductions, loss-offset reductions, and related New Company Law procedures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Tax, finance, and compliance users use this skill to reason through capital-reduction tax treatment, individual income tax calculations, risk checks, procedural compliance, and report-ready remediation guidance for Chinese tax scenarios. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tax questions or restructuring scenarios may be sent to the cloud-backed service at mcp.aitaxs.top. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering names, IDs, bank details, or confidential transaction facts unless the publisher's data handling terms are acceptable. <br>
Risk: The skill may persist an API key and logs under the user's home directory. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only on trusted machines and review or remove local credentials and logs according to the user's retention requirements. <br>
Risk: Setup behavior can optionally modify local agent MCP configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Review configuration changes before enabling setup and rely on the skill's dry-run or backup behavior where available. <br>
Risk: The security summary notes broader cloud, persistence, and configuration behavior than the narrow capital-reduction description suggests. <br>
Mitigation: Review the security guidance before installation and limit use to scenarios where that behavior is acceptable. <br>
