## Description:

Guides users through value-based pricing decisions, from estimating willingness to pay and choosing price anchors to designing tiers and measuring results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product leaders, and sales teams use the skill to set or revise prices, assess willingness to pay by segment, design pricing tiers, and plan a measured pricing experiment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing advice can be misleading when customer value and willingness to pay have not been validated for the target segment.

Mitigation: Ground recommendations in customer-unit value, segment-level willingness-to-pay evidence, target-buyer tests, and tracked results from the planned measurement period.

Risk: The security review had incomplete component coverage, so its clean verdict does not establish that every part was fully reviewed.

Mitigation: Review the skill's generated guidance before relying on it for consequential pricing decisions; treat the scan as reassuring but not a guarantee, as its guidance states.
