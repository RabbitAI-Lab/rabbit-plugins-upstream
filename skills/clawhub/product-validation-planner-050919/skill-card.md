## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support before heavy investment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns product-validation questions into practical workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning, and next-step guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Implicit invocation is enabled and trigger terms are broad, so the skill may activate for general product, startup, prototype, or validation prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the request is about product validation or startup planning before applying the workflow, and ask only for missing information that materially changes the result. <br>
Risk: Product-validation guidance can be misleading when based on incomplete user inputs or untested assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions, success criteria, limits, remaining risks, and concrete validation steps in the response. <br>
