## Description:

Research ecommerce competitors' public Meta and TikTok ads through Glasser, identify observable creative patterns, and propose evidence-linked ad tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce marketers and creative teams use this skill to research public competitor ads, identify observable creative patterns, and plan original, evidence-linked ad tests without claiming campaign performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid data searches may incur charges, including empty-result calls.

Mitigation: Inspect pricing, set a clear budget cap before collection, and report actual charges.

Risk: Public ad examples cannot establish sales performance or prove an advertiser's identity from a keyword match alone.

Mitigation: Verify advertiser identity, link observations to sources, and label performance and coverage limits.

Risk: Competitor ads may contain creative assets or copy that the user is not permitted to reuse.

Mitigation: Propose original tests rather than copying competitor scripts, footage, or imagery.
