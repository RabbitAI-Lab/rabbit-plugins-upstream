## Description:

帮助独立教师根据学员的真实学习记录起草阶段报告和续课建议，供教师审核后与家长沟通。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

独立教师用指定学员的课后记录、作业跟进和进步证据，起草有据可查的阶段报告、下一阶段计划及续课沟通话术。教师审核后自行决定是否向家长发送。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese-language K–12 education; localize consent, curriculum, and emergency procedures before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive longitudinal records of minors could be disclosed to the wrong parent or used without consent.

Mitigation: Identify one student before reading records; check parent-facing and classroom-state sharing consent before drafting, and have the teacher review and send any approved content.

Risk: Unsupported progress claims or estimated scores could mislead families about a student's learning.

Mitigation: Tie figures and progress claims to recorded evidence; use qualitative descriptions when scored assessments are unavailable.

Risk: Chinese K–12 consent and emergency practices may not fit other jurisdictions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, consent rules, grade bands, and parent-reporting practices before using the skill elsewhere.
