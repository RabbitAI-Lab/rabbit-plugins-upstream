## Description:

Drafts evidence-based learning progress reports and renewal guidance for independent teachers working with a named student.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers in Chinese K–12 teaching use a named student's recorded lessons and learning progress to draft parent-facing stage reports and optional renewal talking points. The teacher reviews and sends any message personally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports can disclose a minor's longitudinal learning records or classroom observations without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Identify the student before reading records; confirm parent communication consent and any separate consent for sharing classroom observations before preparing parent-facing content.

Risk: Unsupported progress claims or automated delivery could mislead families.

Mitigation: Trace figures to recorded evidence, omit unsupported estimates, and require the teacher to review and send messages manually.
