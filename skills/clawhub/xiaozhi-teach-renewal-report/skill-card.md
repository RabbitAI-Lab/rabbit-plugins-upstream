## Description:

根据指定学员的真实学习记录起草有据可查的阶段报告、续课建议和家长沟通话术。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

独立教师用指定学员的课后记录、作业跟进和课时信息撰写中期、续课或期末报告，并起草供老师审阅和亲自发送的家长沟通话术。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A report could expose a student's learning history to an unauthorized recipient.

Mitigation: Confirm the named student and current workspace consent before preparing parent-facing content; use a pseudonym and have the teacher send it manually.

Risk: Unsupported scores or progress claims could mislead families about learning outcomes.

Mitigation: Trace figures and comparisons to the student's records; use qualitative descriptions when scored evidence is unavailable.
