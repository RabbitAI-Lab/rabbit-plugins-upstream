## Description:

Helps independent teachers turn a named student's recorded learning progress into a factual stage report and optional renewal guidance for parent review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to draft evidence-based mid-course, renewal, or term-end learning reports for one student at a time, with optional renewal talking points. Teachers review and send any parent-facing material themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize safety contacts, curricula, and minor-data consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Longitudinal learning records about a minor could be exposed to the wrong audience.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's alias before reading only relevant records; check parent-sharing consent before drafting parent-visible content and keep sending manual.

Risk: Reports could overstate progress or present unsupported scores as facts.

Mitigation: Tie numerical claims to recorded evidence, use qualitative descriptions where scores are unavailable, and have the teacher review the draft.

Risk: China-specific safety and consent defaults may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, education conventions, and minor-data consent rules before deployment outside mainland China.
