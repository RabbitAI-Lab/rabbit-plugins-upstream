## Description:

根据指定学员的学习记录起草有据可查的阶段报告及续课建议，供教师审核后使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

独立教师根据指定学员的课后记录、作业跟进及课时信息，起草事实、进步和计划三段式报告，并准备不施压的续课沟通建议；家长可见内容须经授权检查和教师审定。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize legal, crisis-support, curriculum and consent assumptions before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafting a report involves a selected student's sensitive learning history and consent fields.

Mitigation: Identify the student before reading records, limit access to relevant fields, check consent for parent-facing content, and require teacher review before sharing.

Risk: The legal, crisis-support, curriculum and consent guidance assumes Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these assumptions before using the skill with students or parents elsewhere.
