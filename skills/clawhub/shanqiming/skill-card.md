## Description:

A Chinese cultural naming assistant that helps generate formal names and nicknames, evaluate existing names with bazi and five-elements analysis, and compare parent-child birth charts for family-level balancing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jianfengmacn](https://clawhub.ai/user/jianfengmacn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to request culturally grounded Chinese baby or child names, assess existing names, and receive traditional bazi and five-elements context. The skill frames outputs as cultural reference and psychological meaning rather than deterministic life guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can involve sensitive family names, birth dates, and birth times.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum details needed for the requested analysis; skip parent details or exact birth times when a full family comparison is not desired.

Risk: Bazi, five-elements, and naming analysis may be overinterpreted as deterministic guidance.

Mitigation: Treat results as cultural reference and personal meaning; do not use the output as a guarantee of life outcomes or as a substitute for legal or personal decision-making.
