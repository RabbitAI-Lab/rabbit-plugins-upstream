## Description: <br>
Decide which recurring reports, briefings, and reviews should run on autopilot, which should be assisted by AI, and which should remain manual. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, teams, and operations leads use this skill to inventory recurring work, classify each ritual as Automate, Assist, or Keep Manual, define guardrails, and choose a low-risk rollout order. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Automation recommendations may affect audience-facing reports or sensitive recurring business workflows if adopted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed source access, publishing behavior, schedules, review gates, failure behavior, and ownership before automating real workflows. <br>
