## Description:

Guides Hague System international design registration practice, from filing and publication to refusals, renewals, searches and fee checks, with an optional China-specific module.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huiningip](https://clawhub.ai/user/huiningip)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Design agents, intellectual property lawyers and in-house counsel use this skill to plan and check international design filings under the Hague System, assess subsequent procedures and estimate fees against current official sources. A separate optional module covers China-specific procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fee schedules and legal requirements may change or differ by designated jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify current fees, deadlines and requirements against official WIPO or relevant national office sources before relying on an answer.

Risk: The sync script can overwrite or remove local files in the installed skill folder.

Mitigation: Run the sync script with --dry-run first and back up local customizations before syncing.
