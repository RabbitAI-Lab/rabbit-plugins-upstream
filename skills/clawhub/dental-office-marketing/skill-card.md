## Description:

Drafts marketing pages, ads, and patient emails for US dental offices, with prompts to review claims and disclosures before publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

US dental practice owners, marketing staff, and front-office managers use the skill to draft patient-facing marketing copy and organize claim evidence, disclosure notes, and items requiring practice approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The compliance checker can miss risky claims or patient identifiers while reporting “No flags.”

Mitigation: Treat checker output as a drafting aid, not compliance approval; have the practice review supporting evidence, disclosures, and consent before publication, and keep patient identifiers out of briefs.

Risk: The optional Windows application is a separate, unsigned download and is not part of the reviewed skill bundle.

Mitigation: Assess the application separately before use and verify the downloaded archive against the documented SHA-256 hash.
