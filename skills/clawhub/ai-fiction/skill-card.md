## Description:

Edits supplied fiction scenes, chapters, or passages for dialogue, pacing, and readable prose while preserving voice and story continuity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and editors use this skill to revise fiction drafts for dialogue, pacing, and clarity while retaining the author's voice and established story details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Default house style may override intended stylistic choices.

Mitigation: Provide the editing brief and author preferences, then review the edit before publication.

Risk: Consulting story notes could expose unrelated material or alter standing instructions.

Mitigation: Limit retrieval to relevant author or project notes and do not change memory or instructions without a separate request.
