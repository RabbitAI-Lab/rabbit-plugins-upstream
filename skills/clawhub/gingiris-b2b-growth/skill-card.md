This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mutable package installation can change between review and installation.

Mitigation: Install through a trusted platform-native path or pin the package and source revision before use.

Risk: Enterprise payment-leverage tactics could affect customer service availability or create business and legal exposure.

Mitigation: Avoid hidden or customer-critical dependencies, disclose any staged delivery terms, and have counsel review suspension, escrow, milestone, and collections language.

Risk: Growth benchmarks and case-study claims are heuristic and may not transfer to every SaaS segment.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against the user's segment, date, price point, sample size, activation metric, and retention data before scaling spend or sales headcount.
