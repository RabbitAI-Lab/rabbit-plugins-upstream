## Description: <br>
Builds TikTok-native Photo Mode carousel plans with slide structure, overlay copy, captions, and WoopSocial PHOTO publish configuration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media teams and agents use this skill to plan TikTok-native Photo Mode carousels that fit a brand, goal, and niche. It also helps prepare the corresponding WoopSocial PHOTO publish configuration while keeping image creation, disclosure, and metrics boundaries explicit. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incorrect privacy, posting mode, image asset, or branded-content disclosure settings could publish a TikTok Photo post in a way the user did not intend. <br>
Mitigation: Review the privacy level, DIRECT_POST versus MEDIA_UPLOAD choice, image assets, and isYourBrand/isBrandedContent flags before validating or publishing. <br>
Risk: The skill can support publishing guidance, but the artifact states that images are created outside WoopSocial and performance metrics must not be fabricated. <br>
Mitigation: Generate or approve image assets separately, use native analytics for reporting, and keep claims about reach or virality qualified. <br>
