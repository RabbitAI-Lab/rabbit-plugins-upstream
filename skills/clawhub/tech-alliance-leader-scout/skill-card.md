## Description:

Identifies technology alliance partner candidates for chain leader enterprises and generates executive decision reports using policy, standards, market, patent, industry, and candidate evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business strategy, corporate development, and industry research teams use this skill to assess alliance timing, identify capability gaps, shortlist partner candidates, and design cooperation paths for a chain leader enterprise in a target technology domain.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public-source business research can include outdated or incorrect policy, market, patent, or candidate evidence.

Mitigation: Verify cited facts, publication dates, and source quality before using the report for executive decisions.

Risk: The skill may lead an agent to process confidential user-provided business materials.

Mitigation: Only provide confidential internal documents when that use is intended and appropriate access controls are in place.

Risk: Partner scoring and cooperation recommendations may be misleading when evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Keep unsupported claims and candidate rankings marked for verification until the underlying evidence is reviewed.
