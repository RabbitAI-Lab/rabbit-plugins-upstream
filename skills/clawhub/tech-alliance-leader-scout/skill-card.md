## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Internal strategy notes, interview materials, or other sensitive business context may be shared with the agent or external research tools during analysis.

Mitigation: Review inputs before use, remove unnecessary confidential details, and limit external research to sources approved for the engagement.

Risk: Partner rankings and executive recommendations can be misleading if market figures, deployment claims, patents, or policy signals are not verified.

Mitigation: Require source citations for key KPIs, mark unverifiable claims as pending or requiring verification, and keep uncertain candidates in a verification pool rather than the primary recommendation list.
