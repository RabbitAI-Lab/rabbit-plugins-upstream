## Description: <br>
ToB POC budget justification assistant that takes customer, industry, and POC goal details and produces a Markdown report covering scope, resource estimates, budget rationale, and success criteria. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[william202404](https://clawhub.ai/user/william202404) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, presales, and project teams use this skill to turn early POC conversations into bounded scope, resource, timeline, budget, and success-criteria guidance. It is intended for commercial ToB planning workflows where generated estimates are reviewed against the specific customer and project context. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: POC budget, timeline, and industry benchmark guidance may be inaccurate for a specific customer, region, contract, or delivery team. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated reports against current project data, commercial policy, and accountable sales or project owners before sharing or relying on them. <br>
Risk: Interactive and CLI inputs can include customer, industry, budget, and decision-maker details. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized or approved customer information, and avoid entering confidential data unless the user is authorized to use it in the local environment. <br>
