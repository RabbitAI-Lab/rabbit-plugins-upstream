## Description:

Prepare verified analytical outlines and create or track visual reports through ExcelDashboard AI MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[exceldashboard.ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/exceldashboard.ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and business users use this skill to turn verified Excel or CSV data, metrics, or research findings into visual reports and track report generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Report outlines, metrics, chart data, and source context are sent to ExcelDashboard AI.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential, regulated, or customer-identifying data unless your organization approves the service.

Risk: Generated reports can be accessed through read-only report links.

Mitigation: Confirm organizational approval before sharing report links or including sensitive information.
