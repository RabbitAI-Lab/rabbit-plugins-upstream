## Description:

Creates 4×4 GPT Image sprite sheets, slices them into animation frames, and exports looping GIFs and texture atlases for Phaser and Godot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game developers can use the skill to generate character animation sheets from a prompt or reference image, then prepare frames, GIFs, and atlases for Phaser or Godot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference images are sent to an external image service.

Mitigation: Do not send private or proprietary images or prompts unless you are authorized to share them.

Risk: The installer version is not pinned, and the security review had medium confidence with part of script analysis incomplete.

Mitigation: Check the installer source and version before installing, and review the skill before use.
