## Description:

A Chinese-language bibliotherapy recommendation skill that suggests books offering methods, understanding, or hope based on a user's emotional distress or real-life difficulties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangdaozhenren](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangdaozhenren)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to receive supportive Chinese book recommendations for emotional states such as anxiety, low mood, loneliness, burnout, grief, perfectionism, procrastination, and life difficulties such as work, study, family, relationships, health, finances, and transitions. It is a recommendation aid, not a medical, mental-health, movie, series, or book-download service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake bibliotherapy-style recommendations for medical or mental-health treatment.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as supportive reading only and direct crisis, medical emergency, self-harm, severe psychiatric distress, or persistent functional impairment to qualified professionals or local emergency services.

Risk: The listed hotline and emergency resources are China-specific.

Mitigation: Use locale-appropriate emergency and mental-health resources outside China before relying on the listed contacts.

Risk: Expanded recommendations beyond the seed library may include inaccurate or nonexistent book entries.

Mitigation: Verify that any added title is a real published work before presenting it as a recommendation.
