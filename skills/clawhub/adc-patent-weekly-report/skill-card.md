## Description:

Generates weekly ADC patent intelligence reports for WO office publications in a specified week, using filtering, P025 triad extraction, and AI-assisted insight rewriting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent intelligence, BD, and R&D users use this skill to generate concise ADC weekly reports from authorized patent and life-science source material. It supports triage of newly published patents, key development signals, and opportunity or risk notes without replacing legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent, market, or technical claims may be inaccurate if source data is incomplete, unavailable, or outside the intended retrieval scope.

Mitigation: Use authorized PatSnap/Zhihuiya data and user-provided materials, and clearly state data sources, retrieval scope, time range, and uncertainty.

Risk: FTO, infringement, validity, or similar legal discussion could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat these sections as research support only and route legal conclusions to qualified counsel.

Risk: Competitive intelligence summaries may overstate opportunities or risks when generated from filtered patent signals alone.

Mitigation: Review outputs against primary patent records and domain expert judgment before using them for business decisions.
