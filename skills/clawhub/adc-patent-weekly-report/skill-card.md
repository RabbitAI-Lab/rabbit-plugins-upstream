## Description:

ADC Patent Weekly Report helps agents generate weekly ADC patent intelligence reports from WO office publications for a specified week, using relevance filtering, P025 triad extraction, and concise insight rewriting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent intelligence, BD, and R&D users use this skill to turn ADC patent publications, product or technology inputs, and supporting materials into a weekly report with key developments, risk and opportunity notes, and source transparency.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent intelligence may be incomplete or misleading if the search scope, publication week, source materials, or data provenance are wrong.

Mitigation: Verify search scope, dates, data sources, and cited materials before relying on generated summaries.

Risk: FTO, infringement, validity, or other legal conclusions may be mistaken if treated as legal advice.

Mitigation: Use generated legal-risk notes as triage guidance only and route legal conclusions to qualified counsel.
