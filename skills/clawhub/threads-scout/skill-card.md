## Use Case:

OpenClaw users and agents use this skill for social listening, creator research, and tracking public conversations on Threads and Bluesky.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Repeated data calls can consume ClawLink credit.

Mitigation: Check current per-call prices and seek approval before large batches or costly calls.

Risk: Use requires the ClawLink plugin and one-time pairing.

Mitigation: Explain the setup and obtain the user's approval before installing or pairing.

Risk: Troubleshooting reports are sent to ClawLink.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive information in reports.
