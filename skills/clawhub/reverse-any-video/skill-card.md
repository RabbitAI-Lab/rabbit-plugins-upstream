## Description:

Rebuild any video file as an editable HyperFrames project - motion graphics, talking heads, live action or AI-generated footage. Measure and watch it, rebuild every layer, then check the render against the original frame by frame. Use to reverse, recreate, replicate or copy a video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Video editors and motion designers use this skill to turn footage they have the right to use into editable HyperFrames projects that closely match the source video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid media generation and cloud processing may use credits, while footage or voice may be sent to external services without clear approval.

Mitigation: Set a spending cap, make credit use visible, and get approval before sending footage or voice to external services; verify usage reporting and use a reviewed, fixed CLI version.
