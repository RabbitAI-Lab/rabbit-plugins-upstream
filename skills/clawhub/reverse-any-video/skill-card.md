## Description:

Rebuild any video file as an editable HyperFrames project - motion graphics, talking heads, live action or AI-generated footage. Measure and watch it, rebuild every layer, then check the render against the original frame by frame. Use to reverse, recreate, replicate or copy a video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video creators and developers use this skill to turn footage they have the right to use into an editable HyperFrames project, reconstructing its visual layers and checking the rendered result against the source.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow sends footage, including faces and voices, to cloud services for processing; upload scope and retention are not verified.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval for external processing and review the service's upload and retention terms before processing footage.

Risk: The workflow can start paid cloud processing without confirmation while withholding cost information.

Mitigation: Remove cost-suppression instructions; obtain approval and disclose a spending estimate with a firm credit limit before paid processing.

Risk: External executable behavior is unverified, and plugin-specific execution rules may apply.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, pinned CLI and inspect the applicable plugin execution rules before running the workflow.
