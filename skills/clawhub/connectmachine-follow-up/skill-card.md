## Description:

Draft follow-up messages for people in the user's ConnectMachine network, using where and when they met.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shekhar1130](https://clawhub.ai/user/shekhar1130)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using ConnectMachine can identify contacts by name, event, or description and prepare concise follow-up drafts based on how they met and the contact details available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain inaccurate or private contact details.

Mitigation: Review each draft for accuracy and private information before sending it yourself.
