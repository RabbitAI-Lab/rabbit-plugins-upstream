## Description:

Draft follow-up messages for people in the user's ConnectMachine network, using where and when they met.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shekhar1130](https://clawhub.ai/user/shekhar1130)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

ConnectMachine users can draft concise, personalized follow-up messages to people in their network, referring to where and when they met.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts use contact details and personal notes, which may include sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review each draft for accuracy and sensitive details before sending it yourself; the skill does not send messages.
