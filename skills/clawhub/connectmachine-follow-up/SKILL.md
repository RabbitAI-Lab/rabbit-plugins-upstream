---
description: Draft follow-up messages for people in the user's ConnectMachine network, using where and when they met.
argument-hint: "[who to follow up with, e.g. a name, an event, or 'people I met this week']"
---

Draft follow-ups for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Find the people. For a name, use `find_contact`. For an event or a description, use `search_contacts` or `list_contacts` with `search`. If nothing was given, ask who to follow up with.
2. Read each person's details with `get_contact`: where and when they met, notes, company and role.
3. Write one short, personal draft per person that refers to how they met. Keep each under 100 words.
4. Show the drafts to the user. Don't send anything. Offer to adjust the tone or add a next step.
