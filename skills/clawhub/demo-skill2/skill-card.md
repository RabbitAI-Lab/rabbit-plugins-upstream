## Description: <br>
Reviews a short product idea and returns structured feedback. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jshiu0915](https://clawhub.ai/user/jshiu0915) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, founders, and product teams use this skill to critique a short product idea, summarize its target user and core value, and identify practical suggestions plus one validation risk. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The declared Read capability is unnecessary for most product-idea critique workflows and could expand data-access expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with user-provided idea text and confirm the agent follows the stated boundary of not reading private files. <br>
