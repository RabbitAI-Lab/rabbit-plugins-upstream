## Description:

Helps people memorize Bible verses with Remember Me by adding verses, practicing due reviews, finding daily readings, and browsing published collections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pschaffl](https://clawhub.ai/user/pschaffl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using Remember Me can add and organize verses to memorize, practice recall, follow a daily Bible reading plan, and discover public verse collections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Signing in allows an assistant to read and change personal verses and collections.

Mitigation: Review additions, edits, and deletions before approving them; use the public server for read-only collection browsing.
