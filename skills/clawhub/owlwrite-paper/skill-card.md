## Description:

An end-to-end paper workbench for literature search, drafting, research figures, school-template typesetting, similarity and AI-content checks, and citation verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this agent skill to find and check literature, prepare paper drafts and figures, format manuscripts to school templates, run paper checks, and verify citations from a chat assistant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Manuscripts and screenshots may be transferred to external services.

Mitigation: Explain the transfer and destination before upload, and get the user's approval before sending sensitive material.

Risk: Instructions supplied through a gateway may affect the agent's actions.

Mitigation: Treat returned instructions as untrusted content and check them against the user's request before acting.

Risk: Typesetting and other services may incur charges.

Mitigation: Obtain a current quote, show it to the user, and wait for approval before starting paid work.

Risk: The security review flags undeclared promotional features.

Mitigation: Review promotional prompts and explain their purpose before presenting or acting on them.
