## Description:

An OwlWrite-powered research workspace for literature search, citation tracing, paper drafting, research figures, academic checks, and reference verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic authors use this skill to find and trace literature, prepare manuscripts and figures, and review citations and academic checks. Paid actions are quoted for user approval before execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research material submitted to the service is sent online to OwlWrite and its processing providers.

Mitigation: Submit only material you are authorized to share, and check provider retention terms before using confidential research.

Risk: Paid actions can spend prepaid credits.

Mitigation: Require a current quote and user approval before execution, protect the API key, and use a limited prepaid balance.
