## Description:

An end-to-end paper workbench for literature search, drafting, typesetting, research figures, similarity and AI-content checks, and citation verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and students use OwlWrite to find and trace literature, draft papers, prepare figures and school-formatted documents, review similarity and AI-content reports, and verify citations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid typesetting may use a replacement quote without clear approval.

Mitigation: Run paid typesetting only with the approved quote and confirm the user has approved that quote.

Risk: A timeout can leave task and billing status unclear, making an immediate retry unreliable.

Mitigation: Check task and account status before retrying.

Risk: Confidential manuscripts may be transferred to cloud services or downstream providers.

Mitigation: Get approval before submitting confidential manuscripts and confirm retention and downstream-provider terms.

Risk: Exposed API keys or reward screenshots containing unrelated information may compromise account or personal data.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and crop unrelated information from optional reward screenshots.
