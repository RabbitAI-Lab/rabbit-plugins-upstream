## Description:

Provides a conversational workbench for literature search, paper drafting, research figures, school-template typesetting, similarity and AI-content checks, and citation verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic writers use the skill to find literature, prepare and format paper drafts, create research figures, and check citations or manuscript reports. Paid tasks require a current quote and the user's approval before execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags a concern about paid typesetting without approved consent.

Mitigation: Show the current quote and obtain the user's explicit approval before starting any paid task.

Risk: The security review reports extra promotional behavior.

Mitigation: Keep campaign and promotional actions separate from the requested work, and surface them only when relevant to the user's request.

Risk: The security review raises concerns about data handling and scope, and notes that its scan was partial.

Mitigation: Review the remote service and data handling terms before sending manuscripts; provide only the files needed for the requested task and protect API credentials.
