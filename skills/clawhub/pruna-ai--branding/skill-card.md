## Description: <br>
Use when applying official Pruna brand assets - logo selection, colors, and overlay rules for launches, social, and video. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, and media producers use this skill to select official Pruna logo variants, apply brand colors, and place Pruna marks in launch assets, social media, video overlays, and HTML or HyperFrames compositions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: An agent may copy Pruna logo assets into projects or apply them through media overlays where brand use needs approval. <br>
Mitigation: Review the intended placement and brand-use instructions before accepting generated assets or overlay commands. <br>
Risk: Generated or modified marks could misrepresent the official Pruna logo. <br>
Mitigation: Use the bundled logo kit files as-is, scale uniformly, and avoid regenerating, recoloring, stretching, or warping the mark. <br>
