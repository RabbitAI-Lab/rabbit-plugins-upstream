## Description:

Hreflang and international SEO audit, validation, and generation that detects common mistakes, validates language and region codes, and generates correct hreflang implementations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to audit multi-language or multi-region sites for hreflang correctness, localization parity, locale formatting, and culturally appropriate international SEO recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO, cultural tone, and localization recommendations may not match every brand, market, audience, or live site constraint.

Mitigation: Review generated recommendations before applying them, especially cultural adaptation, localization defaults, and implementation changes.
