## Description: <br>
AI travel assistant that provides worldwide visa information, currency conversion, airport status, local events, cultural tips, emergency contacts, stay recommendations, hidden local spots, and food guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Travelers and travel-focused agents use this skill to answer pre-trip and in-trip questions about destinations, entry requirements, local conditions, practical recommendations, and travel planning details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

Mitigation: Independently verify important travel, visa, emergency, and currency information with authoritative current sources before relying on it. <br>
Risk: Teneo Protocol and crypto-native monetization references are promotional and may involve separate payment-network terms or operational risks. <br>
Mitigation: Evaluate the referenced networks, payment terms, and operational requirements separately before using monetization features. <br>
