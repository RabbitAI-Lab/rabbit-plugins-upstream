## Description:

Plans short product videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Meta ads, including scripts, shot guidance, platform checks, cost estimates, and creative variants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers, creative strategists, and media buyers use this skill to plan short-form product ads, write scripts and shot lists, check platform requirements, estimate production costs before generation, and plan distinct creative tests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded product references or identity recordings may be shared or retained by external generation services.

Mitigation: Use only assets approved for external processing, avoid confidential designs and private identity recordings, and review Varg's privacy policy before generating. Use script-only planning or local footage editing when asset upload is not appropriate.
