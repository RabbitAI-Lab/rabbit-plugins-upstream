## Description:

Ecommerce video ad skills for Claude Code and AI agents: plans and makes short product videos that sell on TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels and Meta ads, with AI or real footage. Asks the four questions that change the rules (where it runs, product facts, assets, budget), picks angle, hook, format and on-camera character, writes a second-by-second script, keeps the real product identical in AI shots, applies platform rules (AI labels, TikTok Shop bans on AI voices, slideshows and price mentions, the FTC fake-testimonial rule, music licensing), quotes cost before generating and plans different-concept variants. Hands off to product-video-hooks, tiktok-shop-video, ai-ugc-ad-video, product-photo-to-video, ad-creative-testing and ecommerce-video-edit. NOT for app launch videos, music videos or brand films with nothing to sell.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers, creators, and media buyers use the skill to plan short product ads for TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels, and Meta. It helps turn verified product facts, available footage, and a budget into platform-aware concepts, scripts, and shot plans, with optional video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generation sends selected product assets to Varg for processing and spends credits.

Mitigation: Confirm permission for photos, faces, and voices, then review the cost estimate before approving generation; planning and scripts do not require generation access.
