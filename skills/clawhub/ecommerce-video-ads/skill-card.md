## Description:

Plans short ecommerce video ads for TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels, and Meta, including concepts, scripts, production guidance, and platform checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use the skill to plan product-focused short video ads, write scripts and shot plans, and check creative choices against platform requirements before production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images, faces, voices, or other confidential assets may be sent to Varg or selected generation providers.

Mitigation: Use only assets approved for external processing and review the applicable provider privacy terms before sharing confidential material.

Risk: Video generation can incur usage costs, and the supplied cost guidance is not fully consistent.

Mitigation: Obtain a current estimate and explicit spending approval before generating; rely on the live estimate rather than approximate cost examples.

Risk: Platform requirements for ads and synthetic media can change.

Mitigation: Check current platform rules and required AI disclosures before publishing.
