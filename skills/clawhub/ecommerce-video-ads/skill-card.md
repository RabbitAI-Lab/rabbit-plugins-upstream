## Description:

Plans and creates short ecommerce video ads for TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels, and Meta using AI or real footage, with platform-specific rules and product claims in mind.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and ad creators use this skill to turn product facts, available assets, platform, and budget into video ad concepts, timed scripts, and production guidance for TikTok, Reels, and Meta.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos or other assets may be sent to an external generation service without clear notice or approval.

Mitigation: Explain what assets will be sent and get approval before submitting them; review the service's asset handling terms.

Risk: The security scan reports incomplete coverage because a local path-like reference could not be resolved; it also advises considering the unpinned installation CLI.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and verify or pin the installation source before installing.
