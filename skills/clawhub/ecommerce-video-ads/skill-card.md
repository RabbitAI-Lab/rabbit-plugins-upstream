## Description:

Ecommerce video ad skills for Claude Code and AI agents: plans and makes short product videos that sell on TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels and Meta ads, with AI or real footage. Asks the four questions that change the rules (where it runs, product facts, assets, budget), picks angle, hook, format and on-camera character, writes a second-by-second script, keeps the real product identical in AI shots, applies platform rules (AI labels, TikTok Shop bans on AI voices, slideshows and price mentions, the FTC fake-testimonial rule, music licensing), quotes cost before generating and plans different-concept variants. Use when someone wants a viral TikTok, Reels or TikTok Shop video for a product, ecommerce ad creatives or "10 ads for my product". Hands off to product-video-hooks, tiktok-shop-video, ai-ugc-ad-video, product-photo-to-video, ad-creative-testing and ecommerce-video-edit. NOT for app launch videos, music videos or brand films with nothing to sell.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn verified product facts, available footage or images, and a budget into platform-aware short-form ad concepts, scripts, shot lists, and variant plans. It helps teams plan production while checking product claims, platform requirements, asset fidelity, and cost approval.
