## Description: <br>
Write a specific, non-generic cover letter that connects your evidence to the role and produces a tight 3-4 paragraph letter tailored to the company. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external applicants, and career-support agents use this skill to draft a concise cover letter that connects a candidate's strongest evidence to a specific role and company. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated cover letters may include incorrect, overstated, or insufficiently specific claims if the user provides weak or inaccurate evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Review the final letter against the candidate's actual achievements and the target job description before sending or exporting it. <br>
Risk: The skill may be used in operational workflows involving email, application submission, or file export. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the exact recipient, role, company, reason, and final content before any write, email, deployment, deletion, moderation, or transfer action. <br>
