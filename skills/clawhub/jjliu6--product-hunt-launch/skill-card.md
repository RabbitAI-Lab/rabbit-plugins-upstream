## Description:

Helps agents quickly prepare ready-to-paste Product Hunt launch submissions, including core copy, maker details, media guidance, and launch timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjliu6](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjliu6)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, makers, and launch teams use this skill to draft Product Hunt submission fields quickly with minimal back-and-forth. It helps produce launch copy, topic suggestions, maker and shoutout guidance, media requirements, first-comment copy, and timing recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated launch copy could contain incorrect or unsuitable public-facing claims.

Mitigation: Review all generated Product Hunt copy before pasting or publishing it publicly.

Risk: The skill may inspect product URLs, repository context, and prior conversation details to draft submissions quickly.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for intended Product Hunt launch preparation and avoid sharing sensitive private product details unless they are needed for the launch copy.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for some general launch discussions.

Mitigation: Confirm that Product Hunt launch help is desired before relying on its generated submission guidance.
