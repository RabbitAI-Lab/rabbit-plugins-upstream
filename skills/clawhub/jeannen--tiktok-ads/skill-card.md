## Description:

Run TikTok advertising campaigns with guidance on creative strategy, campaign structure, audience targeting, bidding, and optimization for In-Feed Ads, Spark Ads, and TopView across B2B SaaS, apps, and consumer brands targeting younger and mobile-first audiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth practitioners, and agents supporting advertisers use this skill to plan TikTok campaigns, choose ad formats, structure targeting and bidding, and optimize creative-first tests. It supports manual setup guidance and an AdKit CLI path when execution is requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may prefer the AdKit CLI when the user asks it to execute TikTok ad operations.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before commands change campaigns, budgets, audiences, or account data.
