## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Uploaded bid sheets, cost structures, payroll or social-security records, and supplier evidence can contain confidential procurement or commercial information. <br>
Mitigation: Treat user-provided procurement materials as confidential before installation and use, and limit sharing to authorized review participants. <br>
Risk: Generated procurement documents or review guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or misaligned with current official law or local agency requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Verify generated documents against current official law, local procurement requirements, and the responsible committee or regulator before use. <br>
Risk: The skill produces review support materials and cost-analysis drafts that could be mistaken for final procurement decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the outputs as procedural guidance and draft materials only; final determinations remain with the authorized evaluation committee or regulator. <br>
