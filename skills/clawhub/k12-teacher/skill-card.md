## Description:

Provides structured, syllabus-aligned K–12 lessons, assessments, and feedback adapted to a learner’s grade and performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnsmithfan](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnsmithfan)

### License/Terms of Use:

GPL-3.0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to plan and deliver grade-appropriate K–12 instruction, check understanding, and provide targeted feedback across subjects. Its curriculum references focus on Chinese Ministry of Education standards.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-oriented syllabus references may not match a learner's local curriculum or current requirements.

Mitigation: Review syllabus sources and applicability before relying on lessons or enabling syllabus update automation.

Risk: Running bundled shell scripts may perform local validation or maintenance actions.

Mitigation: Run scripts only when you intend those local actions.
