## Description:

Provides structured, syllabus-aligned K-12 instruction, assessment, and feedback across subjects with adaptive teaching strategies based on learner level.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnsmithfan](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnsmithfan)

### License/Terms of Use:

GNU GPL v3

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to guide an AI agent through grade-aware lesson delivery, assessment, feedback, and remediation for K-12 subjects. The skill is especially oriented to English-language, China/MOE-aligned primary and secondary curriculum support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated lessons, assessments, or feedback may be factually incorrect or mismatched to a learner's needs.

Mitigation: Review factual content and age appropriateness before using outputs with students.

Risk: The curriculum framing may not match every school system or local standard.

Mitigation: Confirm that the English-language, China/MOE-oriented K-12 context fits the intended teaching environment before installation or use.

Risk: Future syllabus update workflows could introduce unreviewed external content.

Mitigation: Review the update script and source list before enabling or running any scheduled update process.
