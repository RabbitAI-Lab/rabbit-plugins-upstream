## Description:

This Chinese-language skill helps agents design auditable contribution measurement and distribution models for communities or platforms, including contribution inventories, token or points rules, and risk boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and community or platform designers use this skill to convert contribution-based value sharing goals into structured contribution inventories, measurable rules, distribution proportions, traceability requirements, and compliance stop conditions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill discusses token and smart-contract distribution models that may be mistaken for legal, investment, or deployment advice.

Mitigation: Use the output for structured design and risk identification only; obtain qualified legal review and technical audit before any real implementation.

Risk: Incorrect contribution data, oracle inputs, or smart-contract logic could automate unfair or irreversible distributions.

Mitigation: Require objective contribution metrics where possible, community review for hard-to-measure contributions, multi-source oracle checks, formal contract review, and emergency stop controls.

Risk: Contribution or token narratives can be misused for financing schemes or retail token issuance.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's compliance stop conditions before distribution design when tokenized financing, retail issuance, or China-specific virtual-currency restrictions may apply.
