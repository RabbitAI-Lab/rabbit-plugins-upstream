## Description: <br>
Helps an agent answer questions about market time cycles, seasonality, peak left/right shifts, and timing windows while emphasizing confirmation by traditional technical signals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent operators use this skill to route and shape educational technical-analysis responses about cycle timing, commodity seasonality, and peak-shift interpretation. It is intended to frame cycles as probability windows that require confirmation, not automatic trading instructions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat cycle windows or seasonality as financial advice or automatic trading signals. <br>
Mitigation: Present the skill as educational support and require traditional technical confirmation before any market action. <br>
Risk: Cycle analysis can encourage bottom-fishing during bear markets or broken downtrends. <br>
Mitigation: Warn users to respect the prevailing trend and avoid acting on cycle timing alone in bearish conditions. <br>
