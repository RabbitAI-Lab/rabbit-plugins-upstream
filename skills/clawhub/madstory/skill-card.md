## Description:

Madstory is a Chinese-first cinematic storyboard engine that helps creators plan video-generation and image-generation prompts for Seedance 2.5/2.0, Seedream 4.x/5.x, and compatible platforms across 11 storyboard workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, directors, marketers, ecommerce teams, and short-drama producers use this skill to turn concepts, scripts, products, or reference media into structured storyboard plans, shot lists, prompts, timelines, and pre-flight checks for video or image generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-first behavior and broad creative routing may produce an unexpected language, mode, or scope for generic image or storyboard requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested mode, output language, and target platform before relying on generated storyboard or prompt output.

Risk: The skill may guide workflows that use user-provided media with external generation platforms.

Mitigation: Review user-provided media and applicable platform privacy or usage terms before uploading it to any external generation service.

Risk: Storyboard and prompt outputs may still require human review for model limitations such as face consistency, text rendering, motion stability, and platform-version capability differences.

Mitigation: Use the skill's version checks, pre-flight checklist, known-risk notes, and defect-log loop before production use.
