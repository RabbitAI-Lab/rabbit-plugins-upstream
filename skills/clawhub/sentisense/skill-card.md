## Description:

SentiSense is a read-only financial intelligence API for stock prices, insider and politician trades, institutional flows, AI insights, and news sentiment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use the skill to retrieve read-only U.S. stock-market data and signals for research and analysis; its output is informational, not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and watchlist tickers are sent to SentiSense.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential watchlists or tickers unless you are comfortable sharing them with the service.

Risk: An API key and its keep link are sensitive credentials.

Mitigation: Protect both; keep them out of logs, commits, and shared screenshots, and only share the keep link with its intended user.
