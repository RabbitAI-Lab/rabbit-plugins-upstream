## Description:

Provides AI agents read-only access to US stock prices, sentiment, filings, institutional activity, options data, earnings, and market research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill to retrieve US market data and research signals for stock analysis. Results are informational and not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Temporary API keys and their keep links can be used by anyone who obtains them.

Mitigation: Keep both out of logs, commits, and shared screenshots; pass the keep link only to the intended account holder.
