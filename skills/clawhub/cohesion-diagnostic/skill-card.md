## Description:

诊断英文心理学论文结论或讨论部分的衔接与连贯问题，并输出结构化中文诊断报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jeoyee](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeoyee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to evaluate cohesion in English psychology conclusion or discussion sections. It checks topic sentences, information flow, referential clarity, connectives, and hedging, then returns targeted revision guidance in Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill analyzes user-provided academic writing, which may include unpublished research, confidential drafts, or identifiable study details.

Mitigation: Use only text the user is authorized to share and avoid submitting confidential or embargoed material unless the deployment permits that data handling.

Risk: The diagnostic report may apply rubric thresholds mechanically or overstate cohesion issues in writing that requires disciplinary judgment.

Mitigation: Treat the output as writing feedback for human review and verify suggested revisions against the paper's argument, target journal norms, and advisor or reviewer expectations.
