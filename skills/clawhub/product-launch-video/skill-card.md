## Description:

Turns a product URL, script, or brief into a branded product-launch or promotional video using HyperFrames.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn product websites, scripts, or briefs into narrated or silent launch videos with branded visuals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Networked HyperFrames commands may update globally installed skills or install live registry blocks.

Mitigation: Run in a sandboxed workspace and confirm skill updates and registry installs before proceeding.

Risk: Capturing a private product URL may expose sensitive site content to networked tools.

Mitigation: Use only authorized URLs and avoid sensitive private sites unless capture is necessary.

Risk: Saved project preferences may affect later sessions.

Mitigation: Review stored preferences before reusing the workflow.
