This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and video creators use this skill to turn product websites, scripts, or briefs into storyboards, branded frames, and a rendered launch video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Networked tooling can update installed skills and add registry blocks without pinned versions.

Mitigation: Review the HyperFrames CLI and registry before use, confirm updates, and run in an isolated project.

Risk: Hosted capture and media services may receive private product material or rely on provider credentials.

Mitigation: Use sensitive material and credentials only when the intended provider integrations are approved.
