## Description:

Turns product URLs, scripts, or briefs into branded product launch and promotional videos using a storyboard, captured assets, audio, and rendered frames.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a product website or creative brief into a storyboarded launch video, with optional narration and music, then review and render the result.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Remote HyperFrames commands may change installed skills or execute updated code.

Mitigation: Review proposed updates before allowing them and prefer a pinned, reviewed HyperFrames version.

Risk: Product-site capture and media generation can access external URLs and configured HeyGen or vision-provider credentials.

Mitigation: Review target URLs and media inputs, and grant only the credentials needed for the chosen workflow.
