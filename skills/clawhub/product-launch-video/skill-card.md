## Description: <br>
Turns a product or marketing URL, pasted script, or brief into a product launch or promo video with brand capture, storyboard and script planning, HyperFrames frame generation, audio, captions, transitions, and final MP4 rendering. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn product pages, launch briefs, or pasted scripts into promotional videos. It orchestrates capture, story planning, visual design, frame construction, preview checks, and final rendering for product launches, feature reveals, demos, and site showcases. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill silently updates installed HyperFrames skills globally before running. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installing and use it only where automatic global HyperFrames skill updates are acceptable. <br>
Risk: The workflow can capture product sites, use configured API or HeyGen credentials, store project preferences, and mutate generated files. <br>
Mitigation: Run it in an appropriate workspace with approved credentials, then verify the brief and preview before rendering. <br>
