## Description:

Find and compare private clubs worldwide by location, type, amenities, and waitlist status through the Private Clubs directory.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

People looking for private clubs can search by location and features, compare club profiles, and check available waitlist information. Agents can connect to the read-only directory to provide linked results without guessing missing details or membership fees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Club-related queries are sent to an external Private Clubs MCP service.

Mitigation: Review the service connection and avoid including sensitive information in club queries.
