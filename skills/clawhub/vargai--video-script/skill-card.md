## Description:

Turns a video brief and fact sheet into a timed script with spoken lines, on-screen text, visuals, sound, three hooks, and checks for pacing and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to draft and check timed scripts for ads, social videos, demos, reviews, explainers, and short drama from a brief and supplied facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
