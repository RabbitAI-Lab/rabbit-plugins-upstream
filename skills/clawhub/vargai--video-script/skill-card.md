## Description:

Turns a brief and facts file into a timed video script with three hook options and checks for pacing, shot length, sourcing, and generic phrasing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and marketing teams use it to turn a brief and verified facts into timed scripts for ads, demos, reviews, social videos, explainers, and short dramas. It aligns spoken lines, on-screen text, visuals, and sound to a defined runtime.

Global
