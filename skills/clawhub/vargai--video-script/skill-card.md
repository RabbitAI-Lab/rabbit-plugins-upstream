## Description:

Creates a timed video script with spoken lines, on-screen text, visuals, sound, three hooks, and pacing checks from a brief and fact sheet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and video producers use the skill to turn a brief and sourced facts into a timed script for ads, demos, reviews, social videos, explainers, or short drama. It helps shape the beats, hooks, visuals, sound, and spoken lines before production.

Global
