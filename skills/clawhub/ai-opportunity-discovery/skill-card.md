## Description:

Interviews business owners or managers about workflows, tools, and pain points, then produces an evidence-based AI opportunity assessment with prioritized use cases, feasibility scoring, risks, build-vs-buy guidance, and a phased roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[arbazex](https://clawhub.ai/user/arbazex)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, managers, and decision-makers use this skill to identify where AI, automation, or off-the-shelf software can improve real business workflows. It guides an agent through discovery questions, opportunity scoring, no-AI filtering, build-vs-buy reasoning, and roadmap creation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share business process details, tools, constraints, and pain points during discovery.

Mitigation: Avoid secrets, credentials, and highly sensitive customer data unless the agent environment is approved for that information.

Risk: The final opportunity assessment and roadmap may influence spending or operational changes.

Mitigation: Treat the roadmap as advisory and validate recommendations with qualified business, legal, security, or operational reviewers before acting.
