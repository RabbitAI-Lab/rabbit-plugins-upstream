# Scoring Rubric

Load this file during Phase 3 of the AI Opportunity Discovery skill.

## Business impact (1–5)

| Score | Meaning                                                                             |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Negligible time/cost saved; no customer or risk effect                              |
| 2     | Minor efficiency gain in one role                                                   |
| 3     | Measurable time/cost savings, or a clear CX improvement, in one team                |
| 4     | Significant savings or revenue effect, or reduces a recurring risk/compliance issue |
| 5     | Major, multi-dimensional impact — cost, revenue, CX, and/or risk together           |

## Feasibility (1–5)

Score the lowest of these four sub-factors, not the average — a single weak link (e.g. no data) caps feasibility regardless of how strong the others are.

**Data readiness**

- 1: no usable data, or data locked in inaccessible/paper form
- 3: data exists but is inconsistent, scattered across tools, or needs heavy cleanup
- 5: data is structured, accessible, and reasonably clean

**Process documentation**

- 1: no one can describe the process consistently
- 3: process is understood by one or two people but undocumented
- 5: process is documented and consistently followed

**Decision complexity**

- 1: highly ambiguous judgment calls with many edge cases
- 3: mostly rule-based with some judgment
- 5: clear rules, few exceptions

**Error tolerance / recoverability**

- 1: a wrong output causes real harm or liability with no catch mechanism
- 3: errors are inconvenient but recoverable, and a human can catch them
- 5: errors are low-stakes or self-correcting

## Impact × Feasibility action

|                 | Low feasibility                                                     | High feasibility             |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **High impact** | Prepare first — fix data/process ownership before committing budget | Quick win — sequence first   |
| **Low impact**  | Deprioritize                                                        | Nice-to-have — only if cheap |

## AI readiness quadrant (for the business as a whole, not just one task)

A workflow is ready to hand to AI when, in addition to scoring well above:

- Someone owns the process and can make decisions about changing it
- The team has capacity to test, adjust, and maintain whatever is built
- The problem is specific enough that success can be measured

If any of these organizational conditions are missing, the recommendation is "prepare, then revisit" — not "build now" — even for a high-impact, high-feasibility task.
