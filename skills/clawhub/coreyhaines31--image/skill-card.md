## Use Case:

External users, developers, marketers, and product teams use this skill to choose image production workflows, write AI image prompts, prepare product mockups and social assets, and optimize web images for delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could expose provider credentials while discussing image generation tools.

Mitigation: Do not paste API keys or secrets into chat; configure credentials through secure provider account settings or environment management.

Risk: AI-generated product UI images may misrepresent the real product.

Mitigation: Use real product screenshots for product mockups and apply framing, callouts, and overlays after capture.

Risk: Marketing images may be too large or poorly matched to platform requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm dimensions and aspect ratio before production, then resize, compress, and export web-appropriate formats such as WebP with fallback formats.
