## Description: <br>
Turns Chinese supplier specs, 1688 copy, or product notes into three polished English Amazon listing variations with titles, bullets, descriptions, backend keyword suggestions, and usage notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Cross-border sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to turn Chinese or English product details into Amazon-ready English listing copy optimized for Western buyers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated Amazon listing copy can include unsupported warranty, certification, compliance, or product claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated claims against product evidence and Amazon policy before publishing. <br>
Risk: Category positioning and compliance checks may rely on model knowledge rather than current marketplace rules. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm category requirements, character limits, and prohibited-claim rules in the target Amazon marketplace before use. <br>
