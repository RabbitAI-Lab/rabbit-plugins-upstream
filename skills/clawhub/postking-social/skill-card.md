## Description: <br>
Generate, approve, and schedule social posts on PostKing across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Threads, and Facebook — plus content weeks and repurposing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and social media operators use this skill to draft, review, approve, schedule, reschedule, and repurpose PostKing social content across connected brand channels. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can guide an agent toward approving, scheduling, rescheduling, canceling, or enabling recurring social posts before the user has checked the final result. <br>
Mitigation: Before any publish-affecting action, verify the selected brand or account, platform, content, visual, and scheduled time. <br>
