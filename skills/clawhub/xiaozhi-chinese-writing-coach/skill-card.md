## Description:

A Chinese-language writing coach that guides students through planning, drafting, and revising their own essays without writing the essays for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students in upper primary school through high school use guided questions, genre-specific feedback, and debate exercises to develop and revise their own writing. Younger students need parent or teacher supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; other regions require localized crisis resources.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing and style profiles may contain minors' personal information.

Mitigation: Enable consent-gated profiles, parent or guardian controls, and accessible pause, deletion, and export options before use.

Risk: Students may disclose distress or immediate danger during a writing session.

Mitigation: Stop coaching when a crisis signal appears and direct the student to a trusted adult and location-appropriate emergency or youth support resources.

Risk: Unsuitable deployment controls may expose younger students to unsupported coaching or reminders.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended K–12 audience, supervision for younger students, and consent-based reminder controls before deployment.
