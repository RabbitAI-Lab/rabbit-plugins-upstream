## Description:

A Chinese writing coach that helps K–12 students develop their own ideas, draft independently, and revise with targeted feedback rather than writing essays for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students in upper primary and middle school use guided questions, outline checks, and focused feedback to write and improve their own essays. Guardians and educators should supervise younger students and review any enabled writing profile or sharing settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; adapt crisis contacts and student-data practices before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A persistent writing profile may contain sensitive student information or be shared beyond the intended audience.

Mitigation: Enable profiles and cross-skill or guardian sharing only with appropriate consent; confirm who can access records and allow students to review, pause, correct, export, or delete them.

Risk: Students may disclose self-harm, abuse, bullying, or other urgent safety concerns while discussing personal writing.

Mitigation: Pause writing feedback, direct the student to a trusted adult and appropriate local help, and localize crisis contacts for the deployment region.

Risk: Younger students may use the coach without suitable adult oversight.

Mitigation: Require a parent or teacher to accompany primary-school students and check applicable guardian-consent and student-data requirements.
