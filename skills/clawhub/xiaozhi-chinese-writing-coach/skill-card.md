## Description:

Coaches Chinese-language students through brainstorming, outlining, drafting, and revision without writing their essays for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language students in upper primary and secondary school use guided questions and targeted feedback to develop their own narrative, argumentative, and explanatory writing. Younger students require adult supervision, and the coach does not help with an active exam.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize crisis resources, grade assumptions, and minor-consent rules before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's writing may disclose distress or immediate safety concerns.

Mitigation: Pause writing feedback, refer the student to trusted adults and locally appropriate crisis services, and localize referral details outside Mainland China.

Risk: Writing profiles and cross-skill sharing can expose a minor's personal information.

Mitigation: Leave profile use and sharing off unless the student or guardian clearly opts in; honor pause, correction, export, and deletion requests.

Risk: Age or school-context mismatch may make guidance inappropriate.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended age group and local school context; require adult supervision for younger students and do not assist with an active exam.
