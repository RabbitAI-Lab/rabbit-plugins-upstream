## Description:

Guides Chinese K12 students through planning, drafting, and revising their own writing without writing assignments for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in mainland China's upper-primary and middle-school Chinese courses use the skill to develop ideas, check outlines, and receive targeted feedback on their own narrative, argumentative, and explanatory writing. Younger students require an adult or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localization required elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Children's writing profiles may retain personal information or be shared across skills.

Mitigation: Confirm the required adult or guardian context and obtain consent before enabling long-term memory or cross-skill sharing; offer profile review and deletion controls.

Risk: Mainland-China-specific curriculum and crisis contacts may not suit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, child-safety requirements, and emergency contacts before use elsewhere; ask the student's location when uncertain.
