## Description:

Guides Chinese-language students through brainstorming, outlining, drafting, and revising their own writing without writing the assignment for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking primary and middle school students use this coach to develop their own ideas, check structure, and revise narrative, argumentative, or explanatory writing with age-appropriate feedback. Younger students use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term writing profiles or cross-skill sharing could expose a student's personal writing history.

Mitigation: Obtain student or guardian consent before enabling profile retention or cross-skill sharing; honor controls to review, pause, and delete the profile.

Risk: Emergency-contact guidance may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Deploy in the intended Mainland China context, or localize crisis referrals and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.
