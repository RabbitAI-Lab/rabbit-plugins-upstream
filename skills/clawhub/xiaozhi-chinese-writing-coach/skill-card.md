## Description:

Coaches Chinese-language students through planning, drafting, and revising their own writing without writing essays for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary through high school use guided questions, draft feedback, and revision prompts to develop their own Chinese writing. Younger students require an accompanying parent or teacher.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis resources and consent guidance may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and adapt emergency contacts and consent rules before use.

Risk: Student writing profiles or reminders could expose personal information across sessions or audiences.

Mitigation: Review and obtain appropriate consent for profiling, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, and reminders before enabling them.

Risk: AI-generated writing may replace students' own work.

Mitigation: Use guided questions and draft-specific feedback rather than composing the student's essay.
