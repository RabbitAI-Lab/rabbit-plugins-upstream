## Description:

Coaches students in Chinese writing through guided planning, logical checks, genre-aware feedback, and revision while keeping the writing their own.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language students from upper primary through high school use this skill to develop ideas, check essay structure, and improve drafts while writing their own text. Primary-school students use it with an adult present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional writing profiles and sharing can retain or reuse a student's writing habits.

Mitigation: Enable profiles or sharing only with the student's consent; use the pause-memory and sharing controls when appropriate, and confirm how the host platform handles storage and deletion.

Risk: Writing discussions may involve sensitive experiences or raise safety concerns, especially for younger students.

Mitigation: Have an adult accompany primary-school students. If crisis signals appear, stop writing coaching and follow the skill's referral guidance to connect the student with a trusted adult and appropriate local help.
