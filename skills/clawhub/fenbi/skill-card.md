## Description: <br>
Fenbi is a civil-service and teacher-qualification exam preparation assistant that helps with study planning, problem-solving methods, writing frameworks, and Fenbi product-line selection. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhangifonly](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangifonly) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External learners and exam-prep assistants use this skill to create study plans, choose practice priorities, review mistakes, and receive structured guidance for Chinese civil-service, essay-writing, teacher-qualification, and related public-sector exams. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Exam dates, score lines, and local recruitment policies can change or vary by jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive or local exam information against official announcements before acting. <br>
Risk: Study guidance may be overread as a guaranteed exam outcome or official policy source. <br>
Mitigation: Present recommendations as general preparation guidance and avoid guarantee language, leaked-question claims, or unofficial policy assertions. <br>
