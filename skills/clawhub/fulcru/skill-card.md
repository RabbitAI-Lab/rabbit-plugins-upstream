## Description: <br>
Audit a brand's AI search visibility, identify unanswered buyer questions, write targeted pages that AI will cite, and measure ranking improvements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gsmmediaro](https://clawhub.ai/user/gsmmediaro) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, content strategists, and agents use this skill to audit whether AI search engines mention a brand, find commercial buyer questions where competitors appear instead, draft one citation-friendly page to close the highest-value gap, and re-measure results after publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated competitive claims or public-facing marketing copy may be inaccurate or misleading if published without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review competitor comparisons, claims, statistics, and citations before publishing, and do not fabricate customers, awards, quotes, or numbers. <br>
Risk: A single-pass retrieval check can overstate AI-search performance if treated as continuous tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. <br>
Mitigation: Present the audit as a retrieval-surface proxy, then re-run the same question set after publishing and report the before-and-after results honestly. <br>
