## Description: <br>
AI logo and brand identity design powered by CellCog, including brand kits, color palettes, typography, brand guidelines, logo design, visual identity, social media assets, web design, and video from a single brief. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and brand teams use this skill to ask CellCog for complete brand identity work, including logos, palettes, typography, brand guidelines, social templates, and related creative assets. The skill is intended for normal ClawHub commercial use with a configured CellCog API key. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brand briefs and related user-provided details may be sent to CellCog as an external AI design service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including confidential business details unless that matches the user's data-sharing expectations and CellCog account terms. <br>
Risk: The skill requires a CELLCOG_API_KEY credential. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the API key through environment configuration, do not include it in prompts or generated artifacts, and rotate it if exposed. <br>
Risk: Generated logos, brand assets, and guidelines may require brand, legal, accessibility, and trademark review before use. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs before publication or deployment, especially for visual similarity, usage rights, accessibility, and fit with the intended brand. <br>
