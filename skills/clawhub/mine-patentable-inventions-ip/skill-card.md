## Description:

Identify, structure, screen, and route potentially patentable inventions from R&D projects, technical improvements, standards work, existing innovations or portfolios, competitor patents, design-around needs, cross-domain technology transfer, or emerging opportunity hypotheses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

R&D engineers, technical leaders, and IP professionals use this skill to mine, structure, screen, and route potentially patentable inventions with patent and prior-art evidence. It supports quick or deep workflows that produce checkpoints, innovation cards, a six-month roadmap, and Markdown or HTML reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential invention details could be shared with patent research tools or other sources without the right authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm disclosure constraints before use, use anonymous labels where appropriate, and share only approved information with PatSnap or other research sources.

Risk: Patentability, infringement, standards, jurisdictional, or translation-sensitive conclusions may be incomplete or unsuitable without legal review.

Mitigation: Have qualified patent counsel verify legal conclusions, jurisdiction-specific assumptions, standards issues, and translation-sensitive findings before acting on the output.
