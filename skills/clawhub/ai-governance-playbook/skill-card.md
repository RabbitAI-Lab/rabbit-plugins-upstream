## Description:

Enterprise AI Governance Playbook（企业AI治理实操手册） helps executives, compliance, legal, and information security leaders answer enterprise AI governance questions and generate practical policy drafts, risk classifications, approval records, checklists, and maturity self-assessments from the bundled governance knowledge base and local toolkit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business leaders, compliance teams, legal teams, and information security owners use this skill to design AI governance programs, draft AI usage policies, classify AI application risk, register and approve AI use cases, and compare obligations across China, the EU, the US, Asia-Pacific, and sector-specific contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes user-run Python utilities for governance drafting and self-assessment.

Mitigation: Run the bundled tools in an appropriate local environment and avoid passing sensitive company details in prompts or command arguments unless that environment is approved for the data.

Risk: Regulatory summaries and governance templates can become outdated or may not fit a specific legal context.

Mitigation: Verify legal and regulatory summaries with qualified counsel before relying on them for material business decisions.
