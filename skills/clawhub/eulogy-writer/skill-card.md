## Description: <br>
Help someone write a 3-5 minute eulogy and delivery copy from their own memories, voice, and room context without inventing biographical details. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
People preparing to speak at a funeral or memorial use this skill to turn a few true memories and tone preferences into a concise eulogy in their own voice, plus podium-ready delivery formatting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive grief-related memories or family details while drafting the eulogy. <br>
Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum memories needed, avoid pressuring the user for private details, and keep all facts grounded in what the speaker provides. <br>
Risk: The eulogy could accidentally invent or embellish biographical details. <br>
Mitigation: Treat every fact and story as user-supplied evidence, and explicitly avoid adding connective details that the speaker did not provide. <br>
