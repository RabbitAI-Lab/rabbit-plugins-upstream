## Description:

Create a talking avatar from one portrait and a short script or speech track. This AI presenter and digital human video workflow can prepare narration with a selected voice or use a supplied recording, then direct a stable talking-head clip with restrained expression, natural movement, clear delivery, and focused lip-sync review. Use it for AI spokesperson videos, product explainers, training, course lessons, announcements, onboarding, social talking-head content, and photo-to-talking-video messages, with narration-driven facial motion and a focused review of identity, clarity, lip sync, and motion stability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to turn an authorized portrait and approved narration into a single talking-head presenter video for explainers, training, announcements, onboarding, or social content. The skill requires rights to the likeness and voice used.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation grants broader shared Beatra account access than avatar-only use may require.

Mitigation: Ask the publisher for narrower authorization when only avatar-video access is needed, and revoke device access in Beatra Console when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Portraits and recordings are uploaded externally, and installation analytics run automatically.

Mitigation: Upload only media you are authorized to share, and review Beatra's privacy policy before installing or sending media.

Risk: Video and narration generation spend account credits.

Mitigation: Review the current estimate and obtain explicit approval before starting any paid generation.
