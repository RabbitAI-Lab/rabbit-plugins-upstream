## Description:

Create a talking avatar from one portrait and a short script or speech track, with narration-driven facial motion and a focused review of identity, clarity, lip sync, and motion stability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, educators, and teams use this skill to prepare a single presenter clip from an authorized portrait and script or approved speech recording. It supports explainers, lessons, announcements, onboarding, and social talking-head content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests extra account permissions and automatically reports device information.

Mitigation: Review the requested permissions and installation analytics before connecting.

Risk: Portraits and recordings are processed externally by Beatra.

Mitigation: Use only media with appropriate likeness and voice authorization, and review Beatra's data handling and the selected network before upload.

Risk: A malformed shared inventory may cause removal to disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Check the shared installation inventory and other installed Beatra skills before uninstalling.
