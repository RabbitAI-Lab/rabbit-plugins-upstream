## Description:

Guides creation of a talking-avatar video from an approved portrait and a short script or speech recording, with optional synthesized narration and review of the final clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and educators use this skill to produce narrated presenter clips for explainers, training, announcements, and social content from a portrait and approved speech or a script.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A shared device token grants broad access to media, tasks, artifacts, voices, and wallet spending.

Mitigation: Install only if these permissions are acceptable; protect the credential and disconnect it when no longer needed.

Risk: The bundled client can silently install package updates.

Mitigation: Review the update setting and turn off automatic updates if package changes require explicit approval.

Risk: Narration and video generation can incur charges, and a likeness or voice may be used without permission.

Mitigation: Confirm rights to the portrait and voice, and obtain approval at each paid generation step.
