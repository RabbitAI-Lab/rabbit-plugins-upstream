# rule-table.md (Japan JCT)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Rules change; when citing, note "subject to the latest announcements of the National Tax Agency (国税庁)."

## Rates
- Standard: 10%
- Reduced: 8% — food & beverages (excluding alcohol and dining out) and certain newspaper subscriptions.
- Supplements, cosmetics, general merchandise: 10%.

## Registration Threshold
- Taxable sales > ¥10,000,000 in the base period (two fiscal years prior) → taxable enterprise.
- New companies with capital ≥ ¥10,000,000: taxable from inception.
- Voluntary election to become a taxable enterprise has a lock-in period — flag uninformed elections.

## Qualified Invoice System (適格請求書等保存方式, since 2023-10-01)
- Buyers can claim input tax credits only with qualified invoices issued by registered invoice issuers.
- Sellers not registered as invoice issuers: B2B customers lose credits → direct commercial risk.
- Transition relief phases down the deductible share for non-qualified invoices over time.

## Marketplace Boundary
- Japan has NO deemed-supplier regime for physical goods marketplaces: Amazon JP / Rakuten do NOT collect JCT for the seller — self-declaration required.
- (Separate regime: platform taxation applies to specified digital services by foreign providers since 2024-04 — out of scope for goods sellers.)

## Import Rules
- Direct cross-border B2C: import consumption tax at customs (consumer-side).
- FBA/domestic stock: domestic taxable sales; seller must self-declare.
- Since 2023-10: foreign sellers generally must use an ACP (Attorney for Customs Procedures) for sales-bound imports — using a generic IOR is a flag.
