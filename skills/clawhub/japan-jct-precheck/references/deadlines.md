# deadlines.md (Japan JCT)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; actual deadlines depend on entity type and prior-year tax — verify with the National Tax Agency or a 税理士.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- Sole proprietors: annual return due by the end of February following the tax year.
- Corporations: due within 2 months after the fiscal year end.
- Interim filings (based on prior annual JCT): > ¥480,000 → semi-annual; > ¥4,000,000 → quarterly; > ¥48,000,000 → monthly.

## Usage Notes
- Deadlines falling on weekends/holidays shift to the next business day.
- Interim brackets are computed from the prior year's final JCT; if the user has not provided it, mark "cannot determine."
- Retention: qualified invoices and books must be kept for 7 years (sole proprietors: per statute).
