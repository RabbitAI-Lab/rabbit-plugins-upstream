## Description:

Put a brand's social media posts on its website with Juicer. Creates a social feed from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube, LinkedIn and 10+ more networks, sets up moderation and returns the embed code for any site, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Brand owners and website managers use this skill to gather selected social media accounts or hashtags into a moderated feed and embed it on a website.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A feed could show posts from the wrong account or unwanted content.

Mitigation: Confirm account handles before adding sources; review initial posts and enable the approval queue and filters when posts need review.

Risk: An exposed Juicer API key could be misused.

Mitigation: Protect the optional JUICER_API_KEY and connect only the intended social accounts.
