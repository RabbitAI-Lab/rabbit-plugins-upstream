## Description:

Creates a moderated Juicer social feed from selected social accounts and returns embed code for a website.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[juicer](https://clawhub.ai/user/juicer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External teams use the skill to combine selected social accounts and hashtags into a moderated feed, review posts, and embed that feed on their website.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect sources or moderation settings could cause unintended posts to appear on the website.

Mitigation: Confirm each selected account and source before adding it, review the initial posts, and enable the approval queue when every post needs review.
