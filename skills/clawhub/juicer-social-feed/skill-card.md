## Description:

Put a brand's social media posts on its website with Juicer. Creates a social feed from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube, LinkedIn and 10+ more networks, sets up moderation and returns the embed code for any site, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[juicer](https://clawhub.ai/user/juicer)

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and website owners use this skill to collect selected social posts into a moderated feed and embed it on their website. It helps configure sources, review posts, and provide embed code for the site's platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts from connected social accounts may appear in the website feed before an individual reviews them.

Mitigation: Review connected accounts and moderation settings before embedding the feed; enable the approval queue when every post should be checked before it appears.
