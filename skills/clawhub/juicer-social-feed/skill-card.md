## Description:

Put a brand's social media posts on its website with Juicer. Creates a social feed from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube, LinkedIn and 10+ more networks, sets up moderation and returns the embed code for any site, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow. Use for "add our Instagram to the website", "embed social media feed", "social media aggregator", "show our TikTok on the homepage", "social proof on the website".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting the wrong social account could expose or display unintended posts.

Mitigation: Connect only intended accounts, confirm account identities before adding sources, and verify the connection status.

Risk: Adding feed sources consumes credits.

Mitigation: Check the credit balance and source costs before adding sources.

Risk: New posts update automatically and may appear without individual review.

Mitigation: Enable the approval queue when each post needs review, and inspect the first posts before handing over the feed.
