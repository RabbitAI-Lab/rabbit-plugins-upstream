## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive documents may be provided as optional context for landscape analysis.

Mitigation: Review and redact confidential or regulated material before adding it to the agent context.

Risk: Landscape summaries can be too general or outdated when no external context is available.

Mitigation: Ground important claims in provided patents, publications, industrial data, or other current sources before using the analysis for decisions.
