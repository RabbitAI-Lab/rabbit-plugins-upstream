## Description: <br>
Structures product data analysis, metric deep-dives, funnel analysis, and cohort studies into a clear question, root-cause assessment, confidence level, and recommended action. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, analysts, and agents use this skill to structure product metric investigations, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, and stakeholder-ready explanations. It helps turn metric movements into a clear question, root-cause assessment, confidence statement, and recommended action. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill structures product metric analysis but does not itself fetch or verify source data. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the input data source, time window, baseline, and segment definitions before relying on the analysis. <br>
Risk: Product analytics can include sensitive business or user data. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the data needed for the investigation and redact sensitive fields before using the skill. <br>
