## Description: <br>
This skill helps travel advisors compare cancellation, refund, credit, insurance, and card-dispute options to choose a recovery path while preserving fallbacks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Travel advisors and agency teams use this skill to structure cancellation recovery decisions, compare supplier penalties, insurance claims, credits, and card disputes, and document neutral client-facing advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cancellation recovery guidance may be mistaken for legal, insurance, or chargeback adjudication. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as advisory decision support only and verify supplier, insurer, and card-dispute rules before acting. <br>
Risk: Estimated recovery paths can be wrong if supplier penalties, covered perils, documentation, or claim sequencing are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Enumerate all recovery branches, compare expected net recovery, preserve fallbacks, and document the advice and client choice. <br>
