## Description:

Helps travel advisors compare supplier refunds, credits, insurance claims, and card disputes when a client needs to cancel or change a trip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travel advisors use this skill to compare likely net recovery from supplier refunds or credits, insurance claims, and eligible card disputes, and to sequence choices while preserving fallback options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Illustrative recovery percentages or coverage assumptions could be mistaken for actual supplier or insurance terms.

Mitigation: Verify current supplier rules, policy terms, and covered reasons; treat example percentages as illustrative.

Risk: Accepting a supplier credit or waiver may affect a later insurance claim.

Mitigation: Check how accepting the offer affects claim eligibility, preserve fallback options, and keep cancellations, credit acceptance, and claim submissions under the traveler's control.
