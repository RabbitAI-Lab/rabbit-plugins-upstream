## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance based on Gary Namie and Workplace Bullying Institute frameworks for recognizing workplace bullying, using WBI survey context, and shaping individual response or organizational anti-bullying policy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[timo2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/timo2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, advisors, HR teams, and organizational policy designers use this skill to analyze real workplace bullying cases, frame target response steps, and avoid intervention patterns such as forced mediation when power imbalance is central.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers, especially the Chinese term for Namie, may invoke the skill for unrelated topics.

Mitigation: Narrow or review trigger terms before deployment so the skill activates only for WBI-style workplace bullying analysis.

Risk: Workplace bullying guidance can be mistaken for legal or medical advice in sensitive employment situations.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a reference aid and route legal, HR compliance, health, or safety decisions to qualified professionals and organizational policy owners.
