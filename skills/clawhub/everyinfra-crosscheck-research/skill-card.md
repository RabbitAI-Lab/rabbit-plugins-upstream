## Description:

Verify a current factual claim or produce a cited research answer through EverySearch crosscheck. Use when the user needs mechanism-distinct retrieval, source comparison, freshness checks, or an evidence-backed conclusion rather than a simple result list.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and analysts use this skill to verify current factual claims, compare independent retrieval results, and produce conclusions supported by cited original sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and result data leave the user's environment; request parameters and service logs may be retained.

Mitigation: Remove confidential details before searching and use a dedicated API key restricted to search, stored in secret settings.

Risk: Paid research calls can incur charges.

Mitigation: Review usage charges and rely on billing evidence exposed by tool results rather than assuming a call was billed.
