## Description: <br>
Provides education-sector tax compliance guidance for Chinese education and training organizations, covering tax exemptions, simplified VAT treatment, nonprofit qualification, tuition revenue recognition, teacher payroll taxes, invoicing, risk checks, case analysis, and compliance reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and tax/compliance practitioners use this skill to ask education-industry tax questions, run lightweight risk self-checks, produce practical remediation guidance, and draft compliance report text for review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Questions, scenarios, and self-check metrics may be sent to the remote mcp.aitaxs.top service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering identifiable client, payroll, tax, or financial details unless the publisher and endpoint policies have been reviewed and approved. <br>
Risk: The artifact can create local API credentials, caches, logs, and optional MCP client configuration changes. <br>
Mitigation: Install and test in a controlled environment, keep automatic setup disabled unless explicitly approved, and review local files and MCP configuration before production use. <br>
Risk: Tax calculations, exemption determinations, risk scores, and compliance reports are advisory and may be incomplete or time-sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified tax professional or legal advisor review outputs before using them for filings, audits, disputes, or major business decisions. <br>
