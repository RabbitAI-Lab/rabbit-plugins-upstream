## Description:

Use when crossing 2+ time zones: computes your day-by-day circadian shift protocol — pre-trip sleep shifting, light SEEK/AVOID windows timed to your core-body-temperature minimum, low-dose melatonin timing, caffeine cutoffs, and in-flight/first-days rules for east and west travel.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External travelers crossing two or more time zones use this skill to plan pre-trip sleep shifts, timed light exposure, melatonin timing, caffeine cutoffs, and in-flight and arrival routines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Circadian and melatonin timing may not suit individual health needs; the schedule is general travel guidance, not medical advice.

Mitigation: Review the health cautions and consult a clinician before following melatonin recommendations for children, during pregnancy, or when medication interactions may apply.
