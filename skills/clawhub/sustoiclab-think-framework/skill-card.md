## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language structured thinking toolbox that routes user problems to root-cause analysis, strategy evaluation, decision support, multi-angle thinking, or efficiency-focus frameworks and guides step-by-step analysis to structured conclusions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this Chinese-language assistant to choose an appropriate thinking framework, work through one question at a time, and produce structured analysis for causes, strategy, decisions, perspectives, or effort allocation. The skill is advisory and keeps final decisions with the user. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for final decisions or factual answers. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory structured analysis; the skill explicitly leaves decisions with the user and is not intended for factual Q&A. <br>
Risk: Framework-guided analysis can still produce incomplete or misleading conclusions if user-provided inputs are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated analysis, confirm assumptions, and use the skill's prompts for missing information and confirmation before acting. <br>
