## Description: <br>
Analyzes cross-industry complaints about products, services, subscriptions, projects, or deliverables by separating customer claims, confirmed facts, standards, evidence gaps, impact, handling stage, and next evidence-based actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Support, operations, customer success, and complaint-handling teams use this skill to organize complaint facts, evidence gaps, impact, escalation triggers, and customer response points before choosing the next action. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat complaint analysis as a final legal, compliance, liability, or compensation decision. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill to organize evidence and next actions only; escalate privacy, safety, regulatory, major financial, or contractual issues to qualified reviewers. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or conflicting complaint evidence can lead to premature conclusions or misleading customer commitments. <br>
Mitigation: Collect the required complaint details, mark missing or conflicting inputs explicitly, and avoid admitting liability, denying responsibility, promising compensation, or blaming the customer without sufficient evidence. <br>
