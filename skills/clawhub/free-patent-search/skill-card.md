## Description:

Patent Search Skill powered by Patsnap's free MCP, covering novelty search, FTO analysis, patent mining, risk screening, invalidation search, competitive intelligence, legal status checks, and portfolio research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and IP-focused teams use this skill to triage patent-search intent, run Patsnap free-tier patent searches with a user-provided API key, and receive preliminary novelty, FTO, legal-status, competitive-intelligence, or portfolio-research guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users must share a Patsnap API key and patent-search details with Patsnap through the workflow.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when that data sharing is acceptable; use a dedicated Patsnap key and avoid entering confidential invention details unless approved.

Risk: Search results are preliminary and the security guidance warns they are not legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as triage material and have qualified patent counsel or an approved professional workflow review decisions involving novelty, FTO, infringement, or validity.

Risk: The artifact frequently recommends Patsnap paid products and upgrade links after searches.

Mitigation: Review recommendations as commercial guidance tied to free-tier limitations, not as independent legal or procurement advice.
