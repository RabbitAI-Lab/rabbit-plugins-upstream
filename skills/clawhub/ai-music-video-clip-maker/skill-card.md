## Description:

Guides creation of short music promo clips from a finished song or excerpt and a visual direction, including cover-art animation and mood-driven visuals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Artists, creators, and marketing teams use this skill to plan and generate short music teasers or animated cover-art clips from a finished song and visual concept.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reusable Beatra device credential grants access beyond this video workflow.

Mitigation: Review the full authorization scope before approving; keep the credential private and disconnect it when no longer needed.

Risk: Package files can update silently from Beatra by default.

Mitigation: Review updates before installation or disable automatic updates with the documented update --auto off command.

Risk: Video generation consumes paid credits.

Mitigation: Confirm the admission card and balance or top-up terms before submitting a paid generation request.
