## Description:

Create a short visual clip guided by a song's mood, rhythm, and visual concept. This AI music video clip maker and song-to-video generator turns a music excerpt and visual direction into a cinematic music promo clip, animates approved cover art or a portrait in time with the music, interpolates motion between opening and ending art, or uses audio as a loose mood reference for a new visual concept. Use it for new-song teasers, album promo clips, cover art animation, mood visuals, virtual performer scenes, and social music teasers, with an audio-visual map built from the song's hook, energy, palette, and landing image.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires broader account permissions than its workflow needs, including speech generation and voice management.

Mitigation: Review the shared music, speech, voice-management, and credit-spending permissions before connecting; do not connect if those permissions are not acceptable.

Risk: Music and image uploads are sent for processing, and generation requests can spend credits.

Mitigation: Upload only media you intend to send to Beatra and review each paid estimate before approving it.

Risk: Installation analytics run automatically while the connection is active.

Mitigation: Review the analytics and security information, and revoke the Beatra connection when it is no longer needed.
