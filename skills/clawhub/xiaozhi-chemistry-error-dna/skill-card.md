## Description:

为初高中化学学习者定位错题的六类根因及子类型，并生成弱项分析与复习建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

中文初高中化学学习者和教育者用它分析错题的符号、微观、概念、读题、实验及计算根因，查看经授权的弱项档案和按需生成的月报。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language use)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles or reminders could be stored or shared without the intended consent.

Mitigation: Confirm the host honors profile, cross-skill, parent-visibility, and reminder consent controls before use; allow students to pause, correct, export, or delete records.

Risk: Chemistry experiment suggestions or distress disclosures may require safeguards beyond study guidance.

Mitigation: Follow the supplied lab-safety guidance; stop tutoring and direct students to a trusted adult or local emergency support when crisis signals appear.
