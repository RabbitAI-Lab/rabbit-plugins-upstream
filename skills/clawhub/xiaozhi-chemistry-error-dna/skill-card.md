## Description:

Analyzes chemistry mistakes across six error dimensions and helps students track specific weak points and progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and early high school students use this skill to identify recurring chemistry misconceptions, review practice questions, and optionally track weak points with consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; use elsewhere only after localizing language, curriculum, crisis contacts, and consent rules for minors.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles of minors may expose sensitive performance or emotional information if shared without permission.

Mitigation: Require student consent for persistent tracking, reminders, cross-skill sharing, and parent-facing summaries; honor pause and deletion requests.

Risk: Crisis advice or school guidance may be inappropriate outside the intended Chinese K12 context.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, crisis contacts, and minor-consent rules before use in another region; refer crisis signals to trusted adults and local emergency services.

Risk: Incorrect generated chemistry exercises or conclusions from sparse records could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check generated questions and answers before presenting them, and mark weak-point trends as insufficiently supported when records are sparse.
