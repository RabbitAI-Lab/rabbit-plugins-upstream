## Description:

Analyzes chemistry mistakes across six dimensions and helps students identify recurring weaknesses through a consent-controlled learning profile.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students studying junior-high chemistry or introductory compulsory high-school chemistry use this skill to find the causes of repeated mistakes, practice weak areas, and request a weakness report. Educators may use its analyses with appropriate student consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis referrals may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use in a Chinese-language, mainland-China-aligned K12 setting or localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis resources first.

Risk: A persistent chemistry error profile may expose a student's learning history through retention or sharing.

Mitigation: Obtain consent and use the provided controls to view, correct, delete, pause, export, and limit profile sharing.
