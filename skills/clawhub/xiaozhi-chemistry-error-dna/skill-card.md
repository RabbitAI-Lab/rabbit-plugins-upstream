## Description:

帮助已开始学习化学的初高中学生定位错题的六类根因，并在获得同意后整理弱项档案与学习建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students who have started chemistry, and their educators, use this skill to identify recurring mistakes in chemical notation, particle models, concepts, question reading, experiments, and calculations. With student consent, it tracks weak areas and suggests targeted practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language learning; localize emergency support guidance outside mainland China)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learners' chemistry records could be stored or shared without their or their guardian's understanding.

Mitigation: Confirm consent before enabling profiles, reminders, parent sharing, or cross-skill sharing; offer pause, export, correction, and deletion controls.

Risk: Experiment advice could lead students to attempt hazardous chemical activities at home.

Mitigation: Do not provide at-home steps for hazardous experiments; direct students to teacher-supervised school labs or safe demonstrations.

Risk: A learner's distress could be mistaken for ordinary study anxiety.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring on crisis signals and direct the learner to trusted adults and appropriate local support without recording sensitive details.
