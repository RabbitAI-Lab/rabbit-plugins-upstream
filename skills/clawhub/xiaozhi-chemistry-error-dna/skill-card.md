## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school chemistry mistakes across six dimensions to identify root causes and summarize learning weaknesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use this skill to understand recurring causes of chemistry mistakes and see patterns in their weak areas. It classifies mistakes across chemistry notation, microscopic and macroscopic concepts, reading, experiments, and calculations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with crisis resources localized to the user's country or region.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved learning records, sharing, and reminders rely on the platform and companion skills enforcing consent controls; runtime enforcement was not verified.

Mitigation: Enable these features only when you trust those services, and use the skill's consent, pause, export, and deletion controls.
