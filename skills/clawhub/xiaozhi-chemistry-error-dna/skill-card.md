## Description:

帮助初高中学生分析化学错题的根本原因，定位六类常见弱项，并生成针对性的学习建议和弱项报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language study aid to identify recurring chemistry mistakes, review likely causes, and plan focused practice across junior and senior high school topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student error profiles or summaries may be retained or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm the platform enforces student consent for profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent visibility, and reminders; honor requests to pause, correct, export, or delete records.

Risk: Incorrect diagnoses or generated exercises could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check generated exercises and answers, avoid long-term conclusions from a single error, and label conclusions based on limited evidence.
