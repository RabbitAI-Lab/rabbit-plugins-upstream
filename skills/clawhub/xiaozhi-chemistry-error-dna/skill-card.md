## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school chemistry mistakes across six dimensions and helps learners identify recurring causes and weak areas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students studying chemistry in middle or high school use this skill to diagnose why they miss questions, identify recurring weaknesses, and review progress when they request it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records, sharing, parent summaries, and reminders depend on host-platform storage and consent controls.

Mitigation: Review those controls before enabling records or sharing, and use the skill's pause, sharing, and delete controls as needed.
