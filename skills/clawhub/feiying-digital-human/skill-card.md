## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill includes face and voice cloning workflows that may involve biometric or likeness rights. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for people whose face and voice you are authorized to clone, and keep consent and rights documentation before generating or publishing media. <br>
Risk: Synthetic digital-human videos can be mistaken for real recordings or endorsements. <br>
Mitigation: Label synthetic media where appropriate and review generated scripts, voice, likeness, and claims before distribution. <br>
Risk: The artifact discusses emotional response and companion-like scenarios, while the security review warns against relying on broad compliance claims for emotionally interactive, minor-facing, therapeutic, or companion deployments. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid companion, minor-facing, therapeutic, or emotionally dependent use cases unless separately reviewed for policy, consent, retention, and safety requirements. <br>
Risk: The security review notes inadequate retention and deletion safeguards for third-party face and voice platforms. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm each platform's retention, deletion, and data-use terms before uploading likeness, voice, or other personal data. <br>
