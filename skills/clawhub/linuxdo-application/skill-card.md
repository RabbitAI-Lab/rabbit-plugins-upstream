## Description: <br>
Craft high-pass-rate, de-AI'd Chinese applications (小作文) for Linux.do registration through an adaptive survey and rule-aware text generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[clarezoe](https://clawhub.ai/user/clarezoe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to answer a Chinese-language adaptive survey and draft a concise Linux.do registration application based on their real background, discovery path, reason for joining, and intended account use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can make AI-assisted registration text appear self-written for an access-controlled community. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when AI assistance is allowed by the community, and disclose or avoid AI assistance when the registration process expects unaided self-written text. <br>
Risk: The generated application could include fabricated or over-polished personal background details. <br>
Mitigation: Base answers only on the applicant's truthful background, discovery path, join reason, and intended account use; remove any invented claims before submission. <br>
Risk: The workflow asks for personal background and account-intent details. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only minimal information needed for the application and avoid sensitive personal data. <br>
