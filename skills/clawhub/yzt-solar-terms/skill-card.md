## Description:

Explains the 24 solar terms in Chinese, including their meaning, traditional customs, seasonal food and living tips, days until the next term, and dates for any year.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users can ask about today's or a named solar term, its customs and seasonal context, or the dates of all 24 terms in a year. The skill helps an agent answer with concise cultural information and everyday seasonal tips.
