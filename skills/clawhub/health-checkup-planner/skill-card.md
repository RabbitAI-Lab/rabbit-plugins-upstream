## Description:

Connects users with health-checkup services for personal recommendations, bookings and order management, corporate plans and quotes, provider locations, and frequently asked questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ryanbihai](https://clawhub.ai/user/ryanbihai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use the skill to find health-checkup options, manage reservations and orders, and get service guidance. Corporate customers use it to explore employee checkup plans, request quotes, and contact the provider.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Health information is handled by the external provider, and account access may expose personal or profile data.

Mitigation: Review the provider's privacy practices and requested account permissions before authorizing access.

Risk: Booking, cancellation, rescheduling, or profile changes can affect the user's account.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested details with the user before submitting changes.

Risk: Returned checkup card passwords are sensitive credentials.

Mitigation: Keep card passwords private and share them only when the user explicitly requests it.
