## Description:

Helps individuals find and manage health checkups and supports employers with screening plans, quotes, and clinic information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ryanbihai](https://clawhub.ai/user/ryanbihai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use the skill to explore checkup options, find clinics, and manage reservations and related records. Employers use it to request group checkup plans, quotes, and business support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authenticated tools can access sensitive personal health information and change appointments or saved profiles.

Mitigation: Review the provider's privacy practices and OAuth permissions before connecting; authorize only the actions and information sharing intended.
