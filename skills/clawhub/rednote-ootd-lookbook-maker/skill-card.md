## Description:

Creates a coordinated REDnote OOTD image carousel from an outfit photo or styling idea, with title, caption, and tag suggestions for the post.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and fashion brands use this skill to turn an outfit photo or styling idea into a coordinated REDnote lookbook and ready-to-publish post suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared Beatra authorization includes permissions for video, music, speech, and voice management beyond this image-making use.

Mitigation: Review the requested authorization before approving it, and revoke the shared device connection in Beatra Console when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Selected photos leave the device, installation metadata is reported automatically, and generation uses credits after confirmation.

Mitigation: Upload only photos you intend to share and review the generation and credit charge before confirming.
