## Description: <br>
Re-pitch one piece of content for four audiences - the board, the engineers, a customer, and a new hire - with a delta table showing what changed between altitudes and why. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, product teams, and communications writers use this skill to turn one memo, decision, update, or announcement into board, engineering, customer, and new-hire versions while preserving factual consistency. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Confidential names, numbers, legal exposure, or business-sensitive details may be included in the source content given to the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Redact sensitive details before use or explicitly list information that must not appear in downstream audience versions. <br>
Risk: Audience-specific rewrites can accidentally soften risks, alter facts, or leak internal jargon into customer-facing text. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's consistency rule, delta table, and leakage check to verify that every version remains true and appropriate for its audience. <br>
