## Description: <br>
Reviews Chinese articles, reports, tutorials, project introductions, and explanatory materials for factual grounding, logical coherence, terminology, translation artifacts, AI-writing patterns, and overall structure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gogoingai](https://clawhub.ai/user/gogoingai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, developers, and documentation teams use this skill to review Chinese technical or explanatory documents, identify factual, structural, terminology, translation, and AI-tone issues, and apply or propose concise revisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Automatic review behavior can rewrite documents to reduce AI-like traces or change authorial voice. <br>
Mitigation: Use manual review mode or require confirmation before edits for academic, legal, compliance, or authorship-sensitive writing. <br>
Risk: Persistent project-local review preferences can affect later reviews of the same document or project. <br>
Mitigation: Review stored preferences before reuse when style, terminology, or review standards need to change. <br>
