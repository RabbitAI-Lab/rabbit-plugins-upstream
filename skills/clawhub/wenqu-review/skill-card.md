## Description: <br>
Reviews Chinese articles, reports, tutorials, project introductions, and explanatory material for factual grounding, logic, terminology, translation artifacts, AI-writing patterns, and overall structure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gogoingai](https://clawhub.ai/user/gogoingai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, developers, and documentation teams use this skill to review Chinese drafts and receive actionable checks or edits for factual accuracy, coherence, terminology, translation tone, AI-writing patterns, structure, style fit, and change closure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Review recommendations or direct draft edits may introduce incorrect factual, terminology, or style changes. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed changes against source materials and accept edits only after human confirmation. <br>
Risk: Article-specific review preferences may record sensitive editorial preferences in the project workspace. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid using the skill on sensitive drafts unless local preference notes are acceptable, and remove those notes when they are no longer needed. <br>
