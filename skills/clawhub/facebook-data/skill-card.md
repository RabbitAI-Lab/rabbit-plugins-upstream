## Use Case:

External developers and research teams use this skill to retrieve structured Facebook page, post, group, marketplace, video, and advertising data for social listening, market research, and creator intelligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requests use your Superagnt API key and consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Store SUPERAGNT_API_KEY securely, and check your credit balance and billing before running requests.

Risk: The documented tools can search for individuals by location, which may involve personal information.

Mitigation: Keep person searches within the intended task and applicable privacy requirements, and limit each query to the necessary scope.
