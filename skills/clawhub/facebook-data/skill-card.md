### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Queries and links are sent to superagnt and may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Keep requests within the intended research scope and monitor credit usage.

Risk: The API key also grants access to other superagnt services.

Mitigation: Protect the key and avoid exposing it in logs, shared shell history, or untrusted environments.

Risk: Some shell examples need correction before execution.

Mitigation: Review and correct command quoting before running an example.

Risk: The packaged artifact review does not establish the provider's live implementation or data retention practices.

Mitigation: Review the provider's current service and retention terms before sending sensitive information.
