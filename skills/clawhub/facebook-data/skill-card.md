## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Facebook links, IDs, search terms, marketplace queries, and people queries are sent to Superagnt and may raise privacy, compliance, or platform-terms constraints.

Mitigation: Use only for approved workflows, avoid sensitive investigations, and confirm Superagnt data handling and platform compliance before deployment.

Risk: Release security evidence reports conflicting information about whether scraping is involved.

Mitigation: Treat the collection mechanism as unresolved until Superagnt documentation or contractual terms satisfy the deployment's risk requirements.
