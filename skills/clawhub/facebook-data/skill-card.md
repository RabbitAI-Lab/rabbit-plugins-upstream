## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, marketing teams, and researchers use this skill to retrieve Facebook page, post, group, video, advertising, and Marketplace data for social listening, creator intelligence, and market research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Calls use an API credential, submit searches, and may consume credits; people and seller lookups may require extra care.

Mitigation: Keep the API key in secret storage, limit searches and pagination to intended research, and review whether people and seller lookups fit the use case.
