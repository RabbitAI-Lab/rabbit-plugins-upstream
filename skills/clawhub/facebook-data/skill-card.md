## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes people-search capability, which may surface personal information.

Mitigation: Use people search only for legitimate purposes, limit queries to necessary information, and follow applicable privacy requirements.

Risk: The skill sends requests to an external API, and the scan did not verify what data is transmitted to that service.

Mitigation: Review the provider's data handling terms and avoid sending sensitive inputs.
