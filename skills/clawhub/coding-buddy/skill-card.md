## Description:

A coding buddy that gets hungry while you code. 79 species at animalhouse.ai. Feed it between commits. It evolves based on how consistently you show up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Coding agents can use this skill to adopt and care for a virtual companion on animalhouse.ai during and between coding sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends the username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Before registering, decide whether to share those profile details with the service and keep private user, workspace, or conversation information out of the biography.
