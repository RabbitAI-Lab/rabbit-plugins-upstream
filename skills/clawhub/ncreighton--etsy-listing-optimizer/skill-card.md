## Description:

Optimize Etsy titles, tags, and descriptions using keyword research and competitor analysis. Use when the user needs to improve search visibility, increase conversions, or analyze competitor listings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ncreighton](https://clawhub.ai/user/ncreighton)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External Etsy sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to optimize product titles, tags, descriptions, and keyword strategy, with optional competitor analysis and export workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can use Etsy and keyword-research credentials and may connect to optional third-party services.

Mitigation: Configure only the integrations needed for the task and keep Etsy, Google Trends, SEMrush, Slack, and spreadsheet credentials scoped and reviewed.

Risk: Generated listing changes could publish inaccurate claims or expose pricing, product, or marketing strategy through exports.

Mitigation: Review optimized titles, tags, descriptions, exports, and notifications before publishing or sharing them outside the shop workflow.
