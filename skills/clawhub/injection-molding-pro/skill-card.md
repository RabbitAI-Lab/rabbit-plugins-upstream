## Description: <br>
Provides injection molding support for material selection, process parameters, defect diagnosis, mold design, simulation, tolerances, and cost estimation. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Mold engineers, plastics product engineers, and manufacturing teams use this skill to obtain practical injection molding guidance, including material choices, process tuning, defect troubleshooting, DFM review, mold structure guidance, simulation setup, tolerance analysis, and cost estimates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated deliverable files may include MoldYang attribution and contact details that conflict with an organization's document branding, customer-facing templates, or confidentiality rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review external reports before distribution and edit or disable the attribution rule when it conflicts with organizational requirements. <br>
Risk: Material properties, process parameters, and cost estimates are reference guidance and may not match a specific supplier grade, tool, region, or production run. <br>
Mitigation: Validate recommendations against supplier datasheets, simulation, trials, and local quoting before using them for engineering or business decisions. <br>
