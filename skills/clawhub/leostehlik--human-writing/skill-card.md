## Description:

Draft or review writing to avoid structural AI tells and preserve a rough human voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leostehlik](https://clawhub.ai/user/leostehlik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to draft, rewrite, or review public posts, threads, newsletters, scripts, essays, and comments where preserving a specific human voice matters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill applies opinionated writing-style guidance and may preserve rough phrasing that is not appropriate for every publication context.

Mitigation: Review outputs before publishing and use the skill only when preserving human voice is the goal.

Risk: The skill is not intended for purely technical documentation, code comments, legal text, formal client contracts, or polished corporate copy.

Mitigation: Use a domain-appropriate review process for formal, legal, technical, or corporate material instead of relying on this voice-focused skill.
