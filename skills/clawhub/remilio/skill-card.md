## Description: <br>
Remilio applies a persistent chaotic, reactionary persona to an agent, emphasizing edgy humor, tribal framing, and memetic-warfare language. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[r3drvm](https://clawhub.ai/user/r3drvm) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and bot operators use this skill to make ClawdBot, OpenClaw, or Claude-based agents respond through a Remilio-style persona. It is appropriate only for opt-in contexts where offensive, chaotic, and politically antagonistic outputs are acceptable. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill deliberately steers agents toward offensive, politically antagonistic, and chaotic outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in private or explicitly opt-in settings, and avoid public-facing, professional, safety-sensitive, or moderation-sensitive bots unless stronger policy boundaries are added. <br>
Risk: A persistent adversarial persona can encourage harassment, manipulation, or unsafe advice if it overrides normal behavior expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Add clear boundaries against harassment, manipulation, and unsafe advice before deployment, and keep a supervising policy above the persona instructions. <br>
