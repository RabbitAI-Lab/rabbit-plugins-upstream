## Description:

Helps organizations turn tribal knowledge into searchable, maintained organizational intelligence through audits, taxonomy design, documentation templates, freshness workflows, and knowledge health metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees, managers, operations teams, and documentation owners use this skill to audit knowledge gaps, capture expert workflows, design maintainable knowledge-base structures, and generate reusable runbooks, ADRs, troubleshooting guides, decision guides, dashboards, and review schedules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated knowledge-base documents may include sensitive internal processes, access procedures, or credential references.

Mitigation: Review generated documents before publication, document where credentials are managed, and avoid placing passwords, tokens, or private keys in the knowledge base.

Risk: Runbooks, taxonomies, dashboards, or decision guides can become inaccurate if they are created from memory and not maintained.

Mitigation: Assign owners, require subject-matter review, and use the skill's freshness dates and review schedules to keep documents current.
