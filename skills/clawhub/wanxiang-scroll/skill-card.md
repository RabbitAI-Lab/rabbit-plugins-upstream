## Description:

Supports interactive storytelling, turn-based life simulation, style switching, and long-form creative writing workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Writers and interactive-fiction players use this skill to co-create stories, simulate character lives, switch writing styles, and plan or revise long-form fiction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Writing references include AI-detection evasion and ghostwriting-style prompts that could be used to misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Review these references before use and do not use them to misrepresent authorship in academic, publishing, or platform contexts.

Risk: Optional crawler and save-export scripts may access outside content or local story files.

Mitigation: Review these scripts before running them and use them only when their access is intended.
