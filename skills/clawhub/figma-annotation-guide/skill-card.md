## Description: <br>
Generate structured developer handoff annotations for a Figma screen or component, covering interactions, states, spacing, accessibility, and edge cases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Designers, product managers, and developers use this skill to turn Figma screen or component descriptions into developer handoff annotations. It helps document interactions, UI states, spacing, layout behavior, content rules, accessibility notes, edge cases, and open developer questions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Confidential product or design details may be included in prompts used to generate handoff notes. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential product details unless they are appropriate to share in the agent session. <br>
Risk: Generated annotations may omit required design states or include inaccurate implementation assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review the output against the Figma design, required states, accessibility expectations, and developer questions before handoff. <br>
