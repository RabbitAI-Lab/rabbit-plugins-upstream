## Description:

Helps analyze competitor content across YouTube, TikTok, Substack, and blogs for engagement trends, posting patterns, and monetization signals, with optional Slack briefings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and digital marketers use this skill to benchmark competitor content, spot content gaps and successful hooks, and prepare competitive briefings or alerts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected platform API keys and Slack webhook credentials could be exposed if stored in shared files.

Mitigation: Keep credentials in a secure environment or secret manager, avoid committing them, and use least-privilege access.

Risk: Exported reports may disclose sensitive business strategy or internal notes to external services.

Mitigation: Review report contents before sharing through Slack or other export destinations.
