## Description:

Writes a YouTube video script in the chat, shaped to its genre (explainer, list, tutorial, review, story), with a hook, sections and one call to action, set out as what is SAID beside what is SEEN; only if the person wants footage does the SEE column go to Phantra as scenes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators use the skill to plan or draft YouTube and video scripts as paired spoken lines and visual directions, tailored to the audience, length, and genre. It can also help plan optional footage scenes and refine a caption against the creator’s posted videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A script could present unsupported facts, quotes, test results, or personal experiences as true.

Mitigation: Use facts and examples supplied by the creator; ask for missing information and leave marked gaps rather than inventing claims.

Risk: Optional studio footage rendering incurs a per-scene price.

Mitigation: Plan scenes with the creator, obtain a quote, and render only after the creator approves the price.
