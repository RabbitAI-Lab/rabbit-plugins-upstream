## Description:

为初中与高中化学教师设计以宏观—微观—符号三重表征为主线的课时教案、单元整合、复习课和讲评课草稿，并要求教师复核生成内容。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初中和高中化学教师，用于依据课程内容、班级学情与课时规划新授、复习和讲评教案。生成内容是供教师审阅的草稿，不代替教师备课或课堂判断。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom records may contain identifying student information.

Mitigation: Use anonymized classroom information and review the platform’s memory and sharing settings.

Risk: Generated questions or laboratory guidance may be incorrect or unsuitable for classroom use.

Mitigation: Have a qualified teacher verify generated questions and laboratory safety before classroom use.

Risk: A generated lesson plan may be saved before it has been reviewed.

Mitigation: Approve saved plans deliberately.
