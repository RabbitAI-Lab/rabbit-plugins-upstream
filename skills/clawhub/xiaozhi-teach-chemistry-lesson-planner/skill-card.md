## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers draft junior and senior secondary lesson plans organized around observable phenomena, particle-level explanations, and chemical symbols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft and adapt Chinese-language junior and senior secondary lesson plans, including unit pacing, question sequences, and experiment placements. Teachers review all drafts before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, language, data-consent rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson plans may not match the local curriculum or class needs.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check curriculum alignment, pacing, and classroom records before use; localize for use outside mainland China.

Risk: Experiment placements could be mistaken for safe-to-run procedures.

Mitigation: Treat placements as planning prompts only; have the teacher verify equipment and school safety rules and use dedicated laboratory guidance for procedures.

Risk: Class records can contain sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Use class-level details or pseudonyms and apply local data-consent requirements.
