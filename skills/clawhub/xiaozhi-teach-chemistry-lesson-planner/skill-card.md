## Description:

This skill helps Chinese K12 chemistry teachers draft lesson-plan outlines, question chains, board plans, and classroom records around the macro-micro-symbolic representation model.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External teachers use this skill to plan junior-high and high-school chemistry lessons, including compressed unit planning, high-school transition lessons, differentiated classroom segments, and experiment placement that remains subject to teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China unless localized for other regions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated lesson plans or chemistry materials may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific class.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and require teacher review before classroom use or resource-bank entry.

Risk: Experiment placement and safety levels may not match a school's equipment, room setup, or safety rules.

Mitigation: Have the teacher verify experiment placement and safetyLevel against school procedures; route detailed procedures and emergency planning to the chemistry lab-guide skill.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis/help contacts are Mainland China oriented.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum standards, terminology, and help contacts before deploying outside Mainland China.

Risk: Classroom records could expose student personal data if teachers paste identifiable rosters or notes.

Mitigation: Use class names, aliases, or seat numbers, and avoid real names, contact information, family circumstances, or health information.
