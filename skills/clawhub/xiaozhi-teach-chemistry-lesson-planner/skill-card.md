## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers draft lessons connecting observable phenomena, particle-level explanations, and chemical notation, including compressed junior-secondary units and introductory senior-secondary transitions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers in mainland China's junior and senior secondary schools use this skill to draft lesson sequences, question prompts, and curriculum transitions for their review before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, minor-consent practices, and crisis referral channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry materials or experiment placements could be mistaken for classroom-ready plans.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check scientific accuracy and school-specific safety requirements; use the separate lab guide for procedures and emergency planning.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, minor-consent assumptions, or crisis contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum standards, consent practices, and help channels before exposing the skill to teachers or students elsewhere.
