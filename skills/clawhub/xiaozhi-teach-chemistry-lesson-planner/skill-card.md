## Description:

Creates chemistry lesson plan drafts organized around connections between observable phenomena, particle-level explanations, and chemical symbols, for middle- and high-school teachers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft and structure middle- and high-school lessons, including unit planning, review lessons, and exam feedback lessons. Teachers review the drafts and confirm any classroom or experiment details before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions or experiment suggestions may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular class or classroom.

Mitigation: Teachers should review generated questions and experiment suggestions, verify them against local curriculum and safety requirements, and confirm lesson plans before use.

Risk: Classroom records or exam materials may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, avoid confidential exam material, and keep personal student details out of lesson drafts.
