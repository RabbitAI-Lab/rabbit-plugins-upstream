## Description:

Drafts junior and senior high school chemistry lesson plans in Chinese using macroscopic observations, particle-level explanations, and chemical symbols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language chemistry teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft curriculum-aligned lessons, integrate units when teaching time is limited, and plan transitions from junior to senior high school chemistry. Lesson plans and proposed materials require teacher review before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, safety contacts, and rules for minors' data before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft lessons or AI-generated materials may contain mistakes or misalign with local teaching requirements.

Mitigation: Teachers verify the lesson plan, curriculum alignment, and generated materials before classroom use.

Risk: An experiment placeholder may be mistaken for an approved procedure.

Mitigation: Keep experiment content at planning level; teachers verify safety decisions and use dedicated laboratory guidance before conducting experiments.

Risk: Using mainland China curriculum, safety contacts, or minors' data assumptions in another region may be inappropriate.

Mitigation: Localize those assumptions before use outside mainland China; use pseudonyms or seat numbers rather than identifying student information.
