## Description:

Helps Chinese-language middle and high school chemistry teachers draft lesson plans connecting observable phenomena, particle-level explanations, and chemical notation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft junior secondary lesson plans, consolidate units under time constraints, and bridge junior to senior secondary chemistry. Teachers review all drafts before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, emergency resources, and consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft lessons or AI-generated questions may contain curriculum or chemistry errors.

Mitigation: Teachers review plans and verify generated questions and materials before classroom use.

Risk: An experiment placement may not fit local school equipment or safety rules.

Mitigation: Teachers check safety levels and school requirements; refer detailed procedures and emergency planning to the chemistry lab guide.

Risk: Class records may expose identifiable student information.

Mitigation: Use class-level information, pseudonyms, or seat numbers rather than students' names or sensitive details, and apply local consent rules.

Risk: Curriculum references and emergency contacts may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, consent rules, and emergency resources before deployment in another region.
