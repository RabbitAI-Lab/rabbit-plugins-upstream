## Use Case:

External educators use this skill to draft Chinese K12 chemistry lesson plans, question chains, triple-representation teaching paths, and unit-compression plans for teacher review. It is scoped to chemistry lesson design and redirects laboratory procedure planning, notation drills, exam design, and other subjects to separate skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson plans, generated questions, and classroom guidance may be inaccurate or misaligned with a local school curriculum.

Mitigation: Use outputs as teacher-reviewed drafts and verify them against the local curriculum, textbook sequence, and school requirements before classroom use.

Risk: Chemistry lesson drafts may mention experiments without enough context for safe classroom execution.

Mitigation: Treat experiment entries as lesson-design placeholders only; have a qualified teacher validate equipment, procedures, safety controls, and emergency handling before any lab activity.

Risk: Use outside Mainland China may expose mismatched curriculum, emergency-channel, or student-data assumptions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum references, crisis response channels, and data-consent rules before deploying in another region.

Risk: Classroom records can include sensitive student information if users paste real names or personal details.

Mitigation: Use aliases, seat numbers, or class-level descriptors instead of real student names, contact information, family details, or health information.
