## Description:

为中国大陆初高中化学教师生成以宏观现象、微观解释与化学符号为主线的教案草稿，涵盖课时整合、初高衔接、复习课和讲评课。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

化学教师用它规划初高中新授课、单元整合、复习课与考后讲评课的教学环节及提问链；所有教案均为须由教师核对的草稿。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy controls and emergency contacts before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class-level and pseudonymous student learning data may be retained or shared.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent, memory, sharing and deletion controls; exclude direct student identifiers.

Risk: Draft experiment placements or AI-generated teaching material could be mistaken for verified classroom instructions.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check scientific accuracy and school safety rules; use the separate laboratory guide for equipment, procedures and emergency planning.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and emergency contacts may not fit other locations.

Mitigation: Verify local curriculum requirements and replace emergency contacts before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Unreleased exam questions or student results could be exposed in lesson planning.

Mitigation: Do not submit unreleased exam materials; use completed exams and anonymized, class-level error summaries for review lessons.
