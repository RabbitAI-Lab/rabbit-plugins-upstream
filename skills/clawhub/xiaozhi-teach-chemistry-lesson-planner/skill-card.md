## Use Case:

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; teaching guidance is tailored to Chinese middle and high school chemistry curricula, so teachers should check local requirements.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry explanations, questions, and lesson plans may contain errors or may not fit the local syllabus.

Mitigation: Have a chemistry teacher check content, questions, and curriculum fit before classroom use; verify generated questions before reuse.

Risk: Experiment placements in a lesson draft could be mistaken for complete procedures or safe classroom instructions.

Mitigation: Treat them as planning prompts only; confirm equipment and school safety rules, and use the dedicated chemistry lab guide for procedures and safety planning.

Risk: Classroom records can contain sensitive student information, and platform memory or sharing behavior may vary.

Mitigation: Use anonymized classroom data and confirm the platform's memory and sharing controls before connecting class data.
