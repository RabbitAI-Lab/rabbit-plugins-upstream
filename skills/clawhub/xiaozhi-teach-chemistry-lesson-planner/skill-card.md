## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson drafts may not match a school's curriculum or local exam requirements.

Mitigation: Have teachers check curriculum, textbook, timetable and current local exam guidance before use.

Risk: Class teaching data may expose student information.

Mitigation: Use class-level data or pseudonyms, exclude student identifiers and sensitive personal details, and follow local data-consent rules.

Risk: Experiment suggestions may be unsafe if treated as laboratory instructions.

Mitigation: Keep experiments as lesson-plan placeholders; have teachers verify safety against school rules and use a dedicated lab-safety process for procedures.

Risk: Unfinished standardized-exam materials may be confidential.

Mitigation: Do not enter or discuss restricted exam materials until all scheduled sittings are complete and applicable rules allow it.

Risk: Student crisis disclosures require support beyond lesson planning.

Mitigation: Stop lesson-planning flow and refer to trusted adults and region-appropriate emergency or support services.
