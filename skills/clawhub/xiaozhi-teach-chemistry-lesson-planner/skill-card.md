## Description:

为初中和高中化学教师起草以宏观现象、微观解释和化学符号为主线的教案，支持单元统筹、课时压缩及初高衔接。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft chemistry explanations, questions or lesson pacing may be inaccurate or unsuitable for the local curriculum.

Mitigation: Teachers should verify content, generated questions, timing and alignment with their curriculum before classroom use.

Risk: Treating an experiment slot as a procedure could expose students to unsafe laboratory activity.

Mitigation: Keep experiments at planning level; have a teacher check materials, safety level and school procedures and use separate laboratory guidance for execution.

Risk: Classroom records may contain students' identifying or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use class-level data, pseudonyms or seat numbers instead of real names, contact details or sensitive personal records.
