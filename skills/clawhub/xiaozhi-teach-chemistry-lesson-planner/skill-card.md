## Description:

为初高中化学教师起草以宏观现象、微观解释和化学符号为主线的教案，支持课时整合与初高衔接。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers in the Mainland China K12 curriculum use this skill to draft lesson sequences, differentiated questions and junior-to-senior-high transition lessons for their review before classroom use. It identifies where experiments support learning but does not provide experimental procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated teaching questions or lesson content may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Teachers review and verify all drafts and generated questions before use or inclusion in teaching materials.

Risk: An experiment placeholder could be mistaken for a safe procedure.

Mitigation: Keep experiment content at the planning level, flag its safety level and refer procedures and on-site safety decisions to a qualified teacher and laboratory guidance.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts and student-data practices assume Mainland China.

Mitigation: Outside that locale, adapt curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts and data-consent rules before using the skill with students.
