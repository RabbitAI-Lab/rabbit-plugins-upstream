## Description: <br>
Turns a product image into a polished short ad or promo video, with guidance for image-to-video generation or product swaps in existing footage. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to create short product ads from hero stills, draft and refine video-generation requests, or swap a product into existing footage while preserving the source scene. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product images or footage may be sent to external video-generation providers through Runware. <br>
Mitigation: Use only media that the user is authorized to process, and avoid confidential, regulated, or personally identifying media unless provider retention, deletion, and consent requirements have been reviewed. <br>
Risk: Generated ad videos may introduce unsupported product claims, testimonials, logos, badges, or readable copy. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against user-supplied brand facts and retry or cut any invented claims, ratings, testimonials, logos, award badges, or unsupplied on-screen text. <br>
