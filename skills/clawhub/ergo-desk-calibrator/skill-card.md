## Description:

Calculates personalized chair, desk, and monitor positions from body height and provides workstation checks and practical posture guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Home-office workers and other desk users use this skill to estimate workstation dimensions, check monitor and laptop placement, and review ergonomic adjustments for discomfort.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Symptom-based suggestions may be mistaken for a medical diagnosis.

Mitigation: Use suggestions for workstation adjustments only; seek a clinician for persistent, worsening, or radiating symptoms.

Risk: Population-average proportions may not fit an individual's body or equipment.

Mitigation: Treat calculated measurements as starting points and adjust them for comfort and proper support.
