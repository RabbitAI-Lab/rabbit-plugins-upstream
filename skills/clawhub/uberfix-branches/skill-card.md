## Description:

Find or list UberFix branches through its operational API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

UberFix staff and authorized users can list service branches or find nearby branches using a supplied location or city and a configured UberFix connector.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A branch listing may include another company's branches if connector access is too broad.

Mitigation: Configure the connector with credentials restricted to branches the user is authorized to access; do not share other companies' branch lists.
