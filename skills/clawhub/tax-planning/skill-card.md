## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tax rates, deadlines, deduction thresholds, mileage rates, retirement contribution limits, and entity election rules can change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current IRS and state requirements before acting on the guidance. <br>
Risk: The skill provides general educational tax planning guidance, not professional tax advice for a specific taxpayer. <br>
Mitigation: Consult a qualified CPA for entity decisions, S-Corp election, payroll setup, complex deductions, notices, audits, or filing decisions. <br>
