## Description: <br>
AI CGO is an AI-driven growth operating system that routes business growth requests into diagnosis, workflow design, or optimization outputs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Growth leaders, founders, marketers, and operators use this skill to diagnose growth bottlenecks, design AI-assisted growth workflows, and audit or optimize funnels, campaigns, and growth systems. It emphasizes business context, unit economics, measurable experiments, and human review of recommendations before real business changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Growth recommendations could affect budgets, pricing, ad bidding, segmentation, campaigns, or persistent knowledge files if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying them to business operations and require explicit human approval for real business changes. <br>
Risk: Recommendations may rely on assumed or incomplete business metrics such as CAC, LTV, margins, or funnel performance. <br>
Mitigation: Provide current business metrics where possible and treat assumption-backed recommendations as planning inputs until validated. <br>
