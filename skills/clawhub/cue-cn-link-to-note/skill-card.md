## Description:

Turns one URL, through an optional parsing channel, or a local text file into a structured Markdown note with source-anchored points and a checkable quotation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huhoo](https://clawhub.ai/user/huhoo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External readers can turn a single article into a note for their personal knowledge base, with source locators for review. The skill does not assess credibility or replace human judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A passing check cannot guarantee that quotations are exact or that missing information is fully disclosed.

Mitigation: Check quotations and missing-information statements against the source yourself, even after a passing result.

Risk: Confidential material sent through the optional parsing channel is subject to that service's data handling.

Mitigation: Use local files for confidential material unless you have approved the parser's data handling.

Risk: The bundled-file assessment does not cover future downloads or the separate parsing service.

Mitigation: Review or pin installer content, and assess the parsing service separately before use.
