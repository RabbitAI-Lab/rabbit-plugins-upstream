## Description:

通过古人角色对话、背诵练习和分步解析，帮助中文学习者理解古诗文并练习文言阅读与鉴赏题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students and their educators use this skill to study a named classical text, poem or author through guided roleplay, recall games, translation practice and exam-answer coaching. For younger learners, confirm guardian or teacher involvement and learning-record consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; adapt curriculum, emergency contacts and minor-consent practices before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles and reminders may involve minors' personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian and learner consent, locale-specific requirements, and opt-in settings before enabling memory, cross-skill sharing or reminders.

Risk: Roleplay or generated exercises may misstate historical facts or original poem wording.

Mitigation: Check author details and quotations against the referenced profiles and textbook; state uncertainty instead of inventing details.

Risk: A learner may disclose distress or danger during an emotionally charged roleplay.

Mitigation: Leave the role immediately, follow the skill's crisis protocol, and localize emergency referrals to the learner's region.
