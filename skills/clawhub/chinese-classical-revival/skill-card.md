## Use Case:

External learners, parents, teachers, and tutoring agents use this skill for Chinese classical literature study: author role-play, poem memorization practice, classical Chinese fundamentals, poetry appreciation, and exam-answer guidance. It is scoped to prompts with a specific classical text, verse, or author.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning-profile sharing and reminder settings may expose student data or create unwanted follow-up prompts.

Mitigation: Review consent controls before use, honor opt-outs for memory, sharing, export, deletion, and reminders, and keep profile writes scoped to the documented learning fields.

Risk: Curriculum, consent expectations, and crisis-help channels may not fit deployments outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, data-consent rules, and crisis-referral channels before deployment in another region.

Risk: Role-play about exile, war, death, or separation could surface learner distress.

Mitigation: Exit character immediately when distress or safety signals appear and follow the documented crisis-referral protocol.
