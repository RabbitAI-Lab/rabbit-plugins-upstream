## Description:

A Chinese classical literature tutor that uses author role-play, recitation practice, vocabulary lessons, and exam-response strategies to help learners understand classical texts and poetry.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students studying classical Chinese and poetry use this skill to explore a named text or author, practice recitation, understand vocabulary and literary context, and learn structured exam-answer methods.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records, cross-skill sharing, or reminders may involve student learning data.

Mitigation: Let the learner choose whether to enable each feature; use “这次不要记忆” for a session without profile reads, writes, or handoffs, and review the host platform and companion skills separately.
