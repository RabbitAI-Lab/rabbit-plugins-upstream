## Description:

A Chinese-language tutor that helps students understand classical poetry and prose through historical role-play, guided recitation, interpretation, and exam-answer practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language skill to explore named classical works or authors, practice memorization, and learn how to answer classical Chinese reading and poetry appreciation questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); deployment elsewhere requires localizing crisis referrals and minors' privacy and consent settings.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent visibility, and reminders may expose minors' information if misconfigured.

Mitigation: Review consent and profile, sharing, parent-visibility, and reminder controls before use; configure them for the deployment region and users.

Risk: Crisis referrals and consent defaults may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency resources and minors' consent requirements before deployment in other regions.
