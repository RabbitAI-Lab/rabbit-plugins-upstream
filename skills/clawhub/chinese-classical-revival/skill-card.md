## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved learning records, cross-skill sharing, and reminders can involve student learning data; the separate learning-DNA and reminder integrations were not covered in the reviewed runtime evidence.

Mitigation: Enable these features only when wanted, use “这次不要记忆” for a session without profile access or updates, and review the separate integrations before enabling them.

Risk: Crisis handling allows an exception to ordinary parent-sharing preferences, and role-play may touch on distressing historical themes.

Mitigation: Exit the role immediately when a student connects the material to personal distress, then follow the documented crisis referral path and explain the privacy exception.
