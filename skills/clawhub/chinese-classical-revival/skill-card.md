## Description:

Tutors students in classical Chinese prose and poetry through historical roleplay, guided recitation, interpretation, and exam-answer practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language skill to study specific classical works, practice recitation and interpretation, and prepare for classical-literature questions, primarily in Mainland China K–12 curricula.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, crisis contacts, consent, and guardian workflows before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis referrals and curriculum guidance may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, consent rules, and guardian workflows before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Student learning profiles, sharing, and reminders may expose sensitive information or persist without the student's intent.

Mitigation: Review and honor controls for profile memory, sharing, reminder consent, deletion, and export.

Risk: Historical roleplay or generated recitation exercises may misstate facts or original wording.

Mitigation: Separate roleplay from factual explanation and check historical claims and quotations against the course text.
