## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed around Chinese K12 classical literature tutoring and mainland-China curriculum assumptions.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended classroom or tutoring context before deployment and localize curriculum expectations when used outside that context.

Risk: The skill handles minors' learning profiles and may produce reminders or cross-skill sharing summaries.

Mitigation: Use the documented privacy controls for view, correction, deletion, pause, export, and sharing restrictions, and obtain appropriate consent for minor-data use.

Risk: Crisis or safety signals can appear during role-play about exile, war, death, bullying, or family safety.

Mitigation: Exit role-play immediately and use localized crisis referral contacts and trusted-adult escalation before resuming any tutoring flow.

Risk: Classical text explanations and exam practice can mislead learners if source text, edition scope, or answer constraints are wrong.

Mitigation: Check source wording, grade scope, and answer uniqueness before drills, and use graduated hints rather than directly supplying exam answers.
