## Description:

Guides Chinese-language students through classical poetry and prose with historical role-play, recitation practice, and reading and appreciation strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to explore specific classical Chinese works and authors, practice memorization, and learn how to answer classical-language and poetry-analysis questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-mainland curriculum context; adapt curriculum and crisis guidance for other regions

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles or practice reminders may be shared without clear student consent.

Mitigation: Confirm profile-sharing and reminder consent before enabling either feature; honor pause, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Mainland-China curriculum assumptions or crisis contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Check local course requirements and adapt crisis referral contacts to the student's location before deployment.
