## Description:

Quickstart for AI orchestrators using @dlazy/cli to install and authenticate, discover capabilities, invoke cloud and local tools, poll asynchronous tasks, and recover from common failures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and users of AI orchestrators such as Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and Copilot use this guide to operate @dlazy/cli, inspect available tools, invoke them safely, and handle asynchronous results and common failures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The guide recommends installing an unpinned CLI release, and its cookie guidance lacks a privacy warning.

Mitigation: Wait until the CLI version is pinned to a reviewed release and the cookie guidance explains the risk and requires informed approval. If proceeding, avoid granting access to an everyday browser session; the scan was partial and does not provide complete assurance.
