## Description:

将长篇小说转换为 Seedance 2.0 平台的 15 秒动态漫画分镜脚本，包含剧情容量分析、纯文本角色卡、6-8 个分镜头、微动作、镜头运动、配音和音效设计。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fslong520](https://clawhub.ai/user/fslong520)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and agents use this skill to turn supplied novel passages or plot summaries into short VOMIC-style dynamic-comic storyboard scripts for Seedance 2.0. It guides episode sizing, style and aspect-ratio choices, narration voice, shot structure, static-image prompts, camera movement, dialogue, inner monologue, sound effects, and final checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact bundles a full novel and the skill is designed to adapt source prose into dynamic-comic scripts.

Mitigation: Use only source material the user has rights to adapt, and confirm copyright or licensing status before uploading generated scripts or derived content to Seedance or other platforms.

Risk: Generated scripts are intended to be copied into Seedance 2.0, which may expose user-provided story content to an external service.

Mitigation: Review the text for sensitive, private, or restricted content before pasting it into external video-generation tools.

Risk: The skill produces creative guidance and scripts, so output may still need editorial review for accuracy, pacing, suitability, and rights clearance.

Mitigation: Review each generated storyboard against the checklist, source material, and publication requirements before production use.
