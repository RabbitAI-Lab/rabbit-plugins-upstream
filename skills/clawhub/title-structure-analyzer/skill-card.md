## Description:

Analyzes academic paper title structures and evaluates whether a title is appropriate for the research context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, academic writers, and editors use this skill to classify a paper title as concept, relationship, finding, question, method, or review oriented, then assess fit and revise the structure when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Title-structure advice may be incomplete for fields, journals, or languages outside the Chinese-language academic-title review posture identified by the security guidance.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the target discipline, publication venue, and language before revising or submitting a title.

Risk: The skill may classify a title without enough research context to judge whether the structure fits the study.

Mitigation: Provide the research aim, method, or abstract when requesting analysis, and have a human reviewer confirm the final title.
