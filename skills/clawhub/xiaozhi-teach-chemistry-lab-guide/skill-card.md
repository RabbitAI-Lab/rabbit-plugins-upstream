## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan safe classroom experiments, group and equipment arrangements, lab reports, and practical exam preparation for middle and high school.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to choose demonstrations or student group work, plan equipment and timing, organize lab safety and practical exam preparation, and draft lab reports for middle and high school courses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft laboratory plans may not match a school's equipment, facilities, or safety procedures.

Mitigation: A teacher should verify each plan against the school's laboratory rules and available equipment before class.

Risk: Class records or examination content may expose personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, exclude confidential examination material, review changes before saving, and use the no-memory option when appropriate.

Risk: Host-platform storage and separately installed companion skills are outside this release's security review.

Mitigation: Check the host platform's storage controls and the data handling of installed companion skills before using shared class records.
