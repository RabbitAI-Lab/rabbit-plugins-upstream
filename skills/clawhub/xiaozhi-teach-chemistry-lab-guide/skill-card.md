## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan and organize laboratory lessons, including demonstration or group-work choices, equipment and group arrangements, classroom safety, lab reports, and practical exam preparation across middle and high school experiments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to prepare safe, practical laboratory lesson plans, organize groups and equipment, guide experiment records, and rehearse publicly disclosed practical-exam requirements. It produces drafts for a teacher to review before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Primarily China; the curriculum and practical-exam guidance reflects Chinese middle and high schools.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposed experiment plan may not match the school's equipment, facilities, or safety rules.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check every plan against school requirements; treat high-risk experiments as demonstrations and do not recommend them for home use.

Risk: Class records or unreleased exam material could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, keep confidential exam material out of the skill, and check the host platform's privacy controls before enabling memory or sharing.

Risk: The three named companion skills are outside the evaluated bundle.

Mitigation: Review their behavior separately before relying on them in a connected workflow.
