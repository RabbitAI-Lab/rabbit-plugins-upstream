## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan supervised school laboratory lessons, including experiment grouping, safety, observation reports, and practical exam preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers in Mainland China use this skill to draft classroom experiment plans, organize supervised groups and equipment, prepare safety briefings, and structure lab reports and practical exam practice. Teachers review plans against school laboratory rules before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, legal references, language, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An incorrect safety classification or lesson plan could expose students to hazardous experiments.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check every plan against school laboratory rules; keep high-risk experiments teacher-led and never use this as an at-home experiment guide.

Risk: Crisis referrals, curriculum, or legal guidance may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, curriculum, legal references, and language before deploying elsewhere.

Risk: Student information or draft assessments may be used without appropriate review.

Mitigation: Apply school privacy practices and require teacher confirmation before recording workspace changes or sharing student-facing material.
