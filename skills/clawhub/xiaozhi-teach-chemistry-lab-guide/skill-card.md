This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中化学教师用它拟定实验课的安全分级、分组轮换、器材与时间安排，以及实验报告和操作考试的教学草稿。教师审核后再用于课堂。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposed lab activity may not match the school's equipment or safety requirements.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check each plan against the school's lab safety rules and available facilities before class.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent rules may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize those details and verify local requirements before using the skill outside mainland China.
