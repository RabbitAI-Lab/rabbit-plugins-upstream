## Description:

Helps Chinese secondary-school chemistry teachers plan and supervise laboratory lessons, including group organization, safety, observation records, and practical-exam preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft junior- and senior-secondary lab plans, choose demonstrations or group activities, allocate equipment, and prepare observation and practical-exam exercises. Teachers review plans before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (designed for Mainland China; verify local safety and examination requirements elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An unsuitable lab plan could expose students to chemicals, heat, or other hazards.

Mitigation: Teachers should verify each plan against school safety rules, facilities, and equipment; keep high-risk experiments as supervised demonstrations.

Risk: China-focused exam and classroom guidance may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Check current local examination requirements and adapt the plan before use.
