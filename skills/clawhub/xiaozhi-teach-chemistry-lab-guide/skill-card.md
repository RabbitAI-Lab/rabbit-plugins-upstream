帮助初高中化学教师规划实验课的演示与分组、器材和时间安排、安全组织、实验报告及操作考试练习。

## Use Case:

初高中化学教师用它拟定实验课组织方案，安排学生轮流操作、课堂安全提示、实验报告反馈要点和实验操作考试模拟；所有方案须由教师结合学校制度审定。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A suggested experiment may not match local equipment, laboratory rules or emergency procedures.

Mitigation: Teachers must check equipment, safety level and school laboratory policy before use; reserve high-risk experiments for demonstration or simulation.

Risk: China-mainland curriculum assumptions or emergency contacts may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Confirm curriculum alignment and replace region-specific safety and emergency guidance with local procedures before classroom use.

Risk: Classroom records or unreleased examination materials could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized classroom data and public examination guidance; do not provide unreleased exam questions or scoring criteria.
