## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan supervised laboratory lessons, including experiment format, group rotations, safety, reports, and practical-exam practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Mainland China's Chinese-language secondary schools use this skill to draft chemistry lab lesson plans, assign safe group activities, and prepare experiment reports and practical-exam exercises. Teachers review all materials against school rules before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe experiment grouping or instructions could expose students to chemical or equipment hazards.

Mitigation: Have teachers verify each safety level and school laboratory rules; reserve high-risk experiments for teacher demonstration and do not provide at-home hazardous experiment steps.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts, and student-data practices may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts, privacy requirements, and lab-safety rules before use in another region.

Risk: Unreviewed drafts or classroom-record updates could misguide teaching or alter class records.

Mitigation: Require teacher review before classroom use and confirmation before saving proposed record changes.
