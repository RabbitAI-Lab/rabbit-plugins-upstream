## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan laboratory lessons, including demonstration versus group work, equipment allocation, classroom safety, lab reports, and practical exam preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft safe, age-appropriate lab lesson plans, group rotations, observation and reporting prompts, and practical exam practice. Teachers review each draft against school rules, available equipment, and current local exam requirements before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A suggested experiment may not suit local school safety rules or available equipment.

Mitigation: Teachers verify each plan against school procedures and equipment before classroom use; high-risk experiments remain teacher demonstrations.

Risk: Practical exam guidance may not match current local requirements.

Mitigation: Teachers check current local exam instructions and scoring criteria before using practice plans.
