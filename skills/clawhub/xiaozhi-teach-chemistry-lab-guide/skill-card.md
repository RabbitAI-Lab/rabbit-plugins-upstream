## Description:

Helps Chinese-language chemistry teachers plan and organize school laboratory lessons, including grouping, equipment, safety, reports, and practical exams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft safe group and demonstration lab plans, allocate equipment and class time, guide observation and reporting, and prepare practical-exam practice for middle and high school students. Teachers review plans against school rules before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lab guidance may not match local equipment, hazards, or school safety rules.

Mitigation: Teachers verify each plan against school procedures; high-risk experiments remain demonstrations rather than student group work.

Risk: Emergency contacts, curriculum assumptions, and student-data consent vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Localize these requirements before use outside the intended Chinese-language school setting.
