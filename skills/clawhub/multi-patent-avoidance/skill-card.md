## Description:

A Chinese-language eight-step workflow for multi-patent FTO and design-around analysis that uses application requirements, patent intelligence matrices, safe-zone mapping, cross-scanning, and targeted repair to propose candidate non-infringement positions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent analysts, product teams, and IP counsel use this skill to structure FTO review for two or more risk patents, identify safe technical zones, draft candidate designs, and prepare counsel-reviewable FTO opinion frameworks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated FTO opinions and non-infringement conclusions can be incomplete or incorrect for product, market-entry, or legal decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and have qualified patent counsel verify claim interpretation, equivalents analysis, prosecution-history issues, and final legal conclusions.

Risk: The workflow expects intentional use of PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP access for live patent data.

Mitigation: Enable that access only in environments where the user is authorized to use the service and understands what patent data will be queried.
