## Description:

A Chinese-language, eight-step workflow for application-driven freedom-to-operate analysis and design-around planning across two or more risk patents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent analysts, product teams, and technical developers use this skill to structure multi-patent FTO reviews, identify safe design zones, compare candidate schemes against a patent set, and draft review-ready FTO opinion frameworks. Final legal conclusions should be reviewed by qualified patent counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: FTO analysis outputs may be incomplete or unsuitable as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill to prepare review materials and have final FTO conclusions reviewed by qualified patent counsel.

Risk: Live patent-data results depend on a user-authorized Zhihuiya/Patsnap MCP service.

Mitigation: Confirm the required MCP service and account authorization are configured before relying on database-backed conclusions; otherwise treat the output as a framework.
