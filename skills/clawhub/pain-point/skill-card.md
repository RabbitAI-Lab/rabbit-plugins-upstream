## Description:

Analyzes Amazon product reviews to identify recurring customer pain points and unmet needs, rank them by frequency and sentiment, and support findings with review evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and ecommerce teams use this skill to understand recurring customer frustrations and unmet needs in collected product reviews, then identify evidence-backed product or listing improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may consume account credits during previews presented as free or execute a previously requested analysis without fresh approval.

Mitigation: Before analysis, set and verify the account option to ask before every charge. Use the dedicated quote command for price checks, choose manual collection for one-time work, and approve recurring costs separately.
