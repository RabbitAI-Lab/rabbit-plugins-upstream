## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to get concise A-share financial education, trading-rule explanations, and structured learning guidance. It should be treated as educational support rather than a source of brokerage, credential-handling, or investment-decision authority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad agent capabilities while addressing finance workflows that may involve sensitive user data or investment-advice-like outputs.

Mitigation: Review before installing, restrict use to educational finance explanations, and avoid sharing brokerage credentials, account secrets, or unnecessary personal trading history.

Risk: Users may over-rely on generated explanations or suggestions for actual investment decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational guidance only and require independent review by qualified humans before any financial action.
