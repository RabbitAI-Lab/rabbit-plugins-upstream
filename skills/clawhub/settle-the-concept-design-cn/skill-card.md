## Description:

从讨论中收敛概念设计，核对业务目标、同步规则、取舍与验收标准。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向产品和设计团队，整理讨论内容并形成可供用户确认的概念设计方案。方案涵盖业务目标、概念与同步规则、取舍、验收标准及仍未解决的事项。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposal or assumption could be mistaken for a confirmed design decision.

Mitigation: Keep confirmed decisions distinct from proposals, assumptions, and information gaps; ask the user to confirm only unresolved items.

Risk: Design confirmation could be mistaken for authorization to implement or release.

Mitigation: Treat implementation and release as requiring their own existing authorization, and seek authorization when it is missing.
