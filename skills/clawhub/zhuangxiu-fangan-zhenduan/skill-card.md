## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and renovation planners use this skill to diagnose whether a decoration or remodeling plan is practical, space-efficient, and resilient against common renovation mistakes. It provides structured advice for layout, storage, style, floor-plan changes, designer selection, and risk avoidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Renovation suggestions may touch structural, plumbing, ventilation, drainage, wet-area, appliance, or open-kitchen changes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those suggestions as planning ideas and confirm them with a qualified designer, contractor, structural engineer, and property manager before acting. <br>
Risk: Advice may not account for local permit rules, building management requirements, or site-specific constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Check applicable permitting and property-management requirements before removing walls, changing wet areas, relocating appliances, or altering ventilation and drainage. <br>
