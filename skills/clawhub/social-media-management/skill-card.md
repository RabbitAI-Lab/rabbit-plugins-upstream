## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Lead-generation, survey, customer-story, email, or gated-download guidance can involve customer data, confidential information, consent, unsubscribe, suppression-list, or regional privacy and email-marketing obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Anonymize or permission customer data, exclude confidential information, and review applicable privacy and email-marketing requirements before using the guidance in real campaigns. <br>
Risk: Draft social or thought-leadership content can include unsupported claims, advertising compliance issues, copyright concerns, or regulated-industry legal risk if published without review. <br>
Mitigation: Fact-check claims and statistics, confirm rights to reused material, and obtain legal or compliance review for regulated industries before publishing. <br>
