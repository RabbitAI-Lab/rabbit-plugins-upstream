## Description:

Summarizes SuperBooks financial reports, including profit and loss, spending, cash runway, account balances, and top customers, without changing records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams and business owners use their connected SuperBooks reports to review monthly or quarterly performance, spending, balances, and runway.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SuperBooks grants access to financial report data.

Mitigation: Review the SuperBooks OAuth scopes before connecting.

Risk: Bookkeeping summaries may be mistaken for tax or legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat summaries as bookkeeping information, not tax or legal advice.
