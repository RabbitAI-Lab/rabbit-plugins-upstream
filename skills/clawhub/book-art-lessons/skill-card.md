## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for art lessons through Lokuli, check provider availability, and create a booking after confirming the provider, time slot, and contact details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A booking could be created with the wrong provider, time slot, or customer contact details. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, time slot, customer name, email, and phone number before submitting a booking. <br>
