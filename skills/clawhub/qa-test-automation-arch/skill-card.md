## Description:

Guides the design and refactoring of maintainable test automation architectures, including framework selection, test layers, data management, and CI integration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers and developers use this skill to design or refactor test automation frameworks, selecting appropriate test layers, patterns, tools, and maintenance and CI strategies before writing tests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Examples or prompts may contain customer, payment, identity, or production data.

Mitigation: Use masked or synthetic test data and avoid pasting real sensitive information into prompts.

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may be difficult for teams that need another language.

Mitigation: Request the preferred response language and review the design with the team before implementation.
