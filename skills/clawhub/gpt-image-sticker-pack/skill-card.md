## Description:

Guides users through planning, generating, checking, and exporting animated sticker packs from a photo or character for supported messaging platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to turn an approved photo or character into a consistent animated sticker pack, then prepare files for supported messaging platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal photos are sent for external processing, and retention and deletion practices were not verified.

Mitigation: Use only photos you have permission to process; avoid sensitive images unless the provider's handling terms are acceptable.

Risk: Exports with matching names can overwrite files in the destination folder.

Mitigation: Choose a dedicated output folder and keep a copy of any files that must be preserved.

Risk: Sticker generation consumes paid credits.

Mitigation: Review the expected credit cost before generating a full pack.

Risk: Exported Discord GIF duration should be checked independently before upload.

Mitigation: Inspect the exported GIF duration and confirm it meets the current Discord requirements before uploading.
