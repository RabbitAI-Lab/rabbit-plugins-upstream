## Description:

Check a local business website against its Google Business Profile (name, address, phone, LocalBusiness schema, click-to-call, map) with the free, open-source Local SEO CLI and MCP server by WorkPods. Use when asked to audit a location page, check NAP consistency, or verify local schema before or after a deploy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, local business owners, and marketing teams use the skill to compare a location page with its Google Business Profile, identify name, address, and phone mismatches, and check local business schema, click-to-call links, and map embeds.

Global
