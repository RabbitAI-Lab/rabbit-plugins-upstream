# rule-table.md (Southeast Asia VAT/GST)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). SEA rules change fast — items marked **[verify]** are recent or periodically renewed; confirm with the local tax authority before relying on them.

## Singapore (GST)
- Rate: 9% (since 2024-01-01).
- Overseas Vendor Registration (OVR) for low-value goods (LVG ≤ S$400, air/post): register if global turnover > S$1M AND Singapore B2C LVG sales > S$100k/year. Charge GST at point of sale.
- Electronic marketplaces are deemed suppliers for LVG sold through them.
- Goods > S$400: import GST at the border.
- GST-inclusive price display required for consumer sales.

## Thailand (VAT)
- Rate: 7% (statutory 10%, reduced by decree; periodically renewed) **[verify current period]**.
- Low-value imports ≤ ฿1,500: import VAT collected at customs since 2024-07-05 (previously exempt) **[verify current status]**.
- Foreign e-service VAT regime (since 2021-09, ≥ ฿1.8M TH revenue): digital services only — NOT physical goods.
- No goods-marketplace deemed-supplier regime: platforms do not collect VAT for goods sellers.

## Indonesia (VAT/PPN)
- Rate: 11% general (since 2022-04). 12% from 2025-01 applies to luxury goods only; non-luxury effectively remains 11% via base adjustment (PMK 131/2024) **[verify]**.
- De minimis: FOB ≤ US$3 exempt from duty and import taxes (PMK 199/2019).
- Above threshold: import duty + 11% import VAT + Art. 22 income tax withholding (rate depends on import license/API status).

## Philippines (VAT)
- Rate: 12%.
- De minimis: ≤ ₱10,000 — exempt from duty and import VAT.
- 12% VAT on digital services by non-resident providers (RA 12023, signed 2024-10, effective 2025): digital services only — NOT physical goods **[verify implementation]**.
- E-marketplaces withhold 1% creditable income tax on seller remittances (RR 16-2023) — income tax, not VAT.

## Vietnam (VAT)
- Standard: 10%. Reduced 8% for eligible goods/services under periodic resolutions (extended through 2026 in recent rounds) **[verify current decree & eligibility list]**.
- De minimis VAT/duty exemption (≤ 1M VND) abolished 2025-02 **[verify]**.
- E-commerce platforms required to withhold VAT/PIT for sellers from 2025 **[verify scope & effective date]**.
- Local warehouse stock → domestic sales; local registration required.

## Malaysia (SST)
- Sales tax on goods: 5% or 10% by category; service tax 8% (since 2024-03, some services 6%).
- LVG regime (since 2024-01-01): low-value goods ≤ RM500 imported by air — 10% sales tax charged at point of sale by registered sellers/platforms (SMK registration). Register when MY LVG sales > RM500,000 in 12 months **[verify threshold]**.
- Goods > RM500 or non-air: duties/taxes at the border.
