# deadlines.md (Southeast Asia VAT/GST)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; actual deadlines depend on registration type and country — verify with the local tax authority or a local tax agent.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines (for registered sellers)
- Singapore: GST return quarterly, due 1 month after the accounting period end.
- Thailand: monthly VAT return, due the 15th of the following month (23rd for e-filing).
- Indonesia: monthly VAT return, due by the end of the following month (e-Faktur).
- Philippines: monthly VAT declaration due the 20th; quarterly VAT return due the 25th after quarter end.
- Vietnam: monthly (due the 20th of the following month) or quarterly (due by the end of the first month of the next quarter), assigned by turnover.
- Malaysia (LVG registrants): quarterly sales tax return, due by the end of the month following the quarter **[verify]**.

## Usage Notes
- These deadlines apply only where the seller is actually registered. Unregistered cross-border sellers: the pre-check finding is the registration gap itself, not a filing date.
- Customs-side collections (TH / ID / PH / MY border cases) are per-shipment, not periodic filings.
- If the user's registration status or assigned frequency is unknown, mark "cannot determine."
