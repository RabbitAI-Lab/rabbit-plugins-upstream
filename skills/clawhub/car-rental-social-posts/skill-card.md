## Description:

Creates Instagram and Facebook posts, Reels and TikTok scripts, and weekly content calendars for exotic and luxury car rental businesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External car-rental operators use this skill to draft social content tailored to their fleet, location, events, and booking goals. It creates platform-specific copy and content calendars while preserving real pricing, booking terms, and customer-consent requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts could contain inaccurate prices or booking terms.

Mitigation: Use the operator's actual prices and terms, and verify them before publication; cite and date any market comparison.

Risk: Customer faces, license plates, names, or review quotes could be shared without permission.

Mitigation: Obtain consent before using customer-identifying content or review quotes, and omit personal details without permission.

Risk: A separate social publishing bundle could receive access to an operator's account.

Mitigation: Assess any separate publishing bundle and its requested permissions before granting account access.

Risk: Supporting reference documents are not included in this release evidence package.

Mitigation: Request and review the referenced guidance before relying on it for content checks.
