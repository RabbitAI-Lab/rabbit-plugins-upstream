## Description:

Turn a learner's supplied sentence and existing pronunciation feedback into a short, repeatable practice plan and comparison log. Use for planning practice after a check, not for scoring audio or certifying language ability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cnvxstudio](https://clawhub.ai/user/cnvxstudio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English learners who already have pronunciation feedback can use this skill to plan one focused practice loop for a supplied sentence and keep a simple comparison log. It helps organize practice without assessing speech or determining whether pronunciation improved.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A learner could mistake a practice plan for an audio assessment or proof of pronunciation improvement.

Mitigation: Clarify that the plan uses feedback the learner supplies and cannot hear or score pronunciation or certify improvement.
