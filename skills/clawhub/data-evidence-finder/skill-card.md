## Description:

Find citable statistics and research evidence for market claims, check their scope and conflicting findings, and deliver qualified citations and an evidence table.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, researchers, and proposal authors use this skill to verify market figures and claims against attributable sources, compare conflicting statistics, and prepare cited evidence tables and notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup can install and launch software and change client configuration.

Mitigation: Review the release, install paths, client configuration, localhost endpoint, and write scope before approving the disclosed setup plan.

Risk: Selected research records are retained in JishuDB.

Mitigation: Store only material you are authorized to retain, approve its destination and write scope, and do not share tokens in chat.
