## Description:

Find citable statistics and research evidence for market claims, checking measurement scope and conflicting findings before delivering evidence tables and qualified citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and proposal authors use this skill to verify market statistics and claims, document contrary evidence, and prepare citable tables and source-qualified text. It requires an authorized JishuDB destination and permitted research access.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB is required, and its setup may install software or change client configuration.

Mitigation: Review the exact release, install paths, client configuration, MCP URL, and write scope in the approval envelope before authorizing setup.

Risk: Uninspected reports or image-only charts may lead to unsupported numerical citations.

Mitigation: Cite only inspected passages and verified chart values; label source coverage, uncertainties, and conflicting findings.
