## Description:

Viral Note Imitation Writer helps agents adapt a reference viral social marketing note into original branded content that follows the reference structure, style, and tone while incorporating product details and selling points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content marketers, social media operators, and agents use this skill to turn a reference viral note and product details into a differentiated marketing-style note for brand adaptation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends reference text, product details, selling points, and user requests to a third-party rewriting service with limited privacy disclosure.

Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, personal, regulated, proprietary, or otherwise sensitive material unless the service terms and data handling are approved.

Risk: The skill promotes duplicate-detection avoidance and close imitation of viral content.

Mitigation: Use it for lawful brand adaptation only, review outputs for originality and platform compliance, and avoid reposting close copies of others' content.
