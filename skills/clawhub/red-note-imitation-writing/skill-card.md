## Description: <br>
AI-powered Xiaohongshu (RED) note imitation writing that analyzes viral post structure, style, and traffic logic, then generates differentiated draft content from a reference note, product information, and selling points. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to draft Xiaohongshu-style note content from a viral reference post, requested tone or length, product details, and selling points. The generated output should be reviewed for originality, legality, and platform compliance before publication. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks users to imitate viral Xiaohongshu posts and references avoiding plagiarism or duplicate-detection systems. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only to create lawful, original marketing drafts; do not use it to launder copied posts, evade platform enforcement, or violate Xiaohongshu rules. <br>
Risk: The skill sends the reference text, request, product information, and selling points to wsdsocial.com. <br>
Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, regulated, or third-party data unless the user is comfortable with that external service receiving the inputs. <br>
Risk: Generated marketing copy may be inaccurate, misleading, or too close to the reference post. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit generated content for factual accuracy, originality, brand fit, and platform compliance before publication. <br>
