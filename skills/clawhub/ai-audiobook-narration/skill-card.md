## Description:

Turn final manuscript or course text into an audiobook with one consistent narrator, from chapter planning and pronunciation decisions through a representative sample, narration, and delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External authors, course creators, and audiobook producers use this skill to plan, price, generate, review, and deliver chapterized narration from finalized text, including pilot samples and focused passage refinements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared credential can authorize spending for video and music as well as audiobook narration.

Mitigation: Review each production estimate and the requested scope before authorizing paid work; revoke the device in Beatra Console when access is no longer needed.

Risk: Manuscripts and voice samples are processed by external providers, and task records may be retained after account closure.

Mitigation: Submit only material whose external processing and retention you accept, and review Beatra's privacy policy before use.

Risk: Paid-call approval depends on the agent following instructions.

Mitigation: Review and explicitly approve each production estimate before billable generation.
