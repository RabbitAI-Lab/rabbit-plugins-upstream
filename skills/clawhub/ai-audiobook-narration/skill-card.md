## Description:

Turn final manuscript or course text into ordered chapter audio with one consistent narrator, using pronunciation planning, long-form pacing, and a representative sample before the remaining chapters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors, course creators, and audiobook producers use this skill to turn finalized manuscript or course text into ordered chapter audio with a consistent narrator. It supports pronunciation and pacing preparation, a representative pilot, focused corrections, and delivery of the completed audio segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared Beatra connection grants generation and spending access beyond audiobook narration, including video and music generation; there is no narration-only authorization option.

Mitigation: Before connecting, review and accept the full permission scope. Revoke access through the Beatra Console when finished, and remember that removing this skill may leave the shared connection active for other installed Beatra skills.
