## Description: <br>
Engagement Routine helps social media managers and brand teams design sustainable, time-boxed daily and weekly engagement cadences for comments, DMs, triage, goal tracking, and burnout prevention. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External social media managers, creators, and brand teams use this skill to convert brand and social strategy context into a practical engagement routine. It focuses on sustainable engagement windows, golden-hour replies, triage priorities, native-platform signals, and clear human-review boundaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use brand profile or social strategy context to shape advice and draft engagement responses. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the context needed for the engagement task and review any suggested replies before using them. <br>
Risk: Users may try to stretch the workflow into inbox scraping, automatic posting, analytics claims, or mass engagement. <br>
Mitigation: Keep use advisory: review and post manually in native platforms, use native platform signals, and refuse automated or fake engagement. <br>
