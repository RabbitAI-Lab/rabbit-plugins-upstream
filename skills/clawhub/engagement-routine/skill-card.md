## Description: <br>
Helps an agent design a sustainable social-media engagement routine with time-boxed daily and weekly windows, golden-hour reply passes, triage priorities, native measurement signals, and anti-burnout boundaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media operators, creators, and brand teams use this skill to turn engagement goals into a practical cadence for comments, DMs, proactive comments, escalation, and recovery from burnout. The agent provides planning guidance and draft-oriented workflow advice while the human reviews and performs engagement in the native social platforms. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the skill for an inbox, DM, analytics, or auto-reply automation workflow. <br>
Mitigation: State that WoopSocial has no comment, DM, inbox, reply-posting, or analytics surface; route execution to the native platform and use the agent only for planning and drafting. <br>
Risk: Users may request mass auto-comments, fake engagement, bought interactions, or other inauthentic growth tactics. <br>
Mitigation: Refuse fake or automated engagement and offer a smaller genuine routine with human review and posting. <br>
Risk: An always-on engagement plan can increase stress and become unsustainable. <br>
Mitigation: Use time-boxed windows, response boundaries, notification discipline, capacity scaling, and proactive triage. <br>
