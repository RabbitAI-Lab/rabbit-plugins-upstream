## Description: <br>
Draft a construction delay notice or delay claim letter with contract clause citation, cause classification, and critical-path impact narrative. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External construction, project management, and contracts teams use this skill to draft factual delay notices or claim letters that cite contract notice clauses, classify the delay cause, describe critical-path impact, reserve rights, and list records to preserve. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Draft notices may affect contractual or legal rights if notice clauses, deadlines, recipients, or facts are wrong. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the contract notice clause, deadline calculation, required recipient, and factual schedule impact before sending. <br>
Risk: The skill can produce legal-adjacent correspondence that may be mistaken for final legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Route the draft through contracts counsel before sending, as required by the security guidance and the skill's own output instructions. <br>
Risk: Premature or unsupported quantum, cause classification, or critical-path claims can weaken a delay claim. <br>
Mitigation: Use placeholders for unconfirmed amounts, classify the cause conservatively, reserve rights to supplement, and tie impact to critical-path activities and records. <br>
