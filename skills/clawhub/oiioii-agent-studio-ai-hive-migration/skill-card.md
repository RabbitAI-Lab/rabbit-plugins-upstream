## Description:

Chinese-language advisory skill for evaluating an OiiOii-to-AI-HIVE MCP migration by rebuilding production roles, testing a like-for-like sample, and documenting responsibilities, costs, quality checks, approvals, and rollback boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, developers, and migration evaluators use this skill to assess whether an OiiOii animation or short-video workflow can be partially rebuilt with host agents plus AI-HIVE MCP. It guides same-input pilot testing, handoff records, approval gates, quality metrics, and rollback decisions before any broader migration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The migration comparison could become misleading if platform capabilities, pricing, or availability change after the documented evidence date.

Mitigation: Recheck OiiOii and AI-HIVE sources on the day of evaluation and avoid claims of full replacement, superiority, or lowest cost without same-day like-for-like testing.

Risk: AI-HIVE MCP actions can involve uploads, paid generation, publishing, or data writes.

Mitigation: Review tool schemas and require human approval before uploading assets, spending budget, publishing results, or writing data.

Risk: Using unauthorized people, brands, music, IP, images, or reference videos could create rights and policy issues.

Mitigation: Use only owned or authorized assets, preserve source IDs and hashes, and stop the workflow when authorization is missing.
