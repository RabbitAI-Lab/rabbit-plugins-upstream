## Description:

Evaluates human-inspired AI architecture designs against human-brain research and contemporary AI approaches, identifying gaps and improvement priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers and AI architecture reviewers use this framework to compare proposed agent designs with human-brain research and AI architectures, identify limitations, and prioritize improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
