## English

### When to use
- Evaluate any "human-like AI brain design" (methodology / multi-agent organization / in-house architecture).
- Benchmark the design as a single unified agent against neuroscience of the real brain plus 2026 leading AI architectures.

### Usage
- A framework-spec skill; no scripts/.
- Run the three-way benchmark per `references/维度与对标.md`; avoid absolute terms ("best / world-first"); triage gaps into three tiers and give an improvement roadmap.

### Flow
1. Treat the design under test as one unified agent; decompose its capability surface.
2. Benchmark against the real brain + most intelligent-brain neuroscience.
3. Benchmark against 2026 leading AI architectures; emit honest conclusions, three-tier gap triage and a roadmap.
