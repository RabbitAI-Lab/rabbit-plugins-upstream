## Description:

Speech Analyzer evaluates oral expression quality across 20 dimensions for livestream, podcast, and speech audio, producing structured analysis reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, speakers, podcast teams, and reviewers use this skill to analyze recorded oral expression quality, identify speech habits and delivery issues, and get actionable improvement suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recordings may contain sensitive personal data, including voices, private conversations, or multi-speaker content.

Mitigation: Use only recordings the user is authorized to process, apply consent expectations, and minimize or redact sensitive content before analysis.

Risk: Generated scoring or recommendations may be misleading if the analysis is not grounded in actual audio data and transparent formulas.

Mitigation: Require analysis to be based on the supplied audio, keep scoring formulas reviewable, and treat recommendations as decision support rather than final assessment.
