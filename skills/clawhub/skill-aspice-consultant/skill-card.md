## Description: <br>
Assists automotive software quality and process improvement engineers with ASPICE assessment preparation, evidence-based gap analysis, improvement planning, and archive-ready text and Markdown reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Automotive software quality, engineering, and process improvement teams use this skill to prepare for ASPICE self-assessments, customer audit readiness, third-party assessment preparation, process-area gap analysis, and evidence-backed improvement planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share ASPICE evidence and internal process details with the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended deployment environment is approved for the organization's ASPICE evidence and internal process information before use. <br>
Risk: The inspected artifact references helper files that were not present in the inspected package. <br>
Mitigation: Verify required reference and report-generation files are available before relying on reference-backed guidance or generated reports. <br>
Risk: Assessment preparation output may be mistaken for an official ASPICE assessment conclusion. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as preparation and planning material, and validate final conclusions against the organization's official PAM and qualified assessor process. <br>
