## Description:

Helps plan quick, low-cost costumes from clothes and household items, with wearer and time filters, group ideas, safety reminders, and a shopping list.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Families, caregivers, and partygoers use this skill to assemble last-minute costumes from available items, check practical safety considerations, and plan any small purchases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Entered costume inventory is saved locally in the skill directory.

Mitigation: Enter only items you are comfortable storing there, and remove the saved inventory when no longer needed.

Risk: Some advertised filters and group options may be incomplete or unreliable.

Mitigation: Review suggested recipes, group assignments, and shopping needs before relying on them.
