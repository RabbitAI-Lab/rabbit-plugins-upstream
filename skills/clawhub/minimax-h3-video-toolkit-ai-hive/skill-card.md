## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-HIVE generation commands can create billable API usage.

Mitigation: Show final prompts, mode, routing, and price snapshot for user confirmation before submitting generation tasks; use small samples before batch work.

Risk: API keys may be stored locally or accidentally exposed in files, logs, screenshots, or version control.

Mitigation: Use placeholders in examples, prefer protected environment variables or locked-down local config, and avoid echoing or committing secrets.

Risk: Reference images or videos may be unauthorized, copyrighted, or too closely copied.

Mitigation: Confirm rights to source media before upload; when rights cannot be confirmed, provide only abstract structure guidance and new creative directions.

Risk: Marketing outputs may contain unsupported product, performance, platform, or business claims.

Mitigation: Require source-backed facts and avoid guarantees about sales, rankings, review approval, traffic, investment return, prices, stock, or efficacy.
