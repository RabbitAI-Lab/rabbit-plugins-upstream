## Description:

Helps create promotional posters and discount graphics for product launches, seasonal campaigns, and brand marketing with Qinghu AI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and merchants use this skill to generate promotional posters from campaign copy, with or without a product image, and review the result before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing qhkit or Node may change the local environment or require a global package install.

Mitigation: Confirm installation with the user, prefer existing Node/npm, and review global installs or PATH changes before proceeding.

Risk: Product images and prompts sent for generation may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use the Qinghu service only when appropriate for the content, and avoid submitting sensitive product imagery or prompts.

Risk: Generated poster text, prices, logos, and product details may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Check campaign copy, discount figures, branding, and product fidelity before publishing; regenerate or correct inaccurate results.
