## Description:

Creates age-specific parenting content drafts for Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Kuaishou, and Bilibili, including platform copy and image-generation prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Parents and parenting-content creators use this skill to plan sourced, age-specific posts for children ages 0–12 and draft platform-specific copy and image-generation prompts. Its content is for education and creation, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Risk: Parenting health claims may be inaccurate or mistaken for medical advice.

Mitigation: Independently verify health claims before publishing, and direct readers to a licensed pediatrician for illness, medication, nutrition, or developmental concerns.

Risk: Generated files may expose private material if saved or shared without checking the output location and exclusions.

Mitigation: Confirm the save location, verify output exclusions before sharing the skill folder, and check the hosting agent’s privacy settings.
