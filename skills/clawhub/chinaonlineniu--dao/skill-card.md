## Description: <br>
A Daoist strategy-advice skill that applies the Dao De Jing and related Daoist classics to business competition, organizational leadership, and major personal decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chinaonlineniu](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinaonlineniu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and developers can use this skill to frame business strategy, management, leadership, and personal decision questions through a Daoist strategic lens. It is intended to provide reflective guidance, options, cautions, and uncertainty notes rather than binding professional advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can activate on broad business and life-advice requests where users may over-rely on philosophical guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as reflective strategy framing, include uncertainty, and redirect legal, financial, medical, crisis, or quantitative decisions to qualified professional or domain-specific analysis. <br>
Risk: The release declares local write, edit, shell, and web capabilities that are not needed for ordinary advisory responses. <br>
Mitigation: Run the skill with restricted tool access for normal use, especially disabling local write/edit/shell permissions unless a separate task explicitly requires them. <br>
Risk: Reciprocity and concession frameworks could be used to create hidden obligations or manipulate counterparties. <br>
Mitigation: Require advice involving influence, concessions, or value exchange to be transparent, consensual, and reviewed for ethical and compliance fit. <br>
