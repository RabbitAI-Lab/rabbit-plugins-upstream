## Description:

Assess a defined product or technology field for project initiation by combining current market evidence, a reproducible global patent landscape, individually validated key players, technical themes and gap hypotheses, jurisdictional filing footprint, competitive tiers, risks, and differentiated project options in a self-contained scientific HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and R&D strategy teams use this skill to assess a technology or product field before project initiation. It guides market research, patent landscape analysis, player validation, technical gap hypotheses, risk review, and differentiated project option generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The technology scope or company strategy supplied by the user may be confidential when PatSnap MCP services or web research are used.

Mitigation: Review the scope before use and avoid submitting sensitive undisclosed details unless the organization has approved those services for the task.

Risk: The generated report may be mistaken for legal, freedom-to-operate, investment, or market-forecast advice.

Mitigation: Use the report as diligence support only and route legal, FTO, investment, and forecast decisions to qualified reviewers.

Risk: Incomplete or unavailable patent, market, company, policy, or standards evidence can make conclusions misleading.

Mitigation: Preserve unavailable states, source IDs, method notes, and limitations in the report, and require follow-up validation before acting on material conclusions.
