## Description:

Assesses a defined product or technology field for project initiation by combining current market evidence, reproducible patent landscape analysis, validated key players, technical themes, jurisdictional filing footprint, competitive tiers, risks, and project options in a self-contained scientific HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and R&D or product teams use this skill to turn a scoped technology field into a cited project-initiation landscape covering market evidence, patent populations, competitors, technical themes, risks, and project options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may be mistaken for legal, investment, FTO, or market-forecast advice.

Mitigation: Treat the report as a diligence aid and review cited sources, assumptions, and FTO or legal questions with qualified experts before relying on it.

Risk: Patent samples or incomplete aggregations can misstate market or patent landscape conclusions.

Mitigation: Use full-population aggregations or complete bucket plans for metrics, and mark unavailable metrics rather than inferring from representative records.

Risk: Current market, company, regulatory, and standards evidence can be stale, scoped differently, or incompatible across sources.

Mitigation: Use recent authoritative sources, keep source scopes and methods separate, and disclose incompatible definitions instead of averaging them.
