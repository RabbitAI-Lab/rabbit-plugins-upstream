## Description:

Helps banking credit-risk managers produce human-reviewed draft workflows for industry rule generation, large exposure management, credit policy analysis, risk collaboration, risk information extraction, post-loan monitoring, post-loan management, and VLM verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Bank credit-risk employees use this skill as a reference workflow for structuring credit-risk analysis, monitoring, exposure management, post-loan review, and multimodal verification. Outputs are drafts for authorized institutional processes and require qualified human review before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive banking, personal, account, credit-bureau, medical, or transaction data may be exposed if entered without controls.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in authorized institutional environments and minimize or desensitize sensitive data before input.

Risk: Draft reports or suggested actions may be mistaken for final credit, legal, financial, or operational decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference drafts and require review by qualified personnel before applying, recording, sending, or acting on them.

Risk: Operational actions such as saving, filing, external sending, risk classification changes, or provisioning could be performed outside approved systems.

Mitigation: Perform all such actions only through existing institutional systems by authorized users with required approvals and audit trails.
