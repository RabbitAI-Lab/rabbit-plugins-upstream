## Description:

Provides Chinese-language reference workflows and draft analyses for banking credit-risk management, from industry rules and exposure review to post-loan monitoring and multimodal verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Bank credit-risk employees use this reference to draft risk analyses, exposure reviews, policy assessments, post-loan monitoring reports, and verification checklists for qualified human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer, account, identity, health, or transaction data may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Minimize and desensitize inputs before use; handle retained data only in authorized institutional systems.

Risk: Draft risk assessments may be mistaken for approved credit decisions or operational instructions.

Mitigation: Require qualified human review and institutional approval before applying conclusions or taking action.
