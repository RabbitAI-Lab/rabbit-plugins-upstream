## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad auto-activation for programming-help requests may apply the skill in contexts beyond beginner competitive-programming tutoring. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when broad programming-help activation is desired, and confirm the task is an algorithm-practice or C/C++ learning request before relying on its output. <br>
Risk: The Feishu export/archive behavior is under-specified and may send generated notes to an unintended workspace or team document. <br>
Mitigation: Before using Feishu export, confirm the exact content, destination workspace or document, and whether a preview or cancellation step is available. <br>
