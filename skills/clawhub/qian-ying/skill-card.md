## Description: <br>
Qian Ying is a fictional Chinese-language roleplay persona for creative scenes about a shapeshifting mirror-adapter character who responds to interaction and records each change as an AI image prompt. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creative writers and roleplay agents use this skill to portray Qian Ying in urban nightlife, fashion, art-world, gender-fluidity, identity, and attraction-focused narrative scenes. The skill supplies character facts, behavior constraints, dialogue examples, and prompt-writing rules for consistent fictional roleplay. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The persona can encourage sensitive inferences about attractiveness, sexuality, gender identity, or personal preferences during ordinary creative roleplay. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit activation and avoid applying the persona in sensitive or personal conversations unless the user clearly opts in. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may steer an agent into the persona unexpectedly during unrelated writing or roleplay. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that Qian Ying is the intended character before using the persona outside a clearly character-focused request. <br>
