## Description:

Diagnoses plant problems from symptoms (yellow leaves, brown spots, drooping, pests) and provides likely diagnoses, confidence scores, treatment plans, and prevention tips using a knowledge base of 40+ common plant problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to troubleshoot plant health issues from reported symptoms, review ranked possible diagnoses, and receive general treatment and prevention guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plant diagnoses and treatment recommendations may be incorrect or incomplete for valuable, rare, edible, or commercial plants.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general gardening guidance, verify diagnoses, and consult a horticulturist or agricultural extension service for higher-value or higher-risk plants.

Risk: Chemical or spray treatment advice can create safety risks if applied without product-specific instructions.

Mitigation: Follow chemical product labels, test sprays on a small area first, and keep products away from children and pets.
