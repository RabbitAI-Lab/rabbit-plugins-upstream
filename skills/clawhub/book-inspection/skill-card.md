## Description: <br>
Book Inspection helps an agent find inspection services through Lokuli and book an appointment using the Lokuli MCP server. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[edwardrodriguez703-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/edwardrodriguez703-design) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Users and service-booking agents use this skill to search for inspection providers, check available times, and create inspection bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can create bookings and send contact details to Lokuli or a selected provider. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, time slot, customer name, email, phone number, and data-sharing expectations before allowing a booking. <br>
