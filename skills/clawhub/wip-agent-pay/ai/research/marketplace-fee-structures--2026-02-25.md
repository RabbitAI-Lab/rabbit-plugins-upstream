## Detailed Breakdown

### Uber Eats

**Restaurant fees (tiered plans):**
- Lite: 15% commission on delivery
- Plus: 25% (better visibility, Uber One perks)
- Premium: 30% (max exposure, marketing support)
- Pickup: 6-7% (all plans)

**Buyer-side fees:**
- Service fee: 10-15% of subtotal (up to 25% in some markets)
- Delivery fee: variable (distance, demand, courier availability)
- Small order fee: below certain subtotal threshold
- Local operating fee: offsets regulatory costs in certain markets

**Double-sided.** Both restaurant and buyer pay. Total platform take is enormous.

### DoorDash

**Restaurant commission plans:**
- Basic: 15% (lowest visibility)
- Plus: 25% (DashPass eligibility)
- Premier: 30% (highest exposure, lower customer delivery fees)
- Pickup: 6% across all plans (includes payment processing)

**Buyer-side fees:**
- Service fee: 5-15% of order total
- Delivery fee: variable
- Small order fee: under ~$10-15
- Heavy order fee: items over 40 lbs

### Etsy

**Mandatory seller fees:**
- Listing fee: $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or upon sale)
- Transaction fee: 6.5% of item price + shipping + gift wrapping
- Payment processing (US): 3% + $0.25 per transaction

**Conditional fees:**
- Offsite Ads: 15% if buyer came via offsite ad. Drops to 12% if shop earned $10K+ trailing 12 months. Shops over $10K cannot opt out.
- Currency conversion: 2.5%

**Total effective rate:** ~10-11% typical US order without Offsite Ads. Up to 25%+ with.

### Shopify

**Monthly plans:**
- Basic: $39/mo ($29/mo annual)
- Grow: $105/mo ($79/mo annual)
- Advanced: $399/mo ($299/mo annual)
- Plus: $2,300/mo (3-year) or $2,500/mo (1-year). 0.25% of GMV once monthly revenue exceeds $800K.

**Shopify Payments (online):**
- Basic: 2.9% + $0.30
- Grow: 2.6% + $0.30
- Advanced: 2.4% + $0.30
- Plus: 2.15% + $0.30

**Third-party payment provider surcharge (on top of provider's fees):**
- Basic: 2.0%
- Grow: 1.0%
- Advanced: 0.6%
- Plus: 0.15-0.20%

### Amazon Marketplace

**Account fees:**
- Individual: $0.99 per item sold
- Professional: $39.99/month

**Referral fees by category:**
- Most categories: 15% (min $0.30)
- Electronics: 8%
- Clothing: tiered. 5% ($15 or less), 10% ($15-$20), 17% (over $20)
- Books: 15%
- Grocery: 8% ($15 or less), 15% (over $15)
- Personal Computers: 6%
- Jewelry: 20% ($250 or less), 5% (portion over $250)
- Amazon Device Accessories: 45%

**Media closing fee:** $1.80 per item (books, DVDs, video games)

### Stripe Connect

**Standard processing:** 2.9% + $0.30 (online), 2.6% + $0.10 (in-person)

**Connect-specific fees (Express/Custom accounts):**
- $2 per active connected account per month
- 0.25% + $0.25 per payout to connected accounts

**What platforms typically charge on top:** 5-20% platform fee. Stripe doesn't dictate this.

### Apple App Store / Google Play

**Apple:**
- Standard: 30%
- Small Business Program: 15% (under $1M/year across all apps)
- Auto-renewing subs (year 2+): 15% for devs NOT in Small Business Program

**Google Play:**
- Standard: 30%
- First $1M/year: 15% (all developers, resets annually)
- Auto-renewing subs: 15% from day one (all developers)

### Gumroad

- Platform fee: 10% flat on every sale
- Discover (marketplace) fee: 30% if buyer found via Gumroad Discover
- Payment processing on top: 2.9% + $0.30
- Effective: 13-15% direct, 33%+ via Discover

### Patreon

**New creators (after Aug 2025):**
- Standard: 10% of all earnings
- Processing: 2.9% + $0.30 on top
- Effective: ~13-14%

**Legacy creators:**
- Pro: 8% (grandfathered)
- Premium: ~11%

### Square

**Processing:**
- In-person: 2.4-2.6% + $0.15
- Online: 2.9-3.3% + $0.30
- Keyed: 3.5% + $0.15

No chargeback fees ($15-25 at most processors).

### PayPal

**Standard domestic:** 2.99% + $0.49
**Volume tiers:**
- Under $50K/mo: 2.49% + $0.10
- $50K-$200K/mo: 1.99% + $0.10
- Over $200K/mo: 1.59% + $0.10

**Micropayments (under $10):** 5% + $0.05
- $0.50 transaction: ~15% effective
- $0.10 transaction: ~55% effective

### Substack

- Platform: 10% of paid subscriber revenue
- Processing: 2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe)
- Billing fee: 0.7% for recurring
- Effective: ~13-16%

## Industry Categories

### Payment Facilitators (PayFacs)
- Typical: 2-3% + fixed fee
- Slim margin (~0.5%), high volume
- Examples: Stripe, Square, PayPal

### Marketplaces
- Typical: 10-30%
- Includes discovery, trust, fulfillment value
- Food delivery: 15-30%
- Creator platforms: 10-15%
- E-commerce: 10-17%
- App stores: 15-30%

### Key Principle
PayFacs charge for processing only (2-3%). Marketplaces charge for the entire value chain. The deeper the marketplace goes (matching, fulfillment, support, marketing), the higher the justified take rate.

## Micropayment Economics

**The problem:** Fixed per-transaction costs ($0.25-$0.49) make sub-$1 transactions uneconomical on traditional rails. $0.30 fee on $0.50 transaction = 60% effective rate.

**Solutions:**
- **Aggregation/batching:** Patreon batches monthly. One processing fee.
- **Pre-funded wallets:** Load $5-20 upfront, spend in micro-increments. No per-tx processing.
- **Account-to-account:** Brazil's Pix ... 6 billion tx/month, many for a single cent. Bypasses card networks.
- **x402 Protocol:** ~$0.0001/tx on Base L2, ~$0.00025 on Solana. A $0.01 charge nets $0.0099.

## What This Means for AI CASH

**Where we sit:**
- AI CASH buyer fee: $0.25 + card processing (~3%)
- Proposed seller fee: 2-5% tiered

**Our total effective take (both sides combined):**
- On a $5 article: $0.25 buyer + $0.25 seller (5%) = $0.50 ... 10% effective
- On a $50 product: $0.25 buyer + $1.75 seller (3.5%) = $2.00 ... 4% effective
- On a $250 product: $0.25 buyer + $5.00 seller (2%) = $5.25 ... 2.1% effective

**Competitive positioning:**
- Cheaper than every marketplace (Etsy 10%, Amazon 15%, Uber 15-30%)
- Cheaper than every creator platform (Gumroad 13%, Patreon 13%, Substack 13%)
- Comparable to pure payment processing (Stripe 2.9%, Square 2.6-3.3%)
- Way cheaper than app stores (15-30%)

**We're a conversion layer, not a marketplace.** Our value is: "your AI agent can now complete purchases." We don't do discovery, fulfillment, or support. The 2-5% is justified as an execution premium, not a marketplace tax.

**Double-sided precedent:** Uber Eats and DoorDash both charge both sides. 15-30% from restaurants + 10-25% from buyers. Our double-sided take is 2-10% total. Nobody should blink.
