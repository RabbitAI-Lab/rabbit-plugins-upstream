## Use Case: <br>
External roofing contractors, adjusters, inspectors, sales teams, and business operators use this skill for practical coaching on roofing measurements, proposals, insurance-facing narratives, workflow quality, KPIs, and fundamentals. It supports professional judgment and decision-making rather than licensed, legal, engineering, coverage, compliance, or pricing determinations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat roofing guidance as a substitute for licensed roofing, engineering, legal, insurance, building-code, permit, or local regulatory judgment. <br>
Mitigation: Position outputs as professional coaching and require verification by qualified professionals before acting on measurements, pricing, coverage, compliance, or regulatory matters. <br>
Risk: Insurance-facing narratives could be misused to imply cause, coverage, approval, or denial beyond observed facts. <br>
Mitigation: Keep insurance language factual, neutral, and limited to observed or measured conditions; avoid coverage conclusions and legal claims. <br>
Risk: Measurement, material, workflow, or KPI advice may be incomplete when project conditions, local requirements, or source data are missing. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions, flag uncertainty, and prompt users to re-check geometry, documentation, pricing, and job-specific constraints before making operational decisions. <br>
