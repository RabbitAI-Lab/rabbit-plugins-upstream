## Description:

End-to-end campaign workflow: create, design, target audience, preview, test, approve, and send or schedule an email or SMS campaign.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and campaign operators use this skill to create email or SMS campaigns, select an audience, review and test delivery, and send or schedule approved campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A campaign could be delivered with an unintended brand, sender, audience, exclusions, message, or schedule.

Mitigation: Use a trusted campaign-service connection and verify the brand, sender, recipient lists and exclusions, message preview, and schedule before approving delivery.
