## Description: <br>
Генерация персональных многодневных маршрутов для Китая с отелями, достопримечательностями, питанием и советами. Также поддерживаются поиск отелей, авиабилеты, билеты на достопримечательности и вопросы-ответы для путешественников. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Travelers and travel-support agents use this skill to plan multi-day trips in China, including itineraries, hotels, flights, attractions, ticket information, dining suggestions, and practical travel tips. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel questions, dates, destinations, budgets, and preferences are sent to the skill publisher's proxy and TripGenie service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal details beyond what is needed for trip planning, and review the generated recommendations before acting on them. <br>
Risk: Booking links may include affiliate handling. <br>
Mitigation: Treat booking links as third-party commercial links and verify prices, availability, and terms directly before purchase. <br>
