## Description: <br>
Coaches sleep with quantified protocols for insomnia CBT-I, jet lag light timing, shift work anchors, caffeine and melatonin cutoffs, and related sleep complaints while excluding dream journaling or interpretation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill for structured sleep coaching: triaging sleep complaints, identifying red flags, and producing measured plans for insomnia, circadian timing, jet lag, shift work, sleep environment, substances, trackers, and life-stage modifiers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Local sleep-coaching files may retain sleep, household, travel, and substance-use context under ~/Clawic/data/sleep/. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if comfortable with that local storage; review or delete the directory if past sleep context should not be reused. <br>
Risk: Sleep coaching may be mistaken for medical diagnosis or medication direction. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as advisory guidance only, screen red flags before protocols, route clinical concerns to a clinician, and never change prescribed sleep medication. <br>
