## Description:

Analyzes advertisers' public ads across Meta, Google, LinkedIn, and TikTok, with linked examples, messaging themes, landing-page comparisons, and cross-advertiser findings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and competitive researchers use this skill to review advertisers' public campaigns, compare messaging across platforms, and collect linked examples of ads and landing pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Glasser charges per call, so broad searches and landing-page fetches can incur unexpected costs.

Mitigation: Review provider pricing, limit advertisers, platforms, and pagination, and confirm before broad runs or landing-page fetches.

Risk: Name-based searches can attribute ads to an unrelated advertiser.

Mitigation: Confirm platform-specific identities against linked handles, destinations, and regions before reporting results.
