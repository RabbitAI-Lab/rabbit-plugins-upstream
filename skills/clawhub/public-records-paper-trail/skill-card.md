## Description:

Guides public-records research by identifying records holders, choosing the applicable request process, drafting a focused request, and building a dated, cited timeline from the documents returned.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zwmaronek](https://clawhub.ai/user/zwmaronek)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, journalists, and community members use this skill to locate public records, prepare appropriately scoped requests, and assemble document-backed timelines about places, companies, or government decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (federal, state, and local public-records processes)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Filing a public-records request may disclose requester information and incur fees.

Mitigation: Review the request, contact details, scope, and any fee waiver before submission.

Risk: A request addressed to the wrong records holder or under the wrong law may be delayed.

Mitigation: Confirm the responsible records office and applicable federal or state process before filing.
