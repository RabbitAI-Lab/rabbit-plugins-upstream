## Description:

Investigates paid ad references from public Meta and TikTok sources, breaks them down with the Formula 100K and GOAT frameworks, and produces winning angles, hooks, ad ideas, and optional scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, creative strategists, and paid acquisition operators use this skill to research competitor ads, identify reusable creative patterns, and draft ad concepts or scripts for campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow researches competitor paid ads and may encounter copyrighted or platform-hosted media.

Mitigation: Prefer ad URLs, screenshots, and metadata; do not download or store competitor MP4s unless there is a clear rights or compliance reason.

Risk: Large-batch ad collection may use paid external scrapers.

Mitigation: Confirm cost and scope before running scraper-based batch collection.
