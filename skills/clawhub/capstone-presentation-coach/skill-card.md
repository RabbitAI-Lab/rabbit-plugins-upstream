## Description:

Capstone defense presentation coach: reviewer-perspective 8-12 slides scaled to time, testing and demo emphasis, demo contingency guidance, and backup slides; outlines only.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students preparing an engineering capstone defense can use this skill to plan a time-scaled presentation outline, rehearsal points, and a live-demo contingency plan. It is intended to help organize material from the student's own report and test results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project details or demo video content may be exposed through AI processing or cloud backup.

Mitigation: Share only material intended for processing and use suitable privacy settings for cloud video storage.

Risk: Suggested slide content may conflict with course AI-use rules or the student's measured results.

Mitigation: Check the course policy and verify every claim and number against the student's report and tests.
