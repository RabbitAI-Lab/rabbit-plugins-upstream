## Description:

Capstone defense presentation coach: reviewer-perspective 8-12 slides scaled to time, testing+demo over 35 percent, demo anti-failure checklist, backup slides. Outline only. Use when a student prepares a capstone defense presentation, live demo contingency, or presentation time allocation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External engineering students use this skill to plan a capstone defense presentation, allocate time across slides, prepare demo contingencies, and anticipate examiner questions. It provides coaching and an outline while leaving the deck and project-specific data to the student.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested slide content or figures could be inaccurate or conflict with the student's report.

Mitigation: Check every claim and number against the report and real test results; use the student's own figures and data.

Risk: Sharing project details or using AI assistance may conflict with privacy expectations or course rules.

Mitigation: Share only project material appropriate for the agent and confirm the course's AI-use requirements.
