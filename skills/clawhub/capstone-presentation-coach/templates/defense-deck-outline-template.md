## Scaling

- 8 min: cut slide 2; background to 30s; slides 4–6 to 1min each; keep testing+demo at 3.5min
- 15 min: split slides 5–6 into two pages each; demo to 3min; conclusion to 1min

## Rules

- Testing + demo ≥ 35% of total time — always
- Every number on every slide must match the report (three-place consistency)
- Final 30 seconds of rehearsal must land on the closing line
