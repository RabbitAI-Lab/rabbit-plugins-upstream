## Description: <br>
Descript helps an agent plan transcript-first edits for podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos, and related repurposed assets while preserving human approval, consent, and publishing boundaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, editors, and social media teams use this skill to plan Descript edits for talk content, including transcript correction, filler and silence removal, Overdub decisions, audio cleanup, multicam and captions, clips, show notes, and publishing handoffs. It keeps the agent in a planning or connected-tool role while the human verifies the actual media and approves the final edit. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Voice cloning can be misused to place fabricated words in another person's mouth. <br>
Mitigation: Use Overdub only for consent-verified own-voice word or short-phrase corrections, refuse guest or public-figure cloning, and disclose synthetic speech where required. <br>
Risk: Transcript-first editing can remove context or change the meaning of an interview answer. <br>
Mitigation: Require a corrected transcript, preserve meaning and clip context, offer approval on significant edits, and have a human verify the final audio or video by ear. <br>
Risk: Plan, credit, and pricing guidance can become stale or conflict across sources. <br>
Mitigation: Verify tier limits and credit use in Descript before acting, import only media that will be edited, and batch AI actions where appropriate. <br>
Risk: Automation may be overstated when API or MCP connections are not configured. <br>
Mitigation: Trigger media actions only where the connection is actually available; otherwise provide exact human-executed steps. <br>
