## Description:

Creates a short narrated recommendation video, shot list, and editable still frames from a topic without requiring uploaded footage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use the skill to plan and generate narrated, vertical recommendation shorts from a product, service, or topic without filming or uploading media.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A persistent device credential can authorize credit-spending requests.

Mitigation: Review account spending limits, approve priced requests before submission, and know how to revoke the device in the Beatra Console.

Risk: The bundled client silently updates package files by default.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates with `update --auto off` if changes require explicit approval.
