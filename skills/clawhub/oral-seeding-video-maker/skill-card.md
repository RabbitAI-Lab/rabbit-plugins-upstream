## Description:

Make a spoken recommendation video from nothing but a topic. This talking-style seeding video maker and short video script generator picks the script pattern that fits your product or subject, writes the hook, the body beats, and the closing ask with the on-screen action and the spoken line written separately, then produces ready-to-edit still beat frames, a narration track in a voice you choose, an optional music bed, and one vertical clip animated from the opening frame with the full narration. Use it for product seeding posts, creator recommendation videos, review-style shorts, service explainers, and account-building content for Douyin, WeChat Channels, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Reels, and Shorts — with no footage, no camera, and nothing to upload.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Creators, marketers, and small businesses use this skill to plan and produce short spoken recommendation videos for social platforms from a topic, without supplying footage or a recorded voice. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared account connection requests broad access, including voice-editing access that this workflow does not need.

Mitigation: Review the account grant before connecting and avoid approving permissions that are not needed for the task.

Risk: Prompts and narration are sent to Beatra, and installation/runtime metadata is reported automatically.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive information in prompts or narration, and review the data-sharing implications before use.

Risk: Final paid usage can exceed estimates.

Mitigation: Approve each priced stage individually and review the returned billing details.

Risk: A shared credential may remain after removing one package, and damaged installation inventory can cause uninstall to disconnect other Beatra packages.

Mitigation: Check shared connections before uninstalling and revoke credentials from Beatra Console when appropriate.
