## Description:

ai-amazing-tech routes technology and patent-intelligence requests into technology landscape analysis, patent mining, or technical intelligence brief workflows and produces unified HTML reports backed by PatSnap patent data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

IPR teams, R&D leaders, strategy stakeholders, and engineers use this skill to analyze technology landscapes, identify patent opportunities, and monitor competitive or industry intelligence from PatSnap-backed sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill depends on PatSnap MCP authorization and external patent data access for live, sourced reports.

Mitigation: Confirm account authorization and MCP availability before relying on generated conclusions; use framework-only output when the integration is not configured.

Risk: Generated HTML reports may load third-party CDN assets when opened.

Mitigation: Review generated HTML before sharing or opening in controlled environments, and use approved asset hosting or offline copies when CDN loading is unsuitable.

Risk: Patent and technology intelligence reports may be mistaken for legal advice or a substitute for confidential IP strategy review.

Mitigation: Use the reports as sourced intelligence and have qualified legal or IP professionals review findings before making filing, enforcement, or strategy decisions.
