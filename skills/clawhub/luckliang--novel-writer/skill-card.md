## Description: <br>
Novel Writer helps agents plan, organize, and draft long-form Chinese web fiction through a structured volume, event, and chapter workflow. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[luckliang](https://clawhub.ai/user/luckliang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External authors and writing agents use this skill to develop novel concepts, plan volumes and events, maintain character and relationship context, and produce chapter outlines, drafts, and finalized manuscript files. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is intended to create and update local story planning, configuration, character, and chapter files. <br>
Mitigation: Use it in a dedicated project directory and review generated file changes before relying on them. <br>
