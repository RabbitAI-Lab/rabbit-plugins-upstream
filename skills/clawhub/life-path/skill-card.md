## Description: <br>
人生之路 helps users interpret Confucius's six life stages, assess their current stage, receive personalized action guidance, compare life-stage theories, and optionally generate an interactive HTML life-path report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for reflective life-stage interpretation, self-assessment, action planning, classic quote lookup, cross-cultural life-stage comparison, and optional HTML report generation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include sensitive personal reflections in generated life-stage assessments or HTML reports. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for HTML report generation only when a saved personal report is wanted, and review or remove sensitive details before saving or sharing the report. <br>
Risk: Life-planning guidance may be too general for high-stakes personal, medical, legal, financial, or mental-health decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a reflective framework and seek qualified professional support for high-stakes decisions. <br>
