## Description:

Answer peptide research questions with PepBank study findings, and look up Curo peptides, prices, stock and the lab report (COA) for each lot. Use when the user asks what the research says about a peptide, asks for a lab report or COA, or asks about Curo products, shipping or returns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[curo-you-peptides](https://clawhub.ai/user/curo-you-peptides)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask for peptide research summaries, product and lot report information, and Curo store policies. The skill presents study findings and product links for laboratory research use, without medical or dosing advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Information submitted to the hosted Curo service may be subject to data handling that was not verified.

Mitigation: Limit submissions to information appropriate to share with an external service.

Risk: Research findings or product details could be mistaken for medical guidance, and product links may lead to purchases.

Mitigation: Keep answers within laboratory research, avoid medical or dosing advice, and leave any checkout decision to the user.
