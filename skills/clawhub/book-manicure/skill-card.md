## Description: <br>
Book manicure services through Lokuli MCP for users who need to find and book manicure appointments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for manicure services, check provider availability, and create bookings through Lokuli's external MCP service. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Searches and bookings use Lokuli's external MCP service and may share customer contact details when a booking is created. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, date, time, price, cancellation policy, and the user's consent to share contact details before creating a booking. <br>
