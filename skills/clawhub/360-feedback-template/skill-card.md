## Description: <br>
Designs 360-degree feedback surveys and structured 360 feedback reports with rating scales, open-ended questions, synthesized themes, strengths, development areas, and suggested actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, HR and People teams, and coaches use this skill to create 360 feedback survey instruments or turn reviewer notes into structured, development-oriented feedback reports. It is useful for performance cycles, promotion reviews, peer feedback, and role-specific feedback processes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: 360 feedback content can include sensitive workplace information or quotes that identify reviewers in small teams. <br>
Mitigation: Use only feedback data the user is authorized to process, remove or paraphrase identifying details where needed, and review reports before sharing. <br>
Risk: Generated summaries or ratings can overstate patterns, flatten score variance, or turn limited notes into overly broad conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Check generated themes against the source notes and rating distribution, preserve important context and variance, and edit unsupported claims before use. <br>
