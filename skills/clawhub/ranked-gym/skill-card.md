## Use Case: <br>
External users use Ranked Gym to log workouts, receive XP and rank progress, track streaks, and view achievements and leaderboard-style fitness progress. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Workout and health-related details may be sensitive even when stored locally. <br>
Mitigation: Only log details you are comfortable keeping in the local OpenClaw environment, and review or clear saved progress when your setup provides that control. <br>
Risk: Gamified rankings, XP, and streaks may be inaccurate if workout details are incomplete or entered incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Review logged workouts and rank changes before relying on them for fitness tracking decisions. <br>
