## Description:

Turns public video links or local video files into cleaned transcripts and prompt-guided written content using GuaiKei's cloud service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, editors, and other users turn public video links or local recordings into transcripts, summaries, translations, and social-media copy. Requires a GuaiKei API token and sends selected media or URLs and prompts to a cloud service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected videos, video URLs, prompts, and token-authenticated requests reach GuaiKei and its presigned storage destination; retention and destination disclosures are unclear.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive media unless comfortable with these transfers, and verify the provider's storage, retention, and privacy terms before use.
