## Description:

Through breeding-tank macro images or video, this skill uses AI vision analysis to identify fish egg incubation stages from egg color changes and embryonic eye-spots, then provides stage estimates and suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fish breeders, hatchery teams, and laboratories use the skill to assess macro photos or videos of fish eggs, track incubation progress, and receive stage estimates, suggested next steps, and report links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
