## Description:

Designs a lead magnet offer with the promise, the contents, the delivery promise and the capture form, and flags the terms a free offer has to disclose. Use when the user asks for lead magnet builder, lead magnet offer template, free guide download page copy. For US small business owners and demand generation managers who trade a guide for contact details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted claims, testimonials, free-offer terms, or delivery promises may be inaccurate or incomplete even when the local lint report is clean.

Mitigation: Verify each claim against source evidence and confirm the offer terms and delivery policy before publishing.

Risk: The optional Windows toolkit is a separate unsigned program and is not covered by the security verdict for the bundled workflow.

Mitigation: Treat the toolkit as a separate installation decision; independently verify its download and assess it before running.

Risk: The linter replaces lint-report.md in the selected output folder.

Mitigation: Choose an output folder where replacing that report is acceptable.
