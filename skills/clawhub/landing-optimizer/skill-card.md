## Description: <br>
Landing Optimizer helps agents audit and improve landing pages for influencer-driven traffic by producing message-match analysis, page-structure recommendations, conversion and CTA guidance, and an A/B test roadmap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, developers, and growth operators use this skill to improve landing pages receiving influencer or paid-ad traffic. It turns user-supplied campaign details, page state, creator messaging, promo codes, and conversion goals into a prioritized conversion plan and test roadmap. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save campaign facts such as landing page URLs, promo codes, baseline conversion rates, and creator handles to agent memory files. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential promo codes, unpublished campaign URLs, or sensitive performance data unless those details may be stored, or instruct the agent not to save them. <br>
