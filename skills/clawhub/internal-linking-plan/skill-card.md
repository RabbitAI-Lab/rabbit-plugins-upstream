## Description:

Find orphan pages, buried pages, links to redirects or 404s, nofollowed links and weak anchors, then get an exact list of links to add (source, target, anchor, sentence, reason). Use for "plan my internal links", "find orphan pages", "which pages should link to this page", or after a site crawl.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to review a site's internal links and identify orphaned or deeply buried pages, broken destinations, and weak anchors. It turns a crawler export, sitemap, or page inventory into a prioritized draft plan with suggested source pages, targets, anchor text, and sentence edits.
