## Description: <br>
The narrative-craft layer applies story structure, concrete specifics, emotional framing, and format-aware arcs to truthful social content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External social-content teams, founders, marketers, and agent workflows use this skill to find a real story, structure a concise arc, anchor it in specifics, and route the resulting narrative to the right downstream format writer. It is most useful when content needs emotional clarity, truthful transformation, or serialized narrative structure rather than a forced story. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad social-content improvement requests where narrative structure is not actually needed. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when the task specifically calls for narrative structure, emotional framing, or story-arc work; route simple informational posts to ordinary format writers. <br>
Risk: Story-driven content can become misleading if customer stories, testimonials, or founder struggles are fabricated or published without review. <br>
Mitigation: Use only truthful source material, confirm consent for testimonials, and review downstream publishing steps before content goes live. <br>
Risk: Narrative or platform-performance claims may drift over time. <br>
Mitigation: Recheck platform framing, metrics, and cited research claims periodically before relying on them for publication strategy. <br>
