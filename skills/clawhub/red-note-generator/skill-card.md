## Description: <br>
AI-powered Xiaohongshu (RED) note content generator that creates complete RED-style notes from a topic and optional style requirements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wsd-mj](https://clawhub.ai/user/wsd-mj) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and content operators use this skill to generate Xiaohongshu (RED)-ready note drafts from topics and style guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Topics and style requests are sent to an external wsdsocial.com service, and the skill uses an API key for access. <br>
Mitigation: Do not submit secrets, regulated data, private personal information, or unpublished business-sensitive material; rotate WSD_API_KEY if it is exposed. <br>
