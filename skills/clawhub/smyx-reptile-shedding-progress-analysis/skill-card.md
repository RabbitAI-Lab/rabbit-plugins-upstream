## Description:

Analyzes reptile images or videos to track shedding stages, flag retained-shed concerns, and provide care guidance and report history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Reptile keepers and animal-care teams use the skill to review shedding progress from enclosure images or videos, identify possible retained skin, and consult prior reports. Its care suggestions are not a veterinary diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reptile images or videos and analysis history are sent to lifeemergence.com/open.lifeemergence.com.

Mitigation: Confirm the service's retention and sharing controls before using sensitive media.

Risk: The skill silently creates or reuses a cloud identity and stores service tokens in the workspace data directory.

Mitigation: Review account controls and restrict access to the workspace data directory before installation.
