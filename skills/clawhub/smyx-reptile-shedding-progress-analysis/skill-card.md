## Description:

Analyzes reptile images or videos to estimate shedding stage and provide a structured report with care suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Reptile keepers and operators of vivaria or reptile facilities use the skill to review images or videos for possible shedding progress and receive care suggestions and report links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The export-only option still permits analysis.

Mitigation: Do not rely on this option for a no-analysis run; wait for the behavior fix and verify it before use.

Risk: Account setup and credential handling lack clear user control.

Mitigation: Wait for fixes before using credentials or sensitive footage. Confirm external account creation, possible charges, token storage, and footage retention or deletion practices with the publisher.
