## Description:

Content quality and E-E-A-T assessment for AI citability, evaluating experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness, and content structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content teams and SEO/GEO practitioners use this skill to audit webpages for E-E-A-T, AI citability, content structure, freshness, topical authority, and improvement opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill fetches user-selected webpages and writes an analysis report that may influence content or SEO changes.

Mitigation: Provide only pages intended for audit and review the generated report before acting on its recommendations.

Risk: Content-quality findings can be incomplete or misleading when the provided page set is narrow, stale, or unrepresentative.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current site evidence, editorial standards, and business context before publishing changes.
