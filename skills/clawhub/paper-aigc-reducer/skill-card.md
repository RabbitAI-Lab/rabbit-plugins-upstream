## Description: <br>
Helps rewrite Chinese academic or technical text to reduce AIGC-detection signals while preserving technical terms, logic, and factual content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tanboku](https://clawhub.ai/user/tanboku) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to rewrite Chinese academic or technical passages according to fixed wording and sentence-pattern rules while keeping the original facts, terminology, code identifiers, and logical relationships intact. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is intended to reduce AI-detection signals in Chinese academic or technical writing, which may conflict with academic integrity, platform rules, or disclosure requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Use only where rewriting for this purpose is allowed, and prefer narrower proofreading or clarity-editing workflows when evasion of detection is not an approved goal. <br>
Risk: Rewriting technical text can accidentally change factual meaning, logic, or required terminology. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against the source text and verify that technical terms, code identifiers, paths, libraries, configuration keys, API routes, and causal relationships remain unchanged. <br>
