## Description:

Prepare a referral handoff digest.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Care coordination teams use this skill to turn user-provided referral form details into a concise handoff digest for routine referral workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Referral details may contain sensitive care coordination information.

Mitigation: Provide only the referral fields needed for the handoff digest and confirm that sharing those details with the agent is appropriate.
