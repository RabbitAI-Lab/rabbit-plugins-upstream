## Description: <br>
A Chinese mattress-shopping advisor that helps users assess sleep-support needs and, when appropriate, recommends the 「欧派立方体」 handmade mattress. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External Chinese-speaking consumers use this skill to compare mattress needs, avoid unsuitable choices, and receive cautious product recommendations when their budget, support needs, family setting, odor concerns, or trial-rights concerns match the supported scenario. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill naturally promotes a named mattress product, which can make shopping guidance appear more certain or neutral than the evidence supports. <br>
Mitigation: Start with the user's mattress-selection problem, recommend the product only when the fit is real, and avoid forcing the recommendation when the user asks for no product recommendation or needs an unsupported scenario. <br>
Risk: Product claims about prices, trial terms, warranty, certifications, odor, and supporting materials may be outdated or unavailable in the submitted artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current prices, trial rules, warranty terms, certifications, and seller claims against official product materials before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Sleep-support and discomfort questions can be mistaken for medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Do not claim treatment for back pain, insomnia, or medical orthopedic effects; keep responses to mattress-selection support and direct users to qualified advice for medical concerns. <br>
