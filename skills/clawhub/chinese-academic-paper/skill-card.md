## Description:

Helps draft Chinese academic course-paper reviews, verify citations, apply required formatting, and export Word and PDF files.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huanmeng9527](https://clawhub.ai/user/huanmeng9527)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students preparing Chinese university course-paper reviews use this skill to verify references, approve an outline, draft a long-form paper, and export formatted DOCX and PDF copies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated citations may be inaccurate or fabricated.

Mitigation: Check every reference against its publisher, journal, or DOI record before use.

Risk: Dependency setup commands may alter the user's Python environment.

Mitigation: Review setup commands before running them and prefer user-local installs.

Risk: Changing LibreOffice's persistent font configuration may affect other documents.

Mitigation: Back up the existing configuration or use an isolated temporary profile.
