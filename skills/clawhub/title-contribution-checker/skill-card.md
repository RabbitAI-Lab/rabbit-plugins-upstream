## Description:

检查学术论文标题是否清晰传达了研究的核心理念和贡献。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to review whether a paper title communicates the research focus, variables, relationship, and contribution clearly. It returns a contribution rating, analysis, identified issues, and revision suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The title assessment may be incomplete or misleading for specialized academic domains.

Mitigation: Use the output as writing guidance and have a domain expert review the final title before submission.
