## Description:

Krexa helps users research trading ideas, backtest strategies, and request deployment of live trading agents across supported crypto, prediction-market, tokenized-stock, and exchange venues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krexa](https://clawhub.ai/user/krexa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to research trading ideas, review backtest results, manage paper agents, and request live trading-agent deployments through Krexa.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The service receives submitted trading ideas and can access financial information from connected accounts.

Mitigation: Keep the Krexa key private, share only information intended for the service, and review connected-account access.

Risk: Approved live agents can lose money, and some venues lack per-trade or daily dollar caps.

Mitigation: Review venue-specific limits and deployment settings carefully; use per-order approval where available and approve live deployments only in the Krexa app.
