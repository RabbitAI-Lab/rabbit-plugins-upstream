## Description:

Helps insurance agents, brokers, and operations teams draft marketing copy, compliance review notes, and image-generation prompts for insurance promotion workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance agents, brokers, and insurance operations staff use this skill to create draft social posts, short-video scripts, compliance review feedback, and image prompts for insurance marketing. Outputs are intended as drafts that require human review and the user's institution's legal or compliance approval before public use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing content may include outdated or incorrect regulatory claims, statistics, or product descriptions.

Mitigation: Verify all regulatory claims, statistics, product benefits, rates, and dates against current official sources and policy documents before use.

Risk: Insurance marketing drafts may be mistaken for legal, compliance, or product advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting assistance only and require qualified institutional legal or compliance review before public release.

Risk: Entering real customer personal, health, policy, or contact data could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use only minimized, anonymized, or fictional examples; do not provide real customer names, ID numbers, phone numbers, policy numbers, health status, or other sensitive data.
