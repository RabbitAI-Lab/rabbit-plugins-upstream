## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Example livestream sales copy can include absolute pricing, stock, countdown, discount, or product-effect claims that may be inaccurate or noncompliant if reused without review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated scripts as drafts and verify price, stock, discount, countdown, and product-effect claims before use; avoid absolute or unverifiable claims unless substantiated. <br>
Risk: Operational recommendations may not reflect the latest platform policy or advertising compliance requirements for a specific merchant or product category. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against current Douyin rules, advertising requirements, and product-category restrictions before publishing or using them in a live stream. <br>
