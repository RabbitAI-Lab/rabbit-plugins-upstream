## Description:

Describes Agiexa’s planned Shopify store management features and can join the launch waitlist with the user’s permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devkrutik](https://clawhub.ai/user/devkrutik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shopify store owners and operators use this preview to learn about Agiexa’s planned store management features and related integrations, and may opt in to the launch waitlist. It cannot manage a store before launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A waitlist signup shares the confirmed email and role, plus any optional link or message, with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user explicitly consents and confirms the exact details to be sent.

Risk: Agiexa’s live service and security claims were unverified during review, and this preview does not connect to Shopify.

Mitigation: Review the integration separately before granting it store access after launch.
