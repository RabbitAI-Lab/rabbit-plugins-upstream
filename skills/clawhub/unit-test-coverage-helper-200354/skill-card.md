## Description: <br>
Helps teams add useful unit tests and raise test coverage in existing codebases through a repeatable local workflow. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to plan and implement practical unit-test coverage improvements without breaking existing behavior. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad auto-invocation wording may activate the helper for general testing or quality requests. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's testing goal and review generated code changes and test commands before applying them. <br>
