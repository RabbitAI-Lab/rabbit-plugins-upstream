## Description:

Discovers and ranks TikTok creators in any niche or topic using apidojo's TikTok scrapers on Apify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External influencer marketers, brand partnership teams, and talent managers use this skill to discover, filter, and rank TikTok creators by niche, follower range, region, and engagement for outreach planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok search terms, campaign criteria, and scraper results are processed by Apify and Apidojo under the user's Apify account.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when that third-party processing is acceptable, keep APIFY_TOKEN secret, and avoid confidential campaign plans in search criteria.

Risk: Scraped creator metrics can be cached or stale, which may affect ranking and outreach decisions.

Mitigation: Treat rankings as discovery guidance and verify high-priority creator metrics before contacting or contracting creators.
