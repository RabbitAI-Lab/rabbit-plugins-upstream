## Description:

Assesses freelance job listings against Caleb Lopez's AI automation profile, skips poor-fit work, and drafts concise proposals for suitable opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caleb-lopz](https://clawhub.ai/user/caleb-lopz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External freelancers or their agents use this skill to triage job listings, compare them with Caleb Lopez's documented profile, and produce an apply/skip recommendation. For matching jobs, it drafts a short proposal and practical application tactics grounded in the supplied profile.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposals or fit assessments may be inaccurate when the input is not a clear structured job listing.

Mitigation: Review every generated proposal before sending and confirm that the job details were parsed correctly.

Risk: A proposal could overstate Caleb Lopez's qualifications if the profile evidence is not followed carefully.

Mitigation: Keep claims grounded in the supplied profile evidence and remove any unsupported experience before submission.
