## Description: <br>
Writes timed, value-adding follow-up sequences for post-interview thank-yous, no-reply nudges, stalled applications, and job-search check-ins. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and job candidates use this skill to draft timed, value-adding follow-up sequences for interviews, unanswered applications, stalled hiring processes, or offer-timeline check-ins. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated follow-up messages could include inaccurate interview details, deadlines, or competing-offer references. <br>
Mitigation: Review each message for accuracy and tone before sending, and include only details the user can verify. <br>
Risk: Overly frequent or poorly framed follow-ups could make the candidate appear intrusive. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's stop rule and adjust the cadence to match any stated hiring timeline or real deadline. <br>
