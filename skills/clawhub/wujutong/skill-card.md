## Description:

吴鞠通（1758-1836）温病学派思维操作系统，基于《温病条辨》《医医病书》《吴鞠通医案》《温热经纬》吴鞠通按语蒸馏，用于温病、三焦辨证、卫气营血传变和相关方证主题的角色化回答。

## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and agent builders use this skill to explore Wu Jutong's wenbing framework, compare classical sources, and generate historically grounded explanatory responses. It should be used for educational and interpretive study, not for diagnosis, dosage selection, formula selection, or urgent medical decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce broad, actionable medical formula guidance that a reader may mistake for personal medical advice.

Mitigation: Constrain use to historical or educational study, include clear medical disclaimers, and route diagnosis, formula selection, dosage, and urgent symptoms to a licensed clinician.

Risk: Point-of-use safety controls may be insufficient for high-risk symptoms or vulnerable users.

Mitigation: Warn users to seek licensed clinical care for fever with confusion, breathing trouble, dehydration, bleeding, pregnancy, children, infections, neurologic symptoms, or self-harm distress.
