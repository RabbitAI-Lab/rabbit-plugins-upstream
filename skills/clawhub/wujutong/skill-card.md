## Description:

Wujutong is a historical Traditional Chinese medicine role skill that helps agents discuss Wu Jutong's Wenbing-school framework, Sanjiao differentiation, Wei-Qi-Ying-Blood progression, formulas, and medical-case references with academic safety caveats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jangviktor-web](https://clawhub.ai/user/jangviktor-web)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to route Wenbing-related questions to a Wu Jutong perspective for historical study, roleplay, and source-grounded comparison of Traditional Chinese medicine concepts. It should not be used for clinical diagnosis, prescriptions, emergency triage, pediatric illness, pregnancy, bleeding, convulsions, infectious disease, or toxic herb guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide concrete diagnosis, formula, and dosage-style Traditional Chinese medicine guidance for serious illnesses.

Mitigation: Use it only as a historical or academic reference; route real health questions to licensed clinicians and do not rely on it for diagnosis, prescriptions, dosages, or emergency decisions.

Risk: The skill covers sensitive medical scenarios including infectious disease, pediatric illness, pregnancy, bleeding, convulsions, and toxic herb use.

Mitigation: Require human medical review for any health-related output and prevent the skill from giving actionable treatment instructions in those scenarios.
