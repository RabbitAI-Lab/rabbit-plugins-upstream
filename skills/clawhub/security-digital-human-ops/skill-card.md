## Use Case:

Bank and insurance digital marketing teams use this skill to draft digital human scripts, plan content calendars, prepare live streaming operations, design interaction patterns, and review performance. Its regulatory summaries, templates, and sample scripts are planning aids that require current legal and compliance review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory summaries or financial marketing guidance may be outdated or misapplied to a specific institution, product, or channel.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current official rules and route scripts, calendars, and campaign plans through legal and compliance review before publication.

Risk: Generated scripts or live streaming plans may omit required AI identity disclosures, financial risk notices, source attribution, or content authorization records.

Mitigation: Require human approval, banned-claim checks, disclosure checks, and material authorization tracking before publishing or archiving digital human content.
