## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial marketing and regulatory guidance may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting support and verify current requirements with official compliance staff before publishing or changing campaigns.

Risk: Generated digital-human scripts could include prohibited or misleading financial claims.

Mitigation: Review scripts for guarantees, absolute claims, missing AI disclosures, and required risk statements before use.
