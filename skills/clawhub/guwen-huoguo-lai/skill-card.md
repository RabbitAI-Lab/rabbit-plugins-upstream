## Description:

古文活过来 helps agents turn junior-middle-school classical Chinese texts and historical figures into publishable Chinese AI short-video scripts using four-act structure, debate-style openings, title and hook libraries, storyboard prompts, and posting strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, educators, and content teams use this skill to draft Chinese short-video scripts that reinterpret classical Chinese curriculum texts with hooks, four-act or debate-style structure, scene direction, AI visual prompts, exam-point cards, shareable closing lines, and publishing guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated educational script may contain inaccurate interpretation, omitted exam context, or invented historical details if the user relies on the template without review.

Mitigation: Review generated scripts against the source text, curriculum notes, and known historical context before publishing or using them in instruction.

Risk: The optional helper script produces a fixed local DOCX example and does not automate the full creative workflow described by the prompt.

Mitigation: Use the helper only as a document-formatting example and rely on the skill prompt plus human review for topic selection, script quality, and final content.
