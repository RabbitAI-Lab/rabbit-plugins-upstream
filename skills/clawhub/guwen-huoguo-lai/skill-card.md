## Description:

古文活过来 helps agents create Chinese educational short-video scripts that turn classical Chinese lessons and math or science stories into four-act, publishable AI video concepts with hooks, storyboard prompts, review checks, and posting guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, educators, and agents use this skill to draft Chinese-language educational short-video scripts for classical Chinese lessons or math and science storytelling. It is especially suited to transforming curriculum topics into structured hooks, four-act scripts, storyboard prompts, review checks, and posting notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated educational scripts may include incorrect literary, historical, mathematical, or scientific claims if the agent does not verify facts.

Mitigation: Review factual claims against the original lesson, source text, or scientific reference before publishing or using with students.

Risk: The workflow is intentionally Chinese-language and prescriptive, which may not match every classroom, channel, or audience style.

Mitigation: Tell the agent the target language, grade level, format, and tone before generation when a different delivery style is needed.
