## 字段说明 | Field notes
- **反应类型 | Reaction**：HER / OER / ORR / PEMWE / 光催化
- **催化剂体系 | Catalyst system**：Pt/C、Pt-Co、HEA、SAC、IrO₂/IrOₓ、Ru@IrOₓ、N/S-doped carbon 等
- **关键指标 | Key metrics**：overpotential @10 mA cm⁻²、ECSA、MA (A/mgₚₜ)、TOF、Tafel slope、stability
- **OA状态 | OA status**：已获取 Obtained / 在线 Online / 付费 Paywalled / 手动 Manual
- **相关性 | Relevance**：由摘要判断（高 / 中 / 低），仅保留"高/中"并剔除仅关键词命中但不相关者
  - Judged from abstract (High / Medium / Low); keep only High/Medium and drop keyword-only irrelevant hits

## 配套段落（置于表格之后） | Accompanying sections (after the table)
- **支撑结论 | Supporting conclusions**：对研究问题的证据总结
  - Evidence summary for the research question
- **验证建议 | Validation suggestions**：XRD/TEM/XPS 表征、RDE/MEA 测试、对照实验等
  - XRD/TEM/XPS characterization, RDE/MEA tests, control experiments, etc.
