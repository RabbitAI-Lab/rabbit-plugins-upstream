## Description: <br>
Use this skill when auditing a completed TRPG rule system for gaps, missing rules, or logical inconsistencies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, developers, and game designers use this skill to review finalized tabletop RPG rule systems for missing rules, incomplete subsystems, and cross-chapter inconsistencies before play or release. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent must read user-provided TRPG rule files to perform the audit. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the finalized rule files intended for review, and exclude unrelated or sensitive project materials. <br>
Risk: Completeness findings may miss ambiguous design intent or classify optional material as missing. <br>
Mitigation: Review the checklist results with the game designer or GM before treating findings as required changes. <br>
