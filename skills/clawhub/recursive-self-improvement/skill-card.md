## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad recursive repair and optimization authority can lead to unwanted or excessive changes. <br>
Mitigation: Run the skill only on a clearly scoped project and require explicit approval before file edits or repeated improvement cycles. <br>
Risk: Concurrent or recursive execution can consume more time or resources than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Cap concurrency, recursion depth, retries, and timeouts before use. <br>
Risk: Automated repair or refactoring may introduce regressions. <br>
Mitigation: Use a sandbox or dedicated branch, run tests after each change, and keep version-control rollback available. <br>
