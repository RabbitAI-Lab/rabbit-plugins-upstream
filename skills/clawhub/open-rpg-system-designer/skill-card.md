## Description:

Design original tabletop or digital RPG systems using optional SRD 5.1 or 5.2.1 mechanics as documented design references, not as a replacement rulebook.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to draft original tabletop or digital RPG systems, choose an SRD source route when needed, track provenance, and prepare attribution or review handoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help draft RPG systems and attribution notes, but it is not legal advice for public or commercial releases.

Mitigation: Use qualified IP review for commercial releases or uncertain SRD, trademark, setting, or excluded-content boundaries.

Risk: SRD-derived elements may be copied or adapted without adequate source tracking.

Mitigation: Record each material SRD-derived element in the provenance ledger and keep the ledger separate from release-facing attribution.
