## Description:

Translates a user-selected article from ai-thoughts/docs/ into a Simplified Chinese Markdown copy with a matching -chn.md filename.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and content maintainers use this skill to translate a specific existing Markdown article into Simplified Chinese after confirming the source article and destination path.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create or overwrite a translated Markdown file.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact source article and output path before writing, and ask before overwriting any existing file.
