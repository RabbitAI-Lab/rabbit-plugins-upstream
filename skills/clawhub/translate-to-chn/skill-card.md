## Description:

Translates a user-selected Markdown article from ai-thoughts/docs into Simplified Chinese in a matching -chn.md file.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and developers use this skill to translate a specific ai-thoughts article into a Simplified Chinese Markdown companion, retaining technical terms, code, and links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The wrong article could be translated or an existing translation overwritten.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected source and output paths before writing, and request explicit approval before replacing an existing -chn.md file.
