## Description: <br>
HALO is an A-share fundamental analysis skill that uses Python-collected market, financial, and qualitative data to produce a structured HALO V5.0 stock analysis report with AI-written interpretation slots. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[lululu811](https://clawhub.ai/user/lululu811) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to analyze A-share companies with a repeatable fundamental analysis workflow, separating Python-sourced data from AI-written interpretation. Outputs should be treated as informational research rather than financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill produces investment-style stock analysis and may overstate data reliability. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational research only and independently verify prices, filings, qualitative claims, target prices, and AI-written conclusions before acting. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes an installer pattern intended for remote shell execution. <br>
Mitigation: Review the installer before use and prefer a manual clone from a pinned source rather than curl-to-shell installation. <br>
