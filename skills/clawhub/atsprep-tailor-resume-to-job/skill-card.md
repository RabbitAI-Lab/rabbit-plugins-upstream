## Description:

Tailor a resume to a specific job posting with ATSPrep by ranking keywords, showing gaps, and rewriting the user's own lines without inventing facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devabubakar](https://clawhub.ai/user/devabubakar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use the skill to compare a resume with a specific job posting, find missing required and preferred keywords, and draft fact-preserving edits. The user confirms any missing skills before they are added.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume content is sent to ATSPrep, and selected text is sent to its disclosed AI provider for AI features.

Mitigation: Remove unnecessary personal details, use only intended input files, delete temporary copies, and check any custom endpoint setting.

Risk: The server's privacy and filtering claims remain unverified by this artifact review.

Mitigation: Review the privacy information and share resume content only if comfortable with its disclosed handling.
