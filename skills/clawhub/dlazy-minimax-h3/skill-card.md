## Description:

Generates 5–15 second MiniMax Hailuo video clips with native stereo audio from prompts, frame transitions, or multiple media references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to generate short videos from text prompts, first and last frames, or reference images, videos, and audio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary reports a mismatch between the stated 2K limit and a 4K option, and says referenced CLI behavior was not fully verified.

Mitigation: Confirm current service support and resolution limits before requesting 4K.

Risk: Prompts and media are reported to be sent to dLazy services, but the security summary says this data flow was not independently verified.

Mitigation: Review dLazy's data handling terms before submitting sensitive prompts or media.
