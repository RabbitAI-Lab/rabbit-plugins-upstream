## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to collect public market data and produce a structured market-risk review for Nasdaq, S&P 500, gold, rates, currency, energy, CPI/FOMC context, valuation, and large-cap technology earnings signals. It frames actions as informational analysis rather than personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce suggested portfolio actions such as adding, reducing, pausing, or continuing investments.

Mitigation: Treat those suggestions as informational market-risk analysis, not personalized financial advice, and have users make investment decisions independently.

Risk: The framework depends on public market and macroeconomic data sources that may be delayed, unavailable, or summarized through search results.

Mitigation: Check cited values against current primary or trusted financial sources before relying on the analysis for time-sensitive decisions.
