## Description: <br>
Helps an agent identify halo-effect bias in evaluations by separating global impressions from attribute-specific evidence and structured rubric judgments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external evaluators, founders, investors, and hiring teams use this skill to structure reviews of people, vendors, companies, products, or business narratives where one strong positive or negative impression may distort unrelated attribute ratings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may influence high-stakes evaluations such as hiring, performance reviews, vendor selection, or investment decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the structured rubric to support judgment, then have qualified reviewers apply domain expertise, legal requirements, and due diligence before acting. <br>
Risk: Users may over-weight the skill's analysis if the evaluation lacks attribute-specific evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Require concrete evidence for each rated attribute and treat unsupported ratings as prompts for further investigation rather than final conclusions. <br>
