## Description:

Applies a distilled Duan Yongping value-investing framework to help users reason through whether a stock, company, or business decision is understandable, high quality, reasonably priced, and within explicit investing red lines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiaxinmmhh](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiaxinmmhh)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, investors, and financial-content creators use this skill to apply a value-investing checklist to stock, company, investment, or business-decision questions. It provides a structured thinking aid and explicitly avoids personalized financial advice, stock recommendations, short-term market forecasts, or exact buy and sell prices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can sound evaluative when discussing stocks, companies, or business decisions.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as structured reasoning, not personalized financial advice, and remind users to make their own decisions and bear their own risk.

Risk: Users may rely on distilled investing methodology without checking current facts, prices, financial statements, or regulatory context.

Mitigation: Tell users to verify facts independently before making investment decisions and avoid presenting stale or unsupported facts as current.

Risk: The framework may be over-applied to companies or decisions the user cannot actually understand.

Mitigation: Use the skill's red-line checks, especially 'do not touch what you do not understand,' and decline definite conclusions when the business logic is unclear.
