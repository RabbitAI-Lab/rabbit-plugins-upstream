## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may disclose sensitive personal details while asking for dating or profile advice. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing real names, workplace details, home addresses, identifiable location clues, or other sensitive personal information with the agent or dating matches. <br>
Risk: Dating matches may apply investment, money-transfer, gift, or off-platform pressure. <br>
Mitigation: Treat investment, lending, transfer, red-envelope, or urgent off-platform requests as serious warning signs and verify safety claims independently. <br>
Risk: Offline meetings can create personal safety concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Use public places for first meetings, tell a trusted person the time and location, keep drinks in sight, and leave if the situation feels unsafe. <br>
