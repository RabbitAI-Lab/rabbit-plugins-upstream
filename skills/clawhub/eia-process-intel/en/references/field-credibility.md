# Field Credibility Grades (v1 prior)

Principle: the more a field affects approval outcomes, environmental-protection spending, and regulatory requirements, the more you must assume gaming between the company and the regulator. Grades update as cross-validation evidence accumulates; a falsified grade must be corrected in this table with the case noted.

## Grade table

| Field | game_risk | Typical distortion direction | Gaming motive | Cross-validation path |
|-------|-----------|------------------------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Design capacity | High | Both ways: inflate (grab total-emission quota, reserve expansion headroom); understate (avoid higher-tier approval) | Approval tiering, emission quota | Equipment count × industry cycle time; material inputs; annual operating hours; permit execution reports |
| Product mix | High | Split categories, hide under "pilot/R&D" labels, conceal restricted products | Circumvent catalog controls, special licensing | Infer real products from hazardous-waste types, waste-liquid composition, by-products |
| Pollutant generation / emission volume | High | Understate to cut monitoring frequency and treatment specs | Compliance cost, total-emission quota | Back-calculate from treatment facility design size; peer emission coefficients; actuals in execution reports |
| Raw/auxiliary material usage | Med-high | Understate toxic / hazardous chemicals to shrink protection distances and regulatory tier | Environmental spending, sanitation protection distance | Material-balance self-consistency; hazardous-waste ratios; warehouse facility size |
| Operating hours / utilization | Med-high | Understate annual running hours to depress emission estimates | Total emissions | Implied cycle time from equipment density; electricity and water usage |
| Process route | Medium | Vagueness and mixing to dodge specialized review | Lower technical-assessment difficulty | Infer from pollution signatures; corroborate with equipment models |
| Equipment count | Med-low | Underreport advanced equipment, omit cross-phase equipment | Lower noise/energy estimates | Ratio vs. capacity; cross-check against permit filings |
| Physical info (buildings, outfalls, treatment facilities) | Low | Largely credible (site-checkable, cross-referenced by later filings) | — | **Use as trust anchors** to back-calculate other fields |

## Threshold-hugging signatures (gaming-trace signals)

- Design capacity sits right at an approval-tier threshold (near the full-report ↔ report-form boundary)
- One project split into several small EIAs; multiple approvals for one site
- EIA capacity clearly mismatched with permit capacity (in either direction)
- Mass-production products declared under a "R&D / pilot" label
- Hazardous-chemical list missing common reagents the process requires
- Annual operating hours inconsistent with the industry's standard shift patterns

## Three interest-review questions (for every high-risk number)

1. Which of these does the number affect: approval tiering, total-emission quota, monitoring frequency, sanitation protection distance?
2. Which distortion direction benefits the company, and how large must the distortion be to be worth the risk?
3. Is there physical data (equipment / materials / energy / hazardous waste) that can bracket the number?

## Extraction field checklist (Phase 1 reference)

Project overview and approval info / product scheme and capacity / process flow (step-by-step with pollution-generation markers) / equipment list (name / model / count / source) / raw & auxiliary materials and hazardous chemicals / material and water balance / pollution sources and treatment facilities / construction schedule and investment. Every row carries a source (filename + page + verbatim quote) and the disclosure date.
