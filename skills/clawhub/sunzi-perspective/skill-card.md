## Description:

Provides a Chinese Sunzi-inspired strategic decision lens for competitive strategy, negotiations, go/no-go choices, risk assessment, and post-mortems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they want an agent to analyze competitive decisions, negotiations, resource conflicts, crisis responses, or failure reviews through a Sunzi-style strategic frame. It is intended for adversarial or competitive contexts and includes boundaries for non-zero-sum personal, parenting, and creative domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad strategy wording and apply an adversarial frame where the situation is not actually competitive.

Mitigation: Use it for competitive decisions, negotiations, resource conflicts, crisis response, and post-mortems; for personal, parenting, collaborative, or creative contexts, apply the documented boundary checks before using its advice.

Risk: The forceful first-person style can make uncertain analysis sound more definitive than the available facts support.

Mitigation: Require concrete opponents, missing-information lists, falsifiable predictions, costs, and at least one rejected option before treating an output as decision support.

Risk: Historical battle examples can invite after-the-fact reasoning or fictional-source mistakes.

Mitigation: Use examples as counterfactual prompts only, and apply the documented checks for source, mechanism, and attribution before citing a case.
