## Description:

Design or review where human approval, judgment, or accountability must remain in an automated workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Workflow designers, operations leaders, and automation builders use this skill to decide which manual approvals or judgments must remain in an automated process, then document each retained gate with a trigger, required evidence, owner, outcomes, and downstream effect.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Workflow changes may conflict with current approval, legal, financial, or risk policies.

Mitigation: Verify proposed automation boundary changes against the organization's current policies before implementation.

Risk: Historical precedent may be mistaken for delegated authority.

Mitigation: Retain human approval unless current authority rules explicitly permit automation.
