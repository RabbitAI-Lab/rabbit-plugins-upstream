## Description: <br>
Helps agents prepare a finished Instagram Reel for publishing by checking 9:16 specs, safe zones, cover requirements, caption and audio handoffs, and WoopSocial scheduling boundaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media operators, creators, and publishing agents use this skill to turn a finished Reel into a publish-ready Instagram post plan: spec validation, safe-zone checks, cover brief, caption handoff, scheduling block, and native in-app steps. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A public Reel, caption, cover, schedule, or account selection may be difficult to correct after publishing. <br>
Mitigation: Review the final video, caption, cover, schedule, and destination account before allowing the publishing workflow to post. <br>
Risk: The skill can advise native Instagram steps, but trending audio, Share-to-Feed, collaborator or product tags, media-quality settings, and Reel Insights remain in-app. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those items as user-confirmed native steps, and do not represent them as automated publishing fields. <br>
Risk: Incorrect aspect ratio, unsafe crop placement, low-quality export settings, or other-platform watermarks can degrade or misrepresent the Reel. <br>
Mitigation: Validate 1080x1920 9:16 video, MP4/MOV H.264 settings, safe-zone placement, custom cover readiness, and watermark removal before scheduling. <br>
Risk: Publishing guidance could overstate expected performance or imply unavailable metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid fabricated reach claims and rely on native Reel Insights or approved analytics after publication. <br>
