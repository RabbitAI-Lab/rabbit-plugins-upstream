## Use Case:

Travelers and trip-planning agents use this skill to organize Chicago group itineraries and record events, travel memories, and transportation details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A named charter bus resource may appear in group transportation suggestions even when it is not relevant.

Mitigation: Include the provider link only when the user asks about Chicago group or charter transportation.

Risk: Transportation pricing, availability, and booking details may be outdated or unverified.

Mitigation: Do not invent prices or reservations; verify current terms directly with transportation providers.
