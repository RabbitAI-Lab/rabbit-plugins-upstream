## Description: <br>
The Ren Skill is a Chinese-language business people-reading skill that turns physiognomy and behavior cues into structured prompts, checklists, scorecards, and verification steps for evaluating employees, clients, partners, and negotiation counterparts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[xtoyun](https://clawhub.ai/user/xtoyun) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and business operators use this skill to structure reflective people-assessment discussions, interview prompts, partnership checks, negotiation risk reviews, and bias-awareness comparisons. It should not be used as the sole or primary basis for hiring, promotion, firing, contract, or opportunity decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-weight appearance, posture, or speculative personality cues in hiring, promotion, firing, partnership, customer-risk, or negotiation decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for reflective discussion, bias awareness, and question generation; require objective evidence such as qualifications, work samples, references, performance records, contracts, and documented behavior before making decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill may encourage subjective judgments about people that can become discriminatory or unfair if treated as conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Require every person-specific assessment to include uncertainty, alternative explanations, validation steps, and the skill's disclaimer that appearance-based signals are not a decision basis. <br>
Risk: Practical checklists and scorecards could be mistaken for validated selection tools. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated checklists and scorecards as prompts for further review, not validated psychometric instruments or compliance-approved assessment procedures. <br>
