## Description:

Helps turn a slide deck or topic into a narrated explainer video through OnePress and reports where to find the finished MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getonepress](https://clawhub.ai/user/getonepress)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to request narrated explainer videos from an existing deck or a topic, then locate the completed video in OnePress.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deck or topic content is sent to OnePress for external video production.

Mitigation: Review the content before submission and use the service only if its external processing is acceptable.

Risk: The OnePress API key grants access to the external service.

Mitigation: Store the key in environment or configuration, not in chat messages.
