## Description:

Turns a slide deck or topic into a narrated explainer video through OnePress and downloads the completed MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getonepress](https://clawhub.ai/user/getonepress)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators, developers, and teams use the skill to turn a slide deck or topic into a narrated explainer video and save the resulting MP4 locally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected decks or prompts are sent to OnePress for processing.

Mitigation: Send only material you are comfortable sharing with OnePress.

Risk: An exposed OnePress API key could grant unauthorized access.

Mitigation: Use browser pairing or a secret store, never paste keys into chat, and revoke unused keys in OnePress settings.
