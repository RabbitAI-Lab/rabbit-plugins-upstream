## Description:

帮助创作者逐系统精读参考视频，并将带时码的观察整理为可用于风格迁移的分析、时间线与进度文件。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitruck](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitruck)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

创作者和视频编辑使用此技能，逐系统分析其有权使用的参考视频，记录时码证据，并整理视觉与声音设计的迁移建议。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The source video may not be authorized for analysis.

Mitigation: Use only videos you have permission to analyze.

Risk: Cloud transcription sends selected media for external processing and incurs a cost.

Mitigation: Review the live cost quote before transcription and confirm the media may be processed externally; keep transcripts and extracted evidence in the chosen analysis folder.
