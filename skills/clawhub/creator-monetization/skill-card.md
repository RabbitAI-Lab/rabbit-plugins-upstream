## Description: <br>
Creator monetization helps an agent build a diversified revenue portfolio for creators by sequencing memberships, products, brand deals, and platform payouts while drafting offers and promo posts within clear disclosure and scope limits. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators and social-media operators use this skill to choose realistic revenue streams, design offers and media kits, and draft promotion plans for WoopSocial to publish. Payment, product, membership, payout, tax, and sponsorship execution remain external and human-run. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Promo posts, sponsorship guidance, rates, or platform setup may be inaccurate, outdated, or noncompliant for a creator's situation. <br>
Mitigation: Review the plan, disclosures, rates, tax considerations, and external payment or product setup before publishing or acting on the output. <br>
Risk: Users may treat monetization suggestions as income guarantees or as a substitute for legal, tax, platform-policy, or sponsorship-disclosure review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as planning and drafting support only; keep expectations ranged, avoid fabricated metrics, disclose sponsorships, and obtain qualified review where needed. <br>
