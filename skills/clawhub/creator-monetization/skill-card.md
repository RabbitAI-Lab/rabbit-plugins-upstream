## Description: <br>
Creator monetization builds a diversified creator revenue portfolio across memberships, products, brand deals, and platform payouts using the STACK framework while keeping payments, product hosting, and creator payouts in external human-run tools. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators and social media operators use this skill to plan realistic creator monetization strategies, including a diversified revenue stack, offer design, media-kit guidance, and promotional post drafts. It is intended for advisory planning and copy drafting, not payment processing, product hosting, or guaranteed income. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Creator payout rules, sponsorship disclosure requirements, and platform monetization programs can change. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current platform payout rules and required sponsorship disclosures before publishing or acting on monetization advice. <br>
Risk: The skill may use creator profile details, audience metrics, and revenue goals to draft plans and promotional posts. <br>
Mitigation: Review all generated plans and posts before publishing, and confirm metrics against the external platforms rather than relying on generated numbers. <br>
Risk: Users may assume the skill manages money, products, memberships, or payouts. <br>
Mitigation: Use external payment, membership, product, and payout tools directly; the skill only advises, plans, and drafts promotional content. <br>
