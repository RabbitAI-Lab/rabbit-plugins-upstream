## Description:

Helps HOA and condo owners prepare violation responses, review fine procedures, request association records, project assessment increases, and plan escalation steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

HOA and condo owners use the skill to draft responses to violation notices, request association records, review fine procedures, and model assessment increases. It also provides an escalation checklist for preparing board or mediation discussions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags the HOA fine guidance as potentially inaccurate or preliminary.

Mitigation: Check current statutes and governing documents before relying on a fine assessment; seek qualified legal advice for consequential disputes or lien threats.

Risk: The security review flags advice about recording HOA meetings as potentially mismatched to local requirements.

Mitigation: Check applicable recording laws and meeting rules before recording, and obtain consent where required.

Risk: The security review reports a concern about undeclared network capability and notes that its analysis was partial.

Mitigation: Review the skill's permissions and network access before installation, and avoid providing sensitive information until those capabilities are understood.
