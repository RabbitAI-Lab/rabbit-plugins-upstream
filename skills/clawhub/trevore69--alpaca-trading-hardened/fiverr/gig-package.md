## 4. Gig description

**Make your AI tool behave like a trained employee, not a guessing machine.**

Most people fight ChatGPT or Claude every day. They re-explain the same rules, fix the same mistakes, and get output that sounds like everyone else's. The fix is not better prompting in the chat box. It is a proper instruction file that the AI reads before every answer.

That is what I build. One file, written for your tool and your task, that makes the AI follow your rules every single time.

**What you get**

- A skill file built for your platform: Claude skill (SKILL.md), custom GPT instructions, or a Codex / AGENTS.md file
- Written around your real workflow, not a generic template
- Tested against your own examples of good and bad output
- Revisions until it actually does the job

**Who this is for**

- Founders and marketers who want AI output in their voice, not AI voice
- Developers who want an agent that follows house rules on every task
- Teams who keep copy-pasting the same instructions into every chat

**How I work**

1. You fill in a short requirements form: the tool, the task, what good output looks like, what bad output looks like
2. I build the skill file and test it against your examples
3. You get the file, a plain-English guide on how to use it, and revisions based on real use

**Why me**

I build and run these files daily for my own projects, including a 43-pattern writing style enforcer with a multi-pass editing pipeline and a strict academic mode. You are not buying a prompt typed in five minutes. You are buying a tested system.

**One honest note:** I use AI tools in my process, then test, edit, and verify everything by hand. If you want 100 percent human-typed work with zero AI involvement, I am not your seller. If you want a file that works, keep reading.

Message me before ordering if your task is unusual. I would rather tell you it is a bad fit than take your money and waste your week.

## 6. FAQ

**Q: What is a skill file, in plain English?**
A text file full of rules that the AI reads before it answers you. Think of it as a training manual. Without it, the AI guesses. With it, the AI follows your process.

**Q: Which platforms do you support?**
Claude (skill files), ChatGPT (custom GPT instructions), and Codex or similar coding agents (AGENTS.md or rules files). If your tool is not listed, message me first.

**Q: Will this make the AI stop making mistakes?**
No file stops every mistake. What it does is kill the repeated, predictable ones: wrong tone, wrong format, ignored rules. That is where most of the wasted time goes.

**Q: Do you use AI to write the skill file?**
Yes, as part of my process. I then test and edit everything by hand against your examples. Fiverr allows AI-assisted work and I am upfront about it. What you are paying for is the testing and the judgment, not the typing.

**Q: What do you need from me?**
The requirements form after ordering: your platform, the task, two or three examples of output you liked, two or three you hated, and any hard rules (words to avoid, formats, reading level, anything).

**Q: Can you build a skill for [very specific niche task]?**
Probably, if you can show me what good output looks like. If I cannot do it well, I will say so before you order.

**Q: What if it does not work for my use case?**
That is what revisions are for. We adjust the file against real failures until it behaves. If we truly cannot get there, Fiverr's resolution process applies like any other gig.

5. List any hard rules: words or phrases to avoid, tone, reading level, formatting requirements, length limits.

7. Anything the AI currently does that drives you crazy? (Optional but very useful)

## 9. Process and disclosure policy

- AI-assisted delivery is allowed on Fiverr. Disclosure is required if the buyer asks or demands no-AI work. Our FAQ answers it before they ask.
- Never market as "hand-crafted", "100 percent human written", or similar. That claim would be false and it is also the one that gets gigs flagged.
- Every delivery gets a real test pass against the buyer's examples before it goes out. No raw AI output shipped. Ever.
