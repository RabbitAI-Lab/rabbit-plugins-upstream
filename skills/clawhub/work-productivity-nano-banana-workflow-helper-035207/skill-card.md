## Description: <br>
Helps agent users and skill authors turn Nano Banana Pro-style workflow demand into practical plans, checklists, code changes, and verification notes for productivity tasks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to scope Nano Banana Pro-style productivity requests, produce actionable workflows or artifacts, and verify assumptions and follow-up work without requiring specialized hardware. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for unrelated Nano, Banana, image, Gemini, or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review the activation phrases before installation and narrow or disable implicit invocation when the agent supports that control. <br>
