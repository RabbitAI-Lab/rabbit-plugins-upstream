## Description: <br>
Activates when an agent is asked to evaluate suspected reversal chart patterns, including head-and-shoulders, double or triple tops and bottoms, rounding patterns, and V-shaped reversals, with emphasis on prior trend, neckline or key-level breakout, and volume confirmation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and trading-analysis agents use this skill to decide when a suspected price reversal pattern is complete and how to apply neckline or key-level confirmation before deriving targets. It helps distinguish reversal patterns from continuation patterns or generic support and resistance analysis. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users could mistake educational market-analysis guidance for financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as analysis support only and require independent verification, personal risk controls, and user responsibility before any trading decision. <br>
Risk: Pattern recognition can be misleading when a reversal shape is incomplete or confused with support, resistance, or continuation behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Require prior trend, neckline or key-level breakout, and volume confirmation before treating a pattern as complete. <br>
