## Description:

Luma helps agents plan cinematic Luma Dream Machine video shots with model selection, draft-first credit control, reference workflows, rights checks, and human review before publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to plan Luma/Dream Machine video-generation workflows, including shot briefs, model choices, references, credit budgets, optional API-connected generation steps, and publishing handoffs. The skill keeps clip review, rights checks, and final publishing under human control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated clips may be used without proper Luma account authorization, commercial rights, or current pricing awareness.

Mitigation: Use only authorized Luma or API accounts, verify current tiers and pricing in-app, and confirm commercial rights before client or ad work.

Risk: Synthetic video may include unpermitted likenesses, misleading fake-event footage, or missing AI disclosure.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's human review gate, avoid unpermitted real-person likenesses and photoreal fake events involving real entities, and apply platform or regional AI-disclosure requirements.

Risk: Model limits, credit rates, and rights terms may change after the release evidence was generated.

Mitigation: Treat rate, tier, and capability claims as time-sensitive and verify them with Luma before production use.
