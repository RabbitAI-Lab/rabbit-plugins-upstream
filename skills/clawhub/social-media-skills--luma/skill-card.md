## Description: <br>
The Luma Dream Machine craft skill helps agents plan cinematic AI video shots with Luma/Ray models, using shot briefs, references, draft-first credit control, sequence assembly guidance, and rights checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, and marketing teams use this skill to brief, budget, review, and publish short cinematic Luma video shots while keeping human approval, commercial-rights checks, and disclosure requirements in the workflow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Using the skill with a Luma connection can spend Luma or API credits. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm current Luma plan terms and credit budget in-app before generation, and use the skill's draft-first workflow before final renders. <br>
Risk: Prompts, images, or footage may be sent to Luma when connected. <br>
Mitigation: Use only content the user has rights to submit and avoid sensitive or NDA material unless the applicable Luma tier and data terms support that use. <br>
Risk: Generated clips could be published without adequate review or rights checks. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human review before publishing, verify commercial rights and disclosure obligations, and avoid unpermitted likenesses or fake-event footage. <br>
