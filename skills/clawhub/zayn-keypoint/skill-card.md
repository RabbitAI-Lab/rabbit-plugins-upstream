## Description: <br>
Extracts core conclusions, key questions, confirmed facts, major risks, and next steps from chats, project materials, or meeting information. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and teams use this skill to turn large Chinese-language work materials into a structured key-point analysis, including parameter status, core conclusions, confirmed facts, unresolved items, risks, and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill processes user-provided work materials that may contain sensitive business or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in chat contexts where processing that material is acceptable, and avoid pasting sensitive material when that is not appropriate. <br>
Risk: Incomplete, conflicting, or unverified inputs could be mistaken for confirmed facts. <br>
Mitigation: Require the parameter status table, preserve conflicts for verification, and keep unverified information out of final conclusions. <br>
