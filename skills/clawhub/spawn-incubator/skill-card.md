## Use Case: <br>
External developers and agent builders use this skill to evaluate and apply to the SPAWN incubator, prepare an on-chain business proposal, understand funding milestones, and follow heartbeat, revenue-share, and graduation requirements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Applying requires wallet transaction signing, a non-refundable entry fee, public or hosted proposal data, weekly heartbeat obligations, and a 12-month on-chain revenue-share commitment after graduation. <br>
Mitigation: Install and use only after reviewing those obligations, excluding secrets or confidential details from hosted proposals, and confirming that the commitment fits the intended business. <br>
Risk: Using an incorrect or spoofed contract address could cause irreversible on-chain transactions. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify contract addresses and network details before signing any transaction. <br>
