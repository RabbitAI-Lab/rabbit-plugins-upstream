## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: AI-avatar workflows can misuse a real person's voice, face, brand, or copyrighted material. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm ownership, rights, or clear consent before generating likeness, voice, brand, or copyrighted content. <br>
Risk: Paid media-generation steps can spend credits or trigger external generation jobs. <br>
Mitigation: Review cost checks and generation actions before committing paid credits. <br>
Risk: Trust-heavy categories such as privacy, security, finance, or health can create higher brand and user-risk exposure when delivered through AI-avatar content. <br>
Mitigation: Validate lighter categories first and review trust-heavy scripts for accuracy, consent, and brand suitability before publishing. <br>
