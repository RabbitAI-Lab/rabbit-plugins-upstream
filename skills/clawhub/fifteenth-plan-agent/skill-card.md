## Description: <br>
Fifteenth Plan Agent helps collect, organize, compare, and answer questions about China's 12th through 15th Five-Year Plan materials, related economic and administrative data, and regional planning indicators. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and policy researchers use this skill to research Chinese five-year-plan documents, compare planning periods and regions, query structured economic and administrative indicators, and generate concise analysis reports with official-source citations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Policy and indicator claims may be outdated, provisional, or not yet officially published, especially for Fifteenth Five-Year Plan materials. <br>
Mitigation: Verify claims against current official government sources or authoritative media, cite the document title, publishing body, and date, and label unconfirmed information as provisional or unavailable. <br>
Risk: Generated comparisons or reports could be read as policy judgment or investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outputs informational, distinguish analysis from recommendations, and apply human review before using reports for decisions. <br>
