## Description:

Synthesizes publication-grade vector PDF invoices, estimates, receipts, proposals, NDAs, and 57 business and real estate document archetypes in sub-25ms with instant PNG previews and 1-scan QR code mobile payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business teams can use this skill to calculate totals, preview, and generate invoices, estimates, quotes, receipts, contracts, and other supported business and real estate documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential record fields are sent to Pressmark when creating documents.

Mitigation: Check Pressmark's privacy and retention terms before submitting confidential records.

Risk: Payment links or export charges may be incorrect or unexpected.

Mitigation: Verify payment links and review the disclosed export charges.
