## Description:

Synthesizes publication-grade vector PDF invoices, estimates, receipts, proposals, and NDAs with sub-25ms vector precision, instant PNG previews, custom brand palettes, and 1-scan QR code mobile payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, freelancers, and businesses can create and preview invoices, estimates, quotes, receipts, purchase orders, and agreements. The skill extracts document details and calculates line-item totals, taxes, and discounts before producing a formatted document.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Document fields sent for previews and PDFs may include sensitive financial, medical, personal, or confidential information.

Mitigation: Minimize sensitive details and verify Pressmark's privacy, retention, and suitability before sharing medical or confidential records.
