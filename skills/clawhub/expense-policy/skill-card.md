## Description: <br>
Expense Policy drafts clear company expense and reimbursement policies with covered categories, limits, approvals, submission steps, timelines, exclusions, and review reminders for tax and legal topics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
People operations, finance, and company operators use this skill to draft practical expense and reimbursement policies for employees, including reimbursable categories, limits, approvals, submission steps, reimbursement timing, and exclusions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated expense limits, reimbursement rules, or tax treatment may not match the company's budget, accounting process, or jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Have finance, accounting, or legal review the policy before adoption, and confirm all labelled amounts, thresholds, and tax-related statements. <br>
