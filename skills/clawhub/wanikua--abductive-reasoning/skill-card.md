## Description:

Apply abductive reasoning to infer the best explanation from available observations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wanikua](https://clawhub.ai/user/wanikua)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and other agents use this skill to structure uncertain investigations by cataloging observations, generating candidate explanations, comparing their explanatory power, and identifying validation steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make hypothesis-generation outputs sound more conclusive than the available evidence supports, especially in medical, legal, financial, investigative, or other high-stakes domains.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as candidate explanations, preserve uncertainty, and require domain-specific safeguards or expert review before acting on high-stakes conclusions.
