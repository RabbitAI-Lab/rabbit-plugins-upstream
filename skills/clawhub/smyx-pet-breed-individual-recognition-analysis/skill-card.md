## Description:

Identifies cat and dog breeds in images or video and distinguishes individual pets in multi-pet households.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners and pet care assistants use the skill to analyze pet images or video, identify cat and dog breeds, distinguish individuals in multi-pet households, and view prior reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment reports privacy and scope concerns involving account, credential, and video-analysis behavior beyond the stated pet-recognition purpose; the scan was partial.

Mitigation: Before use, review the requested account and credential access and the service's handling of submitted media and reports. Submit only media you are authorized to share.
