## Description: <br>
Government-procurement evaluation committee decision-support skill for assessing concrete invalid-bid or clarification questions, distinguishing invalid bids from curable defects, and drafting report-ready invalid-bid reasoning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Government procurement reviewers and evaluation committees use this skill to analyze a specific bidder response against corresponding procurement-file clauses, decide whether the issue is an invalid bid or a curable clarification item, and produce concise wording for evaluation reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake decision-support output for a final legal or evaluation-committee decision. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as advisory analysis, require evaluation-committee review, and verify current procurement-file clauses and legal source text before relying on conclusions. <br>
Risk: Connected procurement knowledge bases may be unavailable, incomplete, or not intentionally mounted. <br>
Mitigation: Declare any knowledge-base gap, avoid invented citations or cases, and rely only on verified user-provided documents and confirmed legal sources. <br>
Risk: Incorrect terminology or cross-system reasoning could weaken a procurement review record. <br>
Mitigation: Use government-procurement terminology consistently, confirm the procurement system before analysis, and preserve traceable facts, clauses, legal bases, and reviewer notes. <br>
