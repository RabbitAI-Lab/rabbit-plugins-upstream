## Description: <br>
A Share Data provides a pure data interface for A-share market quotes, financials, sectors, capital flows, and K-line data with automatic fallback across AkShare, Eastmoney, Sina, Tencent, and other providers. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, and finance-focused agents use this skill to retrieve A-share market data and example data-access code without generating investment judgments. It is intended as a data layer for行情, 财务, 板块, 资金流向, K线, index, IPO, margin, and cross-market lookup workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Third-party finance data may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or subject to provider terms. <br>
Mitigation: Treat returned data as research material, review provider terms and reliability, and label the data source and retrieval time. <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on retrieved market data for investment decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a data layer only and do not rely on it alone for investment decisions. <br>
