## Description: <br>
Veo 3 helps agents write brand-grounded Google Veo prompts for short social videos, including 9:16 clips, image-to-video prompts, synchronized audio cues, and disclosure guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media creators, marketers, and agent workflows use this skill to draft precise Veo prompts for short-form clips, product motion, b-roll, ad scenes, and still-image animation. It guides prompt craft and review posture rather than generating, uploading, or publishing video itself. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated clips may contain visual, audio, lip-sync, or physics artifacts, or may require AI disclosure before publication. <br>
Mitigation: Review every generated clip before publishing and disclose AI-generated video where platform policy or law requires it. <br>
Risk: Prompts involving real identifiable people, copyrighted IP, or brand styles without rights can create consent or rights issues. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid those prompts unless the user has clear rights and consent; offer original characters, scenes, or owned product assets instead. <br>
Risk: The skill may point an agent toward separate generation, media upload, or scheduling tools when they are available. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm user intent and permissions before invoking external generation, upload, or publishing workflows. <br>
