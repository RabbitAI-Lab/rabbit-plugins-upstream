## Description: <br>
Research startups, funding rounds, acquisitions, and hiring trends via startups.in <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to research startups, funding rounds, acquisitions, hiring trends, open jobs, and side-by-side company comparisons through startups.in. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Startup research queries and startup names are sent to startups.in. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive or private company research unless the user is comfortable sharing it with startups.in. <br>
Risk: The optional Moltbook identity token is a credential sent to startups.in for enhanced access. <br>
Mitigation: Use public access when possible and provide a Moltbook identity token only when enhanced access is needed and startups.in is trusted to receive it. <br>
