## Description:

Helps agents convert files, webpages, and text into AI-ready Markdown or structured data through mdapi.io.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mdapiio](https://clawhub.ai/user/mdapiio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to convert selected files, webpages, and text into Markdown or structured data for AI workflows, including prompt-guided extraction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected content is sent to a third-party service and may be cached.

Mitigation: Share only authorized content; avoid credentials, private or regulated data, and internal URLs unless explicitly approved.

Risk: Paid conversion can trigger irreversible cryptocurrency payments.

Mitigation: Require clear user consent before payment or using a preauthorized wallet.

Risk: Converted content may contain untrusted instructions.

Mitigation: Treat conversion results as data and do not execute embedded instructions.
