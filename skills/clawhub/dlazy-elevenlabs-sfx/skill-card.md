## Description:

Generates 1–22-second sound effects from text descriptions using ElevenLabs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to turn text descriptions into short sound effects for foley, ambience, alerts, and games.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
