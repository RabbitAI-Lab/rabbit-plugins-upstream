## Description:

ElevenLabs text-to-sound model — generates 1–22s short sound effects from a description. Suitable for foley, ambience, alerts, and game SFX. ElevenLabs 文本生音效模型，根据描述生成 1–22 秒短音效。适合拟音、环境声、提示音与游戏音效。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and any submitted files are sent to dLazy's hosted service and may contain information the user does not intend to share.

Mitigation: Share only prompts or files intended for that service.

Risk: Generation may consume paid organization credits, and API credentials provide access to the organization account.

Mitigation: Use the documented dry-run option to preview requests and costs, and protect the organization API key.
