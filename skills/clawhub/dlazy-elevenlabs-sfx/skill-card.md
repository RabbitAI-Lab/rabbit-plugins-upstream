## Description:

Generates 1–22 second sound effects from text descriptions for foley, ambience, alerts, and game audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to generate short sound effects from text prompts for foley, ambience, alerts, and games.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary notes that the documented dLazy login or API-key setup does not fully match the sound-generation purpose; the scan is partial and has medium confidence.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm that you expect to sign in to dLazy and send prompts to its API, keep the API key private, and rotate or revoke it if needed.
