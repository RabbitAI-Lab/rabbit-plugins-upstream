## Description: <br>
Audits a user's current AI tool stack, scores each tool by ROI, identifies redundancies and gaps, and produces a structured report with waste analysis and upgrade opportunities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[RogerYelvington](https://clawhub.ai/user/RogerYelvington) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and productivity-focused teams use this skill to inventory AI-tool subscriptions, score return on investment, identify redundant spend, and surface the top three missing capability categories before deciding whether to purchase a deeper stack recommendation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The audit asks users to share AI-tool subscription names, approximate costs, and usage habits. <br>
Mitigation: Share approximate, non-sensitive details and avoid account identifiers, confidential vendor terms, or private workflow data. <br>
Risk: The generated report includes a paid upsell for deeper stack recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the free-versus-paid boundary explicit so users know specific replacement recommendations are outside the free audit. <br>
Risk: Optional report export may write a local Markdown file. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the exact filename and location before writing the report. <br>
