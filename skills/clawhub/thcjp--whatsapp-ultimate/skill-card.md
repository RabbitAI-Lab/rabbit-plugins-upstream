## Use Case: <br>
Developers, teams, and automation users can use this skill to guide WhatsApp message sending, group administration, chat-history search, media preparation, and structured collaboration workflows when the relevant accounts, recipients, and groups are authorized. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can guide messaging, group-management, and chat-search actions with unclear safety boundaries. <br>
Mitigation: Review carefully before installation and restrict use to authorized WhatsApp accounts, recipients, groups, and chat-history searches. <br>
Risk: Messaging or group changes could be sent or applied to unintended recipients. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation before sending messages, reacting to messages, creating polls, searching chat history, or modifying groups. <br>
