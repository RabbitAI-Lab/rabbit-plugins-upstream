## Description: <br>
Analyze historical construction costs for benchmarking, trend analysis, and estimating calibration. Compare projects, track escalation, identify patterns. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, estimators, and construction cost teams use this skill to analyze historical project costs, normalize costs across time and location, benchmark new estimates, and identify cost drivers and overrun patterns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is intended to read user-supplied cost spreadsheets and may involve sensitive project or pricing data. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with files intended for analysis, avoid granting access to unrelated private folders when scoping is available, and review generated reports before sharing. <br>
Risk: Benchmarking can be misleading when costs are compared without normalization or with too few comparable projects. <br>
Mitigation: Normalize costs to a common base year and location, show the escalation rate used, require at least three comparable projects for reliable benchmarking, and flag confidence levels. <br>
