## Description: <br>
把模糊的学习意愿转化为明确目标、阶段计划、实践任务和可验证的学习成果。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Learners, coaches, and productivity-focused users use this skill to turn a learning goal, current ability, constraints, resources, and validation criteria into a staged learning path with concrete tasks and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include more personal learning context, constraints, or evidence than needed. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the goals, current ability, constraints, resources, and evidence intended for analysis. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or unverified inputs can lead to a premature or misleading learning plan. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's parameter status checks and treat missing, conflicting, or unverified information as a prompt for clarification before relying on the plan. <br>
