## Description:

Review a plan on its own or compare requirements, design and tasks before implementation. Surface conflicts and missing checks that would change a real outcome.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[peterflow-en](https://clawhub.ai/user/peterflow-en)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and project teams use this skill to review a plan against goals, project rules, standards, requirements, design, and existing tasks before implementation. It surfaces conflicts, traceability gaps, missing ownership or checks, and unresolved choices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A review may miss an issue or report a misleading finding if it does not trace the relevant requirements and plan through a concrete scenario.

Mitigation: Validate each candidate finding against the cited source passages and a specific scenario, and state the reviewed scope and unresolved choices.
