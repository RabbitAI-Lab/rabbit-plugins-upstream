## Description: <br>
Audit and rewrite a LinkedIn profile end-to-end for 2026 across headline, About, Featured, banner, photo, Experience, Skills, custom URL, and recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Professionals, job seekers, founders, and consultants use this skill to audit a LinkedIn profile and produce prioritized profile improvements. The agent returns section-by-section rewrites and guidance for converting resume-style profiles into clearer, goal-aligned profiles. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad prompts such as fix my headline or optimize bio could route to this skill even when the user is not asking about LinkedIn. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended platform is LinkedIn before requesting or analyzing personal profile content. <br>
Risk: Profile URLs, screenshots, draft About sections, and recommendation details can contain personal or sensitive career information. <br>
Mitigation: Ask users to redact information they do not want included and keep recommendations as drafts for user review before posting or sending. <br>
