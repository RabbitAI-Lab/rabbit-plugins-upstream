## Description: <br>
AI Voiceover helps agents plan voiceover work by choosing an appropriate voice and ElevenLabs model, rewriting copy for spoken delivery, directing performance settings, and enforcing consent and disclosure checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media and video production agents use this skill to create AI narration briefs, spoken scripts, model and voice selections, caption and localization plans, and consent-aware publishing guidance for Reels, Shorts, explainers, and dubbed videos. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Unconsented voice cloning or celebrity-style imitation can create impersonation and rights issues. <br>
Mitigation: Use only owned, licensed, or documented-consent voices, and refuse requests to clone or imitate a real person without consent. <br>
Risk: AI voice use may require disclosure, especially for ads, political content, or regulated regions. <br>
Mitigation: Plan clear AI voice disclosure before publication and follow platform and regional transparency requirements. <br>
Risk: Voice samples and cloned-voice materials may contain personal data. <br>
Mitigation: Protect voice samples as personal data and keep consent records for any licensed or third-party voice. <br>
Risk: Generated narration or localization may be inaccurate, unnatural, or unsuitable for publication without review. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human review and mixing before publishing, including checks for script quality, pronunciation, captions, and localized versions. <br>
