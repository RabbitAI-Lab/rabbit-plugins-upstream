## Description: <br>
The AI narration / voiceover mini-skill helps agents choose an AI voice and model, write scripts for spoken delivery, direct ElevenLabs generation, and plan human review, captions, localization, consent, and disclosure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media, video, and marketing teams use this skill to turn voiceover requests into compliant production briefs: voice choice, model selection, script-for-the-ear rewrites, delivery settings, captions, localization, and publish handoff. It is especially useful when requests involve ElevenLabs text-to-speech, voice cloning, dubbing, or AI voice disclosure decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Voice cloning can impersonate a real person or misuse personal voice samples. <br>
Mitigation: Use only owned, licensed, library, designed, or documented-consent voices; refuse commercial celebrity soundalikes and protect voice samples as personal data. <br>
Risk: Generated voiceover may be published without adequate disclosure or review. <br>
Mitigation: Plan AI voice disclosure where required, especially ads or political content, and require human review and mix before publication. <br>
Risk: Voiceover guidance may overstate tool scope or production readiness. <br>
Mitigation: Treat ElevenLabs as the audio generator only; keep editing, mixing, localization quality checks, analytics, scheduling, and publishing in the appropriate human or downstream workflow. <br>
