## Description:

Writes a one-page Phantra client treatment from project details, asking for missing information and never inventing claims, results, or awards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Phantra users can use this skill to turn project materials into a one-page client pitch, with missing client details surfaced as questions and unsupported claims omitted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated download link can be used by anyone who holds it until it expires, for up to seven days.

Mitigation: Review the selected clip and access window before sharing; send the link directly to the intended recipient and keep it out of the treatment text.

Risk: A treatment may include confidential project details.

Mitigation: Review the draft for confidential information before sending it to a client.

Risk: Saving a treatment template retains its text for reuse.

Mitigation: Save a template only when retaining that text is intended.
