## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill connects to remote tax services and stores local or browser API credentials. <br>
Mitigation: Review the remote-service terms before use, avoid entering confidential payroll, employee, customer, or unreleased financial details unless approved, and rotate or remove stored credentials when no longer needed. <br>
Risk: The skill can install related tax skills from ClawHub or SkillHub when matrix installation is invoked. <br>
Mitigation: Run installation only in controlled environments, verify package sources and versions, and use dry-run or restricted target directories during review. <br>
Risk: Tax guidance and calculations may be incomplete or stale for a specific filing position. <br>
Mitigation: Verify policy citations against current official tax authority guidance and qualified professional advice before filing, claiming deductions, or responding to audits. <br>
