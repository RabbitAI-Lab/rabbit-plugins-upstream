## Description:

Generates original instrumental or vocal music from prompts and style parameters, with claimed durations from 5 seconds to 10 minutes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, developers, and media teams can use this skill to draft background tracks, podcast intro or outro music, game soundtrack ideas, and vocal song concepts from natural-language prompts. It is intended for music-generation workflows where outputs still need review for quality, rights, and suitability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad read, write, and command-execution access without a clear boundary around music-generation tasks.

Mitigation: Install and run it only in a sandbox or project directory with the minimum file, command, and credential access needed for the music task.

Risk: Generated or user-supplied music prompts may involve copyrighted or otherwise restricted media.

Mitigation: Review inputs and outputs for rights, licensing, and policy suitability before using them in public or commercial work.
