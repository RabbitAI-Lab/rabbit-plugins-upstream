## Description: <br>
Transforms Mandarin input into concise, philosophical-sounding "废话文学" using repetition, circular reasoning, causal inversion, and empty modifiers while preserving the original topic. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The broad trigger phrasing may activate when a Chinese sentence appears intended for this style conversion. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for explicit nonsense-literature rewriting requests, or confirm the user's intent before applying the style conversion. <br>
