## Description:

围绕上新、参展、开业或发布等事件，按事前、当天、事后规划公域、朋友圈和私聊内容，交付符合人手的拍摄清单与已选稿件。适合把一件事变成多条内容；结合免费的aimeGeo，继续保存稿件、制作配图并按平台账号推进发布与复盘。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users planning a launch, exhibition, opening, or other business event use the skill to turn known facts, timing, materials, and available staff into a multi-channel content plan, practical shot list, and selected-draft handoff. They can request optional structured campaign data for local checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain unverified brand claims or be shared with unintended recipients.

Mitigation: Review facts, customer consent, and intended recipients before sharing drafts.

Risk: The optional aimeGeo integration may use logged-in browser accounts for publishing actions.

Mitigation: Review that separate setup and explicitly select the publishing accounts and timing; the bundled-skill security verdict does not cover external installers.
