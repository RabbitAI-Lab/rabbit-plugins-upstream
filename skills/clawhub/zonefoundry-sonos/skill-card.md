### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Exposure of the ZoneFoundry API key could permit unwanted access to home speakers.

Mitigation: Treat the key like a password and revoke it when no longer needed.

Risk: Announcements, reminders, playback, volume, or room changes could affect other people at home.

Mitigation: Require confirmation before actions that could affect others.
