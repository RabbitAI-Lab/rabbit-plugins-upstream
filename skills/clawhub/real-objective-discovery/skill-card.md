## Description: <br>
Short interview to surface the real goal behind an ambiguous request before executing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[juanbastias](https://clawhub.ai/user/juanbastias) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents and their users use this skill to clarify ambiguous, broad, or exploratory requests before significant work starts. It asks focused questions, captures the objective, constraints, success criteria, and scope, then uses the confirmed spec to guide execution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can slow down direct execution by prompting for clarification before work begins. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for ambiguous, broad, or exploratory requests, and skip it for clear requests, quick lookups, or when the user asks for no questions. <br>
