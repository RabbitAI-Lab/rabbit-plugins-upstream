## Description: <br>
FlowDream AI Novel Writer helps agents plan, draft, continue, polish, and review long-form fiction using story bibles, outline anchors, foreshadowing trackers, chapter checklists, and quality scoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aitoim](https://clawhub.ai/user/aitoim) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External writers and agent users use this skill to develop novels, serialized fiction, and long-form stories. It guides agents through story bible creation, outline design, chapter-by-chapter drafting, continuity checks, prose polishing, writer's-block recovery, and chapter quality review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad writing-related phrases and steer general writing requests into its fiction workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants novel or long-form fiction assistance before creating story assets or applying the full workflow. <br>
Risk: The skill may read or update local story files such as story-bible.md when continuing or reviewing a story. <br>
Mitigation: Review the current project files before use and ask for confirmation before modifying persistent story notes, outlines, or chapter summaries. <br>
