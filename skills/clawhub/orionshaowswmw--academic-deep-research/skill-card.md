## Description:

Transparent, rigorous research with full methodology, mandated two-cycle investigation per theme, APA 7th citations, evidence hierarchy, and user checkpoints for literature reviews, competitive intelligence, and reproducible deep research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orionshaowswmw](https://clawhub.ai/user/orionshaowswmw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, developers, and other external users use this skill to plan and conduct exhaustive multi-source investigations that require visible methodology, source verification, contradiction handling, and academic-style final reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad web and memory-assisted research may exceed the intended topic scope or use unwanted source types if boundaries are unclear.

Mitigation: Before approving the main research phase, define topic scope, source types, time period, geography, and whether prior memory may be used.

Risk: Research outputs can still be misleading if cited sources, confidence levels, limitations, or unresolved contradictions are not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review the cited sources, confidence annotations, limitations section, and contradiction analysis before relying on the final report.
