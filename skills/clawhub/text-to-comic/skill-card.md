## Description:

Turns text, dialogue, diary entries, photo notes, and knowledge notes into comics, picture books, infographics, or hybrid pages through reviewable storyboards and panel-by-panel rendering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Creators and readers use the skill to turn existing stories, dialogue, diary entries, notes, or concepts into a comic, picture book, or infographic, then revise the storyboard or individual panels before final assembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language defaults may not suit the intended audience.

Mitigation: Review the dialogue language and text before rendering or sharing the finished page.

Risk: The security review was partial, and image files were outside the static analyzers' scope.

Mitigation: Review included image assets and generated pages before reusing or sharing them.
