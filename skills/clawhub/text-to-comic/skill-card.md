## Description:

Turns user-provided stories, dialogue, notes, and concepts into storyboards and panel-by-panel comics, picture books, or infographics using style presets and consistent characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and other users turn their own stories, dialogue, diary entries, and educational notes into editable storyboards and illustrated comics or infographics. They can revise a single panel or switch styles without rebuilding the storyboard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Storyboards, prompts, images, and optional custom character cards may be saved locally or processed by an image-generation tool.

Mitigation: Avoid private photos and sensitive personal material unless comfortable with local artifacts and image-generation processing.
