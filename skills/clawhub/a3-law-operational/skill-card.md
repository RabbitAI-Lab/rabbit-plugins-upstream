## Description:

Provides an A³ governance checklist for reviewing AI creation, agent modifications, and other high-impact autonomous actions before and after they occur.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and AI-agent operators use this document-based checklist to assess the intent, impact, reversibility, and oversight of proposed AI changes or consequential autonomous actions, then decide whether to escalate, reject, or proceed with review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests write/edit and web-capable tools beyond its stated document-only purpose.

Mitigation: Review granted tools before use and prefer running without network or write/edit permissions.

Risk: A broadly triggered governance checklist may be mistaken for an enforceable approval gate.

Mitigation: Treat its assessment as advisory, narrow the trigger scope, and require human review for high-impact actions.
