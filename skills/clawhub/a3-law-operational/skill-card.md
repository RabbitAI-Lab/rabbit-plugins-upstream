## Description:

Provides an operational framework for reviewing AI creation, self-modification, and high-impact autonomous actions before they proceed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

All rights reserved

## Use Case:

Developers and governance reviewers use this skill to identify actions needing human review, assess intent, impact, reversibility, and oversight, and document escalation or refusal decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke governance review outside the user's intended task.

Mitigation: Limit activation to explicit AI creation, self-modification, or high-impact autonomy review requests.

Risk: Governance guidance could be mistaken for binding legal or safety standards, or lead to unrequested edits.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as review aids and require explicit user direction before drafting or editing files.
