## Description:

Operationalizes the A3 Laws into governance rules for evaluating AI-created agents, AI self-modification, and high-impact autonomous actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, governance reviewers, and agent operators use this skill to decide when AI self-modification, agent generation, deployment, or other high-impact autonomous actions require A3 review. It provides operational guidance for trigger assessment, pre-action review, human oversight, and post-action review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger keywords may load the skill during unrelated conversations about law or operations.

Mitigation: Confirm the request involves AI self-modification, AI-created agents, or high-impact autonomous actions before relying on its guidance.

Risk: Governance guidance may be mistaken for legal or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as operational review guidance and verify compliance decisions with qualified reviewers for the relevant jurisdiction.
