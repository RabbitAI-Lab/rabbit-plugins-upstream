## Description:

Provides Chinese-language career planning with interest and values assessments, AI-impact ratings, and personalized career recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring careers, education choices, or career changes use this skill to assess their interests and values and receive a personalized plan with possible paths, AI-impact ratings, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures, AI-impact ratings, and insurance-company suggestions may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat them as advisory; check current job-market data and verify company credentials before acting.

Risk: Optional export, email, subscriptions, and profile tracking can save or share personal career information.

Mitigation: Enable these features only on explicit user request and confirm what information will be saved or sent.
