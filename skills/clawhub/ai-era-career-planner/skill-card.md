## Description:

A Chinese-language career-planning assistant that uses interest and values assessments, local salary and industry references, and AI-impact estimates to create personalized career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring education choices, career changes, or work in an AI-affected job market use this assistant for guided self-assessment, career options, and actionable plans. Its primary guidance and salary data focus on China.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (primary market; includes optional overseas career references)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance-industry guidance deliberately prioritizes one company.

Mitigation: Compare insurers independently and confirm their qualifications before contacting them.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, and live-search features can send or retain personal career information.

Mitigation: Enable these features only when explicitly requested and confirm each external action.

Risk: Local salary figures and AI-impact forecasts may not reflect current conditions.

Mitigation: Check salary ranges, hiring trends, and career assumptions against current independent sources before acting.
