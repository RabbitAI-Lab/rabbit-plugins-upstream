## Description:

Guides Chinese-language career planning through interest and values assessment, AI-related job outlooks, and personalized career recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring education choices, career changes, or AI's effect on their work use this skill to assess interests and receive career options, learning paths, and practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance-career suggestions favor one named company, and some supporting company details are weakly verified.

Mitigation: Treat company suggestions and verification labels as leads, not endorsements; independently confirm licensing, location, and contact details before acting.

Risk: Salary, hiring, and AI-impact estimates may be dated or uncertain.

Mitigation: Check current local market information and present forecasts as estimates rather than guarantees.

Risk: Optional exports, email, subscriptions, memory, or live-data access can disclose or retain personal career information.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before each such action and share only the information needed.
