## Description:

Helps users assess career interests and values, consider AI’s impact on work, and develop a personalized career plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users exploring career options, choosing a field of study, or considering a career transition use the skill to receive personalized recommendations and practical next steps based on their background, preferences, and AI-related career changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bundled exporter tests can erase saved reports, and export safeguards are weaker than advertised.

Mitigation: Prefer offline conversation mode until safeguards are corrected. Do not run exporter tests against an installation containing reports; back up reports first, enforce exports-only host permissions, and approve storage or external sending explicitly.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations may need independent verification.

Mitigation: Independently compare the recommendations before relying on them.
