## Description:

Provides Chinese-language career planning with interest and values assessments, AI impact ratings, career options, and practical next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring education, careers, or job transitions use this skill to discuss their interests and goals, compare potential paths in an AI-changing job market, and receive a personalized action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures and insurance-company recommendations may not fit an individual's circumstances or remain current.

Mitigation: Independently verify pay data, company credentials, and recommendations before making career or financial decisions.

Risk: Career planning can involve personal education, goals, preferences, and location; optional integrations could share or retain that information.

Mitigation: Keep email, subscriptions, memory, and live search disabled unless the user explicitly requests them.
