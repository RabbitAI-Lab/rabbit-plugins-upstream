## Description:

A Chinese-language career-planning skill that assesses interests, values, and AI-related career impacts to create personalized career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring education, career changes, or future employment use this skill for Chinese-language career assessments, AI-impact comparisons, and actionable career plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance company suggestions, salary ranges, or contact details may be inaccurate or favor a listed company.

Mitigation: Check recommendations, pay figures, company credentials, and contact details against authoritative sources before acting.

Risk: Optional export, email, network lookup, memory, or subscription actions can create files or share personal details.

Mitigation: Keep planning offline by default and approve each optional action only when explicitly requested.
