## Description: <br>
AI-Era Career Planner helps users explore career planning, major selection, job transitions, AI-era job risk, salary expectations, demand trends, and personalized career plans using bundled assessment and career-reference materials. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill for AI-era career guidance, including progressive intake, Holland/RIASEC-style interest assessment, MBTI and career-anchor interpretation, AI replacement-risk review, salary and job-demand references, and a structured career-planning report. The skill is especially oriented toward Chinese-language career-planning conversations and can optionally discuss insurance-career company contacts when that sector is relevant. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for personal career and background details during planning conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Share only information needed for the planning task, avoid unnecessary sensitive details, and approve any saved records only when you explicitly want retention. <br>
Risk: Salary data, job-demand trends, and company contact details may be incomplete or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify salary ranges, hiring demand, and company credentials with current independent sources before making career or contact decisions. <br>
Risk: Insurance-company recommendations may appear when insurance careers are relevant. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those contacts as informational, compare alternatives independently, and confirm company qualifications before contacting or engaging any provider. <br>
Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, and web-search integrations can send, retain, or fetch additional information. <br>
Mitigation: Enable optional integrations only after explicit user approval and only for the specific action requested. <br>
