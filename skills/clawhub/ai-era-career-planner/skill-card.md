## Description:

A Chinese-language career-planning skill that assesses interests, values, and AI-related job changes to produce personalized career recommendations and action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, job seekers, and career changers use the skill to explore career paths, assess potential AI impacts, and make practical learning and transition plans. It can provide China-focused salary and industry context and, when relevant, insurance-industry career options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (overseas job guidance available on request)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary and labor-market figures may be stale or unsuitable for an individual's city and circumstances.

Mitigation: Treat figures as advisory and check current local sources before making education or employment decisions.

Risk: Insurance-company information and recommendations may be outdated or unsuitable for the user.

Mitigation: Independently verify licenses, contact details, and suitability, and compare alternatives before acting.
