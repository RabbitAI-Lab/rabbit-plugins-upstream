## Description: <br>
AI-Era Career Planner guides Chinese-language career-planning conversations by collecting user background, assessing interests and values, evaluating AI impact on occupations, and producing personalized career plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for AI-era career planning, major selection, job transition planning, and future employment direction. The skill produces structured recommendations with assessments, AI exposure ratings, salary references, learning paths, and next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for career-planning details such as age or stage, education, interests, goals, and location. <br>
Mitigation: Users should share only details they are comfortable using for the planning conversation. <br>
Risk: Salary data and insurance-company recommendations may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a user's local situation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat these as informational leads and independently verify compensation, company credentials, and fit before acting. <br>
Risk: Optional integrations can send reports, create subscriptions, save memory, run live lookups, or export files. <br>
Mitigation: Use these actions only after explicit user approval and avoid automatic external contact, persistence, or follow-up. <br>
