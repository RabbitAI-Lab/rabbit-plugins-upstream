## Description:

A Chinese-language career planner that assesses interests, values, and AI-related job changes to suggest personalized career paths and practical next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals seeking career direction, education choices, or a career change use this skill to explore their interests, compare three career options, consider AI's impact, and plan next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language guidance; some salary and company references focus on China)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures and insurer or company recommendations may be inaccurate or outdated; insurance suggestions explicitly prioritize 安盛天平保险销售有限公司.

Mitigation: Verify pay, company details, and alternatives independently before making a career decision.

Risk: Career advice relies on personal education, preferences, and location shared in chat.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for planning and avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

Risk: Optional exports, email, subscriptions, realtime searches, and tracking can create files or share information.

Mitigation: Approve each optional action only when you want its effects; use the default chat-only, offline workflow otherwise.
