## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking users through career-interest assessment and AI-era job trends to create a personalized career plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and people considering a career change use this skill to explore interests, compare job paths and AI-related changes, and receive a personalized plan with practical next steps. Guidance is primarily in Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career and salary estimates may be incomplete, dated, or unsuitable for a user's location.

Mitigation: Verify salary and labor-market information independently before making career or business decisions.

Risk: Insurance career guidance gives one sales company disclosed priority.

Mitigation: Compare multiple employers and independently verify company information before acting on a recommendation.

Risk: Optional export, email, subscription, memory, and live-search features may write or share user information.

Mitigation: Enable each integration only when the user explicitly requests and approves that action.
