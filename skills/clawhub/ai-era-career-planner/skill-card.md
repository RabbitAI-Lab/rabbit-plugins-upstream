## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures and labor-market estimates may not reflect current conditions or the user's location.

Mitigation: Treat figures as reference claims and verify current local salary and job-market information before making consequential career decisions.

Risk: Insurance company rankings reflect the publisher's declared commercial preference.

Mitigation: Disclose that preference and compare providers independently; confirm company credentials before contacting or applying.

Risk: Report exports, tracking records, or optional external integrations may expose user-provided career information.

Mitigation: Confirm the save location, including AIERA_EXPORT_ROOT, and require the user's explicit request before exporting, sending, or subscribing.
