## Description:

Provides Chinese-language career planning based on interests, values, salary references, and the potential impact of AI on jobs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and people considering a career change use this skill to explore career options, assess AI-related job changes, and develop an actionable plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused, with selected overseas career references

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some insurance-company records appear verified despite unrelated supporting sources.

Mitigation: Treat insurer recommendations as unverified leads and confirm company details with regulators or the companies before relying on them.

Risk: Career-planning conversations collect personal career information, and optional integrations can share reports externally.

Mitigation: Share only necessary personal details and authorize any email, subscription, or live-data integration explicitly.
