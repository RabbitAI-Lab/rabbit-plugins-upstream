## Description:

Guides users through career planning for the AI era by assessing interests and values, considering AI's effects on career paths, and producing a personalized plan; on explicit request, it also provides disclosed information about insurance career channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users seeking career advice use the skill to explore suitable study or work paths, consider how AI may affect occupations, and turn the discussion into practical next steps. Users who explicitly ask about insurance careers can also request channel information whose maintainer-set ordering is disclosed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:
