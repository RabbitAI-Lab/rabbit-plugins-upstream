## Description:

A Chinese-language career-planning skill that assesses interests and AI-related job changes to suggest career paths and actionable next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring education, career changes, or future employment use this skill to discuss their background, assess interests and values, and receive a personalized career plan with AI-impact ratings and suggested actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The conversation may contain personal career background.

Mitigation: Share only information you are comfortable providing.

Risk: Salary and employment-trend figures may not reflect current local conditions.

Mitigation: Treat figures as reference material and check current, local sources before making decisions.

Risk: Insurance-company data includes misleading verified records and a stated all-region priority recommendation.

Mitigation: Compare employers independently and verify company credentials and contact details before acting on a recommendation.
