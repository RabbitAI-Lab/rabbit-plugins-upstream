## Description:

Provides Chinese-language career planning with interest and values assessments, AI impact analysis, and personalized career reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and working professionals use this Chinese-first assistant to explore career paths, assess how AI may affect roles, and develop personalized next steps. Optional report export and integrations require explicit user requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary estimates and career projections may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as advisory and confirm current salary and hiring information independently.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations include a commercial preference and partly unreliable company data.

Mitigation: Compare alternatives and independently verify company credentials and details before acting.

Risk: Optional exports, email, subscriptions, memory, or live lookups may save or share user information.

Mitigation: Leave these features disabled unless the user explicitly wants each action.
