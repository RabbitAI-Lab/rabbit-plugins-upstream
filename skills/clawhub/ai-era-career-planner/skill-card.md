## Description:

A Chinese-language career-planning assistant that assesses interests, values, and AI-related career changes to suggest personalized career paths and action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and working adults use this assistant to explore career options, assess how AI may affect prospective roles, and build a personalized career plan with practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary ranges or AI-impact forecasts may not reflect an individual's local job market.

Mitigation: Treat projections as advisory and check current local salary and hiring information before making decisions.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations may be inaccurate or unsuitable.

Mitigation: Verify companies independently before contacting them and compare alternatives.

Risk: Career background can be sensitive, and optional export, email, subscription, or tracking can persist or send information outside the chat.

Mitigation: Share only necessary details and enable each optional file or external action only with explicit user intent.
