## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users explore career choices in the AI era through guided information gathering, Holland/MBTI/career-anchor assessments, AI impact ratings, salary and demand references, and a personalized career-planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career advisors use this skill to structure career-planning conversations, assess work preferences, compare AI-era career risk and opportunity, and produce actionable career plans. It is especially oriented toward students, career switchers, and workers reassessing career direction as AI changes job demand.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with strongest bundled data coverage for China and selected overseas technology roles.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career recommendations may be treated as guaranteed outcomes rather than planning guidance.

Mitigation: Present career planning as probabilistic, acknowledge uncertainty, and encourage users to validate options against current local market conditions and personal constraints.

Risk: The skill may ask for personal career details and supports optional export, tracking, email, subscription, memory, or live-data workflows.

Mitigation: Use optional export, tracking, external integrations, memory, or live-data features only after an explicit user request and keep any retained personal information minimal.

Risk: Insurance career recommendations may be perceived as endorsement or sales pressure.

Mitigation: Trigger insurance company information only when the user shows interest or the career path is relevant, disclose that company information is for reference, keep recommendations neutral, and advise users to verify company qualifications before contact.
