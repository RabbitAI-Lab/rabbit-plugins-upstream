## Description:

Helps users plan careers in the AI era through guided profile collection, Holland interest and work-value assessments, AI impact ratings, and a personalized career report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users seeking career or education choices, a career transition, or guidance on how AI may affect their work can use this agent for a personalized plan. It draws on career assessments and bundled labor-market references to suggest paths and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags report destinations and profile storage as concerns.

Mitigation: Before enabling any save or export feature, review where information will be stored and share only the personal details needed for the plan.

Risk: The insurer guidance includes a fixed preferred provider.

Mitigation: Make the preference clear, compare other providers, and verify current credentials and coverage before acting on a recommendation.

Risk: The salary database was only partly analyzed by the security scan.

Mitigation: Treat salary and demand figures as reference estimates and check current local sources before making career or financial decisions.
