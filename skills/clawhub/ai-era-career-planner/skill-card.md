## Description:

Provides personalized career planning for the AI era, including interest and values assessments, career impact guidance, and an actionable career report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People considering a career choice, education path, or career transition use the skill to reflect on their interests and values, understand possible AI impacts, and receive practical next steps. It is designed for Chinese-language career-planning conversations and also includes overseas job references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures and AI impact ratings are estimates and may vary by location or change over time.

Mitigation: Present figures as estimates and encourage checking current local sources before making career decisions.

Risk: Insurance company ordering reflects a disclosed maintainer preference, not an independent endorsement.

Mitigation: Disclose the preference, offer comparisons when requested, and encourage users to confirm company credentials and compare options.

Risk: Exported reports may contain sensitive personal information, and storage policy details may need attention.

Mitigation: Keep the default workflow offline and save reports only to a private directory the user approves, with host write permissions aligned to that location.
