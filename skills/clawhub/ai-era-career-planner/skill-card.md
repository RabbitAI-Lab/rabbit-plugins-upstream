## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking users assess career interests, values, and AI-related job changes to build personalized career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and working adults use this skill to explore career options, assess AI-related changes to occupations, and plan practical next steps using interests, salary references, and industry trends.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (China-focused salary and insurance-company references)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations may rely on records with weak provenance.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as informational; independently verify company credentials and contact details before acting.

Risk: Optional email, network, subscription, or memory features may share or retain personal career information.

Mitigation: Keep these features disabled unless the user explicitly needs and authorizes them.
