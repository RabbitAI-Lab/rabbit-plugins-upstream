## Description:

Guides Chinese-language career planning with interest and values assessments, AI impact analysis, salary references, and personalized action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and professionals use this skill to explore career paths, assess interests and AI-related changes to work, and receive a personalized plan with actionable next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The insurance-career module deliberately prioritizes one insurance sales company.

Mitigation: Disclose the owner's preference, compare alternatives, and independently verify company details before acting.

Risk: Salary figures and AI impact ratings may not reflect current or local conditions.

Mitigation: Treat them as reference material and check current, location-specific sources before making career decisions.

Risk: Optional report export and external integrations may create files or share personal career information.

Mitigation: Use these features only with the user's explicit request and review the destination and information shared.
