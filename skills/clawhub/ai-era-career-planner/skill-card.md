## Description:

A Chinese-first career-planning skill that uses interest and values assessments, AI-impact ratings, and career data to suggest personalized paths and action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals exploring education choices, career changes, or job options use this skill for Chinese-first career assessments, AI-impact comparisons, and actionable career plans. An optional report export creates a local Markdown file only when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures and career outlook may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Independently verify salary ranges and local job-market conditions before making career decisions.

Risk: Some insurance company references have poor source matching, and the skill owner prioritizes one company.

Mitigation: Check company credentials and recommendations independently, and compare alternatives before acting.

Risk: Exported reports may contain personal career details.

Mitigation: Request local export only when needed and review the report and destination before sharing.
