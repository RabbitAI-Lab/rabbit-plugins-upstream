## Description:

Provides Chinese-language career planning with interest and values assessments, AI-related career impact estimates, salary references, and personalized action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Helps students and career changers explore career options through a guided discussion of interests, values, education, and AI-related job changes. Produces a personalized plan with three career directions, skill-building suggestions, and near-term actions; insurance-company suggestions may appear when relevant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language guidance; many salary and insurance references focus on China)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations include misleading verification records and a preferred company.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as biased reference material, not independent verification or financial advice; independently check company credentials before contacting one.

Risk: Career planning involves sharing personal background in chat.

Mitigation: Share only career details you are comfortable disclosing and review the plan before acting on it.
