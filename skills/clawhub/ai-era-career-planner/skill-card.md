## Description:

Provides Chinese-language career planning with interest and values assessments, AI-related career impact analysis, and personalized action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and working adults use this skill to explore career options, assess AI-related job changes, and develop personalized career plans. It can also suggest insurance-sector employers when the user expresses interest in that field.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (primarily Chinese-language guidance with China-focused career and salary references)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The disclosed insurance referral flow favors a particular company.

Mitigation: Compare alternatives and independently verify company details and suitability before acting.

Risk: Salary, job-market, and insurer reference details may be incomplete or out of date.

Mitigation: Treat them as reference material and confirm consequential decisions against current independent sources.

Risk: Optional export, email, network, subscription, or memory actions can store or share personal career information.

Mitigation: Authorize only the specific actions needed, and check the data and recipients first.
