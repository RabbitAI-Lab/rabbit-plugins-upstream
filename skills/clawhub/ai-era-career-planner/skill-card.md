## Description:

Helps people explore career choices and AI-related job changes through interest assessments, labor-market references, and personalized career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and workers use this skill to assess interests and career values, compare job paths and salary ranges, and develop a personalized plan that considers AI-driven changes to work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Packaged salary and career data may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Check current, location-specific salaries and job openings before making career decisions.

Risk: Insurance-company suggestions include a disclosed business-priority recommendation.

Mitigation: Compare alternatives independently and verify company credentials and contact details before reaching out.

Risk: Optional report export or integrations can store or share personal career information.

Mitigation: Export, send, or retain a report only with the user's explicit permission.
