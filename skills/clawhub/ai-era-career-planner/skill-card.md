## Description:

Provides personalized career planning that combines interest and values assessments with AI impact, industry, and salary guidance, with an optional insurance-channel information module.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users considering study choices, career planning, or a career transition use the skill to explore suitable directions and receive a personalized action plan. Users may separately request insurance-channel information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill’s reports are primarily Chinese-language, which may not suit every user.

Mitigation: Confirm the preferred language; the skill documentation says English reports can be provided on request.

Risk: The insurance-channel list uses a maintainer-selected commercial preference for its ordering, which could be mistaken for a personalized or objective ranking.

Mitigation: Keep the preference disclosure with the list and offer side-by-side comparisons when the user asks.
