## Description: <br>
AI Era Career Planner helps users plan careers for an AI-shaped labor market by collecting career context, assessing interests and values, rating AI impact risk, and producing a personalized career planning report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for career planning, major selection, career transitions, and AI-era job-risk analysis. The skill produces structured recommendations, learning paths, salary and demand context, and next actions based on the user's stated stage, interests, values, and constraints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Career, salary, job-demand, and insurance contact details may be incomplete, outdated, or context-dependent. <br>
Mitigation: Verify salary data, demand claims, credentials, and company contact information with current authoritative sources before making career, education, or employment decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for personal education, career, location, and preference details to personalize recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the planning task and avoid providing sensitive personal data that is not necessary for the recommendation. <br>
Risk: Optional report export, tracking records, email sending, subscriptions, and live-data lookups can disclose or persist user information. <br>
Mitigation: Approve these optional actions only when specifically desired, review the destination or storage location, and keep the default offline workflow when persistence or external sharing is not needed. <br>
