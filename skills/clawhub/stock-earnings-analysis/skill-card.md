## Description:

Provides read-only, fiscal-quarter-organized earnings analysis for US stocks, including reported results, guidance, call summaries, filing changes, upcoming reports, and historical price reactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, analysts, and other readers use this skill to review US company earnings by fiscal quarter, compare guidance and filing changes, and prepare dated earnings previews or weekly summaries. Its output is research, not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The API key and requested stock tickers are sent to SentiSense.

Mitigation: Confirm that use of the key and sharing ticker and request metadata with SentiSense are acceptable before installing; grant no broader file, shell, account, wallet, or trading permissions.

Risk: Earnings summaries and signals may be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat results as research, check dated claims against cited sources, and do not use the skill to make trades.
