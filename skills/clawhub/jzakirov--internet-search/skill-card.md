### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries can disclose secrets, private documents, or sensitive personal details to the configured search service or upstream routes.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive information in queries unless the configured SearXNG instance and upstream routing are approved for that data.

Risk: Web, news, academic, or social search results may be incomplete, stale, biased, or misleading.

Mitigation: Use focused searches, compare sources when stakes are meaningful, and verify important claims before acting on them.
