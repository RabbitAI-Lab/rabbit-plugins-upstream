## Description:

Helps agents record durable decisions, findings, and facts in an existing wiki with focused edits, source citations, links, and change notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dexio](https://clawhub.ai/user/dexio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and agents maintaining a shared wiki use this skill to preserve decisions and reusable findings without duplicating pages or overwriting existing content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive information could become visible to everyone with access to the wiki.

Mitigation: Record only shareable information; omit secrets and private personal details.

Risk: Unverified or outdated claims could persist as authoritative wiki content.

Mitigation: Cite and date claims, label uncertain information, and review conflicting sources before updating a page.
