## Description:

Creates tailored CVs, cover letters, and plain-text applications from structured profile and job data, with optional local PDF rendering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[clawjames1](https://clawhub.ai/user/clawjames1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and their assistants use this skill to draft truthful, role-specific application documents from a candidate profile and a job posting. Sending or submitting those documents requires separate authorization for each destination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Application documents can expose personal profile and contact data.

Mitigation: Keep real profiles and outputs in private directories outside the installed skill, and attach only requested final documents.

Risk: Generated documents may contain inaccurate claims or formatting errors.

Mitigation: Ground claims in the supplied profile, keep final-document validation enabled, and review the files before use.

Risk: Delivery can disclose documents to an unintended recipient.

Mitigation: Authorize each chat delivery or application submission for its specific destination before sending.
