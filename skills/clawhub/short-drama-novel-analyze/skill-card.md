## Description:

将长篇小说或连载作品整理为可追溯的章节分析、改编价值评估和短剧分集候选，供创作者审阅和取舍。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

创作者和改编团队使用本技能分析有权处理的长篇原著，追踪章节与人物的戏剧功能，评估短剧改编价值并提出分集候选；最终改编方案由创作者决定。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A local copy of a licensed or sensitive manuscript and derived analysis may remain in the workspace.

Mitigation: Process only manuscripts you are authorized to use, restrict access to the workspace, and delete the copied input when retention is unnecessary.
