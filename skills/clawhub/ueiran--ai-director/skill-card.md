## Description: <br>
This skill helps with film and video directing tasks, including storyboard design, shot planning, dialogue staging, camera setup, continuity checking, editing rhythm, and pre-visualization. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ueiran](https://clawhub.ai/user/ueiran) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, directors, and film/video production teams use this skill to turn scripts or scene descriptions into practical directing artifacts such as scene analysis, storyboards, camera plans, continuity checks, editing plans, concept prompts, and production shot lists. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for scripts, scene details, or uploaded images that contain sensitive production material. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only project materials that are approved for analysis in the target agent environment. <br>
Risk: Creative directing guidance, continuity checks, or shot plans may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific production. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans with the director or production team before using them on set. <br>
Risk: The skill may default to Chinese output when language preference is unclear. <br>
Mitigation: Specify the desired response language when using the skill in multilingual workflows. <br>
