## Description: <br>
Carousel Writer helps agents draft swipeable social carousel posts for Instagram, LinkedIn document carousels, and TikTok photo carousels, including slide copy, design direction, image prompts, caption handoff, and scheduling handoff. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External content teams, social media operators, and agents use this skill to turn an idea or source material into a platform-fit carousel with concise slide text, visual direction, and publishing handoffs. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brand-profile or voice inputs may contain information that should not appear in generated marketing copy. <br>
Mitigation: Use sanitized brand and voice inputs, then review the carousel and caption before publishing. <br>
Risk: Scheduling handoffs could lead to publication before a human checks the final post. <br>
Mitigation: Review slide text, visuals, caption, links, and queue settings before accepting any scheduling handoff. <br>
Risk: AI-generated slide images may render text inaccurately or require platform disclosure. <br>
Mitigation: Check generated visuals for text accuracy, apply required AI disclosures or Content Credentials, or use a Canva/Figma template when exact text matters. <br>
Risk: Marketing slides can overstate claims or include unsupported statistics. <br>
Mitigation: Use only verified claims from approved sources and remove unsourced numbers before publishing. <br>
