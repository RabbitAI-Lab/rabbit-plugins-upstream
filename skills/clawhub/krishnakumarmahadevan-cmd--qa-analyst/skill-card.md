## Description:

Professional entry-level quality assurance career roadmap platform that generates personalized learning paths based on experience, skills, and goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, entry-level QA professionals, career switchers, HR teams, and organizations use this skill to generate personalized QA learning roadmaps from assessment data covering experience, skills, and goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment details, session identifiers, timestamps, and optional user IDs are sent to a third-party API.

Mitigation: Submit only data needed for the roadmap and confirm the provider's privacy and retention practices before using sensitive or personal information.

Risk: Generated QA career roadmaps may be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's role, local market, or organization.

Mitigation: Review roadmap recommendations against trusted QA training resources, organizational requirements, and current career guidance before relying on them for decisions.
