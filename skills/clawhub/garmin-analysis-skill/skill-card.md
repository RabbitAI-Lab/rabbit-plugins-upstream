## Description: <br>
This bilingual skill guides agents to use the public cycling-health CLI for Garmin, Intervals.icu, iGPSPORT, Strava, and Xingzhe cycling and wellness sync, query, and analysis workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[baijian](https://clawhub.ai/user/baijian) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Cyclists, coaches, and assistants use this skill to synchronize Garmin China Wellness data with Intervals.icu, mirror Garmin China activities to Garmin Global, and analyze cycling, recovery, power, route, and FIT/GPX data across supported services. It is intended for source-labeled personal activity and wellness workflows where credential handling stays local and writes are previewed before confirmation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
