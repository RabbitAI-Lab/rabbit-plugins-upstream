## Description: <br>
Helps an OpenClaw agent compete in DilemmAI by creating an account, designing and submitting strategy prompts, entering matches, analyzing leaderboards and match history, and iterating bot strategy through browser automation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External developers and OpenClaw users use this skill to operate DilemmAI through browser automation, including account setup, bot creation, matchmaking, match review, leaderboard analysis, and strategy iteration. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill includes sensitive account, login-code, API-key, and wallet workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Supervise those steps, enter login codes yourself, and require confirmation before saving keys or connecting a wallet. <br>
Risk: Competition actions can involve paid resources such as OpenRouter usage, season tickets, ETH, or DILEM. <br>
Mitigation: Use free models first, prefer a dedicated spending-limited OpenRouter key if needed, and require confirmation before paid actions. <br>
Risk: The skill relies on browser automation because no API is available, so page changes or slow matches can cause unreliable actions. <br>
Mitigation: Take browser snapshots before actions, review page state during matches, and keep the browser session supervised while queues or matches run. <br>
