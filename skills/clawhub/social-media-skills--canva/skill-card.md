## Description: <br>
The Canva craft skill helps agents produce on-brand social graphics, carousels, quote cards, infographics, and thumbnails in Canva at scale using the SCALE framework, with human approval for every visual and publishing handed off to WoopSocial. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external social media teams, and marketing operators use this skill to design, resize, batch-create, QA, and export branded Canva assets while keeping final visual approval with a human. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Connected Canva or WoopSocial tools may operate on external design and publishing accounts. <br>
Mitigation: Review connected Canva and WoopSocial permissions before use and keep human approval enabled for every visual before export or publishing. <br>
Risk: Canva features, plan gates, and AI credit claims can change quickly. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current Canva feature availability and credit details in-app, as the skill guidance recommends. <br>
Risk: Design outputs can create IP, likeness, disclosure, or accessibility issues if asset rights and visual checks are skipped. <br>
Mitigation: Use licensed assets, avoid watermark stripping, brand impersonation, and unpermitted likenesses, apply required AI disclosure, and hold the accessibility floor before export. <br>
