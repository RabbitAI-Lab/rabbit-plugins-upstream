## Description: <br>
Canva helps agents plan and produce on-brand Canva social graphics at scale, using Brand Kit, template customization, Bulk Create, Magic Resize, and human approval before publishing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media teams, creators, and marketing operators use this skill to turn brand systems and copy into Canva-ready design specs, batches, resized variants, and publishing handoffs while preserving licensing, accessibility, and approval checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Connector-assisted Canva actions or publishing handoffs could affect real brand assets or public social content. <br>
Mitigation: Operate Canva only when the connector or API is actually connected, keep the documented human approval step for every visual, and hand finished exports into the publishing workflow only after review. <br>
Risk: Brand assets, Canva templates, AI imagery, or real-person likenesses may carry licensing, disclosure, or permission requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Verify asset and template licenses before use, avoid watermark removal, impersonation, and unpermitted likenesses, and apply AI disclosure where required. <br>
Risk: Canva features, plan gates, regional availability, and credit allowances change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current feature availability and credit claims in Canva before relying on them, especially for Magic Studio and connector-driven workflows. <br>
