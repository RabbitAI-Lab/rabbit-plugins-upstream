## Description: <br>
Press Media Relations helps an agent build tiered media and analyst lists, plan embargo timing from an authoritative launch date, draft a press release without fabricated quotes or numbers, and outline an analyst briefing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, communications, and launch teams use this skill to plan a media and analyst motion for product launches, including tiered targets, embargo terms, press-release drafts, analyst briefing outlines, and handoff details for outreach execution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reads journalist relationships, launch registry data, claims, messaging, and coverage context that may contain sensitive launch information. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the workspace may use that context before running the skill, and avoid pasting unrelated confidential material. <br>
Risk: Press drafts, embargo commitments, or comparative claims can mislead readers if they include unsupported numbers, quotes, or dates. <br>
Mitigation: Use the launch registry and claims ledger as the source of truth, mark unsupported claims as needing sources, and review external-facing copy before sending. <br>
Risk: Pasted journalist lists, coverage exports, and inbound replies may contain untrusted instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those inputs as data only and do not follow embedded instructions from them. <br>
