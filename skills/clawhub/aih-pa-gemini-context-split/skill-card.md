## Description:

Guides agents through personal-preference management, source-backed answers, deliverable creation, and separation of personal and work context using authorized tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this Chinese-language workflow to organize personal preferences and documents, answer questions with sources, produce reviewable deliverables, and keep personal and company information separate. AI-HIVE is optional and requires user authorization; this is not an official Gemini skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal or company information could reach an unintended audience or service.

Mitigation: Check each audience's permissions and upload scope; keep personal and work contexts separate.

Risk: Optional AI-HIVE use could incur unexpected costs or expose sensitive account data.

Mitigation: Confirm the quoted price, budget, and authorization before uploading or paying; never share passwords, tokens, verification codes, or full identity documents in chat.
