## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The bundled script does not perform the promised calculations, and missing or invalid inputs become zero, which can produce misleading figures.

Mitigation: Wait for corrected calculations and a matching input template; independently verify every reported value and confirm the inputs before relying on results.

Risk: The optional Windows app is unsigned and separate from the local workflow.

Mitigation: Assess it separately before use; a matching checksum alone does not establish safety.
