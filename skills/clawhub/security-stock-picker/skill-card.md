## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and investing-focused agents use this skill to structure stock-screening discussions, compare candidate equities, evaluate risk signals, and format portfolio analysis for China A-share prompts. It should support research workflows rather than replace independent verification or professional financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financially consequential outputs may be mistaken for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Require users to verify market data independently, treat outputs as research support, and consult qualified professionals before making investment decisions.

Risk: Broad investing trigger terms may activate the skill during general market discussions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a stock-screening framework before applying the skill's scoring, technical-signal, or portfolio-construction guidance.

Risk: Historical factors, technical signals, and sample thresholds may not predict future market performance.

Mitigation: Label assumptions, report data dates and sources where available, and include uncertainty and risk disclosures in generated analysis.
