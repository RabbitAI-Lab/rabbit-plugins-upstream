## Description: <br>
The Synthesia craft skill helps agents plan consent-first AI-avatar videos for training, onboarding, explainers, localized series, and faceless educational content while keeping human approval and disclosure requirements in place. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, marketers, training teams, and content operators use this skill to decide when Synthesia is a good fit, write locked spoken-word scripts, plan consented avatar production, localize videos, and prepare disclosed publishing workflows. It is especially aimed at scalable informational video, not trust-led founder content, testimonials, or undisclosed synthetic presenters. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Unconsented likeness use, impersonation, fake endorsements, or undisclosed synthetic presenters can create legal, platform, and trust risks. <br>
Mitigation: Use only consented stock or personal avatars, refuse celebrity, competitor, or non-consented likeness requests, and apply platform or regional AI-disclosure labels before publishing. <br>
Risk: Automated plans or connected publishing workflows could move flawed or misleading avatar videos toward release without adequate review. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the skill guidance-only unless integrations are intentionally connected, require human approval for every video, and do not auto-publish without review. <br>
Risk: Pricing, API access, feature gates, disclosure rules, and regulated-content moderation may change or delay production use. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current Synthesia pricing and API access in-app, confirm disclosure and regulated-content requirements before production, and budget time for moderation review. <br>
Risk: Localized avatar videos may contain translation, idiom, formality, or market-disclosure errors. <br>
Mitigation: Use native-speaker QA for each localized language and check subtitles, on-screen text, and per-market disclosure before release. <br>
