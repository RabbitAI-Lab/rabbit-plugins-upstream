## Description:

Provides local travel destination safety ratings across five dimensions, with rankings, comparisons, risk notes, travel tips, and emergency numbers for supported countries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[travel-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/travel-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to look up static destination safety summaries, compare supported destinations, and retrieve practical safety tips before a trip.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides static safety summaries and may not reflect current travel advisories or sudden events.

Mitigation: Verify current destination risks with official government travel advisories before making travel decisions.

Risk: Public-facing wording includes a real-time risk warning claim, but security evidence says the skill has no network access.

Mitigation: Treat results as convenience reference material and avoid presenting them as live advisory data.
