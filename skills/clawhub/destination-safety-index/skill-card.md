## Description:

A Chinese-language local lookup for destination safety ratings, rankings, comparisons, risks, safety tips, and emergency phone numbers across more than 25 countries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[travel-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/travel-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travelers and travel-planning assistants use this skill as a static Chinese-language reference for comparing destination safety, checking common risks, and collecting practical travel safety reminders before consulting current official advisories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake static destination ratings for current official travel advisories.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning reference only and check current government, embassy, and local emergency advisories before travel.

Risk: The built-in country coverage and safety scores may not reflect sudden unrest, disasters, outbreaks, or other fast-changing conditions.

Mitigation: Verify high-impact travel decisions against live official sources, especially for unstable regions or active incidents.
