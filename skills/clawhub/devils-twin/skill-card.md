## Description: <br>
Devils Twin helps writers stress-test an important document by producing the strongest realistic opposing memo, a battle map of affected claims, a drop-in pre-emption paragraph, and an honest verdict. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Writers, product teams, and decision-makers use this skill before a document ships to understand how a credible real-world opponent would argue against it. It is intended for consensus rooms where the strongest counter-argument may otherwise go unspoken. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill analyzes full documents supplied by the user, which may include confidential or sensitive material. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential material unless the agent environment is approved to process it. <br>
Risk: A persuasive opposing memo or verdict could overstate weaknesses if treated as final advice instead of a stress test. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated memo, battle map, pre-emption paragraph, and verdict before relying on them for decisions. <br>
