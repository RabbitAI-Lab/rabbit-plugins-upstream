## Description: <br>
Designs Facebook Group strategy, content plans, join-funnel posts, and governance guidance for value-first community growth while keeping in-group execution human/native. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and community teams use this skill to decide when a Facebook Group is the right organic surface, design the group setup and growth plan, and draft group and Page/cross-channel content that funnels people to join. It also helps agents route in-group posting, moderation, analytics, and crisis handling to human/native workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafted Page and cross-channel content could be published without human review or could overstate volatile marketing metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Review drafted content before publishing and re-verify Facebook Groups metrics before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Group operations could drift into direct automation, fake growth, or non-consensual member email collection. <br>
Mitigation: Keep in-group posting and moderation human/native, avoid bulk posting and bought members, and collect member emails only with clear opt-in consent. <br>
