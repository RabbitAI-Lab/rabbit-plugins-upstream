## Use Case:

Video creators and editors use this skill to trim and reframe footage, add captions and overlays, adjust color and audio, and export edited scenes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video URLs, prompts, and project details may be processed by an external editing service.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive media unless you trust the configured service's handling of the content.

Risk: Automated edits or exports could change an important project unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Request plan approval and review the proposed changes before applying edits to important projects.
