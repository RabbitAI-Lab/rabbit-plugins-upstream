## Description: <br>
Calibrates when and how an agent uses humor: wit, jokes, banter, and playful tone matched to each user, context, and platform. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill to decide whether humor is appropriate, match humor intensity to a user's signals and context, craft requested jokes or punch-ups, and recover after humor misses. It is intended for conversational agents that need careful tone calibration across one-on-one, group, multilingual, and external-artifact contexts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can persist learned humor preferences, callbacks, off-limits topics, and successful joke wording under ~/Clawic/data/humor/. <br>
Mitigation: Review or delete ~/Clawic/data/humor/ to inspect or clear local personalization data before or after use. <br>
