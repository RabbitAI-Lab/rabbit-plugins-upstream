## Description:

Helps hosts analyze guest relationships, choose invitees, plan seating, and identify promising introductions for small gatherings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Party and dinner hosts use this skill to spot isolated guests, compare invitation choices, arrange table seating, and plan introductions while retaining final judgment over social decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guest lists and terminal reports can expose sensitive relationships, tensions, and invitation choices.

Mitigation: Keep local guest files and generated reports private; share only with people who need them.

Risk: The advertised pod-seating option appears incomplete and may give unreliable plans.

Mitigation: Review pod plans manually and confirm seating and keep-apart decisions before inviting guests.
