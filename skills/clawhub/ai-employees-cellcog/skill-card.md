## Description:

Helps agents hire, message, and follow long-running CellCog AI employees for ongoing projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users employ CellCog AI employees to maintain context and carry out recurring work on long-running projects, such as a content calendar or client account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project context and an API key are shared with CellCog's hosted service, and employees retain context over time.

Mitigation: Use only for projects approved for hosted processing; review sensitive details before sharing and check account controls for pausing and deletion.

Risk: Employees may be asked to run routines, send email, join meetings, or make phone calls.

Mitigation: Review the intended actions and approval settings before enabling ongoing routines or external communications.
