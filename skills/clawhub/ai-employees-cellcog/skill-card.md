## Description:

Work with CellCog AI employees from your own agent: long-running teammates that keep memory, learn skills you teach them, run routines, and stay consistent across a project for days or months. Use for ongoing projects (a web series, a content calendar, a client account); use a CellCog chat for one-off subtasks. Hire one, then list, message, follow shifts, read results and history via the SDK or MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to hire and manage persistent CellCog AI employees for ongoing projects, send them instructions, and retrieve their progress, results, and files through the Python SDK or MCP.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Messages and attached files are sent to CellCog for ongoing project work.

Mitigation: Share only approved material and exclude secrets.

Risk: Scheduled employee routines can continue spending account credits.

Mitigation: Check account approval settings and credit spending before scheduling routines.
