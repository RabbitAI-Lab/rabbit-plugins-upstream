## Description:

Vehicle inspection and damage process for a rental fleet: a pickup and return checklist per car (photo angles, wheels, glass, interior, mileage, fuel or charge), a condition report template, and a calm, evidence-first damage claim message to a renter when something is found. Triggers on "rental car inspection checklist", "damage report", "renter damaged my car", "Turo damage claim". Not for the contract itself (use car-rental-agreement).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts or supporting evidence could contain incorrect or misleading claims.

Mitigation: Review each draft against the agreement, photos, estimates, and other source records before sending.

Risk: Inspection photos may reveal precise location information.

Mitigation: Protect location-tagged photos and share them only with appropriate recipients.

Risk: Platform claim requirements may change.

Mitigation: Check the platform's current host policy before relying on a claim process or deadline.
