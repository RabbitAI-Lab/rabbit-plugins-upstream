## Description: <br>
Message House Builder turns an accepted positioning canvas and narrative/claims evidence into a launch message house, working-backwards PR-FAQ spine, per-channel message angle packs, and claim handoff items. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, product, and launch teams use this skill to derive launch messaging from a completed positioning canvas, including a message house, PR-FAQ narrative spine, per-channel angles, and claim review handoffs. It is intended for messaging assembly between positioning work and launch asset production. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive or unreleased positioning, persona, narrative, or claims inputs may be shared with the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the positioning canvas and memory projections are appropriate to share before using the skill. <br>
Risk: Draft message-house output or claim proposals may be mistaken for approved public launch claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review saved message-house output and keep unresolved claims marked [needs source] until claim review approves them. <br>
