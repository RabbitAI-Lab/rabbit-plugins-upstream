## Description: <br>
专利撰写专家 helps users prepare China-focused patent application drafts, including patent type selection, invention disclosure analysis, claims, specification, abstract, drawing guidance, pre-submission checks, and OA response support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, inventors, patent teams, and technical writers use this skill to turn invention disclosures into draft Chinese patent materials and quality-review checklists. It is best suited for drafting support and review preparation, not final legal filing decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share confidential invention details while using the skill. <br>
Mitigation: Use only in an agent environment approved for sensitive invention information and avoid sharing details that should not be processed there. <br>
Risk: Generated patent claims, filing documents, or OA responses may be incomplete or legally unsuitable for submission. <br>
Mitigation: Have generated materials reviewed by a qualified patent professional before relying on them or submitting them. <br>
