## Description:

Writes a UGC-style tutorial scene in Phantra: a person showing their phone camera how they use a product, one step per shot, the result first and last, rendered against their own face and product photos, with every step, quantity and tip coming from them. Use when someone asks for a tutorial, a how-to, a routine, a step-by-step clip or a get-ready-with-me. NOT for a verdict (ugc-review), a narrated explainer with no person (explainer) or a general talk-to-camera ad (ugc-ad).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators use the skill to turn their own tutorial steps, claims, and photos into a short, shot-by-shot Phantra scene prompt. They review the render quote and explicitly approve a paid render before it runs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow sends selected face and product photos to Phantra for rendering.

Mitigation: Use only photos the creator is authorized and comfortable sharing, and review Phantra's privacy and retention terms.

Risk: Rendering is paid and the cost depends on the scene and its shots.

Mitigation: Review the render quote and wait for the creator's explicit approval before rendering.

Risk: Malformed frontmatter may prevent the skill from loading.

Mitigation: Correct and validate the frontmatter before loading the skill.
