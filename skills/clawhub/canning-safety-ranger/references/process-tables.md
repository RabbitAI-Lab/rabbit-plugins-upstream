## Venting & operation (the steps people skip)

1. Jars in canner with 2–3" water (pressure) or 1–2" over jars (water bath, brisk boil
   the whole timed period).
2. Pressure canners: **vent steam 10 minutes** before closing the petcock/adding weight —
   air pockets are cold pockets; an unvented canner reads pressure without reaching temp.
3. Start timing when gauge hits target / weight jiggles at the right cadence.
4. Cut heat, let pressure fall to ZERO naturally before opening — quick-release = siphoning
   (liquid loss → seal risk + exposed food above liquid line).
5. Cool jars 24h untouched, then check seals (concave + hold under thumb), remove rings,
   wash, label with food + date + process.

## Acidification specifics (tomatoes & tomato things)

- Bottled lemon juice (uniform acidity — fresh varies): **2 Tbsp per quart, 1 per pint**
- Citric acid: **1/4 tsp per quart, 1/8 per pint** (no flavor impact)
- Vinegar 5%: 4 Tbsp/quart — allowed but changes flavor
- Add directly to the jar before filling, regardless of pack style
- Salsa/picante: only tested recipes; the vinegar+tomato+veg ratios ARE the safety —
  no substitutions that lower acid (sweet peppers for vinegar, extra onions, etc.)

## Never-home-can list (no tested process exists)

- Pumpkin butter / pureed winter squash (density)
- Dairy, cream soups, butter (fat insulates spores; dairy supports them)
- Flour/rice/pasta thickeners in jar (density)
- Eggs (texture + Salmonella economics)
- Nut meats, pesto (oil + low acid: anaerobic botulism playground)
- Olives without lye/brine protocol, avocado, banana purees
- Quick breads/cakes in a jar (the "canning bread" internet trend — refuse)

## Suspect-jar triage matrix

| Sign | Meaning | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Lid flat/concave, content normal, no smell | sealed properly | EAT (within quality window) |
| Lid dome/bulge, or lid pops under thumb | gas from microbial growth | DISCARD (toxin risk — boil 10 min can't fix gas volume spoilage) |
| Unsealed but refrigerated whole time ≤ 72h | never shelf-stored | EAT within days, refrigerated |
| Mold on surface (jam) | airborne contamination at fill | Jam: remove 1/2" + 2" margin IF <25% surface & jar otherwise sound... conservative answer: DISCARD — mold roots run deeper in soft products |
| Fizz/bubbles rising after settling, cloudy brine (pickles) | fermentation continued in-jar | DISCARD |
| Seeped/stained residue under lid, streaks down jar | spoilage | DISCARD |
| "Just smells weird" on a low-acid jar | botulism toxin is odorless — the smell is OTHER spoilage, which means process failure | DISCARD |

**Discard protocol:** open under water in a bucket, bleach the surfaces, never taste-test.
Toxin aerosol risk is minimal but splashing on faces/utensils is avoidable.
