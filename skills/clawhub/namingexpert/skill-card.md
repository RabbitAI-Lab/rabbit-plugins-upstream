## Description: <br>
Naming Expert helps agents generate and refine culturally aware names for brands, products, companies, people, pets, projects, domains, and creative IP. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, founders, and teams use this skill to request 3-5 candidate names with meaning, cultural context, pronunciation review, cross-language caveats, and a recommendation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger the skill on short or vague naming requests, including requests outside its intended scope. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for creative naming help and apply the documented scope checks before generating candidates. <br>
Risk: Generated names may still have trademark, domain availability, cultural, or cross-language issues. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as candidates and perform trademark, domain, market, and native-speaker review before adoption. <br>
