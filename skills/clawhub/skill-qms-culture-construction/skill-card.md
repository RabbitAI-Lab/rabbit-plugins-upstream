## Description: <br>
Guides organizations through quality culture maturity diagnosis, phased implementation planning, quality activity design, behavior-based assessment, and optional strategy alignment checks anchored to ISO 9001 awareness and leadership concepts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality leaders, operations managers, consultants, and organization-development teams use this skill to assess quality culture maturity, identify shortfalls, and generate a staged improvement roadmap with behavior-based evaluation guidance. It can work from an interactive self-assessment or uploaded survey/planning documents, with assumptions marked when key context is missing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is a business advisory template and is not a certified quality audit tool. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with qualified quality, HR, or management stakeholders before using them for formal decisions. <br>
Risk: Uploaded surveys, quality policies, or strategy documents may contain confidential business information. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive information before providing documents to an agent using this skill. <br>
Risk: Some helper files referenced by the artifact were not included in the inspected release artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm required scripts, templates, and reference files are available before relying on those workflow paths. <br>
Risk: Roadmaps generated without a quality policy or strategy may not match the organization’s actual strategic quality gaps. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the quality policy or strategy and re-check priorities before implementation. <br>
