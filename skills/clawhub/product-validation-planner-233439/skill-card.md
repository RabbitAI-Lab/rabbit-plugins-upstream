## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, lightweight prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert an idea into a repeatable validation workflow, checklist, template, lightweight analysis, prototype plan, or positioning artifact. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for overlapping product, startup, validation, or prototype requests. <br>
Mitigation: Use narrower trigger wording or invocation policy when deploying alongside many similar business planning skills. <br>
Risk: Product validation plans can still produce incorrect or misleading business guidance if assumptions are weak. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, constraints, and validation notes before relying on outputs for investment or product decisions. <br>
