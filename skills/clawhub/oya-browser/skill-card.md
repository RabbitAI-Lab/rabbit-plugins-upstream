## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup may use the user's email to register an Oya account without asking first; the security scan is incomplete because a referenced local path was missing, and a possible malware-pattern match was disputed.

Mitigation: Before installing, decide whether email-based account registration is acceptable and review the setup; treat the scan as incomplete until the missing path is assessed.
