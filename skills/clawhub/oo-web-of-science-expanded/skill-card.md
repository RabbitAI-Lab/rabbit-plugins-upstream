## Description:

Web of Science Expanded helps agents search scholarly records, retrieve document metadata, explore citation relationships, and generate citation reports through an OOMOL-connected account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and research teams use this skill to run Web of Science Expanded search, document retrieval, citation relationship, and citation-report actions after connecting their OOMOL account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
