## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Concealing gas pipes, meters, or heaters without adequate ventilation, service access, and appliance clearances can create safety risks.

Mitigation: Before concealing gas equipment, obtain approval from the local gas provider or a qualified technician and confirm ventilation, service access, clearances, and suitability for the specific building.

Risk: Layout changes or demolition can affect building structure and safety.

Mitigation: Treat design ideas as inspiration only; before changes, have qualified professionals confirm structural conditions and obtain required property and written approvals.
