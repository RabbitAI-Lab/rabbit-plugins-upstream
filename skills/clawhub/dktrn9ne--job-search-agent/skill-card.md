## Description:

Run a high-volume, high-fit job search workflow using a master resume and validated candidate knowledge to find, rank, tailor, and prepare applications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dktrn9ne](https://clawhub.ai/user/dktrn9ne)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to source and rank relevant roles, tailor truthful application materials, draft answers, and prepare applications for approval before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal career information may appear in application materials.

Mitigation: Review all drafted materials and their recipients before approving submission.

Risk: Unverified salary, work authorization, or availability answers may be inaccurate or sensitive.

Mitigation: Confirm these answers yourself before approving any application.

Risk: Tailored resumes and answers could overstate the candidate's experience.

Mitigation: Check every claim against the master resume and validated profile before use.
