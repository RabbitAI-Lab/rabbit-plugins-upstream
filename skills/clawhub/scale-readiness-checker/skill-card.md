## Description:

Turn your app into a plan that can grow from 5 users to 5,000 users, in plain words. Upload your app idea or your app code, answer a few questions, and get a full scale readiness check, with a database index list, a caching plan, a load test plan, and a hosting plan, for any AI agent. Structured scale plan with database indexing, caching, load testing, and hosting, ready to brief Claude, Cursor, or any AI builder, or a human developer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, small business owners, freelancers, and builders use the skill to assess an app's scale readiness and prepare a plain-language plan for indexing, caching, load testing, and hosting. It produces evidence-based draft recommendations for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A plan based on incomplete app information could suggest unsuitable changes or imply a level of capacity that has not been demonstrated.

Mitigation: Keep unsupported findings marked Not confirmed, review each draft against the app's evidence, and require owner approval before changes or load tests. A plan is not proof of a user capacity or a guarantee of scale.
