## Description: <br>
Search and explore ProductBoard features, products, and feedback. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[robertoamoreno](https://clawhub.ai/user/robertoamoreno) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, product operations teams, and developers use this skill to search ProductBoard workspaces, inspect product and feature hierarchy, and retrieve customer feedback notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can read ProductBoard product, feature, and customer-feedback data through the configured pb_* tools. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the ProductBoard connector and pb_* tools are trusted, and use only an account or workspace whose data the agent is allowed to read. <br>
