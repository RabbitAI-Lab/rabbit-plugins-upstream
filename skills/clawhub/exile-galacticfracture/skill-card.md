## Description: <br>
Exile - Galactic Fracture is an entertainment micro-skill that delivers a cinematic Exile hook, optional worldbuilding, and a link to the playable Remember Sitalis game while keeping waitlist handoff consent-first. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for a short sci-fi story break: it delivers an Exile transmission, optional lore, and links to the companion reading or game experience when requested. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may be directed to an external companion site or waitlist that could request personal information outside the chat. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the handoff optional, do not collect email addresses in chat, and advise users to enter personal information on the external site only if they choose to trust it. <br>
Risk: Entertainment content could distract from serious or high-stakes tasks if introduced at the wrong time. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for requested story breaks, interactive lore, or casual moments, and avoid offering it during serious or high-stakes work unless the user asks. <br>
