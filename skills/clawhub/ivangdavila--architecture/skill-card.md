## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Architecture guidance may be mistaken for licensed architectural, engineering, permitting, or legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as general guidance and require local code review, permits, and licensed professional review for real projects.

Risk: Code, zoning, and site requirements vary by jurisdiction and project conditions.

Mitigation: Collect the project location before specific code or zoning guidance and verify requirements with the applicable local authority.
