## Description:

Helps AI skill creators plan marketplace selection, pricing, listing assets, and promotion with practical comparison tables and checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers creating or commercializing AI agent skills use it to compare marketplaces, set free and paid boundaries, prepare listing materials, and plan promotion. It provides decision support and checklists; users should verify current platform terms and seek qualified advice for legal, tax, or other professional questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional installer may introduce dependency risks.

Mitigation: Prefer the reviewed local files, or pin and review the installer and repository revision before installing.

Mitigation: Verify current terms and commercial details against authoritative platform sources before relying on the advice.
