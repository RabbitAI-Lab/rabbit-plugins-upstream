## Description:

Helps AI skill developers plan marketplace selection, pricing, listing materials, and promotion with actionable checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers preparing to monetize an AI skill use it to compare marketplace requirements and revenue share, set free and paid boundaries, prepare listing assets, and plan promotion. It returns structured checklists and action items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace terms or revenue-share details may be outdated or vary by platform, and the supporting checklist is unfinished.

Mitigation: Verify current terms with each marketplace and review the checklist before relying on its recommendations.

Risk: Using the optional external installer may introduce unreviewed package or repository changes.

Mitigation: Load the reviewed local files directly, or review and pin the installer package and repository versions.
