## Description:

为初中至高中学生提供物理解题辅导，按读题画图、建模、列式计算和检验反思四步引导完成当前一道题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中和高中学生可用它逐步分析一道具体物理题，建立图景与模型，并完成计算和检验。它适用于题目或具体物理量明确的求解请求，不用于泛化提分建议、概念补课或实验设计。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stored learning records, reminders, or cross-skill sharing could retain or expose student information if enabled without clear consent.

Mitigation: Keep session-only mode by default; enable record storage or reminders only with explicit student consent, review companion skills before enabling sharing, and obtain guardian consent where required.
