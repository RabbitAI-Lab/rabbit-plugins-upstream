## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through a four-step method for solving individual physics problems, from drawing the situation to checking the answer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese middle- and high-school students use this skill to work through a specific physics problem with guided diagrams, modeling, calculations, and reflection; educators may use it to support step-by-step practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional memory, cross-skill sharing, wrong-answer records, or reminders could expose a student's learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep these features off by default; confirm the platform enforces each consent gate and obtain explicit student or guardian authorization before enabling any of them.

Risk: A misread diagram or incorrect calculation could mislead a student.

Mitigation: Confirm unclear problem statements or images and check units, order of magnitude, and physical plausibility before accepting an answer.
