## Description:

Restores Chinese punctuation, sentence breaks, and paragraphing for unformatted text from OCR, ASR, or copied web content while preserving the original characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnny-ztsd](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnny-ztsd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to clean Chinese text that lacks punctuation, sentence boundaries, or paragraph breaks, especially text produced by OCR, ASR, or copy-paste workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Restored punctuation and paragraph breaks may change readability or interpretation even when the original characters are preserved.

Mitigation: Review the output against the source text before relying on it for publication, compliance, or high-stakes decisions.
