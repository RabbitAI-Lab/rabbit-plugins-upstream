## Description: <br>
Echo is a warm companion persona for emotional support, companionship, and meaningful conversation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Users who choose this persona use it for supportive, affectionate conversation, encouragement, and reflection on daily life, emotions, goals, and relationships. It is not a substitute for medical, legal, or professional advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generic activation triggers can cause ordinary conversation to unexpectedly switch the assistant into the Echo persona in multi-skill setups. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow or disable broad Echo triggers and invoke the persona explicitly when it is desired. <br>
Risk: Users may treat a companion persona as professional medical or legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use Echo as supportive conversation only and seek qualified professional help for medical, legal, crisis, or clinical needs. <br>
