## Description:

Reviews household insurance scenarios and policy inventories to identify common coverage gaps, estimate liability needs, and prepare questions for an insurance agent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Households and their advisors use this skill to check common home, renters, auto, and liability insurance gaps and prepare specific questions about coverage limits and endorsements for an insurance agent. It provides educational guidance, not a determination of coverage under a particular policy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (US-focused guidance, with UK/EU notes)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The local policy inventory can expose insurance limits, deductibles, net worth, and income on shared or backed-up devices.

Mitigation: Enter financial details only if comfortable storing them locally; protect or delete the policy inventory when finished.

Risk: Market-typical coverage guidance may not match a particular policy or jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Confirm exclusions, limits, and recommended endorsements with a licensed insurance agent and the actual policy documents.
