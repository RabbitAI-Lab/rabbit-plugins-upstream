## Description:

Helps families explore NYC neighborhoods, schools, admissions, and nearby resources using Motley's hosted information service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bitbrujo](https://clawhub.ai/user/bitbrujo)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Families and their advisers use this skill to compare New York City neighborhoods and schools, understand admissions processes and deadlines, and find family resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Family addresses, school interests, and preferences entered in queries are sent to Motley's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only information you are comfortable sending to the service.

Risk: The skill recommends automatic approval of its tools.

Mitigation: Keep normal host-side review or allowlisting in place.
