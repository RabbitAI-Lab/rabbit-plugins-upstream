## Description: <br>
Helps restaurant operators turn food safety requirements into self-check checklists, licensing workflows, complaint-response guidance, HACCP and GB 31654 gap reports, and group risk directives. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[monsterdt](https://clawhub.ai/user/monsterdt) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External restaurant operators, store managers, food safety teams, and chain compliance leads use this skill to prepare inspections, review licensing and kitchen-layout issues, handle food-safety complaints, and produce structured audit or group-risk reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Food-safety and complaint-response outputs may be mistaken for legal, regulatory, or official inspection decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as operational guidance, review recommendations with qualified compliance or legal staff, and defer to the applicable regulator for official determinations. <br>
Risk: The security review flagged crisis-response wording that could downplay serious incidents. <br>
Mitigation: Remove or correct downplaying language before use, and escalate suspected unsafe food, illness, or regulator-facing incidents through legal, compliance, and crisis-response channels. <br>
Risk: The skill may request sensitive business or personal details such as employee identifiers, certificate numbers, supplier names, or full traceability codes. <br>
Mitigation: Minimize inputs, redact unnecessary identifiers, and handle any retained operational data under the organization's privacy and information-security controls. <br>
