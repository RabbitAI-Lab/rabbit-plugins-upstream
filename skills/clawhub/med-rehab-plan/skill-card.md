## Description:

将医生或康复师的建议整理为每日康复训练安排、强度进阶规则、停练警示和进度记录。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

供医疗健康从业者根据医生或康复师的建议及患者当前能力，整理可执行的每日康复训练表与调整规则；不替代临床方案。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A plan based on incomplete recovery details or clinician advice may be unsafe.

Mitigation: Provide full recovery context and confirm the plan with a qualified clinician before use.

Risk: Continuing exercise after warning symptoms could cause harm.

Mitigation: Stop immediately for the skill's red-line symptoms and contact a clinician.
