## Description:

Under genuine uncertainty with no reliable forecast, inventory means, cap downside at affordable loss, act for commitments, and let goals emerge from controllable moves.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and operators can use this skill to structure decisions when forecasts are unreliable. It guides an effectuation brief that starts from available means, caps affordable loss, seeks real commitments, and decides whether to continue, switch to causal planning, or halt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the planning framework as a substitute for domain-specific financial, legal, or operational review in high-stakes situations.

Mitigation: Require qualified review before acting on high-stakes recommendations, and keep each proposed action inside explicit affordable-loss caps.

Risk: The framework can be misapplied when reliable forecasts, fixed goals, or settled requirements already exist.

Mitigation: Use the skill's over-application guard: switch to causal planning when the path is predictable, the goal is fixed, or routine execution is sufficient.

Risk: Progress may be overstated if the brief relies on predictions or non-committing feedback.

Mitigation: Require either a real partner commitment or a cheap information-gathering action before treating the next step as valid progress.
