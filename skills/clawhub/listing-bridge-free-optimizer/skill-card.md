## Description: <br>
ListingBridge Free Optimizer rewrites Chinese product information or rough English drafts into polished English Amazon titles, bullet points, and product descriptions for cross-border sellers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kiwi-phantomworks](https://clawhub.ai/user/kiwi-phantomworks) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External cross-border sellers use this skill to turn Chinese product specs or an existing English draft into buyer-facing Amazon listing copy. It produces a title, five benefit-led bullets, and a product description. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated listing copy may contain inaccurate product claims, unsupported trust signals, or marketplace policy issues. <br>
Mitigation: Review the output against product facts and current Amazon marketplace policy before publishing. <br>
Risk: Using the skill in a workflow connected to production listings or external accounts could propagate unreviewed copy. <br>
Mitigation: Keep a human approval step before applying generated listing changes to live commerce channels. <br>
