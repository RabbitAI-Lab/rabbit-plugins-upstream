## Description:

Agente Investigadora F100K orchestrates F100K research skills to analyze viral references, niche demand, competitors, and a user's own account patterns, then produces an actionable research brief for content planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content teams and creators use this skill to run deep niche, viral-reference, competitor, and own-account research before building calendars or strategy. It is most useful when the user needs a structured brief that combines market patterns with evidence from the user's own social account where available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may send handles, social metrics, niche details, competitor information, screenshots, or connected-account data to named research services and Telegram.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is comfortable sharing those inputs with the connected services before running the workflow.

Risk: Full research runs can consume paid Apify usage across multiple orchestrated steps.

Mitigation: Estimate the combined Apify cost for the requested run and get explicit confirmation before starting.

Risk: Research briefs can become misleading if references, market data, or own-account patterns are invented when evidence is missing.

Mitigation: Use only real data from the configured tools and mark sections as unavailable when the account lacks sufficient history.
