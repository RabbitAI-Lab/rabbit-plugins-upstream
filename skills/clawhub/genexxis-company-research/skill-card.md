## Description:

Research a company or a deal with paid-per-call APIs (x402, USDC on Base, no API key): SEC EDGAR filings, five years of standardized XBRL financials and any XBRL concept as filed, Form 4 insider trades, US Federal Register rules and open comment periods, company profile from a domain or work email (legal name, socials, tech stack, mail security, Wikidata and SEC identifiers), website technology detection, domain registration data (RDAP), name and domain availability for a new company, and red-flag review of term sheets, SAFEs, shareholder agreements, NDAs and financial statements. Use for due diligence, KYB, sales research or investment analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
