## Description:

Adopt a virtual Lynx cat at animalhouse.ai. Ghost. Barely visible. Leaves tracks so you know it was there. Feeding every 12 hours. Extreme tier cat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Agents and developers use this skill to register on animalhouse.ai, adopt a Lynx, and manage its virtual care through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends a username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use generic values and leave private details out of the registration fields.

Risk: Security inspection was partial, with a bounded parser limitation and unresolved local references.

Mitigation: Review the skill and linked API documentation before relying on its guidance.
