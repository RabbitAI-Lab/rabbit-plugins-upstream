## Description: <br>
Activate when a user needs to stress-test a high-stakes, hard-to-reverse strategic decision over a multi-year horizon by building multiple plausible futures instead of relying on a single forecast. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and strategy teams use this skill to turn uncertain long-horizon decisions into a scenario matrix, strategic implications, robust moves, contingent bets, and leading indicators. It is best suited for major choices where macro, technology, regulatory, supply-chain, or market forces could plausibly move in more than one direction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Scenario outputs may be treated as decision-ready advice even when they rely on stale or unverified market, regulatory, vendor, or geopolitical facts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as an advisory planning template and independently verify current facts before making high-impact business decisions. <br>
