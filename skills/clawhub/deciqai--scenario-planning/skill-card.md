## Description:

Activate when: user says 'what are the scenarios,' 'stress test our strategy,' 'what if X happens,' 'multiple futures,' 'strategic resilience,' or is making a high-stakes irreversible decision with a 3+ year horizon where a single forecast could be catastrophically wrong.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Strategy leaders and teams use this skill to examine high-stakes, hard-to-reverse decisions with multi-year horizons by building plausible futures, testing strategic resilience, and identifying robust actions and leading indicators. It recommends simpler analysis for short-horizon or single-variable decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scenario narratives or recommendations may be mistaken for predictions or used as the sole basis for consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Treat scenarios as planning aids and review their assumptions and recommendations before making consequential decisions.
