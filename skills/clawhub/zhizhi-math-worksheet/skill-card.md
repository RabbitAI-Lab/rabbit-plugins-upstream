## Description:

Creates printable elementary math worksheets and separate answer keys from worksheet photos, PDFs, or question lists, with English or Chinese output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linzi007](https://clawhub.ai/user/linzi007)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents, educators, and other external users create printable math practice from an existing paper or selected questions, with a separate answer key for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stored source papers, student work, worksheets, and answer keys may contain personal information or expose answers.

Mitigation: Use a suitable private output folder, omit unnecessary personal data, keep answer files separate from student papers, and delete old worksheet folders when no longer needed.

Risk: Unreadable source details or unreviewed diagrams and solutions can produce inaccurate practice materials.

Mitigation: Clarify missing conditions, independently solve each question, and inspect the final rendered PDFs before use.
