This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

品牌营销人员和内容创作者使用产品图片、卖点及目标受众信息生成品牌宣传片和投放素材，支持多种广告风格、语言与画幅。

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and account credentials are handled by an external service.

Mitigation: Use only approved, nonsensitive assets; protect the API token and review the service before use.

Risk: Video generation uses paid credits and may be costly to repeat.

Mitigation: Get a current estimate and the user's approval before submitting; check parameters before generation.

Risk: Unlicensed images or unsupported advertising claims may create legal exposure.

Mitigation: Use licensed product images and review claims before publishing the advertisement.

Risk: Installing or upgrading the external qhkit package introduces supply-chain trust requirements.

Mitigation: Prefer pinned or reviewed package versions in controlled environments.
