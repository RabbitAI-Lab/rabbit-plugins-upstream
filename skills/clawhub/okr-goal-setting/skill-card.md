## Description:

Guides teams to write measurable, outcome-focused objectives and key results, calibrate ambition, align dependencies, and review progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams use this skill to turn vague goals into measurable quarterly OKRs, distinguish outcomes from activities, align cross-team dependencies, and review results honestly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft objectives or targets may not fit the team's business context.

Mitigation: Review proposed OKRs against the team's situation and adjust targets before adopting them.
