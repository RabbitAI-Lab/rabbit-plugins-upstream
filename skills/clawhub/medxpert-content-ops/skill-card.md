## Description:

An internal standard for running a company website and its content operations, covering how pages, copy and publishing workflows are documented and kept consistent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

MedXpert content and marketing teams use this reference to keep website, official-account and public-facing materials consistent. It guides page design, copy review, regulatory references, SEO checks and the content synchronization workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory or other factual content may be outdated, or public materials may expose identifying client details.

Mitigation: Verify regulatory references against current sources and remove identifying client information before publication.

Risk: The guidance recommends rewriting third-party material without attribution, which can create attribution or rights issues.

Mitigation: Preserve appropriate third-party attribution and confirm reuse rights.

Risk: The documented synchronization workflow relies on a separately supplied script whose behavior is outside this release's security assessment.

Mitigation: Review the script before running it and review generated content before publication.
