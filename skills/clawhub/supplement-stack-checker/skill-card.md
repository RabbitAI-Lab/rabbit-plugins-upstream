## Description:

Use when someone is taking multiple supplements/vitamins together, planning a new stack, or worried about interactions — checks 60+ ingredients for pair interactions (calcium-iron blocking, zinc-copper depletion, St. John's Wort drug clearing, vitamin K-warfarin), harmful dose flags vs ULs, redundant overlap (5 forms of the same thing), and builds a timed daily schedule that separates conflicting minerals by 2+ hours.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People reviewing or planning adult, multi-product supplement routines can use the skill to see possible ingredient interactions, compare summed amounts with adult upper limits, identify repeated ingredients, and get a suggested timing schedule. It is an educational aid and directs users to a pharmacist or doctor for decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incomplete interaction checks, incorrect dose totals, or unreliable schedules could falsely reassure users about supplement safety.

Mitigation: Treat results as an incomplete educational aid; no flags do not establish safety. Have a pharmacist review medicines, combined doses, and product timing before changing a routine.
