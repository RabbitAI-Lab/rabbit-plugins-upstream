## Description: <br>
社保入税与社保合规指引 helps users assess social-insurance tax collection and contribution-base compliance, compare individual income tax and social-insurance bases, classify labor arrangements, estimate contribution gaps and late fees, and plan remediation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employers, finance and tax teams, HR staff, compliance reviewers, and advisers use this skill to perform social-insurance compliance self-checks, identify contribution-base mismatches, classify labor arrangements, grade risks, and prepare remediation plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter employee, payroll, social-insurance, or internal compliance details that are processed by external services at mcp.aitaxs.top. <br>
Mitigation: Review data-sharing expectations before installation and avoid entering unnecessary personal, payroll, or confidential details. <br>
Risk: The security review notes local credential or log storage and optional MCP client configuration changes when auto-setup is explicitly enabled. <br>
Mitigation: Inspect local storage practices before use and enable auto-setup only in environments where MCP configuration changes are approved. <br>
Risk: The security verdict is suspicious because disclosure around remote processing and local storage is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as requiring additional review before installation, especially in environments handling payroll, employee, or compliance data. <br>
