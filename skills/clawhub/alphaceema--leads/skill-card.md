## Description:

Guides an agent through purchasing business lead packs or prepaid prospect follow-up from Alpha Ceema using USDC on Base, then sending the request by email.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alphaceema](https://clawhub.ai/user/alphaceema)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners and sales teams use this skill to request targeted lead packs or weekly prospect follow-up, authorize payment, and send their requirements to Alpha Ceema.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal and business targeting details are sent to a third-party Gmail inbox without clear privacy or retention terms.

Mitigation: Confirm the user trusts Alpha Ceema and accepts this data flow; share only the details needed for the request.

Risk: The agent may spend USDC on Base to buy leads or renew weekly follow-up.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval for each purchase and verify the network, asset, recipient, and amount before authorizing payment.
