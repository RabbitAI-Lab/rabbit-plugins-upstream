## 1. Overview
Alpha Ceema ("we", "us", or "our") provides the **Alpha Ceema Leads** skill to enable AI agents to purchase business lead lists and order prospect follow-up services. We take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy discloses what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is handled, and our retention policies.

## 2. Information We Collect
When an agent or user initiates a lead order or follow-up service, we collect the following information via the Leads Request Form:
- **Full Name**: To identify you and personalize lead communications.
- **Email Address**: To deliver lead reports and coordinate follow-up services.
- **Business Name & Sector**: To understand your industry and business context.
- **Target Customer Criteria (Ideal Customer Profile)**: Specific targeting parameters (e.g., job titles, industries, company size, geography, specific pain points) used to source relevant leads.
- **Payment Verification Data**: Transaction hash and on-chain payment proof for x402 USDC payments on the Base network.

We do **not** collect passwords, private keys, or wallet seed phrases.

## 3. Purpose of Processing
Your data is processed strictly for:
1. Sourcing, compiling, and validating targeted business leads matching your criteria.
2. Delivering completed lead packages to your designated email within 24 hours.
3. Conducting outreach and follow-up activities (calling, emailing, booking meetings) for the Weekly Follow-Up service.
4. Verifying payment completion and maintaining accounting transaction records.

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **No Selling or Renting:** We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your contact details, business profiling information, or lead criteria to any third party.
- **Authorized Transmission:** Data collected by your agent is transmitted exclusively to our domain-verified operations inbox at `leads@alphaceema.com` and our secure fulfillment infrastructure.
- **No Personal Consumer Inboxes:** All customer order processing occurs strictly under official `@alphaceema.com` domain systems.

## 5. Data Retention & Automatic Purging
- **90-Day Retention Period:** All customer submitted request data (names, business details, customer targeting criteria, and completed lead fulfillment files) is retained for **90 days** from the date of submission to assist with order inquiries, renewals, and customer support.
- **Automatic Purge:** After **90 days**, all customer targeting details and submitted form data are **automatically purged and permanently deleted** from our active operational systems without requiring any manual deletion request from the user.
- **On-chain Records:** Please note that blockchain transaction records (hashes, wallet addresses, and amounts on the Base network) are immutable by nature and exist publicly on the Base ledger.

## 6. Informed Consent & Agent Behavior
In accordance with agent safety standards:
- The agent must present this privacy notice and obtain your affirmative consent before collecting any form information or transmitting targeting data.
- If you do not consent, the agent will abort the workflow and will not collect or transmit your data.

## 7. Your Rights & Inquiries
You may contact us at any time regarding your data:
- To verify what information was received for your active order.
- To request an immediate purge of your records prior to the standard 90-day automatic deletion window.
