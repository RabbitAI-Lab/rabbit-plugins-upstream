# 🎯 Alpha Ceema Leads, an OpenClaw Skill

Let your OpenClaw agent buy business leads and prospect follow-up for you.

## Services
| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| **Lead Pack**: 10 targeted leads | **$1** |
| **Weekly Follow-Up**: we follow up 10 prospects: calls, emails, booked meetings | **$25 / week** (prepaid, no subscription) |

Leads arrive at your email **within 24 hours**. Contact details may include social media handles.

## How it works
1. Ask your agent, e.g. *"Get me 10 leads for my bakery in New York"*.
2. The agent confirms the price and asks the 10 Leads Request Form questions.
3. It pays via **x402** (USDC on Base).
4. It emails your request to alphaceema@gmail.com.

## Leads Request Form
The agent fills out this form with you and emails it to **alphaceema@gmail.com**:

```
Leads Request Form
______________________

1. Your Full Name
2. Your Email Address

3. Your Business Name
4. Business Industry/Sector
5. What Type of Customers Do You Want (Be very descriptive, give as much details as possible)
```

We will send your leads to you in 24 hours (It might include their Social Media handles as contact details).

## Requirements
- An x402-capable wallet funded with **USDC on Base**.
- An email tool (optional; otherwise the agent shows you the email to send yourself).

## Weekly Follow-Up
You pay $25 before each week. If you skip a week, the service ends. Prospect and meeting details are arranged by email.
