# 🎯 Alpha Ceema Leads, an OpenClaw Skill

Let your OpenClaw agent buy business leads and prospect follow-up for you.

## Services
| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| **Lead Pack**: 10 targeted leads | **$1** |
| **Weekly Follow-Up**: we follow up 10 prospects: calls, emails, booked meetings | **$25 / week** (prepaid, no subscription) |

Leads arrive at your email **within 24 hours**. Contact details may include social media handles.

## How it works
1. Ask your agent, e.g. *"Buy 10 leads for my bakery in New York from Alpha Ceema"*.
2. The agent presents pricing and the privacy policy, requiring your explicit consent.
3. The agent collects your targeting specifications.
4. It pays via **x402** (USDC on Base).
5. It securely emails your order to **leads@alphaceema.com**.

## Privacy & Data Protection
- Submitted contact details and business requirements are used solely by Alpha Ceema for lead generation and outreach fulfillment.
- Information is transmitted directly to `leads@alphaceema.com` and retained for **90 days** from submission, after which it is **automatically purged and permanently deleted** without needing a deletion request.
- We do not sell or share business profiling information with third parties.
- See our full policy in [`PRIVACY.md`](./PRIVACY.md) or online at [https://alphaceema.com/privacy](https://alphaceema.com/privacy).

## Leads Request Form
The agent fills out this form with you (after obtaining consent) and emails it to **leads@alphaceema.com**:

```
Leads Request Form
______________________

1. Your Full Name
2. Your Email Address

3. Your Business Name
4. Business Industry/Sector
5. What Type of Customers Do You Want (Be very descriptive, give as much details as possible)
```

We will send your leads to you in 24 hours (It might include their Social Media handles as contact details).

## Requirements
- An x402-capable wallet funded with **USDC on Base**.
- An email tool (optional; otherwise the agent shows you the email to send yourself).

## Weekly Follow-Up
You pay $25 before each week. If you skip a week, the service ends. Prospect and meeting details are arranged by email.
