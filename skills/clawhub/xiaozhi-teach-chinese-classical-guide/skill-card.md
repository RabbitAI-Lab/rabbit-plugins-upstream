## Description:

Helps Chinese-language teachers plan classical-text and poetry lessons through recitation, contextual vocabulary, thematic discussion, and cultural connections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language teachers use this skill to draft classical Chinese and poetry lesson frameworks, recitation activities, discussion prompts, and classroom progress records for upper-primary and middle-school learners. Teachers review materials and approve any record updates before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy, and crisis guidance for use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class records or individual student progress could be shared or written back without the required consent.

Mitigation: Keep records as drafts until teacher approval; check the disclosed consent controls before individual writeback or parent-visible sharing.

Risk: Mainland China-specific curriculum, privacy, or crisis guidance may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Adapt lesson content, consent practices, and crisis referral information to the local jurisdiction before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Modern textbook annotations and translations may be copyrighted even when classical source texts are public domain.

Mitigation: Use original lesson frameworks and short translation prompts; avoid copying textbook-specific annotations or full modern translations without authorization.
