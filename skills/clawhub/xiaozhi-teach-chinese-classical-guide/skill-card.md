## Description:

为语文教师提供文言文与古诗词教学设计框架，涵盖诵读、训诂、串讲、主题讨论、文化联结和班级古文积累。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

小学高段、初中和高中语文教师可用它设计文言文与古诗词教学，准备诵读与训诂指导、串讲框架和主题讨论问题，并整理班级古文积累。输出供教师审阅的草稿。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student class records may contain identifying details or inaccurate updates.

Mitigation: Use student aliases and have the teacher review proposed record changes.

Risk: Optional student-profile exchange may send data through an unverified handover schema or receiving integration.

Mitigation: Keep profile exchange disabled until both the schema and receiving integration are verified.
