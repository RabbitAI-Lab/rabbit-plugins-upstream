## Description:

面向语文老师的文言文与古诗词教学设计工具，用于生成诵读正音、训诂、串讲、主题分析、文化背景联结和班级古文积累记录的教学框架与讨论问题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-language teachers use this skill to plan classical Chinese and classical poetry lessons for upper-primary and middle-school learners. It produces classroom-facing teaching frameworks, discussion prompts, vocabulary and exegesis guidance, recitation support, and class record structures while leaving final instruction and student-facing use under teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes classroom-scoped recordkeeping and may be used with student information.

Mitigation: Confirm school consent, access, retention, and parent-sharing rules before enabling persistent class records or student writeback.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language classical Chinese and poetry teaching and includes mainland-China safety and curriculum assumptions.

Mitigation: Localize safety, curriculum, and referral assumptions before deploying in other regions or school systems.

Risk: Teacher-facing drafts or generated questions could be used directly without review.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review outputs before classroom use, especially AI-generated exercises, student-facing materials, and any shared records.
