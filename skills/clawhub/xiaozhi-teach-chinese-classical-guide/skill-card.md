### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated translations or lesson drafts may be inaccurate or unsuitable for classroom use.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review translations and lesson drafts before using them with students.

Risk: Class records or student information could be written or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm platform controls enforce teacher confirmation and consent for class records, student writeback, and parent-visible content.
