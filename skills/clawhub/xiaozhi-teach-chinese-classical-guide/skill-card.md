## Description:

帮助语文老师设计文言文与古诗词教学框架、诵读与训诂活动，以及主题讨论问题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-language K–12 teachers use this skill to plan classical Chinese and poetry lessons, including recitation, vocabulary in context, guided interpretation, and discussion. Drafts require teacher review; the skill does not replace full-text teaching, provide complete translations, or grade students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class records or student details may expose private information or be shared without consent.

Mitigation: Check school policy and consent for student aliases, records, parent-sharing, and writeback; avoid sensitive student details.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials could be disclosed.

Mitigation: Do not enter active unreleased exam questions, answers, or marking criteria.

Risk: Teaching drafts may contain mistakes or reproduce protected textbook annotations.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify content before classroom use and avoid copying protected textbook translations or annotations.
