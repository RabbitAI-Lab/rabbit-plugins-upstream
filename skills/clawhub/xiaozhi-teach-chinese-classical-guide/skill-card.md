This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

外部语文教师可用此技能设计文言文和古诗词教学，生成诵读、训诂、主题讨论及文化联结的教学框架，并整理班级古文积累记录草稿。教师应审阅教学内容和记录变更后再使用。

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student or confidential examination details may be included in prompts or classroom records.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, avoid identifying student details and confidential exam material, and check consent controls before persistence or cross-skill sharing.

Risk: Suggested classroom record changes may be inaccurate or unsuitable.

Mitigation: Review proposed record changes before saving or sharing them.
