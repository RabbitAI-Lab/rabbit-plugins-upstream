## Description:

为语文老师设计文言文与古诗词课程的诵读、训诂、主题讨论及文化联结框架。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

语文老师用它起草中国大陆小学高段至初中的文言文和古诗词教学框架、讨论问题及班级积累记录；课堂使用前由老师审定。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, student-data practices, and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student progress records may expose minors' learning information.

Mitigation: Confirm school permission, use pseudonyms without direct identifiers, limit retention, and require consent before sharing or writing back records.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and safety-contact defaults may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Adapt grade expectations, data rules, and local emergency contacts before deployment outside Mainland China.

Risk: Generated lesson content may contain inaccuracies or reproduce protected textbook commentary.

Mitigation: Have teachers check examples and questions before classroom use; avoid copying modern textbook notes and translations.
