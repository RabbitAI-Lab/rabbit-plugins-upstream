## Description: <br>
Jimeng Prompter helps agents turn user ideas into production-oriented prompts and guidance for Jimeng Seedance video generation and Seedream image generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sallyface0](https://clawhub.ai/user/sallyface0) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and creators use this skill to refine image or video ideas into Jimeng-ready prompts, model-routing choices, storyboard timelines, negative prompts, concept-image prompts, and production guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may keep and reuse local visual-style preferences in references/evolution-memory.md. <br>
Mitigation: Use the documented skip or clear commands when preference memory is not wanted, and review the privacy policy before use. <br>
Risk: Generated prompt and production guidance may not match every Jimeng model behavior or user intent. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated prompts before use and iterate with test generations before relying on final outputs. <br>
