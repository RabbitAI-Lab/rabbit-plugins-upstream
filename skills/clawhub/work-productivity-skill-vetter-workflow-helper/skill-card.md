## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams structure Skill Vetter-style workflows for fixing bugs, hardening setup and safety, improving reliability, and creating adjacent skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, skill authors, maintainers, teams, and agent users use this skill to turn skill-vetting or productivity requests into actionable workflows, checklists, analyses, or implementation support with clear assumptions and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit invocation and generic trigger words may activate the skill during unrelated security, GitHub, or bug-fix conversations.

Mitigation: Review activation settings or invoke the skill explicitly when skill-vetting workflow support is intended.
