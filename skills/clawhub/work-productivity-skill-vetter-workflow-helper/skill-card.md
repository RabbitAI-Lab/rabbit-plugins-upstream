## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams plan practical Skill Vetter-style workflows for bug fixing, setup hardening, reliability improvement, and adjacent skill creation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn broad Skill Vetter-style needs into concrete plans, checklists, implementation support, and validation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill during ordinary security, GitHub, or bug-fix requests where the user did not ask for this workflow.

Mitigation: Narrow invocation phrases or confirm user intent before applying the workflow to unrelated requests.

Risk: Generated workflow, checklist, or code-change guidance could be incomplete or misleading for a specific skill or deployment context.

Mitigation: Restate the task-specific success criteria, validate the result against them, and review or scan affected skills before deployment.
