## Description:

Helps agent users and skill teams turn skill-vetting, safety, reliability, and workflow requests into practical plans and deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt popular skill patterns into practical workflows, checklists, analyses, and implementation support for improving reliability and safety.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause activation for loosely related requests.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or a clearly stated skill-vetting or workflow task.

Risk: Demand scores and linked examples could be mistaken for evidence of security-review quality.

Mitigation: Treat them as demand signals only; evaluate security separately.
