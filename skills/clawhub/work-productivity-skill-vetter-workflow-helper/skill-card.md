## Description:

Guides users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams through planning practical skill-vetter inspired fixes, safety improvements, reliability work, or adjacent skills, then checking the deliverable against the stated need.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn a work-productivity or skill-vetting need into a practical plan, checklist, analysis, or implementation. It guides the work through delivery and a check against the user's success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to run for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Check that the request clearly concerns the documented work-productivity or skill-vetting tasks before applying the workflow.
