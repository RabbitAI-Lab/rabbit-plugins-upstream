## Description:

A Chinese-language usability testing interview assistant that helps UX researchers design neutral task scenarios, guide think-aloud sessions, record observations, and organize post-test notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[20232931028](https://clawhub.ai/user/20232931028)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

UX researchers, product teams, and usability test moderators use this skill to prepare task scripts, capture participant behavior during sessions, and turn observations into structured issue lists and improvement recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Usability sessions may include participant names or other sensitive personal details.

Mitigation: Use participant IDs such as P1 and P2 instead of real names, and avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Risk: Moderator prompts or task wording can unintentionally lead participants and bias test results.

Mitigation: Review generated task scenarios and prompts before use to ensure they describe goals without giving operation steps or product hints.
