## Description:

Traveler generates structured day-by-day travel itineraries from trip details such as origin, destination, duration, budget, traveler group, and interests, including time blocks, transportation connections, dining, lodging, budget estimates, avoidance tips, alternatives, and disclosed third-party service links for execution steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onsoul](https://clawhub.ai/user/onsoul)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn rough travel plans into executable itineraries for leisure trips, family trips, couple travel, friend travel, and similar independent travel scenarios. The skill is for planning and guidance only; booking, payments, ride hailing, reservations, ticketing, and price or availability confirmation remain with third-party providers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trip plans may contain stale or unverified prices, availability, opening hours, weather, reservation rules, or booking terms.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive details directly with relevant providers before travel or purchase.

Risk: The skill may request travel details such as origin, destination, dates, traveler group, interests, and budget.

Mitigation: Share only the trip details needed for planning and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Risk: The skill may present promotional third-party service links for transportation, flights, lodging, tickets, or dining.

Mitigation: Treat links as optional execution paths, compare terms independently, and complete transactions only with providers the user trusts.
