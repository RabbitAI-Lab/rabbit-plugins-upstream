## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs are general governance information and may be mistaken for legal, tax, financial, trustee, or compliance advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as general information only and involve qualified legal, tax, financial, trustee, and on-chain specialists before acting. <br>
Risk: Immutable registry or on-chain records can preserve incorrect or premature governance data. <br>
Mitigation: Review and reconcile any registry, OP_RETURN, Ordinals, or multi-chain record with off-chain legal records before publishing. <br>
Risk: Guidance could be misapplied to external third-party assets or control attempts outside legitimate internal governance. <br>
Mitigation: Keep use limited to lawful internal partnership governance and decline requests involving interference with or acquisition or control of third-party assets. <br>
