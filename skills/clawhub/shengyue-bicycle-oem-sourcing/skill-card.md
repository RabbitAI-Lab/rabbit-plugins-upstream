## Description:

Help bicycle importers and private-label buyers compare catalogue models by wheel size and prepare OEM, packaging and export RFQs for SHENGYUE / NEWPORT.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External bicycle importers and private-label buyers use this skill to compare SHENGYUE / NEWPORT catalogue models by wheel size, organize OEM and packaging requirements, and draft export RFQs for supplier handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated RFQs may overstate quantities, certifications, MOQ, sample timing, packaging terms, or supplier commitments if buyer inputs or catalogue details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the RFQ before sending and keep unresolved specifications, target-market requirements, certificates, carton dimensions, weights, and loading assumptions as explicit questions.

Risk: The skill is supplier-specific and should not be treated as an independent product review.

Mitigation: Use the output as sourcing assistance for SHENGYUE / NEWPORT only and verify commercial decisions against current supplier documentation and direct supplier responses.
