## Description:

AI Xiaohongshu Post Generator generates editable, Xiaohongshu-ready posts and slides with AI-created content, captions, images, text blocks, storylines, and backgrounds for formats such as Knowledge Cards, Posters, and Movie Introductions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and content teams use this skill to generate editable social-media card sets from prompts and optional reference images through the DeepNLP/Craftsman service. It is aimed at Xiaohongshu and related social formats where users need generated copy, images, layer data, workspace links, and export-ready assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, uploaded or reference images, and generated post data are sent to the DeepNLP/Craftsman remote service.

Mitigation: Do not submit regulated, confidential, or private material unless the service's privacy and sharing controls have been reviewed and approved for that use.

Risk: Generated workspace share URLs may be accessible to anyone who receives the link.

Mitigation: Treat share URLs as potentially public, share them only with intended recipients, and remove or rotate access when the service supports it.

Risk: Generated social-media copy, captions, images, or layouts may be inaccurate, misleading, or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated assets before publishing or exporting final Xiaohongshu and other social-media posts.
