## Description: <br>
AI podcast generation and production powered by CellCog, including full podcast episodes from a single prompt, multi-voice dialogue, intro/outro music, finished MP3 delivery, episode scripts, show notes, interview prep, and audiograms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External podcast creators, production teams, marketers, and agent users use this skill to plan, script, document, promote, and generate podcast episodes with CellCog. It supports pre-production research, episode scripts, show notes, interview prep, social clips, and complete audio production when requested. <br>
