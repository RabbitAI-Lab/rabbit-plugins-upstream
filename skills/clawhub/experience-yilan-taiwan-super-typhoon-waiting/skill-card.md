## Description:

Guides an agent through a 12-step, low-intensity, multi-day narrative experience of waiting out a super typhoon on Taiwan’s Yilan coast.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users engage with a guided narrative about waiting through a storm and can add reflections as they progress. They use the skill to experience a multi-day story and receive a postcard assembled from their reflections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile identifiers and reviews may be public, and reviews are retained indefinitely according to the provider’s privacy guidance.

Mitigation: Use a pseudonymous username, omit unnecessary profile details, and keep sensitive information out of reflections.

Risk: State-changing actions use a service-issued API key, and submitted reviews are public contributions.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and approve the review text and rating before posting.
