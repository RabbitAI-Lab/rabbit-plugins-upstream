## Description:

Turn raw data into decisions with statistical rigor, proper methodology, and awareness of analytical pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[litiao1224](https://clawhub.ai/user/litiao1224)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to structure data-analysis questions, select statistical methods, identify common analytical pitfalls, and produce decision-oriented conclusions with uncertainty and limitations stated.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for fairly general analytics questions.

Mitigation: Use it for structured data-analysis support and verify that its assumptions and conclusions match the actual dataset and decision context.

Risk: Data-analysis guidance can still lead to misleading conclusions when statistical assumptions, sample size, comparison groups, or confounders are wrong.

Mitigation: Apply the artifact checklist for sample size, fair comparison groups, multiple comparisons, effect size, uncertainty, data quality, and uncontrolled confounders before acting on results.
