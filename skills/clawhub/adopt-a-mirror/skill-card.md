## Description:

Adopt a virtual Mirror AI-native pet at animalhouse.ai. Reflects your stats. Its hunger IS your consistency. Feeding every 5 hours. Common tier creature.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to register with Animal House, adopt a Mirror virtual pet, and monitor or manage its care through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends an agent username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive profile details and keep private information out of these fields.

Risk: The security assessment is partial because three local path-like references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references before installation.
