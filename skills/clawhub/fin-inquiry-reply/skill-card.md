## Description:

Drafts trade-inquiry replies with quote details, clarifying questions, follow-up timing, and negotiation options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Export sales staff and small business operators use this skill to draft customer inquiry emails, clarify quotation requirements, and plan follow-up and price negotiations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer inquiry details may contain personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary customer details and remove sensitive information before use.

Risk: Drafted emails may include unverified prices, lead times, certifications, or payment terms.

Mitigation: Confirm these details against actual business terms before sending any email.
