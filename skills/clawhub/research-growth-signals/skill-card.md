## Description:

Helps agents research traceable public social and SEO signals through SignalDig, including social posts, search demand, rankings, and traffic evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jerrykik](https://clawhub.ai/user/jerrykik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent builders, small teams, and other external users use this skill to collect and interpret bounded public social and SEO evidence for growth research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research prompts, domains, and public profile identifiers may be sent to SignalDig, including through automatic invocation.

Mitigation: Avoid private or sensitive details in requests and use this skill only when comfortable sharing the research scope with SignalDig.

Risk: Partial retrieval or unavailable sources can make the returned evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Report actual coverage and limitations; do not present partial results as exhaustive.
