## Description:

Research traceable public social and SEO signals, including bounded Zhihu and Xiaohongshu resources and Threads topic search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jerrykik](https://clawhub.ai/user/jerrykik)

## Use Case:

External developers, independent builders, and small teams use the skill to gather traceable public social and SEO evidence for growth research, including discussions, keyword demand, search results, competitors, rankings, backlinks, and traffic.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references and connected service before deployment.
