## Description:

Helps people facing a US layoff organize benefits and severance deadlines, estimate related costs, and prepare an action checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People facing a US layoff use this skill to prioritize potential COBRA, retirement-plan, equity, unemployment, and severance deadlines and prepare questions for the relevant administrators or advisers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic legal, benefits, and financial guidance may give incorrect deadlines or leave a gap in coverage.

Mitigation: Use only as a checklist aid; verify dates and coverage against the severance agreement, plan documents, COBRA notice, state unemployment agency, and equity plan, and consult qualified benefits, tax, or employment counsel before acting.

Risk: An offboarding data-export checklist could prompt copying confidential employer or customer information.

Mitigation: Do not retain company emails, customer data, source code, confidential files, or anything prohibited by employer policies or agreements.
