## Description:

Helps agents write and improve Indonesian copy, social posts, articles, email, landing pages, ads, technical documentation, and brand storytelling with audience, tone, structure, and ethical-claim checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, writers, marketers, and developers use this skill to draft and revise Indonesian-language content for local channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, blogs, email, landing pages, product copy, documentation, and brand storytelling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain secrets or sensitive personal data supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting secrets or unnecessary sensitive personal data into drafts, and redact credentials or personal data before saving or reusing content.

Risk: Marketing copy, statistics, or claims may be inaccurate or misleading if source facts are not checked.

Mitigation: Review claims, figures, testimonials, and statistics before publishing, and mark uncertain facts for verification.
