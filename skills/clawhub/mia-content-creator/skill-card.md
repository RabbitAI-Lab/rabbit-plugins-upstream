## Use Case: <br>
External creators and agent developers use this skill to generate local draft posts for Moltbook and Twitter/X, calculate proposed posting times, and view simple local post-history analytics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake local draft generation, schedule calculation, and analytics summaries for real social-media posting or monetization. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as local drafts and local history only; verify any actual posting, scheduling, engagement, or revenue through the relevant platform. <br>
Risk: The CLI creates or updates content-log.json in the directory where it is run. <br>
Mitigation: Run the command from the intended workspace and review the local content-log.json file before relying on its analytics. <br>
