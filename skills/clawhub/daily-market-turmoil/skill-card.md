## Description:

Creates a daily Chinese-language Markdown report on global stock markets, highlighting market-moving news, the previous trading day, and today's outlook.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susie-ss](https://clawhub.ai/user/susie-ss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers and analysts use this skill to compile current A-share, Hong Kong, US, European, and Asia-Pacific market news into a Chinese-language daily report with a prior-session summary and today's outlook.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or inaccurate market reports can mislead readers.

Mitigation: Confirm the dates and market figures against current sources before relying on the report.

Risk: The report is written to a Documents folder rather than returned only in chat.

Mitigation: Review the output path, trigger phrases, and Chinese-language format before enabling the skill.
