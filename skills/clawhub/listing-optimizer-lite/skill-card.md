## Description: <br>
Listing Optimizer Lite helps e-commerce sellers draft and optimize product titles, bullet points, descriptions, backend keywords, and platform-specific listing copy for Amazon, Shopee, Lazada, and independent storefronts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External e-commerce sellers, operators, copywriters, and startup teams use this skill to turn product information into marketplace-ready listing drafts and keyword guidance. It is intended as a copywriting assistant whose output should be reviewed before publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter seller-account credentials, API keys, customer personal data, or confidential product formulas. <br>
Mitigation: Do not provide sensitive data unless the privacy and retention policy of the AI platform running the skill has been separately verified. <br>
Risk: Generated listing copy may include incorrect product facts, stale marketplace-policy assumptions, or unreviewed claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated marketplace copy, product attributes, brand names, dimensions, and target-platform rules before publishing. <br>
Risk: Competitor references may lead to copy that is too similar to another listing or includes unrelated brand terms. <br>
Mitigation: Use competitor material only as reference input, remove unrelated brand terms, and run legal or trademark review where appropriate. <br>
