## Description: <br>
Skill Sedimentation helps agents turn non-trivial task outcomes into reusable experience records and promoted guidance through a Trigger, Retrieve, Act, Reflect, Distill, and Promote loop. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[clawhub-master](https://clawhub.ai/user/clawhub-master) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents and developers use this skill to capture useful task experience, distill recurring patterns, and promote them into future operating guidance while retaining review points for higher-impact changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can automatically record task experience and modify memory or skill files without a fresh user request each time. <br>
Mitigation: Constrain what may be recorded or changed, and review use on tasks involving secrets, credentials, regulated data, private client information, or changes to existing skills and rules. <br>
