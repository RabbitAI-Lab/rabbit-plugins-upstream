## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Waitlist registration sends contact details to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user reviews the exact details and explicitly confirms both the submission and consent statement.

Risk: The preview does not verify security claims or permissions for any future account integration.

Mitigation: Review the provider's claims and requested permissions separately before connecting accounts after launch.
