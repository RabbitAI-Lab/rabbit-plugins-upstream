## Description: <br>
Guides agents in choosing natural-light direction for photography by explaining front light, 45-degree side light, 90-degree side light, and backlight effects and when to use them. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and photography assistants use this skill to answer natural-light direction questions, recommend shooting angles for portraits or outdoor subjects, and distinguish direction guidance from studio lighting or exposure settings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Photography guidance can be style-dependent or condition-dependent, so a recommended light direction may not match every creative goal or scene. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as starting points and verify them against the photographer's preferred sources, style, and on-location lighting conditions. <br>
Risk: The skill may be misapplied to adjacent topics such as studio lighting, exposure controls, or time-of-day light quality. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's boundaries to route artificial-light setup, exposure settings, and golden-hour timing questions to more appropriate guidance. <br>
