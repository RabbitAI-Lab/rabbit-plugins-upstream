## Description:

Creates promotional, social-ad, or product-demo videos from a website URL by capturing the site, deriving its brand, and building and validating a storyboarded video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn a website URL into a promotional, social-ad, or product-demo video through a website-to-video template.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Messages and options are sent to dLazy, and attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive information and attach only files you are comfortable sharing with the service.

Risk: The security assessment noted a mismatch between the website-to-video description and reported CLI login and project-listing commands, and could not fully resolve some references or inspect downstream CLI behavior.

Mitigation: Review the CLI and confirm its commands and behavior before installing or using it.
