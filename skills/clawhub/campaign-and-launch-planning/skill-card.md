## Description: <br>
Plan and orchestrate a product launch or social media campaign end to end as a three-phase story, with campaign briefs, week-by-week maps, social posts, and coordinated WoopSocial scheduling while external systems handle hubs, email, checkout, ads, press, livestreams, and conversion analytics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media operators, marketers, founders, and launch teams use this skill to plan a time-boxed launch or campaign around one primary goal, build the tease-launch-sustain arc, draft the brief and social posts, and schedule coordinated public posts through WoopSocial. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent may draft and schedule public social posts through WoopSocial with incorrect platforms, dates, copy, or permissions. <br>
Mitigation: Review final posts, platform selections, scheduled dates, and account permissions before anything is scheduled or published. <br>
Risk: Launch plans may include urgency, social proof, or performance claims that would mislead users if fabricated or not supported by current evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use only credible urgency, do not invent scarcity or results, and verify campaign metrics through native analytics and the store before publishing claims. <br>
