## Description: <br>
Maps a category's narrative landscape by identifying dominant stories, language conventions, competitor narrative patterns, and messaging drift from current and archived competitor copy. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, positioning, and narrative strategy teams use this skill to map category stories, competitor messaging, and language conventions before developing positioning or brand narrative work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Competitor messaging and archived copy can be outdated, promotional, or not independently verified. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated competitor claims before reusing them and preserve the skill's Measured, User-provided, Estimated, and needs-source labels. <br>
Risk: Saved narrative research may persist user-provided competitive context. <br>
Mitigation: Save results only after confirmation and review the content for sensitive business information before making it durable. <br>
