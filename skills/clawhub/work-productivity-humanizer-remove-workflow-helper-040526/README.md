# Humanizer Text Revision Helper

## What It Does

Revise AI-looking or over-polished text into clear, audience-appropriate writing while preserving truth, author intent, citations, and compliance constraints.

This package was generated from demand signals in run `20260623-040526` and then rewritten for publication with domain-specific workflow guidance instead of generic task scaffolding.

## Best For

Writers, marketers, students, support teams, and skill authors who need natural text revision rather than evasive detector gaming.

## Workflow Summary

1. Identify the audience, channel, author voice, required facts, claims needing citations, forbidden wording, and desired tone.
2. Mark symptoms of weak AI-style writing: vague claims, repeated rhythm, empty intensifiers, unsupported certainty, generic transitions, and unnatural politeness.
3. Preserve factual content and citations first, then rewrite for concrete nouns, varied structure, direct verbs, and context-specific examples.
4. Remove manipulative, deceptive, or detector-evasion framing; focus on readability and authenticity.
5. Offer a minimal edit and a stronger rewrite when useful.
6. Check the final text for factual drift, missing caveats, tone mismatch, and accidental changes to commitments or legal meaning.

## Deliverables

- A revised version of the user's text in the requested voice.
- A short edit note explaining what changed and why.
- Optional voice guidelines for future drafts.
- A risk note for claims, citations, compliance, or sensitive context.

## Quality Bar

- The rewrite keeps facts, intent, and required claims intact.
- The text is more specific and readable without pretending to bypass detectors.
- Tone matches audience and channel constraints.
- Unsupported or risky claims are flagged rather than smoothed over.
