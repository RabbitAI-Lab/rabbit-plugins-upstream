## Description: <br>
Revise AI-looking or over-polished text into clear, audience-appropriate writing while preserving truth, author intent, citations, and compliance constraints. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, marketers, students, support teams, and skill authors use this skill to revise generic or AI-looking drafts into natural, audience-appropriate text while preserving facts, citations, intent, and compliance constraints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad routing language may invite use outside a strict text-revision workflow, including debugging, security hardening, or legal/compliance-sensitive wording. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for writing and revision tasks; route software, security, and legal-exact wording decisions to specialized review before relying on the output. <br>
Risk: Rewriting can accidentally alter facts, citations, caveats, commitments, or legal meaning. <br>
Mitigation: Compare the final text against the source for factual drift, preserve required claims and citations, and add human review for sensitive contexts. <br>
