## Description:

Analyzes pet bowl videos to estimate feeding duration and speed, then provides threshold-based slow-feeding guidance and reports without diagnosing disease.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

## Use Case:

Pet owners and pet-care developers use this skill to review a cat or dog's feeding speed from a bowl-area video, view historical reports, and receive non-diagnostic slow-feeding suggestions.
