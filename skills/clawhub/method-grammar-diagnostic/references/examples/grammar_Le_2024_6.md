# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

After providing informed consent, participants read that we were interested in their impressions about targets based on how they acted when faced with various dilemmas. We then presented participants adapted versions of the 12 scenarios used in Studies 2a and 2b. In each scenario, participants were given information about a target's self-control conflict and then the strategy the target decided to implement. Recall that half of these scenarios were typical inherent conflicts, whereas the other half were typical situational conflicts. Within each set of six, the stimuli presentation program randomly selected three to describe the target as opting for abstinence and the other three to describe the target as opting for moderation.

## 来源文献

Le, D. T. L., Scholer, A. A., & Fujita, K. (2024). The role of conflict representation in abstinence versus moderation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

程序段一般过去时主动/被动交替（read / were given information / the program randomly selected），跨研究材料复用（adapted versions of the 12 scenarios used in Studies 2a and 2b）与随机化规则（randomly selected three...the other three）清晰，是实验程序的语法范本。
