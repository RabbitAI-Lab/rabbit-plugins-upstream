# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

The majority of items were phrased in the affirmative (71%), but we also reversed 25 items, spanning all four content areas, to be framed negatively. This design choice reflected the utility of reversed items to reduce acquiescence bias in measures such as the Berkeley Expressivity Questionnaire (Gross & John, 1997). ... All items conveyed explicit or implicit goals to change one's emotions (Gross, 1998) through social interaction. For instance, "I manage my emotions by expressing them to others." explicitly states an emotional goal.

## 来源文献

Williams et al., 2018, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
