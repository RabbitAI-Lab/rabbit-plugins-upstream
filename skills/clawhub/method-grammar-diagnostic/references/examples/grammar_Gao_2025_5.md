# 片段类型：语法（效度检验的并列句式与阈值引用）

## 原文片段

> To evaluate the validity of the constructs, confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) was conducted, focusing on convergent and discriminant validity. Convergent validity was assessed using composite reliability (CR) and average variance extracted (AVE) for each factor. The CR values exceeded the recommended threshold of 0.7, while AVE values surpassed the benchmark of 0.5, as shown in Table 2. These results indicate that the constructs were well explained by their respective indicators and shared sufficient variance with them (Kline, 2023). Establishing convergent validity ensures that the measurement items effectively represent the underlying constructs they were designed to measure.
> Discriminant validity was examined by comparing the square roots of AVE values with inter-factor correlation coefficients. The square roots of AVE values were greater than the highest squared inter-construct correlations, which ranged from 0.07 to 0.52 (see Table 2). This result confirms that the constructs were distinct from one another, thereby satisfying the requirements for discriminant validity. Together, these validity tests validate the measurement model, demonstrating its ability to accurately represent the hypothesized relationships among constructs.

## 适配诊断点

- 目的状语开头（"To evaluate the validity of the constructs, ..."）+ 被动语态（was assessed, was examined, was conducted）构成方法学写作的标准句式模板。
- 结果判断句使用明确比较结构：exceeded the recommended threshold of 0.7 / surpassed the benchmark of 0.5 / were greater than the highest squared inter-construct correlations，阈值与比较对象都精确给出。
- 每个检验结果后紧跟解释句（"These results indicate that ..."、"This result confirms that ..."），数据-含义一一对应。
- "Together, these validity tests validate the measurement model" 作为总结句收束两个并行检验，语法结构平行、层次清晰。
