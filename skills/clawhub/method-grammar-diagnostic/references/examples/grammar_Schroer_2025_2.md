# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

The eye trackers are worn like a pair of glasses with a scene camera capturing the participant's field-of-view and two infrared cameras to record eye movements, as well as a microphone to record audio. The scene camera records at an average rate of 25 frames/sec with a resolution of 1088 × 1080 pixels. The Invisible requires no calibration and uses a machine learning algorithm for tracking both eyes that is robust to participant movement, changes in lighting, and slippage of the eye tracker (Tonsen, Baumann, & Dierkes, 2020). Each eye tracker was attached to an Android phone for data recording. Toddlers wore a vest with a pocket in the back to hold the phone and their parents wore a running armband.

## 来源文献

Schroer et al., 2025, Developmental Psychology

## 适配诊断点

设备性能与工作原理（are worn, records, requires, uses）用一般现在时，而研究中的具体安装与佩戴操作（was attached, wore）用一般过去时，设备描述与研究操作的时态分工典型。
