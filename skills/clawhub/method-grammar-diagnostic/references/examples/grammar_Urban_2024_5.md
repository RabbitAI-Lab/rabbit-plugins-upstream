# 片段类型：语法（实验程序的时态一致与结构交代）

## 原文片段

> In a first step, participants were asked to fill out questions about their demographic characteristics. They were then randomly assigned to one of three different IATs (environmental protection/environmental degradation, environmental protection/war, and environmental protection/leisure time). Accordingly, we used a between design with the factor reference category that had three levels (same content negative valence: "environmental degradation"; different content negative valence: "war"; different content positive valence: "leisure time"). The order of the blocks, that is, whether the block in which the relevant target category environmental protection and the positive attribute category were on the same response key was the initial combined block or the reversed combined block, was counterbalanced. Upon completion of the respective IAT, participants were asked to answer a series of questionnaire items and exited the experiment.

## 来源文献

Urban, Koch, & Rothermund (2024), Study 3, Method (Design and Procedure). *The Implicit Association Test: A Methodological Investigation of the Relationship Between Test Difficulty and Criterion Validity.* https://osf.io/pcjwf/

## 适配诊断点

- 程序叙述统一使用一般过去时被动语态（were asked, were randomly assigned, was counterbalanced, were asked），时态一致、主语明确。
- 时间顺序连接词（In a first step, Then, Upon completion）构成清晰的程序时间线。
- 在描述具体操纵后立即用"Accordingly, we used a between design with the factor ..."抽象出设计类型，具体-抽象两层表达结合。
- 用插入语（that is, ...）解释反平衡的具体含义，长句内部结构层次分明，标点使用规范。
