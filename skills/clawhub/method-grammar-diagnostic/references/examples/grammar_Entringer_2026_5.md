# 片段类型：语法（指标合成的规范句式与偏离预注册的披露）

## 原文片段

> Affective Well-Being. The SOEP measures affective well-being with four items asking participants, "How often during the last 4 weeks did you feel (a) angry, (b) sad, (c) anxious, and (d) happy." Participants answered these questions on a scale from 1 = very rarely to 5 = very often. We aggregated the four indicators into one affective well-being indicator. To do so, we first reverse-scored the items measuring negative affect. Next, we formed the average across all four items. Hence, higher scores reflect better affective well-being.
>
> (Footnote 8) Here, we deviated from the preregistration by aggregating positive and negative affect into a single indicator (α = .67), despite their moderate correlations (r = –.27 to –.41). This decision was made because positive affect was measured with only five response options, limiting the variance in this dependent variable. Importantly, analyses run separately for positive and negative affect yielded unchanged results; therefore, we report the results based on the composite score in the article.

## 来源文献

Entringer, T. M., Halbmeier, C., Buchinger, L., & Reitz, A. K. (2026), Method (Measures, Affective Well-Being). *Within-Nation Variation in War Exposure and Psychological and Physical Adjustment.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 131(2), 355-379. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000601

## 适配诊断点

- 指标合成步骤化：测量题项原文（四题列出）→ 量尺锚点 → 合成流程（To do so, we first reverse-scored ... Next, we formed the average ... Hence, higher scores reflect ...），First/Next/Hence 递进。
- 合成后的解释方向明确（higher scores reflect better affective well-being），结果解读口径无歧义。
- 偏离预注册的处理范式：deviated from the preregistration + 偏离原因（响应选项少、方差受限）+ 稳健性验证（分开分析结果不变）+ 最终报告决策（composite score），脚注披露完整。
- 信度（α = .67）与相关（r = –.27 to –.41）随合成说明一并给出。
