# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

Setting power at 95% and Type I error rate at p < .05, and assuming an OR = 1.74, the analysis for logistic regression (R2 other X = 0.37) revealed a minimal sample size of N = 607. We collected data of 835 individuals via Prolific. No participants were excluded afterward.

## 来源文献

Reinhardt et al., 2023, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

功效设定以现在分词引导（Setting power ... and assuming ...），主句用一般过去时被动/主动（was revealed, we collected），句法紧凑且时态分工明确。
