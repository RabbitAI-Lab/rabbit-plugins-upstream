# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

We ﬁtted hierarchical linear regression models to assess and compare the direct prediction of intelligence, personality traits, and family SES at age 16 for educational and social–emotional outcomes at age 23. We ﬁrst controlled for within-cohort age variability at age 23 and gender (Model 0), before independently adding intelligence (Model 1), personality traits (Model 2), and family SES (Model 3). Finally, we modeled intelligence, personality, and family SES together (Model 4) to assess the total and independent contributions of all three predictors to each outcome.

## 来源文献

Haider, Z. F., & von Stumm, S. (2022). Predicting educational and social–emotional outcomes in emerging adulthood. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

层次回归的模型推进用一般过去时主动语态（ﬁtted, controlled for, adding, modeled），Model 0→4 的变量加入顺序一目了然，目的从句（to assess ...）贯穿，是统计模型描述语法范本。
