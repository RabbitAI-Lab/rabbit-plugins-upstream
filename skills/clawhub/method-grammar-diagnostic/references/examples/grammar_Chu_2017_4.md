# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

All participants provided electronic informed consent before initiating study procedures, which involved completing an online battery of questionnaires, and were compensated with course credit. Following study completion, participants received a debriefing form that included mental health resources. ... Respondents provided informed consent, filled out a series of self-report questionnaires, and were provided with mental health resources during debriefing.

## 来源文献

Chu, C., et al. (2017). Perceived problem-solving deficits and suicidal ideation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

程序段统一一般过去时主动/被动（provided, were compensated, received, were provided with），知情同意→施测→补偿→事后说明（含心理健康资源）的伦理程序链条完整，是问卷程序段语法范本。
