# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

We measured WMC with an automated symmetry span test (adapted from Redick et al., 2012). Each trial presented two to five processing items in alternation with memory items. Each processing item presented a black-and-white pattern within an 8 × 8 matrix that participants judged (via mouse-click) as either vertically symmetrical or asymmetrical; each memory item was a 4 × 4 matrix with one to-be-remembered square in red, presented for 650 ms. Following each trial sequence, a recall screen appeared that presented a blank 4 × 4 matrix. Participants recalled the two to five red squares from that trial by clicking on their locations in the blank matrix in serial order (participants could click on a "blank" button for any item in sequence that they could not recall).

## 来源文献

Kane et al., 2017, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General
