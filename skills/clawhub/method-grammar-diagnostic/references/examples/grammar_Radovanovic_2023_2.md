# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

Coding was performed by human raters to classify overall trying time, gear stacking rate, overall process praise (all ICC's ≥ .91, all p's < .001), and temporal overlap (90% coded identically). Coding was performed for each participant by a primary coder and a secondary coder; 100% of data were double coded for gear stacking rate and temporal overlap, and 25% of data were double coded for overall trying time (as this variable was only descriptive). Disagreements were resolved by retaining the primary coder's data.

## 来源文献

Radovanovic et al., 2023, Developmental Psychology

## 适配诊断点

编码流程全部使用一般过去时被动（was performed, were double coded, were resolved），双重编码比例与分歧处理策略清楚，时态与程序描述高度规范。
