# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

After providing consent, participants completed a battery of tasks (in the same order) designed to assess their social orientation, self-construal, and cognitive style as part of a larger study titled "Individual Differences in Social and Cognitive Orientation" conducted between December 2019 and February 2021, either in the lab (18.95%) or on their own devices outside the lab (81.05%) using an online questionnaire prepared on Qualtrics. Items within each task were randomized unless indicated otherwise below. With the exception of consent items, the sociogram task (for which at least two ellipses had to be drawn) as well as items necessary for checking inclusion criteria, participants were free to not answer any question in the questionnaire.

## 来源文献

Uskul et al., 2023, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
