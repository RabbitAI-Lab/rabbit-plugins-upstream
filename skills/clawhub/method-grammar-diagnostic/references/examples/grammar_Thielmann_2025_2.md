# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

At T0, participants completed the 60-item version of the HEXACO Personality Inventory–Revised (HEXACO-60; Ashton & Lee, 2009) to measure basic personality traits (including Honesty-Humility; Cronbach's α = .75) and a 70-item measure of the Dark Factor of Personality (Cronbach's α = .95), the D-70 (Moshagen et al., 2020). An example item assessing Honesty-Humility is "I wouldn't use flattery to get a raise or promotion at work, even if I thought it would succeed"; an example item assessing the Dark Factor is "I'll say anything to get what I want." Responses were collected on 5-point Likert-type scales ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). In addition to the HEXACO-60 and the D-70, participants also completed other personality scales not pertinent to the present investigation. All questionnaires were presented in a random order.

## 来源文献

Thielmann et al., 2025, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

量表名称与缩写（HEXACO-60/D-70）、条目数、示例条目（一般现在时 is）、作答方式（过去时 were collected）与信度（α）的混合时态规范段落。
