# 片段类型：语法
## 原文片段
Using MPLUS 6.1 (Muthén & Muthén, 2010), we compared EFA models of one to four factors, with oblique rotation to allow factors to correlate, maximum likelihood robust estimation to account for nonnormality distributions in the data (Kline, 2016a), and the type = complex option to account for the dependence between cotwins within a family. The decision on the number of factors was based on several considerations: First, we examined the Eigenvalues scree plots. Next, we compared the fit indices: comparative fit index (CFI), root-mean-square error of approximation (RMSEA), and standardized root-mean-square residual (SRMR) between the models (Kline, 2016a). We also considered the RMSEA_015 criterion, which suggests that a complex model is preferred over a simpler model if the reduction in the RMSEA index is higher than .015 (Finch, 2020). Third, and most importantly, we opted to choose the most parsimonious model that shows a clear and theoretically meaningful loading structure and replicates across the two assessments.
