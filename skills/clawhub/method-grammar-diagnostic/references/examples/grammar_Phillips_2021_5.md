# 片段类型：语法（量表改编与条目删减的理由句式）

## 原文片段

> Attributions to Discrimination. We measured participants' attributions to discrimination using Simon et al.'s (2019) Attributions to Discrimination Scale. We removed the third item from the original scale because it was specific to the domain of race. Participants thus rated "to what extent do you think the boss was discriminatory in his decision?" and "to what extent do you think the boss' decision was based on discrimination?" (r = .91), using a 7-point scale, 1 = not at all to 7 = extremely.

## 来源文献

Phillips, L. T., & Jun, S. (2021), Study 2 Method (Manipulation and Measures, Dependent Variables). *Why Benefiting From Discrimination Is Less Recognized as Discrimination.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000298

## 适配诊断点

- 量表报告公式化：测量工具（used Simon et al.'s scale）→ 改编声明（removed the third item）→ 删因理由（because it was specific to the domain of race）→ 保留条目原文（引号呈现）→ 内部一致（r = .91）→ 量尺锚点（7-point, 1 = not at all to 7 = extremely）。
- 删条目主动说明理由，避免"为何只有两题"的读者疑问。
- 被动语态与主动语态规范交替（We measured / were removed / Participants thus rated），动作主体清晰。
- 跨研究一致性：同一 DV 在 Study 3（r = .92）、Study 4（r = .71）、Study 7（r = .89）重测并分别报告，信度信息随每次使用更新。
