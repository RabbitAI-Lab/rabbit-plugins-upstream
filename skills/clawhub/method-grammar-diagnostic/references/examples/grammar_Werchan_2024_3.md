# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

Trained experimenters coded the child's attention during the task using a laptop that was programmed to receive online observational ratings every 5s (Feagans et al., 1994). The experimenters coded each 5s interval into the following mutually exclusive behaviors based on the dominant behavior across the 5s interval: look at book, look at caregiver, gaze aversion, look at experimenter/camera, off task, and shared attention. Look at caregiver was defined by intervals in which the child was looking at the caregiver's face. Gaze aversion was defined by intervals in which the child's attention was focused away from the book or the caregiver's face.

## 来源文献

Werchan et al., 2024, Developmental Psychology

## 适配诊断点

编码操作用一般过去时（coded, was programmed, was defined by intervals in which ...），行为类别以冒号列举且每类给出操作性定义，是观察编码语法规范段。
