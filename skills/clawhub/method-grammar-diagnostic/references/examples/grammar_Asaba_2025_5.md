# 片段类型：语法
## 原文片段
In the Test Question Phase, the experimenter then told the child that Teacher Jane and Teacher Susan were nearby and could give feedback on the child's tracings from earlier. The experimenter left the room with the envelopes containing the child's tracings, and returned after approximately 15 seconds with a red sticker attached to one envelope and a green sticker attached to the other. The experimenter first pointed to the envelope with a green sticker and placed a photo of Teacher Jane next to it, and said: "Teacher Jane looked at this tracing and said that this one is great." She then pointed to the other envelope (with a red sticker and Teacher Susan's photo) and said: "Teacher Susan looked at this tracing and said that this one is great" (teacher order and identity counterbalanced). Finally, with the tracings still in the envelopes, the experimenter asked the key test question: "Now you can bring back your best tracing to show your teacher! Which one do you think is the best?" Children responded by pointing to one of the envelopes.
## 来源文献
Asaba, M., Stegall, J., Hembacher, E., Frank, M. C., & Gweon, H. (2025), Experiment 1a Methods (Procedure, Test Question Phase). *Young Children Infer the Informativeness of Others' Praise.* Developmental Psychology. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/dev0002073
## 适配诊断点
- 程序叙述一般过去时贯穿（told / left / returned / pointed / placed / said / asked），动作链时序清晰。
- 时间状语精确（after approximately 15 seconds / first / then / Finally），步骤节奏可复现。
- 言语材料以直接引语逐字呈现（"Teacher Jane looked at this tracing and said that this one is great."），操纵内容零歧义。
- 平衡设计随描述括号标注（teacher order and identity counterbalanced），程序与设计要素同步交代。
- 反应方式明确（Children responded by pointing to one of the envelopes），测量动作无歧义。
