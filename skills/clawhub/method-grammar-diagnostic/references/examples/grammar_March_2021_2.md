# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

As primes, we used 30 Black and 30 White male neutral-faces from the Chicago Face Database (Ma et al., 2015) after cropping each to 500 × 500-pixels confined vertically between the eyebrows and bottom lip and horizontally between the outsides of the eyes. As targets, we used March et al.'s (2017) images of threat, nonthreatening-negativity, and positivity (30 of each; see Figure 1 for example stimuli and online supplemental materials for all stimuli), which (as described by March et al., 2017) were validated through extensive pilot testing such that the threatening and negative sets were both rated (on 1 to 7 scales) as low in positivity (Mthreat images = 1.99, Mnegative images = 1.48), high in negativity (Mthreat images = 4.45, Mnegative images = 4.95), and differed in threat (Mthreat images = 5.78, Mnegative images = 3.20).

## 来源文献

March et al., 2021, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

本研究对刺激的具体处理（we used ... after cropping）用一般过去时，刺激集的恒常验证属性（were validated ... such that ... were both rated）用一般过去时被动，材料来源与验证信息分层表述，是刺激材料描述的时态与结构范例。
