# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

The preferential-looking paradigm was used to measure a toddler’s looking behaviors when shown pairs of faces. The toddler sat on the mother’s lap at a 60-cm viewing distance from a corneal-reﬂection eye-tracker with a 1700 TFT ﬂat screen monitor (Tobii T120 Eye Tracker, Tobii Technology, Danderyd, Sweden). The apparatus recorded gaze data for both eyes at 120 Hz with an average accuracy of .5 visual angles. The gaze data was converted into ﬁxation sequences using the default settings of the Tobii software. Experiment trials began after successful calibration using an audiovisual animation presented sequentially at every corner and the center of the computer screen. The mother was asked to close her eyes during the eye-tracking procedure, and her compliance was monitored with the live camera embedded in the eye-tracking system.

## 来源文献

Wang, L., et al. (2022). On becoming socially anxious. Developmental Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

程序描述统一一般过去时被动/状态动词（was used, sat, recorded, was converted, began after, was asked），设备型号与技术参数（120 Hz、.5 视角）嵌入句中，是仪器类程序段语法范本。
