# 片段类型：语法
## 原文片段
We recruited an online community sample via Bilendi. Consent of each participant was requested in digital form on the first page of the survey, and anonymity of participants was guaranteed. We excluded several participants during data collection because they (a) failed attention checks, (b) applied pattern crossing, (c) were suspected to cheat, or (d) seemed to respond carelessly. Please refer to the Supplemental Material for a detailed description of our procedure that was tailored to ensure sufficient data quality. We strived for a sample size of at least 900 participants to ensure robust estimation of covariances on bivariate level (please see more details on this in the Procedure section). Final sample size was N = 915 (396 females, 517 males), with a mean age of 47.14 years (SD = 15.35, range = 18–79).

- 样本量目标的理由先行（to ensure robust estimation of covariances），与实际 N 的差距如实呈现（至少 900 → 915）。
