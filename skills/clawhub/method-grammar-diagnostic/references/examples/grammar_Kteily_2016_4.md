# 片段类型：语法（跨研究测量的量纲统一与被动语态）

## 原文片段

> Study 3
> Method
> Participants.
> We used a collection service (Solid Data SIA) to obtain a large sample online from Hungary in August, 2014 for an omnibus study (we focus on the variables relevant to the current work). The survey was translated into Hungarian by a native-speaking social psychologist. Of the 1,002 respondents, 12 were excluded for being Roma, and 84 for answering at least one of two attention check questions incorrectly, leaving 906 participants (M age = 42.04, SD = 12.75; 50.7% men).
>
> Measures.
> For all measures not already assessed on a 0–100 scale, scores were converted from their original scales (reported below) to a 0–100 scale for ease of comparison with previous studies. Variables were presented in fixed order.

## 来源文献

Kteily, N., Hodson, G., & Bruneau, E. (2016), Study 3 Method. *They See Us as Less Than Human: Metadehumanization Predicts Intergroup Conflict via Reciprocal Dehumanization.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 110(3), 343-370.

## 适配诊断点

- 翻译程序透明：语言（Hungarian）、译者资质（native-speaking social psychologist）一并交代，跨文化测量的基本要求。
- 排除标准逐类列明且量化（12 excluded for being Roma, 84 for attention check），"leaving 906 participants" 完成样本流汇报。
- 量纲统一的决策及理由显式说明："converted from their original scales ... to a 0–100 scale for ease of comparison with previous studies"，跨研究可比性的权衡被诚实呈现。
- 被动语态（was translated, were converted, were presented）与主动语态（We used）交替使用，符合方法学写作惯例。
