# 片段类型：语法（条件材料的直接引述与对照呈现）

## 原文片段

> While the control persons' introductions were friendly and did not include any information about relevant abilities (example: "My name's [name randomized] and I'm looking forward to solving the task:"), the target behaved either in a norm-violating way ("Really sucks to wait all the time. I don't care who's in the group. When does the damn task start?") or indicated that they were inept at Math ("Hey everybody, I'm [name randomized]. I hope the task is not too difficult, I am really bad with Math ...").
>
> Furthermore, participants answered the same manipulation checks about rudeness, friendliness, and Math skills of the group members as in Studies 3 and 4. Additionally, they were asked to select the nature of the task they were presented with as a manipulation check (multiple-choice, "Please select what kind of task you have been asked to solve." two options: "Task about Communication," or "Task about Performance").

## 来源文献

Rudert, S. C., Möring, J. N. R., Kenntemich, C., & Büttner, C. M. (2023), Study 5 Method (Materials and Procedure). *When and Why We Ostracize Others: Motivated Social Exclusion in Group Contexts.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

- 操纵材料以完整引述呈现（参与者实际看到的自我介绍原文），条件对照（control friendly vs. norm-violating vs. inept at Math）在一句话内用 While 结构并列，语法对称。
- 材料中的随机元素用占位符标出（[name randomized]），明确说明材料模板化与随机化机制。
- 操纵检查分层描述：人物感知检查（rudeness, friendliness, Math skills，沿用 Studies 3-4）+ 新增任务类型检查（multiple-choice，选项原文给出）。
- "Furthermore ... Additionally" 连接词组织多层检查，层次分明。
