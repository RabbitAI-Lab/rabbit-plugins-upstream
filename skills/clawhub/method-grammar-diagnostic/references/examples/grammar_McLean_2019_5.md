# 片段类型：语法
## 原文片段
Growth was rated on a 4-point scale, and was broadly defined as any kind of positive change, development, new insight or lesson, and so forth that enhances the person in some way. A rating of one was given if the narrative displayed no evidence whatsoever of growth. A rating of 2–4 was given if positive growth was present, with the rating determined by the extent to which growth was elaborated on, conveyed as important, and personally transformative. A rating of 2 captured some suggestion of growth that was minimally elaborated and not prominently featured in the narrative; a rating of 3 was given if positive growth was clearly present and either elaborated or emphasized as important or transformative; a rating of 4 was given if growth was an elaborated theme of the narrative and the growth described was clearly conveyed as important and transformative (intraclass correlations, ICCs, range .80–.95 across data sets).
## 来源文献
McLean, K. C., et al. (2019). The empirical structure of narrative identity. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000247
## 适配诊断点
编码锚点逐级定义（rating 1/2/3/4 各自的行为描述）且各级以平行句式展开、锚点定义的"程度"维度（elaborated / emphasized / transformative）贯穿各级、信度区间随定义一并给出，是评分量规（rubric）报告的语法范本。
