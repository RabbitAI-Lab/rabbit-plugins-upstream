# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

We used the R software environment (R Version 4.4.1, RStudio Version 2024.4.2.764) for defining areas of interest (AOIs) and for preprocessing, analyzing, and visualizing the data. As the main dependent measure, we used infants' mean proportional looking time at the novel object during the recognition phase (novelty preference score). ... To calculate the dependent measure, we defined two square-shaped AOIs: one covering the novel object and one covering the familiar object. All AOIs were defined as 1° of visual angle larger than the maximum dimensions of the stimuli to account for potential inaccuracies in calibration (Gredebäck et al., 2010). We assessed the total duration of fixations in both AOIs for each trial and participant, including fixation data from the entire recognition test phase. Data from both eyes were averaged. When one eye could not be measured, we used the data from the other eye.

## 来源文献

Thiele, M., Gredebäck, G., & Haun, D. B. M. (2025). Social connectedness without eye contact. Developmental Psychology.
