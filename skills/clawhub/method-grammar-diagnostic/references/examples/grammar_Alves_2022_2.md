# 片段类型：语法

## 原文片段

Participants were first asked to agree to an informed consent. Instructions then told participants that they would be presented with different pictures over several trials while each trial would feature two pictures. Participants in Experiment 1a were then instructed that their task was to judge how likable they found the person that was depicted on the right (i.e., target). In Experiment 1b, participants were told that their task was to judge how pleasant they found the person (Kanji) presented on the right (i.e., target). And participants in Experiment 1c were instructed to judge the dog (landscapes) presented at the bottom. Thus, while target and context stimuli were shown next to each other in Experiments 1a and 1b, they were shown on top of each other in Experiment 1c.

## 来源文献

Alves et al., 2022, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

全部程序操作统一使用一般过去时被动（were asked, were told, were instructed, were shown），并以 "Thus" 收束对比三个实验的刺激排布差异，时态一致性与对比衔接兼备。
