## Description: <br>
Helps teams add useful unit tests and improve test coverage in existing codebases through a repeatable workflow, implementation support, checklists, and validation advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to plan, add, and verify practical unit-test coverage for existing codebases. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording could cause the helper to be invoked for testing-adjacent requests where it is not wanted. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow trigger terms or disable implicit invocation when the skill should be used only for explicit unit-test or coverage requests. <br>
