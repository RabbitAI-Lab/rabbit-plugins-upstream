## Description: <br>
Write clear customer-facing outage and service-disruption notifications, including status-page updates, customer emails, in-app banners, and resolved summaries in plain, reassuring language. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Support, incident response, customer success, and product teams use this skill to draft customer-facing outage notices from partial incident details. It helps produce phased status-page updates, direct customer email copy, in-app banner text, and a resolved incident summary. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Draft notices may include inferred assumptions, placeholder facts, or inaccurate customer-impact statements when the incident brief is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review and correct all assumptions, scope, timing, affected-service details, customer impact, and next-update commitments before publishing live notices. <br>
