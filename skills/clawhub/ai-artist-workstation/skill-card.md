## Description: <br>
Ai Artist Workstation helps commercial artists and designers route image-generation orders through portrait or general text-to-image workflows, apply style templates, review prompts, and prepare delivery outputs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Commercial artists, designers, and agent operators use this skill to process structured image-order requests into prompts, engine selections, image-generation calls, review steps, and delivery-ready image links. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow can handle customer selfies, prompts, and order details through third-party image providers. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for image-generation order workflows, confirm consent and disclosure before uploading customer data, and avoid unnecessary workspace access. <br>
Risk: The skill asks for broad agent authority, including command execution and file writing. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installing, restrict the agent environment to the minimum required access, and do not rely on claimed encryption, access control, logging, or command restrictions unless the surrounding platform enforces them. <br>
