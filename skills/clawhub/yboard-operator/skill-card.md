## Description: <br>
Operate yboard.online to navigate scenes, edit text fields, switch modes, and add clear, text-free hero visuals following yboard illustration rules. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zonder](https://clawhub.ai/user/zonder) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators and agents working in yboard.online use this skill to update video or presentation plans by selecting scenes, editing planning fields, and adding or replacing scene illustrations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent could edit the wrong YBoard project or scene through the attached browser tab. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the correct project and scene are selected before making changes. <br>
Risk: Generated text or illustrations may be inaccurate, unreadable, or unsuitable for the intended presentation. <br>
Mitigation: Review edited fields and generated images before relying on the result. <br>
