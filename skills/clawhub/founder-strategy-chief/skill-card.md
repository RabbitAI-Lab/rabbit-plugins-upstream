## Description:

Provides founder-focused strategic advice on problem framing, trade-offs, prioritization, evidence synthesis, and Research System governance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tradelink8](https://clawhub.ai/user/tradelink8)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and their advisors use this skill to frame decisions, compare options and risks, prioritize next steps, and review proposed changes to a Research System. Research, implementation, and final commitments remain with the appropriate people or roles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategic recommendations may be mistaken for verified facts or sound commitments.

Mitigation: Verify decision-critical claims and obtain the Founder's approval before spending, contracting, public commitments, or production changes.

Risk: An advisory handoff may be mistaken for completed research or execution.

Mitigation: Assign follow-up work to the appropriate authorized role and confirm results before treating a handoff as complete.
