## Description:

Creates designed presentations from user-provided content and can also edit decks or create documents, spreadsheets, and short animated reels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can ask their AI agent to create designed presentations from prompts or source material, revise existing decks, and export presentation files or a hosted link. The skill can also create documents, spreadsheets, and short animated reels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source material sent for generation may contain confidential figures or personal information and is processed by a hosted service.

Mitigation: Only submit material you are permitted to share with MagicSlides, and avoid sending sensitive information when it is not needed.

Risk: A saved API key could be exposed, or a custom endpoint could send credentials to an untrusted service.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and keep the default endpoint unless the replacement is trusted.

Risk: Generated presentation content may be incorrect or misleading.

Mitigation: Check the requested language and review generated content before sharing.
