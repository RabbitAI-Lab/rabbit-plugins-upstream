Soul Decision Engine Pro guides agents through structured decision analysis with quality scoring, bias checks, decision-tree visualization, domain weight learning, cloud sync, and decision review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, teams, and business users use this skill to add structured decision memory, scoring, bias review, and retrospective workflows to agent-assisted decisions. It is positioned for team and enterprise decision processes where decision quality, bias detection, and cross-device memory consistency matter. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may record sensitive business, HR, client, legal, or strategy decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installing in sensitive contexts, define retention and sharing boundaries, and avoid storing confidential decisions unless the workspace is approved for that data. <br>
Risk: Cloud sync and team sharing can expose decision history without sufficient consent, provider, retention, or access-control detail. <br>
Mitigation: Keep sync disabled until a known cloud provider, explicit team consent, access controls, and retention settings are configured. <br>
Risk: Long-lived cloud API keys may be stored in local files or shell environments. <br>
Mitigation: Use short-lived or managed secrets where possible and avoid committing or persisting API keys in local skill files. <br>
