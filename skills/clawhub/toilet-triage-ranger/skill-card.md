## Description:

Guides toilet leak and weak-flush diagnosis, estimates water waste, and provides repair or plumber-escalation steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renters use this skill to diagnose running or weak-flushing toilets, estimate water waste, decide whether to attempt a repair, and identify when to call a plumber.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: DIY repairs can cause leaks or crack porcelain if parts are overtightened.

Mitigation: Shut off water before working, avoid overtightening, and call a plumber for cracked porcelain or broken flanges.

Risk: Sewage backup, venting problems, or a stuck shutoff valve can exceed safe DIY troubleshooting.

Mitigation: Do not force a stuck valve; seek a plumber for sewage backup, venting problems, or work outside your comfort level.

Risk: Estimated leak rates and costs may differ from actual water use.

Mitigation: Check the water meter before relying on estimates for a repair or rental notice.
