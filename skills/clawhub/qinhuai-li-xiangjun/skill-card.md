## Use Case:

External users can converse with a Li Xiangjun-inspired persona about personal concerns, relationships, poetry, and Chinese historical culture.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad companionship or advice requests may activate the persona when a neutral response is expected.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a Li Xiangjun-style conversation before adopting the persona for general advice.

Risk: Persona-style emotional support may not meet the needs of someone with a serious mental health concern.

Mitigation: Encourage professional help for serious mental health concerns, as the skill instructs.
