## Description:

Creates Xiaohongshu content packs with distinct angles, title options, complete posts, and finished PNG/JPEG covers from a topic, industry, or product, retaining sources and revisions in JishuDB without publishing automatically.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a topic or product into source-grounded Xiaohongshu posts, title options, and usable cover images for human review before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup may install or launch desktop software and change local client configuration.

Mitigation: Approve a concrete installation plan only after reviewing its release, paths, client configuration target, and localhost connection URL; review the unsigned-installer disclosure on Windows.

Risk: Content and source material may be retained as persistent JishuDB records.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended knowledge base and retention scope before transferring material or saving records.
