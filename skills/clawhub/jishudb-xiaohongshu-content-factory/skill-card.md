## Description:

Create complete Xiaohongshu posts, title options, and finished PNG/JPEG covers while retaining sources and revisions in JishuDB; no automatic publishing is included.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn a topic, industry, or product brief into source-grounded Xiaohongshu post copy, title options, usable cover files, and reviewable JishuDB records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow writes records to JishuDB and creates local cover and post files.

Mitigation: Review the selected knowledge base, output directory, retained sources, and write authorization before approving a run.

Risk: The skill could be misapplied to protected Xiaohongshu crawling, account posting, or engagement claims.

Mitigation: Use only authorized public research and keep publishing, protected-platform crawling, and guaranteed reach outside the workflow.

Risk: Generated covers or content packs may be incomplete, inaccessible, or inconsistent with the post text.

Mitigation: Inspect exported PNG/JPEG files, source mappings, and companion Markdown before treating the run as complete.
