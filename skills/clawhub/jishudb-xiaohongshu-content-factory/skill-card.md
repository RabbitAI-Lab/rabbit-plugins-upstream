## Description:

Creates Xiaohongshu content packs with distinct angles, title options, complete posts, and finished PNG/JPEG covers, while retaining sources and revisions in JishuDB without publishing automatically.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a topic, industry, or product into source-grounded Xiaohongshu posts with usable cover images and retained drafts. Users review and publish the content themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup may download and install a desktop application or change local client configuration.

Mitigation: Review the proposed installation and configuration changes, and approve them only when JishuDB-backed retention is wanted.

Risk: Credentials or private material could be exposed during setup or public research.

Mitigation: Do not share passwords or tokens in chat, and keep private user material out of public-search queries.
