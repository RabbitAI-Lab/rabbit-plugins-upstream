## Description:

Creates source-grounded Xiaohongshu content packs with distinct angles, title options, complete posts, and finished PNG/JPEG covers, while retaining sources and revisions in JishuDB without publishing automatically.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a topic, industry, or product into review-ready Xiaohongshu posts and usable cover images, with sources and copy revisions retained in JishuDB.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The content workflow requires JishuDB desktop installation or connection and may change local client configuration.

Mitigation: Use only when that setup is intended; review the installation plan and grant explicit approval before changes.

Risk: Drafts and source material are stored in JishuDB, and finished cover and text files are written locally.

Mitigation: Confirm the storage destination and retention consent, and review local output paths before running the workflow.
