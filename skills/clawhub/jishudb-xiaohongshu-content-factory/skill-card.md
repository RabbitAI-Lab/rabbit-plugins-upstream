## Description:

Creates source-grounded Xiaohongshu content packs with distinct angles, complete posts, title options, and finished PNG/JPEG covers, retaining revisions in JishuDB without publishing automatically.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a topic, industry, or product into complete Xiaohongshu posts, usable cover images, and traceable sources for their own publishing review. JishuDB access and approved storage are required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup may install and launch Desktop and configure a client connection.

Mitigation: Review the concrete installation plan and grant approval before any installation or client changes; declining setup blocks this skill.

Risk: Source material and drafts may be retained in a selected JishuDB knowledge base.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination and write authorization before transferring material or saving records.

Risk: Connecting JishuDB can require OAuth consent or a manually entered token.

Mitigation: Complete authorization through the client or protected entry flow; do not include credentials in task files or chat.
