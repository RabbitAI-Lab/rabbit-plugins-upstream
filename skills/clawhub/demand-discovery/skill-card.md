## Description: <br>
Validate startup, SaaS, app, or product ideas with Demand Discovery AI before building by calling its hosted MCP tools for demand reports, scores, verdicts, and related guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cecilough](https://clawhub.ai/user/cecilough) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to assess whether an idea has real demand before building, pivoting, or stopping. It can request market research, paid demand validation, status checks, framework explanations, and user-reviewed outreach drafts from Demand Discovery AI. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Startup idea details, problem statements, solution descriptions, target markets, and email addresses may be sent to Demand Discovery AI's hosted service. <br>
Mitigation: Share only information approved for that service and avoid sensitive or confidential details unless authorized. <br>
Risk: The paid demand validation flow can return a checkout link for the user to open. <br>
Mitigation: Review any checkout link manually before purchasing; the artifact does not indicate automatic payment. <br>
Risk: Generated reports, scores, verdicts, and outreach drafts could be over-relied on for business decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review returned content before acting on it, and keep outreach user-reviewed and user-sent. <br>
