## Description: <br>
Helps agents explain support and resistance, trendlines, channels, percentage retracements, speed lines, gaps, and reversal days for technical chart analysis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to provide educational technical-analysis guidance on chart levels, trendlines, retracement zones, gaps, and reversal-day signals. It is intended to frame analysis and boundaries, not to provide financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Technical-analysis outputs could be mistaken for financial advice or trading instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational analysis prompts and verify them against independent risk rules before trading. <br>
Risk: Trendline, support and resistance, retracement, gap, and reversal-day signals can be subjective or false. <br>
Mitigation: Require confirmation such as repeated touches, volume or time context, the 3% or two-day filter, and follow-up signals before acting. <br>
