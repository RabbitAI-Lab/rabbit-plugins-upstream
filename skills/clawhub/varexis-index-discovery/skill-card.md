## Description:

Find and preview public VAREXIS-INDEX evidence and explain the capabilities, prices, and limitations of Tool Admission and Audit Coverage without running the checkers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can find public INDEX evidence, inspect its sources and limitations, and understand the published scope and terms of the two declaration checkers. The skill does not run checkers or certify deployments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public lookup paths may receive sensitive information submitted in a query.

Mitigation: Use only public, non-sensitive queries; do not submit private logs, customer declarations, credentials, paid keys, or payment details.

Risk: A bounded or dated evidence preview may be mistaken for a live security assessment or deployment approval.

Mitigation: Preserve the record's scope, dates, sources, and limitations; explain that discovery and checker descriptions do not verify live permissions or certify a deployment.
