## Description:

A practical skill for planning, drafting, distributing, and evaluating accurate, audience-focused news releases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Communications professionals and organizational teams use this skill to assess news value, verify facts, draft releases, plan relevant distribution, prepare for follow-up, and evaluate outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A news release may include inaccurate facts, unapproved quotations, or information that is not authorized for disclosure.

Mitigation: Verify material claims, confirm quotation approval, and review disclosure permissions before publication.

Risk: A distribution service mentioned as an example may not suit a particular announcement or audience.

Mitigation: Independently assess the service and select distribution based on audience relevance, budget, and reporting context.
