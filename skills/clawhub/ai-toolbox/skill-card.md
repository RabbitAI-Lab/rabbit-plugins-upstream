## Description: <br>
AI电商助手，提供竞品分析、爆款文案、客服话术、数据监控和选品推荐，支持多平台内容一键生成。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
E-commerce sellers, operators, and solo store owners use this skill to research public marketplace signals, monitor competitors, generate product listings and customer-service replies, draft daily reports, and evaluate product-selection ideas across Chinese commerce platforms. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated sales content and product recommendations may be inaccurate, non-compliant, or unsuitable for a specific marketplace listing. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated copy, claims, pricing advice, and platform wording before publishing or using it with customers. <br>
Risk: Recurring competitor monitoring and daily reports can create ongoing automated activity beyond a one-time request. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the monitoring scope, schedule, and public-data sources before enabling recurring reports. <br>
Risk: The skill is intended for public e-commerce research and does not require store credentials. <br>
Mitigation: Do not provide marketplace account credentials or private store data unless a separate trusted tool explicitly requires them. <br>
