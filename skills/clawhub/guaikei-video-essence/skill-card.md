## Description:

Transcribes local or linked videos into cleaned text, summaries, translations, and other prompt-directed copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, editors, and teams use this skill to transcribe videos and turn their content into summaries, meeting notes, translations, or other prompt-directed copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A hostile video link could expose private-network responses.

Mitigation: Use trusted video links; prefer a version that blocks private-network destinations and validates redirects.

Risk: The skill's network privacy claims overstate the safeguards.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential recordings until upload destinations and privacy protections are clearly verified.

Risk: Provider charges, retention terms, and locally downloaded copies may affect users.

Mitigation: Confirm provider billing and retention terms, and remove downloaded copies left in the local temporary directory.
