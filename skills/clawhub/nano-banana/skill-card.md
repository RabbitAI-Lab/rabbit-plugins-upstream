## Description: <br>
Helps an agent write brand-grounded prompts for Nano Banana / Gemini image models to generate or edit social-media images such as quote graphics, carousel slides, thumbnails, infographics, product shots, and ad creative. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and social-media teams use this skill to turn a post brief, brand profile, and platform format into concrete image-generation prompts. It emphasizes natural-language prompt craft, text-in-image reliability, consistent visual series, model fit, and publication checks before social use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated social images may contain misspelled text, incorrect infographic data, or misleading details. <br>
Mitigation: Review rendered images, spelling, data, and claims before publishing. <br>
Risk: AI-generated imagery may require disclosure or watermark preservation for the target platform or region. <br>
Mitigation: Keep SynthID/C2PA signals intact and apply platform or regional AI-disclosure requirements before posting. <br>
Risk: Reference images and brand styling can affect rights, privacy, and brand-safety obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Use only authorized brand assets and avoid generating real identifiable people, copyrighted characters, or third-party logos/styles without rights. <br>
