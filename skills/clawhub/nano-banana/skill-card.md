## Description: <br>
nano-banana helps an agent craft brand-aware prompts for Google Gemini/Nano Banana image generation and editing for social media assets, with guidance for text rendering, consistency, aspect ratios, verification, and disclosure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and social-media agents use this skill to draft image-generation prompts for quote graphics, carousel slides, thumbnails, infographics, product shots, ad creative, and conversational edits. It is a prompt-craft guide, not the API or publishing integration. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill points to a separate image-generation and WoopSocial upload integration that may involve credentials or publishing workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review that integration separately before using credentials, generating images, or attaching media to posts. <br>
Risk: Generated images may contain misspelled text, incorrect labels, or inaccurate infographic data. <br>
Mitigation: Verify rendered text and factual data before publishing. <br>
Risk: AI-generated social images can require disclosure and may carry SynthID or C2PA provenance signals. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose AI-generated imagery according to the destination platform and applicable region, and do not strip or disguise provenance signals. <br>
Risk: Requests involving real identifiable people, copyrighted characters, or brand styles can create legal, consent, or brand-safety concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Decline those requests and offer original characters, owned products, or original art direction instead. <br>
