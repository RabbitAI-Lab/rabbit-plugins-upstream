## Description:

Screens startup launches, public traction signals, verified revenue profiles, and crowdfunding campaigns using Product Hunt, TrustMRR, and Kickstarter through the Crawlora API. Use for launch research and acquisition screening, not investment guarantees or private diligence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product teams, and startup analysts use this skill to build dated briefs comparing public launches, traction signals, revenue profiles, and crowdfunding activity. It supports early screening and market research, not private diligence or investment guarantees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crawlora receives the API key and research queries, and requests may consume service credits.

Mitigation: Keep confidential information out of searches and review Crawlora's data handling before use.
