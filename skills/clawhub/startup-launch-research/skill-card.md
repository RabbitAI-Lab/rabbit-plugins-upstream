## Description:

Screens startup launches, public traction, revenue profiles, and crowdfunding campaigns using Product Hunt, TrustMRR, and Kickstarter data through Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, analysts, and acquisition researchers use this skill to compare startup launches and compile dated briefs from public product, revenue, and crowdfunding signals. It is not a substitute for private diligence or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and the Crawlora API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when sharing those queries and credentials with Crawlora is acceptable.

Risk: Public revenue, campaign, review, and ranking signals may be incomplete, revised, or mistaken for investment evidence.

Mitigation: Include collection dates and source URLs, distinguish source-specific metrics, and avoid presenting them as private diligence or investment advice.
