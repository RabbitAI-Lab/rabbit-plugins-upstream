## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat religious-study output as unquestioned authority.

Mitigation: Require exact citations for Quran and hadith claims and review important religious guidance with qualified sources or scholars.

Risk: Memorization tracking may retain study progress in agent environments that support memory.

Mitigation: Avoid storing sensitive personal study details unless the agent memory behavior is understood and acceptable.
