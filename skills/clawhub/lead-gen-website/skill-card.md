### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated lead forms may process personal data and marketing attribution without sufficient privacy controls. <br>
Mitigation: Before production use, remove personal-data console logging and add explicit privacy notice, data minimization, retention, recipient disclosure, and consent handling for attribution data. <br>
Risk: Local lead-generation SEO can become misleading if it uses doorway pages, scaled generic content, fake locations, fake reviews, or unsupported claims. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the bundled anti-spam guardrails: disclose lead-generation status where relevant, use real proof only, avoid city-swapped doorway pages, and review pages before launch. <br>
