## Description:

Decision validation and thinking frameworks for startup founders who need to pressure-test decisions, validate next steps, think through strategic options, or sanity-check approaches across fundraising, customer development, runway management, prioritization, and crypto/web3 challenges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tenequm](https://clawhub.ai/user/tenequm)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache 2.0

## Use Case:

External founders and founder-support agents use this skill to pressure-test startup decisions, structure coaching conversations, and reason through fundraising, runway, customer development, product strategy, and crypto/web3 tradeoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat founder coaching output as professional mental-health, legal, financial, tax, or securities advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as decision-support only and route regulated, high-stakes, or mental-health matters to qualified professionals.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill for decisions outside founder coaching.

Mitigation: Use it when the user is asking for startup-founder decision support and restate the scope before giving substantive guidance.

Risk: Market and crypto ecosystem facts are labeled as current to November 2025 and may become stale.

Mitigation: Verify important funding, market, regulatory, and ecosystem claims against current sources before acting on them.
