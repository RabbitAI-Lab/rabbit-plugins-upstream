## Description:

Scrape social media, generate AI replies, and publish or schedule posts via the HeyRepli API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media managers and developers use this skill to retrieve social content, draft replies, track mentions and analytics, and publish or schedule posts through HeyRepli.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Social content, post URLs, and identifiers are sent to HeyRepli with an API key.

Mitigation: Send only intended content and identifiers; keep the API key private.

Risk: Publishing or scheduling may send unreviewed content to the wrong platform or time.

Mitigation: Review the content, platform, connected account, and scheduled time before publishing or scheduling.
