## Description:

Draft and format Chinese self-media articles as Markdown or HTML from user-approved material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minibeanai](https://clawhub.ai/user/minibeanai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and content creators use this skill to draft or revise Chinese self-media articles from material they provide or approve, with an outline approved before drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Article drafts may contain claims that need verification.

Mitigation: Use only user-provided or approved material, distinguish facts from opinions, and flag claims requiring verification.

Risk: A draft could be saved to an unintended local path.

Mitigation: Require approval of both the final text and the exact destination path before writing a file.
