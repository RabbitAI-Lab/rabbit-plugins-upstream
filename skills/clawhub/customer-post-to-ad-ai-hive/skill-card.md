## Description:

This skill turns authorized customer post images, review videos, or UGC clips into reviewable e-commerce ad plans, editing commands, AI-HIVE generation tasks, and delivery checklists while preserving authenticity and avoiding unsupported claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External merchants, brands, and marketing teams use this skill to convert authorized customer media into structured ad concepts, scripts, prompts, video-editing steps, AI-HIVE generation calls, task records, and acceptance checks. It is intended for e-commerce, social commerce, paid advertising, product seeding, and short-video production workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorized customer media and API-key backed generation may be uploaded to AI-HIVE and may incur cost.

Mitigation: Review prompts, routing, uploaded files, output paths, and pricing snapshots before running commands or submitting generation tasks.

Risk: Customer testimonials, product claims, or ad content could mislead users if source rights, facts, or disclosures are not checked.

Mitigation: Use only licensed media, mark unverified facts for review, avoid fake claims or undisclosed ads, and keep human approval before publication.
