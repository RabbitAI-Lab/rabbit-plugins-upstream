## Description:

Uses everyday analogies, verbal visualization, and step-by-step explanations to help middle- and high-school students understand math concepts rather than memorize formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to build intuition for middle- and high-school math concepts through analogies, spatial reasoning, and short comprehension checks. It is not intended to solve a specific homework problem or assist during an active exam.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and emergency guidance may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the locale and adapt curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Student learning records and reminders may expose minors' data if consent or sharing controls are misconfigured.

Mitigation: Check profile, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, reminder consent, and applicable minor-data rules before enabling these features.
