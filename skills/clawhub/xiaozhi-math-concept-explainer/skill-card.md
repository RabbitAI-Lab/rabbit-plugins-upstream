## Description:

通过生活类比、图解引导和逐步拆分，帮助中文初高中学生理解数学概念并检验理解程度。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students and their educators use this skill to build intuition about mathematical ideas, including algebra, geometry, functions and probability, rather than to solve a specific exercise.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese-speaking K–12 contexts; localize curriculum, privacy and guardian-consent requirements, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, consent practices and crisis help channels may not fit learners outside the intended Chinese-speaking K–12 context.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, privacy and guardian-consent rules, and crisis resources before deploying elsewhere.

Risk: Learning profiles and summaries may be shared with other skills or guardians contrary to a learner's preferences.

Mitigation: Review sharing and reminder controls with learners or guardians; honor pause, deletion and no-sharing choices.

Risk: A learner may disclose a safety crisis during a tutoring conversation.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring and follow the documented crisis referral flow, using help channels appropriate to the learner's location.
