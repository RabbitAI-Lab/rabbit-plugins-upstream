## Description:

帮助初高中学生通过生活类比、图解和逐步拆解理解数学概念，并用复述或新例子检验理解。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to build intuition for algebra, geometry, functions and other mathematical concepts rather than receive solutions to specific problems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, minor-consent handling and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, minor-consent expectations and crisis contacts may not fit users outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these before use in another region or language, and verify local crisis contacts.

Risk: Student learning records or cross-skill sharing may reveal personal learning information.

Mitigation: Use the documented pause, delete and no-sharing controls when profile memory or sharing is unwanted.
