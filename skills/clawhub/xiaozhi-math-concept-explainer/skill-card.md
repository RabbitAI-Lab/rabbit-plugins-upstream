## Description:

Explains Chinese middle- and high-school math concepts using everyday analogies, visualization, and step-by-step reasoning to help students understand why rules work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to build intuition for Chinese middle- and high-school math concepts through analogies, mental diagrams, guided explanations, and comprehension checks; it does not replace dedicated problem-solving support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese K–12 curriculum); adapt curriculum and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles or reminders could be used without appropriate consent or sharing controls.

Mitigation: Enable only where the platform's profile, sharing, reminder, and guardian-consent controls are acceptable; honor pause and deletion requests.

Risk: Mainland-China curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum examples and crisis-help contacts before deployment outside mainland China.
