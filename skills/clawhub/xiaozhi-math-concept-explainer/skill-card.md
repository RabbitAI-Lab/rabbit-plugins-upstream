## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students understand mathematical concepts through everyday analogies, visual explanations, and step-by-step reasoning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students in middle school or the first year of high school use this skill to build intuition for math concepts they do not yet understand, rather than to solve a particular homework problem.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis-help channels may not fit students in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize language, curriculum, minor-consent rules, and crisis referrals before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Learning records and cross-skill sharing may expose a minor's information without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Review platform memory and sharing settings, require appropriate consent, and honor requests to pause, delete, or restrict sharing.
