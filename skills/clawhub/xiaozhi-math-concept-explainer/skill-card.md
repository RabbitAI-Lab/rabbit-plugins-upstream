## Description:

Helps middle- and high-school students in mainland China understand mathematical concepts through everyday analogies, guided visualization, and step-by-step explanations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in mainland China use this skill to build intuition for middle- and high-school math concepts, distinguish similar ideas, and check their understanding. It explains concepts rather than solving specific homework problems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders may be stored or shared beyond student and guardian expectations.

Mitigation: Confirm consent settings before enabling memory, parent visibility, cross-skill sharing, or reminders; honor the student's data controls.

Risk: An everyday analogy may be mistaken for a mathematical proof or applied beyond its valid scope.

Mitigation: State each analogy's limits, return to definitions where needed, and check understanding with a new example or counterexample.
