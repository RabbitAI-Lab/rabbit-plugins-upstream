## Description:

Manufacturing quality management expert for reviewing 8D reports, preparing VDA audits, maintaining IATF 16949 / ISO 9001 / ISO 14001 systems, handling complaints and RCA, managing suppliers and SCARs, and supporting Lean Six Sigma improvement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Manufacturing quality, supplier quality, operations, and audit teams use this skill to structure quality-system work, review 8D and RCA materials, prepare audit responses, and turn quality problems into actionable corrective and preventive actions.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste confidential customer, supplier, or internal quality records into their agent environment.

Mitigation: Share only information approved for the agent environment, and redact customer names, supplier identifiers, proprietary product data, and internal records when they are not necessary for the task.

Risk: The skill provides advisory audit and quality-system guidance that may not reflect the final requirements of a specific auditor, certification body, customer, or legal jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Use the output as preparation and review support, then verify final decisions against official standards, customer requirements, certification-body expectations, and legal or compliance counsel where applicable.
