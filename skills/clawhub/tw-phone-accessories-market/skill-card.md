## Use Case:

External sellers, sourcing teams, and ecommerce operators use this skill to compare Taiwan and Taobao phone-accessory markets, track launches and price changes, evaluate materials and supplier claims, and prepare sourcing, pricing, inventory, B2B, group-buy, and marketing recommendations. It is scoped to accessories and excludes phones, tablets, laptops, watches, and other device bodies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; analysis scope focuses on Taiwan ecommerce and Taobao or China supplier marketplaces.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ecommerce prices, supplier claims, certifications, and safety specifications may be incomplete, outdated, or promotional.

Mitigation: Verify important prices, specifications, certifications, supplier identity, and safety documents against source pages, samples, or official records before purchasing, importing, or selling products.

Risk: Cross-border cost estimates can understate landed cost when shipping, consolidation, taxes, platform fees, payment fees, returns, and warranty friction are missing.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions separate from quoted data and require landed-cost line items before making pricing, inventory, wholesale, or group-buy decisions.

Risk: Battery, wireless charging, MagSafe, and Qi2 accessories can create compliance or safety exposure if title claims are treated as proof.

Mitigation: Require capacity, input/output ratings, thermal behavior, cell details, certification evidence, and compatibility checks for charging and power-bank products.
