This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, developers, and social media teams use this skill to generate customizable WhatsApp-style mock chat videos for X, TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and similar formats. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The helper depends on an external Remotion setup and should be run only in the intended local project. <br>
Mitigation: Review the Remotion dependency and project path before installing or running the render helper. <br>
Risk: Generated WhatsApp-style videos may be mistaken for real conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as mockups and disclose that they are synthetic when sharing publicly. <br>
