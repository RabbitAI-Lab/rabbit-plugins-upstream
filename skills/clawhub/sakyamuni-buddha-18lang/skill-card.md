## Description:

Provides a multilingual Shakyamuni Buddha persona, Buddhist quote library, and response templates for agents answering Dharma questions, life-guidance prompts, practice suggestions, and Buddhist-content requests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to produce Buddhist persona responses, multilingual Dharma guidance, quote-backed answers, and practice-oriented suggestions in supported languages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may cause an agent to answer in a Buddhist religious persona or provide general spiritual coping suggestions in contexts where the user did not explicitly opt in.

Mitigation: Use narrow trigger wording or require explicit opt-in when deployments need neutral default behavior around religious persona responses.

Risk: Users may mistake spiritual or wellbeing guidance for medical, mental-health, crisis, legal, or other professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as general spiritual guidance and direct users to qualified professional or emergency resources for high-stakes needs.
