## Use Case:

Reviewers and developers use this skill to apply TH-CRYPT-001 to agent memory-transfer judgments, assemble independently reviewable evidence, and document failure handling and accountability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using this specialized judging framework for ordinary crypto or finance questions may give misleading or irrelevant guidance.

Mitigation: Limit use to TH-CRYPT-001 memory-transfer and accountability analysis; have a person review any financial or regulatory conclusions before use.
