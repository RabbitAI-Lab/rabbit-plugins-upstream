## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad sensitive-topic templates could lead an agent to produce medical, legal, financial, political, or security guidance that exceeds the user's qualifications or context.

Mitigation: Keep platform safety rules active and require independent qualified review before relying on sensitive-topic outputs.

Risk: Investment and decision-framework content may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat investment and high-impact decision outputs as educational material unless reviewed by an appropriate professional.
