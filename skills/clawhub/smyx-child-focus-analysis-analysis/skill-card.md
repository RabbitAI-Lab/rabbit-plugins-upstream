## Description:

Analyzes study-area video to estimate a child's focus scores and distraction periods, then produces structured reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and teachers use the skill to review visual indicators of a child's attention during a study session.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Children's videos may still be uploaded when analysis is disabled.

Mitigation: Avoid using real children's recordings until this behavior is fixed; confirm guardian consent and the service's retention and deletion terms before uploading.

Risk: Account and credential handling provides unclear user control.

Mitigation: Confirm remote registration behavior and protect local tokens before use.
