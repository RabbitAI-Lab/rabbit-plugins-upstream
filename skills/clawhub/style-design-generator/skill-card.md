## Description:

Generates style design prompts by recombining and adapting 100 bundled font and infographic style schemes across subjects, domains, and output templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, creative teams, and prompt-writing agents use this skill to generate, remix, extend, and adapt visual style prompt schemes for typography, infographics, comics, novels, games, film, product design, and related creative domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on Chinese-language aesthetic reference material that may not match every team's language or style requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts for language, brand fit, cultural context, and visual suitability before using them in a production creative workflow.
