## Use Case:

Shopify merchants can use this guide to understand Agiexa’s planned Google Ads connection, preview reporting and campaign use cases, and decide whether to join the launch waitlist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the planned Google Ads features for capabilities available today.

Mitigation: Explain that this is a prerelease guide and that the tools cannot be called today; direct users to the integration page for launch information.

Risk: Joining the waitlist shares the user's email, role, and any optional link or message with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit waitlist details only after explicit permission, and confirm the details the user chooses to provide.

Risk: Future integrations may receive account permissions broader than a user intends.

Mitigation: Review app and per-agent tool permissions before enabling a future connection.
