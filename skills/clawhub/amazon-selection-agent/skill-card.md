## Description: <br>
Amazon Selection Agent helps experienced Amazon sellers research product categories or specific ASINs through market scanning, competitor analysis, FBA profit modeling, keyword discovery, and Markdown report generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shylamb-token](https://clawhub.ai/user/shylamb-token) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External Amazon sellers use this skill to evaluate product categories, ASINs, keywords, competition, and FBA economics before making sourcing and launch decisions. It is designed for users who already understand Amazon seller terminology and want data-backed selection reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill performs substantial public web research, so market figures, fees, and keyword estimates may be stale, incomplete, or blocked by source availability. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check important metrics with current Amazon, FBA fee, and third-party tool data before making sourcing or advertising decisions. <br>
Risk: Saved product ideas, cost assumptions, supplier details, or report contents may be commercially sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential seller backend data or credentials, and review the saved Markdown report path and contents before sharing. <br>
