## Description: <br>
Reviews PRD and product prototype documents across multiple dimensions, produces structured review reports, and can apply focused document patches after user confirmation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[woai36d](https://clawhub.ai/user/woai36d) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, designers, and product engineers use this skill to review PRD and prototype documentation for completeness, clarity, business-rule consistency, interaction coverage, risk, and implementation readiness. It is especially oriented toward SaaS, B2B back-office, role-permission, and data-sensitive product workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can modify PRD and prototype documents after approval, so unintended files or unreviewed edits could affect product planning artifacts. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the target files before patching and review proposed edits before allowing the agent to apply changes. <br>
