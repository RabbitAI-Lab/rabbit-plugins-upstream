## Description:

Plan, inspect and generate SEO articles on a Timothe Growth Calendar. Use when the user asks about their content calendar, article schedule, planned or published posts, topic clusters, or wants to plan or write SEO articles for their site.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[timothe](https://clawhub.ai/user/timothe)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to inspect a Timothe workspace's SEO calendar, plan topic clusters, schedule or edit article work, and generate SEO articles for their site.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can spend Timothe credits on research, cluster planning, and article generation.

Mitigation: Confirm credit balance and obtain clear user authorization before running expensive actions such as plan_cluster or generate_article.

Risk: The skill can modify scheduled content, generated article drafts, and workspace writing or image styles.

Mitigation: Install it only for trusted Timothe workspaces and review proposed calendar, article, and style changes before applying them.
