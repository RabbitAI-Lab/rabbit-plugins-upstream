## Description: <br>
Plan, inspect and generate SEO articles on a Timothe Growth Calendar for content calendars, article schedules, planned or published posts, topic clusters, and SEO article creation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[timothe](https://clawhub.ai/user/timothe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to connect an agent to their Timothe Growth Calendar workspace, inspect SEO plans, research topics, plan article clusters, and generate or edit scheduled articles. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can spend paid credits on SEO research and article generation. <br>
Mitigation: Check available credits before expensive planning or generation, state the expected cost and output, and get clear user approval before starting paid long-running work. <br>
Risk: The skill can create, edit, or delete calendar and article content in the connected Timothe workspace. <br>
Mitigation: Read the current calendar, plan item, article, or workspace style before changing it, and confirm destructive or expensive edits with the user. <br>
Risk: Low-cost research tools may be used after broad topic approval and still consume credits. <br>
Mitigation: Set a credit budget for research when stricter control is needed and report approximate credits spent when returning findings. <br>
