## Description:

大藏经CBETA2026 supports simplified Chinese CBETA 2026 Buddhist canon retrieval and citation verification, with modes for direct answers and deeper research that require CBETA source citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, educators, and researchers use this skill to look up Buddhist canon passages, verify citations, compare related canonical texts, and produce answers or research notes with CBETA locations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic quotation work may require either Simplified or Traditional Chinese source text.

Mitigation: Verify the required script and edition before relying on copied quotations.

Risk: Results depend on the specified CBETA2026 knowledge base being available and covering the requested passage.

Mitigation: State coverage gaps clearly and avoid reconstructing or inventing canonical text when the retrieved material is insufficient.
