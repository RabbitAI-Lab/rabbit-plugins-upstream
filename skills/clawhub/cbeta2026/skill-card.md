## Use Case: <br>
External users and researchers use this skill to query CBETA Buddhist texts, verify scriptural citations, compare passages across the Buddhist canon, and produce answers or research notes with CBETA-standard source locations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be treated as a general scholarly source even though it is designed to answer from a specific CBETA knowledge base. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to clearly separate external sources from CBETA results and to state when the CBETA corpus does not cover the request. <br>
Risk: Unsupported or incomplete CBETA retrieval could lead to fabricated Buddhist-text quotations or overconfident citation claims. <br>
Mitigation: Require CBETA-standard source locations for every substantive claim and use coverage-limit notes when source evidence is insufficient. <br>
