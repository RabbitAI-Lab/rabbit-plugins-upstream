## Description:

Applies hexagonal architecture isolating domain from infrastructure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and software architects use this skill to decide when hexagonal architecture is appropriate and to plan ports, adapters, contract tests, and dependency boundaries for systems that need infrastructure independence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad architecture trigger can activate this skill in generic architecture conversations.

Mitigation: Narrow activation terms to phrases such as "hexagonal architecture" or "ports and adapters" when tighter routing is needed.

Risk: Architecture guidance can be misapplied to small scripts or prototypes where port and adapter abstraction adds overhead.

Mitigation: Review the skill's "When NOT To Use" guidance before applying the pattern.
