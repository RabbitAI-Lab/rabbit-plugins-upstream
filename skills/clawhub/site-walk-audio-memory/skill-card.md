## Description:

Turns construction site-walk audio and dictated notes into searchable records of punch items, safety observations, and subcontractor commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Construction supervisors and project teams use this skill to store site-walk audio or notes and retrieve punch items, safety observations, and subcontractor commitments for follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Site-walk notes and audio may contain sensitive project or personal information sent to BlueColumn.

Mitigation: Confirm BlueColumn is approved for project data and review its retention, deletion, access-control, and privacy terms before use; do not submit regulated, personal, legal-strategy, or highly confidential incident details without approval.
