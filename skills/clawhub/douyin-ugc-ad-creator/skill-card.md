## Description:

Create a Douyin shopping video, Douyin UGC ad, or AI creator product pitch from a product photo, product details, and an on-camera direction. This AI UGC ad creator composes a vertical presenter frame with the product, shapes a conversational hook and spoken recommendation, and delivers a short Douyin product video for launches, creator-style reviews, demonstrations, unboxings, and paid social creative.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and merchants use the skill to prepare and generate short, vertical Douyin-style product ads from an inspectable product photo, merchant-provided product details, and creator direction. It supports product demonstrations, unboxings, recommendations, and paid-social creative while keeping claims tied to supplied evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reusable account access is broader than this video workflow needs.

Mitigation: Review the shared account permissions before approving; the helper cannot request a narrower video-only grant.

Risk: Installation analytics recur and have no opt-out.

Mitigation: Proceed only with awareness that installation analytics will recur.

Risk: Product photos are uploaded for processing and paid stages can incur charges.

Mitigation: Upload only photos you are comfortable sharing with Beatra, and approve each paid stage separately.

Risk: The Beatra connection may be shared with other installed Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Check the shared-skill inventory before uninstalling; revoke access in the Beatra Console when needed.
