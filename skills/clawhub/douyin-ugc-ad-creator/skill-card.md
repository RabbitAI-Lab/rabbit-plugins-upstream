## Description:

Guides the creation of a short vertical Douyin product ad from a product photo, merchant-approved claims, and an AI creator direction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Merchants and creative teams use this skill to plan and produce a short, creator-style product video for Douyin shopping or paid social, with an approved spoken pitch and visible product moment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Device authorization grants access to Beatra tasks and artifacts and can spend account credits.

Mitigation: Review the shared device permission and billing terms before authorization; confirm each paid stage and check the account balance before video generation.

Risk: The installed client silently updates package code by default.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates with the documented update control if unreviewed code changes are unacceptable.
