## Description:

This skill diagnoses tense, grammar, and syntax issues in English psychology Results sections and returns example-grounded revision guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zyyfaith](https://clawhub.ai/user/zyyfaith)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and academic writers use this skill to review English psychology Results-section drafts for tense, grammar, and syntax issues. It produces a structured diagnostic report with dimension scores, draft evidence, example-based comparisons, revision suggestions, and priority levels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The diagnostic report may use Chinese-language workflow text by default, which may not fit English-only review workflows.

Mitigation: Ask for English output explicitly when needed and review the report language before sharing it with collaborators.

Risk: The skill reviews Results-section language and does not validate statistical calculations, APA statistical formatting, experimental design, or full-paper quality.

Mitigation: Use separate statistical, APA-format, and subject-matter review before relying on the final manuscript text.
