## Description:

Checks English psychology-paper Results sections for tense, grammar, and syntax issues, and provides evidence-based diagnostics and revision suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zyyfaith](https://clawhub.ai/user/zyyfaith)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic writers use the skill to review draft psychology Results sections for tense consistency, grammar, and sentence structure, then receive a structured report with explanations and suggested revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested language edits may change the intended meaning if applied without considering the surrounding research context.

Mitigation: Review each suggested edit in context before applying it.

Risk: Grammar feedback does not validate statistical calculations or research design.

Mitigation: Verify statistical results and research design separately using appropriate domain expertise.
