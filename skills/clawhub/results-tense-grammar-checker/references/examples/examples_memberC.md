# 一、Tense（时态）

## Example ID：C-T01

Source：Cheung & Lucas，2016，Journal of Personality and Social Psychology（Results, Analysis 1）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Results showed that higher household income was significantly associated with higher levels of life satisfaction (b = 0.31, SE = 0.002)."

Why it is useful：
用一般过去时（showed, was）报告本研究的具体回归结果，是 Results 报告结果的规范时态。

Reusable Rule：
描述本研究获得的具体统计分析结果时使用一般过去时（showed, was, were, revealed, indicated）。

Possible Application：
当用户草稿中出现 "Results show that…"（现在时）描述本研究结果时，引用此例提示改为过去时 showed。

## Example ID：C-T02

Source：Verduyn et al.，2015，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Study 1 Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"This analysis revealed a significant interaction, F(2, 126) = 4.04, p = .02, η2 = .06, uncorrected."

Why it is useful：
统计检验结果（ANOVA 交互作用）用过去时 revealed 报告，并附完整统计量，写法规范。

Reusable Rule：
报告统计检验结果时使用过去时动词（revealed, showed, indicated）＋ 完整统计量（F, p, η2）。

Possible Application：
检查用户是否把统计结果写成现在时（如 "The analysis reveals…"），引用此例修正。

## Example ID：C-T03

Source：Luhmann & Hawkley，2016，Developmental Psychology（Results, Gender 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Women were significantly lonelier than men (see Table 1), even after controlling for all other covariates (see Table 2)."

Why it is useful：
组间比较结果用过去时 were ＋ significantly 比较结构，规范简洁。

Reusable Rule：
报告组间差异时使用 were/was significantly ＋ 比较结构（X were significantly … than Y）。

Possible Application：
对照检查用户组间比较句的时态与比较结构。

## Example ID：C-T05

Source：Mathur & Reichling，2016，Cognition（Experiment 1B Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Ratings of likability showed little within-subject clustering (ICC = 0.08; 95% CI: 0.06, 0.11), suggesting that individual subjects did not differ greatly in overall propensity to like robot faces in general."

Why it is useful：
过去时 showed 报告结果＋现在分词 suggesting 作概括说明，展示"具体结果过去时＋概括现在分词"的规范搭配。

Reusable Rule：
具体测量结果用过去时（showed, revealed）；用 suggesting/indicating 现在分词短语引出概括性解释。

Possible Application：
示范"结果句＋suggesting 概括"的常见规范句式。

## Example ID：C-T07

Source：Cheung & Lucas，2016，Journal of Personality and Social Psychology（Results 开头）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Table 1 and Table 2 present descriptive statistics and bivariate correlations for the variables used in the current analyses."

Why it is useful：
引用表格用一般现在时 present（表格现在"呈现"内容），与报告结果用过去时形成对照。

引入图表/表格时使用一般现在时（Figure X shows…, Table X presents…, As shown in…, X illustrates…）。

Possible Application：
"present/show 引图表用现在时"的核心教学例，帮助用户区分图表引用句与结果报告句。

## Example ID：C-T08

Source：Verduyn et al.，2015，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Study 1 Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"As Figure 1 and Table 2 illustrate, neither passive use nor active use participants displayed changes in affect immediately following the manipulation."

Why it is useful：
图表邀请语 As Figure X illustrates（现在时）与结果陈述 displayed（过去时）在同一句中分工清晰。

Possible Application：
示范"图表邀请＋结果报告"混合句的时态分工。

## Example ID：C-T11

Source：Cheung & Lucas，2016，Journal of Personality and Social Psychology（Results, Analysis 1）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"This is consistent with a large body of research that shows that one's own income is positively correlated with life satisfaction."

Why it is useful：
引用前人研究/普遍结论时用一般现在时（is consistent, shows, is correlated），与报告本研究结果（过去时）形成段落内时态分工。

Reusable Rule：

Source：Beatty-Martínez et al.，2020，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Results, Picture naming 段末）

Original Sentence / Paragraph：

## Example ID：C-T13

Source：Bleidorn et al.，2016，Journal of Personality and Social Psychology（Results, RCR Model 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-tense

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"In line with prior research, we found a small but significant gender difference suggesting that across all nations, males scored on average 1.85 T-score points higher on self-esteem than did females (average Cohen's d = 0.25)."

Why it is useful：
整体规范（过去时 found/scored），但 suggesting 现在分词引导的概括内容中混有结果数值；属"可优化"而非错误，用于说明时态边界的灰度判断。

Reusable Rule：
结果数值陈述保持过去时；概括性解释可用现在分词/现在时，注意句内一致性。

Possible Application：
当用户句子"过去时＋现在时"混用时，判断属于规范分工还是混乱，引用此例作灰度参考。

# 二、Subject-Verb Agreement（主谓一致）

## Example ID：C-S01

Source：Hartshorne et al.，2018，Cognition（Results, 3.1）

Dimension：tense-grammar-subject-verb-agreement

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Note that all analyses are conducted in terms of log-odds rather than percent correct."

Why it is useful：
复数主语 analyses 搭配复数谓语 are conducted，主谓一致正确。

Reusable Rule：
复数主语（analyses, data, results, scores）必须搭配复数谓语（are/were）。

Possible Application：
检查用户复数主语是否误配单数谓语。

---

## Example ID：C-S02

Source：Schonert-Reichl et al.，2015，Developmental Psychology（Results, Salivary cortisol 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-subject-verb-agreement

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"A series of ANCOVAs were conducted to check for mean baseline differences between the MindUP and BAU children."

Why it is useful：
"a series of ＋ 复数名词"结构，谓语与复数名词 ANCOVAs 一致（were conducted），常见易错点。

Reusable Rule：
"a series of / a number of ＋ 复数名词"时谓语用复数（were conducted, were performed）。

Possible Application：
检查用户 "a series of…" 结构的主谓一致。

## Example ID：C-S03

Source：Beatty-Martínez et al.，2020，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Results, Picture naming 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-subject-verb-agreement

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Although the data reported so far can be explained in terms of how bilinguals use their languages, it is possible that individual variability in language dominance might have washed out the frequency asymmetries."

Why it is useful：
data 作复数名词，谓语 can be explained（复数），主谓一致正确。

Reusable Rule：
data 是复数名词，谓语用复数（are/were/can be）；常见错误是 "data is/shows"。

Possible Application：
当用户写 "data shows…" 时引用此例，提示 data 为复数。

## Example ID：C-S07

Source：Schonert-Reichl et al.，2015，Developmental Psychology（Results, Math grades 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-subject-verb-agreement

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Analysis of final math grades on a grade metric (9 = A, 8 = A−, . . . 1 = C) were analyzed via ANCOVA, controlling for age, gender, and ESL."

Why it is useful：
主语 Analysis（单数）与谓语 were analyzed（复数）不一致；且 "Analysis…were analyzed" 语义重复（分析被分析），为真实的主谓一致＋措辞问题。

Reusable Rule：
主语为单数名词（analysis）时谓语用单数（was）；避免 "Analysis of X was analyzed" 式语义重复。

Possible Application：
当用户出现单数主语配复数谓语或语义重复时引用此例。

# 三、Articles（冠词）

## Example ID：C-A01

Source：Verduyn et al.，2015，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Study 1 Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-articles

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Gender did not moderate the effect of type of Facebook usage on affective well-being across time, F(2, 122) = .31, p = .73."

Why it is useful：
定冠词 the 用于特指本研究中的变量（the effect, the manipulation），冠词使用规范。

Reusable Rule：
特指本研究中的具体变量/效应时用 the。

Possible Application：
检查用户是否在特指变量处漏掉 the。

---

## Example ID：C-A02

Source：Mathur & Reichling，2016，Cognition（Experiment 1A Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-articles

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Treating face as a unit of analysis, face ratings spanned nearly the entire possible range of both human-resemblance and mechanical-resemblance."

Why it is useful：
不定冠词 a 用于泛指（as a unit）、定冠词 the 用于特指（the entire range），泛指/特指区分清楚。

Reusable Rule：
首提/泛指可数概念用 a/an；特指或已明确对象用 the。

Possible Application：
检查冠词首提/回指用法是否正确。

# 四、Parallel Structure（平行结构）

## Example ID：C-P01

Source：Verduyn et al.，2015，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Study 1 Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-parallel-structure

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Neither passive use nor active use participants displayed changes in affect immediately following the manipulation."

Why it is useful：
neither…nor… 平行结构规范：neither passive use nor active use，两个并列成分结构对称。

Reusable Rule：
neither…nor… / either…or… / both…and… 两侧成分结构需对称。

Possible Application：
检查用户 neither/nor 结构是否对称。

## Example ID：C-P02

Source：Luhmann & Hawkley，2016，Developmental Psychology（Results, Work status 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-parallel-structure

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Overall, loneliness levels were highest among those who do not work at all and lowest among those with full-time jobs, with those with other occupations in between these two groups."

Why it is useful：
highest among… and lowest among… 平行对比结构，两侧均为"最高/最低＋among 群体"。

Reusable Rule：
对比最高/最低时保持结构对称：highest among X and lowest among Y。

Possible Application：
检查用户对比句结构是否对称。

faster, but no less accurate 平行对比（比较级＋比较级），简洁对称。

## Example ID：C-P07

Source：Cheung & Lucas，2016，Journal of Personality and Social Psychology（Results, Analysis 2）

Possible Application：
检查用户比较句两侧结构是否平行，过长定语建议拆分。

# 五、Sentence Fragment（句子片段）

## Example ID：C-F01

Source：Mathur & Reichling，2016，Cognition（Experiment 1B 小节标题）

Dimension：tense-grammar-sentence-fragment

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Experiment 1B: perceptions of likability"

Why it is useful：
小节标题为无动词名词短语，是"合法的句子片段"：标题位置允许名词短语，正文不允许。

Reusable Rule：
名词短语作小节标题是规范写法；同样的短语若出现在正文独立成句，即为句子片段错误。

Possible Application：
当用户把标题式短语写进正文时，引用此例说明正文需要完整主谓结构。

# 六、Run-on Sentence（run-on）

## Example ID：C-R01

Source：Hartshorne et al.，2018，Cognition（Results, 3.1）

Dimension：tense-grammar-run-on-sentence

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Although prior work on critical periods has tended to use percent correct, this is problematic."

Why it is useful：
Although 引导让步状语从句＋主句，长句连接规范，无 run-on。

Reusable Rule：
长句依靠连接词（although, because, whether, which, when）组织；两个独立句仅用逗号连接即为 run-on。

Possible Application：
当用户草稿出现逗号连接两个完整句时，引用此例示范用连接词改写。

---

## Example ID：C-R02

Source：Verduyn et al.，2015，Journal of Experimental Psychology: General（Study 1 Results）

Dimension：tense-grammar-run-on-sentence

Type：Positive Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Although we expected passive Facebook usage would lead participants to view their own life as worse compared to others, a 2 × 2 repeated measures ANOVA on this variable did not reveal a significant effect of condition or condition by time interaction."

Why it is useful：
复合长句组织规范：Although 从句＋主句＋or 并列宾语，各成分边界清晰，无 run-on。

Reusable Rule：
复合句用连接词/从句组织；检查连接成分是否明确，避免两个独立句以逗号相连。

Possible Application：
与 C-R01 组成 run-on 正例对。

## Example ID：C-R07

Source：Schonert-Reichl et al.，2015，Developmental Psychology（Results, Peer nominations 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-grammar（重复词/编辑残留）

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"No other significant baseline differences were found between groups on our assessment of peer acceptance (i.e., liked by peers) were found, F(1, 94) = .001, ns."

Why it is useful：
"were found" 在句中出现两次（were found…were found），为真实编辑残留错误；说明权威期刊也需逐词检查。

Reusable Rule：
同一谓语动词在句内重复出现是编辑残留高频问题，诊断时需检查重复成分。

Possible Application：
当用户草稿出现动词重复时引用此例，提示逐词检查。

## Example ID：C-R08

Source：Bleidorn et al.，2016，Journal of Personality and Social Psychology（Results, Hofstede 段）

Dimension：tense-grammar-grammar（拼写错误）

Type：Problem Example

Original Sentence / Paragraph：
"Nations with relatively higher scores on uncertainty avoidance were marked by a lager gender gap in adolescence (R2 = 13%)."

Why it is useful：
"a lager gender gap" 中 lager 为 larger 的拼写错误（lager 本义"贮藏啤酒"），真实拼写疏漏；说明拼写检查也应纳入诊断。

Possible Application：
当用户出现形近词误用时引用此例。
