## Description:

A low-pressure spoken English practice skill that supports free conversation, scenario role-play, gentle expression correction, difficulty switching, and phrase review for everyday fluency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rrnomoney](https://clawhub.ai/user/rrnomoney)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English learners use this skill to practice everyday spoken English through natural conversation or role-play scenarios, receive light corrections at appropriate pauses, and review reusable phrases from the session.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad Chinese requests about practicing spoken English.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants spoken English practice and ask for the preferred mode and difficulty before continuing.
