## Description:

大话职场 is a Chinese-language workplace coaching skill that turns classic management, leadership, productivity, strategy, influence, career-planning, and Chinese workplace-relationship frameworks into practical advice for workplace questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and workplace coaching users use this skill to reason through productivity, management, leadership, communication, career-planning, strategy, and Chinese workplace relationship scenarios. It provides concise frameworks, practical next steps, and conversation guidance rather than legal, medical, financial, or company-specific determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate for broad work, productivity, career, and communication questions, which may make generic workplace concerns look like cases for a specific management framework.

Mitigation: Use only the most relevant one to three frameworks, confirm the user's role and situation, and keep advice grounded in the user's stated context.

Risk: Chinese workplace relationship norms and advice about discretion or hierarchy could be misapplied as permission for unethical behavior, policy violations, or harm to coworkers.

Mitigation: Frame cultural guidance as context-sensitive judgment, preserve legal and ethical boundaries, and avoid advice that enables harassment, retaliation, deception, or rights violations.

Risk: Users may ask about labor rights, harassment, health, finance, or company-policy disputes where workplace coaching is not authoritative.

Mitigation: State that the skill is not a substitute for legal, medical, financial, or qualified professional advice and direct users to appropriate professionals or official policy channels.
