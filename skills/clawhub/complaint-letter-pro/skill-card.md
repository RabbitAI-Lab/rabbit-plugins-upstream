## Description:

Complaint & Rights Letter Pro helps users draft structured complaint and consumer rights letters for consumer disputes, logistics issues, service complaints, property disputes, labor rights matters, 12315 complaints, and similar scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn dispute facts, evidence details, and requested remedies into polished complaint letters, review text, complaint-channel suggestions, evidence checklists, and communication scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save complaint facts, user preferences, notes, dispute history, and improvement feedback during a sensitive dispute workflow.

Mitigation: Require clear user consent before saving any sensitive facts or preferences, define retention limits, and disable or remove automatic learning by default.

Risk: Generated complaint letters and legal references may be incomplete or incorrect for a user's jurisdiction or facts.

Mitigation: Ask the user to review all facts, requested remedies, deadlines, and legal references before sending or filing the complaint.
