## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill uses CellCog with an API key and may send prompts or uploaded images to that service. <br>
Mitigation: Use an appropriate CELLCOG_API_KEY and only submit prompts or images that are acceptable to send to CellCog. <br>
Risk: Reference photos, likenesses, brands, mascots, or team images can involve rights or consent concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Use reference material only when you own it or have permission from identifiable people, brand owners, or mascot rights holders. <br>
