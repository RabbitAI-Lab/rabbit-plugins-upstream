AI sticker generator and pack creation powered by CellCog for custom sticker packs, emoji sets, WhatsApp stickers, Telegram stickers, Discord emoji, Slack reactions, character-consistent expressions, transparent backgrounds, batch generation, and platform-ready packaging. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to prompt CellCog to create consistent sticker packs, emoji sets, and platform-ready reaction assets from original characters, brand mascots, or uploaded reference photos. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Photo-based prompts may send personal, pet, team, or brand reference images to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Only upload images the user has permission to use and is comfortable sending to CellCog. <br>
Risk: Generated stickers for mascots, people, or brands may require rights review before use. <br>
Mitigation: Review source image permissions and generated assets before publication or distribution. <br>
