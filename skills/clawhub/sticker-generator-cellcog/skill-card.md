## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, uploaded reference images, personal photos, brand assets, or private team material may be sent to the CellCog service during sticker generation.

Mitigation: Review CellCog's terms and data handling before use, and avoid sensitive or unapproved material unless the user has confirmed it is appropriate to share.

Risk: The skill requires CELLCOG_API_KEY for external API access.

Mitigation: Store the API key in the agent environment or secret manager, avoid logging it, and do not commit it to skill files or generated outputs.
