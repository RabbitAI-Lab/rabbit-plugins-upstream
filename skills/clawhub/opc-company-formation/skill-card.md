## Description:

Guides solo founders through China one-person company formation and operation, including entity selection, registration decisions, filing workflow, tax basics, compliance, changes, cancellation, licensing, and liability safeguards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External solo founders and small-business operators use this skill to understand the lifecycle of forming, operating, changing, and exiting a China one-person company. It is a structured reference guide and checklist, not legal, accounting, or tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company formation, filing, tax, and compliance guidance can become outdated or vary by local authority.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with the relevant market regulator, tax authority, accountant, or lawyer before acting.
