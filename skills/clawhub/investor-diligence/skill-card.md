## Description:

Produces sourced company diligence memos covering funding, leadership, traction, and risks, plus a comparison when researching multiple companies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors and analysts use this skill to verify a company's identity, funding, team, traction, and potential red flags before an investment discussion. It prepares a sourced memo for each company and a comparison when several are requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow depends on Glasser and its external data providers.

Mitigation: Review and trust the Glasser dependency before use, and submit only company targets you are comfortable researching through external providers.

Risk: Provider calls may incur charges, especially across multiple companies and research dimensions.

Mitigation: Check pricing before calls and limit collection to the dimensions needed for the diligence question.
