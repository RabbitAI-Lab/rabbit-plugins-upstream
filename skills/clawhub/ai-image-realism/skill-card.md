## Description:

Repairs visible AI-image artifacts with focused retouching while aiming to preserve the image's identity, product details, and composition.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and teams use the skill to repair visible defects in existing AI portraits, product images, and marketing visuals without unnecessarily changing accepted parts of the image.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests account permissions beyond image editing.

Mitigation: Review the full account grant before approval and do not approve access you are unwilling to provide.

Risk: Removing the skill can disconnect other Beatra skills if shared installation inventory is damaged.

Mitigation: Check other Beatra skill installations before removal, and avoid cleanup when the shared inventory appears damaged.

Risk: Images are processed by Beatra, and image operations can be paid.

Mitigation: Review the image and proposed operation, and confirm before any paid work.
