## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language skill to answer an evidence-based eight-question capability assessment across product thinking, domain depth, second skill, and AI leverage. The agent calculates totals, identifies strengths and gaps, and produces a prioritized 90-day action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose sensitive career, client, or paid-work details while providing evidence for scores.

Mitigation: Ask users to anonymize client names, financial amounts, and private business details unless they explicitly choose to share them.

Risk: Assessment results may be mistaken for a professional credential, diagnosis, income forecast, or career advice.

Mitigation: Label conclusions as self-assessment, avoid rankings or predictions outside the provided score bands, and recommend professional support for serious career or financial distress.

Risk: Non-Chinese users may misunderstand the workflow, labels, or generated recommendations.

Mitigation: State that the source workflow is Chinese-language and offer translated summaries only as an aid, not as a replacement for the original assessment labels.
