## Description:

Guides apparel sellers through turning garment photos into AI try-on videos with consistent models, fit-check motion, and size captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fashion and apparel sellers use the skill to create product try-on videos from garment and model photos, with fit-check shots and size captions for sales channels such as Shopify, TikTok Shop, and Amazon.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reported workflow sends model and garment images to an external service.

Mitigation: Use only images you are authorized to share, and review the service's handling of uploaded images.

Risk: The installer and repository are not pinned, so later installs may differ from the reviewed version.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed release and verify the version before installing again.
