## Description:

Creates clothing try-on video ads from garment photos by dressing a model, animating fit-check clips, and adding size captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fashion and apparel sellers use an agent to turn product or flat-lay clothing photos into try-on video ads with fit-check motions and size captions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Identifiable people or confidential product images may be sent to Varg and other named AI services.

Mitigation: Use images you are authorized to share and check each provider's privacy policy before uploading sensitive or identifiable material. Never use a real person's likeness without consent.

Risk: Generated garment details or fit may differ from the real product and mislead shoppers.

Mitigation: Compare the result with the garment photo, show realistic drape, and caption the model's height and size. Add required AI-generated content labels.

Risk: Image and video generation consumes paid credits.

Mitigation: Check the expected credits and cost before generating stills and clips.
