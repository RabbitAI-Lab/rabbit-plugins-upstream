## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generic triggers such as patterns, shared, and validation may activate the skill more often than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use narrower trigger phrases or confirm the user wants shared-pattern guidance before applying it. <br>
Risk: Reusable guidance may be copied into a skill or hook without matching the caller's actual contract. <br>
Mitigation: Review the selected pattern against the target skill or hook and run the caller's tests after adoption. <br>
