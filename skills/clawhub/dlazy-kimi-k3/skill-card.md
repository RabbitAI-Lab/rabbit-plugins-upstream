## Description:

Moonshot AI thinking model with text, image, and video understanding, suited to complex analysis, coding, and writing that needs long reasoning chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can send prompts to Kimi K3 for complex reasoning, coding, and writing, with optional image and video inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The external @dlazy/cli package was not inspected by the scanner, and the review was partial.

Mitigation: Review the package and its publisher before installing; the skill specifies CLI version 1.2.3.

Risk: Prompts and supplied local image or video files are sent to dLazy hosted services, and use requires an API key.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive content unless permitted by your organization's policies and the service terms; protect and rotate credentials through the dLazy account controls.
