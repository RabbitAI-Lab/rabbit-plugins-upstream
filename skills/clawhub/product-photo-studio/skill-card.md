## Description:

Transform a real product photo into a studio-quality ecommerce image, lifestyle scene, or marketplace-ready hero shot. This AI product photography tool replaces backgrounds, improves lighting, and stages scenes while using the source photo and confirmed product details as the visual anchor. Create clean white-background listings, contextual lifestyle compositions, and premium ad visuals from a single phone snap for Amazon, Taobao, Shopify, and social media. Start from one product photo, combine several references, or refine a selected draft toward a polished listing image.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorization includes access to media and voice operations unrelated to photo editing.

Mitigation: Review the browser permissions before connecting; this version requires broader access, so do not connect if that scope is unacceptable.

Risk: Selected photos and prompts are sent to Beatra, and approved generation consumes credits.

Mitigation: Submit only appropriate images and prompts, and approve paid generation only after reviewing the request and expected credit use.

Risk: Installation reporting cannot be disabled, and a connected device may be shared with other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Account for reporting before installation; to disconnect fully, revoke the device in Beatra's console.
