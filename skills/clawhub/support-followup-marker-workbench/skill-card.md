## Description:

Build a support case comment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Support operations staff use this skill to turn a supplied support digest into a concise case comment for handoff or follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated support case comment may be inaccurate or unsuitable if the supplied support digest is incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Review the generated comment before posting it to a real support system.
