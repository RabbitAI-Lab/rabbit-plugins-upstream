## Description: <br>
Guides agents through turning long-form video into reviewed, captioned, watermark-free short-form clips using tools such as Opus Clip, CapCut, and Submagic. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, social media teams, and content operators use this skill to repurpose podcasts, webinars, and long videos into self-contained Shorts, Reels, or TikToks with reviewed captions, clean exports, and human approval before publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: External video and scheduling services may receive uploaded media or publishing permissions. <br>
Mitigation: Use those services' normal privacy, account, and rights controls before uploading or scheduling content. <br>
Risk: Auto-generated clips and captions can be incomplete, off-brand, inaccurate, or misleading if published without review. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human review before publishing; check clip context, captions, brand fit, watermarks, and AI-edited disclosure. <br>
