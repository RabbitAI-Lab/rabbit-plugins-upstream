## Description:

降AI味 helps agents revise Chinese text that sounds machine-generated by identifying stock AI-tone patterns, asking for missing author details, and producing a more specific rewrite with review notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External writers, editors, and agents use this skill to revise Chinese drafts that read as generic or machine-generated while preserving facts, author voice, and disclosure boundaries. It is intended for writing quality, not for bypassing school, publication, or workplace AI-use rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Revisions can become misleading if missing facts, data, quotations, or personal details are invented.

Mitigation: Ask the author for concrete details, preserve original facts, and use pending-detail placeholders when evidence is missing.

Risk: Users may try to use rewriting to hide AI assistance where disclosure is required.

Mitigation: Decline requests to bypass school, publication, or workplace AI-use rules and remind users to follow applicable disclosure requirements.

Risk: Surface-level style metrics can be mistaken for proof that text was or was not AI-generated.

Mitigation: Present script results only as advisory writing signals and avoid claims about AI-detection outcomes.
