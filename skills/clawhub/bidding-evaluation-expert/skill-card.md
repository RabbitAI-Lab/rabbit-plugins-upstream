## Description: <br>
Provides PRC Bidding and Tendering Law evaluation support for bid review, bid rejection risk, collusion indicators, complaints, tender document review, and compliance analysis for construction-related projects. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and procurement, legal, compliance, and bid-evaluation teams use this skill for decision support on PRC bidding-law evaluation questions, including bid rejection, collusion indicators, complaint strategy, tender-file review, and regional compliance risks. It is not a substitute for formal legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for formal legal advice in bid evaluation or complaint decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat responses as decision support only and obtain qualified legal review for matters involving significant rights, remedies, or compliance exposure. <br>
Risk: Tender or bid materials may contain confidential information when using external knowledge-base connectors. <br>
Mitigation: Do not send confidential tender or bid materials through external connectors unless the organization has approved that workflow. <br>
Risk: The skill is limited to the PRC Bidding and Tendering Law framework and excludes government procurement or unrelated enterprise purchasing scenarios. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the project type and legal framework before relying on the analysis; redirect out-of-scope matters to government-procurement specialists, enterprise counsel, or other qualified reviewers. <br>
