## Description:

Uses Amazon product details and customer review evidence to identify title information gaps and recommend improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and operators use this skill to review product details and customer feedback, spot gaps in a product title, and receive evidence-based title recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broader account tools and confirmation gaps may lead to unexpected charges.

Mitigation: Configure ARI to ask before every charge and review the returned quote before approving paid work.

Risk: Collection schedules and competitor monitoring can create recurring charges.

Mitigation: Choose manual collection for one-time work and review active schedules and competitor monitoring.
