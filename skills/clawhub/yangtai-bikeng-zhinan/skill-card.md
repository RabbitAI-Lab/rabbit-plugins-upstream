## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Under-qualified advice on wall removal or exterior mounting could lead to structural or property-rule violations.

Mitigation: Check structural limits, local codes, permits, and property rules; seek licensed professional review before work.

Risk: Plumbing, waterproofing, kitchen or bath conversion, and fire-related suggestions may overlook drainage, ventilation, or fire safety.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as inspiration and verify drainage, waterproofing, ventilation, and fire safety with qualified professionals before acting.
