## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation examples may encourage unsafe wall removal or balcony enclosure.

Mitigation: Require property-management approval, local code checks and a licensed structural professional before structural or enclosure work.

Risk: Plumbing, waterproofing and electrical suggestions may omit site-specific safety requirements.

Mitigation: Do not skip waterproofing or alter plumbing or wiring based on this guide alone; consult licensed local professionals.

Risk: Converting a balcony into a bedroom or bathroom may violate local requirements.

Mitigation: Treat conversions as ideas only until permits, property rules and local codes are checked.
