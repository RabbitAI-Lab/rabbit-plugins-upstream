## Use Case:

External users and home-renovation advisors use this skill to answer balcony renovation questions by matching the user's layout, budget, and project stage to curated balcony reference material. It is intended for practical planning guidance, not as a substitute for licensed structural, facade, plumbing, waterproofing, electrical, permitting, or property-management review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Balcony guidance may touch structural demolition, facade or window changes, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical work, and property-management or permit decisions.

Mitigation: Require licensed professional review and local permit or property-management approval before acting on safety-critical or regulated recommendations.

Risk: The security summary reports off-topic, promotional, and sometimes unsafe renovation guidance in the reference set.

Mitigation: Review referenced material before deployment, remove or clearly separate promotional content, and avoid presenting unsupported renovation claims as authoritative.

Risk: Users may rely on general balcony-renovation advice for a specific home with unknown building constraints.

Mitigation: Ask for layout, building age, existing waterproofing, window/facade constraints, and budget, then frame answers as planning guidance with escalation points for professionals.
