## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: NAS, Docker, and remote-download setup guidance can affect local services, mounted directories, stored files, or network exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Review exact commands before execution, verify volume and port mappings, back up existing data, and prefer least-privilege configuration over privileged containers when possible. <br>
Risk: Download guidance can be misapplied to copyrighted or unauthorized content. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for legal resources, respect copyright and service terms, and avoid requests for cracked memberships or bypassing access restrictions. <br>
Risk: Download and upload tuning can disrupt shared home or office networks and consume storage unexpectedly. <br>
Mitigation: Set bandwidth limits, check available disk space, and coordinate heavy download schedules with other network users. <br>
