## Description:

Helps create jewelry design drafts, hand-drawn and multiview sketches, and interactive 3D ring previews from text and image references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ai-hub-admin](https://clawhub.ai/user/ai-hub-admin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Jewelry designers and customers use this skill to turn design ideas and reference images into ring and other jewelry concepts, sketches, and hosted 3D previews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Design prompts, reference image URLs, session names, tags, and results are sent to an external hosted service.

Mitigation: Share confidential designs, personal photos, phone numbers, or private engraving text only when approved for external processing.

Risk: Returned design and session share URLs point to externally hosted content.

Mitigation: Review generated links and their contents before sharing them with others.
