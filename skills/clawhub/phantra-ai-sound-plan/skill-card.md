## Description:

Helps creators plan scene-specific diegetic sound, identify video models that render audio, and place music, recorded voice, effects, and captions in the appropriate post-production steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to write sound plans for video scenes, including per-shot diegetic audio and a closing sound line. It also guides them on which audio elements belong in the prompt and which should be added during editing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid audio or video renders can spend studio credits.

Mitigation: Show the current quote and obtain explicit approval before a render; do not approve on the user's behalf.

Risk: The security review advises correcting the skill's frontmatter formatting before relying on installation.

Mitigation: Have the publisher correct the frontmatter formatting before installation.
