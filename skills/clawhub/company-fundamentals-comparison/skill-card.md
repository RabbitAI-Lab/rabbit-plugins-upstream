## Description:

Compare public companies' reported financial statements across matched fiscal periods using SEC EDGAR data and Yahoo Finance industry context. Use for sourced peer metrics, not stock valuation or buy/sell recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and researchers use this skill to compare public companies' reported financial statements across matched fiscal periods. It produces a sourced peer matrix and concise comparison while preserving reporting differences and gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
