## Description: <br>
Product Validation Planner helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analyses, or implementation-support artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before major investment. It structures discovery inputs into practical validation plans, reusable workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning, code changes, or decision-support artifacts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad product, startup, or validation-related requests. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly or narrow its trigger language when tighter deployment control is required. <br>
Risk: Advisory product-validation outputs may shape business decisions from incomplete user inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions, constraints, success criteria, validation notes, and follow-up work visible in the final response. <br>
