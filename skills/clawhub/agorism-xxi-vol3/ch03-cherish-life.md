# Chapter 3: When Did It Become A Crime To Cherish Life? — Paul Rosenberg

## Core Idea
Life is the only counter-entropic force in the universe, and human consciousness its greatest expression — so the restraining of life "is a sin against the universe itself." Dominance hierarchies permit cherishing life only until it obstructs a "higher purpose"; after that, it's crime. The whole history of rule is the history of higher purposes eclipsing life.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Superman opening**: Jor-El accepting preventable deaths rather than disobey "the council" — the higher purpose made explicit ("the great law which made civilization possible"); clumsy scene because it grasps at superstition to justify idolatry of abstractions over persons.
- **Life as counter-entropy**: matter wears down; life organizes against entropy (plants, bees, and preeminently human creativity — "able to counter entropy at will"). Individuals are the problem-solving units (healthy family, small business, all voluntary activity); Einstein: "Everything that is really great and inspiring is created by the individual who can labor in freedom."
- **Elite capture (Rosenberg's coinage)**: hierarchies assimilate individual breakthroughs, then attribute the benefits to themselves — early Internet veterans (BBS, crypto, digital cash) "watched this happen and wept." The containment-structure image: beams erected over a living dome-organism until sealed; smart regiments keep it barely alive, dumb ones kill it and blame the organism.
- **A short history of higher purposes**:
  1. *Tribal*: troop cohesion via loyalty/treason binary — insiders cherished, outsiders not.
  2. *Violence*: thuggery plain.
  3. *Extensions of violence*: terror via reputation — "thugs with legends" (Nimrod) and horror stories (flaying, walling-up).
  4. *Confusion*: sympathetic magic — priesthoods convincing people they're worthless, need gods, owe production to gods (with a sacrifice-collector class attached).
  5. *Plato's magic society*: replacing gods with a mythical perfected society — "the same something for nothing deal... the 'secret sauce' of politics and it's deeply fraudulent." Post-Socratic philosophy holds mass-control as an unexamined primary.
- **Atrocity requires abstraction**: "Neither Stalin, nor Mao, nor Hitler... could have killed one tenth as many people without a higher cause to cloud and confuse the minds of normal humans." Without "reign of abstracts above concretes," the great atrocities don't happen.
- **The Tolstoy/Jesus close**: "The State is a conspiracy designed not only to exploit, but above all to corrupt its citizens" / "That which is highly esteemed among men is abomination in the eyes of God."

## Key Concepts
- **Higher purpose**: any abstract elevated above concrete living persons — the operative licensing condition of atrocity.
- **Sacrifice collectors**: the priestly class administering god-payment.
- **Enculturation**: the "humans inherently suck" belief is modern-urban enculturation, not a default.

## Mental Models
- Use *abstract-vs-concrete test* on any political demand: does the abstract outrank visible persons? If yes, that's the crime-licensing move.
- Use *containment-structure image* to audit institutions: are the beams sealing the organism? Is it alive-but-choked, or dead-and-blamed?
- Use *problem-solving-unit lens*: treat individuals (families, small firms, voluntary groups) as the primary units; hierarchies as assimilators, not creators.

## Anti-patterns
- **Cherishing-within-limits**: accepting the tribe's loyalty binary as morality's ceiling.
- **Something-for-nothing politics**: Plato's deal in any era — perfect-society membership benefits in exchange for obedience.
- **Attribute-the-breakthrough**: crediting hierarchies for the individual creations they captured.

## Worked Example
The early-Internet arc told as elite capture in four beats: individuals build (BBS, fiber, strong encryption, digital cash) → life blesses the world through them → beams arrive (regulation, licensing, surveillance, "higher purposes" of security/order) → the organism is sealed; the hierarchy claims the internet as ITS creation. Those who lived it "wept along the way" — the containment structure erected over their own life's work.

## Key Takeaways
1. Life = counter-entropy; consciousness = its sharpest instrument; suppressing it is cosmic-scale sin.
2. Cherishing life is permitted until it blocks the hierarchy's higher purpose — then it's crime.
3. Higher purposes evolved: tribe → terror → gods → Plato's perfect society; all are the same fraud.
4. Atrocity scales only through abstraction; persons kept invisible.
5. All real progress comes from individuals laboring in freedom; hierarchies only capture it.
