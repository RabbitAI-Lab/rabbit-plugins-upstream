# Chapter 2: Black Flag White Paper — Cody Wilson

## Core Idea
The Gatalog (Jstark's successors) invoked copyright over 3D gun files — and in doing so became a gun-control organization: "in the name of resisting traceable firearms, gun registration and confiscation, we… register our design files with the federal government." A legal-theoretic takedown AND a Lacanian reading of ideology: "The Gatalog didn't need copyrights when it had influence. When it lost influence, it required them."

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Copyright as property (two theories)**: (1) *Dignitary* — personal, unassignable moral rights (Berne, VARA); reputational + communicative dimensions. (2) *IP-theory* — assignable exclusive-use property in intangibles, "entirely a creature of federal statute" dressed in Lockean natural-rights romance ("theft," "piracy" as ideology terms). The Gatalog made both claims simultaneously — dignitary for FGC-9, IP for TacDaddy.
- **Creative Commons ≠ open source**: CC licenses ARE copyright licenses — "some rights reserved" reproduces the commodity form and EXPANDS IP scope. FOSS licenses (GPL's "all rights reversed," copyleft) exist to INVERT copyright — a hack using corporate-liberal law against arms control. Gun printing began FOSS precisely for this.
- **Federal CAD**: Dec 2023, Gatalog registered files with the U.S. Copyright Office (SF5, Amigo Grande, P99) — first federally registered guncad. Wilson: "We grant users no rights at all. The use of our files isn't your right but rather our mercy... The best term is 'gun control.'"
- **Star Athletica separability test**: copyright protects applied art, NOT industrial design; artistic features must be (1) perceivable as separate 2D/3D art AND (2) independently protectable. 3D gun models = industrial design (functional decisions only; "we wouldn't want to take artistic liberties with pin hole placement") → NOT copyrightable. The Plastikov practicum: a creator models a copyright statement INTO the assembly — the only copyrightable element is the statement itself; "by gently removing this, we return the model to legal insignificance" — producing a new sculptural copyright (the extracted text) + a re-authored file whose "heightened artistic value" comes through "determinate negation."
- **Gravedigger diagnosis**: normative/moralistic approach absent concrete politics; "mendacious idealism" ("teaching 'love of community' as fear of neighbor"); Free Men Don't Ask → "Free Men Must Ask, a preachment of timidity." Bankruptcy hazard: copyrights are assets — lose ONE Everytown suit and a trustee auctions the files' rights.
- **Ideology in action (Lacan/Edelman)**: legal categories ("the author") interpellate subjects as owners — "through copyright as imaginary relation, we recreate each other as 'owners' of valuable 'works'"; the university discourse quadripode (S2→a, S1→S) — beneath dispensing knowledge, the Gatalog's agent "only produces more castration"; master signifiers adopted via hysterical identification. "There is an artist's right to destroy. My vandalism of the Plastikov is the only artistic part of it now."
- **FEDCAD.com epilogue**: Wilson announces a directory for "respecting and contacting all 3D gun rightsholders" — the parody made flesh. "Sail under this flag, young pirates and fear no copyright! The Royal Navy has no power here."

## Key Concepts
- **Separability**: the legal hinge — can the art be lifted out of the useful object? If not, no copyright.
- **Determinate negation**: removing the legal element to produce the artistic one — destruction as authorship.
- **Copyrights as bankruptcy assets**: registration creates the seizure vector (inversion of Wilson's earlier defense work).

## Mental Models
- Use *separability audit* on any "IP" over functional files: if the only art is the license text, delete it and both works improve.
- Use *influence→copyright curve* as institutional diagnostic: organizations acquire IP claims exactly as they lose moral authority.
- Use *license-purpose check*: does this license invert the regime (FOSS hack) or reproduce it (CC commodity form)?

## Anti-patterns
- **Believing copyright is real**: folk meanings of legal terms controlling strategy instead of the hack's original purpose.
- **Registering files with the state** to police the community you claim to liberate.
- **Locke/Jefferson as theater**: "using the dead to bury the living."

## Worked Example
The Plastikov v4 practicum end-to-end: creator embeds copyright statement in the 3D model → the statement is the ONLY separable element → you delete the text → you now hold (a) a new copyright in the extracted sculptural statement-fragment and (b) an unencumbered, legally-insignificant gun file with "heightened artistic value through determinate negation" — while the original creator's claim evaporates. Property law's own machinery turned against property claims over weapons files.

## Key Takeaways
1. CC licenses are copyright licenses; FOSS licenses are anti-copyright hacks — the difference is political.
2. Industrial design (functional 3D models) isn't copyrightable; only separable art is.
3. Federal registration of guncad = gun control by other means.
4. Copyright claims index lost influence (Lacanian reading).
5. Destruction can be authorship; negation can be art.
