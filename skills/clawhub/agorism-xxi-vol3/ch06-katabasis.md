# Chapter 6: Katabasis Chronicles (Extract) — Beth McCarthy & Joshua Dávila

## Core Idea
A science-fiction transmission from ETHCC 2140 in Neo-Brussels: the network-state world fully materialized (Bitcoin's last inflation event, AR-everything, longevity drugs, NFT-gated reality) — and something older stirring beneath it, seen only through the right keys. Fiction as the volume's closing proof-of-concept: the agorist future rendered as lived texture.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Worldbuilding as argument**: the extract doesn't argue — it instantiates. Layer 5-6 blockchains, dying web2 giants, decentralized social adoption via Argos AR glasses, "Ethereum account given at birth to everyone born after the year 2100" (network-state citizenship as physiological fact).
- **NFT-locked perception**: the sigil on the Bourse visible only to holders of the right NFT — Gibson's consensual hallucination meets comhrún; entire layers of reality gated by cryptographic possession. What you can SEE depends on what you hold.
- **The 2140 texture**: Bitcoin's final inflationary schedule countdown (the last satoshi era as festival); Optimism's 20-year vested grants; longevity-drug ambassador programs; Eleusis cryogenics preparing the first live resurrection; Longzempic daily injections; pod-living. McKenna's archaic revival by other means — psychedelia replaced by metabolic tech, ritual by conference-and-raave liturgy (Schelling Point, "Raave").
- **Katabasis**: the title's Greek — the descent (Odysseus into the underworld). The chronicle's premise: something from the depths (of history? of the network? of death itself — cryogenic return) is calling; Tiaa answers. The agorist cycle's next turn begins underground.

## Key Concepts
- **Sigil**: occult-symbol-meets-access-token — the sacred and the cryptographic merged into one artifact class.
- **Neo-Brussels**: the city-state form persisting (linguistic split, EU husk, independent innovation hub) — patchwork's European patch.

## Mental Models
- Use *fiction-as-stress-test*: when a future feels coherent as narrative detail (you can smell the conference floor), the framework behind it has survived extrapolation.
- Use *perception-gating* as coming-UI paradigm: AR + tokens = reality with access control; design accordingly (who sees what).

## Anti-patterns
- Reading it as mere branding fiction: the specific details (final inflation, birth-accounts, longevity economics) are each load-bearing extrapolations from the volume's frameworks.

## Worked Example
The sigil scene as comhrún literalized: Tiaa holds NFT memberships in secret societies joined at university — old-world initiatic structure persisting INSIDE the network-state world. The symbol is invisible to friends who "have a lot of the same NFTs" but lack the specific one: vertical encryption (initiates-only) reborn as ERC-gated AR. When the sigil pulses, the ancient call arrives through the newest medium — katabasis via blockchain.

## Key Takeaways
1. The network-state future is already narratable — that's the argument.
2. Perception itself becomes token-gated infrastructure.
3. Initiation structures (secret societies) persist into crypto-native modernity.
4. Katabasis: the next cycle begins with a descent.
