# Chapter 0: Introduction — Beyond the End of the World — Rachel-Rose O'Leary

## Core Idea
The volume asks "what lies beyond the end of the world?" — using The Sovereign Individual's economic forecasting as the prophecy framework, and declaring the war between surveillance and anonymity "the fundamental axis from which the order of violence flows" in the Information Age.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **End-times prophesies as the ambient condition**: all major religions (Mahdi, Third Temple, Second Coming), secular Rome-analog decline-reading — "we live in an era of end-times prophesies."
- **The Sovereign Individual (1997) as crypto's prophecy**: forecast via economic incentives ("drop a hundred-dollar bill... someone will soon pick it up"); correctly anticipated crypto, P2P, mass immigration, inequality, COVID, cyberwarfare, encryption. Its 500-year-cycle reading: the nation-state tide is receding.
- **Logic of violence (the volume's master concept)**: "the underlying economic conditions under which violence pays" — mega-politics (technology, climate, microbes) reshapes it. Feudalism fell when plague raised labor costs; printing broke the Church's info monopoly while gunpowder rewarded scale → the nation-state. Information Age reverses both: wealth goes intangible/seizure-resistant, cyberspace favors "light infantry" cyber-guerrillas over industrial force.
- **The unfulfilled prophecy + the new front**: 25+ years on, the logic hasn't recalibrated — because mass surveillance retains the state's advantage. Encryption-abundance and perfect anonymity are the precondition. Hence: "the war between surveillance and anonymity... the twin goddesses of digital power."
- **Volume map**: Jorjani (oceanic outlaw society), Wilson (pirate copyright paradox), Rosenberg (Life vs structures), Lujan (network states from the future), Dunkel (modernity vs tradition war), McCarthy & Dávila (transmission from 2140).

## Key Concepts
- **Rome's founding end-time myth**: twelve eagles = twelve centuries; the Great Year rebirth myth (Eliade's eternal return again).
- **"Dark Age will not be the End of Time but rather the beginning of a new cycle"** — the journal's stance.
- **Editorial trinity restated**: reject political divisions, embrace dissonance, uncompromising.

## Worked Example
The introduction's own method: taking The Sovereign Individual seriously AS prophecy (not economics paper) — testing its claims like scripture ("has the Republic fallen?") while its forecasting tool stays incentive analysis. The journal extends it: if the prophecy lags, identify the blocking variable (surveillance) and make that the battlefield.

## Key Takeaways
1. History's "logic of violence" shifts with technology; information tech shifts it toward the small and seizure-resistant.
2. The prophecy's missing precondition is anonymity — hence surveillance-vs-anonymity IS the war.
3. End-times framing is a feature: new cycles, not terminus.
4. The volume stages alliance and conflict across the pluralistic agorist landscape by design.
