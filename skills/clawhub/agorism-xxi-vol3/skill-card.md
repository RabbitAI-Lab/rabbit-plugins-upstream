Mitigation: Use it for critical, historical, or legal-theory discussion only, and require qualified legal review before applying governance, jurisdiction, or compliance ideas.

Risk: The skill includes material about firearm CAD files and modification of embedded copyright text.

Mitigation: Do not use it for operational weapons design, file modification, distribution, or legal compliance decisions; keep responses at a critical or historical analysis level.
