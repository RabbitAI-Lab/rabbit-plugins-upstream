## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language renovation advice skill that helps homeowners identify quote, contract, construction, and budget-overrun add-on traps before paying or signing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Homeowners and renovation clients use this skill to ask concise Chinese-language questions about renovation quotes, contracts, hidden add-ons, construction-stage upcharges, and whether specific renovation choices are worth the cost. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill provides practical renovation, contract, safety, and product-health advice that may not match a user's local rules or professional requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Users should verify contract, legal, safety, and health-related guidance against local professionals or official consumer-protection sources before acting. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill in general renovation cost discussions where its opinionated add-on avoidance posture is not the user's intent. <br>
Mitigation: Agents should confirm the user is asking about renovation add-ons, quotes, contracts, or cost traps before relying on the skill's advice. <br>
