## Description: <br>
Comprehensive audit of all construction data sources and systems. Map data flows, identify silos, assess quality, and create integration roadmap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Construction data, operations, and digital transformation teams use this skill to inventory project systems, map data flows, identify silos, assess data quality, and plan integration improvements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Survey responses and exported reports may contain sensitive system inventory or business-process details. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for approved construction data-source audit work, avoid including secrets or credentials, and review Excel, CSV, JSON, or Markdown outputs before sharing. <br>
Risk: The skill works from user-provided files, datasets, or paths, so inaccurate or inappropriate inputs can lead to misleading audit findings. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only specific approved files or datasets, validate inputs before processing, and review findings against source-system owners before acting on recommendations. <br>
