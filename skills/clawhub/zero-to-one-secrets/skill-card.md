## Description:

Guides users in testing whether a non-consensus business insight is a falsifiable basis for a startup or product idea.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product teams, and people exploring business ideas can use this skill to surface and stress-test a proposed market insight. It guides them through a falsifiable claim, reasons others may overlook it, and a low-cost validation test while keeping research and experiments under their direction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A business idea assessment may be mistaken or misleading if treated as proof of market demand.

Mitigation: Use the analysis as brainstorming and evaluation guidance, then validate claims with evidence; keep any customer research and experiments under the user's direction.
