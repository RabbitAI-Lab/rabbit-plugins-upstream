## Description: <br>
Helps production supervisors and quality engineers identify, label, verify, and trace 4M manufacturing change points to reduce uncontrolled change risk. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Production supervisors, line leads, process quality engineers, process engineers, and manufacturing or quality managers use this skill to capture manufacturing change points, assign risk prompts, record verification status, and produce traceable change-point ledgers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Risk levels produced by the skill may be mistaken for formal release approval. <br>
Mitigation: Treat risk levels as prompts only and require responsible quality personnel to confirm verification criteria, results, and release decisions. <br>
Risk: Generated ledgers may contain local production change records. <br>
Mitigation: Handle TXT and Markdown ledgers under the organization's normal quality-control document retention and access process. <br>
