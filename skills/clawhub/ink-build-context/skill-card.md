## Description:

Collect up to five public sources into a local, cited Markdown context pack for an AI task.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lukax](https://clawhub.ai/user/lukax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers can collect public documentation, repositories, PDFs, or videos into a source-attributed pack to support an AI task.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The dependency workflow may disclose names from local project manifests to public registries, and the helper was only partly inspected.

Mitigation: Review the helper and decide whether the external disclosure of package names is acceptable before using the workflow.
