## Description: <br>
Transform feature briefs into structured design briefs that give designers the context they need before opening Figma. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, designers, and product teams use this skill to turn feature briefs or PRDs into design handoff briefs. The brief captures user goals, emotional context, success criteria, constraints, edge cases, and explicit out-of-scope boundaries before design work starts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Feature briefs, PRDs, legal constraints, or customer research may contain confidential information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only workspace-approved agent sessions for sensitive planning material and remove confidential details when approval is not established. <br>
Risk: Incorrect or incomplete constraints can lead to misleading design guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated brief with product, design, and engineering stakeholders before using it as a design handoff. <br>
