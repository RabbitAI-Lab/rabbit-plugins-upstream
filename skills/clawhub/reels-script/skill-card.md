## Description: <br>
Use to write an Instagram Reels script: a sound-off-first short-video production doc optimized for a 3-second hook, watch-through, sends, Instagram search keywords, cover framing, and optional Veo prompts for faceless or b-roll shots. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, social media teams, and marketing operators use this skill to turn a Reel concept into a shootable Instagram-native script with muted-first on-screen text, spoken lines, audio direction, timing, a cover-frame brief, and handoffs for captioning, publishing, and scheduling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Scripts may rely on brand profile, voice, or compliance documents supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated claims, guardrails, disclosures, and brand voice before filming or publishing. <br>
Risk: The skill may route later captioning, publishing, scheduling, or AI-video work to separate skills or tools. <br>
Mitigation: Review those downstream outputs and permissions separately before using them. <br>
Risk: Trending Instagram audio and AI-generated video disclosures require manual checks outside this skill. <br>
Mitigation: Add licensed audio natively in Instagram when appropriate and follow platform, brand, and regional synthetic-media disclosure rules. <br>
