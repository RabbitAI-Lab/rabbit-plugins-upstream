    Discover

    Make

FAQ

Before we begin.

Ask through the brief form if your question isn’t covered.

How quickly can we start?

Usually two to three weeks after a signed proposal. Launch kits may start sooner.

What does a project cost?

Launch kits: $2,400. Identity from $4,800, websites from $6,500, product design from $8,000. Scope and deposit are agreed in writing.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. A Launch Kit provides a focused starting point for early-stage teams.

Will I own the final files?

Editable source files, exports and a usage guide are included on final payment.

Do you build websites or only design them?

I build focused marketing sites and collaborate with developers for more complex products.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds per stage. Any additional work is agreed before billing.

Can you work in our time zone?

Based in Harbourgate, with recorded reviews and scheduled overlap for remote teams.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief, audience, budget range and target date. Existing materials are helpful.

A few practical details.

Where should a new founder start?

With brand identity if you have nothing yet, or the website if you already have a mark you like. A quick call settles which comes first.

How long does a website take?

Six to eight weeks from kickoff to launch for a marketing site, with your content arriving by week three. Product UI is scoped per sprint.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. A monthly design hour for small updates, or a retainer for teams shipping every week. Both run month to month.
