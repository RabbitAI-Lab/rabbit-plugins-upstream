## Description:

Helps ecommerce teams create product images and promotional visuals from text prompts or reference images using the Seedream 5.0 Pro image workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce designers, product photographers, brand teams, and sellers use this skill to plan and generate product images, store graphics, and promotional artwork from prompts or reference photos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The service requires an API key and may receive uploaded product or reference images.

Mitigation: Use an approved account and avoid submitting sensitive credentials or imagery unless the service is trusted for that data.

Risk: Installing qhkit, Node/npm, or image-compression tools introduces dependency risk.

Mitigation: Prefer pinned versions or preinstalled trusted tooling in managed environments.

Risk: Generating images consumes credits and submits a paid task.

Mitigation: Check the estimate and confirm model, image count, settings, references, and cost with the user before submission.
