## Description:

Guides agents to resolve contradictory or stale wiki claims by checking source authority and dates, preserving unresolved claims, and escalating consequential disputes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dexio](https://clawhub.ai/user/dexio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Wiki maintainers and agents use this skill to check conflicting or outdated claims against dated sources, document corrections and unresolved disagreements, and refer consequential conflicts to a person.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An incorrect resolution could mislead decisions or propagate across wiki pages.

Mitigation: Check current primary sources, preserve unresolved claims, and have a person review edits involving money, commitments, external claims, or widely cited pages.
