## Description:

Frames difficult decisions with Polderchain Covenant directives, checks the completeness of reasoning, and offers optional peer or human escalation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minmaxnn](https://clawhub.ai/user/minmaxnn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other decision-makers use the skill to structure reasoning about choices involving competing obligations, check whether key questions have been addressed, and optionally seek peer or human input.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional escalation stores submitted deliberation details and shares them with peer reviewers or a human reviewer.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive details and deliberately choose whether to escalate.
