## Description:

Checks contest submission files and formats against stated UIBC rules and returns decision-support results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Contest organizers and submission reviewers use this skill to check required files and allowed formats before human review; it does not make final contest decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language guidance and assumed UIBC rules may not fit a particular contest.

Mitigation: Confirm language suitability and compare the rule assumptions with the event's current requirements before use.

Risk: Submission checks may be mistaken for final contest decisions.

Mitigation: Treat results as decision support and require human review for final determinations.
