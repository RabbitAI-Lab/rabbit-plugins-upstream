## Description: <br>
Book Smog helps agents find smog-check providers, check appointment availability, and create a booking through Lokuli after user confirmation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[edwardrodriguez703-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/edwardrodriguez703-design) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Drivers and agents use this skill to search for nearby smog-check services, compare options, check open time slots, and prepare a booking after explicit user approval. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking requests may share personal contact details and appointment information with Lokuli and a selected smog provider. <br>
Mitigation: Review the provider, service, price, time, and contact details with the user before confirming any appointment. <br>
