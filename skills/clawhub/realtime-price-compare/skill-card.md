## Description:

Compares current product prices, discounts, and purchase links across Taobao, JD.com, and Pinduoduo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nathan-liu-sd](https://clawhub.ai/user/nathan-liu-sd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External shoppers use the skill to find current prices, compare effective offers across three Chinese commerce platforms, and review purchase links and buying conditions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping requests, including the product query, budget, preferences, and original wording, are sent to the publisher's recommendation service.

Mitigation: Share only shopping details needed for the search and avoid including sensitive personal information.

Risk: Prices and purchase links may change or lead to a different offer by the time the user buys.

Mitigation: Verify the current price, offer conditions, and link destination on the commerce platform before purchasing.
