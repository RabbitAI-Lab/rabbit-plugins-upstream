## Description: <br>
Ad Creative helps agents generate, iterate, and scale paid-ad creative, including headlines, primary text, full ad variations, static concepts, review pages, and video-ad scripts grounded in product, audience, platform, and performance context. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketers, growth teams, agencies, and operators use this skill to create platform-ready ad copy and creative concepts, iterate from performance data, plan creative roadmaps, and prepare stakeholder review artifacts before launching campaigns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: External research, scraping, or social listening can collect or reuse material without adequate authorization. <br>
Mitigation: Use only sources the operator is authorized to access, follow platform terms, and treat organic mining as vocabulary and format research rather than copying a creator's specific creative. <br>
Risk: Voice cloning and synthetic media can create consent, rights, and disclosure issues in ads. <br>
Mitigation: Clone voices only with written consent and commercial rights, prefer licensed or actor-compensated voices when consent is unclear, and disclose AI-generated media where required. <br>
Risk: Generated ads can include unsupported claims or be published before policy, brand, legal, or client review. <br>
Mitigation: Require human approval before publishing or uploading ads, substantiate claims against source material, and check platform, brand, and regulated-industry requirements before launch. <br>
