## Description: <br>
Plan and run a customer advisory board (CAB), including objectives, member selection criteria, a meeting agenda, discussion guides, roles, logistics, invitations, follow-ups, and value-capture planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Customer-facing, product, marketing, and executive teams use this skill to design a customer advisory board program, select members, plan meetings, guide discussion, and capture follow-up value. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: CAB planning may involve confidential customer names, competitive strategy, roadmap details, or sensitive relationship context. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential customer names, competitive strategy, or sensitive roadmap details unless they are appropriate for the current agent session. <br>
Risk: Generated CAB plans, agendas, or follow-up templates may include incomplete or misleading business guidance if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated plan before use and confirm that member selection, confidentiality constraints, competitor overlap, and follow-up commitments match the organization's requirements. <br>
