## Description: <br>
Audit a Figma component library for consistency, coverage gaps, and naming issues, producing a structured report with prioritized issues, naming recommendations, and a fix plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Designers, design-system maintainers, and developers use this skill to assess Figma component libraries for naming consistency, coverage gaps, variant completeness, and actionable remediation priorities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided component lists or design-system descriptions may include private product or design information. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive component data before sharing it with an agent, especially because the skill relies on user-provided inputs. <br>
Risk: Audit recommendations may be incomplete or misprioritized when the supplied component inventory is partial or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Provide current component lists, product type, design-system maturity, and the primary concern, then review recommendations before applying changes. <br>
