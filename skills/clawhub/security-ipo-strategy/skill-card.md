## Use Case:

External investors, IPO subscribers, and financial analysts use this skill to evaluate China A-share IPO opportunities, compare subscription strategies, estimate expected returns, screen break-price risk, and plan post-listing or lock-up-period actions. Outputs are non-personalized research support and should be checked against current official rules and the user's own risk tolerance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat IPO scores, subscribe-or-avoid labels, allocation percentages, or sell-discipline examples as personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as non-personalized research support, require users to apply their own risk tolerance and account constraints, and avoid claiming guaranteed returns.

Risk: IPO rules, exchange requirements, regulator expectations, and market conditions can change after the skill content date.

Mitigation: Verify current exchange and regulator rules, official disclosure documents, and live market data before acting on any IPO analysis.

Risk: Expected return examples can be misleading if they ignore allotment probability, break-price risk, or lock-up selling pressure.

Mitigation: Report expected value with probability assumptions, highlight break-price signals, and include lock-up or sell-discipline caveats when relevant.
