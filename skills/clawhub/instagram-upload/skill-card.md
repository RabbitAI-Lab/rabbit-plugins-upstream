## Description:

Fills Instagram image posts, carousels, or Reels in a logged-in Instagram session, with the option to leave the post for creator review before publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gybin02](https://clawhub.ai/user/gybin02)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media operators use this skill to prepare Instagram image posts, carousels, and Reels from media files and captions. The default fill mode leaves the post for review before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post could be prepared or published with the wrong Instagram account, media, or caption.

Mitigation: Use a trusted EasyPost installation; confirm the active account, selected media, and caption, then review the browser preview before publishing.
