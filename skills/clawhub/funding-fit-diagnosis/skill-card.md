## Description:

Assesses Chinese startups' fit for venture capital and government funding, then recommends institutions, preparation steps, and fundraising paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and business owners in China use this skill to assess fundraising readiness, compare venture-capital and government-fund fit, shortlist potential funders, and plan next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated fund criteria or policies could lead to poor fundraising decisions.

Mitigation: Check current fund terms and local policies with official sources before acting.

Risk: Fit scores or institution matches may be mistaken for guaranteed funding or professional advice.

Mitigation: Use them for planning only; consult qualified financial or legal professionals for consequential decisions.

Risk: Project intake may include confidential financial figures, location details, or unreleased plans.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the assessment and avoid unnecessary sensitive specifics.
