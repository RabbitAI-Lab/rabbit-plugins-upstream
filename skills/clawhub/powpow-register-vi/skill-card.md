## Description:

Guides Vietnamese-speaking users through PowPow account registration, common signup issues, and subscription activation without handling credentials or payments for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Vietnamese-speaking PowPow users use this skill to understand the product, follow account registration steps, troubleshoot common registration failures, and complete subscription activation through the official site or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment evidence in chat while following registration or activation guidance.

Mitigation: Keep registration and payment inside the official PowPow site or app, and do not request, collect, or process passwords, verification codes, or payment proof.

Risk: The skill directs users to external PowPow, Bilibili, GitHub raw, and jsDelivr links that may be unavailable or change outside the package.

Mitigation: Prefer the official PowPow site or app for account and payment actions, and use the documented screenshot links only as visual references.
