## Description:

Provides Vietnamese guidance for registering a PowPow account, resolving sign-up issues, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Vietnamese-speaking prospective PowPow users receive step-by-step registration guidance, troubleshooting help, and directions to the official subscription page. Users complete account creation and payment themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration and payment conversations may expose sensitive account or payment details.

Mitigation: Direct users to official PowPow pages; never request passwords, verification codes, payment receipts, or private account details.

Risk: Registration and subscription instructions may become outdated as the platform changes.

Mitigation: Check current instructions and options on the official PowPow pages rather than asserting outdated requirements or prices.
