## Description:

Searches Meta's public careers postings through Crawlora for filtered openings, full catalog listings, and individual job details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and researchers use this skill to find Meta openings, inspect individual postings, and monitor changes to the public job catalog.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Job searches send the API key and search parameters to Crawlora as an intermediary.

Mitigation: Use a Crawlora key from the environment and avoid sensitive or private information in search values.
