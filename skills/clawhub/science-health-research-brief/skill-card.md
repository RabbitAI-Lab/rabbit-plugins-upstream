## Description:

Use when the user needs a cited research brief drawing on scientific papers, clinical trial records, drug and compound databases, or WHO/CDC public-health data. Calls the Powerdrill Science & Health Research Agent through Finch.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can turn plain-language science and health questions into cited research briefs drawing on scientific literature, clinical-trial records, drug and compound databases, and WHO/CDC public-health data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research questions are sent to Finch and Powerdrill and may contain identifying medical details or confidential research.

Mitigation: Remove identifying medical details and confidential research, and review provider privacy terms before submitting questions.

Risk: Research results may be mistaken for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment guidance; a paid run may incur a charge.

Mitigation: Treat results as informational only, not medical advice, and explicitly approve any paid run.
