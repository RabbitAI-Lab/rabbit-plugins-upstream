Homeowners and residential design professionals can use this skill to explore practical interior layout, storage, style, furnishing, outlet-planning, and designer-selection ideas, and to prepare questions for project discussions. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers could mistake design ideas involving gas facilities, structural changes, or opening up a balcony for approval or instructions to carry out the work.

Mitigation: Use the guidance for planning and discussion only. Have qualified professionals confirm conditions, obtain the required utility, property, and structural approvals, and perform any such work.
