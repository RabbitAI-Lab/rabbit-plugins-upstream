## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some reference material may advise bypassing gas inspections or casually describe load-bearing wall removal.

Mitigation: Review or remove that content before release, and require local-code compliance plus approval from licensed professionals, utilities, property management, and permitting authorities for regulated work.

Risk: Users may apply renovation guidance to gas, structural, electrical, waterproofing, or demolition work without site-specific professional review.

Mitigation: Frame such guidance as planning support only and direct users to licensed professionals and applicable permits before acting.
