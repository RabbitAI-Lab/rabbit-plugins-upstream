## Description:

Provides Chinese-language home renovation design ideas on layouts, storage, style, space planning, and common pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renovation planners use this skill to explore room layouts, storage options, design trade-offs, and questions to discuss with qualified professionals before construction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Design examples may normalize demolition or structural changes that are unsafe for a particular property.

Mitigation: Treat examples as inspiration; obtain local approvals and review by qualified structural professionals before any changes to walls, balconies, or load-bearing elements.

Risk: Kitchen relocation, gas, electrical, waterproofing, drainage, and exhaust suggestions can create safety or compliance hazards if followed as construction instructions.

Mitigation: Do not perform these changes from skill guidance; have qualified professionals assess the property, follow local requirements, and secure required approvals.
