## Description:

从短剧剧本拆出人物/造型、地点/视图、道具/状态和跨场连续性，写成创作者可读的视觉设定。用户说“拆角色/场景/道具”“做资产设定”“判断复用还是新变体”“更新造型/道具状态”，或拿现成剧本直接做视觉资产准备时使用；不写图片提示词，不生成媒体。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

MIT

## Use Case:

短剧编剧、创作者和制作人员可用它从现成剧本整理可复用的人物、造型、地点和道具设定，追踪变体与跨场连续性，并写入视觉设定文档。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow requires Chinese category headings and asset names, which may not fit every project's documentation conventions.

Mitigation: Confirm the project can use the required Chinese headings and visual-settings document format before adopting the workflow.
