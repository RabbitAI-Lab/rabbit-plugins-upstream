## Description:

Turns short-drama scripts into creator-readable visual asset settings for characters, looks, locations, props, states, and continuity without generating media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and production teams use this skill to turn an accepted short-drama script into a reusable visual-setting document, tracking asset identities, variants, and continuity across scenes and episodes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect asset identities or continuity decisions can propagate into downstream production.

Mitigation: Review the updated visual-setting document and unresolved references before using it for prompts, storyboards, voice work, or production.

Risk: Generated asset notes could invent brands or expose sensitive on-screen information.

Mitigation: Apply project policies to text and logos, and confirm sensitive on-screen details with the creator before reuse.
