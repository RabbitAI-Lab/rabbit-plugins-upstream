## Description:

Finds movie and TV scene clips, helps select one clean take, and guides rights-aware use for remakes, parodies, and edits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video editors use this skill to find a film or TV scene, prepare a clean reference take, and record its source and rights information for an edit or AI video workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The available security analysis was partial; its benign verdict does not establish that every component is safe, and it notes install supply-chain risk.

Mitigation: Before installing, review the installer and repository at a fixed version, as advised by the security evidence.

Risk: Movie and TV clips may be copyrighted or subject to platform and model restrictions.

Mitigation: Prefer owned footage, licensed stock, or verified public-domain sources; check rights before use and record the source and license.
