The editor tapped the page with a blue pencil. "One space after a sentence," she said. "Not two."

Mira nodded. "And the ship name goes in italics, the *Ragged Right*, not with an underline."

"A dash--like that--is two hyphens with no spaces," the editor said. "When the speaker changes, start a new paragraph."

"Even for a short reply?"

"Especially then. It keeps the voices from sliding together once the speech tags drop away."

#

Mira took the copyright line off the last page and let the story stop. A leading apostrophe still had to face the right way, as in 'em and 'tis, or the production editor would catch it on the first pass.
