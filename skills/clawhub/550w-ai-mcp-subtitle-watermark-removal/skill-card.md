## Description:

Guides users through 550W AI's remote service to remove hardcoded video subtitles, retrieve supported public video links without watermarks, or erase image watermarks and text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sunshinehu](https://clawhub.ai/user/sunshinehu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with rights to their media use this skill to guide subtitle removal, supported public video-link retrieval, and image watermark or text removal through 550W AI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media or links submitted for processing are sent to 550W AI.

Mitigation: Tell users before submission and review the connection and consent pages.

Risk: Processing can consume account credits.

Mitigation: Check available credits and applicable costs before paid submission; reuse task identifiers on retries to avoid duplicate billing.

Risk: Removing watermarks or subtitles from unauthorized media may violate others' rights.

Mitigation: Process only media the user owns or is authorized to modify.
