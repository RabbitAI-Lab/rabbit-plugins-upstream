## Description:

Plan every visual detail of a Cliptude video and have Cliptude voice, source, and render the approved plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mwitiderrick](https://clawhub.ai/user/mwitiderrick)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating Faceless Documentary, Faceless Top 10, Avatar Video, or Vector Explainer videos can use the skill to specify and review the video's visual plan before production. The plan covers the look, narration timing, shot sources, graphics, transitions, and sound effects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded media may be stored and processed by Cliptude.

Mitigation: Upload only media you intend Cliptude to store and process.

Risk: Video production and re-rendering can consume credits.

Mitigation: Check credit costs before starting a job.

Risk: Signed-in browser access can expose account capabilities.

Mitigation: Review the requested access scope before approving browser access.

Risk: Provider credentials grant access to media or generation services.

Mitigation: Supply only the provider keys needed for the task.
