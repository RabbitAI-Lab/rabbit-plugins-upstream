## Use Case:

External users can create a public-source pre-due-diligence brief for company screening, credit assessment, customer onboarding, supply-chain finance, or peer comparison. Treat the brief as a starting point for review rather than a substitute for formal due diligence.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to share an API key in chat, which could expose the credential.

Mitigation: Configure the key through protected local settings instead of chat, and rotate any key already shared.

Risk: The documented workflow discourages cancellation and includes promotional prompts, which can reduce user control.

Mitigation: Keep the ability to stop a task and decline promotions.

Risk: Research queries are sent to Cue and consume credits; confidential company information may be exposed or incur charges.

Mitigation: Use public, non-confidential query details and check available credits before running research.
