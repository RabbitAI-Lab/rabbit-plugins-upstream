## Description:

Writes or revises a YouTube video script (long-form or Shorts) with a hook, structured sections, on-screen cues and a call to action, saved as script.md in the project folder. Use when the user asks for a YouTube script, outline, voiceover text or rewrite of a video draft.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ahmadhussain0335](https://clawhub.ai/user/ahmadhussain0335)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and video teams use this skill to draft or revise long-form YouTube videos and Shorts from user-provided material or research.md, with structured narration, visual cues, source markers, and a call to action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsupported factual claims in a draft could mislead viewers.

Mitigation: Use only user-provided sources or sources in research.md, mark sourced claims, and flag claims without a source.

Risk: Research or publishing workflows may rely on companion skills whose code and authentication behavior are outside this release.

Mitigation: Review companion skills separately before enabling research or uploads; script drafting itself needs no Google credentials.
