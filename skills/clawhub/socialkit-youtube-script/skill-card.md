## Description:

Writes or revises a YouTube video script (long-form or Shorts) with a hook, structured sections, on-screen cues and a call to action, saved as script.md in the project folder.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ahmadhussain0335](https://clawhub.ai/user/ahmadhussain0335)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and their agents use this skill to draft or revise long-form YouTube and Shorts scripts with sourced claims, narration, visual cues, and a call to action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the related YouTube setup guidance for a requirement of this script-writing skill.

Mitigation: Google credentials are unnecessary for this skill. Review the separately required socialkit-social-core skill before use, and assess any upload skill independently before granting channel access.
