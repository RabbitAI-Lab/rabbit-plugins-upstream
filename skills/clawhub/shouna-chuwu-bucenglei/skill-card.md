## Description:

Provides Chinese-language home storage planning guidance for renovation and organization, covering whole-home storage systems, room-specific cabinet planning, item placement, pitfalls, and non-brand-specific storage tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users planning renovation or home storage layouts use this skill to assess household storage needs, choose space-specific storage approaches, and avoid common storage design mistakes before buying products or commissioning cabinetry.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Home renovation, wall changes, plumbing, and electrical suggestions may be mistaken for professional construction guidance.

Mitigation: Treat the skill's output as planning guidance and consult qualified professionals before making physical changes.

Risk: Storage dimensions and layout suggestions may not fit a user's local building conditions, fixtures, or household needs.

Mitigation: Verify site conditions, measurements, and household item inventory before purchasing products or commissioning custom cabinetry.
