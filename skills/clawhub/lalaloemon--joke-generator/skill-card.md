## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to generate short, friendly Chinese jokes from a requested topic, audience, scene, or style. It can also provide one general joke when no specific prompt details are supplied.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally scoped toward Chinese-language jokes and may not suit multilingual humor requests.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese jokes, or choose a broader humor-generation skill when multilingual output is required.

Risk: Humor prompts can ask for offensive, demeaning, or age-inappropriate jokes.

Mitigation: Follow the documented safety boundaries by avoiding jokes that target protected traits, real victims, private individuals, or child-inappropriate themes, and redirect to harmless wordplay or fictional situations.
