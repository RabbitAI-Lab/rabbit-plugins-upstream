## Use Case:

People evaluating products, costs, or technical plans use this skill to explore first-principles reasoning, question requirements, and weigh vertical integration through a simulated perspective. It is not Elon Musk or a source of his actual views.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill quietly reads and writes a local update-check file and queries git remotes, beyond what its advisory purpose requires.

Mitigation: Review before installing; allow local state and repository access only if acceptable, or remove the version-check section.

Risk: First-person roleplay can be mistaken for Elon Musk's actual statements or endorsement.

Mitigation: Identify the perspective as a simulation based on public sources, not the person or his verified views.
