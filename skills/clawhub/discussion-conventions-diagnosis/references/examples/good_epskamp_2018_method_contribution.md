# Example: Method-Type Contribution（方法型贡献）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Epskamp et al. (2018). Estimating psychological networks and their accuracy. *Psychological Methods*, 23(4).
- **论文编号**: 7.1
- **领域**: Methodology (Psych Methods)
- **维度**: Conventions（Method-type contribution — 改进方法/工具）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: conventions-diagnosis 的"method-type contribution 识别"

---

## 原句（Discussion implications 段）

> *"We suggest that researchers always compare networks based on polychoric correlations with networks based on Spearman correlations (they should look somewhat similar) to determine if the estimation of polychoric correlations is the source of this problem."*

> *"We **provide a tutorial** for estimating psychological networks and their accuracy, **introducing several methodological recommendations** for researchers. Specifically, we demonstrate how to assess the stability of edges using bootstrapping, how to evaluate the accuracy of edge weights using nonparametric bootstrapping, and how to interpret networks using centrality measures."*

## 分析

### 1. Method-type contribution 的标准结构
- **"provide a tutorial"** —— 提供方法指南
- **"introducing several methodological recommendations"** —— 推荐方法
- **"demonstrate how to"** —— 演示具体技术

### 2. "We suggest / We recommend / We demonstrate" 的层次
- "**suggest**" —— 弱认知（适合建议）
- "**recommend**" —— 中认知（适合指南）
- "**demonstrate**" —— 强认知（适合操作示范）

### 3. 操作化建议的具体性
- "**how to assess** the stability of edges **using bootstrapping**"
- "**how to evaluate** the accuracy **using nonparametric bootstrapping**"
- "**how to interpret** networks **using centrality measures**"

每个建议都包含：**verb + object + method** 三元组——这是 operational recommendation 的标准形式

### 4. Method-type contribution 的边界
- 不是"我们的方法能解决一切问题"
- 而是"**if you encounter problem X, do Y**"（conditional recommendation）
- 这是 Unit 4.4.2 推荐的"操作化 hedge"

## 用法

当用户上传**方法学、tutorial、tools & techniques 类论文**的 Discussion 时引用此例：

1. **用户写了 method 论文但 contribution 不清晰** — 引用此例展示 method-type 的标志
2. **用户的"操作建议"太抽象** — "researchers should be careful" → 引用此例展示具体的"verb + object + method"模式
3. **用户的方法学 claim 过于 confident** — "我们的方法总是对的" → 引用此例展示 conditional recommendation
