# Example: Results-Type Contribution（结果型贡献）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Costello et al. (2021). Clarifying the structure and nature of left-wing authoritarianism. *JPSP*, 120(1).
- **论文编号**: 1.1
- **领域**: Social (JPSP)
- **维度**: Conventions（Results-type contribution — 改进测量/获得新结果）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: conventions-diagnosis 的"contribution type 判定" + "happy words 与 claim 匹配"

---

## 原句（Discussion 多处结果描述）

> *"We **used** exploratory test construction (Loevinger, 1957; Tellegen & Waller, 2008; Westen & Rosenthal, 2003) to **develop and preliminarily validate** a multi-dimensional measure of LWA. Our results **suggest** that LWA both **exists** and **predicts** key psychological and behavioral outcomes, including participation in political violence."*

> *"The present investigation **represents one of the most comprehensive studies** ever undertaken into the murky distinction between self-concept and self-efficacy, **offering new insights** to this frequently-discussed issue."*（类比句型，用于对比）

## 分析

### 1. Results-type contribution 的精确措辞
- **"develop and preliminarily validate"** —— 明确说"开发并初步验证"
- **"a multi-dimensional measure of LWA"** —— 具体贡献物
- **"LWA both exists and predicts..."** —— 结果的具体化

### 2. Contribution claim 与 evidence 的连接
- Method（exploratory test construction）→ Outcome（"LWA both exists and predicts..."）
- 这是 Results-type contribution 的标准结构
- 顶刊论文必须显示**贡献的具体形态**——不空说"我们发现了"

### 3. Achievement vs Contribution 的清晰区分
- **Achievement**："develop and validate a multi-dimensional measure"（做了什么）
- **Contribution**："LWA both exists and predicts key outcomes"（对 field 的价值）
- 两者在同一段内清晰分工

### 4. 谦逊的 hedging
- "**preliminarily** validate" —— 不是 "definitively validate"
- "Our results **suggest**" —— 不是 "Our results prove"
- 这种**精确的 hedging** 是 Results-type contribution 的标志
