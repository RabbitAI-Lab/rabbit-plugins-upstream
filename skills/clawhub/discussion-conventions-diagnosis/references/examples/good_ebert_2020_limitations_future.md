- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## 原句（Discussion limitations + future work 段）

> *"Although our gravestone approach has key advantages over previous approaches, our approach also has its limitations. **First, gravestone analyses are necessarily cross-sectional** (people only die once). **Second**, our religiosity measure (gravestone imagery) **may not capture** all aspects of religiosity (e.g., private religious practice, intrinsic religiosity). **Third**, our samples were restricted to certain geographic regions. **Future studies** should investigate whether our findings extend to other cultural contexts and religious traditions. **Specifically, studies should examine** whether the longevity benefits of religiosity generalize to non-Western cultures, Eastern religions, and contemporary samples."*

## 分析

### 1. Limitations 的多维度呈现
- **First**: Method limitation（cross-sectional）
- **Second**: Construct limitation（measure 不全面）
- **Third**: Sample limitation（geographic restriction）

3 种不同类型的 limitation，避免单一维度

### 2. 每个 limitation 的具体化
- "people only die once" —— 用 common sense 解释为什么 cross-sectional
- "may not capture all aspects of religiosity" —— 用 example 具体化（"private religious practice, intrinsic religiosity"）
- 不是 generic "limitation"——是具体的

### 3. Future work 的 4 个精确元素
- **What**: "extend to other cultural contexts and religious traditions"
- **Where**: "non-Western cultures, Eastern religions"
- **When** (年代): "contemporary samples"
- **Specific verb**: "examine whether"（不是 "explore"）

### 4. Limitations → Future work 的连接
- "First... Second... Third..."（3 个 limitations）
- 紧跟 "Future studies should..."（对应 future directions）
- 这种**有序的连接**让 reviewer 觉得作者有全面规划

### 5. 与 Unit 4.5 的契合
- 教科书明确说："Future work should be specific, directional, not 'more research is needed'"
- 本例完美展示：
  - ❌ "More research is needed"
  - ✅ "Specifically, studies should examine whether the longevity benefits... generalize to non-Western cultures, Eastern religions, and contemporary samples"
