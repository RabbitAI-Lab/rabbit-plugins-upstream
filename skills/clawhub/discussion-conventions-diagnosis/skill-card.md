## Description:

Diagnose academic-writing conventions in the Discussion of an English psychology research paper, including contribution statements, limitations, future work, citation density and function, and appropriate hedging.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and writing reviewers use this skill to diagnose whether a psychology research paper's Discussion section follows expected academic-writing conventions. It provides structured feedback on contribution claims, limitations, future-work specificity, citation use, and hedging without generating or rewriting prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "check conventions" may invoke the skill outside its intended niche.

Mitigation: Use prompts that specify psychology research papers, Discussion-section conventions, or the intended diagnostic area.
