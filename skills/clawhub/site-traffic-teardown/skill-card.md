## Description:

Analyzes a website's competitors using traffic and SEO data, then summarizes their growth patterns and practical opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuyansen](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuyansen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Growth and SEO teams use this skill to compare competitor traffic and search performance, understand how rivals attract visitors, and prioritize three opportunities. It produces a side-by-side report for selected domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analytics queries use the user's AIsa account and send selected domains to the provider.

Mitigation: Review the domains before requesting data and use a trusted AIsa CLI installation.

Risk: Price ceilings limit estimates and do not guarantee final charges; requests and results remain in the chosen output folder.

Mitigation: Review and explicitly approve each stage's quote, then check and report the actual charge and protect the output folder.
