## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms are sent to SocialDataX and may include confidential or unnecessary personal information.

Mitigation: Use focused queries that exclude confidential and unnecessary personal information.

Risk: The available security scan is incomplete because some local references were unresolved.

Mitigation: Review the package source and requested search scope before installation or use.
