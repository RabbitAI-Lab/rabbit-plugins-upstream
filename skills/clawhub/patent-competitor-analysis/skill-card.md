## Description:

Analyzes a company’s patent landscape and competitors in a specified technology area, producing a decision-ready report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business, R&D, and IP teams use the skill to compare a target company’s patent activity with competitors in a defined technology area and prepare a management-ready landscape report. The report supports research and planning, but does not replace legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded materials may contain unpublished inventions or business plans without clear sharing approval.

Mitigation: Submit only materials you are authorized to share, and omit confidential documents unless approval is clear.

Risk: The trial-expiry flow may request account credentials through chat for persistent storage.

Mitigation: Do not share credentials in chat; set a limited API key through the documented PATENTMAX_API_KEY environment variable and protect its local credential cache.
