## Description: <br>
Guanshi Finance Expert provides financial strategy analysis for building models, evaluating investment returns, estimating valuations, allocating capital, quantifying risk, stress-testing cash flow, and assessing M&A finance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Strategy teams, finance analysts, and agent workflows use this skill for financial-model construction, ROI analysis, valuation, capital-allocation strategy, risk quantification, cash-flow stress testing, and M&A financial assessment. It is intended as financial analysis support and not as personal finance or professional financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Financial analysis outputs may be mistaken for professional financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support analysis only, review assumptions and market inputs, and obtain qualified financial review before acting on recommendations. <br>
Risk: Financial conclusions can be sensitive to outdated or incorrect market and business inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Require current, sourced inputs for rates, comparables, cash-flow assumptions, and scenario variables, and include sensitivity analysis in final outputs. <br>
