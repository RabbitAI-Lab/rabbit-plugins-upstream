## Description:

Connects an agent to PDF.co through Maton to convert, combine, edit, and extract information from PDF documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[byungkyu](https://clawhub.ai/user/byungkyu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users and developers can prepare PDFs, extract text and tables, and automate document workflows through a connected PDF.co account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted documents or URLs are processed by PDF.co, and Maton stores authorization for the connected account.

Mitigation: Confirm the account, document, and destination before connecting or submitting sensitive material.

Risk: Document writes, hosted functions, triggers, and forwarding destinations can change data or keep acting after setup.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval for each write or automation, including its target, scope, and duration.
