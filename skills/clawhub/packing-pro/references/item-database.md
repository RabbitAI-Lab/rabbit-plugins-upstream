## Weight Notes

- All weights are approximate and represent a single unit.
- For flights, remember liquid restrictions: containers ≤ 100 ml, all fitting in a 1 L clear bag.
- Heavy items (shoes, jacket, books) are best worn or carried on travel day.
- The script calculates total estimated weight; always leave 10-15% margin for items not listed.
