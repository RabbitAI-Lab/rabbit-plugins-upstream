## Description: <br>
Provides a nine-dimension China A-share stock analysis workflow covering policy, fundamentals, technical indicators, fund flows, ESG signals, risk, valuation, and investment-report generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yanyangyunhai](https://clawhub.ai/user/yanyangyunhai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External investors, analysts, and finance researchers can use this skill to draft informational China A-share stock analysis reports, compare risk and valuation signals, and organize buy/sell considerations that require independent verification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may produce high-stakes buy/sell signals, target prices, stop-loss levels, and investment suggestions that can be inaccurate or unsuitable. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as informational research drafts only; independently verify market data, assumptions, and recommendations before any investment decision. <br>
Risk: The skill requires web access for market data and may rely on stale or incomplete inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Check timestamps and source quality for all market data used in the analysis. <br>
