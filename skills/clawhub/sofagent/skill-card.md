## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation may make persistent changes to local agent configurations and hooks.

Mitigation: Review the installer and intended configuration writes before installation.

Risk: Background operation and broad tool permissions may enable high-impact changes or data movement.

Mitigation: Restrict unneeded training, cloud, PR, device, and data-push tools; require human approval for restores, merges, registrations, and daemon or webhook behavior.
