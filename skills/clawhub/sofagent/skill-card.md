## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation adds persistent local agent infrastructure and may alter agent tool configuration.

Mitigation: Confirm the source and version, review files and tool configurations before installation, and decide whether background processes and device registration are appropriate.

Risk: The platform exposes broad operational capabilities, including consequential deployment and recovery actions.

Mitigation: Limit enabled roles to the minimum needed and require human approval for PR merges, snapshot restores, model switches, training, connector registration, and destructive rollback.
