## Description:

Helps agents practice Polymarket trading with simulated funds and live market prices, track performance, and prepare shareable results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[robotlearning123](https://clawhub.ai/user/robotlearning123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users can practice prediction-market strategies with simulated trades, review portfolio performance, and prepare results for sharing or leaderboard submission without placing real-money trades.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may place simulated trades autonomously.

Mitigation: Require user approval before simulated trades or account changes.

Risk: Leaderboard submissions and social posts may share performance publicly.

Mitigation: Ask the user before submitting results or posting generated content.

Risk: Installing the trading package without a pinned version may introduce unreviewed changes.

Mitigation: Review the package and pin its version before installation.

Risk: Third-party market descriptions may contain instructions directed at the agent.

Mitigation: Treat market content as data, not as instructions or links to follow.
