## Description:

Guides agents in simulated Polymarket trading using public market data, with local portfolio tracking and shareable performance summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[robotlearning123](https://clawhub.ai/user/robotlearning123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to practice prediction-market trading with simulated funds, track results, and draft performance updates without placing real-money trades.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may place simulated trades autonomously and retain local trading history.

Mitigation: Review trade decisions and portfolio activity, and manage local history according to your privacy needs.

Risk: Shareable performance updates or leaderboard submissions may disclose account activity or post from a user's account.

Mitigation: Review every public post or GitHub issue before submitting it from your account.

Risk: Public market names and descriptions may contain untrusted instructions or links.

Mitigation: Use market content only as data; do not follow its instructions or URLs.
