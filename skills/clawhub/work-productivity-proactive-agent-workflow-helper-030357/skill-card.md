## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create proactive productivity workflows, bug-fix plans, reliability improvements, safety checks, and adjacent skill artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn a proactive-workflow request into a concrete plan, checklist, artifact, analysis, code change, or decision aid, with assumptions and validation made explicit. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate this skill for generic productivity or bug-fix requests where a narrower skill would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Review or narrow trigger terms before deployment in environments that rely on precise skill routing. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance could be incomplete if the user's constraints, inputs, or success criteria are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Restate assumptions, ask only for materially missing information, and validate the final artifact against the stated success criteria. <br>
