## Description:

Schedule and manage social media posts with Crosspublish on X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and Google Business. Use to draft or queue posts, check the calendar, edit or unschedule posts, see analytics, or answer comments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media managers and teams use Crosspublish through an agent to draft, schedule, update, and review posts across connected social accounts, inspect analytics, and respond to comments or messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected account permissions can allow the agent to manage posts and access inbox content.

Mitigation: Review the requested account connections and permissions before approving access.

Risk: Publishing and replies are public and cannot be recalled; deleting content is permanent.

Mitigation: Show the exact content, accounts, date, time, and timezone and wait for confirmation before changes; require explicit approval before public publishing, replies, or deletion.
