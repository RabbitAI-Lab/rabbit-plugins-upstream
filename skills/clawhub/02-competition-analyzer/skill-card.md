## Description:

竞品深度分析与差异化定位：扫描直接/间接竞品、拆解功能与定价、输出差异化策略。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product teams, founders, and market researchers use this skill to structure competitor research, compare positioning, pricing, user segments, product capabilities, and identify evidence-backed differentiation opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market metrics, pricing, and competitor claims may be estimated or change over time.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, verify current pricing and scale signals, and label estimates clearly before using the report for business decisions.

Risk: Competitive positioning can become biased if only weaknesses are emphasized.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's requirement to document both strengths and weaknesses for each competitor.
