## Description:

Routes Chinese-first requests to five AI expert personas covering social collaboration, hardware integration, status visualization, knowledge management, and human co-working.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can route ambiguous collaboration requests to the appropriate AI persona and receive guidance on social interactions, hardware integration, status displays, knowledge organization, or ongoing human-AI teamwork.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms could select an unintended persona.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent and the persona's scope before following its guidance.

Risk: Advisory answers on hardware or legal, medical, financial, and other safety-sensitive matters may be mistaken for professional judgment.

Mitigation: Identify the personas as AI and leave safety-critical decisions to qualified humans.
