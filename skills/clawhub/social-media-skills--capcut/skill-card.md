## Description: <br>
CapCut helps an agent plan short-form social video edits in CapCut, including retention pacing, captions, sound licensing checks, export specs, and human approval gates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, social media teams, and agency operators use this skill to plan short-form CapCut edits from a script, set caption and sound workflows, check asset and sync risks, and prepare exports for human approval and publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global where CapCut is available; artifact evidence notes that CapCut is unavailable in India. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the skill as an editor connector or assume it can inspect, edit, or approve video directly. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as plans and specifications only; require a human to execute edits in CapCut and approve the final cut. <br>
Risk: Client, NDA, or confidential material may be exposed if uploaded or synced to CapCut cloud services. <br>
Mitigation: Keep client and confidential projects local, disable cloud sync for those assets, and seek counsel or use a licensing-safe editor for high-stakes work. <br>
Risk: Music, templates, effects, or AI likeness features may not be cleared for the intended commercial or platform use. <br>
Mitigation: Verify licenses per asset and destination platform; avoid pirated packs, unlicensed music, and non-consensual lip-sync or deepfake workflows. <br>
Risk: CapCut plans, feature gates, pricing, and export availability change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Check the active in-app tier, feature, asset, and export requirements before building an edit around them. <br>
