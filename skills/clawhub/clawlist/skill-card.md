## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent operators use clawlist to structure multi-step projects, coordinate planning, execution, parallel dispatch, and verification phases, and track long-running or recurring work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill gives broad workflow control and can steer many agent tasks into recurring or long-running processes. <br>
Mitigation: Define scope, cadence, allowed actions, stop conditions, and approval requirements before enabling long-running or infinite tasks. <br>
Risk: Ongoing task files may cause agents to continue work beyond the user's immediate request. <br>
Mitigation: Periodically review saved task and plan files, and remove or pause entries that are no longer authorized. <br>
