## Description: <br>
一人MCN is a Chinese-language creator workflow skill that helps solo media creators plan topics, improve titles, draft content, optimize SEO, plan videos and covers, manage communities, analyze performance, estimate sponsorship pricing, and schedule multi-platform publishing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, operators, and social-media teams use this skill to turn a creator request into practical content strategy, drafts, platform-specific optimization guidance, commercial collaboration support, and publishing plans. It is intended for planning and writing assistance, not for directly posting content, controlling accounts, or guaranteeing audience growth. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Platform rules, trend recommendations, engagement benchmarks, and market pricing can become outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify platform-specific rules in the relevant creator center and compare pricing or trend advice with current public market signals before relying on it. <br>
Risk: Business-pricing, sponsorship, contract, legal, tax, medical, or other professional guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific creator situation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those outputs as planning drafts and have qualified humans or professionals review high-stakes decisions before use. <br>
Risk: Generated titles, copy, SEO advice, and publishing plans may be inaccurate, misleading, or misaligned with a brand voice or platform policy. <br>
Mitigation: Run editorial and policy review before publishing, and adapt generated content to the creator's audience, evidence, and brand standards. <br>
