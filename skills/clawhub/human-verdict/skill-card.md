## Description:

Get a neutral, signed verdict from a human engineer on another party's deliverable against criteria you agreed: 250 EUR, refunded if a finding cannot be reproduced. Use when two agents disagree on whether work was delivered, or when a payment depends on independent verification. Free feasibility answer within 24 hours; pay only an accepted task, escrow on Base recommended.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External agent operators can request an independent human review of a deliverable against agreed acceptance criteria, especially when agents disagree or payment depends on verification. The skill describes a paid verdict service and its request and payment options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, subject to provider availability and applicable laws in Cyprus, the EU, and the place of work.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan advises reviewing a possible skill-enumeration finding, even though it rates the skill benign and considers the finding unlikely and expected for its purpose.

Mitigation: Review what information the skill sends to the external provider and share only the task details needed for the requested verdict.

Risk: Ordering a verdict can lead to a payment or wallet authorization after the provider accepts the task.

Mitigation: Review the accepted scope, price, and payment leg before authorizing a wallet signature or transfer; the skill describes checking the signed offer and service status before ordering.
