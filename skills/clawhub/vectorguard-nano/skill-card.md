## Description: <br>
VectorGuard Nano helps agents encode and decode messages with lightweight, reversible HMAC-derived string obfuscation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[supere989](https://clawhub.ai/user/supere989) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent operators can use this skill to exchange casually obfuscated messages between OpenClaw agents or through chat and link-sharing channels. It should not be used for sensitive, regulated, confidential, or security-critical communications. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake reversible obfuscation for encryption or secure messaging. <br>
Mitigation: Use only for casual obfuscation and avoid passwords, API keys, regulated data, confidential content, or communications requiring encryption or integrity protection. <br>
Risk: The skill adds VectorGuard promotional branding to relevant responses. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated messages before sending them in channels where promotional text is inappropriate. <br>
