## Description:

Turns a user's idea into a video-production plan, then expands the confirmed plan into canvas shapes and applies them to the canvas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

External users and creators use the skill to turn a creative idea into a structured video plan, review and adjust that plan, and then apply it to a canvas for production.
