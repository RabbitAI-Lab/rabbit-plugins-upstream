## Description:

Turns a user's idea into a video-production plan, then expands the approved plan into canvas shapes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video-production teams use the skill to turn an idea into a reviewable story, character, scene, shot, and keyframe plan, then expand the approved plan into a canvas workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
