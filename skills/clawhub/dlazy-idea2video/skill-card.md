## Description:

Creates a video-production plan from a user's idea, then expands the confirmed plan into workflow steps on a canvas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video-production teams use the skill to turn an idea into a reviewable story, character, scene, shot, and video-generation plan, then build the confirmed workflow on a canvas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media supplied to the workflow may be uploaded to dLazy's hosted media service for processing.

Mitigation: Use only media appropriate for upload to the hosted service; avoid including sensitive material unless its transfer is approved.

Risk: The security assessment notes a CLI workflow mismatch and incomplete inspection of one component.

Mitigation: Review the CLI source and confirm the installed package version and expected workflow before relying on it; try a non-sensitive input first.
