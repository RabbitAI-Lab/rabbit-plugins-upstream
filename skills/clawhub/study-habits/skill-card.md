## Description: <br>
Build effective study habits with spaced repetition, active recall, and session tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students and independent learners use this skill to track study sessions, choose evidence-based techniques, schedule spaced review, monitor progress, and prepare for exams. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Study topics, confidence ratings, session history, and exam schedules may reveal personal academic plans if entered in detail. <br>
Mitigation: Use ordinary study-planning details only and avoid entering sensitive information that should not be retained locally. <br>
