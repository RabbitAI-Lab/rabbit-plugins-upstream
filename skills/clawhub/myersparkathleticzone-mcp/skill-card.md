## Description:

Provides public Myers Park High School athletics schedules, team and roster information, coaches, news, and game broadcast links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, families, and other athletics followers can ask an agent about Myers Park teams, game schedules, coaches, scores, school sports news, and broadcast links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Schedules and scores may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive game details with the school and identify missing scores as unknown rather than zero.

Risk: Past-season team listings may have partial coverage.

Mitigation: Explain coverage limits rather than treating an empty listing as proof that no team existed.
