## Description:

Guides agents in finding public Myers Park High School athletics schedules, teams, rosters, coaches, news, and broadcast links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People asking about Myers Park Mustangs athletics use this skill to find upcoming games, team schedules, coaches, available results, and school sports news through public information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published schedules and scores may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Check current listings and game status before relying on a fixture; state when results are unavailable.

Risk: Missing or partially entered scores could be mistaken for zero or a final result.

Mitigation: Treat missing scores as unknown and do not infer a win or loss without both scores.

Risk: Incomplete historical team coverage could be mistaken for no past team activity.

Mitigation: Disclose coverage limits and do not infer that a team did not exist from an empty historical listing.
