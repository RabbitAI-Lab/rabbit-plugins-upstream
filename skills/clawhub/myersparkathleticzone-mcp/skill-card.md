## Description:

Schedules, teams, rosters, coaches, news and game broadcast links for Myers Park High School (Mustangs) athletics. Use when the user asks about Myers Park games, when a team plays, who a team's coach is, opponents, home/away fixtures, or school sports news.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Athletics schedules, cancellations, and scores can be incomplete or change after the public site is read.

Mitigation: Verify important game times, cancellations, and scores against the official Myers Park athletics source before relying on them.

Risk: Past-season and score data may be partial, and null scores are unknown rather than zero.

Mitigation: State coverage limits and avoid inferring wins, losses, or missing teams from incomplete data.
