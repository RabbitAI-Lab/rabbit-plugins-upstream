## Description:

Helps answer questions about Myers Park High School athletics schedules, teams, rosters, coaches, news, and game broadcast links using public information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, families, and other users can ask an agent about Myers Park games, teams, coaches, and school athletics news.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published schedules or scores may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Check the public athletics site for current details and distinguish missing scores from zero scores.
