## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Schedules and past-season listings may be incomplete, and school-recorded scores may be missing; an unknown score or an empty partial search does not establish a result or absence of a team.

Mitigation: Check cancellation, postponement, and TBA status; keep null scores unknown and state the coverage or missing-record caveat when relevant.

Risk: The reviewed instructions do not cover the separate MCP server's implementation or data handling.

Mitigation: Use a trusted implementation of the server; the documented lookups need no account credentials or private browser access.
