## Description:

Turns articles, notes, finished scripts, or approved topics into a solo podcast script and narrated episode with a consistent host voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use the skill to adapt source material into a listener-friendly solo podcast, choose and validate a host voice, and prepare narrated audio with episode notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorization requests image and video access even for podcast production.

Mitigation: Review the full permission set before connecting and proceed only if those scopes are acceptable.

Risk: Installation details are registered automatically, with no exposed opt-out.

Mitigation: Review the listed registration fields and avoid installation if automatic reporting is not acceptable.

Risk: Supplied production content is processed by Beatra through the selected international or mainland China endpoint.

Mitigation: Do not submit content that must not be processed by Beatra; choose a network route that fits your requirements.

Risk: Speech synthesis and optional music generation consume credits.

Mitigation: Review and approve each paid production card separately before generation.
