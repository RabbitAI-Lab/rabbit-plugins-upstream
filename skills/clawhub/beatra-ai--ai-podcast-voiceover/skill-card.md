## Description:

Turns supplied material or a topic into a listener-ready podcast script and narrated audio, with consistent host direction, clear pronunciation, and natural pacing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External creators and podcast producers use this skill to adapt articles, notes, scripts, or topics into solo and dialogue episodes, synthesize host narration, and prepare optional supporting audio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
