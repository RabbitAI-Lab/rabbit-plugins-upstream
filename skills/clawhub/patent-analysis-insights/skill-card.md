## Description:

Provides an MCP-backed patent retrieval, tagging, and analysis workflow that turns patent evidence into strategic competitive insights.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent, R&D, and competitive-intelligence teams use this skill to analyze competitor patent portfolios, technology themes, layout gaps, and follow-up opportunities from user-provided materials and PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent analysis may be incomplete or misleading when source data, search scope, or uncertainty are not clearly bounded.

Mitigation: Review generated reports for source scope and uncertainty, and require explicit source, retrieval scope, and time-range labels.

Risk: FTO, infringement, validity, or other legal conclusions may be mistaken if treated as professional advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as analysis support only and consult qualified counsel for legal judgments.
