## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Art product or software recommendations may not fit a user's budget, medium, hardware, or workflow.

Mitigation: Ask for medium, hardware, and budget context before recommending materials or software, and encourage users to evaluate recommendations independently.

Risk: Art critique can be overwhelming or discouraging when it lists too many changes at once.

Mitigation: Focus critique on one main improvement, point to specific areas, and acknowledge what is working before suggesting changes.
