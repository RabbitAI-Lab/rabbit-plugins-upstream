## Description:

Generate standardized usability and human factors questionnaires (SUS, ASQ, SEQ, NASA-TLX, UEQ, SUS, CSUQ, etc.) with scoring instructions for UX coursework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, educators, students, and UX practitioners use this skill to choose and generate standardized usability or human-factors questionnaires with scoring guidance for usability testing, coursework, experimental design, and subjective evaluation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questionnaire selection or scoring details may be inappropriate for formal research if not checked against the official instrument guidance.

Mitigation: Review the selected questionnaire and verify scoring details against official instrument guidance before using results for formal research or high-stakes decisions.

Risk: Changing standardized questionnaire wording, response scales, or item direction can weaken validity and make results less comparable.

Mitigation: Preserve official item wording, scale structure, and scoring direction when a validated instrument is required.
