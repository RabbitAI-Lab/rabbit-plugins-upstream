## Description:

Helps prepare responses to Chinese patent office actions by drafting a response statement, proposed claim amendments, and an amendment comparison table.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Patent applicants and their representatives can use this skill to prepare a response to a Chinese patent examination opinion, review proposed response strategies and claim amendments, and produce draft documents for professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China only; the skill supports Chinese patent applications and does not support foreign office actions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Server-provided filenames may cause output files to be saved outside the chosen folder.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated output directory and verify each generated file path remains within it before opening or sharing files.

Risk: Full patent materials are processed by an external service, which may be unsuitable for confidential documents.

Mitigation: Confirm external processing is acceptable before submitting materials, and never send API keys through chat.
