## Use Case: <br>
Local business owners and operators use this skill to diagnose store operations, draft short-video scripts, prepare livestream sales flows, review performance data, and get practical marketing guidance for catering, beauty, hospitality, kids, and retail stores. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may direct users to off-platform Notion or WeChat contact flows and can involve merchant or customer data. <br>
Mitigation: Treat merchant and customer data as sensitive, get consent before collecting or saving contact details, and review off-platform links before use. <br>
Risk: The skill can generate local report files and use hosted HTML or CDN assets. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated files and hosted assets before sharing them, and avoid including unnecessary sensitive data in reports. <br>
Risk: Marketing guidance could create consumer-disclosure concerns if it conceals material product facts such as near-expiry food status. <br>
Mitigation: Do not follow advice that hides material product facts; ensure promotions disclose relevant product conditions clearly. <br>
