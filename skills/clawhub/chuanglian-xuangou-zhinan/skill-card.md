## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance on selecting curtains, balancing light control and privacy, and planning tracks, measurements, and installation for different windows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and decorators use this skill to compare curtain materials and styles, plan coverage for bay windows and balconies, and avoid common measurement and installation mistakes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General renovation guidance may not account for site conditions or local rules.

Mitigation: Have qualified professionals assess wall changes, waterproofing, electrical and gas work; follow local requirements.

Risk: Curtain and renovation choices can overlook child safety.

Mitigation: Check child-safety requirements with a qualified professional before installation.
