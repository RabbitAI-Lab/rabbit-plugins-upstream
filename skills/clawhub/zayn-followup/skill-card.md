## Description: <br>
根据客户状态、上次沟通、时间节点和可补充价值，判断是否跟进、何时跟进以及跟进什么。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales and customer communication users use this skill to decide whether, when, and how to follow up with customers after quotes, inquiries, stalled projects, or lapsed contact. It emphasizes evidence quality, timing, new value, stop conditions, and human review before outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Customer records and contact history may contain private or regulated data. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the customer information needed for the follow-up decision and avoid unnecessary private or regulated data. <br>
Risk: Follow-up guidance can influence customer outreach and resource allocation. <br>
Mitigation: Keep final outreach, escalation, and resource decisions under human review. <br>
