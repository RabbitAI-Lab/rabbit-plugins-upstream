## Description:

Validates SemVer version strings and recommends MAJOR, MINOR, or PATCH bumps based on the type of change.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and release maintainers use this skill to check version strings and choose a version bump for changelogs and releases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Primarily Chinese instructions may be misunderstood by readers who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Translate or review the guidance before relying on a version-bump recommendation.
