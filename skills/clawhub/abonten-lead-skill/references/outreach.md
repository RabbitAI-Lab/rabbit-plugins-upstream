# Outreach

Use this reference when ALS drafts or sends a call, email, or professional social message from a researched lead.

## Modes

- **Draft:** create messages and a review table; do not send.
- **Approved batch:** show the exact recipients, channels, subjects/openings, bodies, and evidence used, then send only after explicit approval.
- **Call list:** prepare a short opener, discovery questions, and the public number to call; do not place calls unless the user explicitly asks and the calling capability is available.

## Personalization rules

- Use one specific, cited observation from the sign or public business page.
- Describe the user's offer accurately; ask for the offer details if they are missing.
- Frame a possible improvement as a suggestion, not a guaranteed result.
- Address the business or verified role. Do not expose how a private-looking contact was found.
- Do not claim that the recipient requested contact, that ALS represents a referral, or that a result is guaranteed.

## Email draft

Subject: Idea for {{business_name}}

Hello {{name_or_team}},

I came across {{specific_observed_offer_or_sign}} in {{location}}. I help {{target_businesses}} with {{user_offer}}. Based on {{cited_public_observation}}, there may be an opportunity to {{specific_low-pressure_outcome}}.

Would it be useful if I sent over a short idea tailored to {{business_name}}?

If this is not relevant, reply “no” and I will not follow up.

Regards,  
{{sender_name}}

Replace every placeholder before showing the draft. If the only contact is a general inbox, address the team and ask who handles the relevant decision rather than pretending to have found a direct contact.

## Call opener

“Hello, I’m {{sender_name}}. I saw {{business_name}}’s public promotion for {{offer}}. I help businesses with {{user_offer}}. Who is the best person to speak with about that?”

If connected to the right person, ask one or two discovery questions before presenting a solution. Keep the call concise and record the outcome.

## Review and logging

Before any send or call, check:

- the recipient is a public business or professional contact;
- the business identity and role are supported by evidence;
- the message contains no invented claim or sensitive personal information;
- the user approved the channel, batch, and message;
- the message includes a clear opt-out or honors the recipient's prior opt-out;
- the record will log date, channel, source, outcome, and follow-up status.

Skip or flag leads with Do not contact, an unclear business identity, contradictory contact data, or only a private/unverified contact path. Never send to a guessed address or a purchased/scraped list.
