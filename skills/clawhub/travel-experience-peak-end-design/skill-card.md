## Description:

Guides travel advisors in designing memorable itineraries around a deliberate peak and a strong ending while reducing arrival friction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External travel advisors use this guidance to shape leisure, honeymoon, milestone, and luxury itineraries that clients are more likely to remember and refer. It helps identify a signature high point, improve the trip's ending, and reduce friction around arrival and other low points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel costs, reservations, or client communications proposed from itinerary guidance may be inaccurate or unsuitable.

Mitigation: Confirm current costs and reservations, and review client communications before acting.
