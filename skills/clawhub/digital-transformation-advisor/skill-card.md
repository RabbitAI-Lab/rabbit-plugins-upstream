## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The public search helper may contact api.cgltzk.vip for reference metadata.

Mitigation: Use only generic industry or topic keywords and do not send confidential client names, budgets, internal system details, credentials, or full requirements.

Risk: Reference matches are based on public metadata and may not reflect the full document contents or access terms.

Mitigation: Treat search results as candidate references, open only legitimate public detail pages, and verify relevance, download rights, and membership requirements separately.

Risk: Transformation plans may contain assumptions, estimates, or time-sensitive policy, pricing, and compliance statements.

Mitigation: Label assumptions clearly and verify laws, subsidies, standards, market prices, and client-specific evidence with current authoritative sources before relying on the plan.
