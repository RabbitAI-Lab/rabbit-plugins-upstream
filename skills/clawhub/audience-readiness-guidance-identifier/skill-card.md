## Description:

Classify a stakeholder brief by audience and define which sections are ready for client circulation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Briefing authors and communications teams use this skill to select audience-appropriate sections before preparing stakeholder updates or client briefings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A heuristic audience policy may be mistaken for confirmation that every section is approved for client circulation.

Mitigation: Check recipients and approval status of each section before sharing a brief externally.
