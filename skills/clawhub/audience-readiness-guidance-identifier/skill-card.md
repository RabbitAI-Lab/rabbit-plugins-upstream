## Description:

Classify a stakeholder brief by audience and define which sections are ready for client circulation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Briefing authors and stakeholders use this skill to choose an audience mode and guide which sections of a stakeholder brief to include before circulation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Briefing text may be misclassified as suitable for external circulation.

Mitigation: Confirm approval and audience suitability before sharing a brief with clients.

Risk: Extra input fields may influence the inferred audience.

Mitigation: Provide only briefing content intended for classification and review the returned audience mode.
