## Description:

Classify a stakeholder brief by audience and define which sections are ready for client circulation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and communications teams use this skill to classify stakeholder briefs by intended audience and decide which sections are ready for client, executive, or internal circulation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Briefing inputs may contain unapproved or ambiguous material that is not ready for the intended audience.

Mitigation: Confirm approvals and audience suitability before circulating generated briefing guidance.
