## Description:

Turns verified project requirements, method cards, and human design direction into comparable, falsifiable design-test frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caninlew](https://clawhub.ai/user/caninlew)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Architects, designers, and project teams use this skill to turn verified requirements, method cards, and design direction into comparable test frameworks, comparison matrices, minimum-model tasks, and round logs. It helps teams evaluate spatial or operational alternatives without selecting a final scheme.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project output and log directories may be inappropriate for the round, especially when the skill writes versioned design artifacts or records architect feedback verbatim.

Mitigation: Confirm the project output and log directories before use, and review captured feedback before treating it as round evidence.

Risk: Design-test outputs could be mistaken for final schemes or professionally validated design decisions.

Mitigation: Keep outputs at the variable, relationship, or mechanism layer, and route final choices, statutory conclusions, and professional sign-off to the named human reviewer.
