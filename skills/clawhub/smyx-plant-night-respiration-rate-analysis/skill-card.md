## Description:

Estimates relative nighttime plant respiration intensity from canopy thermal imagery and optional ambient CO₂ data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Plant-factory, greenhouse, and controlled-environment agriculture operators use this skill to estimate nighttime plant respiration from thermal images or video, optionally combined with CO₂ measurements. It can also retrieve previous analysis reports; results are for research and crop-care reference and do not replace instrument measurements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
