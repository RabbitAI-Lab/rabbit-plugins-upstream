## Description: <br>
Guides agents through human-approved crisis triage, moderation advice, holding-statement drafting, misinformation response, and queue pause recommendations for social media incidents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media teams and brand operators use this skill when negative attention, misinformation, deepfakes, offensive-post backlash, or community moderation issues need structured triage and human-approved response drafting. It helps classify severity, prepare holding statements, recommend fair moderation actions, and decide whether scheduled posts should be paused. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Autonomous crisis replies or legal-sensitive statements could create reputational or legal harm. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for triage, drafting, and advice only; require human approval for every public response and escalate high-severity matters to legal, leadership, or PR professionals. <br>
Risk: Queue changes during a crisis or sensitive news moment could pause, delete, or reschedule intended posts without clear authorization. <br>
Mitigation: Follow the human confirmation flow before changing scheduled posts, and limit actions to the user's own pending posts. <br>
Risk: Users may confuse moderation advice with direct platform moderation capability. <br>
Mitigation: State that WoopSocial has no inbox, listening, or moderation surface; humans perform comment hiding, blocking, monitoring, and moderation in the native platform. <br>
