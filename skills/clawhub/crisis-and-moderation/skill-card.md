## Description: <br>
Guides agents through social media crisis triage, response drafting, fair moderation advice, escalation, and pausing scheduled posts while keeping humans responsible for approvals and platform actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Social media and community teams use this skill when brand accounts face pile-ons, misinformation, offensive-post backlash, deepfakes, sensitive news moments, or moderation decisions. It helps them confirm facts, rate severity, draft responses, pause scheduled posts, and escalate high-severity cases for human, legal, or leadership approval. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Autonomous crisis posting or moderation could create legal, reputational, or safety harm. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for triage, drafting, and queue guidance only; require human approval and legal or leadership review for high-severity issues before any public action. <br>
Risk: The skill can advise on moderation and scheduled-post handling, but WoopSocial cannot moderate comments, monitor channels, or manage an inbox. <br>
Mitigation: Keep moderation and monitoring in native platform tools with a human operator; use WoopSocial only for supported scheduling actions. <br>
