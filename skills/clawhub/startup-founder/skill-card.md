A startup skill library for independent developers covering idea validation, MVP building, founder-led sales, and fundraising preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Independent developers and small startup teams use this skill to assess ideas, plan MVPs, develop founder-led sales, and prepare for fundraising. It provides playbooks and focused subskills while leaving business direction and resource commitments with the founder.
