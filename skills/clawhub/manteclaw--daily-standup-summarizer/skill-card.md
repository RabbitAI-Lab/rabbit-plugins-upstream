## Description:

Turn yesterday's recorded activity into a concise three-section standup covering done, doing, and blocked work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill each morning to turn recorded work activity into a brief status update. It highlights verified completions, the next milestone for active work, and blockers with owners and unblocking steps.
