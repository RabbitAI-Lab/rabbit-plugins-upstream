## Description: <br>
Autopsy a slide deck from photos or screenshots of its slides: the narrative arc, the numbers, and what each slide is hiding. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, analysts, and teams use this skill to critique slide deck images, reconstruct the deck's argument, test its numbers, identify weak links, and prepare counter-questions or improvements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Slide images may contain confidential business information. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide decks you are allowed to analyze, and avoid submitting sensitive material to environments that are not approved for that data. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or unreadable slide images can lead to unsupported conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Provide slides in order where possible, require findings to cite slide numbers, and flag illegible content instead of guessing. <br>
