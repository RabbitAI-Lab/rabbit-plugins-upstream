## Description:

Draw Lenormand cards from QiyueAstro - single card, three-card, relationship, decision, nine-card grid, and more. Browse the full 36-card Lenormand deck with meanings in English or Chinese. No API key needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to draw Lenormand cards, browse card meanings, and retrieve available spreads from the QiyueAstro public API in English or Chinese.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User questions may be sent to qiyueastro.com when requesting a reading.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive personal, health, financial, or account details in reading questions.

Risk: Lenormand readings may be mistaken for professional or factual advice.

Mitigation: Use the returned card content for entertainment and self-reflection only.
