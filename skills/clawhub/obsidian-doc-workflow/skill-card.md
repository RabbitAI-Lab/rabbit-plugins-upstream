## Description:

Create, update, classify, and archive Obsidian documentation following structured vault conventions and templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ehsanghaffar](https://clawhub.ai/user/ehsanghaffar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Documentation maintainers and developers use this skill to organize an Obsidian vault, create and update templated notes, and archive obsolete material while preserving links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Archiving or moving important notes can disrupt existing links or place active material in an archive.

Mitigation: Review archive decisions and affected links before moving notes; preserve original content and confirm replacement links.

Risk: The English-primary documentation policy may not match a team's language needs.

Mitigation: Confirm the vault and language policy match team expectations before adopting the workflow.
