## Description:

Reports current health, incidents, alerts, uptime history, on-call coverage, and maintenance for an Uptimely project without changing it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Operators and developers use this skill to check an Uptimely project's current status, open incidents and alerts, on-call coverage, upcoming maintenance, and monitor history.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad questions such as 'is anything down' may trigger a check of the wrong Uptimely project.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for a status check and confirm the project when context is ambiguous.
