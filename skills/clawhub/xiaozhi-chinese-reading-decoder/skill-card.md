## Description:

Guides Chinese-language students through close reading, evidence-based answers, and review of modern-text comprehension questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper-primary through high school use this skill to understand Chinese modern texts, practice answering reading-comprehension questions, and review mistakes with text-based evidence. Younger students should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-mainland curriculum context; localize guidance and support resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Advice or support resources may not fit deployments outside the China-mainland educational context.

Mitigation: Use for Chinese K12 reading support only when the deployment matches or has localized its curriculum and support assumptions.

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill or parent sharing, and reminders involve sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Obtain the relevant student or guardian consent before enabling these features, and provide deletion, pause, and export controls.
