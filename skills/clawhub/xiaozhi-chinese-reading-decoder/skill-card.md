## Description:

Guides Chinese-language K–12 learners through close reading, reading-comprehension practice, and evidence-based answers before showing worked solutions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, educators, and guardians use this skill to practice Chinese modern-text reading, diagnose recurring comprehension errors, and improve written responses without bypassing the learner's own attempt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learner profiles may contain minors' reading performance and may be shared across skills.

Mitigation: Confirm learner and guardian consent before using profile memory or cross-skill sharing; honor requests to pause, correct, delete, or restrict sharing.
