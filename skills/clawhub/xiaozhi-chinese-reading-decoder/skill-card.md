## Description:

Coaches students through Simplified Chinese reading comprehension, from understanding modern texts to supporting answers with textual evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary through high school use this coach to read Chinese prose and informational texts, identify evidence, and improve their own responses to comprehension questions. Younger students use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localization of curriculum, minor-data safeguards, and crisis contacts before student use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's reading profile or learning activity could be shared with other skills, parents, or reminders without their intended consent.

Mitigation: Check the opt-in profile, sharing, parent-visibility, and reminder settings before use; honor requests to pause, correct, export, or delete the profile.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and safety guidance may be unsuitable for students in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, minor-data requirements, and crisis contacts before deployment outside mainland China.
