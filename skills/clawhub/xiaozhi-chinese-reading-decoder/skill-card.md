## Description:

Coaches Chinese K–12 students through modern-text reading comprehension, grounding feedback in the passage before helping them structure answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper-primary and middle-school Chinese classes use this skill to practice reading modern prose and informational texts, review their own answers, and improve evidence-based responses. Younger pupils require a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; localize curriculum and crisis referrals before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning-profile memory or sharing could expose a minor's study history.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian involvement and consent, and review sharing, reminders, export, correction, and deletion controls before use.

Risk: Mainland China crisis-referral contacts may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize referral information to the student's region before deployment outside mainland China.
