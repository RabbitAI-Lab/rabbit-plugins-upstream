## Description:

Creates English-learning materials from a lesson, including vocabulary, spoken audio, a translated lesson page, and interactive quizzes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bjbook](https://clawhub.ai/user/bjbook)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners and educators use the skill to turn English lesson text into vocabulary practice, spoken examples, a bilingual lesson page, and reading and sentence quizzes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson text and vocabulary sent for online speech generation may be exposed to the TTS provider.

Mitigation: Use only text you are comfortable sending to the provider; do not send sensitive, private, or proprietary lessons without authorization.

Risk: Untrusted lesson or question content may appear in generated learning pages.

Mitigation: Generate pages from trusted lesson and question data and review the results before sharing.
