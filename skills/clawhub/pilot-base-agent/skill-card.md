## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Manufacturing, compliance, financing, and safety recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific real-world project. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and have qualified domain professionals verify decisions before implementation. <br>
Risk: The skill can produce investor-, customer-, or partner-facing deliverables that may overstate readiness if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated deliverables against project evidence, test data, regulatory requirements, and commercial diligence standards before external use. <br>
