## Description:

Plan, draft, schedule, and publish platform-native social posts across connected accounts, then review analytics and comments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jackvitick](https://clawhub.ai/user/jackvitick)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, and social media managers use the skill to plan platform-specific content calendars, publish approved posts to connected accounts, and review performance and comments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting Socialync grants access to the social accounts the user authorizes and sends posting content and media through its hosted service.

Mitigation: Connect only when ready to publish; review each post and reply, including its account, audience, and time; revoke access when finished.

Risk: An exposed API key could provide account access.

Mitigation: Keep API keys out of chat and revoke access when finished.
