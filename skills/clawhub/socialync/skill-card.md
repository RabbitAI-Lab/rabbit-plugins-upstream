## Description:

Plan platform-specific social media content, schedule and publish posts to connected accounts after user approval, and review performance and comments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media teams and account owners use this skill to draft platform-specific content calendars, publish approved posts across connected accounts, and review analytics and comments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A published post may disclose or direct audiences to external content.

Mitigation: Review the X link-posting guidance and the exact post and destination before approving publication.

Risk: Publishing and comment replies are public actions on connected social accounts.

Mitigation: Review the exact content, account, platform, and timing, and approve each action before it is sent.
