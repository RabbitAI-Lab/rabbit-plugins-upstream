## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Search terms can be sent to DuckDuckGo and to any configured proxy. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use the skill with secrets, credentials, private personal data, or confidential business material, and review proxy configuration before use. <br>
Risk: Search results can be incomplete, outdated, or point to third-party content with separate copyright or usage constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important results against authoritative sources and review third-party content terms before reuse. <br>
