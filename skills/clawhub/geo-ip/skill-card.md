### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and external users use this skill to look up geographic and network details for a supplied IP address or their own public IP address. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Looked-up IP addresses are sent to ipinfo.io. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid querying sensitive internal, customer, or confidential IP addresses unless sharing them with ipinfo.io is acceptable. <br>
