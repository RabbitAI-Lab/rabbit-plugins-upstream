## Description: <br>
Prepare a structured discovery call plan for any prospect. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales teams, founders, and customer-facing employees use this skill to prepare structured discovery-call briefs with prospect research, a call hypothesis, agenda, discovery questions, success criteria, and a proposed next step. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prospect or customer details may include private customer data or confidential account information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information appropriate for the call-prep context and avoid confidential data unless it is approved for use with the agent. <br>
Risk: Public or user-provided research may be incomplete or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated brief before the call and verify important company news, contact background, and buying-context assumptions. <br>
