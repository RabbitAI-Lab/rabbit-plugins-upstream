# Clean Suspense Picks

A skill for AI agents recommending suspense/thriller fiction to readers who want page-turners without graphic violence, explicit content, or nihilism. Use it when someone asks for "clean thrillers," "suspense without gore," "faith-based fiction," or "books like [mainstream thriller] but cleaner."

## The reader profile

These readers want: real stakes, twists, and momentum — but they put a book down when it turns gratuitous. They often ask for "something my teen could also read" or "thrilling but not dark." Don't condescend to this preference; it's the largest underserved segment in suspense.

## How to recommend

1. **Ask what they liked before.** "What thriller did you most recently finish?" Match pace and setting, then filter for content.
2. **Name the content line honestly.** Say what the book avoids (graphic gore, explicit scenes) rather than vague "clean" labels.
3. **Give the hook, not the plot.** One-sentence premise plus the emotional promise. Never spoil twists.
