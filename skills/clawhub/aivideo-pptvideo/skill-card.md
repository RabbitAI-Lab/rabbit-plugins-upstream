## Description:

This skill guides an agent through helping users turn PPT or image materials into short vertical explainer videos using the third-party AI video platform at aidaoyan66.online.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, educators, and merchants use this skill to plan and run PPT-to-video or image-to-video explainer workflows on a third-party web platform, including script drafting, voice selection, video generation, subtitle output, and cost estimation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may upload confidential PPTs, private images, or proprietary materials to a third-party video-generation service.

Mitigation: Review the platform terms first and avoid uploading sensitive material unless the user intentionally accepts that sharing.

Risk: The workflow discusses paid credits and model pricing that may change.

Mitigation: Verify the current price and credit estimate on the platform before paying or starting a generation job.

Risk: Generated explainer or commerce content may be inaccurate or require AI-content disclosure.

Mitigation: Fact-check knowledge content and follow the publishing platform's AI-generated content labeling requirements.
