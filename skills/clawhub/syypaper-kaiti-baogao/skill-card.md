## Description:

中文毕业论文开题报告自动写作与排版工作流。用于本科、硕士或博士开题报告任务，包括近五年中英文文献检索与真实性核验、GB/T 7714—2015 文献整理、用户文献核验、学校 DOC/DOCX/WPS/PDF 模板识别、正文结构确认、研究设计、技术路线图生成、可编辑 DOCX 填充排版和逐页质量检查；也用于用户只要求检索与整理开题报告参考文献的场景。

## Use Case:

Students and academic writers use this skill to plan Chinese dissertation proposal reports, verify and organize recent references, adapt school templates, and produce editable DOCX reports with a technical route diagram. It can also be used for a narrower reference-search-only workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports and final responses include syypaper branding, a syypaper.com link, and a QR-code image.

Mitigation: Disclose the branding behavior before installation and confirm it is acceptable for the intended academic or institutional use.

Risk: The workflow may browse the web and process uploaded school templates while preparing a report.

Mitigation: Use only appropriate public sources, avoid paywall or access-control bypass, and process copies of uploaded templates rather than modifying originals.

Risk: Literature metadata, citations, research designs, or generated proposal content could be inaccurate if not reviewed.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation of search strategy, final references, document structure, and writing plan before generating the final report.
