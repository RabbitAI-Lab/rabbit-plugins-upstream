## Description: <br>
Combines brainstorming deep-dive with domain modeling for precise requirement exploration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and product teams use this skill to turn ambiguous feature or refactoring ideas into clearer requirements, domain vocabulary, and architecture decisions. It guides an agent through brainstorming, domain modeling, and consistency checks across the resulting project documents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow may create or modify project planning documents, including design docs, CONTEXT.md, and ADR files. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it only when the full brainstorming and domain-modeling workflow is intended, and review generated documentation before accepting changes. <br>
