## Description: <br>
Produce a structured budget variance analysis from actual vs budget figures, including categorized variance tables, root cause analysis, and management commentary. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Finance, accounting, and management users use this skill to turn actual-versus-budget figures into board-ready variance commentary, root cause analysis, and forecast impact notes. <br>

Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Confidential financial data could be exposed if users paste budget or actuals data they are not authorized to process in their agent environment. <br>
Mitigation: Use only financial data the user is authorized to process, and follow internal controls for handling budget, actuals, and forecast information. <br>
