# Pipeline Usage Example

```
User: I want to write a paper about AI in higher education quality assurance, from research to submission

Step 1: ars-deep-research -> Research report
Step 2: ars-academic-paper -> Paper first draft
Step 3: integrity check -> 100% verification of references/data
Step 4: ars-academic-reviewer (full) -> 5 review reports + Revision Roadmap
Step 5: ars-academic-paper (revision) -> Revised manuscript
Step 6: ars-academic-reviewer (re-review) -> Verification review
Step 7: (if needed) ars-academic-paper (revision) -> Second revised manuscript
Step 8: integrity check (final) -> Final 100% verification
Step 9: ars-academic-paper (format-convert) -> Final paper
```
