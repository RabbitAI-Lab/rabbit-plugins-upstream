## Description:

Creates a measurable moodboard and visual board for AI image and video projects, then checks sample stills against visual targets before video generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and video developers use the skill to turn a visual brief and references into a concrete style guide for AI-generated imagery and video. It produces a ranked moodboard, a visual board, and checks for sample stills before users commit to video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
