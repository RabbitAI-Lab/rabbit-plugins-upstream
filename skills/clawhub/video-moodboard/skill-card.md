## Description:

Creates a reference-led visual moodboard with measurable guidance for AI image and video generation, then checks test stills against its specifications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, art directors, and video teams use this skill to turn a visual brief into measurable reference, lighting, framing, palette, texture, type, motion, and sound guidance before generating video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference briefs or images may not be approved for the external services used to generate test stills.

Mitigation: Use briefs and images approved for the chosen services, and confirm image rights before submitting them.

Risk: Generating test stills through online models can incur costs.

Mitigation: Review the selected model’s pricing and expected generation costs before proceeding.

Risk: Opening the visual board loads remote fonts and images.

Mitigation: Review the board’s linked and embedded remote content before opening it in a restricted environment.

Risk: Optional linked tools and live service behavior were not audited.

Mitigation: Review optional tools and service behavior separately before installing or authorizing them.
