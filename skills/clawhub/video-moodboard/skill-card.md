## Description:

Creates a ranked moodboard for AI image and video work, with measurable visual directions, a visual board, and test stills checked against the brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Video creators, designers, and production teams use the skill to turn a visual brief and selected references into a specific style guide before generating video. They can check the visual direction on still images before committing to paid video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The unpinned npx install command may resolve to a different package version over time.

Mitigation: Pin the package version or review the resolved package before installation.

Risk: Private briefs or reference images may be sent to external services when generating test stills.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive material and check image rights and the service's privacy terms before uploading.
