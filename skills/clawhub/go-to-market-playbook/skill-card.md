## Description: <br>
Go To Market Playbook provides a fill-in launch planning template for positioning, ICP definition, messaging, channel-market fit, and competitive battle cards for B2B or B2C products. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External product, marketing, and founder teams use this skill to structure launch planning before committing budget to channels. It helps agents produce positioning canvases, ICP worksheets, messaging frameworks, channel-market fit plans, and competitive battle cards. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad marketing and positioning triggers may activate the skill in unrelated discussions. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it deliberately for GTM planning and ignore or disable it when the conversation is not about launch strategy, positioning, ICPs, messaging, channels, or competitive analysis. <br>
Risk: Launch plans and messaging can become misleading if generated without customer evidence or business review. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with the launch team, validate messaging with target customers, and check channel recommendations against current budget, acquisition costs, and product stage before execution. <br>
