## Description: <br>
Reels Script Studio generates short-form vertical video scripts with shot lists, platform-ready AI video prompts, camera and lighting guidance, quality checks, and user-facing production instructions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bbroot](https://clawhub.ai/user/bbroot) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn product, education, narrative, vlog, review, or trending-topic ideas into publishable short-video scripts and AI video generation prompts. It is especially oriented toward Chinese-first workflows for Douyin, WeChat Channels, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube, Jimeng, Xiaoyunque, Seedance, and Jianying. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Feedback notes and personalization details may be retained locally in evolution.json. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid recording sensitive client or campaign details, and review or clear evolution.json before sharing the workspace. <br>
Risk: Generated marketing scripts may include platform compliance issues, prohibited advertising claims, copyrighted music recommendations, or brand-tone mismatches. <br>
Mitigation: Run human review for brand fit, cultural context, prohibited words, factual claims, and BGM or asset licensing before publication. <br>
Risk: Viral-performance metrics and benchmarks are planning signals rather than guaranteed outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the metrics as script-quality prompts and validate results with platform analytics and controlled tests. <br>
