## Description: <br>
The LinkedIn company-page strategy skill helps agents plan company-page growth, employee advocacy, page posts, and scheduled page publishing while keeping employee posting, paid campaigns, native LinkedIn features, analytics, and engagement decisions human-owned. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and social media operators use this skill to position a LinkedIn company page as a credibility hub and paid launchpad, design employee advocacy programs, draft page posts, and prepare shareable advocacy content. It is suited to company-page strategy, not personal-profile posting or automated engagement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Scheduled company-page posts or advocacy suggestions could publish inaccurate, off-brand, or noncompliant content if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review scheduled posts, confirm brand voice and publishing permissions, and keep employee advocacy voluntary and personalized before publication. <br>
Risk: LinkedIn platform behavior, reach benchmarks, and native feature availability change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Re-check LinkedIn guidance and current page analytics before relying on benchmark claims or making paid and native feature decisions. <br>
Risk: Automation or pressure around engagement can create inauthentic behavior or violate platform expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid engagement bait, pods, bought followers, forced-identical employee posts, and fabricated metrics; keep first-hour engagement, ads, and analytics human/native. <br>
