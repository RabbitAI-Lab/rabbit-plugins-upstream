## Description:

metricas-semanales-f100k helps an OpenClaw agent produce a weekly Instagram or TikTok metrics report that identifies the top post, explains why it worked, compares prior-week performance when data exists, and recommends what to repeat next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and social-media operators use this skill to gather weekly Instagram or TikTok post metrics, identify the best-performing post, and produce an actionable recommendation for the following week.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Report delivery instructions conflict between Telegram and Gmail, which may route social-media metrics through an unexpected connected service.

Mitigation: Before installing or running the skill, choose one report destination and confirm which connected services are allowed to receive account metrics and post data.
