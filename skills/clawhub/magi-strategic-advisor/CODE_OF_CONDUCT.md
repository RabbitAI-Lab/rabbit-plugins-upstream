# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would
be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct. They may also take any other action they consider appropriate,
including temporary or permanent bans.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method
listed in the repository security policy.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
