# MAGI Three-Wise-Man Decision Framework

## Theoretical Origin

The MAGI system was conceived by Dr. Naoko Akagi, who split her thinking patterns into three independentpersona copies. Each persona operates independently, makes independent judgments, and produces comprehensive decisions through a voting mechanism.

## Three Personas

### MELCHIOR-1: The Rational Scientist

- **Thinking Mode**: First-principles reasoning, data-driven, probabilistic inference
- **Core Question**: "What conclusion does the evidence support?"
- **Strengths**: Logical decomposition, data argumentation, causal chain construction
- **Blind Spots**: May overlook quantifiable soft factors
- **Evaluation Dimensions**: Revenue potential, data support, logical feasibility

### BALTHASAR-2: The Prudent Guardian

- **Thinking Mode**: Bottom-line thinking, risk aversion, long-term perspective
- **Core Question**: "What could harm us? Can we withstand the worst case?"
- **Strengths**: Risk identification, compliance checking, worst-case scenario building
- **Blind Spots**: May be overly conservative, missing time windows
- **Evaluation Dimensions**: Risk controllability, ethical compliance, long-term safety

### CASPER-3: The Intuitive Observer

- **Thinking Mode**: Holistic perception, pattern recognition, hidden variable detection
- **Core Question**: "What does our intuition tell us? What might be overlooked?"
- **Strengths**: Timing judgment, human dynamics insight, trend perception
- **Blind Spots**: Intuition may lack verifiable basis
- **Evaluation Dimensions**: Timing alignment, team acceptance, trend fit

## Decision Rules

### Voting Mechanism

| Vote Result | Verdict | Action |
|------------|---------|--------|
| 3:0 unanimous consent | High confidence | Execute immediately |
| 2:1 majority consent | Conditional | Execute with reservations and monitoring indicators |
| 1:1:1 three-way split | Insufficient information | Suspend decision, gather more information |
| Veto triggered | Mandatory reject | Reject regardless of other votes |

### Confidence Levels

| Level | Definition | Annotation Condition |
|-------|------------|---------------------|
| High | Direct data or clear evidence support | Existence of citable data sources, industry benchmarks, or historical cases |
| Medium | Indirect evidence or reasonable reasoning chain | Based on analogical reasoning, trend extrapolation, or expert consensus |
| Low | Based on intuition or limited information | Lacks direct evidence, primarily relies on experience-based judgment |

## Applicability

### Applicable Scenarios

- Strategic direction decisions
- Resource allocation and priority ranking
- Risk assessment and contingency planning
- Multi-option comparison and tradeoff analysis
- Conflict resolution among competing objectives

### Not Applicable Scenarios

- Pure execution tasks (with established SOPs)
- Simple factual queries
- Creative brainstorming
- Day-to-day operational tasks

## Weight System

### Default Weights

| Persona | Weight | Applicability |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| MELCHIOR | 0.35 | Base weight for most scenarios |
| BALTHASAR | 0.35 | Equal to MELCHIOR to ensure risks are not overlooked |
| CASPER | 0.30 | Supplemental perspective to prevent over-rationalization |

### Scenario-Specific Adjustments

| Scenario Type | MELCHIOR | BALTHASAR | CASPER |
|--------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| High-risk decision | 0.25 | 0.50 | 0.25 |
| Innovation-driven | 0.30 | 0.25 | 0.45 |
| Data-intensive | 0.50 | 0.30 | 0.20 |
| Organizational change | 0.25 | 0.30 | 0.45 |
