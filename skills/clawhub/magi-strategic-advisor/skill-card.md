## Description:

Provides structured strategic analysis, risk assessment, and multi-option comparison using the MAGI three-perspective decision framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and decision-makers use the skill to structure consequential decisions, compare alternatives, and assess risks through rational, cautious, and intuitive perspectives. Its recommendations and scores are decision aids, not substitutes for verifying assumptions or applying appropriate professional judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scores and recommendations may be mistaken for definitive advice, including suggestions to execute immediately.

Mitigation: Treat them as decision aids; verify assumptions and apply appropriate professional judgment before acting.

Risk: English-language activation conventions may trigger the skill for ordinary comparisons.

Mitigation: Check that the structured strategic analysis applies to the decision before relying on its results.

Risk: Generating an HTML risk report can overwrite an existing file at the chosen output path.

Mitigation: Choose a distinct output path and confirm it does not contain a file that must be preserved.
