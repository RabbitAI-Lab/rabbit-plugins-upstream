## Description:

Creates study guides, summaries, cheat sheets, practice questions, and interactive quizzes from selected uNotes documents in chat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners use selected uNotes documents to prepare summaries, study sheets, and practice questions or quiz themselves in chat.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searching a library could select private or unrelated notes for inclusion in study materials.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected document with the user before reading its contents, especially when search is used.
