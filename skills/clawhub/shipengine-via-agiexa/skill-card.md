## Description:

Previews the planned ShipEngine and Shopify integration through Agiexa, explains future shipping workflows, and supports waitlist signup only with explicit user permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Merchants evaluating Agiexa's planned ShipEngine and Shopify integration can use this skill to understand future shipment tracking, rate comparison, address validation, and label workflows. It also explains the launch waitlist, which is submitted only after the user explicitly confirms their details and consent; the integration actions are not callable in this preview.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The integration is a preview and its listed shipping actions are not currently callable.

Mitigation: Describe these workflows as future capabilities; do not claim an action ran or invent store data.

Risk: Waitlist signup shares the user's displayed contact details with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after explicit confirmation of the details and consent statement; omit optional links and messages when not provided.

Risk: The future service's security claims have not been independently verified.

Mitigation: Do not present those claims as independently verified.
