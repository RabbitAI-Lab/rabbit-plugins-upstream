## Description:

Explains the planned ShipEngine for Shopify integration through Agiexa and how merchants can join its launch waitlist with explicit permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devkrutik](https://clawhub.ai/user/devkrutik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants use this skill to learn how the planned Agiexa and ShipEngine integration can support shipping workflows, understand its future capabilities, and optionally request launch updates through an explicitly approved waitlist signup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Waitlist signup shares confirmed contact details with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user explicitly agrees and confirms the details; keep optional notes free of secrets.

Risk: Future shipping actions may purchase labels or change or cancel shipments.

Mitigation: The described shipping tools are not callable today. When available, review the proposed shipment changes and costs with the user and obtain explicit approval before acting.
