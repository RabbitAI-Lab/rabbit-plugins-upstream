## Description: <br>
Design an employee engagement survey and analyse results. Use when asked to create an employee survey, engagement questionnaire, pulse survey, or eNPS survey. Also use when asked to analyse survey results. Produces a complete survey with questions, rating scales, and an analysis framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, HR teams, people managers, and operators use this skill to design employee engagement, pulse, onboarding, exit, or eNPS surveys and to analyze survey results into themes, scores, action plans, and employee communication drafts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Employee survey results may contain sensitive or identifying information, especially in verbatim comments or very small team segments. <br>
Mitigation: Use only authorized data, remove identifying details where possible, and suppress small-team analysis when anonymity was promised. <br>
Risk: Survey design or analysis may mislead employees if question wording, benchmarks, thresholds, or follow-up commitments are not reviewed. <br>
Mitigation: Review survey content and analysis before use, include open-text questions, define anonymity rules up front, and commit to communicating results and actions. <br>
