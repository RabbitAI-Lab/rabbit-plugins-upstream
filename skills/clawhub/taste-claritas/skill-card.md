## Description:

A formal observation framework derived from Aquinas's three conditions of beauty — integritas, consonantia, and claritas — as a perceptual checklist for composition analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnnyzijianwu](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnnyzijianwu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to critique compositions through an aesthetic checklist based on wholeness, proportion, and radiance of form.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Aesthetic critique may be presented as more objective or complete than the checklist supports.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an interpretive aid and review conclusions against the actual composition and intended audience.

Risk: The skill changes an agent's reasoning style but does not provide executable safeguards or data-access controls.

Mitigation: Rely on the host agent and platform controls for permissions, data handling, and deployment review.
