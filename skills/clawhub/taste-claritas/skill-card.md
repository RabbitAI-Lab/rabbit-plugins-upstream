## Description:

A formal observation framework derived from Aquinas's three conditions of beauty — integritas, consonantia, and claritas — as a perceptual checklist for composition analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnnyzijianwu](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnnyzijianwu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and reviewers use this framework to assess whether a composition feels complete, proportionate, and visually legible through three linked checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Treating formal proportion and clarity as universal standards could misfit compositions that intentionally prioritize asymmetry, texture, or emotional expression.

Mitigation: Use the checklist as an optional diagnostic lens and assess the composition against its intended aims.
