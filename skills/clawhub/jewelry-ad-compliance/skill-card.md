## Description: <br>
Reviews jewelry and accessories marketing copy for advertising-law and industry compliance risks using a red, orange, and yellow term library, then produces annotated remediation guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cullolo](https://clawhub.ai/user/cullolo) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, compliance, and legal-review teams use this skill to screen jewelry product pages, live scripts, social posts, promotion copy, and posters before publication. It helps identify high-, medium-, and low-risk claims and suggests safer wording for human review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with China-focused advertising-law and jewelry-sector assumptions. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save compliance reports in the workspace, which can persist sensitive marketing drafts or complaint details. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm before writing files, use a non-shared workspace when content is sensitive, and redact confidential or personal data from reports. <br>
Risk: The skill describes adding user-provided historical complaint examples to its future word library. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit approval before modifying the word library, record the source of any new entry, and avoid storing personal or confidential complaint content. <br>
Risk: Compliance findings are rule- and word-library-assisted guidance, not final legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Have legal or compliance staff review outputs before publishing marketing material, and keep the word library aligned with current regulatory practice. <br>
