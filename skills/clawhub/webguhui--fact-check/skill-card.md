## Description: <br>
Verifies claims, statements, and information against reliable sources through cross-reference searches, source credibility assessment, and evidence-based verdicts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[WebGuHui](https://clawhub.ai/user/WebGuHui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to check concrete claims, statistics, quotes, dates, news events, historical facts, and product or company information. It guides the agent to compare reliable sources, assess credibility, and return a clear verdict with evidence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Fact-checking outputs can be incorrect or incomplete if the agent relies on weak, stale, or insufficient sources. <br>
Mitigation: Require cited evidence, compare multiple reliable sources, distinguish unverified claims from false claims, and preserve caveats when evidence is limited. <br>
Risk: Claims involving current events, quotes, statistics, or company information may change or require primary-source confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Use current source checks where available and prefer original studies, official records, transcripts, filings, or authoritative organizational statements. <br>
