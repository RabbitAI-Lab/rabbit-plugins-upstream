## Description:

This skill helps agents turn a static ad first frame or poster into a Chinese AI-HIVE workflow with diagnostics, motion concepts, image-to-video prompts, runnable commands, task records, and acceptance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Advertisers, short-video producers, and merchants use this skill to convert authorized product or poster assets into first-frame animation plans and production-ready AI-HIVE video generation steps. It supports Chinese marketing video workflows that need scripts, prompts, model-routing choices, task records, and platform-oriented quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: API credentials may be exposed through logs, screenshots, or repositories.

Mitigation: Use placeholder keys in examples, keep AI_HIVE_API_KEY in the environment or a permission-restricted local config file, and review output before sharing.

Risk: AI-HIVE generation calls may upload media and incur costs.

Mitigation: Confirm the final prompt, generation mode, routing choice, and price snapshot before submitting tasks; use small samples for batch work.

Risk: Marketing videos can contain unsupported product claims, unauthorized media, or platform compliance issues.

Mitigation: Upload only media the user has rights to use, mark unverifiable facts for review, and check claims, platform rules, and required AI labeling before publication.
