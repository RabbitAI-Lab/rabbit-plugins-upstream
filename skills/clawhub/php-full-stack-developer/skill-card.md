## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: OpenClaw workspace memory and local logs can capture sensitive project details if used carelessly. <br>
Mitigation: Keep secrets, credentials, and unnecessary personal data out of memory and log files, and review logged entries for sensitive details. <br>
Risk: Governance prompts may produce incorrect or misleading engineering guidance if applied without project-specific review. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations before execution and verify changes with the commands, smoke checks, and rollback notes required by the skill. <br>
