## Description: <br>
Creates structured influencer campaign briefs with deliverables, key messages, creative direction, timelines, disclosure requirements, usage rights, and compensation terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, influencer managers, and agents use this skill to turn campaign, product, platform, deliverable, timeline, compensation, and compliance inputs into creator-ready influencer briefs. It is useful for standardizing brief formats, briefing platform-specific content, onboarding ambassador partners, and reducing revision cycles. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read existing marketing narrative, claims projections, and creator or channel context that contain outdated or unapproved messaging. <br>
Mitigation: Review the accepted narrative canon, claims projection offset, audience, market, media, offer window, and disclaimer before treating the brief as creator-ready. <br>
Risk: Campaign briefs or proposed durable marketing facts could persist sensitive, incorrect, or premature campaign details when saving is approved. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user permission before saving and route durable creator, channel, claim, or campaign facts as proposals rather than automatic canonical updates. <br>
Risk: Sponsored-content disclosure, usage-rights, or compensation terms could be incomplete if source inputs are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unresolved items visible, verify disclosure placement and usage-rights duration or territory, and complete compensation terms before sharing the brief with creators. <br>
