## Description:

Chinese-language user research methods assistant for clarifying research questions, selecting methods, drafting interview, survey, usability-testing, qualitative-analysis, and report materials, with explicit ethics and honesty boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ria14-29](https://clawhub.ai/user/ria14-29)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, UX practitioners, and researchers use this skill to turn vague user-research needs into practical Chinese-language research plans, interview guides, questionnaire reviews, usability-test scripts, analysis guidance, and report outlines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could paste identifiable participant data while asking for research help.

Mitigation: Use de-identified excerpts where possible and follow consent, privacy, and institutional ethics-review requirements before sharing participant information.

Risk: Generated plans, questionnaire reviews, or analysis guidance could be mistaken for completed statistical analysis or formal ethics approval.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as method guidance; run analyses in appropriate statistical tools using real data and obtain required IRB or institutional review.

Risk: Users may request fabricated interviews, survey responses, or research results.

Mitigation: Maintain the artifact-backed refusal posture: do not generate fake data or fake findings, and suggest compliant alternatives using real small samples with limitations stated.
