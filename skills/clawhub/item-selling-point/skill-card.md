## Description:

Creates conversion-focused e-commerce product images with Chinese selling-point text and promotional layouts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and marketing designers use the skill to turn product photos and verified product and promotion points into listing images with Chinese text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and prompts may be sent to an external image-generation provider.

Mitigation: Choose a provider whose data handling you accept, and avoid submitting sensitive product or business images and prompts.

Risk: Generated marketing text or imagery could misstate product features or imply a promotion that is not real.

Mitigation: Check every product claim and promotion against verified source information, then inspect the generated image before publishing.
