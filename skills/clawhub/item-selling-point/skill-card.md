## Description:

Turns a product photo and selling points into a Chinese-language e-commerce main image with promotional copy and layout guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and designers use this skill to turn a product photo, verified product benefits, and real promotions into a readable marketplace main image.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos, prompts, and outputs are sent to the selected cloud image provider.

Mitigation: Review the provider and use only product assets and copy approved for external processing; avoid internal or private-network image URLs.

Risk: Generated imagery or promotional copy may be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Check product appearance, Chinese text, claims, and offer validity before publishing.

Risk: Generated marketing images are saved locally.

Mitigation: Choose an appropriate destination path and review saved files before distribution.
