# Workout planning rules

## Safety

- Warm up 5–10 minutes (light cardio + dynamic stretches) before every session; cool down 5 minutes after.
- Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain; do not train through injury. For joint/back/knee issues prefer low-impact options (swimming, cycling, machine-based or seated variations).
- Rest 48 hours between sessions hitting the same muscle group and take at least 1 full rest day per week.
- Progress gradually: increase weight or reps in small steps every 1–2 weeks only when form is consistent.

## Match to experience

| Level | Frequency | Prescription |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner (0–3 months) | Full body 3×/week (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri) | 2–3 sets × 8–12 reps, 60–90 s rest; bodyweight + light dumbbells |
| Intermediate (3–12 months) | Upper/lower 4×/week or push/pull/legs 3×/week | 3–4 sets × 8–12 reps, 60–90 s rest |
| Advanced (1+ years) | 5–6 days/week split | 4–5 sets, varied rep ranges, deload week every 6–8 weeks |

## Session template (beginner full body — gym or home)

1. Warm-up: 5 min brisk walk/bike + arm circles, leg swings
2. A: squats (or goblet squats) 3×10
3. B: push-ups (or bench press) 3×10
4. C: bent-over rows (or dumbbell rows) 3×10
5. D: plank 3×30 s
6. E: glute bridges 3×12
7. Cooldown: hold stretches 20–30 s each
8. Optional cardio: 20–40 min moderate after lifting or on off days

Weekly cardio target: about 150 min moderate or 75 min vigorous.

## Adaptations

- Equipment: gym (barbells, dumbbells, machines); home (bodyweight + resistance bands); none (bodyweight progressions — push-up from incline to knees to full; squat to split squat to lunge).
- Limited time: 20-minute express circuit — 3 rounds, 30 s work / 30 s rest.
- Older adults or beginners with low fitness: lower intensity, longer rests, include balance work; recommend professional screening before starting.
- On rest days: light walking, stretching, or mobility only.
