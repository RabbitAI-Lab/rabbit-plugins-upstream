## Description: <br>
Formats review deliverables with consistent structure for comparable findings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineers, and reviewers use this skill to turn final analysis into consistent reports, PR descriptions, release notes, incident reports, and action items that stakeholders can compare and act on. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate while formatting or reporting analysis results and affect the structure of final deliverables. <br>
Mitigation: Review the selected template, findings, action items, and appendix before sharing or committing the output. <br>
Risk: Formatted findings can be misleading if evidence references, severity labels, or recommendations are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Require evidence references for findings and verify severity, recommendations, and placeholder removal before publication. <br>
