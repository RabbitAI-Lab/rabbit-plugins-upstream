## Description: <br>
Formats review deliverables with consistent structure for comparable findings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and reviewers use this skill to turn analysis, review findings, release notes, incident reports, or other stakeholder deliverables into a consistent, actionable structure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can broadly inspect or update documentation and agent-instruction files when formatting review deliverables. <br>
Mitigation: Limit the requested scope or ask for report-only output when only copyediting, formatting, or narrow documentation review is intended. <br>
Risk: High-confidence documentation or governance edits may affect stakeholder-facing deliverables. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed changes and evidence references before accepting updates or publishing final reports. <br>
