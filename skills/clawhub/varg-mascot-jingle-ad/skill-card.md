## Description:

Turns a product photo into a 15–30-second singing mascot ad with a branded jingle, lyric captions, and a product end card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn a product photo and approved product facts into a short singing mascot ad for social channels, including a jingle, lyric captions, and a product end card.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos may be sent to external AI services for processing.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential images unless you understand and accept how the services may handle them.

Risk: The installer version is unpinned.

Mitigation: Review the installer source and pin a trusted version before running it.

Risk: Scanner coverage was partial because a local reference could not be resolved, so a benign verdict is not a guarantee of safety.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and inspect the skill before installation.
