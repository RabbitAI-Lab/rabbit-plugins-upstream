## Description:

Turns a product into a singing mascot ad with a 15–30 second brand jingle, lyric captions, and a product end card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketing teams use the skill to turn a product photo and seller-approved product facts into a short singing mascot ad for social video. It guides mascot creation, jingle writing, video generation, captions, and finishing edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and brand information may be sent to external generation services.

Mitigation: Use product images and brand information approved for external processing, and check the providers’ privacy terms.

Risk: Cloud generation can incur costs.

Mitigation: Check provider pricing and generation costs before creating assets.

Risk: The optional full plugin includes additional skills outside this release review, and the unversioned installer was not audited.

Mitigation: Prefer installing this individual skill from a reviewed revision.

Risk: Generated advertising lyrics or visuals could include unsupported product claims or omit required AI disclosure.

Mitigation: Use only seller-provided facts for claims and include the AI label before publishing.
