## Description:

Turns a product into a short singing mascot ad with a brand jingle, lyric captions, and a real product end card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketing creators use this skill to turn a product photo and seller-approved facts into a short singing mascot ad for social platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The installer is unpinned and may install a changing package version.

Mitigation: Check the package version and review the installer before running it.

Risk: Product images and brand information may be sent to external AI services.

Mitigation: Submit only assets you are authorized to share, and avoid confidential or unreleased material.
