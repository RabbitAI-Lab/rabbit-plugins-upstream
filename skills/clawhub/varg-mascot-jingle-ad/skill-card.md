## Description:

Turns a product photo into a short singing mascot ad with a custom jingle, animated product character, captions, and a product end card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and marketers use it to create short product ads for TikTok or Reels from product photos and seller-approved brand facts. It guides jingle writing, mascot image and video generation, audio finishing, captions, and a product end card.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos, brand details, prompts, and audio may be sent to external generation services.

Mitigation: Share only material you are authorized to send, and review the providers' data handling terms before use.

Risk: The installer version is not pinned.

Mitigation: Verify the intended skill release before installing it.
