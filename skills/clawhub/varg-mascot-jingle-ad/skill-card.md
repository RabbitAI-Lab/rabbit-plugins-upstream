## Description:

Guides an agent through creating a short singing mascot product ad with a branded jingle, animated product character, captions, and a real-product end card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketers use this skill to turn a product photo and approved product facts into a short singing mascot advertisement for social media. It guides image, jingle, and video generation, then finishing steps such as captions and a product end card.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product assets may contain confidential material or content the user is not authorized to share with Varg and its model providers.

Mitigation: Use only assets you are authorized to share and review provider privacy terms before submitting confidential material.

Risk: An API key used for generation could be exposed.

Mitigation: Protect the VARG_API_KEY.

Risk: Generation spend may exceed the intended budget.

Mitigation: Agree on a generation budget before creating assets.
