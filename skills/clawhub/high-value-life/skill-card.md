## Description:

A Chinese-language framework for comparing life decisions by costs, benefits, evidence quality, and applicability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiaxinmmhh](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiaxinmmhh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users and their assistants use this skill to weigh everyday choices by money, time, effort, expected benefits, and strength of evidence. It provides a structured answer rather than retrieving or verifying specific claims from the underlying guide.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical, legal, financial, insurance, or emergency guidance may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Use the framework to frame questions and tradeoffs; confirm consequential decisions against current authoritative sources and qualified professionals.

Risk: The framework does not verify specific figures or account for every person's circumstances.

Mitigation: Check figures against the original guide or primary sources, disclose uncertainty and relevant exceptions, and never invent percentages.
