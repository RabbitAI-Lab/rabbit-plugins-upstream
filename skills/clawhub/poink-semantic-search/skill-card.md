## Description: <br>
Build and query local document knowledge bases with semantic and hybrid search across PDF, Markdown, TXT, DOCX, ODT, and FODT files. Use when searching, ingesting, organizing, or inspecting documents with the poink CLI. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Private documents or provider-backed embedding and enrichment settings may be used unintentionally during local ingestion and search. <br>
Mitigation: Choose document paths and command-scoped config files deliberately, and review provider-backed settings before running poink. <br>
