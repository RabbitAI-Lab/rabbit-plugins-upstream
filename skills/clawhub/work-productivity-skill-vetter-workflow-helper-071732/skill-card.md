## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical Skill Vetter-style workflows for fixing bugs, hardening setup and safety, improving reliability, and creating adjacent skill workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn broad skill-vetting, bug-fix, safety-hardening, and workflow-improvement requests into concise plans, checklists, templates, analyses, code-change guidance, and validation notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate too easily on ordinary requests involving security, GitHub, or bug fixes. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or tighten trigger terms before relying on it in workflows where precise skill routing matters. <br>
