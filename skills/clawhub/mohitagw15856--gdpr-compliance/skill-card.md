## Description: <br>
Assess GDPR compliance and build the core records for a ROPA, lawful-basis mapping, DSAR workflow, DPIA trigger screen, and prioritised gap list. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Compliance, privacy, product, and engineering teams use this skill to turn processing details into auditable GDPR documentation. It helps draft a ROPA, assign lawful bases, outline DSAR handling, screen DPIA triggers, and prioritize compliance gaps for review by a DPO or counsel. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: GDPR outputs may be mistaken for final legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated assessments as compliance drafts and have a DPO or counsel confirm lawful basis, transfers, DPIA conclusions, and final obligations. <br>
Risk: Users may paste unnecessary raw personal data while requesting an assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Use processing summaries where possible and avoid submitting personal data that is not required for the assessment. <br>
