## Description: <br>
Jiebei is an educational consumer-loan guide with a local rate calculator for converting daily rates to annualized cost, comparing borrowing options, explaining credit-report effects, and supporting responsible borrowing decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to explain Jiebei borrowing costs, convert disclosed daily rates to annualized rates, compare financing options, and check debt-risk signals before borrowing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Financial guidance may be mistaken for personalized lending advice or current lender terms. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the content as general education, verify current rates and terms with the lender, and use only numbers intentionally provided by the user. <br>
Risk: Calculator output can be misleading if inputs, repayment assumptions, or loan terms are entered incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Review inputs before relying on results and compare calculations with official loan disclosures before making borrowing decisions. <br>
