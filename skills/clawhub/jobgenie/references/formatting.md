# Readable résumé and cover-letter formatting

Apply this guide in drafting, review and delivery, using the user's requested presentation. Deliver documents with visible paragraph and section separation. A packet must not look like a wall of text or have all its sections crunched together.

## Cover letter

- Distinguish the name/contact block, date, recipient, salutation, body paragraphs, closing and signature with intentional spacing.
- Leave a visible gap between body paragraphs: the bundled standard layout uses **10 pt after each paragraph** and about **1.1 line spacing**, with 11 pt body text. A comparable readable layout in another document tool is suitable.
- Keep contact lines together, with modest separation; use a larger gap before the date, recipient and salutation. Keep the closing and signature together.
- Count the actual contact paragraphs and include `contact_lines_count` in the document packet, including zero when there are none. The Claw supplies this; do not ask the user to configure it. Put each complete paragraph in one array entry, without empty entries or embedded line breaks. Paragraph formatting supplies the gaps.
- Use the standard spacing profile by default. The optional compact profile still leaves **6 pt between body paragraphs**. Use it only when appropriate to the requested presentation; do not collapse paragraph spacing to force a page count.

## Résumé

- Give section headings a visible break from the preceding content. The bundled layout uses **12 pt before section headings**, about **8 pt before role headings**, and **5–7 pt after ordinary paragraphs/bullets**, with 12 pt body text and about 1.1 line spacing.
- Make names, section headings, employers, roles and dates easy to distinguish. Keep each accomplishment under its correct employer/role. Unequal bullet counts and space are appropriate when relevance differs.
- Keep a heading with its opening content and keep an individual bullet together when practical. Allow longer job entries to continue naturally on the next page; do not bind every bullet in a role into one large block.
- Preserve relevant content and readable type when fixing pagination. Do not shrink fonts, eliminate whitespace or weaken accomplishments to squeeze the packet into an arbitrary page limit.

## Export and inspect

These numbers describe the bundled defaults, not universal requirements for every country, house style or user preference. Use another available document tool when the requested format needs it.

Preserve the final wording and working links through export. In an available rendered-page preview, check paragraph gaps, section separation, page breaks, alignment, clipping, awkward whitespace and the closing/signature. Compare the actual document text with the final draft. Fix mechanical defects within delivery; do not add another editorial round.

When no suitable page renderer is available, check the document structure and spacing and report that visual pagination remains unverified. Do not describe XML-only checks as a visual pass. For chat/plain-text delivery, use blank lines between the letter's paragraphs and between résumé sections rather than pasting one dense block.
