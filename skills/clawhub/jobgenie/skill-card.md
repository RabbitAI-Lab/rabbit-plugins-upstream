## Description:

Researches employers, matches applicants’ skills to their needs, and creates tailored résumés and cover letters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use JobGenie to research roles and employers, connect their experience to hiring needs, and prepare tailored résumés and cover letters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career histories, feedback, and application drafts may contain sensitive personal information processed by the user’s configured AI tools.

Mitigation: Keep records in a trusted project and share them with a separate reviewer only when that reviewer is trusted with the full material; single-agent review is available.

Risk: Generated application documents may need factual or formatting corrections before use.

Mitigation: Review the finished documents before submitting them, and verify the exported pages when a visual preview is available.
