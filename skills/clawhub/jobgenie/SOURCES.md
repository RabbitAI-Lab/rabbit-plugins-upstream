# JobGenie sources and research

JobGenie combines an employer-OSINT application workflow, guided career discovery and saved research on application writing and persuasion. This index shows what is bundled and where it enters the workflow. It is separate from the applicant's private career records described in [career sources and feedback](references/sources.md).

## How the research is implemented

The research is both translated into operative instructions and included as detailed local references. The [skill entrypoint](SKILL.md), [five-round method](references/five-round-workflow.md), [stage prompts](references/stage-prompts.md) and [writing methods](references/writing-methods.md) tell the Claw when to read and apply it. Isolated tasks must receive the actual relevant research text, rather than paths they cannot access.

| Work | Research and guidance used |
|---|---|
| Career discovery | [Questions, related-skill suggestions and matrix construction](references/skills-matrix.md), with [saved feedback](references/sources.md) |
| Job discovery | [Capabilities-led search and sourced shortlists](references/job-discovery.md), using the résumé, matrix and user preferences before choosing an application |
| Research and selection | Actual JD and current public employer sources, followed by the employer-problem → useful contribution → relevant support sequence |
| Drafting | [Persuasion synthesis](references/persuasion-cover-letter.md), [hiring-behavior research](references/hiring-behavior.md), [résumé-specific application](references/persuasion-resume.md), and [writing guidance](references/writing-methods.md) |
| Review | The employer research, full candidate reference and actual draft; the same writing guidance plus the [full Remove AI Slop guide](references/remove-ai-slop.md) |
| Correction and delivery | The consolidated review and current user feedback; [document/runtime guidance](references/runtime.md) and the [formatting guide](references/formatting.md) |

Company research is performed afresh when material facts need updating. The general research summaries are bundled for reuse, with their original dates and source links. No application requires re-reading every external paper online. The public port preserves the approved writing method; packaging and one-Claw instructions do not add another review or claim-verification stage.

## Job discovery and transferable skills

The [job-discovery instructions](references/job-discovery.md) use the person's actual capabilities and accepted preferences to search relevant work and adjacent roles. The employer's current posting remains the source for each opening; occupational suggestions do not replace reading it. Sources consulted on **20 October 2026**:

- [CareerOneStop Skills Matcher](https://www.careeronestop.org/Toolkit/Skills/skills-matcher.aspx) and [mySkills myFuture help](https://www.myskillsmyfuture.org/SkillsTransferability/GetStartedPageHelp.aspx): related skills-based career resources identified through public search. These are optional discovery aids, not required accounts, APIs or a limitation to one past title.
- [U.S. Department of Labor Employment Workshop, March 2026 participant guide](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/VETS/files/DOL_EW_ParticipantGuide_6.0_Update1-March2026.pdf): a related job-search resource identified through search. The full guide and CareerOneStop pages blocked automated access during this addition; their full contents are not treated as newly read evidence.
- [Skills or Degree? The Rise of Skill-Based Hiring for AI and Green Jobs, arXiv:2312.11942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11942): evidence about skills-based hiring in the studied sectors. JobGenie does not turn that sector-specific evidence into an automatic rule about every employer.

The user supplies their career facts. Discovery asks what work they could reasonably perform and how they could contribute; it does not introduce a claim-verification stage or mechanical percentage-match gate.

## Hiring, persuasion and human psychology

The bundled [hiring-behavior reference](references/hiring-behavior.md) records its check dated **8 October 2026** and includes nine primary sources. The [persuasion synthesis](references/persuasion-cover-letter.md) preserves further source dates, findings and practical applications. Representative sources already used in those references include:

- **Brown & Campion (1994):** [Recruiters' perceptions and use of biographical information in résumé screening](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.79.6.897). Make relevant capabilities intelligible within experience.
- **Bright & Hutton (2000):** [Competency statements and short-listing decisions](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-2389.00132). State meaningful capability explicitly rather than relying on a project or employer name to imply it.
- **Wiles, Munyikwa & Horton (2025):** [Algorithmic writing assistance on jobseekers' résumés increases hires](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04528). Clear expression can help employers recognize the applicant's contribution; this was not a test of JobGenie.
- **Waung and colleagues (2017):** [Impression management in résumés and cover letters](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10869-016-9470-9). Present genuine strengths affirmatively with relevant supporting work.
- **Kristof-Brown (2000):** [Person-job and person-organization fit](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1744-6570.2000.tb00217.x). Explain capability for the work and genuine organizational interest distinctly.
- **Woolley & Fishbach (2018):** [Expressing intrinsic motivation in job interviews](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2018.06.004). Make genuine professional interest visible.
- **Joyal-Desmarais and colleagues (2022):** [Meta-analysis of motivational message matching](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000377). Address the reader's consequential goals and needs.
- **Obermaier & Koch (2024):** [How weak arguments undermine strong arguments](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-73348-1). Remove irrelevant filler while retaining complementary strong reasons.
- **Packard & Berger (2021):** [How concrete language shapes customer satisfaction](https://doi.org/10.1093/jcr/ucaa038). Use specific actions and mechanisms to make the useful contribution understandable.
- **O'Keefe & Hoeken (2021):** [Variation in the effects of message-design choices](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.664160). Apply editorial judgment rather than promising a universally winning format.

These are concise applications of the saved research, not a new bibliography replacing it. The bundled references retain the fuller citations, study contexts and interpretation. Research about general persuasion informs editorial choices; JobGenie itself has not been shown to produce a particular interview or hiring rate.

The practical method remains positive advocacy: understand the employer's work, show what the person can contribute, and use their strongest closely relevant experience to support it. Research notes do not authorize hedging, minimization or a separate verification of the user's supplied career facts.

## Readability and Remove AI Slop

The full [Remove AI Slop guide](references/remove-ai-slop.md) is bundled, so users do not need to install that skill separately. Its saved research cutoff is **4 October 2026**. It covers canned phrasing, empty claims, defensive framing, repetitive constructions and natural readability while preserving useful substance and voice.

- [Remove AI Slop source project](https://github.com/RedRookAI/openclaw-skills/tree/main/skills/remove-ai-slop).
- [Upstream research notes](https://github.com/RedRookAI/openclaw-skills/blob/main/skills/remove-ai-slop/references/research.md).
- The original [MIT notice](LICENSE-REMOVE-AI-SLOP.txt) is retained.

The bundled guide is the version used by the approved method, not a claim that the linked project's current revision is byte-identical. Hemingway/readability checks are editorial instructions; they do not require the Hemingway app, an AI detector or a separate paid service.
