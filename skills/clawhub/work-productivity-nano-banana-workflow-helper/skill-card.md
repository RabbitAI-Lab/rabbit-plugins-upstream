## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill for unrelated Nano, image, Gemini, or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or narrower routing terms, and confirm the user's goal before producing workflow guidance. <br>
Risk: Workflow recommendations may be treated as implementation-ready even when based on incomplete user context. <br>
Mitigation: Surface assumptions, request material missing inputs, and validate the output against the stated success criteria before use. <br>
