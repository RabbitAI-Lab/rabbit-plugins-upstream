## Description: <br>
Builds a debt payoff plan across multiple debts using the avalanche or snowball method, including payoff order, estimated time, total interest, and an educational recommendation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to compare avalanche and snowball debt payoff plans across credit cards or loans, estimate payoff time and interest, and choose an educational repayment sequence based on cost savings or quick wins. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Debt payoff output could be mistaken for personalized financial advice or could rely on incomplete assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Frame the result as an educational planning model, collect only balances, APRs, minimum payments, and monthly budget, and state assumptions such as fixed APR and no new debt. <br>
Risk: Incorrect calculations or ignored minimum payments could mislead a user about payoff timing, interest cost, fees, or credit impact. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the math, quantify both avalanche and snowball methods, and explicitly remind users to keep paying every minimum. <br>
