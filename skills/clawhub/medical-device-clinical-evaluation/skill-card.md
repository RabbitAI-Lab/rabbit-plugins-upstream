This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs, registration, clinical evaluation, and product development teams use this skill to decide a medical-device clinical evaluation path and draft supporting CER, literature-search, equivalence, and clinical-data assessment materials for China, EU, and US submissions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory references and guidance may change after publication.

Mitigation: Verify current NMPA, CMDE, FDA, and EU sources before relying on outputs for submissions or business decisions.

Risk: Clinical evaluation decisions can depend on product-specific facts, classification, reviewer expectations, and local regulatory interpretation.

Mitigation: Use qualified regulatory affairs review or official consultation for case-specific decisions before submitting materials.
