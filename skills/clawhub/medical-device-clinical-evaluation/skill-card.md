## Description:

Medical device clinical evaluation support covering decision paths for exemption, equivalence, or clinical investigation, CER writing, literature searches, and clinical data assessment, with links to official NMPA, MDCG, and FDA references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs professionals and medical-device teams use this skill to choose a clinical-evaluation path and draft CER sections, literature-search plans, and clinical evidence assessments for submissions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Clinical and regulatory requirements may change, making guidance outdated.

Mitigation: Check current requirements and source documents on the relevant official NMPA, EU MDCG, or FDA site before relying on conclusions.

Risk: The bundled security claims are not independent certification, and the manifest does not verify the current SKILL.md.

Mitigation: Treat the audit as supporting information; request an updated manifest and verify release provenance when it matters.
