## Description:

医疗器械临床评价（Clinical Evaluation）专题技能，提供中、美、欧三条临床评价路径判定、临床评价报告（CER）撰写框架、文献检索与临床数据评估清单。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs, registration, R&D, and quality teams use this skill to plan medical-device clinical evaluation work for China, the United States, and the European Union. It helps produce path decisions, CER outlines, literature search plans, and clinical evidence assessment checklists while reminding users to verify current rules against official sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical-device regulatory requirements and guidance can change over time, so generated path decisions or CER structure may become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify key conclusions against current official NMPA, CMDE, FDA, and European Commission sources before relying on the output.

Risk: The skill is primarily Chinese-language and focused on medical-device clinical evaluation, which may not fit every market, device class, or internal workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the language, jurisdiction, device classification, and intended submission pathway before using the generated material.
