## Description: <br>
Make true scalable vector art such as logos, icons, app glyphs, and scalable illustrations, or convert raster images into clean editable SVG. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, and agents use this skill to generate editable SVG vector art from prompts or vectorize existing PNG/JPG artwork into scalable paths for logos, icons, glyphs, and illustrations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Uploaded source images or brand assets may be sensitive because the workflow relies on external vectorization and image providers. <br>
Mitigation: Review whether source images, logos, and brand assets can be shared with the selected provider before uploading them. <br>
Risk: Vector models may render logo text or taglines incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Use a typography-focused workflow for exact wordmarks and verify all text before delivery. <br>
Risk: Busy photos or noisy raster inputs can produce bloated or inaccurate SVG paths. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer clean, high-contrast, flat-color source images and inspect the SVG for editable paths, sane path count, and crisp scaling. <br>
