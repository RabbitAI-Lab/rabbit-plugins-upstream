## Description:

Plans a car-rental business by comparing local markets and vehicles, modeling per-car economics, evaluating booking channels, and outlining setup, marketing, and launch steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Prospective and existing car-rental operators use this skill to assess local opportunities, compare fleet and booking options, model costs, and plan a launch or expansion.
