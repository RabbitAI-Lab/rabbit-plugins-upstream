## Description:

Creates clear, fact-checked press releases and related media content for blockchain and Web3 announcements, avoiding unsupported claims and hype.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jaimeballdev](https://clawhub.ai/user/jaimeballdev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Blockchain and Web3 project teams use this skill to turn verified announcement details into clear press releases and related media content, then review claims and links before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may include information that is not approved for release or claims that lack support.

Mitigation: Confirm release approval, verify factual claims and quotes, and review the draft before publication.

Risk: The promoted distribution-service link may not fit a particular announcement.

Mitigation: Keep the link only when it serves the announcement; remove it when it is not relevant.
