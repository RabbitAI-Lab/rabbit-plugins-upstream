## Description:

Work out what renting an exotic or luxury car really costs: the published daily rate plus deposit, mileage overage, insurance, delivery, taxes and fees, for your dates and route, compared with the market range for that model in that city. Triggers on "how much to rent a Lamborghini for a day", "real cost of renting a Ferrari", "is $1,500 a day fair for a Cullinan". Not for finding listings (use exotic-rental-finder).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External renters use this skill to compare a luxury or exotic car's published rental rate with the market and understand likely rental charges, deposits, and terms to confirm before paying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Catalog tools or referenced supporting documents may be unavailable, limiting the completeness of a comparison.

Mitigation: Confirm the tools and supporting documents are available, and disclose any information that cannot be verified.

Risk: Unconfirmed rental terms can make the expected charges incomplete, especially for insurance, mileage overage, delivery, taxes, and fees.

Mitigation: Keep unknown amounts labeled as questions and confirm them with the rental company before paying.
