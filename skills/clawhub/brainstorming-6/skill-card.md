## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a design-first brainstorming workflow for turning feature ideas into approved specs before implementation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivansslo](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivansslo) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and product teams use this skill to have an agent clarify goals, compare approaches, and produce a reviewed design spec before coding begins. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow can interrupt simple coding requests by requiring design review before implementation. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when a design-first process is desired, and confirm the scope before applying it to small maintenance tasks. <br>
Risk: The skill may inspect project context and create committed spec files. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated specs and commits before relying on them or continuing to implementation. <br>
