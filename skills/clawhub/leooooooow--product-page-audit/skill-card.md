## Description:

Score product detail pages on image quality, copy effectiveness, SEO, mobile layout, and trust elements with improvement priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, marketplace sellers, and conversion specialists use this skill to audit product detail pages across images, copy, SEO, mobile experience, trust signals, and price presentation. The skill produces scored findings and a prioritized improvement roadmap for product pages on platforms such as Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Walmart, and other ecommerce channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may receive product page URLs, competitor links, analytics, or conversion data during an audit.

Mitigation: Provide only information that is appropriate to use for an audit and avoid sharing sensitive store, customer, or business data unless it is necessary.

Risk: Recommendations may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific ecommerce platform or product category.

Mitigation: Review the generated findings before making store changes, and validate recommendations against platform requirements and current business goals.
