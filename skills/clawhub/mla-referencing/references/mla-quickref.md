# MLA 9 quick reference

## Works Cited templates

| Type | Template |
|---|---|
| Book | `Surname, Name. Title of Book. Publisher, Year.` |
| Journal | `Surname, Name. "Title of Article." Journal Name, vol. x, no. x, Year, pp. xx-xx.` |
| Chapter | `Surname, Name. "Chapter Title." Book Title, edited by Editor Name, Publisher, Year, pp. xx-xx.` |
| Web page | `Surname, Name. "Title of Page." Site Name, Day Month Year, URL.` |
| Two authors | `Surname, Name, and Second Name.` |
| 3+ authors | `Surname, Name, et al.` |
| No author | `"Title of Page." Site Name, ...` (alphabetize by title, ignore A/An/The) |

## In-text

| Situation | Form |
|---|---|
| Parenthetical | (Smith 12) |
| Narrative | Smith (12) argues ... |
| Two authors | (Smith and Doe 12) |
| 3+ authors | (Smith et al. 12) |
| No page (web) | (Smith) |
| Same surname | (J. Smith 12) |
| Two works, same author | (Smith, "Title Word" 12) |

## Signature rules

- No comma between author and page: `(Smith 12)`. No years in in-text citations.
- Headline capitalization for all titles (major words capitalized), never sentence case.
- Quotation marks: article, chapter, web page titles. Italics: journals, books, sites (containers).
- URLs/DOIs without `https://`: `doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`.
- Dates: Day Month Year (12 March 2024). Access dates only when required and the source is undated.
- Works Cited alphabetical, hanging indent, title "Works Cited" centered.
- Every entry cited in text; every citation has an entry.
