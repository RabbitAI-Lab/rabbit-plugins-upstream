## Description:

Turn a feature request or change of direction into an implementation plan grounded in the current code, with observable acceptance criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[peterflow-en](https://clawhub.ai/user/peterflow-en)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to turn a requested change into an actionable implementation plan. It grounds acceptance criteria and verification steps in the project’s standards, current code, callers, and requirements, and records unresolved decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A plan may miss a requirement or propose work that does not match the codebase or project standards.

Mitigation: Check acceptance criteria against the cited requirements, standards, callers, and current code; state unresolved choices and distinguish planned checks from checks already run.
