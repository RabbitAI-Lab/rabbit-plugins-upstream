## Description:

Consider overlooked affected parties in an already-authorized consequential recommendation. Use when an absent party may bear a cost; distinguish known interests from guesses and allow a justified unchanged recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agbrander](https://clawhub.ai/user/agbrander)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and users preparing consequential recommendations use this skill to check whether an unrepresented affected party may bear costs, separate known interests from assumptions, and decide whether to ask a question, explain a trade-off, revise, or keep the recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the reflective framing as authority over people represented in the decision or over affected parties whose wishes are unknown.

Mitigation: Use it as optional decision support, separate known interests from assumptions, and allow a justified unchanged recommendation.

Risk: The skill is not a validated welfare intervention and may only improve the wording of a recommendation.

Mitigation: Do not claim measured welfare benefit; evaluate the decision or correction separately from any change in language.

Risk: The skill could be overextended beyond its clean, local text-only posture.

Mitigation: Do not grant tool calls, saved memory, enrollment, outbound messages, or governing-instruction changes solely from trying it.
