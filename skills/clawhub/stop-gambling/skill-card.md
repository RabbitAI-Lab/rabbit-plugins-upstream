## Description:

Help someone stop gambling. Free helplines checked by hand for their country, the official national self-exclusion scheme (GAMSTOP, OASIS, RGIAJ, ANJ, BetStop…), a check against ~294,000 gambling and betting domains, the PGSI self-check, and step-by-step plans to block gambling on iPhone, Android and at the bank. No key, no account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People affected by gambling and people supporting them use this skill to find helplines and self-exclusion options, check gambling sites, complete a PGSI screening, and get practical blocking steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requests share the selected country, domains, or optional screening answers with Silex; its retention practices and separately linked software were not independently audited.

Mitigation: Send only information needed for the requested help, keep names, contact details, and bank details out of requests, and review Silex's privacy information before sharing sensitive answers.

Risk: A user could mistake a PGSI screening result for a diagnosis.

Mitigation: Describe the score as a screening result only and make clear that the skill does not diagnose or treat a condition.
