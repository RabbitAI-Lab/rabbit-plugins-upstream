# Major Forklift Markets and Official Resources

This directory covers the United Kingdom, European Union, United States, Canada, Australia, and Japan. Use it alongside `brands.md`, `standards.md`, `standard-retrieval.md`, and `models-spec-retrieval.md`.

Prepared: 2026-11-14. These are reference starting points, not a live market report. Website availability, redirects, current model availability, standard editions, legal status, and product ownership have not been verified online during this update. Verify them with the relevant official source before making a procurement or compliance decision.

## 1. United Kingdom

Typical applications include distribution centres, retail logistics, manufacturing, food handling, construction, and long-load handling. Compare electric counterbalance, reach, pallet, VNA, articulated, and multidirectional trucks against the actual site requirements. Identify Great Britain versus Northern Ireland before discussing product conformity.

Training is task-, truck-, and site-specific. A training certificate alone does not replace employer authorization and assessment. Do not describe one universal interval as applying to every truck and attachment.

### Product and workplace compliance

- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC remains relevant during the transition to Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230. The Regulation generally applies from 20 January 2027; check article-specific dates, amendments, and transitional provisions in EUR-Lex.
- Review applicable machinery, EMC, electrical, noise, battery, and non-road engine emissions requirements for the exact product. ATEX suitability depends on the hazardous area and equipment category; an ordinary lithium truck is not an explosion-protected truck.
- CE marking is a manufacturer conformity declaration under applicable legislation, not a universal third-party certificate. Determine the conformity assessment route and required declaration, technical documentation, instructions, and markings.
- EN ISO 3691, EN 1175, and EN 12895 are useful standards families to investigate. Verify the exact edition, scope, and harmonised status rather than assuming every listed standard is mandatory.
- Workplace use, operator competence, inspections, and insurance are also subject to member-state law.

## 3. United States

Use US market definitions and units explicitly. Common truck categories include electric rider trucks, narrow-aisle trucks, motorized hand trucks, IC cushion-tire trucks, IC pneumatic-tire trucks, tractors, and rough-terrain trucks. Do not label this seven-class US industry scheme as the complete ISO 5053 classification.

OSHA operator training and evaluation are employer responsibilities. Under 1910.178, evaluate each operator's performance at least once every three years and provide refresher training when the regulation's triggers apply. A commercial training card alone is not employer certification. Inspect trucks before service and at least daily; trucks used around the clock require examination after each shift. Remove unsafe trucks from service. Check state-plan rules and the applicable industry standard, especially for construction and rough-terrain applications.

ANSI/ITSDF B56.1, UL 583, and UL 558 are starting points for relevant truck types; verify edition, legal incorporation, purchaser requirements, and hazardous-location classification. Do not claim that every US forklift requires one generic UL certificate.

## 6. Market Data and Comparison Rules

1. State geography, reporting period, source publication date, retrieval date, and unit.
2. Distinguish orders, shipments, deliveries, registrations, revenue, exports, and installed fleet. Do not mix them in a ranking.
3. Distinguish brand origin, parent group, manufacturing location, and destination market. A Chinese-made truck sold in the US belongs to the US destination-market total.
4. Avoid double-counting parent groups and their brands. Jungheinrich is separate from KION; Cascade is not a Jungheinrich brand. Verify current ownership from company disclosures.
5. Do not mix worldwide revenue rankings with national unit-sales rankings. If comparable public figures are unavailable, say so.
6. Separate manual pallet trucks, powered pallet trucks, counterbalance, warehouse trucks, AGVs/AMRs, telehandlers, and container handling where sources do.
7. Use GBP for UK, EUR for euro-area examples, USD for US, and local currency elsewhere. State exchange-rate date and taxes for any conversion; preserve original brochure units in specification extraction.
8. Check local dealer coverage, lead time, parts, warranty, charging infrastructure, emissions requirements, conformity documentation, and residual-capacity chart before recommending a model.
9. Industry statistics may be restricted or paid. Link the official access route; do not invent missing market shares.
