# Forklift Sales and Market Reports

Use this module for sales, shipments, orders, exports, market shares, rankings and electrification. Establish the destination market, reporting period, truck classes and metric before comparing figures. See [international-markets.md](international-markets.md) for UK, EU, US and other official sources.

## Research workflow
1. When research tools are available, collect current primary evidence. For news, normally review the last 30-90 days. For annual statistics, use the requested year. Prefer associations, customs and company filings; record publication and retrieval dates.
2. Normalize geography, units and definitions. Distinguish orders, shipments, revenue, fleet and registrations. Explain whether exports are included. Preserve original currencies and state any exchange-rate date.
3. Provide a source-backed table and suitable SVG following [svg-chart.md](svg-chart.md). Use ASCII on request or when SVG rendering is unavailable.

Do not fabricate missing figures or rankings. Use "Not disclosed" or "Not found in reviewed sources". If browsing is unavailable, disclose that current figures have not been verified.

## Comparison boundaries
Global revenue cannot establish domestic unit-sales rank. Avoid double counting groups and brands. Linde, STILL and Baoli belong to KION; Jungheinrich is separate. Cascade is an attachment supplier, not a Jungheinrich brand. Distinguish production origin from destination market. Market share needs a consistent numerator and denominator; explain incomplete coverage and revisions.

## Template S
- Title, geography, period, publication date and data cut-off.
- Overview table: indicator, value, unit, year-on-year change, definition and source.
- Ranking table only if comparable evidence supports it.
- Up to three charts with units, exact labels, dates and sources.
- Comparisons by product class, powertrain and destination where supported.
- Evidence-supported trends, limitations and source links.

Use horizontal bars for rank, grouped bars for comparisons, lines for time series and donuts only for complete compositions. Quantity bars start at zero. For ASCII, scale the maximum to at most 30 characters and retain the numeric table. Separate reported facts from interpretation; a product launch does not prove market leadership.
