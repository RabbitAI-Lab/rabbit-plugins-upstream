## 2. Electrification and Lithium Adoption

### 2.1 Penetration

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2030 forecast |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Electric forklifts / industrial trucks in China | ~64% | **72.4%** | ~75% | — |
| Lithium share of China's electric forklifts | 42.5% | 64% | ~70% | 80%+ |
| Lithium share of counterbalance forklifts in China | — | 19% | 41% | — |
| Global electric forklifts / industrial trucks | 68% | 70% | — | — |
| Global lithium forklifts / electric forklifts | 20% | — | — | **50%** |

### 2.2 Key Drivers of Lithium Adoption

1. **TCO advantage:** over a five-year service period, lithium models can cost 20%+ less to operate than diesel models.
2. **Policy:** `GB 38031-2025` mandates battery safety requirements; lithium systems are designed to comply.
3. **Environmental rules:** indoor, food, and pharmaceutical applications increasingly require electric equipment.
4. **Charging efficiency:** 1–2 hour fast charging supports continuous two-shift operation.
5. **Mature supply chain:** China's LFP supply chain is a global leader; China accounts for over 70% of global lithium-battery capacity.

### 2.3 Sodium-Ion Forklifts

- BYD plans mass production of sodium-ion forklifts in 2026.
- Claimed benefits: a wide operating range (-40 to 60°C), 15-minute fast charging, and approximately 10,000 cycles.
- Potential applications: extreme cold or heat and cost-sensitive operations.
- Limitation: energy density is about 70% of LFP.

- Lithium penetration among AGVs: over 50%.
- Intelligent logistics robotics CAGR: 35%+.

| Segment | Examples |

### 3.3 Typical Applications

- E-commerce warehouses with frequent, standardized flows
- Fixed-route line-side delivery in manufacturing
- Cold-chain work where automation reduces human exposure to temperature zones
- Hazardous-material warehouses where automation reduces risk

## 4. Export Landscape

### 4.1 China's Export Mix

- The export shares of Class I counterbalance trucks and Class II warehouse trucks are increasing.
- Class III walkie trucks account for the largest export share, but have lower unit prices.
- In 2023, Chinese exports of Class I/II lithium forklifts represented only 5.9% of overseas sales, indicating substantial room to grow.

### 4.2 Regional Market Characteristics

| Region | Customer preferences | Opportunity for Chinese brands |
|------|------|------|
| Europe | Low emissions, lithium power, comfort, low noise, lifecycle service | Expand lithium-forklift penetration |
| North America | Durability, leasing, service network | Strong potential for lithium counterbalance models |
| Southeast Asia | Value and localized service | Complete-truck exports and local assembly |
| Middle East | Heavy loads, heat tolerance, responsive service | Large IC and lithium models |
| Latin America | Price sensitivity | Value models |
| Africa | Economy models and light duty | Class III and entry-level Class I |

### 4.3 Overseas Manufacturing

- Hangcha: Thailand manufacturing base planned for 2026, with a Southeast Asia service centre.
- Anhui Heli: planned Thailand joint venture with 10,000 annual units and lithium battery-pack production.
- BYD: overseas distribution and assembly in multiple locations.
- KION Baoli: bases in Jingjiang and Jinan.

## 6. Key Policies and Standards, 2024–2026

### 6.1 China

- **GB/T 43756-2024:** forklift design requirements; effective 2024-10-01.
- **GB/T 44679-2024:** forklift prohibition and end-of-life; effective 2025-04-01.
- **GB/T 43657 series:** industrial-truck energy efficiency, equivalent to ISO 23308.
- **GB 38031-2025:** traction-battery safety; mandatory for new models from 2026-07-01.
- **GB/T 45565-2025:** lithium-battery coding; effective 2025-11-01.
- **GB/T 45915-2025:** transport of traction lithium batteries; effective 2026-02-01.
- **GB/T 46909-2025:** forklift attachment design.
- **GB/T 35205.1-2025 / GB/T 38055.1-2025:** rough-terrain and telescopic forklifts.
- **TSG 11-2020:** requirements for special motor vehicles used in plants and worksites, including revised inspection intervals.

### 6.2 International

- EU Battery Regulation (effective 2023): carbon footprint, recycling, and traceability.
- US Inflation Reduction Act: incentives for locally produced batteries.
- ISO 23308-1 second edition: 2025 FDIS entered approval stage.
- UN GHS: updated lithium-battery transport classification.

## 7. Subsidies, 2024–2026

- **Central:** expanded "Two New" policies for equipment renewal and trade-ins. Some provinces include industrial trucks in green-warehouse retrofits and integrated smart-logistics projects.
- **Local examples:** Zhejiang, Jiangsu, and Guangdong support new-energy logistics equipment; some rural/county programmes offer RMB 2,000–5,000 per electric-forklift replacement.
- **Direction:** subsidies are shifting from buying individual vehicles to system upgrades; per-vehicle support is weakening while integrated-project support grows.

## 8. Technology Trends

### Near term (1–2 years)

- Continued decline in lithium prices; LFP near RMB 0.5/Wh and approaching lead-acid costs.
- Standardized chargers compatible with `GB/T 27930`.
- Domestic BMS suppliers gaining share.
- Wider use of 80 V platforms, up from 48 V, to reduce current and increase power.

### Medium term (3–5 years)

- Sodium-ion forklift production expected to peak in 2026–2028.
- Hydrogen fuel-cell pilots for ports and heavy duty.
- Scale-up of autonomous forklifts in high-bay warehouses and e-commerce.
- Digital twins and remote maintenance.

### Long term (5–10 years)

- Industry-wide carbon-footprint management aligned with EU rules.
- AI dispatch and fleet coordination.
- Modular batteries and mature second-life use.
- Service models such as MaaS (Material as a Service).

## 9. Risks

1. **Trade disputes:** risk of anti-dumping or countervailing investigations into Chinese lithium forklifts in Europe and North America.
2. **Raw materials:** lithium and nickel price volatility affects battery costs.
3. **Foreign exchange:** export operations are exposed to RMB/USD/EUR swings.
4. **Technology substitution:** hydrogen and solid-state batteries may disrupt current technologies.
5. **Overcapacity:** some lower-end domestic models faced inventory pressure in 2024–2025.
6. **Certification:** carbon rules in export markets, especially the EU, may raise compliance costs.
