# Forklift Standards and Regulations

This is a reference index of GB, ISO, EN, and regional standards, not an authoritative statement of current legal status. English titles for Chinese standards below are working translations. Verify the identifier, official title, edition, scope, replacement relationship, and implementation date through `standard-retrieval.md` before citing a requirement. Inherited 2024-2026 publication claims require official confirmation.

## 9. China: Registration and Licensing

Forklifts within the special-equipment catalogue may require use registration, inspection and appropriately qualified operators. Verify the current TSG 11 edition and amendments with SAMR and the local authority. Do not apply an inherited four-year truck-inspection interval or the old N1/N2 descriptions without checking current rules. A certificate renewal period is not necessarily a truck inspection period.

## 10. Mandatory versus Recommended Standards

- GB generally identifies mandatory national standards within their stated scope.
- GB/T generally identifies recommended national standards. Contracts or other legal provisions can require their use, but the identifier alone does not establish universal legal obligation.
- JB/T and group standards have different status. Do not describe them as mandatory national standards.
- A foreign standard, test report or voluntary certification does not automatically satisfy destination-market law.
