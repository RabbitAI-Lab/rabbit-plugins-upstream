# Natural, Precise Technical Writing

Edit expression while preserving facts, uncertainty, negation, scope, time, attribution and the author's intended voice. Priority: safety, truthful source data, accurate charts, language preference, then style and optional imagery. Do not promise to evade AI detectors.

## Protected content
Preserve verbatim specifications, standard identifiers and status, source links and dates, repair-case identifiers and confidence, missing-field labels, code, frontmatter and table values. Do not invent experience, quotes, measurements, outcomes or citations to make prose sound human.

## Pattern checklist
These are editing signals, not a blacklist. Keep deliberate author choices and necessary technical terms.

| Group | Patterns to review |
|---|---|
| Argument | False contrasts; dramatic fragments; pseudo-profundity; throat-clearing; imaginary opponents |
| Rhythm | Forced triads; repeated sentence openings; excessive dashes; stacked hedges; invented compounds; unnecessary passive voice |
| Wording | Buzzwords; inflated significance; vague relationships; praise tails; promotional language; vague authorities; avoiding simple verbs |
| Formatting | Decorative bold; ornate headings; distracting punctuation |
| Chat residue | Service-chat filler; irrelevant training-data disclaimers or speculation; repeated title; draft-history chatter |
| Density | Stacked modifiers; nominalized verb phrases; passive chains; synonym piles; generic era-setting openings; canned conclusions |

## Editing workflow
1. Read the whole passage and lock its factual claims and uncertainty.
2. Identify clusters of patterns that impair clarity rather than replacing isolated words mechanically.
3. Ask who acts, what they do, who receives the action, what changes, and what evidence supports it.
4. Rewrite using concrete subjects, direct verbs and connected paragraphs. Keep technical detail needed by the intended reader.
5. Audit every new detail against the original evidence. Restore qualifications and remove unsupported additions.
6. Compare the result with protected content and verify that meaning, quantities and source links survived.

For pasted text, return the edited text in the requested format. For a file, edit only the authorized content and report material changes. As an embedded final pass, improve the answer without exposing internal editing commentary.

## Optional illustrations
Use images only when they help explain the subject and suitable tools are available. They must depict mechanically plausible trucks and safe operating conditions. Illustrations cannot replace factual charts or serve as evidence of a real product or event. Label illustrative images. If generation fails, retry at most once, then give an honest fallback. For standalone HTML, embed appropriately sized data images or package local assets; if embedding exceeds about 8 MB, prefer a packaged alternative and explain it. Never claim an image was generated or saved unless it exists.
