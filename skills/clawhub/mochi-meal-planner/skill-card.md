## Description:

Save dietary exclusions and pantry stock, scale servings and export menus and groceries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hznetnewpower](https://clawhub.ai/user/hznetnewpower)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Home cooks and households use this skill to save dietary exclusions and pantry stock, plan or scale meals, and create a grocery list for shopping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dietary preferences and exported plans remain in the active workspace and may reveal personal information.

Mitigation: Keep workspace access private and review saved preferences and exports.

Risk: Ingredient exclusions or quantities may be incomplete or inaccurate, and unknown prices do not establish a verified budget total.

Mitigation: Review exclusions and ingredient quantities before relying on a plan, and verify prices separately.
