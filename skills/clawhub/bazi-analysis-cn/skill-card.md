## Description:

Generates Chinese Bazi birth charts and traditional interpretations from solar or lunar birth dates, with a three-pillar option when birth time is unknown.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fbbyqsyea](https://clawhub.ai/user/fbbyqsyea)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People seeking a traditional Chinese Bazi reading provide a birth date, gender and, if known, birth time to receive a chart and interpretations of elemental balance, life cycles and topics such as relationships or career.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Birth date, optional birth time and gender are sensitive personal details.

Mitigation: Share only details you are comfortable providing for a local analysis.

Risk: A traditional-culture reading could be mistaken for reliable decision advice.

Mitigation: Treat interpretations as entertainment and base important decisions on real-world information.

Risk: The skill requires installation of a Python environment and lunar_python.

Mitigation: Review and approve local dependency installation before running the skill.
