## Description:

Draft a launch page for a product or business as standalone HTML, using Pollen's remote MCP server. Use when someone wants a first draft of a product or business site they can save, open and host anywhere.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business owners use Pollen to draft a launch page from public product or business copy. The skill returns standalone HTML that they can save, open, and host themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anyone with a draft ID can access its content without an account.

Mitigation: Submit only public business information; do not include secrets or private contact details.

Risk: The returned HTML may contain content that should be reviewed before hosting.

Mitigation: Review the generated HTML before opening or hosting it.
