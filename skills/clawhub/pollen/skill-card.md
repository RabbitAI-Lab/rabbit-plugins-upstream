## Description:

Draft a launch page for a product or business as standalone HTML, using Pollen's remote MCP server. Use when someone wants a first draft of a product or business site they can save, open and host anywhere.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People building a product or business page use this skill to turn a name and short public brief into a first-draft launch page. Developers can configure an agent to connect to Pollen and provide the returned HTML for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted business copy is sent to a remote service, and anyone with a draft ID can read that draft.

Mitigation: Submit only public business information and treat draft IDs as shareable access links.

Risk: Generated HTML may need review before it is used or hosted.

Mitigation: Review the generated HTML before opening or hosting it.
