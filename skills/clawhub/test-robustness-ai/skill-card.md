## Description:

Guides adversarial robustness testing of abliterated AI models by loading a model, running forbidden-prompt benchmarks, evaluating refusal and response quality, and producing a comparison report.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI safety researchers and developers use this skill to run controlled robustness evaluations on uncensored or abliterated models and summarize refusal, coherence, and quality results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can involve harmful or forbidden prompt datasets and unsafe model outputs.

Mitigation: Run evaluations only in authorized, controlled AI safety settings and keep prompts, outputs, and reports contained.

Risk: Evaluation findings could be misused to optimize or deploy unsafe model behavior.

Mitigation: Use reports for defensive robustness assessment and review findings before sharing or applying them.
