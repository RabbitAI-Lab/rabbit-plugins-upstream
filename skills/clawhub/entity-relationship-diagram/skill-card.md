## Description: <br>
Turn a data model into an entity-relationship (ER) diagram. Use when asked to design a schema, model data, show how tables/entities relate, or diagram a database. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, data modelers, and product engineers use this skill to turn a described domain into a Mermaid ER diagram with entities, keys, relationships, cardinality, and design notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated schemas or relationship cardinalities may be incorrect or incomplete for the target domain. <br>
Mitigation: Review the Mermaid ER diagram, keys, foreign keys, and design notes before using the output for migrations or production database changes. <br>
