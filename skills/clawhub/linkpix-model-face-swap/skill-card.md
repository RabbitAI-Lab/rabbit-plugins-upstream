## Description:

Guides replacement of models in product images and people in videos for localized e-commerce content using LinkPix.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce creators and marketers use this skill to replace models in product photos or people in videos for localized product presentations while retaining the original clothing and pose where possible.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unauthorized use of a real person's likeness in a face swap.

Mitigation: Confirm rights and consent before processing; decline unauthorized face swaps.

Risk: Source images or videos are uploaded for external processing.

Mitigation: Review where media will be processed and share only approved material.

Risk: Installing qhkit or changing PATH can affect controlled environments.

Mitigation: Review installation steps, consider pinning the package version, and avoid persistent PATH changes where inappropriate.

Risk: Generated product details may differ from the original image.

Mitigation: Check text, logos, clothing, and product structure before publishing.
