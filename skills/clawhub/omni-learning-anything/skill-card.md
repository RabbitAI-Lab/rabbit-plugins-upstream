## Description:

Build or continue a personal, source-grounded course from a learning goal, a named course from a university or AI organization, an open reference, a Chinese K–12 textbook, or the learner's materials, with illustrated lessons and optional light checks, feedback and review. Use for systematic study and learning plans, not a one-off factual answer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shawnren57](https://clawhub.ai/user/shawnren57)

MIT

## Use Case:

Learners use this skill to turn a topic, named course, textbook, or their own materials into a source-grounded study plan with illustrated lessons, brief checks, and guided review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cloud delivery sends course text, images, and agreed source excerpts to the selected provider.

Mitigation: Review the provider's account grants and confirm the destination before publishing.

Risk: Course archives can contain private learning information.

Mitigation: Keep private course archives outside the skill installation.

Risk: Scheduled access and live integrations may vary by host and were not tested for this release.

Mitigation: Verify scheduled access on the actual host and use patched dependencies and reviewed installer versions.
