## Description:

Writes personalized cold emails that actually get replies

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and commercial teams use this skill to draft concise cold outreach and follow-up sequences based on recipient details, the offer, the prospect's problem, personalization facts, and a single call to action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach may include inaccurate personalization, privacy-sensitive details, or non-compliant claims if the supplied prospect or business information is wrong or excessive.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary recipient and offer details, and review every draft for accuracy, privacy, and applicable outreach compliance before sending.
