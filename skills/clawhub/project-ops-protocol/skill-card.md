## Description:

Project Ops Protocol is a lightweight operating protocol for serious multi-session projects that coordinates AI agents and humans through battle maps, ledgers, alignment boards, relay notes, and quality gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, project leads, and agent operators use this skill to organize projects that span multiple sessions, agents, days, or contributors. It helps establish reusable planning, execution, synchronization, memory, wrap-up, and publication-quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may cause the skill to activate during general alignment or wrap-up conversations.

Mitigation: Narrow the trigger wording or enable the skill only for explicit project-operations work when deployments need opt-in behavior.

Risk: The protocol may add unnecessary structure to one-off questions or single-tool tasks.

Mitigation: Use it for projects that span multiple sessions, agents, days, or contributors, and skip it for small one-off work.
