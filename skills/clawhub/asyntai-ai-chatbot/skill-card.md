## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scanner flagged CSP advice about allowing inline scripts without explaining that this weakens protection.

Mitigation: Review the site's CSP before installation. Prefer the documented external-script option where suitable, and allow inline scripts only if the site owner understands and accepts the reduced protection.

Risk: Enabling a third-party chat script or uploading site content may share information with Asyntai and affect site visitors.

Mitigation: Review what content will be added to the dashboard and what information the widget may expose before enabling it for visitors.
