## Description:

Helps an agent support self-reflection conversations by encouraging self-understanding, self-acceptance, careful distinctions between facts and interpretations, and clear boundaries around diagnosis or crisis support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c-jeril](https://clawhub.ai/user/c-jeril)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill when discussing self-relationship, self-acceptance, self-understanding, personal growth, or difficulty interpreting emotions and choices. It guides the agent to understand and clarify before offering perspective or possible next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake reflective guidance for clinical diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.

Mitigation: Use the skill as educational support only and direct serious distress, crisis, self-harm, imminent danger, diagnosis, or treatment needs to qualified professional or crisis resources.
