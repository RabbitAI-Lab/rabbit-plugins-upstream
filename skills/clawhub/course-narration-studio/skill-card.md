## Description:

Turn lesson materials into polished course narration audio. This course narration studio organizes lessons into speakable scripts, records the teacher-voice narration for each section, and delivers slide-ready audio for courses, training, and knowledge-product audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators, educators, and training teams use this skill to turn finished lecture materials into a consistent, ordered narration pack. It prepares speakable lesson scripts, supports a reviewed pilot, and delivers narration audio by lesson and section.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The account authorization may grant broader access, including credit spending on image, video, and music tools beyond narration.

Mitigation: Prefer narration-limited access and review the requested authorization scope before connecting.

Risk: Uninstalling this skill may disconnect other Beatra skills that share the same connection.

Mitigation: Check the current shared-connection uninstall guidance and confirm the documented bug is fixed before uninstalling.

Risk: Course materials or voice recordings may contain content the user is not authorized to share.

Mitigation: Submit only materials the user is authorized to share, and confirm speaker consent before uploading a voice sample for cloning.
