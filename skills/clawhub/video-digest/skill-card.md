## Description:

Turns a YouTube video or supplied transcript into timestamped Quick, Deep, or Research notes, with selected material claims checked against public primary sources in Research mode.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

## Use Case:

External users can turn YouTube videos or supplied transcripts into timestamped summaries, structured argument notes, or selected claim checks. They can also retrieve relevant passages from a previously fetched transcript.
