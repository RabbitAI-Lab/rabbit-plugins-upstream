## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill describes scheduled collection and reuse of a logged-in browser session for Xiaohongshu monitoring. <br>
Mitigation: Use a dedicated browser profile, keep the remote debugging port local, review scheduled jobs before enabling them, and periodically verify account login state and platform compliance. <br>
Risk: Automated collection and notifications may capture or redistribute platform content, engagement metrics, or account activity in ways that exceed user expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Limit monitoring to intended operational accounts, minimize retained data, and confirm collection, alerting, and reuse comply with account, platform, and privacy requirements. <br>
