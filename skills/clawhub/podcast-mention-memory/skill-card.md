## Description:

Indexes podcast and radio audio so agents can find and report mentions of a brand, product, or person with speaker, episode, timestamp, and sentiment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

PR agencies, brand-monitoring teams, and founder-led companies use the skill to index audio mentions, answer questions about who said what, and prepare cited client reports with analyst-reviewed sentiment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Podcast audio URLs, transcripts, client names, sentiment judgments, and analyst notes are sent to BlueColumn.

Mitigation: Confirm the transfers comply with privacy and client-data policies; review retention and deletion controls before sending confidential or regulated material.

Risk: Speaker attribution and sentiment judgments may be inaccurate in client reports.

Mitigation: Check cited audio and timestamps, and record analyst corrections before reporting findings.
