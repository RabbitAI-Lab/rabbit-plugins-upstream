## Description: <br>
Rong Bing is a Chinese-language customer-service roleplay skill for simulating de-escalation of angry or distressed customers through emotion labeling, reflective listening, and choice-based resolution framing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and customer-support teams can use this skill to roleplay escalated customer-service conversations, practice de-escalation language, and compare emotional support strategies in single- or multi-role simulations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation terms may cause the persona to enter a customer-service de-escalation mode when the user has not clearly requested it. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow activation terms and require explicit user opt-in before applying the persona or analyzing a user's emotional state. <br>
Risk: Emotion-labeling and calming language can influence upset or vulnerable users, especially when paired with sales or probing scenarios. <br>
Mitigation: Keep responses supportive, avoid bypassing resistance, and remove language that prepares users for probing by another role. <br>
