## Description:

Helps biology teachers draft inquiry- and experiment-centered lesson plans, unit sequences, classroom activities, and questioning chains for middle and high school.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology teachers use this skill to prepare middle- and high-school lessons, unit plans, inquiry activities, laboratory safety considerations, and review or exam-feedback lessons. Teachers review drafts before using them with students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated lessons or questions may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review lesson plans and AI-generated questions before classroom or repository use.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, privacy defaults, and crisis-support channels may not fit locations outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before using the skill outside its intended teaching context.

Risk: Classroom inputs could expose a student's personal or health information.

Mitigation: Use class-level information, pseudonyms, or seat numbers rather than identifiable student details.
