## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson plans or classroom records could expose student identities or sensitive health and family information.

Mitigation: Use class labels or pseudonyms instead of real names; do not paste sensitive details, and confirm class-workspace permissions require teacher approval before storing plans.

Risk: An unsuitable experiment proposal could put students at risk.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review each experiment's safety level and supervision requirements before use; do not assign blood collection or microorganism cultivation at home.
