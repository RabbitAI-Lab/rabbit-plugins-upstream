## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school biology teachers draft standards-aligned lessons built around observation, experiments, concept development, and evidence-based questioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology teachers use this skill to draft junior- and senior-secondary lesson plans, lab activities, unit outlines, review lessons, and exam-feedback lessons for teacher review before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated experiment plans may misclassify laboratory hazards or suggest unsuitable student activities.

Mitigation: Teachers review safety classifications and procedures against local school rules before any experiment; restrict higher-risk operations to trained staff.

Risk: Classroom records and exam materials may expose student data or confidential test questions.

Mitigation: Remove identifiable student details, review data before storing it, and do not enter unreleased exam questions or materials from unfinished exam sessions.

Risk: Draft lessons or exam commentary may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular class or curriculum.

Mitigation: Teachers verify biology content, assessment guidance, and local curriculum fit before classroom use.
