## Description:

为初高中生物教师设计以观察和实验为主线的教案草稿，包括单元统筹、课堂活动、实验安全分级和概念教学提问链。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中生物教师用它规划新授课、复习课和考后讲评课，设计探究活动、实验安排与分层提问；教案须经教师审定后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, privacy requirements, and crisis referrals before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unreviewed lesson plans or lab activities could contain unsafe or inaccurate guidance.

Mitigation: Teachers review all generated teaching materials and apply the documented lab-safety levels before classroom use.

Risk: Sensitive class records, identifiable student health information, or unreleased exam content could be exposed.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam material or identifiable health data; anonymize records and use pause, no-share, or deletion controls when needed.

Risk: Mainland-China curriculum and crisis guidance may not fit other locations.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy rules, and emergency referral contacts before use outside mainland China.
