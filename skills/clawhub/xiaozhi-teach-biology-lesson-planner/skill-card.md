## Description:

Helps Chinese-language middle and high school biology teachers draft competency-focused lesson plans with experiments, unit sequencing, and evidence-based question chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology teachers in Mainland China use this skill to draft junior and senior secondary lesson plans, including inquiry activities, experiment safety levels, differentiated lesson segments, and concept-building questions. Teachers review and confirm drafts before classroom use or saving them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis referral contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe experiments could be assigned to student groups or for home use.

Mitigation: Classify each experiment by safety level; keep high-risk work teacher-led or use video, and supervise medium-risk work in the school lab.

Risk: Lesson plans or classroom notes could expose students' sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use class labels, pseudonyms, or seat numbers instead of names, contact details, family circumstances, or health information.

Risk: Curriculum, consent, and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum requirements, consent rules, and referral contacts before deploying in another region.
