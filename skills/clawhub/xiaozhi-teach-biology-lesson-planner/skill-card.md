## Use Case:

生物教师可用此技能规划初中和高中生物课、实验探究、单元衔接以及复习或考后讲评课。生成内容是供教师审阅的草稿，不替代教师备课或课堂判断。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom records or generated examples could expose identifying or health information about students.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, omit identifying and health information, and confirm that the platform's memory, sharing, and save controls are honored.

Risk: Generated lesson content or laboratory activities may need correction before classroom use.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review lesson content and verify each activity against the applicable curriculum and laboratory safety guidance before use.

Risk: Exam content may remain confidential while any session or make-up sitting is still in progress.

Mitigation: Do not provide or discuss exam questions until all sittings and participating schools have completed the exam; use only permitted post-exam materials.
