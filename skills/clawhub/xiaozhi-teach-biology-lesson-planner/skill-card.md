## Description:

Drafts inquiry-centered biology lesson plans for Chinese middle- and high-school teachers, including unit sequencing, student experiments, lab-safety levels, and evidence-based question chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology teachers use this skill to draft grade-appropriate lessons, practical activities, and concept-building questions for Chinese middle- and high-school classes. Teachers review all plans and confirm any classroom-record updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum assumptions and crisis-contact guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and crisis-contact guidance may not fit locations outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum requirements and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: Proposed lab activities may be unsafe if conducted without the proper setting or supervision.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check each activity's safety level and supervise school laboratory work.

Risk: Classroom data or unreviewed lesson plans could be saved inaccurately.

Mitigation: Avoid identifying student details and confirm lesson-plan writeback with the teacher before saving.
