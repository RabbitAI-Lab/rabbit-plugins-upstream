## Description:

Helps Chinese secondary-school biology teachers draft competency-based lessons, inquiry activities, experiment plans, and evidence-led question sequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school biology teachers in mainland China use the skill to draft curriculum-aligned lesson plans, safe student investigations, and differentiated questioning. Teachers review drafts before classroom use or saving.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class profiles and student weakness data may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use class-level information and pseudonymous student tiers; exclude names, contact details, family circumstances, and health information, and require teacher review before saving or reuse.

Risk: Unsafe or inaccurate biology experiment plans could harm students.

Mitigation: Have the teacher verify experiment safety ratings and procedures; do not assign high-risk group or at-home activities such as blood collection or microbial cultivation.

Risk: Curriculum or crisis guidance may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and emergency referrals before use elsewhere, and have teachers review all student-facing material.
