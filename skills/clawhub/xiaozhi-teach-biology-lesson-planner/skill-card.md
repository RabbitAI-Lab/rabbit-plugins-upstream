## Description:

Helps biology teachers plan competency-focused lessons built around observation and experiments, including unit sequencing, inquiry activities, lab safety, and concept question chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Biology teachers use this skill to draft junior and senior high school lesson plans, inquiry and student laboratory activities, and concept question sequences aligned with curriculum goals. It also supports single review and post-exam discussion lessons once an examination is complete.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (content is aligned with Chinese junior and senior high school biology curricula).

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning inputs or drafts could expose student or confidential examination information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers and exclude student health details and confidential exam materials.

Risk: Generated lesson content or experiment plans may be inaccurate or unsuitable for classroom conditions.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review lesson drafts and experiment plans before use, and follow the skill's laboratory safety guidance.

Risk: A student safety concern may arise during a planning conversation.

Mitigation: Stop lesson-planning steps and follow the included crisis referral protocol.

Risk: Host-platform storage and separately installed companion skills are outside this package review.

Mitigation: Review the platform's storage and sharing controls and assess companion skills separately.
