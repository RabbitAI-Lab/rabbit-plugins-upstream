## Description:

Helps middle- and high-school biology teachers draft evidence-led lesson plans, inquiry activities, lab safety classifications, and concept-building question chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External biology teachers in Mainland China use this skill to draft junior- and senior-secondary lesson plans, student experiments, and scaffolded questions aligned with classroom needs. Teachers review plans and student-facing materials before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adaptation of curricula and crisis-help guidance is needed elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom records may expose student identifiers.

Mitigation: Use aliases or seat numbers instead of real names, contact details, or health information.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's location and substitute local emergency and youth-support channels before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Unsafe or unsuitable student experiments may be proposed.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review experiment safety levels and supervise laboratory work; do not assign home microbial cultures or blood collection.
