## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school biology teachers draft inquiry-based lesson plans, classroom experiments, and concept-building question sequences aligned with curriculum competencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology teachers use this skill to plan middle- and high-school lessons around observation, evidence, experiments, and differentiated questions. Teachers review drafts and safety requirements before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); localize curriculum, language, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated experiments may be unsafe or unsuitable for a classroom.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review procedures and apply the laboratory safety levels before use; do not assign home microbial cultures or blood sampling.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and emergency contacts may be wrong outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, language, and crisis referral contacts before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Lesson plans or classroom notes could expose student personal information.

Mitigation: Use class-level details or pseudonyms, omit names and health information, and require teacher confirmation before saving records.
