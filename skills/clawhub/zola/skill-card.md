## Use Case:

People planning a wedding use this skill to check and update their Zola guests, events, vendors, budget, registry, website, and invitation projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation may expose guest contact details, RSVPs, registry information, or invitation projects outside the intended request.

Mitigation: Use only for explicit Zola account tasks and limit access and disclosure to the details needed.

Risk: Account-changing actions can delete data, send bulk invitations, alter the registry, or publish website changes.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation before deletes, bulk invitations, registry changes, or public website changes.
