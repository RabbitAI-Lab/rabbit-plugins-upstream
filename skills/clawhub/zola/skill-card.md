## Description:

Helps an agent review and manage Zola wedding planning data, including vendors, budgets, guests, events, seating, RSVPs, and registry activity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People planning a wedding use this skill to ask an agent about their Zola vendors, budget, guests, RSVPs, seating, inquiries, and gifts, or to request updates to wedding plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Access can expose private wedding and guest information.

Mitigation: Connect only when needed and avoid sharing private wedding details beyond the intended audience.

Risk: Account-changing actions can alter vendors, guest addresses, events, seating, and invitations.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation before additions, removals, address changes, event changes, seating assignments, vendor changes, and invitation updates.
