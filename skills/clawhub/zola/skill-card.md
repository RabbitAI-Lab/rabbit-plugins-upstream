## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can change or delete sensitive wedding planning data without clear confirmation safeguards.

Mitigation: Use it only for explicit Zola account tasks, preview affected records, and require user confirmation before changing or deleting vendors, guests, addresses, budget items, seating assignments, events, invitations, registry, or gift-tracker data.
