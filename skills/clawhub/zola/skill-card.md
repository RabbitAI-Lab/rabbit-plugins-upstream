## Description:

Helps agents review and manage Zola wedding planning data, including vendors, budgets, guests, RSVPs, seating, and registry gifts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People planning a wedding use this skill to ask an agent about their Zola vendors, budget, guest list, events, seating, inquiries, and gifts, or to request updates to that data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guest addresses, RSVPs, invitations, budgets, vendors, registry, and gifts contain sensitive wedding information.

Mitigation: Use only with an intended Zola account and limit disclosure of wedding details to authorized recipients.

Risk: The skill can guide live changes to guests, invitations, seating, vendors, events, and budgets without stated confirmation safeguards.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before adding, updating, removing, bulk inviting, assigning seats, or changing budgets.
