## Description:

Guides consent-based preparation and neutral interpretation of repeatable photo-based facial measurements for adults.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gpt-img-2](https://clawhub.ai/user/gpt-img-2)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adults and agents assisting them use this skill to plan consistent face photographs, contextualize user-supplied measurements, compare comparable sessions, and prepare neutral reports without treating scores as judgments of personal worth.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional MCP installs and runs unpinned GitHub-hosted code through npx.

Mitigation: Review and trust the source before installing; prefer a pinned release or commit.

Risk: Face measurements can be misused for nonconsensual or sensitive personal judgments.

Mitigation: Require consent and adult subjects; do not identify people, infer sensitive traits, diagnose conditions, or equate scores with personal worth.
