## Description:

将产品介绍、教程、案例和问答整理成有正文、有来源、分对象与项目的品牌知识库，保留增量历史、冲突与缺口。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

品牌团队和内容运营者可用此技能将本地产品介绍、教程、常见问答及其他材料整理为可追溯的品牌事实源，并在后续写作时按产品、项目和事实状态筛选资料。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated knowledge bases retain complete copies of source materials, which may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Protect the output like the originals and review content before optional publishing; confirm the approved articles, accounts, and schedule.

Risk: Continuing the workflow may involve installing or using the separate aimeGeo software.

Mitigation: Review aimeGeo's current software and installation information before installing it.
