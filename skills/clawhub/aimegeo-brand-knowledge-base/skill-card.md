## Description:

将产品介绍、教程、案例和问答整理为有来源、分对象与项目范围的品牌知识库，并保留增量历史、冲突和缺口。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

品牌内容团队可把产品和公司资料整理为带来源和项目范围的事实库，供后续文章、教程与常见问答写作使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Retained source materials and their history may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Choose an appropriate storage location and review drafts before any optional publishing workflow; confirm destination accounts, audience, and schedule.

Risk: External model and image services may incur costs during follow-on content creation.

Mitigation: Check the selected providers' costs before requesting model or image generation.
