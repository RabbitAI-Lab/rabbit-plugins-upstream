## Description: <br>
Helps plan, map, or restructure a website's page hierarchy, navigation, URL structure, and internal linking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, marketers, designers, and site owners use this skill to plan new website structures or reorganize existing sites into clear page hierarchies, navigation systems, URL maps, visual sitemaps, and internal-linking plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context that contains sensitive business strategy. <br>
Mitigation: Review and limit local product-marketing files before use, and avoid sharing generated architecture plans outside approved audiences. <br>
Risk: Site architecture recommendations can affect SEO, navigation, and redirect decisions if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed page hierarchy, URL changes, navigation updates, and redirects before implementation. <br>
