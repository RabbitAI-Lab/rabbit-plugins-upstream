## Description: <br>
Essential travel guide for visiting China, covering visas, mobile payments, transport, food, safety, hotels, flights, attractions, and itinerary planning for inbound tourists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to get Russian-language China travel guidance and to search hotels, flights, attractions, and itinerary suggestions through the publisher proxy. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel questions and itinerary details are sent to the skill publisher's proxy and TripGenie over HTTPS. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive personal information, passport details, account credentials, or private booking references. <br>
