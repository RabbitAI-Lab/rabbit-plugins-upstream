## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users and skill authors create, troubleshoot, harden, and verify proactive-agent-style productivity workflows with practical checklists, templates, implementation plans, and local-hardware-friendly guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad triggers and implicit invocation can cause the skill to shape unrelated productivity, bug-fix, or implementation requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installing when automatic skill selection is enabled, and narrow triggers or invoke the skill explicitly for proactive-agent workflow work. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance, checklists, templates, or code-change suggestions may be incorrect or incomplete for a user's environment. <br>
Mitigation: Validate outputs against the stated success criteria, review assumptions and limits, and test generated commands or code changes before relying on them. <br>
