## Use Case: <br>
External creators, founders, and social media writers use this skill to turn rough ideas into clearer insights and platform-specific drafts for X (Twitter) and LinkedIn. It supports exploration, voice calibration, drafting, and refinement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The idea-discovery flow may review personal notes, journals, or session material that the user did not intend to share. <br>
Mitigation: Before using discovery, specify exactly which notes, folders, journals, or date range the agent may review, and keep confidential or highly personal material out of scope. <br>
Risk: Generated social posts may be inaccurate, off-brand, or inappropriate to publish as-is. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit all drafts before posting. <br>
