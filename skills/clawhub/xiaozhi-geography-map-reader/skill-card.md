## Description:

帮助中国大陆初高中学生按图名、图例、比例尺、方向和关键数据解读地理图表，并分析地理错题的原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

学生和教育工作者可用它逐步阅读地图、等高线图和气候图，并针对具体地理错题定位读图或计算等方面的误区。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localization required for use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A map or chart may be misread when its legend, scale, direction, or values are unclear.

Mitigation: Ask the student to describe missing map details and verify each conclusion against visible symbols or values; do not guess.

Risk: A minor's learning profile, wrong answers, reminders, or parent sharing could be used without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep the interaction limited to the current question by default; enable memory, handoffs, reminders, and sharing only with the relevant student and guardian permissions.

Risk: Region-specific curriculum and safety guidance may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use for Chinese-language mainland China K12 tutoring or localize curriculum and referral guidance before deploying elsewhere.
