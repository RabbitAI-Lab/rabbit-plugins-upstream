## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary-school students through reading maps and geography charts, then helps identify the underlying cause of mistakes in geography questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to read maps, interpret geographic charts, and work through the causes of mistakes in a specific geography question. Educators can use its step-by-step prompts to support geography practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K–12 education setting)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, and consent rules may be inappropriate outside the intended language and region.

Mitigation: Localize crisis referrals, school-stage and curriculum references, and minor-consent rules before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Optional cross-skill profile sharing and reminders may record a student's learning information.

Mitigation: Review profile-sharing settings and require the relevant student and guardian consent before enabling learning records or reminders.
