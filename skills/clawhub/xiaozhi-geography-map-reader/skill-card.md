## Description:

面向中国大陆初高中学生，循序指导阅读地图、等高线和气候等地理图表，并分析读图错因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

学生和教师可用它围绕一道具体地理题，先辨认图名、图例、比例尺和方向，再读取关键数据并复盘错误；经单独同意后可交接学习档案、错题或提醒。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12 education)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning-profile sharing or reminders involving minors may conflict with local consent and guardian settings.

Mitigation: Check platform consent, guardian, profile-sharing and reminder settings before enabling optional handoffs.

Risk: Crisis contacts and curriculum assumptions may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Limit routine use to Chinese-language Mainland China K12 tutoring; adapt crisis contacts, curriculum and data-consent rules before use elsewhere.
