## Description:

引导中学生依据图名、图例、比例尺、方向和图上数值解读地理图表，并分析地理错题的原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to work through Chinese secondary-school map and geography-chart questions, with stepwise hints and geography error analysis rather than immediate answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (simplified-Chinese K12); localize curriculum, consent practices, and crisis-referral channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Geography weak-point records and reminder requests may involve a minor's learning data.

Mitigation: Review profile and reminder consent settings; permit handoffs only when the student explicitly opts in and applicable guardian consent is obtained.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis-referral channels may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, consent rules, and referral channels before deployment outside Mainland China.
