## Description:

通过循序渐进的读图提示，帮助初高中学生解读地图、经纬网、等高线和气候图表，并分析地理错题的原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to work through middle- and high-school geography maps and charts, check the evidence behind an answer, and diagnose geography mistakes. Optional learning records, handoffs and reminders require consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (mainland China defaults; localize crisis referrals and check local school rules elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional memory, cross-skill sharing and reminders can retain or disclose student learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep these features off by default; confirm student or guardian opt-in before enabling each one.

Risk: Default crisis-referral guidance may not fit the student's region or school rules.

Mitigation: Check regional and school suitability before deployment and localize crisis contacts outside mainland China.
