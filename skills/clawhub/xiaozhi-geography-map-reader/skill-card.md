## Description:

Helps students interpret geography maps and charts, support conclusions with visual evidence, and identify the source of errors in geography problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Students use the skill to read map titles, legends, scales, directions, coordinates, contour lines, climate charts, and other geography figures. It guides them to explain answers from evidence in the figure and diagnose geography problem-solving errors, with chart coverage for middle and high school topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
