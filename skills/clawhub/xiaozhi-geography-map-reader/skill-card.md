## Description:

以循序提示帮助中国大陆初高中学生读懂地图和地理图表，并分析地理错题的原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中学生和陪同的教师或监护人，用于逐步读取经纬网、等高线、气候图等题目资料，并定位地理错因；不直接代答正在进行的考试。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆（简体中文 K12 教学场景）

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and crisis-referral advice may not fit students outside the intended mainland China context.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis-referral channels before use elsewhere.

Risk: Learning profiles, handoffs, and reminders can expose or retain a minor's learning information.

Mitigation: Review sharing, deletion, and reminder controls with students or guardians and require explicit consent before updating records.
