## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through reading geography maps and charts, then helps identify the source of mistakes in geography questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this tutor to read maps and geography charts step by step, check answers against visual evidence, and understand geography mistakes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and emergency guidance may not fit settings outside the intended Chinese-language K–12 context.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts, and consent rules before use in other settings.

Risk: Student profile memory, cross-skill sharing, or reminders could expose or retain student information without consent.

Mitigation: Keep these features off unless the student or guardian has clearly opted in.
