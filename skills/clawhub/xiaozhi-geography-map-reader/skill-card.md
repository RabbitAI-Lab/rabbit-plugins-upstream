## Description:

引导学生按图名、图例、比例尺、方向和关键数值解读地理图表，并分析地理错题的原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中学生，辅导阅读地图、经纬网、等高线和气候图，并根据具体题目分析读图错误。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders may be shared without clear consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile memory, cross-skill and parent sharing, and reminders off unless the student or guardian explicitly enables each feature.

Risk: Misreading a map or missing image details may lead to incorrect geography guidance.

Mitigation: Ask for the map title, legend, scale, direction, and relevant values; confirm unclear details rather than guessing.

Risk: Crisis or safety disclosures from a minor may be mishandled as ordinary tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring and direct the student to a trusted adult and appropriate local help channels.
