## Description:

Generates Spanish paid-ad scripts for Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, and promoted Reels using the FÓRMULA 100K H x M x C framework for hooks, meat, CTAs, and campaign variants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External marketers, media buyers, creators, and content teams use this skill to turn a business brief into Spanish paid-ad hooks, narrative script components, CTAs, complete timed scripts, and modular variants for campaign testing.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated scripts may include sensitive business brief details or campaign context.

Mitigation: Provide only the business context needed for the script and review generated Markdown before sharing or publishing it.

Risk: Automated file organization may be unreliable because the artifact mentions inconsistent output directories.

Mitigation: Confirm the expected save path before relying on local campaign-script organization.

Risk: Paid-ad claims, metrics, or testimonials can become misleading if unsupported by real proof.

Mitigation: Use user-provided evidence for cases and results, or keep explicit placeholders until proof is available.
