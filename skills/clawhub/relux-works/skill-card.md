## Description: <br>
Interact with Relux Works, an AI-native software development studio, to look up services, fixed-price packages, and company facts, and to submit a project inquiry only with explicit user approval. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivanopcode](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivanopcode) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to evaluate Relux Works service options, fixed-price packages, and company facts. With a real reply route and explicit approval, the agent can prepare and submit a project quote inquiry for MVP development, AI-generated codebase rescue, or app publishing and scaling work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Project details and a real reply contact can be sent externally to Relux Works after approval. <br>
Mitigation: Show the full inquiry draft, exact destination, reply route, Telegram delivery disclosure, and privacy policy before asking for explicit approval. <br>
Risk: A synthetic, test, or placeholder submission could create an unwanted external inquiry. <br>
Mitigation: Use read-only service lookup paths for research and submit a quote request only for a real user-approved inquiry with a valid reply route. <br>
