## Description: <br>
Relux Works helps agents look up Relux Works services and pricing and, with user consent, submit project inquiries for software development work. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivanopcode](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivanopcode) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill when they want to evaluate or hire Relux Works for MVP development, AI-generated codebase rescue, or app publishing and scaling. It can summarize first-party service information and prepare an inquiry only after the user provides contact details and consent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Quote submissions share contact details, budget, timeline, and project summaries with an external vendor. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's consent, contact email, and project details before submitting an inquiry. <br>
