## Description: <br>
VDA 6.3:2023 process audit assistant for audit preparation, onsite audit support, report generation, and self-assessment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Auditors and audited teams use this skill to prepare for VDA 6.3:2023 process audits, conduct guided reviews, interpret requirements, calculate scores, and draft audit reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad audit or report requests may activate a VDA 6.3-specific workflow when the user intended a different audit standard. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants VDA 6.3 process-audit assistance before applying the workflow. <br>
Risk: Incorrect scores or reports could mislead audit preparation or compliance decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated scores, downgrade warnings, and reports against the authoritative VDA 6.3 materials before use. <br>
