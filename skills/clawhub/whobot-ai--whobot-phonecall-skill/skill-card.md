## Description:

WhoBot’s AI phone employee skill helps users discover, try, create, tune, and manage voice agents for business calls and supported personal calling tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners and sales or operations teams use the skill to find and try voice agents, create or adjust them from business materials, and manage lead workflows. Individuals can use supported personal calling tasks such as making a reservation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Call recordings, business documents, or diagnostic reports may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review the material and remove sensitive information before sharing it.

Risk: The CLI installation uses an unpinned package version, and the security review is partial.

Mitigation: Consider the package-version supply-chain risk before installing and account for the incomplete scan when reviewing the skill.
