## Description:

Helps business users explore, try, create, and optimize WhoBot AI phone agents and manage related calling workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Business owners, operators, and sales teams use the skill to learn about WhoBot, find and try benchmark phone agents, create or tune agents from business materials, and manage calling and lead workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags an unpinned CLI installation from the latest channel.

Mitigation: Verify the package source and use a reviewed, pinned version for installation; follow the supported upgrade flow for later updates.

Risk: The security review flags privacy notice gaps around uploaded materials and saved call recordings.

Mitigation: Before uploading materials or saving recordings, confirm permission, access, retention, and deletion practices; minimize or redact sensitive content.
