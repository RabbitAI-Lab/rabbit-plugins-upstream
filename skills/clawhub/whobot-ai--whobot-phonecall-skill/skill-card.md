## Description:

Helps business users find, try, create, tune, and manage WhoBot AI phone agents for customer calls, lead follow-up, and phone support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales leaders use this skill to evaluate phone-agent options and work with WhoBot agents for customer follow-up, invitations, reminders, lead qualification, sales, and phone support. It supports agent discovery and trials, creation and tuning, lead campaigns, and account tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China and international markets; the skill limits service to legitimate businesses complying with Chinese laws and regulations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recordings, documents, and customer lists may contain personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Share only authorized materials and review WhoBot’s privacy and retention terms before uploading.

Risk: Outbound calls can reach unintended recipients or incur fees.

Mitigation: Check recipients, calling schedules, duplicate handling, and charges before confirming a campaign.

Risk: Setup installs the latest CLI package rather than a fixed reviewed version.

Mitigation: Review the current package release and rerun the skill’s version check before business use.
