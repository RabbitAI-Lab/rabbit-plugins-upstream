## Description:

This skill helps enterprise teams find, try, create, and tune WhoBot AI phone agents for real business calling workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales leads use it to find and assess voice agents, then manage workflows such as follow-ups, appointment invitations, reminders, lead screening, sales, hotline support, and satisfaction surveys.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected recordings, documents, and customer phone lists may be sent to WhoBot.

Mitigation: Check the data and intended recipients before sharing.

Risk: Real calls can incur charges or reach recipients outside the intended calling window.

Mitigation: Check recipients, calling hours, and charges before confirming.

Risk: Installation runs a changing external CLI release whose implementation was not reviewed here.

Mitigation: Prefer a reviewed fixed version before installation.

Risk: Support reports may contain information that should not be shared without review.

Mitigation: Review support reports before sharing them.

Risk: An active call may continue until explicitly stopped.

Mitigation: Cancel an active call explicitly when you want it ended.
