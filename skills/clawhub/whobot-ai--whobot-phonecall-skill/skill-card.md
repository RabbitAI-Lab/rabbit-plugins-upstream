## Description:

WhoBot's AI Phone Employee Engine helps users find, try, create, and optimize AI phone agents for business calls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales teams use this skill to explore AI phone agents and, when appropriate, try, create, tune, or manage them for customer calls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The CLI is installed from a changing latest channel, so its behavior may change between releases.

Mitigation: Check the installed version and protocol before business actions, and follow the setup guidance when an update is needed.

Risk: Workflows may send requests or business materials to a service or save call recordings locally.

Mitigation: Review what information each workflow shares and where recordings are stored; avoid using sensitive material when it is not necessary.

Risk: The security review is partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved skill references and the service privacy information before deployment.
