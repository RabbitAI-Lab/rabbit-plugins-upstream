## Description:

Helps business teams discover, test, create, and tune WhoBot voice agents for inbound and outbound calling tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales leaders use it to explore voice agent options, test agents, create or tune digital employees, and manage calling workflows through WhoBot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Full business requirements sent to the benchmark service may disclose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Send only the details needed to find a relevant benchmark, and remove confidential or customer-identifying information first.

Risk: The installer command does not pin a package version.

Mitigation: Review the installer source and package identity before running it, and use a vetted pinned version when available.
