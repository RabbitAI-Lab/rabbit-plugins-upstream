## Description: <br>
Dlazy Generate helps agents generate images, video, and audio from prompts or media-generation parameters while selecting models automatically. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, independent creators, enterprise teams, and automation workflow users can use this skill to ask an agent for multimodal media generation, including images, videos, and audio. The artifact also positions the skill for video processing, audio editing, media conversion, and voice generation workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests broad read, write, command execution, API, and API-key handling authority without enough scope detail. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in controlled media-generation workflows, restrict sensitive directories and credentials, and verify providers, commands, and output locations before installation. <br>
Risk: Generated or processed media may involve copyright-sensitive content, which the artifact states is outside the intended use. <br>
Mitigation: Use only prompts and media the user has rights to process, and review generated outputs before publication or redistribution. <br>
