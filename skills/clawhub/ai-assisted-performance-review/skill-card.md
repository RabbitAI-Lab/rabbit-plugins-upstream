## Description: <br>
Evaluate performance fairly when output is AI-assisted, separating human contribution from tooling effects and producing criteria audits, rewritten standards, calibration rules, and review conversation scripts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Managers, HR partners, and calibration committees use this skill to evaluate AI-assisted work fairly, rewrite review criteria around human judgment and verification, and prepare evidence-based review conversations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide sensitive personnel-review details while supplying role, rubric, AI-usage, and policy context. <br>
Mitigation: Share only information permitted by the organization, minimize personally identifiable details, and run the skill in an agent environment appropriate for personnel data. <br>
Risk: Generated review criteria or scripts may be treated as final HR decisions without policy or human review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as management guidance and review it against organizational AI, performance-management, privacy, and employment policies before applying it. <br>
Risk: The guidance may overfit to incomplete evidence about how much work was performed by a person versus an AI tool. <br>
Mitigation: Collect the skill's requested walk-back evidence, error or rework history, decision logs, and peer trust signals before using the recommendations in calibration or review conversations. <br>
