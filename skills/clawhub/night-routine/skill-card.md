## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to structure evening wind-down, sleep preparation, next-day planning, and routine consistency tracking. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Routine tracking and next-day planning can include personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Keep routine and planning details local, and avoid entering sensitive information unless it is needed for the routine. <br>
Risk: The broad "wind down" trigger may activate during casual conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants night-routine guidance before collecting preferences or planning details. <br>
