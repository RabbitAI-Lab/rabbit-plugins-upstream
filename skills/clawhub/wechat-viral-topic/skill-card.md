## Description:

Helps WeChat and social-content creators turn supplied material into topic ideas, headlines, and multi-format content plans with scoring and a risk self-check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Editors and creators use this skill to develop WeChat and other social-media topics from their own material, compare scored concepts, and draft headlines, outlines, scripts, and format plans before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated headlines or scripts may contain inaccurate or unsupported claims, especially on sensitive topics.

Mitigation: Check factual claims against reliable sources and review health, finance, and political content before publishing.

Risk: Attention-grabbing content may breach platform rules or use an unethical tone toward identity groups or emotionally charged issues.

Mitigation: Review the proposed content against the target platform's rules and revise sensational or harmful framing before publication.
