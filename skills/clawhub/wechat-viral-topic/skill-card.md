## Description:

Helps creators turn verifiable trending topics into audience-specific headlines and content plans for WeChat articles, short videos, comics, and H5 experiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

WeChat editors, video creators, comic artists, and H5 planners use this skill to assess trending ideas and draft headlines, angles, outlines, and cross-format content plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trending claims or implied popularity may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Check dates, source material, and public popularity indicators before publishing; avoid unsupported claims.

Risk: Viral-content tactics can yield misleading, insensitive, or identity-targeted headlines.

Mitigation: Review headlines against the article, platform rules, and the skill's risk checklist; avoid exploiting sensitive news or targeting protected groups.
