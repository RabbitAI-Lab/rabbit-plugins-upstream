## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Redistributing the written framework as though the code license covers it.

Mitigation: Review the bundled license and obtain permission before modifying or redistributing the written materials.

Risk: Emotional hooks or unverified trends could become misleading or exploitative engagement bait.

Mitigation: Verify trend claims and sources, match headlines to content, and apply editorial and platform-policy review before publishing.
