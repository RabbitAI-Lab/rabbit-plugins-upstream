## Description:

Helps WeChat editors and other creators turn trending topics into differentiated content ideas, headlines, and outlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

WeChat editors, short-video creators, comic authors, and H5 planners use it to assess a topic's audience fit and verifiable popularity, then draft distinct angles, headlines, and content plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trending claims or popularity estimates may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify current facts and audience signals against traceable sources before publication.

Risk: Growth-oriented headlines may mislead or conflict with platform, brand, or legal requirements.

Mitigation: Check that headlines match the content and review platform rules and brand/legal boundaries before publishing.
