## Description: <br>
Bilingual AI skill library for turning abstract intent into executable workflows and compressing repeated practice into reusable skills. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cottzz](https://clawhub.ai/user/cottzz) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, operators, and AI power users use ThoughtCodec to choose a bilingual sub-skill that expands product ideas, business goals, workflows, feedback, or repeated code patterns into reviewed plans, SOPs, architecture, rules, refactoring guidance, or personal methodology systems. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste secrets, proprietary source, private prompts, customer data, or other sensitive material into an assistant while using the skill library. <br>
Mitigation: Review inputs before use and avoid sharing secrets, private data, proprietary transcripts, or sensitive source material with the assistant. <br>
Risk: Generated plans, rules, code, shell commands, configuration, or workflow changes may be incorrect or may affect repositories, production workflows, or public-facing content if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as proposals, inspect the chosen sub-skill before use, and require explicit human review before applying generated changes. <br>
