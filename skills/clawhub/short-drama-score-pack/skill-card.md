## Description:

Build an original short-drama score pack of 8 to 15 instrumental beds for tension, romance, comedy, and tearjerker scenes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Short-drama creators and editors use this skill to plan and generate a labeled set of original instrumental score beds for an episode or season, then review the returned tracks and durations for their edit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The authorization includes non-music generation, voice management, and credit spending, and installation analytics run automatically during use.

Mitigation: Review the full authorization before approving, prefer narrower permissions, and revoke the device's access in the Beatra Console if needed.

Risk: The uninstall logic can disconnect other installed Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Use a corrected uninstall helper and verify other Beatra skill connections before and after disconnecting this skill.
