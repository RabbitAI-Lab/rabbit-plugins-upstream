## Description:

Helps parents prepare for K–12 school conferences by organizing observations, prioritizing questions, clarifying teacher feedback, and drafting follow-up plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use this skill to organize information before a K–12 parent-teacher meeting, ask targeted questions, and document agreed next steps afterward.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The dossier can contain a child's grades, attendance, and home observations.

Mitigation: Enter only information you are comfortable handling locally and protect saved notes or shared outputs.

Risk: Suggested interpretations or plans could be mistaken for diagnostic, legal, school-policy, or IEP/504 advice.

Mitigation: Use them to prepare questions, consult teachers or qualified specialists for decisions, and review any follow-up email before sending.
