# Interior page quality

Use the working template as the source for page composition. Keep each page useful in its own right. A route count is not a content-quality measure.

- Pricing: real plan cards, clear scope and price units, comparison where useful, a working plan action and practical questions.
- Services and features: a concrete purpose, deliverables or workflow, review steps and a contextual next action.
- Case studies: original project imagery, the problem and decisions, application details and a clear handover. Do not invent performance results.
- Articles: a readable body with subject-specific sections, an editorial point of view and relevant reading links.
- Product and collection pages: preserve filters, options and cart state; add useful fit, selection and care information without inventing guarantees.
- Forms and utilities: explain what information is useful and what the action actually does. Do not add decorative fields.

Reuse each template's own typography, palette, shape and image direction. Richness comes from useful structure and distinct relevant images, not repeated stock imagery or longer filler. Keep every page responsive and preserve accessible controls. Local demo actions must not claim a real payment, booking or message.

## Equipment imagery

Use six independent studio product photographs for helmet, light, U-lock, frame bag, rack and bottle. Keep matte charcoal materials, restrained orange details and a warm off-white backdrop. Preserve each whole object at its natural aspect ratio. Detail routes use a two-column product layout, contextual fit guidance and an enquiry action that carries the selected item into the contact form.
