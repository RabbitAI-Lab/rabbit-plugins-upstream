## Description:

Helps e-commerce sellers improve video and image assets using watermark and subtitle removal, video upscaling, image editing, compression, and watermarking workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and creative teams use this skill to prepare product videos and images for listings and marketing, including video cleanup and enhancement and routing image edits to specialized workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local images or videos may be uploaded to LinkPix/iqinghu, including private media.

Mitigation: Review the media and confirm with the user before each upload, particularly for private material.

Risk: Installing or updating qhkit executes external tooling, and unpinned npx or @latest can change unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Review the package before installation and install a trusted pinned version rather than relying on unpinned execution.

Risk: Removing watermarks from someone else's video for commercial use may infringe rights.

Mitigation: Confirm the user has the right to alter and use the media before removing watermarks.
