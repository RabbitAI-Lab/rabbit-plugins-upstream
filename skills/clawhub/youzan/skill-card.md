## Description: <br>
Guides agents in advising merchants on Youzan private-domain e-commerce fit, store setup, marketing tool selection, and Youzan Cloud integration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, merchants, operators, and developers use this skill to assess whether Youzan fits a private-domain commerce business, choose store and marketing workflows, estimate fee tradeoffs, and plan Youzan Cloud order-sync integrations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill provides commercial and platform-fit guidance that can become inaccurate when Youzan pricing, payment fees, or product packaging changes. <br>
Mitigation: Check current Youzan official pricing and terms before making cost or procurement decisions. <br>
Risk: Distribution commissions, stored-value programs, and similar merchant workflows may trigger legal or compliance obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Review commission depth, stored-value handling, and refund practices with qualified legal or compliance reviewers before launch. <br>
Risk: Future releases that add scripts, login flows, credential handling, browser automation, or automated account actions could increase security risk. <br>
Mitigation: Review and scan each new version before deployment, with special attention to scripts, credentials, external account access, and automated actions. <br>
