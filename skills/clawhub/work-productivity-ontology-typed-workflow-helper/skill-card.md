## Description:

Helps users adapt ontology-style workflow patterns into practical plans, artifacts, and reliability improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

AI agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams can use this skill to turn a workflow need into a practical plan or deliverable, then check it against the stated goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation keywords may cause the skill to activate for an unintended request.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation when keyword matching could be ambiguous, and confirm the intended task before preparing a deliverable.

Risk: Popularity and demand scores may be mistaken for evidence that a proposed workflow is secure.

Mitigation: Assess and review proposed workflow changes independently; do not treat popularity or demand scores as security validation.
