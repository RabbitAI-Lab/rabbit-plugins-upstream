## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical ontology-style workflows, artifacts, checklists, analyses, and implementation support for work-productivity needs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn ontology-style work-productivity needs into concrete workflows, checklists, analysis, code changes, or decision support while keeping assumptions and validation visible.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording could route general productivity, ontology, or knowledge-graph requests to this skill unintentionally.

Mitigation: Use tighter trigger wording or explicit invocation when installing in environments with many overlapping productivity skills.

Risk: Workflow and code-change suggestions may be incorrect or incomplete for a user's local project constraints.

Mitigation: Review generated plans, commands, and patches before applying them, and validate the result against the stated success criteria.
