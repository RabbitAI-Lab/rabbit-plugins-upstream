## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate more often than intended because its triggers include broad words such as knowledge, graph, structured, creating, and bug fix.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger keywords or disable implicit invocation in routing configurations when accidental activation would disrupt another workflow.
