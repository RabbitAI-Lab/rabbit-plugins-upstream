## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn ontology-style workflow demand into practical plans, checklists, implementation support, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, maintainers, skill authors, and agent teams use this skill to adapt ontology, knowledge graph, and structured workflow patterns into practical deliverables such as plans, code changes, checklists, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation terms may cause the skill to trigger for general workflow, knowledge, graph, or bug-fix requests where a narrower skill would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrow activation terms when precise routing matters. <br>
Risk: Workflow and implementation guidance may be incomplete or mismatched if the user provides too little task context. <br>
Mitigation: Restate the desired outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing the final artifact. <br>
