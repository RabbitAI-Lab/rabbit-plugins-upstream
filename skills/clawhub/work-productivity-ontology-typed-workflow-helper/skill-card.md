## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn demand for ontology-style productivity workflows into practical plans, checklists, analyses, code changes, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to structure ontology-style work-productivity requests into actionable local workflows, implementation help, reusable checklists, and validation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for loosely related productivity, knowledge, graph, or bug-fix prompts because its trigger wording is broad.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly or narrow activation wording during installation when accidental use would be disruptive.
