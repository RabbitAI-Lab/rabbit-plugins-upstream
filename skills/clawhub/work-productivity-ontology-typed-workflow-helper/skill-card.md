## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to activate during generic productivity, knowledge-graph, or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for ontology-style workflow tasks, or narrow or disable implicit invocation when broad activation is not desired. <br>
Risk: Workflow advice could be applied without enough review for the user's specific skill, setup, or reliability constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, constraints, and the verification note before applying generated workflows, checklists, code changes, or configuration guidance. <br>
