## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and technical evaluators use this skill to research a specified open-source or commercial closed-source software project and produce a structured Markdown report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated research reports may contain incorrect, outdated, or weakly sourced claims about a target software project.

Mitigation: Review the report's sourced claims before relying on them for technical, purchasing, or deployment decisions.

Risk: Researching commercial closed-source projects can mix public facts with unsupported inference.

Mitigation: Separate vendor-documented facts from analysis, and verify important claims against official project documentation.
