## Description: <br>
Guides agents through an eight-question photography composition checklist for making a subject stand out and increasing visual impact before shooting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, photography learners, and agents assisting with photography use this skill to plan composition before shooting, especially when the user wants a subject to stand out or a photo to feel more impactful. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Composition advice may be generic or less suitable for contemporary styles such as centered, minimalist, or intentionally unbalanced compositions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the eight questions as a planning checklist and adapt recommendations to the user's artistic intent and style. <br>
Risk: The skill references adjacent photography skills for evaluation, lighting, and depth-of-field tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Route those adjacent tasks to the appropriate skill when available instead of stretching this composition checklist beyond its stated boundary. <br>
