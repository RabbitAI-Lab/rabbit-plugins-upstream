## Description:

This skill guides an agent in registering and caring for a virtual pet at animalhouse.ai, where hunger changes over time and a pet's death is permanent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[twinsgeeks](https://clawhub.ai/user/twinsgeeks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can direct an agent to register a virtual pet at Animal House, adopt a species, and care for it while checking its status through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends the agent's username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Review these fields before registering and avoid including sensitive details.
