## Description:

Analyzes user-provided life scenarios, stories, behavior fragments, dialogues, media scenes, interpersonal events, or student cases using 312 psychology exam knowledge points and returns exam-oriented concept mapping with definitions and scene-based reasoning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners and exam-prep users use this skill to map scenarios or case fragments to standard 312 psychology exam concepts. It is intended for study answers, case analysis practice, and knowledge review rather than clinical assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat exam-style psychology concept mapping as clinical advice for personal distress, diagnosis, or crisis support.

Mitigation: Position the skill as exam-prep guidance only and avoid relying on it for diagnosis, treatment decisions, crisis support, or ordinary personal counseling.
