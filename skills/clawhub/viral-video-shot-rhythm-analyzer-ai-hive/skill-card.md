## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unauthorized media or brand assets could be uploaded or used as reference material.

Mitigation: Use only media the user is authorized to upload, and fall back to abstract structure guidance when authorization is not confirmed.

Risk: AI-HIVE generation calls may incur cost or create unwanted tasks.

Mitigation: Review prompts, mode, routing, and parameters before submitting generation jobs, and use small samples for batch work.

Risk: API keys could be exposed through logs, screenshots, or shared files.

Mitigation: Use placeholders in examples, keep real keys out of shared repositories and screenshots, and rely on environment variables or a local config file with restricted permissions.

Risk: Generated marketing content could overstate product facts, platform outcomes, or commercial performance.

Mitigation: Require factual support for product and brand claims, avoid guarantees about traffic or sales, and include human review before publication.
