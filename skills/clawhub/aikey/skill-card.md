## Description:

AI KEY routes content creators and business operators to suitable content or business workflows, with guided onboarding and task handoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Independent knowledge creators and small-business operators use AI KEY to choose a relevant workflow, get a ready-to-send task prompt, or find a next step after completing work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can save user feedback as persistent rules that affect future work without requiring approval.

Mitigation: Require approval before saving or promoting feedback into persistent rules, and use a dedicated storage directory with deletion controls.

Risk: The version check may direct users to an all-skills update command.

Mitigation: Review the command scope and proposed changes before running an update.
