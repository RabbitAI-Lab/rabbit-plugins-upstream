This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to audit and update page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, social cards, structured data, and robots directives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect canonical or robots directives can affect search indexing.

Mitigation: Review canonical URLs and noindex rules against each page's intended visibility before publishing.

Risk: Incorrect social metadata can produce misleading share previews.

Mitigation: Check Open Graph and Twitter cards on a publicly accessible URL.
