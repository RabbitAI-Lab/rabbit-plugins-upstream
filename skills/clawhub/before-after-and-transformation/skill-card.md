## Description: <br>
This skill helps agents structure credible before-and-after transformation content using the SHIFT framework, with emphasis on real media, consent, substantiated results, required disclosures, and honest framing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media and marketing teams use this skill to plan before-and-after posts, client-results reveals, progress stories, makeovers, and product transformation content. It structures the narrative, disclosure needs, and media specifications while leaving real media production and final approval to the human or external tool. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be used with sensitive client stories, images, health claims, or business results. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with real consent, tracked evidence, and review of current advertising and platform rules before publishing. <br>
Risk: Before-and-after content can mislead readers if results are staged, exaggerated, cherry-picked, unsupported, or missing required context. <br>
Mitigation: Require real media, consistent conditions, substantiated results, clear disclosure of typical outcomes when needed, and human review before publication. <br>
