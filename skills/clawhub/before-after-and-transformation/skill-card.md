## Description: <br>
Helps agents structure honest before/after transformation content using the SHIFT framework, with emphasis on real media, consent, substantiation, typical-results disclosures, and human approval before publishing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and social media operators use this skill to turn real transformation evidence into credible posts, reels, captions, carousels, or content specs. It guides the agent to preserve the journey, required disclosures, consent checks, and human approval before publication. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Before/after content may mislead viewers if it stages the before state, changes visual conditions, cherry-picks outliers, or implies unsubstantiated typical results. <br>
Mitigation: Use real un-staged evidence, keep angle/lighting/framing consistent, disclose the actual typical result when needed, and verify substantiation before publishing. <br>
Risk: Posts about people, client stories, or likenesses may create consent and approval issues. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm consent for images and stories, disclose non-actual consumers or AI lookalikes, and require final human approval. <br>
Risk: Health, beauty, weight-loss, or other YMYL transformations can create higher compliance and safety risk. <br>
Mitigation: Require competent evidence for health claims, disclose material aids, avoid cure or guarantee claims, and include appropriate professional-advice framing. <br>
