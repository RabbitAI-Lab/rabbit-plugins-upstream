## Description:

Helps teams assess competitive position across point, line, plane, and solid dimensions, then plan a sequenced move toward a more durable advantage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders and strategists use this skill to diagnose whether their advantage is a single capability, an integrated pipeline, a platform, or a reinforcing system. It helps them identify structural gaps and choose a practical, measurable next step.
