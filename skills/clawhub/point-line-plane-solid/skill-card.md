## Description: <br>
Maps a business's competitive position across point, line, plane, and solid dimensions so users can identify the next defensible strategic transition. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business leaders, strategists, founders, and operators use this skill to diagnose whether they are competing as a point, line, plane, or solid and to plan the next concrete transition toward a more defensible position. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill guides business-strategy decisions and may reference external deciqAI or source links, so unreviewed advice or links could lead to unsuitable plans. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as decision support, verify strategic fit against current product-market fit and resource constraints, and review external links before relying on them. <br>
