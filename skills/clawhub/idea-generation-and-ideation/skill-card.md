## Description: <br>
The idea-system craft helps agents build content idea systems from real audience signals using the SPARK framework, with AI expanding specific inputs instead of inventing generic ideas. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to turn audience comments, DMs, FAQs, polls, and other real signals into a scored idea bank, probe plan, and execution-ready content concepts. The agent clusters and expands signals, while the human supplies proprietary inputs and makes final publish or invest decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste real comments, DMs, FAQs, sales questions, or analytics signals that include sensitive or private information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only information appropriate for the agent environment, redact unnecessary personal or confidential details, and review generated idea banks before acting on them. <br>
Risk: Ideas can become misleading if the agent treats invented questions, unsourced demand, or fabricated probe results as evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Require real human-supplied signals, keep source evidence attached to bank entries, and have the human make every invest, kill, and publish decision. <br>
Risk: Competitor content or trend urgency could be misused as a shortcut, producing copied or deceptive ideas. <br>
Mitigation: Use competitor posts only for pattern and gap analysis, avoid rewording, do not manufacture urgency, and apply a human review before production. <br>
