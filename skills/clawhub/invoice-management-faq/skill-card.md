## Description:

Provides quick answers to common invoice application and return-process questions based on bundled invoice management FAQ documentation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sales staff and new employees use this skill to look up invoice application prerequisites, required invoice request fields, one-to-one and one-to-many invoicing procedures, pro forma invoice guidance, and invoice return workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The FAQ may become outdated if company invoice policy changes after release.

Mitigation: Verify the bundled FAQ against current company finance policy before operational use.

Risk: Questions outside the bundled FAQ could require context-specific finance or tax judgment.

Mitigation: For uncovered questions, direct users to the finance department instead of extrapolating beyond the documented answers.
