## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide broad remodeling advice involving demolition, wall openings, windows, waterproofing, electrical work, or gas-related changes.

Mitigation: Treat the output as design inspiration only and require review by licensed contractors or structural engineers, property management, official gas or building authorities, and applicable local-code reviewers before acting.

Risk: Markdown reference material may contain under-qualified home-improvement recommendations for site-specific building conditions.

Mitigation: Ask users for project context and respond with conservative checklists that flag structural, safety, approval, and code questions for professional verification.
