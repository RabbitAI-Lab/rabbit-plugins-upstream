## Description:

Provides practical home-renovation guidance on daylight, dark rooms, ventilation, lighting, and window coverings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renovation advisers use this skill to discuss ways to improve natural light, ventilation, and lighting in dim rooms and to compare window-covering choices. Its guidance supports planning and conversations with qualified contractors, not construction instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some reference examples understate the hazards of demolition, new openings, or structural changes.

Mitigation: Treat examples as design inspiration only; require structural assessment, qualified professionals, and local approvals before any work.

Risk: Renovation suggestions may imply bypassing gas, electrical, waterproofing, or property approvals.

Mitigation: Do not follow such suggestions; have qualified specialists check the work and obtain required local approvals.
