## Description: <br>
Generates a structured interview prep document for any company using context from Gmail, Notion, and existing notes, then writes a timeline.md file to the interviewGPT folder. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chenyuan99](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenyuan99) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Job candidates use this skill to prepare for upcoming interviews by collecting company, role, timeline, logistics, contacts, and prep material into a structured Markdown document. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The generated prep document may include private interview logistics such as meeting links, access codes, recruiter emails, or tracker details. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated Markdown before sharing it, and remove access codes, meeting links, recruiter emails, or other private details that should not be disclosed. <br>
