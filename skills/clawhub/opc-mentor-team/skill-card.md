## Use Case: <br>
External entrepreneurs and startup mentors use this skill for OPC-style coaching across project screening, diagnosis, planning, weekly follow-up, and review. It provides structured guidance for AI tooling, community operations, project mentoring, incubator upgrades, and IP development. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask about business plans, progress, risks, goals, finances, customers, or trade secrets during startup mentoring. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential financial, customer, or trade-secret details unless the user is comfortable including them in the assistant conversation. <br>
Risk: Mentoring outputs are advisory and may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific business decision. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference suggestions and have the entrepreneur or mentor make final decisions after independent review. <br>
