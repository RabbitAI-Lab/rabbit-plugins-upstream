## Description:

Reviews Chinese content for advertising-law compliance, platform policy issues, and AI-writing signals, then produces a structured report and a platform-adapted publish-ready rewrite.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yeshenyue12345](https://clawhub.ai/user/yeshenyue12345)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and editors use this skill to review Chinese copy for advertising-law risk, platform policy fit, and AI-writing signals before publication. It returns a structured compliance report plus a revised version adapted for the target platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill performs web searches for current advertising and platform rules during a full check.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill intentionally for compliance review, and keep search activity limited to rule and policy lookup terms.

Risk: Advertising-law and platform-policy assessments can become stale or miss context-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Review the cited current rules and have a qualified human reviewer approve regulated or high-impact content before publication.
