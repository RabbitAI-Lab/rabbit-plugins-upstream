## Description:

Guides Odia-speaking users through PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People registering for PowPow use this skill to learn about the service, follow account setup steps, resolve common registration errors, and find the appropriate subscription activation path. The user completes registration and payment on PowPow's website or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may disclose a password, verification code, or payment credentials while seeking help.

Mitigation: Never request or collect these details; direct the user to enter them only on PowPow's official website or app.

Risk: Subscription activation requires payment, which the skill cannot safely complete for the user.

Mitigation: Explain the available payment paths and direct the user to the official subscription page or app to complete payment themselves.
