## Description:

Guides teams through a four-stage product UX process, from requirements research and user scenarios to information architecture, wireframes, visual design, prototyping, and developer handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fize](https://clawhub.ai/user/fize)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, UX designers, and developers use this skill to plan end-to-end product UX, map user journeys, structure information architecture, draft low-fidelity wireframes, align with a design system, and prepare implementation handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A UX plan may rest on assumptions or success criteria that do not fit the product's users and constraints.

Mitigation: Review assumptions and success criteria with users and stakeholders at approval checkpoints before implementation.

Risk: Referenced companion skills are separate packages and may provide guidance that is unsuitable for the project.

Mitigation: Review each companion skill separately before using its guidance.
