## Description:

Verify coursework code and numerical results with reproducible inputs, boundary tests, and honest measured reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to check coursework code and numerical results against assignment requirements, validate boundary cases and data handling, and prepare transparent reports of what was measured and tested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course and assignment policies may restrict assistance with code, calculations, or prose.

Mitigation: Check the applicable course and assignment rules before using the skill, and keep code, calculation, and prose permissions distinct.

Risk: Real student records may contain private information.

Mitigation: Keep real student records outside shared or distributable materials.
