UIUX设计助手(免费版) helps individual developers choose UI/UX layout direction, color systems, typography scales, responsive breakpoints, and ASCII-friendly design tokens for project interfaces. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Risk: The skill requests command execution and file-writing abilities that are broader than its UI/UX advice purpose. <br>

Risk: Generated design guidance or tokens may be unsuitable for a specific product or accessibility context. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated layouts, colors, typography, and tokens before applying them, including contrast and responsive behavior checks. <br>
