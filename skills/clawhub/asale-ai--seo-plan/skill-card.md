## Description:

Strategic SEO planning for new or existing websites with industry-specific templates, competitive analysis, content strategy, and an implementation roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams use this skill to create SEO strategy plans, competitor-analysis summaries, content calendars, implementation roadmaps, and site-structure guidance for new or existing websites.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad content or site-architecture planning requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants an SEO planning workflow and gather the business type, goals, competitors, and website URL when available before generating recommendations.

Risk: Optional DataForSEO tools may send SEO research queries to an external provider.

Mitigation: Use external SEO research tools only when available and appropriate, avoid sending sensitive business information unless approved, and label externally sourced estimates as such.

Risk: SEO, legal, or competitive claims may be incorrect, outdated, or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Review outputs before acting, verify competitor and keyword claims against current sources, and consult qualified counsel for trademark or comparative advertising questions.
