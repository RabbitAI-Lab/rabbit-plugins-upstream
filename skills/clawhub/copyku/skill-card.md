## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Persuasive copy may contain inaccurate, exaggerated, or non-compliant claims, especially in health, finance-adjacent, property, legal, or regulated advertising contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Review all generated copy before publishing, verify factual claims, and provide accurate compliance notes for sensitive or regulated campaigns. <br>
Risk: Guarantees, scarcity, testimonials, or CTAs generated for marketing use may overstate what the product or offer can support. <br>
Mitigation: Keep guarantees realistic, use only substantiated testimonials or evidence, and adjust calls to action to match the actual offer and approval requirements. <br>
