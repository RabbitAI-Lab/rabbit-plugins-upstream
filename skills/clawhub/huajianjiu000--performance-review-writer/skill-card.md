## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Performance review prompts or drafts may include sensitive employee information.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting unnecessary sensitive employee data and follow applicable workplace privacy practices.

Risk: Generated feedback may be unfair, inaccurate, or inconsistent with workplace policy.

Mitigation: Have a manager or HR reviewer check the final review for fairness, accuracy, and compliance before sharing it.
