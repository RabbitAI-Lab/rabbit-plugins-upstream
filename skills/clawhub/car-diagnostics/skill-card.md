## Description: <br>
Car diagnostics and maintenance assistant for troubleshooting car problems, maintenance schedules, repair estimates, and mechanic advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External car owners use this skill to triage vehicle symptoms, understand warning lights and OBD-II codes, estimate repair costs, plan maintenance, and decide when a mechanic is needed. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Automotive troubleshooting advice can be incomplete or unsafe for urgent vehicle conditions. <br>
Mitigation: For brake failure, steering issues, overheating, oil-pressure warnings, fuel smells, electrical burning odors, high-voltage components, lifting a vehicle, or uncertain repairs, stop using the vehicle if needed and consult a qualified mechanic. <br>
Risk: Repair causes and cost estimates may not match the user's specific vehicle, location, or inspection findings. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as general triage and verify recommendations with an inspection, OBD-II scan, service manual, repair estimate, or qualified mechanic before acting. <br>
