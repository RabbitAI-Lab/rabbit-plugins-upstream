## Description:

Creates weekly Monday-Sunday Instagram Stories calendars using the Formula 100K method, assigning story sequences by calendar mode and coordinating script generation with guionizacion-historias-formula100k.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and marketing operators use this skill to plan a week of Instagram Stories around launches, nurture campaigns, mixed weekly content, or urgency windows. It assigns story sequence slots, coordinates dependent script generation, and produces shareable planning files for production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated calendars, scripts, and spreadsheets may include launch plans, offers, customer examples, or business strategy.

Mitigation: Review generated files before sharing them outside the intended team or publishing workflow.

Risk: The workflow depends on other Formula 100K skills for story script generation.

Mitigation: Trust and review dependent skills separately before relying on the complete calendar-generation workflow.
