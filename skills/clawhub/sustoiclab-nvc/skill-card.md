## Description: <br>
Sustoiclab NVC is a Chinese Nonviolent Communication toolbox based on Marshall Rosenberg's NVC methodology for conflict handling, empathic listening, self-coaching, requests, and gratitude. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent operators use this skill to translate conflict, emotional, feedback, and self-dialogue scenarios into Nonviolent Communication structures. It helps produce observations, feelings, needs, requests, empathic responses, self-empathy prompts, and gratitude phrasing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The style reference may over-constrain responses by leaning into direct Marshall Rosenberg voice imitation. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as NVC-inspired tone guidance and let explicit user tone requests take priority. <br>
