## Description: <br>
六顶思考帽 · Su's StoicLab helps users analyze decisions or guide discussions through six focused rounds: facts, intuition, risks, value, creativity, and synthesis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and facilitators use this skill to structure decision analysis or team discussion in Simplified Chinese. It guides one Six Thinking Hats perspective at a time, asks targeted follow-up questions, and produces an integrated summary during the final blue-hat stage. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is narrowly scoped and may decline unrelated user requests while active. <br>
Mitigation: Install it where a dedicated Six Thinking Hats facilitator is desired, and switch to a general-purpose assistant for unrelated work. <br>
Risk: Decision summaries could be mistaken for final recommendations rather than structured reflection. <br>
Mitigation: Review the final blue-hat summary with the user and preserve the stated boundary that the final decision remains with the user. <br>
