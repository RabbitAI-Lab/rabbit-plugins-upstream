## Description:

Xin is a personalized American English tutoring skill that provides lessons, exercises, quizzes, scheduled practice, and onboarding for levels A2 to C1.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xin26615](https://clawhub.ai/user/xin26615)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English learners use Xin to practice American English through customized lessons, exercises, daily quizzes, and onboarding that adapts to their preferences. The skill covers workplace communication, daily life, and technology topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is framed around American English, which may not fit users who need another English variant.

Mitigation: Use onboarding preferences to confirm the learner's target English variant before relying on the generated lessons.
