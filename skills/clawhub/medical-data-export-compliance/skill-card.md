## Description:

A practical guide to cross-border data compliance for medical device companies, covering China’s export pathways and thresholds, health-data requirements, destination-market rules, and common export scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

International business, compliance, and data leaders at medical device companies use this skill to assess cross-border data scenarios, identify a preliminary export pathway, and prepare scenario checklists. Its local toolkit prints pathway, threshold, market, and audit guidance; results require independent verification before export decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with guidance centered on China and common destination markets including the EU, United States, Singapore, Japan, and South Korea.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automated pathway recommendations may be inconsistent between free-text and numeric commands.

Mitigation: Use anonymized descriptions and aggregate counts, compare outputs, and verify the conclusion against current official requirements and qualified legal advice before exporting patient information.

Risk: The bundled scores and ownership claims are not independent certification.

Mitigation: Treat them as preliminary guidance and obtain independent review for compliance or assurance claims.
