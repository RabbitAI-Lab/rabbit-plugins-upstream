## Description:

A practical medical-device data export compliance playbook and local toolkit for selecting China cross-border transfer pathways, checking personal-information thresholds, reviewing medical-data requirements, and preparing scenario checklists for global expansion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

International business, compliance, legal, and data governance teams at medical device companies use this skill to triage China-origin data export pathways, threshold exposure, medical-data obligations, target-market requirements, and audit readiness. Agents can also suggest local Python toolkit commands for structured pathway, threshold, scene, market, and audit outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with coverage focused on China-origin data exports and destination-market checks for the EU, United States, Singapore, Japan, and Korea.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements can change, so compliance guidance may become stale.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official sources or qualified counsel before relying on outputs for regulated data transfers.

Risk: The local toolkit provides rules-based triage, not legal advice or a final filing decision.

Mitigation: Use toolkit output to scope review, then perform a full data inventory, threshold calculation, and formal compliance assessment.
