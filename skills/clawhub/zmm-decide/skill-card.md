## Description:

A Chinese-oriented decision-support skill that helps owner-operators clarify assumptions, compare decision criteria, diagnose stalled work, and turn decisions into follow-up commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External owner-operators and business decision makers use this skill when they are unsure whether to take an action, need to reframe an unclear business problem, or know what to do but cannot start.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store decision assumptions, criteria, and follow-up notes in a configured memory or tracking system.

Mitigation: Use it only with memory and tracking locations that are approved for the user's business information.

Risk: Broad phrases such as “卡住了” or “想不清楚” may invoke the skill during ordinary conversation.

Mitigation: Confirm that decision coaching is intended before applying the workflow to sensitive or unrelated conversations.

Risk: Decision-support guidance can still be mistaken for professional legal, investment, clinical, or other regulated advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as decision criteria and route regulated or persistent mental-health concerns to qualified professionals.
