## Description:

Helps non-technical B2B owner-operators clarify hard decisions, expose assumptions, separate uncertainty from risk and opportunity cost, and turn decisions into reviewable commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External owner-operators and business leaders use this skill when deciding whether to act, reframing an unclear business problem, or diagnosing why a known task is stalled. It produces decision criteria, assumption checks, next-step branches, and a commitment record for later review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persist sensitive business decisions, financial considerations, staffing issues, or personal blockers to memory or /zmm-track after broad natural-language triggers.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before writing decision records or recurring patterns, and avoid using the skill for confidential material unless persistent memory is acceptable.
