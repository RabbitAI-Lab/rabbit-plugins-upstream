## Description:

A decision-coaching skill for owner-operators that helps clarify whether to act, identify the real question, or diagnose why a known task is stalled, then turns the outcome into decision criteria and a review commitment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External owner-operators and business leads use this skill when they are unsure whether to take an action, need to restate a vague business problem, or know what to do but cannot start. It structures the conversation around assumptions, reversibility, risk tolerance, opportunity cost, decision criteria, and a dated follow-up commitment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Decision sessions may retain business context, assumptions, criteria, and review dates in configured memory and tracking records.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where that retention behavior is acceptable, and avoid highly sensitive commercial, legal, medical, or personal matters unless the environment's storage practices are approved.

Risk: The skill frames decisions and stalled work, but it is not a substitute for professional legal, medical, investment, or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Escalate regulated, clinical, or otherwise high-stakes questions to qualified professionals and use the skill only for general decision structuring.
