## Description: <br>
Maps available evidence around a known keyword in the domain market while keeping search-audience, advertiser, open-market registration, supply, active offers, registrable or expiring inventory, and transaction evidence as distinct layers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, domain researchers, and market analysts use this skill to build a sourced, layer-by-layer evidence map for one known keyword. The skill supports research and reporting without producing a merged demand score, investment verdict, or instruction to act. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: External domain-data providers or web research may have missing fields, partial coverage, pagination limits, or incompatible matching semantics. <br>
Mitigation: Mark missing capabilities as Unavailable, disclose source coverage and observation dates, and avoid generalizing observed-sample ratios to a full market cohort. <br>
Risk: Keyword evidence can be mistaken for domain value, investment advice, or an acquisition recommendation. <br>
Mitigation: Keep search-audience, advertiser, registration, supply, inventory, and transaction layers separate, and do not produce a merged demand score, buy/sell verdict, or instruction to act. <br>
Risk: Brand-dominant keywords may carry trademark or defensive-registration risk. <br>
Mitigation: Use brand-risk and defensive-observation framing for brand-dominant terms and state that public research is not trademark clearance or legal advice. <br>
