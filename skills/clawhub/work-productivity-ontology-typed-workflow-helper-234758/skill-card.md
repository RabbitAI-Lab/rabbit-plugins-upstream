Developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to structure ontology-style or typed knowledge workflow requests into concrete plans, reusable templates, checklists, implementation support, and validation steps. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate for loosely related productivity, ontology, or knowledge-graph requests. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill when an ontology-style or typed-workflow helper is intended, and narrow trigger keywords/examples before publishing variants. <br>
Risk: Generated plans, checklists, code suggestions, or configuration guidance may be incomplete or misaligned with the user's environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against the stated success criteria, verify assumptions, and scan or test any code or commands before use. <br>
