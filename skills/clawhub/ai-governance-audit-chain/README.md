# ai-governance-audit-chain

Generate auditable evidence-chain templates aligned with ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System, AIMS) and NIST AI RMF for any AI system or agent: six classes of evidence artifacts — decision logs, risk registers, model cards, change records, human-oversight proofs, incident ledgers — plus clause mappings and a forensic self-check list. Operationalizes the "AI passport mechanism / XCGS governance system" from concept into an auditable, certifiable, externally-evidentiary evidence foundation. Trigger words: AI audit evidence, auditable evidence chain, ISO 42001 evidence, AI governance forensics, compliance forensics, audit working papers, AI management system, NIST AI RMF evidence.

## Usage

- Entry point: `SKILL.md` — read its frontmatter `name` / `description` to decide applicability, then follow the steps.
- No `scripts/` directory: this skill ships documentation only.
