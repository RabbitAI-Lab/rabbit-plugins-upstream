## Description:

Generates audit-evidence chain templates for AI systems and agents, including governance records and evidence checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI governance teams, auditors, and developers use this skill to draft decision logs, risk registers, model cards, change records, human oversight evidence, incident logs, and regulatory mapping checklists for AI systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad AI governance and audit triggers can activate the skill outside an intended evidence-template task.

Mitigation: Invoke it for explicit audit-evidence template work and limit file permissions to the relevant workspace.

Risk: Release metadata and bundled documents disagree about licensing and reuse rights.

Mitigation: Confirm rights for the document-only release with the publisher before reuse or redistribution.

Risk: Regulatory mappings may be incomplete or unsuitable for a particular jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Independently review applicable requirements; do not treat generated mappings as legal advice.
