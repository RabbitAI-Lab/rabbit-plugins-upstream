## Description:

Generates AI governance evidence templates, clause mappings, and a self-check list aligned with ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and AI governance teams use this skill to prepare decision logs, risk registers, model cards, change records, human-oversight records, and incident ledgers for audit review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Template mappings may be mistaken for verified compliance or certification evidence.

Mitigation: Check mappings against current official standards and obtain qualified compliance or certification review before relying on them.

Risk: Generated records may imply an event or review occurred without supporting evidence.

Mitigation: Populate templates only with authentic records and accurate timestamps; mark missing or unverified evidence as pending.
