## Description:

Generates multilingual auditable evidence-chain templates for AI systems and agents, including logs, risk registers, model cards, change records, human-oversight proof, incident records, and ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF mappings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, AI governance teams, and compliance reviewers use this skill to create audit-ready evidence templates and self-checklists for AI systems or agents. It helps structure governance records for audit, regulatory, and incident-response questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated compliance, governance, or audit material may be incomplete or inaccurate for a specific jurisdiction or organization.

Mitigation: Have qualified governance, legal, or compliance staff review outputs before relying on them for audits, regulatory responses, or incident investigations.

Risk: Generic trigger keywords may cause the skill to activate in conversations that do not need an audit evidence-chain template.

Mitigation: Enable the skill only for governance evidence-chain workflows or narrow routing triggers to audit, compliance, and AI management-system tasks.

Risk: Chinese-first terminology and multilingual output may need domain review when used in other languages.

Mitigation: Review translated standards, regulatory terms, and framework mappings before external use.
