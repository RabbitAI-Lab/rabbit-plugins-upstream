## Description:

Ecommerce livestream avatar / AI spokesperson for product selling videos — 18 production-grade base-portrait recipes plus the full script→TTS→avatar→B-roll pipeline. Use when the user wants a digital human to sell a product on TikTok Shop, Douyin, Amazon, Shopee or a brand store.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use this skill to plan and create product-selling videos with a reusable AI presenter. It provides portrait recipes and guidance for the script, voice, avatar, B-roll, and assembly stages.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected portraits, product images, audio, or videos are sent to dLazy for processing and may contain personal or confidential material.

Mitigation: Submit only material you are authorized to share, especially portraits and confidential product photos; use local ffmpeg when hosted editing is not needed.

Risk: The saved API key could be exposed, and paid generation steps can incur charges.

Mitigation: Protect the API key saved in ~/.dlazy/config.json and check estimated charges before generation.
