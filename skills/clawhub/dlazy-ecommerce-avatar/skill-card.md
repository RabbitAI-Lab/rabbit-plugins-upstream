## Description:

Guides creators through making ecommerce spokesperson videos with reusable avatar portraits, sales scripts, voiceovers, product footage, and video assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and ecommerce teams use this skill to plan and produce short-form product-selling videos featuring a reusable AI spokesperson, from portrait and script through voiceover, footage, and assembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the external CLI and sending product or media files to dLazy services may expose sensitive materials.

Mitigation: Review the CLI installation and sharing requirements; submit only materials approved for external processing.

Risk: A dLazy API key may be stored locally or passed to commands.

Mitigation: Protect the key, avoid exposing it in shared prompts or logs, and use the documented authentication options carefully.

Risk: Generated sales claims, credentials, or imagery may mislead customers.

Mitigation: Verify product claims and visual details, avoid false professional endorsements, and review AI-content disclosure requirements before publishing.

Risk: Invalid generation parameters can waste paid credits.

Mitigation: Use dry-run to check commands and estimated cost before generating media.
