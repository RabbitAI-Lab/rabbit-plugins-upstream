## Description:

Guides creation of ecommerce spokesperson videos with reusable avatar portraits, scripts, voiceovers, lip-synced clips, and product B-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to plan and produce product-selling videos with a consistent virtual presenter for ecommerce and social platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and product media sent to the dLazy cloud service may contain sensitive material.

Mitigation: Review and obtain permission for uploaded content; do not send confidential material without approving the service's handling of it.

Risk: CLI access requires an API key stored locally or provided through an environment variable.

Mitigation: Keep the key private, limit access to its storage, and rotate it if exposed.

Risk: Video generation can consume paid credits, including for unsuitable or failed outputs.

Mitigation: Check model help and dry-run options, inspect the base portrait, and estimate credits before generating long videos.

Risk: Synthetic spokesperson videos can contain misleading product claims or require disclosure.

Mitigation: Review claims and visuals before publication and follow applicable platform rules for labeling AI-generated content.
