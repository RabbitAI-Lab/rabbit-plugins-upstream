## Description:

Guides creation of ecommerce spokesperson videos with portrait recipes and a script-to-voice-to-avatar-to-B-roll workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and ecommerce teams use this skill to plan, generate, and assemble product-selling videos with a reusable AI spokesperson, voiceover, and product footage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images, prompts, audio, and video assets may be sent to the dLazy cloud service.

Mitigation: Use the service only for assets you are permitted to upload and review its data handling before use.

Risk: Signing in can save an API key locally, and a global CLI install persists on the device.

Mitigation: Protect the local key; prefer npx or a reviewed pinned CLI install if persistent installation is unwanted.

Risk: AI-generated spokesperson videos may contain misleading claims or fabricated credentials.

Mitigation: Review scripts, product depictions, and visible text before publication, and disclose AI-generated content where required.
