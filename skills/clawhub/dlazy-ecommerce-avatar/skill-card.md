## Description:

Guides the creation of ecommerce spokesperson videos using portrait recipes and a script, voice, avatar, and B-roll workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Merchants and content creators use this skill to plan and produce product-selling avatar videos for social commerce and online stores.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the CLI and uploading prompts or media to dLazy exposes content to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Verify the CLI package and service before installation, and review media and prompts before upload.

Risk: An API key may be stored locally after login.

Mitigation: Use DLAZY_API_KEY for temporary access if local key storage is not desired.

Risk: Generated product claims or avatar videos may breach advertising or platform AI-labeling requirements.

Mitigation: Review claims and visuals before publishing, and apply required AI-content labels.
