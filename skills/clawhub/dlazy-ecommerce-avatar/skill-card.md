## Description:

Guides creators through portrait, sales-script, voiceover, avatar, product-shot, and editing workflows for ecommerce spokesperson videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce creators and marketers use this skill to plan and produce vertical product-selling videos with a reusable digital spokesperson, from base portrait and script through voiceover, lip sync, product footage, and assembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product, likeness, voice, and video assets are sent to dLazy services for processing.

Mitigation: Obtain permission for supplied likenesses and voices, avoid uploading sensitive or unreleased assets without approval, and delete sensitive local outputs when finished.

Risk: The dLazy API key may be stored locally in ~/.dlazy/config.json.

Mitigation: Protect the key and avoid signing in on shared or untrusted machines.

Risk: Generated spokesperson videos can imply unsupported product claims or undisclosed human endorsement.

Mitigation: Review claims, credentials, and imagery before publishing, and label AI-generated content where required.
