## Description:

Guides the creation of ecommerce avatar sales videos using portrait recipes and a script, voice, avatar, B-roll, and assembly workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and ecommerce teams use this skill to plan and produce reusable AI spokesperson videos for product listings, livestream promotions, and social commerce campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cloud media generation may incur charges and requires careful handling of a dLazy API key.

Mitigation: Confirm costs before generation and keep the key in protected configuration or an environment variable rather than published content.

Risk: AI-generated product promotions may omit required disclosures or make unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Review the final content for platform AI-labeling rules, product claims, and credentials before publishing.
