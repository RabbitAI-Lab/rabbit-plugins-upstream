## Description:

Guides creation of ecommerce avatar product videos with reusable portrait recipes and a script, voiceover, avatar, B-roll, and assembly workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to plan and generate reusable digital presenters and product-selling videos for ecommerce channels using the dLazy CLI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: API keys can be exposed through unsafe handling.

Mitigation: Keep the dLazy API key private and avoid sharing credentials in prompts or published assets.

Risk: Paid generation can consume dLazy credits, including on failed requests.

Mitigation: Check commands and estimated costs with --dry-run before running paid generation.

Risk: Product, avatar, audio, and video assets are uploaded to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Upload only assets you are authorized to share with dLazy.

Risk: Generated product claims or avatar presentations may mislead viewers or breach platform rules.

Mitigation: Verify product claims, avoid false endorsements, and apply required AI-content disclosures before publishing.
