## Description:

Provides portrait recipes and a step-by-step workflow for creating ecommerce spokesperson videos, from scripts and voiceover through avatar animation and B-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and creators use the skill to plan and produce short product selling videos with a reusable digital spokesperson, including portrait selection, scripts, voiceover, avatar animation, and supporting product shots.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portraits and other media may be sent to external services, and the partial privacy review leaves data-handling details unverified.

Mitigation: Review the destination service's privacy and retention terms before uploading media, and submit only media you are authorized to share.
