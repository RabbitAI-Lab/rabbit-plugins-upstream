## Description:

Creates ecommerce spokesperson videos with portrait recipes and a workflow covering scripts, speech, avatar animation, and B-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and video creators use the skill to plan and produce digital spokesperson videos for product listings and social commerce. It provides reusable portrait prompts and step-by-step guidance for scripting, voice generation, avatar animation, and supporting product footage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
