## Description:

Guides agents through creating ecommerce spokesperson videos using portrait recipes, sales scripts, voiceovers, avatar animation, product shots, and final assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to plan and produce product-selling digital-human videos for ecommerce and social channels using the dLazy service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product and persona media are uploaded to the dLazy cloud service.

Mitigation: Obtain permission to use the media and review what may be shared before uploading.

Risk: The workflow stores an API key locally and can spend generation credits.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and review estimated credit usage with dry-run before generating.

Risk: Generated product claims or avatar footage may mislead viewers or require AI-content disclosure.

Mitigation: Review claims, visual details, and platform disclosure requirements before publishing.
