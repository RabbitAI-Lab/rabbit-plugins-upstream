## Description:

Provides portrait prompt recipes and an end-to-end workflow for creating AI spokesperson videos for ecommerce product promotion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce creators, marketers, and production teams use this skill to plan and assemble product spokesperson videos for storefronts and social commerce platforms. It provides portrait prompts, scripts, voiceover and avatar workflow guidance, and copyable CLI commands.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports that workflows may send local images, audio, or video to external services without clearly warning users or obtaining approval first.

Mitigation: Tell users which media will be sent to external services and obtain approval before upload. Avoid identifiable portraits, private recordings, or confidential product images unless the user is authorized to share them and accepts the services’ handling of those files.
