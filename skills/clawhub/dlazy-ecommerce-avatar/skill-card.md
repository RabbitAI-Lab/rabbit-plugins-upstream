## Description:

Ecommerce livestream avatar / AI spokesperson for product selling videos — 18 production-grade base-portrait recipes plus the full script→TTS→avatar→B-roll pipeline. Use when the user wants a digital human to sell a product on TikTok Shop, Douyin, Amazon, Shopee or a brand store.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded media may receive public URLs, exposing personal or confidential material.

Mitigation: Upload only assets you are authorized to share, avoid personal or confidential material, and check dLazy storage and deletion terms.

Risk: The skill's login flow saves an API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the saved key and avoid exposing it in commands, logs, or shared files.

Risk: Video generation and retries can incur usage charges.

Mitigation: Preview requests and estimated credits with --dry-run before generating.
