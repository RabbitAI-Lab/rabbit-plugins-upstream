# Cannabis Withdrawal Timeline & Science

## The Biology

Your brain has an endocannabinoid system that regulates mood, sleep, appetite, pain, and stress. Regular THC use reduces CB1 receptor density — your brain turns down its own system because THC is flooding it from outside.

When you stop, there's a gap: external THC is gone but internal production hasn't recovered yet. That gap IS withdrawal.

## Key Stats

- **47%** of frequent cannabis users experience clinically significant withdrawal (JAMA Network Open, 2020 — 23 studies, 27,000+ participants)
- CB1 receptors begin recovering within **2 days** of quitting
- Full receptor recovery by **~Day 28** (Hirvonen et al., Molecular Psychiatry, 2012)
- New habits take a **median of 66 days** to become automatic (Lally et al., European Journal of Social Psychology, 2009)
- Use tracking tools for **at least 90 days** to cover both receptor recovery and habit formation

## Withdrawal Timeline

### Days 1-3: Onset
- Irritability arrives
- Sleep disruption (trouble falling asleep, waking up)
- Appetite drops
- Restlessness

### Days 3-7: The Worst Days
- Symptom peak
- Anger outbursts
- Vivid nightmares / intense dreams
- Dopamine at lowest point
- Anxiety peaks
- Night sweats common

### Week 2: The Turn
- Windows of normalcy start appearing
- Appetite returns
- CB1 ~50% recovered
- Mood begins stabilizing

### Weeks 3-4: Stabilizing
- Mood levels out
- Mental clarity returns
- Sleep still catching up (can lag up to 45 days)
- CB1 ~80-97% recovered

### Day 28+: Recovery
- Receptors rebuilt
- You feel like yourself again
- Sleep architecture restoring

## Symptom Prevalence (Daily Users)

- Irritability: 78%
- Insomnia: 68%
- Anxiety: 52%
- Appetite loss: 51%
- Restlessness: 48%
- Night sweats: 44%
- Depressed mood: 37%
- Headaches: 33%
- Vivid dreams: 29%

Most people experience 3+ symptoms simultaneously.

## CB1 Receptor Recovery Milestones

| Day | Recovery | What's Happening |
|-----|----------|------------------|
| 2 | ~15% | Recovery begins, receptors start upregulating |
| 7 | ~40% | Appetite returning, irritability easing, headaches fading |
| 14 | ~60% | Mood stabilizing, anxiety reducing, mental clarity improving |
| 21 | ~80% | Emotional regulation normalizing, cravings weakening |
| 28 | ~97% | Near-complete recovery, baseline function restored |

## Key Insight

Cannabis withdrawal is uncomfortable but **never medically dangerous** (unlike alcohol or benzo withdrawal). You won't die from it. You will feel like shit for a couple weeks, and then you won't. The brain recovers fully.
