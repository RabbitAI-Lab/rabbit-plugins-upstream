# Reduction Methods

For users in **reduce** mode — gradually cutting back rather than quitting cold turkey.

## Method 1: Pace Reduction (Most Common)

- Start by tracking baseline for 3-7 days (how much, when, triggers)
- Set a weekly reduction target (e.g. 20% fewer sessions per week)
- Track daily: did you hit the target?
- If you miss 2+ days, hold the current level for another week before reducing again

## Method 2: Time-Delay

- Pick a minimum wait time between sessions (start with 2 hours)
- Increase the delay by 30 minutes each week
- Goal: stretch the gap until sessions naturally drop off

## Method 3: Situation Elimination

- After 7 days of logging, identify your top 3 trigger situations
- Eliminate one trigger situation per week (replace with a different activity)
- Example: "No smoking before noon" → "No smoking before 3pm" → "No smoking before bed"

## Method 4: Potency Step-Down

- If using high-THC products, step down to lower potency
- Then reduce frequency
- Then stop

## Method 5: The 30-Day Break

- Commit to 30 days completely off
- After 30 days, decide: continue or resume at lower level
- Most people who make it 30 days don't want to go back

## Switching to Quit Mode

If reduce mode stalls (3+ weeks without progress), suggest switching to quit mode:
- "You've been holding at [X] for a few weeks. The reduction isn't moving. Want to set a quit date instead?"

## Pacing Caps

- Never reduce more than 25% per week — too fast leads to rebound
- If withdrawal is severe, slow down the reduction
- If it's too easy, speed up
