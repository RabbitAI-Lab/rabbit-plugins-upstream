## Description: <br>
Cannabis cessation tracker and coach. Track sober days, log cravings, identify triggers, manage withdrawal, and get evidence-based craving support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jake17007](https://clawhub.ai/user/jake17007) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users who want to quit, reduce, or take a break from cannabis use it to track sobriety, cravings, triggers, check-ins, and progress. The agent provides non-judgmental coaching support and evidence-based craving-response guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill stores sensitive cannabis-use, craving, mood, sleep, and trigger notes in a local ~/quit-weed/ folder. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only if local storage is acceptable, protect the device and account, and avoid sharing the generated local notes unintentionally. <br>
Risk: Withdrawal and reduction guidance is coaching information and is not medical treatment. <br>
Mitigation: Seek professional or urgent help for severe distress, self-harm thoughts, serious physical symptoms, uncertainty involving other substances, or medical questions. <br>
