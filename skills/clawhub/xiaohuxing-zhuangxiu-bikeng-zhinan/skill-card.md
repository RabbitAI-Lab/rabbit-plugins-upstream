External users and renovation-focused agents use this skill to answer small-apartment renovation questions with practical guidance on visual space expansion, storage, layout changes, partitions, compact apartments, and common pitfalls. Risk: Renovation advice may involve structural, electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, or safety decisions that can cause harm or property damage if applied without local expertise.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general guidance and verify load-bearing walls, building rules, waterproofing scope, electrical work, and plumbing changes with qualified professionals before acting.

Risk: Source references may contain leftover promotional contact details or off-topic material.

Mitigation: Ignore promotional details and use the skill only for renovation planning, pitfall identification, and general home-improvement guidance.
