## Description:

Work with the user's ConnectMachine network of professional contacts, networks, events and digital business cards. Use when the user asks who they know, mentions meeting someone, wants to save or update a contact, or wants to follow up with people.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shekhar1130](https://clawhub.ai/user/shekhar1130)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing professional relationships can use this skill to find and organize contacts, record meeting context, and prepare short follow-up drafts for their review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can access private contact details and notes in the connected ConnectMachine account.

Mitigation: Connect the intended account. Explicitly request contact changes or deletions, and review follow-up drafts before using them; the skill does not send messages.
