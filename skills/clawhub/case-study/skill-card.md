## Description:

Craft portfolio-ready case studies that tell the story of a design project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, product professionals, and portfolio authors use this skill to structure design case studies for portfolios and presentations. It helps organize project context, challenge, process, solution, impact, reflection, and visual storytelling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portfolio case-study drafts may include inaccurate claims, metrics, or contribution framing if the source project details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated claims and metrics against project records before publishing, and clearly distinguish individual contributions from team work.
