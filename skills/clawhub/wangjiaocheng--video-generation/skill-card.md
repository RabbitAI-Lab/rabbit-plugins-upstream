## Description: <br>
Creates video plans, scripts, style specifications, and output files for promotional, short-form, educational, and story-driven videos. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and creators use this skill to turn a video request into audience analysis, creative planning, visual/audio/subtitle specifications, script-style content, and file output guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on common video-generation wording. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended video task, output language or narration style, and whether final files should be generated before proceeding. <br>
Risk: The skill may create or overwrite output files. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the target folder, filename, output format, and overwrite behavior before saving final files. <br>
Risk: Generated plans, scripts, or specifications may not satisfy the user's platform, brand, factual, or compliance requirements without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated content and video specifications against the intended audience, platform rules, brand requirements, and source facts before production use. <br>
