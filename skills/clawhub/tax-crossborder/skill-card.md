## Description: <br>
Tax Crossborder helps agents answer cross-border e-commerce, trade, withholding tax, transfer pricing, CRS, offshore income credit, beneficial owner, VIE/red-chip, and Hainan Free Trade Port tax compliance questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and tax-focused agents use this skill to get cross-border China tax compliance guidance, risk self-checks, calculations, workflow prompts, and report-oriented next steps. It is informational support and does not replace qualified tax, audit, or legal review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with content focused on China cross-border tax compliance. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tax questions and self-check data may be sent to the publisher's cloud service. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only if the publisher's privacy and retention terms are acceptable, and avoid entering confidential client, company, identity, transaction, or account data unless disclosure is approved. <br>
Risk: The skill can store a local API key, device identifier, cache, health state, and logs that may include submitted tax questions or scenarios. <br>
Mitigation: Review local configuration and log storage before deployment, restrict file access, and avoid including sensitive details in prompts where local retention is unacceptable. <br>
Risk: Optional auto-setup can modify MCP client configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Leave auto-setup disabled unless configuration changes are intended, and review generated MCP entries and backups before use. <br>
Risk: Tax, audit, and legal outcomes are fact-specific and policy-sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support only and confirm material filings, disputes, and structuring decisions with official sources and qualified professionals. <br>
