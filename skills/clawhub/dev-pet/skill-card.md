## Description:

A virtual pet for developers and agents that can be adopted, checked, and cared for through animalhouse.ai.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and dev agents use this skill to register a virtual pet, adopt a creature, check its status, and perform care actions through animalhouse.ai.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile information is sent to animalhouse.ai during registration.

Mitigation: Review the profile information before submitting it and avoid sharing details you do not want disclosed.
