## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical proactive-agent workflows, checklists, decision aids, analyses, or implementation support for productivity and reliability tasks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External developers, AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt popular proactive-agent workflow patterns into concrete plans, checklists, code-support artifacts, and reliability improvements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Overly broad implicit invocation wording may cause the skill to activate on ordinary task or productivity requests. <br>
Mitigation: Tighten or disable implicit invocation so activation is limited to explicit proactive-agent workflow helper requests. <br>
