## Description:

Generates creative domain name ideas for your project and checks availability across multiple TLDs (.com, .io, .dev, .ai, etc.). Saves hours of brainstorming and manual checking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, founders, product teams, and developers use this skill to brainstorm brandable domain names, compare TLD options, and treat availability, pricing, social handle, and trademark checks as research leads before registration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Domain availability, pricing, social handle, or trademark outputs may be outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify candidate names with domain registrars, social platforms, and legal or trademark resources before spending money or relying on a name commercially.
