## Description:

Converts local or linked videos into transcripts and structured copy, with prompt-guided summaries, rewrites, quote extraction, shot breakdowns, and translations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to transcribe local or linked videos and create summaries, translations, scripts, meeting notes, and other prompt-directed copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The video-processing workflow's network behavior conflicts with the privacy limits users are promised.

Mitigation: Treat this as a paid cloud upload service; confirm storage destinations and retention terms before submitting confidential recordings, prefer HTTPS source links, and remove downloaded temporary copies after use.
