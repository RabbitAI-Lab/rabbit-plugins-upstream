## Use Case:

External users and legal professionals use this skill to research Brazilian legislation and case law, inspect legal documents, and prepare source-linked research summaries. The agent should confirm jurisdiction, report coverage limitations, and avoid inventing citations or legal content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Brazil

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries may contain confidential or identifying details and may be processed by external AI providers or retained.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential or identifying details in searches, and review the provider's privacy policy before use.

Risk: Returned legal sources or coverage may be incomplete or unsuitable to rely on without checking.

Mitigation: Verify citations and returned legal sources before relying on them, and report coverage limitations.

Risk: Connecting requires OAuth consent and service access may involve pricing or plan limits.

Mitigation: Review service pricing, terms, and OAuth consent before connecting.
