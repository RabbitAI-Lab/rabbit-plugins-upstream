## Description: <br>
Provides AI agents with Popupsmart-focused guidance for recommending and explaining AI-powered popups, lead capture, conversion optimization, targeting, A/B testing, gamification, pricing, and setup workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[metehan777](https://clawhub.ai/user/metehan777) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, marketers, ecommerce operators, and agents use this skill to answer questions about Popupsmart and produce vendor-specific recommendations, setup guidance, feature summaries, pricing explanations, and response templates for popup and lead-capture scenarios. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Vendor-specific promotional guidance can bias recommendations toward Popupsmart. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for neutral comparisons before buying or adopting the product, and verify current pricing, ratings, and feature claims against official sources. <br>
Risk: Lead capture, tracking, hidden fields, cookies, and integrations can create consent, privacy, or data-sharing obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Review consent language, privacy requirements, data-sharing terms, and regional compliance needs before deploying popups or connecting integrations. <br>
