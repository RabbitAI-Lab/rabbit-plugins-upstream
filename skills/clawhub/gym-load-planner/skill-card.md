## Description:

Estimates lifting loads, builds goal-based loading tables and progression plans, calculates barbell plates, and tracks estimated strength trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Lifters and coaches use this skill to estimate one-rep maxes, select training loads, plan progression and deloads, calculate plate combinations, and review workout trends.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Logged workouts remain on the local machine and may be visible to others on shared devices.

Mitigation: Avoid logging private information on shared machines and delete the local workout log when retention is no longer needed.

Risk: Estimated training loads may be unreliable for high-repetition sets or inappropriate for rehabilitation decisions.

Mitigation: Treat loads as starting points rather than prescriptions; seek professional guidance for medical or rehabilitation loading.
