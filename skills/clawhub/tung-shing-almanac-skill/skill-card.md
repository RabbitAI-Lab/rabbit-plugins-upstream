## Description:

Provides Chinese almanac guidance for auspicious dates, daily activities, lucky hours, zodiac signs, lunar dates, and solar terms using 12Zodiacs.com.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals and agents use the skill to explore traditional Chinese almanac dates and hours for weddings, moving, travel, and other planned events, or to check lunar-calendar and zodiac information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Almanac queries and optional personal information, including birth dates, are sent to 12Zodiacs.com.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the query and avoid submitting sensitive personal information without consent.

Risk: Traditional date guidance may be mistaken for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical advice.

Mitigation: Treat results as cultural or entertainment guidance and consult qualified professionals for consequential decisions.

Risk: Mutable installation examples may fetch an unreviewed release.

Mitigation: Install from a trusted source and prefer a pinned or reviewed version.
