## Description:

Diagnoses grammar issues in the Discussion section of English psychology research papers, including tense choice, subject-verb agreement, article usage, voice, and punctuation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, editors, and writing-support agents use this skill to diagnose grammar in English psychology research-paper Discussion sections. It provides focused feedback on tense-claim alignment, tense consistency, subject-verb agreement, article usage, voice, punctuation, and severity-ranked issues without rewriting prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may route the skill to general grammar requests outside its intended Discussion-section context.

Mitigation: Confirm the request concerns grammar diagnosis for a Discussion section in an English psychology research paper before relying on the output.
