# Example: Modal + Present Simple（情态动词与现在时的标准搭配）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Epskamp et al. (2018). Estimating psychological networks and their accuracy: A tutorial paper. *Psychological Methods*, 23(4).
- **论文编号**: 7.1
- **维度**: Grammar（modal + bare infinitive 的标准结构）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: grammar-diagnosis 的"modal verb + bare infinitive 结构"、"modal verb 选词"

---

## 原句（Discussion implications 段）

> *"We **suggest** that researchers always compare networks based on polychoric correlations with networks based on Spearman correlations (they **should** look somewhat similar) to determine if the estimation of polychoric correlations is the source of this problem. ... Obtaining very different networks indicates that the estimation of the polychoric correlations **may** not be trustworthy."*

## 分析

### 1. Modal + bare infinitive 的标准结构
- "should look" (modal + bare infinitive)
- "may not be" (modal + bare infinitive + adverb + be)
- **never** "should looks" 或 "may be not"

### 2. 不同 modal 的精确使用
- **"suggest"** —— Present Simple，但作为 cognitive verb（verb of recommendation）
- **"should"** —— 强建议（研究者必须这样做）
- **"may"** —— 弱可能性（可能不可信）

### 3. Modal verb 选词的精细度
- 用 "may not be trustworthy" 而不是 "is not trustworthy" —— 保留 epistemic humility
- 用 "should look similar" 而不是 "must look similar" —— 留出判断空间
- 这种 modal variety 是 Unit 4.5 重点

### 4. 与无 modal 的对照
- 同一段前面："This problem **is** related to the previous problem" (declarative)
- 同一段后面："the network **should** be compared" (recommendation)
- "obtaining very different networks **indicates**" (observation)

三个 verb 各司其职：declarative / recommendation / observation

---

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户写"should looks" 或 "should compared"** —— modal + 错误动词形式 → 引用此例
2. **用户混用 modal 选词** — 全用 "can" 或全用 "may" → 引用此例展示 modal variety
3. **用户的 recommendation 缺 modal** — "Researchers compare A and B"（应该是 should compare）→ 引用此例
