# Example: Active/Passive Balance（语态平衡）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Schmidt et al. (2016). The Parallel Episodic Processing (PEP) model 2.0. *Cognitive Psychology*, 91.
- **论文编号**: 2.7
- **领域**: Cognitive (Cog Psych)
- **维度**: Grammar（主动/被动语态的合理平衡）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: grammar-diagnosis 的"语态选择是否恰当"、"是否过度使用被动"

---

## 原句（Discussion 第 1-2 段）

> *"The present series of simulations **aimed to demonstrate** how one well-defined, fixed-parameter model can be successfully applied to a range of different phenomena from different literatures. **We were able to simulate** power curves, the Stroop asymmetry, and the decrease in the congruency effect with practice in Simulation 1. ... Of course, we certainly do not suggest that **we have provided** a model of cognition that is in any way 'complete.'"*

## 分析

### 1. Active voice 主导
- "aimed to demonstrate" (active infinitive)
- "we were able to simulate" (passive, but with "we" as agent)
- "we have provided" (active present perfect)

整段主动语态占主导——清楚表明"谁做什么"

### 2. 被动语态的合理使用
- "we were able to simulate" —— 用被动突出"ability"（能...做），而非 agent
- 这种用被动表达**modality**（能力）而非隐藏 agent 的用法是规范的

### 3. "Of course, we certainly do not suggest" 的 multiple negation
- "Of course" (interjection) + "we certainly do not suggest" (negative hedge)
- 这是**双重 hedge**——"我们当然不认为..."——既委婉又自信
- 语法正确（of course + negative + cognitive verb）

### 4. Subject-Verb Agreement 严谨
- "We were able" —— past simple + were (plural we)
- "we have provided" —— present perfect + have (plural we)
- "These effects might be"（隐含）—— plural subject + plural verb
- 长主语（"the bounded nature of the probability scale"）也保持了一致性

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户过度使用被动** — "It was found by us that..." → 引用此例展示主动语态主导
