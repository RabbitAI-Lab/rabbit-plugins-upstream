## Description:

Delivers a Phantra video in the shape and length each platform wants (9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts; 16:9 for YouTube; 1:1 or 4:5 for a feed): sets a default length, writes each scene to its platform's length, reformats a finished clip per platform and hands over a download link. Use when someone names a platform, asks what size or length a video needs, or wants versions per platform (the studio does not post). NOT for the edit steps (edit-and-deliver) or a cover image (thumbnails).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid rendering consumes credits.

Mitigation: Show the quoted cost and wait for the person's explicit approval before rendering.

Risk: Download links may expose rendered files to unintended recipients.

Mitigation: Share links only with intended recipients.

Risk: The separate studio connector's implementation was outside this security review.

Mitigation: Review the connector implementation before enabling it.
