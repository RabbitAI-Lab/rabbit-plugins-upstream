## Description:

Reviews a proposal or decision from three to five conflicting perspectives and highlights consensus, disagreements, blind spots, and the single most important change.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junci003](https://clawhub.ai/user/junci003)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People reviewing proposals, documents, or decisions use this skill to surface competing priorities and unresolved trade-offs before making a final decision. It is not a substitute for fact-checking or qualified professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plausible-sounding perspectives may be mistaken for verified facts or professional decisions.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims and figures independently, and seek qualified review for consequential decisions.

Risk: The skill permits file edits even though its review does not require automatic changes.

Mitigation: Review proposed file changes before applying them.
