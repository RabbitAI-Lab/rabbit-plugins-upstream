## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a Knowing-Doing Audit for diagnosing intention-action gaps and turning incomplete knowledge into concrete test actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, leaders, coaches, and agents use this skill to diagnose claims of knowing that have not produced action, especially in personal execution, team retrospectives, hiring, and strategy follow-through. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may steer conversations toward behavioral evidence in team or hiring contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as a coaching and diagnostic framework and keep fair evaluation standards separate. <br>
Risk: The audit can misclassify genuine external constraints as incomplete knowing. <br>
Mitigation: Check for capital, regulation, timing, and other demonstrable external constraints before applying the audit. <br>
Risk: Users could treat the framework as medical or therapeutic advice. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use it as a substitute for medical advice or care from qualified professionals. <br>
