## Description:

Generate a professional, client-facing GEO report combining all audit results into a single deliverable with scores, findings, and prioritized actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, marketing leaders, and consultants use this skill to consolidate GEO audit outputs into a polished client deliverable with readiness scores, platform findings, and prioritized action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business-impact, traffic, citation, and revenue projections in generated client reports may be mistaken for verified forecasts.

Mitigation: Review projections before client delivery and tie them to first-party analytics or clearly stated assumptions.
