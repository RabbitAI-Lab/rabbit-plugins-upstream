## Description:

Create an evidence-backed executive company patent-strategy report in a continuous-scroll HTML format for senior decision-makers, binding material claims to patent, literature, standard, policy, company, or market evidence and labeling unsupported claims as Unverified.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Executives, strategy teams, R&D leaders, and IP teams use this skill to create an evidence-backed company patent profile covering portfolio position, technical strengths, competitors, market opportunities, risks, and strategic recommendations. It is intended for patent-strategy reporting where claims need traceable evidence and clear limits on unsupported conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may handle confidential company materials and patent strategy inputs.

Mitigation: Use only company materials the user is authorized to provide, and keep sensitive business claims within the approved handling environment.

Risk: Patent evidence can be overread as proof of market position, deployment, product performance, regulatory compliance, or commercial success.

Mitigation: Bind material claims to appropriate source types, state evidence limits, and label unsupported conclusions as Unverified.

Risk: PatSnap MCP access and commercial solution claims may depend on current authorization, product scope, or approved materials.

Mitigation: Verify PatSnap MCP access and any approved commercial content before including current business, pricing, capability, or solution-alignment claims.
