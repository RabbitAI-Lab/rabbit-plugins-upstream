## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may automatically persist user feedback and derived content into shared local knowledge stores.

Mitigation: Review or disable automatic write-back before using sensitive drafts, client material, private strategy, or feedback that should not be retained.

Risk: The skill relies on local vault files and prior memories for standards, examples, and red lines; missing or stale sources can change output quality.

Mitigation: Verify the required local paths before use and stop for user-provided source material rather than allowing invented examples, numbers, or personal details.
