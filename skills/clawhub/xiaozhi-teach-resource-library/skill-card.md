## Description:

帮助独立教师分类保存、检索并改编复用讲义、题目、讲评话术和脱敏错因案例。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent Chinese-language K–12 teachers use this skill to organize their teaching materials, find resources matched to a topic and difficulty, and adapt them for reuse after teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (designed for mainland China; adapt curricula, privacy rules and safety contacts elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student details in stored cases could reveal a child's identity or be shared without consent.

Mitigation: Use anonymized cases without real identifiers; confirm workspace consent and sharing settings before storing or sharing.

Risk: Storing copyrighted source text or scans could reproduce material without permission.

Mitigation: Record copyright status and keep restricted textbooks and past exam questions as source references only.

Risk: Incorrect AI-generated or adapted questions could reach students.

Mitigation: Flag unverified items, exclude them from student recommendations and have the teacher solve and check each question before use.
