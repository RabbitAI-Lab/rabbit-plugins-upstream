## Description:

Helps independent teachers catalog, find, adapt, and track reusable teaching materials and anonymized learning cases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to organize lesson materials, questions, feedback phrasing, and anonymized learning cases; retrieve suitable resources by topic and difficulty; and record adaptations and outcomes. Teachers review materials before using them with students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K–12 teaching context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resource records and matching may expose student or lesson details.

Mitigation: Confirm writes and cross-skill sharing; remove names, schools, contact details, and identifying case details before storage.

Risk: Copied third-party questions or scans may infringe copyright.

Mitigation: Record copyright status for each item; index third-party questions and scans by source instead of storing their content.

Risk: Unverified AI-generated or adapted questions may be incorrect for students.

Mitigation: Flag AI-generated questions and require teacher verification before student use; recheck adapted questions.
