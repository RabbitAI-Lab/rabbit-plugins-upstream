## Description:

帮助独立教师分类保存、检索和改编讲义、题目、讲评话术及匿名错因案例，并记录资源复用效果。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to organize and retrieve teaching resources by topic and difficulty, adapt them for lessons, and track reuse. Student cases must be anonymized, and teachers review any AI-generated questions before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student details in stored teaching cases could identify a child.

Mitigation: Use only anonymized cases, check them before storage, and honor pause, export, delete, and cross-skill sharing controls.

Risk: Unlicensed teaching materials could be copied into the library.

Mitigation: Record copyright status and store copyrighted textbook questions and past exam questions as references only, not copied content.

Risk: Incorrect AI-generated questions could reach students.

Mitigation: Flag AI-generated questions and require the teacher to check each one before student use.
