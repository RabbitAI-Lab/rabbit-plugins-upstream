## Description: <br>
Fitness and workout assistant for exercise plans, nutrition advice, workout programming, and training progression. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[blalf](https://clawhub.ai/user/blalf) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for general fitness guidance, including workout planning, exercise selection, training progression, recovery practices, and basic sports-nutrition questions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat general workout or nutrition guidance as medical, injury, pregnancy, medication, eating-disorder, minor-specific, or clinical diet advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use this skill only for general fitness and basic sports-nutrition help; consult a qualified clinician, physical therapist, or registered dietitian for medical, injury, pregnancy, medication, eating-disorder, minor-specific, rapid weight-change, or clinical diet decisions. <br>
