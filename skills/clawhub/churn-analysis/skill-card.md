## Description: <br>
Produce a structured churn analysis that separates avoidable from unavoidable churn. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Customer success, product, revenue operations, and leadership teams use this skill to investigate customer churn, separate avoidable from unavoidable loss, identify at-risk segments, and prioritize retention interventions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Churn analysis inputs may include customer-identifying details or confidential revenue data. <br>
Mitigation: Use aggregated data where practical and avoid including unnecessary PII in prompts or generated reports. <br>
Risk: Incorrect churn inputs or mixed avoidability categories can produce misleading retention priorities. <br>
Mitigation: Review calculations, confirm churn reasons against customer-reported sources, and keep avoidable, unavoidable, and involuntary churn separated before acting on recommendations. <br>
