## Description:

Reviews research and intelligence reports for traceable sources, bounded conclusions, counter-evidence, disclosed interests, and reproducible claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and reviewers use this skill to check research deliverables before publication or handoff and return a documented PASS or an itemized FAIL requesting missing evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic trigger words may activate the review when it is not needed.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a research-quality review before applying the checklist.

Risk: Chinese-first guidance may be difficult for non-Chinese readers to apply.

Mitigation: Present the review in the user's preferred language and clarify ambiguous checks.

Risk: A self-reviewed PASS can overstate the strength of the underlying evidence.

Mitigation: Check primary sources and use an independent reviewer for consequential conclusions.
