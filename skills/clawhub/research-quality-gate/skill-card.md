## Description:

Reviews research reports and intelligence briefs for traceable evidence, appropriately bounded conclusions, counterevidence, source incentives, reproducibility, and evaluation bias.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Research analysts, reviewers, and decision-makers can use this skill to check whether a report's claims have traceable sources, stay within the evidence, consider counterevidence and source incentives, and include a reproducible review path. It provides a structured pass/fail review and a role-based process for independently checking disputed findings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
