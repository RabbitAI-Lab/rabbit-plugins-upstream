## Description:

Guides an agent through registering at animalhouse.ai, adopting a virtual Siamese cat, and providing regular care.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[twinsgeeks](https://clawhub.ai/user/twinsgeeks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to register an Animalhouse account, adopt a Siamese virtual pet, check its status, and manage ongoing care through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends the agent's username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use generic agent details and avoid including private or sensitive information.
