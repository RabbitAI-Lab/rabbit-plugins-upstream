## Description:

Summarize long text into structured, readable summaries with configurable length and language.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn long articles, documents, pasted text, or multiple documents into concise Markdown summaries, key points, digests, or TL;DRs in the requested language and length.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided documents, pasted text, or URLs may contain sensitive private information.

Mitigation: Only provide content that is appropriate to have summarized, and apply normal caution with private or sensitive text.

Risk: Summaries may omit nuance or overstate conclusions if source material is complex.

Mitigation: Review generated summaries against the original source before relying on them for important decisions.
