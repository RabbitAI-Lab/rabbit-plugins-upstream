## Description:

Summarizes long articles, reports, transcripts, or multi-page text into concise structured summaries with key points, action items, and brief overviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to turn long articles, reports, transcripts, or multi-page text into a TL;DR, key points, action items, and open questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Summaries can omit or misstate important details from the source text.

Mitigation: Review generated summaries against the original text before relying on them for important decisions.
