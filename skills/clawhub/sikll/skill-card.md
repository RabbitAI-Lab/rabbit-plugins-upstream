## Description:

佛学讲评助手 evaluates Buddhist lecture, reteaching, class, and speech audio or video by transcribing it, scoring six quality dimensions, checking source support, and producing a visual HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, educators, and reviewers use this skill to assess Buddhist talk recordings or videos for doctrinal accuracy, structure, delivery, audience fit, source grounding, and improvement opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded recordings and generated transcripts may contain sensitive personal or religious-context information.

Mitigation: For sensitive recordings, prefer the local or offline path and avoid optional external ASR, knowledge-base, or speech-evaluation services unless their retention and privacy terms are acceptable.

Risk: Automated scoring and source checks may produce misleading assessment results if used without review.

Mitigation: Review generated scores, source comparisons, and recommendations before relying on them for instructional, public, or evaluative decisions.
