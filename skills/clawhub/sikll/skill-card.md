## Use Case:

External users, educators, and reviewers use this skill to assess Buddhist lecture audio or video for delivery style, doctrinal/content structure, source grounding, scoring, and improvement guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private Buddhist lecture audio or video may be transcribed and analyzed by the configured local or platform tools.

Mitigation: Prefer local transcription for private recordings and review optional API or provider settings before use.

Risk: Generated transcript, analysis, score, and HTML report files can remain in the workspace after evaluation.

Mitigation: Review workspace retention and remove or protect generated files before sharing the workspace or reports.
