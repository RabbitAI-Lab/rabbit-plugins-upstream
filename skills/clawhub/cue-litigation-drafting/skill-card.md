## Description:

Helps litigation lawyers and legal teams draft Chinese pleadings, responses, evidence objections, and legal letters from case facts and supporting materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Litigation lawyers and legal teams use the skill to prepare editable Chinese litigation document drafts with suggested legal provisions and case references. A qualified lawyer should check the facts, citations, and final wording before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive case facts and materials are shared with a third-party service with limited user control.

Mitigation: Obtain client approval, review Cue's privacy and retention terms, and avoid submitting highly sensitive materials without an approved retention policy.

Risk: The skill stores a Cue API key locally and fetches and runs mutable remote helper code.

Mitigation: Have the user create and safeguard their own key; review the remote repository and code before running it.

Risk: Drafts and suggested legal authorities may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific case.

Mitigation: Have a qualified lawyer verify facts, citations, and legal arguments before filing or sending a document.
