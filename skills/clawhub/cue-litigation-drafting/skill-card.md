## Description:

诉讼文书起草 helps an agent turn case facts, party positions, and uploaded opposing materials into Chinese litigation document drafts such as answers, evidence challenges, lawyer letters, complaints, and appeals, with cited legal provisions and similar-case support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal professionals and legal operations teams use this skill to draft and organize Chinese litigation materials from case facts, uploaded documents, and party positions. It is intended to produce reviewable drafts for lawyer editing, not final legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, case facts, and uploaded legal materials may be sent to the external Cue service at cuecue.cn.

Mitigation: Confirm Cue privacy, retention, and authorization terms before use, and do not submit sensitive client information unless authorized.

Risk: The skill produces litigation drafts with legal provisions and similar-case references that may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific matter.

Mitigation: Have a qualified legal professional verify the facts, citations, strategy, and final wording before filing, serving, or relying on the document.
