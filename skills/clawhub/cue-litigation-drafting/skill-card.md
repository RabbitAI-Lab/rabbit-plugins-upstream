## Description:

Helps litigation lawyers draft Chinese-language pleadings and related documents from case facts or opposing materials, with suggested legal authorities and similar-case references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External litigation lawyers and legal teams use the skill to prepare first drafts of complaints, answers, appeals, evidence comments, demand letters, and evidence lists from case facts and supplied documents. A licensed lawyer should review drafts and independently verify cited law and similar-case references before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (primarily for Chinese-language litigation)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Case details are sent to Cue, while report retention and access controls are not clearly explained.

Mitigation: Before sharing confidential case material, confirm Cue's processing, retention, deletion, and report-access policies; redact unnecessary personal details and do not assume no_store means case text stays on your device.
