## Description:

Generates cloud-palace and xianxia-themed prompts for still images and MiniMax H3 video tasks, including T2VA, I2VA, FL2VA, L2VA, and Ref2VA formats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, designers, and developers use this skill to turn cloud-palace, heavenly realm, and xianxia scene requests into structured English image prompts or MiniMax H3-compatible cinematic video prompts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic creative requests such as making a video or poster may route to this specialized cloud-palace prompt skill unintentionally.

Mitigation: Narrow router triggers to requests that mention cloud-palace, heavenly realm, xianxia, MiniMax H3, or related visual themes.

Risk: Generated prompts may be unsuitable if the target image or video model has different formatting requirements.

Mitigation: Review the generated prompt against the target model's current prompt format before production use.
