## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect structural or staircase advice could create physical safety hazards.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as planning guidance; confirm load-bearing changes, stair and guardrail safety, and local code requirements with qualified professionals before work begins.

Risk: Waterproofing, electrical, and demolition suggestions may not fit local property rules or the specific dwelling.

Mitigation: Check property-management permissions, waterproofing scope, and electrical plans with qualified local professionals before acting.
