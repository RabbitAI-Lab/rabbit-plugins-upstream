## Description:

Helps buyers discover Dongdaemun wholesale fashion items, new arrivals, and stall locations through apMZoomAI in eight languages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apmleokeo-gif](https://clawhub.ai/user/apmleokeo-gif)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Buyers use this skill to find clothing, shoes, and bags from Dongdaemun wholesale stalls, see new arrivals, and locate stalls by building and floor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Item and stall results depend on an external sourcing service and may change or be incomplete.

Mitigation: Use the results for discovery only; check current item and stall details on the linked apMZoomAI pages.

Risk: Buyers may mistake listings for verified availability, pricing, or ordering terms.

Mitigation: Do not infer prices, stock levels, or ordering terms; direct buyers to the item page for those details.
