## Description:

Plan photorealistic image prompts for Duct Tape AI (GPT Image 2) at ducttapeai.art — product studio, flat lay, lifestyle, ads with readable headlines, packaging, UI mocks, multilingual text, and character consistency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use the skill to turn a visual brief into a ready-to-paste prompt for Duct Tape AI, including product images, ads, packaging, UI mockups, multilingual text, and consistent characters or products. It also provides a checklist for reviewing generated results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated image may misstate product attributes or use people, reference images, logos, or marks without authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm factual attributes with the user, use only reference images they have permission to use, and preserve only marks they have rights to use.

Risk: The product site's controls, account limits, and commercial terms may change.

Mitigation: Check the live interface and current terms before generating or publishing work.
