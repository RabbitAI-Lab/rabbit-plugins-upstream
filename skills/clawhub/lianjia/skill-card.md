External users and agents use this skill to review Beike/Lianjia property listings, estimate major second-hand-home fees, understand transaction safety checkpoints, and check rental-contract terms. Mitigation: Use the skill's estimates as structure for review, then confirm current local rules and written transaction documents. <br>
