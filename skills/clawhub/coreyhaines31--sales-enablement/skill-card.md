## Description:

Helps sales teams create practical B2B sales collateral such as pitch decks, one-pagers, objection handling docs, demo scripts, ROI calculators, playbooks, proposals, and buyer persona cards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, marketing, and go-to-market teams use this skill to draft and structure sales enablement assets for common B2B selling motions. It helps turn product positioning, sales context, buyer personas, objections, and proof points into rep-ready collateral.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ROI, pricing, security, competitor, proof, or customer-result claims may be inaccurate or unsupported if the user has not provided verified source material.

Mitigation: Review all externally shared claims against approved product, finance, legal, security, and customer-reference sources before use.

Risk: The skill may use local product-marketing context when present, which can influence externally shared collateral.

Mitigation: Confirm that generated collateral does not expose confidential positioning, pricing, roadmap, customer, or internal sales strategy details.
