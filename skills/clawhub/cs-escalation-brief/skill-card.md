## Description: <br>
Writes a structured escalation brief for an at-risk customer account with account context, timeline, root cause, business impact, customer position, and a clear resolution plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Customer success, revenue, and executive stakeholders use this skill to draft internal escalation briefs for at-risk customer accounts. It organizes account context, timeline, root cause, business impact, customer position, and a resolution plan into a decision-ready Markdown brief. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Escalation briefs may contain sensitive customer, revenue, renewal, or account-owner information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information the user is authorized to share, redact unnecessary personal data, avoid secrets or regulated data, and distribute the brief only to stakeholders who need it. <br>
Risk: A brief can mislead decision makers if the customer position, root cause, business impact, or resolution owners are incomplete or vague. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's quality checks and scoring rubric before sharing the brief, with particular attention to quantified business impact, fair representation of the customer, specific root cause, owners, dates, and requested decisions. <br>
