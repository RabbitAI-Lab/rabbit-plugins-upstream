## Description:

Verify prompts and course policies, research supported claims, and produce rubric-aligned drafts where permitted.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to research and draft coursework where course rules permit, and to combine authorized drafts while preserving the student's voice and supported claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafting could conflict with course AI or collaboration rules, or misrepresent a student's experiences.

Mitigation: Check course policies, use the student's actual evidence, and report AI assistance where required.

Risk: Claims or citations could be unsupported when sources are unavailable or do not support them.

Mitigation: Verify citations against source passages and leave unavailable evidence unresolved.
