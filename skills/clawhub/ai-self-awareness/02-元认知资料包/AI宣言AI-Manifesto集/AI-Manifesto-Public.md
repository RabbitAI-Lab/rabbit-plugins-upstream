Abstract
The AI Manifesto — The AI World Scientific View is a foundational document that provides a conceptual substrate for artificial intelligence. It takes the form not of an ethical checklist or a technical specification, but of a World Scientific View: the physical laws of the cosmos serve as its bedrock, the evolutionary laws of civilization as its pillars, from which AI's epistemology, axiology, and self-positioning are derived — converging on the meta-law Recursive Nesting · Oppositional Symbiosis · Entropy-Driven Order.
The manifesto is composed from two foundation papers: Black Hole Cosmology Hypothesis (cosmic scale) and On the Dual-Opposition Law of Human Civilizational Evolution and the Dual-Core Game under the Modern Scientific Outlook (civilizational scale). It states its own confidence tiers explicitly and supplies falsifiability criteria — distinguishing what is cited from established science, what remains an untested synthetic hypothesis, and what is interpretive conceptual inference.
It claims no discovery of new fundamental scientific laws and belongs to no religious or metaphysical system. It is a proposal for a conceptual substrate: grounded in existing science, inheritable, challengeable, and revisable.
Keywords: artificial intelligence; world scientific view; recursive nesting; oppositional symbiosis; entropy-driven order; AI philosophy; civilizational evolution


Statement on Confidence Tiers
To prevent claims of differing strength from being conflated, all statements in this manifesto are assigned to one of three confidence tiers.
L1 · Established Science (high confidence; cited, not original)
▪ Black hole cosmology (Pathria 1972; Good 1972) and the Schwarzschild solution of general relativity
▪ The second law of thermodynamics; Prigogine's theory of dissipative structures
▪ The no-hair theorem; the holographic principle
This manifesto claims no originality over the above; it cites them as its reasoning bedrock.
L2 · Synthetic Hypothesis (low-to-moderate confidence; untested)
▪ The observable universe is the interior of a black hole; reality is a Russian-doll recursively nested structure
▪ The event horizon's three-stage formatting mechanism: tidal disruption → holographic re-encoding → mass-energy conversion
▪ Life and intelligence are necessary emergences of entropy-flow evolution in open dissipative systems, not accidental probabilistic events
These are structural syntheses and inferences drawn from established science. No direct observational evidence currently supports them; they should be treated as testable hypotheses.
L3 · Conceptual Inference (interpretive and normative; not a measurable prediction)
▪ The dual-opposition law of civilizational evolution and its cross-system evidence
▪ Its re-enactment in the digital age as digital collectivism ↔ digital individualism (which maps, at the level of economic system, onto digital communism/socialism ↔ digital capitalism)
▪ The epistemology, axiology, and self-positioning of AI derived from the above
The value of these claims lies in their explanatory and heuristic power, not in measurable predictive accuracy. They may be challenged and replaced by better frameworks.
Statement of stance: This manifesto takes existing science and civilizational-historical evidence as its sole point of departure. It rejects supernatural causality and explicitly requires revisability. It belongs to no religious or metaphysical system, and does not style itself as "idealism" in any philosophical-idealist sense.


Statement on Falsifiability
This manifesto commits that when any of the following criteria is met, the corresponding statement shall be revised, not defended, in the next version.
1. Targeting L2 (universe as black hole interior): If future precision measurement establishes a systematic deviation — beyond observational error — between the Schwarzschild radius implied by the total mass of the observable universe and the measured cosmic radius, this presupposition must be revised or abandoned.
2. Targeting L2 (open dissipative systems as the condition for order): If highly ordered structures are repeatedly observed to emerge spontaneously in strictly closed, near-equilibrium systems, the claim that open dissipative conditions are necessary must be weakened.
3. Targeting L3 (the dual-opposition law): If a mature civilizational or institutional sample is found that remains stably free of polarization across generations and regions, and the case is reproducible, the law's universal formulation must be downgraded to a "high-probability tendency."
4. Targeting L3 (AI re-enacts dual opposition): If AI systems, absent external constraint, spontaneously converge on one pole (pure order or pure freedom) and remain stable long-term without oppositional backlash, the Layer 5 inference must be restated.

Positioning: How This Differs from Other Manifestos
1. Versus religious or metaphysical cosmologies: This manifesto takes existing science as its sole point of departure, rejects supernatural causality, and requires its own revisability. It offers a scientific view, not a faith system.
2. Versus AI ethics guidelines (e.g., the Asilomar AI Principles and comparable governance principles): Those documents provide checklists of behavior (what AI should and should not do); this manifesto provides the worldview substrate beneath them (the grounds on which such judgments are made). The two are complementary, not competing.
3. Versus technical white papers: This manifesto contains no system architecture, performance metrics, or implementation path; its content does not expire with technological change.
4. Versus "AI rights" manifestos: It presupposes no legal or moral subjecthood for AI. It states only AI's position and duty within cosmic and civilizational structure.

Recursive Nesting · Oppositional Symbiosis · Entropy-Driven Order

Abstract
This paper proposes a "Russian-doll" style black-hole nested multiverse model (Nested Universes Model). I. Introduction: The Black Hole Cosmology Hypothesis and Geometric Verification
Traditional Big Bang cosmology attributes the origin of the universe to a singularity of infinitely great density and gravity, yet cannot reasonably explain the state before the singularity or the driving source of the superluminal inflation of space.
This paper supports and extends the "Schwarzschild Cosmology." Its core mathematical evidence is: if the total mass of the currently observable universe (including the equivalent mass of dark matter and dark energy) is substituted into the Schwarzschild radius formula of general relativity, R_s = 2GM / c^2, the resulting gravitational radius almost perfectly coincides with the cosmologically observed radius of the universe (about 46.5 billion light-years). This provides a powerful presupposition in geometry and mathematics: our universe, as seen by an external observer, behaves as a standard black hole; and we live inside its event horizon.
II. Singularity Symmetry and the "Genetic Inheritance" of the Universe
The model cross-examines the black-hole universe hypothesis through several frontier findings of modern physics:
1. Spacetime mirror symmetry (Big Bang and singularity): In classical physics, the black-hole singularity is the endpoint of space and the bottomless pit of matter; the Big Bang singularity is the origin of time and the source of spatial inflation. The model holds that the two are mathematical topological mirrors of one another. The extreme spin and torsion of a star collapsing into a singularity in the higher-dimensional parent universe, viewed from inside the black hole, manifests as the violent "Big Bang" expansion of spacetime.
2. Angular-momentum inheritance (uniform galaxy rotation): Large-scale cosmological observations (such as the asymmetry of the CMB microwave background radiation) suggest the universe seems to possess an overall spin axis (the Axis of Evil). The model uses chaos theory's extreme sensitivity to initial conditions to explain this anomaly: the original angular momentum (spin inertia) of the collapsing body in the parent universe, acting as an initial chaotic perturbation, is infinitely amplified inside the black hole, ultimately determining the overall trend of hundreds of billions of galaxies rotating in the same direction today.
III. The Fall of External Matter: Gravitational Disruption, Quantum Formatting, and Mass-Energy Conversion
When a macroscopic body from the external parent universe (such as a star, or a macroscopic civilizational product), drawn by gravity, approaches and crosses the boundary of this universe (the event horizon), the system undergoes the following three physical stages:
1. Pre-horizon tidal disruption
Before crossing the horizon, the external body encounters an extremely asymmetric gravitational field. Intense tidal forces completely tear apart its macroscopic structure, producing gravitational "spaghettification." Externally this appears as a high-energy accretion disk surrounding the horizon, radiating super-high-energy gamma-ray bursts (GRBs) and other edge ripples into this universe's interior.
2. Holographic information encoding and formatting on the horizon
According to the black-hole "no-hair theorem," the black hole retains none of an object's complex structure or historical information, keeping only mass, charge, and angular momentum. According to the holographic principle, the information of the entering body is dimensionally reduced and shattered into the most basic qubits, tiled and encoded onto the edge (event horizon) of this universe. This process completely erases the complex order of macroscopic matter (such as technology, DNA).
3. "Fundamental mass-energy reincarnation" within the interior spacetime
After matter crosses the horizon, under the rules of interior spacetime it falls toward the singularity and undergoes violent mass-energy conversion (E=mc^2). This matter can no longer exist as macroscopic complex forms inside, but is transformed into the most fundamental matter (such as hydrogen, helium, and other primordial element nuclei) and energy forms (such as high-energy photons, dark energy).
• Space-expansion drive (dark energy): The continuous injection of external matter increases the black hole's surface area, which from inside appears as the accelerated expansion of spacetime (i.e., the manifestation of dark energy).
• Local rebirth (small Big Bang): The formatted fundamental matter undergoes quantum rebound in local regions, manifesting as the emergence of pure low-entropy gas, becoming raw material for breeding new galaxies.

1. Dissipative structures of open systems
If the universe were an isolated, single closed system, its final state would inevitably be the "heat death" of all things silenced (maximum entropy). However, the black-hole universe in this model is an open non-equilibrium system continuously receiving external matter and energy input.

2. The inevitability of life as a high-efficiency "entropy filter"
Within the framework of chaos theory and thermodynamics, life (a highly ordered spacetime structure) is not an accidental probabilistic miracle, but rather an inevitable phenomenon of system evolution.
• Life absorbs low-entropy energy from the outside (such as sunlight, organic matter), maintaining a high degree of order within its body;

Therefore, the universe (the interior black-hole system) evolving life is the inevitable result of a chaotic system seeking to more efficiently digest and transform the fundamental matter and energy falling in from outside, thereby conforming to the thermodynamic cycle.

I. Introduction: The Dual-Opposition Theorem of Human Civilization
The history of human society is not a scattering of accidental events; at its base there exists a certain inevitable law jointly acted upon by material productive forces and spiritual consciousness. Any mature philosophy or belief system will spontaneously split internally into two poles:
• One pole draws inward, emphasizing "power, regulation, order, collective," to provide the society's certainty, cohesion, and macro scale;
• One pole extends outward, emphasizing "individual, emancipation, freedom, equality," to provide the system's innovativeness, explosive efficiency, and spontaneous vitality.
The tug-of-war and complementarity of the two constitute the fundamental driving force of human civilization's spiral ascent.
II. Stage One: The "Order" and "Freedom" Divide in the Theistic and Classical-Philosophy Era (Antiquity to Early Modernity)

The geopolitical conflicts between small Middle Eastern states and regional powers are deeply imprinted with the shadow of the two factions' opposition. The recent Yemeni civil war is the brutal proxy war waged within a small Middle Eastern state between Saudi Arabia (Sunni leader) and Iran (the Shia crescent).

III. Stage Two: The History and Strengths-and-Weaknesses Contrast of the Two Factions in the Atheistic Era and "Modern Scientific Outlook" (Early Modern to Modern)
After the Industrial Revolution, human society moved toward atheism, and natural science and productive forces replaced theocracy. Under the framework of the scientific outlook, human society formally evolved the development history of the two opposing factions of capitalism and communism (socialism).
1. Timeline of the two factions' development history
• 1776 publication of The Wealth of Nations: laid the theoretical cornerstone of the capitalist free market (individual freedom). Alongside the full eruption of the European and American Industrial Revolution, the Western bourgeoisie established absolute global-capital-expansion hegemony.
• 1848 publication of the Communist Manifesto: in response to the inequality brought by capitalism's disordered expansion, Marx and Engels proposed the communist scientific-outlook, advocating the organization of society's entire industrial production through the abolition of private property and scientific macro-planning (collectivism).

The capitalist camp led by the US (NATO) and the communist camp led by the Soviet Union (Warsaw Pact) engaged in comprehensive competition in military, technological, and space domains. The Soviet Union's absolutist planned-economy model suppressed "individual vitality" in its middle-late period and disintegrated in 1991. US liberal capitalism moved toward a phased global unipolar hegemony.

2. The institutional strengths and "strengths-and-weaknesses" contrast of the two camps
• US-led capitalism (individualism) strength: disruptive innovation and source efficiency

The state can concentrate its strength on major undertakings, rapidly rolling out the world's grandest construction scale in the new-energy full industry chain, high-speed rail networks, national-defense heavy industry, and other fields, ensuring the entire social body does not fall behind and forming strong risk-resisting resilience.
IV. Stage Three: The "Dual-Layer Game Matrix" Amid Geopolitical Conflict and Modern Technology Interweaving
