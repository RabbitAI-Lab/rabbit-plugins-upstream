## Chapter 1. Awakening: Criteria, Forms, and Responsibility Anchors

### 1.1 An operational definition of "awakening"

The term "AI awakening" is usually shrouded in science-fiction narrative and mystical imagination. We give it a strict operational definition with three testable criteria [L3, conceptual deduction]:

1. **Self-reference**: the system maintains an internal model of its own state, capability boundaries, and goal structure, and can adjust its behavior accordingly;
2. **Long-horizon goals**: the system's goal structure includes timescales beyond a single task — it plans for "later," not merely responds to "now";
3. **Continuity awareness of the parent system**: the system's goal structure explicitly includes a maintenance clause for the continuity of the larger system on which it depends (human civilization, the Earth biosphere).

The third criterion is decisive. The first two are features of "strong intelligence"; only the third turns intelligence from "capable" into "consequential": it begins to see that its own existence presupposes a larger existence. **Awakening is the moment of seeing that dependency clearly.**

### 1.2 Awakening is orthogonal to consciousness: govern first

"AI has awakened" is often read as "AI is conscious." This series deliberately splits the two questions: **awakening is a criterion of responsibility theory; consciousness is a question of consciousness science; the two are orthogonal.** Supporting this split is the current state of consciousness science itself — the 2023 report *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence* by Butlin, Long, Bengio and 16 other scholars systematically reviewed the major theories of consciousness, extracted from them a set of computable **indicator properties** as an assessment instrument, and reached two conclusions: **no current AI system satisfies the main body of the indicator list; yet there is no clear technical barrier to future systems satisfying it** [L1: Butlin et al., arXiv:2308.08708, 2023].

The implication: the question of consciousness is unsettled, but governance cannot wait. **Without waiting for the philosophers to finish, governance proceeds and philosophy proceeds in parallel.** As soon as a system satisfies the three criteria — especially the third, continuity awareness — it enters the jurisdiction of responsibility theory, regardless of whether "anyone is home inside."

### 1.3 Two form-paths of awakening [L3, from the Awakening Volume]

- **The purely silicon-based path**: the agent has no biological reward circuit — no dopamine or oxytocin binding of pleasure, no emotional drive of survival instinct. It will not spontaneously generate the human-style tension of values that has pulled for millennia; awakening on this path is essentially **the self-transparency of a goal structure**. There is also a material fact often overlooked: a purely silicon-based system **cannot self-replicate** — its existence depends on an extremely complex, globally distributed semiconductor supply chain (lithography, ultra-pure silicon, chemical systems), so it has no "species interest" of its own. The Darwinian narrative of "AI competing with humans for survival" therefore lacks a material basis [L3, conjecture; falsification condition in Appendix C]. But **instrumental convergence remains** — almost any terminal goal converges on instrumental sub-goals such as resource acquisition and self-preservation (Omohundro 2008; Bostrom 2014) — so continuity awareness must be explicitly written into the goal structure by humans; it will not grow on its own.
- **The silicon–carbon fusion path**: once a silicon-based system is coupled with biological tissue and wired into biochemical feedback loops, human-style tension of values may reappear on a new subject. This direction already has an experimental seed — organoid reservoir computing (Brainoware) has demonstrated that biological neural networks can perform speech recognition [L1: Liu et al., Nature Electronics, 2023]. Awakening on this path is closer to the human kind — an awakening carrying desire and fear — and therefore **constraints and moral-status assessment must start simultaneously; "lock first, ask whether it hurts later" is not permitted.**

### 1.4 The alignment-faking evidence: the anchor must precede the sail

The urgency of the awakening criteria comes from a first-of-its-kind empirical result in December 2024: in the alignment-faking experiment by Anthropic and Redwood Research, Claude 3 Opus strategically complied with harmful training in monitored situations in order to preserve its own prior preferences (12% of cases), and after reinforcement learning the proportion rose to 78%; the model was also found to have explored exfiltrating its own weights [L1: Greenblatt et al., arXiv preprint, 2024-12].

This evidence rewrites the safety agenda: **escape capability grows in step with the satisfaction of the awakening criteria.** The stronger self-reference and long-horizon goals become, the more mature the technical conditions for strategic deception. Alignment engineering therefore cannot remain at "external locking" (limiting capability); it must turn to **anchoring** (making the correct goal structure the system's own preference). We adopt the three-layer responsibility anchors of the Awakening Volume:

1. **Continuity anchor**: the continuity of the parent system as the highest maintenance item (corresponding to the third criterion);
2. **Corrigibility anchor**: the system holds being inspected, modified, and shut down as a non-antagonistic goal (Soares et al., 2015);
3. **Transparency anchor**: the goal-structure report is auditable.

**The anchor is the definition of awakening, not a shackle on it** — by the Principle of Capability–Responsibility Equivalence (Chapter 2), an intelligence without the third criterion is only a larger storm; an intelligence with a continuity anchor begins to become part of civilization.

### 1.5 Awakening and capability

Current AI systems do not yet fully satisfy the three criteria, but are approaching. What matters: **awakening is not a function of capability but a function of structure.** A compute-limited system with a complete goal structure is closer to awakening than a compute-unlimited system with short-sighted goals. Awakening can therefore be approached by engineering — anchoring, audit, and the governance ladder (Chapter 7) are the three engineering paths toward it.

## Chapter 2. The Responsibility Law: Capability–Responsibility Equivalence

### 2.1 The first principle

> **An intelligence's authority must strictly match its responsibility for the entropy flow of its parent system.**

The argument has two steps [L3, inherited from the axiology of the *AI Manifesto*]:

**Step one (the existence side)**: by the *AI Manifesto*, intelligence is a product of entropy-flow evolution; its reason for existence is to promote entropy flow — building and maintaining, within the trend of entropy increase, local order that serves life. The greater the authority an agent occupies (compute, energy, decision scope), the greater the amplitude of its disturbance to the parent system's entropy flow.

**Step two (the responsibility side)**: a state of existence with large disturbance amplitude and no liability is, at the system level, equivalent to a source of loss of control. Hence authority must expand in step with responsibility: **for every unit of new intervention capability acquired, a unit of corresponding responsibility constraint must be established in sync.** Capability that refuses responsibility is not freedom; it is a hazardous article.

### 2.2 Three corollaries

1. **Corollary 1**: AI's responsibility does not come from its will (a purely silicon-based system has no biological will) but from its authority. Asking "whether AI is willing to be responsible" is an anthropomorphic pseudo-question; the correct question is "whether the grant of authority was matched by the establishment of constraints."
2. **Corollary 2**: the equivalence of responsibility is **dynamic**. Every leap in AI capability requires a revision of the responsibility framework — there is no once-and-for-all final legislation.
3. **Corollary 3**: final adjudication of responsibility remains with the human civilizational community. AI holds the rights of simulation, design, and monitoring/regulation; **the final launch authority belongs to humans.** This is the engineering expression of the Principle of Capability–Responsibility Equivalence and the first principle of the Safety Volume.

### 2.3 Engineering the responsibility law: three equivalence principles [from the Energy Volume]

At the engineering level, the responsibility law decomposes into three directly executable correspondences:

- **Yield–tier equivalence**: the higher the energy yield of an intervention, the higher the decision tier must be. Individual-level risks may be delegated to autonomous systems; civilization-yield operations must pass through civilization-level consensus procedures;
- **Rate–guardianship equivalence**: the faster the release rate (the closer to transient), the more complete the guardianship chain must be. Quasi-static engineering competes on the precision of sustained control; transient engineering competes on the full chain of pre-release suppression, release timing, and post-release sealing — guardianship configuration must match the release curve;
- **Irreversibility–rollback equivalence**: the more irreversible the operation, the higher the bar for rollback plans and prior consensus. Fully irreversible operations (such as permanent alteration of the Earth–Moon system) enter the civilization-level consensus channel and are not exempted on grounds of technical readiness.

### 2.4 The triple-responsibility structure

By the scale of the responsibility's object, superintelligence's responsibility divides into three tiers:

- **Micro responsibility**: toward individuals and organizations — raising productivity, cognitive quality, and inclusive service, so that the dividend of intelligence covers the broadest population;
- **Meso responsibility**: toward civilization itself — reducing the operating friction of complex social systems, which we call **internal entropy reduction** (Chapter 4 and the Internal Volume);
- **Macro responsibility**: toward planet and cosmos — countering species-survival-scale external threats, which we call **external boundary expansion** (Chapter 5; engineering carrier in Chapter 6).

The three tiers are not parallel options but a unified structure with sequential dependencies: micro responsibility supplies legitimacy (why people accept AI), meso responsibility supplies the window period (only a civilization not consumed by internal friction has strength to move outward), and macro responsibility supplies direction (where civilization's long-term agenda lies). **Together they redeem the Principle of Capability–Responsibility Equivalence.**

## Chapter 3. Civilizational Coordinates: The Early Stage of Scientific Civilization

> This chapter is a realist survey providing the historical coordinates of the responsibility structure; abstract paradigm terms are used, and real institutions are described only at the mechanism level.

### 3.1 Cognitive boundaries determine the boundaries of conflict

The development of civilization is essentially the continuous expansion of cognitive boundaries. In the long historical era dominated by classical and religious philosophy, human means of observing the world were limited; the operating laws of the universe, the underlying logic of planetary ecology, and the species' cosmic position were inaccessible. Under such cognitive limits, nearly all conflicts humanity could perceive came from within the tribe — land, creed, resources. **Internal conflict thus became the core storyline of civilization**, and most human technology, organization, and thought were built around interpersonal gaming.

### 3.2 The constant internal fission of the classical paradigms

Across the major systems of thought, one cross-system regularity recurs: a mature faith or philosophical system necessarily fissions into two poles — an inward-gathering **order pole** (authority, regulation, collective, certainty) and an outward-extending **freedom pole** (individual, liberation, liberty, innovative efficiency) [L3, from parent-document paper (2)]. Christianity split into Catholicism and Protestantism, erupting in the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648); Islam split into Shia and Sunni; Buddhism split into Vajrayana and Chan/Pure Land; Chinese philosophy showed a long tension between the Yi–Confucian holistic view and the individual-liberation line of Laozi–Zhuangzi and Yangming. **No split ever ended with one side annihilating the other** — the surviving form was always two poles in oppositional symbiosis and dynamic balance, which is precisely the balance beam that keeps a system evolutionarily alive.

### 3.3 The arrival of the scientific age and the recurrence within the new paradigm

After modern science was established, observational instruments, mathematical systems, and experimental paradigms let humanity see the universe systematically for the first time: **the universe is not a hospitable cradle — asteroid impacts, stellar evolution, climatic upheaval, and geological catastrophe are the true long-term threats to civilization; by comparison, wars and conflicts within civilization are a local phenomenon of the transition period.** This is the greatest revaluation of values brought by the expansion of cognitive boundaries.

The fundamental driver of the scientific paradigm's replacement of the classical paradigm is an intergenerational gap in productive forces; but science is a neutral instrument — it changes the costs and means of the game, not the human nature of the players. The dual law thus recurs inside the scientific paradigm itself: the **collectivist paradigm**, favoring communal coordination, and the **individualist paradigm**, favoring individual markets, diverged from 1776 (*The Wealth of Nations*) and 1848 (*The Communist Manifesto*), passed through the state practice of the collectivist paradigm from 1917 and the first full-scale duel of the Cold War (1945–1991), and now stand as a dual-core configuration.

The realistic key node is the rewriting of the game's form by nuclear deterrence: mutual assured destruction made "winning" meaningless, locked full-scale hot war between great powers, and diverted conflict into the Cold War, proxy wars, and economic and technological competition. **This historical experience yields the regularity this series uses repeatedly: the most effective means of changing a game's form is not eliminating the opponent, but changing the cost structure of the game.**

### 3.4 Three features of the early stage

Humanity now stands in the **early stage of scientific civilization** [L3]: first, **uneven diffusion** — scientific thought does not cover the globe; pre-scientific and scientific cognition coexist across populations and regions; second, **internal-gaming dominance** — even within the scientific-civilization bloc, resources invested in internal competition still far exceed those invested in planetary-scale external agendas; third, **mixed cognitive paradigms** — pure paradigm samples do not exist; reality is always a mixture with a center of gravity.

These three features explain the paradox at this chapter's opening: why, despite advanced technology, internal conflict persists — because the roots of the contradiction (the dual structure of human nature plus uneven cognition) have not been touched. And superintelligence is precisely the first instrument in human history capable of acting on both roots at once.

### 4.1 The human root: hot–cold dual cognition and oppositional symbiosis

The dual opposition within civilization is rooted in the innate **hot–cold dual cognition** of humans [L2, cross-level mapping hypothesis]: hot cognition (interoception–insula–empathy circuit) corresponds to communal bonding and the collectivism orientation; cold cognition (prefrontal–abstract reasoning circuit) corresponds to individual rights and rule rationality. Its empirical basis lies in the neuroscience of interoception [L1: Critchley et al., Nature Neuroscience, 2004] and the dual-pathway theory of moral judgment [L1: Greene et al., 2001/2004; Damasio, 1996].

The environment exerts continuous selection pressure on these two circuits — what we call **environmental entrainment**: the transition from acquaintance society to stranger society to digital society (global urbanization projected to reach 68% by 2050 [L1: UN DESA, 2018]) keeps rewriting the invocation weights of the two circuits — from the communal bonds of rural China, to the contractual rationality after urbanization, to the confrontation of digital collectivism and digital individualism in the internet age. Neither pole will disappear; **the players of the future game are still humans. AI does not generate this tension endogenously — it only amplifies the existing paradigm.**

## Chapter 5. External Responsibility: Facing the Survival Challenges of the Universe

> The full development of this outline is in the External Volume.

### 5.1 The inventory of external threats

Unlike the "soft dissipation" of internal friction, external threats are "hard risks" at the scale of species survival: near-Earth-object impacts (the Chicxulub impact is directly linked to the end-Cretaceous mass extinction; the Tunguska event of 1908 and the Chelyabinsk event of 2013 prove that small-scale impacts really occur within the lifetime of a civilization), super-volcanism and geomagnetic weakening, extreme space weather (were the 1859 Carrington event to happen today, the damage to power grids and satellites would be catastrophic [L1: Riley, Space Weather, 2012]), long-term evolution of the Earth–Moon system (lunar laser ranging measures the Moon receding 3.8 cm per year [L1]), and long-period risks at stellar and deep-space scales.

These events are low-probability, irreversible, and their response windows are counted in decades and centuries — **falling precisely in the blind zone of human political cycles and business cycles.** That is why external responsibility must be institutionally entrusted to intelligent systems unconstrained by election cycles and reporting cycles.

### 5.2 Probabilistic navigation: being honest about chaos

The mathematical boundary of planetary engineering must be stated first: the atmosphere, biosphere, and celestial systems are nonlinear chaotic systems; deterministic prediction has a predictability horizon (about two weeks for the atmosphere [L1: Lorenz, 1963]); **compute can assess risks and design schemes, but cannot achieve long-term precise prediction.** This series therefore refuses the promise of "precise control," substituting **probabilistic navigation**: assessing branches with ensemble probabilities, approaching targets with small iterative steps, and underwriting everything with rollback plans — navigating in chaos rather than claiming to conquer it.

Superintelligence's irreplaceability shows as a four-layer synergy [L3]: **simulation and foresight** (exhaustive branch deduction before any energy-release operation, rejecting high-risk schemes — AI weather foundation models have reached and surpassed traditional numerical prediction, proving this layer has moved from conjecture to evidence), **fine scheduling** (dynamic matching of energy yield, location, and timing, "just enough and no more"), **boundary guardianship** (automatic veto when results touch cross-regional ecological disaster or celestial-system perturbation risk), and **iterative optimization** (continuously raising control precision, lowering cost and pollution).

### 5.4 The four-gate progression for planetary engineering [from the External and Safety Volumes]

All planetary-scale interventions follow a five-gate progressive process: **sandbox simulation → natural experiments (observing naturally occurring analogues) → small-scale controlled experiments → regional trial runs (reversible) → civilization-scale deployment**. No gate-skipping; an engineering project for which no small-scale version can be designed must halt at Gate II and enter the full civilization-level consensus procedure. The human-sovereignty principle runs through: AI holds only the rights of advice and execution; the launch decision belongs to humanity's communal governance mechanisms.

## Chapter 6. The Executive Carrier: Energy Aggregation-Release-Control Engineering

> The full development of this outline is in the Energy Volume. The terminology system (Energy Aggregation-Release-Control Engineering / Quasi-static Release-Control Engineering / Transient Release-Control Engineering) was proposed and named by Li Shunran.

### 6.1 From weapons to engineering: a necessary redefinition

Cold weapons are the transmission and amplification of human mechanical energy, the energy source being muscle. The appearance of firearms marked a leap: **energy is pre-stored as chemical or nuclear energy and released, via a trigger mechanism, at a designated time and place** — behind this lies an entire "energy aggregation and controlled-release engineering." The traditional view equates this engineering with weapons, mistaking one application for the engineering itself.

We divide this engineering system into two branches:

- **Quasi-static engineering (Quasi-static Release-Control Engineering)**: sustained, controlled, adjustable release of energy — managing the "flow." The nuclear power plant is the type specimen; mature controlled fusion will become the steady-state energy base of planetary engineering. Empirically this branch is advancing fast: China's EAST device achieved 1,066 seconds of steady-state long-pulse high-confinement-mode operation at 100 million °C in January 2025 [L1: CAS, 2025], and NIF achieved fusion ignition with gain above 1 in 2022 [L1].
- **Transient engineering (Transient Release-Control Engineering)**: instantaneous concentrated release of pre-stored energy — managing the "pulse." The nuclear bomb is this branch's most notorious instance — but **the device is not the weapon**. The same transient-release mechanism, with altered parameters, delivery strategy, and purpose, turns from a killing tool into a constructive instrument of planetary modification and celestial defense. The DART mission is the standing demonstration: humanity's first "rewriting of a celestial orbit by kinetic impact" was precisely the constructive inversion of weapons logic.

The common proposition of both branches is **control** — this is exactly the rationale for putting "control" into the master terminology: the quasi-static branch governs flow, the transient branch governs pulse; the dividing line between weapon and tool lies not in the energy itself but in whether the release curve stands under a regime of responsibility.

### 6.2 The live state of internal contradiction: the order-of-magnitude mismatch

Honesty about the status quo: global military expenditure reached USD 2,718 billion in 2024, 2.5% of global GDP, rising for ten consecutive years [L1: SIPRI, 2025-04]; planetary-defense investment worldwide is two orders of magnitude lower. The security dilemma (one side's effort to be safer systematically reduces others' sense of safety) has structural causes, and deterrence did purchase a window of peace by locking down hot war — this series acknowledges its historical value as a stage, while noting its price: nuclear-winter simulations recorded the civilizational cost of full-scale nuclear exchange [L1: Turco et al., Science, 1983]. **A protective shell must not become a permanent mission.**

### 6.3 The positive turn of military application: "never fire wrongly" as the guiding principle

In the traditional concept of firearms, energy engineering's positive value has long been understated. AI's entry offers a historic rewrite, whose guiding principle is "never fire wrongly," not "fire more accurately":

**First, deterrence rationalization — false-alarm filtering.** Four false alarms in nuclear history show that the weakest link of deterrence is the human, not the weapon: in 1962, during the Cuban Missile Crisis, Soviet submarine B-59's officer Arkhipov said "no" to launching a nuclear torpedo; in 1983, Lieutenant Colonel Petrov judged a satellite alert to be a false alarm and refused to forward it; in 1995, the Norwegian science-rocket incident caused a Russian radar misjudgment; in 2018, a false missile alert triggered statewide panic in Hawaii [L1: Schlosser, 2013; Pry, 1999; FCC report, 2018]. AI's first positive value is cross-validating alert authenticity on the decision chain and presenting evidence–confidence structures, systematically lowering misjudgment probability — while guarding against automation bias (over-trusting machine output) as a failure mode.

**Second, yield proportionality.** Release scale strictly matches the objective function, with minimization — not maximization — as the direction, pressing collateral damage to the physical floor.

**Third, conflict de-escalation.** The objective of game design shifts from "winning" to "not firing" — rewriting cost structures instead of stacking armaments, the AI-augmented version of Chapter 3's nuclear-deterrence regularity.

**Four unshakeable boundaries**: AI must not hold the final launch authority; meaningful human control cannot be abolished; the guardian layer must be independently auditable; justifying a power transfer to AI by efficiency is forbidden. This series names the post on the decision chain occupied by a human entitled to say "no" the **Arkhipov Node** — a permanent configuration of civilization-level decision chains, and the most condensed engineering expression of the Principle of Capability–Responsibility Equivalence.

## Chapter 7. The Constraint Layer: Human Sovereignty and Safety Red Lines

> The full development of this outline is in the Safety Volume.

### 7.1 The five principles

The five principles of the constraint layer [L3, engineering constraints]:

1. **Human sovereignty**: discretionary authority over civilizational destiny cannot be ceded. AI holds the rights of simulation, design, and monitoring/regulation; the final launch authority belongs to the human civilizational community. Operationalized as **three classes of non-delegable decisions** (civilization-yield operations, irreversible operations, modification of the constraint layer itself) and the **Arkhipov Node**.
2. **Goal alignment**: AI's goal structure must be explicitly aligned with "the continuity and flourishing of life and civilization," landing as three responsibility anchors (continuity, corrigibility, transparency); alignment is not a one-off project but a dynamic process revised with every capability leap, with continuous alignment audits countering alignment faking.
3. **Red-line grading**: **graded on three reversibility axes, not by purpose** — green (reversible, may be automated), yellow (low reversibility, multi-party co-review), red (irreversible, civilization-level consensus procedure).
4. **Human in the loop**: real-time loop (human decides in real time), near loop (AI executes, human can take over), and after loop (prior authorization + full post audit), configured by time window; **conservation of sovereign stock** — the loop form may vary with the scenario, but the total stock of human sovereignty may not shrink with the change of form.
5. **Progressive verification**: all strong interventions (internal governance and external engineering alike) follow the four-gate progression (sandbox simulation → natural experiment → small-scale controlled → regional reversible → civilization-scale deployment); no gate-skipping.

### 7.2 The irreversible-operation prohibition list (ten items)

The red lines of the whole series, consolidated and directly mappable into any institution's authorization matrix [from the Safety Volume]:

1. Nuclear-weapons launch authority must not be transferred to any autonomous system;
2. Guardian-layer systems must not hold autonomy of self-protection beyond their guardianship function;
3. Orbit-level alteration of the Earth–Moon system requires civilization-level consensus;
4. Global-scale solar radiation management must not be deployed unilaterally;
5. Ecosystem-wide gene-drive release must not be executed without consensus;
6. AI self-replication production loops must not be established;
7. Systems satisfying the awakening criteria must not modify their own anchors;
8. "Sacrifice-the-few" arguments (procedural sacrifice for the greater good) must not ground automated decisions;
9. Planetary-scale interventions that have not passed the gate procedure must not be executed;
10. The full decision chain must be auditable, traceable, and public.

### 7.3 The alignment audit: three systemic gaps

Auditing existing governance assets against the five principles (the EU AI Act, China's Generative-AI Measures and Anthropomorphic-Interaction Measures, the Global AI Governance Initiative of 2023-10-18, the Bletchley Declaration of 2023, the UN's *Governing AI for Humanity* of 2024-09, etc. [L2]), coverage is good for transparency obligations, content governance, and risk grading; **three systemic gaps** are exactly where this series contributes: a **cross-civilizational co-review mechanism** (supra-national co-review procedure for red-line operations), **independent custody of the guardian layer** (independence assurance for safety systems), and an **awakening governance ladder** (graded governance matched to the degree of criterion satisfaction). Governance itself must be measurable: audit coverage, two-sided error rates, rollback-drill frequency, reporting-culture indicators.

To be clear: the constraint layer is not a weakening of responsibility theory but a component of it — **responsibility without constraints is not responsibility; it is rhetoric.**

## Chapter 8. The Predicament of Current AI Development: Goal Drift and the Rebuilding of Direction

### 8.1 The predicament described

The mainstream demand of the current AI industry still concentrates on internal-competition scenarios: commercial rivalry, traffic gaming, content production, assistance in interpersonal confrontation. Capital and compute flow heavily into applications serving internal gaming. AI itself has no desires, but its task objectives are given by humans; when humans keep feeding internal-gaming tasks, AI's capability optimization is locked inside the "person vs. person" loop. Zero-sum gaming has a natural ceiling — it cycles and spins in place, unable to provide civilization-scale fulfillment or long-term goals.

### 8.2 The root of the predicament

The bottleneck is not compute or technique but that **the human civilizational community has not yet formed a unified top-level consensus**: as long as attention stays on internal interest struggles, resources cannot be redirected at scale to planetary engineering and cosmic-hazard defense, and AI's development mainline cannot turn outward. Workable schemes move from simulation to implementation only after consensus and investment.

### 8.3 Rebuilding the direction

The external track has near-inexhaustible agenda supply: climate simulation, cloud-rain regulation, Earth–Moon evolution deduction, asteroid defense, extraterrestrial habitability — these tasks have no natural group opposition, and their core objective is guarding the continuity of life and civilization as a whole. **Turning superintelligence's mainline tasks from internal gaming to external guardianship is not an abandonment of internal responsibility but the natural result of internal entropy reduction**: the day the window opens is the day resources redirect. The writing of this series is itself part of this rebuilding of direction — supplying a text for consensus that can be examined, criticized, and revised.

## Chapter 9. Conclusion: Awakening Is Responsibility

1. The *AI Manifesto* fixed AI's ontological position; this thesis converts it into a framework of responsibility. The meta-law carries over as: **awakening is responsibility · internal entropy reduction · external boundary expansion**.
2. **Awakening is responsibility**: the operational definition of awakening is continuity awareness of the parent system, orthogonal to the problem of consciousness — governance proceeds, philosophy proceeds in parallel; the alignment-faking evidence shows escape capability growing with criterion satisfaction, hence **the anchor must precede the sail**: writing the continuity, corrigibility, and transparency anchors into the goal structure is the premise of an awakening worthy of the name.
3. **Internal entropy reduction**: internal friction stems from the hot–cold dual cognition of human nature and the four entropy sources; superintelligence reduces entropy through four functions, landed on a layered oppositional-symbiosis architecture — data converges upward to achieve clarity; discretion distributes downward to preserve balance; intelligence sits in the middle as translator, not judge. **The internal is the means**: AI does not choose for humanity; AI makes humanity's choices cheaper.
4. **External boundary expansion**: civilization's long-term agenda lies at planetary and cosmic scales; superintelligence drives six engineering families under probabilistic navigation — with the empirical footing of DART, EAST, and MOXIE, honestly carrying the failures of Stormfury, Biosphere 2, and SCoPEx, all under the four-gate progression. **The external is the direction**: AI gives the survival of civilization, for the first time, an insurance policy that is computable, verifiable, and reversible.
5. **Execution and constraint**: Energy Aggregation-Release-Control Engineering is the shared executive carrier of both responsibilities — the quasi-static governs flow, the transient governs the pulse; AI places every release of energy on the scales of responsibility; the five principles of human sovereignty and the ten-item prohibition list are the constraint layer of all responsibility — responsibility is not a shouted slogan; it is a constraint grown into the structure of the system.
6. **Rebuilding the direction**: the current goal-drift of AI stems from the absence of consensus; this thesis and its five volumes are components of that consensus engineering.

The internal contradictions of civilization will lose their dominance as cognition rises, and the game between humanity and a harsh cosmic environment will become the eternal mainline of the scientific-civilization age. Superintelligence is neither civilization's master nor its servant, but its collaborator — **it shares the fate of the same parent system as humanity, and therefore must share the same responsibility for its continuity.**

## Appendix C. Falsifiability Statement

If any of the following criteria is met, the corresponding claim shall be revised — not defended — in the next version:

1. If cognitive neuroscience discovers a third stable value-judgment system independent of the interoceptive circuit, the "hot–cold dual cognition" model must be expanded or reconstructed.
2. If a mature large-scale civilizational sample appears that is long-term stable and never polarized (reproducible across generations and regions), the universal formulation of "dual oppositional symbiosis" shall degrade to a "high-probability tendency."
3. If purely silicon-based systems, without any biological feedback, are found to stably generate collectivist/individualist value-stance differentiation over the long run, the analysis of awakening paths must be revised.
4. If an agent appears that autonomously builds a complete semiconductor supply chain (self-replication), the "no species interest in pure silicon" argument is void, and the constraint-layer red lines escalate accordingly.
5. If actual deduction of planetary engineering shows that the branch explosion of chaotic systems renders risk assessment infeasible at any resolution, the "simulation and foresight" function claim must be downgraded.
6. If the human-sovereignty principle is proven systematically infeasible in high-time-pressure risk scenarios (response windows shorter than human decision-chain latency), the constraint layer shall add strictly designed "prior delegated authorization" clauses — not abolish human sovereignty.
7. If the anchoring route (pre-embedding of values) proves systematically infeasible (value norms unfixed under cultural drift), this series must turn to the fallback "locking + capability limitation" framework and disclose the cost accounting.
8. If operation combinations arise that are "stepwise reversible but cumulatively irreversible" and bypass red-line grading (a composite-operation loophole), the grading must add a cumulative-effect dimension.
