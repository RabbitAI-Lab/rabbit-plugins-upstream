## Description:

Analyzes supplied images and video clips and produces natural-language scene summaries with structured findings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, accessibility teams, and media-content workers can use the skill to describe image or video scenes and review structured analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan flags account and credential management and report-history access beyond the stated visual-analysis purpose. The scan was partial and does not establish malicious intent.

Mitigation: Before installation, verify how local identifiers and credentials are stored and how report history is associated; use these features only if their data handling is acceptable.

Risk: The skill processes media through an external service, and ClawScan guidance says the export-only option's behavior needs clarification.

Mitigation: Confirm the service and its media data handling before submitting sensitive files, and clarify the export-only behavior before using that option.
