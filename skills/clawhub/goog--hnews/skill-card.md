## Description: <br>
Convert Markdown-formatted Hacker News snapshots into a dark-themed, readable HTML news webpage without external dependencies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[goog](https://clawhub.ai/user/goog) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and editors use this skill to turn Markdown-formatted Hacker News or technology-news snapshots into local dark-themed HTML index pages for review or sharing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A chosen output path may overwrite an existing local file. <br>
Mitigation: Use a deliberate output path and review it before running the converter. <br>
Risk: Generated pages preserve links from the input Markdown and may send readers to untrusted sites. <br>
Mitigation: Use trusted Markdown sources and review generated HTML before opening or sharing it. <br>
