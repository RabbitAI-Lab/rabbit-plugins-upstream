## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to search for DJ services, check availability, and create DJ bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking creation can send customer name, email, phone number, selected DJ, date, and time to Lokuli. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the DJ, date, time, price or cancellation terms when available, and the exact contact details with the user. <br>
Risk: Search and availability results may not fully capture final provider suitability or booking terms. <br>
Mitigation: Review the selected provider and service details with the user before calling the booking tool. <br>
