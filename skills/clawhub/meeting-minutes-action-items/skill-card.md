## Description:

Turns meeting text and documents into evidence-linked minutes, decisions, action items, risks, and open questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[youteacher](https://clawhub.ai/user/youteacher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Meeting participants and teams use this skill to turn transcripts or meeting documents into summaries, decisions, action items, risks, and optional deliverables. It links key conclusions to source excerpts and flags missing owners or deadlines for confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting text or files may contain private business information and are uploaded to a hosted API.

Mitigation: Obtain permission before sending sensitive meetings to the service and review its data-handling terms.

Risk: The hosted service requires an API key and may charge for successful operations.

Mitigation: Keep the key confidential and confirm pricing before processing or exporting meetings.
