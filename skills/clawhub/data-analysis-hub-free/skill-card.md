## Description: <br>
Data Analysis Hub Free helps agents structure decision-oriented data analysis with statistical rigor checks, hypothesis testing guidance, A/B test interpretation, cohort analysis, and analysis pitfall identification. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, operations teams, and junior data analysts use this skill to turn datasets and experiment results into decision-ready analysis with effect sizes, uncertainty, limitations, and recommended next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad read, write, and execution access could expose or modify local data during analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with non-sensitive datasets unless commands and file writes are reviewed, and avoid exposing production credentials or unrestricted environment secrets. <br>
Risk: Generated statistical conclusions can mislead decisions if sample size, controls, uncertainty, or multiple comparisons are not checked. <br>
Mitigation: Require review of statistical assumptions, confidence intervals, effect sizes, limitations, and recommended actions before using results for business decisions. <br>
