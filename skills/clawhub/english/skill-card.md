## Description: <br>
Writes and corrects English that reads like a native wrote it - any variety, any register. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to revise, correct, and explain English across varieties, registers, professional contexts, learner errors, idioms, mechanics, pronunciation, and practice. It is intended for English coaching and editing rather than grammar-only fixes, translation, exam preparation, or long-form drafting in the user's personal voice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill keeps local learning and contact-context notes across sessions. <br>
Mitigation: Review or clear the local Clawic data files if long-term language, preference, or contact notes should not persist. <br>
Risk: User-provided drafts or transcripts may contain credentials or sensitive one-time values. <br>
Mitigation: Store pointers such as environment-variable, keychain, password-manager, or file references instead of saving credential values. <br>
