## Use Case:

External users and lottery-analysis enthusiasts use this skill to generate structured FC3D gameplay analysis, candidate-number reports, and rational-purchasing reminders. Outputs are advisory and require human judgment because lottery outcomes are random.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on lottery-number suggestions or treat statistical filtering as a reliable way to win.

Mitigation: State that lottery outcomes are random, outputs are reference-only, and the skill is not financial advice.

Risk: Users may act on candidate numbers without considering gambling cost or local lottery rules.

Mitigation: Require human review and keep rational-purchasing, legal-channel, and no-guarantee reminders with the generated analysis.
