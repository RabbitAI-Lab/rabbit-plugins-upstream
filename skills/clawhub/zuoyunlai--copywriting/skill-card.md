Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may include unverified claims, scarcity statements, testimonials, prices, or performance numbers.

Mitigation: Verify claims, offers, testimonials, prices, and performance numbers against authoritative business records before publishing.

Risk: Persuasive copy can overstate urgency or benefits if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review final copy for accuracy, compliance, and brand tone before using it in customer-facing materials.
