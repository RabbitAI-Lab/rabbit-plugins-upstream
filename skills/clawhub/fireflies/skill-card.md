## Description: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and operators use this skill to query Fireflies meeting records for transcripts, summaries, action items, participant details, contacts, active meetings, and analytics. Meeting transcripts, recordings, participant emails, contacts, analytics, and share links should be treated as confidential and used only within approved consent and data-handling policies. <br>
