## Description:

Monthly content performance and output-analysis skill. Aggregates published content, the content calendar, channel-effect data, product-line coverage, and knowledge-base health; outputs a monthly report and next-cycle optimization suggestions. Does not auto-modify the schedule or trigger writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand-side content marketing teams use this skill to review monthly published output, schedule variance, available channel performance, product coverage, and knowledge-base health, then prepare next-month recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product classifications and related coverage or priority conclusions may be uncertain when the authoritative product map is unavailable.

Mitigation: Confirm product classifications and conclusions against the correct map when the authoritative product map is unavailable.
