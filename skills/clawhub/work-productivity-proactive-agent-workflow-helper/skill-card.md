## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The helper may activate on generic productivity or task-planning requests because its trigger wording is broad.

Mitigation: Review and narrow invocation triggers when deploying it in environments where it should only handle explicit proactive-agent workflow design or skill-hardening requests.
