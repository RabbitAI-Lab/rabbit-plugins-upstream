## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad implicit activation language and may appear during general productivity or task-planning requests.

Mitigation: Before installation or deployment, narrow or disable implicit activation if users do not want this helper invoked for broad productivity requests.

Risk: The skill produces workflow, analysis, checklist, code, shell command, or configuration guidance that could be incorrect or mismatched to the user's environment.

Mitigation: Review generated artifacts before use and validate them against the stated success criteria, constraints, and local environment.
