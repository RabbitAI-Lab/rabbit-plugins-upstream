## Use Case:

External developers, maintainers, skill authors, and AI-agent users use this skill to shape proactive-agent workflow requests into local-hardware-friendly plans, checklists, analysis, implementation changes, and validation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger words and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate for unrelated productivity or task requests.

Mitigation: Narrow or disable implicit invocation in deployments that need predictable routing, and review activation examples before enabling the skill.
