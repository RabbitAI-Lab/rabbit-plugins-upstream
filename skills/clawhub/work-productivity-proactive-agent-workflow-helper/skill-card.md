## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn proactive-agent workflow demand into practical plans, checklists, implementation support, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to structure productivity and proactive-agent workflow tasks into an actionable plan, artifact, checklist, analysis, code change, or decision aid. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for generic productivity or bug-fix requests because implicit invocation is enabled and trigger wording is broad. <br>
Mitigation: Review whether broad workflow assistance is desired before installing; narrow trigger terms or disable implicit invocation when tighter activation is needed. <br>
Risk: Workflow or implementation guidance could be incomplete or mismatched if the user's constraints are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm material constraints, state assumptions, and validate the produced artifact against the user's success criteria before use. <br>
