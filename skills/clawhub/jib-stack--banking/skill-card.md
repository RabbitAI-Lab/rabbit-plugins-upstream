## Description:

Manage retail and business banking workflows with payment operations, account controls, reconciliation, fraud response, and compliant communication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Banking operations teams use this skill to classify onboarding, payment, reconciliation, fraud, dispute, and compliance requests; check controls before proposing transfer steps; structure incident response; and draft clear customer updates. It provides operational guidance and templates rather than executing transactions or giving legal determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
