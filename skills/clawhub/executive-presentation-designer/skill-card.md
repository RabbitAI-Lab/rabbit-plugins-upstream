## Description:

Create or redesign PowerPoint, slide decks, keynote-style presentations, pitch decks, reports, and visual narratives into premium, modern, executive-ready decks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and presentation authors use this skill to redesign slide decks into executive-ready narratives with clearer story structure, visual hierarchy, layout discipline, and presentation polish.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deck changes may not match the user's brand standards or intended executive tone.

Mitigation: Review the redesigned deck for brand fit, typography, color use, and stakeholder expectations before delivery.

Risk: Presentation outputs may preserve or introduce factual inaccuracies, readability problems, overflow, or weak chart interpretation.

Mitigation: Verify source facts and render or preview slides to inspect contrast, alignment, overflow, sharpness, and chart readability.
