## Description:

Helps users prepare Chinese patent office-action responses by drafting a response statement, amended claims, and an amendment comparison table in Word.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users preparing a response to a Chinese patent office action use the skill to review the notice and application, choose a response strategy, confirm proposed claim amendments, and produce Word drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent documents are sent to PatentMax for external processing and may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Confirm external processing is acceptable before providing documents, especially for confidential material.

Risk: The security evidence flags unsafe download paths and credential handling for review before installation.

Mitigation: Prefer a corrected version that confines downloads, restricts authenticated requests to approved HTTPS destinations, and securely stores keys.
