## Description:

Helps draft responses to Chinese patent office actions, including a response statement, amended claims, and an amendment comparison table.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent applicants and their advisers use this skill to prepare a Chinese patent office-action response from the full office action, claims, and specification. Users review the proposed strategy and amended claims before the final response is drafted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese patent applications only)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Full office actions, claims, specifications, and cited-reference material are sent to PatentMax/ip930.com and may contain unpublished or client-sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review confidentiality, client consent, and data-handling requirements before submitting any materials to the service.

Risk: The skill can use or create an API key and stores local task and key files under ~/.patentmax.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and task files, and restrict access to the machine or account running the skill.

Risk: A submitted task can incur a ¥15 charge.

Mitigation: Confirm the fee and obtain explicit user approval before submission; resume an existing task rather than submitting it again.

Risk: A generated patent response may contain errors or unsuitable claim amendments.

Mitigation: Have the user review the strategy and claims, and obtain patent-professional review before filing.
