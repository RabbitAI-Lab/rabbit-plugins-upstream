## Description:

Drafts Chinese patent office-action responses, revised claims, and amendment comparison tables as Word documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External patent applicants and patent professionals use the skill to prepare responses to Chinese patent office actions from the complete notice and application documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated files may be written outside the selected output folder.

Mitigation: Run the skill in a restricted workspace and inspect output paths before sharing generated files.

Risk: Credential handling protections need improvement, and patent materials are uploaded for processing.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing API keys in chat, use a restricted workspace, and do not submit confidential patent materials unless authorized for upload.
