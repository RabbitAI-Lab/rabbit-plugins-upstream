## 8. Governance Model

### 8.1 Governance Structure

| Layer | Body | Members | Cadence | Responsibility |
|-------|------|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Strategic decision** | Digital Transformation Steering Committee | CEO/GM, CIO/CDO, CFO, business VPs | Quarterly | Strategy approval, major investment decisions, cross-unit coordination |
| **Management execution** | Digital Transformation Office (DTO) | CDO (head), CTO, business-line PMOs | Bi-weekly | Program mgmt, resource coordination, progress tracking, risk mgmt |
| **Tech review** | Technology Architecture Committee | CTO, architects, external experts | Monthly | Tech selection approval, architecture review, tech risk |
| **Data governance** | Data Governance Committee | CDO, business data owners, data stewards | Monthly | Data standards, quality, sharing, security |
| **Business interface** | Department digital liaisons | Designated liaisons | Monthly | Requirement collection, rollout support, feedback |
| **Change enablement** | Change Management Committee | COO, HRD, change agents | Bi-weekly | Change comms, resistance resolution, culture |

### 8.2 Decision Flows

**Major decision (>€5M investment or impact on core business):**

```
DTO proposal → Tech Architecture Committee review → CDO sign-off → Steering Committee review → CEO/GM approval
```

**General decision (€0.5M–5M):**

```
DTO proposal → Tech Architecture Committee review → CDO approval
```

**Day-to-day decision (<€0.5M):**

```
DTO internal review → CDO on record
```
