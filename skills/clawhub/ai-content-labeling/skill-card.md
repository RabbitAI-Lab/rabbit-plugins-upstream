## Description:

A practical compliance playbook and offline toolkit for deciding, applying, and auditing AI-generated content labels under China's AI-generated synthetic content labeling rules and EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content operations, product, compliance, and legal teams use this skill to determine whether AI-generated content needs labeling, choose explicit and implicit labeling practices across content types, compare China and EU obligations, and produce checklists or metadata templates with a local helper script.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements may change after the package's stated 2026-08-27 review baseline.

Mitigation: Verify current official requirements and obtain appropriate legal review before using the guidance for business decisions.

Risk: The local helper script uses rule matching and checklists, so outputs can miss context-specific legal or platform obligations.

Mitigation: Use the output as a compliance aid and review results against the referenced source modules and applicable official requirements.
