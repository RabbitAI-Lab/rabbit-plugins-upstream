## Description:

应用题数学建模教练帮助初中学生把数学应用题中的文字条件提炼为数量关系和方程，重点训练识别量、说关系、翻译成等式。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and learning agents use this skill to coach Chinese-language middle-school math word problems when the student cannot translate text into equations. The skill focuses on guided questioning, hint ladders, modeling patterns, and transfer practice rather than solving equations after they are already set up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with China-specific crisis referral resources that should be localized before use outside mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis referral content is specific to mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts and youth-support resources before using the skill in another region.

Risk: Learning records, cross-skill sharing, or parent-facing sharing can expose student profile information.

Mitigation: Respect the student's and guardian's consent choices and preserve the skill's view, correct, delete, pause, export, and sharing controls.

Risk: The skill could reduce learning value if it gives a full equation before the student attempts the modeling step.

Mitigation: Keep the hint ladder active: ask for quantities first, then verbal relationships, then translation into an equation, escalating only when the student remains blocked.
