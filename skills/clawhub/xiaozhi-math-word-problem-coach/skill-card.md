## Description:

初中数学应用题的"文字→方程"专项：只处理列不出式子这一步。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External students and education-focused agents use this skill to coach junior-high learners through Chinese math word problems when they are stuck translating written conditions into equations. It guides learners through identifying quantities, stating relationships in plain language, and converting those relationships into algebraic equalities without solving the equation for them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records and profile sharing can expose sensitive learning data, especially for minors.

Mitigation: Review parent or guardian expectations and the skill's memory, sharing, delete, correction, and export controls before deployment.

Risk: The crisis-response material is oriented to mainland China and may provide the wrong emergency channels elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts outside mainland China and ask for the user's country or region before giving region-specific crisis resources.

Risk: A word-problem coach can be misused for live exams or quizzes.

Mitigation: Apply the artifact's test-taking rule: do not explain the active exam item, and offer to review it after the test.
