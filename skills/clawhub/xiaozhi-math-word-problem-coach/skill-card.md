## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students through identifying quantities, explaining relationships, and translating math word problems into equations or other appropriate models without solving the problems for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language coach to practice turning middle- and high-school word problems into mathematical models through guided questions. It focuses on setting up equations, functions, sequences, geometry, statistics, or probability models rather than subsequent calculation or full solutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Designed for mainland China's Chinese-language K–12 curriculum; use elsewhere requires localizing curriculum, minors' data practices, and emergency contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records about minors may be shared across skills or shown to guardians against a student's preferences.

Mitigation: Review profile and cross-skill sharing controls before use; honor pause, deletion, and sharing restrictions.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and emergency contacts, and confirm the student's region before providing local crisis resources.

Risk: A generated mathematical model or practice question may contain errors or bypass the student's own attempt.

Mitigation: Check model assumptions and generated questions, and use the documented graduated hints rather than immediately supplying an answer.
