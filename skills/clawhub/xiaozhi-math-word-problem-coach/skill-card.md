## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students from junior secondary school through the first year of senior secondary school to translate math word problems into equations or function models through stepwise questioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to identify quantities, explain their relationships, and build equations or first-year senior secondary function models without having the original problem solved for them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or summaries may be shared beyond the intended audience.

Mitigation: Configure profile memory and cross-skill or parent sharing deliberately; honor student privacy controls and required guardian consent.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and school conventions may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Before deployment outside mainland China, localize emergency referrals and review curriculum alignment and minor-consent requirements.

Risk: Incorrect generated practice problems could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check generated problems for a valid, unique solution and appropriate grade level before presenting them.
