## Description:

Reviews resume structure and evidence against an optional job description, asks for missing facts, supports evidence-based mock interviews, and exports user-confirmed content to DOCX, HTML, or PDF.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yottameta](https://clawhub.ai/user/yottameta)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to review resume structure and evidence, compare a resume with a target job description, practice a single-round mock interview, and prepare a confirmed resume for export. Users provide any missing facts; the skill does not submit applications or predict hiring outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
