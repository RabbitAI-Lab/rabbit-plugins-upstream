## Description:

Enables agents to search Statista and retrieve chart data, survey questions, and market indicators through an OOMOL-connected Statista account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to search Statista statistics, Consumer Insights survey questions, and Market Insights indicators, then retrieve statistic chart data and metadata through connected account access.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup instructions include running a remote installer directly in a shell.

Mitigation: Review the setup step before installing; prefer verified official documentation, a trusted package manager, or an inspected installer, and avoid elevated privileges unless independently assessed as safe.
