## Description:

Research short-form video markets across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels using Virlo data on niches, trends, creators, hashtags, sounds, and hooks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and researchers use this skill to find viral content, vet creators, compare short-form video trends, and monitor niches over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid lookups and recurring monitoring can consume a prepaid Virlo balance.

Mitigation: Set a budget and confirm estimated costs before paid calls or recurring setup; check the balance and monitor subsequent charges.

Risk: Changes to monitoring or tracked resources can persist or delete remote state.

Mitigation: Confirm management changes with the user, and prefer pausing or updating a resource over deleting it.

Risk: Creator lists, campaign plans, or URLs submitted for research may be shared with Virlo.

Mitigation: Do not submit private or confidential inputs unless the user has approved sharing them with Virlo.

Risk: The skill requires a Virlo API key tied to a prepaid account.

Mitigation: Configure the key through the agent environment and do not paste it into chat or research requests.
