## Description:

Helps agents research short-form video trends, creators, hashtags, sounds, and viral content across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels using Virlo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Creators, marketers, and research teams use this skill to discover successful short-form content, assess creators, and monitor niche trends across social platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid API calls and recurring monitors or tracking can consume prepaid credits over time.

Mitigation: Show estimated costs and schedule, obtain explicit approval before spending or enabling recurring activity, and review active resources and balance regularly.

Risk: Changing or deleting monitors and tracking can alter ongoing research or remove resources.

Mitigation: Summarize the specific resource and proposed change, then confirm with the user before modifying or deleting it.

Risk: The Virlo API key grants access to the account and its prepaid balance.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated key where possible, configure it privately, and never paste it into chat or reports.
