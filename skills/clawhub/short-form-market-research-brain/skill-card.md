Short-form video market research via the Virlo API for viral niche research, trend tracking, creator vetting, hashtag intelligence, and sound intelligence across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, agencies, and developers use this skill to research short-form video niches, discover rising creators, monitor trends, evaluate hashtags and sounds, and generate market intelligence from Virlo API data. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, keywords, creator handles, video URLs, and tracking targets are sent to Virlo as a third-party paid research service. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when this data sharing is acceptable, and avoid sending sensitive or private information unless approved for Virlo processing. <br>
Risk: Paid API calls, recurring monitor runs, tracking cycles, deep-dive lookups, and Data Intelligence add-ons can consume prepaid Virlo credits. <br>
Mitigation: Check balance and pricing before use, and explicitly confirm charged, recurring, or higher-depth actions before running them. <br>
Risk: Recurring monitors, autopilot proposal application, cadence changes, and delete or stop actions can alter ongoing research workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm user intent before creating monitors, applying proposals, changing cadence, enabling autopilot, or deleting/stopping tracked resources. <br>
