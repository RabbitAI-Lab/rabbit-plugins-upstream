## Description:

Helps agents research short-form video niches, trends, creators, hashtags, sounds, and hooks across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels using Virlo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[virlo-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/virlo-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, creators, and research teams use this skill to analyze short-form video markets, compare creator performance, discover viral patterns, and monitor niches over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research requests may transmit topics to Virlo and use a key with access to the account balance.

Mitigation: Review what will be sent and configure VIRLO_API_KEY securely before enabling the skill.

Risk: Recurring monitoring, creator tracking, and optional deep-history or intelligence features can incur charges, including charges beyond a user's original request.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval for each billable feature and recurring schedule; check the account balance after starting recurring work.

Risk: Updates and deletions can change or permanently remove saved monitoring resources.

Mitigation: Confirm state-changing calls first and prefer pausing agents or tracking over deletion.
