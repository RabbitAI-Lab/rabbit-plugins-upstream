## Description:

Helps agents research short-form video niches, creators, trends, hashtags, sounds, and hooks across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels using Virlo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[virlo-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/virlo-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content strategists, marketers, and researchers use this skill to analyze short-form video performance, discover creators and emerging trends, and monitor niches over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A stored Virlo API key tied to a prepaid balance can be used to initiate paid searches and recurring monitoring without mandatory confirmation.

Mitigation: Before each paid or recurring action, state the estimated charge, recurrence, and affected resource; wait for user approval and monitor the balance and auto top-up settings.

Risk: Active monitoring jobs can continue to incur charges, and deletion may be difficult to undo.

Mitigation: Review active jobs regularly; identify the exact resource and obtain approval before changing it, and prefer pausing over deleting when unsure.
