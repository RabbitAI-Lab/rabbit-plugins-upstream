## Description:

长篇网文规划与写作，支持结构讨论、大纲与细纲规划，并在用户明确要求后撰写章节。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External authors use this skill to plan and write long-form Chinese web fiction, from story direction and settings through outlines, chapters, and revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional outline format converter may lose identifiers from older projects.

Mitigation: Back up the writing workspace before converting outlines and review the converted files.
