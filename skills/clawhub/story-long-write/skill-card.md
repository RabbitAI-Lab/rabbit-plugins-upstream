## Description:

Helps writers plan, manage, draft, revise, and quality-check long-form Chinese web fiction projects from premise and outline through chapter prose.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers and writing-support agents use this skill to structure long-form web-novel projects, generate outlines and chapters, maintain continuity files, and run local quality checks for style, repetition, punctuation, outline copying, and word-count targets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source-work beat adaptation guidance may encourage copying protected expression or overly close adaptation of copyrighted works.

Mitigation: Use public-domain or properly licensed benchmark material, extract general genre conventions only, and apply human copyright review before publishing generated story content.

Risk: The workflow can create and update many files across a story workspace.

Mitigation: Run it in a scoped project directory, keep version control or backups, and review file changes before accepting or publishing outputs.

Risk: Quality checks are heuristic and may miss prose issues or flag intentional stylistic choices.

Mitigation: Treat checker output as review evidence and keep a human editorial pass for style, continuity, originality, and platform suitability.
