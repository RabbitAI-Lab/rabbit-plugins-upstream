## Description:

规划并协助创作长篇网络小说，从选题、设定和大纲推进到用户明确要求的章节写作。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Novelists and writing teams use the skill to plan long-form online fiction, develop settings and outlines, and draft or revise requested chapters. It supports both planning-only work and explicitly requested prose writing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cleanup behavior may remove working directories or generated views.

Mitigation: Review the target workspace and keep backups before running write or revision workflows.

Risk: Author-preference records can persist locally beyond an individual session.

Mitigation: Review what the skill records and use its documented forget flow for preferences you no longer want retained.
