## Description:

Helps writers plan long-form serialized fiction, build outlines, and draft or revise chapters when requested.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers use this skill to develop a serialized novel from story concept through chapter outlines and drafts, or to revise existing chapters while maintaining continuity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create or change outlines, chapters, and local continuity records.

Mitigation: Use it in a writing workspace where those edits are expected, and review changes to story files.

Risk: Author preferences and story continuity can persist in local project state.

Mitigation: Periodically review the .story and 追踪 directories and remove stored state you no longer want.
