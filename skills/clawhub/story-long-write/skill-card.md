## Description:

Helps authors plan and write long-form Chinese web fiction, from story structure and outlines to requested chapters and revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Authors and writing teams use this skill to develop story concepts, plan long-form fiction, draft chapters on request, and revise manuscripts while tracking continuity and writing preferences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Writing workflows can create or edit story files and store local continuity state and explicit writing preferences.

Mitigation: Use only in workspaces where these changes are acceptable; review project paths before author-memory or chapter-commit actions.
