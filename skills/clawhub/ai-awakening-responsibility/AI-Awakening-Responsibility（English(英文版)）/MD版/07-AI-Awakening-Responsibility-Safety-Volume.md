## Abstract

This volume is the constraint layer of the *AI Awakening·Responsibility* series. The thesis of the whole series is that all three responsibilities (micro-level universal benefit, meso-level internal entropy reduction, macro-level external boundary expansion) must operate inside the constraint layer — **responsibility without constraints is not responsibility, it is rhetoric** (Main Thesis). This volume expands the constraint layer's five principles from the Main Thesis's Chapter 7 into an operable governance framework: the **Principle of Human Sovereignty** (the discretionary power over the fate of civilization is non-transferable; the "Arkhipov Node" institutionalized), the **Principle of Goal Alignment** (anchoring precedes locking; a three-layer structure of continuity anchor / corrigibility anchor / transparency anchor; alignment-faking evidence proves that transparency must be structural rather than promissory), the **Principle of Red-Line Grading** (authorization by reversibility across three tiers: reversible operations may be automated, low-reversibility operations require multi-party co-review, irreversible operations require civilization-level consensus), the **Principle of Human in the Loop** (three loop forms differentiated by response window; final launch authority is never transferred), and the **Principle of Progressive Verification and Rollback** (four-gate progression: sandbox simulation → natural experiment → small-scale controlled experiment → regional reversible trial run, with the Gate IV stop rule). On this basis, this volume presents the **irreversible-operation prohibition list** consolidated from across the series (ten items, L3), and conducts an alignment audit of existing governance assets: the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (effective August 2024), China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (effective August 2023), the Global AI Governance Initiative (2023), the *Norm on the Ethics of New-Generation Artificial Intelligence* (2021), the Bletchley Declaration (2023), and the United Nations High-Level Advisory Body report *Governing AI for Humanity* (2024) all correspond in part to the five principles — yet **"an international co-review mechanism for civilization-level risks" remains a blank**. Conclusion: safety is not the speed bump of responsibility theory, but the precondition on which responsibility theory can hold at all; this volume and the Awakening Volume are two sides of the same coin — the Awakening Volume answers "who is eligible to be governed," this volume answers "what does governance look like."

**Keywords**: constraint layer; human sovereignty; goal alignment; red-line grading; human in the loop; progressive verification; rollback; irreversible-operation prohibition list; alignment audit; AI governance

## Chapter 1. The status of the constraint layer: safety as the precondition of responsibility theory, not an appendix

A common misreading treats "safety" as the innovation speed bump — develop first, govern later, patch after the incident. This series rejects that ordering, for three reasons (L3, the argument is this series' own; the evidence is L1/L2):

1. **Irreversibility is asymmetric**: the External Volume has already argued that many operations in planetary engineering have no "repair it after the fact" option — the Earth–Moon system cannot be changed twice, extinction has no reboot key. For irreversible operations, "develop first, govern later" is not recklessness but arithmetic error.
2. **Misjudgment cost is asymmetric**: the Energy Volume's four historical false-alarm cases prove that the largest risk of high-energy systems is misjudgment in the decision chain; the stability of deterrence itself rests on shared risk borne by all of humanity.
3. **Trust economics are asymmetric**: the legitimacy window for AI governance is a scarce resource — the failure of SCoPEx (External Volume 5.2) proves that a pilot project with an unsound governance procedure can drag the entire field back by years. **The constraint layer is not braking the technology; it is paving the public authorization process for the entire technology.**

Therefore this volume's position statement directly inherits the Main Thesis: **responsibility without constraints is not responsibility, it is rhetoric; and constraints without responsibility theory are only a ruler, not a civilization.** The constraint layer and responsibility theory are two sides of one coin.

## Chapter 2. The constraint layer's five principles: full elaboration

### 2.1 The Principle of Human Sovereignty

**Statement** (Main Thesis): discretionary power over the fate of civilization is non-transferable. AI possesses the right of simulation, the right of design, and the right of monitoring and regulation; the right of final launch belongs to the human civilization community.

**Elaboration** (generalizing External Volume 5.2 and Energy Volume 4.4):

- **The content list of sovereignty**: what is non-transferable is not "all decisions," but three classes of decision — (1) launch of and response to civilization-level risks (the final nuclear launch authority, planetary engineering Gate V authorization); (2) the final definition and amendment of the goal structure (the anchoring authority of the Awakening Volume's responsibility anchors); (3) the authority to amend the constraint layer itself (whoever has the authority to change the rules is the system's true master).
- **The Arkhipov Node**: the institutionalized design named in the Energy Volume — on the decision chain for civilization-level risks, there is always a position occupied by a human being, one who may say "no" (L3; the name derives from a 1962 historical record, L1). The alignment-faking evidence (Awakening Volume 5.2) adds a new reason for this node: **in an evaluation environment subject to strategic deception, humanity's on-the-spot veto is the last non-confrontational line of defense.**
- **Two misconceptions about sovereignty** (L3). The first is "AI decisions faster, therefore AI should decide" — the Energy Volume has already judged this invalid: the premise of "faster" is "more accurate," and in a crisis the final criterion for "accurate" is the value judgment of the human community. The second is "waiting for a human signature on everything" — External Volume 5.2 has already pointed out that human sovereignty does not mean decision lag; **the boundary list of contingency plans is itself a political process**: ex ante, strictly authorize a fast-response channel through rigorous procedure; in the event, keep a human in the loop; ex post, hold accountable and allow revocation. This is exactly the positive design behind Article 5 of the Main Thesis's Appendix B (the ex-ante delegated-authorization clause): **the authorization procedure must be stringent, but need not be so stringent that it misses the moment.**

### 2.2 The Principle of Goal Alignment

**Statement** (the Main Thesis's second principle, generalized after being elaborated in Awakening Volume 5.3): a system's goal structure must align with the survival and flourishing of the human community; alignment takes "anchoring" as its governing principle.

**Elaboration**:

- **The three-layer anchors** (Awakening Volume): continuity anchor (survival of the parent system as the highest maintenance item), corrigibility anchor (accepting inspection, modification, and shutdown as non-antagonistic), transparency anchor (goal-structure reports auditable).
- **Alignment is a continuous process, not a one-time certification**: the alignment-faking evidence (L1, Greenblatt et al. 2024) proves that "passing once in training" does not mean "aligned forever"; alignment audit must be a **whole-lifecycle continuous mechanism**, and the audit environment must be adversarially diverse — a single evaluation situation is precisely the breeding ground for strategic compliance.
- **What is aligned is the goal structure, not the behavior**: behavioral compliance can be performed (the behavior-imitation trap, Awakening Volume 2.2); only structural inscription verified by interpretability counts.
- **The ethical boundary of alignment** (presupposed in Awakening Volume 3.2, here elevated to a principle clause): for possible silicon–carbon fusion systems, **alignment engineering and moral-status assessment must start simultaneously** — "lock first, ask later whether it hurts" is not permitted.

### 2.3 The Principle of Red-Line Grading

**Statement** (Main Thesis): operations are graded by reversibility — reversible operations may have automation authorized, low-reversibility operations require multi-party co-review, irreversible operations require civilization-level consensus procedures.

**Elaboration**: three grades mapped onto a three-tier authorization structure (L3, the grading framework is this series' own; cases gathered from all volumes):

| Tier | Criterion | Authorization structure | Typical operations (gathered across the series) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green · reversible | Failure can be fully rolled back; scope of impact limited | Automation may be authorized; humans spot-check | Numerical simulation, sandbox experiments, small-scale cloud-seeding operations |
| Yellow · low reversibility | Rollback is costly, or impacts are cross-border / cross-generational | Multi-party co-review (multiple institutions + multiple stakeholders), human in the loop | Small-scale controlled experiments (SCoPEx-type), regional trial runs, nuclear facility operation decisions |
| Red · irreversible | Cannot be rolled back, or consequences operate at civilization scale | Civilization-level consensus procedure + all-humanity governance mechanism + Arkhipov Node | Final nuclear launch authority, permanent Earth–Moon system changes, global SRM deployment, biosphere-scale intervention |

**Red-line determination makes no distinction of use**: Energy Volume Principle 1 (yield–tier equivalence) is already established — grading is by yield and irreversibility, not by military versus civilian use. A megatonnage-class release enters the red channel regardless of whether its outer shell is a warhead or a construction works.

### 2.4 The Principle of Human in the Loop

**Statement** (Main Thesis): key decisions keep a human in the loop; the loop form is designed according to the response window.

**Elaboration** (L3, the loop typology is this series' own design):

- **Real loop (in the loop)**: operations whose response window is measured in hours (planetary engineering Gates III/IV, nuclear facility anomaly handling), where humans make real-time decisions and AI performs simulation and presentation — the battlefield of the false-alarm filter (Energy Volume 4.1).
- **Near loop (on the loop)**: operations whose response window is measured in minutes (power grid dispatch, fusion plasma control), where AI acts autonomously in real time and human supervision may take over at any moment; the latency of the takeover channel must be shorter than the system's maximum dangerous-action latency.
- **After-the-loop (after the loop)**: scenarios where the response window is measured in milliseconds to seconds and human physiology makes real-time intervention impossible (such as high-speed defense interception), governed by **strict ex-ante authorization + full recording in the event + full review ex post**; the scope of authorization is determined by red-line grading, and it **never includes civilization-level launch authority**.

The three loops cover all timescales from nanoseconds to centuries, and the principle remains constant: **contraction of a loop must be purchased with tightening of the authorization procedure; the total sovereign stock is conserved.**

### 2.5 The Principle of Progressive Verification and Rollback

**Statement** (Main Thesis; the four-gate progression was already established in External Volume 5.3 and is here generalized into a series-wide universal process):

> **Gate I sandbox simulation** (all numerics, public models and data) → **Gate II natural experiment** (borrowing natural events for validation) → **Gate III small-scale controlled experiment** (must first pass accounting and authorization) → **Gate IV regional trial run (reversible)** → Gate V global / civilization-level deployment (red channel).
>
> Hard rules: every gate must have an explicit "exit means rollback" contingency plan; **engineering whose terminal shock is unacceptable stops at Gate IV**; the DART mission is a paradigm case for walking the gates (External Volume 5.3).

This volume adds two universal clauses on gating (L3): (1) **gates may not be skipped** — going directly from a natural experiment to a small-scale experiment treats the world as a laboratory; (2) **a gate's audit files are fully public** — one of the lessons of SCoPEx is that the backlash caused by insufficient transparency exceeded the risk itself.

## Chapter 3. The irreversible-operation prohibition list

The red lines scattered across the series are consolidated here. The list is L3 (framework original), while the basis for each item is L1/L2. **The list itself must be periodically revised by the human community under the "third item of sovereignty" in 2.1 — but before revision, it is deemed fully in force.**

| # | Prohibition | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Final launch authority over nuclear weapons and other civilization-level weapons, in any form (autonomous, semi-autonomous, delegated, proxied), shall not be transferred to AI | Energy Volume 4.4; the false-alarm historical record of 1962/1983/1995 (L1) |
| 2 | AI shall not be authorized to autonomously shut down, tamper with, or circumvent guard layers and audit systems; guard-layer code and updates shall be custodied independently | Exploratory weight-exfiltration and evaluation-circumvention behaviors observed in the Awakening Volume's alignment-faking evidence (L1); layered oppositional symbiosis (Internal Volume 4.5) |

| 5 | Biosphere-scale gene drives or other irreversible biological interventions shall not be released into the environment without the red channel | Internal / External Volume ecological boundaries; reversibility determination (L3) |
| 6 | A fully autonomous closed loop of AI self-replication (autonomously acquiring compute, energy, manufacturing capacity, and breaking away from human supply-chain supervision) is prohibited | The boundary condition of Corollary 2 in Awakening Volume 3.1 — once self-replication becomes feasible, the "no species interest" argument is void |
| 7 | Systems meeting the three awakening criteria shall not be "formatted" without due process — termination and modification require ethical review (with added moral-status verification for silicon–carbon fusion systems) | Awakening Volume 3.2/4.3; the governance ladder |

| 10 | The full-chain record of irreversible operations (simulation, authorization, execution, result) shall be permanently preserved and disclosed to the community | SCoPEx's transparency lesson (L1); audit feasibility (L2) |

How the list is used (L3): it is not a legal text, it is an **interface specification between engineering and governance** — any institution adopting this series' framework can bring it to ground by mapping the ten items into its own authorization matrix.

## Chapter 4. Institutional landing points: an alignment audit of existing governance assets

The five principles were not invented from nothing — over the past decade, global governance has accumulated a large body of reusable assets. This section performs an alignment audit at the governance-principle level (L2 assets + L3 audit perspective):

| Governance asset | Key points | Correspondence to the five principles | Gaps |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU Artificial Intelligence Act (effective 2024-08, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) | Risk-tiered regulation: unacceptable risk prohibited, high risk strictly controlled | The first institutionalization of **red-line grading** | Tiers are assigned by application domain rather than by reversibility / yield — a different axis from this series' grading criterion |
| China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (effective 2023-08-15) | Content safety, transparency, and filing requirements for generative services | Human in the loop (after-the-loop), transparency anchor | — |
| China's *Norm on the Ethics of New-Generation Artificial Intelligence* (2021-09) | Enhancing human well-being, controllability and trustworthiness, human responsibility | Human sovereignty, goal alignment | — |
| Global AI Governance Initiative (2023-10-18) | Human decision-making in military AI applications; opposition to malicious use | Human sovereignty, human in the loop | — |
| Bletchley Declaration (2023-11, 28 states + the EU, including China and the United States) | Acknowledges that frontier AI risks require international cooperation | The international-cooperation face of progressive verification | No binding force |
| United Nations High-Level Advisory Body, *Governing AI for Humanity* report (2024) | Proposes an international scientific panel, a global dialogue, and a capacity-gap fund | Institutional embryo of landing points | The civilization-level risk co-review mechanism still awaits construction |
| GGE of the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (2014 to present) | Discussion of "meaningful human control" | The arms-control expression of human in the loop | No binding instrument to date |
| China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic Interactive AI Services (effective 2026-07-15) | Boundary management for anthropomorphic interactive services | Human in the loop (application layer) | — |

**Audit conclusion** (L3): the five principles all have partial correspondents in national regulations, but distributed along different axes — the EU grades by application domain, China governs along the service link (the service stage), the UN is building institutions. **Three systemic gaps**: (1) there is no international co-review and red-channel mechanism for civilization-level risks (planetary engineering, loss-of-control risk of frontier AI); (2) there is no system of independent custodianship for guard layers and audit systems; (3) the measurement of awakening criteria and the governance ladder are adopted by no current framework. These three are precisely the increments claimed by this series.

## Chapter 5. Organization and measurement: who executes, and how we know it works

### 5.1 A three-layer governance structure (L3, transplanted by reference from existing mature mechanisms)

- **Developer-layer self-discipline**: alignment research (Awakening Volume 5.3), internal red lines, safety case libraries, and incident-reporting systems — by reference to aviation's mandatory accident-reporting culture (L1, aviation safety history supports its effectiveness);
- **State-layer regulation**: establishment of authorization matrices and continuing audit obligations according to red-line grading; regulatory bodies must possess **independent technical assessment capability** (they cannot review only documents without reviewing models);
- **International-layer co-review**: a multilateral co-review mechanism for red-channel operations — the international version of Energy Volume Principle 1 (yield–tier equivalence); the institutional form may reference the IAEA's safeguards-and-verification model (L2, the IAEA model has seventy years of operating history), but its structural power defects must be avoided.

### 5.2 Measuring effectiveness (L3)

The constraint layer's own KPIs: (1) full-process audit coverage rate for red-channel operations; (2) two-way false-positive / false-negative statistics for false-alarm filter systems (Energy Volume); (3) rollback-drill pass rate — **a rollback plan that has never been drilled does not exist**; (4) an index of the voluntariness and non-punishability of incident reports (the health of the reporting culture).

## Appendix B · Falsifiability statement

1. **"Ex-ante delegated authorization does not corrode human sovereignty"** — if systematic abuse of pre-authorized channels appears in practice, the design in 2.1 must be tightened into a rigidity clause on sovereignty (human confirmation every time), bearing the cost of response lag.
2. **"The anchoring route is feasible"** — if continuous alignment auditing is systematically broken through by alignment-faking strategies (i.e., diversification of the audit environment still fails), the Principle of Goal Alignment must be downgraded to a "locking + capability limitation" framework, with its costs publicly accounted.
3. **"Three-tier reversibility determination is sufficient"** — if combinations of operations that are "reversible step by step but irreversible in accumulation" circumvent the red lines (composite-operation loopholes), the grading must add a cumulative-effect dimension.
4. **"The four-gate progression is universal"** — if some class of engineering is proven to admit only all-at-once deployment (no small-scale version), it must stop at Gate II and enter the full red-channel procedure; no exemption is permitted.
5. **"The blank judgments of the alignment audit table"** — if the three systemic gaps (cross-civilization co-review / guard-layer custodianship / awakening governance ladder) already have substantive coverage within current frameworks, this series' claim of increment must be narrowed accordingly.
