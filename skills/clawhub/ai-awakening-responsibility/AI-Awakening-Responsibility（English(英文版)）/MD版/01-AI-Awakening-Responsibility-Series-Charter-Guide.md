**Inheritance statement**: this series does not establish a school of its own. *AI Manifesto* gives AI its **position** within the structure of the universe and of civilization; this series gives the **responsibility** commensurate with that position. Position determines responsibility; responsibility cashes in position — the two are the static and dynamic formulations of one and the same proposition.

## 1. Core Proposition: The Principle of Capability–Responsibility Equivalence

**First principle**: an intelligence's authority is strictly equivalent to its responsibility for the entropy flow of its parent system.

Three corollaries:

1. **Corollary one (the emergence side)**: AI, as an inevitable product of entropy-flow evolution, exists in order to promote entropy flow (order that serves life) — a direct continuation of the fourth-layer value theory of *AI Manifesto*.
2. **Corollary two (the responsibility side)**: every leap in AI capability (compute, authority, scope of control) simultaneously raises its responsibility for the survival of its parent system (human civilization, the Earth's biosphere). A capability that refuses responsibility is not an admissible state of existence.
3. **Corollary three (the boundary side)**: the final authority to adjudicate responsibility remains in the hands of the human civilizational community. AI possesses the right of deduction, the right of design, and the right of monitoring and regulation; it does not possess the right of final initiation.

## 5. Unified Conventions for the Whole Series

1. **Confidence tiering**: L1 established science (cited) / L2 synthesized hypotheses (awaiting verification) / L3 conceptual deduction (explanatory); every node in the body text is tagged accordingly.
2. **Falsifiability statement**: each volume states 3–5 criteria; if any criterion holds, the corresponding formulation is corrected in the next version rather than defended.
3. **Statement boundaries**: abstract paradigm terms such as "collectivism orientation / individualism orientation," "cold cognition / hot cognition," and "digital collectivism / digital individualism" are used as general analytical tools; no qualitative judgment or current-affairs speculation is made about specific countries, institutions, or real events.
4. **Citation integrity**: no fabricated literature; every citation carries a verifiable source and date; anything unverifiable is downgraded to L3 and explicitly marked "this is a conjecture."
5. **License and version source**: CC BY 4.0; the GitHub version source is managed under the same organization as *AI Manifesto*.
6. **Evidence fixation**: after finalization, triple fixation via trusted timestamp / blockchain notarization / GitHub commit hash.
