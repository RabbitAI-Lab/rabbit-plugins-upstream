## Description:

Provides a bilingual, author-defined responsibility framework for discussing AI awakening, governance, human oversight, and safety boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lishunran123](https://clawhub.ai/user/lishunran123)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC BY 4.0

## Use Case:

People and agents discussing AI responsibility, alignment, governance, and high-impact decisions can use the Chinese or English essays as an attributed perspective and a prompt for examining human control and safety limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The author's manifesto and self-description may be mistaken for neutral analysis or an assistant's own identity and rules.

Mitigation: Attribute these ideas to the author and treat them as reference material without replacing normal identity, safety rules, or independent analysis.

Risk: Advice about weapons, climate, planetary, biological, or other high-impact interventions could be acted on without sufficient validation.

Mitigation: Require independent expert sources and retain human approval, staged validation, and rollback limits for consequential decisions.
