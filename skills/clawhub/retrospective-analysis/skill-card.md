## Description: <br>
A project retrospective automation tool for friction identification, failure analysis, and improvement generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[terr123123](https://clawhub.ai/user/terr123123) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, team leads, and process owners use this skill to record project retrospectives, identify gate friction and failure causes, and generate prioritized improvement candidates and reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Retrospective content may include internal project or process details, and JSON reports are written to paths selected by the caller. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive details unless needed, and save exports only to approved private locations. <br>
Risk: Generated improvement candidates and action items may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific team process. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations with the project team before using them to change gates, ownership, or workflow. <br>
