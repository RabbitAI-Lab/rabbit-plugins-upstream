## Description:

Summarizes Sendly email delivery, engagement, campaign results, bounce history, and sending-limit usage in a read-only report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sendly users and teams use this skill to review email delivery, opens, bounces, campaign performance, individual email history, and daily or monthly sending limits without changing their campaigns or messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports can expose recipient addresses and delivery history to the agent.

Mitigation: Use only in workspaces where the agent is authorized to access the relevant email-performance data.
