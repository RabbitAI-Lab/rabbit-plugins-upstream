## Description: <br>
Helps agents audit and improve post-signup onboarding, user activation, first-run experience, and time-to-value for products where users sign up but do not reach value. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product, growth, UX, and customer-facing teams use this skill to diagnose onboarding drop-offs and design activation flows, onboarding checklists, empty states, re-engagement tactics, experiment plans, and measurement frameworks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read product-marketing context files in the workspace to tailor onboarding advice. <br>
Mitigation: Review the relevant product-marketing context before use and avoid placing sensitive or unnecessary information in those files. <br>
Risk: The trigger wording may overlap with general retention, re-engagement, or signup optimization questions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the task is about post-signup onboarding and activation; use related signup or email skills for registration forms or ongoing email sequences. <br>
