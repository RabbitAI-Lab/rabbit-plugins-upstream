## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review updates can overwrite an existing local growth plan file.

Mitigation: Confirm the target file before saving updates and keep a copy when the plan contains important business or personal data.

Risk: Annual growth plans may contain sensitive business or personal planning data.

Mitigation: Store plans locally and include only the details needed for planning and review.
