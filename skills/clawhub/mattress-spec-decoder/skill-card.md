## Description:

Decodes mattress specifications, estimates firmness and durability for a sleeper, compares options, and explains warranty terms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Mattress shoppers and their agents use this skill to assess materials, choose a firmness range, compare beds for one or two sleepers, and understand warranty coverage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Firmness guidance could be mistaken for medical advice.

Mitigation: Use it as consumer shopping guidance; consult a clinician about sleep disorders or health concerns.

Risk: Estimated lifespan and warranty coverage may differ from a product's actual terms.

Mitigation: Verify material specifications and warranty clauses with the manufacturer before buying or filing a claim.
