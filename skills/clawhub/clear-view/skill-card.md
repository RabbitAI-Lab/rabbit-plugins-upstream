## Description:

Use when the user wants to quickly understand a conversation, article, paper, tutorial, product brief, long thread, or messy discussion: what is going on, who said what, topics, positions, agreements/disagreements, or a clearer structural explanation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0xcjl](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xcjl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People reviewing long conversations, articles, tutorials, or product briefs can use the skill to identify the main topic, structure, speakers, disagreements, and open questions, then receive a concise text explanation and an offline HTML infographic.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated HTML infographic can reproduce sensitive content from the supplied source material.

Mitigation: Review the infographic before sharing it.
