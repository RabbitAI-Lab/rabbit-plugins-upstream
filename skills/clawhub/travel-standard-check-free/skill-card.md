## Description:

差旅费报销与标准表逐项核对（逐行复算、合计勾稽、重复与空缺检测），每条结论引用原文。本免费版执行引擎声明的免费检查项。触发词包括 差旅费标准与报销核对、差旅费报销与标准表对不上。

## Use Case:

Finance and reimbursement staff use this skill to locally check copied Chinese travel expense reimbursement tables against the table's stated standards, including row arithmetic, totals, duplicate rows, blanks, and negative amounts. It is an arithmetic consistency helper and not a complete finance approval control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the free checker as a complete finance approval control.

Mitigation: Use it only as an arithmetic consistency helper and separately review meal-subsidy formulas, trip dates, accommodation and transport over-standard checks, and approval requirements.

Risk: The free version intentionally withholds several checks that may matter for reimbursement review.

Mitigation: Read the listed checks_given and checks_withheld before relying on results, and do not interpret a clean result as full policy compliance.

Risk: A license conflict exists between server evidence and skill frontmatter.

Mitigation: Confirm the authoritative release license before publication or downstream distribution.
