## Description:

Skill especializada en guionización estratégica con los frameworks de FORMULA 100K para crear, estructurar, verificar y reescribir guiones de video para Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, content strategists, and marketing teams use this skill to turn video ideas into structured short-form scripts with hooks, viral-reference checks, rewrite passes, and a text-based neuro checklist before delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use web or connected social-data tools to research viral references, which can expose prompts, topics, handles, or business context as search terms.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal stories, confidential business strategy, private handles, or other information that should not be used in external lookups.

Risk: Generated scripts may be saved locally as drafts, which can retain private content or strategy details.

Mitigation: Review saved drafts, store them only in approved locations, and remove sensitive details before sharing or publishing.
