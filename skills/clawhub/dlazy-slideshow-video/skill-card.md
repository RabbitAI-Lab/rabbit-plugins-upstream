## Description:

Helps turn slides, photos, or documents into narrated slideshow videos with voiceover and transitions through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users can ask an agent to turn presentations, documents, or photos into narrated slideshow videos for explainers, reports, or training.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Attached documents and prompts are sent to the dLazy service.

Mitigation: Upload documents only when you are comfortable sharing them with dLazy; avoid confidential materials unless authorized.

Risk: Using the skill requires a dLazy API key and an npm CLI.

Mitigation: Keep the API key private and consider the pinned npx option instead of a persistent global install.
