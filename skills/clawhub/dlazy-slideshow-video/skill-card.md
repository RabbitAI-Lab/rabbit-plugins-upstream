## Description:

Turns slides or documents into narrated slideshow videos with voiceover and transitions through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn presentations, reports, and other documents into narrated explainer or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Attach only files you are comfortable uploading to dLazy.

Risk: Logging in stores an API key locally for the CLI.

Mitigation: Protect local CLI credentials and rotate or revoke the key if exposed.
