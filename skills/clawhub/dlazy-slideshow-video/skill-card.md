## Description:

Turn slides or documents into narrated slideshow videos with voiceover and transitions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can turn presentations and documents into narrated explainer, report, courseware, or training videos, and continue work in project sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy services for processing.

Mitigation: Only attach documents you are authorized to share, and follow the organization's guidance for sensitive information.

Risk: The workflow uses organization credits and a saved API key.

Mitigation: Consider credit use before starting jobs, protect the saved key, and rotate or revoke it if exposed.
