## Description:

Turns slides or documents into narrated slideshow videos with voiceover and transitions using the dLazy service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users turn presentations, documents, and reports into narrated explainer, training, or slideshow videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Upload only documents you are authorized to share and review the service terms before use.

Risk: The CLI may store an API key locally and a global installation persists on the device.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and use the pinned npx option instead of a global installation if persistence is unwanted.
