## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to plan RWA landing paths for physical or data assets, including tokenization readiness checks, staged work planning, and China-to-offshore compliance boundary review. It is educational planning support and not legal, investment, or regulatory advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with China/Hong Kong RWA compliance assumptions that require local legal and regulatory review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: RWA planning touches finance, tokenization, and cross-border compliance, so outputs may be mistaken for legal or investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational planning support and require qualified legal, regulatory, and financial review before any real project action.

Risk: The skill can produce jurisdiction-specific process guidance whose assumptions may become stale as tokenization and sandbox rules change.

Mitigation: Validate every compliance boundary, filing requirement, and offshore issuance step against current local law and regulator guidance.

Risk: A generated roadmap may understate asset-quality, audit, or liquidity-mismatch concerns if user-provided project facts are incomplete.

Mitigation: Require explicit review of the six-construct eligibility checklist, cash-flow durability, ownership evidence, auditability, and liquidity promises before proceeding.
