## Description: <br>
Build and run a local-first digital persona that learns from a user's interviews, documents, writing samples, and AI chat exports to answer questions and draft messages in that user's voice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use Alter to build a private digital persona from their own answers, documents, writing samples, and chat exports. They use it to talk with the persona, correct its memory, monitor progress, and draft messages or emails in their own voice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process highly personal writings, documents, chat exports, and voice material for persistent persona memory. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when comfortable using that material for persona memory, and use the available review, correction, and delete controls for sensitive data. <br>
Risk: Configured model or voice providers may receive prompts or spoken-reply text. <br>
Mitigation: Review the configured model and voice providers before use, and leave voice disabled unless that data flow is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Persona outputs could be mistaken for the real person. <br>
Mitigation: Keep disclosure that Alter is a digital persona, never claim to be the person, and require explicit consent before voice cloning. <br>
