## Description:

Alter builds and runs a local digital persona of the user by interviewing them, learning from uploaded materials, and drafting or answering in their voice and style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jasonquantum](https://clawhub.ai/user/jasonquantum)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use Alter to create a personal digital persona that can answer questions, preserve preferences, and draft messages in their voice. The skill is intended for ongoing persona building from interviews, corrections, documents, writing samples, voice memos, and AI chat exports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to collect and index sensitive personal material, including voice recordings, documents, emails, writing samples, and AI chat exports.

Mitigation: Review data handling, retention, deletion, and model-provider sharing before installing or using the skill.

Risk: Privacy, deletion, and local-only claims depend on companion services that are not included in the artifact evidence.

Mitigation: Inspect or obtain the installer and Alter service implementation from a trusted source before relying on those claims.
