## Description: <br>
Instagram Seo helps agents research Instagram keyword clusters, draft search-first captions, and advise profile, alt text, on-screen text, and spoken keyword signals for Instagram search visibility. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media operators, creators, and marketing agents use this skill to optimize Instagram posts or Reels for search by producing keyworded captions and concrete guidance for native profile and visual metadata updates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated captions or profile recommendations may be public-facing and could be misleading, off-brand, or poorly matched to the actual visual content. <br>
Mitigation: Review captions, keyword claims, hashtag labels, and visual-match guidance before posting; do not fabricate search volume or rankings. <br>
Risk: The skill advises native Instagram settings and metadata that it cannot directly inspect or change. <br>
Mitigation: Apply profile fields, alt text, category, auto-captions, and Instagram Insights checks manually in Instagram or through approved publishing workflows. <br>
