## Description:

Helps users assess creative direction, reframe unclear problems, and restart when proposals are repeatedly rejected or become increasingly complex.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mapleleo-hub](https://clawhub.ai/user/mapleleo-hub)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, writers, product teams, researchers, and planners can use this skill to clarify evaluation criteria, uncover assumptions, assess constraints, and decide whether to refine or reframe a creative direction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:
