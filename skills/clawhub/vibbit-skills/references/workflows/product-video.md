# Product video

Use real product facts, intended audience, placement/ratio, supported claims, existing media, and requested count. Reuse complete user material without mandatory library or competitor research.

When direction is needed, choose one understandable message and demonstrate it through the product's real use. For example, portability can be shown through actual carrying/storage without invented weight comparisons. Preserve an existing script or prompt.

Choose the shortest workable route:
1. A single demonstration with adequate references: [video generation](../capabilities/generation/video-generation.md).
2. Presenter-led content: [talking heads](oral-broadcast.md).
3. Exact charts/text, animation, or a complete template: [programmatic video](../capabilities/generation/programmatic-video.md); use [B-roll](broll-production.md) for an insert.
4. Multiple real shots with precise assembly: [hybrid production](hybrid-video.md). Specific Vibbit packaging needs the cloud path; ordinary captions/branding can be local.
5. Platform project/product linking or batch-version persistence: public contracts are unavailable; deliver media/planning without claiming those saves.

Check downstream dependencies before generating paid intermediates. Do not invent efficacy, offers, prices, or promises, and do not turn one requested video into variants.

[Review](../capabilities/analysis/video-review.md) product appearance, readable demonstration, supported claims, and overlays that might obscure it. Revise the affected script/asset/layer while preserving other usable results.

For Meta planning, select only needed [methods](../platforms/meta/index.md): VB-02 audience, VB-03 hook, VB-04 script, or VB-06 product-image direction. A Brand Master is not mandatory when current facts suffice.
