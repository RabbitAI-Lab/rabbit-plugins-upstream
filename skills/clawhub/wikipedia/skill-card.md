## Description:

Connects agents to Wikipedia and Wikimedia for article search, summaries, media discovery, and article history and quality checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[evanfoglia](https://clawhub.ai/user/evanfoglia)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, writers, and developers use this skill to look up Wikipedia articles, explore related topics and media, and review citations, edits, and maintenance notices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, article titles, and editor usernames or IP addresses are sent to Wikimedia services.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential topics, secrets, and private identifiers in lookup requests.

Risk: Unpinned dependencies can change after installation.

Mitigation: Pin and review dependencies before deployment.
