## Description:

Provides Wikipedia and Wikimedia lookups through an MCP server for article research, media discovery, and current or historical topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[evanfoglia](https://clawhub.ai/user/evanfoglia)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, writers, and developers use the skill to find and inspect Wikipedia articles, citations, editor activity, and Wikimedia media through an agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, article titles, usernames or IP addresses, dates, and coordinates may be sent to Wikimedia-operated services.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive queries and share only the information needed for a lookup.

Risk: Public editor and IP contribution data can still be sensitive.

Mitigation: Handle contribution details carefully and avoid unnecessary redistribution.

Risk: Unpinned dependencies can make installations less reproducible.

Mitigation: Pin dependencies when reproducible installs are required.
