## Description:

Gives agents read-only access to Wikipedia and Wikimedia content for article research, discovery, media, and public edit-history checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[evanfoglia](https://clawhub.ai/user/evanfoglia)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Researchers, writers, and developers use this skill to find and summarize Wikipedia articles, discover related content and media, and check public article sources and edit activity across supported languages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, article titles, coordinates, and contributor lookups are sent to public Wikimedia APIs.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting private or sensitive queries when using these tools.

Risk: Public username and IP contribution data can be used to profile editors.

Mitigation: Use contribution lookups for public-data audits, not private or sensitive profiling.
