## Description: <br>
上市公司护城河深度解剖。对任意上市公司/股票代码/上传资料进行六维竞争壁垒分析（资源、技术、客户认证、规模、产业链卡口、成长空间），输出结构化的资产底牌研究报告。适用于个股研究、财报分析、研报解读、调研纪要分析等场景。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and investment researchers use this skill to analyze listed companies, stock tickers, or uploaded financial materials and produce structured moat research across resource, technology, customer certification, scale, supply-chain position, and growth dimensions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill produces investment-research style analysis that could be mistaken for personalized financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance only and review conclusions against current public filings, market data, and qualified financial advice before making investment decisions. <br>
Risk: Uploaded financial reports, research notes, or proprietary documents may contain confidential or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid uploading sensitive, personal, or proprietary materials unless they are necessary for the requested analysis and appropriate for processing in the target environment. <br>
