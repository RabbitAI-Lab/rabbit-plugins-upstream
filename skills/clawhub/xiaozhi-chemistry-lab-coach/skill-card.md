## Use Case:

学生和教师可用它复习初中化学必做实验及高中入门实验，理解实验设计、安全规范和常见失分原因；它不替代教师指导下的实际操作。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；在其他地区使用前须本地化紧急求助渠道、课程要求和未成年人同意规则。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may try hazardous experiments at home or rely on coaching during an active laboratory incident.

Mitigation: Do not provide at-home hazardous procedures; keep higher-risk experiments teacher-led, and direct students to stop and alert a present teacher if an incident is underway.

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, or reminders may expose minors' learning data without consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, sharing, and reminders opt-in; confirm consent and each proposed record update, and honor pause, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Mainland-China emergency contacts and minor-consent assumptions may not suit other regions.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location before giving emergency contacts and localize emergency resources and consent requirements before deploying elsewhere.
