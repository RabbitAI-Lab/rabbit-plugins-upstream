## Description:

面向中文初高中化学学习，帮助学生理解实验装置、操作顺序、现象与结论、检验与除杂，并准备实验操作考试。

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use the skill to reason through chemistry experiments and prepare for practical assessments; teachers can use its explanations to support supervised learning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese K12 curriculum; localize emergency referrals)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Experiment guidance could be mistaken for permission to perform hazardous chemistry at home.

Mitigation: Keep practical work under teacher supervision; provide principles rather than at-home procedures, quantities or mixtures, and treat high-risk experiments as demonstrations.

Risk: Learner records and reminders may expose minors' educational information if shared without consent.

Mitigation: Review memory, cross-skill sharing, parent-sharing and reminder consent settings before use; keep records and handoffs off unless expressly authorized.
