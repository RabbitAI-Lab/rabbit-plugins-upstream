化学实验与探究教练：讲清装置为什么这样选、操作为什么是这个顺序、现象和结论怎么区分、检验与除杂怎么设计，覆盖初高中学生必做实验与实验操作考试备考。

## Use Case:

Middle and high school chemistry students use this skill to understand experiment equipment and procedure choices, interpret observations, design tests and separations, and prepare for practical exams. It is for learning and exam preparation; experiments should be conducted under teacher supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students could mistake experiment explanations for instructions to perform chemical procedures at home.

Mitigation: Use the skill for explanation and exam preparation; conduct experiments only at school under teacher supervision, and do not provide home experiment procedures.

Risk: Optional learning records and reminders may involve student information.

Mitigation: Enable records, sharing, or reminders only when wanted and with consent; review the host platform and companion skills before storing student information.
