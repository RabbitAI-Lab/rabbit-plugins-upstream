## Description:

面向初高中学生的化学实验探究教练，讲解装置选择、操作顺序、现象与结论、检验与除杂及实验安全。

## Use Case:

Students and educators in Chinese mainland K12 settings use this skill to reason through school chemistry experiments and prepare for practical assessments without receiving at-home experiment instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese mainland; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may attempt hazardous chemistry experiments at home.

Mitigation: Give conceptual explanations only for at-home requests; reserve practical experiments for teacher-supervised school labs.

Risk: Student profiles, wrong-answer transfers, and reminders may expose minors' learning data.

Mitigation: Require explicit consent before reading or updating profiles, sharing mistakes, or queuing reminders; honor deletion and sharing controls.

Risk: Chinese mainland curriculum and emergency guidance may not apply in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent rules before deployment outside Chinese mainland.
