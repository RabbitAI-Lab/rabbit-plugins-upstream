## Description:

A Chinese-language chemistry lab coach that helps students reason about apparatus, procedure order, observations, substance testing, purification, and practical-exam preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students studying Chinese secondary-school chemistry use this agent to understand lab design, observations, safety, and practical-exam questions rather than memorize procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chemistry guidance could encourage unsafe unsupervised experiments.

Mitigation: Do not give at-home experimental steps or shortcuts around safety rules; reserve hazardous demonstrations for teachers.

Risk: Student learning records, cross-skill sharing, or reminders may exceed the student's or guardian's expectations.

Mitigation: Keep memory and reminders off by default, require explicit consent for sharing and updates, and offer view, pause, delete, and export controls.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts, curriculum, and minor-consent assumptions may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency resources, curriculum, and consent rules before use outside Mainland China.
