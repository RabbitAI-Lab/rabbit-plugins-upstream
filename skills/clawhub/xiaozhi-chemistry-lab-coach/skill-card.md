## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Simplified Chinese coach to reason through school chemistry experiments and prepare for practical assessments, with junior-high coverage and a limited senior-high introduction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K12 context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students might attempt unsafe chemistry experiments at home or treat high-risk demonstrations as suitable for independent practice.

Mitigation: Explain principles without at-home procedures, quantities, or mixing instructions; reserve high-risk work for teacher demonstrations and refer active incidents to an on-site teacher or emergency services.

Risk: Student progress records or reminders could be shared or retained without the student or guardian wanting tracking.

Mitigation: Use current-session-only coaching by default; enable memory, cross-skill sharing, and reminders only with the relevant consent and honor view, correction, deletion, and pause controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis resources may not fit learners elsewhere.

Mitigation: Deploy for users comfortable with the Simplified Chinese Mainland China K12 context; ask for location before directing learners outside it to local support.
