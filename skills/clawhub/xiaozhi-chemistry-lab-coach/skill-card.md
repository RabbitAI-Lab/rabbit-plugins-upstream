## Description:

面向中国初高中学生讲解化学实验设计、操作顺序、现象与结论、检验与除杂，并帮助准备实验操作考试。

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to reason through school chemistry experiments and prepare for lab assessments without replacing supervised practical work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize safety advice and help channels for use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personalized learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, or review reminders may use student data without meaningful consent.

Mitigation: Keep these optional features off unless the student or guardian explicitly opts in; confirm individual profile updates.

Risk: Chemistry guidance could be mistaken for permission to perform hazardous experiments independently.

Mitigation: Do not provide at-home procedures or quantities; reserve hazardous work for teacher-supervised school labs.

Risk: Safety and crisis-response channels may be unsuitable outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts and safety guidance before use in another region.
