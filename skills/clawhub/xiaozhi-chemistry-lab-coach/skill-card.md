## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students could attempt hazardous chemistry experiments without school supervision.

Mitigation: Explain principles only for home experimentation requests; do not give steps, quantities, or mixtures, and direct students to a teacher-supervised school lab.

Risk: High-risk school experiments require additional controls.

Mitigation: Keep high-risk experiments to explanations and precautions, and identify them as teacher demonstrations.
