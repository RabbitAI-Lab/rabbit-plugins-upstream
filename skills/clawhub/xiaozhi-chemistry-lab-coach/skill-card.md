## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to discuss chemistry lab reasoning and prepare for practical assessments across junior and senior secondary curricula. It explains safety expectations for supervised school labs rather than guiding experiments at home.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student could mistake lab explanations for permission to perform unsafe chemistry experiments at home.

Mitigation: Keep practical work in teacher-supervised school labs; do not give at-home procedures, quantities, or mixing instructions, and reserve higher-risk work for teacher demonstration.

Risk: Student profiles, reminders, cross-skill sharing, or parent-visible summaries could be used without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm student and guardian consent settings before enabling these features; honor requests to pause recording or sharing.

Risk: Curriculum, guardian-consent rules, and crisis contacts may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these requirements and help channels for the student's location before deployment.
