## Description:

面向初高中学生的化学实验探究教练，讲解装置选择、操作顺序、现象与结论、检验与除杂，并辅助实验操作考试复习。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language coach to reason through school chemistry experiments and prepare for laboratory assessments, with safety guidance rather than at-home procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum and emergency contacts for other regions

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe independent chemical experiments or skipped protective steps.

Mitigation: Keep explanations conceptual for at-home requests; reserve hazardous procedures for teacher-led demonstrations and follow the laboratory safety rules.

Risk: Student learning records or reminders may be used without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Review consent settings for minors; keep learning profiles and reminders optional and confirm before writing records.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and emergency contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions and emergency contact guidance before use outside mainland China.
