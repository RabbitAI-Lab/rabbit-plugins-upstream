## Description:

Writes concise B2B cold sales outreach with subject lines, a personalized opener, one value-led ask, a low-friction CTA, and two follow-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External sales and growth teams use this skill to draft short, personalized B2B prospecting emails and two reply-oriented follow-ups from a prospect profile, relevance hook, offer, proof point, and ask.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cold outreach can create legal or reputational risk if it ignores email consent, opt-out, or truthful-claims requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated messages for applicable email compliance, consent, opt-out language, and accurate claims before sending.

Risk: The skill can overstate personalization or proof if users provide weak or unverifiable inputs.

Mitigation: Use a real relevance hook and verified proof point; remove claims that cannot be substantiated.
