## Description:

Plans qualitative user research, creates interview and screener guides, synthesizes customer feedback, and supports optional handoff to AI-moderated interviews through User Intuition.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kevin6314](https://clawhub.ai/user/kevin6314)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Research and customer-insights teams use this skill to plan interviews and screeners or synthesize transcripts, notes, survey responses, reviews, and support tickets into themes. It can also prepare an optional handoff for AI-moderated interviews with real participants through User Intuition.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research material may not be authorized for sharing with an external interview service.

Mitigation: Upload only research material you are authorized to share with User Intuition.

Risk: Connected interview workflows may involve costs or field interviews.

Mitigation: Review workflow skills and costs before approving interviews.

Risk: Qualitative synthesis can misattribute or alter source quotes.

Mitigation: Quote only exact text from materials provided and identify the source for each quote.
