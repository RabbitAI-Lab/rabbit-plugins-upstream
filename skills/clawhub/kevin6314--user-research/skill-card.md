## Description:

Use when the user wants to plan qualitative research, market research, or user and customer interviews, write a discussion guide or screener, synthesize interview transcripts, call notes, or research notes into themes, understand why people buy, switch brands, or churn, test a concept, pack, or message, or run AI-moderated interviews with real people as a focus-group alternative, from shopper and usage and attitudes research for CPG brands and retailers to product research for tech teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, product teams, and brand or retail teams can use this skill to plan qualitative studies, create screeners and discussion guides, and synthesize supplied research materials into themes and evidence-backed findings. It can also route approved studies to User Intuition workflows for moderated interviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview findings can be mistaken for population-level evidence, and summaries can misrepresent source material.

Mitigation: Base synthesis only on supplied material, preserve exact quotations with source IDs, report prevalence as a share of those sources, and label findings directional when fewer than five sources are available.

Risk: Fielding interviews may incur cost or contact participants.

Mitigation: For panel studies, obtain a current estimate and explicit approval before launch; for customer interviews, approve the plan and exact participant batch before invitations are sent.
