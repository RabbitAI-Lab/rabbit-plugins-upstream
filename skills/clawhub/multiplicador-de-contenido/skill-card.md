## Description:

Spanish-language skill that turns a core script or content idea into five adapted content proposals with distinct formats, angles, hooks, structures, production levels, and estimated recording times.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, content teams, and creator-support agents use this skill to repurpose one script or idea into multiple ready-to-record content formats. It is especially suited to Spanish-language content workflows that need varied angles without starting from scratch each time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposals can reflect the wrong industry or audience if the user's rubro is unclear.

Mitigation: Identify the user's rubro from provided context or ask a brief clarifying question before drafting examples, hooks, or vocabulary.

Risk: The skill may save generated pieces under guiones/ and send a summary through Telegram in an OpenClaw setup.

Mitigation: Use it only where that file-saving and Telegram-summary behavior is expected, and avoid providing sensitive content unless the deployment is authorized for it.

Risk: Long videos or YouTube links may require transcription before useful repurposing.

Mitigation: Provide a transcript or concise source text instead of relying on video download or unsupported media processing.
