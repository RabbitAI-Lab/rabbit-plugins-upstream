## Description:

Helps creators fill and publish text, image, and short-video content across supported social platforms through the EasyPost CLI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Creators use this skill to prepare and distribute text, image, and video posts across supported social platforms. It provides a review-first workflow and instructions for direct publishing when explicitly requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The CLI and browser extension can act through logged-in social accounts, and their implementation is outside the supplied skill artifact.

Mitigation: Review CLI and extension permissions before connecting accounts, use the fill-and-review workflow by default, and authorize automatic posting only for intended content and platforms.
