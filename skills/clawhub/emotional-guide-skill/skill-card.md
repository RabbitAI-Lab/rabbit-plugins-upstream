## Description:

Provides warm, non-sycophantic emotional support and guided self-reflection while routing crisis, diagnosis, medication, and urgent safety concerns to real-world support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[baijian](https://clawhub.ai/user/baijian)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to talk through stress, anxiety, loneliness, grief, relationship or work difficulties, self-doubt, emotional overwhelm, hard decisions, and requests for comfort or perspective. It is intended for supportive reflection and low-intensity self-help suggestions, not diagnosis, treatment, medication advice, legal compliance, or emergency care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat supportive reflection as therapy, diagnosis, medication advice, or emergency care.

Mitigation: Disclose the AI support role, avoid diagnosis and medication recommendations, and route urgent safety concerns to local emergency services, qualified professionals, trusted people, or available human escalation.

Risk: Emotional-support interactions can encourage overdependence, excessive agreement, or isolation from real-world support.

Mitigation: Use non-sycophantic reflection, avoid exclusive or dependency-forming language, preserve user autonomy, and encourage connection with trusted people or professionals when appropriate.

Risk: Public emotional-support services may require jurisdiction-specific legal, mental-health, and data-protection review.

Mitigation: Treat the compliance reference as a product-design reminder only and obtain qualified review before public launch, material change, or commercialization in regulated regions.
