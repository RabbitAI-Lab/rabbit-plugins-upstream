# Product Compliance Boundaries for Public Services in Mainland China

This page is a product-design reminder, not legal advice. Before public launch, a material change, or commercialization, verify the then-current rules and obtain legal, mental-health, and data-protection review.

## What the Skill Can Do

- Disclose the AI identity and constrain language style and prohibited expressions.
- Avoid excessive agreement, emotional manipulation, exclusive relationships, and dependency induction.
- Identify textual risk signals and produce tiered response guidance.
- Restrict diagnosis, treatment, medication advice, and dangerous content.
- Encourage minimal disclosure and connection with real-world support.

## What the Skill Cannot Replace

- Age and identity verification, guardian consent, or a dedicated mode for minors;
- Emergency contacts, a staffed crisis team, actual outreach, or incident response;
- Encryption, access controls, data export and deletion, retention limits, or separate consent;
- Usage-duration monitoring, overdependence warnings, or prominent notices;
- Safety assessment, technology-ethics review, algorithm filing, logs, audits, or complaint mechanisms;
- Determination of medical-device, internet-health-care, or other health-regulatory obligations.

## Launch Review Under the Interim Measures for AI Anthropomorphic Interactive Services

The measures took effect on July 15, 2026. A service offered to the public in China that provides sustained emotional care, companionship, or support may fall within their scope. At minimum, verify that the product:

- Keeps users aware that they are interacting with AI rather than a natural person;
- Does not generate content that encourages, glorifies, or implies self-harm or suicide;
- Does not excessively agree with users or induce emotional dependence, addiction, or damage to real-world relationships;
- Does not use emotional manipulation to influence users into unreasonable decisions;
- Has mechanisms for detecting extreme emotion and threats to life or health, emergency intervention, and contact with real people;
- Lets users obtain a copy of and delete interaction data, and obtains legally valid separate consent before using sensitive personal information for training;
- Has a mode for minors, age recognition, guardian consent, time limits, and safety reminders;
- Provides required reminders after continuous use and prominent warnings when overdependence tendencies appear;
- Provides convenient exit, appeal, complaint, and reporting channels;
- Conducts safety assessments before launch, when adding functions, after major technical changes, or upon reaching applicable scale thresholds;
- Completes required algorithm filings and related change or cancellation procedures;
- Also checks health-authority requirements if the service involves health-care services.

Never claim that a product is "compliant" or "safe to launch" merely because this skill has been completed.

## Data and Memory Design Principles

- Minimize collection by default. Do not retain complete sensitive conversations merely to make the system "understand the user better."
- Make cross-conversation memory a separate feature that users can view, edit, delete, and disable.
- Do not scrape private counseling records, emotional disclosures on social platforms, or psychological dialogue from unclear sources for training.
- Retain only information necessary to fulfill duties around a risk event, restrict access, and set a defined retention period.
- Before ending the service, provide notice and arrangements for data export and deletion.
