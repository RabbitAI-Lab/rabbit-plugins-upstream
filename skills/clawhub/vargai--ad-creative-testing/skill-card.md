## Description:

Turns a winning ad and supplied performance information into a TikTok and Meta creative testing plan, with guidance for evaluating and iterating on concepts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and creative teams use the skill to plan distinct ad concepts, set test budgets and decision points, interpret supplied performance metrics, and organize creative names. It produces planning guidance and can use exported ad metrics when the user shares them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shared advertising metrics may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Share only the metrics needed to create or assess the test plan.

Risk: Production handoffs may involve costs from downstream skills.

Mitigation: Review the production skills and their costs before using a handoff.
