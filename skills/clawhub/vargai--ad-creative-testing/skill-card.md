## Description:

Creates a TikTok and Meta ad creative testing plan from a winning ad, including distinct concepts, test budgets, evaluation signals, and naming conventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and creative teams use this skill to turn a winning ad and, when available, exported metrics into a structured TikTok or Meta test plan. It helps identify what to iterate, pause, or scale based on creative and funnel signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
