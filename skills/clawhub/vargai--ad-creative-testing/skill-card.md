## Description:

Plans distinct TikTok and Meta ad creative tests from a winning ad, including concepts, budgets, evaluation timing, naming, and performance-based iteration or scaling guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Advertisers, creative strategists, and marketing teams use this skill to plan distinct ad tests on TikTok and Meta, set evaluation criteria, and decide whether to iterate or scale based on shared metrics.
