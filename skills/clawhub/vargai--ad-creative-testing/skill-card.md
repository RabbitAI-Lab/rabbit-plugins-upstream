## Description:

Turns a winning TikTok or Meta ad and its metrics into a creative testing plan with distinct concepts, budgets, naming, performance checks, and kill, iterate, or scale guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and creative strategists use this skill to plan and assess distinct TikTok and Meta ad tests based on a winning ad or shared campaign metrics. It helps select concepts, set test budgets and timing, interpret performance, and decide when to iterate or scale; it does not produce videos or set up ad accounts.
