## Description:

Creates a TikTok and Meta ad testing plan with distinct creative concepts, budgets, performance checks, and naming guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External advertisers and creative teams use the skill to plan distinct ad concepts, set test budgets and review points, interpret performance metrics, and decide when to iterate, stop, or scale a creative.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
