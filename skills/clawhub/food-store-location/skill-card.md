## Description:

Helps restaurant operators compare candidate storefronts using a structured site-evaluation checklist, cost framework, and on-site verification steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Restaurant operators and independent business owners use this skill to assess prospective locations, check utilities and permit requirements, estimate occupancy costs, and plan lease negotiations and site visits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified rent, foot traffic, or utility assumptions could distort a location decision.

Mitigation: Check rent, foot traffic, utilities, and safety conditions on site and with local sources before committing.

Risk: A checklist cannot establish permit eligibility or guarantee financial returns.

Mitigation: Confirm permits and legal requirements with local authorities and independently validate financial projections.

Risk: Sharing storefront or business details could expose unnecessary personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only the details needed for the assessment and remove personal information where possible.
