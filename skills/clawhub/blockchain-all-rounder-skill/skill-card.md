## Description:

Blockchain All-Rounder is a blockchain assistant for BTC, Ethereum, BSC, Solana, and major L2s that helps with market and on-chain analysis, wallet and contract tracking, DeFi strategy review, Solidity development, smart contract audit checklists, and security risk triage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to analyze crypto markets and on-chain activity, review wallet and contract risk, compare DeFi strategies, and draft or review Solidity contract code. It is intended for informational and technical assistance with explicit risk and compliance caveats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial and chain-state outputs may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a real-funds decision.

Mitigation: Independently verify prices, balances, contract behavior, and DeFi risks before acting with real funds, and do not treat outputs as investment advice.

Risk: Sharing wallet secrets or signing approvals with an assistant can expose assets.

Mitigation: Do not share private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, keystore files, or signing approvals with any assistant.

Risk: Generated Solidity code or audit findings may miss exploitable behavior.

Mitigation: Test thoroughly and obtain independent review before deploying contracts or strategies to mainnet.
