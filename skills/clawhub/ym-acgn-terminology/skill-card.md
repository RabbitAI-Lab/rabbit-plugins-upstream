## Description:

Translates Chinese ACGN jargon into plain language with origin, usage context, examples, and risk notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand Chinese ACGN slang, translate jargon for non-community audiences, and judge whether terms are appropriate in family, workplace, or social contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The bundled dictionary is current to its 2024-06 reference and does not track live slang changes.

Mitigation: Treat out-of-dictionary terms as contextual inferences and label them clearly as provisional.

Risk: Some ACGN terms can be inappropriate in workplace, family, or mixed-audience settings.

Mitigation: Use the skill's risk notes and safer alternative phrasing for high-risk terms before sharing explanations.
