## Description: <br>
Social Content Converter rewrites one article or script into Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili variants with titles, body copy, hashtags, cover suggestions, and posting-time guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, social media operators, and marketing teams use this skill to turn one source draft into platform-specific copy for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili. Generated posts should be reviewed manually before any publishing workflow is used. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill describes workflows that may publish through logged-in Douyin, Xiaohongshu, or Bilibili sessions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generation and publishing as separate steps, review every generated post manually, and allow logged-in publishing sessions only when publication is explicitly intended. <br>
