## Description:

Create blog publications and generate, iterate on, and publish articles on PostKing, either hosted or pushed to WordPress, Medium, or Substack.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bitsandtea](https://clawhub.ai/user/bitsandtea)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and marketing operators use this skill to manage PostKing blog publications, generate and revise articles, publish hosted posts, and push articles to connected WordPress, Medium, or Substack destinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide agents through publishing, scheduled publishing, external pushes, and deletion workflows for PostKing blog content.

Mitigation: Before delete, hosted publish, scheduled publish, or push to WordPress, Medium, or Substack, require the agent to restate the target article or publication and destination and obtain explicit approval.
