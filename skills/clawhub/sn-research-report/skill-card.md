## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research reports in medical, legal, policy, or investment contexts may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat generated structures and prose as drafting guidance, verify sources, and apply qualified review before relying on conclusions.

Risk: Report templates can organize unsupported or uncertain claims into authoritative-looking documents.

Mitigation: Require source checks and explicit certainty labels for key conclusions, especially in domain-specific reports.
