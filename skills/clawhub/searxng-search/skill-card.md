## Description:

Route web searches through local SearXNG, choose suitable engines for general, biomedical, technical, and current-news research, and periodically recheck engine health.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huipan1973](https://clawhub.ai/user/huipan1973)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and researchers use this skill to route web searches through a local SearXNG instance and choose sources suited to general, biomedical, technical, or current-news questions. They can also perform periodic spot-checks of selected engine availability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries sent to external providers may expose private or confidential information.

Mitigation: Remove sensitive details from queries and get approval before sending confidential content to external providers.
