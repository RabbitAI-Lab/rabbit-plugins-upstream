## Description:

闲鱼副业从零开始教练系统，帮助新手按阶段完成定位、选品、找货源、发布、客服、成交、发货、售后和复盘。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to coach first-time Xianyu sellers through a low-cost, compliance-oriented path from initial profile and product selection to first order, fulfillment, and review. It provides staged guidance, checklists, listing and customer-service advice, and risk-aware next steps rather than earnings guarantees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat coaching examples or revenue stages as guaranteed earnings.

Mitigation: Present the skill as business coaching, preserve the no-earnings-guarantee posture, and require users to validate demand, costs, platform fees, and local rules before acting.

Risk: Users may paste sensitive buyer or account information into chat while seeking fulfillment or customer-service help.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing buyer phone numbers, addresses, IDs, payment data, account credentials, or other private information with the agent.

Risk: Marketplace rules, fees, restricted categories, and local legal requirements can change after the artifact's references were last verified.

Mitigation: Verify current Xianyu rules, relevant legal requirements, and platform fees before publishing listings or following selling advice.
