## Description: <br>
Analyses CSAT, NPS, and CES survey results, computes scores, interprets survey verbatims, and turns customer satisfaction signals into prioritized actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Customer experience, product, and support teams use this skill to analyze NPS, CSAT, or CES results, understand drivers in survey comments, and produce an action-oriented voice-of-customer readout. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Survey verbatims may contain confidential customer information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential customer comments unless approved for the agent environment; anonymize or aggregate sensitive comments before analysis. <br>
