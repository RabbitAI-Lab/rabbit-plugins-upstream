## Description:

Remove dead air, pauses, and filler words from video and audio timelines with automatic music ducking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[brajendrak00068](https://clawhub.ai/user/brajendrak00068)

## Use Case:

Video editors, creators, and agents use this skill to tighten spoken-word audio or video by removing silence and filler words, balancing dialogue, and ducking background music. The skill provides setup guidance and a tool workflow for editing a project timeline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
