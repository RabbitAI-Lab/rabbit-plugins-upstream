## Description:

Remove silences, dead air, pauses, and filler words (um, uh) on word boundaries, clean audio noise, generate AI voiceovers and TTS, add background music and sound effects (SFX), and auto-duck music under speech (-18 LUFS).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External video creators and editors use the skill to remove pauses and filler words, improve dialogue audio, and add voiceovers, music, or sound effects to a media timeline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow sends media and editing instructions to an external editing service, and the security review notes that public URL exposure is not clearly explained.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm that recordings are appropriate to share with the service and check who can access any media URLs provided.
