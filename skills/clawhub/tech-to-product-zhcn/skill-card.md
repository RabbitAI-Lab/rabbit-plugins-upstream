## Description:

This Chinese-language skill helps users turn materials or materials-science technologies into practical product guidance, covering material selection, processing methods, performance requirements, and integration into functional systems under real manufacturing and operating constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and technical analysts use this skill to translate materials-science concepts into structured productization guidance, including applications, system architecture, implementation path, specifications, risks, and market adoption considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use documents, prior messages, or tool outputs already available in the conversation when generating productization analysis.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential technical or business material unless it is appropriate for use in advisory product guidance.

Risk: Productization guidance can be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific manufacturing, regulatory, safety, or market context.

Mitigation: Have qualified domain reviewers validate recommendations before relying on them for product, engineering, compliance, or commercial decisions.
