## Description:

Generates Chinese, application-focused guidance that helps users translate materials science or engineering technologies into real products by covering material selection, processing, performance needs, system integration, and deployment constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External users, developers, engineers, and product teams use this skill to turn technical or materials-science inputs into practical Chinese product guidance, including application maps, architecture and integration notes, implementation paths, specifications, constraints, and market adoption insights.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages use of available context and external sources, which can expose confidential materials if used in an inappropriate workspace.

Mitigation: Use it only in agents and workspaces approved for the data being supplied, and avoid providing confidential materials when that approval is absent.

Risk: Productization guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific manufacturing, regulatory, safety, or operating environment.

Mitigation: Have qualified domain, engineering, and compliance reviewers validate recommendations before using them for product decisions.
