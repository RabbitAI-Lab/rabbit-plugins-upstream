## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Exported knowledge bases may include confidential project records, contracts, specifications, safety information, and document metadata. <br>
Mitigation: Store exports securely, limit sharing, and redact or exclude sensitive construction material before export or reuse. <br>
