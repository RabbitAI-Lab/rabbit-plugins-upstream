# Examples — Results 宏观结构案例库（8 篇真实论文）

## D1 总体结果陈述案例

### A1-01（Published positive）
- 论文：A1（Borghi et al. 2024, Frontiers in Psychology）
- 位置：Results 3.2.1 Within-subject effects，p.7
- 原文："In summary, at the within-subject level, we found associations concerning the use of Actaware and Nonreact to perceived stress on the next day (partial support for hypothesis H1), but no effects from perceived stress to the use of mindfulness facets on the next day (no support for H2)."
- 结构功能：分析块末尾给出总体结果陈述（先总结方向、再回扣假设），是 D1/D3 的规范收束范式。

### A2-05（Published positive）
- 论文：A2（Oldemburgo de Mello et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > Twitter use, well-being, and emotions，p.4）
- 原文："To estimate how meaningful the effects were, we used a benchmark from our own sample."
- 结构功能：用内部基准（社会互动效应）作对照来锚定总体结果的量级，先建立比较框架再报具体系数。

### A3-01（Published positive）
- 论文：A3（Lashani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > Effects of acute stress… > Summary acute stress，p.5）
- 原文："Summary acute stress. Taken together, we found evidence that children's acute stress reactivity, particularly subjective stress and cortisol release, was associated with impaired accuracy but not reaction time in the category-switching task."
- 结构功能：用独立"Summary"小节作为总体结果陈述，先给总体模式再收束分析块（D1/D4 双功能）。

### B2-01（Published positive）
- 论文：B2（Vellani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 首段，p.4）
- 原文："As observed in Table 1 below, participants were more likely to share and seek information when (i) the receiver's uncertainty was high, (ii) when information was instrumental to the receiver, and (iii) when the information would likely convey good news—that is when the expected/true value of the stocks was high."
- 结构功能：先给三因素总体方向（(i)(ii)(iii)），再进入 Table 1 具体 β 值，是典型的"先概括后具体"总体陈述。

### B3-01（Published positive）
- 论文：B3（Glickman & Sharot 2025, Nature Human Behaviour, Results 小节标题，p.348）
- 原文："AI trained on biased human data amplifies the bias."
- 结构功能：小节标题本身即为结果陈述（finding-statement header），读者未读段落即知总体结论。

### C2-01（Published positive）
- 论文：C2（Chalkia et al. 2023, Communications Psychology, Results > SCR，p.4）
- 原文："SCR. In Experiment 1, as expected, participants exhibited stronger SCR responses to CS+ than CS− items (main effect of stimulus, F(1, 39) = 27.44, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.41, 95% CI [0.17, 0.58]), indicating successful fear acquisition."
- 结构功能：以操纵检查（manipulation check）作总体结果陈述（"indicating successful fear acquisition"），先确认实验成立再报关键效应。

## D2 整体呈现顺序案例

### A2-01（Published positive）
- 论文：A2（Oldemburgo de Mello et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 小节序列，p.4–6）
- 原文："Description of everyday Twitter use" → "Twitter use, well-being, and emotions in daily life" → "Twitter use and sense of belonging" → "Twitter use and polarization" → "Twitter use and outrage"
- 结构功能：先描述性统计，再按结局（well-being/belonging/polarization/outrage）有序展开，顺序与摘要/引言一致。

### A3-02（Published positive）
- 论文：A3（Lashani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 小节序列，p.5–6）
- 原文："Group differences in cognitive performance and prolonged stress" → "Effects of acute stress on cognitive performance" → "Effects of prolonged stress on cognitive performance" → "Relationship of mother and child stress"
- 结构功能：按"组间差异 → 急性 → 长期 → 母子关系"递进，先操纵检查后主分析，符合分析层级。

### B1-01（Published positive）
- 论文：B1（Tausch et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 小节序列，p.6–8）
- 原文："Preliminary analyses of baseline data" → "Main analyses: intervention effects"
- 结构功能：明确区分预备分析（基线差异/流失）与主分析（干预效应），先排除混淆再报因果效应。

### C1-01（Published positive）
- 论文：C1（Grob et al. 2023, eLife, Results 小节序列，p.5–10）
- 原文："cTBS to the angular gyrus reduces insight-related memory boost" → "Angular gyrus stimulation disrupts neural pattern reconfiguration following imagination-based insight" → "cTBS to the angular gyrus diminishes fronto-temporal connectivity associated with imagination-based insight" → "Control variables"
- 结构功能：按"行为 → 神经（RSA）→ 连接性 → 控制变量"层级推进，由现象到机制逐层深入。

## D3 假设/RQ 对应案例

### A1-02（Published positive）
- 论文：A1（Borghi et al. 2024, Frontiers in Psychology, Results，p.7–8）
- 原文："(partial support for hypothesis H1)" … "no support for H2" … "again not supporting H3"
- 结构功能：结果显式锚定假设编号（H1/H2/H3），读者可即时核对假设验证状态。

### A3-03（Published positive）
- 论文：A3（Lashani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > Relationship of mother and child stress，p.6）
- 原文："Since no evidence for a relationship between maternal and child prolonged stress was found, H3 was rejected."
- 结构功能：以独立小节回扣 H3 并明示"rejected"，实现假设→结果的完整闭环。

### B1-02（Published positive）
- 论文：B1（Tausch et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > Short-term effects，p.7）
- 原文："Consistent with hypotheses, and demonstrating a positive short-term effect of our intervention, participants in the treatment condition exhibited a greater increase in warmth toward the outgroup…"
- 结构功能：结果开头用"Consistent with hypotheses"锚定假设，再报具体效应（B = 10.23…）。

### C1-02（Published positive）
- 论文：C1（Grob et al. 2023, eLife, Results 首段，p.5）
- 原文："To test this hypothesis and the causal role of the angular gyrus in insight-related memory reconfigurations, we combined the life-like video-based narrative-insight task with representational similarity analysis of EEG data and (double-blind) neuro-navigated TMS over the left angular gyrus…"
- 结构功能：Results 开头以"To test this hypothesis…"回指假设并复述方法，作为假设→结果的桥梁。

### C2-05（Published positive）
- 论文：C2（Chalkia et al. 2023, Communications Psychology, Introduction，p.2；映射到 Results 四 outcome 小节）
- 原文："We hypothesized that we would observe impaired recall and recognition following a forget instruction, as well as an attenuation of fear responding for items that were instructed to be forgotten (i.e., reduced SCRs to CS+ items that had been followed by the F cue)."
- 结构功能：单一假设被拆解为 SCR / free recall / recognition 三个 outcome 逐一检验，Results 各小节与假设各成分一一对应。

## D4 先概括后具体案例

### A1-03（Published positive）
- 论文：A1（Borghi et al. 2024, Frontiers in Psychology, Results 3.2.1，p.7）
- 原文："Considering the cross-lagged effects, the more frequent use of Actaware predicted higher daily stress level on the following day (β=0.03, p=0.029), whereas the more frequent use of Nonreact predicted lower daily stress level on the following day (β=−0.04, p=0.025)."
- 结构功能：先给方向（"predicted higher/lower…"）再附 β/p，方向先于统计细节。

### A2-04（Published positive）
- 论文：A2（Oldemburgo de Mello et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > well-being，p.4）
- 原文："When participants used Twitter, they reported a decrease of .10 SD in well-being, bwithin = 0.10, CrI = [−0.15, −0.04] (Fig. 4). That effect was found only at the within-person level."
- 结构功能：先给总体方向（"decrease of .10 SD"），再给系数/区间，再限定层级（within-person）。

### A3-04（Published positive）
- 论文：A3（Lashani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > Accuracy，p.5）
- 原文："Accuracy. Regarding accuracy, children's error rates in the category-switching task were related to mothers' acute stress reactivity, showing more errors in children with stronger maternal cortisol increase (Table 1a)."
- 结构功能：先给总体关联方向（"more errors… stronger maternal cortisol increase"），再指向 Table 1a 具体模型。

### B2-02（Published positive）
- 论文：B2（Vellani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results > Individual differences，p.6）
- 原文："The analysis on sharing decisions revealed a similar pattern by which participants clustered into the following three groups (Table 4, Fig. 3 a, c, e, g). The first cluster ("Uncertainty-Dominant Group"), included 37.71% of participants in Exp1 and 48.03% of participants in the Replication study…"
- 结构功能：先给总体模式（"clustered into three groups"），再逐一展开三组的权重特征。

### B3-03（Published positive）
- 论文：B3（Glickman & Sharot 2025, Nature Human Behaviour, Results，p.348）
- 原文："In other words, the AI algorithm greatly amplified the human bias embedded in the data it was trained on."
- 结构功能：在报具体 65.33% 之后用一句"In other words"总结总体模式，概括句与具体结果呼应。

### C2-02（Published positive）
- 论文：C2（Chalkia et al. 2023, Communications Psychology, Results > Free recall，p.5）
- 原文："Free recall. In terms of item recall performance in Experiment 1, participants recalled a significantly higher proportion of CS+ than CS− items (main effect of stimulus, F(1, 39) = 7.55, p = 0.009, ηp2 = 0.16…), and remember than forget items (main effect of instruction, F(1, 39) = 39.49, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.50…) (see Fig. 3a)."
- 结构功能：先给两个主效应方向（"higher proportion of CS+… and remember…"），再附 F/p/ηp2，最后指向 Fig. 3a。

## D6 主要/次要/探索性结果分层案例

### A1-05（Published positive）
- 论文：A1（Borghi et al. 2024, Frontiers in Psychology, Results 3.4 标题，p.8）
- 原文："3.4 Results of the CPLM for comparison"
- 结构功能：把传统 CPLM 结果显式降级为"for comparison"的次要/稳健性分析，与主分析（RI-CLPM）分层。

### B1-04（Published positive）
- 论文：B1（Tausch et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 小节标题，p.6）
- 原文："Preliminary analyses of baseline data" vs "Main analyses: intervention effects"
- 结构功能：用"Preliminary/Main"显式区分预备分析与主分析，层级信号清晰。

### B2-04（Published positive）
- 论文：B2（Vellani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 首段，p.4）
- 原文："We performed an exploratory analysis of these interactions, and the results are reported in the Supplementary Results section (Table S3)."
- 结构功能：把探索性交互分析显式标注为 exploratory 并路由到 Supplementary，与主分析分离。

### B3-04（Published positive）
- 论文：B3（Glickman & Sharot 2025, Nature Human Behaviour, Results，p.351）
- 原文："The learning of bias induced by the AI was also supported by a computational learning model (Supplementary Models)."
- 结构功能：把计算建模支持证据路由到 Supplementary Models，主文只保留核心实验证据。

### C2-03（Published positive）
- 论文：C2（Chalkia et al. 2023, Communications Psychology, Results > SCR，p.5）
- 原文："In a secondary analysis, when entering Block (2, 3) as an additional factor in the ANOVA, the pattern of results remained the same."
- 结构功能：显式标注"secondary analysis"，与预注册主分析分层；并区分 preregistered 与 non-preregistered 比较。

## D7 多实验/多研究/多分析组织案例

### A1-06（Published positive）
- 论文：A1（Borghi et al. 2024, Frontiers in Psychology, Results 3.2/3.3 标题，p.6–7）
- 原文："3.2 Associations of the use of the mindfulness facets with daily stress" vs "3.3 Associations of the perceived helpfulness of the mindfulness facets with daily stress"
- 结构功能：两个平行分析（use / helpfulness）采用完全一致的小节结构（within-subject → between-subject），形成平行组织。

### A2-03（Published positive）
- 论文：A2（Oldemburgo de Mello et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 小节标题，p.5–6）
- 原文："The what of Twitter use" → "The why of Twitter use" → "The who of Twitter use"
- 结构功能：用 what/why/who 三层分解框架组织多分析（行为→功能→个体差异），框架统领各分析块。

### B1-05（Published positive）
- 论文：B1（Tausch et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 小节标题，p.7–8）
- 原文："Short-term effects" vs "Longer-term effects"
- 结构功能：按时间尺度（T1–T2 vs T1–T3）分层组织干预效应，短期与长期结果对称报告。

### B2-05（Published positive）
- 论文：B2（Vellani et al. 2024, Communications Psychology, Results 首段，p.4）
- 原文："We then pre-registered a replication study… As indicated below, all findings replicate across the two studies."
- 结构功能：显式声明双实验（Exp1 + 预注册复现）并预告"全部复现"，为并行报告两个实验建立框架。

### B3-02（Published positive）
- 论文：B3（Glickman & Sharot 2025, Nature Human Behaviour, Results，p.350）
- 原文："Next, we sought to generalize the above results to different types of algorithm and domain."（小节标题 "Biased algorithms bias decisions, whereas accurate ones improve them"）
- 结构功能：用"Next, we sought to generalize…"作为跨实验承接，引出新实验（RDK），实验边界与推进清晰。

### C2-04（Published positive）
- 论文：C2（Chalkia et al. 2023, Communications Psychology, Results，p.5–7）
- 原文："In our second experiment, participants again showed successful acquisition in SCR…"（SCR 小节内，Exp1 后接 Exp2）
- 结构功能：两个实验按 outcome（SCR/free recall/recognition/liking）组织，每 outcome 内 Exp1→Exp2 并排报告，便于跨实验比较。

## D8 Results–Discussion 边界案例

### A1-07（Published boundary，干净边界）
- 论文：A1（Borghi et al. 2024, Frontiers in Psychology）
- 位置：Results 止于 3.4（CPLM 对比），Discussion 自 "4 Discussion"（p.8）另起
- 证据：Results 内无机制解释/文献比较/实践启示；机制类内容（"One may argue that mindfulness more or less discourages options one to four…"）全部出现在 Discussion（p.8）。
- 结构功能：独立 Results 严格停留在结果陈述与模型对比，Discussion 承担解释——标准边界范例。

### B3-05（Published boundary，有限贴附总结）
- 论文：B3（Glickman & Sharot 2025, Nature Human Behaviour, Results，p.348）
- 原文："These results demonstrate an algorithmic bias feedback loop; training an AI algorithm on a set of slightly biased human data results in the algorithm amplifying it. Subsequent interactions of other humans with this algorithm further increase the humans' initial bias levels, creating a feedback loop."
- 结构功能：结果末尾用"These results demonstrate…"做贴附式总结，仍在结果陈述范围内，未进入机制/文献/实践越界。

### C1-04（Published boundary，有限机制贴附）
- 论文：C1（Grob et al. 2023, eLife, Results > RSA，p.9）
- 原文："suggesting that cTBS to the angular gyrus before gaining imagination-based insight interfered with efficient pruning of the integrated narrative."
- 结构功能：以"suggesting that…"给结果一个贴附式解释（pruning），属有限解释，未展开深层机制或文献比较。

## Constructed counterexamples（D8 过早进入 Discussion）

### EX-01（constructed）
- 原文："This effect may be explained by the fact that the right dorsolateral prefrontal cortex down-regulates the amygdala, suppressing further processing in the hippocampus."
- 结构功能：深层机制解释提前出现在 Results（应移至 Discussion）。constructed example，非真实文献。

### EX-02（constructed）
- 原文："These results are consistent with Smith et al. (2019), who found a similar effect, and contrast with Jones and Lee (2021), who reported the opposite pattern."
- 结构功能：跨文献比较提前出现在 Results（应移至 Discussion）。constructed example，非真实文献。

### EX-03（constructed）
- 原文："These findings have important implications for clinical practice, suggesting that interventions targeting this mechanism should be prioritized."
- 结构功能：理论/实践启示提前出现在 Results（应移至 Discussion）。constructed example，非真实文献。
