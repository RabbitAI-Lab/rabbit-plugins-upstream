## Description:

Diagnose macro-structure & organization of English psychology Results sections (8 dimensions).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bin77-chris](https://clawhub.ai/user/bin77-chris)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, editors, researchers, and academic writing assistants use this skill to assess the macro-structure of English psychology or cognitive neuroscience Results sections. It produces eight-dimensional structure scores, localized evidence, hypothesis/RQ mapping, checklist status, referral notes, and structure-only revision priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide manuscript text that they are not permitted to share with an agent environment.

Mitigation: Only submit Results sections that the user is authorized to process in the selected agent environment.

Risk: The skill may issue misleading structural judgments when the Results input is partial or lacks enough context.

Mitigation: Preserve information-insufficiency markers and avoid absolute conclusions unless the complete Results section has been checked.
