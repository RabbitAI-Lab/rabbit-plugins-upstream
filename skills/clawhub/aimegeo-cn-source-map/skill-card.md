## Description:

核对中文文章论点与信源的适用范围，整理可引用表达和未核实项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, researchers, and content teams use this skill to check whether Chinese-language sources support an article's claims and to prepare attributed source notes, usable paragraphs, and a list of unresolved evidence gaps. They can optionally continue reviewed drafts in aimeGeo.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional publishing may send article content to connected services.

Mitigation: Review the final article, destination accounts, and visibility before publishing.

Risk: Exporting reports may replace existing files in the chosen output directory.

Mitigation: Choose an output directory where replacing existing reports is acceptable.
