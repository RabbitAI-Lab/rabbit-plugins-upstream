## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ASO recommendations may not fit a specific app category, region, audience, or storefront data pattern.

Mitigation: Compare recommendations against the user's own App Store Connect or Play Console analytics before acting.

Risk: Storefront analytics exports may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Share only the fields needed for the task and remove sensitive details when possible.

Risk: Marketing experiments can be misread if download changes are treated as proof without retention, conversion, timing, and campaign-overlap evidence.

Mitigation: Use the skill's evidence gate and keep changes only when conversion improves without a material decline in retained users, rating quality, or revenue.

Risk: Black-hat ASO tactics such as bought ratings, artificial installs, or chart manipulation can create compliance and enforcement risk.

Mitigation: Use official storefront experiments, authentic-user review prompts, and transparent paid acquisition.
