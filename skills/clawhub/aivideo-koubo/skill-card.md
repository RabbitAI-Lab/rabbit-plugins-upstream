## Use Case:

External users, creators, merchants, and assisting agents use this skill to turn product or social-media scripts into AI presenter video workflows on a third-party platform. It helps draft or refine speaking scripts, estimate duration and credits, select presenter and voice options, and prepare platform-compliant publishing guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter business, personal, payment, credential, script, or voice-sample data into a third-party video platform.

Mitigation: Verify the platform, account requirements, terms, and data-handling expectations before entering sensitive data or uploading voice samples.

Risk: Voice cloning and AI presenter content can create consent, attribution, and platform-disclosure issues.

Mitigation: Use only authorized voice references and follow destination-platform requirements for AI-generated labels and virtual-presenter disclosure.

Risk: The platform uses credits and may charge based on video duration or selected model settings.

Mitigation: Review the real-time order estimate before generation, test with short samples when possible, and treat listed prices as estimates.

Risk: Optional API use can expose platform API keys or publishing-channel tokens if copied into files, logs, repositories, or chat output.

Mitigation: Pass keys through environment variables, avoid storing or printing them, and rotate credentials if exposure is suspected.
