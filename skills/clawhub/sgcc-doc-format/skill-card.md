## Description: <br>
Formats user-provided Word .docx files into SGCC document style, including Founder fonts, heading hierarchy, 28 pt line spacing, -1- page numbering, tables, Chinese quotation marks, and related rules. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[diamantanee](https://clawhub.ai/user/diamantanee) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and document-preparation agents use this skill to convert a submitted .docx file into a new SGCC-formatted Word document and return the formatted copy through Feishu. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Confidential or regulated documents may be returned to the wrong Feishu recipient or channel. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the target recipient or channel before sending the formatted copy, and use the skill only where that transfer is approved. <br>
Risk: Formatted output may not display as intended if Word fields are not refreshed or the recipient environment lacks the named Founder fonts. <br>
Mitigation: Open the output in Word, refresh fields when needed, and confirm required fonts are available before treating the formatted document as final. <br>
