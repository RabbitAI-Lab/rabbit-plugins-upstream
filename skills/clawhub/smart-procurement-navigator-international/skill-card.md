## Description: <br>
Smart Procurement Navigator International supports full-cycle procurement work across China and international procurement frameworks, from opportunity assessment and tender interpretation through bid drafting, compliance review, and final submission checks. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Market development, commercial, proposal, bid preparation, and bid decision teams use this agent to analyze procurement notices and tender documents, build evaluation models, draft response materials, and run compliance and submission readiness checks. The skill is designed for human-reviewed procurement workflows and does not replace legal, pricing, or final bid/no-bid decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Procurement materials may contain confidential bid, qualification, pricing, tender, or historical response data. <br>
Mitigation: Install and run the skill only in an approved workspace, confirm authorization before processing real procurement materials, and redact unnecessary confidential or regulated data. <br>
Risk: Generated procurement analysis or bid materials may influence commercial, legal, pricing, or submission decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human approval gates for bid/no-bid decisions, quotation strategy, commercial commitments, qualification authenticity, final response documents, seals, signatures, consortium agreements, and bid bonds. <br>
