## Description: <br>
Telegram Replies guides an agent in drafting outbound Telegram replies in a real person's voice, including tone selection, batching, templates, pacing, and account restriction checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aihlp](https://clawhub.ai/user/aihlp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Account owners, assistants, and agent operators use this skill to draft Telegram DM replies that match a chosen human voice. It is intended for authorized, human-reviewed replies after a separate workflow has already decided that a reply is warranted. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can normalize undisclosed AI-authored personal messages sent from a real person's Telegram account. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for authorized, human-reviewed replies and disclose AI involvement where appropriate. <br>
Risk: Anti-spam guidance could be misused to bypass Telegram limits, recipient privacy settings, or account restrictions. <br>
Mitigation: Respect Telegram restrictions, avoid unsolicited cold outreach, and do not retry blocked sends or route around limits. <br>
