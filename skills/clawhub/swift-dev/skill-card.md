## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Signing, device registration, or app upload commands may affect developer accounts or releases.

Mitigation: Review and approve commands and credentials before signing, registering devices, or uploading apps.

Risk: Instructions involving Accessibility, screen capture, or login items can grant sensitive app capabilities.

Mitigation: Confirm the app's purpose and permissions before enabling those capabilities.

Risk: App Store Connect keys and Keychain examples involve private credentials.

Mitigation: Keep private keys and tokens out of repositories and review credential-storage changes.
