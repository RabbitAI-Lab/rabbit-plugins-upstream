## Description:

技能升级巧匠 diagnoses and upgrades rough AI prompts or skill drafts into structured Chinese skill packages for government training and business practice scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jeasonhaitao](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeasonhaitao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, trainers, and business users use this skill to evaluate rough prompts or existing skill drafts, ask clarifying questions, plan an upgrade tier, and deliver a structured prompt package with diagnostics and test prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce revised prompts or skill drafts that may include incorrect, over-preserved, or context-mismatched guidance.

Mitigation: Review generated diagnostics, rewritten prompts, and test prompts before deploying them in an agent or training workflow.

Risk: The workflow's Chinese typography and structured intake rules may be too rigid for quick edits or non-Chinese outputs.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired language, formatting expectations, and amount of customization before using the output.
