## Description:

Creates short, unnarrated motion graphics such as kinetic typography, animated charts, maps, logo reveals, and social or news overlays.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan, build, preview, and render short design-led motion graphics, including text, data, brand, geographic, and sourced-media animations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing or updating the skill can run changing external HyperFrames code.

Mitigation: Use a constrained workspace and a pinned, preinstalled HyperFrames CLI; review updates before applying them.

Risk: Asset searches or generation may send private materials to external providers.

Mitigation: Use public or approved assets, and disable or explicitly approve external provider access for sensitive work.
