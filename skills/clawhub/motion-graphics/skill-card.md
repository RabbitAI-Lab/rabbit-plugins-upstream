## Description: <br>
Creates short, design-led motion graphics such as kinetic typography, data visualizations, brand stings, social and news overlays, animated maps, UI animations, and asset-fusion pieces, rendering to MP4 or transparent overlays. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Designers, marketers, and video-production agents use this skill to plan, source assets for, build, verify, and render short unnarrated motion graphics for product, brand, data, map, social, or news visuals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill includes silent update behavior that can change installed skills before use. <br>
Mitigation: Disable or remove the silent update instruction, or pin and review installed skill versions before execution. <br>
Risk: The Gemini auto localization path can send images to an external Google service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid using that path for confidential images unless the user explicitly accepts external upload. <br>
Risk: External asset searches are networked sourcing steps with licensing and provenance obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Review asset provenance and licensing before using sourced material in a generated motion graphic. <br>
