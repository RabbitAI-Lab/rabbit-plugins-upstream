## Description: <br>
Produces internal enablement materials for a sales-led launch, including battle cards for named alternatives, a PR-FAQ-derived talk track, objection handling, internal FAQ and CS macros, and an embargo-aware internal launch announcement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, support, CS, and launch teams use this skill to prepare consistent internal launch enablement from user-provided message house, PR-FAQ, claims, launch registry, and competitor context. It is intended for building traceable sales battle cards, talk tracks, objection responses, internal FAQs, CS macros, and launch announcements without creating new unsupported facts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated battle cards, objection responses, or launch announcements may include incorrect competitive, pricing, embargo, or launch-commitment claims if source materials are incomplete or stale. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated enablement materials before use and verify competitor claims, pricing terms, embargo dates, and launch commitments against authoritative source records. <br>
Risk: The skill can propose updates to project memory or event logs for claims and launch facts. <br>
Mitigation: Approve only proposed memory or event updates that are traceable to provided evidence and keep unsupported lines marked for follow-up rather than treating them as confirmed. <br>
