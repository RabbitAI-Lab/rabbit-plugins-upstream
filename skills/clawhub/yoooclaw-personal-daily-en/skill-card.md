## Description: <br>
Generates a concise personalized daily news report from user-selected topics, using date-specific searches and source filtering to highlight important events and trends. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vivalavida-say-hi](https://clawhub.ai/user/vivalavida-say-hi) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Users who maintain a list of topics use this skill to get a quick daily briefing on what changed, why it matters, and which signals deserve follow-up. It is intended for personalized morning news review across topics such as AI, technology, business, companies, or products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Configured interest topics can be reflected in external web-search queries. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the configured topics before use and avoid including sensitive personal, business, or confidential interests. <br>
Risk: Broad requests such as asking what is new today may invoke the skill unexpectedly. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill intentionally for daily reports and review the selected topics before relying on the generated briefing. <br>
