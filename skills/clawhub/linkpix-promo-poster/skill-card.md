## Description:

快速生成双11、黑五、圣诞节等营销活动海报与折扣营销图，适用于新品发布、促销活动及品牌宣传。当用户要求做促销海报、活动图、折扣图、节日营销图、大促素材时必须触发。关键词：LinkPix、qhkit、促销海报、活动海报、折扣图、营销图、双11、黑五、圣诞、618、大促素材、新品海报、品牌宣传图。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation involves product image uploads, service credentials, and credit charges.

Mitigation: Upload only approved product images, protect the service token, and account for credit charges.

Risk: Software updates or returned upgrade commands may change the local qhkit installation.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed qhkit version and review updates or returned upgrade commands before running them.

Risk: Generated poster text or visual details may not accurately preserve important product information.

Mitigation: Check poster wording, numbers, logos, and product structure after generation; resubmit if needed.
