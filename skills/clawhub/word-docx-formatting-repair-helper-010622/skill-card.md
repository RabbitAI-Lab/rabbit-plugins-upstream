Helps users plan, automate, and verify Word/DOCX formatting cleanup, find-and-replace, styles, and related document-editing workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Knowledge workers, legal and operations teams, students, and documentation maintainers use this skill to reduce repetitive DOCX cleanup work by producing practical workflows, checklists, scripts, templates, and verification notes for document formatting tasks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated automation or bulk find-and-replace guidance could unintentionally damage document content or formatting. <br>
Mitigation: Work on a copy of the document and review changes before saving over originals. <br>
Risk: The activation wording is broader than ideal and may invoke the skill for loosely related productivity requests. <br>
Mitigation: Keep invocation specific to Word/DOCX formatting cleanup, styles, and find-and-replace workflows. <br>
