## Description: <br>
Helps agents create or optimize in-app paywalls, upgrade screens, upsell modals, and feature gates for freemium, trial, and tier-upgrade conversion moments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, growth practitioners, and developers use this skill to design and improve in-product upgrade prompts after users have experienced value. It focuses on feature gates, usage limits, trial-expiration screens, timing, copy, A/B tests, and anti-patterns for paywall optimization. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Payment-flow suggestions such as one-click upgrade or pre-filled payment information can create payment-security and consent risks if implemented carelessly. <br>
Mitigation: Use a PCI-compliant payment processor, avoid storing raw card details, and require clear user confirmation before any charge. <br>
Risk: Paywall optimization guidance can accidentally encourage dark patterns or excessive prompting. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve a clear escape hatch, avoid confusing plan selection or guilt-trip copy, and apply frequency caps after dismissal. <br>
