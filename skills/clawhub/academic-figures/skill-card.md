## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rated the skill suspicious and reported concerns about stated limits versus diagram features, shell use in tests, and possible exposure of research data in shared reproduction bundles; analysis was partial.

Mitigation: Review the reported concerns and diagram behavior before installation, inspect commands before running them, and avoid sharing reproduction bundles that contain original research data.

Risk: Security analysis covered 97.4% of components, with four components only partially inspected.

Mitigation: Treat the scan as incomplete and review relevant files and behavior before relying on the skill.
