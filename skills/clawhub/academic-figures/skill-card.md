## Description:

Creates publication-ready scientific figures from JSON, CSV, or Excel data using a local chart-generation workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers and developers use the skill to turn data files into scientific figures for papers and submissions, including journal-formatted charts and multi-panel layouts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment rates the skill suspicious with medium confidence and reports incomplete inspection.

Mitigation: Review the installation source before use; avoid confidential research data and independently check results and validation outputs.

Risk: Reproduction bundles may contain original research data.

Mitigation: Inspect reproduction bundles and remove original data before sharing them.
