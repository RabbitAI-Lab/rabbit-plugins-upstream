## Description:

Creates publication-ready scientific figures from JSON, CSV, or Excel data, with journal presets, many chart types, and static image exports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and developers use it to turn study data into publication figures and batch-rerender charts. It is intended for data-backed scientific figures rather than text editing, business dashboards, or decorative graphics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
