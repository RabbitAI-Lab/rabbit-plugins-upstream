## Description: <br>
Make a creator-style or UGC ad video: a direct-to-camera testimonial, a product demo, an unboxing, or a faceless voiceover cut. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and developers use this skill to plan and generate short UGC-style ad videos for social and web placements using supplied scripts, audio, product details, and media. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Ad scripts and supplied media may be sent to video-generation services. <br>
Mitigation: Use only media and copy that the user is comfortable sharing with the provider, and avoid private or unlicensed assets. <br>
Risk: Generated ads could include unsupported claims, testimonials, metrics, or reviews. <br>
Mitigation: Require real, user-supplied wording for claims and have advertising claims reviewed before publication. <br>
