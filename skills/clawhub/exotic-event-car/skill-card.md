## Description:

Match exotic and luxury rental cars to an event, location, and budget, with a booking timeline and event-specific questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users planning a prom, wedding, birthday, proposal, or photo/video shoot can compare suitable exotic or luxury rental options in their city. The skill helps them check event terms and plan when to book.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rental listings, rates, driver eligibility, and event permissions may be unavailable or different from what a user expects.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning guidance and confirm availability, current rates, driver eligibility, delivery, and event or shoot permissions directly with the rental company before booking or paying.

Risk: The supplied artifact references supporting rental-data and renter-checklist documents that were not included.

Mitigation: Verify vehicle details and all contract terms with the rental company rather than relying on the missing references.
