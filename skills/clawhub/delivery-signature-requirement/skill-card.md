## Description:

Combines determine a signature requirement with compose a delivery signature section.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Delivery operations users use this skill to decide whether a delivery handoff requires a recipient signature and to compose the corresponding signature section for a delivery document.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated signature requirement may not match a user's carrier, service-level, or business policy.

Mitigation: Verify the signature decision against the applicable carrier rules and internal delivery policy before relying on the generated delivery language.
