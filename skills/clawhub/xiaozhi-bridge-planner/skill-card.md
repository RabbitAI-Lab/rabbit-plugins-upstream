## Description:

Helps students in China identify subject-specific gaps between junior and senior high school and plan focused summer or early-term study, with subject instruction handed off to specialist skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students transitioning from China's junior to senior high school use the skill to assess gaps across six subjects, prioritize foundational review, and schedule manageable study before and shortly after school starts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, crisis contacts, and minor-consent rules before use in other regions or languages.

Risk: Sharing a minor's study profile or queuing reminders without permission may expose personal information or create unwanted notifications.

Mitigation: Keep profile access and reminders off by default, require separate explicit consent, and review downstream sharing and reminder permissions.
