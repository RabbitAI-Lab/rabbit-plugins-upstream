## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking students identify gaps in math, physics, chemistry, and English and plan the transition from middle school to the first semester of high school.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students finishing China's middle-school entrance examination or starting high school use the skill to identify prerequisite gaps, build a weekly summer or early-term study plan, and find appropriate subject-specific help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12 curriculum; localize before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning-profile details or reminder requests for minors could be used or shared without consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile access and reminder handoff off by default; request separate explicit consent and honor view, pause, delete, export, and sharing controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, consent defaults, and emergency resources may not fit another region.

Mitigation: Restrict use to the intended Chinese-language Mainland China K12 context, or localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent requirements before deployment.
