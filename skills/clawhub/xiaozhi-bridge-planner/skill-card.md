## Description:

帮助中国大陆中考后至高一上学期的学生诊断数学、物理、化学和英语的衔接薄弱点，并制定先补基础、再适度预习的每周计划。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

中考后的学生及其学习支持者用它识别四科衔接缺口，安排暑假或高一开学初的每周学习重点与休息，并将具体学科内容交给相应辅导技能。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent rules may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Use for Chinese-language mainland China K12 education, or localize these details before use elsewhere.

Risk: A student's learning profile or reminders could be used without their agreement.

Mitigation: Keep profile access and reminder requests off by default and require separate, explicit consent before either action.

Risk: The plan could be mistaken for comprehensive subject tutoring or a guarantee of academic results.

Mitigation: Limit the plan to bridging priorities, refer subject-specific instruction to relevant skills, and avoid promising outcomes.
