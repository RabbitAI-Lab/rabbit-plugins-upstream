## Description:

Helps students transitioning from Chinese middle school to high school identify subject-specific gaps and plan manageable summer or early-term study priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students preparing for Chinese high school, and their educators, can use the skill to identify gaps across eight subjects and sequence short study goals with weekly rest. It refers subject-specific teaching and optional reminders to other skills rather than providing either itself.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese Mainland; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, consent assumptions, and language before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific curriculum and emergency guidance may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Use in Chinese Mainland or localize the curriculum, language, emergency contacts, and consent assumptions before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: A student's study profile or reminder preferences could be used without informed permission.

Mitigation: Obtain separate, explicit consent before reading a profile or handing off reminders; allow the student to decline or pause either option.
