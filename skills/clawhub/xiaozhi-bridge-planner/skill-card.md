## Description:

帮助中考后至高一上学期的学生找出数学、物理、化学和英语的衔接缺口，安排先补基础、再预习入门内容的每周计划。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

中考后的学生及高一上学期的学生用它自查四科学习基础，制定暑假或开学初的每周衔接与补救计划，并在需要具体教学时转向相应学科技能。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localized crisis contacts and consent rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's study profile or reminders could be used without permission.

Mitigation: Keep profile reading, cross-skill sharing, and reminder handoffs off unless the student and, where required, guardian explicitly consent.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and consent assumptions may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis referrals and consent rules before use outside Mainland China; confirm the student's region before giving emergency contacts.

Risk: A self-reported skills check may misprioritize subjects or overstate readiness.

Mitigation: Base plans on the student's answers, avoid guarantees or choosing courses for them, and refer specific instruction to subject skills.
