## Description:

Scores a US keyword list by volume, difficulty and business fit, then ranks the terms worth writing about first.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

US SEO strategists and in-house marketing managers use it to prioritize content topics by scoring keyword exports against search volume, difficulty, and business fit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The current scoring can rank harder keywords higher when other inputs match.

Mitigation: Validate and correct the difficulty scoring before relying on rankings for content planning.

Risk: The optional Windows application is outside the security verdict.

Mitigation: Review the optional application separately before choosing to run it.
