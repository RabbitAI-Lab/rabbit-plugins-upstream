## Description:

Helps people plan safer ladder selection and setup for home DIY tasks using reach, lean-angle, load-rating, roof-access, and power-line checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and DIY users plan ladder size, placement, load capacity, roof access, and pre-climb checks for household work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect ladder sizing or setup can cause serious falls.

Mitigation: Treat the plan as a homeowner aid, check manufacturer labels and local rules, and hire a professional when the setup is marginal.

Risk: Ladder work near overhead electrical lines can cause electrocution.

Mitigation: Keep clear of power lines, follow local electrical safety requirements, and seek professional help when clearance is uncertain.
