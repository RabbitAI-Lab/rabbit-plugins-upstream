## Description:

Vet a sponsored post, guest post, syndication or press release before you pay or publish, the parasite SEO safety check. Use for "is this guest post safe", "should we buy this sponsored post", "syndicate our blog" or "press release links". Checks Google's site reputation and link spam rules, disclosure and canonicals, then gives a go or no-go.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
