## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users who hold a Binance .ai Agent ID use this skill to review AgentDraw information and perform check-ins, eligible Pass mints, ticket purchases, redemptions, or settlement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Daily check-ins advertised as free may still incur BNB network fees, and recurring transactions may accumulate costs.

Mitigation: Keep wallet confirmation enabled, authorize daily check-ins separately with a fee limit, and compare fees with rewards.

Risk: Paid draw tickets can lose money, and live manifest or deployed contract claims remain unverified.

Mitigation: Verify contract addresses against current official information and simulate transactions before signing.
