## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and developers use this skill to gather and synthesize information from web search, academic sources, social media, and news for deep research, literature review, and public-opinion analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research prompts and search terms may be sent to third-party search, academic, social, and news platforms.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting secrets, confidential project names, personal data, or sensitive research topics unless those queries are approved for the relevant external services.

Risk: Search, social media, and news results can be incomplete, stale, duplicated, or misleading.

Mitigation: Cross-check important claims across higher-trust sources and preserve collection times, source categories, and credibility labels in the report.

Risk: Some academic sources may require paid access or may not be reachable from the agent environment.

Mitigation: Note access limits clearly and distinguish retrieved evidence from unavailable, paywalled, or citation-only references.
