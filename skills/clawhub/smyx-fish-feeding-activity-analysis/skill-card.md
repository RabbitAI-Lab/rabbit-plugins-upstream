## Description:

Analyzes fish feeding videos to estimate gathering, feeding intensity, and leftover feed, then reports a 0–100 activity score and low-appetite alerts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Aquarists and aquaculture operators use the skill to analyze post-feeding camera footage, review feeding activity reports, and check historical results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The advertised non-executing mode may still upload footage.

Mitigation: Confirm a fixed non-executing mode and clear upload disclosure before use, and provide only footage you are authorized to send.

Risk: Automatic account setup stores reusable credentials without clear user control.

Mitigation: Review account enrollment and token storage behavior before use, and avoid supplying unrelated credentials.
