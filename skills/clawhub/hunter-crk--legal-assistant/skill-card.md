## Description: <br>
Legal Assistant helps legal and business teams classify contracts, extract key terms, review clause risks, compare templates, draft revisions and approval notes, answer legal questions, and update contract records. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Legal, sales, procurement, and business stakeholders use this skill to turn contract submissions and legal questions into structured intake records, key-term summaries, risk lists, revision suggestions, approval-note drafts, and ledger updates for human review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated legal analysis, approval opinions, contract revisions, or legal Q&A may be incomplete, incorrect, or unsuitable as final legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and require qualified human review before final legal conclusions, high-risk clause approval, external responses, signing, or seal approval. <br>
Risk: Contract ledger writes, reminder creation, archive updates, or Feishu/system actions could affect business records if executed without authorization. <br>
Mitigation: Require previews, authorized human approval, permissions, audit logs, and data masking before connecting the skill to real contracts or business systems. <br>
Risk: Contract, employee, customer, financial, or commercially sensitive information may be exposed during review or demonstrations. <br>
Mitigation: Use desensitized or fictional demo data, restrict output to authorized users, and mask sensitive fields before display or storage. <br>
