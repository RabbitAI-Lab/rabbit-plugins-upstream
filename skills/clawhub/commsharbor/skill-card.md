## Description:

Use domain mail without operating a mail server, or inspect public email-security records. Evaluate free DNS checks and mailbox activation before buying service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and organization administrators use this skill to inspect public email-security records, set up domain mailboxes, and manage authorized email-service connections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad credentials or unverified domain and Google connection authority could expose organization mail settings or lead to unauthorized service changes or charges.

Mitigation: Use organization-scoped credentials, confirm domain authority, review Google connection permissions and current prices, and verify accepted jobs through their status.
