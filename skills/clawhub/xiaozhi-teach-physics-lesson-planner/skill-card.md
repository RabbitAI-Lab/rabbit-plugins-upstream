## Description:

Drafts concept-led physics lesson plans for Chinese middle-school and first-year high-school compulsory-module teachers, with differentiated activities and question sequences for teacher review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Physics teachers in mainland China's Chinese-language K12 classrooms use this skill to draft middle-school or first-year high-school compulsory-module lessons that build physical concepts through teaching sequences and differentiated questions. Teachers review drafts before classroom use; the skill only reserves a teaching slot for experiments, not a ready-to-run experiment procedure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft lesson plans may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular class.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review the content and confirm any class-workspace writeback before use.

Risk: An experiment slot in a draft could be mistaken for a safe, complete procedure.

Mitigation: Route real experiment setup, equipment, and safety procedures to the dedicated experiment-coach workflow and check local school requirements.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and privacy assumptions may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy rules, and crisis contacts before deployment outside the intended setting.
