## Description:

帮助初高中物理教师围绕物理观念设计涵盖概念建构、规律教学、模型建构、应用训练与课堂小结的分层教案，并提供提问链；实验内容仅为需教师复核的草稿。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初中及高中物理教师，用于规划新授、复习和讲评教案，构建物理概念、规律、模型、分层活动与课堂提问链。实验内容只作为需教师复核的教学设计草稿，不替代实际实验方案或安全流程。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class or student-profile information could be exposed or changed without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Use class summaries and student aliases; approve each saved change; enable student-profile updates only with the required consent.

Risk: An experiment draft could be mistaken for a classroom-ready operating procedure.

Mitigation: Review experiments under school safety procedures; do not use reference examples directly as classroom operating instructions; defer operational setup and safety procedures to the appropriate experiment guidance and school rules.
