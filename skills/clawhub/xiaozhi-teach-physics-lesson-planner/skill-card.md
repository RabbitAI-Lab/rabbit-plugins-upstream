## Description:

Helps teachers draft concept-led physics lesson plans for Chinese middle school and first-year high school compulsory physics, including differentiated activities and question sequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Physics teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft lessons for middle school or first-year high school compulsory physics, with concept-building, modeling, differentiated instruction, and classroom questions. Lesson plans are teacher-reviewed drafts, not executable laboratory procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K–12 curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student or class records may expose personal information if real names or unconsented data are used.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, use aliases instead of real names, and have the teacher review each lesson-plan writeback.

Risk: An experiment placeholder in a lesson plan could be mistaken for a safe, ready-to-run procedure.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review all drafts and use the dedicated experiment-coach skill or school laboratory rules for equipment, procedures, and safety planning.
