## Description:

帮助初中物理教师围绕物理观念设计课堂教案，包括概念建构、规律教学、模型建构、应用训练、课堂小结、分层安排和提问链。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External middle-school physics teachers use this skill to draft Chinese lesson plans that organize a class around physics concepts, inquiry prompts, demonstrations, student misconceptions, and summary activities. It is a planning aid; classroom-ready content, especially experiment content, requires teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; deployments elsewhere require localization of curriculum alignment, privacy practices, and emergency-contact guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce practical lab-procedure details even though it is intended only for lesson planning.

Mitigation: Treat experiment output as a draft, require qualified teacher review, follow local school lab rules, and route concrete procedures to the experiment-coach workflow before classroom use.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, privacy, and emergency-contact defaults may not fit other jurisdictions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum alignment, minor-data handling, and emergency-contact guidance before deployment outside Mainland China.

Risk: Generated teaching materials or questions may contain calculation, pedagogy, or alignment errors.

Mitigation: Mark AI-generated materials and require teacher verification before reuse or student-facing publication.
