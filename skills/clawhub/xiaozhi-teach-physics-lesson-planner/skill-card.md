## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A lesson plan may contain unsuitable experimental activities or unsafe assumptions.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check local equipment and school safety rules; refer operational experiments to the dedicated experiment coach.

Risk: Class records may expose student information or be saved without approval.

Mitigation: Anonymize class records and require teacher confirmation before reading, sharing, or saving lesson entries.

Risk: Chinese curriculum, emergency, or privacy guidance may not fit another region.

Mitigation: Localize curricula, emergency contacts, and privacy and consent rules before use elsewhere.
