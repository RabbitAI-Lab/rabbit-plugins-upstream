## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中物理教师，提供新授课、复习课和讲评课的环节安排、概念建构路径与提问链草稿，供教师复核后使用。实验仅安排教学位置和目的，具体实验方案交由专门流程处理。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft lesson content may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular class.

Mitigation: Teachers review and adapt lesson plans before classroom use.

Risk: Student information and confidential exam materials may be exposed if shared without authorization.

Mitigation: Exclude personal student data and confidential exam papers unless school policy permits their use.

Risk: An experiment mention in a lesson plan could be mistaken for a safe, ready-to-run procedure.

Mitigation: Refer apparatus, operations, grouping, and safety planning to the dedicated experiment-coach workflow.
