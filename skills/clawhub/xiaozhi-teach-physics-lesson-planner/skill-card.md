## Description:

Drafts Chinese-language junior and senior high school physics lessons around concept formation, physical laws, models, applications, differentiated activities, and teacher-led questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language junior and senior high school physics teachers in Mainland China use this skill to prepare differentiated lesson-plan drafts, concept-building sequences, question chains, and placements for experiments. Teachers review all drafts before classroom use; experiment procedures and safety planning are outside this skill's scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Experiment placeholders may be mistaken for classroom-ready procedures.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check school laboratory rules, equipment, and safety requirements before classroom use; seek dedicated experiment-planning guidance for procedures.

Risk: Classroom records may contain identifiable student information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms for class data and use the available pause or delete controls when records should not be retained.

Risk: Generated lesson content or exercises may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review lesson drafts and verify generated exercises before using them with students.
