## Description:

Helps middle- and high-school physics teachers draft concept-led lesson plans with teaching sequences, differentiated questions, and classroom summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school physics teachers use this skill to draft lesson structures, concept-building activities, question sequences, and review lessons. Teachers review every draft before classroom use; hands-on experiment procedures are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information or unreleased exam content could be exposed if included in prompts or stored classroom records.

Mitigation: Do not enter real student names, contact details, identifiable answer sheets, or unreleased exam materials; use the documented pause, delete, and export controls.

Risk: Inaccurate lesson or experiment drafts could be used as classroom instructions.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review all lesson and experiment drafts before use; refer actual apparatus, operating steps, and safety plans to the dedicated experiment skill.
