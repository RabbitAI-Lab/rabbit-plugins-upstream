## Description:

Helps junior-middle-school physics teachers draft concept-led lesson plans with staged questions, differentiated activities, and teacher-reviewed experiment placeholders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Junior-middle-school physics teachers use this skill to draft lesson sequences for concept building, physical laws, models, applications, and classroom reflection. Teachers review all materials before classroom use; hands-on experiment instructions are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language; tailored to mainland China junior-middle-school teaching)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Experiment suggestions in lesson drafts may not match local equipment or school safety rules.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check all experiment ideas against school safety requirements and use a dedicated experiment-planning workflow for practical procedures.

Risk: Class records may be remembered, shared, or written back when classroom-data features are used.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended class and apply the provided privacy controls before accessing or retaining class records.
