## Description:

Creates teacher-reviewed physics lesson-plan drafts for Chinese middle-school and first-year high-school compulsory-module-1 classes, with concept building, differentiated activities, and question chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Physics teachers in Chinese middle schools and first-year high-school compulsory-module-1 courses use this skill to draft lesson sequences, concept explanations, differentiated activities, question chains, and experiment placements. Teachers review the drafts before classroom use or sharing; detailed experiment procedures are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K–12 teaching context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class records and persistent student profiles may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use memory and writeback only with appropriate teacher consent; review entries before saving or sharing.

Risk: Lesson-level experiment suggestions may be mistaken for safe, ready-to-run procedures.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check the school's equipment and safety rules, and refer detailed procedures to qualified experiment guidance.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy expectations, and crisis-support guidance may not transfer outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before use in other regions or languages.
