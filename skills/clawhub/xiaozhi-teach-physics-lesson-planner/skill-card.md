## Description:

为初高中物理教师规划以物理观念为主线的课堂教案，包含概念建构、规律教学、模型应用、分层活动与提问链。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

供初中和高中物理教师设计或调整课堂教案，围绕概念、规律、模型与应用安排教学环节、分层活动和提问链。输出是供教师审阅的草稿；实验内容仅规划教学中的实验位置，不提供可直接执行的实验方案。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can write back class physics profiles containing sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms and minimal data, review each proposed writeback, and confirm authorization before saving or sharing profile information.

Risk: Experiment ideas in lesson drafts could be mistaken for ready-to-run procedures.

Mitigation: Treat them as teacher-reviewed planning drafts; verify hazards and use the designated experiment guidance and school safety rules for equipment, procedures, and safety controls.

Risk: The security scan was partial because two local references were unavailable during scanning.

Mitigation: Review the complete referenced materials and validate the release before relying on guidance from materials not covered by that scan.
