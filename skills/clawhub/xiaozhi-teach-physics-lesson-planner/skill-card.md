## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school physics teachers draft concept-led lesson plans with sequenced explanations, questioning, differentiation, and classroom summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school physics teachers in Chinese K–12 classrooms use this skill to draft lesson structures, concept-building activities, questioning sequences, and differentiated instruction. Teachers review every draft before classroom use; hands-on experiment procedures are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese K–12 classrooms)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom learning records may contain sensitive information about minors.

Mitigation: Confirm aliases, consent, sharing limits, and teacher review before accessing or retaining records.

Risk: Unreviewed lesson or experiment suggestions could be unsafe or inaccurate in class.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts for teacher review and use a dedicated experiment-safety workflow for procedures and equipment.
