## Description:

Drafts concept-led physics lesson plans for middle- and high-school teachers, with timed lesson flow, modeling, question sequences, differentiation, and teacher-reviewed experiment placeholders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school physics teachers use this skill to draft a lesson around physics concepts, laws, models, and classroom application. It provides lesson structure and question sequences; experiment ideas remain drafts for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class records may include student information that should be limited and shared carefully.

Mitigation: Use student aliases and only the class data needed for the lesson; confirm saved records and sharing permissions.

Risk: Experiment drafts may involve hazards such as heat, lasers, glass, or electricity.

Mitigation: Treat experiment ideas as drafts and have a qualified teacher check them against school safety rules before use.
