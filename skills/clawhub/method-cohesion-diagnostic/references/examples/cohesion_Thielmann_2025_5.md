# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

At T0, participants first provided informed consent and basic demographics. At T1, 132 days after T0 on average, participants first completed the mind game to measure dishonest behavior, followed by other behavioral paradigms that are beyond the scope of the current investigation. At T2, 168 days after T0 and, thus, 34 days after T1 on average, we again measured dishonest behavior, now using the coin-toss task. Finally, T3 took place 1,258 days (∼3 years and 5 months) after T0 and, thus, 1,090 days (∼3 years) after T1 and 1,056 days (∼2 years and 11 months) after T2 on average. Here, we implemented a lottery task inspired by the randomized response technique to measure dishonest behavior.

## 来源文献

Thielmann et al., 2025, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

四个测量时点以 At T0 / At T1 / At T2 / Finally 串联，并持续回扣相对间隔（days after T0），时间线信息流连贯、逻辑递进清晰。
