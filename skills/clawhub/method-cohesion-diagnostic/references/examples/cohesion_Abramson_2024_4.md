# 片段类型：衔接
## 原文片段
To assess genetic and environmental influences on temperament, we first examined univariate ACE models, where the observed variance is decomposed into additive genetics (A), shared environment (C), and nonshared environment and error (E). The latent factors are estimated by comparing the observed variances and covariances of MZ and DZ twins. The ACE model assumes that MZ and DZ cotwins similarly share 100% of C. In contrast, the model assumes that MZ cotwins share 100% of A (genetic variance) but that DZ cotwins share, on average, only 50% of A. Accordingly, the correlation between the twins' C components is constrained to 1.0, whereas the correlations between the A components are constrained to 1.0 for MZ twins and .5 for DZ twins. Significance of the ACE components was estimated by inspecting the likelihood-based 95% confidence intervals of the ACE variances.
## 来源文献
Abramson, L., et al. (2024). The structure, development, and etiology of observed temperament during middle childhood. Developmental Psychology.
## 适配诊断点
ACE 模型以 To assess→we first examined→assumes→In contrast→Accordingly→whereas 的约束链递进，A/C/E 定义、MZ/DZ 共享假设、相关系数约束（1.0/.5）与检验（likelihood-based 95% CI）环环相扣，是双生子建模的衔接样板。
