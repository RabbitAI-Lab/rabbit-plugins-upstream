# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

To control for the possibility that our results may be confounded by other personality characteristics relevant to leadership, we included measures of conscientiousness, emotional stability, and openness to experience, all of which have been shown to predict leader emergence (Judge et al., 2002). Although agreeableness does not predict leadership emergence in meta-analytic research, it does predict leadership effectiveness (Judge et al., 2002), so we included it here to rule out the possibility that leadership network changes are due to this variable. ... Prior research shows that important verbal behaviors, such as length of communication, can shape leadership perceptions (e.g., Anderson & Kilduff, 2009; Mullen et al., 1989). ... To ensure that our results are not explained by the possibility that extraverted individuals have larger networks at Time 1, we also included the number of people in the network each person had at Time 1 in each of our models (termed initial network size).

## 来源文献

Landis, B., et al. (2022). Revisiting extraversion and leadership emergence. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

每个控制变量的入选都带 "To control for the possibility that ... / Although ... does not predict ... it does predict ..., so we included it to rule out ..." 的论证句，控制变量清单 = 混淆源清单，是控制变量选择的衔接样板。
