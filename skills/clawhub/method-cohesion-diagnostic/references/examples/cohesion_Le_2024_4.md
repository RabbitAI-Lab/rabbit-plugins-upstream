Participants then completed a simulation task that assesses social distancing behaviors (Fazio et al., 2021; an online demonstration is available at https://psychvault.org/social-distancing/). This computer-based task simulates 10 ecologically valid COVID-19 relevant contexts and records participants' behavioral decisions. Fazio et al. (2021) documented that performance on this task predicted the actual likelihood of contracting COVID-19 more effectively than self-report assessments of social distancing adherence. ... Following Fazio et al. (2021), we standardized participants' responses to make them comparable and averaged them to create a composite social distancing index (α = .72; M = −.003, SD = .60).
