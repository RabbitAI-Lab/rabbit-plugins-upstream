# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

We adapted Murray et al.’s (2008) Stranger Anxiety paradigm to include: (a) a stranger talking to a toddler’s mother for 2 minutes while the toddler was about 5 feet away on a play-mat with some toys; (b) the stranger asking the mother to introduce her to the toddler and then interacting with the toddler for 1 minute—ﬁrst approaching and talking to the toddler and then touching the toddler; (c) the stranger picking up the toddler and holding him/her for another minute (For T1, the stranger held the toddler while standing and tried to draw the toddler’s attention to some decoration hanging from the ceiling for 1 minute. For T2, the stranger let the toddler sit on her lap and read aloud a picture book for 1 minute. If the toddler cried during the pick-up phase, the toddler would be put down on the play-mat.); (d) the stranger putting down the toddler (if the toddler had not already been put there due to crying) and playing with him/her on the mat for 2 minutes.

## 来源文献

Wang, L., et al. (2022). On becoming socially anxious. Developmental Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

改编范式的 (a)-(d) 分阶段程序链 + 括号内时间点差异与哭闹应急规则（If the toddler cried...），结构嵌套清晰、信息流连贯，是范式改编描述的衔接样板。
