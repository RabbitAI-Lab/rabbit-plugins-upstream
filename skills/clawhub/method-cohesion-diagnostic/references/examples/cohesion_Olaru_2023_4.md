# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

We first tested for measurement invariance across time (i.e., longitudinal) and age (i.e., cross-sectional) for each latent personality trait, as well as LS and WS (social satisfaction was only measured with one item). We then used bivariate LGCM (McArdle, 2009) based on the scale scores to study correlations between the levels and change in the personality traits and LS, SS or WS. We used LSEM (Hildebrandt et al., 2009, 2016) to study how the associations varied across age. We provide a more detailed description of each of these steps below.

## 来源文献

Olaru, G., et al. (2023). The link between personality, global, and domain-specific satisfaction across the adult lifespan. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
