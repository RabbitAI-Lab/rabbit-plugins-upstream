# 片段类型：衔接
## 原文片段
Comparable to Study 2, we restricted the sample's age range to fall between 18 and 39 years to control for age effects, and we aimed for an equal distribution in terms of education. However, as we did not want to recruit subjects from Study 2 again (to control for possible training effects), there were only a restricted number of subjects in the lowest educational group available for recruitment. Therefore, within the final sample of Study 3, educational attainment was distributed as follows: 8% had middle school diplomas or lower, 46% completed apprenticeship, and 46% had high school diplomas or higher.

- Therefore 收束为实际样本分布（8%/46%/46%），约束→后果→结果的因果链完整。
- 控制变量选择理由（age effects / training effects）随决策一并给出。
