# 片段类型：衔接（样本代表性与选择效应的自查衔接）

## 原文片段

> For the models described below we considered only observations within an interval of 5 years before or after the year of divorce, ensuring a sufficient number of observations across time points. Table 2 in the online supplemental materials shows the number of responses for life satisfaction at each measurement wave. Because we selected individuals followed from the transition to marriage onward, our divorce sample may differ from the divorce sample in the general population. Therefore, we compared our final divorce sample to a larger sample of people who were observed to change their marital status from "married" to "divorced" but were not necessarily observed across the preceding transition to marriage (N = 2,610; all statistics measured in the year before divorce). Compared with this larger divorce sample, our divorce sample was younger in the year before divorce (36 vs. 41 years), had a similar level of education, a slightly lower percentage of males (40 vs. 44%), a slightly lower equivalized household income (20,600 EUR vs. 21,000 EUR) and a slightly lower number of children living in the household (1.1 vs. 1.2).

## 来源文献

van Scheppingen, M. A., & Leopold, T. (2020), Method (Data and Sample). *Trajectories of Life Satisfaction Before, Upon, and After Divorce: Evidence From a New Matching Approach.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 119(6), 1444-1458.

## 适配诊断点

- 选择效应自查的完整逻辑链：识别潜在偏差来源（"Because we selected individuals followed from the transition to marriage onward"）→ 推断可能后果（"may differ from the divorce sample in the general population"）→ 用 "Therefore" 引出对照检验 → 逐变量量化比较。
- 对照样本的构造明确（N = 2,610，测量时点统一在离婚前一年），比较口径一致。
- 差异表述精确且带方向（younger, similar level, slightly lower），并给出具体数值（36 vs. 41 years; 40 vs. 44%）。
- 分析窗口（5 years before or after divorce）与理由（ensuring a sufficient number of observations）一并交代。
