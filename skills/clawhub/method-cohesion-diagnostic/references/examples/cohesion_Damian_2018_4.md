## 原文片段

The main analysis consisted of five parts. First, we assessed the rank-order stability of the PTPI scales across 50 years. ... Second, we tested the mean-level change of the 10 PTPI scores across 50 years using paired-samples t tests. ... Third, we tested individual-level change using the Reliable Change Index (RCI; Christensen & Mendoza, 1986; Jacobson & Truax, 1991). ... Fourth, following Furr (2008) and Klimstra and colleagues (2010), we used q-correlations to assess personality profile stability based on the PTPI self-reports. ... Fifth, we systematically tested gender differences.

## 来源文献

Damian, R. I., et al. (2018). Sixteen going on sixty-six. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
