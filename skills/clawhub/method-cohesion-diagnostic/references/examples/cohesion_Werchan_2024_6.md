# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

Prior to the task, mothers were given a wordless picture book to review. They were then instructed to go through the book and talk about it with her child as they normally would. Caregivers were assigned to share: Just a Thunderstorm (Mayer & Mayer, 2003) or The New Baby (Mayer, 2001) at 24 months; and A Boy, a Dog, a Frog, and a Friend (Mayer & Mayer, 1993) or Frog, Where Are You? (Mayer, 1969) at 35 months. Book assignment was counterbalanced across participants at each time point. The mothers were allowed to spend up to 10 minutes reading the book with her child.

## 来源文献

Werchan et al., 2024, Developmental Psychology
