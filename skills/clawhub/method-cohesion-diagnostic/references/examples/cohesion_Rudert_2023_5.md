# 片段类型：衔接（系列实验中材料修改的清单式衔接）

## 原文片段

> Materials and procedures were identical to Study 3, with the following changes: First, we chose gender-neutral names for the presented potential group members (nicknames Taylor, Elliot, Alex04) and randomized the names of the target and the control persons. Second, we introduced an additional control condition in which all three presented potential group members introduced themselves in a friendly, norm-consistent way (e.g., "I like the survey so far and I'm excited to see what the task is like!").

## 来源文献

Rudert, S. C., Möring, J. N. R., Kenntemich, C., & Büttner, C. M. (2023), Study 4 Method (Materials and Procedure). *When and Why We Ostracize Others: Motivated Social Exclusion in Group Contexts.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

- 系列实验的程序描述用"基线 + 修改清单"结构："Materials and procedures were identical to Study 3, with the following changes: First, ... Second, ..."，避免重复完整程序，大幅压缩篇幅。
- 修改点逐条编号并说明修改动机：中性名字（gender-neutral）+ 随机化（randomized the names）→ 排除名字性别刻板影响；新增控制条件 → 提供更干净的行为基线。
- 每条修改都给出具体操作（昵称 Taylor, Elliot, Alex04；控制条件介绍语示例），修改可直接执行。
- 这种"复用 + 差异"表述让系列实验的递进逻辑一目了然。
