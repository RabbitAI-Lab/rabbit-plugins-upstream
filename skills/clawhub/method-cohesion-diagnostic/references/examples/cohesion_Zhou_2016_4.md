# 片段类型：衔接（条件描述间的对比连接与抽象总结）

## 原文片段

> The experimental manipulation stipulated that participants in the eyeliner condition should describe how applying versus not applying eyeliner could make them feel differently, whereas those in the aftershave cream condition answered a parallel question about aftershave cream. As the DV, all participants reported their current body weights in pounds.
>
> We launched a Web experiment on MTurk, advertised as "an anonymous survey consisting a few simple tasks." The experiment was programmed in Qualtrics and randomly assigned participants who accessed the survey to either the few or the many condition.

## 来源文献

Zhou, H., & Fishbach, A. (2016), Experiments 2A and 2B Method (Procedure). *The Pitfall of Experimenting on the Web: How Unattended Selective Attrition Leads to Surprising (Yet False) Research Conclusions.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 111(4), 493-504.

## 适配诊断点

- 跨研究程序的一致性表述："The experiment was programmed in Qualtrics and randomly assigned participants ... to either ... condition" 在 2A/2B/3/4 中重复出现，读者可立即识别这是同一套在线实验模板，体现系列实验的程序标准化。
- 条件对照用 "whereas" 平行衔接，两个条件的操纵内容对称呈现；对照组任务用 "a parallel question" 表明结构等价、内容不同。
- "As the DV, all participants reported ..." 的引出方式把因变量从程序叙述中干净切分出来。
- 广告词用引号原样给出（"an anonymous survey consisting a few simple tasks"），保持对参与者呈现内容的透明。
