# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

The videos in the encoding phase lasted 11 s, with an identical timing across all conditions: Initially, both actors looked at the visible object (1 s) before moving their heads and gaze synchronously either toward or away from one another (1 s), resulting in interpersonal eye contact or no eye contact (1 s). Then, both actors moved their heads and gaze back to the object (1 s) and looked at it again (1 s). The turn-taking pattern between object and partner was repeated for two more times, ending in a moment of (no) eye contact (1 s). Throughout all videos, the actors maintained a neutral facial expression, remained silent, and never looked in the direction of the infant.

## 来源文献

Thiele, M., Gredebäck, G., & Haun, D. B. M. (2025). Social connectedness without eye contact. Developmental Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

刺激时序用 "Initially...before...resulting in...Then...and...again...was repeated for two more times, ending in..." 的显式时间链描述，每拍标注精确秒数，条件间计时完全一致，是视频刺激材料的衔接与可复现性样板。
