# 片段类型：衔接（机制变量测量波次选择的取舍衔接）

## 原文片段

> Process Indicators. To examine the three potential mechanisms underlying the associations between (parental) early-life war exposure and (children's) adjustment, we included socioeconomic status, control beliefs, and general stress responses as mediators into the analyses. Socioeconomic status was assessed in the same year as adjustment, whereas assessments of control beliefs and general stress responses were taken from the closest available waves (2010/2009 for the birthplace and place-of-residence samples; 2015/2017 for the children sample). We decided to rely on assessments from these waves, because earlier assessments showed substantial missingness and would have reduced statistical power.

## 来源文献

Entringer, T. M., Halbmeier, C., Buchinger, L., & Reitz, A. K. (2026), Method (Process Indicators). *Within-Nation Variation in War Exposure and Psychological and Physical Adjustment.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 131(2), 355-379. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000601

## 适配诊断点

- 机制变量的引入由研究问题驱动（"To examine the three potential mechanisms underlying the associations"），中介角色先行声明。
- 测量波次选择显式交代：SES 同年测量，控制信念与应激反应取自最近可用波次（各样本年份逐一列出），"whereas" 对比两个策略。
- 波次选择的取舍理由明确："because earlier assessments showed substantial missingness and would have reduced statistical power"，数据质量与功效的权衡透明。
- 不同样本不同波次的映射（2010/2009 vs. 2015/2017）精确到年，读者可复现。
