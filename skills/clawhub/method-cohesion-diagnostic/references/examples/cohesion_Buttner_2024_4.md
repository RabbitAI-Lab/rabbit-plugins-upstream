# 片段类型：衔接

## 原文片段

In the prescreening, participants ﬁrst provided informed consent, reported if they own a smartphone, and were informed about the procedure of the 14-day study. Participants completed several questionnaires about their personality, attitudes, and life circumstances that are unrelated to the present contribution. Ending the prescreening, participants provided demographic information and reported on the seriousness of their participation. Participants had the opportunity to withdraw their answers from data analysis and to leave comments. All participants who completed the prescreening were thanked and invited to participate in the experience sampling part. Next, participants downloaded the scientiﬁc app Expiwell (https://www.expiwell.com).

## 来源文献

Büttner, C. M., et al. (2024). Ostracism in everyday life. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

"ﬁrst ... Then ... Ending the prescreening ... Next ..." 的时序副词链贯通预筛各环节（知情同意→筛选→无关问卷→人口学→退出权→邀请→下载 App），环节间过渡自然，是流程叙述的衔接样板。
