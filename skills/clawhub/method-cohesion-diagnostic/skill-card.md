## Description:

Diagnoses cohesion and coherence issues in psychology-paper Method sections by checking sequence language, paragraph transitions, module continuity, and referential clarity, then returns scores and journal exemplar references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External academic writers, reviewers, and writing-support agents use this skill to evaluate whether psychology Method sections are easy to reconstruct, logically connected, and clear in their references. It produces a cohesion-focused diagnostic report with scores, severity-ranked findings, revision guidance, and limited journal exemplars.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided Method sections may contain unpublished research details or sensitive participant information.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive content before using the skill in shared or less trusted agent environments.

Risk: The diagnostic references include bundled files and a textbook path; unavailable local references can reduce traceability of some findings.

Mitigation: Confirm that the checklist, rubric, example files, and any required local textbook reference are available before relying on a diagnostic result.

Risk: Cohesion recommendations may be mistaken for final edits even though the skill is diagnostic-only.

Mitigation: Have a human author review the findings and apply any revisions intentionally, then rerun the diagnostic if needed.
