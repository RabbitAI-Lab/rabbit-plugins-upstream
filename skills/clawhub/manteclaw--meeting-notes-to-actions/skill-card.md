## Description:

Convert raw meeting notes or a transcript into decisions, owned action items, and open questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Meeting facilitators and attendees use this workflow after calls, standups, or planning sessions to record decisions, action items, open questions, and ambiguities for confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incomplete or ambiguous notes could lead to incorrect decisions or attribution in the recap.

Mitigation: Confirm ambiguous points with attendees, do not infer owners or dates or treat suggestions as decisions, and preserve figures and names as stated.
