## Description: <br>
Social Selling Planner helps founders and sellers build human-executed B2B social-selling operating blocks, including target-account engagement plans, warm-touch-before-ask rules, trigger-response plays, and quarterly diagnostics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External founders and sellers use this skill to plan daily social-selling blocks for founder-led B2B motions, convert target-account or trigger-signal inputs into human-executed engagement plays, and diagnose quarterly performance from user-provided logs and exports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save planning results or propose cadence records that persist across sessions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm saves only for information the user wants retained, and require confirmation before writing or proposing persistent records. <br>
Risk: Bilingual Chinese and English wording in outputs may be unexpected for some deployments. <br>
Mitigation: Review output language expectations before deployment and adjust generated wording to the user's preferred language. <br>
Risk: Social-selling plans could drift into prohibited mass messaging, connection automation, or engagement automation if misused. <br>
Mitigation: Keep all execution human-run, review plans before use, and reject workflows that automate platform engagement or messaging. <br>
