## Official Source Priority

| Source tier | Source | Use for | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | official notice / regulator / exam owner | enforceable status, dates, rules | check publication date and latest notice |
| Tier 2 | official syllabus / sample questions / FAQ | content, format, implementation details | may lag behind notices |
| Tier 3 | institution / school / employer policy | acceptance and score-use rules | applies only to that institution |
| Tier 4 | training provider / media / community | interpretation and user experience | not authoritative for official status |
