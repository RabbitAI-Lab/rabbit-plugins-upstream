## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory and investment examples may become outdated or may not match a user's jurisdiction or portfolio context.

Mitigation: Verify current official regulatory sources and authoritative investment data before relying on the skill's ESG, carbon, green bond, or portfolio guidance.

Risk: Broad ESG and sustainability trigger terms may activate the skill in general sustainability conversations.

Mitigation: Use the skill when ESG investing or sustainable finance analysis is intended, and review outputs for fit before applying them.
