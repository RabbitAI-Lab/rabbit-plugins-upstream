## Description:

Help researchers identify their next decision, develop or revise ideas, and complete deliverables across the research lifecycle, from research questions and study design to analysis, writing, replication, and extensions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[marike86](https://clawhub.ai/user/marike86)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers can use this skill to identify the next decision in a project and produce a useful deliverable, from a research question or study plan through analysis, manuscript revision, and follow-up. It adapts its guidance to the available evidence, project stage, and constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research recommendations or novelty claims may be incomplete or unsupported by the available evidence.

Mitigation: Check factual and novelty claims against traceable primary sources or data, and distinguish observations, conclusions, inferences, and hypotheses.

Risk: A proposed study workflow may not account for all applicable review, consent, permissions, or confidentiality requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable requirements before collecting or using data or starting study procedures.
