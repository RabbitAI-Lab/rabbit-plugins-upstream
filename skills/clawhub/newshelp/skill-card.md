## Description: <br>
Helps agents navigate NewsHelp by using direct URLs, the tags index, and search engine shortcuts to reach homepage and topic pages. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[nerkn](https://clawhub.ai/user/nerkn) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents and users use this skill to find NewsHelp homepage, tag index, and topic pages quickly for news browsing and topic-oriented reading. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: NewsHelp content may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable as the only source for important decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review source quality and corroborate important claims with additional trusted sources. <br>
Risk: Search engine shortcuts may disclose sensitive search terms to an external search provider. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid private or sensitive terms in search queries; prefer direct NewsHelp URLs or the tags index when possible. <br>
