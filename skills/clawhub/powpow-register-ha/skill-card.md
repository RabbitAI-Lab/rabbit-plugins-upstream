## Description:

Provides Hausa-language guidance for registering a PowPow account, resolving sign-up issues, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Hausa-speaking PowPow users can follow step-by-step account registration, troubleshoot verification or sign-in problems, and learn how to activate a subscription. The skill offers guidance only; users complete registration and payment themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration could expose passwords or email verification codes if shared in chat.

Mitigation: Have users enter credentials and verification codes directly on the official PowPow interface; never request them in chat.

Risk: Subscription guidance could lead users to share payment credentials or pay outside the intended interface.

Mitigation: Direct users to the official PowPow, PayPal, WeChat, or Huawei interfaces for payment; never collect payment credentials or pay on their behalf.
