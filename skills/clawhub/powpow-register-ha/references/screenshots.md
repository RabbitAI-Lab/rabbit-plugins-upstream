# Hotunan allon samfur — powpow-register

A ɗauka a buƙata yayin aiki. Mai amfani ba shi da hangen nesa kan kumfa, ko yana buƙatar "gani" samfurin da ya cika yana kama da yadda, ka aika da hoto mai dacewa kai tsaye.

## Inda hotuna suke

- Hotuna shida **an haɗe su tare da skill**, suna cikin `assets/screenshots/powpow-0N.jpg` (`N` 1–6 ne). Ka aika da su kai tsaye a matsayin fayilolin hoto na gida — ba buƙatar intanet, suna aiki a kowane yanayi (har ma da yanayin da GitHub ba za a iya isa ba).
- Kawai idan fayilolin cikin kunshin sun ɓace ba zato ba tsammani a koma: kwafin da aka shirya `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-0N.jpg` ko madubi `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/durenzidu/durenzidu@main/screenshots/powpow-0N.jpg` (a lura: biyun a China mai yiwuwa ba za a iya buɗe su ba — ka fara da fayilolin cikin kunshin).
- A lokaci guda ka aika hotuna 1–3 kawai waɗanda suka shafi batun na yanzu.

## Jeri

### powpow-01.jpg — Shafin cikakken bayani na mutum na dijital

![Shafin cikakken bayani na mutum na dijital](../assets/screenshots/powpow-01.jpg)

Katin mutum na dijital da aka gina daga mutum na gaskiya/tarihi: halin kan layi, wuri, takaitaccen bayanin mutum, "开始对话" (fara tattaunawa) da sauransu.
**Ana amfani da shi**: don bayyana "bayan rijista za ka iya tattaunawa da mutane na dijital a kan taswira".

### powpow-02.jpg — Shafin gyaran wallafa sako

![Shafin gyaran wallafa sako](../assets/screenshots/powpow-02.jpg)

Hanyar gyara rubuta sako/rubutun tafiya, ƙungiyar maɓalli masu iyo a ƙasan ƙusa ta dama.
**Ana amfani da shi**: mai amfani ya tambaya "rijista ta kamma ina iya yin me", "ina ina rubuta abu".

### powpow-03.jpg — Shafin taswira (kumfa + mutane na dijital)

![Shafin taswira](../assets/screenshots/powpow-03.jpg)

Duhun taswirar jama'a gaba ɗaya: sako mai wuri an ɗaure shi a kan taswira a matsayin "kumfa", mutane na dijital an dasa su a wuraren gaskiya da avatar.
**Ana amfani da shi**: **hoton farko na gabatar da samfur** — "rubutun tafiyarka da kuka aika zai zama kumfa guda a kan taswira".

### powpow-04.jpg — Shafin sirri (kadarorin dijital)

![Shafin sirri](../assets/screenshots/powpow-04.jpg)

Shafin mai amfani da kansa: abokai/na bin/ma biyoyi, da "kadarorin dijital" — ma'aunin badge, kumfa na, mutane na dijital na.
**Ana amfani da shi**: lokacin "rijista tana ba da badge 3" a nuna badge inda suke; mai amfani ya tambaya "inani ne kadarori".

### powpow-05.jpg — Shafin ci gaban labari

![Shafin ci gaban labari](../assets/screenshots/powpow-05.jpg)

Hanyar wasan labari a cikin dandalin (misali "永乐·1421"): ci gaban sura, samun badge, wasan matsayi.
**Ana amfani da shi**: mai amfani ya tambaya "a wajen wallafa sako ina iya wasa da me", "badge ana samun su ta yaya".

### powpow-06.jpg — Shafin cikakken bayanin sako (misalin samfurin da ya cika)

![Shafin cikakken bayanin sako](../assets/screenshots/powpow-06.jpg)

Rubutun tafiya da aka wallafa: marubuci, tambarin wuri, jiki, hotuna, ƙasa za a iya yaba/sharhi/rabawa/adanawa.
**Ana amfani da shi**: **hoton na biyu na farkon gabatarwa** — "wasu suka danna kumfarka a kan taswira, wannan ne abin da suke gani".

## Bincike mai sauri na yanayi masu dacewa

| Abin da mai amfani yake cewa / matakin na yanzu | Wanne hoto a aika |
|---|---|
| Gabatarwa "kumfa me yake" | powpow-03 + powpow-06 |
| "bayan rijista ina iya yin me?" | powpow-02 + powpow-01 |
| "badge me yake / ina da me?" | powpow-04 |
| "a wajen wallafa sako ina iya wasa da me?" | powpow-05 |
| Binciken kuskuren rijista/membanci | Galibi ba buƙatar hoto ba; idan a buƙatar bayyana wurin shafi to a aika |

## Rubutun kula na kulawa

- Hotunan a cikin `assets/screenshots/` su ne kwafin da aka fi so na wallafawa. Lokacin da hotuna suka buƙaci sabuntawa, ka maye gurbin fayil ɗin da suna ɗaya a ƙarƙashin `assets/screenshots/` (a rike sunan `powpow-0N.jpg`) ka wallafa sigar sabuwa.
- Ma'ajiyar jama'a `durenzidu/durenzidu` (babban fayil ɗin `screenshots/`) kwafin da aka shirya ana riƙe shi a matsayin madubin dawowa, ana sabunta shi tare da fayilolin cikin kunshin.
- Labarin hoto na yanzu ba shi da hotunan **shafin rijista** da **shafin membanci**. Idan daga baya an ƙara su (misali `powpow-07.jpg` shafin rijista, `powpow-08.jpg` shafin membanci), ka saka fayil ɗin a cikin `assets/screenshots/` ka ƙara sashe ɗaya a wannan tsari, rubutun jagora na SKILL.md za a iya ambata shi tare.
