# Hotunan Allon Samfur — powpow-register

Yi amfani da su yayin gudanarwa yadda ake buƙata. Idan mai amfani bai san menene Bubble ba, ko kuma yana buƙatar «ganin» yadda samfurin gama aiki yake, ka aika haɗin da ya dace kai tsaye.

## Dokokin Haɗi

- Babban haɗi (raw):
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-0N.jpg`
- Maɓallin tsayawa (amfani da shi idan raw bai buɗe ba, `N` daga 1–6):
  `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/durenzidu/durenzidu@main/screenshots/powpow-0N.jpg`
- Hotuna suna cikin rumbun jama'a, ana iya aika su waje kai tsaye; **kada ka sauke** su zuwa kundin skill, kada ka aika hanyoyin gida a matsayin hoto.
- Kowace lokaci aika hotuna 1–3 masu alaƙa da batun yanzu kawai.

## Jerin

### powpow-01.jpg — Shafin Bayanin Mutum na Dijital

![Shafin bayanin mutum na dijital](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-01.jpg)

Katin mutum na gaske/masanin tarihi da aka mayar da shi mutum na dijital: yanayin kan layi, wurin da yake, tarihin mutum, «Fara tattaunawa» da sauransu.
**Yi amfani da shi lokacin**: gabatar da «bayan rijista za ka iya tattaunawa da mutane na dijital a kan taswira».

### powpow-02.jpg — Shafin Gyaran Rubutu (Post)

![Shafin gyaran rubutu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-02.jpg)

Interface ɗin gyara don rubuta rubutu/rubutun tafiya, kusurwar ƙasa dama akwai ƙungiyar maɓallai masu iyo.
**Yi amfani da shi lokacin**: mai amfani ya tambaya «na gama rijista, me zan iya yi» «ina za a rubuta abu».

### powpow-03.jpg — Shafin Taswira (Bubble + Mutane na Dijital)

![Shafin taswira](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-03.jpg)

Cikakken kallon taswirar jama'a: rubutun da ke da wurare suna taruwa a kan taswira a matsayin «kumfa», mutane na dijital an liƙa su da hoton su a wuraren gaske.
**Yi amfani da shi**: **hoto na farko da za a yi amfani da shi wajen gabatar da samfur** — «rubutun tafiya da ka buga zai zama kumfa a kan taswira».

### powpow-04.jpg — Shafin Kai na Mai Amfani (Dukiyoyin Dijital)

![Shafin kai na mai amfani](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-04.jpg)

Shafin mai amfani da kansa: abokai/waɗanda ake bi/mafiya, da kuma «dukiyoyin dijital» — ragowar baji, Bubble ɗina, mutane na dijital na.
**Yi amfani da shi**: nuna inda baji ke lokacin da ka ce «rijista tana ba da baji 3»; mai amfani ya tambaya «ina da wane dukiya».

### powpow-05.jpg — Shafin Ci Gaban Labari

![Shafin ci gaban labari](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-05.jpg)

Wasan labari na cikin dandali (misali «永乐·1421»): ci gaban babi, samun baji, yin wasan kwaikwayo na hali.
**Yi amfani da shi**: mai amfani ya tambaya «ban da buga rubutu, me kuma zan iya yi» «ta yaya ake samun baji».

### powpow-06.jpg — Shafin Bayanin Rubutu (Misalin Samfurin Gama Aiki)

![Shafin bayanin rubutu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/durenzidu/durenzidu/main/screenshots/powpow-06.jpg)

Rubutun tafiya da aka buga: marubuci, labaran wuri, rubutu, hotuna; a ƙasa ana iya so/bayar da sharhi/rabawa/ajiye.
**Yi amfani da shi**: **hoto na biyu na buɗewa** — «lokacin da wani ya danna kumfar ka a kan taswira, wannan ne zai gani».

## Bayanin Kulawa

Kundin hotunan na yanzu ba shi da hotunan **shafin rijista** da **shafin subscription**. Idan a nan gaba za a ƙara su a ƙarƙashin `screenshots/` na rumbun `durenzidu/durenzidu` (misali `powpow-07.jpg` shafin rijista, `powpow-08.jpg` shafin subscription), ka ƙara sashe a wannan takarda da tsari iri ɗaya, kuma rubutun jagorar na SKILL.md zai iya yin nuni bisa dacewa.
