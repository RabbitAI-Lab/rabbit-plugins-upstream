## Description: <br>
Guides agents and developers through selecting, comparing, deploying, and calling mainstream open-source LLMs across Chinese and global model families. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chen6896qqwee](https://clawhub.ai/user/chen6896qqwee) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to compare open-source LLM options, choose models by task, cost, deployment mode, and region, and draft API or local deployment steps for selected models. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: API examples may send prompts, metadata, or other user content to third-party model providers. <br>
Mitigation: Treat all prompt content as provider-bound data; avoid secrets, regulated data, or proprietary content unless the provider is approved for the use case. <br>
Risk: Model comparison and pricing guidance can become stale as providers update models, licenses, and costs. <br>
Mitigation: Check the linked provider documentation before making deployment, licensing, or purchasing decisions. <br>
