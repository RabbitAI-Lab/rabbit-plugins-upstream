## Description:

Analyze two-person conversations with Berne-style transactional game analysis and suggest antithesis options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and external users can use this skill to analyze two-person conversation transcripts, identify likely transactional game loops, and generate practical Adult-to-Adult antithesis options for stepping out of the pattern.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Conversation transcripts may contain private interpersonal content.

Mitigation: Review inputs before use and avoid sharing sensitive transcript details beyond what is needed for reflective analysis.

Risk: Transactional game labels can be mistaken for diagnoses or blame assignments.

Mitigation: Treat labels as hypotheses, preserve uncertainty, and keep recommendations focused on observable transcript behavior.

Risk: The skill is not intended for professional mental-health, safety, legal, or abuse intervention advice.

Mitigation: When threats, self-harm, violence, coercive control, abuse, or serious mental-health risk appear, prioritize safety, boundaries, support, and professional or local help over game classification.
