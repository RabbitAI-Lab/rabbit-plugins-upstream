# Named Game Catalog

Use this catalog as a matching aid, not as a diagnostic checklist. A named-game match needs the transaction shape, role pattern, payoff, and likely antithesis to fit. Prefer a descriptive label when only the topic matches.

## Matching Method

For each candidate, check signature, roles, payoff, and antithesis. Report the strongest match, runner-up near misses, and what evidence would change the classification.

## Life Games

### Alcoholic
Signature: A central player repeatedly creates a crisis around drinking, failure, shame, rescue, accusation, or punishment; the substance may be less important than the surrounding cast and payoff.
Roles: central sufferer/offender, rescuer, persecutor, patsy, connection/provider.
Payoff: attention, absolution, punishment, confirmation of hopelessness, structured drama.
Antithesis: refuse rescue/persecution roles; support treatment and clear consequences instead of joining the drama.

### Debtor
Signature: Debt, obligation, or repayment becomes a life structure rather than a solvable account.
Roles: debtor, creditor, collector, moral audience.
Payoff: ongoing justification, grievance, pursuit, righteousness, avoidance of closure.
Antithesis: convert the drama into explicit terms, payment plan, boundary, or clean refusal.

### Kick Me
Signature: One person behaves in ways that invite rejection or criticism, then treats the resulting rejection as proof.
Roles: provoker/victim and kicker/persecutor.
Payoff: confirmation of unworthiness or grievance.
Antithesis: do not kick; name the behavior and offer a clean choice or boundary.

### Now I've Got You
Signature: A small mistake is seized as permission for disproportionate accusation, punishment, or triumph.
Roles: mistake-maker and righteous catcher.
Payoff: vindication, superiority, licensed anger.
Antithesis: acknowledge the concrete issue without accepting the punitive frame.

### See What You Made Me Do
Signature: One person blames another for their own outburst, mistake, neglect, or aggression.
Roles: actor disowning agency and blamed trigger.
Payoff: escape from responsibility, moral leverage, grievance.
Antithesis: separate responsibility: "I own my part; your next action is yours."

## Marital / Close-Relationship Games

### Corner
Signature: A person maneuvers the other into a no-win position, then attacks whichever option is chosen.
Roles: cornerer and trapped chooser.
Payoff: control, proof that the other is wrong, avoidance of direct request.
Antithesis: expose the double bind and ask for the actual preferred outcome.

### Courtroom
Signature: People argue to an audience, adviser, therapist, friend, or authority as if seeking a verdict.
Roles: plaintiff, defendant, judge/audience.
Payoff: righteousness, alliance, avoidance of mutual responsibility.
Antithesis: refuse judge role; redirect to facts, wants, agreements, and next actions.

### Frigid Woman / Frigid Man
Signature: Sexual or intimate invitation is managed so refusal, pursuit, accusation, and vindication repeat.
Roles: withholder/protester and pursuer/accused.
Payoff: control of intimacy, proof about the other, avoidance of vulnerability.
Antithesis: stop pursuit/pressure; move to explicit consent, desire, limits, and nonsexual closeness.

### Harried
Signature: A person overloads themselves or performs overload, then gains grievance, rescue, or exemption.
Roles: overburdened one, demanding/rescuing others.
Payoff: moral credit, avoidance, complaint rights.
Antithesis: reduce the task list to explicit priorities and shared choices.

### If It Weren't for You
Signature: One person says another's restriction prevents their desired life, while the restriction may also protect them from feared action.
Roles: restrictor and restricted complainer.
Payoff: reassurance, excuse, vindication, avoidance of feared choice.
Antithesis: remove the restriction and return ownership of the choice.

### Look How Hard I've Tried
Signature: Displayed effort substitutes for a result, decision, repair, or honest refusal.
Roles: effort-displayer and evaluator/rescuer.
Payoff: absolution, sympathy, avoidance of accountability.
Antithesis: validate effort briefly, then ask for outcome, commitment, or next step.

### Sweetheart
Signature: Hostile or belittling content is delivered under affectionate, joking, polite, or flattering cover.
Roles: sugar-coated attacker and pressured receiver/audience.
Payoff: aggression with deniability, social immunity.
Antithesis: respond to the hostile content directly and calmly, not the sweet wrapper.

## Party / Social Games

### Ain't It Awful
Signature: Shared complaining bonds people while keeping them passive.
Roles: complaint leaders and confirming chorus.
Payoff: strokes, belonging, righteous helplessness.
Antithesis: ask whether the goal is venting, action, comfort, or a change of topic.

### Blemish
Signature: One person scans for flaws in others, ideas, objects, or situations to maintain distance or superiority.
Roles: flaw-finder and flawed target/audience.
Payoff: superiority, protection from envy/intimacy, avoidance of admiration.
Antithesis: narrow to the specific decision or need; decline global fault-finding.

### Schlemiel
Signature: Repeated careless damage or intrusion is followed by apologies that pressure forgiveness.
Roles: mess-maker/apologizer and forgiving host/victim.
Payoff: permission to trespass, forced forgiveness, resentment cycle.
Antithesis: require repair and changed behavior; do not accept apology as settlement.

### Why Don't You - Yes But
Signature: One person solicits solutions and rejects each one, ending with proof that nothing works.
Roles: helpless asker, solution-givers.
Payoff: confirmation of helplessness or superiority over advice.
Antithesis: stop solution-giving; ask what kind of support or experiment they will choose.

## Sexual / Romantic Games

### Let's You and Him Fight
Signature: One person triangulates two others into rivalry, jealousy, or confrontation.
Roles: instigator, two fighters/rivals.
Payoff: attention, power, avoidance of direct choice.
Antithesis: refuse triangulation and speak directly to the relevant person.

### Perversion
Signature: Sexual rules, taboo, or shock become a recurring transaction for control, rebellion, accusation, or specialness.
Roles: transgressor/initiator and shocked, judging, rescuing, or colluding other.
Payoff: excitement, shame, control, identity confirmation.
Antithesis: move to explicit consent, boundaries, legality, and non-shaming clarity; exit if unsafe or coercive.

### Rapo
Signature: Flirtation or invitation is followed by accusation, withdrawal, punishment, or public exposure. Treat this category carefully and never use it to minimize real coercion or assault.
Roles: inviter/accuser and accused pursuer.
Payoff: vindication, control, punishment, proof about desire or danger.
Antithesis: prioritize consent and safety; do not pursue ambiguity; stop when limits appear.

### The Stocking Game
Signature: A provocative display is presented as accidental or innocent, inviting attention while preserving deniability.
Roles: displayer and observer/commenter.
Payoff: attention, erotic charge, innocence defense.
Antithesis: avoid rewarding the deniable provocation; respond only to the explicit social level or name boundaries respectfully.

### Uproar
Signature: A disagreement rapidly becomes noisy mutual indignation, often replacing the original topic.
Roles: escalators, sometimes an audience.
Payoff: discharge, attention, avoidance of the real issue.
Antithesis: lower volume, pause, return to one concrete issue, or stop the exchange.

## Underworld / Rule-Breaking Games

### Cops and Robbers
Signature: Rule-breaking and enforcement form a repeated chase where getting caught is part of the script.
Roles: offender, pursuer, court/audience.
Payoff: excitement, identity, punishment, proof of cleverness or badness.
Antithesis: refuse chase drama; impose predictable consequences or exit the role.

### How Do You Get Out of Here
Signature: A person asks how to escape a system but may be organized around staying, returning, or proving escape impossible.
Roles: trapped asker and adviser/authority.
Payoff: structure, sympathy, avoidance of outside uncertainty.
Antithesis: distinguish fantasy of escape from a concrete plan; ask for one verifiable next step.

### Let's Pull a Fast One on Joey
Signature: Two people bond by planning to exploit, fool, or outsmart a third.
Roles: conspirators and mark.
Payoff: alliance, superiority, thrill, displaced hostility.
Antithesis: refuse collusion; bring the absent party or explicit ethics into the conversation.

## Consulting Room / Helping Games

### Greenhouse
Signature: Feelings, insights, or vulnerabilities are cultivated as delicate specimens for display rather than change.
Roles: exhibitor and appreciative/overcareful observer.
Payoff: specialness, protected fragility, avoidance of action.
Antithesis: respect the feeling, then connect it to present choices or behavior.

### I'm Only Trying to Help You
Signature: Help is offered or insisted on in a way that creates failure, dependency, resentment, or superiority.
Roles: helper/rescuer and helped/resistant person.
Payoff: moral credit, control, proof that the other cannot be helped.
Antithesis: ask consent, define the request, and return agency.

### Indigence
Signature: Need, poverty, incapacity, or deprivation becomes a recurring basis for support while change is avoided or undermined.
Roles: needy one, helper/system, skeptical observer.
Payoff: care, exemption, grievance, identity stability.
Antithesis: combine compassion with clear terms, options, and responsibility.

### Peasant
Signature: A person performs naive gratitude, humility, or rustic simplicity to invite patronage or avoid equal Adult exchange.
Roles: humble dependent and patron/expert.
Payoff: protection, indulgence, avoidance of equality.
Antithesis: treat the person as capable; ask for direct choices and agreements.

### Psychiatry
Signature: Psychological explanation becomes the game itself: one person analyzes, resists, performs insight, or collects labels without changing.
Roles: analyst/expert and interesting patient/client.
Payoff: fascination, superiority, avoidance of direct action.
Antithesis: shift from explanation to present behavior, contract, and experiment.

### Stupid
Signature: A person performs incompetence or foolishness to get indulgence, correction, rescue, or confirmation.
Roles: fool/incompetent one and corrector/rescuer.
Payoff: lowered expectations, attention, avoidance of responsibility.
Antithesis: do not overcorrect; state the task, choice, and consequence plainly.

### Wooden Leg
Signature: A real or claimed limitation is used as a total exemption from agency or accountability.
Roles: exempted person and excuser/judge.
Payoff: protection from demand, sympathy, avoidance.
Antithesis: validate the real limitation and identify what choices remain.

## Good / Constructive Games

### Busman's Holiday
Signature: A person recreates their work role during leisure in a way that gives real pleasure or service.
Roles: skilled doer and appreciative others.
Payoff: competence, strokes, useful structure.
Antithesis: usually not needed unless it crowds out rest or consent; make participation explicit.

### Cavalier
Signature: Courtly admiration or elegant compliment becomes a mutually pleasing exchange, often with aesthetic rather than crude sexual emphasis.
Roles: admirer/performer and appreciative subject.
Payoff: mutual admiration, charm, safe distance from rejection.
Antithesis: not usually needed unless unwanted; clarify whether the exchange is welcome.

### Happy to Help
Signature: Help is genuinely useful but may also carry prestige, control, rivalry, penance, or claim on gratitude.
Roles: helper and beneficiary/audience.
Payoff: gratitude, status, moral credit, constructive influence.
Antithesis: separate help from obligation; clarify consent and expectations.

### Homely Sage
Signature: A person adopts the plainspoken wise elder/local authority role, mixing useful counsel with identity performance.
Roles: sage and seekers/audience.
Payoff: respect, stability, influence.
Antithesis: usually not needed unless it blocks reciprocity; ask for specifics and evidence.

### They'll Be Glad They Knew Me
Signature: Success is pursued partly to gratify or honor former supporters rather than punish enemies.
Roles: achiever and remembered supporters/audience.
Payoff: constructive vindication, gratitude, belonging.
Antithesis: not usually needed unless external validation displaces intrinsic purpose.

## Extra Recurrent Names / Near-Matches

### PTA
Signature: socially acceptable moralizing or civic concern carries projection, superiority, or complaint bonding.
Antithesis: ask for concrete responsibility, evidence, and action.

### There I Go Again
Signature: self-reproach becomes a repeated identity payoff rather than a repair step.
Antithesis: acknowledge the pattern once and move directly to repair or prevention.

### I'll Show Them
Signature: achievement or power is organized around making others envy, regret, or suffer.
Antithesis: separate the real goal from revenge fantasy.

### Gee You're Wonderful
Signature: admiration is used to hook, flatter, subordinate, or invite reciprocal performance.
Antithesis: accept appreciation lightly and return to concrete reality.
