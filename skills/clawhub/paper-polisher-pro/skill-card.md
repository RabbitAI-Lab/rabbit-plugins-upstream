## Description:

Helps academic authors review drafts for AI-writing signals, terminology and style consistency, and disclosure needs without automatically rewriting their work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution (MIT-0)

## Use Case:

Academic authors and editors use this skill to self-check Chinese academic drafts, locate passages for human review, and produce writing-quality and AI-disclosure reports. English-language detection is advisory only.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-score comparisons and rewrite suggestions can be used to evade academic AI-detection policies.

Mitigation: Restrict use to legitimate author self-review and disclosure-compliant editing; do not use scores to disguise AI-generated work.

Risk: AI-detection scores can mislead on short, medical, or mixed-authorship text.

Mitigation: Do not treat scores as proof of authorship; honor the no-verdict short-text limit and review flagged passages manually.
