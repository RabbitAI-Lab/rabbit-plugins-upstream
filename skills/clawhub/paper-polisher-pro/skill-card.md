## Description:

Helps academic authors self-check AI-writing signals, review style and terminology, and prepare writing-quality and disclosure reports locally.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Academic authors and editors use this skill to review manuscripts for potential AI-writing signals, terminology and style issues, and disclosure-label considerations before publication. Scores and rewrite suggestions support human review rather than a definitive authorship judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-risk scores can be misleading, especially for short text, English prose, mixed authorship or medical writing without the optional model.

Mitigation: Treat scores and paragraph rankings as advisory, check the reported operating mode and review the manuscript yourself rather than using scores as institutional verdicts.

Risk: Terminology auto-fix and report generation can create or change local files.

Mitigation: Keep the original manuscript under version control and review corrected files before adopting edits.

Risk: Writing guidance could be misused to conceal AI-assisted work.

Mitigation: Use the skill for author self-review and follow institutional AI-use and disclosure policies.
