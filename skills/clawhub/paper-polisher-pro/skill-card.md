## Description:

Helps academic authors self-check AI-writing signals and improve drafts with sentence-level suggestions, terminology and style checks, and review reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution (MIT-0)

## Use Case:

Academic authors and editors use this local skill to review drafts for AI-writing signals, terminology consistency, writing conventions, and submission-readiness. It offers rewrite guidance without rewriting the text for them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-writing scores or rewrite guidance could be mistaken for an authorship verdict or used to evade academic AI-use policies.

Mitigation: Treat findings as advisory self-review, improve the writing itself, and follow school and journal disclosure rules.

Risk: Terminology auto-fixes and generated reports may contain mistakes affecting a manuscript.

Mitigation: Review reports and any *_fixed terminology files against the original draft before relying on them.
