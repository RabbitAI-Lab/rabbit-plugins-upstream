## Description:

Helps authors self-check academic writing for AI-writing signals, review style and terminology, and prepare quality and disclosure reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and editors use this local toolkit to review Chinese and English academic drafts, inspect paragraph-level writing signals, improve clarity and terminology, and check AI-use labeling before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-writing scores can be misleading, especially for short, mixed-authorship, English, or medical text.

Mitigation: Treat scores as advisory, use paragraph-level review when appropriate, and avoid assigning a verdict to text under 100 characters.

Risk: Revision guidance could be used to conceal AI authorship.

Mitigation: Use it for author self-review and comply with institutional AI-use disclosure and labeling rules.

Risk: The toolkit reads academic documents and can write reports or corrected copies.

Mitigation: Provide only documents you are authorized to process and review output destinations before using write or auto-fix options.
