## Description:

Helps academic authors review drafts for possible AI-writing signals, terminology and style issues, and disclosure needs, with local reports and revision guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Academic authors and editors use this skill to self-review manuscripts, inspect advisory AI-writing scores, check terminology and disclosure labels, and plan revisions without automatically rewriting the draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-writing scores can be mistaken for proof of authorship or institutional compliance.

Mitigation: Treat scores as advisory and follow the institution's own AI-use and disclosure policies.

Risk: Drafts and generated reports may contain sensitive writing.

Mitigation: Point the local tool only at intended files or directories and review report destinations before running it.

Risk: Automatic terminology fixes may alter intended wording.

Mitigation: Review any --auto-fix output before incorporating it into a manuscript.
