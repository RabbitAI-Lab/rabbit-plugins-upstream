## Description:

Helps academic authors self-check AI-writing signals and improve style, terminology, and disclosure through local writing reports and polishing guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Academic authors and editors use this skill to review drafts for writing quality, terminology, possible AI-generated passages, and AIGC labeling before submission. Scores and attribution are advisory, not proof of authorship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-writing scores or attribution may be misleading when treated as definitive judgments.

Mitigation: Review the underlying text and report context; do not use scores alone to decide authorship or compliance.

Risk: AI-rate reduction guidance could be used to bypass institutional detection or disclosure rules.

Mitigation: Use it only for legitimate writing improvement and follow applicable AI-use and disclosure policies.

Risk: Freshness, calibration, or fingerprint updates can change local detector behavior.

Mitigation: Run modification commands only on trusted corpora and review intended changes before applying them.
