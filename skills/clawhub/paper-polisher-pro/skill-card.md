## Description:

Helps academic authors self-check AI-writing signals and improve manuscripts with sentence-level editing guidance, terminology checks, and submission-readiness reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Academic authors and editors use this skill to review drafts for potential AI-writing signals, terminology and style issues, and AIGC labeling before submission. It suggests improvements rather than automatically rewriting manuscripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Manuscripts and selected folders may contain sensitive or unpublished material, and reports or optional model files are written locally.

Mitigation: Point the skill only at materials you are authorized to process and review local output and model-file locations before use.

Risk: AI-writing scores and evaluation utilities can be misused to disguise generated work or mistaken for institutional verdicts.

Mitigation: Use results for author self-review and detector validation, not evasion; follow institutional disclosure rules and treat scores as advisory.
