## Description:

Helps authors review academic drafts for AI-writing signals, terminology and style issues, and AI-assistance disclosure needs using local tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors, editors, and researchers use the skill to self-check academic writing, improve clarity and terminology, inspect paragraph-level signals, and review AI-assistance labeling before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-risk scores can be misleading, especially for short, mixed-authorship, English, or specialized drafts.

Mitigation: Treat scores as advisory, inspect paragraph-level evidence and mode notices, and avoid using them as an institutional verdict.

Risk: Auto-fix or output options may change or overwrite selected drafts.

Mitigation: Review input and output paths and proposed edits before applying changes; keep a copy of the original.

Risk: Rewrite-adjacent and attack-generation features could be used to disguise AI assistance.

Mitigation: Use the skill for quality review and disclosure, not detector evasion; follow institutional AI-use policies.
