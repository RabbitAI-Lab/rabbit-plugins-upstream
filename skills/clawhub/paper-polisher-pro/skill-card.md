## Description:

Helps authors review academic drafts for AI-like patterns, writing conventions, terminology consistency, and disclosure labels, with targeted revision guidance and reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and editors use this local tool to self-review academic drafts, locate sentences needing attention, check terminology and style, and prepare pre-submission reports. Its AI-risk scores are advisory, not institutional determinations of authorship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Targeted rewrite advice and before/after AI-detection scores can be used to tune text against detectors.

Mitigation: Use only for disclosed writing improvement under institutional AI-use rules; do not treat lower scores as proof of human authorship.

Risk: AI-risk scores may be mistaken for a definitive authorship or submission verdict.

Mitigation: Review flagged passages and the draft's context manually; do not use scores alone to make academic-integrity decisions.
