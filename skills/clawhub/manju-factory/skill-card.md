## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may try to fetch and summarize links supplied by the user, including links that expose private, login-only, or sensitive content. <br>
Mitigation: Use public or non-sensitive links, or paste a safe title and description instead of giving the agent access to restricted material. <br>
Risk: Generated adaptations may be too close to source material or unsuitable for a target platform if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review the story outline, storyboard, and production guidance for originality, copyright suitability, and platform-policy fit before publication. <br>
