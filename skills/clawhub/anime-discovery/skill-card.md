## Description:

Helps users find anime titles, recommendations, episode information, recent channel uploads, and places to watch using AniList, Anikoto, and YouTube.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jrrqd](https://clawhub.ai/user/jrrqd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Anime viewers use this skill to search for shows, browse trending or popular titles, review episode details, and discover watch links or recent uploads from anime channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may suggest direct links to unofficial or unverified third-party streaming sites without warning.

Mitigation: Review suggested watch links and their legality and safety before opening or sharing them; prefer verified official streaming sources where available.
