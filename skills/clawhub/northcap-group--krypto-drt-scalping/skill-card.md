## Use Case:

External users and trading-strategy reviewers use this skill to review a crypto DRT scalping plan, risk rules, trading windows, and historical backtest claims before independently deciding whether to test the strategy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may treat speculative strategy guidance or historical backtest claims as verified financial advice or a guarantee of future performance.

Mitigation: Verify the backtest claims independently and treat the strategy as informational material before risking money.

Risk: An agent could be given exchange credentials or authority to place trades outside the documented text-only behavior.

Mitigation: Do not provide exchange credentials or trading authority unless separate trading automation is intentionally added, reviewed, and controlled.
