## Description:

Assists with Chinese biopharma invention intake, novelty assessment, claim design, and CNIPA application draft preparation for qualified attorney or patent-agent review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent professionals and biopharma teams use this skill to turn invention disclosures, chemical structures, biological sequences, and draft materials into CNIPA-oriented novelty analysis, claim frameworks, specifications, abstracts, and source-mapped draft deliverables for professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished invention documents, chemical structures, biological sequences, or draft claims may be sent to configured PatSnap/Zhihuiya/API services.

Mitigation: Confirm confidentiality obligations, service configuration, and credential permissions before use.

Risk: Generated patent documents may be incomplete or legally unsuitable for CNIPA filing.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and require review by a qualified Chinese patent attorney or patent agent before submission.

Risk: Missing or unclear source materials can lead to unsupported technical features, examples, effects, or claim scope.

Mitigation: Keep source mappings, cite user files and API outputs, and mark missing information or assumptions before drafting continues.
