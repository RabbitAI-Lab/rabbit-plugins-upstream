## Description:

Builds proposals, reports, retrospectives, or product narratives into offline, single-file HTML presentation decks for users who need shareable local decks without PDF lock-in, slide software drift, or external resource dependencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Operations, product, research, and proposal authors use this skill to turn source material into concise, narrative-driven local HTML decks. It is intended for offline presentation deliverables that can be opened directly in a browser and shared as files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may add private customer names, internal identifiers, account details, or confidential screenshots to decks or public templates.

Mitigation: Review deck content before sharing and remove sensitive or confidential material from templates and generated files.
