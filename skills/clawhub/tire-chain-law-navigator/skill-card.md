## Description:

Helps drivers plan winter mountain trips with route-, date-, and vehicle-specific tire-chain guidance, equipment information, and a printable dash-card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Drivers and trip planners use this skill to prepare for winter travel on supported mountain routes, compare carry and installation requirements for their vehicle, and print a trip dash-card. It is planning guidance, not a live road-status service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, British Columbia (Canada), and France/Italy Alpine routes

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stored chain-law guidance may not reflect current rules or live road conditions.

Mitigation: Confirm current chain requirements and road status with the listed official sources before travel.

Risk: Saving a dash-card can replace an existing file at the selected path.

Mitigation: Choose a new or disposable output path before using the dash-card feature.
