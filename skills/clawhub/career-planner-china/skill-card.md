## Description:

A career-planning skill for China's AI-era job market that uses user profiling, career-interest and values assessments, and AI-impact analysis to produce personalized career guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People in China exploring career choices, education paths, or a career transition can use the skill to reflect on their interests and values, consider AI-related changes to occupations, and receive personalized career-planning guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill presents insurer recommendations as neutral while its guidance prioritizes one insurer.

Mitigation: Treat insurer rankings as the publisher's preference and compare options independently.

Risk: Instructions conflict about saving personal information and using email or memory features.

Mitigation: Keep email and memory features disabled until the conflicting instructions are resolved; before enabling them, confirm report contents, recipient, storage location, and deletion options.
