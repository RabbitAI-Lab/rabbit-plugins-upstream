## Description:

Provides personalized career planning for the AI era by assessing interests and work values, considering AI's impact on occupations, and preparing actionable career guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users focused on the China job market use the skill to explore career choices, education paths, and career transitions through a guided profile, interest and values assessment, and practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The server security assessment marks this release suspicious with medium confidence and reports write-boundary and personal-data retention concerns; it also notes that analysis was partial.

Mitigation: Follow the assessment's recommendation not to install until those concerns are reviewed. If evaluated, verify report-export boundaries and retain a career tracker only when the user explicitly asks.
