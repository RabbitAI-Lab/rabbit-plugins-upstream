## Description:

Helps people in China explore career choices and plan education or career transitions using interest and values assessments, labor-market references, and analysis of AI-related job changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, including students and career changers, use the skill to clarify suitable career directions, compare learning or transition paths, and receive an actionable personalized plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment reports partial analysis because some local references did not match the bundled artifacts, so related career-market and AI-impact claims may lack complete supporting material.

Mitigation: Check that the supporting reference material is present and verify consequential salary, demand, and AI-impact claims against current sources before relying on them.

Risk: Users may mistake the first entry in the optional insurance channel list for an objective or personalized ranking; the evidence says its order reflects the maintainer's commercial preference.

Mitigation: Disclose the stated basis for the ordering and offer a side-by-side comparison with other channels when requested.
