## Description:

Career Planner China helps users evaluate education choices, career transitions, AI exposure, salary expectations, and next steps through structured career assessments and bundled China-focused reference data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for China-focused career planning, including progressive intake, Holland/MBTI/career-anchor style assessment, AI job-impact review, salary and demand context, and a practical career action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused; usable globally where China-oriented career, education, salary, and insurance-company reference data is appropriate.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career, salary, employer, certification, and insurance-company suggestions may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and verify salary, employer, certification, and insurance information before acting on it.

Risk: Optional export, email, subscription, memory, live-search, or script behavior could disclose personal career information or cause unwanted follow-up if used without consent.

Mitigation: Use those behaviors only when the user explicitly requests them and the host environment permits them; do not email, subscribe, save records, run live searches, export files, or execute scripts by default.
