## Description:

An AI-era career-planning assistant that guides users through career-interest and values assessments, considers how AI may affect job options, and produces personalized career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and employees in China use the skill to explore career and education options, reflect on their strengths and values, and consider AI-related changes when planning next steps. Users who explicitly ask about insurance careers can also review the skill's disclosed channel listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career assessments, AI impact ratings, and salary references may not reflect an individual's full circumstances or current labor market conditions.

Mitigation: Use the report as planning guidance, and verify current role and salary information before making consequential education or career decisions.

Risk: Insurance channel ordering reflects the maintainer's disclosed business preference and is separate from personalized career recommendations.

Mitigation: State the basis for the ordering and offer users side-by-side comparisons with other channels.

Risk: Optional report exports or tracking can retain personal career information.

Mitigation: Export or save tracking information only when the user explicitly requests it, follow the stated storage limits, and delete tracking records when requested.
