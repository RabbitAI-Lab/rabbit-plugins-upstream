## Description:

Career Planner China helps users plan study, career, and transition paths for the AI era using progressive intake, career-interest assessment, AI-impact ratings, salary and job-demand references, and personalized report output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career advisors use this skill to explore China-oriented education choices, career transitions, AI exposure, salary expectations, and immediate action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for personal career background and preferences.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for the planning task and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal details.

Risk: Optional report export, memory/tracking, email sending, subscriptions, and live recruiting-data lookup can move data outside the current conversation or persist it.

Mitigation: Approve those actions only when explicitly desired and supported by the host environment.

Risk: Insurance-company suggestions are leads rather than endorsements.

Mitigation: Verify company details independently and compare options before making career or commercial decisions.
