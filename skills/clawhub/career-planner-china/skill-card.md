## Description: <br>
Career Planner China helps Chinese-speaking users create AI-era career plans using progressive intake, Holland/RIASEC, MBTI, career-anchor analysis, salary and job-demand references, and AI impact ratings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and career advisors use this skill to structure career-planning conversations for students, job seekers, and career changers in China. It produces personalized career direction recommendations, AI-era risk ratings, learning paths, salary context, and concrete next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for personal career, education, preference, and location details to personalize advice. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the planning task and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal details. <br>
Risk: Salary data, job-demand trends, and insurance-company referrals may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a user's specific situation. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify compensation, hiring demand, certifications, and company details before making career or financial decisions. <br>
Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, or exported-report features can expose or persist user information if used unintentionally. <br>
Mitigation: Use those optional features only after an explicit user request and only in an environment that permits external sending, subscriptions, or persistence. <br>
Risk: Career recommendations are probabilistic guidance rather than guarantees. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as decision support and compare it with human advice, local market research, and the user's own constraints before acting. <br>
