## Description:

Career Planner China helps Chinese-speaking users explore education and career paths with interest assessment, career values analysis, AI impact ratings, salary references, industry trends, and personalized planning reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to guide career-planning conversations for Chinese-speaking students and workers, including career interest assessment, AI-era job risk analysis, salary and demand context, and next-step action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional integrations can send reports, create subscriptions, store profile data, or perform live recruitment searches.

Mitigation: Only enable report export, email, subscriptions, memory storage, or live search after explicit user request and host approval.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations include a preferred provider and some entries that may be historical or unverified.

Mitigation: Present insurance recommendations as informational only and advise users to compare providers independently before acting.
