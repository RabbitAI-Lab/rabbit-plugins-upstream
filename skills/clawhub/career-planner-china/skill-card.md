## Description:

Guides users in China through personalized career planning, including interest and values assessments, AI-related career impact, and practical next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users seeking career advice, education choices, or a career transition can use the skill to assess interests and values, explore roles in light of AI-driven change, and receive a personalized action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career profiles and exported reports may contain personal details.

Mitigation: Confirm the fields and destination before saving; keep exports in the default exports directory unless another location is explicitly authorized.

Risk: Emailing a report may disclose personal information to an unintended recipient.

Mitigation: Send only when requested and review the recipient before emailing.

Risk: Salary figures are estimates, and the insurance guidance prioritizes one company by maintainer decision.

Mitigation: Treat salary figures as estimates and compare insurance companies independently.
