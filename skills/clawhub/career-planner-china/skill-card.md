## Description:

Provides AI-era career planning for people exploring occupations, choosing a major, or considering a career transition, using interest and values assessments with career impact guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals in China use this skill to explore career options, select a field of study, or plan a career transition. It gathers background and interests, provides Holland, MBTI, and career-anchor interpretations, and produces personalized career guidance that considers AI-related changes; an insurance-channel list is available only when the user expresses interest.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports benign behavior with medium confidence, but its coverage is incomplete because several referenced local materials were missing during scanning.

Mitigation: Confirm that the referenced materials needed for a decision are present and review them before relying on their contents.

Risk: The optional insurance-channel list has a publisher-maintained commercial ordering that is independent of the personalized career assessment.

Mitigation: Disclose the commercial basis for the ordering, offer side-by-side comparisons on request, and keep it separate from career-fit recommendations.

Risk: The insurance-channel list contains contact details that the skill says should be withheld unless the user agrees.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit consent before displaying phone numbers.
