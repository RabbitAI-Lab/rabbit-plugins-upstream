## Description: <br>
Career Planner China helps users with Chinese-language career planning by collecting background and preference details, applying career assessment frameworks, evaluating AI-era career impact, and producing personalized career planning reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and career advisors use this skill to structure career-planning conversations, assess interests and work values, compare career paths, and create actionable next-step plans for AI-era work transitions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for personal career, education, preference, and location details. <br>
Mitigation: Share only details needed for the planning task, and avoid providing sensitive personal data that is not necessary for career guidance. <br>
Risk: Optional email, memory, subscription, and live-data integrations can externalize or persist user information. <br>
Mitigation: Keep these integrations disabled unless the user explicitly requests them and the host environment permits the action. <br>
Risk: The skill may recommend specific insurance companies when the user is exploring insurance-related careers. <br>
Mitigation: Treat these recommendations as career-planning references only, compare options independently, and avoid presenting them as financial or insurance advice. <br>
