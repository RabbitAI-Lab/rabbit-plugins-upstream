## Description:

AI-era career planning skill for China-market users that gathers user background, assesses career interests and values, evaluates AI impact on career paths, and produces a personalized career planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career advisors can use this skill to structure China-market career planning conversations, assess career fit, compare AI-era job risks and opportunities, and generate practical next-step plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary figures and AI-risk labels may be estimates or become outdated.

Mitigation: Present salary and AI-impact information as reference estimates and encourage users to verify decisions against current local market data.

Risk: Insurance-career guidance includes a preferred company recommendation.

Mitigation: Keep insurance company recommendations conditional on user interest, disclose that recommendations are informational, and encourage comparison across multiple providers.

Risk: Optional integrations could send reports, create subscriptions, store memory, perform realtime searches, or export files.

Mitigation: Require explicit user authorization before any email, subscription, memory, realtime search, or file export action.
