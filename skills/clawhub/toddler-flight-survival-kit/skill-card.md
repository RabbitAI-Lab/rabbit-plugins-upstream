## Description:

Creates a toddler flight-day plan with timing, seat and restraint considerations, packing quantities, and strategies for common difficult moments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use this skill to plan a flight with a toddler, including nap timing, seating and restraint considerations, packing, and responses to likely difficult moments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (restraint guidance references U.S. FAA rules)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Health and restraint guidance may not fit a child's circumstances or an airline's requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm medication dosing and health questions with a pediatrician, and check restraint rules with the airline before travel.

Risk: The main guide and supporting playbook give inconsistent age guidance for lollipops.

Mitigation: Use the playbook's age-four minimum; for younger children, use an age-appropriate feeding or straw-cup option.
