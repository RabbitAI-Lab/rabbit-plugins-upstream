# Flight-Day Playbook — reference

## 1. Transitions, not hours: the timeline skeleton

| Phase | Duration | Child's experience | Your move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airport arrival | 30–60 min pre-boarding | Novel, exciting — SPEND energy | walking, escalators, window planes; no screens yet |
| Boarding | last 5 min of general boarding | containment shock begins | board LAST; carrier/car seat staged by partner |
| Pushback→10k ft | 15–25 min | noise, straps, ear pressure (mild) | first snack + top novelty at door close; seatbelt "game" |
| Cruise, part 1 | to nap or 90 min | boredom builds | staged wrapped items; aisle walk on YOUR terms at first fidget |
| Meal service | 45–60 min | good distraction | order kid meal in advance; trays are activities |
| Cruise, part 2 | post-nap / post-cliff | the long middle | second novelty wave; screen unlocked if rationed |
| Descent | last 30–35 min | ears + strapped + done | T-35 sucking item; calm object; low-stim only |
| Landing→door | 10–25 min taxi | everyone's limit | narrate the airport sights; do not open the bag again |

## 2. Ears — the physiology, briefly

- Cabin pressure equals ~6,000–8,000 ft altitude at cruise. ASCENT: pressure drops, middle ear air expands and vents out easily — usually painless.
- DESCENT: pressure rises fast (last 30 min = most of the change); air must be ADMITTED to the middle ear via the eustachian tube, which in toddlers is horizontal-ish and narrow → the pain event.
- Equalization: swallowing (sucking > chewing > yawning). Infants: breast/bottle/pacifier at descent announcement. Toddlers: straw cup (sucking with resistance), lollipop ≥4 yr (choking rules), "blow bubbles through the straw" game.
- Congestion (cold/allergy) narrows the tube: sick = worse both ways. Timing flights after recovery when possible; pediatrician conversation for the congested flyer (decongestant use in toddlers is a doctor's call).
- Pain despite protocol: it ends at landing; screaming also equalizes (cold comfort).

## 3. The germ layer (honest numbers)

- Cabin air is hospital-grade HEPA-mixed 50/50 with fresh, exchanged every 2–4 min — the AIR is fine.
- The risk surfaces: tray tables, seatbelt buckles, lavatory handles, touchscreens. Wipes at boarding + before eating cuts the dose.
- Typical post-flight cold rate in kids: meaningful; 1-in-4-ish. Not a reason not to fly; a reason for wipes and day-3 vigilance if daycare follows.
- Dryness: cabin 10–15% RH → saline spray at nap, extra fluids; dry air thickens congestion → worse ears. Fight dryness.

## 4. Jet-lag-lite for small kids

- Under 3: circadian drive is weaker, naps are portable — they often adapt faster than you, then crash day 3. Protect one anchor sleep block, get morning sunlight on arrival.
- East: shift sleep 30 min earlier/day for 3 days pre-trip if possible. West: don't bother, easier direction.
- The 2 a.m. wide-awake child in a hotel: dim light, boring company, no play — treat like a night waking at home. Do NOT start the day at 2 a.m.

## 5. Special cases

- **Lap infant long-haul:** bassinet (bulkhead, weight limits ~20 lb, air-bump rules) — reserve early, bring a sheet; the floor is never allowed during flight.
- **Two adults, two kids:** split 1+1 across rows with an empty-between strategy on light flights; on full flights, pair each adult with a kid.
- **Solo adult + toddler:** check everything, carrier not stroller, gate-check the car seat, pre-board ONE bag only, ask for help early (crew will hold things, seat-mates often will too).
- **First flight ever:** a "plane practice" at home — seatbelt game, headphones game, tray-table restaurant — 2–3 short sessions in the week before. Narrate every step during the real thing; predictability is sedation.
- **Delays/cancellation with toddler:** snacks rationed not deployed; the carrier is the tool; ask the gate agent about family room / kids area (many hubs have them); know that YOU can leave the secured area with a kid ticket-free re-entry complexity — usually stay airside.

## 6. Screen doctrine (controversial, practical)

- If screens are rare at home, a flight is the one place the exception pays: novelty density per gram is unmatched.
- Volume-limited headphones fitted BEFORE the airport (fussy fit = failed plan). Under-2: skip screens mostly (attention won't sustain), short clips fine.
- Download, offline-test, 2× content, charger brick. Autoplay-next ON (menus at altitude = frustration loops).
- Holding screens in RESERVE (unlocked only post-cliff or meltdown step 4) preserves their power; opening at boarding spends the best card first.

## 7. What to say (scripts)

- Seatmates at meltdown: "We're working on it — thanks for your patience." Once. Then child-only attention. Most seatmates have flown with or been a toddler.
- Crew: flag the lap infant at booking AND boarding; ask for the meal to arrive with yours; empty-bottle warm-up request — they do this constantly.
- The 3:1 rule of audience: 3 people annoyed, 1 person helpful, everyone else headphones in. The plane lands regardless.

## 8. After landing

- Deplane LAST (no standing in the aisle with a toddler and 4 bags while the jet bridge crawls).
- Stroller gate-delivery point: usually at the door, occasionally at the gate — ask crew before deplaning.
- The arrival meltdown: it's hunger + overstimulation + timezone. Snack NOW, dim/quiet first hour, one anchor nap, morning light. Day 3 is the real test, not landing day.
