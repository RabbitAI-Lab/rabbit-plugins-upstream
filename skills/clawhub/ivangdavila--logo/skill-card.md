## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and designers use this skill to plan, prompt, review, and prepare logo concepts and file deliverables for brands, products, and app icons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded reference images or brand materials may contain sensitive business information when used with third-party image tools.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential assets with external tools unless the user has permission and understands the tool's data handling terms.

Risk: Generated logo concepts may contain inaccurate text, weak small-size readability, or unclear commercial licensing.

Mitigation: Visually inspect generated outputs, manually refine typography and vector files, and review licensing before commercial use.
