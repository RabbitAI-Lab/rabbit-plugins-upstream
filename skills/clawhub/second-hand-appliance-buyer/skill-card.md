## Description:

Use when buying or selling a used washer, dryer, fridge, range, dishwasher, or TV — checks fair price bands by age and class, tells you which appliances are safe buys used and which are money pits, and generates a 15-minute in-person inspection protocol plus a test-load checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Consumers, appliance sellers, and landlords use this skill to assess used appliance listings, estimate fair prices, and follow appliance-specific inspection steps before purchase or resale.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; pricing guidance is US-typical.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Price bands are general estimates and may not reflect a specific model, local market, or condition.

Mitigation: Verify the specific model, age, and condition and compare current local prices before deciding.

Risk: Gas appliance connections require qualified safety checks.

Mitigation: Have gas connections checked professionally before use.
