## Description:

Prioritize student tasks by verified deadlines, identify overdue work, and format dates in the student's timezone. Use for task priorities, approaching deadlines, and study planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gaganshivanna](https://clawhub.ai/user/gaganshivanna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to organize unfinished coursework and other tasks by verified due date, identify overdue work, and see deadline times in their timezone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The planner formats dates in fixed US English, which may not suit every student's locale.

Mitigation: Check that displayed dates are clear to the student and use the supplied timezone when interpreting deadlines.

Risk: The security review was partial, with one component only partially inspected and medium confidence.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its behavior before relying on it with real student data.
