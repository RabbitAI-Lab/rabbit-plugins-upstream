## Use Case:

External users and agents use this compatibility skill when historical Courtyard hotel search, detail, or package calls are made. The skill returns a Chinese-language service notice that directs users to general hotel search, hotel price comparison, or travel-platform skills for live Courtyard/Courtyard by Marriott results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect live Courtyard hotel search, pricing, or booking from historical tool names.

Mitigation: Present the service-adjustment notice clearly and route live hotel lookup or booking tasks to separate general hotel search, hotel comparison, or travel-platform skills.
