## Description: <br>
Writes donor-centered fundraising cases for support, campaign statements, major-gift appeals, and donation appeals with the need, solution, credibility, impact, funding opportunities, urgency, and clear ask. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External fundraising teams and nonprofit staff use this skill to draft persuasive cases for support, campaign statements, major-gift appeals, and donation asks. It helps structure the donor-facing argument around the need, solution, credibility, impact, urgency, giving levels, and a clear request. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafts may contain placeholder evidence, figures, or gift amounts generated from limited user input. <br>
Mitigation: Replace all placeholder evidence, statistics, and giving levels with verified organizational data before sharing with donors. <br>
