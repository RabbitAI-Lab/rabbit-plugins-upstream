## Description:

Provides preliminary industrial equipment fault classification, 3–5 on-site checks with expected observations, safety cautions, and directions for further diagnosis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Plant operators and maintenance engineers use this skill to triage inverter, PLC, HMI, and Modbus symptoms before deciding what to inspect next. It provides preliminary guidance rather than a definitive repair or safety assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Preliminary classification or checks could be mistaken for an authoritative repair procedure or safety approval.

Mitigation: Verify fault codes and procedures against the equipment manual; use qualified personnel for final diagnosis and safety-function acceptance.

Risk: Checks near high-voltage components or rotating machinery may expose personnel to hazards.

Mitigation: Follow site safety procedures and the equipment manual; isolate power, allow discharge, and verify de-energization before hands-on work.

Risk: Unnecessary disclosure of sensitive plant details or credentials.

Mitigation: Share only the equipment model and relevant symptoms; do not supply passwords, proprietary programs, or sensitive plant information.
