## Description:

Uses cases from the Shiji and Zizhi Tongjian to explore present-day decisions through historical parallels, documented outcomes, trade-offs, and cautions.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[simonstang](https://clawhub.ai/user/simonstang)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

People weighing personal or professional decisions use historical cases to compare possible paths, costs, and limits of analogy without delegating the final choice to the agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical parallels may be mistaken for reliable predictions or professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat conclusions as perspectives rather than instructions to act; seek qualified advice for financial, medical, or legal decisions.

Risk: Unverified quotations or loosely matched cases could mislead a decision.

Mitigation: Check quotations against the cited chapters and identify where historical conditions differ from the user's situation.

Risk: Unnecessary file access could expose unrelated material.

Mitigation: Keep file access read-only and scoped to the bundled reference corpus.
