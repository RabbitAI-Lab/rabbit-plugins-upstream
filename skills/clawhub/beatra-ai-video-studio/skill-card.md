## Description:

Plan and create short AI videos from a written shot, a supplied image, exact first and last frames, multimodal references, or existing footage. Beatra AI Video Studio supports text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-guided generation, AI video editing, and AI video extension for product videos, ad creative, social clips, b-roll, transitions, reveals, and cinematic concepts. Review each delivered clip for action, subject stability, camera, continuity, audio when requested or returned, and destination fit, then choose the smallest useful edit, extension, or new render.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External video creators, marketers, and product teams use this skill to plan, generate, edit, and extend short AI videos for product stories, ads, social posts, b-roll, and cinematic concepts. It supports text- and image-led workflows, reference media, and existing footage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The video workflow requires broader Beatra account access, including voice-write permissions, than the video task needs.

Mitigation: Review the requested permissions before connecting and proceed only if the broader account access is acceptable.

Risk: Selected media leaves the device, and installation/runtime analytics are automatic with no documented opt-out.

Mitigation: Avoid sending confidential or sensitive media, confirm rights and consent for supplied assets, and account for analytics before use.

Risk: The Beatra credential is shared with other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Use the shared credential only with trusted Beatra skills and revoke it in Beatra Console when no longer needed.
