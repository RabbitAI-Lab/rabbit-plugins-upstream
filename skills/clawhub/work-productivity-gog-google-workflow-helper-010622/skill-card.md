## Description: <br>
Helps agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical workflows, checklists, analysis, code changes, and verification notes for Gog-style Google Workspace and CLI productivity work. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn Gog-style Google Workspace and CLI productivity requests into local-hardware-friendly plans, artifacts, checklists, code changes, and validation notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to trigger on general Google, CLI, or productivity requests. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when this workflow helper is desired, and review whether its guidance fits the task before acting. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow or implementation guidance could be applied to account-changing Google Workspace actions without enough review. <br>
Mitigation: Require separate human review before using outputs for account, data, permission, or workspace changes. <br>
