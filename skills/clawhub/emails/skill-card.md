## Description: <br>
Helps agents create and optimize multi-email sequences, drip campaigns, automated flows, and lifecycle email programs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, founders, lifecycle teams, and developers use this skill to plan email sequences, draft individual emails, set timing, choose subject lines, and define metrics for lifecycle or campaign email programs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated email plans may use customer activity, billing, cancellation, or product usage data in ways that require privacy and permission review. <br>
Mitigation: Use only data the organization is allowed to use, and review sequences before implementation for privacy, consent, and compliance fit. <br>
Risk: The skill provides planning and copy guidance but does not configure sending tools or enforce operational controls. <br>
Mitigation: Configure email platforms separately with appropriate access controls, sending permissions, suppression lists, and compliance checks. <br>
