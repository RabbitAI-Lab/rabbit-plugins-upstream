## Description: <br>
UDeals helps agents query and compare membership benefits across platforms such as Meituan, Taobao, JD, Heytea, UnionPay, 12306, Alipay, and Ctrip. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shawngoethe](https://clawhub.ai/user/shawngoethe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use UDeals to retrieve current membership benefits, filter them by keyword, platform, tag, or type, and summarize comparisons for users looking for available perks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Benefit searches and filter terms are sent to the disclosed UDeals API. <br>
Mitigation: Use general platform names or benefit categories, and avoid private account, travel, purchase, or membership details in search terms. <br>
