This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, product teams, and strategy teams use this skill to structure competitor research, compare market positioning, identify product and pricing gaps, and turn findings into actionable strategic recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor research notes, preferences, and business context are saved locally under ~/competitor-research/.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when local storage of that information is acceptable, and review the local notes before sharing or syncing them elsewhere.

Risk: Users could accidentally include confidential credentials, private third-party access, or non-public business material in the research workflow.

Mitigation: Keep credentials, private system access, and confidential third-party information out of the workflow; use it for public competitive research and local note keeping.
