## Description: <br>
Helps agents plan earned-media workflows, including journalist research, pitch drafting, newsjacking, press-request responses, and press-page setup. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and teams use this skill to evaluate PR readiness, identify appropriate journalists and outlets, draft respectful pitches or press-request responses, and set up media-kit materials for software, SaaS, AI, and startup products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Journalist research and media-list workflows can expose or misuse professional contact data. <br>
Mitigation: Keep contact lists private, collect only professional information needed for outreach, and avoid storing unnecessary personal data. <br>
Risk: Pitching or press-platform responses can become spam or violate platform, privacy, or anti-spam rules if used for bulk outreach or against stated preferences. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for targeted PR workflow support, respect opt-outs and 'no PR pitches' notices, and follow applicable platform and legal requirements. <br>
Risk: Reactive newsjacking can create reputational harm when applied to tragedies, politically charged stories, or topics outside genuine expertise. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the artifact's skip criteria before drafting or sending pitches, and require a genuine expertise or data basis for any public response. <br>
