## Description:

A Javanese-language guide that introduces PowPow, walks users through account registration and subscription activation, and helps troubleshoot common signup issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users receive Javanese-language guidance on the product, registration, common signup problems, and subscription activation. Users complete account creation and payment themselves on the platform; the skill does not handle their credentials or payments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, email verification codes, or payment proof in chat while seeking signup help.

Mitigation: Keep registration and payment in the user's browser or app; do not ask for or process credentials, codes, or payment proof.

Risk: The guide links to product pages and screenshots on public third-party services.

Mitigation: Confirm the user intends to visit these services and check the destination before opening product or screenshot links.
