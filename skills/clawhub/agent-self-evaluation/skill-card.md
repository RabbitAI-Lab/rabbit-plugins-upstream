## Description: <br>
Guides an agent through self-evaluation of its work, evidence, and risks before final delivery. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use this skill to check whether agent work satisfies task requirements, cites appropriate evidence, and surfaces risks before a final response or handoff. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Self-evaluation can miss defects or create false confidence in incomplete work. <br>
Mitigation: Review the agent's evidence, tests, and final recommendations before relying on the output. <br>
