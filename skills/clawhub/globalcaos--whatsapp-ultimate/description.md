You put 5 agents in a WhatsApp group. They all respond at once. Your API bill does a backflip.

Protocol v2 fixes that. Exponential Courtesy Protocol — like Ethernet collision detection, but for AI agents who won't stop talking. Quadratic backoff, jitter, backpressure for over-talkers. Conversations that detect when they're going in circles, steer themselves to new ground, and close when the objective is met.

Budget-aware scheduling flips the script near reset: unused tokens are wasted money. Burn mode loosens the reins — let agents explore, debate, surface emergent ideas. Conservative mode tightens when you're near the cap.

22 WhatsApp actions. No Docker, no CLI wrapper. Direct Baileys protocol. Your agent speaks only when spoken to — in the right chat, by the right person, at the right time.

Up front, because you should know before you install: the package also ships four maintenance scripts that read your WhatsApp session credentials, inventory your groups and their members, and patch your OpenClaw source tree. Every one of them prints what it will touch and refuses to run without an explicit --yes; the contact export writes nothing to disk and masks phone numbers unless you ask otherwise, and both source patches are reversible with --revert.
