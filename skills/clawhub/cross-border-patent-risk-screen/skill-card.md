## Description:

Screens cross-border e-commerce products for preliminary patent infringement risk across design, invention, and utility model patents using product image URLs, e-commerce links, or descriptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent analysts and cross-border e-commerce teams use this skill before product launch or market entry to identify potentially relevant active or pending patents and produce a preliminary risk report with suggested next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local product images may be uploaded to a public URL during analysis.

Mitigation: Confirm user consent before upload and avoid confidential, unreleased, personal, or metadata-sensitive images unless the operator has approved that exposure.

Risk: Patent risk reports can be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary screening only and require qualified patent counsel review before infringement, launch, licensing, or market-entry decisions.
