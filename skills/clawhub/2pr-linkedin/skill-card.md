## Description:

Write, schedule and publish LinkedIn posts in your own voice, reply to comments and read post analytics through 2pr.io. Use when the user wants a LinkedIn post drafted or rewritten, a carousel or image post, a post scheduled or published, replies to comments on their posts, or to know which posts performed and when to post.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

LinkedIn users can draft and refine posts, prepare comment replies, review analytics, and manage publishing through their connected 2pr.io account. Publishing, scheduling, replies, and deletion require the user's explicit approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Actions can publish or manage public social content on a connected account, and drafts may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Check the profile, destination platform, final text, and scheduled time before approving; review 2pr.io's privacy policy before sending confidential drafts, and revoke access in Preferences when it is no longer needed.
