## Description:

Creates a vertical keynote-style video of a custom character delivering a brief spoken hook and physical punchline with audience applause.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use it to turn a short hook into a vertical keynote-style video featuring a custom character, staged backdrop, spoken line, and audience applause.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and supplied reference images are sent to varg’s cloud service, and renders consume credits.

Mitigation: Share suitable content, keep the API key private, and review the estimated credits before approving each render.
