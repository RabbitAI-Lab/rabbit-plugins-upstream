## Description:

Creates a vertical 9:16 keynote video featuring a custom character delivering a short spoken hook with a physical punchline and audience applause.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to make short speaker-style ads, spokesperson clips, or mascot keynote videos with a custom character, a punchy spoken line, and applause.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dialogue and character prompts are sent to Varg or its generation providers during cloud rendering.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive content unless you have authorization and are comfortable sharing it with those providers.

Risk: The installer version is unpinned, and scanner coverage is incomplete because two local path-like references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the installer and unresolved references before use; treat the benign scan verdict as limited rather than a guarantee of safety.
