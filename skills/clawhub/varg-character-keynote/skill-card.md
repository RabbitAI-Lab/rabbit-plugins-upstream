## Description:

Generates an 8-second vertical keynote video of a custom character delivering a short spoken hook with a physical punchline and audience applause.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers can use the skill to make a short speaker-style ad or spokesperson clip with a custom character and a punchy line. It is suited to a single keynote scene rather than long scripts, multi-scene ads, or two-person dialogue.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Character prompts and dialogue are sent to Varg for cloud rendering.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential or sensitive content unless you are comfortable with that processing.
