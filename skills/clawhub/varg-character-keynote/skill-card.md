## Description:

Makes a vertical 9:16 keynote video in which a custom character delivers a short hook with a physical punchline, voice, and audience applause.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to make short, vertical keynote or spokesperson clips featuring a custom character, a brief spoken line, and a physical punchline. It is intended for single-speaker stage clips rather than long scripts, multi-scene ads, two-person dialogue, or songs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dialogue and character prompts are sent to varg and its generation providers.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive information unless you are comfortable with that processing.

Risk: The installer is unpinned and the scan could not resolve two local path-like references, leaving evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the installer/source and resolve the referenced paths before use.
