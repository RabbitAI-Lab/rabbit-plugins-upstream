## Description:

Evaluate a patent package for licensing, assignment, or collaboration opportunities; identify evidence-backed candidate counterparties; rank them with a transparent and sensitivity-tested rubric; propose diligence and outreach hypotheses; and generate a self-contained HTML transfer-intelligence brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to assess authorized patent packages for licensing, assignment, collaboration, option, pool, or exploration opportunities. It supports evidence-backed candidate screening, transparent ranking, diligence planning, and an HTML transfer-intelligence brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat research output as legal, valuation, sanctions, tax, antitrust, or transaction advice.

Mitigation: Review the generated brief as research support only and require separate specialist approval before relying on it for outreach, valuation, legal conclusions, or transaction decisions.

Risk: The workflow can involve confidential patent, IP, or business context and external PatSnap connectors or public-source research.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when authorized to research the listed patents and share confidential context with the configured connectors or approved research workflow.

Risk: Candidate-counterparty findings may imply willingness, fit, urgency, or authority beyond the available evidence.

Mitigation: Keep unknown values distinct from low scores, mark unresolved ownership and rights issues as diligence gates, and require explicit authorization before any outreach.
