## Description:

Edits prose to remove canned phrasing, repetitive rhetoric, and generic polish while preserving meaning and the author's voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to revise generated or draft prose for clarity and natural flow without changing its point or voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edits may change intended meaning, punctuation, or the author's voice.

Mitigation: Review the revision before publishing and specify house style, dialect, or literary preferences when needed.
