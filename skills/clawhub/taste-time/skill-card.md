## Description:

Analyzes works to reveal time as a visible, material presence in art and literature, showing mortality and transience without nostalgia or sentimentality.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Artists, writers, designers, and brand teams use this skill to apply a cross-domain aesthetic lens that makes time, mortality, transience, and duration visible in creative work. It provides style tokens, anti-tokens, examples, and application rules for writing, UI design, and branding.
