## Description: <br>
Helps software teams add useful unit tests, raise coverage, and validate changes with practical workflows, checklists, code changes, shell commands, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to plan, implement, and verify unit tests for existing codebases. It is intended for repeatable test coverage improvement work where assumptions, validation commands, and remaining risks should be made explicit. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms such as testing, regression, and quality may activate the skill in unrelated development conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for unit-test coverage work, or tighten or disable implicit invocation when strict routing is required. <br>
