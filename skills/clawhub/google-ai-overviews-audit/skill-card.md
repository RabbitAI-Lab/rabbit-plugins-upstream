## Description:

Find out what Google AI Overviews and AI Mode are doing to your clicks and conversions, page by page. Use for "are AI Overviews killing our traffic", "are we cited in AI Overviews" or "should we opt out of AI Mode". The Google side of AEO and GEO, built on Search Console's generative AI report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO and marketing teams use this skill to assess how Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode relate to search visibility, clicks, and conversions. It guides a page-by-page audit using Search Console data, citation spot checks, eligibility checks, and conversion context, then produces prioritized recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional DataForSEO workflow shares search queries and the target domain with an external service.

Mitigation: Review the service's privacy terms before use; use the manual spot-check workflow if that sharing is unsuitable.

Risk: The security scan was partial because three local path-like references could not be resolved, leaving some evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references and complete a full security scan before relying on the scan as a complete assessment.
