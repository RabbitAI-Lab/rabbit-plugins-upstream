# Common cross-platform risks

Use this file as a screening map, not as proof that a platform prohibits a specific item. Confirm applicable law and official platform rules separately.

## Safety, legality, and exploitation

- Instructions, encouragement, sale, or facilitation involving violence, self-harm, dangerous challenges, illegal goods, controlled substances, weapons, fraud, gambling, or exploitation.
- Sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, or any sexualized treatment of minors.
- Evasion of enforcement, ban circumvention, deceptive account migration, or concealment intended to pass moderation.

## Misleading claims and commercial content

- Fabricated credentials, testimonials, scarcity, prices, results, test data, media coverage, endorsements, or before-and-after evidence.
- Absolute or guaranteed claims without adequate substantiation, especially for health, finance, education, beauty, food, and investment outcomes.
- Undisclosed sponsorships, affiliate relationships, paid endorsements, synthetic spokespersons, or material commercial relationships.
- Calls to action that obscure the seller, price, recurring payment, refund conditions, eligibility, material limitations, or risk.

## Health and financial sensitivity

- Diagnosis, treatment, prescription, dosage, disease cure, investment return, loan approval, or wealth guarantees presented without appropriate authorization and evidence.
- Advice likely to cause users to delay professional care, stop prescribed treatment, take unsafe financial actions, or misunderstand material risk.
- Personal medical, biometric, insurance, banking, credit, or transaction data exposed without necessity and consent.

## Privacy, identity, and minors

- Passwords, authentication codes, identity numbers, addresses, private messages, recovery details, payment information, or identifiable customer records.
- Faces, voices, names, locations, school details, or private spaces shared without a valid basis and necessary consent.
- Minors used in commercial, risky, humiliating, sexualized, or privacy-invasive contexts.
- Impersonation, deceptive synthetic media, or realistic AI content without disclosures required by law or platform rules.

## Copyright and third-party rights

- Music, footage, photographs, artwork, broadcasts, software interfaces, trademarks, or user comments used beyond permission or applicable exception.
- False attribution, removal of ownership marks, or an instruction to evade copyright detection.
- Defamation, harassment, doxxing, hate, targeted humiliation, or unsupported accusations presented as fact.

## Presentation and distribution mechanics

- Title, cover, thumbnail, subtitles, or cropped screenshots that materially misrepresent the underlying content.
- QR codes, external links, coded language, comment prompts, or private-message funnels used to move users into a prohibited transaction or conceal essential information.
- Repetitive engagement manipulation, fake interactions, purchased traffic, or coordinated inauthentic behavior.

## Context checks

For every possible issue, ask: Who is speaking? What is claimed? What proof exists? Who may be harmed? Is it commercial? Which jurisdiction and platform apply? Is consent documented? Could the combination of words and visuals change the meaning?
