## Description: <br>
Consulting Proposal helps draft outcome-focused consulting proposals, client engagement pitches, project proposals, and RFP responses with clear scope, milestones, tiered investment options, and next steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Consultants, agencies, and business development teams use this skill to turn client needs, proposed approach, outcomes, timeline, pricing model, and scope boundaries into a ready-to-export consulting proposal framed around client value. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated proposals may include confidential client, pricing, or project information supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposal content before sharing externally and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details. <br>
Risk: Proposal content may overstate outcomes, value, timelines, proof, or scope if the input is incomplete or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Validate claims, pricing, deliverables, milestones, and out-of-scope boundaries against the actual engagement before use. <br>
