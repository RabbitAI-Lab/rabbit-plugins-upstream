## Description:

Provides Chinese-language checklists and guidance for choosing, installing, and maintaining home appliances while avoiding common renovation and service pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renovation planners use the skill to compare appliances, anticipate installation costs, and prepare questions and checklists for installers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation suggestions may normalize unsafe structural, gas, electrical, or waterproofing work.

Mitigation: Use the advice to frame questions, not as work instructions; obtain property approval and consult licensed professionals and local codes before acting.

Risk: Unconventional appliance uses in the references may be unsafe or damage equipment.

Mitigation: Check the manufacturer's manual and seek qualified advice before using an appliance outside its intended purpose.
