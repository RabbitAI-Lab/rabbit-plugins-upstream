## Use Case: <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Agents may overstate completion if they apply the checklist without actually verifying source symbols, tests, or error modes. <br>
Mitigation: Require concrete verification evidence in the handoff, including test output, checked APIs or imports, and explicit escalation for anything the environment blocks. <br>
Risk: The stricter process may add unnecessary overhead for genuine throwaway work. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the full discipline to code that will be read or maintained, while preserving the artifact's red-line safety rules for all changes. <br>
