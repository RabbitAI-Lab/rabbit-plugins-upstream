## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and practical validation workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before major investment. It guides the agent to clarify constraints, convert the problem into a repeatable validation workflow, produce actionable artifacts, and call out assumptions and residual risks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad product, startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests where its fit is imperfect. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing validation artifacts. <br>
Risk: Planning guidance can create false confidence if assumptions, evidence quality, or validation limits are hidden. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions and limits visible, validate the output against the stated success criteria, and include remaining risks or follow-up work. <br>
