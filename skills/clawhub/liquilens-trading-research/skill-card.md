## Description:

Route sourced research about money markets, covered-bank risk, BTC exits, GIFT City, reference forex and caller-assumption gold scenarios across Seiche, LiquiLens and Undertow, preserving source dates, rights, missing evidence and scenario assumptions without inferring execution prices or eligibility.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers and financial-market practitioners use this skill to prepare sourced briefs on funding conditions, bank coverage and risk, BTC exit costs, GIFT City, reference forex, and assumption-driven gold scenarios. It separates dated evidence from hypothetical calculations and flags gaps and limitations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research questions or scenario assumptions are sent to public services.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing secrets, private account details or other sensitive information.

Risk: Stale, missing or restricted data may be mistaken for current market conditions or assurance of bank safety.

Mitigation: Preserve source dates and coverage gaps; treat findings as dated research, not trading advice or a deposit-safety guarantee.

Risk: Reference rates or hypothetical gold proceeds may be mistaken for executable prices or settled cash.

Mitigation: State all caller assumptions and distinguish reference and scenario calculations from executable quotes and available cash.
