## Description:

历史材料解析题教练 helps Simplified Chinese K12 students solve history source-analysis questions by reading provenance, extracting evidence, connecting curriculum knowledge, and forming evidence-based conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners, tutors, and education platforms use this skill to coach Chinese middle-school and high-school history source-analysis questions and diagnose history error patterns. It also guides consent-gated handoff of wrong-answer analysis, profile signals, and reminders to compatible learning-support skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland Simplified Chinese K12 contexts; localize crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, and consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Use outside China-mainland Simplified Chinese K12 contexts can surface unsuitable crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, or consent expectations.

Mitigation: Localize crisis referral channels, curriculum scope, and consent rules before deploying in other regions or education systems.

Risk: Optional profile reads, cross-skill sharing, reminders, and export or deletion controls involve student learning data.

Mitigation: Enforce the stated consent gates and platform controls for profile access, sharing, reminders, deletion, and export.

Risk: Generated practice or source-analysis examples can be misleading if historical sources are invented or not checked.

Mitigation: Apply the bundled AI item self-check, require source attribution for generated materials, and keep conclusions tied to evidence and curriculum scope.
