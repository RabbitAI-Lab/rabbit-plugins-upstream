## Description:

Guides Chinese secondary-school students through historical source questions using a four-step evidence-based method and diagnoses recurring answer errors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to analyze Chinese history source-material questions, build answers supported by the material, and identify specific causes of mistakes. Optional profile sharing and review reminders require consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy and guardian consent, and crisis referral resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific curriculum assumptions or crisis resources may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize teaching standards and referral resources to the student's region before deployment.

Risk: Student error profiles or review reminders could expose personal learning data without consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile sharing and reminders off unless the student or guardian explicitly consents; follow local privacy and guardian-consent rules.
