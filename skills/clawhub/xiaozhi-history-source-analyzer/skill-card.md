## Use Case:

面向中文初高中学生的历史材料题辅导：逐步分析史料出处和证据，组织有依据的答案，并按错误类型提供针对性反馈。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K-12); localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis referrals before use elsewhere.

Mitigation: Keep profiles and reminders off by default; enable each only after explicit student or required guardian opt-in.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contact numbers may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis referrals before deploying in another region.
