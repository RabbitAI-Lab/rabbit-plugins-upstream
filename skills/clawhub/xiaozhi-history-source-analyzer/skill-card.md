## Description:

引导初高中学生按出处、信息、所学知识和结论四步解析历史材料题，并分析历史错题的具体原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to practice answering Mainland China junior- and senior-high history source questions and diagnose errors using evidence from the material. It provides graduated hints rather than answering an active test question.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K–12 history learning)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis resources may be inappropriate outside the intended region.

Mitigation: Use in the Simplified Chinese, Mainland China K–12 context unless curriculum, support channels and minor-consent rules are localized.

Risk: Optional study memory, cross-skill sharing or reminders may expose a minor's learning information without informed consent.

Mitigation: Require the relevant consent before each optional feature; provide controls to pause, export, correct and delete stored information.

Risk: Incorrect interpretation or fabricated historical sources could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check source attribution and textual support for conclusions, and review generated practice materials before using them.
