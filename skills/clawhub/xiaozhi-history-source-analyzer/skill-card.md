## Description:

引导初高中学生按出处、信息、所学知识和证据结论四步分析历史材料题，并定位历史错题的具体原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生使用本技能练习史料阅读、组织有依据的材料题答案，并分析历史错题的失分原因；教师或家长可陪同使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); adapt curriculum and support channels before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning-profile updates, cross-skill sharing and reminders may retain or share student information.

Mitigation: Leave memory, sharing and reminders off unless the student explicitly opts in; confirm each wrong-answer handoff separately.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis-support channels may not fit locations outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt curriculum, assessment conventions and local support contacts before using the skill elsewhere.

Risk: Unclear images or unsupported claims can lead to misleading historical conclusions.

Mitigation: Ask for a readable source and question, identify the provenance of material, and tie each conclusion to evidence rather than guessing.
