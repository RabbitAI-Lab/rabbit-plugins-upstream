## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through a four-step analysis of historical sources and helps identify the causes of mistakes in history questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese middle- and high-school students use this skill to interpret historical texts, images, charts, and maps, organize evidence-based answers, and diagnose history-question errors. Teachers and guardians can support its use with minors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localized curriculum, minor-consent rules, and crisis contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning profile, error history, or reminders could be shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Review profile, cross-skill sharing, parent-sharing, and reminder consent settings before enabling these features; keep memory and reminders off by default.

Risk: Crisis referrals and curriculum guidance may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts, curriculum expectations, and minor-consent settings before use in another region.
