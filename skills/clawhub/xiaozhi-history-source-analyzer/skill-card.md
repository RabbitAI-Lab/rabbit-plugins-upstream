## Description:

辅导学生按出处、材料信息、所学知识和证据组织历史材料题答案，并分析历史错题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

初中和高中学生用它练习解读史料、组织材料题答案，并定位历史错题的原因。它按课程标准和统编教材的口径提供分步辅导。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records, sharing, and reminders can involve student data.

Mitigation: Keep these features off unless wanted, follow guardian-consent requirements for younger students, and review companion services' handling of student data.
