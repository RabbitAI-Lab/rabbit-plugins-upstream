## Description:

引导初高中学生按出处、信息、所学知识和证据结论四步解析历史材料题，并分析历史错题的原因。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to work through Chinese middle- and high-school history source-analysis questions, identify evidence for answers, and diagnose mistakes without immediately supplying the answer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, minors' consent, and crisis-support guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum or emergency guidance may be unsuitable outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, consent requirements for minors, and crisis-support contacts before use in other regions.

Risk: Learning profiles or reminders could expose a minor's information without consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile sharing and reminders off unless the student or guardian explicitly opts in; confirm each mistake handoff separately.

Risk: Unsupported historical claims or invented sources could mislead students.

Mitigation: Tie each conclusion to the supplied source, confirm unreadable materials, and avoid fabricating quotations or citations.
