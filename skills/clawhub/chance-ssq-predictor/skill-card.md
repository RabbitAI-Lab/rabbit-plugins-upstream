## Description:

Helps users review user-provided Double Color Ball lottery results, describe number patterns, and generate example combinations for entertainment without claiming predictive advantage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Lottery enthusiasts use this Simplified Chinese guide to explore descriptive statistics from results they supply, check proposed number combinations, and understand the limits of lottery odds. It does not include a results dataset or fetch current results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or unsupported lottery statistics, rules, or prize figures could mislead users.

Mitigation: Ask users for verified results, state the analysis window and cutoff, and check current rules and prizes against official sources.

Risk: Number combinations or trends could be mistaken for a better chance of winning.

Mitigation: Present combinations as entertainment only and explicitly note that past draws do not predict future draws or improve the odds.
