## Description: <br>
Design better AI skills with proven architecture patterns for deciding Workflow versus Agent, selecting common workflow patterns, writing SKILL.md files, and applying a governance-aware quality checklist. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, skill authors, and reviewers use this skill to choose an agent or workflow architecture, select workflow patterns, structure SKILL.md files, and review skill quality before publication or production use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the guide to load for generic skill or architecture phrases. <br>
Mitigation: Installers can narrow activation to explicit skill-design or architecture-review language. <br>
Risk: Design recommendations or review findings could be applied without enough context for a target skill. <br>
Mitigation: Use the guide as a design and review aid, then human-review and scan the resulting skill before deployment or publication. <br>
