## Description:

Explain data findings, justify tradeoffs, prioritize competing data work and prepare truthful experience stories in a natural voice. Use for communicating analysis or work decisions; preserve source facts and the speaker's actual contribution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, professionals, and job seekers can use this skill to explain data findings, weigh competing work, and prepare experience stories from facts they provide. It helps turn evidence into clear recommendations while preserving uncertainty and each speaker's actual contribution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsupported claims or recommendations could mislead readers if the supplied evidence is incomplete or misread.

Mitigation: Check recommendations against the actual evidence, preserve uncertainty that could change a decision, and identify missing facts instead of inventing them.
