## Description:

Explain data findings, justify tradeoffs, prioritize competing data work and prepare truthful experience stories in a natural voice. Use for communicating analysis or work decisions; preserve source facts and the speaker's actual contribution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People preparing analysis updates, work-decision explanations, prioritization recommendations, or truthful experience stories can use this skill to communicate evidence, tradeoffs, uncertainty, and their own contribution clearly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Facts shared for a story or recommendation may include information the user does not want to disclose, or generated recommendations may be acted on without review.

Mitigation: Share only facts you are comfortable giving the agent, and review recommendations before acting on them.
