# Practice: explain a decision

Synthetic facts: a vendor supplied 1,000 rows. After resolving identity and scope, 25 new target clinicians have usable current affiliations. Price is known; product reuse terms remain unclear. A colleague says, “This proves we should buy it immediately.”

Explain your assessment and next action in three ordinary sentences. Keep the useful value and the unresolved condition visible. Then explain what evidence could change your recommendation.

For an experience-story exercise, choose one incident from your actual work. State the situation, your own decision/actions and the outcome you can support. A missing result should remain a question, not a fabricated achievement.
