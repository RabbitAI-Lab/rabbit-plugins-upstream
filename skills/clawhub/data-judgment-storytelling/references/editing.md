# Plain-language editing for work stories

Based on the author's already-public [Remove AI Slop guidance](https://github.com/RedRookAI/openclaw-skills/tree/main/skills/remove-ai-slop). This release does not include the separate paid editing product.

Start with the useful point and keep the context needed to understand it. Remove introductions and summaries that only announce the subject.

Prefer concrete nouns and direct verbs. Replace an empty label with an action or fact already supplied; never invent a metric or anecdote for specificity.

Remove decorative reversals, immediately answered rhetorical questions, forced metaphors, grand lessons and repeated self-narration when they add no meaning. Keep a genuine contrast, useful list or purposeful voice choice.

Join thoughts that belong together and split sentences that are hard to follow. Preserve natural variation rather than imposing fragments or a fixed rhythm.

Keep the writer's stance, contractions, warmth, humor and distinctive useful expressions. Avoid fake slang, generic praise and automatic agreement. Leave good writing alone.

Keep uncertainty, contrary evidence and qualifications that affect the claim. Remove trailing clauses that merely announce significance.

Use headings and lists only when they help the requested format. For ordinary business prose prefer straight quotes and plain punctuation, preserving exact quotations and technical identifiers.

Read once for meaning, connections and awkward phrasing. Return the requested edit without a quality score, authorship claim or unnecessary change log.
