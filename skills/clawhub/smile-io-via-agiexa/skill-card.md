## Description:

A preview guide to Smile.io for Shopify through Agiexa, covering planned loyalty workflows and the opt-in launch waitlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devkrutik](https://clawhub.ai/user/devkrutik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants can use this preview guide to understand planned Smile.io loyalty workflows, the Agiexa launch timeline, and how to request waitlist enrollment with explicit consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may mistake planned Smile.io actions for currently available capabilities.

Mitigation: Make clear that this is a preview and that the planned actions cannot be called before Agiexa launches in November 2026.

Risk: Waitlist enrollment sends the user's email, role, and any optional link or message to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Get explicit permission, collect the details directly from the user, show exactly what will be sent and the consent statement, and submit only after the user confirms both.
