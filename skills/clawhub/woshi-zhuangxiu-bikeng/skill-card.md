## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reference corpus includes non-bedroom renovation topics and home-buying claims outside the declared scope.

Mitigation: Keep answers focused on bedroom renovation and treat out-of-scope material as unvetted background rather than authoritative guidance.

Risk: The reference corpus repeatedly includes promotional contact snippets.

Mitigation: Avoid surfacing promotional contact prompts unless the user explicitly asks for source attribution.

Risk: Renovation advice can affect structural work, utilities, waterproofing, and child safety.

Mitigation: Tell users to verify structural, electrical, waterproofing, property-management, and safety decisions with qualified local professionals before work begins.
