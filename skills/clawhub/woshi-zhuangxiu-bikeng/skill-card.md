## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General material and formaldehyde advice may not fit a home's actual exposure or health needs.

Mitigation: Confirm material and health decisions with qualified local professionals.

Risk: Renovation changes or child-safety suggestions may miss structural and local code requirements.

Mitigation: Have qualified local professionals verify structural, code, and child-safety decisions before work begins.
