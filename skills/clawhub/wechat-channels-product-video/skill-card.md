## Description:

Creates a vertical WeChat Channels product showcase video from a real product photo and confirmed product information, with a title and publishing copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product marketers and merchants use a real product photo and confirmed facts to plan and generate a vertical WeChat Channels showcase video, then prepare a title and publishing copy. The user reviews and publishes the content separately.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A shared Beatra device token can spend credits and access multiple tool categories.

Mitigation: Review the account access before installation and require explicit approval of the estimated cost before each paid generation stage.

Risk: The package can silently update its installed files.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates when each package change must be reviewed deliberately.
