## Description: <br>
Guides a Value Proposition Canvas workshop for one persona or segment, turning user-provided research into Jobs, Pains, Gains, Pain Relievers, Gain Creators, Products and Services, and a next-step prioritization choice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[monikazapisekstudio](https://clawhub.ai/user/monikazapisekstudio) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product designers, founders, and product teams use this skill to structure interviews, notes, persona documents, or early research into a focused Value Proposition Canvas. It is also useful when a team has no research yet and needs a research plan before mapping a canvas. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User research may include names, emails, employer secrets, client-confidential details, or other sensitive material. <br>
Mitigation: Redact sensitive research content before use and run the workshop in a dedicated project folder. <br>
Risk: The skill saves derived Markdown files locally, which can persist private research conclusions or quotes. <br>
Mitigation: Choose the output path explicitly before the workshop starts and review generated files before sharing them. <br>
