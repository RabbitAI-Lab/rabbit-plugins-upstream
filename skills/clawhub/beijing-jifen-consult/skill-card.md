## Description:

This skill guides users through a bundled local HTML simulator for Beijing points-settlement scoring, interpreting eligibility, score breakdowns, future projections, and planning scenarios without presenting results as official.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users evaluating Beijing points-settlement eligibility use this skill to open the bundled local simulator, enter verified personal facts, and receive plain-language interpretation of score details, projections, and next-step planning. The skill is for local simulation and consultation only, not official review or guaranteed outcome prediction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The simulator handles Beijing settlement-related personal details in the user's browser.

Mitigation: Use it only when comfortable entering those details locally, and clear the saved form cache after use, especially on shared or managed computers.

Risk: Scores, projected thresholds, and planning suggestions may be mistaken for official results.

Mitigation: Treat all outputs as non-official simulations for reference only and verify eligibility, policy changes, and formal decisions through official channels or qualified professionals.
