## Description:

Helps users estimate Beijing residency-points outcomes with a bundled local simulator, explain score drivers and gaps, and frame planning suggestions as unofficial simulations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users evaluating Beijing points-based settlement eligibility use this skill to collect confirmed facts, run a local simulation, interpret score components and gaps, and identify next steps. The skill is designed for advisory simulation only and does not replace official review or professional legal, tax, or immigration advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users enter residency, employment, tax, age, and legal-history details into a browser page that auto-saves them in the local browser profile.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when comfortable with local browser storage, and clear saved records after use, especially on shared devices.

Risk: Simulation outputs may be mistaken for official Beijing residency-points results or guaranteed settlement outcomes.

Mitigation: Present results as unofficial simulations, cite confirmed facts and assumptions, and direct users to official channels or qualified professionals for formal decisions.
