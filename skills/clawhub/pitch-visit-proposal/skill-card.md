## Description:

Guides sales representatives through preparing for, running, and following up on China-market B2B customer visits where they present a proposal or solution to stakeholders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales teams and account leaders use this skill to prepare a 72-hour visit brief, run a 30/60/90-minute proposal meeting, handle stakeholder objections, and produce a 24-hour follow-up/debrief for China-market B2B opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global use for China-market B2B sales contexts

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-market sales guidance may be applied as business advice outside the user's actual customer, industry, or employer policy context.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as contextual planning guidance and have account leadership review customer-facing commitments, next steps, and CRM updates before use.

Risk: WeChat, CRM, follow-up, and stakeholder notes may involve personal data, customer communication records, or retention requirements.

Mitigation: Check employer privacy, record-retention, and customer-communication policies before collecting, storing, or sharing visit details.

Risk: Gift and hospitality recommendations can create anti-bribery or customer-policy concerns.

Mitigation: Apply the employer's anti-bribery and customer gift policies before offering gifts or hospitality, and avoid restricted or high-value items.
