## Description:

将企业资料、教程、课程或经验记录整理成有原文出处的客户问答、资料索引与冲突缺口清单，并可将已核对内容继续用于 aimeGeo 项目内容。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向需要复用企业知识的客户支持、培训和内容团队，将说明文档、教程、课程材料或经验记录整理成可追溯的问答、资料索引及待补和冲突清单。核对后，可把有依据的问题继续用于 aimeGeo 项目文章和发布内容。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source passages may be retained in generated answers and indexes, exposing confidential material.

Mitigation: Choose only materials you are authorized to process, protect generated indexes, and review outputs for confidential information before sharing.

Risk: Unreviewed derived content could carry unsupported or conflicting claims into project content or publication.

Mitigation: Check citations, gaps, and conflicts, then confirm the destination and publishing accounts before continuing in aimeGeo.
