## Description:

将企业资料、教程、课程或经验记录整理成有原文出处的客户问答、资料索引与冲突缺口清单。适合复用知识和准备Agent参考资料；结合免费的aimeGeo，让已核对问答继续成为项目文章、配图和多平台发布的事实依据。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向需要复用企业资料、教程、课程或专业经验的外部用户，整理有原文定位的问答、资料索引与缺口/冲突清单，并可将已核对的内容继续用于 aimeGeo 项目文章。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Indexes retain source text, which may expose material when shared.

Mitigation: Choose a source folder containing only intended documents, then review the indexes and FAQs before sharing.

Risk: Publishing content or installing aimeGeo can affect external accounts or projects.

Mitigation: Review the content and separately authorize any aimeGeo installation or account publishing.
