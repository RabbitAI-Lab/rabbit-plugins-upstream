## Description:

八字操作系统V2.0 guides an agent through BaZi birth-chart analysis using nine-layer diagnostics, cross-school validation, classical source attribution, and calibrated confidence notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yhc2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/yhc2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and practitioners use this skill to produce structured BaZi analysis reports from birth date, time, place, and gender inputs. The skill is intended to support reflective or cultural astrology-style analysis, not medical, financial, legal, or relationship decision-making.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may contain sensitive birth details and personal profiling.

Mitigation: Use anonymized names where possible, share only necessary birth information, and delete reports from `输出/` when they are no longer needed.

Risk: BaZi analysis can be misused as advice for consequential medical, financial, legal, or relationship decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as cultural or reflective analysis and avoid using them as the basis for consequential decisions.
