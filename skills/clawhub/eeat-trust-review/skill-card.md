## Description:

Review a page and its site for experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust the way a Google quality rater would. Use for "check our E-E-A-T", "do we need author bios", "is our site trustworthy enough for health or finance content" or traffic lost after a core update. Returns trust gaps with fixes and spam-policy risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Site owners, editors, and SEO teams use this skill to review a page and its surrounding site for trust signals, identify evidence-based gaps and spam-policy risks, and prioritize concrete improvements.
