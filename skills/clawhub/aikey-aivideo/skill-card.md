## Description:

Guides a solo creator through planning, reviewing, generating, and finishing an AI-generated video from an idea, with cost approval before image or video generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo creators use the skill to turn an idea or approved screenplay into a fully AI-generated video, from story and shot planning through prompts, generation review, editing, and final review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI KEY configuration and shared reference documents may be missing or unavailable for review.

Mitigation: Check the configuration and shared documents before production; stop if required setup or safety guidance cannot be confirmed.

Risk: Prompts and reference images may be sent to an external generation provider, and generation can incur costs.

Mitigation: Check the provider's data terms, avoid sensitive material until understood, and approve estimated image and video generation costs before submission.
