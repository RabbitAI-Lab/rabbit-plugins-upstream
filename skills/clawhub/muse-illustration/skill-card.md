## Description:

Turns poems, imagery, or a brief idea into illustration prompts and images using a reusable style library.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stayfoool](https://clawhub.ai/user/stayfoool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and designers use this skill to translate Chinese poetry, imagery, or short ideas into styled illustration or poster concepts and generated artwork.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation can consume credits, especially when requesting multiple variants.

Mitigation: Tell users the estimated per-image cost before generating and confirm the desired image count.

Risk: A style reference image may influence the generated composition too closely.

Mitigation: Use references for style and brushwork only, and instruct the image tool not to copy their composition.

Risk: Saving generated images and case notes changes the skill's case library.

Mitigation: Archive only results the user explicitly accepts.
