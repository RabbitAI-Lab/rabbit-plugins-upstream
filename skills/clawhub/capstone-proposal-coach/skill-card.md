## Description:

Capstone proposal coach: section frameworks, system diagram and task split, milestones with acceptance artifacts, zero-fabrication references, defense prep, AI declaration draft. Coaching only. Use when a student writes a project proposal, literature review, technical route, or timeline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Undergraduate students use this skill to plan embedded engineering capstone proposals, organize literature they supply, map technical work and milestones, and prepare for proposal reviews while keeping the final academic work their own.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposal guidance may not match a student's course requirements or AI-use policy.

Mitigation: Follow the institution's template and AI-use rules, and review the framework with the supervisor before submission.

Risk: Unverified references or technical advice could weaken assessed work.

Mitigation: Use references supplied by the student and verify each source and technical claim before including it.
