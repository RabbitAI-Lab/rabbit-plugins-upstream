## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation or furniture advice may be mistaken for a site-specific safety assessment.

Mitigation: Seek qualified on-site assessment for structural changes, waterproofing, electrical or gas work, child safety, and damaged furniture; follow applicable local requirements.
