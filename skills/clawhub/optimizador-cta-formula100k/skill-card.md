## Description:

Analyzes, diagnoses, and improves Spanish video calls to action using FORMULA 100K patterns, producing CTA diagnostics and revised variants for stronger comment engagement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and content teams use this skill to review a CTA or full video script, identify missing CTA components, and receive three improved Spanish CTA variants tailored to the creator's real industry.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate on broad requests about improving video endings or engagement.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants CTA optimization before applying the full diagnostic and rewrite workflow.

Risk: CTA suggestions may be poorly targeted if the user's industry, offer, lead magnet, or keyword is missing.

Mitigation: Ask for missing context or use explicit placeholders instead of inventing business details.

Risk: Persuasive CTA rewrites may use marketing vocabulary that does not fit technical, scientific, craft, or non-marketing industries.

Mitigation: Identify the user's real industry first and adapt examples, terminology, and objections to that industry.
