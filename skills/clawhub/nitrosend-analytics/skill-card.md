## Description:

Reviews live Nitrosend account, campaign, flow, or message metrics and turns them into bounded recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Email marketers and account operators use this skill to review Nitrosend account, campaign, flow, or message performance and identify testable improvements from live metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected Nitrosend account data may be exposed through permissions broader than the review needs, or analyzed for an unintended request.

Mitigation: Review connected Nitrosend permissions, keep requests explicitly scoped to Nitrosend, and configure recurring reports only when wanted.
