## Description:

AI Face Swap & Viral Meme Video Generator for OpenClaw agents. Create instant motion transfer and face swap meme videos (Drake, Bulin 47, Coldplay Kiss Cam, Ozil, Flying Tesla, Spooky Skeletons) with 100% facial identity preservation and synchronized audio from a single photo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to browse TrendSwap video templates, check account credits, submit portrait images for AI video generation, and retrieve generation status and video URLs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portrait image URLs are sent to TrendSwap for external processing.

Mitigation: Use only portraits you are authorized and comfortable sharing with TrendSwap.

Risk: Video generation can consume account credits, and API keys or alternate endpoints require care.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and leave TRENDSWAP_BASE_URL unset unless you trust the alternate endpoint.
