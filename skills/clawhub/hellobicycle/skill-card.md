## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prices, city coverage, vehicle availability, and app policies can change by location and time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current coverage, prices, ride-pass terms, and dispute options in the official app before relying on the guidance. <br>
Risk: Shared-ride decisions can involve personal safety tradeoffs, especially for late-night or long-distance trips. <br>
Mitigation: Apply conservative safety checks such as confirming vehicle details and sharing the trip, and avoid high-risk trips when safety conditions are uncertain. <br>
