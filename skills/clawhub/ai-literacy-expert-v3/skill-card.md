## Description: <br>
A Chinese AI-literacy teaching skill that supports course explanation, lesson planning, assessment design, and p5.js single-file HTML interactive courseware for secondary school, university, and teacher audiences. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gqliang2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/gqliang2026) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Educators, students, and course developers use this skill to teach AI literacy, prepare lessons, design assessments, and generate interactive p5.js classroom demonstrations with explicit validation and privacy guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated browser courseware may include network, upload, camera, microphone, or student-data handling behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated HTML and JavaScript before classroom use, avoid real student personal data, and require synthetic or public data for demonstrations. <br>
Risk: Users may receive runnable p5.js courseware when they expected static slides or printable notes. <br>
Mitigation: Ask explicitly for static slides, printable notes, or no code when interactive HTML output is not desired. <br>
Risk: Educational examples, citations, or AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate or unsupported. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the skill's verification guidance in the teaching flow and require source checks before using claims in formal coursework. <br>
