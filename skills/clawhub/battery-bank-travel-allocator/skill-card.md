## Description:

Use when planning battery power for a day out (travel, hiking, festival, conference, photography) — realistic deliverable Wh from power banks after voltage-conversion, heat, age, and cycle derating; per-device demand from screen hours × activity multipliers (navigation, photos, hotspot); stop-charge ceilings; over/under-provisioned verdicts and solar-panel yield reality check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Travelers, hikers, festival-goers, conference attendees, and photographers use this skill to estimate whether their power banks can cover a day's device use and decide what to pack.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated battery capacity and device demand may differ from real-world performance.

Mitigation: Check remaining capacity before leaving a bank behind and allow extra margin for heavy use.

Risk: Battery-bank travel rules may vary by airline.

Mitigation: Verify your airline's battery requirements before traveling.
