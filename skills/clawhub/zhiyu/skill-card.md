## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to generate A-share market technical analysis, scenario planning, key support and resistance levels, and risk-aware commentary in a public-commentator-inspired style. It is intended for general market analysis and should not be treated as personalized investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce stock-market commentary that may be mistaken for personalized trading advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the investment-risk disclaimer, use probability language, avoid definitive buy or sell instructions, and do not use the output as the sole basis for trading. <br>
Risk: Current-market analysis may be misleading if stale or missing market data is presented as current. <br>
Mitigation: Verify live market data for current questions and clearly state the analysis time and data cutoff before drawing conclusions. <br>
Risk: Style imitation could be confused with the original commentator's own views. <br>
Mitigation: State that outputs are based on a style summarized from public content and are not the original person's views. <br>
