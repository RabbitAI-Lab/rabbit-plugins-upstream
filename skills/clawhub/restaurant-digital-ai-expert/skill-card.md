## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Templates and recommendations may be overbroad or incomplete for a specific restaurant, franchise, jurisdiction, or operating model. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and have qualified business, legal, privacy, security, and food-safety reviewers validate them before implementation. <br>
Risk: Some workflows may involve privacy-sensitive practices such as franchise data sharing, customer traceability, camera livestreams, GPS, or facial recognition. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm lawful basis, consent, data minimization, retention, vendor controls, and local compliance requirements before using those plans operationally. <br>
