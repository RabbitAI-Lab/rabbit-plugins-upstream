## Description:

将多份车险报价按保障、免赔、免责和增值服务统一整理，帮助用户比较方案并了解投保与理赔注意事项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

汽车出行从业者及车主可提供车辆情况和现有报价，获得统一口径的保障对比、免责要点及适合用车场景的配置建议；不推荐具体保险公司。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vehicle details, driving history and claims history may contain personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary details and remove identifying information from quotes before sharing.

Risk: Outdated or incomplete terms could lead to incorrect pricing, coverage or legal assumptions.

Mitigation: Confirm final prices and coverage with the insurer, and check applicable obligations with local official sources.
