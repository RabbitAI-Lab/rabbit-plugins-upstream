## Description:

Retrieves public Twitch channel, clip, VOD, chat replay, schedule, team, game, and search data through the Crawlora API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, creators, and developers use this skill to investigate public Twitch channels, live streams, clips, VODs, chat replay, schedules, teams, and categories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Twitch lookup terms and the Crawlora API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only for lookups you are comfortable sharing with Crawlora; keep CRAWLORA_API_KEY out of logs, screenshots, shell history, and committed files.
