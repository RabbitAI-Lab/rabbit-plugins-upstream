## Description: <br>
ADHD Assistant provides ADHD-friendly planning, task breakdown, time management, prioritization, body doubling, routine support, and emotional regulation guidance for OpenClaw users. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thinktankmachine](https://clawhub.ai/user/thinktankmachine) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Individuals using OpenClaw use this skill to organize daily life, break overwhelming work into concrete next actions, manage time blindness, create accountability check-ins, and review routines with ADHD-aware support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can steer broad productivity requests into ADHD-specific support. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when ADHD-specific framing is appropriate, and review responses for overbroad or unwanted mental-health framing. <br>
Risk: The skill may store health-adjacent details such as ADHD status, treatment context, emotional sensitivities, or productivity patterns. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid saving diagnosis, medication, therapy, or emotional-sensitivity information unless memory controls allow users to inspect, edit, and delete it. <br>
