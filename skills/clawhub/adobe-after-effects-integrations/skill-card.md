## Description:

Guides developers in choosing, building, packaging, installing, and debugging After Effects panels, native plugins, and bridges for external applications and AI agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to choose an After Effects integration approach and to build, package, install, troubleshoot, or secure CEP and UXP panels, C++ plugins, and automation bridges.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Enabling PlayerDebugMode can allow unsigned CEP extensions to load and weaken protection, and the setting may remain enabled.

Mitigation: Use debug mode only for trusted development, avoid untrusted extensions while it is enabled, and restore the setting afterward.
