## Description: <br>
Tech Manager Office coordinates five expert roles to help validated technology assets move through assessment, transaction strategy, due diligence, contract drafting, and handoff planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External technology transfer professionals, founders, and business teams use this skill to evaluate validated TRL 8-9+ technologies, design licensing or transfer strategies, prepare due diligence materials, and draft deal and handoff plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can generate IP, valuation, negotiation, and contract guidance that may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific transaction. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with qualified business, intellectual-property, and legal professionals before relying on them. <br>
Risk: The workflow is advisory and may not reflect jurisdiction-specific technology transfer rules or deal constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm regulatory, contractual, and compliance requirements for the relevant transaction and geography. <br>
