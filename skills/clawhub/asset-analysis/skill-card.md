## Description:

Provides a standard operating procedure for fundamental and technical analysis of individual stocks and ETFs across A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and U.S. stocks, combining real EPS checks, multi-model valuation, financial statements, technical indicators, and market-regime calibration into a standardized quantitative investment research report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Analysts, investors, and agent users use this skill to structure stock and ETF research that checks real market and financial data before producing valuation, scenario analysis, technical analysis, and regime-adjusted conclusions. It is intended as analytical assistance rather than personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad investment-analysis terms and produce financial analysis that users could mistake for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as analytical assistance, require users or reviewers to verify market and financial data, and avoid treating the report as individualized financial advice.

Risk: Market prices, financial statements, and technical indicators can be stale, missing, or unavailable.

Mitigation: Use current authoritative market and financial data sources, and block report generation when key data such as EPS, net profit, OHLC prices, or technical indicators cannot be verified.
