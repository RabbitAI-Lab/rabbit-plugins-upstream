## Use Case:

Developers and designers use this skill to create or revise images and diagrams for documentation, web pages, reviews, and release artifacts. It helps preserve requested formats and validate the generated assets and their downstream use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The image checker verifies file presence and freshness, but cannot determine whether an image is visually clear or suitable.

Mitigation: Inspect the generated images and their rendered downstream documents before committing or releasing them.
