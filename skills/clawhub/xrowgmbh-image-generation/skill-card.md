## Description:

Create or revise document, PDF, web, or review images with the requested format, sharp raster output, and artifact validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to create, regenerate, or review images and diagrams for documentation, web pages, PDFs, and release artifacts. It helps preserve requested formats and validate image assets in downstream documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: File-presence and timestamp checks do not establish visual quality, and downstream documents may not display the intended image correctly.

Mitigation: Scope the checker to the intended repository and review the generated images and rebuilt documents visually.
