## Use Case:

Developers and content teams use this skill to create, revise, and review images for documentation, PDFs, websites, and release artifacts. It guides them in preserving requested formats and checking the final rendered output.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: File-presence and freshness checks do not establish image quality or correctness.

Mitigation: Specify the intended repository and image formats, visually review generated images, and inspect rebuilt documents or other final artifacts.
