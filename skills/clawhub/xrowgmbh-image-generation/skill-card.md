## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generating image siblings or rebuilding documents may change existing files.

Mitigation: Review image and document changes before accepting the result.

Risk: Validation scans the selected workspace for image prompt files and may report unrelated missing or stale siblings.

Mitigation: Choose the intended workspace root and review reported paths before acting.
