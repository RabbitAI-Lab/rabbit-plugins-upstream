This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to create or revise images, diagrams, screenshots, and graphics for documentation and release artifacts, then check that generated files are present and current.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image or document changes may be incorrect or misleading even when generated files are present and current.

Mitigation: Inspect the final image and any documents that embed it; treat the checker as a presence and freshness check, not a quality review.
