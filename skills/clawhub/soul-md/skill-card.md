## Description: <br>
SOUL.MD helps an agent embody a user-provided digital identity by reading SOUL.md, STYLE.md, examples, and data as persona and voice context. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaronjmars](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaronjmars) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and creators use SOUL.MD to build or operate a persona and voice specification for drafting content, brainstorming, and exploring ideas in a documented voice. The skill can also interview the user or analyze user-provided material to draft SOUL.md, STYLE.md, and calibration examples. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks agents to ingest personal writing, social posts, influences, and other sensitive source material to build a persona profile. <br>
Mitigation: Use only consented and redacted material, and do not place secrets, credentials, health, financial, legal, private-message, or third-party personal data in the data folders. <br>
Risk: Generated persona files and outputs may misleadingly claim to be a real person or imply unauthorized endorsement. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated SOUL.md, STYLE.md, examples, and outputs before use, and disclose AI-generated persona use where appropriate. <br>
