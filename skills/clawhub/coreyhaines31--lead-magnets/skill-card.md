## Description: <br>
Helps plan, create, and optimize lead magnets for email capture and lead generation, including format selection, gating strategy, distribution, and measurement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and growth practitioners use this skill to choose and plan lead magnets that capture qualified leads and connect naturally to product adoption. It guides recommendations, content outlines, gating and capture plans, distribution channels, and measurement plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use existing product marketing context files when present. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those context files limited to business information that is appropriate to use in lead-generation planning. <br>
Risk: Marketing recommendations may be mismatched to the user's audience, funnel stage, or conversion goals if key context is missing. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the target customer, buyer stage, current lead-generation performance, available assets, and measurement goals before acting on the plan. <br>
