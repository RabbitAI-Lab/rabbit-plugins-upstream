## Description: <br>
A practical guide for maintaining composure and effectiveness when project boundaries expand unexpectedly. Apply Stoic philosophy to one of the most common sources of workplace frustration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, and project contributors use this skill to respond calmly and practically when project scope expands. It provides Stoic framing, reflective exercises, and communication scripts for clarifying tradeoffs and documenting scope changes. <br>
