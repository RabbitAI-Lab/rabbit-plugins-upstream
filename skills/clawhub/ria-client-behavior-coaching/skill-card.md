## Description: <br>
Helps registered investment advisers coach clients through market-drop anxiety using loss-aversion framing, pre-commitment, and goal-based communication. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Financial advisers and advisory teams use this skill to prepare client communication, review risk-tolerance conversations, and distinguish behavioral panic from legitimate plan changes during market downturns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may shape how an agent discusses client behavior during market drops and could be mistaken for client-specific investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with professional fiduciary judgment, suitability analysis, and compliance review. <br>
Risk: Loss-aversion framing could suppress legitimate client concerns or real plan changes. <br>
Mitigation: Explicitly distinguish behavioral panic from a genuine change in goals, constraints, or risk capacity before recommending a response. <br>
