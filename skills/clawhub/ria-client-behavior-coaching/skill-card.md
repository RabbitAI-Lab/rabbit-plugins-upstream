## Description:

Helps financial advisers coach clients through loss aversion during market downturns using plan-based communication and pre-commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Financial advisers use this skill to prepare for conversations with clients who want to sell during market declines, and to frame performance reviews and risk discussions around the client's plan. It is a coaching aid, not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A request to sell may reflect a genuine change in the client's circumstances or plan rather than loss aversion.

Mitigation: Assess whether a genuine plan change is warranted, and apply the client's plan and professional fiduciary judgment before recommending action.

Risk: Client details could be shared beyond what is needed for coaching.

Mitigation: Share only necessary client details and review communications professionally.
