## Description: <br>
Fact Checking Explainers audits explainer drafts against cited sources and code references so checkable factual claims are supported, corrected, or removed. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[analyticalmonk](https://clawhub.ai/user/analyticalmonk) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to verify explainer claims before delivery. It produces a claim-by-claim audit that identifies unsupported, contradicted, or missing-source claims and recommends concrete fixes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may inspect cited sources, draft content, or relevant code while verifying claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only on materials you are comfortable including in the fact-checking process, especially for sensitive drafts or private repositories. <br>
