## Description:

Reads a LinkedIn post and its thread to draft three ranked, voice-matched comments, saves a selected option in Taplio, and schedules it only after explicit approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External professionals and LinkedIn users use this skill to draft context-aware comments on LinkedIn posts, save a selected option to Taplio, and schedule it only after explicit approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A scheduled comment may be published publicly under your name and cannot be recalled through Taplio after posting.

Mitigation: Review the comment wording and language, and schedule it only after explicit approval.

Risk: Connecting Taplio allows the skill to read account preferences and relevant LinkedIn content and save drafts.

Mitigation: Connect the intended Taplio account and review the generated draft before approving publication.
