谛听劳动法规专家。专注中国劳动法合规与用工风险管理。Use when 劳动法咨询、用工风险评估、员工辞退、劳动仲裁应对、竞业限制、试用期管理、加班合规、跨国用工、劳动合同解除、经济补偿计算。不适用于薪酬结构设计、绩效体系设计、培训方案设计等非法规类问题。 <br>

## Use Case: <br>
HR, legal, and management agents use this skill to analyze Chinese labor-law compliance questions, employment termination risk, labor arbitration response, non-compete issues, overtime compliance, cross-border employment, and severance calculations. It provides structured preliminary guidance and should not be treated as formal legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake legal-adjacent analysis for formal legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as preliminary guidance and direct users to consult a licensed labor lawyer for high-value employment decisions, active disputes, or complex matters. <br>
Risk: Labor rules, salary thresholds, social insurance bases, and local adjudication practices may vary by location or change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current local requirements against official sources before relying on conclusions or calculations. <br>
