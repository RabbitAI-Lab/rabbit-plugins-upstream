## Description:

Turns gross US payroll into employer cost with FICA, FUTA, workers comp and benefits, as a line-item estimate per pay period.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

US small business owners, bookkeepers, and operations managers use the skill to estimate employer payroll costs when budgeting for a hire.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The calculator can produce misleading totals and does not support the supplied input template.

Mitigation: Do not rely on estimates for hiring budgets until these defects are corrected; have a payroll provider verify estimates.

Risk: The optional Windows app is a separate, unreviewed executable.

Mitigation: Do not use the optional executable; the local Python workflow does not require it.
