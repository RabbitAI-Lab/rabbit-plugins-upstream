## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a sobriety companion to track alcohol-free streaks, log cravings and triggers, review recovery milestones and savings, and find support resources. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sobriety dates, cravings, triggers, reasons for quitting, and meeting-location searches are sensitive personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Check how the OpenClaw environment stores chat history or memory before relying on confidentiality, and avoid storing or sharing sensitive details beyond what is needed. <br>
Risk: Alcohol withdrawal can be medically dangerous, especially when symptoms are severe. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for support and tracking only; consult a healthcare provider for medical withdrawal concerns or severe withdrawal symptoms. <br>
Risk: Support-resource or meeting searches can reveal location information. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the minimum location detail needed to find resources and review any connected tool behavior before use. <br>
