## Description:

Search public Facebook Marketplace listings by location and filters, and retrieve listing details for local shopping and resale price research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People shopping locally or researching resale prices use this skill to find public Marketplace listings, compare prices, and inspect item details without a Facebook account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill requires installing the ClawLink plugin and pairing an OpenClaw device with ClawLink.

Mitigation: Install and pair only after the user agrees to connect their device to ClawLink.

Risk: Marketplace searches and item lookups spend ClawLink credit, especially when repeated across many listings.

Mitigation: Check live tool prices and get approval before larger searches or item-by-item loops.

Risk: Marketplace listings can change after a search.

Mitigation: State when results were checked and confirm details before relying on a listing.
