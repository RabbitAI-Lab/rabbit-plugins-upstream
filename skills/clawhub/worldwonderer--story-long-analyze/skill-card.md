## Description:

Analyzes long-form Chinese web novels chapter by chapter, producing plot, pacing, character, setting, and writing-style insights for authors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Novelists and editors use this skill to examine the opening chapters or a complete web novel, track story and character development, and identify writing techniques they can adapt. It can resume previous analysis and optionally organize inspiration across books.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analysis may copy excerpts of private or copyrighted novels into local writing-style profiles.

Mitigation: Use only texts you are permitted to analyze, review stored excerpts, and restrict access to the analysis directory.
