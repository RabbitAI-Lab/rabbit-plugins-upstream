## Description:

Turns a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer video through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, production, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn documents or briefs into narrated explainer, courseware, and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to the dLazy service.

Mitigation: Confirm the service is trusted and avoid attaching sensitive material without authorization.

Risk: The CLI may store an API key locally, and a global install leaves a persistent executable.

Mitigation: Protect the local API key and use the pinned npx invocation when a persistent installation is unnecessary.
