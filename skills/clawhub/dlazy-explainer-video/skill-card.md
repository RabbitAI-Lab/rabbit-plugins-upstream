## Description:

Turns a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer video with an outline, storyboard, voiceover, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn documents or briefs into narrated explainer, courseware, and training videos through dLazy's hosted service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only content approved for third-party processing; attach files only when needed.

Risk: Using the service requires a dLazy API key.

Mitigation: Use a scoped key that can be rotated or revoked.
