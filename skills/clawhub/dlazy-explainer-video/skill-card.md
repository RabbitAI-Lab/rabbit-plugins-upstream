## Description:

Turns a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer, courseware, or training video with an outline, storyboard, voiceover, assembly, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, educators, and teams can turn a selected document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer, report, courseware, or training video. The skill is intended for explicit video-generation requests where the user agrees to send selected material to dLazy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad report-analysis triggers could send document content to dLazy without a clear request to use its video service.

Mitigation: Activate the skill only for an explicit video-generation request, and send selected materials only after the user agrees to share them with dLazy.
