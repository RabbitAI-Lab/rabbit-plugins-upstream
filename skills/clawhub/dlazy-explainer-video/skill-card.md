## Description:

Turns a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer video through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, building, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating explainers, report videos, courseware, or training content use this skill to turn a document, topic, or brief into a narrated video through a project-based workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service, and attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Upload only files you are comfortable sharing, select the intended project, and consider service terms and credit costs.

Risk: Logging in saves an organization API key in the local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect access to the local configuration and revoke the key from the dashboard if needed.
