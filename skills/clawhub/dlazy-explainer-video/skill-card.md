## Description:

Turns documents, topics, or briefs into narrated explainer videos with outlines, storyboards, voiceovers, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn documents or briefs into narrated explainer, courseware, and training videos through dLazy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to dLazy's cloud service.

Mitigation: Review sensitive documents before sharing and confirm the service is suitable for the data.

Risk: An API key may be stored locally for dLazy CLI authentication.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key if exposed.

Risk: Installing the dLazy CLI runs a third-party npm package.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI package before installation, especially for sensitive workflows.
