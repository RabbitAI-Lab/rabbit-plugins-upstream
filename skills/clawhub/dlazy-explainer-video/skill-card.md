## Description:

Turns documents, topics, or briefs into narrated explainer videos using dLazy's hosted video workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer, training, or courseware video through dLazy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review files and prompts for sensitive content before submitting them to the service.

Risk: Signing in stores an API key locally for dLazy access.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key from the dLazy dashboard if needed.
