## Description:

Turns documents, topics, or briefs into narrated explainer videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, generation, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn documents or briefs into narrated explainer, courseware, and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected attachments may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review service use before installing and attach only files you intend to upload.

Risk: Logging in may persist an API key in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Use DLAZY_API_KEY per invocation if you do not want the key saved locally.
