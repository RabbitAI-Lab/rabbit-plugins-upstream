## Description:

Turns documents, topics, or briefs into narrated explainer videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, production, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business teams use this skill to turn documents, reports, or briefs into narrated explainer and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to the dLazy hosted service.

Mitigation: Attach only files you intend to upload and review sensitive content before sending.

Risk: The external npm CLI may be installed locally and API credentials may persist on disk.

Mitigation: Review the package before use; consider on-demand npx execution and per-invocation DLAZY_API_KEY to reduce local persistence.
