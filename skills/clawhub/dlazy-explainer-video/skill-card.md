## Description:

Turns a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer video by creating an outline, storyboard, voiceover, and validated build.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating explainers, report broadcasts, courseware, or training videos can use this skill to turn documents, topics, or briefs into narrated video projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was partial; the purpose of CLI login and organization project listing was not fully explained, and the Chinese guide contains a section that warrants review.

Mitigation: Before installing, review those operations and the noted section in the Chinese guide; use the CLI only if its access fits the intended workflow.

Risk: Files attached to a project are uploaded to dLazy media storage.

Mitigation: Review attached files before upload and omit material that should not be sent to the hosted service.
