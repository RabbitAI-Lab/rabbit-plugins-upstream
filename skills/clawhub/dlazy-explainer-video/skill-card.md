## Description:

Turns a document, topic, or brief into a narrated explainer video through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, building, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn documents or briefs into explainer, courseware, and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documents attached to a request are uploaded to dLazy storage; using the service may incur charges.

Mitigation: Send only documents approved for dLazy and account for service charges.

Risk: Requests use an organization API key, and this review does not cover the external CLI executable or the service's retention practices.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and revoke it when no longer needed; review the CLI and service terms before sending sensitive material.
