## Description:

Helps turn documents, topics, or briefs into narrated explainer and training videos with outlines, storyboards, and voiceover through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn a document or brief into a narrated explainer, courseware, or training video with dLazy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to dLazy for video generation.

Mitigation: Attach only files intended for dLazy processing and confirm the service is appropriate for their contents.

Risk: Signing in can save an organization API key on the local device.

Mitigation: Protect local access to the key and rotate or revoke it if compromised.
