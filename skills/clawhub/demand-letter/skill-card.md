## Description: <br>
Drafts firm, professional demand letters that state the facts, the legal or contractual basis, a specific demand, a deadline, and factual consequences. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill to draft demand letters for payment demands, contract breaches, cease-and-desist requests, refunds, return of property, or other formal pre-action resolution requests. The output is intended for review before sending and is not legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A generated demand letter may contain incorrect names, dates, amounts, deadlines, notice periods, or legal basis. <br>
Mitigation: Verify every factual and legal detail before sending, and have a qualified lawyer review important letters. <br>
Risk: Legal-adjacent wording may be interpreted as advice or may overstate remedies or consequences. <br>
Mitigation: Keep consequences factual, avoid threats or defamatory language, and treat the draft as a review aid rather than legal advice. <br>
Risk: Clearly-labelled placeholders may remain in the final letter when the user has not provided enough facts. <br>
Mitigation: Replace all placeholders with confirmed details or remove unsupported claims before delivery. <br>
