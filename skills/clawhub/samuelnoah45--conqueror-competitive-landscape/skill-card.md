## Description:

Maps who wins an SEO market by comparing recurring domains, content themes, keyword coverage, backlink strength, and opportunity areas using a connected Conqueror MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, SEO strategists, and developers use this skill to identify SEO market leaders, winning content patterns, ranking barriers, and winnable opportunity areas before choosing deeper competitor or keyword workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can query connected SEO and Search Console data for the selected project or domain.

Mitigation: Install it only for intended Conqueror MCP SEO research, review connected data sources, and confirm first-party Search Console access before use.

Risk: Competitive, traffic, and backlink conclusions can be incomplete or directional when data sources are unavailable or the query set is small.

Mitigation: Label estimates and small-query-set findings as directional, distinguish SEO competitors from business competitors, and avoid overstating exact traffic.

Risk: Local SEO markets can mix organic-page winners with Maps or local-pack winners.

Mitigation: Use local business and local SERP evidence where location matters, and clearly separate organic-page conclusions from Maps/local-pack conclusions.
