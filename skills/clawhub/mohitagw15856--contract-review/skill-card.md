## Description: <br>
Review and summarise any contract or legal agreement. Use when asked to review a contract, check an agreement, flag legal risks, or summarise key clauses. Produces a structured review with key terms, flagged clauses, risk rating, and plain English summary. Not a substitute for qualified legal advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and legal or business reviewers use this skill to summarize contracts, identify key terms, flag unusual or high-risk clauses, and surface missing clauses before seeking qualified legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Contract text can contain confidential legal or business information. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with contract text the user is comfortable sharing with the agent or provider. <br>
Risk: The review could be mistaken for legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as a review aid and consult a qualified lawyer before signing important agreements. <br>
