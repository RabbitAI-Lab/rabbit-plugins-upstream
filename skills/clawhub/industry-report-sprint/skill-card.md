## Description:

Researches a sector and drafts a source-cited industry report with a representative-product comparison, evidence gaps, and saved revisions in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and decision-makers use this skill to turn a sector name into a cited market brief covering demand, competition, products, barriers, and information gaps while retaining evidence and revisions in JishuDB.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup can download, install, configure, and launch Desktop; the Windows installer is described as unsigned.

Mitigation: Review the setup approval scope and installer details before authorizing installation; use an existing connection when available.

Risk: Incomplete or restricted research sources can produce misleading market conclusions.

Mitigation: Use authorized sources, cite inspected material, and label evidence gaps and uncertain claims explicitly.
