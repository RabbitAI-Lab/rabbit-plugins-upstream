## Description:

Research a sector and produce a source-cited industry report with a product comparison, evidence gaps, and retained revisions in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and decision-makers use this skill to turn a sector name into a cited market analysis, representative product comparison, and decision brief without supplying a report pack.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup and mandatory storage may change local application or client configuration and retain research material.

Mitigation: Review the installer approval envelope, including app and data destinations, MCP endpoint, client configuration target, and write scope; authorize storage only when appropriate.
