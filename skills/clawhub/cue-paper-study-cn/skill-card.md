## Description:

Creates a five-part study card from a supplied academic paper, anchoring claims to pages or sections and recording reproducibility details, references, open questions, and a review scaffold.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huhoo](https://clawhub.ai/user/huhoo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Researchers and students use this skill to turn supplied papers into traceable study cards, record missing reproducibility details, map references found in the paper, and prepare a literature review scaffold. It leaves academic evaluation and final arguments to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional online setup has API-key exposure risks.

Mitigation: Prefer the local/manual workflow until credential destination checks cover both documented methods and redirects.

Risk: External parsing may transmit documents without clear upload consent.

Mitigation: Approve the specific documents before external parsing and review the separately installed Cue component and its data handling.
