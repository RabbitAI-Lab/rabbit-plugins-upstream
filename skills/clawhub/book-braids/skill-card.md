## Description: <br>
Book braids services through Lokuli MCP. Use when user needs to find and book braids. Triggers on requests like "book a braids", "find braids near me", or any braids service request. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to search for braid services, check appointment availability, and create bookings through Lokuli. Users should confirm provider, time, price, and contact details before a booking is created. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may send personal contact details and appointment information to Lokuli when creating a booking. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, time, price, customer name, email, phone number, zip code, and appointment details before creating a booking. <br>
