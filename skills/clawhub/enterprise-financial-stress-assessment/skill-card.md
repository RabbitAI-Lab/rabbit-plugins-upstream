## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill produces financial and credit-risk assessments from public information, so conclusions can be wrong if sources are stale, incomplete, or uncited. <br>
Mitigation: Verify citations, source freshness, and any material claims before using the report for diligence, investment,招商, supplier, or partnership decisions. <br>
Risk: Reports about non-public companies may have limited financial disclosure and can contain inferred risk judgments. <br>
Mitigation: Treat missing financial data as a limitation, distinguish confirmed public facts from indirect signals, and avoid relying on the output as legal, investment, or trading advice. <br>
Risk: The generated Word-style report may persist sensitive company analysis on the local system. <br>
Mitigation: Handle generated documents according to the user's data retention, access-control, and confidentiality requirements. <br>
