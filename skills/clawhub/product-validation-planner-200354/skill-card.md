## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and lightweight decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before committing significant resources. It turns the request into a repeatable workflow, checklist, template, analysis, code change, or decision-support artifact with explicit assumptions and validation notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated product-validation advice may be incomplete or misleading when the user's market, constraints, or evidence are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, compare recommendations against the user's actual customer evidence, and ask for missing inputs when they materially change the plan. <br>
Risk: Optional code, workflow, or positioning changes could affect product decisions if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated business advice and any optional code or workflow changes before acting on them. <br>
