## Description:

A person goes to a place in Cyprus and reports what is there: photographs and answers with liveness proof (latest Base block hash in frame, an unedited video), signed. Limassol 200 EUR in 3 days, elsewhere in Cyprus from 200 EUR. Use when a task needs physical ground truth, proof of presence or an on-site inspection. Free feasibility answer within 24 hours; pay only an accepted task.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ordering can lead to payment for a physical-world task, including authorizing an escrow payment.

Mitigation: Set the scope, deadline, acceptance criteria, payer address, and spending limit with the operator; verify the signed offer and payment details; authorize payment only after acceptance.

Risk: Requests may disclose payer addresses and, above stated thresholds, controlling-person identification.

Mitigation: Share only the information required for the task and review the provider's terms before submitting it.
