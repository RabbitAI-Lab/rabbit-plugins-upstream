## Description:

Search and read Avoma meetings, transcriptions, recordings, users, and meeting insights through an OOMOL-connected account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with an Avoma account connected through OOMOL can find meeting records and review recordings, transcripts, user details, and meeting insights.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An Avoma read request can trigger installation of unverified software without separate approval.

Mitigation: Review and approve CLI setup separately; prefer a reviewed, version-pinned installation and assess the agent-skill installation, syncing, and hosted credential handling.
