## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow/Bubble users use this skill to learn about the service, complete registration themselves, troubleshoot sign-up problems, and navigate subscription activation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing a password, email verification code, payment proof, or card details in chat exposes sensitive information.

Mitigation: Never request these details; have users complete registration and payment on the official PowPow, PayPal, WeChat, or Huawei flows.

Risk: Subscription guidance may overlook recurring charges or a delay before payment activates an account.

Mitigation: Direct users to review the subscription terms on the official page, and explain that WeChat activation may require manual confirmation.
