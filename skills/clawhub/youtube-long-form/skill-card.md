## Use Case: <br>
Creators, channel operators, and social media teams use this skill to plan YouTube long-form videos around packaging, retention, and session contribution. It produces title and thumbnail concepts, cold-open and script structure, descriptions, chapters, and publishing handoff guidance. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: External publishing and scheduling workflows could affect a live YouTube channel if an agent proceeds without human review. <br>
Mitigation: Review and approve publish, delete, recreate, and scheduling actions in the downstream scheduling tool before execution. <br>
Risk: Incorrect or fabricated analytics could lead to bad content decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Read CTR, AVD, retention, traffic sources, and session metrics directly in YouTube Studio and do not invent missing metrics. <br>
Risk: Misleading packaging, bought views, or repetitive subscribe prompts can undermine retention and channel standing. <br>
Mitigation: Use accurate-but-curious packaging, refuse manipulative growth tactics, and keep calls to action limited and relevant. <br>
Risk: AI-generated footage may require viewer disclosure. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the Altered Content disclosure when AI-generated footage is used. <br>
