## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps produce practical validation plans, lightweight prototypes, positioning, reusable workflows, checklists, analyses, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad product, startup, SaaS, prototype, or business-planning prompts may activate the skill when the user only needs narrower advice. <br>
Mitigation: Review the invocation context and constrain the answer to the user's stated outcome, inputs, and success criteria. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs may be mistaken for validated market research. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and validate conclusions with users, market evidence, experiments, or other independent sources before major decisions. <br>
