## Description:

Turn written radio-drama ad-break lines into one spoken bumper clip per labeled slot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External radio-drama producers use the skill to turn their written bumper copy into a labeled pack of short spoken clips, with one clip for each slot. It supports pre-roll, post-roll, and other listed reads without inventing new sponsor copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires unrelated account permissions for an audio workflow.

Mitigation: Ask the publisher for speech-only authorization and review the requested permissions before granting access.

Risk: Uninstall checks can wrongly disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Ask the publisher for corrected uninstall checks and avoid relying on the current checks to remove a single skill safely.

Risk: Recurring device analytics and a shared credential may affect account privacy and access.

Mitigation: Review these implications before authorizing, and revoke device access in Beatra Console when needed.

Risk: Audio generation uses paid work.

Mitigation: Approve paid work explicitly before starting billable stages.
