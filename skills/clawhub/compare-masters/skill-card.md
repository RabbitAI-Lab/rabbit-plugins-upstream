## Description:

Compare Masters helps an agent compare Buddhist masters, schools, and perspectives with source-cited, non-ranking multi-tradition answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill when a user asks for comparative Buddhist perspectives. It selects relevant masters and structures a cited, non-ranking Markdown comparison with shared points, divergent stances, suitability notes, and follow-up questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Buddhist doctrinal or historical comparisons may be misleading if citations are missing or not checked for important use.

Mitigation: Require declared source citations and have users verify citations in FoJin or other primary references for religious or scholarly reliance.

Risk: Comparative answers can imply ranking or fabricate debate between masters.

Mitigation: Preserve the skill's non-ranking, no-fabricated-dialogue constraints and label divergence types in every comparison.
