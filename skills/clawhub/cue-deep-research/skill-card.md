## Description:

Produces source-linked research reports on public company, market, and industry information, with optional writing-style mimicry.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, researchers, and business users investigate publicly available company, market, industry, and regulatory information and create research reports with links to original sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The separately installed Cue runner can change independently of this reviewed skill release.

Mitigation: Review the external runner before installing or updating it.

Risk: Research topics and local mimic documents are sent to Cue; mimic documents may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use only research topics and reference documents you are comfortable sending to Cue; do not submit confidential, personal, regulated, or proprietary files.
