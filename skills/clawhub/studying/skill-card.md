## Description: <br>
Plans study schedules and runs study sessions with retrieval practice, spaced review, and exam countdown protocols. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students and working learners use this skill to plan exam, midterm, final, and certification study, then run study sessions based on retrieval practice, spaced review, timed practice, focus support, and test-day strategy. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Existing files in ~/studying/ or ~/clawic/studying/ could be moved into the skill data directory if they are mistaken for prior study memory. <br>
Mitigation: Before installation or migration, inspect those old paths and move or remove unrelated files. <br>
Risk: Personalized study planning may depend on local preference and session-history records. <br>
Mitigation: Keep only intended study preferences and session notes in ~/Clawic/data/studying/ and review them when plans appear inconsistent. <br>
