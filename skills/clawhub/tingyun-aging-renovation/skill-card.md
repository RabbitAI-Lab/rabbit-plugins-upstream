## Description:

Helps families plan home accessibility renovations for older adults by assessing needs, prioritizing safety improvements, comparing options and cost ranges, and preparing supervision and acceptance checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adult children funding renovations and older adults living in the home use this planning aid to assess functional needs, prioritize accessibility improvements, compare options and cost ranges, and prepare for professional review and construction acceptance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Household and health details shared for planning may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the planning task.

Risk: Ability assessments, measurements, construction safety, and subsidy guidance may need household-specific or current local confirmation.

Mitigation: Have qualified local professionals confirm assessments, measurements, and construction safety, and check current local subsidy requirements before committing money.
