## Description:

Provides decision support for home accessibility renovations through ability assessments, safety prioritization, renovation and material checklists, cost ranges, subsidy lookup guidance, and acceptance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adult children, caregivers, and older adults can use this skill to assess home accessibility needs, prioritize safety improvements, compare renovation options, and prepare questions and checks for qualified professionals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts or supporting documents may include sensitive household or health details.

Mitigation: Share only necessary details and redact identifiers from supporting documents.

Risk: Renovation requirements and subsidy eligibility depend on the property and current local rules.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning aids; ask qualified professionals to confirm technical requirements and current local authorities to confirm subsidy eligibility.
