## Description:

Liken identifies shared taste across things a user likes and suggests explainable recommendations in the same and other domains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iyabao](https://clawhub.ai/user/iyabao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who want to discover books, music, films, products, or experiences based on things they already enjoy can use this skill to understand their tastes and find explained recommendations across domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media titles or search queries may be shared with public lookup services during existence checks.

Mitigation: Avoid using private or sensitive interests that you do not want shared with Open Library, iTunes, Wikidata, or TVmaze.
