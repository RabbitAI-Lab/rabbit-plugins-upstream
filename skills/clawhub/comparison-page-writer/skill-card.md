## Description:

Write honest "best X", "X vs Y" and "X alternatives" pages from research, with the verdict first, stated criteria, real drawbacks for every option, dated and sourced competitor facts, a compact table and a fact-check sheet. Use for "write a comparison page", "best tools for", "alternatives to", "X vs Y".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Content marketers, SEO writers, and editors use this skill to produce sourced comparison pages that help readers choose among products. It structures the verdict, criteria, checked facts, drawbacks, comparison table, and an editor-ready fact-check sheet.
