## Description:

Guides founders and product teams through a repeatable process for validating product ideas and producing practical plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use the skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building, then turn their goals, constraints, and success criteria into a practical plan or deliverable.
