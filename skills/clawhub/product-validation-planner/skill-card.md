## Description:

Guides founders, product managers, makers, and small teams through a repeatable process for validating product ideas and producing practical plans, prototypes, or user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this agent to assess whether a product idea is worth building before making a large investment. It turns their goals and constraints into a practical workflow, checklist, lightweight analysis, prototype plan, or positioning artifact, then checks the result against their criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the planner for unrelated requests and produce irrelevant product guidance.

Mitigation: Keep activation focused on product idea validation and confirm that the user's request fits this purpose before producing a plan.
