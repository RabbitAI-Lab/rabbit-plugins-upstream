## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analyses, or decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It produces validation plans, prototype guidance, positioning, checklists, lightweight analysis, and follow-up risks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad product, startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation wording may activate the skill in contexts where a narrower planning skill was intended. <br>
Mitigation: Review the invocation context before relying on the output, and narrow trigger wording if tighter routing is needed. <br>
Risk: Validation plans can still depend on assumptions and generalized demand signals rather than the user's own customer evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions visible, collect user-specific evidence when it would change the recommendation, and verify the final plan against stated success criteria. <br>
