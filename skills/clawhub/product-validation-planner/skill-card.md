## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototype workflows, user-facing positioning, checklists, analysis, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It turns product validation requests into practical workflows, checklists, templates, lightweight analysis, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad startup and product-planning triggers, so it may activate for requests where product validation planning is only loosely relevant.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's desired outcome and constraints before producing a plan, and keep assumptions visible when proceeding without additional input.

Risk: Product validation guidance can overstate confidence if based on incomplete market or user evidence.

Mitigation: Present validation outputs as decision support, include success criteria and limits, and identify follow-up evidence needed before heavy investment.
