## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before significant investment. It guides demand validation, prototype planning, positioning, reusable workflows, checklists, lightweight analysis, and implementation support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad triggers may activate this skill for generic startup, SaaS, validation, or prototype requests where a narrower skill may be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome and constraints before applying the workflow, and ask only for missing information that materially changes the result. <br>
Risk: Planning and positioning guidance can be incomplete or misleading if the user's market, constraints, or success criteria are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions, validate outputs against explicit success criteria, and list remaining risks or follow-up work. <br>
