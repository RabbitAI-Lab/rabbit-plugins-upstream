## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analyses, code changes, or decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns product-validation requests into practical workflows, reusable checklists, templates, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning, code changes, or decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may make the skill appear for general startup or product planning requests.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for product-validation planning and confirm the user's intended outcome, constraints, inputs, and success criteria before producing the artifact.

Risk: Product-validation outputs can be mistaken for proof of market demand if assumptions and source limits are hidden.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions, evidence limits, validation checks, remaining risks, and follow-up work visible so users can review them before making business decisions.
