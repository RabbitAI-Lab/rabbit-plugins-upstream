## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test product ideas before investing heavily. It helps convert rough concepts into validation workflows, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning, and reusable planning artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for general product or startup conversations where a narrower skill would be preferable.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended product-validation outcome and switch context when the request is not about validating an idea, prototype, positioning, or planning artifact.

Risk: Planning outputs can contain incorrect assumptions or misleading validation guidance.

Mitigation: Make assumptions explicit, ask for missing information that materially changes the result, and review recommendations before acting on them.
