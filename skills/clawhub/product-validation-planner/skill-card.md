## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns rough inputs, constraints, and success criteria into practical validation workflows, checklists, prototype plans, positioning, analysis, or decision-support artifacts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad product or startup planning requests where precise routing matters. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow trigger phrases or require explicit use of $product-validation-planner before publishing broadly. <br>
