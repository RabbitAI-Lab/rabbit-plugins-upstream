## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, analyses, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns product-validation work into practical workflows, checklists, templates, lightweight analyses, code changes, and next-step guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad keywords and implicit invocation may cause the skill to appear in general product, SaaS, startup, or prototype conversations where it is not the intended tool.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants product-validation planning help before relying on the skill's workflow.

Risk: Product-validation outputs can become misleading if assumptions, constraints, or success criteria are incomplete.

Mitigation: Restate assumptions and success criteria, then validate the final artifact against the user's stated outcome.
