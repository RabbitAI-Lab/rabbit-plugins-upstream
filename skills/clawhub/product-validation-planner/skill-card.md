## Description:

Helps founders and product teams validate product ideas and turn findings into practical plans, prototypes, and positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External users such as solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to assess product ideas before investing heavily, then create a practical validation plan, prototype, or user-facing positioning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms could make the skill activate for requests unrelated to product validation.

Mitigation: Keep activation focused on product idea, validation, prototype, SaaS, and startup requests; invoke the skill explicitly when the connection is less clear.
