## Description:

Helps founders and small teams turn product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to assess product ideas before committing significant time or money, then create a practical validation workflow and deliverable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation keywords may cause the skill to appear for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Use it for product idea validation and closely related planning requests.

Risk: The demand score and market-evidence claims may be mistaken for independently verified research.

Mitigation: Check source relevance and validate demand with target users before making investment decisions.
