## Description:

Helps founders and small teams assess product demand and turn ideas into validation plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams can use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before committing significant resources. It helps turn the request and available inputs into a practical validation workflow, checklist, analysis, prototype, or positioning artifact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation keywords may trigger the skill outside product-demand planning.

Mitigation: Use it for product idea, demand validation, prototype, or positioning work; narrow custom trigger phrases to those topics.
