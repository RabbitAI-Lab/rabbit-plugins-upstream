## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users such as solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to validate product ideas before investing heavily. It helps convert an early idea into a practical plan, prototype direction, user-facing positioning, checklist, analysis, or implementation-support artifact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation wording and produce off-topic guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user's request is about product validation or planning before applying the workflow.

Risk: Advisory product-validation outputs may be incomplete or misleading if user constraints, audience, or success criteria are underspecified.

Mitigation: State assumptions, ask only for missing information that materially changes the result, and include validation notes and remaining risks.
