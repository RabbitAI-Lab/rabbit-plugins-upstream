## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototype paths, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns rough concepts into validation workflows, checklists, prototype plans, positioning support, and decision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad startup, business, validation, SaaS, or prototype prompts may select the skill even when the user's request needs narrower domain expertise.

Mitigation: Review whether the generated plan fits the specific product context, constraints, and success criteria before relying on it.

Risk: Product validation guidance may be incomplete if the user provides limited market, customer, or operational context.

Mitigation: Surface key assumptions and request only missing information that materially changes the validation plan.
