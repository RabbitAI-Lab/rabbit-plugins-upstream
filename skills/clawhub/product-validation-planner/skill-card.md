## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, analysis, or implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It structures validation work into repeatable plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, and decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit triggers may cause the skill to activate in unrelated startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation discussions.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when product validation planning is needed, or narrow triggers before deployment in environments where accidental activation is a concern.
