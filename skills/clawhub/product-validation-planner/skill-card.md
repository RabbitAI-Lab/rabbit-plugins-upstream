## Description:

Helps solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, and repeatable validation workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It guides them toward practical validation plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, analyses, and implementation support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms and implicit invocation can make the skill appear during general startup or product discussions.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when the product validation workflow is intended, and review outputs against the user's stated outcome, constraints, and success criteria.
