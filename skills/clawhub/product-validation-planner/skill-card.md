## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, and decision-support artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns rough ideas into practical workflows, checklists, analysis, prototypes, positioning, and next-step plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to apply to general business, startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests where a narrower skill would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's desired outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing the planning artifact. <br>
Risk: Product validation plans can overstate confidence when based on assumptions or general demand signals instead of customer-specific evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions visible, include validation steps, and list remaining risks or follow-up work in the final output. <br>
