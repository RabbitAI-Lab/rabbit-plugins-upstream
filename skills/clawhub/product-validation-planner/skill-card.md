## Description:

Helps solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It structures validation work into practical plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, lightweight analysis, and follow-up review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to answer general startup, SaaS, prototype, or business-workflow requests when that is not desired.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation for product-validation planning tasks and narrow activation policy where the host agent supports it.
