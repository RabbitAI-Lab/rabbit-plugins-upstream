## Description:

Helps founders and small product teams plan practical ways to test product ideas and shape validation plans, prototypes, or user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to plan how to test whether a product idea merits further investment, and to create a practical workflow, checklist, or prototype plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation terms can invoke the skill outside explicit product-demand planning requests.

Mitigation: Use it when the user clearly asks for product-demand or idea-validation planning.

Risk: The stated 100/100 demand score is unverified and could overstate market demand.

Mitigation: Treat the score as unverified and assess the resulting business advice independently.
