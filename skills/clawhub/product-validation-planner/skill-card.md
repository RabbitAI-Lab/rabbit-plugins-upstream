## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and practical decision-support artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users such as solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps convert early product questions into workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for general startup or planning requests in workspaces with many business or product skills.

Mitigation: Review invocation settings and trigger wording before installation in multi-skill environments.

Risk: Product-planning outputs can influence roadmap or investment decisions even when the underlying market evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support, validate assumptions with users or customers, and keep remaining risks visible in the final artifact.
