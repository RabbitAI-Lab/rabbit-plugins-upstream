## Description:

Helps founders and small product teams validate product ideas with a repeatable workflow for planning, prototyping, positioning, and review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams can use this skill to decide whether an idea is worth building before making a large investment. It helps clarify goals and constraints, create a practical validation plan or prototype, and review the result against success criteria.
