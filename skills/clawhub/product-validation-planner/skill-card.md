## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototype workflows, positioning, checklists, analysis, or implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to structure product validation before investing heavily. It helps turn rough ideas into practical plans, prototype steps, user-facing positioning, checklists, analysis, or implementation support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to trigger for general startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests where this workflow is not intended.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when product validation planning is desired, and narrow trigger wording in future versions.

Risk: Planning outputs can be mistaken for confirmed market evidence or business advice.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, validate claims with primary customer or market evidence, and treat plans as decision support rather than proof of demand.
