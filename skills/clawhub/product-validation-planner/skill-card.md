## Description:

Helps founders and product teams turn early product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use the skill to assess whether a product idea is worth building. It helps turn the idea and available evidence into a practical validation workflow and a tailored plan or other requested deliverable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation keywords may invoke the skill for requests unrelated to product-demand planning.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for product-demand planning when relevance is uncertain.

Risk: Demand scores and cited source lists could be mistaken for proof that a specific product idea has market demand.

Mitigation: Treat these as publisher claims and independently validate demand for the user's product idea.
