## Description:

Helps founders, builders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, analysis, or implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External founders, builders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before significant investment. It helps convert an early product concept into a repeatable validation workflow, concrete deliverable, checklist, prototype plan, positioning, analysis, code change, or decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause this skill to be invoked for overlapping product, startup, prototype, or validation prompts.

Mitigation: Use precise invocation or narrower activation wording when running multiple business or product-planning skills in the same agent environment.
