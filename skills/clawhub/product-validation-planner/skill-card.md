## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision-support artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It converts product-validation requests into practical workflows, checklists, templates, analysis, code changes, or implementation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad triggers and may activate for general startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests where a narrower product-validation workflow was not intended.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for idea-validation planning, MVP or prototype validation, positioning, checklists, or founder workflow support.

Risk: Product-validation outputs may depend on incomplete user inputs or assumptions rather than confirmed market evidence.

Mitigation: Require visible assumptions, success criteria, and validation notes, then review recommendations against direct customer or market signals before committing resources.
