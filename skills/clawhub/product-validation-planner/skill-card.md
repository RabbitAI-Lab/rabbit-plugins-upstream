## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert uncertain startup or SaaS ideas into practical workflows, validation checklists, prototype plans, user-facing positioning, and next-step analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may select this skill for general business, startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests when a narrower skill would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Narrow invocation rules or require explicit product validation, prototype planning, or startup validation intent in deployments that need precise routing.

Risk: Planning outputs may be less reliable when the user's goals, constraints, inputs, or success criteria are underspecified.

Mitigation: Restate assumptions, ask only for missing information that materially changes the output, and validate the result against the stated success criteria.
