## Description: <br>
Pitch Skill helps advertising and marketing agencies build evidence-based competitive pitch strategies, from brief intake and stakeholder research through strategy selection, win-probability assessment, proposal expression, Q&A practice, and delivery packaging. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agency strategists, pitch teams, and business development leads use this skill to prepare competitive RFP or agency pitch responses where multiple vendors are bidding and client stakeholders will evaluate a formal proposal. It is intended for competitive pitch planning, not general internal marketing plans, financing roadshows, slide polishing, or non-competitive research. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be used with confidential bids, regulated client materials, or personal stakeholder data. <br>
Mitigation: Use only information the team is authorized to share, sanitize client materials before use, and apply extra review for regulated or sensitive accounts. <br>
Risk: The workflow encourages insider-style stakeholder research without complete privacy and confidentiality guardrails. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid insider, improperly obtained, or personal details; rely on authorized brief materials and public, traceable sources. <br>
Risk: External search or image-generation steps could expose sensitive pitch materials. <br>
Mitigation: Remove confidential client details before using external tools and review generated material before including it in a proposal. <br>
