## Description:

Run a local business's weekly SEO in one pass - what moved in its tracked searches (daily positions per city, the Google Maps top 3, AI Overviews), reviews waiting for a reply, this week's Google Business Profile update, and calls from the profile - then stage every fix for approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Local business owners and the agencies that support them use this skill to review weekly search visibility, customer reviews, Google Business Profile activity, and calls, then decide which suggested changes to approve.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested website and Business Profile changes or review replies could be applied before the business owner has checked them.

Mitigation: Present changes as approval cards and wait for the person's explicit approval before applying or posting them.

Risk: Starting paid search tracking or drafting actions can consume account credits.

Mitigation: Show the quoted credit cost and get agreement before confirming a paid action.
