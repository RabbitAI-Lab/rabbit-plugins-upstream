This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and marketing teams use this skill to manage Dub links and connected business records, track conversions, and retrieve campaign attribution analytics through superagnt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting Dub grants superagnt access to the user's connected workspace.

Mitigation: Connect only if you trust superagnt with access to that workspace.

Risk: Changes to links, domains, folders, customers, partners, bounties, or commissions can disrupt business data.

Mitigation: Confirm the scope and consequences with the user before deletions or sensitive changes.
