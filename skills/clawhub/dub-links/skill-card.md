## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional MCP setup may expose broader workspace access than this Dub-focused skill describes, including customer information and controls for partner rewards and commissions.

Mitigation: Before connecting, verify that the MCP server exposes only the Dub tools you intend to use. Keep SUPERAGNT_API_KEY private, and require explicit approval for payment-related changes and deletions.
