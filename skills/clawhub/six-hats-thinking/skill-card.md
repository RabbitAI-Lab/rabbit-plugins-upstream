## Description:

Structures multi-perspective decisions and brainstorming into separate passes for facts, feelings, risks, benefits, options, and synthesis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and teams use this skill to examine a decision or idea through distinct factual, emotional, risk, benefit, and creative perspectives before choosing a next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests for a full perspective check may trigger an unnecessarily lengthy hat-by-hat response.

Mitigation: Use the structured format for multi-perspective decisions and brainstorming; use the light path for smaller requests and skip it for simple factual questions.
