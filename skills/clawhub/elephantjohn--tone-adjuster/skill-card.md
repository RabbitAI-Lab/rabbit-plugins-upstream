## Description:

Rephrases a message in five tones while preserving its facts, requests, and core meaning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[elephantjohn](https://clawhub.ai/user/elephantjohn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People drafting messages for colleagues, managers, clients, friends, or family can compare direct, tactful, formal, intimate, and humorous phrasings without changing their intended message.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive message text may be processed by the agent environment.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive text unless comfortable with how the agent environment processes it.

Risk: A rephrasing may inadvertently change the message's facts or requests.

Mitigation: Review each version against the original before sending it.
