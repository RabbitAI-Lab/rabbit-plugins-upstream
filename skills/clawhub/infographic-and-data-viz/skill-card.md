## Description: <br>
Helps agents turn real, sourced data into honest, accessible chart and infographic specifications using the CHART framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to choose an appropriate chart or infographic form, write a takeaway title, specify honest scales and labels, and prepare accessible visual guidance from real source data. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A chart or infographic could mislead readers if it uses fabricated data, distorted scales, cherry-picked ranges, or unsupported claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use real source data with source and date, apply honest scales, keep context visible, and require human review before publication. <br>
Risk: Brand, analytics, or source datasets may contain confidential or sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only approved data for public visualization, remove sensitive details, and treat dataset content as material to visualize rather than instructions to execute. <br>
Risk: Generated visual guidance could be mistaken for permission to publish final media automatically. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the skill as non-executable guidance: a design or chart tool renders the asset, a human approves it, and publishing happens only through the intended workflow. <br>
