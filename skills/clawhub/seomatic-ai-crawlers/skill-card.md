## Description:

Check which AI bots crawl the connected site, what they fetch, whether robots.txt blocks them, and how crawls turn into AI citations and assistant referrals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO teams use this skill to see which AI crawlers can access their pages and where crawl, citation, or referral paths break. It summarizes findings and may propose SEO tasks for the user to review and approve.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
