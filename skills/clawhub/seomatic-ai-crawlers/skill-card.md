## Description:

Checks which AI crawlers reach a connected site, whether access is blocked, and where crawls fail to become citations or referrals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO teams use this skill to assess AI crawler access, find crawl-to-citation gaps, and prioritize fixes. They may stage proposed SEO tasks for explicit user approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic exposes the site's search, crawl, citation, and referral data to the integration.

Mitigation: Confirm the site's owner accepts that access before connecting SEOmatic.

Risk: Staged fixes could be applied without the site owner's intended approval.

Mitigation: Review each proposed task and require explicit approval before any live change; use the available rollback if needed.
