## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected media files are sent to AI-HIVE for generation.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading sensitive, private, or unlicensed material, and confirm that reference media may be used for the intended output.

Risk: Video generation may consume AI-HIVE credits or incur costs.

Mitigation: Review current model pricing, routing mode, batch size, and budget before submitting large or repeated jobs.

Risk: An exposed AI-HIVE API key could allow unauthorized account use.

Mitigation: Prefer environment-variable keys when possible, keep config files private, and revoke or rotate the key if exposure is suspected.

Risk: Generated advertising, product, or short-drama content can contain inaccurate claims or rights-sensitive material.

Mitigation: Review factual claims, brand use, likenesses, and publishing rights before distribution.
