## Description:

Research public Spotify podcasts and episodes without a Spotify account or API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Podcast researchers, publicists, and other users can find shows and episodes, review show details, and check release dates and episode lengths for guest research or outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using this skill requires installing and pairing the ClawLink plugin.

Mitigation: Install and pair it only with the user's consent.

Risk: Public Spotify data calls spend ClawLink credit, especially when searches are repeated or batched.

Mitigation: Keep searches focused, check current prices, and confirm before large batches or higher-cost calls.
