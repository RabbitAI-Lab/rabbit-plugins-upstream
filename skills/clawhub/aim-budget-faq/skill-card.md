## Description:

Provides clear answers to common questions about the AIM budget target system, including its three layers, weighting rules, conversion flow, and classification criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[longtel-skill](https://clawhub.ai/user/longtel-skill)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees and business teams use this skill to answer operational questions about the AIM budget target system and apply the A1, A2, and A3 classification and weighting rules. It is especially useful for front-line business colleagues who need quick guidance on budget updates, opportunity classification, and conversion rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The FAQ reflects a specific AIM budget policy version and may become stale if company rules change.

Mitigation: Verify important budget classifications, calculations, and decisions against the current official policy or finance department before relying on the answer.
