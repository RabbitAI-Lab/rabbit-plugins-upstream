## Description:

Guides agents through brochure requirements, layout design, user approval, and folded and lifestyle mock-up delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and marketing teams use this skill to plan brochure content and layouts, then create brochure artwork and folded or lifestyle mock-ups after approving the layout.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected reference media are sent to dLazy services for processing.

Mitigation: Upload only material you intend to share with the service.

Risk: The package and its credential-storage protections were outside the artifact security review, and the docs contain a stale version reference.

Mitigation: Verify the CLI package version and review its credential handling before installation.
