## Description:

Guides marketing brochure design from requirements and fold selection through approved layouts to folded and lifestyle mock-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and designers use this skill to plan brochure content and fold layouts, generate artwork, and create mock-ups after approving the layout.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and referenced media may be sent to dLazy's cloud service, and results are hosted there.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive unreleased materials unless third-party processing and hosting are acceptable.

Risk: CLI login may save an API key locally, and global installation persists on the device.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and prefer the on-demand npx option when a global installation is unnecessary.

Risk: Mock-ups generated before layout approval may misrepresent the intended brochure.

Mitigation: Confirm the fold type and obtain explicit approval of the unfolded layout before generating mock-ups.
