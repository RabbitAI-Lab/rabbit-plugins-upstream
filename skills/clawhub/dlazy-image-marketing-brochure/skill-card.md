## Description:

Guides marketing brochure design from requirements and unfolded layouts through approved folded and lifestyle mock-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and designers use this skill to plan brochure content and fold types, create unfolded layouts, and produce mock-ups after layout approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow uses an installable dLazy CLI and requires an API key.

Mitigation: Review and trust the CLI before installation; protect the key and rotate or revoke it if needed.

Risk: Prompts and referenced media may be sent to dLazy's hosted services.

Mitigation: Confirm that the content is appropriate to share with dLazy before generating images.

Risk: Critical execution instructions are Chinese-only, and some workflow wording is inconsistent.

Mitigation: Translate and review the steps before use; confirm the layout and each generation step with the user.
