## Description: <br>
Computes freelance hourly, day, monthly, and fixed-project pricing targets from take-home income, billable-hour, expense, tax, margin, and scope-buffer assumptions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ssidharhubble](https://clawhub.ai/user/ssidharhubble) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External freelancers, consultants, and AI agent operators use this skill to set or re-evaluate target rates, sanity-check offered rates, and convert hourly estimates into fixed-price project quotes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Pricing results can be mistaken for tax, legal, or market-rate advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as estimates and verify tax assumptions, jurisdiction-specific obligations, and market fit before using the rates commercially. <br>
