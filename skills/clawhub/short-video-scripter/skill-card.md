## Description: <br>
Short Video Scripter helps an agent turn a short-video idea, draft, or transcript into timestamped vertical-video beat sheets with hooks, spoken lines, on-screen text, asset keywords, platform format notes, and AI-content disclosure guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and social content teams use this skill to convert an idea or draft into a shootable short-form video script for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Douyin, or WeChat Channels. It is best suited for script specification and handoff, not rendering, text-to-speech, scheduling, or upload automation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use channel or claim memory to tailor generated scripts. <br>
Mitigation: Review retrieved memory context and only approve saving outputs or proposed memory events after confirming they are accurate. <br>
Risk: Short-video scripts can contain product or offer claims that need source support. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unsupported claims marked as needs source and route claim updates through the documented proposal flow before reuse. <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the output for a publishing or automation pipeline. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as a script specification only; rendering, TTS, editing, scheduling, uploading, and deletion stay with separate user-controlled tools. <br>
