## Description: <br>
Draft a defensible construction change order with entitlement basis, scope delta, itemized pricing, schedule impact, and reservation of rights. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External construction project teams, contractors, and contract administrators use this skill to draft complete change order requests or proposed change orders for changed work, pricing, entitlement, notice, and time-impact documentation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Construction contracts, pricing, notices, and schedule details may contain sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Limit input sharing to the minimum needed, handle project records as confidential, and follow the installer's normal document-control process. <br>
Risk: A drafted change order can affect contractual rights, payment recovery, schedule entitlement, or waiver positions. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated request against the governing contract, supporting records, and contracts counsel before sending. <br>
Risk: Thin or estimated inputs can produce unsupported pricing, entitlement, or schedule claims. <br>
Mitigation: Keep confirmation markers for missing facts until resolved, attach supporting quotes or records, and label estimates clearly. <br>
