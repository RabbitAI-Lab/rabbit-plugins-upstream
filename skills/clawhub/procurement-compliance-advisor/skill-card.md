## Description: <br>
A Chinese government procurement compliance advisor that helps with legal Q&A, procurement document review, case lookup, compliance checks, policy implementation review, and protest or complaint preparation under the Government Procurement Law framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Procurement staff, suppliers, and compliance reviewers use this skill to analyze Chinese government procurement questions, review procurement documents, identify policy implementation gaps, and prepare practical risk-oriented checklists or complaint materials. It is scoped to government procurement under Chinese Government Procurement Law and redirects out-of-scope tendering or enterprise purchasing matters. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process procurement documents or case details that users are not authorized to share. <br>
Mitigation: Users should provide only materials they are permitted to share and remove sensitive or confidential details when possible. <br>
Risk: Legal and policy guidance may become outdated or may not fit a specific dispute or procurement context. <br>
Mitigation: Users should verify current official law and policy sources and consult qualified counsel or the relevant finance authority before relying on outputs as formal advice. <br>
