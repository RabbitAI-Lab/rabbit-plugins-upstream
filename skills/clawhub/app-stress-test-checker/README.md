Stress Test Your App Before Real Users Break It, in Plain Words

Upload your app. Answer a few questions. Get 5 stress tests that find what breaks first, before your users find it.

Most apps work fine when one person uses them in a calm way. Then a real user sends strange data, or many users arrive at once, or one small action costs too much, and the app slows down, saves the wrong thing, shows private data, or stops. This skill checks first, then makes a stress test plan for your app only, with draft fixes for owner review, never a live change by itself.

## What you get

- A stress test check on 5 points: schema stress test, security lockdown audit, cost explosion simulation, traffic spike resilience, and breakpoint detection
- A stress test plan, labelled DRAFT, NOT LIVE: schema test case list, lockdown list, cost limit plan, traffic spike test plan, and breakpoint record
- A clear answer on what will break first, in plain words, with evidence
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## Who this is for

Founders with no code, who want to know if their app will hold up when real users use it in real ways, in plain words. Small business owners with an app that is getting more users. Freelancers and builders who need a clear stress test plan to hand to a developer.

## Why this saves money

Finding a break in a test is cheaper than finding it in front of a real customer. A clear plan stops the builder from guessing, rebuilding the app, or paying for a cost spike you did not see coming.

## Technical, for coders

Structured stress test plan with schema stress test cases, security lockdown audit, cost explosion simulation, traffic spike resilience test, and breakpoint detection, ready to brief Claude, Cursor, or any AI builder, or a human developer. No guarantee of a user number, and no claim that a plan alone makes an app safe or cheap. The plan is a draft for owner review, not a live change.

## The 5 tests

1. Schema stress test, hard data, missing fields, long text, wrong types, duplicates
2. Security lockdown audit, who can see what, who can change what, what stays private
3. Cost explosion simulation, what costs more when use grows, on paper only
4. Traffic spike resilience, many fake users at once, 50, 500, and 5,000
5. Breakpoint detection, the exact point where the app breaks, slows down, or stops

It also gives separate notes: slow pages, heavy work that should run in the background, mobile use, and error messages.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent your app idea, your app code, or your app link.
3. Answer, in plain words: what does it do, how many users today, how many users at once do you want, what do users do most, where is your data, where is it hosted, what must stay private?
4. Ask: "Check my app with app-stress-test-checker."
5. Read the plan. Approve the draft fixes first, before any test is run, any schema is changed, any limit is set, or any code is changed.

## What it will not do

- It will not change code, change the database, change permissions, change limits, run a test on live users, attack the app, or deploy anything by itself.
- It will not invent a user number, a speed, a cost, a table, a schema field, a test result, or a breaking point.
- It will not claim your app can handle 5,000 users unless a test proved it.
- A stress test plan is not a promise of safety, growth, users, or money.
