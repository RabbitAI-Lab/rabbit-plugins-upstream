## Description:

Creates static ad creatives, placement size variations, catalog ads, and short animated banners from reusable templates through Orshot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, agencies, and developers use this skill to create on-brand static ads, campaign variations, catalog creatives, and display banners across common placements. It renders supplied copy and brand assets into reusable Orshot templates and returns the resulting creative links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand or product assets may be sent to Orshot for rendering.

Mitigation: Use assets suitable for external processing and publicly accessible image links.

Risk: Rendering campaigns or recurring jobs can incur usage costs.

Mitigation: Confirm rendering costs and explicitly choose any recurring automation.

Risk: Connected workspace integrations may grant access to workspace data or actions.

Mitigation: Review the connected workspace and integration permissions before use.
