## Description:

Seven Habits workplace coaching skill that diagnoses habit gaps, customizes check-in plans, guides mission statement drafting, audits emotional bank accounts, and organizes time matrices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and knowledge workers use this skill to translate the Seven Habits framework into practical workplace coaching, habit diagnosis, check-in plans, relationship audits, and time-matrix planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coaching prompts may include private workplace situations, relationship details, personal values, or other sensitive context.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an approved agent environment, avoid confidential employer, HR, or regulated information, and do not record or store other people’s statements or reactions without consent.

Risk: The skill may be asked to address concerns outside a workplace habit-coaching scope.

Mitigation: Use it for practical habit guidance and redirect serious mental health, legal, HR, or regulated issues to qualified professionals or approved internal processes.
