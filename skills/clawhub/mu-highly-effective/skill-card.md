## Description:

Seven Habits workplace coaching skill. Diagnoses habit gaps, customizes check-in plans, guides mission statement drafting, audits emotional bank accounts, and organizes time matrices. Part of the Mu Book Series.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and individual contributors use this skill to turn Seven Habits concepts into practical workplace coaching. It supports habit diagnosis, scenario-specific advice, check-in plans, personal mission statement guidance, emotional bank account audits, and time matrix planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose sensitive workplace, relationship, habit, values, or mission statement information during coaching.

Mitigation: Encourage users to share only necessary details and avoid entering secrets, credentials, or highly sensitive personal information.

Risk: Users may treat productivity or habit coaching as mental health or crisis support.

Mitigation: Preserve the skill's stated boundary: it should acknowledge serious psychological concerns as outside scope and direct users to professional care.

Risk: Scenario-based coaching can produce advice that is too generic or not suitable for a user's actual workplace constraints.

Mitigation: Ground responses in the user's concrete situation, ask brief clarifying questions when needed, and keep actions small, verifiable, and reversible.
