## Description: <br>
Stand up and run a LinkedIn employee advocacy program for a marketing or sales team, including a 14-day launch playbook, brand-guideline governance, per-post time budget, cadence benchmarks, and team ROI tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing leaders and sales teams use this skill to launch or improve an employee LinkedIn advocacy program. It helps define a 14-day launch plan, operating model, cadence targets, governance rules, and KPI tracking for reach, engagement, and pipeline attribution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Employee advocacy guidance may lead to public posts that include confidential information, regulated claims, or customer and financial details. <br>
Mitigation: Apply consent, brand, legal, and compliance review rules before publication, especially for regulated claims, customer names, internal metrics, roadmap statements, and confidential information. <br>
Risk: General team-posting requests may trigger the skill even when the user has not defined governance expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm team goals, brand constraints, review responsibilities, and compliance needs before using the playbook to operationalize a posting program. <br>
