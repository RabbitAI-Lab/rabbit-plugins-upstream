## Description:

Drafts car-rental agreements and website terms from an operator’s policies, with state-specific points flagged for legal review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Small car-rental operators use the skill to turn their business policies into a readable agreement, website terms summary, fee schedule, and lawyer-review checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Missing supporting documents may leave safeguards or optional market-lookup instructions incomplete.

Mitigation: Have a lawyer and insurer review each draft before use, including state-specific clauses.

Risk: Renter identity and payment records contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Provide business policies rather than renters’ identity or payment records.
