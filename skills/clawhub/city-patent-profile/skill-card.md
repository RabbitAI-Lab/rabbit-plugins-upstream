## Description:

Creates a Markdown patent profile and data table for a city or district, summarizing technology areas, leading organizations, recent growth, and invention share.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Government, industrial-park, and investment-promotion teams use this skill to prepare city or district patent-landscape briefings covering leading technologies and organizations, recent trends, and invention share.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid account-key protection needs review.

Mitigation: Prefer the free trial until credential storage is corrected; never paste paid account keys into chat.

Risk: Search terms are sent to PatentMax and may contain confidential invention details.

Mitigation: Obtain approval before submitting confidential search terms.

Risk: Timeouts and unexpectedly empty statistics can obscure charges or produce misleading results.

Mitigation: Set a spending cap, check charges after timeouts, and verify unexpectedly empty statistics.

Risk: Redirect protection and cache-path handling warrant correction.

Mitigation: Prefer the free trial until redirect protection and cache-path handling are corrected.
