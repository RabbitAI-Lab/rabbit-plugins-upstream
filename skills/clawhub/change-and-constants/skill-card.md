## Description: <br>
Guides agents through strategic decisions about what must adapt and what must remain constant when identity, values, or environmental shifts are in tension. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Teams, leaders, and agents use this skill to facilitate strategic conversations about when to pivot, what to preserve, and how to document the reasoning behind each classification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the Change-Constants Map as a final strategic decision rather than a facilitation artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Review the map with accountable stakeholders before acting on strategic changes or constants. <br>
Risk: The framework labels may be unfamiliar or sensitive in some organizational contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Explain or adapt the labels while preserving the explicit change-versus-constant tests. <br>
