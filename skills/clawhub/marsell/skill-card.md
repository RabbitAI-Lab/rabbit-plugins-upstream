## Description:

Work with Marsell, an AI for marketing, sales and content creation, through its MCP server. Use when someone wants to run their Marsell projects from their own AI (see what Marsell planned, made and found, answer the decisions waiting for them, update their brand, plan work, make pictures and films, or check results), or asks what Marsell does and how to connect it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners and their AI agents use this skill to connect to Marsell, manage marketing projects, review pending decisions, create content, and check results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An agent with Can act access can create or publish content and take other project actions within the granted permissions.

Mitigation: Choose Reads only unless acting permissions are needed, review OAuth permissions before allowing access, and confirm actions that require owner approval.

Risk: The security scan was partial because one local reference could not be matched to a bundled artifact.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and the skill's source materials before deployment.
