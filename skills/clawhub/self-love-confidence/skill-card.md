## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill in an agent to receive confidence-building affirmations, log daily wins, reframe negative self-talk, and review confidence progress over time. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate from broad wellness or self-worth phrases during emotional conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Review trigger behavior before deployment and confirm that users expect a wellness-oriented response in those contexts. <br>
Risk: Confidence-building and self-talk reframing guidance could be mistaken for professional mental health advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as supportive self-reflection guidance and keep appropriate human or professional support paths available for sensitive situations. <br>
