## Description:

Rewrite the closing of a LinkedIn post to maximize the chosen outcome (comments, shares, profile visits, DM, link clicks, follows). Diagnoses why the current CTA is weak, offers 3 to 5 alternatives ranked by likely performance. Use when the user has a finished post but the CTA is generic or absent. Requires the Taplio MCP to ground the rewrite in which of the user's own past CTAs actually drove engagement and apply the new CTA to the draft.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

LinkedIn creators and professionals use this skill when a finished post needs a stronger call to action for one selected goal. It reviews the current closing, offers three to five ranked alternatives grounded in the user's Taplio settings and past post performance, and can apply the selected CTA to a Taplio draft.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The connector may access account settings, posts, and analytics, and send a post topic to Taplio; confidential details could be exposed.

Mitigation: Review connector permissions and use a generalized topic for confidential material.

Risk: Changes to a draft or its schedule could alter planned content or timing.

Mitigation: Review the proposed content and timing before approving scheduling or publication; immediate publication requires explicit confirmation.
