## Description:

Guides users through Kotter's eight-step framework to diagnose and sequence planned organizational change.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Change sponsors, organizational leaders, and managers use this skill to assess resistance and build a sequenced roadmap for a planned transformation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Change coaching may involve confidential organizational details or recommendations that affect personnel and security processes.

Mitigation: Share only necessary organizational details, avoid recording confidential examples, and review proposed personnel or security-process changes before acting.
