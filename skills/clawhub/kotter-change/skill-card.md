## Description: <br>
Guides agents through Kotter's eight-step change model to diagnose stalled organizational change, structure a readiness assessment, and produce a sequenced transformation roadmap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, leaders, and external advisors use this skill to plan or troubleshoot large organizational changes such as digital transformation, merger integration, cultural shifts, and enterprise AI adoption. It helps an agent score eight failure modes, identify skipped steps, and draft a 24-month change roadmap. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include confidential organizational details when asking for change-readiness diagnostics or tailoring the examples. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential company details into prompts or examples unless that storage and disclosure is intentional. <br>
Risk: The skill provides change-management guidance that could be applied without sufficient local context or stakeholder review. <br>
Mitigation: Review the roadmap with accountable business, HR, legal, and security stakeholders before using it to drive organizational decisions. <br>
