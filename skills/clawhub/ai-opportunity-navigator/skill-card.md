## Description:

Screens public coding and AI evaluation opportunities against a user's skills, location, and available time, then offers source-backed application guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yl124915300-dot](https://clawhub.ai/user/yl124915300-dot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People seeking freelance coding or AI evaluation work use this skill to compare public application opportunities with their eligibility, skills, and available time before deciding where to apply.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; the optional paid written review is offered only to US-based individuals.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public opportunity listings may be outdated, unavailable, or inapplicable to the user's location; advertised rates do not guarantee work or earnings.

Mitigation: Recheck original pages and eligibility rules, flag inaccessible sources as unknown, and distinguish application candidates from confirmed assignments and income.

Risk: Live checks contact public opportunity websites.

Mitigation: Use permitted browsing and public pages only; do not transmit the user's profile or submit applications through the skill.

Risk: Optional human review and payment are separate from the free skill experience.

Mitigation: Provide the free shortlist first; seek explicit user choice before sharing operator or payment links, and never request credentials, identity documents, or payment details.
