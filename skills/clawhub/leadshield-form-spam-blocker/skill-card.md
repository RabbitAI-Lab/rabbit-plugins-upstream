## Description:

Block contact form spam, SEO cold pitches, and B2B solicitations across GoHighLevel (GHL), WordPress, HubSpot, and custom websites using LeadShield's 2-stage guard ($0.00 Stage 0 deterministic bot drops + Stage 1 buyer-intent screening).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and site operators use this skill to screen contact form submissions, review blocked spam, tune per-site filtering policies, and generate website or CRM integration instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Filtering policies may quarantine a legitimate inquiry.

Mitigation: Preview policy changes and monitor quarantine reports for false positives.

Risk: Screening may involve provider data retention and per-submission charges.

Mitigation: Review the provider's privacy and retention terms and confirm screening costs before connecting live forms.
