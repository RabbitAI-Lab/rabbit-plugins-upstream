## Description:

Filter contact form spam and unwanted sales pitches on HighLevel, WordPress, HubSpot, and custom websites. Set rules for each site and review blocked submissions. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External site owners and developers use LeadShield to screen form submissions for spam, review retained blocked submissions, manage per-site rules, and generate website setup instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted form content is sent to LeadShield’s external service.

Mitigation: Review the service’s privacy, retention, and pricing terms; use synthetic test messages, exclude unnecessary sensitive information, and grant access only to intended sites.
