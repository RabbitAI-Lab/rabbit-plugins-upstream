## Description:

Filter contact form spam and unwanted sales pitches on HighLevel, WordPress, HubSpot, and custom websites. Set rules for each site and review blocked submissions. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Website owners and CRM administrators use LeadShield to screen contact form submissions, manage site-specific spam rules, review quarantined messages, and add form protection to websites and workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Form submissions are processed by an external screening service and screening charges may apply.

Mitigation: Confirm the service is approved to receive customer form data and review its retention terms and screening charges.

Risk: Legitimate inquiries may be quarantined or labeled as spam.

Mitigation: Test with synthetic submissions, preview rule changes, and review quarantined messages for legitimate inquiries.

Risk: A spam verdict does not verify a submitter's identity or guarantee their intent.

Mitigation: Use independent checks when identity or intent must be established.
