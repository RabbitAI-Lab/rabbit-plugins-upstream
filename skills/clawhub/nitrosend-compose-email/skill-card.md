## Description:

Create or edit email templates with Nitrosend's section-based designer, including layout, content, brand styling, previews, and test sends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External email creators use this skill to design or update Nitrosend email templates, then preview content and optionally send a test message.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A template or campaign could be saved under the wrong Nitrosend account or target.

Mitigation: Confirm the active account and selected template or campaign before saving.

Risk: Uploaded images and descriptions remain in the Brand Library, and generation can incur metered costs.

Mitigation: Upload only intended assets and review generation costs before proceeding.

Risk: A test email could reach unintended recipients or contain unreviewed content.

Mitigation: Review the message and test recipients before agreeing to send.
