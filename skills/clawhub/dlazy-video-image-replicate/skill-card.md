## Description:

Analyzes a user-provided reference image or video and helps recreate its look and structure using the user's own subject, product, or characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and other users can use the skill to study a reference image or video and recreate its visual style or structure with their own subject, product, or characters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using an authenticated account and listing organization projects may expose project context to the chosen service or account.

Mitigation: Confirm the account and project context are appropriate before signing in or listing projects.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Invoke it deliberately for reference image or video recreation, and narrow its trigger terms in integrations where needed.

Risk: The security review had incomplete evidence because some referenced material was unavailable.

Mitigation: Review the skill and relevant CLI details before installing if you need greater assurance.

Risk: Attached local reference files are uploaded to the service for processing.

Mitigation: Only attach files you are permitted to share and that are appropriate for the service.
