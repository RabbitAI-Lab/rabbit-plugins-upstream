## Description:

Helps recreate the look and structure of a reference image or video with the user's own subject, product, or characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to adapt a reference image or video to their own product, subject, or characters through dLazy's hosted agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached reference files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Attach only files you are comfortable sharing with dLazy and review sensitive content before submission.

Risk: An API key grants access to the user's dLazy organization.

Mitigation: Keep the key scoped to your organization and rotate or revoke it if exposed.

Risk: Continuing the wrong project can expose previous conversation context.

Mitigation: Continue only projects you recognize.
