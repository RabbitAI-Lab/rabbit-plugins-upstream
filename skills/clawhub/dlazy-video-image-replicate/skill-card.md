## Description:

Helps recreate the look and structure of a reference image or video using the user's own subject, product, or characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users can analyze a reference image or video and recreate its visual style and structure with their own subject, product, or characters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scanner flags broad activation triggers and actions that may not be clearly related to recreating media.

Mitigation: Review when the skill activates and inspect any project-listing or authentication action before use.

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to dLazy, and the scanner's coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Review external-service handling and avoid sending prompts or media that you do not want shared with the service.
