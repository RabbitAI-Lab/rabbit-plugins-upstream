## Description:

Analyzes a user-provided reference image or video and recreates its look and structure with the user's own subject, product, or characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and content teams use the skill to study a reference image or video and produce a similar result featuring their own subject, product, or characters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference files attached to a request are uploaded to dLazy media storage, and prompts are sent to its hosted service.

Mitigation: Only upload material you are comfortable sharing with dLazy.

Risk: The skill relies on a CLI package and hosted agent that were not fully verified, and its service retention practices were not fully verified.

Mitigation: Review the CLI package and service terms before use, and prefer device-code login over pasting an API key into chat.
