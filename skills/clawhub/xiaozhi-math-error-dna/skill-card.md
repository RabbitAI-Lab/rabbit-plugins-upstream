## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school math error patterns by assigning detailed subtypes to the correction notebook's four general error dimensions and identifying links between them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in middle and high school use this skill to understand recurring math errors, review patterns in their correction records, and identify focused next steps. It provides subtype analysis and requested weakness reports while leaving error collection and occurrence counts to the correction notebook.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports a scope mismatch around progressive math practice and partial scanner coverage because two local references could not be resolved; confidence is medium.

Mitigation: Review whether the reported scope mismatch fits the intended workflow and check the unresolved references before installation.

Risk: The skill handles student learning profiles and may produce student or parent-facing summaries.

Mitigation: Create or share long-term records only with the consent required by the skill, check sharing preferences before parent summaries, and keep crisis referral ahead of normal analysis.
