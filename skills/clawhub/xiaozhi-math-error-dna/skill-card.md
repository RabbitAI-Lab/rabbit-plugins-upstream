## Description:

Analyzes recurring math mistakes for Chinese middle- and high-school students, identifying underlying error patterns and suggesting targeted practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to examine recurring math errors, identify specific weak areas, and request progress reports or focused practice. It relies on a separate correction notebook for recorded mistakes and historical counts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); use elsewhere requires localized curriculum, consent practices, and crisis resources.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term learning profiles and parent or cross-skill summaries can expose a minor's sensitive learning information.

Mitigation: Check consent before retaining profiles, sharing information, or queuing reminders; honor pause, correction, export, and deletion requests.

Risk: Curriculum, consent expectations, and crisis referrals may be inappropriate outside the intended Chinese K12 setting.

Mitigation: Localize those elements before using the skill in another region, and route serious distress to appropriate local human support.
