## Description:

Find and compare relevant historical cases such as contracts, proposals, projects, incidents, decisions, designs, or exceptions to inform a current problem without treating similarity as authority.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and teams with access to historical knowledge sources use this skill to find comparable prior cases, compare decision-relevant similarities and differences, and extract reusable lessons for a current decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical contracts, proposals, prices, dates, staffing, or decisions may be stale or non-authoritative.

Mitigation: Use precedent as analogy only, and verify current legal, commercial, policy, and technical requirements before reuse.

Risk: The skill needs access to historical organizational material that may be sensitive or out of scope for a given user.

Mitigation: Scope repository, RAG, search, and document access to sources the user is authorized to use.

Risk: Semantic similarity can surface cases that differ materially from the current decision.

Mitigation: Compare decision-relevant dimensions explicitly and make material differences visible in the output.
