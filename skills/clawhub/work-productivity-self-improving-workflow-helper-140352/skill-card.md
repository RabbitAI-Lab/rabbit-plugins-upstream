## Description: <br>
Helps agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn demand for self-improving and proactive workflows into practical plans, checklists, analyses, code changes, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External agent users, developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify work-productivity goals, create practical self-improvement or proactive-agent workflows, and produce locally feasible artifacts such as checklists, templates, analyses, code changes, or decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to appear during ordinary productivity, bug-fix, or improvement requests. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow or disable implicit invocation when installing if broad activation is not desired. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow, checklist, analysis, or code-change guidance could be incorrect or incomplete for the user's environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs before execution, validate them against the stated success criteria, and keep assumptions, limits, and remaining risks visible. <br>
