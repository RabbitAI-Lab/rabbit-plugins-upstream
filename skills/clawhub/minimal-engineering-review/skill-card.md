## Description: <br>
Helps an agent produce concise engineering reviews focused on bugs, behavioral regressions, risk, and missing tests. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[juanbastias](https://clawhub.ai/user/juanbastias) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering teams use this skill to get focused code-review feedback before changing or shipping software. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Review findings may be incomplete or may flag issues that are not actual defects. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as review input and verify them against the code, tests, and product requirements before merging changes. <br>
