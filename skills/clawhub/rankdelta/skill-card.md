## Description:

SEO and AI visibility through the Rankdelta MCP server. Check how a brand shows up in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, see which competitors the AI recommends instead, track Google rankings, research keywords, audit pages and pull backlink and domain overviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can connect an agent to Rankdelta to monitor AI visibility, Google rankings, and competitors, research keywords, audit pages, review backlink and domain data, and draft GEO-oriented articles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The API key provides access to the account connected to the skill.

Mitigation: Store RANKDELTA_API_KEY securely, never paste it into chat, and review account permissions before connecting.

Risk: Some analyses and research lookups consume account allowance or credits.

Mitigation: Confirm before running credit-consuming tools and review the account spend limit.

Risk: The hosted MCP server may change independently of this skill, and its implementation is not verified by the skill evidence.

Mitigation: Review provider changes and independently verify important results before relying on them.
