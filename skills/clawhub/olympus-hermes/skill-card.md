## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An anonymous query is sent to an external provider, uses the user's zkAPI balance, and may save their privacy-mode choice.

Mitigation: Use the anonymous query only when requested, explain the provider transfer and cost, obtain the user's privacy-mode choice, and check the connected service's privacy guarantees before sending sensitive information.
