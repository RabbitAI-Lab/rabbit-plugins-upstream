## Description: <br>
Use when crafting still-image prompts for generative models, including composition, identity sheets, edits, try-on, and photoreal personas. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creative operators, and external users use this skill to draft and review still-image generation prompts for photos, edits, character consistency, virtual try-on, upscaling, and persona plates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Shown API examples could send prompts, images, voice scripts, or garment/person references to Pruna or related services and may incur provider costs. <br>
Mitigation: Before running API examples, confirm the provider, expected cost, credential scope, and whether the referenced content is acceptable to transmit. <br>
