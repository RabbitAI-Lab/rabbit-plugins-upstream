# deadlines.md (Middle East VAT)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; actual periods are assigned by FTA / ZATCA — verify in the respective portal.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- UAE: quarterly (standard; some large filers monthly), return and payment due the 28th after the tax period end.
- Saudi Arabia: quarterly if annual turnover ≤ SAR 40,000,000; monthly above. Return and payment due by the end of the month following the period.

## Usage Notes
- Payment and return share the same deadline in both countries — late payment accrues penalties immediately.
- Nil returns are required once registered.
- If the user's assigned frequency is unknown, mark "cannot determine."
