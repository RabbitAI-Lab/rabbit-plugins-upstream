## Description:

Reviews requirement documents across completeness, clarity, consistency, testability, and feasibility, and produces a structured Chinese-language QA assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, testers, and product teams use this skill to review PRDs and other requirements before test design, identify gaps and contradictions, and prioritize improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked broadly before requirements-related test design, even when a full review is not needed.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested review scope before producing an extensive report.

Risk: Chinese-language headings and templates may not suit every audience.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended report language and adapt or translate the final report as needed.
