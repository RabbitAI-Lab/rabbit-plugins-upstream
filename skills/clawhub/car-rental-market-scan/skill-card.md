## Description:

This skill analyzes publicly listed exotic and luxury car-rental competitors, fleet mix, prices, rental terms, market gaps, and recent price changes in a U.S. city.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Car-rental business owners and prospective operators use this skill to compare publicly listed competitors, prices, fleet offerings, and rental terms in a U.S. market. They can use the dated findings to identify opportunities to investigate before making fleet or pricing decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Two referenced support documents are missing, so catalog connection details are unconfirmed.

Mitigation: Obtain the catalog reference documents before the first call and confirm the connection details.

Risk: Published catalog data may be incomplete or reflect claims made by rental operators themselves.

Mitigation: Report only published information, identify sample sizes and data dates, and attribute operator claims rather than presenting them as independently verified.

Risk: Identified market gaps could be mistaken for proven customer demand.

Mitigation: Present gaps as hypotheses for the owner to test, not as guarantees of demand.
