## Description: <br>
Shopping Memory helps agents maintain shopping lists, purchase history, prices, stores, and restock timing for errands and budget planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use this skill to capture shopping needs, recall current lists and past purchases, and record prices or restock timing for household or business purchasing workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Shopping lists, purchase history, stores, prices, and restock timing may be sent to and retained by the BlueColumn/Supabase-backed service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid recording sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential business purchasing details unless authorized and comfortable with remote storage. <br>
Risk: The skill requires a BlueColumn API key for API calls. <br>
Mitigation: Store the API key securely and avoid exposing it in shared prompts, logs, repositories, or screenshots. <br>
