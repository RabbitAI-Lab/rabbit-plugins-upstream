## Description:

Supports evidence-grounded outlining, drafting, revision, and review of Chinese academic papers, research proposals, and literature reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and academic authors use this skill to organize and revise Chinese academic writing against their supplied materials, check citations and evidence, and obtain focused review feedback. It does not replace the author's original research or institutional academic-integrity requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain sensitive unpublished manuscript material.

Mitigation: Share only material authorized for processing and review drafts before distribution.

Risk: Unsupported claims or citations may enter academic work.

Mitigation: Check claims against supplied sources and verify citations before submission.

Risk: Optional public-source searches or persistent project notes may exceed the intended task scope.

Mitigation: Authorize searches and persistent .academic-writing/ state only when needed; review the resulting files.
