## Description:

协助依据作者材料或明确授权来源，起草、改写和审阅中文论文、开题报告与文献综述，并核对论证、证据和引文。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use the skill to outline, draft from supplied or explicitly authorized materials, revise, and review Chinese academic papers, proposals, and literature reviews, with evidence and citation checks where needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has file-reading capability, and its tool scope is undeclared; manuscript or source files may contain sensitive material.

Mitigation: Provide only the files needed for the task and avoid including confidential material unless you are comfortable with the skill accessing it.
