## Description:

Assists with Chinese academic outlines, drafting from provided or explicitly authorized sources, revision, review, evidence checks, citation checks, and long-manuscript consistency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to structure, draft from author-provided materials, revise, and review Chinese academic papers, proposals, and literature reviews while keeping claims tied to supplied or explicitly verified sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic-integrity misuse, including ghostwriting or presenting unsupported contributions as the author's own work.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with author-provided materials or explicitly authorized sources, and keep final work under institutional, instructor, or advisor review.

Risk: Unverified or unsourced claims may enter drafts when source checking was not explicitly performed.

Mitigation: Require explicit authorization for source research, retain evidence-to-claim mappings, and review citations and claims before use.

Risk: Project-state files may persist research context when the user authorizes long-form continuity.

Mitigation: Review and approve any .academic-writing project-state files before use, and avoid storing sensitive or unauthorized material.
