## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated academic text could be submitted as undisclosed original work or mistaken for verified research.

Mitigation: Keep the author responsible for original contributions and institutional disclosure rules; review all claims and do not fabricate methods, data, results, or references.

Risk: Unverified sources or limited source coverage can lead to incorrect citations and overstated conclusions.

Mitigation: Check claims against provided or authorized sources, retain attribution, and label evidence gaps instead of asserting unsupported findings.

Risk: Drafts and research materials may contain sensitive information, and external lookups may disclose task context.

Mitigation: Share only materials appropriate for processing and authorize the scope of any web or source lookup before it occurs.

Risk: Mechanical audit findings may be mistaken for a complete quality or institutional-compliance assessment.

Mitigation: Treat optional read-only audits as review aids and have the author or institution verify substantive and policy requirements.
