## Description:

将本地视频或抖音、小红书等平台视频转写为文字，并按自定义提示生成摘要、改写、金句、分镜或翻译等文案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

内容创作者、运营人员及需要整理视频资料的用户，可用此技能转写视频并按提示生成摘要、会议纪要、改写文案、金句、分镜或翻译。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video-upload destinations do not match the single-domain privacy assurance, leaving transfer and retention details unclear.

Mitigation: Do not send confidential recordings until the publisher identifies and restricts upload destinations and confirms retention guarantees.

Risk: Downloaded copies may remain locally until a later cleanup run.

Mitigation: Prefer non-sensitive files and check or remove temporary downloads after processing.
