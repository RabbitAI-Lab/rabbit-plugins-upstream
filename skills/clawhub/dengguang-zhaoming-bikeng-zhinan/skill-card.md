## Use Case:

External users and home-improvement assistants use this skill to answer Chinese-language lighting and renovation questions with practical, pitfall-focused guidance. It helps map a user's room, budget, and project stage to advice on main lights, no-main-light layouts, light strips, color temperature, fixture choice, and safety checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is named as a lighting guide but includes broader renovation, shopping, real-estate, and contractor advice.

Mitigation: Use it with a clear lighting or renovation scope and review answers for topic drift before relying on recommendations.

Risk: Answers may touch electrical, fire, waterproofing, structural, or building-rule decisions.

Mitigation: Treat those answers as informational and verify them with qualified professionals and local requirements before acting.
