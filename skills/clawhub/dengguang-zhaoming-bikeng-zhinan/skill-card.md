## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Opinionated lighting advice may not suit every home, budget or accessibility need.

Mitigation: Check recommendations against the room's layout, occupants' needs and budget before purchasing or installing fixtures.

Mitigation: Have a qualified electrician assess wiring and verify local requirements before electrical work.
