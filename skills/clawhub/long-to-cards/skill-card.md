## Description: <br>
Transforms long-form content, outlines, transcripts, research notes, or article drafts into source-grounded card packages, platform publishing copy, and image-production handoffs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to convert supplied articles, transcripts, notes, or drafts into skimmable card packages for Xiaohongshu, WeChat, Zhihu Idea, or generic carousel workflows. It preserves source claims, prepares publishing assets, and by default hands complete packages to image production. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated cards can carry unverified source claims, trend tags, captions, or platform framing into published material. <br>
Mitigation: Review claim verification, hot tags, captions, source caveats, and final publishing assets before posting. <br>
Risk: The default publish_ready flow continues into image generation, which may be unnecessary when only copy or prompts are needed. <br>
Mitigation: Set delivery_mode=copy_only when image production is not desired. <br>
