## Description: <br>
Decode an employment benefits package into what it is actually worth, where the fine print bites, and what questions to ask HR before signing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and job candidates use this skill to review offer letters, benefits summaries, equity terms, and plan excerpts. It produces a plain-language benefits decode with dollar-value estimates, ranked red flags, and HR questions to resolve before signing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive offer letters, salary, equity, and personal benefits context with their agent. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide documents and personal context when comfortable with the agent environment, and minimize unnecessary sensitive details. <br>
Risk: The skill may produce plain-language benefits guidance that users could mistake for legal or financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important employment, tax, legal, and financial terms with HR or a qualified professional before relying on the output. <br>
