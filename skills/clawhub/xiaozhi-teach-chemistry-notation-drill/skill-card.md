## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers design a year-long, level-based class system for mastering chemical notation, with spaced review and differentiated practice from middle school through high school elective content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External chemistry teachers use this skill to plan progressive class-wide mastery checks, differentiated notation drills, spaced review, and follow-up for students who have not yet passed. It produces teacher-reviewable drafts rather than teaching or evaluating students autonomously.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry questions, formulas, or balanced equations may need correction before classroom use.

Mitigation: Run the prescribed self-check and have the teacher verify generated items before using them in class or assignments.

Risk: Class records or teacher-provided lists may include identifying student information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases or seat numbers; omit real names, contact details, and family information, and disable memory or sharing when unnecessary after confirming platform controls.
