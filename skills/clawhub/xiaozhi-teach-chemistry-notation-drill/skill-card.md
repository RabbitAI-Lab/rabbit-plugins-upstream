## Description:

帮助初高中化学老师设计分级的化学用语过关训练、间隔复习和分层补救任务。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆中文课堂的初高中化学老师，用于按教学进度设计元素符号、化合价、化学式和方程式过关卡，并安排分层训练与未过关学生的回看。生成的题目和教学建议须经老师审核后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, crisis resources, and privacy requirements before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated chemistry questions or answers may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Check chemical formulas and equation balancing, and have the teacher approve every card before use.

Risk: Classroom records may contain identifiable information about minors.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers, keep records under platform consent and deletion controls, and require teacher confirmation before storing them.

Risk: Curriculum, crisis contacts, and privacy assumptions may not fit classrooms outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize those resources and requirements before using the skill with students elsewhere.
