## Description:

帮助初高中化学老师设计分级、分段的化学用语过关训练，涵盖元素符号、化合价、化学式与方程式，并安排分层练习和间隔回看。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中化学老师，设计五级化学用语过关计划、分层过关卡和未过关学生的针对性回看；生成内容须经老师核对后用于教学。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize language, curriculum, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry questions may contain incorrect formulas or unbalanced equations.

Mitigation: Check each generated item and have a teacher verify formulas and balancing before classroom use.

Risk: Class records could expose students' personal information or unreleased exam materials.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers; do not enter real names, contact or family details, or unreleased exam materials.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize the language, curriculum, and crisis-referral channels before deployment outside Mainland China.
