## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan a year-long, leveled mastery program for chemical symbols, valence, formulas, and equations, with spaced review and targeted follow-up practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school chemistry teachers use the skill to organize staged practice, design short mastery checks, and plan spaced review for chemical notation. It provides teacher-facing drafts and follow-up ideas for classroom review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (middle and high school chemistry curricula)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information could be included in class records.

Mitigation: Use student aliases or seat numbers, avoid personal and family details, and confirm how the teaching platform stores and deletes records.

Risk: Generated chemistry questions or answers may contain errors.

Mitigation: Check each generated item and its answer; have a teacher verify it before classroom or homework use.

Risk: An assignment draft could be saved before it has been reviewed.

Mitigation: Require teacher approval before saving each assignment.
