## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers build staged notation drills, differentiated practice cards, and spaced follow-up for middle and high school classes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External chemistry teachers use this skill to plan grade-appropriate notation checkpoints, generate short practice cards, and follow up on class-level errors. Teachers review generated questions and approve assignments before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; curricula, student-data practices, and crisis-help channels require localization before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated chemistry questions may contain incorrect formulas or balancing.

Mitigation: Check each generated question and have a teacher verify it before classroom use.

Risk: Classroom records may expose students' personal information.

Mitigation: Use aliases or seat numbers instead of student names or contact details.

Risk: Mainland China crisis-help contacts may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis-help channels before using the skill outside Mainland China.
