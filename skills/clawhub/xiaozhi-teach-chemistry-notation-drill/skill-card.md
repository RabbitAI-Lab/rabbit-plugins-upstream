## Description:

帮助初高中化学教师分级设计元素符号、化合价、化学式和方程式过关训练，并安排分层练习与间隔回看。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft progressive notation drills, short mastery cards, and follow-up practice for middle and high school classes. Teachers check generated questions and approve materials before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum and crisis-referral guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry questions or answers may contain errors.

Mitigation: Check chemical formulas and equation balancing, and have a teacher approve every generated question before use.

Risk: Student identifiers or unreleased exam materials could be disclosed through prompts or class records.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam materials or real student identifiers; use pseudonyms and the available pause, export, and delete controls for records.

Risk: Crisis-referral defaults may not fit every location.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's location and use locally appropriate emergency and support contacts when crisis signals arise.
