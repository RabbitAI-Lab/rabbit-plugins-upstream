## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers design staged notation drills covering element symbols, valence, formulas, and equations, with differentiated practice and spaced review through upper-secondary chemistry.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft level-based practice cards, follow-up exercises, and spaced review plans for classes studying Chinese junior- and senior-secondary chemistry. Teachers check generated questions and approve any classroom use or records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry questions may contain incorrect formulas or unbalanced equations.

Mitigation: Teachers manually check each question and answer before assigning or saving it.

Risk: Class records could expose students' identities or be retained or shared unnecessarily.

Mitigation: Use aliases or seat numbers instead of names, and use pause, delete, export, and sharing controls as appropriate.

Risk: Curriculum and support contacts may not fit classrooms outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use this for Chinese-language chemistry teaching and localize curriculum and support contacts before use elsewhere.
