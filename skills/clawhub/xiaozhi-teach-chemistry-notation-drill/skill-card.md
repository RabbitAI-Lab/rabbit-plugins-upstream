## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school chemistry teachers use this skill to draft tiered notation drills, review schedules, and follow-up exercises. Teachers verify questions and approve any classroom use or record changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize crisis contacts, curriculum, and privacy expectations before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated chemistry formulas, equations, or answers may be incorrect.

Mitigation: Check each generated item and balance equations; teachers verify drafts before classroom use.

Risk: Classroom records could expose student identities or sensitive exam materials.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers, avoid personal details, and do not input unreleased standardized exam questions.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and curriculum assumptions may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, curriculum alignment, legal defaults, and language before deployment elsewhere.
