## Description:

帮助初高中化学教师设计分级、分段的化学用语过关训练和间隔回看任务。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中化学教师使用该技能规划元素符号、化合价、化学式和化学方程式的分级过关卡、分层补练与间隔回看；所有题目与作业条目由教师核验并确认后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; deployment elsewhere requires localization of curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis referral channels.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated chemistry questions may contain incorrect formulas or unbalanced equations.

Mitigation: Check formulas, balancing, and answers and obtain teacher approval before classroom use.

Risk: Class workspace data or student identifiers could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers, avoid names and contact details, and confirm any homework entry before saving.

Risk: Curriculum and crisis referral assumptions are specific to mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy expectations, and referral channels before deployment elsewhere.
