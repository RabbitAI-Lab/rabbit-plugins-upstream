## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers plan staged notation drills for element symbols, valence, formulas, and equations, with spaced review and differentiated follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chemistry teachers use this skill to draft five-level classroom practice cards, schedule spaced review, and tailor follow-up exercises for students who have not passed a level. Teachers check generated questions before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry questions may contain incorrect formulas or unbalanced equations.

Mitigation: Check each generated question and answer; require teacher confirmation before classroom use.

Risk: Student records could disclose personal information.

Mitigation: Use aliases or seat numbers, keep records at class level, and avoid entering student names or contact details.

Risk: China-specific curriculum and crisis contacts may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, privacy rules, and emergency guidance before use outside mainland China.
