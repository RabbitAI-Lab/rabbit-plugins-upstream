## Description:

Helps chemistry teachers design staged, trackable notation drills for element symbols, valence, chemical formulas, equation balancing, spaced review, and differentiated follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External Chinese-language K12 chemistry teachers use this skill to plan a yearlong, leveled practice system for chemical notation. It produces drill structures, short pass-card drafts, follow-up plans, and teacher-confirmed class-workspace entries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; localize language, curriculum assumptions, crisis-help channels, and data-consent defaults before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry drill items may contain incorrect formulas, valences, balancing, or reaction conditions.

Mitigation: Require the built-in item self-check and teacher verification before using generated questions in class, homework, or resource banks.

Risk: Classroom records for minors may include sensitive personal data if teachers paste real names or family details.

Mitigation: Use aliases or seat numbers, avoid real names, contact details, and family information, and keep writes as teacher-confirmed entries.

Risk: The skill is designed around Chinese-language K12 chemistry, mainland China crisis-help channels, and mainland China legal and data-consent assumptions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum scope, language, crisis-help channels, and data-consent defaults before deploying outside mainland China or for non-Chinese-speaking users.

Risk: Pre-release unified exam questions, answer keys, or scoring standards may be confidential.

Mitigation: Do not input confidential exam materials; use the skill only for methods, blueprint structure, and self-authored practice until exams are complete.
