## Description:

Helps users compare technology routes and plan patent portfolios, including design-around, international filing, and filing-budget decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

R&D leaders, patent professionals, and business teams use the skill to compare technology routes and plan patent filings, timing, and budgets using patent-search evidence. Its recommendations support technical and business decisions and are not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can transmit potentially confidential invention materials for external processing, and confidentiality limits do not prevent transmission.

Mitigation: Use public or redacted materials unless external processing is approved; review PatentMax's privacy policy before sharing sensitive information.

Risk: Reusable credentials and sensitive inputs may be exposed if credentials are pasted into chat or endpoint and redirect handling is unsafe.

Mitigation: Configure credentials privately rather than pasting them into chat, and require HTTPS destination checks and safe redirect handling before sensitive use.
