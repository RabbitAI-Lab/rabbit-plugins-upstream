## Description:

Find link prospects from SERPs and competitor backlinks, discover public contact paths, and draft personalized outreach. Use when promoting a page, study, or tool. Requires a connected Conqueror MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samuelnoah45](https://clawhub.ai/user/samuelnoah45)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External SEO practitioners, marketers, and growth teams use this skill to identify realistic link prospects, verify public contact paths, and draft personalized outreach for a page, study, product, guide, or tool.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach drafts may contain incorrect or unverified contact details or claims.

Mitigation: Record only contact details actually found with source URLs, and review outreach drafts before sending.

Risk: Prospecting may share project, domain, competitor, and contact-discovery data with Conqueror and web search tools.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only for intended SEO prospecting work, and review the data provided before tool calls.

Risk: Link outreach can become spammy or target direct competitors or likely paid placements.

Mitigation: Personalize outreach by page and reason, avoid mass messaging, and flag direct competitors or likely paid-placement prospects.
