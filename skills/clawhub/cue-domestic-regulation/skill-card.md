## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions may contain personal identifiers or confidential business information.

Mitigation: Submit anonymized questions only; remove names, identifiers, and confidential details before sending them.

Risk: Execution may not provide clear cancellation controls.

Mitigation: Review and pin the runner before execution, and ensure the agent honors stop requests.

Risk: Legal research results may be incomplete or mistaken.

Mitigation: Check conclusions against the cited official sources and seek qualified legal advice when needed.

Risk: API keys could be exposed in chat.

Mitigation: Keep API keys out of chat and use the service's designated key-management flow.
