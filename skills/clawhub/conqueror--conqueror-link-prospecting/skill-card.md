## Description:

Find link prospects from SERPs and competitor backlinks, discover public contact paths, and draft personalized outreach for a page, study, product, guide, tool, or topic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[conqueror](https://clawhub.ai/user/conqueror)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, SEO specialists, and growth teams use this skill to identify link prospects, qualify outreach angles, discover public contact paths, and draft personalized outreach for a page, product, study, guide, or tool. It requires a connected Conqueror MCP server plus available browsing or search tools for contact discovery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach research could lead to spammy or poorly sourced contact attempts.

Mitigation: Avoid mass outreach, personalize by page and reason, and verify sources before contacting prospects.

Risk: Contact names, emails, social handles, or forms may be fabricated or misattributed if not source-checked.

Mitigation: Record only public contact details that were actually found and include the source URL for each email, profile, or contact form.

Risk: Prospect lists may include direct competitors, likely paid placements, or low-quality pages.

Mitigation: Filter for topical relevance and editorial value, deprioritize spam or thin affiliate pages, and flag direct competitors or likely paid placements.
