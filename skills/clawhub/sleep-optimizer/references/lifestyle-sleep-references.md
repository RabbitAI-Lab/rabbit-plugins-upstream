# 生活阶段与睡眠需求 - 科学研究参考文献

## 大学生群体睡眠特征

### 1. 大学生睡眠状况流行病学
**Lund, H.G., et al. (2010).** Sleep patterns and predictors of disturbed sleep in a large population of college students. *Journal of Adolescent Health*, 46(2), 124-132.
- **期刊**: Journal of Adolescent Health (IF: 5.2)
- **发现**: 大学生平均睡眠6.5-7小时；25%报告失眠症状；GPA与睡眠质量正相关

### 2. 睡眠与学业表现
**Gilbert, S.P., & Weaver, C.C. (2010).** Sleep quality and academic performance in university students: A wake-up call for college psychologists. *Journal of College Student Psychotherapy*, 24(4), 295-306.
- **发现**: 睡眠不足6小时的学生GPA显著低于睡眠7-8小时者

### 3. 昼夜节律延迟与大学生
**Crowley, S.J., et al. (2018).** A longitudinal assessment of sleep timing, circadian phase, and phase angle of entrainment across human adolescence. *PLOS ONE*, 13(4), e0195204.
- **期刊**: PLOS ONE (IF: 3.7)
- **发现**: 青春期昼夜节律相位延迟，大学生自然睡眠窗口后移（约00:30-09:00）

## 考研/高压备考阶段

### 4. 考试压力与睡眠
**Akerstedt, T., et al. (2006).** The effects of a self-reported sleep duration and sleep quality on cognitive performance in a sample of college students. *Sleep Medicine*, 7(8), 589-595.
- **发现**: 考试周睡眠效率下降15-20%；记忆巩固受损

### 5. 慢性压力与睡眠结构
**Kim, E.J., & Dimsdale, J.E. (2007).** The effect of psychosocial stress on sleep: A review of polysomnographic evidence. *Behavioral Sleep Medicine*, 5(4), 256-278.
- **发现**: 慢性压力增加REM睡眠潜伏期，减少慢波睡眠（深度睡眠）

### 6. 睡眠与记忆巩固
**Rasch, B., & Born, J. (2013).** About sleep's role in memory. *Physiological Reviews*, 93(2), 681-766.
- **期刊**: Physiological Reviews (IF: 37.3)
- **核心发现**: 睡眠对陈述性记忆和程序性记忆的巩固至关重要
- **应用**: 备考期间保证睡眠对记忆提取至关重要

## 体力活动与睡眠

### 7. 运动与睡眠质量
**Kredlow, M.A., et al. (2015).** The effects of physical activity on sleep: A meta-analytic review. *Journal of Behavioral Medicine*, 38(3), 427-449.
- **期刊**: Journal of Behavioral Medicine (IF: 3.4)
- **发现**: 规律运动提升睡眠质量（效应量d=0.47）；但睡前3小时内剧烈运动可能延迟入睡

### 8. 运动时机与睡眠
**Stutz, J., et al. (2019).** Effects of evening exercise on sleep in healthy participants: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Sports Medicine*, 49(2), 269-287.
- **期刊**: Sports Medicine (IF: 9.8)
- **发现**: 适度晚间运动（结束于睡前1-2小时）不损害睡眠；高强度运动需提前3小时

### 9. 运动员睡眠需求
**Lastella, M., et al. (2018).** Sleep/wake behaviours of elite athletes from individual and team sports. *European Journal of Sport Science*, 18(2), 228-235.
- **发现**: 精英运动员需要8-10小时睡眠；训练后小睡（20-30分钟）促进恢复

## 假期与社交时差

### 10. 社交时差与健康
**Wittmann, M., et al. (2006).** Social jetlag: misalignment of biological and social time. *Chronobiology International*, 23(1-2), 497-509.
- **期刊**: Chronobiology International (IF: 3.2)
- **发现**: 周末与工作日作息差异>2小时与肥胖、吸烟、饮酒相关

### 11. 假期睡眠模式
**Lehnkering, H., & Siegmund, R. (2007).** Influence of chronotype, season, and sex of subject on sleep behavior of young adults. *Chronobiology International*, 24(5), 875-888.
- **发现**: 假期睡眠时长增加但节律混乱；返回工作/学习后需要3-5天重新适应

## 不同年龄阶段睡眠需求

### 12. 青少年睡眠需求
**Paruthi, S., et al. (2016).** Recommended amount of sleep for pediatric populations: A consensus statement of the American Academy of Sleep Medicine. *Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine*, 12(6), 785-786.
- **推荐**: 青少年(13-18岁) 8-10小时；年轻成人(18-25岁) 7-9小时

### 13. 睡眠与认知发育
**Short, M.A., et al. (2013).** Time for bed: parent-set bedtimes associated with improved sleep and daytime functioning in adolescents. *Sleep*, 36(5), 717-724.
- **发现**: 父母设定的就寝时间与更好的睡眠质量和日间功能相关

## 轮班工作与特殊作息

### 14. 轮班工作睡眠障碍
**Wright, K.P., et al. (2013).** Entrainment of the human circadian clock to the natural light-dark cycle. *Current Biology*, 23(16), 1554-1558.
- **期刊**: Current Biology (IF: 9.6)
- **发现**: 现代生活方式使多数人处于"社交性时差"状态

### 15. 补觉与睡眠银行
**Rupp, T.L., et al. (2009).** Banking sleep: realization of benefits during subsequent sleep restriction and recovery. *Sleep*, 32(3), 311-321.
- **发现**: 预先储存睡眠（sleep banking）可部分缓解后续睡眠剥夺的影响

## 睡眠与情绪调节

### 16. 睡眠与情绪加工
**Walker, M.P., & van der Helm, E. (2009).** Overnight therapy? The role of sleep in emotional brain processing. *Psychological Bulletin*, 135(5), 731-748.
- **期刊**: Psychological Bulletin (IF: 22.4)
- **发现**: REM睡眠对情绪记忆的处理和调节至关重要

### 17. 睡眠剥夺与情绪
**Yoo, S.S., et al. (2007).** The human emotional brain without sleep — a prefrontal amygdala disconnect. *Current Biology*, 17(20), R877-R878.
- **发现**: 睡眠剥夺导致前额叶-杏仁核连接减弱，情绪调节能力下降

## 午睡与认知恢复

### 18. 午睡与学习能力
**Mednick, S., et al. (2008).** Comparing the benefits of caffeine, naps and placebo on verbal, motor and perceptual memory. *Nature Neuroscience*, 11(1), 120-126.
- **期刊**: Nature Neuroscience (IF: 21.2)
- **发现**: 60-90分钟午睡包含REM，促进创造性问题解决

### 19. 小睡与警觉性
**Lovato, N., & Lack, L. (2010).** The effects of napping on cognitive functioning. *Progress in Brain Research*, 185, 155-166.
- **发现**: 10-20分钟小睡快速提升警觉性和工作记忆

## 光照与昼夜节律

### 20. 晨间光照与大学生
**Figueiro, M.G., et al. (2014).** The impact of daytime light exposures on sleep and mood in office workers. *Sleep Health*, 1(3), 204-215.
- **发现**: 白天光照暴露与夜间睡眠质量正相关；建议白天至少30分钟自然光

## 生活阶段睡眠需求总结

| 生活阶段 | 推荐睡眠时长 | 特殊考虑 | 科学依据 |
|----------|-------------|----------|----------|
| 大一/大二 | 7-9小时 | 适应新环境，建立节律 | Paruthi et al., 2016 |
| 考研备考 | 7-8小时 | 记忆巩固优先，避免<6小时 | Rasch & Born, 2013 |
| 体力活动期 | 8-10小时 | 训练后恢复，考虑午睡 | Lastella et al., 2018 |
| 假期 | 7-9小时 | 保持节律，避免社交时差>2h | Wittmann et al., 2006 |
| 高压期 | 7-8小时 | 压力管理，睡前放松 | Kim & Dimsdale, 2007 |
