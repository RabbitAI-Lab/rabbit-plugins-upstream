# 睡眠科学研究参考文献

## 核心睡眠模型与机制

### 1. 双过程模型 (Two-Process Model)
**Borbély, A.A. (1982).** A two process model of sleep regulation. *Human Neurobiology*, 1(3), 195-204.
- **影响因子**: 经典理论，被引用超过5000次
- **核心内容**: 提出Process S（睡眠稳态/腺苷累积）和Process C（昼夜节律）共同调节睡眠-觉醒周期
- **应用**: 解释为何固定起床时间比固定上床时间更重要

### 2. 睡眠稳态与腺苷
**Porkka-Heiskanen, T., et al. (1997).** Adenosine: a mediator of the sleep-inducing effects of prolonged wakefulness. *Science*, 276(5316), 1265-1268.
- **期刊**: Science (IF: 41.8)
- **核心发现**: 清醒时腺苷累积，睡眠时清除；咖啡因通过阻断腺苷受体起效

## 失眠治疗

### 3. 失眠认知行为疗法 (CBT-I) 荟萃分析
**Irwin, M.R., et al. (2006).** Comparative meta-analysis of behavioral interventions for insomnia and their efficacy in middle-aged adults and in older adults 55+ years of age. *Health Psychology*, 25(1), 3-14.
- **补充**: Morin, C.M., et al. (2006). Psychological and behavioral treatment of insomnia: update of the recent evidence (1998-2004). *Sleep*, 29(11), 1398-1414.
- **期刊**: Sleep (IF: 5.3)
- **结论**: CBT-I为慢性失眠的一线治疗，效果持久优于药物

### 4. 睡眠限制疗法
**Spielman, A.J., et al. (1987).** A behavioral perspective on insomnia treatment. *Psychiatric Clinics of North America*, 10(4), 541-553.
- **核心内容**: 限制在床时间以提高睡眠效率，逐步延长
- **有效性**: 被后续研究反复验证，CBT-I核心组件

### 5. 刺激控制疗法
**Bootzin, R.R. (1972).** Stimulus control treatment for insomnia. *Proceedings of the American Psychological Association*, 7, 395-396.
- **核心原则**: 床只用于睡眠；20分钟无法入睡即离开床
- **应用**: 打破"床=清醒"的条件反射

## 睡眠时长与质量

### 6. 美国国家睡眠基金会推荐
**Hirshkowitz, M., et al. (2015).** National Sleep Foundation's sleep time duration recommendations: methodology and results summary. *Sleep Health*, 1(1), 40-43.
- **期刊**: Sleep Health (IF: 4.5)
- **推荐**: 成人(18-64岁) 7-9小时；老年人(65+) 7-8小时

### 7. 睡眠效率与质量指标
**Buysse, D.J., et al. (1989).** The Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index: a new instrument for psychiatric practice and research. *Psychiatry Research*, 28(2), 193-213.
- **被引次数**: 超过15000次
- **内容**: 睡眠效率 = 实际睡眠时间/在床时间 × 100%，≥85%为正常

## 午睡科学

### 8. 午睡与认知功能
**Mednick, S.C., et al. (2008).** Comparing the benefits of caffeine, naps and placebo on verbal, motor and perceptual memory. *Nature Neuroscience*, 11(1), 120-126.
- **期刊**: Nature Neuroscience (IF: 21.2)
- **发现**: 60-90分钟午睡包含REM睡眠，促进创造性问题解决；10-20分钟午睡快速提升警觉性

### 9. NASA午睡研究
**Rosekind, M.R., et al. (1994).** Alertness management: strategic naps in operational settings. *Journal of Sleep Research*, 3(S1), 62-66.
- **应用**: 宇航员26分钟午睡提升34%表现和54%警觉性

### 10. 午睡时机与时长
**Dutheil, F., et al. (2021).** Benefits of nap or rest on physiological and psychological outcomes during and after laboratory and real-life stressors: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*, 18(16), 8124.
- **结论**: 10-20分钟午睡最佳；13:00-15:00窗口；避免>30分钟以防睡眠惯性

## 昼夜节律与光照

### 11. 光照对昼夜节律的影响
**Terman, J.S., et al. (2001).** Circadian time of morning light administration and therapeutic response in winter depression. *Archives of General Psychiatry*, 58(1), 69-75.
- **期刊**: JAMA Psychiatry (IF: 17.5)
- **发现**: 晨间2500-10000 lux光照30分钟可有效重置生物钟

### 12. 蓝光与褪黑素
**Cajochen, C., et al. (2011).** Evening exposure to a light-emitting diodes (LED)-backlit computer screen affects circadian physiology and cognitive performance. *Journal of Applied Physiology*, 110(5), 1432-1438.
- **期刊**: Journal of Applied Physiology (IF: 3.9)
- **发现**: 夜间蓝光暴露抑制褪黑素分泌，延迟入睡

### 13. 昼夜节律相位标记
**Benloucif, S., et al. (2005).** Measuring melatonin in humans. *Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine*, 1(1), 66-69.
- **内容**: 褪黑素分泌 onset（DLMO）是昼夜节律相位的金标准

## 睡眠环境

### 14. 温度与睡眠
**Harding, E.C., et al. (2019).** The temperature dependence of sleep. *Frontiers in Neuroscience*, 13, 336.
- **发现**: 核心体温下降促进睡眠；最佳室温18-22°C

### 15. 噪音与睡眠
**Basner, M., et al. (2014).** ICBEN review of research on the biological effects of noise 2011-2014. *Noise & Health*, 16(72), 71-74.
- **内容**: 噪音导致微觉醒，影响睡眠结构和恢复功能

## 睡眠与心理健康

### 16. 失眠与抑郁共病
**Manber, R., et al. (2008).** Cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia enhances depression outcome in patients with comorbid major depressive disorder and insomnia. *Sleep*, 31(4), 489-495.
- **期刊**: Sleep (IF: 5.3)
- **发现**: 治疗失眠可显著改善抑郁症状

### 17. 睡眠与焦虑
**Alvaro, P.K., et al. (2013).** A systematic review assessing bidirectionality between sleep disturbances, anxiety, and depression. *Sleep Medicine Reviews*, 17(6), 453-462.
- **期刊**: Sleep Medicine Reviews (IF: 10.5)
- **结论**: 睡眠障碍与焦虑、抑郁存在双向因果关系

## 睡眠监测与评估

### 18. 睡眠日记金标准
**Carney, C.E., et al. (2012).** The consensus sleep diary: standardizing prospective sleep self-monitoring. *Sleep*, 35(2), 287-302.
- **内容**: 标准化睡眠日记格式，用于CBT-I和睡眠研究

### 19. 体动记录仪
**Ancoli-Israel, S., et al. (2003).** The role of actigraphy in the study of sleep and circadian rhythms. *Sleep*, 26(3), 342-392.
- **应用**: 客观评估睡眠-觉醒模式，验证主观报告

## 特殊人群与情况

### 20. 社交时差 (Social Jetlag)
**Wittmann, M., et al. (2006).** Social jetlag: misalignment of biological and social time. *Chronobiology International*, 23(1-2), 497-509.
- **发现**: 周末与工作日作息差异>2小时与肥胖、吸烟、饮酒相关

### 21. 轮班工作睡眠障碍
**Wright, K.P., et al. (2013).** Entrainment of the human circadian clock to the natural light-dark cycle. *Current Biology*, 23(16), 1554-1558.
- **期刊**: Current Biology (IF: 9.6)
- **内容**: 现代生活方式导致多数人处于"社会性时差"状态

## 中文研究

### 22. 中国成人睡眠状况
**Liu, X., et al. (2017).** Sleep duration and chronic diseases among Chinese adults: a cross-sectional study. *Scientific Reports*, 7, 10356.
- **发现**: 中国成人平均睡眠6.5-7小时；睡眠不足与慢性病风险增加相关

### 23. 中国失眠患病率
**Cao, X.L., et al. (2017).** The prevalence of insomnia in the general population in China: A meta-analysis. *PLoS ONE*, 12(2), e0170772.
- **发现**: 中国成人失眠患病率15.0%，女性高于男性
