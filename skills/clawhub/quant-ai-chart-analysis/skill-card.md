## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to analyze stock, crypto, or forex chart screenshots and explain trends, key levels, patterns, indicators, risk zones, and trade setup context in plain language. The output is educational information, not financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chart screenshots may include account names, balances, order IDs, watermarks, or proprietary trading information when uploaded to Quant AI.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive account and trading details before using the skill, and confirm the user is comfortable sending the screenshot to Quant AI.

Risk: Detected trade setups or chart patterns may be mistaken for guaranteed outcomes or financial advice.

Mitigation: Present results as educational analysis only, do not guarantee outcomes, and do not place trades or manage brokerage accounts.
