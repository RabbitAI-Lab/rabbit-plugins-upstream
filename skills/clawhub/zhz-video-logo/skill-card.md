## Description:

Applies image logos or text watermarks to individual videos or video folders, with controls for placement, opacity, and size.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bjbook](https://clawhub.ai/user/bjbook)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and media teams use the skill to brand individual videos or batches with image or text watermarks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Replacement mode may overwrite or lose videos, and interrupted outputs may be skipped on a later run.

Mitigation: Keep backups, preview the selected files, use a separate output folder, avoid --replace until its safety checks are fixed, and inspect partial outputs before rerunning after an interruption.
