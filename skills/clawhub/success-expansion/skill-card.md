## Description:

Guides sales and customer-success teams through identifying, scoring, discussing, and handing off expansion opportunities for live China-market B2B accounts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales representatives and customer-success teams use this skill after a customer is live and producing value to find upsell, cross-sell, seat expansion, module expansion, and departmental expansion opportunities. It produces an expansion opportunity list, scoring table, account-specific conversation plan, and renewal or downstream deal handoff notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer communications, screenshots, tendering signals, or customer statements may contain sensitive commercial or personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm account region, communication preferences, procurement rules, and privacy or compliance limits before using WeChat, screenshots, customer statements, or tendering signals as evidence.

Risk: Expansion recommendations can be misleading if the account is not healthy, has not reached stable adoption, or lacks validated evidence.

Mitigation: Require observable triggers, a corresponding sellable product, and account health before treating an account as expansion-ready.

Risk: China-market procurement requirements can change the timing and viability of an expansion motion.

Mitigation: Check customer-specific tender thresholds, budget cycle, and procurement path before advancing pricing, proposal, or close timing.
