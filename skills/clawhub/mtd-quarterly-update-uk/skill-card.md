## Description:

Work out a UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax quarterly update from the user's own figures — cumulative year-to-date totals per HMRC category, the deadline (7 August, 7 November, 7 February, 7 May), whether one total-expenses line is allowed (under £90,000 turnover), and whether MTD applies to them yet (£50k → April 2026, £30k → April 2027, £20k → April 2028). For sole traders and landlords.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

UK sole traders and landlords use the skill to calculate cumulative year-to-date totals for an Income Tax quarterly update, identify the relevant deadline, and understand whether the stated turnover rules apply to them. It is a calculation aid, not tax advice, and does not submit anything to HMRC.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax thresholds, reporting dates, or rules may change after the guidance in the skill was checked.

Mitigation: Check current HMRC guidance before relying on the figures or deadlines.

Risk: Financial records shared with the linked external tool may be subject to privacy terms that were not inspected.

Mitigation: Share only the category totals needed and review the tool's privacy terms before uploading financial records.

Risk: Results depend on the accuracy and categorization of the figures supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Check the totals against dated source records and use the result as a calculation aid, not tax advice.
