## Description:

Guides analysis of publicly disclosed Chinese real-estate listings and filing prices, including standardized unit data, four absorption-rate measures, realizable-value estimates, and Excel reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chriskinhaha](https://clawhub.ai/user/chriskinhaha)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Real-estate analysts and lending teams use this skill to assess publicly disclosed property inventory, compare four absorption-rate measures, estimate realizable value, and prepare sourced Excel reports. It checks existing project records before seeking new public data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad searches of prior workspace records and memory may expose unrelated sensitive project information.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated project workspace and restrict access to records needed for the current analysis.

Risk: Stored raw public-source materials may retain personal data, cookies, tokens, or excess metadata.

Mitigation: Review and remove sensitive content from archived responses, and apply an appropriate retention policy.
