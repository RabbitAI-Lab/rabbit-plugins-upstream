## Description:

bazi-engine guides users through birth-profile collection, calculates Four Pillars Bazi charts deterministically, and produces cited cultural fortune-telling analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to generate Bazi chart readings, yearly or daily fortune views, marriage compatibility checks, and five-element suggestions for cultural exploration. Developers can also use the bundled offline UI and JavaScript engine to test deterministic chart calculations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill collects birth and profile details that can be sensitive in combination.

Mitigation: Use only the minimum birth/profile data needed for the requested chart, avoid real names or third-party data when possible, and keep the data within a trusted assistant environment.

Risk: Health, marriage, financial, and fortune outputs may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as cultural entertainment and route medical, financial, legal, and relationship decisions to qualified professionals.

Risk: Optional photo or external-site workflows may create privacy expectations beyond the core skill.

Mitigation: Do not use optional photo or external-site workflows unless users receive a clear privacy notice first.

Risk: The security verdict is suspicious and calls for review before deployment.

Mitigation: Perform human review and policy checks before installation in shared, enterprise, or customer-facing environments.
