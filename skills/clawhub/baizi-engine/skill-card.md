## Description:

Generates Four Pillars (Bazi) birth charts and traditional interpretations with citations to classical sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ruanxiaoer888](https://clawhub.ai/user/ruanxiaoer888)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (code); CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (data)

## Use Case:

Individuals seeking a traditional Chinese astrology reading use the skill to generate a birth chart and explore interpretations of relationships, career, and life cycles. Developers can use its bundled offline charting interface, subject to the separate data license.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Birth details are personal information.

Mitigation: Request only the details needed for charting and avoid sharing or retaining them unnecessarily.

Risk: Fortune interpretations may influence health, financial, marriage, or other important decisions.

Mitigation: Present readings as cultural reference, not medical, financial, or other professional advice; consult qualified professionals for consequential decisions.

Risk: Bundled interpretive data has non-commercial share-alike terms, including when embedded in the offline interface.

Mitigation: Review the separate data license and obtain permission before commercial reuse of the data.
