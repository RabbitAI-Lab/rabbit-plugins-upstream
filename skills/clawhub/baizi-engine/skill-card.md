## Description:

Guides users through birth-detail collection, calculates a BaZi chart and timing cycles, and produces traditional interpretations with cited classical sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ruanxiaoer888](https://clawhub.ai/user/ruanxiaoer888)

## Use Case:

People interested in traditional Chinese astrology use the skill to generate four-pillar birth charts, examine timing cycles or compatibility, and read interpretations linked to classical sources. Developers can use its local chart-calculation tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Birth dates, times, locations, and names are sensitive personal details shared with the assistant.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when comfortable sharing those details with the assistant; avoid supplying unnecessary personal information.

Risk: Astrological interpretations may be mistaken for medical, financial, legal, or relationship advice.

Mitigation: Treat results as traditional-culture or entertainment guidance, not professional advice or a decision-making basis.

Risk: Developer test scripts execute bundled local UI JavaScript.

Mitigation: Run developer tests only in a normal, trusted development context.
