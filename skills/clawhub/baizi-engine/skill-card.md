## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for personal birth details for fortune analysis.

Mitigation: Install and use it only when users are comfortable sharing those details, and avoid collecting more personal information than needed for the requested analysis.

Risk: Users could treat cultural fortune analysis as medical, financial, relationship, or other professional advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as cultural or entertainment guidance and direct users to qualified professionals for consequential decisions.

Risk: Optional monitoring scripts may send local fingerprints or search queries to external search services.

Mitigation: Do not run optional monitoring scripts unless the operator understands what data would be sent and has configured any required tokens deliberately.

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for casual mentions of fortune telling or Bazi.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent before requesting birth details or producing an analysis.
