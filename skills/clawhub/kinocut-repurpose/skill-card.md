## Description:

Use when turning one local video into a platform-ready short. Kinocut builds clip packages with manifests, review artifacts, and human approval gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and video editors use this skill to turn a local video into short, platform-ready clips. It guides planning, rendering, and human review of the clip package before public use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing Kinocut introduces ordinary third-party dependency risk.

Mitigation: Install from the verified project source, use a reviewed version, and prefer an isolated environment.

Risk: Rendered clips may need correction before they are shared publicly.

Mitigation: Review the manifest, thumbnails, storyboards, and quality-check results, and require human approval before public use.
