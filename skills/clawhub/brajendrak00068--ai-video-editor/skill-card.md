## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video URLs and editing prompts are shared with the editing backend.

Mitigation: Provide only footage URLs and prompts you are comfortable sharing.

Risk: Complex edits may change the timeline in unexpected ways.

Mitigation: Use requirePlanApproval to review the proposed edit before changes are applied.
