## Description:

将视频转为文字与结构化文案，并可通过自定义提示词生成总结、改写、金句、分镜或翻译内容。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

外部用户和内容团队可用它转写已完成的视频或音频，并将内容整理为摘要、会议纪要、社交媒体文案、金句、分镜或翻译。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video URLs may redirect to private-network resources that are then uploaded automatically. Submitted media goes to provider-selected storage, may incur fees, and has retention terms the client cannot verify.

Mitigation: Prefer explicitly selected local files, or use an isolated environment that blocks private-network access. Verify upload destinations, costs, and retention terms before submitting confidential recordings.
