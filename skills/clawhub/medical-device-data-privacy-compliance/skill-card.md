This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External developers, regulatory teams, and medical-device compliance reviewers use this skill to draft cross-border privacy and data-transfer checklists, data-flow maps, DPIA/PIA points, and privacy-policy considerations for connected medical-device releases.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory thresholds and legal interpretations may change or vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official regulators or qualified counsel before using outputs for market access, data-transfer, or contractual decisions.

Risk: Prompts could include real patient identifiers, customer names, internal paths, or confidential commercial details.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive personal, customer, system, and commercial information before using the skill.

Risk: Generated compliance text could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting support and require human legal or compliance review before publication or operational use.
