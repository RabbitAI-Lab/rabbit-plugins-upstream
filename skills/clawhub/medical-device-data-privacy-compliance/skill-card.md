## Description:

医疗器械出海数据合规：GDPR / HIPAA / 中国个保法（PIPL）、数据出境安全评估、隐私影响评估（PIA/DPIA）。输出数据流映射清单与合规要点，降低联网器械出海的数据合规风险。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External medical device product, regulatory, privacy, and compliance teams use this skill to structure cross-border data privacy work for connected medical devices. It helps produce data-flow maps, GDPR/HIPAA/PIPL checkpoints, DPIA/PIA prompts, and privacy-policy drafting points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce incomplete or outdated privacy compliance guidance for GDPR, HIPAA, PIPL, or data export assessments.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official regulatory sources or qualified legal and compliance counsel before using outputs for market access decisions.

Risk: Users may include real patient data, customer identities, or confidential business details while requesting data-flow maps or privacy assessments.

Mitigation: Use de-identified examples and avoid entering patient names, customer names, internal system paths, pricing, or other confidential details.
