## Description: <br>
Builds a reusable narrative enablement kit from an existing narrative canon, message house, brand voice, and claims ledger, including elevator pitches, spokesperson Q&A, boilerplate, bios, and do/don't language guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, sales, support, founder, and partner teams use this skill to turn an approved narrative canon into consistent spokesperson and enablement materials. It is intended for brands that already maintain canon, message-house, brand-voice, and claims-ledger files and need repeatable human-facing messaging without introducing new claims. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read internal narrative canon, message-house, brand-voice, and claims-ledger files. <br>
Mitigation: Run it only in workspaces where those marketing-memory files are intended inputs for messaging work. <br>
Risk: Generated enablement materials or proposal events may be saved into memory after confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed saves and claim or narrative events before approving persistence. <br>
Risk: Unapproved claims can appear as candidates when needed for a hard question or enablement asset. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unverified claims marked as needing a source and route them through the claims workflow before using the kit externally. <br>
