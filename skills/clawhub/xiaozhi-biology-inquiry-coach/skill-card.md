## Description:

A biology tutoring skill that helps students explain inquiry steps, design controls and variables, and draw conclusions supported by evidence across middle- and high-school experiments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External: Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to understand biology inquiry methods, experimental controls and variables, lab procedures, safety, and evidence-based conclusions. It supports learning and exam preparation through guided questions and student-designed solutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China; deployments in other regions or languages require localization of crisis-response resources and relevant curriculum guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records may be stored or shared with companion skills.

Mitigation: Keep memory disabled unless records are wanted; review the platform's storage, deletion, and consent controls before enabling it.

Risk: Laboratory topics may involve hazards if treated as instructions for unsupervised activity.

Mitigation: Use the material as supervised school instruction and follow the skill's lab-safety boundaries; it withholds at-home procedures for activities such as microbial culture and blood collection.
