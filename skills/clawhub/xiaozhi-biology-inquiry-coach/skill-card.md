## Description:

为初高中学生讲解生物实验探究、对照设计、操作理由和证据支持的结论，并辅助实验操作考试复习。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

面向使用中文的初高中学生及指导他们的教师，帮助学生理解生物实验中的变量、对照、步骤目的、安全要求和结论表达；不代替现场实验教学。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize crisis contacts and consent rules outside mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profile memory, cross-skill sharing, reminders, or parent sharing may expose sensitive learning data.

Mitigation: Leave these features disabled unless the student or guardian intentionally enables them; confirm individual profile updates.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and consent practices may not fit other locations.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts and consent rules before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Unsafe at-home biology procedures or reliance on written guidance instead of supervision.

Mitigation: Do not provide at-home microbial culture or blood collection steps; defer practical work to supervised school settings.
