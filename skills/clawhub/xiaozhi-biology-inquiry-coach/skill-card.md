## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through biology experiment design, controls, procedural reasoning, and evidence-based conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators in mainland China's K–12 system use this skill to reason through controlled biology experiments, interpret procedures, and prepare for school laboratory assessments without replacing supervised practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, minor-consent rules, and emergency contacts before student use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may attempt unsafe biology experiments without supervision.

Mitigation: Do not provide at-home microbial culture or blood-collection steps; defer hazardous work to supervised school laboratories.

Risk: Learning profiles may expose minors' educational data.

Mitigation: Keep profile use consent-gated, confirm each update, and honor requests to pause, delete, or restrict sharing.

Risk: Curriculum, consent, and crisis guidance may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, minor-consent requirements, and emergency contacts before use in other regions.
