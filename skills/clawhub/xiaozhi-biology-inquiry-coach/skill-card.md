## Description:

面向初高中学生讲解生物实验探究、对照设计、操作步骤与结论表达，并辅导实验操作考试备考。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生使用本技能理解生物实验的变量控制、对照设计、操作目的和结论写法，并复习实验操作考试；教师或家长可辅助监督实际操作。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere only after localizing curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis-support contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students could attempt unsafe biology experiments outside supervised lessons.

Mitigation: Keep hazardous procedures in teacher-supervised school settings; do not provide at-home microbial culture or blood-collection instructions.

Risk: A minor's learning profile, reminders, or progress could be shared without their agreement.

Mitigation: Use the consent controls for memory, sharing, reminders, export, and deletion; confirm individual profile changes with the student.

Risk: Curriculum, consent rules, or crisis contacts may not fit use outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these requirements before deployment elsewhere, including local emergency and support contacts.
