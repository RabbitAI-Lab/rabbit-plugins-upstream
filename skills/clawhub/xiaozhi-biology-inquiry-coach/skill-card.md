## Description:

This skill helps middle and high school students reason through biology investigations, including experimental controls, variables, evidence-based conclusions, and lab safety.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school students use this skill to understand biology experiment design, explain the purpose of each step, prepare for practical assessments, and interpret what conclusions the evidence supports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China; localize emergency contacts and exam guidance for the student's region.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may try to carry out biology activities without appropriate supervision.

Mitigation: Follow the lab-safety guidance; the skill withholds at-home procedures for microbial culture and blood collection and directs students to teacher-supervised settings.

Risk: Learner profiles and disclosures about personal safety may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Profile access and sharing require explicit consent, writes require confirmation, and crisis disclosures follow the documented referral protocol.
