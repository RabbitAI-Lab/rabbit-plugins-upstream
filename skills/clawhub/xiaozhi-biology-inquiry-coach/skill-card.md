## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through biology inquiry, controlled experiments, lab safety, and evidence-based conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External middle- and high-school students use this skill to reason through biology experiments, identify controls and variables, explain procedures, and practice writing conclusions. Teachers may use its guidance for supervised classroom preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional student learning records, wrong-answer handoffs, and reminders could expose minors' information without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep records off by default; obtain the required consent before saving, sharing, or scheduling, and honor students' privacy controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent assumptions may not suit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, help channels, and minor-consent requirements before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Unsafe at-home biology experiments or laboratory accidents could harm students.

Mitigation: Do not provide steps for at-home microbial culture or blood collection; refer practical work and active accidents to an in-person teacher.
