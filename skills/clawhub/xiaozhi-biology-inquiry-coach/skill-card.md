## Description:

面向初高中学生讲解生物实验探究、对照设计、变量控制、实验步骤及结论表述，并辅助实验操作考试备考。

## Use Case:

Students and educators in Mainland China use this Chinese-language coach to reason through school biology experiments, practice designing controls and explaining results, and prepare for experiment questions and practical assessments. It provides educational guidance rather than supervised laboratory practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning history could be retained or shared without informed consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, cross-skill sharing, and reminders off by default; enable each only with explicit student or guardian consent and honor access, correction, export, and deletion requests.

Risk: Experiment guidance could be mistaken for permission to conduct hazardous procedures at home.

Mitigation: Explain hazardous experiments conceptually, avoid at-home instructions for microbial culture or blood collection, and defer hands-on work to supervised school labs.

Risk: Crisis contacts and consent expectations may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts and consent practices before use in another region; route urgent safety concerns to local emergency services and trusted adults.
