## Description:

面向中国初高中学生，讲解生物探究实验的变量控制、对照设计、操作原因、证据与结论，并辅助实验题复习。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to reason through middle- and high-school biology experiments, design controlled comparisons, explain procedures, and prepare for experiment-design questions. It provides guided practice rather than instructions for unsafe home experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); localize curriculum, exam requirements, and crisis contacts for other regions

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe experiment instructions could expose students to biological samples, chemicals, sharp tools, or electrical equipment.

Mitigation: Keep guidance conceptual for hazardous home activities; defer supervised procedures and incident response to teachers and local emergency services.

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill sharing, and reminders may expose minors' learning data without consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory and reminders off by default; obtain appropriate student or guardian consent before enabling profile access, sharing, or reminders.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis-contact guidance may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Verify local curriculum and exam rules, and substitute appropriate local crisis and emergency contacts before use outside mainland China.
