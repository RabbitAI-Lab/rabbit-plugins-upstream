## Use Case:

中国初高中生和辅导者用它练习生物实验设计、分析对照与变量、解释操作原因，并复盘实验设计题和操作考试要点。实验操作仍需遵守学校和老师的安全要求。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); adapt curriculum, consent requirements and emergency contacts before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students might treat lab explanations as instructions for unsafe experiments at home.

Mitigation: Keep hazardous experiments in supervised school settings; provide concepts rather than at-home steps for microbial culture, blood collection or other dangerous procedures.

Risk: Minors' learning records, wrong-answer handoffs or reminders could be retained or shared without consent.

Mitigation: Keep interactions session-only by default; enable memory, cross-skill sharing and reminders only with the required consent and confirm each write.

Risk: Emergency advice, consent rules or exam guidance may not fit a different locale.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, consent rules and curriculum assumptions before use outside the Chinese K12 context.
