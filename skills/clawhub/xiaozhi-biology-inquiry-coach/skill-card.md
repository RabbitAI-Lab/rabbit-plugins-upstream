面向中国初高中生，辅导生物实验探究、对照设计、变量控制、实验安全和依据证据撰写结论。

## Use Case:

Students and educators use the skill to reason through school biology experiments, design controlled comparisons, explain each step and prepare for practical assessments without replacing supervised hands-on practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; tailored to the Chinese mainland curriculum. Localize emergency contacts, school standards and minor-data safeguards before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional memory, sharing and reminders may retain or pass along a student's learning progress and wrong-answer details.

Mitigation: Keep the default session-only mode unless the student opts in; use the pause, sharing, export and deletion controls when appropriate.

Risk: Students may mistake discussion of school experiments for permission to perform unsafe procedures at home.

Mitigation: Explain principles rather than providing at-home instructions for microbial culture, blood collection or other hazardous procedures; direct practical work to supervised school settings.
