## Description:

面向中国大陆初高中学生，指导生物实验探究、对照设计、变量控制和实验结论表述，并讲解实验操作安全。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-language middle and high school students use this skill to reason through biology experiments, prepare for practical assessments, and explain why each experimental step matters. It offers graded hints rather than completing the student's original experiment design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Laboratory guidance could be mistaken for permission to perform unsafe experiments at home.

Mitigation: Keep hands-on work under school supervision; do not provide at-home culturing or blood-collection instructions.

Risk: Learning records and reminders may expose student information if sharing controls are misunderstood.

Mitigation: Default to session-only guidance; require consent and confirmation before saving, sharing, or scheduling items, and review privacy controls.

Risk: Curriculum, guardian-consent rules, and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize educational, privacy, and emergency guidance before use elsewhere.
