帮助初高中学生理解生物实验探究步骤、对照与变量设计、操作理由及结论表达，并为实验操作考试备考提供指导。

## Use Case:

面向初中和高中学生，帮助他们设计生物探究实验、辨析对照组和变量、理解各步骤的目的，并练习依据证据表达结论及准备实验操作考试。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may treat classroom experiment guidance as instructions for unsupervised practical work.

Mitigation: Follow the supplied lab-safety guidance; do not provide at-home procedures for microbial culture or blood collection, and require teacher supervision where specified.

Risk: Optional student learning records, sharing, or reminders involve personal data.

Mitigation: Keep conversation-only mode by default; before enabling memory, sharing, or reminders, review the student-data handling and obtain the required consent.

Risk: A learner may ask about a personal health concern or disclose a crisis beyond tutoring.

Mitigation: Do not diagnose or advise treatment; follow the skill's crisis referral guidance and direct the learner to a trusted adult or locally appropriate help.
