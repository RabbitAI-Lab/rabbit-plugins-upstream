## Description:

帮助中学生理解生物实验的探究步骤、对照组与变量设计、操作目的和证据支持的结论，并练习实验设计题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this skill to reason through biology experiment design, safe classroom procedures, and exam-style questions with guided prompts rather than immediate answers. Teachers and guardians can use it to support supervised learning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China as configured; localize curriculum, language, consent rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe at-home procedures or unsupervised school laboratory activity.

Mitigation: Keep guidance at the conceptual level for hazardous experiments and defer hands-on work to supervised school settings.

Risk: Student profiles, wrong-answer handoffs, or reminders could expose learning data without consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, sharing, handoffs, and reminders opt-in and under the student's control.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize teaching scope, language, consent rules, and emergency referrals before use in other regions.
