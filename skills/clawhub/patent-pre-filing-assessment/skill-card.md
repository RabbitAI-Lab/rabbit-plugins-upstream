## Description:

Evaluates a technology disclosure, patent number, or technical description before filing by assessing patentability, technical quality, market value, and strategic fit, then producing a visual HTML assessment report with recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

R&D teams, university or enterprise IP departments, and patent practitioners use this skill to decide whether a new technical result is ready for patent filing or should be revised, delayed, protected as a trade secret, or not filed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished invention disclosures, patent numbers, or trade-secret details may be sent to configured patent MCP or search services during assessment.

Mitigation: Confirm that the selected services, account authorization, and data-sharing terms are appropriate before analyzing confidential material.

Risk: The skill produces pre-filing recommendations that may influence patent strategy.

Mitigation: Have qualified IP counsel or an internal patent review owner verify the cited evidence and final filing decision before acting.
