## Description:

Generate or edit AI media (image, video, audio, 3D) on WaveSpeed, through the WaveSpeed MCP tools when they are connected or the wavespeed CLI on the user's machine. Use whenever the user asks to create, edit, animate, extend, upscale, or transform an image or video, generate audio/TTS/music or 3D, or produce marketing creatives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected media are sent to WaveSpeed; uploads receive hosted URLs, and generation can spend credits.

Mitigation: Use a trusted WaveSpeed connection, review privacy settings, and estimate costs before submitting sensitive media.
