## Description:

Search U.S. government contract opportunities, grants, jobs and past awards through MatchAwards' read-only public API, including company award histories and NAICS codes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[matchawards](https://clawhub.ai/user/matchawards)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Businesses, procurement teams and other users can find U.S. government opportunities, research prior awards and company histories, and identify relevant NAICS codes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search filters, including sensitive business terms, are sent to MatchAwards.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting private business information unless sharing it with MatchAwards is acceptable.

Risk: Public award records can be incomplete, and some opportunity notes are AI-generated.

Mitigation: Flag incomplete records and AI-generated notes; check source notices before making bid decisions.
