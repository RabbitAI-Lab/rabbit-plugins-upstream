## Use Case:

Creators, video producers, and prompt engineers use this Chinese-language skill to refine video ideas into story structure, shot-by-shot scripts, dialogue, sound cues, and Seedance-ready prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad video-related trigger terms may activate the skill in general video conversations.

Mitigation: Use or enable the skill when Chinese-language video storyboarding or Seedance prompt creation is intended, and review the activation context before relying on its output.

Risk: The workflow can write or edit creative output files when the host agent permits those actions.

Mitigation: Review proposed file changes and keep generated storyboards, prompts, and production notes separate from source materials until approved.
