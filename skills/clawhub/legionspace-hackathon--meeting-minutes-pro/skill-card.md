## Description: <br>
Generate concise, actionable meeting minutes for internal meetings, including metadata, attendees, agenda, decisions, action items with owner and due date, and follow-up steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[legionspace-hackathon](https://clawhub.ai/user/legionspace-hackathon) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, team leads, and project coordinators use this skill to produce standardized minutes for internal meetings up to 60 minutes. It turns agendas, transcripts, recordings, or raw notes into concise records of decisions, action items, owners, due dates, unresolved issues, and follow-ups. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Meeting minutes can expose confidential details, personal data, or internal links if shared without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review draft minutes and linked artifacts before publishing, confirm the intended recipients and destination, and avoid publishing raw PII unless required and authorized. <br>
Risk: Generated minutes may contain uncertain or incomplete decisions and action items when source material is missing. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for missing meeting metadata, agenda or transcript availability, and reviewer details; mark gaps as TBD or Unknown and route significant decisions for organizer verification. <br>
