## Description:

Use when planning seating for a wedding, dinner party, holiday table, or rehearsal dinner and you must respect keep-apart pairs, balance talkers across tables, seat couples near-but-not-alone, and keep exes/estranged family apart — and need an actual assignment chart, not vibes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Event hosts and planners can use the skill to turn a guest list, table capacities, and social constraints into seating assignments, per-table rosters, and a constraint audit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Annotated guest reports can expose private relationship notes.

Mitigation: Keep relationship notes and annotated reports private; share only names and seat assignments, and review the chart before printing.

Risk: The optimizer does not account for physical venue details such as aisle seats, sightlines, or service lanes.

Mitigation: Review the assignments against the venue map with a second host before finalizing the chart.
