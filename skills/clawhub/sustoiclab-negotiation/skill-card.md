## Description: <br>
Chinese-language negotiation and persuasion toolbox that helps users prepare conflict, negotiation, and persuasion conversations through emotion labeling, audience analysis, strategy selection, and structured response drafting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to prepare transparent, mutual-benefit negotiation, persuasion, conflict-resolution, and dialogue scripts. It guides the user through scene diagnosis, emotion scripts, audience posture, tactic selection, sample dialogue, and predicted counterpart responses. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill includes manipulative persuasion tactics that may be harmful in financial, workplace, intimate, vulnerable-person, or power-imbalanced situations. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for transparent, mutual-benefit conversations; add ethical boundaries, respect refusal, and avoid high-pressure tactics in sensitive or power-imbalanced contexts. <br>
Risk: Negotiation scripts may be incorrect, misleading, or inappropriate for the user's real relationship and local context. <br>
Mitigation: Review suggested scripts before use, keep claims honest, and adapt language to the actual facts, relationship, and consent boundaries. <br>
