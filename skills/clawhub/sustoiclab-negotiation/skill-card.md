## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users use this skill to identify persuasion and negotiation tactics in sales, workplace, relationship, or conflict scenarios, then return to their own decision criteria and draft defensive responses. It is intended for awareness, self-protection, and fair communication rather than manipulation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasion tactics are dual-use and could be misapplied as manipulation guidance.

Mitigation: Keep use defensive: identify what tactic may be affecting the user, return to the user's own decision criteria, and avoid coaching coercive use.

Risk: Users may over-rely on communication guidance for legal, financial, psychological, or other high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as awareness and self-protection support, and recommend appropriate professional advice for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: The skill may incorrectly infer another person's intent from limited context.

Mitigation: Ask clarifying questions, separate observable behavior from interpretation, and present conclusions as possibilities rather than certainty.
