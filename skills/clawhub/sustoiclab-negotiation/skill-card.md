## Description:

This Simplified Chinese skill helps users prepare ethical negotiation, persuasion, conflict-resolution, and wording strategies using audience analysis, tactical communication techniques, and anti-pattern self-checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users use this skill to prepare for voluntary workplace negotiation, conflict de-escalation, proposal discussion, relationship communication, and reasonable bargaining. It guides scenario diagnosis, emotion labeling, audience-specific strategy selection, wording drafts, and self-checks against manipulative or counterproductive tactics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary flags pressure-based persuasion patterns that may conflict with the skill's anti-manipulation limits.

Mitigation: Use only for transparent, voluntary, low-stakes communication and avoid money, contracts, vulnerable people, power imbalances, or situations involving pressure rather than free decision-making.

Risk: The skill may produce persuasive wording that sounds stronger than the user's evidence or intent supports.

Mitigation: Keep outputs honest, avoid false promises or deception, and add professional referral reminders for legal, medical, health, or major financial decisions.
