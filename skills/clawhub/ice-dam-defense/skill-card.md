## Description:

Helps homeowners plan residential pavement de-icing and assess roof ice-dam risk, snow load, and safe response options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and other residential users can compare de-icers by pavement temperature and area, check refreeze and material hazards, and triage roof ice-dam and snow-load concerns. The estimates do not replace professional inspection or emergency response.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Icy roof or ladder work can cause serious injury.

Mitigation: Stay on the ground; use a roof rake only where safe and call a qualified roofer for hazardous removal.

Risk: Roof-load estimates and leak guidance may miss structural or electrical hazards.

Mitigation: Seek professional or emergency help for active leaks near electricity, sagging, cracking, jammed doors, or uncertainty; do not treat the estimate as a structural assessment.

Risk: Incorrect de-icer choice or overnight refreezing can create slippery surfaces or harm pets, plants, and concrete.

Mitigation: Check pavement temperature and refreeze conditions, follow product labels, and protect sensitive surfaces and animals.
