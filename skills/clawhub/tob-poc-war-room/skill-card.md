## Description: <br>
POC war-room skill for ToB AI delivery. Use when a user needs to diagnose POC status, track pass rate, classify blockers, build a 48-hour closure plan, detect customer silence risk, generate daily POC war-room reports, or decide when a POC is ready to enter contract closing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, delivery, technical, and project teams use this skill to triage ToB AI proof-of-concept status, identify blockers, assign owners, plan the next 48 hours, and decide when a POC is ready for contract-closing work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter customer or project details while using the POC planning flow. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting unnecessary confidential information; use only the details required for triage, blocker ownership, and next-step planning. <br>
Risk: The skill produces sales and delivery recommendations that could be misleading if acceptance criteria, pass rate, blocker age, or customer feedback are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated plan against current project evidence before using it with a customer or advancing a POC to contract closing. <br>
