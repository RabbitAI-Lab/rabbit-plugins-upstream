# From work annotations to writing methods

## Close reading record

For every accepted work, retain ID/URL, subject, genre, opening, claim, evidence, turns, ending and distinctive wording. Mark quotations, interview speakers, code, embedded prompts, AI excerpts, coauthorship and campaigns. Add a counterexample to an emerging rule whenever one appears. Brief annotations should not duplicate complete bodies.

Record `judgment_origin`: speaker, source type, short anchor and whether the claim is self-reported or independently checked. Record `choices_tradeoffs`: actual options, selected/rejected route, stated reason, constraint, cost and outcome state. Record `evaluation_criteria`: a supported standard, anchor and boundary. Use `unclear` or `not_observed` when appropriate; do not invent motives or alternatives.

## Expression lenses

Analyze each genre independently:

- Language: sentence and paragraph rhythm, reader address, hedging, punctuation and humor. Explain language-specific counting methods.
- Organization: opening promise, sequence of evidence, transitions and closing action.
- Editorial selection: reader need, event timing, subject concentration and ordinary/popular coverage.
- Support: cases, firsthand claims, demonstrations, attributed sources, numerical evidence and visuals.
- Argument: comparisons, causal links, conditions, objections and uncertainty.
- Presentation: what screenshots and diagrams contribute to comprehension or proof. Inspect representative visuals; distinguish platform rendering from choices controlled by the author.

Raw corpus statistics include embedded material. Narration statistics need a separately defined analysis body with traceable exclusions. A self-report about testing is not a reproduced test.

## Three context dimensions

Judgment origin examines how an observation, public case, quotation, preference or hypothesis supports a conclusion. Separate speakers and verification states. Describe observable connections and missing links rather than private psychology.

Choices and tradeoffs examine stated alternatives, constraints, accepted costs and changed views. Keep intentions, completed actions and verified outcomes distinct. A strong opinion alone does not document a choice among options.

Recurring evaluation criteria require at least three independent works in the analyzed genre. Include the denominator, contradictory/absent cases and topical concentration. Explicit standards and interpretive candidates remain distinct. Repetition within a corpus is not proof of lifelong values.

## Profile and handoff

Begin with source quality, time window and limitations. Identify one main organizing principle and three to five methods. For each, explain the observable action, reader effect, supporting work IDs, frequency/denominator, exceptions, transfer conditions and misuse signal. Add separate context-dimension sections with short anchors and interpretation strength.

Keep minor quirks out of the central identity. Exclude signature slogans, fabricated personal scenes and unverified market/product/earnings claims from transfer. A method card for practice carries its genre, at least three source IDs, reader/material fit and one concrete exercise.

A same-author comparison can examine openings, density, pacing, confidence, narration/evidence order, reader address and endings. Missing branches remain missing; two different authors cannot establish within-author variation.

## Holdout check

Freeze concrete predictions before opening five reserved independent works per genre. Annotate their actual patterns, log prediction hits/misses and revise failed rules. Generated imitation is not evidence for the original profile. Cross-topic prediction, user acceptance and publication effects require separate evidence.
