## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and practical next steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users such as solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test product ideas before spending heavily. It produces practical validation workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning support, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad startup or product-planning language. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrower routing when teams need tighter control over when product validation planning guidance is applied. <br>
Risk: Product validation outputs can still reflect incomplete assumptions about market demand, user segments, or feasibility. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, compare outputs against the stated success criteria, and validate conclusions with real customer or market evidence before committing major resources. <br>
