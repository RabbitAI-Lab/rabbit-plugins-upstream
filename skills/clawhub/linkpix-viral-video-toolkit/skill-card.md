## Description:

Analyzes short-video links to develop similar marketing-video scripts and videos, or extract audio from the source video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and content creators use this skill to analyze popular short videos, adapt their scripts for their own products, generate marketing videos, or extract audio from video links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the external qhkit CLI and using the iqinghu service requires trusting those providers.

Mitigation: Confirm the npm package and service are trusted before installation or use.

Risk: The service may receive video links, prompts, selected local images, and a qhkit token.

Mitigation: Review material before submission and keep the token private.

Risk: Reusing source scripts, videos, or extracted audio commercially may raise rights issues.

Mitigation: Confirm usage rights and adapt scripts rather than copying protected material.
