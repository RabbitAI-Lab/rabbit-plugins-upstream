## Description:

Creates on-brand YouTube thumbnails from video frames, creator photos, and real text, with optional A/B variants and batch or upload automation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and their automation developers use this skill to create consistent YouTube thumbnails from real video frames or approved photos, then render variants or connect thumbnail generation to an upload workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source images may be uploaded to Orshot and made accessible through public image links.

Mitigation: Use only images you are comfortable uploading and make publicly accessible.

Risk: Recurring thumbnail jobs can incur rendering costs and publish an unsuitable thumbnail if left unchecked.

Mitigation: Review account permissions and rendering costs, and inspect finished thumbnails before enabling recurring updates.
