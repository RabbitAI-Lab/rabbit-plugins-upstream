## Description:

Helps agents find and summarize public conversations about brands, products, and topics across Reddit, X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, and Telegram.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand and communications teams use this skill to monitor public mentions, summarize feedback and complaints, and track conversations about products or competitors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing and pairing the ClawLink plugin connects the agent to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is comfortable pairing with ClawLink before installing or connecting.

Risk: Public-data calls consume ClawLink credit, and repeated searches can accumulate costs.

Mitigation: Check call prices and obtain approval before costly calls or large batches.

Risk: Brand or topic searches are sent through ClawLink's public-data tools.

Mitigation: Avoid private accounts and sensitive non-public investigations.
