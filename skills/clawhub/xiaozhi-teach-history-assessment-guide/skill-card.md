## Description:

Helps history teachers draft curriculum-aligned questions, layered scoring rubrics, exam blueprints, and feedback on common answer-writing problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school history teachers use this skill to draft source-based and open-ended questions, differentiated scoring criteria, and class-level feedback. Teachers verify historical accuracy, sources, and all AI-generated questions before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, language, privacy requirements, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft questions may contain incorrect historical claims or unverifiable sources.

Mitigation: Check facts and citations against the curriculum and teaching materials; require teacher review before using AI-generated questions.

Risk: Entering unreleased exam content could compromise exam confidentiality.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam questions or answer keys; use general design guidance and teacher-created practice instead.

Risk: Student responses or class feedback may expose personal information.

Mitigation: Remove student identifiers and use pseudonyms or seat numbers before sharing material with the skill or a class.
