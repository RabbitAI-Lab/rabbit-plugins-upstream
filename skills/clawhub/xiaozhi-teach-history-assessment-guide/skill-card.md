## Description:

Helps middle and high school history teachers create curriculum-aligned source-based and open-ended questions, build tiered scoring rubrics, and turn common answer-writing issues into feedback activities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

History teachers use the skill to plan curriculum-aligned questions, develop tiered scoring rubrics, and turn recurring answer-writing issues into classroom feedback activities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student work or class records may contain identifiable or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use anonymized student work, exclude confidential details, and verify the host's memory, sharing, and deletion controls.

Risk: Draft questions or historical sources may be inaccurate, and confidential exam material must not be supplied.

Mitigation: Exclude confidential exam content and check generated questions, historical facts, and source citations before classroom use.

Risk: Companion skills have separate behavior and security controls.

Mitigation: Review companion skills separately before using them with class data.
