## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 history teachers draft source-based and open-ended questions, tiered scoring rubrics, and feedback on common answer-writing issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

History teachers use this skill to plan curriculum-aligned questions and assessment blueprints, draft tiered rubrics, and prepare class-wide feedback on student answers. Teachers review generated questions and materials before classroom or exam use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; designed for mainland China, with crisis contacts and school-stage assumptions requiring localization elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified historical claims or source attributions in generated questions could mislead students.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify historical facts, source citations, and scoring guidance before classroom or exam use.

Risk: Unreleased official exam content or identifiable student data could be exposed when supplied to the skill.

Mitigation: Do not submit unreleased official exam papers or student real names; use anonymized answer examples.

Risk: Mainland-China-specific crisis contacts and school-stage assumptions may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize crisis referral contacts and school-stage assumptions before use outside mainland China.
