## Description:

Helps history teachers design standards-aligned source-analysis and open-response assessment items, layered rubrics, and answer-norm feedback for Chinese secondary history classes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External educators use this skill to draft history assessment blueprints, item prompts, scoring rubrics, and post-assessment feedback plans. It is intended for teacher-mediated use, with generated items reviewed before classroom, homework, or exam deployment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; other regions require localization of curriculum assumptions, privacy rules, and crisis-help contacts before student-facing use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated history questions may contain incorrect facts, unreliable source attribution, or unsupported historical interpretation.

Mitigation: Require teacher review of historical facts, source provenance, curriculum alignment, and AI-generated item labels before classroom, homework, or exam use.

Risk: Users may enter confidential unpublished exam papers, answer keys, scoring standards, or real student personal data.

Mitigation: Do not input confidential unpublished exam materials; use aliases or seat numbers for student work and keep teacher confirmation before any record is stored.

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese K12 history-teacher workflows and may be inappropriate outside that curriculum and legal context.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, privacy practices, and crisis-help contacts before deploying outside mainland China or in another language.

Risk: Student answer text may include self-harm, abuse, bullying, or other safety-crisis signals.

Mitigation: Stop the assessment workflow and follow the crisis referral protocol, including localized emergency guidance and referral to trusted adults.
