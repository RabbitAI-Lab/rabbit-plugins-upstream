## Description:

帮助初高中历史教师设计史料题与开放性试题、制定分层评分标准，并针对学生答卷的共性问题准备答题规范讲评。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

历史教师使用本技能规划符合中国大陆课程标准的初高中历史试题、起草开放题分层评分标准，并按共性错因设计讲评活动。教师须核对史实、出处和评分标准后才能用于教学或考试。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localization of curriculum and crisis-referral guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions or historical sources may contain errors or unsupported attributions.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify historical facts, source citations, questions and rubrics before classroom or exam use.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials or student-identifying data may be exposed if pasted into an AI tool.

Mitigation: Do not submit unreleased exam papers or real student names, contact details or family details; use pseudonyms or seat numbers.

Risk: Mainland-China curriculum and crisis-referral guidance may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions and referral channels before use outside mainland China.
