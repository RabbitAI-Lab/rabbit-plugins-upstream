## Description:

Helps Chinese secondary-school history teachers draft source-based and open-ended questions, level-based scoring rubrics, and feedback on common answer-writing problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

History teachers in mainland China's junior and senior secondary schools use this skill to draft curriculum-aligned assessment items, scoring rubrics, and whole-class feedback plans. Teachers review generated questions, historical sources, and grading criteria before classroom or exam use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localization required elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions, historical sources, or scoring rubrics may be inaccurate or unsuitable for assessment.

Mitigation: Teachers verify each question, source, and rubric against the curriculum before classroom or exam use.

Risk: Crisis contacts, curriculum guidance, and legal assumptions may be wrong outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, curriculum references, and legal assumptions before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Student answer samples or unreleased examination material may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers in feedback and do not submit or revise unreleased unified-exam questions.
