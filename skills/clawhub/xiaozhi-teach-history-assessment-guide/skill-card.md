## Description:

Helps history teachers draft curriculum-aligned assessment questions, tiered scoring rubrics, and feedback on common answer-writing issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

History teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft source-based and open-ended questions, exam blueprints and tiered rubrics, and to prepare class-wide guidance on common answer-writing problems. Teachers review generated materials before using them with students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curricula, language, legal requirements and emergency guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inaccurate historical questions or unsupported source citations could mislead students.

Mitigation: Teachers verify each AI-generated question, historical claim and source before classroom, homework or exam use.

Risk: Confidential exam content or identifiable student work could be exposed.

Mitigation: Do not enter live confidential exam materials or student identifiers; use de-identified examples for feedback.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and help-channel guidance may not fit use outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, language, legal requirements and emergency referral guidance before deployment elsewhere.
