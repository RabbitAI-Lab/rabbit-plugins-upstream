## Use Case:

初高中历史教师使用该技能拟定命题蓝图、史料题与开放性试题草稿、分层评分标准，以及针对答题共性问题的讲评方案；所有试题和史料由教师核对后使用。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated questions or historical sources may be inaccurate or unsuited to the curriculum.

Mitigation: Teachers should verify historical facts, source citations and all generated questions before using them in class or examinations.

Risk: Student-identifying information or unreleased examination materials could be exposed.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers instead of real identifiers; do not enter unreleased exam materials.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, privacy requirements and crisis contacts may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before deployment elsewhere, including crisis-contact guidance.
