## Use Case:

中国大陆初高中历史教师可用它起草命题蓝图、史料题、开放题分层评分标准和答题规范讲评方案；题目和评分须由教师审核后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, emergency resources, and consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated questions, source attributions, or scoring rubrics may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Teachers verify historical facts, cited sources, and scoring rubrics before classroom or exam use.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and emergency guidance may not suit other regions or languages.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, emergency resources, and consent rules before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Student records or confidential pre-exam questions could be disclosed if entered into the skill.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers for student work and never input unused unified-exam questions or answers.
