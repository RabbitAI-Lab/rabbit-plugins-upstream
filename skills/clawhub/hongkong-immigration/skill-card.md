## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to triage Hong Kong entry, visa, residency, and immigration-pathway questions, then receive document-path recommendations, application steps, materials, official channels, timelines, and risk notes. It is intended for general information and planning, not legal representation or individualized immigration advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Immigration rules, fees, quotas, eligibility thresholds, and visa-free arrangements can change after the skill's reference files are published. <br>
Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive facts against official Hong Kong Immigration Department, National Immigration Administration, or other relevant government sources before presenting them as current. <br>
Risk: Users may treat general immigration guidance as legal advice for a case-specific decision. <br>
Mitigation: State that outputs are general information and direct users to the relevant authority or a qualified professional for important case-specific decisions. <br>
