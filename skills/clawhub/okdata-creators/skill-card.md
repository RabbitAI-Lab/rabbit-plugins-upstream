## Description:

Find and vet creators and KOLs for a niche on Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RedNote), TikTok, X (Twitter) and Weibo with the okdata API. Searches posts in the niche, gathers their authors, then checks followers, recent posts and engagement rate to build a shortlist with links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing teams and creator researchers use this skill to find public creators in a niche, review their audience and engagement signals, and prepare a linked shortlist for outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and account identifiers may be sent to okdata, and the security review raised privacy and purpose-fit questions, including a Weibo trends request.

Mitigation: Check that the requested research task fits the skill's purpose and that the search terms and public account identifiers are acceptable to share with okdata before making API calls.
