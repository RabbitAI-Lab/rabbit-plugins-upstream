## Description:

Helps UIBC medical-device domain teams assess memory-transfer identity and accountability using TH-MED-001/002/003 criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

UIBC MED-domain participants and reviewers use the skill to prepare and assess memory-migration identity decisions, supporting evidence, and accountability statements against TH-MED-001/002/003.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competition judging guidance could be mistaken for general medical, legal, regulatory, financial, or contract advice.

Mitigation: Limit use to TH-MED-001/002/003 adjudication and obtain human review before relying on consequential advice.
