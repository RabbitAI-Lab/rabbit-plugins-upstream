## Use Case:

People signing up for PowPow use this skill for Korean-language, step-by-step registration help, troubleshooting, and directions to activate their accounts through a subscription. Users complete signup, verification, and payment themselves on PowPow pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Passwords, verification codes, or payment details could be exposed if entered into chat.

Mitigation: Complete signup, verification, and payment on official PowPow pages; never share these details in chat.

Risk: Registration or subscription guidance may not match the current website.

Mitigation: Follow the current instructions and available payment options on official PowPow pages rather than relying on outdated steps.
