## Description:

Guides users through creating a PowPow account, understanding the product through screenshots, resolving registration issues, and activating their subscription without collecting credentials or payment details in chat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External PowPow users use this skill for guided account registration, troubleshooting common signup errors, and understanding subscription activation paths. The agent provides Korean-language onboarding guidance while the user completes all account, verification, and payment actions in the official PowPow or provider UI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may be directed to external links and a subscription page during onboarding.

Mitigation: Use the official PowPow and provider pages for registration, verification, subscription, and payment steps.

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment details in chat while asking for help.

Mitigation: Keep credentials, verification codes, and payment details only in the official PowPow or provider UI; the skill should provide guidance without requesting or handling secrets.
