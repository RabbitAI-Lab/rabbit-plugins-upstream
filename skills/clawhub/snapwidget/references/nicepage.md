# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Nicepage website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Nicepage site with the HTML element.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **HTML** element where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Nicepage

1. Open your site in Nicepage and go to the page where you want the widget.

2. Open the **Add Elements** menu and add the **HTML** element to a block.

   ![The HTML element in the Add Elements menu](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/nicepage/5.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=dc0fbc2ee9dddfc51c84cdf8e688df70)

3. Select the element. In the **Property Panel** on the right, paste your code in the **Code** field.

   ![The Code field of the HTML element in the Property Panel](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/nicepage/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=45b72f1c97d5dfc579bb52125f5f0367)

4. Drag the handles of the element to set its width and height.

5. Click **Preview** to see the widget. The editor itself does not show it.

6. Publish your site.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your published site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
