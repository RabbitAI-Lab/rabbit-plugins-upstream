# How do I add an Instagram widget to my WonderCMS website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a WonderCMS page. You paste the code into the page and click outside to save.

Paste your SnapWidget code into the content of a WonderCMS page. WonderCMS keeps HTML and JavaScript code as you paste it. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In WonderCMS

1. Log in to WonderCMS and open the page where you want the widget.

2. Click the content area of the page.

3. If you use the Summernote Editor plugin, click **Code View** (`</>`) in the editor toolbar first.

   ![The Code View button in the Summernote toolbar](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/wondercms/5.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=eb517fa7c72e1d1abf5f92d7bd9c70c1)

4. Paste your code where you want the widget to show.

5. Click anywhere outside the content area. WonderCMS saves the change and publishes the page.

### Check your widget

Log out, or open the page in a private browser window. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
