# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Bludit website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Bludit page. Switch to the EasyMDE editor first, because TinyMCE blocks the widget.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a Bludit page where you want the widget to show. The default TinyMCE editor adds a `sandbox` setting to every iframe, which stops the widget. Use the EasyMDE editor for this. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Bludit

1. In the Bludit admin, click **Plugins** under **Settings**.

   ![The Plugins item in the Settings menu](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/bludit/1.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=e92919cea040fa76e8c5750b703f0ede)

2. Find **TinyMCE** and click **Deactivate**.

   ![The Deactivate link for TinyMCE](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/bludit/2.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=ecc3e2b80e63dbf30a2a973d693da73c)

3. Find **EasyMDE** and click **Activate**.

   ![The Activate link for EasyMDE](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/bludit/3.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=4e6866a5490a6aa64ec9d9757e2d3c79)

4. Click **Content** and open the page. To make a new page, click **New content**.

5. Paste your code where you want the widget to show.

   ![The EasyMDE editor with the widget code](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/bludit/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=265767dacf1433cd5c4cd4d322c49423)

6. Click **Save**. For new content, click **Publish**.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
