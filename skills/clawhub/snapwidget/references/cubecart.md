# How do I add an Instagram widget to my CubeCart website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a CubeCart document page, such as your homepage or About Us page.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a CubeCart document, such as your homepage or your About Us page. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Use a document, not a product description. CubeCart removes `<script>` lines and most iframes from product descriptions.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In CubeCart

1. In your CubeCart admin, go to **File Manager** > **Documents**.

   ![The Documents item under File Manager](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/cubecart/2.jpg?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=8f0c9cf30e6f0acf366ac8ad38702f95)

2. Click the document where you want the widget. To make a new page, click **Create Document**.

3. Click the **Content** tab. In the editor toolbar, click **Source**.

   ![The Content tab with the Source button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/cubecart/5.jpg?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=0ec3a96a2079ffd6d265c240f415c8a2)

4. Paste your code where you want the widget to show.

   ![The widget code in the Source view](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/cubecart/7.jpg?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=38c5e08e03fe363e65bf41c9ad45f5e0)

5. Click **Save**.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live store. The editor shows only a placeholder for the widget. The live page shows your latest Instagram posts.
