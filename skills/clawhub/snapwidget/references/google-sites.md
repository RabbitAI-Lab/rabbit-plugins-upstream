# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Google Sites website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Google Sites page with the Embed code option.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **Embed** box on your Google Sites page. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Google Sites

1. On a computer, open your site in Google Sites and go to the page where you want the widget.

2. On the right, click **Insert**, then click **Embed**.

3. Click the **Embed code** tab and paste your code. Click **Next**.

   ![The Embed from the web window with the Embed code tab](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/google-sites/6.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=6114d4620cfedad74d130c210e6cbf84)

4. Check the preview and click **Insert**.

5. Drag the blue handles of the embed box until the whole widget shows without a scroll bar.

   ![The embed box with its resize handles](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/google-sites/8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=60701edc9f2aecd43253f2db00fe1d54)

6. Click **Publish**.

> [!TIP]
>
> Google Sites shows embedded code inside its own frame. If your widget uses **Lightbox** as **Photo Link**, the lightbox can only open inside the embed box. Make the box large enough, or set **Photo Link** to **Photo Popup** on the **Advanced** tab of your Pro widget.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your published site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts. After each change in the editor, click **Publish** again.
