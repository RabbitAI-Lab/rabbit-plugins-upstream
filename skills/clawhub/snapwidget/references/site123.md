# How do I add an Instagram widget to my SITE123 website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a SITE123 page with the Custom Code option of the content editor.

Paste your SnapWidget code into the content editor of a SITE123 page with the **Custom Code** option. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In SITE123

1. Log in to SITE123 and click **Edit** on your website.

2. Click **Pages**, then click **Edit** next to the page where you want the widget.

3. Open the text where the widget should go. On an Articles page, click **Add New Article**.

4. In the toolbar of the content editor, click **Custom Code** (**\</>**).

   ![The Custom Code button in the content editor toolbar](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/site123/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=9add6d7325c9b41515bc19d5f6e77d63)

5. Hover over the **Custom HTML** box and click **Change**. Replace the sample code with your code and click **Update**.

   ![The Custom HTML window with the Update button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/site123/11.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=22d849db0333582fd692199e3a299d68)

6. Click **Save**, then publish your website.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts. The editor preview can look different from the live page.
