# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Big Cartel website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Big Cartel custom page or product description. You need a paid Big Cartel plan.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a custom page or a product description in Big Cartel. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Custom pages and embed code need a paid Big Cartel plan (Platinum or Diamond). The free Gold plan does not have them.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Big Cartel

1. Log in to your Big Cartel admin and click **Shop designer**.
2. Click **Pages**, then click **Add a new page +**. To change a page that you made before, click the page.
3. Enter a page name.
4. In the page editor, switch to HTML mode and paste your code.
5. Click **Done**, then click **Publish**.

### Add the widget to a product page

1. Click **Products** and open the product.
2. Paste your code into **Description**.
3. Click **Save**.

### Check your widget

Open the page or product on your live store. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
