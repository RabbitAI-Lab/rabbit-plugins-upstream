# How do I add an Instagram widget to my ZOHO website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Zoho Sites page with the Code Snippet element.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a **Code Snippet** element where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Zoho Sites

1. Open your site in the Zoho Sites builder and go to the page where you want the widget.
2. Click the **Add** icon at the top of the builder and choose **Elements**.
3. Drag the **Code Snippet** element onto the page.
4. Type a name for the snippet and click **OK**.
5. Paste your code in the **Code Snippet** window and click **OK**.
6. Click **Publish**.

### Check your widget

The builder does not show the widget. Open the page on your published site to see it.
