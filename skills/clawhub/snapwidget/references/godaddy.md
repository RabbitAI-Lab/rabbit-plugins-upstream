# How do I add an Instagram widget to my GoDaddy Website Builder website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a GoDaddy Websites + Marketing site with an HTML section.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **HTML** section where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In GoDaddy

1. Go to your GoDaddy product page. Expand **Websites + Marketing** and click **Manage** next to your site.

2. Open the page where you want the widget. Add a section at the place where the widget should show.

3. Search for **HTML** and click **Add**.

   ![The HTML section with the Add button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/godaddy/5.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=cc045d86ad2f39015de396a2ac2bf506)

4. Paste your code in the **Custom Code** field. Leave **Forced Height** empty for an automatic height. If the widget is cut off, enter a height in pixels.

   ![The Custom Code and Forced Height fields of the HTML section](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/godaddy/8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=642b5d08169c71438b8a96c4ee306f46)

5. GoDaddy saves your changes on its own. Click **Preview** to see the page, then click **Publish**.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
