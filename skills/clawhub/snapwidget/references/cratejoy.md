# How do I add an Instagram widget to Cratejoy?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to your Cratejoy store website by pasting the code into a theme template.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a template of your Cratejoy store theme, where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> You need a Cratejoy plan that includes your own website, for example **Essentials** or **Advanced**. The **Marketplace Only** plan has no website.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Cratejoy

1. In your Cratejoy admin, click **Design**, then click **Code**.
2. Open the template for the page where you want the widget. For your homepage, open `index.html`.
3. Paste your code where you want the widget to show.
4. Save the template.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live store. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
