# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Wix website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Wix site with the Embed HTML element in the Wix Editor, Wix Studio or Wix Harmony.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **Embed HTML** element where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In the Wix Editor

1. Open your site in the Wix Editor and go to the page where you want the widget.

2. Click **Add Elements** on the left. Click **Embed Code**, then **Popular Embeds**, then **Embed HTML**.

   ![Embed HTML under Popular Embeds](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/wix/11.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=59afac604e30974a836e92b507fc6370)

3. Click the new element on the page and click **Enter Code**.

4. Paste your code under **Add your code here** and click **Apply**.

5. Drag the handles of the element until the whole widget shows without scroll bars.

6. Click **Publish**.

### In Wix Studio

1. Open the **Add Elements** panel and select **Embed & Social**. Click **Embed Code** to add it to the page.
2. Click **Enter Code**, paste your code and click **Apply**.
3. Resize the element until the whole widget fits, then click **Publish**.

### In Wix Harmony

1. Click **+ Add** at the top left and choose **Elements**. Hover over the **More Options** icon and select **Embed**. Drag **Embed code** onto the page.
2. Click the gear icon on the element. Paste your code in **Enter HTML embed code**.
3. Resize the element until the whole widget fits, then publish your site.

> [!TIP]
>
> Wix shows embedded code inside its own frame. If your widget uses **Lightbox** as **Photo Link**, the lightbox can only open inside the element. On a Pro widget, set **Photo Link** to **Photo Popup** on the **Advanced** tab. The photo then opens in its own window. The element also does not change its size on its own, so check the page on a phone too.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
