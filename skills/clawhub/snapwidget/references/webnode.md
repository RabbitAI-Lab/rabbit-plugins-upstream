# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Webnode website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Webnode page with an HTML block. You need a paid Webnode plan.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **HTML** block where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> On websites created after 26 June 2023, the HTML block needs a paid Webnode plan. If you do not see **HTML**, upgrade your plan.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Webnode

1. Log in to Webnode, open your project and click **Edit site**.

2. Go to the page where you want the widget.

3. Hover over the place where the widget should go and click **+**. In **Add content**, click **HTML**.

   ![The HTML option in the Add content menu](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/webnode/6.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=bca67a962f444b9b85ec5a7a519841f4)

4. Paste your code in **Enter your HTML code** and click **OK**.

   ![The Insert HTML window with the OK button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/webnode/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=36a4577acb01d38f4c6650f4f514f156)

5. Click **Publish**.

### Check your widget

The editor does not show the widget. Open the page on your published site to see it. After each change, click **Publish** again.
