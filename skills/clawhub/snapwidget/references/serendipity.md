# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Serendipity website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Serendipity (s9y) blog entry with the Entry Body field.

Paste your SnapWidget code into the body of a Serendipity blog entry where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Serendipity

1. In the Serendipity admin, click **New Entry**. To change an entry that you made before, click **Edit Entries** and open the entry.

   ![The New Entry and Edit Entries links](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/serendipity/4.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=cb89b2bcd2191e9848994586efeaf295)

2. Paste your code into **Entry Body**. If you use the WYSIWYG editor, click **Source** first.

   ![The Entry Body field with the widget code](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/serendipity/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=4eaea9f00a2ae21d4d35e3753e7f1e1a)

3. Make sure the entry is set to **Publish**, not **Draft**. Then click **Save**.

   ![The Preview and Save buttons](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/serendipity/10.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=0a91187ac8dd29250360db964671cede)

> [!TIP]
>
> The NL2BR markup plugin adds line breaks around the code, which can add empty space above the widget. To turn it off for this entry, select **Markup: NL2BR** under **Disable Markup plugins for this entry:**. This option comes from the "Extended properties for entries" plugin.
>
> ![The Disable Markup plugins for this entry option](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/serendipity/8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=ad1525b0fdd50278cd62991764b98608)

### Check your widget

Open your blog. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts in the entry.
