# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Tumblr blog?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Tumblr blog page or to your custom theme. Tumblr posts do not allow the code.

Tumblr removes embed code from posts. To show your widget, add it to a blog page or to your custom theme. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Tumblr does not allow JavaScript on blog pages. Before you copy the code, open the widget in SnapWidget, turn off **Responsive** and click **Install Widget**. If the code still has `<script>` or `<link>` lines, delete them and keep only the `<iframe>` line.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Tumblr

1. In Tumblr, open **Settings** and select your blog. Under **Custom theme**, turn on **Enable custom theme**. Then click **Edit theme**.

   ![The Custom Theme setting with the Edit theme button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/tumblr/6.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=c17d21eedca46098cb2c1f2e2f5b4f96)

2. In the theme editor sidebar, click **Add a page**.

   ![The Add a page option in the theme editor](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/tumblr/23.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=341d8478513c9ee11d9f0af4d40e553c)

3. Select **Standard Layout**. Enter the page URL and a **Page Title**, and turn on **Show a link to this page**.

4. Paste your code into the page body.

   ![A Tumblr page with the widget code](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/tumblr/24.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=698aac375a0035ea3c064343c2653ba0)

5. Click **Save**.

### Add the widget to every page of your blog

To show the widget on all pages, add the code to your theme. In the theme editor, click **Edit HTML** and paste your code where you want the widget to show. Click **Update Preview**, then click **Save**.

### Check your widget

Open the new page on your blog. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
