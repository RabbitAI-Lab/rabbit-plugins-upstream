# How do I add an Instagram widget to my ClassicPress website page?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a ClassicPress page or post with the Text tab of the classic editor.

Paste your SnapWidget code into the **Text** tab of the ClassicPress editor, on the page or post where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> You need the Administrator or Editor role. On a multisite network, only a Super Admin can add the code. For other roles, ClassicPress removes the code when you save.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In ClassicPress

1. In your dashboard, click **Pages**. Hover over the page and click **Edit**. For a post, click **Posts** instead.

   ![The Edit link under a page title](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/classicpress/12.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=68a3e47b0382bf0ba13ef21f8c91ed4a)

2. Click the **Text** tab above the editor.

3. Paste your code where you want the widget to show.

   ![The Text tab with the widget code](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/classicpress/13.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=5ab794031e5e93c818fa0e9437081ccc)

4. Click **Update**. For a new page, click **Publish**.

   ![The Update button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/classicpress/14.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=58ee11200a629bca34026c81e7b712ec)

> [!WARNING]
>
> Do not switch to the **Visual** tab after you paste the code. The visual editor can remove the `<link>` line, and then photos do not open in the lightbox.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
