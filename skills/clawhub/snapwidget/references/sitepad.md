# How do I add an Instagram widget to my SitePad Builder website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a SitePad page with the Embed widget.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **Embed** widget where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In SitePad

1. In your SitePad dashboard, click **Pages** > **All Pages**. Click the edit icon on the page where you want the widget.

2. In the left panel, open the **Widgets** tab. Under **OTHER**, drag the **Embed** widget onto the page.

   ![The Embed widget in the OTHER group](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/sitepad/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=80c340185bf5e6607e7c1cb828359e1f)

3. On the **Settings** tab of **Edit Embed**, paste your code in the **Paste HTML code** box.

   ![The Edit Embed panel with the Paste HTML code box](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/sitepad/10.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=f0d693ed7e980d527db98edef91f1249)

4. Click **Update** to save the page.

### Check your widget

Click **Preview Changes**, or open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
