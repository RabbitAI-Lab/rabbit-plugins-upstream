# How do I add an Instagram widget to my PageCloud website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Pagecloud site with the AI assistant or the Embed app in the classic editor.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an embed on your Pagecloud page. Pagecloud has two editors: Pagecloud AI and the classic editor. Use the steps for the editor you have. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Pagecloud AI

1. Open your site in Pagecloud AI.
2. Paste your code in the chat and ask the assistant to add it where you want the widget. For example: "Add this embed code below the About section."
3. Publish your site.

### In the Pagecloud classic editor

1. Open the page where you want the widget.

2. In the menu on the left, click **Apps**. Under **Generic**, drag the **Embed** app onto the page.

   ![The Embed app under Generic](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/pagecloud/6.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=45d0a83103ba7fb1029af6dd26ff94ba)

3. Paste your code in the **Embed Code** field and click **Ok**.

   ![The Embed code window with the Ok button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/pagecloud/8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=13c45668425dafdcbd0ab9988793668d)

4. Drag the handles of the embed until the whole widget fits.

5. Click **Save**, then publish the page.

To change the code later, click **Click to replace** on the embed. This removes the old code, so paste the full new code.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
