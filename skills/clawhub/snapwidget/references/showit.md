# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Showit website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Showit page with the Embed Code element.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **Embed Code** element where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Showit

1. Open your site in the Showit design app. Go to the page and the canvas where you want the widget.

2. Hover over the element icon in the bottom toolbar and choose **Embed Code**.

   ![The Embed Code option in the element menu](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/showit/8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=7aac6bf63ea8873c8e06f1611a0219c7)

3. Select the new box. In the panel on the right, open the **Embed Info** tab.

4. Click the **Custom Code** box. Paste your code and click **Save**.

5. Drag the box to the place where you want the widget, and drag its handles to set the size. Do this in the desktop view and in the mobile view.

6. Click **Publish**.

> [!TIP]
>
> Put the widget on a canvas that is always visible. Embedded code may not show on hidden canvases or in canvas views.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site, on a computer and on a phone. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
