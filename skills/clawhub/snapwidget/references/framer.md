# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Framer website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Framer site with the Embed component.

Paste your SnapWidget code into an **Embed** component where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Framer

1. Open your project in Framer and go to the page where you want the widget.
2. Open the **Insert** menu and search for **Embed**. Drag the **Embed** component onto the page.
3. Select the component. In its settings on the right, choose **HTML** and paste your code in the **HTML** field.
4. Drag the edges of the component until the whole widget fits.
5. Click **Publish**.

### Check your widget

Open your published site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts. If the widget does not show on the canvas, check the preview or the published site.
