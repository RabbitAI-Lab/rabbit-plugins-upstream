# How do I add an Instagram widget to my WebsiteBaker website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a WebsiteBaker page in a WYSIWYG section, with the editor set to None.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a WYSIWYG section of a WebsiteBaker page. The CKEditor editor can change or remove the code, so turn the editor off first. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!WARNING]
>
> Do not use a **Code** section. A Code section is for PHP only and shows errors with HTML code.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In WebsiteBaker

1. Click **Settings**, then click **Show Advanced Options**.

   ![The Show Advanced Options button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/websitebaker/2.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=f6134a0d7a5ed4667fe2f5d697127843)

2. Set **WYSIWYG Editor** to **None** and click **Save**.

   ![The WYSIWYG Editor setting set to None](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/websitebaker/5.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=5fa7ff3726a901f0673fbfaa3968ba4d)

3. Click **Pages** and open the page where you want the widget.

4. In the WYSIWYG section, paste your code where you want the widget to show. Click **Save**.

   ![A WYSIWYG section with the widget code](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/websitebaker/12.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=32e1845d4900b02cdca34d2754be9954)

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. WebsiteBaker does not show the widget in the editor. The live page shows your latest Instagram posts.
