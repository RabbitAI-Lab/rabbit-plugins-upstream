# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Webflow website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Webflow site with the Code Embed element. You need a paid Webflow plan.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a **Code Embed** element where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> The **Code Embed** element needs an active Site plan, or a Core, Growth, Agency or Freelancer Workspace plan. Free Starter sites cannot use it.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Webflow

1. Open your site in the Webflow Designer and go to the page where you want the widget.
2. Open the **Add** panel and drag a **Code Embed** element onto the canvas.
3. Paste your code in the code editor. Save and close the editor.
4. Click **Publish** and publish the site to your domain.

The canvas can show a placeholder instead of the widget. The widget shows when you preview or publish the site.

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts.
