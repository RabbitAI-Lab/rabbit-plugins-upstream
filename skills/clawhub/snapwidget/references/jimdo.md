# How do I add an Instagram widget to my Jimdo website?

> Add your SnapWidget Instagram widget to a Jimdo Creator website with the Widget/HTML element.

Paste your SnapWidget code into a **Widget/HTML** element where you want the widget to show. The same steps work for Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube widgets.

> [!NOTE]
>
> These steps are for Jimdo Creator. The Jimdo Website Builder does not have a Widget/HTML element.

### In SnapWidget

1. [Create your widget](https://snapwidget.com/docs/getting-started/create-an-instagram-widget.md), or open an existing widget from your [dashboard](https://snapwidget.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Install Widget**.
3. In the **Install Widget** window, click **Copy Code**.

### In Jimdo

1. Log in to Jimdo and choose your website. Click **Edit**.

2. Go to the page where you want the widget. Hover over the content area and click **Add Element**.

3. Click **More Elements and Add-ons**, then click **Widget/HTML**.

   ![The More Elements and Add-ons button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/jimdo/6.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=b50fa579c45cbaefbb4fb3ca4e5e085c)

   ![The Widget/HTML element](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/jimdo/7.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=9bcf2552d333dbb2f74df06384534931)

4. Paste your code in the box and click **Save**.

   ![The Widget/HTML code box with the Save button](https://mintcdn.com/snapwidget/QJTE73Rcy1kqujES/images/install/jimdo/8.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=QJTE73Rcy1kqujES\&q=85\&s=e91267c7b7d892db0926a44a8db42a4c)

### Check your widget

Open the page on your live site. The widget shows your latest Instagram posts. If the widget does not show, reload the page.
