## Description: <br>
ZhenZhen EHS Assistant helps EHS officers and safety inspectors generate scenario-based safety procedures, regulatory references, checklists, templates, and operational guidance across 21 EHS work scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[hegenerkondo335-dot](https://clawhub.ai/user/hegenerkondo335-dot) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
EHS officers, safety inspectors, and project safety managers use this skill to turn an EHS scenario into practical steps, applicable regulatory references, inspection or training templates, and common pitfall guidance. It is intended for Chinese-language, China-oriented EHS SOP support and should be reviewed against current local rules and company procedures before operational use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China-oriented; verify local jurisdiction requirements before use elsewhere. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: EHS guidance, regulatory citations, and operational templates may be outdated or mismatched to a user's local requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current local regulations and company procedures before using any recommendation or template operationally. <br>
Risk: Some scenarios involve high-risk work controls or fire-drill planning where incorrect advice could affect worker safety. <br>
Mitigation: Require review by qualified EHS personnel before applying controls, drill plans, or emergency procedures on site. <br>
Risk: Incident, personnel, equipment, and training records may contain sensitive operational or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Store generated records only in approved systems with appropriate access controls. <br>
