## Description:

Helps sales teams find, preview, export, and enrich U.S. small-business leads and manage related outreach through SMB Sales Boost.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tomsonx232](https://clawhub.ai/user/tomsonx232)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sales teams and their agents use this skill to discover U.S. small-business prospects, preview and export leads, enrich website lists, and manage searches, outreach schedules, and CRM integrations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead searches, exports, and enrichments can spend credits; purchases and billing changes can charge money.

Mitigation: Preview leads first, cap credits on searches and exports, and approve charges and billing changes before confirmation.

Risk: Email schedules and CRM or webhook integrations can send business contact details to others.

Mitigation: Review recipients and destinations before enabling delivery, and protect exported files containing contact details.

Risk: Deleting settings can remove saved configurations.

Mitigation: Review the affected resources and obtain explicit approval before confirming deletions.
