## Description:

Helps users identify target customers, understand their motivations, and turn product insights into prioritized opportunities and an optional lean startup plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oracis](https://clawhub.ai/user/oracis)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, founders, and marketers use this skill to explore customer segments and emotional motivations, prioritize product opportunities, and plan a lean launch for a small business.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hypothetical customer insights or business recommendations could be mistaken for validated market evidence.

Mitigation: Test proposed opportunities with real customers and business data before committing significant resources.

Risk: Chinese-first output may not meet an English-speaking audience's needs.

Mitigation: Specify the preferred output language when invoking the skill.
