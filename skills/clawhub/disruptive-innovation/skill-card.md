## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a disruption assessment to decide whether a market entrant has a structural low-end or new-market disruption path, or whether the situation is better labeled as sustaining innovation or a price war. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, investors, product teams, and incumbent strategy teams use this skill to evaluate whether an entrant can avoid head-to-head competition through a defensible disruption path. It helps structure evidence about overserved customers, ignored segments, incumbent rational neglect, and upward migration. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may mention current companies, market behavior, and external sources that can become stale or incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive business facts and market evidence before relying on the assessment's conclusions. <br>
Risk: Users may over-label an entrant as disruptive without enough evidence of overshooting, ignored segments, rational incumbent neglect, and upward migration. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's stop-rule and verification checklist, and label the case as partial or not disruptive when the evidence does not satisfy the required conditions. <br>
