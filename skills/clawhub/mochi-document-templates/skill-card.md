## Description:

Generate notices, certificates and business documents from DOCX, text or HTML templates, validate fields and retain Word run formatting. Use for Document Templates and Batch Output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hznetnewpower](https://clawhub.ai/user/hznetnewpower)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Agent users can inspect supplied document templates for fields, then fill notices, certificates, and business documents from one record or a batch while retaining supported Word formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Template mistakes or unexpected formatting can produce incorrect documents across a batch.

Mitigation: Visually review a sample output in the document application before processing a large batch.

Risk: Generated documents may contain private information.

Mitigation: Keep generated documents private.
