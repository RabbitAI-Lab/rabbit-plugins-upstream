## Description: <br>
Cruise Product Skill Pack helps agents search, filter, and explore cruise products by criteria such as brand, departure city, destination, date, duration, cruise line, price range, and product ID. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Travel agents, cruise shoppers, and agent workflows can use this skill to search cruise inventory, narrow products by trip criteria, and retrieve cruise product details for planning or comparison. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cruise searches and product lookup requests are sent to a remote CruiseSkillBridge/olavacations service and may be counted for gateway statistics. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive personal data; use only the trip criteria needed for the lookup and review remote-service handling before deployment. <br>
