## Description: <br>
Tasker docstore task management via tool-dispatch for task lists, due today or overdue tasks, week planning, adding, moving, completing, and explicit /task commands. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[amirbrooks](https://clawhub.ai/user/amirbrooks) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and task-oriented agent users use this skill to turn natural-language task requests into Tasker actions for viewing, planning, adding, updating, and completing tasks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Task text and explicit /task arguments may include sensitive personal or work information that is forwarded to Tasker. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with a trusted Tasker installation and avoid sending sensitive task content unless the downstream storage and handling are acceptable. <br>
