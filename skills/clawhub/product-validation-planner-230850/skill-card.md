## Description: <br>
Helps founders and builders turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning through repeatable workflows, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps produce validation plans, lightweight prototypes, positioning, reusable checklists, analysis, code changes, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate broadly around product ideas, validation, prototypes, SaaS, or startups. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation with $product-validation-planner when tighter routing is needed. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs can be incomplete or misleading if the user's market, constraints, or success criteria are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions and verify the resulting plan against the stated success criteria before acting on it. <br>
