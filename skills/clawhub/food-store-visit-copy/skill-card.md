## Description:

Creates structured restaurant-visit video scripts with hooks, shot lists, voiceover copy, captions, and promotional-disclosure reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Restaurant operators and local creators use the skill to plan short-form restaurant videos from a venue, signature dishes, price range, platform, duration, and filming setup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Promotional claims about dishes or prices may be inaccurate or exaggerated.

Mitigation: Check claims and current prices against the venue before publishing.

Risk: Paid or cooperative visits may be presented without disclosure.

Mitigation: Label sponsored or cooperative visits as required by the relevant platform.

Risk: Unnecessary personal information may be included in prompts or scripts.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary details and remove identifying information before sharing.
