## Description:

Creates restaurant visit video scripts with hooks, filming directions, voiceover, captions, and disclosure reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Restaurant owners and local food marketers use this skill to draft short-form visit videos tailored to a venue, featured dishes, price range, platform, runtime, and filming setup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Promotional scripts may contain unverified pricing, advertising disclosures, or health and legal claims.

Mitigation: Verify prices and claims and confirm required advertising disclosures before publishing.

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Check that the request concerns a restaurant visit video before using its suggestions.
