## Description: <br>
Guides quality engineers and MRB leaders through nonconforming-product disposition, including rework, repair, concession, downgrade, scrap decisions, approval paths, disposition forms, and MRB records. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality engineers, MRB leaders, and operations teams use this skill to classify nonconformance issues, select an appropriate disposition route, document approvals, and produce practical nonconformance disposition and MRB records. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Disposition guidance may be mistaken for final engineering, regulatory, customer, or safety authority. <br>
Mitigation: Require qualified engineering, quality, regulatory, and customer stakeholders to confirm scrap, recall, concession, and shipped-product decisions before use. <br>
Risk: Incomplete product, defect, batch, or approval information may lead to weak traceability in the generated records. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated forms for missing fields, replace placeholders with enterprise and customer-specific requirements, and retain approval evidence with the disposition record. <br>
