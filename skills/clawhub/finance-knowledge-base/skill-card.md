## Description:

Manages financial knowledge bases with document organization, knowledge graph construction, semantic search, and intelligent Q&A for internal financial institutions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, developers, and knowledge-management teams at financial institutions use this skill to organize internal policy, product, process, metric, and governance knowledge for searchable, reviewable use. It supports document taxonomy, knowledge graph planning, semantic search/RAG evaluation, lifecycle controls, and answer-quality review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial knowledge-base outputs may be mistaken for current financial, legal, insurance, or compliance advice.

Mitigation: Verify current regulatory requirements with official sources and qualified professionals, and require human review before production use.

Risk: Customer identity or account data could be exposed if added to a general knowledge base.

Mitigation: Keep customer identity and account data out of general knowledge bases; apply data minimization, de-identification, and role-based access controls.

Risk: Stale, unsourced, or superseded internal policies may produce misleading answers.

Mitigation: Require source and version citations, lifecycle review, stale-content marking, and explicit refusal when the knowledge base lacks support.
