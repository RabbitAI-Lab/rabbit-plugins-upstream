## Description:

Connects an agent to Inflyu to create AI influencer images and videos and edit media in the user's account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[surohak](https://clawhub.ai/user/surohak)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Inflyu users can direct their agents to create persona content, studio images and videos, and edit media in their Inflyu accounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generating or editing media can spend credits in the user's Inflyu account.

Mitigation: Show the quoted credit cost and obtain explicit user approval before each credit-spending action.

Risk: Face swaps or influencer cloning could misrepresent a real person.

Mitigation: Do not use cloning, person swaps, or face swaps to impersonate someone or mislead viewers.
