## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a three-principles photography composition framework for evaluating photo quality, checking a shot before capture, and suggesting improvements around subject clarity, attention guidance, and simplicity. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, photographers, and photography learners use this skill to evaluate whether a photo has a clear subject, guides attention to that subject, and keeps the frame simple. It also supports pre-shoot composition self-checks and practical improvement suggestions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may overapply the three-principles framework as a universal standard for every photography style. <br>
Mitigation: Present it as an educational composition framework and note that documentary, conceptual, mobile, and social-media photography may use different evaluation criteria. <br>
