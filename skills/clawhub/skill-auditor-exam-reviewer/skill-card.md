## Description: <br>
Chinese-language study skill for registered auditor subjective exam preparation, including past-question browsing, mock practice, answer review, explanations, knowledge lookup, and study planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Learners preparing for Chinese registered auditor subjective exams use this skill to browse past questions, run mock practice, compare answers, review explanations, look up knowledge points, and plan study sessions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reads local study reference files and may return question and answer content in chat. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants exam-preparation content from the available study files and only share content appropriate for the user's learning task. <br>
Risk: Mock-practice value can be reduced if reference answers are shown before the learner responds. <br>
Mitigation: Keep questions and answers separated during practice, and show answers only after the learner requests them or submits an attempted answer. <br>
