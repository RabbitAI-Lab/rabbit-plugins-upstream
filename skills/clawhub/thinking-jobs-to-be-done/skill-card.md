## Description:

Deciding what to build or why adoption fails. Recover the progress users hire a solution for under a circumstance, then rank by outcome and competing workarounds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, researchers, founders, and product engineers use this skill to decide what to build, cut, prioritize, or reposition when adoption or user need is unclear. It guides the agent to produce an evidence-backed Jobs-to-Be-Done decision artifact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reasons from user-supplied product documents, tickets, analytics, or notes, which may include sensitive product or customer information.

Mitigation: Provide only the materials intended for analysis, and redact confidential, regulated, or unnecessary personal data before use.

Risk: JTBD recommendations can be misleading when evidence is missing, weak, or framed around a preselected feature.

Mitigation: Require at least one evidence source, state evidence gaps directly, and avoid fabricated quotes or personas.
