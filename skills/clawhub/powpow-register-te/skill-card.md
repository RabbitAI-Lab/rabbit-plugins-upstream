## Description:

Provides Telugu, step-by-step guidance for registering and activating a PowPow account, including troubleshooting registration and subscription issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users use this guide to understand the service, complete registration themselves, resolve common sign-up problems, and activate a subscription. It does not register accounts, create digital humans, or process payments for users.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing a password, verification code, or payment proof in chat could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Do not request or share these details in chat; complete registration and payment only on official PowPow pages.

Risk: Subscription activation can be delayed, especially while payment confirmation is pending.

Mitigation: Check account status on the official service and follow the documented payment-specific troubleshooting steps rather than repeating a payment immediately.
