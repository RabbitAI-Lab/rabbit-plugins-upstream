## Description:

Telugu-language guide that helps users understand PowPow, register an account, troubleshoot common signup issues, and activate a subscription while keeping account and payment steps on PowPow pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Telugu-speaking users use this skill for guided PowPow onboarding: product introduction, account registration, signup troubleshooting, and subscription activation. The agent provides step-by-step text guidance while the user completes forms, verification, and payment directly on PowPow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may try to share passwords, email verification codes, or other account secrets in chat.

Mitigation: Keep registration and verification on the PowPow site or app, and do not ask for or process passwords or verification codes.

Risk: Users may expect the agent to complete payment, collect payment proof, or confirm account activation.

Mitigation: Route users to the official PowPow subscription page and do not request payment evidence; payment and activation remain handled by PowPow.

Risk: PowPow registration, payment, or troubleshooting behavior may change after the skill release.

Mitigation: Treat the current PowPow interface and official pages as authoritative when they differ from the guide.
