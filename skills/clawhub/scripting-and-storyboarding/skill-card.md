## Description: <br>
Turns a video idea into a shootable, editable pre-production plan using the SCENE framework: a two-column AV script, storyboard or shot table, numbered shot list, batch-shoot plan, timing pass, and edit handoff. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, social media teams, and video production collaborators use this skill to turn an idea or script spine into concrete production artifacts before shooting, editing, or generating clips. It is most useful when a team needs planned visuals, batch-shoot organization, runtime discipline, and clear human approval boundaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated production plans may include claims, consent assumptions, likeness use, or AI-disclosure choices that are unsuitable for the final publication. <br>
Mitigation: Review all plans before shooting, generation, editing, or publishing; confirm consent, likeness permissions, disclosure needs, and factual claims with the responsible human approver. <br>
Risk: Storyboards or scripts could drift into deceptive staged-as-candid content, plagiarism, or fabricated production claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use disclosed dramatizations or original scripts, study only structure from other creators, and keep final approval with a human who can verify what was actually filmed or generated. <br>
Risk: The skill is a planning aid and cannot evaluate actual footage, camera performance, edit quality, or platform analytics. <br>
Mitigation: Treat its output as a pre-production handoff; humans must shoot or generate the media, judge takes and clips, edit the result, and rely on real platform analytics after publication. <br>
