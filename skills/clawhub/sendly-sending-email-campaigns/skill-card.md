## Description:

Helps select an audience, draft and test a Sendly email campaign, and send it only after explicit confirmation of the recipient count.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and other Sendly users can prepare campaigns for existing contacts, review audience size and copy, send a test, and approve the final send.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad announcement requests may trigger an email campaign workflow or result in messages to real contacts.

Mitigation: Confirm the user intends a Sendly campaign; review the audience size, subject, and from address, and send only after explicit approval of the stated recipient count.
