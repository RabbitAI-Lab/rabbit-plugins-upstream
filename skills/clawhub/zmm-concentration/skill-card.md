## Description:

Helps owner-operators assess customer, channel, and product revenue concentration, estimate runway if the largest customer leaves, and produce risk and prohibited-action lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External owner-operators and business leaders use this skill to analyze recent revenue by customer, source channel, and product, then decide where concentration risk needs monitoring, planning, or immediate action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive revenue concentration details and business-risk observations may be saved for future reviews without clear retention or opt-out controls.

Mitigation: Use anonymized customer labels, avoid real customer names unless necessary, and confirm memory settings or deletion controls before using confidential business data.
