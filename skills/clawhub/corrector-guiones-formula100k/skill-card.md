## Description:

Diagnoses and corrects Spanish video scripts using the FÓRMULA 100K methodology, identifying TOFU/MOFU/BOFU purpose, value pillar, structure, hooks, virality triggers, Viral Scale score, predicted neuro-read, and corrected script versions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, editors, and content teams use this skill to audit Spanish short-form video scripts, diagnose structural weaknesses, score virality factors, and produce corrected versions that preserve the original intent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may read user-provided scripts, transcripts, profile details, or document paths that contain private or unrelated material.

Mitigation: Provide only the files and excerpts intended for script review, and remove unrelated personal, business, or credential-like content before use.

Risk: The script diagnosis and Viral Scale scoring are heuristic and may be misleading if treated as a guaranteed prediction of performance.

Mitigation: Use the output as editorial guidance, then validate final scripts against audience context, creator judgment, and observed performance data.
