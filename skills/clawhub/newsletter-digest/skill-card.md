## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to summarize forwarded newsletters and linked articles, extract key insights and topics, compare viewpoints, and manage reading lists and content preferences. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Newsletter, article, and inbox content can contain confidential, proprietary, or sensitive personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Do not forward confidential emails, proprietary newsletters, sensitive personal content, or links you are not authorized to share. <br>
Risk: Reading-list and preference signals may reveal sensitive interests or priorities. <br>
Mitigation: Review preference updates and avoid storing or sharing sensitive preference details through the skill. <br>
