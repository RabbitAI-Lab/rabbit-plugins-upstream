## Description:

Creates looping animated GIFs from a prompt or still image with GPT Image 2.6, with optional conversion to animated WebP or MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to make short looping animations of characters, logos, objects, or prompt-based scenes. It guides image sizing, loop prompts, and optional conversion of GIFs to web-friendly formats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
