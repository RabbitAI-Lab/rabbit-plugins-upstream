## Description:

Creates looping GIFs from prompts or still images with GPT Image 2.6 and explains how to convert them to animated WebP or MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to turn a prompt or still image into a looping GIF for mascots, logos, or reaction images, and to convert the result for web or video use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and source images are sent to an external image service.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive images or prompts unless the service is approved for that content.

Risk: The conversion script and installation instructions may depend on tools or versions that have not been fully pinned or inspected.

Mitigation: Review the conversion script and pin or verify required tools before running it in a sensitive environment.
