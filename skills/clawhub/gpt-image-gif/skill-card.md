## Description:

Helps create looping GPT Image 2.6 GIFs from prompts or still images, choose frame sizes and loop prompts, and convert results to WebP or MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to turn a prompt or approved still image into a looping GIF for mascots, logos, reactions, or other short animations. It also guides them in choosing frame settings and exporting WebP or MP4 versions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images or prompts may be sent to external services.
