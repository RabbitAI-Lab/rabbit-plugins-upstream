## Description:

Comprehensive Chinese-law assistant for contract review, consumer rights, labor disputes, family and property matters, debt issues, online rights protection, traffic incidents, and plain-language legal research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to understand common Chinese legal scenarios, review contract clauses, plan rights-protection steps, collect evidence, and prepare for arbitration or litigation discussions. It is informational only and does not replace advice from a licensed lawyer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The persistent learning utility can store sensitive legal-use details and user preferences across sessions.

Mitigation: Disable or rewrite the learning utility before use with confidential legal facts, or add explicit consent, retention, deletion, and storage controls.

Risk: The artifact encourages self-modification of the skill after repeated errors or use patterns.

Mitigation: Require human review and change control before any skill file is modified or redeployed.

Risk: Users may treat generated legal guidance as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep the disclaimer visible and direct users to licensed lawyers or appropriate authorities for fact-specific, criminal, or high-stakes matters.
