## Description:

Compare a connected site with its real search competitors, identify keyword, SERP feature, and backlink gaps, and turn them into ranked opportunities using Google Search Console data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Site owners, marketers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to compare their site's Google search performance with actual competitors and prioritize credible content or link opportunities. Search Console must be connected for the analysis.
