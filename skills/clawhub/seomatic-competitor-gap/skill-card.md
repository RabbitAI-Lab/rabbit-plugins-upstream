## Description:

Compares a connected site's search visibility with competitors to identify keyword, content, SERP feature, and backlink gaps and prioritize SEO opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO teams use this skill to find competitors ranking for their target searches, explain content and authority gaps, and prioritize improvements based on their connected Search Console data. It can stage proposed SEO tasks for explicit approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic exposes Search Console and site data to the service.

Mitigation: Connect only a site whose data you are authorized to share and review the access granted.

Risk: Staged SEO task cards may incur costs or lead to site changes if approved.

Mitigation: Review each task and its cost label; approve only the specific change you want SEOmatic to execute.
