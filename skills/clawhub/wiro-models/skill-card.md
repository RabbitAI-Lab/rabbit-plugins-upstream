## Description:

Search the Wiro AI catalog of 1,000+ models, compare them and read their inputs and prices, for free. Use when the user asks which Wiro AI model fits a job, what Wiro AI models cost, or how two models compare.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
