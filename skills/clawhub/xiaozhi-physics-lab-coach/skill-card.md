## Description:

A Chinese-language physics lab coach that guides middle-school and first-year high-school students through experiment design, data analysis, error analysis, and evaluation using questions rather than ready-made answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students use this skill to reason through physics experiments, interpret tables and graphs, assess errors, and prepare for laboratory assessments. Teachers and guardians may support supervised learning with it; it does not replace hands-on practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (tailored to the Mainland China physics curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, and reminders may expose a minor's learning information when enabled.

Mitigation: Review platform privacy settings and obtain consent before enabling these features; use the skill's view, correction, deletion, sharing, and reminder controls.

Risk: Unsupervised physical experiments involving mains electricity or open flames can harm students.

Mitigation: Avoid at-home procedures for hazardous experiments; reserve practical work for teacher-supervised laboratories or use demonstrations.
