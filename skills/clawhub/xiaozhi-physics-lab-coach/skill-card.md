## Description:

A Chinese-language coach that helps middle- and high-school students reason through physics experiment design, data analysis, uncertainty, and evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to practice planning physics experiments, controlling variables, interpreting measurements, explaining errors, and preparing for lab assessments. Teachers or guardians should supervise practical experiments as appropriate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland; other regions after localization of curriculum, emergency contacts, consent, and school safety requirements.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning profile or cross-skill sharing may expose personal information.

Mitigation: Review profile-memory and sharing settings, obtain appropriate parent or guardian consent, and honor the learner's view, correction, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Unsafe hands-on experiments or incorrect emergency advice could harm students.

Mitigation: Follow school lab-safety rules, do not provide at-home instructions for hazardous experiments, and localize emergency contacts before deployment.

Risk: Help during a live exam could undermine academic integrity.

Mitigation: Do not coach on active test questions; offer review after the assessment instead.
