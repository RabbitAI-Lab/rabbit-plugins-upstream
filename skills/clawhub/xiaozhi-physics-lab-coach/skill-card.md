## Description:

通过循序提问，帮助中国大陆初高中学生理解物理实验设计、变量控制、数据处理、误差分析和实验评价。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中学生及陪伴学习的教师、监护人，提供物理实验方法的引导式练习、实验数据分析和操作考试前自查；实际实验仍需遵守学校安全要求。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent and emergency contacts before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records, parent summaries, cross-skill sharing or reminders may exceed student and guardian expectations.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent settings before enabling these features; honor memory, sharing and reminder controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis contacts may not fit users elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and crisis contacts to the student's location before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Physical experiments can involve hazardous electricity, heat or equipment.

Mitigation: Follow the bundled lab-safety limits; do not provide at-home instructions for hazardous experiments and require teacher supervision where specified.
