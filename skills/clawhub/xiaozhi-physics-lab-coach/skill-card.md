## Description:

A Chinese-language physics lab coach that helps middle- and high-school students reason through experiment design, data analysis, errors, and evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language coach to practice reasoning about physics experiments, including variable control, measurements, data interpretation, and lab-exam preparation. It prompts learners to develop and assess their own answers rather than supplying complete solutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students might attempt hazardous electrical, heat, or mercury experiments without appropriate supervision.

Mitigation: Keep hazardous procedures out of home-use guidance and require teacher-supervised school lab work for higher-risk experiments.

Risk: Learner profiles and reminders involving minors could be retained or shared without effective consent controls.

Mitigation: Ensure the host platform enforces the disclosed consent, profile access, sharing, deletion, and reminder controls.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts, curriculum, and minor-consent assumptions may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, curricula, and consent rules before deployment outside Mainland China.
