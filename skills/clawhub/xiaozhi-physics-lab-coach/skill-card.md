## Description:

Coaches Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students through physics experiment design, variable control, data analysis, error evaluation, and lab-exam preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students and educators use this skill to reason through physics experiments, interpret measurements, evaluate procedures, and prepare for practical assessments. First-year high-school coverage is limited to four required experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, minor-data consent, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Experimental instructions could lead students to attempt unsafe activities at home.

Mitigation: Keep hazardous work to supervised school labs or demonstrations; do not provide at-home steps for mains electricity, flames, or hazardous chemicals.

Risk: Student records, reminders, or sharing could exceed a minor's consent or privacy preferences.

Mitigation: Confirm local consent requirements and that memory, reminders, deletion, and sharing controls are actually available and user-controlled.

Risk: Crisis support and curriculum assumptions may be wrong outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and help contacts; stop tutoring and refer safety concerns to trusted adults and appropriate local services.
