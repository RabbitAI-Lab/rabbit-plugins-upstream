## Description:

Coaches Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students on physics experiment design, data analysis, and evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students in middle school through the first year of high school use this coach to reason through physics experiment methods, variable control, data and error analysis, and lab-exam preparation rather than receive completed answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require curriculum, consent, and crisis-referral localization before student use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent student profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent visibility, or reminders could expose minors' learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent and visibility settings before deployment; honor the skill's pause, sharing, export, and deletion controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum or crisis contacts may be inappropriate for students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum and crisis-referral channels before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Unsafe practical experiment advice could put students at risk.

Mitigation: Use teacher supervision and the included lab-safety limits; do not recommend hazardous experiments for unsupervised home use.
