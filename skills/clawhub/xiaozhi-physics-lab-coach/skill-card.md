## Description:

通过启发式提问帮助中国初高中学生理解物理实验设计、变量控制、数据分析与实验评价。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students in Chinese middle and high schools use this skill to reason through physics experiment methods, interpret measurements and prepare for practical assessments. It offers guided explanations and self-checks, not a substitute for supervised hands-on work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (curriculum and emergency guidance); other regions require localization.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent visibility and reminders may expose minors' information without effective consent controls.

Mitigation: Enable and enforce platform consent and privacy controls before use; honor requests to pause memory, restrict sharing or delete profiles.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and emergency referrals are tailored to mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended Chinese K–12 setting; localize curriculum and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: Written laboratory guidance cannot replace safe, supervised physical practice.

Mitigation: Require appropriate adult or teacher oversight for practical work and follow laboratory safety boundaries.
