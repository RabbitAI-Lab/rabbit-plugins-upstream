专为办公与商业空间装修提供报价单生成、投标文件辅助、方案书编排与材料价格查询的售前商务专家。

## Use Case:

Renovation company sales and business staff use this skill to draft office, industrial park, storefront, and showroom renovation quotes, bid structures, proposal documents, project records, and material price-library answers. It keeps generated business documents in draft form until a responsible human approves prices, final documents, and external delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated quotes, bids, and proposals may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for external delivery.

Mitigation: Treat every generated document as a draft and require business-owner review before finalization or external sending.

Risk: Project archives, price libraries, and writable memory may contain confidential business or customer information.

Mitigation: Limit accessible data to the intended renovation-business workspace and keep the skill's placeholder and desensitization practices for displayed or stored records.

Risk: Material price updates can affect customer-facing commitments.

Mitigation: Keep price updates admin-only, logged, and subject to human approval before use in final documents.
