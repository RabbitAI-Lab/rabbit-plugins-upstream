## Description:

A structured framework for turning nonfiction writing ideas or rough drafts into argument-led, evidence-grounded long-form articles.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[cgartlab](https://clawhub.ai/user/cgartlab)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

## Use Case:

External writers use Quill to shape ideas or existing drafts into structured, evidence-grounded nonfiction articles, with an explicit claim, audience, and style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may search past writing and save a profile of the author's values and preferences without prior approval.

Mitigation: Limit historical reading to articles and folders the user selects; make profiling and profile retention optional.

Risk: Conversation links or private configuration files could expose information, and draft preservation tests are unreliable.

Mitigation: Review links before sharing, keep private configuration outside the skill's scope, and back up drafts.
