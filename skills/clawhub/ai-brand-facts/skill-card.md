## Description:

Find what ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude and Google AI Overviews get wrong about your business, trace each claim to its source and fix it there. Use for "ChatGPT has our pricing wrong", "AI confuses us with another company", a brand fact sheet or an AEO / LLM SEO / GEO entity check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners and marketing or SEO teams use this skill to check how AI answer engines describe a business, trace inaccurate claims to their sources, and plan corrections based on confirmed facts.
