## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad prompt templates may be pasted into agents with more tools, credentials, or filesystem access than the task requires. <br>
Mitigation: Review the selected prompt before use and grant extra access only when the user explicitly chooses that access. <br>
Risk: Generated review, debugging, or architecture guidance can be incorrect or incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as recommendations and validate them against the codebase, tests, and project requirements before applying changes. <br>
