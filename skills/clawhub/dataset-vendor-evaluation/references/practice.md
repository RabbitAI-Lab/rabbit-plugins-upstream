# Practice: choose the next investment

Synthetic scenario. The customer needs current orthopedic contacts in two states. Your baseline is available.

One source offers 50,000 rows, a vendor-picked sample and unknown update dates. Another provides 200 checked records, including 30 verified new target clinicians and 40 useful new affiliations for existing clinicians. Its price is known, but the supplied license does not explain product redistribution.

Give a recommendation, the next evidence you need and how you would judge a pilot. Distinguish new entities, useful enrichment and financial return.
