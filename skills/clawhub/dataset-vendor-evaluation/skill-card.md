## Description:

Evaluate a data vendor or candidate dataset against a customer need, existing holdings, quality, access conditions and ongoing cost. Recommend acquire, pilot, defer or reject based on incremental usable value rather than advertised size.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Data and product teams use this skill to compare candidate datasets with an existing baseline and customer needs, then make an evidence-based acquire, pilot, defer, or reject recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dataset terms may not permit the intended product use, redistribution, or retention.

Mitigation: Review supplied terms and resolve use, redistribution, retention, and derived-data questions before relying on a source.

Risk: Vendor-selected samples or unresolved identities can overstate useful coverage.

Mitigation: Compare records with an available baseline, verify identities and dates, and use a targeted independent check when it could change the decision.

Risk: A recommendation could be mistaken for authorization to purchase, contact a vendor, or begin recurring collection.

Mitigation: Treat the result as decision support; obtain separate direction before purchasing, vendor outreach, or recurring collection.
