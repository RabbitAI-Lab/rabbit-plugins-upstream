## Description:

Evaluate a data vendor or candidate dataset against a customer need, existing holdings, quality, access conditions and ongoing cost. Recommend acquire, pilot, defer or reject based on incremental usable value rather than advertised size.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, data, and strategy teams use this skill to compare a vendor or dataset with a customer need and existing holdings. It produces an evidence-based recommendation to acquire, pilot, defer, or reject, with quality, access, cost, and uncertainty considered.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dataset use or redistribution terms may be unclear, creating a risk of unauthorized reuse.

Mitigation: Use only data authorized for evaluation, and resolve unclear licensing or redistribution terms before reuse.

Risk: A positive recommendation could be mistaken for authorization to purchase data, contact a vendor, or begin recurring collection.

Mitigation: Treat purchases, vendor contact, and recurring collection as actions requiring separate user direction.
