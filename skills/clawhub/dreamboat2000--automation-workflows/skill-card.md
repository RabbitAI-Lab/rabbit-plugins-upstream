## Use Case:

External users and solopreneurs use this skill to identify repetitive business tasks, design no-code workflows, choose automation tools, test automations, and maintain them over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Workflow guidance may be applied to customer, payment, public posting, CRM, or accounting processes where mistakes can expose data or trigger unwanted business actions.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege app permissions, test with non-production data, add approval steps before mass messaging or financial updates, and keep logs plus rollback procedures.
