## Description: <br>
Generates a complete platform architecture design from requirements, including C4 views, a six-layer architecture, module boundaries, technology comparisons, data flows, API design, and communication mechanisms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, architects, and product engineering teams use this skill to turn a requirements analysis into a structured platform architecture design report with diagrams, module definitions, API outlines, and technology tradeoff tables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated architecture recommendations may include incomplete or unsuitable choices for security, authentication, infrastructure, or technology selection. <br>
Mitigation: Review the resulting architecture document with qualified engineering and security stakeholders before implementation. <br>
