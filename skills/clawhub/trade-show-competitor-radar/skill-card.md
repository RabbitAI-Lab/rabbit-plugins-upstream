## Description: <br>
Structures on-site competitor booth observations into tagged, actionable intelligence notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[weilun88313](https://clawhub.ai/user/weilun88313) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Exhibitors and field teams use this skill during or soon after trade shows to turn raw competitor booth observations, brochure text, pricing clues, and overheard remarks into tagged competitive intelligence notes and internal action items. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Personal data may be recorded for people seen at competitor booths. <br>
Mitigation: Use only information the organization is allowed to collect and use, and avoid adding personal data unless there is a valid business and privacy basis. <br>
Risk: Unverified overheard or inferred booth observations could be treated as confirmed competitive facts. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve evidence tags such as OBS, INF, HEARD, EST, and UNK, keep thin evidence explicit, and verify consequential claims before using them in sales, product, or leadership decisions. <br>
