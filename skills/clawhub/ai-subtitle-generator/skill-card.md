## Description:

Converts local audio or video into timed SRT subtitles, TXT transcripts, or JSON segments, and can generate voiceover audio with aligned subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yanghhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/yanghhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External creators and developers can transcribe audio or video for subtitles and transcripts, or generate scripted voiceovers with matching subtitle timing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected media is sent to Miragea for processing; the tool scope is undeclared and the security review was partial.

Mitigation: Before use, review Miragea's privacy and retention terms, confirm the tool scope, and share only media you are authorized to submit.
