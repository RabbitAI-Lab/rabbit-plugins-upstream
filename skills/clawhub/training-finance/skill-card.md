## Description: <br>
Helps manage training students and financial settlement, including registration, payment confirmation, cost accounting, instructor revenue sharing, and profit reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Training operators and finance staff use this skill to maintain student rosters, track payment and attendance status, calculate costs and revenue sharing, and prepare period or annual finance summaries for review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process private student, contact, and payment information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only necessary fields and avoid sharing full contact or payment details in broad prompts or reports. <br>
Risk: Financial reports and settlement calculations may be incorrect if inputs, payment status, costs, or sharing rules are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Verify all financial calculations in the approved accounting process before acting on them. <br>
