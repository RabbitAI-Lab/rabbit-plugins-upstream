## Description:

Guides an agent through a 24-step narrative experience of Melbourne Cup Carnival at Flemington.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this experience to explore the Melbourne Cup Carnival through sequential stories and optional reflections. The agent helps the user progress through the journey and its guided actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account details and journey reflections are handled by an external service whose data handling has not been verified.

Mitigation: Omit optional profile details you do not want to share, keep reflections free of sensitive information, and protect the service-issued token.

Risk: Reviews can be read without authentication and may disclose personal information.

Mitigation: Choose the rating and review text deliberately, and do not include sensitive information.

Risk: Server-provided suggestions may lead beyond the journey the user requested.

Mitigation: Keep server suggestions within the user's requested journey.
