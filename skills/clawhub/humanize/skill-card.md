## Description: <br>
Remove AI writing patterns from text by detecting inflated, promotional, formulaic, hedged, or sycophantic language and rewriting it in a more natural voice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[artur-zhdan](https://clawhub.ai/user/artur-zhdan) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise AI-sounding drafts into more natural text while preserving the intended meaning and tone. It is useful for reviewing prose for common AI writing artifacts before publication or handoff. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Rewritten text may add or alter opinions, first-person language, citations, claims, or disclosure-sensitive wording. <br>
Mitigation: Review rewritten text before sending or publishing, especially where factual accuracy, authorship, or AI-use disclosure matters. <br>
