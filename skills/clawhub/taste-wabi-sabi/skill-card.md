## Description:

The formal vocabulary of wabi-sabi aesthetics -- asymmetry, rough texture, imperfection, and the beauty of transience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnnyzijianwu](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnnyzijianwu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, writers, and creative agents use this skill to recognize and apply wabi-sabi aesthetic qualities such as asymmetry, patina, restraint, handmade irregularity, and visible transience.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally biases an agent toward wabi-sabi aesthetics, which may be inappropriate for work that requires polished, symmetric, pristine, or highly ornamented design.

Mitigation: Install or activate it only when that design influence is desired, and remove it from workflows with conflicting style requirements.
