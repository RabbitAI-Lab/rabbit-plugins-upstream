$$\frac{d\theta_i}{dt} = \omega_i + \frac{K}{N} \sum_{j=1}^N \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i), \quad i = 1, \dots, N$$

where:
- $\theta_i$ is the rhythmic phase of agent $i$ (dancer or circle member).
- $\omega_i$ is their intrinsic tempo ($140\text{--}150\,\text{BPM}$).
- $K$ is the session coupling strength (vocal volume, proximity, and visual feedback).

### 2.1 The Hype Order Parameter $R$
The collective macroscopic order parameter is defined as:
$$R(t) e^{i\psi(t)} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{j=1}^N e^{i\theta_j(t)}$$
- When $R \approx 0$: The session is cold, uncoordinated, and out-of-sync.
- As hype escalates through call-outs and synchronized stomps, $K$ exceeds the critical coupling threshold $K_c$.
- **Climax Phase Transition**: At peak session intensity, the system enters complete phase lock:
  $$R \ge 0.9999$$
