## Description:

Helps builders use a Three Ledgers diagnostic framework, self-inspection questions, category-swap checks, and a lasting-value matrix to avoid attention traps and focus work on durable value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External builders, creators, and product teams use this skill to diagnose whether public-building activity is optimizing the work itself, the story around the work, or commercial outcomes. It guides reflection on attention traps, signal calibration, and what durable value a project leaves behind.
