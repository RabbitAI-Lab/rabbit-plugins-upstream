## Description:

一人公司商业模式建模，对应书稿第四章。帮读者梳理自己的OPC商业模式画布，生成9要素分析报告。

## Use Case:

External users and solo business operators use this Chinese-language skill to collect skills, available time, income goals, resources, and work preferences, then turn them into a nine-part OPC business model canvas. The skill helps identify gaps in resources, channels, revenue, costs, partners, activities, and risks, and can produce a structured Markdown report.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Choose the export path deliberately and review the report for private information before saving or sharing it.
