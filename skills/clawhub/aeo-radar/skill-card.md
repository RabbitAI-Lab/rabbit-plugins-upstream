## Description:

Research how AI answer engines respond to customer questions, including brand mentions, cited sources, and AI search demand, through ClawLink's public-data tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and brand teams use this skill to assess how AI answer engines mention their brands, cite sources, and respond to relevant search prompts, then plan content for AI search.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill requires installing and pairing the ClawLink plugin to an account.

Mitigation: Confirm the plugin and account pairing are acceptable before installing or approving access.

Risk: Queries spend ClawLink credit, and larger batches or higher-priced calls can add up.

Mitigation: Check the live per-tool price and get approval before larger batches or higher-priced calls.

Risk: This public-data workflow is not intended for private account information.

Mitigation: Use public-market research prompts only; do not submit private account data.
