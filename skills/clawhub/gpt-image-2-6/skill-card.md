## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded photos may contain private or sensitive information and are processed by Varg and its providers.

Mitigation: Upload only images you are authorized to share, remove unnecessary sensitive content and metadata, and check retention and deletion terms before sending private photos.

Risk: Paid image generation and editing requests can incur usage charges.

Mitigation: Estimate each request before submitting it and set a spending cap.

Risk: Returned image links may be accessible to others.

Mitigation: Treat returned links as potentially public and avoid including sensitive material in generated or edited images.
