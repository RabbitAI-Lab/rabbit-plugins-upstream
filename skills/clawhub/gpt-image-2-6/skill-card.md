## Description:

Guides agents in choosing GPT Image 2.6 modes, settings, sizes, and prompts for text-heavy images through varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and designers use this guide to choose GPT Image 2.6 modes and settings, estimate image size and cost, and adapt prompts for posters, infographics, prints, character sheets, and storyboards. It also provides varg MCP and REST examples and troubleshooting guidance.
