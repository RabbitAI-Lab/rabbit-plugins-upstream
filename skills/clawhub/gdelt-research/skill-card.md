## Description:

Searches global news and US television coverage through Crawlora's GDELT API, returning structured JSON for stories, coverage trends, sentiment, and broadcasts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, journalists, and media analysts use this skill to investigate global news coverage and US television broadcasts, compare outlets, and track coverage volume or tone over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Crawlora API receives the API key and submitted research queries and filters.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key, keep it out of shared files and commands, and avoid confidential or sensitive queries unless sharing them with Crawlora is intended.
