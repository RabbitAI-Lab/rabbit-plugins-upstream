## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The scanner flags coercive persuasion, bribery-like, infiltration, and competitor-disruption material as unsafe for real business, finance, hiring, or negotiation use. <br>
Mitigation: Rewrite those sections as prohibited behavior or defensive awareness only before deployment, and require human review for outputs involving persuasion, hiring, finance, or negotiation. <br>
Risk: The scanner identifies unnecessary git mutation permissions and broad triggers. <br>
Mitigation: Remove git mutation permissions and narrow or remove broad triggers before installation. <br>
Risk: The skill may produce confident strategic or financial guidance from incomplete user-provided context. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as decision support only, require independent verification, and apply compliance, legal, or financial review where relevant. <br>
