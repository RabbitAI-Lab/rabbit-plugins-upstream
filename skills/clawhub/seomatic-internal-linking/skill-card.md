## Description:

Plans contextual internal links using a connected site's Search Console and page data, with proposed changes staged for user approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO teams use the skill to identify pages needing link support, select relevant source pages, and review proposed internal links and broken-link fixes before approving changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic gives the service access to the site's Google Search Console and page data.

Mitigation: Connect only sites whose data you are authorized to share and review the access granted before use.

Risk: Staged links or broken-link fixes may be unsuitable, and approving tasks can change live pages; paid plans may enable staging.

Mitigation: Review source and target relevance, anchor text, and quoted costs for each task before explicitly approving it.
