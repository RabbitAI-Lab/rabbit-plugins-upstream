## Description:

Plan internal links for the connected site from real data: find pages that rank on page 2 or are not indexed and need link support, find the strong pages that can pass it, and stage specific contextual links with anchors for approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to identify pages that could benefit from internal links and stronger pages that can link to them, using the connected site's Google Search Console data. It produces contextual link recommendations and can stage them in SEOmatic for the user's approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
