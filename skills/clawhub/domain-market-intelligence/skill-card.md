## Description: <br>
Produce a time-bounded domain-market news briefing with source tiers, event types, and explicit inference limits, covering notable sales, registration trends, and recent domain movements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, researchers, and domain-market operators use this skill to produce current, source-tiered domain-market briefings with sales classifications, registration trend figures, recent domain movements, and explicit limits on inference. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Domain-market briefings can be mistaken for investment advice or can overstate confidence in public sales and registration signals. <br>
Mitigation: Treat output as market intelligence only, preserve the required disclaimer, and review source quality before relying on conclusions. <br>
Risk: Fresh web searches and domain lookup providers may return incomplete, stale, unavailable, or disclosure-biased data. <br>
Mitigation: Report missing fields as Not Provided or Unavailable, separate source tier from verification status, and avoid inferring sales, ownership changes, launches, or causes from partial evidence. <br>
