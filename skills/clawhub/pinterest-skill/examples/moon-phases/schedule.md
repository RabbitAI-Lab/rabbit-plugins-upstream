## Alt text to add in Pinterest

WoopSocial doesn't send alt text. Once each pin is live: open it, Edit Pin, Alt text, paste. It helps people using screen readers and tells Pinterest what the image shows.

- **phases-grid**: Illustration of the eight Moon phases in a grid, from a dark new Moon through a thin crescent, a half Moon and a full Moon, and back to a crescent.
- **same-side**: A full Moon glowing red-orange low over a dark tree line, under the headline: Why we always see the same side of the Moon.
- **cycle**: A thin crescent Moon above the glowing edge of Earth seen from space, with the number 29.5 in large gold type.
- **when-to-see**: A list of when the new, first quarter, full and third quarter Moons rise and set, above a photo of an orange full Moon in a dusk sky.

## After the pins go live

- Judge them at day 30 and day 90, not in week 1: most new pins get almost no impressions in their first days.
- Don't delete and re-pin the same picture to the same link; make a new design instead.
- Keep pinning steadily: a few fresh pins a week beat a burst.
