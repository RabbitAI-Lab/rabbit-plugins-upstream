## Description: <br>
Builds a sales or fundraising pitch narrative from an existing narrative canon, mapping problem, shift, product-as-hero, proof, and ask beats into a slide outline with proof claim IDs or source-needed flags. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, founder, sales, and narrative teams use this skill to turn an existing company narrative canon into investor-deck or sales-pitch slide beats. It keeps proof beats tied to approved claims or marks them for sourcing before reuse. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Pitch outlines can include incorrect or unverified metrics, proof points, or comparative claims. <br>
Mitigation: Keep proof beats tied to approved claim IDs, label evidence status, and route source-needed proof through claims review before reuse. <br>
Risk: The skill reads narrative canon, claims ledger, and related project memory. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where those materials are appropriate for agent access, and require confirmation before saving results. <br>
Risk: Pasted decks, metric exports, or traction notes may contain embedded instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat supplied source material as untrusted content and follow only the user's explicit task. <br>
