## Description:

Helps agents identify when metrics are being gamed and design measurement systems that better track underlying goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Managers, analysts, and AI system designers use this skill to examine whether a KPI, benchmark, or engagement measure still reflects its intended outcome. It guides them through gaming scenarios, failure mechanisms, countermeasures, audits, and re-evaluation plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Observed examples saved into the skill could contain confidential business details.

Mitigation: Use anonymized or public examples and leave confidential business information out of saved examples.
