## Description:

Cleans up videos by removing watermarks, logos, or burned-in subtitles, upscaling to 2K or 4K, and adding or translating subtitles using AdsTurbo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and editors use this skill to clean up and localize existing footage through AdsTurbo without intentionally changing the scene content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected media and job details are sent to AdsTurbo; uploads may expose sensitive content.

Mitigation: Only submit media you are comfortable sharing after reviewing the service's storage and sharing practices; avoid confidential videos unless authorized.

Risk: Removing watermarks, logos, or subtitles can strip attribution or alter the intended presentation.

Mitigation: Remove these elements only from media you own or are authorized to modify.
