## Description:

Generates targeted manual search strategies by country, product or service, customer type, and platform, including Google, LinkedIn, local-language, directory, trade show, procurement, and recruiting-site search terms without performing bulk automated search or scraping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, sales operators, and market researchers use this skill to turn a clearly scoped target country, offering, customer profile, and search purpose into a small set of high-quality manual search terms and discovery paths. It is intended for planning and judging manual searches, not for automated collection or outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Translated or local-language search terms may be inaccurate or unsuitable for the target market.

Mitigation: Verify important translated terms with a qualified speaker or local market reviewer before using them.

Risk: Keyword lists and discovery paths could be misused for bulk scraping or automated outreach outside the skill's stated posture.

Mitigation: Use the skill for manual search planning only, follow platform terms, and keep searches targeted and reviewable.
