## Use Case:

Developers, educators, reviewers, and students use this skill to audit mathematical proofs or proof-like technical derivations against Korpi's 63 fake proof methods and identify concrete proof defects and repairs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides analytical feedback and should not be treated as a formal verification system.

Mitigation: Use it as a proof-review aid, and confirm important mathematical conclusions with accepted theorems, complete derivations, formal methods, or qualified reviewers.

Risk: The named taxonomy can encourage over-labeling ordinary proof gaps as fake proof methods.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's calibration guidance: do not force a Korpi label when an ordinary proof defect explanation is more accurate.
