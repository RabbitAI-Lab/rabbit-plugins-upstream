# Korpi's 63 Fake Methods Of Mathematical Proof

Source: Korpi's World, "63 Methods of Mathematical Proof", https://www.korpisworld.com/Mathematics/math%20leftovers/methods_of_mathematical_proof.htm . The source credits/adapts material from Dick A. Wood, Steve Phipps, and Dana Angluin/SIGACT News.

Use this as the canonical numbered taxonomy for `fake-math-proof-audit`.

## 1-10

1. **Proof by Obviousness** - The proof is claimed too clear to state. Detect missing reasoning hidden behind "obvious," "clear," or "trivial."
2. **Proof by General Agreement** - The audience's agreement is treated as proof. Detect consensus used instead of derivation.
3. **Proof by Imagination** - The writer asks the reader to pretend the statement is true. Detect assumed conclusions.
4. **Proof by Convenience** - The statement is treated as true because it would be useful or pleasant. Detect usefulness substituted for proof.
5. **Proof by Necessity** - The statement is asserted because otherwise a larger structure would fail. Detect consequences used as proof.
6. **Proof by Plausibility** - The result sounds reasonable, so it is accepted. Detect plausibility without derivation.
7. **Proof by Intimidation** - The reader is pressured not to object. Detect dismissive language replacing steps.
8. **Proof by Lack of Sufficient Time** - The proof is assigned to the reader because time is short. Detect skipped central proof.
9. **Proof by Postponement** - The proof is deferred to appendix/later material. Detect unavailable or never-provided support.
10. **Proof by Accident** - A chance observation is mistaken for proof. Detect accidental pattern-finding without justification.

## 11-20

11. **Proof by Insignificance** - The need for proof is dismissed as unimportant. Detect "who cares" treatment of necessary steps.
12. **Proof by Mumbo-Jumbo** - Formal symbols are used as fog. Detect notation that does not establish the claim.
13. **Proof by Profanity** - Aggressive language stands in for proof. Detect emotional force as replacement for inference.
14. **Proof by Definition** - The conclusion is made true by definition. Detect circular or ad hoc definitions.
15. **Proof by Tautology** - The claim is restated as its own reason. Detect "P because P."
16. **Proof by Plagiarism** - A result is copied or cited as if proved. Detect unattributed or unexplained borrowed proof.
17. **Proof by Lost Reference** - The author vaguely remembers a source. Detect unverifiable memory citations.
18. **Proof by Calculus** - A technique name is invoked to skip proof. Detect "requires calculus/advanced math" without showing it.
19. **Proof by Terror** - Stronger intimidation. Detect threats, humiliation, or status pressure.
20. **Proof by Lack of Interest** - The audience's boredom is used to avoid proof. Detect "nobody wants details" evasions.

## 21-30

21. **Proof by Illegibility** - The proof cannot be read or parsed. Detect unreadable notation, bad scans, or incoherent symbols.
22. **Proof by Logic** - The statement is assumed true because it appears in a problem/spec/context. Detect institutional placement as proof.
23. **Proof by Majority Rule** - A fallback form of general agreement. Detect voting/counting opinions as proof.
24. **Proof by Clever Variable Choice** - Variables are chosen to make the proof work. Detect circular choice of constants/functions/parameters.
25. **Proof by Tessellation** - The proof is asserted to be the same as a previous one. Detect analogy to a prior proof without checking changed conditions.
26. **Proof by Divine Word** - A divine or absolute declaration replaces proof. Detect sacred/absolute authority as proof.
27. **Proof by Stubbornness** - Refusal to concede replaces reasoning. Detect repeated insistence after objection.
28. **Proof by Simplification** - The problem is reduced to something too simple by dropping essential content. Detect false simplification.
29. **Proof by Hasty Generalization** - One or few cases are used for all cases. Detect example-to-universal jumps.
30. **Proof by Deception** - The unsupported move is hidden by misdirection. Detect sleight of hand, buried assumptions, or shifted attention.

## 31-40

31. **Proof by Supplication** - The result is begged for. Detect hope or pleading as proof.
32. **Proof by Poor Analogy** - Similarity is treated as proof. Detect analogy without preserving the relevant structure.
33. **Proof by Avoidance** - Postponement taken indefinitely. Detect repeated failure to supply the proof.
34. **Proof by Design** - A new system is invented so the result becomes true. Detect changed axioms/definitions presented as proof of the original claim.
35. **Proof by Intuition** - Gut feeling is treated as proof. Detect intuition without argument.
36. **Proof by Authority** - A famous person says it is true. Detect expert name as substitute for proof.
37. **Proof by Vigorous Assertion** - Loud certainty replaces evidence. Detect emphatic formatting, repetition, or forceful tone.
38. **Proof by A.F.K.T. Theorem** - "Any fool knows that." Detect assumed common knowledge where a real theorem is needed.
39. **Proof by Divine Intervention** - A miracle supplies the missing step. Detect unexplained leaps labeled as magic, miracle, or black box.
40. **Proof by Example** - A special case is offered as representative of the general proof. Detect a worked example standing in for a theorem.

## 41-50

41. **Proof by Intimidation** - The word "trivial" is used as a weapon. Detect hidden hard steps called trivial.
42. **Proof by Vigorous Handwaving** - Gestures or verbal sweeping replace detail. Detect "clearly follows" with no mechanism.
43. **Proof by Cumbersome Notation** - Excess symbols conceal rather than clarify. Detect notation bloat with no proof work.
44. **Proof by Exhaustion** - Extreme length is used to imply rigor. Detect bulk pages/checks without a clear argument.
45. **Proof by Omission** - The reader is told to supply the details. Detect omitted details that are not routine.
46. **Proof by Obfuscation** - A long sequence of true or meaningless statements never proves the claim. Detect non-sequitur chains.
47. **Proof by Wishful Citation** - A cited result proves the wrong direction or a different/generalized claim. Detect citation mismatch.
48. **Proof by Funding** - Institutional funding is treated as proof. Detect grants, agencies, or sponsors as truth evidence.
49. **Proof by Eminent Authority** - A prestigious figure's casual opinion is used as proof. Detect celebrity expert gossip.
50. **Proof by Personal Communication** - Private communication is cited as proof. Detect unverifiable private-source dependence.

## 51-63

51. **Proof by Reduction to the Wrong Problem** - The proof reduces to a different or inappropriate problem. Detect reductions that do not preserve the original claim.
52. **Proof by Reference to Inaccessible Literature** - The proof relies on a source readers cannot inspect. Detect private, obscure, missing, or inaccessible references.
53. **Proof by Importance** - Useful consequences are treated as evidence for truth. Detect importance or elegance as proof.
54. **Proof by Accumulated Evidence** - Failure to find counterexamples is treated as proof. Detect finite search used as universal proof.
55. **Proof by Cosmology** - The negation is called unimaginable or meaningless. Detect metaphysical dismissal instead of proof.
56. **Proof by Mutual Reference** - References form a circle. Detect theorem A citing B citing C citing A.
57. **Proof by Metaproof** - A method for constructing a proof is presented instead of the proof. Detect algorithm/procedure claims without executing or proving them.
58. **Proof by Picture** - A diagram is treated as proof. Detect visual intuition without formal conditions.
59. **Proof by Vehement Assertion** - Authority-backed intensity replaces proof. Detect forceful assertion from someone with power over the audience.
60. **Proof by Ghost Reference** - The cited reference does not contain the theorem. Detect nonexistent support in a cited source.
61. **Proof by Forward Reference** - A future paper/work is cited as proof. Detect forthcoming or unpublished support.
62. **Proof by Semantic Shift** - Definitions change for the result. Detect quiet redefinition of standard terms.
63. **Proof by Appeal to Intuition** - Intuitive pictures or feelings replace proof. Detect cloud-shaped drawings, hand sketches, or informal intuition used as final evidence.

## Fast Matching Checklist

- Missing step hidden by tone: #1, #7, #13, #19, #37, #41, #42, #59.
- Missing step deferred: #8, #9, #20, #33, #45, #61.
- Bad definitions or shifted terms: #14, #15, #24, #34, #62.
- Bad examples or visuals: #10, #29, #40, #54, #58, #63.
- Bad citations or authority: #16, #17, #36, #47, #49, #50, #52, #56, #60.
- Wrong target or reduction: #25, #28, #32, #51.
- Technical fog: #12, #18, #21, #43, #44, #46, #57.
- Consequence, faith, or desire: #3, #4, #5, #6, #11, #26, #31, #35, #48, #53, #55.
