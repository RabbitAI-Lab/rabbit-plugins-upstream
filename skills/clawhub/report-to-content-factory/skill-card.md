## Description:

Turns industry-report evidence into original, cited WeChat articles, Xiaohongshu posts, and short-video scripts, with optional visual suggestions and revisions retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to adapt authorized industry research into audience-facing drafts with traceable claims, citations, and optional visual plans. Drafts and source evidence are retained in an approved JishuDB knowledge base; the skill does not publish content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research evidence, brand preferences, and draft revisions are retained in JishuDB.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended knowledge base and consent for source and output retention before writing; keep private materials out of public searches.

Risk: If JishuDB Desktop is not configured, setup may download software, launch an app, or change client configuration; the Windows installer path is disclosed as unsigned.

Mitigation: Review the setup approval scope and installer expectations, and authorize changes only on the intended machine.

Risk: Research summaries or inaccessible reports can lead to overstated citations and claims.

Mitigation: Check source coverage and original publishers, preserve dates and uncertainty, and cite only material actually inspected.
