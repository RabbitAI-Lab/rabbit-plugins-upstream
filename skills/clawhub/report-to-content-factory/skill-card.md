## Description:

Turns industry-report evidence into original WeChat articles, Xiaohongshu posts, and short-video scripts with citations and visual suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn authorized industry research into cited, original drafts for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and short-form video. It retains source evidence and draft revisions in JishuDB.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB is required, and setup may involve installing the Desktop app and connecting it to the agent.

Mitigation: Review the proposed release version, installation paths, digest, and permissions before approving installation and connection.

Risk: Content based on incomplete or restricted research may misstate findings or reuse material without permission.

Mitigation: Use authorized sources, preserve citations and access limitations, and review claims and usage rights before distribution.
