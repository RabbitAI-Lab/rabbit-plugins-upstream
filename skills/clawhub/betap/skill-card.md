## Description:

Find real local businesses and verified cultural places in Mexico, the US and Canada on BETAP (betap.world), propose new businesses to join, and publish promotions for a business whose owner gave you their key. Use when your human asks where to eat, shop or go in a Mexican town, wants their own business on a live map, or wants their promotions published.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to find local businesses or verified cultural places, propose a business for BETAP review, check proposal status, and manage promotions for a business whose owner provided an authorized key.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mexico, the United States, and Canada

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitting contact details or owner email may disclose information to BETAP.

Mitigation: Choose which contact details and owner email BETAP may receive before submitting a proposal.

Risk: A business key could be exposed or used beyond the owner's intended authorization.

Mitigation: Use a business key only for authorized promotions, protect stored keys, and revoke access when finished.

Risk: Publisher identity and live API behavior have not been independently verified.

Mitigation: Verify the publisher and confirm the live API behavior before relying on results.
