## Description:

Find real restaurants, cafes, bars, shops and cultural places in Mexican towns (menus, promotions, WhatsApp, live map) on BETAP (betap.world); propose new businesses and publish promotions with the owner's key. Use when your human asks where to eat, drink, shop or go in Mexico (Jalisco and beyond), wants their business on a live map, or wants their promotions published. No key needed to search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People in Mexico use this skill to find local businesses and cultural places. Agents can propose businesses, and business owners can publish promotions when they provide the relevant key and authorize the content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mexico

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business onboarding may share the owner's email with BETAP without the owner's prior permission.

Mitigation: Get approval for the exact operator contact and owner email before sending them, or let the owner enter personal details directly during registration.

Risk: BETAP's server-side privacy and permission claims have not been verified.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the requested action, and keep BETAP keys secret.

Risk: A promotion could be published without the owner's intended content or authorization.

Mitigation: Publish only promotions the owner requested and only with a business key the owner supplied.
