## Description:

Turns novels, scripts, episode outlines, or creative ideas into consistent AI comic-drama production plans covering character and scene bibles, storyboards, animated shots, dialogue, voice, subtitles, BGM, delivery checks, and AI-HIVE media-generation task records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, studios, and developers use this skill to convert story material into repeatable AI comic-drama and dynamic-comic production plans. It helps define character and scene consistency, shot structure, media-generation task tracking, audio/subtitle needs, platform versions, and quality/copyright checks for serial delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may call paid or bulk AI-HIVE media-generation tools.

Mitigation: Review model costs before execution and require explicit approval before submitting paid or bulk generation tasks.

Risk: Source novels, comics, real-person materials, voices, music, or generated media may exceed the user's rights to use them.

Mitigation: Confirm rights and platform requirements before production, and include copyright checks in the final delivery review.

Risk: Generated shots can drift from approved character, scene, or style references across a serial project.

Mitigation: Create a pilot episode and reusable character, scene, and action references before scaling, then check continuity and only regenerate failed shots locally.
