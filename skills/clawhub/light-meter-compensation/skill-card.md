## Description: <br>
Provides photography guidance for correcting light-meter exposure errors in snow, backlight, and high-contrast scenes using close metering, 18% gray cards, exposure compensation, and cumulative metering. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Photographers and photography learners use this skill to decide how to meter and compensate exposure when reflected-light meter readings are misleading, especially in snow, backlight, and high-contrast scenes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may produce exposure advice that does not match a user's specific camera, metering mode, sensor behavior, film stock, or lighting conditions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the recommendations as guidance, confirm with test frames or a histogram when available, and adjust for the actual camera and medium. <br>
Risk: The skill draws on a specific light-metering methodology that includes film-era assumptions for high-contrast exposure decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Clarify whether the user is shooting digital, negative film, or reversal film before applying compensation rules. <br>
