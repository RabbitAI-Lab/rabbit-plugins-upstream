## Description:

Find transferable technical solutions from other industries by reframing a concrete R&D problem as functions, effects, constraints, and contradictions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

R&D and engineering teams use this skill to reframe concrete technical problems into domain-neutral functions, search patents and technical evidence across industries, and identify transferable solution mechanisms. It supports functional patent search strategy, solution shortlisting, transferability assessment, validation planning, and bounded IP review questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential R&D details may be exposed through external patent or technical search services.

Mitigation: Use approved services only, minimize sensitive details, and prefer user-supplied evidence when sufficient.

Risk: Cross-industry matches can be plausible hypotheses without proven feasibility in the target system.

Mitigation: Require engineering analysis, experiments, measurable success criteria, and explicit confidence ratings before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Patent search outputs may be mistaken for patentability, freedom-to-operate, infringement, validity, or licensing conclusions.

Mitigation: Frame IP items as review questions and obtain qualified patent counsel for material decisions.

Risk: Direct copying of a patented implementation could create legal or design risk.

Mitigation: Transfer principles cautiously, assess claims and jurisdictions, and design distinct architectures where appropriate.
