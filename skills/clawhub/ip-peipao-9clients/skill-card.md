## Description:

Helps customer operators plan individual follow-ups, draft messages, and review progress through a tiered sales funnel for nine core clients.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer operators use this skill to prioritize follow-ups, draft one-to-one messages, review weekly sales-funnel progress, and update client records as circumstances change.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Full use asks users to send a generated installation ID to the publisher for registration.

Mitigation: Review the registration request before sharing the ID; do not include customer details in the message.

Risk: Customer follow-up notes are kept in local files without adequate privacy controls.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive personal, financial, or confidential client details unless you control storage access and retention.
