## Description: <br>
Aso Audit analyzes App Store and Google Play listings, scores ASO dimensions, and produces a prioritized optimization plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[troatpopcorn](https://clawhub.ai/user/troatpopcorn) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, app developers, and growth practitioners use this skill to audit public App Store or Google Play listings, compare competitors, and prioritize changes that improve search visibility and conversion. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recommendations for non-English or market-specific listings may be incomplete without target country and locale context. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the intended countries and locales, and treat scores as advisory inputs for human review before changing a live listing. <br>
