# 示例：从 Brief 到 Storefront Copy

## 输入 Brief

- source_slug：skill-publish-readiness
- audience：准备发布 ClawHub skill 或 plugin 的开发者
- trigger：正式发布前
- single_promise：用快速闸门或深度证据审查判断是否值得发布
- differentiation_pillars：auto 选模式；quick/deep 双模式；证据边界
- proof：真实发布失败案例与结构化报告模板
- must_not_claim：保证审核通过、保证安装增长、已完成正式发布

## Message spine

发布前按风险自动选择快检或深审，用可追溯证据决定现在能不能发。

## Direct

`Reviews ClawHub skills and plugins before release, from a fast launch checklist to deep evidence-based validation. Invoke before publishing or when asked whether a package is ready.`

## Distinctive

`Turns “it passed dry-run” into an evidence-based release decision with automatic quick or deep review for ClawHub skills and plugins.`

## Compact

`Checks whether a ClawHub skill or plugin is actually ready to publish, using quick or deep review.`

## 推荐

Direct 最适合 frontmatter：既说明能力，也包含调用时机。Distinctive 适合首屏。slug 保持 `skill-publish-readiness`。

## Claim check

所有主张均来自 brief；未使用“保证通过”或“已经发布”等禁用声明。
