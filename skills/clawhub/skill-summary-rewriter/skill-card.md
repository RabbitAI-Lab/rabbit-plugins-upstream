## Description:

Turns an approved Storefront Copy Brief into accurate storefront wording for skill titles, descriptions, and first-screen copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Skill publishers and developers use this skill to turn an approved positioning brief into evidence-backed storefront copy without changing the skill's underlying positioning or slug.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incomplete or unsupported brief claims could produce misleading storefront copy.

Mitigation: Review the supplied brief and supporting evidence; confirm material claims and return strategy-critical gaps for review before publishing.
