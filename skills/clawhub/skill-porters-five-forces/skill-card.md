## Description: <br>
Porters Five Forces guides users through an interactive industry-structure analysis across supplier power, buyer power, new entrant threats, substitute threats, and competitive rivalry, producing industry attractiveness and competitive-position judgments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and strategy practitioners use this skill to structure Porters Five Forces assessments for industry entry decisions, competitive strategy, and investment portfolio evaluation. The skill is especially oriented toward manufacturing, supply-chain, and quality-management scenarios while remaining applicable to other industries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on unsupported industry facts or generated strategic framing when making business, investment, or market-entry decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Base each force assessment on user-provided industry facts and review the final conclusions before acting on them. <br>
Risk: Generated local Markdown and HTML reports may include sensitive business context supplied during analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Review report contents before sharing and handle generated files according to the organization’s confidentiality requirements. <br>
