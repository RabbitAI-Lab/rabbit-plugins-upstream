## Description:

Helps investors assess a startup through stage-weighted research on its market, team, traction, economics, and defensibility, then presents an investment thesis, risks, and verdict.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors use this skill to research a startup, compare it with peers, and produce a stage-aware investment assessment with a scored thesis, key risks, and a verdict.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Glasser depends on paid external data providers and may incur charges during research.

Mitigation: Verify the separate Glasser dependency and review provider pricing and the user's spending limits before making calls.

Risk: Research may process public company and person data through external providers.

Mitigation: Review the data shared with providers and follow applicable privacy and organizational policies.

Risk: Incomplete public information and inferred metrics can make an investment verdict uncertain.

Mitigation: Label inferences, disclose missing data, and use the analysis as a starting point for independent diligence.
