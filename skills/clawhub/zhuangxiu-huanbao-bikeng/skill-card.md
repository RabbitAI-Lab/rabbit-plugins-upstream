## Description: <br>
装修环保避坑 helps agents answer home-renovation environmental questions about formaldehyde, material selection, pollution source control, ventilation, and safer alternatives using a built-in Chinese-language knowledge base. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Homeowners, renters, and renovation advisors use this skill to get practical Chinese-language guidance on reducing renovation pollution risk, choosing lower-emission materials, and avoiding common formaldehyde treatment or material-selection traps. The skill is especially oriented toward households with children, older adults, pregnancy planning, or floor-heating concerns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad renovation-material terms and provide strong advice outside a narrow formaldehyde or pollution-control question. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for renovation environmental guidance, confirm the household context before relying on recommendations, and keep answers scoped to the user's material, pollution, or ventilation question. <br>
Risk: The skill gives forceful, one-sided renovation guidance that could be over-relied on for significant health, safety, legal, or environmental decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as practical renovation guidance, not a substitute for qualified environmental, medical, or legal judgment when health or safety consequences are significant. <br>
