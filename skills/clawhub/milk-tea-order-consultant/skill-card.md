## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a Chinese-language milk tea ordering helper that asks preference questions, then recommends drinks across brands based on taste, temperature, health needs, budget, and mood. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Menu items and prices may be stale or vary by store. <br>
Mitigation: Check the shop app or in-store menu before ordering. <br>
Risk: Health, sugar, calorie, and budget preferences may be missed if the user gives incomplete answers. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's three-question preference flow and confirm constraints before acting on a recommendation. <br>
