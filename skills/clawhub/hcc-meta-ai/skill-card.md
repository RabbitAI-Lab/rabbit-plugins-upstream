## Description: <br>
AI-assisted meta-analysis workflow for hepatocellular carcinoma comparative effectiveness research using a six-element prompt framework from literature screening through data extraction. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[565073825](https://clawhub.ai/user/565073825) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External researchers and clinical evidence-review teams use this skill to structure HCC comparative-effectiveness meta-analysis tasks, including screening, eligibility assessment, NOS appraisal, and baseline or outcome data extraction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Extracted clinical research data or screening decisions may be incomplete, ambiguous, or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Manually verify all extracted study data, eligibility decisions, and NOS scores before using them in a manuscript or evidence synthesis. <br>
Risk: The workflow asks users to upload or share full-text manuscripts and PDFs with an AI tool or linked external platform. <br>
Mitigation: Review manuscript-sharing, copyright, confidentiality, and institutional requirements before uploading documents. <br>
