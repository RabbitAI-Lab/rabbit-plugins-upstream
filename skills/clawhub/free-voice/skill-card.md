## Description: <br>
Generate Russian male voice MP3 audio through a local ComfyUI Qwen3 TTS workflow and deliver it as a voice message. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[onimka](https://clawhub.ai/user/onimka) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and creators use this skill to automate Russian text-to-speech generation with a local Windows ComfyUI setup, then save the generated MP3 and send it as a voice message. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow depends on a trusted local Windows ComfyUI shortcut, plugin, Qwen3 model, localhost service, and output folder. <br>
Mitigation: Verify those local components are expected and trusted before installing or running the skill. <br>
Risk: User text is converted into an audio message, which may expose sensitive or unintended content. <br>
Mitigation: Only generate audio from text that is appropriate to send as a voice message, and review the result before sharing. <br>
