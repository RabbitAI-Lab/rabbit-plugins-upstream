## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sample conversion commands can create or overwrite local icon, WebP, and manifest outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Review commands, input filenames, output directories, and existing assets before running any local file-generation examples. <br>
Risk: Logo design guidance can miss trademark, brand, or production compliance issues. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for design workflow support and obtain appropriate brand, legal, or accessibility review before production release. <br>
