## Description:

Guides coding agents toward minimal, maintainable changes by prioritizing existing code, standard libraries, and necessary work over speculative abstractions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill for coding, refactoring, bug fixes, and reviews that favor the smallest sound change without omitting security, validation, or explicitly requested work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The minimal-change style remains active across coding responses and may limit broader design exploration or detailed implementation discussion.

Mitigation: Switch to normal mode or stop ponytail when broader exploration or more detailed discussion is needed.
