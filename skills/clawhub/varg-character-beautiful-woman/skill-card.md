## Description:

Guides VARG users in writing prompts for photorealistic, fashion-editorial images of fictional adult women.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use this skill to write detailed prompts for fictional adult fashion, glamour, and swimwear imagery on VARG. It provides character-description, styling, lighting, and model-selection guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation sends requests to VARG and may require an API key or connected service account.

Mitigation: Review VARG privacy and pricing terms before use, and avoid sharing sensitive information in prompts.

Risk: Prompt rewording advice for moderation blocks could be used to evade valid safeguards.

Mitigation: Keep prompts within the documented scope of fictional adults who are clothed and non-sexualized; do not use rewording to bypass a valid safety block.
