## Description:

Makes very beautiful AI women who look like real models, not plastic dolls, on varg: striking features written one by one, real skin, styling (hair, makeup, outfit) and light that make a photo look like a fashion, swimwear or red-carpet shoot, plus the wording that gets these prompts past false moderation blocks. Adults only, no nudity. Use when someone asks for a beautiful, gorgeous, attractive or hot woman, a model, an AI influencer girl, a glamour or swimwear shot, or a woman who looks real and stunning. Part of varg-character-design (definition, sheet, reuse in video).
