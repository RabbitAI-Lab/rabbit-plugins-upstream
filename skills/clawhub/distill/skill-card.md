## Description:

Guides agents in consolidating session lessons into persistent rules, memories, skills, and decision records while avoiding duplication and bloat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users invoke this at the end of a session to select useful lessons for their persistent knowledge base, or to leave low-value or duplicate lessons out.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent rules or memories may affect future sessions beyond the intended project.

Mitigation: Review the allocation table and confirm the scope of each proposed global rule or memory before accepting changes.
