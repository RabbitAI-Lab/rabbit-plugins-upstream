## Description:

Helps people trace unexplained household odors by ranking likely sources from smell, location, and timing, then providing confirmation checks, safety triage, and cleanup guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Residents, tenants, and property managers use this skill to investigate recurring household odors, check likely causes, and decide when to seek emergency or professional help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A dangerous gas, electrical, smoke, or sewage-backup odor may require immediate action rather than further investigation.

Mitigation: Screen for urgent patterns first; follow emergency guidance and contact the appropriate professional rather than relying on a diagnosis alone.

Risk: Episode notes are stored locally in the skill folder and may contain sensitive household details.

Mitigation: Avoid personal details in notes and remove the local log when no longer needed.
