## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking K–12 students record wrong answers, identify likely causes of mistakes, track recurring weak areas, and prepare targeted practice and review reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to review specific incorrect answers, identify recurring learning gaps, and generate practice sets or semester summaries. For younger students, the skill calls for adult supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term learning records and cross-skill or parent sharing may expose a minor's personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm consent and local requirements for record retention, sharing, parent visibility, and reminders before enabling them; honor view, correction, deletion, and pause requests.

Risk: Incomplete problem details or inaccurate generated questions may lead to incorrect learning advice.

Mitigation: Ask for the student's working, flag uncertain conclusions, confirm unreadable problem text, and check generated questions and answers before presenting them.

Risk: A student may disclose serious distress or immediate danger during a learning conversation.

Mitigation: Pause the learning workflow and direct the student to a trusted adult and appropriate local support rather than treating the disclosure as an ordinary wrong-answer entry.
