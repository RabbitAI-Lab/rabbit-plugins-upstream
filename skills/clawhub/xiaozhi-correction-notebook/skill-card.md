## Description:

Organizes students’ incorrect answers, identifies likely causes, tracks recurring weak areas, and guides review across school subjects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to record a specific wrong answer, understand its likely cause, review recurring weak areas, and practice with related questions. It supports learning across subjects from primary through high school, with an adult present for primary-school use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize crisis referral channels to the learner’s region.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security review confidence is medium because two references in SKILL.md did not resolve to bundled files, leaving evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Check the referenced guidance before relying on procedures that depend on it.

Risk: Wrong-answer records contain student learning information and may be shared across skills or with family members.

Mitigation: Honor the student’s pause, delete, export, and sharing controls; share records only when the required consent is present.
