## Description:

Helps students record incorrect answers, identify likely error causes, track recurring weak areas, and plan targeted review across school subjects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and their supervising educators or guardians use this skill to review specific wrong answers, categorize mistakes, monitor recurring weak areas, and request practice or study reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localization of curriculum assumptions, student-data consent rules, and crisis-referral information.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wrong-answer histories contain student data and may persist or be shared with related tutoring skills.

Mitigation: Obtain appropriate student-data consent, honor pause, deletion, and sharing controls, and share only the minimal records needed.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis-referral assumptions may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis-referral contacts before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Younger students or students in crisis may need support beyond a study assistant.

Mitigation: Ensure adult supervision for primary-school users and refer crisis signals to trusted adults and locally appropriate emergency support rather than continuing tutoring.
