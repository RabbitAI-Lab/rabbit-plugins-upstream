## Description:

Organizes students’ specific incorrect answers, identifies common error patterns, and tracks recurring weak areas across subjects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External students use the skill to review a specific wrong answer, identify its general cause, track recurring weak areas, and practice a similar question. Primary-school learners use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records can contain sensitive information about a student’s performance.

Mitigation: Make record retention, sharing, parent summaries, and reminders subject to the learner’s applicable consent controls; offer session-only use and deletion as described by the skill.

Risk: An unclear or misread question could lead to misleading error analysis.

Mitigation: Ask the learner to confirm unclear question text before analyzing it, and do not guess at unreadable content.

Risk: Primary-school learners may need adult support when using the skill.

Mitigation: Require a parent or teacher to be present for primary-school use.
