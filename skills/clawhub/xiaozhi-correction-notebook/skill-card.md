## Description:

帮助学生归档具体错题、定位通用错误原因并跟踪知识薄弱点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to review specific mistakes, classify likely causes, organize error records, and spot recurring weak areas. It can prepare practice questions, review collections, and term summaries, with adult supervision for elementary students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mistake records and learning or anxiety summaries may contain sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Review the host's storage and image-processing privacy practices, use the pause and delete controls, and disable cross-skill sharing or reminders when they are not wanted.

Risk: Incomplete problem-solving details can make an error classification uncertain.

Mitigation: Mark conclusions as insufficiently supported and keep them out of long-term records until the student confirms them.

Risk: Elementary students need adult support while using the tutoring workflow.

Mitigation: Have a parent or teacher present during use with elementary students.
