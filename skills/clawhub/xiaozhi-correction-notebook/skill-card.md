## Description:

帮助学生归档各科错题、分析错误原因、追踪薄弱知识点，并生成针对性复习建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and their guardians use this Chinese-language K12 skill to review specific wrong answers, identify recurring error patterns, and plan targeted practice across subjects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term records of a student's wrong answers may be unwanted or sensitive.

Mitigation: Confirm the student or guardian wants records kept; use pause, export, and delete controls when appropriate.

Risk: Cross-skill sharing, learning-profile updates, reminders, or parent visibility may exceed the student's preferences.

Mitigation: Check the relevant consent and sharing settings before enabling these features; use sharing and reminder controls to limit them.
