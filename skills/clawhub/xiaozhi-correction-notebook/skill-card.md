## Description:

Helps students record wrong answers across subjects, identify error patterns, and plan targeted review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Simplified Chinese K12 education use this skill to review specific mistakes, track recurring weaknesses, and create practice sets or term reports. Elementary students need a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localization of safety resources, curriculum assumptions, consent defaults, and language.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wrong-answer records and reminders can expose student learning data through storage or sharing.

Mitigation: Review student-data controls; confirm entries before saving and honor consent and pause or deletion requests before sharing or queuing reminders.

Risk: Mainland China emergency resources and education or consent defaults may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, curriculum, language, and consent rules before use outside the intended setting.
