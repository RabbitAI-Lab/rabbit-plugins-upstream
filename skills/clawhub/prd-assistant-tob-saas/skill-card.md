## Description: <br>
This skill helps product teams create, review, and improve PRDs for ToB SaaS products across new-product planning, new-feature development, and existing-feature iteration workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[woai36d](https://clawhub.ai/user/woai36d) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, product operations teams, and SaaS builders use this skill to draft PRDs, structure market and competitor analysis, review requirement quality, assess upstream and downstream impact, and plan releases for ToB SaaS products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read PRD documents, reference materials, and screenshot paths provided by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with materials relevant to the ToB SaaS PRD workflow and avoid providing unrelated sensitive documents. <br>
Risk: Generated business, compliance, and competitor-analysis recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific organization. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with product, legal, compliance, and engineering stakeholders before acting on them. <br>
