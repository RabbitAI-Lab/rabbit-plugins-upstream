### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The activation description is broad and may trigger more often than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Tighten the description and examples before deployment when precise activation is important. <br>
Risk: Skill creation guidance can produce incomplete or misleading skill instructions if user requirements are vague. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated skill content against concrete examples and scan the skill before publishing. <br>
