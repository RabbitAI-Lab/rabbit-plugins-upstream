## Description:

Post and schedule Instagram posts, Reels, and carousels through 1Social, with checks for Instagram media and caption rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to prepare and schedule content on a connected Instagram account, then review delivery status and manage scheduled posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An approved local media upload becomes publicly accessible, including when the post is later canceled.

Mitigation: Show the exact files and explain that upload creates a public link immediately; wait for the user's approval before uploading.

Risk: A published Instagram post may remain live after attempted removal through 1Social.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination account, media, caption, and timing before publishing, and explain that the user must remove published Instagram posts manually.

Risk: Publishing after the 1Social trial requires a paid plan.

Mitigation: Review the connected account's plan and remaining quota before publishing.
