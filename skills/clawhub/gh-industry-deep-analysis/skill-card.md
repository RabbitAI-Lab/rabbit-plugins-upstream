## Description: <br>
Provides a Chinese-language ten-dimension framework for deep industry and sector analysis, including market outlook, value-chain structure, cost structure, competitive landscape, core companies, policy context, and risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ghgh2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/ghgh2026) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and analysts use this skill to generate long-form industry research reports from an industry, sector, value-chain, or theme query. The report framework emphasizes current data, source/date attribution, cross-checking key figures, company comparisons, policy context, and investment risk notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated reports may be saved locally, added to history, published to Feishu/Wiki, and announced to a configured chat after analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Use only in the intended GH/OpenClaw and Feishu workflow, or require a local-only mode and explicit approval before upload, Wiki publication, history updates, or chat notification. <br>
Risk: Industry and investment analysis can be misleading when data is stale, single-sourced, or interpreted without domain review. <br>
Mitigation: Require current source/date attribution, cross-check important figures against independent sources, and have a qualified reviewer assess conclusions before using them for investment decisions. <br>
