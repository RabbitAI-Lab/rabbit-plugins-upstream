## Description: <br>
Structures collaboration by selecting an appropriate counterpart, running a bounded exchange on a plan or decision, and landing either a decision or a recorded disagreement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, reviewers, writers, and decision owners use this skill to structure critique, second opinions, audience checks, pairing, and deadlock handling before committing to consequential work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Default local decision logging can capture sensitive decisions or objections. <br>
Mitigation: For confidential work, set log_decisions to false, use the team's existing ADR or record convention, or avoid recording sensitive objections verbatim. <br>
