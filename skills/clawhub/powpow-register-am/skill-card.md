## Description:

Guides Amharic-speaking users through PowPow/Bubble registration, common sign-up problems, and subscription activation without handling accounts or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amharic-speaking PowPow users receive step-by-step guidance for registration, troubleshooting, and account activation. Users complete sign-up and any subscription payment themselves on the service's site or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat during sign-up or activation.

Mitigation: Complete registration and payments yourself on the official site or app; do not share these details with the assistant.

Risk: External product and screenshot links may be mistaken for places to provide account or payment details.

Mitigation: Use the official PowPow site or app for sign-up and payment; treat linked screenshots as illustrations only.
