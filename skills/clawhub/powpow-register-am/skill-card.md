## Description:

Guides Amharic-speaking users through PowPow (Bubble) account registration, common signup troubleshooting, and membership activation without collecting credentials or acting on the user's behalf.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand PowPow/Bubble, open the correct registration and subscription pages, complete their own signup steps, and troubleshoot registration or activation problems in Amharic.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may try to share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials while following registration or activation guidance.

Mitigation: Keep registration, verification, and payment on the official PowPow site or app, and do not ask the user to provide passwords, codes, or payment credentials to the agent.

Risk: The store listing and screenshots describe broader PowPow product features beyond the registration helper's core task.

Mitigation: Use product links and screenshots only as orientation, and keep the skill's assistance limited to registration, troubleshooting, and membership activation guidance.
