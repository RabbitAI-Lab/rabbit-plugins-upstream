## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The framework's time-sensitive examples or factual claims may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current claims against primary sources before relying on them for decisions.

Risk: Users may treat the analysis as investment, medical, legal, or other professional advice.

Mitigation: Use qualified professionals to verify conclusions in those areas.

Risk: The skill responds primarily in Chinese, which may not suit every user.

Mitigation: Confirm or specify the preferred response language.

Risk: Prompts may contain sensitive personal, health, financial, or business information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary information and redact or abstract sensitive details.
