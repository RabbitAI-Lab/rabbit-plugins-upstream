# Hydration Schedule Guide

## Why Timing Matters

Drinking all your water at once is ineffective — the kidneys can only process
about 0.8-1.0 liters per hour. Excess water is simply excreted. Spreading
intake throughout the day maintains steady hydration.

## Optimal Daily Schedule

### Morning Routine (Wake to Breakfast)
- **On waking**: 300-500ml (you wake up dehydrated after 7-8 hours)
- **With breakfast**: 200-300ml
- **Before leaving home**: 200ml

**Morning total**: ~700-1000ml (35-40% of daily target)

### Mid-Morning (9:00-12:00)
- **Every 1-1.5 hours**: 200-250ml
- **Meetings/desk work**: Keep a glass/bottle visible

**Mid-morning total**: ~500-750ml (25-30% of daily target)

### Lunch (12:00-13:00)
- **Before lunch**: 200ml (helps digestion, prevents overeating)
- **With lunch**: 200-300ml

**Lunch total**: ~400-500ml

### Afternoon (13:00-18:00)
- **Every 1-1.5 hours**: 200-250ml
- **Post-lunch energy dip**: Often dehydration, not food coma
- **Before exercise**: 300-500ml (30 min before)
- **During exercise**: 150-200ml every 15-20 min
- **After exercise**: 500ml (replace what was lost)

**Afternoon total**: ~600-1000ml

### Evening (18:00-22:00)
- **With dinner**: 200-300ml
- **Evening**: 200ml per hour
- **Limit after 21:00**: Reduce to sips (avoid night bathroom trips)

**Evening total**: ~400-600ml (20-25% of daily target)

## Customization Factors

### If You Exercise in the Morning
- Add 500ml before exercise (30 min prior)
- Add 250ml during exercise (every 20 min)
- Add 250ml after exercise
- Reduce afternoon/evening intake accordingly

### If You Work Night Shifts
- Shift the schedule by your wake time
- First drink: 30 min after waking
- Last significant drink: 1 hour before sleep
- Maintain 1.5-2 hour intervals

### If You're in a Hot Climate
- Reduce interval to every 45-60 min (instead of 90 min)
- Increase per-drink amount by 50-100ml
- Add electrolytes (a pinch of salt, or sports drink) for extended heat exposure

### If You Drink Coffee
- For each cup, drink an equal amount of water within 30 min
- Space coffee 1+ hour from meals (tannins can reduce iron absorption)
- Don't count coffee toward your hydration target

### If You Drink Alcohol
- Drink 1 glass of water for each alcoholic drink
- Drink 500ml before bed
- The next morning, start with 500ml (rehydration)

## Hydration Habit Building

### Week 1: Awareness
- Don't change anything — just log your current intake
- You'll likely discover you're drinking much less than you thought

### Week 2: Morning Routine
- Focus on the morning drink (400ml on waking)
- Place a glass/bottle by your bed the night before

### Week 3: Visibility
- Keep a water bottle on your desk at all times
- Use a marked bottle to track visually
- Set phone reminders for every 90 min

### Week 4: Consistency
- Aim for the full schedule
- Track your streak
- Celebrate 7-day milestones

### Long Term
- The schedule becomes automatic
- You develop thirst awareness
- You notice the benefits: energy, focus, skin, fewer headaches
