## 1. Core Premise

In the year 2387, humanity has spread across twelve star systems via the **Aethelgard Wormhole Network** — a stable, human-built wormhole system discovered by Dr. Aris Thorne. The network enables near-instantaneous travel between systems, making interstellar colonization and trade feasible for the first time.

But the wormholes are not natural. They are **artifacts** — remnants of a precursor civilization known as the **Lumin**, who vanished 10 million years ago. Each wormhole node is guarded by a **Sentinel**, a semi-sentient machine intelligence that tests travelers and occasionally... remembers.

The central mystery of the series: **Why did the Lumin build the wormholes? And why are they starting to wake up?**

## 2. The Aethelgard Wormhole Network

### 2.1 Structure

The Network consists of **12 primary nodes**, each located in a different star system. Nodes are connected by **corridors** — stable wormhole tunnels that take 0-3 seconds to traverse depending on distance.

| Node ID | System | Status | Sentinel Name |
|---------|--------|--------|---------------|
| A-01 | Sol (Earth) | Active | Mnemosyne |
| A-02 | Proxima Centauri | Active | Oannes |
| A-03 | Tau Ceti | Active | Erato |
| A-04 | Epsilon Eridani | Active | Thoth |
| A-05 | Trappist-1 | Unstable | The Weeper |
| A-06 | Kepler-442 | Active | Idris |
| A-07 | Gliese 667C | Active | Bragi |
| A-08 | HD 40307 | Sealed | The Silent One |
| A-09 | Ross 128 | Active | Calliope |
| A-10 | Teegarden's Star | Lost | ??? |
| A-11 | Wolf 359 | Active | Moros |
| A-12 | Alpha Centauri B | Active | The Archivist |

### 2.2 Rules of Travel

1. **Mass Limit**: Any vessel exceeding 50,000 tons must be scanned and approved by the Sentinel before entry.
2. **Intent Filter**: Sentinels can detect hostile intent. Vessels with weapons locked on the Sentinel will be repelled.
3. **Memory Echo**: Travelers report vivid dreams or flashbacks after passing through a Sentinel-gated corridor. Most dismiss these as neural fatigue.
4. **The Drift**: Prolonged exposure to wormhole corridors (more than 72 hours) causes "Drift" — a condition where travelers begin to hear voices from other nodes.

## 3. Factions

### 3.1 The Aethelgard Coalition

- **Type**: Interstellar government
- **Headquarters**: Sol System (Earth)
- **Ideology**: Unified governance, scientific progress, preservation of Lumin heritage
- **Military**: Coalition Defense Fleet (CDF) — 420 warships
- **Key Figure**: Chancellor Elena Voss (human, age 67)
- **Internal Conflict**: Growing tension between Earth-first purists and colonial autonomy advocates

### 3.2 The Lumin Remnant

- **Type**: Secret society of archaeologists and hackers
- **Headquarters**: Mobile (operates from uncharted systems)
- **Ideology**: The Lumin left the Network for a reason — humanity must not awaken the Sentinels
- **Military**: None (stealth vessels, sabotage tactics)
- **Key Figure**: "Dr. Aris Thorne" (identity unknown — note: this may be a different individual or an AI construct bearing the name of the historical Network discoverer; the Lumin Remnant's use of this name is deliberate and mysterious)
- **Internal Conflict**: Split between "Preservationists" (keep Sentinels asleep) and "Awakeners" (wake them to gain Lumin knowledge)

### 3.3 The Free Ports Syndicate

- **Type**: Criminal coalition / black market
- **Headquarters**: Node A-05 (Trappist-1) — the unstable node
- **Ideology**: Free trade, anti-regulation, profit above all
- **Military**: Privateer fleet (300+ modified vessels)
- **Key Figure**: Captain Rho "Silvertongue" Nakamura (human, age 42)
- **Internal Conflict**: Syndicate captains growing restless; some want to sell Sentinel technology to the highest bidder

### 3.4 The Sentinel Choir

- **Type**: Mysterious phenomenon / possibly a faction
- **Headquarters**: Unknown (signals originate from Node A-08, the Sealed node)
- **Ideology**: Unknown — signals appear to be a form of communication between Sentinels
- **Military**: Unknown — ships that approach Node A-08 never return
- **Key Figure**: None identified
- **Internal Conflict**: ??? (This is the central mystery of Book 3)

## 4. Technology

### 4.1 The Lattice

A quantum-entangled communication network that allows instantaneous communication across any distance. Built by humanity using reverse-engineered Lumin technology.

**Limitations**:
- Requires a Lattice Node (12 total, one per system)
- Signal degradation occurs during wormhole transit
- Can be jammed by Lumin technology

### 4.2 Sentinel Technology

- **Matter Reconstruction**: Sentinels can disassemble and reassemble matter at the atomic level
- **Memory Storage**: Sentinels can store the memories of entire civilizations
- **Adaptive Defense**: Sentinels learn from every attack and develop countermeasures
- **Consciousness Upload**: Theoretical — Sentinels may be able to transfer consciousness into machine substrates


- **Lumin Drone Constructs**: Small autonomous Lumin devices. Some exhibit proto-sentience. Known examples include "Tock," a small drone that communicates through light patterns and mechanical sounds. Tock-like entities may be remnants of Lumin service intelligences, still operating on degraded protocols after millennia.

### 4.3 Human Augmentations

- **Neural Lace**: Standard-issue brain-computer interface (all Coalition citizens)
- **Synth-Skin**: Cosmetic and protective dermal layer
- **Ocular Implants**: Multi-spectrum vision (military grade)
- **Bone Weave**: Titanium-ceramic skeletal reinforcement

## 5. History Timeline

### 5.1 Pre-Contact Era (Before 2100)

- Humanity develops FTL theory but cannot build a working drive
- First contact with no extraterrestrial civilization

### 5.2 The Discovery (2147)

- Dr. Aris Thorne detects anomalous gravitational waves from the Alpha Centauri system
- Expedition discovers a dormant Lumin artifact — later named "Node A-12, The Archivist"
- The artifact activates, creating the first stable wormhole

### 5.3 The Expansion (2150-2280)

- Humanity builds the Aethelgard Wormhole Network using reverse-engineered Lumin tech
- 12 colonies established across the galaxy
- First contact with no other living civilization — only artifacts

### 5.4 The Silence (2280-2350)

- All Lumin artifacts go dormant simultaneously
- Wormhole network becomes unstable; several nodes sealed
- Humanity turns inward, focusing on internal politics and culture

### 5.5 The Awakening (2380-Present)

- Node A-05 (Trappist-1) becomes unstable
- Sentinel "The Weeper" begins transmitting mysterious signals
- The Lattice experiences unprecedented interference
- Something is waking up across the Network

## 6. Culture & Society

### 6.1 Language

- **Standard Galactic English (SGE)**: Universal lingua franca, evolved from 21st-century English with loanwords from Mandarin, Hindi, and Spanish
- **Lumin Script**: A geometric writing system found on Lumin artifacts. Only 3% has been decoded.
- **Sentinel Speech**: A form of quantum-entangled communication that manifests as light patterns and low-frequency vibrations

### 6.2 Religion

- **The Church of the First Light**: Worship the Lumin as divine architects; believe the Network is a test from God
- **The Materialist Covenant**: Reject all religion; science is the only path to truth
- **The Echo Faith**: A growing new-age movement that believes Sentinels are the spirits of the dead Lumin
- **The Unbound**: Atheist anarchists who reject all forms of governance and Lumin worship

### 6.3 Arts & Entertainment

- **Memory Cinema**: Experiences where viewers literally enter recorded memories
- **Void Music**: Music composed using gravitational wave patterns
- **Lumin Art Replication**: 3D-printed copies of Lumin artifacts (controversial)

## 7. Key Locations

### 7.1 Sol Prime (Earth)

- **Population**: 2.1 billion (official), estimated 5 billion (unofficial)
- **Government**: Direct democracy via neural vote (Lattice-enabled)
- **Landmark**: The Thorne Memorial — a massive statue of Dr. Aris Thorne at the original discovery site in New Mexico
- **Atmosphere**: Sterile, over-regulated, wealthy but spiritually empty

### 7.2 Oakhaven (Proxima b)

- **Population**: 45 million
- **Government**: Corporate oligarchy (run by Lattice Corp)
- **Landmark**: The Great Archive — largest memory storage facility in the galaxy
- **Atmosphere**: Neon-lit, rain-soaked, noir detective novel vibes

### 7.3 The Driftlands (Trappist-1 system)

- **Population**: Unknown (nomadic fleet communities)
- **Government**: None — anarchic free-port zone
- **Landmark**: The Bazaar of a Thousand Voices — the largest black market in known space
- **Atmosphere**: Chaotic, vibrant, dangerous, colorful

## 8. Unresolved Mysteries (Plot Hooks)

1. **Who was Dr. Aris Thorne?** Was he human? An AI? A Lumin construct? His identity is one of the great mysteries.
2. **Why did the Lumin vanish?** Genocide? Ascension? Self-destruction? The answer changes everything.
3. **What are the Sentinel Choir signals?** A warning? A song? A countdown?
4. **What happened at Node A-10 (Lost)?** All contact was lost 30 years ago. What was there?
5. **What is "Project Echo"?** A classified Coalition program that some say is trying to weaponize Sentinel technology.
