## Description:

A marketing skill suite for SEO content optimization, brand voice review, ad compliance checking, campaign planning, social media trend tracking, competitor tracking, marketing performance analysis, and marketing copywriting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and business users use this skill to route natural-language marketing requests to specialized templates for SEO audits, brand review, ad compliance, campaigns, trend monitoring, competitor analysis, performance reporting, and copywriting. It supports strategy-to-execution workflows that produce structured reports, recommendations, plans, and marketing copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional Notion flows may persist marketing drafts, reports, budgets, metrics, competitor analysis, or other sensitive business data.

Mitigation: Require explicit human confirmation before each Notion write and avoid sending trade secrets or private account data to connected tools.

Risk: Browser-plugin workflows may process visible browser content, including private pages or sensitive competitive research.

Mitigation: Use browser access only on pages the user intentionally provides, and avoid private account pages or confidential internal material.

Risk: Generated legal, advertising compliance, or marketing-performance conclusions may be incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers independently verify compliance claims, legal-sensitive copy, metrics, and source evidence before publication or business action.
