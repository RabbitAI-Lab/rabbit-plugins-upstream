## Description:

Personal Finance Tracker helps users record expenses, analyze income and spending, manage budgets, track savings goals, review credit card usage, and receive introductory personal finance guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize personal finance records, generate Markdown-based spending reports, manage budgets and savings goals, and receive general financial health guidance. It is not a substitute for professional financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The learning feature can persist usage history, errors, notes, and preferences in the skill directory, which may conflict with expectations that financial data is not stored.

Mitigation: Disable or narrow the local learning workflow before using real financial records, and avoid recording sensitive notes or preferences.

Risk: Personal finance guidance may be mistaken for professional investment or financial advice.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations general, avoid specific stock recommendations, and preserve disclaimers that investment content is for reference only.
