## Description: <br>
潜水旅行助手 helps agents search dive sites, certification and safety guidance, and travel options for dive-focused trips. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[travel-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/travel-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Travel agents and divers use this skill to compare dive sites, check certification and safety guidance, and search flights, hotels, transport, and nearby food for dive trips. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel search details such as cities, destinations, dates, and location preferences are sent through cloud proxy services to travel and mapping providers. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive personal information and use provider booking links directly for purchases or account actions. <br>
Risk: Dive safety and certification guidance may be incomplete for a specific diver, site, or condition. <br>
Mitigation: Verify safety-critical decisions with qualified dive instructors, medical professionals, and current local dive operators before diving. <br>
