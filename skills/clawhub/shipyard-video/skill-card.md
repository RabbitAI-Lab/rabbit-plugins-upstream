## Description:

Creates narrated product videos of an app using rebuilt screens, practice-page recordings, or a requested real run, then prepares web and YouTube publishing materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[philipposk](https://clawhub.ai/user/philipposk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

App developers use this skill to create promotional videos, feature walkthroughs, and how-to videos for their products. It prepares widescreen and phone cuts, light and dark versions, captions, and materials for publishing a web copy or sharing on YouTube.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may expand app changes or loosen sign-in rules beyond video files.

Mitigation: Review the full plugin before installing; approve broader app upgrades separately, limit public-access exceptions to intended video paths, and use installed, pinned tooling.
