## Description:

ppt to video, word to video, excel to video, pdf to video, document to video — parse, outline, storyboard, voiceover, build, validate. Use when the user gives a document and wants an explainer, report broadcast, courseware, or training video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users provide a presentation, document, spreadsheet, or PDF and use the file-to-video template to create an explainer, report, courseware, or training video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy services, and attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive documents unless the service's data handling is acceptable for that material.

Risk: The security review is partial and notes that some CLI actions may not align with the stated video-making purpose.

Mitigation: Review the CLI's behavior and data handling, and confirm its login and project-listing actions fit your needs before use.
