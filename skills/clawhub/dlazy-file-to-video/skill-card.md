## Description:

Turns presentations, documents, spreadsheets, and PDFs into narrated explainer or training videos through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn source documents into narrated explainers, report recaps, courseware, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy services.

Mitigation: Upload only documents you are comfortable sharing with dLazy.

Risk: Hosted video creation may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Check costs and available credits before starting a project.

Risk: Installing the third-party CLI globally leaves a persistent local tool.

Mitigation: Use the pinned on-demand npx command if a global installation is unnecessary.
