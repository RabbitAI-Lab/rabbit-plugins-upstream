## Description:

ppt to video, word to video, excel to video, pdf to video, document to video — parse, outline, storyboard, voiceover, build, validate. Use when the user gives a document and wants an explainer, report broadcast, courseware, or training video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and teams use this skill to turn presentations, spreadsheets, PDFs, and other documents into explainer, report, courseware, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends prompts and options to dLazy services, uploads attached files, and can list organization projects.

Mitigation: Review prompts and attachments before sending, share only files you are authorized to provide, and confirm project listing is appropriate for your organization.
