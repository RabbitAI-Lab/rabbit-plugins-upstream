## Description:

Helps turn PPT, Word, Excel, PDF, and other documents into narrated explainer, training, or report videos through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People preparing presentations, reports, lessons, or training materials use this skill to guide document-to-video creation and continue work across project conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached local documents may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Attach only documents you intend to upload to dLazy.

Risk: Signing in stores an API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local key or provide it per invocation; rotate or revoke it if exposed.
