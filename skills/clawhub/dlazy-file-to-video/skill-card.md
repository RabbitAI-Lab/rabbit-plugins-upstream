## Description:

ppt to video, word to video, excel to video, pdf to video, document to video — parse, outline, storyboard, voiceover, build, validate. Use when the user gives a document and wants an explainer, report broadcast, courseware, or training video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can provide a presentation, document, spreadsheet, or PDF and use the dLazy file-to-video workflow to create an explainer, report, courseware, or training video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected documents and prompts are sent to dLazy, so confidential material may be shared with the service.

Mitigation: Review dLazy privacy and retention terms and avoid sending confidential documents unless their use is approved.

Risk: Using the hosted workflow spends service credits, and continuing a project uses its saved session.

Mitigation: Use device login, check credit use, and select the intended project before continuing existing work.
