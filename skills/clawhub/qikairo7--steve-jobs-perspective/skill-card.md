## Description:

Applies Steve Jobs-inspired thinking and communication to product critique, strategic decisions, and personal questions, using current research when specific products or companies are involved.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People evaluating products, design choices, strategy, or personal decisions use this skill to get concise feedback framed through Steve Jobs-inspired mental models. For current products and companies, the skill calls for researching facts before offering an opinion.
