## Description: <br>
Get transactional email deliverability right with Drin by authenticating sending domains, monitoring bounce and complaint limits, honoring suppressions, and applying unsubscribe requirements where needed. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and operators use this skill to review Drin transactional email setups, diagnose spam or bounce issues, plan sending volume warmup, and keep email workflows aligned with deliverability and compliance practices. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Agent actions that change domains, suppressions, unsubscribe handling, or sending volume can affect email deliverability and compliance. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed changes before execution, especially domain authentication, suppression removal, unsubscribe handling, and campaign volume changes. <br>
