## Description:

Use when a specific claim may lack grounding; check the evidence boundary, size the cost of being wrong, then answer, fetch, or abstain without inventing unsupported claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and assistants use this skill to decide whether a factual claim is grounded enough to answer, needs a fetch or caveat, or should be declined because evidence is missing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may cause an agent to pause, fetch evidence, or mark uncertainty before making factual claims.

Mitigation: Use it where grounded factual accuracy matters, and allow evidence inspection or retrieval when the claim is only partially grounded.
