## Description:

AI-powered bank compliance and regulatory review assistant for screening marketing materials, KYC documents, AML alerts, and NFRA/CBIRC compliance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bank compliance officers, legal counsel, KYC/AML analysts, and product teams use this skill to review banking materials, customer due-diligence inputs, AML alerts, AI banking scenarios, and regulatory reporting drafts before qualified compliance staff make final decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter sensitive customer identifiers or complete account details during compliance review.

Mitigation: Minimize and de-identify inputs before use, including names, ID numbers, phone numbers, account numbers, and full transaction details.

Risk: Draft KYC, AML, reporting, or customer-impacting conclusions may be incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Require qualified compliance staff to verify outputs and follow internal approval procedures before taking action.

Risk: A suspected list match could be mistaken for a final sanctions or AML determination.

Mitigation: Treat list-match outputs as suspected matches only and complete manual review through the institution's established process.
