## Description:

Drafts investment-advisory communication templates for China-based wealth managers, including product discussions, market commentary, and responses to client objections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Securities brokers, wealth advisors, and relationship managers in China use the skill to draft client-facing product scripts, market updates, objection responses, and communication checklists for human compliance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive customer information may be exposed if included in drafting prompts.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum necessary customer details; replace identifying information with placeholders.

Risk: Unverified market or regulatory claims and unsuitable product language may mislead clients.

Mitigation: Check claims against official sources and require institutional suitability and compliance review before sending scripts to clients.
