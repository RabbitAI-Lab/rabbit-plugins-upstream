## Description:

Create TikTok and Instagram photo slideshows with ReelsFarm: write slide text, pick a look (classic, tweet, iMessage, notebook, magazine), recreate a public TikTok slideshow for your product, find photos, and render slides or an MP4. Needs a ReelsFarm account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Creators and social media marketers use this skill to draft, design, and render TikTok or Instagram photo slideshows through ReelsFarm.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected text and photos are processed by ReelsFarm, and media generation spends account credits.

Mitigation: Review the selected content and preview, check credit pricing, and approve each action before final rendering.

Risk: The workflow requires a ReelsFarm API key.

Mitigation: Keep REELSFARM_API_KEY private and store it in the environment as documented.

Risk: The supplied frontmatter needs quoting corrected before installation.

Mitigation: Correct the frontmatter quoting before installing the skill.
