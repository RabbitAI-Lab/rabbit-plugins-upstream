## Description:

Help users prepare a clear palm photo and personal details for a thoughtful AI palm reading on AI Palm Reader (handreaderai.com), then reflect on the report privately.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adults seeking an entertainment-focused palm reading can use this skill to prepare a clear photo, decide what requested details to share, navigate the reading service, and reflect privately on its report. It frames the reading as reflection rather than prediction or regulated advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The external service requests a palm photo, first name, and birthday.

Mitigation: Share only the details the service requests, review its current privacy terms, and submit the photo yourself.

Risk: A shareable report link may be opened by anyone who has it.

Mitigation: Keep the link private unless you intentionally choose to share it.
