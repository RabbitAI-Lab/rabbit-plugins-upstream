## Description:

Summarizes BioFlow page views, clicks, referrals, tip revenue, and captured leads from read-only account data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

BioFlow page owners and their agents use this skill to review traffic, link engagement, tip revenue, and signups, or to list captured contacts and uploaded files on request.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may expose personal contact details and account revenue metrics.

Mitigation: Grant only needed BioFlow scopes and show contact details only when requested.
