## Description:

Reports BioFlow page views, clicks, top links, referrers, tips, signups, contacts, and uploaded file names from a connected account without changing the page.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

BioFlow page owners use this skill to review page performance, identify popular links and traffic sources, and inspect requested lead or file information from their connected account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead and contact reports may disclose personal data from the connected BioFlow account.

Mitigation: Request narrow reports and show only the contact details the user asked for; avoid full contact lists unless needed.
