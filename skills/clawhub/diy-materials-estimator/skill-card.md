## Description:

Estimate quantities and purchase units for common DIY projects from project dimensions, applying material coverage rates and waste factors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners, renters, landlords, and property managers can estimate materials for painting, landscaping, concrete, drywall, flooring, tile, and fencing before a purchase. The estimates support shopping and project planning, not structural design or live pricing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage rates, product yields, measurements, and site conditions can make calculated quantities differ from actual needs.

Mitigation: Check dimensions and product specifications before purchasing; treat the results as purchasing estimates.

Risk: A rerun for the same project type replaces its previous saved estimate.

Mitigation: Keep a copy of estimates that need to be retained before rerunning that project type.

Risk: Material quantity estimates do not establish that a structure is safe or meet engineering requirements.

Mitigation: Use a qualified professional for structural design and load calculations.
