## Description:

Provides Arabic step-by-step guidance for PowPow registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation, with product screenshots.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Arabic-speaking new users use the skill to learn about PowPow, register an account, resolve common sign-up problems, and navigate subscription activation. The user completes registration and payment on the service pages; the agent only provides guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration and payment flows involve passwords, verification codes, and payment details.

Mitigation: Enter sensitive information only on official service pages; never provide it in chat.

Risk: The skill directs users to external payment and public media links.

Mitigation: Verify the destination before opening links or paying, and keep payment within the official service flow.
