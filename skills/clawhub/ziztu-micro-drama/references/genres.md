# Genre playbooks

Each genre sells one fantasy. Pick the genre for the fantasy the user wants the audience to feel, then give the stock engine one twist the audience has not seen: viewers forgive a familiar engine, they abandon a familiar story. Trend notes reflect industry reports from 2025 and 2026; charts move, so when the user cares about what is hot right now, say what you know and suggest they check the current app charts.

## 1. How to use a playbook

For the chosen genre, take: the fantasy (write it into the promise), two or three tropes (the genre stack), one signature set piece for episodes 1 to 3, the episode 1 hook family, the paywall cliffhanger family, the title pattern, and the twist that keeps it from being a clone. Never reuse a hit's title, names or logline; the patterns are free, the specifics are not.

## 2. Romance lanes

### Billionaire and CEO romance
- **Fantasy**: the overlooked woman is chosen by the most powerful man in the room, who then rearranges the world for her.
- **Audience**: the core Western app audience, mostly women from their mid-twenties to sixties.
- **Tropes and set pieces**: the boardroom humiliation, the gala entrance, "he buys the whole store", the jealous assistant, the jacket over her shoulders, the public "she's with me".
- **Episode 1 hook**: she is insulted or fired in front of colleagues, and the person who did it is, or answers to, the man she will end up with.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: he says the one sentence that shows he knows her secret, or signs a document that names her.
- **Titles**: "The Billionaire's [Unexpected Role]", "My [Relation] Is a Secret Billionaire", "Married to the [Power]".
- **Trend notes**: the foundation genre and still the largest by volume in Western apps; in China the stock "domineering CEO" is reported as tired. Use it with a twist.
- **Twists that work**: she is the billionaire, he is the one hiding; he is disguised as staff; the CEO is a woman and the underestimated one is the man; the contract is her idea.

### Contract and flash marriage
- **Fantasy**: forced closeness with a powerful partner turns into real love against the terms.
- **Tropes**: the clauses tested one by one, the first public appearance as a couple, the fake ring, the rival who knows it is fake, the end date.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the bargain made under pressure (a medical bill, a will, a visa, a deadline), or the bride swapped at the last minute.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: a clause nobody read comes due (an end date, a hidden heir, "no feelings").
- **Titles**: "Married by [Day]", "[Number] Days as His Wife", "Fake Married to [Power]".
- **Trend notes**: evergreen, and cheap to produce (a few rooms, two faces).
- **Twists**: the contract has a secret third party; both are lying about why they signed; the clock is the engine (a will deadline, a 100 day term).

### Forbidden and age-gap romance
- **Fantasy**: the wrong person at the wrong moment is the right one; desire against the family's rules.
- **Tropes**: her best friend's father, his brother's fiancée, the stepbrother, the ex's uncle, the secret weekend, the wedding in danger.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the betrayal that sends her into a stranger's arms (the cheating fiancé discovered), then the reveal of who the stranger is.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the family is about to find out, or the ex walks in.
- **Titles**: "[Forbidden] With My [Relation]'s [Relation]", "Bound by My [Relation]".
- **Trend notes**: strong in Western app charts. Keep both partners clearly adult and the romance consensual.
- **Twists**: the forbidden partner is the one person who can expose the villain; the family secret is worse than the romance.

### Sweet romance and campus
- **Fantasy**: soft, safe love; attention that does not hurt.
- **Tropes**: the childhood friend, the school star, the roommate, the slow-burn misunderstanding, the protective crush.
- **Episode 1 hook**: an embarrassing public moment that the crush turns into a rescue.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the confession overheard by the wrong person, or the move away.
- **Trend notes**: rising in China as the antidote to shock content; less proven in Western paid apps, strong on social feeds.
- **Twists**: the rescue was planned; the bully is the crush's sibling.

## 3. Status lanes

### Hidden identity, secret heiress, hidden billionaire
- **Fantasy**: be underestimated, then watch the snobs collapse when the truth lands.
- **Tropes**: the staggered reveal (a small flash, a bigger flash, the full reveal), the family that does not know the "loser" owns the building, the true heiress against the fake one, the black car, the staff who bow.
- **Episode 1 hook**: she is fired, divorced or mocked while the viewer already knows she owns the place.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the line or object only the owner would know (the access card, the founder's ring), or the convoy pulling up.
- **Titles**: "Never [Wrong] a Secret Heiress", "[Back Off], the Heiress Returns", "The [Low Role] Owns the [Place]".
- **Trend notes**: top tier in Western apps; "anti-trope" variants (the disguised CEO, the amnesiac heiress who returns) rose in 2026. In China the female heiress lane is rising while the male version is tired.
- **Twists**: she does not want the inheritance; the reveal costs her the one person who loved her as she was; the fake heiress is also a victim.

### Revenge (betrayed wife, betrayed daughter)
- **Fantasy**: a calm, intelligent plan that dismantles the betrayer's life step by step.
- **Tropes**: the betrayal seen in person (the husband with the best friend), the frame-up, the plan's stages (each one an episode), the recruited ally, the recording, the public takedown at the betrayer's own celebration.
- **Episode 1 hook**: she walks in on the betrayal, or is framed in public.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: she smiles at the camera of her own mind: "Step one is done."
- **Titles**: "Too Late, [Name] Forsakes You", "Don't Mess With My [Relation]", "[Name]'s [Event] Revenge".
- **Trend notes**: rising; female-led revenge has been among the strongest lanes in 2026 app charts.
- **Twists**: the betrayer had a reason the lead must face; the ally is using her; the revenge succeeds and it is not enough.

### Rebirth and second chance
- **Fantasy**: the wronged lead returns with every memory and rewrites fate.
- **Tropes**: dying betrayed, waking before it began, "the first scene again", knowledge of the future, the betrayer who does not know, the ex who will regret.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the death, then the gasp awake years earlier, on the morning everything started.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: a choice that already differs from the first life, and a sign that something else has changed too.
- **Titles**: "Reborn, I [Refuse/Choose] ...", "This Time, I [Action]".
- **Trend notes**: strong demand in Western apps, often stacked with fantasy romance; heavily over-produced in Chinese AI anime. Differentiate the engine.
- **Twists**: someone else was reborn too; changing one event breaks a good one; the villain of life one is the ally of life two.

### Divorce regret
- **Fantasy**: the man who failed her must earn her back, or lose her forever, in public.
- **Tropes**: divorce papers on the anniversary, the glow-up, the new rival, the slow discovery that she was his hidden benefactor, the public begging.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the public divorce, often on a date that should have been happy.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: he finds the document or the photo that shows what she did for him.
- **Titles**: "After the Divorce, ...", "My Ex Begs ...", "Never Divorce a ...".
- **Trend notes**: healthy in the West; in China the stock devices (the slap, the kneeling apology, the third party, the amnesia substitute) are reported to be losing viewers. Keep the premise, refresh the devices.
- **Twists**: she has already moved on and the regret is his story, not hers; the new rival is his own family.

### Secret baby and pregnancy
- **Fantasy**: the child changes the balance of power; the father learns too late.
- **Tropes**: the test on the worst day, the years away, the child with his eyes, the child who recognizes the father, the DNA test, the cute child who outwits adults.
- **Episode 1 hook**: she learns she is pregnant on the day she is thrown out.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the child's face beside the powerful man's, or the test result about to be read.
- **Titles**: "Pregnant by My [Relation]", "[Daddy], When Will You Find [Mommy]".
- **Trend notes**: steady; strongest as an add-on to romance or revenge.
- **Twists**: the child is the one investigating; the father knew all along. Keep a small child calm and close on screen; one child, never a crowd of them.

### Family and in-laws
- **Fantasy**: the family that despised her has to admit what she is worth.
- **Tropes**: the dinner humiliation, the mother-in-law's test, the favored sibling, the stolen credit, the will, the family vote.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the public put-down at a family event.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the family elder about to reveal who really holds the money.
- **Trend notes**: in China, warm family stories (period family, the stepmother, the young "great-grandmother" who outranks the CEO) have risen against pure humiliation; cooperation and warmth are payoffs too.
- **Twists**: the lead wins the family over instead of crushing it; the elder is her secret ally.

### Hidden master (male power fantasy)
- **Fantasy**: the humble man everyone despises is secretly the most powerful, and someone kneels every episode.
- **Tropes**: feigned weakness, the in-laws' contempt, villains who refuse obvious proof, the subordinates' entrance, the phone call that stops a deal.
- **Episode 1 hook**: humiliation at a family event or work party.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: a phone rings, and the most powerful person in the city says "Sir".
- **Trend notes**: the stock son-in-law and god-of-war formats are reported as tired in China. In Western apps the male audience is growing and leans toward fantasy, sci-fi and gang stories.
- **Twists**: the hidden power has a price he is hiding from her; she is the one who discovers him; the power-flash goes wrong.

## 4. Fantasy lanes

### Werewolf and fated mates
- **Fantasy**: fated love, the rejected mate who rises, the pack's protection.
- **Tropes**: the mate bond, the rejection ceremony, the moon trial, the rival mate, the rejected Luna's return, the mark.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the rejection ceremony, in front of the pack.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the mark appears, or the Alpha catches her scent.
- **Titles**: "Fated to My [Forbidden] Alpha", "The Alpha's [Rejected/Contract] Luna".
- **Trend notes**: mature and saturating in the West; AI makes wolves cheap, so the lane is crowded. Only with an original twist.
- **Twists**: she rejects him first; the curse is the bond; the wolfless girl is something else entirely. Stage transformations as a start mid-change or a cut on a flash.

### Vampire and dragon romance
- **Fantasy**: an immortal, dangerous lover who is gentle only with her.
- **Tropes**: the bargain ("be my mate or die"), the exile who returns with his child, the hunter who falls for the hunted.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the bargain offered at the moment of death.
- **Trend notes**: secondary to werewolves, same romance engine; dragon kings appear in chart-topping app titles.

### Mafia romance
- **Fantasy**: the dangerous man is gentle only with her; she becomes untouchable.
- **Tropes**: the forced marriage to the boss, witnessing a crime, the safe house, the rival family, the betrayal inside the family, the protection in public.
- **Episode 1 hook**: she witnesses something she should not, or is sold into a marriage to settle a debt.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: a gun pointed at someone she loves, or the boss's secret enemy named.
- **Titles**: "Married to the [Mafia Role]", "The [Boss]'s [Obsession/Bride]".
- **Trend notes**: rising, and reaching male viewers too. Keep violence implied: cars, suits, threats, aftermath.
- **Twists**: she is the heir of the rival family; the boss is protecting her from his own father.

### Cultivation, system and fantasy
- **Fantasy**: the waste becomes the strongest; a level-up every cycle.
- **Tropes**: the crippled disciple, the tournament, the sect betrayal, the system screen, the beast that evolves, the bigger opponent on the next map.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the protagonist crippled, disinherited or mocked at the trial.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the dormant power wakes.
- **Trend notes**: large in AI anime; the "system" mechanic shows saturation. Spectacle is cheap in AI, so make the emotions specific.
- **Twists**: the power has a moral cost; the mentor is the enemy; the "waste" chose weakness to hide.

### Palace and historical
- **Fantasy**: survive and rise in a court of schemes.
- **Tropes**: the true and false heiress, the poisoned tea, the reborn consort, the emperor's test, the forbidden letter.
- **Trend notes**: healthy in China and in AI anime; costumes and sets cost little in AI.
- **Twists**: the scheming is a mask for protecting someone; the villain is the lead's mother.

### Time travel and transmigration
- **Fantasy**: modern knowledge in another world, or another body.
- **Tropes**: waking in the villainess's body, the era (the 1980s, the palace), modern skills as payoffs.
- **Trend notes**: evergreen in China, thinner in the West.

## 5. Suspense lanes

### Thriller, POV and mystery
- **Fantasy**: to be the one who sees through the lie; danger survived by wit.
- **Tropes**: the unknown caller, the missing person, the perfect husband with a second phone, the locked room, the countdown, the twist reframe.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the call, the body, the vanished child, the message "I know what you did".
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: a reframe that turns an ally into a suspect.
- **Trend notes**: a plausible growth lane, pitched by AI-native studios; less hard data than romance.
- **Twists**: the lead is the unreliable one; the victim planned it.

### Horror
- **Fantasy**: fear in a safe dose, and the satisfaction of surviving it.
- **Tropes**: the house, the visitor, the rule that must not be broken, the thing in the photo, the child who sees.
- **Episode 1 hook**: the rule broken in the first seconds.
- **Paywall cliffhanger**: the thing is already inside, or the protector is the thing.
- **Trend notes**: under-served in apps, a strong social niche. Faces and creatures are risky in AI video: show less, suggest more, keep a light source on faces.

## 6. Light lanes

### Comedy and parody
- **Fantasy**: laughing at the drama while enjoying it.
- **Tropes**: a reality-show format (the coupling ceremony, the elimination), a soap parody, absurd characters played completely straight, the audience vote.
- **Trend notes**: a social-feed format more than a paid-episode format. The AI fruit dating-show parody reached hundreds of millions of views in weeks, then faced "AI slop" backlash and removals. Originality and craft are the defense.
- **Twists**: the parody has a real emotional story underneath.

### Pets, animals and objects
- **Fantasy**: a soap opera where the characters are cats, fruit, toys or animals; cute plus scandal.
- **Tropes**: the same melodrama engines (betrayal, the secret baby, the evil stepmother) in an animal world.
- **Trend notes**: viral spikes on TikTok and Douyin, high copying. Works well in 3D animated and cartoon looks on Ziztu.
- **Twists**: give each animal a fixed signature colour and prop, or viewers cannot tell them apart.

### AI anime drama (manga drama, motion comic)
- **Fantasy**: whatever its web-novel genre sells: revenge, rebirth, cultivation, the true heiress, the system.
- **Tropes**: a narrated premise in the first 30 to 45 seconds, inner voice, power-ups, transformation cliffhangers, villains who refuse obvious proof.
- **Trend notes**: hyper-saturated in China (hundreds of thousands of titles, a tiny share reach 100 million views). What wins: a complete world, stable characters and a premise that is not one of the three most common tropes.
- **On Ziztu**: say "2D anime" in the brief; 16:9 suits YouTube and anime fans, 9:16 suits TikTok. Anime stages magic, transformations and creatures more convincingly than photoreal.

## 7. Regional notes

| Market | What works |
|---|---|
| US and global English apps | Billionaire, heiress, revenge, mafia, werewolf, secret baby, forbidden romance; 50 to 90 episodes of 1 to 2 minutes; premium from around episode 10 |
| China | Warmth and family cooperation rising next to rebirth and revenge; 60 to 100 episodes; free with ads common |
| India | Humiliation then redemption, flash marriage, hidden wealth with a childhood clue; 25 to 35 episodes of about 2 minutes; a slap, a confrontation or a romantic beat late in each minute; a younger, more male audience |
| Korea | Chaebol romance, divorce from a star, sharehouse romance; 2 minute episodes, 60 episode runs; polish is the selling point; women 35 and over are the core |
| Japan | Workplace revenge and school melodrama; strong TikTok-native series |
| Southeast Asia and Latin America | The fastest-growing download markets; localize names, places and family structures, keep the engines |

## 8. Stacking genres and the twist rule

- A genre stack is one main lane plus two or three tropes: "revenge + hidden heiress + contract marriage". More than four and the promise blurs.
- The twist rule: name the stock version of the premise, then change one load-bearing piece (who holds the secret, who wants the contract, what the reveal costs, where it happens). The twist goes in the logline.
- Write the twist into the bible's "Series rules" so later episodes do not drift back to the stock version.
