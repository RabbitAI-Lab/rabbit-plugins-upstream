# The posting kit

A finished episode still has to win the feed. This file covers what to hand the user with each episode when they will publish: the caption, the cover text, part numbering, the trailer, hook variants for ads, the AI label, and the comment loop.

## 1. Per episode

- **Caption** (under about 100 characters before the cut-off): restate the hook as a question or a claim, plus the part number. "He threw money at the cleaner. He didn't know she owns the building. Part 1". No false promises: the caption describes what is in the episode.
- **On-screen text**: burned-in captions for every line (many people watch muted), a name strap on each character's first appearance, and on social parts an optional hook banner at the top for the whole episode. On Ziztu, ask the Director for captions and name straps (`references/ziztu.md`, step I).
- **Cover text**: three to five words, the promise or the title (`references/titles-and-loglines.md`).
- **Part numbering**: "Part 3" or "Ep 3" in the caption and on the cover; keep the parts in a playlist or series collection where the platform has one.
- **Pinned comment**: a question that makes people take sides ("Would you have torn the money too?"), or where to find the next part.
- **Hashtags**: a few that describe the genre and the format (micro drama, the genre, the trope); no padding with unrelated trends.

## 2. The series trailer

15 to 30 seconds, cut from the strongest moments of episodes 1 to 3 (on Ziztu, from shots that already exist):

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | The single strongest hook image |
| 2 to 10 | The wound: the injustice in one exchange |
| 10 to 20 | The promise: the turn, the secret shown to the viewer |
| 20 to 27 | The biggest payoff, teased and cut before it lands |
| 27 to 30 | The title card and "Episode 1 now" |

Write it as a short Fountain scene list with the source episode for each beat.

## 3. Hook variants for ads and tests

Top-performing app titles run thousands of ad variants, most of them different openings of the same story. For any episode the user will promote, write six to ten alternative first three seconds from different hook families (the injustice, the flash-forward, the evidence, the danger, the inner voice, the question line), each reusing existing shots where possible and naming the one new shot it needs. Mark two to test first.

## 4. Labels and platform rules

- Turn on each platform's AI-content disclosure when publishing AI-made video: TikTok's AI-generated content label, YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting for realistic footage, and Meta's AI label on Instagram and Facebook. Several countries, China among them, require labels on AI-generated content.
- Original characters only; no real people's likeness, no real brands in a bad light, no copyrighted characters in work the user will monetize.
- Platforms remove mass-produced, repetitive AI content. A distinct premise, distinct faces and voices, and real craft in every episode are the defense, and they are what this skill exists for.

## 5. Release rhythm

- Launch with episodes 1 to 3 together, so the first viewers can binge into the proof the series delivers.
- Then a steady cadence the user can sustain (daily, or several parts a day at launch). A gap in a series loses the audience faster than a weak episode.
- Keep the season grid a few episodes ahead of what is published, so comments can still change what comes next.

## 6. The comment loop

After each release, ask the user for the comments and any retention numbers they can see, and run the comment-driven rewrite in `references/doctor.md` on the next block.
