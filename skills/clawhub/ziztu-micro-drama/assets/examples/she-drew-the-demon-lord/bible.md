# She Drew the Demon Lord

A short bible for an anime example: enough to write and produce episode 1. A full season would fill every section of `assets/templates/series-bible.md`.

Alternate titles: The Painter's Demon · Ink Bride
Logline (27 words): A mocked palace apprentice whose paintings wake at midnight spills her ink in front of the court, and raises the demon lord the royal family sealed away.
The promise: watch the apprentice everyone laughed at command the demon the palace fears, then paint the kingdom's lies back into the open.
Hook image (episode 1, first 3 seconds): a painted scroll ripped in half above a jade floor, and a kneeling girl catching one half before it lands.

| | |
|---|---|
| Genre stack | Fantasy romance · hidden power · palace intrigue |
| Medium and look | 2D anime, bright and painterly: ink-wash backgrounds, gold light through paper screens, every spell drawn as living black ink |
| Shape | Vertical 9:16 · 20 episodes × 60 to 75 s, for TikTok and YouTube Shorts (a 16:9 cut for YouTube later) |
| Dramatic irony engine | The viewer knows from episode 1 that whatever Mei paints comes alive at midnight; the court learns it one painting at a time |
| The twist | The stock version is "a weak girl gains a demon and takes revenge". Here the demon is the wronged one: the royal family sealed him in ink three hundred years ago to hide what they stole, and the only person who can free or bind him for good is the girl they mock |

## Characters

### MEI (cue: MEI)
- Role: lead, apprentice to the old court painter
- Want: to be named a court painter, and to learn who her mother was
- Secret: everything she paints wakes at midnight; she burns most of her paintings before then
- Signature line: "It isn't mine anymore."
- **Character Lock:** Mei, 19, small and quick, warm brown skin, a smudge of black ink on her left cheek, a chipped front tooth, short black hair tied with a red cord, an oversized grey apprentice robe with rolled sleeves, a bamboo brush behind her ear.
- Signature colour: red (the hair cord)
- Voice: a young woman, bright and quick, very quiet when she is afraid

### RAKHAN (cue: RAKHAN)
- Role: the demon lord, sealed in a painting for three hundred years
- Want: his name back, and the royal family's truth told
- Secret: he cannot harm the person who painted him free
- Signature line: "Who woke me?"
- **Character Lock:** Rakhan, ageless, very tall, waist-long ink-black hair that drips like wet paint at the ends, pale skin with brushstroke markings across his throat, gold eyes, a black robe edged in red, a cracked jade collar.
- Signature colour: black and gold
- Voice: a man's deep, slow, amused voice with a faint echo

### LADY SEREN (cue: SEREN)
- Role: antagonist, rung 1; the Crown Prince's fiancée
- Flaw: she cannot stand to be laughed at
- **Character Lock:** Lady Seren, 22, tall, ivory skin, ice-blue eyes, a beauty mark under her left eye, silver hair in a crown braid with pearl pins, a white and pale-blue silk gown, a fan of white feathers.
- Signature colour: white and ice blue
- Voice: a young woman, sweet and clear, cruel when she laughs

### CROWN PRINCE JUN (cue: JUN)
- Role: love interest or rival (the season decides); watches more than he speaks
- **Character Lock:** Crown Prince Jun, 24, broad-shouldered, golden-brown skin, a sharp jaw, a thin scar through his lower lip, a black topknot with a gold crown pin, a crimson and gold robe, a jade-hilted sword.
- Signature colour: crimson
- Voice: a young man, low and measured

## Season shape (20 episodes)

| Episodes | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | The unveiling, the demon rises, the court demands Mei's execution; Rakhan's bargain |
| 4 to 7 | Seren's schemes; Mei paints her way out of each trap; Seren falls in public at 7 |
| 8 to 10 | The Prince learns what his ancestors did; the midpoint: Mei's mother was the last painter they sealed |
| 11 to 17 | The Queen's counterstrike; Rakhan's seal starts to fail; Mei must choose between freeing him and saving the palace |
| 18 to 20 | The great painting that tells the truth in front of the whole kingdom; the season 2 hook: a second sealed scroll opens by itself |
