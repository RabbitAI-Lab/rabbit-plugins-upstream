# Marry Me by Friday

Alternate titles: The Cleaner Owns the Tower · Never Fire a Secret Heiress
Logline (28 words): A cleaner the CEO treats like furniture inherits 51% of his company, but only if she marries by Friday, and the only man she can ask is him.
The promise: watch the cleaner he threw money at make him beg to be her husband, then take back everything his family stole from hers.
Hook image (episode 1, first 3 seconds): a hundred-dollar bill lands on wet marble beside a kneeling cleaner's yellow gloves.

## Series rules

- The pact: Eleanor Hale and Richard Blake signed a founders' pact that the controlling shares pass only to a married heir, and only to a marriage with a Blake, "so the two families never split the company again". Grant is legally a Blake; marrying him gives Vivian control. That is why Damien is the only man Mia can ask.
- The 30 day clause (revealed in episode 4): the couple must live under one roof for 30 days, or the shares revert. It becomes the clock again in episodes 17 to 26.
- Money is shown, never explained: the black card, the board's standing ovation, the security guards' bow.
- Mia never begs. She bargains.
- Recurring devices: the hundred-dollar bill (episode 1, and the finale, where Damien places one on the floor and kneels beside it), Eleanor's gold ring on a cord, Chloe's red phone, Vivian's jade bangle tapping the table before every threat.
- Never: a slap from Mia (she wins with words and evidence), a dream fake-out, amnesia.
