## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Writers and creators use the skill to plan and draft vertical micro drama series, revise scripts, and prepare episodes for optional production with Ziztu.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using Ziztu may involve sending scripts or other creative material to its service.

Mitigation: Review Ziztu's privacy practices and share only material you are comfortable sending to the service.
