## 核心要点

- In n8n, data flows through your workflow from node to node. Each node receives data, processes it, and passes the results to the next node. Understanding how data moves and transforms in your workflows is essential for building effective workflows. **Data flows through nodes**: When you connect nodes, data automatically passes from one to the next. Each node processes the incoming data and outputs results based on its configuration.
