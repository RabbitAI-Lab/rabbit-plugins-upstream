# Execution data

## 何时读取

当用户的问题涉及 n8n 文档 `hosting/scaling/execution-data.md` 的主题、配置、概念或操作步骤时读取。

## 核心要点

- Depending on your executions settings and volume, your n8n database can grow in size and run out of storage. To avoid this, n8n recommends that you don't save unnecessary data, and enable pruning of old executions data.

## 快速定位

- Reduce saved data
- Enable executions pruning
