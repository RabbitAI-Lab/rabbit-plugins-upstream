## 节点标识

- n8n node id: `n8n-nodes-langchain.minimax`
- node group: `app-nodes`

## 核心要点

- The MiniMax node lets you interact with MiniMax AI models from n8n. This documentation explains how to integrate MiniMax into your n8n workflows to generate images, produce speech from text, generate videos, and send messages to language models.
