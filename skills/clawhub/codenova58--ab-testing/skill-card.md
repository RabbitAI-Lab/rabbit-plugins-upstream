## Use Case:

Developers, product managers, analysts, and experiment owners use this skill to plan, review, and communicate A/B tests with clear hypotheses, metrics, randomization, guardrails, and decision rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A/B testing guidance can lead to misleading conclusions if hypotheses, metrics, segments, or decision rules are vague.

Mitigation: Require explicit success criteria, constraints, randomization approach, guardrails, and analysis rules before recommending rollout decisions.

Risk: Experiment plans can overlook user impact, ethics, or operational rollback needs.

Mitigation: Review ethics, monitoring, stakeholder communication, and rollback points as part of the workflow before launch.
