## Description:

核查医疗器械原材料和组件供应商合规，包括 ISO 13485 证书、材质证明、ROHS/REACH 符合性和 UDI 供应链追溯，并输出供应商合规台账与缺口清单。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory, quality, and supplier-management teams use this skill to structure medical-device supplier evidence review across qualifications, material certificates, environmental declarations, and supply-chain traceability. It helps produce a compliance ledger and gap list for follow-up, while final conclusions should be checked against current official regulations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat checklist output as professional legal or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a document checklist aid and verify compliance conclusions against current official regulations or qualified professional review.

Risk: Supplier names and commercial relationships may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Anonymize supplier identities and commercial relationship details in prompts, outputs, and shared review artifacts.
