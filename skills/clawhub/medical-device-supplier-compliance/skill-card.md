## Description:

Guides medical-device purchasing and quality teams through supplier qualification and requalification, producing an evidence checklist, a gap list, and a risk ranking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Medical-device purchasing and quality teams use this skill to assess supplier licences, QMS certificates, material evidence, environmental declarations, and UDI traceability during onboarding or periodic reviews, then document gaps and prioritize audits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplier review records may expose confidential supplier names or business relationships.

Mitigation: Use supplier codes or other anonymized identifiers in inputs and outputs.

Risk: Regulatory conclusions may be incomplete or out of date.

Mitigation: Have qualified staff verify conclusions against current regulator guidance.

Risk: The bundled checksum does not match SKILL.md, and linked resources and companion skills were outside this package review.
