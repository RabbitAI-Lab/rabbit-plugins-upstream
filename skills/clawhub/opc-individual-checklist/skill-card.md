## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking Web3 creators, solo entrepreneurs, and freelancers through a checklist for identifying modular work, choosing contribution-based communities, and tracking token or credential-based rewards within stated compliance boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking creators, solo entrepreneurs, freelancers, and digital nomads use this skill to turn individual capabilities into verifiable, priced micro-tasks and decide when adjacent community-design, contribution-allocation, or China crypto-finance compliance skills are required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for legal, tax, investment, token issuance, or fundraising advice.

Mitigation: Keep the skill framed as educational checklist guidance and route token, fundraising, exchange, wallet custody, or jurisdiction-specific compliance questions to qualified review or a dedicated compliance skill.

Risk: Web3 contribution or token language may be used by third parties to promote buy-in schemes, promised appreciation, or recruitment-driven rewards.

Mitigation: Require evidence of real industrial work, auditable contribution records, and non-recruitment revenue before treating an opportunity as a legitimate micro-task workflow.

Risk: Subjective creative or reputation contributions may be difficult to measure fairly.

Mitigation: Prefer deliverables that are independently verifiable, priced, and accepted against clear completion criteria.
