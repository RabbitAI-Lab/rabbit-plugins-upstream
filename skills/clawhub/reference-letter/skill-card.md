## Description: <br>
Writes a credible, specific letter of recommendation or reference tailored to the reader's decision, using relationship context, concrete evidence, comparative endorsement, and a clear recommendation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill to draft reference letters, recommendation letters, character references, or tenancy references. It turns a brief into a complete, tailored letter while marking invented examples for replacement with truthful details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may draft plausible placeholder examples when the user provides limited detail. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated letter and replace every marked example with truthful, specific details before use. <br>
Risk: Reference letters may include sensitive personal or contact information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid adding sensitive information unless it is necessary for the recipient's decision and appropriate to share. <br>
