## Description:

Analyze public advertising libraries across Meta, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn to summarize active ads, recurring creative patterns, landing pages, and test ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mesmerlord](https://clawhub.ai/user/mesmerlord)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and ad strategists can research competitors or a category using public ad libraries, then turn observed creative, offers, and landing pages into ideas for their own tests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and the authentication key are sent to LurkAPI, and API calls consume credits.

Mitigation: Keep the key out of chat and agree on a research budget before making calls.
