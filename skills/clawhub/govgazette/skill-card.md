## Description:

Federal contracting opportunities, award research, and SAM exclusion reviews. Follow linked official sources and source dates. Public research is free and needs no GovGazette account or API key. GovGazette is independent of the U.S. government. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers, vendors, and contractors use this skill to find U.S. federal contracting opportunities, research awards, and review SAM exclusions through GovGazette. It guides them to confirm findings against linked official sources and dates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research results from an independent external service may be incomplete or out of date.

Mitigation: Check linked official sources and their dates before relying on findings.
