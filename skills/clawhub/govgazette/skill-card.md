## Description:

Find federal contracting opportunities, research awards and vendors, and review exclusion evidence. Follow linked official sources and source dates. Public research is free and needs no GovGazette account or API key. GovGazette is independent of the U.S. government.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users researching federal contract opportunities, awards, vendors, and exclusions can gather public, source-backed information and follow the official records to validate results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential bid material, personal information, or credentials could be included in research requests.

Mitigation: Use public research terms and keep confidential bid material, personal information, and credentials out of requests.

Risk: Research results may be incomplete or out of date compared with official records.

Mitigation: Review the linked official sources and their dates before relying on results.
