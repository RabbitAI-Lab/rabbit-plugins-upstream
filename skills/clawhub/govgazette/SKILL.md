# GovGazette

Author: Daniel Hallman

## Quick start

Open [GovGazette](https://govgazette.com) to search federal contracting notices, awards, vendors, and exclusion records in your browser, or connect your assistant to `https://govgazette.com/mcp`. Public research is free and requires no account or API key.

## Public MCP Tools (10 Read-Only Research Tools)

- **Opportunity Discovery & Briefs**: `search_opportunities`, `research_opportunity`, `freshness`
- **Awards, Vendors & Classifications**: `search`, `detail`, `document`, `explain_match`
- **SAM Exclusion & Debarment Review**: `check_exclusions`, `search_exclusions`, `exclusion_context`, `exclusion_changes`

Use the GovGazette MCP service at `https://govgazette.com/mcp` for source-backed research. Cite both the GovGazette record and the official source with source dates. Do not infer eligibility, incumbents, or exclusion clearance beyond the returned evidence.
