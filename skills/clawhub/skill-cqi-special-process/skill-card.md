## Description: <br>
Assists process engineers, quality engineers, and auditors with evidence-backed AIAG CQI-9/11/12/15 special process assessments, producing closed-loop audit reports with scoring, gap analysis, corrective action plans, and re-evaluation steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external auditors, and quality teams use this skill to structure CQI special process assessments for heat treatment, plating, coating, and welding. It guides evidence collection, scoring, gap analysis, corrective action planning, PFMEA linkage, and re-evaluation reporting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
