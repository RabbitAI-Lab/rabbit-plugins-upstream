# Discord Style OG Image visual system

## Observable grammar

Large white copy sits on a bright cobalt field while a phone and dark desktop chat interface stack on the right above soft cloud-like shapes.

## Style anchors

- cobalt-hero
- community-ui
- cartoon-avatars
- cloud-silhouettes

## Composition rules

- Build one immediate focal point and one supporting visual path.
- Keep the brand and headline readable at small link-preview size.
- Preserve intentional negative space; do not fill every empty area.
- Create a new layout for the user's subject instead of tracing a reference.
- Keep essential type and faces away from the outer 6% safe margin.

## Identity replacement

References are evidence for layout, color relationships, material, and pacing
only. Replace every name, logo, slogan, URL, person, mascot, proprietary
interface, campaign asset, and recognizable product silhouette. Do not imply
official status, sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement.

## Prompt checklist

State the exact brand text and headline, the 16:9 crop, layout, color
relationships, material or lighting, subject, text location, and explicit
identity-removal rules. Ask for no watermark and no extra text. When a real
person or protected asset is not authorized, use a newly invented subject.
