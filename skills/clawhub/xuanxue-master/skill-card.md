玄学大师 is a Chinese-language metaphysics advisor skill that supports charting, divination, almanac selection, compatibility, naming, styling, fortune interpretation, and structured reports across Bazi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Qimen Dunjia, Liuyao, Zhouyi, Meihua, Tarot, astrology, wuxing-yinyang, feng shui, physiognomy, and related systems.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request exact birth details, location, home-layout information, or face and palm photos.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum information needed, avoid unnecessary personal images or exact identifiers, and review the skill before installation when this data would be sensitive.

Risk: Generated readings may be mistaken for reliable medical, legal, financial, or major life decision advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for entertainment or cultural reference, and do not rely on them as the sole basis for important decisions.

Risk: Report generation can create files containing sensitive personal details.

Mitigation: Treat generated reports as sensitive files and delete them when no longer needed.
