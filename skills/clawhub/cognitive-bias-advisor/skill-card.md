## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language cognitive-bias coaching skill that routes everyday decision, learning, action, communication, influence, and management questions into guided diagnostics and practical strategy output. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jacksu](https://clawhub.ai/user/jacksu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent operators use this skill for structured reflection on decisions, learning problems, procrastination, communication, influence, and team-management situations. It helps identify likely cognitive-bias patterns and produce diagnostic summaries, self-check questions, and practical response strategies. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill during ordinary decision, learning, communication, influence, or management conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended domain before diagnosis and allow restart, continuation, or domain switching when the conversation changes. <br>
Risk: Persuasion, negotiation, and influence templates could be used to pressure or manipulate others. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's non-harm, transparency, and reciprocal self-check principles, and avoid deceptive or exploitative use. <br>
Risk: Cognitive-bias coaching can sound more decisive than the evidence supports if treated as a final decision. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as analysis, self-check questions, and strategy options; the user remains responsible for final decisions and should review important actions. <br>
