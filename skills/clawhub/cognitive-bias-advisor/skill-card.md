## Description: <br>
Cognitive Bias Advisor helps users identify cognitive biases across decision-making, learning, action, communication, influence, and management, then produces diagnostic reports, strategies, and communication templates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jacksu](https://clawhub.ai/user/jacksu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and teams use this skill to structure decisions, learning, execution, communication, influence, and management conversations around cognitive-bias checks. The skill routes the user's situation to a domain workflow, asks guided diagnostic questions, and returns bias signals with practical mitigation strategies. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill includes persuasion and influence playbooks that could be misused in deceptive, coercive, sales-pressure, workplace-pressure, or asymmetric situations. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for transparent, consent-based communication and review influence scripts before applying them. <br>
Risk: Cognitive-bias diagnoses and suggested strategies can be incomplete, context-sensitive, or misleading if treated as final decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as thinking aids, preserve user agency, and review important decisions with appropriate human judgment. <br>
