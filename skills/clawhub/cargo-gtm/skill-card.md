## Description:

Helps B2B go-to-market teams research accounts, verify professional contacts, qualify leads, draft permission-based outreach, and coordinate CRM and campaign workflows through Cargo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cargo-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/cargo-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and go-to-market teams use this skill to find and research B2B accounts, enrich and check contact data, prioritize prospects, draft compliant outreach, and prepare CRM or audience activation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prospecting and audience uploads may expose professional contact data or reach people without an appropriate basis.

Mitigation: Review acceptable-use rules and confirm consent or another valid basis, suppression lists, and recipient relevance before outreach or audience activation.

Risk: Cargo credentials and signed download links may grant access to sensitive workspace data.

Mitigation: Keep credentials and signed links private, and verify the active workspace before making changes.

Risk: Paid enrichment and recurring workflows can incur ongoing costs.

Mitigation: Apply cost gates, approve paid runs and recurring schedules, and review usage receipts.

Risk: Installing the latest Cargo CLI may introduce unreviewed changes in controlled environments.

Mitigation: Pin and review the CLI version where change control is required.
