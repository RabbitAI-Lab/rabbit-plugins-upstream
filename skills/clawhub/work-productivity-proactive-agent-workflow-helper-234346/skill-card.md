## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn proactive-agent productivity needs into practical workflows, checklists, analyses, implementation support, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt proactive-agent productivity patterns into reliable local workflows, templates, checklists, analyses, code changes, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad auto-invocation wording may cause the skill to activate for ordinary productivity requests outside proactive-agent workflow work. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow the trigger wording or disable implicit invocation during installation when stricter scope control is needed. <br>
