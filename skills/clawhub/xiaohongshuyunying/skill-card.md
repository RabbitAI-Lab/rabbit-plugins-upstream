## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Health-adjacent marketing content may request or expose children's ages, exam numbers, reports, or other sensitive vision-related details in comments or drafts. <br>
Mitigation: Use privacy-preserving intake language, avoid asking for child-specific reports or identifiers in public comments, and remove or anonymize sensitive details before publication. <br>
Risk: Optional professional knowledge-base or IMA lookups are under-specified and may introduce external-source or credential-handling risk. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before optional external knowledge-base lookup, disclose the source used, and avoid sending unnecessary personal or client data. <br>
Risk: Myopia-prevention copy can become misleading if it overstates outcomes or sounds like medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid absolute claims such as cure, guarantee, or 100% effective; verify professional details before publication and keep final content subject to human review. <br>
