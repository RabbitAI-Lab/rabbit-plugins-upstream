## Description: <br>
Writing Style And Tone helps agents draft and edit prose with the STYLE framework, tuning rhythm, specificity, tone, and AI-tell review while requiring human-supplied lived details and final approval. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, social, and content teams use this skill to improve AI-assisted or human drafts for posts, captions, newsletters, scripts, and replies while preserving brand voice and adapting tone to the moment. It is intended as a prose craft and review layer before publishing, not a source of personal anecdotes, claims, or metrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: AI-assisted style edits could introduce invented personal stories, credentials, emotions, or metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Keep placeholder slots visibly unfilled until a human supplies lived specifics, verify claims before use, and require human final approval. <br>
Risk: Users may misuse the skill to hide AI provenance or chase unreliable detector scores. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose AI use where platform or policy requires, apply AI-tell review as a cluster with judgment, and optimize for reader trust rather than detector scores. <br>
Risk: A poor tone choice in complaints, crisis, or bad-news writing could sound evasive or manipulative. <br>
Mitigation: Use plain, accountable language for high-stakes moments, avoid spin or cleverness, and route sensitive drafts through human review before publishing. <br>
