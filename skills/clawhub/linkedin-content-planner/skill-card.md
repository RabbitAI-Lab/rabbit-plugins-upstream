## Description: <br>
Generates a 7-day LinkedIn content plan from a theme, audience, and content pillars, including posting schedule, hook type, CTA, comment targets, and an inbound-readiness check. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, founders, and marketing teams use this skill to plan a week or month of LinkedIn content around a clear theme, target audience, and pillar mix. It is intended for planning a publishing cadence, not drafting a single post. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated LinkedIn posts, comments, or DMs could be inaccurate, off-brand, or inappropriate if published without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated content before publishing or sending it. <br>
Risk: Voice-sample files used for style calibration may contain sensitive or private writing examples. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only voice samples that are appropriate to use for style calibration. <br>
