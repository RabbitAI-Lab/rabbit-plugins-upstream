## Description: <br>
Creative Writing helps agents use CellCog to draft and develop stories, including fiction, screenplays, poetry, worlds, characters, and narrative structures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent users use this skill to request CellCog-assisted creative writing, story planning, character development, world building, screenwriting, fan fiction, poetry, and iterative draft refinement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requires a CELLCOG_API_KEY and sends creative writing prompts or drafts to the CellCog service. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if you trust CellCog, protect the API key, and avoid submitting private or sensitive material unless that matches the intended use. <br>
Risk: Generated creative writing may need editorial review before reuse or publication. <br>
Mitigation: Review and revise generated stories, scripts, characters, worlds, and guidance before relying on them. <br>
