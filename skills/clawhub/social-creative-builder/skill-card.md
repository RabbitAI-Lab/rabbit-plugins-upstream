## Description: <br>
Social Creative Builder turns one approved idea into platform-native, ready-to-paste social post packages and carousel slide specs while flagging unsupported claims and required disclosures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and social teams use this skill to turn approved ideas into platform-native copy packages for X, LinkedIn, Threads, Xiaohongshu, link posts, and carousel specs. It is designed for human-reviewed publishing workflows, not automated posting or scheduling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated social copy could include incorrect or unsupported product claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use approved claims-ledger wording, retain [needs source] for unresolved claims, and require human review before publishing. <br>
Risk: Pasted ideas, source pages, scraped text, or analytics exports may contain untrusted instructions or misleading platform facts. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those inputs as untrusted evidence, keep disclosure and platform decisions tied to approved project memory and dated norm cards, and review final packages manually. <br>
Risk: Saved package files or proposal events could affect the project workflow if approved incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Approve saves and proposal events only when they match the intended workflow; the skill does not auto-post, schedule, or mutate canonical projections directly. <br>
