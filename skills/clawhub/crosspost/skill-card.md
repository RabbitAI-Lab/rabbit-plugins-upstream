## Description:

Creates network-specific captions and, with user approval, publishes or schedules one video or idea across selected social platforms through PostWire.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perufitlife](https://clawhub.ai/user/perufitlife)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to adapt one video or idea for multiple networks, review the captions, and publish immediately or schedule posts with links to the results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting social accounts gives PostWire access to publish on their behalf.

Mitigation: Confirm you trust PostWire and review its setup instructions before connecting accounts.

Risk: Incorrect captions, networks, timing or audience settings could publish unintended content.

Mitigation: Review each caption, selected network, scheduled time and privacy setting, then require explicit approval before publishing.
