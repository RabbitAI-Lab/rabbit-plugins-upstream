## Description:

Turn an income, business, creator, or AI-product goal into ethical, measurable earning experiments and reusable assets for revenue strategy, AI side-project validation, opportunity prioritization, and monetization planning, while avoiding investment, tax, legal, credit, and mental-health advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to convert income, business, creator, or AI-product goals into small ethical experiments, scorecards, monetization plans, and 7-day action plans. It is intended for educational planning and coaching, not regulated financial, investment, tax, legal, credit, or mental-health advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat coaching output as financial, investment, tax, legal, credit, or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational planning frameworks, keep regulated advice out of scope, and recommend qualified professionals for high-stakes or regulated decisions.

Risk: Users may disclose sensitive exact financial details unnecessarily.

Mitigation: Ask for exact numbers only when they change the decision; otherwise use ranges and keep the request limited to the planning need.

Risk: Revenue, ROI, audience growth, or platform payout projections may be mistaken for facts.

Mitigation: State assumptions clearly, use small reversible experiments, and evaluate progress with real customer behavior such as payment, usage, renewal, referral, or qualified commitments.
