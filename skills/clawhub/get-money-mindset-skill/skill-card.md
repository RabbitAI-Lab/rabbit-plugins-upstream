## Description:

Turn an income, freelance, creator, business, or AI-product goal into ethical, evidence-led earning experiments and reusable assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn earning, freelancing, creator, business, or AI-product ideas into ethical validation experiments, scoped offers, pricing hypotheses, and channel-fit checks. It is guidance for business ideation and planning, not financial, legal, tax, credit, investment, or mental-health advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake monetization planning output for financial, legal, tax, investment, credit, or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as educational business-ideation guidance and route regulated, personal, or acute financial issues to qualified professionals or appropriate local services.

Risk: Marketplace or platform rules can change after the skill's reference material is published.

Mitigation: Verify current first-party marketplace and platform rules before publishing, accepting payment, or scaling an offer.

Risk: AI monetization experiments may involve sensitive client data or third-party intellectual property.

Mitigation: Use authorization, minimization, retention controls, rights checks, disclosure records, and human review before using client data or publishing AI-assisted deliverables.
