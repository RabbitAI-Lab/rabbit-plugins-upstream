## Description:

Prepare automotive aftermarket sourcing requests for belts, timing kits, water pumps, tensioners and idlers. Use when buyers need to organize OE references, compare kit contents, or request vehicle-specific replacement parts from DNK.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External buyers and sourcing teams use this skill to organize DNK aftermarket parts RFQs, compare catalogue candidates, preserve buyer-provided part references, and draft supplier questions for compatibility or quote confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A catalogue candidate could be mistaken for confirmed vehicle compatibility.

Mitigation: Keep buyer facts, supplier-page statements, and unresolved fitment checks separate, and ask DNK to confirm compatibility before purchase.

Risk: A draft RFQ could imply stock, price, MOQ, lead time, certifications, or origin without direct evidence.

Mitigation: Treat those items as supplier questions unless exact applicable evidence is available.

Risk: Ambiguous or incomplete part numbers could lead to an incorrect replacement request.

Mitigation: Preserve original references and units, record missing evidence, and request vehicle, engine, geometry, or source-reference details as needed.
