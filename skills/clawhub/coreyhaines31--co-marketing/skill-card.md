## Description: <br>
Helps SaaS teams identify co-marketing partners, evaluate fit, plan joint campaigns, structure outreach and agreements, and measure campaign results. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, partnerships, and go-to-market teams use this skill to find complementary SaaS partners, prioritize partner opportunities, design joint campaigns, draft partner outreach, and plan lead-sharing and measurement practices. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Co-marketing planning can involve lead lists, account-overlap data, campaign metrics, or customer information that may be sensitive or contractually restricted. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm company policy, partner agreements, and privacy obligations before sharing data; prefer aggregated or minimized data unless identifiable information has a clear agreement and lawful basis. <br>
