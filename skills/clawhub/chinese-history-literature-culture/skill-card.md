## Description:

Writes, polishes, fact-checks, and cites Simplified Chinese articles on Chinese history, literature, and culture from a user's outline or draft while preserving the user's structure and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to turn Chinese history, literature, and culture notes or drafts into structure-faithful Chinese articles with source checking, citations, and conditional correction tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical or literary claims and citations may be incorrect if sources are unavailable, derivative, or do not support the drafted claim.

Mitigation: Open cited sources, verify the supporting passage, prefer primary texts, cross-check contested claims with independent reliable sources, and move unverifiable items to the corrections workflow.

Risk: The skill may propose article, corrections-document, or README changes inside a writing repository.

Mitigation: Review proposed edits and factual corrections before accepting them, especially when they replace or qualify the user's original claims.
