## Description:

Write, polish, fact-check, and cite Chinese-language articles about Chinese history, literature, and culture while preserving the user's structure and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to turn outlines, notes, or drafts into polished Simplified Chinese articles with web-researched citations, citation audits, and factual correction notes when claims cannot be verified.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may browse sources, open cited material, and create or update article and corrections files in a writing repository.

Mitigation: Review final citations, proposed factual corrections, and any file changes before publishing.

Risk: Historical or literary claims can be incorrect, unverifiable, or supported by weak citations.

Mitigation: Require opened-source verification for citations and move unverifiable claims into a corrections note rather than asserting them.
