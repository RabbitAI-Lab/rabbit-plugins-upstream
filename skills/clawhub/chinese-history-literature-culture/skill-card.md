## Description:

Helps draft, polish, fact-check, and cite Chinese-language articles on Chinese history, literature, and culture while preserving the writer's outline and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to turn their notes or drafts into sourced Chinese-language articles, or to polish and check existing articles without changing their intended structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default Simplified Chinese output may not match the reader's preferred script or language.

Mitigation: State the desired script or language before drafting.

Risk: Corrections documents or README edits may need editorial review.

Mitigation: Review any generated corrections documents and README changes before accepting them.
