## Description:

Write, polish, and cite Chinese-language articles on Chinese history, literature, and culture while preserving the user's structure and voice and grounding claims in checked sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers use this skill to turn their outlines, notes, or drafts into polished Chinese articles on Chinese history, literature, and culture. It supports research, source selection, citation checking, and fact-checking while preserving the writer's thought-flow and voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A cited source may not support the historical or literary claim in the draft.

Mitigation: Open each source and verify the exact passage; leave unsupported or conflicting claims for the corrections list.

Risk: Source-selection preferences and repository conventions may need to fit the article repository.

Mitigation: Use the skill within the intended article repository and adapt its source preferences and conventions to the repository's guidance.
