## Description:

Helps write, polish, fact-check, and cite Chinese-language articles on Chinese history, literature, and culture while preserving the writer's outline and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to turn their notes or drafts into sourced Chinese-language articles, polish existing prose, or check historical and literary citations without changing their intended structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical claims, quotations, or citations may be inaccurate or misattributed.

Mitigation: Check claims against the cited primary text or reliable sources, name the edition where relevant, and flag unverifiable claims instead of asserting them.

Risk: A proposed factual correction could change the writer's meaning or an existing draft without approval.

Mitigation: Present the discrepancy and proposed wording for the writer's review before applying corrections.

Risk: The default Simplified Chinese output may not match the writer's preferred script.

Mitigation: Confirm the preferred script when it is unclear or Simplified Chinese is not wanted.
