## Description:

Guides agents to monitor reasoning, calibrate confidence, and change strategies when users are stuck or checking their thinking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use the skill to work through difficult or recurring problems, check whether their confidence is justified, and decide when to change approach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
