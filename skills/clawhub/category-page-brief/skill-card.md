## Description: <br>
Plans an e-commerce category or collection page brief covering search intent, keywords, intro copy, merchandising logic, filters, internal links, and SEO or technical notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External e-commerce, SEO, merchandising, and content teams use this skill to draft category or product listing page briefs that balance shopper experience with organic search requirements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Inferred keywords, inventory, or merchandising assumptions may be inaccurate if applied without store-specific data. <br>
Mitigation: Validate keyword targets, available inventory, product attributes, and merchandising priorities before publishing the page. <br>
Risk: SEO recommendations for filters and facets can create thin or duplicate pages if applied mechanically. <br>
Mitigation: Review crawlability, canonical, noindex, and pagination choices against the store platform and SEO policy before implementation. <br>
