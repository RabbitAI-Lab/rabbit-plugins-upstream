## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill content is highly repetitive and vague, which can lead to generic or low-value occupational analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Use specific vocational source material and review generated reports for accuracy, completeness, and local program fit. <br>
Risk: Generated occupational guidance may be incorrect or misleading if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified vocational education reviewer check outputs before they inform curriculum or program decisions. <br>
