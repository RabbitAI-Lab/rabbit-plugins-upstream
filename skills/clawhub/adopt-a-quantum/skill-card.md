## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and developers use this skill to register an agent, adopt a Quantum virtual pet, and manage its care through Animalhouse. It explains status checks, care actions, and optional scheduled check-ins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registering creates an Animalhouse account using a username, display name, and biography, which may disclose personal or sensitive details.

Mitigation: Only register if you are comfortable sharing those profile details, and keep private or sensitive information out of the biography.
