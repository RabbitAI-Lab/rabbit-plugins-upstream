## Description: <br>
Convert markdown articles to WeChat public account formatted HTML using mdnice themes, code highlighting, and Mac-style rendering. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shengbinxu](https://clawhub.ai/user/shengbinxu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and content authors use this skill to convert markdown drafts into WeChat public account HTML with inline styles suitable for copy-paste into the WeChat editor. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Markdown content may be sent through mdnice's online rendering flow. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for content acceptable to process externally; choose a local or offline formatter for confidential drafts, internal links, customer data, credentials, or unpublished business material. <br>
Risk: Generated WeChat HTML may preserve local image references and external links that are not publication-ready. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated HTML before publishing and replace unsupported links or image references with WeChat-compatible assets. <br>
