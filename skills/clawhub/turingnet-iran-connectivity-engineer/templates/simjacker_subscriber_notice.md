# Subscriber security notice draft

We are reviewing a potential SIM/security issue. We will share confirmed information through official channels.

What you can do now:
- Keep your device and carrier settings updated through official sources.
- Do not share passwords, one-time codes, or SIM/account details with unsolicited contacts.
- If your SIM unexpectedly stops working or you see unusual carrier-related behavior, contact our official support channel.
- Do not install unknown apps, profiles, certificates, or configuration tools.

This notice does not confirm individual impact. Please use the official case reference if one is provided.
