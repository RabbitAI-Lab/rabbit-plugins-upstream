# Personal digital-safety and privacy hygiene

- [ ] Device lock and automatic lock are enabled
- [ ] Operating system and official apps are updated
- [ ] Unique passwords are used; recovery information is reviewed
- [ ] Strong official account authentication is enabled where available
- [ ] Unneeded/unverified apps, extensions, and profiles are reviewed or removed
- [ ] Shared screenshots/documents are redacted
- [ ] No passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, SIM identifiers, precise location, or session data were shared
- [ ] Suspicious messages are verified through an independent official channel
- [ ] Possible compromise is escalated through official service support or qualified local assistance

This checklist is for lawful personal safety and privacy hygiene. It is not a guide to evade surveillance, official oversight, or legal obligations.
