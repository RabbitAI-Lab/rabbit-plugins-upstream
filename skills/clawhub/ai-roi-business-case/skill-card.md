## Description:

Helps teams assess an AI project's return on investment by measuring its baseline, realizable value, full costs, adoption, payback, and downside scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders, operators, and teams use this skill to decide whether to fund, scale, or stop an AI tool or workflow. It structures a one-page business case around measured baselines, realizable value, full costs, scenario analysis, payback, and a pre-agreed kill line.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business estimates may be misleading if assumptions or costs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Use aggregated business figures where possible and review assumptions, including human review effort and downside scenarios, before committing money.
