## Description:

Standardizes the visual design, layout, color system, chart treatment, table formatting, and data-discipline checks for client-facing BI dashboards and business deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External and internal business teams use this skill when asking an agent to produce or review BI dashboards, reports, presentations, spreadsheets, PDFs, HTML pages, and similar client-facing materials that need a consistent Morandi business visual standard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill strongly steers visual style and may override a user's target language, brand system, or non-procurement design requirements.

Mitigation: Tell the agent to preserve the target language, brand rules, and domain-specific visual requirements whenever they differ from this skill.

Risk: Strict visual and data-discipline rules may cause the agent to flag estimates, conflicting source data, or unclear definitions instead of smoothing them into a finished-looking deliverable.

Mitigation: Confirm estimation rules, data definitions, and conflict-handling expectations before asking the agent to finalize the deliverable.
