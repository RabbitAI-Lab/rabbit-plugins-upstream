## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source material may include under-disclosed ads, personal contact prompts, or unrelated renovation advice.

Mitigation: Filter responses to the user's cabinet customization or renovation question and avoid repeating ads, contact solicitations, or unrelated material.

Risk: Some advice may touch safety-sensitive renovation work such as demolition, electrical planning, waterproofing, load-bearing walls, or slip and child safety.

Mitigation: Present safety items as review checklists and direct users to confirm with qualified professionals, property management, and applicable local rules before work begins.

Risk: The skill provides practical renovation guidance, not neutral product evaluation or a verified construction specification.

Mitigation: Encourage users to compare recommendations against contractor quotes, product specifications, site conditions, and budget constraints before making purchasing or construction decisions.
