## Description: <br>
Helps software maintainers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams add useful unit tests, improve test coverage, and verify regression risk in existing codebases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, and product teams use this skill to plan and implement unit-test coverage improvements for existing codebases. It produces practical workflows, checklists, implementation support, and verification notes so teams can raise coverage without losing sight of useful regression protection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad testing and quality triggers may activate the skill for requests that are not specifically about unit-test coverage. <br>
Mitigation: Review invocation settings before deployment and tighten trigger wording if the skill should activate only for explicit unit-test coverage work. <br>
