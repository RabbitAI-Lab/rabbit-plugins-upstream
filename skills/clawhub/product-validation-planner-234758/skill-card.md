## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn product ideas into validated plans, prototype steps, positioning, and practical decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps structure assumptions, validation steps, lightweight prototypes, checklists, positioning, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger language may activate the skill for general business or startup conversations where product validation support was not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or tighter trigger wording when routing precision matters. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs can depend on incomplete user-provided assumptions and may overstate confidence in product demand. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for materially missing context, state assumptions clearly, and validate outputs against the user's success criteria. <br>
