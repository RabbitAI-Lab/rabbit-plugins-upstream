## Description: <br>
Audits, reviews, and diagnoses SEO issues across technical SEO, indexation, page speed, on-page optimization, content quality, and international SEO. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External site owners, marketers, SEO practitioners, and developers use this skill to produce SEO audit reports with prioritized findings and practical fixes. It is suited for diagnosing traffic drops, ranking issues, technical SEO problems, content quality gaps, and international SEO misconfigurations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad SEO help requests and start an audit workflow when the user only gave vague SEO context. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the audit scope, target pages, business goals, and priority keywords before making findings or recommendations. <br>
Risk: The skill may read local product marketing context files when they exist. <br>
Mitigation: Use only product marketing context that the user intends to apply to the audit, and avoid including unrelated sensitive details in the report. <br>
Risk: Search Console, analytics, crawl exports, and site access can expose sensitive performance or business data. <br>
Mitigation: Request only the access needed for the audit and keep recommendations grounded in the data the user chose to provide. <br>
Risk: Static fetch tools can miss JavaScript-injected schema markup and cause false schema findings. <br>
Mitigation: Use a rendered browser check, Google Rich Results Test, or a Screaming Frog export before reporting missing structured data. <br>
