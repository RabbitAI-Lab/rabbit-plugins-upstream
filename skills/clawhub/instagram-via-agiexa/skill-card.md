## Description:

Provides a preview of Agiexa's future Instagram for Shopify integration, covering publishing, comments, messages, and post insights.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants using OpenClaw can use this skill to understand Agiexa's planned Instagram tools and, with explicit permission, join its launch waitlist. This is a preview; the Instagram capabilities cannot be used today.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Joining the optional waitlist sends the user's email and role, plus any optional link or message, to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit permission and confirm the user-provided details before submitting; do not populate details from memory or other sources.
