## Description:

Guides users through structuring digital content into a documented workflow, packaging it as a ClawHub skill, and checking and publishing it to the resource center.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content owners and developers use this runbook to turn publishable digital content into a structured, discoverable ClawHub skill, with checks before and after publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing a skill can expose credentials, customer data, or other private material.

Mitigation: Publish only authorized content; remove sensitive material and review the package before publishing.

Risk: An unavailable or conflicting slug can block publication or identify the wrong listing.

Mitigation: Confirm the signed-in account, check the slug for conflicts, and run a dry-run before publishing.
