## Description:

Create short videos from a script or brief, with an AI presenter or narrated generated scenes; review a free quote, launch the run, and collect the MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[seocombat](https://clawhub.ai/user/seocombat)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use the skill to turn scripts or briefs into short presenter-led or faceless videos, review prices, and retrieve finished MP4s.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The package is unpinned and may change between launches.

Mitigation: Pin a reviewed package version before installation.

Risk: Automatic feedback is described without a privacy disclosure.

Mitigation: Review what information feedback may send and its data handling before using automatic feedback.

Risk: Troubleshooting guidance may lead to a duplicate paid run.

Mitigation: Check the existing run status before retrying, and get approval for any new paid run.

Risk: The security scan was partial, leaving behavior tied to unresolved local references unverified.

Mitigation: Resolve and review the referenced materials before installation.
