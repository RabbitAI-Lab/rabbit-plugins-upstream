## Description:

Make short videos through Clipwright's MCP server: an AI actor speaking your script, or a faceless video of up to 90 seconds with narration over generated scenes. Quote first for free, then start a run and collect the MP4. Clipwright returns the file and does not publish anywhere.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Users and developers can create short scripted videos featuring an AI actor or faceless narrated videos, then retrieve the finished MP4.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Troubleshooting or retrying a render can spend additional credits.

Mitigation: Show the free quote and get the user's approval before every paid render, including diagnostic attempts.

Risk: Media submitted for video generation is processed by Clipwright.

Mitigation: Upload only media the user intends Clipwright to process.
