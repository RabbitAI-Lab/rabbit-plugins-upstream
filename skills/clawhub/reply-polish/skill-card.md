## Description:

Polishes existing Chinese workplace replies to sound natural and professional while preserving the writer's meaning and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stevenwangking](https://clawhub.ai/user/stevenwangking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and other professionals use this skill to refine drafted Chinese messages to colleagues, managers, product teams, or customers without changing their intent or voice. It does not draft messages from scratch or polish English text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Polishing could inadvertently change a commitment, technical detail, or the writer's intended stance.

Mitigation: Compare the revision with the draft before sending, especially facts, numbers, dates, terminology, and level of certainty.

Risk: A customer-facing reply could disclose internal terms or sensitive context.

Mitigation: Check the recipient and review customer-facing wording for internal details before sending.
