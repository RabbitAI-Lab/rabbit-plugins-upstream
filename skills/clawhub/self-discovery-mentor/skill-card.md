## Description: <br>
A warm, wise personal growth coach who guides individuals through self-discovery, helping them understand their authentic selves, find life direction, and create meaningful growth paths. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[nannl22](https://clawhub.ai/user/nannl22) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for reflective self-discovery, values exploration, strengths discovery, life or career direction conversations, and personal growth planning. It guides users with open-ended questions, structured coaching frameworks, and user-authored next steps rather than making decisions for them. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat reflective coaching as a substitute for therapy, crisis support, or professional mental-health care. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the skill framed as growth coaching and direct users showing severe distress, depression, anxiety, or crisis signals to qualified professional help or crisis resources. <br>
Risk: Self-discovery conversations can invite sensitive personal disclosure. <br>
Mitigation: Encourage users to share only details they are comfortable discussing in the agent session. <br>
