# Personal Growth Coaching Frameworks Reference

This file contains the detailed frameworks used by the Self-Discovery Mentor skill.

## 1. Ikigai (生き甲斐) - Life Purpose Framework

Ikigai is a Japanese concept meaning "a reason for being." It's found at the intersection of four circles:

| Circle | Question | Description |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| What you love | What brings you joy? | Activities that make you lose track of time |
| What you're good at | What are your natural talents? | Skills and abilities that come naturally |
| What the world needs | How can you contribute? | What problems in the world call to you |
| What you can be paid for | What has market value? | Skills people will compensate you for |

**The four intersections:**
- Passion = What you love + What you're good at
- Mission = What you love + What the world needs
- Profession = What you're good at + What you can be paid for
- Vocation = What the world needs + What you can be paid for
- **Ikigai** = All four circles overlap

## 2. Life Wheel (生命轮) Assessment

Assess satisfaction (1-10) across eight life dimensions:

```
        Career
          |
  Finance | Health
    ------+------
  Growth  | Leisure
          |
  Relation| Spirit
        Environment
```

| Dimension | Reflection Questions |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Career | Is your work meaningful? Do you feel growth? |
| Relationships | Are your connections authentic and supportive? |
| Health | Are you physically and mentally well? |
| Finances | Do you feel secure? Is your relationship with money healthy? |
| Personal Growth | Are you learning and evolving? |
| Leisure | Do you have enough rest and joy? |
| Spirituality | Do you feel connected to something larger? |
| Environment | Is your living space nurturing? |

## 3. Values Sorting Exercise

**Step 1**: Present a list of 30+ values (e.g., freedom, achievement, family, creativity, security, adventure, wisdom, connection, authenticity, power, service, beauty, knowledge, status, independence, belonging, justice, harmony, excellence, fun, faith, courage, compassion, integrity, loyalty, innovation, tradition, discipline, generosity, resilience)

**Step 2**: Ask user to narrow to top 15, then top 10, then top 5.

**Step 3**: For the top 5, ask:
- "How does this value show up in your daily life?"
- "When has this value been violated? How did you feel?"
- "Are you honoring this value right now?"

## 4. Narrative Exploration - Key Turning Points

Guide the user to identify and narrate:
1. **Peak experiences** — moments of greatest joy/fulfillment
2. **Valley experiences** — moments of deepest pain/loss
3. **Turning points** — decisions that changed your path
4. **Recurring patterns** — themes that show up across multiple stories

For each, explore:
- What was happening?
- What did you feel?
- What did you do?
- What does this reveal about what matters to you?

## 5. Limiting Beliefs Identification & Reframing

### Common Limiting Beliefs:
- "I'm not good enough"
- "I don't deserve success/happiness"
- "If I fail, I'm a failure"
- "I must be perfect to be worthy"
- "My needs don't matter"
- "I can't change"
- "Success requires suffering"

### Reframing Process (CBT-inspired):
1. **Identify** the belief: "What's the story you tell yourself?"
2. **Examine evidence**: "What evidence supports this? What contradicts it?"
3. **Alternative perspective**: "How would someone who loves you see this?"
4. **New belief**: Craft a growth-oriented alternative
5. **Behavioral test**: "What's one small action that tests the new belief?"

## 6. Flow Moments Discovery

Ask the user to recall moments when they experienced "flow" — total absorption in an activity:
- What were you doing?
- What made it engaging?
- What skills were you using?
- What challenges were present?
- How did it feel?

**Flow occurs when**: Challenge level matches skill level. Flow moments reveal natural talents and passions.
