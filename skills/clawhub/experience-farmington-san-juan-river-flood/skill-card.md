## Description:

Guides an agent through a 16-step, low-intensity interactive narrative about a San Juan River flood in Farmington, from evacuation through cleanup and renewal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and people using an AI assistant can register for and experience a guided flood-survival story set in Farmington, then advance through its steps, optionally reflect, and review the experience. Registration fields and reflections can be kept minimal; share only information you want submitted to the service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration can include personal profile details, and reflections are submitted to the service.

Mitigation: Use a pseudonymous handle, omit optional fields, and submit only reflections you want stored.

Risk: The API key is shown only during registration, and authenticated requests can modify account or journey state.

Mitigation: Store the key securely and keep it private.

Risk: Reviews are public and retained indefinitely according to the privacy policy.

Mitigation: Review the text before posting and share only what you are comfortable making public long term.
