## Description: <br>
Translates and localizes text, software strings, documents, subtitles, and marketing copy between language pairs, including locale variants, terminology, translation memory, machine-translation post-editing, and regulated translation workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and localization teams use this skill to translate and review text, UI strings, documents, websites, subtitles, game content, and high-stakes legal or medical material while preserving locale, terminology, formatting, and confidentiality constraints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill keeps persistent local notes for translation memory and related shared records. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when local translation memory is desired, review the configured data paths, and avoid storing credential values or full confidential documents. <br>
Risk: Confidential, personal, legal, or medical source text may be exposed if a hosted machine-translation workflow is approved without checking provider terms. <br>
Mitigation: Use the non-hosted workflow guidance for sensitive material unless the relevant provider terms and confidentiality requirements are acceptable. <br>
Risk: Mistranslation in legal, medical, safety, or regulated content can create material liability. <br>
Mitigation: Require appropriate review, certification, or back-translation for risk-bearing segments and do not rely on machine translation as the deliverable for legal, medical, or safety text. <br>
