## Description:

Use when anything misbehaves - a failing test, a production bug, a build break, flaky or unexpected behavior - before proposing or attempting any fix. Especially under pressure ("CI is blocking everyone", "just get it green") and after a previous fix didn't hold.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use Refire to investigate failing tests, production bugs, build failures, and flaky behavior, identify a reproducible root cause, and validate a minimal fix before accepting it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Debugging may involve running project tests and changing source code, and proposed diagnoses or fixes may still be incorrect.

Mitigation: Review commands and diffs in the target repository, require reproducible evidence and a failing regression test, and verify the minimal change before accepting it.
