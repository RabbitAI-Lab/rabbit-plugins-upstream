## Use Case: <br>
Quality, operations, and audit teams in automotive organizations use this skill to plan, draft, and review IATF16949 quality-management-system materials. It supports initial QMS setup, certification preparation, process gap analysis, internal audit planning, and process improvement discussions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated planning guidance or templates may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific organization's quality system, customer requirements, or regulatory context. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as drafting support and have qualified quality, audit, legal, or regulatory reviewers approve materials before adoption. <br>
Risk: The skill can help prepare for certification activities, but it cannot replace an accredited certification body or formal audit decision. <br>
Mitigation: Coordinate certification scope, audit scheduling, and final compliance determinations with the appropriate certification body. <br>
