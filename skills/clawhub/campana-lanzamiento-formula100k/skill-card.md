## Description:

Plans complete Spanish-language launch campaigns for Skool communities, courses, digital products, and services using the F100K methodology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and launch operators use this skill to turn a product brief into positioning, landing-page copy, email or DM drafts, reel scripts, story sequences, sales-audio scripts, and a launch calendar. It is intended for Spanish-language campaign planning and review, with human approval before scheduling or sending.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Launch dates, times, prices, and offers may be missing or incorrect if the user's business context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact offer, dates, times, and pricing with the user before finalizing the campaign or scheduling reminders.

Risk: The skill may invoke companion F100K skills as part of the launch workflow.

Mitigation: Review the outputs and security posture of companion skills separately before relying on them in a release workflow.

Risk: Marketing drafts could overstate claims or create unsupported urgency.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's quality gate and require evidence for campaign claims, proof points, scarcity, and deadlines before publication.
