## Description:

Build a short-term (13-week) cash flow forecast to see if you can cover what's due, using user-provided figures or labelled placeholders to produce a week-by-week forecast structure with formulas, a worked example, assumptions, key cash-crunch read-outs, and practical levers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, business operators, finance teams, and advisors use this skill to structure a short-term cash-flow forecast around expected receipts, scheduled payments, assumptions, low-point analysis, and liquidity actions. It is a planning worksheet, not financial, investment, or accounting advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat projected balances or cash-crunch analysis as guaranteed outcomes or professional financial advice.

Mitigation: State assumptions clearly, mark placeholders, surface low points and negative weeks, and advise review with a qualified accountant or advisor for material decisions.

Risk: Business cash-flow figures entered into an agent session may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Use only the figures needed for the forecast and handle business financial data according to the user's confidentiality requirements.
