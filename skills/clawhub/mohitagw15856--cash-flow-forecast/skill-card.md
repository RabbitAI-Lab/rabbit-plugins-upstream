## Description: <br>
Builds a short-term cash flow forecast that lays out opening cash, expected inflows, scheduled outflows, net movement, closing cash, low points, assumptions, and practical levers when cash goes tight. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business operators, finance teams, founders, and advisors use this skill to structure a 13-week cash flow forecast from provided figures or clearly labelled placeholders so they can identify tight cash periods and planning levers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cash-flow forecasts may include sensitive business figures. <br>
Mitigation: Use only figures the user provides or clearly labelled placeholders, and avoid exposing confidential financial data unnecessarily. <br>
Risk: Forecast outputs can be mistaken for financial, investment, or accounting advice. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions clearly, label projections as planning aids, and direct users to a qualified accountant or advisor for material decisions. <br>
