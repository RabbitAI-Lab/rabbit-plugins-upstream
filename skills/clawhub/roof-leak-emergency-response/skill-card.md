## Description:

Guides people through active roof-leak triage, water containment, source tracing, safe escalation, insurance documentation, and drying decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners, renters, and their assisting agents use this skill to prioritize immediate safety, contain and trace a roof leak, decide when to call professionals, document damage accurately, and plan drying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: DIY work around wet wiring, sagging ceilings, or hazardous roof access can cause serious injury.

Mitigation: Avoid the affected area and roof access when unsafe; involve an electrician, emergency roofer, or emergency services as appropriate.

Risk: Wet materials, suspected mold, or contaminated water may require professional handling.

Mitigation: Seek qualified remediation advice, especially for older building materials or respiratory risks.

Risk: Insurance guidance could encourage incomplete or misleading claim descriptions.

Mitigation: Report facts accurately and preserve photographs, receipts, and a timeline; confirm coverage with the insurer.
