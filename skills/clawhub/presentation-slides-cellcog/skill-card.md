This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, business users, and agents use this skill to ask CellCog to generate presentation decks such as pitch decks, keynotes, business reviews, training slides, and image slideshows in PDF, PPTX, or interactive HTML. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, uploaded files, business metrics, and generated presentation content are shared with CellCog as an external provider. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for content that may be shared with CellCog under the user's data-handling requirements. <br>
Risk: Research-backed claims, metrics, charts, and presentation text may be incorrect or unsupported. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated decks and verify claims, figures, and sources before presenting or distributing them. <br>
Risk: The skill requires python3, the cellcog dependency, and CELLCOG_API_KEY to generate decks. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the runtime and credential setup before relying on the skill in a delivery workflow. <br>
