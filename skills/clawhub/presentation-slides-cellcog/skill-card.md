## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, outlines, metrics, attachments, and generated deck content may be processed by CellCog.

Mitigation: Do not include confidential, regulated, customer, financial, or investor-sensitive material unless the organization has approved CellCog for that data.

Risk: Generated presentation content may contain incorrect, outdated, or misleading information.

Mitigation: Review generated decks, claims, charts, and source material before sharing or presenting them.
