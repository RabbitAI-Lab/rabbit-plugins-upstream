## Description:

Content Marketer helps agents design content marketing strategies and topic plans from product positioning, including content matrices, distribution plans, and performance tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and content operators use this skill to turn product positioning into a concrete content strategy, topic calendar, platform distribution plan, and measurement framework.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing plans may include unsupported business claims, platform metrics, or competitive analysis.

Mitigation: Review plans before publishing and verify claims, metrics, and competitive statements against current business evidence.
