## Description: <br>
Simulate the exact customer who will quietly cancel in month 4: their internal monologue through the lifecycle, the honest exit interview they never gave, and a debrief with early churn signals and interventions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, customer success teams, founders, and growth teams use this skill to simulate a plausible silent churn customer for a specific product and onboarding path. It helps them identify early behavioral signals and concrete onboarding interventions to validate against real churned-customer interviews. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The generated customer narrative may be mistaken for factual churn analysis or used as a basis for product or business decisions without validation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as ideation and interview-planning material, and validate findings against real customer data and churned-customer interviews before acting. <br>
Risk: Missing product or onboarding details can cause the skill to fill gaps with assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the product, price point, buyer, onboarding path, and any real churn profile; when information is missing, require assumptions to be labeled. <br>
