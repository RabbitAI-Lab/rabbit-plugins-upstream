## Description:

Turn text into natural speech or a voice-over with a Wiro AI text to speech model. Use when the user wants text read aloud, a voice-over or narration made with Wiro AI or through the Wiro MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can turn supplied text into speech, narration, or a voice-over using a Wiro AI text-to-speech model.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Text supplied for speech generation may be sent to Wiro AI, creating a possible privacy disclosure.

Mitigation: Avoid sending confidential or sensitive material unless you are authorized to share it.
