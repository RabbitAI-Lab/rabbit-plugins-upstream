## Description: <br>
Helps agents turn project retrospectives, expert interviews, workflows, policies, product knowledge, prior courseware, training notes, or course topics into structured enterprise training-course materials. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cylunjoe](https://clawhub.ai/user/cylunjoe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
CC BY-NC 4.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External training developers and internal enablement teams use this skill to convert source materials into course direction, extracted methods, teaching cases, exercises, quizzes, PPT page drafts, and instructor-guide content. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-supplied documents read by the helper may be printed into the agent conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Use only intended training materials, avoid unrelated private files, and review generated course content before sharing. <br>
Risk: Generated course methods, cases, exercises, or external expansions may contain inaccurate, inferred, or simulated material if not reviewed. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve source labels such as original material, reasonable inference, simulated case, or external expansion, and verify sensitive standards, policies, data, and cases before use. <br>
