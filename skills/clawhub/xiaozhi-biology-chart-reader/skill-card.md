## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through biology charts, diagrams, tables, and source-based questions by identifying visual evidence before applying textbook concepts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese middle- and high-school students use this tutor to interpret biology graphs, diagrams, pedigrees, tables, and reading passages, then support their answers with evidence from the question. High-school elective content is tailored to students taking elective biology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize language, curriculum, emergency resources, and minor-consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum guidance or crisis referrals may not fit locations outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the language, curriculum, emergency resources, and minor-consent rules before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Optional learning-profile memory, reminders, and cross-skill sharing involve student data.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit consent for each optional action and provide view, correction, deletion, pause, sharing-control, and export choices.
