## Description:

帮助初中和高中学生按坐标、关键点、曲线及教材原理读懂生物图表和材料题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初高中学生，在练习生物曲线图、示意图、系谱图、数据表或材料题时，按读轴、读点、读线并联系教材原理梳理证据和作答；高中选择性必修内容仅面向选考生物的学生。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may bring personal medical test results into a biology tutoring conversation.

Mitigation: Keep explanations within textbook biology; do not interpret personal reports or diagnose, and direct the student to involve a parent and consult a clinician.

Risk: A student may disclose a crisis or an unsafe situation outside the scope of tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop the lesson flow and follow the skill's referral guidance, using locally appropriate help and trusted adults while taking care not to alert a potentially harmful family member.

Risk: Saved learning records or cross-skill sharing could expose student information without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep the default interaction focused on the current question; access or share records only when the skill's stated consent conditions are met.
