## Description:

陪学生按看轴、看点、看线、联系原理的步骤，读懂生物曲线、示意图、系谱图、数据表和材料题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this tutoring agent to interpret biology charts and source-based questions step by step, connect observations to course concepts, and compose answers grounded in the figure or passage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent learning records or reminders may involve student data, including data from minors.

Mitigation: Use current-question mode by default; enable memory or reminders only after checking consent, access, and deletion controls for the associated services.

Risk: Students may ask the tutor to interpret personal or family medical test results.

Mitigation: Do not interpret or diagnose personal medical reports; direct the student to tell a parent or guardian and consult a clinician.
