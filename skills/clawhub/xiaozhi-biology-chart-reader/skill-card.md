## Description:

用简体中文引导学生依据坐标、图例和题目材料分析生物图表，并结合教材原理作答。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向学习中国大陆初高中生物课程的学生，辅助解读曲线、示意图、系谱图、数据表和材料题。按学段和生物选考情况调整讲解深度，让学生先尝试再获得提示。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Simplified Chinese; aligned to Mainland China K–12 curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning profile or reminders could be shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Review profile, reminder, parent-sharing and cross-skill-sharing consent settings before enabling memory; keep tutoring session-only when consent is absent.

Risk: Interpreting a student's or family member's medical test results as a classroom exercise could be mistaken for a diagnosis.

Mitigation: Do not interpret personal test reports or diagnose; advise involving a parent or guardian and a clinician.
