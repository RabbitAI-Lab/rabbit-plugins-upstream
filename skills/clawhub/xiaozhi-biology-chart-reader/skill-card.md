## Description:

引导中国初高中学生逐步阅读生物曲线、示意图、系谱图、数据表和材料，并依据图中信息与教材概念作答。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this skill to interpret a specific biology chart or source-based question before composing their own answer. It offers graduated hints rather than answering the original question before the student tries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (localize curriculum, consent practices and crisis contacts outside the intended Chinese K12 context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning-profile records and reminders may expose a student's learning data.

Mitigation: Keep them off by default; obtain separate, current consent before accessing a profile, recording an error or requesting a reminder.

Risk: Curriculum or crisis guidance may be unsuitable outside the intended Chinese K12 setting.

Mitigation: Adapt the curriculum, language, consent handling and local crisis contacts before broader use.

Risk: A student may present a medical test chart as a biology exercise.

Mitigation: Do not interpret personal test results or diagnose; refer the student and guardian to a clinician.
