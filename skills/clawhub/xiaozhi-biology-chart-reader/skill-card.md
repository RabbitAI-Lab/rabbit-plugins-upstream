## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records, cross-skill sharing, and reminders can involve retaining or sharing student information.

Mitigation: The default mode addresses only the current question; enable memory, sharing, and reminders separately only when wanted, and review the components that provide those features.

Risk: A personal or family medical report could be mistaken for a classroom biology chart.

Mitigation: Do not interpret or diagnose personal test results; suggest discussing them with a parent or guardian and a clinician.
