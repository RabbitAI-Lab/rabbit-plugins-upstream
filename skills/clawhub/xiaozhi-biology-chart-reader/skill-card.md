## Description:

引导初高中学生读懂生物曲线、示意图、系谱图、数据表和材料题，先核对图表信息，再用教材原理解释。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to interpret biology diagrams, graphs, tables and passages for the question at hand. It prompts students to cite visual evidence and develop their own answers before receiving further hints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's study profile, cross-skill sharing or reminders could be enabled without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Review profile, sharing, parent-sharing and reminder consent settings before enabling these features; keep them off by default.

Risk: Unreadable diagrams may lead to unsupported interpretations.

Mitigation: Ask the student to describe axes, labels and key points instead of guessing from an unclear image.

Risk: A student may submit personal medical results instead of a textbook biology chart.

Mitigation: Do not interpret or diagnose from test results; direct the student to a parent or guardian and a clinician.
