## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students through interpreting biology graphs, diagrams, pedigrees, tables, and passage-based questions before answering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to interpret biology charts and source materials, connect observations to curriculum concepts, and practice answering the current question without receiving the original answer first.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12 curriculum context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional student learning profiles, parent sharing, cross-skill sharing, and reminders involve student records.

Mitigation: Review and enable each sharing, profile, and reminder setting only with appropriate consent; keep them off when not needed.

Risk: The Chinese-language mainland-China K12 framing may not fit other learners or curricula.

Mitigation: Use with learners and educators comfortable with that language and curriculum context.
