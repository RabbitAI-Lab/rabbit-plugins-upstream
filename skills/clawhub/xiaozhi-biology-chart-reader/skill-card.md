## Description:

帮助初高中学生读懂生物曲线、示意图、系谱图、数据表与材料，并依据图中证据和教材原理完成解题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆中文教学场景的初高中学生，逐步识读当前生物题目的图表或材料，找出依据后作答；错题交接和回看提醒须另行获得同意。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12); localize safety referrals and confirm consent controls before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning profile, cross-skill handoffs or reminders could be used without informed consent.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent controls are enabled; keep profile access, individual handoffs and reminder requests separately opt-in.

Risk: Mainland China crisis-referral guidance may be inappropriate in another location.

Mitigation: Confirm the learner's region and localize referral channels before deploying outside mainland China.
