## Description:

Guides creators in assessing and drafting short-form video and social post openings grounded in the source material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo knowledge creators use this skill to check whether a draft has enough supporting material, compare opening-hook approaches, and refine an opening without making claims the content cannot support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow directs feedback into shared writing rules, while its referenced external update procedure is unverified.

Mitigation: Review the shared-framework update behavior, save feedback only in a designated preference file, and require approval before changing shared rules.
