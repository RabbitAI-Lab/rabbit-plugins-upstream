## Description:

Assess product links, compare prices by model, and recommend products based on a shopper’s stated needs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fenxianglife](https://clawhub.ai/user/fenxianglife)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External shoppers use the skill to evaluate product links, compare prices by model, find lower-priced options across supported marketplaces, and get recommendations based on a budget or need.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping requests and follow-up answers are sent to an external service without a user-facing privacy notice, according to the security summary.

Mitigation: Tell users before use that their shopping requests and follow-up answers are sent to Fenxiang’s service, and avoid including sensitive information.

Risk: The security summary says one referenced file was only partly analyzed, so the available scan evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the partly analyzed file and complete a security assessment before relying on the skill.
