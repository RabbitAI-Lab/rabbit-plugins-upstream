## Description:

Turn flat resume bullets ('Responsible for X') into quantified STAR-method achievement narratives, score resumes against job descriptions for ATS keyword coverage and weak-phrase hazards, and expand each bullet into interview-ready stories with likely follow-up questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Job seekers, career changers, interview preppers, and agents assisting them use this skill to strengthen resume bullets, compare a resume with a target job posting, and prepare STAR-style interview stories from truthful resume claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume and job-description files can contain personal or sensitive career information.

Mitigation: Process only files the user intentionally provides, avoid unrelated sensitive personal information, and keep processing local unless the user explicitly chooses otherwise.

Risk: Generated resume bullets or metric suggestions can become misleading if the user adds numbers or claims they cannot support.

Mitigation: Use only truthful, defensible metrics supplied by the user and review every final claim for accuracy before submitting a resume or rehearsing an interview story.

Risk: Over-tailoring a resume for keyword coverage can read as keyword stuffing or misrepresent experience.

Mitigation: Mirror only job-posting terms that are backed by real experience, and treat high ATS coverage as a reason to review for authenticity.
