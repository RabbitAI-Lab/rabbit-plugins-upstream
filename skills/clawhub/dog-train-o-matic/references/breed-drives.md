# Breed Drive Profiles

What each breed group was built to do, how much exercise that implies, and which games actually satisfy the instinct. "Problem behaviors" are usually normal drives with no outlet — the table reframes them as management targets.

## The Groups

### HERDING (border collie, aussie, kelpie, corgi, heeler, sheltie, GSD, malinois...)
- **Bred for:** controlling movement of livestock via stalking, eye, chase, nip
- **Exercise floor:** 75+ min/day, but *mental work tires more than distance*
- **Outlets:** treibball/pushing balls, directional fetch (left/right/away), grouping family members (sanctioned), obedience chains, heelwork
- **Expect:** heel-nipping at running children, chasing cars/bikes/joggers, light/shadow obsession when under-stimulated, herding other pets
- **Key insight:** a tired border collie is a *mentally* tired one. Ten minutes of directional-fetch decisions beats an hour of jogging.

### SLED/WORKING (husky, malamute, samoyed...)
- **Bred for:** endurance pulling in teams
- **Exercise floor:** 90+ min/day mixed cardio
- **Outlets:** bikejoring/canicross, flirt pole, weighted-pack hikes (adults), sanctioned digging pits, skijoring
- **Expect:** howling, escape artistry (digging under, climbing over), hard pulling, prey drive
- **Key insight:** huskies are engineered engines. Under-exercised = demolition. If you can't meet the floor honestly, choose another breed.

### SCENT HOUND (beagle, basset, dachshund, bloodhound...)
- **Bred for:** following scent trails for hours
- **Exercise floor:** 60 min, and *sniffing counts double*
- **Outlets:** nose-work/hidden-treat searches, snuffle mats, scatter feeding, tracking long-lines
- **Expect:** nose-to-ground pulling, "deaf" recall mid-trail, baying when bored, digging
- **Key insight:** a 20-minute "sniffari" (dog chooses the route, sniffs everything) satisfies more than a 45-minute brisk march.

### SIGHT HOUND (greyhound, whippet, saluki, borzoi...)
- **Bred for:** sprint-chasing
- **Exercise floor:** 45 min; sprinters, not marathoners
- **Outlets:** flirt pole, lure coursing, sprint fetch
- **Expect:** chasing small running animals (cats), counter-surfing, couch-potato indoors with random zoomies
- **Key insight:** two 5-minute hard chases beat an hour of leash walking. Never trust them off-leash in unfenced areas regardless of training.

### RETRIEVER (labrador, golden, chessie, toller...)
- **Bred for:** marking falls, soft-mouth delivery, working with a human
- **Exercise floor:** 60 min
- **Outlets:** structured fetch with obedience cues, swimming, carrying games, dock diving
- **Expect:** mouthing when excited, jumping on guests, counter-surfing, mid-fetch recall failures
- **Key insight:** the retrieve IS the reward for many labs; use fetch-as-payment in training instead of treats alone.

### GUARDIAN (rottweiler, doberman, boxer, mastiffs, danes...)
- **Bred for:** watching and deterring
- **Exercise floor:** 60 min
- **Outlets:** patrol-walk circuits, weight pull (conditioned adults), advanced obedience as "work"
- **Expect:** stranger barking, wariness, leash reactivity, territorial guarding of home/car
- **Key insight:** socialization investment before 16 weeks pays off more for this group than any other; genetics loads the gun, socialization aims it.

### TERRIER (JRT, rat terrier, fox terrier, westie, yorkie, airedale...)
- **Bred for:** killing vermin — independently, tenaciously
- **Exercise floor:** 60 min
- **Outlets:** digging pits, flirt pole, earthdog games, shake-toy time, puzzle feeders
- **Expect:** digging, barking, small-animal chase, grip-and-shake on toys/pant legs
- **Key insight:** tenacity is the product. You cannot train out what a terrier IS; you redirect it.

### COMPANION (pug, frenchie, bichon, maltese, shih tzu, cavalier, chihuahua...)
- **Bred for:** sitting on laps, being adorable
- **Exercise floor:** 30 min
- **Outlets:** trick training, sniffy walks, puzzle toys, fun agility (low impact)
- **Expect:** separation distress, attention barking, house-soiling regressions, lap/bed guarding
- **Key insight:** brachycephalic breeds (pugs, frenchies) overheat easily — swap intensity for brain work on warm days.

### GUNDOG/POINTING (GSP, vizsla, weimaraner, setters, spaniels...)
- **Bred for:** ranging, finding, flushing/retrieving birds
- **Exercise floor:** 60 min
- **Outlets:** marked retrieves, water work, steady-to-wing games
- **Expect:** pulling, mouthiness, strong bird/small-animal interest
- **Key insight:** "velcro vizslas" — biddable but prone to separation distress; alone-time training is not optional.

### BULLY/MIXED (staffies, bullies, bostons, mixes, rescues...)
- **Exercise floor:** 50 min
- **Outlets:** flirt pole, tug-with-rules, hiking
- **Expect:** pulling, jumping, adolescent dog-selectivity
- **Key insight:** for mixes, observe the dog: does he sniff (hound), chase (sight/sled), mouth (retriever), herd (herding)? Train the dog in front of you, not the DNA report.

## Exercise Conversion Table

| If the floor is... | And you have... | Then... |
|---|---|---|
| 90 min | 30 min free | outsource: doggy daycare 2×/week, dog runner, or reconsider the breed choice |
| 60 min | 30 min free | 15 min hard flirt pole + 15 min nose work ≈ 60 min of walking |
| 30 min | 15 min free | trick session + snuffle mat covers it |

The universal multiplier: **5 minutes of species-appropriate instinct work ≈ 15 minutes of generic walking.**
