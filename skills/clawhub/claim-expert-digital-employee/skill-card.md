## Description:

Provides a reference workflow for insurance claims, from first notice and document review through medical and liability analysis, fraud review, adjudication support, and closure communications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Claims employees use the skill to organize case information and draft review checklists, analyses, and customer communications across claim-handling stages. Qualified staff verify outputs and perform any business decisions or system actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft assessments or customer communications could be mistaken for approved claim decisions or sent before review.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts; qualified claims staff verify policy terms, calculations, and notices and approve all external or system actions.

Risk: Claim materials can contain sensitive identity and health information.

Mitigation: Redact identifiers and minimize health details before input; handle any retained records only through authorized institutional systems.
