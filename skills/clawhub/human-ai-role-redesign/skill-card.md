## Description:

Guides teams through redesigning roles, KPIs, skills, incentives, and communication after an AI agent takes over part of a job.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and business leaders use this skill to redefine human duties and measures of success after an agent takes over tasks, including training, incentives, and change communication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Role redesign proposals may affect staffing, pay, and performance decisions.

Mitigation: Have accountable leaders review proposals before implementation and provide only the employee information needed for the analysis.
