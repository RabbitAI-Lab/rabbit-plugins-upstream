## Description: <br>
A Facebook platform strategy skill that helps agents plan Reels, Feed posts, Groups activity, reply copy, and measurement routines using the REACH framework while keeping scheduling and community actions scoped to human-controlled surfaces. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media practitioners and agents use this skill to create Facebook strategy plans, draft Page/Reel/Group/reply copy, route Page publishing to the scheduling skill, and keep metrics grounded in native Meta Insights. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Agent output could be mistaken for permission to automate Group posting, comment replies, DMs, or metrics collection. <br>
Mitigation: Keep Group posting, community participation, comment replies, and DMs human/in-app, and read performance from native Meta Insights. <br>
Risk: Facebook ranking and reach assumptions can become stale. <br>
Mitigation: Re-verify platform-specific reach and ranking claims quarterly before relying on campaign recommendations. <br>
