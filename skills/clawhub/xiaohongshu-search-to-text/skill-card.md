## Description:

Searches web-indexed Xiaohongshu (RedNote) posts by topic and turns selected posts into text, including image text, comments, key points, and engagement counts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jackchew7143](https://clawhub.ai/user/jackchew7143)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, marketers, and creators can find public Xiaohongshu posts by topic, then review selected posts as text for product research, competitor research, creator discovery, or customer feedback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and selected links or share text are sent to LinkDigest; provider-side retention and deletion claims are not verified by the release evidence.

Mitigation: Send only queries and links you are comfortable sharing, and review LinkDigest's privacy information if retention matters.

Risk: Searching and reading posts can spend credits.

Mitigation: Choose which posts to read and use the per-read credit cap or API-key quota to limit spend.
