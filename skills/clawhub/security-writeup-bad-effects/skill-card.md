## Description:

Adds a concise Bad effect paragraph to each substantive section of a defensive security or incident-analysis article, explaining the concrete harm and failure mode.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security writers and incident analysts use this skill to make the harm and failed trust assumption clear in each section of a defensive write-up without adding unsupported claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Added harm statements or duplicate annotations could misrepresent the article's evidence.

Mitigation: Review article edits for one grounded statement per section, preserve uncertainty in evidence sections, and deduplicate existing annotations.
