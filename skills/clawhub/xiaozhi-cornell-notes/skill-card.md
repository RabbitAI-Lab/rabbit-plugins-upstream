## Description:

Organizes students' classroom notes into Cornell-style cues, content, and a brief summary for subject-based retrieval and review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking upper-primary and secondary students use the skill to organize classroom notes, find relevant notes while reviewing, and request a note-usage report when a profile is enabled. Upper-primary students receive a simplified format; younger students are outside the stated scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize curriculum, consent requirements, and support contacts before deployment elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's notes may be stored longer than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian and profile settings before enabling long-term memory; leave it off for one-session formatting.

Risk: Cross-skill sharing or review reminders may exceed the student's intended use.

Mitigation: Confirm sharing and reminder consent separately, and leave both disabled unless wanted.
