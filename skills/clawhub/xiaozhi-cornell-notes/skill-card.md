## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 students turn classroom notes into Cornell-style review prompts and summaries, then retrieve saved notes for study.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary through high school use this skill to organize class notes, create review questions, and find relevant notes later. With consent, it can keep a study-notes profile and provide a usage report or reminders through a separate reminder skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (other regions require localization)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language curriculum, consent rules, and emergency contacts may not suit students in other regions.

Mitigation: Use in the intended Chinese K–12 setting, or localize those details before deployment.

Risk: Stored student notes, usage history, sharing, and reminders involve minors' personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm profile, sharing, reminder, and guardian-consent settings before enabling long-term memory or cross-skill use.
