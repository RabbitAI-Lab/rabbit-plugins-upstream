## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking students organize class notes into Cornell-style cues, content, and summaries for later retrieval and review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Chinese K12 learning settings use this skill to turn photographed or typed class notes into searchable Cornell-style notes and retrieve them when reviewing. With appropriate consent, it can store notes and provide requested usage reports or review cues.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localized crisis-referral information.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student notes may be retained long term when profile storage is enabled.

Mitigation: Require the documented profile and guardian-consent controls where applicable; provide view, correction, deletion, pause, and export options.

Risk: Cross-skill sharing or review reminders could expose learning information or contact students unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Use opt-in sharing and reminder controls; share only note counts and recurring gaps, not full notes, and send reminders only after consent.
