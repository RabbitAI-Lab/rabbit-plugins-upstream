## Description:

帮助中文学生将课堂笔记整理为康奈尔格式，按学科归档并在复习时按需调取。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking upper-primary and secondary students use this skill to organize class notes into cue questions, content and a short summary, then retrieve relevant notes for revision. With consent, it can maintain a note archive and provide usage reports, limited cross-skill summaries and reminder cues.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize crisis resources and consent requirements outside Mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student notes and usage history may contain sensitive information about minors.

Mitigation: Explain storage and sharing controls to students and required guardians before enabling long-term profiles, parent visibility or cross-skill sharing; honor deletion and pause requests.

Risk: Reminders or crisis guidance may be inappropriate without consent or local context.

Mitigation: Send reminder cues only with student consent, and localize crisis resources and consent rules before use outside Mainland China.
