## Description:

帮助中国学龄学生将课堂笔记整理为康奈尔格式，并按学科和课题归档、检索及复习。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary school, middle school and high school use this skill to organize photographed or typed class notes, retrieve relevant notes during study, and review them with cue questions. With the appropriate permissions, it can save notes, share limited summaries across study skills, and supply prompts for consented reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student notes or study summaries may be retained or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm profile and any required guardian consent before storage; enable cross-skill sharing separately and share only note counts and recurring gaps, not full notes.

Risk: Review reminders or a self-test template could override the session-only rule for recall status.

Mitigation: Require reminder consent and review the self-test reminder template; keep self-reported recall status within the current session and do not create recall-based retest reminders.
