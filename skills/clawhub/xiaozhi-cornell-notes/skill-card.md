## Description:

帮助学生把课堂笔记整理为康奈尔格式，按学科和主题归档，并在需要时检索相关笔记。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

中国大陆 K–12 学生用它把课堂笔记整理为线索问题、课堂内容和一句话总结，按学科主题归档，并在复习时查找相关笔记。小学高段使用简化两栏流程；小学低段不适用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: When a profile is enabled, study notes and counts of retrievals and prompt responses may be saved across sessions.

Mitigation: Use “这次不要记忆” for a session without profile access or saving; enable sharing and reminders only when wanted, and confirm that deleting notes also removes related usage counts on the host platform.
