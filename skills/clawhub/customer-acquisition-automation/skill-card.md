## Description:

Customer Acquisition Automation helps indie tool builders collect public pain-point leads from Reddit, X, and public competitor threads, draft channel-native outreach, and prepare a human approval queue before any publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to run a public customer-acquisition workflow: find source-linked pain-point leads, draft channel-specific responses, and review an approval queue before anything is sent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public lead collection could cross platform rules or private-data boundaries.

Mitigation: Use public pages or official APIs only, avoid private contact data, and stop when access requires bypassing logins, CAPTCHAs, or rate limits.

Risk: Generated outreach could become spammy or misleading.

Mitigation: Draft only source-specific responses, avoid duplicate blasts, and do not invent prices, discounts, ratings, testimonials, or performance claims.

Risk: Drafts could be published before appropriate review.

Mitigation: Keep publishing behind an explicit approval queue; do not post unless the user confirms and controls the official API or sending step.
