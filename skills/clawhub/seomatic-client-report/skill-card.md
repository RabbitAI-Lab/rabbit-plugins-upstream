## Description:

Write a monthly SEO report for a client or a boss from the connected site's real data: Search Console clicks and positions against the prior period, what was changed and whether it worked, AI search visibility, and next month's plan, in plain language with every number sourced. Use when the user asks for a client report, monthly SEO report, agency report, or an update for their boss or stakeholders. Runs on SEOmatic, the SEO agent that works from the site's real Google Search Console data; nothing goes live until the user approves it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports draw on connected site data and may cover the wrong site or period if the workspace or dates are unclear.

Mitigation: Confirm the site and reporting dates, and check each figure against its cited source before sharing the report.

Risk: A report could overstate the effect of SEO changes if outcomes are attributed without supporting evidence.

Mitigation: Use the task evidence for attribution, report ineffective or inconclusive work, and avoid claiming a change caused results when the data does not support it.
