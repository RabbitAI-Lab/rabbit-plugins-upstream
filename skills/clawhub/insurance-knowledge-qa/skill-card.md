## Description:

AI-powered insurance knowledge Q&A skill that explains terminology, compares product types, analyzes coverage gaps, and supports decision-making for China-market insurance consumers and professionals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, insurance consumers, agents, and professionals use this skill to get China-market insurance education, terminology explanations, product-type comparisons, coverage analysis frameworks, and FAQ-style guidance. It is intended for general education and decision support, not policy-specific legal, financial, sales, underwriting, or claims advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general China-market insurance education as legal, financial, sales, underwriting, or claims advice.

Mitigation: State that answers are educational and advise users to verify policy-specific decisions with the contract, insurer, regulator, or a qualified professional.

Risk: Users may share unnecessary health or financial details while asking insurance questions.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum context needed and discourage sharing sensitive personal details that are not necessary for general guidance.

Risk: Insurance rules, limits, product terms, and regulatory practices can differ by policy and change over time.

Mitigation: Qualify numerical or policy-specific statements and direct users to the latest official source, insurer materials, and contract terms.
