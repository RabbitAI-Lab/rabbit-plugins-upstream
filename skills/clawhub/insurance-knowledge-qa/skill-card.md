## Use Case:

Insurance consumers and professionals in China use this skill to understand terminology and policy clauses, compare coverage dimensions, and identify questions to verify before making decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance rules, numeric limits, and policy terms may be outdated or differ by contract.

Mitigation: Verify current rules with official sources and check the applicable contract terms.

Risk: Educational comparisons could be mistaken for personalized product, claims, legal, medical, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for general education; refer decisions to the insurer or appropriately licensed professionals.
