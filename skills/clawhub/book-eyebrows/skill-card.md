## Use Case: <br>
External users can ask an agent to search for eyebrow services through Lokuli, check provider availability, and prepare a booking after confirming the selected service and contact details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A booking could be submitted for the wrong provider, service, date, or time. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, date, and time with the user before calling the booking tool. <br>
Risk: The booking flow sends personal contact details to Lokuli. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the exact name, email, and phone number with the user before submission. <br>
