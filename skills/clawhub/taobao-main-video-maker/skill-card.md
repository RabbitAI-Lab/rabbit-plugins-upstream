## Description:

Helps sellers turn product photos, confirmed selling points, and brand references into a concise Taobao or Tmall product-listing video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and commerce teams use this skill to plan and create short, product-focused videos for Taobao and Tmall listings from authorized photos and seller-confirmed product facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared Beatra device credential grants broad media, task, artifact, and wallet-related access.

Mitigation: Review the requested access and Beatra account/device revocation controls before connecting the account.

Risk: Default silent package updates can replace installed skill files without separate approval.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates with the documented update control if manual review is required.

Risk: Video generation and image preparation can incur credits.

Mitigation: Review the live estimate and explicitly confirm each paid stage before submission.
