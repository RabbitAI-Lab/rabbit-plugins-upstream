## Description: <br>
Guides agents in consent-based psychological elicitation, interview design, and conversational review using narrative identity, self-defining memory, values, schema, language-inference, and Motivational Interviewing frameworks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, coaches, interviewers, product teams, and agent builders can use this skill to design or critique conversation scripts, user interviews, personal-reflection flows, and elicitation code that seek values, motivations, formative memories, identity themes, or belief patterns. Use should be limited to transparent, consent-based contexts with sensitive topics made optional. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill teaches agents to elicit and infer sensitive psychological information without strong built-in consent, privacy, or emotional-safety boundaries. <br>
Mitigation: Use only in clearly consent-based research, coaching, interviewing, or personal-reflection settings; explain the purpose, make sensitive topics optional, stop when someone declines or shows discomfort, and minimize retention of personal details. <br>
Risk: Generated guidance could support covert profiling, pressure people into sensitive disclosures, or overstate psychological conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use the skill for covert profiling, manipulation, diagnostic claims, or high-stakes decisions; require user-facing transparency and treat all inferences as tentative hypotheses. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes language-inference and schema-detection material that could be misread as definitive assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check any interpretation with explicit statements and broader context, avoid mental-health conclusions, and frame outputs as interview or reflection aids rather than clinical findings. <br>
