## Description:

把客户沟通录音转写、聊天记录、自由笔记或零散信息整理成结构化养老与传承会谈纪要，包含家庭架构、五板块信息矩阵、风险信号、信息缺口、客户画像和微信风格跟进消息。

## Use Case:

External advisors and internal advisory teams use this skill to turn eldercare and inheritance planning conversations into structured meeting notes for follow-up planning. It organizes user-provided material into family structure, health, finance, housing, family relationship, existing coverage, risk signal, information gap, action item, and client follow-up sections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intended for sensitive client advisory notes that may include personal, health, family, finance, insurance, and inheritance details.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with client permission, and prefer anonymized or redacted inputs before sharing material with an agent.

Risk: Meeting notes and follow-up messages could imply legal, medical, insurance, regulatory, or trust conclusions beyond the evidence provided by the client.

Mitigation: Manually review customer-facing output and verify regulated or professional advice with qualified human reviewers before use.

Risk: Internal observations such as information gaps, inferred client stage, or risk signals could expose sensitive process notes if copied directly to a client.

Mitigation: Keep internal note sections separate from external follow-up messages, de-identify client details, and adjust tone before sending.
