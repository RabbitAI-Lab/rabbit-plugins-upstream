## Description:

Generate and edit videos with Kling 4.0 through WaveSpeed using text, images, reference media, or existing clips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wavespeed](https://clawhub.ai/user/wavespeed)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators can use this skill to generate short videos from prompts or reference media, animate images with keyframes, edit existing clips, and extend videos through WaveSpeed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected media are sent to WaveSpeed for generation or editing.

Mitigation: Share only media intended for upload and review prompts and references before submitting.

Risk: Video generation spends account credits, and costs vary with the endpoint and inputs.

Mitigation: Preview the estimate or quote the exact inputs before submitting a generation job.
