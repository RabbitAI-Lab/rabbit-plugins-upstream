## Description:

Get a cited research report from many web sources in one call with Sofya research. Use when the user asks to research a topic, compare options, survey a field or answer a question that one search won't settle, for example "research", "deep dive", "compare", "what are the options for".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to research an open-ended question, compare options, or survey a field. The skill returns a structured report with citations and source links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries are sent to Sofya, an external service.

Mitigation: Do not include secrets or personal or confidential details in queries.
