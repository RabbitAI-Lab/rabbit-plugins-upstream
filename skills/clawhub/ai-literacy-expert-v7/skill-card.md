## Use Case:

Educators and teaching teams use this skill to prepare AI literacy curricula, interactive learning activities, adaptive assessments, and learning plans. In WorkBuddy, they can also schedule recurring teaching tasks and deliver materials through connected services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scheduled teaching tasks can run unattended and produce repeated or unintended outputs.

Mitigation: Review the preview, schedule and destination before enabling an automation; confirm how to pause or delete it and verify the task against the actual scheduler.

Risk: Course outputs sent to cloud documents, calendars, reminders or shares may reach an unintended audience.

Mitigation: Confirm each destination, sharing scope and authorization before writing or distributing materials.
