## Description:

Customer segmentation, colored tag management, bidirectional Chatwoot CRM sync, and scheduled 1-to-1 or broadcast remarketing campaigns with Spintax templates.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

External customer-support teams and developers can use this skill to organize Zalo conversations, synchronize them with Chatwoot, and prepare scheduled customer follow-ups or tag-based campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The intended API host and whether the provided request examples are demonstrations need clarification before connecting a real account.

Mitigation: Verify the API host and confirm the examples are appropriate before supplying a real account token.

Risk: Bulk messaging can reach unintended recipients or send unapproved content, and queued campaigns may be difficult to stop.

Mitigation: Require approval of recipients and message content before each broadcast, and confirm how to stop queued campaigns.
