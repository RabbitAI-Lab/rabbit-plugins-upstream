## Description: <br>
Yin Yuan is a Chinese-language adversarial customer roleplay skill that generates a shifting, defensive persona for sales and customer-service simulation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wavegeometry](https://clawhub.ai/user/wavegeometry) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External developers, trainers, and evaluators can use this skill to simulate a difficult customer who resists profiling during sales or customer-service conversations. It is best suited for controlled robustness testing, roleplay practice, and analysis of information-asymmetry dynamics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill teaches deceptive personas and false-pretext information gathering in sales or customer-service interactions. <br>
Mitigation: Use only in clearly labeled, controlled simulations; do not deploy it in real customer, vendor, support, procurement, or impersonation contexts. <br>
Risk: Roleplay outputs may encourage collecting quotes, proposals, or personal details without appropriate consent. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review, consent, data minimization, and retention controls before adapting any generated scenario outside a sandbox. <br>
