## Description: <br>
Fliggy helps agents give travel-booking decision guidance for flight, hotel, vacation-package, voucher-style deal, refund-rule, and travel-dispute questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Travelers and agents assisting them use this skill to evaluate Fliggy travel purchases, compare booking options, assess voucher-style travel deals, interpret refund and change rules, and identify escalation paths for ordinary travel disputes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat travel prices, promotions, voucher availability, refund terms, or booking rules as current guarantees. <br>
Mitigation: Verify live prices, availability, cancellation terms, refund rules, and merchant conditions on the official booking page before purchasing. <br>
Risk: Travel, visa, border, weather, or force-majeure guidance may be incomplete or outdated for a user's specific trip. <br>
Mitigation: Use official airline, hotel, consular, immigration, and travel-service channels for final decisions and safety-critical requirements. <br>
