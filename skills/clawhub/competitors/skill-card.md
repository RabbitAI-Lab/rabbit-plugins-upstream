## Description: <br>
Guides agents in creating honest competitor comparison and alternative pages for SEO and sales enablement across singular alternative, plural alternatives, you-vs-competitor, and competitor-vs-competitor formats. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and content strategists use this skill to plan and draft competitor alternative pages, comparison pages, and comparison hubs that support SEO and sales enablement. The skill emphasizes structured research, balanced positioning, centralized competitor data, and human review of claims before publication. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Competitor comparisons may include outdated or inaccurate pricing, feature, or competitor descriptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review factual claims, pricing, and competitor descriptions before publication, and refresh competitor data on a regular schedule. <br>
Risk: Persuasive comparison copy can become misleading if it omits competitor strengths or the user's product limitations. <br>
Mitigation: Require balanced positioning that acknowledges competitor strengths, states limitations clearly, and receives legal or compliance review for sensitive comparisons. <br>
