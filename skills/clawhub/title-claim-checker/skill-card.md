## Description:

检查学术论文标题中的声明是否有足够的证据支持，防止夸大研究结论。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, academic authors, editors, and agents use this skill to compare a paper title's claim strength with the supplied study design and evidence, then suggest more accurate title wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The analysis depends on the title and study evidence supplied by the user and may miss context that is not provided.

Mitigation: Review the recommendation against the paper's actual study design, methods, and results before changing a title.

Risk: Users may provide broader documents or account information even though the skill only needs title and evidence excerpts.

Mitigation: Provide only the title and relevant study evidence needed for the claim-strength check.
