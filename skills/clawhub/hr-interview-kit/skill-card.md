## Description:

Generates role-specific structured interview questions, follow-ups, scoring anchors, interviewer notes, and a list of answers to examine more closely.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters and interviewers use this skill to prepare consistent, role-specific interview questions and scoring guides from a job description, competencies, candidate level, and interview constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview questions or scoring guidance may touch protected attributes or create unfair evaluations.

Mitigation: Review questions and scoring criteria for job relevance and applicable hiring requirements; avoid questions about protected or sensitive personal attributes.

Risk: Candidate information in interview notes may contain personal data.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum necessary candidate information, redact identifying details where possible, and review outputs for privacy compliance.

Risk: Generic requests for a scoring table may invoke the skill unintentionally in recruiting conversations.

Mitigation: Confirm that an interview kit is intended before using the generated scoring table.
