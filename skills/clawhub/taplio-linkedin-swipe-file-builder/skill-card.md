## Description:

Helps creators build an organized LinkedIn swipe file and draft original posts inspired by the structures of saved posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use this skill to organize LinkedIn posts by hook, format, angle, and personal relevance, then develop original drafts that adapt those structures to their own niche and voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Taplio connector accesses the user's account and selected content destination.

Mitigation: Review account permissions, authorize the connector deliberately, and choose where the swipe file will be stored.

Risk: Adapted drafts may not reflect the user's intent or voice, or may be saved before review.

Mitigation: Review each draft for originality and fit, and approve only the drafts to save; publishing requires an explicit request.
