## Description:

Generates patent-analysis-backed project proposal reports by guiding technology scoping, PatSnap search strategy, seven analysis modules, and final Markdown report assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, patent analysts, and R&D teams use this skill to prepare data-driven research project proposal reports supported by PatSnap patent searches and structured IP analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project technical descriptions and company names may be sent through external PatSnap MCP patent-search tools.

Mitigation: Review confidentiality requirements and use only approved PatSnap accounts and environments before running searches.

Risk: The FTO section may be mistaken for formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep the FTO section labeled as preliminary and require qualified patent counsel for formal opinions.

Risk: Patent legal status and search results can become stale after retrieval.

Mitigation: Record the search date in the report and refresh searches before using results for decisions.
