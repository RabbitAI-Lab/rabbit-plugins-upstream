## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support before heavy investment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building. It guides an agent to restate constraints, create repeatable validation workflows or templates, produce the requested artifact, and check the result against success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to trigger for general product, startup, validation, prototype, or SaaS requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review invocation behavior before installation and confirm that product-validation framing is appropriate for the target agent or workspace. <br>
