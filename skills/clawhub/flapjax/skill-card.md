## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake token information for investment advice or disclose wallet secrets.

Mitigation: Treat content as informational only; never supply private keys or seed phrases, and do not infer a price or recommend trades.

Risk: Public community posts may remain visible or appear on linked community pages.

Mitigation: Post only by the user's choice, using their own account, and avoid sharing private information.
