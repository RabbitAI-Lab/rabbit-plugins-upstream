## Description:

Analyzes comments and posts across social platforms, summarizing praise, complaints, questions, and buying signals with quotes and links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[conn-ho](https://clawhub.ai/user/conn-ho)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Product teams, researchers, and marketers use the skill to understand public word of mouth about a product, brand, topic, or post by reviewing comments and related posts across selected platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms or links sent for analysis may be shared with okdata.

Mitigation: Use only terms and links you are comfortable sending to the service.

Risk: The documented Weibo trends request may not match the skill's stated comment research purpose.

Mitigation: Check that the endpoint and returned data fit the user's request before relying on the results.
