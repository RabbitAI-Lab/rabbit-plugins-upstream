## Description:

What are people praising and complaining about under this post? Reads comments under one post, page by page, into a table of comments, authors, likes, replies, and time, across 11 social platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[conn-ho](https://clawhub.ai/user/conn-ho)

## Use Case:

Creators, analysts, and developers can use this skill to collect comments from a post on supported social platforms and review what viewers praise or criticize. It returns a structured comment table for an AI to summarize.
