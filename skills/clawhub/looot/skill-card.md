## Description:

Use looot to search and run provider APIs for live web, business, social, SEO, finance, and related data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents use looot when a task needs current external data, such as contact or company enrichment, web and social research, SEO metrics, news, or market information. They compare providers and prices, inspect required inputs, and obtain user approval before paid runs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan flags broad activation and result saving without clear approval; the scan was partial, so safety evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Limit activation to tasks that need external data, keep sensitive details out of searches, review where results will be saved, and require user approval before paid runs.
