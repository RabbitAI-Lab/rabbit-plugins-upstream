## Description:

Find Brazilian addresses and registered companies near a location using prepared geographic joins. Start with free lookups and the neighborhood allowance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can look up Brazilian postal addresses, registered companies, nearby postal codes, and local business activity. The skill helps answer location-based questions while preserving coverage and source limitations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Brazil

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Address and location lookups share the queried location with PontoFato; neighborhood queries may incur charges beyond the free allowance.

Mitigation: Only submit locations the user is comfortable sharing, check the free neighborhood allowance, and set a spending limit before paid queries. Keep credit tokens private. Live service privacy practices and current billing behavior were not verified.
