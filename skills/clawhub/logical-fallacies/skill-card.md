## Description: <br>
Logical Fallacies helps an agent audit persuasive arguments by identifying structural, linguistic, cognitive, and rhetorical reasoning failures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Analysts, writers, developers, and agents use this skill to turn a suspicious or high-stakes argument into a structured fallacy audit that separates failed inference from the truth of the conclusion. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can shape how an agent critiques arguments, so an overconfident audit could mislabel weak reasoning as proof that a conclusion is false. <br>
Mitigation: Review the audit for the fallacy-fallacy distinction and require the output to state what evidence would actually support or settle the claim. <br>
