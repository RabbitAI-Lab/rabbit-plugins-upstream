## Description:

Guides an agent through a four-pass audit of an argument’s structure, language, cognitive patterns, and rhetoric, then evaluates what the argument establishes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to examine persuasive or consequential claims systematically. It helps identify where an inference fails, distinguish that from whether the conclusion is true, and describe what evidence could support the conclusion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential material could be copied into reusable examples based on observed failure patterns.

Mitigation: Remove confidential details before saving observed patterns as examples.
