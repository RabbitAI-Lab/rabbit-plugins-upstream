## Description:

Searches public Douyin videos and creators, retrieves work details, comments, and trending topics, and returns structured data for short-video research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users, short-video operators, marketers, and analysts use this skill to search public Douyin videos and creators, inspect works and comments, and track trending topics for content planning, competitor monitoring, and audience research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplied Douyin links or IDs are sent to a third-party API.

Mitigation: Only submit links or IDs you are comfortable sharing with the GUAIKEI service.

Risk: The security scan has medium confidence and incomplete coverage, and it notes that some capabilities may exceed or not support the advertised purpose.

Mitigation: Review the extra account-search capability and confirm the daily ranking behavior before relying on those results.
