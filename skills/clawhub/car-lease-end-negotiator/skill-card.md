## Description:

Use when a car lease is ending in the next 6 months — computes the three-way decision (return vs buy out vs buy-and-sell for equity) from residual, market value, and remaining payments, prices the excess-mileage and wear bills both ways (lease rate vs pre-purchase packs vs tire-shop fix), flags equity-positive leases people walk away from, and lays out the pre-turn-in inspection playbook that keeps the disposition fee negotiable.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

U.S. lessees with a vehicle lease ending within six months can use this skill to compare returning the car, buying it out, and buying it out to sell, using their contract figures and current market estimates. It also helps prepare for mileage, wear, disposition fees, and turn-in negotiations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect contract figures or market estimates can change the lease-end comparison.

Mitigation: Check the residual, fees, mileage terms, and payoff against the contract and a written payoff quote; compare current values from multiple sources.

Risk: Buyout taxes and third-party transfer rules vary by state and finance company.

Mitigation: Confirm the tax treatment and permitted payoff or title-transfer path with the relevant state authority and finance company before acting.

Risk: Wear charges and repair estimates may differ from the inspector's assessment.

Mitigation: Review the contract's wear guide, obtain independent repair estimates, and keep inspection findings and quotes in writing.
