## Description: <br>
Expert project manager for ADHD engineers managing multiple concurrent projects. Specializes in hyperfocus management, context-switching minimization, and parakeet-style gentle reminders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineering managers, and project leads use this skill to plan and manage concurrent engineering work for ADHD engineers through task chunking, context-switching reduction, reminder cadences, and hyperfocus-aware check-ins. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill describes monitoring workplace activity and sending reminders without clearly defining consent, scope, or privacy controls. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with explicit opt-in from affected engineers, minimize monitored data sources, document retention and escalation rules, and make reminder frequency configurable. <br>
Risk: Forceful break or physical-intervention language may be coercive in workplace use. <br>
Mitigation: Reframe interventions as supportive opt-in check-ins and avoid non-consensual physical or mandatory actions. <br>
