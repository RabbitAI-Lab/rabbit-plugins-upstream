## ❓ Clarification checklist

**Blockers (needed before we can quote or sample):**

1. What scooter model is this for, and how does the seat mount (rail spec, bracket,
   or bolt pattern)? A drawing or a photo of the current/target seat base is ideal.
2. Do you have a reference sample or the outgoing seat we should match for fit?

**Spec details:**

3. Target seat dimensions (length × width) or the rider comfort profile you want.
4. Foam firmness preference, and any weight target.
5. Storage compartment size / what it needs to hold.
6. Logo format for the rear branding (embossed, printed, badge?).

**Commercial:**

7. Target landed price band per unit, so we can propose a matching build.
8. Which certifications or test evidence does your market require for the seat?
9. Desired sample date and mass-production date.
