## Description:

根据学生背景与目标院校或专业，策划、撰写、改写和润色英文留学申请文书，覆盖个人陈述（PS/SOP）、推荐信草稿、联系潜在导师的套磁邮件以及留学英文简历或学术 CV。用于用户提供课程、竞赛、科研、论文、项目、实习、职业目标或导师信息，希望突出申请优势、说明项目匹配度、减少中式表达并生成可直接微调提交的海外院校风格文书时；也用于审校现有文书、压缩至字数限制、统一整套申请材料叙事或按院校题目定制。

## Use Case:

Students and application advisors use this skill to draft, revise, and polish English study-abroad application materials, including PS/SOP essays, recommendation-letter drafts, faculty outreach emails, resumes, and academic CVs. It is intended for application documents grounded in user-provided or verifiable academic, research, project, internship, and career information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process personal academic history, application targets, and recommendation materials.

Mitigation: Provide only information needed for the document, avoid unnecessary sensitive identifiers, and review all personal details before sharing or submitting outputs.

Risk: Application documents can mislead reviewers if school, professor, achievement, publication, or recommendation details are inaccurate or unapproved.

Mitigation: Verify program and professor details against official sources, use placeholders for unconfirmed facts, and have recommendation-letter drafts reviewed and approved by the actual recommender.
