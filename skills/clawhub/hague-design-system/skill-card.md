## Description:

Guides design practitioners through Hague System international design registration, including filing, publication, refusals, renewals, searches, and fee checks, with optional China-specific guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Design attorneys, IP lawyers, in-house counsel, and applicants use this skill to navigate WIPO's Hague international design procedures, check fees, and identify jurisdiction-specific steps, including optional CNIPA procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated fees, deadlines, or jurisdictional requirements could mislead applicants.

Mitigation: Confirm current fees and deadlines with official WIPO and CNIPA sources before relying on guidance.

Risk: Legal-practice guidance may be mistaken for professional legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a practice aid and obtain qualified legal review for consequential decisions.

Risk: Sharing case details with support or running fee helpers with output options could expose confidential information or create files.

Mitigation: Do not send confidential application details or credentials to support; run helper scripts only for intended fee checks and review file-writing options.
