## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Market briefings are time-sensitive and may become stale or incomplete as news develops. <br>
Mitigation: Verify dates, source freshness, and market session timing before relying on the briefing. <br>
Risk: Pre-market summaries can be misleading if treated as trading advice or as a substitute for authoritative market data. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as research context only and confirm important claims against primary financial sources or broker-grade data. <br>
Risk: The artifact behavior appears to be a wrapper or placeholder around web search rather than a full market-data implementation. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated search terms and resulting sources before operational use. <br>
