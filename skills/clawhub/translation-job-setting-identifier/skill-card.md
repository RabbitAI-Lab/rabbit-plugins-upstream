## Description:

Plan a translation setting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Localization and content-operations users use this skill to turn a localization note into a concise translation setting with locale, glossary, and register choices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Localization notes may include more business context than the skill needs.

Mitigation: Provide only the locale, glossary name, register, and other localization details needed for the current planning task.
