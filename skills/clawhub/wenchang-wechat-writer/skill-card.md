## Description: <br>
Drafts WeChat-style long-form articles from a confirmed topic, outline, evidence pack, or content_state. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External content creators and editorial agents use this skill to turn a confirmed topic, outline, evidence pack, or content_state into a WeChat-style long-form article draft for later review and publish packaging. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The draft may include unsupported or misleading claims when the topic is fact-heavy and no evidence pack is supplied. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated claims before use, route fact-heavy topics to research first, and mark missing evidence instead of inventing facts. <br>
Risk: Private account identity or project material could be incorporated into a draft if supplied unintentionally. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only private material intended for the current drafting run and review the draft before downstream review or publish packaging. <br>
