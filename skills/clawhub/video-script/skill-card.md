## Description:

帮助创作者根据已有视频分析制定剪辑与声音方案，编写并校验带时间戳的中文解说，也可修订短片宣发文案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

视频创作者和剪辑师可用它把现有画面、对白和时间线分析转化为中文解说及剪辑计划；已有短片也可用于标题、花字与宣发文案返修。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional AI review sends narration, transcripts, visual analysis, research, and planning excerpts to a configured MiMo-compatible API.

Mitigation: For confidential projects, obtain authorization before running remote review, or skip it and use local validation instead.
