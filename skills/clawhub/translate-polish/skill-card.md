## Description:

AI Chinese-English translation and polishing workflow covering pre-translation briefing, segmented translation, two-pass proofreading, terminology consistency checks, and cultural adaptation for papers, business letters, technical documentation, and marketing content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional learning helper can save local notes and preferences across sessions in learned_patterns.json.

Mitigation: Avoid recording confidential source text, personal data, contracts, or medical/legal details; delete the local JSON file if retained history is not desired.

Risk: Legal, medical, confidential, or sensitive translations may require controls beyond automated translation and polishing.

Mitigation: Use local models or de-identified text for sensitive content and keep human review for legal, medical, and other critical documents.
