## Description: <br>
GlobePilot AI Agent 2 is an AI-powered global travel assistant for visa requirements, currency conversion, airport status, events, cultural tips, emergency contacts, seasonal advice, lodging recommendations, hidden local spots, and food guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to request destination-specific travel information and recommendations before or during a trip. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel guidance may be inaccurate or outdated for visas, airport status, safety, events, or emergency information. <br>
Mitigation: Verify critical details with official government, airport, airline, and local emergency sources before acting. <br>
Risk: Travel queries may include personal identity or itinerary details when used with an external runtime. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive personal or trip information unless the user trusts the provider. <br>
