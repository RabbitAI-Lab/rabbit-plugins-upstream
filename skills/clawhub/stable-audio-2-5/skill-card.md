## Description:

Guides agents in generating light instrumental background music for dialogue, reels, and explainers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to make understated instrumental audio beds for voiceovers, reels, and explainers through Replicate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing additional skills from an untrusted source can expose the agent to unsafe instructions.

Mitigation: Verify the PrunaAI/pruna-skills source and each installation command before use.

Risk: Audio prompts sent to Replicate may disclose sensitive content or credentials.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated Replicate token, review prompts before submission, and omit secrets and private content.
