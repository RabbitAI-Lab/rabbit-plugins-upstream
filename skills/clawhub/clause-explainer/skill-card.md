## Description: <br>
Explains contract clauses in plain English, identifies who a clause favors, rates practical risk, and suggests negotiation points while reminding users to confirm with counsel. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and business reviewers use this skill to understand a contract clause, assess whether it is standard or aggressive, identify practical risk, and prepare concrete negotiation questions for qualified legal counsel. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste sensitive contract text into an agent session. <br>
Mitigation: Share only text the user is authorized to process and avoid unnecessary confidential details. <br>
Risk: Plain-English explanations and negotiation ideas may be mistaken for legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as preparation material and confirm jurisdiction-specific or high-value matters with a qualified lawyer. <br>
Risk: A clause can change meaning based on the full contract, governing law, and party position. <br>
Mitigation: Review the clause with surrounding terms, contract type, reader position, and counsel before relying on the interpretation. <br>
