## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student problem-solving records may expose personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent settings, use student aliases, and provide controls to view, export, pause, correct, or delete records.

Risk: AI-generated physics exercises or explanations may contain errors.

Mitigation: Label generated questions and have a teacher check calculations and teaching materials before classroom use.
