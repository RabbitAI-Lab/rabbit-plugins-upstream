## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school physics teachers through five-step problem-solving instruction, model selection, worked examples, and variation exercises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to draft step-by-step explanations, diagnose physics problem-solving difficulties, and design differentiated practice for Chinese middle- and high-school students. Teachers review teaching materials and student records before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (default Chinese K–12 teaching context; localize safety referrals for other regions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student identifiers or sensitive learning records may be exposed.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, omit real student identifiers, review records before saving, and confirm consent before student-side sharing.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials could be disclosed.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam questions, answer keys, or scoring guides.

Risk: Generated practice problems or teaching drafts may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Teachers should verify generated exercises and review materials before students use them.
