This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆中文教学场景的初高中物理教师，提供审题、建模、过程分析、列式与求解反思的教学草稿、变式练习建议及班级解题档案。教师应审核生成内容后再用于课堂。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student identities or class-solving records may be retained or shared inappropriately.

Mitigation: Confirm platform memory and file controls, use student aliases, and use the pause, delete and export controls when records should not be retained or shared.

Risk: Confidential or not-yet-completed exam questions may be disclosed.

Mitigation: Do not input confidential exam content or questions from exams that are still underway.

Risk: AI-generated physics exercises or explanations may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check generated problems and teaching drafts before classroom use.
