## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom and student problem-solving records may contain sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, review proposed updates before saving, share only with appropriate consent, and confirm the platform supports the documented memory and deletion controls.

Risk: AI-generated physics problems or teaching updates may need correction before classroom use.

Mitigation: Check generated problems and proposed updates before use or saving; label generated problems for manual verification.

Risk: Unreleased examination questions and related materials are sensitive.

Mitigation: Do not enter or adapt examination content until all relevant sessions are complete; follow local exam confidentiality requirements.
