## Description:

Guides Chinese junior-high and first-year high-school physics teachers through model-based problem-solving instruction, worked-example planning, and variation exercises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Physics teachers use this skill to plan stepwise explanations, identify student misconceptions, design practice variations, and maintain class problem-solving profiles. Its high-school coverage is limited to the first required physics module.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information may be exposed through class profiles or parent-facing sharing.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, review record changes before saving, and share or write back only when the relevant consent flags are true.

Risk: AI-generated physics exercises or explanations may contain errors.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check calculations and approve exercises and student-facing material before use.
