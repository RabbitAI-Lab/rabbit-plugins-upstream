为初高中物理教师提供五步解题教学框架、建模与过程分析建议、变式训练设计及班级解题档案草稿。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中物理教师用它起草解题讲解思路、分层变式与错因分析，并在教师确认后整理班级解题档案；面向学生或家长的材料由教师审定后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize student-data consent and crisis-contact guidance for the deployment region.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records may expose sensitive information or be shared without consent.

Mitigation: Use aliases, verify consent settings, and confirm any writeback or sharing before saving.

Risk: Generated physics exercises or explanations may contain errors.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify calculations and review generated items before classroom use or storage.

Risk: Crisis contacts or student-data rules may not fit deployments outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the learner's region and localize crisis guidance and consent requirements before deployment.
