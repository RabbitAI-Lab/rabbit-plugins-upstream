## Description:

Helps Chinese-language middle and high school physics teachers teach problem solving through structured reasoning, model selection, practice variations, and class learning records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language middle and high school physics teachers use this skill to draft step-by-step teaching prompts, diagnose problem-solving difficulties, design practice variations, and maintain class-level learning records for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records or personal information could be shared or written back without appropriate controls.

Mitigation: Confirm platform support for consent, pseudonyms, export, deletion, and teacher-confirmed writeback before enabling memory; avoid unnecessary student personal information.

Risk: Unreleased examination materials could be disclosed to the skill.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam questions, answers, or marking criteria.

Risk: Generated practice questions or teaching drafts could contain physics errors.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review materials and independently verify AI-generated questions before classroom use.
