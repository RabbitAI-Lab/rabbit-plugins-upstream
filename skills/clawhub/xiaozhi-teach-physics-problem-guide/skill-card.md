## Description:

Guides junior-middle physics teachers through structured problem-solving instruction using a five-step method, model selection, variation practice, and class learning records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External educators use this skill to plan how to teach junior-middle physics problems, diagnose common solving errors, design variation exercises, and prepare teacher-reviewed class learning records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland by default; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records may contain minor education data and cross-skill or parent-visible sharing controls.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent settings before use, prefer aliases over real student names, and review profile or homework entries before saving or sharing.

Risk: AI-generated physics questions, worked examples, or variation exercises may contain calculation or curriculum-scope errors.

Mitigation: Label generated items for human checking and require teacher verification before adding them to a resource library or assessment.

Risk: Student text may include self-harm, abuse, bullying, or other safety signals outside the learning workflow.

Mitigation: Stop the teaching flow, follow the bundled crisis referral protocol, localize emergency guidance by region, and avoid storing sensitive details beyond the referral fact.

Risk: Textbook, tutoring-material, or exam problems may have copyright restrictions.

Mitigation: Record copyright status for each problem and store restricted original items as indexes rather than reusable full text.
