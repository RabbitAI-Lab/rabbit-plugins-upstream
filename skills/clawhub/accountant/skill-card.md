## Description: <br>
Accountant helps agents keep defensible books by coding transactions, preparing balanced journal entries, reconciling accounts, closing periods, producing financial statements, and preparing filing figures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and finance operators use this skill when an agent needs to maintain bookkeeping records, reconcile accounts, prepare close support, or reason through accounting and tax-adjacent treatments. It is intended for ledger work and filing support, not forecasting, fundraising, personal finance, invoicing operations, or bank payment operations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may update local bookkeeping records under ~/Clawic/data/, including reconciliations, closes, filing records, and memory organization. <br>
Mitigation: For sensitive books, ask the agent to preview file changes or keep backups before accepting durable updates. <br>
Risk: Accounting outputs can affect filed periods, tax records, or lender and audit support if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review entries, tie-outs, and filing figures before use; escalate locked, filed, payroll-tax, ownership-change, insolvency, or attestation matters to a licensed professional. <br>
