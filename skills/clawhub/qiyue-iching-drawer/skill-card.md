## Description:

Casts I Ching hexagrams through the QiyueAstro public API using coins, time, or numbers, then returns the hexagram name, judgment, line texts, changing lines, and transformed hexagram.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bloodymarygg](https://clawhub.ai/user/bloodymarygg)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to cast or browse I Ching hexagrams for entertainment and self-exploration, including coin, time, and number-based draws. The agent presents the API-returned classical text and hexagram imagery without adding its own divination interpretation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions entered for casting may be sent to QiyueAstro's external service.

Mitigation: Keep questions general and avoid personal, financial, medical, business, or confidential information.

Risk: Users may over-rely on divination-style output for consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Present results as entertainment and self-exploration, and avoid adding model-authored interpretations or advice.
