## Description:

Compare a brand's social media with its competitors using Juicer. Finds each competitor's verified accounts on Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook and Bluesky, then reports followers, posting frequency, top posts and hashtags side by side, with a short "what to copy" list. Use for "check my competitors", "competitor social media analysis", "what is [brand] posting", "benchmark us against".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Brand owners and social media marketers can compare their public social accounts with up to four competitors across selected networks. They use recent follower, posting, engagement, top-post, format, and hashtag data to identify patterns and develop three post-linked ideas to try.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile and post lookups consume Juicer credits, with costs varying by network.

Mitigation: Review the Juicer connection and credit costs, confirm selected accounts before further lookups, and check the available balance.

Risk: Recurring reports can trigger future account lookups.

Mitigation: Enable weekly or monthly reports only when wanted.
