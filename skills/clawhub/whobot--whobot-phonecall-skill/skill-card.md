## Description:

Helps users find, try, create, and manage WhoBot AI phone agents for business calling and personal-assistant tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales teams use the skill to explore and try phone agents, create or refine agents, and manage supported calling workflows. Individuals can use its Personal Agent workflows for phone-based tasks such as making reservations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The CLI installation does not pin a version; preview network contacts and local call-recording storage may have privacy implications.

Mitigation: Review the install source and pin a version where appropriate; check that preview connections and local recording storage meet your privacy requirements before using sensitive call data.
