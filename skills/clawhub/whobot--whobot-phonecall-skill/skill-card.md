## Description:

Helps business teams find, try, create, and tune WhoBot AI phone employees for customer outreach and support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales leaders use this skill to assess WhoBot for phone-based customer outreach or support, find suitable examples, and guide the creation or tuning of a phone employee. It can also help with approved trials, lead workflows, and account tasks when the service makes those actions available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the skill can involve an external WhoBot CLI package.

Mitigation: Review the package and use an approved, versioned CLI when reproducible installation matters.

Risk: Phone-based login and selected customer materials are sent to WhoBot.

Mitigation: Confirm the data may be shared and submit only materials needed for the requested task.

Risk: Calling and lead workflows can involve recipients, schedules, and fees.

Mitigation: Review recipients, schedules, and fees before confirming consequential actions; obtain clear consent before uploading leads.

Risk: Diagnostic reports may contain information that should not be shared broadly.

Mitigation: Inspect diagnostic reports before sharing them with support.
