## Description:

Helps business teams discover, try, create, and optimize AI phone agents for customer calling workflows through WhoBot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales teams use the skill to find suitable phone agents, review examples, and manage calling workflows such as follow-ups, reminders, lead screening, and customer support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reported call and upload workflows may involve privacy-sensitive lead data and locally stored call recordings.

Mitigation: Review the lead-consent flow and recording storage practices before uploading lead data or using recordings.

Risk: The security assessment notes an unpinned installer version risk.

Mitigation: Review the installer package version before running installation or update commands.
