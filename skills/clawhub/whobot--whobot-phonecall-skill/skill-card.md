## Description:

Helps businesses discover, try, create, and manage AI phone agents for customer calls and outbound campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and sales leads use the skill to find and try phone agents, create or refine agents from recordings or documents, and manage outbound lead campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China and abroad, subject to applicable laws.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the skill can download the current WhoBot CLI release.

Mitigation: Install only if you trust WhoBot as the CLI publisher.

Risk: Recordings, documents, and customer lists may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share only material you are authorized to provide and remove unnecessary sensitive information.

Risk: Real calls can affect recipients and incur charges.

Mitigation: Review privacy terms, calling targets, schedules, and charges before confirming.
