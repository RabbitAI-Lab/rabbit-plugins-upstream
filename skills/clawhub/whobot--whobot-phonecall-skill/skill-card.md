## Description:

Helps business teams discover, try, create, and tune WhoBot AI phone employees for customer calls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whobot](https://clawhub.ai/user/whobot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business owners, operations teams, and sales leads use the skill to find and try phone agents, create or adjust them from business materials, and manage lead uploads and accounts through guided workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release guidance installs the CLI from an unpinned latest channel, so the installed version can change between uses.

Mitigation: Review the current package and use a specific version when reproducibility matters.

Risk: Recordings, documents, and lead data may be uploaded or saved locally, while retention and privacy details are unclear.

Mitigation: Confirm the vendor's privacy and retention terms before sharing data, and provide only information authorized for transfer.
