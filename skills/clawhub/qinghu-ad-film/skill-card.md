## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing or upgrading an unpinned qhkit package may introduce unreviewed changes.

Mitigation: Verify the npm package is trusted before installation and prefer a pinned version where possible.

Risk: Video generation can consume credits and submitted jobs cannot be canceled.

Mitigation: Show the exact prompt, inputs, estimated credits, and non-cancelable status; obtain explicit approval before submitting a paid job.
