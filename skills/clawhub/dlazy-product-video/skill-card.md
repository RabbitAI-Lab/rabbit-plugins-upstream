## Description:

Helps create product showcase and advertising videos from product images, documents, or listing links through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and sellers use this skill to request product demonstrations and ecommerce ads, including multilingual voiceovers and an optional virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The CLI uses a dLazy API key for access to the hosted service.

Mitigation: Use a key you are comfortable supplying, keep it private, and rotate or revoke it when needed.

Risk: Prompts and optionally attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only prompts and files you intend to send to dLazy.
