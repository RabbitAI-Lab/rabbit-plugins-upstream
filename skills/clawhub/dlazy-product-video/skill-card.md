## Description:

Helps create product demo and advertising videos from product photos, documents, or shopping links using dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and marketing teams use this skill to turn product photos, specifications, catalogs, or shopping listings into product showcase and advertising videos, with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, selected options, and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Confirm the service is approved for the intended data; avoid attaching confidential product materials unless permitted.

Risk: Login stores a dLazy API key in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the local account and key; rotate or revoke the key if it may have been exposed.
