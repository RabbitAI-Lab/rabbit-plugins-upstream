## Description:

Turns product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or marketplace listings into conversion-focused shopping videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and marketers use this skill to create product demonstration, showcase, and advertising videos from product materials or marketplace listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Review the login and project-listing behavior and the unresolved references before installing or authenticating; send only prompts and files approved for the dLazy service.
