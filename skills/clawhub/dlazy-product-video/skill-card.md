## Description:

Helps create product showcase and advertising videos from product images, documents, or listing links through the dLazy service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketers use this skill to create product demos and e-commerce ads from product materials or listing links, with optional multilingual voiceover and a virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached product files are sent to the external dLazy service.

Mitigation: Only submit prompts and product files you are comfortable sharing with dLazy.

Risk: Signing in saves a service API key locally, and global installation leaves a persistent CLI binary.

Mitigation: Protect the local credential and use the on-demand npx option if a persistent CLI installation is undesirable.
