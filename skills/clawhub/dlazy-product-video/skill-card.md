## Description:

product video, product demo video, product ad video, product showcase — turn a product's photos or link into a polished demo / ad video. Use when the user wants a product demo, showcase, or ad video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketers use the skill to create product showcase and advertising videos from product photos, listings, links, or product materials, with optional multilingual voiceover and a virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service, and attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Send only content you intend to share externally and attach files only when needed.

Risk: Login saves an organization API key locally, and video generation may consume credits.

Mitigation: Authorize the intended organization and project, keep the key in the intended local account, revoke or rotate it when needed, and account for generation credits.
