## Description:

Helps create product demonstration and advertising videos from product images, specifications, or store listings through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketing teams use this skill to develop product demos and ads from product materials or store listings, with optional multilingual voiceover and a virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected attachments are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review prompts and attach only files intended for upload.

Risk: An API key may be stored in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key if exposed.

Risk: Project conversation context may persist with the provider.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive project details unless provider retention is acceptable.
