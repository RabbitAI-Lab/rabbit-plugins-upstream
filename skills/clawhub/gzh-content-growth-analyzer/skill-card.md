## Description: <br>
Analyzes WeChat public-account articles or supplied text with an eight-dimension virality rubric and returns scores, causal diagnosis, reusable strengths, concrete optimization edits, and content growth strategy. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[frankchenfei](https://clawhub.ai/user/frankchenfei) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External content operators, marketers, and account owners use this skill to diagnose WeChat article performance, compare successful examples, evaluate titles or topics, interpret engagement ratios, and get actionable revisions for improving reach, completion, interaction, and growth. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad article-analysis requests may activate the skill when the user did not intend to route content through this framework. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants WeChat public-account operations analysis before applying the full diagnostic workflow. <br>
Risk: Users may submit private drafts, unpublished strategy, or internal engagement metrics for analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive or unpublished content unless it is appropriate for processing in the agent context. <br>
