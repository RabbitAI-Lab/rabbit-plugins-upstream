## Description:

Generate competitive SEO content briefs with per-section word counts, competitor scoring, keyword density guidance, and page-type templates for new pages or existing-page improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, content teams, SEO specialists, and business owners use this skill to produce research-backed content briefs or outlines for target keywords, new pages, and existing-page improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may browse supplied URLs and competitor pages, which can expose private staging URLs, confidential pages, or sensitive business material to connected browsing or SEO tools.

Mitigation: Use only approved browsing and SEO tools for sensitive data, and avoid providing private or confidential URLs unless those tools are authorized for that material.

Risk: Generated SEO recommendations may be based on incomplete page access, unavailable sitemaps, or filtered competitor data.

Mitigation: Review the brief against the live site, sitemap, and business offering before assigning it to writers or publishing derived content.
