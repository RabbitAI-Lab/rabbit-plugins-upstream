## Description: <br>
Handles voice-to-voice conversations on WhatsApp. Automatically transcribes incoming audio and responds with local TTS audio. Use when the user wants to "talk" instead of type. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[rubenfb23](https://clawhub.ai/user/rubenfb23) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and operators use this skill to conduct WhatsApp conversations by voice. It transcribes incoming voice notes, handles the transcription as a prompt, and returns both text and local TTS audio. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Spoken WhatsApp messages may include private information that participants do not expect to be transcribed or used for automated replies. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when participants are comfortable with local transcription and automated voice responses. <br>
