## Description: <br>
Closing playbook for converting a successful ToB POC into a contract by structuring readiness checks, gap lists, one-page recap, quote action, and launch-node planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[william202404](https://clawhub.ai/user/william202404) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, product, and technical teams use this skill after a technically accepted POC to turn evidence, issue status, procurement path, quote readiness, and launch planning into a contract-closing plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Customer-facing recap, quote, or message drafts may contain incomplete, incorrect, or unauthorized deal details. <br>
Mitigation: Review all customer-facing outputs before sending and provide only the customer, budget, procurement, and deal details appropriate for the workspace. <br>
Risk: The closing plan can overstate readiness when decision-maker status, procurement path, or unresolved P0 issues are unknown. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's gap-list and not-ready paths until the buying process, issue disposition, and responsible owners are confirmed. <br>
