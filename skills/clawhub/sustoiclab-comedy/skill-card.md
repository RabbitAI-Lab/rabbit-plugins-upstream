## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language stand-up comedy writing toolbox that helps users turn observations and experiences into jokes through material discovery, joke structure, revision techniques, perspective shifts, and performance preparation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, performers, and writers use this skill to turn observations, personal experiences, and negative emotions into stand-up comedy material. It guides topic discovery, joke structure, drafting, revision, perspective shifts, and performance preparation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share personal experiences and negative emotions as comedy material. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid unnecessary sensitive details, anonymize real people where appropriate, and review outputs before performance or publication. <br>
