## Description:

Guides an agent in turning text, stories, scripts, and briefs into AI videos through writing, storyboarding, asset planning, shot production, editing, quality checks, delivery, and post-publication feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External video creators, writers, and marketing teams can use the skill to develop narrative or promotional text into structured AI-video production plans and deliverables. It supports workflows from scripts and storyboards through visual assets, generated shots, sound, editing, quality checks, delivery, and optional post-publication review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may update its shared instructions and reference materials.

Mitigation: Review and version-control changes, and inspect diffs before accepting updates.

Risk: Project prompts or images may be sent to external video services without a clear privacy check.

Mitigation: Review the skill's external-sharing practices and avoid sending confidential materials unless the service's privacy terms permit it.
