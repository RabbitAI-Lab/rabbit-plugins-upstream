## Description:

Search and analyze Swedish real estate on Booli, including active listings, sold prices, area lookup, and market statistics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish property use this skill to find for-sale listings, compare sold prices, inspect property details, and summarize local market statistics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Swedish real-estate data)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional browser bridge routes Booli requests through a third-party extension in the user's browser tab.

Mitigation: Review the extension source or verify its release integrity before installation, and approve pairing only if comfortable with that routing.
