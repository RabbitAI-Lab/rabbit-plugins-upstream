## Description:

Searches Swedish property listings, sold prices, areas, and market statistics on Booli.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Property researchers and homebuyers use this skill to find Swedish homes for sale, compare recent sales, and summarize local market prices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Swedish property data)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Booli lookups may require a separate browser extension and an open Booli tab.

Mitigation: Verify the extension source or release checksum and approve pairing only when intending to use Booli lookups.
