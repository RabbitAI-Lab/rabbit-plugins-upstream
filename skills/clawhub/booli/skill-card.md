## Description:

Search and analyse Swedish real estate on booli.se — active for-sale listings, sold prices (slutpriser), area resolution, and market statistics. Use when the user asks about Swedish property, Booli, bostäder, lägenheter, villor, or slutpriser.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to find Swedish homes for sale, inspect property details, compare sold prices, and review market statistics by area. It helps answer questions about Booli and Swedish residential property.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The browser bridge relies on a separate extension and a user-approved pairing flow.

Mitigation: Verify the extension download, compare the pairing code, and approve only the Booli domain and capabilities needed for searches.
