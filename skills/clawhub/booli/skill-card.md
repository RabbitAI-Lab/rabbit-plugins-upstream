## Description:

Search and analyze Swedish property listings, sold prices, locations, and market statistics from Booli.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish real estate use this skill to find for-sale properties, compare sold prices, and review local market statistics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional browser bridge relies on a third-party extension and access to an open Booli tab.

Mitigation: Install only if comfortable with the extension; build or verify its release, limit its use to the Booli tab, and consider a separate browser profile for sensitive sessions.
