## Description: <br>
Guides agents through comparative-advantage analysis for delegation, build-versus-buy, outsourcing, team specialization, and related allocation decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, operators, founders, and external users use this skill to decide who should perform which work by comparing opportunity-cost ratios, transaction costs, and re-evaluation triggers. It is suited to delegation, team division of labor, build-versus-buy, outsourcing, supply-chain, and personal specialization decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce misleading allocation guidance if the user's productivity estimates, opportunity costs, or transaction costs are incomplete or wrong. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit producers, tasks, productivity estimates, opportunity-cost ratios, transaction costs, and re-evaluation triggers before relying on the recommendation. <br>
Risk: The examples and economic framing may not fit every organization, legal context, or supply-chain constraint. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill's economic assumptions and examples for local fit before using it for business or operational decisions. <br>
