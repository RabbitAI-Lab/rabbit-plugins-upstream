## Description:

Formats bibliographic metadata for books, journal articles, theses, web resources, conference papers, and newspaper articles into GB/T 7714-2015 or APA 7 reference entries and in-text citations, marking missing fields instead of inventing them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and writers use this skill to convert raw or inconsistent source metadata into citation entries and in-text citations for Chinese GB/T 7714-2015 or English APA 7 workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated citations may be incorrect or incomplete when source metadata is missing or institutional formatting rules differ.

Mitigation: Verify generated entries against the selected citation standard and local publisher or institution requirements; keep missing fields visibly marked for user completion.
