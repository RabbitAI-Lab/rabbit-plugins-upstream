## Description:

Research public social profiles, posts, trends, Marketplace listings, and explicitly requested Reddit buying-intent leads through the Crawlora API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, marketers, and developers use this skill for public social listening, competitor research, trend tracking, and explicitly requested listings or lead discovery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search targets and the API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only for public, non-confidential research and provide the API key through the environment.

Risk: Lead scoring or profile data may be misused to target people or support high-impact decisions.

Mitigation: Run lead discovery only on explicit request; avoid sensitive personal-data targeting and high-impact decisions about people.
