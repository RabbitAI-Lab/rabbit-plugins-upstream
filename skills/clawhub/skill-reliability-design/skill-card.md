## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Some referenced resources are empty or missing, so parts of the reliability metrics or checklist workflow may be incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the packaged references and templates before relying on the skill for formal design reviews or reliability calculations. <br>
Risk: Reliability analysis can be misleading when FMEA scores or quantitative metrics are based on small samples or subjective assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Validate assumptions with cross-functional reviewers and sufficient field, test, or historical data before using recommendations for engineering decisions. <br>
