## Description: <br>
Turns vague social media ambitions into measurable business-aligned goals, SMART objectives, channel-specific North Star KPIs, supporting metrics, benchmark-set targets, guardrails, and action thresholds. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and social media teams use this skill to convert business outcomes into a focused social goal and KPI cascade. It is used to set targets and guardrails before analytics-and-reporting measures progress. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the skill's target-setting output for measured analytics or publishing automation. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as planning guidance only, then hand the targets to analytics-and-reporting for measurement and keep publishing outside this skill. <br>
Risk: KPI targets can become misleading if baselines are fabricated, stale, or not tied to business outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Set targets from real baseline or benchmark evidence, use a learning goal when no baseline exists, and pair growth metrics with quality guardrails. <br>
