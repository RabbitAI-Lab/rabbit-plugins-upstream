## Description:

Helps create detailed adult fitness coach and athlete characters with consistent appearance, clothing, and activity for character sheets and workout scenes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fitness creators, developers, and marketers can use this skill to define adult coaches, athletes, and fitness influencers, then prepare consistent character sheets and workout imagery or video. It requires a varg account through MCP or a VARG_API_KEY.

Global
