## Description:

Makes AI fitness characters on varg (coaches, gym creators, athletes, bodybuilders) with real muscle and skin, a locked signature outfit and colour, and catalog-style wording that gets sports bras, leggings and shirtless athletes past false moderation blocks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and product teams use this skill to develop consistent adult fitness coach, athlete, or gym influencer characters and prepare character sheets and workout imagery for fitness apps, activewear, and supplement campaigns. It provides prompt guidance for ordinary athletic clothing and activity descriptions that may otherwise be mistakenly blocked by image moderation.
