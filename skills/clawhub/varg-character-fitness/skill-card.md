## Description:

Creates fitness character prompts and reusable character sheets for activewear, workout scenes, and video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use the skill to define adult fitness characters for image generation and reuse consistent descriptions across activewear, workout, and video concepts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and images are sent to external services and may use Varg credits.

Mitigation: Check provider privacy terms and costs before submitting, and avoid sending confidential material.
