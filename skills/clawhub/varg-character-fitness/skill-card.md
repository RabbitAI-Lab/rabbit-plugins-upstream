## Description:

Helps create consistent AI fitness characters and prepare character sheets, workout scenes, and video prompts using Varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, fitness app teams, and marketers use the skill to define adult fitness characters and prepare consistent character sheets, workout stills, and video prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generation uses a Varg account and credits and sends submitted content to external services that were not included in this review.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, pinned single-skill installation, and check the external services and companion skills before sending content.
