## Description:

Organizes the division of shared belongings, accounts, expenses, and move-out tasks when a cohabiting relationship ends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People ending a shared household use this skill to inventory and divide belongings, audit joint accounts, reconcile expenses, and plan a move-out. It is an organizer, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The final report may miscalculate expense balances when shares are not 50/50.

Mitigation: Use the dedicated expense settle command and independently verify custom-share amounts before including them in an agreement.

Risk: The local record contains sensitive information about a former shared household.

Mitigation: Keep untangle.json private and archive or delete it when the process is complete.
