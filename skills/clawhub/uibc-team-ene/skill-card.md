## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The draft criterion does not cover semantic conflicts; a passing structure check alone cannot establish migration identity.

Mitigation: State the criterion's L3 ceiling and limitations, and require independently replayable evidence and human review of identity conclusions.

Risk: Applying domain-specific judging guidance outside the intended migration-accountability setting could mislead reviewers.

Mitigation: Limit use to UIBC ENE-domain TH-ENE-001 judging and accountability, as recommended by the security guidance.
