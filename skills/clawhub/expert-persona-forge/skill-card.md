## Description:

Creates reusable expert-persona skills with explicit decision rules, deliverables, and limits instead of one-off role-play prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junci003](https://clawhub.ai/user/junci003)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to turn recurring expert-role requests into installable SKILL.md files, or to diagnose and improve existing expert prompts. It is not a substitute for verified domain knowledge or licensed professional judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill defaults to creating a persistent file in the user's skills directory without clear confirmation or overwrite safeguards.

Mitigation: Preview the SKILL.md, confirm the destination path before writing, and do not overwrite existing files without explicit approval.
