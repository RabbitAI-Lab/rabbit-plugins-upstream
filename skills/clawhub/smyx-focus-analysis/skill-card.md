## Description: <br>
Real-time analysis of gaze direction and facial pose to quantify focus, distraction, and mind-wandering in classroom, workplace, meeting, or driving footage. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to analyze video or URL inputs for attention state, focus score, distraction indicators, trend information, and historical analysis reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security evidence says the skill uploads potentially sensitive classroom, workplace, meeting, or driving footage and report metadata to configured lifeemergence.com services. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with appropriate consent and avoid sensitive footage until retention, deletion, account isolation, and consent controls are documented. <br>
Risk: The security evidence says the skill silently creates or reuses persistent local identity and token state. <br>
Mitigation: Review local identity and token storage before installation, and run the skill only in an environment where that persistent state is acceptable. <br>
