## Description:

Analyzes video gaze direction and facial pose to estimate focus, distraction, and mind-wandering, and produces structured attention reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators, meeting organizers, and safety teams can analyze videos to review estimated attention levels, distraction patterns, and changes over time. The skill can also retrieve previously generated analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The no-analysis option may still upload videos, and automatic account handling may expose credentials.

Mitigation: Wait for these behaviors to be fixed before using sensitive recordings. Confirm the provider’s video retention and deletion practices, obtain authorization from recorded people, and use an isolated workspace with a dedicated account.
