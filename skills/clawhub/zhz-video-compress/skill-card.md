## Description:

Compress and organize individual videos or folders with configurable encoding, preview, and default preservation of the originals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bjbook](https://clawhub.ai/user/bjbook)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing video libraries use this skill to preview and batch compress videos, choose encoding and output options, and optionally organize results by capture month.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional replacement mode can erase another video or delete the source before the compressed result is safely saved.

Mitigation: Back up files, preview the selected folder, keep the default mode that preserves originals, and avoid --replace until the replacement issues are fixed.
