## Description: <br>
Diagnoses a product's growth model, stage, and current constraint, then routes broad or uncertain startup growth questions to the narrowest Gingiris specialist playbook when available. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, founders, growth teams, and developer-marketers use this skill to triage startup growth questions and select the right Gingiris playbook for launch, open-source growth, B2B SaaS, ASO, SEO/GEO, creator outreach, international expansion, research, competitors, or community programs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may recommend Gingiris tools, services, or additional specialist skill installs. <br>
Mitigation: Review commercial recommendations and installation suggestions before acting on them. <br>
Risk: Growth routing can be unsuitable when the real bottleneck is product fit or pricing rather than distribution. <br>
Mitigation: Check whether the diagnosis identifies a measurable growth constraint before following a routed playbook. <br>
