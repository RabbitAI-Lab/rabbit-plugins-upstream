## Description: <br>
Gingiris Growth Finder diagnoses startup growth questions by product type, stage, and channel gap, then routes the agent to the appropriate Gingiris specialist playbook. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill when they have a growth, launch, or go-to-market question and need an agent to triage the situation before choosing a specialist Gingiris playbook. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to appear for general marketing or launch discussions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the suggested specialist playbook matches the user's product type, growth stage, and channel gap before following routing, install, or paid-tool recommendations. <br>
Risk: A product-fit or pricing bottleneck could be mistaken for a distribution problem. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's stated scope checks to redirect product, pricing, fundraising, or pitch-deck questions outside this growth-routing workflow. <br>
