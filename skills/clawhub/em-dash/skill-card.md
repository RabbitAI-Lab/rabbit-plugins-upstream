## Description:

Expert on the history, origin, and correct use of the em dash. Use when writing or reviewing code, comments, or data files to avoid em and en dashes, defaulting to never using them and replacing any found with a hyphen (-). Includes strong knowledge of punctuation marks and the proper usage of punctuation characters when writing comments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, reviewers, and writing agents use this skill to keep code, comments, and data files free of em and en dashes unless a user or project style rule clearly requires otherwise.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally opinionated and may conflict with project, user, or publication style rules that permit em or en dashes.

Mitigation: Review local style requirements before applying the skill, and override its default hyphen preference when explicit project or user instructions require another punctuation style.
