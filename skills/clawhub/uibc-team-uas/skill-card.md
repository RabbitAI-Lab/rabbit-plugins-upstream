## Description:

Helps UIBC low-altitude-domain reviewers assess memory-transfer identity and accountability against the TH-UAS-001 criterion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Reviewers and agents working on UIBC UAS-domain accountability use the TH-UAS-001 rubric to draft migration decision rules, reproducible evidence packages, and failure-handling and responsibility statements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applying this domain-specific judging rubric to unrelated migration questions could mislead readers.

Mitigation: Use it for TH-UAS-001 or UAS-domain accountability tasks, not generic migration assessments.

Risk: A passing criteria-file check does not establish that a real migration is safe or compliant.

Mitigation: Independently review the underlying evidence and obtain human review before operational or regulatory decisions.
