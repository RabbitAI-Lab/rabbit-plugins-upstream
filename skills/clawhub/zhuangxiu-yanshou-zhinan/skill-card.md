## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation advice may not match local building codes, property management rules, or the specific conditions of a home.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations against local regulations, property rules, contracts, and site conditions before acting.

Risk: Structural changes, electrical work, waterproofing, chemical products, and cement work can create safety, property, or liability issues.

Mitigation: Use qualified tradespeople for high-risk work, follow product labels and PPE requirements, and document inspections such as water pressure tests and waterproofing checks.

Risk: Renovation questions can include sensitive contract, address, budget, or dispute details.

Mitigation: Redact personal and contract-sensitive information before sharing prompts or documents with third-party AI tools.
