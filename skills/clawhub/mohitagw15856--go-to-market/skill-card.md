## Description: <br>
Create a complete go-to-market asset pack for a product or feature, including positioning, messaging pillars, feature-to-benefit mapping, and role-specific use cases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Marketing, product, and launch teams use this skill to turn product briefs into GTM-ready positioning statements, messaging pillars, buyer benefit tables, and role-specific use cases. <br>

Risk: The skill can infer missing differentiators, proof points, features, or buyer details when the source brief is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review all assumptions and inferred proof points before using the generated GTM content externally. <br>
