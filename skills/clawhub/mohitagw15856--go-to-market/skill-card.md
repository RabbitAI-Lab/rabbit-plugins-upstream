## Description:

Create go-to-market assets for any product or feature, including positioning, messaging pillars, feature-to-benefit mapping, and role-specific use cases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product marketers, founders, and product managers use this skill to draft launch-ready GTM materials from a product or feature brief. It produces positioning, messaging pillars, buyer benefits, and role-specific use cases that can be adapted for sales decks, landing pages, launch emails, and internal alignment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inferred differentiators, competitors, metrics, or proof points could be inaccurate or unsuitable for external marketing.

Mitigation: Confirm assumptions against product, sales, legal, and public messaging sources before using the output in sales, investor, or public-facing materials.
