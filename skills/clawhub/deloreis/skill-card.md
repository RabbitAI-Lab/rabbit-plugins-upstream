## Description:

Keep fiction and worldbuilding consistent with De Lore Is. Use when maintaining lore for AI-assisted novels, series bibles, campaigns, competing story ideas, retcons, or checks on generated additions to a fictional world. Record and query persistent lore through local MCP tools. Also use for explicit De Lore Is, DLI, or deloreis project requests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External fiction writers and worldbuilders use this skill to query and maintain structured lore, check proposed additions for continuity, and explore alternate story accounts while keeping manuscript prose in its existing files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A staged change can be discarded while a directly recorded decision remains in the project.

Mitigation: Keep project backups and inspect decision history; use a disposable project for the first-session example.

Risk: Accepting an unreviewed lore proposal can introduce incorrect continuity into a story.

Mitigation: Have the writer decide which details are established, inspect findings, and use their rulings to resolve conflicts.
