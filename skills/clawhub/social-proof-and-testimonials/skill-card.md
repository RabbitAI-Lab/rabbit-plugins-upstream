## Description: <br>
Turns real customer reviews, testimonials, UGC, case studies, and results into believable, objection-matched social proof content using the VOUCH framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, social media operators, and agents use this skill to turn real, consented customer proof into compliant social content that answers buyer objections. It helps frame testimonials, reviews, UGC, and case-study material without fabricating claims, ratings, customers, or results. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A testimonial, result, rating, or UGC asset could be used before its authenticity, consent, rights, or disclosure status is confirmed. <br>
Mitigation: Require a named human or external review step before publication to confirm the proof is real, consented, cleared for the intended use, and disclosed where material connections exist. <br>
Risk: Proof content could imply typical, guaranteed, medical, financial, or legal outcomes that the underlying evidence does not support. <br>
Mitigation: Keep claims in the customer's real context, avoid guarantees, disclose generally expected results when required, and route regulated or YMYL claims to qualified review. <br>
