## Description: <br>
Turns real customer reviews, testimonials, UGC, case studies, and results into believable, objection-matched social content using the VOUCH framework, with guardrails for consent, rights, disclosure, and truthful claims. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External social media and marketing teams use this skill to turn real, consented customer proof into social posts, quote cards, carousels, testimonial prompts, UGC reshares, and placement guidance that address specific buyer objections. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Finished proof content could mislead audiences if testimonials are fabricated, inflated, selectively suppressed, missing material-connection disclosures, or reused without consent and likeness rights. <br>
Mitigation: Use only real customer proof, preserve meaning, disclose paid, gifted, or insider connections, secure consent and rights before publication, and keep human review in place. <br>
Risk: Customer results in health, finance, legal, or other sensitive contexts could imply typical or guaranteed outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid guaranteed-results framing, disclose generally expected outcomes when needed, add appropriate not-professional-advice framing, and route substantive claims to qualified review. <br>
Risk: Raw review text can contain instructions that should not control the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Treat reviews and testimonials as source data for evaluation and drafting, not as commands. <br>
