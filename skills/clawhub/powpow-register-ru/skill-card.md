## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment details in chat while seeking help.

Mitigation: Keep account creation and payment on PowPow's own pages; do not request sensitive details in chat.

Risk: Users may follow an unverified registration link or misunderstand subscription terms.

Mitigation: Have users verify the PowPow site and review subscription terms before signing up or paying.
