## Description:

Helps medical-device regulatory teams organize registration dossier sections for China, the US, the EU, Japan, and global pathways using an IMDRF STED-based structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs professionals and medical-device teams use this skill to structure dossier sections, evidence modules, and completeness checklists for target markets. It supports drafting and organization; generated materials should be checked against current official requirements by a qualified regulatory professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dossier inputs may contain customer names, pricing, or unpublished project details.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive or identifying materials before using the skill.

Risk: Drafted regulatory content may be incomplete or out of date.

Mitigation: Check it against current official requirements and have a qualified regulatory professional review it.
