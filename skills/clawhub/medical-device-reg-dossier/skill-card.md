## Description:

医疗器械注册申报资料编写助手，按中国 NMPA、美国 FDA、欧盟 MDR、日本 PMDA 和全球协调路径汇编注册申报资料模块、分类判定和资料清单。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs, quality, and medical-device documentation teams use this skill to structure submission dossiers and checklists for NMPA, FDA 510(k)/eSTAR, EU MDR, PMDA, and IMDRF STED-aligned workflows. It helps assemble summaries, study materials, clinical evaluation content, labeling modules, classification notes, and document lists for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements may change or may be misapplied to a specific device, market, or submission pathway.

Mitigation: Verify generated dossier content against current official agency sources and qualified regulatory affairs or legal review before live submissions.

Risk: Drafting with real project details may expose sensitive business, customer, pricing, or unpublished product information.

Mitigation: Remove or anonymize sensitive business and project details before using the skill.
