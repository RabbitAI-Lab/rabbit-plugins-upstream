## Description: <br>
Provides a structured competitive analysis playbook for solopreneurs to identify competitors, dissect their strategy, product, pricing, marketing, reviews, and trajectory, and turn findings into positioning gaps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jk-0001](https://clawhub.ai/user/jk-0001) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, solopreneurs, and business operators use this skill to structure competitor research, benchmark an offering against direct and indirect alternatives, and identify exploitable gaps for positioning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad competitor-related prompts and lead to more extensive research than the user intended. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a deep competitive analysis before expanding into multi-layer competitor research. <br>
Risk: Competitive research can involve private business context or sources that may not be appropriate to use. <br>
Mitigation: Keep research limited to lawful public sources unless the user intentionally provides private business context for the analysis. <br>
