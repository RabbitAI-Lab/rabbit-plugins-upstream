## Description:

Local SEO analysis covering Google Business Profile optimization, NAP consistency, citations, reviews, local schema, location pages, multi-location SEO, and industry-specific recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and SEO practitioners use this skill to audit a local business website and generate prioritized recommendations for GBP, NAP, citations, reviews, schema, location pages, and local authority signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may fetch and inspect public web pages, business directories, and optional paid-tool data during analysis.

Mitigation: Run it only on URLs and business listings appropriate for review, and confirm any paid-tool or directory-derived findings before acting on them.

Risk: Generated SEO recommendations can include claims based on external statistics or third-party data.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current business context and authoritative sources before implementation.
