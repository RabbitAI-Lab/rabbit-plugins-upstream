## Description: <br>
超能去AI rewrites Chinese AI-generated text into more natural creative, social, and informal prose using 13 editor personas. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and writers use this skill to rewrite Chinese AI-generated drafts for creative writing, social posts, speeches, and other informal prose. It is not intended for academic, legal, medical, news, technical, business, citation-heavy, or other factual documents where exact facts, numbers, provenance, and structure must be preserved. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may restructure text or add invented details while making prose sound more human. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for creative, informal, or marketing-style Chinese text, and review the rewritten output before sharing or publishing. <br>
Risk: Facts, numbers, citations, provenance, and document structure may not be preserved exactly. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use it for academic, legal, medical, news, evidence, technical, or business documents; for any factual draft, compare output against the source and restore exact details manually. <br>
