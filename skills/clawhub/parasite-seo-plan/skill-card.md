## Description:

Plan parasite SEO (barnacle SEO) that lasts. Pick which third-party platforms, such as Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Quora or Medium, are worth publishing on for your target searches and AI answers, scored on ranking power, AI citations, rules and ban risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO and marketing teams use this skill to decide which third-party platforms are worth testing for their target searches and AI answers. It turns search evidence, platform policies, effort, and account risk into platform recommendations, publishing rules, and a measurement plan.
