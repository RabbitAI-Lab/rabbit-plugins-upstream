## Description: <br>
Helps agents slow down obvious-seeming but consequential decisions by identifying fast System 1 judgments, recruiting System 2 checks, and documenting calibration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to structure high-stakes or unfamiliar decisions, especially when a fast answer, team consensus, or fluent AI output needs deliberate verification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on advisory decision logs for sensitive personal, legal, medical, or financial decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as structured reasoning aids, avoid unnecessary sensitive details in prompts, and require qualified review for high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: A fluent AI answer may be accepted as correct without checking load-bearing claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's System 2 recruitment steps: verify primary sources, test code or numbers, compare alternatives, and document why the final answer is justified. <br>
