## Description: <br>
Design statistically rigorous A/B tests for product features, UI changes, onboarding flows, and pricing experiments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, growth teams, analysts, and product engineers use this skill to plan A/B tests, estimate sample size and duration, define guardrail metrics, and interpret experiment outcomes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sample-size or duration estimates may be used for important business decisions without validation in an experimentation platform. <br>
Mitigation: Validate sample-size calculations and test setup with the team's analytics or experimentation platform before launch. <br>
Risk: Premature result checking, missing guardrails, or unclear rollback thresholds can lead to false positives or harmful rollouts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the planned sample size, guardrail metrics, rollback trigger, and interpretation thresholds before declaring an experiment outcome. <br>
