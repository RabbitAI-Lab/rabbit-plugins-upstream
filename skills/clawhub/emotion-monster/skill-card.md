## Description:

Emotion Monster helps caregivers guide children ages 2-6 through recognizing, recording, and expressing emotions with Traditional Chinese terminal tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Caregivers and parents use this skill with young children to show emotion cards, keep a local mood diary, review mood trends, and follow step-by-step emotion coaching prompts during difficult moments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A child's mood history may be sensitive and is retained on the local machine.

Mitigation: Use a private account or device, avoid unprotected sync or shared folders, and delete ~/.bookshelf-plus/kids/mood_diary.json when the records are no longer needed.
