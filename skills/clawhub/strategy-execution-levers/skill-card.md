## Description:

Diagnoses strategy execution breakdowns across four phases and guides selection and measurement of a targeted intervention.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Strategy leads, managers, and advisors use this framework to identify where execution is breaking down, choose a focused intervention, and define behavioral measures for a four-week trial.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The framework's effectiveness figures may be mistaken for controlled research results.

Mitigation: Treat the figures as directional estimates, as the source material states, and validate proposed measures against local results.

Risk: Organizational changes proposed through the diagnostic may be unsuitable for the local context.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes and measures with people who understand the organization before acting on them.
