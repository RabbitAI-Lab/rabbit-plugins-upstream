## Description: <br>
Activates when a clear strategy is not changing behavior, launch energy fades, recurring post-mortem findings persist, or execution falls short despite repeated planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, leaders, and strategy operators use this skill to diagnose which phase of strategy execution is failing and select a focused intervention with an owner, metric, baseline, target, and trial window. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat directional effectiveness figures as experimentally proven outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Present the figures as directional estimates and require local behavioral measurements with a baseline, target, timeframe, and four-week trial result. <br>
Risk: The skill may be applied when execution failure is caused by an invalid strategy or resource constraints rather than behavioral or process gaps. <br>
Mitigation: Use the documented fit checks before diagnosis and decline or redirect when the strategy itself is unvalidated or resources are the primary blocker. <br>
