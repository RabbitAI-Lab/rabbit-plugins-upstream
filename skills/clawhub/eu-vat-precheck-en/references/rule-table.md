## Standard VAT Rates (2026)
- Germany 19%
- France 20%
- Italy 22%
- Spain 21%
- UK 20% (note: the UK has left the EU; its rules are independent of the EU VAT system)

## OSS Threshold & Place of Supply
- **Sellers established in only one EU member state**: once EU-wide distance sales exceed €10,000/year, the place of supply becomes the destination country and sales must be declared via OSS.
- **Non-EU established sellers (e.g. China-based cross-border sellers)**: NOT eligible for the €10,000 threshold. From the very first intra-EU distance sale (e.g. sales from pan-European FBA stock), the place of supply is the destination country — OSS registration or separate VAT registration in each destination country is required.
- Intra-EU cross-border stock transfers (e.g. FBA pan-European movements) may themselves trigger a registration obligation in the destination country, regardless of sales volume.

## IOSS Scope
- IOSS applies only to B2C sales of low-value goods imported into the EU with a consignment value ≤ €150.
- Orders above €150 must go through standard import clearance (import VAT + duties) and cannot use IOSS.
