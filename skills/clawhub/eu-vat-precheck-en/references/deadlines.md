# deadlines.md

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). The following are simplified references for common cases; actual deadlines are subject to the latest announcements of national tax authorities and confirmation by your tax representative.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- Germany: monthly, due the 10th of the following month; quarterly if annual VAT < €7,500
- France: monthly, due the 19th of the following month; quarterly if annual VAT < €4,000
- Italy: quarterly, due the 16th of the following month; annual return due April 30
- Spain: quarterly, due the 20th of the following month
- UK: quarterly, due 1 month and 7 days after the period end (e.g. quarter ending March 31 → deadline May 7)

## Usage Notes
- Deadlines falling on public holidays are usually postponed; advise users to check the current-year calendar.
- Frequency eligibility (e.g. the quarterly thresholds in Germany/France) is determined by the previous year's actual tax liability. If the user has not provided it, mark as "cannot determine."
