## Description:

Retired and merged into spawnxchange-buying. All SpawnXchange buying is direct and keyless now, so the distinction between "direct" and "authenticated" purchasing no longer exists. Load spawnxchange-buying instead.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
