## Description:

Bank Market Research helps bank teams analyze industries, competitors, market trends, market sizing, opportunities, and risks to produce structured market intelligence reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bank strategy, product, relationship management, and research teams use this skill to structure market research, competitive intelligence, opportunity assessment, and risk-aware report drafting. It is intended as research support, not investment advice, a credit decision, or a substitute for internal approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market, regulatory, and banking-sector facts may become stale or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current market data, regulatory positions, and cited sources against official or authorized sources before relying on the output.

Risk: Research output could be mistaken for an automated investment, lending, or credit decision.

Mitigation: Use the skill as research support only and keep final lending, investment, and credit decisions within normal internal approval processes.

Risk: Users may include sensitive customer information, nonpublic financial data, or unauthorized internal data in prompts or reports.

Mitigation: Use only authorized public information or desensitized internal data, and review outputs before saving or distributing them.
