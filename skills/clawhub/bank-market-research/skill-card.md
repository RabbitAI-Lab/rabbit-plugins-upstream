## Description:

Helps bank teams research industries and competitors, size markets, and prepare structured opportunity and risk assessments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bank strategy, product, relationship, and research teams use this skill to prepare industry and competitor analyses, market-sizing estimates, regional risk assessments, and market intelligence reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (China-focused banking and regulatory examples)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad market and industry prompts may invoke the banking research template unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a banking-focused analysis before applying the template.

Risk: Business inputs or reports could expose sensitive customer information.

Mitigation: Use only public, authorized, or de-identified data, and review reports before saving or sharing them.

Risk: Market, regulatory, or credit-risk conclusions may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify key figures and current rules against authoritative sources and require internal review before bank decisions.
