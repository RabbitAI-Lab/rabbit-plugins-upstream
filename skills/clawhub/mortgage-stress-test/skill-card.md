## Description:

Use when buying a home, comparing rent vs buy, or checking how much house you can honestly afford. Computes true monthly cost (PMI, tax, insurance, maintenance), 28/36 DTI verdicts, rate-shock stress (+1/+2%), and rent-vs-buy break-even horizon.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Home buyers and renters use this skill to evaluate affordability beyond the advertised mortgage payment, compare renting with buying, and assess rate increases against their budget. It helps identify when a scenario exceeds common debt-to-income targets and highlights assumptions that need verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documented examples and calculator results may disagree, which can make an affordability or rent-versus-buy estimate misleading.

Mitigation: Treat results as planning estimates, independently verify calculations and assumptions, and confirm loan terms against the lender's Loan Estimate before making a decision.

Risk: Custom insurance assumptions may not be reflected consistently across every calculation.

Mitigation: Check the insurance amount used in each reported result and recalculate independently when it materially affects the decision.
