## Description: <br>
Reviews webpages or design screenshots from a product-manager perspective and produces structured quality findings across strategy, user experience, content, metrics, performance, market fit, iteration, and optional specialist audits. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhenyangze](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhenyangze) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, designers, and developers use this skill to review live pages or screenshots and produce prioritized PM-style findings, scoring, alternatives, and measurable next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Reviews of URLs or screenshots may use external web or image-analysis tools. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting private screenshots, internal-only URLs, logged-in pages, customer data, credentials, or confidential product plans unless the configured tools are trusted. <br>
Risk: Product review recommendations can be incomplete or misleading if the supplied page context is partial. <br>
Mitigation: Review findings before acting on them and provide business goals, target users, success metrics, and screenshots or page access when higher confidence is needed. <br>
