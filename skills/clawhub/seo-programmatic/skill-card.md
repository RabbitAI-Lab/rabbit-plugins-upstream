## Description:

Programmatic SEO planning and analysis for pages generated at scale from data sources, covering template engines, URL patterns, internal linking automation, thin content safeguards, and index bloat prevention.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, SEO practitioners, developers, and content operations teams use this skill to plan or audit data-driven page sets before publishing them at scale. It helps assess data quality, page template uniqueness, URL structure, internal linking, canonical and sitemap strategy, and thin-content or index-bloat risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives advisory SEO guidance about publishing large numbers of generated pages, which could be misapplied as automatic publishing approval.

Mitigation: Require human review of dated enforcement claims, quality gates, and rollout decisions before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Programmatic page sets can create thin content or index bloat when source data or templates are insufficiently differentiated.

Mitigation: Use the skill's quality gates, sample review, progressive rollout, canonicalization, sitemap, and noindex guidance before publication.
