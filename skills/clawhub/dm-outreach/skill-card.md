## Description: <br>
Draft personalized Instagram DMs for qualified leads with focused templates, saved as files for manual sending and follow-up outreach. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[visualdeptcreative](https://clawhub.ai/user/visualdeptcreative) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and sales operators use this skill to draft concise, personalized Instagram outreach and follow-up messages for qualified leads. The drafts are saved for human review and manual sending. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated outreach may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for a specific recipient. <br>
Mitigation: Review every generated DM before sending and adjust personalization, tone, and claims as needed. <br>
Risk: Automated Instagram messaging can trigger platform enforcement or account bans. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only as a drafting aid and send messages manually through Instagram. <br>
Risk: The generated CSV contains contact and outreach data. <br>
Mitigation: Store and share the CSV according to the user's contact-data handling practices. <br>
Risk: Repeated runs on the same day may overwrite an existing draft file. <br>
Mitigation: Check whether the target output file already exists before generating additional batches. <br>
