## Description:

Generate original Farsight commercial video intros by studying the structure of inspiration videos while avoiding reuse of their identifying content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and production teams use this skill to plan and produce original commercial intro sequences from the structural pacing and cinematography of a permitted reference video, project documents, and their own creative direction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Downloading a reference video may use material without permission or carry source identifiers into a commercial work.

Mitigation: Use only references you have rights or permission to use; study structure without reusing names, text, characters, or distinctive content.

Risk: Reading project documents and sending prompt material to selected media engines may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review the documents and prompt material before use, and choose image and video engines you trust.

Risk: The workflow downloads media and writes project folders and generated assets to disk.

Mitigation: Confirm the target directory and reference link before running the workflow.
