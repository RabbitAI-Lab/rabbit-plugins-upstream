## Description:

Guides teams in structuring AI pilots with measurable baselines, precommitted success and stop criteria, staged autonomy, and evidence-based scale, revise, or kill decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business and operations leaders use this skill to scope an AI pilot, set ownership and baseline metrics, define success and stop conditions, and make a documented scale, revise, or kill decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Timelines, thresholds, and the autonomy ladder are planning heuristics and may not fit a particular workflow or risk profile.

Mitigation: Validate proposed criteria against local operational and safety requirements, and keep integration and rollout decisions under human control.
