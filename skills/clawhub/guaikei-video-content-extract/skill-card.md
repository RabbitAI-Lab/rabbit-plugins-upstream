## Description:

Turns complete video files or public video links into polished transcripts, summaries, subtitles, and prompt-guided written content through GuaiKei's cloud service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to transcribe completed videos, extract spoken content, and draft summaries or adaptations for publishing and review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected videos, prompts, task IDs, and the API token are shared with GuaiKei's cloud service.

Mitigation: Submit only videos intended for remote processing; avoid sensitive recordings unless the provider's privacy and retention terms are acceptable, and protect the API token.

Risk: Transcription can lose accuracy with noisy audio, overlapping speakers, or specialized terminology.

Mitigation: Review important transcripts and generated claims against the original recording before relying on or publishing them.
