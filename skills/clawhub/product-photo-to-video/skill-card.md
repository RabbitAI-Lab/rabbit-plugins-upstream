## Description:

Turn one to three product photos into a product video ad while preserving the product's appearance and label.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and ecommerce teams use the skill to turn product photos into short social ads, listing videos, and product b-roll. It guides image preparation, storyboarding, video generation, and final packshot compositing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos and prompts are processed by varg and applicable model providers.

Mitigation: Use images you are authorized to share, remove sensitive details, and review processing and retention terms.

Risk: Video generation and repair steps can incur paid usage.

Mitigation: Estimate each paid step and get approval before running it.

Risk: Generated footage can alter labels, product details, or imply unsupported results.

Mitigation: Inspect outputs for fidelity and factual claims; composite the real packshot where needed and remove misleading effects.
