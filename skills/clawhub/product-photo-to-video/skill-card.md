## Description:

Turns one to three product photos into short product videos and listing clips while keeping the real product and its label prominent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and ecommerce teams use this skill to turn product photos into short-form social ads and product-page videos without filming.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos may include confidential or unreleased imagery sent to external generation services.

Mitigation: Confirm that sharing the images is acceptable before submitting them, especially for confidential or unreleased products.

Risk: The documented installer is unpinned, and security scan coverage is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the installer and skill files before use; treat the benign scan verdict as reassuring but not as a guarantee.
