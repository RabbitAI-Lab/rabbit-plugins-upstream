## Description:

Turns one to three product photos into short product videos and ads while preserving the product’s appearance and label.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and ecommerce teams can use the skill to turn product photos into short ads, listing videos, unboxing clips, or product loops without filming.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos and related details are sent to external services as part of the workflow.

Mitigation: Use only images and product details you are comfortable sharing with those services.

Risk: The installation source uses an unpinned CLI version, and the security scan had incomplete coverage.

Mitigation: Review the installation source and unresolved dependency reference, and pin the CLI version before use.
