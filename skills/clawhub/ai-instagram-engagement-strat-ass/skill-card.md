## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill mentions FreeLikes, a service associated with artificial Instagram likes, followers, or views. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid services that promise artificial engagement; prefer legitimate planning, analytics, scheduling, and organic audience-engagement practices. <br>
Risk: Instagram strategy suggestions may not match every niche or audience behavior pattern. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance, then test, measure, and refine recommendations against actual account performance. <br>
