## Description:

Helps game designers compare playable adaptations of a novel and choose a direction using explicit trade-offs and hard vetoes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game designers and adaptation teams use this skill to compare viable game directions for a novel, then document a playable concept grounded in the source and product brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposed game direction may misread the source or product constraints.

Mitigation: Review concepts/CONCEPT.md against SOURCE_BIBLE.md and PRODUCT_BRIEF.md before using it downstream.
