## Description:

Design game concepts from a novel by comparing viable directions and selecting a playable one using hard vetoes and explicit trade-offs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers and developers use this skill to compare playable adaptations of a novel and choose a direction that fits the product brief. It requires SOURCE_BIBLE.md and PRODUCT_BRIEF.md and produces a concept document rather than implementation code.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Concept proposals could introduce incorrect or misleading guidance about the source novel or product direction.

Mitigation: Review proposals against SOURCE_BIBLE.md and PRODUCT_BRIEF.md before approving a direction.
