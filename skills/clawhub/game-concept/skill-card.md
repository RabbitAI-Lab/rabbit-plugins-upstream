## Description:

Turns a novel's source bible and product brief into a playable game direction by comparing viable concepts, applying hard vetoes, and documenting the trade-offs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game designers and adaptation teams use this skill to select and validate a playable direction for a novel within an existing product brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Missing or unclear source and product documents can undermine the concept decision.

Mitigation: Provide a clear SOURCE_BIBLE.md and PRODUCT_BRIEF.md; stop and request either document if it is missing.

Risk: Borrowed game precedents could be mistaken for permission to reuse protected assets.

Mitigation: Reject concepts dependent on copying another game's protected characters, maps, interface, text, or assets.
