## Description:

Creates photoreal, bizarre-but-believable deadpan AI characters and a repeatable posting format with consistent character sheets, scenes, and actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to design original, photoreal deadpan characters and establish a repeatable short-video format. It provides character concepts, prompt seeds, consistency guidance, and advice on using footage the creator owns or has licensed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected footage or images will be processed by external providers, which may involve privacy exposure and credit costs.

Mitigation: Upload only media you are authorized to share, check each provider's privacy terms and credit costs, and review the separate varg integration before connecting it.
