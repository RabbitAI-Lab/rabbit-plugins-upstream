## Description:

Creates photoreal, deadpan AI character concepts, character sheets, and repeatable social video formats using Varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to develop original, deadpan AI characters and plan repeatable posts with consistent looks and actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated persona or format could imitate an existing character or be mistaken for a real person.

Mitigation: Create original designs and label accounts featuring AI-generated people as AI.

Risk: Using unlicensed footage or sharing prompts and media with an external generation service can create rights or privacy concerns.

Mitigation: Use footage you own or have licensed, and review prompts and media before sending them to Varg.

Risk: Companion skills, remote services, and the larger plugin bundle are outside this release's security review.

Mitigation: Prefer the single-skill installation, review and pin versions where possible, and assess related services separately.
