## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab hackathons, choose small and reviewable contributions, and track eligibility and scoring requirements. It emphasizes valid contributions and avoiding score manipulation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A GitLab token could have access beyond the projects needed for the hackathon work.

Mitigation: Use a token limited to the intended projects.

Risk: Public contributions or issue changes may be inappropriate or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review public contributions and issue changes, and inspect the separately referenced gitlab-agent skill before using it.
