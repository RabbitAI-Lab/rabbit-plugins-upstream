## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External contributors and developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab hackathons, identify qualifying and reviewable contributions, and track eligibility, scoring, and deadlines. It emphasizes checking current official rules and avoiding spam or misleading activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab activity may affect public projects, and credentials may grant access beyond the intended work.

Mitigation: Limit the GitLab token to intended projects, review public changes before posting, and inspect the separately required gitlab-agent skill before GitLab operations.
