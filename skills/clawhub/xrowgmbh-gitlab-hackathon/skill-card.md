## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and GitLab contributors use this skill to check hackathon requirements, choose feasible contributions, and organize fair, reviewable work that can qualify under the current rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab credentials can enable account actions across projects.

Mitigation: Limit the token to intended projects and confirm the target project before running commands.

Risk: Hackathon submissions, issue changes, or merge request activity could be misleading or violate event rules.

Mitigation: Check current rules and review each submission and change; avoid spam, inaccurate labels, and unjustified issue closures.
