## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab hackathons, find reviewable contributions, and track eligibility and scoring requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab credentials may grant broader access than needed for planned contributions.

Mitigation: Use credentials limited to the projects and permissions needed for your contributions.

Risk: GitLab operations rely on the separately required gitlab-agent skill, whose behavior is outside this package.

Mitigation: Review the gitlab-agent skill before use.
