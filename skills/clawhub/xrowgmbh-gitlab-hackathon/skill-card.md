## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers participating in GitLab hackathons use this skill to verify current event requirements, choose manageable legitimate contributions, and organize their work around review and merge deadlines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optimizing for points can lead to low-value or rule-breaking activity that harms maintainers or risks disqualification.

Mitigation: Prioritize legitimate, reviewable contributions; verify current rules and avoid spam, padded commits, incorrect labels, and invalid issue closures.
