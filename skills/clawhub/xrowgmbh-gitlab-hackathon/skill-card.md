## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and external contributors use this skill to plan GitLab hackathon participation, choose qualifying work, track scoring requirements, and avoid unfair or spam-like activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab actions such as comments, labels, issue closures, and merge requests can affect public projects or hackathon scoring.

Mitigation: Review proposed GitLab actions before execution and use a token with limited scopes.

Risk: Outdated event rules, dates, score values, or prize requirements can lead to ineligible or unfair participation.

Mitigation: Verify current hackathon pages, API data, and contribution point guidance before acting.
