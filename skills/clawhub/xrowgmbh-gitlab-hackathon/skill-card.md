## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab hackathons, identify achievable contributions, and track eligibility and scoring requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hackathon dates, eligibility rules, scoring, and prize requirements can change.

Mitigation: Recheck the linked live event pages and contribution guide before choosing work or relying on point values.

Risk: Opening merge requests, commenting, labeling, or closing issues can affect shared projects and reviewers.

Mitigation: Make genuine, reviewable contributions; link only relevant issues; and comment, label, or close only when valid.
