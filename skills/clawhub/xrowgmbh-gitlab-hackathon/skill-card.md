## Description: <br>
Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External contributors and developers use this skill to plan GitLab hackathon participation, select qualifying issues and merge requests, track scoring opportunities, and avoid unfair or rule-abusing behavior. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A GitLab token with broad scopes could allow actions outside the intended projects. <br>
Mitigation: Use a token limited to the projects and scopes needed for the planned hackathon work. <br>
Risk: GitLab actions such as comments, labels, issue closures, or merge requests can affect public project workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed GitLab actions before execution and apply only changes that are accurate, fair, and relevant. <br>
