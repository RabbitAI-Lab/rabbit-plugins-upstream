## Description:

Helps contributors plan fair GitLab hackathon participation by checking rules, selecting qualifying work, tracking scoring, and avoiding rule abuse.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

GitLab contributors use this skill to plan eligible hackathon contributions, choose reviewable issues and merge requests, and check current rules before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A GitLab token or automated actions could affect projects beyond the intended contribution.

Mitigation: Use a minimally scoped GitLab token and review GitLab actions before execution.

Risk: Outdated rules or score-driven comments, labels, closures, and merge requests could cause ineligible or disruptive activity.

Mitigation: Verify current hackathon rules and keep contribution actions manual or clearly user-directed; avoid spam and unsupported changes.
