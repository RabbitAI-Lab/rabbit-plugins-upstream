## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Hackathon participants and developers use this skill to check current GitLab event rules, prioritize reviewable contributions, and track legitimate scoring opportunities and submission requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab credentials used for account actions may grant more access than needed.

Mitigation: Use an appropriately scoped GitLab token.

Risk: Outdated rules or unreviewed comments, labels, issue closures, and merge requests may lead to ineligible or harmful actions.

Mitigation: Check current hackathon rules and manually review every GitLab content change before execution.
