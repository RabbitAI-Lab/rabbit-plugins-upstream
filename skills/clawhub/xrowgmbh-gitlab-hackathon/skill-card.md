## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers participating in GitLab Quarterly or Transcend Hackathons use the skill to confirm current eligibility and rules, choose reviewable contributions, and track scoring while avoiding abusive or misleading activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hackathon dates, eligibility, scoring, and prize requirements may change between events.

Mitigation: Check the current official rules, dates, deadlines, and prize requirements before planning or acting.

Risk: GitLab credentials and proposed contributions can affect public projects and issue or merge-request records.

Mitigation: Use credentials limited to the necessary projects and permissions, and review intended public contributions and changes before submitting them.
