## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers use this skill to plan fair GitLab hackathon participation, identify qualifying contributions, and track the rules and deadlines that affect eligibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hackathon rules, dates, deadlines, and scoring can change, which could make a planned contribution ineligible.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements on the official GitLab hackathon pages and API before choosing work or acting on scoring guidance.
