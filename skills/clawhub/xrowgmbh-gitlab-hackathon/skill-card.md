## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab hackathons, identify manageable contributions, track eligibility and scoring requirements, and avoid exploitative activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A GitLab token may grant access beyond the projects needed for hackathon work.

Mitigation: Limit token permissions to intended projects and define which contribution and issue changes the agent may perform.

Risk: Changing event rules or deadlines can make otherwise sound participation guidance stale.

Mitigation: Recheck current event rules, dates, eligibility, and prize requirements before participating.
