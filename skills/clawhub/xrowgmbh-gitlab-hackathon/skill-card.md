## Description:

Plan fair GitLab hackathon participation by checking event rules, selecting qualifying contributions, tracking scoring, and avoiding rule abuse.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Hackathon participants and developers use this skill to plan legitimate GitLab contributions, choose reviewable issues and merge requests, and check eligibility before taking action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab actions using the user's token can alter public projects or affect account reputation.

Mitigation: Review and approve comments, labeling, issue closures, and merge requests before posting; use only a token with needed permissions.

Risk: Event rules, eligibility, deadlines, and scoring may change between hackathons.

Mitigation: Verify the current event pages and contribution points guide before planning or submitting work.
