## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

GitLab contributors use this skill to plan legitimate hackathon work, identify reviewable issues and merge requests, check eligibility and scoring rules, and track potential rule-abuse pitfalls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab account access can modify issues, merge requests, comments, labels, or issue status.

Mitigation: Use a least-privilege GitLab token, review proposed actions, and assess the referenced gitlab-agent skill separately.

Risk: Hackathon dates, eligibility, and scoring rules may change.

Mitigation: Verify current rules on the official hackathon pages before acting or submitting work.
