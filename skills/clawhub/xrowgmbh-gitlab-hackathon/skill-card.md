## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues and merge requests, tracking scoring levers, and maintaining an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, identify feasible contributions, and track eligibility and scoring requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A GitLab token with broad access could permit actions beyond the projects or permissions needed.

Mitigation: Use a token limited to the relevant projects and required permissions.

Risk: Comments, labels, and issue closures affect shared GitLab projects and can create cleanup work if inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review each proposed comment, label, or issue closure for accuracy and relevance before applying it.
