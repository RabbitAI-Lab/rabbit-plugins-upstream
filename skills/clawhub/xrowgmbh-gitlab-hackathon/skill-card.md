## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to plan legitimate contributions to GitLab hackathons, choose reviewable issues and merge requests, and track eligibility and scoring requirements without spam or score manipulation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab contributions, comments, labels, and justified issue closures persist in the account’s projects.

Mitigation: Review each action before execution, limit account access to intended projects, and follow the separate gitlab-agent skill for GitLab operations.
