## Description:

Helps contributors plan fair GitLab hackathon participation by checking rules, choosing qualifying issues and merge requests, tracking scoring, and avoiding rule abuse.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

GitLab contributors use this skill to check current hackathon requirements, choose reviewable contributions, and plan legitimate issue and merge-request activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A GitLab token may grant broader access than hackathon work requires.

Mitigation: Use a least-privilege GitLab token.

Risk: Comments, labels, issue closures, or merge requests may affect public projects.

Mitigation: Review each proposed action before posting and follow project rules.

Risk: Hackathon dates, scoring, and eligibility requirements may change.

Mitigation: Confirm current requirements on the linked GitLab pages before participating.
