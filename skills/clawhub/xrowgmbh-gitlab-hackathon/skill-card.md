## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

GitLab contributors use this skill to plan fair participation in Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons: verify live eligibility and rules, find reviewable issues and merge requests, prioritize legitimate contributions, and track scoring without manipulating activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hackathon dates, eligibility conditions, scoring rules, and prize requirements can change, making copied event details outdated.

Mitigation: Check the linked official hackathon pages and contribution-point documentation before planning or acting; the skill itself directs users to verify current rules.
