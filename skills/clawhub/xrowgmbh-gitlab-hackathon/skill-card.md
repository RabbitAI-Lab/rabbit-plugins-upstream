## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and hackathon participants use this skill to check GitLab event requirements, choose focused contributions, plan merge requests, and track legitimate scoring opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GitLab credentials can enable changes to project issues and public posts.

Mitigation: Limit credentials to intended projects and permissions, and review public posts and issue changes.
