## Use Case:

External healthcare institutions, companies, and project preparers use this skill to understand China and Shandong internet diagnosis, internet hospital, and remote-medical-service requirements, then plan registration paths, materials, regulatory coordination, review preparation, and remediation. It supports advisory preparation and drafting, not patient diagnosis, prescriptions, guaranteed approval, or administrative decision-making.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China, with primary applicability to Shandong Province.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The advisory content may become outdated or differ by locality.

Mitigation: Verify current national, Shandong, and local official requirements before relying on the guidance for an application.

Risk: Historical flowchart notes include unsafe or overbroad operational shortcuts, including password handoff and immediate launch after licensing.

Mitigation: Use the curated SKILL.md and references; keep account access with authorized institution operators and confirm licensing, platform connection, testing, training, and launch readiness separately.

Risk: Users may expose sensitive healthcare, identity, account, API, merchant, or private-key material during consultation.

Mitigation: Do not share patient records, full identity numbers, platform passwords, API keys, merchant keys, or private keys through the skill or with a vendor unless a secure, authorized project channel is established.

Risk: Outputs could be mistaken for official approval, legal advice, clinical judgment, or a guarantee of licensing.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preparation guidance only; have qualified institutional staff and the relevant authority confirm clinical, legal, security, and administrative decisions.
