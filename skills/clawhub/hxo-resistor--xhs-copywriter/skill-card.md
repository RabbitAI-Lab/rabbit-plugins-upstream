## Description:

Generates Xiaohongshu-style promotional posts with attention-grabbing titles, short-form copy, and hashtags from a user's product, topic, or audience need.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to draft Xiaohongshu promotional posts for a product, theme, or customer pain point.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive promotional copy may contain misleading claims or omit needed disclosures.

Mitigation: Review generated claims for truthfulness and required disclosures before publishing.

Risk: The default female-targeted tone may not suit every audience.

Mitigation: Check tone and audience suitability, and revise the copy before use.
