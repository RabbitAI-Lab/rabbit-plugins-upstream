## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to fetch public cat facts and cat breed information for lightweight bot responses or examples. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill makes outbound requests to the public catfact.ninja service and depends on that service's availability and returned content. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for non-sensitive cat facts or breed lookups, avoid sending private data, and review returned content before reusing it in user-facing responses. <br>
