## Description:

Analyzes rankings and new-book data on Qidian, Fanqie, Jinjiang, Qimao, and other web-fiction platforms to identify market trends and popular genres.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Writers use this skill to review public rankings and new-book samples, identify recurring genre signals, and create a market scan report and topic decision document.
