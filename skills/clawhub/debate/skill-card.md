## Description:

Coordinates cross-vendor adversarial review of a user-approved proposal, with evidence-tagged rulings and a final report that distinguishes disagreement from verified consensus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other users who explicitly request an external adversarial review use this skill to challenge a plan, proposal, or design and separate verified findings from open questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The brief and any approved source files are sent to another AI vendor and may remain in its session history.

Mitigation: Use a brief without secrets or personal data, approve each file explicitly, and check that vendor's retention terms before sharing sensitive material.
