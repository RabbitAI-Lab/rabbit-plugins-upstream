## Description:

Provides cross-vendor adversarial review of user-approved plans, proposals, or designs, with evidence-based rulings and a final report separating disputes, unresolved points, and verified consensus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users and teams seeking independent feedback can ask a model from another vendor to challenge a plan, proposal, or design and receive evidence-tagged rulings and a report of disputed, unresolved, and verified points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A third-party AI vendor receives the brief and any approved source material, which may remain in that vendor's session history.

Mitigation: Before each run, confirm the vendor, model, exact files or excerpts, and redactions; exclude secrets, personal data, and confidential source unless disclosure is acceptable.
