# Ad copy scaffolds (US)

All copy assumes the normalized brief. Numbers in brackets are brief fields.

---

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram) ad set

**Primary text** (first 125 characters must carry the value):

```
[audience_short]: [proof_first].

[offer]

[cta]
```

**Headlines** (pick 3-5, 27 characters is the safe display limit):

```
[proof_first_short]
[product_short] for [audience_short]
[offer_short]
Built for real use
See why people switch
```

**Description** (short, one line):

```
[proof_first]. [cta].
```

**CTA button:** `Shop Now` / `Learn More` / `Sign Up` - match the landing page,
not the ad.

**Placement:** Advantage+ / manual. Start manual (Instagram Reels + Facebook
Feed) for the first test so the creative reads clean in vertical.

## Google RSA (Responsive Search Ad)

15 headlines, 4 descriptions, 3 paths. Headlines max 30 characters, descriptions
max 90. Pin the two most important headlines to positions 1 and 2.

```
Headlines:
 [proof_first_short]
 [product_short] for [audience_short]
 [offer_short]
 Free Shipping Over $50
 30-Day Trial
 Ships From The USA
 Top Rated [category]

 [use_case_short] Made Easy
 Compare [competitor]

 Customer Favorite

Descriptions:

 For [audience_short]: [job_to_be_done]. [cta].
 [tone] and specific. [offer].
 [offer]. [cta].
```

**Paths:** `/product` `/[category]` `/buy`

## TikTok Spark Ads

```
Ad text: [hook text]
CTA: [cta]
Creative: 9:16 video, hook in first 2 seconds, captions burned in
Sound: use a licensed trending sound or original audio - never a copyrighted song
Identity: Spark ad from your own TikTok account (no fake account personas)
```

## Landing page (if you write one)

```
1. Headline: the outcome, not the product
2. Subhead: who it is for + the proof
3. Hero image or 5-second loop
4. Proof stack: logos, review count, guarantee
5. Three benefits, one line each
6. Price + shipping + trial
7. FAQ (shipping, returns, support, compatibility)
8. CTA repeated 3 times
9. Trust row: secure checkout, US support, real shipping address
```

## US legal notes for ad copy

- Do not claim "FDA approved", "cures", "treats", or any health outcome without
  legal review.
- Do not use "free" unless the offer is genuinely free - "free shipping" and
  "free trial" are fine when true.
- `Earnings may vary.` is required on income claims.
- Do not claim "Made in USA" unless the product is; use "Ships from USA"
  otherwise.
- Financing claims (0% APR) must state the term length and the credit pull.
