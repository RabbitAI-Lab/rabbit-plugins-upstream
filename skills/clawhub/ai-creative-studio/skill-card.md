## Description:

Turn one brief into a complete US-market creative package — ad copy, social posts, video hooks, image prompts and a reusable brand voice kit for TikTok, Instagram, Meta, YouTube and email. Built for US founders, DTC brands, agencies and solo marketers who need on-brand content in minutes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

US founders, direct-to-consumer brands, agencies, and solo marketers use the skill to turn a product brief into channel-ready marketing drafts, including ads, social posts, video scripts, image prompts, email sequences, and campaign plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated drafts may include unsupported claims, trial or shipping offers, ratings, or other promotional details.

Mitigation: Verify every claim, offer, price, and rating against approved evidence before publishing.

Risk: Using drafts in tracking, retargeting, or paid campaigns can create privacy, consent, and spend risks.

Mitigation: Confirm privacy and consent arrangements before implementing tracking or retargeting, and explicitly authorize advertising spend.
