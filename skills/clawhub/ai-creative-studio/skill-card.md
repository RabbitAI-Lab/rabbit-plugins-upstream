## Description:

Turn one brief into a complete US-market creative package — ad copy, social posts, video hooks, image prompts and a reusable brand voice kit for TikTok, Instagram, Meta, YouTube and email.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, DTC brands, agencies, and solo marketers use this skill to turn a product brief into channel-specific marketing copy, social content, scripts, and creative prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain unsupported shipping, trial, rating, or performance claims.

Mitigation: Verify every claim and offer against the brief before publishing.

Risk: Business brief content may be sent to a separately chosen hosted generator.

Mitigation: Use a separate output folder for each campaign, protect saved briefs, and review the hosted generator's data handling before sending prompts.
