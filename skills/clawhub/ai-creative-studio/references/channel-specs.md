## Hook patterns that earn the first 2 seconds

```
Contrarian    "Everyone gets this wrong."
Problem       "If your [thing] keeps [pain], watch this."
Proof-first   "[proof] - here's the setup."
Curiosity     "I changed one thing and [result]."
Direct offer  "[offer]. Here's if it's worth it."
Anti-hype     "Skip the hype. Here's what actually happens."
Identity      "For [audience]: this one's for you."
Comparison    "[product] vs [alternative] - I tested both."
Number        "3 things I changed in [timeframe]."
```

## CTA bank (US)

```
Tap the link to try it
Get the details
Shop now
Start free today
Claim your offer
See the full breakdown
Join the list
Try it risk-free
Book a demo
Compare models
See why people switch
```

Match the CTA to the landing page action. Never send ad traffic to a page
that asks for something different than the ad promised.

## Hashtag rules (US)

- TikTok / IG: 3-5, mix one broad (`#skincare`) + one niche (`#skincareforbeginners`)
  + one branded (`#yourbrand`).
- No banned/spam tags, no 30-tag walls.
- Never use a competitor brand as a hashtag.

## US-specific dates and money in copy

- `Save $20 on your first order - ends Sunday, March 8.`
- `Only 12 left in this run.`
- `Free US shipping over $50.`
- Date format: `March 8` or `3/8` (US format, month first).
