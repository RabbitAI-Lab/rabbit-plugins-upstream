# Brand voice library (US)

Use this to build the brand voice card in section 2 of the creative pack.

## How to build a voice card in 5 minutes

1. Write 3 sentences the founder would actually say out loud.
2. Write 3 sentences the founder would never say.
3. Translate each into a trait pair: `Plainspoken / Not corporate`.
4. Add 5 banned phrases (the words that make copy sound like an ad).
5. Write 3 sample lines that a customer could screenshot as a quote.

## Trait library

Pick 5. Each trait gets a "we sound like / we never sound like" line.

| Trait | We sound like | We never sound like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | "Here is what it does." | "We're excited to share..." |
| Plainspoken | "It won't leak." | "Engineered for optimal hydro-retention." |
| Confident | "Built for daily use." | "We believe it might be great!" |
| Specific | "24 hours on ice." | "Long-lasting performance." |
| Warm | "We put you first." | "Dear valued customer." |
| Blunt | "No, it doesn't do that." | "It may be possible that..." |
| Data-led | "12,400 reviews, 4.7 stars." | "Customers love it!" |
| Minimal | "Bottle. Ice. Coffee." | "Our revolutionary hydration solution." |
| Playful | "Your water bottle called." | "😀😀😀" |
| Expert | "18/8 steel, vacuum-sealed." | "Proprietary synergy." |

## Banned phrase library (US)

Never auto-generate these without a factual reason:

```
revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class,
unmatched, unparalleled, unbeatable, guaranteed results, risk-free income,
100% guaranteed, miracle, secret trick, life-changing (without proof),
limited time (unless a real deadline), act now (without a real deadline),
literally, obviously, simply, just, very, really, actually
```

## Tone ladders

| Use case | Tone | Example line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold ad | Blunt + specific | "12 hours cold. 24 with ice. $39." |
| Warm email | Direct + friendly | "Hey Sam - quick one about the bottle." |
| Landing page | Plain + confident | "Your water stays cold. That's the whole pitch." |
| Support reply | Warm + concrete | "That won't happen - here's the fix." |
| Founder post | Plain + unpolished | "I was wrong about this. Here's what changed." |

## US vs non-US conventions

- US spelling: color, favorite, organize, license (noun).
- US measurements: ounces, cups, pounds, feet, inches, Fahrenheit.
- US money: `$39`, `$1,200`, `$0.99` - no trailing currency code.
- US dates: `March 3`, `Mar 3, 2026` - never `03/04/26` (ambiguous).
- US phone/fax: `(415) 555-0132`.
- US address: `123 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94105`.
- Address people as "you", never "one".
