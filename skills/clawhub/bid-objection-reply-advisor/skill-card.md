## Description: <br>
Helps bid owners and tendering agencies draft compliant responses to objections in Chinese tendering workflows, including intake checks, validity analysis, corrective or rejection language, and internal complaint-risk forecasts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Procurement, legal, and tendering staff at bid owners or tendering agencies use this skill to assess received objections, prepare written response drafts, and produce internal complaint-risk analysis before formal review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill produces legal and compliance drafts that may be incomplete or incorrect if treated as final advice. <br>
Mitigation: Have legal or procurement staff review every reply before sending it. <br>
Risk: Outputs may misapply rules when project facts, platform requirements, or jurisdictional context are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the objection, tender documents, evaluation or opening records, and verify the applicable jurisdiction and platform rules. <br>
Risk: The workflow handles sensitive tender records and internal complaint-risk analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only documents needed for the objection response and keep internal risk forecasts and JSON quality records within the bid owner or agency. <br>
