## Use Case:

External users can provide birth date, birth time, and birth city details to receive an astrology-style personality report in Chinese. The generated report is intended for entertainment and personal reflection, not medical, psychological, psychiatric, or diagnostic use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce psychology-sounding personality claims and may use severe language about mental state or behavior.

Mitigation: Treat generated reports as entertainment or personal reflection only, and do not use them as medical, psychological, psychiatric, or diagnostic advice.
