## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The release is an unfinished starter template and may not provide a useful or specific agent capability. <br>
Mitigation: Complete the TODO sections with a clear purpose, trigger conditions, workflow steps, resources, and usage boundaries before practical use. <br>
Risk: Template-based guidance could be installed before review and lead to vague or misleading agent behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review and scan the completed skill before deployment. <br>
