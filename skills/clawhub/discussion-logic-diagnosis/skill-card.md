## Description:

Diagnoses logical argumentation in the Discussion section of an English psychology research paper by checking argument chains, causal claims, and data/interpretation/speculation boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External authors, students, and writing reviewers use this skill to diagnose the reasoning in English psychology research-paper Discussion sections. It maps argument chains, audits causal claims, checks epistemic layers, and flags top logic issues without generating or rewriting prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers or mixed-language prompts may lead to less focused academic-writing diagnostics.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill with a clear psychology Discussion section and specify the preferred output language or review context.
