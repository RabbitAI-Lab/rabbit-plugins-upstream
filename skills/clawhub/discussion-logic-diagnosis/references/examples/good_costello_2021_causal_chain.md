# Example: Causal Chain Reasoning（因果链推理）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Costello et al. (2021). Clarifying the structure and nature of left-wing authoritarianism. *JPSP*, 120(1).
- **论文编号**: 1.1
- **维度**: Logic（因果推理链 — Mechanism → Outcome）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: logic-diagnosis 的"因果链完整性"、"mechanism-outcome 清晰度"

---

## 原句（Discussion 中部，关于 LWA 和 SDO/RWA 对比段）

> *"Antihierarchical aggression reflects motivations to forcefully overthrow the established hierarchy and punish those in power, perhaps mirroring SDO's definitional core. Moreover, antihierarchical aggression's nomological network was extremely similar to SDO's nomological network, especially in the personality domain (i.e., low agreeableness, low honesty-humility, low conscientiousness, and psychopathic disinhibition and meanness), further suggesting that both constructs reflect authoritarianism's power- and dominance-related features (Altemeyer, 1998), even as they differ in their orientation toward the established social hierarchy (i.e., challenging vs. upholding)."*

## 分析

### 1. 三层因果链的清晰呈现
- **Layer 1（机制）**: "Antihierarchical aggression reflects motivations to forcefully overthrow..."
- **Layer 2（证据）**: "...especially in the personality domain (i.e., low agreeableness, low honesty-humility...)"
- **Layer 3（结论）**: "...further suggesting that both constructs reflect authoritarianism's power- and dominance-related features..."

### 2. "perhaps mirroring" 的逻辑 hedge
- "perhaps mirroring SDO's definitional core" —— 不直接说"this IS SDO"，而是"perhaps mirroring"
- 这种**推测性机制描述**符合 Unit 4.1.1 "data → interpretation → speculation" 三层结构

### 3. "i.e." 与具体化的逻辑作用
- "(i.e., low agreeableness, low honesty-humility, low conscientiousness, and psychopathic disinhibition and meanness)"
- 把抽象的"nomological network" 具体化为可观察的 trait
- 这是逻辑论证的关键：从抽象 claim 落地到具体 evidence

### 4. "even as they differ" 的对偶逻辑
- "even as they differ in their orientation toward the established social hierarchy (i.e., challenging vs. upholding)"
- 承认**相似性的同时承认差异性** —— 这是**辩证逻辑**的标准做法
- 避免了"只强调 similarity, 忽略 difference"的简化错误

### 5. Unit 4.2.2 Q&A "What if I'm not confident about what my results mean?" 的范例
- 用 "perhaps mirroring" + "further suggesting" 表达**有限度的 confidence**
- 同时给出具体的 evidence trail
- 让读者知道作者的 claim 是基于什么的

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户的因果链断裂** — 只说"X causes Y"，没说 why → 引用此例展示 mechanism 层
2. **用户的 claim 无具体 evidence** — "Our results show that X is similar to Y"（无具体特征）→ 引用此例展示 "i.e., ..." 模式
3. **用户只强调 similarity 忽略 difference** — 引用此例展示"even as they differ" 的辩证逻辑
