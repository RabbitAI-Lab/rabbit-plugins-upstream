# Example: Addressing Alternative Explanations（处理替代解释）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Ebert et al. (2020). Religious people only live longer in religious cultural contexts. *JPSP*, 119(1).
- **论文编号**: 1.7
- **维度**: Logic（替代解释的处理）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: logic-diagnosis 的"反方观点处理"、"alternative explanations 完整性"

## 原句（Discussion limitations 段）

> *"Although our gravestone approach has key advantages over previous approaches, our approach also has its limitations. First, gravestone analyses are necessarily cross-sectional (people only die once). **While we controlled for a wide variety of potential confounds in our 14 robustness checks, we cannot fully rule out reversed causality (i.e., older people in some areas being particularly likely to put religious imagery on their gravestones).** However, this interpretation is unlikely because gravestones with religious imagery are observed at all age age groups, including younger adults."*

## 分析

### 1. 替代解释的明确呈现
- 作者先承认了 limitation（cross-sectional）
- 然后明确指出可能的替代解释：**reversed causality**
- 用 "i.e., older people in some areas being particularly likely to put religious imagery on their gravestones" 给替代解释具体化

### 2. "cannot fully rule out" 的精确 epistemic stance
- **"cannot fully rule out"** —— 不说"rule out"，也不说"confirm"
- 这是**精确的 epistemic claim**——承认 limitation 但不夸大

### 3. 立即用 evidence 反驳替代解释
- "However, this interpretation is unlikely because gravestones with religious imagery are observed at all age age groups, including younger adults."
- 用**反证数据**回应替代解释
- 不是简单的"limitation + 道歉"，而是"limitation + 反驳证据"

### 4. 完整的 limitations handling 结构
- **承认 limitation** → **呈现替代解释** → **反驳替代解释**
- 三层结构让 limitations 段不仅是承认问题，而是**展示批判性思维**

### 5. Unit 4.2.2 Q&A "How do I acknowledge limitations without undermining my findings?" 的范例
- 答案：限制 + 反驳证据（不是单纯限制）
- 这种结构让 reviewer 觉得作者**有全面思考**

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户的 limitations 段只承认问题** — "Our study has limitations" 然后没了 → 引用此例展示"承认 + 反驳"模式
2. **用户的 limitations 段过度自我贬低** — "Our study is flawed because..." → 引用此例展示如何承认 limitation 但保持 confidence
3. **用户没有处理替代解释** — 直接说 finding，无 alternative → 引用此例
