# Example: Honest Limitation without Over-Claim（诚实的局限性但不削弱 claim）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Schmidt et al. (2016). The Parallel Episodic Processing (PEP) model 2.0. *Cognitive Psychology*, 91.
- **论文编号**: 2.7
- **领域**: Cognitive (Cog Psych)
- **维度**: Logic（honest limitation + 不削弱 claim 的平衡）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: logic-diagnosis 的"诚实 vs 自我贬低"

---

## 原句（Discussion 第 2 段 + Model Limitations 段）

> *"Of course, we certainly do not suggest that we have provided a model of cognition that is in any way 'complete.' Indeed, the Model Limitations section below discusses further refinements that might be needed to further generalize the model. **Importantly, however, we demonstrate that a single fixed model of a small number of processes can have wide applicability to different performance paradigms.**"*

## 分析

### 1. Honest limitation 的标准表达
- **"Of course"** —— interjection 表明"显然"
- **"we certainly do not suggest"** —— 双重否定（certainly + do not）
- **"in any way 'complete'"** —— 完全否定（"in any way" 强调普遍性）
- 这是**教科书式的自我否定开场**

### 2. 立即用 hedge + 强 claim 平衡
- **"Importantly, however"** —— "however" 转折，立刻从 limitation 转到 claim
- **"we demonstrate"** —— 强 cognitive verb
- **"can have wide applicability"** —— modal verb 保留 epistemic humility
- 这种"limitation + immediate claim"的**平衡结构**是顶刊 standard

### 3. 不削弱 claim 的措辞
- 不是 "we have not provided a complete model" —— 太弱
- 而是 "we demonstrate that a single fixed model... can have wide applicability" —— 保留 strength
- 关键：用 **positive framing**（"can have"）替代 negative framing（"is not complete"）

### 4. 多重 epistemic hedge 的协同
- "Of course" + "we certainly do not suggest" + "in any way 'complete'" —— 3 个 hedge 元素叠加
- "Importantly, however" + "we demonstrate" + "can have" —— 3 个 confidence 元素叠加
- 这种**多元素对冲**让读者感受到作者的**精确立场**

### 5. 与简单 disclaimer 的区别
- ❌ 简单 disclaimer："Our study has limitations"（过于 generic）
- ✅ 本例："Of course, we certainly do not suggest... Importantly, however, we demonstrate..."（精确对冲）

---

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户的 limitations 段过度自我贬低** — 用了过多 "we did not / cannot / failed to" → 引用此例展示平衡
2. **用户的 claim 后无 limitation 配套** — 直接 claim 无 hedge → 引用此例展示"limitation + claim" 对冲
3. **用户的 disclaimer 过于 generic** — "Our study is not perfect" → 引用此例展示精确对冲
