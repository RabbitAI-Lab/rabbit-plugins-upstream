# Example: Data → Interpretation → Speculation 三层结构

## 元数据
- **来源**: Midgley et al. (2020). When every day is a high school reunion. *JPSP*, 119(2).
- **论文编号**: 1.3
- **维度**: Logic（三层结构清晰分层）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: logic-diagnosis 的"data/interpretation/speculation 边界"

---

## 原句（Discussion 第 7 段，in-sum wrap 段）

> *"My studies suggest that upward rather than downward comparisons have the greatest impact on individuals, and that this impact is overwhelmingly negative. Indeed, any downward or lateral comparisons did little to mitigate the sting of the more prevalent upward comparisons."*

> *"These studies also allowed me to assess, for the first time, the impact of upward comparison extremity within each individual, evaluating the degree to which the 'upwardness' of the comparison would affect the impact of that comparison. I found that, for any given individual, a comparison that was more extremely upward resulted in a more negative effect on their self-views immediately following the comparison."*

## 分析

### 1. 三层结构的清晰呈现
| 层 | 标志词 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **Data** | "I found" / "did" | "did little to mitigate the sting" |
| **Interpretation** | "suggest" / "indicate" | "My studies suggest that..." |
| **Speculation** | "may" / "might" / "could" | （其他段中的 "may not fully describe"） |

### 2. Data 层的具体性
- "any downward or lateral comparisons **did little** to mitigate..."
- 这是**直接引用数据**，没有 speculation
- 数据支撑（"did little"）明确

### 3. Interpretation 层的精确性
- "My studies **suggest** that upward rather than downward comparisons **have** the greatest impact"
- "suggest" + Present Simple（"have"）—— 这是**作者对数据的解释**
- 不是 data 本身，是 data 推出的 interpretation

### 4. 三层边界的清晰
- 第一段：纯 interpretation（"My studies suggest"）
- 第二段：data + interpretation 混合（"I found" + "evaluating the degree to which"）
- 没有混淆——读者能清楚分辨"作者看到了什么"vs"作者认为这意味着什么"

### 5. 与因果推理的衔接
- 三层结构让因果推理更清晰：
  - Data 显示 X 是高频
  - Interpretation 说 X 影响 mood
  - Speculation 可能 X 通过 mechanism M 影响 mood

---

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户的 data 和 speculation 混在一起** — "Our results suggest X might cause Y, which means Z" → 引用此例展示分层
2. **用户不知道哪里是 data / interpretation / speculation** — 引用此例展示标志词
3. **用户的 speculation 包装成 data** — "We prove that X leads to Y" → 引用此例展示正确边界
