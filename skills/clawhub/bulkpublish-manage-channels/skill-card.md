## Description:

Lists connected social media channels, checks their health, and helps manage BulkPublish channel sets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[azeemkafridi](https://clawhub.ai/user/azeemkafridi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

BulkPublish users and their agents can inspect connected accounts, check connection health and platform options, search mentions, and manage saved channel sets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Channel-set creation, changes, or deletion can affect an organization's saved posting targets, beyond simply inspecting channels.

Mitigation: Before any create, update, or delete action, confirm the organization, channel-set ID, channel list, and whether the change is reversible in BulkPublish.
