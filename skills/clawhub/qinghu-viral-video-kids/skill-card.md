## Description:

Guides users through creating children's clothing promotional videos by transferring actions from a reference video to a child model depicted in a supplied image.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Clothing sellers and content creators use this skill to guide the creation of short promotional videos featuring a child model, based on an authorized reference video and model image.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland service)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference video and child model image are sent to an external service for processing.

Mitigation: Share media only with informed consent and after confirming that external processing is acceptable.

Risk: Commercial use of reference footage or a minor's likeness without authorization may violate rights.

Mitigation: Use owned or licensed footage and obtain guardian authorization for any child depicted.

Risk: Generation can incur paid credits and installing the command-line package introduces a supply-chain dependency.

Mitigation: Review the credit estimate and obtain approval before generating; prefer a pinned or preinstalled package.
