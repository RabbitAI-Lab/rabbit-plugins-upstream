## Description:

按场景起草或改写工作邮件/企微长消息，包括请示、催办、道歉、同步进度、对外答复。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and business users use this skill to draft or rewrite Chinese work emails and long workplace messages for approvals, follow-ups, apologies, progress updates, external replies, invitations, and refusals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated drafts may include incorrect facts, commitments, or tone for a workplace recipient.

Mitigation: Review each draft before sending and provide exact facts, deadlines, numbers, attachment names, and desired tone.

Risk: Sensitive topics such as salary, disciplinary action, legal conclusions, contracts, or customer-facing commitments may require specialist review.

Mitigation: Treat drafts for sensitive topics as text proposals and confirm them with the appropriate human reviewer before sending.
