## Description:

Helps legal AI teams assess system registration, supporting evidence, and review gates using the LGD three-law checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Legal teams and developers use this skill to self-check legal AI systems for documented identity, verifiable evidence, and human review before release. It provides checklist guidance, not legal advice or a substitute for current official sources and licensed counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill declares broad agent tool access.

Mitigation: Review and limit granted tools to those needed in the deployment environment.

Risk: Checklist results may be mistaken for definitive legal compliance advice.

Mitigation: Confirm material decisions against current official sources and with licensed legal counsel.
