## Description:

协助起草、修改和审校中文公文、事务性材料及新闻稿件，并按需整理 Word 正文与格式。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Staff at public institutions, businesses, schools and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, shorten and review Chinese official documents and related materials. It also helps check document type, wording, facts and formatting against supplied materials and templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional online research could expose confidential, personal, classified or internal-only draft material.

Mitigation: Confirm whether online research should be disabled or minimized before processing sensitive material.

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect facts, figures, policy citations, commitments or official formatting.

Mitigation: Manually verify the final draft against source materials and applicable templates before sending or filing.
