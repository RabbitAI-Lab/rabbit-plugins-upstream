## Description:

Helps draft, revise, shorten, proofread, and format Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public institutions, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to prepare and review Chinese official correspondence, work documents, and news copy while preserving the status of supplied facts and decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential, classified, or sensitive personal material could be exposed through web research.

Mitigation: Keep searches limited to public facts and do not submit sensitive material to searches.

Risk: Generated official documents may contain inaccurate facts, citations, or unresolved claims.

Mitigation: Check facts, figures, sources, and outstanding decisions against the supplied materials before use.
