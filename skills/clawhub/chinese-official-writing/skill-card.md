## Description:

Helps draft, revise, review, and format Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking staff in government, companies, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, and review formal documents, workplace materials, and news copy. It also helps condense drafts and prepare text for Word formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read files and run bundled scripts.

Mitigation: Review the files you share and the scripts you allow it to run; check generated facts and citations before official use.
