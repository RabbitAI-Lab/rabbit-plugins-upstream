## Description:

Helps draft, revise, proofread, and format Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Staff at public institutions, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft and edit Chinese official documents and related materials, check document type and factual boundaries, and prepare Word-ready text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential draft text or internal details may be exposed through external research.

Mitigation: Keep public searches generic and do not send private details to external services.

Risk: Unverified facts, citations, or formatting may make an official-use document misleading or unsuitable.

Mitigation: Manually verify facts, citations, required fields, and formatting before use.
