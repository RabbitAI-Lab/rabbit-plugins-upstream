## Use Case:

Employees and external users in government agencies, businesses, schools, and news organizations use the skill to draft, revise, condense, proofread, and review Chinese official documents, work materials, and news copy based on supplied context. It also provides guidance for organizing Word documents; verify facts, figures, citations, policy bases, responsibilities, and commitments before official use or sharing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated or revised documents may contain errors or omissions in facts, figures, citations, policy bases, responsibilities, or commitments.

Mitigation: Review those details against authoritative materials before official use or sharing.

Risk: Selected materials may contain confidential information, and Word documents may retain metadata.

Mitigation: Limit access to the materials selected for the task and inspect confidential content and Word metadata before sharing.
