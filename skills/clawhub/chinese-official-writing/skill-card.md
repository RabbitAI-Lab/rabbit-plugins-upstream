## Description:

Helps draft, revise, review, and format Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Staff in government offices, businesses, schools, and news organizations use it to prepare or refine Chinese administrative documents, workplace materials, and news writing, including genre and formatting checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read user-supplied files, and the scanner does not establish how the host runtime enforces file permissions.

Mitigation: Check the host's file-access controls and avoid providing sensitive or restricted documents unless their use is authorized.
