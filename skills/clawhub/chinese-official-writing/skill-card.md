## Description:

帮助机关、企事业单位、学校和新闻机构起草、修改、审校中文公文、工作材料及新闻稿件，并按需要核对文种与版式。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

机关、企事业单位、学校和新闻机构的写作人员可用它起草、改写、压缩和审校中文公文、工作材料及新闻稿件，也可依据现有材料检查文种、事实边界和 Word 版式要求。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts can misstate facts, figures, policy citations, signatures or official document numbers.

Mitigation: Verify these details against approved source materials before formal use.

Risk: Local drafts may contain confidential information, and current or unfamiliar topics may prompt public web research.

Mitigation: Check confidentiality before sharing drafts or using public sources; confirm any external references independently.

Risk: Word formatting may not meet the receiving institution's requirements.

Mitigation: Review the final DOCX against the required template and formatting rules.
