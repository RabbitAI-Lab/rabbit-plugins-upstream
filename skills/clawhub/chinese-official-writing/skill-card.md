## Description:

Drafts, revises, compresses, and reviews Chinese official documents and formal workplace materials, including document-type checks, format checks, de-colloquialization, and reduced AI-like phrasing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and agent operators use this skill to draft or review Chinese official and formal work documents such as requests, reports, notices, plans, minutes, procurement materials, AI-compute materials, and related style or format checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive official or formal drafts may include procurement, finance, policy, legal, date, amount, signing, or approval details that require correctness before use.

Mitigation: Manually verify facts, legal and financial conclusions, dates, amounts, and final approvals before relying on or issuing generated text.
