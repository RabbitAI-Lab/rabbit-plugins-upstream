## Description:

Assists with drafting, editing, condensing, proofreading, and formatting Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, and review Chinese formal documents and related work materials, including notices, requests, reports, speeches, and news articles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain inaccurate facts, policy citations, dates, or official formatting.

Mitigation: Verify source facts and citations and review document formatting before formal use.

Risk: Internal drafts or sensitive details may be exposed through local file processing or web research.

Mitigation: Limit input files to those needed for the task and omit unnecessary private context from web queries.
