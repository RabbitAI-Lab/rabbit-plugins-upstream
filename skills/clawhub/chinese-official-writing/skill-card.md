## Description:

Helps draft, revise, and review Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy, including document-type and formatting checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, organizations, schools, and newsrooms use this skill to prepare and check Chinese formal documents and news copy against supplied materials and intended document types.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts can contain unsupported facts, figures, policy citations, or commitments.

Mitigation: Check all claims and commitments against source materials before formal use.

Risk: Sensitive draft material may be exposed during public-source checks.

Mitigation: Keep confidential material out of public searches and verify only necessary public facts.

Risk: Word layouts may not match the applicable template or formatting requirements.

Mitigation: Review the finished document against the organization's template before distribution.
