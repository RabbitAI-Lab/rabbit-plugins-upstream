## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect facts, figures, citations or policy references.

Mitigation: Verify source material, dates, figures and policy references before formal use.

Risk: Supplied drafts may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Review sensitive content before providing it and before sharing the final draft.

Risk: Final Word layout may not match the required template.

Mitigation: Inspect and approve the final document formatting before release.
