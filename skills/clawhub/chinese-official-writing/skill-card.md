## Description:

Assists with drafting, revising, reviewing, and formatting Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft, edit, and check Chinese official and workplace documents against their source materials and formatting needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may misstate facts, dates, numbers, policy citations, or signatories.

Mitigation: Check each draft against source materials and applicable policies before formal use.

Risk: Sensitive drafts or Word metadata may be exposed through temporary files or online fact-checking.

Mitigation: Review confidentiality and document metadata; avoid disk writes and online searches for sensitive material unless necessary.
