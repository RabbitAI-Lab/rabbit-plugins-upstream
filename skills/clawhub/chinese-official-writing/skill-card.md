## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, enterprises, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, review, and format Chinese official documents and related formal materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect facts, figures, policy citations, names, dates, commitments, or confidentiality markings.

Mitigation: Check each item against approved source materials and review the final text before official use.

Risk: External searches may reveal details of sensitive government, personnel, procurement, enterprise, or internal drafts.

Mitigation: Avoid external searches for sensitive drafts unless approved; keep authorized search queries generic.
