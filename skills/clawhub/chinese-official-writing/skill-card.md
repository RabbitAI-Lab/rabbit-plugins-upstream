## Description:

帮助起草、修改和审校中文公文、工作材料及新闻稿件，并按需整理 Word 正文。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, shorten, and review Chinese official documents and related materials. It also helps check document type and prepare Word-ready text when document tools are available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential drafts may be exposed if live web research is used.

Mitigation: Avoid or disable web research for confidential materials unless current public-source verification is explicitly needed.

Risk: Generated drafts may contain errors in facts, figures, citations, policy grounds, or commitments.

Mitigation: Have a responsible reviewer verify source materials and every consequential claim before official use.

Risk: Generated Word or official-format documents may have incorrect layout or document elements.

Mitigation: Review the finished document against the applicable template and formatting requirements before distribution.
