## Description:

用于中文公文、事务性材料和新闻稿件的起草、改写、压缩、润色、审校、文种核对、去口语化、降 AI 味及 Word 格式处理，适用于机关、企事业单位、学校和新闻机构。涵盖申请、请示、报告、通知、通告、意见、决定、决议、议案、公报、命令、函、复函、批复、说明、方案、纪要、公告、公示、通报、制度、规定、办法、细则、操作规程、工作要点、总结、调研、讲话、致辞、主持词、述职、可研、审查材料、技术需求、新闻消息、编者按、新闻评论，以及采购、整改、反馈和 AI 算力等场景。

## Use Case:

Employees and teams in government, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, review, and format Chinese official documents, work materials, and news copy. It helps match the document type and requirements to the supplied facts and source materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect or incomplete facts, figures, citations, or policy references.

Mitigation: Verify claims and sources against current authoritative materials before formal use.

Risk: Source documents may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Limit file access to documents selected for the task and follow the host's boundaries for handling sensitive materials.
