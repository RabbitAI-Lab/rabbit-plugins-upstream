## Description:

Assists with drafting, revising, shortening, reviewing, and formatting Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff in public institutions, businesses, schools, and newsrooms use this skill to draft and review Chinese formal documents and to prepare text or Word deliverables using supplied facts and templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts can contain factual errors, omissions, or unsupported policy claims.

Mitigation: Verify facts, figures, citations, and applicable requirements before formal use.

Risk: Confidential or personal drafts may be processed in temporary local files or DOCX checks; current-source tasks may involve web research.

Mitigation: Avoid supplying sensitive material unless local processing is acceptable; review what may be researched online.
