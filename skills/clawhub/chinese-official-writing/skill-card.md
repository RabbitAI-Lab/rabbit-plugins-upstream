## Description:

Helps draft, revise, review, and format Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft or review Chinese official documents and related materials while preserving supplied facts and formatting requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may misstate unverified facts or apply the wrong document format.

Mitigation: Check source materials, current public facts, applicable templates, and final wording before circulation.

Risk: Reviewing DOCX files or using temporary local files may expose sensitive source material.

Mitigation: Use approved environments for confidential documents and inspect local file handling before processing them.

Risk: Targeted web research may disclose sensitive details if confidential text is used as a query.

Mitigation: Keep confidential content out of web searches and verify any external information before using it.
