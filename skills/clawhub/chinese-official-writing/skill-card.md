## Description:

协助起草、修改和审校中文公文、工作材料及新闻稿件，并按需要整理正文与格式。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, businesses, schools and news organizations use this skill to draft, revise, shorten and review Chinese official documents, workplace materials and news copy. It can also help check document type and prepare text for Word formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated documents may contain incorrect facts, dates, figures or policy citations.

Mitigation: Check these details against source materials before formal use.

Risk: Sensitive material or unsupported official-document details may enter a draft.

Mitigation: Review confidentiality and verify document numbers, signatures, seals, dates and final Word formatting before release.
