## Description:

Helps draft, revise, shorten and review Chinese official documents, workplace materials and news copy, including document-formatting guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff at public agencies, businesses, schools and news organizations use this skill to prepare and review Chinese official documents, work materials and news copy from their source materials and formatting requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Official or public-facing drafts may contain incorrect facts, figures, policy citations or confidentiality markings.

Mitigation: Review the draft against source materials and verify sensitive details before use or publication.

Risk: Word documents may retain comments or tracked revisions requiring attention.

Mitigation: Inspect comments and revisions before sharing the final document.
