## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Formal drafts may contain incorrect facts, figures, citations or commitments.

Mitigation: Check these details and any applicable policy against authoritative materials before official use.

Risk: Research queries or draft review could expose private or confidential material.

Mitigation: Review sensitive drafts locally where possible; limit public searches to general facts without private names or draft text.
