## Description:

Drafts, revises, condenses, proofreads, and formats Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy across a wide range of genres.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users in government, businesses, schools, and news organizations use the skill to draft or review Chinese official documents and work materials from supplied source material and requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-drafted or revised documents may contain incorrect facts, figures, citations, policy bases, responsibilities, or commitments.

Mitigation: Before official use, check facts, figures, citations, policy bases, responsibilities, commitments, and document metadata against the source materials.
