This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records or cross-skill sharing can retain or share a student's progress.

Mitigation: Leave these features off unless wanted; enable them only with the student's explicit consent and use the skill's pause, sharing, viewing, correction, and deletion controls.

Risk: A student may mistake genetics tutoring for an assessment of their own or a family member's disease risk.

Mitigation: Do not assess personal disease risk or recommend testing; direct the student to tell a trusted adult and consult a doctor.

Risk: Companion skills used for records or reminders have separate storage behavior that is outside this release's inspected artifacts.

Mitigation: Review the companion skills' data handling before enabling their record or reminder features.
