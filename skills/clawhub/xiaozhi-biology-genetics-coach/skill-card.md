初中和高中学生可用它练习遗传题推理：先判断显隐性和基因组成，再列遗传图解、按题目指定的范围计算概率，并循序获得提示。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved records, cross-skill sharing, and reminders depend on host-platform and companion-skill data controls that were not included in this review.

Mitigation: Use current-problem mode by default; check the platform and companion-skill data controls and obtain the required consent before enabling saved records, sharing, or reminders.

Risk: A student could mistake genetics tutoring for an assessment of their own or a family member’s medical risk.

Mitigation: Do not assess an individual’s chance of developing a condition; direct personal or family health questions to a clinician.
