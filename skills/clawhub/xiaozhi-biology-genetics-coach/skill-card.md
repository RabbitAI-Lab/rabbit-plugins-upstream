引导初高中学生逐步分析遗传题，画遗传图解并计算概率，涵盖遗传规律、系谱图和高中必修相关内容。

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to work through specific genetics problems with guided reasoning, diagrams and probability calculations rather than receiving answers immediately.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis resources, school policies and consent expectations may not fit other languages or regions.

Mitigation: Use in Chinese-language Mainland China K–12 settings; localize and review crisis referrals, school policies and consent requirements before deploying elsewhere.

Risk: Learning profiles and reminders involve minors' information.

Mitigation: Review permissions with students or guardians and enable memory, sharing, error logging and reminders only after the relevant consent.

Risk: Educational genetics explanations could be mistaken for a personal medical assessment.

Mitigation: Do not assess an individual's inherited disease risk or recommend tests; refer personal health questions to a doctor.
