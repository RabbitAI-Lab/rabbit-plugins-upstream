## Description:

引导初高中学生逐步分析遗传题中的显隐性、基因组成、遗传图解、概率和系谱图。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初高中学生及陪同学习的家长或老师，循序练习遗传图解、概率计算和系谱图推理；不用于判断个人遗传病风险。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders could be shared or scheduled without meaningful consent.

Mitigation: Keep tutoring stateless by default; require separate explicit consent for profile use, error handoff and reminders, and honor pause, deletion and sharing controls.

Risk: A student's question about a family member's genetic condition could be mistaken for a request for clinical prediction.

Mitigation: Limit explanations to textbook genetics; do not assess real people's disease risk and direct such questions to a doctor.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis contacts may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, consent requirements and crisis-referral contacts before using this skill outside mainland China.
