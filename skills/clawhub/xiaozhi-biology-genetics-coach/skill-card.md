## Description:

面向初高中学生，通过辨认性状、推断基因组成、绘制遗传图解和核对概率分母，逐步讲解遗传题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students use this Chinese-language tutor to work through a specific junior- or senior-high genetics problem with graduated hints, inheritance diagrams and probability checks. Optional learning records and review reminders require separate consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (curriculum, privacy and crisis-help guidance require localization elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, guardian-consent expectations and crisis-help channels may not fit locations outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Use in the stated setting or localize the curriculum, consent rules and emergency contacts before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Student learning records, cross-skill sharing and reminders may expose sensitive educational information.

Mitigation: Keep each option opt-in and allow students or guardians to pause, export, correct or delete records.

Risk: Textbook genetics explanations could be mistaken for personal medical advice.

Mitigation: Do not assess a student's or family member's disease risk; direct personal health questions to a doctor.
