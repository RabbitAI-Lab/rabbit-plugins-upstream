## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through school-level genetics problems by identifying traits and genotypes, drawing inheritance diagrams, and calculating probabilities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking Mainland China middle- and high-school students use this skill to work through genetics questions with stepwise hints, inheritance diagrams, and probability checks. It covers curriculum exercises, not personal genetic disease assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize curriculum, consent rules, and help channels before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders could be created or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory off by default and require separate, explicit consent for profile updates, cross-skill sharing, and reminders.

Risk: School genetics explanations could be mistaken for personal medical risk advice.

Mitigation: Do not assess an individual's disease risk or recommend tests; direct personal health questions to a doctor.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, consent assumptions, or emergency channels may not apply in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize the language, curriculum, minor-consent rules, and help channels before deployment elsewhere; confirm location before giving emergency numbers.
