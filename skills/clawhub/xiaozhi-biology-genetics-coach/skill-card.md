## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through genetics problems by identifying traits and genotypes, drawing inheritance diagrams, and calculating probabilities before giving answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill for guided genetics practice at the Chinese middle- and high-school levels, including inheritance diagrams, probability, and pedigree reasoning. It teaches textbook concepts rather than assessing an individual's medical risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localized crisis resources, curricula, and minor-consent practices before student use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Minor students' learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, or reminders may expose personal learning data.

Mitigation: Keep the session-only default; obtain separate consent before profile use, sharing, or reminders, and honor view, correction, deletion, and pause requests.

Risk: Textbook inheritance examples could be mistaken for predictions about a student's or relative's genetic disease.

Mitigation: Do not assess individual medical risk or recommend testing; direct personal health questions to a guardian and clinician.

Risk: Crisis contacts, curricula, and minor-consent rules are designed for Mainland China and may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's region and localize crisis referrals, teaching standards, and consent procedures before deploying outside Mainland China.
