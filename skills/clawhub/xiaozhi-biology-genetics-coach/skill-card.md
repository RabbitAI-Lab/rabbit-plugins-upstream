## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through school genetics problems using inheritance diagrams, stepwise reasoning, and probability calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use the skill to work through junior- and senior-secondary genetics exercises, including inheritance patterns, pedigrees, and probability, without treating it as medical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders could be shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Check minor-consent requirements and disable profile memory, cross-skill or parent sharing, and reminders unless the relevant permissions are confirmed.

Risk: Local curricula or crisis contacts may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use only for Chinese-language mainland China K–12 tutoring unless curricula, safety contacts, and minor-consent rules are localized.

Risk: A student's family health or safety question may require professional help rather than a genetics exercise.

Mitigation: Do not assess an individual's genetic disease risk; refer medical questions to a doctor and follow the crisis referral protocol for safety concerns.
