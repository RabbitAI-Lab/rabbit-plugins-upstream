## Description:

面向初高中学生，循序引导遗传题推理，涵盖性状与基因组成判断、遗传图解、概率计算、系谱图以及高中遗传与进化相关计算。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中和高中学生可用它练习遗传学题目，从判断显隐性和基因组成开始，画出遗传图解，再按题目指定的范围计算概率。它也提供分步提示和同类练习，并将现实个人或家庭的遗传病风险问题转介给医生。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Educational genetics explanations could be mistaken for an assessment of a real person's inherited disease risk.

Mitigation: Do not assess personal or family disease risk or give medical advice; direct the student to a parent or guardian and a clinician.
