## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students through genetics problems using stepwise trait identification, genotype reasoning, inheritance diagrams, and probability calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to work through junior- and senior-high biology genetics exercises with progressively disclosed hints rather than immediate answers. It covers inheritance diagrams, probability, pedigrees, and selected high-school genetics topics; it does not assess personal genetic disease risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize language, curriculum, consent practices, and emergency resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and emergency guidance may not fit students outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language, emergency contacts, and privacy or guardian-consent practices before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: A minor's learning progress or reminders could be recorded without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory and reminders off by default, and confirm the relevant student and guardian consent before enabling them.

Risk: Genetics tutoring could be mistaken for personal medical advice.

Mitigation: Do not assess an individual's inherited disease risk or recommend testing; direct personal health questions to a parent or guardian and a clinician.
