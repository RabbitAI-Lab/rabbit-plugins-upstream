## Description:

引导初高中学生通过判断性状和基因组成、绘制遗传图解及核对分母，逐步解答遗传规律与概率题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students in Chinese junior and senior secondary biology use this skill to work through a specific genetics problem, building inheritance diagrams and reasoning about probabilities with graduated hints rather than receiving an immediate answer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localization required before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing or reminders can expose minors' learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory and sharing off by default; confirm the relevant consent settings and each requested action before enabling them.

Risk: Crisis contacts and minor-data rules may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency referrals and check local consent and child-data requirements before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Genetics tutoring could be mistaken for individual medical risk assessment.

Mitigation: Limit responses to textbook problems and refer personal or family disease questions to a doctor.
