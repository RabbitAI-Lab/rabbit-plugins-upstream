## Description:

Turns an approved previz, characters, moodboard, script, and sound into AI-assisted footage and motion-design-ready plates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and developers use this skill to turn an approved previz and locked soundtrack into footage, choosing code, AI video, or a hybrid approach per shot and preparing takes for editing and motion design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generation sends media to external providers, whose retention terms may matter for confidential footage or identifiable people.

Mitigation: Review each provider's retention terms and approve the media and receiving providers before generation.

Risk: Generation and fallback routes consume credits.

Mitigation: Approve a credit cap and any fallback route before generation; estimate costs before spending.
