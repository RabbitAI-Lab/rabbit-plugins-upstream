## Description:

Animate approved previz with locked sound into footage and plates ready for editing and motion design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Filmmakers and creative developers use this skill to turn an approved Blender or three.js previz and locked soundtrack into generated footage, then prepare takes and clean plates for editing and motion design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project media is processed by Varg and selected providers, whose retention settings may differ.

Mitigation: Use media approved for processing and check each provider's retention settings.

Risk: External services and dependency implementations were not audited; optional background removal uses Hyperframes.

Mitigation: Keep budget approvals, and use a reviewed, pinned Hyperframes installation if using background removal.
