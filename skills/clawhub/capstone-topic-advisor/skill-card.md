## Description:

Capstone/FYP topic advisor: three-tier ranked candidates with difficulty, timeline, BOM and risk; cliche blacklist with increment fixes; recommend/check/compare/budget modes. Coaching only. Use when a student asks for capstone topics, feasibility checks, or budget estimates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External undergraduate students in electronics, automation, mechatronics, and IoT use the skill to brainstorm, evaluate, compare, and budget embedded capstone topics. It provides coaching and feasibility guidance, while students confirm course requirements and the final topic with their supervisor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course rules, ethics requirements, or supervisor expectations may differ from the skill's general advice.

Mitigation: Check the course outline and ethics requirements, and confirm the proposed topic with the supervisor.

Risk: Estimated component prices may be outdated or vary by seller and location.

Mitigation: Recheck current local prices before ordering hardware.

Risk: An installation archive filename may not match the release version downloaded.

Mitigation: Confirm the archive filename matches the version before installation.
