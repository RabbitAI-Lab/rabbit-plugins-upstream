## Description:

Capstone/FYP topic advisor: three-tier ranked candidates with difficulty, timeline, BOM and risk; cliche blacklist with increment fixes; recommend/check/compare/budget modes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Engineering students use this skill to brainstorm, assess, compare, and budget embedded capstone or final-year project topics. It helps them prepare a feasible topic for discussion with their supervisor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Topic rankings, timelines, and component costs are estimates and may not fit local course requirements or prices.

Mitigation: Check current local prices and course requirements, and confirm the final topic with the supervisor.

Risk: A suggested topic may conflict with course AI-use rules or require ethics approval for human participation.

Mitigation: Check the course policy and ask the supervisor whether ethics approval is needed before collecting data.
