## Use Case: <br>
External users and investment researchers use this skill to structure long-form semiconductor industry and company analysis, including profit-pool mapping, company comparison, qualitative valuation discipline, and non-trading investment conclusions. The skill is for research writing and decision support, not licensed financial advice or automated trading. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated semiconductor investment conclusions may be mistaken for licensed financial advice or trading signals. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as research support only, review conclusions independently, and avoid treating the skill as an automated trading or advisory system. <br>
Risk: Investment analysis can become misleading if market prices, valuation metrics, or company financials are stale or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current prices, financial data, and material assumptions before publication or investment use. <br>
Risk: External posting or document storage may expose unreviewed investment commentary. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before publishing or storing generated analysis outside the local workflow. <br>
