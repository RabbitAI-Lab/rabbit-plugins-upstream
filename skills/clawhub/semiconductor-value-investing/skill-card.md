## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce investment research that readers may mistake for personalized financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as research drafts and require user review before relying on or sharing any financial conclusion. <br>
Risk: Market prices, financial data, and valuation conclusions can become stale or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current prices, financial statements, and valuation inputs before using or publishing the analysis. <br>
Risk: Drafts may be published to Xueqiu or archived in Tencent Docs without sufficient review. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit approval before any publishing or local archiving action. <br>
