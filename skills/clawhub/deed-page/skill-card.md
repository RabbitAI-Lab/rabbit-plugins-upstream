## Description:

Publish files to a permanent deed.page URL. No account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[f-o-x11](https://clawhub.ai/user/f-o-x11)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can publish generated files, folders, or inline HTML as static websites and share them through a permanent deed.page URL.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad publishing requests could result in lasting public uploads without clear authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm authorization for public hosting and the specific files; review the complete upload directory for private content before publishing.

Risk: The site is public, and its management token can update or delete it.

Mitigation: Keep the management token secure and set an expiry when appropriate.
