## Description:

Generates 4MP raster images for print-ready and large-format creative work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and designers use this skill to generate high-resolution images from text prompts for print and large-format projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and files explicitly supplied for generation may be sent to dLazy-hosted services.

Mitigation: Confirm you trust the dLazy service before installing and avoid supplying sensitive material without authorization.

Risk: Login stores an API key in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the local key and rotate, revoke, or remove it when no longer needed.
