## Description: <br>
Builds ready-to-paste Boolean and X-ray search strings for candidate sourcing, including synonyms, must-haves, exclusions, platform variants, and a refinement plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Recruiters, sourcers, and hiring teams use this skill to turn role briefs into job-related Boolean and X-ray searches for LinkedIn, GitHub, Google, and relevant profile sites. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recruiting searches could include protected characteristics or proxy signals. <br>
Mitigation: Use only job-related criteria and avoid filters for age, gender, ethnicity, or similar protected or proxy attributes. <br>
Risk: Generated X-ray queries may conflict with platform-specific Boolean behavior or platform terms. <br>
Mitigation: Review platform syntax and terms before use, then adjust queries for each site. <br>
