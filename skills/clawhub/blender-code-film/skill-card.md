## Description:

Guides an agent to build and render short Blender films in Python, from still previews and critique through PNG frames and optional MP4 encoding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to turn a brief into a short 3D Blender film built from Python, review shots through stills and contact sheets, and render the final sequence with optional music.
