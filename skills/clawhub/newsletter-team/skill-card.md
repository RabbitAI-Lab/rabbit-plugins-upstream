## Description:

Researches, drafts, edits, and schedules newsletters using web and file tools across multiple channels with team role coordination.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[t3ratech](https://clawhub.ai/user/t3ratech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, communications, and editorial teams use this skill to coordinate agents that monitor sources, research topics, draft newsletter copy, review changes, and schedule approved newsletter work across channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts, saved articles, or research summaries may contain stale, incorrect, or unsuitable content if sources are not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review researched sources and final newsletter copy before publishing or scheduling.

Risk: The skill may retain personalization or workflow details through memory where enabled.

Mitigation: Confirm what memory is stored and avoid retaining sensitive subscriber, editorial, or business data.

Risk: Scheduling integrations may affect calendar or publishing workflows.

Mitigation: Keep event or schedule mutations approval-gated and verify the connected calendar or scheduler before enabling.
