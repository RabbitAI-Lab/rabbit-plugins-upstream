## Description: <br>
Researches GitHub repositories as product opportunities by mapping markets, comparing competitors, analyzing repository and issue signals, and recommending opportunities for product managers or indie builders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[margaretzybgl](https://clawhub.ai/user/margaretzybgl) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, indie builders, and developers use this skill to research open-source products on GitHub, compare market alternatives, evaluate demand and maintenance signals, and identify credible product opportunities. It supports Chinese and English research workflows with bilingual search expansion. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can cause an agent to perform GitHub and web-based product research, which may rely on incomplete, inaccessible, or time-sensitive public evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Review the reported sources, observation dates, and stated limitations before using the research for product or investment decisions. <br>
Risk: Connected GitHub account permissions may affect what repository or issue evidence the agent can access. <br>
Mitigation: Review connected GitHub permissions separately and use least-privilege access for research workflows. <br>
