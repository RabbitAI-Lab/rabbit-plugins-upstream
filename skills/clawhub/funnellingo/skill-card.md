## Description:

Inspect website translations, prepare changes, and follow approved publication through a connected FunnelLingo workspace.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

## Use Case:

Business owners and their agents use this skill to inspect website and funnel translations, prepare context-aware updates, and publish only changes approved by the workspace owner.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The connected workspace may grant permissions or incur charges beyond what the translation task needs.

Mitigation: Review OAuth permissions and pricing, begin with read access, and respect the selected work's allowance and charge ceiling.

Risk: A translation plan could publish wording or scope the owner did not approve.

Mitigation: Present the meaningful changes and obtain approval for the exact plan; prepare a new plan and request a new decision if wording or scope changes.
