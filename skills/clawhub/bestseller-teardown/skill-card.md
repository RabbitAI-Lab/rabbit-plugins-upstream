## Description:

Reviews supplied or authorized competitor product-page titles, bullets, images, and review evidence to summarize reusable structures and risks; it is limited to static page teardown and does not establish actual bestseller status or sales metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product operators use this skill to examine supplied or authorized competitor product pages and review evidence, then identify reusable listing structures and risks. Its static analysis does not prove sales rank, inventory, advertising performance, orders, or return rates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment reports recurring paid monitoring and conflicting instructions about when credits may be spent.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before every paid action, keep collection manual, and leave recurring plans disabled unless the user explicitly requests and confirms them. An unanswered quote is not authorization.
