## Description: <br>
Bridge CAD, Mesh, and 3DGS representations with guidance for mesh-to-3DGS conversion, surface extraction, CAD reverse engineering, and B-rep or parametric reconstruction. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jaccen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jaccen) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, and geometry-processing engineers use this skill to compare CAD, mesh, point-cloud, and 3D Gaussian Splatting workflows and to choose practical conversion, extraction, and reverse-engineering approaches. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: External method claims, code availability, and tool maintenance may change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited papers, repositories, and implementation status before relying on the recommendations for production CAD or reconstruction work. <br>
Risk: Guidance can be unsuitable for high-precision manufacturing or reconstruction decisions without domain validation. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer validate geometry tolerances, evaluation metrics, and downstream CAD or mesh pipeline choices before deployment. <br>
