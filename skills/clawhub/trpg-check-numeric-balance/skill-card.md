## Description: <br>
Use this skill when auditing the numeric balance of a completed TRPG rule system, including level curves, economy, combat, equipment, skills, consumables, and other numeric subsystems. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, game designers, and agents use this skill to review completed TRPG rule systems for numeric balance issues and produce an itemized audit with concrete adjustment recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow is written primarily in Chinese and may produce analysis in Chinese by default. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to respond in the preferred language before starting the audit. <br>
Risk: The skill requires the agent to read relevant TRPG rule files to assess numeric balance. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the rule files needed for review and do not include credentials or unrelated sensitive material. <br>
Risk: Balance recommendations can be subjective and may not fit the intended play style without designer review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat findings as review guidance, then test high-priority changes through playtesting or scenario simulation before publishing rules. <br>
