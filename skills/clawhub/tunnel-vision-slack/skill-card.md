## Description:

Helps people identify recurring urgent work and design protected time for strategic priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, leaders, and teams use this skill to diagnose recurring reactive workload and create a practical plan for protecting strategic time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recurring calendar blocks and automatic meeting declines could disrupt existing commitments if applied without clear boundaries.

Mitigation: Before applying changes, agree on the calendar, time slots, end date, invitation exceptions, and how to disable automatic declines.
