## Description: <br>
Helps users diagnose recurring firefighting and design protected slack so important strategic work is not displaced by urgent demands. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, leaders, and teams use this skill to identify structural bandwidth scarcity, map recurring urgent-work triggers, and create a Slack Architecture Map with protected time, delegation mechanisms, and review checkpoints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may encourage changes to personal or team calendar norms, including protected time blocks or auto-decline policies. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as user-directed coaching outcomes; review proposed calendar, delegation, and team-policy changes with the affected people before adopting them. <br>
