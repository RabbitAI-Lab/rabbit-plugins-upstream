# Evidence and Compliance

## Evidence states

- `measured`: directly recorded for the lot.
- `calculated`: derived from recorded values.
- `target`: planned set point or window.
- `estimated`: planning value awaiting confirmation.
- `missing`: required evidence absent.

Never present targets or estimates as completed-wine measurements.

## Environmental language

Farming descriptions and formal certification are separate.

- `regenerative`, `biodynamic` or `ecological` may describe documented practices.
- `organic wine` requires certified grapes, certified operators, traceability and compliant winemaking.
- A custom visual mark should be called a `regenerative project label` unless an independent certification scheme authorizes stronger wording.

## Geographic designation

Protected names restrict origin, varieties, operators and processing. Confirm current specifications on the live constructor and authoritative sources.

- DOP Cava applies to eligible traditional-method sparkling routes and requires registered vineyards/operators and process controls.
- DO Penedes has origin and variety constraints; organic requirements apply to the relevant route.
- DOQ Priorat requires eligible parcels, varieties, operators and wine category.
- Without verified designation, use a lawful non-DO route and do not imply protected status.

## Commercial gates

Before promising an edition, confirm:

- harvest/vintage availability;
- feasible bottle count and losses;
- release timing;
- production and label approvals;
- trademark authorization;
- destination alcohol rules and taxes;
- shipping and storage;
- price, payment and cancellation terms;
- ownership of unused labels and remaining stock.

An agent may prepare and enter a request with consent. The commission exists only after producer acceptance and contract confirmation.
