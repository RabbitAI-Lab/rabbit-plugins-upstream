# New Wine Commission Profile

## Strategic purpose

- Organization, product or host
- Occasion and desired outcome
- Audience and relationship to the host
- One-off edition or recurring vintage
- Success measure: attendance, gifting, client retention, press, hospitality or sales

## Quantity and timing

- Event date
- Desired delivery and release date
- Guest/recipient count
- Bottle count and reserve stock
- Destination and storage
- Budget range

## Wine architecture

- Still, ancestral sparkling or traditional-method sparkling
- Colour
- Dryness
- Freshness
- Texture
- Aromatic direction
- Structure/tannin
- Alcohol range
- Food and service moment
- Maturation horizon
- Geographic designation target
- Organic/certification requirement

## Available materials

- Preferred region, if any
- Preferred or excluded varieties
- Existing vineyard relationship
- Co-creation parcels checked
- Vintage measurements available
- Blend flexibility

## Vineyard state before reception

- Soil moisture depths and matric potential, where available
- Rainfall, evapotranspiration and vine water-stress evidence
- Compaction and infiltration
- Rhizosphere diagnosis and stated purpose of any amendment
- Bunch exposure, humidity, disease pressure and fruit temperature
- Acidity and route-specific maturity trajectory
- Skin integrity, rot pressure and microbial indicators where available
- Harvest segments by zone, exposure, maturity, sanitary state and intended route
- Optional chemical or optical fingerprint method and reference population

## Production capacity

- Winery and responsible winemaker
- Vessel availability
- Temperature control
- Laboratory access
- Oxygen/inert-gas capability
- Sparkling bottling and pressure-rated packaging
- Maturation/storage capacity

## Brand edition

- Licensed organization/product name
- Logo and brand guide
- Visual and material cues
- Label languages
- Personalization and numbering
- QR/NFC evidence page
- Packaging and gift presentation
- Approval owner and deadlines
- Trademark authorization

## Regenerative evidence

- Practices to document
- Certification requested
- Vineyard/region story
- Measurements shown publicly
- Back-label and linked-dossier wording

## Commercial and legal

- Billing entity and country
- Delivery country/venue
- Alcohol age and recipient controls
- Appellation review
- Label and allergen review
- Taxes/shipping responsibility
- Price and payment approval
- Contract signatory

## Contact and consent

- Full name
- Organization
- Email
- Permission to open Vin-Q registration
- Permission to enter supplied profile data
- Explicit confirmation before submission

Passwords, payment credentials and authentication codes are entered only by the user.
