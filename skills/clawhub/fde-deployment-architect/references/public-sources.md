# Public method sources

- [Azure Well-Architected Framework](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/): Tradeoffs in workload design from reliability, security, cost, operations, and performance.
- [Microsoft: Architects collaborate with workload teams](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/architect-role/collaboration): POCs are used to validate architectural decisions, but POC code cannot be considered production-ready; technical debt should be intentional, documentable, and repayable.
- [Martin Fowler: Architecture Decision Record](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ArchitectureDecisionRecord.html): Keep decisions, context and consequences in short records; replace them with new records when they change.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): Governing, mapping, measuring, and managing organizational AI risk.
- [OWASP LLM06: Excessive Agency](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/): Reduce excessive functionality, permissions, and autonomy of agents using human confirmation, quotas, and least privileges.
- [Azure: Architecture Testing Strategy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/operational-excellence/testing): Make test environments, data, security, and configurations as representative of real-world conditions as possible and prevent configuration drift.
