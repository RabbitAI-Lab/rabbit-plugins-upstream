## Description:

Create an evidence-backed weekly monitor of newly published antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) patent applications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, IP teams, R&D teams, competitive-intelligence teams, and business-development teams use this skill to monitor newly published ADC patent applications, screen noise, prioritize relevant disclosures, and prepare weekly patent intelligence briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may use PatSnap or related MCP connectors that require authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm connector access and authorization before installation or live retrieval.

Risk: Weekly patent-monitoring outputs could be mistaken for legal, clinical, or commercial conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as monitoring and triage; use qualified review for freedom-to-operate, infringement, validity, patentability, ownership, clinical efficacy, or commercial decisions.
