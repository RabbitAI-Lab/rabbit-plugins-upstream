## Description:

Guides teams through selecting, privately deploying, and operating an AI short-drama production platform, with an offline cost calculator.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Technical leads and developers use this Chinese-language guide to evaluate build-versus-buy options, plan private deployment, estimate production costs, and review operational and licensing considerations for an AI short-drama platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional vendor links and support channels could expose credentials or lead to unverified purchases.

Mitigation: Verify API providers independently and never share existing secrets through WeChat or other chats.

Risk: Deployment examples include privileged server and database commands.

Mitigation: Review sudo, systemctl, and database commands against the target environment before running them.

Risk: Illustrative prices and third-party platform licensing may not match current terms.

Mitigation: Check current provider rates and have a qualified reviewer confirm applicable licenses before commercial deployment.
