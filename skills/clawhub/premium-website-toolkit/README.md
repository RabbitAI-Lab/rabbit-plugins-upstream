Check a Website Against Premium Standards, Then Pick the Right Toolkit, in Plain Words

Give your AI agent one website. It checks the site against a premium bar, in plain words, then tells you which toolkit picks fit that site, and why, with a fix draft for owner review.

Most AI built websites look the same. A purple gradient, a centered headline, and three cards in a row. The layout looks finished, but it does not look premium, and it does not look like a real business. This skill checks the site first, then picks tools for that site only.

## What you get

- A premium check for one website, 10 checks: no purple gradients, no centered headline plus three cards cliche, premium, polished, liveable layout, project details as statistics, consistent number formatting site wide, one color palette, motion with a purpose, consistent components, clean SVG assets, and plain copy
- A slop tell list, with evidence from the site, so you know what looks cheap, and where it is
- Toolkit picks for this site only, in six areas: design MCPs, slop removal patterns, animation libraries, SVG asset generators, color palette visualizers, and UI component libraries
- A fix draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: premium check summary, slop tells found, toolkit picks, what to change first, and what to keep
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## Who this is for

People who build websites with an AI agent, for a small business, and want the site to look premium, polished, and liveable. People who run a business website, and want to know what looks cheap on it, and what to change first. People who want tool picks for one site, not a long list of tools to learn.

## Why this saves money

A premium check stops you from rebuilding a whole site when only a few things look cheap. A short pick list stops you from buying, or learning, tools you do not need for this site. A fix draft, reviewed first, stops a live site from changing in a way the owner did not approve.

## The premium bar

1. No purple gradients.
2. No centered headline plus three cards cliche.
3. Premium, polished, and liveable layout.
4. Project details as statistics.
5. Strictly consistent number formatting site wide.
6. One color palette, used the same way on every page.
7. Motion with a purpose.
8. Consistent components.
9. Clean SVG assets.
10. Plain copy, in the voice of the business.

Tool lists go stale fast. The picks in this skill are current picks dated 12 Nov 2026, not a permanent list. Check the tool's own page before using it. The six tool names in the source reels are Animaster, Skiper UI, Vengeance UI, Motion Primitives, Haikei, and Realtime Colors.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the site URL, the goal of the site, and the audience, in plain words.
3. Ask: "Check this site with premium-website-toolkit."
4. Read the premium check first. Every Pass needs evidence from the site.
5. Read the toolkit picks. A pick that fits no check on this site is Not needed.
6. Read the fix draft. Approve the first change before anything goes live.

If you already give the site URL, the goal, and the audience up front, the skill does the full check and the toolkit picks in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not change a live site, publish anything, or buy a tool, without a separate approval from the owner.
- It will not invent a tool name, a price, a link, a stat, a partner, a testimonial, or a result.
- It will not claim a tool does something unless the tool's own page was checked, and says so.
- A fix draft is not a promise of more sales, more visitors, or a higher rank.
