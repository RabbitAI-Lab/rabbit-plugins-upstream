## Description:

Check a website against premium standards, then pick the right toolkit for it, in plain words, with any site and any AI agent. Your AI agent checks for AI slop, reviews layout, color, motion, and components, then gives toolkit picks and a fix draft for owner review. No live change, draft only.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Small-business website owners and AI website builders use this skill to review one site's design against ten checks, choose site-specific toolkit options, and prepare a fix draft for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Toolkit recommendations can become outdated or may not fit a particular site.

Mitigation: Check each tool's own page and base picks on evidence from the site; keep proposed changes as a draft for owner approval.
