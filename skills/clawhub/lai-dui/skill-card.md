## Description: <br>
中文名「来怼一下」。毒舌红队 / 魔鬼代言人式想法压力测试。当用户说'来怼一下'、'压力测试这个想法'、'帮我找找这个想法的漏洞'、'质疑一下XX'、'这个想法靠谱吗'、'从各角度挑刺'等时触发。针对任何想法（产品、方案、架构、功能、决策、文章论点）进行多角度批判性质疑，找出逻辑漏洞、隐藏假设与潜在失败点，并随用户修正迭代推进。不限项目、不限领域。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, developers, and decision makers use this skill to pressure-test ideas, plans, product directions, architecture choices, features, decisions, or written arguments. It restates the proposal, exposes assumptions, critiques it from multiple angles, ranks the most damaging issues first, and prompts the user to iterate. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce forceful critique that may be mistaken for qualified advice in high-stakes domains such as health, legal, financial, or major personal decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the critique as a pressure-testing framework and obtain qualified professional review before acting on high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: Blunt red-team framing can overemphasize failure modes if the user needs balanced evaluation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as adversarial feedback and combine it with evidence gathering, domain expertise, and constructive alternatives. <br>
