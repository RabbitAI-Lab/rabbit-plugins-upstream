## Description: <br>
Poka Yoke Design helps process, quality, and design engineers turn defects or error modes into mistake-proofing concepts using E-R-F-D-M principles, classic poka-yoke methods, and effectiveness ranking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
