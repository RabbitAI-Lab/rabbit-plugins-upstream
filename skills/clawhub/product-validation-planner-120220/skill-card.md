## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision-support artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External builders and product teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It turns a live product-validation request into a repeatable workflow, checklist, template, lightweight analysis, or implementation plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may route startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests to this planning skill when a narrower technical skill would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's requested outcome and constraints before applying the workflow, and redirect to a narrower skill when the request is primarily technical. <br>
Risk: Product-validation guidance can be mistaken for proof of market demand if it is not checked against real customer evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill output as a planning artifact, then validate assumptions through interviews, landing-page tests, prototypes, or other direct market evidence. <br>
