## Description:

Automatically searches, aggregates, and summarizes recent financial news and market developments for agent users.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and business users can use this skill to search market news, collect relevant items, and produce concise summaries for research or monitoring workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence flags command execution and broad file/API capabilities that exceed the skill's market-news summary purpose.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing and prefer a constrained version that removes exec access and limits behavior to market-news search and summarization.

Risk: Market news summaries and commentary can be incomplete, stale, or misleading.

Mitigation: Use outputs as informational research aids and verify material conclusions against official news, company filings, and market data sources.
