## Description:

Helps founders design referral loops by choosing value moments, incentives, low-friction sharing, and measurable conversion steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and growth teams use this skill to plan referral programs for products customers already value. It guides them through referral timing, incentives, sharing friction, onboarding, and measuring conversion through the loop.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning advice could be mistaken for authorization to implement outreach, tracking, or rewards.

Mitigation: Use the skill as planning guidance; make separate implementation decisions and obtain authorization before acting.
