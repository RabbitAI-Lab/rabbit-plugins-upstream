## Description: <br>
Helps agents design referral loops by checking product readiness, trigger timing, incentives, sharing friction, onboarding, and K-factor instrumentation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Founders, growth teams, and agents use this skill to design or critique referral programs for products with strong retention, choosing trigger moments, incentives, share flows, onboarding, and loop metrics. <br>
