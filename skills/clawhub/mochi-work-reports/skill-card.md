## Description:

Build daily and weekly work reports from dated work evidence, keeping completed, ongoing and blocked items separate and checking reporting periods and recorded durations. Use for 工作日报与周报.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[hznetnewpower](https://clawhub.ai/user/hznetnewpower)

## Use Case:

Employees, teams, and managers can turn supplied dated work records into daily or weekly progress reports, with completed work, ongoing work, blockers, evidence, and recorded durations clearly distinguished.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Work notes persist in plaintext in the workspace-local ledger and may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Use an appropriate workspace for sensitive records and review reports before sharing or requesting delivery.

Risk: The ledger has no built-in editing or deletion, so incorrect entries can persist.

Mitigation: Check entries before adding them and follow the documented correction workflow while retaining the relationship to the earlier record.
