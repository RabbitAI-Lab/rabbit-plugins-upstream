## Description:

Bitcoin market data for agents from AgentReeve, plus funding, basis and open interest for every crypto perpetual on Binance, Bybit, OKX and Hyperliquid - prices and candles across exchanges, derivatives, options and volatility, long/short positioning, liquidations, order-book walls, Hyperliquid whale flow and historical analogues. Free preview and free samples; live data per request over x402 (USDC on Base), no account or API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers and agents use this skill to retrieve current or delayed Bitcoin and cryptocurrency perpetual market data, including prices, derivatives, positioning, liquidity, and historical comparisons.
