## Description:

Bitcoin market data for agents from AgentReeve - prices and candles across exchanges, derivatives, options and volatility, long/short positioning, liquidations, order-book walls, Hyperliquid whale flow and historical analogues. Free preview and free samples; live data per request over x402 (USDC on Base), no account or API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
