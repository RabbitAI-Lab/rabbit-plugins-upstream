## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cloud service use for answers and self-checks may expose sensitive pre-IPO tax, control, related-party, or irregularity facts. <br>
Mitigation: Use sanitized scenarios unless the organization has approved the data flow and the applicable confidentiality controls. <br>
Risk: Local API credentials and logs may be stored by the skill. <br>
Mitigation: Review local storage locations, rotate or revoke credentials when no longer needed, and avoid sharing logs that contain sensitive business facts. <br>
Risk: Optional setup can register MCP settings in supported agent clients when explicitly enabled. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed client configuration changes before enabling setup and keep backups of existing agent configuration. <br>
Risk: Tax and IPO-control guidance may be incomplete or stale for a specific issuer, jurisdiction, or listing path. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as self-check support and have qualified tax, audit, legal, or securities professionals verify conclusions before action. <br>
