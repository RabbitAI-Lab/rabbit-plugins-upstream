## Description: <br>
Helps agents choose organic social channels from audience evidence, objectives, platform capabilities, staffing capacity, and boundary routing, then prepares proposed channel-state events. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Apache-2.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, social, and founder-led teams use this skill to decide which organic social channels to run before posting, balancing audience evidence, platform access, objectives, and staffing capacity. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Audience exports, analytics evidence, and local channel-memory records can influence channel recommendations and proposed state. <br>
Mitigation: Treat supplied evidence as untrusted, review the portfolio and proposed events before relying on them, and keep canonical channel changes in the channel registry. <br>
Risk: The skill may ask to save results or queue proposed channel events. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before saving results and review proposed events before registry acceptance. <br>
