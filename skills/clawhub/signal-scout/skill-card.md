## Description:

Turn a startup URL or product description into an evidence-backed, source-verified shortlist of first customers (people to message), market segments (audiences to target with content), and companies (partners to pitch) — using public signals only. Use when the user wants to find early customers, validate demand, research prospects, or plan first outreach for a product.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
