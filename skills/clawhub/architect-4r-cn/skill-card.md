## Description:

仅在显式调用时使用。基于李运华的 4R 方法梳理系统层级、角色、联系与协作规则，为每个 R 分别绘制 Mermaid 图，并提供模板与检查项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

开发者和架构师可用此框架明确系统观察层级、组成角色、角色联系与协作规则，并为架构讨论生成结构化图示和检查项。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated architecture models may be inaccurate or may be mistaken for the actual system; bundled payment examples are illustrative.

Mitigation: Review each generated model against the actual system and treat bundled examples as illustrative.
