## Description: <br>
Generate music from a text description - full songs with vocals and lyrics, instrumental beds, jingles, intros, and background tracks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and agent users use this skill to turn a music brief into model-ready audio generation guidance for vocal songs, instrumental beds, jingles, intros, and cover or restyle workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, lyrics, and uploaded source audio may be sent to external generation providers. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive material, copyrighted source tracks, or proprietary audio unless the user has permission to process it with the provider. <br>
Risk: Generated music or cover/restyle requests can raise rights and likeness concerns when they target identifiable artists, bands, songs, or recordings. <br>
Mitigation: Describe broad style, genre, instrumentation, tempo, and mood; keep lyrics original and avoid close imitation of named performers or specific existing songs. <br>
