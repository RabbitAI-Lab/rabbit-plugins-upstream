## Description: <br>
Orchestrates the announcement, assets, channels, and timing for shipping a product, covering the arc from teaser to launch day to post-launch momentum. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Founders, marketers, product teams, and operators use this skill to plan product launches from positioning and asset production through channel sequencing, launch-day execution, and post-launch measurement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Launch advice may not fit a specific market, audience, platform, budget, or support capacity. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations against current product data, channel rules, budget constraints, and staffing plans before acting. <br>
Risk: Marketing timing, paid promotion, public posting, and community outreach choices can create reputational or compliance exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Have the responsible product, marketing, legal, or community owner approve launch timing, claims, and posting plans before publication. <br>
