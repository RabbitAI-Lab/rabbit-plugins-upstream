## Description: <br>
Agent Era Pricing helps pricing and packaging teams redesign seat-based pricing for human-and-agent usage by selecting a durable value metric, designing agent/API tiers and fences, modeling cannibalization, and planning migration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Pricing, packaging, and product strategy teams use this skill to migrate from seat-based pricing to usage- or outcome-based pricing when automation and agent traffic change customer behavior. It produces a value-metric recommendation, agent-tier design, revenue cannibalization bridge, migration plan, and pricing tripwires. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may require sensitive pricing, usage, and customer-profile data to produce useful strategy outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only data approved for the agent environment, redact unnecessary customer identifiers, and review generated pricing recommendations before using them in commercial decisions. <br>
Risk: Pricing strategy outputs may be misleading if cohort, usage, or unit-economics inputs are incomplete or inaccurate. <br>
Mitigation: Use representative customer cohorts, label assumptions, and validate the cannibalization bridge against current revenue before migration. <br>
