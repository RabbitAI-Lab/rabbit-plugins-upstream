## Description:

Analyzes US stocks through quick data reads or an adversarial investment committee that tests competing theses against sourced market sentiment, investor activity, and fundamentals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors and research agents use this skill to review a US stock ticker, compare bull and bear cases, and produce a sourced, plain-English research verdict or a shorter market-data brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock tickers and related research requests are sent to SentiSense using an API key.

Mitigation: Review the data being shared before using the skill and keep the API key private.

Risk: Research outputs may be incomplete or mistaken and are not financial advice.

Mitigation: Check cited evidence and verify important investment decisions independently; do not treat a partial preview as a complete data window.
