## Description:

Guides users on video watermarking, ownership marking, anti-theft practices, and evidence collection when video content is copied without authorization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators, rights holders, and agents assisting them use this skill to explain practical watermarking, provenance, and evidence-preservation steps for videos. It is a guidance skill and does not itself modify video files or enforce copyright claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad video- or theft-related prompts may activate the skill when the user did not intend to request watermarking or anti-theft guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent and scope before giving detailed watermarking, provenance, or evidence-preservation advice.

Risk: Governance, legal, and regulatory references in generated guidance may need current jurisdiction-specific verification.

Mitigation: Treat legal or regulatory content as general information and verify important claims against authoritative sources or qualified counsel.
