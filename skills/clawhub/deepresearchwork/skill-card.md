## Description: <br>
Comprehensive research framework that combines web search, content analysis, source verification, and iterative investigation to conduct in-depth research on any topic. Use when you need to perform thorough research with multiple sources, cross-validation, and structured findings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The bundled JavaScript workflow can produce mock sources, mock content, and mock confidence values while presenting a deep-research process. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the JavaScript as a demo or template, and require real web/search/fetch results with citations before relying on research outputs. <br>
Risk: Research outputs may include incorrect or misleading claims if source verification is simulated or skipped. <br>
Mitigation: Review critical claims against authoritative sources, preserve citations, and flag uncertainty in the final report. <br>
