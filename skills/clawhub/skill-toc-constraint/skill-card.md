## Description: <br>
交互引导式运用TOC五大聚焦步骤（找出/挖尽/迁就/松绑/回头）识别系统约束并制定突破策略，强制交互锁保证分析真实，不替用户决策 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Operations, quality improvement, and process improvement teams use this skill to work through the Theory of Constraints five focusing steps and produce a structured constraint analysis report. The skill guides the user through identifying, exploiting, subordinating, elevating, and revisiting constraints without making business decisions for them. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for operational or business-process details while building a TOC report. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business information unless that is appropriate for the agent environment. <br>
Risk: The generated constraint analysis can be incomplete or misleading if user inputs, operational data, or site observations are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Have responsible business owners validate the report against real process data before using it for investment or process-change decisions. <br>
