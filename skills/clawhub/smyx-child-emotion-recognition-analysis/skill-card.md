## Description:

Analyzes images or videos of children for signs of negative emotions and returns a structured report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Caregivers can submit a child's image or video to request an emotion analysis report and review previously generated reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags privacy, identity and account-handling concerns, as well as a mismatch between advertised alerts and demonstrated reporting.

Mitigation: Before uploading children's images or video, review external processing and identity/account handling; verify that caregiver alerts are available, and keep direct adult supervision for urgent situations.
