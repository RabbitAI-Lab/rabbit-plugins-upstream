## Description:

Helps Chinese-language creators assess whether a draft, popular post, or story idea gives audiences a clear takeaway and reason to engage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent, Chinese-language content creators use the skill to diagnose draft resonance, examine why another post connected with viewers, or test whether a personal observation reflects a broader pattern. It grounds judgments in source passages and separates inferences from claims needing validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests about whether content will resonate may activate the skill unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants an audience-resonance assessment before applying its workflow.

Risk: The skill may retain narrow local notes about corrected judgments or validated content patterns when agent memory is available.

Mitigation: Review saved notes and avoid retaining sensitive draft content or personal details unnecessarily.
