## Description:

AI KEY·戳不戳得中人 diagnoses whether Chinese content will resonate by identifying the audience takeaway, likely action impulse, and evidence-backed claims across draft review, viral-content decoding, and hunch-to-pattern analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and content strategists use this skill to check whether a Chinese-language draft, viral example, or unresolved content hunch gives the audience a clear takeaway and a reason to save, share, comment, or follow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read AIKEY reference and memory files and save small memory notes about corrected resonance judgments or validated content-performance patterns.

Mitigation: Use it only with workspace and memory scopes you are comfortable sharing, and review any saved memory notes for sensitive content.

Risk: The skill produces content resonance judgments that are evidence-labeled but still depend on user-provided drafts, examples, and available engagement data.

Mitigation: Treat its audience-response claims as hypotheses until validated with comments, saves, shares, follows, or other post-publication data.
