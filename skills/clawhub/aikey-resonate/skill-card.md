## Description:

Analyzes a draft, a published hit, or an early observation to identify what audiences may take away and what actions the content may prompt, grounding each judgment in source text and labeling inference separately.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Independent knowledge creators use this skill to assess their drafts, decode successful posts, and test whether an observation may matter to a wider audience. It helps identify a clear audience takeaway and possible save, share, comment, or follow impulses while separating source evidence from inference.
