## Description:

Your AI agent phones businesses for you: books, reschedules, cancels, asks, and waits on hold.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[skeptrunedev](https://clawhub.ai/user/skeptrunedev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People use this skill to have an agent call businesses for bookings, changes, cancellations, and questions, then report the outcome and transcript.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Remote account setup and phone calls can use prepaid credits.

Mitigation: Check the available balance and confirm the business and request before placing a call.

Risk: Profile details provided for calls may be saved by the remote service.

Mitigation: Share only details needed for the call and decline optional profile fields.
