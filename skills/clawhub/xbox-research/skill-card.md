## Description:

Research Xbox game listings, regional prices, compatibility, accessibility features, curated collections, subscription inclusion, and review samples through Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Players and researchers use this skill to compare Xbox and PC game editions, regional prices, device compatibility, accessibility features, Game Pass inclusion, and review samples without making purchases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill requires a Crawlora API key and sends Xbox research queries to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Provide a key only if comfortable using Crawlora, and keep secrets and unrelated personal data out of query parameters.

Risk: Regional prices, subscriptions, and compatibility details may change or be incomplete.

Mitigation: Record the locale, source, and collection time; verify current store details before making purchase or device decisions.
