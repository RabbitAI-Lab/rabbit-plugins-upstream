## Description:

Tech To Product helps agents translate materials and materials-science technologies into real-world product applications, covering material selection, processing methods, performance requirements, and system integration constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to turn materials or materials-science technologies into practical product and application analysis, including product examples, integration approaches, requirements, trade-offs, constraints, and adoption considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce productization guidance from general industry practice when no external context is provided.

Mitigation: Prefer supplied context and sources when available, and treat unsourced examples as representative patterns that need domain review before product decisions.

Risk: Materials-to-product recommendations can miss use-case-specific safety, regulatory, cost, or manufacturing constraints.

Mitigation: Have qualified domain reviewers validate constraints, certifications, and implementation assumptions before using the analysis for deployment or commercialization.
