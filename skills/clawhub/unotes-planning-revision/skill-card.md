## Description:

Builds a revision plan from the signed-in user's uNotes flashcard decks, quizzes, and study streak, with quota details when requested.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners use this skill to review their own uNotes decks, quizzes, and study streak and get a short course-by-course revision plan. They can also ask about their remaining feature quotas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires access to the signed-in user's study data through the uNotes connector.

Mitigation: Grant connector access only if you want the agent to read your flashcards, quizzes, streak, and quota for revision guidance.
