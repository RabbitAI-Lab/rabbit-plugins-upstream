## Description:

W-ABCD 真假 Web3 识别器 helps users, investors, and content creators evaluate whether a claimed Web3, industrial blockchain, or RWA project is a real Web3 industrial application or a misleading concept or scam risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, investors, content creators, and project teams use this Chinese-language skill to review Web3, industrial blockchain, exchange, platform, community, and RWA claims against the W-ABCD factors. It focuses on whether a genuine collaborative community exists and surfaces scam-risk signals without providing investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may mix technical authenticity checks with China-specific compliance framing when applied outside China.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to separate technical authenticity from local legal compliance and verify current rules with qualified sources.

Risk: A project authenticity review can be mistaken for legal, financial, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary risk-screening guidance and independently verify project claims, current regulations, and professional advice before acting.
