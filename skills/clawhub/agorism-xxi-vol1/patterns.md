# Patterns & Techniques — Agorism in the 21st Century, Vol. 1

## Phase diagnosis (Konkin)
**When to use**: situating any freedom-tech project in the transition model.
**How**: identify density (0-4): conversion phase? alliance formation? autonomous zones? resource parity? integration? Pair every theory-exchange with counter-economic practice and vice versa.
**Trade-offs**: phase labels tempt teleology; Konkin's own end state self-negates.

## Alliance formation (Konkin)
**When to use**: cooperating across ideological difference.
**How**: sovereign units, explicit shared goal, disband-on-attainment; "everyone appearing disagrees" — dissonance is a feature.
**Trade-offs**: shallow trust; goal drift dissolves the alliance (by design).

## Gun/tool/escape sorting (Brekke)
**When to use**: evaluating any market mechanism or token design.
**How**: which face shows — coercion (organizing social relations), tool (design-space for outcomes), or escape (multipolarity/exit)? Utopia-sniff the white paper; audit the dark-market coercion vacuum.
**Trade-offs**: fence-sitting reads as impurity; Brekke claims it as honesty.

## Subtract the super-ordinate (Land)
**When to use**: decentralizing any system.
**How**: find the pseudo-transcendent element (trusted third party, registrar, oracle, central planner), route around it, collapse onto immanent peer-to-peer arrangement. "Entity becomes network."
**Trade-offs**: cold anarchy doesn't choose values; the vacuum gets filled by SOMETHING (Brekke's warning).

## Outward-orientation audit (Land)
**When to use**: assessing institutional health.
**How**: inward obsession (domestic policy, self-contemplation, aching kidneys) = morbidity; cognitive resources belong on external relations/the game.
**Trade-offs**: applied to persons it's brutal; applied to institutions it's diagnostic gold.

## Weaving geometry selection (Ireland via D&G)
**When to use**: designing organizations.
**How**: loom (striated: roles, metrics, state-shaped) vs felt (smooth: entangled, nomadic) vs patchwork (piece-by-piece, no centre, intensive additions). Match the geometry to the multiplicity you want.
**Trade-offs**: striated delivers predictability; smooth/patchwork deliver adaptability at visibility cost (subject groups are invisible — that's cover AND fragility).

## Feature-vs-unit grouping (Land)
**When to use**: any classification, membership, or coalition decision.
**How**: ask whether membership is an attribute (feature) or a function (unit). Prefer functional entanglement for action-groups; watch identity-classification for policing vectors.
**Trade-offs**: unit groups lack the legal/moral legibility feature groups have (Nanjing problem).

## Affinity not identity (Haraway via Ireland)
**When to use**: building alliances across difference.
**How**: connect by what works together; skip filiation/origin stories; "unity-through-affiliation" undermines organic/natural definition systems.
**Trade-offs**: affinity coalitions are harder to explain, easier to sustain.

## Empire→church→reformation mapping (Halpin)
**When to use**: long-game strategy against platform power.
**How**: identify the falling empire, the protocol-church that persists, and the literacy-equivalent (computation/crypto) whose distribution breaks the priesthood.
**Trade-offs**: analogies run on rails; the Chinese axis (the open question) may not fit the Western pattern.

## Techniques-without-technology test (Halpin/Invisible Committee)
**When to use**: adopting any tool.
**How**: does it extend capability without feeding a control system? Turn pattern-detection back on the detectors (mixnets, ZK) where possible.
**Trade-offs**: pure flight (Tarnac) fails; engagement risks capture — the test is ongoing, not one-time.
