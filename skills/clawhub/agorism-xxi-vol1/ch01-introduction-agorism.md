# Chapter 1: An Introduction to Agorism in both Theory and Practice — Dylan-Ennis & Barlowe

## Core Idea
Konkin's agorism is a phased theory of revolutionary transition through counter-economics — and it maps onto cryptocurrency with uncanny precision: "Konkin is crypto avant la lettre."

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Agorism = theory + practice**: libertarian theory (voluntaryism, non-violence, pro-market, socially-permissive, anti-state, anti-war) integrated with counter-economic practice ("put the book down and smuggle oil or bootleg some movies. Or start an unregulated DeFi exchange").
- **The Five Phases (Konkin's transition model)**:
  - *Phase 0* (Zero-Density): nascent proto-agorists; task = convert + educate, pairing theory with counter-economic activity.
  - *Phase 1* (Low-Density): libertarian ideas gain traction; counter-economists reject party politics; New Libertarian Alliances (NLAs) form — "an association of entrepreneurs of liberty for the purpose of specializing, coordinating, and delivering libertarian activities" — agitation within existing organizations.
  - *Phase 2* (Mid-Density): agorist "ghettoes"/districts appear, contaminating wider society with sympathy for authenticity outside the state.
  - *Phase 3* (High-Density): State vs Agora resource parity; the State enters "terminal crises"; NLAs guard against statism's final gasps.
  - *Phase 4* (Agorist Society with Statist Impurities): remnants rooted out and *re-integrated* into agorist society.
- **Counter-Economy defined**: "All (non-coercive) human action committed in defiance of the State" — tax evasion, inflation avoidance, smuggling, freed production, illegal distribution. "Agorism is our end, Counter-Economics is our means."
- **Konkin's 1986 encryption prophecy**: "should the Counter-Economy lick the information problem it would virtually eliminate the risk it incurs under the State's threat... advertise your products, reach your customers and accept payment, all outside the detection capabilities of the State" — the earliest description of darknet markets (Ulbricht read Konkin; both studied chemistry).
- **NLA = DAO**: Konkin's alliance definition prefigures the DAO — goal-oriented, formable/disbandable, grey-to-black market activity (DeFi).

## Key Concepts
- **AnarchoVillage**: Long Beach apartment block; Konkin's archive preserved by Victor Koman (KoPubCo).
- **Primary literature**: New Libertarian Manifesto (1980), An Agorist Primer (1986/pub 2008), unfinished Counter-Economics (2018 Kindle).
- **Self-negation**: agorism's end state dissolves the counter/regular distinction — "a world where you are allowed to be consistent with your true self."

## Mental Models
- Use *phase-diagnosis* on any freedom-tech project: which density phase is it in? (Crypto overall: mid-Phase-2 per the authors — autonomous zones gaining footholds.)
- Use *theory-practice pairing* for recruitment: teach theory to practitioners, practice to theorists — never one alone.

## Anti-patterns
- **Partyarchy**: engaging electoral politics — Konkin's permanent exit, repeated as warning to crypto.
- **Theory-only libertarianism**: heads right, no counter-economy.
- **Practice-only counter-economics**: risk without consciousness — converts miss the map.

## Worked Example
DeFi yield farming read through the phases: an autonomous zone inside pre-existing states, switching users on to counter-economic activity by attraction (rates), opening "an aperture within mainstream society about what is economically possible" — Phase 2 contamination in Konkin's exact sense, arriving 40 years after he described it.

## Key Takeaways
1. Agorism is phased, not spontaneous: conversion → alliance → zones → parity → integration.
2. The 1986 encryption prophecy is the hinge between Konkin and crypto.
3. NLAs/DAOs, darknets, and DeFi all instantiate Konkin structures he named decades early.
4. The agora wins by attraction (authenticity), not confrontation.
5. End state: the counter-economy becomes just... the economy.
