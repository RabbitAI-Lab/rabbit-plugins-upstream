## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The source material discusses illegal or unregulated markets, tax evasion, and related ideological arguments.

Mitigation: Use the skill for literary, philosophical, historical, or conceptual analysis; do not use it to plan real-world evasion, illegal markets, or financial activity.

Risk: The artifact preserves tensions among essays and can be misleading if treated as a single operational doctrine.

Mitigation: Preserve attribution and disagreement among authors when summarizing or applying concepts, and avoid converting philosophical discussion into step-by-step operational advice.
