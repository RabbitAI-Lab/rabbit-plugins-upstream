# Cheatsheet — Agorism in the 21st Century, Vol. 1

## Foundations (Konkin)
| Question | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What's the practice? | Counter-economics: non-coercive action in defiance of the State | Agorism = theory + practice (Ch 1) |
| Party politics? | No — partyarchy was Konkin's original exit | "Agora! Anarchy! Action!" (Ch 0) |
| How to ally? | Sovereign units, shared goal, disband after | Alliance ≠ party (Ch 0) |
| Recruiting? | Pair theory with practice — never one alone | Unconscious agorists become conscious (Ch 0-1) |
| End state? | Counter-economy becomes just the economy | Agorism self-negates (Ch 1) |
