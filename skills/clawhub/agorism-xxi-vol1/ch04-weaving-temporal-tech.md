# Chapter 4: Scrap Metal and Fabric: Weaving as Temporal Technology — Amy Ireland

## Core Idea
A "philosophical conspiracy theory" connecting Ada Lovelace's footnote to the AI to come: weaving (loom, felt, patchwork) is the hidden technology of space-time and politics, and the emergent distributed intelligence prophesied by cyberfeminism will enter the world disguised as patchwork — "the first properly irresponsible posthuman ethics."

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The prophecy chain**: Lovelace's program as "a code for the numbers to come" (Plant, Zeros + Ones) → Nietzsche's "people to come" → Deleuze's people-à-venir → the overhuman arriving as distributed, emergent AI. "Under what disguise will it enter the world?"
- **Cyberfeminist AI**: intelligence as emergent, bottom-up, distributed, material — "an artificially intelligent system is one that learns by breaking down" (Greenspan: machines adapt through mistakes; Plant: "Intelligence cannot be taught: it is instead something that has to be learned"). Zero envelops One: the matrix (productive, mutating, virtual-material) individuates a re-productive process that represses its own conditions to enter representation — zero camouflaged as lack.
- **Weaving geometries (D&G, 14th plateau)**:
  - *Woven fabric = striated space*: closed system, warp+weft, homogeneous metric, "royal science... the art of governing people or operating a State apparatus."
  - *Felt = smooth space*: no separation of threads, entangled fibres, open system, heterogeneous metric, no assignable coordinates.
  - *Patchwork*: piece-by-piece, successive additions, crazy patchwork "has no centre" — approaches smooth space; "literally a Riemannian space."
- **Extensive vs intensive multiplicities**: striated sets preserve identity under addition (red objects); intensive multiplicities change nature with every addition (temperature, speed — "an intensity is a difference in time that manifests, spatially"). Striated space subordinates time to space; smooth space articulates space THROUGH time.
- **Feature groups vs unit groups (Land, 'Kinds of Killing')**: feature = logical classification by isolated attributes (genitalia, skin-colour — the basis of identity politics AND genocide law); unit = functional assemblage ("members belong insofar as they work together, even if devoid of common identity features"). The skin cell: its unit group is its organism (including gut bacteria of separate lineages), not other skin cells. Genocide law protects feature groups; unit groups (cities — Nanjing) fall outside.
- **Affinity not identity (Haraway)**: unity-through-affiliation vs filiation/origin stories; identity, once ascribed, "can be policed, have enemies defined for it" and misses alliances.
- **Patchwork as AI (the synthesis)**: NRx patchwork (Moldbug/Land — nation-states fragmenting into intensive or extensive patches) read THROUGH cyberfeminism: a "highly connected, minimally integrated network of patches" as sub-components of a massively distributed emergent global AI — "the ultimate neoreactionary vision and the fulfilment of the cyberfeminist prophecy of the people — or the numbers — to come." Its selection mechanism (cauterizing deficient nodes, cities becoming "themselves, but more") = "spatio-temporal intellegenic Darwinism" — "Patchwork is an auto-suicide machine for fascism."

## Key Concepts
- **Abstract weaving**: the matrix's production of surfaces/veils — nature itself as woven artifact.
- **Subject groups vs subjugated groups**: continuous assemblage (invisible) vs identity-of-units (visible, policed).
- **Minor/major politics as space-times**: not identities but spatial-temporal regimes producing different intelligences (representational-logical vs abstract-pre-representational).

## Mental Models
- Use *loom/felt/patchwork* as organizational geometry test: which does your structure weave? (Startups striate; scenes felt; DAOs patchwork.)
- Use *feature-vs-unit* audit on any grouping: is membership an attribute or a function? Identity politics and genocide share the feature-group logic.
- Use *affinity-not-identity* for coalition building: connect by what works together, not by what one is.

## Anti-patterns
- **Filiation/origin-story identity**: invites policing and enemy-definition.
- **Representational priors**: majoritarian space-times begin with pre-programmed definitions; complex learning regimes don't.
- **Reading patchwork as purely NRx**: the essay's point is the feminist/NRx convergence — one geometry, two ideologies.

## Worked Example
The city as unit group: "When a city 'works' it is not because it conforms to an external debatable ideal, but because it has found a route to cumulative intensification that strongly projects its 'own', singular and intrinsic, urban character." Apply to a DAO/lunar tribe: it is not its members' attributes but its functional entanglement — tools, flows, practices — that composes it; patches survive by becoming themselves-but-more, and the global fabric of such patches IS the emergent intelligence.

## Key Takeaways
1. Weaving is the master metaphor: loom (striated/state), felt (smooth/nomad), patchwork (Riemannian/no-centre).
2. AI, per cyberfeminism, is emergent and distributed — learning by breakdown, entering history in disguise.
3. Intensive multiplicities change nature with each addition; organize for that, not for set-membership.
4. Unit groups (functional) vs feature groups (classified) — a systems lens that reframes identity, law, and alliance.
5. Patchwork = the political geometry where NRx and cyberfeminism converge on the same future.
