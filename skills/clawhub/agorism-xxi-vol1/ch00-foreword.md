# Chapter 0: Foreword — The Roots of Crypto Are Agorist

## Core Idea
Crypto's growth is propelled by its roots, not its flowers: agorism is "crypto's mythical substructure, its source of nourishment and its anchor" — and the journal's mission is converting crypto's unconscious agorists into conscious ones.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Agorism defined (SEK3)**: "the consistent integration of libertarian theory with counter-economic practice; an agorist is one who acts consistently for freedom and in freedom." Counter-economics = "the sum of all black-market activity" wielded consciously; The Agora = the self-conscious counter-economy.
- **The journal's three editorial principles**:
  1. *Reject political divisions* — Konkin situated agorism on the extreme left strategically (widening recruitment); "any anti-state revolutionary is a potential ally."
  2. *Welcome dissonance* — "Everyone appearing in this publication disagrees!" (Konkin); alliance = "a handling together of sovereign units for a goal and then disbanding."
  3. *No compromises* — rejection of "partyarchy"; Konkin left the Libertarian Party forever in 1973. Against crypto's party-politics temptation: "Agora! Anarchy! Action!"

## Key Concepts
- **Unconscious vs conscious agorists**: Silk Road's Ulbricht ("we are all agorists... I'm out to turn unconscious agorists into conscious active ones") as the paradigm case.
- **Agorism avant la lettre**: crypto as the continuation of agorism by technological means.

## Worked Example
The Foreword's own practice: a journal including Land (right-accelerationist) alongside anarchist communists — dissonance enacted per principle 2, alliance-for-a-goal per Konkin's definition.

## Key Takeaways
1. Agorism = theory + practice integrated; counter-economics is the means, the agora the end.
2. Alliances are temporary sovereign-unit coalitions, not parties.
3. Crypto is mostly unconscious agorism; consciousness is the project.
