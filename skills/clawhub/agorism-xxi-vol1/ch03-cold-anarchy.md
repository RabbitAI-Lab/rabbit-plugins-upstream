# Chapter 3: Cold Anarchy — Nick Land

## Core Idea
Cold anarchy is not a goal but the baseline fact of the international system: anarchy "is all there can ever be" — the order of external relations that "rules whenever and wherever inter-nodal dynamics dominate intra-nodal organization, ultimately always and everywhere." The direction of history is deepened disintegration: more, smaller, sovereign units.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Hot vs cold anarchy**: Hot = domestically-framed, universalistic, utopian activism (wants to mend the world so badly that anything is sanctioned; "holocaust of the real in the flame of the idea"). Cold = recognizing anarchy behind every mask of order — "order is something anarchy can do, and nothing else."
- **The realist convergence**: cold anarchy = the plane where realist international relations theory meets French transcendental philosophy — "intolerance for the illusion of unity is the coherent thread." Realist IR begins and remains with anarchy: sovereign plural powers, tragic (irreducible diversity of interests), historical (precedent, not unprecedented utopia), factual (what is, not what should be).
- **Liberalism/convervatism as cold anarchy**: "All real liberals are cold anarchists. Their primary loyalty is to competition-in-itself, rather than to any competitor" — markets above businesses, science above scientists, the Internet above FAANGs, schism above religion, "War is God." Serious conservatives preserve disintegration against dreamt unities: "Every Union is a conservative defeat."
- **The disintegration imperative (§13)**: "Proceed always in the direction of deepened disintegration. Pass from nationalism, through micro-nationalism, to nano-nationalism... from hundreds of nations, through thousands, to millions. There cannot be too many nationalities."
- **Combinatorial explosion (§11)**: nations as wholes-and-parts (monads/holons) rhizomatically connected — "to 'explode the system' is not at all to destroy it, but rather to intensify it." Set at One (consummate globalism), the system can do nothing: "There is no game, and no exit."
- **Outward-oriented intelligence (§15-17)**: animal cognition automates internals to free resources for external application; "an animal attending to the operation of its own organs is sick." Leviathan's health = absence of domestic consideration; "when domestic policy is discussed, it is as if Leviathan complains of aching kidneys."
- **Yarvin schism (§18-20)**: domestically-framed monarchism (wants an American king) vs internationally-framed cold anarchism (appreciates not having world government) — Yarvin's zero-incitement irony as the trap of domestication ("Only irony remains").
- **Blockchain as cold anarchic sovereignty (§24-26)**: collapsing pseudo-transcendence onto immanence = removing "the trusted third-party"; "cold anarchic sovereignty rests not in a monarch, but in distributed hash-power plutocracy"; disintegrated government is what modernity has actually been doing — "the blockchain phase was reached in the new millennium. It will certainly not stop there."

## Key Concepts
- **Inter-nodal framing**: politics viewed as external relations among sovereign nodes, not internal constitution of a polity.
- **Splinternet**: the Internet's own disintegration — "on the Splinternet no one knows you're a bot."
- **Trusted third-party = pseudo-transcendent element**: the subtractable super-ordinate term in any multiplicity.

## Mental Models
- Use *subtract-the-super-ordinate* as the general decentralization move: routing around pseudo-transcendent elements produces flat peer-to-peer systems ("entity becomes network").
- Use *kidney-complaint heuristic* on institutions: inward obsession = morbidity; outward orientation = health.
- Use *millions-of-nationalities* horizon when evaluating exit tech: does it lower the cost of new sovereign units?

## Anti-patterns
- **Hot anarchy**: revolutionary enthusiasm that sanctions anything — the antipodes of realism.
- **Global One-ness**: consummate unity suspends spontaneous order ("Absolute domestication has eliminated all surprises").
- **Domestic framing**: starting political analysis from one's own regime instead of the ecology of governments.

## Worked Example
§24 applied to blockchain engineering: in any multiplicity, identify the pseudo-transcendent element (the trusted third party — exchange, registrar, oracle) and collapse it onto real immanence (protocol, cryptography, hash-power). The work of critique and the work of the engineer are the same act on Land's reading — which is why "the blockchain phase" is cold anarchy's natural continuation.

## Key Takeaways
1. Anarchy sets the horizon; it isn't on it — the international system IS anarchy, permanently.
2. Disintegration is intensification: more units = more capability; unity = paralysis.
3. Decentralization = subtracting pseudo-transcendent (trusted third-party) elements.
4. Outward-facing entities are healthy; inward-facing ones are dying.
5. Blockchain = disintegrated government's current phase, sovereignty reconstituted as hash-power.
