## Description:

Turns a video brief into a timed YouTube script with a hook, beats, a title and a description inside the real limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

## Use Case:

US YouTube channel owners and writers or editors on small marketing teams use the skill to turn a video brief into a timed script, title options, a description, and production notes that flag sourcing, disclosure, and licensing needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The helper overwrites calculation.md in its output directory.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated output folder and retain any files that must not be replaced.

Risk: The helper does not calculate a word budget or validate platform limits, so generated drafts may miss the intended length or limits.

Mitigation: Manually check word counts, title and description lengths, and chapter requirements before publishing.

Risk: The optional Windows app is a separate installation and its checksum alone does not establish safety.

Mitigation: Review that download independently before installing or running it; it is not included in this skill bundle.
