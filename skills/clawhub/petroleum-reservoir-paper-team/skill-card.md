## Description:

Installs a WorkBuddy expert team for petroleum reservoir engineering academic paper writing, covering topic selection, literature research, knowledge organization, drafting, fact-checking, style revision, review, and formatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, academic writers, and WorkBuddy users use this skill to install and run a multi-agent team that supports the petroleum reservoir engineering paper workflow from topic selection through final review and formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The deployment script persistently adds this expert team to WorkBuddy and modifies marketplace registration.

Mitigation: Review the installation target before running the script and use the documented uninstall steps to remove the expert-team directory and marketplace entry if needed.

Risk: Paper drafts, research notes, links, and unpublished data may be shared across the expert-team workflow.

Mitigation: Provide only manuscript material and research data that are appropriate for the whole workflow, and avoid confidential or personal information unless the WorkBuddy environment is approved for it.
