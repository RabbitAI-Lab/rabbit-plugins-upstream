## Description:

Guides QA teams through bug severity and priority classification, lifecycle tracking, and defect trend analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA teams, developers, and project leads use this skill to standardize bug reports, manage defects from discovery to closure, and review quality trends.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bug reports or examples may expose customer information, identifiers, payment details, logs, or screenshots.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive production information before providing it to the skill.

Risk: Broad activation wording may trigger this skill outside the intended QA workflow.

Mitigation: Check that the request calls for bug lifecycle management before applying its guidance.
