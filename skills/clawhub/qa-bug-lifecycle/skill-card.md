## Description:

This skill helps QA teams manage the full defect lifecycle, including bug triage, severity and priority classification, state transitions, timelines, closure criteria, and defect trend analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, developers, and project leads use this skill to standardize defect handling from submission through closure. It supports consistent severity and priority decisions, lifecycle tracking, escalation, closure review, and defect trend analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bug reports and examples may contain customer, payment, identity, financial, or production incident data.

Mitigation: Redact or mask sensitive data before using the skill, especially customer identifiers, payment details, screenshots, logs, and production incident records.

Risk: Incomplete defect context can lead to incorrect severity, priority, escalation, or closure recommendations.

Mitigation: Review lifecycle decisions with the responsible QA, development, product, or release owner before taking operational action.
