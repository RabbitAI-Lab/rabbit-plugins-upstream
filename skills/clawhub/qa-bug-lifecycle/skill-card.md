## Description:

Helps QA teams standardize bug lifecycle management, including severity and priority classification, state transitions, time expectations, defect metrics, and trend analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, developers, and project leads use this skill to manage reported defects from submission through closure and to analyze defect trends for quality improvement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bug reports may contain sensitive business, customer, payment, identity, screenshot, or production-log data.

Mitigation: Redact or mask real order numbers, payment amounts, screenshots, IDs, phone numbers, customer details, and production logs before using the skill.

Risk: Lifecycle, severity, priority, and closure guidance may influence QA and release decisions.

Mitigation: Have the responsible QA or release owner review classifications, state changes, and closure criteria before acting on them.
