## Description:

Guides bounded research across Crawlora public-web, paper, and indexed developer-document sources, with cited evidence and partial-coverage warnings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and developers use this skill to prepare focused, cited briefs or structured research results across public web pages, papers, and indexed documentation, or to map and crawl a bounded site.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries, selected URLs or domains, and API-key-authenticated requests are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only if that data sharing is acceptable under your Crawlora account policies; avoid private or sensitive content otherwise.

Risk: Research and crawling can consume account credits.

Mitigation: Set conservative credit and page limits before starting jobs.

Risk: Search, paper, documentation, and crawl coverage can be incomplete or partial.

Mitigation: Check source availability and preserve partial-coverage warnings; do not present missing evidence as verified or coverage as exhaustive.
