## Description: <br>
Ele helps agents guide Ele.me takeout users and merchants on discount decisions, fulfillment and after-sales rules, and merchant onboarding or operations basics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Consumers and merchants use this skill to compare order discounts, assess membership value, navigate late-delivery or damaged-order support, and understand merchant onboarding, commission, ranking, and platform-integration basics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Coupon values, membership prices, compensation rules, and merchant fees may be city-specific or change after release. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current values and rules in the Ele.me app or official merchant channels before acting on the guidance. <br>
