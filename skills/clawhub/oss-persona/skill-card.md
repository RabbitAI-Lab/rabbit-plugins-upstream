## Description:

Helps draft open-source maintainer responses using documented communication patterns while avoiding invented personal identities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qikairo7](https://clawhub.ai/user/qikairo7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Open-source maintainers and contributors can draft Issue and pull-request replies, status updates, closure explanations, and contributor invitations in an appropriate maintainer style. Reviewers must verify factual claims and authorize any public posting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style-based drafts may be mistaken for a real maintainer's authorized words.

Mitigation: Obtain the maintainer's approval before posting and do not present drafts as their own words without consent.

Risk: Broad persona triggers or reused examples may introduce unsupported claims or fabricated project references.

Mitigation: Confirm the target project and role, verify every status claim and reference, and omit details that cannot be checked.
