## Use Case:

External users can use this companion for English reading, learning, exam preparation, study-related writing, research, and explicitly requested Obsidian knowledge management. Browsing, persistence, exports, and reminders remain opt-in.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requested note changes could affect unintended vault files.

Mitigation: Specify the vault folder and exact files, and review the proposed targets before changes are made.

Risk: Generated summaries or study materials could contain inaccuracies or omissions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and review them against the supplied source before relying on or saving them.

Risk: A reminder could be created with unintended scheduling details.

Mitigation: Confirm its time, time zone, recurrence, content, and end condition before scheduling.
