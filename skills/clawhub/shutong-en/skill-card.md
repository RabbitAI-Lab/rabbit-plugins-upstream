## Description: <br>
AI Knowledge Companion helps English-speaking users maintain an Obsidian knowledge vault, organize reading notes, explain concepts, practice recall, plan exams and reading, draft documents, research sources, and manage spaced repetition. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yamyeed](https://clawhub.ai/user/yamyeed) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Readers, students, writers, and knowledge workers use this skill to organize an Obsidian vault, turn source material into notes, build study plans, practice recall, prepare for exams or interviews, and draft or polish documents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can propose or perform local Obsidian vault changes, including note creation, deletion, broad exports, and reminder setup. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended vault path before use and review destructive note changes, broad exports, and reminders before approving them. <br>
Risk: Generated summaries, study plans, reference traces, and exam material may be incomplete or need verification. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated learning materials as drafts, review them before relying on them, and prefer authoritative sources for cited references. <br>
