## Description:

Before a phase ends, compare its goals, current implementation, and actual verification evidence to find gaps and add remaining work to the existing plan, task, or execution unit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[peterflow-zh](https://clawhub.ai/user/peterflow-zh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill at phase closeout to compare goals, implementation, and current evidence, classify remaining work, and update the existing plan or task with actionable follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or incomplete verification evidence can lead to an inaccurate completion status or follow-up plan.

Mitigation: Check evidence against the relevant code, tests, configuration, and environment; mark changed or unverifiable evidence as pending revalidation.
