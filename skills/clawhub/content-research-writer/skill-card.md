## Description:

Assists with writing high-quality content by conducting research, adding citations, improving hooks, iterating on outlines, providing section-level feedback, and preserving the writer's voice and style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, content marketers, educators, and developers use this skill to research, outline, draft, cite, and refine articles, newsletters, case studies, tutorials, and technical documentation while preserving the writer's voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research summaries, quotes, statistics, or citations may be inaccurate or outdated if not checked against original sources.

Mitigation: Independently verify cited sources, quotes, and statistics before publishing.

Risk: Drafts and source materials may contain sensitive unpublished information.

Mitigation: Keep private drafts and source files out of shared workspaces unless you intend the agent to review them.
