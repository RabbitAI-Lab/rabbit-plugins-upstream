## Description: <br>
Assesses physical and transition climate risk for a site, product, or portfolio using scenario-based hazard, exposure, and vulnerability analysis across 2030, 2040, and 2050 horizons. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external advisors, and analysts use this skill to structure climate risk assessments for assets, products, or portfolios. It supports decision-making and climate disclosure preparation by separating physical and transition risks, impact ranges, assumptions, and monitoring triggers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may involve sensitive business, financial planning, site, or supply chain details. <br>
Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the agent environment and avoid unnecessary confidential details. <br>
Risk: Decision-support output could be mistaken for compliance-grade, engineering-grade, or actuarial analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Verify disclosure use and reportable figures with compliance teams, and use specialist engineering or actuarial studies before capital decisions. <br>
