## Description:

Work out how often a car needs washing from where it is parked and how it is driven, pick the right level of wash (basic, wash and wax, deep interior, full detail) from the car's condition, and build a dated wash calendar with a cost estimate from the user's own local prices. Use when someone asks how often to wash their car, which wash or detailing service they need, or what a few months of washing will cost.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External car owners can use this skill to choose a wash frequency and service level based on their vehicle and its condition, then plan visits and estimate costs using prices they provide.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A schedule or service recommendation may not fit a vehicle's specific condition.

Mitigation: Treat intervals and service levels as rules of thumb; consult a professional when a vehicle needs an assessment.

Risk: An estimate could be mistaken for a local quote if prices are not supplied.

Mitigation: Use prices provided by the user; when prices are unavailable, give visit counts by service level instead of inventing costs.
