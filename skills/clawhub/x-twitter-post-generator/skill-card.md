## Description:

AI X Twitter Post Generator generates editable, X-ready posts and slides with AI-created content, captions, images, text blocks, storylines, and backgrounds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to call a hosted content-generation agent that creates editable social media cards, slides, captions, image layers, and exportable assets for X/Twitter and related channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, uploaded reference images, and generated content are sent to documented external services.

Mitigation: Use only content that is appropriate to share with those services and avoid submitting sensitive, private, or regulated data.

Risk: Share URLs and hosted image URLs may be accessible outside the local machine.

Mitigation: Treat generated links as externally reachable and review sharing permissions before distributing or storing them.

Risk: The artifact describes export and publishing workflows, but security evidence says direct X/Twitter publishing should not be assumed safe without separate permissions.

Mitigation: Review generated assets and use explicit, authorized publishing steps before posting to X/Twitter or another social channel.
