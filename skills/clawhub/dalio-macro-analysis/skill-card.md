## Description:

This skill applies Ray Dalio's macro analysis framework to analyze global economic conditions, investment allocation, and geopolitical risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to structure Chinese-language macroeconomic analysis, cycle positioning, asset-allocation discussion, and geopolitical risk review through a Dalio-style five-forces framework.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Macro and allocation outputs may be mistaken for personalized financial advice or may rely on stale or unverified market data.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical framing only, verify current market data from authoritative sources, and consult qualified professionals before investment decisions.

Risk: The skill can steer analysis toward Dalio-style allocation views and framework assumptions.

Mitigation: Compare outputs against alternative macro frameworks, document assumptions, and use the skill's disconfirming-signal checks before acting on conclusions.
