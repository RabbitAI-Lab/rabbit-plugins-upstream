## Description:

This skill applies Ray Dalio's macro analysis framework to analyze global economic conditions, investment allocation, and geopolitical risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and users seeking a structured macroeconomic discussion use the skill to organize economic-cycle, debt, political, geopolitical, and technology factors and draft scenario and asset-allocation analyses for qualified review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation keywords may cause the skill to run on requests outside its intended macro-analysis scope.

Mitigation: Check that the skill is relevant to the request and review its output before relying on it.

Risk: Several referenced items were unavailable during scanning, so reference checks were incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the referenced materials where available and verify claims against current sources before relying on the analysis.

Risk: Allocation examples and market views could be mistaken for current or personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as draft framework analysis, verify current data, and have a qualified professional review them before making decisions.
