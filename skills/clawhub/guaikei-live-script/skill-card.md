This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

内容创作者、运营人员和团队可用此技能转写本地视频或公开视频链接，并将转写内容整理为口播稿、摘要、会议纪要、改写文案或其他提示词指定的文本。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video content is sent to GUAIKEI and its selected storage host, so privacy and retention claims should be confirmed before sharing confidential recordings.

Mitigation: Only submit videos approved for those services, and confirm current retention terms and fees before processing sensitive content.

Risk: Reusing a download cache for URLs with identical filenames can cause a previously downloaded video to be uploaded again.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing cache entries across URLs with the same filename, and keep original files outside the skill's temporary directory.
