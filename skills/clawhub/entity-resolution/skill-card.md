## Description:

Match provider, facility, organization or device entities across sources and resolve field conflicts using identifiers, provenance and time. Use for identity and relationship questions, including uncertain matches; a source name alone is not a matching key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redrookai](https://clawhub.ai/user/redrookai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Data stewards, analysts, and developers use this skill to determine whether records refer to the same, related, different, or unresolved entities. It supports evidence-based crosswalks and field conflict resolution while preserving source, time, and uncertainty for operational decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ambiguous or incorrect entity matches could be applied to operational records.

Mitigation: Use datasets you are authorized to process, and review ambiguous matches against the available evidence before applying results.
