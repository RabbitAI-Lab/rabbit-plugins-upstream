# Worked example: same name, different grain

Synthetic data. Two records named Taylor Lee share a practice address. Their verified individual IDs, issued by the same registry, are P20 and P21. Match outcome: different individuals. Address and name do not override the distinct identity evidence.

A third record is Taylor Lee Medical LLC, organization ID O20. A dated corporate source says P20 owns O20. Record a relationship, not a duplicate individual.

P20 also has an older North address and a current dated South address. Match the person by the supported identity evidence and retain the address history. Which address is relevant depends on the requested time. None of this establishes current licensure.

If the IDs are missing and only “T. Lee” remains, the right result may be unresolved. The next useful evidence is a registry record, dated roster or other source that can distinguish the people.
