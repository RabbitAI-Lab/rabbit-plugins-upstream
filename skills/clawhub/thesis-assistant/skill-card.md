## Description: <br>
Assists with thesis chapter drafting, polishing, literature-logic organization, and experiment-description refinement from user-provided references and research notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External students and academic writers use this skill to organize thesis arguments, convert notes and experiment details into structured academic prose, and draft or refine thesis sections in Chinese. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated thesis prose may contain inaccurate claims, citation gaps, or details that do not match the student's actual experiments. <br>
Mitigation: Verify every factual claim, result, method detail, and citation against source materials and experiment records before use. <br>
Risk: Use of AI-generated thesis content may conflict with institutional academic-integrity or AI-use policies. <br>
Mitigation: Check school and advisor rules, disclose or limit AI assistance as required, and keep final authorship decisions with the student. <br>
Risk: Reconstructed advisor intent from fragmentary notes may be only one possible interpretation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat reconstructed advisor logic as hypotheses and confirm important points with the advisor or additional notes before relying on them. <br>
