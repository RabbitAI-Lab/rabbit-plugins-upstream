## Description: <br>
Turn interview notes into a structured candidate scorecard with per-competency evidence, ratings, open questions, and a hire recommendation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Hiring teams and interviewers use this skill to turn role-specific interview notes into a consistent, evidence-based scorecard for debriefs and hiring recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Candidate notes may contain sensitive personal information or irrelevant hiring signals. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only relevant job-related evidence and review the scorecard for fairness, accuracy, and compliance with hiring policies. <br>
Risk: A scorecard can overstate confidence when interview evidence is thin or a competency was not assessed. <br>
Mitigation: Tie every rating to specific interview evidence, mark unassessed competencies as not assessed, and carry open questions into the next round. <br>
