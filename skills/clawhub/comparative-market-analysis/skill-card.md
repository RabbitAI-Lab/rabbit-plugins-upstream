## Description: <br>
Build a comparative market analysis (CMA) to price a property from provided comparables, market context, and seller goals, producing a structured value range and pricing recommendation for real-estate professional review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Real-estate professionals use this skill to structure a comparative market analysis for a property, compare supplied comps with adjustments, summarize market context, and produce a defensible value range and pricing recommendation. It is a pricing-analysis aid, not a formal appraisal or legal, financial, or valuation advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Property pricing outputs can be misleading if comparables, market context, or property details are incomplete, stale, or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Use verified MLS or public-record data where available, mark missing figures to source, and have a licensed real-estate professional review the CMA. <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the CMA for a formal appraisal or financial or legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as pricing-analysis support only, include caveats, and obtain a formal appraisal or professional review when required. <br>
