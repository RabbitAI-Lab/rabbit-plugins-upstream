## Description: <br>
Helps agents explain the philosophy, assumptions, and boundaries of technical analysis, especially when users ask whether chart-based analysis is useful, how it differs from fundamental analysis, or when it can fail. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent builders use this skill to frame technical analysis as an empirical worldview based on market action, trends, and recurring behavior. It is used for educational explanations and boundary-setting, not for specific buy or sell signals, pure fundamental research, or standalone trading decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat educational technical-analysis framing as financial advice or as a standalone trading system. <br>
Mitigation: Present the material as educational context, preserve the skill's stated boundaries, and remind users not to rely on it alone for real trading decisions. <br>
