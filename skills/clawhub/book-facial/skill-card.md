## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for facial services through Lokuli, check appointment availability, and submit booking details when ready. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can send customer contact details to an external booking service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm which contact details will be sent and that the user accepts using Lokuli before sending booking data. <br>
Risk: The skill can create real appointments without clear confirmation safeguards. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, appointment time, price or cancellation terms if available, and customer contact details. <br>
