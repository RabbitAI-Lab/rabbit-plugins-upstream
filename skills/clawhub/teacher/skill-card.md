## Description: <br>
Plans lessons, units and syllabi, designs assessments and rubrics, marks and gives feedback at scale, and supports classroom management across K-12, higher education, bootcamps, and adult training. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Teachers, instructors, trainers, and education staff use this skill to plan instruction, build assessments and rubrics, draft feedback and guardian communications, handle classroom participation and behavior issues, and manage teaching workload. It is intended for people teaching others rather than for learners seeking tutoring or study planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can store and reuse sensitive education and contact records in local shared files. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed writes, avoid storing identifiable student or guardian data unless institutional policy allows it, and keep regulated records in approved school systems. <br>
Risk: Local teacher memory under ~/Clawic/data/teacher and shared contacts or projects can persist information beyond a single session. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when this memory behavior is desired, review the data folder before use, and back up or version it before relying on the memory features. <br>
