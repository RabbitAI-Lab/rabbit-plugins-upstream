## Description: <br>
Plans lessons, units and syllabi, designs assessments and rubrics, marks and gives feedback at scale, and runs a room that behaves. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Teachers, instructors, professors, bootcamp leads, and workplace trainers use this skill to plan instruction, design assessments and rubrics, diagnose learning breakdowns, manage classroom participation and behavior, communicate with guardians or stakeholders, and reduce marking workload. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can store and reorganize sensitive student, guardian, and teaching records in local notes. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with an approved local storage location and a restricted machine/account, and review announced writes and deletions during use. <br>
Risk: Education records may include regulated or highly sensitive information if users paste more detail than the skill needs. <br>
Mitigation: Keep safeguarding disclosures, diagnoses, counselling content, credentials, and minor contact details out of the local notes; record only operational adjustments and pointers where needed. <br>
