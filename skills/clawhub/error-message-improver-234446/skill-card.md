## Description: <br>
Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague errors into messages that explain what failed, why it failed, and what to do next. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users use this skill to draft clearer error messages, troubleshooting workflows, checklists, and implementation guidance for faster debugging and support resolution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The activation wording is broader than necessary and may cause the skill to influence general troubleshooting or support-writing tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrow trigger phrases when the skill should apply only to error-message improvement. <br>
Risk: Generated error-message guidance may be incomplete or misleading if the agent lacks product logs, user impact, or known remediation steps. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed messages against source logs, product behavior, and support policy before publishing them to users. <br>
