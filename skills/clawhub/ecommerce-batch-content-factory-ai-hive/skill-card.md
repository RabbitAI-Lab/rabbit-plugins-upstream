## Description:

Helps e-commerce teams turn multi-SKU content needs into reviewable production plans, template variables, image and video batches, AI-HIVE commands, task tracking, and quality checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External e-commerce, agency, and enterprise content teams use this skill to plan and run batch product image/video workflows for multiple SKUs, with cost, speed, success routing, sample-first production, and human review checkpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Billable image or video generation may be submitted with the wrong prompt, route, or batch size.

Mitigation: Review final parameters, routing, budget posture, and a small sample before expanding batch execution.

Risk: API keys and generated task artifacts are sensitive.

Mitigation: Use environment variables where possible, avoid sharing logs or screenshots that contain credentials, and only use local key initialization when local storage is acceptable.

Risk: Uploaded media may include content the user is not authorized to share with AI-HIVE.

Mitigation: Confirm rights to product images, videos, logos, and reference materials before upload or generation.
