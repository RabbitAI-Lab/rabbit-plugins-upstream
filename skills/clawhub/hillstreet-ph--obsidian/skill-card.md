Developers and Obsidian users use this skill to read, search, create, edit, and organize notes and other vault content with the official Obsidian CLI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vault move or delete operations can change note locations or remove notes, and screenshot capture can save on-screen application content.

Mitigation: Review the target vault and operation before running commands; confirm move or delete actions and capture screenshots only when needed.
