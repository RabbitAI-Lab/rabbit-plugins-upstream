## Description:

Helps independent teachers in China draft specific, low-anxiety messages to parents about student learning, while leaving review and sending to the teacher.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to draft private learning updates, responses to parent concerns, renewal messages, and general group announcements. The teacher checks and sends each message; the skill may store a limited communication log.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); use elsewhere requires localization of laws, emergency resources, and terminology.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information or emotional observations could be shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm workspace parent-communication consent before drafting, check separate consent before including emotional observations, use student aliases, and keep individual feedback out of group announcements.

Risk: Communication logs may retain sensitive student or family information.

Mitigation: Store only limited communication details when permitted; omit contact information, verbatim parent replies, and family circumstances, and honor requests not to record a conversation.

Risk: China-mainland education rules and crisis resources may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Use only in an appropriate Chinese-language independent-teacher setting unless local laws, terminology, and emergency resources have been adapted; route crisis signals to the applicable referral protocol.
