## Description:

帮助独立教师根据学习记录起草具体、低焦虑且可操作的中文家长沟通消息，由教师审核并自行发送。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

独立教师使用该技能起草课后反馈、家长答复、续课说明和通用家长群公告；教师审核后自行发送，技能仅记录沟通状态。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt the language, school-stage assumptions, and crisis referral channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts based on student records may disclose sensitive learning or emotional information.

Mitigation: Check parent-communication and emotion-sharing consent, minimize identifying details, and have the teacher review each draft before sending.

Risk: Chinese K–12 assumptions and crisis referral channels may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize the language, school-stage assumptions, and crisis referral channels before use outside mainland China.
