## Description:

Helps independent teachers draft clear, low-pressure parent communications about student progress and next steps without sending messages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this skill to draft individual parent updates, replies to concerns, renewal discussions, and general group announcements in Simplified Chinese. Teachers review and send the drafts themselves; the skill can record limited communication-log entries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (workflow and guidance orientation)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records and communication summaries may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Check parent-communication consent, minimize personal details, and review privacy and retention controls before use.

Risk: Sharing an individual student's progress in a group could expose private information.

Mitigation: Keep individual feedback in private one-to-one messages; use only anonymized general information in group announcements.

Risk: Drafts involving a student's emotional state or safety may be misunderstood as clinical guidance.

Mitigation: Check permission before sharing emotional observations and refer safety concerns to trusted adults and appropriate local emergency support.
