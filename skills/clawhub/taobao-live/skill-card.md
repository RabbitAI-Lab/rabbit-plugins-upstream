## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated sales copy may include absolute or unverifiable claims, such as lowest-price promises or product-effect statements. <br>
Mitigation: Review all scripts before public use, remove absolute claims, and verify product evidence and current Taobao Live rules. <br>
Risk: Platform operating guidance may become outdated as Taobao Live rules, traffic mechanics, or advertising tools change. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm current platform policies and campaign requirements before applying the guidance to a live broadcast. <br>
