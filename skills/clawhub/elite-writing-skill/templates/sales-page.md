# Sales Page Template

## Pre-Writing Checklist
- [ ] Audience pain points identified
- [ ] Framework selected (AIDA / PAS / 4 P's)
- [ ] Social proof collected
- [ ] Offer details finalized

---

## Structure

### HEADLINE
[Big Promise - specific, bold, benefit-driven]

### SUBHEADLINE
[Qualify the reader + add credibility]

### OPENING (Hook + Agitation)
[Relatable pain point scenario]
[Amplify the consequences]
[Introduce the solution]

### ABOUT THE PRODUCT (3-5 paragraphs)
[What it is]
[What it does]
[Who it's for]
[What makes it different]

### FEATURES & BENEFITS
[Feature 1] -> [Benefit] -> [Emotional outcome]
[Feature 2] -> [Benefit] -> [Emotional outcome]
[Feature 3] -> [Benefit] -> [Emotional outcome]

### SOCIAL PROOF
[Testimonial 1 - with specific result]
[Testimonial 2 - with specific result]
[Testimonial 3 - with specific result]

### THE OFFER
[Everything included]
[Value stack with dollar amounts]
[Total value vs. price]
[Bonuses]

### GUARANTEE
[Risk reversal - specific terms]
[Why you're confident]

### FAQ (5-7 questions)
[Address top objections as questions]

### FINAL CTA
[Restate big promise]
[Remove final objections]
[Strong CTA with urgency]
[P.S. with bonus or reminder]

---

## Post-Writing Checklist
- [ ] Persuasion stack complete
- [ ] All objections addressed
- [ ] CTA is singular and clear
- [ ] Urgency is real (not fake)
- [ ] Proofread 3x
