# Blog Post Template

## Pre-Writing Checklist
- [ ] Audience profile defined
- [ ] Keyword researched
- [ ] Framework selected (Inverted Pyramid / AIDA / etc.)
- [ ] Hook written (5 variations)

---

## Structure

### HEADLINE
[Number] [Adjective] [Ways/Tips/Secrets] to [Achieve Outcome] [Timeframe/Year]

### INTRO (150-200 words)
[HOOK - Contrarian statement, surprising fact, or relatable pain point]
[CONTEXT - Why this matters NOW]
[THESIS - What the reader will learn]
[TRANSITION - "Here's exactly how..."]

### BODY SECTION 1 (300-400 words)
[H2: Main Point]
[Sub-point with evidence]
[Example or case study]
[Key takeaway in bold]

### BODY SECTION 2 (300-400 words)
[H2: Main Point]
[Sub-point with evidence]
[Example or case study]
[Key takeaway in bold]

### BODY SECTION 3 (300-400 words)
[H2: Main Point]
[Sub-point with evidence]
[Example or case study]
[Key takeaway in bold]

### CONCLUSION (150-200 words)
[Summary of key points]
[Final insight or "aha" moment]
[Clear CTA - what should they do next?]
