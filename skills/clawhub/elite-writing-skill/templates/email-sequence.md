# Email Sequence Template

## Email 1: Welcome / Introduction
Subject: [Personal, not salesy]

[Opening - personal connection]
[Value delivery - immediate useful content]
[Set expectations - what they'll receive]
[Soft CTA - reply or click]

---

## Email 2: Problem Agitation
Subject: [Pain point focused]

[Relate to their struggle]
[Amplify the pain]
[Hint at solution (don't reveal yet)]
[CTA - stay tuned / read related content]

---

## Email 3: Solution Introduction
Subject: [Benefit focused]

[Present the solution]
[How it specifically solves the pain]
[Social proof / case study]
[CTA - learn more / watch demo]

---

## Email 4: Objection Handling
Subject: [Address common objection]

[Acknowledge the objection]
[Counter with evidence]
[More social proof]
[CTA - overcome the objection]

---

## Email 5: Urgency / Close
Subject: [Deadline / scarcity focused]

[Restate the offer]
[Create genuine urgency]
[Remove final barriers]
[Strong CTA - buy / sign up / act now]
[P.S. - Final reminder]
