## HEADLINE FORMULAS

### Formula 1: The How-To Headline
```
How to [Achieve Desired Outcome] Without [Objection/Pain Point]

Examples:
- "How to Write Copy That Converts Without Spending Years Learning"
- "How to Double Your Revenue Without Increasing Ad Spend"
- "How to Get Fit Without Giving Up Your Favorite Foods"
```

### Formula 2: The List Headline
```
[Number] [Adjective] Ways to [Achieve Outcome]

Examples:
- "7 Proven Headline Formulas That Double Click-Through Rates"
- "17 Copywriting Mistakes That Are Costing You Sales"
- "3 Simple Tweaks That Increased Our Conversions by 340%"
```

### Formula 3: The Secret Headline
```
The [Adjective] Secret to [Desired Outcome]

Examples:
- "The Copywriter's Secret to Writing Headlines That Sell"
- "The Little-Known Secret Behind Every 7-Figure Launch"
- "The Brutal Truth About Why Your Copy Isn't Converting"
```

### Formula 4: The Question Headline
```
Are You [Pain Point]? [Promise/Outcome]

Examples:
- "Are You Making These 5 Copywriting Mistakes? (Here's How to Fix Them)"
- "Tired of Writing Content That Gets Ignored? Try This Instead"
- "Struggling to Convert Visitors? The Problem Isn't Your Product"
```

### Formula 5: The If-Then Headline
```
If You [Condition], Then You [Promise]

Examples:
- "If You Can Write a Sentence, Then You Can Write Copy That Sells"
- "If You're Reading This, Then You're Already Behind Your Competitors"
- "If You Ignore This, Your Competitors Won't"
```

### Formula 6: The Comparison Headline
```
[Common Approach] vs. [Better Approach]: Which [Outcome] Better?

Examples:
- "Generic Copy vs. Framework-Driven Copy: Which Converts 3x Better?"
- "Hiring a Copywriter vs. Using This Skill: The Real Cost Breakdown"
```

### Formula 7: The Time-Specific Headline
```
[Action] in [Timeframe]: The [Adjective] Guide

Examples:
- "Write High-Converting Copy in 30 Minutes: The Step-by-Step Guide"
- "Launch Your Product in 7 Days: The No-BS Blueprint"
```

### Formula 8: The Warning Headline
```
Warning: [Consequence] If You [Action/Inaction]

Examples:
- "Warning: Your Landing Page Is Costing You $1,000/Day"
- "Warning: 89% of Startups Fail Because of This Copywriting Mistake"
```

### Formula 9: The "They Laughed" Headline
```
They [Negative Reaction] When I [Unconventional Action], But Then [Outcome]

Examples:
- "They Laughed When I Said I'd Write My Own Sales Page. Then It Generated $50K."
```

### Formula 10: The Direct Benefit Headline
```
[Number/Adjective] [Noun] That [Benefit]

Examples:
- "One Simple Tweak That Tripled Our Email Open Rates"
- "The 3-Word Phrase That Makes Any Offer Irresistible"
```

## CTA (CALL-TO-ACTION) FORMULAS

### Formula 1: The Direct CTA
```
[Action Verb] [Object] [Time Modifier]

Examples:
- "Get Your Free Audit Now"
- "Download the Template Today"
- "Start Your Trial — No Credit Card Required"
```

### Formula 2: The Benefit-Driven CTA
```
[Action] and [Benefit]

Examples:
- "Sign Up and Start Converting Visitors Today"
- "Download Now and Write Copy That Sells"
- "Get Instant Access and 10x Your Output"
```

### Formula 3: The Risk-Reversal CTA
```
[Action] — [Risk Removal]

Examples:
- "Try It Free for 14 Days — Cancel Anytime"
- "Join Now — 100% Money-Back Guarantee"
- "Get Started — If You Don't Love It, We'll Refund Every Penny"
```

### Formula 4: The Curiosity CTA
```
[Action] to [Discover/Learn/Unlock] [Benefit]

Examples:
- "Click to Discover the Framework Top Copywriters Use"
- "Download to Unlock the 7 Headline Formulas"
```

### Formula 5: The Urgency CTA
```
[Action] Before [Deadline/Consequence]

Examples:
- "Claim Your Spot Before Prices Increase Tomorrow"
- "Download Before This Goes Behind a Paywall"
```

## EMAIL SUBJECT LINE FORMULAS

### Formula 1: The Curiosity Gap
```
[Teaser] + [Open Loop]

Examples:
- "I was wrong about copywriting..."
- "The $10,000 mistake (and how to avoid it)"
- "This changed everything for me"
```

### Formula 2: The Direct Benefit
```
[Number] [Ways/Tips/Secrets] to [Outcome]

Examples:
- "5 emails that generated $2M in sales"
- "The 3-word subject line that gets 50% open rates"
```

### Formula 3: The Personal Story
```
[Personal Event] + [Lesson/Outcome]

Examples:
- "I fired my copywriter yesterday"
- "What I learned from writing 1,000 headlines"
```

### Formula 4: The Question
```
[Provocative Question]?

Examples:
- "Is your copy actually hurting sales?"
- "Why do some emails get 10x more opens?"
```

### Formula 5: The FOMO
```
[Scarcity] + [Opportunity]

Examples:
- "Last chance: The copywriting masterclass closes tonight"
- "Only 3 spots left at this price"
```

## SALES PAGE SECTION FORMULAS

### The Opening Section
```
[Big Promise Headline]
[Subheadline that qualifies the reader]
[Opening paragraph that agitates the pain]
[Transition to solution]
```

### The Problem Section
```
"You know that feeling when..."
[Describe the pain vividly]
"And the worst part is..."
[Amplify the consequences]
"What if I told you there's a better way?"
```

### The Solution Section
```
"Introducing [Product Name]"
[What it is — 1 sentence]
[What it does — 3 bullet points]
[Who it's for — 1 sentence]
[What makes it different — 1 paragraph]
```

### The Proof Section
```
"Don't just take my word for it..."
[3-5 testimonials with specific results]
[Before/after comparison]
[Data/statistics if available]
```

### The Offer Section
```
"Here's everything you get:"
[Detailed breakdown of deliverables]
[Value stack — assign dollar values]
[Total value vs. price]
[Bonus items]
```

### The Guarantee Section
```
"Here's my promise to you:"
[Specific guarantee terms]
[Risk reversal — "If you don't love it, I'll refund every penny"]
[Why you're offering this — confidence in product]
```

### The FAQ Section
```
[Address top 5-7 objections as questions]
Format: "What if [objection]?" → "[Confident answer]"
```

### The Final CTA Section
```
[Restate the big promise]
[Remove final objections]
[Strong CTA with urgency]
[P.S. with bonus or reminder]
```

## POWER WORDS & PHRASES

### Urgency Words
- Now, Today, Immediately, Instantly, Limited, Exclusive, Last chance, Final call, Before it's gone, Don't miss out

### Trust Words
- Proven, Guaranteed, Certified, Verified, Endorsed, Recommended, Trusted by, As seen on, Backed by science, Clinically tested

### Exclusivity Words
- Secret, Insider, Members-only, Exclusive access, Behind the scenes, First to know, Limited edition, VIP

### Simplicity Words
- Easy, Simple, Effortless, Step-by-step, Done-for-you, Plug-and-play, No experience needed, Beginner-friendly

### Transformation Words
- Transform, Revolutionize, Unlock, Discover, Reveal, Unleash, Master, Dominate, Skyrocket, Explode

### Emotional Triggers
- Finally, Imagine, Picture this, What if, Remember when, You deserve, Don't settle, Stop struggling, Break free, Take control

## THE SWIPE FILE PRINCIPLE

**Always maintain a mental (or actual) swipe file:**

1. **Save headlines** that made YOU click
2. **Save emails** that made YOU open
3. **Save ads** that made YOU stop scrolling
4. **Save sales pages** that made YOU consider buying
5. **Analyze WHY they worked** — what specific technique?

**The best copywriters are the best copy READERS first.**
