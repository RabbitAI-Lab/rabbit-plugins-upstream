## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive and marketing copy may include unsupported claims, false urgency, unverified testimonials, or missing required disclosures.

Mitigation: Review generated claims, urgency, testimonials, citations, and platform disclosure requirements before publishing.

Risk: Writing outputs may contain factual, statistical, citation, or copyright issues if source material is incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Fact-check numbers and claims, verify links and citations, avoid plagiarism, and perform editorial review before external use.
