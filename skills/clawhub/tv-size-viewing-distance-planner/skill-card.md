## Description:

Use when buying a TV or projector and unsure what size fits the room, when a 65" vs 77" debate needs geometry instead of vibes, or when sizing a projector screen and lumens — computes exact viewing angles (SMPTE 30° reference, THX 40° cinematic), the distance at which 4K resolution actually becomes distinguishable from 1080p (1.56× diagonal) so nobody pays an 8K premium from across the room, recommends a size band per distance and content mix, checks whether a current TV is undersized, and prices size upgrades per square inch of image; projector mode does screen-gain × ambient-light lumens math and throw-distance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

TV and projector buyers use this skill to estimate screen size, viewing angle, resolution visibility, placement, and projector brightness or throw distance from room measurements and intended use. It also compares TV upgrades by image area gained per dollar.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Viewing and projector estimates rely on generalized visual acuity and room assumptions, so they may not match a person's eyesight, comfort, or actual lighting conditions.

Mitigation: Treat the results as planning estimates; check projector specifications and room constraints, and assess viewing comfort before purchasing or mounting equipment.
