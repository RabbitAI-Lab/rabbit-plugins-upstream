## Description: <br>
A course-design assistant for enterprise training, vocational skills, and talent-development programs that gathers requirements, researches industry trends, structures curricula, and prepares implementation-ready proposals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Learning and development teams, training consultants, and curriculum designers use this skill to turn a training brief into a structured course design proposal with needs analysis, trend research, module architecture, assessment guidance, and optional Word document output. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reads user-provided templates and materials and may browse/search external sources for industry trends. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if you are comfortable with that behavior and review gathered source material before relying on the generated proposal. <br>
Risk: The Word document generator creates a local file and could overwrite an important file if pointed at the wrong path. <br>
Mitigation: Keep generated output in a workspace folder and avoid using paths that point to important existing files. <br>
