## Use Case:

Homeowners, renters, interior designers, and renovation advisors use this skill to answer bay-window and window-renovation questions with practical layout, curtain, storage, material, safety, and demolition considerations. It is intended for planning guidance and should be checked against the actual home structure, property rules, and local code before work begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation guidance can touch demolition, window changes, guards, waterproofing, electrical placement, and child-safety decisions.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning guidance and verify structural limits, property-management rules, and local building-code requirements with qualified professionals before physical work begins.

Risk: Examples from the reference material may not fit a specific building structure, window opening direction, household safety need, or budget.

Mitigation: Ask for room dimensions, window type, whether the bay window is structural, user budget, and household safety constraints before recommending a detailed plan.
