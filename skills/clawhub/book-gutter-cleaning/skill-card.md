## Description: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for gutter-cleaning providers, check availability, and submit bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can send contact details to an outside booking service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the exact customer name, email, and phone number before any booking request is submitted. <br>
Risk: The skill can create a real booking without clearly requiring final user consent. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user approval of the provider, service, appointment time, and any price or terms shown before creating a booking. <br>
