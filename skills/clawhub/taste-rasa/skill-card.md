## Description:

A formal observation framework derived from Abhinavagupta's rasa theory — how codified formal elements trigger generalized aesthetic states through sadharanikarana.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Art critics, creators, and agents can use this framework to assess how a composition's formal choices evoke generalized aesthetic states and balance multiple emotional registers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The rasa framework brings a specific aesthetic perspective that can shape art critiques.

Mitigation: Present conclusions as one interpretive lens and consider other aesthetic frameworks where useful.
