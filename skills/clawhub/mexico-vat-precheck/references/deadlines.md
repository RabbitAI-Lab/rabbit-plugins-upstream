# deadlines.md (Mexico VAT/IVA)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; verify with SAT or a contador.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- Monthly VAT return: due the 17th of the following month (registered sellers).
- Platforms issue withholding statements (constancias de retención) — collect them monthly; they are required to credit withheld VAT/ISR in the return.

## Usage Notes
- The 17th is a hard deadline; penalties apply from the first day late.
- Nil returns are required once registered.
- If the user is unregistered (withholding-only), the pre-check finding is the RFC gap, not a filing date — mark "cannot determine" for deadlines.
