## Description:

Reviews cross-border sellers’ Mexico VAT (IVA) data and platform statements across six common risk areas, then produces a structured pre-check report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[snowhola](https://clawhub.ai/user/snowhola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Cross-border sellers use this skill to review Mexico VAT (IVA) rates, RFC status, platform withholdings, imports, filing timing, and CFDI records. It provides a preliminary report and next steps; it does not file returns or register sellers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mexico

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The pre-check may be incomplete or rely on tax rules and deadlines that have changed.

Mitigation: Treat findings as preliminary and confirm current rules and deadlines with SAT or a Mexican tax advisor before acting.

Risk: User-provided statements may contain unrelated personal or account details.

Mitigation: Share only necessary information and redact unrelated personal or account details from statements.
