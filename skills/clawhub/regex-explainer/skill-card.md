## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive production samples may be exposed when sharing regex test cases.

Mitigation: Use synthetic examples or remove secrets and sensitive data before sharing samples.

Risk: Regex behavior can differ between language flavors, leading to misleading explanations.

Mitigation: Specify the regex flavor and test important matches in the target environment.
