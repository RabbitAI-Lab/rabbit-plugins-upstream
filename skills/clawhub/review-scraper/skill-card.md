## Description:

Collects, filters, and exports Amazon product reviews across eight marketplaces, with tools for monitoring and review analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and analysts use the skill to collect and filter product reviews, export review data, and understand customer feedback and changes over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Amazon marketplaces: US, UK, Germany, Japan, Canada, France, Spain, and Italy)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid use may spend ARI credits without price approval, and competitor tracking can create recurring charges.

Mitigation: Set the account to ask before every charge and obtain a free quote; request manual collection for one-time work and approve competitor monitoring costs separately.
