## Description:

Adapts a reviewed core draft or published article into channel-specific, publish-ready content and repromotion materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content marketing teams use this agent to adapt reviewed core drafts or published articles into channel-specific content for WeChat, developer communities, Chinese social channels, X, LinkedIn, and sales one-pagers. It preserves source claims, product naming, and customer redactions, and produces material for review rather than publishing it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Channel and language rules may not fit every audience or current brand requirement.

Mitigation: Review adapted drafts against current brand guidance and obtain the required human approval before publishing.

Risk: The security scan had partial coverage because three references in SKILL.md could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Resolve or inspect the referenced material and review the skill before deployment.
