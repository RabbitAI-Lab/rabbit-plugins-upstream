## Description: <br>
blooming-elf（绽放精灵） is a plant-care assistant for watering reminders, plant records, care logs, and troubleshooting guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shirley1011](https://clawhub.ai/user/shirley1011) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Plant owners use this skill to onboard plants, maintain plant profile and care-log documents, receive watering and care reminders, and get concise diagnosis or rescue guidance for common houseplant issues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill keeps persistent plant records, reminder settings, local paths or note IDs, and may use weather or location context. <br>
Mitigation: Ask where records will be stored, keep unrelated private details out of plant notes, and provide only the location context needed for care guidance. <br>
Risk: Document links or stored note identifiers can expose plant records if shared unintentionally. <br>
Mitigation: Request document links only when you intend to open or share that record, and review generated records before distributing them. <br>
