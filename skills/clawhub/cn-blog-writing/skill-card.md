## Description: <br>
写、改、查中文技术博客：按自然中文写作节奏组织文章，检查草稿中的 AI 痕迹，并根据已有博客内容规划后续选题。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leapya](https://clawhub.ai/user/leapya) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Chinese technical bloggers and developers use this skill to draft blog posts, revise drafts for a less AI-like tone, inspect article style, and choose follow-up topics based on existing blog content and SEO gaps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for a blog URL or sample posts and may reuse the resulting style and SEO analysis in later writing tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only public blog URLs or writing samples the user is comfortable having inspected and reused for style guidance. <br>
Risk: Generated SEO or style recommendations may be inaccurate or may not match the user's preferred voice. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed posts, topic plans, and edits before publishing, especially when they describe personal experience or site strategy. <br>
