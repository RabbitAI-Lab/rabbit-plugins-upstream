## Description:

IEEE numeric referencing for engineering and CS, including bracketed citation order, numbered references, and DOI format for papers requiring IEEE style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vhsgreed](https://clawhub.ai/user/vhsgreed)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, engineers, and academic writers use this skill to format in-text citations and reference lists according to IEEE numeric citation conventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Citation-formatting guidance can omit edge cases or conflict with institution or publisher requirements.

Mitigation: Verify final citations against the applicable institution, publisher, or IEEE requirements before submission.

Risk: Generated references can contain numbering, ordering, or uncited-entry mistakes.

Mitigation: Check that bracketed citation numbers are consecutive, ordered by first appearance, and matched to exactly one reference-list entry.
