## Use Case: <br>
External educators, coaches, and student teams use this skill to prepare for vocational skills competitions by generating project directions, PPT outlines, presentation scripts, and practical training plans. It is intended for contest planning and rehearsal support across technical, artistic, and business-oriented events. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The release evidence reports exposed Dify API keys and an external HTTP workflow endpoint that may receive project materials or team-member details. <br>
Mitigation: Rotate or remove exposed keys, require HTTPS, and require explicit user approval before sending contest or team data externally. <br>
Risk: Contest-preparation guidance may be incorrect or misaligned with a specific event's official rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans against current competition rules and imported event documents before using them in training or submission. <br>
