## Description: <br>
Chinese-language government procurement challenge and complaint advisor that helps suppliers search comparable complaint cases, review draft complaints, and create structured complaint-letter templates using configured IMA knowledge bases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External suppliers and potential suppliers use this skill to research similar government procurement complaint cases, check whether draft complaints have procedural or evidentiary gaps, and produce fill-in complaint templates. Outputs are drafting and research support and should be verified before filing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat drafting support or case comparisons as legal advice or as a prediction of complaint outcome. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as research and drafting assistance, verify conclusions independently, and consult a qualified lawyer for legal advice. <br>
Risk: Cited cases, legal provisions, or filing deadlines may be incomplete, stale, or unavailable from the knowledge base at the time of use. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited media_id records, current legal text, and statutory deadlines before filing or relying on the result. <br>
