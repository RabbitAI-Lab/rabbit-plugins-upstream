# Nutrition & Fueling

## Daily
- Carbs 5-7 g/kg (base) → 7-10 g/kg (build / peak)
- Protein 1.4-1.6 g/kg (spread across meals)
- Fat 20-30% of calories; adequate fibre (cut 2 days before race)
- Water: by thirst + pale-yellow urine

## Pre-race carb loading (half / full marathon)
- 2-3 days before: 10-12 g/kg carbs, lower fibre/fat/total, reduce training
- Pre-race meal (3-4h): low-fibre, easily absorbed carbs 1-4 g/kg + small protein; no new foods

## In-run fueling (by duration)
- < 60 min: water is enough
- 60-90 min: carbs 30-60 g/hr (gel / chews)
- > 90 min or hot: carbs 60-90 g/hr + electrolytes (sodium 500-700 mg/hr)
- Caffeine: 3 mg/kg 45-60 min before (boosts endurance)
- Sip every 15-20 min; don't wait for thirst

## Rules
- Practice all fueling in long runs; never try anything new on race day.
- On hot days, proactively add electrolytes and ease pace accordingly.
