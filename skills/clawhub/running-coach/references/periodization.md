# Periodized Training Phases

## Phase Progression

| Phase | Duration | Weekly Volume | Quality Sessions | Key Adaptation |
|-------|----------|--------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Base** | 8-12 weeks | 55-65 km | 1/wk (Strides or Steady) | capillary density, fat oxidation, durability |
| **Build** | 6-8 weeks | 65-75 km | 2/wk (Tempo + Intervals) | LT elevation, running economy, race-pace specificity |
| **Peak** | 4-6 weeks | 70-80 km | 2/wk (goal-pace specific) | race-pace endurance, mental simulation |
| **Taper** | 2-3 weeks | 40-60% reduction | short intervals only | supercompensation, freshness |

## Quality Session Types by Phase

| Phase | Session A | Session B |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Base | Steady progressive or Strides (9×40s) | — |
| Build | Cruise Intervals (Z4) or Tempo 20-30min | Track intervals (Z5) or Hill repeats |
| Peak | Goal-pace long segments (2×5km @ MP) | Short intervals (neural activation) |
| Taper | 4-6×30s strides | — |

## Load Management Rules

- Weekly volume increase ≤ 10%
- Down week every 4th week: volume reduced 20-30%
- Two hard sessions never back-to-back (< 48h apart)
- Long run ≤ 30% of weekly total distance

## Transition Checkpoints

Before advancing to next phase, verify:
- [ ] Weekly volume at target range for ≥ 2 consecutive weeks
- [ ] No injury niggles lasting > 3 days
- [ ] HRV at or above baseline for ≥ 5 of last 7 days
- [ ] RPE on easy runs trending downward at same pace
