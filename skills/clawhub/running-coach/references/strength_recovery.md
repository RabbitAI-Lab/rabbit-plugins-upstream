# Strength Training & Recovery

## Runner strength (2x/week, non-consecutive days)
- Core: plank progressions, dead bug, side plank
- Glutes/legs (single-leg priority): Bulgarian split squat, step-up, single-leg RDL, calf raises (eccentric)
- Full body: goblet squat / deadlift (low reps, 8-10)
- Periodization: build base in base phase; add volume in build; maintain in peak; cut 50% in taper

## Plyometrics (from build phase, 1-2x/week)
- Depth jumps, broad jumps, bounding (develop running economy); place after quality work or on a separate day

## Mobility
- Daily: hip flexor stretch (key for sitters), ankle dorsiflexion, thoracic rotation
- Roll: ITB / TFL / calves

## Recovery modalities
- Sleep 7-9h (main HRV driver); carbs + protein within 30-60 min post-run (3:1)
- Hydrate by thirst + pale-yellow urine; replenish electrolytes after long runs
- Active recovery: easy cycling / swimming / walking
- Massage / compression / contrast bath:辅助 supportive, not essential

## Cross-training during injury
- When unable to run, use cycling / elliptical / swimming to maintain aerobic; avoid full stop
