# Race Strategy

## Pacing tactics
- Goal: even or slightly negative split (second half ≤ first half, gap ≤ 1%)
- First 5k 5-10 s/km slower than goal pace to find rhythm; avoid surging on downhills / excited overtakes
- Hills: ease slightly uphill to protect HR, control speed downhill (no flailing)
- Heat (> 12°C): ease pace ~0.5-1% per 1°C above

## Pre-race checklist (T-7 days)
- Taper per plan; gear (no new shoes that chafe), bib, timing chip, anti-chafe
- Study course profile: know aid stations, hills, turnarounds
- Fueling rehearsed; gut adapted

## Congested / mass-start races (large city events, wave starts)
Symptom: pace plan becomes unusable within the first km because the runner starts behind or into a much slower field (e.g. an earlier-start marathon wave still occupying the first 3-5 km).

Rule: when the pace plan is dead, replace the anchor — do not simply abandon structure.
- Swap pace target for an effort target: HR ceiling = LT-2 for the first third of the race. Accept any pace that keeps HR under that line.
- Treat the first ~5 km as a sunk cost: 20-25 s/km slower is normal; never surge to "make up" time.
- Run a chosen line (usually the outside) and hold it — lateral weaving is the real cost. Watch footpod/GPS speed loss % as the tell (weaving and wet grip push it well above the course norm).
- Switch back to pace execution once the field thins (typically 8-12 km), adjusting only to target pace — no recovery of lost seconds.
- Take gels/water at fixed points regardless of position; do not let the crowd change the fueling rhythm.

Wet course addendum: grip loss raises cadence and lowers stride length at the same pace; expect higher vertical oscillation and treat 3-5% speed loss as environmental, not fitness.

## Mindset
- Segment strategy: break the race into manageable chunks (every 5k / every bridge)
- Cue words for low patches: "relax shoulders" / "short quick steps"
- Accept fluctuation: at the wall, don't panic — follow the fueling plan, lower expectations to finish

## Post-race
- Walk 10 min to cool down + carbs/protein; light activity within 24h aids recovery
