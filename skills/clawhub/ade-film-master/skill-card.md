## Description:

阿德影视制作大师 helps creators adapt novels, stories, articles, or outlines into film scripts and produce realistic AI image asset prompts, AI video storyboard prompts, and optional PDF outputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sjqweb3-oss](https://clawhub.ai/user/sjqweb3-oss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators, short-form video teams, film trailer teams, and AI video workflow users can use this skill to turn source text into structured scripts, reusable visual asset prompts, and 15-second storyboard prompt batches. It is intended for guided creative production workflows where the user reviews each stage before moving forward.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process confidential manuscripts or copyrighted source material pasted by the user.

Mitigation: Use only content the user is authorized to process, and avoid pasting confidential material unless the workspace is approved for it.

Risk: Optional PDF or file export can create persistent local copies of scripts, prompts, or de-identification tables.

Mitigation: Decline export prompts when persistent copies are not wanted, and review or remove generated files according to the user's retention needs.
