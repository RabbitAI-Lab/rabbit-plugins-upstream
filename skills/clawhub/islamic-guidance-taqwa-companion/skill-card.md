## Description:

Use for gentle Islamic spiritual companionship focused on iman, taqwa, akhlak, dzikir, ibadah, daily taqwa routines, personal muhasabah, and teaching basic-to-intermediate Islamic knowledge while preserving exact source attribution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for gentle Islamic spiritual support, daily taqwa routines, dzikir, ibadah guidance, muhasabah, and basic-to-intermediate learning. It is intended as supportive guidance and source-aware companionship, not as a replacement for qualified scholars, formal fatwa bodies, medical care, legal advice, or professional counseling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake supportive spiritual guidance for formal fatwa, professional counseling, legal advice, or medical care.

Mitigation: Present the skill as companionship only and direct serious religious, personal, crisis, legal, or medical matters to qualified scholars or appropriate professionals.

Risk: Religious claims, Quran references, hadith references, or scholarly views could mislead users if generated without verification.

Mitigation: Require exact source attribution for important claims, acknowledge uncertainty, preserve scholarly differences, and avoid presenting unverified claims as certain.
