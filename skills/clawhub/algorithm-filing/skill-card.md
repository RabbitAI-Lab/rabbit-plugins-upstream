## Description: <br>
AI large model / algorithm regulatory filing assistant for China's CAC requirements. Helps determine filing type, prepare required materials, generate security assessment report frameworks, guide system submission, and manage post-filing compliance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, operators, and compliance teams use this skill to assess China CAC large-model and algorithm filing obligations, prepare filing material checklists, draft security assessment report frameworks, follow submission steps, and manage post-filing compliance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill provides regulatory filing assistance and may be mistaken for legal advice or current official guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting and organization support; verify current requirements with official CAC guidance, the relevant local authority, or qualified counsel before filing. <br>
Risk: Filing workflows can involve sensitive materials such as business licenses, identity information, test accounts, security reports, and operational details. <br>
Mitigation: Provide or store sensitive materials only in trusted, user-chosen systems and review generated content before sharing it outside the organization. <br>
