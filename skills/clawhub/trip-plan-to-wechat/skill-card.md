## Description:

Helps an agent publish a confirmed trip itinerary to a WeChat-friendly LivePage link, update that link during the trip, and optionally collect companion votes or sign-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[verkyyi](https://clawhub.ai/user/verkyyi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travelers and trip organizers use the skill to share a user-approved itinerary with companions through one persistent link, optionally collecting votes or sign-ups and reviewing responses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An itinerary shared publicly or without adequate access controls may expose trip details beyond the intended companions.

Mitigation: Confirm the content and intended audience before publishing, prefer passcode access, and never choose public visibility without the user's direction.

Risk: Votes, sign-ups, comments, and viewing activity may reveal companion information to the trip organizer.

Mitigation: Tell companions what activity can be collected and visible to the organizer before enabling forms or reviewing engagement.
