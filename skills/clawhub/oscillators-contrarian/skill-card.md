## Description: <br>
Guides agents in answering questions about oscillator overbought or oversold readings, divergences, and contrarian sentiment extremes while keeping trend context central. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to explain RSI, KD, %R, MACD, ROC, divergence, and contrarian sentiment signals. It is intended for educational technical-analysis guidance, especially when trend versus range-bound market context changes how oscillator signals should be interpreted. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Educational trading-analysis guidance may be mistaken for an automated trading recommendation. <br>
Mitigation: Present oscillator and sentiment readings as interpretive guidance only, and require user risk controls, trend context, confirmation signals, and independent decision-making before any financial action. <br>
Risk: Overbought, oversold, divergence, or sentiment extremes can be misleading when read without market regime context. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's trend-first posture: use oscillator extremes differently in trending and range-bound markets, and avoid treating a single indicator as confirmation. <br>
