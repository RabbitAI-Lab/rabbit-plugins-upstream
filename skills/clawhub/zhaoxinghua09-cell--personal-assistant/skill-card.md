## Description:

Organizes local schedules and to-do records to answer questions about today's plans, missed tasks, and progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to review today's plans, identify overdue tasks, track progress, and confirm new items before recording them locally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad schedule, to-do, and progress phrases may activate the skill unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Review proposed record changes and confirm them before they are saved.
