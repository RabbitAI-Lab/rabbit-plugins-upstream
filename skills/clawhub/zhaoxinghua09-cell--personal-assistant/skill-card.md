## Description:

Helps users review recorded schedules and tasks, identify overdue items, and check progress without inventing missing records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to check today's plans, overdue tasks, and progress against recorded items, or to record new tasks after confirming their details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers and local read/write access could expose or unintentionally alter private schedule and todo records.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated notes path, restrict access to unrelated private files, and explicitly confirm each write or change.
