## Description: <br>
Wenchang Research gathers sources, key facts, contrarian evidence, quote candidates, contradictions, and confidence notes before content writing or publishing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, editors, and writing agents use this skill to test whether a topic, outline, or draft has enough evidence before writing or publishing. It organizes sources, key facts, limits, contradictions, quote candidates, and content_state research updates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided topics, drafts, notes, or source links may contain private or sensitive material. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing private drafts or sensitive notes unless they are approved for use in the agent environment. <br>
Risk: Evidence may be incomplete or stale when sources cannot be checked live. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important claims against primary or high-trust sources and lower confidence when live checking is unavailable. <br>
