## Description: <br>
AI Piano Teacher covering music theory, technique, repertoire analysis, practice plans, exam prep (ABRSM/CM/CCM), sight reading, ear training, mistake diagnosis, and repertoire recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External learners, parents, and music educators use this skill for piano learning support, including theory explanations, technique guidance, repertoire analysis, practice planning, exam preparation, sight-reading, ear-training, mistake diagnosis, and repertoire recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad Chinese music and piano triggers may route unrelated music questions to this skill. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user is asking for piano-learning help before applying detailed teaching, practice, or exam guidance. <br>
Risk: Personal learner details such as age, level, practice frequency, goals, and current repertoire may be used to personalize responses. <br>
Mitigation: Ask only for details needed for the requested guidance and avoid including unnecessary personal information in reports. <br>
Risk: Generated HTML or Tencent Docs reports may contain incorrect, outdated, or over-specific teaching recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated reports before sharing or using them for instruction, especially for exam preparation or technique correction. <br>
