## Description:

Produce practical hosting timelines, guest information and day-of run sheets for dinners, parties and holiday events. Use for whole-event coordination, not just recipes or gift shopping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External event hosts use this skill to coordinate a gathering, including its schedule, costs, guest logistics, and approved communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is incomplete because a local reference could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the referenced material and complete a security review before relying on the skill.

Risk: Guest-facing notes could expose private event details if they include unapproved information.

Mitigation: Use only confirmed, approved logistics in guest notes; keep private records out.
