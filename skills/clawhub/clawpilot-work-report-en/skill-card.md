## Description: <br>
Automatically extracts daily or weekly work progress, communication points, to-dos, approvals, and risks from work-related notifications and turns them into a concise work report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vivalavida-say-hi](https://clawhub.ai/user/vivalavida-say-hi) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and knowledge workers use this skill to summarize selected work-app notifications into a daily or weekly briefing that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with managers or teammates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read broad local notification history for the selected day or week, including names, projects, deadlines, approvals, and other sensitive work details. <br>
Mitigation: Configure the app and group scope during setup, use narrow prompts when possible, and review or redact the generated report before sharing it. <br>
