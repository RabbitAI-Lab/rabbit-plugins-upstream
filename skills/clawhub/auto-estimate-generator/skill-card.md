## Description: <br>
Automatically generate estimates from QTO data and apply pricing rules to BIM quantities for cost estimates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[datadrivenconstruction](https://clawhub.ai/user/datadrivenconstruction) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Construction estimators, BIM specialists, and project teams use this skill to turn quantity takeoff data into structured cost estimates with category summaries and unmatched items flagged for manual pricing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Excel export can overwrite the output path selected by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Choose an explicit project output path and avoid existing files or sensitive system locations unless overwriting them is intended. <br>
Risk: Incorrect or incomplete pricing rules can produce misleading construction estimates. <br>
Mitigation: Review unmatched items, fuzzy matches, unit prices, markups, and totals before using estimates for project decisions. <br>
