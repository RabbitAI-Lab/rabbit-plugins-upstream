## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crypto payment, token-launch, trading-activity, and airdrop guidance can create legal, tax, securities, sanctions, market-manipulation, or consumer-protection exposure.

Mitigation: Require jurisdiction-specific legal and compliance review before acting; document token utility, disclosures, user restrictions, tax treatment, anti-sybil controls, and anti-manipulation controls.

Risk: KOL, Crypto Twitter, and community-growth guidance can lead to undisclosed promotion, artificial engagement, or platform-rule violations.

Mitigation: Use disclosed promotions only, prohibit bots and undisclosed paid shilling, and confirm each campaign complies with platform and advertising rules.

Risk: Agentic developer-relations guidance may involve tracking developers or community members.

Mitigation: Apply privacy review, data minimization, access controls, notice or consent where required, and human oversight for outreach or escalation.

Risk: The guide contains time-sensitive February 2026 claims about platforms, grants, regulations, market structure, and launch mechanics.

Mitigation: Re-verify current platform rules, grant availability, regulations, fees, market statistics, and launchpad mechanics before relying on the guidance.
