## Use Case: <br>
Developers, protocol teams, product builders, and solo founders use this skill to plan web3 go-to-market work across developer community, Crypto Twitter narrative, ecosystem partnerships, grants, launch sequencing, token launches, pricing, and metrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Token-launch and crypto-payment guidance may under-scope sanctions, AML, KYC, geofencing, paid-promotion, or payment-regulation obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the guidance as educational, obtain qualified legal review, perform sanctions screening, apply AML/KYC controls, geofence where required, and disclose paid promotions clearly. <br>
Risk: The skill provides markdown-only GTM guidance that can be misapplied as compliance or financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations before use, adapt them to the specific jurisdiction and business model, and keep human approval on regulated launch, token, and payment decisions. <br>
