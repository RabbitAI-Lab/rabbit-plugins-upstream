## Description:

Guides central-enterprise technology teams through external technology acquisition workflows, from strategic needs and intelligence scanning to evaluation, due diligence, transfer-path planning, and decision evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Technology management teams use this skill to scan external technology opportunities, assess suppliers and patent assets, plan transfer paths, and assemble decision evidence for acquisition or introduction projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may send technology topics, patent numbers, organization names, and optional internal templates to configured PatSnap MCP tools or related skills.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with approved PatSnap account authorization and avoid confidential internal materials unless that external research workflow is approved.

Risk: Technology-transfer reports may be used as decision support for legal, IP, valuation, or compliance-sensitive actions.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers validate patent status, ownership, valuation, and compliance conclusions before relying on the reports for approvals or negotiations.
