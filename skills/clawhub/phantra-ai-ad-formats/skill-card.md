## Description:

Chooses a Phantra ad format and creates a timed shot plan grounded in the user's product photos and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to choose an ad format for a product and create a timed shot plan grounded in their photos and claims. It guides preparation of a scene prompt and variants, with paid rendering gated by review of a quote and user approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference photos may contain faces or private spaces.

Mitigation: Use images intended for sharing and review the service's storage terms before uploading sensitive material.

Risk: Paid rendering uses credits.

Mitigation: Review the quote and approve the render only after confirming its cost.

Risk: The skill may not load reliably if its frontmatter formatting is invalid.

Mitigation: Check and correct the frontmatter before relying on the skill.

Risk: Ad copy could include unsupported product claims.

Mitigation: Use only claims supplied by the user and do not invent benefits, numbers, reviews, ratings, or awards.
