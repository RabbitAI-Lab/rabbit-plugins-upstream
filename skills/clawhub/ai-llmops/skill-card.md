## Description:

AI Model Asset Management（AI模型资产管理） is an LLMOps playbook and local checklist toolkit for inventorying AI assets, managing model cards and versions, controlling launch and retirement, monitoring drift, and governing cost.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, AI platform teams, operations leaders, and finance stakeholders use this skill to build an AI asset registry, prepare model card templates, manage asset versions and release gates, monitor drift, and structure token cost controls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat this playbook as a deployed asset-management platform.

Mitigation: Use it as governance guidance and a local template generator, then implement tracking, approvals, monitoring, and controls in approved internal systems.

Risk: Suggested thresholds and operational controls may not fit every business or regulatory environment.

Mitigation: Validate drift, budget, release, and retirement thresholds against internal requirements before relying on them.

Risk: Reuse or redistribution may require license and content-rights review.

Mitigation: Review the MIT license and the artifact's ownership and knowledge copyright notices before reuse or redistribution.
