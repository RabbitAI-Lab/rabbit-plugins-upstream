## Description:

GEO Command Center helps marketing and reputation teams monitor Reddit mentions that may be cited in AI answers, analyze sentiment and competitor exposure, and prepare alerts, dashboards, and executive reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketing, growth, and brand reputation teams use this skill to monitor brand and competitor mentions on Reddit, check whether those mentions appear in AI answer citations, and plan response or amplification workflows. It produces operating playbooks, alert guidance, dashboards, and board-ready summaries for GEO and reputation programs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to provide ChatGPT session cookies for browser-based AI answer verification without adequate handling safeguards.

Mitigation: Do not provide session cookies; use local-only browser execution or delegated authentication with secret redaction, retention limits, and explicit handling rules.

Risk: Refunds, credits, gifts, or outreach in reputation-management workflows could be perceived as attempts to influence criticism.

Mitigation: Make any benefits unconditional, document the business rationale, and never tie them to editing, deleting, or suppressing negative comments.
