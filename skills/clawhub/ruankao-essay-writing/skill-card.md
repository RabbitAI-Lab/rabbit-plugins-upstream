## Description:

Guides Chinese Ruankao advanced-qualification candidates through project preparation, prompt analysis, outlining, drafting, and revision of exam essays across five qualifications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nieen](https://clawhub.ai/user/nieen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ruankao advanced-qualification candidates use this Chinese-language skill to prepare project examples, interpret essay prompts, draft outlines and essays, and review drafts for common issues. It covers five advanced qualifications but does not provide essay scoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft guidance may not match current official exam rules.

Mitigation: Verify generated content and length guidance against official exam requirements before relying on it.

Risk: A generic essay request may receive Ruankao-specific guidance.

Mitigation: Clarify the intended exam or essay type when requesting writing assistance.
