## Description:

Reviews Chinese-language posts and scripts for prohibited terms, platform-specific rules, privacy exposure, and overpromising across six social platforms, with suggested edits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guipi888](https://clawhub.ai/user/guipi888)

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators and social-media teams use this skill to review drafts for 视频号, 抖音, 小红书, 快手, B站, and 公众号 before publication and receive platform-specific findings and revision suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may include the publisher's promotional link.

Mitigation: Remove or approve promotional material before sharing reports.

Risk: Privacy checks may miss sensitive details in drafts.

Mitigation: Manually review and redact personal data and credentials before publication.

Risk: Some guidance could facilitate platform-rule evasion or phone-number follow-up.

Mitigation: Do not use evasion or phone-number follow-up advice; verify edits against platform rules.

Risk: Optional external checking services may receive unpublished drafts.

Mitigation: Use local checks or obtain explicit consent before sending drafts to external services.
