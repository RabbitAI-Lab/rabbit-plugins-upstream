## Description: <br>
Roadshow Planning helps founders and project teams plan fundraising pitches, structure investor presentations, draft pitch scripts, prepare investor Q&A, and outline pitch deck content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, entrepreneurs, and project teams use this skill to turn business-plan inputs or conversational project details into structured fundraising roadshow plans, pitch scripts, investor Q&A preparation, financial and competitive-analysis presentation guidance, and PPT page outlines. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive business-plan, fundraising, team, and financial information while preparing a pitch. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide BP file paths, financial data, and company details that the user intends the agent to use for pitch preparation. <br>
Risk: Pitch, market, competitive, financial, valuation, and investor-answer guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific fundraising context. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated roadshow materials, verify source data and assumptions, and adapt the content with appropriate business, finance, or legal review before using it with investors. <br>
