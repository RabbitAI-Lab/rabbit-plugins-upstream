## Use Case: <br>
External SaaS operators, product teams, growth teams, and developers use this skill to design compliant retention workflows, optimize cancellation experiences, recover failed payments, and prioritize churn-reduction interventions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Retention recommendations may change subscription behavior, payment retry rules, account access, or customer communications. <br>
Mitigation: Require clear user approval before applying changes to billing, cancellation, dunning, or customer communication workflows. <br>
Risk: Cancel flows and marketing follow-up can create legal or consent issues if they make cancellation difficult or contact customers without permission. <br>
Mitigation: Review applicable easy-cancellation, data-retention, and marketing-consent requirements before launch, and keep a clear path to complete cancellation. <br>
Risk: Overly aggressive save offers or dunning actions can harm customer trust. <br>
Mitigation: Keep cancellation options visible, avoid dark patterns, use customer-friendly dunning copy, and review proposed flows before deployment. <br>
