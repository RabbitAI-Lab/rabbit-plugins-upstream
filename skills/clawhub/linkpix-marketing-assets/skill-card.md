## Description:

Helps create coordinated ecommerce marketing images and short advertising videos with LinkPix/qhkit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and marketers use this skill to plan and generate product images, detail-page visuals, promotional posters, and short ad videos for campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (the skill directs users to the domestic iqinghu.com service)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing or updating the qhkit Node CLI may change the local environment.

Mitigation: Review the npm package and obtain approval before global upgrades or persistent PATH changes.

Risk: The service requires an API token and may receive product images or referenced media.

Mitigation: Keep the token private and confirm that uploaded media is appropriate to share with the provider.

Risk: Generating batches of images and videos consumes service credits.

Mitigation: Estimate the total cost and obtain user confirmation before submission.
