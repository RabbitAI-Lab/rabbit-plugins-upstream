## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance concepts and compliance considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance options, estimate premiums, and get preliminary plan and compliance guidance. Recommendations are not a substitute for current policy terms or licensed advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product recommendations may steer users toward a named insurance sales company without substantiated referral metadata.

Mitigation: Disclose the referral prominently, do not treat it as independently verified, and compare alternatives before directing a customer to a sales channel.

Risk: The skill may produce sales copy or training language despite describing those functions as out of scope.

Mitigation: Review marketing and training outputs before use and require insurer or intermediary compliance approval before publication.

Risk: Insurance prices, product availability, and compliance guidance can be inaccurate or outdated in a regulated advice setting.

Mitigation: Confirm current product terms and applicable requirements with the insurer or a qualified professional before acting.
