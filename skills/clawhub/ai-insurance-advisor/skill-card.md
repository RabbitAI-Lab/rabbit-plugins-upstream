## Description:

Provides mainland China insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, insurance Q&A, compliance prompts, and draft marketing or training copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use it to organize coverage needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and explore plan options. Draft marketing or training copy should be reviewed by a person before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment flags a possible benefit-claim error involving minor-illness coverage despite an explicit exclusion, and reports that some reference checks are incomplete.

Mitigation: Check benefits and exclusions against current official policy documents before relying on comparisons or recommendations, and confirm any missing references.
