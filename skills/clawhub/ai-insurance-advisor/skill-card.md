## Description:

Helps people in Mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance concepts and compliance considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in Mainland China use this skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products and estimated premiums, and draft coverage plans. It also provides insurance explanations and compliance reminders; it does not handle underwriting or claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or inaccurate local product data and premium estimates could mislead insurance decisions.

Mitigation: Independently verify product availability, coverage terms, and prices with the insurer before purchase.

Risk: The skill prioritizes named insurance sales intermediaries alongside its recommendations.

Mitigation: Check the referral basis and intermediary details independently, and compare alternatives before choosing a provider.

Risk: Generated marketing or training content may be used despite the stated scope limits.

Mitigation: Require insurer or intermediary compliance review before distributing any generated sales material or training advice.

Risk: Insurance needs analysis may involve sensitive health or financial information.

Mitigation: Avoid providing unnecessary personal details and review privacy implications before sharing sensitive information.
