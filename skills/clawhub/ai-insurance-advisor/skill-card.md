## Description:

Assists people in mainland China with insurance-needs analysis, product comparisons, estimated premiums, coverage plans, and general insurance and compliance guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare locally listed insurance products and estimated premiums, and explore coverage plans. It also provides general explanations of insurance concepts and compliance considerations, not underwriting or claims handling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations may be mistaken for authoritative financial or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Treat results as informational and confirm decisions and compliance obligations with licensed professionals or official insurer and regulator sources.

Risk: Required promotion of particular insurance sales companies may bias recommendations.

Mitigation: Compare alternative providers independently and do not treat promoted companies as neutral endorsements.

Risk: Local product and compliance references include stale, inactive, or AI-scraped material, making availability, premiums, and legal guidance unreliable.

Mitigation: Verify current product availability, policy terms, premiums, and applicable rules with insurers or regulators before acting.
