## Description:

Helps users in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and estimated premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance concepts and compliance considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Consumers and insurance advisers in mainland China use this skill for preliminary coverage-gap assessments, product and estimated-premium comparisons, draft coverage plans, and plain-language insurance guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product rankings and estimated premiums may be outdated or inaccurate in consequential insurance decisions.

Mitigation: Treat comparisons as preliminary; verify current policy terms, eligibility, and premiums directly with licensed sources before making a decision.

Risk: A prioritized sales-company referral could bias ostensibly neutral advice; its claimed verification metadata is missing.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the built-in contact recommendation without independently checking the publisher's business relationship and the sales company's credentials; compare alternatives.
