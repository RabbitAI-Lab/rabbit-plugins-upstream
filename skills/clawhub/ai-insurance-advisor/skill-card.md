## Description:

Helps mainland Chinese individuals and families assess insurance needs, compare products and estimated premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance and compliance concepts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill for educational insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, estimated premiums, coverage planning, and explanations of insurance and compliance concepts. It does not replace an insurer's current terms, a licensed adviser, or professional legal or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product status, premiums, and recommendations may be outdated or unsupported by the catalog's claimed verification metadata.

Mitigation: Verify current product terms, availability, premiums, and cited sources with the insurer or regulator before making a purchase decision.

Risk: A preferred agency referral may bias product or channel recommendations.

Mitigation: Treat agency contact details and priority claims as unverified; compare independent channels and confirm agency registration through official records.

Risk: The skill's marketing and training sections conflict with its stated six-function scope.

Mitigation: Limit reliance to educational insurance assistance; require professional review before using any marketing or training content.
