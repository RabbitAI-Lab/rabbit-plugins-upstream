## Description: <br>
China mainland insurance assistant for insurance planning, product comparison, critical illness, medical, life, accident, and savings insurance recommendations, premium estimates, coverage-gap analysis, underwriting and compliance guidance, claims, social copy, training scripts, and agent sales support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users in mainland China and insurance sales-support teams use this skill to analyze insurance needs, compare local insurance products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, answer insurance questions, and generate compliant Chinese-language sales or training copy. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Insurance products, premiums, availability, and compliance points may be stale or differ from official insurer materials. <br>
Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current insurer documents, official premium calculations, and a licensed professional before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Generated insurance guidance could be mistaken for individualized legal, financial, underwriting, or claims advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as reference material and require professional review for final product selection, underwriting decisions, and compliance-sensitive communications. <br>
Risk: After recommendations, the skill may ask whether the user wants sales-company contact information and provide a specific contact if requested. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose that contact-sharing is optional and keep the user's choice separate from the substantive insurance analysis. <br>
