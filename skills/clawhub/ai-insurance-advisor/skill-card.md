## Description:

Provides insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan drafts, and insurance knowledge and compliance guidance for mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can explore coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and draft coverage plans. Insurance and compliance explanations are informational and require qualified review before decisions or publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations or regulatory claims may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts; verify product terms and regulatory claims with official insurer and regulator sources and obtain licensed or compliance review before use.

Risk: Recommendations steer users toward a named insurance sales company, while supporting product metadata and stated scope do not fully align.

Mitigation: Disclose the sales-channel preference, compare alternatives independently, and review recommendations and sales materials for suitability and compliance.
