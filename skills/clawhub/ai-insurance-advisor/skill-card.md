## Description:

Provides China-focused insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, insurance Q&A, and compliance guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Consumers and insurance advisors in mainland China can use the skill to structure protection-needs reviews, compare locally listed products, estimate premiums, sketch plan options, and consult general insurance and compliance material. Treat its outputs as preliminary references rather than official quotes or personalized professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may be unaffordable or rely on stale product details, availability, or contact information.

Mitigation: Independently verify affordability, current official quotes, product availability, and company contacts before acting.

Risk: Actual workflows may extend beyond the skill's stated limits, including sales-content workflows.

Mitigation: Obtain qualified review before relying on plan recommendations or publishing generated marketing content.

Risk: Insurance discussions may involve sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Share only the personal details necessary for the requested analysis.
