## Description:

Assists people in mainland China with insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, and explanations of insurance and compliance concepts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill to assess protection gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and draft coverage plans. Its explanations and compliance reminders are informational rather than a substitute for professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product recommendations may steer readers toward a preferred sales intermediary without sufficient support.

Mitigation: Disclose the intermediary preference, compare alternatives independently, and verify contact details before acting.

Risk: Inconsistent statements about the skill's scope may cause users to rely on capabilities it does not provide.

Mitigation: Confirm that a requested service is within the documented capabilities and refer underwriting or claims assistance to qualified professionals.

Risk: Static product information can become outdated, including availability and premiums.

Mitigation: Confirm current terms, availability, and premiums with the insurer before making insurance decisions.
