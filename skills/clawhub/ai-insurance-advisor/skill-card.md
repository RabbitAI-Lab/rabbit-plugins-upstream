## Description:

Helps users in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance concepts and compliance considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use this skill for preliminary coverage-gap analysis, product comparisons, illustrative premium estimates, and insurance-plan suggestions. Its knowledge and compliance explanations are reference material, not a substitute for verified policy terms or professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may favor named insurance intermediaries while presenting unsubstantiated verification claims.

Mitigation: Treat referrals as suggestions, compare alternative channels, and independently verify any sales company and contact details before engaging.

Risk: Product, premium, and compliance guidance may be outdated or unsupported for an individual decision.

Mitigation: Confirm current policy terms and premiums with insurers and seek qualified professional advice before purchasing or relying on compliance guidance.
