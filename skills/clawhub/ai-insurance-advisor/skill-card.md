## Description:

Provides Chinese-language insurance education and planning for people and families in mainland China, including needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan suggestions, and compliance prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to understand insurance concepts, analyze protection needs, compare products in its local dataset, estimate premiums, and review plan suggestions. It does not provide underwriting pre-review, claims handling, or insurance-agent sales tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage descriptions may overstate the protection in a budget-adjusted plan.

Mitigation: Review the final adjusted coverage and its disclosures, then confirm product terms with the insurer or a qualified adviser.

Risk: Generated marketing or training copy may be inaccurate or noncompliant.

Mitigation: Check generated copy against the compliance reference and obtain compliance review before use.
