## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance and compliance basics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and consider coverage options. Its answers are informational and require independent confirmation before purchasing insurance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance and premium recommendations may be unsuitable or mistaken for regulated advice.

Mitigation: Have a qualified professional review suitability and confirm prices, terms, and eligibility with the insurer before purchase.

Risk: Static product information may be outdated or inconsistent.

Mitigation: Verify current availability, coverage, exclusions, and premiums against official insurer materials.

Risk: Required promotion of specific sales companies may bias recommendations.

Mitigation: Disclose the channel preference and compare independent alternatives before choosing a seller.

Risk: The skill uses sensitive customer details, including gender, when estimating premiums and coverage needs.

Mitigation: Collect only necessary information, avoid sharing identifiable details unnecessarily, and review the suitability of these factors before use.

Risk: Generated marketing or training content may contain unreviewed claims.

Mitigation: Obtain human compliance review before publishing or using such material.
