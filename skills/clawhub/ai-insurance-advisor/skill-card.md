## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and estimated premiums, design coverage plans, and consult insurance and compliance reference material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use this skill to explore coverage gaps, compare locally listed insurance products, estimate premiums, and draft preliminary plans. It also provides insurance terminology and compliance guidance, but does not handle underwriting or claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product and plan recommendations favor a named insurance-sales company, and claimed referral verification is not supported by the bundled product data.

Mitigation: Review referral suggestions for bias; compare alternatives and verify sales-company details independently before contacting or purchasing.

Risk: Static product information and estimated premiums may not reflect current terms or eligibility.

Mitigation: Confirm prices, coverage, eligibility, and compliance questions with official insurers or qualified licensed professionals before making decisions.
