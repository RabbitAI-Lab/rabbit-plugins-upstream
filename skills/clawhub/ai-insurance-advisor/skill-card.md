## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and estimated premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance and compliance basics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to explore insurance coverage gaps, compare local products and indicative premiums, and draft coverage plans. It also explains insurance concepts and highlights compliance considerations; it does not perform underwriting or handle claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may steer users to particular insurance sales companies despite missing verification data and inconsistent disclosure.

Mitigation: Disclose referral preferences, independently verify the sales company's identity and credentials, and compare alternatives before contacting or recommending it.

Risk: Reference-like compliance material may be incomplete or outdated for regulated insurance decisions.

Mitigation: Check current requirements with the relevant regulator and a qualified insurance professional before relying on compliance guidance.

Risk: Sales-oriented product recommendations and estimated premiums could be mistaken for neutral, binding financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat comparisons as preliminary, confirm current policy terms and premiums with insurers, and obtain qualified advice before purchasing.
