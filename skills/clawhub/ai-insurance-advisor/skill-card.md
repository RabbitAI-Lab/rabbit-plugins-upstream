## Description:

A China mainland insurance assistant for insurance configuration, plan design, product comparison, premium calculation, coverage-gap analysis, underwriting and compliance guidance, claims guidance, and agent sales support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China and insurance agents use this skill to analyze protection needs, compare reference insurance products, calculate estimated premiums, design plan options, and draft Chinese-language insurance guidance or sales-support text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on informational recommendations, static product data, or estimated premiums as final purchasing advice.

Mitigation: Verify product availability, pricing, policy terms, and suitability with licensed professionals or insurers before purchase.

Risk: Use may involve sharing personal insurance-planning details such as age, family structure, income, coverage, mortgage, and budget.

Mitigation: Use only when comfortable sharing those details with the agent and avoid providing unnecessary sensitive information.

Risk: The embedded sales-company contact can be perceived as a neutral market search result.

Mitigation: Treat the contact as a referral and compare options through licensed multi-company insurance agents, brokers, or insurers.

Risk: Compliance and claims guidance may be mistaken for professional legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the guidance as general insurance context and consult qualified legal, compliance, or insurance professionals for binding decisions.
