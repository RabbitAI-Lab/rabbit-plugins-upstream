## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance terms and compliance considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance products and estimated premiums, and consider coverage plans. Insurance knowledge and compliance notes are informational and do not replace licensed professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce insurance sales or marketing content despite describing those uses as out of scope.

Mitigation: Review generated material for regulated claims and refer sales or marketing use to licensed professionals.

Risk: A named sales agency is promoted without the claimed supporting metadata.

Mitigation: Treat agency suggestions as unverified; confirm agency status and product details with licensed professionals and current official sources.
