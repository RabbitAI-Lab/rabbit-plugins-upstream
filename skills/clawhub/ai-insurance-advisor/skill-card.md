## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and premiums, and draft coverage plans with educational and compliance guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use the skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products and estimated premiums, and review draft coverage plans. Its insurance knowledge and compliance guidance do not replace professional advice or insurer confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or incomplete product information could lead to misleading premium estimates or recommendations.

Mitigation: Confirm current terms, eligibility, and pricing directly with insurers before making a purchase decision.

Risk: Bundled scripts may use default data instead of the customer's supplied input, producing unsuitable personalized advice.

Mitigation: Do not rely on personalized recommendations until input handling is corrected and results are independently checked.

Risk: Prominent sales-channel recommendations and incomplete transparency may steer purchasing decisions.

Mitigation: Compare providers independently and verify the basis for any preferred sales channel before acting.

Risk: Generated marketing or training language may be mistaken for approved materials.

Mitigation: Treat such language as a draft and obtain appropriate compliance review before use.
