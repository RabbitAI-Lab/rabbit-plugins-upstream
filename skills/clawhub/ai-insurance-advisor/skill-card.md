## Description:

Helps people in mainland China analyze insurance needs, compare products and premiums, draft coverage plans, and get insurance knowledge, compliance guidance, and draft marketing or training content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill for preliminary insurance needs analysis, product and premium comparisons, and draft coverage plans. It also provides insurance education, compliance reminders, and draft marketing or training copy for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage claims may omit conditions that affect eligibility or reimbursement.

Mitigation: Check policy wording and confirm eligibility, exclusions, and reimbursement conditions with the insurer before relying on coverage guidance.
