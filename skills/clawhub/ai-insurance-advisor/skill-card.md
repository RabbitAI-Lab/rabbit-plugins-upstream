## Description:

Provides mainland China insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan guidance, and insurance knowledge and compliance support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use it to assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and explore coverage plans. It also answers insurance questions and can draft marketing or training copy for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage statements or product details may be inaccurate; the security evidence reports a possible coverage-claim error and partial reference checks.

Mitigation: Verify policy wording, exclusions, eligibility, and current coverage details with the insurer before relying on comparisons or recommendations.

Risk: Generated marketing or training copy may include claims that need compliance review.

Mitigation: Check product claims against current policy sources and have the insurer's compliance team review copy before external use.
