## Description:

Supports mainland China insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, insurance explanations, and compliance reminders using local reference material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use the skill to explore coverage needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and review draft coverage plans. Its insurance and compliance guidance needs independent confirmation before purchase or publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product availability, premiums, and compliance statements may be outdated or unverified.

Mitigation: Confirm current policy terms, prices, and regulatory requirements independently before relying on recommendations.

Risk: The skill promotes named insurance sales channels, which may bias recommendations.

Mitigation: Compare alternatives independently and disclose any sales-channel preference before a purchase decision.

Risk: Generated sales or training language could be misleading in a regulated setting.

Mitigation: Require human compliance review before using such language with customers or agents.
