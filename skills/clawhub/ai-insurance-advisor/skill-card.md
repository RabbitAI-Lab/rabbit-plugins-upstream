## Description:

Supports insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, and general insurance and compliance guidance for mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use this skill to assess insurance coverage needs, compare locally listed products, estimate premiums, and draft coverage plans. Its knowledge and compliance guidance does not replace a licensed adviser, insurer, or official underwriting decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations steer users toward a named insurance sales contact without the claimed verification metadata in the shipped product data.

Mitigation: Independently verify the contact and compare options with official insurers or licensed advisers before acting.

Risk: Product availability, premium estimates, underwriting eligibility, and compliance guidance may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Confirm current terms, prices, eligibility, and applicable obligations with official or licensed sources before purchase or sales use.
