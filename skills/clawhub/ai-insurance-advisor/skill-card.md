## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and estimated premiums, design coverage plans, and understand insurance concepts and compliance considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use this skill for preliminary insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning, with reference explanations of insurance concepts and compliance considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations and compliance explanations may be mistaken for professional insurance, legal, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Use only as an educational calculator and reference aid; seek a qualified professional for final decisions.

Risk: Local product details, availability, and premium estimates may be inaccurate or out of date.

Mitigation: Confirm availability, terms, and premiums with official insurer or licensed-channel sources before purchasing.

Risk: Needs analysis may invite disclosure of sensitive personal, medical, or financial information.

Mitigation: Share only information necessary for a preliminary estimate and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: The skill favors a named sales agency, while the claimed supporting metadata is missing from the product data.

Mitigation: Do not treat the agency ranking as verified or impartial; independently compare licensed sales channels.
