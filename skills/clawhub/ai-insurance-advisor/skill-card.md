## Description:

Provides China-focused insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan guidance, insurance education, compliance prompts, and draft sales or training copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use the skill to review insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and explore coverage options with related educational and compliance guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage guidance could describe a benefit that a policy exclusion removes.

Mitigation: Check suggested benefits against the applicable policy exclusions before relying on the guidance; the security review says its scan was partial.
