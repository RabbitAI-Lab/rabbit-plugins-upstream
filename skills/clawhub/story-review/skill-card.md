## Description:

Reviews fiction drafts from multiple editorial perspectives and provides evidence-based suggestions on plot, characters, prose, and continuity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fiction authors and editors use this skill to review chapters or longer works for narrative quality, consistency, and actionable revision opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reads local fiction drafts and project materials, which may contain unpublished or sensitive work.

Mitigation: Use only with fiction projects whose contents you are comfortable sharing with the review workflow.

Risk: Review modes may coordinate additional reviewer agents.

Mitigation: Choose /story-review solo when you do not want reviewer-agent spawning.

Risk: Reviews may leave persistent project state or author preferences in .story-review/, .story/作者记忆/, or 追踪/.

Mitigation: Review or clean these folders when persistent state is unwanted; solo mode does not maintain 追踪/.
