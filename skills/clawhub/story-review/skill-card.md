## Description:

Reviews fiction from multiple perspectives and provides prioritized feedback on narrative quality, style, and consistency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers use this skill to review fiction chapters and supporting story materials for narrative quality and consistency, then receive actionable findings and suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates this release suspicious because it may change manuscript files and persistent story-tracking data beyond producing a review, and the analysis was incomplete.

Mitigation: Use it only where those file and tracking updates are expected, and keep backups or version control so unexpected edits can be reviewed or restored.
