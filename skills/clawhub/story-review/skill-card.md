## Description:

Reviews fiction from multiple perspectives for plot, character, continuity, and prose issues, using available reviewer agents or a solo fallback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fiction authors and editors use this skill to review manuscripts for structural, character, continuity, and prose problems and receive actionable revision suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may read broad manuscript and project context and share it with available reviewer agents.

Mitigation: Use only on projects and with reviewer agents authorized to access the manuscript.

Risk: Reviews may persist changes to project tracking, batch-review state, and author-preference memory.

Mitigation: Review proposed file updates and keep a backup or version-controlled copy of project metadata.

Risk: Relationship guidance may introduce biased or coercive romance-trope advice into a review.

Mitigation: Read the relationship guidance before use and independently assess relationship advice in the report.
