## Description: <br>
Turns a social content need into an image brief, model-agnostic prompt guidance, and a tool-routing recommendation for image or video generation workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External social media teams and content agents use this skill to decide whether an image should be AI-generated, write a concise visual brief, produce a natural-language prompt, and route the work to an appropriate image or video tool before publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can steer an agent toward external image or video generation and later media upload workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated assets, verify any in-image text or data, and require confirmation before publishing. <br>
Risk: AI-generated visuals may create disclosure, likeness, or copyrighted-IP concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Follow applicable AI disclosure rules, avoid real identifiable people and copyrighted IP, and prefer real photos, screenshots, charts, or UGC when they are the more accurate asset. <br>
