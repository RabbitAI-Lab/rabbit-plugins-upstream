## Description:

Chat with the dLazy sandbox agent, a project-scoped assistant for running skills over multiple turns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other dLazy users can use the skill to have project-scoped, multi-turn conversations with the dLazy sandbox agent, discover available skills and projects, and continue ongoing work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation phrases may select the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Limit use to relevant project conversations and review the skill's activation phrases before enabling it.

Risk: Messages and attached files may be sent to dLazy services.

Mitigation: Review what content will be sent and avoid attaching sensitive files unless sharing them with dLazy is appropriate.
