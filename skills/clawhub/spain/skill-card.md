## Description: <br>
Plans Spain travel with local-level picks: named restaurants, regional rules, timing, booking windows, and tourist-trap avoidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to plan Spain trips, build itineraries, choose regions, restaurants, transport, lodging, and activities, and sanity-check advice against local timing, regional customs, and tourist-trap patterns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Local preference memory can contain sensitive travel details such as dates, dietary needs, mobility needs, group context, or trip style. <br>
Mitigation: Review and remove saved details in ~/Clawic/data/spain/ when no longer needed, and avoid storing sensitive information unless it is necessary for recommendations. <br>
