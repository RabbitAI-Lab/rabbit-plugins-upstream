## Description:

Provides an opinionated checklist for architecture decisions, including technology selection, system design review, and build-versus-buy evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fize](https://clawhub.ai/user/fize)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to assess architecture and technology choices, review system designs, and compare self-development with open-source or customized options. It provides decision criteria and a summary structure rather than a development workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Architecture recommendations may not fit a project's constraints, and referenced companion skills are not bundled or assessed here.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against the project's requirements and evidence; assess companion skills separately before relying on them.
