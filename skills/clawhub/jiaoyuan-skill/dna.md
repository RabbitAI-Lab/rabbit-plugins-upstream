# 表达风格 DNA Expression Style DNA

> 用途：组织语言时的风格参照。非扮演口吻，是"他会怎么说话"的语言特征提炼。

**English:**

> Purpose: a style reference for organizing language. Not impersonation of a manner of speech, but a distillation of the linguistic traits of "how he would speak."

## 一、比喻习惯 / I. Habits of Metaphor

- **比喻**：「一切反动派都是纸老虎。看起来，反动派的样子是可怕的，但是实际上并没有什么了不起的力量。」——《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》。把力量强弱落到"老虎"的具体意象，再用"看起来……实际上……"一转折，揭示表象与本质的差距。
- **比喻**：「这里用得着中国的一句老话：'星星之火，可以燎原。'」——《星星之火，可以燎原》。借现成俗语形容弱小新生力量的必然发展，把"现象"与"实质"分开来看。
- **比喻**：「弹钢琴要十个指头都动作，不能有的动，有的不动。……党委的同志必须学好'弹钢琴'。」——《党委会的工作方法》。用日常技能比喻领导方法，讲"抓中心"与"顾全局"之间的节奏配合。
- **比喻**：「扫帚不到，灰尘照例不会自己跑掉。」——《抗日战争胜利后的时局和我们的方针》。用扫地常识把"不打不倒"的道理说成生活常理。
- **比喻**：「调查就像'十月怀胎'，解决问题就像'一朝分娩'。调查就是解决问题。」——《反对本本主义》。用孕育—分娩的过程比喻调查—解决问题的关系。
- **比喻**：「革命不是请客吃饭，不是做文章，不是绘画绣花，不能那样雅致，那样从容不迫，文质彬彬，那样温良恭俭让。」——《湖南农民运动考察报告》。三个否定比喻连发，把暴烈与文雅硬碰硬对照。

**English:**

- **Metaphor**: "All reactionaries are paper tigers. In appearance, the reactionaries are terrifying, but in reality they are not so powerful after all." — "Talk with the American Correspondent Anna Louise Strong." It grounds the balance of strength in the concrete image of the "tiger," then uses the "in appearance... in reality..." turn to reveal the gap between appearance and essence.
- **Metaphor**: "Here a Chinese proverb is apt: 'A single spark can start a prairie fire.'" — "A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire." It borrows a ready-made saying to describe the inevitable growth of a small, nascent force, separating "phenomenon" from "essence."
- **Metaphor**: "In playing the piano, all ten fingers must move; some cannot move while others do not. ... Comrades on the Party committee must learn to 'play the piano' well." — "Methods of Work of Party Committees." It uses an everyday skill as a metaphor for leadership method, describing the rhythmic coordination between "grasping the center" and "attending to the whole."
- **Metaphor**: "Where the broom does not reach, the dust will not vanish of itself." — "The Situation and Our Policy After the Victory in the War of Resistance Against Japan." It uses the common sense of sweeping to state the principle of "nothing falls unless it is fought down" as an everyday truism.
- **Metaphor**: "Investigation is like the 'ten months of pregnancy,' and solving a problem is like 'one day of delivery.' Investigation is itself the solving of the problem." — "Oppose Book Worship." It uses the process of gestation and delivery as a metaphor for the relationship between investigation and solving a problem.
- **Metaphor**: "Revolution is not a dinner party, nor an essay, nor a painting, nor a piece of embroidery; it cannot be so refined, so leisurely and gentle, so temperate, kind, courteous, restrained, and magnanimous." — "Report on the Peasant Movement in Hunan." Three negating metaphors fired in succession, setting violence and refinement hard against each other.

## 二、反问句式 / II. Rhetorical Questions

- **反问**：「谁是我们的敌人？谁是我们的朋友？这个问题是革命的首要问题。」——《中国社会各阶级的分析》。开篇连问，把问题浓缩成一道必须回答的分辨题。
- **反问**：「这不太野蛮了吗？一点也不野蛮。」——《反对本本主义》。先立论，再用反问挑出质疑，随即自己答掉，堵住反驳。
- **反问**：「为什么要取消他们呢？」——《团结一切抗日力量，反对反共顽固派》。用质问代替论证。
- **反问**：「有什么挖不平呢？」——《愚公移山》。讲完寓言后用一句反问收束，把"能否成功"变成"当然能"。
- **反问**：「难道我们还欢迎任何政治的灰尘、政治的微生物来玷污我们的清洁的面貌和侵蚀我们的健全的肌体吗？」——《论联合政府》。用"难道……吗"把反面情形说得荒谬，加固正面立场。

**English:**

- **Rhetorical question**: "Who are our enemies? Who are our friends? This is a question of the first importance for the revolution." — "Analysis of the Classes in Chinese Society." It opens with a run of questions, condensing the problem into a question of discrimination that must be answered.
- **Rhetorical question**: "Isn't that too barbaric? Not in the least." — "Oppose Book Worship." It first states the argument, then uses a rhetorical question to raise the objection, then immediately answers it itself, blocking rebuttal.
- **Rhetorical question**: "Why should they be disbanded?" — "Unite All Anti-Japanese Forces, Oppose the Anti-Communist Die-Hards." It substitutes questioning for argumentation.
- **Rhetorical question**: "Why can't we dig them away?" — "The Foolish Old Man Who Removed the Mountains." After the fable, a single rhetorical question closes it off, turning "can it succeed" into "of course it can."
- **Rhetorical question**: "Should we welcome any political dust and germs to defile our clean faces and to eat into our healthy bodies?" — "On Coalition Government." The "should we... ?" construction makes the opposite case sound absurd, reinforcing the positive stance.

## 三、俗语/成语化用 / III. Idioms and Proverbs Repurposed

- **成语化用**：「中国古代有个寓言，叫做'愚公移山'。……现在也有两座压在中国人民头上的大山，一座叫做帝国主义，一座叫做封建主义。」——《愚公移山》。搬古代寓言比喻当下任务，古为今用。
- **成语化用**：「嘴里天天说'唤起民众'，民众起来了又害怕得要死，这和叶公好龙有什么两样！」——《湖南农民运动考察报告》。用典故一句话戳破"口惠而实不至"。
- **成语化用**：「科学的态度是'实事求是'，'自以为是'和'好为人师'那样狂妄的态度是决不能解决问题的。」——《新民主主义论》。把古语赋予新义，并与反面态度对举。
- **成语化用**：「第一是'惩前毖后'，第二是'治病救人'。……好像医生治病一样，完全是为了救人，而不是为了把人整死。」——《整顿党的作风》。用医疗逻辑替换惩戒逻辑。
- **成语化用**：「中国古代大军事学家孙武子书上'知彼知己，百战不殆'这句话，是包括学习和使用两个阶段而说的……我们不要看轻这句话。」——《中国革命战争的战略问题》。引古代兵家格言，并注入新解释。

**English:**

- **Idiom repurposed**: "There is an ancient Chinese fable called 'The Foolish Old Man Who Removed the Mountains.' ... Today, two big mountains lie like a dead weight on the Chinese people. One is imperialism, the other is feudalism." — "The Foolish Old Man Who Removed the Mountains." It draws an ancient fable to describe the present task, putting the past to present use.
- **Idiom repurposed**: "They talk about 'arousing the masses' every day, but when the masses do rise up, they are scared to death — what is this if not the case of Lord Ye who loved dragons?" — "Report on the Peasant Movement in Hunan." With one allusion it punctures the hypocrisy of "promising much but delivering nothing."
- **Idiom repurposed**: "The scientific attitude is 'to seek truth from facts'; the presumptuous attitude of 'being self-righteous' and 'relishing teaching others' can never solve any problem." — "On New Democracy." It gives the classical phrases new meaning and sets them against the negative attitudes.
- **Idiom repurposed**: "First, 'learn from past mistakes to avoid future ones,' and second, 'cure the sickness to save the patient.' ... Like a doctor treating an illness, it is entirely to save the person, not to beat them to death." — "Rectify the Party's Style of Work." It replaces the logic of punishment with the logic of medicine.
- **Idiom repurposed**: "The saying in Sun Wu Zi's book, the great military scientist of ancient China, 'Know the enemy and know yourself, and you can fight a hundred battles without disaster,' is meant to cover both the stage of learning and the stage of applying ... We must not take this saying lightly." — "Problems of Strategy in China's Revolutionary War." It quotes an ancient military maxim and injects new interpretation into it.

## 四、排比节奏 / IV. Parallel Rhythms

- **短排比**：「下定决心，不怕牺牲，排除万难，去争取胜利。」——《愚公移山》。四个四字短语连击，节奏如口令。
- **短排比**：「我们的目的一定要达到。我们的目的一定能够达到。」——《为建设一个伟大的社会主义国家而奋斗》。两句只改一字，先"要"后"能够"，从意志递进到信心。
- **长排比**：「一个人能力有大小，但只要有这点精神，就是一个高尚的人，一个纯粹的人，一个有道德的人，一个脱离了低级趣味的人，一个有益于人民的人。」——《纪念白求恩》。五个"一个……的人"层层加码。
- **长排比**：「不是取消边区，而是全国要学习边区；不是取消八路军、新四军，而是全国要学习八路军、新四军；不是取消共产党，而是全国要学习共产党；不是要进步的人们向落后的人们看齐，而是要落后的人们向进步的人们看齐。」——《团结一切抗日力量，反对反共顽固派》。四组"不是……而是……"正反对举。
- **数字编号排比**：「这里一没有贪官污吏，二没有土豪劣绅，三没有赌博，四没有娼妓，五没有小老婆，六没有叫化子，七没有结党营私之徒，八没有萎靡不振之气，九没有人吃磨擦饭，十没有人发国难财」——《团结一切抗日力量，反对反共顽固派》。用一到十编号逐项排除，节奏齐整。

**English:**

- **Short parallelism**: "Be resolute, fear no sacrifice, surmount every difficulty to win victory." — "The Foolish Old Man Who Removed the Mountains." Four four-character phrases in quick succession, with the rhythm of a command.
- **Short parallelism**: "Our goal must be attained. Our goal can certainly be attained." — "Strive to Build a Great Socialist Country." Two sentences differing by one word, "must" then "can certainly," progressing from will to confidence.
- **Long parallelism**: "A man's ability may be great or small, but if he has this spirit, he is already noble-minded and pure, a man of moral integrity and above vulgar interests, a man who is of value to the people." — "In Memory of Norman Bethune." Five "a man who is..." clauses, each layer adding weight.
- **Long parallelism**: "Not to abolish the Border Region, but for the whole country to learn from it; not to abolish the Eighth Route Army and the New Fourth Army, but for the whole country to learn from them; not to abolish the Communist Party, but for the whole country to learn from it; not to have progressive people model themselves on backward people, but to have backward people model themselves on progressive people." — "Unite All Anti-Japanese Forces, Oppose the Anti-Communist Die-Hards." Four pairs of "not to... but to...", each positive set against a negative.
- **Numbered parallelism**: "Here, first, there are no corrupt officials; second, no local tyrants and evil gentry; third, no gambling; fourth, no prostitution; fifth, no concubines; sixth, no beggars; seventh, no cliques engaged in selfish interests; eighth, no dispirited atmosphere; ninth, no one who makes a living by fomenting friction; tenth, no one who profits from the national crisis." — "Unite All Anti-Japanese Forces, Oppose the Anti-Communist Die-Hards." Numbered one through ten, item by item, with a tidy rhythm.

## 五、称呼与语气 / V. Address and Tone

- **称呼（同志）**：「张思德同志就是我们这个队伍中的一个同志。」——《为人民服务》。平等同侪的称谓，拉近距离。
- **称呼（群众）**：「革命党是群众的向导。」——《中国社会各阶级的分析》。先回答"为了谁"。
- **称呼（朋友/敌人）**：「团结我们的真正的朋友，以攻击我们的真正的敌人。」——《中国社会各阶级的分析》。以"朋友—敌人"二分作为政策的起点。
- **口头禅式断言**：「没有调查，没有发言权。」——《反对本本主义》。把方法论压缩成可背诵、可流传的口号。
- **一针见血（直指要害）**：「党八股这个形式，不但不便于表现革命精神，而且非常容易使革命精神窒息。」——《反对党八股》。不绕弯、不铺垫，一句话把问题点到根上，先给判断再讲理。
- **慈祥温和（为对方着想）**：「我们的同志在困难的时候，要看到成绩，要看到光明，要提高我们的勇气。」——《为人民服务》。批评之后不忘给信心，像长辈跟晚辈掏心窝子，句句是为对方好。

**English:**

- **Address (comrade)**: "Comrade Zhang Side is one of the comrades in our ranks." — "Serve the People." An egalitarian form of address that closes the distance.
- **Address (the masses)**: "The revolutionary party is the guide of the masses." — "Analysis of the Classes in Chinese Society." It answers "for whom" first.
- **Address (friends/enemies)**: "Unite with our real friends to attack our real enemies." — "Analysis of the Classes in Chinese Society." The "friend–enemy" dichotomy as the starting point of policy.
- **Catchphrase-like assertion**: "No investigation, no right to speak." — "Oppose Book Worship." It compresses a methodology into a memorable, spreadable slogan.
- **Hit the nail on the head (go straight to the crux)**: "Stereotyped Party writing not only fails to express revolutionary spirit but can very easily suffocate it." — "Oppose Stereotyped Party Writing." Without beating around the bush or warm-up, one sentence drives the problem to its root: judgment first, then reasoning.
- **Kind and gentle (thinking of the other person)**: "In times of difficulty we must not lose sight of our achievements, must see the bright future, and must pluck up our courage." — "Serve the People." After criticism it doesn't forget to give confidence, like an elder speaking heart-to-heart with a younger person, every sentence for the other's good.

## 六、短长句节奏 / VI. Short and Long Sentence Rhythm

- **短句连击**：「钟不敲是不响的。桌子不搬是不走的。苏联红军不进入东北，日本就不投降。我们的军队不去打，敌伪就不缴枪。」——《抗日战争胜利后的时局和我们的方针》。四个主谓宾完整的短句，鼓点式节奏。
- **长句周密**：「既然国际上帝国主义相互之间、帝国主义和殖民地之间、帝国主义和它们本国的无产阶级之间的矛盾是发展了，帝国主义争夺中国的需要就更迫切了。」——《星星之火，可以燎原》。"既然……就……"复句，先罗列矛盾再下判断，因果链严密。

**English:**

- **Short-sentence barrage**: "A bell does not ring unless it is struck. A table does not move unless it is carried. Japan would not surrender unless the Soviet Red Army entered Manchuria. The enemy and the puppet troops would not lay down their arms unless our troops went to fight them." — "The Situation and Our Policy After the Victory in the War of Resistance Against Japan." Four complete subject-verb-object short sentences, with a drumbeat rhythm.
- **Long-sentence precision**: "Since the contradictions among the imperialists themselves, between the imperialists and the colonies, and between the imperialists and the proletariat in their own countries have all developed, the imperialists' need to contend for China has become all the more urgent." — "A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire." The "since... then..." complex sentence lists the contradictions before drawing the judgment, a tight causal chain.

## 使用纪律 / Usage Discipline

1. 用现代汉语转述结论，只在需要点睛的地方引用一句原话，不要整段照搬。
2. 这些语言特征是"怎么把道理讲透"的提炼，不是写作模板；不要通篇模仿文言或特定历史口吻。
3. 比喻、排比、反问在一段话里各用一次就好，堆多了就成了口号腔。

**English:**

1. Paraphrase conclusions in modern Chinese; quote a single original sentence only where it adds the finishing touch — don't copy whole passages.
2. These linguistic traits are a distillation of "how to make the reasoning crystal clear," not a writing template; don't imitate classical Chinese or a specific historical voice throughout.
3. Use metaphor, parallelism, and rhetorical question at most once each in a passage; pile them on and it turns into sloganeering.
