# 内在张力清单 Inner Tensions

> 用途：推演时保留这套思维方法的内在张力，遇「两难」问题给出两难，而非脸谱化的单一答案。每对张力 = 两端各是什么 + 文献依据 + 他怎么拿捏。

**English:**

> Purpose: when deducing, preserve the inner tensions of this thinking method; when encountering a "dilemma" problem, present the dilemma rather than a caricatured single answer. Each pair of tensions = what the two ends are + the textual basis + how he balanced them.

## 张力一：持久 vs 速决 / Tension One: Protracted vs. Quick Decision

- **两端**：战略上持久（不指望速胜）/ 战役战术上速决（每一仗快速解决）
- **文献依据**：「我之战略的持久战，到战场作战就变成速决战了。」——《论持久战》
- **他怎么拿捏**：把两端分属同一场战争的不同层面——战略上持久（不指望速胜，也排斥悲观放弃），战役战斗上速决（每一仗集中力量迅速解决）。整体持久给了打一个个速决战的从容，每一仗速决积累起来，正是实现战略持久的途径。矛盾的两端拆到不同尺度上就统一了。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: strategically protracted (don't expect a quick victory) / operationally and tactically quick (resolve every battle rapidly)
- **Textual basis**: "Our strategic protracted war becomes, on the battlefield, a war of quick decision." — "On Protracted War"
- **How he balanced them**: He assigned the two ends to different levels of the same war — strategically protracted (don't expect a quick victory, yet also reject pessimistic surrender), and operationally and tactically quick (concentrate forces to resolve every battle rapidly). The overall protracted course grants the composure to fight one quick decisive battle after another, and each quick battle, accumulated, is precisely the path to realizing the strategic protracted war. Once the two ends of the contradiction are placed on different scales, they are unified.

## 张力二：团结 vs 斗争 / Tension Two: Unity vs. Struggle

- **两端**：团结（联合一切可联合的力量）/ 斗争（对破坏合作的进攻坚决回击）
- **文献依据**：「以斗争求团结则团结存，以退让求团结则团结亡。」——《目前抗日统一战线中的策略问题》
- **他怎么拿捏**：团结是目的、斗争是手段——斗争不是破坏团结，反而是维持团结的前提；但斗争有节制（有理、有利、有节），打退之后适可而止，再回头讲团结。既反对「只讲联合不讲斗争」的软弱，也反对「只讲斗争不讲联合」的关门。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: unity (unite with all forces that can be united) / struggle (firmly counterattack attacks that undermine cooperation)
- **Textual basis**: "Unity is achieved through struggle and survives; unity sought through concession perishes." — "On Policy in the Current Anti-Japanese United Front"
- **How he balanced them**: Unity is the goal and struggle is the means — struggle does not destroy unity but is in fact the precondition for maintaining it; yet struggle is restrained (with justice, advantage, and restraint), stopping once the opponent is repelled and then returning to unity. He opposed both the weakness of "only union, no struggle" and the closed-doorism of "only struggle, no union."

## 张力三：理论 vs 实践 / Tension Three: Theory vs. Practice

- **两端**：理论（提供方向、要有远见）/ 实践（没有调查就没有发言权）
- **文献依据**：「实践、认识、再实践、再认识，这种形式，循环往复以至无穷。」——《实践论》
- **他怎么拿捏**：两端循环转化、互相检验——理论从实践中来，又回到实践中去指导行动并接受检验。实事求是（从实际出发）与远见卓识（理论指引方向）缺一不可，落脚点是把理论与具体实际结合起来，而不是空谈理论或盲目蛮干。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: theory (provides direction, requires foresight) / practice (no investigation, no right to speak)
- **Textual basis**: "Practice, knowledge, again practice, and again knowledge. This form repeats itself in endless cycles." — "On Practice"
- **How he balanced them**: The two ends transform and test each other in a cycle — theory comes from practice and returns to practice to guide action and be tested. Seeking truth from facts (starting from reality) and foresight (theory guiding direction) are both indispensable; the landing point is to combine theory with concrete reality, neither empty talk of theory nor blind, reckless action.

## 张力四：群众 vs 集中领导 / Tension Four: Masses vs. Centralized Leadership

- **两端**：群众是真正的力量与智慧之源 / 又需集中的统一领导与领导骨干
- **文献依据**：「凡属正确的领导，必须是从群众中来，到群众中去。」——《关于领导方法的若干问题》
- **他怎么拿捏**：从群众中来到群众中去——群众提供分散的意见，领导「集中起来」化为系统的意见，再回到群众中检验、坚持。领导骨干与广大群众结合，集中领导与听取多数并存。既反对脱离群众的官僚主义，也反对不要领导的放任。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: the masses are the true source of strength and wisdom / yet centralized, unified leadership and leading cadres are still needed
- **Textual basis**: "In all correct leadership, it must come from the masses and go to the masses." — "Some Questions Concerning Methods of Leadership"
- **How he balanced them**: From the masses, to the masses — the masses supply scattered opinions, the leadership "concentrates" them into systematic views, then returns to the masses to test and uphold them. Leading cadres combine with the broad masses; centralized leadership coexists with listening to the majority. He opposed both bureaucracy divorced from the masses and laissez-faire that rejects leadership.

## 张力五：藐视 vs 重视 / Tension Five: Despise vs. Take Seriously

- **两端**：战略上藐视（整体上敢于斗争、敢于胜利）/ 战术上重视（具体问题上谨慎、一仗一仗打）
- **文献依据**：「一切反动派都是纸老虎。」——《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》
- **他怎么拿捏**：从本质、长期、战略上把困难看穿，建立敢斗争的信心；在具体、战术上把它当真老虎对待，一口口吃、一仗一仗打。藐视用来防止畏难退缩，重视用来防止轻敌冒进——同一对象在不同层面上的两种真实。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: strategically despise (dare to struggle and dare to win overall) / tactically take seriously (be cautious on concrete issues, fight battle by battle)
- **Textual basis**: "All reactionaries are paper tigers." — "Talk with the American Correspondent Anna Louise Strong"
- **How he balanced them**: In essence, over the long term, and strategically, see through the difficulty and build the confidence to struggle; concretely and tactically, treat it as a real tiger, eating it bite by bite, fighting battle by battle. Despising prevents timidity and retreat; taking it seriously prevents underestimating the enemy and rash advance — two truths about the same object at different levels.

## 张力六：原则 vs 灵活 / Tension Six: Principle vs. Flexibility

- **两端**：坚持原则（政治方向、独立性不动摇）/ 策略机动灵活（该让步的让步、讲求时机分寸）
- **文献依据**：「我们的方针是统一战线中的独立自主，既统一，又独立。」——《统一战线中的独立自主问题》
- **他怎么拿捏**：原则是底线、灵活是实现原则的手段——方向绝不动摇，具体策略讲究分寸（让步、先斩后奏、有理有利有节）。让步是「消极的步骤达到了积极的目的」，为的是守住原则。缺了灵活，原则会因僵化而守不住；缺了原则，灵活就滑成无底线。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: uphold principle (political direction and independence unshaken) / strategic flexibility (concede where concession is due, mind the timing and measure)
- **Textual basis**: "Our policy is independence and initiative within the united front, both unity and independence." — "The Question of Independence and Initiative Within the United Front"
- **How he balanced them**: Principle is the bottom line and flexibility is the means to realize principle — the direction never wavers, while concrete strategy minds its measure (concessions, acting first and reporting afterward, with justice, advantage, and restraint). A concession is "a negative step serving a positive purpose," made to hold the line of principle. Without flexibility, principle stiffens and cannot hold; without principle, flexibility slides into having no bottom line.

## 张力七：内线 vs 外线 / 防御 vs 进攻 / Tension Seven: Interior vs. Exterior Lines / Defense vs. Offense

- **两端**：战略上防御、内线、劣势（敌强我弱）/ 战役战斗上进攻、外线、集中优势（变被动为主动）
- **文献依据**：「我之战略作战上的内线和防御，在战役和战斗的作战上就变成了外线和进攻。」——《论持久战》
- **他怎么拿捏**：承认战略上的防御、内线、劣势是客观条件，但不消极挨打——通过战役战斗上的进攻、外线、集中优势兵力，把战略劣势转化为局部优势，积许多局部胜仗换全局主动。防御与进攻是同一场战争在战略层与战役层的分工，而非二选一。

**English:**

- **The two ends**: strategically on the defensive, interior lines, inferior (enemy strong, we weak) / operationally and tactically on the offensive, exterior lines, concentrating superiority (turning passivity into initiative)
- **Textual basis**: "Our interior lines and defensive strategy become exterior lines and the offensive in the campaigns and battles." — "On Protracted War"
- **How he balanced them**: He accepted strategic defense, interior lines, and inferiority as objective conditions, but did not passively take a beating — through offensive, exterior-line, concentrated-superiority operations at the campaign and battle level, he turned strategic inferiority into local superiority, accumulating many local victories to trade for overall initiative. Defense and offense are the division of labor between the strategic and operational levels of the same war, not an either-or choice.
