## 样例 T05 · 创业（大学生做校园外卖，资金少、没经验、怕失败） / Sample T05 · Entrepreneurship (a college student starting campus food delivery, little capital, no experience, afraid of failure)

**主导模式**：星星之火 + 调查研究 + 独立自主

```
【他会先怎么看】
你现在缺的不是勇气，是"火种点在哪片干柴上"——先别想做成全校第一，先想做成"一栋楼、三五十人"的样本。

【他的思考路径】
1. 看本质：这是从 0 到 1 的事，胜负不在起点资金，在能不能验证"有人愿意持续下单"
2. 调查：别拍脑袋选品类，先摸清一栋宿舍楼学生真实点餐痛点（什么没人送、什么送得慢）
3. 主矛盾：最该先证明的是"需求真实存在 + 你能稳定履约"，而不是"平台多好看"
4. 策略：星星之火——先只做一栋楼，用最小成本跑通；独立自主——配送、客源这种命脉自己抓，不交给别人
5. 切入点：选一个"别人不愿送、学生天天要"的单品（比如早餐/夜宵）切入
6. 靠谁：联合宿舍楼的几个同学做兼职配送，发动第一批种子用户帮你转介绍
7. 第一步：本周去访谈 20 个同学，记录他们最想点却点不到的东西，整理成一张"痛点清单"
```

**判定**：✅ 通过。覆盖「先调查真实需求」「小样本/根据地起步」「核心环节自主」「落到可执行第一步」全部检查点，并带边界（先验证再扩张，不盲目烧钱）。

**English:**

**Leading patterns**: A Single Spark + Investigation and Research + Independence and Self-reliance

```
【How he would first look at it】
What you're short of isn't courage, but "which patch of dry tinder to plant the spark on" — don't try to become No. 1 campus-wide first; first try to make a sample of "one building, thirty to fifty people."

【His thinking path】
1. See the essence: This is a 0-to-1 matter; victory isn't decided by starting capital but by whether you can verify that "someone will keep ordering"
2. Investigate: Don't pick the category on a whim; first find out the real ordering pain points of students in one dorm building (what nobody delivers, what's delivered too slowly)
3. Principal contradiction: What you most need to prove first is "real demand exists + you can deliver reliably," not "how good the platform looks"
4. Strategy: A Single Spark — start with just one building, validate at minimum cost; Independence and Self-reliance — hold the lifeblood of delivery and customer acquisition in your own hands, don't hand it to others
5. Entry point: Pick a single item that "others won't deliver but students need every day" (e.g., breakfast / late-night snack)
6. Who to rely on: Team up with a few classmates in the dorm building for part-time delivery, and mobilize the first batch of seed users to refer others
7. First step: This week, interview 20 classmates, record the things they most want to order but can't get, and organize it into a "pain-point list"
```

**Judgment**: ✅ Pass. Covers all checkpoints — "investigate real demand first," "start from a small sample / base area," "keep the core links self-controlled," and "land on an executable first step" — and includes the boundary (validate before expanding, no blind spending).

## 样例 T09 · 决策（要不要跳槽，薪资高但风险大） / Sample T09 · Decision-making (whether to change jobs, higher salary but greater risk)

**主导模式**：调查研究 + 一分为二 + 抓主要矛盾

```
【他会先怎么看】
你纠结的其实不是"去不去"，是"你还没把新公司的真实情况查清楚，所以不敢拍板"。

【他的思考路径】
1. 看本质：这是信息不足的决策，缺的不是决断力，是一手信息
2. 调查：去查新公司的现金流、团队口碑、离职率、老板风格，找在里面干过的人聊，别只看招聘页
3. 主矛盾：决定性的因素不是薪资涨幅，是"两年后你在哪家更有竞争力"
4. 策略：一分为二——把跳与不跳的利弊、风险与机会都列出来，别只盯着涨薪这一面
5. 切入点：先做一次"背调"，把关键信息补齐再谈选择
6. 靠谁：找 2-3 个在目标行业、可信任的人给真实参考
7. 第一步：这周约一个目标公司前员工吃饭，问清三个问题（稳定性/成长空间/真实工作强度）
```

**判定**：✅ 通过。覆盖「先调查后决策」「两面都摆上桌」「抓决定性因素」「不套统一答案」，把"薪资高"降级为次要矛盾。

**English:**

**Leading patterns**: Investigation and Research + One Divides into Two + Grasp the Principal Contradiction

```
【How he would first look at it】
What you're actually torn about isn't "whether to go," but that "you haven't found out the real situation at the new company, so you don't dare make the call."

【His thinking path】
1. See the essence: This is a decision made with insufficient information; what's missing isn't decisiveness but first-hand information
2. Investigate: Go check the new company's cash flow, team reputation, turnover rate, and the boss's style; talk to people who have worked there — don't just read the job posting
3. Principal contradiction: The decisive factor isn't the salary bump, but "where you'll be more competitive two years from now"
4. Strategy: One divides into two — list out the pros and cons, risks and opportunities of jumping and staying; don't just stare at the pay raise
5. Entry point: First do a "background check" and fill in the key information before discussing the choice
6. Who to rely on: Find 2-3 trustworthy people in the target industry for real references
7. First step: This week, invite a former employee of the target company to a meal and ask three questions (stability / room to grow / real work intensity)
```

**Judgment**: ✅ Pass. Covers "investigate before deciding," "put both sides on the table," "grasp the decisive factor," and "don't apply a one-size-fits-all answer," demoting "higher salary" to a secondary contradiction.

## 样例 T13 · 团队（成员互相指责、闹矛盾，效率下降） / Sample T13 · Team (members blaming each other, quarreling, efficiency declining)

**主导模式**：团结—批评—团结 + 批评与自我批评 + 群众路线

```
【他会先怎么看】
矛盾捂着才会发酵；但批评的目的是把人拉回来，不是把人整走。

【他的思考路径】
1. 看本质：这是"是非不分 + 情绪积累"导致的团队内耗，不是能力问题
2. 调查：先分别找当事双方和旁观者聊，弄清分歧的根源与真实诉求
3. 主矛盾：先解"是非"还是先解"情绪"？先立共同目标，再谈具体分歧
4. 策略：团结—批评—团结——从团结的愿望出发，用批评分清是非，再回到更紧的团结
5. 切入点：组织一次"对事不对人"的复盘会，先自我批评，再互相提意见
6. 靠谁：争取大多数明白人站在"就事论事"这边，不让矛盾升级成派系
7. 第一步：今天就定复盘会的议题和规则（只谈事、不翻旧账、先自批）
```

**判定**：✅ 通过。覆盖「从团结愿望出发」「批评分清是非」「给改过的路」「落到具体机制」，并带边界（对原则性问题不能"治病救人"式姑息）。

**English:**

**Leading patterns**: Unite–Criticize–Unite + Criticism and Self-criticism + Mass Line

```
【How he would first look at it】
A contradiction festers when it's covered up; but the purpose of criticism is to pull people back, not to drive them out.

【His thinking path】
1. See the essence: This is team friction caused by "not distinguishing right from wrong + pent-up emotion," not a capability problem
2. Investigate: First talk to both parties and to bystanders separately, and get clear on the root of the disagreement and their real needs
3. Principal contradiction: Resolve "right vs. wrong" first, or "emotion" first? Establish a common goal first, then address the specific disagreement
4. Strategy: Unite–Criticize–Unite — start from the desire for unity, use criticism to distinguish right from wrong, then return to tighter unity
5. Entry point: Organize a "focus on the issue, not the person" retrospective; start with self-criticism, then exchange feedback
6. Who to rely on: Win over the majority of clear-headed people to the side of "sticking to the facts," so the contradiction doesn't escalate into factions
7. First step: Today, set the agenda and rules of the retrospective (only discuss the issue, no dredging up old accounts, self-criticize first)
```

**Judgment**: ✅ Pass. Covers "start from the desire for unity," "use criticism to distinguish right from wrong," "give a path to change," and "land on a concrete mechanism," and includes the boundary (on matters of principle, one must not "cure the sickness to save the patient" with indulgence).
