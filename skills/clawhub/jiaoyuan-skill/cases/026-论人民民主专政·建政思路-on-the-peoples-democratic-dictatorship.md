# 论人民民主专政·建政思路 On the People's Democratic Dictatorship: The Approach to Founding the State

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四九年六月，人民解放战争已取得基本胜利，中国共产党即将在全国建立政权。建国前夕，需要回答一个根本问题：要建立一个什么样的国家。教员发表《论人民民主专政》，总结党二十八年的经验，系统阐述新国家的性质、各阶级地位和内外政策。

**English:**

In June 1949, the People's War of Liberation had already won a basic victory, and the Communist Party of China was about to establish political power throughout the country. On the eve of founding the state, a fundamental question had to be answered: what kind of country should be built. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) published "On the People's Democratic Dictatorship," summing up the Party's twenty-eight years of experience and systematically setting forth the nature of the new state, the position of the various classes, and its domestic and foreign policies.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他把二十八年经验归结为一句话：“总结我们的经验，集中到一点，就是工人阶级（经过共产党）领导的以工农联盟为基础的人民民主专政。这个专政必须和国际革命力量团结一致。这就是我们的公式，这就是我们的主要经验，这就是我们的主要纲领。”（《论人民民主专政》）他进一步界定这个国体的内涵：“对人民内部的民主方面和对反动派的专政方面，互相结合起来，就是人民民主专政。”（《论人民民主专政》）他明确必须由工人阶级领导、以工农联盟为基础，同时团结民族资产阶级。

**English:**

He summed up the twenty-eight years of experience in a single sentence: "To sum up our experience and concentrate it into one point, it is: the people's democratic dictatorship led by the working class (through the Communist Party) and based on the alliance of workers and peasants. This dictatorship must unite as one with the international revolutionary forces. This is our formula, our principal experience, and our main programme." (《论人民民主专政》 "On the People's Democratic Dictatorship") He further defined the substance of this state system: "Combine democracy for the people with dictatorship over the reactionaries, and the result is the people's democratic dictatorship." (《论人民民主专政》 "On the People's Democratic Dictatorship") He made clear that the state must be led by the working class and based on the alliance of workers and peasants, while also uniting with the national bourgeoisie.

## 行动 / The Action
他提出对外“一边倒”——倒向社会主义一边，联合世界上以平等待我的民族；对内明确对人民实行民主、对反动派实行专政，同时让被推翻的反动阶级在劳动中改造成为新人；提出严重的问题是教育农民，必须有步骤地解决国家工业化问题；号召学会自己不懂的东西，向一切内行的人学经济工作。

**English:**

In foreign policy he proposed "leaning to one side"—leaning to the side of socialism and uniting with the nations of the world that treat us as equals. In domestic policy he made clear that democracy was to be practiced for the people and dictatorship exercised over the reactionaries, while at the same time the overthrown reactionary classes were to be remolded through labor into new people. He raised the point that the serious problem was educating the peasants and that the question of national industrialization must be solved step by step. He called for learning what one does not know and for learning economic work from all who are expert in it.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这篇文章为即将建立的中华人民共和国明确了国体，成为新中国成立建政的基本理论依据。

**English:**

This article clarified the state system for the People's Republic of China that was about to be established, becoming the basic theoretical basis for the founding of the new China and its state-building.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 在组织或事业定型的关键节点，先把“我们要做什么、不做什么”的纲领说清楚——纲领明确，后续决策才有依据。
- 总结历史经验要能“集中到一点”：把一堆经历提炼成一句可执行的公式，比罗列过程更有指导力。
- 进入新阶段后，主动承认并去学自己不懂的东西——从“会打仗”到“会建设”，敢于清零、向内行请教。

**English:**

- At the key juncture when an organization or undertaking takes final shape, first spell out clearly the programme of "what we will do and what we will not do" — with a clear programme, subsequent decisions have a basis.
- Summing up historical experience must be able to "concentrate it into one point": distilling a pile of experiences into a single actionable formula is more instructive than enumerating the process.
- After entering a new stage, actively admit and learn what you don't yet know — from "knowing how to fight" to "knowing how to build," be willing to start from zero and consult the experts.
