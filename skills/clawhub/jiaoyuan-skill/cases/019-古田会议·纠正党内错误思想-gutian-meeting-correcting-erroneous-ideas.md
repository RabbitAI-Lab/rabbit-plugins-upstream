# 古田会议·纠正党内错误思想 The Gutian Meeting: Correcting Erroneous Ideas in the Party

## 局势 / The Situation
一九二九年十二月，红军第四军在闽西古田召开第九次代表大会。红军建军两年多来，因党员成分大部分是农民和其他小资产阶级出身，加之一部分俘虏兵带来旧军队习气，党内滋生出单纯军事观点、极端民主化、绝对平均主义、主观主义、个人主义、流寇思想等各种非无产阶级思想，妨碍正确路线的执行。

**English:**

In December 1929, the Fourth Red Army held its Ninth Party Congress at Gutian in western Fujian. In the more than two years since the Red Army was founded, because most of its Party members came from peasant or other petty-bourgeois backgrounds and because some captured soldiers had brought with them the habits of the old armies, various non-proletarian ideas had grown up within the Party—the purely military viewpoint, ultra-democracy, absolute equalitarianism, subjectivism, individualism, and the mentality of roving rebels—impeding the implementation of the correct line.

## 判断 / The Assessment
教员在决议中首先点明问题的严重性：“红军第四军的共产党内存在着各种非无产阶级的思想，这对于执行党的正确路线，妨碍极大。若不彻底纠正，则中国伟大革命斗争给予红军第四军的任务，是必然担负不起来的。”（《关于纠正党内的错误思想》）他分析这些错误思想的来源，一是组织基础大部分由农民和小资产阶级出身的成分构成，二是党的领导机关缺乏一致的坚决斗争和对党员的正确路线教育。他由此确立了从思想上、教育上纠正错误思想的建党思路，并明确红军的性质：“中国的红军是一个执行革命的政治任务的武装集团。”（《关于纠正党内的错误思想》）

**English:**

In the resolution, Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) first pointed out the gravity of the problem: "Within the Communist Party of the Fourth Red Army there exist various non-proletarian ideas, which greatly hinder the implementation of the Party's correct line. If these are not thoroughly corrected, the Fourth Red Army will inevitably fail to shoulder the tasks assigned to it by the great revolutionary struggle in China." (《关于纠正党内的错误思想》 "On Correcting Mistaken Ideas in the Party") He analyzed the source of these erroneous ideas: first, the organizational base was composed largely of elements of peasant and petty-bourgeois origin, and second, the Party's leading organs lacked a consistent, resolute struggle against them and adequate education of Party members in the correct line. On this basis, he established the approach of correcting erroneous ideas through ideological work and education, and defined the nature of the Red Army: "China's Red Army is an armed body for carrying out the political tasks of the revolution." (《关于纠正党内的错误思想》 "On Correcting Mistaken Ideas in the Party")

## 行动 / The Action
针对每种错误思想，逐条列出表现、来源和纠正方法：从教育上提高党内政治水平，加紧官兵政治训练；在组织上厉行集中指导下的民主生活；教育党员用马克思列宁主义的方法分析形势，注意社会经济的调查研究；党内批评防止主观武断，说话要有证据、批评要注意政治；通过改善物质生活、纠正分配不当等办法消除消极情绪。

**English:**

For each kind of erroneous idea, he listed its manifestations, its source, and the method of correction: raise the Party's political level through education and intensify political training for officers and men; in organization, strictly practice democratic life under centralized guidance; educate Party members to analyze the situation with the methods of Marxism-Leninism and to pay attention to social and economic investigation; guard against subjectivism and arbitrariness in inner-Party criticism, so that statements are backed by evidence and criticism is concerned with politics; and eliminate negative feelings by improving material conditions and correcting inequities in distribution.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这个决议总结了红军建设的经验，使红军肃清旧式军队的影响，建立在马克思列宁主义的基础上，后来各部分红军先后照此执行，使整个红军成为真正的人民军队，其基本路线长期延续。

**English:**

This resolution summed up the experience of building the Red Army, purged it of the influence of the old-style armies, and placed it on a Marxist-Leninist foundation. Later, the various Red Army units acted on it one after another, so that the entire Red Army became a genuinely people's army, and its basic line endured for a long time.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 组织出问题，先查思想根源——错误行为背后往往是错误思想，靠制度压一时、靠教育改长久，二者要结合。
- 先明确“这个组织是干什么的”，再谈怎么干——把组织性质和使命说清楚，才能纠正偏离方向的种种表现。
- 批评要讲证据、注意政治，反对无原则纠纷和人身攻击——组织内的纠错机制要指向“改进工作”而非“攻击个人”。

**English:**

- When an organization runs into trouble, first look for the ideological root — behind wrong behavior there is usually wrong thinking; systems can suppress for a while, but education corrects for the long term, and the two must be combined.
- First make clear "what this organization is for," then discuss how to do it — only by spelling out the organization's nature and mission can the various deviations from its direction be corrected.
- Criticism must be grounded in evidence and concerned with politics, opposing unprincipled disputes and personal attacks — an organization's mechanism for correction should point toward "improving the work," not "attacking individuals."
