# 暂时放弃延安·不计一城一地得失 Temporarily Abandoning Yan'an: Not Counting Gains and Losses of a Single City or Place

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四七年三月，国民党军对陕北和山东实行重点进攻。进攻陕甘宁边区的国民党军兵力约二十五万人，而西北战场人民解放军在陕甘宁边区的部队只有两万多人。兵力对比悬殊，中共中央决定主动撤出延安，用运动战与敌周旋。

**English:**

In March 1947, the Kuomintang army launched key offensives against northern Shaanxi and Shandong. The Kuomintang forces attacking the Shaanxi-Gansu-Ningxia Border Region numbered about 250,000, while the People's Liberation Army forces in the Northwest theater within the border region numbered only a little over 20,000. With such a disparity of strength, the Central Committee of the Communist Party decided to withdraw from Yan'an voluntarily and maneuver against the enemy with mobile warfare.

## 判断 / The Assessment
不计较一城一地的得失，把保存有生力量、消灭敌人放在第一位。延安虽是中共中央所在地，但被占领并不动摇大局："蒋介石军队在被我歼灭了三十五个旅之后，在其进攻能力快要枯竭之时，即使用突袭方法，占领延安，亦无损于人民解放战争胜利的大局，挽救不了蒋介石灭亡的前途。"（《中共中央关于暂时放弃延安和保卫陕甘宁边区的两个文件》）在西北战场的打法上，提出了拖垮敌人的"蘑菇"战术："这种办法叫"蘑菇"战术，将敌磨得精疲力竭，然后消灭之。"（《关于西北战场的作战方针》）

**English:**

Do not count the gains and losses of a single city or place; put the preservation of effective strength and the destruction of the enemy first. Although Yan'an was the seat of the CPC Central Committee, its occupation would not shake the overall situation: "After thirty-five of his brigades have been wiped out by us, and when his offensive capacity is about to be exhausted, even if Chiang Kai-shek's army should seize Yan'an by a surprise attack, it would not harm the overall situation of the victorious People's War of Liberation, nor save Chiang Kai-shek from his doom" (《中共中央关于暂时放弃延安和保卫陕甘宁边区的两个文件》, Two Documents of the CPC Central Committee on Temporarily Abandoning Yan'an and Defending the Shaanxi-Gansu-Ningxia Border Region). On the fighting method for the Northwest theater, he put forward the "mushroom" tactic of wearing the enemy down: "This method is called the 'mushroom' tactic, wearing the enemy down to exhaustion and then destroying them" (《关于西北战场的作战方针》, Operational Principles for the Northwest Theater).

## 行动 / The Action
主动撤出延安，放弃县城，用"蘑菇"战术与敌周旋，使敌疲劳缺粮；教员和中共中央留在陕北，继续指挥全国和西北战场的作战。

**English:**

Yan'an was evacuated voluntarily and the county towns given up, while the "mushroom" tactic was used to maneuver against the enemy, exhausting them and starving them of supplies; Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) and the CPC Central Committee stayed in northern Shaanxi and continued to direct operations across the whole country and in the Northwest theater.

## 结果 / The Outcome
国民党军虽先后占领延安和陕甘宁边区所有县城，却既未消灭中共中央首脑机关，也未把解放军赶出边区，反而受到多次沉重打击，损失兵力约十万人，最后不得不逃出边区。

**English:**

Although the Kuomintang army successively occupied Yan'an and all the county towns of the Shaanxi-Gansu-Ningxia Border Region, it neither destroyed the leading organs of the CPC Central Committee nor drove the Liberation Army out of the border region; instead it suffered repeated heavy blows, lost about 100,000 troops, and was finally forced to flee the border region.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当"守阵地"与"保实力"冲突时，借鉴"不计一城一地得失"：分清什么是根本（核心能力、有生力量、关键人才），什么是可舍弃的（面子、非核心地盘），宁丢面子不失里子。
2. 面对强敌的进攻，借鉴"蘑菇战术"：不与强敌硬拼，而是用周旋、拖延消耗对方，待其疲惫、资源耗尽，再抓住时机反击。
3. 领导者身处困境时不撤退，借鉴"留在陕北"的示范：关键时刻决策者留在现场稳定人心，比远距离遥控更有力量。

**English:**

1. When "holding ground" conflicts with "preserving strength," borrow "not counting gains and losses of a single city or place": distinguish what is fundamental (core capabilities, effective strength, key talent) from what can be given up (face, non-core territory); rather lose face than lose substance.
2. Facing a powerful enemy's attack, borrow the "mushroom tactic": do not fight the strong enemy head-on; use maneuvering and delay to wear them down, and when they are exhausted and their resources spent, seize the moment to counter-attack.
3. When a leader is in a difficult situation and does not retreat, borrow the example of "staying in northern Shaanxi": at a critical moment, the decision-maker stays on the spot to steady people's morale, which is more powerful than remote control from afar.
