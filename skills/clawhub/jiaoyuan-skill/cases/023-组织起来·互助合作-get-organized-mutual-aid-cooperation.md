# 组织起来·互助合作 Get Organized: Mutual Aid and Cooperation

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四三年，陕甘宁边区和各抗日根据地处于敌后战争的困难环境，农民几千年来都是个体经济、一家一户一个生产单位，生产分散、力量单薄。边区一年的生产运动证明，把分散的劳动力组织起来能带来明显的增产。教员在中共中央招待陕甘宁边区劳动英雄大会上讲话，把这一年的经验概括为“组织起来”。

**English:**

In 1943, the Shaanxi-Gansu-Ningxia Border Region and the other anti-Japanese base areas were in the difficult environment of war behind enemy lines. For thousands of years the peasants had practiced an individual economy, each household a single unit of production—scattered and weak. A year of the border region's production campaign had proved that organizing scattered labor could bring a marked increase in output. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) spoke at a reception held by the Central Committee for the labor heroes of the border region, summing up that year's experience in the phrase "get organized."

## 判断 / The Assessment
他明确把组织群众作为根本方针：“把群众力量组织起来，这是一种方针。”（《组织起来》）他判断，把分散的群众组织成集体力量，是摆脱穷苦、取得胜利的根本出路：“我们有了人民群众的这四种合作社，和部队机关学校集体劳动的合作社，我们就可以把群众的力量组织成为一支劳动大军。这是人民群众得到解放的必由之路，由穷苦变富裕的必由之路，也是抗战胜利的必由之路。”（《组织起来》）他同时强调必须依靠群众自愿，决不能强迫。

**English:**

He clearly made organizing the masses a fundamental policy: "To organize the strength of the masses is a policy." (《组织起来》 "Get Organized") He judged that organizing scattered masses into a collective force was the fundamental way out of poverty and toward victory: "With these four kinds of cooperatives of the masses and the cooperatives of collective labor of the troops, government organs, and schools, we can organize the strength of the masses into a great labor army. This is the only road to the liberation of the masses, the only road from poverty to prosperity, and the only road to victory in the War of Resistance." (《组织起来》 "Get Organized") At the same time he stressed that everything must depend on the voluntary action of the masses and that there must be no coercion.

## 行动 / The Action
他号召把一切老百姓的力量、一切部队机关学校的力量、一切男女老少的全劳动力半劳动力都动员起来、组织起来；推行变工队、扎工队等农业劳动互助组织和生产、消费、运输、信用等合作社；军队实行“一面打仗，一面生产”；提倡向群众学习、帮助群众组织生产，反对只向群众要东西的官僚主义作风。

**English:**

He called for mobilizing and organizing the strength of all the common people, of all troops, organs, and schools, and of all the able-bodied and semi-able-bodied men and women, old and young; for promoting mutual-aid organizations of agricultural labor such as labor-exchange teams and work teams, and cooperatives for production, consumption, transport, and credit; for the army to "fight on one side and produce on the other"; and for learning from the masses and helping them organize production, while opposing the bureaucratic style of only taking from the masses.

## 结果 / The Outcome
边区通过组织互助合作，军队和机关学校大都实现了生产自给，军民都达到丰衣足食，边区生产走上轨道，涌现出大批劳动英雄，互助合作的经验在敌后各根据地逐步推广。

**English:**

Through mutual aid and cooperation, the army and most organs and schools in the border region achieved self-sufficiency in production, army and people alike attained ample food and clothing, production got onto a proper track, large numbers of labor heroes emerged, and the experience of mutual aid and cooperation was gradually extended to the base areas behind enemy lines.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 单打独斗力量分散，把散的力量组织起来、形成合力，是突破资源瓶颈的关键——协作、平台、网络化往往是放大个体效能的路。
- 组织群众靠自愿和互利，不能强迫——合作机制要让人看到“组织起来”比单干强，靠实效吸引，不靠命令压。
- 做领导工作先把“帮助群众解决实际问题”放在前面（用九成精力帮群众发展），而不是只想着向群众索取——先予后取，关系才长久。

**English:**

- Going it alone scatters your strength; organizing scattered forces into a combined effort is the key to breaking through resource bottlenecks — collaboration, platforms, and networking are often the way to multiply individual effectiveness.
- Organizing people depends on voluntariness and mutual benefit, not coercion — the mechanism of cooperation must show people that "getting organized" beats working alone, attracting them by real results rather than forcing them by command.
- In leadership, put "helping people solve their practical problems" first (devote nine-tenths of your energy to helping them develop) rather than only thinking of what to extract from them — give before you take, and the relationship will last.
