# 反对本本主义·没有调查没有发言权 Oppose Book Worship: No Investigation, No Right to Speak

## 局势 / The Situation
一九三〇年前后，红军中盛行一种教条主义——当时叫“本本主义”：遇到问题不是先去了解实际情况，而是开口闭口“拿本本来”，盲目地表面上完全无异议地执行上级指示；做领导工作的人遇到困难问题，只是叹气、请求调动。教员为反对这种作风而写此文，提出“没有调查，没有发言权”。

**English:**

Around 1930, a form of dogmatism—then called "book worship"—was prevalent in the Red Army: when a problem arose, instead of first learning the actual situation, people would say "bring out the book," and would carry out instructions from above blindly, superficially, and without the slightest objection; those doing leadership work, when they met with difficulties, would merely sigh and ask for a transfer. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) wrote this essay to oppose such a style of work, putting forward the principle "No investigation, no right to speak."

## 判断 / The Assessment
他把发言权建立在调查之上，把不调查就下判断斥为瞎说：“没有调查，没有发言权”（《反对本本主义》）并指出调查与解决问题的关系：“一切结论产生于调查情况的末尾，而不是在它的先头。”（《反对本本主义》）他进一步把调查说成是解决问题的前提：“调查就像“十月怀胎”，解决问题就像“一朝分娩”。调查就是解决问题。”（《反对本本主义》）同时强调本本必须同实际结合：“马克思主义的“本本”是要学习的，但是必须同我国的实际情况相结合。”

**English:**

He grounded the right to speak in investigation and denounced judging without investigation as nonsense: "No investigation, no right to speak." (《反对本本主义》 "Oppose Book Worship") He also pointed out the relationship between investigation and solving problems: "All conclusions come after investigation, and not before it." (《反对本本主义》 "Oppose Book Worship") He further described investigation as the prerequisite for solving problems: "Investigation may be likened to the long months of pregnancy, and solving a problem to the day of birth. To investigate a problem is to solve it." (《反对本本主义》 "Oppose Book Worship") At the same time, he stressed that books must be combined with reality: "It is necessary to study the Marxist 'books,' but this study must be combined with the actual conditions of our country."

## 行动 / The Action
他要求做领导工作的人亲身出马作实际调查，或召集明了情况的人开调查会；提出开调查会、定调查纲目、亲身出马、要深入、自己做记录等一套调查技术；强调调查的目的是明了社会各阶级的状况，得到正确的阶级估量，再据此定出正确的斗争策略。

**English:**

He required those doing leadership work to go out personally to make investigations, or to convene investigation meetings with people who knew the situation well. He laid out a set of investigation techniques—holding investigation meetings, drawing up an outline, going in person, going deeply, and taking notes oneself—and stressed that the purpose of investigation was to understand the conditions of the various social classes, arrive at a correct estimate of class forces, and then work out correct tactics for struggle on that basis.

## 结果 / The Outcome
“没有调查，没有发言权”成为此后党的一个重要工作方法，反对脱离实际的本本主义、强调实际调查，纠正了红军中的形式主义与瞎说作风。

**English:**

"No investigation, no right to speak" became an important working method of the Party thereafter; opposing book worship divorced from reality and stressing on-the-ground investigation corrected the formalism and loose talk in the Red Army.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 没摸清事实之前，不轻易下判断、不给方案——先调查问题现状和历史，结论从材料里来。
- 调查本身就是解决问题的一部分：把问题的“来源”找到、“现状”弄明白，办法往往就浮现了。
- 权威、书本、上级意见都不能替代实际调查——再好的理论和指示，也要同自己的实际情况结合后再执行。

**English:**

- Before the facts are clear, don't rush to judgment or hand out solutions — first investigate the present state and history of the problem; conclusions come from the material.
- Investigation is itself part of solving the problem: once the "source" of the problem is found and its "present state" understood, the way forward often emerges.
- Authority, books, and instructions from above cannot replace actual investigation — however good a theory or directive, it must be combined with your own actual conditions before being carried out.
