# 纸老虎·战略藐视战术重视 Paper Tiger: Despise Strategically, Take Seriously Tactically

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四六年八月，全面内战爆发不久，美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗到延安采访。当时美国在支持蒋介石打内战，一些人恐惧美国、恐惧原子弹，对中国革命的前途产生悲观情绪。

**English:**

In August 1946, not long after the full-scale civil war broke out, the American correspondent Anna Louise Strong came to Yan'an for an interview. At the time the United States was backing Chiang Kai-shek in the civil war, and some people, fearing the United States and fearing the atom bomb, fell into pessimism about the future of the Chinese revolution.

## 判断 / The Assessment
从本质和长远看，帝国主义和一切反动派都是外强中干的纸老虎："一切反动派都是纸老虎。看起来，反动派的样子是可怕的，但是实际上并没有什么了不起的力量。"（《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》）对原子弹的恐惧也被点破："原子弹是美国反动派用来吓人的一只纸老虎，看样子可怕，实际上并不可怕。"（《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》）对力量对比，他看得很透："我们所依靠的不过是小米加步枪，但是历史最后将证明，这小米加步枪比蒋介石的飞机加坦克还要强些。"（《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》）据此形成一套战略思想：在战略上、全体上藐视敌人，敢于斗争、敢于胜利；在战术上、每一个局部和具体斗争问题上又重视敌人，谨慎对待、讲究斗争艺术。

**English:**

In essence and in the long run, imperialism and all reactionaries are paper tigers—strong in appearance but weak in reality: "All reactionaries are paper tigers. In appearance, the reactionaries are terrifying, but in reality they are not so powerful" (《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》, Talk with the American Correspondent Anna Louise Strong). The fear of the atom bomb was likewise punctured: "The atom bomb is a paper tiger which the U.S. reactionaries use to scare people. It looks terrible, but in fact it isn't" (《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》). He saw the balance of forces clearly: "What we rely on is only millet plus rifles, but history will finally prove that this millet plus rifles is more powerful than Chiang Kai-shek's airplanes plus tanks" (《和美国记者安娜·路易斯·斯特朗的谈话》). From this he formed a complete strategic outlook: strategically and as a whole, despise the enemy, dare to struggle and dare to win; tactically, in every particular and concrete question of struggle, take the enemy seriously, treat them cautiously, and attend to the art of struggle.

## 行动 / The Action
用"一切反动派都是纸老虎"的论断武装人民的思想，增强必胜信心；同时在具体斗争中保持谨慎，采取适当的斗争形式，一步一步孤立和消灭敌人。

**English:**

The thesis that "all reactionaries are paper tigers" was used to arm the people's thinking and strengthen their confidence in victory; at the same time, caution was maintained in concrete struggles, appropriate forms of struggle were adopted, and the enemy was isolated and destroyed step by step.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这一论断武装了中国人民的思想，加强了人民的胜利信心，在人民解放战争中起了巨大的作用。

**English:**

This thesis armed the thinking of the Chinese people, strengthened their confidence in victory, and played an enormous role in the People's War of Liberation.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 面对强大对手的威慑时，借鉴"战略藐视"：在整体判断上不畏惧、不被吓倒，相信长期力量对比的走向，敢于斗争、敢于夺取胜利。
2. 借鉴"战术重视"：在每一个具体问题上把对手当回事，谨慎对待、讲究方法，不因战略上的藐视而在执行上轻敌。
3. 区分"长期"与"短期"：从长远看力量在于人民（用户、趋势、基本面），从短期看要尊重眼前实力差距——不把长期判断直接当作短期行动的依据。

**English:**

1. When facing the deterrence of a powerful opponent, borrow "strategic contempt": in the overall judgment, do not fear or be cowed; believe in the long-term direction of the balance of forces, dare to struggle and dare to win.
2. Borrow "tactical seriousness": on every concrete question, take the opponent seriously, treat them cautiously, and attend to method; do not, because of strategic contempt, underestimate the enemy in execution.
3. Distinguish "long-term" from "short-term": in the long run, strength lies in the people (users, trends, fundamentals); in the short run, respect the present gap in strength—do not treat the long-term judgment directly as the basis for short-term action.
