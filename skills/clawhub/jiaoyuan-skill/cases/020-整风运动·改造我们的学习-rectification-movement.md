# 整风运动·改造我们的学习 The Rectification Movement: Reform Our Study

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四一年，抗日战争进入相持阶段，中国共产党已走过二十年，但党内长期存在着主观主义、宗派主义和党八股的作风：不注重研究现状，不注重研究历史，不注重马克思列宁主义的应用。许多同志学习马列主义只是为了单纯地学理论，只会片面引用个别词句，不会用其立场、观点、方法去分析解决中国的实际问题。教员在延安干部会上作报告，号召改造全党的学习方法和学习制度。

**English:**

In 1941, the War of Resistance Against Japan entered the stage of strategic stalemate. The Communist Party of China had by then existed for twenty years, yet within the Party there long persisted the styles of subjectivism, sectarianism, and stereotyped Party writing: no attention to studying the present situation, no attention to studying history, and no attention to the application of Marxism-Leninism. Many comrades studied Marxism-Leninism only in order to learn theory in the abstract, quoting isolated words and phrases one-sidedly, without using its stand, viewpoint, and method to analyze and solve China's practical problems. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) delivered a report at a cadres' meeting in Yan'an, calling for a reform of the Party's methods and system of study.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他对照两种态度，否定主观主义、肯定马克思列宁主义的态度，把后者概括为“实事求是”：““实事”就是客观存在着的一切事物，“是”就是客观事物的内部联系，即规律性，“求”就是我们去研究。”（《改造我们的学习》）他主张学习理论必须联系实际、有目的地去用：“这种态度，就是有的放矢的态度。“的”就是中国革命，“矢”就是马克思列宁主义。”（《改造我们的学习》）他由此提出，共产党领导机关的基本任务就是了解情况和掌握政策两件大事。

**English:**

He contrasted the two attitudes, rejecting subjectivism and affirming the Marxist-Leninist attitude, which he summed up as "seeking truth from facts": "'Facts' are all the things that exist objectively; 'truth' means their internal relations, that is, the laws governing them; and 'to seek' means to study." (《改造我们的学习》 "Reform Our Study") He held that studying theory must be linked with reality and applied with a purpose: "This attitude is the attitude of 'shooting the arrow at the target.' The 'target' is the Chinese revolution, and the 'arrow' is Marxism-Leninism." (《改造我们的学习》 "Reform Our Study") From this he concluded that the basic task of the Communist Party's leading organs is two great matters: understanding the situation and mastering policy.

## 行动 / The Action
他提议在全党系统地周密地研究周围环境，引导同志做实际事物的调查研究；对近百年的中国史聚集人材、分工合作地研究；在职干部教育和干部学校教育确立以研究中国革命实际问题为中心、以马克思列宁主义基本原则为指导的方针，废除静止地孤立地研究马列主义的方法；以此为整风运动确立思想原则。

**English:**

He proposed that the whole Party systematically and thoroughly study its surrounding environment and guide comrades in investigating actual conditions; that talents be assembled and the work divided to study the Chinese history of the past hundred years; and that education for cadres in service and in cadre schools be oriented around the study of the practical problems of the Chinese revolution, guided by the fundamental principles of Marxism-Leninism, abolishing the method of studying Marxism-Leninism in a static and isolated way. These became the ideological principles for the Rectification Movement.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这个号召在党内党外引起大讨论，巩固了马克思列宁主义思想阵地，使广大干部在思想上大大提高一步，党达到了空前的团结，为整风运动的开展和实事求是思想路线的确立打下基础。

**English:**

This call sparked wide discussion inside and outside the Party, consolidated the Marxist-Leninist ideological position, and greatly raised the ideological level of the broad ranks of cadres. The Party achieved an unprecedented degree of unity, laying the foundation for the unfolding of the Rectification Movement and the establishment of the ideological line of seeking truth from facts.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 学任何理论、方法、框架，都要落到“解决自己的实际问题”上——学而不用、学而不会用，等于没学。
- 把“实事求是”拆成三步：先承认客观事实（实事），再找事实内部的规律（是），然后下功夫去研究（求）——先有事实，再谈结论。
- 警惕“无实事求是之意，有哗众取宠之心”的作风——做决策、写方案要内容扎实，反对堆砌名词和空话。

**English:**

- Whatever theory, method, or framework you study, it must land on "solving your own practical problems" — learning without using, or learning without knowing how to use, amounts to not learning at all.
- Break "seeking truth from facts" into three steps: first accept the objective facts ("facts"), then find the internal laws of those facts ("truth"), and then put in the effort to study ("seek") — facts first, conclusions second.
- Guard against a style that "lacks the intention of seeking truth from facts but has the desire to impress the crowd" — in decision-making and proposals, the substance must be solid; reject the piling up of jargon and empty phrases.
