# 党委会的工作方法·十二条 Methods of Work of Party Committees: The Twelve Rules

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四九年三月，全国胜利在望，党的各级委员会要领导更大的地区、担负更繁重的任务，从打仗转入建设和执政。教员在中国共产党七届二中全会上作结论，专门讲党委会的工作方法，把领导班子怎么开好会、怎么决策、怎么抓工作讲成十二条具体方法。

**English:**

In March 1949, with nationwide victory in sight, the Party committees at all levels were to lead larger areas and shoulder more onerous tasks, shifting from fighting to construction and governance. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) made the concluding speech at the Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, devoted specifically to the methods of work of Party committees, laying out twelve concrete methods for how a leading body should run its meetings, make decisions, and grasp its work.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他提出领导要抓中心又要统筹全局：“党委要抓紧中心工作，又要围绕中心工作而同时开展其他方面的工作。”（《党委会的工作方法》）他强调抓工作必须抓实：“抓而不紧，等于不抓。”（《党委会的工作方法》）他还提出胸中要有“数”，注意事物的数量方面，主张把问题摆到桌面上来、互通情报、力戒骄傲、划清成绩和缺点的界限等，形成一整套领导班子的工作方法。

**English:**

He held that leadership must grasp the central work while taking the whole into account: "A Party committee should keep a firm grasp on its central task and at the same time, around the central task, unfold the work in other fields." (《党委会的工作方法》 "Methods of Work of Party Committees") He stressed that grasping the work must be done firmly: "Not to grasp firmly is not to grasp at all." (《党委会的工作方法》 "Methods of Work of Party Committees") He also urged leaders to keep "figures" in mind and attend to the quantitative aspect of things, to put problems on the table, to exchange information, to guard against arrogance, and to draw clear lines between achievements and shortcomings—forming a complete set of working methods for leading bodies.

## 行动 / The Action
他把党委会工作方法归纳为十二条：善于当“班长”；把问题摆到桌面上来；互通情报；不懂就问下级；学会“弹钢琴”；要“抓紧”；胸中有“数”；出“安民告示”（开会事先通知）；精兵简政（讲话文章简明扼要）；注意团结和自己意见不同的同志一道工作；力戒骄傲；划清两种界限。要求各地照此把党委领导工作提高一步。

**English:**

He summed up the methods of work of Party committees in twelve points: be good at being a "squad leader"; put problems on the table; exchange information; ask subordinates when you don't know; learn to "play the piano"; "grasp firmly"; keep "figures" in mind; issue "notices to reassure the public" (notify people in advance of meetings); practice "better troops and simpler administration" (be concise in speeches and writing); be careful to unite with comrades who hold different opinions; guard against arrogance; and draw the two lines of distinction. He asked all localities to raise the standard of Party committee leadership accordingly.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这十二条成为此后党委会开展工作的基本方法，指导领导班子学会抓中心、抓节奏、抓数量、抓团结，把领导工作做扎实。

**English:**

These twelve points became the basic methods for the work of Party committees thereafter, guiding leading bodies in grasping the center, the rhythm, the numbers, and unity, and in doing their leadership work solidly.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 领导要会“弹钢琴”：抓主要任务，同时兼顾其他方面，十个指头有节奏地配合——带团队、管项目不能只盯一件事丢掉全局。
- “抓而不紧，等于不抓”：重要的事要持续盯、盯到底，半抓不抓等于白抓——关键任务要有跟进和闭环。
- 决策要看数量、做基本统计：“胸中有数”再拍板，别凭感觉——任何质量都表现为一定的数量。

**English:**

- A leader must be able to "play the piano": grasp the main task while giving due attention to other aspects, with all ten fingers working in rhythm — in leading a team or managing a project, don't fixate on one thing and lose sight of the whole.
- "Not to grasp firmly is not to grasp at all": important matters must be watched continuously and followed through to the end; half-hearted attention is as good as none — key tasks need follow-up and a closed loop.
- Decision-making must attend to numbers and basic statistics: "keep figures in mind" before deciding, and don't go by feel — every quality manifests itself through a certain quantity.
