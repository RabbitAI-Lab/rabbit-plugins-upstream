# 抗日游击战争·内线外线 Guerrilla Warfare Against Japan: Interior and Exterior Lines

## 局势 / The Situation
抗日战争中，中国是一个大而弱的国家，日本是一个小而强的国家；正规战争是主要的，游击战争是辅助的。但由于敌强我弱、敌小我大，敌人在中国占地甚广而兵力不足，在占领区留下大量空虚地带，加上中国有共产党领导的军队和广大人民，抗日游击战争因此具有了战略地位，不能只当作战术问题看待。

**English:**

In the War of Resistance Against Japan, China was a large but weak country while Japan was a small but strong one; regular warfare was primary and guerrilla warfare supplementary. But because the enemy was strong while we were weak, and the enemy was small while we were large, the enemy occupied vast areas of China with insufficient troops, leaving many empty zones in the occupied territories; combined with China's army led by the Communist Party and its vast masses, guerrilla warfare against Japan thereby acquired strategic standing and could no longer be treated as a purely tactical matter.

## 判断 / The Assessment
把游击战争从战术问题提升到战略问题来考察。核心是看清整体不利、局部可为的辩证关系："能够而且必须在战略的防御战之中采取战役和战斗的进攻战，在战略的持久战之中采取战役和战斗的速决战，在战略的内线作战之中采取战役和战斗的外线作战。"（《抗日游击战争的战略问题》）这一套方针，就是主动地、灵活地、有计划地执行防御中的进攻战、持久中的速决战、内线作战中的外线作战。

**English:**

He elevated guerrilla warfare from a tactical question to a strategic one. The core was to grasp the dialectical relationship between an unfavorable whole and a favorable part: "We can and must adopt offensive operations in campaigns and battles within the strategic defensive, quick-decision operations in campaigns and battles within the strategically protracted war, and exterior-line operations in campaigns and battles within interior-line operations on the strategic level" (《抗日游击战争的战略问题》, Problems of Strategy in Guerrilla War Against Japan). This set of principles meant actively, flexibly, and systematically carrying out offensive warfare within the defensive, quick-decision warfare within the protracted, and exterior-line operations within interior-line operations.

## 行动 / The Action
把游击战争作为战略问题来规划和部署：在敌后建立根据地，主动、灵活、有计划地开展游击战，集中大力打敌小部，并逐步向运动战发展。

**English:**

Guerrilla warfare was planned and deployed as a strategic question: base areas were established behind enemy lines, guerrilla warfare was carried out actively, flexibly, and systematically, forces were concentrated to strike at small detachments of the enemy, and the guerrilla war gradually developed into mobile warfare.

## 结果 / The Outcome
敌后游击战争广泛发展，成为支撑持久抗战的重要力量，并在战争中逐步向运动战提升，改变了敌我力量对比的局部格局。

**English:**

Guerrilla warfare behind enemy lines developed widely, becoming an important force sustaining the protracted War of Resistance, and progressively elevated itself into mobile warfare in the course of the war, changing the local pattern of the balance of forces between the enemy and ourselves.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当整体处于弱势、被动地位（小团队对大公司、后来者对领先者）时，借鉴"内线中的外线"：整体态势被动，但在局部、具体环节主动出击，用局部的进攻性换取全局的生存和发展空间。
2. 当面对"总体持久、资源有限"的处境时，借鉴"持久中的速决"：长线布局，但每一个具体动作要快、要迅速见效，避免战线拉长、久拖不决。
3. 当看似"只能防守"时，借鉴"防御中的进攻"：防御不等于被动挨打，而是要在防守中主动寻找进攻机会，用局部的主动扭转整体的被动。

**English:**

1. When you are in a weak and passive position overall (a small team against a big company, a latecomer against an incumbent), borrow "exterior lines within interior lines": the overall situation is passive, but on the local and concrete level you take the initiative to attack, using local offensiveness to win space for survival and development in the whole.
2. When facing a situation of "protracted overall, limited resources," borrow "quick decision within the protracted": plan for the long term, but make each concrete move fast and quick to yield results, avoiding a drawn-out front and endless delays.
3. When it seems you "can only defend," borrow "offense within defense": defense does not mean passively taking blows; actively look for opportunities to attack while defending, and use local initiative to reverse overall passivity.
