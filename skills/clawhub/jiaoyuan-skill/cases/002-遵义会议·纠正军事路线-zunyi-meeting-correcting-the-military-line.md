# 遵义会议·纠正军事路线 The Zunyi Meeting: Correcting the Military Line

## 局势 / The Situation
第五次反"围剿"失败后，红军被迫长征，处于最危急的关头。此前从一九三一年初到一九三四年底，"左"倾机会主义路线在军事指挥上犯了严重的错误，使土地革命战争遭受极端严重的损失，红军未能战胜敌人的第五次"围剿"，反而丧失了根据地。

**English:**

After the failure of the fifth counter-"encirclement and suppression" campaign, the Red Army was forced onto the Long March and stood at its most critical juncture. From early 1931 to the end of 1934, the "Left"-opportunist line had committed serious errors in military command, inflicting extremely severe losses on the Agrarian Revolutionary War; the Red Army failed to defeat the enemy's fifth "encirclement and suppression" campaign and instead lost its base areas.

## 判断 / The Assessment
面对失败，不是回避，而是从失败中总结经验教训，抓住当时最紧迫的军事问题。"遵义会议纠正了在第五次反"围剿"斗争中所犯的"左"倾机会主义性质的严重的原则错误，团结了党和红军"（《中国共产党在民族战争中的地位》）；"一九三五年的遵义会议，则主要地是反对战争中的机会主义，把战争问题放在第一位，这是战争环境的反映"（《战争和战略问题》）。这次会议"改变了中共中央的领导机关的成分，确立了以毛泽东为代表的领导，把党的路线转到马克思列宁主义的正确轨道上"（《学习和时局》）。

**English:**

In the face of defeat, instead of evading it, he drew lessons from the failure and seized the most pressing military problem of the moment. "The Zunyi Meeting corrected the serious errors of principle of a Left-opportunist nature committed in the struggle against the fifth 'encirclement and suppression' campaign and united the Party and the Red Army" (《中国共产党在民族战争中的地位》, The Role of the Chinese Communist Party in the National War); "the Zunyi Meeting of 1935 was mainly directed against opportunism in war and placed the question of war first, a reflection of the war situation" (《战争和战略问题》, Problems of War and Strategy). This meeting "changed the composition of the leading organs of the Central Committee, established the leadership represented by Mao Zedong, and put the Party's line back on the correct track of Marxism-Leninism" (《学习和时局》, Our Study and the Current Situation).

## 行动 / The Action
会议集中解决当时最紧迫的军事路线和军事指挥问题：否定错误的军事路线，肯定以教员为代表的正确军事主张，改组中央领导机关，把战争问题放在第一位。

**English:**

The meeting focused on resolving the most pressing problems of military line and military command at the time: it repudiated the erroneous military line, affirmed the correct military propositions represented by Jiaoyuan (the Teacher), reorganized the central leading organs, and placed the question of war first.

## 结果 / The Outcome
会议团结了党和红军，使党中央和红军主力胜利地完成了长征，转到了抗日的前进阵地。遵义会议成为党的历史上一个生死攸关的转折点。

**English:**

The meeting united the Party and the Red Army, enabling the Party Central Committee and the main force of the Red Army to complete the Long March victoriously and move to the forward positions of resistance against Japan. The Zunyi Meeting became a turning point of life-and-death significance in the Party's history.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 遇到重大失败时，先分清"战略方向错了"还是"执行不到位"。遵义会议抓住的是路线与方法问题，而不是追究个别战术失误——当你的项目或创业遭遇重大挫折，先复盘大方向和方法论，而不是陷于细枝末节。
2. 失败后要敢于否定自己过去的错误结论，用事实重新校准。会议"肯定正确意见、否定错误意见"的做法，可迁移为：团队复盘时用结果检验假设，而不是维护面子。
3. 危机时刻先解决主要矛盾。当时最紧迫的是军事路线，就集中解决军事问题，不铺开一切、四面出击。

**English:**

1. When facing a major failure, first distinguish "the strategic direction is wrong" from "the execution fell short." The Zunyi Meeting seized on questions of line and method rather than pursuing individual tactical errors—when your project or venture suffers a major setback, first review the big direction and methodology rather than getting bogged down in details.
2. After a failure, dare to repudiate your own past wrong conclusions and recalibrate against the facts. The meeting's practice of "affirming correct opinions and negating erroneous opinions" can be transferred as: in a team review, test assumptions against results rather than protecting face.
3. In a crisis, first resolve the principal contradiction. Since the military line was the most pressing issue at the time, the meeting concentrated on resolving the military problem rather than spreading out and attacking on all fronts.
