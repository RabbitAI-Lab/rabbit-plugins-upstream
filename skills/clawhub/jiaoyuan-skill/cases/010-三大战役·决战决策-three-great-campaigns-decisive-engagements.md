# 三大战役·决战决策 The Three Great Campaigns: Decisive-Engagement Decisions

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四八年秋，解放战争进入战略决战阶段。经过两年作战，国民党军已被大量歼灭，人民解放军在数量和质量上都开始占据优势，具备了与敌进行大规模决战的时机和条件。决战如何打、先打哪里，关系全局。

**English:**

In the autumn of 1948, the War of Liberation entered the stage of strategic decisive engagements. After two years of fighting, the Kuomintang army had been largely annihilated, and the People's Liberation Army was beginning to hold the advantage in both quantity and quality, possessing the moment and the conditions for large-scale decisive engagements with the enemy. How the decisive engagements should be fought and where to strike first bore on the whole situation.

## 判断 / The Assessment
抓住决战时机，每场战役都抓住一个决定全局的枢纽。辽沈战役的关键是打锦州："你们的中心注意力必须放在锦州作战方面，求得尽可能迅速地攻克该城。即使一切其他目的都未达到，只要攻克了锦州，你们就有了主动权，就是一个伟大的胜利。"（《关于辽沈战役的作战方针》）淮海战役的重心是集中兵力完成中间突破："本战役第一阶段的重心，是集中兵力歼灭黄百韬兵团，完成中间突破"（《关于淮海战役的作战方针》）。平津战役则重在防止敌人南逃："唯一的或主要的是怕敌人从海上逃跑。因此，在目前两星期内一般应采围而不打或隔而不围的办法。"（《关于平津战役的作战方针》）

**English:**

He seized the moment for decisive engagement, and in every campaign grasped a pivot that would decide the whole. The key to the Liaoshen Campaign was taking Jinzhou: "Your center of attention must be on the operations to take Jinzhou and on endeavoring to capture the city as quickly as possible. Even if none of your other objectives is achieved, as long as Jinzhou is taken, you will have the initiative, and that will be a great victory" (《关于辽沈战役的作战方针》, The Concept of Operations for the Liaoshen Campaign). The center of gravity of the Huaihai Campaign was to concentrate forces and achieve a breakthrough in the middle: "The center of gravity of the first stage of this campaign is to concentrate forces to annihilate Huang Baitao's army group and complete the breakthrough in the middle" (《关于淮海战役的作战方针》, The Concept of Operations for the Huaihai Campaign). The Pingjin Campaign focused on preventing the enemy from fleeing south: "The only or main thing to fear is that the enemy may escape by sea. Therefore, in the present two weeks we should generally adopt the principle of encircling without attacking, or cutting off without encircling" (《关于平津战役的作战方针》, The Concept of Operations for the Pingjin Campaign).

## 行动 / The Action
分别制定辽沈、淮海、平津三大战役的作战方针并组织实施：攻锦州关门打狗，中间突破分割歼灭，围而不打、隔而不围，把国民党军主力集团分割包围后各个歼灭。

**English:**

Operational principles for the Liaoshen, Huaihai, and Pingjin campaigns were separately formulated and implemented: taking Jinzhou to shut the door and beat the dog, breaking through in the middle to divide and annihilate, and encircling without attacking or cutting off without encircling—splitting, surrounding, and then destroying the main groups of the Kuomintang army one by one.

## 结果 / The Outcome
三大战役歼灭和改编了国民党军大量主力，解放了东北全境、华北和长江中下游以北广大地区，使国民党反动政府的首都南京直接处于威胁之下，为渡江作战和全国解放奠定了基础。

**English:**

The three great campaigns annihilated and reorganized a large part of the Kuomintang army's main forces, liberated all of Northeast China, North China, and the vast regions north of the middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River, placed the capital of the reactionary Kuomintang government, Nanjing, under direct threat, and laid the foundation for crossing the Yangtze and liberating the whole country.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 重大时机来临时要敢于决战，借鉴"抓枢纽"：每场大战役都抓住一个决定全局的关键点（锦州、黄百韬、塘沽海口），把主要精力放在枢纽上，枢纽一破全局皆活。
2. 借鉴"防止对手逃脱"：决策时先想清楚对手最可能的退路，提前堵住，不满足于眼前优势，避免功亏一篑。
3. 借鉴"分期分批、各个击破"：面对多个目标，按次序分批解决，每阶段集中解决一个重心，不贪多求全、四面开花。

**English:**

1. When a great moment arrives, dare to fight the decisive engagement, and borrow "seizing the pivot": every great campaign grasps a key point that decides the whole (Jinzhou, Huang Baitao, the Tanggu seaport), putting the main effort on the pivot—once the pivot is broken, the whole situation comes alive.
2. Borrow "preventing the opponent's escape": when deciding, first think through the opponent's most likely route of retreat and block it in advance; do not be satisfied with the present advantage and risk losing everything at the last step.
3. Borrow "staging by batches and destroying one by one": facing multiple objectives, solve them in order and by batches, concentrating on one center of gravity per stage, neither greedy for completeness nor blooming on all sides.
