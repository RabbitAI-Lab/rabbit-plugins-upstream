# 论持久战·三阶段 On Protracted War: Three Stages

## 局势 / The Situation
一九三八年抗日战争进入相持阶段前夕，全国对战争前途众说纷纭：有人散布"中国必亡"的悲观论调，也有人幻想"速胜"。战争究竟如何进行、中国能否胜利，成为迫切需要回答的根本问题。

**English:**

On the eve of the War of Resistance Against Japan entering its stalemate stage in 1938, opinions across the country diverged widely on the war's prospects: some spread the pessimism that "China is doomed to perish," while others indulged in the illusion of "quick victory." How the war should actually be fought, and whether China could win, became fundamental questions that urgently needed answering.

## 判断 / The Assessment
从敌我双方全部因素出发，批驳两种片面的观点："抗战十个月以来，一切经验都证明下述两种观点的不对：一种是中国必亡论，一种是中国速胜论。前者产生妥协倾向，后者产生轻敌倾向。"（《论持久战》）并得出结论："只有战略的持久战才是争取最后胜利的唯一途径"（《论持久战》），把持久战具体地划为三个阶段："第一个阶段，是敌之战略进攻、我之战略防御的时期。第二个阶段，是敌之战略保守、我之准备反攻的时期。第三个阶段，是我之战略反攻、敌之战略退却的时期。"（《论持久战》）

**English:**

Proceeding from all the factors on both sides, he refuted two one-sided views: "In the ten months since the War of Resistance began, all experience has confirmed the error of the following two views: the theory of China's inevitable subjugation and the theory of China's quick victory. The former tends to produce a tendency toward compromise, the latter a tendency to underestimate the enemy" (《论持久战》, On Protracted War). He then drew the conclusion that "only strategically protracted war is the only way to win final victory" (《论持久战》), and concretely divided the protracted war into three stages: "The first stage is the period of the enemy's strategic offensive and our strategic defensive. The second stage is the period of the enemy's strategic consolidation and our preparation for the counter-offensive. The third stage is the period of our strategic counter-offensive and the enemy's strategic retreat" (《论持久战》).

## 行动 / The Action
发表《论持久战》，系统阐明持久战的三个阶段，指明每阶段敌我力量消长的方向，用这一战略判断统一认识，指导抗战的战略部署。

**English:**

He published On Protracted War, systematically expounding the three stages of the protracted war, pointing out the direction in which the relative strength of the enemy and ourselves would shift at each stage, and using this strategic judgment to unify understanding and guide the strategic deployment of the War of Resistance.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这一判断为抗日战争的实践所证实：战争按持久战的轨迹展开，中国最终取得抗战胜利。

**English:**

This judgment was confirmed by the practice of the War of Resistance Against Japan: the war unfolded along the trajectory of a protracted war, and China ultimately won victory in the War of Resistance.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当遇到"非黑即白"的两个极端结论（悲观放弃 vs 盲目乐观）时，借鉴"驳两论、立己见"：把双方全部因素摊开再下结论，避免只抓一个因素就下判断。
2. 面对长周期目标，借鉴"分阶段"思维：把漫长过程切成可识别的阶段，明确每个阶段的主要矛盾和任务，既不急于求成，也不丧失方向感。
3. 决策时区分"客观规律"与"主观努力"：持久战是客观规定，但主观努力能影响快慢——把长期性当作现实约束接受下来，把努力程度当作可控变量去争取，既不空想速成，也不消极等待。

**English:**

1. When confronted with two "black-or-white" extreme conclusions (pessimistic abandonment vs. blind optimism), borrow the approach of "refuting both theories and establishing your own view": lay out all factors on both sides before concluding, avoiding judging on the basis of a single factor.
2. Facing a long-horizon goal, borrow "staging" thinking: cut the long process into identifiable stages, clarify the principal contradiction and task of each stage, and neither rush for results nor lose a sense of direction.
3. When making decisions, distinguish "objective law" from "subjective effort": protractedness is objectively determined, but subjective effort can affect the pace—accept the long-term nature as a realistic constraint, and treat the degree of effort as a controllable variable to strive for, neither fantasizing about quick success nor waiting passively.
