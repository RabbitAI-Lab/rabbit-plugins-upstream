# 从群众中来到群众中去·领导方法 From the Masses, to the Masses: Methods of Leadership

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四三年，抗日战争进入相持阶段的困难时期，党在根据地开展整风运动和各项中心工作，但一些干部领导方法有问题：有的不善于团结积极分子组成领导核心，有的不善于总结群众经验而主观主义地自作聪明，有的满足于一般号召而停留于嘴上、纸上、会议上，形成官僚主义的领导。教员为中共中央写决定，系统阐述科学的领导方法。

**English:**

In 1943, the War of Resistance Against Japan was in the difficult period of strategic stalemate, and the Party was carrying out the Rectification Movement and various central tasks in the base areas. But some cadres had problems in their methods of leadership: some were not good at uniting the activists to form a leading core; some were not good at summing up the experience of the masses, and instead tried to be clever in a subjectivist way; some were content with general calls and stopped at words, paper, and meetings, resulting in bureaucratic leadership. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) drafted a decision for the Central Committee systematically expounding scientific methods of leadership.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他提出正确领导的根本路径：“在我党的一切实际工作中，凡属正确的领导，必须是从群众中来，到群众中去。”（《关于领导方法的若干问题》）他解释为把群众分散的意见集中起来、化为系统的意见，再回到群众中宣传解释、见之于行动，并在群众行动中检验是否正确，如此循环、一次比一次更正确。他还提出统筹全局、抓住中心的领导艺术：“在任何一个地区内，不能同时有许多中心工作，在一定时间内只能有一个中心工作，辅以别的第二位、第三位的工作。”（《关于领导方法的若干问题》）

**English:**

He set out the fundamental path of correct leadership: "In all the practical work of our Party, all correct leadership is necessarily 'from the masses, to the masses.'" (《关于领导方法的若干问题》 "Some Questions Concerning Methods of Leadership") He explained this as gathering the scattered opinions of the masses, turning them into systematic opinions, then going back to the masses to explain and spread them and put them into action, testing in the masses' practice whether they are correct, and repeating this cycle so that each round is more correct than the last. He also set out the art of leading by taking the whole into account while grasping the center: "In any given region, there cannot be many central tasks at the same time; at a given time there can be only one central task, supplemented by other tasks of secondary or tertiary importance." (《关于领导方法的若干问题》 "Some Questions Concerning Methods of Leadership")

## 行动 / The Action
他提出两个必须采用的方法：一是一般和个别相结合——既发一般号召，又选择几个单位深入实施、突破一点取得经验，再以经验指导全局；二是领导和群众相结合——团结少数积极分子作领导骨干，再凭借骨干去提高中间分子、争取落后分子。同时反对主观主义和官僚主义的领导方法，主张按各地区的具体条件正确地决定每一时期的工作重心和工作秩序。

**English:**

He put forward two methods that must be adopted. The first is combining the general with the particular: issue a general call, but also select several units to carry it out in depth, break through at one point to gain experience, and then use that experience to guide the whole. The second is combining the leadership with the masses: unite a small number of activists as the leading backbone, and through that backbone raise the intermediate elements and win over the backward ones. At the same time he opposed subjectivist and bureaucratic methods of leadership, urging that the central point and the order of work in each period be correctly determined according to the specific conditions of each area.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这套领导方法纠正了干部中脱离群众、空发号召、主次不分等毛病，成为此后党在各项工作中普遍应用的基本领导方法。

**English:**

This set of leadership methods corrected such faults among cadres as being divorced from the masses, issuing empty calls, and failing to distinguish the primary from the secondary, and became the basic method of leadership widely applied by the Party in all its work thereafter.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 好的方案不是凭空想出来的，是从一线和群众中收集意见、归纳成系统办法、再回到实践中检验——建立“从一线来、到一线去”的闭环。
- 一般号召要和个别指导结合：别只发大方向，先挑几个点做出样板、拿到经验，再用样板带全局。
- 一段时间只抓一个中心，分清主次、排好次序——多线并进往往是主次不分，集中力量办决定项。

**English:**

- A good plan is not dreamed up out of thin air; it comes from gathering opinions on the front line and among the masses, distilling them into a systematic approach, and testing it again in practice — build the closed loop of "from the front line, back to the front line."
- General calls must be combined with particular guidance: don't just announce a broad direction; pick a few points, make them into models, gain the experience, and then use the models to lead the whole.
- In any given period, grasp only one center and put the primary and secondary in order — running many lines at once usually means confusing the primary with the secondary; concentrate your forces on the decisive item.
