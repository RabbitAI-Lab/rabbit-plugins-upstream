# 井冈山的斗争·工农武装割据 The Struggle in the Jinggang Mountains: Armed Independent Regime of Workers and Peasants

## 局势 / The Situation
一九二八年十一月，井冈山根据地正处在湘赣两省国民党军队的"进剿"和经济封锁之中。此前八月，因一部分同志分兵冒进湘南，边界和湘南同时失败，红军损失近半，各县相继失陷。教员在写给中共中央的报告中，总结一年来边界割据的经验与教训。

**English:**

In November 1928, the Jinggang Mountains base area was under "suppression" by the Kuomintang armies of Hunan and Jiangxi provinces and under economic blockade. In the preceding August, because some comrades had divided their forces and rashly advanced into southern Hunan, the border area and southern Hunan failed simultaneously, the Red Army lost nearly half its strength, and the counties fell one after another. In his report to the CPC Central Committee, Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) summed up a year's experience and lessons of the independent regime in the border area.

## 判断 / The Assessment
教员指出，边界的斗争本质上是军事斗争，割据必须靠武装来支撑："所谓割据，必须是武装的。哪一处没有武装，或者武装不够，或者对付敌人的策略错了，地方就立即被敌人占去了。"（《井冈山的斗争》）同时，他判断工农武装割据能否存续和发展，取决于五个客观条件："工农武装割据的存在和发展，还需要具备下列的条件：（1）有很好的群众；（2）有很好的党；（3）有相当力量的红军；（4）有便利于作战的地势；（5）有足够给养的经济力。"（《井冈山的斗争》）据此，他把红色政权、武装斗争、土地革命连成一个整体来理解割据的存续。

**English:**

Jiaoyuan pointed out that the struggle in the border area was in essence a military struggle, and that an independent regime must be sustained by arms: "A so-called independent regime must be an armed one. Wherever there is no armed force, or an inadequate armed force, or wrong tactics in dealing with the enemy, the enemy immediately occupies the place" (《井冈山的斗争》, The Struggle in the Jinggang Mountains). He also judged that the survival and development of an armed independent regime of workers and peasants depended on five objective conditions: "The existence and development of an armed independent regime of workers and peasants requires the following conditions: (1) a sound mass base; (2) a sound Party organization; (3) a Red Army of considerable strength; (4) terrain favorable to military operations; (5) economic resources sufficient to provide sustenance" (《井冈山的斗争》). On this basis he linked red political power, armed struggle, and the agrarian revolution into a single whole to understand the survival of the independent regime.

## 行动 / The Action
在军事上采取集中兵力、反对分兵，波浪式推进，避免被敌人各个击破；在政治上深入土地革命，军队的党帮助地方党发展，军队的武装帮助地方武装发展；在军队内部建立党代表制度和"支部建在连上"，实行官兵平等的军队民主主义；同时巩固井冈山、九陇山两个军事根据地，修筑工事、储备粮食、建设红军医院。

**English:**

Militarily, forces were concentrated and dispersal opposed, advancing in waves to avoid being destroyed piecemeal by the enemy; politically, the agrarian revolution was deepened, with the army's Party organizations helping the local Party develop and the army's arms helping the local armed forces develop; within the army, the Party representative system and "branch on the company" were established, with army democracy of equality between officers and soldiers; at the same time the two military base areas of the Jinggang Mountains and the Jiulong Mountains were consolidated, fortifications built, grain stored, and Red Army hospitals constructed.

## 结果 / The Outcome
边界红旗子坚持不倒，土地革命一天天深入，民众政权一天天推广，红军和赤卫队一天天扩大，割据地区呈波浪式扩大之势。

**English:**

The red flag in the border area held firm and did not fall, the agrarian revolution deepened day by day, popular political power was extended day by day, the Red Army and the Red Guards grew day by day, and the independent-regime areas expanded in waves.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 判断一块"根据地"能否立足，先看客观条件是否具备，再看主观策略是否正确——选赛道、选市场先评估资源禀赋和竞争格局，不靠侥幸。
- "割据必须是武装的"——核心能力必须过硬，没有护城河的阵地守不住。
- 集中力量建立中心区域的坚实基础，反对盲目铺摊子、分散用力——资源有限时先做深一块，再图扩张。

**English:**

- To judge whether a "base area" can take root, first see whether the objective conditions are present, then whether the subjective strategy is correct—when choosing a track or a market, first assess resource endowment and the competitive landscape, rather than relying on luck.
- "An independent regime must be an armed one"—core capabilities must be strong; a position without a moat cannot be held.
- Concentrate forces to build a solid foundation in the central area, opposing blind sprawl and dispersed effort—when resources are limited, first dig deep in one place, then plan expansion.
