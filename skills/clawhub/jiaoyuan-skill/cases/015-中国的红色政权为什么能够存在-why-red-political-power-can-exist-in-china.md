# 中国的红色政权为什么能够存在 Why Red Political Power Can Exist in China

## 局势 / The Situation
大革命失败后，国民党新军阀统治中国，白色恐怖笼罩全国，许多乘时而起的红色政权先后遭到摧残。井冈山等小块根据地在强敌“进剿”和经济封锁中坚持，党内一些同志因困难和危急而产生悲观情绪，怀疑红色政权能否长期存在。教员为湘赣边界党的第二次代表大会写决议，回答“红旗到底打得多久”的问题。

**English:**

After the failure of the Great Revolution, the new warlord clique of the Kuomintang ruled China, White Terror enveloped the whole country, and many Red political powers that had risen with the tide were crushed one after another. Small base areas such as the Jinggang Mountains held out under the enemy's repeated "suppression campaigns" and economic blockade, while some comrades within the Party, discouraged by difficulty and danger, fell into pessimism and doubted whether Red political power could survive over the long term. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) wrote a resolution for the Second Congress of the Party's Hunan-Jiangxi Border Area, answering the question of "how long the red flag can keep flying."

## 判断 / The Assessment
教员先把这个现象点破为“奇事”，再追问其所以然：“一国之内，在四围白色政权的包围中，有一小块或若干小块红色政权的区域长期地存在，这是世界各国从来没有的事。”（《中国的红色政权为什么能够存在》）他不把怀疑当结论，而是把怀疑当作尚未找到正确解释的信号：“有些同志在困难和危急的时候，往往怀疑这样的红色政权的存在，而发生悲观的情绪。这是没有找出这种红色政权所以发生和存在的正确的解释的缘故。”（《中国的红色政权为什么能够存在》）他进而分析出红色政权能够存在的若干客观条件：白色政权之间的长期分裂和战争、受过民主革命影响的群众基础、全国革命形势继续向前发展、相当力量的正式红军的存在、共产党组织的有力量和政策的正确。

**English:**

Jiaoyuan first called this phenomenon a "strange thing" and then asked why it was so: "In a country, the prolonged existence of one or more small areas under Red political power encircled by White political power is something that has never occurred in any other country of the world." (《中国的红色政权为什么能够存在》 "Why Red Political Power Can Exist in China") He did not treat doubt as a conclusion, but as a signal that the correct explanation had not yet been found: "Some comrades, in times of difficulty and danger, often doubt the existence of such Red political power and lapse into pessimism. This is because they have not found the correct explanation for why this Red political power has emerged and persisted." (《中国的红色政权为什么能够存在》 "Why Red Political Power Can Exist in China") He then analyzed the objective conditions that made Red political power possible: the prolonged division and warfare among the White regimes, a mass base influenced by the democratic revolution, the continued advance of the nationwide revolutionary situation, the existence of a regular Red Army of considerable strength, and the strength of the Communist Party organization and the correctness of its policies.

## 行动 / The Action
他系统梳理红色政权发生和存在的条件，并据此指导边界工作：强调“工农武装割据”的思想必须充分具备；巩固大小五井、九陇两个军事根据地，修筑工事、储备粮食、建设红军医院；正视经济封锁下军民日用必需品缺乏的困难，提出要设法使生活特别是红军给养有所改善。

**English:**

He systematically sorted out the conditions for the emergence and existence of Red political power and, on this basis, guided work in the border area: he stressed that the idea of "armed independent regime of the workers and peasants" must be fully established; consolidated the two military base areas of Daxiao Wujing and Jiulong by building fortifications, stockpiling grain, and setting up Red Army hospitals; and faced squarely the shortage of daily necessities under the economic blockade, proposing that living conditions—especially the Red Army's supplies—be improved.

## 结果 / The Outcome
对红色政权存续条件的分析，解除了党内一部分人的悲观疑虑，边界割据在强敌包围中坚持并逐步发展，工农武装割据的思想成为指导边界斗争的重要思想。

**English:**

The analysis of the conditions for Red political power's survival dispelled the pessimism and misgivings of some within the Party; the border-area regime held out amidst enemy encirclement and developed step by step, and the idea of armed independent regime of the workers and peasants became an important guiding thought for the struggle in the border area.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 面对“这到底行不行”的怀疑，不把怀疑当结论，而是去追“行或不行”背后的真实条件——用条件分析代替情绪判断。
- 判断一件事能否长期立足，要逐项列出支撑条件（外部环境、群众基础、趋势、核心能力、组织质量），条件齐不齐决定结论。
- 悲观往往源于解释不足：先找出现象的正确解释，再决定是坚持还是调整。

**English:**

- When faced with the doubt "will this really work," don't treat doubt as a conclusion; instead, trace the real conditions behind "whether it works" — use analysis of conditions in place of emotional judgment.
- To judge whether something can stand the test of time, list its supporting conditions one by one (external environment, mass base, trend, core capability, organizational quality); whether the conditions are complete determines the conclusion.
- Pessimism often stems from an inadequate explanation: first find the correct explanation for the phenomenon, then decide whether to persist or adjust.
