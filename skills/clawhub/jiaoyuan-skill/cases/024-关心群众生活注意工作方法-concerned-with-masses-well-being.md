# 关心群众生活注意工作方法 Concern with the Well-Being of the Masses, Attention to Methods of Work

## 局势 / The Situation
一九三四年一月，中央苏区正面临国民党大规模“围剿”，动员群众参加革命战争是中心任务。但一些地方的干部只讲扩大红军、动员运输队、收土地税、推销公债，对群众的柴米油盐、衣食住行等切身生活问题不闻不问，结果动员工作反而做不好。教员在第二次全国工农兵代表大会上作结论，专门讲群众生活和工作方法两个问题。

**English:**

In January 1934, the Central Soviet Area was facing a large-scale "encirclement and suppression" campaign by the Kuomintang, and mobilizing the masses to take part in the revolutionary war was the central task. But in some places, cadres talked only about expanding the Red Army, mobilizing transport teams, collecting the land tax, and selling public bonds, while paying no attention at all to the people's daily needs—firewood, rice, cooking oil, salt, clothing, food, housing, and transport—with the result that mobilization actually went badly. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) made the concluding speech at the Second National Congress of Workers' and Peasants' Deputies, devoted specifically to the two questions of the people's well-being and methods of work.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他把群众放在最根本的位置：“真正的铜墙铁壁是什么？是群众，是千百万真心实意地拥护革命的群众。这是真正的铜墙铁壁，什么力量也打不破的，完全打不破的。”（《关心群众生活，注意工作方法》）他据此判断，只有解决群众切身利益问题、真心实意地为群众谋利益，群众才会真心拥护、响应动员。同时他强调任务要落到方法上：“我们不但要提出任务，而且要解决完成任务的方法问题。我们的任务是过河，但是没有桥或没有船就不能过。不解决桥或船的问题，过河就是一句空话。”（《关心群众生活，注意工作方法》）

**English:**

He placed the masses in the most fundamental position: "What is a true bastion of iron? It is the masses, the millions upon millions of people who genuinely and sincerely support the revolution. That is the real iron bastion, which no force can smash, no force whatsoever." (《关心群众生活，注意工作方法》 "Be Concerned with the Well-Being of the Masses, Pay Attention to Methods of Work") From this he concluded that only by solving the problems that touch the people's immediate interests and by genuinely and sincerely working for their benefit would the people give their sincere support and answer the call to mobilize. At the same time he stressed that tasks must be translated into methods: "We must not only put forward tasks, but also solve the problem of the methods for carrying them out. Our task is to cross a river, but we cannot cross it without a bridge or a boat. Unless the problem of the bridge or boat is solved, it is idle talk to say that we will cross the river." (《关心群众生活，注意工作方法》 "Be Concerned with the Well-Being of the Masses, Pay Attention to Methods of Work")

## 行动 / The Action
他号召各级组织把群众生活问题（土地、劳动、穿衣、吃饭、住房、柴米油盐、疾病卫生、婚姻等）提到议事日程上，讨论、决定、实行、检查；树立长冈乡、才溪乡等关心群众疾苦、动员成绩又好的模范；反对汀州市式只讲任务不顾群众生活的官僚主义，反对命令主义，采取耐心说服和实际具体的工作方法。

**English:**

He called on organizations at every level to put the problems of the people's daily life (land, labor, clothing, food, housing, firewood, rice, oil and salt, illness and hygiene, marriage, and the rest) on their agenda—to discuss, decide, carry out, and check. He held up Changgang Township, Caixi Township, and other model units that cared about the people's hardships and at the same time did well at mobilization, and opposed the bureaucratic style of the Tingzhou type, which talked only of tasks and ignored the people's livelihood, as well as commandism, adopting instead patient persuasion and practical, concrete methods of work.

## 结果 / The Outcome
关心群众生活的模范乡既解决了群众困难，又使扩大红军、推销公债等动员工作取得突出成绩，说明关心群众切身利益与完成中心任务并不矛盾，反而互相促进。

**English:**

The model townships that cared about the people's well-being both solved the people's difficulties and achieved outstanding results in mobilization work such as expanding the Red Army and selling public bonds, showing that caring for the people's immediate interests is not at odds with accomplishing the central task—on the contrary, the two reinforce each other.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 想动员别人投入、响应号召，先解决对方最关心、最切身的问题——团队协作、用户运营、群众工作，先关心人、再提要求。
- 真正的“护城河”是人心和拥护，不是硬件和壁垒——用户和伙伴的真心拥护比任何资源都难打破。
- 布置任务必须同时给出方法：“过河”先想“桥和船”——只喊目标、不给路径和方法，任务就会落空。

**English:**

- If you want to mobilize people to invest and answer your call, first solve the problems they care about most — in team collaboration, user operations, and mass work, care about people first, then make demands.
- The true "moat" is people's hearts and support, not hardware and walls — the sincere support of users and partners is harder to break through than any resource.
- Assigning a task must be accompanied by the method for doing it: before "crossing the river," first think of "the bridge and the boat" — shouting goals without giving paths and methods will leave the task unfulfilled.
