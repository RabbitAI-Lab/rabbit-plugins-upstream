# 湖南农民运动考察·放手发动群众 Report on the Peasant Movement in Hunan: Rely on and Mobilize the Masses

## 局势 / The Situation
一九二七年三月，北伐进行中，湖南农民运动迅猛兴起，农会会员激增到二百万。但党内党外对农民运动一片责难，或说“糟得很”，或说“过分”，以陈独秀为首的一些人不敢支持已经起来的农民斗争。教员为答复责难，到湖南实地考察了三十二天，写下考察报告。

**English:**

In March 1927, while the Northern Expedition was under way, the peasant movement in Hunan surged forward, with membership in the peasant associations soaring to two million. Yet inside and outside the Party the movement drew a chorus of condemnation—some called it "terrible," others "going too far"—and some people, led by Chen Duxiu, did not dare support the peasant struggle that had already arisen. To answer these accusations, Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) went to Hunan and conducted on-the-ground investigation for thirty-two days, then wrote his report.

## 判断 / The Assessment
教员认为，农民运动打翻的是封建势力，正是国民革命的真正目标：“孙中山先生致力国民革命凡四十年，所要做而没有做到的事，农民在几个月内做到了。这是四十年乃至几千年未曾成就过的奇勋。这是好得很。”（《湖南农民运动考察报告》）针对“过分”的责难，他指出革命本身的特质决定了不能温吞吞地进行：“革命不是请客吃饭，不是做文章，不是绘画绣花，不能那样雅致，那样从容不迫，文质彬彬，那样温良恭俭让。革命是暴动，是一个阶级推翻一个阶级的暴烈的行动。”（《湖南农民运动考察报告》）他据此肯定农民运动的大方向，主张站在农民前头领导他们，而不是指手画脚地批评。

**English:**

Jiaoyuan held that what the peasant movement was overthrowing was the feudal forces, the very target of the national revolution: "Dr. Sun Yat-sen devoted forty years to the national revolution, and what he wanted to do but failed to do, the peasants accomplished in a few months. This is a marvelous feat never achieved in the past forty or even several thousand years. It is very good." (《湖南农民运动考察报告》 "Report on an Investigation of the Peasant Movement in Hunan") Against the charge of "going too far," he pointed out that the nature of revolution itself ruled out lukewarm progress: "A revolution is not a dinner party, or writing an essay, or painting a picture, or doing embroidery; it cannot be so refined, so leisurely and gentle, so temperate, kind, courteous, restrained and magnanimous. A revolution is an insurrection, an act of violence by which one class overthrows another." (《湖南农民运动考察报告》 "Report on an Investigation of the Peasant Movement in Hunan") On this basis, he affirmed the general direction of the peasant movement and called for standing at the head of the peasants to lead them, rather than criticizing them with a lot of pointing and gesturing.

## 行动 / The Action
他亲身到湘潭、湘乡、衡山、醴陵、长沙五县实地调查，召集有经验的农民和农运工作同志开调查会，拿到第一手材料；在报告里逐件肯定农民在农会领导下所做的十四件大事（组织起来、政治上打击地主、经济上打击地主、建立农民武装等）；主张放手发动群众，把贫农作为农民协会的中坚力量加以依靠。

**English:**

He personally carried out on-the-ground investigation in five counties—Xiangtan, Xiangxiang, Hengshan, Liling, and Changsha—convening investigation meetings with experienced peasants and comrades working in the peasant movement, and obtained first-hand material. In the report he affirmed, item by item, the fourteen great things the peasants had accomplished under the leadership of the peasant associations (organizing themselves, striking at the landlords politically, striking at the landlords economically, building up peasant armed forces, and so on). He advocated boldly arousing the masses and relying on the poor peasants as the backbone of the peasant associations.

## 结果 / The Outcome
考察报告驳斥了对农民运动的责难，澄清了“糟得很”与“好得很”、“过分”与革命需要等是非，为发动和依靠农民群众提供了依据，农民运动继续迅猛发展。

**English:**

The report refuted the accusations against the peasant movement and clarified the distinctions between "terrible" and "very good," between "going too far" and what the revolution required, providing a basis for arousing and relying on the peasant masses; the peasant movement continued to develop rapidly.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 下结论之前先到现场看、听一线当事人讲，而不是只听“绅士阶级”的转述——做用户调研要走到真实用户中去，不听二手转述和既得利益者的单方面说法。
- 对新兴力量的一时“乱象”，先分清主流与支流，肯定大方向——对基层探索先看方向对不对，不因局部的“过分”而否定整体。
- 先想清楚依靠谁：找准最主要的力量来源并坚定依靠，而不是迁就既得利益者——创业、变革都要搞清真正的依靠力量是谁。

**English:**

- Before drawing conclusions, go to the scene and listen to the people on the front line rather than only to the second-hand accounts of the "gentry class" — in user research, go to the real users; don't rely on second-hand reports or the one-sided claims of vested interests.
- When an emerging force shows temporary "disorder," first distinguish the mainstream from the tributaries and affirm the general direction — for grassroots experiments, first check whether the direction is right, and don't reject the whole because of partial "excess."
- Think first about whom to rely on: identify the main source of strength and rely on it firmly rather than appeasing vested interests — in entrepreneurship and in change, be clear about who the real base of support is.
