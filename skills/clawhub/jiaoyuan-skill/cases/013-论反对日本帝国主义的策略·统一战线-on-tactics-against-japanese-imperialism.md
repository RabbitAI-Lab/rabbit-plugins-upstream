# 论反对日本帝国主义的策略·统一战线 On Tactics Against Japanese Imperialism: The United Front

## 局势 / The Situation
一九三五年，日本帝国主义加紧侵略华北，民族危机空前加深。红军长征到达陕北后，党需要系统解决政治路线问题。当时党内存在关门主义倾向，认为民族资产阶级不可能和中国工人农民联合抗日，主张革命队伍要"纯粹又纯粹"。

**English:**

In 1935, Japanese imperialism stepped up its aggression in North China, and the national crisis deepened unprecedentedly. After the Red Army reached northern Shaanxi on the Long March, the Party needed to resolve its political line systematically. At the time, a tendency of closed-doorism existed within the Party, holding that the national bourgeoisie could not possibly unite with China's workers and peasants to resist Japan, and advocating that the revolutionary ranks be "pure and ever purer."

## 判断 / The Assessment
必须建立广泛的抗日民族统一战线，坚决反对关门主义。"组织千千万万的民众，调动浩浩荡荡的革命军，是今天的革命向反革命进攻的需要。"（《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》）"因此，只有统一战线的策略才是马克思列宁主义的策略。关门主义的策略则是孤家寡人的策略。"（《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》）关门主义"为渊驱鱼，为丛驱雀"，把"千千万万"和"浩浩荡荡"都赶到敌人那一边去，只博得敌人的喝采（《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》）。

**English:**

A broad Anti-Japanese National United Front had to be built, and closed-doorism resolutely opposed. "To organize the masses in their thousands and tens of thousands and mobilize a vast revolutionary army is what the revolution needs in its attack on the counter-revolution today" (《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》, On Tactics Against Japanese Imperialism). "Hence only the tactics of the united front are Marxist-Leninist tactics. The tactics of closed-doorism are the tactics of the regal isolationist" (《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》). Closed-doorism "drives the fish into deep waters and the sparrows into the thickets," driving the "thousands and tens of thousands" and the "vast army" all over to the enemy's side, winning only the enemy's applause (《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》).

## 行动 / The Action
瓦窑堡会议确定建立抗日民族统一战线的策略，批评关门主义，调整对民族资产阶级等中间力量的政策，动员和团结一切可能的力量联合抗日。

**English:**

The Wayaobao Conference determined the tactics of establishing the Anti-Japanese National United Front, criticized closed-doorism, adjusted the policy toward the national bourgeoisie and other intermediate forces, and mobilized and united all possible forces to jointly resist Japan.

## 结果 / The Outcome
抗日民族统一战线逐步建立起来，为全民族抗战的发动和最后胜利准备了政治条件。

**English:**

The Anti-Japanese National United Front was progressively established, preparing the political conditions for launching the nationwide War of Resistance and for its final victory.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当单打独斗力量不足时，借鉴"组织千千万万"：把尽可能多的同盟者团结起来、做大自己的阵营，而不是追求"纯粹"而自我孤立。
2. 借鉴"反对关门主义"：不要因为理念洁癖把潜在的合作伙伴、用户、生态伙伴赶到对手那一边，善用统一战线扩大基本盘。
3. 借鉴"择敌"思维：明确最中心的目标（主要矛盾），不分散火力，把力量对准最主要的对手，而不是四面树敌、浪费弹药。

**English:**

1. When going it alone is insufficient, borrow "organizing the thousands and tens of thousands": unite as many allies as possible and enlarge your own camp, rather than pursuing "purity" and isolating yourself.
2. Borrow "opposing closed-doorism": do not, out of ideological fastidiousness, drive potential partners, users, and ecosystem collaborators over to the opponent's side; use the united front well to expand your base.
3. Borrow the "choosing the enemy" mindset: identify the central objective (the principal contradiction), do not scatter firepower, aim your strength at the main opponent, rather than making enemies on all sides and wasting ammunition.
