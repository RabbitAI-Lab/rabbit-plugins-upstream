# 反围剿·十六字诀与运动战 Counter-Encirclement: The Sixteen-Character Formula and Mobile Warfare

## 局势 / The Situation
土地革命战争时期，红军从井冈山起步，面对的是兵力和装备远占优势的国民党军队，后者对革命根据地发起一次又一次"围剿"。红军力量弱小、根据地狭小，没有本钱和优势之敌硬拼消耗，必须依靠一套适合自己的作战方法才能生存并发展。

**English:**

During the Agrarian Revolutionary War, the Red Army, starting from the Jinggang Mountains, faced a Kuomintang army far superior in both numbers and equipment, which launched one "encirclement and suppression" campaign after another against the revolutionary base areas. With weak forces and a small base area, the Red Army could not afford a war of attrition against a superior enemy; it could survive and grow only by relying on a set of fighting methods suited to its own conditions.

## 判断 / The Assessment
从十年血战经验中总结出一套战争指导原则。从一九二八年五月起，"适应当时情况的带着朴素性质的游击战争基本原则，已经产生出来了，那就是所谓"敌进我退，敌驻我扰，敌疲我打，敌退我追"的十六字诀"（《中国革命战争的战略问题》）。在防御与进攻的关系上，提出"积极防御，又叫攻势防御，又叫决战防御"，并强调"基本的原则是承认积极防御，反对消极防御"（《中国革命战争的战略问题》）。在消灭敌人的方法上，主张打歼灭战："对于人，伤其十指不如断其一指；对于敌，击溃其十个师不如歼灭其一个师"（《中国革命战争的战略问题》）。

**English:**

A set of principles for directing war was summed up from ten years of bloody fighting. From May 1928 onward, "the elementary principles of guerrilla warfare, simple in nature and suited to the conditions of the time, had already been produced—namely, the sixteen-character formula: 'The enemy advances, we retreat; the enemy camps, we harass; the enemy tires, we attack; the enemy retreats, we pursue'" (《中国革命战争的战略问题》, Problems of Strategy in China's Revolutionary War). On the relationship between defense and offense, he put forward "active defense, also called offensive defense, also called decisive-engagement defense," and stressed that "the basic principle is to recognize active defense and oppose passive defense" (《中国革命战争的战略问题》). On the method of destroying the enemy, he advocated a war of annihilation: "As for men, wounding all ten fingers is not as effective as cutting off one; as for the enemy, routing ten of his divisions is not as effective as annihilating one of them" (《中国革命战争的战略问题》).

## 行动 / The Action
反"围剿"中，不采取分兵把口、处处设防的消极防御，而是实行战略退却（诱敌深入），把敌军引进根据地内部，再集中兵力打运动战、速决战、歼灭战，先打弱敌，各个击破。

**English:**

In the counter-"encirclement and suppression" campaigns, instead of adopting passive defense by splitting forces to hold every pass and fortifying everywhere, the Red Army carried out strategic retreat (luring the enemy deep), drew the enemy into the interior of the base area, and then concentrated its forces to fight mobile warfare, quick-decision warfare, and annihilation warfare—attacking the weaker enemy first and destroying the enemy forces one by one.

## 结果 / The Outcome
依靠十六字诀和积极防御原则，打破了敌人前几次"围剿"；而第五次反"围剿"中，因错误路线采取了消极防御、消耗战的方针，招致了丧失根据地的失败。正反两面都验证了这条原则。

**English:**

Relying on the sixteen-character formula and the principle of active defense, the Red Army broke the enemy's first several "encirclement and suppression" campaigns. But in the fifth counter-campaign, an erroneous line adopted a policy of passive defense and a war of attrition, bringing about the failure of losing the base area. Both the positive and the negative sides confirmed this principle.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当你在竞争中处于劣势（资源、资金、体量不如对手）时，可以借鉴"积极防御"：不硬碰硬拼消耗，而是主动收缩、保存实力，寻找对手最薄弱的环节，集中力量打歼灭战，积小胜为大胜。
2. 当面对"既要守住什么、又想赢"的两难时，借鉴十六字诀的动态思维：进与退、驻与扰、疲与打不是僵化教条，而是依据对手状态灵活切换，退是为了更好地进。
3. 当资源有限时，借鉴"伤其十指不如断其一指"：与其四处平均用力、处处浅尝辄止，不如把有限资源聚焦到一个点上彻底做透，赢得决定性效果。

**English:**

1. When you are at a disadvantage in competition (inferior in resources, capital, or scale), borrow "active defense": do not fight a war of attrition head-on, but take the initiative to draw back, preserve strength, find the opponent's weakest link, and concentrate force to fight a war of annihilation—accumulating small victories into a great victory.
2. When facing the dilemma of "wanting both to hold something and to win," borrow the dynamic thinking of the sixteen-character formula: advancing and retreating, camping and harassing, wearing down and attacking are not rigid dogmas but flexible switches based on the opponent's state—retreating is for the sake of better advancing.
3. When resources are limited, borrow "wounding all ten fingers is not as effective as cutting off one": rather than spreading effort evenly and stopping at the surface everywhere, focus limited resources on one point and do it thoroughly, winning a decisive effect.
