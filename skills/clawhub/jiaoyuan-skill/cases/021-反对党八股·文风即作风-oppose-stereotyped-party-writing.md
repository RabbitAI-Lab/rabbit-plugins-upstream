# 反对党八股·文风即作风 Oppose Stereotyped Party Writing: Style Reflects Conduct

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四二年二月，延安整风运动开展。主观主义和宗派主义借党八股做宣传工具和表现形式，党内不少文章、演说空话连篇、装腔作势、无的放矢、语言无味，还开中药铺式地堆砌条条。教员在延安干部会上讲演，清算党八股，主张代之以生动活泼新鲜有力的马克思列宁主义文风。

**English:**

In February 1942, the Rectification Movement got under way in Yan'an. Subjectivism and sectarianism used stereotyped Party writing as their vehicle of propaganda and form of expression. Many articles and speeches within the Party were full of empty talk, struck pretentious poses, shot arrows with no target, and used dull language, piling up items the way a Chinese pharmacy lists its stock. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) delivered a speech at a cadres' meeting in Yan'an to settle accounts with stereotyped Party writing, advocating that it be replaced with a lively, fresh, and vigorous Marxist-Leninist style.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他把党八股列为必须清算的坏作风，第一条就点它的空：“党八股的第一条罪状是：空话连篇，言之无物。”（《反对党八股》）他反对靠装腔作势吓人，主张靠真理和科学说话：“共产党不靠吓人吃饭，而是靠马克思列宁主义的真理吃饭，靠实事求是吃饭，靠科学吃饭。”（《反对党八股》）他强调写文章做演说要看对象，主张废止洋八股、代之以老百姓喜闻乐见的中国作风和中国气派，把文风同作风、同是否联系实际联系起来看。

**English:**

He listed stereotyped Party writing as a bad style that had to be settled with, and pointed first to its emptiness: "The first crime of stereotyped Party writing is that it is filled with empty talk and has nothing of substance." (《反对党八股》 "Oppose Stereotyped Party Writing") He opposed intimidating people with pretentious posturing, and argued for speaking through truth and science: "The Communist Party does not rely on blustering and bluffing for a living, but on the truth of Marxism-Leninism, on seeking truth from facts, and on science." (《反对党八股》 "Oppose Stereotyped Party Writing") He stressed that writing and speeches must take the audience into account, urged abolishing foreign stereotyped writing and replacing it with a Chinese style and Chinese spirit that the common people love, and linked style of writing with style of work and with whether one keeps in touch with reality.

## 行动 / The Action
他仿照八股笔法列出党八股的八大罪状（空话连篇言之无物、装腔作势借以吓人、无的放矢不看对象、语言无味像个瘪三、甲乙丙丁开中药铺、不负责任到处害人、流毒全党妨害革命、传播出去祸国殃民），逐条分析坏处并提出改法；提倡向人民群众学习语言、写文章做演说像洗脸一样负责任、反复删改、不生造谁也不懂的形容词。

**English:**

Imitating the manner of the eight-legged essay, he set out the eight crimes of stereotyped Party writing (empty talk with no substance; pretentious posturing to intimidate; shooting without a target and ignoring the audience; drab language like a scrawny vagrant; listing items A-B-C-D like a pharmacy catalogue; irresponsibility that harms people everywhere; spreading poison through the Party and obstructing the revolution; and circulating abroad to the ruin of the country and the people), analyzed the harm of each and proposed remedies. He advocated learning language from the masses, treating the writing of articles and the making of speeches as seriously as washing one's face, revising repeatedly, and not coining adjectives that no one can understand.

## 结果 / The Outcome
对党八股的清算，配合整风运动，推动了全党抛弃空洞教条的文风，代之以联系实际、为老百姓喜闻乐见的作风。

**English:**

The settling of accounts with stereotyped Party writing, together with the Rectification Movement, pushed the whole Party to cast aside the empty and dogmatic style and replace it with a style that keeps in touch with reality and is welcome to the common people.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 文风就是作风：一个人或组织的报告、邮件、方案里堆满空话套话，往往反映的是不联系实际、不解决问题的作风——先从写清楚、说人话改起。
- 写东西先想清楚“给谁看、解决什么问题”，言之有物、看对象——对内对外沟通都要“有的放矢”。
- 别靠大词和声势压人，靠事实和逻辑说话——用证据、数据和真实结论说服，不用“装腔作势借以吓人”。

**English:**

- Style reflects conduct: when a person's or organization's reports, emails, and proposals are stuffed with empty words and boilerplate, it usually reflects a style of work that doesn't connect with reality or solve problems — start by writing clearly and speaking plainly.
- Before writing, be clear about "who it is for and what problem it solves," say something of substance and keep the audience in mind — in internal and external communication alike, "shoot the arrow at the target."
- Don't overpower people with big words and bluster; speak through facts and logic — persuade with evidence, data, and real conclusions, not by "pretentious posturing meant to intimidate."
