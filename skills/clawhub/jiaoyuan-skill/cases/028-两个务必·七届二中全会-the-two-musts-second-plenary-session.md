# 两个务必·七届二中全会 The Two Musts: Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四九年三月，辽沈、淮海、平津三大战役后，国民党军队主力已被消灭，中国革命即将取得全国胜利。就在胜利前夜，教员在党的七届二中全会上作报告，提醒全党：因为胜利，党内的骄傲情绪、以功臣自居的情绪、停顿起来不求进步的情绪、贪图享乐的情绪可能生长；人民感谢、资产阶级捧场，可能征服队伍中的意志薄弱者。

**English:**

In March 1949, after the three great campaigns of Liaoxi-Shenyang, Huaihai, and Beiping-Tianjin, the main force of the Kuomintang armies had been wiped out and the Chinese revolution was about to win nationwide victory. On the very eve of victory, Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) delivered a report at the Second Plenary Session of the Party's Seventh Central Committee, warning the whole Party that, because of victory, feelings of pride, of considering oneself a hero of merit, of resting on one's laurels and ceasing to seek progress, and of craving pleasure might grow within the Party; and that the people's gratitude and the bourgeoisie's flattery might conquer the weak-willed in our ranks.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他预见胜利后的主要危险是糖衣炮弹：“可能有这样一些共产党人，他们是不曾被拿枪的敌人征服过的，他们在这些敌人面前不愧英雄的称号；但是经不起人们用糖衣裹着的炮弹的攻击，他们在糖弹面前要打败仗。”（《在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告》）他把眼前胜利放在长过程中看：“夺取全国胜利，这只是万里长征走完了第一步。……剧是必须从序幕开始的，但序幕还不是高潮。中国的革命是伟大的，但革命以后的路程更长，工作更伟大，更艰苦。”（《在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告》）据此他提出：“务必使同志们继续地保持谦虚、谨慎、不骄、不躁的作风，务必使同志们继续地保持艰苦奋斗的作风。”（《在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告》）

**English:**

He foresaw that the principal danger after victory was the sugar-coated bullet: "There may be some Communists, who were not conquered by enemies with guns and were worthy of the name of heroes for standing up to these enemies, but who cannot withstand sugar-coated bullets; they will be defeated by sugar-coated bullets." (《在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告》 "Report to the Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee of the Communist Party of China") He placed the victory at hand within the long course of history: "To win country-wide victory is only the first step in a long march of ten thousand li. ... The play must begin with a prologue, but the prologue is not the climax. The Chinese revolution is great, but the road after the revolution will be longer, the work greater and more arduous." (《在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告》 "Report to the Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee of the Communist Party of China") On this basis he proposed: "The comrades must be taught to remain modest, prudent and free from arrogance and rashness in their style of work; the comrades must be taught to preserve the style of plain living and hard struggle." (《在中国共产党第七届中央委员会第二次全体会议上的报告》 "Report to the Second Plenary Session of the Seventh Central Committee of the Communist Party of China")

## 行动 / The Action
他在报告中及时向全党发出警告和号召，要求全党预防被糖衣炮弹击倒的情况，保持优良作风、去掉不良作风；同时部署党的工作重心由乡村移到城市、以生产建设为中心，号召学会自己原来不懂的东西，继续用批评和自我批评这个武器保持清醒。

**English:**

In the report he promptly issued a warning and a call to the whole Party, requiring it to guard against being struck down by sugar-coated bullets, to preserve the fine style of work and cast off the bad, and at the same time he arranged for the Party's center of gravity to move from the countryside to the cities and for production and construction to become the center, calling for the learning of things one did not yet know and for continuing to use the weapon of criticism and self-criticism to stay clear-headed.

## 结果 / The Outcome
“两个务必”成为党在胜利和执政条件下防骄防腐的长期要求，为从夺取胜利转入巩固胜利、由革命转入建设作了思想上的准备。

**English:**

"The Two Musts" became a long-term requirement for the Party, under conditions of victory and governance, to guard against pride and corruption, and provided the ideological preparation for the transition from winning victory to consolidating it and from revolution to construction.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 越是取得大成果、被称赞追捧的时候，越要警惕“糖衣炮弹”——捧场和利益比明面的困难更能击垮意志薄弱的人。
- 把眼前的成功放在更长的时间尺度里看：“这只是万里长征走完了第一步”，成功后保持清醒、继续赶路，不把阶段成果当终点。
- 胜利会带来新的问题和新的能力要求，主动清零、去学原来不懂的东西，才能守住并放大成果。

**English:**

- The more you achieve and the more you are praised and courted, the more you must be on guard against "sugar-coated bullets" — flattery and inducements can bring down the weak-willed more readily than visible hardship.
- Place present success on a longer time scale: "This is only the first step in a long march of ten thousand li" — after success, stay clear-headed and keep moving, and don't mistake a stage result for the finish line.
- Victory brings new problems and new demands on capability; only by actively starting from zero and learning what you didn't know before can you hold on to and multiply the gains.
