# 统一战线中的独立自主 Independence and Initiative in the United Front

## 局势 / The Situation
抗战时期，国共结成抗日民族统一战线，但国民党是当权的党，处心积虑地限制共产党和革命力量的发展。党内有人主张"一切经过统一战线""一切服从统一战线"，实质上是放弃独立自主、把领导权让给国民党。如何在合作中既维护团结、又保存并发展自己，成为尖锐的现实问题。

**English:**

During the War of Resistance, the Kuomintang and the Communist Party formed the Anti-Japanese National United Front, but the Kuomintang was the party in power and schemed relentlessly to restrict the development of the Communist Party and the revolutionary forces. Some within the Party advocated "everything through the united front" and "everything subordinate to the united front," which in essence meant giving up independence and initiative and yielding leadership to the Kuomintang. How to both safeguard unity and preserve and develop oneself within the cooperation became an acute practical problem.

## 判断 / The Assessment
在统一战线中必须保持自己的独立性，否则合作就会变成混一。"保存党派和阶级的独立性，保存统一战线中的独立自主；不是因合作和统一而牺牲党派和阶级的必要权利，而是相反，坚持党派和阶级的一定限度的权利"（《统一战线中的独立自主问题》）；"我们的方针是统一战线中的独立自主，既统一，又独立。"（《统一战线中的独立自主问题》）更早提出统一战线策略时，就强调共产党和红军必须成为统一战线中的中心支柱："共产党和红军不但在现在充当着抗日民族统一战线的发起人，而且在将来的抗日政府和抗日军队中必然要成为坚强的台柱子"（《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》）。

**English:**

Within the united front one must preserve one's own independence; otherwise cooperation would dissolve into amalgamation. "Preserve the independence of the parties and classes, and preserve independence and initiative within the united front; do not sacrifice the necessary rights of the parties and classes for the sake of cooperation and unity, but on the contrary uphold the defined rights of the parties and classes to a certain extent" (《统一战线中的独立自主问题》, The Question of Independence and Initiative Within the United Front); "Our policy is independence and initiative within the united front, both unity and independence" (《统一战线中的独立自主问题》). When the united-front tactics were first put forward earlier, it was already stressed that the Communist Party and the Red Army must become the central pillar of the united front: "The Communist Party and the Red Army not only serve now as the initiators of the Anti-Japanese National United Front but will necessarily become the staunch mainstay in the future anti-Japanese government and anti-Japanese army" (《论反对日本帝国主义的策略》, On Tactics Against Japanese Imperialism).

## 行动 / The Action
在统一战线中坚持独立自主：该先得同意再做的先奏后斩，该造成既成事实再告知的先斩后奏，该暂时不做的斩而不奏，既决不一味迁就、自己束缚手脚，也决不轻易破裂统一战线。

**English:**

Independence and initiative were upheld within the united front: for matters requiring prior consent, report first and act afterward; for matters where a fait accompli should be created before notification, act first and report afterward; and for matters to be temporarily deferred, refrain from acting and from reporting. There was neither unprincipled accommodation and self-restriction, nor reckless rupture of the united front.

## 结果 / The Outcome
既维护了抗日民族统一战线，又保证了党和军队的独立发展，使革命力量在合作中不仅没有被削弱，反而发展壮大。

**English:**

The Anti-Japanese National United Front was maintained while the independent development of the Party and the army was guaranteed, so that the revolutionary forces, far from being weakened, actually grew and strengthened within the cooperation.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当进入合作或联盟关系时，借鉴"既统一，又独立"：合作不等于把自己融合消失，要在合作中守住自己的核心能力和独立判断，否则合作会慢慢变成被吞并。
2. 面对"要不要让步"的纠结，借鉴"坚持一定限度的权利"：让步有底线，以不伤害自己的根本为原则，为了大局可以做必要妥协，但不能牺牲赖以生存的核心。
3. 在联盟中争主导权，借鉴"做台柱子"：进入合作时先想清楚自己靠什么立足、能否成为不可替代的核心，否则容易在合作中被边缘化。

**English:**

1. When entering a cooperation or alliance, borrow "both unity and independence": cooperation does not mean dissolving yourself into the other; guard your core capabilities and independent judgment within the cooperation, otherwise cooperation will gradually turn into absorption.
2. When torn over "whether to concede," borrow "upholding defined rights to a certain extent": concessions have a bottom line, with the principle of not harming your own fundamentals; necessary compromises may be made for the larger picture, but never sacrifice the core on which you depend.
3. When vying for the leading role in an alliance, borrow "becoming the mainstay": before entering a cooperation, think through what you rely on to establish yourself and whether you can become an irreplaceable core, otherwise you will easily be marginalized in the cooperation.
