# 皖南事变·有理有利有节 The Southern Anhui Incident: Justified, Advantageous, and Restrained

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四一年一月，国民党顽固派发动皖南事变，袭击奉令北移的新四军，掀起第二次反共高潮。一面要打退反共顽固派的进攻，一面又要维持抗日民族统一战线、不使抗战大局破裂，这是摆在面前的严峻考验。

**English:**

In January 1941, the Kuomintang die-hards launched the Southern Anhui Incident, attacking the New Fourth Army as it moved north on orders and whipping up a second anti-Communist onslaught. Repelling the attack of the anti-Communist die-hards on one hand while maintaining the Anti-Japanese National United Front and keeping the overall War of Resistance from breaking down on the other was a severe test.

## 判断 / The Assessment
对反共顽固派的进攻必须针锋相对地斗争，但斗争要讲章法。对顽固派斗争的原则概括为三条：自卫、胜利、休战——"第一是自卫原则。人不犯我，我不犯人，人若犯我，我必犯人"；"第二是胜利原则。不斗则已，斗则必胜，决不可举行无计划无准备无把握的斗争"；"第三是休战原则"，打退进攻之后"适可而止"。并指出："这三个原则，换一句话来讲，就是"有理"，"有利"，"有节"。"（《目前抗日统一战线中的策略问题》）"但是斗争必须是有理、有利、有节的，三者缺一，就要吃亏。"（《关于打退第二次反共高潮的总结》）

**English:**

The attacks of the anti-Communist die-hards had to be fought tit for tat, but the struggle had to be methodical. The principles for the struggle against the die-hards were summed up in three: self-defense, victory, and truce—"First, the principle of self-defense. We will not attack unless we are attacked; if we are attacked, we will certainly counter-attack"; "Second, the principle of victory. We do not fight unless we are sure of victory; we must never fight a struggle that is unplanned, unprepared, and uncertain of victory"; "Third, the principle of a truce"—after repulsing the attack, "stop before going too far." He further pointed out: "In other words, these three principles are 'justified,' 'advantageous,' and 'restrained'" (《目前抗日统一战线中的策略问题》, Current Problems of Tactics in the Anti-Japanese United Front). "But the struggle must be justified, advantageous, and restrained; if any of the three is missing, we shall suffer" (《关于打退第二次反共高潮的总结》, Conclusions on the Repulse of the Second Anti-Communist Onslaught).

## 行动 / The Action
政治上揭露亲日派的反共阴谋，表明新四军抗战有功、遭袭属实；军事上坚决反击顽固派的进攻；同时任命新的军部整饬新四军，坚持抗战到底。打退进攻后适可而止，不使斗争扩大化，维持抗日统一战线。

**English:**

Politically, the pro-Japanese clique's anti-Communist plot was exposed, making clear that the New Fourth Army had served with distinction in the War of Resistance and had genuinely been attacked; militarily, the die-hards' attack was resolutely counter-attacked; at the same time, a new army headquarters was appointed to reorganize the New Fourth Army and carry the War of Resistance through to the end. After repulsing the attack, they stopped before going too far, kept the struggle from escalating, and maintained the Anti-Japanese United Front.

## 结果 / The Outcome
打退了第二次反共高潮，新四军在斗争中继续发展壮大，抗日民族统一战线得以维持，抗战大局没有破裂。

**English:**

The second anti-Communist onslaught was repulsed, the New Fourth Army continued to grow and strengthen in the struggle, the Anti-Japanese National United Front was maintained, and the overall situation of the War of Resistance did not break down.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 面对挑衅时借鉴"有理"：先让自己站住道理（对方先挑衅、我方被迫还击），赢得舆论和第三方支持，师出有名。
2. 借鉴"有利"：不斗则已、斗则必胜，绝不打无准备无把握之仗，选择最弱的对手、最有利的时机下手。
3. 借鉴"有节"：打退对方后适可而止，不为一时胜利扩大战线，给关系留下回旋余地，避免陷入无休止的消耗。

**English:**

1. When facing provocation, borrow "justified": first establish that you have justice on your side (the other party provoked first, and you were forced to hit back), win public opinion and third-party support, and fight with just cause.
2. Borrow "advantageous": do not fight unless sure of victory; never fight a war that is unprepared and uncertain; pick the weakest opponent and the most favorable moment to strike.
3. Borrow "restrained": after repulsing the opponent, stop before going too far; do not widen the front for a momentary victory; leave room for maneuver in the relationship and avoid sinking into endless attrition.
