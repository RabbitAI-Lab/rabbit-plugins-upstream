# 集中优势兵力各个歼灭 Concentrate Superior Forces to Destroy the Enemy One by One

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四六年解放战争全面爆发后，面对武器装备得到加强的国民党军，如何以相对弱小的力量打胜仗，需要一套明确有效的作战方法。此前我军以分散兵力打游击战为主，内战时期情况改变，作战方法也必须随之改变。

**English:**

After the War of Liberation broke out in full in 1946, facing a Kuomintang army with strengthened weapons and equipment, how to win battles with relatively weaker forces required a clear and effective method of fighting. Previously our army had fought mainly guerrilla warfare with dispersed forces, but conditions changed in the civil war period, and the method of fighting had to change accordingly.

## 判断 / The Assessment
集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人。"我军必须集中绝对优势的兵力，即集中六倍、或五倍、或四倍于敌的兵力、至少也要有三倍于敌的兵力，于适当时机，首先包围歼击敌军的一个旅（或团）。这个旅（或团），应当是敌军诸旅中较弱的"（《集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人》）。这种战法的效果："这种战法的效果是：一能全歼；二能速决。"（《集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人》）"集中兵力各个歼敌的原则，是我军从开始建军起十余年以来的优良传统"（《集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人》）。

**English:**

Concentrate superior forces and destroy the enemy one by one. "Our army must concentrate absolutely superior forces—that is, forces six times, five times, four times, or at least three times those of the enemy—and at an opportune moment first surround and annihilate one brigade (or regiment) of the enemy. This brigade (or regiment) should be the weaker one among the enemy's brigades" (《集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人》, Concentrate a Superior Force to Destroy the Enemy Forces One by One). On the effect of this method: "The effect of this method of fighting is: first, complete annihilation; second, quick decision" (《集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人》). "The principle of concentrating forces to destroy the enemy one by one has been a fine tradition of our army ever since its founding more than ten years ago" (《集中优势兵力，各个歼灭敌人》).

## 行动 / The Action
在战役部署和战术部署上都贯彻这一原则：先集中绝对优势兵力包围歼击敌军较弱的一路或一点，全歼速决，再依情况转用兵力，各个歼灭其他各路之敌，坚决反对平分兵力、处处攻击处处不得力的战法。

**English:**

This principle was carried through in both operational and tactical deployment: first concentrate absolutely superior forces to surround and annihilate a weaker column or point of the enemy, achieving complete annihilation and quick decision, then shift forces according to the situation to destroy the other enemy columns one by one—resolutely opposing the method of dividing forces evenly and attacking everywhere without achieving results anywhere.

## 结果 / The Outcome
这一战法成为解放战争时期人民解放军打歼灭战的基本战法，通过一次次各个歼灭敌人，逐步改变了敌我力量的对比，为转入战略反攻和决战创造了条件。

**English:**

This method became the basic method of the People's Liberation Army for fighting wars of annihilation during the War of Liberation; by destroying the enemy one by one, it gradually changed the balance of forces between the enemy and ourselves and created the conditions for shifting to the strategic counter-offensive and decisive engagements.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 资源有限时借鉴"集中优势兵力"：与其平均分配、处处撒胡椒面，不如在关键点上形成数倍于对手的相对优势，集中力量解决一个，再解决下一个。
2. 借鉴"先打弱敌"：选择对手最薄弱、最孤立、最无援助的环节作为突破口，先易后难，打一个成一个。
3. 借鉴"全歼速决"：每个动作力求彻底解决（做透、做成），不留尾巴、不拖泥带水，用一个个彻底的小胜积累成大胜。

**English:**

1. When resources are limited, borrow "concentrating superior forces": rather than distributing evenly and sprinkling pepper everywhere, form a relative advantage several times that of the opponent at the key point, concentrate force to solve one, then the next.
2. Borrow "attack the weak enemy first": choose the opponent's weakest, most isolated, least-supported link as the breakthrough point—first the easy, then the hard—winning one by one.
3. Borrow "complete annihilation and quick decision": strive to resolve every action thoroughly (doing it deeply and completely), leaving no loose ends and no drag; accumulate decisive small victories into a great victory.
