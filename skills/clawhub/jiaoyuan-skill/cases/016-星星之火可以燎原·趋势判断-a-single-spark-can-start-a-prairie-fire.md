# 星星之火可以燎原·趋势判断 A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire: Reading the Trend

## 局势 / The Situation
一九三〇年初，井冈山等根据地屡遭强敌“进剿”，革命主观力量大为削弱，红军和党内一部分同志对时局估量悲观，提出“红旗到底打得多久”的疑问，不赞成建立巩固的红色政权，主张用流动游击的方式扩大政治影响、等全国争取群众工作做好了再武装起义。教员写信答复，批评这种悲观思想。

**English:**

In early 1930, base areas such as the Jinggang Mountains came under repeated "suppression campaigns" by powerful enemies, and the revolution's own subjective forces were greatly weakened. A section of the Red Army and of the Party took a pessimistic view of the situation, raising the question "how long can the red flag keep flying"; they were opposed to establishing consolidated Red political power and advocated expanding political influence through roving guerrilla action, holding that armed uprisings should wait until the work of winning the masses nationwide was complete. Jiaoyuan (the Teacher) replied in a letter, criticizing this pessimistic thinking.

## 判断 / The Assessment
他提出判断形势不能只看表面现象：“我们看事情必须要看它的实质，而把它的现象只看作入门的向导，一进了门就要抓住它的实质，这才是可靠的科学的分析方法。”（《星星之火，可以燎原》）在分析引起革命高潮的各种矛盾确实在向前发展之后，他判断革命高潮快要到来，但又拒绝机械预言时日：“马克思主义者不是算命先生，未来的发展和变化，只应该也只能说出个大的方向，不应该也不可能机械地规定时日。”（《星星之火，可以燎原》）他明确反对只靠流动游击、轻视建立巩固根据地，主张以红色政权的巩固和扩大去促进全国革命高潮。

**English:**

He argued that one must not judge the situation by surface appearances alone: "When we look at a thing, we must examine its essence and treat its appearance merely as an usher at the threshold, and once we cross the threshold, we must grasp the essence of the thing; this is the only reliable and scientific method of analysis." (《星星之火，可以燎原》 "A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire") After analyzing that the various contradictions driving a revolutionary upsurge were indeed developing, he concluded that the upsurge was fast approaching, yet he refused to predict the timing mechanically: "A Marxist is not a fortune-teller. The future development and change can only be described in general direction, but should not and cannot be mechanically fixed in time." (《星星之火，可以燎原》 "A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire") He explicitly opposed relying solely on roving guerrilla action and belittling the building of consolidated base areas, advocating instead that the consolidation and expansion of Red political power be used to hasten the nationwide revolutionary upsurge.

## 行动 / The Action
他提出“朱德毛泽东式、方志敏式”的有根据地的路线：有计划地建设政权、深入土地革命、扩大人民武装，由乡赤卫队、区赤卫大队、县赤卫总队到地方红军直至正规红军，政权波浪式地向前扩大；在兵力使用上坚持“分兵以发动群众，集中以应付敌人”，反对不顾条件的分兵。

**English:**

He put forward the base-area line of "the Zhu De–Mao Zedong pattern and the Fang Zhimin pattern": building political power in a planned way, carrying the agrarian revolution deeper, and expanding the people's armed forces, from the township red guards, district red guard battalions, and county red guard columns up to local Red Army units and eventually the regular Red Army, with political power expanding outward in waves. In the use of troops, he insisted on "dividing the forces to arouse the masses and concentrating the forces to deal with the enemy," and opposed dividing the forces regardless of conditions.

## 结果 / The Outcome
对革命高潮趋势的判断和对建立根据地的强调，纠正了党内一部分人的悲观与流寇思想，红色政权的巩固扩大成为促进全国革命高潮的重要路线。

**English:**

The assessment of the approaching revolutionary upsurge and the emphasis on building base areas corrected the pessimism and roving-rebel mentality of some within the Party; the consolidation and expansion of Red political power became an important line for hastening the nationwide revolutionary upsurge.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
- 判断趋势要看矛盾是否真的在向前发展，而不是看当下力量的大小——看增长曲线和结构性变化，不为一时的账面数字所迷惑。
- 下趋势判断只给方向和依据，不机械地承诺时点——“大概什么时候成”可以谈方向，不能精确算命。
- 认准趋势后，要落到可积累的行动（建立根据地）而不是空等形势——趋势是干出来的，不是等出来的。

**English:**

- To read a trend, look at whether the underlying contradictions are really advancing, not at the size of present strength — watch the growth curve and structural change, and don't be misled by momentary bookkeeping numbers.
- When giving a trend judgment, state only the direction and the grounds, and don't mechanically commit to a timetable — "roughly when it will happen" can be discussed as a direction, but it cannot be predicted like fortune-telling.
- Once the trend is clear, translate it into actions that accumulate (building a base area) rather than waiting idly for the situation to ripen — trends are made, not awaited.
