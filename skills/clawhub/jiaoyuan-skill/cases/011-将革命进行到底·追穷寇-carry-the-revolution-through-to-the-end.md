# 将革命进行到底·追穷寇 Carry the Revolution Through to the End: Pursuing the Foe

## 局势 / The Situation
一九四八年底，辽沈战役已经胜利，淮海战役即将结束，国民党统治摇摇欲坠。这时出现一种主张：让革命就此止步，搞所谓"中间路线"，与国民党划江而治。是乘胜追击、将革命进行到底，还是中途停顿、给敌人喘息之机，成为摆在全中国人民面前的抉择。

**English:**

At the end of 1948, the Liaoshen Campaign had been won and the Huaihai Campaign was about to conclude; the Kuomintang's rule was tottering. At this point a view emerged: stop the revolution there, pursue a so-called "middle road," and divide rule with the Kuomintang along the Yangtze. Whether to press the pursuit while victorious and carry the revolution through to the end, or to halt halfway and give the enemy a breathing spell, became a choice confronting the entire Chinese people.

## 判断 / The Assessment
必须将革命进行到底，绝不能半途而废。"现在摆在中国人民、各民主党派、各人民团体面前的问题，是将革命进行到底呢，还是使革命半途而废呢？"（《将革命进行到底》）做到底，"那就是用革命的方法，坚决彻底干净全部地消灭一切反动势力"；半途而废，"那就是违背人民的意志，接受外国侵略者和中国反动派的意志，使国民党赢得养好创伤的机会，然后在一个早上猛扑过来，将革命扼死"（《将革命进行到底》）。

**English:**

The revolution must be carried through to the end; it must by no means be abandoned halfway. "The question now confronting the Chinese people, the democratic parties, and the people's organizations is whether to carry the revolution through to the end or to abandon it halfway" (《将革命进行到底》, Carry the Revolution Through to the End). Carrying it through to the end "means using revolutionary methods to resolutely, thoroughly, wholly, and completely wipe out all reactionary forces"; abandoning it halfway "means going against the will of the people, accepting the will of the foreign aggressors and the Chinese reactionaries, giving the Kuomintang a chance to lick its wounds, and then, one morning, suddenly pouncing upon and strangling the revolution" (《将革命进行到底》).

## 行动 / The Action
号召全国人民、各民主党派、各人民团体一致行动，将革命进行到底，彻底消灭反动势力，随即发起渡江作战，向全国进军，解放全中国。

**English:**

The people of the whole country, the democratic parties, and the people's organizations were called upon to act in unison to carry the revolution through to the end and thoroughly destroy the reactionary forces; the crossing-the-Yangtze operation was then launched, advancing on the whole country and liberating all of China.

## 结果 / The Outcome
一九四九年人民革命取得了全国胜利，在全国范围内推翻了国民党的反动统治，建立了人民民主专政的共和国。

**English:**

In 1949 the people's revolution won nationwide victory, overthrowing the reactionary rule of the Kuomintang throughout the country and establishing a republic of the people's democratic dictatorship.

## 可迁移点 / Transferable Lessons
1. 当胜利在望时，借鉴"追穷寇"思维：接近目标时最忌松懈和半途而废，要给对手致命一击、彻底解决问题，不留后患。
2. 面对"要不要见好就收"的诱惑，借鉴"不给对手喘息之机"：对手一旦获得喘息，就会卷土重来，付出的代价远大于眼前的让步。
3. 重大决策前，先想清楚"做到底"与"半途而废"两种后果的代价，把选择当作不可回避的十字路口来对待，而不是含糊拖延。

**English:**

1. When victory is in sight, borrow the "pursuing the foe" mindset: near the goal, slackness and abandoning halfway are most to be feared; deliver a fatal blow to the opponent and solve the problem thoroughly, leaving no future trouble.
2. When tempted by "quit while you're ahead," borrow "not giving the opponent a breathing spell": once the opponent catches its breath, it will come back; the price paid will far exceed the present concession.
3. Before a major decision, first think through the costs of the two consequences—"carrying it through" versus "abandoning halfway"—and treat the choice as an unavoidable crossroads rather than vaguely procrastinating.
