## Description:

Jiaoyuan is a historical thinking-method skill that helps agents analyze real-world dilemmas through distilled methods from Mao Zedong's published works, with deduction and coaching modes bounded away from impersonation, political advocacy, and professional advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to frame career, entrepreneurship, decision-making, team, competition, learning, setback, relationship, change, and uncertainty problems through a reusable historical methodology. It produces reflective decision support and question-led coaching, not political authority, historical adjudication, or medical, legal, financial, psychological, or other professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's Mao Zedong-themed methodology corpus could be mistaken for political authority, propaganda, or historical impersonation.

Mitigation: Use it only as reflective thinking-method support; keep outputs framed as methodology analysis and decline requests for political stance, impersonation, or invented original statements.

Risk: Users may over-rely on decision guidance for medical, legal, financial, psychological, or other professional matters.

Mitigation: State that outputs do not replace professional advice and redirect high-stakes decisions to qualified professionals.

Risk: Corpus materials have jurisdiction-dependent copyright and public-domain considerations before the documented 2026-12-31 date.

Mitigation: Confirm rights before commercial reproduction or redistribution of corpus text, and consult the bundled corpus provenance and edition notes.

Risk: Future versions could add automatic data collection, file mutation, or new execution behavior not present in the scanned release.

Mitigation: Review changelogs and rerun security scanning before installing or deploying later versions.
