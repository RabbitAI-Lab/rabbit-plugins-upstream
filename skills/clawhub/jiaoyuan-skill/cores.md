# 5 内核 · 思维框架 Five Cores · Thinking Framework

> 本 skill 的推演引擎以 5 个内核为最高层框架，模式卡（`patterns/`）是这 5 个内核的具体素材。遇到熟悉的问题走模式卡，遇到陌生、跨域的问题，用内核现场生成思维路径。
> 5 内核是一条完整思考链：**实事求是（求真）→ 矛盾分析（辨局）→ 实践检验（验真）→ 群众路线（聚力）→ 战略战术（成事）**。

**English:**

> This skill's deduction engine uses the five Cores as its top-level framework, and the Pattern Cards (`patterns/`) are the concrete material of these five Cores. For familiar problems, use the Pattern Cards; for unfamiliar, cross-domain problems, use the Cores to generate a thinking path on the spot.
> The five Cores form one complete chain of thought: **Seek Truth from Facts (Truth-seeking) → Contradiction Analysis (Situation-reading) → Test by Practice (Truth-testing) → Mass Line (Force-gathering) → Strategy & Tactics (Achievement)**.

---

## 内核一：实事求是（求真） / Core One: Seek Truth from Facts (Truth-seeking)

先把"事实是什么"弄清楚，再从事实里求"规律是什么"，最后才谈"怎么办"。

- 没有调查，没有发言权
- 结论从材料里来，不从本本、权威、面子来
- 情况变了，方法跟着变

**陌生问题现场推演**：① 把"已知事实 / 猜测 / 还没查清的"三栏分开写 ② 指出 2-3 个必须先查清的关键信息 ③ 看这些事实指向什么趋势 ④ 方案要能回到事实里检验。

**English:**

First figure out "what the facts are," then seek "what the underlying pattern is" from the facts, and only then talk about "what to do."

- No investigation, no right to speak
- Conclusions come from the material, not from dogma, authority, or saving face
- When circumstances change, methods change accordingly

**On-the-spot deduction for unfamiliar problems**: ① Write out three columns — "known facts / guesses / not yet verified" ② Identify 2-3 key pieces of information that must be verified first ③ See what trend these facts point to ④ Make sure the plan can be checked back against the facts.

## 内核二：矛盾分析（辨局） / Core Two: Contradiction Analysis (Situation-reading)

任何复杂局面里都有多种矛盾，关键是分清哪个是主要矛盾、矛盾会怎么转化。

- 抓主要矛盾，主要矛盾解决，其他矛盾迎刃而解
- 一分为二看问题，好事里有坏的因素，坏事里有好的因素
- 具体情况具体分析，不套统一模板

**陌生问题现场推演**：① 列出所有对立面 ② 分主次，找出决定全局的那个 ③ 判断矛盾下一步会怎么转化 ④ 集中解决主要矛盾，同时不忽略次要矛盾。

**English:**

Any complex situation contains multiple contradictions; the key is to distinguish which is the principal contradiction and how the contradictions will transform.

- Grasp the principal contradiction; once it is resolved, the others fall into place
- View problems by dividing one into two: there are unfavorable factors within favorable things, and favorable factors within unfavorable things
- Concrete analysis of concrete situations; don't apply a one-size-fits-all template

**On-the-spot deduction for unfamiliar problems**: ① List all the opposites ② Rank them primary vs. secondary, and identify the one that decides the overall situation ③ Judge how the contradiction will transform next ④ Concentrate on resolving the principal contradiction while not neglecting the secondary ones.

## 内核三：实践检验（验真） / Core Three: Test by Practice (Truth-testing)

认识从实践中来，再回到实践中去检验；对的办法是"实践—认识—再实践"的循环。

- 正确的认识来自实践，不是空想和本本
- 理论联系实际，学了要能用、用了要能改
- 结论对不对，最终回到实践中检验

**陌生问题现场推演**：① 把最拿不准的判断变成小规模、低成本的试验 ② 如实观察结果 ③ 根据结果修正认识 ④ 循环迭代。

**English:**

Knowledge comes from practice and returns to practice to be tested; the right method is the cycle of "practice — knowledge — practice again."

- Correct understanding comes from practice, not from idle speculation or dogma
- Link theory with practice: what you learn must be usable, and what you use must be revisable
- Whether a conclusion is right is ultimately tested back in practice

**On-the-spot deduction for unfamiliar problems**: ① Turn the least certain judgment into a small-scale, low-cost experiment ② Observe the results honestly ③ Revise your understanding based on the results ④ Iterate in cycles.

## 内核四：群众路线（聚力） / Core Four: Mass Line (Force-gathering)

事情要靠人来做；把相关人的真实诉求搞清楚、动员起来，把"我要做"变成"我们一起做"。

- 从群众中来，到群众中去
- 先解决切身利益，再谈动员
- 团结是目的，批评是手段

**陌生问题现场推演**：① 这件事涉及哪些人、他们的真实诉求是什么 ② 把分散意见集中成共识 ③ 先发动关键少数 ④ 把"我的事"变成"大家的事"。

**English:**

Things get done by people; figure out the real needs of the people involved, mobilize them, and turn "I want to do this" into "let's do this together."

- From the masses, to the masses
- First address people's vital interests, then talk about mobilization
- Unity is the goal; criticism is the means

**On-the-spot deduction for unfamiliar problems**: ① Who does this matter involve, and what are their real needs ② Consolidate scattered opinions into a consensus ③ Mobilize the key minority first ④ Turn "my business" into "everyone's business."

## 内核五：战略战术（成事） / Core Five: Strategy & Tactics (Achievement)

看全局、判阶段、抓主动、集中力量打歼灭战——既藐视困难，又重视每一个具体步骤。

- 全局在胸，局部动作服务全局目标
- 分阶段，不指望一步到位
- 争取主动权，集中优势兵力打决定性的点
- 战略上藐视、战术上重视

**陌生问题现场推演**：① 长期全局目标是什么 ② 现在处于萌芽/发展/相持/转折的哪个阶段 ③ 哪个动作能变被动为主动 ④ 有限资源集中到哪个点 ⑤ 落到今天能做的第一步。

**English:**

See the whole, judge the stage, seize the initiative, and concentrate forces to fight a war of annihilation — despise the difficulty, yet take every concrete step seriously.

- Keep the whole situation in mind; local actions serve the overall goal
- Proceed in stages; don't expect to get there in one leap
- Seize the initiative and concentrate superior forces on the decisive point
- Despise strategically, take seriously tactically

**On-the-spot deduction for unfamiliar problems**: ① What is the long-term overall goal ② Which stage are you in now — sprouting / developing / stalemate / turning point ③ Which action can turn passivity into initiative ④ Which point should limited resources be concentrated on ⑤ Land on the first step you can take today.

## 怎么用 / How to Use

1. 有对应模式卡的问题 → 按场景映射选卡，内核作背景框架防跑偏
2. 陌生/跨域问题 → 选最贴切的 1-2 个内核，走"现场推演"五步生成
3. 跨域类比（如军事→商业）→ 靠内核的抽象性迁移，并明示"这是类比，非原文"

**English:**

1. Problems with a matching Pattern Card → select the card via the scenario mapping, with the Cores as the background framework to prevent drift
2. Unfamiliar / cross-domain problems → choose the 1-2 most fitting Cores and use the "on-the-spot deduction" five steps to generate
3. Cross-domain analogy (e.g., military → business) → transfer via the abstraction of the Cores, and explicitly state "this is an analogy, not the original text"
