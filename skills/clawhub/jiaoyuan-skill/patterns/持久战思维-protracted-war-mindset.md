# 持久战思维 Protracted War Mindset

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 面对明显强于自己的对手或极其困难的目标，短期难以取胜
- 资源、实力、经验都不足，却有必须达成的长期目标
- 内心在两个极端之间摇摆：要么想速胜，要么想放弃

**English:**

- Facing an opponent clearly stronger than you, or an extremely difficult goal that cannot be won in the short term
- Short on resources, strength, and experience, yet having a long-term goal that must be achieved
- Wavering between two extremes: wanting a quick victory on one hand, wanting to give up on the other

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 全面盘点敌我双方的特点，不只看表面的强弱，而看长期起作用的因素
2. 同时驳斥两个错误倾向：速胜论（冒进）与亡国论（悲观）
3. 得出"必须经过长期阶段"的判断，画出分阶段路线图
4. 用主动的战术动作扭转局部的被动：防御中的进攻、持久中的速决、内线中的外线
5. 积小胜为大胜，靠时间换空间、靠量变积累质变
6. 动员最广大的力量，把胜利的根基扎在最深处
7. 把必胜信念与长期韧劲合二为一：既不因一时强弱而动摇，也不指望一朝翻盘

**English:**

1. Take a full inventory of the characteristics of both sides, looking not only at surface strength or weakness but at the factors that work over the long term
2. Refute the two erroneous tendencies at the same time: the theory of quick victory (rash advance) and the theory of national subjugation (pessimism)
3. Reach the judgment that "a protracted stage is necessary," and draw a phased roadmap
4. Use active tactical moves to reverse local passivity: offense within defense, quick decisions within a protracted war, exterior lines within interior lines
5. Turn small victories into a big victory, trading time for space and letting quantitative accumulation bring qualitative change
6. Mobilize the broadest forces and plant the roots of victory at the deepest level
7. Fuse the conviction of certain victory with long-term perseverance: neither waver with temporary strength or weakness, nor expect to turn everything around in a single stroke

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 不指望速胜，不因一时强弱而悲观，判断建立在客观条件分析上
- 以时间换空间，用阶段目标替代一步到位的幻想
- 每一仗都求局部的相对优势，用连续小胜换全局胜势

**English:**

- Do not count on a quick victory and do not turn pessimistic over temporary strength or weakness; base judgment on analysis of objective conditions
- Trade time for space, and replace the fantasy of getting there in one leap with staged goals
- Seek local relative superiority in every fight, and convert a string of small victories into an overall winning position

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：实力不对称、时间站在己方、可以通过持续积累改变力量对比的长期竞争（创业、个人成长、市场追赶）。
- 反例：需要立即决断的短期危机——拖延会错失唯一窗口；"持久"不是消极等待，必须配主动的战术动作，否则只是拖延症。

**English:**

- Applies to: long-term competition in which strength is asymmetric, time is on your side, and the balance of forces can be changed through sustained accumulation (starting a business, personal growth, catching up in the market).
- Counterexamples: short-term crises that demand immediate decision — delay would miss the only window; "protracted" does not mean passive waiting; it must be paired with active tactical moves, otherwise it is mere procrastination.
