# 量变到质变 From Quantitative to Qualitative Change

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 学东西、练技能，迟迟看不到明显进步就想放弃
- 只想"一步到位"搞个大突破，看不起每天一点点的积累
- 看不到问题的恶化是长期小毛病攒出来的，等到崩了才惊觉
- 产品、业务卡在瓶颈期，误以为永远到不了下一阶段

**English:**

- Learning or practicing a skill and wanting to give up because no obvious progress is visible for a long time
- Wanting only a "one-shot" big breakthrough and looking down on the little daily accumulation
- Failing to see that a problem's worsening is built up from long-term small faults, and only being startled when it collapses
- A product or business stuck in a bottleneck, mistakenly thinking it will never reach the next stage

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 认清事物运动有两种状态：相对静止的状态（量变）和显著变动的状态（质变）
2. 量变阶段只有数量的变化、没有性质的变化，所以看着"好像没变"
3. 数量的变化积累到某一个最高点，就会引起统一物的分解，发生质变
4. 所以持续、不起眼的积累不是白费，是在为质变做准备
5. 反过来，坏事也是从小处开始积累的，要警惕量变的早期信号
6. 判断处在哪个阶段：是"旧的量和质"在减少，还是"新的量和质"在增长
7. 该加速积累时耐住性子，该促成质变时果断出手

**English:**

1. Recognize that things move in two states: a relatively static state (quantitative change) and a markedly changing state (qualitative change)
2. In the quantitative stage there is only change in quantity, not in quality — which is why it looks "as if nothing has changed"
3. When quantitative change accumulates to a certain peak, it causes the unity of opposites to break down and qualitative change to occur
4. So sustained, unremarkable accumulation is not wasted; it is preparing for qualitative change
5. Conversely, bad things also accumulate from small beginnings, so be alert to the early signals of quantitative change
6. Judge which stage you are in: are the "old quantity and quality" decreasing, or are the "new quantity and quality" growing?
7. Endure patiently when accumulation should be accelerated, and act decisively when qualitative change should be brought about

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 不因一时看不到结果就否定积累的价值
- 把大目标拆成可计量的量变，持续做
- 警惕微小的恶化信号，别等问题"质变"成事故

**English:**

- Do not deny the value of accumulation just because results are not yet visible
- Break a big goal into measurable quantitative changes and keep at it
- Be alert to small signals of deterioration; do not wait for the problem to "qualitatively change" into an accident

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：技能学习、习惯养成、业务增长、债务/风险积累等有连续积累曲线的事。
- 反例：有些变化不是渐进的，是突变、跃迁或纯随机事件（黑天鹅、技术范式颠覆），机械套用"量变积累"会错判时机；也并非所有量变都必然导向想要的质变，方向和结构比堆数量更重要。

**English:**

- Applies to: things with a continuous accumulation curve — skill learning, habit formation, business growth, debt/risk accumulation.
- Counterexamples: some changes are not gradual but sudden, discontinuous leaps or purely random events (black swans, paradigm-shifting technologies); mechanically applying "quantitative accumulation" will misjudge the timing. Nor does every quantitative change necessarily lead to the desired qualitative change — direction and structure matter more than piling up quantity.
