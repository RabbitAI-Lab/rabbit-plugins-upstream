# 改造主观世界与客观世界 Transform the Subjective and the Objective World

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 总想着改变环境、改变别人，从不回头改变自己的认知和方法
- 换了个环境还是老样子，因为自己没变
- 认知跟不上现实，却用旧眼光看新世界
- 只想"搞定外面"，忽视"更新自己"

**English:**

- Always wanting to change the environment and other people, but never looking back to change your own cognition and methods
- Changing the environment yet remaining the same, because you yourself have not changed
- Cognition lagging behind reality, yet viewing the new world through old eyes
- Wanting only to "fix the outside" while neglecting to "update yourself"

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先分清两件事：改造客观世界（外部的事）和改造主观世界（自己的认知、能力、方法）
2. 承认二者是一体的：改造外部的同时，也在改造自己
3. 要改造外部，先要改造自己的认识能力——看不准，就做不对
4. 改造主观世界，具体是改造"主观世界同客观世界的关系"——让自己的认识贴近现实
5. 在实践里改造：一边做，一边更新自己的认识
6. 用改造后的主观，反过来更有力地改造客观
7. 循环往复，每一次循环都让主观和客观更接近

**English:**

1. First distinguish two things: transforming the objective world (external matters) and transforming the subjective world (your own cognition, abilities, and methods)
2. Accept that the two are one: while transforming the outside, you are also transforming yourself
3. To transform the outside, first transform your own ability to understand — if you cannot see clearly, you cannot act correctly
4. Transforming the subjective world concretely means transforming "the relationship between the subjective world and the objective world" — bringing your understanding closer to reality
5. Transform through practice: as you act, keep updating your understanding
6. Use the transformed subjective to transform the objective still more powerfully
7. Repeat in cycles, each cycle bringing the subjective and the objective closer

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 改变结果，从改变自己的认知和方法开始
- 把"自我更新"当作一项和"做事"并列的任务，不靠它自然发生
- 认识落后于现实时，先补认识再行动

**English:**

- To change results, start by changing your own cognition and methods
- Treat "self-renewal" as a task on par with "getting things done," not something that happens by itself
- When understanding lags behind reality, first catch up on understanding before acting

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：个人成长、转型期、认知升级、需要"换脑子"才能做对的事。
- 反例：别把"改造主观"变成自我攻击或无限内耗——有些问题的根源在外部结构性条件（制度、资源、环境），该改外部就要改外部；主观改造要"治病救人"式地对待自己，不是否定自己。

**English:**

- Applies to: personal growth, transition periods, cognitive upgrading, and things that require "changing your mindset" to do right.
- Counterexamples: do not turn "transforming the subjective" into self-attack or endless internal friction — some problems are rooted in external structural conditions (institutions, resources, environment); where the outside needs changing, change the outside. Subjective transformation should treat yourself in a "cure the sickness to save the patient" spirit, not negate yourself.
