# 理论联系实际 Linking Theory with Practice

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 学了很多课、读了很多书，一到动手就不会
- 空谈概念、满口术语，却解决不了眼前的具体问题
- 埋头干活不总结，凭经验重复，遇到新情况就抓瞎
- 把理论当教条，遇到实际问题硬套，套不上就怪现实

**English:**

- You've taken many courses and read many books, yet freeze the moment you have to put them into practice.
- You talk in abstractions and jargon, yet cannot solve the concrete problem in front of you.
- You work head-down without summing up, repeating from experience alone, and get lost when a new situation arises.
- You treat theory as dogma, force it onto real problems, and blame reality when it won't fit.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 理论是箭，实际是靶，放箭要对准靶
2. 先问：我手里这套理论/方法，要解决的"靶子"是什么？
3. 用理论去分析实际，而不是用实际去迁就理论
4. 在应用中检验理论，合不上就修正理论或换方法
5. 反对两种病：只讲理论不碰实际的教条主义，只闷头干不总结的经验主义
6. 最好的状态是"主观和客观、理论和实践、知和行的具体的历史的统一"

**English:**

1. Theory is the arrow; practice is the target. Aim the arrow at the target.
2. First ask: what "target" is this theory or method in my hands meant to hit?
3. Use theory to analyze practice, not practice to accommodate theory.
4. Test theory in application; when it doesn't fit, correct the theory or change the method.
5. Oppose two ailments: dogmatism, which speaks only of theory without touching practice, and empiricism, which works head-down without summing up.
6. The ideal state is "the concrete, historical unity of the subjective and the objective, of theory and practice, of knowing and doing."

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 学是为了用，用要回到学，双向循环
- 讲任何道理先落到一个具体对象上
- 别用"我懂理论"回避"我做不好"，也别用"我有经验"拒绝新知识

**English:**

- Learn in order to use, and return from use to learning — a two-way loop.
- Anchor every principle to a concrete object before arguing it.
- Don't hide behind "I understand the theory" to dodge "I can't do it well," nor reject new knowledge with "I have experience."

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：技能/知识的学习转化、方案落地、方法论应用。
- 反例：纯基础研究、前沿探索（暂时没有"实际"可对）；以及需要先充分理解理论框架再动手的领域，过早"联系实际"可能走偏——联系实际不等于急功近利。

**English:**

- Applies to: converting skills and knowledge into practice, landing a plan, applying a methodology.
- Counterexamples: pure basic research and frontier exploration (where there is no "practice" to match yet); and fields where you must first fully grasp the theoretical framework before acting, where linking theory with practice too early can lead astray — linking theory with practice is not the same as chasing quick results.
