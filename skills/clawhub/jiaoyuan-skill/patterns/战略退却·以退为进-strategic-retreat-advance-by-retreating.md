# 战略退却·以退为进 Strategic Retreat: Advance by Retreating

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 硬拼拼不过，继续耗下去只会把本钱耗光
- 在错误的方向上越用力越糟，需要止损撤出
- 被更强的对手逼到墙角，正面对抗必输

**English:**

- Unable to win by slugging it out, and continuing to grind will only drain the capital dry
- The harder you push in the wrong direction the worse it gets, so you need to cut losses and pull out
- Pushed into a corner by a stronger opponent, where head-on confrontation means certain defeat

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先判断：当前继续硬拼，是"坚持"还是"送死"？把力量对比摆出来看
2. 承认敌强我弱时，硬扛等于被消耗；保存实力才是第一位的
3. 主动后撤、收缩、止损，把有生力量保住——退是手段，不是认输
4. 退的同时留一手：退到对自己有利的位置，诱使对手进入不熟悉的区域
5. 等待或制造对手疲惫、犯错、战线拉长的时机
6. 一旦对手露出破绽，集中力量反击，用一次胜仗把"退"的账连本带利赚回来
7. 记住退是"有计划的战略步骤"，不是溃逃：退之前就想好了怎么回

**English:**

1. First judge: in the current situation, is continuing to slug it out "persistence" or "walking into death"? Lay out the balance of forces and look at it
2. Accept that when the enemy is strong and we are weak, holding on hard means being ground down; preserving strength comes first
3. Voluntarily pull back, contract, and cut losses to preserve effective strength — retreat is a means, not an admission of defeat
4. While retreating, keep a move in reserve: fall back to a position favorable to yourself and lure the opponent into unfamiliar territory
5. Wait for or create the moment when the opponent is exhausted, makes a mistake, or overextends the front
6. The moment the opponent shows an opening, concentrate force to counterattack, and use one victory to win back the "retreat" with interest
7. Remember that retreat is a "planned strategic step", not a rout: before retreating, you have already thought out how to return

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 实力不济时，保存实力优先于争一时之气
- 撤退要主动、有计划、留后手，不是被动挨打
- 退是为了更好地进，退的时候就要埋下反攻的伏笔

**English:**

- When strength is inadequate, preserving strength takes priority over winning a momentary contest of pride
- Retreat must be voluntary, planned, and leave a reserve move — it is not passively taking a beating
- Retreat is for the sake of a better advance; when retreating, plant the seed of the counteroffensive

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：竞争/市场处于劣势时的战略收缩、止损退出、以时间换空间的僵持局面。
- 反例："退"不能退成习惯性逃避；如果退之前没想好怎么回，一退再退就成了真溃败；该坚持的窗口期不该退。

**English:**

- Applies when: strategic contraction, loss-cutting exit, or trading space for time in a competitive or market disadvantage, or in a stalemate.
- Counterexamples: "retreat" must not become habitual avoidance; if you have not thought out how to return before retreating, retreating again and again becomes a real rout; the window periods that should be held must not be surrendered.
