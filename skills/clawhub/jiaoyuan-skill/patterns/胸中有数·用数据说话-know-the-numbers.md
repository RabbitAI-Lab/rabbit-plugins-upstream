# 胸中有数·用数据说话 Know the Numbers

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 凭感觉、凭印象做决策，说不清关键数字
- 讨论问题只有形容词，没有数量、比例、界限
- 定政策、分资源、判好坏，缺少量化的门槛
- 汇报里"大概""差不多"满天飞，没法验证

**English:**

- Making decisions by feel and impression, unable to state the key figures
- Discussing problems only in adjectives, with no quantities, proportions, or thresholds
- Setting policies, allocating resources, and judging good from bad without quantified thresholds
- Reports full of "roughly" and "more or less", impossible to verify

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先问：这件事的数量方面是什么？占比、人数、频率、界限各是多少
2. 明白任何质量都表现为一定的数量，没有数量就没有质量
3. 找出决定事情性质的那个"数量界限"——超过多少就变质
4. 做基本调查、基本统计，不凭印象、不主观拍板
5. 把关键数字拿到手再定政策，数字不清就先别下结论
6. 用数据检验判断：积极、中间、反对各占多少，心里要有本账

**English:**

1. First ask: what is the quantitative side of this matter? What are the proportions, headcounts, frequencies, and thresholds?
2. Understand that every quality manifests through some quantity — without quantity there is no quality
3. Find the "quantitative threshold" that determines the nature of a thing — past how much does it change in nature
4. Do basic investigation and basic statistics; do not decide by impression or subjective fiat
5. Get the key figures before setting policy; if the figures are unclear, hold off on conclusions
6. Test judgments with data: know how many are active, how many in the middle, how many opposed — keep a clear ledger in your head

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 关键决策必须有数量分析，拒绝"胸中无数"
- 先调查统计，再定政策，顺序不能反
- 分清"数量界限"，很多对错就是一条线
- 数字要为判断服务，不是为数字而数字

**English:**

- Key decisions must rest on quantitative analysis; reject having "no numbers in your head"
- Investigate and count first, then set policy — the order cannot be reversed
- Draw the "quantitative threshold" clearly; many rights and wrongs are just one line apart
- Figures must serve judgment, not be pursued for their own sake

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：定政策、分资源、定门槛、做判断前需要量化支撑的场景。
- 反例：数字不等于事实——错误的数据比没数据更危险；不是所有事都能量化（方向、价值观、审美）；有数之后还要会分析，光堆数字、不做解读是另一种偷懒。

**English:**

- Applies when: setting policy, allocating resources, defining thresholds, or making judgments that need quantitative support.
- Counterexamples: numbers are not the same as facts — wrong data is more dangerous than no data; not everything can be quantified (direction, values, aesthetics); after you have the numbers you still need to analyze them — piling up figures without interpretation is another form of laziness.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "在任何群众运动中，群众积极拥护的有多少，反对的有多少，处于中间状态的有多少，这些都必须有个基本的调查，基本的分析，不可无根据地、主观地决定问题。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 群众
>
> "In any mass movement it is essential to make a basic investigation and analysis of how many actively support, how many oppose, and how many remain in the middle; problems must never be decided without basis or subjectively." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), The Masses
