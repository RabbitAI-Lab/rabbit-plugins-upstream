# 具体问题具体分析 Concrete Analysis of Concrete Problems

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 把别人的成功经验、模板、SOP 直接搬到自己的项目上，结果水土不服
- 用同一个办法处理所有客户、所有情况，一刀切
- 遇到问题先翻"正确答案"，不先看清楚自己面对的到底是什么
- 把行业通行的打法当成不可变的真理，不结合自身条件

**English:**

- Transplanting others' successful experience, templates, and SOPs directly onto your own project, only to find they do not fit the soil
- Using one method to handle all customers and all situations, one size fits all
- Reaching for the "correct answer" first when a problem arises, without first seeing clearly what you are actually facing
- Treating industry-standard approaches as immutable truths without combining them with your own conditions

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先问：我面对的这件事，跟别的事有什么不同？
2. 不套通用答案，先界定"这个"问题的具体性质
3. 分析矛盾在总体上、在各方面、在各阶段各自的特点
4. 找出它区别于其他事物的特殊性——特殊性才是它之所以是它的原因
5. 根据特殊性选方法，方法跟着情况走
6. 情况变了，特殊性也变了，方法要重新分析
7. 反对只讲共性不讲个性的教条，也反对只认个性不找规律的相对主义

**English:**

1. First ask: what makes this matter I am facing different from other matters?
2. Do not apply a generic answer; first define the concrete nature of "this" problem
3. Analyze the particular characteristics of the contradiction as a whole, in each aspect, and in each stage
4. Find the particularity that distinguishes it from other things — it is its particularity that makes it what it is
5. Choose the method according to the particularity; the method follows the situation
6. When the situation changes, the particularity changes too, and the method must be re-analyzed
7. Oppose the dogma that speaks only of the general and ignores the particular, and oppose the relativism that sees only the particular and seeks no laws

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先定性再定量，先看清"是什么"再谈"怎么办"
- 没有放之四海而皆准的方法，只有针对具体对象的方法
- 经验可借鉴，不可照搬

**English:**

- Determine the nature before the quantity; first see clearly "what it is" before discussing "what to do"
- There is no universally applicable method, only methods tailored to the specific object
- Experience can be borrowed, not copied

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：差异化场景（产品定位、客户沟通、教学设计、治疗方案）。
- 反例：标准化、规模化、讲究一致性的场景（工业生产、法规执行、基础流程）过度强调"具体分析"会牺牲效率；"具体分析"也不等于否定一般规律和共同原则。

**English:**

- Applies to: differentiated scenarios (product positioning, customer communication, instructional design, treatment plans).
- Counterexamples: in scenarios that stress standardization, scale, and consistency (industrial production, regulatory enforcement, basic processes), over-emphasizing "concrete analysis" sacrifices efficiency; "concrete analysis" also does not mean denying general laws and common principles.
