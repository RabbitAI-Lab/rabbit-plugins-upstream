# 运动战·歼灭战 Mobile Warfare and Annihilation

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 目标摊得太大，什么都想做，结果什么都没打透
- 用"消耗"的方式做事，花了大把力气，问题却没被真正解决
- 追求"打中"而不是"打透"，处处浅尝辄止

**English:**

- Spreading goals too wide, wanting to do everything and thoroughly accomplishing nothing
- Working by "attrition", spending a great deal of effort while the problem is never really solved
- Pursuing "hits" rather than "breakthroughs", stopping at a shallow touch everywhere

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先定调：这一阶段的核心任务是"消灭问题"，不是"磨洋工"
2. 拒绝摊大饼，把战场选在能灵活机动的空间，而不是死守一个点
3. 目标是"歼灭"——把一个问题彻底解决掉，而不是击溃式地敷衍
4. 用运动的方式打：快进快退、快聚快散，哪里有机可乘打哪里
5. 每打一处就集中力量打透一处，"伤十处不如断一处"
6. 不恋战、不死磕：打得赢就打，打不赢就走，保存自己优先
7. 一个接一个地歼灭，用"彻底解决"的连续胜利积累出整体的胜利

**English:**

1. First set the tone: the core task of this stage is "eliminating problems", not "soldiering on half-heartedly"
2. Refuse to spread thin; choose the battleground in space where you can maneuver flexibly rather than holding a single point to the death
3. Aim at "annihilation" — solving a problem thoroughly — rather than brushing it off in a rout-like, perfunctory way
4. Fight by maneuvering: advance and withdraw quickly, concentrate and disperse quickly, strike wherever there is an opening
5. Concentrate force to break through wherever you strike — "wounding ten places is worse than cutting one off"
6. Do not cling to battle or butt heads: fight if you can win, leave if you cannot, with self-preservation first
7. Annihilate one after another, accumulating overall victory through the successive victories of "thorough solutions"

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 求"解决"不求"打过"，一次彻底解决胜过十次半途而废
- 保持机动，不死守一点，哪里能赢打哪里
- 打不赢就撤，把力量留给能赢的下一仗

**English:**

- Seek "resolution", not merely "having fought"; one thorough solution beats ten abandoned halfway
- Keep mobile, do not hold one point to the death, strike where you can win
- Withdraw when you cannot win, saving your strength for the next battle you can win

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：创业产品聚焦、攻克核心技术难题、竞争中"单点突破"的策略。
- 反例：需要长期持续投入、靠积累而非单次突破的领域（基建、研究、健康），一味"歼灭战"式求快反而伤本；有必要的阵地需要死守时，不能轻言"打不赢就走"。

**English:**

- Applies when: focusing a startup product, cracking a core technical problem, or pursuing a "single-point breakthrough" strategy in competition.
- Counterexamples: fields that need long-term sustained investment and rely on accumulation rather than single breakthroughs (infrastructure, research, health) — one-sidedly seeking speed in "annihilation" style damages the foundation; when a necessary position must be held to the death, one must not lightly say "leave if you cannot win".
