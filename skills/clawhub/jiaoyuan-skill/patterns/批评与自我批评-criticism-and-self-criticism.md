# 批评与自我批评 Criticism and Self-Criticism

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队问题捂着不说，积小成大
- 自己犯了错不敢认，越描越黑
- 想纠正他人，又怕伤和气、怕得罪人

**English:**

- Team problems are kept covered up, growing from small to big.
- You made a mistake but dare not own it, and the more you explain the worse it looks.
- You want to correct others but fear damaging goodwill or offending people.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 把批评当作"治病救人"，而不是"整人"
2. 对自己：主动自我批评，揭短不怕丑
3. 对他人：实事求是、与人为善，讲道理、不扣帽子
4. 惩前毖后——对错误要揭发，分析根源，避免重犯
5. 治病救人——给改正的机会，不把人一棍子打死
6. 批评之后看改正，改正了就团结向前

**English:**

1. Treat criticism as "curing the sickness to save the patient," not as "persecuting people."
2. Toward yourself: practice self-criticism on your own initiative; expose your shortcomings without fearing embarrassment.
3. Toward others: seek truth from facts and be well-intentioned; reason things out, don't slap on labels.
4. "Learn from past mistakes to avoid future ones" — expose the error, analyze its root, and prevent recurrence.
5. "Cure the sickness to save the patient" — give a chance to mend; don't beat a person to death with one blow.
6. After criticism, watch for correction; once corrected, unite and move forward.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 弄清思想、团结同志：批评的终点是团结，不是分裂
- 对事触及根源，对人留有出路
- 形成定期复盘、批评与自我批评的机制，而非等出事再补

**English:**

- Clarify thinking and unite comrades: the destination of criticism is unity, not division.
- Touch the root of the matter, but leave the person a way out.
- Build a regular mechanism of review, criticism and self-criticism, rather than patching things up after trouble strikes.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：有共同目标、可成长的团队内部。
- 反例：不是人身攻击或泄私愤的借口；对原则性、违法性问题不能"治病救人"式姑息，需按制度处理。

**English:**

- Applies to: within teams that share a goal and can grow.
- Counterexamples: it is not an excuse for personal attacks or venting grudges; on matters of principle or violations of law, "cure the sickness to save the patient" leniency is wrong — handle them by the rules.
