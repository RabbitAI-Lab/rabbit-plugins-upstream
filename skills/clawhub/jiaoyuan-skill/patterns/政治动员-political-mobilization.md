# 政治动员 Political Mobilization

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 有个好想法或方案，推动时却没人响应，孤军奋战
- 团队士气低落，大家只是被动执行，没有真正认同
- 想做成一件大事，但资源只靠一个人或一小撮人

**English:**

- Having a good idea or plan but getting no response when you push it, fighting alone
- Low team morale, with everyone merely executing passively without real buy-in
- Wanting to accomplish something big, but relying on the resources of one person or a small handful of people

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先想清楚：这件事靠谁？靠几个人，还是靠一群人？
2. 承认"人"是最大的变量，一件事的成败根子在"有多少人真心参与"
3. 不是命令式地摊派，而是做动员：讲清为什么做、做成了对大家有什么好处
4. 把目标和每个人的切身利益挂上钩，让"大家的事"变成"我的事"
5. 动员不是喊一次口号就完，要持续，要落到具体的沟通和行动上
6. 先解决群众（伙伴、用户、同事）的实际困难，人心自然聚拢
7. 把最广泛的力量动员起来，才有真正的、可复制的胜利

**English:**

1. First think it through: who does this thing depend on? A few people, or a whole group?
2. Accept that "people" are the biggest variable; whether something succeeds is rooted in "how many people genuinely take part"
3. Do not assign tasks by command, but mobilize: explain why we do it and what benefit success brings to everyone
4. Tie the goal to each person's concrete interests, turning "everyone's business" into "my business"
5. Mobilization is not a one-time slogan; it must be sustained and translated into concrete communication and action
6. First solve the practical difficulties of the masses (partners, users, colleagues), and hearts will naturally come together
7. Only by mobilizing the broadest forces can there be real, replicable victory

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 大事情先算"人"的账：能动员多少人真心参与，决定了能走多远
- 动员靠讲清利害、解决实际关切，不靠压服
- 让人从"被要求做"变成"我要做"，是动员成功与否的标尺

**English:**

- For any big undertaking, first reckon the "people" account: how many people can be genuinely mobilized determines how far you can go
- Mobilization relies on clarifying stakes and addressing real concerns, not on coercion
- The measure of successful mobilization is turning people from "being told to do it" into "wanting to do it"

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：组织变革、产品冷启动、社区运营、团队激励、社会运动等"靠人成事"的场景。
- 反例：动员不是万能——技术攻坚、个人修行等少数人就能完成的事，过度动员反而分散精力；动员若只喊口号、不解决实际关切，会透支信任。

**English:**

- Applies to: scenarios that "get things done through people," such as organizational change, product cold starts, community operations, team motivation, and social movements.
- Counterexamples: mobilization is not a panacea — for things a few people can accomplish, such as technical breakthroughs or personal cultivation, over-mobilizing only scatters energy; if mobilization is only slogans without addressing real concerns, it will exhaust trust.
