# 不打无准备之仗 Fight No Unprepared Battle

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 机会来了才发现自己没准备好，只能仓促上阵
- 明知道要打硬仗，却一直拖，拖到不得不做才动手
- 别人问"有把握吗"，心里其实没底

**English:**

- Only discovering you are unprepared when the opportunity arrives, and having to scramble into action
- Knowing a hard battle is coming but dragging on until you are forced to move
- When others ask "are you sure?", feeling no real confidence inside

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 动手前先问一句：这一仗打起来，我到底有没有把握？
2. 没把握就承认没把握，不硬上；把"准备"当成独立阶段，不当可省略的预热
3. 盘清要赢需要哪些条件：资源、信息、人手、时机，缺一不可
4. 提前把条件备齐，而不是赌"到时候自然会有"
5. 把握来自条件对比：我方条件占优才出手，占不到就继续准备或换目标
6. 准备的时机宁早勿晚——早了最多浪费一点，晚了直接被动
7. 准备充分之后仍要敢打：准备是为了打胜仗，不是为了不冒险

**English:**

1. Before acting, first ask: if this battle is fought, do I actually have confidence of winning?
2. If not confident, admit it and do not force it; treat "preparation" as a distinct stage, not an optional warm-up
3. Itemize the conditions needed to win — resources, information, people, timing — none of them dispensable
4. Assemble the conditions in advance, rather than betting that "they will come by themselves when the time comes"
5. Confidence comes from comparing conditions: act only when your conditions are superior; otherwise keep preparing or change the target
6. Prepare as early as possible, never late — early costs a little waste at most, late means instant passivity
7. Even after thorough preparation, still dare to fight: preparation is for winning battles, not for avoiding risk

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 没把握不硬上，先备条件，再谈出手
- 把"我有没有把握"换成"我在什么条件下有把握"
- 准备宁早勿晚，宁可多准备，不可仓促应战

**English:**

- Without confidence, do not force it; assemble the conditions before talking about moving
- Change "am I confident?" into "under what conditions am I confident?"
- Prepare early rather than late; rather over-prepare than scramble into battle

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：重要决策、关键项目、考试面试、谈判、上线发布等"只能赢不能输"的节点。
- 反例：把"准备"当拖延的借口，一直备到错过窗口；也有需要快速试错、边做边学的情境，过度准备反成负担。

**English:**

- Applies when: junctures that "must win and cannot lose" — important decisions, key projects, exams and interviews, negotiations, and product launches.
- Counterexamples: treating "preparation" as an excuse for procrastination, preparing until the window is missed; there are also situations that need rapid trial and error and learning by doing, where over-preparation becomes a burden.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "不打无准备之仗，不打无把握之仗，每战都应力求有准备，力求在敌我条件对比下有胜利的把握。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 人民战争
>
> "Fight no battle unprepared, fight no battle you are not sure of winning; make every effort to be prepared for each battle and to win in the given balance of conditions between the enemy and ourselves." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), People's War
