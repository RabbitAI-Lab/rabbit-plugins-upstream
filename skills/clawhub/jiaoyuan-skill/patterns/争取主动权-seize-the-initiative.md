# 争取主动权 Seize the Initiative

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 事情总被推着走，节奏、议程、标准都由别人定
- 长期处于"应付"状态，忙但没方向，只能被动接招
- 对手（或市场、环境）先出手，自己只能跟在后面反应

**English:**

- Things are always pushed along, with the pace, agenda, and standards all set by others
- Stuck in a long-term state of "reacting" — busy but directionless, only able to respond passively
- The opponent (or the market, or the environment) moves first, and you can only react from behind

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先分清：强弱是相对的，被动地位常常是"打出来的"，不是命里注定的
2. 承认眼前的被动，但不把它当定局，而是当待解决的问题
3. 问自己：哪些变量是我能动的？环境再不利，也总有一两个环节由我说了算
4. 用正确的判断加果断的动作去改换局部对比，把劣势一点点翻成优势
5. 制造对手的被动：调动对方、拖垮对方、把对方拉进不熟悉的节奏
6. 一旦丢掉主动权，第一步不是想怎么赢，而是想怎么尽快恢复主动
7. 主动不等于永远进攻，而是"想打就打、想停就停、想走就走"的选择权

**English:**

1. First get clear: strength and weakness are relative, and a passive position is usually "fought into being", not fated
2. Acknowledge the present passivity, but treat it not as a settled outcome but as a problem to be solved
3. Ask yourself: which variables can I move? However unfavorable the environment, there are always one or two links where you have the final say
4. Use correct judgment plus decisive action to change the local balance, turning disadvantage bit by bit into advantage
5. Create passivity for the opponent: maneuver them, wear them down, drag them into an unfamiliar rhythm
6. Once the initiative is lost, the first step is not to think about how to win but how to restore the initiative as quickly as possible
7. Initiative does not mean always attacking; it is the power of choice — "fight when you want, stop when you want, leave when you want"

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 把"谁掌握节奏、谁有选择权"当成比一时输赢更重要的指标
- 陷入被动时，先止损恢复主动，再谈赢
- 主动靠行动争来，不靠等待等来

**English:**

- Treat "who controls the rhythm and who has the power of choice" as a more important indicator than momentary wins and losses
- When trapped in passivity, first cut losses and restore the initiative, then talk about winning
- Initiative is won by action, not by waiting

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：竞争、博弈、谈判等对手互动场景，以及人生方向、职业路径等可控性议题。
- 反例：面对政策、合同、物理条件等纯客观约束，硬碰硬只会自损；"争取主动"是找可动的变量，不是逞强硬刚。

**English:**

- Applies when: interactive scenarios with an opponent such as competition, gamesmanship, and negotiation, as well as controllability issues like life direction and career path.
- Counterexamples: facing purely objective constraints such as policy, contract, and physical conditions, butting heads only harms yourself; "seizing the initiative" is about finding movable variables, not bravado head-on.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "优势而无准备，不是真正的优势，也没有主动。懂得这一点，劣势而有准备之军，常可对敌举行不意的攻势，把优势者打败。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 人民战争
>
> "Superiority without preparation is not real superiority, and it carries no initiative. Understanding this, an inferior but well-prepared army can often launch surprise attacks against the enemy and defeat a superior one." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), People's War
