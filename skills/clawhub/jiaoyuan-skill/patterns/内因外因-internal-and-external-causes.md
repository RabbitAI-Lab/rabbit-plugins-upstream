# 内因外因 Internal and External Causes

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 项目失败全怪环境不好、资源不够、对手太强，不回头找自己的问题
- 把个人成长全归因于外部机会，不承认内因（能力、态度、方法）才是根本
- 迷信"风口""资源""人脉"能解决一切，忽略自身内功

**English:**

- You blame a failed project entirely on a bad environment, scarce resources, or a strong rival, without looking back for your own problems.
- You attribute personal growth entirely to external opportunities, refusing to admit that internal causes (ability, attitude, method) are fundamental.
- You blindly trust that "tailwinds," "resources," and "connections" can solve everything, neglecting your own inner strength.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先分清：这件事的变化，哪些来自外部条件，哪些来自自身内部
2. 承认外因的作用——温度能把鸡蛋孵成小鸡
3. 但追问根本：温度不能把石头孵成小鸡，因为二者的"根据"不同
4. 所以胜负成败，根本在于内因——自身的强弱、方法对错
5. 外因只有通过内因才起作用：资源、机会、环境，都要经过自身这个"根据"才生效
6. 想改变结果，优先改变内因，而不是干等外部条件变好

**English:**

1. First sort out: of this change, what comes from external conditions and what from within.
2. Acknowledge the role of external causes — warmth can hatch a chick from an egg.
3. But press to the root: warmth cannot hatch a chick from a stone, because the two have different "bases."
4. So victory and defeat ultimately rest on internal causes — your own strength or weakness, and whether your method is right.
5. External causes act only through internal causes: resources, opportunities, and environment all take effect only through the "basis" that is yourself.
6. To change the outcome, first change the internal cause, rather than waiting idly for external conditions to improve.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先找内因，再谈外因；环境是条件，自身是根据
- 外部资源是放大器，不是发动机
- 同样环境下结果不同，差异就在内因

**English:**

- Look for internal causes first, then external ones; the environment is the condition, you are the basis.
- External resources are an amplifier, not an engine.
- Under the same environment outcomes differ; the difference lies in internal causes.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：个人成长、团队复盘、创业成败归因——能通过改变自身改变结果的事。
- 反例：不可抗力、系统性外部冲击（经济危机、政策突变、重大天灾）中，一味归因内因会变成自责和苛责他人；内因外因要结合起来看，不能把"内因决定"当成否定环境现实。

**English:**

- Applies to: personal growth, team retrospectives, attributing success or failure in a venture — matters whose outcomes can be changed by changing yourself.
- Counterexamples: amid force majeure and systemic external shocks (economic crises, sudden policy shifts, major natural disasters), insisting on internal causes turns into self-blame and blaming others; internal and external causes must be weighed together. Don't use "internal causes decide" to deny environmental reality.
