# 两个务必·谦虚谨慎 The Two Musts: Stay Modest and Prudent

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队刚刚拿下大成果，开始自满松懈
- 成功后铺张、享乐、失去危机感
- 被赞美和追捧包围，听不进逆耳话
- 从"打天下"转入"守天下"，心态没跟上

**English:**

- The team has just won a major success and begins to grow complacent and slack
- After success, indulging in extravagance and pleasure and losing the sense of crisis
- Surrounded by praise and flattery, deaf to unpleasant words
- Shifting from "winning the world" to "holding the world," with the mindset not keeping up

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 清醒判断位置：胜利只是漫长过程的第一步，不是终点
2. 预见胜利带来的新危险——骄傲、居功、停滞、贪图享乐
3. 警惕"糖衣炮弹"：明枪易躲，捧场和利益最能击垮意志薄弱的人
4. 主动保持谦虚、谨慎、不骄、不躁，防止被胜利冲昏头
5. 继续保持艰苦奋斗，不因条件好了就松懈
6. 用批评与自我批评这个武器，及时去掉冒出来的不良作风
7. 把"取得胜利"和"守住并继续前进"当成两件不同难度的事来对待

**English:**

1. Soberly judge your position: victory is only the first step of a long process, not the finish line
2. Foresee the new dangers that victory brings — pride, resting on laurels, stagnation, craving comfort
3. Beware of "sugar-coated bullets": open spears are easy to dodge, but flattery and favors are what break the weak-willed
4. Take the initiative to stay modest, prudent, free from arrogance and impetuosity, and prevent victory from going to your head
5. Keep up hard struggle; do not slacken just because conditions have improved
6. Use the weapon of criticism and self-criticism to remove undesirable tendencies the moment they appear
7. Treat "winning victory" and "holding it and continuing forward" as two matters of different difficulty

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 越是顺境越要防骄，胜利是最大的诱惑也是最大的风险
- 把每一阶段的成功当起点，不当终点
- 建立自我纠错的常态机制，靠制度而不只靠自觉
- 警惕外部吹捧对团队的腐蚀

**English:**

- The smoother the going, the more you must guard against pride; victory is both the greatest temptation and the greatest risk
- Treat each stage's success as a starting point, not an end point
- Build a standing mechanism for self-correction, relying on institutions and not only on self-awareness
- Be wary of how external flattery corrodes the team

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：组织或个人在取得重大成功后，需要防自满、保清醒、续后劲的场景。
- 反例：谦虚谨慎不等于自我贬低、不敢亮剑——该决断、该竞争时不能缩手缩脚；对确实的成绩也应如实肯定，否则"谦虚"会变成假客套、挫伤士气。

**English:**

- Applies to: organizations or individuals who, after major success, need to guard against complacency, stay clear-headed, and sustain momentum.
- Counterexamples: modesty and prudence are not self-deprecation or fear of baring the sword — when decision or competition is needed, do not shrink back; genuine achievements should be honestly affirmed, otherwise "modesty" becomes fake politeness and dampens morale.
