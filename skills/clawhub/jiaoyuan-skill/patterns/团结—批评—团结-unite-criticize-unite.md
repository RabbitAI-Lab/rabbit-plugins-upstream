# 团结—批评—团结 Unite, Criticize, Unite

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队或合作方出现分歧、错误
- 既不能一团和气、掩盖问题，也不能闹分裂、一哄而散
- 想纠正错误，又担心破坏关系

**English:**

- Divergences or errors appear within the team or among partners.
- You can neither keep a false harmony that covers up problems, nor split and scatter.
- You want to correct the error but worry about damaging the relationship.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 从团结的愿望出发——目标是团结，不是整垮
2. 通过批评与自我批评，分清是非、指出错误
3. 在分清是非的基础上，达到新的、更牢靠的团结
4. 对错误"惩前毖后、治病救人"，给人改过的路
5. 每解决一次矛盾，队伍就团结得更紧一层
6. 循环应用：团结—批评—团结，螺旋提升

**English:**

1. Start from the desire for unity — the goal is unity, not destruction.
2. Through criticism and self-criticism, distinguish right from wrong and point out the error.
3. On the basis of sorting out right and wrong, reach a new and more solid unity.
4. Treat errors by "learning from past mistakes to avoid future ones and curing the sickness to save the patient," leaving the person a way to mend.
5. Each time a contradiction is resolved, the team is united one layer tighter.
6. Apply it in a cycle: unite — criticize — unite, rising in a spiral.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 批评是为了团结，先立"共同目标"再谈分歧
- 分清是非但留有余地，给台阶、给改正机会
- 不能以"团结"为名掩盖原则问题

**English:**

- Criticism serves unity; establish the "common goal" before discussing differences.
- Sort out right and wrong but leave room; give an exit and a chance to correct.
- Never cover up matters of principle in the name of "unity."

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：人民内部、团队内部的矛盾与分歧。
- 反例：对敌对性质、违法违纪问题不适用（需用纪律、法律手段）；不能以"团结"为名和稀泥、放弃原则。

**English:**

- Applies to: contradictions and differences among the people and within the team.
- Counterexamples: it does not apply to antagonistic or unlawful and disciplinary problems (which require disciplinary and legal measures); you must not muddle through or abandon principle in the name of "unity."

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "我们曾经把解决人民内部矛盾的这种民主的方法，具体化为一个公式，叫做“团结—批评—团结”。讲详细一点，就是从团结的愿望出发，经过批评或者斗争使矛盾得到解决，从而在新的基础上达到新的团结。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 团结
>
> "We once crystallized this democratic method of resolving contradictions among the people into a formula called 'unity — criticism — unity.' To elaborate: start from the desire for unity, resolve the contradiction through criticism or struggle, and thereby attain a new unity on a new basis." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Unity

> "我们反对主观主义、宗派主义、党八股，有两条宗旨是必须注意的：第一是“惩前毖后”，第二是“治病救人”。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 批评
>
> "In opposing subjectivism, sectarianism, and stereotyped Party writing, two principles must be observed: first, 'learn from past mistakes to avoid future ones'; second, 'cure the sickness to save the patient.'" —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Criticism
