# 自己动手·丰衣足食 Do It Yourself, Ample Food and Clothing

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 外部资源断供、预算被砍、依赖的平台涨价，坐等救济看不到头
- 团队遇到问题习惯"等上面给资源、给方案"，越等越被动
- 在资源贫瘠的条件下被要求交付，"条件不成熟"成了拖延的理由
- 核心能力是买来的、外包的，一旦断供整个组织就瘫掉

**English:**

- External resources are cut off, budgets slashed, dependent platforms raise prices, and waiting for relief shows no end in sight
- The team habitually "waits for resources and plans from above" when problems arise, growing more passive the longer it waits
- Being asked to deliver under resource-poor conditions, with "conditions are not ripe" becoming an excuse for delay
- Core capabilities are bought or outsourced, and the whole organization collapses the moment supply is cut off

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先把"等、靠、要"的念头放下，承认当前约束就是现实，不幻想外部援助突然出现
2. 盘点手头已有的劳动力、工具、技能、闲置产能——困难往往不是没有资源，而是没有把已有资源组织起来
3. 把大目标拆成"能自给的部分"和"必须外求的部分"，先解决能自己解决的那块，哪怕只是降一部分依赖
4. 让负责人亲自下场、一线带头，而不是只发号施令——"自己动手"首先是领导骨干动手
5. 把生产/交付变成一场全员动员，靠团队的力量放大个人的动作
6. 主业与自救并行推进，不自救反而会反过来拖垮主业
7. 用"自给之后更舒服"的正反馈巩固行为，形成可复制、可扩张的自力更生循环

**English:**

1. First put down the "wait, lean, and beg" mindset, accept the present constraints as reality, and stop fantasizing that external aid will suddenly appear
2. Take stock of the labor, tools, skills, and idle capacity already at hand — the difficulty is often not a lack of resources but a failure to organize the resources you have
3. Break the big goal into "the part that can be self-supplied" and "the part that must be sought outside", and first solve what you can solve yourself, even if it only reduces some of the dependence
4. Have those in charge personally step into the field and lead on the front line, not merely issue orders — "doing it yourself" means, above all, the leading cadres doing it themselves
5. Turn production/delivery into an all-hands mobilization, using the team's strength to amplify individual actions
6. Advance the main undertaking and self-rescue in parallel; failing to self-rescue will instead drag down the main undertaking
7. Reinforce the behavior with the positive feedback of "things feel better once self-sufficient", forming a replicable, scalable cycle of self-reliance

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 能不依赖外部解决的，先自己解决，哪怕形式上"不划算""不专业"
- 领导带头干，而不是只做动员
- 自力更生不是苦行，目的是改善处境、赢得主动，要用收益感留住人
- 资源紧时先保住"自给"这个底盘，再谈扩展

**English:**

- Solve yourself whatever you can without external dependence, even if it looks "uneconomical" or "unprofessional" in form
- Leaders take the lead and do, rather than only mobilize
- Self-reliance is not asceticism; its purpose is to improve your situation and win the initiative, using a sense of gain to retain people
- When resources are tight, first secure the base of "self-supply", then talk about expansion

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：资源受限、外部供给不可靠、需要建立"不靠别人"的兜底能力时。
- 反例：主业需要高度专业分工、自给成本远超外购（造不如买的环节）；"自己动手"若变成拒绝合作、闭门造车，就从补短板滑向封闭。要区分"补短板保底"和"替代分工"。

**English:**

- Applies when: resources are constrained, external supply is unreliable, and you need to build a "not dependent on others" fallback capability.
- Counterexamples: when the main undertaking requires highly specialized division of labor and self-supply costs far exceed buying in (links where making is worse than buying); if "doing it yourself" becomes refusing cooperation and working behind closed doors, it slips from filling gaps into isolation. Distinguish "filling gaps as a floor" from "replacing the division of labor".
