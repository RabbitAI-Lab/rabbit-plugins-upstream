# 农民根本·土地问题 Land and the Peasantry

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 做产品/服务时盯着少数"大客户、高端用户"，忽略数量最大的普通用户
- 战略讨论时争论技巧，却没人问"最广大用户的根本诉求到底是什么"
- 关键时候想不清楚"谁是我们必须依靠的多数人"，结果站到少数人一边
- 做决策时对"多数人的真实处境"缺乏体感，靠办公室想象

**English:**

- When building a product or service, fixating on a few "key accounts and high-end users" while ignoring the largest group of ordinary users
- Arguing over tactics in strategic discussions while no one asks "what the broadest users' fundamental needs really are"
- At critical moments failing to see clearly "who the majority we must rely on are", and ending up siding with the minority
- Lacking a felt sense of "the majority's real situation" when making decisions, relying on office-bound imagination

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先做用户/人群的构成分析：谁占绝大多数、谁是沉默的大多数
2. 找到"多数人的根本诉求"——不是他们零散的抱怨，而是他们最根本的利益所在
3. 判断自己是不是站在多数人一边：站在多数人一边的变革才可能成事，脱离多数人就会孤立
4. 把"为多数人解决问题"当成判断对错的标准——多数人说好，才是真的好
5. 落实时区别对待不同情况，不搞一刀切：老用户、新用户、边缘用户各有各的做法
6. 让受益的多数人成为拥护者和传播者，形成不可逆的力量

**English:**

1. First analyze the composition of the user base or population: who constitutes the vast majority, who are the silent majority
2. Identify "the fundamental needs of the majority" — not their scattered complaints, but where their most basic interests lie
3. Judge whether you stand with the majority: change that stands with the majority can succeed; separating from the majority leads to isolation
4. Treat "solving problems for the majority" as the standard of right and wrong — it is truly good only when the majority say it is good
5. In implementation, treat different situations differently rather than applying one rule to all: established users, new users, and fringe users each need their own approach
6. Turn the benefiting majority into advocates and amplifiers, forming an irreversible force

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先弄清"最大多数是谁、他们最根本要什么"，再谈战略
- 站在多数人一边，而不是迁就少数有话语权的人
- 同一套方案要对不同处境区别施策，不一刀切
- 用"多数人的实际收益"检验决策，而不是用少数人的评价

**English:**

- First clarify "who the great majority are and what they fundamentally want" before discussing strategy
- Stand with the majority rather than appeasing the few who have a louder voice
- Apply the same plan with differentiated measures for different situations, never one size for all
- Test decisions by "the majority's actual benefits", not by the judgment of a minority

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：用户/客户构成以大众为主、需要抓住最大公约数诉求时。
- 反例：面向极小众、高净值人群的领域，"多数人"不一定是目标客户，盲目讨好大众会失去真正买单的人；"多数人"要用真实数据界定，不能凭感觉贴标签。

**English:**

- Applies when: the user or customer base is mainly the general public and you need to capture the greatest common denominator of needs.
- Counterexamples: in fields serving a very small, high-net-worth audience, "the majority" are not necessarily the target customers — blindly courting the masses loses the people who actually pay; "the majority" must be defined with real data, not labeled by feel.
