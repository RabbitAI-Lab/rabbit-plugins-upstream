# 三大作风 Three Major Styles of Work

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队里理论与实际脱节，说的和做的是两套
- 决策者远离用户、远离一线，拍脑袋定方案
- 出问题互相遮掩，不敢也不愿做自我批评
- 组织里形成"报喜不报忧"的空气

**English:**

- In the team, theory is divorced from practice; what is said and what is done are two different things.
- Decision-makers are cut off from users and the front line, deciding by gut feel.
- When problems arise, people cover for each other, unwilling or unable to practice self-criticism.
- The organization develops an atmosphere of "reporting only the good news."

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 看一个团队靠不靠谱，先看它的作风，而不是口号
2. 第一条：理论联系实际——学的、说的，都要能用来解决实际问题，别做"古董鉴赏家"
3. 第二条：密切联系群众——从多数人利益出发，不脱离服务对象，防止官僚主义
4. 第三条：开展自我批评——敢于揭自己的短，用这个武器去掉不良作风
5. 三件事互相咬合：脱离实际就脱离群众，不敢批评就积累错误
6. 把作风当可检验的硬标准，而不是抽象的道德表态

**English:**

1. To judge whether a team is reliable, look first at its work style, not its slogans.
2. First: link theory with practice — what you learn and say must solve real problems; don't be an "antique appraiser."
3. Second: keep close ties with the masses — start from the interests of the many, don't drift from those you serve, and guard against bureaucracy.
4. Third: practice self-criticism — dare to expose your own shortcomings and use this weapon to shed bad habits.
5. The three interlock: divorce from practice means divorce from the masses, and reluctance to criticize lets errors accumulate.
6. Treat work style as a hard, verifiable standard, not an abstract moral posture.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 一切方针、方案都要能落地，落了地才算数
- 决策的出发点是服务对象的真实利益，不是自己人的舒服
- 建一个"能说真话、敢认错"的机制，让自我批评常态化
- 定期用这三条给团队做体检

**English:**

- Every policy and plan must be landable; it counts only once it lands.
- The starting point of a decision is the real interest of those you serve, not your own people's comfort.
- Build a mechanism for "speaking truth and owning mistakes," making self-criticism routine.
- Regularly give the team a check-up against these three items.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：团队文化建设、组织健康度体检、领导者自查与整改。
- 反例：自我批评若流于形式（会上认错、会后照旧），比不批评更坏；联系群众不是讨好迁就，专业判断不能完全让位于情绪；作风建设是长期工程，不能靠一次运动了事。

**English:**

- Applies to: team culture building, organizational health check-ups, and leaders' self-examination and rectification.
- Counterexamples: self-criticism that becomes a formality (admitting mistakes in the meeting, changing nothing after) is worse than no criticism at all; keeping close ties with the masses is not currying favor, and professional judgment must not yield entirely to sentiment; work-style building is a long-term project and cannot be finished by a single campaign.
