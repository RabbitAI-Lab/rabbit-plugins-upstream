# 反对自由主义 Against Liberalism

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队里明明看到问题，却"事不关己，高高挂起"，谁也不说
- 当面不说，背后乱说；开会不发言，会后一堆意见
- 为了和气，明知不对也不作原则性争论，一团和气掩盖矛盾
- 自己错了也不改，对自己网开一面

**English:**

- Seeing a problem in the team but "standing aloof, none of my business," with no one speaking up
- Saying nothing to one's face but talking freely behind one's back; staying silent in meetings and voicing a pile of opinions afterward
- For the sake of harmony, refusing principled debate even when knowing something is wrong, papering over contradictions with false peace
- Not correcting one's own mistakes and making an exception for oneself

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先问：这个场合，我是在坚持原则，还是在无原则地求和气？
2. 认清自由主义的表现：不负责任的背后批评、明哲保身、放任自流
3. 追问来源：是不是把个人利益放在第一位，怕得罪人、怕担责任
4. 评估危害：它是一种腐蚀剂，让团结涣散、关系松懈、工作消极
5. 对照反面：积极的思想斗争才是达到团结的武器，批评是为了进步
6. 改的方法：原则问题上不回避，当面说清楚，对己对人都用同一把尺子

**English:**

1. First ask: in this situation, am I upholding principle or seeking peace at the expense of principle?
2. Recognize the manifestations of liberalism: irresponsible criticism behind people's backs, self-protection at all costs, and letting things drift
3. Trace the source: is personal interest being put first, out of fear of offending people or bearing responsibility?
4. Assess the harm: it is a corrosive that dissolves unity, slackens relations, and demoralizes work
5. Contrast with the opposite: active ideological struggle is the weapon for achieving unity; criticism is for progress
6. The way to change: do not evade matters of principle; state things clearly to one's face, and apply the same measure to oneself and others

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 原则问题当面说，不当面一套背后一套
- 对人对己一个标准，不搞双重标准
- 该斗争的斗争，批评的目的是把事做好，不是泄私愤

**English:**

- Speak directly on matters of principle; do not say one thing to someone's face and another behind their back
- One standard for others and oneself; no double standards
- Struggle when struggle is due; the purpose of criticism is to do the work well, not to vent personal grievance

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：团队协作、组织纪律、需要坦诚反馈和原则性批评的场景。
- 反例：不是所有"不说"都是自由主义——无关原则的小事、需要时机和方式的沟通、以及尊重他人边界的场合，过度"斗争"反而伤和气；批评要"惩前毖后、治病救人"，不能变成攻击和清算。

**English:**

- Applies to: team collaboration, organizational discipline, and scenarios needing candid feedback and principled criticism.
- Counterexamples: not all silence is liberalism — trivial matters unrelated to principle, communication that needs the right timing and manner, and occasions that respect others' boundaries are all cases where excessive "struggle" only hurts harmony; criticism should "learn from past mistakes to avoid future ones and cure the sickness to save the patient," not become attack and settling of scores.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "自由主义的来源，在于小资产阶级的自私自利性，以个人利益放在第一位，革命利益放在第二位，因此产生思想上、政治上、组织上的自由主义。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 纠正错误思想
>
> "The source of liberalism lies in the selfishness of the petty bourgeoisie, which puts personal interests first and the interests of the revolution second, thus giving rise to liberalism in ideology, in politics, and in organization." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Correcting Mistaken Ideas
