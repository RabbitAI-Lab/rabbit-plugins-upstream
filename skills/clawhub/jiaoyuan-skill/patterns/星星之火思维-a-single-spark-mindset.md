# 星星之火思维 A Single Spark Mindset

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 起点极其弱小，现状与目标之间差距巨大
- 被身边人反复质疑"这没希望""太渺茫"
- 看不到即时回报，容易自我怀疑

**English:**

- The starting point is extremely weak, and the gap between the current state and the goal is enormous
- Being repeatedly questioned by those around you: "this is hopeless", "too remote"
- Seeing no immediate return, and easily falling into self-doubt

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 不被当前微弱的力量迷惑，把目光投向事物发展的趋势与本质
2. 判断"星星之火能否燎原"，依据是客观条件是否具备，而不是主观愿望
3. 力量尚小时，先做扎实的局部工作，建立"根据地"（最小可行样本）
4. 用局部成功证明全局可行，示范带动更多人加入
5. 高潮未到时不盲动、不冒进，耐心蓄势
6. 保持"看得见桅杆尖头"的远见，把信念建立在趋势判断上

**English:**

1. Do not be misled by the current meager strength; turn your gaze to the trend and essence of how things develop
2. Judge "whether a single spark can start a prairie fire" by whether the objective conditions exist, not by subjective wishes
3. While strength is still small, first do solid work in a limited area and build a "base area" (a minimum viable sample)
4. Use local success to prove overall feasibility, and demonstrate to bring more people on board
5. Before the upsurge arrives, do not act recklessly or rush ahead; patiently build up momentum
6. Keep the farsightedness of "seeing the masthead on the horizon", and rest conviction on trend judgment

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 判断趋势靠客观条件，不靠主观愿望或他人评价
- 小处着手、大处着眼：从能赢的最小局部做起
- 局部突破、示范带动，用样本说服

**English:**

- Judge trends by objective conditions, not by subjective wishes or others' opinions
- Start small while keeping the big picture in view: begin with the smallest local point where you can win
- Break through locally and lead by demonstration, persuading through samples

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：处于早期、客观趋势向上的事业或新方向。
- 反例：客观趋势向下的领域——再坚持也燎原不了；先冷静判断"火种是否在干柴上"，否则只是盲目乐观，不是远见。

**English:**

- Applies when: an undertaking or new direction is in its early stage and the objective trend is upward.
- Counterexamples: in fields where the objective trend is downward, no amount of persistence will start a prairie fire; first calmly judge "whether the spark is landing on dry tinder" — otherwise it is blind optimism, not foresight.
