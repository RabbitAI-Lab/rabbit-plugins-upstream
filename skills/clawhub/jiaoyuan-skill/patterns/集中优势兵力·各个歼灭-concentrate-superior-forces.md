# 集中优势兵力·各个歼灭 Concentrate Superior Forces to Destroy the Enemy One by One

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 同时推进一堆任务，每个都只投入一点，结果每个都没做成
- 资源、预算、人手都紧张，却要同时应对多个目标或对手
- 某个关键项目上想赢，但力量被摊得太薄，迟迟打不穿

**English:**

- Pushing a pile of tasks at once, investing only a little in each, and completing none of them
- Resources, budget, and manpower all tight, yet needing to deal with multiple goals or opponents at once
- Wanting to win on a key project, but the force is spread too thin and cannot break through

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先承认一个基本事实：整体实力不占优时，处处设防等于处处薄弱
2. 盘清手头全部可调用的资源，不按"应该做"、而按"哪一点能打穿"来分配
3. 选定一个相对对手有优势的局部，把力量压上去，形成几倍于对手的对比
4. 其余方向主动收缩、拖住或示弱，为关键方向腾出资源
5. 关键方向上追求"歼灭式"的结果：要么彻底拿下，要么不碰，不做半吊子
6. 打完一个再打下一个，逐个击破，不让战线越铺越宽
7. 靠连续几个局部胜利，一点点改变整体力量对比

**English:**

1. First accept a basic fact: when overall strength is not superior, defending everywhere means being weak everywhere
2. Take stock of all resources you can call on, allocating them not by "what should be done" but by "which point can be broken through"
3. Choose one local point where you have an advantage over the opponent, press the force onto it, and form a several-to-one ratio
4. Voluntarily contract, hold, or feign weakness on the other directions to free up resources for the key direction
5. On the key direction pursue an "annihilation-style" result: either take it completely or do not touch it — no half measures
6. Finish one before starting the next, destroying them one by one, never letting the front spread ever wider
7. Rely on a string of local victories to gradually shift the overall balance of forces

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 宁做透一件，不做散十件；把资源集中到能形成绝对优势的那个点上
- 关键局求"全胜"不做"击溃"——半成品等于没做
- 每个阶段只设一个主攻方向，其余都是配合

**English:**

- Better to do one thing thoroughly than ten things half-heartedly; concentrate resources on the point where you can form absolute superiority
- In the decisive engagement, seek "total victory," not a "rout" — a half-finished product equals nothing done
- Set only one main direction of attack in each phase; everything else is support

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：资源有限、需要在关键节点突破的竞争——创业打单、产品聚焦、个人能力培养、团队攻坚。
- 反例：日常运营/维护类工作处处都要守住底线，不能为了集中兵力而放掉基本面；集中不等于偏科，放弃的必须是可承受的次要方向。

**English:**

- Applies to: competition with limited resources that needs a breakthrough at the key node — winning deals as a startup, product focus, personal skill-building, and team assaults on hard problems.
- Counterexamples: day-to-day operations and maintenance must hold the baseline everywhere; do not give up fundamentals for the sake of concentrating forces. Concentration is not one-sidedness; what you give up must be a secondary direction you can afford to lose.
