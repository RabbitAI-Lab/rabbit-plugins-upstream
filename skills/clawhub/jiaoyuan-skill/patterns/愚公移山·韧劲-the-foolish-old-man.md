# 愚公移山·韧劲 The Foolish Old Man Who Removed the Mountains

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 目标巨大、障碍看起来像"大山"，短期看不到任何推进
- 被旁人嘲笑"以你的资源根本不可能"，自己也跟着动摇
- 事情是长期工程，需要一代人甚至几代人接力，无法速胜
- 越到艰难阶段越想放弃，怀疑"坚持到底还有没有意义"

**English:**

- The goal is enormous and the obstacles look like a "mountain", with no visible progress in the short term
- Being ridiculed by others — "impossible with your resources" — and wavering along with them
- The undertaking is a long-term project requiring the relay of one or even several generations; there is no quick victory
- The harder the stage, the more one wants to quit, doubting "whether holding on to the end means anything"

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先下定决心：认准目标值得做，就把"要不要做"的纠结关掉，转入"怎么做、做多久"
2. 把巨大障碍拆成可重复的动作——"挖一点就会少一点"，用可积累的进步对抗"不可能"的判断
3. 接受"山不会自己变小"的现实，但不被"山很高"吓住，只盯住"每次能挖走多少"
4. 做长期准备：自己这一程挖不完，就为下一程留好接力的基础和接班人
5. 承认困难、分析困难，不搞"不承认主义"，但也不让困难定义终局
6. 动员多数人一起挖——把"我一个人的坚持"变成"多数人的共同事业"
7. 把信念建立在"趋势加群众"上，而不是靠一时热情硬撑

**English:**

1. First make up your mind: once sure the goal is worth doing, switch off the "should we do it" struggle and turn to "how and for how long"
2. Break the enormous obstacle into repeatable actions — "dig a little and there is a little less" — and counter the "impossible" verdict with accumulable progress
3. Accept the reality that "the mountain will not shrink by itself", but do not be frightened by "the mountain is high"; fix your eyes only on "how much can be dug away each time"
4. Prepare for the long haul: if you cannot finish in your stretch, leave a foundation and successors for the next stretch
5. Acknowledge and analyze the difficulty — no "denialism" — but do not let the difficulty define the endgame
6. Mobilize the majority to dig together — turn "my own persistence" into "the common cause of the majority"
7. Rest conviction on "trend plus the masses", not on hard-won enthusiasm alone

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 用"可积累的进步"对抗"不可能"，把大目标拆成每天能挖的一锹土
- 长期目标必须设计接力，不押注在单个人或单次冲刺上
- 困难要承认、要分析，用"多挖一点就少一点"的确定性对冲恐惧

**English:**

- Counter "impossible" with "accumulable progress", breaking the big goal into a shovelful of earth each day
- Long-term goals must be designed as a relay, not bet on a single person or a single sprint
- Difficulties must be acknowledged and analyzed, hedging fear with the certainty of "dig a little more and there is a little less"

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：目标正确、趋势有利、需要长期积累和接力的事业。
- 反例：方向本身就错了（山不该挖、或该绕路），坚持越久错得越远；"排除万难"不是蛮干，前提是先判断"这座山值不值得挖、有没有更好的路"。

**English:**

- Applies when: an undertaking whose goal is correct, whose trend is favorable, and which needs long-term accumulation and relay.
- Counterexamples: when the direction itself is wrong (the mountain should not be dug, or one should go around it), the longer you persist the further you go astray; "overcoming every difficulty" is not brute force — the premise is first judging "whether this mountain is worth digging and whether there is a better road".
