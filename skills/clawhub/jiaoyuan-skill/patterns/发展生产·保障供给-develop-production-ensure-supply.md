# 发展生产·保障供给 Develop Production, Ensure Supply

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 现金流紧张，团队天天讨论"怎么省"，没人讨论"怎么赚、怎么造"
- 账上的钱越来越紧，但基本面（用户/产品/供给能力）并没有真正起来
- 遇到困难只盯着"分蛋糕"，不想"把蛋糕做大"
- 有"账上数字"焦虑，却回避"源头活水"的问题

**English:**

- Cash flow is tight, and the team discusses "how to save" every day while no one discusses "how to earn, how to create."
- The money in the books grows tighter, but the fundamentals (users / product / supply capacity) never really take off.
- In difficulty, you fixate on "dividing the cake" instead of "growing the cake."
- You're anxious about "the numbers in the books" but dodge the question of "the living source of water."

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先分清"经济"与"财政"：决定账上有没有钱的，是背后有没有持续创造价值的能力，不是怎么精打细算
2. 承认一个事实：没有经济基础，靠收缩开支解决不了根本问题，越收越死
3. 把注意力从"开支端"挪到"生产端/供给端"，问"什么是我们的活水来源"
4. 发展不是乱铺摊子：反对"空嚷发展"的大计划，也反对只守不进的保守，走一条实事求是的发展路线
5. 发展的落脚点是改善多数人的实际处境（民众生活/客户收益），不是账面数字
6. 在发展的同时守住底线：负担虽重不伤及根本，一旦有了余力就回哺民生、休养生息

**English:**

1. First distinguish "the economy" from "finance": what decides whether there's money in the books is the underlying capacity to keep creating value, not how carefully you budget.
2. Accept a fact: without an economic foundation, cutting spending cannot solve the fundamental problem — the more you cut, the more stuck you get.
3. Shift attention from the "spending side" to the "production/supply side," and ask: "What is our source of living water?"
4. Development is not spreading yourself thin: oppose the grand plan of "shouting about development" in vain, and oppose the conservatism of only holding and never advancing; follow a development path that seeks truth from facts.
5. The destination of development is improving the real circumstances of the many (people's livelihoods / customer returns), not the numbers in the books.
6. While developing, hold the bottom line: no matter how heavy the burden, don't injure the foundation; once you have spare capacity, feed it back to livelihoods and let things recuperate.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先开源后节流，节流解决不了根子问题
- 发展要以"改善实际生活、创造真实价值"为检验，不是规模或数字
- 反对两个极端：不切实际的大计划，和一味收缩的保守观点

**English:**

- Increase income before cutting costs; cost-cutting cannot solve the root problem.
- Development is tested by "improving real life and creating real value," not by scale or numbers.
- Oppose two extremes: the unrealistic grand plan, and the conservative view of contracting endlessly.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：困难源于"没有活水"而非"浪费"，需要把精力从分蛋糕转向做蛋糕时。
- 反例：资源极度紧张、活不下来的时候，先节流保命更优先；"发展"若变成乱投资、乱扩张，就是空嚷发展的大计划，比保守更有害。

**English:**

- Applies to: when the difficulty comes from "no living source" rather than "waste," and energy must shift from dividing the cake to baking it.
- Counterexamples: when resources are extremely tight and survival is at stake, cutting costs to stay alive comes first; if "development" turns into reckless investment and expansion, it becomes the very grand plan of shouting about development — more harmful than conservatism.
