# 独立自主思维 Independence and Self-Reliance

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 过度依赖外部、他人，核心命脉不在自己手里
- 在合作中被裹挟，逐渐丧失自己的主张
- 想借力，又担心被绑死

**English:**

- Over-relying on the outside or on others, with the lifeline not in your own hands
- Being swept along in cooperation and gradually losing your own position
- Wanting to borrow strength, yet fearing being tied down

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 认清"依靠谁"——最终靠自己，外部力量可借、不可赖
2. 在合作与联盟中保持自己的主张与行动自主
3. 把关键能力（根据地/核心技术/主动权）掌握在自己手里
4. 独立自主地组织和使用自己的力量，不被动受制
5. 既联合又独立，不因联合而丧失自我
6. 以自力更生打牢基础，再争取外援锦上添花

**English:**

1. Get clear on "whom to rely on" — ultimately on yourself; outside strength can be borrowed, not leaned on
2. In cooperation and alliances, keep your own positions and independence of action
3. Keep key capabilities (base areas / core technology / the initiative) in your own hands
4. Organize and use your own strength independently, without being passively constrained
5. Unite and remain independent at the same time; do not lose yourself through unity
6. Lay a solid foundation through self-reliance, then seek outside help as a bonus

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 核心能力自建，命运不交他人
- 合作中守住底线，明确不可让渡的东西
- 区分"必须自主"与"可以借力"，不搞一刀切

**English:**

- Build core capabilities yourself; do not hand your destiny to others
- Hold the bottom line in cooperation; be clear about what cannot be given away
- Distinguish "must be self-reliant" from "can borrow strength"; do not apply a single rule to everything

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：核心能力、战略命脉、品牌与价值观。
- 反例：独立自主不等于闭门造车、拒绝合作；对完全标准化的环节不必自建；关键在判断"哪些必须自主、哪些可借力"。

**English:**

- Applies to: core capabilities, strategic lifelines, brand, and values.
- Counterexamples: independence does not mean working behind closed doors or refusing cooperation; there is no need to build fully standardized components yourself; the key is judging "what must be self-reliant and what can be borrowed."
