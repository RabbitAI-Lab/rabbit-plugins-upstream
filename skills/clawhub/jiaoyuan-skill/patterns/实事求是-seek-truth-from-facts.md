# 实事求是 Seek Truth from Facts

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 开会定方案，大家凭感觉和印象拍脑袋，没人去看真实数据
- 网上的说法、领导的意见、书上的理论，跟手头的事实对不上，却照搬照套
- 拿旧经验套新问题，情况已经变了还按老办法来
- 汇报只挑好听的、凑漂亮数字，回避真实困难

**English:**

- At the meeting to set the plan, everyone decides by feel and impression; no one looks at the real data.
- Online claims, the boss's opinion, and book theory don't match the facts at hand, yet they're copied wholesale.
- You apply old experience to new problems; the situation has changed, but you still do it the old way.
- Reports pick only the good news and pretty numbers, avoiding the real difficulties.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先问"实际情况是什么"，把"是什么"排在"怎么办"前面
2. 不满足于道听途说和表面印象，去收集第一手材料
3. "实事"是客观存在的事实，"是"是事实内部的规律，"求"是去研究——把三者接起来
4. 从事实里引出规律，而不是先有结论、再找事实来证明自己
5. 用研究得出的规律指导行动，不凭主观想象，不凭一时的热情
6. 结论对不对，最终回到事实里去检验

**English:**

1. First ask "What is the actual situation?", putting "what is" before "what to do."
2. Don't settle for hearsay and surface impressions; collect first-hand material.
3. "Facts" are the objectively existing facts; "truth" is the law within the facts; "seek" is to study — join the three together.
4. Draw the law out of the facts, instead of reaching a conclusion first and then hunting for facts to prove yourself.
5. Guide action with the law drawn from study, not by subjective imagination or momentary enthusiasm.
6. Whether the conclusion is right ultimately returns to the facts for testing.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 没有调查，没有发言权；没摸清事实前不轻易下判断
- 结论从材料里来，不从本本和权威里来
- 数字和事实优先于感觉和面子
- 情况变了，方法跟着变

**English:**

- No investigation, no right to speak; don't judge lightly before getting the facts straight.
- Conclusions come from the material, not from books or authority.
- Numbers and facts come before feeling and face.
- When the situation changes, the method changes with it.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：可观察、可测量、有数据支撑的决策（业务判断、产品决策、绩效评估）。
- 反例：没条件调查、时间窗口极短、或纯主观价值选择（审美、信仰）的场景，不能把"没调查"当成拖延和无所作为的挡箭牌；"实事求是"也不等于只盯着眼前事实而放弃长远判断。

**English:**

- Applies to: observable, measurable, data-backed decisions (business judgment, product decisions, performance evaluation).
- Counterexamples: in scenarios with no chance to investigate, an extremely short time window, or purely subjective value choices (aesthetics, faith), "no investigation" must not become a shield for procrastination and inaction; "seeking truth from facts" also doesn't mean staring only at immediate facts and giving up long-term judgment.
