# 弹钢琴·统筹兼顾 Play the Piano, Coordinate All Aspects

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 手里同时管很多事，忙不过来，顾此失彼
- 只盯着最紧急的一两件事，其他重要的悄悄烂掉
- 团队里"单点冲刺"，长期目标没人管
- 资源有限，但各方面都要照顾到

**English:**

- You manage many things at once, can't keep up, and lose one while attending to another.
- You stare at the one or two most urgent things while other important ones quietly rot.
- The team sprints on a single point while no one tends the long-term goals.
- Resources are limited, yet every aspect must be looked after.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 承认事情是多线程的：十个指头都要动，不能只动一个
2. 但十个指头同时按下去也不成调子——要分清主次、有节奏
3. 先抓住中心工作（当前最重要的一件事），把主力压上去
4. 围绕中心，把其他方面的工作同时带起来，不能丢
5. 给各项工作排个先后轻重，凡是有问题的地方都点一下
6. 掌握节奏、互相配合，让全局像一个整体在运转
7. 判断什么是"中心"要根据当时当地的条件，不是一成不变

**English:**

1. Accept that matters are multi-threaded: all ten fingers must move, not just one.
2. But pressing all ten fingers down at once makes no tune — you must distinguish primary from secondary and keep a rhythm.
3. First seize the central task (the single most important matter right now) and throw the main force at it.
4. Around the center, carry the other tasks along at the same time; don't drop them.
5. Rank the tasks by priority and weight, and touch every place where there's a problem.
6. Master the rhythm and coordination so the whole operates as one.
7. What counts as the "center" is judged by the conditions of the time and place, not fixed once and for all.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 一个时期只抓一个中心，其余排成第二、第三位
- 抓中心不等于只干一件事，要"围绕中心带动其他"
- 定期扫一遍全局，凡有问题的角落都要点到，防死角
- 主次和节奏随条件变化，及时调整重心

**English:**

- Grasp only one center in a given period; rank the rest as second and third.
- Grasping the center doesn't mean doing only one thing; "drive the others around the center."
- Regularly sweep the whole; touch every corner that has a problem, to prevent blind spots.
- Priority and rhythm change with conditions; adjust the center of gravity in time.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：多线并行、资源有限的日常管理，团队、项目、经营的整体调度。
- 反例：把"兼顾"做成平均用力，等于没有重点；抓中心不是放弃其他，等中心崩了再补救往往已经晚了；个人单点任务、需要极致专注的场景，不必硬套统筹。

**English:**

- Applies to: day-to-day management with parallel threads and limited resources; the overall coordination of teams, projects, and operations.
- Counterexamples: turning "coordination" into even distribution of effort is having no priority at all; grasping the center is not abandoning the rest, and by the time the center collapses, remedy is usually too late; single-point personal tasks that demand extreme focus need not be forced into this framework.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "不能同时有许多中心工作，在一定时间内只能有一个中心工作，辅以别的第二位、第三位的工作。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 思想与工作方法
>
> "There cannot be many central tasks at the same time; in any given period there can be only one central task, supplemented by other tasks of a second or third order of importance." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Thought and Methods of Work
