# 组织起来 Get Organized

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 资源、人力不少，但散着，形不成合力
- 个体都很努力，整体却没效率
- 有共同目标，但各干各的、重复浪费
- 想动员大家，却总靠硬压、效果差

**English:**

- Resources and manpower are ample, but scattered, failing to form a combined force
- Every individual works hard, yet the whole is inefficient
- There is a common goal, but everyone works separately, duplicating and wasting
- Wanting to mobilize everyone, but always relying on coercion, with poor results

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先看问题本质：很多困难不是资源不够，是力量没组织起来
2. 把分散的个体、零散的力量，用某种形式连成整体
3. 找合适的组织载体：互助、协作、网络，因地制宜
4. 关键原则是自愿——硬凑、强迫的组织撑不久
5. 靠实效说话：组织起来带来的好处，要让人看得见
6. 领导者要会组织、会整合资源，而不只是下命令
7. 把组织能力本身当成一项需要学习的基本功

**English:**

1. First look at the essence of the problem: many difficulties come not from insufficient resources but from unorganized strength
2. Connect scattered individuals and dispersed forces into a whole through some form
3. Find a suitable organizational vehicle — mutual aid, collaboration, networks — suited to local conditions
4. The key principle is voluntariness — organizations patched together by force do not last
5. Let results speak: the benefits that organizing brings must be visible to people
6. Leaders must know how to organize and integrate resources, not merely give orders
7. Treat the ability to organize itself as a fundamental skill to be learned

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先解决"组织起来"，再谈效率提升
- 组织形式服从自愿原则，不搞强制拼凑
- 用可感知的实效巩固组织，而非只靠号召
- 领导者亲自抓组织工作，学会整合与动员

**English:**

- First solve "getting organized", then talk about raising efficiency
- Organizational form follows the principle of voluntariness; no forced piecing-together
- Consolidate the organization with perceptible results, not just slogans
- Leaders personally attend to organizational work and learn integration and mobilization

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：社区、团队、产业的资源整合，动员群众、把分散力量拧成一股绳的场景。
- 反例：组织起来不是为组织而组织，没有共同目标和实际利益的"组织"会沦为形式；强制捆绑违背自愿原则，必然散架；过度组织化也可能扼杀个体的主动性和创造力。

**English:**

- Applies when: integrating resources in communities, teams, and industries, and mobilizing the masses to twist scattered forces into one rope.
- Counterexamples: getting organized is not organizing for its own sake; an "organization" without a common goal and actual interests degenerates into formality; forced bundling violates the principle of voluntariness and will surely fall apart; over-organization can also stifle individual initiative and creativity.
