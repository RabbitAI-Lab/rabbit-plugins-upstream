# 一般与个别相结合 Combine the General with the Particular

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 定了一个大方向或口号，但落不下去，各地执行走样
- 想推行一套新做法，心里没底，怕全面铺开踩坑
- 决策者脱离一线，方案是"拍脑袋"定的，没人验证过
- 试点成功经验想复制到别处，却发现水土不服

**English:**

- Having set a general direction or slogan but failing to land it, with implementation going off-course everywhere
- Wanting to roll out a new approach but lacking confidence, afraid that a full rollout will stumble
- Decision-makers cut off from the front line, with plans decided "off the top of the head" and never validated by anyone
- Wanting to replicate a successful pilot elsewhere, only to find it does not fit local conditions

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先给一个清楚的总体号召，让人知道方向在哪、要干什么
2. 别急着全面铺开——选两三个有代表性的点，亲自蹲下去
3. 在点上把任务做深做透，突破一点、拿到真实经验
4. 用点上的经验检验和充实最初的号召，看它对不对、够不够具体
5. 从若干个别的做法里提炼出一般性的方法，再拿回更多单位去试
6. 在"一般号召—个别指导—再总结—再推广"里来回，别停在嘴上或纸上
7. 让领导骨干和广大群众结合：靠少数积极分子带动中间、争取落后

**English:**

1. First issue a clear overall call, so people know the direction and what to do
2. Do not rush a full rollout — pick two or three representative points and personally go down and stay there
3. Do the task deeply and thoroughly at those points, breaking through at one point to gain real experience
4. Use the experience from those points to test and enrich the original call — see whether it is right and concrete enough
5. Distill a general method from several particular practices, then take it back to more units to try
6. Move back and forth through "general call — particular guidance — summing up — wider promotion", rather than stopping at words or paper
7. Combine leading cadres with the broad masses: rely on a minority of activists to pull the middle and win over the laggards

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 大方向先行，但落地必须找点突破，先试点后推广
- 一般号召不落地等于空话，个别经验不提炼等于孤立
- 亲自抓典型、解剖麻雀，比发一堆文件有用
- 提法要能回到实践里检验，检验不了的就是口号

**English:**

- The general direction comes first, but landing it requires breaking through at a point — pilot first, then scale
- A general call that never lands is empty talk; particular experience that is never distilled is isolation
- Personally seizing typical cases and dissecting the sparrow is more useful than issuing a pile of documents
- Formulations must return to practice to be tested; anything that cannot be tested is just a slogan

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：推行新政策、新方法、组织变革，需要先验证再铺开的场景。
- 反例：只停在一般号召、不深入个别指导，就是官僚主义——"使自己的号召停止在嘴上、纸上或会议上"；试点的点要有代表性，样本选偏了结论会误导全局；紧急情况需要当机立断时，不能为了试点而拖延。

**English:**

- Applies when: rolling out new policies, new methods, or organizational change that need validation before a full rollout.
- Counterexamples: stopping at a general call without going deep into particular guidance is bureaucratism — "letting one's call stop at the lips, on paper, or in meetings"; the pilot points must be representative, because a biased sample will mislead the whole; in emergencies requiring immediate decision, one must not delay for the sake of piloting.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "任何领导人员，凡不从下级个别单位的个别人员、个别事件取得具体经验者，必不能向一切单位作普遍的指导。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 思想与工作方法
>
> "No leader, if he fails to derive concrete experience from particular individuals, particular events, and particular units at the lower level, will be able to give general guidance to all units." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Methods of Thinking and Methods of Work
