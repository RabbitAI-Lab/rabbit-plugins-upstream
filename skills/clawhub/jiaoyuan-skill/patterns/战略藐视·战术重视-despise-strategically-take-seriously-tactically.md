# 战略藐视·战术重视 Despise Strategically, Take Seriously Tactically

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 面对强大的对手或艰巨任务，内心恐惧、想退缩
- 或者反过来，轻视困难、冒进蛮干
- 需要在"敢不敢打"和"怎么打赢"之间找平衡

**English:**

- Facing a powerful opponent or daunting task with inner fear and a wish to retreat
- Or, conversely, underestimating the difficulty and rushing in recklessly
- Needing to find the balance between "daring to fight" and "how to win"

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 在战略全局上藐视困难：看清其本质与长期趋势，建立"可战胜"的信念
2. 在具体战术上重视困难：每一个环节都认真对待、充分准备
3. 把大目标拆解成一个一个打得赢的具体战斗
4. 用一个个战术胜利，积累成战略胜利
5. 同时防范两种偏差：只看到强而怯战，只看到弱而轻敌
6. 信念用于支撑坚持，严谨用于保证落地，两手都不可偏废

**English:**

1. Despise the difficulty on the strategic, overall plane: see its essence and long-term trend, and build the conviction that it "can be defeated"
2. Take the difficulty seriously on the tactical, concrete plane: treat every link earnestly and prepare fully
3. Break the big goal into one winnable concrete battle after another
4. Accumulate tactical victories into a strategic victory
5. At the same time guard against two deviations: seeing only the enemy's strength and fearing battle, or seeing only weakness and underestimating the enemy
6. Use conviction to sustain persistence and rigor to ensure execution — neither hand may be neglected

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 战略上以一当十（建立信心），战术上以十当一（集中力量落地）
- 藐视用来克服恐惧，重视用来避免翻车
- 二者缺一不可：有藐视无重视是冒险，有重视无藐视是怯懦

**English:**

- Strategically one against ten (build confidence); tactically ten against one (concentrate force to land)
- Despise to overcome fear; take seriously to avoid overturning
- The two are indispensable: despise without seriousness is recklessness, seriousness without despise is cowardice

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：可争取、可通过努力战胜的困难或对手。
- 反例：藐视不等于轻敌、重视不等于畏缩；对客观规律不可违之事（违反物理、市场铁律）不能靠"藐视"硬上。

**English:**

- Applies when: difficulties or opponents that can be won over or defeated through effort.
- Counterexamples: to despise is not to underestimate the enemy, and to take seriously is not to cower; for things that violate objective laws (breaching physics or iron market rules), one must not force ahead on "despise" alone.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "为了同敌人作斗争，我们在一个长时间内形成了一个概念，就是说，在战略上我们要藐视一切敌人，在战术上我们要重视一切敌人。也就是说在整体上我们一定要藐视它，在一个一个的具体问题上我们一定要重视它。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 帝国主义
>
> "In the long period of struggle against the enemy we have formed a concept, namely, strategically we should despise all our enemies, but tactically we should take them all seriously. That is to say, with regard to the whole we must despise them, and with regard to each specific question we must take them seriously." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Imperialism
