# 调查研究思维 Investigation and Research Mindset

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 遇到问题靠拍脑袋、凭旧经验、照搬别人的方案
- 决策缺乏一手信息，方案与实际脱节
- 迷信书本、上级、权威，不敢自己下判断

**English:**

- Deciding by gut feeling, relying on old experience, or copying others' plans when a problem arises
- Making decisions without first-hand information, with plans disconnected from reality
- Blindly trusting books, superiors, and authority, and not daring to judge for yourself

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 承认"没有调查就没有发言权"，把"先弄清情况"设为第一步
2. 带着问题到现场、到一线、到群众中去，而不是坐在屋里想
3. 收集材料、分类整理，去粗取精、去伪存真
4. 结论产生于调查的末尾，而不是先有结论再找证据
5. 把调查结论用于解决问题，在实践中检验并修正
6. 反对本本主义：书本、上级、别人的方案都要经过自己的调查验证

**English:**

1. Accept that "no investigation, no right to speak," and make "first understand the situation" the first step
2. Go with the problem to the scene, to the front line, to the masses, rather than sitting indoors and thinking
3. Collect and classify materials, discarding the dross and keeping the essence, eliminating the false and retaining the true
4. Conclusions come at the end of an investigation, not by first reaching a conclusion and then looking for evidence
5. Use the findings to solve the problem, testing and correcting them in practice
6. Oppose book worship: books, superiors, and others' plans all must be verified through your own investigation

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先调查后决策，结论从事实中来
- 调查要深入真实，反对走马观花式的形式主义
- 反对教条照搬，但也不排斥学习——学习要同实际结合

**English:**

- Investigate before deciding; let conclusions come from the facts
- Investigation must be deep and real; oppose perfunctory formalism
- Oppose dogmatic copying, but do not reject learning — learning must be combined with reality

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：信息不足、需一手了解的决策。
- 反例：紧急情况需当机立断时，不能以"还要调查"为名拖延，可先行动、事后补调查复盘；调查不等于无限收集信息，要设定边界、按时拍板。

**English:**

- Applies to: decisions where information is insufficient and first-hand understanding is needed.
- Counterexamples: in emergencies requiring prompt decision, do not delay in the name of "more investigation" — act first and make up the investigation and review afterward; investigation is not endless information-gathering — set boundaries and decide on schedule.

>
> "All conclusions arise at the end of an investigation into the situation, and not at its beginning." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Investigation

> "要了解情况，唯一的方法是向社会作调查，调查社会各阶级的生动情况。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 调查
>
> "To understand the situation, the only method is to make investigations of society, to investigate the living conditions of the various classes." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Investigation
