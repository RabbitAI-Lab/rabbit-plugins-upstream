# 群众路线思维 The Mass Line Mindset

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 决策脱离实际，方案推不动
- 团队、用户、客户不买账，动员不起来
- 闭门造车，自说自话

**English:**

- Decisions are divorced from reality, and the plan won't move.
- The team, users, and customers don't buy in, and you can't mobilize them.
- You work behind closed doors, talking only to yourself.

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先解决"为什么人"的问题——为最广大的人谋利益
2. 从群众中来：收集分散的意见与真实需求
3. 把分散意见集中起来、系统化，形成决策
4. 到群众中去：把决策回到群众中解释、发动执行
5. 在执行中检验，再收集反馈，如此循环往复
6. 关心群众的具体利益，才能动员群众——先解决切身问题，再谈共同目标

**English:**

1. First settle the question of "for whom" — work for the benefit of the broadest masses.
2. From the masses: collect scattered opinions and real needs.
3. Concentrate and systematize the scattered opinions into a decision.
4. To the masses: take the decision back to the masses, explain it, and mobilize its execution.
5. Test it in execution, collect feedback again, and repeat the cycle.
6. Only by caring about the concrete interests of the masses can you mobilize them — solve their immediate problems first, then talk about the common goal.

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 决策来源要接地气，落实要回到群众
- 先解决切身利益，再谈动员号召
- 集中群众智慧，但不做意见的简单拼凑，要系统化提炼

**English:**

- Decisions must be grounded at the source, and implementation must return to the masses.
- Solve immediate interests first, then talk about mobilization and calls to action.
- Concentrate the wisdom of the masses, but don't just patch opinions together — distill them systematically.

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：涉及多数人的决策、产品、管理。
- 反例：不是无原则迎合；专业判断不能完全被众意取代，需与专家意见结合；"从群众中来"不等于照单全收。

**English:**

- Applies to: decisions, products, and management involving the many.
- Counterexamples: it is not unprincipled pandering; professional judgment cannot be wholly replaced by majority opinion and must be combined with expert advice; "from the masses" does not mean accepting everything as it comes.
