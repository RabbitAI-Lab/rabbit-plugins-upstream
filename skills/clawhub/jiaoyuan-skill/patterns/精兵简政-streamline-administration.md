# 精兵简政 Streamline Administration

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队规模越滚越大，人越来越多，产出却没有同步增长
- 流程、会议、汇报层层加码，一线被"内部事务"占满，没时间做正事
- 收入在收缩，成本结构却还停在扩张期，越撑越虚
- 产品功能堆了一堆，核心价值被稀释，人人都累却都不聚焦

**English:**

- The team keeps ballooning — more and more people, yet output does not grow in step
- Processes, meetings, and reports keep piling on, and the front line is swamped by "internal affairs" with no time for real work
- Revenue is contracting while the cost structure is still stuck in expansion mode, growing ever more hollow
- Product features keep stacking up while the core value gets diluted; everyone is exhausted and no one is focused

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先判断"机构（组织/产品/流程）"与"形势（需求/资源）"是否已经脱节——机构是为过去的形势建的，形势变了机构没变
2. 承认瘦身会疼：现有规模是自己一手建起来的，砍自己建的东西最难下手
3. 算清"鱼大水小"的账：盘子缩了还养原来的规模，只会把自己拖垮；缩小了反而更有力量
4. 明确瘦身是为了"更适合打仗"，不是为瘦而瘦，标准是"小了但更有战斗力"
5. 把精简落成可量化的目标（精简、统一、效能、节约、反官僚化），而不是喊口号
6. 动手要彻底、普遍，不留"敷衍、不痛不痒、局部"的尾巴

**English:**

1. First judge whether the "apparatus" (organization / product / process) and the "situation" (demand / resources) have become disconnected — the apparatus was built for a past situation, but the situation has changed while the apparatus has not
2. Accept that slimming down will hurt: the current scale is something you built yourself, and cutting what you built is the hardest thing to start
3. Reckon the "big fish in small water" account: if the plate has shrunk, keeping the old scale will only drag you down; smaller can actually be stronger
4. Be clear that slimming down is to be "better fitted for battle," not thinness for its own sake; the standard is "smaller but more combat-effective"
5. Turn streamlining into quantifiable goals (streamlining, unity, efficiency, economy, de-bureaucratization), not slogans
6. Carry it out thoroughly and universally, leaving no "perfunctory, half-hearted, partial" loose ends

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 机构（组织/流程）跟着形势走，形势变了先调结构，不硬撑旧规模
- 砍自己建的东西要下决心，别被"习惯"和"沉没成本"绑架
- 精简的终点是更有战斗力，而不是更省钱本身

**English:**

- The apparatus (organization / process) follows the situation; when the situation changes, adjust the structure first rather than propping up the old scale
- Resolve to cut what you yourself built; do not be held hostage by "habit" and "sunk cost"
- The end point of streamlining is greater combat strength, not saving money for its own sake

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：规模与形势脱节、臃肿消耗大于产出、需要从"铺摊子"转向"聚焦战力"时。
- 反例：成长上升期、需要抢市场铺规模的阶段，盲目"精简"会砍掉未来增长点；把"精简"当纯裁员省成本、丢了战斗力，就是为瘦而瘦。

**English:**

- Applies to: when scale is out of step with the situation, bloat consumes more than it produces, and the need is to shift from "spreading the stall" to "focusing combat strength."
- Counterexamples: in a rising growth phase when you need to seize the market and build scale, blind "streamlining" will cut off future growth points; treating "streamlining" as pure layoffs to save cost, at the expense of combat strength, is thinness for its own sake.
