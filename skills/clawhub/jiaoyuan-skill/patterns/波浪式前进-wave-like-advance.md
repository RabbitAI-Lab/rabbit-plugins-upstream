# 波浪式前进 Wave-Like Advance

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 期待直线上升，遇到反复就动摇
- 看不清事物发展的节奏，要么冒进、要么停摆
- 一个阶段顺利就过度扩张，一遇低谷就全盘否定

**English:**

- Expecting a straight upward line and wavering whenever there are setbacks
- Failing to grasp the rhythm of development, either rushing ahead or grinding to a halt
- Over-expanding when one stage goes smoothly, and repudiating everything at the first low point

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 承认事物发展是波浪式、螺旋式的，有起有伏、有进有退
2. 高潮时预见低谷，不冒进、留余地
3. 低谷时看到回升，不绝望、蓄力量
4. 主动运用节奏：巩固一批、再扩大一批
5. 用"防御—进攻""退却—反攻"的节奏掌控主动权
6. 在曲折中坚持总方向，不因一时反复改弦更张

**English:**

1. Recognize that things develop in waves and spirals, with rises and falls, advances and retreats
2. At the crest, foresee the trough: do not rush ahead, keep room to maneuver
3. In the trough, see the coming rebound: do not despair, build up strength
4. Actively use rhythm: consolidate one batch, then expand another
5. Seize the initiative through the rhythm of "defense—offense" and "retreat—counteroffensive"
6. Hold to the overall direction through twists and turns; do not change course because of temporary setbacks

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 按节奏推进，而非直线冲刺
- 进退有据：退是为了更好地进，不是溃退
- 逆周期布局：低谷蓄势、高潮兑现

**English:**

- Advance according to rhythm, not in a straight sprint
- Advance and retreat with justification: retreating is to advance better, not to collapse
- Position counter-cyclically: build strength in the trough, cash in at the crest

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：长期发展类事务（扩张、成长、市场周期）。
- 反例：必须一次到位的硬任务（如工程安全、合规底线）不能"波浪式"打折；波浪不等于人为制造反复，节奏必须服务于目标。

**English:**

- Applies to: long-term development matters (expansion, growth, market cycles).
- Counterexamples: hard tasks that must be done right the first time (such as engineering safety or compliance baselines) must not be discounted in a "wave-like" fashion; waves do not mean artificially creating setbacks — rhythm must serve the goal.

>
