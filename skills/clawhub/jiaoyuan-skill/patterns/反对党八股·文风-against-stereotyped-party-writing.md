# 反对党八股·文风 Against Stereotyped Party Writing

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 写的东西空话连篇，篇幅很长却没有内容
- 讲话、PPT、报告堆满套话和正确的废话，听众昏昏欲睡
- 不看对象，对什么人都是一个腔调、一套说辞
- 形式重于内容，甲乙丙丁开中药铺，条条框框却无实质

**English:**

- Writing that is full of empty talk — long in length but empty of content
- Speeches, slides, and reports stuffed with clichés and "correct" platitudes that put listeners to sleep
- Paying no attention to the audience, using the same tone and the same script for everyone
- Form over substance — laying things out item by item like a Chinese herbalist's shelves, full of categories but empty of substance

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 先看这文章、这讲话有没有真东西，还是只有形式
2. 第一条罪状先对照：是不是空话连篇、言之无物
3. 再看对象：写给谁看、讲给谁听，射箭要看靶子，弹琴要看听众
4. 问语言：是生动活泼的，还是语言无味、像个瘪三
5. 别把形式当内容——列提纲、分条目是工具，不是文章本身
6. 反对"甲乙丙丁开中药铺"式的表面罗列，要看有没有解决问题
7. 好的文风要让实质获得发展，而不是让它窒息

**English:**

1. First see whether this article or talk has anything real in it, or only form
2. Check against the first indictment: is it full of empty talk, with nothing to say?
3. Then look at the audience: who is it written for, who is it spoken to? Aim the arrow at the target, play the music for the listeners
4. Ask about the language: is it lively and vivid, or flat and flavorless, like a scrawny wretch?
5. Do not mistake form for content — outlines and bullet points are tools, not the article itself
6. Oppose the superficial enumeration of "laying things out item by item like a herbalist"; look at whether the problem is solved
7. Good writing should let substance develop, not suffocate it

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先有内容再谈形式，反对空话套话
- 写东西、讲话先想清楚对象是谁
- 短而有内容，胜过长而空洞

**English:**

- Substance before form; oppose empty talk and stock phrases
- Before writing or speaking, be clear about who the audience is
- Short and substantive beats long and hollow

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：写作、演讲、汇报、产品文案、内部文档等一切面向人的表达。
- 反例：不能走到"唯短是举"——原文明确"我们反对的是空话连篇言之无物的八股调，不是说任何东西都以短为好"，该长的内容要长；法律条文、技术规范等有固定格式要求的文体，不能简单套用"反对八股"。

**English:**

- Applies to: all human-facing expression — writing, speeches, reports, product copy, internal documents.
- Counterexamples: do not swing to "the shorter the better" — the original text is explicit that "what we oppose is stereotyped writing that is empty and devoid of substance, not that everything should be short"; content that needs length should be long. Styles with fixed format requirements, such as legal provisions and technical specifications, cannot simply be subjected to "oppose stereotyped writing."

> "Our demand is the unity of politics and art, the unity of content and form, the unity of revolutionary political content and the highest possible perfection of artistic form.
