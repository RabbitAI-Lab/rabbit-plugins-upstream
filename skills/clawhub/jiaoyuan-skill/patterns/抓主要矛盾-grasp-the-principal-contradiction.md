# 抓主要矛盾 Grasp the Principal Contradiction

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 千头万绪、事务缠身，不知道先做什么
- 平均用力，什么都抓，结果什么都没抓好
- 团队目标多而散，资源被摊薄

**English:**

- A thousand threads and tangled affairs, not knowing what to do first
- Spreading effort evenly and grabbing at everything, only to handle nothing well
- Team goals numerous and scattered, with resources spread thin

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 列出当前面对的所有矛盾与问题
2. 分析各矛盾的地位与相互关系，找出起决定作用的主要矛盾
3. 集中力量优先解决主要矛盾
4. 主要矛盾解决了，其他问题往往迎刃而解
5. 注意矛盾会转化，主要矛盾变了要及时重新判断
6. 抓主要矛盾的同时统筹兼顾次要矛盾，防止单打一

**English:**

1. List all the contradictions and problems currently faced
2. Analyze the position of each contradiction and their interrelations, and identify the principal contradiction that plays the decisive role
3. Concentrate force to solve the principal contradiction first
4. Once the principal contradiction is solved, the other problems often resolve themselves
5. Note that contradictions transform; when the principal contradiction changes, reassess in time
6. While grasping the principal contradiction, also take the secondary ones into overall account, guarding against a single-track approach

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先分主次再动手，把主要力量投向决定性问题
- 动态重估优先级，不被惯性绑死
- 抓主要不等于只抓一个，兼顾是为了不塌方

**English:**

- Distinguish the principal from the secondary before acting, directing the main force at the decisive problem
- Reassess priorities dynamically, not bound by inertia
- Grasping the principal does not mean grasping only one; taking all into account is to avoid a collapse

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：多问题交织、资源有限的分配场景。
- 反例：多约束硬条件（如安全、质量、成本缺一不可）不能靠"抓一个"解决；抓主要矛盾是抓决定项，不是忽略其余。

**English:**

- Applies when: multiple problems are interwoven and resources are limited in allocation.
- Counterexamples: multiple hard constraints (such as safety, quality, and cost, none dispensable) cannot be solved by "grasping one"; grasping the principal contradiction means grasping the decisive item, not ignoring the rest.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "矛盾着的两方面中，必有一方面是主要的，他方面是次要的……事物的性质，主要地是由取得支配地位的矛盾的主要方面所规定的。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 思想与工作方法
>
> "Of the two contradictory aspects, one must be principal and the other secondary... The nature of a thing is determined mainly by the principal aspect of a contradiction, the aspect which has gained the dominant position." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Methods of Thinking and Methods of Work
