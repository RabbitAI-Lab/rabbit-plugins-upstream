# 一分为二思维 One Divides into Two

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 看问题片面、绝对化，非黑即白
- 遇到挫折就全盘否定，顺风顺水就盲目乐观
- 只看一面，决策时漏掉风险或漏掉机会

**English:**

- Viewing problems one-sidedly and absolutely, in black-and-white terms
- Wholesale negation when encountering setbacks, and blind optimism when things go smoothly
- Seeing only one side, and missing either risk or opportunity when deciding

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 承认任何事物都有对立统一的两个方面
2. 看成绩也看问题、看有利也看不利、看自己也看对手
3. 判断矛盾的主要方面——决定事物性质的那一面
4. 看到两面在一定条件下互相转化，坏事可以变成好事
5. 主动创造条件，促成有利的转化
6. 反对一点论、反对绝对化，但不停留在"各打五十大板"

**English:**

1. Recognize that everything has two aspects that are both opposed and unified
2. See achievements as well as problems, advantages as well as disadvantages, oneself as well as the opponent
3. Identify the principal aspect of the contradiction — the aspect that determines the nature of the thing
4. See that the two aspects transform into each other under given conditions: bad things can turn into good ones
5. Actively create the conditions that bring about the favorable transformation
6. Oppose one-sidedness and absoluteness, but do not stop at "giving each side fifty strokes of the board"

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 决策前先看两面，把风险与机会都摆上桌
- 既防冒进又防悲观，用转化思维化危为机
- 看两面之后要判主次，避免陷入"怎么都对"的相对主义

**English:**

- Before deciding, look at both sides and put risks and opportunities on the table
- Guard against both rash advance and pessimism, using transformative thinking to turn crisis into opportunity
- After seeing both sides, determine the principal and the secondary, avoiding the relativism of "everything is right"

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：一般认识方法与风险评估。
- 反例：不是和稀泥式的"各有各的道理"；对明确的是非、法律红线、安全底线不适用"两面看"。

**English:**

- Applies when: general methods of understanding and risk assessment.
- Counterexamples: it is not fence-sitting "everyone has a point"; "looking at both sides" does not apply to clear-cut right and wrong, legal red lines, or safety bottom lines.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "必须学会全面地看问题，不但要看到事物的正面，也要看到它的反面。在一定的条件下，坏的东西可以引出好的结果，好的东西也可以引出坏的结果。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 思想与工作方法
>
> "We must learn to look at problems all-sidedly, seeing the reverse as well as the obverse side of things. Under given conditions, a bad thing can lead to good results and a good thing to bad results." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Methods of Thinking and Methods of Work
