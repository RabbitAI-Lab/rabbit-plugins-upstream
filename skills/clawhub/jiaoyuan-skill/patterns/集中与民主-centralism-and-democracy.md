# 集中与民主 Centralism and Democracy

## 触发情境 / Trigger Situations
- 团队要么一言堂，要么议而不决、各说各话
- 讨论时畅所欲言，执行时却打折扣
- 大事小事都投票，效率和责任都糊了
- 决策前不让说话，决策后又抱怨连连

**English:**

- The team is either a one-man show, or it debates endlessly without deciding, with everyone talking past each other
- Free and open in discussion, yet half-hearted in execution
- Voting on everything big and small, blurring both efficiency and accountability
- Not letting people speak before the decision, then hearing complaints all around after it

## 思维路径 / Thinking Path
1. 把民主和集中当成一件事的两面，不是对立的两个选项
2. 决策前要充分民主：让大家把意见摆到桌面上，充分讨论
3. 决策后要坚决集中：形成决议就统一执行，少数服从多数
4. 有不同意见可以保留，但行动上不反对已定决议
5. 警惕两个极端：只集中不民主是一言堂；只民主不集中是极端民主化、一盘散沙
6. 会议要开好：事先通知、充分准备，书记带好"一班人"
7. 民主和集中的分寸随任务和时机调整，但底线是"少数服从多数"

**English:**

1. Treat democracy and centralism as two sides of one thing, not two opposing options
2. Full democracy before the decision: let everyone lay their views on the table and discuss fully
3. Firm centralism after the decision: once a resolution is formed, execute it uniformly, the minority obeying the majority
4. Differing views may be retained, but in action do not oppose the resolution already made
5. Guard against two extremes: centralism without democracy is a one-man show; democracy without centralism is extreme democratization and a sheet of loose sand
6. Hold meetings well: notify in advance, prepare fully, and let the leading comrade guide "the whole group"
7. The balance between democracy and centralism adjusts with the task and the moment, but the bottom line is "the minority obeys the majority"

## 决策原则 / Decision Principles
- 先民主、后集中，讨论充分、执行坚决
- 决议前允许保留意见，决议后行动一致
- 防止一言堂和极端民主化两个极端
- 决策要靠机制（会议、程序），不能靠个人好恶

**English:**

- Democracy first, centralism second: discuss fully, execute resolutely
- Allow views to be retained before the resolution; act in unison after it
- Prevent the two extremes of one-man rule and extreme democratization
- Decisions must rest on mechanisms (meetings, procedures), not personal likes and dislikes

## 适用边界 + 反例提醒 / Applicability Boundaries & Counterexamples
- 适用：团队决策、会议机制、组织治理中处理"议"与"决"的关系。
- 反例：紧急情况需要果断集中，不能为民主而贻误时机；"少数服从多数"不抹杀对少数意见的保护，也不替代对真理的判断——多数也可能错，要靠实践检验；机械套投票可能造成多数人暴政。

**English:**

- Applies to: handling the relationship between "deliberation" and "decision" in team decision-making, meeting mechanisms, and organizational governance.
- Counterexamples: emergencies need resolute centralism; do not miss the moment for the sake of democracy. "The minority obeys the majority" neither eliminates protection for minority views nor replaces the judgment of truth — the majority can also be wrong, and must be tested by practice; mechanically applying voting can produce the tyranny of the majority.

## 语录金句 / Quotations
> "在人民内部，民主是对集中而言，自由是对纪律而言……这种民主和集中的统一，自由和纪律的统一，就是我们的民主集中制。" —— 《毛主席语录》1965版 · 纪律
>
> "Within the ranks of the people, democracy is correlative to centralism and freedom to discipline... this unity of democracy and centralism, of freedom and discipline, constitutes our democratic centralism." —— Quotations (1965 ed.), Discipline
