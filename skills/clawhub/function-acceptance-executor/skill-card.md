## Description: <br>
Executes functional acceptance checklists item by item, records pass, conditional pass, or fail conclusions, and logs defect details for acceptance and regression testing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
QA engineers, developers, and product owners use this skill to execute functional acceptance checklists, verify modules against expected results, record defects, and summarize pass rates before release decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Acceptance testing against unauthorized or production systems can affect real users, accounts, or data. <br>
Mitigation: Use only systems and accounts you are authorized to test, prefer staging or test accounts, and use production data only when the acceptance plan explicitly requires it. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or ambiguous acceptance checklists can produce misleading pass rates and release confidence. <br>
Mitigation: Review checklist coverage, expected results, and required test data before relying on the final acceptance summary. <br>
