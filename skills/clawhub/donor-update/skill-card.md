## Description: <br>
Write a warm donor update or stewardship message that makes a supporter feel their gift mattered. Use when asked to write a donor update, a thank-you/stewardship email, a supporter newsletter, or a gift acknowledgement. Produces a donor-centred update — sincere thanks, the specific impact of their support, a brief story, and a light, optional next step — that strengthens the relationship and sets up the next gift. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External nonprofit, fundraising, and stewardship teams use this skill to draft donor updates, thank-you emails, supporter newsletters, and gift acknowledgements that emphasize gratitude, concrete impact, a short story, and an optional light next step. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may include example figures, donor details, or impact stories when the prompt lacks specifics. <br>
Mitigation: Review the draft before sending and replace any marked example material with verified organizational data. <br>
Risk: A stewardship draft could unintentionally read like a renewed donation request rather than a thank-you message. <br>
Mitigation: Check that gratitude and impact remain the focus and that any next step is light, optional, and appropriate for the audience. <br>
