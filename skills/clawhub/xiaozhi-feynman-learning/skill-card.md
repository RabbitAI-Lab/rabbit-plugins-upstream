## Description:

通过让学生用自己的话解释、举例并迁移知识点，评估理解深度并指出卡住的位置。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking K–12 students use guided teach-back questions to check their understanding of a concept and identify gaps. Guardians or teachers should accompany primary-school students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis resources before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis contacts may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy handling, and crisis resources before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Student learning records or reminders could be shared without clear consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile sharing and reminders off unless the student or guardian clearly opts in.
