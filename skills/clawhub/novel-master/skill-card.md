## Description: <br>
novel-master helps agents plan, draft, and manage publication-grade Chinese long-form fiction from worldbuilding and character design through outlining, chapter writing, foreshadowing, continuity checks, and quality review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bbroot](https://clawhub.ai/user/bbroot) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External writers and agent users use this skill to create Chinese novels, especially medium to long-form projects that need structured planning, character consistency, foreshadowing management, and chapter-level quality checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Private or proprietary fiction material may be retained in local project files created for writing continuity. <br>
Mitigation: Review the files under ~/.qclaw/workspace/novels and delete project directories when the retained material is no longer needed. <br>
