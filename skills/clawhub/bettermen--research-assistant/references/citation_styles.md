# 引用格式规范

## 支持格式

### APA 7th (American Psychological Association)

**期刊论文**:
```
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Title of Journal, Volume(Issue), Page-Page. https://doi.org/xxxx
```

**书籍**:
```
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle. Publisher.
```

**会议论文**:
```
Author, A. A. (Year, Month). Title of paper. In A. Editor (Chair), Title of symposium/conference. Conference Name, Location.
```

### MLA 9th (Modern Language Association)

**期刊论文**:
```
Author, Firstname. "Title of Article." Title of Journal, vol. X, no. X, Year, pp. XX-XX. DOI: xxxx.
```

### Chicago (Notes-Bibliography)

**期刊论文**:
```
Author, Firstname. "Title of Article." Title of Journal Volume, no. Issue (Year): Pages. https://doi.org/xxxx.
```

### IEEE

**期刊论文**:
```
[1] F. Lastname, "Title of paper," Title of Journal, vol. X, no. X, pp. XX-XX, Mon. Year, doi: xxxx.
```

### Harvard

**期刊论文**:
```
Author, A. and Author, B. (Year) 'Title of article', Title of Journal, Volume(Issue), pp. XX-XX. doi: xxxx.
```

### GB/T 7714-2015 (中国国家标准)

**期刊论文**:
```
作者. 题名[J]. 刊名, 年, 卷(期): 起页-止页.
```

### Vancouver (生物医学)

**期刊论文**:
```
Author AA, Author BB. Title of article. Title of Journal. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages.
```

## 常见问题

### 缺少作者
- 使用机构名（World Health Organization）
- 匿名作品用 Anon. 或 佚名

### 缺少日期
- 用 n.d. (no date) / 出版年不详

### 缺少DOI
- 优先使用 DOI
- 无DOI时用URL或数据库名

### 多作者
- APA: 列出前20位作者，超过用 ...
- MLA: 3位以上用 et al.
- IEEE: 6位以上用 et al.
- GB/T 7714: 列出前3位，超过用 ,等
