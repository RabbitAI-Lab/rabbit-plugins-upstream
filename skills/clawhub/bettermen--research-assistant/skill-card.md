## Description: <br>
AI科研全流程助理 helps researchers with literature search, paper reading, literature reviews, topic selection, study design, data analysis, manuscript writing, editing, citation management, journal selection, funding applications, presentations, and interactive HTML report generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Graduate students, faculty, and research staff use this skill to plan and execute academic workflows from research idea through publication preparation. It supports literature discovery, evidence synthesis, research design, analysis guidance, writing assistance, citation formatting, funding drafts, presentation planning, and local HTML report generation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated reports and academic recommendations may contain incorrect citations, factual claims, journal information, or statistical interpretations. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated reports before relying on them, manually verify citations and factual claims, and validate methods against domain standards. <br>
Risk: Uploaded or pasted research data may include sensitive or unpublished information. <br>
Mitigation: Redact sensitive data before use and keep report output paths in normal project or downloads folders. <br>
Risk: The skill can activate on broad academic phrases and write a local HTML report file. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended workflow before generating a report and inspect the output file before sharing or submitting it. <br>
