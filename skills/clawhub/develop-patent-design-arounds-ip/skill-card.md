## Description:

Develop and screen single-patent design-around concepts using an application-requirements baseline, claim-feature and functional reconstruction, TRIZ trimming, evidence-backed function-oriented search, differentiated concept engineering, and jurisdiction-specific claim risk review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, patent practitioners, and product engineers use this skill to develop technically plausible alternatives to a patented implementation and preliminarily screen those concepts against a specific patent claim set. It is intended for early design-around exploration and evidence-backed risk triage, not legal opinions or launch decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive patent and product-design information through patent research connectors.

Mitigation: Use only authorized materials, avoid sending confidential data to connectors unless permitted, and work locally from approved sources when confidentiality prevents connector use.

Risk: Design-around concepts and claim-risk screens may be incomplete, outdated, or legally incorrect if treated as conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary technical and claim-risk screening, verify source material and claim versions, and involve qualified patent counsel before launch, filing, or infringement decisions.

Risk: Missing application requirements, jurisdiction, prosecution history, or engineering evidence can make feasibility rankings and legal-risk screens unreliable.

Mitigation: Record assumptions and unavailable evidence explicitly, mark affected analysis as provisional or unresolved, and require engineering validation before relying on ranked concepts.
