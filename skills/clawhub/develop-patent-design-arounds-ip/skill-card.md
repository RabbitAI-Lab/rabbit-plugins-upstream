## Description:

Develops and screens single-patent design-around concepts using requirements baselines, claim-feature and functional reconstruction, TRIZ trimming, evidence-backed function-oriented search, differentiated concept engineering, and jurisdiction-specific claim risk review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External patent, product, and engineering teams use this skill to generate technically plausible alternatives to a patented implementation and perform preliminary claim-risk screening for one target patent. It is intended for evidence-backed design exploration, not legal opinions or launch decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent and product details may be shared with configured patent-research connectors.

Mitigation: Use only authorized, shareable materials with the connectors, and work locally from authorized documents when confidential data cannot be sent externally.

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for legal opinions or final freedom-to-operate conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary engineering and legal-risk screening that requires qualified counsel review and engineering validation.

Risk: Incomplete claim text, jurisdiction, prosecution history, or product facts can make risk screening unreliable.

Mitigation: Record assumptions and unavailable evidence explicitly, block legal-risk screening when essential inputs are missing, and preserve counsel questions in the final report.
