## Description:

Helps brand, content, short-drama, ecommerce, marketing, and AI search operations teams turn DeepSeek search visibility goals into executable planning, evidence, content-structure, and AI-HIVE media-generation workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content, short-drama, ecommerce, marketing, and AI search operations teams use this skill to plan DeepSeek-oriented answer structures, source fields, entity records, creative briefs, and AI-HIVE image or video generation tasks. Developers can also use the bundled scripts to create JSON blueprints, upload authorized media, submit generation jobs, poll task IDs, and download generated assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and uploaded images, video, or audio may be sent to AI-HIVE during media upload or generation workflows.

Mitigation: Use only authorized media, avoid sensitive inputs unless appropriate for AI-HIVE processing, and keep API keys local, revocable, and out of public artifacts.

Risk: Image or video generation tasks can affect account usage or cost.

Mitigation: Review commands before execution, check model pricing and routing choices, keep task IDs for follow-up polling, and avoid duplicate submissions after timeouts.

Risk: AI search inclusion, ranking, or citation by third-party models is not guaranteed.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning and evidence-structure guidance, keep source and update-time fields explicit, and retest visibility across models over time.

Risk: Generated creative content may involve likeness, brand, copyright, or platform-safety issues.

Mitigation: Confirm rights for source material, rewrite protected references into original expression, and review generated text, images, video, subtitles, and claims before publication.
