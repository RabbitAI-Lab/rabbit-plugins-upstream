## Description: <br>
Smart Procurement Navigator helps procurement and bidding teams screen opportunities, analyze tender documents, build scoring models, draft bid responses, polish content, and run pre-submission compliance reviews. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Personal Use Only <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Market, business, solution, bid-writing, and decision teams use this skill to turn procurement notices, tender documents, requirements, and company materials into bid-readiness analysis, response structure, draft sections, task lists, and compliance review outputs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may encourage collecting non-public influence information about evaluators or decision-makers. <br>
Mitigation: Use only authorized procurement documents and approved public or internal sources; remove prompts that ask for evaluator relationships, internal decision processes, or non-public key-person intelligence. <br>
Risk: Generated bid analyses, pricing simulations, and compliance guidance can be incorrect or over-relied on. <br>
Mitigation: Keep final participation decisions, pricing, commitments, signatures, and submissions under qualified human review and approval. <br>
