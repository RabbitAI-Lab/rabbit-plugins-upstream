## Description:

A professional storyboard skill for film, advertising, short video, and educational narrative scenarios, built around a strict plan-first, render-later flow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Filmmakers, advertisers, short-video creators, and educators can use the skill to turn a creative brief into a staged storyboard plan, character references, panel prompts, and generated visuals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Use can require dLazy CLI login or an API key, and prompts or local media may be sent to dLazy hosted services for generation.

Mitigation: Review the authentication steps, confirm hosted processing is acceptable for the inputs, and check unresolved references before use.
