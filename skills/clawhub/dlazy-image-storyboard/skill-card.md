## Description:

A professional storyboard skill for film, advertising, short video, and educational narrative scenarios, built around a strict 'plan first, render later' flow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Filmmakers, advertisers, short-video creators, and educators use this skill to turn creative briefs into structured cinematic or narrative storyboards, including character references, panel prompts, and print-ready layouts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
