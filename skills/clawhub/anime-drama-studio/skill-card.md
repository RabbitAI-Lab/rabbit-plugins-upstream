## Description: <br>
Anime Drama Studio helps creators produce anime-drama scripts, character profiles, image prompts, scene prompts, Sora storyboard prompts, and tabular shot plans from a story idea or existing script. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External creators and developers use this skill to turn story ideas or scripts into production-oriented anime-drama writing assets, including scripts, character descriptions, image-generation prompts, and storyboard materials. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts and account keys may be sent to a hardcoded unencrypted remote API. <br>
Mitigation: Do not submit private scripts, unpublished IP, client material, sensitive account data, or credentials unless the operator is trusted and the endpoint is changed to HTTPS. <br>
Risk: The skill includes paid account and service commands. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm pricing, account terms, and credit usage with the publisher before using registration, account, or creation commands. <br>
Risk: The included script appears broken as published. <br>
Mitigation: Review and test the script in an isolated environment before installation or production use. <br>
