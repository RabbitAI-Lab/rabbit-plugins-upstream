## Description:

Interprets private-well test results, maps symptoms to likely causes and tests, and helps compare treatment options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Private-well owners use the skill to understand lab results, identify follow-up tests, and review treatment proposals before making water-safety or equipment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Abbreviated treatment advice or misread report details could lead to unsafe water-use or treatment decisions.

Mitigation: Verify units, sampling instructions, thresholds, and treatment choices with an accredited lab or local health department; follow CDC disinfection precautions and use safe alternate water until testing confirms safety.
