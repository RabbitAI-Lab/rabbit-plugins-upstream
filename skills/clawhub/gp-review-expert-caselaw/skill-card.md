为政府采购评审委员会在评审争议中提供即时类案判定口径、投诉风险等级、法条依据和澄清/客观佐证提示。 <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Government-procurement review committee members and review leaders use this skill during live review disputes to classify the issue, compare similar complaint-handling patterns, assess future complaint risk, and decide whether lawful clarification or objective supporting evidence is needed. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill provides legal-domain guidance that may be wrong if cited laws, case anchors, or knowledge-base records are missing or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory, verify cited legal sources and case anchors, and confirm the named knowledge bases are mounted and current before relying on risk ratings or clarification suggestions. <br>
Risk: The skill is scoped to Chinese government procurement and can be misapplied to engineering tendering or other legal domains. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for government-procurement review disputes and route engineering tendering or unrelated disputes to an appropriate domain-specific process. <br>
