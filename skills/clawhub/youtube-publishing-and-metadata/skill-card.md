## Description: <br>
The YouTube publish-metadata skill writes video titles, descriptions with chapters, tags, category, privacy, and madeForKids settings for YouTube publishing via WoopSocial. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, social media operators, and agent users use this skill to prepare YouTube upload metadata and publishing configuration from a brand profile, goals, and an existing script or video. It helps produce keyword-led titles, above-the-fold descriptions, relevant tags, category, privacy, and madeForKids decisions without claiming to generate video, thumbnails, analytics, or ranking guarantees. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incorrect privacy or madeForKids choices can affect public visibility, channel features, and legal compliance. <br>
Mitigation: Review privacy and madeForKids decisions before publishing, and set madeForKids truthfully based on whether the content is child-directed. <br>
Risk: Metadata guidance can be mistaken for a ranking guarantee or substituted for actual analytics. <br>
Mitigation: Treat metadata as launch preparation, review claims before use, and rely on native YouTube analytics rather than fabricated metrics. <br>
