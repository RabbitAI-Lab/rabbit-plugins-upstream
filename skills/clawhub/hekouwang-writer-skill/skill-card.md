## Description:

帮助中文写作者基于素材撰写和改进文档、创作或修订文章，并检查表达与数字的一致性。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huiyonghkw](https://clawhub.ai/user/huiyonghkw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向需要处理中文方案、报告、办事指南、邮件、汇报或文章的写作者，帮助他们从素材起草和修订内容，检查表达与数字，并保留出处和改动记录。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback from writing tasks may lead to persistent changes to the skill’s rules for future sessions without human review.

Mitigation: Review feedback-derived rule changes before they are promoted, and disable the promotion behavior if future instructions should remain fixed.
