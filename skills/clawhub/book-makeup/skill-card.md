## Description: <br>
Book makeup services through Lokuli MCP for users who need to find and book makeup providers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[edwardrodriguez703-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/edwardrodriguez703-design) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to search for makeup services, check provider availability, and create a booking through Lokuli after confirming the provider, service, time, and contact details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The booking flow sends personal contact details to Lokuli for booking creation. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, date and time, customer name, email, and phone number with the user. <br>
