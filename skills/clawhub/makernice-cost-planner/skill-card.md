## Description:

Plan which Makernice models, resolutions and durations fit a budget before generating anything. Use when the user gives a budget in USD, asks how many videos or images they can make, or wants to compare the price of options. Planning never creates jobs or charges anything.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlesaitime](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlesaitime)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Makernice users use the skill to compare current image and video generation options and plan jobs within a USD budget before creating anything.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generation can spend money, and a successful job may be charged even when safety review hides its output.

Mitigation: Refresh prices for each plan, approve the total budget before running, and check each job's estimated cost against the approved limit.
