## Description:

Drafts consumer complaint letters from incident timelines and helps plan escalation, regulator outreach, and case follow-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Consumers and their assistants use this skill to organize documented disputes, draft factual complaint and escalation letters, identify possible regulator routes, and track follow-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may cite outdated or inapplicable legal rights, regulator routes, or deadlines.

Mitigation: Check current rules for the relevant jurisdiction before sending letters or filing complaints.

Risk: Letters, filings, or public reviews may disclose personal or account information or repeat unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Verify the incident timeline and redact account numbers and private personal data before sharing.
