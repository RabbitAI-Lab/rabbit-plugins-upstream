## Description:

Provides a countdown, transition phase, timeline, and 12-item self-inspection checklist for China's 2025 medical-device GMP rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Medical-device quality teams use this skill to plan China's GMP transition, check time remaining until November 1, 2026, and prepare internal reviews and self-inspection actions. It provides planning prompts, not a compliance determination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (China medical-device regulatory context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Frozen regulatory dates or example provincial deadlines may be mistaken for current requirements.

Mitigation: Check the latest official NMPA and local regulator notices before making compliance decisions.

Risk: A planning checklist could be mistaken for a compliance assessment.

Mitigation: Have qualified personnel review the applicable rules and the organization's evidence; do not treat the checklist as a compliance determination.
