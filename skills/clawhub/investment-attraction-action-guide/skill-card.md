## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated company lists, investment judgments, and招商 opportunities may be incomplete, outdated, or based on weak public signals. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support only and verify key facts through official or authoritative sources before outreach or commitments. <br>
Risk: The skill can propose招商 paths, policy tools, or landing conditions that may not match a specific region's current resources or approval constraints. <br>
