## Description:

Creates a multilingual social campaign by generating visuals once and localizing them while preserving the master layout and design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[canvora](https://clawhub.ai/user/canvora)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and content creators use the skill to create campaign visuals once, then localize them for selected language markets while preserving the original design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign material is sent to Canvora for generation and localization, and credit use grows with the number of visuals and languages.

Mitigation: Use a trusted Canvora CLI, share only authorized campaign material, and review the announced credit total before starting.
