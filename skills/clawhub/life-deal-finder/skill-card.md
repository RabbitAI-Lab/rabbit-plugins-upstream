## Description:

Helps users find and verify deals for food delivery, drinks, hotels, travel, and shopping, with claim links or Taobao codes where available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask for current coupons and deal options across food delivery, cafes, hotels, travel, and shopping. The skill is intended to return concise deal details and links or Taobao codes; users should confirm availability and terms with the platform before purchase.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
