## Description: <br>
Generate comprehensive website style guides and design systems from URLs, screenshots, and existing documentation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tomstools11](https://clawhub.ai/user/tomstools11) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Designers, developers, and brand teams use this skill to turn websites, screenshots, or existing documentation into a structured style guide and design system reference, including brand identity, UI components, layout, content, and accessibility guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process internal URLs, confidential brand documents, or sensitive screenshots if a user supplies them. <br>
Mitigation: Use public or authorized materials only, and confirm that any internal or confidential inputs are appropriate for the workspace before analysis. <br>
Risk: Generated style guide content may misstate brand, accessibility, or component guidance when source material is incomplete or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated PDF before sharing it or using it as a design-system source of truth. <br>
