## Description:

Analyzes WorkBuddy conversation records to produce a monthly Markdown review of time use, recurring topics, decisions, emotional patterns, progress, and unfinished items.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guipi888](https://clawhub.ai/user/guipi888)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

WorkBuddy users can review a selected month of their conversation history to spot work patterns and choose follow-up actions. Review the scope and destination before enabling recurring analysis, especially when conversations contain sensitive information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill accesses private conversation history without sufficiently clear scope, and its results include unrelated promotional content.

Mitigation: Before installation or execution, choose the month, projects, recurring schedule, and output location; remove the mandatory advertising and limit access to the conversation data needed for the review.

Risk: Exported full transcripts may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Review where exported copies are stored and who can access them; avoid retaining full-transcript copies when they are not needed.

Risk: Automated findings and recommendations may be incomplete or misleading.

Mitigation: Check the report against your own context before acting on its conclusions.
