Panduan berbahasa Indonesia untuk membantu pengguna mendaftar akun PowPow Bubble, memahami produk, menelusuri masalah pendaftaran, dan menyelesaikan aktivasi langganan tanpa mengambil alih kredensial atau pembayaran.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a guided Indonesian-language registration assistant for PowPow Bubble. It helps them learn what the product does, open the official registration and subscription pages, troubleshoot common signup and activation errors, and decide the next step without sharing private credentials in chat.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could expose passwords, email verification codes, or payment proof if they try to complete registration or payment inside the chat.

Mitigation: Keep registration and payment on the official PowPow pages or provider flows, and do not ask users to share passwords, verification codes, or payment evidence.

Risk: Signup or subscription guidance may become stale if PowPow changes its registration, activation, or payment behavior.

Mitigation: Use the referenced registration and payment guidance as support material, but direct users to the live official pages and platform support when page behavior or payment status differs.
