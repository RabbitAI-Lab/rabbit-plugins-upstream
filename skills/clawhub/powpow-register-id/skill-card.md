## Description:

Guides people through PowPow Bubble account registration, common signup issues, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People new to PowPow Bubble use this skill to learn about the service, complete registration themselves, troubleshoot signup issues, and find the subscription activation page.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment proof in chat.

Mitigation: Complete signup and payment on official PowPow pages; do not share these items with the assistant.

Risk: Registration or subscription guidance may become outdated as the service changes.

Mitigation: Follow the current instructions shown on the official PowPow pages and check with platform support when activation is delayed.
