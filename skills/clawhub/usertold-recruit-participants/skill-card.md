## Description:

Plan participant recruitment for a UserTold Study and produce participant-readable recruitment copy plus canonical Invitation, Visibility, and neutral Intake inputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[usertold](https://clawhub.ai/user/usertold)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, research, and customer teams use this skill to plan truthful participant recruitment for UserTold studies, including channel selection, reward terms, invitation copy, visibility settings, neutral intake questions, and canonical UserTold configuration inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated recruitment copy, reward terms, visibility rules, or intake questions could be inaccurate, misleading, or include identifiers if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review the generated packet before use, confirm reward and consent terms, keep personal identifiers out of URLs and source tags, and require explicit approval before changing live UserTold study configuration.
