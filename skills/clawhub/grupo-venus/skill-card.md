## Description:

Natal charts, transit forecasts and, with a ticket, written reports from the third-party site grupovenus.com. Use for Grupo Venus or carta astral readings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apresmoi](https://clawhub.ai/user/apresmoi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People interested in astrology can use the skill to view natal charts, explore transit forecasts and interpretations, and, with a user-provided ticket, access written reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Birth details and other personal information are sent to third-party services for charting and geocoding; the security review also notes that the helper was only partially inspected.

Mitigation: Explain the data transfers and obtain consent before use; review the helper's permissions and network use before installing if those matter to you.
