## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive gentle, symbolic tarot reflections for meaning-making, grounding, and non-clinical emotional support. It supports single-card and three-card spreads using the Major Arcana.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may apply tarot framing to general requests for clarity or feeling stuck.

Mitigation: Use only when tarot-style reflection is appropriate, and clarify the framing for ambiguous requests.

Risk: Users could mistake reflective tarot language for clinical, legal, financial, or predictive advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses non-clinical, non-directive, and non-predictive; shift to safety-first support if explicit self-harm intent appears.
