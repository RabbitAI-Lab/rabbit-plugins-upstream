## Description:

Aide les utilisateurs francophones à créer un compte PowPow (Bubble), à résoudre les erreurs d'inscription et à finaliser l'activation de leur abonnement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this French-language guidance skill to understand PowPow/Bubble, complete account registration in the official web or app flow, troubleshoot common registration issues, and reach subscription activation without sharing credentials with the assistant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials while asking for registration help.

Mitigation: Keep all registration, verification, and payment actions on official PowPow pages and instruct users not to share credentials or codes with the assistant.

Risk: Users may expect the assistant to complete payment or submit account forms on their behalf.

Mitigation: Make clear that the skill provides guidance only and does not execute code, submit forms, collect credentials, or perform payments.

Risk: Registration or subscription behavior may change after the skill's reference files were written.

Mitigation: Treat the live PowPow pages as authoritative when they differ from the documented guidance.
