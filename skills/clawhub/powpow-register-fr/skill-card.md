## Description:

Guides French-speaking users through PowPow/Bubble registration, common sign-up problems, and subscription activation without handling their account or payment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow/Bubble users receive French-language guidance for creating an account, troubleshooting registration, and navigating subscription activation themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration and subscription links can expose users to credential theft or fraudulent payment pages if misdirected.

Mitigation: Direct users to official PowPow/Bubble pages and never request passwords, email verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Risk: Users may mistake account creation for activation or assume a delayed payment confirmation has failed.

Mitigation: Explain that activation follows subscription payment and that WeChat QR payments require manual confirmation.
