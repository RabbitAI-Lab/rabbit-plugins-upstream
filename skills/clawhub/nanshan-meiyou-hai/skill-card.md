## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for coaching-style personal growth reflection across cognition, decisions, habits, emotions, business, and relationships. It frames the issue, compares mentor-style perspectives, and returns a short action plan.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake general persona-style growth advice for professional medical, legal, financial, or crisis guidance.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for general coaching-style reflection and seek qualified professionals for professional or crisis topics.

Risk: Persona-style responses may imply positions or endorsements by the named public figures.

Mitigation: Treat the personas as writing-style references only; the artifact states they do not represent the individuals' actual positions.
