## Description:

Edits rendered Phantra clips by trimming, joining, reformatting, and creating temporary download links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video editors use the skill to finish rendered clips by trimming or joining selected videos, changing aspect ratio, and sharing a temporary download link.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anyone holding a temporary download link can download the file until the link expires.

Mitigation: Treat download links as private and choose the shortest useful expiry.
