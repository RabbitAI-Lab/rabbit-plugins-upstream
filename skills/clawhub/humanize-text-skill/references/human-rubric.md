# Human Review Rubric

This rubric is for human review, eval writeups, and side-by-side rewrite
comparisons. It is **not** the engine score. Do not fold it into `score`.

## How to use

Score each dimension from 1 to 5:

- `1` = poor
- `3` = acceptable
- `5` = strong

Use short notes, not essays. The point is to explain why one rewrite is better than another.

## Dimensions

### 1. Directness

- `5`: states the thing directly; little or no throat-clearing
- `3`: mostly direct, with some residue
- `1`: padded with opener/summarizer/announcement language

### 2. Rhythm

- `5`: sentence lengths vary naturally; the paragraph breathes
- `3`: some variation, but still a bit even
- `1`: metronomic, same-sized sentences throughout

### 3. Trust

- `5`: respects the reader; does not over-explain or hand-hold
- `3`: occasional explanatory scaffolding
- `1`: constantly announces, softens, or explains the obvious

### 4. Specificity

- `5`: uses concrete facts, actions, numbers, names, or examples
- `3`: some specifics, some empty judgment left
- `1`: mostly abstractions, praise, or symbolic meaning

### 5. Human voice

- `5`: sounds like a person with a scene-appropriate stance
- `3`: clean but a little flat
- `1`: generic, templated, or obviously generated

## Reading the result

- `22-25`: ready to send
- `17-21`: solid, but one visible layer remains
- `12-16`: cleaner than before, still stiff or generic
- `<12`: needs another pass

## Notes

- A low `score` can still earn a weak `Human voice` rating if the text is clean but flat.
- A strong `Human voice` rating never excuses factual drift.
- If protected spans force stiffness, note that in review instead of penalizing unfairly.
