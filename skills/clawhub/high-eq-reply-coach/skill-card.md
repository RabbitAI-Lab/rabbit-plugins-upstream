## Description:

Drafts three annotated reply options for workplace and relationship conversations from chat text or screenshots, using communication frameworks and referring crisis signals to professional help.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who are unsure how to respond to a workplace or personal message use the skill to compare safer, more direct or playful, and brief drafts with explanations, then choose and edit what to send.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic checks can miss crisis messages, particularly in English.

Mitigation: Make an independent crisis assessment for each conversation; stop drafting and refer to professional help when crisis signals appear.

Risk: Chat screenshots may expose identifying details or be handled according to the host platform's data practices.

Mitigation: Mask unrelated names, avatars, and handles, review the host platform's data handling, and remove temporary chat or draft files after use.

Risk: Suggested replies may not fit the full context or could be sent without review.

Mitigation: Review and adapt each draft to the real conversation before sending.
