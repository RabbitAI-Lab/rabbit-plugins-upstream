## Description:

Helps agents draft professional Chinese-language analysis for local industrial fund audiences, including weekly investment updates, local investment case studies, and thematic investment articles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perrykono-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/perrykono-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External and internal content teams can use this skill to prepare draft public-account articles for local industry investment professionals, government guidance funds, state-owned GP teams, investment managers, and investment committees. The drafts are intended for human review before publication or operational use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft content may be sent to an external document service if publication connector instructions are followed without a separate confirmation gate.

Mitigation: Review the generated Markdown first, remove sensitive or nonpublic information, and require explicit human approval before using Tencent Docs or any other external connector.
