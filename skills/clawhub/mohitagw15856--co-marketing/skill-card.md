## Description: <br>
Plan a co-marketing partnership — two brands reaching each other's audiences for mutual gain. Use when asked to plan a partnership, joint campaign, co-branded content/webinar, integration launch, or partner outreach. Produces the partner fit rationale, a fair value exchange, the joint campaign plan, the partner pitch, and how success is split and measured. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, partnerships, and growth teams use this skill to plan mutually beneficial co-marketing partnerships, including partner fit, value exchange, joint campaign structure, outreach, lead sharing, and success metrics. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Co-marketing inputs may include business-sensitive audience size, lead-sharing, pipeline, or partner information. <br>
Mitigation: Limit inputs to information appropriate for the agent environment and review generated plans before sharing them externally. <br>
Risk: Generated outreach, value exchange, or deal terms may be inaccurate, imbalanced, or unsuitable for a specific partner relationship. <br>
Mitigation: Have the responsible marketing or partnerships owner review the pitch, lead split, campaign obligations, and success metrics before sending or committing to them. <br>
