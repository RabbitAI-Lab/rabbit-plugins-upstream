## Description:

Finds and qualifies YouTube creators for partnerships, sponsorships, and affiliate campaigns, producing an evidence-backed shortlist and business contact handoff without sending outreach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and partnership managers use this skill to research and qualify YouTube creators for product campaigns, then hand off sourced business contacts for separate outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business contact collection can expose creator email addresses to inappropriate use.

Mitigation: Use shortlist-only mode when contacts are unnecessary, and use collected addresses only for lawful, policy-compliant business outreach.

Risk: Paid data lookups can incur unexpected costs or conflict with provider terms.

Mitigation: Agree on a budget before discovery and review Glasser pricing and provider terms.
