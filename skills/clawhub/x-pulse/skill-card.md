## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill for social listening, trend tracking, competitor monitoring, and research into public conversations on X without an X account or API key.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Repeated or broad lookups can consume ClawLink credit.

Mitigation: Check current per-call prices and obtain approval before costly calls or loops over many items.

Risk: Troubleshooting reports may be sent to ClawLink.

Mitigation: Review report contents and avoid sharing sensitive information when reporting failures.
