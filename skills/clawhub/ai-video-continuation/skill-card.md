## Description:

Extends a short source clip before or after its existing action, matching the continuity state and next visual beat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and video editors use this skill to extend a single source clip with a natural lead-in or continuation, preserving its action and visual continuity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The authorization is shared across Beatra skills and includes unrelated music, speech, and voice operations.

Mitigation: Review the full authorization before installing; the shared connection can be revoked in the Beatra Console.

Risk: Selected footage leaves the computer, and installation and environment analytics are sent automatically.

Mitigation: Upload only footage you are comfortable sending and review the automatic analytics disclosure before installing.

Risk: Video extensions are paid operations.

Mitigation: Require confirmation of the live estimate before each paid extension; do not submit until the user confirms sufficient credits or a completed top-up.
