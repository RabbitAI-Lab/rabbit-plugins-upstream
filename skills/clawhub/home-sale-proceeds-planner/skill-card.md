## Description:

Estimates home-sale proceeds and taxes, checks Section 121 exclusion eligibility, and compares renting with selling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

U.S. home sellers can estimate proceeds after selling costs and mortgage payoffs, review first-pass capital-gains exclusion eligibility, and compare renting with selling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax estimates and local closing costs may be inaccurate, especially for rental history or location-specific fees.

Mitigation: Independently check local fees and tax treatment before relying on estimates; get qualified tax advice for rental history or complex exclusions.

Risk: Missing ownership or occupancy dates can produce an assumed-eligible exclusion result.

Mitigation: Provide and verify the relevant dates before relying on the eligibility estimate.
