## Description: <br>
This skill helps agents create, evaluate, and refine 1-3 minute Chinese oral short-video scripts using an eight-step framework across social strategy, workplace, human behavior, cognition, NPD recognition, and social-game topics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[muwerr-spec](https://clawhub.ai/user/muwerr-spec) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to turn a topic or source material into a ready-to-record Chinese short-video script with hooks, pain-point framing, arguments, methods, emotional close, CTA, title, pause markers, emphasis markers, and an AI-style self-check. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs may address sensitive social or mental-health-adjacent topics and could overstate claims or use stigmatizing language. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated scripts for accuracy, neutral framing, platform-sensitive claims, and compliance with the skill's rule to analyze reactions rather than make factual judgments on sensitive events. <br>
