## Use Case: <br>
External founders, startup operators, and advisors use this skill to understand financing economics, model dilution, compare SAFE or priced-round terms, size option pools, and produce a plain-English ownership breakdown with step-by-step math. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat educational cap table modeling as legal or financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs as illustrative modeling, confirm assumptions and financing terms with counsel, and preserve the skill's legal and financial advice disclaimer. <br>
Risk: Missing or assumed financing inputs can produce misleading ownership or dilution estimates. <br>
Mitigation: State all assumptions clearly, label illustrative figures, and verify before/after ownership tables and SAFE or option-pool calculations before relying on the result. <br>
