## Description:

Platform-specific AI search optimization - audit and optimize for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Bing Copilot individually.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External site owners, SEO teams, and developers use this skill to audit a target domain's readiness for AI-search citations across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Web Search, Perplexity, Gemini, and Bing Copilot. It produces per-platform scores, identified gaps, and prioritized optimization actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may recommend account setup, public community posts, Wikidata or Wikipedia edits, Business Profile changes, sitemap submission, or IndexNow implementation.

Mitigation: Confirm the target domain and require separate user approval and review before acting on recommendations that change public profiles, third-party accounts, or site indexing.
