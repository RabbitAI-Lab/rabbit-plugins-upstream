## Description:

Helps people assess where a major change may lead by tracing measurable trends, comparing historical patterns, and setting dated signals to revisit each judgment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People facing a major technology or industry shift use the skill to turn a vague trend question into measurable indicators, practical bets and non-bets, and dated signals for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill expects configuration and shared interaction rules that are absent from the supplied release, so its setup is unverified.

Mitigation: Supply and review the required configuration and shared rules before use; the skill directs the agent to stop when its configuration is missing.

Risk: Business judgments and behavioral notes may persist in the configured memory directory.

Mitigation: Confirm the memory directory is private and set preferences for reviewing and deleting saved notes.
