## Description:

Guides users through a structured trend-analysis workflow that turns a perceived major shift into measurable curves, concrete bets and non-bets, and dated signals for later validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and individual operators use this skill when they face a major market, technology, platform, or industry shift and need to decide what to observe, what to bet on, what not to bet on, and what signal should later validate the judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save trend judgments and outcomes in local memory or append proven missing variables to its reference material.

Mitigation: Review the configured memory and skill-reference paths before use, and keep local persistence limited to information the user is comfortable retaining.

Risk: Trend analysis can be mistaken for direct financial advice when users ask about stocks, crypto, funds, or other investments.

Mitigation: Use the skill for variables, scenarios, and validation signals only; do not use it to make buy, sell, or hold decisions.

Risk: Outdated numbers can mislead decisions in fast-moving markets or technologies.

Mitigation: Require current sources and dates for quantitative claims, and mark unknown values as pending rather than filling them from memory.
