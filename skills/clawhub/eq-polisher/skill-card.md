## Description: <br>
Eq Polisher helps Chinese-language users rewrite blunt messages, respond tactfully to praise, difficult questions, or awkward moments, and draft polished replies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chiling7](https://clawhub.ai/user/chiling7) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and external users use this skill to make Chinese communication more tactful while preserving the original intent. It supports message polishing, situational response planning, and ready-to-send reply drafts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad phrasing such as asking how to reply, even when the user intended a different type of answer. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit prompts when requesting non-EQ answers or when another skill should handle the request. <br>
Risk: Generated wording can be socially inappropriate if the user applies it without checking the real relationship, context, or stakes. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt each suggested reply before sending, especially in sensitive workplace, customer, or personal situations. <br>
