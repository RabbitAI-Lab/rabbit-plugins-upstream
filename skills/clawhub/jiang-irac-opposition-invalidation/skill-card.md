## Use Case: <br>
Trademark practitioners and legal teams use this skill for CNIPA opposition and invalidation matters: selecting attack routes, mapping legal elements to evidence, screening standing and time-bar risks, and drafting examiner-readable reasoning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incorrect statutes, deadlines, evidence treatment, or citations could affect a legal filing. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify all law, deadlines, evidence, and citations, and have a qualified professional review any filing before submission. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or unverifiable case materials can lead to conservative, incomplete, or misleading route assessments. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the minimum necessary registry data, timeline, party facts, and evidence inventory with source, date, type, and proof purpose; treat material gaps as blockers. <br>
Risk: The skill is text-only and does not itself validate external records or evidence authenticity. <br>
Mitigation: Use official or independently verifiable records and confirm authenticity, relevance, completeness, timing, and cross-examination resistance outside the model output. <br>
