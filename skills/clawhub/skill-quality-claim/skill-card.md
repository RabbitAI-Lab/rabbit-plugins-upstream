## Description: <br>
Helps SQE and customer-quality teams draft customer claim letters and supplier chargeback documents, calculate PPM and loss amounts, complete the evidence chain, and produce plain-text and Markdown reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality engineers, SQE teams, customer-quality teams, procurement, finance, and legal reviewers use this skill to prepare standardized quality claim or supplier chargeback drafts with traceable calculations and evidence checklists. It supports customer complaint, incoming inspection, line-stop loss, batch return, and supplier quality deduction workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive supplier, contract, quality, and financial claim information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information appropriate for the operating environment and avoid entering data that is not approved for the agent workspace. <br>
Risk: Draft claim letters, chargeback amounts, and responsibility assessments could be incomplete or incorrect if inputs are missing or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Review all calculations, evidence gaps, and missing fields before use; keep enterprise-specific unknowns marked for completion instead of accepting guessed values. <br>
Risk: Actual claim, chargeback, or settlement action can have legal, procurement, and finance consequences. <br>
Mitigation: Require legal, procurement, and finance approval before sending claims or executing chargebacks. <br>
