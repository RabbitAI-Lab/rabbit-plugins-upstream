## Description: <br>
Helps agents plan, build, and optimize SMS and MMS marketing programs, including lifecycle flows, platform choices, campaign copy, measurement, and compliance checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, operators, and developers use this skill to design SMS and MMS programs for DTC, SaaS, mobile app, and services businesses. It helps them check consent and platform readiness, choose sequences, draft message copy, select platforms, and plan measurement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: SMS campaign guidance or templates could be used without valid recipient consent or jurisdiction-specific review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as marketing and compliance guidance, not legal advice; confirm lawful consent, required disclosures, consent records, and jurisdiction-specific rules before sending. <br>
Risk: Messages could be sent before STOP/HELP handling, quiet hours, behavioral tracking permissions, or A2P 10DLC/platform registration are complete. <br>
Mitigation: Verify STOP/HELP handling, recipient-local quiet hours, lawful tracking, and all carrier or platform registration requirements before launch. <br>
Risk: Platform pricing, throughput, and feature details may change after publication. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm current vendor pricing, limits, and capabilities before committing to a platform or campaign budget. <br>
