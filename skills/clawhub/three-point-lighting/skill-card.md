## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may apply educational lighting guidance with real studio equipment that can create electrical, heat, cable, or stand-safety hazards. <br>
Mitigation: Follow the security guidance: avoid overloaded circuits, use correctly rated fixtures and modifiers, secure stands and cables, keep hot lights away from flammable materials, and let lamps cool before handling. <br>
Risk: The skill is not an equipment safety manual and may not cover every modern lighting system or studio constraint. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as photographic setup guidance, confirm equipment-specific limits against manufacturer documentation, and apply normal studio safety practices before using physical lights. <br>
