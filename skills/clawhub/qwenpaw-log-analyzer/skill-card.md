## Description:

Analyzes QwenPaw logs for errors, warnings, and unusual patterns and suggests troubleshooting steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[trend0522](https://clawhub.ai/user/trend0522)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and operators use this skill to inspect QwenPaw logs when the app crashes, plugins fail to load, performance degrades, or a security event needs investigation. It summarizes issues and suggests fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: QwenPaw logs and state files may contain local diagnostic or security-sensitive details.

Mitigation: Inspect and share only the QwenPaw files needed for troubleshooting, and review reports before sharing them.
