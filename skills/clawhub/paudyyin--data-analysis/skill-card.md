## Description: <br>
Data Analysis helps agents analyze, visualize, and explain data from SQL, spreadsheets, notebooks, dashboards, exports, and ad hoc tables. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, and business users use this skill to frame data questions, define metrics, choose analysis methods and charts, and turn findings into decision-ready reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent may work with sensitive datasets or propose database and spreadsheet changes. <br>
Mitigation: Review sensitive data handling and any database or spreadsheet modifications before allowing the agent to act on them. <br>
Risk: Ambiguous metrics, small samples, confounders, or weak source data can produce misleading conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Require metric definitions, uncertainty, caveats, and source-quality checks before relying on recommendations. <br>
