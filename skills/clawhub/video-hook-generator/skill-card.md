## Description: <br>
Generates short-video opening hooks from a topic or script, covering conflict, numeric, suspense, emotion, reveal, pain-point, benefit, and result-oriented hook styles. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to generate high-click opening hooks and optional first-three-second script suggestions for short videos on Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may invoke the skill during general writing requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review or narrow trigger wording if the skill should only run for explicit video title or opening-hook generation requests. <br>
Risk: Generated hooks may overstate outcomes or encourage promotional claims that need context-specific review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated hooks before publication for accuracy, platform policy compliance, and suitability for the intended audience. <br>
