## Description: <br>
Produces structured, decision-useful market briefs for crypto, equities, FX, sectors, or macro topics with drivers, scenarios, uncertainty, and a practical read. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[oonyl](https://clawhub.ai/user/oonyl) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to ask an agent for a concise market view on an asset, sector, protocol, or macro question. The skill structures the response around the call, current state, drivers, scenarios, key uncertainty, and practical next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Market briefs may include speculative price targets, conviction labels, position sizing, or entry and exit suggestions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as general market commentary, not personalized financial advice. <br>
Risk: Market data, news, and on-chain or fundamental signals can become stale or be misread by the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current market data and load-bearing sources independently before acting. <br>
