## Description:

Beatra Universal helps agents create and manage images, video, music, and speech, and retrieve public social media data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and teams use the skill to produce and manage media, prepare narration and music, and retrieve public social data for research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan marks the release suspicious due to persistent installation telemetry and behavior beyond its described media and public social-data purpose; the scan was partial and did not establish clear malicious intent.

Mitigation: Review the telemetry disclosures and security guidance before use, especially for sensitive work.

Risk: The guidance notes that local media or text may be sent to Beatra without clear transmission warnings.

Mitigation: Review what local media or text a task will send before submitting sensitive material.

Risk: The guidance notes an unpinned manual update command and a weakness in the uninstall inventory.

Mitigation: Review the update source and verify that any needed connection cleanup is complete after uninstalling.
