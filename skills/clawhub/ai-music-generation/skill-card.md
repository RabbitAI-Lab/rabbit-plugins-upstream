## Use Case: <br>
External creators, developers, and content teams use this skill to ask an agent for inference.sh CLI commands that generate instrumentals, songs with vocals, jingles, and soundtracks from text prompts or lyrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill relies on a third-party inference.sh CLI installer and remote music-generation service. <br>
Mitigation: Review the installer or use the checksum-verified manual install path before running it. <br>
Risk: Prompts, lyrics, unreleased creative work, or proprietary briefs may be sent to a third-party provider with account or billing implications. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive or private creative material unless the user accepts the provider's handling and any related costs. <br>
