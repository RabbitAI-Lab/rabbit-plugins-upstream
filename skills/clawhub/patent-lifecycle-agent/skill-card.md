## Description:

Patent Lifecycle Agent supports patent professionals across disclosure intake, portfolio planning, prior-art search, drafting assistance, office-action response, maintenance review, infringement monitoring, and patent drawing generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Patent agents, IP teams, and invention reviewers use this skill to structure patent lifecycle work, including novelty and FTO search, technical disclosure drafting, office-action planning, patent maintenance review, infringement-oriented analysis, and patent drawing generation. The skill is especially oriented toward China-focused patent workflows unless the user specifies another target jurisdiction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent workflows may involve confidential invention disclosures and client-sensitive legal strategy.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with external patent search, web search, and Patsnap/Zhihuiya services that are approved for the relevant account, agreements, and confidentiality obligations.

Risk: Generated patent analysis, legal-deadline reminders, office-action strategy, and infringement-oriented assessments may be incomplete or unsuitable for filing decisions.

Mitigation: Require review by a qualified patent professional or lawyer, and verify legal deadlines, authorities, and final filing content against official sources.

Risk: Generated PNG and DXF patent drawings are engineering-style drafts and may not satisfy jurisdiction-specific patent office drawing rules.

Mitigation: Have a professional patent draftsperson or responsible patent practitioner refine and approve drawings before submission.
