## Description:

Guides sales and customer-success teams through recurring QBR/EBR value reviews with customer decision-makers, including goal alignment, evidence-based progress scoring, next-quarter planning, and renewal, expansion, or referral handoff triggers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer-success, sales, and account teams use this skill after onboarding or first value proof to prepare, run, and follow up on recurring business reviews for China-market B2B customers. It helps structure customer-facing review materials, red/yellow/green progress evidence, joint action plans, and downstream renewal, expansion, or referral handoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer screenshots, complaint tickets, or account notes may contain personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Redact unnecessary personal and confidential details before using them in review artifacts.

Risk: Internal prep notes, risk language, competitive intelligence, or landmines could leak into customer-facing materials.

Mitigation: Keep internal briefs physically separate from customer materials and run a human leakage check before external sharing.

Risk: Unsupported or overstated value claims could mislead customer decision-makers.

Mitigation: Require source, as-of date, baseline, current value, confidence, and attribution for each claim; label estimates clearly and do not present them as confirmed.
