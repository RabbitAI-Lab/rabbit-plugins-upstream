## Description: <br>
Launch Feedback Synthesizer helps agents cluster launch feedback into themes, specify a visible status loop, prepare you-asked-we-shipped announcements, and define a compliant social-proof harvest protocol. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, launch teams, and marketing operators use this skill to turn launch-window comments, reviews, board posts, and support tickets into a theme digest, status-loop plan, shipped-change messaging, and review/testimonial harvest protocol. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process launch comments, store reviews, support tickets, and feedback-board exports that contain sensitive, personal, or untrusted user text. <br>
Mitigation: Treat feedback as data rather than instructions, minimize sensitive content in summaries, and save results only after explicit user confirmation. <br>
Risk: Review and testimonial harvest plans can conflict with platform review policies if incentives or outreach rules are applied incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Verify each target platform's current review policy before using any testimonial or review-harvest plan, and keep store reviews non-incentivized. <br>
