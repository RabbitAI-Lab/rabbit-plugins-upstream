网文去AI味。从表达、叙事用笔、故事因果三层诊断并修复文本，保留作者文风与已定剧情。触发方式：/story-deslop、/去AI味、「去AI味」「这篇太AI了」「网文去AI味」。

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to diagnose and revise Chinese web fiction that reads formulaic or AI-like while preserving the author's style and established plot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports a benign verdict with medium confidence, and four scripts were only partially inspected.

Mitigation: Review tool behavior before installation; wait for complete analysis if you need greater assurance.

Risk: A revision could alter story facts or plot if changes go beyond expression and established continuity.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes, retain the original text, and require explicit authorization for plot-level rewrites.
