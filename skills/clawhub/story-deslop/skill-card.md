## Description:

Reviews and revises Chinese web fiction across expression, narrative focus, and story logic while preserving the author's style and established plot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chinese web-fiction authors and editors use this skill to diagnose formulaic prose and revise manuscripts within their requested scope. It can provide a detection-only report or edit a specified manuscript while preserving established story details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A general editing request can change the specified manuscript.

Mitigation: Specify the exact file or passage, request detection-only mode if edits are unwanted, and review the backup before relying on the revision.

Risk: Revisions can unintentionally change a character's motivation or established plot.

Mitigation: Compare the revision with the original and authorize story-level changes separately.
