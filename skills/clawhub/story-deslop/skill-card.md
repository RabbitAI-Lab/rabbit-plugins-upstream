## Description:

从表达、叙事用笔和故事因果三层诊断并修复网文文本，同时保留作者文风与已定剧情。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese web-fiction authors and editors use this skill to diagnose and revise AI-sounding prose while preserving the author's voice and established plot. It can provide diagnostics only or revise the requested text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
