## Description:

Reviews Chinese web-fiction prose for formulaic AI-writing patterns and, when requested, revises it to sound more natural while preserving the story.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese web-fiction authors and editors use this skill to identify formulaic phrasing and revise chapters or pasted passages while respecting the author's established style. It can also provide a detection-only report without changing the prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Editing may overwrite or undesirably change manuscript prose.

Mitigation: Review in detection-only mode first and keep backups or version control before in-place edits.

Risk: Optional author memory stores long-term writing preferences locally.

Mitigation: Enable author memory only when the author wants those preferences retained.
