## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cached medical or first-aid advice may be unsuitable for an emergency or an individual's circumstances.

Mitigation: For emergencies, seek immediate local assistance; verify medical advice with current official sources and qualified local professionals.

Risk: China-oriented legal, financial, tax, benefits, or immigration advice can become outdated or inapplicable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Check current official rules for the relevant jurisdiction and consult qualified local professionals before consequential decisions.
