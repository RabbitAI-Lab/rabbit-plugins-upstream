## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking cultural reflection or entertainment can cast a six-line hexagram with coins or a local simulation and receive a structured interpretation drawing on the I Ching, Confucianism, and Taoism.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Divination may be mistaken for reliable medical, financial, legal, or life-planning advice.

Mitigation: Use readings for reflection or entertainment only; seek qualified guidance for consequential decisions.

Risk: Virtual casting runs a local script, while manually running the evolution script appends to a local log.

Mitigation: Review local execution before virtual casting and run the evolution script only when local log changes are acceptable.
