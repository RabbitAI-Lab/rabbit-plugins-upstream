This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People interested in the I Ching can ask an agent to cast or interpret a Liuyao hexagram and explore its traditional symbolism and philosophical context for learning or reflection, not professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Divination readings may be mistaken for medical, financial, legal, or major life decision advice.

Mitigation: Frame readings as cultural or reflective content and seek qualified professional advice for consequential decisions.

Risk: Manually running the optional maintenance script may append notes to the skill's logs.

Mitigation: Run the script only when those local log updates are intended.
