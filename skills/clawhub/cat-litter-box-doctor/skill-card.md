Use when a cat pees outside the litter box, refuses the box, or fights housemates over it — veterinary-rule-based diagnosis ladder (medical first), box-count formula cats+1, litter-type transition protocol, location rules, and soiled-area decontamination with the enzyme-vs-ammonia trap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Owners may mistake general pet-care guidance for a veterinary diagnosis or delay care for a medical problem.

Mitigation: Treat results as general guidance, follow the medical-first and emergency referral instructions, and consult a veterinarian about symptoms.
