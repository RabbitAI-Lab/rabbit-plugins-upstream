## Description:

Build presentation decks with Canvora — pitch decks, sales decks, training and webinar slides, or a whole PDF/DOCX report turned into slides.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[canvora](https://clawhub.ai/user/canvora)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating investor, sales, training, webinar, or general presentation decks can use the skill to turn a brief, URL, or document into audience-focused slides. It guides slide format, narrative, branding, cost communication, and delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documents and text submitted for deck generation are processed by Canvora's cloud service.

Mitigation: Submit only material the user is authorized to share with Canvora, and review Canvora's privacy terms before sending confidential content.

Risk: Deck generation spends Canvora credits, with carousel formats priced per slide.

Mitigation: State the planned slide count and its credit cost in the same message as the generation request.
