## Description:

Assembles WBFlow shot videos into a final MP4 with dialogue subtitles, consistent color, and normalized audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junci003](https://clawhub.ai/user/junci003)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and video production teams use this final WBFlow pipeline stage to join ordered shot clips, align dialogue and narration subtitles, and produce a finished video. It requires ffmpeg, the expected project inputs, and the WBFlow controller and companion skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An existing final.mp4 may be overwritten during assembly.

Mitigation: Back up or rename any existing final.mp4 before running the assembly workflow.
