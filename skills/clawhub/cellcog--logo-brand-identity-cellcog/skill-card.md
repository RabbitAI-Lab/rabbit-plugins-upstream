## Description:

AI logo and brand identity design powered by CellCog for producing brand kits, logos, palettes, typography, guidelines, and related visual assets from a brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and external users use this skill to ask CellCog to create complete brand identity packages, including logos, color systems, typography recommendations, brand guidelines, and related marketing assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand briefs and generated asset requests may include sensitive brand or business material and are sent to CellCog through its package/API.

Mitigation: Review CellCog's privacy and billing terms before use, limit unnecessary sensitive detail in prompts, and use an approved CELLCOG_API_KEY.

Risk: The skill relies on an external package/API and requires a configured CELLCOG_API_KEY.

Mitigation: Install the expected CellCog package through approved channels, manage the API key as a secret, and review generated brand assets before publication.
