## Description:

AI logo and brand identity design powered by CellCog for creating brand kits, color palettes, typography, brand guidelines, logo designs, visual identity assets, social media assets, web design, and video from a single brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and brand teams use this skill to ask CellCog for complete logo and brand identity work, including logo variations, color palettes, typography recommendations, social templates, and brand guidelines.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CellCog setup commands and packages may change over time.

Mitigation: Verify the CellCog package and skill source before installation, and pin versions in controlled environments.

Risk: Prompts and branding inputs are handled by CellCog.

Mitigation: Use a scoped CELLCOG_API_KEY and avoid sending sensitive brand material unless approved for that service.
