## Description:

Measure what AI search really sends the business (visits, signups, revenue) with free tools. Use for "how much traffic do we get from ChatGPT", "track AI referrals in GA4" or "report on our AEO and GEO work". Sets up GA4, Search Console, Bing AI Performance and log tracking, accounts for dark AI traffic, and produces a monthly report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing analysts, SEO teams, and business owners use this skill to set up measurement of AI search visits, conversions, revenue, impressions, citations, and crawler fetches. It guides a monthly report that states attribution limits and avoids treating correlation as proof of causation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Monthly reports may include customer conversation excerpts and expose private information.

Mitigation: Use anonymized paraphrases by default; include direct quotes only after privacy review and any required authorization.

Risk: The security scan was partial because it could not resolve a local reference, so its evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and complete the security scan before relying on the assessment.
