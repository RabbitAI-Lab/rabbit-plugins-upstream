## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to log meals, check general nutrition patterns, plan meals, and get healthy food suggestions while keeping meal data local. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Meal logs and notes about how a user feels can contain private health-adjacent information. <br>
Mitigation: Keep logs local and avoid entering highly sensitive medical or eating-disorder details unless the user is comfortable retaining them. <br>
Risk: Nutrition suggestions may be mistaken for professional medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat suggestions as general wellness support and consult a qualified professional for medical conditions or specialized dietary needs. <br>
