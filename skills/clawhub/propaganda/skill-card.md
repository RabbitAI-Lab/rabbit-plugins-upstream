## Description: <br>
Detects and analyzes propaganda, influence operations, and narrative manipulation in news articles, social media posts, and media narratives. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[0xdeadcafe](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xdeadcafe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, analysts, and researchers use this skill to deconstruct provided articles, posts, transcripts, headlines, press releases, or official statements for framing, omissions, influence techniques, and evidence-to-claim alignment. It is intended for structured critical analysis, not external fact verification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the structured assessment for factual verification. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as critical analysis of the provided text and independently verify factual claims with reliable sources when truth or current facts matter. <br>
Risk: Source text may include personal information, identifiers, or operationally sensitive details. <br>
Mitigation: Redact personal information, addresses, identifiers, and operational target specifics from outputs. <br>
