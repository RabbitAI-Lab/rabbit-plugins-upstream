## Description:

Provides China-focused insurance guidance, product comparisons, needs analysis, premium estimates, and plan design for individuals and families.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to understand insurance options, compare products, estimate premiums, identify coverage needs, and review plan proposals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates the skill suspicious with medium confidence, citing a risk that plan output could incorrectly claim coverage for an illness excluded by a product; the product-data scan is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the coverage-claim issue and complete the product-data scan before installation; verify coverage against the insurer's current policy terms before relying on a recommendation.
