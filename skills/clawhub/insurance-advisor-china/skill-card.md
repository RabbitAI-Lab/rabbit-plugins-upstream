## Use Case: <br>
External users in mainland China use this skill to compare insurance products, estimate premiums, assess coverage gaps, design family protection plans, and get general underwriting, compliance, and claims guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Mainland China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for personal financial, family, policy, and possibly health information. <br>
Mitigation: Collect only the information needed for the planning task and avoid sharing sensitive details outside the agent session. <br>
Risk: Product availability, premiums, policy terms, and regulatory requirements can change after the packaged data is published. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current product details with official insurer materials or licensed professionals before buying or recommending a policy. <br>
Risk: The skill may provide one named insurance sales contact when the user asks for purchase channels. <br>
Mitigation: Present the contact only as an informational option and encourage users to compare multiple licensed sales channels independently. <br>
