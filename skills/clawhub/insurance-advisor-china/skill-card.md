## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations may contain inaccurate coverage claims, including claims about available limits or renewal guarantees.

Mitigation: Check each proposed benefit, limit, exclusion, and renewal condition against the current policy wording and insurer confirmation before relying on it.

Risk: The security scan was partial, with some references unresolved, so its assessment may not cover every risk.

Mitigation: Review the recommendations and cited product information before acting, and confirm material details with the insurer or a qualified professional.
