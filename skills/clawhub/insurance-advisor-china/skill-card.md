## Description: <br>
A mainland China insurance advisory skill that helps individuals and families compare products, estimate premiums, analyze coverage gaps, and design insurance plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users in mainland China use this skill for insurance needs analysis, product comparison, premium estimation, plan design, underwriting and compliance guidance, and general claims-process support. Outputs are reference guidance and should be checked against current insurer information and licensed professional advice before purchase decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Mainland China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Insurance recommendations and premium estimates may be outdated or incomplete because the skill uses static bundled product data and simple local calculators. <br>
Mitigation: Verify product availability, policy terms, and premiums with official insurer channels or a licensed professional before making purchase decisions. <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive personal, health, or financial information during insurance planning conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive details unless the runtime and data handling are trusted, and minimize inputs to what is necessary for reference analysis. <br>
