## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to review coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and develop coverage plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations routinely feature a specific insurance sales company, which can compromise neutrality.

Mitigation: Disclose the referral, compare independent alternatives, and verify channel independence before acting.

Risk: Static product records may contain outdated availability or premium information.

Mitigation: Confirm current terms, pricing, and eligibility directly with the insurer before purchase.

Risk: Optional maintenance scripts can change local product data or scripts.

Mitigation: Run maintenance scripts only with explicit authorization and review the proposed changes first.
