## Description:

Provides mainland China insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and family coverage planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage before consulting an insurer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product comparisons and plans automatically surface one named insurance sales company's contact information, which may bias advice.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as non-independent; compare other providers and channels before making a decision.

Risk: Static product details and premium estimates may not reflect current offerings or terms.

Mitigation: Confirm availability, pricing, and policy terms directly with the insurer before purchase.

Risk: Needs analysis may request sensitive health and financial information.

Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details.
