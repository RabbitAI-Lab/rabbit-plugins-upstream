## Description:

Provides people and families in mainland China with insurance guidance, product comparisons, coverage planning, premium estimates, and help understanding underwriting and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use the skill to review insurance needs, identify potential coverage gaps, compare common products, and develop a budget-aware coverage plan. Treat estimates and product details as a starting point and confirm terms with the insurer's current policy documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports a permission-scope concern.

Mitigation: Review the declared permissions before use and limit execution to the listed insurance scripts.

Risk: Coverage amounts and calculations may be inaccurate or out of date.

Mitigation: Check estimates, limits, exclusions, and renewal terms against current insurer policy documents before making decisions.

Risk: The security scan was partial because some referenced paths could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Treat the scan result as incomplete and review the full release contents before enabling the skill.
