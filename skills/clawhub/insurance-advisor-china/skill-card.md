## Description:

Provides insurance consultation for individuals and families in mainland China, including coverage needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and plan design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use this skill to understand insurance needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and explore coverage plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Advice about renewal periods may vary with the order of renewal notes.

Mitigation: Check renewal timing and important coverage details against the insurer's current policy documents before relying on the advice.
