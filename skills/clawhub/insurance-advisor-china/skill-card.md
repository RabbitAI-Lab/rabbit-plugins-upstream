## Description:

Provides insurance guidance for individuals and families in mainland China, including needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China use the skill to assess household coverage needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and explore policy options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance coverage or policy conditions may be misstated; the available security review is partial.

Mitigation: Verify treatment coverage and all relevant conditions against the current policy terms before relying on recommendations.
