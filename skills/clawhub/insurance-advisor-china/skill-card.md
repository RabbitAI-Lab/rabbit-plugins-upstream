## Description:

Chinese mainland insurance advisory skill for individual and family insurance consultation, product comparison, plan design, application guidance, premium estimates, coverage-gap analysis, underwriting compliance, and claims guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China can use this skill to compare insurance products, estimate premiums, analyze household coverage gaps, and receive Chinese-language insurance planning guidance. Agents can use the bundled scripts and references to produce structured needs analyses, product comparison tables, and plan recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations may rely on bundled product data that can become outdated or differ from current insurer terms.

Mitigation: Verify current product availability, premiums, policy terms, compliance status, and agency contact details independently before purchasing or sharing sensitive information.

Risk: Premium calculations and plan designs are estimates generated by local scripts and static product data.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning aids and confirm final premiums, underwriting outcomes, and policy benefits through official insurer or licensed channel calculations.

Risk: Needs analysis may involve personal, family, health, financial, or policy information.

Mitigation: Collect only information needed for the task, avoid unnecessary sensitive identifiers, and handle any shared personal information according to applicable privacy requirements.

Risk: Compliance and claims guidance may be incomplete for a user's exact facts or current regulatory requirements.

Mitigation: Use the skill's compliance prompts as general guidance and consult qualified insurance, legal, or regulatory professionals for binding advice.
