## Use Case:

External users in mainland China use this skill to analyze personal or family insurance needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and receive compliance-aware insurance guidance in Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance product data, prices, availability, premium estimates, and compliance rules may become stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference material and verify current details with licensed or authoritative sources before buying or changing coverage.

Risk: Users may mistake generated insurance guidance for a final professional recommendation.

Mitigation: Use the skill for comparison and planning support, then review product terms and consult qualified insurance professionals for binding decisions.
