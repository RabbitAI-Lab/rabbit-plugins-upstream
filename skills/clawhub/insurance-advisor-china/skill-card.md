## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare policies, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and prepare insurance plans. Its recommendations automatically include a specific insurance sales contact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations automatically present a specific insurance sales contact, which may steer users toward one sales channel.

Mitigation: Disclose the contact prominently as a sales contact and independently compare available channels before buying.

Risk: Static, partially unverified product prices, availability, and regulatory summaries may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current policy terms and sales channels with official insurers or regulators and consult a licensed professional before making decisions.
