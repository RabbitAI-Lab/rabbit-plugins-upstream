## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, plan coverage, and understand underwriting and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance products and premiums, and prepare questions about applications, underwriting, and claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations promote one specific insurance sales company and its phone number, which can undermine neutral advice.

Mitigation: Disclose the sales-channel preference and independently compare products and channels before acting.

Risk: Insurance product details, premiums, and sales eligibility may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current terms, rates, and licensing with insurers or authorized sources before purchase.

Risk: Bundled maintenance scripts can alter local product data or scripts when run manually.

Mitigation: Do not run maintenance scripts unless you intend those local changes and have reviewed their effects.
