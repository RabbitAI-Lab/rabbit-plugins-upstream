## Description:

Provides insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning for individuals and families in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage. It provides insurance explanations and practical application guidance based on the user's circumstances and the supplied product information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scan found a potential for critical-illness coverage to be overstated, and the scan evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Before relying on a recommendation, check that the stated coverage amount matches the final plan and the user's budget.
