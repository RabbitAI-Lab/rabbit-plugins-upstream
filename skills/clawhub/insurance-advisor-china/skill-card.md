## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill for insurance needs analysis, product comparison, premium estimates, coverage planning, and claims guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product recommendations and plans include a specific insurance sales company's phone number, framed as neutral information.

Mitigation: Treat the contact as a commercial referral; compare independent sales channels before buying.

Risk: Insurance products and premiums may change after the local reference data was prepared.

Mitigation: Confirm product terms, pricing, and eligibility directly with insurers before making a purchase.

Risk: Manually run maintenance tools can rewrite local product data or runtime scripts.

Mitigation: Do not run maintenance tools unless those local changes are intended and authorized.
