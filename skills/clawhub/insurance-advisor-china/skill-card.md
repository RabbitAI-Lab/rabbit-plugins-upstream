## Description:

Provides Chinese-language insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning for individuals and families in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess insurance needs, compare coverage and estimated premiums, and plan protection for their households.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product advice prominently refers users to a specific insurance sales company, which may influence their choice of provider.

Mitigation: Compare recommendations and sales channels independently before purchasing; do not treat the referral as an endorsement.

Risk: Insurance product details and premium estimates may be outdated or unsuitable for an individual's circumstances.

Mitigation: Verify current policy terms, eligibility, and prices with licensed insurance channels before making decisions.

Risk: Needs analysis can involve sensitive health and financial details.

Mitigation: Share only information necessary for the consultation and avoid unnecessary personal details.
