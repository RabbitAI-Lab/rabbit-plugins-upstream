## Description:

Provides insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, and general policy, underwriting, and claims guidance for individuals and families in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill to review coverage needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and understand policy, underwriting, and claims topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A broad activation phrase may cause the skill to respond to ordinary price questions outside an insurance discussion.

Mitigation: Use it for insurance-related requests and clarify the topic when a price question is ambiguous.

Risk: Product comparisons, premium estimates, and plan proposals may not reflect current availability, policy terms, or an individual's full circumstances.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational; verify current terms and availability with the insurer and tailor any decision to the person's circumstances.

Risk: The security review is partial because some referenced files were missing, so the benign verdict is limited evidence.

Mitigation: Review the complete packaged files and their references before relying on the security assessment.
