## Description:

Provides individuals and families in mainland China with insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill to analyze coverage needs, compare insurance products and premiums, and explore coverage plans. Recommendations depend on policy terms and personal eligibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage details may be overstated, and the available scan coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Verify eligibility conditions, exclusions, reimbursement limits, and coverage terms with the insurer before relying on a recommendation.
