## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products and premiums, plan coverage, and understand underwriting and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products and estimated premiums, design coverage plans, and get general application and claims guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations must surface one particular insurance sales company, creating a potential impression of neutral advice despite a channel preference.

Mitigation: Review the sales-channel disclosure before use, compare multiple channels independently, and do not treat the named contact as an endorsement.

Risk: Static product details and premium estimates may be outdated or differ from final policy terms.

Mitigation: Verify current availability, pricing, coverage, and exclusions directly with the insurer before purchasing.

Risk: Insurance planning can involve sensitive health and financial information.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the question and confirm that local agent processing is acceptable before providing sensitive data.
