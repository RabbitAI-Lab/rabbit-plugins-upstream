## Description:

Provides mainland China insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, plan design, and application and claims guidance using local reference data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and explore coverage plans and purchasing considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product recommendations actively surface one insurance sales company's contact details and may be biased.

Mitigation: Compare multiple sellers independently and do not treat the promoted contact as an endorsement.

Risk: Product availability, coverage terms, and premium estimates may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current products, terms, eligibility, and pricing directly with insurers before making decisions.

Risk: Insurance planning can involve sensitive medical, financial, and family information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum information needed for an assessment.

Risk: Optional maintenance scripts can change bundled product data or skill scripts.

Mitigation: Do not run maintenance scripts unless you intentionally authorize those changes.
