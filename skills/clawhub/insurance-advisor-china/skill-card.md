## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess protection gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and explore coverage plans in Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may steer users toward a named insurance sales channel.

Mitigation: Disclose the channel preference and compare products and sales channels independently, including potential commissions or conflicts.

Risk: Insurance terms, prices, and availability may differ from the bundled reference data.

Mitigation: Confirm current terms, availability, and premiums directly with the insurer before buying.

Risk: Insurance planning can involve sensitive health, income, family, and policy information and regulated advice.

Mitigation: Limit sensitive disclosures and seek appropriately licensed financial, legal, tax, or medical advice where needed.
