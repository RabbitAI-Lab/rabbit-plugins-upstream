## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare policies, estimate premiums, and understand coverage and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China can use the skill to explore protection gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and learn about underwriting and claims. Its recommendations and estimates are preliminary, not a substitute for current insurer terms or professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may steer users toward one named insurance sales company.

Mitigation: Disclose the sales contact and compare alternatives independently before choosing a channel.

Risk: Stored product information and estimated premiums may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Confirm current availability, policy terms, eligibility, and quoted premiums through official insurer channels.

Risk: Personalized insurance discussions may involve sensitive health and income information.

Mitigation: Share only necessary details after considering how the host agent handles conversation data.
