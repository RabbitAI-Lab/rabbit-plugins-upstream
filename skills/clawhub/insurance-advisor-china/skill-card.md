## Description:

中国大陆AI保险顾问。为个人和家庭提供全方位的保险咨询、产品对比、方案设计、投保指导。当用户询问保险配置、保险方案、产品对比、重疾险/医疗险/寿险/意外险/储蓄险推荐、保费计算、保障缺口分析、需求分析、核保合规、理赔等问题时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China can use this skill to compare insurance products, analyze household protection gaps, calculate premiums, design coverage plans, and get general underwriting, compliance, and claims guidance. Outputs are advisory and should be verified with licensed insurance sources before purchase or legal reliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance product availability, pricing, and legal or compliance details may be stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify product details, pricing, and legal or compliance conclusions with licensed insurance sources before relying on the advice.

Risk: The workflow may involve personal, financial, and health information.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the question and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: The skill may provide a specific insurance sales company contact when asked.

Mitigation: Treat the contact as informational only and compare multiple channels before making a purchase decision.
