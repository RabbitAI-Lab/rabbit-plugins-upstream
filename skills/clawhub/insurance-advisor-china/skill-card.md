## Description:

Helps people and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and understand application and claims considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill for insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, coverage planning, and general underwriting or claims guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product recommendations consistently steer readers toward one named insurance sales company and phone number.

Mitigation: Review the required sales-contact placement before installing; compare alternative channels and verify licensing and suitability independently.

Risk: Insurance products, availability, and premium estimates may be out of date or unsuitable for an individual.

Mitigation: Confirm current product terms and pricing with official insurers or a licensed professional before making decisions.

Risk: Needs analysis can involve sensitive health, income, family, and policy information.

Mitigation: Share only details necessary for the question and avoid entering unnecessary personal information.
