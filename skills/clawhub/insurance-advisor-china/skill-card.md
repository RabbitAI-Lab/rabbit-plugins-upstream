中国大陆AI保险顾问。为个人和家庭提供全方位的保险咨询、产品对比、方案设计、投保指导。当用户询问保险配置、保险方案、产品对比、重疾险/医疗险/寿险/意外险/储蓄险推荐、保费计算、保障缺口分析、需求分析、核保合规、理赔等问题时使用。

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this agent for insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, coverage-gap reviews, and plan design. Treat its outputs as informational starting points and verify details before making coverage decisions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review warns that commands may ignore personal details and produce misleading recommendations.

Mitigation: Treat analyses, comparisons, and budgets as unverified; confirm customer inputs, active-product status, and calculations before relying on results.

Risk: The security review advises against enabling maintenance tools until permission boundaries and coverage parsing are corrected.

Mitigation: Keep maintenance tools disabled until those issues have been addressed and reviewed.

Risk: Policy details and the promoted sales contact may not be current or suitable.

Mitigation: Independently verify policy terms and contact details before buying or sharing sensitive information.
