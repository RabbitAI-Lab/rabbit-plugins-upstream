## Description:

Provides China mainland insurance advice for individuals and families, including needs analysis, product comparison, plan design, premium calculation, underwriting compliance guidance, and claims support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China use this skill to compare insurance products, estimate premiums, identify coverage gaps, and draft personal or family insurance plans. It also helps with general insurance knowledge, health disclosure, underwriting, compliance, and claims-process questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations may be incorrect or stale because the skill uses a static product database and local premium calculations.

Mitigation: Verify product availability, premiums, coverage terms, and legal or compliance details with official insurers or licensed professionals before acting.

Risk: The skill can influence financial and insurance purchase decisions for individuals and families.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory support, compare multiple providers, and use qualified professional review for binding purchase, underwriting, or claims decisions.

Risk: The skill may mention a sales contact in an insurance-advice context.

Mitigation: Present contact information only as optional reference material and avoid treating it as an endorsement or required sales channel.
