## Use Case:

External users in mainland China use this skill to discuss family and personal insurance needs, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, design protection plans, understand underwriting and compliance considerations, and get general claims-process guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill processes sensitive personal, family, health, and financial details supplied by users.

Mitigation: Collect only the minimum information needed for the insurance task and avoid entering unnecessary identifying or medical details.

Risk: Bundled insurance product, premium, and regulatory reference data may become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify products, premiums, coverage terms, and regulatory claims with licensed professionals or current insurer sources before acting.

Risk: Insurance guidance can be mistaken for legal, financial, or licensed insurance advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational planning support and require qualified human review before purchase, underwriting, claims, or compliance decisions.
