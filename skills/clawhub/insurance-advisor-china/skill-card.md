## Description:

Provides insurance education, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning for individuals and families in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the agent to understand insurance, analyze coverage needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and review plan options. Users should confirm coverage and current product terms with insurer documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage explanations or product comparisons may contain inaccurate or incomplete details that affect purchasing decisions.

Mitigation: Verify coverage, exclusions, renewal terms, and current availability against the insurer's policy documents before making a purchase decision.
