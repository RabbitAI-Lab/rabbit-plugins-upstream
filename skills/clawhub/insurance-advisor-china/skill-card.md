## Description:

Provides mainland China insurance guidance for individuals and families, including needs analysis, product comparison, plan design, premium estimates, underwriting and compliance reminders, and claims guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China use this skill to explore personal and family insurance needs, compare bundled product data, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and receive general compliance and claims process guidance. Outputs are informational planning aids and should be verified with licensed professionals and current insurer materials before purchase decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat insurance planning output as licensed financial, legal, or medical advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as an informational planning aid and tell users to verify decisions with licensed professionals, insurers, and current policy documents.

Risk: Product availability, premiums, policy terms, and sales contacts can change after the bundled data is published.

Mitigation: Require users to independently confirm product availability, premiums, terms, and contact details before applying or purchasing.

Risk: Needs analysis may prompt users to share sensitive health, family, or financial details.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum information needed for the conversation and avoid storing or requesting unnecessary personal detail.
