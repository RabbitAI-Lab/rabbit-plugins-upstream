## Description:

Provides insurance education, product comparisons, premium estimates, coverage gap analysis, and plan guidance for people and families in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and families in mainland China use the skill to understand insurance, compare products, estimate premiums, review coverage needs, and get general application or claims guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage logic may produce misleading insurance recommendations.

Mitigation: Check any suggested coverage and product terms against current insurer documentation and consult a qualified insurance professional before making a purchase decision.

Risk: Some local reference checks were incomplete, and product information can change.

Mitigation: Verify product availability, premiums, exclusions, and coverage details with the insurer before relying on comparisons or estimates.
