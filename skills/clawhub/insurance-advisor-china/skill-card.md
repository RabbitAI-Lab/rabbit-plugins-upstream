## Description:

A mainland China insurance advisor skill for personal and family insurance consultation, product comparison, plan design, premium calculation, coverage-gap analysis, underwriting guidance, and claims questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users in mainland China use this skill to get Chinese-language insurance guidance for individual and family protection planning, including needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, compliance reminders, and general claims-process support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for health, family, income, mortgage, budget, and existing-policy details.

Mitigation: Collect only the details needed for the specific question and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

Risk: Bundled product data and premium calculations may be outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations and estimates as reference material and verify current terms, availability, and prices with insurers or a licensed professional.

Risk: Insurance planning, underwriting, compliance, and claims guidance can affect financial decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill as decision support and review important decisions with a qualified insurance or legal professional.
