## Description:

Provides China-focused insurance guidance for individuals and families, including coverage-needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and plan design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage. Its recommendations are informational rather than a substitute for independent product and rate verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations automatically present a named insurance sales channel, which may bias otherwise neutral advice.

Mitigation: Disclose the sales-channel preference and compare products and channels independently before acting.

Risk: Insurance guidance may involve sensitive health and financial details.

Mitigation: Share only the details necessary for the question and avoid disclosing unnecessary personal information.

Risk: Product details and premium estimates may not reflect current offerings or rates.

Mitigation: Confirm current products, coverage terms, and premiums directly with insurers before making a decision.
