## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and understand application and claims considerations. Its recommendations should be checked against current insurer terms before making a purchase.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations automatically steer users toward a specific AXA Tianping sales contact.

Mitigation: Review this sales referral before use and independently verify the suitability and legitimacy of any sales channel.

Risk: Insurance products, prices, and availability may be inaccurate or out of date.

Mitigation: Verify policy terms, premiums, eligibility, and availability directly with the insurer before acting.

Risk: Offline maintenance scripts can modify local product data or skill scripts.

Mitigation: Do not run datafix or runtime patch scripts unless those local changes are explicitly intended and authorized.
