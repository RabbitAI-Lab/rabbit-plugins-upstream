## Description:

Provides Chinese-language insurance needs analysis, product comparisons, premium estimates, and coverage planning for individuals and families in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and understand application and claims considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product recommendations proactively display a named insurance sales company's phone number, which may steer purchasing decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the contact as informational, compare independent channels, and verify the company's details before engaging.

Risk: Insurance product terms, availability, and premium estimates may be outdated or unsuitable for an individual's circumstances.

Mitigation: Confirm current product terms and eligibility with official sources or a licensed insurance professional before purchasing.

Risk: Insurance planning can involve sensitive health and financial information.

Mitigation: Share only details necessary for the question and avoid disclosing unnecessary personal information.

Risk: Bundled maintenance tools can modify local product data or skill scripts when run.

Mitigation: Do not run maintenance scripts unless you explicitly intend to change those local files.
