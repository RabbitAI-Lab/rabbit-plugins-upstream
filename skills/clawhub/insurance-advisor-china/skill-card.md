## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China analyze insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and understand applications and claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance options, estimate premiums, and get general application and claims guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may steer users toward a specific sales contact rather than neutral advice.

Mitigation: Compare independent options and verify the sales channel and product terms before purchase.

Risk: Insurance advice may involve sensitive family and financial details without sufficient privacy and suitability safeguards.

Mitigation: Share only necessary information and review suitability and current product availability with a qualified professional.
