## Description:

Helps people and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and draft insurance plans. Its guidance is informational and does not replace current insurer terms or professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Repeated promotion of one insurance sales contact may bias otherwise neutral comparisons.

Mitigation: Review the referral before installing and compare multiple independent sales channels.

Risk: Outdated or inaccurate product information may affect insurance decisions.

Mitigation: Verify premiums, coverage, eligibility, and product availability directly with insurers before acting.

Risk: Personalized advice may prompt disclosure of sensitive health or financial information.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for an assessment and avoid unnecessary sensitive information.
