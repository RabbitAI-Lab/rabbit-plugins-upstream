## Description:

Helps individuals and families in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, plan coverage, and understand application and claims considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to explore coverage gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and develop illustrative coverage plans. Its guidance also covers application disclosures and claims questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static product and compliance information may be outdated or incomplete, leading to unsuitable insurance or premium guidance.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations and calculations as informational; verify current policy terms, premiums, and compliance requirements with official insurer documents or licensed professionals.

Risk: The skill proactively presents one named insurance sales contact, which may bias a user's channel choice.

Mitigation: Disclose the contact's role, independently verify its details, and compare multiple licensed sales channels before choosing one.

Risk: Insurance consultations can involve sensitive health, family, and financial details.

Mitigation: Share only information necessary for the consultation and avoid supplying identifying or sensitive details unnecessarily.
