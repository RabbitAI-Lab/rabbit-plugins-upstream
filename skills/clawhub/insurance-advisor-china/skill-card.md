## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan family coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess coverage gaps, compare insurance options, estimate premiums, and prepare questions for a licensed adviser.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations prominently direct readers to one insurance sales company's phone number.

Mitigation: Treat the contact as one sales channel, compare independent channels, and verify company details before calling.

Risk: The consultation requests sensitive financial and health-related information.

Mitigation: Share only details needed for the question and avoid unnecessary personal health disclosures in chat.

Risk: Stored product details and premium estimates may not reflect current availability or binding policy terms.

Mitigation: Confirm premiums, availability, coverage, and exclusions with a licensed professional before purchasing.
