Graph engineering is currently being used for two different things:

• a graph of work — agents, branches, reviewers, gates and merges
• a graph of knowledge — entities, typed relations, provenance and multi-hop paths

Those structures can complement each other, but they are not interchangeable.

A provenance note matters here. The recent viral “Graph Engineering” PDF has been repeatedly described as a new Karpathy or Anthropic publication. Its own cover says it was independently compiled and is not affiliated with or endorsed by either. The useful synthesis maps Karpathy's bounded autonomous-loop ideas onto Anthropic's actual Knowledge Graph Construction cookbook.

The attribution error is almost too perfect: a document about provenance lost its provenance in transit.

That conversation made the operational pieces we have been building feel timely. I am open-sourcing five Agent Skills today for the work-graph side:

• Agent Orchestra — isolated roles, parallel lanes, adversarial review and one integration owner
• Plus Ultra — two independent proposals, one arbiter, apply once, verify the resulting world fresh
• Cross Agent Sync — deterministic, source-linked hand-offs across agent harnesses
• Visual QA — rendered evidence and independent review for agent-built UIs
• Doppel — a consent-first digital twin of your own writing voice, with the human as final author

These do not implement a knowledge graph, and I have not tested them as an integration with Anthropic's cookbook or Karpathy's `autoresearch`. They are optional control pieces around agent work: topology, epistemics, continuity, perception and authorship.

Doppel takes the authorship boundary. It is a digital twin of writing voice, not a whole-person simulation. Real corpora remain local and out of Git. Sources must be subject-authored, consented and hashed. Generated text remains draft input until the subject ratifies the exact artefact.

## Reddit — r/AgentSkills

### Title

[Open Source] Five Agent Skills for the work graph around graph-grounded systems

### Body

“Graph engineering” is currently collapsing two different structures into one phrase:

1. A **work graph**: agents, branches, dependencies, reviewers, gates and integration.
2. A **knowledge graph**: entities, typed relations, provenance and multi-hop traversal.

They can complement each other, but they solve different problems.

A provenance correction is relevant. The recent viral “Graph Engineering” PDF has been repeatedly presented as a Karpathy or Anthropic publication. The document itself says it was independently compiled and is not affiliated with or endorsed by either. It synthesizes Karpathy's bounded-loop ideas with Anthropic's actual Knowledge Graph Construction cookbook.

The attribution failure is a useful demonstration of the problem: a document about durable provenance lost its own provenance while moving through social feeds.

I have open-sourced five Agent Skills for the **work-graph** side:

1. **Agent Orchestra** — isolated roles, parallel lanes, dependencies, negative lenses and one integration owner.
2. **Plus Ultra** — two independent proposals, one arbiter, a single application pass and a fresh reality verifier.
3. **Cross Agent Sync** — deterministic, source-linked hand-offs between agent harnesses without pretending they share one mind.
4. **Visual QA** — deterministic rendered states and independent visual review for outputs with a UI.
5. **Doppel** — a local, consent-first digital twin of the subject's writing voice, with the human retaining publication authority.

Important boundary: these projects do **not** implement a knowledge graph, and I have not tested them as an integration with Anthropic's cookbook or Karpathy's `autoresearch`. They address orchestration topology, verification, continuity, visual evidence and authorship around broader agent work.

Doppel's writing-voice safety contract is deliberately strict:

- actual corpora, manifests and context packs are ignored by Git;
- every source requires explicit subject authorship and a SHA-256 digest;
- changed bytes fail closed;
- third-party voice evidence is rejected;
- output is marked as draft input until the subject ratifies the exact artefact.

Reproduce its checks:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/AntreasAntoniou/doppel.git
cd doppel
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
python3 scripts/validate_package.py
```

The tests use neutral synthetic fixtures only. No real voice corpus is published or required.
