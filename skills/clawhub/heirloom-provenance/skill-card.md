## Description:

Helps families document heirloom histories, interview relatives, prepare provenance reports and labels, and plan fair distribution of family objects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Families, caregivers, and executors can record the stories and ownership of heirlooms, prepare reports and printable labels, and explore wish-list conflicts before distributing objects. It does not replace professional appraisal or estate administration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Family stories, names, estimated values, and inheritance preferences are stored in plaintext locally.

Mitigation: Keep the local registry private and backed up; do not place it in shared folders or public repositories.

Risk: Recorded values and draft allocations may be mistaken for definitive appraisals or estate decisions.

Mitigation: Use professional appraisals for insurance or estate valuations and review distribution proposals with the family and appropriate advisers.
