This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Board members, company secretaries, governance leads, and executives use this skill to turn agendas, notes, transcripts, or discussion summaries into formal board minutes with decisions, resolutions, actions, and review notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Board materials may include confidential, legal, financial, employment-sensitive, or governance-sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated minutes with the relevant governance or legal owner before circulation, and confirm or redact sensitive items before relying on the draft. <br>
Risk: Incomplete source notes can lead to unconfirmed attendees, quorum, votes, actions, or resolutions in the draft minutes. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unknown items marked as [to confirm] and verify formal decisions, resolutions, action owners, and due dates before approval. <br>
