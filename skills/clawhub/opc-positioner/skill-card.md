## Description:

Helps one-person company builders inventory capabilities, perform SWOT analysis, research market signals when browsing is available, and choose expert, tool, or platform positioning paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cglt2026opc](https://clawhub.ai/user/cglt2026opc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and entrepreneurs use this skill to define a specific one-person company positioning strategy from their skills, experience, market demand, and AI leverage. It produces a SWOT-based recommendation report with practical starting actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may collect personal career details and use web research to support positioning analysis.

Mitigation: Ask for confirmation before browsing, and avoid providing sensitive contacts, employer details, private financial information, or other unnecessary personal data.

Risk: The skill can save a local Markdown report containing career and positioning details.

Mitigation: Confirm the report filename and location before saving, especially in shared or synced workspaces.

Risk: Career-positioning recommendations and income ranges may influence business decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the report as advisory, verify market claims against current primary sources, and review assumptions before acting.
