## Description:

Profiles pasted CSV samples for headers, likely types, missing values, structural issues, and suggested cleaning steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hmahmadcs](https://clawhub.ai/user/hmahmadcs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and data analysts use this skill to inspect pasted CSV samples before choosing parsing options or writing data-cleaning transforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CSV samples may contain customer identifiers or other sensitive values.

Mitigation: Redact identifiers before pasting samples, especially email addresses, phone numbers, and tokens.
