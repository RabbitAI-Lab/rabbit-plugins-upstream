## Description:

Helps knowledge creators re-explain familiar concepts by agreeing on four core ideas, drafting a script, and refining it through the creator’s own spoken take.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo knowledge creators can use this skill to turn a familiar concept into a clear spoken explanation, then refine the draft using their own spoken version. It also supports editorial follow-up after publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can make persistent editorial updates in a content vault and create 24-hour and 7-day calendar reminders.

Mitigation: Choose the intended vault before starting, and request a draft-only run when saved edits or reminders are not wanted.

Risk: Related family documents and companion skills are not included in this release bundle, so their behavior was not assessed.

Mitigation: Review those materials separately before relying on their instructions or dependent workflows.
