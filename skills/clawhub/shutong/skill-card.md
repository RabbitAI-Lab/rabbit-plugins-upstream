## Description:

Shutong is a Chinese-language reading, study, and Obsidian knowledge-management assistant that acts only on clearly requested tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yamyeed](https://clawhub.ai/user/yamyeed)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking readers and learners use Shutong to create reading notes, study materials, quizzes, exam plans, and organize an Obsidian vault. It performs file, research, or reminder actions only when explicitly requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Files could be changed at an unintended Vault location, or a reminder could be created with incorrect settings.

Mitigation: Use only the user-authorized Vault and confirm exact file targets, reminder time zone, recurrence, content, and end condition before acting.

Risk: Private notes or drafts could be exposed in external research queries.

Mitigation: Keep private notes, unpublished research, client information, and full drafts out of queries; disclose concise query terms before searching on sensitive topics.
