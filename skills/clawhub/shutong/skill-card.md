## Description: <br>
AI 书童 assists with reading, study, exam preparation, writing, Obsidian note management, and information gathering. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yamyeed](https://clawhub.ai/user/yamyeed) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, researchers, and knowledge workers use this skill to manage an Obsidian study vault, create reading notes, plan reviews, practice interviews or exams, draft and polish writing, and find study resources. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Obsidian vault updates, file deletions, and bulk note changes could alter or remove study material. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm destructive or bulk changes before execution and keep backups or previews for important notes. <br>
Risk: Exports, scheduled reminders, and web searches may expose sensitive study topics or personal learning data. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm export destinations, reminder content, and sensitive searches before use. <br>
Risk: Generated study, exam, or writing guidance may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate for assessed work. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against authoritative sources and use the skill for learning support rather than cheating or misrepresentation. <br>
