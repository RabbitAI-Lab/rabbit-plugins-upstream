## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask the agent to read a named local corpus path for chapter lookup. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if the agent is allowed to read that local corpus path, and treat the path-dependent lookup behavior as optional. <br>
Risk: Trading material can be misunderstood as financial advice or treated as authoritative market guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as educational reference material and verify financial claims independently before making decisions. <br>
Risk: The release describes extracted book content, and distribution rights may require confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the publisher has rights to distribute the extracted book content before relying on or redistributing the skill. <br>
