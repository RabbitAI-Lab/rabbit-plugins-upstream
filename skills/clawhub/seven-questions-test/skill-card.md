## Description:

Guides founders through an evidence-backed, seven-question assessment of a business idea before major commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, investors, and business evaluators use this skill to assess whether to start, fund, join, or pivot a venture. It guides them to write evidence-backed answers to seven questions and identify gaps before committing resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the scores or verdict as a guarantee of business success.

Mitigation: Treat scores as a discussion framework, verify claims with evidence, and share only business details you are comfortable giving the agent.
