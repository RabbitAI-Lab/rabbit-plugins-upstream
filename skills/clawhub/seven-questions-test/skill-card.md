## Description: <br>
The Seven Questions Test helps agents guide users through a structured, evidence-backed go/no-go evaluation of startup ideas using the seven Zero to One questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, operators, investors, and startup advisors use this skill to test whether a business idea, pivot, product line, or funding decision has written evidence across technology, timing, market, team, distribution, durability, and secret insight. It produces a scored decision artifact before users commit significant time, capital, or reputation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the scorecard as guaranteed investment, legal, or financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present the framework as decision support and require human review before relying on its go/no-go guidance. <br>
Risk: Blunt startup guidance can overstate certainty when the user's evidence is incomplete or self-reported. <br>
Mitigation: Require written evidence for each question, score unanswered questions conservatively, and name the specific evidence that would improve weak scores. <br>
