## Description:

Guides AI agents in planning, drafting, auditing, and safely operating email-marketing campaigns and automations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cosmoblk](https://clawhub.ai/user/cosmoblk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Marketers and teams use this skill to audit email programs, design campaigns and automated flows, draft copy, diagnose deliverability, and review sends before approving them. It also provides guidance for related SMS, WhatsApp, and RCS messaging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A connected agent could send to the wrong audience or transmit unwanted messages.

Mitigation: Grant only necessary tool access, preview recipients and exclusions, and require explicit human approval before any send.

Risk: Outdated compliance or deliverability advice could lead to improper campaigns.

Mitigation: Verify current legal and platform requirements before using guidance for live campaigns.
