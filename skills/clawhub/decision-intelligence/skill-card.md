## Description:

把原始材料转化为带证据链、明确标注不确定性的可行动判断，并以九阶段流程支持多领域研判。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

分析师、研究人员和业务团队可用它评估财务、市场、合规、医疗、代码等材料，形成可追溯的研判报告，并显式标注证据质量、不确定性、风险与适用边界。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical, legal, or investment conclusions could be mistaken for qualified professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep conclusions within the skill's material-analysis scope and have a qualified professional review them.

Risk: Sensitive information in source materials could be exposed through external searches or reusable examples.

Mitigation: Limit analysis to selected documents and redact sensitive information before external searches or saving examples.
