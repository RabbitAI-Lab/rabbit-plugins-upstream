## Description:

Generates Chinese-language weekly care plans and health reminders for cats and dogs from owner-provided pet details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xndjfjcjcnxjx](https://clawhub.ai/user/xndjfjcjcnxjx)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners use this skill to plan a week of feeding for a cat or dog and receive vaccination, deworming, checkup, and common-risk reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General feeding and health reminders may not fit sick pets or unusual breeds.

Mitigation: Treat the plan as general guidance and consult a veterinarian for individualized care.

Risk: Vaccination requirements and schedules can depend on local law and an individual pet's records.

Mitigation: Confirm vaccination timing and legal requirements with a veterinarian and local authorities.
