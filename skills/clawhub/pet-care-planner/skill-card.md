## Description:

Generates Chinese-language weekly feeding plans and health reminders for cats and dogs from their age, breed, weight, diet, and health details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xndjfjcjcnxjx](https://clawhub.ai/user/xndjfjcjcnxjx)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners use this skill to plan a cat's or dog's weekly feeding routine and identify upcoming vaccination, deworming, and check-up reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General feeding estimates may not fit an individual pet's dietary or health needs.

Mitigation: Check food labels and confirm diet and portion decisions with a veterinarian.

Risk: Vaccination, deworming, and check-up timing may depend on prior records and local requirements.

Mitigation: Verify past care records and the next steps with a veterinarian before acting.
