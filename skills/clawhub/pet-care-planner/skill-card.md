## Description:

Creates a personalized, Chinese-language weekly care plan and health reminders for cats and dogs using the pet's breed, age, weight, and care history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xndjfjcjcnxjx](https://clawhub.ai/user/xndjfjcjcnxjx)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners use this skill to prepare a week of feeding and care for a cat or dog, with reminders for vaccinations, deworming, and checkups. It asks for missing details and does not replace veterinary advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic feeding amounts or vaccination, deworming, and checkup timing may not fit an individual pet, especially one that is young, senior, sick, pregnant, or medically complex.

Mitigation: Check feeding amounts and health schedules with a veterinarian before acting; confirm prior care records when timing is uncertain.
