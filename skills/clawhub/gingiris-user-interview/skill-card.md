## Description: <br>
Gingiris User Interview is a multilingual playbook that guides agents through user interviews, beta testing, cold-start user operations, churn analysis, and user grading for product-market-fit discovery. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product, growth, and customer discovery teams use this skill to plan and run user interviews, beta tests, cold-start outreach, churn analysis, and PMF validation workflows. It provides structured research questions, follow-up cadences, grading systems, and reusable templates for turning user feedback into product decisions. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Screen recordings and shared screens can expose personal data, secrets, or confidential customer information. <br>
Mitigation: Make recording optional with explicit informed consent, warn participants not to show secrets or confidential windows, and redact sensitive material before storing or sharing transcripts. <br>
Risk: Sending interview transcripts to AI tools or issue trackers can spread personal or confidential information beyond the research team. <br>
Mitigation: Use approved systems only, redact transcripts before ingestion, and define storage, access, retention, and deletion rules for all interview data. <br>
Risk: Broad outreach and personal-data profiling can conflict with participant expectations or opt-out requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Use approved contact channels, honor opt-outs, and align outreach with the team's privacy-safe research procedures. <br>
