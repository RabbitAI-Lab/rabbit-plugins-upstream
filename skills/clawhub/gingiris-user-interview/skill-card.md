## Description:

User Interview & Cold-Start Operations Playbook helps agents guide user screening, interview execution, beta testing, churn analysis, and user grading for product-market fit discovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product teams, founders, growth operators, and agents use this skill to structure user interviews, beta-test operations, cold-start user cohorts, churn follow-up, and feature-request synthesis. It is best suited for turning qualitative customer discovery into interview plans, scripts, templates, and product iteration guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview recordings and screen shares may capture sensitive personal, customer, or company data.

Mitigation: Make recording opt-in, warn participants to close or hide sensitive screens, and offer a no-recording alternative.

Risk: AI transcript processing and issue-tracker handoff may expose personal data or secrets.

Mitigation: Redact secrets and personal data before sending transcripts or excerpts to AI systems or project-management tools.

Risk: User profiles, grading tables, and interview records can create retained personal-data stores without clear controls.

Mitigation: Define storage location, access limits, retention period, deletion rules, and review ownership before adopting the playbook as an SOP.
