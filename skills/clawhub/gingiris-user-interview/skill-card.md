A user research and cold-start operations playbook that helps agents guide customer interviews, beta testing, churn analysis, user grading, and product-market-fit discovery. <br>

Product teams, founders, researchers, and growth operators use this skill to structure user interviews, beta tests, cold-start user operations, churn analysis, and feedback-to-roadmap workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The playbook encourages screen recording, screen sharing, and collection of participant information during interviews. <br>
Mitigation: Make recording optional, obtain explicit consent before screen sharing or recording, warn participants not to reveal secrets or confidential material, and define storage, access, redaction, retention, and deletion rules for interview data. <br>
Risk: Some outreach guidance can lead to opaque participant screening or unclear interview purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose the real purpose of outreach and ensure privacy and research-operations controls are in place before using the skill. <br>
