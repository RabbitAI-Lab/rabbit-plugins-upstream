## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The playbook encourages screen recording and transcript processing that may capture personal, confidential, or customer-sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use explicit consent language, make recording optional where appropriate, tell participants to close sensitive screens, and define retention and access controls before interviews.

Risk: Interview transcripts or recordings may be processed by AI tools without approved data handling terms.

Mitigation: Route transcripts only through approved systems, avoid unapproved AI processing, and redact sensitive details before analysis or sharing.

Risk: Broad outreach across email, LinkedIn, Telegram, Discord, and similar channels can create privacy, compliance, or stop-contact issues.

Mitigation: Limit outreach to lawful and appropriate channels, include clear opt-out handling, and stop contact when requested.
