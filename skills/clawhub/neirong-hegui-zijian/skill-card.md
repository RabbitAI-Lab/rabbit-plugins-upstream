## Description:

Checks draft content before publication for advertising-law wording, privacy exposure, image and caption risks, platform-specific constraints, marketing ethics, and audience reaction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[888guoyajun](https://clawhub.ai/user/888guoyajun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content, marketing, and compliance reviewers use this skill as a pre-publication checklist for articles, short-form posts, video scripts, training materials, and knowledge-base entries. It helps identify risky claims, personal data exposure, unsuitable images, platform-rule concerns, and revision suggestions before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes guidance to remove AI-origin watermarks or provenance labels.

Mitigation: Preserve AI provenance labels where required and remove or rewrite watermark-removal instructions before installation.

Risk: The skill describes mandatory automatic integration with content-production workflows.

Mitigation: Use it as an explicitly invoked checklist and require approval before edits or publication decisions.

Risk: Users may treat checklist output as legal advice for regulated industries.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers validate legal, medical, financial, education, and platform-specific claims before publication.
