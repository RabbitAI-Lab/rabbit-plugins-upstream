## Description: <br>
Turn fuzzy ambitions into practical two-week sprint plans with concrete goals, work/personal balance, and lightweight accountability. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jrrembert](https://clawhub.ai/user/jrrembert) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and individual contributors use this skill to turn broad personal or professional ambitions into a short sprint plan with a theme, concrete work and personal goals, and lightweight accountability check-ins. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate in broad productivity or uncertainty-related conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for goal planning when narrower assistance is preferred. <br>
Risk: Sprint goals or accountability drafts could be misaligned with the user's real constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated sprint plan before sharing it with accountability partners or treating it as a commitment. <br>
