## Description: <br>
Ziroom is a long-term apartment rental guide that helps users calculate true monthly cost, review contract pitfalls, and plan dispute resolution steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhangifonly](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangifonly) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External renters and housing advisors use this skill to compare Ziroom listed rent against estimated true monthly cost, check rental-contract risk areas, and organize evidence-based dispute steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Rental costs, service fees, electricity prices, and contract terms can vary by city, listing, and signing flow. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill and calculator as practical guidance, then verify rates and obligations against the actual signing page and contract. <br>
Risk: Contract disputes and air-quality concerns can involve legal or health questions outside the skill's authority. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only necessary details to the agent and confirm legal, testing, or health-sensitive decisions with qualified local services or authorities. <br>
