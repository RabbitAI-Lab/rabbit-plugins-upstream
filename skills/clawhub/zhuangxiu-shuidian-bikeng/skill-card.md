## Description: <br>
This skill gives concise Chinese-language guidance for homeowners planning, pricing, inspecting, and avoiding common pitfalls in residential water, electrical, socket, waterproofing, and renovation handoff work. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and renovation customers use this skill to ask practical questions about water and electrical renovation planning, cost negotiation, acceptance checks, socket layout, waterproofing, and common contractor upsell tactics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat practical renovation guidance as a substitute for local building codes or qualified electrical and plumbing work. <br>
Mitigation: Present the skill as homeowner decision support and verify code-sensitive or safety-critical work with local requirements and qualified professionals. <br>
Risk: Broad water and electrical triggers may activate the skill for general household questions outside renovation context. <br>
Mitigation: Check that responses are framed for residential renovation water, electrical, waterproofing, or acceptance scenarios before relying on them. <br>
