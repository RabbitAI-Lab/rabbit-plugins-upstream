## Description: <br>
Helps individual creators and small businesses classify copyrightable works, prepare Chinese copyright registration materials, generate Word documents, and navigate registration steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, small businesses, and agents use this skill to gather registration details, identify the work category, draft supporting copyright registration documents, and receive process guidance for Chinese copyright registration. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill automatically installs a Python dependency at runtime. <br>
Mitigation: Install dependencies through a trusted package-management process before use and run the skill only in an environment approved for that dependency. <br>
Risk: Generated documents may contain identity, contact, authorship, and rights-ownership details stored in local files. <br>
Mitigation: Use a private workspace with appropriate access controls, review generated files before sharing, and remove or archive sensitive outputs according to organizational policy. <br>
Risk: Registration guidance and generated legal documents may be incomplete or outdated for a user's specific situation. <br>
Mitigation: Verify requirements with official registration channels and have important filings reviewed by a qualified professional before submission. <br>
