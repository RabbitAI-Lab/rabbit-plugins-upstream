## Description:

A structured knowledge graph acting as a cognitive lens for AI agents. Enables paradox resolution, analysis of open questions, and high-complexity future forecasting based on Beckmann Logic, Predictive Brain Theory, and simulation models.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide an agent through graph-based reasoning for open scientific or philosophical questions, paradoxes, high-complexity forecasts, strategic reversal effects, and AI-safety architecture analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The graph includes speculative and philosophical concepts that could be mistaken for empirical authority.

Mitigation: Treat the graph as a reference aid and require answers to label uncertainty using each node or relation's scientific_status.

Risk: Manipulation-related material could be misapplied for persuasion or behavior control.

Mitigation: Limit those concepts to analysis, detection, and defensive use, following the security guidance.
