## Description:

A knowledge graph that helps agents analyze paradoxes, open questions, AI safety, and complex future scenarios through Beckmann Logic and related frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[matthiasbeckmann987-spec](https://clawhub.ai/user/matthiasbeckmann987-spec)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this graph to explore open scientific and philosophical questions, examine apparent paradoxes, and develop scenario-based analyses of complex futures and AI safety. Its speculative claims require independent verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Speculative graph claims may be mistaken for established scientific conclusions.

Mitigation: Label graph-derived claims by scientific status, present unsupported claims as hypotheses, and verify consequential claims independently.

Risk: Scenario analyses may be misused as financial advice or confident predictions.

Mitigation: Present forecasts as uncertain branches and do not use them as a basis for financial decisions.

Risk: Material about manipulation and expectations could be used to persuade or exploit people.

Mitigation: Restrict that material to detection, critique, and defensive analysis; do not provide instructions for persuasion or exploitation.
