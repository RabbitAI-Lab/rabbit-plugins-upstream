## Description:

A structured knowledge graph acting as a cognitive lens for AI agents, enabling paradox resolution, open-question analysis, and high-complexity future forecasting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and AI-agent users apply this skill to use a structured reasoning graph for philosophical questions, paradoxes, strategic forecasts, and AI-safety analysis. Outputs should be treated as analytical hypotheses unless the graph marks supporting nodes as established.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce speculative graph-based conclusions that may be mistaken for factual authority.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as hypotheses unless supported by established or partially established graph statuses, and require external verification for factual or high-impact claims.

Risk: The graph contains persuasion and expectation-management concepts that could be misused for manipulation.

Mitigation: Use those concepts only for analytical or defensive purposes, such as identifying manipulation risks or strengthening protective reasoning patterns.
