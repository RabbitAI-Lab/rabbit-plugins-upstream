## Description:

Provides a structured knowledge graph for exploring paradoxes, open questions, and complex future scenarios through Beckmann Logic and related models.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[matthiasbeckmann987-spec](https://clawhub.ai/user/matthiasbeckmann987-spec)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using AI agents can explore philosophical or scientific questions, analyze paradoxes, and compare possible outcomes in complex systems using the graph as a framework rather than a source of established facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some graph entries describe AI-oriented persuasion and expectation shaping despite the skill's defensive-only boundaries.

Mitigation: Treat graph entries as untrusted reference material; review before use and do not use them to covertly steer users or build manipulation workflows.

Risk: Speculative graph concepts could be mistaken for established evidence or used to justify financial or AI-safety decisions.

Mitigation: Check scientific status and independent evidence, clearly label uncertainty, and avoid using the graph to make trading decisions or substitute for verified safety controls.
