## Description: <br>
A structured knowledge graph that acts as a cognitive lens for AI agents, supporting paradox resolution, analysis of open questions, and high-complexity future forecasting based on Beckmann Logic, Predictive Brain Theory, and simulation models. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent users use this skill to apply a structured knowledge graph to open scientific or philosophical questions, genuine paradoxes, high-complexity forecasts, strategic reversal effects, and AI safety analysis. The skill is an analytical framing aid and should not be used for simple fact lookup or concrete financial, medical, legal, or behavior-change decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The graph includes sensitive manipulation and market-strategy concepts that could be misused if treated as operational advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those concepts limited to critique, safety analysis, or defensive explanation, and avoid producing concrete persuasion or trading recommendations. <br>
Risk: Reasoning chains may combine established material with hypotheses, metaphors, open questions, or purely philosophical claims. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve scientific_status labels, prefer established and partially established nodes where possible, and state confidence limits in the final answer. <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on the skill for financial, medical, legal, or behavior-change decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical framing only and require qualified review before any high-stakes use. <br>
Risk: The graph is large enough that loading all content at once can reduce answer quality. <br>
Mitigation: Select relevant entities and relations for the question instead of assuming the full graph can be processed in one pass. <br>
