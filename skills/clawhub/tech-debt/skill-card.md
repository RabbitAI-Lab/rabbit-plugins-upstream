## Description: <br>
Reckless versus prudent categorization, tracking the inventory, and a payoff cadence that does not freeze delivery. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineers, and technical leads use this skill to classify technical debt, maintain a focused debt register, and choose a payoff cadence that reduces delivery friction without freezing feature work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may lead an agent to suggest refactors, deletions, debt-register edits, or tracker changes that affect engineering plans. <br>
Mitigation: Review resulting suggestions as normal engineering work before applying changes. <br>
Risk: Technical-debt guidance can be misapplied if debt items lack measurable interest, triggers, or expected outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Require concrete carry, blast radius, payoff cost, owner, and trigger before prioritizing debt work. <br>
