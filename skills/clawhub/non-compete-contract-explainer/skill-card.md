## Description:

Explain U.S. state-by-state and select international non-compete and restrictive-covenant law using bundled, source-cited jurisdiction snapshots.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and legal-adjacent teams use this skill to understand general non-compete and restrictive-covenant rules for a covered jurisdiction. It explains legal information from dated, source-cited snapshots and directs users to verify current law and consult a licensed attorney for advice on their facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal content is a dated snapshot and may not reflect current law.

Mitigation: Present the packaged, law-checked, review, and next-review dates; point users to the canonical jurisdiction URL before relying on important answers.

Risk: Users may ask for a verdict on their own contract or employment move.

Mitigation: Explain general factors only, avoid yes/no advice on personal facts, and direct users to a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction.

Risk: Optional refresh or package installation could involve trusting an external publisher or URL.

Mitigation: Ask before any refresh, fetch only fixed canonical URLs without user facts or contract text, and confirm package and publisher identity before external installation.
