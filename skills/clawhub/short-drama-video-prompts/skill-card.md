## Description:

Turns approved short-drama storyboards and keyframes into shot-by-shot video prompts and optional timeline music cues without generating media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Short-drama creators and video-prompt writers use this skill to convert locked shots and reference frames into copy-ready motion, performance, camera, and sound prompts, with timeline music guidance when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Default language or video-model assumptions may not fit the project or provider.

Mitigation: Check the project's prompt language, target model, duration, and reference-image profile before using the prompts.

Risk: Planned or missing reference media may be mistaken for assets ready for generation.

Mitigation: Confirm the referenced images exist and are bound to the intended shots before submitting prompts for production.
