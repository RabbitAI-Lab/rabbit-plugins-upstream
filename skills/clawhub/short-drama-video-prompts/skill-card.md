## Description:

把短剧分镜和冻结关键帧写成可直接复制的视频提示词 Markdown，也可按用户要求写时间线配乐/主题曲意图。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

短剧编剧、导演和视频制作人员可用它将已确认分镜与冻结关键帧整理为逐镜视频生成提示词，并按需编写时间线配乐意图。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project images, audio, video, or prompts may be sent to a selected provider during later media generation.

Mitigation: Before separately approving generation, review which project materials and prompts will reach that provider.
