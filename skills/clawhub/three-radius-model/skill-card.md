## Description:

Guides users through diagnosing gaps between what they understand, can do, and actually do, then planning evidence-based actions to address them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals, coaches, and team leads use this skill to locate whether stalled progress comes from a knowledge, capability, or execution gap. It produces a Three-Radius Diagnostic and a prioritized 30-day closure plan grounded in observable evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
