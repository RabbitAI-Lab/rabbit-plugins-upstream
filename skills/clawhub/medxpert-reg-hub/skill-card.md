## Description:

医疗器械注册工程师的随身资料库，覆盖中国 NMPA、美国 FDA、欧盟 MDR、日本 PMDA、东南亚和拉美注册路径与分类，并按全生命周期 7 阶段组织 UDI、STED、生物相容性、GMP、上市后监管、标签与 IFU、供应商合规等专题资料。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External regulatory affairs, quality, product, and commercialization teams use this skill to locate structured medical-device registration references, compare market pathways, and prepare Markdown or HTML checklists with official source links. It is a reference and drafting aid, not a substitute for current regulator guidance or qualified RA/legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory references can become outdated or incomplete for a specific product, market, or classification edge case.

Mitigation: Verify cited official sources before relying on the output and use qualified RA or legal review for high-stakes registration decisions.

Risk: License and knowledge-content terms are not fully aligned between the MIT license file and the separate attestation restrictions on copying, training, and commercial reuse.

Mitigation: Review the packaged license and attestation before redistribution, training use, or commercial reuse.

Risk: The skill is primarily Chinese-language and trigger-oriented, so users outside that context may receive less direct coverage.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired language or output format explicitly and confirm that the selected reference file matches the user's market and product scope.
