## Description:

MedXpert-REG-001 医械注册全球通关枢纽 helps medical device registration teams navigate official-source references for China, the United States, the European Union, Japan, Southeast Asia, and Latin America across classification, UDI, STED, quality systems, post-market obligations, and related registration topics.

## Use Case:

External regulatory affairs professionals, registration engineers, and product teams use this skill to find official medical device registration references, plan market-entry paths, and produce structured Markdown or HTML checklists, tables, and summaries. It is a regulatory reference aid and should not be treated as legal advice or a substitute for professional RA review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements, official links, fees, timelines, and classification details can change after the skill's reference material is published.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive claims against the current regulator source before using outputs in submissions or business decisions.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill's reference summaries for legal advice, professional RA advice, or a registration代理 service.

Mitigation: Present outputs as reference aids and route filing decisions through qualified regulatory affairs or legal review.

Risk: Optional local knowledge-base workflows could expose private customer, pricing, supplier, or submission data if users add sensitive material.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive business and customer data out of local knowledge-base inputs unless the operating environment is approved for that data.
