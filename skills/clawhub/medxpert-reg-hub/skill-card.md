## Description:

All-in-one reference hub for medical device registration engineers covering global regulatory pathways, classification, UDI, STED technical files, biocompatibility, GMP quality systems, post-market surveillance, labeling, suppliers, and related lifecycle topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External medical device regulatory affairs teams, registration engineers, and product teams use this skill to find structured official-source references for classification, registration pathways, technical documentation, quality systems, and post-market obligations across major markets. It is a reference aid and should be checked against current regulator publications before filing decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory references can become outdated or vary by jurisdiction and product specifics.

Mitigation: Verify official regulator sources, publication dates, product classification, intended use, contact characteristics, software, sterility, and target market before relying on outputs for filings.

Risk: The skill may be triggered by broad terms such as labeling, repair, after-sales, or supplier compliance outside a medical-device context.

Mitigation: Confirm that the question concerns medical devices and ask for missing product and market details before applying regulatory pathway guidance.

Risk: Outputs are a regulatory reference aid, not legal advice or a registration代理 service.

Mitigation: Require qualified regulatory affairs or legal review before using generated conclusions in formal submissions or commercial decisions.
