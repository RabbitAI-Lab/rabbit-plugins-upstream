## Description:

Helps insurance companies, brokers, and insurtech teams draft and review insurance bid materials for procurement in mainland China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance company, broker, and insurtech bid teams use the skill to draft technical and commercial bid sections, compare policy clauses, and self-check insurance-specific compliance and disqualification risks before qualified staff review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bid materials may expose personal insurance records or confidential pricing assumptions.

Mitigation: De-identify claimant and customer data, limit disclosure of commercial secrets, and preview drafts before saving.

Risk: Inaccurate bid wording, compliance conclusions, or pricing assumptions may affect procurement decisions.

Mitigation: Have responsible business, actuarial, legal, and compliance staff verify all materials before use; determine rates and approve submissions outside the skill.
