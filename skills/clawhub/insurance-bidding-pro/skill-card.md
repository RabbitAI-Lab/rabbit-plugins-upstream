## Description:

AI-powered insurance bidding expert for China market — covers the complete insurance bid lifecycle including solvency disclosure (C-ROSS Rules II), reinsurance arrangements, regulator compliance, policy clause comparison, risk reserve explanations, and current procurement rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Insurance company, broker, and insurtech staff use this skill to draft and review insurance bid materials for China procurement, including clause response tables and insurance-specific compliance self-checks. Outputs are reference drafts for review by qualified business, actuarial, legal, and compliance staff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was benign but incomplete because the only component was only partially inspected.

Mitigation: Have a reviewer inspect the complete skill contents and confirm referenced paths before installation.

Risk: Bid materials may contain personal information, claims data, or confidential pricing assumptions.

Mitigation: Use de-identified examples and disclose only the data required by the procurement documents.

Risk: Regulatory and procurement guidance can become outdated or be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Check current official sources and have the responsible qualified teams review compliance conclusions, pricing assumptions, and bid drafts.
