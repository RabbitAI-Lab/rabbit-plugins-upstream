## Description:

Use when making pizza dough for any pan size and count — neo-neapolitan, NY-style, Detroit, sicilian, or gluten-free — from one dough ball; computes ball weight from pan dimensions (Detroit pan area math, 16-inch round area), hydration for your flour and oven, salt/oil/yeast by baker's percentages, cold-ferment vs same-day yeast schedules, and a per-step timing sheet so 4 doughs for a 16-inch + 2 Detroit pans never turn into guesswork.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Home pizza makers and other cooks use this skill to scale dough for different pizza styles, pan sizes, and batch counts, then plan ingredient amounts and fermentation around a target bake time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect pan dimensions or fermentation settings can produce an unsuitable dough recipe or schedule.

Mitigation: Check the entered pan dimensions and fermentation settings against your pans, ingredients, and planned bake time before using the recipe.
