## Description:

Standup Mobi helps teams assign coverage for an absent colleague's duties, document undocumented knowledge, and prepare a ramp plan and printable checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Team leads and employees use this skill to inventory an absent colleague's duties, assign temporary owners, capture critical knowledge, and prepare substitute ramp plans and checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage inventories, runbooks, or checklists could expose internal operations or credentials if shared broadly.

Mitigation: Keep planning files in an appropriate private workspace; record vault entry names or system names, never passwords, keys, or tokens.

Risk: Incomplete duty inventories or proposed assignments may leave important work uncovered.

Mitigation: Review the plan with the team, resolve uncovered duties, and confirm owners' capacity and access before relying on the checklist.
