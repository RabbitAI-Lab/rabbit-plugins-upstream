## Description:

Turn authorized blackboard photos and teacher-supplied facts into one blackboard one-shot clip per photo. This one-shot blackboard studio makes a silent 2-15s blackboard clip from each photo, so every board photo clip opens on the board exactly as captured. Use it for classroom blackboard video and lesson-board clip sets that stay one photo one clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
