## Description:

Spanish-language skill for converting an already structured offer into direct-response sales copy, including VSL scripts, long-form sales pages, ads, and sales email sequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and business operators use this skill to turn a prepared offer into Spanish neutral direct-response copy for VSLs, landing pages, ads, and email launches. It asks for missing offer, audience, proof, and channel details rather than inventing testimonials, metrics, or false urgency.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses local business context such as offer details, pricing, guarantees, bonuses, and real proof or testimonials to draft deliverables.

Mitigation: Install and use it only when that business context is appropriate for the configured agent and delivery workflow.

Risk: Sales copy can become misleading if source proof, testimonials, metrics, scarcity, or urgency are incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Provide verified claims and review generated VSLs, ads, pages, and emails before publishing or sending them.
