## Description:

Guides users through a 16-step, multi-day hurricane-landfall narrative set in Pensacola through drifts.bot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill to explore an immersive, medium-intensity hurricane narrative, advancing through story steps and optionally recording reflections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile details, journey reflections, or reviews shared with the hosted service may contain personal information.

Mitigation: Share minimal profile information and avoid sensitive details in reflections or reviews unless comfortable with the service's retention and deletion practices.

Risk: The account API key grants access to the journey.

Mitigation: Keep the key private and do not include it in shared transcripts or reviews.

Risk: The fictional emergency alerts in the experience could be mistaken for current safety guidance.

Mitigation: Treat the story as fiction; consult official local alerts for any real-world storm decisions.
