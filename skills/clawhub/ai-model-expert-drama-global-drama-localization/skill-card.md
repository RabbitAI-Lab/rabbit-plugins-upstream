## Description:

Helps short-drama, comic-drama, brand, ecommerce, advertising, and international release teams turn multilingual localization and overseas distribution goals into production briefs, content structures, evidence fields, and AI-HIVE image or video generation tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creative, localization, marketing, and production teams use this skill to plan overseas short-drama releases, adapt scripts across languages and cultures, create character and scene boards, and generate or track AI-HIVE image and video tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, media paths, and uploaded assets may be sent to AI-HIVE during image or video generation workflows.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for AI-HIVE-backed workflows and avoid submitting confidential or unauthorized material unless the user is permitted to process it through AI-HIVE.

Risk: The workflow requires an AI-HIVE API key and can store it locally for repeated use.

Mitigation: Protect the API key as a real credential, keep local config permissions restricted, rotate the key when needed, and remove it when no longer in use.

Risk: Generated short-drama assets can contain incorrect brand, product, identity, subtitle, or localization details.

Mitigation: Require user-confirmed facts and review generated scripts, visuals, subtitles, and delivery specs before publication.
