## Description:

A gentle, Chinese-first personal coach that helps people choose up to three current priorities and start one small action today.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to clarify personal priorities, choose a manageable next action, and review their progress without overloading their day. It is not a team project manager or a substitute for making major life decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal planning records may contain sensitive details and remain visible to local backups or others with access to the same machine account.

Mitigation: Save only after explicit consent, avoid raw sensitive details, and use local delete or export controls carefully.
