## Description:

A gentle personal coach that helps people identify their current priorities and choose a small action to begin today.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to sort competing personal priorities, plan a manageable next step, and reconnect with a focus they chose earlier. It supports daily check-ins and longer-term reviews, but does not manage team projects, synchronize task tools, or make major life decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved priorities may expose personal details when surfaced in broad summaries, and the security scan was partial with reported file-access, deletion-consent, and network-access concerns.

Mitigation: Keep saved information minimal and private, review summaries before sharing, and check the packaged tools' access behavior before installing; only save records with explicit consent.
