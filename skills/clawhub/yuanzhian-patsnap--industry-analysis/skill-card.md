## Description:

Generates comprehensive industry analysis reports using patent data, market information, industry-chain company maps, company data, and user-provided evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External analysts and planning teams use this skill to assemble global or province/city-level industry analysis, competitive landscape summaries, opportunity assessments, and related biomedical topic reports from provided materials and PatSnap/Zhihuiya data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill scope is unclear because the artifact combines industry analysis reporting with biomedical target-discovery instructions.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and require the publisher to clarify the intended scope before using it in regulated or high-impact workflows.

Risk: Inputs may include confidential compounds, targets, market plans, or patent strategy.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential inputs unless the publisher documents scope, data handling, and external tool behavior clearly enough for the deployment environment.

Risk: The skill may produce patent or FTO-related discussion that could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat FTO, infringement, validity, and other legal conclusions as non-legal analysis and route them to qualified counsel.
