## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language consumer-sector startup investment and coaching advisor that produces project diagnosis, strategic judgment, tactical action lists, and risk guidance using the bundled Fengpan Capital Accelerator methodology. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[monsterdt](https://clawhub.ai/user/monsterdt) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, operators, and advisors in consumer sectors use this skill to evaluate startup projects, unit economics, market position, expansion paths, RBF structures, M&A options, and 100-day operating priorities. It is intended for structured business coaching and decision support, not as legal, tax, audit, or binding investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill gives strong, opinionated investment and coaching guidance that could be mistaken for definitive professional advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support analysis and independently verify legal, tax, audit, valuation, market, and investment claims with qualified professionals before acting. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes disclosed branded promotion and a recurring Fengpan Capital Accelerator call-to-action. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill's branding and solicitation posture before deployment, and use it only where that commercial framing is acceptable to users. <br>
Risk: Users may provide confidential project materials during startup diagnosis or financing discussions. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive non-public business information unless the deployment environment and data-handling policies are appropriate for that material. <br>
