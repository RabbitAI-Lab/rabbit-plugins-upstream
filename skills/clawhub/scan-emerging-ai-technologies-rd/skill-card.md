## Description:

Route a technology-intelligence request to a technology landscape, invention-mining workflow, or current-intelligence briefing, and produce an evidence-backed English HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

R&D, IP, strategy, and technology-intelligence teams use this skill to scope fast-moving technical domains, compare technology routes and actors, mine invention concepts, or monitor recent signals across patents, literature, policy, standards, and industry activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Technology-intelligence workflows may use external patent, scientific, regulatory, or current-awareness services when authorized.

Mitigation: Use only approved connected services for the workflow and avoid providing confidential invention details unless the environment is approved for that use.

Risk: Patentability, infringement, freedom-to-operate, validity, enforceability, term, and standards-essentiality conclusions require qualified legal review.

Mitigation: Treat the skill's outputs as evidence organization and issue spotting, and route legal conclusions to qualified professionals.

Risk: Weak or incomplete evidence can lead to misleading route, competitor, or opportunity conclusions.

Mitigation: Require source IDs, search coverage, counting units, confidence labels, limitations, and counterevidence before presenting factual findings.
