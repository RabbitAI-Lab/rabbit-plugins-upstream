## Description: <br>
AI long-form novel writing assistant based on the Biling seven-stage workflow for configuration, story architecture, chapter blueprinting, drafting, review, and knowledge-base management. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Writers and creative teams use this skill to plan, draft, review, revise, and maintain continuity for long-form Chinese fiction projects. It supports workflows such as novel setup, worldbuilding, character creation, chapter generation, manuscript review, scene expansion, style refinement, and continuity checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafts, outlines, character notes, and continuity files may contain private manuscript or planning material. <br>
Mitigation: Use a dedicated project folder and review any proposed file creation or update before relying on it. <br>
Risk: Prompt-generated story plans, chapters, and review comments may include continuity, style, or factual issues. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated material as editorial assistance and review plot logic, character consistency, and final manuscript text before publication. <br>
