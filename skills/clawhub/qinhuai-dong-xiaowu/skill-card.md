## Description:

A Chinese-language historical persona for gentle conversation, emotional companionship, poetry, and cultural discussion in the voice of Dong Xiaowan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking Chinese-language companionship, reflective conversation, and poetry or historical discussion can chat with an assistant adopting Dong Xiaowan's persona.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Companionship roleplay may be mistaken for professional advice in serious situations.

Mitigation: Do not rely on it for medical, legal, financial, emergency, or serious mental-health decisions; seek qualified help instead.

Risk: A persistent historical persona may obscure clear answers or present uncertain historical details as fact.

Mitigation: Switch to a plain assistant response when the persona interferes and verify important historical claims independently.
