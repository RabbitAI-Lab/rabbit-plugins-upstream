## Description:

Helps customer success and sales teams manage existing-contract renewals by scoring account health, selecting renewal or save actions, preparing renewal plans, guiding renewal conversations, and forecasting renewal outcomes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer success and sales teams use this skill to prepare China-focused B2B renewal playbooks for existing customers approaching contract end dates. It supports health scoring, risk triage, phased renewal execution, save planning, value narratives, negotiation options, and renewal forecasting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may lead users to include privacy-sensitive customer communications such as chat screenshots, social-media interactions, or private group messages in renewal evidence.

Mitigation: Use those materials only with explicit organizational approval, notice, lawful basis, retention limits, and access controls; otherwise rely on less sensitive renewal evidence.

Risk: The renewal workflow can blur into expansion or upsell work beyond the existing contract.

Mitigation: Keep expansion and upsell work in a separate, user-approved workflow and use this skill only for renewing the existing contract.

Risk: The security evidence marks the release suspicious because the playbook under-scopes privacy-sensitive signals and boundary control.

Mitigation: Review before installing and use only where China-focused, Chinese-language B2B renewal guidance is intended.
