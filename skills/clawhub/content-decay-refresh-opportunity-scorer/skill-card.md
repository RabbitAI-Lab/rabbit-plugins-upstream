## Description:

Analyze content decay patterns and prioritize refresh opportunities by scoring search ranking loss, engagement drop-off, competitor movement, and outdated data. Use when the user needs content audit recommendations, SEO refresh strategy, or audience re-engagement campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content, SEO, and editorial teams use this skill to audit published content for search ranking loss, engagement decline, competitor movement, and stale data, then prioritize refresh work with concrete recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may require credentials for analytics, search, SERP, CMS, or messaging services.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege credentials and avoid pasting secrets into shared prompts or files.

Risk: Audit results may include sensitive marketing performance data or unpublished content strategy.

Mitigation: Review and redact results before sharing them through Slack or other third-party tools.
