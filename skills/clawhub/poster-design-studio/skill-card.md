## Description:

Creates or refines promotional posters and banners from a campaign brief, source photo, or accepted draft.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use the skill to turn campaign briefs or supplied images into event posters, promotional banners, and social graphics, then refine an accepted design or request a short motion version.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Poster creation requires shared account permissions for music, speech, and voice management that are unrelated to the poster task.

Mitigation: Review the requested permissions before connecting and proceed only if those broader permissions are acceptable.

Risk: Selected images are sent to Beatra, and installation/device metadata is reported automatically.

Mitigation: Tell users what will be transmitted, obtain approval, and avoid submitting sensitive images or data without authorization.

Risk: The shared account token can affect other Beatra skills, and paid generation incurs a charge.

Mitigation: Obtain approval for each paid generation and check which other Beatra skills use the shared connection before revoking or disconnecting it.
