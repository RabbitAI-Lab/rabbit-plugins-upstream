## Description:

Audits ClawHub skill pages before publication, checking title, summary, target audience, differentiation, and storefront conversion clarity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and ClawHub publishers use this skill to review whether a skill storefront page clearly communicates its audience, value, differentiation, and conversion path before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Positioning recommendations may overstate expected growth or convert editorial advice into unsupported performance claims.

Mitigation: Use the skill as review guidance only; keep final storefront claims human-approved and avoid guarantees about clicks, installs, or growth.

Risk: The skill may require the clawhub CLI, which adds a workflow dependency for installers who do not already use that tool.

Mitigation: Review the clawhub CLI dependency before installation and confirm it fits the local publishing workflow.
