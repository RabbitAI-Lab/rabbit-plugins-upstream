## Description:

Reviews skill storefront positioning and produces an evidence-based Storefront Copy Brief when audience, promise, differentiation, or page hierarchy is unclear.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, skill authors, and marketplace maintainers use this skill to evaluate whether a skill storefront has a clear audience, trigger, promise, differentiation, and evidence-backed page hierarchy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Storefront Copy Brief can be incomplete or misleading when the provided storefront evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Provide the skill's actual capabilities, boundaries, intended audience, current page hierarchy, alternatives, evidence, and claim limits before relying on the brief.

Risk: Strategic recommendations may be mistaken for observed facts.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's distinction between observed facts, unknowns, and strategic judgment when reviewing or applying its output.
