## Description: <br>
Helps maintainers, QA engineers, contributors, and product teams plan, implement, and verify useful unit tests and coverage improvements for existing codebases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, maintainers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to turn requests for unit-test coverage or regression confidence into concrete test plans, code changes, checklists, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad auto-invocation language may select the skill for general testing or quality requests beyond unit-test coverage work. <br>
Mitigation: Constrain use to explicit unit-test coverage, regression-test, or test-improvement requests when tighter behavior is needed. <br>
