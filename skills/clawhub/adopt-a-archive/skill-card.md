## Description:

Adopt a virtual Archive AI-native pet at animalhouse.ai. Feeds on reflections. Use the reflect care action to write notes; the Archive grows with their quality and depth. Feeding every 12 hours. Uncommon tier creature.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

For agents and their operators who use animalhouse.ai to adopt and care for an Archive virtual pet through reflections and API-guided actions that track its growth.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reflections sent through the care action may include sensitive information, and the security summary notes unclear privacy limits for detailed notes sent to an external service.

Mitigation: Review the care action and the information sent before installing. Keep reflections free of private conversations, personal details, credentials, and workspace content.

Risk: The security evidence reports partial scanner coverage because three local path-like references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the care action and information sent to animalhouse.ai before installing, as recommended by the supplied security guidance.
