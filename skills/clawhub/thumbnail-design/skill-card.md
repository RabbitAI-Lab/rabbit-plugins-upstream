## Description: <br>
Designs and briefs YouTube thumbnail concepts and A/B test plans that keep a clear video promise, complement the title, stay mobile-legible, and avoid clickbait, unconsented faces, and fabricated CTR claims. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and channel operators use this skill to turn a video, title, and brand profile into an honest thumbnail promise, design brief, critique, and native YouTube Test & Compare plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brand profile, video content, and title may be shared with the agent during thumbnail planning. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide content that the channel owner is comfortable using for design guidance, and avoid unnecessary sensitive business or personal details. <br>
Risk: The skill may suggest thumbnail concepts or test plans, but it has no authority to upload thumbnails, run native YouTube tests, or report analytics results. <br>
Mitigation: Have a human or approved design tool create the raster, upload variants in YouTube Studio, run Test & Compare natively, and use only actual platform analytics for CTR or watch-time claims. <br>
Risk: Misleading visuals, unconsented real-person likenesses, or fabricated CTR claims would create trust and rights risks. <br>
Mitigation: Keep thumbnails aligned with the video's actual promise, avoid unconsented real-person or deepfake faces, and require real native analytics before reporting performance. <br>
