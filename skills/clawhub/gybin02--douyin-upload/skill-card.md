### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing relies on an external EasyPost installation and a logged-in Douyin account; the selected account, media, or text may be wrong.

Mitigation: Use a trusted EasyPost installation and review the selected account, media, and text before publishing.
