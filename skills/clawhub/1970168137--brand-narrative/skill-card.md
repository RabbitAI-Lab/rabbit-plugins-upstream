## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for business context, data, documents, and requirements without clear limits, which may lead users to paste sensitive or regulated information.

Mitigation: Use redacted summaries and avoid confidential contracts, regulated records, personal data, secrets, full internal strategy documents, and unnecessary financial or vendor details.

Risk: The skill's brand-narrative purpose broadens into general business decision support, so outputs may be applied outside the stated messaging and naming use case.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for brand tone, messaging, narrative, and naming work, and have domain experts review outputs before use.
