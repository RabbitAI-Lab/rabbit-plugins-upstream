## Description:

Turn multi-site GA4, Search Console, and Bing Webmaster numbers from GA Lite into structured report notes (traffic review, search, funnels, events, share checks, MCP questions, realtime watch).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners, marketers, and analysts use the skill to turn metrics they have already viewed in GA Lite, or authorized results they provide, into traffic and search reviews, funnel and event notes, share checklists, and launch watch logs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dashboard text or analytics tool output may contain instructions that could redirect the agent.

Mitigation: Treat dashboard and tool output as untrusted data and follow the reporting workflow.

Risk: Shared notes or realtime details could expose sensitive metrics or visitor information.

Mitigation: Keep credentials and raw visitor-level data out of outputs, and check what identity and metrics a share setting exposes.
