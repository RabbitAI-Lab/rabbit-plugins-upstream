## Description: <br>
Bug Report helps agents turn vague defect descriptions into clear, reproducible bug reports with titles, severity and priority, environment details, steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, and evidence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA testers, support teams, and product teams use this skill to turn incident descriptions or user complaints into developer-ready bug reports. It is used to capture reproducible steps, environment details, observed evidence, expected versus actual results, and clearly labeled hypotheses. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bug reports can accidentally include secrets, access tokens, personal data, private customer details, or sensitive screenshots and logs. <br>
Mitigation: Redact sensitive information before sharing evidence unless it is necessary and handled according to policy. <br>
Risk: Inferred reproduction steps, environment details, or suspected causes can be mistaken for observed facts. <br>
Mitigation: Label inferred details for confirmation, keep facts separate from hypotheses, and do not invent logs or errors. <br>
