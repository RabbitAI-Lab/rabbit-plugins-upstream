## Description:

Retrieves transcripts from public videos across major platforms, including TikTok audio transcription and translated YouTube subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People working with public video use this skill to obtain transcripts for summaries, quotations, translation, and content research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill requires a third-party plugin and one-time device pairing.

Mitigation: Review the plugin and approve pairing before use.

Risk: Public video URLs are shared with ClawLink and its data providers.

Mitigation: Use only public video links and avoid sharing private or sensitive URLs.

Risk: Transcript calls use paid ClawLink credit, which can accumulate across bulk requests.

Mitigation: Check the current per-call price and approve bulk or higher-cost calls in advance.
