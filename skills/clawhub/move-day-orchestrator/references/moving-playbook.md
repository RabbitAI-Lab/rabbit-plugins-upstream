# The Moving Playbook — Reference

## 1. Volume & truck math

Standard loaded-volume presets (m³, incl. normal packing air):

| Home | Light | Normal | Heavy (books, vinyl, tools) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | 6 | 8 | 11 |
| 1-bed | 9 | 12 | 16 |
| 2-bed | 14 | 18 | 23 |
| 3-bed | 20 | 26 | 32 |
| 4-bed house | 28 | 36 | 45 |

Common big-item volumes (bottom-up estimating):

- Sofa 2-seat ~1.6 m³, 3-seat ~2.2 m³ (less if legs off), sectional 3–4.5 m³
- Bed 160 + mattress ~1.8 m³; bed disassembled ~0.9 m³; mattress alone 0.5–0.7
- Fridge ~1.2 m³ (upright, doors on), washer 0.5, dryer 0.5, dishwasher 0.4
- Dining table + 4 chairs ~1.5 (disassembled 0.9); desk 1.0; wardrobe 1.5–2.5 (disassembled flat-pack ~0.4/pack)
- Bike 0.6 (front wheel off 0.4); TV 55" 0.3 (boxed); boxes: XL 120L=0.12, L 75L=0.075, M 40L=0.04, S 20L=0.02

Truck classes (usable volume, not marketing): cargo van ~3.5–4 m³, 10' ~6–7, 15' ~9–11, 20' ~14–16, 26' ~20–23. **Always take the next size up from your estimate or plan a second trip** — a 15' marketed truck is ~10 m³ usable and real loading is 10–15% air. Two van trips across a city often beat one box truck (parking, height limits, unfamiliarity).

Crew-hours heuristic: loaded m³ × 0.6 h per pro for a ground-floor-to-truck flow; multiply by stairs factor 1.5 (no elevator, 3rd floor: ×1.8–2.2), elevator factor 1.15, long-carry (>30 m truck-to-door: ×1.3). Friends move at ~half pro speed; both need the run-of-show to avoid the 4PM "what's left" fog.

## 2. The corner problem (furniture physics)

A rigid item (mattress, sofa, wardrobe box) negotiates a corner only if its **diagonal** clears in the relevant plane:

- Stairwell turn: item's smallest cross-section diagonal < stair width − handrail protrusion, AND vertical clearance at the turn (measure the diagonal from tread-nose to ceiling at the outside of the turn).
- Doorway + hallway corner: treat as a rotating segment — if the item's length exceeds the hallway widths' hypotenuse allowance, it must go vertical (mattresses) or come apart (sofas: feet off, sometimes arms; IKEA: it was designed to move flat).
- Measure: item L×W×H, door H×W, hallway W, stair W and the turn geometry. If diagonal ≈ clearance, assume it doesn't fit; disassembly is cheaper than drywall repairs and landlord claims.

## 3. Countdown timeline (T−8 to T+1)

**T−8…T−6 wks**: mover quotes (3 of them; binding > non-binding), building logistics both ends (elevator slots, CoI insurance certs, truck parking/permits), declutter wave 1 (sell/donate big stuff while time exists), start a "move file" (quotes, leases, meter numbers).

**T−4 wks**: confirm crew/truck booking in writing; order packing materials (box math from the estimate + 20%); declutter wave 2; schedule utilities: **old stays on through moving day**; new: electricity/water active at arrival, internet install day-after-arrival slot; address-change list drafted (bank, employer, subscriptions, licenses, insurance, voter reg, doctor/dentist).

**T−2 wks**: pack waves 1–2 (storage, guest, books, out-of-season, decor); label system live (ROOM-SEQ + contents keywords + open-first flags); defrost plan for fridge/freezer (48h before, towels in the base, doors taped ajar); arrange kids/pets care for the day; pharmacy refills; valuables plan (car, not truck).

**T−1 wk**: pack wave 3 (kitchen-daily partial, most clothes); confirm elevator reservation + parking; cash for tips; cleaning plan for the old place (or book cleaners); first-night box assembled and SEALED with a list taped on.

**T−1 day**: disassemble what's possible (hardware bag taped to item); fridge off, dry, towels in; freezer meals eaten or donated; phone charged + powerbank; runshow printed; showers tonight (hot water may be off tomorrow).

**Moving day**: runshow (protect floors → load heavy-first → car valuables → final sweep → meters → keys).

**T+1 day**: beds first, then bathroom essentials, then kitchen basics; internet installer meet; address changes fire; old-place cleaning/inspection walkthrough; meters read/photographed.

**T+1 wk**: box-priority queue from the inventory's open-first flags; donation pickup for the inevitable "why did we move this" pile; review mover-liability window for damage claims (typically 9 months US interstate, but do it this week while evidence is fresh).

## 4. Packing waves (need-date ranked)

1. **Storage, guest room, garage/balcony, out-of-season clothes, books** (T−3 wks) — weeks-unneeded: high. Books in SMALL boxes (a large book box breaks backs and bottoms).
2. **Decor, art, collections, spare electronics, linen excess** (T−2 wks) — art glass-framed or cardboard-collared; TVs boxed or blanket-wrapped + screen cardboard.
3. **Kitchen-daily partial, wardrobe (most), workshop, furniture disassembly** (T−1 wk) — keep one box's worth of daily dishes out; wardrobe boxes pay for themselves in ironing time.
4. **Bedding (one set out), bathroom (one set out), last kitchen items, cleaning supplies** (T−2 days…morning) — into the first-night box and the open-first wave.

First-night box pulls per room: bedroom (bedding set, pillow, phone charger, change of clothes each), bathroom (TP, soap, towel each, toothbrush, meds), kitchen (kettle, mugs, coffee/tea, snacks, cutlery×4, plates×4, dish soap + sponge, bin bags), utility (box cutter, screwdriver, allen keys, masking tape, marker, flashlight, extension cord, lightbulbs), kids (one comfort toy each), pets (food bowl, leash, litter).

## 5. Box system

- Label: `ROOM-SEQ` (KIT-01…KIT-12, BED-01…, STO-…) on TOP and TWO SIDES; marker never runs out mid-box: two markers per packer.
- Contents keywords on the side (5–8 words, not a manifest: "kettle mugs coffee filters sugar") + flags: `OPEN-FIRST`, `FRAGILE`, `HEAVY`, `THIS-SIDE-UP`.
- Log every box in the inventory (`box` command): the search function replaces the week-long box archaeology, and the log is the damage-claim evidence.
- Packing physics: heavy-small low and forward in the truck; light-large high and rear; fill voids with soft bags/linens; strap a wall of boxes every layer; no box > 20 kg unless you hate your friends; plates VERTICAL on padding (they're strong in-plane); glasses boxed with dividers or rolled in socks.
- The 10-minute rule: if an item needs wrapping, wrap it when it enters the box, not in a "wrap later" pile that becomes a landfill.

## 6. Movers vs. DIY decision

Choose pros when: volume > ~18 m³, stairs both ends, appliances (they have dollies and straps and the backs), piano/safe/marble, deadline (closing dates), cross-country (their truck, their driver, liability).
Choose DIY+friends when: < ~12 m³, elevator both ends, flexible dates, budget-hard, you own blankets/straps/dollies or can rent them cheaply.
Hybrid (most common, best value): you pack everything + pros load/drive/unload (labor-only crews are booked by the hour and are dramatically cheaper than full-service; they load better than you do — the physics of a well-built truck wall is real).

Liability: released-value (included) ~$0.60/kg is insult coverage — a $900 TV weighs 15 kg → $9. Declared/full-value or your renter's/home insurance rider is the real coverage; photograph electronics serials + condition before the crew arrives.

## 7. Run-of-show details (the day)

Sequence: protect (floors, frames, corners, banisters) → appliances prepped (washer drum bolted/locked per manual, fridge dry/towels/doors taped) → disassembled furniture staged at the door → heavy furniture into truck first (against the cab wall, strapped), appliances next, mattress/sofa vertical-ish along walls, boxes build the walls, soft/light on top of the box walls, bikes/last-mile items last (they come OFF first).

- The **car manifest**: documents, jewelry, cash, meds, electronics/laptops, chargers, plants, candles (they melt), flammables (movers won't take them: propane, petrol cans, paint), perishable food, and the first-night box.
- **Meter photos** (electric/gas/water) both ends + the utility's final-reading form.
- Final sweep checklist per room: closets, tops of wardrobes, under beds, behind doors, balcony/attic/cellar, dishwasher/washer/drum, fridge coils?, water meter, mail slot, spare keys, wall sockets for chargers.
- Kids/pets: off-site for the day. The one-period-of-day where moving injuries happen is the 4PM fatigue window — schedule the heavy two-person carries before lunch, not after.
- Tips: industry norm ~5% or $20–40/mover/day, cash, handed at the end; water/refreshments out from the start (it's decency and performance).

## 8. Long-distance additions

- Drive plan: hotel bookings with truck-route constraints (height/weight limits, parkings), fuel stops, weather windows.
- Overnight box duplicates the first-night box + next-day clothes.
- Arrival triage: utilities confirmed on before arrival (a dark unheated house at 22:00 with kids is the failure scenario); mattress and bedding first; hot-water heater on; the internet slot for next morning.
- Paper trail: lease/keys handoff at origin, walkthrough photos of the new place's condition BEFORE unpacking (deposit protection works both directions).
