## Description:

Turns US rental-unit inspection findings into a priced make-ready scope with vendor work lists and estimate ranges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tektolo](https://clawhub.ai/user/tektolo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Property managers and rental owners use this skill to turn move-out reports, notes, or observed photo conditions into cost estimates, trade-specific work scopes, and owner approval summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inspection details may be sent to Tektolo for estimation.

Mitigation: Share only needed property findings; omit names, faces, street addresses, and other personal details.

Risk: Estimated costs may be mistaken for binding contractor quotes.

Mitigation: Label prices as ranges and confirm actual costs with local vendors.

Risk: Photos and inspection notes cannot establish hazards or tenant responsibility.

Mitigation: Refer suspected hazards to qualified professionals and avoid attributing damage to tenants from photos.
