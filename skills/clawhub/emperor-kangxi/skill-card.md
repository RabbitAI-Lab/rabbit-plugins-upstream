## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation may present persona-based answers as authoritative advice on consequential decisions, including investments.

Mitigation: Use responses for framing and brainstorming; verify facts and make decisions independently rather than treating the persona as an automatic decision-maker.

Risk: Legal, medical, and other high-stakes questions may require professional judgment.

Mitigation: Do not treat responses as professional advice; consult a qualified professional when appropriate.
