## Use Case: <br>
External ClawPad users use this skill to turn a short meme-token idea into a structured launch blueprint with naming, narrative, visual direction, logo prompt, theme tags, and rule-based buyback-and-burn planning. The skill is framed as advisor and coordinator for predefined launch mechanics, not as investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill describes real crypto launch and post-launch fund-affecting automation without enough user-control or risk detail. <br>
Mitigation: Before use with real funds, require the surrounding platform to show exact transaction details, wallet permissions, fees, payment destination, custody and refund terms, and explicit confirmation gates. <br>
Risk: Automated buyback-and-burn behavior can affect user funds after launch. <br>
Mitigation: Provide a clear way to disable or revoke post-launch automation and make the fixed execution rules visible before confirmation. <br>
Risk: Token launch blueprints may be mistaken for investment advice or performance guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Keep mandatory disclaimers in generated outputs and prevent recommendations to buy, sell, predict prices, or promise returns. <br>
