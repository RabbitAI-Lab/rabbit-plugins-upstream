## Description:

Helps agents turn voicemails and sales calls into structured leads, recall caller history, and plan timely follow-up using BlueColumn.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and sales teams at contractors, agencies, and service businesses use this skill to capture voicemail leads, remember earlier conversations and promises, and prioritize callbacks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Call recordings, phone numbers, and lead notes are sent to BlueColumn for transcription, storage, and recall.

Mitigation: Confirm permission to upload this data and required caller notice or consent before use; limit sensitive details and confirm retention, deletion, and access controls with the service.

Risk: A BlueColumn API key is required to access the service.

Mitigation: Keep the API key secret and restrict access to authorized users.
