## Description:

Turns voicemails and sales-call recordings into structured leads and follow-up guidance using BlueColumn.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents handling inbound voicemail and call leads for contractors, agencies, and service businesses use this skill to capture inquiries, recall prior contact, and plan follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Call recordings, phone numbers, and follow-up notes are sent to and stored by BlueColumn.

Mitigation: Send only authorized, necessary lead details and check BlueColumn's retention and privacy terms before use.
