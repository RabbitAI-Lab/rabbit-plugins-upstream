## Description:

智能估算20+热门目的地旅行预算，按穷游/经济/舒适/豪华四档给出日均消费分解，含机票签证保险费用，支持多目的地性价比对比，零配置即装即用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and travel-planning agents use this skill to estimate Chinese-language travel budgets for supported destinations, including daily cost breakdowns, airfare, visa, insurance, style comparisons, and savings suggestions. It is best suited for rough planning, especially China-origin trips, rather than live booking decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat rough historical estimates as current booking prices.

Mitigation: Present budgets as planning estimates and verify current airfare, accommodation, exchange rates, and seasonal prices before booking.

Risk: Airfare assumptions are oriented toward China-origin trips and may not fit departures from other countries.

Mitigation: Use the skill primarily for China-origin planning or replace airfare assumptions with current route-specific prices.
