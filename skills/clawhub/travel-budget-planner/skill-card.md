## Description: <br>
智能估算20+热门目的地旅行预算，按穷游/经济/舒适/豪华四档给出日均消费分解，含机票签证保险费用，支持多目的地性价比对比，零配置即装即用。暑假旅行预算管理，花费规划更合理 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External travelers and travel planners use this Chinese-language skill to estimate trip budgets for supported destinations, compare budget tiers, and review cost-saving tips before booking. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Budget estimates may be mistaken for live prices. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as rough planning estimates and verify flight, lodging, visa, insurance, and exchange-rate costs before booking. <br>
Risk: Destination coverage is limited and may be overstated. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the requested destination is supported; use current external sources for unsupported, niche, or fast-changing destinations. <br>
