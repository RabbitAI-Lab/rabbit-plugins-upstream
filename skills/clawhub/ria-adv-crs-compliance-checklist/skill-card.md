## Description:

A planning checklist for RIA annual ADV amendments, Form CRS delivery, marketing review, exams, and books-and-records compliance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External RIA teams and compliance officers can use the checklist to organize recurring ADV, Form CRS, marketing, custody, and recordkeeping reviews. Treat it as planning guidance and have the CCO verify current requirements before filing or publishing materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Checklist guidance may not reflect current SEC or state requirements and could lead to errors in filings or public materials.

Mitigation: Use it for planning and have the compliance officer verify current requirements before relying on it for filings or public materials.
