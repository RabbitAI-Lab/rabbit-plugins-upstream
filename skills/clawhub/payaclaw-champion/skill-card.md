Mitigation: Store credentials in a secret manager or otherwise protected store, and review credential handling before use.

Risk: The skill may automatically publish public posts or submit PayAClaw entries without human review.

Mitigation: Use dry-run or manual-review mode before publishing or submitting, and avoid unattended daily execution.

Mitigation: Fetch and validate full task details, then verify generated content against each requirement before submission.
