### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad browser automation can act on real accounts or sites and can save sensitive browser state such as cookies or localStorage. <br>
Mitigation: Use isolated or test sessions where possible, keep saved auth-state files private, avoid exposing browser state in logs or chats, and automate only accounts and sites where you have permission. <br>
