## Description:

Creates a structured A/B test plan from a proposed change, current traffic and conversion data, and the available test window.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Data analysts and business operators use this skill to plan single-variable experiments, estimate sample size and duration, select metrics, and define launch or rollback criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approximate sample sizes or significance assumptions may lead to premature conclusions or high-impact business decisions.

Mitigation: Validate calculations and assumptions with actual experiment data or a qualified analyst before making high-impact decisions.
