## Description:

Improve factual clarity, answer structure, and source traceability for generative search content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

External content authors use this workflow to turn verified source material into clear, answer-focused content with traceable claims. They can prepare drafts for aimeGeo and, when authorized, follow its article, publishing, and later citation-observation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts or source materials may be shared with aimeGeo, and publishing can make content public.

Mitigation: Choose whether to use aimeGeo or keep work local, share only approved materials, and authorize publication separately with the specific article, destination, and account.
