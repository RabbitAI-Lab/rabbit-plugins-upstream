## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language cognitive-bias coaching skill that routes decision, learning, action, communication, influence, and management questions to domain-specific diagnostics and produces bias assessments, strategies, and wording templates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jacksu](https://clawhub.ai/user/jacksu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and coaches can use this skill to structure reflective conversations about everyday decisions, learning, action, communication, influence, and management. It helps identify likely cognitive-bias patterns and returns practical self-checks, strategies, and sample wording without making decisions for the user. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad everyday decision, learning, communication, influence, and management phrases. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when Chinese-language cognitive-bias coaching is desired, and confirm the relevant domain before applying its guidance. <br>
Risk: Decision and persuasion outputs could be mistaken for professional advice or used to pressure others. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as reflective guidance rather than legal, medical, financial, employment, or relationship advice, and avoid using influence templates to mislead or pressure people. <br>
