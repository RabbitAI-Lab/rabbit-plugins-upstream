## Description:

Helps sales reps create a forward progression plan for a stalled live deal, mapping next steps from the current state to signed contract rather than debriefing a single call, handling live objections, or pushing a final close.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales reps and managers use this skill to convert stuck B2B opportunities, especially China-market deals, into concise progression strategies with weekly actions, remaining buying stages, blocker analysis, and stop-loss checkpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated progression plans may include customer names, stakeholders, budgets, and internal deal assessments.

Mitigation: Review each progression-strategy.md before storing or sharing it, and handle it according to the organization's customer-data and deal-confidentiality rules.

Risk: The skill is China-focused and may provide poor guidance if used as a generic global sales playbook.

Mitigation: Use it for China-focused B2B sales progression, or adapt procurement assumptions, data sources, and decision-chain guidance before using it in other markets.
