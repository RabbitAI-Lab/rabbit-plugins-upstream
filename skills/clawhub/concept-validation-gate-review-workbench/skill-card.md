## Description: <br>
概念验证中心验证总监工作台 helps a validation director run 5-Gate reviews, make four-light decisions, manage blue-light conditions, and track multi-route resource allocation. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Concept validation directors and project review teams use this skill to prepare Gate reviews, consolidate technical, commercial, team, and resource inputs, record four-light decisions, and manage conditional approvals through the next stage. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Gate review outputs can influence Go/No-Go decisions, resource allocation, and IP or safety conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill's conclusions as advisory and require review by the responsible human stakeholders before acting on project status decisions. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or inaccurate technical, commercial, team, or resource inputs can lead to misleading four-light decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Check evidence completeness, record missing materials, and document assumptions in the Gate review report before finalizing a decision. <br>
Risk: IP and safety red flags may require specialist judgment beyond the skill's decision template. <br>
Mitigation: Pause or escalate reviews involving IP or safety concerns until qualified reviewers confirm the issue is resolved or mitigated. <br>
