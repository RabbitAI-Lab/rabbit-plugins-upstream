## Description: <br>
Generates professional freelance invoices in Markdown or HTML from JSON job files, including line items, discounts, tax, due dates, notes, and late-fee terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Freelancers, consultants, and developers use this skill to generate client-ready invoice files from structured billing data without external accounts or APIs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Untrusted job JSON fields can be inserted into generated HTML invoice output. <br>
Mitigation: Use trusted job JSON files and review or sanitize invoice fields before sharing HTML output or converting it to PDF. <br>
