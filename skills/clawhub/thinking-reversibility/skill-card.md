## Description:

Before heavy deliberation, classify the decision as cheap or costly to undo; decide two-way doors fast and stage one-way doors to preserve options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and decision owners use this skill to match decision process depth to reversibility, choosing fast action for low-cost reversals and staged deliberation for costly or irreversible choices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the reversibility framework as a substitute for required security, legal, data-integrity, or other correctness checks.

Mitigation: Use the skill only to choose deliberation depth; keep required correctness, security, legal, and data-integrity reviews in place.
