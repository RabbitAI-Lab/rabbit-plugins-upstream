## Description:

Turns business questions and existing data structures into evidence-backed semantic models, mappings, metric checks, and handoffs, with a CLI and local visual workbench.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[franklinxkk](https://clawhub.ai/user/franklinxkk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Data product managers and data engineers use it to reuse schemas and business context, build and review semantic models, validate joins and metric results, and prepare evidence-backed assets for agent or application consumers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
