## Use Case:

Academic authors, editors, and research assistants use this skill to evaluate SCI/SSCI manuscript titles and receive structured title revision guidance before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Title suggestions may not reflect field-specific or journal-specific publication standards.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the target journal's title guidance and recent publications in the relevant discipline.

Risk: The skill refers to analysis of high-level papers without supplying the underlying dataset.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as editorial guidance and require human academic review before adopting title claims.
