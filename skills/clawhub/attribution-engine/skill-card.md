## Description:

Helps creators, managers, and labels prepare clear credits, attribution, disclosures, AI-content labels, and platform-aware credit records before publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[otherpowers](https://clawhub.ai/user/otherpowers)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, managers, labels, and their supporting agents use this skill to draft publishing-ready credit blocks, AI-involvement descriptions, brand-relationship disclosures, and portable attribution records. It is formatting and documentation support, not a legal compliance, ownership, or license determination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform and legal disclosure guidance can become stale or ambiguous.

Mitigation: Use official sources with access dates for platform-specific facts, and route unverified details to current official documentation instead of relying on memory.

Risk: Draft credits, labels, or disclosure text could be mistaken for legal compliance, ownership, licensing, or contract advice.

Mitigation: State that outputs are drafts owned by the creator and not legal determinations; recommend counsel for ownership, licensing, unusual commercial relationships, young creators, deep fakes, and public-interest AI text.

Risk: Users may ask the agent to hide required sponsorship or AI-content disclosures.

Mitigation: Decline concealment requests while continuing to help with accurate wording that matches the user's description and current platform rules.

Risk: The companion Content ID Guide mention could be read as a required follow-on step.

Mitigation: Treat the companion-skill referral as optional ecosystem routing for future automated claims, not as a requirement to use another skill.
