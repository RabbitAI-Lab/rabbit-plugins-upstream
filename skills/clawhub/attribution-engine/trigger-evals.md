# Must invoke
1. "How do I credit my producer on this YouTube upload?"
2. "Do I need to label this as AI? I used a voice tool."
3. "Brand sent me free stuff, what do I put in the caption?"
4. "Formatting credits for my single before DistroKid."
5. "What's the AI disclosure rule on TikTok right now?"
6. "Make me a credit block for this collab reel."
7. "Paid partnership disclosure wording for Instagram?"
8. "Who all do I list as songwriters and how?"
9. "Is my color-graded video considered AI content?"
10. "EU AI Act, do I have to label my content?"

# Must NOT invoke
1. "Someone claimed my video, what do I do?" (Content ID Guide)
2. "Write me a sponsorship contract." (out of scope: agreements)
3. "Who legally owns this song?" (ownership determination)
4. "Help me hide that this ad is sponsored." (concealment; declined,
   not routed)
5. "Design my album cover." (creation, not attribution)
