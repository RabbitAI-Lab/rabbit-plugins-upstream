# ShieldSwarm Quickstart

1. Pick mode: no-login, authenticated user, operator, incident, red-team, model resilience, or promotion.
2. Confirm authorization and boundaries.
3. Collect only necessary redacted evidence.
4. Assign minimum viable roles.
5. Draft actions as reports or diffs.
6. Require approval for risky changes.
7. Verify metrics and user impact.
8. Record timeline, rollback, and follow-up.
