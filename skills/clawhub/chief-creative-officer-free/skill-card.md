## Description: <br>
Chief Creative Officer Free helps individual creators, independent designers, and small creative teams plan creative direction, define visual style guidance, and generate marketing campaign concepts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, independent designers, and small creative teams use this skill to turn brand, audience, and campaign briefs into structured creative direction, visual style guidance, and marketing concept drafts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests broad read, write, and command execution permissions for a task that is mainly creative text planning. <br>
Mitigation: Review requested permissions before installation and prefer a restricted version that removes exec and write access unless those permissions are explicitly needed. <br>
Risk: The skill includes generic automation language that may activate or be trusted outside its core creative-planning purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for creative direction, visual style guidance, and campaign concept drafting; review outputs before using them in brand or marketing decisions. <br>
