## Description: <br>
Chief Creative Officer Free helps creators, designers, and small creative teams plan creative direction, define visual style guidance, and generate marketing campaign concepts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, independent designers, and small creative teams use this skill to turn a brand, product, or campaign brief into structured creative direction, visual style guidance, and marketing concept drafts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests shell execution access that is not justified by its documented creative-planning purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installing and prefer a version that removes exec access or clearly limits and documents when local commands may be run. <br>
Risk: Creative strategy, campaign, and visual style outputs may be too generic, misaligned with brand tone, or impractical for the available budget. <br>
Mitigation: Provide a complete brief with target audience, brand constraints, tone boundaries, budget, and evaluation priorities, then review the generated direction before use. <br>
Risk: The skill produces text guidance rather than finished design assets or validated market research. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning drafts and validate them with design review, stakeholder feedback, and any required user or market research. <br>
