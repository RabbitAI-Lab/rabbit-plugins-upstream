## Description: <br>
Beat procrastination with task breakdown, 2-minute starts, and accountability tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a local productivity coach to break down avoided tasks, define two-minute starts, record blockers, track progress, and surface completed work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as 'just start' or 'stop procrastinating' may invoke the skill unexpectedly. <br>
Mitigation: Review the active context before sharing task details, and narrow usage if unexpected activation becomes disruptive. <br>
Risk: Accountability notes may include sensitive personal, health, financial, or work details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid recording highly sensitive details unless local retention is acceptable, and periodically review or remove stored task history. <br>
