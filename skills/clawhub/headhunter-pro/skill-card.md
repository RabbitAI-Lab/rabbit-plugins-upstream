## Description:

Headhunter Pro is an AI-powered recruitment toolkit for talent gap analysis, candidate screening, recommendation reports, outreach scripts, interview assessment, offer negotiation, and onboarding support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Recruiters and talent advisors use this skill to structure end-to-end recruiting workflows, including role intake, talent mapping, candidate evaluation, outreach, interview assessment, client reporting, offer support, and onboarding follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill supports broad candidate profiling and recruiting records that may include sensitive candidate or client information.

Mitigation: Use it only with explicit candidate and client consent, minimize stored data, and define retention and deletion rules before deployment.

Risk: The skill describes passive monitoring of private or semi-private candidate communication and activity signals.

Mitigation: Remove or disable passive monitoring unless legal, privacy, and compliance reviewers approve the specific sources and workflow.

Risk: The skill can generate automated outbound recruiting messages.

Mitigation: Require human review of outreach drafts and legal approval for any automated or bulk messaging workflow.

Risk: Recruiting analysis may be influenced by confidential insider information or unverifiable claims.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential insider information, document permissible sources, and verify material claims before using them in candidate or client decisions.
