## Description:

Headhunter Pro v24.0 is an AI-powered end-to-end recruitment toolkit for talent gap analysis, candidate screening, recommendation reports, outreach scripts, interview assessment, offer negotiation, onboarding support, and talent pool activation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Recruiters, talent partners, and hiring teams use this skill to structure recruiting workflows, screen candidates against job requirements, draft recommendation reports and outreach, assess interviews, support onboarding, and maintain talent pipelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Candidate screening, scoring, and profiling may use sensitive or excessive personal data.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with explicit candidate notice or consent, keep records limited to job-relevant data, and apply retention and deletion rules.

Risk: Recruiting recommendations may be influenced by protected characteristics or weakly supported assumptions.

Mitigation: Avoid protected characteristics in screening or scoring and require human review before any hiring, rejection, compensation, or outreach decision.

Risk: External platform use and persistent monitoring are under-disclosed in the release security summary.

Mitigation: Disable automated platform monitoring unless separately approved, disclosed, and compliant with platform and privacy requirements.

Risk: Live GitHub tokens or other credentials could be exposed if pasted into chats, prompts, or logs.

Mitigation: Do not paste live tokens into the skill workflow; use approved secret-management practices outside the conversation.
