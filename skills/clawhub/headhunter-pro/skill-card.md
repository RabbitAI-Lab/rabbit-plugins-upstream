## Description:

Headhunter Pro v15.0 is an AI-powered end-to-end recruitment toolkit for talent gap analysis, candidate screening, recommendation reports, outreach scripts, interview assessment, offer negotiation, and onboarding support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Recruiters, recruiting teams, and talent consultants use this skill to structure candidate sourcing, screening, outreach, interview evaluation, offer negotiation, client advisory work, and post-hire follow-up.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can process sensitive candidate, resume, contact, interview, background-check, and onboarding information.

Mitigation: Use only approved recruiting datasets and channels, minimize candidate data in prompts and outputs, and apply retention, deletion, and access controls before operational use.

Risk: The skill includes outreach, monitoring, follow-up, and re-contact workflows that could affect candidate consent and communications compliance.

Mitigation: Require human approval before outreach or monitoring, respect contact-source limits, and document candidate consent and opt-out handling.

Risk: The security assessment flags under-scoped automation and misleading safety claims.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and add organization-specific controls for recruiting policy, privacy, and legal review.
