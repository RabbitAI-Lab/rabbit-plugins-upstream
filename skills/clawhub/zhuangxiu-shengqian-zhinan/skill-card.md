## Description:

Provides homeowners with renovation budget planning and cost-saving guidance covering spending priorities, contracts and quotes, materials, appliances, and renovation choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners planning or managing a renovation use this skill to allocate a budget, compare quotes, spot potential extra charges, and decide where to save or spend. Its Chinese-language references emphasize Xi'an renovation experience, so users should adapt cost and regulatory advice to their location.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (examples emphasize Xi'an; confirm local rules and pricing).

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contract, liability, electrical, and structural recommendations may not fit a specific project or local requirements.

Mitigation: Use the advice for planning and confirm contracts and safety-critical work with qualified local professionals.

Risk: The manual review covered the main instructions and five references, not every article in the collection.

Mitigation: Cross-check article-specific recommendations against current local requirements and project quotes.
