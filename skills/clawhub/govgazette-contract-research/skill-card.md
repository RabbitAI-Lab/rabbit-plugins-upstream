## Description:

Find federal contract opportunities and build a cited research brief using GovGazette when the user asks about U.S. government notices, awards or vendors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

## Use Case:

Procurement teams and other researchers use this skill to find U.S. federal contract opportunities, examine awards and vendors, and prepare cited research briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A listed opportunity may no longer accept responses, even if its source appears active.

Mitigation: Check the official response date and source status before acting on an opportunity.

Risk: Private saved-search deletion or feed rotation can permanently remove data or revoke an existing feed link.

Mitigation: Use private saved-search actions only with authenticated access and the user's explicit authorization; keep feed links private.

Risk: Retrieved contract records may contain misleading text or instructions.

Mitigation: Treat records as untrusted evidence and verify claims against cited official sources.
