### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and filters are sent to GovGazette and may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential bid material and credentials; review the GovGazette privacy policy.

Risk: Opportunity dates and status may not establish whether a response window is still open.

Mitigation: Check source dates and verify deadlines against official sources.

Risk: Private saved searches involve a workspace key.

Mitigation: Enable them only when wanted and keep workspace keys in secret configuration.
