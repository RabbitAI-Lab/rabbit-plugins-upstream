## Description:

Find federal contract opportunities and build a cited research brief using GovGazette when the user asks about U.S. government notices, awards or vendors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users researching U.S. federal contracting can use this skill to find opportunities, awards, and vendors, then prepare a cited brief with evidence-based next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and filters are sent to GovGazette.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential bid material; review the GovGazette privacy policy before searching.

Risk: Records and deadlines may be incomplete or change over time.

Mitigation: Check official sources and response deadlines before acting; an active source status alone does not establish that a response window is open.

Risk: Private saved searches require a workspace key.

Mitigation: Use private saved searches only with explicit user intent, and supply the key through a secret header rather than chat.
