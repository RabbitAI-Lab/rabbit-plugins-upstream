## Description:

Produce an evidence-based article package with platform variants and reviewable assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, content teams, and their agents use this skill to turn a brief and verifiable sources into a reviewable article package, platform-specific drafts, and publishing handoff materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsupported claims or outdated platform requirements could make an article inaccurate or unsuitable for its target platform.

Mitigation: Tie factual claims to checked sources, verify current platform requirements, and label unknown limits for follow-up.

Risk: Using aimeGeo workflows could expose unapproved source material or publish to the wrong account.

Mitigation: Share only material approved for the service, confirm the target account and publishing authorization, and stop at account mismatch or a security challenge.
