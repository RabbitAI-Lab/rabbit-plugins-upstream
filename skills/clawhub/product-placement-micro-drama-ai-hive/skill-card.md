## Description:

## Use Case:

External marketers, e-commerce sellers, short-drama producers, and content agencies use this skill to convert product facts, audience constraints, platform needs, budget, and authorized assets into reviewable short-drama concepts, scripts, prompts, generation tasks, and continuity checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-selected media may be uploaded to AI-HIVE for generation.

Mitigation: Confirm asset rights and avoid uploading private, infringing, or unauthorized reference media before running generation commands.

Risk: Initialization or generation can rely on an AI-HIVE API key stored locally or provided through the environment.

Mitigation: Use placeholders in examples, keep credentials out of logs and repositories, and review local key storage before execution.

Risk: Image and video generation may incur cost and create misleading advertising if product claims are unverified.

Mitigation: Confirm prompts, routing, budget, factual claims, platform disclosures, and human review before submitting paid generation tasks.
