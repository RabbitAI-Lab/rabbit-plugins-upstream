## Description:

Draft, preview, and publish or schedule posts on a user's personal LinkedIn profile through PostWire, with optional images or a PDF carousel.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perufitlife](https://clawhub.ai/user/perufitlife)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and their agents use this skill to draft and review personal LinkedIn posts, then publish or schedule them through a connected PostWire account after explicit approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting PostWire grants a third-party service access to publish or schedule on a personal LinkedIn profile.

Mitigation: Connect only if comfortable granting that access, and review the post before approving publication.

Risk: An approved post could contain unwanted text or media, go out at the wrong time, or reach additional platforms.

Mitigation: Check the exact text, media, scheduled time, and platform list before saying yes, especially when cross-posting is suggested.
