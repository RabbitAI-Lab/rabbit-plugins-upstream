## Description:

solo-os helps solo developers and small teams run expert roundtable debates, votes, verification, and confirmation before project decisions are recorded or applied.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, solo maintainers, and small teams use this skill to structure architecture, priority, and trade-off decisions as expert roundtable discussions with recorded votes and human confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may propose updates to project decision records, plans, specs, workflow files, and indexes.

Mitigation: Keep the confirmation step meaningful and review proposed document or hook changes before allowing them to land.
