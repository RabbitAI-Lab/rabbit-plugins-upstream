## Description:

Guides solo developers and small teams through expert-style decision roundtables that compare options, identify missing evidence, and leave final decisions to the human.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Solo developers and small teams use this skill to weigh architecture, priority, and trade-off decisions through structured debate, evidence checks, and documented human-confirmed outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source material sent to an optional outside model may disclose sensitive information to a third-party provider.

Mitigation: Remove credentials, customer data, and private configuration before sharing material with the outside model.

Risk: Simulated expert agreement may be mistaken for verified evidence or a final decision.

Mitigation: Check factual claims against source documents, record missing evidence, and require human confirmation before documenting or acting on a decision.
