## Description:

Facilitates expert roundtables with distinct perspectives to debate and vote on project decisions before recording human-approved outcomes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo developers and small teams use this skill to review architecture, priority, and trade-off decisions through structured debate, fact checks, voting, and approved documentation updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposed decisions or document cascades could alter sensitive plans or authorization records.

Mitigation: Review affected documents and approve decisions before allowing persistent changes.

Risk: Proposed persistent rules, hooks, or CI gates could affect future project behavior.

Mitigation: Review their scope and effects before enabling them, especially in repositories with sensitive records.
