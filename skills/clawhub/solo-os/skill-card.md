## Description:

Guides solo developers and small teams through expert-style debates, evidence-annotated votes, and human-confirmed project decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo developers and small teams use this skill to weigh architecture, priorities, and trade-offs through a simulated expert roundtable, then record decisions after human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An optional outside-model review may disclose private project material to another provider.

Mitigation: Remove credentials, customer data, and private configuration before sending any material; share only a read-only copy.

Risk: Simulated consensus or unsupported claims may lead to incorrect decisions or document changes.

Mitigation: Check factual claims against source documents, distinguish verified votes from reasoned ones, and confirm decisions before persisting changes.
