## Description:

通过理解用户查询、检索并分析相关合金数据（可选通过 MCP 工具），生成专业的合金成分检索回应，并以清晰的筛选、分类与洞察呈现结构化成分表。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, patent analysts, and materials engineers use this skill to search, filter, classify, and summarize alloy composition data from user queries and available literature or MCP results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Alloy queries and provided research data may be processed through the disclosed MCP tools.

Mitigation: Confirm that the intended users and data are suitable for processing through those tools before installation or deployment.

Risk: Incomplete alloy or literature data could lead to overstated matches or unsupported composition claims.

Mitigation: Use exact values or ranges when available, disclose missing data and partial matches, and avoid fabricating composition data or sources.
