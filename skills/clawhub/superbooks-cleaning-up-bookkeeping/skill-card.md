## Description:

Helps SuperBooks users categorize transactions, match receipts and bills to purchases, find documents, and review bookkeeping changes before applying them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SuperBooks users review uncategorized spending, correct transaction categories or statuses, match receipts and bills, and locate supporting documents. The skill presents proposed changes for approval before updating records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An incorrect category or status update can change financial records.

Mitigation: Review each proposed change or batch carefully and approve only intended updates.

Risk: A receipt or bill could be matched to the wrong transaction.

Mitigation: Check the amount and date of proposed matches before approving them.
