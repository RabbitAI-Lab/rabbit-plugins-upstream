## Description:

Helps organize SuperBooks transactions and receipts by proposing categories, matching documents, and applying approved bookkeeping changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing business bookkeeping use this skill to review uncategorized transactions, propose or correct categories and statuses, match receipts and bills, and locate supporting documents in SuperBooks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect categories or receipt matches could affect business bookkeeping records.

Mitigation: Review each proposed change or batch and approve only correct categories and matches before updates are applied.

Risk: The skill can inspect business transactions, receipts, categories, and documents.

Mitigation: Confirm that access to these SuperBooks records is intended before connecting or using the skill.
