## Description: <br>
Antifragile helps agents stress-test systems, businesses, portfolios, and strategic plans for hidden fragility using exposure classification and Taleb-inspired design moves. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and operators use this skill to evaluate whether a plan, portfolio, business, or operational system is fragile, robust, or antifragile under stress. It is most useful for structured stress-testing under uncertainty and should not replace current facts or expert judgment for money, safety, or operational decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the skill's stress-testing output as financial, business, safety, or operational advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as a reasoning aid and check high-stakes decisions against current facts and qualified expert judgment. <br>
Risk: The skill can overstate confidence when classifying complex systems as fragile, robust, or antifragile. <br>
Mitigation: Review the exposure classification, bounded-downside claim, and cited assumptions before relying on the audit. <br>
