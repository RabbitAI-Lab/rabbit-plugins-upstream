# POC Contract Entry Guide

## Required information

| Category | Minimum required | Acceptable alternatives | Unacceptable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Issues | Confirmed users, tasks, roadblocks, and impacts | Clear re-certification plan | Feature aspirations only |
| Business goals | The next decision the POC will influence | Directional goals plus baseline acquisition plan | “Demonstrate AI capabilities” |
| Users | Actual users and business confirmers | Controlled proxy users and description of restrictions | Only sales or project managers can answer on behalf of |
| Data | Data samples, quality, sensitivity levels and owners | Synthetic/de-identified data and declare extrapolation restrictions | Unknown sources and permissions |
| System | Integrated objects, access methods, environmental constraints | Mock interface does not clearly prove real integration | Accessible by default |
| Resources | Persons in charge of both parties, time and frequency of feedback | Clarify the person to be confirmed and the deadline | "Coordinate at that time" |
| Risk | Initial security, privacy, legal, and procurement review | An escalation path exists | Silent bypass |

## Pre-Contract Meeting Recommendations

Participants include at least: business sponsors, target user representatives, customer technology/data leaders, and FDE leaders; for high-risk scenarios, add security, privacy, legal affairs, or compliance.

Pre-meeting materials: problem discovery package, status quo baseline, sample data, system inventory, time constraints, customer decision nodes.

## Key clarifying questions

1. Which investment, procurement, design, or go-live decision will change after the POC is over?
2. Which three signals are most likely to prove useful in real work?
3. What failures would immediately stop a POC? What can be adjusted within the range?
4. Who, what data and what access must the customer provide and when?
5. What content has not been proven even if the demo is successful?
