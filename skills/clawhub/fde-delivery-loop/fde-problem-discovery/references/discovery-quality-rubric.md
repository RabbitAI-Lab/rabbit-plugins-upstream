# Problem discovery quality score

## Rating dimensions

| Dimensions | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| User roles | Generally referred to as "users" | There are roles but unclear responsibilities | Users, decision-makers, and risk people are clear |
| Real tasks | Only wishes | Some steps | The latest end-to-end task can be reproduced |
| Evidence | Opinion only | Single source | Behavior/observation/data cross-validation |
| Problem boundaries | Include solutions | Generally clear | Scenarios, non-goals and upstream and downstream are clear |
| Impact | "Inefficiency" | Directional | Have a metric definition, baseline or quantitative plan |
| Alternatives | Undocumented | Know your existing tools | Understand why you are still using them and the cost of switching |
| Constraints | Unknown | List Parts | Technical, Data, Regulatory and Organizational Complete |
| Counter-evidence | None | General risks | Specific counterexamples and alternative explanations |
| Customer investment | No one is responsible | Contact person | Verifiable user, business, and technology commitments |
| Next decision | Directly recommend construction | Still needs research | Entry/renewal/stop conditions are clear |

It is recommended to enter the POC contract only if you have a score of 16 or above and pass the hard gate. A total score of 0 for data permissions, customer input, or problem evidence is not allowed to pass.

## Found package review issues

- If I delete all the words "AI, agents, automation", does the question still hold?
- Can you identify the date, people and materials of the most recent occurrence?
- Is the respondent describing his or her own behavior or what others should do?
- Is the pain point just a necessary risk control in the process?
- Who will the value belong to after the settlement, and who will bear the additional costs?
- Are there simpler options such as training, policies, content or traditional automation?
- What piece of evidence would make the team stop going in this direction?

## Common low quality output

“Customer service is slow to respond, so AI automated responses are needed.” Mixed problem, cause, and solution; no user, scenario, frequency, impact, or evidence.

Rewritten as: "Billing ticket entered after 17:00 on working days are manually queried by night shift customer service in three systems, and the median first response time in the past four weeks is 9.2 hours; 28% were reassigned due to missing customer levels or contract terms. The ticket log and three follow-ups support this conclusion."
