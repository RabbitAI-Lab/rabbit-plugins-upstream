# User research and interviews

Used when first-hand research information is needed; the output can be directly fed to [requirement-analysis.md](requirement-analysis.md)for requirements analysis, or used as the basis for the PRD background chapter.

---

## 1. User interview script design

### Opening (2-3 minutes)

-Introduce yourself and the purpose of the interview (learning, not selling)
- Set expectations: "There's no right or wrong answer, we just want to learn from your experience."
- Obtain recording permission (if applicable)

### Ice-breaking phase (5 minutes)

- "Please describe your job responsibilities and what a typical day is like."
- Goal: Build trust and understand interviewee background

### Core Exploration: JTBD (15-20 minutes)

**Current situation and behavior** (past tense):

- "Please take me back to your last [incident]."
- "What tools or methods did you use?"
- "How long did it take? Who was involved?"

**Pain points and frustrations**:

- "What was the hardest part of that process?"
- "If you had a magic wand, what would you change?"

**Expected Output**:

- "What does 'doing well' in this field look like to you?"

**Willingness to Pay**:

- "How much time/money are you currently spending on this?"

### Questioning skills

- **"Can you elaborate a little more?"** — Expand any topic
- **"Why?"** — dig deep into the root cause
- **"Can you give me a specific example?"** — Moving from opinion to fact
- **"What happened next?"** — Follow the story

### Mom Testing Principles

- Ask about **their lives**, not what you think
- Ask about the past, not the future
- **Speak less and listen more** — Assigned by80/20
- **Never sell during the interview**
- Pay attention to **strong emotions** — they represent real pain points
