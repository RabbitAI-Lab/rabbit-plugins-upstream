## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to evaluate compositions, artworks, or designs for governed abundance, multiple simultaneous perspectives, fractal self-similarity, and the balance between variety and order.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer subjective critique or composition language toward a single philosophical aesthetic.

Mitigation: Use it as one interpretive lens and review outputs against the intended audience, medium, and evaluation goals.

Risk: Generated critiques may overstate structural unity, fractal similarity, or compossibility when the source work does not support those claims.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to cite visible evidence from the work before accepting evaluative claims.
