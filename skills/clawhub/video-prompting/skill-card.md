## Description:

Guides the creation of video and motion prompts with scene dramaturgy, camera direction, physics-safe motion, frame anchors, and clip continuity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to write and review prompts for short generated videos, including camera movement, audio, reference frames, and multi-clip continuity.
