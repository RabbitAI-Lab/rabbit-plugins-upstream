## Description: <br>
Use when crafting video or motion prompts for any generative model - dramaturgy, camera, physics-safe motion, frame anchors, and clip chaining. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and agents use this skill to draft and review short video-generation prompts, including motion dramaturgy, camera and lighting language, frame anchors, clip chaining, and Pruna p-video prompt variants. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may upload portraits, voices, source videos, or user photos without adequate rights or consent. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm permission for all uploaded media and review the selected provider's data handling and retention terms before use. <br>
Risk: Generated video prompts can still lead to visual artifacts, identity errors, motion failures, or misleading outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Use the included quality checklists and human review before publishing or reusing generated media. <br>
