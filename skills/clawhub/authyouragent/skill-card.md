## Description:

Browse, sign in and buy on websites through the owner's Auth Your Agent vault; the owner approves sensitive steps on their phone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People using an agent to browse websites, sign in, and make purchases through their vault, with phone approval for sensitive actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The configured vault service handles website sessions, saved secrets, and purchase workflows.

Mitigation: Confirm you trust the Auth Your Agent vault provider and configured server before installing.

Risk: Approval gates for sensitive actions depend on the configured service behaving as described.

Mitigation: Review sensitive actions and their approval requests before authorizing them.
