## Description:

Use the OOMOL-connected GPTZero service to detect whether submitted text was generated by AI and retrieve document-, paragraph-, and sentence-level scores.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can submit text to GPTZero through their connected OOMOL account and review its AI detection scores at the document, paragraph, and sentence levels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The CLI dependency may automatically install other agent skills without clear notice.

Mitigation: Review the CLI's installation behavior before setup; use a verified, pinned installation and consider the documented OO_SKILLS_SYNC_DISABLED=1 setting to disable startup synchronization.

Risk: Text submitted for detection is shared with OOMOL and GPTZero.

Mitigation: Submit only text you are authorized to share with both services.
