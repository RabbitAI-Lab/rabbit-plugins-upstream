## Description: <br>
Evaluates longevity interventions using evidence tiers and provides a research evaluation framework for supplements, sleep, exercise, protocols, and healthspan research. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[lofimichael](https://clawhub.ai/user/lofimichael) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a research-literacy aid for evaluating longevity and healthspan interventions, including supplements, sleep, exercise, protocols, papers, and biomarkers. It is educational and should not be used as a substitute for clinician review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad health, supplement, dosing, biomarker, medication, or chronic-condition guidance may be mistaken for medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only as a research-literacy aid and seek medical review before choosing doses, building supplement stacks, interpreting abnormal labs, or making medication or chronic-condition decisions. <br>
