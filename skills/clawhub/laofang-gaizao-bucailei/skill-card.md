## Description: <br>
This skill provides Chinese-language, knowledge-base-backed guidance for homeowners renovating older homes, secondhand homes, kitchens, bathrooms, utilities, demolition plans, budgets, and furnished-home updates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and renovation planners use this skill to get practical advice about old-home remodeling decisions, including demolition scope, utility upgrades, waterproofing, cost control, contractor coordination, and common renovation traps. The skill is advice-only and is not a substitute for structural drawings, property approval, local code review, or licensed professional judgment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Renovation advice about demolition, structural elements, waterproofing, utilities, or fire/HVAC systems could be mistaken for approval to perform work. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as informational only; confirm structural drawings, property rules, local code, and licensed professional review before acting. <br>
Risk: Cost ranges and renovation practices can vary by city, building age, contractor, and site condition. <br>
Mitigation: Use the guidance for planning and questions to ask, then verify prices, scope, and responsibility in local written quotes and contracts. <br>
