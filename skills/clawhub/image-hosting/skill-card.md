## Description: <br>
Upload images to img402.dev and get a public URL for sharing in messages, embedding in documents, posting to social platforms, or other contexts that require a public image link. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use this skill to upload local image files and receive public image URLs for messages, documents, social posts, and other shareable outputs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Uploaded images become publicly accessible to anyone with the URL and may be permanent. <br>
Mitigation: Upload only images intended for public access; avoid secrets, personal information, internal systems, API keys, and anything that may need reliable retraction. <br>
Risk: The skill sends user-selected image files to a third-party hosting service. <br>
Mitigation: Review the selected file before upload and confirm it is appropriate for external hosting. <br>
