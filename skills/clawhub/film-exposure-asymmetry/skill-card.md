## Description: <br>
Guides agents in explaining exposure strategy differences between negative film and reversal film, especially for high-contrast analog photography scenes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and photography-focused agents use this skill when discussing film exposure latitude, deciding whether to bias exposure for shadows or highlights, or comparing negative film with slide film. It is scoped to analog film workflows and should not be applied directly to digital sensor exposure behavior. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may apply film-specific exposure guidance to digital photography where sensor latitude behaves differently. <br>
Mitigation: State that the guidance is for analog film workflows and redirect digital camera questions to digital exposure guidance. <br>
Risk: High-contrast reversal film scenes can be damaged by small exposure errors because the artifact describes narrow slide-film latitude. <br>
Mitigation: Recommend bracketing exposures and prioritizing important highlights when advising on reversal film. <br>
