## Description:

Guides Chinese-language feasibility studies using structured research, assumption checks, scoring, and decision gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business teams, founders, and analysts use this skill to research project feasibility, test critical assumptions, compare options, and prepare evidence-backed go/no-go recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential project information may be saved with local research materials.

Mitigation: Agree on storage locations first and avoid retaining sensitive raw documents unnecessarily.

Risk: Web research may collect information outside permitted legal or site-policy boundaries.

Mitigation: Keep collection within applicable laws and website policies.
