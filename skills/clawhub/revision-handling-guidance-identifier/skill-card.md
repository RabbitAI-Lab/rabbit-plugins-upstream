## Description:

Generates account merge guidance from a supplied revision profile.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing account updates use this skill to turn a supplied revision profile into concise guidance on merging incoming fields and preserving existing fields.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect or unintended revision preferences could produce misleading merge guidance.

Mitigation: Review the supplied revision profile and resulting guidance before applying it to an account.
