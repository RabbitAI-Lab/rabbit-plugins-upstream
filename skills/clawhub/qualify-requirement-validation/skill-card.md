## Description:

Helps sales reps validate whether an expressed customer need is real, urgent, owned by the right stakeholders, and aligned with the team's capability before moving to ROI proofing or buying-intent assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales reps and revenue teams use this skill to structure China-focused B2B need validation from customer conversations, meeting notes, outreach follow-up, or customer-initiated requests. It produces an evidence-based determination of true pain, partial truth, false pain, or low urgency, with concrete next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific sales assumptions may be misapplied to non-China prospects, regulated industries, or multilingual teams.

Mitigation: Confirm the customer's region, applicable laws, privacy expectations, and local sales context before using the guidance outside its intended China B2B workflow.

Risk: Incomplete customer evidence can lead to incorrect need classification or premature sales actions.

Mitigation: Require customer quotes, source labels, confidence levels, role mapping, and missing-field markers before acting on a validation report.
