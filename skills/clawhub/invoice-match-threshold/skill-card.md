## Description:

Helps derive an invoice-match threshold from a reconciliation rule and apply a selected threshold to invoice candidates to produce a reconciliation summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance, operations, and accounting users use this skill for routine invoice reconciliation work: deriving a match threshold from permitted variance rules and applying that threshold to invoice candidates for a reconciliation summary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Invoice or reconciliation data may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Only provide invoice or reconciliation data you are comfortable using with this skill.

Risk: A generated threshold or reconciliation summary may be unsuitable for accounting decisions if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review the threshold and summary before relying on them for accounting decisions.
