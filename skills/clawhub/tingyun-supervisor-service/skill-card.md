## Description:

Helps families organize guardianship supervision with duty checklists, asset oversight guidance, and supervisor report templates; it does not provide professional legal, tax, or medical advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, including families arranging guardianship supervision, use this agent to organize supervisor duties, recordkeeping, asset checks, and next steps. It flags urgent or professional matters for review by qualified local professionals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Family documents may expose sensitive identity, financial, medical, or third-party information when shared with an AI service.

Mitigation: Share only a minimal, de-identified summary; redact names, identification and account numbers, signatures, and unrelated medical or third-party details from necessary excerpts. Confirm disclosure is authorized, check the AI service's privacy settings, and have qualified professionals review consequential arrangements.
