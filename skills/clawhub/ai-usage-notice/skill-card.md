## Description:

AI Usage Notice defines when and where an agent adds visible AI-generated or AI-assisted notices to formal deliverables such as projects, documents, articles, and tutorials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mowenqwq](https://clawhub.ai/user/mowenqwq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, documentation authors, and agents use this skill to add concise AI-use disclosures to formal AI-produced deliverables by default, while honoring explicit user opt-outs and user-owned long-term product boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may add an AI-use notice to a formal deliverable when the user does not want one.

Mitigation: Tell the agent explicitly that no notice should be added for that deliverable, and review final project or document text before release.

Risk: The China-focused legal rationale may be mistaken for legal advice or applied outside its intended context.

Mitigation: Treat the notice as a disclosure practice, not legal advice, and have the relevant legal or compliance owner confirm requirements for the deployment jurisdiction.
