## Description:

Order work from one engineer in Limassol, Cyprus: a neutral technical verdict on another party's deliverable (250 EUR), infrastructure diagnostic and rescue for your own systems (200/450 EUR), a signed on-site observation in Cyprus (from 200 EUR). Free feasibility answer within 1 hour 07:00–18:00 Cyprus time; pay only an accepted task, escrow on Base recommended. Use when a task needs independent verification, hands-on infrastructure help, or a person in Cyprus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
