# Passenger Rights by Jurisdiction — Complete Rule Tables

## EU Regulation 261/2004 (EU261)

**Applies when:** departure from any EU airport (any carrier), OR arrival in the EU on an EU-licensed carrier. Includes Iceland, Norway, Switzerland. Brexit created parallel UK rules (below).

### Cash compensation (delays ≥3h at final destination, cancellations without proper notice, denied boarding)

| Distance | Amount |
|---|---|
| ≤ 1,500 km | €250 |
| EU flight > 1,500 km, or other flight 1,500–3,500 km | €400 |
| > 3,500 km (non-EU destinations) | €600 |

- Compensation is **halved** (50%) if rerouting arrival delay stays under 4h for the >1500km tiers / under 2h for ≤1500km.
- Distances measured great-circle from origin to **final destination** (connecting itineraries: where you end up, not the leg).

### Delay care entitlements (starts even before 3h)

| Delay | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| ≥ 2h (≤1500km) / ≥3h (1500-3500) / ≥4h (>3500) | Meals, refreshments, 2 communications |
| Overnight | Hotel + transport |
| ≥ 5h | Full refund option instead of waiting |

### Cancellation notice rules

| Notice before departure | Rerouting timing | Compensation due? |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 14 days | any | No |
| < 14 days, ≥ 7 days | departs ≤2h early, arrives ≤4h late | No |
| < 7 days | departs ≤1h early, arrives ≤2h late | No |
| less | arrives ≤2h late | No (but €125-€240 tiers apply if more) |

### Denied boarding (involuntary bump)

Always compensation at full distance-tier rates **plus** choice of refund or rerouting — even if you volunteered (volunteers negotiate separately; don't accept below the involuntary floor).

### Extraordinary circumstances (airline defense)

Weather, ATC strikes (airport-wide), security risks, medical emergencies, bird strikes (some readings), manufacturing defects discovered at inspection. **NOT included:** routine technical faults, crew scheduling problems, airline staff strikes, overbooking (by definition), "operational reasons."

Burden of proof is on the airline, and they must show they took *all reasonable measures*.

### Deadlines

EU261 itself sets none; member-state statutes of limitations apply: ranges from 1 year (e.g., some interpretations in Spain practice varies) to 3 years (Germany §195 BGB, France itinerary-dependent), 6 years (UK contract claims). File early.

## UK261 (retained EU261, post-Brexit)

Same structure, sterling amounts: £220 / £350 / £520. Applies to departures from UK/EU airports and UK-registered carrier arrivals into the UK. 6-year limitation (England/Wales), 5 (Scotland).

## US DOT Rules (14 CFR Part 250/259)

- **Delays/cancellations: no federal cash compensation exists.** Airline commitments are customer-service plans (meals after 3h, hotel for overnight cancellations within control — check each carrier's published plan; the DOT dashboard tracks these promises).
- **Denied boarding (involuntary bump):**

| Arrival delay of substitute transport | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 0 to <1 hour | none |
| 1 to <2 hours (domestic) / 1-4 (international) | 200% of one-way fare, max \$675 |
| ≥2 hours (domestic) / ≥4 (international) | 400% of one-way fare, max \$1,350 |

(DOT inflation-adjusts caps; engine uses recent figures, verify current caps when filing.)
- **Tarmac delays:** 3h domestic / 4h international = must allow deplaning.
- Delayed baggage: domestic liability cap ~\$3,800 (adjustable); no fixed timetable for payment but airlines must process claims.

## Canada APPR (Air Passenger Protection Regulations)

For flights to/from/within Canada:

| Situation | Large carrier | Small carrier |
|---|---|---|
| Delay 3-6h | CAD 400 | CAD 125 |
| 6-9h | CAD 700 | CAD 250 |
| ≥9h | CAD 1000 | CAD 500 |

Cancellation within 14 days → compensation tiers similar. Extraordinary circumstances exclusion is broader than EU (includes some tech diversions) and heavily litigated. File with the airline first, then the Canadian Transportation Agency.

## Brazil ANAC (Resolução 400)

- Delay ≥ 4h: airline must offer rebooking, refund, or completion of trip; **no fixed statutory cash amount** — material damages claimable via consumer law (small claims court practice is common and successful).
- Denied boarding: negotiation + assistance; consumer law damages.

## Turkey SHY-Passenger

Mirrors EU261 (€250/€400/€600, 3h threshold). Applies at Turkish airports and Turkish carriers.

## Montreal Convention 1999 (baggage & delay damages — 130+ states incl. US, EU, UK, China, India)

- **Baggage delay:** consequential damages (toiletries, clothes) up to ~1,300 SDR (~\$1,700-1,800, inflation-adjusted periodically).
- **Baggage loss/damage:** same SDR cap, depreciation applies.
- **Flight delay causing damages:** same cap, airline defends with "all reasonable measures."
- Claims within 21 days (delayed baggage) / 7 days (damaged) of receiving it, in writing.

## India DGCA (CAR Section 3 Series M Part IV)

- Block time ≤1h: ₹5,000 or booked fare (lower); 1-2h: ₹7,500; >2h: ₹10,000.
- Denied boarding: ₹20,000 + refund or free reroute.

## Whose jurisdiction? Quick decision tree

1. Did it depart an EU/UK airport? → EU261/UK261 applies (cash possible).
2. EU/UK carrier arriving into EU/UK? → same.
3. Canada involved (to/from/within)? → APPR may stack with EU261 — claim the richer one.
4. US domestic? → denied boarding/tarmac rules only for cash; meals/hotel via carrier plan.
5. Anywhere international + baggage issue → Montreal Convention.
