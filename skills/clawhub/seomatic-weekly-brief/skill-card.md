## Description:

Creates a short weekly SEO brief from a connected site's Google Search Console data, explaining changes and recommending one action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners and SEO teams use this skill to compare two weeks of Search Console performance, understand traffic changes, and choose one data-backed action for the week.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic grants access to the site's Google Search Console data.

Mitigation: Connect only if you are comfortable sharing that data with SEOmatic.

Risk: A broad request such as 'weekly brief' may trigger the skill unintentionally in agents that auto-load skills.

Mitigation: Use explicit SEO or Search Console wording when requesting the brief.
