## Description:

Write a short weekly SEO brief from connected Search Console data, explaining what changed, why, and one action for the week.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO teams and site owners use this skill to review weekly Google Search Console changes and choose one data-backed action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad weekly-update phrases may trigger the skill for unrelated requests and read connected Search Console data.

Mitigation: Invoke it with SEO-specific wording, such as ‘weekly SEO brief’ or ‘Search Console update.’
