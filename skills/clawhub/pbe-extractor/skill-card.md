## Description: <br>
Extract invariant principles from any text - find the ideas that survive rephrasing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, writers, researchers, and knowledge workers use this skill to distill supplied text into reusable principles, confidence-rated observations, and validation next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill analyzes user-supplied text, which may contain confidential content. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide confidential content when the configured agent and model provider are approved for that data. <br>
Risk: Extracted principles are pattern observations rather than verified truth. <br>
Mitigation: Review extracted principles before applying them and validate important findings against additional sources. <br>
Risk: Evidence notes a wording inconsistency about review-file handling. <br>
Mitigation: Only permit creation of any local review file when that artifact is explicitly wanted. <br>
