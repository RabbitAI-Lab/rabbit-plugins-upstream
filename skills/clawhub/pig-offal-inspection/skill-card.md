## Description: <br>
依据 GB/T 9959.4-2019 标准提供鲜、冻猪副产品检验判定技巧服务，覆盖猪副产品感官、理化、微生物等指标合规性检验判定全流程。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Food inspection teams, testing organizations, meat processors, and slaughter enterprises use this skill to check fresh or frozen pig by-products against GB/T 9959.4-2019. It helps structure product classification, source verification, sampling, sensory checks, reinspection, and compliance conclusions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, for workflows that apply GB/T 9959.4-2019 and related Chinese food-safety standards. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Inspection conclusions may be outdated or incomplete if official food-safety standards, laboratory procedures, or regulatory requirements have changed. <br>
Mitigation: Verify conclusions against current official standards and qualified food-safety procedures before using them for compliance decisions. <br>
Risk: Users may treat guidance as a substitute for certified laboratory or regulatory judgment. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as inspection support only and route final determinations through qualified inspectors, laboratories, or regulatory personnel. <br>
