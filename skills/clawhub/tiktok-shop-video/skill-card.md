## Description:

Creates TikTok Shop video scripts and shot lists for sellers and affiliates, with optional AI-generated product b-roll and platform compliance guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

TikTok Shop sellers and affiliates use the skill to create shoppable video scripts and phone-shot plans, plan content, and troubleshoot video reach or policy concerns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional AI footage processing may send product photos to an external service and incur usage costs.

Mitigation: Use public or explicitly authorized images, review the cost estimate, and approve the cost before generating footage.

Risk: TikTok Shop requirements can change and vary by market.

Mitigation: Check the current requirements in Seller Center before posting.

Risk: Related skills, installer code, and remote services were not included in this review.

Mitigation: Use a single-skill installation and review the installer and source revision before use.
