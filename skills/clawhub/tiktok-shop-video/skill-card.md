## Description:

Creates TikTok Shop scripts and shot lists for shoppable or affiliate videos, with optional AI-generated b-roll and a 9:16 MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

TikTok Shop sellers and affiliates use it to turn a product link into a video script and phone shot list, plan content, or review why a Shop video is underperforming or received a violation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary flags an unpinned installer as a supply-chain concern.

Mitigation: Review the installer source and pin it to a version you trust before running it.

Risk: The optional b-roll workflow can send product photos to external generation services.

Mitigation: Check the photos and destination first, remove sensitive content or metadata, and approve the data transfer and any cost.
