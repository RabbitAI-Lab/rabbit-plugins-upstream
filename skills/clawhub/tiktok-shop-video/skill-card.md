## Description:

Creates TikTok Shop video scripts and shot lists for sellers and affiliates, with optional AI-generated product b-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

TikTok Shop sellers and affiliates use the skill to plan and script product videos, prepare phone-shoot shot lists, and optionally create supporting AI b-roll. It also provides guidance for reviewing content and improving posts after publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional AI b-roll sends product photos and prompts through Varg and generation services and may consume credits; generated shots can also misrepresent product details.

Mitigation: Use product materials you are authorized to share, review and approve the cost estimate first, check generated shots against the real product, and label AI-generated content as required.

Risk: TikTok Shop requirements vary by market and may change, so outdated guidance could lead to rejected or penalized posts.

Mitigation: Check the current TikTok Shop Seller Center rules for the intended market before posting.
