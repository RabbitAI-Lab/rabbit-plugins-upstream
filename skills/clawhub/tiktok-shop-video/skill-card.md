## Description:

Creates TikTok Shop scripts and phone shot lists, with optional AI-generated product b-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

TikTok Shop sellers and affiliates use this skill to plan compliant shoppable videos, write scripts, and prepare phone-shot lists. It can also help creators review content plans and troubleshoot low reach or policy violations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented installation command is unpinned.

Mitigation: Inspect the installer and pin it to a reviewed version before running it.

Risk: Product photos may be sent to external image or video generation services for b-roll.

Mitigation: Confirm the transfer is approved and use public or sanitized product images when appropriate.

Risk: Scanner coverage is incomplete because a local path-like reference could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and relevant files manually before relying on the scan result.
