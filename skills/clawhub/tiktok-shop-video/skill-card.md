## Description:

Creates TikTok Shop video scripts and shot lists for sellers and affiliates, with optional AI-generated product b-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

TikTok Shop sellers and affiliates use this skill to plan shoppable product videos, including scripts, shot lists, and optional AI-generated b-roll.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional AI visual generation sends selected product photos and prompts through Varg and the selected providers.

Mitigation: Use only images you are authorized to share and review the privacy terms of the services you select.

Risk: AI b-roll can incur a per-video cost.

Mitigation: Review and approve the quoted generation cost before starting AI visual generation.

Risk: TikTok Shop requirements can vary by market and may change; some referenced companion skills and external services were not part of this review.

Mitigation: Check the current Seller Center rules for your market and review companion skill or service guidance before relying on it.
