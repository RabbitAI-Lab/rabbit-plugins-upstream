## Description:

Creates TikTok Shop video scripts and phone shot lists from product links, with optional AI-generated product b-roll.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sellers and affiliates use this skill to plan shoppable TikTok Shop videos, write scripts and phone shot lists, and review common content-policy constraints. It can also suggest optional product b-roll and follow-up content based on video performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented installer command is not version-pinned.

Mitigation: Check the source and release before installing, and pin a reviewed version when the installer supports it.

Risk: Optional AI b-roll workflows may share product photos and prompts with external generation services.

Mitigation: Use only product images and prompts you are authorized to share, and review the selected provider's data handling terms before sending them.
