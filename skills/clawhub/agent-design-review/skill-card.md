## Description: <br>
Reviews LLM agent designs for reliability, cost, and safety, producing a structured critique with prioritized findings and fixes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to review, critique, debug, or harden LLM agent architectures before launch. It helps identify reliability, tool-use, context, failure-handling, cost, and safety issues with prioritized fixes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Review prompts may include sensitive architecture, credential, or business details. <br>
Mitigation: Share only necessary design details, avoid secrets, and treat the review output as advisory until validated by the team responsible for the agent. <br>
