## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad autonomy patterns such as model routing, memory, tool discovery, and sandboxed self-improvement can lead to unintended side effects if adopted without controls.

Mitigation: Keep provider credentials scoped, run candidate changes in a sandbox, and enforce approval gates for deletion, spending, publication, legal, and other high-impact actions.

Risk: Provider and tool orchestration can expand supply-chain and dependency exposure.

Mitigation: Record provenance, licenses, hashes, dependency manifests, and model metadata; scan third-party tools before promotion.

Risk: Self-reported improvements may be unreliable without independent checks.

Mitigation: Promote changes only after objective benchmarks, regression gates, independent verification, and rollback checkpoints pass.
