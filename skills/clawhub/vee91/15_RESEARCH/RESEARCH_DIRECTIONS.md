# Research Directions Beyond V90

Potential future work:
- world-model-assisted planning
- learned routing policies with safe offline evaluation
- compiler optimization of agent workflows
- formal verification of workflow invariants
- privacy-preserving evaluation
- federated/peer worker pools
- synthetic curriculum generation
- capability reputation networks
- hardware-aware model specialization
- multimodal environment simulators
- automatic benchmark generation
- co-evolution of human workflow and agent workflow

These are research directions, not claims of solved general intelligence.
