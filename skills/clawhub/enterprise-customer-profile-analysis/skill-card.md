## Description: <br>
Generates a structured enterprise customer profile analysis report covering customer sources, customer types, industry and regional distribution, relationship stability, value tiers, structural risks, and expansion recommendations using public and lawful information. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business analysts, investment and due-diligence teams, market expansion teams, and enterprise researchers use this skill to build customer profile reports for a target company from public information. It supports analysis of customer mix, customer quality, concentration risk, regional and industry exposure, and practical customer expansion recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The report can become misleading if private customer lists, confidential business data, or ambiguous prompts are supplied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review inputs before use, avoid confidential customer data unless intentionally included, and confirm ambiguous business-analysis requests should use this customer-profile workflow. <br>
Risk: Public information may be incomplete, and inferred customer relationships can be mistaken for confirmed business relationships. <br>
Mitigation: Require source checks, preserve explicit confidence labels for confirmed, inferred, and potential customers, and review the report before using it for due diligence, investment, or market decisions. <br>
