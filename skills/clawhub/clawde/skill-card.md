## Description: <br>
AI-powered betting advisory for gaming platforms that provides bet recommendations, risk analysis, bankroll management, and strategy optimization for crash, dice, and casino games. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill for advisory-only gambling analysis, bankroll management, and strategy configuration. It should support human decision-making only and should not be treated as reliable prediction, financial advice, or authorization to place wagers automatically. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Gambling-related recommendations may be mistaken for reliable predictions or financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as advisory decision support, require user judgment, and remind users not to bet money they cannot afford to lose. <br>
Risk: Connecting recommendations to automated wagering could create uncontrolled financial loss. <br>
Mitigation: Do not connect the skill to automated wagering without explicit user-controlled limits; use stop-loss, take-profit, cooldown, and session limits. <br>
Risk: Historical game patterns do not guarantee future results. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose uncertainty in recommendations and keep bankroll, bet-size, and session controls active. <br>
