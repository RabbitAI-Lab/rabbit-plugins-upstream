## Description:

Reviews generated text for group generalizations and stereotypes, then suggests more inclusive wording before publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators, reviewers, and developers use this skill to check AI-generated or public-facing text for potential unfair generalizations and to draft less stereotyped alternatives. Findings require human review and are not legal or compliance advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Simple phrase matching can miss context or flag benign mentions of groups.

Mitigation: Have a person review findings and the full text before making consequential publishing or fairness decisions.

Risk: An audit result could be mistaken for a legal or compliance determination.

Mitigation: Use findings only as a review aid; seek appropriate professional review for regulated or consequential decisions.

Risk: The release metadata and package documents differ on the license identifier, and the package reserves rights to documentation and theory text.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable terms before reuse; do not assume the code license grants permission to redistribute the documentation.
