## Description:

Turns a book's key ideas, quotations, and structure into chart-led, publication-style reading notes with a slide-based layout.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yjkj999999](https://clawhub.ai/user/yjkj999999)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers, educators, and content creators use this skill to turn supplied book highlights and chapter outlines into visual reading notes, presentation slides, and shareable summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may apply the chart-first, Chinese-oriented format to unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Invoke explicitly when that style is wanted and check that the output suits the audience.

Risk: Marketing or persuasion-oriented notes may contain claims or framing that need editorial review.

Mitigation: Review claims, source attribution, and persuasive language before publishing.
