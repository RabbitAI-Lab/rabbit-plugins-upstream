## Description:

Transforms a book’s key points, quotations, and structure into publication-quality, chart-led reading notes with concise text and polished layouts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yjkj999999](https://clawhub.ai/user/yjkj999999)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Turn a book’s key ideas, quotations, and structure into visual notes for study, presentations, and sharing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Graphics scripts may disable browser sandboxing.

Mitigation: Do not use the affected scripts until browser sandboxing is retained.

Risk: Graphics scripts may overwrite or delete existing images or design files.

Mitigation: Work on copies, back up source assets, and preserve existing DESIGN.md files.

Risk: Optional or external image processing may be unsuitable for private documents.

Mitigation: Review optional installations and use local image processing for private documents.
