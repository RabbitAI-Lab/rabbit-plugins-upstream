## Description:

Turns a book's key points, quotations, and structure into publication-style visual reading notes using slide layouts, charts, and a 16:9 HTML template.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yjkj999999](https://clawhub.ai/user/yjkj999999)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers, educators, and content creators provide a book's main ideas, structure, and quotations to create visually organized reading-note slides and shareable exports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Book quotations or chart data may be presented without reliable attribution.

Mitigation: Verify quoted text against the book, identify its chapter, and cite sources for chart data before sharing.

Risk: Chinese-first fonts, color conventions, or export commands may not suit other audiences or environments.

Mitigation: Review and adapt the locale, fonts, financial color convention, and export commands for the intended audience and system.
