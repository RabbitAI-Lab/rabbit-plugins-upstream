## Description:

Organizes personal source texts into reusable, connected methods, scenarios, and entities through a confirmed skeleton followed by source-grounded enrichment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People organizing personal knowledge use this skill to turn source texts into deduplicated, linked scenes, concepts, and entities. They approve the initial skeleton before the agent adds detailed steps and relations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal source texts may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Provide only texts you are comfortable having the agent read and organize.

Risk: Generated knowledge and progress files may be written to an unintended folder or contain inaccurate summaries.

Mitigation: Choose the output directory deliberately and review the skeleton and source-grounded content before relying on them.
