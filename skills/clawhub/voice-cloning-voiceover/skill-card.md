## Description:

Generate voice-cloned narration from an authorized reference recording or an account voice, with timed subtitles and a per-line manifest.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yanghhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/yanghhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, educators, podcasters, and brand teams can narrate scripts with their own voice or another authorized voice and produce timed subtitles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A publicly accessible reference recording may expose private voice data.

Mitigation: Avoid publishing voice samples publicly and use only a voice you own or are authorized to use.

Risk: Advertised voice generation options may not match the service's available capabilities.

Mitigation: Check the current capabilities and preview a short line before relying on an option for a full script.
