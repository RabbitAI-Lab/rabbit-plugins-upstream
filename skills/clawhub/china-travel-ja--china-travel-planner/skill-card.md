## Description: <br>
Generates personalized multi-day China travel itineraries and supports hotel, flight, attraction-ticket, and travel Q&A requests. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[china-travel-ja](https://clawhub.ai/user/china-travel-ja) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Travelers and agents assisting travelers use this skill to build Japanese-language China trip plans, search hotels and flights, compare attractions, and answer practical travel questions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trip details, hotel or flight searches, and travel questions are sent to a third-party TripGenie proxy and responses may include affiliate booking links. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive personal information, review booking links before purchase, and verify prices and policies with the booking provider. <br>
