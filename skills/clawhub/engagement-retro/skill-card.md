## Description: <br>
Run a close-out retrospective on a client engagement to capture results, lessons, commercial reality, and the renewal, testimonial, or referral path. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Consultants, agencies, and project leads use this skill at the end of a client engagement to assess outcomes against goals, identify process and pricing lessons, and prepare follow-on, testimonial, or referral asks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include client outcomes, pricing, margin, referral, or testimonial details that they are not allowed to share with an agent. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only business details that are permitted for the agent and omit or anonymize restricted client information. <br>
Risk: Retrospective outputs may overstate results or miss margin and scope issues if inputs are incomplete or biased. <br>
Mitigation: Review the close-out against the original goals, statement of work, actual time, pricing, and client feedback before using it with a client or in future planning. <br>
