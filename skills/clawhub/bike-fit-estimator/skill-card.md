## Description:

Estimates bicycle frame and contact-point fit from rider measurements and suggests first adjustments for common ride discomfort.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Cyclists and prospective bike buyers use this skill to estimate frame size, saddle and handlebar position, standover clearance, and possible fit adjustments for discomfort during rides.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fit estimates or symptom-based advice may be mistaken for professional or medical assessment.

Mitigation: Treat guidance as educational; seek a qualified bike fitter or clinician for persistent pain, numbness, injury history, or symptoms off the bike.

Risk: Fit changes could worsen symptoms or make handling unstable.

Mitigation: Make small, reversible adjustments and stop if symptoms worsen or handling feels unstable.
