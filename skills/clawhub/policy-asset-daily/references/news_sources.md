## Search Strategy Guidelines

### For Political Policy
- Search queries should include: "国务院常务会议", "政治局会议", "新华社", site:gov.cn
- Look for press conference transcripts and official policy documents
- CCTV News Network (新闻联播) headlines are a key policy signal

### For International Macro
- Search for: "Federal Reserve", "FOMC", "ECB", "geopolitical", "trade tensions"
- Monitor central bank press conference calendars

### For Energy
- Search for: "crude oil price", "Brent", "WTI", "OPEC", "natural gas"
- Include both price movements and policy/regulatory news

### For Commodities/FX
- Search for: "gold price", "copper", "US dollar index", "DXY", "USD/CNY", "RMB exchange rate"

### For Capital Markets
- Search for: "上证指数", "深证成指", "恒生指数", "S&P 500", "Nasdaq", "Dow Jones"
- Include both index movements and major company events

## Notes on Paywalled Content

If encountering a paywall or inaccessible page:
1. Try WebFetch to retrieve publicly accessible portions
2. Search for the same news via Tier-2 sources that may republish
3. If content cannot be verified, mark as "待核实" (pending verification) rather than fabricating
