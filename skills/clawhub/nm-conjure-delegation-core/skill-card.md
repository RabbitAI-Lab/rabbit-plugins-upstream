## Use Case:

Developers and engineering agents use this skill to decide when to delegate low-complexity, high-context tasks to external LLM services and how to plan, validate, log, and troubleshoot those delegations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Delegating task context to external LLM services can expose sensitive data or security-critical implementation details.

Mitigation: Review the delegation plan before execution and avoid delegating secrets, authentication code, private customer data, or security-sensitive reviews unless the target service and scope are explicitly approved.

Risk: Delegated results may be incorrect, incomplete, or poorly matched to the requested output format.

Mitigation: Define success criteria and validation methods before delegation, then integrate results only after format and correctness checks pass.

Risk: External service failures, quotas, or authentication issues can interrupt delegated workflows.

Mitigation: Verify authentication, service reachability, quota capacity, and fallback-to-local processing before relying on a delegation.
