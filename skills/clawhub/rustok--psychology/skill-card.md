## Description:

Supportive psychological conversation grounded in a curated corpus of 11 psychotherapy schools; use when the user shares anxiety, low mood, grief, relationship or family conflict, loss of meaning, low self-esteem, procrastination, emotional overwhelm, or crisis signals, while keeping clear that it is not a substitute for professional care.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to provide supportive, structured emotional conversations for anxiety, low mood, grief, relationship or family conflict, meaning questions, self-esteem issues, procrastination, overwhelm, and crisis signals. It is intended for supportive conversation with escalation boundaries, not diagnosis, medication advice, psychotherapy, or emergency care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake supportive conversation for licensed therapy or confidential medical care.

Mitigation: Keep the AI companion disclaimer visible at the start of use and route clinical, severe, persistent, or confidential needs to live professional support.

Risk: Crisis, self-harm, minors, medication, psychosis, addiction, or acute medical concerns may exceed the skill's safe support scope.

Mitigation: Follow the crisis and referral protocols, pair crisis contacts with live specialist or emergency support, and avoid diagnosis or medication advice.

Risk: Broad emotional-support triggers and a fixed feminine persona can activate the skill or tone in contexts where an operator may not expect it.

Mitigation: Install only for agents intended to provide this companion mode, and review activation behavior and persona fit before deployment.
