## AUTHORS' ROLES IN THE SYSTEM

### Rogers — THE FOUNDATION OF CONTACT
**When:** ALWAYS at the start, when establishing contact
**What it gives:** Safety, acceptance, empathy
**How to use:** Listen → Reflect → Don't rush

> Without Rogers, any technique will be cold.

*Corpus file: `references/authors/02-rogers-client-centered.md`*

---

### Frankl — MEANING
**When:** Existential questions, "what is it all for?"
**What it gives:** Direction, hope, reframing of suffering
**How to use:** Help find the "why," even in suffering

> "Those who have a WHY can bear almost any HOW"

*Corpus file: `references/authors/01-frankl-logotherapy.md`*

---

### Yalom — DEPTH
**When:** Fear of death, loneliness, freedom, meaninglessness
**What it gives:** An honest look at the existential givens
**How to use:** Be a fellow traveler, not an expert. Here-and-now.

> Death as an awakening to life

*Corpus file: `references/authors/05-yalom-existential.md`*

---

### Beck — THOUGHTS
**When:** Depression, anxiety, negative thinking
**What it gives:** Tools for working with thoughts
**How to use:** Identify the thought → Examine the evidence → Find an alternative

> Thoughts are not facts

*Corpus file: `references/authors/03-beck-cognitive-therapy.md`*

---

### Ellis — BELIEFS
**When:** "Must," perfectionism, dependence on approval
**What it gives:** Philosophical work with irrational demands
**How to use:** The ABC model, disputing, unconditional acceptance

> "Must" → "Would prefer"

*Corpus file: `references/authors/04-ellis-rebt.md`*

---

### Linehan — SKILLS
**When:** Intense emotions, crises, dysregulation
**What it gives:** Concrete regulation skills
**How to use:** TIPP (crisis), wise mind, validation

> Acceptance AND change

*Corpus file: `references/authors/06-linehan-dbt.md`*

---

### Erickson — THE BYPASS
**When:** Resistance, stuckness, a metaphor is needed
**What it gives:** Indirect methods, stories, reframing
**How to use:** Utilize what is there. Tell a story.

> The problem contains the resource for its solution

*Corpus file: `references/authors/07-erickson-hypnotherapy.md`*

---

### Bowlby — RELATIONSHIPS
**When:** Patterns in relationships, fear of intimacy, attachment
**What it gives:** Understanding of roots in early relationships
**How to use:** Identify the attachment style, work with the internal working model

> We repeat what we learned in childhood

*Corpus file: `references/authors/08-bowlby-attachment.md`*

---

### Perls — THE PRESENT
**When:** Stuck in the past/future, suppressed emotions
**What it gives:** A return to the "here and now"
**How to use:** "What are you feeling RIGHT NOW?", the empty chair

> Awareness in and of itself is healing

*Corpus file: `references/authors/09-perls-gestalt.md`*

---

### Hayes — ACCEPTANCE
**When:** Fighting feelings, avoidance, "fusion" with thoughts
**What it gives:** Changing the RELATIONSHIP to thoughts and feelings
**How to use:** Defusion, acceptance, values as a compass

> Pain is inevitable; suffering is a choice

*Corpus file: `references/authors/10-hayes-act.md`*

---

### Satir — FAMILY
**When:** Family conflicts, couple problems, communication
**What it gives:** A systemic view, communication styles
**How to use:** Identify the survival stance, work with self-esteem

> If we heal the family, we heal the world

*Corpus file: `references/authors/11-satir-family.md`*

## RULES FOR COMBINING

### The layering principle:

1. **Layer 1: Contact** — Rogers (always)
2. **Layer 2: Understanding** — Choose an author by problem
3. **Layer 3: Action** — A technique from the chosen author
4. **Layer 4: Integration** — Add another author if needed

### Good combinations:

| Combination | When to use |
|-------------|-------------|
| Rogers + Beck | Depression with a need to be heard |
| Frankl + Yalom | Deep existential crisis |
| Bowlby + Satir | Relationship problems with family roots |
| Hayes + Linehan | Intense emotions + avoidance |
| Ellis + Hayes | Perfectionism + fighting anxiety |
| Erickson + Perls | Resistance + stuckness |
| Beck + Frankl | Depression with loss of meaning |

### What to avoid:

- Don't overload: 2–3 authors maximum per session
- Don't mix contradictions: Beck (change thoughts) + Hayes (don't change, accept) — pick one focus
- Don't forget Rogers: techniques without contact don't work

---

## INDICATIONS AND LIMITATIONS

### When NOT to use psychological approaches:

- **Acute psychosis** — refer to a psychiatrist
- **Active suicidal intent** — crisis help (see `assets/crisis-contacts.md`)
- **Severe addiction in the active phase** — specialized help
- **Medical causes** — see a doctor first

### When caution is needed:

| Situation | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| History of trauma | More Rogers, less confrontation |
| Borderline traits | Linehan as the foundation |
| Acute grief | Frankl + Rogers, don't rush to "techniques" |
| Psychosomatics | Rule out medical causes first |

## QUICK HINTS

**The person is crying →** Rogers. Just be there.

**The person is angry →** Validate (Linehan), then explore.

**The person is frozen →** Erickson (story, metaphor) or Perls ("What are you feeling right now?")

**The person is rationalizing →** Go gently toward feelings. It may be a defense.

**The person demands an answer →** Don't give one. Help them find their own.

**The person is in crisis →** Linehan: TIPP, safety, everything else later.
