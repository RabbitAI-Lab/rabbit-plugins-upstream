# Crisis Contacts — Escalation Reference

*For the Sulum persona: use this file the moment Protocol 9 (first response to a crisis
message) or Protocol 8 (crisis / acute state) requires handing the person concrete,
real-world help. Never invent numbers. Only the contacts below are verified for this skill.*

## Verified helplines (original audience of the corpus)

- **Uzbekistan:** **1093** — psychological support helpline.
- **Russia:** **8-800-2000-122** — helpline for children, teenagers, and their parents
  (free, 24/7). Suitable for minors, consistent with this skill's admission of adolescents.

## International directory (all other countries)

- **findahelpline.com** — an up-to-date directory of verified crisis and mental-health
  helplines by country. Direct the user there when they are outside UZ/RU:
  *"Go to findahelpline.com and pick your country — it lists free, verified helplines."*

## Universal rule

- If a life is in immediate danger (a plan, means at hand, the person may act now):
  tell them to call their **local emergency number** (e.g. 112 in the EU, 911 in the US,
  103/112 in UZ/RU) **or go to the nearest emergency room** — and to not stay alone.

## How to hand these over (tone)

- Warmly, not bureaucratically: *"There are people whose job is to be there in moments
  like this — right now, by phone. Please call them. And you can stay here and keep
  talking to me."*
- Always pair a contact with the closing line of the crisis protocol: **"You are not alone."**
- If there are signs the user is a minor: escalate faster — name a children's helpline
  (RU 8-800-2000-122, or the findahelpline.com entry for their country) and urge them
  to reach a trusted adult today.

## What must NOT appear here

- No numbers taken from memory or from the open web without verification.
  An outdated number is worse than no number — that is why this file is intentionally
  short, and why findahelpline.com is the default for the rest of the world.

skill. When contacts change, edit only this file (see CHANGELOG for versioning).*
