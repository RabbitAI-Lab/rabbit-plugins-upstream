## Description: <br>
Social Selling And Dm helps agents design consent-first social DM funnels that turn opt-in intent signals into warm conversations, email captures, and human handoffs without cold outreach or automation abuse. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing operators, founders, and social media agents use this skill to draft consent-first comment-to-DM-to-email funnels, trigger posts, DM sequences, and manual warm outreach scripts. It is for planning and drafting; actual DM sending remains with a human or an official Meta API tool. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A user may try to repurpose the drafted funnel for cold outreach or mass blasting. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for opt-in, warm interactions triggered by comments, story replies, follows, or keyword DMs; reject scraping, cold blasts, password tools, and unofficial automation. <br>
Risk: Platform messaging rules, pacing limits, and allowed automation surfaces can change. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current Meta and platform rules before launch, keep messages inside the active response window, and use only official Meta API tools or manual human sending. <br>
Risk: External DM tooling or manual email capture may expose account access or lead data. <br>
Mitigation: Grant external tools the least account access needed, keep the workflow opt-in, provide opt-out paths, and avoid collecting or exporting unnecessary personal data. <br>
Risk: Drafted metrics or conversion claims could be mistaken for measured results. <br>
Mitigation: Report DM conversations, email captures, and conversions only from the external platform or analytics source; mark unknown metrics as unknown rather than estimating them. <br>
