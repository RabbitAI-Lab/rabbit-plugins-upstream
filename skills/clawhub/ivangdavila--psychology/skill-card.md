## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and teachers use this skill to shape psychology-related agent responses toward level-appropriate explanations, educational rigor, APA-style citations, research quality checks, and non-diagnostic clinical boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Psychology-related answers may be mistaken for diagnosis, treatment, or individualized clinical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for education and research framing only; for medical or clinical decisions, direct users to qualified professionals and avoid diagnosing specific cases.

Risk: Educational or research responses may include unsupported studies, inaccurate citations, or overstated conclusions.

Mitigation: Verify primary sources, DOIs, statistics, and evidence strength before relying on citations or research claims, and state uncertainty when sources cannot be confirmed.

Risk: The skill defaults toward APA 7th edition citation style, which may not match every user or publication requirement.

Mitigation: Ask for or specify the required citation style when APA 7th edition is not appropriate.
