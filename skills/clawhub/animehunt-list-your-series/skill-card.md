## Description:

Guides creators through listing or claiming an indie manga, webcomic, webtoon, light novel, or anime series in the AnimeHunt directory.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[matsclaes2](https://clawhub.ai/user/matsclaes2)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators use this skill to check whether their series is already listed, understand listing requirements, and find the appropriate submission or claim path on AnimeHunt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guidance steers creators toward AnimeHunt resources.

Mitigation: Disclose that this is an AnimeHunt listing guide and let creators choose whether to use the directory.

Risk: A title search may send a creator's series title to AnimeHunt.

Mitigation: Tell the creator before searching and avoid sending unpublished or sensitive titles without consent.
