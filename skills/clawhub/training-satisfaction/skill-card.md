## Description: <br>
Manages training satisfaction surveys from questionnaire design through distribution, response collection, NPS and word-cloud analysis, trend tracking, and improvement follow-up. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Training and HR teams use this skill to design and run post-training satisfaction surveys, summarize feedback, and track assigned improvements through verification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can process trainee contact details and feedback that may identify participants. <br>
Mitigation: Collect only necessary contact data, anonymize or pseudonymize survey answers where possible, and share reports in aggregate by default. <br>
Risk: Survey responses and improvement records may be retained or viewed longer or more broadly than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Define retention, deletion, access-control, and consent rules before using the skill with real trainees. <br>
