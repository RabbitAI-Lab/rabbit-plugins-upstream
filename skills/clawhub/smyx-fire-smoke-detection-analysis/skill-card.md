## Description:

Analyzes images and videos for fire and smoke and returns detection reports for early-warning workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security operators, fire-safety teams, and developers use the skill to assess surveillance footage or images for possible flames and smoke, review reports, and query past results. Alerts require human confirmation and established emergency procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images, videos, or submitted URLs are sent to the Life Emergence cloud service.

Mitigation: Avoid private camera URLs or sensitive surveillance footage unless its data handling is acceptable.

Risk: The skill creates or reuses an online identity and stores service tokens locally.

Mitigation: Review the publisher's account model and local token storage before installation.

Risk: Fire and smoke detection is an early-warning aid, not a confirmed emergency assessment.

Mitigation: Confirm alerts and follow established emergency procedures.
