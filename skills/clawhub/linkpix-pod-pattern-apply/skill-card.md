## Description:

Helps create realistic product mockups by applying a supplied print design to clothing, hats, mugs, and other merchandise.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Online sellers and print-on-demand creators use this skill to generate merchandise mockups from print artwork and product photos or descriptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source images are uploaded to the LinkPix service for mockup generation.

Mitigation: Get approval before uploading sensitive artwork or product photos.

Risk: The skill recommends installing or upgrading external Node/npm tooling and changing PATH.

Mitigation: Review proposed environment changes and prefer a preinstalled or pinned qhkit version.

Risk: Providing service tokens in shell commands can expose them in shell history.

Mitigation: Avoid typing tokens directly into shell commands where possible and handle credentials securely.
