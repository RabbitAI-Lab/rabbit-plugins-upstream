## Use Case:

Solopreneurs, operators, and business builders use this skill to identify repetitive work, map no-code workflows, choose automation tools, test automations, and maintain them over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real automations can use broad app permissions and perform business actions such as sending emails, updating CRM or accounting records, and posting publicly.

Mitigation: Review connected app permissions, test with non-sensitive data first, and require review before enabling customer-facing, financial, or public-posting workflows.

Risk: Broken or silent automation failures can create missed tasks, incorrect records, or stale operational data.

Mitigation: Configure error notifications, test edge cases, review workflow logs weekly, and document troubleshooting steps for each workflow.
