## Description:

Analyze Chinese stock prices (A-shares, HK stocks) and provide investment recommendations. Use when the user asks about stock analysis for Chinese companies, including buying/selling recommendations and market trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zuoyunlai](https://clawhub.ai/user/zuoyunlai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to ask an agent for structured stock analysis of Chinese A-share and Hong Kong-listed companies, including current data, market context, technical signals, and buy/hold/sell commentary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce buy, hold, or sell commentary based on current stock data and market context.

Mitigation: Verify financial information independently and do not treat the output as personalized investment advice.

Risk: Generated analysis can be incorrect or misleading if market data, news, or technical interpretation is stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review current price data and relevant news from reliable sources before acting on the analysis.
