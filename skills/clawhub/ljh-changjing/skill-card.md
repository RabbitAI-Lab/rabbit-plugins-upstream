## Description:

ljh-changjing guides commerce teams through turning competitor content data into a quantified scenario opportunity map for deciding which demand scenarios to prioritize.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[handsomeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/handsomeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Commerce, marketing, and brand strategy teams use this skill to compare competitor ad-material scenarios, quantify volume and efficiency, classify scenarios, and produce a scenario opportunity map for campaign focus.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create local onboarding state and brand archive or deliverable files.

Mitigation: Before use, decide whether local file storage is allowed and tell the agent to skip onboarding or archive writes when persistence is not acceptable.

Risk: Competitor, GMV, audience, and positioning details may be commercially sensitive when persisted in the workspace.

Mitigation: Share only data permitted by the workspace storage policy and avoid confidential business details unless local retention is acceptable.
