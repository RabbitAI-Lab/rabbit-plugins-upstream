## Description:

Find what the owner knows about a person or a project across Telegram, MAX, mail and notes through memo, and record facts, memories and key documents. Use when asked about a person, a project, what was agreed, or to search messages, mail and notes together.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wirecat](https://clawhub.ai/user/wirecat)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to find cited information about people and projects across their stored messages, imported mail, notes, calendar events and meeting transcripts, and to stage supported facts or project updates for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deal, lead, or pipeline changes may be applied immediately without owner review.

Mitigation: Require explicit owner approval for each sales-record change, or restrict the integration to read-only access.

Risk: Searches may expose information from private accounts beyond the intended scope.

Mitigation: Confirm which private accounts may be searched and keep each read within that approved account scope.
