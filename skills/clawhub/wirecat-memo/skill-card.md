## Description:

Find what the owner knows about a person or a project across Telegram, MAX, mail and notes through memo, and record facts, memories and key documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Owners and their assistants use this skill to search stored conversations, mail, and notes for context about people, projects, agreements, and records, then cite the matching sources and record reviewed facts or memories.
