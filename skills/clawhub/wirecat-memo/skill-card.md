## Description:

## Use Case:

People using an AI agent with the memo CLI can search connected messages, mail, notes, meetings, and business records for cited context about people, projects, and agreements. The agent can also record selected facts and memories or make CRM changes, subject to the owner's authorization and configured permissions.
