## Description:

Find what the owner knows about a person or a project across Telegram, MAX, mail and notes through memo, and record facts, memories and key documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wirecat](https://clawhub.ai/user/wirecat)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who need an agent to recall context about a person or project can use the skill to search stored messages, mail, notes, and meeting records, then cite the matching records. It can also stage facts, memories, and project findings for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad activation and includes mailbox changes and sales record updates beyond searching and recording stored knowledge.

Mitigation: Review its activation and mailbox and sales record permissions before installing. Follow the owner's account scope and approval choices for changes.
