## Description:

Find what the owner knows about a person or a project across Telegram, MAX, mail and notes through memo, and record facts, memories and key documents. Use when asked about a person, a project, what was agreed, or to search messages, mail and notes together.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Personal assistants and knowledge workers use the skill to find cited context about people, projects, and agreements across stored messages, mail, meetings, and notes, and to stage supported facts or document references for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags potential mailbox and CRM changes and automatic identity merging that could misattribute private context.

Mitigation: Review the mailbox, CRM, and identity-merging guidance before installing; enable only the capabilities needed and review proposed record or message changes and identity matches.
