## Description: <br>
Biz Retro Analyzer turns business conversations, meeting notes, field observations, and follow-up reasoning into evidence-backed retrospectives, project analysis, stakeholder understanding maps, and next-step recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business users, consultants, founders, and project teams use this skill to turn messy meeting transcripts, notes, and observations into structured facts, project retrospectives, motive and understanding analysis, evidence audits, and validated next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process confidential business conversations, meeting notes, transcripts, or field observations supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with material the user is allowed to share with the agent, and remove or generalize sensitive names, internal identifiers, and private project details when preparing public-facing outputs. <br>
Risk: Stakeholder motive, pricing, budget, or political-positioning conclusions can affect commercial decisions if treated as facts. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those conclusions as analysis requiring human validation, preserve evidence-strength labels, and avoid commitment-shaping actions until high-impact claims are anchored and checked. <br>
