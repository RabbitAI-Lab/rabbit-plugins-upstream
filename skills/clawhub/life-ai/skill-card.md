## Description:

Helps people track health, routines, finances, and life decisions in portable local Markdown files, with periodic reviews and personalized guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[2577526231](https://clawhub.ai/user/2577526231)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill for daily wellness journaling, personalized habit planning, weekly or monthly progress reviews, and structured research before major personal decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local journals may contain sensitive health, routine, decision, or financial information.

Mitigation: Review the life-data/ folder periodically and avoid real identifiers, medical history, and exact financial values; use synthetic financial figures.

Risk: Estimated nutrition and personalized health or decision guidance may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat estimates as approximate, consult a clinician for health concerns, and keep final decisions with the user.
