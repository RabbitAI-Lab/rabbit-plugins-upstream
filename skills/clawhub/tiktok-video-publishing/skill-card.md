## Description: <br>
Configures TikTok video publishing through WoopSocial by preparing the VIDEO publish settings, writing a search-optimized caption, setting cover and disclosure guidance, and preserving boundaries around video creation, native sounds, comments, edits, and metrics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, social media operators, and marketing teams use this skill to configure a TikTok VIDEO publish, write a keyword-led caption, choose reach settings, and set truthful brand or AI disclosures before publishing through WoopSocial. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Posts may publish publicly on TikTok through WoopSocial with incorrect account, privacy, or disclosure settings. <br>
Mitigation: Review the account, privacyLevel, isBrandedContent, isYourBrand, and caption disclosure before publishing or scheduling. <br>
Risk: Paid-brand or AI-content disclosures could be false or incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Set disclosure flags truthfully, put #ad or #sponsored at the start when applicable, and reject prohibited AI uses such as fake endorsements or unlabeled deepfakes of real people. <br>
Risk: Users may expect the skill to create or edit the video, add trending sounds, pin comments, update published posts, or provide guaranteed performance metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the documented scope clear: the video file is an input, trending sounds and pinned comments are native or human steps, edits require delete and recreate, and metrics must come from native analytics. <br>
