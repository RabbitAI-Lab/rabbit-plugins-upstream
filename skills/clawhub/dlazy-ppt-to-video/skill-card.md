## Description:

ppt to video, powerpoint to video, slides to video, presentation to video — parse the deck, outline, storyboard, voiceover, build, validate. Use when the user gives a PPT / PowerPoint / Keynote and wants an explainer, pitch, courseware, or training video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan reports a benign verdict with medium confidence and partial coverage; CLI login and organization project listing are capabilities not clearly tied to video conversion.

Mitigation: Review the install and authentication instructions and confirm that organization-level project listing fits your needs before installing.

Risk: Attached local files are uploaded to dLazy media storage for processing.

Mitigation: Only attach presentation and reference files that you are authorized to share with dLazy.
