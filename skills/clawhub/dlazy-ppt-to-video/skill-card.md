## Description:

Turns PowerPoint, Keynote, and other documents into explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can turn presentations and other documents into explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos through a hosted project workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service, with attached files uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential or sensitive material unless your organization has approved the service and its terms.

Risk: The security review was partial: the CLI package and some referenced resources were not inspected, and the guidance flags login and project-listing commands plus a broad report trigger.

Mitigation: Review the CLI package and flagged commands and resources before installation or use when their behavior matters.
