## Description:

Helps turn presentations and other documents into explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos using dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business teams use this skill to develop narrated videos from slide decks and other documents, including PDFs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Confirm before uploading sensitive content and remove confidential material where possible.

Risk: The CLI may store an API key on the user's device.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key if exposed.
