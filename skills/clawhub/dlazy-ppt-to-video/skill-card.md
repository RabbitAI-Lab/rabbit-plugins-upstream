## Description:

Guides users through turning presentations and other documents into narrated videos using dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users can guide a video project from a presentation or other document, including outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, and production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Attached files, prompts, and project context may be sent to dLazy's external service.

Mitigation: Use only files approved for that service; do not upload confidential documents without organizational permission.

Risk: The workflow handles documents beyond PowerPoint despite its PPT-focused name.

Mitigation: Review the selected files and intended scope before starting a project.
