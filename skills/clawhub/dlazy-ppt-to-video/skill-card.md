## Description:

Helps turn presentations and other documents into narrated explainer, training, or pitch videos through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business teams use this skill to turn slide decks and other documents into narrated explainer, courseware, pitch, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Attached documents and prompts are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review sensitive material before attaching files and obtain approval for third-party upload.

Risk: A saved API key or continued project may expose credentials or unintended prior context.

Mitigation: Prefer per-invocation DLAZY_API_KEY when appropriate and continue only projects you recognize.
