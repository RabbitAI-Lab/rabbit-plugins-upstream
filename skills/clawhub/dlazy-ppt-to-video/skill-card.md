## Description:

Turns PowerPoint and Keynote presentations into explainer, pitch, courseware, and training video projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos use this skill to guide a presentation through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, video building, and validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Attached presentation files are uploaded to dLazy storage for processing and may contain sensitive material.

Mitigation: Attach only files approved for processing by dLazy.

Risk: Authentication can save an organization API key locally, and video generation uses service credits.

Mitigation: Review the CLI before installing, use credits intentionally, and rotate or revoke the key when it is no longer needed.
