## Description:

Helps users turn presentations into narrated explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos using dLazy's file-to-video service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and business users can turn PowerPoint or Keynote decks into narrated explainers, pitches, courseware, and training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Attached presentations and prompts may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Confirm the service's data handling is acceptable before attaching confidential documents or submitting sensitive prompts.

Risk: The dLazy CLI may save an API key locally.

Mitigation: Review the CLI package before installing and protect or rotate the locally stored key as needed.
