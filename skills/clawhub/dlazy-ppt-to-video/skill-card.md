## Description:

Guides an agent through turning a presentation or other document into an explainer, training, or pitch video using dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and teams use this skill to turn PowerPoint, Keynote, and other documents into narrated explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos through dLazy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review dLazy's terms and organizational policies before uploading sensitive documents.

Risk: The CLI can save an API key locally, and installing it executes third-party software.

Mitigation: Protect and rotate the API key as needed; review the pinned CLI package and source before installation.
