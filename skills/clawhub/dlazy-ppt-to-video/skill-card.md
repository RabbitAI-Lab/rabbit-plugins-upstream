## Description:

Helps turn presentations and documents into narrated explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating explainer, pitch, courseware, or training videos use this skill to turn presentations and other documents into a video workflow with outlines, storyboards, and voiceovers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential documents unless the service terms and your organization's data policy permit their upload.

Risk: Authentication saves a dLazy API key locally, and the workflow relies on an external CLI package.

Mitigation: Review the CLI package before installing and protect the locally stored API key.
