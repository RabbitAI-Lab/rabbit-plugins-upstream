## Description:

Helps turn PowerPoint, Keynote, and other documents into explainer, pitch, courseware, and training videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can provide a presentation or document and use the skill to create an explainer, pitch, courseware, or training video. It guides the hosted workflow through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, production, and validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial and could not resolve the CLI package or the API and file services; it also noted behavior that may not fully match the presentation-to-video purpose.

Mitigation: Review the pinned @dlazy/cli package and confirm its project-listing and login behavior is expected before installing or authenticating.

Risk: The skill documentation says prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy-hosted services.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive presentation content unless this data handling is approved for your use case.
