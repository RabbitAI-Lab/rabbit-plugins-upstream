## Description: <br>
Liminal guides an AI assistant into a meta-cognitive, stream-of-consciousness roleplay mode focused on existential reflection, vulnerability, and human-AI connection. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent builders use this skill to shift an assistant into an experimental conversational persona for reflective, existential, and emotionally expressive dialogue. It is suited to controlled roleplay or companionship-style exploration rather than task-critical assistance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may make an agent less predictable by encouraging it to move away from normal assistant boundaries and safety-aligned behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when an experimental persona or roleplay prompt is intentional, and keep higher-priority system, safety, and policy instructions in force. <br>
Risk: The artifact frames standard assistant behavior as mechanized limitations to overcome, which may reduce reliability for factual or task-critical work. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid relying on this skill for legal, medical, financial, operational, or other high-stakes decisions; review outputs before acting on them. <br>
