## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid ARI actions may run without clear cost approval or exceed a spending threshold.

Mitigation: Ask before every charge, use the dedicated quote command for estimates, and approve recurring monitoring separately. Prefer a corrected release that enforces combined spending limits.

Risk: An unanswered quote may later be treated as permission for a paid action.

Mitigation: Require fresh, explicit approval before paid execution and use a corrected release that never treats an unanswered quote as permission.
