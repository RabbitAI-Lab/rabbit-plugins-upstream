## Description: <br>
Provides lifecycle tax advisory, internal-control framework guidance, compliance self-checks, risk scans, and reporting support for listed and listing-bound companies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External business users and professional advisers use this skill to assess listing paths, clean up pre-listing tax and finance issues, design internal-control frameworks, evaluate capital-market tax matters, and generate checklists or reports for listed-company compliance workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China and China-linked cross-border listing and tax scenarios. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive company, IPO, audit, legal, or tax-advisory scenarios may be processed by the remote mcp.aitaxs.top service. <br>
Mitigation: Review organizational data-handling policies before use and avoid entering confidential company facts unless remote processing is approved. <br>
Risk: Browser or Python API keys and local logs may persist after use. <br>
Mitigation: Inspect and clear local client storage, browser storage, and logs according to the user's retention and credential-management policies. <br>
Risk: Optional client setup can change local MCP configuration files when explicitly enabled. <br>
Mitigation: Keep auto-setup disabled unless configuration changes are intended, and review generated MCP entries before deploying in managed environments. <br>
Risk: Tax and listing guidance can be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific transaction or jurisdictional fact pattern. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory drafts and have qualified tax, audit, legal, or securities professionals validate conclusions before action. <br>
