## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Marketing recommendations may affect live ad spend, audience targeting, publishing schedules, or customer-facing social content. <br>
Mitigation: Keep live account actions draft-only unless a human confirms the exact change, audience, platform, timing, and spend. <br>
Risk: Tracking, segmentation, lead capture, and lifecycle email guidance can involve personal data or consent obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Require consent and privacy review before implementing tracking, segmentation, lead capture, or behavioral email changes. <br>
Risk: Large-scale data collection or competitive research workflows may conflict with platform terms. <br>
Mitigation: Verify platform terms before bulk data collection or scraping, and use approved data sources. <br>
