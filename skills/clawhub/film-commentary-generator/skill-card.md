## Description: <br>
输入电影/剧名、目标时长和解说风格，生成影视解说文案、分段字幕、BGM 推荐和镜头剪辑提示，适用于影视解说类短视频创作。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators and video editors use this skill to plan short-form film or TV commentary videos. It turns a title, target duration, and style into a structured script, subtitle segments, music suggestions, and editing cues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated commentary, subtitles, and scene plans may raise copyright or platform suitability concerns if they copy protected source material too closely. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs before publication, keep narration transformative, avoid long verbatim excerpts from source works, and confirm platform policy fit. <br>
Risk: BGM recommendations can include copyrighted music or tracks whose license does not cover the intended channel or commercial use. <br>
Mitigation: Use cleared music from licensed services or platform commercial libraries, and verify rights for each selected track before release. <br>
