## Description:

Turn data, comparisons, processes and timelines into explanatory visuals with Canvora — process flows, comparison charts, timelines, stat cards, checklists and long-form infographics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[canvora](https://clawhub.ai/user/canvora)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People preparing reports, presentations, or social content use this skill to turn supplied figures, steps, comparisons, timelines, and checklists into explanatory visuals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may submit user data to Canvora and spend credits without a clear request or authorization.

Mitigation: Use only for explicitly requested visuals, set a credit budget or approval requirement, submit only information approved for Canvora processing, and remove confidential data and access tokens from URLs.
