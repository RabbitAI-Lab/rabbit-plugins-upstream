## Description: <br>
Exports AI chat sessions to Markdown, JSON, or plain text using ChatPipe v1.1, with support for OpenClaw, Hermes, ChatGPT, Chatbox, Markdown, and plain-text sources. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent users use this skill to export or back up AI chat sessions, convert conversations for tools such as Obsidian or Notion, and move chat history between supported AI platforms. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can save sensitive chat transcripts to local disk. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the exact chat, format, and destination before export, and redact secrets or private information before sharing the output. <br>
Risk: The transcript is passed to a local ChatPipe script for conversion. <br>
Mitigation: Verify that the local ChatPipe script is trusted before using the skill with private or sensitive conversations. <br>
