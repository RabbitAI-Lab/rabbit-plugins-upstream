## Description: <br>
Pinterest Seo helps agents research Pinterest search queries, write keyword-led pin titles and natural descriptions, specify on-pin keyword text, and advise board and link alignment while leaving native Pinterest account setup to the user. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to prepare Pinterest SEO copy and routing guidance for pins: keyword research method, pin title, description, on-pin keyword, semantic board choice, and aligned destination link. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated pin copy or board/link choices could be published publicly with incorrect, misleading, or poorly aligned SEO claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated title, description, board selection, and destination link before publishing, and confirm the Pin -> Board -> Page promise matches. <br>
Risk: The skill could be mistaken as automating Pinterest account setup or analytics actions outside its stated scope. <br>
Mitigation: Keep profile and bio changes, board creation or renaming, alt text, Rich Pins, Pinterest Trends, and analytics checks as native or human actions. <br>
Risk: Keyword stuffing or invented search volumes could reduce quality or mislead users. <br>
Mitigation: Use Pinterest autocomplete, Guided Search, and Trends as the research method, keep copy natural, and do not fabricate volume or ranking numbers. <br>
