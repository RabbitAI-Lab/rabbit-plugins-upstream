## Description:

Installs MANOR Edge on a supported Apple silicon Mac and guides the owner through pairing and camera setup while keeping pairing keys and camera credentials in the owner's hands.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External home owners use this skill to install and check MANOR Edge on a supported Apple silicon Mac, understand Free and Premium service options, and receive guidance for pairing and adding cameras.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pairing keys and camera passwords are sensitive home access credentials.

Mitigation: The owner enters pairing keys and camera details directly in MANOR Edge; do not request or handle those credentials.

Risk: The downloaded app was not inspected as part of this skill review.

Mitigation: Review the publisher's setup disclosures and privacy policy before approving Premium setup, and follow the skill's checksum, signing, and notarization checks.
