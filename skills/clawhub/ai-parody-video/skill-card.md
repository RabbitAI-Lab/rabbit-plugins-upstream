## Description:

Plans short AI parody videos by shaping the joke, choosing a production format, drafting prompts, and guiding editing, captions, and responsible posting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and video producers use this skill to plan and produce short AI parody videos, from joke structure and generation prompts through subtitles and platform-ready captions. It also gives practical guidance on depicting public figures, using reference material, and labeling AI-generated content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The installer command is unpinned and could run a changed package version.

Mitigation: Review the install source and pin a version or commit before running it.

Risk: Prompts and reference images may be sent to an external video provider.

Mitigation: Check the provider's data handling and avoid sensitive material unless you are comfortable submitting it.

Risk: The security scan is incomplete because it could not resolve a local path-like reference.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and related files before relying on the benign scan result.
