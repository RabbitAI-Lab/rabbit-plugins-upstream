## Description:

对比主 ASIN 与已授权竞品商品页的图片信息和评论反馈，整理图片表达缺口与拍摄说明。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers use this skill to compare a product listing with authorized competitor listings and customer review feedback, find gaps in how product images communicate benefits, and prepare photography notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The image review package also exposes recurring paid monitoring, which may consume account credits without fresh approval.

Mitigation: Keep use within the image review workflow, require approval before paid operations, set billing to ask every time, and separately approve recurring collection.
