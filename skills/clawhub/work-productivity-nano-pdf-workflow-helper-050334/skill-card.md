## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation terms may cause the helper to run for ordinary PDF, edit, or natural-language requests where it is not needed. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow or disable implicit invocation before installation in shared skill environments. <br>
Risk: Advisory workflow outputs can be incomplete or mismatched to the user's actual PDF tooling, safety constraints, or reliability goals. <br>
Mitigation: Restate the desired outcome and success criteria, expose assumptions, and validate the result against the user's stated constraints before applying it. <br>
