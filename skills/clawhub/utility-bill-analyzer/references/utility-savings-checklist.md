# Utility Savings Checklist — 100+ Actionable Tips

A comprehensive checklist organized by category. Each tip includes an estimated savings where available.

---

## ⚡ Electricity

### Lighting (12 tips)
1. Replace incandescent bulbs with LEDs (save 75% energy, last 25× longer)
2. Use natural light whenever possible — open curtains during the day
3. Install motion sensors for outdoor lights
4. Use timers on decorative/landscape lighting
5. Choose lower wattage bulbs in hallways and closets
6. Install dimmer switches to reduce consumption
7. Turn off lights when leaving a room (obvious but easy to forget)
8. Use task lighting (desk lamp) instead of lighting the whole room
9. Keep light fixtures and bulbs clean — dirt reduces output by up to 50%
10. Use solar-powered outdoor pathway lights
11. Paint walls light colors to reduce lighting needs
12. Remove unused bulbs in overlit areas (e.g., multi-bulb fixtures)

### Heating & Cooling (20 tips)
13. Set thermostat to 20-22°C (68-72°F) in winter; each degree above 20°C adds ~10% to heating
14. Set thermostat to 24-26°C (76-78°F) in summer; each degree below 26°C adds ~10% to cooling
15. Install a programmable or smart thermostat (Nest, Ecobee)
16. Lower thermostat by 2-3°C at night or when away
17. Use ceiling fans — they make rooms feel 4°C cooler
18. Reverse ceiling fan direction in winter to push warm air down
19. Clean/replace HVAC filters monthly (clogged filters waste 15%+ energy)
20. Seal gaps around windows and doors with weatherstripping
21. Add insulation to attic — top-up pays for itself in 2-5 years
22. Install thermal/insulated curtains
23. Close curtains/blinds on hot summer days to block solar heat gain
24. Open curtains on winter days to let solar heat in
25. Use draft stoppers under exterior doors
26. Service heating/cooling systems annually
27. Consider a heat pump — 3× more efficient than resistance heating
28. Zone heating/cooling — close vents in unused rooms
29. Install a door sweep on the garage-to-house door
30. Caulk around window frames and baseboards
31. Install storm windows or use window insulation film in winter
32. Shade outdoor AC unit with trees or awning (improves efficiency 10%)

### Appliances & Electronics (18 tips)
33. Unplug "vampire" electronics (TVs, chargers, game consoles) — save 5-10% on electricity
34. Use smart power strips to cut standby power automatically
35. Wash clothes in cold water (90% of washing energy heats the water)
36. Air-dry clothes instead of using the dryer
37. If using dryer: clean lint trap before every load, use sensor dry setting
38. Run dishwasher and washing machine only with full loads
39. Air-dry dishes (turn off heated dry cycle)
40. Choose ENERGY STAR appliances when replacing
41. Use the right-sized burner for pots — small pot on large burner wastes energy
42. Keep refrigerator at 3-4°C (37-40°F), freezer at -18°C (0°F)
43. Clean refrigerator coils twice a year
44. Don't overload the fridge — air needs to circulate
45. Microwave or air-fry instead of oven for small meals
46. Use a pressure cooker or slow cooker (much less energy than oven)
47. Keep oven door closed while cooking (each peek drops temp 25%)
48. Match pot size to burner and use lids
49. Thaw frozen food in the fridge (reduces cooking energy + helps fridge stay cool)
50. Turn off oven 5-10 minutes before cooking is done (residual heat finishes the job)

### Water Heating (8 tips)
51. Lower water heater temperature to 50°C (120°F) — save 4-22% annually
52. Insulate hot water pipes and the tank (if older than 10 years)
53. Install a heat-trap on water heater
54. Take shorter showers (each minute ≈ 9 liters of water + heating energy)
55. Install a low-flow showerhead (save 25-60% on shower water heating)
56. Consider a tankless/on-demand water heater
57. Drain a quart from water heater tank every 3 months to remove sediment
58. Use cold water for laundry — hot water for whites only when needed

## 💧 Water

### Indoor Water Saving (15 tips)
59. Fix dripping faucets — 1 drip/sec = 1,300+ gallons/year wasted
60. Fix running toilets — a silent leak can waste 200 gallons/day
61. Use the dual-flush or half-flush option on toilets
62. Install low-flow faucet aerators ($2-5, saves 30% water)
63. Install a low-flow or dual-flush toilet (or add a displacement bag)
64. Turn off tap while brushing teeth or shaving
65. Don't use the toilet as a trash can
66. Rinse vegetables in a bowl, not under running water
67. Save pasta/vegetable cooking water for plants
68. Only run full loads in dishwasher and washing machine
69. Scrape plates instead of pre-rinsing (modern dishwashers don't need pre-rinse)
70. Install a water-efficient washing machine (front-loaders use 50% less)
71. Check for hidden leaks — read meter before/after a 2-hour no-use period
72. Insulate hot water pipes so water heats faster (less waste while waiting)
73. Keep a pitcher of water in the fridge instead of running tap until cold

### Outdoor Water Saving (12 tips)
74. Water lawn/garden early morning or evening (less evaporation)
75. Use a rain barrel to collect water for plants
76. Install drip irrigation instead of sprinklers
77. Choose drought-resistant/native plants (xeriscaping)
78. Mulch around plants to retain moisture
79. Use a broom instead of hose to clean driveways and patios
80. Adjust sprinklers to water plants, not pavement
81. Install a rain sensor on automatic irrigation
82. Group plants by water needs (hydrozoning)
83. Mow grass taller (5-8cm) — longer grass retains moisture
84. Reduce lawn area — replace with groundcover or patio
85. Wash the car with a bucket, not a running hose

## 🔥 Gas

### Gas Heating (10 tips)
86. Service your furnace/boiler annually (maintains peak efficiency)
87. Bleed radiators to remove trapped air
88. Install a condensing boiler (90%+ efficient vs 60-70% for older models)
49. Add radiator reflector panels behind wall-mounted radiators
90. Use a smart thermostat with weather compensation
91. Lower thermostat in unused rooms with TRVs (thermostatic radiator valves)
92. Insulate hot water cylinder with a jacket (save £100-150/year in UK climates)
93. Insulate floors — up to 15% of heat escapes through uninsulated floors
94. Upgrade single-glazed windows to double or triple glazing
95. Consider a ground-source or air-source heat pump as a gas replacement

### Gas Cooking (3 tips)
96. Use the smallest burner that fits the pot
97. Keep burners clean — a dirty burner is less efficient
98. Use a kettle to boil water, then transfer to a pot (faster, less gas)

## 📊 Rate & Plan Optimization (10 tips)
99. Compare plans from competing providers annually
100. Consider a time-of-use plan if you can shift usage to off-peak hours
101. Ask about budget billing / level payment plans to spread seasonal costs
102. Check for low-income energy assistance programs (LIHEAP, Warm Home Discount)
103. Negotiate with your provider — loyalty discounts are real
104. Check for solar buyback/net metering programs
105. Request a free energy audit from your utility company
106. Install solar panels if your roof has good solar exposure
107. Check for billing errors — compare meter readings to billed amounts
108. Ask about green/renewable energy options (sometimes same or lower cost)
