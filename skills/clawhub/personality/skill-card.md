## Description: <br>
Personality helps users run DISC-based quizzes to tune AI communication for themselves or to prepare communication with another person. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[lukethomas14](https://clawhub.ai/user/lukethomas14) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Users and teams use this skill to estimate a DISC communication style, choose a matching communication-personalization skill, and get install guidance for follow-on personality or communication coaching skills. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Follow-on personality or communication skills can affect how the AI communicates in later sessions. <br>
Mitigation: Review any recommended `my-personality-*` or `communicate-with-*` skill before installing it. <br>
Risk: The four-question DISC quiz is a rough estimate and may not accurately identify a user's or colleague's communication style. <br>
Mitigation: Present quiz results as approximate and direct users to a fuller DISC assessment when precision matters. <br>
