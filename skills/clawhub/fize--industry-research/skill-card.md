## Description:

Provides a standardized industry and supply-chain research SOP for mapping value distribution, cycle inflection points, competitive landscape, policy constraints, and listed equities or ETFs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fize](https://clawhub.ai/user/fize)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to produce evidence-grounded industry landscape reports covering supply chains, market structure, company comparables, policy constraints, and public-market mappings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research may rely on online search, browser, PDF, news, and market-data sources that can be stale, incomplete, or inconsistent.

Mitigation: Verify cited market, policy, report, and company data against authoritative sources before relying on conclusions.

Risk: Public-market mappings, ETF references, valuation multiples, and financial indicators could be mistaken for trading instructions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research material only and require independent financial review before making investment decisions.

Risk: Missing core data could lead to unsupported industry conclusions.

Mitigation: Use the artifact's fail-fast posture: block report generation when required facts cannot be verified.
