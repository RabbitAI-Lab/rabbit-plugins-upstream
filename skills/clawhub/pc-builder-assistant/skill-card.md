## Description:

Helps plan and check desktop PC builds, upgrades, gaming performance, and local AI hardware fit using China-market component and price references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

PC buyers and builders use this skill to compare desktop components, plan budget builds or upgrades, and check compatibility, gaming performance, and local model memory needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (bundled prices are China-market CNY references)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bundled China-market prices can become outdated or differ from local retailer quotes.

Mitigation: Verify current retailer prices, currency, and exact component SKUs before buying.

Risk: Reference FPS figures and compatibility guidance may not match a specific build or workload.

Mitigation: Treat performance figures as estimates and check the selected parts and specifications before purchasing.

Risk: User-supplied catalog overlays involve local files supplied or saved by the user.

Mitigation: Review overlay contents and file locations before providing or saving them.
