## Description:

Helps plan desktop PC builds and upgrades, check component compatibility, and assess gaming and local AI hardware needs using China-market reference data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

PC builders and hardware advisers use this skill to draft budget-aware desktop parts lists, evaluate upgrades and component fit, and discuss gaming performance or local AI memory requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (bundled pricing and availability references are China-market focused and denominated in CNY)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-market reference prices and stock may differ from actual checkout terms or other regions.

Mitigation: Verify exact SKU, warranty region, availability, currency, and checkout price before purchasing.

Risk: Sample gaming frame rates may not reflect performance of the selected build.

Mitigation: Treat FPS figures as advisory and verify measured performance for the exact configuration.

Risk: Incomplete component specifications may leave physical or electrical compatibility uncertain.

Mitigation: Confirm precise model variants and unresolved fit or power details before purchasing.
