## Description:

Helps plan desktop PC builds and upgrades with component recommendations, compatibility checks, China-market price references, and gaming or local AI sizing guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Desktop PC buyers and builders use this skill to plan budgets, select parts, complete or upgrade configurations, and check compatibility for gaming, creative work, and local AI workloads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (China-market CNY price references)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Server, NAS, rackmount, or workstation data may lead to unsuitable procurement recommendations.

Mitigation: Use this skill for desktop PC builds, not server or NAS procurement; independently verify any recommendation involving server, NAS, rackmount, or workstation components.

Risk: Benchmark inconsistencies may make predicted game FPS unreliable.

Mitigation: Treat FPS figures as references and independently check performance claims before purchasing.

Risk: China-market reference prices may differ from actual purchase prices or other markets.

Mitigation: Confirm current local prices and availability before purchasing; do not treat CNY references as live quotes.
