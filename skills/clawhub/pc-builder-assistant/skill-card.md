## Description:

Helps plan desktop PC builds and upgrades, check component compatibility, and assess hardware for gaming, creative work, and local AI using China-market price references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People planning or upgrading desktop PCs use this skill to compare components, draft budgeted builds, check compatibility, and estimate gaming or local-LLM suitability. Prices are primarily China-market CNY references, not purchase quotes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (bundled pricing references); hardware guidance may be used elsewhere with locally verified prices

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bundled China-market prices may be stale or unsuitable for another market.

Mitigation: Re-check current local prices, currency, and availability before buying.

Risk: Some game FPS samples are prediction-derived rather than measured benchmarks.

Mitigation: Treat FPS figures as estimates and check independent measured benchmarks for the intended games and settings.

Risk: Incomplete component details can leave physical or electrical compatibility uncertain.

Mitigation: Verify exact part specifications and any unresolved fit or power requirements before purchasing.
