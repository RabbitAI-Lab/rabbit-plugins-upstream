## Description: <br>
PC Build Assistant helps agents plan China-market desktop PC builds, upgrades, compatibility checks, and hardware guidance with CNY price references for gaming, creator, local AI, and small-form-factor use cases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers can use this skill to generate desktop PC part lists, upgrade paths, compatibility reviews, and hardware-selection answers for China-market DIY builds. It is suited to planning and guidance, not purchasing, payment, laptop procurement, or server procurement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recommended parts, prices, regional availability, and warranty terms can differ from the China-market reference data. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current retailer listings, equivalent local SKUs, warranties, stock, and final prices before purchase. <br>
Risk: Physical compatibility can depend on case clearances, power-supply dimensions, cooling layout, cables, and model revisions that may require purchase-time confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Review the compatibility result and any listed verification points against manufacturer specifications before ordering parts. <br>
Risk: Current pricing may require web searches beyond the bundled catalogs. <br>
Mitigation: Treat searched prices as time-sensitive references and preserve the original CNY price context when comparing markets. <br>
