## Description:

Helps plan desktop PC builds and upgrades with component recommendations, compatibility checks, and guidance for gaming, creative work, and local AI workloads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People planning or upgrading desktop PCs use this skill to compare parts, check build compatibility, and estimate budgets for gaming, creative work, and local AI. It does not handle purchases, laptops, or server procurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; bundled prices are China-market CNY references.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bundled China-market prices may be stale or unsuitable for another market.

Mitigation: Check current local prices and exact product SKUs before buying; keep currencies separate.

Risk: Incomplete component specifications may hide fit or compatibility issues, especially for server- or NAS-like cases.

Mitigation: Verify compatibility-critical dimensions and manufacturer specifications before purchase.

Risk: Bundled or prediction-labeled game performance figures may not match a specific build.

Mitigation: Treat FPS figures as references and verify relevant game, resolution, and hardware claims.
