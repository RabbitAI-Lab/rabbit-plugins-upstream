## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Integrated-graphics assumptions may be incorrect for a particular CPU.

Mitigation: Verify the selected CPU's graphics support before buying a build without a separate graphics card.

Risk: Incomplete BIOS-update guidance could lead to a failed update that prevents startup.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact motherboard model and revision, follow the manufacturer's firmware and instructions, and maintain uninterrupted power during the update.

Risk: Bundled price references may not reflect current prices.

Mitigation: Recheck current prices before making a purchase.

Risk: A catalog export can replace the explicitly chosen output file.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected output path and that replacing its file is intended before exporting.
