## Description: <br>
PC Build Assistant helps users plan DIY desktop builds, upgrades, compatibility checks, and hardware guidance with China-market CNY reference pricing across gaming, creator, local AI, and compact PC scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to generate desktop PC part lists, upgrade plans, compatibility reviews, budget tradeoffs, and hardware-selection guidance. It is scoped to desktop PC advice and does not perform purchasing, payments, remote control, laptop selection, server procurement, or security-isolation work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: China-market CNY catalog prices and component availability can become stale or differ from a user's local market. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current retailer pricing, stock, warranty terms, and equivalent local SKUs before purchase. <br>
Risk: Compatibility advice can depend on physical dimensions, cables, firmware, and vendor-specific details that may be incomplete in the offline catalog. <br>
Mitigation: Review the listed compatibility findings and manually confirm dimensions, connector requirements, firmware or BIOS support, and case clearances before ordering parts. <br>
Risk: The skill is designed for advice and compatibility checking, not transactions or operational control. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use it for purchasing, payments, remote control, laptop selection, server procurement, or security-isolation decisions. <br>
