## Description:

帮助初中物理教师设计和组织实验教学，涵盖实验目标、器材分组、安全分级、数据处理和实验报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初中物理教师用它拟定实验教学方案，包括实验目标、变量控制、课堂安全、分组和器材安排、数据分析及实验报告指导；教师审核方案并负责课堂实施。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe experiments or unsuitable at-home activities could cause physical harm.

Mitigation: Review each experiment's safety level; keep high-risk work teacher-led and do not recommend dangerous at-home experiments.

Risk: Student experiment profiles or reports could expose personal information through classroom memory or sharing.

Mitigation: Confirm school privacy settings and cross-skill sharing before use; use pseudonyms and de-identify reports.
