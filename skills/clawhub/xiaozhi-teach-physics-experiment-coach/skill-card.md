## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school physics teachers plan, supervise, and review experiment lessons, from experimental design and safety to data analysis and lab reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school physics teachers use this skill to draft experiment plans, assign groups and equipment, guide data processing, and prepare lab-report feedback. Teachers review all student-facing materials and any class-record updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, emergency guidance, and privacy rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe handling of physical equipment or hazardous experiments.

Mitigation: Require teacher supervision and school laboratory rules; use demonstration rather than student group work for high-risk experiments.

Risk: Student records may expose personal information or be shared or updated without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent settings before persistence or sharing, pseudonymize students, and require teacher review before any writeback.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency guidance, and privacy rules may not fit use outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these requirements before use in another jurisdiction.
