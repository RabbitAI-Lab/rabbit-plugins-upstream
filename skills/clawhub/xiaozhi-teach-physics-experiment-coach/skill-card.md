## Description:

Helps middle and high school physics teachers plan student experiments, from experimental design and lab safety through data analysis and lab reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External middle and high school physics teachers use the skill to prepare and guide student experiments, including equipment planning, safe procedures, data analysis, conclusions, and report feedback. It provides drafts and guidance for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student reports or photos may contain identifying details, and saved class records may be shared more broadly than intended.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, remove identifying details before sharing reports or photos, and review record changes and sharing permissions.

Risk: A proposed experiment may be unsafe with the available equipment or classroom setup.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check the safety level and local setup before use; keep high-risk experiments to teacher demonstrations or approved video/simulation alternatives.
