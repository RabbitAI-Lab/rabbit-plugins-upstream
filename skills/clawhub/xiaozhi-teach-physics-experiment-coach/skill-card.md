## Description:

为初中及高一物理教师提供实验目标、设计、实施、数据处理和实验报告的教学指导，并纳入安全分级与器材分组安排。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初中及高一物理教师用它准备实验课、安排分组与器材、指导数据分析并起草实验报告反馈，最终由教师审定。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, safety contacts, and legal assumptions before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class records and student reports may contain sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Pseudonymize student data and review proposed record updates before confirming them.

Risk: Incorrect experiment safety guidance could expose students to hazards.

Mitigation: Have the teacher verify each experiment's safety level and keep high-risk activities as supervised demonstrations only.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts, and legal assumptions may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt those details to local requirements before using the guidance elsewhere.
