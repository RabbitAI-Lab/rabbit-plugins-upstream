## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom records may expose student identities or sensitive assessment materials.

Mitigation: Configure student aliases, consent, and teacher review before using memory or class-workspace features; do not enter identifying information or unreleased exams.

Risk: Incorrect experiment guidance could lead to unsafe classroom activities.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check each plan and its safety level; keep high-risk experiments as demonstrations rather than student group or home activities.
