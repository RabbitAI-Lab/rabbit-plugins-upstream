## Description:

帮助初中及高一物理教师规划实验目标、器材与分组、操作安全、数据处理和实验报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中及高一物理教师用它拟定实验课方案，安排器材和分组，引导学生处理数据并编写实验报告；所有实验安排与学生评价由教师审定。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China curriculum focus; localize safety and student-data practices elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real student records or reports may contain sensitive classroom information.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, sharing, deletion and teacher-approved writeback controls before use; de-identify student records and reports.

Risk: A proposed experiment or scoring rubric could be unsafe or mistaken if used without teacher review.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check experimental safety levels and plans before class; use rubrics as teacher-owned references, not automatic grades.
