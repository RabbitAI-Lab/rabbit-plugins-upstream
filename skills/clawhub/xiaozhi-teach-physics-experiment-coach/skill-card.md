## Description:

Helps junior physics teachers plan and guide experiment instruction across goals, design, implementation, data processing, conclusions, lab reports, safety levels, grouping, and equipment logistics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External middle-school physics teachers use this skill to draft experiment lesson flows, safety classifications, grouping and equipment plans, data-processing guidance, and lab-report support for classroom use. Teachers remain responsible for official scoring, student-record updates, parent sharing, and safety-sensitive lab decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland by default; localize curriculum alignment, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent rules before broader deployment.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted lab-report feedback or scoring guidance could be mistaken for official grading.

Mitigation: Keep official scoring under teacher review and use the skill's rubrics as drafting aids rather than final grade decisions.

Risk: Student lab records and reports may contain minor student information.

Mitigation: Use aliases, honor consent and sharing controls, and avoid sending parent-facing or cross-skill summaries unless the required authorization is present.

Risk: Unsafe experiment recommendations could expose students to hazardous electrical, chemical, pressure, glassware, laser, or heating conditions.

Mitigation: Apply the artifact's safetyLevel workflow, keep high-risk experiments as teacher demonstrations or simulations, and do not let students perform live household electrical measurements at home.

Risk: Safety or crisis-related student disclosures could be softened or mishandled as normal learning-support content.

Mitigation: Follow the bundled crisis referral protocol for crisis signals and keep hazardous lab decisions under explicit adult supervision.
