## Description:

Helps middle and high school physics teachers plan safe, inquiry-based experiments, organize equipment and groups, analyze data, and draft lab-report guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school physics teachers use this skill to draft experiment objectives, procedures, safety precautions, data-analysis activities, and lab-report guidance. Teachers remain responsible for supervising experiments, evaluating students, and approving any shared materials or class-record changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records or unreleased exam material may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Confirm workspace consent settings, use student aliases, avoid entering unreleased exam materials or sensitive personal data, and require teacher approval before record writeback or sharing.

Risk: Unsafe or incorrect experiment instructions could put students at risk.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review each plan and safety level, supervise hands-on work, and keep high-risk experiments as demonstrations only.
