## Description:

Helps Chinese-language junior-high physics teachers organize experiment instruction from goals and design through implementation, data processing, conclusions, and lab reports, including safety levels, grouping, and equipment planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External educators use this skill to plan and facilitate junior-high physics experiments, prepare student supports, guide data processing and conclusion writing, and review lab reports. It is intended for teacher-led classroom use and does not replace lab safety supervision, hands-on experiment execution, or teacher grading judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be installed for a context outside its intended Chinese-language junior-high physics teaching use case.

Mitigation: Confirm the classroom context and curriculum level before installation or use.

Risk: Student experiment records could expose personal or family details if entered verbatim.

Mitigation: Keep records pseudonymous, avoid real names and family or contact details, and verify consent before sharing across skills or with parents.

Risk: Electrical or household experiment suggestions could be unsafe if treated as unsupervised student activities.

Mitigation: Treat household electrical activity as teacher-supervised demonstration or simulation unless it is safely low-voltage, and keep high-safetyLevel experiments out of student group work and home assignments.

Risk: AI-generated experiment questions or plans could contain calculation or curriculum errors.

Mitigation: Require teacher review and calculation checks before adding generated material to a class resource bank or using it for assessment.
