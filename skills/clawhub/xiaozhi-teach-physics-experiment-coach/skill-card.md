## Description:

帮助初高中物理教师规划实验目标、设计与实施，指导数据处理、结论归纳和实验报告，并安排安全分级、分组与器材。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中物理教师用它起草实验课方案、分组和器材安排，设计数据处理与实验报告指导，并根据班级情况调整教学支持；方案和面向学生的内容由教师审核。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe laboratory activities could expose students to electrical, laser, or equipment hazards.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's safety tiers, keep high-risk experiments teacher-led demonstrations, and have the teacher check apparatus and supervise student work.

Risk: Class records and student ability tiers may expose sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms and de-identified reports; limit workspace access and obtain teacher confirmation before saving lesson plans, assignments, or weakness entries.

Risk: Draft lesson materials or emergency advice may not fit local curricula and procedures.

Mitigation: Have teachers review materials and localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent practices before deployment outside mainland China.
