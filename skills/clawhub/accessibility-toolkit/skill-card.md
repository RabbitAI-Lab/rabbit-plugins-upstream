## Use Case: <br>
Developers, agents, and caregivers use this skill to reduce interaction friction for people with physical disabilities through voice-first workflows, smart-home automation templates, batch operations, and fallback patterns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Low-friction smart-home automations can affect doors, alarms, access codes, or emergency actions without enough user confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user opt-in and stronger verification before enabling security-sensitive routines, and keep generic voice commands away from irreversible or high-impact actions. <br>
Risk: Conversation-history analysis and proactive reminders can expose sensitive personal, health, schedule, or accessibility data. <br>
Mitigation: Enable history-based analysis only with clear consent, prefer local processing, redact sensitive content, and provide deletion controls. <br>
