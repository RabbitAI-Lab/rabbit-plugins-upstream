## Description:

Generate and edit images from text prompts and supplied images using EmpirioLabs AI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[empiriolabs](https://clawhub.ai/user/empiriolabs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users create, restyle, or combine images for illustrations, product shots, posters, and social content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and supplied images are sent to EmpirioLabs AI.

Mitigation: Use only trusted prompts and images; avoid submitting sensitive local files.

Risk: Image generation can incur charges, especially with multiple images or premium models.

Mitigation: Check model prices and confirm cost-sensitive requests before generation.
