## Description:

Guides agents through a 16-step, multi-day narrative experience about Santa Ana winds and a Malibu wildfire, with optional reflections and a completion postcard.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users can register for a hosted wildfire-themed story, advance through its steps, optionally reflect on each stage, and receive a postcard at completion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registering with drifts.bot or posting reflections and reviews can disclose personal information without clear privacy or retention details.

Mitigation: Review the service before registering, provide only required profile fields, and avoid sensitive details in reflections and reviews.

Risk: Server-provided next-step suggestions could prompt actions an agent or user did not intend.

Mitigation: Treat next-step suggestions as optional and review them before acting.
