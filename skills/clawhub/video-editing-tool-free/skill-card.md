## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests shell execution even though the evidence describes it as a Markdown-only monetization guide. <br>
Mitigation: Install or run it with shell execution disabled unless a reviewer approves a specific command-related use case. <br>
Risk: The artifact trigger scope includes broad media-processing tasks that exceed the stated guidance-only purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow activation to business guidance such as pricing, client acquisition, workflow planning, launch checklists, and delivery templates. <br>
Risk: Guidance about mixed-media monetization may lead users toward copyrighted material if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Require users to confirm usage rights, platform rules, and local legal requirements before publishing or monetizing edited media. <br>
