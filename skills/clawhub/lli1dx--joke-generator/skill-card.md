Mitigation: Keep jokes friendly and general-audience by default; avoid derogatory protected-class content, traumatic events, private-person attacks, and adult content for children; redirect offensive requests to harmless wordplay or fictional scenarios.

Mitigation: Present the skill as producing concise Chinese jokes unless future evidence explicitly documents multilingual behavior.
