## Description: <br>
Guides suppliers through Chinese government-procurement response-file compliance checks, producing structured risk checklists and deadline workflows for format, qualification, policy, signature, sealing, copy-count, substantive-response, and complaint-support issues. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External suppliers and procurement or compliance teams preparing Chinese government-procurement response files use this skill to check submission completeness, identify invalid-response and policy-function risks, and structure final remediation before a deadline. It also supports procurement-document legality review, legal Q&A, violation spotting, and challenge or complaint preparation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process bid prices, trade secrets, supplier qualification records, and other sensitive procurement-response materials. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in an environment approved for that information, and avoid sharing sensitive bid materials unless the platform controls are appropriate. <br>
Risk: Generated reports are fixed Chinese-language checklist outputs with a required author and feedback footer. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated reports before submission or external sharing so the language, footer, and recipient-facing content are appropriate. <br>
Risk: Procurement-law guidance can become outdated or may not fit a specific tender's controlling documents. <br>
Mitigation: Verify legal references against current official sources and the procurement file, and use formal legal review for binding conclusions. <br>
