## Description:

Combines select a rollout region with register a deployment region.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Engineering, release, implementation, and delivery teams use this skill to select rollout regions for release briefs and record chosen regions in deployment briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A rollout region could be recorded against the wrong or unclear deployment brief.

Mitigation: Confirm the target brief and selected region before registration, especially when the request could refer to multiple releases or regions.
