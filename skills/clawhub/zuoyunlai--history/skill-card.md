## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and teachers use this skill to adapt historical explanations to audience level, discuss contested interpretations, analyze sources, construct arguments, and support history instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical interpretation can be contested, and unsupported claims or citations may mislead academic or professional users.

Mitigation: Verify citations and claims against primary sources, peer-reviewed scholarship, or trusted reference works before relying on outputs.

Risk: Explanations may oversimplify complex events or apply present-day assumptions to past contexts.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source criticism, multiple-perspective framing, and fact-versus-interpretation guidance when reviewing outputs.
