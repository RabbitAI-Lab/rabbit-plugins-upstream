## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to describe real projects and decisions, which may include confidential employer, client, or personal details.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential or personal information unless the user intends the agent to process it.

Risk: The skill provides professional self-assessment guidance rather than a validated psychological or credentialing assessment.

Mitigation: Treat results as coaching guidance and review conclusions against real project evidence and trusted feedback.
