## Description:

Helps K12 teachers turn one-way lectures into structured classroom interaction through follow-up questions, group activity guidance, cold-call alternatives, feedback language, and short post-class observation records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External educators and teaching staff use this skill to plan and run classroom interactions for Chinese K12 lessons, including question follow-ups, group work timing, participation paths, cold-room recovery, and post-class reflection. It is teacher-facing and leaves student selection and classroom judgment to the teacher.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localization of curriculum assumptions, privacy/legal defaults, and crisis referral channels.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is tailored to Mainland China Chinese K12 classroom support, including curriculum assumptions, privacy defaults, and crisis referral channels.

Mitigation: Localize those assumptions and referral channels before deploying it in other regions or school systems.

Risk: Classroom interaction notes and tier references can harden student labels if treated as definitive individual assessments.

Mitigation: Keep records scoped, use the included privacy controls, avoid naming students in low-confidence observation logs, and leave participation decisions to the teacher.
