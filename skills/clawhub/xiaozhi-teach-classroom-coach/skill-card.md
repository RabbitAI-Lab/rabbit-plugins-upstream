## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 teachers plan inclusive classroom questions, group activities, responses to silence, and brief post-class observations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Mainland China use this skill to adapt participation and questioning strategies to lesson goals, student groups, and class size, then draft teacher-approved observations after class. It supports rather than replaces teachers' instruction or assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, safety referrals, and data-consent practices before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom records may contain sensitive information about students.

Mitigation: Keep observations aggregate and free of student names; let teachers confirm log entries and control viewing, sharing, deletion, and retention.

Risk: Classroom strategies and generated examples may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular class.

Mitigation: Have teachers review suggestions before classroom use; leave student selection, teaching, and assessment decisions to teachers.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions, crisis referrals, and consent rules may not fit other locations.

Mitigation: Localize these practices and contacts before using the skill outside Mainland China.
