## Description:

为教师提供课堂提问、互动活动、小组合作、冷场应对与课后观察记录建议，帮助更多学生参与课堂。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

教师可依据课程目标、学段与班级情况，获取追问、互动活动、小组合作和课堂参与路径建议，并整理课后观察记录。输出供教师审阅，不代替教师授课、点名或评价学生。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom suggestions or proposed observation records may be inaccurate, unsuitable, or expose student-identifying information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, review generated materials and records before use or saving, and confirm sharing, memory, and consent controls in the host platform.
