## Description:

为中小学教师提供分层提问、候场追问、小组活动与课后课堂观察记录的互动指导。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

中小学教师用它根据教学目标和班级情况设计参与路径、追问及小组活动，并在课后整理课堂观察供下次教学参考；教师审定建议和记录。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom observations could reveal student information or be shared beyond the teacher's intent.

Mitigation: Keep names out of observation logs, review each pending writeback, and use pause, delete, or sharing controls when records should not be retained or shared.

Risk: Classroom suggestions or student-facing materials may be inaccurate or inappropriate for the class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review and adapt suggestions before using them with students or families.
