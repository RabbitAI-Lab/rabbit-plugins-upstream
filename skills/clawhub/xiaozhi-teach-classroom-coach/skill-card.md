## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 teachers plan classroom questions, group activities, participation prompts, and post-class observations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 teachers use this skill to adapt lesson objectives and student-grouping context into questioning strategies, timed group activities, and teacher-confirmed classroom observation records. It does not replace teacher judgment or produce lesson plans, assessments, or student analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, crisis contacts, and privacy rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class profiles and student grouping data may contain sensitive educational information.

Mitigation: Limit access to needed class context; keep observation records aggregate and require teacher confirmation before saving.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, crisis contacts, and privacy practices may not fit other regions or languages.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before deployment outside mainland China.
