## Description:

Helps teachers plan differentiated classroom questions, group activities, responses to quiet classrooms, and post-class observation notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

For primary and secondary school teachers who want students to participate more actively. It drafts tiered questions, group activities, wait-time prompts, classroom feedback, and aggregate observation notes for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland school curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom records and drafts may include student-related information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases and aggregate observations; review drafts and pending records, control cross-skill sharing, and confirm the platform supports the stated privacy controls.

Risk: Student- or parent-facing material may be used before it is reviewed.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review and approve student- or parent-facing drafts before use.
