## Description:

Actionable plans across 16 scenarios, from product requirements to contingency plans, each with acceptance criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can turn goals and constraints into executable plans across product, education, assessment, training, marketing, care, legal, emergency, budget, and organizational scenarios. The skill structures plans around actions, owners, timelines, resources, acceptance criteria, and risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical, legal, and teaching plans can affect high-impact decisions.

Mitigation: Have a qualified professional review these plans before applying them; do not use them as a substitute for professional judgment.

Risk: Reusable examples may contain identifiable or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Anonymize examples before reusing them.
