## Description: <br>
Create professional resumes and CVs. Generate ATS-friendly formats, optimize bullet points, tailor for specific jobs, and export to multiple formats (Markdown, HTML, LaTeX, PDF). <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Job seekers and career-support users use this skill to draft, tailor, optimize, and export resumes and CVs for specific roles and ATS workflows. <br>

Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Resume and CV drafts may contain sensitive personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Review content before sharing it with external systems and use local export assets for private resumes. <br>
Risk: The optional HTML styling example references a remote stylesheet. <br>
Mitigation: Use a local stylesheet when exporting private HTML resumes. <br>
Risk: Server-resolved provenance is unavailable for this version. <br>
Mitigation: Use the server-resolved publisher handle and ClawHub profile for ownership context; do not rely on the placeholder homepage for provenance. <br>
