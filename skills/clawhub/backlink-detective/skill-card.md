## Description:

Researches public backlink profiles, referring domains, traffic, organic competitors, and ranking keywords through ClawLink for SEO analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners, marketers, and SEO analysts use this skill to assess public domain authority, find backlink opportunities, compare organic competitors, and review ranking keywords.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing the ClawLink plugin requires account and device pairing, and requested domain queries are sent to ClawLink.

Mitigation: Explain the pairing and data-sharing steps before installation or querying; use only public domains relevant to the request.

Risk: ClawLink charges credit per query, and broad backlink or competitor batches can accumulate costs.

Mitigation: Check current per-tool pricing, keep result limits modest, and request a cost estimate and user approval before large batches.
