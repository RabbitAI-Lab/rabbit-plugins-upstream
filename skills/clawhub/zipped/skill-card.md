## Description:

Generates ready-to-shoot Chinese short-video scripts from a topic and video type, including a catchy title, a 30-second or 60-second duration recommendation, a shot-by-shot storyboard table, and shooting tips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sgdbcund](https://clawhub.ai/user/sgdbcund)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and social-media operators use this skill to turn a topic into a structured Chinese short-video script for talking-head, drama, product-seeding, or science videos. It is intended for fast drafting of scripts with concise voiceover lines and a strong first-three-seconds hook.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests such as "write a script" may be handled as Chinese short-video script generation.

Mitigation: Use explicit task wording or disable this skill when the desired script is not a short-video script.

Risk: Generated product-seeding or science scripts may include claims that need factual, legal, or brand review before publication.

Mitigation: Review factual claims, product claims, and compliance-sensitive wording before filming or publishing.
