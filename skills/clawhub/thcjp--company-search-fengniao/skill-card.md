## Description: <br>
Company Search Fengniao helps agents look up company registry, business, credit, ownership, investment, change-record, and enforcement-risk information using Fengniao/Riskbird-style workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is flagged as suspicious because company lookup, SEO, and financial-risk claims are mixed without clear boundaries. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for lawful company due diligence or registry-style lookup, and do not rely on it as a general SEO or financial-advice tool. <br>
Risk: Company and personal risk information can be sensitive or misleading if queried or shared without a legitimate reason. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the lookup subject, apply appropriate privacy controls, and review outputs before acting on or sharing them. <br>
