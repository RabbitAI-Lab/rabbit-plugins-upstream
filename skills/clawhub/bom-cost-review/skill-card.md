## Description: <br>
Reviews bills of materials for cost rollups, cost drivers, sourcing risks, cost-down opportunities, and tariff or logistics sensitivity. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Procurement, sourcing, and engineering teams use this skill to review a BOM against cost targets, supplier exposure, lifecycle status, MOQ fit, and landed-cost sensitivity before acting on sourcing or design changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: BOMs can contain confidential pricing, supplier, forecast, or product-stage information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only BOM and sourcing data that is appropriate for analysis in the agent environment. <br>
Risk: Lifecycle, tariff, supplier, lead-time, and availability conclusions may become outdated or depend on incomplete user-provided data. <br>
Mitigation: Verify claims against current vendor, distributor, lifecycle, tariff, and logistics sources before making procurement or engineering decisions. <br>
Risk: Cost-down recommendations may be misleading when costs, volumes, MOQs, lifecycle status, or sourcing details are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Label missing fields clearly and treat savings estimates or sourcing actions as review support until confirmed by engineering and sourcing owners. <br>
