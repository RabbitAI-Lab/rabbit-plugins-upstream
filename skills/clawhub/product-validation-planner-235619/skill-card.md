## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert early product uncertainty into validation plans, prototypes, checklists, positioning, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad product, startup, SaaS, prototype, and validation triggers may activate the skill for requests where a narrower planning workflow is expected. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome, constraints, inputs, and success criteria before producing the final plan or artifact. <br>
Risk: Product-validation guidance can be misleading if assumptions, market signals, or user evidence are weak. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions visible, validate the output against stated success criteria, and list remaining risks or follow-up work. <br>
