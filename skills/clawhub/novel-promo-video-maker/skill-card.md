## Description:

Turn story text into narrated vertical video scenes with consistent illustrated characters, beat-by-beat narration, and clips matched to their narration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and story-channel creators use this skill to adapt a novel chapter, web-novel excerpt, or script into a narrated vertical video for promotion, recaps, or serialized posting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broader shared-account permissions than its workflow needs, including voice-writing authority.

Mitigation: Review requested account permissions before approving access; ask the publisher to narrow unnecessary permissions.

Risk: Submitted story text and selected files are processed by Beatra and its model providers.

Mitigation: Review the privacy policy and avoid submitting sensitive material unless that processing is acceptable.

Risk: A removal defect can disconnect other Beatra skills, and inventory validation needs correction.

Mitigation: Check other connected Beatra skills before removal; ask the publisher to fix inventory validation.

**Output Type(s):** [Video, Images, Audio, Text]

**Other Properties Related to Output:** [Illustrated character appearance is kept consistent across beats, and each clip is matched to its narration.]
