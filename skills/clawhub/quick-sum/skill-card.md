## Description:

Summarizes long text, articles, and documents into concise bullet points that preserve key facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People summarizing long articles or documents use this skill to get a quick overview of the most important points without losing key facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Important details may be lost or misstated in a short summary.

Mitigation: Verify important summaries against the original source.

Risk: Sensitive input text is processed by the agent.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting highly sensitive text unless you are comfortable having the agent process it.
