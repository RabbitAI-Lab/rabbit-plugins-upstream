## Description:

Automotive Patent Valuation evaluates automotive patents with a 14-factor model across technical, legal, and economic dimensions, including SASAC high-value patent label mapping, competitor benchmarking, and strategic recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent, IP strategy, and automotive business teams use this skill to screen and score automotive patent portfolios, compare competitors, map SASAC high-value patent labels, and produce action recommendations for portfolio management.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live patent retrieval depends on external PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP tools and account authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm the required tool access and account authorization before relying on live patent data; without that setup, use the skill as an analysis framework only.

Risk: Litigation, SEP, transfer, and abandonment recommendations may affect legal or commercial decisions.

Mitigation: Have qualified IP, legal, and business reviewers validate generated recommendations before taking action.
