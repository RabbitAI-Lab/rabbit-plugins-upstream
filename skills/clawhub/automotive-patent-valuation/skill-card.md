## Description:

Assists agents with high-value automotive patent evaluation using a 14-factor model across technical, legal, and economic dimensions, including competitive benchmarking and strategic recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and patent strategy teams use this skill to score automotive patent portfolios, compare competitors, map SASAC high-value patent labels, and prepare portfolio action recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill depends on a configured PatSnap or Zhihuiya MCP patent-data account for live retrieval.

Mitigation: Confirm account authorization and tool availability before relying on generated portfolio conclusions; without access, use the skill only as an evaluation framework.

Risk: Patent valuation, SEP, litigation, and portfolio actions can affect legal and business decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and have patent counsel or internal reviewers validate scores, assumptions, and recommended actions before execution.
