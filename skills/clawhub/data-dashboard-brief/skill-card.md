## Description:

Drafts actionable data-dashboard requirements from user roles, business questions, available data sources, and refresh needs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Data analysts and business users use this skill to turn a dashboard request into a structured brief for implementation, covering key metrics, drill-downs, alerts, acceptance criteria, and MVP priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad dashboard and reporting phrases may invoke the skill during unrelated reporting discussions.

Mitigation: Confirm that a dashboard requirements brief is wanted before using its output.

Risk: Suggested metrics or data dependencies may not match available sources.

Mitigation: Confirm field definitions and source availability with data owners before implementation.
