## Description: <br>
Guides an agent to plan accurate educational and how-to social content by choosing one teachable topic, structuring it as a clear lesson, and routing it to the right downstream content format. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and social content agents use this skill to turn brand, audience, and content-pillar context into educational posts, tutorials, explainers, myth-busters, or authority-building lessons. It helps select the lesson angle, enforce sourcing and YMYL care, and hand the result to format writers such as carousel, video, thread, or caption writers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Educational marketing content can become misleading when the agent or user invents steps, statistics, or expertise. <br>
Mitigation: Require real sources for claims, keep lessons within the brand's genuine expertise, and omit or narrow topics that cannot be taught accurately. <br>
Risk: Health, finance, legal, or safety how-to content can cause harm if presented as professional advice. <br>
Mitigation: Add a not-professional-advice disclaimer, point readers to a qualified professional, and apply extra human review before publication. <br>
Risk: Downstream publishing through WoopSocial could release sensitive or regulated educational content without adequate review. <br>
Mitigation: Keep publishing and scheduling under explicit user review, especially for regulated or sensitive topics. <br>
