## Description: <br>
The teach-to-grow content type picks a genuinely useful topic, teaches one thing completely, structures it as a clear lesson, keeps it accurate, and routes it to the right format. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External content and social media teams use this skill to plan educational posts, tutorials, explainers, myth-busters, and authority-building lessons. It helps choose one teachable topic, structure the lesson, prepare teaching copy and calls to action, and route the work to the appropriate downstream format writer. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Educational posts may contain incorrect or unsupported claims, especially when the topic touches health, finance, legal, or safety matters. <br>
Mitigation: Review public posts before scheduling, cite real sources for claims, stay within genuine expertise, and add professional-advice disclaimers with a pointer to qualified professionals for YMYL topics. <br>
Risk: The skill routes work to downstream content-production workflows, so generated lesson plans can be mistaken for final, verified assets. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as planning and drafting guidance; have the appropriate format writer or design workflow build the final asset and keep human or expert accuracy review before publishing. <br>
