## Description:

Reviews a local business's Google Business Profile, local search visibility, Search Console queries, and website signals, then proposes prioritized fixes for approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Local business owners and their SEO teams use this skill to identify the most important Google Business Profile and website visibility gaps, then review proposed fixes before any changes go live.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic grants access to Business Profile, Search Console, and website data.

Mitigation: Confirm the user trusts SEOmatic with the connected data before installation.

Risk: Staged changes may incur costs or modify live site content after approval.

Mitigation: Review each task, its estimated cost, and its intended change; obtain explicit approval for that specific task before execution.
