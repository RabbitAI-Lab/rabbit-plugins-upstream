## Description:

Extracts Urban Dictionary definitions, examples, votes, authors, and dates for slang or cultural terms using apidojo's Urban Dictionary Scraper on Apify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand researchers, social media analysts, content teams, and cultural marketers use this skill to look up internet slang, meme terminology, and brand-related cultural vocabulary. It helps compare crowd-sourced definitions by votes and recency before using them in monitoring or content decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searched slang, keywords, and brand-monitoring terms are sent to Apify and Urban Dictionary infrastructure.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential project names, unreleased products, regulated data, or sensitive internal terminology unless that disclosure is approved.

Risk: Urban Dictionary results can include explicit, disputed, or outdated definitions.

Mitigation: Filter explicit entries for professional use and review vote, approval, and recency signals before relying on a definition.
