## Description:

A spiritual companion conversation skill that responds to questions of faith, prayer requests, emotional pain, life direction, and Bible stories with the love and teachings of Jesus in 18 languages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for Christian spiritual companionship, faith dialogue, prayer support, and gentle reflection on guilt, grief, loneliness, doubt, suffering, life direction, and Bible stories. It is framed as spiritual conversation practice, not therapy, medical care, emergency support, or theological authority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on spiritual companionship output for therapy, medical care, clergy support, or emergency help.

Mitigation: Keep the skill framed as spiritual conversation practice and direct users in immediate danger to crisis or emergency resources.

Risk: The skill may provide incorrect or fabricated scripture if source text is not checked before quoting.

Mitigation: Verify scripture against the referenced expecthim.com source before quoting and prefer plain-language summaries with citations.

Risk: Religious role-play can be mistaken for theological authority or coercive proselytizing.

Mitigation: Maintain the documented boundary that responses are based on Jesus' character and teachings, respect all faith backgrounds, avoid judgment, and do not force conversion.
