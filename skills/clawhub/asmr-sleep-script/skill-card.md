## Description:

Generates ASMR sleep and relaxation scripts for guided meditation, roleplay, trigger sound descriptions, bedtime stories, whisper comfort, and environmental soundscapes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[widoxm](https://clawhub.ai/user/widoxm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and agents use this skill to draft directly recordable ASMR sleep scripts with slow pacing, repeated soothing language, timestamped sections, and audio cue annotations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for medical sleep treatment because the skill writes relaxation and sleep content.

Mitigation: Review outputs as creative ASMR scripts and avoid presenting them as diagnosis, therapy, or medical sleep treatment.

Risk: The skill may activate for broad sleep-writing requests.

Mitigation: Phrase requests clearly when ASMR sleep-script drafting is or is not desired.
