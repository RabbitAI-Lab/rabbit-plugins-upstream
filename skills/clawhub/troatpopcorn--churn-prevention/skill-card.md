## Description: <br>
Helps agents design SaaS churn-prevention systems, including cancellation flows, dynamic save offers, failed-payment recovery, and retention measurement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[troatpopcorn](https://clawhub.ai/user/troatpopcorn) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Growth teams, founders, customer success operators, and developers use this skill to diagnose churn and design cancellation, dunning, save-offer, and proactive retention workflows for subscription products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Retention guidance may be turned into customer-facing cancellation, billing, or payment-recovery changes that affect user trust or compliance. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed flows, offers, copy, and billing automation with product, legal, and support stakeholders before deploying them to production. <br>
