## Description:

AI-powered bank credit memo and credit analysis report writer that generates structured credit analysis reports, credit ratings, and facility recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bank credit officers, credit committee members, and relationship managers use this skill to draft internal credit memos, rating rationales, facility recommendations, and specialized credit-analysis report variants from borrower and financial inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive borrower, financial, or personal guarantor information during credit memo drafting.

Mitigation: Use sanitized or appropriately authorized credit-analysis data, minimize inputs to aggregated financial and conclusion-level information, and avoid full bank statements, raw reports, customer lists, supplier lists, employee rosters, and natural-person identity details.

Risk: Generated credit ratings, facility recommendations, and memo conclusions could be incorrect or incomplete if source data is stale, unsourced, or not independently checked.

Mitigation: Treat all outputs as drafts for qualified credit officers, risk managers, and authorized approvers to verify against source data before any lending decision or approval workflow.
