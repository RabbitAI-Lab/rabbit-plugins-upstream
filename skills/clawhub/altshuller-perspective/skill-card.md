## Description:

A TRIZ reasoning persona skill that helps agents analyze technical, engineering, product, and innovation problems through Genrich Altshuller's contradiction, ideal final result, invention-level, and technology-evolution frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and innovation practitioners use this skill to structure problems with TRIZ-style contradiction analysis, ideal final result thinking, invention-level assessment, and technology evolution patterns. It is best suited for technical and product reasoning, not as a substitute for dedicated TRIZ software or authoritative historical analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can present TRIZ-style persona reasoning as if it were Altshuller's own view on modern domains.

Mitigation: Label modern or extrapolated analysis as inference, and distinguish any tool-derived database evidence from Altshuller's historical writings.

Risk: Optional Zhihuiya MCP use may require third-party account authorization and live database lookups.

Mitigation: Enable that service only in environments where the account authorization, data access, and output labeling requirements are acceptable.

Risk: The skill is a reasoning guide and does not replace full TRIZ software, contradiction-matrix lookup, or expert review.

Mitigation: Use outputs as structured guidance and validate important engineering decisions with domain experts or dedicated TRIZ tools.
