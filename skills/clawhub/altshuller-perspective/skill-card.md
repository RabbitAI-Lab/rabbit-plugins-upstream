## Description:

Provides a Genrich Altshuller/TRIZ thinking perspective for analyzing engineering, product, and innovation problems through contradictions, ideal final results, invention levels, and technology evolution patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers, product teams, and innovation practitioners use this skill to frame technical, engineering, product, and methodology questions with TRIZ-style contradiction analysis, ideal final result reasoning, invention-level assessment, and technology evolution patterns.
