## Description:

Checks whether specified fields are present on Chinese medical-device labels and instructions, returning a checklist for human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0 (release metadata); documentation and theory text: all rights reserved

## Use Case:

Medical-device teams can use this local checklist to identify missing label and instruction fields before human review against current Chinese regulatory requirements; it does not establish compliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A field-presence checklist may be mistaken for a full regulatory or legal compliance finding.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer verify label content and applicability against current official NMPA and provincial authority text before making regulatory decisions.

Risk: Bundled code-license descriptions conflict with release metadata, and documentation and theory text reserve rights.

Mitigation: Confirm applicable code terms with the publisher before reuse, and obtain permission before redistributing reserved documentation or theory text.
