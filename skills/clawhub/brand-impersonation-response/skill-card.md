## Description: <br>
Respond to brand or executive impersonation incidents with a structured incident response covering verification, takedown sequencing, stakeholder communications, and follow-up hardening. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Security, communications, legal, trust and safety, and product teams use this skill to coordinate a response when a fake account, app, domain, support channel, executive deepfake, or scam content impersonates their brand. It helps preserve evidence, prioritize takedowns, draft audience-specific communications, and define controls that reduce repeat incidents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated takedown, public, customer, or law-enforcement response materials may be inaccurate if incident evidence is incomplete or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Use preserved incident evidence and route drafts through appropriate legal, communications, and security review before filing reports or publishing statements. <br>
Risk: Public communications can unintentionally amplify a low-reach impersonation attempt. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's proportionality guidance: warn targeted or affected audiences first, answer inbound questions, and broadcast publicly only when reach or harm justifies it. <br>
