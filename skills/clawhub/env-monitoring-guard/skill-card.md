## Description:

Reviews environmental monitoring reports before release against Chinese environmental laws, monitoring management rules, data falsification rules, and HJ technical specifications, then flags non-compliant content with suggested revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Environmental compliance reviewers, monitoring organizations, and report preparers use this skill to check Chinese environmental monitoring reports, pollution-source monitoring reports, environmental quality reports, EIA baseline monitoring reports, and public-facing monitoring materials before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides compliance-review guidance for environmental monitoring documents and may not reflect the latest applicable Chinese standards, laws, or jurisdiction-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Users should verify cited standards and laws are current for the report type and jurisdiction, and obtain qualified legal or regulator review before relying on the report for formal compliance decisions.
