## Description:

Helps homeowners and renters plan a design direction for their space and provides a Roomvana studio link to preview changes using their own photo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjldc](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjldc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renters use this skill to get a brief for room, exterior, or yard redesigns, paint changes, virtual staging, or decluttering, followed by a preselected Roomvana link to preview the result on their own photo.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may upload a photo revealing private details of their home to Roomvana.

Mitigation: Review Roomvana's account and privacy terms before uploading; avoid photos showing addresses, people, valuables, documents, or other sensitive details.

Risk: A generated redesign may not precisely reflect a buildable or color-accurate result.

Mitigation: Treat the result as a preview rather than a construction plan and check physical paint samples in the actual light.
