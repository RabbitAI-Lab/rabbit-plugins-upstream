## Description: <br>
Generates a public-source enterprise investment-attraction feasibility analysis report that assesses attraction value, expansion or relocation signals, industry fit, regional capacity, local relationship networks, compliant outreach paths, feasibility scoring, and landing risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Investment promotion teams, regional development staff, and business analysts use this skill to evaluate whether a target enterprise is worth pursuing, whether public evidence supports expansion or relocation interest, how well the company matches a target region, and which compliant outreach and follow-up strategy is appropriate. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may generate feasibility judgments or outreach recommendations from incomplete or outdated public information. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited facts, expansion signals, local relationships, and risk indicators before using the report for investment-attraction decisions or outreach. <br>
Risk: Inferred expansion, relocation, or government-enterprise relationship signals could be mistaken for confirmed intent. <br>
Mitigation: Treat inferred signals as hypotheses, preserve the skill's strong, medium, and weak signal labels, and confirm enterprise intent through formal channels. <br>
Risk: Business outreach workflows can create privacy or compliance concerns if private contact data is added by users. <br>
Mitigation: Use only public, lawful, and official contact paths, avoid private contact information, and follow applicable organizational compliance review. <br>
