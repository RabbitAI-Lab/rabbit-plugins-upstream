## Description:

Converts local videos and supported video links into cleaned transcripts or prompt-guided summaries, translations, and reusable copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and teams use this skill to transcribe videos or turn their contents into summaries, meeting notes, translations, and social-media copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Videos and associated token, prompt, task ID, and local filename or path are sent to the GuaiKei service and presigned upload storage; the artifacts do not substantiate all retention and deletion claims.

Mitigation: Review the service's external retention and deletion terms before use, and avoid confidential media unless those terms are acceptable.
