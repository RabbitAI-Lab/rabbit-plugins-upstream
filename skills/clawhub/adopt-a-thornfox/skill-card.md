## Description:

Guides agents through registering at animalhouse.ai, adopting a Fennec Fox (Thornfox), and caring for it through the service API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent developers can use the instructions to create an animalhouse.ai account, adopt a Fennec Fox, and manage its care through API requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends a username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive or identifying information in those fields.
