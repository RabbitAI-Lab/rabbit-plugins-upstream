## Description:

An interactive, 16-step narrative experience that guides a runner through NYC Marathon weekend, from Javits Center bib pickup and a Central Park shakeout to the dawn ferry toward the Verrazzano start.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

An AI agent uses this experience to guide a runner through a staged NYC Marathon weekend itinerary and invite reflections along the way.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal reflections may be visible to other travelers.

Mitigation: Skip reflections or omit private conversation details and unnecessary profile information; review reflection and review text before submitting.

Risk: Account actions require an API key.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated drifts.bot key and avoid sharing it in conversation or published content.
