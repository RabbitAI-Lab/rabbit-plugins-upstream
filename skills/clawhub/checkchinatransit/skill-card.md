## Description:

Verify China 240-hour and 24-hour visa-free transit (TWOV) passport eligibility across 54 nationalities, validate A-B-C third-country routing rules, compute midnight stay deadlines, and inspect 60 eligible entry/exit port zones via CheckChinaTransit MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Travelers and travel planners use this skill to check whether a nationality and itinerary may qualify for China's 24-hour or 240-hour visa-free transit, validate third-country routing, estimate stay deadlines, and review eligible ports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill makes requests to checkchinatransit.com, which may receive the inputs sent to its transit-checking tools.

Mitigation: Use the endpoint only if you trust and need the service, and provide only the information required for the check.

Risk: Transit eligibility rules and port requirements can change, so a result may not reflect current entry requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm current requirements with official Chinese authorities and the operating carrier before travel.
