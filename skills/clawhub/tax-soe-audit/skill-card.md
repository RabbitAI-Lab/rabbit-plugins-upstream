## Description: <br>
Assists with Chinese state-owned enterprise economic responsibility audit tax-risk review, including invoice, off-book account, state capital return, fiscal fund, overseas asset tax, rectification, and official expense compliance scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External tax, compliance, and audit users can use this skill to ask state-owned enterprise economic responsibility audit tax questions, run structured self-checks, and generate risk review or rectification guidance. It is focused on Chinese tax compliance and audit workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China (for Chinese tax and state-owned enterprise audit contexts) <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive audit, tax, company identity, or financial facts may be processed by the cloud service and reflected in local credentials, identifiers, or question logs. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only under an approved service and retention model, and avoid entering confidential company names, credit codes, investigation facts, or detailed financial/audit evidence unless approved. <br>
Risk: The security verdict flags the release for review because disclosure around cloud processing and local persistence is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before deployment, confirm acceptable data-handling terms, and limit use to non-confidential or approved scenarios until that review is complete. <br>
