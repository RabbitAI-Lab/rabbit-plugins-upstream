## Description:

A reference hub for medical-device registration pathways and classification across major markets, with focused guidance on UDI, technical files, quality systems, testing, labeling, and post-market topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Medical-device registration engineers and market-access teams use this skill to locate jurisdiction-specific pathways, topic references, and official sources for product classification and registration work. It can also help prepare cross-market comparison tables from the bundled references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, including China, the United States, the European Union, Japan, ASEAN, and Latin America

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements and product classifications can change, and the bundled verification records or links may be outdated.

Mitigation: Check current official regulator sources and verify product-specific classifications before making compliance decisions.
