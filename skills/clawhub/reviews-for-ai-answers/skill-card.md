## Description:

Build an honest review presence on the platforms AI engines check before recommending a business, such as G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Yelp, Google, app stores and Amazon. Use for "get more reviews", "AI says customers complain about us" or the review side of an AEO, GEO or LLM SEO plan. Finds cited platforms, fixes profiles, sets up honest asks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners, marketers, and reputation teams can use this skill to identify which review platforms appear in their own AI search results, improve those profiles, and plan a fair review-request and response process. It also helps turn review themes into buyer language and a recurring measurement plan.
