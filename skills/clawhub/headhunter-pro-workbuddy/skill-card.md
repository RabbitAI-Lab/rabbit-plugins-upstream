## Description:

Headhunter Pro supports end-to-end recruiting workflows, including talent mapping, candidate screening, interview assessment, outreach, offer negotiation, onboarding, and compliance planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[walterjiang-sketch](https://clawhub.ai/user/walterjiang-sketch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters and talent advisers use it to structure hiring searches, assess candidates against job requirements, draft personalized communications, and manage candidate and client workflows. It produces decision support for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with China-focused recruiting workflows and selected cross-border guidance

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports undeclared CI and container-test generation.

Mitigation: Clarify those reported capabilities and their permissions before installation.

Risk: ClawScan flags privacy risks in candidate-data workflows.

Mitigation: Set safeguards for consent, access, sharing, retention and deletion, and future contact before using candidate records.

Risk: ClawScan reports recommendations for overly broad GitHub access.

Mitigation: Limit GitHub permissions to what the recruiting research task needs.

Risk: The security guidance says the scan is partial because some local references were missing.

Mitigation: Review the complete release contents and resolve the reported concerns before installation.
