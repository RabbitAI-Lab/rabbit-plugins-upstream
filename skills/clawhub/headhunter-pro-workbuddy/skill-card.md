## Description:

Headhunter Pro supports end-to-end recruiting workflows, including talent mapping, role analysis, candidate screening, outreach, offer negotiation, onboarding, and pipeline management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[walterjiang-sketch](https://clawhub.ai/user/walterjiang-sketch)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External recruiters and recruiting teams use the skill to map talent, analyze roles, screen and assess candidates, draft outreach and recommendation reports, support offer and onboarding workflows, and track recruiting pipelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Candidate personal information and real recordings may be handled without clearly defined consent, retention, or contact preferences.

Mitigation: Use reviewed drafts, minimize personal information, keep real candidate recordings out of unapproved demos, and define consent, retention, and contact preferences.

Risk: The recruiting guidance recommends excessive GitHub permissions and leaves approval boundaries for scheduled monitoring and record removal unclear.

Mitigation: Avoid broad GitHub tokens in favor of narrowly scoped access; explicitly approve scheduled monitoring and record removal, and prefer reversible archiving where possible.
