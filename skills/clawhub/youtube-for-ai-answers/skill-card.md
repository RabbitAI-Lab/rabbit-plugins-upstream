## Description:

Plans YouTube videos and supporting site content to improve their chances of being cited in AI-generated search answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and search teams use this skill to identify questions where video is useful, plan and package videos for discovery, and prepare supporting embeds and measurement plans. It also guides teams on accurate captions and transparent creator-review disclosures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Standard YouTube embeds may have visitor privacy implications.

Mitigation: Review the privacy implications before adding embeds to a site.
