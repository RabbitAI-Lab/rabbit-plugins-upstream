## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to improve Russian text outputs, proofread or rewrite Russian content, and score text quality against bundled editorial, typography, UX writing, and business-writing references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Always-on Russian text normalization can change punctuation, spacing, quotes, and wording when exact formatting matters.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to preserve exact formatting or verbatim content when reviewing legal text, quotations, source material, or documents where byte-level formatting matters.

Risk: Editorial rewrites may change the author's intended tone or style.

Mitigation: Provide the desired register or style constraints, and review the returned change summary before adopting edits.
