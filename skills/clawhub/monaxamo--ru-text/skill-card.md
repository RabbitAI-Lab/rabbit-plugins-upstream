## Use Case:

Russian-speaking writers, editors, product teams, and agent developers use this skill to proofread, rewrite, score, and produce Russian text with consistent typography, clearer style, and domain-aware editorial guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad automatic activation can change Russian wording, punctuation, or typography when exact preservation is required.

Mitigation: Use explicit preservation instructions for quoted material, source text, and formatting-sensitive content; review corrected output before applying it.

Risk: Editorial guidance can alter tone or style beyond the user's intended register.

Mitigation: State the desired style, audience, and constraints in the prompt, since the artifact treats user-specified style as higher priority than default rules.
