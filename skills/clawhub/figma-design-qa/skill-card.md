## Description: <br>
Runs a pre-handoff QA checklist for Figma designs and produces a structured report on file hygiene, component usage, accessibility, and handoff readiness. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Designers, product managers, and engineering teams use this skill to check whether a Figma feature or screen is ready for engineering handoff and to capture specific fixes before implementation starts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Figma links or proprietary design details may expose confidential product information during review. <br>
Mitigation: Share only designs and context that the user intends the agent to review for the QA task. <br>
