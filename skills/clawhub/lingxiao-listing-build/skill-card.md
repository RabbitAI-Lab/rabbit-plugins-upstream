## Description: <br>
Builds TikTok Shop and Etsy listing drafts from product data and returns platform-specific validation reports for titles, tags, descriptions, image slots, and import readiness. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sellers, marketplace operators, and listing specialists use this skill to assemble import-ready TikTok Shop or Etsy listing drafts, check hard platform limits, and review Etsy title, tag, description, and image readiness before upload. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product details and listing text provided by the user may be sent to Lingxiao's MCP service. <br>
Mitigation: Review product data before use and avoid sending confidential, regulated, or unnecessary sensitive information. <br>
Risk: Paid copy-generation actions require an account, confirmation, and quota use. <br>
Mitigation: Review the quoted paid action before confirming and monitor account quota consumption. <br>
Risk: Platform rules can change and successful pre-validation does not guarantee marketplace approval. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill output as a pre-upload check, verify final requirements in the platform backend, and test one listing before bulk upload. <br>
