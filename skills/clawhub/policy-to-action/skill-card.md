## Description:

政策翻译官把复杂政策条文转化为面向企业或个人的标准化行动报告，说明能不能用、能省多少、该怎么做，并输出 HTML 报告和可转发文本总览。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking business users, individuals, and advisors use this skill to turn policy documents into practical impact assessments, benefit estimates, and prioritized next steps. It is intended for policy interpretation workflows that require official-source verification before decisions are made.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated policy, tax, or legal conclusions may be incomplete, outdated, or misleading if source material is misunderstood.

Mitigation: Verify conclusions against official sources and qualified professional advice before making business, tax, or legal decisions.

Risk: Subject profiles can contain sensitive personal or business details.

Mitigation: Provide anonymized or minimal subject details where possible, and review reports before sharing them externally.
