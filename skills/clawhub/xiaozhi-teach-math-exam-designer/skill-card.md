## Description:

帮助初高中数学教师设计测评蓝图和双向细目表、标注题目来源与版权，并分析逐题表现与试卷信度。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初高中数学教师用它规划诊断性、形成性或终结性测评，编制知识点与能力层级细目表，核对试题来源，并根据逐题得分分析试卷表现；教师逐条确认后才写回记录。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused curriculum and Chinese-language workflows; localize curriculum, consent practices and safety referrals for other regions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect assessment items or misleading statistical interpretations could affect teaching decisions.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify items, calculations, sample size and conclusions before use.

Risk: Assessment records may contain student information or unlicensed exam questions.

Mitigation: Use de-identified data, check question rights and confidentiality, and obtain teacher confirmation before writing records.

Risk: Curriculum terms, consent assumptions and safety referrals may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before deploying outside the intended Chinese-language, China-curriculum setting.
