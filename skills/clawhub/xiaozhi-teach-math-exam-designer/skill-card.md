## Description:

帮助初中数学教师设计测评蓝图与双向细目表、选编候选题，并分析考后逐题统计结果。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中数学教师可用它规划诊断性、形成性或终结性测评，制作知识点与能力层级对应的命题蓝图，并分析考后逐题难度、区分度及班级统计。候选题与数据写回须经教师审核确认。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese-language K–12 mathematics teaching, especially Mainland China curricula

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions or statistics may be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Teachers should check each blueprint, candidate question, calculation, and report before use; verify AI-generated questions before including them in an exam.

Risk: Student records or confidential exam material may be exposed or written without authorization.

Mitigation: Do not provide unreleased exam questions or identifiable student data; use aliases, check consent, and require item-by-item teacher confirmation before saving or sharing results.

Risk: Copied questions may infringe third-party rights.

Mitigation: Check each question's source and reuse rights; retain only an index when the full question cannot be stored.
