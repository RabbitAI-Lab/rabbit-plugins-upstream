## Description: <br>
Kano Model guides users through Kano requirements analysis, classifies needs from positive and negative responses, calculates Better-Worse coefficients, and produces a requirement priority matrix. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, product teams, service designers, and quality practitioners use this skill to collect Kano responses, classify requirements, calculate Better-Worse coefficients, and prepare priority recommendations for planning or review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill references an analyzer script and templates that were not present in the inspected artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the installed package includes the expected analyzer and templates, then run a sample Kano classification before relying on automated classification or formatted report generation. <br>
Risk: Kano classifications depend on user-provided positive and negative evaluations and can be misleading if treated as a substitute for representative survey data. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's output as planning support, and collect or verify representative questionnaire data before making major product, service, or quality decisions. <br>
