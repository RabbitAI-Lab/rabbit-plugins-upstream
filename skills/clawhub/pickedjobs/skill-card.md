## Description:

Find and compare curated tech jobs through PickedJobs using role, location, seniority, technology, salary, and quota preferences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yashgandhe666](https://clawhub.ai/user/yashgandhe666)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External job seekers use this skill to find and compare curated technology roles against their preferences, check search quota, and review application links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting the PickedJobs service may involve handling an API credential.

Mitigation: Use the service's setup instructions for OAuth or API keys, and never share credentials in chat.

Risk: Job postings may be closed or omit important details such as location, salary, or visa information.

Mitigation: Verify promising roles with the full job details, call out unknowns, exclude closed roles, and direct users to the employer's application page.
