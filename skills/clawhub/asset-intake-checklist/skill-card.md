## Description: <br>
Normalize new resources into an asset/resource library with intake metadata, naming, storage, ownership, and follow-up checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, librarians, and operations teams use this skill to register files, links, datasets, templates, documents, scripts, images, and reusable components in shared asset libraries with consistent metadata and follow-up checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Resources with unclear license or redistribution rights could be published externally without approval. <br>
Mitigation: Flag missing license or usage constraints and confirm redistribution rights before external publication. <br>
Risk: Private, secret, or personal data could be cataloged into an inappropriate collection or synced publicly. <br>
Mitigation: Review sensitivity classification, mark restricted resources clearly, and avoid public sync for sensitive assets. <br>
Risk: Duplicate, overwrite, move, delete, or replacement actions could disrupt the canonical asset record or remove the only copy. <br>
Mitigation: Compare duplicate candidates by title, path, hash, and version, and ask before overwriting, moving, deleting, or replacing a sole copy. <br>
Risk: Incorrect metadata or destination paths could make assets difficult to find after intake. <br>
Mitigation: Record required metadata and verify the entry can be found by name, keyword, and path. <br>
