## Description:

Assesses which business tasks can be delegated to an AI agent by scoring risk, setting autonomy levels, and defining approval gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operations teams, and agent developers use this skill to decide which repeatable tasks to delegate, assign an autonomy level, and specify approval, access, logging, and promotion rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A delegation map may be mistaken for an implemented permission or approval control.

Mitigation: Configure and verify actual permissions, approval gates, and monitoring separately before relying on an agent to act.
