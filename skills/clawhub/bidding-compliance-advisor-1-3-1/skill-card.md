## Description: <br>
This skill helps bidders check bid documents against tender requirements for formatting, qualifications, signatures, seals, packaging, copies, substantive responsiveness, key deadlines, legal-review questions, and protest-support workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Bid teams, proposal managers, and compliance reviewers use this skill to prepare structured pre-submission checklists, identify missing or risky bid materials, and prioritize final countdown tasks. It is scoped to bidder-side tender compliance and does not replace legal counsel or issue binding legal conclusions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bid documents, pricing, qualifications, and strategy may contain sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the excerpts needed for compliance review, avoid unnecessary confidential pricing details, and handle materials in a secure environment. <br>
Risk: Legal or bid-compliance conclusions may be incomplete or outdated if they are not checked against current official sources. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as review support, verify legal conclusions with current official sources or qualified counsel, and do not rely on the skill as a binding legal determination. <br>
