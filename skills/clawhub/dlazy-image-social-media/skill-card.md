## Description:

Guides agents in planning platform-specific social media visuals, layouts, on-image copy, and captions for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Xiaohongshu, and other platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media creators and marketers use this skill to plan visuals for multiple platforms, including format, safe areas, hooks, layout, and separate on-image text and captions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was benign but had medium confidence and covered only half of the components.

Mitigation: Review the installation instructions and referenced items before installing, as the scan guidance recommends.

Risk: Prompts and parameters are sent to dLazy-hosted services, and supplied local media files are uploaded for model processing.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive prompts or media unless you are comfortable sharing them with those services.

Risk: The documented generation workflow requires Chinese-language confirmation steps.

Mitigation: Check that the confirmation workflow is suitable before using the skill.
