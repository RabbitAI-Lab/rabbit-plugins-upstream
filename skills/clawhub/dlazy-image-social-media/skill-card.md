## Description:

A structured skill for adapting social media visuals and copy to the native formats of Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Xiaohongshu, and other platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media creators and marketers use the skill to plan platform-specific visual layouts, short in-image text, and separate captions, then request image generation through the dLazy service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review found a benign verdict with medium confidence, but some components and references were not fully analyzed.

Mitigation: Review the installation and data-handling details before installing, especially before sending prompts or local media.

Risk: Prompts and local media files may be sent to dLazy's hosted service for processing.

Mitigation: Check prompts and media for sensitive information and confirm you are authorized to share them before submitting.
