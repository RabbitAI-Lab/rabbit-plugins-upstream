## Description:

A structured skill for planning platform-specific social media visuals, layouts, and copy across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Xiaohongshu, and other channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and social media teams use this skill to plan platform-adapted image layouts, in-image text, and separate captions, then guide image generation and iteration across channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was partial, and referenced material and installation details were not fully assessed.

Mitigation: Review the CLI package and its installation details before installing.

Risk: The skill describes sending prompts and local media to dLazy-hosted APIs and storage for generation.

Mitigation: Only submit prompts and media you are authorized to share, and avoid sensitive content unless the service is approved for it.
