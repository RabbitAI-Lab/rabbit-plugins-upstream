# Prompt library

Use the master prompt as the stable artistic brief. Replace only bracketed variables. Do not translate or invent names, locations, dates, or user-supplied wording.

## Variables

- `[ORIENTATION]`: `vertical 9:16` or `landscape 16:9`, selected from the source photo.
- `[SUBJECT]`: the minimal recognizable subject and key gesture/object relationship.
- `[PALETTE]`: two to four extracted dominant colors, named plainly.
- `[OPTIONAL TYPE]`: a verified short title, place, year, or number; otherwise omit the sentence.

## Master prompt

Create one standalone `[ORIENTATION]` high-end editorial art-publication cover, based only on the supplied reference photo. Reinterpret `[SUBJECT]` as a tiny, centered, minimal hand-drawn paper illustration occupying roughly 10–20% of the lower half of the canvas. Preserve only the most recognizable silhouette, proportions, key pose or gesture, essential object, and core narrative relationship; remove every nonessential detail.

Surround it with expansive negative space on rough warm-white, off-white, or pale natural paper. Use at most four source-derived dominant colors: `[PALETTE]`. Render with refined imperfect hand-drawn lines, a few bold acrylic-like flat color shapes with crisp boundaries, visible dry brush marks, rough paper grain, and slightly irregular organic edges. The mood is quiet, poetic, restrained, innocent, relaxed, thoughtful, highly recognizable, and premium—like a contemporary independent art book or a carefully made children's picture book for adults.

`[OPTIONAL TYPE]` If typography is used, add only one understated, small editorial text element, naturally interacting with the empty space. Never invent facts.

No collage, no multiple photos, no photograph embedded in the poster, no dense composition, no excessive decoration, no more than four dominant colors, no colored-pencil look, no crayon/wax texture, no bleeding watercolor, no pure line art, no realistic complex illustration, no heavy oil paint, no smooth polished digital vector art, no 3D, no gloss, no commercial cartoon character, no e-commerce advertising, no generic poster template, and no excessive typography.

## Invocation starter

`Use $handmade-editorial-poster. Treat every attached photo independently. Generate one vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9 poster per photo, in attachment order, with no collage and no invented text.`

## Targeted retry phrases

| Need | Retry phrase |
| --- | --- |
| More negative space | `Shrink the illustrated subject to 15% of the canvas and remove all nonessential background marks.` |
| More handmade | `Replace smooth vector edges with imperfect ink contours, dry-brush acrylic marks, and visible fibrous paper grain.` |
| Better recognition | `Keep the source pose, silhouette, and the relationship between the person and object unmistakable while simplifying details.` |
| Remove unsafe text | `Remove every word and number; let the image work as a text-free editorial cover.` |
