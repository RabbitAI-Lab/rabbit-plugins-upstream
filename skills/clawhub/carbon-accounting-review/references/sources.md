# Authority and evidence guide

Public landing pages checked 2026-11-12. These URLs are references to inspect, not automatically fetched dependencies. This release does not claim a full clause-by-clause standards review.

| Authority | Public entry | Use and limit |
|---|---|---|
| GHG Protocol Corporate Standard | https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard | Organizational inventory accounting; not verification-process certification |
| GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance | https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-2-guidance | Purchased energy and contractual-instrument quality; inspect edition, corrections and relevant rules |
| GHG Protocol Scope 3 Standard | https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-value-chain-scope-3-standard | Corporate value-chain categories; not automatically valid inter-company comparison |
| GHG Protocol Product Standard | https://ghgprotocol.org/product-standard | Product lifecycle accounting; product rules and comparison conditions need separate attention |

The Scope 2 page identifies the 2015 guidance and a consultation process; the Product page describes work on a joint ISO/GHG Protocol product standard. Do not infer that a consultation or development announcement is a final applicable replacement. Recheck each engagement's reporting year and authority.

For ISO 14064-1 and ISO 14067, obtain the exact authorized edition from the user or official ISO catalog and record accessible clause coverage. No ISO text is bundled, and licensed clause completeness was not verified for this release. Do not claim ISO conformity merely because a checklist resembles a standard.

Record authority/title/edition, access date, page/table/clause, relevant original paraphrase, evidence coverage and unresolved updates. Separate what the source says from project policy and reviewer judgment. Do not scrape or redistribute restricted text.
