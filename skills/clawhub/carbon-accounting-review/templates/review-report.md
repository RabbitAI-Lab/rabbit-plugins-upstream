## 2. Authorities and evidence
| ID | Source / edition | Locator | Observed fact | Coverage / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Separate source statements, reviewer inference and project policy. Do not invent clause numbers.

## 3. Findings
| ID | Status | Check | Evidence IDs / locator | Reason / impact | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses: PASS = this named check supported; ISSUE = observed inconsistency; NOT ASSESSABLE = necessary evidence unavailable; NOT REVIEWED = outside inspected scope. PASS never means inventory-wide compliance or assurance. Give separate arithmetic and applicability/eligibility verdicts, especially for market-based electricity; missing contract evidence means eligibility NOT ASSESSABLE even if the arithmetic passes. Explain severity/materiality basis if used.
