## Description: <br>
Build and execute a content marketing strategy for a solopreneur business, including audience research, content pillars, calendars, distribution, repurposing, and performance metrics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Solopreneurs and business operators use this skill to turn content marketing into a repeatable business system. It guides goal setting, audience research, format and channel selection, calendar planning, distribution, repurposing, and monthly performance review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Audience research may involve customer conversations, support tickets, sales calls, or other business records that contain private or sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only material you have permission to process, remove customer identifiers where possible, and avoid pasting raw names, emails, transcripts, or sensitive records unless there is a clear need. <br>
Risk: Publishing, email sends, community posts, and direct messages can affect customer trust or brand reputation if automated or applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Keep publishing, email sends, and direct messages under explicit human control, and review proposed content before using it externally. <br>
