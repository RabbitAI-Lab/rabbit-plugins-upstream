## Description:

Researches overseas product categories using English search signals and YouTube and TikTok review videos, then summarizes buyer pain points, needs, concerns, and opportunities in Chinese with source quotations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jackchew7143](https://clawhub.ai/user/jackchew7143)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, product researchers, and product teams can evaluate overseas product categories for product selection, listing claims, review-risk planning, and new product decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Weekly reruns can create ongoing API usage and credit costs.

Mitigation: Before enabling weekly runs, review the schedule, API usage, and cancellation details with LinkDigest.

Risk: The security scan had incomplete reference coverage.

Mitigation: Treat findings as partial and verify the relevant source scope before relying on a report.
