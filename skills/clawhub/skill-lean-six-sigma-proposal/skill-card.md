## Description: <br>
Generates Chinese-language Lean Six Sigma project charters for DMAIC project initiation, covering problem statements, goals, scope, team structure, milestones, and expected benefits. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Quality, operations, and process improvement teams use this skill to draft Chinese-language Lean Six Sigma project charters for manufacturing, service, and healthcare DMAIC initiatives. It helps structure project background, baseline data, SMART targets, recommended DMAIC tools, scope, roles, milestones, and expected benefits. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated project charters may contain unsupported baseline metrics, targets, or financial assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review the draft against source data, finance estimates, and internal approval criteria before using it for project authorization. <br>
Risk: Generated content may miss company-specific templates or regulated-industry requirements, especially in healthcare or other compliance-sensitive settings. <br>
Mitigation: Have the relevant process owner, quality lead, or compliance reviewer adapt the charter to required internal and industry standards. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or inconsistent user input can produce a charter that appears more complete than the available evidence supports. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's missing-data and logic-conflict prompts to collect baseline data, confirm targets, and mark unresolved financial benefits as pending review. <br>
