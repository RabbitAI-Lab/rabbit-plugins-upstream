## Use Case:

Developers, designers, QA teams, and product teams use this skill to structure evidence-based accessibility reviews for Android apps, iOS apps, mobile websites, and Web Apps. It helps produce AA-level check results, remediation guidance, regression plans, and acceptance reports without presenting the result as official certification or legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Accessibility findings may be mistaken for official certification or legal advice.

Mitigation: Present results as engineering and QA support, and confirm official certification requirements with the relevant authority before submission.

Risk: Audit screenshots, recordings, code, or accounts may expose passwords, tokens, personal data, or payment information.

Mitigation: Use test accounts and sanitized evidence; avoid real credentials, personal data, tokens, and live payment details during audits.
