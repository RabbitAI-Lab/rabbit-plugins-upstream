## Description: <br>
国学大师 provides Chinese-language traditional-culture guidance using Zhouyi, Qimen Dunjia, Meihua Yishu, Liuyao, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Bazi, feng shui, and auspicious-date methods for reflective advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users can ask for traditional Guoxue-style interpretations, divination-style reflections, fortune analysis, naming and date-selection ideas, and feng shui layout suggestions. The guidance is cultural and advisory, not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or major business advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on traditional divination or lifestyle guidance for high-stakes decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as cultural or reflective advice and direct users to qualified professionals for medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or major business decisions. <br>
Risk: Prompts may request unnecessary personal details such as full birth data or private business information. <br>
Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum information needed and remind users not to share unnecessary sensitive personal or business details. <br>
