## Description:

Analyzes Chinese foreclosure and non-performing asset deals with discount checks, three-scenario returns, bid ceilings, and an auditable Excel valuation model.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investment analysts and deal teams use this skill to assess Chinese foreclosure and distressed-asset opportunities, compare recovery scenarios, prioritize due diligence, and estimate bid limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (market and legal assumptions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Borrower, collateral, litigation, and deal information may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Minimize personal and transaction data and keep working files local with appropriate access controls.

Risk: Incorrect inputs or spreadsheet calculations could misstate returns or bid ceilings.

Mitigation: Review assumptions and generated spreadsheets, run the model verification step, and independently check consequential decisions.

Risk: Valuation outputs can be mistaken for legal, appraisal, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs only as decision support and seek qualified local professional review before a transaction.
