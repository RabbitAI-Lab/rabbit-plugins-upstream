## Description: <br>
This skill turns raw commits, merged pull requests, issue lists, or plain change notes into concise user-facing release notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qdw497874677](https://clawhub.ai/user/qdw497874677) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, release managers, and product teams use this skill to convert raw technical changes into clear release notes for users, developers, operators, or internal stakeholders. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Raw commits, issues, or change notes may contain private information. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive source text before asking the skill to draft release notes. <br>
Risk: Generated release notes may overstate impact if the source changes are incomplete or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Review drafted notes against the source changes before publishing. <br>
