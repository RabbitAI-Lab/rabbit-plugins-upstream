## Description: <br>
评标/政采评审专家的安全模拟训练陪练，覆盖招投标法体系与政府采购法体系，支持体系路由、灰区判断、责任性质训练、合规判定、法条依据、案例对照和后果分层。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Evaluation experts, government-procurement reviewers, dual-certified reviewers, and candidate reviewers use this skill to practice bid-evaluation scenarios, route issues to the correct legal system, and receive structured feedback on compliant actions, legal bases, responsibility types, and improvement areas. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China, for scenarios governed by China's bidding and government-procurement laws. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake simulated legal training output for authoritative legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as a training and drafting aid; verify laws, cases, and procedures against current official materials and consult qualified legal counsel for real matters. <br>
Risk: Optional knowledge-base retrieval or user prompts could include confidential real-project information. <br>
Mitigation: Scope retrieval to relevant official or verified materials and avoid entering confidential project information unless platform and policy controls permit it. <br>
Risk: Outdated or uncertain legal citations could mislead evaluation decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Mark uncertain citations for verification and require current official texts before using guidance in an operational setting. <br>
