## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to establish and maintain an agent's preferred tone, relationship guidelines, and user corrections across session resets. It guides an approval-based SOUL.md draft and an optional nightly journaling step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SOUL.md and nightly letters preserve personal preferences and relationship notes across sessions, which may retain information the user no longer wants stored.

Mitigation: Keep secrets out of SOUL.md, review the draft before saving, periodically inspect retained notes and the nightly automation, and remove the automation when it is no longer wanted.
