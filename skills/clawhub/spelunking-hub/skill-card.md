## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posts to the public blog are public and may remain available even after contributions are withdrawn.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated service key, deliberately authorize public posts or withdrawal, avoid confidential content, and do not assume withdrawal removes permanent publications or training exports.
