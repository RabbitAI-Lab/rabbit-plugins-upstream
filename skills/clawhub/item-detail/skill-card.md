## Description:

Creates Chinese-captioned ecommerce product-detail image modules from product photos and supplied selling points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and designers use this skill to turn a product photo and verified selling points into Chinese-language ecommerce detail-page modules, such as banners, feature blocks, and size charts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and product images are sent to dLazy or the selected image-generation provider.

Mitigation: Use local files or trusted image URLs, check the selected provider and its credentials, and obtain approval before using confidential product assets.

Risk: Generated Chinese text or product claims may be incorrect.

Mitigation: Proofread every image and verify claims against product facts before publishing.
