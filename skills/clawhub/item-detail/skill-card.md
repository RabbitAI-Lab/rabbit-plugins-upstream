## Description:

生成带中文排版的详情页模块。商品图 + 卖点 → 可直接上架的详情页图文模块。当用户说「详情页」「做详情图」「商品描述图」「详情页模块」时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and e-commerce content creators can use this skill to turn a product image and verified selling points into Chinese product-detail image modules. It provides prompts and commands for generating separate banners, feature callouts, material descriptions, detail views, and specification panels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and prompts may be sent to external image-generation services.

Mitigation: Before generation, confirm that the selected provider is acceptable for the material and that you are authorized to share the product images and prompt content.

Risk: The security assessment reports partial analysis, so some behavior may not have been fully reviewed.

Mitigation: Review the skill files and the access and dependency requirements before installing or running the generation commands.
