## Description:

Assess a physical consumer-product idea using public search-interest signals, marketplace listings, and sampled customer feedback in a specified market. Use for product-launch hypotheses, not one-off shopping, market-size estimates, or profit forecasts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product founders, operators, and researchers use this skill to screen a physical consumer-product launch or line-extension hypothesis. It compares public interest, visible marketplace listings, and sampled customer feedback while keeping evidence limits explicit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, subject to supported marketplace coverage in the specified market.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search phrases are shared with Crawlora and relevant search services.

Mitigation: Generalize confidential product concepts before submitting searches.

Risk: Using Crawlora requires an API key and consumes service credits.

Mitigation: Keep the key private and monitor credit use.

Risk: Public search and marketplace samples are limited and do not forecast sales.

Mitigation: Present findings as screening evidence and validate promising hypotheses with independent customer or seller data.
