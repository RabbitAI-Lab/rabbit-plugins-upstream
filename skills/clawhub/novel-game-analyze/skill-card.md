## Description:

Analyzes a novel or writing project for game adaptation, producing a source bible of evidence-backed world rules, player actions, characters, spaces, and visual anchors without prescribing a genre.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Authors and game designers use this skill to extract a traceable foundation for adapting a novel into a game, distinguishing source facts from design possibilities and checking coverage of the requested work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analysis files may expose details from private manuscripts in the project workspace.

Mitigation: Only provide manuscripts you are comfortable analyzing in that workspace and review access to the generated files.
