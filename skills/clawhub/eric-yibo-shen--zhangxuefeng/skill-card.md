## Description: <br>
Gaokao Mentor provides direct, persona-styled Chinese college admissions guidance for students and families, using a city > school > major framework, employment-backward planning, AI-era major risk checks, and reach-match-safety option design. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, especially Chinese high school students and families, use this skill to structure Gaokao admissions decisions around province, score or rank, subject track, family constraints, target cities, major risk, school choice, employment outlook, and university planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Admissions cutoffs, university policies, and labor-market signals can change, making confident guidance stale. <br>
Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current provincial exam authority data, the Ministry of Education Sunshine Gaokao platform, target university admissions pages, and current employment quality reports. <br>
Risk: The skill may need information about minors, family finances, location, scores, and preferences to provide tailored guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary details and avoid unnecessary personal identifiers or sensitive financial information. <br>
Risk: The persona is intentionally blunt and opinionated, which can make recommendations sound more certain than the evidence supports. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support, compare multiple options, and consult current professional or official counseling sources for high-stakes final choices. <br>
