## Use Case:

Social media operators use Postroom to inspect connected accounts, prepare and review post previews, schedule approved content, and check delivery status and available analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing actions can affect social accounts connected to the operator's workspace.

Mitigation: Use read-only access for inspection when possible, confirm destination accounts, and review the exact preview before approving a post.

Risk: Workspace access may expose account information and permissions to the connected agent session.

Mitigation: Use a private, operator-controlled session, grant only the scopes needed, and keep credentials in the browser-based OAuth flow.
