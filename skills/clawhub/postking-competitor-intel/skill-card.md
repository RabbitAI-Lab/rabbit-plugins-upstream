## Description:

Discover, register, and analyze a brand's competitors on PostKing by probing rival domains, classifying candidates, and pulling comparison intelligence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, operators, and agents use this skill to discover competitor domains, register tracked competitors for a PostKing brand, refresh competitor analysis, and review comparison or landscape intelligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide an agent through competitor deletion, bulk import, or refresh actions that may affect tracked competitor records.

Mitigation: Before deletion or bulk import/refresh, confirm the brand, target IDs or domains, and expected impact in plain language.
