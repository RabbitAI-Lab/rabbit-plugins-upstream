## Description: <br>
基于企业公开研究、合作风险、历史业务证据和我方产品、供应、交付与售后能力，判断潜在客户、供应商或合作伙伴的匹配程度，说明匹配原因、风险、信息缺口和下一步；不重新执行完整公开调查，也不以单一总分替代判断。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business development, procurement, and partner-management users apply this skill to compare existing company research, risk evidence, and internal product, supply, delivery, and after-sales capabilities against a potential customer, supplier, or partner. It returns fit level, supporting and contrary reasons, risks, information gaps, re-evaluation conditions, and next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may receive sensitive business research and commercial context while performing fit analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with information you are comfortable sharing with the agent and follow the platform's data-handling expectations for business research. <br>
Risk: Outputs can be mistaken for final qualification or prioritization decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review results as recommendations, then make final qualification, prioritization, and account decisions through the appropriate downstream process. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or conflicting evidence can produce low-confidence or information-insufficient conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Provide clear target identity, cooperation goals, company research, risk research, and internal supply, delivery, and after-sales capability evidence; treat marked gaps and conflicts as blockers until resolved. <br>
