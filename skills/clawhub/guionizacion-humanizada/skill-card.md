## Description:

Humanizes scripts and written content by building or using a voice guide so the rewritten text matches the user's or client's own communication style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and client-service writers use this skill to adapt scripts, captions, and other text to a specific user's or client's authentic voice. It can create or reuse a persistent voice guide based on provided writing samples, captions, transcripts, or permitted public content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill builds and persists a voice profile from user or client content.

Mitigation: Confirm consent for any client account, provide only the minimum writing samples needed, and review or delete cerebro/voice-guide.md when it is no longer needed.

Risk: The workflow frames some outputs as reducing signs of AI authorship.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill to hide required AI disclosure; review final text for policy, legal, and platform disclosure requirements before publishing.

Risk: Voice adaptation can misrepresent a person if the source examples are insufficient or from the wrong account.

Mitigation: Use consented examples from the intended speaker, confirm the Instagram handle or account owner before analysis, and keep explicit placeholders when business context is missing.
