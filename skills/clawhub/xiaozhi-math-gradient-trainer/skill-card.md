## Description:

为已掌握基础知识的初中生定位数学练习层级，并提供循序渐进的分层练习与成长反馈。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle-school students use this skill to assess their current level on a familiar math topic, practice progressively harder problems, and review learning progress. Educators may use its practice and progress summaries to support instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12); adapt crisis resources, curriculum, and minor-data consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records and progress may include minors' personal information and be shared between skills.

Mitigation: Require appropriate consent for records and cross-skill sharing; honor requests to view, correct, delete, export, or pause records.

Risk: Reminders could be unwanted or sent without student agreement.

Mitigation: Offer weekly reminders only with the student's consent, and allow reminders to be paused.

Risk: Mainland-China emergency contacts, curriculum, and consent defaults may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis resources, learning standards, and minor-data consent rules before deployment outside mainland China.
