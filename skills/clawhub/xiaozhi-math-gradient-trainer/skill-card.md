## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students through five levels of math practice, identifying their current level and building toward harder problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to assess a student's current level on a familiar math topic, practice progressively harder problems, and review learning progress. With consent and platform support, it can record training progress and arrange weekly check-in reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles of minors may be stored, shared, or used to schedule reminders.

Mitigation: Review profile-sharing and reminder settings before use; enable recording, sharing, and reminders only with the relevant consent.

Risk: Generated math questions or level assessments may be inaccurate or premature.

Mitigation: Check each question and its solution before presenting it, and assess a level from multiple attempts rather than one answer.
