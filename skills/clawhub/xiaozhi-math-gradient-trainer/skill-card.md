## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students assess their current math practice level and progress through five levels of increasingly challenging exercises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to locate their current level on a math topic, practice targeted harder problems, and review their progress through a growth diary and weekly checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language; localize consent requirements and emergency contacts outside Mainland China)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records and progress summaries concern minors and may be shared across skills or with parents.

Mitigation: Confirm applicable consent rules and sharing settings; honor requests to pause, correct, delete, or withhold records.

Risk: Weekly reminder requests could reach students without their agreement.

Mitigation: Queue reminders only after the student agrees and allow reminders to be paused.

Risk: Crisis contacts and consent assumptions may be unsuitable outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and localize emergency contacts and consent requirements before use elsewhere.
