## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language IT and AI transformation consulting workbench that helps agents produce structured consulting deliverables for IT strategy, enterprise architecture, cloud migration, data governance, security assessment, vendor selection, business cases, change management, and executive reporting. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Consultants, CIO advisors, enterprise architects, PMO leads, and technical due-diligence teams can use this skill to draft IT consulting analyses, templates, roadmaps, RFPs, business cases, risk registers, and executive-ready markdown deliverables across a project lifecycle. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workbench can guide broad IT consulting activities using client context that may include confidential, personal, or operationally sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm client authorization, minimize unnecessary personal or production data, and redact sensitive details before using the skill outputs. <br>
Risk: Generated consulting recommendations may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction, industry, or client control environment. <br>
Mitigation: Require qualified human review, validate key assumptions and source data, and adapt recommendations to the client jurisdiction and compliance obligations. <br>
Risk: Cloud cost optimization or resource cleanup guidance could affect live systems if converted into action without review. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit approval, impact analysis, backups, and rollback plans before applying shutdown, reclamation, or production-change recommendations. <br>
