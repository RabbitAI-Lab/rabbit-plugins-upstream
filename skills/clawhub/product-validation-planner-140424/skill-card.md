## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, workflows, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert product ideas into repeatable validation workflows, checklists, templates, prototypes, positioning, analyses, and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger language may invoke the skill for general product, startup, prototype, or validation requests outside the intended product-idea validation workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for product-idea validation and planning tasks; maintainers should narrow trigger wording if they need more precise routing. <br>
