## Description:

Plan template pages at scale (integrations, locations, use cases, data pages) only where real search demand meets unique data for every page, with quality gates, URL and hub structure, batch rollout and spam-policy checks. Use for "programmatic SEO", "generate pages from my database", "why are my template pages not indexed".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
