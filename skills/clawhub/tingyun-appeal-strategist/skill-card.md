## Description:

Helps e-commerce sellers assess appeal deadlines, organize supporting evidence, and draft factual appeals for Douyin Shop and WeChat Store.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shop owners and customer service teams use this skill to check appeal timing, match evidence to a case, and prepare a factual submission draft. It is a planning aid that requires human review and does not promise an appeal outcome.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Appeal materials can contain personal information such as order details and chat records.

Mitigation: Share redacted excerpts with the assistant, keep originals secure, and submit unredacted evidence only through the platform’s official appeal channel when required.

Risk: Platform deadlines and submission requirements may change, and an unchecked draft could include inaccurate details.

Mitigation: Verify current rules and deadlines in the platform’s official channel and review every draft before submission.
