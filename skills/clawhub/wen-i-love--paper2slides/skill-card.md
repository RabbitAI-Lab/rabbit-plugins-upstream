## Description: <br>
Transform any academic paper PDF into a polished presentation slide deck. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wen-i-love](https://clawhub.ai/user/wen-i-love) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, researchers, educators, and developers use this skill to convert user-provided academic paper PDFs or PDF URLs into editable presentation decks for paper reading, group meetings, and research sharing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reads user-provided papers and may process PDF URLs through network-enabled search or extraction workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use trusted paper sources, avoid sensitive PDFs unless the execution environment is approved for that data, and review network use before deployment. <br>
Risk: Generated slides may contain incorrect extracted numbers, claims, citations, or summaries from the source paper. <br>
Mitigation: Manually review generated slides against the original paper before presenting or sharing them. <br>
Risk: The skill writes generated slide files to user-specified locations. <br>
Mitigation: Run it in a workspace with appropriate file permissions and review output paths before accepting generated files. <br>
