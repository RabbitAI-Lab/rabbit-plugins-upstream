## Description:

Visual design guidance for bold, minimal presentations, including layout patterns, typography hierarchy, color specifications, and slide composition rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, designers, presenters, and developers use this skill to turn presentation content into clear visual structure with bold minimal layouts, typography hierarchy, and section-specific accent colors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated slide guidance may influence visual style choices in ways that are off-brand, hard to read, or mismatched to the audience.

Mitigation: Review slide outputs for readability, accessibility, brand fit, and audience needs before use.
