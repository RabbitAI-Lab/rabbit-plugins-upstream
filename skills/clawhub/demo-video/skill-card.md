## Use Case: <br>
Developers and product teams use this skill to plan, record, and encode browser-based product demos, walkthroughs, showcases, and interactive videos. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Browser recording can capture private dashboards, production accounts, notifications, or other sensitive visible content. <br>
Mitigation: Use a demo browser profile with sanitized data, confirm the target page before recording, and review generated frames or videos before sharing. <br>
Risk: Generated frame output may overwrite prior local demo frame folders. <br>
Mitigation: Keep output paths pointed at disposable demo folders and preserve any needed recordings before rerunning the capture script. <br>
