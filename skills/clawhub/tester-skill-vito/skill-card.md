## Description: <br>
Run outcome-focused Firecrawl workflows that produce deliverables such as research reports, SEO audits, QA reports, lead lists, knowledge bases, website design systems, and other structured web-data artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, product teams, and marketing teams use this skill to select and run Firecrawl-backed workflows that collect web evidence and turn it into finished reports, audits, briefs, lead lists, knowledge bases, design systems, or other structured deliverables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can use Firecrawl with an API key to collect web evidence, including for auth-gated content, lead lists, competitor monitoring, or large research jobs. <br>
Mitigation: Provide explicit targets, limits, and collection scope before use, and review the sources and rerun inputs included in generated deliverables. <br>
Risk: Generated reports, audits, recommendations, and extracted lists may contain incomplete or misleading claims if the collected evidence is narrow or stale. <br>
Mitigation: Review the evidence base and source citations before acting on the deliverable, especially for business, marketing, product, or research decisions. <br>
