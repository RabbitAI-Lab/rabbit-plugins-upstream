## Description:

Audit how a local business shows up in Google Maps and the map pack. Use for "audit my Google Business Profile", "why am I not in the map pack", "local SEO audit" or before opening, moving or merging locations. Covers profile fields, reviews, NAP citations, location pages, service areas and geo-grid rank checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
