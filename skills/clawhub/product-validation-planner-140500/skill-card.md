## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning through repeatable workflows and practical artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before heavy investment. It helps produce validation plans, prototype workflows, positioning notes, reusable checklists, lightweight analyses, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The broad product, SaaS, startup, and validation triggers may activate the skill for requests where product-planning guidance is not the user's main need. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome and success criteria, ask only for missing information that materially changes the result, and state assumptions before presenting planning artifacts. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs can be irrelevant or misleading if they rely on weak market assumptions or incomplete user inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Include verification notes, visible assumptions, remaining risks, and concrete follow-up checks before treating the plan as decision support. <br>
