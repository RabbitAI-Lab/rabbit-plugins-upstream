## Description:

Guides agents through HyperFrames Studio film planning, storyboard and build decisions, and editable timeline, caption, and safe-zone conventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan and build HyperFrames films in Studio while keeping scenes, captions, audio, and safe zones organized for editing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Building or revising a film may modify project plans, storyboards, and compositions.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested change or approved plan before editing, and honor requests to make no changes.

Risk: Build and revision workflows may run HyperFrames lint and require further project edits.

Mitigation: Use the skill in projects where lint and follow-up edits are expected; review resulting changes.
