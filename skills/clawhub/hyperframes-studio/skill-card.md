## Description:

Guides an agent through planning and reviewing HyperFrames Studio films, organizing editable timelines, and keeping important content within safe zones.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and their agents use this skill to plan product films, discuss edits before changing files, and build readable HyperFrames Studio timelines with captions and safe content placement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unwanted changes to HyperFrames project files during discussion or review.

Mitigation: Edit film files only for requested changes or approved plans; answer questions and honor hold requests without editing files.
