## Description: <br>
Audit an Amazon listing or draft for claim support, shopper clarity, keyword intent, visual proof, and missing evidence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[techterrydev](https://clawhub.ai/user/techterrydev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketplace operators, listing specialists, and ecommerce teams use this skill to audit Amazon listing drafts against supplied product evidence before rewriting titles, bullets, A+ content, or image briefs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the audit for legal, compliance, marketplace-policy, or product-safety approval. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as an evidence review aid only, and route compliance, policy, IP, safety, localization, and product-testing questions to qualified human reviewers. <br>
Risk: Unsupported product, certification, performance, environmental, comparative, or medical claims could be repeated as facts. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve unsupported wording as an audit target, mark missing facts as unknown, and require supplied evidence before drafting evidence-bounded alternatives. <br>
