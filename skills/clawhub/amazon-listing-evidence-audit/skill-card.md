## Description: <br>
Check an Amazon listing or draft for shopper clarity, supported claims, keyword intent, visual proof, and publish-blocking gaps before rewriting or publishing listing content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[techterrydev](https://clawhub.ai/user/techterrydev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Amazon operators, listing managers, and ecommerce teams use this skill to audit product listing drafts for missing facts, unsupported claims, keyword fit, visual proof needs, and publish-blocking review items before revision or publication. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the readiness check as legal, compliance, medical, safety, or certification approval. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a listing review checklist and route regulated, safety, certification, IP, localization, or policy questions to qualified human reviewers. <br>
Risk: Unsupported product facts or claims could lead to misleading listing changes. <br>
Mitigation: Require supplied evidence for material claims, mark missing facts as unknown, and avoid generating publish-ready claims until gaps are resolved. <br>
