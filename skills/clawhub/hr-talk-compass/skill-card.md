## Description: <br>
HR谈心罗盘 helps HR teams prepare employee one-on-ones, rehearse difficult conversations, equip managers, and turn post-conversation reviews into organizational development signals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
HR professionals and people managers use this skill to plan employee conversations, distinguish facts from assumptions, rehearse high-risk retention or development discussions, generate manager-friendly one-on-one guides, and summarize conversation records into OD insights. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process and archive sensitive employee conversation details. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with workplace authorization, redact names and unnecessary personal details, and store outputs only in restricted HR systems. <br>
Risk: Conversation outputs could be mistaken for legal, medical, or employment-action advice. <br>
Mitigation: Validate legal, health, psychological-crisis, and employment-action guidance through approved HR, legal, EAP, or clinical processes. <br>
Risk: Retaining detailed conversation records can create privacy and access-control exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Define deletion periods, limit access to authorized HR users, and avoid entering medical or highly sensitive personal information. <br>
