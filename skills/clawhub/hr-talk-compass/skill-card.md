## Description: <br>
HR谈心罗盘 helps HR users prepare employee one-on-ones, rehearse sensitive conversations, equip managers, and turn de-identified follow-up notes into OD signals using a design-thinking coaching framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
HR, HRBP, OD, and people managers use this skill to plan employee check-ins, retention or career conversations, post-conversation reviews, follow-up scripts, and aggregated organization-development insights. It is intended for de-identified HR coaching workflows and requires local HR, privacy, and legal review for sensitive cases. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with China-specific HR and labor-law references that should be reviewed for local deployment. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Employee privacy or sensitive HR information could be exposed if users paste real names, contact details, health records, compensation details, screenshots, or other personal data into an AI system. <br>
Mitigation: Use employee codes, provide only necessary de-identified context, avoid shared channels for real employee information, apply approved storage and access controls, and follow retention and deletion rules. <br>
Risk: Psychological crisis, harassment, discrimination, labor dispute, health, compensation, or other regulated signals may exceed what an AI-assisted HR prompt should handle. <br>
Mitigation: Stop AI analysis for those cases and route to EAP, legal, compliance, HR specialists, or other approved human processes according to local policy. <br>
Risk: Generated HR coaching guidance could be incomplete, misleading, or interpreted as a company commitment. <br>
Mitigation: Require human HR review, separate facts from inferences, avoid promises about promotion, compensation, transfer, or termination, and confirm any legal or policy-sensitive advice with authorized reviewers. <br>
Risk: Retention-oriented conversations could be used manipulatively or without adequate employee consent and context. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a coaching and reflection aid, keep the conversation employee-centered, avoid psychological manipulation, and treat respectful separation as an acceptable outcome when interests do not align. <br>
