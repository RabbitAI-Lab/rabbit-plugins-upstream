## Description: <br>
Generates a public-data regional investment-competition comparison report that evaluates multiple regions across industrial base, policy support, business environment, costs, resource availability, park carrying capacity, project landing efficiency, risks, and differentiated attraction strategies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External regional investment-promotion, project-siting, park-positioning, and policy teams use this skill to compare two or more target regions and produce a decision-oriented competition report. Optional inputs narrow the analysis by target industry, project type, local benchmark region, focus dimensions, and report scenario. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public regional investment data may be incomplete, stale, or unevenly disclosed, which can make rankings and scores misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Require generated reports to cite public sources, label support strength, and treat inferred rankings as decision support rather than absolute conclusions. <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on policy amounts, low costs, or promotional materials when comparing regions. <br>
Mitigation: Review conclusions against official statistics, policy fulfillment records, project landing evidence, and local constraints before using the report for investment decisions. <br>
