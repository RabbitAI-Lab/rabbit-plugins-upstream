## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users can send prompts, optionally with images, to dLazy's hosted Qwen 3.8 Max service for reasoning, coding help, and long-context analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected images are sent to dLazy, and requests may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Submit only content you intend to share with dLazy; review the pinned CLI before installation and protect or revoke any locally saved API key.
