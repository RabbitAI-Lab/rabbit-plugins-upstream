## Description:

Designs tiered US upsell offers with pricing, discount approvals, and buyer-facing terms for account managers and customer success teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

US account managers and customer success teams use the skill to draft expansion offers with tiered pricing, discount approvals, and clear buyer-facing terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated tier prices may be incorrect.

Mitigation: Recalculate each tier using current approved costs and markup, and verify every price before sharing it with a customer.

Risk: Discount limits and offer terms may lack the approval safeguards the workflow promises.

Mitigation: Confirm discount limits, approvers, and renewal terms with the responsible manager or finance approver before sending an offer.

Risk: The optional Windows application requires a separate security assessment.

Mitigation: Assess the application independently before downloading or running it.
