## Description:

Gorgias for Shopify via Agiexa (preview): the 44 tools your OpenClaw agent gets when you connect Gorgias to your Shopify store through Agiexa, launching November 2026. Explains use cases and joins the launch waitlist only with the user's permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External store owners and support teams use this preview to understand how Agiexa plans to connect OpenClaw with Gorgias and Shopify, explore support workflows, and optionally join the launch waitlist. The skill does not provide a working integration before the planned November 2026 launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the preview for a working Gorgias or Shopify connector.

Mitigation: State that the integration is not live and its listed tools cannot be called before the planned November 2026 launch.

Risk: Joining the waitlist sends personal contact details to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user reviews and confirms the details and explicitly agrees to the consent statement.
