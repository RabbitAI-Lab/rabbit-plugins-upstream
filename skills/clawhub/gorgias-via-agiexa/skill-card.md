This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants and their support teams use this skill to understand Agiexa's planned Gorgias integration, explore ticket support workflows, and optionally join its launch waitlist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Waitlist enrollment shares the user's confirmed email, role, and any optional details with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user explicitly agrees, confirm the details in the conversation, and omit optional fields they do not provide.

Risk: Future messaging, deletion, or automation capabilities may affect helpdesk data or customers.

Mitigation: Review permissions separately before enabling those capabilities and require approval for consequential actions.
