## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, URLs, text, audio, and other media are sent to Spraay and upstream AI providers. <br>
Mitigation: Use non-sensitive inputs first and avoid proprietary, customer, legal, or confidential material unless external processing is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Each request can spend USDC, and multi-step content workflows can chain several paid calls. <br>
Mitigation: Keep wallet balances limited, review endpoint costs before use, and estimate total workflow cost before running video or multi-step jobs. <br>
