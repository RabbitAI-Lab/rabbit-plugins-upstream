## Description: <br>
Activate different high-agency thinking modes to unlock better reasoning during brainstorming, reviews, decisions, and persona-triggered analysis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[artyomx33](https://clawhub.ai/user/artyomx33) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, reviewers, planners, and general agent users use this skill to shift reasoning style for brainstorming, plan review, decision analysis, risk assessment, and system-coherence checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Persona-based responses may become more contrarian, critical, or precedent-focused without clearly labeling the active mode. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the assistant to label the active persona or stay neutral for sensitive conversations. <br>
