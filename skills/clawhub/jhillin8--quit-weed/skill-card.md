## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to start and maintain a cannabis tolerance break or quitting goal, track progress, and receive craving support and milestone-based guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Streaks, goals, and craving notes can reveal sensitive personal information about cannabis use. <br>
Mitigation: Record only details you are comfortable keeping locally, and review the host environment's controls for viewing or deleting saved data. <br>
