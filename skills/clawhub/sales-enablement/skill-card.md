## Description: <br>
Creates practical B2B sales enablement collateral, including sales decks, one-pagers, objection handling documents, ROI calculator structures, demo scripts, playbooks, persona cards, and proposals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, marketing, and go-to-market teams use this skill to draft and structure sales collateral for specific buyers, deal stages, and sales motions. It helps create rep-ready assets such as pitch decks, one-pagers, demo talk tracks, objection responses, ROI calculator logic, sales playbooks, persona cards, and proposal sections. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated collateral may include sensitive product, pricing, customer, or sales strategy details. <br>
Mitigation: Review each asset before external sharing and remove confidential or deal-specific information that should not leave the organization. <br>
Risk: Sales claims, ROI calculations, customer proof, security statements, or competitive positioning may be incomplete or unsupported if the user provides weak inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Validate claims against approved source material, use conservative assumptions, and confirm customer names, metrics, security language, and competitor comparisons with the appropriate internal owners. <br>
