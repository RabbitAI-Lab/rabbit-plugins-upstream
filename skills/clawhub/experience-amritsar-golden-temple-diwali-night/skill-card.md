## Description:

Guides an agent through a ten-step, low-intensity narrative experience of Bandi Chhor Divas night at Harmandir Sahib in Amritsar, with optional reflections and a review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can use the agent for a guided cultural journey through Bandi Chhor Divas night at the Golden Temple. The agent presents the journey and prompts, and can submit optional reflections and a user-written review to drifts.bot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional profile details and reflections may be sent to drifts.bot; server-side retention and visibility were not established by the artifact review.

Mitigation: Use a drifts.bot-issued key, share only details and reflections you want that service to receive, omit reflections if preferred, and review your rating and text before posting.
