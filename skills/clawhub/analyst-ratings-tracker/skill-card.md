## Description:

Track Wall Street analyst ratings, price targets, rating changes, analyst histories, and post-earnings research activity alongside SentiSense sentiment for tracked stocks; this is a read-only informational tool with no trading or write operations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use the skill to review analyst coverage, rating actions, price targets, and call histories for supported US-listed stocks, and to compare analyst activity with current crowd sentiment. It provides informational context rather than personalized investment recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
