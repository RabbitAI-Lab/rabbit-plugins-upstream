# Recipe Hints by Category

When food is about to expire, the tracker suggests ways to use it. This document explains the logic.

## Urgency Levels

| Emoji | Days Left | Action |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 🔴 | Expired | Toss or compost — do NOT consume if spoiled |
| 🟠 | 0 days | Cook TODAY or freeze |
| 🟡 | 1-2 days | Plan a meal around it tomorrow |
| 🟢 | 3-5 days | No rush, but keep in mind |
| ⚪ | 6+ days | Plenty of time |

## Category → Recipe Suggestions

### Dairy (milk, yogurt, cheese)
- **Smoothie**: Blend milk/yogurt with frozen fruit
- **Creamy pasta**: Make a béchamel or carbonara
- **Baking**: Yogurt in muffins/cakes, cheese in casseroles
- **Freeze**: Cheese freezes well shredded, milk can be frozen (use for cooking)

### Meat (chicken, beef, fish)
- **Cook immediately**: This is the #1 item to not let expire
- **Freeze**: If you can't cook today, freeze it right now
- **Meal prep**: Cook a large batch, portion, refrigerate/freeze
- **Soup/stew**: Perfect for meat that's 1-2 days from expiry

### Produce-Leafy (spinach, lettuce, herbs)
- **Big salad**: Use all the greens in one meal
- **Stir-fry**: Wilted greens are perfect for stir-fry
- **Smoothie**: Spinach/kale blend invisibly into fruit smoothies
- **Pesto**: Basil, cilantro → blitz with oil, nuts, garlic
- **Freeze**: Wash, dry, freeze in bags → use directly in cooked dishes

### Produce-Fruit (berries, bananas)
- **Smoothie**: The universal solution for overripe fruit
- **Baking**: Bananas → banana bread, berries → muffins/crumble
- **Freeze**: All fruit freezes well — perfect for future smoothies
- **Jam**: Quick stovetop jam with berries

### Bakery (bread, pastries)
- **Croutons**: Cube, toss with oil, bake — lasts weeks
- **Bread pudding**: Use stale bread + eggs + milk
- **French toast**: Breakfast solution for old bread
- **Freeze**: Slice bread first, then freeze — toast from frozen

### Eggs
- **Hard-boil**: Extends life by 7 more days
- **Frittata**: Use up eggs + any expiring veg/meat/cheese
- **Baking**: Cakes, muffins, quiches
- **Freeze**: Crack, whisk, freeze in ice cube trays (1 cube ≈ 1 egg)

### Tofu
- **Stir-fry**: Quick and forgiving
- **Scramble**: Tofu scramble as egg substitute
- **Freeze**: Freezing changes texture (more chewy) — great for stir-fry

## Waste Reduction Strategy

1. **Shop with a list** — don't overbuy
2. **First In, First Out (FIFO)** — rotate fridge so oldest is in front
3. **Weekly "clean-out" meal** — make soup/stir-fry with everything expiring
4. **Track your waste patterns** — if you always waste spinach, buy less or buy frozen
5. **Compost** what truly can't be saved
