## Description:

文献速读 helps agents turn Chinese or English academic papers from PDF, text, or screenshots into structured Chinese reading notes covering summaries, research questions, methods, conclusions, innovations, and topic-specific reference value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cryptocxf](https://clawhub.ai/user/cryptocxf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to convert user-provided papers into concise or close-reading notes for literature review, topic planning, and research reference. It is especially suited to requests for paper speed-reading, method and conclusion extraction, innovation analysis, and topic-specific relevance assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paper details may be misattributed or over-inferred when source text, pages, or screenshots are incomplete.

Mitigation: Keep claims close to the provided source, cite pages or sections when available, and mark unclear screenshot text or inferred conclusions explicitly.

Risk: The skill is designed for academic papers and may be misapplied to general document summarization.

Mitigation: Use it for papers or scholarly articles, and choose a general summarization workflow for non-academic documents.
