## Description:

Tool Selector turns a DIY project description into a tool list, materials list, cost estimate, and step-by-step plan filtered by available tools, with substitutions and missing-tool flags.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, including homeowners and first-time DIYers, use this skill to plan ordinary DIY, home repair, and maker projects by identifying tools, materials, costs, steps, and practical substitutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat DIY planning output as professional construction, electrical, plumbing, structural, or code-compliance advice.

Mitigation: Present results as planning guidance and consult a licensed professional for electrical, gas, structural, code-sensitive, or uncertain plumbing work.

Risk: DIY steps may involve power tools, drilling, mounting, electricity, or water systems where incorrect execution can cause injury or property damage.

Mitigation: Use appropriate PPE, follow manufacturer instructions, and verify power or water is off where relevant before starting work.

Risk: Mounting or fastening guidance can be unsafe if wall contents, anchors, studs, or load ratings are not independently checked.

Mitigation: Check wall contents and load ratings before drilling or mounting, and verify fasteners and anchors are suitable for the material and load.
