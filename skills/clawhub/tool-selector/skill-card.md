## Description:

Describe a DIY project, get an exact tool list, materials needed, cost estimate, and step-by-step guide filtered by your available tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users, homeowners, first-time DIYers, apartment dwellers, makers, and budget-conscious project planners use this skill to turn a DIY project idea into a tool list, materials list, cost estimate, substitutions, and step-by-step plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: DIY project plans may be unsafe or insufficient for electrical, gas, structural, roof, major plumbing, load-bearing, or code-regulated work.

Mitigation: Treat output for those projects as preliminary planning only; consult manufacturer instructions, local code, permits, and a licensed professional.

Risk: Tool substitutions, cost estimates, and material quantities may be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's exact site conditions.

Mitigation: Verify tool suitability, material quantities, fastener specifications, and safety gear before purchasing materials or starting work.
