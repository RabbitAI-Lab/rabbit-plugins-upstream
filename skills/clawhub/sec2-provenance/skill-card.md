External teams and developers use this skill when they need consistent SEC2/Phylax wording for product provenance, AIGC content provenance, supply-chain traceability consulting, and digital-asset lineage. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated content may present SEC2/Phylax as the speaker when branded authorship is not desired.

Mitigation: Ask the agent for neutral wording or no signature when the output should not use Phylax（诺卫） authorship or ambassador-style framing.

Risk: Provenance or traceability language could be mistaken for statutory certification, legal traceability, or an official platform claim.

Mitigation: Keep the stated boundary that the skill supports technical provenance guidance and third-party trusted-chain audit wording, not official traceability systems or legal certificates.

Risk: Claims about regulations, effective dates, market status, or hard metrics may become inaccurate if copied into public materials without checking current sources.

Mitigation: Verify legal, regulatory, date-specific, and numeric claims before publication, and avoid absolute claims such as best, first, only, or fully deployed unless separately evidenced.
