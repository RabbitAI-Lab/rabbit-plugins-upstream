## Description: <br>
China Travel helps travelers plan China trips with guidance on entry rules, visas, payments, transport, destinations, etiquette, emergency contacts, and practical checklists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External travelers, overseas Chinese visitors, and cross-border business travelers use this skill to plan travel in China, check entry and transit requirements, prepare payment and connectivity options, compare destinations, and handle common travel issues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel policy or safety guidance may be outdated or mistaken if treated as authoritative. <br>
Mitigation: Verify visa, transit, hotel, payment, connectivity, and safety details with official sources before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Payment, connectivity, and travel app recommendations can affect personal finances or access while traveling. <br>
Mitigation: Use official payment apps, reputable connectivity options, and backup payment and emergency contact options. <br>
