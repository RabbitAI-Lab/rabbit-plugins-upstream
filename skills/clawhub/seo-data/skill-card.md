### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Queries, URLs, and prompts are sent to superagnt, and requests incur per-call charges.

Mitigation: Submit only information you are comfortable sharing and review usage costs.

Risk: The API key may provide access to other superagnt services as well as SEO Data.

Mitigation: Store the key securely and treat it as a credential with broader service access.

Risk: Some documented shell examples are malformed and may not expand the API key correctly.

Mitigation: Correct the shell quoting and verify key-variable expansion before running those examples.
