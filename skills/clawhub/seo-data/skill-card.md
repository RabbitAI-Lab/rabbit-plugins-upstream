## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, SEO analysts, and developers use this skill to research search results and keywords, compare competitors and backlinks, audit pages, and track brand visibility in AI answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SEO queries, domains, URLs, audit targets, and AI prompts are sent to Superagnt.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive internal-only URLs or regulated data unless organizational policy permits sharing them.

Risk: The skill uses a Superagnt API key to access the service.

Mitigation: Keep the key in an environment variable and do not paste high-privilege keys into chat.
