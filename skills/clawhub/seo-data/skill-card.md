## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends queries and target URLs or domains to an external service; calls may incur per-call charges, and the security review was partial.

Mitigation: Review the documented requests and expected costs, and use the service only if you trust its handling of your data. Keep SUPERAGNT_API_KEY private.
