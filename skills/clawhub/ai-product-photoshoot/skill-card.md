## Description:

Turn one product photo into a full ecommerce image set with the PopNow API: clean white-background main image, lifestyle scenes, angle and detail shots, infographic slots and marketplace sizes for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy and social. Quotes every image in USD first. Use when the user needs product photos, listing images or a catalog shoot without a studio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and catalog teams use this skill to turn real product photos into listing image sets sized for marketplaces and social channels. It guides image planning, paid generation, review against the source product, and delivery with alt text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid image generation may exceed the user's intended spend if the complete quote is not reviewed before generation.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key with model and spending limits, review the complete quoted budget, and proceed only after explicit human approval.

Risk: Product photos, prompts, and reference media are sent to PopNow and the generation provider needed for the request.

Mitigation: Share only material suitable for those services that the user has rights to use, and avoid sensitive personal data.
