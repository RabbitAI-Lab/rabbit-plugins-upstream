## Description:

Part of the Overpowered skill suite, this skill requires evidence proportional to material claims and clearly separates evidence, inference, and uncertainty for research, analysis, recommendations, reports, audits, and decisions where unsupported confidence would be harmful.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers use this skill to make agents identify material claims, expose provenance, distinguish direct evidence from inference or assumptions, and state uncertainty when evidence is insufficient.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make agent outputs more structured and may add evidence and uncertainty sections to analytical work.

Mitigation: Apply it to research, analysis, recommendations, reports, audits, and decisions where claim support matters; avoid applying it to purely stylistic tasks unless evidence handling is requested.

Risk: Users could treat one precedent or weak source as enough support for a current policy or standard.

Mitigation: Require authoritative evidence for normative claims and label unsupported conclusions as assumptions or unknowns.
