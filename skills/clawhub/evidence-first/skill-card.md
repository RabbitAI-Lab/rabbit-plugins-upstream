## Description:

Require evidence proportional to material claims and clearly separate evidence, inference, and uncertainty.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, reviewers, and other external users use this skill to make agents support material claims with evidence, distinguish inference and assumptions, and expose uncertainty in research, analysis, recommendations, reports, audits, and decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Material conclusions can still be misleading if the available evidence is weak, outdated, or not authoritative.

Mitigation: Prefer authoritative primary sources, preserve provenance, and mark missing or insufficient support as unknown.

Risk: Purely creative or stylistic tasks can become unnecessarily structured if the skill is applied too broadly.

Mitigation: Use the skill for research, analysis, recommendations, reports, audits, and decisions; avoid evidence scaffolding for purely stylistic rewrites.
