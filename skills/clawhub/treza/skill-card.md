## Description:

Creates videos, images, voiceovers, music, narrated shorts, and captioned clips through Treza's paid media services.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and media makers use the skill to generate original video, image, voice, and music assets, or turn long-form video into short captioned clips.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, subject to Treza's stated sanctioned and unsupported region restrictions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, text, topics, or source links submitted for media generation are sent to Treza and the rendering provider; wallet sign-in sends an email and one-time code to Coinbase.

Mitigation: Before sending a request, confirm its contents, the destination and provider, the quoted price, and the applicable terms; send only the user-approved request body.

Risk: Security review confidence is medium and the scan was partial, so the evidence does not establish what data external URL requests send.

Mitigation: Review the external requests and intended data use before installing or using the skill.
