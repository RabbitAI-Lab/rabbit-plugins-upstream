## Use Case: <br>
Entrepreneurs, product managers, and technical leads use this skill to turn unclear business requirements into structured system architecture plans, including requirements analysis, platform design, risk assessment, and cost estimation. It supports planning discussions and produces proposal, outsourcing, reverse-engineering, acceptance, and collaboration deliverables for human review. <br>
