## Description: <br>
Find free and cheap things to do in Charlotte, NC, including daily event listings with times, prices, venues, searchable deals, festivals, kids activities, and local guides from Charlotte On The Cheap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to find Charlotte-area free or low-cost events, deals, festivals, family activities, and local guides, then summarize relevant options with times, prices, and venues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Event times, prices, venues, and availability can change after lookup. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important event details with Charlotte On The Cheap or the underlying venue before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Month overviews are previews and may not include every event for a day. <br>
Mitigation: Use the reported total for volume and fetch the specific date before presenting a complete daily schedule. <br>
Risk: Expired deals may appear only when explicitly requested for historical research. <br>
Mitigation: Label expired-deal results clearly and avoid presenting them as currently available offers. <br>
