## Description:

Generate e-commerce fashion photos with Fashio (fashiolabs.com) through its hosted MCP server — put a garment on an AI model, virtual try-on, full catalog photoshoot packs, jewelry on model, flat lays, background removal, upscaling, reframing and short product videos. Use when someone wants on-model product photos from a clothing or jewelry photo, a consistent catalog look across products, or to batch-produce listing images for a store. Requires a Fashio API key; generations spend the account's credits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: When no public image URL is available, product images may be sent to Fashio for processing; the security review flags a privacy notice gap.

Mitigation: Before uploading an image, confirm the user is comfortable sending it to Fashio; prefer a public image URL supplied by the user when available.
