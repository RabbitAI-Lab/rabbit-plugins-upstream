## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The calendar integration is planned for a future launch; its tools and triggers are not currently available.

Mitigation: Present capabilities as upcoming and direct users to the integration page for current details.

Risk: Joining the waitlist sends a confirmed email, role, and any optional link or message to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Collect and submit these details only after the user explicitly consents and confirms the information.

Risk: The publisher’s security claims could not be independently verified during review.

Mitigation: Identify security assurances as publisher claims and consult current Agiexa information before connecting accounts.
