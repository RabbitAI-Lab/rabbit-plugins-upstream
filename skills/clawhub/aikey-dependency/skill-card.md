## Description:

Helps owner-operators identify business dependencies that can unilaterally change terms or cut off service, then assess switching costs and time for high-risk dependencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners and operators use this skill to map dependencies across platforms, premises and licences, key people, suppliers, and payment channels. It produces a structured checkup of exposure, switching cost and time, alternatives, and practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checkup may involve sensitive business details that the skill could save for later reuse, while storage access and retention controls are unclear in the supplied files.

Mitigation: Before sharing confidential details, inspect the missing configuration and interaction rules. Establish who can access saved memory, how long it is retained, and how to review or delete it; require approval before saving or use a no-memory workflow.
