## Description: <br>
Navigate problems along lines of flight by composing operations from arbitrary domains into structurally coherent assemblages. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for exploratory brainstorming and problem reframing when conventional domain-bound approaches feel exhausted. It helps compose operations from unrelated domains into concrete problem-solving paths, but its output should not replace qualified expertise for consequential decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Creative cross-domain output may be mistaken for authoritative guidance in consequential situations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as exploratory brainstorming and rely on proven domain methods or qualified experts for medical, legal, financial, safety, security, or other high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: Composed operations from unrelated domains may produce plausible but untested action plans. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed operations, validate assumptions, and test low-stakes steps before acting on the guidance. <br>
