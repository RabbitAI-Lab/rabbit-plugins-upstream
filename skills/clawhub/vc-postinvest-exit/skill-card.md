## Use Case: <br>
VC 投资人、投后管理负责人和基金运营团队用于整理被投公司的经营指标、里程碑、风险预警和退出路径测算，支持组合管理与 LP 沟通。 <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Post-investment briefs and exit plans may contain sensitive portfolio company operating metrics, financing details, risk flags, and return estimates. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as sensitive business information, redact or classify data before sharing, and require explicit confirmation before any optional publication. <br>
Risk: Exit strategy recommendations and return calculations may be misleading if based on incomplete or unverified company data. <br>
Mitigation: Label unverifiable values as estimates or assumptions, include source and date for operating data, and review calculations before using them in investment or LP communications. <br>
