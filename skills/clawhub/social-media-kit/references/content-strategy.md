# Content Strategy: The 70-20-10 Rule & Content Pillars

## The 70-20-10 Rule

The 70-20-10 rule is a proven social media content framework that balances value
delivery with promotion and engagement. It ensures your audience gets consistent
value without feeling sold to.

### Breakdown

| Content Type | Percentage | Purpose | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Educational** | 70% | Teach, inform, add value, build authority | "5 ways to..." tips, how-tos, industry insights |
| **Promotional** | 20% | Drive action, conversions, sales | Product launches, offers, demos, case studies |
| **Engaging** | 10% | Spark conversation, build community | Questions, polls, hot takes, discussions |

### Why It Works

- **Avoids ad fatigue**: Your audience doesn't feel constantly sold to
- **Builds trust first**: Educational content positions you as an authority
- **Keeps feed fresh**: Variety prevents content from feeling monotonous
- **Drives organic reach**: Educational posts get saved and shared more
- **Balances conversion**: Promo content still gets its spotlight without dominating

### Applying 70-20-10 to a Weekly Schedule (21 Posts)

```
Educational : ~5 posts per platform (15 total)
Promotional : ~1-2 posts per platform (4 total)
Engaging    : ~1 post per platform (2 total)
```

## Content Pillars

Content pillars are 3-5 core themes that define what you post about. Every post
should map to at least one pillar. This creates consistency and makes ideation
faster.

### How to Define Your Pillars

1. **Identify your niche**: What's your expertise?
2. **Know your audience**: What problems do they have?
3. **List 3-5 themes**: These are your pillars
4. **Rotate through them**: Don't post about the same pillar twice in a row

### Example Pillars

#### For a Sustainable Fashion Brand
| Pillar | Topics |
|--------|--------|
| Sustainability Education | Materials, supply chain, environmental impact |
| Style & Trends | Seasonal looks, styling tips, capsule wardrobes |
| Brand Story | Founder journey, manufacturing process, values |
| Community | Customer spotlights, UGC, partnerships |
| Product Showcase | New arrivals, bestsellers, behind-the-design |

#### For an AI Productivity App
| Pillar | Topics |
|--------|--------|
| AI & Productivity | Tips, research, workflow optimization |
| App Features | Tutorials, use cases, updates |
| Industry Insights | Future of work, automation trends |
| User Success | Testimonials, case studies, metrics |
| Thought Leadership | Opinions, predictions, hot takes |

## Content Types (Extended)

Beyond the 70-20-10 rule, these content types add variety:

| Type | Description | When to Use |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Educational** | Tips, tutorials, how-tos, facts | Daily — your content engine |
| **Promotional** | Product, offer, demo, CTA | 1-2x per week per platform |
| **Engaging** | Questions, polls, hot takes | 1x per day to drive comments |
| **Behind-the-Scenes** | Process, team, workspace | 1-2x per week for authenticity |
| **User-Generated** | Customer content, testimonials | When available — highest trust |
| **Inspirational** | Quotes, success stories | Fill content gaps, boost shares |
| **Trending** | News, current events | When relevant — ride the wave |

## Weekly Storyline Framework

A good content calendar tells a story across the week, not just isolated posts.

### Story Arc Pattern

| Day | Theme | Purpose |
|-----|-------|---------|
| Monday | **Awareness** | Introduce the week's theme, educate |
| Tuesday | **Deep Dive** | Educational content, establish authority |
| Wednesday | **Community** | Engaging content, user-generated, social proof |
| Thursday | **Value Bomb** | High-impact educational content, tips |
| Friday | **Promotion** | Soft pitch, product showcase, offer |
| Saturday | **Behind-the-Scenes** | Authenticity, personality, culture |
| Sunday | **Reflection** | Recap, inspiration, set up next week |

## Engagement Hooks & CTAs

### Hook Patterns (First Line)

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Question | "What if everything you knew about X was wrong?" |
| Bold statement | "90% of people get this wrong." |
| Number | "5 tools that will save you 10 hours a week:" |
| Contrarian | "Stop doing X. Here's why:" |
| Story | "Last year, I almost gave up on X." |
| Data | "New study: X increases Y by 300%" |

### CTA Patterns (Last Line)

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Comment | "What's your take? Drop a comment 👇" |
| Share | "Know someone who needs this? Share it." |
| Save | "Save this for later 📌" |
| Follow | "Follow for daily tips on X" |
| Link | "Full guide in bio →" |
| Vote | "Agree or disagree? Let's debate." |
