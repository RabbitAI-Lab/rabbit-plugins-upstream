# Platform Guide: Limits, Hashtags & Best Practices

## Platform Comparison

| Feature | Twitter/X | Instagram | LinkedIn |
|---------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Character Limit** | 280 | 2,200 | 3,000 |
| **Optimal Length** | 71-100 chars | 138-150 chars | 1,200-1,900 chars |
| **Hashtags** | 1-2 | 10-15 | 3-5 |
| **Best Times** | 9AM, 12PM, 5PM | 11AM-1PM, 7-9PM | 8-10AM, 12PM |
| **Best Days** | Tue-Thu | Wed, Fri | Tue-Thu |
| **Media** | Images, video, GIFs | Photos, Reels, Stories | Documents, images, video |
| **Tone** | Punchy, trending | Visual, emotional | Professional, insightful |

## Twitter / X

### Best Practices
- **Keep it short**: 71-100 characters gets the most engagement
- **Use 1-2 hashtags**: More reduces engagement
- **Thread strategically**: Break long content into numbered threads
- **Engage fast**: Reply within the first hour for maximum reach
- **Use media**: Tweets with images get 150% more retweets
- **Front-load value**: Put the key message in the first line

### Hashtag Strategy
- Use **trending hashtags** when relevant
- **1-2 hashtags max** — more decreases engagement
- Create a **branded hashtag** for campaigns
- Research with Twitter's search before posting

### Character Budget (280 chars)
```
Hook:      40-60 chars
Body:     120-160 chars
CTA:       20-30 chars
Hashtags:  30-40 chars
Total:    ~280 chars
```

### Example Post
```
🧵 5 productivity tips that actually work:

1. Time-block your calendar
2. Batch similar tasks
3. Take real breaks
4. Single-task
5. Review your week

What's your #1 productivity hack? 👇

#productivity #tips
```

## Instagram

### Best Practices
- **Caption length**: 138-150 characters for feed posts; longer for Stories
- **10-15 hashtags**: Use a mix of broad and niche
- **First 2 lines matter**: They're visible before "more"
- **Use emoji**: Increases engagement, breaks up text
- **Carousel posts**: Get 3x more engagement than single images
- **Reels**: Prioritize for reach — algorithm favors video
- **Stories**: Post daily for top-of-mind awareness

### Hashtag Strategy
- **10-15 per post** (max 30, but 10-15 is optimal)
- Mix sizes:
  - 3-5 large (1M+ posts)
  - 3-5 medium (100K-1M posts)
  - 3-5 small/niche (<100K posts)
- Put hashtags at the **end** or in the **first comment**
- Create **branded hashtags** for UGC campaigns

### Image Suggestions
Every Instagram post should include image direction:
- **Type**: Photo, graphic, carousel, reel
- **Composition**: What's in the frame
- **Mood**: Color palette, vibe, tone
- **Text overlay**: Any text on the image

### Example Post
```
Swipe to learn the 5 principles of sustainable fashion 👉

Fashion doesn't have to cost the earth. Here's how to build a wardrobe that's good for you AND the planet:

1. Choose natural, organic materials
2. Support ethical brands
3. Buy less, choose well
4. Care for what you own
5. Thrift and resell

Which principle are you practicing? Share below 👇

Save this for your next shopping trip 📌

#sustainablefashion #ecofriendly #slowfashion #sustainability #ethicalfashion #consciousfashion #ecofashion #greenliving #sustainablestyle #thrifting #capsulewardrobe #sustainableliving
```

## LinkedIn

### Best Practices
- **1,200-1,900 characters**: The sweet spot for engagement
- **Professional tone**: Insightful, value-driven, career-relevant
- **3-5 hashtags**: Professional and industry-focused
- **Formatting matters**: Use line breaks, bullet points, bold headers
- **No external links in post**: LinkedIn penalizes external links — put in comments
- **Post weekdays**: Tuesday-Thursday 8-10 AM is peak
- **Documents**: PDF carousels get high engagement
- **Personal stories**: Authentic professional narratives perform best

### Hashtag Strategy
- **3-5 hashtags** optimal
- Use **industry-specific** tags
- Follow hashtags relevant to your niche
- Create **company-branded** hashtags

### Structure Template
```
[Hook — bold opening line]

[Context — why this matters]

[Body — 3-5 key points with line breaks]

[Takeaway — actionable insight]

[CTA — question or invitation to discuss]

[Hashtags]

#industry #topic #professional
```

### Example Post
```
I spent 3 months analyzing how AI is transforming fitness. Here's what I found:

The fitness industry is undergoing its biggest shift since the introduction of personal training. AI-powered apps are changing how we:

1. TRAIN — Personalized workout plans that adapt to your progress, not a one-size-fits-all template.

2. TRACK — Real-time form correction using computer vision. Your phone is now your coach.

3. RECOVER — Predictive recovery recommendations based on your sleep, heart rate, and training load.

The result? Users of AI-powered fitness apps report 40% higher consistency and 2x faster progress compared to traditional methods.

But here's the real insight: AI doesn't replace human coaches. It augments them. The best results come from AI-powered insights combined with human accountability.

What's your experience with AI in fitness? Are you using any AI-powered tools?

#AI #FitnessTech #HealthTech #FutureOfFitness #DigitalHealth
```

## Cross-Platform Content Adaptation

### Same Topic, Different Platforms

| Element | Twitter | Instagram | LinkedIn |
|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Hook** | Punchy one-liner | Visual + emoji hook | Bold professional statement |
| **Body** | Thread or list | Carousel or caption story | Long-form analysis |
| **CTA** | Reply / RT | Comment / Save | Comment / Share |
| **Hashtags** | 1-2 broad | 10-15 mixed | 3-5 industry |
| **Media** | Image or GIF | Carousel or Reel | PDF or infographic |
| **Tone** | Casual, witty | Visual, emotional | Professional, data-driven |

### Repurposing Flow
```
Long-form blog post
  → LinkedIn article (adapt)
  → Twitter thread (break down)
  → Instagram carousel (visualize)
  → Instagram Reel (demonstrate)
  → Twitter tips (extract)
```
