## Description: <br>
QMS Objective Decomposition helps quality-management teams decompose company-level quality objectives into department, process, and time-based matrices with SMART checks, and audit existing objective documents against ISO 9001:2015 Clause 6.2. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality managers, QMS owners, and internal auditors use this skill to turn company-level quality objectives into traceable department, process, and milestone targets, then review existing objective documents for ISO 9001:2015 Clause 6.2 alignment. The skill supports draft matrices, SMART validation reports, and severity-based audit advice for user review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process business quality objectives, organizational structures, and internal QMS documents. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with materials appropriate for the active workspace and review generated content under the organization's confidentiality and document-control practices. <br>
Risk: Generated target values, decomposition coefficients, and audit suggestions may be mistaken for final management decisions or certification audit findings. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated values and findings as drafts; have responsible quality leaders confirm baselines, coefficients, targets, and required corrective actions before use. <br>
Risk: The inspected artifact references helper scripts and templates that were not present in the artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the required helper files are available in the operating environment or have the agent produce text and Markdown outputs directly from the confirmed workflow. <br>
