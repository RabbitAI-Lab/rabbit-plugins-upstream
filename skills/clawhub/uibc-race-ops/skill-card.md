## Description:

A six-role AI team helps contest organizers plan seasons, design problems, govern judging, support contestants, draft communications, and coordinate partnerships.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Contest organizers and their teams use this skill to draft season plans, scoring rubrics, judging procedures, contestant support, announcements, and sponsorship proposals for UIBC-style competitions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contestant information or confidential judging material could be disclosed.

Mitigation: Apply organizer-approved privacy controls and review disclosures before publication.

Risk: Leaderboards, prize logistics, or sponsorship proposals could be mistaken for approved commitments.

Mitigation: Require organizer approval and human review before publishing results, distributing prizes, or making commercial commitments.
