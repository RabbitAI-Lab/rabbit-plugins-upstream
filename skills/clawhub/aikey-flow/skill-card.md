## Description:

Reviews talking-head scripts beat by beat to find where viewers may lose the thread and offers optional marked-up edits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use this skill to review a spoken video script for missing transitions, repeated points, and hard-to-follow lines. It returns a listener-focused report and can prepare clearly marked edits after the creator agrees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback, performance figures, and writing samples may be retained without a clear permission step or deletion process.

Mitigation: Agree on what may be saved, where it is stored, and how to delete it; otherwise keep the review session-only.

Risk: The workflow depends on family guidance documents that may be missing or unread.

Mitigation: Review the required family documents before enabling the workflow, and stop if they are unavailable.
