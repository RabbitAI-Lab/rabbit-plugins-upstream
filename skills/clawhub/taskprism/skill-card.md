## Description: <br>
Task Prism helps agents turn ambiguous business or project requests into structured project plans, work breakdowns, skill profiles, delivery flows, budgets, risk registers, change controls, and Go/No-Go recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and project teams use this Chinese-language planning skill to clarify vague requirements and generate actionable project-management outputs across complete, quick, agile, and cross-cultural planning modes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can activate for broad planning requests and may produce more project-management structure than a lightweight task needs. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the desired mode with the user and use quick or single-dimension modes when a full twelve-dimension plan is unnecessary. <br>
Risk: The templates are Chinese-language oriented and may not match users who expect another language or locale. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for the preferred language and adapt terminology, examples, and regional assumptions before treating the output as final. <br>
Risk: Plans, estimates, legal assumptions, and risk scores can be wrong when user-provided constraints or jurisdiction details are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Validate assumptions, dependencies, budgets, timelines, and regulatory context with project owners before execution. <br>
