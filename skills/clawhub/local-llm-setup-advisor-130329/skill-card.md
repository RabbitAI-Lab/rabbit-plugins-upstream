## Description: <br>
Helps users plan and validate local AI and LLM workflows on consumer CPU or family GPU hardware without depending on cloud-only systems. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, privacy-conscious users, hobbyists, and small teams use this skill to get practical plans, checklists, commands, configuration guidance, and verification notes for running LLM workflows on ordinary local hardware. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad local AI, privacy, or software workflow requests where its local LLM setup guidance is not the best fit. <br>
Mitigation: Review the user's goal and constraints before applying the workflow, and redirect when the request is unrelated to local LLM setup. <br>
Risk: Generated setup plans, commands, or configuration suggestions may not match the user's exact hardware, operating system, or model constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for missing hardware or environment details when they materially affect the recommendation, and include verification commands or checks before treating the setup as complete. <br>
