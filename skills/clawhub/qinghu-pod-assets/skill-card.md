## Description:

面向 POD（Print on Demand）卖家生成设计素材：印花提取、印花贴合（mockup 效果图）、印花裂变、商品效果图，覆盖服饰、家居、饰品等 POD 品类的设计与上架。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

POD sellers use this skill to extract print designs from reference images, create design variations, and preview designs on products such as apparel, home goods, and accessories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference images are uploaded to Qinghu AI, and the service's retention practices were not verified.

Mitigation: Upload only images you intend to share with Qinghu AI.

Risk: Setup can install or upgrade software globally and may change the shell PATH.

Mitigation: Review setup changes in your environment and use a dedicated service key.

Risk: Generated redraws may change print details, and using another creator's design commercially may raise copyright concerns.

Mitigation: Check key design elements in generated images and confirm you have rights to designs intended for commercial use.
