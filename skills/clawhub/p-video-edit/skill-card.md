## Description:

Guides an agent in editing an existing video with text instructions, including changes to objects, appearance, environment, lighting, or on-screen text, with optional reference images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to guide an agent through instruction-based edits to an existing short video while preserving unrequested content and motion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Upload only media you are authorized to share and review Pruna's data-handling terms before use.

Risk: Installing related skills from untrusted or changing sources may introduce supply-chain risk.

Mitigation: Prefer pinned or trusted versions of the related skills and review them before installation.
