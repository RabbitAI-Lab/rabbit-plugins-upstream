## Description: <br>
Helps IT and quality information leaders perform structured QMS software selection by clarifying requirements, defining weighted scoring dimensions, and producing an evaluation matrix with MD and HTML recommendation reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
IT and quality information leaders use this skill to compare candidate QMS platforms with transparent weighted scoring, traceable assumptions, and recommendation reporting. It supports demand clarification, scoring model setup, candidate evaluation, and management-facing MD and HTML reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Selection recommendations may rely on incomplete vendor claims, costs, certifications, or scoring inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Verify vendor claims, costs, certifications, and scoring assumptions with the organization and vendors before procurement decisions. <br>
Risk: The referenced report-generation script was not included in the inspected artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Review the report-generation behavior before relying on generated MD or HTML deliverables in a production workflow. <br>
