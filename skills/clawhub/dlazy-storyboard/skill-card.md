## Description:

Helps creators develop consistent-character, multi-shot animated shorts, from storyboard and character prompts through generated media and final video assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and production teams use this skill to plan and produce multi-shot animated videos with consistent characters, voice, music, effects, and subtitles through dLazy's storyboard service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files can be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Review prompts and attachments for sensitive content before sending them.

Risk: The CLI may store a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and rotate or revoke keys if exposed.

Risk: Generation may consume account credits.

Mitigation: Check available credits and expected usage before starting generation.
