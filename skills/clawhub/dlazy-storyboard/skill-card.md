## Description:

Helps create multi-shot animated shorts with consistent characters, from storyboard and shot prompts through voice, music, subtitles, and video assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to develop a multi-shot animated short with consistent characters and coordinate its script, visual assets, audio, and final video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to the external dLazy service.

Mitigation: Only submit prompts, files, and projects approved for sharing with dLazy.

Risk: The dLazy API key may be saved in local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key in the dLazy dashboard if needed.
