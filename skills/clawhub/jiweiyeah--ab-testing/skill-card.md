## Description:

Helps agents plan, design, implement, and analyze A/B tests, multivariate experiments, and growth experimentation programs with statistical rigor.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiweiyeah](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiweiyeah)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, growth teams, and marketers use this skill to turn proposed product or marketing changes into experiment plans. It helps define hypotheses, metrics, sample-size needs, traffic allocation, monitoring practices, and result interpretation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use local product-marketing context files when present.

Mitigation: Review shared context for sensitivity and provide only appropriately collected product and analytics information.

Risk: Experiment planning guidance may be mistaken for legal or privacy compliance advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as statistical planning guidance and involve legal or privacy reviewers for data collection, consent, and segmentation decisions.
