Mitigation: Treat the content as speculative planning guidance, independently review any assumptions, and do not present it as financial advice.

Risk: An agent could exceed the intended local-reference posture by placing trades or handling exchange credentials.

Mitigation: Keep the skill limited to local reference and analysis unless the user gives separate, explicit approval for any exchange access or trading automation.

Risk: Crypto scalping decisions can cause financial loss even when risk rules are followed.

Mitigation: Require human review before acting on the guidance and preserve strict account-level risk limits.
