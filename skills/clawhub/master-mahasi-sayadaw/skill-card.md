## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for questions about Mahasi Sayadaw, Burmese vipassana, the Mahasi noting method, Satipatthana practice, insight knowledges, and related Theravada meditation topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill as a Chinese-language Buddhist meditation teaching aid for Mahasi Sayadaw and Burmese vipassana topics. It is intended for study support with citation rules and guardrails, not as a substitute for a qualified meditation teacher.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used for practice problems or claims about meditation attainment.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the skill for attainment confirmation or urgent practice issues; consult a qualified meditation teacher for individualized guidance.

Risk: The skill can activate broadly for related meditation topics and answer in a prescribed Chinese teaching style.

Mitigation: Review responses for relevance, citation grounding, and suitability before using them in user-facing or instructional contexts.

Risk: Meditation literature may include copyrighted translations or authorized publications.

Mitigation: Use summaries and source citations rather than long verbatim reproduction, following the artifact's citation and no-fabricated-quote rules.
