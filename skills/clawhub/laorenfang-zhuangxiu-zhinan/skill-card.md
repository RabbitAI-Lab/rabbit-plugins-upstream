## Use Case:

Homeowners, caregivers, and renovation advisors use this Chinese-language skill to get practical elderly-room renovation guidance for fall prevention, lighting, accessibility, bathroom safety, layout, and material choices. It is best used as a planning checklist and discussion aid, not as a substitute for professional design, construction, medical, or local-code review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release is a mixed renovation reference pack rather than a vetted elder-safety guide.

Mitigation: For real elderly-room renovations, verify safety-critical advice with qualified professionals and local building requirements.

Risk: Off-topic decor, showcase, child-safety, and purchase-prompt content may dilute safety-focused advice.

Mitigation: Prioritize fall prevention, bathroom safety, lighting, accessibility, and other elder-safety requirements over style or product suggestions.
