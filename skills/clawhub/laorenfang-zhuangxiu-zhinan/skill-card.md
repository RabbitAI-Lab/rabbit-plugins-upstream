## Use Case:

Homeowners, family members and caregivers use this skill for Chinese-language guidance on planning safer rooms for older adults, including slip prevention, lighting and accessible layouts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation guidance may not meet local building, electrical, plumbing or accessibility requirements.

Mitigation: Check applicable requirements and confirm safety-critical decisions with qualified local professionals.

Risk: A child-room reference may introduce recommendations unsuitable for older adults.

Mitigation: Filter out child-specific advice and apply the skill only to questions directly concerning elderly rooms.
