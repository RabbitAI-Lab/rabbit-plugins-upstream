## Description:

Helps agents frame constrained strategic decisions, compare distinct options, and recommend an explicit bet with risks and review triggers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Teams, leaders, and individuals use this skill to choose a direction under constraints, weigh strategic alternatives, and identify risks, non-goals, and review triggers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reviewing available roadmaps, metrics, or market notes may expose sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Limit document access to authorized materials and avoid disclosing sensitive details in shared outputs.

Risk: Unsupported market assumptions could lead to misleading strategic recommendations.

Mitigation: Verify material claims against available evidence, state unknowns explicitly, and review decisions before acting.
