## Use Case:

Creative agents can use this guide to shape abstract calligraphic and geometric compositions, then evaluate rhythm, pattern, symmetry, and material character.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Abstract calligraphic marks may be mistaken for authentic or meaningful religious text.

Mitigation: Label invented marks as abstract; verify wording and cultural context before using real script or sacred text.
