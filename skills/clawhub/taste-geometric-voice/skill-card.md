## Description:

Aesthetic style skill that guides AI agents toward Islamic calligraphic and geometric visual conventions using style tokens, anti-tokens, application rules, and evaluation criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, designers, and agents use this skill to shape visual-art prompts, critiques, or generated outputs around Islamic calligraphic rhythm, geometric patterning, arabesque density, and material preciousness. It is best suited for non-figurative creative direction and evaluation rather than automation or data access.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creative outputs may be steered strongly toward Islamic calligraphic and geometric visual conventions.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when that visual tradition is intended, and review outputs for fit, cultural sensitivity, and alignment with the disclosed anti-tokens.
