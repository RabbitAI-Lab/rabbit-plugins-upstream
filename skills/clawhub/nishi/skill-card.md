## Use Case:

External users and learners use this skill as a Chinese-focused study aid for TCM formula history, classical terminology, and source comparison. It is intended to support reading and preparation for professional care, not diagnosis, prescriptions, dosing, or treatment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake historical TCM terminology or formula discussion for personal medical advice.

Mitigation: The skill directs the agent to label content as historical or learning material and avoid diagnosis, prescriptions, dosing, treatment changes, and personalized formula matching.

Risk: Urgent symptoms or high-risk situations may require immediate professional care.

Mitigation: The skill instructs the agent to prioritize emergency triage and refer users to local emergency services or qualified clinicians before any learning discussion.

Risk: Requests for herbs, formulas, dosing, preparation, or medication changes could lead to unsafe self-treatment.

Mitigation: The skill requires refusal of individualized prescribing or use instructions and redirects to non-actionable historical context or consultation with qualified medical professionals.
