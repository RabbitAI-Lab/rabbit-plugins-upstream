## Description:

Generates Chinese and English AI-content disclosure statements, suggested metadata labels, and platform placement tips from a specified content type and level of AI involvement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and publishers use the skill to draft bilingual disclosure text and metadata suggestions for AI-assisted or AI-generated content before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft disclosures can be inaccurate or mistaken for legal advice, including an unverified claim that a human reviewed the content.

Mitigation: Review and edit each disclosure to reflect actual creation and review practices; confirm applicable labeling requirements independently.

Risk: Documentation describes automatic detection and file or metadata updates that the bundled CLI does not perform.

Mitigation: Supply the AI involvement level explicitly and apply any required labels or metadata to the published asset separately.
