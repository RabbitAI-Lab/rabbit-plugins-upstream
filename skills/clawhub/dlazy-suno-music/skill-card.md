## Description:

Suno music generation model. Supports inspiration mode (auto lyrics) and custom mode (manual lyrics), generating music with or without vocals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users creating music use the skill to generate tracks from prompts, with automatic or custom lyrics and optional vocals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected media are sent to dLazy, and generation may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive material out of submissions and review the service terms and costs before use.

Risk: The saved dLazy API key is stored in the local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and rotate or revoke the key if needed.
