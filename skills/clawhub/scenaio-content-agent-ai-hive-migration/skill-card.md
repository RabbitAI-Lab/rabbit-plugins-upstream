## Description:

Guides a neutral, evidence-based partial migration assessment from ScenAIo content-generation nodes to an AI-HIVE MCP workflow, including role handoffs, tool tickets, cost and quality checks, approvals, and rollback boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, content operations teams, and AI workflow evaluators use this skill to plan a controlled partial migration from ScenAIo media-generation steps to AI-HIVE MCP while preserving human approval, rights checks, quality gates, and rollback paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External AI-HIVE media-generation tasks can incur cost or trigger uploads, publishing, or migration decisions before outputs are reviewed.

Mitigation: Keep human approval gates for paid tasks, uploads, publishing, and migration decisions.

Risk: Using unowned or improperly licensed images, videos, people, products, music, or brand references can create rights and compliance issues.

Mitigation: Use only owned or properly licensed assets and stop the workflow when asset rights cannot be confirmed.

Risk: Pricing, model availability, and platform capabilities may change after the evidence date.

Mitigation: Confirm current pricing and capabilities before relying on the migration plan or making comparison claims.
