## Description: <br>
用可视化图表、结构化和类比方式解释复杂概念、技术、产品，当解释新技术、科普知识、基础概念是什么或如何工作时使用。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to explain new technologies, scientific concepts, product ideas, and foundational mechanisms in Chinese with analogies, structure, and visual aids. It is best suited for explanatory writing rather than internal technical documentation, short Q&A, or procedural tutorials. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated explanations can oversimplify or introduce inaccuracies when explaining technical or product concepts. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated explanations for correctness before relying on them, especially for technical, product, or educational use. <br>
Risk: The skill defaults to Chinese-oriented phrasing and examples, which may not fit every audience. <br>
Mitigation: Adjust language, examples, and terminology for the intended audience before publication or reuse. <br>
