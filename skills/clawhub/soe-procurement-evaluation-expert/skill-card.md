## Description: <br>
国企采购评审专家提供中文国有企业采购合规决策支持，覆盖招标与非招标采购评审、否决投标与废标判定、串通识别、异议投诉、采购文件审查、风险评析、必须招标边界和三重一大合规审查。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, procurement reviewers, compliance teams, and enterprise legal reviewers use this skill for Chinese-language state-owned enterprise procurement review and risk analysis under the PRC Tendering and Bidding Law framework and SOE procurement management norms. It helps structure eligibility checks, legal basis indexing, risk grading, objection or complaint handling, terminology correction, and actionable procurement compliance recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
