Risk: The skill may prompt users to provide confidential company, customer, revenue, deal, or legal information. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized or minimized inputs where possible and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive details. <br>
Risk: The output may be mistaken for financial, legal, or M&A advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as a planning stress test and route real transaction decisions through qualified M&A counsel, bankers, and advisors. <br>
