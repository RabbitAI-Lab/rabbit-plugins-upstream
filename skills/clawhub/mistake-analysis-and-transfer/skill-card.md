## Description:

Helps students and learning assistants turn real mistake evidence from screenshots, photos, or pasted text into structured mistake analyses, consolidation advice, and optional local mistake-notebook records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caroljiang150-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/caroljiang150-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, tutors, and learning assistants use this skill to identify the concrete causes of wrong answers, break down the relevant knowledge points, and produce focused review guidance. When the user asks to save or update records, it can maintain a local mistake notebook for later search, statistics, and review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved notebook entries may contain student answers, problem text, and recognized screenshot content.

Mitigation: Install only when local mistake-notebook maintenance is intended, and choose a workspace or directory appropriate for storing that study data.
