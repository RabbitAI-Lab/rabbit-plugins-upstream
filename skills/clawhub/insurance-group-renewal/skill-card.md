## Description:

Provides China-focused group insurance renewal analysis, loss-ratio assessment, procurement comparisons, and negotiation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Group insurance account managers, corporate HR benefits teams, and brokers use this skill to draft renewal analyses, compare insurance proposals, assess loss ratios, and prepare negotiation strategies. Its guidance does not replace actuarial, underwriting, or compliance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing estimates, industry benchmarks, or regulatory guidance may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify current rules and actuarial assumptions independently, and obtain actuarial, underwriting, and compliance review before decisions or external use.

Risk: Insurance analysis may expose employee or claimant personal and health information.

Mitigation: Use aggregate, de-identified data; do not enter employee names, IDs, medical details, or claim-level records.

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate this skill for an unrelated request.

Mitigation: Confirm the request concerns China-focused group insurance renewal before applying its guidance.
