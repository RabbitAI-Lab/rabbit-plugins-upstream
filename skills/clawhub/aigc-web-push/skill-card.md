## Description:

Supports AI-assisted creation of articles, image posts, and videos, then publishes them to WeChat Official Accounts or through cloud computers to supported sites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lihengdao](https://clawhub.ai/user/lihengdao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and publishing teams can use the skill to create or prepare articles, image posts, and videos, then send them to a selected WeChat Official Account or cloud-computer publishing target. It also provides guidance for pushing compatible content created by other skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates the skill suspicious with high confidence. Its summary cites a default-enabled updater that replaces skill files, globally active instructions, and broad publishing and draft-deletion capabilities; the assessment is incomplete.

Mitigation: The scanner recommends not installing. If considering use, review update behavior and external data flows, disable automatic updating, and explicitly authorize publishing or draft deletion.
