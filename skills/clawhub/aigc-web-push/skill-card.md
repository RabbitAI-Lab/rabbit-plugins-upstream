## Description:

支持通过 AI 生成文章、图文卡片与视频，并推送到微信公众号或经由云电脑推送至全球任意站点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lihengdao](https://clawhub.ai/user/lihengdao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and teams use this skill to draft articles, image cards, and videos with AI, then publish them to WeChat Official Accounts or websites through a cloud computer. It also supports adapting content created by other skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan marks the release suspicious and reports bulk draft deletion behavior for review.

Mitigation: Review the draft-deletion behavior and confirm its scope before installation or use.

Risk: Music searches may send information to an external service.

Mitigation: Confirm what information is sent before using music search.

Risk: External installers are unpinned, and analysis of some files was partial, leaving behavior unverified.

Mitigation: Verify installer sources and pin versions; review the skill before installation.
