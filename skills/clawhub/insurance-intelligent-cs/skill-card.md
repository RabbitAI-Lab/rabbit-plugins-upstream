## Description:

Provides conversation guidance for Chinese life and property insurers, covering policy inquiries, claims, product questions, renewals, and privacy-aware data handling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance customer-service teams, supervisors, and chatbot configurators use the skill to draft and review responses, routing rules, claims checklists, renewal reminders, and privacy-aware handling guidance. Outputs are reference material for review against policy terms and institutional procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A fixed 60-day renewal statement may not match the applicable policy's grace-period, coverage, termination, or reinstatement rules; scanner confidence is medium and inspection was partial.

Mitigation: Before relying on renewal guidance, verify those conditions against the applicable policy or with an authorized reviewer.
