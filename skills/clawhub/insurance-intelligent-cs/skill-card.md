## Description:

Provides China-focused insurance customer service scripts and guidance for policy inquiries, claims, product questions, renewals, and customer-data handling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance customer service teams and chatbot designers use this skill to draft policy and claims responses, renewal reminders, escalation rules, and privacy-conscious service scripts for China life and property insurance customers. Staff must review the guidance before using it in their own systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance, regulatory, or retention language may not match current requirements or a specific policy.

Mitigation: Have legal and compliance staff verify guidance against current rules, institutional policies, and contract terms before production use.

Risk: Customer identity, health, and payment information could be unnecessarily exposed in service conversations.

Mitigation: Verify authorization, minimize requested data, mask sensitive identifiers, and follow the organization's customer-data handling rules.

Risk: Automated claims or product responses could imply a guaranteed payout or definitive coverage decision.

Mitigation: Avoid payout estimates and guarantees; refer decisions and disputed or sensitive cases to authorized staff.
