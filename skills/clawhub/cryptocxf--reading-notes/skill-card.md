## Description:

上传书籍文本、摘抄段落或书籍截图，梳理书籍核心框架、人物关系、核心论点和金句摘抄，并分章节生成思维导图式结构化笔记。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, educators, researchers, and general readers use this skill to turn user-provided book text, excerpts, or screenshots into structured Chinese reading notes for reports, assignments, study review, and citation planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may upload private or copyrighted book content without having rights to use it.

Mitigation: Use only material the user is allowed to provide, and avoid uploading sensitive private text.

Risk: Generated quotes, citations, or source labels may be inaccurate when input is incomplete, OCR is unclear, or chapter and page data are missing.

Mitigation: Review quoted text, chapter labels, and citation details against the original source before using them in school or research work.
