## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners use this skill to understand what may have gone wrong in a renovation and compare practical repair, replacement, or escalation options. It is intended for text-based advice on common home renovation defects, costs, and repair tradeoffs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may encourage DIY handling of electrical, plumbing, cutting, mold, waterproofing, wall, or ceiling repair situations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat repair steps as informal ideas only and use licensed professionals for electrical wiring, active leaks, waterproofing, mold, structural cracks, ceiling instability, and cutting stone or integrated fixtures. <br>
Risk: Renovation defects can be site-specific, and text-only advice may miss safety or liability conditions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm serious defects on site, stop water or power during urgent incidents, preserve photos and records, and escalate to qualified contractors or relevant complaint channels when needed. <br>
