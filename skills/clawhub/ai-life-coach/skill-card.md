## Description:

AI Life Coach uses Socratic, structured dialogue to support self-awareness, goal clarification, and action planning, with crisis language routed to a crisis-first safety assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for reflective life-coaching conversations that help them clarify goals, explore current stressors or life direction, and turn insights into next actions. It is not intended for technical tasks, document work, data analysis, therapy, medical advice, legal advice, investment advice, or replacing professional crisis support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can auto-activate on broad emotional language and steer ordinary distress, anxiety, or discouragement into a coaching conversation.

Mitigation: Review activation triggers before deployment and prefer explicit user invocation or clear opt-in for coaching interactions.

Risk: The skill describes saving concise summaries of sensitive disclosures to a local memory file.

Mitigation: Install only where local memory persistence is acceptable, and provide clear consent, review, and deletion paths for saved memories.

Risk: The skill engages with distress and crisis language but is not a substitute for professional mental-health or emergency support.

Mitigation: Keep the crisis-first assessment and escalation behavior intact, and avoid using the skill as therapy, diagnosis, or medical guidance.
