## Description:

AI人生教练 is a Chinese-focused Socratic life-coaching skill for self-awareness, goal clarity, emotional support, and action planning, with crisis-first routing, under-18 safeguards, anti-sycophancy guidance, and local-only memory behavior.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for structured life-coaching conversations around confusion, motivation, relationships, school stress, emotional support, self-awareness, goal setting, and concrete next steps. It is not a substitute for licensed mental-health care or emergency services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store sensitive emotional summaries in local memory files under user-chosen filenames.

Mitigation: Use a non-identifying alias, avoid shared accounts or devices, decline memory when privacy matters, and review or delete local memory files as needed.

Risk: The skill can promote a separate web version with different privacy exposure.

Mitigation: Treat the web version as a separate external service and review its privacy behavior before transferring or entering sensitive information.

Risk: Users may treat life-coaching dialogue as a replacement for licensed mental-health care or emergency help.

Mitigation: Keep the skill positioned as coaching support and use professional care, trusted local support, or emergency resources for clinical, crisis, or immediate safety needs.
