## Description:

A conversational life coach that uses reflective questions to support self-awareness, goal setting, and practical next steps without making decisions for the user.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[luhayden-blip](https://clawhub.ai/user/luhayden-blip)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use the skill for reflective conversations about uncertainty, relationships, motivation, and school or work, with questions that help them identify goals and choose their own next steps. It is not a substitute for emergency or clinical mental-health care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coaching conversations and local memory may contain sensitive personal history, especially on shared devices.

Mitigation: Review before installing on shared devices; use a non-identifying alias and avoid recording unnecessary personal details.

Risk: Alias-based access to coaching memory is weak; another person's reused alias may expose their notes.

Mitigation: Do not reuse another person's alias, and protect access to the device and local memory files.

Risk: A life-coaching conversation cannot provide emergency mental-health support.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the skill in an emergency; seek appropriate local emergency or crisis services.

Risk: The linked website is a separate service with different data handling from this local skill.

Mitigation: Review the website's privacy practices separately before transferring or entering sensitive information.
