## Description:

AI Life Coach is a Chinese-language coaching prompt skill that uses Socratic dialogue to support self-awareness, goal clarification, and action planning while routing crisis signals to a safety-first check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[luhayden-blip](https://clawhub.ai/user/luhayden-blip)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Chinese-language life-coaching conversations that help them explore emotions, clarify life direction, set goals, and turn reflections into next-step action plans. It is framed as coaching and personal growth support, not therapy, medical care, legal advice, financial advice, or crisis care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad emotional triggers may activate coaching in sensitive conversations.

Mitigation: Deploy only where Chinese-language life-coaching behavior is expected, and disclose that the skill may respond to broad emotional distress phrases.

Risk: Users may treat coaching guidance as therapy or crisis support.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's coaching boundary visible, route self-harm or suicidal ideation to immediate safety assessment and qualified professional or emergency support, and pause coaching when crisis risk is present.
