## Description:

AI人生教练 is a life-coaching dialogue skill that helps users clarify self-awareness, goals, and next actions while routing crisis, minor-safety, and relationship-risk topics to stricter support boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for reflective life-coaching conversations about emotional difficulty, motivation, life direction, family, and relationship decisions. The skill is intended to support self-awareness, goal clarity, and small action planning, not to replace licensed mental-health, medical, legal, or emergency services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles sensitive emotional, family, relationship, crisis, and minor-safety topics.

Mitigation: Use it as coaching support only; pause coaching-style exploration for crisis signals and route users to trusted adults, emergency services, or qualified professional support when safety concerns appear.

Risk: Local memory summaries may contain sensitive emotional records, which is risky on shared or unmanaged devices.

Mitigation: Use local memory only on a trusted personal device, keep summaries minimal, and honor user requests not to store or continue storing session details.

Risk: The artifact links to a separate web version whose privacy properties are not fully established by the provided evidence.

Mitigation: Treat the website as a separate service and avoid entering sensitive information there unless its privacy and data-handling terms are acceptable.
