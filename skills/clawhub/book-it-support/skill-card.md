## Use Case: <br>
External users can ask an agent to search for IT support providers through Lokuli, check availability, and create a booking after confirming service details and contact information. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Creating a booking sends the user's name, email, and phone number to an external booking service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, time slot, and contact details with the user before creating a booking. <br>
Risk: Provider availability and service details may change before booking is completed. <br>
Mitigation: Search and check availability immediately before presenting booking options for user confirmation. <br>
