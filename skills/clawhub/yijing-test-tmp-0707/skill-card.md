## Description: <br>
Provides I Ching 64-hexagram divination analysis, including six-line numeric decoding, classical text presentation, Zhu Xi and Nan Huai-Chin style interpretation, daily-life advice, and usage statistics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qiuzijun-nm](https://clawhub.ai/user/qiuzijun-nm) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to decode six-line I Ching readings, retrieve related hexagram material, and generate plain-language cultural interpretation and practical guidance. It is suited for cultural reference and reflective analysis, not for professional health, legal, financial, or dispute advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill stores local reading history and statistics, which may include sensitive user-provided context. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive health, legal, financial, relationship, or dispute details unless local retention is intended; clear or disable logs when privacy matters. <br>
Risk: Divination-style output can be mistaken for professional advice in high-stakes situations. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs for cultural reference and reflection only, and seek qualified professional advice for health, legal, financial, or dispute matters. <br>
