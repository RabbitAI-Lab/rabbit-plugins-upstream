## Description:

Writes platform-ready social posts, saves them as Postify drafts for connected channels, and suggests posting times without publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing social channels use this skill to prepare platform-specific posts in their own voice, save them as reviewable Postify drafts, and see suggested posting times.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting Postify grants access to available channels and media and allows saving drafts.

Mitigation: Confirm the connection and permissions before use; review the post and selected channel before saving the draft.
