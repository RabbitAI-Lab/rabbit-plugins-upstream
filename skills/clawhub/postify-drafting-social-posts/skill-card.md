## Description:

Writes platform-ready social posts, saves them as Postify drafts for connected channels, and suggests posting times without publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media managers and other users draft posts for connected channels, review them in Postify, and receive suggested posting times.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A request for general copywriting may unintentionally save a draft in Postify.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for plain text only or avoid invoking this skill when Postify access is not wanted.

Risk: Draft copy may contain unsupported claims or links.

Mitigation: Provide source URLs and verify claims; review the saved draft in Postify before scheduling or publishing.
