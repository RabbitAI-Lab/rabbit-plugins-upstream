## Description:

Helps sellers create product main images inspired by a reference image's composition and visual style using their own product photo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and designers use a reference listing image and their own product photo to generate a new e-commerce main image with a similar layout and visual style, without copying the reference brand elements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing or updating the qhkit CLI can change the local execution environment.

Mitigation: Review the package and prefer a vetted, pinned version in managed environments.

Risk: Uploaded product and reference images are sent to Qinghu's service.

Mitigation: Share only images authorized for upload and avoid sensitive or confidential content.

Risk: Replicating a competitor's protected branding or imagery may infringe rights.

Mitigation: Borrow only layout and style; remove the reference product, brand names, logos and watermarks.

Risk: Generated product details or text may differ from the actual item.

Mitigation: Check product structure, copy and branding before publishing.
