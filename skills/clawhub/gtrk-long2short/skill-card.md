## Description:

Turns long videos into multiple semantically selected highlight clips and creates editable gtrk, Jianying, and Premiere Pro project files, with optional split-screen assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitruck](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitruck)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and editors use this skill to turn long recordings into several editable highlight projects for further polishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audio is uploaded for cloud processing; split-screen mode also uploads a 720p video proxy.

Mitigation: Disclose the uploads and current cost estimate before processing, especially when split-screen is enabled.

Risk: Rerunning can overwrite edited drafts and incur cloud charges.

Mitigation: Back up edited drafts and confirm current pricing before rerunning.

Risk: The external CLI and referenced companion skills were not audited here.

Mitigation: Review the CLI and companion skills before use.
