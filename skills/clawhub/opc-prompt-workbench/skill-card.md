## Use Case:

External users and OPC operators use this skill to choose and customize prompt templates for one-person-company workflows such as user research, PRDs, project follow-up, content planning, quotations, business retrospectives, emotional reflection, and contract triage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Emotional-reflection templates could be mistaken for mental-health diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Use them only for reflection and action planning; recommend professional support when self-harm risk appears or distress persists.

Risk: Contract-review templates could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Present contract output as triage only and direct important contracts to qualified local professionals for review.

Risk: Users may submit sensitive contract, client, employee, health, or financial details to an external model.

Mitigation: Redact or generalize sensitive details before using the skill with any external model.

Risk: Prompt-generated business outputs may include incorrect examples, prices, data, or legal facts.

Mitigation: Keep missing information as placeholders or ask targeted follow-up questions, and require user review before relying on generated outputs.
