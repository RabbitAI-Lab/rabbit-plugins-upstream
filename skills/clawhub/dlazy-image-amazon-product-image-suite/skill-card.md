## Description:

Guides users through planning and generating Amazon product detail page images, including main, secondary, and A+ content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External Amazon sellers and ecommerce teams use this skill to plan and generate product listing images and A+ brand content for a product and its audience.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and rotate or revoke the key if it may have been exposed.

Mitigation: Check the CLI version reference before installation.
