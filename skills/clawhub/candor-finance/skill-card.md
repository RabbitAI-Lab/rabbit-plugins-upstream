## Description:

Opens the person's Candor workspace, says where their money stands, and routes to the right Candor workflow. Use when the person asks about their money, spending, balances, bills, debts, goals, or plans and no more specific Candor skill fits, including a quick question about a balance, a bill, or a charge, or a life decision that costs or earns money even when money is not named.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People using Candor use this skill to understand their finances and manage goals, budgets, records, bills, and other money decisions through their Candor workspace.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment marks the skill suspicious, citing broad financial data changes, local handling of sensitive financial data, external actions beyond its stated purpose, and incomplete analysis.

Mitigation: Install only if these capabilities match your needs and you are comfortable with the data handling; review changes to financial records and get clear consent before taking action outside Candor.
