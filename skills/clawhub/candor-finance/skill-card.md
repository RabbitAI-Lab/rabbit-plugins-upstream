## Description:

Helps users organize financial records and spending, review investments, plan budgets and goals, and investigate potential savings with supporting evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[candor](https://clawhub.ai/user/candor)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use Candor to examine their financial records, understand spending and obligations, compare options, and maintain approved budgets, goals, and follow-up. An authenticated Candor workspace and active service subscription are required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Access to sensitive personal financial records may expose private information.

Mitigation: Grant Candor access only if comfortable with its handling of financial records; limit shared details to those needed for the task.

Risk: Record edits, imports, durable notes, or scheduled checks may persist beyond the immediate conversation.

Mitigation: Review requested writes and schedules, verify their results, and keep recommendations separate from approved decisions.

Risk: Financial actions or account changes may have external consequences.

Mitigation: Use secure Candor pages for credentials, connections, payments, trades, filings, cancellations, and subscription changes.
