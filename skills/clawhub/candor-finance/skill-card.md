## Description:

Opens the person's Candor workspace, summarizes their finances, and routes them to a relevant workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[candor](https://clawhub.ai/user/candor)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

People with an authenticated Candor account use this skill to review their balances, spending, bills, debts, goals, and plans using their workspace records. It routes them to focused workflows for tasks such as budgeting, transaction sorting, record management, check-ins, and tax preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release is rated suspicious with medium confidence, and inspection was incomplete; reported concerns involve persistent financial record changes and external financial actions.

Mitigation: Review the relevant workflows before installing or using them with a real financial workspace, and inspect saved changes. Require explicit approval before external actions such as payments, transfers, cancellations, disputes, messages, applications, or trades.
