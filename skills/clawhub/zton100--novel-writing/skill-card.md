## Description: <br>
AI-assisted Novel Writing helps authors plan, draft, and track long-form serial fiction by managing settings, outlines, chapters, foreshadowing, conflicts, and reader feedback. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zton100](https://clawhub.ai/user/zton100) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Authors and writing agents use this skill to initialize and maintain file-backed novel projects, plan outlines, draft chapters, track foreshadowing and conflicts, and incorporate reader feedback. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can read and modify files inside a local novel project. <br>
Mitigation: Use it in a dedicated project folder and review generated or modified files before committing, sharing, or relying on them. <br>
Risk: File-backed writing state, outlines, and feedback may contain private or unpublished story material. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the project in an appropriate local workspace and avoid sharing generated project files unless the contents have been reviewed. <br>
