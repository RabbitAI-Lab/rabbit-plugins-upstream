## Description:

Guides users through turning a broad trend question into measurable curves, concrete bets and non-bets, and dated signals for later validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, creators, product builders, operators, and individuals use this skill to analyze major changes such as AI, platform shifts, and industry disruption, then translate the analysis into practical next actions and dated validation signals. It is not intended to provide investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad trend or opportunity questions and frame outputs as decision guidance.

Mitigation: Keep outputs grounded in measurable curves, dated sources, and explicit confidence levels; avoid presenting trend analysis as certainty.

Risk: The skill may save dated judgments, signal outcomes, and selected reference updates in the configured memory path.

Mitigation: Write only relevant records with stated evidence, check for duplicates, and respect the configured memory path.

Risk: Users may ask for stock, crypto, fund, or other investment recommendations.

Mitigation: State that the skill does not provide investment advice and redirect to structural analysis, observable signals, and user-owned decisions.
