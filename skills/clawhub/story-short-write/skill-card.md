## Description:

Helps agents plan, draft, revise, and quality-check Chinese short-form web fiction with emphasis on emotional tension, pacing, reversals, platform formatting, and manuscript polish.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, writers, and writing agents use this skill to develop Chinese short web-fiction projects from emotional target and story framework through draft, revision, and quality checks. It is suited for workflows that create or update outlines, settings, manuscript text, reference-analysis folders, and local review outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create or modify local manuscript and planning files such as 正文.md, 设定.md, 小节大纲.md, 拆文库/, and 对标/ in the active workspace.

Mitigation: Review the target workspace before use and keep backups for important manuscripts.

Risk: Generated fiction guidance or local quality reports may be incomplete, stylistically unsuitable, or misleading for a publication workflow.

Mitigation: Have a human writer or editor review outlines, drafts, formatting, and quality-check findings before publication or submission.
