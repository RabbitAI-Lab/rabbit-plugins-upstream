## Use Case: <br>
External authors and writing agents use this skill to plan, draft, format, and polish Chinese short web-fiction manuscripts with genre-specific structure, hooks, emotional pacing, and local quality checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can write manuscript and planning files, so an agent could overwrite an existing draft if the target path is unclear. <br>
Mitigation: Tell the agent whether to reply in chat or write files, and require confirmation of target paths before overwriting existing drafts. <br>
Risk: Story-trope guidance may mention recordings, evidence, online exposure, or similar plot devices that should not be treated as real-life legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use those elements only as fictional writing material and seek qualified advice for real legal, safety, or privacy decisions. <br>
Risk: Local quality-check scripts report style and degeneration findings but do not replace human editorial review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated prose manually, especially flagged passages, before publication or submission. <br>
