## Description:

Helps authors plan, draft, and revise Chinese short-form web fiction around a chosen emotional effect and narrative pace.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Authors and writing assistants use this skill to develop Chinese short stories from an emotional premise through an outline, draft, and revision, with optional local author preferences and reference-story comparisons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Story drafts and outlines may be changed by the writing workflow.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated fiction-writing workspace and review changes before relying on important drafts.

Risk: Requested long-term author preferences may be stored locally.

Mitigation: Only ask the skill to remember preferences you intend to retain in the workspace.
