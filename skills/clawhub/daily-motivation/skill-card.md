## Use Case: <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Motivation prompts may include sensitive personal goals, wins, streaks, or accountability details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive medical, financial, credential, or deeply private information when using this skill. <br>
Risk: Common phrases such as "motivate me" may activate the skill unexpectedly. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated motivational response before relying on it or sharing it with others. <br>
