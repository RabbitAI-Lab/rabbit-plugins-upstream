## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad auto-invocation wording could cause the skill to trigger more often than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow or disable implicit invocation so the skill is used only for explicit Humanizer-style workflow requests. <br>
Risk: Workflow proposals may be incomplete or poorly matched if the user's success criteria are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Restate the outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing the final artifact. <br>
