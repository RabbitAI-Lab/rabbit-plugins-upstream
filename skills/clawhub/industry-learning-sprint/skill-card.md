## Description: <br>
A structured one-week process that helps investors, founders, executives, and advisors build a working mental model of an unfamiliar industry using financial reports, expert dialogue, and a falsifiable non-consensus view. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and business operators use this skill when entering an unfamiliar industry, preparing for an expert conversation, or forming an investment, acquisition, partnership, or market-entry thesis under time pressure. It guides the agent to sequence primary financial-document review before expert dialogue and final hypothesis formation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The generated industry thesis may be misleading if users rely on unverified source documents or incomplete expert input. <br>
Mitigation: Use primary annual reports and earnings transcripts, stress-test hypotheses with several expert categories, and document known gaps before relying on the output. <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the sprint output for professional financial, legal, or regulatory advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as research guidance, verify claims independently, and consult qualified domain, legal, regulatory, or financial experts for high-stakes decisions. <br>
