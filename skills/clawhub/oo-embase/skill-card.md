## Description:

Embase helps agents search biomedical literature and retrieve individual Embase article records through an OOMOL-connected account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers and agents use this skill to search Embase biomedical literature and retrieve documented article records through the OOMOL Embase connector.
