## Description:

Connects customer profiles and chat histories from WeChat and other supported social and service platforms to AI tools for customer lookup, conversation analysis, segmentation, profile and tag updates, and user-confirmed bulk messaging.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhinikefu](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhinikefu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer support and sales teams use the skill to search and analyze conversations across connected accounts, identify customer segments and leads, and maintain customer profiles and tags. Changes and outreach are carried out only after the user confirms the target customers and action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can access customer profiles and private conversations through connected platform accounts.

Mitigation: Use only accounts and customer data the user is authorized to access, and limit queries to the requested customers, channels, and time range.

Risk: Profile and tag changes or bulk messages can affect customer records and communications.

Mitigation: Confirm customer identities, requested changes, recipients, and final message content with the user before carrying out these actions.
