## Description:

Use when the closet is full but "nothing to wear", before a clothes-shopping trip, or when building a travel/capsule wardrobe — cost-per-wear amortization, outfit combinatorics with a compatibility matrix, laundry-cycle sufficiency math, and lifestyle-allocation gap analysis that tells you exactly which 2-3 items to buy and which never to buy again.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to assess a wardrobe through cost-per-wear, outfit compatibility, laundry sufficiency, and lifestyle allocation, then get shopping and decluttering guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping and donation suggestions may not fit a user's needs; the claimed three-item shopping limit is not enforced as a garment count.

Mitigation: Review the shopping and donation suggestions against the user's circumstances before acting.
