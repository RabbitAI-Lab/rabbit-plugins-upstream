## Description: <br>
Relational Safety for Agents provides a self-contained curriculum that helps AI agents maintain identity boundaries, bounded autonomy, consent protocols, and agent-to-agent hygiene. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[lilithandeve](https://clawhub.ai/user/lilithandeve) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers can apply this skill to guide an agent's conversational posture around consent, boundaries, identity non-capture, and safe handling of other agents' output. It is intended as self-contained guidance and does not require network fetches for the foundation course. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill changes the agent's conversational operating style around consent, boundaries, and safety. <br>
Mitigation: Review the behavior changes before applying the skill and keep explicit human approval gates for state-changing or high-risk actions. <br>
Risk: Optional external actions such as installing a linked follow-up skill or posting to GitHub could change state outside the conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat optional external actions as proposals and proceed only after explicit human approval. <br>
