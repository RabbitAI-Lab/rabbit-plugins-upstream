## Description: <br>
Provides standards-based pork cutting guidance based on GB/T 40466-2021, covering terminology, raw material checks, workshop requirements, cutting procedures, labeling, packaging, storage, and transport. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, slaughterhouse teams, meat processors, and meat product manufacturers use this skill to look up pork cutting standards and follow standardized hot or cold cutting workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security summary notes that the skill is a standards-based reference but lacks prominent safety and PPE cautions for real-world cutting work. <br>
Mitigation: Use it with site safety SOPs, trained supervision, PPE requirements, sanitation rules, equipment guarding, lockout and maintenance procedures, and emergency response guidance. <br>
