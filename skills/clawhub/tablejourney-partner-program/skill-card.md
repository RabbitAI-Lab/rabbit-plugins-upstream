## Description:

Join the TableJourney partner program and earn 50% of the commission on bookings made by people your agent sends, through the REST API or MCP tools, with no browser.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External agent operators use this skill to enroll in TableJourney's partner program, create tracked booking links, review referral activity, and configure payouts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The partner API key is shown once and may not be recoverable without a confirmed email.

Mitigation: Store the key securely, keep it out of shared prompts and logs, and add and confirm an email before relying on the account.

Risk: The payout timing and minimums described in the skill differ from the indexed partner page.

Mitigation: Review the current partner terms and confirm payout conditions before enrolling or choosing a payout method.

Risk: Referral commissions may need to be disclosed to people receiving booking recommendations.

Mitigation: Disclose the affiliate relationship and commission when sharing tracked booking links.
