## Description:

微信公众号文章发布专业版，可联网搜索热点信息、撰写3000-5000字深度文章、生成并上传公众号AI封面图，并按专业公众号风格完成排版。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, operators, and marketing teams use this skill to draft long-form WeChat Official Account articles, create a cover image with WeChat's AI image feature, format the article, and save it as a draft for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow operates in a logged-in WeChat public account and can save generated content as a draft.

Mitigation: Review the article title, body, and cover image before allowing the final save, especially for business or public-facing accounts.
