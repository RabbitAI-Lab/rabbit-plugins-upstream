## Description:

Deliver a reviewed article to a user-selected local file, document service, or publishing platform after final confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minibeanai](https://clawhub.ai/user/minibeanai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Article writers and publishing teams use this skill to deliver a finalized article to a specifically chosen file, document, or platform after confirming the content, destination, visibility, and publication mode.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An article could be sent to the wrong destination or made public unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm the final content, exact destination and account, visibility, and draft-versus-public choice before any write, upload, or publication.

Risk: Replacing an existing local file could destroy its previous contents.

Mitigation: Request a separate confirmation before replacing the named file.
