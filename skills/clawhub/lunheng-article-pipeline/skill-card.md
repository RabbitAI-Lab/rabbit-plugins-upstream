## Description:

通过多角色检索、分析、写作和审查流程，协助产出有证据支撑的学术论文、深度长文与行业分析，并提供投稿或发布渠道建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

研究人员、作者和分析人员使用该技能规划并撰写较长的研究或分析文章，在资料检索、论证、同行评审和最终交付环节保留人工确认。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow saves draft material and workflow metadata in local project, status, and audit files.

Mitigation: Review generated files and retention needs before using sensitive or unpublished material.

Risk: Depending on the initial consent choices, search terms or draft content may be sent to external services.

Mitigation: Choose the local/no-external mode for sensitive work and confirm external-service choices before starting.
