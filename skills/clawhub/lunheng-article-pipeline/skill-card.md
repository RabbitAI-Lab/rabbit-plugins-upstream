## Description:

Coordinates research, drafting, peer review, and editorial checks for evidence-based academic papers, in-depth articles, and industry analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zuoyunlai](https://clawhub.ai/user/zuoyunlai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Researchers, authors, and analysts use this skill to produce long-form, evidence-based writing with human checkpoints, research notes, drafts, and editorial assessments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project runs create numerous drafts, status records, and audit files that may contain sensitive material.

Mitigation: Confirm the project scope before starting and review generated files before sharing.

Risk: Depending on user choices, search terms or draft content may be sent to external services.

Mitigation: Choose the no-external/local path for sensitive work and explicitly approve any external services before use.

Risk: Editorial and quality checks are not machine-enforced guarantees of accuracy or integrity.

Mitigation: Have a person verify citations, factual claims, and final deliverables.
