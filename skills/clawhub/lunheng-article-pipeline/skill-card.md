## Description:

Coordinates a multi-agent workflow to research, draft, review, and audit evidence-based academic papers and long-form analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zuoyunlai](https://clawhub.ai/user/zuoyunlai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Researchers, writers, and analysts use this skill to develop sourced papers, in-depth articles, and industry analysis with staged review and human approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approved web research may send search terms or content to external services.

Mitigation: Use the no-external-services option for sensitive or confidential material.

Risk: The workflow creates numerous project-local files, including status and optional heartbeat records that may contain run metadata.

Mitigation: Confirm the workspace and review status and heartbeat files before sharing project archives.
