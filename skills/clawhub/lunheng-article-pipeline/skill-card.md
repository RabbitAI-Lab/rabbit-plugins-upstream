## Description:

面向学术论文、深度长文和行业分析的写作流水线，组织资料检索、写作、审计与同行评审，并提供发布渠道匹配建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

研究者、作者和分析人员使用该技能，协调资料检索、论证、长文写作、事实审计和同行评审，形成可供人工审阅的终稿与证据材料。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approved external research services may receive search terms or draft content.

Mitigation: Choose external-service consent deliberately; use the local/no-external option for confidential material.

Risk: The workflow coordinates subagents and creates many project files in the workspace.

Mitigation: Review and approve the proposed file list and workflow choices before starting.
