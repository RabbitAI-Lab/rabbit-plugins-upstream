## Description:

Coordinates research, drafting, peer review, and evidence checks for academic papers, in-depth articles, and industry analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zuoyunlai](https://clawhub.ai/user/zuoyunlai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers and researchers use this multi-agent workflow to develop evidence-backed long-form articles, academic papers, and industry reports with human checkpoints, audits, and advisory publication matching.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A long-running workflow creates or modifies many project files.

Mitigation: Confirm the Phase 0 file list before starting and review the generated project files.

Risk: External lookups may send search terms or draft content to third-party services.

Mitigation: Choose the no-external-services option for sensitive work, or explicitly approve the external-service categories in Phase 0.

Risk: The Chinese-language G14 style gate may affect the final wording.

Mitigation: Review its results and the final draft when the style gate applies.
