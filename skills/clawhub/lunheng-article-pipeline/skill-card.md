## Description:

A multi-agent research and writing workflow for academic papers, in-depth articles, and industry analysis, with evidence checks, peer review, and optional publication-channel advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zuoyunlai](https://clawhub.ai/user/zuoyunlai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Researchers, writers, and analysts use this skill to develop evidence-backed long-form manuscripts with human review, source checks, and separate audit reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External research may disclose search terms, URLs, or model inputs to providers.

Mitigation: For sensitive or unpublished material, select the no-external or local options at the initial consent step.

Risk: The workflow creates numerous files in the project workspace.

Mitigation: Review the proposed file list and use a dedicated workspace before starting.

Risk: Declared tool restrictions may not be enforced by the host platform.

Mitigation: Verify the OpenClaw host tool policy independently before enabling the skill.
