## Description:

Compares college financial aid offers by separating grants, work-study, and loans; estimating net price and debt; and drafting an appeal for changed family circumstances.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Students, families, and school counselors can use the skill to compare aid offers, understand grants versus work and borrowing, estimate costs and repayment, and prepare an appeal when family circumstances change.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Appeal workflows may involve sensitive financial, employment, or medical records, and privacy guidance is limited.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive records in prompts; send only necessary documents through the school's verified financial-aid channel.
