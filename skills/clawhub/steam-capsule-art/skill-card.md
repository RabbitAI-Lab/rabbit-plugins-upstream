## Description:

Guides agents in designing Steam store capsule art and auditing dimensions, composition, legibility, and platform constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[catsmansion](https://clawhub.ai/user/catsmansion)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game developers and artists use it to plan Steam store assets, compose each format for its intended layout, and audit exported images before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automated image checks may not fit every project's needs or establish that an asset meets all visual and current platform requirements.

Mitigation: Use the audit as one review aid, check dimensions against official Steam templates, and inspect the exported thumbnail.
