## Description: <br>
Transcribe audio files with speaker diarization, automatic language detection, timestamps, and optional JSON output using AssemblyAI. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[legionspace-hackathon](https://clawhub.ai/user/legionspace-hackathon) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and end users use this skill to transcribe meetings, interviews, podcasts, voice messages, or audio URLs into speaker-labeled transcripts with timestamps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Audio selected for transcription may be sent to AssemblyAI. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for recordings you have permission to process, and review AssemblyAI data-handling terms before using confidential, regulated, or non-consenting speakers' audio. <br>
