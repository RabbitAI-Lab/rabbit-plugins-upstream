## Description:

Helps owner-operators identify who can unilaterally change, interrupt, or constrain their business across platforms, landlords, licences, key people, suppliers, and payment channels, then classify each dependency by switching cost, switching time, alternatives, and urgency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business owners and operators use this skill to run a dependency checkup on production-side business risks: who controls access, rules, supply, premises, key labor, or payment flow, and what it would cost in time and money to switch away.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may process or store sensitive notes about suppliers, platforms, contracts, licences, key people, payment channels, and switching costs.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing details that should not be retained, review memory settings before use, and redact names when appropriate.

Risk: Business dependency assessments may be incomplete if switching cost or switching time is unknown.

Mitigation: Mark such entries as unassessed rather than controlled, then collect concrete cost and timing data before relying on the conclusion.
