## Description:

Use when the user describes a problem, decision or stuck situation but names no method: "how should I approach this?", "help me think this through", "we can't decide", "where do I start?". Picks the best-fit thinking method and runs it. Skip for plain execution tasks or when a method is named.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and developers can use this skill when they need help choosing a thinking method for an open-ended problem, decision, or stuck situation. It selects and runs one method based on the user's next decision or output.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reference check was incomplete, so relevant background material may not have been reviewed.

Mitigation: Inspect any relevant references for the use case before relying on the selected method.

Risk: A method may be a poor fit when the user's situation is unclear.

Mitigation: Base the choice on the user's next decision or output, and ask at most one clarifying question when equally suitable methods would lead to different choices.
