## Description:

Provides Persian-language guidance for creating a PowPow account, troubleshooting registration, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users use this Persian-language guide to learn about the platform, register on its website, resolve sign-up issues, and navigate subscription activation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose passwords, email verification codes, or payment details in chat.

Mitigation: Never request these details; users enter them only on official PowPow pages they intentionally opened.

Risk: Users may expect the assistant to register or pay on their behalf.

Mitigation: Keep the skill guidance-only and direct users to complete forms and payments themselves.

Risk: Subscription activation may be delayed, especially when manual confirmation is required.

Mitigation: Explain that activation can take time and refer unresolved payments to PowPow support rather than claiming immediate failure.
