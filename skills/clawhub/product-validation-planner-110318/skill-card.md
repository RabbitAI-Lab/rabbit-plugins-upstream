## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, and implementation-ready next steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building before heavy investment. It produces repeatable validation workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, and decision-support artifacts for product planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for general product, startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests. <br>
Mitigation: Disable implicit invocation or narrow the trigger terms when stricter routing is required. <br>
