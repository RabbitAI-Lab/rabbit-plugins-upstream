# Workflow 08 — Polish and Performance Review

Read [performance](../references/performance.md), [accessibility](../references/accessibility.md), and [anti-patterns](../references/anti-patterns.md).

1. Test the full arc at normal, slow, reverse, and rapid input speeds.
2. Tune hierarchy, dwell, overlaps, continuity, and intensity curve before micro-details.
3. Measure the heaviest scenes on representative desktop and mobile profiles.
4. Remove idle work, forced layout, excess triggers, oversized media/textures, and permanently promoted layers.
5. Verify lifecycle across navigation, refresh, deep links, resize/orientation, font/media load, hidden tabs, and repeat visits.
6. Test keyboard, focus, zoom, screen-reader semantics, coarse pointer, reduced motion, and motion-disabled state.
7. Simplify effects that miss the budget while retaining their perceptual job.

Output evidence, fixes made, remaining tradeoffs, and known device limitations.
