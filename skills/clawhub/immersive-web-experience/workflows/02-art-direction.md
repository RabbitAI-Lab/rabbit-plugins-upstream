# Workflow 02 — Art Direction

Read [visual direction](../references/visual-direction.md), [motion language](../references/motion-language.md), and the Project Analysis.

1. Derive two or three distinct directions from the subject and brand—not current design clichés.
2. Express each direction as a visual premise, composition rule, palette roles, typography roles, imagery/material treatment, depth model, and motion temperament.
3. Evaluate each against content fit, distinctiveness, implementation cost, accessibility, and device constraints.
4. Select or recommend one direction; record rejected directions and why.
5. Produce a direction sheet including explicit anti-references and responsive implications.

Do not implement final motion yet. Small static frames or isolated experiments are acceptable when they answer a specific decision.
