# Workflow 05 — Transition Design

Read [transition design](../references/transition-design.md), [camera/depth](../references/camera-depth.md) when spatial, and relevant patterns.

For each edge in the Scene Map:

1. State the semantic relationship between scenes.
2. Choose a continuity property and an appropriate pattern.
3. Define outgoing anchor, transformation/occlusion, and incoming anchor.
4. Specify progress/duration, easing, overlap, interruption, reverse, resize, and re-entry behavior.
5. Define mobile and reduced-motion equivalents.
6. Remove redundant transitions and vary intensity across the arc.

Output a **Transition Matrix**. At least one transition may be signature-level; most should be quieter connective tissue.
