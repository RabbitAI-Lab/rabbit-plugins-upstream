# Workflow 03 — Experience Concept (Mandatory Gate)

Read the Project Analysis, selected Art Direction, [experience design](../references/experience-design.md), and [immersion levels](../references/immersion-levels.md).

Before production code, create and explicitly record:

1. **Premise:** one sentence describing what the visitor experiences.
2. **Intended feeling:** emotional progression, not just adjectives.
3. **Audience action:** what the visitor should understand or do.
4. **Spatial metaphor:** the world's governing model.
5. **Signature mechanic:** the repeatable interaction/motion rule.
6. **Narrative arc:** invitation, orientation, escalation, revelation, resolution.
7. **Continuity device:** what persists or transforms across scenes.
8. **Agency model:** scroll, pointer, touch, keyboard, time, or explicit navigation.
9. **Immersion level:** chosen level and why the lower level is insufficient.
10. **Technology rationale:** DOM/GSAP first; WebGL justification if applicable.
11. **Responsive and reduced-motion translation.**
12. **Success criteria:** observable qualities for the final review.

Reject concepts that are merely an effect list or that depend on generic visual tropes. No production animation may begin until this artifact is coherent. If coding was explicitly requested, continue after recording it without waiting for ceremonial approval unless a material product choice remains unresolved.
