# Particle Convergence

Organize many small units into a recognizable object, message, or system—and optionally release them again.

**Use when:** collectives, data, ingredients, networks, or formation are the core idea.

**Build:** use instancing or efficient point rendering; precompute targets where practical; interpolate a normalized convergence parameter; keep counts and overdraw within budget; place essential text/actions in DOM.

**Continuity:** the same particle population moves between states.

**Avoid:** decorative particle backgrounds, uncontrolled randomness, CPU-updated thousands of DOM nodes, and missing fallback. Reduced motion shows representative start/end compositions.
