# Typography Relay

Carry a word, phrase, baseline, or letterform from one scene into the next.

**Use when:** language itself links chapters or a term changes meaning through context.

**Build:** use Flip for shared nodes or precisely matched proxies; preserve the accessible reading order; animate crop, scale, weight, tracking, or position; settle into a stable text layout.

**Continuity:** shared wording or typographic geometry is the anchor.

**Avoid:** long paragraphs in flight, duplicated screen-reader text, and forced line breaks that fail responsively. Reduced motion repeats or repositions the phrase without travel.
