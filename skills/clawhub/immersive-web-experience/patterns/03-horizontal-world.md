# Horizontal World

Translate vertical input into travel across a genuinely lateral sequence.

**Use when:** chapters are adjacent places, a chronological strip, panorama, map, or process with a meaningful left/right relationship.

**Build:** pin a containing stage, calculate travel from actual overflow, use `x` transforms, expose progress, and provide keyboard controls or equivalent links. Recalculate after fonts/media load.

**Continuity:** maintain a horizon, baseline, track, or persistent guide.

**Avoid:** turning ordinary vertical content sideways, hiding orientation, or forcing long horizontal travel on mobile. Reduced motion may render chapters vertically.
