# Scroll Assembly

Use scroll to assemble parts into a whole or disassemble a whole into explained components.

**Use when:** construction, anatomy, ingredients, or system relationships are the content.

**Build:** define final geometry first; assign each part a meaningful origin/path; stage assembly in comprehensible groups; label only after spatial context exists; keep the result available for inspection.

**Continuity:** the resulting object persists into the next beat when possible.

**Avoid:** arbitrary flying fragments, too many simultaneous parts, and long scrub distances without new understanding. Reduced motion uses a complete diagram with sequential static explanations.
