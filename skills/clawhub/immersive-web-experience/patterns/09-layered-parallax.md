# Layered Parallax

Move depth layers at related rates to imply a 2.5D environment.

**Use when:** imagery has a believable foreground, subject, environment, and distant field.

**Build:** declare layer depth values; derive movement from them; keep the subject most stable; clamp travel; crop for all breakpoints; use transforms only. Pointer influence should be damped and optional.

**Continuity:** shared vanishing direction and occlusion reinforce the world.

**Avoid:** arbitrary speeds per element, text moving against reading, large vestibular motion, and parallax on every section. Disable or greatly reduce for reduced motion and coarse pointers.
