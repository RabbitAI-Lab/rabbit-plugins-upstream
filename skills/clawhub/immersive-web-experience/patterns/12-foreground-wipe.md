# Foreground Wipe

Move a believable foreground object across the viewport to conceal a scene change.

**Use when:** architecture, foliage, fabric, a product surface, or graphic material naturally crosses the camera.

**Build:** time the outgoing change under full occlusion; maintain trajectory and speed through the cut; reveal the incoming scene as the object exits. A DOM image/SVG layer is usually sufficient.

**Continuity:** the wiping object or motion vector bridges scenes.

**Avoid:** generic colored rectangles without visual motivation, slow blocking wipes, and foreground layers intercepting interaction after exit. Reduced motion uses a short cut or dissolve.
