# Portal Aperture

Open an aperture inside the current scene and expand it into the next world.

**Use when:** crossing a threshold is part of the narrative.

**Build:** use an overflow-hidden wrapper, CSS/SVG mask, or clip path; place the incoming scene behind it; expand from a motivated anchor; hand off background and foreground layers before removing the mask. Use a shader only when the edge/material must deform organically.

**Continuity:** the aperture's content becomes the next scene.

**Avoid:** arbitrary circles in every transition, unreadable content during expansion, or trapping focus behind clipped layers. Reduced motion uses a direct reveal or cut.
