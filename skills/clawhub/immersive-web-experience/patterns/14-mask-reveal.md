# Mask Reveal

Reveal content through a moving or transforming mask whose geometry directs attention.

**Use when:** crop logic, material edges, a brand shape, or a narrative aperture explains the reveal.

**Build:** prefer overflow containers, CSS masks, or SVG masks; animate transforms or mask parameters; keep the final content unclipped and interactive. Test browser support and provide a plain fallback.

**Continuity:** reuse the mask geometry or direction across connected beats.

**Avoid:** masking every image, complex paths that stutter, and leaving semantic text inaccessible. Reduced motion starts with the content revealed or uses a brief opacity change.
