# Kinetic Text Field

Use short display typography as an environmental layer rather than a conventional block.

**Use when:** editorial voice, scale, repetition, or language is the main visual material.

**Build:** split only the short phrase required; derive stagger from syntax or spatial origin; coordinate type with the scene focal object; keep a clean accessible text representation and restore split state on teardown.

**Continuity:** repeated words, baselines, or typographic rhythm connect beats.

**Avoid:** animating body copy, character-by-character effects by habit, and motion that prevents reading. Reduced motion shows the composed phrase immediately.
