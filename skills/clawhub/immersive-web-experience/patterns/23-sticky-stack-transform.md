# Sticky Stack Transform

Accumulate layers or states in a sticky area so the visitor sees history and change together.

**Use when:** comparison, revision, chronology, or cumulative systems benefit from visible residue.

**Build:** keep the stage sticky; add, transform, or retire layers at clear thresholds; vary scale/offset/contrast according to semantic age; cap visible layers; preserve normal DOM reading order.

**Continuity:** prior states remain as spatial context.

**Avoid:** repeated generic cards, unreadable piles, and every layer receiving the same effect. Mobile may use a sequence of snapshots; reduced motion uses static grouped states.
