# Zoom Passage

Enter a detail until it becomes the next environment, or withdraw to reveal a larger context.

**Use when:** scale change carries meaning—product detail, world-within-world, overview-to-evidence.

**Build:** select a precise focal anchor; coordinate scale, translation, crop, and surrounding contrast. For DOM, keep transforms bounded and raster assets sharp. Use Three.js only when perspective, occlusion, or geometry must remain physically coherent.

**Continuity:** the focal object becomes the next scene's frame or landmark.

**Avoid:** rapid full-screen zoom, excessive scale ratios, and zoom without an orienting landing. Replace with a cut/crossfade under reduced motion.
