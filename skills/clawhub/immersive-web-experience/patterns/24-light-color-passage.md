# Light and Color Passage

Shift palette, illumination, or atmosphere to mark a meaningful chapter change.

**Use when:** emotional temperature, time, environment, or product state changes.

**Build:** assign semantic palette roles; interpolate custom properties or rendered lights; protect text contrast throughout; synchronize the shift with an object or spatial event. WebGL is only needed for real material/light response.

**Continuity:** one light source, gradient field, or color accent migrates across scenes.

**Avoid:** generic purple/blue glow, whole-page color cycling, and low-contrast intermediate frames. Reduced motion switches at a clear boundary.
