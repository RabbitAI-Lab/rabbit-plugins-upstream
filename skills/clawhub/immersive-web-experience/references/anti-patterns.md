# Anti-patterns

Reject or redesign these defaults:

- section, fade upward, section, fade upward;
- generic SaaS hero followed by repeated feature cards;
- purple/blue glow or gradient used without brand or narrative reason;
- every element moving, every headline split, or every scene pinned;
- scroll distance unrelated to content or change;
- animation that delays access to content or action;
- parallax layers with no coherent depth model;
- horizontal scroll that hides orientation or breaks touch behavior;
- WebGL as prestige decoration when DOM techniques express the concept;
- custom cursor as the only feedback or a blocker to native interaction;
- smooth-scroll stacking, nested pins, and orphaned triggers;
- mobile as a scaled/cropped desktop choreography;
- `will-change` permanently applied to many elements;
- inaccessible duplicated text from splitting;
- autoplay sound or motion without control;
- transitions that cannot reverse, cancel, or survive resize;
- loading screens that exist only to showcase a logo animation;
- reference imitation without a content-specific premise.

When reviewing, name the experiential consequence and propose the smallest coherent replacement.
