# Typography Motion

Typography is content first and geometry second.

## Roles

Use type to introduce a scene, carry continuity, reveal hierarchy, become a mask, mark progress, or transform meaning. Preserve readable line lengths and stable final states.

## Splitting

Split by line for editorial reveals, by word for semantic emphasis, and by character only for short display phrases. Keep an accessible unsplit reading representation when the technique changes the DOM or screen-reader output. Re-split deliberately when responsive line wrapping changes.

## Choreography

Base staggering on syntax, emphasis, or spatial origin. Avoid the habitual upward fade of every heading and paragraph. Alternatives include baseline assembly, tracking compression, crop reveal, weight interpolation with variable fonts, shared-word handoff, and typographic scale passage.

## Guardrails

Do not animate long body copy, continuous reading, or focusable labels in ways that impair comprehension. Respect font loading, avoid cumulative layout shift, and ensure the final text remains selectable when practical.
