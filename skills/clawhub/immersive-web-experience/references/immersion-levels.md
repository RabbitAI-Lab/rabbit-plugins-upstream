# Immersion Levels

Choose the lowest level that fully expresses the Experience Concept.

## Level 0 — Directed static

Strong composition, typography, imagery, and discrete state transitions. No scroll choreography. Use when content clarity, speed, or constrained devices dominate.

## Level 1 — Choreographed DOM

GSAP timelines, reveals, shared objects, masks, and route/scene transitions. Normal document flow remains primary.

## Level 2 — Scroll narrative

ScrollTrigger, selective pinning, scrubbed transformations, continuity, and a formal scene map. Requires careful pacing and mobile adaptation.

## Level 3 — Spatial 2.5D

Layered perspective, parallax, DOM/canvas composition, spatial navigation, or simulated camera. Requires a declared depth model and stronger performance review.

## Level 4 — Real-time 3D world

Three.js/WebGL camera, geometry, shaders, particles, lighting, or post-processing central to the concept. Requires fallback, resource lifecycle, GPU budget, and explicit justification.

Higher is not better. Record the chosen level and why the level below cannot deliver the signature mechanic. Individual scenes may use lower levels than the overall maximum.
