# Camera and Depth

Depth may be implied with DOM layers or rendered with a true 3D camera.

## 2.5D model

Define foreground, subject, environment, and distant atmosphere. Give each layer a distinct but related response. Depth cues include scale rate, occlusion, blur used sparingly, contrast, shadow, perspective, and differential parallax.

Keep movement physically plausible enough to be readable. Excessive parallax causes discomfort and breaks object relationships.

## Camera grammar

Use camera actions intentionally:

- dolly: approach/leave a subject;
- truck/pedestal: inspect laterally/vertically;
- orbit: reveal form;
- rack focus: redirect attention;
- field-of-view change: alter perceived compression, used carefully.

Choose a subject anchor and horizon logic. Avoid moving the camera and every object independently unless the choreography has a clear hierarchy.

## Input and comfort

Clamp pointer influence, damp changes, and return to neutral. Disable or simplify camera response for coarse pointers and reduced motion. Never make essential reading depend on precise cursor control.
