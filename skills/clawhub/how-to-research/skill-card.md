## Description:

How To Research is a Chinese-language research-methodology skill distilled from 怎样调研 that helps agents plan investigations, choose research methods, conduct surveys or interviews, and draft research reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yxdwind](https://clawhub.ai/user/yxdwind)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide market research, competitive analysis, industry research, user interviews, fieldwork, questionnaire design, and research report writing with a structured methodology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real surveys, interviews, fieldwork, or document review may involve personal, sensitive, or consent-dependent information.

Mitigation: Keep data collection user-directed, obtain consent where appropriate, minimize collected data, and protect privacy throughout the research workflow.

Risk: Government-oriented examples in the source methodology may be misapplied to commercial or other non-government contexts.

Mitigation: Adapt examples and terminology to the target domain before using the guidance for decisions or reports.

Risk: The skill is based on methodology adapted from a copyrighted book.

Mitigation: Use the skill as methodological guidance, preserve attribution, and verify rights before redistributing or publishing derived material.
