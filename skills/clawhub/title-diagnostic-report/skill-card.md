## Description:

Generates a Chinese diagnostic report for academic paper titles, covering contribution, structure, clarity, claim support, keywords, and style recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic writing assistants use this skill to evaluate a proposed paper title and receive a structured Chinese report with scores, issues, revision suggestions, and alternative title options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to produce Chinese reports in a fixed academic-title diagnostic format, which may not fit users who need English or lightweight feedback.

Mitigation: State the preferred language and level of detail before requesting title feedback.

Risk: Title recommendations may be subjective and may not fully reflect the paper's evidence, discipline, or target venue.

Mitigation: Review suggested title changes against the abstract, claims, and venue expectations before adoption.
