## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to format WhatsApp-bound messages with WhatsApp-supported bold, italic, monospace, list, quote, and separator conventions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: WhatsApp-bound text may still expose raw Markdown or unsupported structures if the agent does not apply the formatting rules consistently. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated WhatsApp messages for single-asterisk bold, single-underscore italic, no Markdown headers, no tables, and mobile-readable list formatting before sending. <br>
Risk: Styling changes can alter emphasis or readability in user-facing messages. <br>
Mitigation: Preview or review important messages to confirm the intended meaning and emphasis are preserved. <br>
