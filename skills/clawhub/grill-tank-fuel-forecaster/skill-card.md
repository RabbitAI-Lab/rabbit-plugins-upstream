## Description:

Estimates propane grill fuel remaining, cooking time, event needs, and exchange-versus-refill costs from tank weight and grill usage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Grill owners and event hosts use the skill to estimate fuel remaining, plan cooking sessions with a reserve, and compare tank exchange and refill costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated fuel and operating guidance may be mistaken for certified propane or fire-safety advice.

Mitigation: Treat results as planning estimates; follow manufacturer instructions, local codes, and qualified propane or fire-safety professionals.

Risk: An incorrect tare weight or scale reading can overstate fuel available for a cook.

Mitigation: Check the tank's stamped tare and weigh the tank before cooking; keep a reserve rather than planning to empty.
