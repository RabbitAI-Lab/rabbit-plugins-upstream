Developers, engineers, analysts, and technical teams use this skill to generate clear explanations and evaluations of materials science and engineering technologies, including scientific foundations, implementation considerations, performance trade-offs, and adoption factors.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger examples may make the skill apply to more technology questions than intended.

Mitigation: Enable or invoke it for materials science and engineering explanation workflows where its structured response format is appropriate.

Risk: Technical explanations can be incomplete or outdated when no external source context is provided.

Mitigation: Review important claims against authoritative technical sources before relying on the output for design, procurement, safety, or compliance decisions.
