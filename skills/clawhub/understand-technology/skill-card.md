## Use Case:

External users, developers, and engineers use this skill to get structured explanations of materials science and engineering technologies, including scientific foundations, implementation details, ecosystem context, advantages, limitations, and adoption considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad activation language may trigger this skill for general what, how, or explain questions more often than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for a materials science or engineering technology explanation before applying the full structured response pattern.

Risk: When no external technical context is provided, explanations may rely on general knowledge and omit domain-specific constraints or current performance data.

Mitigation: Use available source documents, extracted data, or user-provided context for concrete materials, parameters, metrics, and implementation details.
