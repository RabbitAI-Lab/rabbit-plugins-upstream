## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected reference images are sent to AI Hive for generation.

Mitigation: Use only assets you are authorized to upload, and avoid confidential brand, customer, or product material unless external processing is approved.

Risk: The AI Hive API key can authorize paid or account-bound requests if exposed.

Mitigation: Treat the key like a password, prefer environment variables or a private config file, and rotate it if it may have been shared.

Risk: Generated marketing images can imply unsupported product claims, customer facts, logos, prices, certifications, or performance data.

Mitigation: Review generated assets against approved source materials and require written evidence for customer names, case data, environmental claims, and performance claims.
