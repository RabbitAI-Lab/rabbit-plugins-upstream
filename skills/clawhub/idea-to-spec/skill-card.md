## Description:

用故事与用户旅程推进产品探讨和方向收敛，按需组织多 Agent 形成 PRD、功能 Spec、技术方案与实施任务，并在已有授权时继续实现、验收与变更同步。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mc0571](https://clawhub.ai/user/mc0571)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product owners and developers use this skill to turn an idea or user story into a scoped product direction, PRD, behavior and acceptance specification, technical plan, and implementation tasks. It can continue into implementation and verification when authorized.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified assumptions or proposal details could be mistaken for confirmed product decisions.

Mitigation: Label facts, assumptions, proposals, and decisions separately, and confirm important choices with the Owner before treating them as a baseline.
