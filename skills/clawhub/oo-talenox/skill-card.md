## Description:

This skill helps agents read Talenox branches, employees, company settings, working days, and working hours through an OOMOL-connected account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, HR operators, and support agents use this skill to read Talenox branches, employees, company settings, working days, and working hours through an OOMOL-connected account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can expose employee records, branch details, working-time settings, and company settings from the connected Talenox account.

Mitigation: Use an account with appropriate Talenox permissions and review outputs before sharing them outside the intended audience.
