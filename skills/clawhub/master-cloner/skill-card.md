## Description: <br>
Master Cloner guides Chinese-language users through a five-step knowledge-engineering workflow for turning master thinkers' philosophical, strategic, spiritual, or management frameworks into executable AI Agent Skills. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chinaonlineniu](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinaonlineniu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, knowledge engineers, and skill authors use this skill to collaborate with users on selecting source texts, decomposing a thinker's framework, building a knowledge graph, implementing a Skill package, and validating the result. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Decision-advisor skills generated from philosophical or strategic traditions could be mistaken for professional guidance in high-stakes domains. <br>
Mitigation: Add explicit high-stakes-domain exclusions and state when outputs are philosophical framing rather than legal, medical, financial, safety, or other professional advice. <br>
Risk: Generated skills may misrepresent a thinker's views through decontextualized quotes, forced modernization, or hidden tension between concepts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the source hierarchy, citation requirements, boundary statements, historical-context notes, and quality guardrails before publishing or relying on generated content. <br>
Risk: Generated Skill packages can contain incorrect or misleading guidance if they are deployed without review. <br>
Mitigation: Run the documented fidelity, boundary, practicality, and anti-misleading test matrix, then review and scan the Skill before deployment. <br>
