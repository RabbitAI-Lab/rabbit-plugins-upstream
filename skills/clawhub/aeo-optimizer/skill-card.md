## Description: <br>
Optimizes articles for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) by rewriting section headings as questions, adding 50-80 word answer capsules, auditing paragraph length, and flagging trust signals for AI citation readiness. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, content marketers, editors, and SEO teams use this skill to adapt article drafts or fetched article pages for answer engines by producing a structured rewrite and an AEO audit report. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can fetch a user-provided URL, which may expose confidential drafts or internal pages to the agent's browsing context. <br>
Mitigation: For confidential drafts or internal sites, paste the article text instead of providing a URL. <br>
Risk: The generated rewrite and audit may change article structure or emphasis in ways that affect published meaning. <br>
Mitigation: Review the rewritten article, answer capsules, trust-signal inventory, and audit recommendations before publication. <br>
