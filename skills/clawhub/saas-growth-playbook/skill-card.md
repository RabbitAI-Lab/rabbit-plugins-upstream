## Description: <br>
A revenue-focused SaaS playbook for moving from $0 to $50K MRR with pricing experiments, churn reduction, activation optimization, and expansion revenue tactics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External founders, SaaS operators, and growth teams use this skill to plan stage-specific revenue growth from first paying customers through $50K MRR. It provides guidance for pricing, retention, activation funnels, expansion revenue, partner channels, and enterprise payment discipline. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Session-recording recommendations may create privacy or consent issues if applied without controls. <br>
Mitigation: Provide clear user notice and consent where required, mask sensitive fields, limit retention, restrict access, and verify obligations before use. <br>
Risk: Business growth guidance may be mistaken for an implementation-ready compliance or legal plan. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the playbook as business advice and have qualified reviewers assess contracts, privacy practices, and regulatory obligations before execution. <br>
