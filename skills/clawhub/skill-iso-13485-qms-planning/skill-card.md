## Use Case: <br>
Medical device quality, regulatory, and operations teams use this skill to plan an ISO 13485:2016 quality management system from initial scope through process design, document structure, internal audit planning, and management review preparation. It supports new QMS builds, existing-system optimization, and certification-readiness planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated QMS plans and templates may be mistaken for legal, regulatory, or certification advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and confirm ISO 13485, NMPA, FDA, EU MDR, and product-specific requirements with qualified regulatory experts before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Template content may not fit the user's company scope, product risk class, target markets, or actual operating processes. <br>
Mitigation: Tailor each plan, procedure, checklist, and record template to the organization's QMS scope, product risk profile, applicable jurisdictions, and implemented workflows. <br>
Risk: Regulatory or standards references can become outdated or differ by market. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current official ISO 13485 and jurisdiction-specific requirements before using generated documents for audits, certification, or operational controls. <br>
