## Description:

Converts a specifically named Simplified Chinese article into a Traditional Chinese counterpart and keeps the pair synchronized.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to create or update the Traditional Chinese counterpart of a named Simplified Chinese article. The Simplified article remains the source of truth, and the pair receives reciprocal links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial, and conversion can write or overwrite article files.

Mitigation: Review the named source and destination, overwrite behavior, and host file and tool permissions before running.
