## Description:

Converts a named Simplified-Chinese article into a Traditional-Chinese counterpart using OpenCC and keeps the pair cross-linked and in sync.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and developers maintaining Chinese Markdown articles use this skill to create or refresh a Traditional-Chinese twin of an explicitly named Simplified-Chinese article and check that the pair stays synchronized.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Syncing may replace an existing Traditional article and modifies the Simplified source to add a cross-link.

Mitigation: Confirm the named source and exact output path, get explicit approval before overwriting, and retain version control or a backup.
