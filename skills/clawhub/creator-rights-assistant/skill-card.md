## Description: <br>
Standardize provenance, attribution, and licensing metadata at creation time so your content travels cleanly across platforms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[otherpowers](https://clawhub.ai/user/otherpowers) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators and rights-operations teams use this skill to organize provenance, attribution, licensing scope, and disclosure metadata before publication or distribution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat organized rights metadata as legal advice or a determination of ownership, fair use, or platform outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Present the mandatory disclaimer before asset-specific assistance and direct users to verify legal conclusions with qualified counsel or authoritative records. <br>
Risk: Incorrect provenance, licensing, attribution, or usage-scope metadata could be reused across platforms and collaborators. <br>
Mitigation: Require creators to verify all provenance and licensing details against their own records before publishing or distributing assets. <br>
Risk: Asset and contributor metadata may include personal or sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Redact personal information on ingestion and keep metadata collection limited to fields needed for rights and attribution tracking. <br>
