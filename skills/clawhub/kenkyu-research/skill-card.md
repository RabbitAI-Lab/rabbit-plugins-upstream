## Description:

Helps an agent research complex questions with the kenkyu API and produce sourced answers from relevant source extracts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[george3d6](https://clawhub.ai/user/george3d6)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, developers, and other users can ask an agent to investigate questions across multiple sources and draft answers citing relevant excerpts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries may be sent to kenkyu.dev and external search targets, exposing sensitive information.

Mitigation: Avoid secrets, personal data, confidential material, and regulated information unless the service is approved for that data.

Risk: Research runs may consume account balance.

Mitigation: Check the available balance and consider the expected cost before starting a run.
