## Description:

输入课本章节、课件文本，剔除冗余内容，压缩精简背诵版提纲，划分一级二级考点，标注必背简答、论述题答题模板。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and other learners use this skill to turn uploaded or pasted course materials into compact Chinese-language exam memorization outlines, including key terms, short-answer points, essay frameworks, and open-book lookup indexes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outlines may include inferred high-frequency exam points that are useful for review but not guaranteed to match actual exam questions.

Mitigation: Review the output against instructor-provided scope and treat inferred question candidates as study aids, not predictions.

Risk: Condensing course materials can omit nuance or context needed for accurate memorization.

Mitigation: Keep the original materials available and verify important definitions, examples, and scope before relying on the condensed outline.
