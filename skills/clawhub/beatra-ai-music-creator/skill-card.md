## Description:

Turns a creative brief, lyrics, or reference audio into reviewable songs, instrumentals, and soundtracks, then helps refine the strongest take.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams making music for videos, brands, podcasts, games, or other projects use this skill to turn a brief, lyric, or reference into original music, then review and refine selected takes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The account grant includes image, video, speech, and voice-management access in addition to paid music access.

Mitigation: Review the full grant before approving, and connect only if those broader permissions are acceptable.

Risk: Installation analytics are collected without an opt-out.

Mitigation: Review the privacy implications before installing and avoid use where this collection is unacceptable.

Risk: Chosen recordings are uploaded to an external service.

Mitigation: Upload only recordings you are comfortable sharing externally.

Risk: Cleanup can disconnect the shared Beatra connection used by other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Check other installed Beatra skills before uninstalling and use the bundled cleanup flow.

Risk: Music generation uses paid access.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended billable generation before submitting it.
