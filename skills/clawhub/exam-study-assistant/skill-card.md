## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can keep local plain-text memory of usage, errors, notes, and preferences in learned_patterns.json without clear consent or retention controls.

Mitigation: Install only when this persistence is acceptable; avoid sensitive personal details, account data, schedules, weaknesses, or confidential exam materials unless persistence is removed or made opt-in with deletion controls.

Risk: The artifact includes self-improvement behavior that may write learned preferences or operational notes into the skill directory.

Mitigation: Review stored notes before sharing or publishing the skill directory and provide a clear way to inspect, disable, or delete the memory file.
