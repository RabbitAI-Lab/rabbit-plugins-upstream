## Description:

Helps bereaved families and executors plan estate-administration notifications, estimate death certificate needs, screen survivor benefits, track deadlines, and draft institutional letters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Bereaved families, executors, and personal representatives use this skill to organize early estate-settlement tasks, identify potential survivor benefits, and prepare account closure, claim, or transfer letters. It offers general administrative guidance, not personalized legal or tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States and United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive personal or financial identifiers could be exposed in prompts or shell history when drafting letters.

Mitigation: Use placeholders for SSNs, account numbers, birth dates, and similar details; add them only to final documents in a controlled editor.

Risk: General benefit, probate, and tax guidance may not match current rules or a specific estate.

Mitigation: Confirm eligibility, deadlines, and local requirements with the relevant agencies and qualified estate or tax professionals before acting.
