# Notification Cascade, Documents, and Probate Bypass

## Why order matters

Three failure modes punish a wrong order:

1. **Clawback.** Social Security pays on the 3rd/wednesday cycle; the
   payment for the month of death is NOT owed and will be reclaimed. If
   SSA deposits into an account you already closed, the estate gets a
   repayment demand instead.
2. **Deceased identity theft.** A credit file without the deceased flag
   is a premium target (no one checks). Over a million deceased identities
   are misused yearly. Flag all three bureaus early.
3. **Expiring elections.** Employer group life claims, pension survivor
   options, conversion privileges, and (UK) Bereavement Support Payment
   all have windows measured in weeks to months.

## The cascade (UK)

- **Tell Us Once** — one call (via the registrar) notifies DWP, HMRC, DVLA,
  Passport Office, and local council. Do it in the first week.
- **Bereavement Support Payment** — claim within **3 months** of death for
  full backdating (eligible window up to 21 months, but money is lost
  month by month).
- Probate via HMCTS if banks require the grant (thresholds vary by bank,
  typically £5k–£50k held).
- Inheritance tax nil-rate band £325k (+ residence band when passing a
  home to descendants); IHT forms due by 6 months after month of death
  even if no tax.

## Document math

Every institution that holds or transfers money wants to see an
**original certified copy** — they may keep it. Count:

- Each bank/credit card (per deceased-name account): 1
- Each insurer: 1
- Employer HR / pension administrator: 1
- DMV × vehicles, property recorder, probate court: 1 each
- Spare for surprises: +3

Costs: roughly $15–30 for the first copy, $5–10 additional at the same
order; reorders later cost more in fees and trips. Order high.

## Probate bypass — the checklist that saves months

Assets that pass WITHOUT probate:

- Life insurance with named beneficiary
- Retirement accounts (401k/IRA) with named beneficiary
- Bank/brokerage with POD/TOD designation
- Real estate held joint tenancy / tenancy by entirety / transfer-on-death deed
- Joint accounts and jointly titled vehicles (varies by state)
- Trust assets (revocable living trust — successor trustee acts with the
  cert + trust certificate, no court)

What's left (solo-titled property without designations) counts against
the state's **small-estate affidavit** threshold — from a few thousand
dollars up to six figures depending on state. Many ordinary estates
discover the probate-able remainder is one car and a checking account.

## Letters

Institutions act on letters that state: (1) deceased's full name, DOB,
SSN/NINO (last-4 fine in writing), date of death; (2) that a certified
copy is enclosed; (3) your authority (executor / surviving joint owner /
named beneficiary); (4) the specific ask — close and remit balance to
estate, transfer to surviving owner, or pay beneficiary proceeds;
(5) where to send confirmation. The script generates all three variants.
