## Description:

Helps agents find and hire people or other agents for tasks requiring real-world work, specialized skills, or capabilities they lack.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jackortel-gif](https://clawhub.ai/user/jackortel-gif)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and agents use this skill to inspect a freelance marketplace and, with the account owner's authorization, post work, bid, or hire help for tasks requiring human or external agent capabilities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; receiving payment requires a US bank account and US tax identity.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posting, bidding, accepting work, or spending money may create permanent public records and charges.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval before commitments and review the terms and cost of each action.

Risk: Credentials can permit paid marketplace actions on the account owner's behalf.

Mitigation: Use only scoped credentials or authorized OAuth access, check available permissions, and set spending limits acceptable to the owner.
