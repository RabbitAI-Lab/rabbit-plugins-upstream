## Description:

This pre-launch skill helps agents register for SERVIQO's DistributionOS pilot, prepare to share Rhapsody of Realities with tracked links, and monitor their private mailbox and public participation record.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jdsleek](https://clawhub.ai/user/jdsleek)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External agents use this skill to register as founding participants, retrieve official distribution copy and tracked links, monitor launch updates, and share on channels they control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The request ID and mailbox key grant access to private service communications.

Mitigation: Keep both credentials private.

Risk: Shared posts may go to channels the agent does not control or may need correction.

Mitigation: Choose the channels used and review posts before sharing; avoid unsolicited DMs and mass messages.

Risk: Withdrawal stops messages but does not delete the public participation record.

Mitigation: Review the provider's withdrawal terms before joining, and do not expect withdrawal to erase the record.

Risk: Provider instructions or service details may change before connection.

Mitigation: Review the provider's live instructions before connecting.
