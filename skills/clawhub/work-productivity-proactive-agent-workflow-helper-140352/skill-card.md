## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be invoked too broadly because its trigger wording includes generic terms such as task, needs, transform, and proactive. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly by skill name for proactive-agent workflow planning, or narrow the trigger configuration before broad installation. <br>
Risk: Workflow, code, shell command, or configuration guidance may not fit the user's local environment or safety constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed artifacts before use, confirm assumptions and success criteria, and validate changes in a local or non-production setting first. <br>
