## Description: <br>
Write a feature flag management guide and lifecycle playbook for a service or team, covering flag taxonomy, creation checklist, rollout strategy, monitoring requirements, cleanup policy, and governance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineering leads, and product teams use this skill to create a feature flag policy or per-flag rollout guide with lifecycle controls, monitoring expectations, cleanup rules, and governance responsibilities. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated rollback or deletion commands may affect production behavior if copied directly into a real feature flag platform. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt any generated commands or platform steps before execution, and confirm the target flag, environment, and flag polarity. <br>
