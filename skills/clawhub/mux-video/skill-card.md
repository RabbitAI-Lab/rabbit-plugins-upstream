## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Video architecture guidance can be misapplied to production playback security, telemetry, or cost controls. <br>
Mitigation: Review signed playback, entitlement boundaries, Mux Data telemetry, and cost-control decisions before applying the guidance. <br>
Risk: Live and premium video workflows may fail or expose content if encoder settings, playback IDs, or access policies are configured incorrectly. <br>
Mitigation: Validate encoder profiles, playback ID policy, observability, and access controls in staging before production rollout. <br>
