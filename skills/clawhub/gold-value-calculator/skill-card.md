## Description:

Work out what gold is worth from today's 24K price: karat prices (24/22/21/18/14K) per gram, tola and troy ounce, the price of a jewellery piece with making charge and 5% UAE VAT, unit conversions (gram, kg, ounce, tola, mithqal) and zakat on gold against the 85 g nisab. Answers in Arabic, English or both. Use when someone asks how much their gold is worth, what 21K costs if 24K is X, what a ring or chain should cost with making and VAT, how many grams are in a tola or ounce, or whether zakat is due on their gold (كم سعر جرام الذهب عيار 21، حساب زكاة الذهب).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Gold-value estimates can be inaccurate when the supplied 24K price is stale or does not match the relevant market.

Mitigation: Ask for a current 24K price or direct the user to the live-rate reference, and present calculations as estimates.

Risk: Applicable VAT treatment and zakat rulings can vary by item, jurisdiction, or religious interpretation.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable tax treatment and consult a qualified scholar about zakat before relying on the result.
