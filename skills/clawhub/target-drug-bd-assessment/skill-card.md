## Description:

整合靶点证据、药物管线、临床、专利、文献和交易信息，生成可追溯的肿瘤靶点或靶向药物 BD 评估材料。

## Use Case:

External teams, business development reviewers, and R&D decision makers use this skill to assess oncology targets or targeted drugs for project initiation, partnership screening, and BD due diligence. It separates observed evidence from strategic interpretation and records source coverage, gaps, and confidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research-heavy BD reports can contain incomplete or misleading business, clinical, patent, or strategic conclusions when source coverage is limited.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, coverage summaries, evidence gaps, and confidence notes before relying on recommendations.

Risk: Patent, FTO, valuation, and medical claims may be mistaken for formal professional advice.

Mitigation: Use the report as decision support only; route formal FTO, legal, valuation, medical, and investment decisions to qualified reviewers.

Risk: Web-supplemented clinical or performance values may be unverified.

Mitigation: Keep web-supplemented values explicitly labeled as pending verification and confirm them against primary sources before use.
