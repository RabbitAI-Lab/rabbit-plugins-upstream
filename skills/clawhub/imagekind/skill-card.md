## Description:

Find artist-credited art prints and canvas made to order by Image Kind's studio through its catalog search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and their agents use this skill to search Image Kind's art-print catalog by title, artist, or subject and review artwork pages, artist credits, sizes, and finishes before contacting the studio about a purchase.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Catalog searches require connecting to an external MCP service.

Mitigation: Confirm you are comfortable connecting to Image Kind's server before enabling it.

Risk: Catalog listings do not confirm final prices or shipping.

Mitigation: Confirm prices and shipping with the studio before purchasing.
