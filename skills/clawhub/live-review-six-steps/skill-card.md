## Description: <br>
Guides Chinese-language Douyin livestream operators through a six-step post-live review covering seven-day retention signals, scene and product fit, fan engagement, paid traffic, compliance wording, and fee tiers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External livestream operators, ecommerce teams, and creators use this skill to review Douyin live sessions across data, cadence, product, audience, short video, and traffic structure, then identify priority improvements. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The checklist may not reflect current Douyin platform rules or enforcement behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as an operations checklist and verify platform requirements before applying recommendations. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger words may activate the skill for general data-analysis requests. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user is asking about Douyin livestream review before applying the six-step framework. <br>
Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language output and Douyin-specific workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Tell the agent explicitly when another language, platform, or reporting style is required. <br>
