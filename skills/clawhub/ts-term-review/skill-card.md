## Description:

Reviews Chinese-language investment term sheets clause by clause, flags negotiation risks, and suggests alternative terms and talking points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and advisers use this skill to review investment terms, distinguish company obligations from personal liability, and prepare prioritized negotiation requests. It is not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; legal examples focus on China and require local review

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal or financial decisions may rely on outdated or jurisdiction-specific term-sheet guidance.

Mitigation: Ask a qualified lawyer to check the agreement against current local law before signing, especially time-sensitive judicial and regulatory citations.
