## Description: <br>
Writes firm, professional complaint letters and short email versions that state the facts, impact, requested resolution, deadline, and next steps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill to draft ready-to-send complaint letters and short email versions for product, service, refund, replacement, or escalation issues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The generated letter may contain inferred or placeholder personal details, dates, reference numbers, amounts, or requested remedies. <br>
Mitigation: Review the letter before sending and replace placeholders with accurate facts and documents. <br>
Risk: Escalation language may be inappropriate if it does not match the user's real next step or available complaint channel. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the escalation path and keep the wording factual before sending. <br>
