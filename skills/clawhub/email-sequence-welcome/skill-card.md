## Description:

Plans a welcome email series with send timing, one goal per email, and the CAN-SPAM elements every message in the series has to carry. Use when the user asks for welcome email sequence, welcome email drip campaign plan, onboarding email sequence timing. For US marketing teams that onboard new subscribers and new customers by email.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
