## Description:

Read a brand's AI search visibility from WebGlazer, how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI Mode mention it, which competitors they name, which sources they cite, and what to fix. Use for any question about a brand's visibility in AI answers when the user has a WebGlazer account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Brand and marketing teams use this skill to review how supported AI engines mention a connected brand, compare competitors and cited sources, track visibility, and identify follow-up opportunities.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Changing an opportunity status updates stored WebGlazer data.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended status with the user before marking an opportunity open, to-do, done, or dismissed.
