## Description:

Read a paid-ads account like an analyst. Turn 14 days of campaign results into what to scale, what to pause, what to fix and what to test next, with a data floor that refuses a verdict on too little delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hermoso-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/hermoso-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Campaign analysts and paid-media account owners use the skill to review 14 days of campaign performance, identify what to scale, pause, fix, or test, and prepare evidence-based recommendations. It requires the account owner to explicitly approve proposals that move money before they are applied.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign reports can contain sensitive business or account information.

Mitigation: Provide only the campaign data needed for analysis.

Risk: Recommendations to change budgets, pause campaigns, or switch campaigns back on could move money.

Mitigation: Present money-moving changes as proposals and require the account owner’s explicit approval before applying them.
