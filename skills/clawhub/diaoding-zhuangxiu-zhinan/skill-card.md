## Use Case:

Homeowners, interior-design practitioners, and renovation advisors use this skill to answer ceiling and related home-renovation questions with practical, budget-aware guidance. It is best suited for planning, comparing options, and identifying issues to discuss with qualified local professionals before physical work begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may answer broader renovation questions beyond ceiling decoration.

Mitigation: Treat broad renovation responses as planning notes and confirm scope, materials, and code requirements with qualified local professionals.

Risk: Some guidance may involve structural, balcony, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, or exterior-facade changes.

Mitigation: Require licensed professionals, property-management approval, and local code or permit checks before acting on physical-modification advice.

Risk: The source corpus includes named budget examples and practical renovation anecdotes.

Mitigation: Review examples before reuse and avoid presenting budget anecdotes as universal pricing or guaranteed outcomes.
