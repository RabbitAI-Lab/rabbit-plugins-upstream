## Description: <br>
Communication Skill Free helps users draft and refine structured replies for everyday text communication using a GATHER-CRAFT-REFINE workflow. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and teams use this skill to turn customer service, workplace, and reporting situations into clear replies with an acknowledge, bridge, and guide structure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests write and command-execution capabilities beyond its communication-drafting purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Install with only the permissions needed for text drafting where possible, and require explicit user approval before any write or exec action. <br>
Risk: User-provided conversation context may contain personal, customer, or business-sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive data and review generated replies before sharing them externally. <br>
