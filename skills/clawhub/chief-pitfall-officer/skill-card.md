## Description: <br>
Chief Pitfall Officer helps buyers clarify project needs, identify vendor pitfalls, audit costs and acceptance criteria, and produce practical implementation plans from the buyer's perspective. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External business owners, non-technical managers, government-enterprise project leads, and other buyer-side stakeholders use this skill to turn vague project needs into auditable risk reviews, vendor-selection criteria, cost and legal checks, acceptance standards, contingency plans, and execution blueprints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks the agent to persist user-derived business context in local learning files. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in controlled installations, avoid entering confidential or identifying business details, and review any files written under learnings/. <br>
Risk: Stored vNext seed instructions may influence future runs. <br>
Mitigation: Disable or ignore automatic vNext seed loading when prior conversations should not affect future outputs. <br>
