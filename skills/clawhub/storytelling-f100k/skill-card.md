## Description:

Storytelling F100K is a Spanish storytelling coach that helps users find, deepen, and structure true personal or professional stories using the F100K three-act method while keeping the final writing in the user's own words.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, coaches, and professionals use this skill to turn real experiences or work convictions into structured story material for content. The agent guides discovery, depth, diagnosis, hooks, and b-roll planning without drafting new copy for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask emotionally probing questions and surface sensitive personal story material.

Mitigation: Keep coaching voluntary, stop or switch topics if the conversation feels too personal or distressing, and follow the crisis protocol when warning signs appear.

Risk: The skill may read or save local story context containing personal material.

Mitigation: Confirm which cerebro files may be read before use, and save completed stories only with the user's permission.

Risk: Users may treat emotionally focused storytelling guidance as therapy.

Mitigation: Do not diagnose or explain mental health; pause coaching and direct users to qualified support or crisis resources when needed.
