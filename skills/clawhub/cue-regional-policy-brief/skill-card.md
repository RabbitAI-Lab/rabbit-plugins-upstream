Mitigation: Configure the key locally rather than sharing it in chat, and confirm the runner's key-handling behavior before execution.

Risk: Recurring reports may run for unapproved regions or frequency and consume credits.

Mitigation: Review and pin the external runner, and enable recurring reports only for explicitly approved regions, frequency, and credit costs.
