## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers could mistake the planned Google Ads features for currently available analytics or actions.

Mitigation: Describe the integration as prelaunch and make clear that advertising tools cannot be called today.

Risk: Waitlist signup sends the user's contact details to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user explicitly agrees and confirms the required details.

Risk: Claims about future permission controls and token storage are not verified by this package.

Mitigation: Present those controls as provider claims rather than independently verified guarantees.
