## Description:

Plans whole-house deep cleans as ordered room-by-room tasks and time blocks, with surface-specific cleaner guidance and upkeep schedules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Households and cleaning assistants use this skill to plan seasonal, move-out, or before-guests deep cleans, check cleaner compatibility, and establish recurring upkeep.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Caustic cleaners such as oven cleaner or bleach gel can injure users or damage surfaces.

Mitigation: Read the product label, confirm appliance and surface compatibility, ventilate, and wear appropriate protection before use.

Risk: Combining cleaning products can generate harmful fumes or reactions.

Mitigation: Never mix cleaners; follow product instructions and rinse surfaces between different products.
