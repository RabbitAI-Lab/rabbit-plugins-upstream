# Cleaning Chemistry — Surfaces, Soils, and the Danger Table

## The pH mental model
- **Alkaline (pH 8-13):** breaks GREASE and PROTEIN. Degreasers, ammonia,
  bleach (oxidizer, ~pH 11-13), oven cleaner (NaOH, pH 13+).
- **Acid (pH 1-6):** dissolves MINERALS. Vinegar (pH 2.5), citric acid,
  limescale removers, toilet cleaners.
- **Neutral (pH 6-8):** safe for FINISHED and STONE surfaces. Dish soap
  solutions, stone soaps, hardwood cleaners.

Greasy cabinet + acid = nothing. Scaled faucet + alkaline = nothing.
Match the soil, respect the surface.

## Surface × soil matrix (tool knowledge, expanded)
| Surface | Soil | Use | Dwell | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tile-grout | mold | chlorine bleach gel | 15m | ventilate; cling gel beats liquid |
| tile-grout | soap-scum | alkaline degreaser or 1:1 vinegar+dish | 10m | vinegar only on ceramic, never stone tile |
| glass | hard-water | vinegar 1:1 or 10% citric | 5m | keep off stone frames/sills |
| hardwood | general | pH-neutral hardwood cleaner | — | damp not wet; no vinegar long-term, no steam |
| laminate | general | barely-damp microfiber + mild detergent | — | water in seams swells the board |
| stone-natural | general | pH-neutral stone soap | — | NO acid — etches marble/limestone; reseal yearly |
| stainless | fingerprints | dish soap + water; dry with grain | — | no bleach, no steel wool |
| oven | carbonized grease | sodium-hydroxide oven cleaner | overnight | caustic: gloves+goggles+ventilation |
| carpet | stains | water extraction first; enzyme for organic | blot, never rub | rubbing frays fibers and sets stain |
| upholstery | general | foam upholstery cleaner | patch-test | sunfade + colorfastness vary |
| mattress | odor/stains | baking soda 1h then vacuum; enzyme | 1h | sun the mattress if possible |
| electronics | dust | dry microfiber; 70% IPA if needed | — | device off, no drips into vents |

## The danger table — NEVER mix
| Combination | Product | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| bleach + vinegar (any acid) | chlorine gas | coughing, chest tightness, eye burns; ER visits at kitchen concentrations |
| bleach + ammonia | chloramine gas | toxic in enclosed bathrooms; common trap: glass cleaners contain ammonia |
| bleach + rubbing alcohol | chloroform-class compounds | CNS effects; forms on mixing |
| hydrogen peroxide + vinegar | peracetic acid | corrosive to skin and lungs even at low concentration |
| two different drain cleaners | exothermic eruption | lye + acid drain cleaners react violently; full flush between products, label timing |

Real-world traps that create these mixes:
1. Toilet bowl cleaner (acid) + a bleach wipe on the seat = chlorine.
2. Glass cleaner (ammonia) + bleach on the same mirror = chloramine.
3. "Natural" vinegar routine + a bleach mold treatment in the same shower.
4. Caustic drain cleaner followed immediately by a second brand.

**One cleaner at a time. Rinse between. Ventilate. Never combine bottles.**

## If a mix happens (first response)
1. Leave the room immediately — evacuate others and pets.
2. Ventilate from outside (open doors/windows without lingering).
3. Fresh air; symptoms: coughing, burning eyes/throat → urgent care call.
4. Persistent breathing difficulty → ER. Take the product labels/photos.

US Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222 (save it locally before you need it).

## DIY alternatives (when store products run out)
| Job | Recipe | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| all-purpose | 1 tsp dish soap + 2 cups water | mild, safe nearly everywhere |
| glass | 1:1 vinegar+water | not on stone/near stone sills |
| degreaser boost | dish soap + warm water + 15m dwell | finish surfaces only |
| grout whitening | baking soda paste + peroxide (NOT vinegar) | peroxide+vinegar must never meet |
| drain freshener | boiling-water-free: hot tap + dish soap flush | never boiling water into PVC |
| mattress refresh | baking soda 1h → vacuum | enzyme for stains, not soda |

## Product label literacy
- "Disinfectant" claims require the CONTACT TIME on the label (often 10
  min wet) — a 30-second wipe sanitizes nothing.
- "Multi-surface" usually excludes stone and unfinished wood; check the
  back panel, not the front.
- Fragrance-free variants clean identically; scent is not efficacy.
- Sponges are the dirtiest item in the house — rotate daily, replace
  weekly, or switch to microfiber + machine-washable pads.
