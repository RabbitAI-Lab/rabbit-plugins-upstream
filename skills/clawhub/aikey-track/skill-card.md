## Description:

Tracks dated commitments, expiry dates, watch items, in-progress work, and queues across sessions, then surfaces what is due.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People using the AI KEY skill set can record commitments, expiry dates, monitoring items, work in progress, and queued work that needs a dated follow-up. They use it to review what is due and resume a prior thread without storing unnecessary sensitive details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-lived tracking notes could retain sensitive personal or business details.

Mitigation: Use aliases for sensitive subjects and keep private details out of persistent notes, as the skill directs.

Risk: A due-condition check could be mistaken for authorization to publish or make a business change.

Mitigation: Treat the check as a reminder to report whether the condition is met; require a separate user decision for consequential actions.
