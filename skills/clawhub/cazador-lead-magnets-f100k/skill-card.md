## Description:

Designs highly tangible lead magnets from real audience evidence, validating latent pain points with short reel hooks and packaging the winning idea as a production-ready brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External marketers, creators, and business operators use this skill to turn audience comments, DMs, and competitor research into validated lead-magnet concepts, reel-validation scripts, scoring decisions, and production briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can involve raw private DMs and social comments, which may expose personal or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Redact names, handles, contact details, health or financial information, and unrelated personal content before providing data; use anonymized summaries where possible.
