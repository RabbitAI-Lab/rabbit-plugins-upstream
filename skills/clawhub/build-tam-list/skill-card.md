## Description:

Build a total addressable market list of companies filtered by industry, headcount, and geography, powered by Cargo.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cargo-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/cargo-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Revenue operations and sales teams use this skill to estimate their addressable market and build target account lists from industry, company size, and location filters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company searches incur usage charges that increase with the number of returned records.

Mitigation: Check current prices and approve the intended search volume; begin with a small sample.

Risk: The installed CLI and optional broader pack have executable contents outside this review.

Mitigation: Review the specific package versions before installing them.
