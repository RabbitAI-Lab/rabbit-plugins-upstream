# 片段类型：写作规范（预注册排除标准与数据质量检查）

## 原文片段

> Participants. We recruited 1,500 U.S. based adults from Prolific to complete an online survey in exchange for 70 cents. Following our preregistered exclusions, we removed duplicate (based on IP address, location coordinates, and platform-based unique participant identifiers) and incomplete responses, as well as those who failed an antibot check, for a final sample of N = 1,066 (MAge = 31.89, SDAge = 11.98; 53% female, 45% male, 2% nonbinary or unknown; 13% Asian, 8% Black, <1% Native American, 8% Latino, 68% White, 2% Other race).

## 来源文献

Phillips, L. T., & Jun, S. (2021), Study 2 Method (Participants). *Why Benefiting From Discrimination Is Less Recognized as Discrimination.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000298

## 适配诊断点

- 排除标准全部标注为预注册（"Following our preregistered exclusions"），排除决策与分析计划绑定，防事后挑选。
- 排除类型逐条列举：重复作答（三种去重依据：IP、定位坐标、平台唯一标识）、未完成问卷、未通过反机器人检查（antibot check），数据质量把关完整。
- 招募信息齐备：平台（Prolific）、地区（U.S. based adults）、人数（1,500）、报酬（70 cents）、最终 N 与完整人口学。
- "for a final sample of N = ..." 收尾句式统一，样本流清晰可审计。
