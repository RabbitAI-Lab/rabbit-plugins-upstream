# 片段类型：写作规范（预注册与探索性分析处理的透明度）

## 原文片段

> Within Studies 3–7, we tested several exploratory mediators of the relation between target behavior and ostracism decision. Particularly, Studies 3, 4, and 5 tested the mediating role of task approach goals and relational goals, whereas Studies 6 and 7 tested the mediating role of perceptions of burdensomeness, threat, and target warmth and competence. In short, target behavior did not affect participants' goals, but target behavior did affect participants' perceptions of the target and those perceptions were related to ostracism behavior. However, neither of the mediation analyses would have provided strong causal evidence, as the exploratory mediators were always measured after the dependent variable. For this reason and for reasons of conciseness, we refrain from reporting the exploratory mediation analyses and the respective measures in detail within the present article. We do, however, offer a full analysis of all mediators within a supplemental material on the OSF at https://osf.io/f6ub7/.

## 来源文献

Rudert, S. C., Möring, J. N. R., Kenntemich, C., & Büttner, C. M. (2023), Study 1 Method (footnote 2). *When and Why We Ostracize Others: Motivated Social Exclusion in Group Contexts.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

- 探索性分析的诚实披露：明确标注 mediators 为 exploratory，并主动说明其因果证据力局限（"measured after the dependent variable"）。
- 报告决策透明：说明为何不在正文详报（因果证据弱 + 简洁性），但完整分析在 OSF 补充材料可获得，正文-补充材料的职责划分明确。
- 探索性发现的简述（目标未受影响、知觉受影响且与行为相关）用 In short 概括，保留信息又不喧宾夺主。
- 预注册文化下的写作规范：探索性 vs. 验证性分析的边界清晰，不混淆证据等级。
