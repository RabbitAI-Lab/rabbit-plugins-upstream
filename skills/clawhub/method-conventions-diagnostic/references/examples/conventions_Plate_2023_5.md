## 原文片段

Analytic strategy and tools are available in the online supplemental materials. Deidentified datasets and analysis scripts are available on the Open Science Framework (https://osf.io/8u627/). The experimental task will be shared upon request. We report how we determined our sample size, all data exclusions, and all manipulations and measures in the study. Design and analyses were not preregistered.
