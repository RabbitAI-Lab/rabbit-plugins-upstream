The study complied with the ethical guidelines of the Helsinki Declaration on Human Research and was approved by the University Ethics Committee (CIPI/2024(611)). ... To control Type I error inflation from multiple testing, we applied the Holm-Bonferroni sequential correction method, adjusting significance thresholds for each comparison based on its rank order (αadj = 0.05/[k - i + 1], where i = rank of the p-value). Effect sizes were calculated using Cohen's d [46], with the following classification: negligible effect (≥ -0.15 and < 0.15), small effect (≥ 0.15 and < 0.40), medium effect (≥ 0.40 and < 0.75), large effect (≥ 0.75 and < 1.10), very large effect (≥ 1.10 and < 1.45), and huge effect (≥ 1.45).
