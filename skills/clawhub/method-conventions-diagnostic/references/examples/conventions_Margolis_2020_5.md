# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

Participants were undergraduates at a medium-sized public university who were given research credit for their participation. No incentives were offered for completing all assessments. This study was approved by the university's Institutional Review Board and all participants consented to the study online.

## 来源文献

Margolis et al., 2020, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General
