# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

Please note that in the preregistration, the same-object condition was labeled as the alignable condition, while the different-object condition was labeled as the nonalignable condition. During the review process, we decided to change these labels as the term alignability includes some unintended surplus meaning.

## 来源文献

Alves et al., 2022, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

预注册标签与最终标签差异的脚注式说明，并解释改名理由，是预注册透明性与术语修订披露的规范做法。
