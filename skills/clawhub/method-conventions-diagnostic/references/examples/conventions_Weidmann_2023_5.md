# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

The NPI is a 40-item, forced-choice, and self-report measure of grandiose narcissism (Raskin & Terry, 1988). Participants chose between one of the two options for 40 pairs (sample item: "The thought of ruling the world frightens the hell out of me v. If I ruled the world, it would be a better place"). We also computed three NPI subscales (Leadership/Authority [LA], Grandiose Exhibitionism [GE], and Entitlement/Exploitativeness [EE]) identified by Ackerman et al. (2011).

## 来源文献

Weidmann et al., 2023, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

量表全称与缩写（NPI）、条目数、作答形式、示例条目、子量表缩写首次定义（LA/GE/EE）并附来源引用，是缩写与工具描述的规范写法。
