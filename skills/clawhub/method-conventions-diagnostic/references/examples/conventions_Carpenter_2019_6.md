# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

Before participating in each session, all participants provided informed consent as approved by the University of California, Los Angeles Institutional Review Board (IRB#15–001476). Participants received monetary compensation in U.S. dollars (range = $37.60–$44.60) for approximately 5 hr (M = 5.33 hr) of participation over a period of 9 to 35 days (control group: M = 15.5 days; experimental group: M = 15.4 days; independent samples t[59] = 0.10, p = .92).

## 来源文献

Carpenter et al., 2019, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General

## 适配诊断点

知情同意、IRB 编号、报酬范围与投入时长的量化报告，并附组间平衡性检验（t[59]）支撑组间可比，是伦理与补偿信息的规范写法。
