# 片段类型：规范
## 原文片段
The study's protocol was approved by the Herzog Hospital ethics committee. Informed consent was obtained from all participating parents. ... Intraclass correlations, reflecting interrater reliability, were computed on a partial sample (Age 6.5, N = 32; Age 8–9, N = 38) and ranged between .62 and .82 (Supplemental Material B). The experimenter-ratings were conducted according to the same guidelines, immediately after the observational sessions were over.
## 来源文献
Abramson, L., et al. (2024). The structure, development, and etiology of observed temperament during middle childhood. Developmental Psychology.
## 适配诊断点
伦理批准机构 + 监护人知情同意一句到位；ICC 按波次分别报告样本量（N=32/N=38）与区间（.62–.82），是儿童观察研究的伦理与信度规范表述。
