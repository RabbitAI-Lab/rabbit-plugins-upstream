# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

The present study made use of publicly available de-identified data. Therefore, the present study's analyses were considered exempt by the ethics review board of the School of Behavioral and Social Sciences of Tilburg University. We addressed our research question using data from the LISS panel. The LISS panel consists of a large representative longitudinal sample of Dutch participants (16–95 years of age at first assessment). We used data from 2008 to 2019.

## 来源文献

Olaru, G., et al. (2023). The link between personality, global, and domain-specific satisfaction across the adult lifespan. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

二手公开数据研究的伦理豁免理由（publicly available de-identified data → exempt）一句话讲清，数据源代表性（representative longitudinal sample）与使用年限窗（2008–2019）一并交代，是数据再利用研究的伦理规范表述。
