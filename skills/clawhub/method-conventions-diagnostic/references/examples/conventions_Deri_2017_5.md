# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

Three hundred one participants from Amazon's Mechanical Turk (137 women, Mage = 37.44; 77% White, 9% Asian, and 7% Black) participated in a preregistered survey (https://aspredicted.org; #3311) in exchange for modest payment. This sample size allows us to detect effects of size d = 0.16 with 80% power.

## 来源文献

Deri et al., 2017, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

预注册编号（AsPredicted #3311）随样本描述一并报告，并与可检测效应量声明并列，是预注册引用与功效信息的规范组合。
