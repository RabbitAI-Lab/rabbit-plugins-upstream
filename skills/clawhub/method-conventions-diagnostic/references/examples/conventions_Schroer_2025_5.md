# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

The study was approved by the university's Institutional Review Board and all parents and other adults present during the recordings provided informed consent for themselves and their children to participate. The goals of the presented analyses were to (1) describe dyadic behaviors at home, (2) measure toddler visual attention and manual activity to determine the quality of naming instances naturally occurring at home, and (3) test whether dyadic coordination before naming events increased toddler attention during naming. All reported models are linear mixed-effects regressions that included a random effect of subject (calculated in R Studio using the lmerTest package; R Core Team, 2021; Kuznetsova, Brockhoff, & Christensen, 2017).
