# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

In all studies, we used the term "balancing" to refer to the moderation strategy in materials shown to participants. However, for consistency with our theoretical framework, we refer to this strategy as "moderation" throughout the current document. Exact materials that participants responded to can be found at https://osf.io/w38t7.

## 来源文献

Le, D. T. L., Scholer, A. A., & Fujita, K. (2024). The role of conflict representation in abstinence versus moderation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

术语不一致的透明说明（材料用 balancing、正文用 moderation）+ 材料全量公开链接（Exact materials）并列出现，是术语约定与材料可用性的规范表述，避免读者混淆。
