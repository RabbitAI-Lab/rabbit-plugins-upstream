# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

As preregistered, we excluded participants if they failed attention checks (n = 2), indicated not having participated in a serious manner (<6 on a 9-point scale, n = 2), or withdrew consent to analyze their data (n = 1). ... As preregistered, only those who completed the entire 14-day sequence, missing less than two evening questionnaires per week (this was also the criterion for being paid in the respective week), were retained in the sample.

## 来源文献

Büttner, C. M., et al. (2024). Ostracism in everyday life. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

"As preregistered, we excluded ..." 句式反复锚定预注册承诺，每条排除规则附具体标准与人数（n），并将保留标准与报酬标准挂钩透明化，是预注册排除规则的规范表述。
