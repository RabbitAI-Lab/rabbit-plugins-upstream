# 片段类型：写作规范（单一严肃性筛查题与排除流程）

## 原文片段

> A total of 302 U.S. citizens were recruited from MTurk to complete Experiment 1a in exchange for $3.00. Of these, n = 7 (2.32%) were excluded for not responding "yes" to the question, "Did you take this study seriously?" The remaining participants were mostly male (170 male, 125 female), mostly White (235 White, 23 Black, 14 Asian, 13 Latinx, 4 American Indian, 4 multiracial, 2 nonrespondent), and had ages spanning from 19 to 72 (M = 36.47, SD = 10.17).
>
> A total of 298 U.S. citizens were recruited to participate in Experiment 1b. Of these participants, the majority (n = 168) were undergraduates at private midwestern university who participated in exchange for course credit; the remaining participants (n = 130) were recruited from the MTurk website in exchange for $3.00. Of these participants, n = 14 (4.70%) were excluded for not responding "yes" to the question, "Did you take this study seriously?"

## 来源文献

Petsko, C. D., Rosette, A. S., & Bodenhausen, G. V. (2022), Experiments 1a and 1b Method (Participants). *Through the Looking Glass: A Lens-Based Account of Intersectional Stereotyping.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 123(4), 763-787.

## 适配诊断点

- 排除标准的操作化透明：单一问题（"Did you take this study seriously?"）及其通过条件（respond "yes"）被完整引用，读者可完全复现。
- 排除报告模板化："Of these, n = X (Y%) were excluded for ...", 两实验格式完全一致，便于横向比较。
- 混合招募来源（课程学分 vs. MTurk 报酬）分别计数并说明各自条件，样本来源无歧义。
- 每个实验的排除比例（2.32% vs. 4.70%）都给出，数据质量信息透明。
