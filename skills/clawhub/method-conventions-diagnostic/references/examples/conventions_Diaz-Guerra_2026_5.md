# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

This study was not preregistered and was exempt from ethics review at Universidad Complutense de Madrid. All materials, including the codebook and analysis scripts necessary to reproduce the findings, are publicly available at https://osf.io/myxzu/overview?view_only=99a3ec62a77241c6b9c05f6a80374e7d. The data sets used can be accessed through their respective institutional repositories. In accordance with the Journal Article Reporting Standards (JARS; Appelbaum et al., 2018), all data exclusions (if any), measures, and data processing procedures are fully reported. Sample size was not determined through a priori power analysis, as only existing, publicly available data sets were employed.
