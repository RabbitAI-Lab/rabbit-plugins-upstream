# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

Participants’ daily experience of gratitude was assessed with three items ("grateful for the partner," "thankful for the partner," and "appreciative of the partner") on a 5-point scale (1 = not at all to 5 = extremely). Within-person reliability of the items (indicated by Rc; Bolger & Laurenceau, 2013) was .84. ... Daily relationship satisfaction was assessed with three items ("happy in my relationship," "content in my relationship," and "satisfied in my relationship"; Rc = .87) on a 5-point scale (1 = not at all to 5 = extremely).

## 来源文献

Park, Y., et al. (2019). Saying "thank you": Partners' expressions of gratitude. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

日记研究中条目层面信度用 Rc（within-person reliability）而非 Cronbach's α，并附引用（Bolger & Laurenceau, 2013），测量指标名称（Rc）与取值（.84/.87）的规范报告，是密集纵向数据信度的规范样板。
