# 片段类型：规范
## 原文片段
Coherence. The coherence of the narratives was evaluated with a coding scheme developed by Reese et al. (2011). This scheme allows identification of three dimensions of coherence on a scale of 0 (complete absence of the dimension) to 3 (fully coherent use of the dimension). The dimensions are: context which places the event in time and location; chronology, the degree to which the narrative is temporally organized; and theme, which assesses the clarity of topic in the narrative (ICCs range from .80–.95 across data sets).
