# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

Transparency and Openness. We report how we determined our sample size, all data exclusions, all manipulations, and all measures in the study, and we follow JARS (Kazak, 2018). Data were analyzed using Mplus, Version 7.0 (Muthén & Muthén, 2012). This study’s design and its analysis were not preregistered. All data, analysis code, and research materials are available on request.

## 来源文献

Wang, L., et al. (2022). On becoming socially anxious. Developmental Psychology.
