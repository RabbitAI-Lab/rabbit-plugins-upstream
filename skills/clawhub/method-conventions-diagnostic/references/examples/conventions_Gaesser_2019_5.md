# 片段类型：规范

## 原文片段

We conducted an a priori power analysis of the effect size (d = .25) corresponding to the central contrast of interest in Experiment 1 (i.e., the difference in willingness to help for in-group vs. out-group targets in the No Helping task) using G*Power (Faul, Erdfelder, Lang, & Buchner, 2007).

## 来源文献

Gaesser et al., 2019, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

先验功效分析的四要素齐全：效应量来源（前实验关键对比 d = .25）、检验对象界定（i.e. 括号说明）、分析工具（G*Power）与引用，是先验功效分析的标准表述。
