## Description:

Helps biology and geography teachers plan junior-high academic proficiency exam and senior-high qualification exam review using the current local exam guidance and class learning needs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking biology and geography teachers use it to align local exam requirements with curriculum and class needs, then draft a dated review schedule that includes laboratory or field-practice preparation. Teachers review plans before sharing or saving them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (local exam rules and support channels require adaptation elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A review plan may misstate local exam coverage or requirements.

Mitigation: Use the current local exam guidance supplied by the teacher; flag missing details and have the teacher review the plan before use.

Risk: Student information or nonpublic exam materials could be exposed.

Mitigation: Use aggregated class needs and pseudonyms, exclude individual scores and unreleased exam questions, and obtain teacher approval before saving.

Risk: Laboratory, fieldwork, or crisis-support advice may be unsafe outside its intended setting.

Mitigation: Keep practical activities under teacher supervision and localize safety and support channels before deployment outside Mainland China.
