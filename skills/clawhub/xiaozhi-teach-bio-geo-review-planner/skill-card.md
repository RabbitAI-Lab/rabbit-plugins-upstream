## Description:

Helps biology and geography teachers plan junior-secondary academic proficiency and senior-secondary qualification exam review using the current local exam guidance, class-level learning needs, practical activities, and time remaining.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology and geography teachers use this skill to map local exam requirements to curriculum topics and class-level gaps, then draft review schedules that include laboratory or field-practice preparation. Teachers review the plan before it is saved or shared.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum assumptions and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A plan may misstate local exam requirements or apply the wrong curriculum.

Mitigation: Require the teacher's current local exam guidance; mark missing requirements for teacher confirmation and localize before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Sensitive student information or unreleased exam materials may be included in planning inputs.

Mitigation: Use only public exam guidance and aggregated topic-level needs; exclude identifiable student data, individual scores, and unreleased exam papers.

Risk: Laboratory and field-practice suggestions may pose physical safety risks.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's laboratory safety guidance and have teachers supervise school-organized practical activities.
