## Description:

Helps biology and geography teachers plan junior-high academic proficiency exam and senior-high qualification exam review using current local exam guidance, class learning needs, and time until the exam.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology and geography teachers in mainland China use this skill to map public, current local exam instructions to curriculum topics and class-wide needs, then draft review calendars that include laboratory and geography practice. It does not plan senior-high elective exams or promise exam results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An outdated or mismatched exam scope could lead to a misleading review schedule.

Mitigation: Use the teacher's public, current local exam instructions; flag missing details for confirmation rather than guessing.

Risk: Unreleased test materials or identifiable student records could be exposed.

Mitigation: Do not provide confidential exam questions or scoring materials; use class-level learning needs and pseudonyms instead of individual names, scores, or rankings.

Risk: An unreviewed plan could be saved or unsafe laboratory activities suggested.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review and explicitly approve plans before saving, and keep laboratory practice supervised at school.
