## Description:

Helps biology and geography teachers plan junior-secondary academic proficiency exam and senior-secondary qualifying exam review around local exam guidance, class needs, practical activities, and the exam date.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biology and geography teachers in Mainland China use this skill to map local exam requirements to curriculum topics, prioritize class-wide review, and prepare supervised practical activities and a countdown study schedule. Teachers review the plan before using it with students or saving it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Exam scope or emergency guidance may be inaccurate outside Mainland China or without current local exam instructions.

Mitigation: Confirm current local exam rules and localize curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts, and policy references before use elsewhere.

Risk: Sensitive student data or unreleased exam materials could be exposed if entered into the skill or saved with a plan.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased papers, real student names, individual scores, rankings, or family details; confirm any workspace save.

Risk: Practical biology activities may introduce laboratory safety hazards.

Mitigation: Have teachers organize practice in a school laboratory and follow the bundled laboratory safety guidance.
