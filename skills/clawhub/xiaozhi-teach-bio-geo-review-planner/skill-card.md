## Description:

帮助初高中生物和地理教师依据本地当年考试说明，规划学业水平考试复习、考点对照、实验与实践准备及倒推日程。

## Use Case:

Biology and geography teachers in China use this skill to map their current local exam requirements to curriculum content, identify class-level review priorities, prepare practical activities, and draft a date-based revision schedule.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese school curriculum and local exam context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A schedule may not match local exam requirements when current local exam guidance is missing.

Mitigation: Ask the teacher for the current local exam instructions and mark unconfirmed details for teacher review.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials or identifiable student records could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use published exam guidance, class-level trends, and aliases; do not enter unreleased exam questions or student personal data.

Risk: Biology experiments and geography field activities can involve physical safety concerns.

Mitigation: Follow the supplied safety guidance and have teachers organize practical work in appropriate school settings with adult supervision.

Risk: The security analysis was partial because two references in SKILL.md did not resolve to bundled files.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its referenced materials before installation, as advised by the security evidence.
