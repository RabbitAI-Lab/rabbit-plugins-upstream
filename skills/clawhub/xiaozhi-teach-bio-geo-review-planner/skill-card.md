## Description:

帮助中国初高中生物、地理教师依据本地当年考试说明与班级掌握情况，制定学业水平考试复习排期及实验、实践准备计划。

## Use Case:

中国初高中生物、地理教师用它将公开的本地考试说明与班级考点掌握情况对照，安排学业水平考试或合格性考试的复习、实验操作和地理实践。教师审定计划后方可使用或保存。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); use elsewhere requires local exam guidance and localized crisis resources

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect local exam scope or schedule could misdirect revision.

Mitigation: Use the teacher's public current local exam instructions; flag missing details for confirmation rather than guessing.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials or identifiable student data could be disclosed.

Mitigation: Do not provide unreleased exam papers or real student identifiers; use topic-level aggregates or pseudonyms.

Risk: Unsafe biology practical preparation or unreviewed plan changes could affect students.

Mitigation: Keep practical work supervised at school under lab safety rules, and require teacher review and approval before saving a plan.
