## Description:

帮助生物与地理教师依据本地当年考试说明，将考试范围、班级掌握情况和实践准备整理成倒推复习计划。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

生物与地理教师可用此技能，把本地学业水平考试要求、班级薄弱考点及实验或地理实践准备转化为复习安排，并在分享前审阅生成内容。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student identities or individual performance data could enter a class review plan.

Mitigation: Use anonymized, class-level weaknesses; omit names, individual scores, rankings, and family details.

Risk: A schedule may not reflect current local exam requirements or may be saved before review.

Mitigation: Base it on the teacher-provided local exam notice, flag missing details for confirmation, and save only after teacher approval.
