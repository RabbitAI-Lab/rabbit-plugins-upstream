## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to generate, organize, and expand ideas for naming, options analysis, creative exploration, and decision framing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages proactive brainstorming, which may produce ideas before all user constraints are known.

Mitigation: Review or tighten the activation wording if the deployment requires agents to ask for constraints before ideating.

Risk: Brainstormed options can be incomplete, impractical, or poorly aligned with unstated requirements.

Mitigation: Have users or reviewers validate generated ideas against domain constraints before acting on them.
