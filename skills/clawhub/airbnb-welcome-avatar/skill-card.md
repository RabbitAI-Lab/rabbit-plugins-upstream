## Description:

Creates short, spoken homestay welcome and amenity clips from authorized still images and confirmed stay details, one clip per still.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Hosts and property managers use the skill to prepare check-in greetings and amenity explainers from photos they can authorize and stay facts they have confirmed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill retains broad Beatra account access, including access to credits and potentially sensitive media.

Mitigation: Use only with an agent you trust, and review the account and media access before authorization.

Risk: Automatic updates can replace package files without a separate confirmation.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates before creative work if you need stronger change control.

Risk: A generic paid-tool path can consume account credits.

Mitigation: Review the current price and production card, and explicitly approve each paid speech or video call.
