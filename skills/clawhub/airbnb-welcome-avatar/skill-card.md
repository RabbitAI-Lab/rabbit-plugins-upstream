## Description:

Turn authorized host or room stills and confirmed stay facts into one homestay welcome talking clip per still. This homestay welcome talking-clip studio writes a speakable check-in and amenity script, then animates each still into a 2–15s welcome or facility clip in stay order. Use it for homestay welcome videos, Airbnb check-in greetings, amenity explainers, and guest arrival talking clips that stay one photo, one clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting grants shared Beatra spending access, and installation analytics are automatic.

Mitigation: Review the account access and analytics implications before connecting; do not connect if the shared access is broader than you accept.

Risk: Selected photos and audio are processed remotely.

Mitigation: Submit only media you are authorized to use and are comfortable having processed remotely; confirm likeness and voice rights where applicable.

Risk: Paid speech and video generation can incur charges, and removal checks may disrupt other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Approve the complete clip count and estimated cost before generation. Use the Beatra Console to revoke device access, and review removal impact before uninstalling.
