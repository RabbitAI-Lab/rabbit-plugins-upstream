## Description: <br>
Manages training partner records, including institution registration, cooperation agreements, settlement records, partner evaluation, and contact maintenance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Training operations teams use this skill to maintain external partner profiles for universities, associations, industrial parks, hotels, course agents, and similar organizations. It helps structure contracts, settlements, evaluations, risk levels, and contact history across the partner lifecycle. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive partner financial, tax, contract, and contact records. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with authorized data and apply redaction, access control, retention, and deletion rules appropriate for the organization. <br>
