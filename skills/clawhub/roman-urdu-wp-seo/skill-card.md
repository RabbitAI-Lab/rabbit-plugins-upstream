## Description:

Optimize WordPress content for Roman Urdu/Hinglish search intent with keyword variants, blended meta tags, image alt-text guidance, and technical SEO checks for Pakistani and South Asian audiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Editors, SEO practitioners, and site operators use this skill to optimize WordPress posts and pages for audiences who search in English, Roman Urdu, or Hinglish. It helps produce keyword variants, blended metadata, slug guidance, heading and alt-text suggestions, technical SEO checks, and pre-publish QA notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO wording may contain incorrect claims, culturally sensitive phrasing, or unsupported search and ranking assumptions.

Mitigation: Review keyword variants, metadata, local modifiers, and sensitive claims before publication; use native or experienced editorial review where phrasing risk is material.

Risk: Time-sensitive product, pricing, schedule, legal, medical, financial, or safety information can become stale or require qualified review.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive and high-impact facts against current authoritative sources immediately before publication, and add caveats or remove claims that cannot be supported.

Risk: Applying suggestions directly to a live WordPress site could change published content or SEO settings.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an editorial aid by default; only grant WordPress publishing access or make site changes after explicit authorization in an authenticated workflow.
