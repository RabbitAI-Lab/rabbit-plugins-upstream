# Roman Urdu and English Code-Mixing Guide

Roman Urdu is Urdu written with Latin characters. Real usage is flexible, so optimization should favor clarity and audience language over artificial consistency. The goal is to reflect search intent without making the page sound machine-translated.

## Editorial principles

1. **Keep the core term stable.** Retain product names, tools, model numbers, technical terms, and widely recognized English phrases such as `WordPress`, `SEO`, `Google Search Console`, `laptop`, and `mobile`.
2. **Add natural connective language.** Words such as `ke liye`, `kaise`, `kahan`, `kitne ka`, `mein`, `aur`, `se`, `par`, and `ka tareeqa` can express intent around the English core.
3. **Prefer spoken phrasing.** `30 hazar ke andar best mobile` is often more natural than a literal, fully translated string that no reader would type.
4. **Use a consistent house style.** Choose spellings such as `kaise` or `kaisay`, `kamayein` or `kamain`, and `gari` or `gaari` based on the brand's existing content.
5. **Do not over-localize.** One clear mixed phrase is usually more useful than a title containing several transliterations.
6. **Avoid stereotypes.** “Desi” is an audience shorthand, not a reason to use slang, assumptions, or claims about every Pakistani or Indian reader.

## Where to mix languages

| Surface | Recommended approach | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEO title | Keep the English keyword core and add a short benefit or intent phrase in Roman Urdu. | A chain of three variants separated by pipes. |
| Meta description | Explain the benefit in clear language and add one natural mixed-language phrase. | Repeating the keyword in every clause. |
| H1/H2 headings | Use the language that best helps the reader scan the article; an occasional mixed heading is acceptable. | Changing every heading into Roman Urdu just for SEO. |
| Body copy | Preserve the article's original language unless translation is requested; explain local terms naturally. | Forcing Roman Urdu into formal, technical, or sensitive content. |
| Image alt-text | Describe the visible image or its function accurately; localize only when natural. | Hidden keyword lists or invented details not visible in the image. |
| Slug | Prefer short lowercase English keywords and stable URLs. | Long sentences, punctuation, or inconsistent transliteration. |
| Anchor text | Use descriptive, varied, reader-first phrases. | Repeating one exact-match phrase across every link. |

## Register and audience

A consumer blog may use conversational phrasing such as `kaun sa phone lena chahiye`, while a B2B or technical site may prefer `Pakistan businesses ke liye managed WordPress hosting`. Match the reader's level and the site's voice. If the site serves multiple countries, do not infer that a Pakistan-specific phrase applies everywhere.

For India-focused content, local currency, spelling, service availability, and regional vocabulary may differ. Use a location modifier only when the page genuinely covers that location. If the audience includes both Pakistan and North India, present variants as alternatives and avoid pretending that one phrase is universal.

## Safety and accuracy

- Do not use Roman Urdu to soften or hide medical, financial, legal, or safety claims.
- Explain unfamiliar abbreviations at first use.
- Preserve numbers, units, dates, prices, and model identifiers exactly unless the user requests conversion.
- Do not translate a brand name or product name into a spelling that could mislead readers.
- Ask for review by a native or experienced editor when a phrase has cultural, dialectal, or double-meaning risk.

## Examples

| Less useful | Better | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Best Mobile Under 30000 Best Phone Sasta Acha` | `Best Mobile Under 30000: 30 hazar ke andar kaunsa phone acha hai?` | One keyword core and one natural supporting phrase. |
| `Online earning online earning paisa online earning` | `Ghar baithe online paisa kamane ke practical tareeqe` | Expresses the reader's desired outcome without repetition. |
| `WordPress ko Wordpress mein tabdeel karein` | `WordPress SEO kaise karein: Google par visibility barhane ka guide` | Retains the technical term and adds clear intent. |
| `Khansi ka ilaj guaranteed` | `Khansi ke liye gharelu care: kab doctor se mashwara karein` | Avoids a guarantee and adds an appropriate safety path. |

Roman Urdu SEO is a localization layer, not a substitute for useful content. The final question should always be: **Would a real reader understand and willingly click this result?**
