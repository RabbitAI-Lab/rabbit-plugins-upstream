## Description:

Helps timeshare owners compare the costs and risks of keeping or exiting, classify their contract, assess exit options, and screen exit-company offers for warning signs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Timeshare owners and heirs use the skill to compare ongoing fees with actual usage value, understand contract type and possible exit routes, and assess exit-company offers before deciding what to investigate next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The cost comparison is simplified and may omit special assessments, financing costs, taxes, or changes in usage value.

Mitigation: Use recent statements and written exit quotes, and review the assumptions before relying on the comparison.

Risk: Security review was partial because a referenced inheritance playbook is missing; inheritance deadlines and default consequences also depend on the contract and jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Check the contract and applicable local rules, and confirm consequential estate or default decisions with a qualified local professional.
