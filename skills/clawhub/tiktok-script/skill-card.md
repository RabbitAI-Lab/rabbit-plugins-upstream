## Description: <br>
Writes TikTok-native short-video scripts optimized for a fast hook, retention, rewatches, sound fit, TikTok search, and optional faceless or b-roll Veo prompt packs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media operators, creators, and marketing teams use this skill to turn a TikTok concept into a shootable production document with shot, on-screen text, spoken script, sound, timing, SEO, caption handoff, and optional Veo prompts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated scripts may include inaccurate claims, mismatched brand voice, or content that does not satisfy the user's compliance rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated scripts against the brand profile, voice, guardrails, and evidence before filming, generating media, or publishing. <br>
Risk: Sound, music, captions, AI-generated media, uploads, and scheduling can create rights, disclosure, or platform-policy issues. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm music and sound licensing, review captions and hashtags, follow synthetic-media disclosure requirements, and inspect scheduled posts before they go live. <br>
Risk: Trend or AI-video recommendations may be unsuitable for the audience or may make the content feel overly polished or ad-like. <br>
Mitigation: Use trends and Veo prompts only when they fit the message, prefer native filming where trust matters, and keep the final creative review human-led. <br>
