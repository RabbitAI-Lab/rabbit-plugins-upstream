## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and agent operators use this skill to ask CellCog for daily briefings, competitive intelligence, market updates, trend monitoring, industry reports, and current-events research. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: News and research prompts are sent to CellCog, which can expose sensitive information if users include secrets, credentials, regulated data, or confidential internal material. <br>
Mitigation: Only send prompts approved for CellCog use; exclude secrets, credentials, regulated data, and confidential internal material unless organizational approval exists. <br>
Risk: Briefings, digests, and trend reports can be incomplete, outdated, or misleading when the timeframe, audience, or citation requirements are underspecified. <br>
Mitigation: Specify timeframe, audience, structure, and citation needs in the prompt, then review source links before relying on the briefing for decisions. <br>
