## Use Case: <br>
Users use this skill to analyze disagreement context, identify logical flaws, organize rebuttal points, and choose a calmer tone for personal, workplace, or social conversations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated replies could escalate personal or workplace conflict if used without judgment. <br>
Mitigation: Review and soften replies before sending, especially when the other person is important to the user or the situation is emotionally charged. <br>
Risk: The skill could be misused for harassment, intimidation, or hostile argument tactics. <br>
Mitigation: Use it to clarify the issue and focus on reasoning rather than attacking a person. <br>
