# Cover and publishing outputs

## Cover

Create a 1080x1920 PNG that remains readable at phone size. Use one concrete hook, one supporting line, and at most two proof points. Prefer exact-text SVG/HTML rendering for Chinese or mixed-language copy.

Keep critical text inside `x=90..990`, `y=180..1640`. Do not use source-account names, watermarks, stars, share prompts, debug labels, frame counts, scene ids, or decorative English headers unless the user explicitly requests them.

Derive the cover hook from the video's teaching promise, not the article title. Avoid clickbait that the video does not deliver.

## Social copy

Write a platform-ready description with:

1. A one-sentence problem or result hook.
2. A compact explanation of the method.
3. The audience who benefits.
4. One question that invites useful comments.

Generate 5-8 focused hashtags. Include product names only when materially discussed. Do not claim sponsorship, pricing, availability, or performance without current evidence.

The pinned comment should summarize the workflow in 2-4 short lines and ask which step viewers want next. The reply prompt should help the creator answer common setup questions without requesting sensitive information.

## Ending CTA

Use a short, natural CTA such as:

`If this was useful, leave a like and tell me what you think in the comments.`

For Chinese output, prefer a warm spoken line equivalent to `感谢观看。如果觉得讲得不错，请给个赞赞和评论吧。` Keep the screen text shorter than the spoken line. Do not add account nicknames, stars, watermarks, or unrelated branding. A voiced CTA requires authorized matching audio; otherwise render it as text only.

## Rights

Record article, screenshot, voice, music, font, and logo rights separately. A public upload requires confirmation for every category used. Never package source media with the Skill itself.
