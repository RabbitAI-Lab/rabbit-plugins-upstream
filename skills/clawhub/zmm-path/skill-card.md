## Description:

This Chinese-first skill turns a vague personal or business wish into a decidable goal, measurable factors, one confirmed bottleneck, candidate paths with costs, an if-then first step, and a prewritten kill criterion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, founders, creators, operators, and individuals use this skill when they have a large goal but do not know where to start. It guides them from a vague wish to one concrete first action, while separating AI-assisted thinking from real-world data the user must collect.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store goal-related learnings in local zmm memory.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive business or personal details unless local persistence is acceptable, and review stored memory entries for necessity and accuracy.

Risk: The workflow can produce planning guidance that depends on user-supplied baselines and real-world feedback.

Mitigation: Treat unmeasured factors as assumptions, collect the requested real-world numbers before committing resources, and revisit the kill criterion at the stated check date.
