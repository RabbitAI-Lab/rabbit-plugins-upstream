## Description:

Turns a vague wish into a measurable path: define a decidable goal, break it into movable factors, confirm one bottleneck, compare candidate routes and costs, choose an if-then first step, and set a failure criterion before starting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users bring broad personal or business goals, such as revenue growth, audience growth, a new side project, or a skill they want to learn. The skill helps them turn the wish into a measurable goal, factor model, confirmed bottleneck, practical route, first action, and review criterion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may save goal-planning records, assumptions, review dates, and later lessons into configured memory and /zmm-track areas.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive business or personal details unless they are intended to be reused in future planning sessions.

Risk: Planning advice can be misleading when goals lack baselines, real-world feedback, or user-confirmed bottlenecks.

Mitigation: Require measured baselines where possible, mark assumptions explicitly, ask the user to confirm the bottleneck, and set a failure criterion before execution.
