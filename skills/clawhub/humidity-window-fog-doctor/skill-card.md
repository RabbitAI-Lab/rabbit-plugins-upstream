## Description:

Analyzes winter window condensation conditions and suggests humidity targets, ventilation steps, or glazing-side fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners, renters, and building professionals can estimate whether indoor humidity or cold glazing is causing winter window condensation and identify practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Humidity targets are estimates, and the reference document contains conflicting numerical examples.

Mitigation: Use results as screening guidance, check measurements and calculations, and verify consequential building or health decisions with a qualified professional.
