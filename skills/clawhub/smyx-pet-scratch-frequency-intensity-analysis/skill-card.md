## Description:

Analyzes cat scratch post videos to report scratching frequency, session duration, and estimated intensity, with observational stress and claw health indicators; it does not diagnose disease or prescribe behavior changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Pet owners and cat-care monitoring services use the skill to summarize scratching patterns from video and review observational stress and claw-health indicators.
