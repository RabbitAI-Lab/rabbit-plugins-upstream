## Description:

Generates new scene images from a description or edits the model, pose, or outfit in a reference image.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and commerce teams use this skill to generate scene imagery from prompts or make targeted changes to a reference image's subject, pose, or clothing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and optional reference images are sent to the selected image provider.

Mitigation: Use only an approved provider for sensitive data; omit private customer images, confidential product plans, and sensitive brand strategy otherwise.

Risk: Generated commerce images may be mistaken for genuine product photography.

Mitigation: Review outputs before publication and do not present generated images as actual product photos or firsthand usage evidence.
