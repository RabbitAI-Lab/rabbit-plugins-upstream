## Description:

Generates images from text prompts and edits reference images to change a model, pose, or outfit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and commerce teams use the skill to generate original scene images from descriptions or make targeted edits to reference images, such as changing a model, pose, or outfit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan marks the skill suspicious: it reports image-service transmission without clear disclosure and incomplete analysis of some files, with medium overall confidence.

Mitigation: Review the configured provider's data handling before use, avoid sensitive or unreleased prompts and reference images unless that handling is acceptable, and inspect the partially analyzed content before installing.
