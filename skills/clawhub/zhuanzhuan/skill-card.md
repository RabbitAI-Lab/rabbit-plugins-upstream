## Description:

Zhuanzhuan Assistant helps users buy and sell second-hand goods, check resale market prices and trends, estimate item value from descriptions or photos, and navigate Zhuanzhuan pages for listings, orders, logistics, carts, favorites, and browsing history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuanzhuan-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuanzhuan-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill through an agent to search Zhuanzhuan second-hand listings, compare prices, request resale valuations, and find relevant account or commerce pages. It is intended for resale shopping and selling workflows involving phones, electronics, luxury goods, collectibles, and used cars.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The external Zhuanzhuan MCP service may receive product searches, page-link requests, and images supplied for valuation.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when this data sharing is acceptable for the conversation and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details or images.

Risk: Service guarantees, prices, valuations, and promotional language come from Zhuanzhuan-provided systems and may affect purchase or resale decisions.

Mitigation: Treat those claims as provider-supplied information and review item details, inspection results, and final service terms before acting.
