## Description:

Zhuanzhuan Assistant helps users buy and sell used goods by finding listings, checking recent market prices, estimating recycling value from item details or photos, identifying models, and linking to order, logistics, cart, favorites, and browsing-history pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuanzhuan-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuanzhuan-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to search Zhuanzhuan used-goods listings, compare market prices, get recycling valuations, identify item models, and reach account pages. It does not place orders, process payments, or return specific order, logistics, recycling-order, or payout details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Item photos and item details may be sent to Zhuanzhuan for recognition or valuation.

Mitigation: Ask users before using photos or detailed item descriptions and continue only with information they are willing to share with the service.

Risk: The skill presents commercial Zhuanzhuan service framing and links for used-goods buying and recycling.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations neutral, avoid implying purchases, payments, bookings, or order actions were completed, and preserve the documented limits on order, logistics, recycling-order, and payout details.
