## Description:

Provides non-animation creative direction for HyperFrames videos, including design specs, palettes, typography, narration, beat planning, audio-reactive visuals, composition patterns, and brand/style decisions; use `hyperframes-animation` for atomic motion patterns and scene blueprints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External video creators and developers use this skill to shape non-animation HyperFrames videos through design specs, palettes, typography, narration, beat planning, audio-reactive visuals, and composition guidance. It is intended for use after the HyperFrames technical contract is established.
