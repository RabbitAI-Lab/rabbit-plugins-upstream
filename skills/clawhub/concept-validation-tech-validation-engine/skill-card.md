## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language agent workflow for early-stage technology feasibility validation, TRL 1-4 assessment, multi-route comparison, light consistency checks, and technical risk reporting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, technical reviewers, and innovation teams use this skill to assess early technical feasibility, compare up to three technical routes, define validation evidence, and produce concept-stage technical validation reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Technology-readiness assessments could be mistaken for final Gate decisions or professional technical and legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as review input only and require qualified technical, governance, and legal review before making project decisions. <br>
Risk: The workflow is scoped to TRL 1-4 concept validation and may not fit later-stage process maturation or production validation. <br>
Mitigation: Apply it only to concept-stage feasibility work and route TRL 5+ process-maturation questions to an appropriate later-stage validation workflow. <br>
Risk: General technical-validation prompts may activate a Chinese-language framework and terminology. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the requested scope and response language before applying the framework to broad validation questions. <br>
