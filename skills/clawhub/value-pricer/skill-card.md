## Description:

帮助一人公司服务提供者基于客户感知价值、市场参考和交付范围制定三档报价方案与沟通话术。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cglt2026opc](https://clawhub.ai/user/cglt2026opc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and independent service providers use this skill to analyze service value, compare market pricing when research tools are available, create basic, standard, and premium offer tiers, and draft client-facing pricing scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include confidential pricing, customer budget, or case-study details and may be saved locally.

Mitigation: Review the report before sharing it externally and avoid writing confidential business details unless local storage is intended.

Risk: Generated reports include mandatory third-party attribution or branding from the skill template.

Mitigation: Remove unwanted attribution or branding before sending the proposal to clients.

Risk: Pricing recommendations can be inaccurate if market references, value estimates, or customer assumptions are weak.

Mitigation: Validate market inputs and quantified value assumptions before using the proposed tiers commercially.
