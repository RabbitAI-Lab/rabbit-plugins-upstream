RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on March 1, 2026, between Landmark
Property Group LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned tenant ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the apartment located at 44 Cedar Street,
Unit 5B, together with appliances as-is, where-is.

2. TERM. The initial term shall be twelve (12) months, commencing April 1, 2026.
This Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms
unless Tenant provides written notice not less than sixty (60) days prior to the
end of the term. Notices must be delivered by certified mail to Landlord's
office.

3. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,850 on the first day of each month.
A late fee of $75 shall apply, plus $10 per day for each day rent remains
unpaid, plus interest at 1.5% per calendar month on the overdue balance. A
returned payment fee of $50 applies.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit $1,850 as security for the performance
of Tenant's obligations. Landlord may withhold from the deposit for cleaning,
including professional cleaning at Landlord's discretion, and for any damage.
Deposit may be applied as liquidated damages and not as a penalty where
permitted.

5. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant abandons or vacates the premises before the end
of the term, all remaining rent for the balance of the term shall become
immediately due and payable, and Tenant shall remain liable for the entire
amount.

6. DEFAULT; ATTORNEY FEES. In the event of any default, Tenant shall pay all
costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees incurred by the
Landlord.

7. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises at any time to inspect the condition,
make repairs, or show the unit, and shall retain a copy of the key.

8. ALTERATIONS; ASSIGNMENT. Tenant shall not assign this Lease without
Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord may assign this Lease and this
agreement and all rents hereunder to any successor without notice to Tenant and
without Tenant's consent.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. Any dispute arising out of this Lease shall be resolved
by binding arbitration in the State of Delaware under the rules of the American
Arbitration Association. TENANT AND LANDLORD EACH WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY
JURY OF ANY CLAIM. Tenant waives the right to participate in any class action.

10. CONDITION. Tenant accepts the premises in its present condition as-is, and
acknowledges that Landlord has made no representations or warranties as to the
condition of the premises or appliances.

11. RULES. Tenant agrees to comply with all rules and regulations now or
hereafter promulgated by Landlord. Landlord reserves the right to change,
modify, amend, or update these rules at any time at its sole discretion, and
Tenant's continued occupancy shall constitute acceptance of such changes.

12. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware. Exclusive jurisdiction and venue shall lie in the courts located in
Delaware.

13. NON-DISPARAGEMENT. Tenant shall not disparage Landlord or make any negative
or harmful statements or comments concerning Landlord or the property during or
after the tenancy.

14. RENTERS INSURANCE; INDEMNIFICATION. Tenant shall indemnify and hold
Landlord harmless from any and all claims, losses, and liabilities arising from
Tenant's occupancy, and shall defend Landlord against any and all claims,
including those arising from Landlord's own negligence, to the extent permitted
by law.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease.
