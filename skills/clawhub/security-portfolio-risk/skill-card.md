This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Fund managers, risk analysts, and institutional investors use this skill to examine portfolio losses, stress scenarios, concentration, and risk contributions for China A-share holdings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Risk estimates or regulatory claims could be mistaken for verified investment or compliance advice.

Mitigation: Independently verify risk results and regulatory claims before using them in investment or compliance workflows.

Risk: Sharing raw portfolio records could expose sensitive holdings or personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only desensitized holdings, weights, returns, or NAV series needed for analysis.
