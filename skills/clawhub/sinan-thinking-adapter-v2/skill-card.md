## Description: <br>
Adapt explanations to Sinan's reasoning style: always start from step zero, use analogies, validate his guesses, evolve with his questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and assistants use this skill to tailor explanations for a named learner by starting from first principles, validating the learner's guesses, using clear analogies, and tracking explicit explanation preferences. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is personalized around a named individual and asks the agent to keep observations about that person's reasoning patterns. <br>
Mitigation: Limit retained observations to explicit, user-approved preferences and provide clear review and deletion controls. <br>
Risk: Persistent explanation preferences could drift into unsupported personal profiling. <br>
Mitigation: Review updates before retaining them and keep the Evolution Log focused on instructional preferences rather than sensitive traits. <br>
