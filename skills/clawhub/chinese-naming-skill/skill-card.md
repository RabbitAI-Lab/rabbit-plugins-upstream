## Description: <br>
Guide users through systematic Chinese baby naming with optional bazi analysis, classics-based candidate discovery, wuxing verification, and multi-dimensional comparison. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[left-uestc](https://clawhub.ai/user/left-uestc) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
People seeking a Chinese baby name use this skill to collect family requirements, optionally analyze bazi and wuxing preferences, find classical source passages, validate candidates, and compare final options while retaining the final decision. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The requirements workflow can collect sensitive family identity, birth time, birthplace, and optional parent bazi details. <br>
Mitigation: Treat filled requirements as private, collect only details the user wants to provide, omit optional parent information unless everyone consents, and avoid unnecessary sharing or uploading of identifying information. <br>
Risk: Bazi, wuxing, and naming-school judgments can involve disputed or subjective interpretations. <br>
Mitigation: Present options and uncertainty clearly, explain the basis for different schools, keep bazi optional, and leave value judgments and final name selection to the user. <br>
