## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create, fix, harden, and validate PollyReach-style productivity workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn PollyReach-style productivity needs into practical workflows, checklists, templates, analysis, code changes, or decision aids. It emphasizes local-hardware-friendly outputs and validates results against stated success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Overly broad activation terms may cause the helper to invoke for unrelated productivity or PollyReach-style requests. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow the trigger keywords and disable implicit invocation unless broad activation is intended. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance can be misapplied if the user's outcome, constraints, or success criteria are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Restate the outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria, then validate the result before use. <br>
