## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to plan domestic China trips by searching attractions, nearby hotels, routes, restaurants, train tickets, and flights from a conversational agent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel searches, route origins and destinations, dates, and location keywords are sent through the skill publisher's cloud proxy to Fliggy/Gaode-backed services. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering private addresses or sensitive itinerary details unless that data sharing is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Prices, availability, route estimates, and map coverage may be incomplete or change after results are returned. <br>
Mitigation: Verify details on the booking page or map service before purchasing tickets, booking travel, or starting a trip. <br>
