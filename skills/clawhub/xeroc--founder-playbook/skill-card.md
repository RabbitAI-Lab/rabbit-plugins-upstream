## Description:

Founder Playbook provides decision validation and thinking frameworks for startup founders pressure-testing strategy, fundraising, customer development, runway management, prioritization, and crypto/web3 choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xeroc](https://clawhub.ai/user/xeroc)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Startup founders and founder-support agents use this skill to structure coaching conversations, pressure-test decisions, surface assumptions, and choose concrete next steps across fundraising, customer development, runway, prioritization, and crypto/web3 contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad decision-support trigger wording may activate this skill for general decision questions outside startup or founder contexts.

Mitigation: Configure host activation to prefer startup, founder, fundraising, customer development, runway, prioritization, or crypto/web3 contexts.

Risk: Founder coaching outputs may be mistaken for professional legal, financial, investment, medical, or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Use responses as coaching prompts and decision-support guidance, and direct users to qualified professionals for legal, regulatory, security, financial, or mental-health decisions.
