## Description:

Turn verbal or informal workflow descriptions into executable SOP documents with role assignments, decision points, exception handling, deliverables, and revision records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liuyuming0823](https://clawhub.ai/user/liuyuming0823)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, operators, and business-process owners use this skill to convert spoken, chat-style, or rough workflow notes into structured SOP Markdown. It is especially useful when a process needs concrete roles, numbered steps, decision criteria, exception handling, and reviewable deliverables before distribution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SOPs may not match current company policy, compliance requirements, or real system screens.

Mitigation: Review each SOP for business accuracy, compliance, and current system behavior before use or distribution.

Risk: Incomplete user input can leave operational details underspecified.

Mitigation: Use the skill's interview questions and keep unresolved fields marked for follow-up rather than treating them as finalized procedure.
