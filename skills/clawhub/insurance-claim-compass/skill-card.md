## Description:

Guides policyholders through documenting an insured loss, drafting claim and appeal letters, estimating depreciation, and tracking claim milestones.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Policyholders use this skill after an insured loss to assemble evidence, prepare written notice, understand estimated ACV and RCV amounts, and draft responses to low offers or denials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; verify local claim deadlines and requirements before use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Claim guidance or suggested deadlines may not match local law or the policy.

Mitigation: Verify reporting deadlines, accident-report requirements, and policy terms with the insurer and relevant local authority; do not treat the output as legal advice.

Risk: Draft letters can contain policy numbers and sensitive loss details.

Mitigation: Review letters before sending and protect any saved copies.
