## Description: <br>
Helps a wedding speaker draft a concise, audience-safe toast built around one strong story, with delivery notes and jokes or stories to cut. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External wedding speakers use this skill to turn their role, relationship details, audience constraints, and one to three candidate stories into a two- to four-minute toast. It helps keep the speech specific, kind, short, and focused on the couple. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include private or embarrassing details about the couple or guests while asking for help. <br>
Mitigation: Share only details the speaker is comfortable using in the draft, and remove sensitive or embarrassing material before generation. <br>
Risk: A draft may include jokes, inside references, ex-adjacent material, or comments that are unsuitable for the stated audience. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's audience checks and cut list, then review the final toast before delivery. <br>
