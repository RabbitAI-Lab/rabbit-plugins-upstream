## Description: <br>
Uses the browser-use CLI with a logged-in local browser session to collect same-day webmail metadata, unread counts, screenshots, and a basic Markdown daily email report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Personal developers and individual mailbox users can use this skill to review a single Gmail, Outlook, or QQ Mail inbox for the current day and generate a lightweight Markdown summary with an archived inbox screenshot. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill uses a logged-in local browser session to access mailbox content and generate reports that may include private senders, subjects, snippets, and timestamps. <br>
Mitigation: Run it only for mailboxes you own or are authorized to access, and store or delete generated screenshots and Markdown reports carefully. <br>
