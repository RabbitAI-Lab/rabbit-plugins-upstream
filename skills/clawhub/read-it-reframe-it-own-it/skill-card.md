## Description:

Read, reframe, and own a topic by building a knowledge tree that absorbs ideas from different sources under your own key framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners use this skill to organize ideas from multiple sources around their own learning goal and key framework, rather than copying a source's structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A knowledge tree may copy a source's structure instead of reflecting the learner's goal.

Mitigation: Define the learning goal and key framework first, then place source ideas according to their roles in that framework.
