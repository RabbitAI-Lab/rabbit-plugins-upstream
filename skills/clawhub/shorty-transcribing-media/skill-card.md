## Description:

Transcribes publicly accessible audio or video links, or starts subtitle jobs for video, through Shorty after user confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with publicly accessible media links use this skill to retrieve a transcript or start a video subtitle job using their Shorty account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Starting a job sends the media URL to Shorty and can consume the user's plan quota.

Mitigation: Show the exact URL and job type, check applicable limits, and obtain the user's confirmation before starting.

Risk: Resulting transcripts may remain in the Shorty app.

Mitigation: Tell users to remove transcripts in the Shorty app when they no longer need them.
