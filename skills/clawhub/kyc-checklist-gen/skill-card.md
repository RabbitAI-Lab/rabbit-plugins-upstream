## Description:

Generates personal or corporate KYC document checklists and reports completeness and missing items.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (code only); All rights reserved (documentation and theory)

## Use Case:

Individuals, businesses, and their onboarding teams use the skill to prepare a basic KYC document checklist and identify missing items before submitting materials to an institution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A completed checklist could be mistaken for confirmation of legal or institutional KYC compliance.

Mitigation: Use it only to prepare materials; confirm current requirements with the relevant institution and qualified compliance professionals.

Risk: KYC information can be sensitive.

Mitigation: Enter only the requested document numbers, not identity documents or personal details, and run the helper locally.
