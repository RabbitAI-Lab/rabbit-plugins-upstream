## Description: <br>
Maps a target beachhead's buyer beliefs, recurring objections, objection reframes, and JTBD switching forces from user-provided interview or win-loss notes, while marking unsupported claims as needing sources. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and product teams use this skill to synthesize buyer research into a belief, objection, and switching-forces map for a defined beachhead before drafting narrative or positioning work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Buyer research, sales notes, and customer quotes may contain sensitive or unverified claims. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if the skill can access that local context, review saved outputs before confirming persistence, and keep unsupported quotes or comparative claims marked [needs source]. <br>
