## Description:

Helps households decide how long to keep common paper records and organize review, filing, scanning, and shredding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Households and people organizing inherited paperwork use the skill to look up suggested retention periods, sort records into keep, scan, or shred actions, and set up a year-based filing system.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can recommend shredding important records before their retention requirements have been met.

Mitigation: Review before relying on purge mode. Confirm warranty expiration, payroll reconciliation, sale or move-out dates, and unresolved claims yourself before shredding anything. The script provides recommendations and does not destroy documents automatically.
