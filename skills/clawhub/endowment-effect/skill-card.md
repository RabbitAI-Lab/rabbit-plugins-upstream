## Description:

Guides agents in identifying and addressing endowment effects in valuation, negotiation, product design, and build-versus-buy decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, founders, product teams, and negotiators use this skill to assess ownership-driven valuation gaps and choose evidence-based pricing, negotiation, trial, or product-design approaches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A valuation gap may be attributed to ownership bias when it reflects genuine information asymmetry or market evidence.

Mitigation: Verify valuation assumptions and market comparables independently, and distinguish factual differences from endowment effects.

Risk: Personalization or trial design could make cancellation harder for customers.

Mitigation: Keep cancellation choices and personalization under the customer's control, with clear and low-friction cancellation.
