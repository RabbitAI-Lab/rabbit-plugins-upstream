## Description: <br>
Guides agents through endowment-effect analysis for pricing, negotiation, product onboarding, and build-versus-buy decisions where ownership may inflate perceived value. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, operators, founders, and negotiation advisors use this skill to identify when ownership is inflating valuation or attachment, then structure neutral reference prices, earnouts, framing changes, or ethical product onboarding choices. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Business, pricing, negotiation, legal, regulatory, or investment advice may be over-applied if treated as automatic authority. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as analytical guidance and have qualified reviewers validate consequential pricing, deal, regulatory, legal, or investment decisions. <br>
Risk: Leveraging endowment effects in product onboarding or cancellation flows can become manipulative or create regulatory exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's ethical check before using leverage tactics and keep cancellation and consent flows transparent. <br>
