## Description:

Unit-economics calculator for short-video e-commerce that helps users calculate monthly channel profitability, breakeven ROI, and LTV-based customer-acquisition pricing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[handsomeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/handsomeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External short-video e-commerce operators, growth teams, and commerce consultants use this skill to collect channel economics, calculate breakeven ROI and monthly total-account profitability, and estimate LTV-based acquisition price ceilings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read or persist product, marketing, and financial dossier information in local files.

Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where local dossier creation is acceptable, and require explicit approval before creating or updating archives.

Risk: Business calculations can be misleading when gross margin, return rate, GMV basis, acquisition cost, or repurchase data are estimated or mixed across accounting bases.

Mitigation: Confirm the financial inputs and label estimates before using the generated ROI, channel, or LTV guidance for operating decisions.
