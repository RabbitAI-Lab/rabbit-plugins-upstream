## Description:

Provides 149 reusable social-post templates, including 101 hooks, for common formats and goals across LinkedIn, X, and other channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[norbertlevente](https://clawhub.ai/user/norbertlevente)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and social media teams use this skill to choose a reusable post structure or hook, then fill it with their own facts to draft posts for common goals and channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain inaccurate claims or confidential details.

Mitigation: Review each draft for accuracy and confidential details before approving scheduling.

Risk: Separate Postemi companion skills may request account access.

Mitigation: Assess those companion skills before granting account access; their implementations are not included here.
