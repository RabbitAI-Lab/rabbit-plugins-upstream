## Description: <br>
Generates 60 high-impact tweet ideas from reference content across 5 categories for Twitter/X, including harsh advice, quotes, pain points, counterintuitive truths, key insights, and creative wildcards. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vincentchan](https://clawhub.ai/user/vincentchan) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and social media operators use this skill to turn pasted content, draft files, notes, newsletters, scripts, or user-provided URLs into categorized short-form tweet ideas with brief rationale and analysis notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may fetch URLs supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide URLs whose content you are comfortable having the assistant retrieve and analyze. <br>
Risk: Generated ideas are saved locally under the tweet-ideas folder. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing sensitive private material unless you are comfortable with derived content being written to a local markdown file. <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to produce persuasive, high-conviction, and sometimes hyperbolic social media copy. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated statements for accuracy, attribution, tone, and brand fit before posting publicly. <br>
