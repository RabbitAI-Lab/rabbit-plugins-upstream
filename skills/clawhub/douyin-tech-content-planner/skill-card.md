## Description:

Turn a technology or AI-tool topic and its source-backed facts into a focused Douyin video or image-post plan, with a clear audience, hook, evidence order, and optional call to action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Creators and editors use this skill to turn supplied, source-backed technology or AI-tool facts into a focused Douyin short-video or image-post plan. It organizes the audience, viewer benefit, hook, evidence-backed sequence, title direction, and any uncertainties.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A plan could present an inaccurate or misleading factual claim if supplied sources are weak or a claim is summarized incorrectly.

Mitigation: Provide the source materials intended for use, keep unsupported or conflicting claims out of factual hooks and conclusions, and verify claims before publishing; the validator checks structure and fact references, not source credibility or accuracy.

Risk: The included examples use fictional products, data, and sources and could be mistaken for real evidence.

Mitigation: Treat the examples as structure demonstrations only and replace them with verified materials for the actual topic.
