## Description: <br>
Turns presentation ideas into McKinsey-style outlines, consistent 16:9 slide images, a playable HTML deck, and duration-aware speaker scripts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[callmexhj](https://clawhub.ai/user/callmexhj) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to turn presentation requirements and source material into reviewed outlines, generated PPT-style slide images, an HTML player, and final speaker notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Source material or facts provided for the deck could be reflected incorrectly in outlines, slide images, or speaker notes. <br>
Mitigation: Review the confirmed brief, outline, expanded content, generated images, and final speaker notes at the skill's approval gates. <br>
Risk: The HTML helper copies images and writes an HTML file to the selected output location. <br>
Mitigation: Choose output folders intentionally and review the generated local files before sharing or publishing them. <br>
