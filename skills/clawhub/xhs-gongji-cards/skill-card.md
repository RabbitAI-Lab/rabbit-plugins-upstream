## Description:

把 5 道公共基础知识/常识判断题做成小红书图文：10 张 1080×1440 竖版卡片（图1题1 / 图2解析1 / 图3题2 …）+ 标题、简短正文、标签。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

考编、公务员及事业单位备考者可用它编排公基与常识判断每日五题，生成题目和解析卡片，以及可编辑的小红书标题、正文和标签。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regenerating a day can replace its images and preview.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated project folder and check output paths when importing quiz JSON.

Risk: Quiz answers or generated copy may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review quiz accuracy and generated copy before publishing.
