## Description:

Reviews Postify content calendars, identifies schedule gaps and failed posts, and reschedules posts after user confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media managers and teams use this skill to review upcoming and past Postify posts, spot gaps in their posting cadence, and move scheduled posts after confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post could be moved to the wrong time or channel.

Mitigation: Check the post ID, channel, timezone, and new time with the user before confirming a reschedule.
