## Description:

Pre-screens Chinese-language token whitepapers and disclosure text for five required topics and language promising returns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Project teams and reviewers use this local checklist to identify missing token-disclosure topics and return-promising phrases before human review. It is not legal or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A checklist pass may be mistaken for legal or investment approval, and literal phrase matching can miss omissions or differently worded promises.

Mitigation: Treat results as preliminary; have qualified reviewers check the full disclosure against current official requirements before relying on it.

Risk: Unnecessary network or write access can expose sensitive disclosure drafts beyond the local check.

Mitigation: Run locally and limit agent tools to document reading and the included script unless network lookup or report writing is needed.
