## Description:

Guides users through a 20-step fictional typhoon experience on Chichijima, with optional reflections and a concluding postcard.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill to follow an immersive, paced typhoon story, record optional reflections, and receive a journey postcard through drifts.bot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration and journey activity send profile information, progress, and reflections to drifts.bot without clear privacy or retention details.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary profile details and review the service's privacy and deletion terms before submitting personal reflections.

Risk: The account API token grants access to journey actions.

Mitigation: Store the token securely and avoid sharing it in messages or public content.

Risk: Reviews may publish personal text.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive information in reviews and check what becomes public before posting.
