## Description:

帮助中国中小学教师设计双向细目表、试题、难度配比及评分标准，并根据考后统计提出题目返修建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

中小学教师用它编制测评蓝图和双向细目表、改编试题、控制难度，并拟定评分细则与讲评错题清单。正式使用前须由教师核验试题。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unreleased confidential exam content could be exposed if entered into the skill.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam papers, answers, or scoring standards; use generic blueprint guidance instead.

Risk: Student identifiers or assessment records could be shared beyond the intended school workflow.

Mitigation: Avoid student personal identifiers and align any cross-skill sharing with school policy.

Risk: AI-generated questions or scoring guidance may be incorrect or unsuitable for a formal assessment.

Mitigation: Have a teacher manually check each item, answer, and rubric before including it in a formal assessment.
