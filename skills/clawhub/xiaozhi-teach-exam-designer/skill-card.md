## Description:

帮助教师按双向细目表设计试卷和课堂练习，并制定题目难度、评分标准与考后题目返修建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

面向中国大陆中小学教师，用于设计单元课堂练习、试卷蓝图、双向细目表、题目与评分细则，并依据已有题目统计提出返修建议；正式使用前由教师审定。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated questions or scoring guidance may contain errors.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify every generated item and scoring rubric before using it in an assessment.

Risk: Confidential exam materials or identifiable student information may be exposed if entered into an AI tool.

Mitigation: Do not enter active confidential exam materials or student real names; keep the skill's privacy and sharing controls enabled.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy requirements and crisis referral guidance may not fit locations outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these settings and referral channels before using the skill elsewhere.
