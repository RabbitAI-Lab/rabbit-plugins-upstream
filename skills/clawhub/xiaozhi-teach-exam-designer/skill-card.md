## Description:

Helps teachers design and revise assessments using test blueprints, balanced difficulty and cognitive levels, adapted questions, scoring rubrics, and item-review lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to draft unit exercises and exams, build two-way test blueprints, balance difficulty and cognitive levels, adapt questions, and prepare scoring rubrics and item-review lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated or adapted assessment questions may be inaccurate or unsuitable for their intended grade level.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review generated questions and check their answers, conditions, and grade-level fit before use.

Risk: Identifiable student information or confidential exam materials could be exposed through use or saved memory.

Mitigation: Avoid including identifiable student data and confidential exam papers; review platform storage and sharing settings before enabling memory.
