## Description:

帮助中国大陆中小学教师按双向细目表设计试卷、调整难度、拟定评分标准，并依据考后统计返修题目。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External K–12 teachers use this skill to draft assessment blueprints, questions and scoring rubrics, then prioritize question revisions after an exam. Teachers review generated questions before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated questions may contain incorrect answers, inappropriate difficulty or scoring errors.

Mitigation: Have a teacher solve and check every generated question, answer and score allocation before adding it to a formal exam.

Risk: Unreleased official exam material or student personal data may be exposed.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased official exam questions or identifying student information; use anonymized, aggregated data for review.

Risk: Copyrighted question-bank material may be reproduced without permission.

Mitigation: Use original or authorized questions and retain only a reference to unauthorized source questions.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis resources may be unsuitable outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language, safeguarding contacts and privacy requirements before use in another region.
