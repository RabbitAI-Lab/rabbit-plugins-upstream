## Description: <br>
Helps agents create, optimize, and analyze customer referral, affiliate, ambassador, word-of-mouth, viral loop, and partner programs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External founders, marketers, growth teams, and operators use this skill to design, compare, optimize, and measure referral and affiliate programs. It supports incentive planning, launch checklists, email prompts, fraud-prevention considerations, program metrics, and platform selection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use local product-marketing context and referral economics such as CAC, LTV, customer details, and payout assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review business, customer, and payout data before using it in agent prompts or sharing it with external referral or affiliate services. <br>
Risk: Program recommendations can affect incentives, payouts, fraud controls, and customer-facing terms. <br>
Mitigation: Have business, finance, and legal owners review final program rules before launch. <br>
