## Description: <br>
Helps users brainstorm and choose marketing ideas, inspiration, and strategies for SaaS or software products based on stage, goals, budget, and team resources. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, marketers, and growth teams use this skill to identify practical SaaS or software growth tactics, prioritize ideas by situation and resources, and get concise implementation steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: May activate on broad growth or marketing-ideation phrasing. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants marketing strategy suggestions before applying recommendations to unrelated business questions. <br>
Risk: May use local product-marketing context files when present. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those files current and free of sensitive information that should not be used in marketing recommendations. <br>
