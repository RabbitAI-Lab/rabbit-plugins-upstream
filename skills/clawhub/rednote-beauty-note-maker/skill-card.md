## Description:

Create Xiaohongshu beauty and skincare content from product facts, routine steps, skin concerns, and audience context. This AI beauty note maker produces a 3:4 post concept, review structure, usage-scene copy, title options, cover wording, relevant hashtags, and a natural comment starter for makeup, skincare, haircare, body care, routines, comparisons, and product recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
