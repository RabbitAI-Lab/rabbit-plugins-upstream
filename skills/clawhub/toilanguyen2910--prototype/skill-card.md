## Description: <br>
Throwaway prototype workflow for rapid validation: 1-3 day hypothesis-driven game development sprints with clear exit criteria, playtest goals, and a proceed, pivot, or kill decision framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[toilanguyen2910](https://clawhub.ai/user/toilanguyen2910) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Game developers, designers, and agents use this skill to structure short prototype sprints that validate one risky mechanic or concept, define measurable success criteria, run lightweight playtests, and document whether to proceed, pivot, or kill the idea. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to be selected for non-game prototyping requests. <br>
Mitigation: Specify whether the requested prototype is for a game mechanic or use a different prototyping workflow for non-game work. <br>
Risk: Prototype outputs may be treated as production-ready even though the workflow emphasizes throwaway validation work. <br>
Mitigation: Archive or discard prototype code after the decision step and start production implementation from documented lessons rather than reusing exploratory code. <br>
