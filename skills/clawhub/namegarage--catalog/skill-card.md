## Description:

Find published Namegarage domains, look up listing details, compare payment options, and suggest alternatives when a user is choosing a domain for a project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[namegarage](https://clawhub.ai/user/namegarage)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and developers choosing a domain can search published Namegarage listings, review listing details, compare current purchase options, and find alternatives for a project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan had medium confidence and was partial because two local references were missing, so those materials were not assessed.

Mitigation: Review the missing references and scan the complete materials before relying on them.

Risk: Search text is sent to Namegarage and may contain confidential project details.

Mitigation: Use general project descriptions and omit confidential details unless the user chooses to disclose them.

Risk: Catalog prices and availability can change, and a listing does not reserve a name or establish trademark rights.

Mitigation: Check the returned page for current availability and terms before purchase, and assess registration and trademark suitability separately.
