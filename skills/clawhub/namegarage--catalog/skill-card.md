## Description:

Find published Namegarage domains, look up listing details, compare payment options, and suggest alternatives when a user is choosing a domain for a project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[namegarage](https://clawhub.ai/user/namegarage)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People choosing a domain for a project can search published Namegarage listings, review details, compare available payment options, and explore alternatives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search text is sent to Namegarage and could contain confidential project details.

Mitigation: Use general project descriptions and omit confidential details unless the user chooses to disclose them.

Risk: Published listing availability, payment terms, and checkout fees may not reflect current conditions; listings do not establish trademark clearance.

Mitigation: Check the returned listing for current availability, payment terms, and checkout fees before buying, and do not treat suggested uses as trademark clearance.
