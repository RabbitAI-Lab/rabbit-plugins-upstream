## Description:

Meeting Note helps agents turn discussion and decision meetings into structured minutes covering conclusions, consensus and dissent, decision history, assumptions, risks, action items, and Zettelkasten links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mikonos](https://clawhub.ai/user/mikonos)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees, external collaborators, and knowledge workers use this skill to convert multi-party discussion or decision meetings into actionable meeting notes with decisions, dissenting views, risks, owners, deadlines, and knowledge-network links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The README recommends unpinned npx install commands that can run changing third-party code.

Mitigation: Review the install path before installing, prefer a pinned and verified ClawHub or skills.sh version, confirm the publisher, and avoid elevated privileges.

Risk: Meeting notes may contain sensitive discussion content and may be linked into a local knowledge index.

Mitigation: Review generated notes before sharing or indexing, and apply the user's local confidentiality and retention rules.
