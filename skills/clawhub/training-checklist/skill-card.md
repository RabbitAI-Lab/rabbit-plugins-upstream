## Description: <br>
Training Checklist helps organizers track training-event preparation across planning, instructor coordination, enrollment, venue, materials, final readiness, and execution tasks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Training organizers and operations teams use this skill to generate and maintain preparation checklists, review item status, identify missing work, and summarize weekly progress before a training event. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generic reminder and to-do trigger phrases may activate the skill in unrelated planning conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow or disable broad trigger phrases when accidental activation would disrupt normal agent use. <br>
Risk: Checklist outputs are advisory and may not reflect the actual readiness of an event. <br>
Mitigation: Have the training owner confirm each status item before relying on the weekly progress summary. <br>
