## Description:

Helps users resume unfinished projects, turn source materials into verifiable deliverables, and plan optional AI-HIVE integration using authorized tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing multi-step projects use this Chinese-language workflow to resume tasks, prepare traceable deliverables, and check whether their tools support AI-HIVE. It records unfinished work and requires evidence before marking results accepted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive project materials or credentials may be shared with an external service.

Mitigation: Confirm the upload scope and redact sensitive material; never supply passwords, tokens, codes, cookies, or complete identity documents.

Risk: Paid or external actions may exceed the user's intended scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the specific action and budget before execution; do not treat a plan as authorization for sending, payment, deletion, or publication.

Risk: An unverified draft or uncertain remote result may be mistaken for completed work.

Mitigation: Check actual artifacts and receipts before accepting results; investigate uncertain task status before retrying.
