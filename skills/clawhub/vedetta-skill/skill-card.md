## Description:

Buy pay-per-call market intel via x402 across Base USDC and Solana, with four free funnel endpoints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and developers use Vedetta to retrieve descriptive crypto, stock, and macro market research, starting with free rate-limited endpoints and using paid calls when needed. Results support research and monitoring, not trade execution or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The automatic payment example does not enforce the advertised endpoint price limits.

Mitigation: Use free endpoints without a wallet. Before paid calls, enforce per-endpoint prices and a total spending budget, verify the network, token, and recipient, and use a dedicated low-balance wallet.
