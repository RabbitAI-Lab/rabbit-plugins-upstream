## Description:

Translates between everyday Chinese or English and formal legal writing, and explains legal documents in plain language.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samjoeyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/samjoeyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can turn everyday accounts into more formal legal-language drafts or understand legal documents in plain language. The skill is a language aid, not a substitute for legal advice or review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Example wording may introduce unsupported facts into a legal-language draft.

Mitigation: Check every factual statement against the original account and remove or correct unsupported details before use.

Risk: A plain-language explanation or example may not reflect the deadlines or requirements that apply to a specific matter.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines and obtain qualified legal review before submitting documents.
