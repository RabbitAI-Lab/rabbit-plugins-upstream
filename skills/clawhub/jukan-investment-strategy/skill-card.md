## Description: <br>
Generates Jukan05-style investment analysis reports for stocks, sectors, or markets using a trend-identification, bottleneck-positioning, technical-verification, source-rating, and risk-control framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kooui](https://clawhub.ai/user/kooui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Investors, analysts, and research-focused users can use this skill to draft structured stock, sector, or market analysis in Chinese or English. It is best suited for semiconductor and AI-related themes where the bundled Jukan05-style reference materials are most developed. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated reports may be mistaken for Jukan05's own work or endorsed financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Label outputs as AI-generated, avoid presenting them as Jukan05-authored or endorsed, and retain the investment-advice disclaimer. <br>
Risk: Market data, source ratings, and investment conclusions can be outdated, incomplete, or misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Verify all financial data and cited sources independently before sharing or relying on an output. <br>
