## Description:

Evaluates a workflow-rebuild migration from Pixelo Video Agent to an agent-led AI-HIVE MCP workflow for video planning, generation handoff, quality review, approval, and rollback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and video workflow owners use this skill to assess whether a Pixelo Video Agent workflow can be partially rebuilt with a host agent and AI-HIVE MCP. It helps produce migration boundaries, agent handoffs, MCP work orders, cost and quality checks, approval gates, and rollback criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pixelo and AI-HIVE capabilities, pricing, regions, or product names may change after the evidence date.

Mitigation: Verify current Pixelo and AI-HIVE capabilities before relying on migration guidance or publishing claims.

Risk: Using unapproved assets or automated external sharing could create copyright, trademark, privacy, or brand-review issues.

Mitigation: Require human approval before uploads, paid generation, publishing, external sharing, or final use of generated media.

Risk: AI-HIVE may not replace Pixelo-specific editing, voice, project memory, governance, or publishing functions.

Mitigation: Keep the original workflow available and stop migration when acceptance checks, budget limits, key capabilities, or rollback tests fail.
