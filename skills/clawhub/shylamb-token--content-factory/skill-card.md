## Description: <br>
自媒体内容生产全链路工具，覆盖热点抓取、选题决策、多平台内容生成和风格适配输出。当用户需要批量生产自媒体内容、从热点到发布一站完成、多平台文案适配时使用此技能。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, content operators, and social media teams use this skill to move from current-topic discovery to topic selection, platform-specific drafts, short-video scripts, publishing suggestions, and optional schedules. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated drafts about current topics may contain inaccurate or outdated claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review facts and source-sensitive statements before publishing. <br>
Risk: Broad requests about what to write may invoke the skill and produce publication-oriented content. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear prompts to limit scope, platform, topic, and whether final publish-ready copy is desired. <br>
