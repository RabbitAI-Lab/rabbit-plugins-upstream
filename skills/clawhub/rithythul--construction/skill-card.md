## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project records may include incident reports, worker names, visitor logs, photos, budget data, change orders, and disputes.

Mitigation: Install only where authorized users can handle the records, and redact sensitive owner or client communications before distribution.

Risk: Construction, safety, quality, schedule, and cost recommendations can affect compliance, site operations, and contractual positions.

Mitigation: Use outputs as working project-management guidance and review them against contract requirements, site conditions, safety policies, and jurisdictional requirements before action.
