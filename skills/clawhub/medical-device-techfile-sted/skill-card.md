## Description:

医疗器械技术文件（Technical Documentation）与 STED（Summary Technical Documentation）专题技能，帮助代理按 IMDRF STED 六章结构映射欧盟 MDR Annex II/III、美国 510(k) Summary/eSTAR、中国注册申报资料和日本技术资料，并生成结构对照、撰写要点和官方入口引用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External regulatory, quality, and product teams use this skill to plan and draft medical-device technical documentation across STED, EU MDR, FDA 510(k), China registration, and Japan technical-file structures. It supports reusable document outlines, market mapping, chapter-by-chapter drafting guidance, and submission-readiness checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical-device regulatory requirements, templates, and guidance can change after the skill content is published.

Mitigation: Verify the linked official sources and current templates before relying on generated guidance for regulatory submissions.

Risk: The skill provides documentation structure and drafting guidance, not a regulator or notified-body determination for a specific product.

Mitigation: Use the output as a drafting aid and route final submissions through qualified regulatory review.
