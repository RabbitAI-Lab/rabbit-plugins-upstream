## Description:

Expose material assumptions, hidden premises, and unresolved unknowns in a plan, analysis, design, or decision before committing to work where an incorrect assumption could change the approach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and decision makers use this skill to identify assumptions that could materially change a plan, analysis, design, or decision before the next irreversible step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may flag unresolved assumptions before an irreversible action, but its output is advisory and can be incomplete or context-sensitive.

Mitigation: Review the audit with the accountable decision maker and resolve only assumptions that could materially change the next step.
