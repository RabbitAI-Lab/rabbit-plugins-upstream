## Description:

Part of the Overpowered skill suite, this skill exposes material assumptions, hidden premises, and unresolved unknowns in a plan, analysis, design, or decision before work proceeds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and teams use this skill to audit plans, analyses, designs, or decisions for assumptions that could materially change the next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make an agent more explicit about assumptions and may recommend fact-checking with available tools in sensitive projects.

Mitigation: Review proposed tool use before execution and limit checks to appropriate artifacts, systems, and sources.

Risk: An assumption audit may miss a material hidden premise or overstate a low-impact unknown.

Mitigation: Apply the skill to decision-changing assumptions and review unresolved items before irreversible work proceeds.
