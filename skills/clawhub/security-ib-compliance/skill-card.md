## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive customer or transaction information could be disclosed in prompts or drafts.

Mitigation: Use only the minimum necessary public or de-identified details; exclude IDs, bank records, full customer lists, nonpublic financial data, and insider information.

Risk: Outdated or incomplete regulatory guidance could lead to incorrect compliance decisions.

Mitigation: Check regulatory claims against current official sources and obtain qualified compliance or legal review before acting on outputs.
