## Description:

AI-powered investment banking compliance review assistant for the China market covering IPO sponsorship, M&A advisory, bond issuance, PE investment, AML/KYC screening, cross-border transactions, and compliance monitoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

IBD compliance officers, in-house counsel, and deal teams use this skill as a reference aid for China-market investment banking compliance checklists, risk triage, regulatory monitoring prompts, and review frameworks. Final compliance decisions should be checked against official sources and qualified counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static regulatory summaries and current-rule claims may become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify securities, AML/KYC, and cross-border compliance guidance against official sources and qualified counsel before relying on it.

Risk: Illustrative AML/KYC and risk-scoring code may be mistaken for production-ready compliance controls.

Mitigation: Treat code blocks as examples only and subject any implementation to legal, compliance, security, and engineering review.
