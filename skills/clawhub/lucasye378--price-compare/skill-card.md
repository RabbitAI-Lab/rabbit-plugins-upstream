## Description: <br>
Compare product prices across major US e-commerce platforms for US shoppers, including Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, eBay, Temu, Google Shopping, Costco, and Kroger. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External shoppers use this skill to compare retailer prices, shipping, ratings, trust signals, and buying recommendations before purchasing a product. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
United States <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Retailer data can be incomplete, stale, or unavailable because pages may block search, change dynamically, or omit shipping, taxes, warranty, seller authenticity, and availability. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the final total price, seller, shipping, taxes, warranty, and availability on the retailer page before buying. <br>
Risk: Very low prices, unbranded listings, marketplace sellers, fake discounts, and pressure tactics can indicate counterfeit or low-quality products. <br>
Mitigation: Use the trust checklist, prefer official or highly rated sellers, and treat suspiciously cheap or poorly reviewed listings as warnings rather than recommendations. <br>
