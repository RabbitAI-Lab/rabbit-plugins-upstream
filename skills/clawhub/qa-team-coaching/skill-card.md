## Description:

Guides QA teams in coaching new testers through checklists, templates, reviews, pair testing, and progress assessments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA leads and mentors use this Chinese-language guide to assess team skills, onboard testers, and build tailored coaching plans and training materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Training examples about release checklists or review processes could be mistaken for approved team standards.

Mitigation: Review proposed process changes with the team and obtain authorization before applying them.

Risk: Chinese-language coaching materials may not match the team's preferred language.

Mitigation: Confirm the preferred language and review generated guidance before adopting it.
