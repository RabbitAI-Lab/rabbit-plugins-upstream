## Description: <br>
Generates anime and manga-style illustrations, including characters, scenes, storyboards, and consistent batches of stylized 2D creative assets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[epstion518](https://clawhub.ai/user/epstion518) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and creative developers use this skill to draft anime and manga characters, scenes, avatars, wallpapers, stickers, and multi-panel storyboard assets from text or image prompts. It is intended for stylized 2D creative work, not real human portrait generation or prohibited content. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The trigger may be broader than the anime and manga use case. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill only for anime, manga, cartoon, storyboard, or stylized 2D illustration tasks, and route generic image-generation requests to a more appropriate skill. <br>
Risk: Generated content may drift from the requested style, character traits, or storyboard continuity. <br>
Mitigation: Use fixed character descriptors, explicit style keywords, and iterative prompt refinement as described in the bundled prompt guide. <br>
