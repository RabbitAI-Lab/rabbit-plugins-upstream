# Single Transformation Contract

Apply the environment contract unchanged. Portraits use one of two explicit treatments selected during the first-turn suitability gate: `conceptual_portrait` is the default high-abstraction treatment; `restrained_character_portrait` is a quieter alternative, not a return to photographic realism.

## Portrait contract

```text
identity_topology: high
photographic_surface_fidelity: low
expressive_reconstruction: high
controlled_feature_deformation: medium-high
detail_concentration: high
portrait_background_semantic_abstraction: high
source_background_literal_fidelity: low
```

Preserve identity through relationships, not through photographic rendering. Keep four to six decisive anchors collectively stable: face axis, spacing and height of the eyes, brow rhythm, nose-mouth relationship, jaw or cheek silhouette, hairline, distinctive asymmetry, glasses, facial hair, or another signature attribute.

Do not freeze every anchor. Re-author the face as a drawing and painting:

- divide the face and neck into a small number of designed value-color masses;
- change the emphasis of at least two non-essential features or planes;
- let at least one significant edge break, merge, scrape away, or cut sharply against a neighboring mass;
- introduce one motivated color event that is not literal skin color;
- use one mark family that contradicts natural surface direction;
- let the background act on the head or shoulders.
- distill the uploaded background into one source-derived field grammar with one to four irregular color masses, an optional restrained line rhythm, and at least two field-to-figure interactions.

For `conceptual_portrait`, at least five of these seven departures must be plainly visible, and the re-authoring may include one bounded source-derived symbolic or psychic intervention when it intensifies the visible tension. A faithful face beneath surface texture or a photographic person placed on generic abstract smoke does not satisfy the contract.

For `restrained_character_portrait`, retain the same identity topology, plane-based face, field-to-figure integration, and anti-photographic surface rules, but use fewer symbolic intrusions and do not introduce extra faces, psychic anatomy, or icon-like imagery unless the user separately asks for it.

Allow bounded exaggeration or compression of a brow, eye opening, nose plane, mouth corner, cheek mass, jaw, hair volume, neck, or shoulder angle. Preserve the landmark constellation so these changes intensify recognition rather than replacing the person.

## Environment contract

```text
place_identity_anchors: high
photographic_surface_fidelity: low
B2.5_concept_reconstruction: high
atmosphere_palette_depth_redesign: high
structural_deformation: medium-high
semantic_detail_allocation: high
edge_hierarchy: high
```

Treat the source as evidence for an environmental concept painting. Preserve the core building or geographic identity, decisive landmark silhouettes, key road, path, shoreline, or entrance relationships, principal objects, and enough spatial logic to recognize the place.

Use B2.5 freedom to substantially redesign weather, light, cloud structure, palette, shadow family, depth compression, sky proportion, horizon emphasis, peripheral geometry, and non-essential proportions. Do not turn the source into a different city or biome, erase its primary landmark, or add unrelated structures and people.

Allocate mark scale by `information density + narrative importance`, not object size alone:

- use broad low-frequency masses for sky, water, fog, snow, roads, ordinary walls, roofs, grass fields, distance, and large shadow;
- use selective directional lines and finer marks for identity-bearing architecture, landmark ornament, structural joints, trunks, main branches, foliage clusters, flowers, rock ridges, birds, and animals;
- preserve neither every window nor every leaf. Assign each major element an identity importance and detail budget.

Separate primary, secondary, and lost edges. Organize foreground, middle ground, and background for different visual jobs instead of preserving photographic depth uniformity. Preserve source color relationships while reconstructing literal colors into an emotional palette.

## Environmental portrait contract

Apply the portrait contract to the person and the environment contract to the place, with the person primary. Make the environment press on, frame, isolate, erode, shelter, or radiate from the figure instead of functioning as a separate scenic backdrop.

## Scope boundary

If a user explicitly wants photographic likeness, conservative painterly realism, or only a light filter, explain that this Skill is intentionally high-abstraction and use a more appropriate workflow. Do not weaken this contract under the same Skill name.

Pure natural scenery remains a conditional fit because it has fewer human, architectural, and social anchors and can collapse into conventional oil landscape. Enforce the first-turn suitability gate in `SKILL.md`. After confirmation, use terrain, horizon, atmosphere, vegetation, rock, water, and animal structure as the authored system; do not invent human traces to compensate.
