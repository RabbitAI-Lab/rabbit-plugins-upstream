# Quality Checklist

Evaluate at thumbnail size, normal viewing size, and close range. Compare against the source before returning the result.

## Shared hard gates

- The result is visibly derived from the supplied photo.
- The number of people and main objects has not changed.
- Pose, gaze direction, gesture, or principal spatial structure remains coherent.
- High abstraction is plainly visible; the result is not a conservative repaint.
- No game character, UI, logo, caption, watermark, or copied asset appears.
- Large value groups remain readable instead of collapsing into mud.

## Portrait double gate

Both gates must pass.

### Gate A: identity topology survives

- The person is recognizable at normal viewing size.
- Four to six selected landmark relationships remain collectively stable.
- Face axis, gaze, signature silhouette, hairstyle, facial hair, glasses, asymmetry, or another decisive attribute has not drifted collectively.
- The source expression has not become a stock grimace, glamour pose, or unrelated emotion.
- Hands and limbs remain plausible and preserve the source gesture when visible.

### Gate B: photographic surface is destroyed

- The result reads as a designed character portrait at thumbnail size, not a photograph with a paint treatment.
- The face, hair, neck, and collar have been reconstructed into five to eight designed value-color planes.
- Smooth skin gradients, camera lighting, literal complexion, realistic pores, and hair-by-hair description are absent.
- One graphic light mass and one or two shadow cavities organize the facial interior.
- At least two facial features or planes have a deliberate new hierarchy through exaggeration, compression, offset, flattening, obscuring, or merging.
- A non-natural color event passes through the face and functions compositionally.
- A visible mark family cuts against natural surface direction.
- A substantial facial, hair, jaw, neck, or shoulder edge is abbreviated, broken, or lost.
- The hair, collar, shoulder, and field act as one silhouette system rather than a clean cut-out figure.
- The uploaded background has been distilled into one source-derived field grammar with one to four irregular color masses and, only when useful, a restrained line rhythm.
- At least two field-to-figure interactions are tied to visible evidence or user-provided narrative.
- The background is neither a photographic scene nor generic smoke, splatter, or unrelated decorative geometry.
- A restrained portrait has not gained unrequested psychic anatomy, extra faces, or icon-like symbolic imagery.

Fail Gate B if the portrait is highly recognizable only because the original realistic face was preserved under texture.

## Portrait treatment conformance

- `conceptual_portrait` visibly reorganizes the face, silhouette, or portrait field beyond the restrained baseline. Any symbolic or psychic intervention is bounded, source-derived, and subordinate to identity topology.
- `restrained_character_portrait` remains an expressive designed portrait, but does not introduce unrequested extra faces, psychic anatomy, or icon-like symbolic imagery.

Fail the treatment check if the user-selected branch is ignored or if the result merely swaps in a generic abstract backdrop.

## Environment hard gates

- The core place, building, geographic formation, or natural system remains recognizable through three to six essential anchors.
- Key road, path, shoreline, entrance, horizon, silhouette, and principal mass relationships survive where relevant.
- The result reads as an environmental concept-art reconstruction rather than a photograph beneath uniform oil texture.
- A visible scene narrative and visual protagonist organize composition, atmosphere, light, and detail.
- Foreground, middle ground, and background have distinct jobs; any deliberate depth compression remains coherent.
- Weather, sky, light, and palette have been actively redesigned and are internally consistent rather than copied literally.
- Source color relationships survive while literal colors have been reconstructed into a dominant hue, shadow family, light family, and restrained accent.
- Low-information continuous fields use broader, fewer, lower-frequency marks than identity-bearing structures.
- High-information or narratively important architecture, vegetation, rock, flowers, birds, and animals retain selective directional structure.
- Primary, secondary, and lost edges are visibly differentiated.
- Repeated generic detail is compressed without erasing place identity.
- The scene has not become a generic fantasy city, ruin, or forest.
- A confirmed natural landscape has not gained invented people, buildings, roads, or symbolic props.

## Artistic checks

- One psychological proposition is visible without explanatory text.
- The technique thesis appears in color, edge, mass, deformation, and mark direction—not only in prompt language.
- Subject and world interact.
- Hard, soft, and lost edges have different jobs.
- Detail is concentrated according to visual and semantic importance rather than distributed uniformly.
- Color has a dominant family, counter-temperature, and controlled saturation.
- Brushwork varies strongly in scale and purpose: broad continuous fields, selective precise anchors.
- At least one large passage is boldly generalized.
- Accidents or fractures reinforce the viewing path or tension.
- The specific person or place dominates the style, not the reverse.

## Failure classes and targeted repairs

| Failure | Diagnose | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Different person | Several topology anchors drifted together | Restore named relationships while freezing abstraction and paint system |
| Too photographic | Face remains realistically modeled beneath texture | Rebuild the facial interior into large planes; alter feature hierarchy, color, edges, and cross-grain marks |
| Painted mask on photo | Background changed but facial structure and lighting stayed literal | Force the pressure field and non-natural color through the face, jaw, hair, and neck |
| Generic caricature | All features exaggerated uniformly | Restore the anchor constellation and keep deformation hierarchical |
| Face pasted on | Clean silhouette separates person from field | Merge selected hair, shoulder, jaw, or shadow edges into the background action |
| Literal portrait background | Uploaded scenery remains readable as photography or soft blur | Distill source walls, openings, objects, and directions into a limited semantic shape field |
| Generic abstract backdrop | Smoke, splashes, or a mechanical set of shapes has no source or figure relationship | Choose one source-derived field grammar, reduce it to one to four irregular masses, and make at least two field actions meet the head, collar, or shoulders |
| Ordinary oil filter | Uniform texture replaces authored decisions | Rebuild mass design, technique thesis, detail concentration, and relational color |
| Place unrecognizable | Landmark, horizon, or perspective was sacrificed | Restore three spatial anchors without returning photographic detail |
| Scene too faithful | Place is recognizable but atmosphere, palette, depth, and emphasis remain photographic | Increase B2.5 redesign while freezing essential anchors |
| Sky too literal | Cloudlets and sky color remain a painted copy of the source | Merge clouds into broad directional masses and redesign weather, light, and color |
| Architecture too blurry | Landmark ornament, entrance, window rhythm, capital, railing, lamp, or sign lost identity | Restore only selected identity-bearing details with finer directional marks |
| Continuous fields too busy | Sky, water, road, wall, roof, fog, snow, or lawn filled with equal small strokes | Replace local texture with fewer broad low-frequency masses |
| Nature became a soft blob | Trunks, branches, foliage clusters, ridges, flowers, birds, or animal anatomy lost structure | Restore selective directional structure without leaf-by-leaf or fur-by-fur rendering |
| Edge hierarchy absent | Every boundary is equally sharp or equally soft | Reassign primary anchors, secondary broken edges, and lost atmosphere zones |
| Depth remains photographic | Every distance layer has similar clarity and detail | Strengthen foreground entry, middle-ground meaning, and background atmospheric simplification |
| Palette remains literal | Source hues copied despite painterly texture | Reconstruct dominant, shadow, light, and accent families while preserving color relationships |
| Generic dark mood | Values collapsed and proposition vanished | Separate three or four large value groups and restore a purposeful counter-light |
| Neon fan art | Saturation is everywhere | Neutralize most regions and keep one motivated maximum |
| Random chaos | Marks lack hierarchy or erase all anchors | Remove undirected marks and align remaining fractures with the technique thesis |
| Too polished | Every edge and feature is equally resolved | Lose peripheral and secondary facial edges; enlarge marks outside the focal zone |
| Too abstract | Identity topology or spatial memory disappeared | Reinstate relationships and silhouette, not photographic surface |
| Added game imagery | Franchise iconography replaced visual logic | Remove characters, UI, typography, and logos while keeping the original paint system |
| Natural scene became ordinary oil art | Painterly texture changed but atmosphere, mark scale, and natural anchors lack hierarchy | Redesign atmosphere and depth; enlarge sky, water, or field marks; selectively restore branches, ridges, rock, flowers, birds, or animal structure without inventing human traces |

## Stop condition

After one focused repair, stop when all hard gates pass and the proposition reads. If identity and artistic departure still conflict, state which topology anchors or painterly decisions remain unresolved and ask what to prioritize in the next iteration. Never repair identity by restoring photoreal skin or smooth camera lighting.
