## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Desktop automation can perform unintended mouse, keyboard, window, or clipboard actions if used without clear approval boundaries. <br>
Mitigation: Keep failsafe and approval mode enabled, review planned actions before execution, and avoid administrator privileges unless narrowly necessary. <br>
Risk: Screenshots and clipboard reads may expose private information, passwords, or other secrets. <br>
Mitigation: Treat screen and clipboard data as sensitive, avoid automating real passwords or secrets, and limit capture to the minimum necessary scope. <br>
