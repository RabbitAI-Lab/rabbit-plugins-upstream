## Description:

Extracts structured knowledge from conversations and meetings, classifies it, and saves or exports results for knowledge management workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, independent users, and enterprise teams use this skill to turn chats or meeting transcripts into structured notes, minutes, action items, and categorized knowledge archives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad file and command authority while saving or exporting conversation and meeting content.

Mitigation: Review before installing, confirm the destination for stored notes or exports, and rely on platform-level sandboxing, audit, and access controls for sensitive chats.

Risk: Captured knowledge may include sensitive conversation details that should not be written to uncontrolled locations.

Mitigation: Use only on content approved for capture and verify output paths, export targets, and retention expectations before running.
