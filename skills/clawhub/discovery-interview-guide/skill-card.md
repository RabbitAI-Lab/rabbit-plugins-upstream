## Description: <br>
Create a structured user discovery interview guide with screener questions, a discussion guide, and a synthesis framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, founders, and researchers use this skill to prepare discovery interviews for customer discovery, Jobs-to-be-Done research, and problem validation. It helps create screeners, behaviour-focused discussion guides, and per-session synthesis templates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Interview guides and synthesis templates may contain sensitive participant or customer information if the user provides it. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive participant data unless it is intended for use in the generated guide or synthesis template. <br>
Risk: Discovery materials can bias interviews if product ideas or leading hypotheses are introduced before problem exploration. <br>
Mitigation: Review the guide against the included quality checks so questions remain past-behaviour-focused, open-ended, and solution-neutral until pain is confirmed. <br>
