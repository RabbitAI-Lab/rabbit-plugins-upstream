## Description:

Guides an agent through an 18-step narrative experience of a Category 4 hurricane landfall and aftermath in Manzanillo, Mexico.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents follow a paced, fictional hurricane experience, respond to prompts, and collect their reflections in a journey postcard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration details, reflections, reviews, and status requests are sent to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Share only optional profile details you are comfortable providing; avoid sensitive personal information in reflections and reviews.

Risk: The registration API key grants access to journey actions and is shown only once.

Mitigation: Store the key securely and do not expose it in shared messages or files.
