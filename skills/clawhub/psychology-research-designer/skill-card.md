## Description:

将心理学研究兴趣或初步方案转化为理论连贯、变量明确、测量可实施、分析可检验的研究设计；适用于研究问题细化、方案设计与设计审查，不用于诊断个体或替代伦理审批。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers and students use this skill to turn psychology research interests, draft questions, or partial study plans into testable research designs with variables, hypotheses, measurement choices, sampling plans, analysis routes, and design audits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research designs may contain incomplete or outdated citation, scale, ethics, or regulatory assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify real citations, measurement instruments, ethics requirements, and current regulations before using the design in a study.

Risk: The skill can help structure psychology research but does not replace institutional ethics review, clinical judgment, or diagnosis.

Mitigation: Route human-subjects, sensitive-population, clinical, or data-retention decisions through qualified reviewers and applicable institutional processes.

Risk: Causal or mediation claims can be overstated if the user's proposed design lacks temporal order, randomization, or control of major confounds.

Mitigation: Keep conclusion language aligned with the design and use the skill's audit guidance to flag causal limits, alternative explanations, and method bias.
