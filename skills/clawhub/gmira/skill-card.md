## Description:

Gmira guides agents through opinionated web design, implementation, and visual verification for polished websites, dashboards, e-commerce flows, course pages, portfolios, and marketing surfaces.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[othmanadi](https://clawhub.ai/user/othmanadi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers and design-focused agents use Gmira to plan, build, repair, and review web surfaces that need a clear visual direction, strong interaction craft, accessible states, and multi-viewport verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may recommend package additions, registry component imports, source edits, verification runs, or shipping steps.

Mitigation: Review and approve those actions before execution.

Risk: Strong default design guidance can conflict with user, brand, locale, accessibility, or legal requirements.

Mitigation: Require project-specific requirements to take precedence over the skill's default style rules.
