## Description:

当用户需要撰写或优化商品详情页、商品介绍和购买 FAQ 时使用。提供商品链接或商品资料，输出商品详情页结构、分段文案与购买 FAQ；可按需参考同类商品。在线商品能力支持抖音与小红书，已有资料可直接写作。来自 SocialDataX 社媒数据助手。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

电商卖家和营销人员可根据已提供的商品资料，或抖音与小红书的受支持商品信息，起草和优化商品详情页文案及购买 FAQ；内容会区分已知事实、建议表达和待补信息。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Online product lookups share query inputs with SocialDataX and may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Use supplied product information for offline drafting when possible; avoid confidential inputs and token-bearing URLs, and confirm before making paid lookups.

Risk: The documented npx @latest command can install a changing CLI version.

Mitigation: Review and pin a CLI version before use.
