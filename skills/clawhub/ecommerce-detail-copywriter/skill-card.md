## Description:

当用户需要撰写或优化商品详情页、商品介绍和购买 FAQ 时使用。提供商品链接或商品资料，输出商品详情页结构、分段文案与购买 FAQ；可按需参考同类商品。在线商品能力支持抖音与小红书，已有资料可直接写作。来自 SocialDataX 社媒数据助手。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向撰写商品详情页的用户，基于其提供的商品资料或支持的平台商品信息，生成可复制的详情页文案、购买 FAQ，并列出事实来源和待补信息。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional online lookups send product URLs, identifiers, or search terms to SocialDataX and may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Use supplied product information when it is sufficient; keep confidential inputs local and make online lookups only when needed for the requested task.

Risk: The external CLI package and its future releases were not inspected as part of the supplied artifact review.

Mitigation: Prefer a reviewed, fixed CLI version and store the API key securely.
