## Description: <br>
Report Generator helps agents turn already-computed influencer or paid-ads campaign metrics into audience-tailored marketing reports with narrative, tables, recommendations, and action items. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, growth, and client-service teams use this skill after a campaign or reporting period ends to create executive, client, team, or board-ready performance reports from provided campaign metrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Reports and campaign conclusions may be written to persistent memory, including campaign, financial, CRM, or analytics data supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in workspaces where that data is appropriate to retain, and review generated reports and promoted summary learnings before relying on them. <br>
Risk: The skill structures and narrates already-computed metrics but does not compute raw performance or ROI figures. <br>
Mitigation: Provide verified metrics or use the appropriate analysis and ROI skills before generating stakeholder-facing reports. <br>
