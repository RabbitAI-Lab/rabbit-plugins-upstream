## Description:

Pick a domain name and ending, check if it can be registered, and find where it is cheapest to register and renew, using tld.expert's free live data (no key). Use when a user is naming a product or business, asks which TLD to use (.com vs .io vs .ai), asks if a domain is available or taken, or asks what a domain costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product teams, developers, and business owners use the skill to explore suitable domain endings, check registration availability, and compare first-year and renewal prices before choosing a domain.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Naming briefs may include confidential details and are sent to tld.expert for live suggestions or checks.

Mitigation: Keep briefs generic and remove sensitive details before sending them to the external service.
