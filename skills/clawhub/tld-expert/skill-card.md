## Description:

Pick a domain name and ending, check if it can be registered, and find where it is cheapest to register and renew, using tld.expert's free live data (no key). Use when a user is naming a product or business, asks which TLD to use (.com vs .io vs .ai), asks if a domain is available or taken, or asks what a domain costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People naming a product or business use this skill to compare domain endings, check availability, and review current registration and renewal prices. It can also provide context on an ending and related brand-name checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A naming brief may be sent to tld.expert, and could contain confidential or identifying details.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for domain suggestions; avoid confidential or identifying information, and review what is sent before using the service.
