## Description:

Pick a domain name and ending, check if it can be registered, and find where it is cheapest to register and renew, using tld.expert's free live data (no key). Use when a user is naming a product or business, asks which TLD to use (.com vs .io vs .ai), asks if a domain is available or taken, or asks what a domain costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use the skill to compare domain endings, check registration status, and review current registration and renewal pricing while naming a product or business.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Names and descriptions submitted for domain research are sent to tld.expert and availability providers.

Mitigation: Use short, nonconfidential descriptions and share candidate names only if you are comfortable sending them to those services.

Risk: Availability and pricing can change, and the supplied evidence does not establish external services' retention practices or guarantee future behavior.

Mitigation: Confirm current availability and prices with the registrar before purchasing.
