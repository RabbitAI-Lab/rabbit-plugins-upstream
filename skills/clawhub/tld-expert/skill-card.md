## Description:

Helps users choose a domain name and ending, check registration availability, and compare registration and renewal prices using tld.expert's free live data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bill-makes](https://clawhub.ai/user/bill-makes)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC-BY-4.0

## Use Case:

Founders, developers, and product teams use this skill to explore domain endings, check domain availability and brand-name signals, and compare registration and renewal prices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A business-naming brief may be sent to the external tld.expert service without an explicit check that an external lookup is wanted.

Mitigation: Get approval before external lookups and submit only approved candidate names or domains; leave confidential business details out of the query.

Risk: The remote service's implementation and data-retention practices are unverified.

Mitigation: Limit lookups to non-sensitive domain research and avoid sending confidential information.
