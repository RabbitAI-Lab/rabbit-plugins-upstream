## Description:

Helps users choose domain names and endings, check live availability, and compare current registration and renewal prices using tld.expert's data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bill-makes](https://clawhub.ai/user/bill-makes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People naming a product, business, or brand use this skill to explore domain names and endings, check live registration availability, compare registration and renewal prices, and review related brand-name signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Domain naming queries are sent to tld.expert, and its data-retention practices were not established by this artifact review.

Mitigation: Use non-confidential naming descriptions.

Risk: Availability and pricing information is live and can change before a purchase.

Mitigation: Confirm availability, final prices, and renewal terms with the registrar before purchasing.
