## Description:

Pick a domain name and ending, check if it can be registered, and find where it is cheapest to register and renew, using tld.expert's free live data (no key). Use when a user is naming a product or business, asks which TLD to use (.com vs .io vs .ai), asks if a domain is available or taken, or asks what a domain costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People naming a product or business use this skill to compare domain endings, check name and domain availability, and review registration and renewal prices. It can also check a name across GitHub, npm, and live US trademark records, with results framed as research rather than legal clearance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Naming briefs may be sent to tld.expert and its registrar checks; remote service behavior and data retention were not verified.

Mitigation: Use sanitized briefs and share only names or domains you are comfortable sending to tld.expert and its registrar checks.
