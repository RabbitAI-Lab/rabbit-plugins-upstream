## Description: <br>
Queries real-time Polymarket prediction market data, including market search, trending markets, event details, odds, live sports markets, sports metadata, series, and tournament brackets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to look up public Polymarket market data, compare current probabilities, inspect events, and find sports, politics, crypto, and other prediction markets without Polymarket credentials. Outputs should be treated as informational market data, not financial advice. <br>
