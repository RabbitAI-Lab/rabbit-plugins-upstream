## Description:

Writes a UGC-style review scene in Phantra: a person telling their phone camera what they think after using a product (the verdict, the one thing they noticed, the product in use, an honest drawback), rendered against their own face and product photos, every word of the verdict theirs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and product users use this skill to turn their own experience into a short UGC-style product review video using photos they have permission to provide. It guides them to supply their own verdict, observations, and optional drawback, then review the scene and quoted cost before approving rendering.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow uses photos of a person's face and product.

Mitigation: Share only photos you have permission to provide, and check Phantra's privacy and retention terms first.

Risk: A review could include claims, ratings, or opinions the reviewer cannot support.

Mitigation: Use only the reviewer's own words and claims they can stand behind; leave out unsupported claims and ratings.

Risk: Rendering has a quoted cost.

Mitigation: Review the prompt and quoted cost, then obtain the reviewer's explicit approval before rendering.
