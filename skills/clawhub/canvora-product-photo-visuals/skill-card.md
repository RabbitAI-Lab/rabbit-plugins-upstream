## Description:

Build Canvora visuals around the user's OWN photos — real product shots, team headshots, venue or event photos embedded into the design instead of generic stock imagery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[canvora](https://clawhub.ai/user/canvora)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and design teams use this skill to create branded visuals featuring their real products, people, venues, or event photos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can spend account credits without approval.

Mitigation: Set an explicit total credit budget and require approval before exceeding it.

Risk: Uploaded photos may contain personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Upload only photos the user may share with Canvora, and review Canvora's privacy policy before sending sensitive images.
