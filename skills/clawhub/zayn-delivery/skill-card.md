## Description: <br>
把采购、工程、质检、包装、物流和客户交期要求整理成可执行的交付计划、关键路径和预警节点。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Order and delivery teams use this skill to turn purchasing, engineering, QC, packaging, logistics, delivery dates, and owner inputs into an executable delivery plan with a critical path and warning points. It helps identify missing, conflicting, or unverified inputs before producing a formal delivery analysis. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may structure delivery plans from incomplete or unverified order, QC, packaging, logistics, or responsibility inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Use complete, human-verified inputs and keep missing, conflicting, or pending facts clearly marked before treating the output as a formal delivery plan. <br>
Risk: Delivery timing proposals may be mistaken for firm customer or logistics commitments. <br>
Mitigation: Route payment execution, customer communications, order completeness checks, and final commitments through the proper dedicated process or skill. <br>
