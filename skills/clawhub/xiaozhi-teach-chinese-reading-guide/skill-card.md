## Description:

Helps Chinese-language teachers design modern-text reading lessons with text analysis, reading strategies, discussion tasks, and reading-progress frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 teachers use this skill to plan modern-text reading lessons, scaffold discussion and comparative reading, and prepare teacher-reviewed reading activities and progress records. It provides teaching frameworks rather than replacing a teacher's judgment or supplying full textbook copies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localized crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, and student-data controls.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student reading records may contain personal or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use aliases and obtain school or platform consent and retention controls before handling real student data.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and legal assumptions may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts and legal assumptions before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Generated questions or lesson guidance may be unsuitable or inaccurate for a class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check generated activities against the source text and student level before classroom use.
