## Description:

Helps Chinese-language teachers plan modern-text reading lessons through text interpretation, reading strategies, tiered tasks, and teacher-reviewed reading progress records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language primary and secondary school teachers use this skill to draft reading lesson frameworks, discussion questions, differentiated tasks, and reading-progress records for their review. It does not replace teacher judgment or provide full textbook copies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland-focused; localize curriculum, privacy rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reading records or student evaluations may expose sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Obtain appropriate consent, confirm record changes with the teacher, and check sharing permissions before producing parent-facing content.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy, or crisis-support assumptions may not fit deployments outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these settings and contacts before deployment, and require teacher context before routing general reading requests to the skill.

Risk: Draft teaching material may contain unsuitable tasks or reproduce copyrighted textbook content.

Mitigation: Have teachers review drafts and retain only citations or indexes for restricted textbook passages rather than copying full texts.
