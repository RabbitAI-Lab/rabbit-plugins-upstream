## Description:

阅读教学指导 helps Chinese-language teachers design modern prose reading instruction through text interpretation, reading-strategy selection, staged practice, task design, and class reading-progress records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External Chinese-language teachers use this skill to turn a modern reading text into a teachable framework: literal, deeper, and extended interpretation; reading-strategy practice; staged class tasks; and teacher-confirmed reading ability records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland by default; review local curriculum, school policy, emergency contacts, and student data consent rules before use in other regions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student reading records may contain personal or education data.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers, honor view/correct/delete/export controls, and enable long-term profiles, cross-skill sharing, teacher writeback, or parent-visible summaries only with the required authorization.

Risk: Generated questions, discussion prompts, or reading tasks could be inaccurate or mismatched to the source text or grade band.

Mitigation: Apply the bundled AI item self-check and require teacher confirmation before classroom use, homework use, or resource-library entry.

Risk: Reading selections may include copyrighted textbook or teaching-aid material.

Mitigation: Require copyrightStatus labeling and store textbook, teaching-aid, and full-book content as source indexes only rather than copied body text.

Risk: Student writing or discussion may reveal self-harm, abuse, bullying, or other safety signals outside ordinary learning support.

Mitigation: Stop the teaching workflow, avoid low-sensitivity reframing, follow the crisis referral protocol, localize emergency contacts by region, and record only the referral action.
