## Description:

AdsTurbo helps agents run multi-step media creation workflows for digital-human spokesperson videos, video generation and extension, ad cloning, video enhancement and translation, image generation, and e-commerce creative assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to plan and run AdsTurbo creative workflows, such as cloning an ad, swapping a spokesperson, translating the result, creating images, and checking asynchronous job status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill exposes high-impact likeness, voice, watermark-removal, ad-cloning, and public-upload workflows that can affect consent, privacy, and rights.

Mitigation: Use only media, voices, faces, brands, and ads the user owns or is authorized to modify, and review requests before execution.

Risk: Uploaded media may contain confidential, personal, regulated, or third-party content that is not suitable for public exposure or AdsTurbo processing.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading that content unless public exposure and AdsTurbo processing are acceptable.
