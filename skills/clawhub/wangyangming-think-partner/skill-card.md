## Description:

Uses Wang Yangming's philosophy to help people identify barriers to action, reflect on their values, and find practical next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to reflect on procrastination, difficult choices, workplace challenges, and personal direction through Wang Yangming's ideas, then define a small practical action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reflective prompts could be mistaken for a definitive answer to a high-impact personal, business, or investment decision.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a draft for reflection; check facts and have qualified, authorized people review consequential decisions before acting.

Risk: Prompts about personal, financial, health, or confidential business matters may involve sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary details and redact identifying or sensitive information, as the skill recommends.
