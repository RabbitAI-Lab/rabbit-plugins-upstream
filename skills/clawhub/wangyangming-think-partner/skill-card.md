## Description:

Offers Wang Yangming-inspired reflection prompts and practical first steps for procrastination, difficult choices, and interpersonal challenges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People seeking reflective guidance use the skill to examine obstacles to action, consider values in difficult decisions, and identify a small next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Philosophical reflection could be mistaken for professional advice in consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general reflection guidance; consult qualified professionals for financial, legal, medical, or mental-health decisions.
