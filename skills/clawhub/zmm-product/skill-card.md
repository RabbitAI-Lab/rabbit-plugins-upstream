## Description:

Helps users turn scattered experience, resources, and existing work into a concrete deliverable that can be packaged and sold.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and solo operators use this Chinese-language product-coaching workflow to assess whether their existing experience, repeated actions, or paid work can become a clear product. The skill guides them through product-shape checks, anti-copying and anti-feature-bloat tests, and a falsifiable validation action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may store rejected product directions and methods that worked, which could expose confidential business plans if agent memory is retained.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business plans unless the host agent's memory storage is acceptable, or ask the agent not to persist details for the session.

Risk: The product assessment may influence commercial direction and can be wrong or incomplete if required local references or user context are unavailable.

Mitigation: Review the resulting product definition and validation action before acting on it, and treat missing required references as a reason to pause rather than infer.
