## Description:

Writes the first shot of a Phantra scene so it holds attention (an arresting image, a turn or a reveal in the first seconds) and puts it into the scene's first timed shot, reading `what_works` and `score_caption` as evidence rather than verdicts. Use when someone asks for a hook, says the opening is slow, wants the first seconds rewritten, or asks for a caption. NOT for the whole scene's shape (write-scene), the cover image (thumbnails) or the platform's shape and length (platform-delivery).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Phantra users use this skill to rewrite a scene's opening into a short, attention-grabbing first shot, using their own studio history as evidence when available. They can also use it to draft and assess a caption based on their own claims and posted-video evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Replacing a saved scene detaches its existing clips.

Mitigation: Show the complete replacement and get approval first; use duplicate_scene when preserving the original and its clips matters.

Risk: Rendering may incur a charge.

Mitigation: Review the quote and get approval before approving paid rendering.
