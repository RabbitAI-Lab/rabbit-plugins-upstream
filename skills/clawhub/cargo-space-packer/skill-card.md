## Description:

Helps plan cargo fit, loading order, and tie-down checklists for pickups, trailers, and moving trucks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People planning a household move or cargo haul use this skill to estimate space needs, arrange items, and prepare a loading and securing checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Heuristic fit estimates and load maps may not reflect actual vehicle capacity or safe axle loading.

Mitigation: Use as a rough planning aid; verify vehicle payload and axle ratings and follow manufacturer instructions before travel.

Risk: Suggested strap counts may not adequately secure cargo under applicable requirements.

Mitigation: Check tie-down and anchor Working Load Limits, applicable cargo-securement laws, and the condition of straps and anchor points; do not treat the suggestions as certified calculations.
