## Description:

Guides developers through a user-confirmed software concept design and produces a structured design record.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to turn requirements into confirmed concept inventories, application scenarios, dependencies, and a design record with concept specifications and synchronizations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified assumptions could become misleading design requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm alignment with the user and label inferences and open decisions before relying on the design record.

Risk: Design documentation could be written before the user agrees to its scope.

Mitigation: Review the proposed scope and obtain confirmation before writing project documentation.
