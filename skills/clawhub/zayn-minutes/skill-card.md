## Description: <br>
将会议内容整理为已确认结论、行动项、责任人、截止时间、待确认事项和后续节点。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and teams use this skill to turn Chinese-language meeting records into structured minutes with confirmed conclusions, action items, owners, deadlines, open questions, risks, and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incomplete or inaccurate meeting records can produce misleading minutes or omit unresolved items. <br>
Mitigation: Require the meeting record, topic, attendees, objective, and at least one reliable evidence item; ask for missing critical information before formal analysis. <br>
Risk: Discussion may be mistaken for a confirmed decision, or owners and deadlines may be assigned without support. <br>
Mitigation: Keep conclusions separate from discussion, preserve open questions, and avoid inventing owners, deadlines, or decisions that are not in the meeting record. <br>
