## Description:

Predicts ornamental and cut-flower bloom timing from bud images or video and optional temperature and light data, estimating a full-bloom date 3–7 days ahead.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Growers and horticulture teams use the skill to estimate full-bloom timing from bud images or video, optionally with accumulated temperature and light readings, to inform pollination, harvest, and visitor planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags account and token management, remote identity or media handling, and pet or health analysis behavior that does not match the flower-date prediction purpose; the scan is partial and leaves some imported behavior unassessed.

Mitigation: Ask the publisher to explain these behaviors, the ignored export-only option, and the undeclared tool scope, and review the incomplete scan findings before installation.
