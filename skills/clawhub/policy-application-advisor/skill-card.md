## Description:

Supports end-to-end policy application assistance for park enterprises through policy matching, project evaluation, material preparation, form-filling support, human confirmation gates, and delivery tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perrykono-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/perrykono-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business, operations, and policy-support teams use this skill to assess policy opportunities, prepare application drafts and checklists, validate required materials, and track application stages while keeping final review and submission with the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The assistant may read relevant local or workspace business and application data while helping with policy applications.

Mitigation: Install only in workspaces where that access is acceptable and keep sensitive business, finance, and tax data subject to user review and handling controls.

Risk: Generated drafts, source dates, subsidy estimates, or application guidance may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Require human review before relying on drafts, estimates, record updates, or any final policy application submission.
