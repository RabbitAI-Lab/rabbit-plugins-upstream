## Description:

Checks whether and how a Notifly notification reached a subscriber across email, SMS, push, chat, and in-app channels without sending messages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Support teams and developers use this skill to investigate a subscriber's notification activity, channel availability, and delivery status without triggering a test send.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Troubleshooting requires read access to subscriber profiles, channel settings, workflows, and notification activity in the selected Notifly environment.

Mitigation: Grant access only to an environment whose notification data the agent is authorized to inspect, and confirm the selected environment when results appear empty.
