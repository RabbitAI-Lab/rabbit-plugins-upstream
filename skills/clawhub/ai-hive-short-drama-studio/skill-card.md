## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may upload creative assets or reference material without sufficient rights for people, voices, music, trademarks, products, novels, or videos.

Mitigation: Confirm rights and authorization before upload or generation, and keep copyright, platform-rule, and pending-confirmation checks in the deliverable.

Risk: Paid AI-HIVE generation may incur costs or duplicate charges if tasks are submitted without approval or retried after local timeouts.

Mitigation: Require user approval before paid generation, review model, price, and deliverable details, capture price snapshots and task IDs, and query existing task status before resubmitting.

Risk: Reference-based remixing or popular-structure replication could copy protected characters, lines, scenes, materials, or misleading endorsements.

Mitigation: Use references only for abstract structure, rhythm, and composition; rewrite characters, dialogue, scenes, and shot expression, and avoid false claims about people, products, certifications, prices, or platform partnerships.
