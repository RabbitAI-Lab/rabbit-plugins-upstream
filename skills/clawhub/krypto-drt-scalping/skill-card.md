## Use Case:

External users and trading-strategy reviewers use this skill as a local markdown reference for evaluating a crypto DRT scalping plan, its backtest claims, risk rules, trade windows, and instrument priorities. It is guidance only and does not execute trades or connect to trading services.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact contains financial strategy content and historical backtest claims that could be mistaken for expected future performance.

Mitigation: Review the backtest claims independently and preserve the artifact's warning that historical results are not a guarantee of future performance.

Risk: Users could connect the strategy guidance to real trading automation without separate review.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as non-executing guidance and avoid live trading automation unless the strategy, controls, and integrations receive an independent review.
