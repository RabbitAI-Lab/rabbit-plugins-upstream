## Description:

A Chinese-language self-coaching skill that guides users through major leadership and decision-making challenges using Robert Iger-inspired leadership principles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users can use this Chinese-language skill to reflect on leadership, team management, creative management, and major decisions through one-question-at-a-time coaching prompts. It structures self-reflection and does not make decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on reflective leadership prompts for legal, financial, HR, or crisis decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for self-reflection only and seek qualified professional advice for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad leadership and decision-making terms in general management conversations.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants Chinese-language leadership self-coaching before applying the one-question-at-a-time flow.
