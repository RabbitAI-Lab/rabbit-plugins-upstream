## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks the agent to maintain an ongoing profile of a named person's reasoning over time. <br>
Mitigation: Install only with the named user's informed consent and provide clear controls to review, disable, and delete Evolution Log entries. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes personal employer and project details. <br>
Mitigation: Remove or generalize personal employer and project details before publishing, sharing, or installing outside the intended private context. <br>
Risk: A personalized teaching profile may be applied outside its intended user context. <br>
Mitigation: Scope activation to this named profile and require explicit selection or confirmation before applying it in unrelated conversations. <br>
