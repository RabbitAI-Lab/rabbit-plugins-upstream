## Description:

Audio Generation helps agents create CellCog-powered speech, voiceovers, cloned/avatar voices, sound effects, music, podcasts, and dialogue from text prompts using OpenAI, ElevenLabs, and MiniMax providers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers, creators, and agent users use this skill to generate narration, dialogue, avatar voices, sound effects, music, and other production audio through CellCog. It is intended for prompt-driven audio workflows where users choose a provider and provide scripts, style instructions, or sound descriptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audio prompts and voice samples may be sent to CellCog and its providers.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive audio or scripts unless the user has reviewed CellCog's retention, deletion, and provider-sharing terms.

Risk: Cloned or avatar voices can be misused without the voice owner's consent.

Mitigation: Use cloned voices only for the user's own voice or where clear permission from the voice owner has been obtained.

Risk: Generated audio may be unsuitable for publication without review.

Mitigation: Review generated speech, music, and sound effects before distribution, especially for attribution, consent, and audience-impact concerns.
