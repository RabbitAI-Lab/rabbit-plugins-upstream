# Pre-launch store check

Use this guide when the user is about to launch a store or start running ads, asks what could get their store, payments, or ads suspended, or is starting dropshipping from scratch.

## Starting from scratch

If the user has no store yet, answer what they asked in a few lines: they list products in their own store, a customer buys, they order from a supplier who ships to the customer, and they keep what is left after product, shipping, fees, and ads. Do not state Shopify or other platform prices or trial terms; send them to the platform's pricing page. Then give the next concrete steps:

1. Pick a few test products using the supplier checks in [suppliers.md](suppliers.md), including the legal and shipping checks.
2. Work out the price and the break-even ad cost with [profit.md](profit.md) before building anything.
3. Build the store and run the check below before the first ad.

## How to run the check

Ask for the store address and the countries they sell to. If you can open web pages, review the store yourself: the home page, a product page, the policy pages, the footer, and checkout up to payment. Otherwise ask the user to paste the policy pages and one product page.

Report a summary first (the number of items to fix, and the three that matter most), then each item as OK, Fix, or Can't check, with the exact fix. Put items that can stop payments or ads at the top.

## Checklist

**Business and contact information**
- Real, consistent business name and contact details, matching the payment and ad accounts. Google treats false identity or contact information as misrepresentation (see [rules.md](rules.md)).
- Where orders ship from is not hidden or presented as local when it is not.

**Shipping and returns**
- A shipping policy with processing time plus delivery time per country, matching what the supplier's shipping method actually achieves, and the same times on product pages.
- All shipping costs shown before checkout.
- A returns policy: the return window, conditions, who pays return shipping, the return address, and when refunds are paid. International return windows should start at delivery, not purchase.
- For customers in the EU or UK: the 14-day right to withdraw from delivery, and in the EU refunds within 14 days of being told. From June 19, 2026, EU customers must also have an online "withdraw from contract here" function throughout the withdrawal period (see [rules.md](rules.md)). Check whether the store's platform or an app provides it.
- Privacy policy and terms of service pages.

**Prices**
- For EU consumers, prices shown include all taxes and charges.
- Compare-at prices are real former prices; in the EU, a price reduction shows the lowest price of the previous 30 days (see [rules.md](rules.md)).
- No countdown timers that restart or stock warnings that are not true.

**Product pages**
- No claims or certifications the supplier cannot document, and the required EU information on listings for EU customers (see [listing.md](listing.md)).
- Images without watermarks or promotional overlays (Google, see [rules.md](rules.md)).
- Only genuine reviews. The FTC rule covers fake reviews, buying reviews conditioned on what they say, and suppressing negative ones (see [rules.md](rules.md)).

**Products**
- No other brands' names, logos, or designs used without the right to do so, and no replicas.
- Products with batteries, liquids, powders, or magnets checked against shipping restrictions.
- For the EU, an EU responsible person for each product; for US children's products, a Children's Product Certificate (see [rules.md](rules.md)).
- Costs include duties on parcels to the US where they apply (see [rules.md](rules.md)).

**Payments**
- A test order placed from start to finish, then refunded.
- Cash set aside to pay suppliers for the first weeks: PayPal can hold a new seller's first payments for up to 21 days, released sooner once tracking shows delivery, and Shopify Payments can apply reserves (see [rules.md](rules.md)).
- Tracking numbers added to every store order, so the payment provider sees them.

**Ads**
- The product in each ad is the one on its landing page (Meta, see [rules.md](rules.md)), and every claim in the ad is supported on the product page.
- The break-even ad cost per order is known before spending (see [profit.md](profit.md)).

## With DSers connected

When the user asks what to do next, check instead of asking, in this order: connected stores, then products in DSers, then orders. Stop at the first one that is missing. A failed or incomplete read does not mean "none"; say what could not be checked.

| What the check found | Next step |
|---|---|
| No connected store | Follow "Other DSers tasks" in [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) to get a store connected, then check again. |
| Store connected, no products | Find and import products: [suppliers.md](suppliers.md). |
| Products, no orders yet | Check prices and product pages ([profit.md](profit.md), [listing.md](listing.md)), then run the check above. |
| Orders exist | Order processing is not covered by this guide; follow "Other DSers tasks" in [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). |
