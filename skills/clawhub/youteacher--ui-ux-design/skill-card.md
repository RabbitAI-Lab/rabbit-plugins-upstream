## Description:

Helps turn web and app product goals into UI/UX design plans and acceptance checklists, with implementation or review when requested.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[youteacher](https://clawhub.ai/user/youteacher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and developers use this skill to plan, review, and implement accessible, responsive web and app interfaces and to obtain UI/UX acceptance checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product and design details sent to the AI Skills platform may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share only approved details and review the platform's data handling before use.

Risk: A configured API key could be exposed in chat, logs, or shared artifacts.

Mitigation: Store the key in OpenClaw configuration and never include its full value in outputs.

Risk: Generating a design plan may deduct platform balance.

Mitigation: Check current pricing and inform the user before calling the paid operation.
