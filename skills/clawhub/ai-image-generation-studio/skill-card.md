## Description:

Create and refine images from a written brief, one to four ordered reference images, or an existing base image. AI Image Generation Studio supports text-to-image generation, reference-guided composition, and focused AI image editing for product photos, ad creative, brand visuals, posters, social graphics, illustrations, concept art, and photo background changes. Review each generated image for message, subject fidelity, composition, style, text, and destination fit, then choose the smallest useful edit, new composition, or new generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting grants account permissions for music, speech, and voice management in addition to images, and device analytics are reported automatically.

Mitigation: Review and accept these permissions and analytics before connecting; revoke access in the Beatra Console if they are not acceptable.

Risk: Supplied images are sent to Beatra for processing, and image generation or editing requests may be paid.

Mitigation: Use only images you are comfortable sending for processing and confirm each paid request before submission.

Risk: Malformed installed-skill inventory during cleanup can disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Check other installed Beatra skills before uninstalling, and verify their connections afterward.
