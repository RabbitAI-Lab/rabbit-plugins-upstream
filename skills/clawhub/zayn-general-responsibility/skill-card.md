## Description: <br>
Assesses responsibility boundaries for product, service, or project issues using contracts, scope, acceptance criteria, delivery records, customer cooperation, third-party dependencies, and causal evidence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, support teams, and delivery teams use this skill to structure evidence-based responsibility assessments before deciding whether responsibility is undetermined, shared, internal, customer, or third-party. It helps identify confirmed facts, contract and duty boundaries, causal links, evidence gaps, risks, internal posture, and next steps without treating the output as a legal ruling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the assessment as legal advice or as authorization to admit liability or promise compensation. <br>
Mitigation: Require review by legal counsel or an authorized internal owner before making admissions, commitments, or compensation decisions. <br>
Risk: Responsibility assessments can involve contracts, claims, incident timelines, and other sensitive evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only necessary facts, redact unrelated sensitive details, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal, contractual, or commercial information. <br>
Risk: Missing contract boundaries, timelines, or causal evidence can lead to premature responsibility conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Use a preliminary status when core evidence is missing and make evidence gaps explicit before confirming responsibility. <br>
