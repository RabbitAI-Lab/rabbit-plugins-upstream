## Description: <br>
Helps agent users and skill authors plan, validate, and produce practical browser automation and work-productivity workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Support work-productivity and Agent Browser-style requests by clarifying outcomes, creating concise plans or artifacts, producing checklists, workflow outlines, code changes, shell commands, or configuration guidance, and validating the result against the user's success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, subject to the user's local laws, organizational policies, and platform terms. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger scope and implicit invocation can cause the skill to activate for general browser, automation, CLI, or bug-fix requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review invocation settings and narrow triggers or disable implicit invocation when deploying in environments where accidental activation would be disruptive. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow, code, shell command, or configuration guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs before execution, test changes locally, and confirm assumptions, constraints, and success criteria before relying on the result. <br>
