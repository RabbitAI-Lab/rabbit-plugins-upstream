## Description: <br>
Book Meditation helps an agent search for meditation services through Lokuli, check availability, and create bookings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[edwardrodriguez703-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/edwardrodriguez703-design) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to find meditation services through Lokuli, check provider availability, and create bookings when they are ready to share appointment and contact details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking requests can disclose personal contact and appointment details to Lokuli. <br>
Mitigation: Use this skill only for explicit provider search, availability checks, or booking requests, and confirm before sending name, email, phone number, zip code, or appointment details. <br>
