## Description:

A Chinese B2B foreign-trade copywriting assistant that drafts multilingual outreach, quotations, follow-ups, customer assessments, and related trade communications with anti-fabrication and culture-adaptation rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guoren1123](https://clawhub.ai/user/guoren1123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External exporters, factory foreign-trade teams, SOHO sellers, and B2B sales staff use this skill to draft multilingual sales outreach, quotations, follow-ups, customer assessments, social posts, and GEO service responses for foreign-trade workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Chinese email-writing activation may invoke the skill for requests outside foreign trade.

Mitigation: Review the generated output when the user request is not clearly about foreign-trade sales, quotations, follow-up, customer assessment, or related B2B export workflows.

Risk: Generated business, legal, certification, or market claims could be sent externally without enough verification.

Mitigation: Confirm factual claims, certifications, pricing, legal terms, and market statements against user-provided evidence or approved business sources before sending.
