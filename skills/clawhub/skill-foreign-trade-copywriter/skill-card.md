## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer background checks and risk assessments may involve privacy-sensitive business or personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary business information, avoid sensitive personal data unless there is a lawful basis, and verify due-diligence outputs against authoritative sources.

Risk: Generated customer-risk, credit, legal, or commercial assessments may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Do not use model output as the sole basis for credit, legal, or high-stakes business decisions; require human review before action.

Risk: Marketing or sales copy can become misleading if the model invents data, certifications, company names, or contacts.

Mitigation: Use the skill's artifact behavior that relies on user-provided information and substitutes qualitative wording when quantitative or company-specific facts are not provided.
