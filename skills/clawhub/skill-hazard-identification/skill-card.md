## Use Case: <br>
EHS practitioners, safety reviewers, and workplace operations teams use this skill to identify environmental factors and workplace hazards in manufacturing, laboratory, warehouse, and limited office settings. It supports checklist-style analysis, risk ranking, and draft mitigation/report content, but does not replace certified testing or legal compliance review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat checklist-style hazard output as a certified safety assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a drafting and review aid; rely on qualified professionals and official measurements for regulated hazards, quantitative exposure testing, radiation, confined-space work, and legal compliance decisions. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or vague site information can lead to partial or uncertain hazard identification. <br>
Mitigation: Collect the workplace type, area list, known substances, equipment, and contradictory details before relying on the report; keep [需补充] items open until resolved. <br>
