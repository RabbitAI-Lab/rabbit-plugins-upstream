## Description:

Drafts incident postmortems from Uptimely incident timelines, monitor history, and alerts, and saves the approved text or summarizes incidents over a period.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Incident responders and operations teams use this skill to document an outage from Uptimely records, review a draft before saving it, or summarize incidents from a selected period.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incident records, monitor history, or alerts may contain sensitive operational details.

Mitigation: Review the source records and draft before sharing or saving the postmortem.

Risk: An inaccurate postmortem may become part of the incident record.

Mitigation: Check the timeline and conclusions against Uptimely records, fill unsupported causes only with user input, and save only after explicit approval of the exact text.
