## Description:

Generates e-commerce sales copy and video scripts from product details or a reference video, including spoken pitches, product recommendations, reviews, and dramatic scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and marketers use the skill to create sales scripts from product images and selling points, or derive a script from a reference video link.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images, details, and reference video links may be submitted to an external service.

Mitigation: Share only approved material and confirm the intended China or international account configuration before use.

Risk: Installing QHKit and changing the shell PATH can affect the local environment.

Mitigation: Review the npm package before installing and avoid persistent PATH changes unless needed.
