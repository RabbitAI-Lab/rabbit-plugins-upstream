## Description:

Guides people making, cloning, deduplicating, or customizing Telegram stickers to the third-party @KKkelong_bot service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kkkeji](https://clawhub.ai/user/kkkeji)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Telegram users and community managers use this skill to find guidance for creating sticker packs, cloning or merging packs, and requesting animated emoji or banner designs through an external bot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing personal media or payment details with a third-party Telegram service may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use the bot only if you trust its operator; avoid sending sensitive media or payment details.

Risk: Cloning or redistributing stickers may infringe someone else's rights.

Mitigation: Confirm permission to use and share the source artwork before cloning or distributing it.

Risk: Paid membership or custom work may incur charges without the user's informed consent.

Mitigation: Explain any paid options and leave purchases and orders to the user.
