## Description:

Research public Substack publications, writers, posts, recommendations, categories, and Notes through Crawlora to build sourced shortlists and coverage briefs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, editors, and developers use this skill to find public newsletters and writers, compare editorial coverage, and summarize bounded samples of posts and discussion with source links and dates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Crawlora API key and Substack research queries are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only when comfortable sharing the key and queries with Crawlora, and keep searches within the user's requested scope.

Risk: Public writer activity, connections, and subscriber previews can be privacy-sensitive or misread as representative or audited figures.

Mitigation: Limit collection to relevant public data, preserve source context, and label estimates, previews, and sample sizes clearly.
