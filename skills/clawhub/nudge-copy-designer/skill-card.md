## Description:

Designs behavior-economics-based nudge copy and choice-architecture suggestions for prompts, signs, defaults, app guidance, policy messaging, and public-space interventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and content designers use this skill to turn behavioral economics principles into practical copy, default choices, environmental adjustments, and measurable evaluation plans for nudging beneficial behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive nudge copy could become manipulative, opaque, or misaligned with user or public benefit.

Mitigation: Keep interventions transparent, non-coercive, and tied to outcomes that benefit the audience or the public.

Risk: Generated social-norm, loss-framing, or impact claims could be inaccurate if not grounded in real measurements.

Mitigation: Review factual claims before publication and validate interventions with A/B tests or other measurable behavior-change indicators.
