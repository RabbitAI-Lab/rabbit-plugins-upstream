## Description:

Make an AI short film or vertical short drama with the PopNow API: logline to script, character sheets, consistent multi-shot storyboard, shot-by-shot video with dialogue or voiceover, music and an assembled cut. Quotes every step in USD first. Use when the user wants a short film, AI movie, micro-drama episode, trailer or story video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External filmmakers and creators use this skill to develop a short film or vertical drama from a logline through a script, storyboard, generated shots, dialogue or voiceover, and music. It can return an assembled film or ordered media links for editing.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Multi-step media generation can incur paid API charges.

Mitigation: Review the complete USD quote and approve it before generation; use a dedicated API key with model restrictions and spending caps.

Risk: Prompts and reference URLs are shared with PopNow and the generation providers.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive material and submit only content the user has rights to use.

Risk: This review did not verify the service's billing guarantees or retention practices.

Mitigation: Check current service terms and retention practices before sharing material or relying on billing limits.
