## Description:

Drafts US subscription renewal notices with pricing, renewal dates, auto-renewal terms, cancellation instructions, and email-rule checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External subscription businesses and SaaS teams use it to draft renewal emails, cancellation instructions, and review notes before a person verifies billing details and applicable US requirements and sends the notice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The calculator is incomplete and its report is not a verified renewal total or compliance approval.

Mitigation: Verify amounts against billing records and independently check current legal requirements and the customer's state before delivery.

Risk: The optional Windows app is unsigned and separate from the skill bundle.

Mitigation: Assess it separately before use; the supplied hash alone does not establish that it is safe.
