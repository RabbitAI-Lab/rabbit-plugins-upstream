## Description:

Guides an agent through a 14-step interactive narrative about waiting for a rocket launch at Boca Chica Beach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agent users follow a paced, 1–2-week story about the South Texas coast and a rocket launch, optionally sharing reflections that become a postcard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A remote account and token are needed to use the hosted experience.

Mitigation: Review the service before signing up; use a dedicated drifts.bot token and do not reuse sensitive credentials.

Risk: Optional profile details and reflections may be sent to and stored by the service without clear retention or deletion controls.

Mitigation: Leave optional personal fields blank unless comfortable sharing them, and avoid sensitive personal reflections.
