### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags that the skill may publish and close merge requests in a fixed GitLab project without clear user authorization.

Mitigation: Use only for the intended project; require approval of publication content and closure candidates, use project-limited credentials, and review the separately referenced gitlab-agent skill.
