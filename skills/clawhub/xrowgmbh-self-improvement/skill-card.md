## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create or close merge requests in a live GitLab project while presenting itself as personal self-improvement.

Mitigation: Review carefully before installing, use only a narrowly scoped GitLab token, and require explicit approval before GitLab write actions.
