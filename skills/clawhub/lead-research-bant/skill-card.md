## Description: <br>
B2B lead research and BANT rating skill that uses public web search to collect company intelligence, identify decision makers, score Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline, and produce an actionable sales follow-up report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, SDR, and customer success teams use this skill to research target companies or domains, qualify B2B leads with BANT scoring, and prepare outreach recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public web searches may expose confidential go-to-market plans, private customer lists, or sensitive product details if those are supplied as context. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential sales strategy, private customer data, or sensitive product details unless that context is acceptable for use in search queries. <br>
Risk: Lead scores and recommended actions may be based on incomplete public information or explicitly marked inferences. <br>
Mitigation: Review source coverage, unavailable fields, inferred claims, and recommended outreach before using the report for sales decisions. <br>
