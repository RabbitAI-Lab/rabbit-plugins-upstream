## Description:

Explains chemistry through text-based particle diagrams and bounded analogies, connecting visible phenomena with atoms, molecules, ions, and chemical symbols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to reason about chemistry concepts through particle-level explanations, diagrams, and checks for misconceptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (adapt crisis contacts, curriculum, consent rules, and language for other regions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis resources and school guidance may be inappropriate outside the intended locale.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts, curriculum, consent rules, and language before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: A student's learning profile could be accessed without appropriate permission.

Mitigation: Confirm profile-memory consent before reading any long-term learning data; otherwise keep the interaction session-only.

Risk: Chemistry examples may prompt unsafe experiments at home.

Mitigation: Direct hands-on experiments to supervised school labs rather than home use of heat or chemicals.
