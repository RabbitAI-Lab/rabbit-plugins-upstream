## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking chemistry students explain visible phenomena using particle diagrams, careful analogies, and chemical symbols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to build particle-level explanations of middle- and high-school chemistry, including conservation of mass, dissolution, bonding, and equilibrium. It is not intended to solve numerical problems or guide hands-on experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China emergency contacts, child-safety guidance, and curriculum assumptions may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Deploy for the intended Mainland China K–12 audience; localize emergency contacts, safeguarding guidance, laws, and curriculum before use elsewhere.

Risk: Reading a student's learning profile without appropriate consent could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Have the host platform enforce profile and cross-skill sharing consent, including agreement for the current session; otherwise keep explanations session-only.

Risk: Chemistry analogies or particle sketches can lead to misconceptions if taken literally.

Mitigation: State each analogy's limits, relate the model back to observations and symbols, and ask the student to explain it in their own words.

Risk: Students may seek unsafe home experiments or disclose an immediate personal crisis.

Mitigation: Keep experiments to supervised school settings; for crisis signals, stop tutoring and direct the student to a trusted adult and locally appropriate urgent help.
