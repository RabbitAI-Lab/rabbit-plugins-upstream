## Description:

Explains chemistry through age-appropriate particle diagrams and analogies that connect observable phenomena with atoms, molecules, ions, and chemical notation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students and their educators use this skill to understand chemical phenomena through particle-level explanations, analogies, and comprehension checks. It covers foundational chemistry and selected high school topics rather than numerical problem solving or experimental procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12 chemistry curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent rules may not suit other regions or languages.

Mitigation: Localize these safeguards before using the skill outside Chinese-speaking mainland China K12 settings.

Risk: Student learning profiles contain minors' information.

Mitigation: Verify that the host enforces profile and cross-skill sharing consent; otherwise keep tutoring within the current session without reading a profile.

Risk: Chemistry examples could be mistaken for instructions to conduct unsafe experiments at home.

Mitigation: Keep experiments to classroom observation and refer hands-on work to supervised school laboratory instruction.
