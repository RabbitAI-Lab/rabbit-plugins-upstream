## Description:

Helps chemistry learners explain phenomena by visualizing particles and connecting observations, particle models, and chemical symbols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school chemistry learners use this skill to understand particle-level explanations of chemical phenomena, including reactions, conservation of mass, dissolution, bonding, and equilibrium. It uses text particle diagrams, bounded analogies, and misconception checks to connect observations with chemical symbols.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personalized explanations may use a learner's chemistry records when memory is enabled.

Mitigation: Keep session-only mode as the default; enable memory only with the learner's consent after reviewing the learning-record dependency and platform storage and sharing controls.

Risk: Chemistry demonstrations can be unsafe if learners try procedures at home.

Mitigation: Keep this skill to explaining classroom observations and direct experiment requests to supervised school-lab guidance; do not recommend heating or chemical handling at home.
