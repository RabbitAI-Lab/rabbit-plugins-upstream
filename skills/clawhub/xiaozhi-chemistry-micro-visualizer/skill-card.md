## Description:

Explains chemistry phenomena to Chinese-speaking K–12 students using text-based particle diagrams and bounded analogies, from molecules and conservation of mass to introductory ionization and electron transfer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to explain chemistry at the particle level, correct common misconceptions, and connect observable changes to particle diagrams and chemical symbols. It covers middle-school chemistry and introductory high-school topics, not numerical problem solving or laboratory instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent rules for minors outside the intended Chinese-language setting.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles may contain minors' personal learning information.

Mitigation: Use session-only explanations by default; access profiles only with consent and honor pause, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent requirements may not apply across regions.

Mitigation: Localize these elements before offering the skill to students in other regions or languages.

Risk: Chemistry analogies may create misconceptions or prompt unsafe home experiments.

Mitigation: State each analogy's limits and refer hands-on experiments to supervised school settings.
