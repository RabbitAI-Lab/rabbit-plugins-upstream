## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary-school students through chemistry concepts using particle-level explanations, bounded analogies, and checks for understanding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to explain junior- and senior-high chemistry phenomena through molecules, atoms, ions, and particle diagrams, then test understanding by connecting observations to symbols. It is not a substitute for worked numerical problems or laboratory instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China K–12 context; localize curriculum, crisis contacts, consent practices, and school requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, crisis resources, and consent assumptions may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum references, emergency contacts, school requirements, and consent rules before use elsewhere.

Risk: Student learning profiles could expose sensitive information if used without consent.

Mitigation: Keep profile memory off by default; read relevant profile data only after explicit student or guardian consent, and do not write to the profile.

Risk: Particle analogies or unsupervised experiments may cause misconceptions or physical harm.

Mitigation: State each analogy’s limits and refer practical experiments to supervised school-laboratory instruction.
