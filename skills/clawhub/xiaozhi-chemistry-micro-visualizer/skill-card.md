## Description:

Explains chemical phenomena through particle-level models, helping students connect observations, particle diagrams, and chemical symbols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use the skill to understand why chemical phenomena occur at the particle level, correct misconceptions, and connect observations to symbolic representations. It focuses on conceptual explanations and redirects calculation, equation-writing, and experiment-procedure requests to other skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analogies can create new chemistry misconceptions if their limits are omitted.

Mitigation: State each analogy's limits and use a check question to confirm the student understands where it stops applying.

Risk: Students may try to reproduce demonstrations involving heat or chemicals at home.

Mitigation: Discuss classroom observations and direct experiment requests to supervised school-lab instruction; follow the included lab-safety guidance.
