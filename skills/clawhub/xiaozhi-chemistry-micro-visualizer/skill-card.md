## Description:

用文字粒子图和有边界的类比，帮助学生理解化学现象背后的分子、原子和离子变化。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中文化学学习者及其教师，借助可数的文字粒子图、类比和追问解释化学变化、质量守恒、溶解等概念，并在高中必修起点讲解电离与电子转移。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; elsewhere only after localizing curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis support resources.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profile information may be accessed without effective consent controls.

Mitigation: Confirm the platform enforces consent-gated profile access; otherwise use session-only explanations without reading a profile.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis guidance may not fit students outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and help resources before deployment, and confirm the student's region before providing crisis contacts.

Risk: Chemical analogies or suggested experiments can mislead students or create safety concerns.

Mitigation: State each analogy's limits and refer practical experiments to supervised school settings rather than home chemical or heating activities.
