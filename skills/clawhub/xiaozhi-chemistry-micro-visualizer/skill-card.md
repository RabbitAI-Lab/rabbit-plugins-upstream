## Description:

帮助初中和高中学生用粒子图景理解化学现象，并在现象、粒子模型和化学符号之间建立联系。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向初高中学生的化学辅导者可用它解释分子、原子和离子层面的化学现象，帮助学生把观察、粒子图景与化学符号相互转换。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；面向其他地区使用前，应按当地情况调整教学与安全指引。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Access to a student's learning records depends on a separate xiaozhi-learning-dna dependency and the host's consent controls.

Mitigation: Keep memory disabled unless the student enables it and agrees; review the dependency and confirm the host enforces consent and pause controls.

Risk: Teaching, crisis-response, and lab-safety guidance is designed for mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt the guidance to the learner's location; ask where they are when local emergency guidance is needed and their location is unclear.
