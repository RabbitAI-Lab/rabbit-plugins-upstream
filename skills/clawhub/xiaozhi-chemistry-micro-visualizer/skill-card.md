## Description:

帮助中文化学学习者用文字粒子图和有边界的类比理解分子、原子、离子、化学变化、质量守恒与溶解，并衔接高中电离和电子转移概念。

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to explain chemistry phenomena at the particle level through text diagrams, analogies, and comprehension checks. It directs calculation, equation-writing, and hands-on experiment requests to other tutoring skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; curriculum, language, and crisis contacts require localization elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contact numbers may not fit users outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, language, and emergency referrals before use in other regions.

Risk: Long-term learning profiles contain student information.

Mitigation: Keep memory off by default; review profile controls and obtain consent before enabling profile reading.

Risk: Particle analogies can mislead learners, and practical chemistry activities can pose safety risks.

Mitigation: State where each analogy stops working and refer experiments to supervised, safety-aware instruction.
