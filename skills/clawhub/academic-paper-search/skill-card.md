## Description: <br>
Conducts comprehensive academic paper searches across multiple databases, downloads PDFs, monitors new publications, and manages standardized citations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, students, and research-writing assistants use this skill to coordinate literature searches, paper downloads, monitoring, CNKI searches, and citation formatting across academic sources. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow may search external academic services, automate CNKI searches, and bulk-download PDFs. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm database access rules, copyright terms, and licensing constraints before running searches or downloads. <br>
Risk: The workflow may set up paper-monitoring reminders or scheduled notifications. <br>
Mitigation: Review and approve notification schedules, destinations, and monitored topics before enabling monitoring. <br>
