## Description: <br>
shop-compliance helps small and midsize ecommerce merchants run a local, question-driven self-check for mainland China product listing, consumer rights, customer data protection, and marketing compliance risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External small and midsize ecommerce merchants use this skill before publishing products, responding to complaints, reviewing customer data handling, or preparing live commerce and promotion scripts. It provides an informational self-check and practical remediation suggestions, not legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Mainland China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the checklist output as legal advice or as proof of compliance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat results as informational and verify important compliance decisions against current law or with a qualified professional. <br>
Risk: Users may enter unnecessary real customer personal data while answering data-protection questions. <br>
Mitigation: Use generalized answers where possible and avoid entering unnecessary personal data. <br>
Risk: Saved Markdown, JSON, or HTML reports may contain sensitive shop or customer details. <br>
Mitigation: Store generated reports locally with appropriate access controls and delete them when no longer needed. <br>
