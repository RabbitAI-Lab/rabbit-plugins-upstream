## Description:

Build a world with the Sogni Worlds Kit — an interactive, cinematic, click-through world painted from a concept or made from the user's photos (each place comes alive; clicking an object plays a MiniMax H3 film to the next place; narration, music, fatal choices, endings and 3D collectibles). Use when the user asks to build, plan, render, review, publish or submit a world to the Sogni Worlds public gallery, a photo world, an interactive travel story, a choose-your-own-adventure video story or a click-through adventure from photos or from an idea, or mentions worlds.sogni.ai or the Sogni Worlds Kit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use the skill to plan and create interactive cinematic story worlds from a concept or personal photos, then review, export, and optionally submit finished worlds to Sogni's public gallery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private photos may be sent to external rendering services.

Mitigation: Before using private photos, confirm which services receive them and review their retention terms.

Risk: Rendering can incur costs, and hosting or gallery submission makes content public.

Mitigation: Review quoted costs before rendering and approve public hosting and gallery submission separately.

Risk: The downloaded executable toolkit and its credential-storage safeguards are outside the full artifact review.

Mitigation: Review the toolkit and its credential handling before providing credentials.
