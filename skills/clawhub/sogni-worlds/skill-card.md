## Description:

Build a Sogni World — an interactive, cinematic, click-through world painted from a concept or made from the user's photos (each place comes alive; clicking an object plays a MiniMax H3 film to the next place; narration, music, fatal choices, endings and 3D collectibles). Use when the user asks to build, plan, render, review, publish or submit a Sogni World to the public gallery, a photo world, an interactive travel story, a choose-your-own-adventure video story or a click-through adventure from photos or from an idea, or mentions worlds.sogni.ai or the Sogni Worlds Kit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use the skill to plan, create, review, and optionally publish interactive cinematic worlds from an idea or personal photos using the Sogni Worlds Kit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation downloads and runs the external Sogni Worlds Kit.

Mitigation: Review the kit and its instructions before installation or execution.

Risk: Personal photos are sent to AI services for processing.

Mitigation: Check the services' privacy policies and use only photos you are comfortable sending for processing.

Risk: Rendering can incur costs, and hosting or gallery submission makes a world public.

Mitigation: Review costs before rendering and separately approve public hosting or gallery sharing.
