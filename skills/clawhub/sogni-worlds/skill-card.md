## Description:

Build a world with the Sogni Worlds Kit — an interactive, cinematic, click-through world painted from a concept or made from the user's photos (each place comes alive; clicking an object plays a MiniMax H3 film to the next place; narration, music, fatal choices, endings and 3D collectibles). Use when the user asks to build, plan, render, review, publish or submit a world to the Sogni Worlds public gallery, a photo world, an interactive travel story, a choose-your-own-adventure video story or a click-through adventure from photos or from an idea, or mentions worlds.sogni.ai or the Sogni Worlds Kit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators use this agent skill to plan, build, review, export, and optionally submit interactive cinematic worlds made from a concept or their photos with the Sogni Worlds Kit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The partial security review flags possible disclosure or consent gaps when photos are sent to an external image-generation service.

Mitigation: Before using photos, confirm which services receive them, what they retain or reuse, and whether upload is optional; get the user's informed consent before uploading.
