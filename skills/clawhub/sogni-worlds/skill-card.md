## Description:

Builds and guides the creation, review, export, and optional gallery submission of interactive Sogni Worlds from a concept or the user's photos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sogni-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/sogni-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use the skill to plan and create interactive, cinematic worlds from a concept or personal photos, then review, export, and optionally submit a finished world to Sogni's public gallery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal photos are sent for cloud processing.

Mitigation: Use only photos you are comfortable sending to the service and review the provider's privacy terms.

Risk: Rendering may incur provider costs.

Mitigation: Request a quote before rendering and review plan costs before building a whole world.

Risk: A submitted world can be listed in Sogni's public gallery.

Mitigation: Approve public sharing separately and review gallery permissions before submitting.

Risk: The downloaded kit and its dependencies are not fully covered by the static scan.

Mitigation: Install only if you trust the kit and its dependencies; the external runtime was not fully audited.
