## Description:

Turns rough meeting notes into a structured Markdown summary with decisions, action items, and owners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hmahmadcs](https://clawhub.ai/user/hmahmadcs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to turn pasted notes from standups, planning meetings, or one-on-ones into a concise summary of decisions, action items, and open questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting notes may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share notes with the assistant only when comfortable having them processed; remove sensitive details first when appropriate.

Risk: A summary could imply decisions, owners, or deadlines that were not in the notes.

Mitigation: Keep wording faithful, include owners and due dates only when provided, and review the summary before sharing it.
