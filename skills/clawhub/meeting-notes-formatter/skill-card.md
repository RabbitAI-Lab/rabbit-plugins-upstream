## Description:

Turns rough meeting notes into a structured Markdown summary of decisions, action items, owners, and open questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hmahmadcs](https://clawhub.ai/user/hmahmadcs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with rough notes from standups, planning meetings, or one-on-ones use this skill to create readable summaries and track decisions, action items, and unresolved questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting notes may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Provide only notes or file paths you are comfortable having summarized.

Risk: A summary could misstate decisions or assign unsupported action items.

Mitigation: Review the summary against the original notes; do not invent decisions, owners, or due dates.
