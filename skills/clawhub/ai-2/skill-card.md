## Description:

Provides a structured, credibility-scored protocol for market, industry, and competitive landscape research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coolhitbird](https://clawhub.ai/user/coolhitbird)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and analysts use this skill to produce structured desktop research reports for markets, industries, products, or competitors. It guides agents through question definition, source tiering, cross-validation, graded conclusions, confidence scoring, and follow-up recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The structured protocol can produce longer, more formal research reports than needed for quick lookups.

Mitigation: Ask for a brief answer or constrained scope when a lightweight response is sufficient.

Risk: Public web research can include stale, single-source, conflicting, or paywalled information.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source tiering, timestamps, cross-validation, rebuttal search, and unknown labels before treating conclusions as reliable.
