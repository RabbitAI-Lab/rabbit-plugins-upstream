## Description:

Helps users find and contact human experts through Instant Expert for written or voice answers or short calls, with a free simulated test mode and user-approved paid outreach in live mode.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[maxko87](https://clawhub.ai/user/maxko87)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill to find practitioners, request first-hand advice or interviews, contact a specific person, and check replies to earlier requests. Live outreach requires approval of each paid order preview.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live requests contact real people and may incur charges when an expert answers or books.

Mitigation: Before sending, obtain the user's explicit approval of the exact recipients, message, price, payment mode, terms and total spending cap shown in the preview.

Risk: Test-mode expert profiles and responses are fictional and may be mistaken for real evidence.

Mitigation: Clearly label test results as simulated and use a user-approved live request for real expert input.
