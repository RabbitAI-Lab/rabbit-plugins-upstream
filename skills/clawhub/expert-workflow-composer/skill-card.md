## Description:

Scans available experts and teams, composes task-specific collaboration workflows, and suggests cross-expert combinations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to discover available experts, plan single-expert or multi-expert work, coordinate expert teams and connected skills, and explore possible expert combinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Expert listings or availability may change or be unavailable during marketplace searches.

Mitigation: Check current marketplace and installed-expert listings; use only verified results and disclose when the marketplace cannot be scanned.

Risk: Coordinated experts or connectors may act on sensitive information or make changes without adequate review.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive business data out of reusable templates and obtain explicit user confirmation before expert dispatch, connector use, writes, publication, or deletion.
