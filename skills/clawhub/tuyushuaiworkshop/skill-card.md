## Description:

A Chinese-language education workbench advisor for diagnosing learning scenarios, recommending check-in, task, points, and badge setups, explaining usage rules, and giving practical operating guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[urselect](https://clawhub.ai/user/urselect)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators, training operators, parents, study groups, and individual learners use this skill to plan lightweight education workflows for learning check-ins, tasks, points, exchanges, and badge incentives. It helps users choose a scenario, design a practical module combination, and decide the next manual setup step without directly changing account data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may ask for custom dashboard code, exports, bulk actions, or third-party integrations based on the skill's customization guidance.

Mitigation: Review any generated changes before use, confirm consent and data handling, and make sure changes are reversible.

Risk: Users may over-treat planning suggestions as automated account actions.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as manual guidance only; users should perform and verify any check-ins, task publishing, points exchanges, or setup changes themselves.
