## Description:

Provides a comprehensive diagnostic summary for psychology paper Method sections by combining six dimension-specific reviews into weighted scores, ratings, and revision priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjinhongmy-pixel](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjinhongmy-pixel)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, editors, and writing-support agents use this skill to evaluate empirical psychology Method sections. It coordinates six Method-section diagnostics and returns an integrated score, dimension breakdown, grade demotion status, and prioritized revision path.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The report quality depends on six referenced diagnostic sub-skills being available and trusted.

Mitigation: Confirm the six sub-skills are installed from trusted sources before using this coordinator for review decisions.

Risk: Confidential manuscript text may be processed by the active agent environment and any invoked sub-skills.

Mitigation: Use only approved environments for confidential manuscripts, or remove sensitive content before submission.

Risk: The coordinator aggregates sub-skill findings and does not replace human review of academic integrity, ethics, or publication-readiness issues.

Mitigation: Review severe findings manually and verify ethics approvals, consent descriptions, preregistration claims, and data availability statements against primary records.
