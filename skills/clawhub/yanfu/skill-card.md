## Description: <br>
Yanfu helps developers turn an existing public landing page and 1-5 product screenshots into a clearer user-facing product story without changing positioning or inventing unsupported claims. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shaohuadavidlee](https://clawhub.ai/user/shaohuadavidlee) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and product teams use this skill to rewrite an existing landing page into a more understandable product story while preserving verified product facts, real CTAs, brand style, and evidence limits. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill uses a public landing page URL and user-provided product screenshots as source material. <br>
Mitigation: Use public, non-confidential inputs unless the user is comfortable using them as source material. <br>
Risk: Generated landing-page copy could overstate product claims if source evidence is weak or incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated claims, links, and CTAs before publishing, and keep unsupported claims in the notes rather than the public page. <br>
Risk: The skill writes local deliverables for review and publication. <br>
Mitigation: Inspect the generated HTML and notes before relying on them or sharing them externally. <br>
