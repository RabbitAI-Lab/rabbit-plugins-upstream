## Description:

Sequence service stops and build dispatch manifest records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Field service operations teams and dispatch coordinators use this skill to sequence supplied service stops into a route plan and turn that route plan into a concise dispatch manifest.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated route plans and manifests may omit real-world operational constraints that were not supplied in the request.

Mitigation: Review generated route plans and dispatch manifests before operational use.
