## Description: <br>
Supports compliance review of demand promissory notes from issuance through settlement, including contract checklists, document validation, fraud-pattern review, and institution mapping. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Legal, finance, compliance, and trade-operations users can use this skill to review demand promissory note documents, settlement evidence, contract clauses, endorsement chains, SWIFT message consistency, and cross-border compliance checkpoints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with emphasis on China and cross-border demand-note workflows. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be used with sensitive contracts, payment messages, and compliance documents. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in an agent environment appropriate for confidential financial materials, and avoid uploading sensitive documents when that environment is not approved. <br>
Risk: Demand-note and cross-border settlement requirements are jurisdiction-specific and can change. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as review support, then confirm legal and regulatory conclusions with qualified counsel and current authoritative sources before acting. <br>
Risk: The artifact describes checklist-driven and future automated validation behavior, but the release evidence reports no executable code in the artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Do not rely on the skill as a standalone validator; manually review source documents, bank confirmations, and settlement evidence. <br>
