## Description:

Creates short, hands-only unboxing and ASMR product videos from product and packaging photos using Varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and marketers use the skill to turn product and packaging photos into short, hands-only unboxing or ASMR ads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos and prompts may be sent to Varg for external processing.

Mitigation: Avoid sending confidential product images or prompts unless their use with Varg is approved.

Risk: The security review was partial and could not resolve a local reference.

Mitigation: Review the referenced source and skill changes before installation or use.

Risk: The installer may not be pinned to a reviewed version.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, pinned installer version.
