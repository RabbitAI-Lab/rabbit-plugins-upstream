## Description:

Turns product and packaging photos into 5–15 second hands-only unboxing videos with timed visual, action, and sound prompts and native ASMR audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketers use the skill to turn product and packaging photos into short unboxing or ASMR video ads. It guides the agent in preparing a timed prompt and generating a vertical clip through Varg.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images may include content the user is not authorized to share with Varg and its generation providers.

Mitigation: Use only images the user is authorized to share with Varg and its generation providers.

Risk: Video generation may incur usage costs.

Mitigation: Review the estimated cost before generating each clip.
