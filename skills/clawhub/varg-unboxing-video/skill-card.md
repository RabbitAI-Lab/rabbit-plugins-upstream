## Description:

Creates short, hands-only unboxing and ASMR product video ads from product and packaging photos using Varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and developers use the skill to turn product and packaging photos into short unboxing, ASMR, or hands-only product demo videos. It provides shot and sound guidance and can submit generation requests through Varg.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The installer version is not pinned.

Mitigation: Review the installer source and pin a specific version before installation.

Risk: Product images are processed by Varg, and the security scan notes its evidence is incomplete because a referenced path could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Send only product images you are comfortable processing through Varg, and review the skill and its referenced files before use.
