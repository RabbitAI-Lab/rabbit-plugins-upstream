## Description:

Turns product photos into short hands-only unboxing or ASMR video ads using timed visual, action, and sound prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and marketers use this skill to turn product and packaging photos into short unboxing, packaging reveal, or ASMR product video ads. It provides prompt structure and guidance for checking product accuracy and advertising claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference images are sent to Varg, which may expose confidential or unreleased product information.

Mitigation: Use only images you are authorized to share and consider confidentiality before submitting unreleased products.

Risk: Video generation consumes credits and can incur usage costs.

Mitigation: Check the required cost estimate before generating each clip.
