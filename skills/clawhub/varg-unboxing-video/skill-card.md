## Description:

Turns product and packaging photos into a short hands-only unboxing or ASMR product video using Varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and creative teams use the skill to turn product and packaging photos into short unboxing, packaging reveal, or ASMR product ads. It guides the agent in preparing a timed prompt and requesting a video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos, packaging images, and image URLs may be sent to Varg's external service, which could expose confidential material.

Mitigation: Use only images you are authorized to share and omit confidential material unless its disclosure is approved.

Risk: The documented installation command does not pin the CLI version.

Mitigation: Review the installer and use a known, approved CLI version before installation.

Risk: Generated footage may alter the product, packaging, quantity, or demonstrated function.

Mitigation: Compare the finished video with the source photos and verify that it shows only the real product, packaging, quantity, and capabilities.
