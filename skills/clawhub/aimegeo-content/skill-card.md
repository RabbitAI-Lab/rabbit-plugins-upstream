## Description:

用户要将网站、产品或项目资料转成 aimeGeo 主题、写作任务、文章或视频脚本时使用；包括事实核对、标题标签与封面建议，不默认对外发布。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向需要制作 aimeGeo 内容的创作者和项目团队，将已授权的项目资料整理为主题、写作任务、文章草稿或视频脚本，并准备事实来源、标题、标签和封面建议。草稿与发布意图分开处理，默认不对外发布。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project material may contain private, customer, or otherwise unauthorized information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized material, exclude secrets and customer privacy, and remove anything that is not approved for public use.

Risk: External image services may incur charges or have usage limits.

Mitigation: Check the selected service, configuration, and available quota before generating images; do not silently switch to a paid service.

Risk: A saved draft could be mistaken for permission to publish it publicly.

Mitigation: Treat public posting as a separate action and proceed only after confirming the user's publishing authorization.
