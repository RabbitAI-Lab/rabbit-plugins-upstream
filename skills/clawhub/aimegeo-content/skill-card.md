## Description:

用户要将网站、产品或项目资料转成 aimeGeo 主题、写作任务、文章或视频脚本时使用；包括事实核对、标题标签与封面建议，不默认对外发布。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

面向需要把网站、产品或项目资料整理为 aimeGeo 内容的创作者与运营人员，用于准备选题、文章或视频脚本，并核对事实、标题标签与封面建议。技能默认只制作并保存草稿，公开发布需要单独授权。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project material may be sent to an external drafting provider.

Mitigation: Use only material approved for external processing and confirm which provider receives it.

Risk: A saved draft could be mistaken for approved public content.

Mitigation: Review the saved draft and obtain separate user authorization before publishing.
