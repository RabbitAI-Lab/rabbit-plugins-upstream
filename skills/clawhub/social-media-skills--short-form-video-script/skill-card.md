## Description: <br>
The master craft of scripting short-form vertical video for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts using the WATCH framework for hooks, retention, pacing, sound-off captions, payoff, loop, and CTA. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and social-media teams use this skill to draft shootable short-form video scripts with spoken lines, on-screen text, beat and shot direction, hook variants, retention structure, and CTA guidance. It is a writing and planning skill; humans shoot, edit, choose the take, verify metrics, and publish through the appropriate platform workflow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Short-form platform benchmarks and trend signals can become stale or be misapplied. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current metrics, trend claims, and platform guidance before relying on them in a script or publishing plan. <br>
Risk: Downstream publishing or analytics workflows may require social-account access outside this writing skill. <br>
Mitigation: Review and authorize any platform-specific or WoopSocial workflow separately before granting account permissions. <br>
