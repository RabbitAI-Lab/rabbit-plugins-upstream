## Description: <br>
Cost Estimation guides agents through estimating architecture costs across compute, storage, network, cloud, AI/LLM, operations, staffing, and third-party services. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, architects, and engineering teams use this skill to turn an architecture design and risk assessment into a cost estimation report with cost breakdowns, option comparisons, forecasts, sensitivity analysis, and optimization recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cost estimates can become inaccurate when vendor pricing changes or pricing sources are stale. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current vendor pricing before relying on totals, and record the estimate date and pricing sources in the report. <br>
Risk: Cost estimates can be misleading if architecture assumptions, usage volume, or scale targets are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions clearly and include sensitivity analysis for the largest cost drivers. <br>
