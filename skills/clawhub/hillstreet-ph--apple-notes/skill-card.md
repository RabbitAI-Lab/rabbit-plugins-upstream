## Description:

Create, view, edit, delete, search, move, or export Apple Notes via the memo CLI on macOS.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

macOS users and agents can use this skill to manage Apple Notes from the terminal, including searching and organizing notes and exporting them to HTML or Markdown.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deleting a note can remove its contents.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected note before deletion and keep a backup of important notes.

Risk: The skill may require macOS Automation access to Notes.app.

Mitigation: Grant Automation access only when you intend to let memo interact with Apple Notes.
