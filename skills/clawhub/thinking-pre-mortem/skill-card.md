## Description:

Before committing to a plan or launch, assume it already failed and reason backward through concrete causes - convert failure paths into mitigations, gates, and stop checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, project leads, and operators use this skill before costly or high-risk commitments to turn concrete failure scenarios into mitigations, owners, checkpoints, and ship or stage gates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may add unnecessary ceremony to small, reversible decisions.

Mitigation: Apply it before costly or high-risk commitments and skip it for local, reversible work.

Risk: Outputs can become generic risk theater if failure paths are not specific to the plan.

Mitigation: Require past-tense failure scenarios, necessary conditions, owners, verification checkpoints, and plan gates for top risks.
