## Description: <br>
Helps agents advise photographers on whether to use UV or skylight filters for lens protection and how to troubleshoot lens flare without turning the response into product shopping advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and photography assistants use this skill to decide when UV or skylight filters are useful for lens protection, when they can degrade image quality, and what safer alternatives apply in normal or harsh shooting environments. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may bias answers toward removing UV filters except in harsh environments. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for UV-filter protection and flare-troubleshooting questions, and route product recommendations or creative filter use to a more specialized photography skill. <br>
