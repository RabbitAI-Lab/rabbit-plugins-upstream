## Description: <br>
Maimai is a workplace-networking guidance skill for building professional connections, improving personal branding, assessing anonymous workplace posts, and conducting career or industry research. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, job seekers, recruiters, and career advisors use this skill for Maimai profile positioning, professional outreach, resume framing, salary and job-change planning, and cautious interpretation of anonymous workplace information. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share confidential company information, personal identifiers, or unverified anonymous claims while seeking career or networking advice. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid confidential details and personal identifiers, and treat anonymous workplace posts as unverified unless corroborated by reliable evidence. <br>
Risk: Anonymous workplace posts and salary discussions may be misleading, exaggerated, or biased. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check claims across multiple sources and use the skill's guidance as decision support rather than as factual confirmation. <br>
Risk: Labor-dispute or legal questions may require professional judgment beyond workplace-networking advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use qualified legal or HR channels for employment disputes and do not treat platform posts as legal evidence. <br>
