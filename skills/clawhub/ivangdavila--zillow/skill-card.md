## Use Case:

External users use this skill to interpret Zillow real estate information for property search, buying, selling, investment analysis, pricing strategy, and local market research. It emphasizes independent verification of Zillow estimates, tax records, financing assumptions, rental comps, and local MLS or county records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real estate and investment guidance may be mistaken for professional financial, legal, tax, or appraisal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support and verify decisions with qualified local professionals, current financing terms, tax records, and local market data.

Risk: Zillow listings, Zestimates, rent estimates, and property status can be stale or materially inaccurate.

Mitigation: Cross-check Zillow data against county assessor records, FEMA flood maps, rental comps, local MLS data, and recent sold comparable properties before acting.

Risk: Investment calculations can understate vacancy, maintenance, taxes, insurance, financing costs, or local regulatory risk.

Mitigation: Use conservative expense assumptions, compare cash and financed scenarios, and verify local rent control, landlord-tenant law, crime, jobs, and population trends.
