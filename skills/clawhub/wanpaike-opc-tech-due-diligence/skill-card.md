## Description: <br>
A technical due diligence skill for assessing technology feasibility, team background, patent claims, business logic, and fraud signals before investment screening. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Investors, founders, and analysts use this skill to perform early technical due diligence on technology projects, including feasibility checks, team and patent verification, risk rating, and follow-up evidence requests. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process confidential project materials and team or personal background data. <br>
Mitigation: Use approved storage and access controls, redact unnecessary personal or confidential information, and confirm authorization before uploading or sharing project materials. <br>
Risk: IMA import and report writeback can persist client documents and generated analysis outside the chat session. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm where imported documents and reports are stored, who can access them, retention duration, and deletion or redaction procedures before using IMA workflows. <br>
Risk: Generated scores, valuation comments, and investment recommendations may be incomplete or misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as draft analysis and require independent verification by qualified reviewers before making investment or legal decisions. <br>
