## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent research may require sharing scoped technical details with PatSnap connectors.

Mitigation: Confirm authorized connector access and confidentiality boundaries before live retrieval.

Risk: Patent research outputs may be mistaken for legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support and involve qualified counsel for patentability, infringement, validity, FTO, or other dispositive legal decisions.
