## Description: <br>
Builds competitor-relative content gap maps of missing topics, keyword gaps, and editorial-calendar opportunities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, SEO, and content teams use this skill to compare their site with competitor domains, identify missing keywords, topics, formats, and funnel coverage, then turn the highest-value gaps into a prioritized content plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save durable research notes and can use optional site or competitor inventory data. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only domains, exports, and analytics data that are appropriate for durable research notes; review saved summaries before retention or sharing. <br>
Risk: Unavailable SEO metrics or competitor coverage can lead to unsupported recommendations if estimates are treated as measured facts. <br>
Mitigation: Label each metric as Measured, User-provided, Estimated, or N/A, and keep competitor evidence attached to every prioritized gap. <br>
