## Description:

Provides an offline Socratic reading companion for manually supplied WeRead passages, with saved reading notes and user-confirmed cross-book comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers use this skill to reflect on passages they paste into a local reading companion, keep notes, and confirm comparisons between books.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved notes and confirmed relationships remain in the browser profile.

Mitigation: Do not paste sensitive content that should not be retained there; clear the browser's site data when finished.

Risk: Downloaded comparisons may include private or copyrighted excerpts.

Mitigation: Review exports before sharing and use only excerpts you have permission to retain or distribute.
