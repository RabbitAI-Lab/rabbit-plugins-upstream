## Description: <br>
Turn a process, workflow, or decision logic into a clean flowchart that includes ready-to-render Mermaid, a short legend, and stated assumptions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, and operators use this skill to turn process descriptions, workflows, or decision logic into readable Mermaid flowcharts with explicit branches, end states, notes, and assumptions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided process details may be sensitive if copied into an external Mermaid renderer. <br>
Mitigation: Use a trusted renderer for confidential workflows and avoid sharing sensitive process details with untrusted services. <br>
Risk: Ambiguous process descriptions can result in diagrams that reflect assumptions rather than confirmed workflow behavior. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions clearly and review decision branches, labels, and end states before using the diagram operationally. <br>
