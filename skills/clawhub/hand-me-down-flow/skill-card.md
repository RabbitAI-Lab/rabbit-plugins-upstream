## Description:

Tracks children's clothing and gear, matches stored items to kids' size and seasonal fit windows, and prepares graded transfer manifests with storage guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Families sharing children's clothes, toys, and gear use this skill to track what they have, time seasonal handoffs, grade item condition, and decide what to pass along or donate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generating a transfer manifest removes selected items from the registry without confirmation or built-in recovery.

Mitigation: Back up the registry first and use a copy when previewing manifests; confirm the handoff before updating the working registry.

Risk: The registry contains children's names and birthdays.

Mitigation: Keep the registry private and limit access to people who need it.
