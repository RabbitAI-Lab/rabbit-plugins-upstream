## Description:

古诗词改编现代新民谣音乐创作：将古典诗词改编成完整的新民谣歌词、曲风卡、演唱描述和 AI 音乐 prompt，适用于单曲或专辑企划。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to adapt Chinese classical poems into modern folk songs, including lyrical structure, music style guidance, vocal direction, and AI music generation prompts. It is suited to Chinese-language single-song drafting and album concept planning rather than literary translation or pure poetry analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese classical poetry and modern Chinese folk lyrics, so users working in other languages may receive less suitable output unless they ask for another language explicitly.

Mitigation: State the target language and musical market when requesting adaptations outside Chinese-language contexts.

Risk: Generated lyrics or adaptations intended for public release may raise copyright, source-text, reference, or platform-policy questions.

Mitigation: Review rights, attribution needs, and platform rules for source poems, references, and generated lyrics before publication.
