## Description:

Turns articles and documents into narrated explainer videos with outlines, storyboards, voiceover, assembly, and validation through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating explainers, training videos, or report summaries from articles and documents can use this skill to produce narrated videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share confidential material only when dLazy's terms and your organization's data policy permit it.

Risk: The CLI may save a dLazy API key in the local user configuration.

Mitigation: Protect access to the user configuration and rotate or revoke the key if exposed.
