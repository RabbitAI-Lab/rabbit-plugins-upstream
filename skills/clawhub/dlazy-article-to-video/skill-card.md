## Description:

Turns articles and documents into narrated explainer videos with an outline, storyboard, voiceover, assembly, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can turn an article or document into a narrated explainer, report, courseware, or training video by working with dLazy's hosted agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Send only material you are comfortable sharing with dLazy.

Risk: Video generation may consume paid credits, and project discovery can reveal organization project listings.

Mitigation: Check credit use before generation and confirm the organization account is appropriate before listing projects.

Risk: Logging in saves an organization API key in the local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI before installing and revoke the key when it is no longer needed.
