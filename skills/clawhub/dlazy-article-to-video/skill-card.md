## Description:

Turns a written article into a narrated explainer video by preparing an outline, storyboard, voiceover, and validated video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to turn an article into a narrated explainer video, guiding the dLazy file-to-video workflow from outline through validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented workflow sends prompts to dLazy services and uploads attached files to dLazy media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive material unless its use with the service is approved for your organization.

Risk: The partial security scan noted that the CLI login and project-listing steps were not clearly tied to the skill’s stated purpose.

Mitigation: Review the CLI login and project-listing flow before use, and run project listing only when needed.
