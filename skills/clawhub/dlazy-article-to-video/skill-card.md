## Description:

Turns articles and documents into narrated explainer videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, video assembly, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who want an explainer, report, course, or training video from an article or document can use the skill to guide the creation process. It supports starting a new video project and continuing an existing project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Files attached to a request are uploaded to dLazy media storage for processing.

Mitigation: Upload only documents you are authorized to share, and review dLazy's privacy and credit terms.

Risk: Logging in saves an organization API key in the local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Use the documented DLAZY_API_KEY environment variable when preferred, and rotate or revoke keys through the organization dashboard when needed.
