## Description:

Turns a written article into a narrated explainer video through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, building, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can turn articles and documents into narrated explainer, report, courseware, or training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, options, and attached files are sent to dLazy services; attached files are uploaded to dLazy media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive content and attach only files approved for upload.

Risk: The partial security scan did not establish why login and organization-project listing are needed for the stated article-to-video purpose.

Mitigation: Check that authentication and organization project access are expected and in scope before signing in.
