## Description:

article to video, text to video, news to video, essay to video — turn a written article into a narrated explainer video: outline, storyboard, voiceover, build, validate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who want to turn an article or document into a narrated explainer, report, course, or training video can use this skill to create an outline and storyboard, add voiceover, and build and validate the video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security guidance notes API communication and optional file uploads, while scanner coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Review the service terms and avoid attaching sensitive files unless their transfer is appropriate for your organization.

Risk: The security summary reports that Skillspector flagged CLI login and project listing as a mismatch with the article-to-video purpose.

Mitigation: Review requested CLI actions and the referenced material before running the skill.
