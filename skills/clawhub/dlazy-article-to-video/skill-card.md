## Description:

Turn a written article into a narrated explainer video through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, building, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and educators use this skill to turn an article or document into a narrated explainer video with an outline, storyboard, voiceover, and validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Articles, prompts, and selected attachments are sent to dLazy hosted services for processing.

Mitigation: Send only the intended content and exclude unrelated confidential files from attachments.

Risk: Authentication is saved in the local CLI configuration unless an environment-variable alternative is used.

Mitigation: Use the intended organization, protect access to the local configuration, or use the environment-variable option; rotate or revoke the API key if needed.
