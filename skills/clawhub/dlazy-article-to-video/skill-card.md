## Description:

Turns articles and other documents into narrated explainer videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, assembly, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People and teams who want an explainer, report, course, or training video from an article or document use this skill to work with dLazy's hosted file-to-video assistant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the hosted workflow involves installing and signing in to the dLazy CLI and may send prompts or attached files to dLazy services; the CLI itself was not established as inspected in the partial scan.

Mitigation: Review the CLI and service information first, and send prompts or files only when you are comfortable sharing them with dLazy.
