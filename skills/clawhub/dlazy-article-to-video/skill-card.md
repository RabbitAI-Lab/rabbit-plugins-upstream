## Description:

Turns articles and documents into narrated explainer videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, assembly, and validation using dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, educators, and other users can turn written articles or documents into narrated explainer, training, or report videos through a project-based dLazy session.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to an external dLazy service.

Mitigation: Only submit prompts and files you are comfortable sharing with dLazy.

Risk: Login stores an API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local key and rotate or revoke it in the dLazy dashboard if needed.
