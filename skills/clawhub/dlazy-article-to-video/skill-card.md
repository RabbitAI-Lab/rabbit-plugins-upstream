## Description:

Helps turn articles and documents into narrated explainer videos through outlining, storyboarding, voiceover, production, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating explainers, lessons, or report videos can use this skill to guide an article or document through video planning and production with dLazy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, project context, and attached files may be sent to dLazy.

Mitigation: Only provide content and attach documents you intend to share with the service.

Risk: Login stores a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key when access changes.
