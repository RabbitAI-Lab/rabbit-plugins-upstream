## Description:

Extracts public Instagram Highlights from Highlight URLs through Apify, including story media and available metadata.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Users with public Instagram Highlight URLs can retrieve their story items and available media metadata for review or download through a paid Apify service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Apify account may incur charges for actor starts and submitted Highlight links.

Mitigation: Verify current pricing and the maximum charge, and obtain explicit approval before each billable run.

Risk: Submitted public Instagram Highlight URLs are shared with Apify.

Mitigation: Disclose the transfer before execution and use the token only with the documented Apify API.
