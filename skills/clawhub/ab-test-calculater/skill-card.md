## Description: <br>
A/B Test Calculator helps agents compare two experiment groups across continuous, binary, or count data and return statistical results with a plain-language verdict. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[eelowl](https://clawhub.ai/user/eelowl) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, product managers, and analysts use this skill to evaluate A/B test results, collect the needed inputs, run the bundled Python calculator, and explain whether observed differences are statistically and practically meaningful. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Statistical output can be misleading when experiment design, sample quality, or assumptions are invalid. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the result as decision support and verify experiment setup, input data quality, and business context before making product or business decisions. <br>
