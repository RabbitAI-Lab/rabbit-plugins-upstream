## Description:

Commercial Image Prompt helps agents build three-layer prompts for product photos, marketing posters, e-commerce covers, and commercial AI visuals across global and Asian commerce platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, marketers, and e-commerce operators use this skill to generate platform-specific image-generation prompts with subject, style, and technical constraint layers. It supports product hero images, lifestyle banners, social covers, and commercial AI visual concepts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may not match the user's product, brand voice, or campaign requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts for brand and product accuracy before using them to create commercial imagery.

Risk: Users may include confidential product or campaign details in prompts.

Mitigation: Avoid including confidential details unless they are approved for image-prompt use.

Risk: Commercial image outputs may contain unwanted text artifacts, watermarks, distorted product geometry, or layouts that conflict with platform UI.

Mitigation: Use the skill's explicit negative constraints, aspect ratios, and safe-zone guidance, then inspect generated images before publication.
