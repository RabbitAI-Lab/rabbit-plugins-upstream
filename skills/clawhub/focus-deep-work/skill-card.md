## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can change the user's device environment by muting notifications, closing or minimizing apps, blocking websites, dimming brightness, or enabling do-not-disturb mode when permissions allow. <br>
Mitigation: Grant environment-changing permissions carefully, configure allowed apps and sites, and require confirmation before muting notifications, closing apps, or blocking access. <br>
Risk: The skill records focus sessions, distractions, and productivity patterns locally. <br>
Mitigation: Review where local focus logs are stored, keep them on-device, and confirm how to delete them when they are no longer needed. <br>
