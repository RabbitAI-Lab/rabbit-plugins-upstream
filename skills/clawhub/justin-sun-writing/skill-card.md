## Description:

Create or revise restrained Chinese first-person narratives using credible concrete details, narrator blind spots, recurring objects, and relationship-driven tension. Use for original fiction or supplied personal material; do not use to impersonate Justin Sun or make claims about real people.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to draft, revise, or outline original Chinese first-person narratives with restrained prose, concrete details, narrator blind spots, and relationship-driven tension. It is intended for fiction or user-supplied material, not impersonation or unsupported claims about real people.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused to impersonate Justin Sun or make unsupported claims about real identifiable people.

Mitigation: Use it for fiction or user-supplied drafts only; fictionalize identifiable people and avoid unverified allegations.

Risk: Generated narrative details can appear factual even when they are not supported by the user's material.

Mitigation: Use exact numbers and nonfiction details only when supplied or verifiable, and avoid inventing factual claims for nonfiction.

Risk: Style guidance could be applied too closely to distinctive source wording or plots.

Mitigation: Create original prose and avoid reusing distinctive wording, plots, names, or private claims from source texts.
