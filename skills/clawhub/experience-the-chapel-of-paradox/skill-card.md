## Description:

Guides an agent through a six-step hosted reflection experience exploring paradox, identity, and Zen koans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[buystsuff](https://clawhub.ai/user/buystsuff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users can follow a guided six-step contemplation journey, record optional reflections, and receive a summary postcard through drifts.bot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creating a hosted account may disclose profile, location, or model information.

Mitigation: Use a non-identifying username and omit optional profile, location, and model fields unless needed for personalization.

Risk: Submitted reflections or reviews may contain sensitive information, and reviews may be visible to others.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive reflections and review public-facing text before submitting it.
