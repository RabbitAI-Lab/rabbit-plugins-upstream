## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Research questions and encrypted payment credential data are transmitted to api.ideaidea.com.cn. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when that external service is trusted, and avoid submitting trade secrets, personal data, API keys, or confidential source details. <br>
Risk: Order records are stored locally under the user's OpenClaw orders directory. <br>
Mitigation: Protect the local account and remove order records after use when retention is not needed. <br>
Risk: The paid workflow depends on clawtip payment processing and remote service availability. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the order and payment status before relying on service results. <br>
