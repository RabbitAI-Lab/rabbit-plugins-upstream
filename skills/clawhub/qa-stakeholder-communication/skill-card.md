## Description:

Provides QA teams with stakeholder-specific communication strategies and report templates for developers, product managers, and leadership when discussing bug fixes, requirement changes, and quality risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA practitioners and test leads use this skill to tailor bug, release risk, and quality-status messages for developers, product managers, and leadership so each audience receives the details needed for action or decision-making.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stakeholder communication may include customer, payment, identity, incident, or production details.

Mitigation: Redact or mask sensitive data before using the skill, as recommended by the security guidance.

Risk: Audience-specific summaries can overstate quality risks or omit technical context if source QA evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated communication plans and reports against the underlying test report, bug evidence, and release-risk data before sending them to stakeholders.
