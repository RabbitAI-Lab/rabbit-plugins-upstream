## Description:

This skill helps QA practitioners tailor communication templates and reporting language for developers, product managers, and leaders when discussing bugs, requirement changes, and quality risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers and test leads use this skill to prepare audience-specific stakeholder communications, including developer-facing bug details, product-facing impact summaries, and leadership-facing quality risk reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include real customer data, payment details, screenshots, identity numbers, or production logs in QA communication examples.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive customer, financial, identity, screenshot, and production log data before using the skill.

Risk: Broad activation phrases may apply the skill to general communication requests outside QA stakeholder reporting.

Mitigation: Confirm the request is QA-focused and identify the intended audience before relying on the generated templates.
