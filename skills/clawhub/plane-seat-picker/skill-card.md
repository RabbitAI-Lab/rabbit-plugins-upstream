## Description:

Helps travelers compare economy seats by aircraft family, identify common seat traps, and choose seating strategies for their flight and party.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travelers use this skill while booking or checking in to interpret economy seat positions, compare comfort trade-offs, and plan seats for solo travel, couples, or families.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Airline subfleets and equipment changes can make family-level seat guidance inaccurate for a specific flight.

Mitigation: Verify the airline's exact seat map before paying for a seat and recheck it near departure.
