This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can browse Phantra’s available creative effects, saved recipes, and image and video models before choosing an option.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A listed credit price may be mistaken for an execution quote, or browsing may be mistaken for approval to run an effect.

Mitigation: Treat menu prices as guides, provide a separate quote before a paid action, and wait for explicit approval before running it.

Risk: A separate effect, upload, or execution step may introduce risks beyond browsing presets.

Mitigation: Review the relevant separate tool or skill before authorizing an upload or paid execution.
