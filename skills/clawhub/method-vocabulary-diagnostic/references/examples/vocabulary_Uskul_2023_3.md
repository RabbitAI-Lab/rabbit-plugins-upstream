# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

Where the study was administered in a non-English-speaking country, we translated the materials from English into the local language following a team translation approach (Survey Research Center, 2022; see also Harkness et al., 2010), in which all tasks were first translated by native speakers of the respective languages (either by a member of the research team or by a professional translator), and then reviewed and checked for accuracy and local conventions of language use by other team members that were fluent in both the local language and English. Where disagreements emerged, additional individuals were consulted before a final version was reached.

## 来源文献

Uskul et al., 2023, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

"following a team translation approach, in which ... first ... and then ..." 的多环节翻译流程 + "Where disagreements emerged, ... before a final version was reached" 的分歧解决机制，是跨文化材料翻译的规范词汇范本。
