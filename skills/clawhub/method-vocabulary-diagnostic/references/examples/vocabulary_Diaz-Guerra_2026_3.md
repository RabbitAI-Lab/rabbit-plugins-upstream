## 原文片段

Data collection was initiated by searching the archives and repositories of institutions known for conducting large-scale social surveys that were likely to contain measures of both SES and FO within Spain. Thus, the websites of key institutions like the CIS and major data repositories were screened, including the GESIS Data Archive (where the European Values Study data were accessed) and the U.K. Data Service (where the European Quality of Life Surveys data were accessed). To standardize occupational data across the diverse surveys, raw occupational-level variables were harmonized into three groups: directors/managers and mid- to senior-level technicians, skilled workers and low-level officers/operators, and elementary occupations. This categorization was based on the International Standard Classification of Occupations–08 (International Labour Office, 2012).
