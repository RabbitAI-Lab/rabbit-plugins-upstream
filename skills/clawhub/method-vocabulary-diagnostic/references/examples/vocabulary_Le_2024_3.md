# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

Participants then reported how they mentally represented the conflict between "avoiding contracting and spreading COVID-19" and "enjoying in-person social interactions" using an 8-point scale, ranging from definitely better reflects an inherent conflict to definitely better reflects a situational conflict (M = 3.46, SD = 2.13). Before completing this measure, participants also made two unipolar ratings for each conflict type. Specifically, they answered the questions "To what extent do you think the dilemma between 'avoid contracting and spreading COVID-19' and 'enjoy in-person social interactions' reflects [an inherent/a situational] conflict?" using a 5-point scale ranging from does not reflect (an inherent/a situational) conflict to completely reflects (an inherent/a situational) conflict. These unipolar ratings correlated strongly with our bipolar measure; inherent rating: r(263) = −.60, p < .001; situational rating: r(263) = .73, p < .001.

## 来源文献

Le, D. T. L., Scholer, A. A., & Fujita, K. (2024). The role of conflict representation in abstinence versus moderation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
