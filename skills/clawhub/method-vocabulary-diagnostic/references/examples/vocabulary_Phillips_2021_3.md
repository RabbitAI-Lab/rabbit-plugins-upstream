# 片段类型：词汇（先验编码维度的操作定义与信度）

## 原文片段

> Frame Coding. Two research assistants, blind to hypotheses, were trained on the concept of inequity frames, specifically the difference between favoring versus disfavoring frames. The research assistants coded each participant's open-ended definition of discrimination on the following two coding dimensions, which were developed a priori: (a) Did the response describe a situation in which someone was unfairly positively treated? (0 = no, 1 = yes; favoring Cohen's k = .68); and (b) Did the response describe a situation in which someone was unfairly negatively treated? (0 = no, 1 = yes; disfavoring Cohen's k = .82). Given high interrater reliability, coders' ratings were averaged to form composite scores of disfavoring frames and favoring frames.

## 来源文献

Phillips, L. T., & Jun, S. (2021), Study 1a Method (Measures). *Why Benefiting From Discrimination Is Less Recognized as Discrimination.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000298

## 适配诊断点

- 编码维度以"先验开发"（developed a priori）标注，编码前即定义，非事后看数据定维度。
- 编码者特征完整报告：人数（two）、盲假设（blind to hypotheses）、培训内容（trained on the concept of inequity frames）。
- 每维度给出精确操作性提问（Did the response describe ...）、编码值（0/1）与信度（Cohen's k = .68/.82），维度间信度独立报告。
- 合成规则明确："Given high interrater reliability, coders' ratings were averaged"，高分合成有依据。
