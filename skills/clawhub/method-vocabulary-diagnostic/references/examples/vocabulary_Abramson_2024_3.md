# 片段类型：词汇
## 原文片段
Our intention was to obtain a global impression of the child's behaviors across a variety of situations. Therefore, a global coding system adapted from Gagne et al. (2011) was used to score each child's temperament. ... Coders first watched the children perform all the tasks. Afterward, they were instructed to rate each behavior (e.g., curiosity, hyperactivity, sadness) according to their overall impression of the relevant behavior across the entire Lab-TAB procedure. Thus, each child was rated on all behaviors by the same coder. This global approach, in which each child receives one assessment per indicator (in contrast to typical Lab-TAB coding that assesses each episode separately, looking at separate epochs within each episode), is more similar to the way child behavior is perceived and judged in everyday life, thereby contributing to external validity.
## 来源文献
Abramson, L., et al. (2024). The structure, development, and etiology of observed temperament during middle childhood. Developmental Psychology.
