# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

Each trial began with a centrally located mosaic image for 500 ms that functioned as a fixation and premask, which was replaced for 200 ms by a face, which was replaced for 200 ms by a target, which was replaced for 100 ms by the mosaic postmask and ended on response to the prompt of whether the target was bad or good. A 1,500 ms blank screen separated trials. To prevent a response bias of bad, 50% of trials displayed a positive target, 25% displayed a threatening target, and 25% displayed negative targets (i.e., 50% of trials required a response of good and 50% bad; practice trials had the same structure).

## 来源文献

March et al., 2021, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

"which was replaced for X ms by ..." 关系从句链精确传递逐帧时序，配合 "functioned as"（充当）与 "To prevent a response bias of bad"（目的状语）等学术表达，是刺激时序与平衡设计的规范句式。
