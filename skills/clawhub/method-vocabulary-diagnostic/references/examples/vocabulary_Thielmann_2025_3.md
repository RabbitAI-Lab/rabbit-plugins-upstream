# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

In the mind game, participants were instructed to select an integer between 1 and 8 and to note it down on a piece of paper. Next, they were presented with an integer between 1 and 8 (randomly drawn from a uniform distribution) and asked to report whether the number they noted down matched the number presented on the screen. Participants knew that responding "yes" earned them a bonus payment of 2€, whereas responding "no" did not. We also explicitly informed participants that it was impossible to trace whether the number they noted down actually matched the number presented on the screen—because the experimenter could, of course, not observe participants while completing the online study—and that their payment exclusively depended on their response.

## 来源文献

Thielmann et al., 2025, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
