# 片段类型：词汇（测量名称与量表锚点的精确表达）

## 原文片段

> Direct Measures: Gut Reactions and Actual Feelings. As in Studies 1 and 2, we measured gut reactions and actual feelings toward the target categories via self-reported items. For the present study, only the items measuring gut reactions and actual feelings toward the relevant target category environmental protection were used to create an identical outcome variable predicted by each IAT, so that the three different IATs could be compared. Items measuring gut reactions and actual feelings toward the reference categories were not included in the outcome variable and thus do not contribute to differences in the predictive power of the IATs. Accordingly, the items under consideration were as follows: (a) "Rate your gut reactions towards environmental protection" and (b) "Rate your actual feelings towards environmental protection". Both items were to be rated on a 10-point bipolar scale, with end points ranging from 1 (strongly negative) to 10 (strongly positive). Higher scores on the items indicate a more positive evaluation of environmental protection.

## 来源文献

Urban, Koch, & Rothermund (2024), Study 3, Measures section. *The Implicit Association Test: A Methodological Investigation of the Relationship Between Test Difficulty and Criterion Validity.* https://osf.io/pcjwf/

## 适配诊断点

- 测量小节标题用冒号给出通俗名与正式名（"Gut Reactions and Actual Feelings"），兼顾可读性与精确性。
- 测量名称使用非正式但有明确定义的表达（gut reactions vs. actual feelings），并逐一引用完整题项原文（(a)、(b)），确保可复现。
- 量表锚点规范：10-point bipolar scale，端点 1 (strongly negative) 到 10 (strongly positive)，并说明计分方向（higher scores indicate ...）。
- 明确说明题目选取逻辑（"only the items ... were used to create an identical outcome variable ... so that the three different IATs could be compared"），为跨条件比较的测量等价性提供论证。
