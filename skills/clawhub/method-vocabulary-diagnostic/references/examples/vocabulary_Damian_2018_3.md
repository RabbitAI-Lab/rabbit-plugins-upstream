# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

The RCI is calculated for each trait, for each person, separately. RCI = (X2 – X1)/Sdiff, where X2 is a person’s score at Time 2, X1 is a person’s score at Time 1, and Sdiff = (2(SE)2)1/2, that is, the standard error of difference between the two test scores. The standard error of measurement, SE = Sdev(1 − αtest–retest)1/2, where Sdev is the standard deviation of the measure and αtest–retest is its test–retest reliability. ... RCI scores larger than 1.96 or smaller than −1.96 are considered indicative of reliable change.

## 来源文献

Damian, R. I., et al. (2018). Sixteen going on sixty-six. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

统计指标的公式呈现采用 "RCI = ..., where X2 is ... and Sdiff = ..., that is, ..." 的公式-变量释义-通俗解释三层结构，判定阈值（±1.96）明确，是统计指标定义的词汇范本。
