# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

A frequency score indicating the number of 5-s intervals that was coded as each behavior was calculated for each dyad. Because the length of the book-sharing activity varied, raw frequency scores were converted to proportion of total intervals. Individually-directed attention was operationalized by the proportion of intervals in which the child's attention was directed to an individually-selected target on the book (i.e., the proportion of intervals coded as "look at book"). Socially-directed attention was operationalized as the proportion of intervals in which the child and adults' attention was directed to a shared target on the book (i.e., the proportion of intervals coded as "shared attention").

## 来源文献

Werchan et al., 2024, Developmental Psychology

## 适配诊断点
