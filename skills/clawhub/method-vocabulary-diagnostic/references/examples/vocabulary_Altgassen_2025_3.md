# 片段类型：词汇
## 原文片段
Personality-Descriptive Adjectives (A-Easy Sets/A-Hard Sets). To measure participants' personality dimensions, we used the 47 (25 easy and 22 hard) personality-descriptive adjectives, which have been used in the final models of Study 2. Participants rated the extent to which the adjectives applied to them on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (does not apply) to 5 (applies). ... Personality-Descriptive Paraphrases (P-Easy Sets/P-Hard Sets). We further implemented paraphrases, that is, the definition for the 47 adjectives as described above. These paraphrases were the adjective's definitions as given in the dictionary and have already been compiled for the adjective vocabulary test. Occasionally, we slightly adjusted the definition, if they contained hard adjectives themselves. The overarching premise for such adjustments was to generate easy-to-understand paraphrases of the adjectives.

## 适配诊断点
- 测量工具标题即命名法（A-Easy/A-Hard/P-Easy/P-Hard），缩写体系自洽且与 Figure 7 模型一致。
- 量尺锚点逐字给出（1 = does not apply, 5 = applies），无歧义。
- 释义材料（paraphrases）的构建规则明确：以词典定义为基础 + 含难词时微调 + 总原则（easy-to-understand），材料可复现。
- "that is" 同位语解释（paraphrases, that is, the definition）帮助读者理解术语。
