# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

The experiment had a 2 (motivation: extroversion-important vs. introversion-important) × 2 (specificity: vague criteria vs. specific criteria) between-subjects design. We measured BTA beliefs by the mean difference between participants' ratings of introversion for themselves and others. ... Obviously, only introversion and being outgoing are relevant to our purposes here; we included the other traits to reduce experimental demand and increase the plausibility of the claim that the two surveys were unrelated.

## 来源文献

Logg et al., 2018, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General

## 适配诊断点

设计表述的规范格式（数字 × 数字 + 变量括号内水平列举）+ "reduce experimental demand / increase the plausibility" 的填充项功能表述，是因子设计与干扰项说明的词汇范本。
