# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

The 15-item INQ (Van Orden et al., 2012) was used to assess TB and PB. Similar to the INQ-18 used in Study 1, a 7-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 to 7 was used. In the 15-item version, the 6-item INQ-PB ranges from 6 to 42 and the 9-item INQ-TB ranges from 9 to 63. Internal consistency was excellent for both the INQ-TB and INQ-PB (αs = 0.91, 0.94, respectively). ... The Beck Depression Inventory-Second Edition (BDI-2; Beck, Steer, & Brown, 1996) was used to measure depressive symptom severity. The 21 items are rated on 4-point Likert scale ranging from 0 to 3. In this study, a total score was generated by summing all the items except Item 9, which assesses suicidal thoughts and wishes, as this item overlaps with the dependent variable.

## 来源文献

Chu, C., et al. (2017). Perceived problem-solving deficits and suicidal ideation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点
