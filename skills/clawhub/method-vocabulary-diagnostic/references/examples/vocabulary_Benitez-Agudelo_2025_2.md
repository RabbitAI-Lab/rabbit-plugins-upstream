# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

Prior to the assessments, a trained researcher provided standardized verbal instructions and remained available to clarify any questions, ensuring that responses remained unbiased. To further minimize response bias, several measures were implemented: all participants received uniform instructions, the survey was self-administered to limit interviewer bias, and a controlled classroom environment was maintained to ensure consistency in assessment conditions.

## 来源文献

Benítez-Agudelo et al., 2025, BMC Psychology

## 适配诊断点

"Prior to" 时间状语 + "To further minimize ..." 目的状语 + 冒号后三项并列的偏差控制措施（uniform instructions / self-administered / controlled environment），是程序标准化的词汇范本。
