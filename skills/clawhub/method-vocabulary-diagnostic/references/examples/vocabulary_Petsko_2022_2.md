# 片段类型：词汇（主因变量任务的精确操作描述）

## 原文片段

> Main Dependent Variable: Who Said What?
> The primary dependent variable in the who-said-what paradigm was a memory task. This dependent variable was measured by showing participants each of the 36 spoken statements from the conversation they viewed—along with 36 distractor statements that were not part of the conversation they viewed—and asking them first, "Have you seen this statement before?" If participants responded "yes," they were then shown the statement again alongside all 12 interaction partners' faces. Their task was to click on the face of the person whom they remembered as having said it.

## 来源文献

Petsko, C. D., Rosette, A. S., & Bodenhausen, G. V. (2022), Experiments 1a and 1b Method (Main Dependent Variable). *Through the Looking Glass: A Lens-Based Account of Intersectional Stereotyping.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 123(4), 763-787.

## 适配诊断点

- 小节标题用 "Main Dependent Variable: Who Said What?" 同时给出正式名与范式名，读者立即知道测量内容。
- 测量程序按参与者视角逐步描述：呈现 36 句原话 + 36 句干扰句 → 先问是否见过（引号原话）→ 若答是则连同 12 张面孔一起呈现 → 点击说话者面孔。每步可复现。
- 干扰项设计被显式说明（"36 distractor statements that were not part of the conversation they viewed"），记忆测量的基本范式要求交代清楚。
- 破折号插入语（"along with ..."）精确界定刺激构成，长句信息密度高但不失清晰。
