# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

After setting up the two eye trackers, the experimenters left the house and waited nearby in their cars. Thus, in contrast to some previous home studies (e.g., Tamis-LeMonda et al., 2019), the experimenters were not present during the recording. Parents were given a phone number to communicate any issues with the recording equipment and to notify when they wanted to stop the session. After data collection, parents filled out a demographic survey, and half of the parents also completed vocabulary measures in English and Spanish (if applicable). Participants were reimbursed $20 and a small prize (book or toy) per recording session.

## 来源文献

Schroer et al., 2025, Developmental Psychology

## 适配诊断点

"Thus, in contrast to some previous home studies (e.g., ...), the experimenters were not present" 以对比前人做法凸显本研究的程序特色，After setting up / After data collection 推进时序，是程序描述的对比衔接表达。
