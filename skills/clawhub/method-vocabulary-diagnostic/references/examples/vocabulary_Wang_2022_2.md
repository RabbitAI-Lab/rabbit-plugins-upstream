# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

The initial-gaze-direction bias was the number of ﬁrst ﬁxations directed at an angry/fearful face as a proportion of all trials with a ﬁrst ﬁxation directed at either face. The initial-ﬁxation-duration bias for angry/fearful faces was the percentage of ﬁrst ﬁxation duration on angry/fearful faces after normalized by total duration of all ﬁrst ﬁxations. The gaze-dwell-time bias for angry/fearful faces was the percentage of ﬁxation duration on angry/fearful faces after normalized by total duration of all trials.

## 来源文献

Wang, L., et al. (2022). On becoming socially anxious. Developmental Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

三个操作化指标的 "was the number of ... as a proportion of ..." / "was the percentage of ... after normalized by ..." 精确定义句式，是行为指标操作化的词汇范本。
