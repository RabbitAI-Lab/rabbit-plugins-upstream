## 原文片段
Laminated images of "good" (high-quality) and "bad" (low-quality) tracings (i.e., a marker tracing that was reasonably aligned or clearly misaligned with the template shape, see Figure 1A) were used in the Warm-up Phase. Additional tracings were used in two videos of teacher-child interactions (Selective Teacher video, Overpraise Teacher video; see Figure 1B) presented on a Macbook Pro laptop. In each video, six tracings were placed in a row on the table, 3 good and 3 bad tracings in alternating order (different tracings were used for each video). In both videos, the same child ("Johnny") asked a teacher about the tracings. One video featured "Teacher Jane" who wore a green shirt, and the other featured "Teacher Susan" who wore a red shirt. Both teachers were portrayed as women, such that their gender was matched and reflected the relatively high proportion of women teachers in participants' preschool settings.
