# 片段类型：词汇（核心构念的操作定义与题项原文）

## 原文片段

> As the main variable of interest, participants attributed their ostracism decision to two distinct categories: perceived norm-violating behavior of the target and/or perceiving the target as expendable. Participants read two definitions for either category and then stated whether the category applied using a yes/no format. Importantly, participants were specifically instructed that they could choose both categories as applying to their described situation, only one of them, or none of them. The two definitions read as follows:
>
> 1. Perceived norm violation: You excluded and/or ignored the person because the person was not behaving as one should behave in the situation, or you were concerned that the person might not behave as one should behave in the situation. For example, that could mean the person was behaving inappropriately, broke rules or agreements, or acted uncooperatively.
>
> 2. Perceived expendability: You excluded and/or ignored the person because the person was expendable. That is, the person did not have the skills required in the situation, or you were concerned that the person might not have the required skills. For example, that could mean the person was underperforming, slow on the uptake, or needed too much assistance.

## 来源文献

Rudert, S. C., Möring, J. N. R., Kenntemich, C., & Büttner, C. M. (2023), Study 1 Method (Materials and Procedure). *When and Why We Ostracize Others: Motivated Social Exclusion in Group Contexts.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

- 核心分类的操作定义以参与者可见的完整定义文本呈现（编号 1、2），而非研究者的抽象转述，测量材料的透明性典范。
- 每个定义包含三要素：核心表述（because ...）+ 澄清句（That is, ...）+ 示例（For example, ...），结构模板化、易读。
- 作答规则被显式说明："could choose both categories ... only one of them, or none of them"，防止"二选一"的误读。
- 术语（norm-violating behavior, expendable）在正文中使用前已给出参与者版定义，概念-测量一致性清晰。
