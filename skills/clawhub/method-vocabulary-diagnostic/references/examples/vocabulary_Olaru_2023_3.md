# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

The Big Five personality traits were measured with the 50-item version of the Big-Five Factor Markers (Goldberg, 1992), using a 5-point scale, ranging from 1 (very inaccurate) to 5 (very accurate). ... Life satisfaction was measured using the Satisfaction with Life Scale, which consists of five items (Diener et al., 1985). Responses were indicated on a 7-point scale, ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 7 (strongly agree). ... Social satisfaction was measured with a single item ("How satisfied are you with your social contacts?") in each year from 2008 to 2019. Responses were indicated on an 11-point scale, ranging from 0 (not at all satisfied) to 10 (completely satisfied).

## 来源文献

Olaru, G., et al. (2023). The link between personality, global, and domain-specific satisfaction across the adult lifespan. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

每种测量的 "was measured with/using...which consists of...Responses were indicated on a X-point scale, ranging from..." 句式统一、施测年份与波次（each year from 2008 to 2019）精确标注，单条目测量如实说明（single item），是测量描述的词汇范本。
