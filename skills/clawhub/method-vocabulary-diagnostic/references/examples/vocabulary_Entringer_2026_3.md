# 片段类型：词汇（前测混淆变量的选择与准随机论证）

## 原文片段

> Pretreatment Variables. Aerial bombardments were correlated with a municipality's population size and economic performance. Including these confounds helped ensure that variation in war destruction was quasirandom with respect to individual adjustment.
>
> Prewar Population. We included the population size of the respondents' or their parents' birthplace or place of residence in 1939. The data were sourced from the GLR data set and were available at the municipality level. For municipalities smaller than 3,000 inhabitants, which are missing in the GLR data set, we assumed a constant population of 1,500 inhabitants. In the analyses, the population size is reported in units of 100,000 inhabitants.
>
> Prewar Regional Economic Performance. Regional economic performance prior to the war was assessed using administrative tax data from 1938. In specific, we used per capita tax revenues from the turnover tax available at the level of 516 tax districts covering West Germany and Berlin (Brockmann et al., 2023).

## 来源文献

Entringer, T. M., Halbmeier, C., Buchinger, L., & Reitz, A. K. (2026), Method (Pretreatment Variables). *Within-Nation Variation in War Exposure and Psychological and Physical Adjustment.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 131(2), 355-379. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000601

## 适配诊断点

- 混淆控制的论证起点：先说明混淆相关存在（bombardments correlated with population size and economic performance），再说明控制目的（ensure quasirandom variation），因果推断语言的精确使用。
- "quasirandom" 一词准确界定观察数据的因果识别目标——近似随机，而非声称随机。
- 每个控制变量报告：数据来源、测量层级（市镇/税区）、缺失处理规则（<3,000 居民按 1,500 常数假设）、进入分析的变换（单位、log）。
- 控制变量全部为"前测"（prewar/pretreatment），时间上先于暴露，无后测偏差。
