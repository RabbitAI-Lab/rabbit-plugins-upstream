# 片段类型：词汇

## 原文片段

The computer programs randomly assigned participants to the different conditions, and randomly drew the target and context stimuli for each participant. Participants in all experiments evaluated a total of eight target stimuli; four of them were presented together with a positive context stimulus, and the other four were presented together with a negative context stimulus. The random stimulus drawing was constrained so that all of the face pictures depicted different individuals.

## 来源文献

Alves et al., 2022, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

"randomly assigned / randomly drew / was constrained so that" 的组合精确表达随机化机制与约束条件，是程序化随机描述的学术词汇搭配。
