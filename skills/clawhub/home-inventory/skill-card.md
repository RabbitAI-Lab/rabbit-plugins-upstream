## Description:

Document everything you own for insurance, moving, or estate planning with a local JSON inventory, depreciation tracking, insurance-ready reports, and moving box labels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Households, movers, estate planners, and insurance claim preparers use this skill to maintain a local inventory of possessions, estimate values, export records, and produce room, category, depreciation, and box reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inventory databases and CSV exports can reveal valuable possessions, room locations, serial numbers, and household value details.

Mitigation: Store inventory.json and exported CSVs in a protected location, limit sharing, and review exported contents before sending them to insurers or other third parties.
