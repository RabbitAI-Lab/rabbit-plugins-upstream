## Use Case:

Employees and external analysts use this skill to research an industry and produce a management-ready, editable PowerPoint deck with checked chart data, speaker notes, source mappings, and retained JishuDB evidence.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential, paid, or restricted report material could be retained without proper rights.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected knowledge base, output directory, and permitted retained report material before approving uploads or JishuDB retention.

Risk: Industry deck figures could be misleading if report coverage, geography, units, dates, or chart extraction are not checked.

Mitigation: Use source and evidence cards, preserve coverage limits, verify chart values against inspected evidence, and include slide-level citations and a source appendix.

Risk: The workflow can stall or produce an incomplete deck if required host capabilities are missing.

Mitigation: Confirm JishuDB write access, authorized browsing, local file output, OCR or vision where image evidence is needed, and editable PowerPoint export before promising completion.
