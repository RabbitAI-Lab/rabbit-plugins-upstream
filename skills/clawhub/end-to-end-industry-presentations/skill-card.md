## Description:

Researches industry reports and verified data to build editable PowerPoint briefings with chart data, speaker notes, and slide-level sources retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and managers use this skill to turn industry research into editable market briefings with checked figures, chart data, speaker notes, and traceable slide citations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research evidence, drafts, and final slide text are stored in JishuDB.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended storage destination and retention permissions before using the skill.

Risk: If JishuDB Desktop is unavailable, setup may download, install, configure, and launch software; the Windows installer is disclosed as unsigned.

Mitigation: Review the full approval details and allow setup only when those actions and the selected software are intended.

Risk: Incomplete report access or unchecked figures can make presentation claims misleading.

Mitigation: Check source coverage, numerical scope, chart data, and slide citations before sharing the deck.
