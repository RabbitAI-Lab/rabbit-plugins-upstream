## Description:

Researches industry reports and checked figures to create editable PowerPoint briefings with chart data, speaker notes, and slide-level sources retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and managers use this skill to research an industry and prepare an editable, evidence-backed briefing on market size, demand, and competition. It requires approved research and file output, JishuDB access, and a host capable of generating editable PowerPoint slides.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: JishuDB setup can download software and change local app or connector settings.

Mitigation: Review the proposed installation, release identity, destinations, and client changes; approve the specific plan before execution.

Risk: Research sources and presentation records may be stored in JishuDB.

Mitigation: Approve the source-import scope, write destination, and retention target before transferring material.
