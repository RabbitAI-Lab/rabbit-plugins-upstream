## Description:

Researches industry reports, verifies data, and builds editable PowerPoint briefings with chart data, speaker notes, and slide-level sources retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and managers use this skill to research an industry and prepare editable market, demand, and competitor briefings with checked figures and traceable sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow requires JishuDB storage and cannot complete without access to it.

Mitigation: Confirm JishuDB is suitable for the material before using the skill and grant only the needed access.

Risk: If JishuDB Desktop is missing, setup may download and install an app, write a local endpoint file, launch it, and add a client entry.

Mitigation: Review the approval details and authorize only the expected release, paths, and digest before installation.
