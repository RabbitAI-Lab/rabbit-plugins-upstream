## Description:

Create, edit, and report on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads from an AI agent through the AdKit MCP connector, including campaigns, ad sets, ads, carousels, media upload, interest search, and results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Advertisers and marketing teams use this skill through an AI agent to create, update, and review Facebook and Instagram campaigns, ad sets, and ads, and to inspect performance results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Facebook Page, pixel, or Instagram account defaults may be saved immediately, before any draft is approved, and can affect future ad identity or tracking.

Mitigation: Before saving account defaults, confirm the target account and the specific Page, pixel, or Instagram selection with the user; make clear that this change takes effect immediately.
