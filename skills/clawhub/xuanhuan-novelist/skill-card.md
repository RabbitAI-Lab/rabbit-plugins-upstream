## Description: <br>
A Chinese xuanhuan and cultivation-fiction writing skill that helps an agent plan, draft, validate, and revise long-form chaptered fantasy novels with cultivation systems, plot pacing, worldbuilding, battles, and style continuity. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[xiaoheorg](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoheorg) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External writers and agent users use this skill to create or continue Chinese xuanhuan, cultivation, immortal-hero, and level-up novels. The skill supports draft analysis, structured planning, chapter generation, word-count validation, and revision workflows for 10-50 chapter projects. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read existing chapter drafts to analyze writing style and extract settings. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces containing drafts the user is comfortable having analyzed. <br>
Risk: The workflow creates and updates local folders, preference files, planning files, chapter drafts, and validation state. <br>
Mitigation: Run it in a dedicated project workspace and review generated plans and output paths before approving the writing mode. <br>
Risk: Automatic polishing or rewriting can change generated chapters after validation checks. <br>
Mitigation: Review final chapters and validation reports before treating the manuscript as complete. <br>
