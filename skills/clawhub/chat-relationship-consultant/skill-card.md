## Description: <br>
Chat Relationship Advisor helps users manage separate chat contexts by contact name, analyze relationship dynamics from imported chat logs, and produce Chinese-language reply guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to organize relationship-specific chat history, evaluate communication quality and relationship status, and draft context-aware Chinese replies. It is intended for conversational relationship advice based on user-provided snippets and background. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide private chat records and relationship details. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the minimum conversation snippets needed for advice and remove unnecessary identifying details before use. <br>
Risk: Relationship advice may be incomplete or misleading if the provided chat history lacks context. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as suggestions, review them against the full relationship context, and avoid acting on high-stakes interpersonal decisions without judgment. <br>
