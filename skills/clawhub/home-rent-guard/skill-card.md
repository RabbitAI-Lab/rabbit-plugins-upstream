## Description:

Helps renters check a property, verify a landlord or agent, review lease terms, and prepare for deposits, handover, and recordkeeping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Renters and housing professionals use this skill to prepare viewing checklists, questions about ownership and authorization, lease-review prompts, deposit discussions, and move-in handover records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rental laws, deposit terms, and recording rules vary by location; guidance may not fit a specific dispute.

Mitigation: Treat the output as preparation guidance, not legal advice; confirm disputes with local housing authorities or qualified counsel.

Risk: Rental discussions can expose personal documents and identifiers.

Mitigation: Share only necessary information in prompts and remove or mask personal identifiers.
