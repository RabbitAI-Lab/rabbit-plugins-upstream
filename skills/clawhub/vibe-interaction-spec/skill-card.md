## Description:

Translates vague visual and motion requests into implementable interface specifications with design tokens, timing, accessibility, performance constraints, and acceptance criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lijinhongucl-pixel](https://clawhub.ai/user/lijinhongucl-pixel)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and frontend developers use this skill to turn subjective visual or motion goals into actionable UI specifications, review interfaces, and guide interaction implementation across CSS/WAAPI, GSAP, three.js, and p5.js.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft-saving advice may lead to retaining sensitive information entered into forms.

Mitigation: Make draft saving visible and optional, exclude sensitive fields, and provide expiration and deletion controls.
