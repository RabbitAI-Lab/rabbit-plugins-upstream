## Description:

Enrich people and companies from a name, domain, or LinkedIn URL — emails, titles, firmographics, and mobile numbers for agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales agents, CRM hygiene agents, and prospecting pipelines use this skill to find or enrich person and company records, including work emails, job details, firmographics, and mobile numbers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead data submitted for enrichment is shared with Superagnt and its providers.

Mitigation: Send only lead data you intend to share with Superagnt and its providers.

Risk: Bulk lookups can consume service credits.

Mitigation: Check credit costs before running bulk lookups.

Risk: The MCP server URL alone does not establish which tools are enabled or isolated.

Mitigation: Verify the enabled tools when choosing the MCP connection.

Risk: Request schemas and command examples may need correction before use.

Mitigation: Obtain complete request schemas and corrected command examples before use.
