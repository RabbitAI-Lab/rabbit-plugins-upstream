## Description:

Enrich people and companies from a name, domain, or LinkedIn URL — emails, titles, firmographics, and mobile numbers for agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales, CRM hygiene, and prospecting agents use this skill to enrich people and company records from a name, domain, or LinkedIn URL. It returns available contact details and company context for research and record completion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends an API key and submitted lead information to Superagnt's service.

Mitigation: Use an API key for a trusted workspace and send only information you are authorized to share with the service.
