## Description:

Enrich people and companies from a name, domain, or LinkedIn URL — emails, titles, firmographics, and mobile numbers for agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Sales, prospecting, and CRM-hygiene agents use this skill to enrich people and company records from a name, domain, or LinkedIn URL, including work contact details and firmographic information.
