## Description:

Enrich people and companies from a name, domain, or LinkedIn URL with professional contact details and firmographics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and prospecting teams use this skill to find professional contact details, enrich company records, and fill gaps in CRM data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead and company data is sent to Superagnt for enrichment.

Mitigation: Use only data you are authorized to process and avoid oversharing bulk lists.

Risk: The API key is a sensitive credential.

Mitigation: Keep SUPERAGNT_API_KEY private and do not include it in shared outputs.
