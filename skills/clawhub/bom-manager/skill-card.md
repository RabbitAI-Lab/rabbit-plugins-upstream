## Description: <br>
Compare BOM versions, split mechanical/electrical materials, and merge multi-supplier BOMs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineers, and operations teams use this skill to manage BOM spreadsheet workflows for non-standard automation projects, including BOM comparison, mechanical/electrical splits, supplier list merges, and BOM change reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: BOM spreadsheets may contain sensitive supplier, sourcing, quantity, or cost data. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the BOM files needed for the specific compare, split, merge, or reporting task. <br>
Risk: Ambiguous requests can cause the skill to choose the wrong BOM operation or input files. <br>
Mitigation: State the desired operation and identify the intended input files explicitly. <br>
Risk: Mechanical/electrical classification can be uncertain when source BOM categories or brand signals are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review any uncertain or separately listed materials before using generated BOM outputs for procurement or engineering decisions. <br>
