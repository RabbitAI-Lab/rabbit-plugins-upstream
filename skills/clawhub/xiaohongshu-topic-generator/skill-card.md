## Description: <br>
Generates Xiaohongshu-ready topic ideas from a user theme, recent discussion signals, audience pain points, and platform fit. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and content strategists use this skill to turn a theme or content direction into Xiaohongshu topic candidates with hooks, card outlines, hashtags, scoring, and evidence checks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trend-aware topic ideas may imply current popularity without adequate evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use current sources when browsing is available; otherwise ask for source material or mark freshness as unverified. <br>
Risk: Private account details, unpublished material, or sensitive project context could be reflected in generated topic ideas. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing private or sensitive material unless it is intended for the current task. <br>
Risk: Generated hooks or claims may become exaggerated, absolute, or unverifiable. <br>
Mitigation: Keep claims conservative and mark unverifiable results, metrics, rankings, or trends as needing evidence. <br>
