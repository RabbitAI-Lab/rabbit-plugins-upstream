## Description:

Write an SEO blog post, how-to, listicle, comparison, refresh or localized article from an approved brief, answer-first and without generic AI prose.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content teams, marketers, and editors use this skill to turn approved briefs into SEO-oriented article drafts or refresh packages, with metadata, schema, links, asset planning, and fact-check notes. It flags brief defects when essential inputs are missing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished or confidential JSON-LD could be shared with an external validator.

Mitigation: Submit JSON-LD to an external validator only when the content is approved for sharing.

Risk: The security scan was partial because a referenced local path could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Check the unresolved path and treat the benign scan verdict as incomplete until then.
