## Description:

Turn a theme, lyric, scene, or reference audio into reviewable songs, instrumentals, background music, and soundtracks with a clear creative direction and focused revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can shape a creative brief, draft or refine lyrics, and prepare songs or instrumental tracks for generation. They can review returned audio and focus revisions on vocals, pronunciation, arrangement, duration, or loop behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates the release suspicious due to broad shared permissions, persistent installation tracking, and shared-token and uninstall concerns; analysis was partial.

Mitigation: Before installing, ask the provider to explain and narrow permissions, disclose and offer control over tracking, and address token isolation and uninstall behavior.
