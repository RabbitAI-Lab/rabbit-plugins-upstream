## Description: <br>
Provides nine local Python modules that apply business frameworks from Drucker, Collins, Christensen, Porter, and Munger to structured business inputs and return diagnostics, insights, and recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business operators, executives, strategy teams, HR leaders, innovation owners, investors, and AI advisor agents use this skill to run structured management diagnostics and decision-support workflows across strategy, organization, leadership, decision analysis, goals, and execution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat rule-based business recommendations as professional legal, financial, or investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as decision-support guidance and require qualified human review before material business, legal, financial, investment, or personnel decisions. <br>
Risk: Helper scripts can read JSON inputs and write an output JSON file when explicitly directed. <br>
Mitigation: Run the tools on intended local input files only and choose output paths deliberately to avoid overwriting important files. <br>
