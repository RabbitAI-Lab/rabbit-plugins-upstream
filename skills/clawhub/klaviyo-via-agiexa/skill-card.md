## Use Case:

Shopify merchants can use this preview to understand planned Klaviyo workflows and Agiexa's integration, and may request launch waitlist signup with explicit consent. The skill cannot access Shopify or Klaviyo today.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the preview for working Shopify or Klaviyo access, or treat future security claims as verified.

Mitigation: Make clear that no app access is available today; evaluate the provider's permissions and backend separately before connecting after launch.

Risk: Waitlist messages could expose sensitive information or be submitted without informed consent.

Mitigation: Show the exact details to be sent, obtain explicit confirmation, and keep secrets out of the optional message.
