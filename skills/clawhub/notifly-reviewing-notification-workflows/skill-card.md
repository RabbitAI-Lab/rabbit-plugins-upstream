## Description:

Reviews a connected Notifly environment's workflows, channels, topics, and recent notification delivery activity without sending notifications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams managing a Notifly environment use this skill to review workflow steps and channels, identify topics, and inspect recent delivery outcomes without changing or sending notifications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Read access can expose workflow names, channels, topics, and recent delivery outcomes from the selected Notifly environment.

Mitigation: Grant access only to the intended environment and share review results only with authorized recipients.

Risk: A recent activity sample may be mistaken for comprehensive delivery analytics.

Mitigation: Describe observed events without claiming delivery rates or trends the sample does not support.
