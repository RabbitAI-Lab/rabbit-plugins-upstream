## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language workflow for preliminary biopharma freedom-to-operate analysis across ADCs, antibodies, oligonucleotides, cell and gene therapies, and small molecules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent, R&D, and business development teams use this skill to structure preliminary FTO searches, jurisdiction status checks, claim charts, risk grading, and counsel review questions for biopharma products or platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Preliminary FTO conclusions may be incomplete or unsuitable for business decisions without legal review.

Mitigation: Require qualified patent counsel to review high- and medium-risk findings before licensing, launch, manufacturing, import, or other commercial decisions.

Risk: The workflow is Chinese-language and may be misapplied by users who cannot review its assumptions, sources, and disclaimers accurately.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with reviewers who can evaluate the Chinese-language report and confirm market, product, claim, and legal-status evidence.
