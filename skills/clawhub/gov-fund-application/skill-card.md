## Description:

Helps applicants assess Chinese government fund eligibility, choose suitable funds, plan local investment commitments, and prepare application materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and business teams use this skill to identify potentially suitable Chinese government funds, assess local investment requirements, and plan application materials and timelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fund criteria, local policies, and application channels may change before submission.

Mitigation: Confirm current requirements and deadlines with the relevant fund manager or local government department before applying.

Risk: Inaccurate claims about eligibility or local investment commitments could compromise an application.

Mitigation: Check qualifications and commitments against official rules, submit truthful materials, and seek qualified legal or financial advice when needed.
