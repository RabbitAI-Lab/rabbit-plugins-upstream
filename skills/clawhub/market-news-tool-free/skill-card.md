## Description: <br>
Helps personal investors and independent researchers search, summarize, and interpret current financial news, market summaries, policies, and company announcements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External personal investors and independent researchers use this skill to ask an agent for concise finance-news briefs, policy interpretation, market overviews, and single-company announcement analysis. It provides informational support and is not a substitute for investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated market summaries or policy interpretations may be incorrect, stale, or incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational, cross-check important claims against primary sources, and do not use them as investment advice. <br>
Risk: The skill may search current financial news and optionally use shell commands for local preference or output files. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm before creating, importing, exporting, modifying, or resetting local files or configuration. <br>
