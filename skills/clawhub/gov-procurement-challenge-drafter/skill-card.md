## Description: <br>
Helps suppliers and procurement practitioners draft, revise, and check Chinese government procurement challenge letters and complaint documents under the Government Procurement Law framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users such as suppliers and procurement practitioners use this skill to create first-pass challenge letters or complaint drafts, check required document elements, and surface common acceptance-risk issues for Chinese government procurement disputes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafts may be unsuitable for real submission if laws, project facts, deadlines, evidence, or local finance authority practice are outdated or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current law, original procurement materials, evidence, deadlines, and local practice before filing; use a lawyer or dedicated compliance support for high-value or complex disputes. <br>
Risk: Requests outside the government procurement challenge and complaint scope could lead to incorrect legal framing. <br>
Mitigation: Keep use limited to Government Procurement Law challenge and complaint documents; route contract performance, acceptance, payment, bidding-law, and other legal-document requests to appropriate professional support. <br>
