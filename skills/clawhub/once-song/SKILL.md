# Once Song

This skill helps an agent guide a user through [Once Song](https://oncesong.com/): a personalized song gift shaped around someone they love, the memories they share, and the feeling they want to capture — with the recipient’s name woven into the chorus.

This skill only plans memory worksheets, lyric-detail tips, and gift steps. It does not generate audio, call an API, process payments, or replace the live guided questionnaire.

## When to Use

- The user wants a personalized song gift for a partner, parent, friend, or other loved one.
- They need help collecting concrete memories before starting the questionnaire.
- They ask how to preview privately, unlock, or share the song link safely.

Do not invent prices, guaranteed delivery times, or lyrics. Confirm timing and unlock options on the live site.

## What the product offers (from the live site)

- Four steps: **share your story** → **choose the feeling** (mood, genre, voice) → **hear privately** → **unlock the complete keepsake**.
- You can **create and listen for free**; no credit card is required to get started. Unlocking the complete song is a one-time purchase shown at checkout — this skill does not list prices.
- Preview of up to about **90 seconds** before unlock (Terms). Production progress updates on the same page after submit. Timing is advertised as **as fast as about 30 minutes** — confirm live.
- Personal details that work well: names (including in the chorus), shared memories, places, favorite qualities, inside jokes, promises, and a heartfelt message.
- Occasions called out on the site include anniversary, birthday, wedding, proposal, holidays, thank-you, celebration, memorial, and more. Seasonal Christmas edition: [https://oncesong.com/christmas](https://oncesong.com/christmas).
- The song identifier / private link is a **bearer credential** — anyone with the link can open the song page. Keep it private until ready to gift.
- Support: support@oncesong.com · Policies: Terms, Privacy, Refund Policy on the site.

Confirm any detail that may change against [oncesong.com](https://oncesong.com/) before promising it.

## Instructions

1. **Clarify the gift.** Ask who it is for, the occasion, mood (romantic, grateful, upbeat, comforting…), and whether they already have a private listening deadline.
2. **Gather concrete memories.** Prefer short scenes over abstract praise: first meeting, a place, a habit, a promise, an inside joke, what you hope they feel hearing the chorus.
3. **Lyric-detail tips.** Include the recipient’s name if they want it in the chorus; keep 3–5 vivid specifics rather than a long biography; avoid secrets the recipient would not want sung aloud.
4. **Send them to the product.** Prefer [https://oncesong.com/](https://oncesong.com/) → **Create Your Personalized Song**. Paste or retell answers in the guided questions; choose mood, genre, and voice; stay on the page for live production progress.
5. **Preview and unlock.** Listen privately first. Unlock the complete song only when the preview feels right. Do not invent checkout prices.
6. **Share safely.** Treat the song link like a password until gift time. For Christmas/seasonal flows, use the dedicated page when relevant.
7. **Boundaries.** Do not generate audio offline, scrape private song pages, or claim medical/therapeutic outcomes from a song gift.

## Examples

### Example 1 — anniversary

**Input:** "I need an anniversary song about how we met at a coffee shop."

**Output:**
> Jot 3–5 concrete details (the coffee shop, a habit that makes you laugh, a promise you share, their name). Then open [Once Song](https://oncesong.com/), start Create Your Personalized Song, answer the guided questions, pick mood/genre/voice, and listen privately before unlocking.

### Example 2 — parents

**Input:** "Song for my mum’s birthday — her garden and an old lullaby."

**Output:**
> Capture a few garden moments, the lullaby line you remember, and what you want her to feel. Create on [Once Song](https://oncesong.com/), preview privately, then unlock when it sounds right. Keep the song link private until you play it for her.
