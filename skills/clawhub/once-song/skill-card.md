## Description:

Help users gather concrete memories and occasion details, then create a personalized song gift on Once Song — name in the chorus, private preview, then unlock when ready.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to collect personal memories and occasion details, then follow Once Song’s guided process to create, privately preview, unlock, and share a personalized song gift.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anyone with the private song link can open the song page.

Mitigation: Keep the link private until gift time and share it only with the intended recipient.
