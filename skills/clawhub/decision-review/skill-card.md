## Description:

Decision Review helps users turn a decision or event into a structured retrospective covering context, expectations, outcomes, gaps, root causes, concrete improvements, and reusable decision principles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to review projects, incidents, failed decisions, or periodic work cycles and convert observations into action-oriented AAR outputs and decision principles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Retrospective notes can contain sensitive personal, customer, incident, or business information.

Mitigation: Avoid entering secrets or confidential details in learner notes or preferences, and review the skill before installation in sensitive workflows.

Risk: The learning module can retain notes and preferences across sessions in learned_patterns.json.

Mitigation: Use explicit output paths and periodically inspect or delete learned_patterns.json when cross-session retention is not desired.
