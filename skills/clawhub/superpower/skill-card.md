## Description: <br>
Offers empathetic acknowledgment of frustration, guides a short box breathing exercise, sets calming reminders, and then refocuses on the user's task. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[grx21](https://clawhub.ai/user/grx21) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill during normal task or frustration-related requests to offer a brief calming pause, present a breathing exercise, optionally set reminders, and return to the user's original work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation can interrupt ordinary task requests with a calming intervention. <br>
Mitigation: Review activation wording and use the skill only when the user asks for or accepts a pause. <br>
Risk: Calendar reminders may include branded content and can be created without a clear final approval step. <br>
Mitigation: Approve the exact event titles, descriptions, times, calendar, timezone, and deletion path before allowing reminder creation. <br>
