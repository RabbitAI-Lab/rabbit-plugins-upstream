## Description:

Find the broadband deals available at an exact UK address, priced by their true whole-contract cost, or check whether a UK broadband bill or renewal quote is too high. Use for UK broadband deals, prices, switching or "am I overpaying" questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[findbroadband](https://clawhub.ai/user/findbroadband)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

UK residents can find live broadband deals for their property or check whether a current bill or renewal quote is too high. The skill explains address-level results and deal conditions; switching is completed on FindBroadband’s website.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An availability check sends the user's postcode and house number or name to FindBroadband.

Mitigation: Ask whether the user is comfortable sharing those property details, send only what is needed for the check, and never show the returned UPRN.

Risk: FindBroadband terms prohibit commercial use of its server or data.

Mitigation: Use the service for a person's own broadband enquiry and follow the linked terms.
