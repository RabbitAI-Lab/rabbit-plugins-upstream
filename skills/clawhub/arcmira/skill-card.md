## Description:

Find what was said on YouTube, including podcasts on YouTube. Returns the exact quote, often who said it, the channel, the date and a watch link cued to the second. Filters by who is speaking, by the person or company discussed, and by sponsored or organic passages. Needs ARCMIRA_API_KEY.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[arcmira](https://clawhub.ai/user/arcmira)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users can search YouTube videos and podcasts for spoken passages by topic, speaker, or person or company discussed, then share the quote with its attribution and timestamped source link.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search topics and names are sent to Arcmira.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive information in search requests.
