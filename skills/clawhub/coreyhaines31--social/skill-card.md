## Description: <br>
Helps create, schedule, optimize, and repurpose social media content, plus perform social listening and engagement triage across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and related platforms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and teams use this skill to plan platform-specific social strategy, draft posts and carousels, repurpose long-form assets, script short-form video, and triage engagement opportunities. It is useful when a user needs practical social content guidance with platform constraints, posting cadence, hooks, calendars, analytics, or listening workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent may view social-media pages through a logged-in browser session for LinkedIn/X listening workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use only source lists and saved searches the user intends the agent to inspect, and avoid exposing accounts, searches, or sessions unrelated to the task. <br>
Risk: Drafted comments or posts could be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for publication. <br>
Mitigation: Require the user to review, edit, and manually approve all drafted comments and posts before anything is published. <br>
