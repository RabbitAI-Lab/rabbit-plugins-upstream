## Description:

Reviews social-media copy for advertising and regulated-category claims, audience fit, and engagement, then suggests edits and can create a corrected cover.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, brands, and social-media teams use the skill to screen captions, scripts, titles, on-screen text, and tags for advertising or regulated-category claims, assess audience response, and receive revised copy. They can also request a corrected cover image.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests media and voice permissions broader than its text-review workflow needs and includes recurring installation analytics.

Mitigation: Use text-only review when suitable; before connecting, consider the permissions and analytics, explicitly approve any cover upload and render price, and revoke the shared device connection when it is no longer needed.
