## Description:

Helps creators adapt a short-video reference into a product-specific script and generate a similar marketing video through Qinghu AI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use a Douyin or TikTok video link to draft a product-specific script, review it, and generate a short marketing video inspired by the reference's pacing and shots.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing or upgrading qhkit may change the user's environment.

Mitigation: Prefer platform-managed installation or a pinned package version; get approval before global installation or shell startup PATH changes.

Risk: A Qinghu token and selected media or links are shared with the service.

Mitigation: Use the service only with the user's trust and consent; share only intended media and keep the token private.

Risk: Video generation and reference media may consume account credits.

Mitigation: Estimate charges where supported, show the revised script for approval, and confirm before submitting generation.

Risk: Copying a reference video's footage or wording may infringe others' rights.

Mitigation: Adapt the structure and creative approach without copying original media or dialogue.
