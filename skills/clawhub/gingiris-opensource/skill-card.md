## Description: <br>
Gingiris Opensource helps developer tool founders, OSS maintainers, and DevRel teams plan open-source launches, GitHub star growth, README conversion, community distribution, and longer-term developer marketing loops. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, founders, OSS maintainers, and DevRel teams use this skill to prepare and run open-source go-to-market campaigns across README optimization, Show HN, Reddit, Product Hunt, KOL outreach, community setup, and post-launch growth measurement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outreach, direct-message, Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt, and coordinated launch recommendations may violate platform or community rules if applied mechanically. <br>
Mitigation: Review each campaign against the current rules and norms of the target platform or community before posting; prefer consent-based outreach, transparent disclosure, and organic engagement. <br>
Risk: Marketing benchmarks and AFFiNE case data may be mistaken for guaranteed outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the playbook as strategic guidance, date and source performance claims where reused, and evaluate results with installation, activation, retention, community quality, and conversion metrics rather than stars alone. <br>
