## Description: <br>
Pressure-test a fundraise by anticipating the hard investor questions and arming the founder with crisp answers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Founders and fundraising teams use this skill to prepare for investor Q&A, anticipate due-diligence pushback, and draft concise answers that address known weak spots honestly. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive fundraising metrics, customer information, or team details supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering highly confidential fundraising or customer details unless they are appropriate for the active agent session. <br>
Risk: Generated fundraising answers may include assumptions about missing company metrics or market facts. <br>
Mitigation: Review all assumed metrics and claims before using the output with investors. <br>
