## Description:

Create or revise construction drawing sheets as self-contained HTML and scaled PDFs, with plans, sections, details, title blocks, and tracked open items. Use for HTML drawing sets and permit-sheet drafting; not for structural design calculations or native CAD/DWG editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oasiseng](https://clawhub.ai/user/oasiseng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External designers, engineers, and builders use this skill to draft or revise evidence-based construction drawing sets from project information. It produces an editable HTML set and, when available, a scaled PDF while tracking unresolved criteria and preserving approval status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports a likely mismatch in generated drawing dimensions and incomplete content inspection; its suspicious verdict does not establish malicious intent.

Mitigation: Consider holding off on installation until the dimension issue is reviewed and the partially inspected content is checked.
