## Description:

Researches software products, business reputation, startup revenue, and crowdfunding campaigns through Crawlora's public-data API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, procurement teams, and other users assessing vendors or products use this skill to compare public reviews, business profiles, revenue listings, product launches, and campaign updates before making decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business names, product names, campaign identifiers, and search queries are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Do not submit secrets, credentials, private customer data, or confidential investigation details in searches.
