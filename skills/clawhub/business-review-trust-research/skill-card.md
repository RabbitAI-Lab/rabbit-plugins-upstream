## Description:

Researches software products, business reputation, startup revenue, and crowdfunding campaigns through Crawlora's public business and review data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business researchers, buyers, and developers compare product reviews, investigate vendor reputation, check startup revenue listings, and review crowdfunding campaigns before decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Crawlora API receives the API key and queried business names, search terms, campaign identifiers, or BBB URLs.

Mitigation: Use an environment-provided API key and avoid querying private or sensitive subjects unless sharing them with Crawlora is acceptable.
