## Description:

Turns briefs, documents, and spreadsheets into editable presentations with source citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[youteacher](https://clawhub.ai/user/youteacher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users preparing cited slide decks can inspect their materials, review a 6–15-slide outline, and request editable presentation files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded source material is sent to the AI Skills service.

Mitigation: Send only material you are comfortable sharing with the service; review it before upload.

Risk: Outline and generation requests may incur API charges.

Mitigation: Review the documented pricing and approve the outline before requesting files.

Risk: Generated claims or partial previews and quality checks may be incomplete.

Mitigation: Check source citations and have business, data, and brand owners review files before external sharing.

Risk: Exposing an API key could allow unauthorized use.

Mitigation: Keep keys out of chat and logs; use a product-specific key for tighter separation.
