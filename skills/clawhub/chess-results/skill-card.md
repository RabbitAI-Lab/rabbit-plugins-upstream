## Description:

Find Chess-Results players, tournaments, and league results, and help check and enter photographed team match reports with user approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[odrobnik](https://clawhub.ai/user/odrobnik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chess players, club officials, and their agents can look up public player and tournament information, check photographed team match reports against league records, and enter approved results into Chess-Results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Entering results uses a Chess-Results account and publishes saved results to the league.

Mitigation: Use the check workflow first, review the completed report and log, and explicitly approve saving only when the results are correct.
