## Description:

汽车经销商客诉话术生成器。当用户描述一个汽车客诉场景（如漏水、异响、保养纠纷、交付延期、新媒体投诉、12315介入等），生成专业的应对话术、处理流程和升级建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External dealership staff such as aftersales managers, CRM managers, customer rights officers, and service advisors use this skill to draft professional response scripts, handling steps, escalation notes, internal coordination requests, and manager summaries for automotive customer complaints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include unnecessary personal data, internal records, contracts, IDs, phone numbers, VINs, or complete chat logs while seeking complaint-handling language.

Mitigation: Use placeholders and include only necessary, authorized details when drafting complaint responses.

Risk: Generated complaint-handling scripts may need review for factual accuracy, authorization boundaries, and dealership policy fit before use with customers or regulators.

Mitigation: Have the responsible dealership role review facts, commitments, escalation needs, and regulatory responses before sending or acting on the draft.
