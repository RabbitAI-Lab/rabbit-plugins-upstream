# Stage SOP — Validate

**Goal:** prove a named audience has a repeated, costly/important problem and will commit to a solution.

1. Write: `{project} helps {ICP} {job} without {pain/current workaround}`.
2. Interview 3–5 genuine ICPs about the **last incident**, workaround, frequency, consequences, authority, and spend. Do not pitch first.
3. Use project-scoped monitoring only as supporting evidence; one viral complaint is not a market.
4. Offer the smallest commitment appropriate to the project type: follow-up meeting, data/time, manual solution, preorder, deposit, paid audit/pilot, workshop, or transaction.
5. Record exact wording, disconfirming evidence, and `not for`.

**Success:** repeated recent pain from the same narrow ICP plus behavior/commitment stronger than compliments.

**Never:** broad launch, scaled ads, friend praise as validation, ten ideas at once, or building a roadmap from generic public pains.
