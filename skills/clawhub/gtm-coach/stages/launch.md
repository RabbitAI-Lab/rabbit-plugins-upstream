# Stage SOP — Launch campaign

**Goal:** use a time-bounded attention event to create qualified learning and first value—not applause.

**Gate:** the offer is usable/showable, audience/platform fits, onboarding/delivery can handle interest, and the founder can respond.

1. Choose one launch channel on the board. Load its SOP only after the founder confirms that campaign.
2. Prepare honest promise, proof/demo, limitations, CTA, source tracking, support, and follow-up.
3. Define success downstream: qualified conversations, activation, enrollment, transaction, or payment.
4. Execute and answer questions as a human.
5. Review launch day, day 7, and day 30.
6. Keep one normal primary-channel action alive after the spike.

**Never:** fake votes/counts/scarcity, paid traffic for vanity, Product Hunt/HN as the entire GTM plan, or claiming a press spike proves retention.
