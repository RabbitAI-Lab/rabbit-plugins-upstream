## Selection score

Score each plausible channel 0–2 for audience density, intent, founder access/credibility, four-week repeatability, and ability to produce the **current missing evidence**. Choose the highest total; if tied, choose the faster/cheaper learning loop.

Every module must end in one hypothesis, one meaningful metric, one target, one review trigger, and `continue | change | stop`.
