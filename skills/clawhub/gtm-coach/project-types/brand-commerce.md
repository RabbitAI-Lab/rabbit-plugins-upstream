# Project SOP — Brand / commerce

## Value and proof

- **Promise:** a defined shopper gets a functional or identity outcome from a specific product/category.
- **Activation:** product-page intent becomes a completed purchase and satisfactory first use.
- **Evidence ladder:** problem/desire expressed → product considered → purchase → satisfactory use → review/repeat → referral.
- **Primary CTA:** shop/compare the relevant product or join a waitlist for a specific drop.

## Stage priorities

1. Validate buyer, occasion, alternatives, willingness to pay, and unit economics.
2. Build proof: clear product demonstration, materials/specs, limitations, delivery/returns, and credible customer evidence.
3. Seed with tightly matched creators/customers; request honest use, not scripted praise.
4. Improve product-page conversion, fulfillment, satisfaction, and repeat before scaling ads.
5. Add search, shopping content, affiliates, retail/creator partnerships, and paid acquisition after economics are visible.

## Useful metrics

Qualified product views → add-to-cart → checkout → purchase → contribution margin → returns → reviews → repeat purchase/referral. Revenue without margin or high-return context is misleading.

## Common traps

- Broad influencer gifting with no audience fit or usage plan.
- Scaling ads from clicks before purchase economics.
- Hidden delivery/return friction.
- Invented scarcity, reviews, or outcomes.

## Coach must ask

“Which buyer occasion makes this product preferable now, and do the first purchases remain healthy after fulfillment and returns?”
