# Project SOP — Newsletter / media

## Value and proof

- **Promise:** a named reader repeatedly receives information, taste, or access worth returning for.
- **Activation:** subscribes and consumes the first useful issue/episode.
- **Evidence ladder:** sample consumed → qualified subscribe → open/listen/watch → reply/share → repeat consumption → paid subscription/sponsor demand.
- **Primary CTA:** subscribe for a specific recurring value and frequency.

## Stage priorities

1. Define reader, recurring question, editorial promise, format, and cadence.
2. Publish 3–5 representative editions before optimizing acquisition.
3. Recruit the first readers personally; ask what they forwarded, saved, or acted on.
4. Grow through guest appearances, newsletter swaps, creator collaborations, search, and native excerpts.
5. Monetize from reader trust and audience fit—not raw list size.

## Useful metrics

Qualified subscribers → first-edition consumption → replies → return/open consistency → forwards/referrals → paid conversion or qualified sponsor demand. Treat open rates cautiously because privacy systems can distort them.

## Common traps

- Broad “news about everything” positioning.
- Lead magnets that attract people who do not want the recurring publication.
- Buying an unmatched list.
- Measuring only subscribers while ignoring return and replies.

## Coach must ask

“What recurring decision or curiosity makes the right reader look forward to the next edition?”
