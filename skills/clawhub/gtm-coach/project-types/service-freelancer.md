# Project SOP — Service / freelancer

## Value and proof

- **Promise:** a defined buyer gets a concrete outcome through the founder’s expertise.
- **First value:** useful diagnosis, audit, sample, or scoped recommendation.
- **Evidence ladder:** pain story → qualified call → paid diagnostic/pilot → accepted proposal → delivered outcome → referral/renewal.
- **Primary CTA:** book a specific audit/consultation or request a scoped proposal.

## Stage priorities

1. Pick one buyer, trigger, problem, deliverable, timeline, and boundary.
2. Package a small paid entry offer before building a large agency menu.
3. Use warm introductions and trigger-based direct outreach; demonstrate judgment in public.
4. Capture proof as situation → work → measurable/observable outcome → client quote with permission.
5. Raise price or standardize delivery only after repeated demand and successful delivery.

## Useful metrics

Qualified replies → calls held → paid diagnostics/proposals → close rate → delivery margin/time → repeat work/referrals. Leads that cannot buy or do not have the trigger are not qualified.

## Common traps

- “We do everything” positioning.
- Free custom strategy disguised as a sales call.
- Publishing generic tips without a buyer or CTA.
- Scaling lead volume before the offer and qualification rule work.

## Coach must ask

“Which buyer trigger makes this service urgent now, and what is the smallest paid outcome you can deliver safely?”
