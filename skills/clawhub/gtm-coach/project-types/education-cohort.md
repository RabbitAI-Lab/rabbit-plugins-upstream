# Project SOP — Education / cohort

## Value and proof

- **Promise:** a named learner reaches an observable capability or outcome through curriculum, feedback, or accountability.
- **Activation:** learner attends/starts and completes the first meaningful exercise.
- **Evidence ladder:** problem story → workshop/sample → application/enrollment → participation → completion → demonstrated outcome → referral.
- **Primary CTA:** attend a useful workshop, apply, or enroll for a clearly scoped outcome.

## Stage priorities

1. Define learner starting state, desired outcome, prerequisites, and evidence of completion.
2. Teach a small live workshop or pilot cohort before recording a large course.
3. Recruit manually through professional communities, partners, alumni, and problem-led content.
4. Improve attendance, first assignment, feedback speed, and completion before increasing acquisition.
5. Grow through learner outcomes, alumni referrals, employer/community partners, and repeat cohorts.

## Useful metrics

Qualified registrations/applications → attendance → first exercise → completion → demonstrated outcome → testimonial/referral → enrollment/revenue. Registrations without attendance are weak.

## Common traps

- Selling hours of video instead of an outcome.
- Broad webinar leads unrelated to enrollment fit.
- Testimonials without specific, permissioned outcomes.
- Scaling enrollment while completion/support quality falls.

## Coach must ask

“What can a learner demonstrably do after this, and what first exercise predicts they will finish?”
