# Project SOP — Creator / artist

## Value and proof

- **Promise:** a defined audience receives a distinctive perspective, craft, story, entertainment, or transformation.
- **Activation:** consumes enough of one work to experience the promise, then follows or joins an owned audience.
- **Evidence ladder:** watches/reads/listens → completes/saves/replies → returns → joins list/community → commissions/buys/supports → refers.
- **Primary CTA:** watch/read the next relevant work, join the list, commission, or buy—choose one.

## Stage priorities

1. Define audience, creative promise, repeatable format, and body of work.
2. Publish a small series to learn what earns completion and response.
3. Participate in adjacent creator/audience communities and collaborate from genuine fit.
4. Turn native reach into an owned relationship with a proportionate CTA.
5. Monetize with offers consistent with audience trust and actual demand.

## Useful metrics

Qualified reach → completion/retention → saves/shares/replies → return viewers/readers → owned-audience joins → commissions/sales/support. Followers without repeat consumption are weak proof.

## Common traps

- Copying trends with no recognizable promise.
- Changing niche after every low-view post.
- Sponsorships that break audience trust.
- Depending on one platform without an owned contact path.

## Coach must ask

“Which repeatable creative promise should the right person recognize after seeing three pieces?”
