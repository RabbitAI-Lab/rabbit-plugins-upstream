# Project SOP — Community

## Value and proof

- **Promise:** a defined group gets belonging, access, accountability, or shared progress they cannot get alone.
- **Activation:** a qualified member joins and completes the first meaningful participation event.
- **Evidence ladder:** accepts invitation → introduces/contributes → receives response/value → returns → invites another fit → renews/pays.
- **Primary CTA:** join for a specific member outcome and ritual—not “network with everyone.”

## Stage priorities

1. Define who belongs, who does not, the shared outcome, and the recurring ritual.
2. Recruit a small founding cohort personally.
3. Seed useful conversations and introductions; do not fake activity.
4. Improve time-to-first-response and member-to-member value before broad promotion.
5. Grow through member outcomes, referrals, partnerships, and public artifacts with consent.

## Useful metrics

Qualified joins → introduction/first contribution → response received → weekly/monthly active members → cohort retention → member referrals → paid renewal. Member count without participation is vanity.

## Common traps

- Empty room after a broad launch.
- Founder broadcasts with no member-to-member exchange.
- Adding channels/events without a repeatable core ritual.
- Optimizing acquisition while new members receive no response.

## Coach must ask

“What happens in the first seven days that makes the right member say this group is worth returning to?”
