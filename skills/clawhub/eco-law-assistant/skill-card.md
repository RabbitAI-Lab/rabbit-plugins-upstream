## Description: <br>
Eco Law Assistant helps agents query PRC Ecological Environment Code provisions, compliance checklists, abolished laws, subject responsibilities, and expert commentary from bundled offline data. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[whichonewang-eng](https://clawhub.ai/user/whichonewang-eng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and legal or compliance teams use this skill to look up PRC ecological environment law articles, run preliminary compliance checks, inspect subject-specific duties, and retrieve bundled expert commentary. Outputs are reference material and should not be treated as final legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with legal content focused on the People's Republic of China. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat legal or compliance output as final legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present results as reference material and verify important answers against current official legal sources or qualified counsel before business or regulatory reliance. <br>
Risk: Bundled legal and commentary data can become outdated as laws, interpretations, or official guidance change. <br>
Mitigation: Check current official sources before using outputs in compliance decisions, filings, enforcement responses, or formal legal documents. <br>
Risk: Expert commentary and book-derived guidance may help explain the law but does not itself have legal force. <br>
Mitigation: Use commentary for context only and cite the official code article text for formal analysis or documentation. <br>
