## Description:

Eco Law Assistant helps agents answer Chinese ecological environment code questions, retrieve code articles, and produce AI-assisted compliance pre-check guidance from bundled legal and commentary data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whichonewang-eng](https://clawhub.ai/user/whichonewang-eng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and compliance reviewers use this skill to look up Chinese ecological environment code provisions, explore code structure, check subject responsibilities, and generate AI-assisted enterprise environmental compliance pre-checks. It is suitable for legal education and compliance triage, not for replacing formal legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat legal and compliance answers as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as AI-assisted references, cite the relevant original code articles, and require qualified legal review for formal decisions.

Risk: Bundled legal or commentary data may become outdated or incomplete for a specific compliance decision.

Mitigation: Verify material answers against the official legal source and current authoritative materials before relying on them.
