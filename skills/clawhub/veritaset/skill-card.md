## Description:

Use for questions about mobile phone plan prices, SIM-only deals, switching plans or saving on a phone bill (and home internet offers) in Europe and the US. Veritaset returns verified current prices with the operator's source link and check date, ranked by a public method with no paid placement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Consumers use Veritaset to compare mobile or home internet offers, find plans that fit their needs, and assess potential savings before switching.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

32 European markets and the United States; home internet availability varies by market.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plan comparisons use details the user shares about their current plan and needs.

Mitigation: Share only details needed for the comparison and omit optional price or travel information when it is unnecessary.

Risk: Indicative plan prices or promotions may differ from the operator's final terms.

Mitigation: Check the operator's source and confirm the final terms before switching.
