## Description:

Use for questions about mobile phone plan prices, SIM-only deals, switching plans or saving on a phone bill (and home internet offers) in Europe and the US. Veritaset returns verified current prices with the operator's source link and check date, ranked by a public method with no paid placement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Consumers use Veritaset to compare mobile plans and selected home internet offers, find potential savings from their current plan, and review plan details and ranking rules. The skill helps people account for their usage, contract preferences, travel, and fees when choosing an offer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Europe and the United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plan comparisons may involve sharing mobile usage and travel estimates with Veritaset.

Mitigation: Share aggregate usage and coarse travel estimates; omit names, phone numbers, and full bills.

Risk: Prices and estimated travel costs may differ from the final offer.

Mitigation: Show the operator source and check date, identify estimates, and verify the final price at the operator's checkout.
