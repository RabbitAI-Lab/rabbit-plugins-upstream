## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language wealth and business decision-support skill pack that routes questions about earning, business models, marketing, pricing, investing, management, personal brand, speaking, relationships, and time management through a layered framework of 24 wealth rules and 22 mental models. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent operators use this skill to generate structured Chinese guidance for business, wealth, strategy, marketing, investment, legal/tax awareness, personal branding, relationships, speaking, and productivity questions. It is best treated as a decision-support and planning framework, not as professional legal, tax, investment, or ethics advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad local file permissions may expose private notes and user profile data. <br>
Mitigation: Review and narrow or remove the local read permissions in .claude/settings.local.json before installation unless those paths are explicitly required. <br>
Risk: The skill provides business, legal, tax, investment, and ethics-related guidance that may be incomplete or jurisdiction-specific. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and consult qualified professionals before acting on legal, tax, investment, or high-stakes business recommendations. <br>
Risk: Policy, platform-rule, market, and regulatory guidance can become stale. <br>
Mitigation: Require current-date source checks for time-sensitive topics and record the retrieval date in generated analyses. <br>
