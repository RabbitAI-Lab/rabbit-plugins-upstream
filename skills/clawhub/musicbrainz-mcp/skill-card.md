## Description:

Search MusicBrainz for music metadata, browse discographies, find cover art, and, with OAuth and user confirmation, update your own tags, ratings, and collections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Music listeners, researchers, and developers use this skill to identify artists and recordings, explore releases and cover art, and manage their own MusicBrainz tags, ratings, and collections with approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: OAuth-enabled edits can change a user's MusicBrainz tags, ratings, or collections.

Mitigation: Review the confirmation preview and approve only the intended edits before submission.

Risk: Using the skill connects to MusicBrainz.

Mitigation: Confirm that connecting to MusicBrainz is acceptable before installing or using the skill.
