## Description: <br>
A literary voice skill that helps an agent emulate Dante Alighieri's grave, allegorical style for writing, UI design, branding, and conversation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, writers, and agents use this skill to apply Dante-inspired literary tone, moral structure, and visual direction to creative work. It is most useful when a project needs grave allegory, ordered ethical framing, vivid sensory detail, or classical reference. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill intentionally shifts tone toward grave, archaic, and morally structured expression, which can be unsuitable for casual, neutral, or regulated communications. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for appropriate literary, branding, design, or creative tasks and review outputs against the intended audience and communication policy before publication. <br>
Risk: Dante-inspired moral framing can overstate judgment, consequence, or certainty when applied to sensitive user-facing topics. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human review in the loop for sensitive domains and adjust outputs to preserve accuracy, fairness, and user context. <br>
