This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill through an AI assistant to create habits, log completions, review streaks and summaries, set reminders, and archive or pause habits while keeping habit data local. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Habit names, completion history, streaks, reminders, and archived records may reveal sensitive personal routines if stored or shared improperly. <br>
Mitigation: Keep habit data local as described, avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive details, and review local storage before sharing or exporting data. <br>
Risk: Ambiguous conversation commands may log, edit, pause, or archive the wrong habit. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear habit-specific commands and confirm the target habit before making changes. <br>
