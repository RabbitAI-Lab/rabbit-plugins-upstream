## Description: <br>
Reviews and edits blog posts, essays, newsletters, and other authorial prose for spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, flow, and style while preserving the author's meaning, vocabulary, personality, tone, and rhetorical choices. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and writers use this skill to review or apply conservative edits to authorial prose while preserving voice, meaning, and formatting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Editorial changes may alter meaning, voice, or factual posture if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review applied edits before publishing, especially for important or sensitive writing. <br>
