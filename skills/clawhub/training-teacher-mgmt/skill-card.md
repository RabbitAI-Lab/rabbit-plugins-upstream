## Description: <br>
Training Teacher Mgmt helps manage a long-term teacher resource database, including instructor registration, credential review, teaching domains, delivery records, ratings, compensation standards, and schedule availability. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
HR, training operations, and education program teams use this skill to maintain lecturer profiles, qualifications, teaching histories, ratings, fee standards, and availability records. It supports long-term teacher database management rather than one-off event scheduling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Teacher personal data, credential files, ratings, and compensation information may be sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Define consent or lawful basis before use with real records, restrict access, secure uploaded documents, and establish retention and deletion practices. <br>
Risk: Lecturer ratings, qualification status, and fee records can affect HR or vendor decisions if inaccurate. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review of imported records, rating changes, credential decisions, and compensation summaries before operational use. <br>
