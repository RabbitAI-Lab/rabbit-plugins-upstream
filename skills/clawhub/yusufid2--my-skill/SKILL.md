## Overview

## Key Features

### Market Data Engine

* Real-time market price monitoring
* Multi-asset support
* Historical price collection
* Market trend tracking

### Technical Analysis

* Moving Average (MA)
* Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
* Relative Strength Index (RSI)
* MACD
* Support and Resistance Detection
* Volatility Measurement

### Signal Generation

Generates:

* BUY
* SELL
* HOLD

Signals are produced using configurable rule-based strategies.

### Risk Management

* Position sizing
* Maximum exposure limits
* Stop-loss simulation
* Take-profit simulation
* Risk-reward ratio calculation

### Paper Trading

Simulated trading environment that allows strategy testing without financial risk.

### Trade Journal

Records:

* Entry price
* Exit price
* Profit/Loss
* Trade duration
* Asset traded

### Performance Analytics

Reports:

* Win rate
* Loss rate
* Net profit
* Maximum drawdown
* Sharpe ratio (optional)
* Total trades executed
