## Description:

Explains how withHuman approval controls work and how agents should respond when tool calls are held, denied, or expired.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[withhuman](https://clawhub.ai/user/withhuman)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI agents and their operators use this skill to understand withHuman approval requests, policies, review workflows, and how to respond to pending or denied tool calls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approval requests may include sensitive tool arguments and optional context.

Mitigation: Use a trusted approval server and trusted reviewers, and consider what request details may be shared when connecting withHuman.
