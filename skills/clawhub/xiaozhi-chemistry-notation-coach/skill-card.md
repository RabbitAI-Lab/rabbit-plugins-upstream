## Description:

A Chinese-language chemistry tutor that helps secondary-school students write and balance chemical notation and equations through guided practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to practice element symbols, valence, formulas, and equation balancing at junior- and senior-secondary levels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students' chemistry progress or review reminders may be shared or scheduled without informed consent.

Mitigation: Keep practice session-only by default; obtain separate explicit consent before sharing progress or scheduling reminders, and honor pause or deletion requests.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent expectations may not fit learners outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize those elements for the learner's region before use.

Risk: Incorrect generated formulas or balanced equations may mislead learners.

Mitigation: Check generated practice items and equations against the skill's notation rules before presenting them.
