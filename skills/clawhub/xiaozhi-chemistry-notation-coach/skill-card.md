## Description:

面向中文初高中化学学习，循序指导元素符号、化合价、化学式和方程式的书写与配平，并延伸至高中相关化学用语。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking secondary-school students and their educators use this skill to practice chemical notation, identify mistakes in formulas and equations, and work through grade-appropriate hints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localization required elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, Chinese-language guidance, and crisis contacts may not fit users outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Before use elsewhere, localize the curriculum, language, crisis contacts, and applicable privacy requirements.

Risk: Optional student progress memory and reminders involve minors' learning information.

Mitigation: Review privacy settings with students or guardians, and enable memory or reminders only with appropriate consent.
