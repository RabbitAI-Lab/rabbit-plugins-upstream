## Description:

A Chinese-language chemistry tutor that helps secondary-school students write chemical formulas and equations, balance reactions, and practice more advanced notation at the appropriate grade level.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill for guided practice with chemical symbols, valence, formulas, equation balancing, and high-school chemistry notation. It redirects full problem-solving and experimental questions to other tutors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (localize curriculum assumptions and crisis-support contacts outside Mainland China)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning-profile memory and reminder queueing may retain student learning information.

Mitigation: Use these features only after explicit student consent; otherwise keep practice within the current session.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis-support contacts may not fit students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum expectations and crisis-support contacts before use outside Mainland China.
