## Description:

帮助中国初高中学生练习元素符号、化合价、化学式和化学方程式的书写与配平。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向已开设化学课程的初中生及高中生，提供分级练习、逐步提示和化学式与方程式纠错；经学生同意后可记录进度和安排复习提醒。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent rules before student-facing use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chemistry examples or exercises may contain incorrect formulas, equations, or grade-inappropriate material.

Mitigation: Check generated exercises against the bundled chemistry rules and the student's course level before presenting them.

Risk: Student progress or reminders may be retained or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Use session-only tutoring by default; obtain separate explicit consent for profile storage, sharing, and reminders, and honor data controls.

Risk: Mainland China emergency contacts and minor-consent defaults may be unsuitable in another region.

Mitigation: Localize emergency referrals, curriculum, and minor-consent rules before use outside the intended Chinese K12 context.
