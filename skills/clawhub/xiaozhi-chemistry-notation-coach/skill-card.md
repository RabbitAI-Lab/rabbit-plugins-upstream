## Description:

化学用语专项教练：元素符号、化合价、化学式、化学方程式的书写与配平，初中起步，高中延伸到必修（原子结构示意图、电子式、有机反应式）与选择性必修（热化学方程式、电极反应式、电离与水解方程式、核外电子排布、结构简式与命名）。

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use this skill to practice writing chemical symbols, formulas, and equations, balancing equations, and interpreting notation. It gives graduated hints and short practice sets aligned with Chinese chemistry curricula.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chemistry exercises or notation may contain errors.

Mitigation: Check each generated item against the included notation rules before presenting it.

Risk: Student progress records may contain sensitive information, including data about minors.

Mitigation: Practice is session-only by default; access, sharing, and updates to records require the skill's consent checks.

Risk: A student may disclose abuse, self-harm, or another crisis during tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop the tutoring flow and follow the crisis referral protocol, including trusted adults or local emergency services as appropriate.

Risk: Unicode chemistry symbols and subscripts may be visually unclear when rendered.

Mitigation: Review the displayed notation in context; ClawScan assessed its low-probability Unicode warning as expected for this skill.
