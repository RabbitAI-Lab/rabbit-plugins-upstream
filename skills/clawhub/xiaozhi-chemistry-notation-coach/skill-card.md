## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders may persist or be shared beyond the current session.

Mitigation: Default to session-only use; obtain separate, explicit consent for profiles, cross-skill or parent sharing, and reminders, and honor requests to view, correct, pause, export, or delete records.

Mitigation: Check formula notation, atom and charge conservation, and balancing against the included rules before presenting exercises or corrections.
