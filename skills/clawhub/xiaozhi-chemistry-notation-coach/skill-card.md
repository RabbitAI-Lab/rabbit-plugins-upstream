## Description:

A Chinese-language chemistry coach that helps secondary-school students write chemical symbols and formulas, apply valence rules, and balance equations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Mainland China's secondary-school chemistry curriculum use this skill to practice chemical notation, formulas, valence, and equation balancing through short guided exercises and feedback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localization of curriculum, minor-consent rules, and crisis resources is needed elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent learner records, reminders, or sharing may expose a minor's study data without informed consent.

Mitigation: Keep practice session-only by default; require separate consent for profiles, reminders, and sharing, and honor pause and delete requests.

Risk: Incorrect generated formulas or unbalanced equations may mislead learners.

Mitigation: Check generated exercises and answers against the notation rules and atom balance before presenting them.

Risk: Chinese Mainland curriculum and crisis resources may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Confirm the learner's region and localize curriculum, consent requirements, and emergency contacts before use outside Mainland China.
