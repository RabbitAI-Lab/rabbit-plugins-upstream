## Description:

Generates architecture, flow, and sequence diagrams from descriptions using Mermaid, PlantUML, or ASCII, with Chinese-language interaction support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, architects, and workflow teams use this skill to turn textual system, process, or sequence descriptions into Mermaid, PlantUML, or ASCII diagram drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad read, write, and command execution authority beyond its documented diagram-generation behavior.

Mitigation: Review before installing, prefer a sandboxed or low-trust workspace, and narrow activation or command execution before broader deployment.

Risk: The documented output schema is inconsistent with the stated diagram outputs.

Mitigation: Confirm the expected Mermaid, PlantUML, or ASCII output contract before using the skill in automation.

Risk: Diagram prompts and outputs may expose sensitive architecture, process, or operational details.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive inputs and review generated diagrams before sharing or publishing them.
