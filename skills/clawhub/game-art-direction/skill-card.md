## Description:

Turns a game's design into a coherent art direction covering visual identity, readable gameplay feedback, sound, and key in-game moments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game designers and art teams use this skill to translate an existing game design and product brief into a practical visual and audio direction, including interface readability, accessibility, key moments, and asset priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language instructions may lead to deliverables in the wrong language.

Mitigation: Follow the language set by PRODUCT_BRIEF.md, or the conversation language if none is set; review the final document.

Risk: Visual references could be mistaken for permission to copy another game's assets or identity.

Mitigation: Borrow only stated visual principles and check originality and licensing before using any proposed assets.
