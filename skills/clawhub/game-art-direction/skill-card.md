## Description:

Defines a game's visual identity, readable visual and audio feedback, and signature moments from its approved design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game designers and art directors use this skill to turn an existing GAME_DESIGN.md and PRODUCT_BRIEF.md into an ART_DIRECTION plan, with optional visual targets for high-risk decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Visual proposals or reference selections may not fit the approved game brief or rights boundaries.

Mitigation: Check proposals against GAME_DESIGN.md and PRODUCT_BRIEF.md; use director mode to approve image selections and confirm reference permissions.
