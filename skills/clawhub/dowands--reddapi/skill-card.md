## Description: <br>
Reddapi helps agents use the reddapi.dev API to search Reddit archives, discover leads and subreddits, run vector or semantic searches, and analyze trends. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, marketers, and go-to-market teams use this skill to query reddapi.dev for Reddit market research, competitive analysis, lead discovery, trend monitoring, and niche opportunity discovery. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Reddit research queries, keywords, and related parameters are sent to reddapi.dev. <br>
Mitigation: Use only non-confidential search terms unless the provider's privacy and retention practices have been reviewed and accepted. <br>
Risk: Lead-generation searches may include private lead lists, business plans, personal data, or other sensitive targeting information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential, secret, or personal data in search prompts and API parameters. <br>
