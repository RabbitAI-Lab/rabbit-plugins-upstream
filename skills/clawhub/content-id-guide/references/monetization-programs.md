# Creator monetization programs (the "is my money even at stake?" layer)

Why this file exists: a monetize claim means something different
depending on whether the creator is in the platform's monetization
program at all. Establishing program status early turns a scary claim
into a classified one, and "how do I get into the program" is often
the real question underneath. Everything below is from official
sources with the S-08 metadata convention; account-specific truth
always lives in the user's own dashboard (S-09 applies to program
status too). Any earnings statement here follows S-01: documented
design, never a payout promise.

## YouTube: the YouTube Partner Program (YPP)

Sources: support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 (overview and
eligibility), support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240 (expanded
program and signup steps), youtube.com/creators/partner-program/
(creator-facing overview) (all accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not
stated by source).

**Officially published thresholds for the full ad-revenue tier:**
1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last
12 months, **or** 1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified public
Shorts views in the last 90 days. An expanded earlier entry point
with lower thresholds also exists (the official creator page cites a
3,000 public watch-hour figure for it); the Earn tab in YouTube
Studio shows the exact, current, region-specific requirements for
the user's own channel, which is the number to trust.

**How applying works (official):** YouTube Studio → Earn → review and
accept the terms → set up or link an AdSense for YouTube account.
Review starts only after all signup steps are complete.

**How long it takes (officially stated):** review typically takes
about 1 month, applications are serviced in the order received, and
delays are possible during high volume. Status is checkable anytime
in the Earn section. Calm framing: a wait around a month is the
documented normal, not a sign something is wrong.

**Two officially documented facts that defuse common panics:**
- Seeing ads on your videos when you're not in YPP does not mean
  someone is taking "your" revenue: per YouTube's Terms of Service,
  YouTube retains the right to serve ads on all content, and
  non-YPP creators aren't earning from those ads in the first place.
  A monetize claim on a non-YPP video moves money between the
  platform and the claimant, not out of the creator's pocket.
- Staying in the program has its own rules: channels inactive for 6
  months or more, or in violation of the channel monetization
  policies, can lose monetization regardless of their counts.

## TikTok: the Creator Rewards Program

Sources: tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/eligibility (official
Creator Academy), TikTok Support FAQ on the program, and
tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en (program
terms) (all accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source).

**Officially published requirements:** at least 18 (19 in South
Korea), a Personal account (Business accounts, organization accounts,
and political or government accounts don't qualify), at least 10,000
followers, at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, account
in good standing, and residence in an eligible region (the official
page currently lists the US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France,
Mexico, and Brazil). Eligible videos are original content at least 1
minute long.

**How applying works (official):** inside the TikTok app via Creator
tools → Creator Rewards Program. **Officially stated response time:
approximately 3 days.** Eligibility decisions can be appealed via the
link in the notification within 30 days.

**Worth knowing calmly:** TikTok periodically re-reviews accounts in
the program for originality and quality, so staying eligible is an
ongoing thing, not a one-time gate.

## Meta: Facebook Content Monetization

Sources: facebook.com/business/help/1049081556813520 (Business Help
Center), creators.facebook.com/introducing-facebook-content-monetization
(official announcement), facebook.com/legal/creator_content_monetization_terms/
(program terms) (all accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by
source).

**The current shape (official):** an **invite-only** Content
Monetization Program in which eligible public Facebook videos, reels,
photos, and text posts earn based on performance. It consolidates and
is replacing the older separate programs (In-Stream Ads, Ads on
Reels, Performance bonus). Each Content Monetization Offer arrives
with its own eligibility criteria and terms through the creator's
dashboard, so there is no single public threshold list to quote;
the Professional dashboard or Meta Business Suite shows what applies
to the user's own account.

**The S-14 tie-in, officially grounded:** Meta states that
invitations come from official, in-product channels: the Facebook
app, Meta Business Suite, and the Professional dashboard. A
"monetization invitation" arriving by email, DM, or any channel
asking for a fee, login, or payment details does not match the
platform's own described process, which is exactly the
verify-in-the-dashboard reflex S-14 teaches.

## How this file is used in session

- When a monetize claim lands, ask program status early (already in
  intake), and if the user isn't in the program, deliver the
  officially documented defusal facts before anything else.
- When the user asks how to join, give the officially published
  requirements and steps above, the officially stated timelines
  where they exist (about 1 month for YPP review; about 3 days for
  TikTok's application response; Meta is invite-only with no public
  clock), and point them to their own Earn tab, Creator tools, or
  Professional dashboard as the account-specific truth.
- Numbers not published by the platforms (unofficial payout rates,
  rumored thresholds) are never stated, per S-06.
