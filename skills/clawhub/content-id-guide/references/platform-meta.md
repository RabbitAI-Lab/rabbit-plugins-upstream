# Meta Rights Manager reference (Facebook and Instagram)

- **Match rule actions (Meta's own terms, verified 2026-08-26):**
  **Block** (content hidden from everyone except the uploader, who can
  dispute the block directly with the rights holder within Facebook),
  **Monitor** (content stays published while the rights holder watches
  activity; the Meta analogue of YouTube's track, usually invisible to
  the uploader and usually needing no action), and **Claim ad
  earnings** (content stays published; a request is placed for a share
  of ad money; Meta states this action is not available to everyone).
  Never say "monetize" as if it were the Meta product term; the
  official term is Claim ad earnings.
  (facebook.com/business/help/475548192814461; accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
- **Attribution runs through ownership links**, a separate Rights
  Manager mechanism that places an ownership banner linking to the
  rights holder; adjacent to match rules, not one of the match
  actions.
- Match rules are configured per reference file by the rights holder;
  behavior can differ between Facebook and Instagram surfaces under the
  same rule.
- **Ownership conflict** (two rights holders matching the same
  reference) follows a separate resolution path from a creator appeal.
  Identify which situation the user is in before explaining flow.
- Creator **appeals** run through the in-app flow; a formal **DMCA
  counter-notification** is a separate and distinct process with legal
  weight, exactly as on YouTube.
**Verified windows (Meta Business Help Centre, verified 2026-08-26;
stated as plain days, so platform-unspecified per S-10):**

- Usage disputes: when a creator disputes a Rights Manager claim, the
  rights holder has **7 days** to respond to the dispute; they can
  approve or reject it. (facebook.com/business/help/2523148971045474; accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
- Ownership conflicts run on a **7-day cadence** throughout: the
  second publisher has 7 days to dispute a reference-file conflict or
  ownership of the overlapping segments reverts to the first
  publisher; once disputed, the first publisher has 7 days to accept
  or reject, and non-response makes the second publisher the
  exclusive rights owner of the conflicting segments. While a
  conflict is in dispute, matches within the conflicting segments are
  withheld from both publishers.
  (facebook.com/business/help/840557212788102; accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)

- **Creator-side ad-earnings deadline (verified 2026-08-26):** when a
  Claim ad earnings match lands, the uploader has **7 days from the
  email and notification** to dispute the earnings claim. If they
  dispute and the rights holder doesn't respond within 7 days, the
  uploader may receive their share; no dispute within 7 days, or a
  rejected dispute, and the rights holder may receive it. Payouts are
  withheld while the dispute is open. This is a deadline on the
  creator, so it always goes on the timeline.
  (facebook.com/business/help/891090414760198; accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
- **Deletion kills payouts for everyone:** if the video is deleted, by
  the uploader or via takedown, no one receives ad-earnings payouts.
  The Meta-specific reinforcement of S-12: deleting mid-dispute
  forfeits the money outcome entirely.

**Permissions ("allowlisting") and surfaces (verified 2026-08-26):**

- Meta's official term is **"Allow others to use your content."**
  Permission is granted in Rights Manager at the Page level or
  granularly, to people, Pages, or Instagram accounts, and must be
  granted **before** crossposting or the content flags as a match.
  This is the correct fix when the claimant is the creator's own
  partner, distributor, or collaborator. In session, follow whatever
  the user's screen says, then use Meta's term. Official pages, each
  opening in a new tab per S-08:
  - How to allow others to use your content:
    facebook.com/business/help/443627026505230
    (accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
  - Allow others to post your content:
    facebook.com/business/help/400473076992376
    (accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
  - Allow specific Instagram accounts:
    facebook.com/business/help/601076417002304
    (accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
- **Instagram matching is separately enabled** and carries its own
  match rules ("Enable Instagram matching," "Match rules for Instagram
  content"), so identical content can behave differently on Facebook
  and Instagram because of a rights holder's configuration, not only
  policy. When a user reports different outcomes on the two surfaces,
  this is the first explanation to check.
  - Protect your Instagram content:
    facebook.com/business/help/212679769276903
    (accessed 2026-08-26; last updated: not stated by source)
