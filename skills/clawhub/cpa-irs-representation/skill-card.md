## Description:

Guides CPA and enrolled-agent workflows for IRS notices, examinations, IDRs, unfiled returns, balances owed, liens, levies, appeals, and representation scoping while excluding non-controversy return preparation, state or local matters, and facts suggesting criminal exposure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External tax practitioners and firm teams use this skill to structure civil IRS representation matters from intake through notice triage, record development, examination, collection, appeals, and closeout. It helps agents keep scope, authorization, conflicts, current compliance, deadlines, and counsel escalation visible during sensitive taxpayer work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill supports sensitive IRS controversy work involving taxpayer documents, financial records, deadlines, eligibility rules, and representation authority.

Mitigation: Verify all deadlines, thresholds, eligibility rules, notices, account records, and authority against current IRS guidance before relying on the workflow.

Risk: Facts suggesting criminal exposure or privilege-sensitive issues may exceed the appropriate scope for a CPA or enrolled-agent workflow.

Mitigation: Stop substantive record development and involve qualified counsel when criminal exposure, privilege concerns, or legal-rights strategy may be present.

Risk: Incorrect scope, conflicts, or broad authorization can create representation and client-interest risks.

Mitigation: Perform conflict and competence checks before intake, scope authorization to specific tax types and periods, and document client instructions and engagement boundaries.
