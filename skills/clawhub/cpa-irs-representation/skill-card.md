## Description:

Provides a structured intake, notice triage, fact-gathering, and routing checklist for civil IRS representation matters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

CPAs and enrolled agents use the skill to organize civil IRS representation work, including notices, examinations, collections, and appeals. It is a workflow aid that calls for professional judgment and verification of current requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deadlines, eligibility, thresholds, and other IRS requirements can change or depend on the specific notice and matter.

Mitigation: Read the notice and verify time-sensitive requirements against current authority for each matter.

Risk: IRS representation can involve sensitive client records and authorization limits.

Mitigation: Use the checklist only within an explicitly authorized engagement and protect client records.
