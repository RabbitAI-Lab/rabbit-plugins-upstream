## Description:

Drafts enterprise policies, SOPs, and staff handbook materials from business rules, with structured compliance screening and review prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and business operators use the skill to turn business rules into internal policies, SOPs, and staff handbook outlines, and to identify common compliance gaps and needed records. Its drafts require qualified human review before adoption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft policies may not fit the user's legal or regulatory circumstances.

Mitigation: Use redacted business information and have a qualified reviewer check policies before adoption.

Risk: The advertised scanner and six templates are missing, so automated checking is unavailable as shipped.

Mitigation: Review drafts manually and review any separately obtained replacement tools before use.

Risk: Release evidence lists MIT-0 while the skill files state personal-use and no-resale terms.

Mitigation: Confirm the governing license terms before redistribution or commercial reuse.
