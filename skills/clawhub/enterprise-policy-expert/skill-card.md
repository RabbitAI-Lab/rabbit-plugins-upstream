## Description:

This skill helps teams turn business rules into enterprise policies, SOPs, and staff handbooks with scope checks, structured clauses, red-line reviews, and evidence and versioning checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and business teams can use this skill to scope policy work, organize SOPs and staff handbooks, review drafts against common risk areas, and prepare actionable checklists. Its outputs are planning drafts and should receive qualified professional review before adoption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The advertised review script and six templates are missing from the supplied artifact, so their behavior and availability could not be verified.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts, check them against the available reference guides, and manually review any screening before use.

Risk: Policy drafts may be incomplete or unsuitable for the relevant jurisdiction or business.

Mitigation: Use redacted summaries and obtain qualified professional review before adopting a policy.
