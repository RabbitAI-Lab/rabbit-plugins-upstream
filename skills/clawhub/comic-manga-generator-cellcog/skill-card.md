## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Comic prompts, character descriptions, and referenced task content may be sent to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including secrets, private business material, regulated personal data, or confidential unpublished work unless CellCog's data handling terms have been reviewed and accepted. <br>
Risk: The skill requires a CellCog API key and external CellCog service access. <br>
Mitigation: Store CELLCOG_API_KEY only in the runtime environment, avoid committing it to files, and install the CellCog dependency through the documented setup path. <br>
Risk: Generated comics, manga pages, webtoons, and character designs may need creative and safety review before publication. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated outputs for accuracy, appropriateness, rights concerns, and consistency with the intended audience before sharing or publishing. <br>
