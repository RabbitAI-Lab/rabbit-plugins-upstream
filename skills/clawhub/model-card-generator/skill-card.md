## Description:

Generates a one-page model card for an AI system covering its identity, purpose, data, limitations, risks, and contact in Markdown with optional JSON.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (code only); documentation and methodology all rights reserved

## Use Case:

Teams and developers use this skill to document AI systems and agents in a concise model card that records their purpose, data, limitations, risks, and responsible contact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documentation and methodology have different reuse terms from the code.

Mitigation: Review the Chinese documentation and layered license terms before reusing or redistributing content.

Risk: Generated cards may expose contact or system information supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive details and choose output content and storage locations appropriately.

Risk: A generated card may be mistaken for professional or regulatory approval.

Mitigation: Have qualified people review consequential claims and decisions; do not treat the card as legal or clinical advice.
