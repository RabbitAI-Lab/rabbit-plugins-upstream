番茄小说短篇爆款创作完整教学课程。用于教授番茄小说平台创作方法论，包括平台算法认知、标题导语创作、脑洞生成、AI辅助工作流、去AI味润色、多平台变现、海外出海等全套技能。当用户想学习番茄小说创作、提升短篇写作能力、诊断作品问题、生成爆款标题导语大纲、去除AI味、或了解出海变现时使用。

## Use Case:

External writers and publishing teams use this skill to learn Fanqie online-fiction techniques, generate titles, hooks, outlines, and revision suggestions, and diagnose short-fiction performance issues. It also provides guidance for AI-assisted drafting, polishing, distribution, monetization, and overseas publishing workflows.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The monetization material discusses imitation and promotion tactics that could lead to plagiarism, mislabeling derivative work as original, or fake engagement.

Mitigation: Use the guidance only for original ideation and legitimate audience development; review outputs for copyright, platform-policy, and originality concerns before publication.

Risk: The overseas publishing material includes payout and tax-form guidance that may be inaccurate or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Verify payout, tax treaty, and form decisions against official platform, IRS, and local tax guidance or consult a qualified professional.

Risk: The skill can generate persuasive commercial publishing advice for online fiction without validating platform rules or market claims.

Mitigation: Treat generated plans as drafts and confirm current platform policies, contract terms, and monetization requirements before acting.
