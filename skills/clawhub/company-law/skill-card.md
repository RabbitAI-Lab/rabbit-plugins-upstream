## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance and drafting aids for PRC company formation, capital contributions, equity control, governance, articles of association, and contract review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking founders, shareholders, and advisers use this reference skill to explore PRC company-law issues, review governance arrangements and draft clauses, checklists, and proposed next steps. Its outputs are educational aids, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (PRC)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or inaccurate statutory provisions or case citations could mislead legal decisions.

Mitigation: Verify current law and cited cases against authoritative sources before relying on the guidance.

Risk: Using generic clauses, checklists, or dispute procedures without case-specific review could cause legal or financial harm.

Mitigation: Treat drafts as educational aids and consult a qualified lawyer before filings, disputes, asset preservation, or other consequential action.
