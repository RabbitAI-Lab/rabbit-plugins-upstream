## Description:

A psychology paper close-reading skill that turns user-provided papers into structured Markdown reports covering hypotheses, research design, variables, statistics, limitations, practical implications, and critical evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qingjiu061](https://clawhub.ai/user/qingjiu061)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, instructors, and research readers use this skill to deconstruct psychology papers into coursework-ready close-reading reports. It is intended for papers supplied by the user as PDFs, Word documents, pasted text, or accessible web links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read papers or fetch content from paper links supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Only provide documents and links that the user is allowed to share, and review any fetched content before relying on it.

Risk: Generated citations, statistics, and coursework-style conclusions may be incorrect or overstate the source paper.

Mitigation: Verify all citations, numerical results, quoted claims, and assignment submissions against the original paper.
