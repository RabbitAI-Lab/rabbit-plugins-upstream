Developers, evaluators, project teams, and investment reviewers use this skill to assess early-stage concepts across technical feasibility, commercial viability, resource fit, team capability, risk profile, and next-step recommendations. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Project, team, market, funding, or intellectual-property details supplied for evaluation may be sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide information that is appropriate to share in the analysis context, and avoid unnecessary confidential details. <br>
Risk: Validation conclusions and recommendations may be incomplete or misleading if the submitted concept information is inaccurate or sparse. <br>
Mitigation: Review the output with qualified technical, commercial, legal, and investment stakeholders before making project or funding decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill targets TRL 1-4 concept and principle validation, not production readiness or final due diligence. <br>
Mitigation: Use the report as an early-stage assessment and require additional validation before pilot, investment, or deployment commitments. <br>
