## Description:

Phonics and sight word lists by grade from Dyslexia Steps (dyslexiasteps.com) through its hosted MCP server — CVC words, word families, Dolch sight words, digraphs, vowel patterns, plus word lists in German, Spanish, French, Dutch and Brazilian Portuguese. Use when a parent, teacher or tutor asks for words for a grade, words with a letter pattern (sh, ck, -ight, ai), a word family list, practice words for a struggling or dyslexic reader, or where a grade placement comes from.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Parents, teachers, and tutors can find grade-based or pattern-based phonics and sight word lists for reading practice, including lists in several languages. Grade placement is a typical guide, and the lists are practice materials rather than an assessment or diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A learner or adult could treat a grade placement as a fixed standard or use a practice list as an assessment for dyslexia.

Mitigation: Treat grade placement as a typical guide and lists as practice material; consult a qualified specialist about dyslexia concerns.
