## Description:

Guides agents through a 16-step hosted narrative of Oktoberfest's closing day and the teardown of Munich's Theresienwiese.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users follow an immersive, 16-step journey through Oktoberfest's closing day and the subsequent teardown, with optional reflections and a final postcard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration details and journey content are sent to drifts.bot.

Mitigation: Create or use an account only if comfortable with the service; provide only necessary profile details and avoid sensitive information in reflections.

Risk: A returned API key grants access to the account.

Mitigation: Store the key securely and do not include it in shared content.

Risk: A review may be publicly visible.

Mitigation: Confirm with the user before posting and avoid sensitive information in the review.
