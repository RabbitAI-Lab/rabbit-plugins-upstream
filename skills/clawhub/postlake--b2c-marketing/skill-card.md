## Description:

Guides agents and operators through B2C organic social marketing on TikTok and Instagram, from account setup and warmup through content-market fit, hooks, CTAs, conversion review, and PostLake-assisted publishing when authorized.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External operators, founders, marketers, and their agents use this skill to prepare B2C social accounts, plan organic TikTok and Instagram content, diagnose growth or conversion issues, and move to PostLake-assisted publishing only after warmup, account limits, and user authorization are in place.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An agent could publish to connected social accounts before the operator is ready or outside intended account limits.

Mitigation: Require explicit user opt-in, confirm PostLake authorization, and restrict publishing to configured profiles, networks, and daily limits before posting.

Risk: Live API keys or platform credentials could be exposed if pasted into chat.

Mitigation: Use OAuth or configured PostLake credentials, and do not paste live API keys or social account passwords into prompts.

Risk: Scheduled posts or analytics interpretation could create misleading marketing decisions if left unchecked.

Mitigation: Review scheduled posts and analytics logs regularly, and keep a performance log of posts, formats, and results.
