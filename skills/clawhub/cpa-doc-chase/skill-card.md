## Description:

Helps tax and bookkeeping practitioners track missing client documents, draft follow-ups, and decide when to extend, proceed with documented gaps, or disengage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tax and bookkeeping practitioners use this agent to identify engagement blockers, organize missing-document requests and follow-ups, and record next steps when a client does not respond.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contacting a spouse, bookkeeper, or other document holder about sensitive client records without authorization could disclose information to the wrong person.

Mitigation: Confirm client permission, recipient identity, and allowed disclosures before contact, and use approved secure channels.

Risk: Follow-up recommendations could affect whether an engagement is extended, delivered incomplete, or discontinued.

Mitigation: Keep extension, incomplete-delivery, and disengagement decisions under practitioner control.
