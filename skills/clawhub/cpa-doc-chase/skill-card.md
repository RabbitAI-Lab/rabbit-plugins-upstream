## Description:

Helps tax and bookkeeping practitioners triage missing client documents, draft focused requests, escalate follow-ups, and record decisions when an engagement is blocked.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External accounting, tax, and bookkeeping practitioners use this skill to organize missing-document chases, identify blockers, draft dated client requests, escalate non-response, and document final engagement decisions. It supports workflow discipline and client communication; it does not prepare returns or provide legal or tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Client-facing messages could include incorrect tax, legal, penalty, retention, deadline, or jurisdiction-specific guidance if drafted without review.

Mitigation: Use the skill as workflow support only; have the practitioner review messages and verify deadlines, penalties, retention periods, and jurisdiction-specific claims against current authority before sending.

Risk: A missing-document workflow can create engagement risk if requests, responses, substitutes, and final decisions are not documented.

Mitigation: Maintain a chase log that records what was requested, when, through which channel, what the client provided, accepted substitutes, and the final disposition.
