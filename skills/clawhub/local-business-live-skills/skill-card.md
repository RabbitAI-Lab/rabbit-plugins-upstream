## Description: <br>
This skill helps local business owners diagnose store operations, draft short-video and livestream scripts, prepare livestream plans, and review performance data for local-commerce marketing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External local-business owners and operators use this skill to run guided store diagnostics, generate short-video and livestream scripts, prepare live-commerce sessions, and review campaign results. It is oriented toward restaurants, beauty, hospitality, parent-child, and retail storefronts using local-commerce channels. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with examples and platform guidance primarily oriented toward Chinese local-commerce platforms and Chinese-language merchants. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security summary marks the release suspicious because it under-discloses remote links, local report files, and broad bundled marketing guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before deployment, confirm expected report save locations, and inspect linked Notion, GitHub Pages, and CDN pages before use. <br>
Risk: Merchant or customer data used in diagnostics, screenshots, or reports may include sensitive business or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Use redacted or non-sensitive inputs unless sharing is intended, and avoid sending screenshots or diagnostic reports to WeChat or Notion without explicit consent. <br>
Risk: Generated marketing scripts and commerce recommendations may conflict with platform advertising, livestream, or off-platform-contact rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs for applicable law and platform policy before publishing, especially claims, discounts, contact prompts, and prohibited terms. <br>
