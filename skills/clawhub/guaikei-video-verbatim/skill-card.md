## Description:

Transcribes local videos and public video links into text, and uses custom prompts to produce structured writing such as summaries, rewrites, quote extracts, shot breakdowns, and translations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to transcribe local videos or public video links, then turn the content into cleaned transcripts or prompt-guided summaries, rewrites, and other structured copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Storage destinations, retention, and training terms for uploaded recordings are not established by the available evidence.

Mitigation: Before uploading confidential recordings, ask the provider to identify storage destinations and confirm retention and training terms.

Risk: The advertised restriction to a single domain is not enforced.

Mitigation: Do not rely on that restriction for sensitive recordings; confirm where video data may be sent before use.

Risk: Downloaded media may remain on the local device until a later run performs cleanup.

Mitigation: Use an appropriately protected workspace and remove downloaded media when processing is complete.
