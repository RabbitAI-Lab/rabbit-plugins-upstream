## Description:

A resonance-diagnosis skill that helps solo knowledge creators assess whether a draft has a focused emotional core, decode why comparable viral content resonated, or turn an observed phenomenon into a public-facing issue.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and writing assistants use this skill to diagnose whether content will resonate with an audience, identify a single core mechanism, and receive concrete revision or topic-framing guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill depends on local zmm family and interaction documents that may be unavailable or may contain requirements the user has not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review the referenced local documents before installation or use, and treat missing documents as a reason to stop rather than infer their contents.

Risk: The skill may save some user feedback or performance observations in local skill memory.

Mitigation: Use it only where local memory retention is acceptable, and avoid providing sensitive draft feedback or performance data when retention is not desired.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases could activate the skill in conversations where resonance diagnosis was not intended.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit triggers such as /zmm-resonate when accidental activation would be a concern.
