## Description:

Assesses automotive-lighting component architecture, sourcing strategy, and preliminary patent and design-right risk for lamp drawings, product images, bills of materials, technical concepts, and make-versus-buy questions, producing evidence-backed recommendations and an accessible tabbed HTML report packaged as a ZIP file.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and engineering teams use this skill to evaluate automotive lighting designs, sourcing options, and preliminary patent or design-right exposure. It supports component decomposition, make-buy-hybrid recommendations, FTO and design-risk screening, mitigation options, and an accessible HTML report.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat preliminary patent or design-right screening as legal clearance.

Mitigation: State the preliminary nature of the review, cite direct records for material IP claims, and route claim construction, infringement, validity, enforceability, and clearance decisions to qualified counsel in each relevant jurisdiction.

Risk: Automotive design and sourcing details may be shared with configured PatSnap research tools during live screening.

Mitigation: Confirm users are comfortable sharing relevant details with those tools before installation or use, and never include real API keys, secrets, or confidential raw responses in reports, logs, archives, or source control.

Risk: Missing inputs, unavailable MCP servers, or unavailable claims, status records, or design views could lead to unsupported conclusions.

Mitigation: Ask focused questions or list explicit assumptions, label unexecuted searches, use 'Status unverified' where needed, and avoid fabricating search results, right identifiers, suppliers, prices, or legal conclusions.
