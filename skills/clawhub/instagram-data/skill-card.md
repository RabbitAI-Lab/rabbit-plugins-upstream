## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Instagram research targets and related queries are sent to Superagnt.

Mitigation: Install only when users are comfortable sharing those targets and queries with Superagnt.

Risk: The skill points users toward broader Superagnt platform and MCP capabilities beyond the Instagram purpose.

Mitigation: Treat broader platform setup separately and avoid enabling additional tool families unless that wider access is intentional.
