## Description:

Instagram profiles, posts, reels, and hashtags for agents — influencer and brand research with no Instagram login, Business account, or Meta app.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers, marketing teams, and research teams can use the skill to retrieve Instagram profile and media data for creator intelligence, social listening, and brand or hashtag research.

Mitigation: Only configure SUPERAGNT_API_KEY if you trust superagnt and are comfortable using your key with its API.
