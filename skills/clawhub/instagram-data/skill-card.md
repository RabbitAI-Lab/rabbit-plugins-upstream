## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research requests are sent to Superagnt and may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated provider key, review credit usage, and keep the key out of chat.

Risk: The documented shell examples may not correctly quote the authorization header or expand the environment variable.

Mitigation: Correct the examples' quote escaping and verify that $SUPERAGNT_API_KEY expands in the authorization header before running them.
