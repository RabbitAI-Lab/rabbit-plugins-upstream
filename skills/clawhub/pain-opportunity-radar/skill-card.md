## Description:

Systematically discover, verify, score, and validate high-frequency user pain points that could become Agent, Skill, or human-in-the-loop workflows. Use when researching product opportunities from forums, GitHub Issues, skill marketplaces, reviews, public tasks, or multilingual user expressions; when comparing opportunity lanes; or when moving from broad scanning into real-task validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product researchers, founders, and workflow designers use this skill to find recurring user problems in public sources, compare evidence-backed opportunities, and plan real-task validation. It emphasizes traceable evidence and human checkpoints when decisions could have significant consequences.
