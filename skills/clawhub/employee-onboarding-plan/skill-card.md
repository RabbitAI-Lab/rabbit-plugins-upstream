## Description:

Builds a 30/60/90-day US onboarding plan with owners, records, and compliance deadlines for a new hire.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rapidworker1](https://clawhub.ai/user/rapidworker1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

US HR generalists, office managers, and hiring managers use this skill to organize a new employee’s first 90 days, identify onboarding tasks and owners, and track items HR must verify.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated calendar may be less detailed than the advertised onboarding plan.

Mitigation: Have HR add owners, records, substantive tasks, and verified deadlines before relying on the plan.

Risk: Sensitive employee information could be exposed if copied into the plan.

Mitigation: Keep Social Security numbers, I-9 document images, background reports, and medical information out of the plan; retain confidential medical records separately with restricted access.

Risk: The optional Windows download is unnecessary for this workflow and was not validated by this review.

Mitigation: Do not use the download for this workflow; assess it separately before use.
