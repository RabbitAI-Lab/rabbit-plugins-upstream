## Description:

Collects engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, reactions) for everything the user published with these skills on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and other connected platforms, and writes one report. Use when the user asks how their posts or videos are performing, wants a social media report, or compares platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People reviewing their published social media content can use this skill to retrieve available engagement metrics across platforms, identify top posts, compare changes with a prior report, and see where metrics could not be read.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Google Business reporting may include broader location activity than individual post-engagement metrics.

Mitigation: Request explicit opt-in, label it as a separate location report, use a dedicated posts folder, and protect generated reports.
