## Description:

Routes software development requests through proportionate scenario-specific planning, implementation, and verification checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fize](https://clawhub.ai/user/fize)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to route greenfield, feature, bug-fix, refactor, and deployment requests to relevant scope, design, and verification checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad development-related triggers can add planning and verification work to routine requests.

Mitigation: Review whether the extra process fits the task and apply only relevant checks for its execution profile.
