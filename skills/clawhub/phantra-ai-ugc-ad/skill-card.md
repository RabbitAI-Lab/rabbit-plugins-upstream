## Description:

Writes a UGC-style ad scene in Phantra featuring a person speaking to camera about a product, with spoken lines and claims supplied by that person.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and product teams use the skill to draft timed, phone-shot product ad scenes from authorized person and product images. The speaker supplies the dialogue and any product claims, and the user reviews the render quote before approving paid work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded face and product photos may be used without appropriate permission or may receive unclear handling by Phantra.

Mitigation: Use photos you have permission to use and check Phantra's handling of uploaded photos.

Risk: An ad could contain unsupported testimonials, ratings, statistics, or product claims.

Mitigation: Use only dialogue and claims supplied by the person and supported by them; leave unsupported claims out.

Risk: Rendering may incur paid costs, and external service behavior was not verified.

Mitigation: Review the rendering quote and obtain approval before rendering; verify the external service behavior before use.

Risk: Metadata formatting may need correction before installation.

Mitigation: Review and correct the metadata before installation.
