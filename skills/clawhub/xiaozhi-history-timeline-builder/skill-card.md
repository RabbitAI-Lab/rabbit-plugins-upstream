## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school students build history timelines, compare simultaneous events in China and elsewhere, convert dates, and interpret historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to practice chronology, historical context, and map-reading through guided exercises aligned with mainland Chinese middle- and high-school history curricula.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning-profile sharing, wrong-answer records, and reminders can expose a student's learning data.

Mitigation: Keep profile sharing and reminders off by default; require explicit student or guardian consent before reading, recording, sharing, or scheduling.

Risk: Incorrect dates or misleading historical comparisons can undermine learning.

Mitigation: Check chronology and date conversions against the student's textbook and curriculum; avoid unsupported historical interpretations.
