## Description:

帮助学生定位历史事件的时间与空间，搭建纵向时间轴和中外同期对照，并练习纪年换算与历史地图识读。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中和高中学生可用它梳理历史事件的先后、阶段与因果，对照同一时期的中外史事，练习公元纪年换算，并按教材识读历史地图。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical dates, period descriptions, or map interpretations may mislead students if they differ from their course materials.

Mitigation: Use the student's textbook and map annotations as the reference, and keep historical characterizations within the stated curriculum.

Risk: Young learners may raise safety concerns during a study conversation.

Mitigation: Require a parent or teacher to accompany primary-school use; if a crisis signal appears, stop the lesson flow and follow the skill's referral guidance.
