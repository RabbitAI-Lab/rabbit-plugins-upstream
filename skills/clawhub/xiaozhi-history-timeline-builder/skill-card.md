## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking history students in building timelines, comparing contemporary events across regions, converting historical dates, and reading historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Mainland China's middle and high schools use this skill to organize historical events by time and place, construct their own timelines and China–world comparisons, and practice date conversion and map reading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and crisis-help guidance assumes a Mainland China K12 setting.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, consent rules for minors, and help channels before use elsewhere; confirm the student's region before giving emergency numbers.

Risk: Optional learning profiles and reminders involve student information.

Mitigation: Keep the default session-only mode; enable profiles, sharing, or reminders only with the required separate consent.
