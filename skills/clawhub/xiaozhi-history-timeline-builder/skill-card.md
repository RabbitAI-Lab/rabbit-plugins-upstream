## Description:

Helps students build historical timelines, compare contemporaneous events in China and elsewhere, convert dates, and read historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use this Simplified Chinese history tutor to construct timelines and China–world comparisons, practice chronological reasoning, and interpret historical maps with reference to their textbooks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent rules before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and emergency guidance may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent requirements before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Student history profiles, cross-skill sharing, or reminders could be activated without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, sharing, and reminders off unless the student or guardian explicitly enables them.
