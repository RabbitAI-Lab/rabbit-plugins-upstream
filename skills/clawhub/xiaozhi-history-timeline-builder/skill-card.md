## Description:

Guides Chinese-language history students in building timelines, comparing contemporaneous events, converting dates, and reading historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students and educators use this skill to practice historical chronology, China–world comparisons, date conversion, and map interpretation through student-led exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent requirements for minors, and crisis-help resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profiles or reminders could be used without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep work in the current conversation by default; seek explicit student consent before recording progress or setting reminders.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis-help guidance may not fit other locations.

Mitigation: Adapt curriculum, consent rules for minors, and local crisis-help resources before use outside mainland China.

Risk: Incorrect chronology or unsupported historical interpretations could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check timelines and historical maps against the student's textbook and approved maps; keep interpretations within curriculum guidance.
