## Description:

Helps Chinese secondary-school students place historical events in time and space, build timelines and China–world comparisons, convert dates and interpret historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use the skill to practice historical chronology, connect causes and contemporaneous events, and read historical maps against their curriculum. Students build the timelines and comparison tables themselves, with guided checks rather than completed answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy practices and crisis-support channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's learning profile, reminders or parent-visible summaries could expose sensitive study information without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep sessions unrecorded by default and verify the student or guardian consent settings before enabling memory, cross-skill sharing, reminders or parent-visible summaries.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy expectations and crisis-support guidance may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, privacy rules and crisis-support channels before use outside mainland China.
