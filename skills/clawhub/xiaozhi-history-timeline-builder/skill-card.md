## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school students organize historical events by time and place through timelines, China–world comparisons, date conversions, and historical-map reading.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to build and check historical timelines, compare simultaneous events in China and elsewhere, convert dates, and interpret historical maps within the student's curriculum.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K–12 curriculum); use elsewhere requires localization

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's history profile or reminders could be used or shared without informed consent.

Mitigation: Keep work within the current session by default; enable memory, cross-skill or parent sharing, and reminders only with the student or guardian's informed consent.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy expectations, and crisis referrals may not fit other regions or languages.

Mitigation: Use the skill for Simplified Chinese Mainland China K–12 history study, and localize these safeguards before use elsewhere.
