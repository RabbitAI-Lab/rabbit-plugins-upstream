## Description:

帮助中学生用时间轴、中外同期对照、纪年换算和历史地图识读建立历史时空线索。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Simplified-Chinese middle- and high-school history learners use this skill to place events in time and space, build timelines, compare contemporaneous events, and practice historical chronology and map reading. Students construct the answers with guided prompts rather than receiving completed tables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (simplified-Chinese K12 education)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill or parent sharing, and reminders may expose minors' learning data without valid consent.

Mitigation: Before enabling these features, confirm the platform enforces separate consent, pause, deletion, export, sharing controls, and data localization; otherwise keep work within the current session.
