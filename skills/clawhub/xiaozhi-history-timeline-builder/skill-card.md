## Description:

Helps students place historical events in time and place by building timelines, comparing events from the same period in China and elsewhere, converting dates, and reading historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese middle and high school students use this skill to organize historical events chronologically and geographically, compare contemporaneous Chinese and world history, practice date conversions, and interpret historical maps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland curricula)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Enabling saved learning records, sharing, or reminders can involve storing or sharing student information.

Mitigation: Use conversation-only mode by default; review companion skills and platform storage settings before enabling memory, sharing, or reminders, and obtain the required consent.
