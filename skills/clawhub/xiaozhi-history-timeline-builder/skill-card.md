## Description:

时空线索构建器 helps students place historical events in the correct time and place, build vertical timelines and cause-effect links, compare Chinese and world events across the same period, practice era and century conversion, and read historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External students and learning-support agents use this skill for Chinese K12 history tutoring: locating events in time and space, building timelines, making same-period China-world comparisons, practicing calendar conversions, and checking historical-map reading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written for a mainland China Chinese K12 history-tutoring context, including curriculum assumptions and crisis-contact guidance.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum references, minor-consent handling, and crisis-contact instructions before use in other regions or languages.

Risk: Profile and reminder behavior can involve student learning records and minor-user data.

Mitigation: Keep profile writes, cross-skill sharing, and reminders consent-gated, and review the permissions with users or guardians before deployment.

Risk: Historical timelines, period summaries, and map-reading guidance can become misleading if they go beyond the applicable textbook or official map context.

Mitigation: Keep historical evaluation and period summaries aligned to curriculum and textbook wording, and use textbook or standard maps for map interpretation.
