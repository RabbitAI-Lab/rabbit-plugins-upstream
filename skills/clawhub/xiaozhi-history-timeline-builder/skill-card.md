## Description:

Guides Chinese secondary-school students in building historical timelines, comparing concurrent events across regions, converting dates and reading historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use the skill to practice historical chronology, periodization, China–world comparisons and map interpretation, with junior- and senior-high-school curriculum scaffolding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize crisis contacts and education-policy assumptions before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles, wrong-answer handoffs and reminders can expose a student's educational data.

Mitigation: Keep sessions unrecorded by default; obtain the learner's specific consent before each integration and honor pause, sharing, deletion and export controls.

Risk: Crisis referrals and curriculum assumptions may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the learner's region and localize emergency contacts and education-policy assumptions before use elsewhere.

Risk: Incorrect dates, map readings or historical comparisons could mislead learners.

Mitigation: Check chronology and sensitive historical claims against the learner's textbook and curriculum standards; ask learners to build and verify their own timelines.
