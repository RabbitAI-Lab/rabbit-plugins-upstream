## Description:

Helps students place historical events in time and space by building timelines, comparing contemporaneous events in China and elsewhere, converting dates, and reading historical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Junior and senior high school students use this skill to organize historical events into timelines, compare events from the same period across China and other regions, practice calendar conversions, and interpret historical maps in line with their curriculum.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: When enabled with consent, learning records can pass confirmed history progress and mistakes to companion skills.

Mitigation: Keep memory and cross-skill sharing off for chat-only use; review companion skills before enabling sharing, and use the documented pause, correction, deletion, and export controls.

Risk: Reminder behavior depends on companion skills whose runtime behavior is outside this release evidence.

Mitigation: Review the reminder companion before enabling reminders, and enable them only with explicit reminder consent.
