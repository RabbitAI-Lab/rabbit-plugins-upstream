## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
OPC创业者、项目负责人和协作团队可用此技能快速起草SPV治理十二件套，并设计贡献权重、分账和退出安排。生成内容适合作为讨论和审阅底稿，不应替代针对具体司法辖区和商业事实的专业法律意见。 <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may rely on legal-style SPV governance templates without professional review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated documents as preliminary drafts and have qualified legal counsel review them before signing or use. <br>
Risk: Users may enter unnecessary sensitive business data while requesting governance drafts. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the jurisdiction and business context needed for drafting and avoid sharing unnecessary confidential details. <br>
