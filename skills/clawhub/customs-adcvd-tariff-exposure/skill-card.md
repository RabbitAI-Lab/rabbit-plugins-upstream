## Description:

A customs screening checklist for identifying potential AD/CVD and Section 301 exposure, scope questions, and downstream importer risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customs brokers and import compliance teams use this checklist to screen products and sourcing changes for possible AD/CVD and Section 301 exposure, including scope, origin, and downstream duty questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tariff rules and AD/CVD scope are current and order-specific, so a screening checklist may not resolve an import decision on its own.

Mitigation: Confirm current tariff rules and order-specific scope with authoritative sources and qualified counsel before making import decisions.
