## Description: <br>
Helps agents screen customs scenarios for AD/CVD scope, Section 301 exposure, transshipment risk, and scope-ruling triggers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Customs brokers, import teams, and compliance reviewers use this skill to screen product and origin changes for AD/CVD scope, Section 301 exposure, evasion risk, and when to consider a scope ruling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with focus on United States customs exposure <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Customs exposure screening is legal and compliance sensitive if treated as a final determination. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as screening guidance and verify decisions against authoritative scope rulings, Section 301 materials, or qualified customs counsel. <br>
Risk: Incorrect product scope, origin, or transshipment assumptions can lead to misleading tariff exposure conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Check product description, HTS, origin, sourcing changes, and evasion indicators before relying on conclusions. <br>
