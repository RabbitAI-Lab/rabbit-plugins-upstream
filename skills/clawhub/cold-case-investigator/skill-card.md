## Description: <br>
Cold Case Investigator helps agents conduct iterative public-source research on unsolved cases, translate multilingual source material, separate evidence from speculation, and prepare structured investigation or creative-research reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, writers, podcasters, game designers, and other external users use this skill to gather and organize public information about cold cases or true-crime source material. It is intended for structured research reports, multilingual source comparison, and clearly labeled creative inspiration, not for accusing or identifying private individuals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public-web cold-case research can surface incomplete, conflicting, or low-quality claims about real events and people. <br>
Mitigation: Manually verify important claims against primary or authoritative sources, preserve source-quality labels, and keep uncertainty visible in the report. <br>
Risk: Track B creative speculation could be mistaken for factual evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Keep speculative material in a separate section, label it as having no evidence basis, and do not use it for factual accusations or case conclusions. <br>
Risk: Research involving names, case details, forums, and media can be misused to accuse, harass, or identify private individuals. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for public-source research or creative development, avoid harassment or doxxing, and maintain respectful language around victims and families. <br>
