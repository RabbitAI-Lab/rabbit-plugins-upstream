## Description: <br>
Demand Analysis turns business requirements, PRDs, or product descriptions into a structured requirements analysis covering functional modules, non-functional requirements, architecture constraints, and domain classification. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, product managers, and architects use this skill to convert ambiguous requirements into a structured report that supports early architecture planning and requirement clarification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided business, product, or compliance documents may contain sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide documents that are appropriate for the agent environment and avoid pasting sensitive material unless that processing is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Generated requirements analysis can include assumptions or to-confirm items when the original requirements are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the report with stakeholders and resolve open confirmation items before using it for architecture or implementation decisions. <br>
