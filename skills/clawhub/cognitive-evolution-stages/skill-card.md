## Description:

A five-stage coaching framework that helps people identify where their learning is stalled and plan deliberate practice toward the next stage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners, professionals, mentors, and teams use the framework to assess how they approach work in a specific domain and design focused practice to move from acquiring or imitating toward comparison, creation, and systematic revision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The five-stage framework could be treated as a definitive or universal measure of ability, though the supplied sources describe it as a synthesis.

Mitigation: Present it as a coaching aid, keep judgments tied to observable work in a specific domain, and avoid treating stage labels as a validated assessment.

Risk: Confidential work details could be retained in reusable coaching examples.

Mitigation: Anonymize examples and omit confidential details when creating reusable material.
