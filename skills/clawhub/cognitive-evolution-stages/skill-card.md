## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a five-stage cognitive development framework to diagnose where a learner is stuck and design deliberate practice for the next capability transition. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, analysts, creators, and teams use this skill to map recent work to cognitive development stages, identify the next capability gap, and design a four-week deliberate practice plan. It is intended for cases where the user has domain familiarity but is stuck moving from imitation toward explicit trade-off reasoning and original methods. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat stage labels as a definitive personal assessment instead of a working diagnosis. <br>
Mitigation: Anchor stage labels to three recent concrete outputs, falsifiable behavior, and a four-week behavioral reassessment. <br>
Risk: The skill can be misapplied when the user lacks basic domain knowledge or when the block is motivational rather than developmental. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's stated non-use criteria and first address Stage 1 content gaps or motivational barriers before using the stage framework. <br>
