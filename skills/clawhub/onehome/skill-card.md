## Description:

Helps agents search OneHome portal listings and retrieve property details, photos, schools, and saved searches.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Buyers and their assistants use this skill to research agent-shared OneHome listings, compare properties, and review photos and school information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: OneHome magic links and tokens grant access to private portal data.

Mitigation: Treat links and tokens as passwords, redact token parameters in shared output, and access portal data only with the user's intent.
