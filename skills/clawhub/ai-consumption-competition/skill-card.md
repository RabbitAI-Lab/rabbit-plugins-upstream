## Description:

首届上海'人工智能+消费'创新大赛报名助手。基于用户项目信息，从第一性原理出发通过对抗式审查提供赛道选择与报名填写专业建议。当用户参加AI+消费创新大赛、选择赛道或填写报名表时调用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External participants, founders, student teams, and individual developers use this skill to evaluate whether an AI+consumer project fits the Shanghai AI+Consumer Innovation Competition and to prepare track-selection, adversarial-review, and application-form guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competition details, deadlines, and submission contact may change outside the skill.

Mitigation: Confirm current competition information through official channels before relying on the application guidance.

Risk: Users may share confidential project details or personal information while seeking application help.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for track selection and form guidance, and redact sensitive business, personal, or credential data.

Risk: Chinese-language checklists and warnings may be misunderstood by users who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Request translated explanations of the guidance, checklist items, and warnings before acting on them.
