## Description:

Create, inspect, and edit Microsoft PowerPoint presentations and PPTX decks with reliable layouts, templates, placeholders, notes, charts, and visual QA.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill when an agent needs to create, inspect, edit, or quality-check PowerPoint decks while preserving templates, layouts, placeholders, notes, charts, and visual fidelity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PowerPoint edits can leave incorrect slide content, broken layouts, hidden notes, comments, or linked media in the final deck.

Mitigation: Review the edited deck before sharing externally, including rendered slides, notes, comments, charts, layouts, masters, and linked media.

Risk: Generated guidance or edit proposals may not match the user's intended template or presentation constraints.

Mitigation: Inspect the source deck or template first and verify the final presentation against the requested content and visual requirements.
