## Description:

Reviews a session for lessons learned and audits skills for actionable improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to review a session's mistakes, friction, successes, and operational lessons, then propose prioritized improvements and skill changes. Durable memory is saved only with appropriate authorization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved lessons, especially cross-project memory, can affect future agent behavior.

Mitigation: Review proposed memory entries carefully and authorize the specific items before they are saved.
