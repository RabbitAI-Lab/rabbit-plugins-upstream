## Description:

Guides teams in designing AI agent evaluations, interpreting benchmarks, comparing frameworks, and drafting scorecards and reports without executing tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

AI engineers, product managers, and ML teams use this skill to plan agent test suites, analyze failures and benchmark results, compare frameworks, and draft production-readiness reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive customer logs, credentials, or regulated data may be exposed when sharing evaluation examples.

Mitigation: Provide aggregate metrics and anonymized transcripts; remove credentials and sensitive data before sharing.

Risk: Outdated benchmark or tooling claims may mislead production release decisions.

Mitigation: Check time-sensitive claims against current official sources and have qualified ML engineers review conclusions.

Risk: Defensive adversarial test plans could be applied to systems without authorization.

Mitigation: Run proposed probes only on systems you own or are authorized to test.
