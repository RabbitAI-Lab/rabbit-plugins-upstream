# deadlines.md (sample)

One deadline per line: `- <when> | <course or project> | <what> | repo: <path>` (the repo part is optional).
`<when>` is `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` or `every <Weekday> HH:MM`.

- every Thursday 23:59 | CS 101 | Weekly problem set |
- every Friday 17:00 | Capstone | Weekly progress push | repo: projects/capstone
- 2026-12-04 17:00 | Capstone | Final demo | repo: projects/capstone
