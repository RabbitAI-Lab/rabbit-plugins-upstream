## Description: <br>
Prepare for industry analyst briefings by producing a briefing kit with objectives, narrative, differentiation, proof points, demo storyline, anticipated questions, and follow-up commitments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product marketing, analyst relations, and go-to-market teams use this skill to prepare credible briefing materials for analyst calls, evaluations, and submission narratives such as Magic Quadrant, Wave, and MarketScape engagements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process business-sensitive briefing inputs such as roadmap themes, customer proof, traction, and prior analyst feedback. <br>
Mitigation: Flag confidential, NDA-covered, or unconfirmed information and avoid exposing or inventing metrics, customer names, roadmap dates, or proof points. <br>
Risk: Briefing materials can become misleading if weak spots, uncertain claims, or analyst taxonomy mismatches are hidden. <br>
Mitigation: Mark uncertain claims as to-confirm, acknowledge gaps with a credible plan, and align the narrative and demo storyline to the analyst's evaluation criteria. <br>
