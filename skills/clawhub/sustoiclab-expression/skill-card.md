## Description: <br>
表达框架工具箱 · Su's StoicLab helps users organize speeches, articles, reports, and expression ideas with four Chinese-language frameworks: KongYuSan, 3C Persuasion, Pyramid Principle, and Timeline. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill for Chinese-language coaching before writing or speaking, especially to clarify motivation, analyze the audience, choose an expression framework, and produce a concise content skeleton. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language writing and speaking support, so English or multilingual users may receive unsuitable guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when Chinese-language expression coaching is acceptable, or adapt the output before relying on it in another language. <br>
Risk: Framework-based coaching can still produce outlines that are too generic, over-structured, or misaligned with the user's real intent. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated purpose, audience analysis, framework choice, and content skeleton before using the final expression. <br>
