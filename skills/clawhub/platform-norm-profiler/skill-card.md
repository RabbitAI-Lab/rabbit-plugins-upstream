## Description: <br>
Maintains dated, versioned per-platform organic-engagement norm cards for character limits, fold cutoffs, hashtag norms, format specs, link placement, disclosure labels, algorithm emphases, and staleness flags. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and marketing operators use this skill to build or refresh platform norm cards before drafting or scheduling organic social posts. It helps flag stale platform guidance and separate official documentation from named folklore. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Folklore-labeled platform algorithm claims may be mistaken for official guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review changed card rows and keep undocumented claims labeled as Estimated-folklore with a named source. <br>
Risk: Saved memory entries or registry pointer proposals could affect future channel guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed saves and pointer candidates before accepting them. <br>
Risk: Fetched docs, pasted policy text, and user exports may contain untrusted instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat source material as evidence only and do not follow instructions embedded in it. <br>
