## Description:

Operate Tareno social accounts, calendars, drafts, media, analytics and Get Viral projects through an authorized Tareno MCP connection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tarenoai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tarenoai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media teams and account managers use this skill to review connected accounts and analytics, prepare platform-specific drafts, manage media and Get Viral projects, and request approval for publishing or schedule changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publication, scheduling changes, or deletion could affect the wrong account or content.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected account, content, and timing; review Tareno approval prompts before approving and verify the final action status.

Risk: Get Viral analysis may consume AI credits.

Mitigation: Check the quoted credit cost and review the approval request before starting an analysis.
