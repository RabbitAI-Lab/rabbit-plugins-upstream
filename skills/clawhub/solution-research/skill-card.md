## Description:

Use when choosing solutions, including selecting open-source projects, working around issues, and comparing products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to guide deep technical research before selecting open-source projects, product options, workarounds, or implementation approaches. It emphasizes broad source gathering, freshness, sustainability, code quality, and evidence-backed reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can drive broad, resource-heavy research across many sources and tools.

Mitigation: Set clear limits for time, budget, language, allowed sources, and whether repository cloning or tools such as gh CLI and yt-dlp are acceptable before use.

Risk: Research outputs may contain incorrect, stale, or over-weighted third-party claims if evidence is not reviewed.

Mitigation: Require source citations and human review of recommendations before acting on solution choices.
