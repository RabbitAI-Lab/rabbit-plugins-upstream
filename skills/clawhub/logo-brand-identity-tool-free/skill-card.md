## Description: <br>
AI-driven logo and brand identity design tool for creating brand kits, color palettes, typography systems, brand guidance, and social media templates for personal and startup projects. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, personal creators, startups, and small teams use this skill to turn a brand brief into logo concepts, color palettes, font pairings, basic brand guidance, and social media template direction. It is intended as a design-starting workflow that benefits from human review before production brand use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests exec permission even though its primary workflow is Markdown-based brand design guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for user-directed design tasks and approve any command execution deliberately, especially network troubleshooting commands. <br>
Risk: Generated logo and brand concepts may be unsuitable for immediate commercial use or may overlap with existing trademarks. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with human design judgment and perform trademark or brand clearance before production use. <br>
Risk: The free version excludes some production assets such as print-ready business cards and email signatures. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as a starter brand kit and use a designer or additional tooling for final production assets. <br>
