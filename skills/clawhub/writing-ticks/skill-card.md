## Description:

Use when the user asks to audit, review, or rewrite prose for AI-writing tells, robotic phrasing, LLM-style wording, generic AI voice, Wikipedia-style AI signs, or human-sounding editorial quality.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[theangrypit](https://clawhub.ai/user/theangrypit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers, editors, and reviewers use the skill to audit prose for common AI-style signals and make concrete edits while preserving meaning and voice. It treats those signals as editorial observations, not evidence of authorship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may mistake stylistic signals for proof that a person used AI.

Mitigation: Present findings as editorial suggestions, not authorship claims, and review proposed rewrites for accuracy and voice.
