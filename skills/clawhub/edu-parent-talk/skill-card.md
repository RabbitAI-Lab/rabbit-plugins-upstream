## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student or parent identifying details may be included in prompts or shared drafts.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary details, remove identifiers where possible, and review drafts before sharing.

Risk: Drafted language may be unsuitable for sensitive, legal, medical, safety, or psychological-crisis situations.

Mitigation: Manually review sensitive communications and seek qualified support where needed; do not use the skill for psychological diagnosis.
