## Description:

Find podcasts suited to a proposed guest using Crawlora's podcast catalog, live show and episode data, and public show websites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to research podcast guest opportunities, build sourced show shortlists, inspect recent episode evidence, and prepare pitch angles when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guest or topic queries, selected scrape URLs, and the Crawlora API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use the skill for public podcast research, avoid confidential or unnecessary personal data, and keep the API key in the environment.

Risk: Generated outreach or pitch angles could include unsupported claims about audience size, booking availability, or editorial fit.

Mitigation: Review recent episode evidence, contact channels, and caveats before sending any outreach.
