## Description:

Find podcasts suited to a proposed guest using Crawlora's podcast catalog, live show and episode data, and public show websites to build sourced shortlists and tailored pitch angles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Podcast publicists, guest bookers, and creators use this skill to identify shows that fit a guest's expertise, check recent episode evidence, and draft relevant pitch angles without assuming booking availability or audience size.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Podcast queries and public page URLs, including any scrape request content, are sent to Crawlora using the user's API key.

Mitigation: Use only appropriate public URLs and non-sensitive data; confirm the transfer fits the user's Crawlora account and policies.
