## Description:

Find podcasts suited to a proposed guest using Crawlora's podcast catalog, live show and episode data, and public show websites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and podcast outreach teams use this skill to build sourced guest-appearance shortlists, research recent editorial fit, and draft pitch angles based on actual show and episode evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Podcast search terms, public URLs to scrape, guest details, contact lists, pitch strategy, or sensitive personal data may be sent to Crawlora under the user's API key.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential strategy, private contact lists, or sensitive personal data unless sharing that data with Crawlora is acceptable.

Risk: Research based on podcast metadata, public show pages, and endpoint responses can be incomplete or mistaken about audience size, booking availability, or whether a show accepts guests.

Mitigation: Preserve caveats, verify booking instructions and public contact channels, and avoid claiming audience metrics or booking likelihood unless directly supported by evidence.
