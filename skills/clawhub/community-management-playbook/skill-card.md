## Description: <br>
Builds a community management playbook for brand social media channels, including response templates, escalation paths, moderation rules, and tone guidelines. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media and community teams use this skill to create brand-specific guidance for comments, DMs, moderation, escalation, crisis handling, response SLAs, and community health metrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: DM response templates could lead teams to request more personal, account, payment, or screenshot data than support actually needs. <br>
Mitigation: Adapt the templates to the organization's privacy policy, collect only necessary support details, redact screenshots where possible, and move identity or account issues to an approved secure support form or ticketing system. <br>
Risk: The playbook may not match the team's real staffing, legal obligations, platform rules, or escalation contacts without review. <br>
Mitigation: Have the responsible community, support, legal, and brand leads review SLAs, moderation rules, escalation thresholds, and contact paths before operational use. <br>
