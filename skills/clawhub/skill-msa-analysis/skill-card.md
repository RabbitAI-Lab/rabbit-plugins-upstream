## Description: <br>
Guides measurement system analysis (MSA), including Gage R&R, bias, linearity, stability analysis, data collection guidance, result interpretation, and report generation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Manufacturing quality engineers, quality analysts, and external users use this skill to plan MSA studies, guide data collection, analyze measurement-system accuracy, and generate actionable MSA reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The artifact references analysis scripts and report templates that are not included in the package. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm any added scripts or templates before use, and review their behavior separately from this guidance-only release. <br>
Risk: Spreadsheet or CSV inputs for measurement data may be malformed or contain unsafe content. <br>
Mitigation: Validate uploaded data formats and avoid executing spreadsheet macros or embedded active content. <br>
Risk: Generated HTML reports may include user-derived fields or sensitive business measurement data. <br>
Mitigation: Sanitize user-derived report fields before embedding them in HTML and handle measurement data as confidential business information. <br>
