## Description:

Audit an online shop's category and product pages, Product and Offer markup, merchant listings, faceted navigation, out-of-stock items and variant URLs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce site owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to review category and product pages, product data, Merchant Center listings, and URL indexation. It produces a prioritized audit with evidence and recommended fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted shop URLs may contain private information or sensitive query parameters, and the workflow's reported tests send submitted URLs to Google.

Mitigation: Review URLs before submitting them and remove private paths or sensitive query parameters.

Risk: Security analysis coverage was partial because a local path-like reference could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Resolve and review the referenced component, and treat audit conclusions as incomplete until coverage is confirmed.
