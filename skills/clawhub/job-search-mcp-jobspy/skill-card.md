## Description: <br>
Search and compare job listings across multiple boards using JobSpy MCP, with filters for criteria, location, salary, remote options, and easy apply roles. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[nikkijasmine](https://clawhub.ai/user/nikkijasmine) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use this skill to configure and operate a JobSpy MCP workflow for finding, filtering, and comparing job listings across multiple job boards. It supports searches by role, location, company, remote status, salary availability, recency, and easy apply status. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The setup installs Python packages for a job-search MCP workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Use an isolated virtual environment and review package sources before installation. <br>
Risk: Job searches can contain sensitive personal or career criteria that may be sent to queried job sites or services. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal data unless the destination services and their data handling are understood. <br>
