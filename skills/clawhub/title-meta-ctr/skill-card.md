## Description:

Find pages with impressions but weak clicks in Search Console, compare their CTR with your own site's CTR-by-position baseline, check what Google really displays, and write 3 title and meta description variants per page with a 28-day test plan. Use for "low CTR", "high impressions low clicks", "rewrite my title tags".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO professionals and site owners use this skill to identify pages whose organic search click-through rate is weak relative to their own position-based baseline. It helps diagnose whether the displayed search result is a likely cause, draft three title and meta description variants per page, and plan a measured test.
