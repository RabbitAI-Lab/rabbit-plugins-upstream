## Description: <br>
Helps agent users and skill authors turn proactive-agent workflow needs into practical plans, checklists, analysis, code changes, and validation notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt popular proactive-agent workflow patterns into practical local workflows, safety hardening, reliability improvements, bug-fix support, and adjacent skill ideas. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may cause the skill to activate too often or be applied to loosely related planning tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the request is about proactive workflows, reliability, safety hardening, bug-fix support, or adjacent skill planning before applying the skill, and review generated plans before acting on them. <br>
