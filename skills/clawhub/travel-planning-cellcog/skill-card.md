## Description: <br>
AI travel planning and itinerary generation powered by CellCog for travel research, logistics planning, and itineraries delivered as PDFs, interactive dashboards, or Markdown. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to request destination research, trip itineraries, travel logistics, budget estimates, visa research, weather and event research, and practical travel planning outputs through CellCog. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel-planning prompts and included trip details are sent to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid unnecessary sensitive details such as full legal names, passport numbers, exact home address, or private account information. <br>
Risk: Travel plans may rely on time-sensitive prices, visa requirements, weather, events, and availability. <br>
Mitigation: Verify critical logistics and official requirements before booking or travel. <br>
