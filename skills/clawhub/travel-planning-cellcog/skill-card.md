## Description:

AI travel planning and itinerary generation powered by CellCog. Vacation planning, travel research, flight planning, hotel recommendations, visa requirements, weather patterns, local events. Complete itineraries as PDFs or interactive dashboards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travelers use this skill to research destinations and build tailored trip itineraries, compare travel options, and gather practical information such as visa, weather, transport, and budget guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel prompts may contain sensitive personal details, including information about children or accessibility needs.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed to plan the trip.

Risk: The skill requires a CellCog API key and may involve service charges or dependency changes.

Mitigation: Protect CELLCOG_API_KEY, check service charges, and use reviewed dependency versions.

Risk: OpenClaw fire-and-forget use may involve background notifications through the CellCog integration.

Mitigation: Review the integration's notification behavior before enabling fire-and-forget use.
