## Description:

Helps teachers draft Chinese K–12 lesson plans using backward design, observable learning goals, assessment evidence, timed activities, question chains, and differentiated tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use the skill to draft single-lesson or unit plans, including assessment criteria and classroom question chains. When authorized class summaries and student tiers are available, it can adapt tasks to different learning needs; teachers review plans before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localization required for use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class-level academic summaries, pseudonymous student tiers, and weakness identifiers may expose sensitive educational information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized classroom data, omit student identities and individual scores from plans, and respect the skill's record and sharing controls.

Risk: AI-generated exercises or lesson activities may be inaccurate or unsuitable for the class.

Mitigation: Have teachers check learning goals, timing, and generated exercises before reuse or distribution.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis referral contacts may not apply in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum and support contacts before deploying outside Mainland China.
