## Description:

Helps teachers design observable, differentiated K–12 lesson plans using backward design, assessment evidence, timed activities, and draft questioning sequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Chinese K–12 schools use this skill to draft single-lesson and unit plans with measurable learning objectives, assessment evidence, timed activities, questioning prompts, and differentiated tasks when authorized class data is available. Teachers review the drafts before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); other regions require localization of curriculum standards, crisis contacts, and privacy assumptions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson records and classroom learning data may expose student information when memory or sharing is enabled.

Mitigation: Review platform memory and sharing settings, use authorized class data only, and remove identifying details from lesson drafts.

Risk: Curriculum standards, crisis contacts, and privacy assumptions may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Use in Chinese-language, mainland China K–12 contexts unless locally adapting and reviewing those elements.
