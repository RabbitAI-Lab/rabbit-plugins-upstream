## Description:

Helps teachers draft observable, standards-aligned Chinese K–12 lesson plans using backward design, assessment evidence, timed activities, question sequences, and differentiated tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to draft single-lesson or unit plans with observable learning goals, assessment evidence, classroom timing, and question sequences. When class data is available, it can tailor tasks for different student groups; teachers review plans and exercises before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; curriculum assumptions and crisis-support contacts require localization elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class summaries, learning weaknesses, and student-group labels may expose sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Limit access to necessary class data, omit identifying details from plans, and honor data-sharing and deletion controls.

Risk: Generated lesson plans or exercises may contain errors or unsuitable teaching recommendations.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check curriculum fit, exercise accuracy, and suitability before classroom use.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis-support contacts are tailored to Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and support contacts before deploying elsewhere.
