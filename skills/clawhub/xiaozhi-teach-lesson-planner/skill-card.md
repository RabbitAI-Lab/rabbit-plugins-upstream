## Description:

帮助中国大陆中小学教师用 UbD 逆向设计编写可评估、可分层的课堂教案和提问链草案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K-12 teachers use this skill to plan new lessons, review lessons, and differentiated instruction with observable objectives, assessment evidence, timed activities, and draft questions. Teachers review and adapt the resulting plans before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson planning can involve sensitive student tiers, class summaries, and performance data.

Mitigation: Confirm workspace consent before reading or saving class data; avoid student identifiers in drafts and use pause, delete, or export controls when needed.

Risk: AI-generated exercises or unreviewed lesson plans may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Have a teacher verify generated exercises and approve objectives, questions, and activities before classroom use.
