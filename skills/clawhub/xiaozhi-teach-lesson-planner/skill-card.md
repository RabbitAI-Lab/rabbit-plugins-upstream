## Description:

Helps teachers draft observable, differentiated lesson plans using backward design, assessment evidence, timed activities, and question sequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

K–12 teachers use this skill to draft single-lesson and unit plans with measurable learning objectives, assessment evidence, classroom timing, question prompts, and differentiated activities. Teachers review and adapt drafts before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class-specific plans may expose student information or use class data without appropriate permission.

Mitigation: Confirm student-data sharing and teacher writeback permissions; use de-identified class summaries and avoid identifiable student details.

Risk: Unverified exercises or unsupported student tiers could misdirect teaching.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check generated exercises and lesson drafts; omit A/B/C tiers when class data is unavailable.

Risk: Local curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt curriculum expectations and local emergency or support contacts before use elsewhere.
