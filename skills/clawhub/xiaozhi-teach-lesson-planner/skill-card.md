## Description:

Helps teachers design observable K–12 lesson objectives, assessment evidence, timed activities, question sequences, and differentiated tasks using backward design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Chinese-language K–12 classrooms use the skill to draft single-lesson or unit plans with assessable learning goals, question-chain outlines, and optional A/B/C activities based on existing class summaries. Teachers review the drafts before classroom or student-facing use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum assumptions and crisis-referral channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student tiers and assessment summaries may contain sensitive class information.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, sharing, and write boundaries before using real class data; keep student identifiers out of lesson plans.

Risk: Generated lesson activities or exercises may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review the plan and verify AI-generated exercises before classroom use.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis-referral channels are tailored to mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum and referral channels before deploying elsewhere.
