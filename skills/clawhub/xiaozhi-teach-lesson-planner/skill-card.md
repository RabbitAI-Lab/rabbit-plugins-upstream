## Description:

Helps teachers draft observable, differentiated K–12 lesson plans using Understanding by Design, assessment evidence, timed activities, and question chains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in mainland China's Chinese-language K–12 classrooms use this skill to draft lesson objectives, assessment evidence, timed activities, preliminary question chains, and differentiated tasks for review and adjustment before teaching.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localization of curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class summaries and student-tier data may contain sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Use authorized, de-identified class data; omit student names and identifying details from lesson plans.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts are tailored to mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize standards, privacy rules, and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: Generated lesson content or exercises may be incorrect or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review objectives, timings, questions, and any AI-generated exercises before classroom use.
