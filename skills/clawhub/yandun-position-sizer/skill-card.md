## Description:

Yandun Position Sizer provides demonstration position sizing, stop-loss/take-profit levels, and risk budget allocation from built-in deterministic rules.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill to generate educational examples of position sizing, stop-loss/take-profit calculations, and risk-budget allocation for sample securities. The outputs are for teaching and demonstration only and are not real investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the demonstration position sizing and stop-loss/take-profit outputs for real financial advice.

Mitigation: Keep the educational-use disclosure visible and require independent financial review before using any output in trading or portfolio decisions.

Risk: Built-in sample prices and deterministic rules may be stale or unsuitable for live market conditions.

Mitigation: Treat generated numbers as examples only and replace sample data and assumptions with reviewed, current data before any production use.
