## Description:

Use when you ride a bike and want to know WHAT to maintain WHEN: converts ridden km/weeks/conditions into a dated service schedule per component (chain wear, cassette, brake pads, cables, tires, bearings, suspension, drivetrain deep-clean), prices wear parts by component-lifetimes math, and diagnoses drivetrain noises/brake rub/shifting ghosts. Solves the 'chain skipped, now the cassette is dead too' cascade problem.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External cyclists can use the skill to turn their riding distance, conditions, storage, and bike setup into a dated maintenance schedule and annual wear-parts budget. It also provides ordered checks for common drivetrain and brake symptoms before buying parts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The service-date calculation can produce dates that are too early.

Mitigation: Check or independently calculate service dates before relying on them. Treat intervals and repair instructions as general guidance, and follow component-manufacturer specifications for safety-critical work.
