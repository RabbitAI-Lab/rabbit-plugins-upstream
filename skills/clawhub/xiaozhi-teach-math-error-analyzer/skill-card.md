## Description:

Helps Chinese-language middle- and high-school math teachers identify patterns in classwork and exam mistakes and plan targeted teaching interventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Math teachers analyze anonymized student errors, map recurring weaknesses to knowledge points, and draft class-level or individual teaching interventions for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student performance records could reveal a child's identity or sensitive learning history.

Mitigation: Use anonymized item-level data and confirm consent settings before saving records, sharing across skills, sending to parents, or writing back to student profiles.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials could be exposed during error analysis.

Mitigation: Do not provide confidential live exam questions or answers; analyze eligible error data only after the relevant exam has finished.

Risk: Incorrect analysis or generated exercises could misdirect instruction.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review diagnoses and check any AI-generated exercise before assigning or sharing it.
