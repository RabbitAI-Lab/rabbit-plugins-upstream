## Description:

帮助初中数学教师从作业和测评错题中识别班级共性与个体错因，并制定下一步教学干预建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中数学教师使用学生化名和具体错题或逐题成绩，分析错因、定位薄弱知识点，并拟定班级及个体教学干预方案；最终判断和教学决策由教师负责。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student work and error profiles can expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use aliases, avoid publishing identifiable mistakes, and check consent before writing to student records.

Risk: AI-generated practice items or inferred error causes may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Teachers verify exercises and review analyses before using them in lessons or assessments.

Risk: Curriculum, consent rules, and crisis contacts may not transfer across regions.

Mitigation: Localize these requirements before using the skill outside Mainland China.
