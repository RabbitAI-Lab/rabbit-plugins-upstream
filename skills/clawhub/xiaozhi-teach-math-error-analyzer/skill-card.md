## Description:

帮助初高中数学教师分析班级错题、定位共性错因和知识薄弱点，并提出后续教学干预建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to review anonymized junior and senior high school mathematics assessment errors, identify common learning gaps, and draft class-level and individual teaching interventions for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student-level records or reports could expose sensitive information about minors.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or student IDs, and enforce consent checks before teacher writeback, parent sharing, or retention; provide deletion controls.

Risk: Small samples or AI-generated practice questions could lead to misleading teaching decisions.

Mitigation: Label sample sizes and data limits, and require teacher review and verification before using generated material with students.
