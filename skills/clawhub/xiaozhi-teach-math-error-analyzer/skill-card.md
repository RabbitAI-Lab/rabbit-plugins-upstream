## Description:

Helps middle- and high-school math teachers analyze mistakes in assignments and exams, identify class and student learning gaps, and plan targeted instruction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Math teachers use students' assignment or exam responses to classify recurring errors, map weak knowledge areas, prepare class and individual reports, and choose what to revisit in the next lesson.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student assessment records or identifying details could be shared or saved without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, honor teacher write-back and parent-sharing consent controls, and have teachers review proposed updates before saving.

Risk: Small answer samples could be mistaken for reliable whole-class error rates.

Mitigation: Label sampled and whole-class results separately; avoid percentages or long-term records when the sample is under ten.
