## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school math teachers analyze class and individual mistakes, map knowledge gaps, and draft targeted teaching interventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Math teachers use assignment and exam responses to classify common errors, identify weak knowledge areas, and prepare class-level and selected-student intervention drafts. Teachers review the analysis and decide what to teach next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland); localize curriculum, student-data controls, and crisis resources before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student and class error records may expose minors' information if shared or retained without appropriate controls.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, use student aliases, require teacher approval before sharing or writeback, and enforce deletion and sharing controls.

Risk: Small samples or unverified AI-generated exercises may mislead teaching decisions.

Mitigation: Label sample sizes separately from whole-class counts, and have a teacher verify generated exercises and intervention drafts before use.
