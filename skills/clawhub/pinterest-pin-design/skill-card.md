## Description: <br>
Pinterest Pin Design helps agents design and brief Pinterest pin concepts, keyword overlays, brand-consistent templates, and 3-5 fresh visual variants for a destination URL while leaving final raster creation and publishing to approved tools or humans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, creators, and social media teams use this skill to turn a destination page, brand profile, and pinterest-seo keyword into a Pinterest-ready visual concept, brief, and fresh-variant plan. It is useful when an agent needs to specify a 2:3 pin design, critique a mockup, or route the finished asset to image/design and publishing tools without fabricating performance claims. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated pin concepts can misrepresent the destination page or omit required AI imagery and affiliate disclosures. <br>
Mitigation: Review each brief against the destination, board, and page promise before routing it to an image tool or publishing workflow; require AI imagery and affiliate disclosures where relevant. <br>
Risk: A connected publishing workflow could post a finished pin without the normal account-owner approval. <br>
Mitigation: Do not allow publishing tools to post automatically; require account-owner approval for the finished image, title, description, board, and link. <br>
Risk: Near-duplicate variants or fabricated engagement claims could create misleading marketing guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Require genuinely distinct image, layout, and overlay angles for fresh variants, and use native Pinterest Analytics rather than invented saves, clicks, or impressions. <br>
