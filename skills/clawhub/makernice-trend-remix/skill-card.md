## Description:

Turn the user's photos into a video using a Makernice trend template, a preset effect with a fixed model, motion and length. Use when the user wants a trending effect, a viral-style clip from a photo, or asks what templates are available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[makernice](https://clawhub.ai/user/makernice)

## Use Case:

People who want a trending effect use a selected Makernice template to turn their own photos, or photos of consenting adults, into a short video. The agent shows the estimated price and waits for approval before generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anyone with a public photo URL may be able to access the image, and the provider may retain submitted images.

Mitigation: Use only photos you have permission to share publicly, including consent from any depicted adults, and check the provider's retention and deletion terms.

Risk: A completed generation may incur a charge even when safety review withholds the result.

Mitigation: Show the exact estimate and get the user's approval before generating; explain that a withheld completed result may still be charged.
