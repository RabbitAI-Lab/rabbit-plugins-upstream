## Description:

Turn the user's photos into a video using a Makernice trend template, a preset effect with a fixed model, motion and length. Use when the user wants a trending effect, a viral-style clip from a photo, or asks what templates are available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People use the skill to browse preset video trends and turn photos they have permission to use into short, trend-styled clips. It guides them through template selection, photo links, options, price approval, and result delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photo URLs must be public, so anyone with a link may access the images, and the service processes them remotely.

Mitigation: Use only photos whose subjects agreed to public hosting and third-party processing; consider temporary hosting and check Makernice's retention terms.

Risk: A generated result hidden during safety review can still be charged.

Mitigation: Show the estimated price and get the user's approval before generation, and explain that a blocked result may still incur the charge.
