## Description:

Guides expert review of AI-generated test cases through risk-based sampling, quality checks, corrections, and feedback for future improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA professionals and developers use this Chinese-language workflow to perform final review of AI-generated test cases before release, identify gaps, and recommend corrections and prompt improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad QA trigger phrases may select this skill for requests that are not final test-case reviews.

Mitigation: Review trigger phrases alongside other QA skills and use it for final review of AI-generated test cases.

Risk: Sampled reviews can leave defects or missing requirements undiscovered.

Mitigation: State the sampling basis, flag uncovered modules, and return systemic gaps for correction and renewed review.
