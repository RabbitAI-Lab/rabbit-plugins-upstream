## Secondary citations

The usability module refers to findings from studies that Marcus (2004) reports. This skill did
not consult those studies directly. They are cited in the form "Author Year, as cited in Marcus
2004"; where Marcus reports another secondary source, the full intermediary chain is retained. The
studies named are: Gilb (1988); Scerbo & Bosert (1991), through Bias & Mayhew (1994);
Pressman (1992); Spool / User Interface Engineering (1998), through Nielsen (1998); Vividence
(2001); Nielsen (1997); Gallaway (1981); Karat (1990, 1994); and Dray & Karat (1994). Full
references are in the white paper's reference list.

## What this skill does not contain

- No passages, checklists, tables, figures, or examples copied from either source.
- No reproduction of the book's worked ROI example; the example in `references/roi-model.md` uses
  invented figures.
- No complete listing of the white paper's statistics; only a short selection, paraphrased.

The skill is a companion to the sources, not a substitute. Anyone applying it seriously should
read the book and the white paper.

## Accuracy note

The 2005 book metadata above was checked against the publisher's table of contents. Every criterion
and chapter reference was then checked against the two loaded sources in a source-grounded notebook
on 2026-10-08. Six mappings were corrected or narrowed: CP-3, FM-1, FM-2, CO-5, TR-2, and TR-4.
White-paper section names and the author/year chains for the selected secondary findings were also
verified. Page numbers are used only where the source or publisher record was checked. If you find
a misattribution, please open an issue.
