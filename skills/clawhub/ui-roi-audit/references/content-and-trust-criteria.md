# Content and trust criteria

Cross-cutting criteria that apply to every page type. Paraphrased from Loveday & Niehaus (2008);
chapter references show where each topic is treated.

Citation key: **LN08** = Loveday & Niehaus 2008. Full reference in `../ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Headline and value proposition

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| VP-1 | The headline states a concrete customer benefit, readable in a few seconds | LN08, ch. 4, ch. 5 |
| VP-2 | The wording is plain; no vague corporate language standing in for the offer | LN08, ch. 4, ch. 5 |
| VP-3 | The value proposition is visible without scrolling | LN08, ch. 5 |

## Calls to action

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CA-1 | The primary call to action is the most visually dominant interactive element | LN08, ch. 4, ch. 5 |
| CA-2 | Its label starts with an action verb and says what happens next | LN08, ch. 4 |
| CA-3 | It is placed above the first screen break | LN08, ch. 4 |
| CA-4 | Competing actions are fewer and visually quieter than the primary one | LN08, ch. 4, ch. 9 |

## Product content and images

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PC-1 | Descriptions are concise and lead with the advantages that matter to the buyer | LN08, ch. 7 |
| PC-2 | Key points are scannable, not buried in a paragraph | LN08, ch. 7 |
| PC-3 | Images show the product itself, in enough detail to judge it | LN08, ch. 6, ch. 7 |

## Price

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PR-1 | Price is stated openly; no charge first appears at the final step | LN08, ch. 6, ch. 7 |
| PR-2 | Discounts and savings are shown explicitly | LN08, ch. 7 |

## Trust and credibility

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| TR-1 | Security assurances appear where personal or payment data is requested | LN08, ch. 8, ch. 9 |
| TR-2 | A privacy-policy link appears next to forms that collect personal data | LN08, ch. 8 |
| TR-3 | Returns and guarantee terms are stated before purchase | LN08, ch. 9 |
| TR-4 | Full contact details are easy to find | LN08, ch. 5, ch. 8, ch. 9 |
| TR-5 | Customer opinions or other third-party proof are present | LN08, ch. 7 |

## Error messages

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ER-1 | The message appears next to the field that caused it | LN08, ch. 8 |
| ER-2 | It says what is wrong and how to fix it | LN08, ch. 8 |
| ER-3 | Correctly entered data is kept after an error | LN08, ch. 8 |

## Mistakes the book warns against

Use these as pattern checks across the whole engagement, not only the audited page.

| ID | Mistake | Source |
|---|---|---|
| MK-1 | Most of the budget goes to the home page while landing pages, forms, and checkout are neglected | LN08, ch. 3 |
| MK-2 | An account is required before a first purchase | LN08, ch. 9 |
| MK-3 | Pages carry so many links that the main action is lost | LN08, ch. 4, ch. 9 |
| MK-4 | Analytics is used to count page views instead of tracing where visitors leave | LN08, ch. 3 |
