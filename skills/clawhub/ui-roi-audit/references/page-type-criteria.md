# Criteria by page type

Read only the section for the page being audited. Each criterion is a paraphrased, checkable
statement of guidance from Loveday & Niehaus (2008). Chapter references point to where the topic is
treated; consult the book for the authors' full reasoning and examples.

Citation key: **LN08** = Loveday & Niehaus 2008. Full reference in `../ATTRIBUTION.md`.

---

## Landing pages (LN08, ch. 4)

Why it matters: this is where paid traffic arrives. Losing the visitor here wastes the ad spend.

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| LP-1 | The headline repeats the promise of the ad or link that brought the visitor | LN08, ch. 4 |
| LP-2 | Images match the ad's subject, so the visitor recognizes they are in the right place | LN08, ch. 4 |
| LP-3 | One primary call to action, visible without scrolling | LN08, ch. 4 |
| LP-4 | Navigation and links that lead away from the goal are removed or reduced | LN08, ch. 4 |

## Home pages (LN08, ch. 5)

Why it matters: it orients visitors and routes them to the sections that convert.

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| HP-1 | A visitor can tell what the organization offers and for whom within seconds | LN08, ch. 5 |
| HP-2 | The logo and identity are clearly visible | LN08, ch. 5 |
| HP-3 | A clear call to action points to the main conversion path | LN08, ch. 5 |
| HP-4 | Navigation is clear and easy to scan | LN08, ch. 5 |
| HP-5 | No splash screen or intro stands between the visitor and the content | LN08, ch. 5 |

## Category pages (LN08, ch. 6)

Why it matters: visitors who bounce between list and detail pages without finding a fit leave.

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CP-1 | Filters relevant to this category let visitors narrow the list | LN08, ch. 6 |
| CP-2 | Sorting is available on the attributes buyers compare | LN08, ch. 6 |
| CP-3 | Product images and thumbnails are large enough and framed clearly enough to remain recognizable in the list | LN08, ch. 6 |
| CP-4 | Price is shown in the list, so no click is needed to learn it | LN08, ch. 6 |

## Detail / product pages (LN08, ch. 7)

Why it matters: this is where the buying decision is made or abandoned.

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DP-1 | Product images are high quality and can be enlarged | LN08, ch. 7 |
| DP-2 | The add-to-cart or equivalent action is prominent | LN08, ch. 7 |
| DP-3 | The description states the properties a buyer needs to decide | LN08, ch. 7 |
| DP-4 | Customer reviews or ratings are shown | LN08, ch. 7 |
| DP-5 | Availability and shipping information appear before the cart | LN08, ch. 7 |
| DP-6 | Price is clearly visible next to the action | LN08, ch. 7 |

## Forms (LN08, ch. 8)

Why it matters: every unnecessary or confusing field costs completions.

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FM-1 | The form follows a clear vertical flow, normally one column; short related fields may share a row | LN08, ch. 8 |
| FM-2 | Labels sit close to their fields and use a consistent top- or right-aligned pattern that is easy to scan | LN08, ch. 8 |
| FM-3 | Data the system already knows is pre-filled | LN08, ch. 8 |
| FM-4 | Help appears next to the field that needs it | LN08, ch. 8 |
| FM-5 | Error messages follow criteria ER-1 to ER-3 in `content-and-trust-criteria.md` | LN08, ch. 8 |
| FM-6 | Reassurance about privacy and data use sits near the submit action | LN08, ch. 8 |

## Checkout (LN08, ch. 9)

Why it matters: the visitor has already decided to buy. Every loss here is lost revenue.

| ID | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CO-1 | Site navigation and unrelated links are removed during checkout | LN08, ch. 9 |
| CO-2 | A step indicator shows where the buyer is and what remains | LN08, ch. 9 |
| CO-3 | Purchase is possible without creating an account | LN08, ch. 9 |
| CO-4 | Security and guarantee assurances are visible where payment data is entered | LN08, ch. 9 |
| CO-5 | No charge appears in checkout that was hidden on earlier pages | LN08, ch. 6, ch. 7, ch. 9 |
