# Source limitations

Read this before quoting any statistic or benchmark from the sources to a user.

Citation keys: **LN08** = Loveday & Niehaus 2008. **M04** = Marcus 2004. Full references in
`../ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## What each source covers

| Source | Focus | Weak on |
|---|---|---|
| LN08 | E-commerce and lead generation; traffic, conversion rate, page-level design | Internal software, support and training cost |
| M04 | Software and enterprise systems; development, maintenance, productivity, support, training | Page-level content guidance |

The two sources complement each other. Do not use one to stretch the other: LN08 does not prove
claims about internal tools, and M04 does not prescribe page content.

## Dated material

| Item | Why it is dated | How to handle |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising costs in LN08's worked examples (ch. 2) | Reflect the paid-search market of the mid-2000s | Use the method, replace the figures with the user's own |
| Case statistics in M04 | Studies from 1981–2001, many from desktop software | Cite with year and original study; never call them benchmarks |
| Distribution and documentation examples in M04 | Refer to physical media and printed manuals | Use for the principle (support cost), not the specifics |
| Screen and device assumptions in both | Predate mobile-first browsing | Re-interpret "above the fold" as "within the first screen on the user's device" and say so |

Neither source covers mobile commerce, cookie consent, modern checkout wallets, or accessibility
law. Do not cite them for these topics.

## Caveats the authors state themselves

- LN08 describes forecasting conversion gains as more art than science and advises conservative
  estimates; simple models leave out effects such as seasonality (ch. 2, ch. 3).
- M04 states that no single cost-benefit formula fits every situation and that the reported figures
  indicate likely outcomes, not guarantees for a given project ("How can we prove the ROI",
  "Conclusion").

Carry these caveats into every report that contains an estimate.
