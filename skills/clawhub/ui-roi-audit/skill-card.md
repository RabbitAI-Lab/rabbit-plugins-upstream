## Description:

Audit web pages and task-driven interfaces against source-cited conversion, usability, and ROI criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[monikazapisek](https://clawhub.ai/user/monikazapisek)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to audit one web page or task flow, prioritize improvements, and estimate potential revenue gained or costs avoided when supporting data is available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ROI estimates may mislead when traffic, conversion, or cost data is incomplete.

Mitigation: Use explicit assumptions and ranges; report qualitative priorities when value data is missing.

Risk: Page content and business metrics shared for an audit may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the review; do not provide credentials or broad local file access.
