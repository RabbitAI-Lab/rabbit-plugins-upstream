## Use Case:

Patent and product strategy teams use this skill to prepare project initiation analysis for a specified technology or product field. It combines market research, domestic and international patent layout analysis, competitive mapping, technology hotspot and whitespace identification, jurisdiction coverage analysis, and differentiated project recommendations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can share patent or market research topics with the PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP service during retrieval.

Mitigation: Confirm MCP authorization and data-sharing approval before using confidential or business-sensitive topics.

Risk: Generated HTML reports may contain competitive analysis and strategy recommendations that are business-sensitive.

Mitigation: Handle generated reports as confidential and review them before broad distribution.

Risk: Patent and market conclusions depend on external retrieval results, source coverage, and the configured search scope.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, retrieval assumptions, and sample-size notes before using recommendations for investment or filing decisions.
