## Description:

Provides official-source guidance and evidence for Japan experiential-tourism market entry and Japanese ecommerce regulations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business operators, advisors, and agent developers use this skill to gather official-source evidence for Japan experiential-tourism market-entry planning and Japanese ecommerce rule review. It supports informational research and integration setup, not legal advice, bookings, permit applications, or compliance decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake informational market-entry and ecommerce evidence for legal advice or a compliance decision.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support and have qualified reviewers confirm legal, permit, booking, and market-entry decisions.

Risk: The skill connects to a remote MCP provider and may involve x402 purchases.

Mitigation: Confirm trust in the remote MCP provider and review commercial terms before any x402 purchase.
