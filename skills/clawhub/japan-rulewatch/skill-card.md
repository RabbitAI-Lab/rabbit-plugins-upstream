## Description:

Japan RuleWatch helps authorized business agents gather concise official-source evidence for Japan tourism market-entry and Japanese ecommerce regulation questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kadopi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kadopi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business agents and developers use this skill to select free Japan RuleWatch research routes, summarize official-source evidence, and request explicit business approval before any paid Iya preparation-pack purchase.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill contacts an external Japan RuleWatch MCP service for research.

Mitigation: Install only where that external service use is acceptable, and disclose that service dependency to users.

Risk: The paid Iya preparation pack can create commercial cost or commitment risk if invoked casually.

Mitigation: Review the tool-returned price, scope, and limits, then obtain explicit business-principal approval before purchase.

Risk: Users may treat informational evidence as legal advice or a compliance decision.

Mitigation: Keep responses to links, evidence, unknowns, and next business decisions; do not provide legal verdicts, permit decisions, bookings, or launch guarantees.
