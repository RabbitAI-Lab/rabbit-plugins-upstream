External authors and writing teams use this skill to plan, draft, continue, and quality-check original Chinese web novels through quick, standard, or batch-production workflows.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow includes AI-style filtering that could be misused to conceal AI authorship from publishing platforms.

Mitigation: Use the filtering as an editing-quality aid, follow platform disclosure rules, and do not present generated work deceptively.

Risk: Style imitation and web-novel production guidance could lead to copying an identifiable author's voice, plot, or protected setting.

Mitigation: Use the skill for original fiction, borrow only high-level structure or pacing concepts, and review drafts for originality before release.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill during adjacent writing tasks where a full novel workflow is not intended.

Mitigation: Enable it intentionally for long-form Chinese fiction workflows and confirm the mode before generating or revising major content.

Risk: Local scripts scaffold project files and check chapter batches, which can affect workspace contents.

Mitigation: Run scripts in a dedicated project directory and review generated files before publishing or committing them.
