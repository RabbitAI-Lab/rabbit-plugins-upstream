## Description: <br>
Designs pre-scale message validation specs for candidate marketing narratives, including hypotheses, panel criteria, comprehension, 5-second or message-market-fit protocols, stimuli, thresholds, and stop/revise rules. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, narrative, and growth teams use this skill to design a message validation test before scaling spend. It helps turn a candidate tagline, one-liner, pillar, or surface message into a measurable panel protocol and handoff plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can propose narrative memory writes and claim proposals, which could persist incorrect or unsubstantiated marketing claims if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed memory writes and claim proposals before saving; keep unsupported claims marked as needing a source until they are adjudicated by the appropriate claim registry. <br>
Risk: The skill designs tests but does not run panels or analyze returned results, so its design spec should not be treated as validation evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use the appropriate experiment builder and performance analysis workflow before promoting any message as validated or increasing spend. <br>
