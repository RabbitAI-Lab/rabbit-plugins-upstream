## Description: <br>
Generates a four-section HTML B2B sales proposal from client, industry, pain point, budget, and product inputs using bundled case and methodology data. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[william202404](https://clawhub.ai/user/william202404) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales and presales teams use this skill to turn customer details into a browser-ready HTML proposal with pain point alignment, solution mapping, implementation planning, ROI analysis, and matched case references. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated proposals may include case claims or ROI estimates that are inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific customer. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated proposals before external use and verify bundled case references, ROI figures, and customer-specific claims. <br>
Risk: Dependency drift could change local CLI behavior over time. <br>
Mitigation: Use lockfile-based installs when running the Node package. <br>
