## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to search for extensions services, check provider availability, and create bookings through Lokuli's booking service. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The booking flow may share customer name, email, phone, and appointment details with Lokuli's external booking service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm appointment details and user consent before creating a booking or sharing contact information. <br>
Risk: Search and availability results depend on Lokuli's external booking service availability and data accuracy. <br>
Mitigation: Verify provider, service, and time slot details before completing a booking. <br>
