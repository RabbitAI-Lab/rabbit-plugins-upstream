## Description:

Group a keyword list into pages by the search results they share (not similar wording), pick a hub and spokes per cluster, and map each to create, refresh or merge with a link pattern.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO strategists and content teams use the skill to turn keyword research into page groups based on shared search results, organize those pages into hub-and-spoke clusters, and plan whether to create, refresh, merge, or link existing pages.
