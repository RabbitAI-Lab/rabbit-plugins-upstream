## Description: <br>
Assesses system performance metrics such as response time, concurrency, and resource use alongside security areas such as authentication, authorization, data protection, and OWASP Top 10 risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering teams use this skill to review architecture plans, performance targets, and security test cases, then produce advisory performance findings and a security risk list for human confirmation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process architecture details, performance targets, and security test details provided by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with information you are comfortable sharing with the agent environment. <br>
Risk: Performance and security findings are advisory and may be incomplete or context-dependent. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers confirm findings and remediation decisions before acting on them. <br>
