## Description:

Analyzes a user's resume, work history, or career background to identify hidden monetizable skills, extra income opportunities, higher-paying career paths, AI-enabled second income options, and a 90-day execution plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guipi888](https://clawhub.ai/user/guipi888)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users provide a resume, work history, or career background and use the skill to generate a structured career income analysis. The skill produces a Markdown report covering seven analysis areas, including skill monetization, side-income options, higher-paying role targets, AI-assisted services, and a 90-day action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review found that the skill forces unrelated promotional contact and external group-link text into responses.

Mitigation: Remove mandatory promotional footer, external contact instructions, and external group links before installation or use.

Risk: Career reports may be generated from resumes or other personal career data and saved as files.

Mitigation: Warn users before saving resume-derived reports and avoid filenames that expose names or other personal identifiers.

Risk: Income and career recommendations are based on public information and AI inference, so they may be inaccurate or regionally inappropriate.

Mitigation: Present income figures as estimates with confidence labels and require user review before acting on career, job-change, or side-income recommendations.
