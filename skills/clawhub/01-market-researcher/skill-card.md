## Description:

Market Researcher helps agents perform pre-launch market research and user validation, including industry scans, market-size estimation, pain-point validation, demand prioritization, and go/no-go recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product development teams use this skill before product initiation to structure market research, validate target users, prioritize demand, and reach a Go/No-Go recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market-size or growth estimates may be misleading if they are uncited or drawn from weak sources.

Mitigation: Require cited sources for market-size data and review those sources before relying on the report.

Risk: User personas, pain scores, or demand priorities may overstate certainty when no user research data is supplied.

Mitigation: Provide existing research when available and require evidence-backed scoring for personas and demand prioritization.

Risk: The report may be less useful if the product, market, geography, or language are underspecified.

Mitigation: Specify the product, target market, geographic scope, and preferred report language before invoking the skill.
