## Description:

Provides a decision-support checklist of suggested non-clinical studies for medical devices using device class, type, and contact information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Medical-device developers and compliance teams use this China-focused checklist to identify suggested non-clinical studies and items to verify for a device. Results require human review and are not regulatory, legal, or medical determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (guidance addresses Chinese medical-device requirements)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Study recommendations may be incomplete or outdated for a particular device.

Mitigation: Verify requirements against current official GB/YY/NMPA sources and have qualified people review conclusions before making clinical or regulatory decisions.

Risk: Code and documentation have inconsistent licensing statements.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable license for each component with the publisher before reuse or redistribution.
