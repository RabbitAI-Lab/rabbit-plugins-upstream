## Description:

Finds content gaps and opportunities from Google SERP analysis using apidojo's Google Search scraper on Apify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO strategists, content marketers, and blog editors use this skill to analyze Google search results, identify weak or underserved topics, and prioritize content briefs that can address those gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keywords or Google search URLs submitted for analysis are sent to Apify.

Mitigation: Use scoped APIFY_TOKEN credentials where possible and avoid analyzing sensitive private business terms unless that data can be shared with Apify.
