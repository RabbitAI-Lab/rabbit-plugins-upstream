Mitigation: Treat the guidance as a starting point and verify requirements with official agencies, platform policies, or qualified advisors before acting. <br>
Risk: Users may need accounts, credentials, or payment details when registering domains, hosting, advertising, analytics, or other services. <br>
Mitigation: Enter credentials and payment information only on official service websites and review service terms before connecting third-party tools. <br>
