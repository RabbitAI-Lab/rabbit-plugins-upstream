## Description:

Analyzes child-monitoring videos for potentially hazardous behavior and provides structured findings, alerts, and report links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Guardians and childcare staff can use this skill to review monitoring footage for potentially hazardous actions and consult analysis reports or historical results. Alerts are an aid to supervision and should be checked promptly by a responsible adult.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Children's images or videos and identity-linked requests are sent to external services.

Mitigation: Review the service's consent, retention, deletion, and endpoint practices before using real children's or private household footage.

Risk: Local default identity and token data may be created and reused in the workspace.

Mitigation: Review account creation and token storage practices before installing or using the skill.

Risk: A missed or incorrect alert could delay a safety response.

Mitigation: Treat alerts as supervision aids and have a responsible adult verify suspected hazards immediately.
