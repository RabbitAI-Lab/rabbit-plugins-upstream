## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill for loosely related school, admissions, or international department queries.

Mitigation: Narrow activation to explicit mentions of 科德高中, 浦江县科德高级中学融合部, or Adcote School Zhezhong Campus.

Risk: Policy, fee, admissions, scholarship, and university application details can become outdated or be misread as commitments.

Mitigation: Confirm current details against official school notices and university requirements before sharing them with families or applicants.

Risk: Generated promotional or consultation copy could overpromise outcomes if reviewed carelessly.

Mitigation: Retain the artifact's stated boundaries against guaranteed admissions, fixed costs, scholarship promises, and internal management or privacy disclosures.
