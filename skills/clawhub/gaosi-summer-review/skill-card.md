## Description: <br>
Builds structured summer review plans for the 高思导引 (Gaosi Guide) competition-math series for grades 4-6, using a fixed topic knowledge tree, daily check-in tables, and optional PDF export. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shuolongbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/shuolongbj) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, parents, tutors, and education-planning users use this skill to create a Chinese-language summer review schedule for the 高思导引 grades 4-6 competition-math series, including topic sequencing, red/yellow/blue tier guidance, and quantifiable daily and weekly check-in tables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is specialized for Chinese-language 高思导引 materials and may not fit other curricula or grade ranges. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants the 高思导引 grades 4-6 summer review workflow before relying on the generated plan. <br>
Risk: Broad study-planning phrases may activate the skill outside its intended scope. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for 高思导引 summer review, knowledge-tree review schedules, or related check-in sheets; choose a more general planning workflow for unrelated study plans. <br>
Risk: PDF export runs a local Chromium-family browser on an HTML file selected by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Run PDF export only on trusted local HTML files and review generated files before printing or sharing. <br>
