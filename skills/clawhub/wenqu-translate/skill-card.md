## Description: <br>
将 README、论文、源码注释、文档和英文文章等材料翻译成自然中文，供中文内容创作与阅读使用。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gogoingai](https://clawhub.ai/user/gogoingai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, technical writers, and Chinese-language content creators use this skill to translate single-article English materials such as READMEs, papers, source comments, documentation, and technical articles into natural Chinese while preserving meaning and formatting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Reusable terminology or translation preferences may be written to project-local wenqu-skills/.../preferences.md files. <br>
Mitigation: Review preference files before committing or sharing a repository, and avoid storing private or project-sensitive translation preferences. <br>
Risk: The skill is scoped to single-article translation and may refuse very long documents, multiple documents, or book-length translation work. <br>
Mitigation: Use a dedicated long-document translation workflow for oversized or multi-document inputs. <br>
