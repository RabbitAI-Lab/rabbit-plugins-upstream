## Description: <br>
将 README、论文、源码注释、文档和英文文章等材料翻译成自然中文，供中文内容创作与阅读使用。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gogoingai](https://clawhub.ai/user/gogoingai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, developers, and technical readers use this skill to translate English README files, papers, source comments, documentation, and articles into natural Chinese while preserving meaning and Markdown structure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use project-local translation preferences and a related review skill, which can affect translation choices. <br>
Mitigation: Review the project-local preferences file and the wenqu-review reference before use when persistent translation preferences or cross-skill review are not desired. <br>
