## Description: <br>
ToB competitor-sniping assistant that takes a competitor name, industry, and customer concern, then produces a differentiated comparison and sales talking points. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[william202404](https://clawhub.ai/user/william202404) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales and presales teams use this skill to draft concise competitor-positioning cards for ToB customer conversations. It helps frame competitor strengths, differentiators, rebuttal questions, and practical next steps for price, feature, and brand-trust objections. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated customer examples, response-time claims, and industry assertions may be unverified templates rather than auditable project records. <br>
Mitigation: Review and rewrite generated cards before customer use, and replace template claims with verified internal evidence or remove them. <br>
