## Description: <br>
Meituan Bike (formerly Mobike) assistant for rental flow, pricing structure, ride-pass cost analysis, and parking-fee pitfalls for China's bike-sharing service. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to explain Meituan Bike rental steps, compare ride-pass value against expected usage, troubleshoot common billing issues, and plan short last-mile trips. It emphasizes checking current city-specific prices, parking rules, and helmet requirements before acting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prices, parking rules, and helmet requirements can vary by city and change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current details in the Meituan app and local regulations before relying on the guidance. <br>
Risk: Billing or parking-fee explanations may be incomplete without the user's current app bill and parking-zone context. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for triage, then confirm disputed charges through Meituan in-app customer support. <br>
