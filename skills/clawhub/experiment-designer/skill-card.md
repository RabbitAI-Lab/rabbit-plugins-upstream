## Description: <br>
Design statistically rigorous A/B tests and interpret experiment results. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, growth teams, analysts, and engineers use this skill to design A/B tests with clear hypotheses, primary metrics, sample size estimates, run time estimates, pre-defined success criteria, and experiment risk flags. They can also use it to interpret completed experiment results and make Ship, Iterate, Kill, or Follow-up recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for product metrics and experiment results that could include sensitive business data. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential metrics unless appropriate for the agent environment and review outputs against internal data handling policies. <br>
Risk: Statistical guidance can affect launch or rollback decisions if inputs are incomplete or misinterpreted. <br>
Mitigation: Review important experiment decisions against the organization's experimentation standards before acting. <br>
