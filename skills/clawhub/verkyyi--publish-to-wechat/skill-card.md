## Description:

Publishes user-approved content as a shareable LivePage link that opens in WeChat, supports updates at the same link, and provides reader engagement and feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[verkyyi](https://clawhub.ai/user/verkyyi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams publish reports, proposals, guides, and presentations as WeChat-friendly pages, then share updates and review readership or feedback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing uploads user content to a third-party hosting service.

Mitigation: Confirm the material and obtain user approval before uploading; publish only content suitable for that service.

Risk: Page visibility, reader tracking, forms, and payment details may expose information or collect sensitive responses.

Mitigation: Confirm access settings, tracking, form fields, and any payment QR details before sharing; use public access only when explicitly requested.
