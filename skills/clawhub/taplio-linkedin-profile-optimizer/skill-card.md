## Description:

Audits a LinkedIn profile and returns prioritized positioning fixes with rewritten copy, using Taplio account settings and peer inspiration through the Taplio MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External LinkedIn users can use the skill to assess their profile positioning and prioritize improvements to their headline, banner, About, Featured, and Experience sections. It requires Taplio MCP access and user-provided profile content; the user reviews and applies suggested edits themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account preferences and niche keywords are shared through Taplio lookups.

Mitigation: Review connector permissions and avoid confidential search terms.

Risk: Suggested profile changes may not reflect the user's intent or current profile details.

Mitigation: Review all recommendations and apply edits manually.
