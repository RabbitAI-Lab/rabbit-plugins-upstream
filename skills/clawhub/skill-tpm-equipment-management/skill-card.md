## Description: <br>
Equipment-management teams use this TPM skill to produce Markdown deliverables for six-loss analysis, autonomous maintenance planning, planned maintenance schedules, OPL one-point lessons, inspection standards, and lightweight TPM pillar guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and operational equipment teams use this skill to turn TPM maintenance data, logs, descriptions, or equipment photos into structured Markdown analysis and maintenance artifacts. It is intended for module-level TPM work, not full TPM deployment decisions or standalone OEE diagnosis. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Operational equipment data, maintenance records, MES/Excel exports, and equipment photos may contain sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive operational details before sharing them with the agent or publishing generated Markdown artifacts. <br>
Risk: Generated TPM analysis can be incomplete when equipment parameters, production line context, cycle-time standards, or customer requirements are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Keep missing enterprise-specific fields marked for user completion and have qualified maintenance or operations staff review outputs before use. <br>
