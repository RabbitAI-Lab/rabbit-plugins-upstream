## Description:

Yandun Risk Assessment generates demonstration five-dimensional stock risk scores, calculated risk indicators, and risk level labels.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can run this skill as a Chinese-language educational demo for simplified stock risk scoring across fundamental, technical, sentiment, liquidity, and tail-risk dimensions. The output should not be treated as real financial risk analysis or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the demonstration scores for real financial risk analysis or investment advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as educational demo scoring only and require real financial data, qualified review, and appropriate controls before any investment or risk decision.

Risk: Built-in simplified scoring rules may produce incomplete or misleading risk labels for actual securities.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for course or demonstration workflows and disclose that results come from simplified deterministic rules rather than live market, financial, or news data.
