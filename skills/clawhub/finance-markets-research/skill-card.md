## Description:

Researches stocks, crypto, SEC filings, insider and congressional trading, and private market profiles through Crawlora, returning normalized JSON.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and analysts use this skill to retrieve public market, regulatory filing, disclosure, crypto, and private market data as normalized JSON for research questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crawlora receives research queries and API requests consume credits.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key, avoid sending confidential information, and review Crawlora's privacy and billing terms.
