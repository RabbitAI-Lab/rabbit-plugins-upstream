## Description:

R&D IP Accelerator helps innovation teams run six patent and IP workflows: litigation intelligence, competitor monitoring, FTO analysis, solution exploration, novelty search, and technical disclosure drafting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External innovation teams, patent practitioners, and R&D organizations use this skill to coordinate IP awareness, competitive intelligence, FTO review, invention exploration, novelty search, and technical disclosure generation around company or technology inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can send company, product, and invention details to PatSnap/MCP and web-search workflows.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact module, searches, and external services before use, and avoid submitting confidential invention details unless the environment and service terms are approved.

Risk: Broad prompts can trigger wide-ranging searches and multiple generated reports without enough user confirmation.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation of the selected module and expected files before full-chain company analysis or report generation.
