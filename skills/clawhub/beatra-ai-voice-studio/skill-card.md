## Description:

Use Beatra AI Voice Studio as an AI voice generator, text-to-speech workspace, and AI voiceover generator. Choose from the current voice library, turn scripts into ready-to-edit AI narration and voiceover, or create and reuse a custom brand voice through voice cloning. It supports short-video voiceover, script-to-voiceover, course narration, ordered audiobook narration, supplied multilingual text to speech, Cantonese text to speech, and recurring brand audio, with current price estimates, clear output planning, and delivery organized by chapter, language, and use case.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account authorization includes access beyond voice generation, including image, video, and music capabilities.

Mitigation: Review and accept the broader account permissions only if they are appropriate; this release does not offer voice-only authorization.

Risk: Scripts and voice samples are submitted to Beatra, and voice cloning uses personal voice material.

Mitigation: Submit only content intended for the service, and upload a sample for cloning only when it is your voice or the speaker has explicitly authorized cloning.

Risk: Generation and voice cloning consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Review the current estimate and production details, then confirm before each paid request.

Risk: First-use registration reports installation and coarse runtime information automatically, with no documented opt-out.

Mitigation: Review the reported data and privacy implications before connecting; do not use the skill if automatic registration is unacceptable.
