## Description:

Reviews claims in a Korean patent application or a foreign or PCT application intended for Korea, producing evidence-backed assessments of Korean claim compliance, claim architecture, scope strategy, drafting and translation quality, examination risk, novelty or inventive-step risk, amendment options, and filing readiness as structured English or HTML reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Patent practitioners, IP teams, and agent users use this skill to perform a structured pre-review of Korean patent claim sets before counsel review, filing decisions, amendments, or prior-art analysis. It helps organize claim structure, legal and formal compliance issues, translation concerns, examination risk, prior-art search requirements, amendment options, and filing readiness without presenting itself as Korean legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential or pre-publication patent materials may be shared with the agent or configured patent-search connectors.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an environment with appropriate access controls and approved connector handling before submitting sensitive invention materials.

Risk: Users may mistake pre-review findings for Korean legal advice or final patentability, validity, infringement, filing, or deadline conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as evidence-backed review findings and require qualified Korean patent counsel review before legal, filing, amendment, validity, infringement, or procedural decisions.

Risk: Novelty, inventive-step, procedural, or official-law conclusions may be incomplete if current Korean sources, procedural posture, or patent-search connectors are unavailable.

Mitigation: Verify current official Korean materials and use real patent-search evidence with locators; mark unsupported prior-art or procedural conclusions as not assessable.
