## Description:

Helps rank work beyond available capacity using RICE, ICE, MoSCoW, impact-effort, or Eisenhower methods, then draws a clear cut line.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams and individuals use this skill to prioritize backlogs, roadmaps, and MVP scope against a goal, deadline, or available capacity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Backlog, ticket, usage, or customer data shared for scoring may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Review and minimize sensitive material before sharing it with the agent.

Risk: Uncertain inputs can produce misleading rankings or cut lines.

Mitigation: Mark estimates, explain score bases, and sanity-check priorities with the team.
