## Description:

Reviews personal rental-property sell-or-hold decisions against stock or fixed-income alternatives using owner-supplied figures, sourced assumptions, and Canadian tax considerations where applicable.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Rental-property owners use the skill to compare holding, selling, and investing proceeds by organizing sourced property facts, modeling cash flow and three-year scenarios, and identifying tax, concentration, and privacy questions for qualified advisers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; Canadian tax considerations apply when the owner is a Canadian resident.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing personal financial details in a shared document could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use placeholders in shared files, provide only necessary figures, and keep the resulting personal analysis private.

Risk: Incorrect calculations, market assumptions, or tax interpretations could influence a high-impact financial decision.

Mitigation: Check figures and assumptions against reliable sources and confirm calculations and tax treatment with qualified advisers before acting.
