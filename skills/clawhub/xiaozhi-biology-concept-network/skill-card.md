## Description:

Guides Chinese-language secondary-school students to connect biology concepts in layered, explainable concept maps rather than memorize isolated facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use the skill to organize middle- and high-school biology topics into linked concept maps, compare commonly confused concepts, and explain each connection in their own words.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, minor-consent requirements, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learner profiles, cross-skill sharing, or reminders could expose a minor's learning information without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep these features opt-in, obtain student or guardian permission as appropriate, and honor pause, sharing, and deletion controls.

Risk: Curriculum, minor-consent rules, and crisis-contact defaults may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize teaching content, consent practices, and support contacts for the learner's location before deployment.
