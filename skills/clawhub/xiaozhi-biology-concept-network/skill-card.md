## Description:

引导初高中学生按层级梳理生物概念，建立带连接词的概念网络，并辨析容易混淆的概念关系。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use guided questions to organize biology concepts and explain relationships between them. Teachers can use the text concept maps and comparisons to support study and review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions, consent rules, and emergency contacts may not suit other countries or school systems.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis-referral channels before use outside mainland China.

Risk: A student's learning profile may be retained or shared beyond the current session.

Mitigation: Keep session-only use as the default; require explicit consent for profile memory and cross-skill sharing, and honor view, correction, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Biology lessons about health could be mistaken for individual medical guidance.

Mitigation: Limit responses to textbook concepts; do not diagnose or recommend treatment, and refer personal health concerns to a trusted adult and clinician.
