## Description:

Helps Chinese-language secondary-school students connect biology concepts into layered, explainable concept maps instead of memorizing isolated facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to organize junior- and senior-high biology concepts into linked explanations, clarify commonly confused relationships, and guide students to construct their own concept maps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis-help channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum and consent rules and replace crisis-help channels before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Student profiles, cross-skill sharing, and reminders can expose or retain minors' learning data.

Mitigation: Keep session-only use as the default; enable each data-sharing or reminder feature only with appropriate consent and review its settings.
