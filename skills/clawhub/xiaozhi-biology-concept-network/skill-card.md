## Description:

Helps students connect biology concepts in a layered network and understand commonly confused relationships through structured, text-based concept mapping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this tutoring skill to organize biology topics, connect concepts through core life-science themes, and clarify commonly confused relationships. It guides students to build the links themselves and provides text-based concept maps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records, sharing, and reminders use companion integrations whose storage and delivery behavior was not included in this review.

Mitigation: Keep tutoring session-only by default; enable optional features only with consent and review the companion skills first.
