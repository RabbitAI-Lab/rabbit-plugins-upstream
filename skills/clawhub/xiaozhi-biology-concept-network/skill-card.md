## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking secondary-school students to build biology concept networks by linking major ideas, supporting concepts, and often-confused relationships.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use guided prompts to organize biology concepts into a hierarchy and explain connections in their own words. With consent, their progress can inform future study and reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's biology learning profile or reminders may persist or be shared across skills without their understanding.

Mitigation: Keep study session-only by default; confirm the student or guardian understands and agrees to saving, sharing, correction, export, deletion, and reminders before enabling them.

Risk: Biology questions about a student's health may be mistaken for requests for medical advice.

Mitigation: Limit explanations to textbook concepts; do not diagnose or recommend treatment, and direct personal health concerns to a parent or clinician.
