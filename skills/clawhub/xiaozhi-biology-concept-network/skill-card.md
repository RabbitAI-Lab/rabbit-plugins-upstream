## Description:

引导初高中学生逐步连接生物学概念，理清知识层级及易混淆的概念关系。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生在学习生物时，用循序提问和文字概念图梳理大概念、关键关系及常见混淆点。经同意后可参考学习档案并记录掌握进度。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；其他地区须先本地化课程内容、求助渠道和未成年人数据同意规则

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning-profile sharing and reminders may expose a minor's study information.

Mitigation: Review memory, sharing and reminder settings; keep consent off for session-only tutoring and confirm each profile update.

Risk: Biology questions may involve personal health rather than school concepts.

Mitigation: Keep explanations grounded in coursework; do not diagnose or recommend treatment and direct personal health concerns to a clinician.
