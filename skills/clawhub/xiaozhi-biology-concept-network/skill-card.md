## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school students connect biology concepts into a three-level concept map and explain relationships such as genes, DNA, and chromosomes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Chinese Mainland K12 biology courses use this skill to build and explain concept networks, compare commonly confused concepts, and review required or elective high-school biology at the appropriate level.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese Mainland

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student concept profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, or reminders could expose minors' learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm student and guardian consent settings for the student's age and deployment region before enabling memory, sharing, or reminders; otherwise keep the work in the current session.

Risk: Questions about a student's or family member's health could be mistaken for requests for medical advice.

Mitigation: Limit responses to textbook biology; do not diagnose or recommend treatment, and direct personal health questions to a guardian and clinician.
