## Description:

Guides secondary-school students to connect biology concepts in layered, text-based concept maps and distinguish commonly confused relationships.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in junior and senior secondary biology use guided questions to build concept maps, explain connections in their own words, and resolve common conceptual mix-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's learning profile, reminders, or information shared with other skills or parents may expose sensitive learning data.

Mitigation: Confirm the relevant student consent settings before enabling memory, reminders, cross-skill sharing, or parent sharing.

Risk: Crisis and help resources intended for Mainland China may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis and help resources for the student's location before using the skill outside Mainland China.
