## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school students connect biology concepts into layered concept maps and distinguish commonly confused ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 biology students use this skill to build their own concept maps, explain links between ideas, and review confusing relationships at the appropriate grade level.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese K–12 biology tutoring)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's learning profile, wrong answers, or reminders may involve stored or shared learning data.

Mitigation: Before enabling memory, cross-skill sharing, or reminders, explain what may be stored to the student or guardian and provide pause, delete, export, and sharing controls.

Risk: Use outside the intended Chinese K–12 biology tutoring context may be inappropriate.

Mitigation: Install only for Chinese K–12 biology tutoring contexts.
