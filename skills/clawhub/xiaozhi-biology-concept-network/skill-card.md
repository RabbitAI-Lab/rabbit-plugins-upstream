## Description:

帮助初中和高中学生搭建生物概念层级，梳理概念之间的联系，并用结构与功能、物质与能量等生命观念解释容易混淆的内容。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初中和高中学生在整理生物章节或比较易混概念时，可用它建立概念层级、补上带连接词的关系线，并用自己的话解释概念网络。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A learner may rely on an inaccurate or overgeneralized biology explanation.

Mitigation: Keep explanations within the curriculum references, ask the learner to explain the relationship in their own words, and defer to the student's textbook where wording differs.

Risk: A student may ask for personal health advice or disclose a crisis or safety concern during a learning conversation.

Mitigation: Do not diagnose or recommend treatment; pause the learning flow and follow the referral steps in the health-boundary and crisis protocols, including contacting a trusted adult or local emergency support when needed.

Risk: Learner profile information could be read or shared beyond the student's expectations.

Mitigation: Keep session-only use as the default, require the stated consent before reading profile data, confirm each write, and honor pause, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: Younger students may use the skill without appropriate adult support.

Mitigation: Require a parent or teacher to be present when the learner is in elementary school.
