## Description: <br>
Generates a concise Chinese-first daily DXC and client business briefing from company sites, DXC news, client updates, policies, bids, competitors, and partnership opportunities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[rozley](https://clawhub.ai/user/rozley) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business development, sales, and operations users use this skill to monitor recent DXC, domestic company, client, policy, bid, and competitor updates and turn them into a short briefing with business opportunities and source links. It supports Chinese requests by default and can answer follow-up requests for specific customers, topics, time windows, or briefing depth. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The briefing may include stale links, incorrect dates, or business recommendations that should not be acted on without verification. <br>
Mitigation: Verify original links, dates, and recommendations before business action, and phrase requests with the intended DXC, client, topic, and time-window scope. <br>
