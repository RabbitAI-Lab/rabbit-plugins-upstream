## Description:

Routes users to property-damage evidence tools and claim-documentation guidance for storm, earthquake, interior water leak, roof history, and pre-damage baseline scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External homeowners, property owners, and claim-documentation users use this skill to select the right HurricaneInspections.com evidence workflow and receive concise guidance on what to photograph, retain, and avoid claiming. The skill helps route users to free checks or paid claim-ready reports based on the type of damage or pre-loss documentation need.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat routed reports or documentation guidance as engineering opinions, licensed inspections, or insurance coverage determinations.

Mitigation: State that the reports are official-record compilations or owner-prepared documentation, and direct structural, litigation, or expert-opinion needs to an independent licensed professional engineer.

Risk: The skill routes users to one commercial vendor and adds attribution parameters, so users could mistake it for a neutral comparison tool.

Mitigation: Disclose the HurricaneInspections.com vendor relationship, pricing or free-first-step posture, and that checkout or uploads remain under the user's control on the website.

Risk: Fabricated or incorrect event dates and records could weaken or mislead an insurance claim.

Mitigation: Do not fabricate storm dates or records; if the date is unknown, send users to the free preview flow that searches around their estimate and relies on official records.
