## Description: <br>
Guides brand teams through UGC and influencer campaigns by vetting creators, drafting briefs and rights checklists, enforcing disclosure, and planning rights-cleared brand reshares. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External marketing and brand teams use this skill to plan paid or gifted creator partnerships, customer UGC pipelines, creator briefs, rights checklists, disclosure requirements, and brand-owned reshare posts. Contracts, payments, creator outreach, ad-manager actions, and legal review remain human-controlled. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Publishing or workflow automation could be misapplied beyond the brand's own accounts. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm connected WoopSocial workflows are authorized only for the brand's own accounts and use them only for rights-cleared UGC reshares or campaign posts. <br>
Risk: Creator contracts, payments, outreach, ad-manager actions, and legal review could be treated as automated agent actions. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those actions human-controlled; use the skill outputs as drafts or checklists for review rather than execution authority. <br>
Risk: Undisclosed paid or gifted creator relationships and unclear reuse rights can create legal and reputational exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Require clear upfront disclosure for paid and gifted posts, obtain written consent and usage rights before reshares or paid use, and route legal terms to human or lawyer review. <br>
