## Description: <br>
Guides brand-to-creator UGC and influencer campaigns by helping source and vet creators, draft creator briefs and rights checklists, enforce paid and gifted disclosure rules, and plan rights-cleared brand posts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, agencies, and brand operators use this skill to plan creator partnerships, customer UGC pipelines, creator briefs, rights checklists, outreach copy, disclosure-safe workflows, and rights-cleared brand reshare posts. Contracts, payment, native ad controls, creator account posting, and legal signoff remain human or platform-native responsibilities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat rights or contract checklists as legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep rights terms as planning checklists and require human or lawyer review before signing, licensing, whitelisting, or paid reuse. <br>
Risk: Paid, gifted, affiliate, employee, whitelisted, or ad-use content could be posted without clear disclosure. <br>
Mitigation: Require upfront disclosure and native platform labels where applicable; decline and correct requests to skip, bury, or euphemize sponsorship disclosure. <br>
Risk: Brands may assume a tag, creator post, or invoice grants reuse rights. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit written consent and usage rights, including likeness and channel/duration scope, before organic resharing, paid ads, whitelisting, website, email, or packaging use. <br>
Risk: Creator performance claims may be inflated or based on fake engagement. <br>
Mitigation: Use engagement quality checks before selection and report only tracked links, unique codes, native analytics, and clearly qualified soft metrics. <br>
