## Description: <br>
Plans Bangkok trips, relocations, and nomad stays: neighborhoods, visas, costs, transport, street food, and Thai law. Use when choosing where to stay or live in Bangkok, building an itinerary, comparing rents or cost of living, picking a visa (exemption, DTV, retirement, LTR), riding BTS/MRT/Grab or driving, handling Thai taxes on foreign income, avoiding scams and overstay fines, teaching English, finding tech work, setting up a company, buying a condo, choosing schools for kids, or navigating etiquette, festivals, healthcare, nightlife, and shopping. Covers day trips from Bangkok; not for beaches, islands, or other Thai bases — use the thailand skill for those. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Risk: Users may rely on volatile visa, employment, rental, childcare, alcohol, tax, or other legal-risk guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official Thai sources or a qualified local professional before spending money, signing contracts, filing taxes, applying for visas, or making legal commitments. <br>
Risk: Scanner evidence says some passages normalize illegal or deceptive real-world conduct. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and prefer lawful, transparent alternatives when giving safety, business, employment, nightlife, or immigration advice. <br>
