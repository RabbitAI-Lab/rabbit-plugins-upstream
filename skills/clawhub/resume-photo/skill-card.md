## Description:

Turns a supplied casual portrait into a professional resume or ID-style headshot with a plain background, business attire, and optional standard-size cropping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caoling7878-arch](https://clawhub.ai/user/caoling7878-arch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and other users can turn their own portrait into a professional headshot for resumes, applications, or profile use, with optional ID-photo dimensions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A personal portrait and editing prompt may be sent to an external image provider.

Mitigation: Process only photos the user has permission to use; check the selected provider and its data-handling terms before submitting sensitive images.

Risk: Generated portraits may be saved to a local Desktop folder.

Mitigation: Confirm the output location and protect or delete saved images as appropriate.
