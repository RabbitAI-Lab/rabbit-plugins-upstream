## Description:

Generate AI videos, images, and speech, create custom-LoRA and e-commerce product photography, and manage generation jobs with the Phosor AI platform.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and creative teams use this skill to submit, monitor, and retrieve Phosor AI jobs for generating or editing media, including e-commerce product and model photography.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Verified network safeguards have flaws that could expose your API key.

Mitigation: Prefer a corrected client that restricts authenticated redirects and validates exact localhost hosts. Use a limited API key, upload only material you intend to share with Phosor, and confirm generation costs. Keeping the default HTTPS endpoint avoids the hostname bypass but does not resolve redirect handling.
