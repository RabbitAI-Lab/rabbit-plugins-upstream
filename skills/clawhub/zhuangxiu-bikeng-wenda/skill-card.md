## Description:

Answers Chinese-language home-renovation questions about contractors, contracts, construction, materials, layouts, furnishings, and common pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

First-time homeowners and other renovation customers use this skill to ask practical questions and identify common pitfalls across planning, contractor selection, building work, and purchases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General renovation advice may be unsafe for structural, electrical, or gas work.

Mitigation: Have qualified local professionals inspect and confirm safety before any work proceeds.

Risk: Contract, cost, and building-code guidance may not fit local requirements or a specific project.

Mitigation: Verify legal, financial, and local-code decisions with qualified local professionals.
