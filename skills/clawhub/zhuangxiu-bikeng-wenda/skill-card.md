## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Renovation and home-buying advice may not reflect local building codes, contractor terms, or product safety details. <br>
Mitigation: Verify electrical, waterproofing, structural, chemical finishing, and major purchase decisions against local requirements, contracts, and product documentation before acting. <br>
Risk: Broad triggers may route adjacent home-improvement questions to the skill even when the answer is outside the supplied reference files. <br>
Mitigation: Keep answers grounded in the bundled reference files and state limits when a question falls outside those files. <br>
