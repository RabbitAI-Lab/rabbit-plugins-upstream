## Description:

Turn one HR or founder portrait and a job brief into one talking-avatar hiring video per open role. This hiring avatar studio and recruitment avatar workflow can clone or pick a voice, then produce a hiring talking head clip that walks through the role, requirements, and next step. Use it for recruiting video, job posting video, job opening presenter clips, and a hiring video studio that keeps each new role on camera.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

HR teams and founders use this skill to create one role-specific talking-avatar hiring video from an authorized portrait and an approved speech track or job brief. The workflow guides voice selection or cloning, narration, video generation, review, and delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow requires approval, but its mandatory shared authorization grants paid services beyond this hiring-video task.

Mitigation: Review the full Beatra permissions before connecting, and approve each paid stage only after reviewing its cost and inputs.

Risk: Portraits or voice recordings may be used without the subject's permission.

Mitigation: Confirm authorization for both likeness and voice before uploading or generating media.

Risk: Installation metadata is reported automatically, and a shared device authorization can remain active after use.

Mitigation: Review the installation reporting details and revoke the device in the Beatra Console when its shared access is no longer needed.
