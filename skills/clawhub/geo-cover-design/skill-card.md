## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cover assets may be uploaded to aimeGeo, and an authorized publishing workflow can make article content public.

Mitigation: Specify whether the task covers local files, an aimeGeo draft update, or authorized publishing; use material approved for the service, identify the intended article and account, and verify the resulting state.
