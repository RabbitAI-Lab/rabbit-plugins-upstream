## Description:

Chinese fortune telling grounded in classical sources, with chart calculations performed by a bundled rule engine and interpreted with an explicitly stated school.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking Chinese metaphysics chart calculations and interpretive readings can use this skill for BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, divination, compatibility, and auspicious-date questions. It is not intended for medical, legal, or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
