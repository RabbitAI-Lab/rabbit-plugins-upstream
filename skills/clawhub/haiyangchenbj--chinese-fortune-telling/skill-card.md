## Description:

This skill computes Chinese metaphysical charts and provides school-declared interpretations grounded in classical source texts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking Chinese metaphysical readings use this skill to calculate and interpret BaZi and other chart systems, single-question divinations, compatibility, and auspicious dates, with an identified interpretive school and cited sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Conflicting guidance about saving sensitive birth details may lead to unexpected retention or disclosure.

Mitigation: Before sharing birth details, review where reports or profiles are saved; provide only information the user has consented to share, and remove stored files when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Calculation and validation inconsistencies may produce misleading chart results or recommendations.

Mitigation: Treat readings as interpretive guidance and verify inputs and material chart results against the cited method sources.

Risk: The security analysis was partial, so some content was not assessed.

Mitigation: Review file handling and relevant skill content before use, and treat the scan verdict as a review warning rather than a finding of malicious authorship.
