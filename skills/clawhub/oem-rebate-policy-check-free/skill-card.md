## Use Case:

财务、出纳和经销商运营人员 use this skill during monthly reconciliation or inventory close to check OEM rebate and commercial-policy spreadsheet rows, totals, duplicates, blanks, and negative amounts. The free release reports only the included local checks and cites source rows for each finding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may assume the free release performed advanced rebate-policy checks that are intentionally omitted.

Mitigation: Review the listed omitted checks before relying on results, and use the full release or manual review for those questions.

Risk: Insufficient or unrecognized spreadsheet material can produce no conclusion rather than a clean result.

Mitigation: Provide the rebate table with the required headers and treat missing-input guidance as a request for more source material.

Risk: The checker verifies table arithmetic and consistency, not legal, tax, accounting, or contract interpretation.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers handle policy interpretation, tax treatment, period allocation, and external system reconciliation.
