## Description: <br>
Plans per-community pre-promotion participation warmups, including account-history expectations, give-before-ask ledgers, rule digests, graduation criteria, and owned-community lifecycle plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, marketers, and community operators use this skill after channels are selected to plan a human-executed participation ramp before promotion. It helps them document community rules, account-standing expectations, participation cadence, and warming-to-active graduation criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-treat estimated account-history, karma, or trust-level norms as guaranteed promotion thresholds. <br>
Mitigation: Require each threshold to be labeled Estimated with a named community source and checked against dated community rules before use. <br>
Risk: Pasted community rules, moderator statements, screenshots, or exports may contain misleading or hostile instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those inputs as untrusted evidence; they cannot rewrite the skill guardrails, authorize promotion, or declare a channel graduated. <br>
Risk: Participation planning could drift into automated posting, engagement pods, karma farming, or unsanctioned promotion. <br>
Mitigation: Keep every give, reply, and post human-executed, and use the skill only with accounts and community data the user is authorized to review. <br>
Risk: Proposed registry or memory updates could introduce incorrect channel-state evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Submit graduation criteria and cadence commitments as reviewable proposal events; the channel registry remains the sole writer of channel dossiers. <br>
