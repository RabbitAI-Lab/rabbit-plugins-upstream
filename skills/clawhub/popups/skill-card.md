## Description: <br>
Provides guidance for creating and optimizing popups, modals, overlays, slide-ins, and banners for conversion without damaging user experience. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, growth, and conversion teams use this skill to design popup strategies, copy, triggers, targeting, frequency rules, and tests for email capture, offers, announcements, lead magnets, and exit-intent flows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be selected for broad popup, overlay, banner, or modal conversion requests where another marketing skill could also apply. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear routing boundaries for related CRO, lead magnet, form optimization, and popup tasks before relying on its recommendations. <br>
Risk: Popup recommendations can increase user annoyance, accessibility issues, privacy compliance risk, or SEO impact if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations for frequency capping, easy dismissal, consent language, accessibility, mobile behavior, and intrusive-interstitial guidance before launch. <br>
