## Description:

A quick-reference handbook for global AI governance that helps agents explain major international mechanisms, WAICO, UN AI governance activity, China initiative timelines, AI safety institutions, major-economy strategies, Global South capacity building, and institutionalization trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Policy researchers, global-market strategy teams, industry observers, and governance-focused agents use this skill to summarize the global AI governance landscape and retrieve structured local reference answers about organizations, initiatives, regional strategies, timelines, and trends.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional CLI and verification scripts execute local Python code.

Mitigation: Review the package before execution and run only from a trusted, unchanged release artifact.

Risk: AI governance facts and policy status can change quickly.

Mitigation: Verify important policy claims against current official sources before operational, legal, or strategic use.
