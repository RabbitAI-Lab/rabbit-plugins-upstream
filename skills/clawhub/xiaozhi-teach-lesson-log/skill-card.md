## Description:

帮助独立教师将课后观察整理为可预览的结构化教学记录、学习轨迹和下节课衔接建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent teachers use this Chinese-language skill to document lessons, review topic-level mastery and learning progress, prepare next-lesson priorities, and stage lesson-unit entries for confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localization of curricula, safeguarding contacts, and minor-data consent practices.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson histories may expose sensitive information about minors.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, check consent settings, review entries before saving, and use pause, delete, or export controls as needed.

Risk: Incorrect lesson-unit entries could affect the teacher's accounting.

Mitigation: Keep unit entries pending until the teacher verifies and confirms them; do not change balances without confirmation.

Risk: Unverified classroom observations could be mistaken for facts in parent-facing material.

Mitigation: Record observable behavior rather than inferred traits, respect parent-sharing consent, and leave any parent summary as a teacher-reviewed internal draft.
