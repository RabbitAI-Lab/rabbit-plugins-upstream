## Description:

帮助独立教师将课后观察整理为结构化教学记录、学习轨迹和下节课衔接建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Independent K12 teachers use this skill to draft and review dated lesson logs, topic-level mastery observations, learning trajectories, and next-lesson priorities. Lesson records and lesson-unit changes require teacher confirmation; parent summaries remain internal drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12 workflow; localize curriculum, consent, and crisis-support guidance before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student or family details may be exposed in stored lesson records or parent drafts.

Mitigation: Use student pseudonyms, avoid real names and sensitive family details, check consent before sharing or drafting parent content, and have the teacher review records before saving.

Risk: Incorrect lesson observations or lesson-unit deductions can affect teaching decisions and balances.

Mitigation: Record observable facts rather than inferred traits and require teacher confirmation before saving records or updating lesson-unit balances.

Risk: Mainland-China-specific consent rules and crisis-support channels may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize consent procedures, curriculum assumptions, and crisis referrals before using the skill outside its intended workflow.
