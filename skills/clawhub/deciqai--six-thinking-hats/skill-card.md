## Description: <br>
Six Thinking Hats helps agents structure meetings and decisions by guiding users through parallel thinking modes for facts, emotions, risks, value, creativity, and process. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, facilitators, and agents use this skill to structure group or individual decisions that need balanced multi-angle review, especially when discussion is circular or creative ideas are being rejected too early. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for general requests about structuring a discussion. <br>
Mitigation: Use narrower trigger wording or confirm the user has a concrete multi-angle decision before running the process. <br>
Risk: Decision-framing guidance can be incomplete when the topic requires specialist analysis or a purely factual answer. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for facilitation and framing, and route factual or specialist analysis to the appropriate domain process. <br>
