## Description:

Guides individuals and teams through a structured Six Thinking Hats discussion to evaluate a decision from multiple perspectives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use the skill to structure decision discussions, explore facts, value, risks, alternatives, and intuitions in turn, and close with a decision or next action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Discussion outputs could be treated as verified conclusions when making consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Use the hats to structure discussion, then review conclusions and verify factual claims before acting.
