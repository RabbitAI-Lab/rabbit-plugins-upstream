## Description:

A Chinese-language guided analysis skill that helps users examine complex events, social issues, historical questions, or life decisions through five dimensions: history, interests, power, confrontation, and essence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ztmalan221-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ztmalan221-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a structured thinking aid for analyzing complex situations through sequential prompts and concise summaries. It is intended for broad analytical reasoning, not as a substitute for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical expert advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used for specialized medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical decisions where structured reasoning alone is insufficient.

Mitigation: Use it only as a thinking aid for those domains and rely on appropriate qualified expert guidance before acting.

Risk: Broad analysis trigger phrases may activate the skill for topics that need a different tool or a domain-specific workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants five-dimension analysis before proceeding, and switch context when a specialized workflow is more appropriate.
