## Description:

将用户提供的中文小说开篇改为三种不同节奏的可发布版本，保留人物设定与核心事件，并给出定位到原文的修改理由和推荐稿；适用于开篇抓力、首段改稿与网文起笔优化，不用于整本续写或六维审稿。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese fiction writers use the skill to reshape a supplied novel opening into three pacing options, compare edits tied to the source text, and choose a recommended version while preserving the story's established facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Prefer copying reviewed files or pin the installer and repository revision.
