## Description: <br>
Helps agents plan, structure, optimize, and analyze paid advertising campaigns across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, TikTok, and related B2B and ABM workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, growth teams, and operators use this skill to plan paid acquisition strategy, choose channels, structure campaigns, evaluate performance, and prepare platform-specific setup or ad-output guidance. It is oriented toward commercial paid media operations, especially B2B, ABM, retargeting, Google Search, Meta, and LinkedIn workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill claims live ad-account access and may guide launches, pauses, budget moves, audience uploads, or tracking changes. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit operator approval before applying live campaign changes, budget changes, launches, pauses, audience uploads, or tracking changes. <br>
Risk: The skill can encourage customer-data uploads and lead-data reuse without enough confirmation, consent, or privacy guardrails. <br>
Mitigation: Do not upload customer lists, CRM exports, emails, phone numbers, or enriched identity data unless the organization has the required consent or lawful basis, honors opt-outs, and has checked platform and regional privacy rules. <br>
Risk: Paid-media recommendations can affect spend efficiency and compliance-sensitive targeting decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations before installation and before operational use, especially where platform rules, privacy obligations, or regulated-industry advertising requirements apply. <br>
