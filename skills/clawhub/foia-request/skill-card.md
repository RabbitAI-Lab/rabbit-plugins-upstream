## Description: <br>
Drafts properly scoped public-records requests with records sought, date range, format, fee-waiver or expedited-processing language when applicable, and citations to the governing statute. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, journalists, researchers, and other requesters use this skill to draft public-records requests to government bodies. It helps structure requests with the right jurisdiction, custodian, date range, format, fee language, and response-time framing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A drafted request may cite the wrong statute, deadline, agency process, or fee rule for the user's jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the governing law, agency submission process, deadlines, fee category, and waiver requirements before sending the request. <br>
Risk: A request may include sensitive personal information or confidential details that are unnecessary for the records request. <br>
Mitigation: Review and remove unnecessary sensitive information before submitting the final request. <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the generated draft for legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as drafting assistance and consult qualified counsel or official agency guidance when the request has legal or commercial consequences. <br>
