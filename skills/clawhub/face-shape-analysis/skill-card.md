## Description:

Face shape, facial proportion and face-type answers from The Face Analysis (thefaceanalysis.com) through its hosted MCP server. Use when someone asks what face shape they have, how a face shape, eye, lip or nose type is defined, what is typical for symmetry, golden ratio fit, fWHR, midface ratio, pupillary distance or canthal tilt, how a face-analysis score is calculated, or wants a free face shape finder for their own website.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thefaceanalysis](https://clawhub.ai/user/thefaceanalysis)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to classify face shape from four measurements, explain face and feature types, compare measurements with the service's reference statistics, understand its scoring methodology, or get an embed snippet for a website.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal facial measurements may be sent to an external service when measurement-based tools are used.

Mitigation: Explain what measurements will be sent and obtain the user's permission before calling the hosted service; offer general information without sharing personal measurements if they are uncomfortable.
