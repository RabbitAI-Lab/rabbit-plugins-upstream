## Description:

将用户提供的镜头描述、情节必留点与总时长预算，拆成可拍摄或可生成的镜头表和衔接要求。适用于单章、单场景或短文的动作过载镜头；仅拆解已有材料，不改写成新脚本。资料不足时明确列出缺项，超出单次范围时要求分批提交。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Writers, storyboard artists, and video production teams use this skill to break an overloaded existing shot into a timed, shootable or generatable shot plan while preserving required story beats and continuity.
