## Description: <br>
Simulate the VC partner meeting that discusses your pitch after you leave the room: four partner archetypes debate, write the internal verdict memo, and produce a debrief of fixable objections. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Founders, operators, and fundraising teams use this skill to stress-test a pitch before investor meetings by simulating an internal VC partner discussion, verdict memo, and objection debrief. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the simulated VC discussion as financial, legal, or investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present the output as adversarial practice feedback and require users to validate fundraising decisions with qualified advisors and current investor context. <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive pitch, traction, valuation, or fundraising details supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use only information the user is comfortable sharing in the agent environment and avoid adding confidential details that are not necessary for the simulation. <br>
Risk: If the pitch omits key facts, the simulation could overstate certainty or create misleading numeric assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Ground numeric claims in supplied facts, flag assumptions explicitly, and call out missing information as part of the feedback. <br>
