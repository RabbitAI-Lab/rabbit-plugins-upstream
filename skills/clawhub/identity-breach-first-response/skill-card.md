## Description:

Helps people triage a personal data breach, prioritize immediate account containment, and prepare identity-theft response steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individuals responding to a personal data breach use this skill to assess exposed information, secure accounts in priority order, plan credit freezes and monitoring, and draft identity-theft dispute letters when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A misleading destination could expose sensitive information during breach response.

Mitigation: Verify bureau, carrier, FTC, IRS, and bank destinations independently rather than following links in breach notices.

Risk: Sensitive identity details entered as command arguments may remain in shell history.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive personal details in shell commands; fill report and letter templates privately.
