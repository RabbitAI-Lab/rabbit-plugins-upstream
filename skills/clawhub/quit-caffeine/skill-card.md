## Description: <br>
Reduce or quit caffeine with withdrawal tracking, tapering plans, and energy milestones. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jhillin8](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhillin8) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
People reducing or quitting caffeine use this skill to log intake, build taper plans, track withdrawal symptoms, and get practical energy and sleep strategies while keeping progress data local. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Caffeine logs and withdrawal notes can include health-adjacent personal details retained in local agent history. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive medical details and review or delete local logs or agent history when the environment supports it. <br>
