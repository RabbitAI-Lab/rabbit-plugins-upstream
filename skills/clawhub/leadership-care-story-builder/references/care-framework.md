## CARE

Context: set the scene, establish relevance, show status quo or challenge.

Action: highlight the conflict, decision, or challenge; describe the specific actions taken.

Resolution: share the measurable, cultural, relational, or operational outcome; connect the outcome to the purpose.

Engagement: elicit emotion, encourage reflection, or ask the audience to take a specific action.

## 90-Second Timing

- Context: 15-20 seconds.
- Action: 30-40 seconds.
- Resolution: 20-30 seconds.
- Engagement: 15-20 seconds.

## Scorecard

Use these review criteria:

- Clarity of Purpose: Was the core message obvious within 30 seconds?
- Context and Relevance: Did the setup hook the listener without extra fluff?
- Action and Tension: Did the story highlight clear decisions and challenges?
- Resolution and Value: Was the takeaway clearly linked to team values/goals?
- Engagement / CTA: Did the ending invite reflection or concrete action?
- Delivery and Tone: Eye contact, pacing, vocal variety, and authenticity.

## Stakeholder Pressure Tests

Skeptical executive: wants bottom line and business value.

Overwhelmed team member: worries about bandwidth and initiative fatigue.

Data purist: wants metrics or evidence behind a subjective example.

The storyteller should acknowledge the reaction, keep composure, and pivot back to Resolution and Engagement.
