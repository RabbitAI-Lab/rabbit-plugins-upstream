## Description:

Helps users build, repair, review, rehearse, adapt, and pressure-test leadership stories using the CARE framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Leaders, managers, employees, and communications coaches use this skill to turn real events, decisions, failures, recognition moments, and change narratives into clear spoken leadership stories. It also helps review, adapt, rehearse, and pressure-test stories for audience fit, evidence, ethical handling, timing, and a concrete ask.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Leadership stories could include unsupported claims, invented details, or overstated causality if the user provides incomplete evidence.

Mitigation: Ask for missing evidence, mark placeholders clearly, qualify weak claims, and do not invent facts, metrics, quotes, motives, chronology, stakeholders, or outcomes.

Risk: A story could reveal private, identifying, personnel, medical, customer, legal, security, or confidential information.

Mitigation: Use only supplied details that are appropriate for the venue, anonymize when needed, and avoid sensitive information unless the user has provided it and confirmed it is appropriate to use.

Risk: Persuasive leadership storytelling can become manipulative, exploit hardship, or hide uncertainty and tradeoffs.

Mitigation: Keep the ask transparent, preserve the dignity of people in the story, avoid manipulative pity, and name uncertainty, tradeoffs, failures, or costs when they matter to credibility.

Risk: Optional cross-skill suggestions may be inappropriate in workspaces where those skills are installed but not relevant to the user's situation.

Mitigation: Review optional cross-skill suggestions before use and invoke them only when they materially improve the story or reduce risk.
