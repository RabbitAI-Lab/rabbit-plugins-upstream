## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-26

### 🎉 Initial Release
- 56 Prompting Frameworks (inspired by 56 Framework Prompting by @\_khaamal)
- 3-mode workflow: Quick, Guided, Advanced
- Auto-select framework based on user need
- Smart keyword detection

### 📝 Framework Categories
- **Writing & Content:** C-A-R-E, T-A-G, R-T-F, CO-STAR, GRADE, COAST
- **Analytical & Problem-Solving:** P-A-C-T, R-I-S-E, D-R-E-A-M, S-O-L-V-E, R-A-C-E, TRACE, A-P-E, PECRA, OSCAR, RODES, TRACI
- **Advanced Prompting:** Rule-Based, CoT, PAP, Self-Consistency, APE, ToT, GoT, Least-to-Most, Few-Shot CoT, Stepwise Refinement, Chain of Verification, Progressive Summarization, Constraint-Based, Deliberation
- **Creative & Exploration:** Error-Driven, Inversion, Roleplay+Constraint, Counterfactual, Problem-Solving, Reverse Engineering, Analogy, Persona Comparison, Time-Travel, Cross-Domain Transfer, Multi-Stage, Layered Abstraction, Debate, Test-and-Improve, Perspective Shift, Recursive, Exploration vs Exploitation, Heuristic, Comparative, Simulation, Creative Constraints

### 🌍 Multi-Language
- 6 languages: EN, ID, ZH, JA, MS, AR

### 🔄 Iteration Mode
- Refine existing prompts
- Automatic improvement suggestions

### ✅ Quality Checklist
- Role clear, Task specific, Format defined, Constraints included, Examples (if applicable), Iterable
