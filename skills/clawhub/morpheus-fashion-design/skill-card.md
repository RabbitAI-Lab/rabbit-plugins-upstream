## Description: <br>
Generates professional fashion and product advertising images with AI models holding or wearing products. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[pauldelavallaz](https://clawhub.ai/user/pauldelavallaz) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creative and marketing users can use this skill to guide an agent through generating fashion, product campaign, and commercial photography images from product and model inputs. It is suited for briefs that need a person shown with the advertised product, deliberate camera and lighting choices, and consistent model references. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow sends product photos, model images, client assets, or identifiable people to ComfyDeploy. <br>
Mitigation: Review data-sharing expectations before use, avoid sensitive inputs unless approved, and confirm that all depicted people and assets may be processed by the external service. <br>
Risk: The package includes under-disclosed local usage tracking behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review the tracking behavior before installation and remove, disable, or make it opt-in before using the skill with sensitive work. <br>
