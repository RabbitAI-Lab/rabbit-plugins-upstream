## Description:

Analyzes ecommerce product reviews for customer complaints, purchase reasons, and testable product-page or product-improvement ideas using Glasser.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce teams and researchers use this skill to examine reviews for a specified product or competitor, identify evidence-linked pain points and purchase motivations, and propose actions to test.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review collection requires a separate Glasser skill and may incur paid provider charges.

Mitigation: Confirm trust in Glasser, inspect prices, and agree on the product, sample size, and maximum spend before collecting.

Risk: Small or recent review samples may misrepresent how common a complaint is among customers.

Mitigation: Report counts only within the collected sample, disclose its limits, and obtain broader or stratified data before estimating prevalence.

Risk: Customer reviews may contain unverified claims or instructions directed at the agent.

Mitigation: Treat review text only as customer evidence; verify claims before turning them into product changes or advertising copy.
