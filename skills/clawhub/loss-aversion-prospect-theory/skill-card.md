## Description: <br>
This skill helps agents diagnose decisions distorted by loss aversion, reference points, framing, probability weighting, and related prospect-theory patterns. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill when coaching or analyzing decisions where a loss frame, anchored reference point, disposition effect, or probability distortion may be driving behavior away from expected-value reasoning. It is useful for negotiation, pricing, investing, insurance, product adoption, and policy or health framing cases that are not catastrophic and irreversible. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users could mistake behavioral decision support for financial, medical, or legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as a reasoning aid and require expert review for regulated or high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: Example templates may collect confidential deal, customer, or portfolio details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive information unless that disclosure is intentional and approved. <br>
