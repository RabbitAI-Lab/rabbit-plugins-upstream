## Description: <br>
Design-and-templates helps agents specify a reusable brand kit, template set, hierarchy rules, and production workflow so non-designers can produce consistent social graphics with external design or image tools. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External social media teams, creators, and non-designers use this skill to turn an existing brand profile and recurring content needs into a small, reusable social template system. The skill specifies brand-kit tokens, template types, legibility rules, and workflow while actual graphics are produced in design or image tools. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may assume the skill renders, generates, or publishes graphics automatically. <br>
Mitigation: Keep design and generation in external design or image tools, and route only finished assets into the publishing flow. <br>
Risk: Generated imagery or copied competitor layouts may create copyright, trade dress, or brand-distinctiveness issues. <br>
Mitigation: Use brand-profile-derived templates, avoid copying competitors, and add human edits to AI-generated assets before publication. <br>
Risk: Design tool capabilities, pricing, AI credits, and indemnification terms may change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Re-verify tool features and terms before relying on them for production work. <br>
