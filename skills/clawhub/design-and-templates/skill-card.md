## Description: <br>
Design & Templates helps agents plan a reusable brand kit, a small set of on-brand social templates, hierarchy rules, and a non-designer workflow for producing consistent graphics across formats. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, social media operators, and marketing teams use this skill to turn a brand profile and recurring content needs into reusable templates, brand-kit tokens, and a practical production workflow. It provides planning guidance and specifications; design tools or image tools produce the final graphics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated images, imported brand-profile inputs, or downstream publishing steps may be used without enough user review. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the brand-profile source, generated-image choices, and any WoopSocial publishing steps with the user before producing or publishing final assets. <br>
Risk: Raw AI-generated visuals may have copyright or ownership limits. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated visuals as draft material and add human review and edits before using them in brand assets. <br>
Risk: Template or design-tool guidance can become stale as Canva, Adobe Express, Figma, and image tools change. <br>
Mitigation: Re-check tool capabilities, pricing, AI credits, and indemnification claims before relying on them for production decisions. <br>
