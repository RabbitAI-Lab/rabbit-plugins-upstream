## Description:

Produces concrete volume and rebar estimate bands from structural drawings with evidence, confidence and review flags. Reports by floor or pour zone for material-planning review, not final settlement, shop fabrication or legal sign-off.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Construction estimators, site engineers, and project teams use the skill to prepare concrete and rebar quantity estimates by floor or pour zone from structural drawings. They review the evidence and flags before using estimates for material planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may claim checks that did not run or present inferred values without review flags.

Mitigation: Have a qualified estimator independently verify quantities and material grades, including entries marked consistent or requiring no review, and request corrected safeguards for unsupported verification claims.

Risk: Installation may download and execute a separate CAD dependency that was not bundled for inspection.

Mitigation: Review the dependency package before installation and confirm its intended version and checksum.
