## Description:

Find which websites and articles AI platforms such as Doubao, DeepSeek, Yuanbao, and Qwen cite when answering questions in a category, and suggest where to publish so AI is more likely to cite the content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hclscut](https://clawhub.ai/user/hclscut)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and content teams use this skill to ask selected AI platforms category questions, review the websites and articles they cite, and plan where to publish useful content. It can also check whether a named brand appears among the sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
