## Description: <br>
Helps users optimize content for AI search engines, LLM citations, AI-generated answers, and answer engine visibility. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[troatpopcorn](https://clawhub.ai/user/troatpopcorn) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, marketers, and content teams use this skill to audit AI search visibility, improve content extractability, evaluate AI crawler access, and plan content that can be cited by AI assistants and answer engines. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recommendations for robots.txt can change whether AI crawlers can access and cite public content. <br>
Mitigation: Review crawler allow and disallow rules against the site's content-use, training, and citation goals before publishing changes. <br>
Risk: Recommendations for pricing.md and llms.txt can expose business, pricing, or product details in machine-readable public files. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended public disclosure level and keep any machine-readable files aligned with approved pricing and product messaging. <br>
