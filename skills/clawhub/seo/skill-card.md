## Description: <br>
Improves organic search performance through SEO audits, keyword research, content writing and refreshes, technical diagnostics, schema guidance, link analysis, competitor analysis, and recovery planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, marketers, SEO specialists, and developers use this skill to audit organic search performance, diagnose ranking or indexing issues, plan keyword and competitor work, write or refresh ranking-focused content, and prioritize technical SEO fixes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: SEO preferences, audit history, keyword tracking, and site context may contain business-sensitive information when stored locally. <br>
Mitigation: Review or delete ~/Clawic/data/seo/ as needed before sharing a machine or workspace, and avoid storing unnecessary sensitive site data. <br>
Risk: Recommendations can be misleading for non-US sites if the target market is left at the default. <br>
Mitigation: Set target_market correctly before audits, SERP checks, keyword research, or content recommendations for non-US markets. <br>
