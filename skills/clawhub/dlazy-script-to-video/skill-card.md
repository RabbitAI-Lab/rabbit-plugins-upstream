## Description:

Turns a script, screenplay, or shot list into a storyboarded, shot-by-shot video workflow with scene breakdown, shot generation, assembly, and validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to turn scripts or scene breakdowns into multi-shot animated videos through dLazy, including storyboard planning, reference-file handling, video assembly, and iterative project continuation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy-hosted services, and an API key may be stored locally.

Mitigation: Avoid attaching sensitive scripts, images, or reference files unless they are intended for dLazy; rotate or revoke the API key from the dLazy dashboard when needed.
