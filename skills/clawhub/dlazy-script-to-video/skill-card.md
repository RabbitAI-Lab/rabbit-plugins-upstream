## Description:

Turn a script, screenplay, or shot list into a storyboarded, shot-by-shot video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video production teams use this skill to turn scripts, screenplays, or shot lists into storyboarded, multi-shot videos. The workflow can generate shots and assemble them with narration, music, sound effects, and subtitles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, attached files, and project context are sent to dLazy hosted services.

Mitigation: Attach only files intended for upload and review the service privacy terms before sending sensitive material.

Risk: The CLI saves an organization API key, and video generation may incur costs.

Mitigation: Prefer device-code login, review generation costs, and revoke the saved key when access is no longer needed.
