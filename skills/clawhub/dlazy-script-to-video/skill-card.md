## Description:

Turns scripts into storyboarded, shot-by-shot videos with character visuals, audio, subtitles, and assembled scenes through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to turn a script or scene breakdown into a multi-shot animated video with consistent characters using dLazy's storyboard workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, project context, and attached files are sent to dLazy services.

Mitigation: Do not provide secrets, private media, or proprietary material unless you intend to upload them.

Risk: Login stores a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local account and rotate or revoke the key if exposed.
