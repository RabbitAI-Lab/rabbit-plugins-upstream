## Use Case:

Developers and content teams use this skill to generate AI poster images, editable text layers, and multi-page image carousels from prompts, reference images, templates, and style settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Poster prompts, reference images, and design details are sent to a hosted DeepNLP/Craftsman service.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive inputs unless the provider's privacy, retention, and access controls have been reviewed and accepted.

Risk: Generated workspace or share links may expose proprietary designs or uploaded reference material.

Mitigation: Review link-sharing behavior before submitting confidential content, and remove or restrict shared workspaces when no longer needed.

Risk: The photo editor includes watermark-removal behavior that can be misused.

Mitigation: Use watermark removal only for content the user owns or is authorized to modify.
