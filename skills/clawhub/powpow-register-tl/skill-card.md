## Description:

Guides Tagalog-speaking users through PowPow account registration, common signup errors, and subscription activation without handling credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this Tagalog guide to understand PowPow, complete signup on the official site, troubleshoot registration issues, and activate their accounts through the subscription page. The skill provides instructions and relevant screenshots but does not submit forms or process payments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Mitigation: Direct users to enter these only on official PowPow signup and payment pages; never request them in chat.

Risk: Signup or payment guidance may become outdated as the platform changes.

Mitigation: Defer to the current behavior of official PowPow pages instead of promising that documented steps or activation timing will remain unchanged.
