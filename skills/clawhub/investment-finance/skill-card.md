## Description:

Helps agents analyze Chinese-language VC/PE financing, valuation, deal terms, due diligence, and investment decisions using practical references, templates, and calculation scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, finance advisers, and investment teams use this Chinese-language skill to compare valuations, review financing terms, prepare due-diligence checklists, and draft investment analyses. Its material is a working aid, not personalized investment or legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive financial records and confidential deal materials could be disclosed during analysis.

Mitigation: Share only necessary, redacted material; keep control of bank statements, credit reports, tax records, and confidential memos.

Risk: Publishing, syncing, browser automation, or file export could expose investment outputs externally.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before Feishu publication, knowledge-base sync, browser automation, or exporting files.

Risk: Non-financial scoring or aggressive negotiation tactics could mislead decisions, and legal, tax, or investment conclusions could be wrong.

Mitigation: Treat these methods as non-authoritative and verify consequential conclusions with qualified professionals.
