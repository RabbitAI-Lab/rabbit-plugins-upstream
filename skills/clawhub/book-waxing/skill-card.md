## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to find waxing providers, check service availability, and create a booking through the configured Lokuli booking provider. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking requests may send personal contact details to the configured booking provider. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the salon, appointment time, service, customer name, email, and phone number with the user. <br>
