## Description: <br>
Generates Chinese after-market A-share reviews with market summaries, theme expectations, hot-sector notes, limit-up capacity, position posture, and next-day trading conditions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[albert-mk](https://clawhub.ai/user/albert-mk) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Experienced A-share short-term traders use this skill after market close to turn daily market data, screenshots, sector moves, or custom stock lists into an opinionated review and next-day outlook. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trading-oriented commentary, position sizing, and next-day conditions may be mistaken for financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as informational market commentary only and verify data independently before making trading decisions. <br>
Risk: Generic Chinese market-analysis prompts may trigger this opinionated review style unexpectedly. <br>
Mitigation: Install it only when that behavior is desired and review outputs for fit before relying on them. <br>
