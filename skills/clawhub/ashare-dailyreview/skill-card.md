## Use Case: <br>
External A-share market participants with short-term trading experience use this skill after market close to produce a structured recap, next-day expectations, position posture, and sentiment-leader tracking. Outputs are trading analysis and watchlist guidance, not investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trading-oriented analysis may be mistaken for investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the disclaimer in generated output and require human judgment before acting on any trading idea. <br>
Risk: Casual market discussion could trigger trading-oriented responses unintentionally. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill deliberately for A-share closing-review tasks and avoid using it for unrelated market conversations. <br>
Risk: Incomplete market data can lead to unsupported precise figures. <br>
Mitigation: Mark missing data as unavailable, use clearly labeled inference only when necessary, and avoid fabricating exact figures. <br>
