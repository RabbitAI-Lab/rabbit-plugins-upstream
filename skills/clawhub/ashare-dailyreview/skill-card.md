## Description:

Generates Chinese A-share daily closing recaps in a fixed six-part structure covering market summary, sector expectations, hot themes, limit-up/capacity/emotion trades, recent emotion leaders, and next-day short-term leader scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[albert-mk](https://clawhub.ai/user/albert-mk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users with short-term A-share trading experience use this skill to have an agent produce end-of-day market recaps, theme rotation analysis, position framing, and next-day watch plans. Outputs are trading-oriented analysis to verify against current market data, not personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill produces active A-share trading recaps with short-term scenarios and position framing.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analysis to verify against current market data, not personalized financial advice.

Risk: The skill may activate on common Chinese market-recap phrases or submitted market screenshots.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent and the relevant trading date before relying on the generated recap.
