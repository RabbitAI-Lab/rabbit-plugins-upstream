## Description:

Build weekly or monthly social media content calendars for ecommerce brands, aligned with product launches, holidays, seasonal promotions, flash sales, and platform-specific trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce brand owners, social media managers, and marketing teams use this skill to plan commercially aligned weekly or monthly social calendars across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, and X.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for business plans, product catalog details, audience analytics, or social performance data.

Mitigation: Provide only data that is appropriate to share in the agent environment and omit sensitive business details when they are not needed.

Risk: Platform dimensions, caption limits, commerce features, and recommendation signals may change after the reference files were authored.

Mitigation: Verify current platform requirements before using the calendar for production scheduling.

Risk: Generated social posts or schedules may include inaccurate, off-brand, or commercially risky recommendations.

Mitigation: Keep human approval in the workflow before scheduling or publishing any post.
