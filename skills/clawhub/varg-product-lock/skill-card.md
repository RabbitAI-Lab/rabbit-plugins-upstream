## Description:

Guides creators through locking a product’s shape, surface, and allowed motion across AI video shots, checking clips for drift, and restoring real packshots where text must stay exact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Product marketers, video creators, and developers use this skill to keep a product’s shape, color, and label consistent across AI-generated video shots. It provides product reference and prompting guidance, drift checks, and packshot compositing examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos may contain unreleased or sensitive product information.

Mitigation: Share only product assets authorized for use with Varg and its model providers.

Risk: API key exposure or repeated generation and retries can create security and spending exposure.

Mitigation: Protect VARG_API_KEY and set a spending limit for generation and retries.
