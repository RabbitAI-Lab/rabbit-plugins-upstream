## Description: <br>
Structured Darktable photo critique and non-destructive RAW edit workflow with evidence-based analysis, a phased collaboration loop, restrained post-processing guidance, Darktable 5.6 module coverage, masks, tone mappers, and photographic-art analysis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cantoneyes](https://clawhub.ai/user/cantoneyes) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Photographers and photo-editing assistants use this skill to critique images, choose restrained Darktable editing directions, and produce executable non-destructive RAW or rendered-file edit plans grounded in visible evidence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask the agent to browse official documentation or art-history sources when current or exact facts are needed. <br>
Mitigation: Disable browsing for fully offline use, or require citations from authoritative sources for current, contested, or publication-ready claims. <br>
Risk: Photo critique and edit plans can overstate recoverability, capture conditions, or intent when only a rendered image is available. <br>
Mitigation: Keep visible facts, judgments, and unknowns separate, and require RAW data, metadata, scopes, or screenshots before making technical claims that need them. <br>
