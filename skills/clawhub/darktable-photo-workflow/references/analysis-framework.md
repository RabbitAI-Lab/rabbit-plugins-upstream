# Evidence framework for photographic-art analysis

## Purpose and limits

The task is comparative visual analysis, not attribution. A single image rarely proves the maker's intention, influence, school membership, or knowledge of another photographer. Historical claims require external evidence.

## Evidence ladder

Work upward only as evidence allows:

1. **Description** — what is visibly present.
2. **Formal mechanism** — how framing, timing, light, color, surface, or sequence produces an effect.
3. **Tradition-level comparison** — a broad practice shares several mechanisms.
4. **Named comparison** — a photographer or body of work clarifies the image more precisely.
5. **Historical claim** — influence, intention, chronology, or affiliation; verify externally.

Never skip directly from a mood adjective to levels 4 or 5.

## Analysis dimensions

Select those that materially explain the image:

- **Subject relation:** anonymous observation, collaboration, confrontation, intimacy, typology, spectacle, or self-staging.
- **Spatial construction:** frontal/oblique view, compressed/deep space, edge activity, centrality, empty space, repetition, scale, and horizon.
- **Temporal logic:** decisive instant, duration, aftermath, serial accumulation, deadpan stillness, or performed scene.
- **Light and tone:** descriptive, theatrical, diffuse, high-key, low-key, silhouette, tonal compression, or extreme separation.
- **Color logic:** descriptive color, palette structure, color as subject, discord, symbolic coding, or near-monochrome restraint.
- **Surface:** fine detail, blur, grain, flash, scratches, compression, retouching, or visibly constructed image material.
- **Information and ambiguity:** social evidence, narrative closure, withheld context, psychological projection, or conceptual instruction.
- **Display logic:** isolated print, grid, sequence, slideshow, book, caption, scale, or installation.

## Comparison threshold

For a named photographer, require at least three independent correspondences, including one structural correspondence. Independent means that several labels are not merely restating one feature. For example, “dark,” “moody,” and “high contrast” count as one tonal cluster.

Also state one difference that limits the comparison. Assign confidence:

- **High:** multiple specific mechanisms plus matching subject relation or display logic; relevant context is known.
- **Medium:** several formal mechanisms align, but context, sequence, or intention is unknown.
- **Low:** the analogy rests mainly on palette, mood, or one common device. Prefer a tradition-level label or omit the name.

This threshold is an editorial reliability heuristic, not a scholarly rule.

## False-positive controls

- Monochrome may be a technical or aesthetic choice; it does not establish documentary, modernist, or fine-art lineage.
- Grain, blur, and high contrast do not alone establish Provoke; examine fractured space, unstable perception, sequence, and relation to postwar urban experience.
- Strong geometry and silhouettes do not alone establish Fan Ho; examine urban observation, layered staging within real space, light direction, and human scale.
- Saturated color does not alone establish Eggleston; examine whether ordinary color relationships organize attention democratically across the frame.
- Roadside banality does not alone establish Shore or New Topographics; examine descriptive distance, serial logic, spatial neutrality, and cultural information.
- Intimacy does not alone establish Goldin; examine reciprocal access, diaristic duration, community context, and vulnerability.
- Frontal portraits do not alone establish Sander or Ruff; distinguish social taxonomy from monumental psychological blankness.
- Large scale and detail do not alone establish Gursky; examine elevated systems view, repetition, abstraction, and constructed totality.

## Translate comparison into editing implications

Recommend an edit only when it strengthens a mechanism already present and the user confirms the direction.

- **Crop:** reinforce spatial logic or remove accidental competition; do not crop merely to imitate a reference.
- **Tone:** preserve information or separation appropriate to the image; avoid generic crushed blacks and cinematic contrast.
- **Color:** articulate existing palette relationships; avoid applying a borrowed palette wholesale.
- **Detail:** retain texture that carries place, body, or material evidence; suppress only distracting artifacts.
- **Local work:** guide attention where the frame's own hierarchy supports it; avoid theatrical relighting without evidence.
- **Effects:** grain, blur, vignette, and diffusion are optional expressive additions, never automatic markers of seriousness.

## Compact output template

```text
形式观察：
- [three to five visible mechanisms]

可能的艺术脉络：
- [tradition or photographer], because [specific mechanisms]

关键差异：
- [where the comparison stops]

置信度：
- [high / medium / low] — [limiting evidence]

修图含义：
- [one to three restrained consequences]
```

If no analogy clears the threshold, write: “这张照片目前更适合用形式语言描述；点名流派或摄影师不会增加解释力。”
