# First-edition photographer comparison cards

Use a card only after the image clears the comparison threshold in `analysis-framework.md`. The “editing consequence” is conditional, not a recipe.

## Walker Evans

- **Verified anchor:** MoMA characterizes Evans's practice as unvarnished yet joined to a “lyrical documentary” approach; *American Photographs* is a central body of work. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/artists/1777-walker-evans)
- **Mechanisms:** frontal description, vernacular signs and architecture, social information, measured distance, precise ordinary detail.
- **Useful comparison when:** cultural artifacts and restrained framing carry more meaning than spectacle.
- **Boundary:** plainness alone is insufficient; Evans's social and serial context matters.
- **Editing consequence:** retain descriptive detail and neutral tonal structure; avoid decorative dramatization.

## Robert Frank

- **Verified anchor:** *The Americans* emerged from Frank's 1955–56 US journey and gains meaning through selection, contact sheets, and sequence. [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/exhibitions/looking)
- **Mechanisms:** subjective sequencing, occlusion, off moments, cultural symbols, social estrangement, expressive grain and tone.
- **Useful comparison when:** interruption and ambiguity form a personal social account.
- **Boundary:** any grainy black-and-white road image is not automatically Frank-like.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve ambiguity and contextual symbols; do not over-correct tonal roughness that carries the experience.

## August Sander

- **Verified anchor:** *People of the Twentieth Century* organizes more than 600 photographs into a social portrait across seven volumes. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/artists/5145-august-sander)
- **Mechanisms:** frontal pose, environmental and occupational detail, typological grouping, social role and individual presence held together.
- **Useful comparison when:** portrait meaning depends on both person and social classification.
- **Boundary:** a neutral frontal portrait without serial taxonomy is a weak match.
- **Editing consequence:** keep clothing, hands, tools, and environment legible; resist theatrical skin treatment.

## Diane Arbus, Lee Friedlander, and Garry Winogrand

- **Verified anchor:** MoMA's 1967 *New Documents* presented their documentary work as serving more personal ends. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/3487)
- **Mechanisms:** respectively, direct social encounter; reflections, obstructions, and self-aware frames; crowded timing, edge incidents, and unstable public space.
- **Useful comparison when:** specify which mechanism applies; do not treat the three as one look.
- **Boundary:** awkwardness or a tilted snapshot alone is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve structurally useful edges, reflections, and frictions; remove only accidental competition.

## William Eggleston

- **Verified anchor:** MoMA links Eggleston to a “democratic way of looking” in which ordinary subjects and color become central. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/artists/1690-william-eggleston)
- **Mechanisms:** color as spatial structure, ordinary objects with distributed attention, oblique framing, vernacular surfaces.
- **Useful comparison when:** color relationships and non-hierarchical attention organize the frame.
- **Boundary:** saturation, red walls, or banal objects alone are insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** adjust specific color relationships and luminance before global saturation; retain material plausibility.

## Stephen Shore

- **Verified anchor:** Shore recorded mundane American culture using changing formats; *American Surfaces* functions as a color visual diary. [MoMA retrospective](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/3769), [American Surfaces](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/4839)
- **Mechanisms:** descriptive color, everyday meals/rooms/roads/signs, serial travel logic, careful ordinary space.
- **Useful comparison when:** accumulated commonplace observations build place and culture.
- **Boundary:** one roadside photograph without serial logic is at most a partial comparison.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve daylight, vernacular color, and spatial information; avoid imposing a cinematic palette.

## Robert Adams

- **Verified anchor:** Adams photographed suburban growth and commercial landscapes while retaining reverence for what remains; quiet black-and-white form is central. [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/american-silence)
- **Mechanisms:** modest tonal scale, measured human-altered landscape, ethical attention, emptiness, and visual restraint.
- **Useful comparison when:** environmental change and remaining beauty coexist without spectacle.
- **Boundary:** empty development or gray skies alone are insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** hold highlight detail and quiet tonal separation; avoid heroic contrast.

## Lewis Baltz

- **Verified anchor:** Baltz is associated with New Topographics and joined minimalist/conceptual concerns to overlooked consumer-economy margins. [Getty Research Institute](https://www.getty.edu/news/getty-research-institute-acquires-lewis-baltz-archive/)
- **Mechanisms:** industrial surfaces, repetition, cool distance, deadpan geometry, marginal built environments.
- **Useful comparison when:** the image treats anonymous infrastructure as both evidence and abstract system.
- **Boundary:** minimal architecture alone is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve surface differences and repeated structure; avoid expressive sky replacement or romantic vignetting.

## Bernd and Hilla Becher

- **Verified anchor:** Their industrial structures were photographed frontally under controlled conditions and commonly assembled into comparative grids. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/136060)
- **Mechanisms:** typology, consistent viewpoint/light, neutral background, repetition with variation, series as primary work.
- **Useful comparison when:** consistency makes comparison possible across multiple images.
- **Boundary:** a single symmetrical industrial photograph is not a Becher typology.
- **Editing consequence:** prioritize cross-image consistency, vertical precision, and even descriptive tone.

## Thomas Struth

- **Verified anchor:** MoMA describes a shift from systematic street/architecture description toward more fluid approaches while retaining sustained attention to social space. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/49427)
- **Mechanisms:** patient architectural description, deep social space, public institutions, complex looking relations.
- **Useful comparison when:** built space and human behavior remain mutually legible.
- **Boundary:** large-format architecture alone is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** retain depth cues and fine structure; guide attention without flattening the social field.

## Thomas Ruff

- **Verified anchor:** Ruff's monumental frontal portraits produce detailed surfaces with restrained psychological disclosure; later work investigates image technologies and circulation. [MoMA portrait](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/50870), [MoMA Inbox](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/3688)
- **Mechanisms:** neutral pose, monumental scale, surface scrutiny, psychological blankness, visible mediation or low-resolution image material.
- **Useful comparison when:** the photograph makes image conditions or unreadability of the subject central.
- **Boundary:** a passport-like pose or compressed file alone is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve neutral surface and scale logic; do not manufacture drama through skin contrast.

## Andreas Gursky

- **Verified anchor:** Gursky's large color works combine dense detail, elevated perspective, abstraction, and digital construction. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/170)
- **Mechanisms:** system-scale view, repetition, crowds or commodities, flattened depth, totalizing detail, constructed coherence.
- **Useful comparison when:** individual elements become a larger economic, spatial, or visual system.
- **Boundary:** panoramas, high resolution, or repetition alone are insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** protect repeated detail and global organization; avoid microcontrast that turns structure into noise.

## Nan Goldin

- **Verified anchor:** *The Ballad of Sexual Dependency* is a 690-slide diaristic installation centered on friends and chosen family. [Whitney Museum](https://whitney.org/collection/works/8274)
- **Mechanisms:** reciprocal intimacy, flash/ambient color, private space, emotional sequence, vulnerability and duration.
- **Useful comparison when:** access and relationship, not merely informality, create meaning.
- **Boundary:** party flash or candid proximity alone is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve skin, ambient color, and environmental specificity; limit cleanup that sanitizes the relationship.

## Cindy Sherman

- **Verified anchor:** In *Untitled Film Stills*, Sherman performs generic cinematic roles without recreating actual films. [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/1997/sherman/)
- **Mechanisms:** self-staging, media archetypes, ambiguous narrative, gaze, costume/setting as code, serial variation.
- **Useful comparison when:** constructed identity and familiar image conventions are the subject.
- **Boundary:** a cinematic-looking portrait alone is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** maintain consistency of the staged code and props; avoid grading that substitutes mood for the conceptual structure.

## Daido Moriyama and Provoke-associated language

- **Verified anchor:** *Provoke* pursued experimental photographic language in late-1960s Japan; Moriyama's work is commonly read through autobiographical, intuitive, fragmented urban experience. [MoMA — Provoke](https://www.moma.org/explore/inside_out/2013/01/25/for-the-sake-of-thought-provoke-1968-1970/), [SFMOMA — Focus on Japanese Photography](https://www.sfmoma.org/publication/focus-japanese-photography/contents/)
- **Mechanisms:** rough grain, blur, unstable crop, movement, glare, fractured signs and bodies, serial urban rhythm.
- **Useful comparison when:** instability is perceptual and structural, not a filter.
- **Boundary:** grainy black-and-white, missed focus, or crushed blacks alone are insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** preserve purposeful motion and glare; do not add aggressive grain unless the image already has corresponding spatial energy.

## Fan Ho

- **Verified anchor:** Fan Ho photographed everyday Hong Kong and often used dramatic light, distance, and cinematic framing to organize the decisive moment. [M+ essay](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/magazine/ho-fan-on-the-decisive-moment/), [M+ collection](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/objects/smoky-world-201533/)
- **Mechanisms:** directional shafts, layered street space, human figures as scale and rhythm, silhouettes, deliberate tonal architecture.
- **Useful comparison when:** real urban observation is transformed through light and spatial staging.
- **Boundary:** silhouettes, stairs, fog, or geometric shadow alone are insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** protect directional light and layer separation; avoid crushing the small human information that gives scale.

## Rinko Kawauchi

- **Verified anchor:** SFMOMA describes Kawauchi's restrained lyrical aesthetics and attention to ordinary life's fragility, distinct from the rough darkness associated with Provoke. [SFMOMA](https://www.sfmoma.org/artist/%E5%B7%9D%E5%86%85%E5%80%AB%E5%AD%90/)
- **Mechanisms:** delicate light, pale or subtle color, close observation, small events, open-ended sequence, fragility and recurrence.
- **Useful comparison when:** ordinary transient details gain meaning through light and serial attention.
- **Boundary:** pastel color, overexposure, or shallow depth alone are insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** protect highlight gradation and fine color differences; avoid heavy contrast or excessive clarity.

## Michael Wolf

- **Verified anchor:** Wolf's Hong Kong and Asian city work moves between compressed high-rise systems and close studies of street vernacular. [M+](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/magazine/from-high-rises-to-the-street-a-look-back-at-michael-wolfs-photographs/)
- **Mechanisms:** facade repetition, dense compression, human traces within systems, found chairs/objects, tension between scale and everyday adaptation.
- **Useful comparison when:** urban structure and improvised daily life are both central.
- **Boundary:** any dense facade or Hong Kong street scene is insufficient.
- **Editing consequence:** keep repeated geometry legible while preserving anomalous human traces; watch moiré and oversharpening.
