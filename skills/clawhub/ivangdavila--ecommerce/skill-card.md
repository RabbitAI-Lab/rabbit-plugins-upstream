## Description: <br>
Guides agents through online-store operations including catalog, checkout, payments, inventory, fulfillment, returns, pricing, conversion, retention, tax, marketplaces, peak planning, subscriptions, and wholesale terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Store operators, developers, and ecommerce teams use this skill to build, review, and troubleshoot online-store workflows across the money path, inventory, fulfillment, returns, growth, analytics, and compliance-sensitive operations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill keeps local ecommerce memory that may include business contacts, supplier or wholesale terms, metrics, deadlines, and incident summaries. <br>
Mitigation: Review the configured local paths before use and keep credentials, payment-card data, customer identities, and customer-level exports out of saved notes. <br>
Risk: Legal, tax, email, SMS, and consumer-law guidance may be jurisdiction-sensitive and may change over time. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current rules for the relevant market before acting, especially for consent, opt-out handling, retention practices, tax registration, filings, and consumer disclosures. <br>
