## Description: <br>
Searches for, researches, and verifies non-tech founders on LinkedIn to identify high-value prospects for technology services and produce professional lead reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[harshhmaniya](https://clawhub.ai/user/harshhmaniya) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, business development, and agency operators use this skill to identify non-tech founders who may need web development, ERP, CRM, app, SEO, or AI services. It guides prospect filtering, company research, gap analysis, and report creation for personalized outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prospect research may involve browsing LinkedIn and company websites in ways that create platform, privacy, or sensitive-profiling concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for explicit business prospecting, avoid mass scraping and sensitive profiling, and confirm applicable platform and privacy obligations before use. <br>
Risk: Generated HTML or PDF reports may include profile-derived or website-derived text that has not been escaped or reviewed. <br>
Mitigation: Sanitize and review generated HTML before opening or sharing reports, especially when automating report generation from external profile data. <br>
