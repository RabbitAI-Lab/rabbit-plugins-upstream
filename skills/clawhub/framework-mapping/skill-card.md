## Description: <br>
Map document sections to compliance framework controls with confidence scoring. Produces bidirectional mappings for gap analysis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[dangsllc](https://clawhub.ai/user/dangsllc) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Apache-2.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Compliance analysts and security teams use this skill to map policy or procedure sections to controls in frameworks such as HIPAA, NIST 800-53, HITRUST, ISO 27001, and SOC 2. It produces bidirectional mappings that support coverage review and gap analysis. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to read compliance documents that may contain sensitive organizational, security, or regulated information. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with documents and repositories within the intended compliance review scope, and review outputs before relying on them for control coverage or gap decisions. <br>
