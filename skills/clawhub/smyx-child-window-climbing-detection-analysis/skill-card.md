## Description:

Analyzes video of window or balcony areas to identify possible child climbing, leaning, or gripping hazards and produce alerts or reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents, caregivers, and childcare operators use the skill to review video of window or balcony areas for possible child climbing or leaning hazards and receive analysis reports. It is an auxiliary warning aid and does not replace adult supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment reports a gap between advertised continuous monitoring and the documented on-demand analysis workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the operating mode and alert behavior with the publisher before relying on it for monitoring.

Risk: The assessment calls out unresolved handling of sensitive child video and identity or account data.

Mitigation: Confirm where video and snapshots are processed and stored, obtain guardian consent, and limit access and retention; clarify identity and account handling before use.

Risk: The assessment notes unexpected side-effect and export-only behavior concerns, and that the scan was partial.

Mitigation: Clarify what data or actions are sent or exported and review the skill before installation; the available assessment does not cover all referenced material.
