## Description:

Scans public procurement and bidding sources through Cue to produce opportunity, competitor-award, contact, and trend reports for B2B sales and market-intelligence workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

B2B sales, business development, bid teams, and market-intelligence users use this skill to request public procurement scans, competitor award monitoring, contact enrichment, and trend summaries for specified industries, regions, and time windows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cue API keys, research queries, and generated report content are handled through Cue's external service.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when Cue's data handling and sharing model is acceptable, and avoid submitting confidential business strategy.

Risk: Generated reports may include Cue share links plus credit, referral, or subscription guidance.

Mitigation: Share report links only with intended recipients and review account or payment prompts before acting on them.

Risk: Public bidding data can be incomplete, delayed, or affected by source platform availability.

Mitigation: Confirm high-value opportunities, contact details, and deadlines against the original procurement platform before acting.
