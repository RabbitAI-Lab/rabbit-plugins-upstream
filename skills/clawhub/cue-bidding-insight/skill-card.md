## Use Case:

B2B sales, business development, bidding, and market intelligence teams use this skill to scan public bidding opportunities, monitor competitor awards, and produce structured reports for follow-up and planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bidding queries, company names, competitor names, and related metadata are sent to Cue and its public bidding-data integrations.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential procurement plans, private target lists, or non-public business intelligence unless Cue's data handling terms meet the user's requirements.

Risk: Public bidding data can be incomplete, delayed, rate-limited, or unavailable from individual source platforms.

Mitigation: Confirm high-value opportunities, deadlines, and contact details against the official source page before acting.

Risk: The skill does not access non-public invitations, internal budgets, review details, or private procurement plans.

Mitigation: Use it only for public bidding intelligence and route non-public procurement questions to authorized internal systems or official procurement contacts.
