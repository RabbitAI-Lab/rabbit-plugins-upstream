## Description:

招投标全景透视 helps agents run Cue-based procurement research to find active tender opportunities, monitor competitor wins, enrich public contact details, and summarize bidding trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

B2B sales, business development, market intelligence, and bid teams use this skill to discover public procurement opportunities, track competitor award activity, collect public buyer contact details, and compare tender trends across industries or regions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bidding research terms and returned buyer contact details may be sensitive business data because the skill queries Cue's remote service.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when remote processing is acceptable, and avoid entering confidential competitor strategy or non-public procurement information.

Risk: Procurement deadlines, source links, award details, and contact records can be stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify source links, official deadlines, and contact details before outreach, bidding decisions, or business planning.

Risk: Returned contact details could be misused for unlawful or non-compliant outreach.

Mitigation: Use contact details only for lawful business outreach and follow applicable procurement, privacy, and communications rules.
