## Description:

根据货物、路线和时效要求，比较跨境运输方案并整理清关、成本和包装注意事项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

跨境贸易从业者和个体经营者可提供起运地、目的地、货物信息与时效要求，获取运输方式对比、清关准备清单、成本构成和推荐方案。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated shipping costs, delivery times, customs requirements, and duties may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Confirm current prices and transit times with carriers, and verify customs rules and duties with official sources before shipping.

Risk: Batteries, liquids, branded goods, and restricted products may require special handling or be ineligible for shipping.

Mitigation: Check applicable carrier restrictions, import rules, and required certifications before committing to a route.
