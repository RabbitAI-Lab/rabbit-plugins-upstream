## Description:

Contract Clause Check screens pasted Chinese contract text for required breach, dispute, and jurisdiction terms and flags selected high-risk clause wording.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, agents, and contract reviewers use this skill as a local pre-screening aid to check pasted contract text for missing core clauses and common high-risk wording before human legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documented install commands can fetch mutable remote content and install it globally without pinning or verification.

Mitigation: Review the installer before use, prefer a pinned commit or release, and avoid global installation unless the publisher and repository are trusted.

Risk: The checker is a rule-based pre-screening aid and may miss contract issues outside its keyword and pattern coverage.

Mitigation: Use results as triage only and route important contracts to qualified legal review before making decisions.
