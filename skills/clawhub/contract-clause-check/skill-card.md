## Description:

Screens Chinese-language contracts for missing breach, dispute, and jurisdiction terms and flags selected potentially risky clauses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (code only); documentation and theoretical text all rights reserved

## Use Case:

People reviewing Chinese-language contracts use this skill to identify missing clause keywords and potentially risky wording before seeking professional legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keyword screening can miss substantive legal problems or flag wording without its context.

Mitigation: Treat findings as a preliminary check and consult a qualified lawyer for important agreements.

Risk: Contract text may contain sensitive business or personal information.

Mitigation: Keep contracts local and avoid granting network access unless needed.
