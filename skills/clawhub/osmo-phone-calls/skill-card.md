## Description:

AI phone call skill. Use when the user wants an AI to make a real phone call for them — call a business, shop, clinic, restaurant, helpline or a person they know; book, reschedule or cancel an appointment or reservation; reach customer service and get through the phone menu; ask a question and bring back the answer with a summary. Also use to see who called the user's OsmO number, what they wanted, and to change OsmO call screening rules. Free to start in the US, Canada, UK and Australia. Not for SMS or email.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People use this skill to have OsmO make calls to businesses or people, manage appointments and reservations, handle customer service, and get summaries of calls to their OsmO number. It can also help review and change call-screening settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, Canada, United Kingdom, and Australia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The hosted service requires signing in with a phone number, and use of the service may involve personal information.

Mitigation: Review OsmO's terms and privacy information and decide whether you are comfortable before signing in.

Risk: A placed call reaches a real person and may communicate information on the user's behalf.

Mitigation: Before placing a call, state who will be called and exactly what OsmO will say or ask, then wait for the user's explicit approval.
