## Description:

AI phone call skill. Use when the user wants an AI to make a real phone call for them — call a business, shop, clinic, restaurant, helpline or a person they know; book, reschedule or cancel an appointment or reservation; reach customer service and get through the phone menu; ask a question and bring back the answer with a summary. Also use to see who called the user's OsmO number, what they wanted, and to change OsmO call screening rules. Free to start in the US, Canada, UK and Australia, where free credits call businesses and toll-free numbers; calling contacts and scheduling need an OsmO app plan. Not for SMS or email.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to place calls to businesses or people, handle appointment and customer-service requests, or summarize calls received on their OsmO number. The agent can also help users review or change call-screening settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, Canada, United Kingdom, and Australia (free credits for calls to businesses and toll-free numbers)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the hosted OsmO service requires connecting an account and signing in with a phone number; calls reach real people.

Mitigation: Review OsmO's privacy information before connecting. Before placing a call, identify the recipient and exactly what will be said, then wait for the user's approval. Never request or transmit passwords, payment credentials, one-time codes, or government ID numbers.
