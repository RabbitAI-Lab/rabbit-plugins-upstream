## Description: <br>
Turn a topic or draft into a high-engagement X Article, then provide final copy and a scored review using an 8-step optimization framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

Creators, marketers, and communications teams use this skill to rewrite draft X Articles or create complete X Articles from briefs, then assess them for clarity, engagement, structure, proof, visuals, and closing strength. <br>
