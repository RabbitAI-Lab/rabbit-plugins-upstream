## Description:

Help users turn one clear car photo into a toy-to-real-car AI reveal video on Toy Car to Real Car — a prepared Genjutsu Light effect with no prompt to write.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users with a car, toy, or model photo use the skill to choose a suitable image, follow the product's prepared reveal workflow, review the result, and optionally draft a short social caption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using a third-party service may involve uploading a personal or identifying car photo.

Mitigation: Before uploading, review the service's privacy and retention terms, use a photo you are authorized to share, and consider removing location metadata or obscuring plates, faces, and identifying surroundings.
