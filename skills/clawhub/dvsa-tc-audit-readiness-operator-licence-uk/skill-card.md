## Description: <br>
Builds DVSA/Traffic Commissioner audit readiness checklists, evidence indexes, and gaps registers for UK operator licence scrutiny. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kowl64](https://clawhub.ai/user/kowl64) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External operators and compliance teams use this skill to prepare practical DVSA or Traffic Commissioner audit response materials for a depot, fleet, operating centre, and review period. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
United Kingdom <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for legal advice or treated as complete compliance evidence without local review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the generated materials as operational preparation aids and review them against internal policies, records, and professional advice where needed. <br>
Risk: Audit preparation may involve sensitive business, driver, or compliance information. <br>
Mitigation: Review sensitive business or personal information before sharing it with an agent or including it in generated audit materials. <br>
