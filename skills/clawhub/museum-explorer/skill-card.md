## Description:

Helps plan museum visits, explain exhibits during visits, and summarize visits afterward.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External museum and gallery visitors use this skill before, during, or after a visit to plan routes, understand objects and exhibits, or summarize what they saw. It is intended for explicit museum-visit scenarios and answers in the conversation by default.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Current museum logistics such as hours, tickets, exhibit availability, routes, or accessibility details may be stale or conflicting.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive logistics against official venue, organizer, or collection sources and include the verification date, source link, and uncertainty notes.

Risk: Object identification, attribution, dating, provenance, cultural ownership, religious meaning, human remains, contested history, and repatriation can be sensitive or uncertain.

Mitigation: Distinguish established facts from interpretation, present competing interpretations fairly, and keep identification tentative when the visitor provides incomplete information.

Risk: A visitor may raise urgent on-site safety, access, conservation, or venue-rule issues.

Mitigation: Direct urgent on-site issues to venue staff and posted official guidance, and respect venue rules and conservation requirements.
