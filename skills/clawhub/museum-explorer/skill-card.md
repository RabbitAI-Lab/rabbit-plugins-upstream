## Description:

Helps plan museum visits, explain exhibits during visits, and summarize visits afterward when the user explicitly requests one of those scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External museum visitors use this skill to plan a visit, understand exhibits while on site, or summarize and reflect on a completed visit. It is intended for explicit museum or gallery visit scenarios only.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Current museum logistics or collection information may be outdated or uncertain.

Mitigation: Use read-only web lookup only when current public information is necessary, prefer official sources, include the verification date, and label uncertainty.

Risk: Attribution, provenance, cultural ownership, religious meaning, contested history, or repatriation topics may be sensitive or disputed.

Mitigation: Distinguish established facts from interpretation, present competing interpretations fairly, and keep identification tentative when evidence is incomplete.

Risk: On-site safety, accessibility, or conservation constraints may require local judgment.

Mitigation: Respect venue rules and direct urgent safety or access issues to venue staff and posted official guidance.
