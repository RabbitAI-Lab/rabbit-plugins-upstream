## Description:

Plans museum visits with verified logistics and tailored routes, explains exhibits on site, and turns visitor notes into sourced memories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Museum visitors use the skill to plan timed visits, understand objects during a visit, and organize their own observations afterward. It also offers guidance on sharing visit content while respecting photography and reuse rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Museum hours, exhibitions, ticket rules, and accessibility details may change.

Mitigation: Check current venue or organizer sources, include access dates, and flag unresolved details before departure.

Risk: Sharing images or museum content may conflict with photographer rights or venue rules.

Mitigation: Check the relevant rights and venue rules; use original work when appropriate and do not publish on the visitor's behalf.

Risk: A visitor may mistake planning guidance for a completed booking or other action.

Mitigation: Keep reservations, sign-ins, publishing, and saved files outside the default response; require an explicit request for a handoff or saved output.
