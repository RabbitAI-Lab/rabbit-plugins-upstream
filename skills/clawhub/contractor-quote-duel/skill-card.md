## Description:

Compares two to four contractor bids for the same home project, highlighting scope gaps, unit-price outliers, payment risks, and questions to send each bidder.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners compare written bids for the same renovation, identify missing work and payment concerns, and draft clarification emails before choosing a contractor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (price bands and deposit guidance are US-oriented)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ballpark prices and deposit thresholds may not reflect local markets or legal requirements.

Mitigation: Treat flags as prompts for follow-up; confirm local prices, contract terms, and applicable rules independently.

Risk: Contractor quotes and project documents may contain private information.

Mitigation: Provide only documents intended for analysis and review bidder details before sharing draft emails.
