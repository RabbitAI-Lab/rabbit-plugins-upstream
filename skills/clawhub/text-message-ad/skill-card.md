## Description: <br>
Creates fictional iMessage-style conversation ads as self-contained HTML animations and 1080x1920 MP4 videos with synthesized message sounds for marketing and product demos. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[oasiseng](https://clawhub.ai/user/oasiseng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and developers use this skill to turn product details, brand assets, and a customer pain point into a short fictional chat-style video ad plus optional social captions. It is intended for disclosed dramatizations, not real testimonials, impersonation, or fabricated evidence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated chat-style ads could be mistaken for real conversations or testimonials if presented without adequate disclosure. <br>
Mitigation: Use invented characters, avoid real people and fake testimonials, review generated copy for ad-platform disclosure requirements, and add a Dramatization line when required. <br>
Risk: A generic ad request could be over-converted into a text-message-style ad without confirming the user's intent. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for confirmation when the request is only a generic ad rather than an explicit text-message or chat-style ad request. <br>
