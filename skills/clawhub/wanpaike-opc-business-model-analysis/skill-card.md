## Description: <br>
Uses an OPC-adapted business model canvas, profitability matrix, unit economics, and validation scoring to help assess a startup or one-person company's business model. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Entrepreneurs, founders, and business analysts use this skill to structure a business model, compare monetization paths, calculate LTV/CAC and related unit economics, and produce an action-oriented analysis report. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive business model details, pricing, costs, customer segments, and validation status. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with business information the user is comfortable sharing with the agent and review the generated analysis before acting on it. <br>
Risk: The prompts and expected outputs are primarily Chinese, which can lead to misunderstanding for users who do not read Chinese. <br>
Mitigation: Non-Chinese users should confirm they understand the prompts, assumptions, and output before relying on the analysis. <br>
