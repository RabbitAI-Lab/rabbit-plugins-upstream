## Description:

Provides Malayalam guidance for registering and activating a PowPow account, including help with verification and subscription issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Malayalam-speaking PowPow users use this skill to understand the product, register an account, troubleshoot sign-up issues, and activate a subscription. Users complete forms and payments themselves on PowPow pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat could expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Do not ask users to share credentials or codes; direct them to complete registration and payment on official PowPow pages.

Risk: Registration or subscription instructions may become outdated as PowPow changes.

Mitigation: Direct users to the current official PowPow pages when instructions differ from the live experience.
