## Description:

Guides Malayalam-speaking users through PowPow account registration, product orientation, signup troubleshooting, and subscription activation without running code or handling credentials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand PowPow, register an account, resolve common signup issues, and activate a subscription through the official PowPow pages. Agents use it to provide concise Malayalam guidance while keeping account, verification, and payment actions in the user's browser or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may try to share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while seeking registration help.

Mitigation: Keep signup, verification, and payment on the official PowPow pages and tell users not to share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Risk: Users may assume the agent can complete payment or submit registration on their behalf.

Mitigation: State that the skill is guidance-only and direct users to complete forms, verification, and payment themselves in the browser or app.
