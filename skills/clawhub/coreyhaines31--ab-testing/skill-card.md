## Description:

Helps users plan, design, implement, and analyze A/B tests, split tests, multivariate experiments, and growth experimentation programs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, growth, marketing, and engineering teams use this skill to frame experiment hypotheses, choose metrics, estimate sample size, design variants, document tests, and interpret results without calling tests prematurely.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context files that contain sensitive strategy details.

Mitigation: Review those context files before use and avoid sharing confidential strategy, customer, or roadmap details that are not needed for the experiment.
