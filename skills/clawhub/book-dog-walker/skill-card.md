## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to search for dog-walker services, check provider availability, and create a booking through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Creating a booking may share customer contact details with Lokuli and the selected provider. <br>
Mitigation: Get user confirmation before booking and submit only the contact details needed for the service. <br>
Risk: A booking could be made for the wrong provider, date, time, or terms if the request details are incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, date, time slot, and any terms with the user before calling the booking tool. <br>
