## Description: <br>
Ai Image Gen Free helps agents generate text-to-image image files through a configured Gemini Image API workflow with standard and 2K resolution options across multiple aspect ratios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, and developers use this skill to turn text prompts into image files for avatars, wallpapers, banners, drafts, and lightweight visual ideation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Image prompts and generation settings are sent to the configured external Gemini Image API endpoint. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including secrets, private personal data, or confidential business content in prompts or generation settings. <br>
Risk: The artifact references an execution script, but only SKILL.md is present in the evidence package. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the referenced script and its behavior before relying on the skill operationally. <br>
Risk: Generated image rights and commercial use depend on the external image service terms. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm applicable Gemini Image service terms before using generated images commercially. <br>
