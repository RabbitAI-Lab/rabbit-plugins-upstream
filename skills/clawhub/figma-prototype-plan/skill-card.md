## Description: <br>
Plan prototype interactions and flows for user testing in Figma. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, designers, researchers, and agents use this skill to scope Figma prototypes for usability testing, define interactions, prepare task scripts, and decide what to build versus fake. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prototype plans may omit needed interactions or over-scope the test if the research question and tasks are unclear. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated scope, task scripts, success criteria, and reset process before applying the plan in Figma. <br>
Risk: The skill provides recommendations but does not connect to Figma or modify files automatically. <br>
Mitigation: Manually apply the plan in Figma and verify each interaction, destination frame, and participant reset path before testing. <br>
