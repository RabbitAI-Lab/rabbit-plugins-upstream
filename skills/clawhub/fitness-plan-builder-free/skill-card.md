Individuals seeking a weekly fitness plan use this skill to tailor workouts to their goals, experience, equipment, and available time, with exercise form cues and general nutrition and recovery guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional paid integration sends requests to an external service and may incur charges.

Mitigation: Disclose the API and per-use price, and obtain user approval before sending requests or paying.

Risk: The payment setup runs an unpinned executable npm installer.

Mitigation: Review the installer and use a pinned, trusted version rather than executing the latest release blindly.

Risk: An unsuitable workout could aggravate an existing injury or encourage excessive exertion.

Mitigation: Present plans as non-medical guidance, start beginners at low intensity, and defer to medical advice for injuries.
