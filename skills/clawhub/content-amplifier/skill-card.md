## Description: <br>
Content Amplifier helps agents turn approved creator content into paid amplification plans or multi-channel repurposing plans with scorecards, budget allocation, targeting, rights tracking, transformation specs, and distribution calendars. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams and agents use this skill after creator content is already approved to decide what to amplify with paid media, set up creator authorization paths, allocate budgets, and repurpose UGC across ads, website, email, social, and sales materials. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Paid media, creator authorization, CRM audience use, and content reuse recommendations can affect spend, permissions, audience targeting, or rights obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations involving ad spend, creator permissions, CRM audiences, or reuse rights before acting; keep decisions user-directed. <br>
Risk: Repurposing assets without confirmed usage rights can create compliance or contractual issues. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm rights level and expiration before recommending ad, website, or email reuse; ask for rights when they are unknown. <br>
Risk: Missing or estimated performance metrics can make budget and optimization guidance look more certain than the inputs support. <br>
Mitigation: Label metrics as Measured, User-provided, or Estimated, and ask for exports when reach, engagement, CPM, ROAS, or rights dates are missing. <br>
