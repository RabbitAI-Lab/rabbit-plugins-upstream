## Description:

Guides an agent through turning a short reference clip and character images into a recast video, from prompt design and generation to comparison and export.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators use the skill to plan and produce character-swapped remakes of short clips with AI video services, then review and export the result. It is suited to parody and motion-control video workflows using reference footage and cast images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference clips, cast photos, or prompts may contain sensitive material that is sent to external generation services.

Mitigation: Check what each service receives and avoid sensitive media unless you have permission to share it and are comfortable with the service handling it.

Risk: Changing routes or inputs after a moderation refusal could be used to evade a provider's safeguards.

Mitigation: Treat a provider refusal as a stop signal; change the creative request instead of trying another route to get the refused content accepted.

Risk: The documented installer command is not pinned to a specific release.

Mitigation: Review the installation source and selected release before installing.
