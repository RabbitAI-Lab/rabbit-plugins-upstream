## Description: <br>
Nine-Level Cognitive Tower helps agents guide users through domain-specific cognitive self-assessment and a 30-day experiment for developing independent judgment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, managers, founders, and individual contributors use this skill to identify cognitive-development bottlenecks, map observable behavior to a current level, and design a focused 30-day practice experiment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the framework for a validated psychological diagnostic tool. <br>
Mitigation: Present it as a reflective coaching and self-assessment framework, and avoid using it for clinical, employment, or other high-stakes diagnosis. <br>
Risk: Users may overrate themselves or use levels to rank other people. <br>
Mitigation: Require specific behavioral evidence, median-work assessment, peer calibration, and a development-focused 30-day experiment. <br>
