## Description:

Guides users through a nine-level cognitive development framework to assess work behaviors and design a 30-day growth experiment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals, founders, managers, and coaches use the framework to examine concrete decisions, identify a specific thinking behavior to develop, and plan a measurable 30-day practice. It is intended for reflection and development rather than ranking people.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the nine-level framework for an empirically validated psychological scale or use it to rank people.

Mitigation: Treat the levels as a reflective development aid, not a validated measure; ground assessments in observed behavior and do not use them to rank or demote people.

Risk: Self-assessments can overstate a person's usual level when based on aspirational self-placement or a best-case example.

Mitigation: Assess the median of recent work using specific examples, and use peer calibration as the skill recommends.
