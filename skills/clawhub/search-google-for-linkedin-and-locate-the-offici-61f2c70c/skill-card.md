## Description: <br>
Search Google for LinkedIn in Chrome and identify the official LinkedIn log in or sign up result. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[luckill](https://clawhub.ai/user/luckill) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to find the official LinkedIn entry point from Google search results without signing in. It is intended for locating the visible linkedin.com log in or sign up result, not for credential entry or account access. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Google results can include sponsored, ambiguous, or misleading entries. <br>
Mitigation: Before relying on the result, visually confirm that the destination is linkedin.com or an expected LinkedIn subdomain and stop if the official domain is not clearly shown. <br>
