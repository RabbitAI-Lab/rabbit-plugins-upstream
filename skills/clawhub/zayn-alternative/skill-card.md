## Description: <br>
Compares alternative hardware, equipment, spare parts, and second-hand products when the original model, part number, configuration, brand, condition, price, or lead time cannot meet the request. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales, sourcing, quotation, and operations teams use this skill to structure comparisons between a requested hardware item and candidate substitutes. It helps separate confirmed differences from items that require customer or human review before compatibility, pricing, warranty, stock, lead time, or acceptance decisions are made. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A candidate substitute may be incorrectly treated as compatible or customer-acceptable. <br>
Mitigation: Keep compatibility, pricing, stock, warranty, lead time, and customer acceptance decisions under normal human review. <br>
Risk: RFQ, quotation, or product details supplied by users may include sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Use existing data-handling controls for business inputs and avoid providing credentials; the skill does not request credentials or automate decisions. <br>
