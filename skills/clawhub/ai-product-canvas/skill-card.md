## Description: <br>
Structures AI and ML product decisions into a canvas covering problem definition, model approach, data requirements, evaluation, UX, responsible AI, and launch monitoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, AI leads, and product teams use this skill to plan AI-powered features and assess readiness before build or launch. It guides users through problem framing, model choice, data needs, evaluation criteria, responsible AI checks, and operational monitoring. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide confidential or regulated product, user, data, or ownership details while filling out the planning canvas. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in workspaces and model-use contexts approved for that information, and avoid entering confidential or regulated data unless policy allows it. <br>
Risk: AI product plans can become misleading if teams skip accuracy thresholds, fallback behavior, privacy review, or responsible AI checks. <br>
Mitigation: Complete the canvas sections for evaluation thresholds, fallback UX, data privacy, responsible AI, launch monitoring, and human review before relying on the plan. <br>
