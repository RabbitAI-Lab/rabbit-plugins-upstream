## Description:

Builds qualified company and contact lists from an ideal customer profile or named sources, with verified email statuses and a cost record.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and go-to-market teams use this skill to identify companies that fit their target profile and find relevant contacts, while tracking data-provider costs and exclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid external data lookups can incur unexpected prospecting costs.

Mitigation: Confirm the provider and budget, set row and spend limits, and pilot a small sample before scaling.

Risk: Contact records can contain personal emails or phone numbers.

Mitigation: Handle contact details according to applicable privacy and outreach requirements, and check email-verification status before use.
