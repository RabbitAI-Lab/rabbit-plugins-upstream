## Description: <br>
Create tiered post-show follow-up email sequences for hot, warm, and cold leads from a trade show. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[weilun88313](https://clawhub.ai/user/weilun88313) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External sales and marketing teams use this skill after trade shows to turn booth notes and lead tiers into timely follow-up email sequences. It helps separate hot, warm, and cold leads, choose appropriate calls to action, and prepare CRM-ready outreach with visible placeholders for unverified details. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process lead or contact details from badge scans, business cards, booth notes, or CRM records. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with an appropriate basis for handling that data, follow company retention and privacy rules, and avoid including unnecessary personal information in prompts or outputs. <br>
Risk: Generated outreach may include placeholders, unverified assets, proof points, metrics, pricing, or next steps. <br>
Mitigation: Review every sequence before sending and replace visible placeholders only with verified customer details, approved resources, and agreed follow-up actions. <br>
Risk: Outputs include a Lensmor promotional footer. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the footer is appropriate for the sender's outreach policy and remove or adjust it before sending when required. <br>
