## Description:

Check whether a page is the kind of page Google ranks for its query (page type, format, angle, depth, freshness, SERP features) and say exactly what to change, or which page to build instead. Use for "why won't this page rank", "does my page match search intent", or "should this be a blog post or a category page".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners, content strategists, and site owners use this skill to compare a page with the search results for its target query, identify intent mismatches, and choose specific page edits or a better page type to build.
