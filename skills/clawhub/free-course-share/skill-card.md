## Description:

Verify whether a course is truly free, screen or compare free courses, build a shortlist, or draft accurate share copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners, educators, and people sharing learning resources use this skill to verify free-course claims, compare options, build shortlists, and draft reviewable course-sharing copy from verified or clearly labeled facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course access, certificate terms, deadlines, and eligibility can change after verification.

Mitigation: Check current official course or provider pages and include verification dates for time-sensitive claims.

Risk: Draft sharing copy could overstate free access, certificate value, or urgency if source facts are incomplete.

Mitigation: Use only verified or visibly labeled user-provided facts, keep restrictions near each claim, and have a person review copy before sharing.

Risk: A course page may require sign-in, enrollment, payment, form submission, or posting to confirm details.

Mitigation: Keep use read-only and stop at public verification; do not sign in, enroll, buy, submit forms, or post content.
