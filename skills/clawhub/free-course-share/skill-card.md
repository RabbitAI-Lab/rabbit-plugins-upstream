## Description:

Verifies and organizes free-course facts, then drafts sharing copy when the user asks for both course curation and share-copy creation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People curating public free-course options use this skill to verify access claims, screen candidates, and draft shareable copy for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course availability or free-access terms may change or be ambiguous, which can make shared recommendations inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current public course or provider pages, define what is free, label uncertainty, and include material restrictions near related claims.

Risk: Draft sharing copy may contain unsupported or overbroad claims if evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Keep every material claim supported or visibly labeled, preserve caveats, and present the share copy as a draft for review.

Risk: A user may expect the skill to post content, modify files, or use private accounts.

Mitigation: Keep the workflow read-only in the conversation and do not perform external actions beyond public fact verification.
