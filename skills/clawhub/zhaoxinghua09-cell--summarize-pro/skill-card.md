## Description:

Turns long-form content into faithful, purpose-specific summaries such as meeting minutes, comparison tables, timelines, and action lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People summarizing meetings, documents, or multiple proposals use this skill to produce audience-appropriate minutes, comparisons, timelines, outlines, or action lists while keeping conclusions tied to source material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
