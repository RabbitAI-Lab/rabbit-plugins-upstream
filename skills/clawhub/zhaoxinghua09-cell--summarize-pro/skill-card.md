## Description:

Summarizes transcripts and long text into intent-matched minutes, comparisons, timelines, bullets, or cards, with source traceability and uncertainty labels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

People who work with meeting transcripts, notes, or long documents can use the skill to create faithful summaries, decision and action records, comparisons, timelines, key points, or knowledge cards. It flags uncertainty and supports checking generated statements against the source.
