## Description: <br>
Create vertical mixed-cut promotional videos from user-provided art or drawing clips with Chinese male voiceover, synchronized Chinese subtitles, and low original background audio. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[extraskittles](https://clawhub.ai/user/extraskittles) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators and marketers use this skill to produce stable vertical promotional MP4 videos from art supplies, drawing tools, sketching lessons, or painting material clips they provide. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The source videos or TTS voice may not be cleared for promotional use. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm media and voice rights before publishing the generated promotional video. <br>
Risk: Generated promotional copy or subtitles may be inaccurate or unsuitable for the final audience. <br>
Mitigation: Review the final copy, subtitles, timing, and product claims before release. <br>
Risk: The skill processes user-provided media files. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only media intended for editing and avoid including sensitive or unauthorized content. <br>
