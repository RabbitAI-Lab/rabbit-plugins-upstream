## Description:

Find b-roll in a user's own footage library for voiceovers, interviews, podcasts, or talking-head videos by matching narration beats to cutaway shots locally on Mac or Windows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[reelary](https://clawhub.ai/user/reelary)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video editors and creators use this skill to find suitable cutaway clips in their own footage for each beat of a narration. It presents a best match, an alternative, and timestamps, and flags beats without a good match.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
