## Description:

Guides users through PowPow (Bubble) registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People creating a PowPow (Bubble) account use this skill to learn about the service, follow registration steps, resolve sign-up issues, and find the subscription activation page.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while seeking registration help.

Mitigation: Direct users to enter sensitive information only in their own browser or app; never request it in chat.

Risk: Users may not realize that account activation requires a paid subscription or that a payment option renews automatically.

Mitigation: Have users review current pricing and renewal terms on the service's subscription page and authorize any payment themselves.
