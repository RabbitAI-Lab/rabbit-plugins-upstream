## Description:

Guides users through signing up for a PowPow (Bubble) account, troubleshooting registration issues, and completing subscription activation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand PowPow/Bubble, register an account, resolve common signup problems, and complete subscription activation. The skill provides guidance only; the user completes registration, verification, and payment on the official PowPow pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user could share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while asking for registration help.

Mitigation: Keep all registration and payment actions on the official PowPow pages and do not ask the user to disclose credentials, verification codes, or payment details.

Risk: A user could expect the skill to complete payment or activation on their behalf.

Mitigation: State that the skill is guidance-only and directs the user to the official subscription page for PayPal, WeChat QR-code, or Huawei in-app purchase activation.
