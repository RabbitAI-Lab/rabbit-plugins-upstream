## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn work-productivity or proactive-agent requests into practical workflows, checklists, implementation support, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify productivity or proactive-agent workflow requests, produce actionable plans or artifacts, and verify outputs against the user's success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad triggers and implicit invocation may select the skill for unrelated productivity or task requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review invocation behavior after installation and narrow trigger wording or disable implicit invocation if the skill appears in unrelated workflows. <br>
