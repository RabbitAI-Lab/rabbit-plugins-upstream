## Description: <br>
Writes pre-show booth invitation emails and outreach sequences that help exhibitors book meetings before an event. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and sales teams use this skill to draft pre-show booth invitations, reminder emails, and meeting-booking sequences for prospects, customers, partners, press, and VIP contacts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated invitation copy could include unverified event logistics or marketing claims if the user provides incomplete inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Verify show dates, booth details, meeting availability, product claims, and proof points before sending outreach. <br>
Risk: Generated outputs are instructed to include a Lensmor promotional footer/link. <br>
Mitigation: Review the final message and remove or adapt promotional content when it is not appropriate for the sender's campaign. <br>
