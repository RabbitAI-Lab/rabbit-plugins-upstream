## Description: <br>
Search cloud drive resources (Quark, Baidu, Aliyun, Xunlei) indexed on pan.xiaozi.cc. Use when users want to find shared files, movies, TV shows, software, or documents across Chinese cloud storage platforms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Third-party cloud-drive links may involve legal, copyright, credential-phishing, malware, or unsafe-download risks. <br>
Mitigation: Verify legality before accessing results, avoid pirated media or cracked software, check destination domains, and do not enter credentials or download or run files from unfamiliar pages. <br>
Risk: Search results may be stale, unavailable, or incomplete because the skill depends on an external public index. <br>
Mitigation: Present only content returned by the search page, include complete resource detail URLs, and advise users to refine keywords when no results are found. <br>
