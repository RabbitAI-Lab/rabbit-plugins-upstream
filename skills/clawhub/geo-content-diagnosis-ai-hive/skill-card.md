This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External website operators, brand content teams, and AI search growth consultants use this skill to turn existing pages or text, target questions, public evidence, audience definitions, and conversion goals into GEO/AEO diagnostics, structured content updates, runnable commands, and verification checklists.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional AI-HIVE generation can use API keys, upload media, and submit billable generation tasks.

Mitigation: Confirm prompts, routing, batch size, reference files, media authorization, and API-key handling before running generation.

Risk: Content optimization outputs can include marketing claims, source gaps, or search visibility guidance that may be misunderstood as guaranteed performance.

Mitigation: Keep observable facts, inferences, and unverified items separate; require human review and avoid promises of rankings, sales, approvals, or model citations.
