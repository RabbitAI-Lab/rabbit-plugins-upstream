## Description: <br>
Create a content style guide and voice and tone guide so teams can write consistently across UI, documentation, marketing, support, and other content surfaces. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content designers, UX writers, product managers, documentation teams, support teams, and marketers use this skill to create a practical content style guide with voice principles, tone-by-context guidance, mechanics, terminology, inclusivity and accessibility rules, and a quick reference. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: An incomplete brief may lead the skill to infer brand voice or terminology that does not match the team's standards. <br>
Mitigation: Review and confirm any inferred brand, audience, voice, or terminology choices before adopting the guide. <br>
