## Description: <br>
Investoday helps agents retrieve and analyze Chinese financial market data across stocks, Hong Kong equities, funds, indices, valuation, attribution, and market briefings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cooperiano](https://clawhub.ai/user/cooperiano) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use this skill to answer finance-analysis prompts, run valuation and fund-attribution workflows, and produce market briefings from Investoday financial data APIs. Outputs should be treated as market analysis rather than investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill depends on the investoday-api PyPI package for runtime behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the package source and version before installation. <br>
Risk: Finance-analysis workflows may invite users to share sensitive account or brokerage information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing brokerage credentials or personal financial account data. <br>
Risk: Market analysis can be mistaken for financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational market analysis only, not investment advice. <br>
