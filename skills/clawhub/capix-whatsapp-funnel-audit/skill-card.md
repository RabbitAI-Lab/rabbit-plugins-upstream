## Description:

Audit Indonesian landing-page and Click-to-WhatsApp funnels, then prioritize fixes for lead quality, tracking, and follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Indonesian businesses and marketing teams use this skill to audit landing-page and WhatsApp conversion journeys, prioritize funnel repairs, and plan measurement and follow-up improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private analytics, customer details, or access credentials could be exposed in audit inputs or reports.

Mitigation: Share only necessary data and remove or redact secrets and personal information from reports.

Risk: Outdated platform or product details could lead to incorrect implementation recommendations.

Mitigation: Verify current Meta, Google, TikTok, WhatsApp, and Capix requirements against official documentation before implementing changes.

Risk: Changes to live funnels or outreach could affect customers without approval.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval immediately before sending outreach, submitting forms, or changing production settings.
