## Description:

Audits Indonesian landing-page and Click-to-WhatsApp funnels and prioritizes fixes for lead quality, tracking, and follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hanifpram-ux](https://clawhub.ai/user/hanifpram-ux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Indonesian business owners and marketing teams use this skill to audit landing pages and WhatsApp sales journeys, identify conversion and attribution gaps, and plan evidence-based improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Indonesia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer data, phone numbers, or access tokens could be exposed during an audit.

Mitigation: Use public or redacted funnel data when possible; avoid sharing secrets and omit sensitive details from reports.

Risk: Changes to live domains, ad pixels, or WhatsApp routing could interrupt lead capture or reporting.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before production changes and use a verified backup and rollback plan for migrations.
