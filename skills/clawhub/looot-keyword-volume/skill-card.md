## Description:

Check keyword volume and CPC.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[walidboulanouar](https://clawhub.ai/user/walidboulanouar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners and content planners use the skill to compare monthly search volume, CPC, paid competition, intent, and ranking difficulty for keyword lists in a chosen country and language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keyword lookup calls incur a per-call charge, and search-volume estimates are not a forecast of website traffic.

Mitigation: Show the endpoint price and get user approval before running; present volume as a relative estimate for the selected country, not as a traffic promise.
