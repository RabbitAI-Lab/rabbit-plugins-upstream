## Description: <br>
Generate viral Chinese short-fiction opening hooks and concise micro-fiction copy from a scene idea. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jinghong137](https://clawhub.ai/user/jinghong137) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and creators use this skill to turn a Chinese short-fiction scene idea into five selectable opening hooks, then produce a final micro-fiction copy under 200 Chinese characters after a selection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate automatically for similar fiction-opening requests and shape the response into its five-option hook format. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when Chinese short-fiction hook generation is desired, and review invocation behavior before enabling it in contexts where a neutral writing assistant is expected. <br>
