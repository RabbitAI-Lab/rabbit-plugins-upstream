## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process private journals, relationship notes, and other sensitive content.

Mitigation: Use reply-only mode and narrow source, directory, time, and count settings when possible; avoid third-party private, crisis, or medical information unless there is a clear reason and consent.

Risk: The default append mode may write analysis back into note files.

Mitigation: Use reply-only or separate output while reviewing results, and keep backups before allowing append-mode writes.

Risk: Reflective mental-health or personality-oriented analysis can be misleading if treated as diagnosis or professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as reflective guidance only and seek qualified professional support for crisis, medical, or clinical concerns.
