## Use Case: <br>
Professionals, teams, and developers use this skill to turn Dify or chat2dify productivity requests into reusable plans, templates, automation outlines, checklists, code changes, or decision support. It is intended to make assumptions, constraints, validation criteria, and follow-up work visible in the final output. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The public name suggests USA business migration planning, while the instructions focus on Dify and chat2dify productivity workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the intended use before installing or invoking the skill, and rename or rescope it before broad distribution. <br>
Risk: Broad implicit triggers could activate the skill for requests that only loosely mention related productivity or Dify terms. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow the trigger keywords and review invocations before relying on generated workflow or implementation guidance. <br>
