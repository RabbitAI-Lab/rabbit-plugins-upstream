## Description:

食品安全抽检检测合规护栏。在食品安全监督抽检报告、食品检验报告、食品快检结果发布前，实时检测是否符合《食品安全法》《食品安全抽样检验管理办法》及GB 2760/GB 2761/GB 2762等食品安全国家标准要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

## Use Case:

Food safety, inspection, quality, and compliance teams use this skill to review Chinese food safety sampling, inspection, quick-test, and public disclosure text before release. It checks report content against stated Chinese food safety laws and GB standards, flags noncompliant wording or process gaps, and provides revision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may cite or apply Chinese food safety laws and GB standards that are outdated for a user's specific report or enforcement context.

Mitigation: Verify the current law, regulation, and standard text for the jurisdiction and product category before relying on the review.

Risk: Users may treat the compliance review report as a substitute for qualified legal or regulatory judgment.

Mitigation: Use the output as a drafting and screening aid, then route material compliance decisions through qualified legal, regulatory, or food safety reviewers.
