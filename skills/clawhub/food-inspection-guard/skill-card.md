## Description:

食品安全抽检检测合规护栏。在食品安全监督抽检报告、食品检验报告、食品快检结果发布前，实时检测是否符合《食品安全法》《食品安全抽样检验管理办法》及GB 2760/GB 2761/GB 2762等食品安全国家标准要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

## Use Case:

Food safety, inspection, compliance, and publication teams use this skill to review Chinese food-safety inspection reports, sampling records, quick-test disclosures, and related public materials before release. It flags noncompliant language or missing procedural elements and proposes corrections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for workflows applying Chinese food-safety inspection and sampling requirements.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance guidance may be incorrect or outdated if Chinese food-safety laws, sampling rules, or GB standards have changed.

Mitigation: Verify current Chinese food-safety laws and standards before relying on the skill for publication or enforcement decisions.

Risk: Users may treat the skill as legal or regulatory authority instead of a compliance aid.

Mitigation: Require qualified human review for final reports, public risk information, enforcement use, and other regulated communications.
