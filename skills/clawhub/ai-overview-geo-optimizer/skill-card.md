## Description:

This skill provides comprehensive instructions for analyzing, restructuring, and optimizing content specifically for Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, and ChatGPT Search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[uzairseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/uzairseo)

## Use Case:

SEO specialists, content strategists, and webmasters use the skill to analyze and restructure draft content for AI-search visibility, including direct-answer sections, entity and citation recommendations, and structured data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested citations, statistics, credentials, testing claims, or structured data may be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims and citations against authoritative sources, validate structured data, and review the result before publishing.
