## Description:

Generates complete ChatGPT-ready prompt packages for AI industry infographics, including topic design, parallel web research, data verification, structured prompt writing, and social media copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External content creators, content teams, and AI industry analysts use this skill to build data-rich infographic material packs about topics such as LLM markets, embodied AI, chip supply chains, compute costs, and industry history.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad activation triggers that could be invoked for ordinary prompt-writing requests.

Mitigation: Use it only for AI-industry infographic material packs and confirm the requested task needs the full research and verification workflow.

Risk: Generated infographic packages can contain incorrect or misleading industry data if source checks are skipped.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's source grading, red-flag pre-screen, and multi-round verification gates before using any data in a public infographic.
