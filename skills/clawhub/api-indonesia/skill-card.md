## Description:

Look up Indonesian public data through API Indonesia (apiindonesia.id). Use when a task needs Indonesian administrative regions (provinces down to villages) and postal codes, region boundaries (GeoJSON), public holidays and cuti bersama, prayer times, Hijri dates, schools (NPSN), universities, hospitals, BMKG earthquakes, weather and warnings, air quality, Bank Indonesia exchange rates, minimum wage (UMP), NIK/NPWP format checks, QRIS parsing, rupiah formatting and terbilang, halal (BPJPH) and BPOM lookups, DJKI trademarks, OJK legal and illegal investment lists, food prices, Transjakarta routes, KBBI, Al-Quran or Alkitab.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
