## Use Case: <br>
External homeowners and renovation planners use this skill to get practical, step-by-step Chinese-language guidance for residential renovation stages, timeline planning, acceptance checks, and common pitfalls. It is intended as planning support rather than a substitute for local building-code, legal, or professional safety review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Renovation guidance may be applied too broadly despite local building-code, safety, legal, property-management, or site-specific requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as planning support and verify safety-critical, legal, structural, and code-sensitive decisions with qualified local professionals and current local rules. <br>
Risk: The skill may answer common renovation questions in a strong advisory style, which can make uncertain or context-dependent recommendations sound final. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations against the user's actual renovation plan, property constraints, and contractor documentation before acting. <br>
