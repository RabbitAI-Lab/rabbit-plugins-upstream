# S3: 卖家精灵流量词反查（含翻页）

## 输入
competitors: S1竞品ASIN列表, target_asin

## 依赖
S1

## 操作
1. 对每个ASIN调用linkfox-sellersprite-traffic-keyword
   - 参数: marketplace, asin, size=100, orderField=rankPosition, orderDesc=false
2. 翻页逻辑: 若返回100条则继续翻页，直到<100条停止
3. 提取每个关键词的: keyword, trafficPercentage, rankPosition(page/position), badges, trafficKeywordType, conversionKeywordType, searches, bid, supplyDemandRatio

## 输出
traffic_keywords: {asin: [{keyword, traffic_pct, page, position, badges, traf_type, conv_type, searches, bid, sdr}]}

## 用途
被S6(首页词归因分析)和S7(交叉对比)消费
