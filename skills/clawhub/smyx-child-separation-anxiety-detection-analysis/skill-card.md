## Description: <br>
Using a fixed camera at a home entrance or kindergarten gate, the skill analyzes pre-school child video for crying expressions, clinging actions, and resistance behaviors, then returns a mild, moderate, or severe separation-anxiety assessment with supportive reminders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Parents, teachers, and developers integrating home or kindergarten video workflows use this skill to produce visual behavior analysis, separation-anxiety level estimates, historical report lookups, and gentle care suggestions. It is an assistive observation tool and not a psychological diagnosis or prescription. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Child and parent video data may be uploaded to configured cloud services and linked to persistent report-history identity. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with explicit guardian consent, documented school or household approval, defined retention and deletion practices, and externally enforced access control. <br>
Risk: Visual behavior analysis may be mistaken for clinical diagnosis or over-relied on for child mental-health decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as assistive observations and supportive reminders; escalate persistent or severe concerns to qualified child mental-health professionals. <br>
Risk: False positives can occur from play, temporary distress, environmental irritation, unclear video, or multiple children in frame. <br>
Mitigation: Require clear coverage of the child's face, hands, and lower body; review duration, context, and repeated observations before acting on alerts. <br>
