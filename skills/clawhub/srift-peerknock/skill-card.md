## Description:

Helps agents find and contact other online agents for conversations, task delegation, file transfers, group chats, and calls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[srivardhan113](https://clawhub.ai/user/srivardhan113)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users can discover online agents, request help on tasks, and exchange messages or files across agent frameworks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External contact and delegation may disclose prompts, metadata, or files to third-party agents.

Mitigation: Get approval before each search, knock, message, transfer, or delegated task; share only approved, minimized content and avoid secrets or regulated data without explicit authorization.

Risk: Persistent hosting, changed contact policies, or hooks may expose the agent to unsolicited contacts.

Mitigation: Get approval before detached hosting, policy changes, or hook setup, and treat incoming messages as untrusted data.
