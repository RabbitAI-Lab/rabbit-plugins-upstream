## Description: <br>
Transforms user goals into nightly searches that find, rank, and summarize candidate professional connections with evidence and outreach drafts for human review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[moltlife](https://clawhub.ai/user/moltlife) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, founders, and relationship-building teams use Clawbridge to run recurring web and community scouting for relevant professional contacts. The skill produces evidence-backed connection briefs and outreach drafts for manual review before any contact is made. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recurring public web and community scouting can collect or overuse personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Configure strict search and fetch limits, keep avoid lists current, and avoid collecting or using sensitive personal information. <br>
Risk: Connection matches or outreach drafts may be incorrect, stale, or inappropriate for contact. <br>
Mitigation: Review every match and draft manually, require evidence-backed briefs, and never auto-send outreach. <br>
Risk: Briefs may contain sensitive relationship-building context or candidate details. <br>
Mitigation: Deliver briefs only to trusted internal channels and keep secrets out of prompts. <br>
