## Description:

Helps teams navigate EU AI Act risk categories, role-specific obligations, and compliance milestones using guidance and an optional local classifier.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and compliance teams evaluating AI systems for the EU market use this skill to get a preliminary risk classification, role-based obligation checklist, and milestone overview. It is not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (guidance concerns AI systems placed on the EU market or deployed in the EU)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Preliminary keyword-based risk classifications and fixed timelines may omit obligations or become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify classifications, deadlines, and obligations against current official EU sources and obtain qualified review before compliance decisions.

Risk: The skill declares broad agent tool permissions and may receive sensitive business use cases.

Mitigation: Restrict tool access as needed and avoid sharing sensitive business data unless processing remains local in the agent environment.
