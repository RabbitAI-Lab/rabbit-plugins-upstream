## Description:

Helps navigate EU AI Act risk tiers, role-specific obligations, and compliance milestones for AI systems entering or operating in the EU.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Developers and compliance teams assessing an AI use case for the EU market can use this skill to get an initial risk classification, role-based obligations, and key regulatory milestones. It is a navigation aid, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (for EU AI Act questions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A brief use-case description may lead to an incorrect risk classification or incomplete role-specific obligations.

Mitigation: Treat the result as preliminary guidance and verify the classification and duties against official EU sources or qualified legal advice.

Risk: Listed regulatory milestones may not reflect subsequent changes or the user's specific situation.

Mitigation: Check current official EU guidance and applicable dates before making compliance decisions.
