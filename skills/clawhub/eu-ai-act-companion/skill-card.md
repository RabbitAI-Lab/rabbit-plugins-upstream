## Description:

Provides Chinese-first EU AI Act navigation with indicative risk classification, role-based obligations, and compliance milestones for AI use cases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and AI product teams considering EU market entry or deployment can explore indicative risk levels, provider or deployer duties, and compliance milestones. Outputs are a starting point for review, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (for AI systems placed on the EU market or deployed in the EU)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Indicative classifications and role duties may be inaccurate for a specific product.

Mitigation: Verify classifications and obligations against current official EU sources or qualified counsel before product decisions.

Risk: Listed milestones may become outdated or omit applicable requirements.

Mitigation: Check current official EU deadlines and requirements before relying on the timeline.
