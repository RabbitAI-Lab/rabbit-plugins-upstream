## Description: <br>
Helps agents plan, create, repurpose, schedule, and optimize social media content across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and similar platforms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and business teams use this skill to shape goals, audience, brand voice, content pillars, platform-specific posts, calendars, and optimization plans for social media campaigns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill claims access to scheduling and publishing workflows, which could result in unreviewed or incorrect social posts if treated as autonomous publishing authority. <br>
Mitigation: Use it draft-first, manually approve every post or scheduled item, and confirm the destination account and platform before publishing. <br>
Risk: The skill encourages large-scale collection of social content for analysis without clearly stating data-source or platform safeguards. <br>
Mitigation: Use only lawful, platform-approved exports or APIs for content research and avoid scraping approaches that violate platform terms or privacy expectations. <br>
