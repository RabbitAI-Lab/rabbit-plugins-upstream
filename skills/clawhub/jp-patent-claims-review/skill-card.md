## Description:

Reviews Japanese patent-application claim documents against Japanese Patent Act and JPO examination-practice criteria, uses patent-search skills for novelty and inventive-step review, and produces a complete HTML review report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Patent professionals and teams preparing Japanese patent filings use this skill to review uploaded PDF, Word, or text claim documents for statutory compliance, drafting quality, prior-art risk, foreign-filing adaptation, and protection strategy. It can produce a structured HTML report with findings, ratings, citations, and suggested revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded patent documents and generated reports may contain confidential or pre-publication invention details.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is comfortable uploading the materials, limit access to the agent workspace and configured tools, and protect or delete generated HTML reports when no longer needed.

Risk: Novelty and inventive-step conclusions depend on configured Patsnap MCP/search tools and the retrieved prior-art results.

Mitigation: Verify tool authorization and review the cited search results before relying on any rights-stability conclusions.

Risk: The skill supports patent-claims review but does not replace formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Have a qualified Japanese patent professional review the report before filing, amendment, prosecution response, or business use.
