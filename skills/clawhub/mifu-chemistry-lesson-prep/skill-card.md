## Use Case:

AI learning coaches use this skill to prepare Chinese-language chemistry lessons for a chosen grade level, region, topic, and student profile. It produces coach-facing lesson plans and student-facing guided learning materials using chemistry-specific workflows such as equation checks, conversion networks, standard answer language, and regional exam adapters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with content tailored to Chinese chemistry curricula and Shanghai Gaokao assumptions unless adapted.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is tuned for Chinese-language chemistry lesson preparation and Shanghai Gaokao assumptions, while other regional adapters are less complete.

Mitigation: Review and adapt curriculum language, exam structure, textbook alignment, and regional assumptions before using it outside the intended context.

Risk: Lesson plans may include chemistry equations or explanations that affect student understanding if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Have the coach verify equations, conditions, conservation checks, and standard answer wording before classroom delivery.
