## Description:

Turns Chinese-language invention ideas or R&D materials into patent technical disclosure drafts, supporting notes, and suggested invention directions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Inventors, R&D teams, and patent professionals use the skill to turn technical materials or early ideas into Chinese-language disclosure drafts and invention directions for review with a patent agent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or unfiled invention materials are sent to a remote service whose data handling is not clearly disclosed.

Mitigation: Review the provider's handling terms and submit only materials you are willing to share; avoid unfiled secrets until you understand the terms.

Risk: API credentials or materials may be exposed if sent to an untrusted configured API host.

Mitigation: Keep PATENTMAX_API_KEY private and only set PATENTMAX_BASE_URL for a destination you fully trust.

Risk: Drafts can contain inferred technical details, and invention-point comparisons are not a patentability determination.

Mitigation: Confirm inferred details in the review notes and have a patent professional assess the draft and any patentability conclusions before filing.
