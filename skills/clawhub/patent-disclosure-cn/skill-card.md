## Description:

Turns R&D materials or an early-stage idea into a Chinese patent technical-disclosure draft for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, inventors, and organizations use the skill to organize technical materials or explore an initial idea into a structured invention-disclosure draft and review notes for a patent professional. It can also suggest possible directions when the user has only an idea; its drafts are not patent applications or legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow handles sensitive invention materials, and the provider's privacy policy describes retention for the account lifetime and deletion within 30 days after account closure.

Mitigation: Review the provider's privacy policy and approve the exact materials before submission.

Risk: The security assessment flags API-key storage and network safeguards for review.

Mitigation: Keep permanent keys out of chat, use a restricted key through the environment, and protect the local cache.

Risk: Submitting a disclosure is a paid task.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact materials and charges before submission.
