## Description:

Helps users turn R&D materials or an idea into a patent technical disclosure in Word.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Inventors, researchers, and developers can use the skill to organize technical materials or an early-stage idea into an invention disclosure for review by a patent professional. It also provides review notes that identify system inferences and information needing confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags API-key storage and transmission safeguards for review. Uploaded documents are retained for the account lifetime and deleted within 30 days after account closure, according to the cited privacy policy.

Mitigation: Use a release that restricts authenticated redirects and securely creates credential files; configure paid keys outside chat, protect ~/.patentmax, upload only materials approved for external processing, and review PatentMax's privacy policy first.
