## Description:

Organizes messy meeting notes into structured Markdown minutes and extracts topics, decisions, action items, owners, and follow-up questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[willthacker111](https://clawhub.ai/user/willthacker111)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to turn meeting transcripts, rough notes, or scattered agenda points into formal minutes that can be copied into productivity tools such as Notion, Feishu, or Confluence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting notes may contain confidential or sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Users should avoid pasting highly confidential meeting content unless they are comfortable sharing it with the agent session they are using.

Risk: Incomplete notes can lead to incorrect owners, dates, or conclusions if missing details are inferred.

Mitigation: The skill directs the agent to use pending-confirmation markers for incomplete details and avoid adding facts not present in the source notes.
