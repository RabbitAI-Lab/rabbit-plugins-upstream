## Description:

Turns informal or fragmented meeting notes into structured Markdown minutes with topics, decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, and follow-up questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[willthacker111](https://clawhub.ai/user/willthacker111)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external collaborators use this skill to convert raw meeting transcripts, informal notes, or bullet points into concise Markdown minutes that are ready to archive, share, or paste into productivity tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting notes can contain sensitive personal, business, or operational information.

Mitigation: Review generated minutes before sharing and provide only meeting content appropriate for the agent environment.

Risk: Generated minutes may preserve omissions or ambiguities from incomplete source notes.

Mitigation: Confirm owners, deadlines, and unresolved questions before treating the minutes as final.

Risk: The skill may prefer Chinese headings or phrasing unless the user asks for another language.

Mitigation: Specify the desired output language when requesting the meeting-minutes summary.
