## Description:

Guides users through Analysis of Competing Hypotheses to compare alternative explanations, test evidence for contradictions, and identify assumptions that could change a conclusion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People evaluating consequential factual claims or decisions use this skill to compare multiple plausible explanations against the available evidence. It helps teams surface disconfirming evidence, key assumptions, and signals that should trigger a reassessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A structured comparison can still lead to a misleading conclusion if its evidence or assessments are wrong.

Mitigation: Use the analysis as a discussion aid and verify the evidence behind conclusions before making consequential decisions.
