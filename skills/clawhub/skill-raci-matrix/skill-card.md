## Description: <br>
Guides users through defining RACI responsibility matrices for quality activities and processes while keeping responsibility assignments user-directed and producing a structured matrix with validation prompts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Quality managers, project managers, and process owners use this skill to collect activities, roles, and user-confirmed RACI assignments, then produce a reviewable responsibility matrix with checks for common assignment issues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter internal role and responsibility information while building the matrix. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential organizational details unless the workspace and release context are appropriate for that information. <br>
Risk: The generated matrix may be mistaken for an approved organizational decision. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as a planning aid and have responsible stakeholders review and approve assignments before relying on them operationally. <br>
