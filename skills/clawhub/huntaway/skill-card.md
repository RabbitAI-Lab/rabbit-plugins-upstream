## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Task content is sent to the selected AI providers, and the separately installed Pipe service has not been verified.

Mitigation: Use an owner-only assistant, limit each job to permitted folders, verify the Pipe's access controls and job-record retention before connecting, and inspect saved changes before merging.
