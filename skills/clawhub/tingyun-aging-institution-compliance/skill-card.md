## Description:

Helps elder-care institution operators prepare a human-reviewed compliance self-check, staffing estimates, certificate alerts, remediation records, and evidence checklists against referenced standards and local requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External operators of nursing homes, elder-care homes, and day-care facilities use it to prepare a manual self-inspection, organize supporting records, estimate care-staff ratios, track certificate expirations, and follow remediation items. Review every result against current local requirements; the skill does not issue legal or official compliance determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checklist may not cover every national-standard requirement, and local requirements can differ.

Mitigation: Check the current official standards and local authority checklist, and review each conclusion manually.

Risk: Example staffing ratios may not match the institution's local requirements.

Mitigation: Replace the example ratios with the applicable local requirements before calculating staffing needs.

Risk: Reports may contain staff names and certificate details.

Mitigation: Limit access to completed reports and store them securely.
