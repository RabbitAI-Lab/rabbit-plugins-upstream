## Description:

Helps elder-care facility operators organize a manual compliance self-check, staffing estimates, certificate expiry reminders, and remediation records against referenced standards and checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External operators of nursing homes, elder-care institutions, and day-care centers use the skill to organize evidence-based self-checks, estimate nursing staff needs, track certificate expiries, and maintain remediation records. Results require local and human review and do not constitute legal advice or an official compliance determination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China; local requirements vary by province and locality.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Standards, local checklists, or staffing assumptions may be outdated or inapplicable to the facility's jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Check current official standards and local requirements, including applicable staffing ratios, and have a responsible person review the assessment.

Risk: Input files and reports may contain staff details.

Mitigation: Limit access to those files and reports to people who need them.
