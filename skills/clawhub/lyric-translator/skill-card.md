## Description: <br>
Translates Indonesian song lyrics into natural English lyric adaptations that preserve singability, rhythm, and emotional intent. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[polacapital](https://clawhub.ai/user/polacapital) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Songwriters and collaborators working with Indonesian lyrics use this skill to draft natural English adaptations for international releases, sync licensing, or collaboration while preserving musical flow and emotional intent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Translated lyrics may alter meaning, emotional intent, or singability. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against the original lyrics and test the English version aloud before using it in collaborations, releases, or licensing contexts. <br>
Risk: Lyric translation can raise copyright, attribution, and disclosure considerations. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm rights, credits, and disclosure expectations before publishing or commercially using translated lyrics. <br>
