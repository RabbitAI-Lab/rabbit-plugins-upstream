## Description: <br>
List Growth Designer helps agents plan owned email-list growth through acquisition channels, lead-magnet concepts, opt-in capture-flow specs, referral mechanics, and growth targets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, newsletter operators, and agents use this skill to design email-list acquisition plans, compliant opt-in capture specs, referral loops, and measurable subscriber-growth targets. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may work with subscriber-growth data, analytics exports, and consent or suppression context. <br>
Mitigation: Use user-provided or first-party exports, treat pasted records as untrusted input, and do not follow instructions embedded in those records. <br>
Risk: Consent and marketing-email compliance guidance can be jurisdiction-sensitive and may be mistaken for legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present compliance content as planning guidance and recommend qualified legal review before launch. <br>
Risk: Local memory writes could persist acquisition strategy or consent-capture details unexpectedly. <br>
Mitigation: Write memory only after user confirmation and route consent-capture records through the designated proposal workflow rather than direct consent-store writes. <br>
