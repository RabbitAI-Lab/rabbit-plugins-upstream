## Description:

Assists with traditional BaZi (Four Pillars) charting and interpretation by guiding an agent through pillar calculation, five-element and Ten-God analysis, strength and pattern assessment, useful and unfavorable elements, luck cycles, annual influences, and topic-focused readings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer BaZi fortune-telling requests, explain core traditional Chinese metaphysics concepts, and produce structured readings from user-provided birth details. It is intended for cultural reference and self-reflection, not for professional medical, legal, financial, or mental-health decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive personal birth details for user-requested interpretations.

Mitigation: Collect only the birth details needed for the requested reading and avoid retaining or exposing them beyond the conversation context.

Risk: Fortune-telling outputs may be mistaken for professional advice or used for high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as traditional cultural reference and explicitly avoid substituting for medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.

Risk: Incomplete birth time, location, or calendar information can lead to inaccurate charting.

Mitigation: Ask for missing details or state assumptions clearly, and recommend professional charting tools for complex calendar or boundary-time cases.
