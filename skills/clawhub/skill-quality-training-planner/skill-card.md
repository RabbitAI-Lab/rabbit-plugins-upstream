## Description: <br>
Quality Training Planner helps agents create actionable quality training plans for Quality Month, targeted skills training, and new-hire quality awareness, with Markdown and HTML outputs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Quality managers, trainers, and operations teams use this skill to draft structured training plans for quality-management topics such as SPC, 8D, FMEA, QC tools, ISO systems, and quality awareness. The skill emphasizes reusable training structure while marking company-specific cases, internal standards, process details, and post-training metrics for user completion. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Referenced helper files are absent from the submitted artifact, so final Markdown or HTML rendering may require manual completion or replacement renderer assets. <br>
Mitigation: Verify required renderer inputs before relying on generated files, and manually review or regenerate the final documents when helper assets are unavailable. <br>
Risk: Generated quality training content may not match a company's applicable internal standards or the exact version of an external standard. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans against current internal standards, approved examples, and applicable standard versions before delivery. <br>
Risk: Company-specific cases, internal standard numbers, process details, and actual training effectiveness data could be incorrect if filled in without source material. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unsupported organization-specific content as placeholders until a qualified user supplies or verifies the source material. <br>
