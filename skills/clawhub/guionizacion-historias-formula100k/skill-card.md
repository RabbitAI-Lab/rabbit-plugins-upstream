## Description:

Creates Spanish-neutral Instagram story sequences of 3-6 stories using the Formula 100K method, including category selection, script structure, slide-by-slide copy, audiovisual format guidance, and a visual design briefing saved as Markdown.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and operators use this skill to turn an idea, offer, or campaign objective into a complete Instagram Stories sequence ready to record, design, save, and pass to a follow-on image generation skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read profile or Segundo Cerebro context to adapt scripts to the user's business.

Mitigation: Avoid providing sensitive business material unless it is appropriate for use in generated marketing drafts.

Risk: The skill saves a Markdown draft and may deliver the full document through Telegram.

Mitigation: Review generated files before sharing and confirm Telegram delivery is acceptable for the intended content.
