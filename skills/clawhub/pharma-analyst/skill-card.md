## Description:

Produce current, evidence-graded pharmaceutical and biotechnology analysis at the company, platform, pipeline, asset, indication, trial, competitive-landscape, catalyst, risk, and valuation levels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sciminer](https://clawhub.ai/user/sciminer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External analysts, diligence teams, and biopharma researchers use this skill to produce evidence-graded company, platform, pipeline, clinical, competitive, catalyst, risk, and valuation analysis. It supports research reports and updates while explicitly excluding patient-specific medical advice and personalized investment, legal, or patent advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research outputs may be mistaken for medical, investment, legal, patent, or valuation advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research context and require qualified human review before relying on conclusions.

Risk: Pharmaceutical pipeline, trial, regulatory, financing, leadership, market, and competitive facts are time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Set an explicit data cutoff, verify cited primary sources, and refresh material claims before use.

Risk: Preclinical, preliminary, sponsor-disclosed, or cross-trial evidence can be overinterpreted.

Mitigation: Use the skill's evidence grades, inference labels, and limitations sections to separate verified facts from claims, assumptions, and speculation.
