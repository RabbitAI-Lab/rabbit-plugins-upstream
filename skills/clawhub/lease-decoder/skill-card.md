## Description: <br>
Decode a residential lease into plain English and rank the clauses that can hurt you. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and renters use this skill to understand residential lease terms before signing, identify clauses that may create financial or practical risk, calculate exit and deposit exposure, and prepare questions for the landlord. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Lease content can include sensitive housing, identity, and financial details. <br>
Mitigation: Remove unnecessary personal information before using the skill where practical. <br>
Risk: Lease enforceability and tenant protections vary by jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a plain-language review aid and confirm important legal or financial decisions with a qualified local professional. <br>
