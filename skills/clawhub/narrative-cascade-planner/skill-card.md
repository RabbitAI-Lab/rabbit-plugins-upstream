## Description: <br>
Maps a sealed narrative canon onto owned marketing surfaces as per-surface message-match specs, drift flags, and handoff briefs for creative builders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and narrative teams use this skill to turn an approved narrative canon into surface-level message-match requirements, drift checks, and builder handoff briefs. It is for planning and routing claims, proof, objections, and voice constraints across owned surfaces, not for writing final copy. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can read local narrative and claims memory that may contain sensitive owned marketing information. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with content the user is authorized to analyze, and avoid applying it to private or third-party content without explicit approval. <br>
Risk: Saved cascade plans can persist planning outputs into local narrative memory. <br>
Mitigation: Save results only after user confirmation and review the plan before allowing it to become durable project context. <br>
Risk: Unapproved product or comparative claims could be propagated across surfaces as if they were substantiated. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unapproved claims marked as needing a source and route them to the claims registry rather than treating the cascade plan as claim adjudication. <br>
