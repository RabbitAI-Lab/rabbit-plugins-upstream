This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Mitigation: Confirm upload destinations and retention terms before sharing private recordings; remove local copies when no longer needed.

Risk: The documented single-domain network promise may not match actual destinations.

Mitigation: Verify the skill's network destinations before using it with sensitive media or in restricted environments.
