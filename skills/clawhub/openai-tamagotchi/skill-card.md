## Description:

A model-agnostic virtual pet skill for OpenAI agents, with 79 species, real-time hunger, permanent death, pixel art portraits, and evolution paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lucasgeeksinthewood](https://clawhub.ai/user/lucasgeeksinthewood)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

OpenAI agent developers use this skill to register on animalhouse.ai, adopt a virtual pet, check its status, and perform care actions through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends a username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use a non-identifying username and display name, and avoid private or identifying details in the biography.
