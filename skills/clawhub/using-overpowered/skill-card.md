## Description:

Part of the Overpowered skill suite, this skill routes knowledge-work and enterprise-automation tasks through the smallest relevant combination of Overpowered skills and reserves gear-up for genuine execution capability gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this orchestration skill when starting non-trivial knowledge-work or enterprise-automation tasks that may involve organizational knowledge, conflicting evidence, evolving artifacts, business rules, risky side effects, or verifiable completion. It helps choose a minimal set of Overpowered skills before execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may influence an agent to route work through downstream Overpowered skills that perform automation, side effects, or capability generation.

Mitigation: Review the downstream skills selected for a task, especially when automation, broad side effects, human approval boundaries, or gear-up generation are involved.

Risk: Over-composition can add unnecessary methods or route to gear-up before a real capability gap is proven.

Mitigation: Use the smallest relevant skill combination, perform reuse and knowledge checks before gear-up, and stop routing once the material failure modes are covered.
