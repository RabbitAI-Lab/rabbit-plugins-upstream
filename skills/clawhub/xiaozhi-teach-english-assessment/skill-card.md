## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 English teachers design listening, speaking, reading, and writing assessments, describe student abilities, and plan targeted instruction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English teachers in Chinese K–12 settings use this skill to plan four-skill assessments, draft evidence-based ability profiles, and suggest teaching interventions. Teachers review student-facing material and make final proficiency judgments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (for Chinese K–12 teaching contexts)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Minors' learning records may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, avoid real names, and confirm any writeback before storing results.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials may be confidential.

Mitigation: Do not enter secret exam questions or answers; use general assessment frameworks instead.

Risk: Automated score-to-level conclusions may misrepresent student ability.

Mitigation: Have the teacher judge final levels against CSE descriptors and supporting task evidence.
