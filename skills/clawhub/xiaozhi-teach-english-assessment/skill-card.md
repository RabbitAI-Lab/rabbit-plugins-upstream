## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 English teachers design listening, speaking, reading, and writing assessments, develop evidence-based learner profiles, and plan teaching interventions using CSE descriptors with CEFR as a reference.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 English teachers use the skill to plan four-skill assessments, interpret performance against CSE descriptors, draft learner ability profiles, and choose classroom interventions. Teachers make final grading and level decisions and authorize any student-record updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt the curriculum, emergency contacts, and privacy rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student assessment records may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, avoid personal identifiers, and obtain teacher, guardian, and school-policy authorization before student-profile writeback.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts, and privacy rules may not fit locations outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize these requirements before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Unreleased official exam materials could be exposed, or provisional assessments mistaken for final judgments.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam materials; have teachers review generated items and determine levels against CSE descriptors rather than converting scores directly.
