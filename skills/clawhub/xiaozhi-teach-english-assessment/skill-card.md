## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 English teachers design listening, speaking, reading, and writing assessments and turn results into skill profiles and teaching recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 English teachers use this skill to plan four-skill assessments, review student performance against CSE descriptors with CEFR as a reference, draft ability profiles and teaching interventions, and prepare teacher-approved updates to class records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China Mainland; adapt curricular, legal, and emergency guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student assessment records may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, follow consent controls, and review proposed record updates before saving.

Risk: Unreviewed assessments or inferred proficiency levels may misrepresent student ability.

Mitigation: Have teachers review generated materials and determine levels from CSE descriptors rather than scores alone.

Risk: Curricular, legal, and emergency guidance may not transfer outside China Mainland.

Mitigation: Localize those references before deployment in another jurisdiction.
