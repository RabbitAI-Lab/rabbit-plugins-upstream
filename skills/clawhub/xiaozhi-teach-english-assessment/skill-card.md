## Description:

Helps English teachers design listening, speaking, reading, and writing assessments, draft ability profiles, and plan instructional follow-up using CSE descriptors with CEFR as a reference.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

English teachers use this skill to draft four-skill assessments, describe student strengths and gaps, and suggest teaching interventions. Teachers retain responsibility for evaluating students and approving any record updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy, and emergency guidance for other regions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated questions or ability-level interpretations may misrepresent a student's skills.

Mitigation: Teachers review generated items and assess levels against CSE descriptors rather than converting scores directly.

Risk: Student assessment records or unreleased exam materials could be disclosed or written without authorization.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, exclude unreleased exam items, and require teacher confirmation and consent before record writeback.

Risk: Chinese curriculum and safety guidance may be unsuitable in other locations.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts, privacy rules, and terminology before use outside Mainland China.
