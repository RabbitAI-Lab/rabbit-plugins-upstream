## Description: <br>
Gives agents real-time and archival access to front-page headlines across 20 countries for breaking news, current events, and comparative media analysis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sfkislev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sfkislev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill to fetch current or archived headline snapshots, compare front-page coverage across countries and sources, and summarize changes in news narratives over time. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Returned headlines and AI overviews may be mistaken for verified facts. <br>
Mitigation: Treat results as news context and verify high-stakes or claim-specific details with additional sources. <br>
Risk: Headlines reflect source framing and editorial prioritization. <br>
Mitigation: Compare multiple sources and countries before drawing conclusions about events or public narratives. <br>
