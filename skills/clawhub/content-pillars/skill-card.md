## Description: <br>
Defines 3-5 ownable content pillars for a brand by reading brand strategy inputs and producing content-pillars.md for downstream content planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and social media strategists use this skill after brand-profile to turn positioning, audience pains, proof, and point of view into 3-5 ownable content themes for planning calendars and post ideas. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on the word "pillars" when the user is not asking about marketing or content strategy. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user intends marketing or content strategy before relying on the skill's output. <br>
Risk: Generated pillars can shape downstream content plans from incomplete or outdated brand materials. <br>
Mitigation: Review the source brand profile and the generated content-pillars.md before using it for calendars or campaigns. <br>
