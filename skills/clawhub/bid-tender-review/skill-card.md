## Description: <br>
Analyses construction bids or tender packages for scope gaps, risk-shifting exclusions, pricing red flags, and front-loading before award. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External construction owners, estimators, project managers, and procurement teams use this skill to level bids, review tender packages, and prepare pre-award clarification questions. It helps turn bid documents and quotes into a structured review with gap, exclusion, pricing, front-loading, and recommendation sections. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may require users to share confidential construction bids, estimates, and contract details with their agent. <br>
Mitigation: Use only approved agent environments for sensitive project data and redact or limit confidential material when a full document set is not required. <br>
Risk: Bid-review recommendations can be incomplete or misleading when the tender documents, scope narrative, schedule of values, or competing bids are missing or stale. <br>
Mitigation: Require every assumption and cost exposure to be labeled, then review the output with the responsible estimator, procurement lead, or contract owner before award. <br>
