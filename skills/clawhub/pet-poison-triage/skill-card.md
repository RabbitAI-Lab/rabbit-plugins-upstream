## Description:

Provides species-aware first-response guidance for suspected poisoning in dogs and cats, including toxin lookup, selected dose calculations, and emergency preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Pet owners and their assistants use this skill to assess a possible dog or cat toxin exposure, prepare details for a veterinarian or poison hotline, and assemble an emergency kit. It does not replace professional veterinary advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Relying on a triage result could delay urgent veterinary care.

Mitigation: Contact a veterinarian, emergency clinic, or poison hotline promptly; treat the guidance as preparation for that call.

Risk: Unsupervised home treatment may harm the animal.

Mitigation: Do not induce vomiting or use home remedies without professional direction.
