## Description:

归纳商品评价中的优缺点、购买顾虑和改进建议，并以评价条数、范围和原话依据呈现主题分析。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devinchen2014](https://clawhub.ai/user/devinchen2014)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, product teams, and developers can use the skill to summarize supplied product-review text or analyze Xiaohongshu product reviews. It highlights evidence-backed themes and bounded improvement suggestions while keeping conclusions tied to the analyzed sample.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Online analysis sends product URLs or share text to SocialDataX and may consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Use online analysis only when needed, submit only relevant product information, and remove unrelated personal details.

Risk: Review samples can be incomplete or unrepresentative, and customer claims do not by themselves verify product defects.

Mitigation: State the analyzed sample, counts, denominator, and missing fields; present conflicting reports separately and label issues as needing verification.
