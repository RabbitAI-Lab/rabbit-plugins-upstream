## Description:

Guides tax preparers through a pre-filing premortem to identify uncertain return positions, missing substantiation, and disclosure questions before filing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tax preparers use this skill before filing complex returns or returns with aggressive or uncertain positions. It helps structure a review of substantiation, authority, disclosure decisions, and residual examination risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review suggestions may not account for all facts or current tax requirements.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a review aid and have a qualified preparer verify recommendations against current IRC and IRS guidance before changing or filing a return.

Risk: The referenced parent premortem workflow is not included with this release.

Mitigation: Use the prompts with the parent workflow available, or treat them as a focused checklist and verify the review method before relying on it.
