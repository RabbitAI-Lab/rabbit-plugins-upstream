## Description: <br>
This skill guides agents through a tax-prep pre-file audit premortem for complex or aggressive return positions, identifying likely exam failures, substantiation gaps, disclosure needs, and residual risk before filing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Tax preparers, review partners, and tax-focused agents use this skill before filing complex returns to test uncertain positions against likely IRS or state exam outcomes. It supports review of authority level, substantiation, disclosure decisions, and client communication about residual exam risk. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
United States <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may produce incomplete, outdated, or overconfident tax-risk analysis if treated as tax advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as a reasoning aid only, and verify positions against current IRS, state, and professional guidance before relying on the output. <br>
Risk: Tax return review can involve sensitive client information. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with appropriate client authorization and avoid sharing unnecessary confidential details. <br>
Risk: A premortem may identify residual exam risk without resolving whether a position should be filed, disclosed, or adjusted. <br>
Mitigation: Document substantiation, authority level, disclosure decisions, and client communication before e-filing. <br>
