## Description:

Generates a one-page team AI usage policy covering approved tools, permitted and prohibited uses, and incident escalation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0 (release metadata); artifact states MIT for code and all rights reserved for documentation and theory

## Use Case:

Team leads and governance staff use this skill to draft a concise AI usage policy from their approved tools, allowed activities, prohibited activities, and incident reporting contact. Review the draft before adoption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated policy may omit organization-specific obligations or be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat it as a draft and obtain human and legal review before adoption.

Risk: Sensitive details included in inputs may appear in the local output policy.

Mitigation: Avoid entering secrets or sensitive business details unless they are intended for the output document.

Risk: Bundled documentation and release metadata describe different license terms.

Mitigation: Review the layered license terms before reuse or redistribution.
