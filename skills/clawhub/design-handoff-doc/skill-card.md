## Description:

Generates developer-facing design handoff documents from screenshots or design descriptions, including component specifications, asset lists, and interaction notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[elise-ctrl](https://clawhub.ai/user/elise-ctrl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and developers use this skill to turn screenshots or design descriptions into implementation-ready handoff documents for mini-program, app, web, or large-screen interfaces.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad handoff-related triggers or Chinese-language templates may not suit mixed-language or mixed-domain workflows.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended task, platform, and document language before using the template.

Risk: Screenshot-derived dimensions and styling may be estimates rather than exact design specifications.

Mitigation: Check measurements against the source design and ask for missing specifications before implementation.
