## Description: <br>
Turn video/audio transcripts into original screenplays. Faster-whisper -> LLM story DNA -> screenplay (.md + .fountain). Supports 6 LLM providers and CPU/GPU. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[hyldmh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hyldmh) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and creators use this skill to turn SRT transcripts or audio/video files into story DNA and original screenplay drafts. It is useful when a user needs human-readable Markdown and Fountain screenplay files for review or import into screenwriting software. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Transcript-derived content may be sent to the selected LLM provider. <br>
Mitigation: Review the provider's data handling terms and redact private, regulated, or confidential content before use. <br>
Risk: Generated story DNA and screenplay drafts may misrepresent source material or create unsuitable creative output. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated Markdown, JSON, and Fountain outputs before sharing, importing, or relying on them. <br>
