## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Educational options analysis could be mistaken for verified legal, regulatory, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Confirm current market rules and trading decisions with official sources and a qualified professional before acting.

Risk: Options strategies can create substantial loss, margin, suitability, and position-limit exposure if applied without review.

Mitigation: Use the skill's risk-control checklist for permissions, suitability, limits, margin stress, maximum loss, and Greeks exposure before considering any strategy.
