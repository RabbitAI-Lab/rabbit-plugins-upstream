## Description:

Analyzes competitor ads from the Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Kalodata, or FastMoss to create original, evidence-based ad concepts without reusing their creative work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and marketing teams use this skill to study high-performing competitor ads, identify gaps in angles and formats, and draft original concepts grounded in claims they can support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
