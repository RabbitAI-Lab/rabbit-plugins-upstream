## Description:

Turns winning competitor ads into original ad angles and concepts for a seller's product, using only claims the seller can support and avoiding reuse of the source ad's expressive content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketers use the skill to analyze competitor ads, identify underused angles and formats, and develop original concepts for their own products. Each concept can include a hook, proof shot, and claims grounded in the seller's evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
