## Description:

Turns winning competitor ads into original ad concepts by analyzing their angles, formats, proof, and audience awareness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and performance marketers use the skill to study competing ads and develop distinct concepts for their own products. It provides hooks, proof shots, and claims grounded in the seller's evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
