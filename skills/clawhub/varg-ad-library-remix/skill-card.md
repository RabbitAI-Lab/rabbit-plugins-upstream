## Description:

Turns winning competitor ads from the Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Kalodata or FastMoss into new ad angles for your own product, without copying them: rank ads by winner signals (running 30+ days, many variants, reach), break the top ones down with varg video_analyze (hook, angle, format, offer, proof, CTA, awareness level), map which angles and formats the market already runs, generate 10-20 new concepts (same angle in a new format, same format with a new angle, one awareness level up or down) using only claims you can prove, check originality, and hand each concept to a varg skill to produce. Use when a seller says "clone this winning ad", "what are my competitors running", "find winning ads for my product", "spy on TikTok Shop ads", "give me new angles", or pastes Ad Library or Creative Center links. Not for re-uploading or re-cutting someone else's footage (never), and not for one ad's 20 variants (use varg-ad-variants).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
