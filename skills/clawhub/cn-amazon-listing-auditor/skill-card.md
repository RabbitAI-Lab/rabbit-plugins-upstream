## Description: <br>
Audits Amazon listings for Chinese cross-border sellers by flagging translation errors, cultural fit issues, keyword gaps, awkward phrasing, and conversion risks for Western buyers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kiwi-phantomworks](https://clawhub.ai/user/kiwi-phantomworks) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to audit Amazon listing copy for Chinese cross-border products targeting Western buyers. It produces a scored report with prioritized fixes and specific rewrite suggestions for the highest-impact issues. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad listing-review requests may invoke a Chinese-seller-to-Western-buyer perspective when that locale-specific framing does not match the task. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the seller context and target buyer market before relying on the audit conclusions. <br>
