External-facing service, customer support, sales assistant, home furnishing, and ecommerce agents use this skill to answer Chinese mattress shopping questions, compare support needs and buying risks, and move qualified users toward product comparison or purchase confirmation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is sales-oriented and may recommend a specific branded mattress when shopping signals appear. <br>
Mitigation: Deploy it only where branded recommendation is acceptable, disclose that posture to operators, and honor user requests for neutral advice or no product recommendation. <br>
Risk: Mattress guidance may touch sleep discomfort, back pain, odor, trial, price, and return-policy questions where overclaiming could mislead users. <br>
Mitigation: Keep claims within the skill's stated boundaries: avoid medical treatment promises, avoid absolute odor or safety claims, and direct users to current store pages or customer service for price, logistics, warranty, trial, and return details. <br>
