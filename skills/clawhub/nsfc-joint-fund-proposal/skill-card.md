## Description:

Helps researchers draft, refine, and review NSFC Hubei Joint Fund proposal materials against the official template, including core sections such as basic information, research content, scientific questions, innovation points, expected outcomes, and Hubei application prospects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers and proposal writers use this skill to collect required project information, generate proposal sections in sequence, polish individual sections, and check word limits, template compliance, terminology, and logical consistency for NSFC Hubei Joint Fund-style submissions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process personal contact details, unpublished research ideas, and confidential proposal material supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Review and limit sensitive inputs before use, and verify generated content before sharing or submitting proposal materials.

Risk: Generated proposal text may include inaccurate claims or may not fully satisfy the current official template requirements.

Mitigation: Check factual claims, word limits, official-template compliance, and institutional requirements before submission.
