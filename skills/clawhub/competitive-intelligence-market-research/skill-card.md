## Use Case: <br>
Product marketing, sales, product, executive, and investor teams at B2B SaaS companies use this skill to structure competitive analysis, market research, positioning work, and sales enablement. It provides reusable frameworks, templates, checklists, and scenario-specific guidance across Sales Tech, HR Tech, Fintech, and Operations Tech contexts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The always-on activation setting may make the skill available outside explicit competitive-intelligence or market-research tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Disable or narrow the always-on activation if tighter routing is required, and invoke the skill only for market-research workflows. <br>
Risk: Competitive and market information generated from templates can be stale, incomplete, or unsupported by current sources. <br>
Mitigation: Validate findings against current public sources, paid research, customer interviews, and internal data before using them in decisions or sales materials. <br>
Risk: Fintech, HR Tech, and other regulated-sector outputs may involve compliance, privacy, or claims-review concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Route regulated-sector positioning, pricing, ROI, and competitor-claim outputs through legal, compliance, or qualified domain review before publication. <br>
