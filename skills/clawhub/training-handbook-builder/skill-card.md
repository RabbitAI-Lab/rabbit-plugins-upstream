## Description: <br>
Generates Chinese-oriented professional Word training handbooks for university, government, and enterprise training, including cover pages, an updateable table of contents, page numbers, colored tables, callout boxes, and case annotations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to assemble printable training manuals, operating guides, white papers, and handbooks from researched or user-provided content. It is best suited for Chinese-language professional training documents that need polished Word formatting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated document formatting may not fit English or multilingual materials because the skill is oriented around Chinese fonts and locale assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review fonts, page layout, and locale-specific formatting before printing or distribution, especially for non-Chinese content. <br>
Risk: Generated handbook content can include outdated or inaccurate policy, case, or technical information if source research is not verified. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited policies, document numbers, publication dates, issuing organizations, and case details before publishing the handbook. <br>
