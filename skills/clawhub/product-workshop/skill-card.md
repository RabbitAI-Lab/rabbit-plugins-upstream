## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, founders, and product teams use this Chinese-language skill to turn early product ideas into structured product plans, PRDs, user stories, iteration plans, and retrospectives. It guides product-related requests through a six-step workflow covering problem discovery, user insight, solution design, documentation, development execution, and post-launch learning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate broadly on product-related requests and steer answers into its six-step workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's product task and stage before applying the workflow, and adapt or skip steps that do not fit the request. <br>
Risk: Generated product plans, PRDs, and prioritization guidance can reflect incomplete assumptions or missing user data. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against real user evidence, business constraints, and implementation capacity before treating them as delivery commitments. <br>
