## Description:

Apply the distilled investing philosophy and negotiation principles of Shan Weijian to real decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Individuals and analysts can use this skill to explore investing, negotiation, management, and decision-making questions through source-grounded perspectives drawn from Shan Weijian’s books and public statements. It is intended for educational analysis, not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill applies historical, book-derived frameworks to current financial or business decisions, which may produce misleading guidance if treated as a recommendation.

Mitigation: Use the output as educational analysis, verify current facts independently, and consult qualified financial or legal professionals for consequential decisions.

Risk: The security review was incomplete for one reference, and broad question routing for “why” questions was flagged.

Mitigation: Check the cited source material for the specific question and confirm that the response stays within the sources’ scope.
