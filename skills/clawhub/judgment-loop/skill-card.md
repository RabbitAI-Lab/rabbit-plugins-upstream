## Description:

Judgment Loop turns consequential choices, uncertain claims, recurring failures, serious learning, and reviews into evidence-tagged provisional judgments, cheap falsification tests, and user-owned action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sirsws](https://clawhub.ai/user/sirsws)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers use this skill to structure consequential or uncertain choices into a provisional judgment, labeled evidence, a falsification test, and a user-owned next action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Implicit activation may feel too intrusive for consequential or uncertain decisions.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation only or adjust the routing policy when implicit activation is broader than the installer wants.

Risk: The skill organizes high-stakes judgment but does not replace current authoritative evidence or professional review.

Mitigation: Require current authoritative sources for medical, legal, financial, and similar high-stakes matters before relying on the output.
