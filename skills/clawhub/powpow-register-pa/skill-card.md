## Description:

Provides Punjabi-language guidance for creating a PowPow account, troubleshooting registration, and reaching subscription activation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users can follow Punjabi-language instructions to explore the service, register on its website, resolve common sign-up issues, and activate their accounts through the platform's subscription page. The skill gives guidance; users complete registration and payment themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Mitigation: Keep those details on PowPow's own registration and subscription pages; do not request or process them in the assistant.

Risk: Following registration guidance can lead users to a paid subscription.

Mitigation: Direct users to PowPow's subscription page to review the available terms and complete any payment themselves.
