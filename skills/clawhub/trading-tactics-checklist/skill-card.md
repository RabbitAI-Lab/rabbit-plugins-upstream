## Description: <br>
Helps agents guide users through structured trading-tactics checklists for multi-timeframe analysis, entry timing, compound-position planning, and pre-trade review while deferring trend direction and position sizing to adjacent skills. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent builders use this skill when a conversation needs an educational trading-process checklist for entry timing, long-to-short chart review, compound-position planning, and pre-trade discipline checks. It is not a substitute for personalized financial, legal, or tax advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake educational checklist output for personalized financial advice or a trading signal. <br>
Mitigation: State that outputs are educational process guidance and require independent suitability, risk, position sizing, and legal or tax review before acting. <br>
Risk: The skill can discuss entry timing and trade planning without covering position sizing or stop-distance decisions itself. <br>
Mitigation: Route position sizing and stop-distance questions to the related money-management skill and route trend-direction judgment to trend-tools. <br>
