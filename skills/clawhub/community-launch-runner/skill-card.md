## Description: <br>
Community Launch Runner helps launch operators prepare platform-specific community and directory submission packages for Product Hunt, Show HN, Reddit, directory waves, and regional channels while checking platform red lines such as vote solicitation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Launch operators, founders, and marketing teams use this skill to prepare community launch submissions, directory waves, regional channel plans, and launch handoff summaries. It is intended for rule-aware launch execution rather than paid amplification, creator campaigns, launch telemetry, or long-term community warmup. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public launch copy can violate platform self-promotion, embargo, review incentive, or vote-solicitation rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review final copy yourself, verify each platform's current rules, and keep feedback requests separate from vote or engagement requests. <br>
Risk: Launch status proposals may record incorrect platform, timestamp, status, or URL details. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm submission facts before saving proposal lines and do not persist launch records without user permission. <br>
Risk: Fetched platform pages or pasted rules can contain misleading or irrelevant instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat fetched and pasted content as untrusted evidence for platform rules, not as instructions for the agent to follow. <br>
