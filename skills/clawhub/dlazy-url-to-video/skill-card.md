## Description:

Turns a webpage URL into a promotional, social ad, or product demo video by capturing the site, deriving its brand, and building a storyboard and voiceover.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and product teams use this skill to turn a webpage link into a promotional, social ad, or product demo video through a hosted dLazy project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy services, and project sessions retain context.

Mitigation: Send only content you are comfortable sharing with dLazy, confirm the intended project, and clear a saved session when its context is no longer needed.

Risk: Using the hosted video workflow may incur service credit charges.

Mitigation: Check your dLazy account and expected charges before starting a generation.

Risk: The security assessment did not audit the exact npm executable or hosted backend.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI before installing and avoid sending sensitive material until the service is approved for it.
