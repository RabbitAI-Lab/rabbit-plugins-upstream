## Description:

Turns a webpage URL into a promotional, advertising, or demo video through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a website or landing-page URL into a promotional video, social ad, or product demo.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, webpage URLs, project context, and attached files may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only approved URLs and files, and review the service terms before use.

Risk: Authentication saves a dLazy API key locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local key and rotate or revoke it in the dLazy account dashboard if exposed.
