## Description:

Turns a webpage URL into a promotional, social ad, or product demo video through a hosted video creation workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can provide a website URL to create a promotional video, social ad, or product demo and continue work in a project session.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial and reported a mismatch between the stated video-making purpose and behavior it observed.

Mitigation: Review the skill's install details and referenced material, and consider whether the reported login and project-listing behavior fits your needs.

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy services, and attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive material unless its use with the service is appropriate.
