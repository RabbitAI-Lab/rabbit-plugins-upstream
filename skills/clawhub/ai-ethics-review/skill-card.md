## Description: <br>
Conducts a structured ethical review of an AI or ML feature, model, or product, covering fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, accountability, and societal impact with risk scoring, mitigation priorities, and a pre-deployment checklist. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, developers, auditors, and governance reviewers use this skill to assess AI or ML systems before deployment, during audits, or when documenting responsible AI impact. It helps organize review findings across risk tier, fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, accountability, societal impact, and mitigation readiness. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the generated ethics review as legal advice or as a substitute for a regulated impact assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a governance checklist and obtain appropriate legal, privacy, security, or specialist ethics review for regulated or high-risk deployments. <br>
Risk: Incomplete system details can produce shallow risk scoring or mitigation priorities. <br>
Mitigation: Provide concrete information about affected people, data sources, decision impact, human oversight, deployment context, and monitoring plans before relying on the review. <br>
