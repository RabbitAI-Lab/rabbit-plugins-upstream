## Description: <br>
Automates client onboarding and project lifecycle management by helping create project folders, briefs, welcome emails, kickoff meetings, task boards, reminders, and a client registry. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Freelancers, agencies, and small businesses use this skill to turn a new-client request into an organized onboarding workflow, including intake follow-up, project setup, communications, scheduling, task tracking, and status reporting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill handles sensitive client, budget, contact, schedule, and project status information across local files and optional third-party integrations. <br>
Mitigation: Use an appropriate client folder or workspace memory location, keep external integrations disabled until needed, and review generated project records before storing or sharing them. <br>
Risk: Email and calendar actions may contact clients or create external commitments if sending and event creation are enabled. <br>
Mitigation: Keep Gmail and Google Calendar creation in draft or disabled mode by default, and review message drafts, attendees, times, and meeting details before external actions. <br>
