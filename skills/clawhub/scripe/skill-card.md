## Description:

Plan, write, schedule and measure LinkedIn posts for a team in Scripe (scripe.io) through its hosted MCP server. Use when the user wants to plan a content calendar for one or several LinkedIn profiles or company pages, draft a post in someone's own voice, turn a transcript, article or idea into posts, schedule or publish a post, read LinkedIn analytics, find outlier posts in their niche, or work with their Scripe ideas, knowledge base, labels or campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use the skill to plan and draft LinkedIn content, manage reviews and scheduling, and review performance for their profiles or company pages. It also helps users connect Scripe's hosted MCP service and work with ideas, campaigns, and the knowledge base.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: With full access, scheduled posts can go live automatically, and material uploaded for drafting or knowledge storage is sent to Scripe.

Mitigation: Choose read-only access for analytics-only work; carefully check the workspace and profile before using full access, and review publishing or scheduling actions before confirming them.

Risk: Installer versions and source revisions may change.

Mitigation: Review and pin installer versions and source revisions where supported.
