## Description: <br>
Perform deep SEO competitor analysis, including keyword research, backlink checking, and content strategy mapping. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, marketers, SEO specialists, and developers use this skill to identify ranking competitors, compare keyword and content coverage, and produce a structured SEO action report. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent may search, fetch, and browse public competitor websites while performing SEO research. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when public competitive research is acceptable, and review target domains and fetched pages before relying on the report. <br>
Risk: Optional scripts or missing referenced files are not part of the reviewed artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Review and scan any separately provided scripts or newly added reference files before using them with this skill. <br>
