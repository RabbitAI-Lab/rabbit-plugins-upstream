## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real manufacturing datasets may contain customer names, supplier identities, or confidential internal process details.

Mitigation: Use only in an agent environment approved for that data, and remove sensitive identifiers before sharing process data.

Risk: Statistical conclusions can be misleading when specification limits, sample size, process context, gauge type, or data quality are missing.

Mitigation: Confirm the required inputs before interpreting results, and review conclusions against AIAG VDA, DMAIC, and local quality standards.
