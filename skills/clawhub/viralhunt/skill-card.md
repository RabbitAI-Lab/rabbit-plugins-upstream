## Description:

Research trends and audience signals across social networks, create branded post drafts, and manage reviews, translations, scheduling, and publishing through ViralHunt.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Teams and their agents use ViralHunt to research current social trends, plan and create on-brand content, review drafts, translate posts, and manage publishing on connected accounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing, scheduling, edits, cancellations, or qualifying review approvals can affect connected social accounts.

Mitigation: Show the proposed content, media, accounts, and time, then get explicit user confirmation; enable recurring review only when automatic release is intended.

Risk: The access token grants account access, and uploaded media and drafts are sent to ViralHunt.

Mitigation: Use the narrowest token permissions and confirm the intended brands and accounts.
