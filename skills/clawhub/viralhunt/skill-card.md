## Description:

Research viral trends and posting opportunities across social networks, then create, review, translate, schedule, or publish posts through a user's ViralHunt account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Brand teams and social media operators use this skill to research current trends and posting opportunities, prepare on-brand content, and manage drafts or authorized publishing through ViralHunt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Enabling automatic sending can release qualifying posts, including posts that were previously approved.

Mitigation: Enable reviewer publishing rights and recurring jobs only when the user intends automatic publishing, and confirm the approval and sending behavior first.

Risk: Drafts, uploaded media, and private review notes are sent to ViralHunt.

Mitigation: Avoid placing sensitive information in drafts, media, or review notes unless the user intends to share it with ViralHunt.
