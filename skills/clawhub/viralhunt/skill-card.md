## Description:

Finds cross-network trends and posting insights, helps create and translate branded social content, and drafts, reviews, schedules, or publishes posts to the user's connected accounts through ViralHunt.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Teams and their agents use ViralHunt to research what is gaining traction, choose where and when to post, and prepare branded social content for connected accounts. They can leave posts for human review or schedule and publish them according to their permissions and approval settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad token permissions or automatic release can allow posts to be published without the intended level of review.

Mitigation: Use the narrowest token permissions needed, disable automatic release when manual approval is desired, and confirm which projects and accounts recurring reviews cover.

Risk: Selected uploads and private editorial notes are stored through ViralHunt.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive media or editorial notes unless that storage is acceptable for the account and its policies.
