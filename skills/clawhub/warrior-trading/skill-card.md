## Use Case: <br>
External users and trading-oriented agents use this skill to reason about high-risk US small-cap day-trading setups, scanner criteria, entries, exits, and risk controls. It should be treated as educational trading-methodology guidance, not personalized financial advice or authorization to place trades. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake day-trading methodology output for personalized financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as educational guidance and require independent human review before any trading decision. <br>
Risk: An agent could be connected to broker credentials or trade execution workflows outside the skill artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Do not grant broker credentials or trading permissions without explicit review and separate controls. <br>
