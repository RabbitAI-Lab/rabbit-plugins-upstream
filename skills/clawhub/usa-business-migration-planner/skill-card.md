## Description: <br>
Helps operators, founders, and small business owners turn questions about sectors resistant to AI adoption into practical workflows, checklists, analysis, and decision-support artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Operators, founders, and small business owners use this skill to turn AI-resistant-sector business questions into repeatable planning workflows, checklists, templates, lightweight analysis, or decision-support artifacts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Loose trigger terms and a broad title may cause the skill to activate for requests outside its AI-resistant-sector planning purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Review and tighten invocation wording before enabling implicit activation in environments that need precise routing. <br>
Risk: Business planning outputs may be incomplete or misleading if the user provides thin context or unclear success criteria. <br>
Mitigation: Check generated workflows, assumptions, and next steps against the user's constraints before acting on them. <br>
