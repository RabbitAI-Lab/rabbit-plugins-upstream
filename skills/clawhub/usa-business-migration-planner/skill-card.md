## Description:

Helps users evaluate whether buying B200 or B300 hardware to host open-source models such as DeepSeek and sell inference services can be a viable business, producing practical plans, checklists, analysis, and validation notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn GPU and model-hosting business questions into a repeatable feasibility workflow, including assumptions, constraints, decision support, checklists, and validation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording could select the skill for loosely related GPU, DeepSeek, or general idea requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for GPU or model-hosting business feasibility before applying the workflow.

Risk: Business feasibility output could be mistaken for definitive investment, legal, or market advice.

Mitigation: Present assumptions and limits clearly, and require validation of costs, contracts, regulations, and market demand before capital commitments.
