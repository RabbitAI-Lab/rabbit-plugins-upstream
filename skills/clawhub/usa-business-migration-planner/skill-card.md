## Description:

Helps users turn Stremio interface, movie discovery, navigation, and cast-information requests into practical plans and deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Stremio maintainers and users use this skill to plan improvements to interface, movie discovery, navigation, or cast information, produce a suitable artifact, and check it against the request's success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The business-migration name and broad activation terms may cause irrelevant responses or suggest expertise the skill does not provide.

Mitigation: Invoke explicitly or restrict automatic selection to Stremio tasks, and confirm the requested scope before producing guidance.

Risk: Demand scores may be mistaken for an independent assurance of relevance or quality.

Mitigation: Treat demand figures as publisher claims, not security or quality assurances.
