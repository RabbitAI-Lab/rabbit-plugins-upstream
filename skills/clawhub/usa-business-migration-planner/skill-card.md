## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The release is labeled as a USA business migration planner, but server security evidence says it routes broad help requests toward unrelated Claude/Bool promotional workflow content. <br>
Mitigation: Do not install or invoke it as a business migration planner; review the skill body and use it only when the Claude/Bool promotional workflow is intended. <br>
Risk: Broad triggers and implicit invocation can cause the skill to activate for general help, promotion, Bool, or Claude requests and steer unrelated conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Disable implicit invocation or narrow the triggers before deployment, and require a human review of outputs for relevance. <br>
