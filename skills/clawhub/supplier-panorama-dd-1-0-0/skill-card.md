## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Legal, procurement, or compliance conclusions may be incomplete or incorrect if public records, legal citations, or knowledge-base results are stale, unavailable, or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Treat reports as decision support, verify official records and cited laws manually, and consult qualified counsel before major procurement, contracting, or investment decisions. <br>
Risk: The default workflow may search public records for related parties such as legal representatives and major shareholders as well as the target company. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for legitimate compliance, supplier review, or investment due diligence, and disable related-party scrutiny when it is not necessary for the review. <br>
Risk: A lack of discovered negative events does not prove that no relevant events exist. <br>
Mitigation: Document search scope, rerun targeted searches for high-impact decisions, and escalate uncertain findings for human review. <br>
