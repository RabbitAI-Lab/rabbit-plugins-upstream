## Description:

Helps tax professionals scope engagements by mapping client facts to likely tax form families, open questions, jurisdiction checks, and supporting document requests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tax professionals use this skill during client intake and engagement scoping to connect reported events and status to likely form families, identify questions that still need answers, and build document requests. It emphasizes jurisdiction checks and routing thresholds, dates, and eligibility questions to current guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, including state and local tax scoping, with cross-border reporting considerations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A preliminary form-family list could be mistaken for a complete filing determination.

Mitigation: Keep open questions and jurisdiction triggers visible, and verify applicable forms and requirements against current IRS and state instructions.

Risk: Client information shared while scoping may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Protect client information and provide only the details needed to scope the engagement.
