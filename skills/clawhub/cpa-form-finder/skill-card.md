## Description:

CPA - Form Finder helps agents scope tax engagements by mapping client facts to likely form families, open questions, jurisdictions, and document-request needs without answering changing thresholds, limits, dates, or eligibility rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tax preparers, CPAs, EAs, and firm intake staff use this skill to scope or quote engagements by decomposing client facts into likely forms, jurisdiction questions, source documents, and items still requiring current guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for users handling U.S. federal, state, local, and foreign-reporting tax scoping.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may handle sensitive tax, state, and foreign-account facts during scoping.

Mitigation: Limit collection and sharing of client facts to what is needed for form scoping and follow the user's normal tax-data handling controls.

Risk: Form-family mapping can become misleading if treated as tax advice or used to answer current thresholds, dates, eligibility rules, or filing obligations.

Mitigation: Use the output as a scoping aid and verify thresholds, dates, eligibility, and obligations against current IRS and state guidance or a qualified tax professional.
