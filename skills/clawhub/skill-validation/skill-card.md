## Description:

Validates skill folders against ClawHub publishing standards, including structure, metadata, triggers, content quality, security, and publishing readiness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, skill authors, and reviewers use this skill to check ClawHub skill folders before creating, editing, reviewing, publishing, or deploying a skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Malformed metadata may make the skill parse or activate unreliably.

Mitigation: Repair the published SKILL.md frontmatter before installation or publication.

Risk: The added compliance layer includes broad memory, observability, and self-improvement language beyond a normal validation checklist.

Mitigation: Remove or clearly scope those behaviors to the skill-validation task before use.
