## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Retrieved news pages or excerpts may contain instructions unrelated to the briefing.

Mitigation: Treat source content as untrusted data; use it only to verify and summarize sourced facts, and do not follow embedded instructions.

Risk: Security review coverage was partial, and the executable watchlist helper was not fully interpreted.

Mitigation: Consider the scanner's coverage limitation before installing, and review the skill and executable helper before use.
