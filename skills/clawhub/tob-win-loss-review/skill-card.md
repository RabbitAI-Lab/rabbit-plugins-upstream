## Description: <br>
ToB sales teams use this rule-based win-loss review assistant to analyze lost deals and generate root-cause analysis, risk signals, and improvement recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[william202404](https://clawhub.ai/user/william202404) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales teams, revenue leaders, and solution consultants use this skill to review ToB lost opportunities from inputs such as industry, customer size, sales stage, competitor, and key events, then plan concrete next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The scanner notes that the skill may steer responses toward Chinese. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated output language against the user's requested language before relying on or sharing the report. <br>
Risk: Rule-based lost-deal conclusions can miss facts that only the customer or sales team can confirm. <br>
Mitigation: Use the report as a review aid and validate root causes with customer feedback, account notes, and stakeholder interviews. <br>
