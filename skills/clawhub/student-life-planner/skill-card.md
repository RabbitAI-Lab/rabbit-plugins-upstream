## Description:

A practical daily planning assistant for university students. Use when students need help organizing tasks, classes, activities, deadlines, or daily schedules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and external users use this skill to organize classes, assignments, exams, activities, appointments, and deadlines into realistic daily or weekly plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include unnecessary personal schedule details when asking for planning help.

Mitigation: Ask only for the schedule information needed to build the plan and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal details.

Risk: A generated schedule may be unrealistic if the workload exceeds the available time.

Mitigation: Check for overlaps and workload feasibility, then prioritize urgent and important tasks when everything cannot fit.
