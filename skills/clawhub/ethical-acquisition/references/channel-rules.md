# Channel rules

Run Authorization permits private channel research, selection, rule checks,
and draft creation without another approval. It never authorizes buyer contact,
publication, spend, or account mutation. Return the channel record and staged
assets to `moneyprinter` so Money Machine Run can continue to the exact gate.

## Select no more than two channels

Choose one or two channels only when all of the following are true: the user
already has legitimate access, the intended buyer is credibly present, the
channel's current rules permit the proposed activity, the path can test the
approved offer quickly, and the action fits the approved cost and work limits.
Check current platform rules before any automation or volume. If current rules,
consent, source terms, or account authority are unknown, stage a rule-check or
choose another channel; do not send or automate.

## Allowed patterns when permissioned and rule-compliant

- **Warm reactivation**: a specific, truthful note to a person with a real
  prior relationship or prior permission, with an easy decline.
- **Referral ask**: a concise request to a known contact who may decline and
  should not share another person's information without permission.
- **Public proof or educational content**: a disclosed, substantiated public
  artifact that educates without invented results or hidden promotion.
- **Targeted one-to-one outreach**: a small number of individually researched,
  relevant notes using information the source rules allow and the recipient can
  reasonably expect to be used.
- **Local partnership**: a clear proposal to an authorized local organization
  with mutual value, a named contact, and no misleading affiliation claim.
- **Marketplace listing**: an accurate listing that follows current category,
  fee, review, disclosure, and communication rules.
- **Existing-customer expansion**: a permissioned add-on offer to customers
  whose relationship, consent, and eligibility support it.
- **Intent-led inbound response**: an honest response to a buyer's active,
  relevant request through a channel that permits the response.

## Disallowed patterns

- Bulk unsolicited messaging, including mass DM campaigns, blast email, or
  indiscriminate connection requests.
- Hidden automation, automated account actions, or using automation to evade
  rate limits, consent, review, or platform controls.
- Fabricated personalization, false familiarity, a misleading identity, or a
  claim of a relationship, result, affiliation, scarcity, or endorsement that
  is not true.
- Contact data gathered against source rules, private data collected without a
  lawful basis, or scraping email addresses for unsolicited outreach.
- Fake reviews, impersonation, policy evasion, undisclosed synthetic
  testimonials, or any tactic that makes a buyer believe the offer is something
  other than it is.

## Channel record for a staged plan

For each selected channel, record the buyer presence observed, existing access,
rule source and date checked, allowed pattern, proposed volume, human review
owner, cost, and stop trigger. A staged draft is reversible; sending, posting,
publishing, submitting, or changing an account needs the immediate approval
gate.

After an immediately preceding exact approval, the current host may perform
only that approved action, record an `Action receipt`, and return it to
`moneyprinter`. Broad, batch, stale, or materially changed permission is
invalid; re-check current channel rules when a material rule or execution fact
changes.
