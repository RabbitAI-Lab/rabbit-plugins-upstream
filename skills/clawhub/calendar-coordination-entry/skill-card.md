## Description:

Normalizes calendar handoff data and creates a coordination calendar entry for attendee and agenda follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external collaborators, and coordination teams use this skill to normalize meeting handoff blocks and create calendar artifacts for attendee and agenda follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated calendar entries may contain incorrect timing, location, attendee, or agenda details if the handoff data is incomplete or malformed.

Mitigation: Review the generated calendar artifact before adding it to a real calendar or sharing it with attendees.
