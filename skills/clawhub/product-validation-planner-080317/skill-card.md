## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, checklists, analysis, and user-facing positioning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps turn early product concepts into practical validation workflows, reusable checklists, lightweight analysis, prototypes, positioning artifacts, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The broad activation wording may select the skill for general product or startup requests outside product validation planning. <br>
Mitigation: Use narrower trigger wording or invocation policy when stricter routing is required. <br>
Risk: Product validation outputs can be mistaken for definitive market proof. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, test recommendations with target users, and treat generated plans as decision support rather than guaranteed market evidence. <br>
