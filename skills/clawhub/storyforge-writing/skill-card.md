## Description:

Guides agents through StoryForge-aligned narrative writing and structured drafts for fiction, interactive stories, comics, and motion drama.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[taosiuman](https://clawhub.ai/user/taosiuman)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers and creative teams use this skill to draft project-aware story frameworks, character and world material, scripts, storyboards, and related production text. It checks the relevant project references and keeps generated material subject to author review before adoption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project-aware drafting may read sensitive story or reference files and surface their contents in outputs.

Mitigation: Limit inputs to relevant, authorized project files and review drafts before sharing.

Risk: Incomplete or outdated project references can produce inaccurate or inconsistent story material.

Mitigation: Check source context and provenance, validate structured outputs, and obtain author confirmation before adoption.
