## Description:

Guides Chinese-language StoryForge writing across fiction, interactive stories, scripts, comics, and motion drama using structured creative workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[taosiuman](https://clawhub.ai/user/taosiuman)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers and creative teams use this skill to develop structured story materials, from character and world plans to scripts and visual-production prompts, with author review before adoption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project and knowledge files may contain sensitive creative material.

Mitigation: Review which local files the workflow may read and limit access to the project materials needed for the task.

Risk: External media-generation or post-production tools may be invoked outside the writing workflow.

Mitigation: Review and authorize those tools separately, and use them only when explicitly requested.
