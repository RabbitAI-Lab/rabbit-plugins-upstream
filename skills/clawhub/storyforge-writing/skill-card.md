## Description:

Guides StoryForge-aligned creative writing and adaptation, producing structured story materials, scripts, visual prompts, and import-ready framework data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[taosiuman](https://clawhub.ai/user/taosiuman)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and creative teams use this skill to plan and draft StoryForge-style fiction, interactive narratives, screenplays, comics, and motion-drama materials, with structured framework data for optional user-managed import.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project stories and research materials may contain sensitive content.

Mitigation: Use only in projects where reading local story and knowledge files is expected, and review generated materials before sharing.

Risk: Optional media-production tools can create or modify outputs.

Mitigation: Review post-production workflows before using provider or DaVinci MCP tools, and keep adoption and imports under user control.
