## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language solution architecture and presales workbench that helps agents draft architecture diagrams, proposals, ADRs, RFP responses, SOWs, presentation structures, and customer communication materials. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yinjianheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/yinjianheng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Personal use only <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Solution architects, presales consultants, enterprise architects, and proposal teams use this skill to turn customer context into structured architecture, proposal, bid, and delivery-planning artifacts. It is best suited for drafting and organizing materials that will be reviewed before customer use. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad architecture and proposal triggers may activate the skill in many presales or solution-design conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Review whether the skill is appropriate for the current task before relying on generated deliverables. <br>
Risk: Generated business, legal, security, or technical claims may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific customer. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers verify claims, assumptions, and commitments before sharing outputs externally. <br>
Risk: The artifact is designed to append the author's legal notice and contact details to responses. <br>
Mitigation: Check generated customer-facing materials for unwanted notices, attribution, or contact details before use. <br>
