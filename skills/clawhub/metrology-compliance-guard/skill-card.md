## Description:

计量检测合规护栏。在检定证书、校准报告、检测文书、计量授权文件发布前，实时检测是否符合《计量法》《计量法实施细则》《法定计量检定机构监督管理办法》及JJF 1069等规范要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Metrology, inspection, laboratory, and compliance teams use this skill to review Chinese metrology certificates, calibration reports, testing documents, authorization files, ledgers, qualification materials, and promotional claims before publication. It produces a structured review report with compliant items, warnings, blocking non-compliance findings, violated provisions, and suggested revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the skill's compliance report as a substitute for current legal, regulatory, or institutional review.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a compliance drafting aid and require qualified human review before releasing certificates, reports, authorization documents, or public claims.

Risk: The skill can flag blocking non-compliance in user-submitted documents, but it depends on the completeness and accuracy of the submitted text.

Mitigation: Review source documents, authorization records, personnel qualifications, instrument validity periods, and applicable current regulations alongside the generated findings.
