## Description: <br>
Helps agents integrate multiple ISO-style management systems by capturing system structures, mapping requirements, analyzing compatibility, and generating Markdown manuals, comparison tables, and reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Quality, environmental, occupational health and safety, information security, food safety, energy management, and compliance teams use this skill to consolidate ISO-style management systems into shared mappings, gap analyses, and draft integrated management documents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated manuals, mapping tables, and compatibility reports may be used as compliance artifacts before they are tailored to the organization. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with qualified internal system owners or auditors before using them in a real compliance program. <br>
Risk: Compatibility analysis is based on preset ISO-style frameworks and may miss organization-specific obligations, controls, or conflicts. <br>
Mitigation: Compare generated drafts against the organization's actual standards, procedures, legal obligations, and audit requirements before adoption. <br>
