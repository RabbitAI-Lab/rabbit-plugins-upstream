## Description: <br>
Investigates public business-cooperation risk signals for a target company, including litigation, arbitration, bankruptcy, penalties, sanctions, export controls, negative news, payment credit, identity, and operational indicators. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business, procurement, compliance, finance, and partnership teams use this skill to structure public due-diligence research on customers, suppliers, and partners. It helps confirm the entity and jurisdiction, separate confirmed facts from third-party reporting or unresolved signals, and document source-backed risk findings without providing legal, banking, sanctions, or credit advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Business-risk research outputs could influence commercial decisions if users treat them as legal, banking, sanctions, or credit advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as public-risk research aids only, and have qualified reviewers verify cited sources, entity identity, jurisdiction, and decision relevance before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Search gaps, same-name entities, stale records, or source conflicts could lead to incorrect attribution or overconfident conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Require formal company identity, jurisdiction, risk scope, time range, and cooperation context; distinguish confirmed facts from third-party reporting and unresolved signals; and never treat missing records as proof of no risk. <br>
Risk: Chinese-language output may be difficult for some reviewers to audit accurately. <br>
Mitigation: Request output in a language the reviewer can understand and verify before using the findings. <br>
