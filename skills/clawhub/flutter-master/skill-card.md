## Description: <br>
Flutter Master provides comprehensive Flutter and Dart guidance for building, reviewing, refactoring, debugging, optimizing, auditing, and shipping mobile applications. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to create, audit, refactor, debug, optimize, and prepare Flutter apps for release. It supports architecture, state management, performance, security, dependency health, testing, UX quality, and store readiness work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill has a broad trigger and may apply more Flutter audit guidance than a quick question needs. <br>
Mitigation: Use the guidance proportionally to the task, and keep quick answers focused unless a broader review is requested. <br>
Risk: The bundled audit script runs Flutter, Dart, dependency, and grep-based checks against a project and produces candidate findings. <br>
Mitigation: Run the script only on projects intended for review, then verify its output before treating any candidate hit as a finding. <br>
