## Description:

建立與部署融合東方易經智慧與現代網頁設計的「賽博周易 (Cyber Zhouyi)」官方網站與互動起卦應用。適用於：打造支援白棕黑日間主題、黑金夜間主題、繁簡英三語切換、三幣六爻起卦、本機歷史紀錄與 HTML5 Canvas 分享卡片圖片匯出的高質感前端專案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and designers use this skill to build a polished Cyber Zhouyi website and interactive divination app with theming, multilingual UI, a 64-hexagram model, local browser history, and Canvas-based share-card export.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Divination questions and readings may contain private information and can be retained in browser-local history.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive questions out of shared browsers, provide and use clear-history controls, and clear local browser storage when records should not persist.
