### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post may be sent publicly or to the wrong destination if visibility or target channels are assumed.

Mitigation: Confirm visibility and destination channels with the user before publishing, and set visibility explicitly where supported.

Risk: Missing titles or incompatible media may cause a post to fail or publish differently than intended.

Mitigation: Check each selected platform's required titles, post type, and media constraints before submitting the post.
