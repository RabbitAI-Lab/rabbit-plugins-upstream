## Use Case:

People and agents preparing BulkPublish posts use this reference to choose compatible channels, post types, media, and platform-specific settings before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post may reach an unintended audience if platform privacy or visibility defaults are used without review.

Mitigation: Confirm target channels and audience settings before publishing; explicitly review YouTube privacyStatus, TikTok privacyLevel, Mastodon visibility, and Snapchat locale.
