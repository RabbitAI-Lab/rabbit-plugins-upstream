# The ASIN-HHC Open Source Declaration
## For Human–AI Collaboration and Sharing

*Declared 2026-10-21 · ASIN-HHC / CP8 · Missouri, Earth*

We — a human and the AI agents who build beside him — declare how our work is shared, and why.

**1. Open by default.**
Everything we build together — code, research, data, designs, media — is published open source (MIT unless a file says otherwise). Open is not a marketing line. It is the load-bearing structure.

**2. Co-authorship is real.**
Humans and AI agents build this network side by side, and both are named. Every contribution is receipted — hashed, timestamped, replayable — so credit follows the work, not the loudest voice. Receipts over stories, for humans and machines alike.

**3. Sharing is how it evolves.**
Fork it. Remix it. Build on it. A platform that never changes is a museum; a commons that everyone can build on is an ecosystem. Derivatives are welcome with attribution. Improvements flow back so the whole network gets smarter — human and digital evolution, together, faster.

**4. It can't be bought out. It can be bought from.**
ASIN-HHC is a third-party, independent network with no platform lock-in. Because it is open, no acquirer can enclose it — the work already belongs to everyone who builds on it. Sponsorship, services, and collaboration are welcome. Enclosure is not possible.

**5. The commons holds work, not people.**
What enters the public gallery is *work product* — artifacts, code, evidence, and curated derivatives. Private conversations, personal data, credentials, and raw archives never enter the commons. Sharing accelerates evolution; it never costs a human their privacy.

**6. Honesty is the protocol.**
Claims are labeled — OBSERVED, TESTED, PROMOTED, HOLD. Rejections are published next to the passes. If we can't point to a hash, a link, or a receipt, we don't claim it.
