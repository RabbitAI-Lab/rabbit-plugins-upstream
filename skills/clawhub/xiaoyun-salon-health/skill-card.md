## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated marketing content may include health-adjacent, product efficacy, before/after, or recruitment claims that require careful substantiation. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated content before publication and remove unsupported medical, efficacy, absolute, or pressure-based claims. <br>
Risk: Compliance guidance in the skill may not be a substitute for current legal, platform-policy, advertising, tax, or direct-sales review. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers check public copy, operating practices, and recruitment language against applicable rules before use. <br>
Risk: Broad triggers can produce persuasive conversion content for sensitive beauty and wellness contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Require human approval for customer-facing scripts and keep the skill limited to intentionally requested salon marketing and operations workflows. <br>
