## Description:

Write, schedule, publish and measure the user's LinkedIn posts through their ContentIn account (ContentIn for Agents MCP server). Use when the user wants a LinkedIn post in their own voice, post ideas, a past post repurposed, a story or number saved for later posts, a post scheduled or published to LinkedIn, or results for their posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kinzi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kinzi)

## Use Case:

LinkedIn users can use the skill to draft posts in their voice, collect ideas and material, repurpose past posts, and review post performance through their ContentIn account. It can also schedule or publish a post after the user reviews and explicitly confirms it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reported no findings, but its evidence is incomplete because one referenced local path could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Check whether the unresolved reference matters before installing.

Risk: A LinkedIn post published through ContentIn cannot be undone from ContentIn.

Mitigation: Review the exact post text and confirm explicitly before publishing; scheduled posts can be unscheduled before they go out.
