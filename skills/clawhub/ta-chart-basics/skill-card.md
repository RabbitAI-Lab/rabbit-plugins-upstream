## Description: <br>
Guides agents through reading and drawing basic financial or futures charts, choosing chart type, time scale, arithmetic versus logarithmic scale, and interpreting volume or open interest displays. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and market-analysis agents use this skill for educational chart-reading guidance: selecting chart types and time scales, choosing arithmetic or logarithmic scales, and understanding volume and open-interest displays. It is not intended to make buy/sell decisions or replace financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the educational technical-analysis basics as trading advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for chart-reading and plotting guidance only, and require separate review before any investment decision. <br>
Risk: A candlestick OHLC question may be routed poorly according to the security guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Check candlestick-specific OHLC requests and redirect to a more specific chart-reading or pattern skill when needed. <br>
