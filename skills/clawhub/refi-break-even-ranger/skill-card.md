## Description:

Helps evaluate mortgage refinance offers by calculating break-even timing, estimated savings over a chosen stay horizon, and the effects of points, cash-out, and loan-term changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Borrowers and financial educators use the skill to compare refinance quotes against their current mortgage and intended time in the loan, accounting for upfront costs and changes in equity buildup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Refinance estimates can mislead if final closing costs, PMI, taxes, insurance, or the time the borrower keeps the loan differ from assumptions.

Mitigation: Treat results as decision support, not financial advice; verify final Loan Estimates and horizon assumptions with current lender documents or a qualified advisor.
