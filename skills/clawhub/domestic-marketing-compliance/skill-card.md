## Description:

Guides agents in reviewing and rewriting China domestic marketing copy for advertising-law and platform-specific compliance across WeChat, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and related channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveljack](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveljack)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and compliance users can use this skill to help an agent identify prohibited or risky terms, apply platform-specific constraints, and suggest safer wording for China domestic marketing content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reusable examples may contradict the compliance rules and cause an agent to generate platform-risky or legally questionable marketing claims.

Mitigation: Review the Xiaohongshu methodology and title-template sections before use, and remove or rewrite examples that conflict with the stated compliance rules.

Risk: Marketing copy for regulated categories may still require review beyond automated wording changes.

Mitigation: Require human compliance review for sensitive sectors such as finance, real estate, education, medical products, food, and health-related claims.
