## Description:

Edit and reframe 16:9 videos into 9:16 vertical short-form videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts (短视频 / 视频剪辑) with active-speaker face tracking, crop/resize, and 280+ animated captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and video editors use this skill to turn landscape footage into short vertical videos, track the active speaker, trim clips, and add animated captions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edit prompts and media URLs are sent to Levea for remote processing, and private footage may be exposed if made publicly accessible.

Mitigation: Use only footage you are authorized to share with the service; avoid confidential footage unless remote processing is acceptable, and do not make private footage publicly accessible unless authorized.
