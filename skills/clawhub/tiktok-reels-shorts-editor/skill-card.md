## Description:

Reframe horizontal 16:9 footage into 9:16 vertical short-form videos with subject tracking and captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and editors use this skill to turn landscape footage into captioned vertical shorts, keeping the speaker in frame and trimming clips to a short-form duration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source videos are processed by the configured video-editing backend.

Mitigation: Use only a backend you trust with the videos you provide.

Risk: An important edit may be committed before the user reviews the plan.

Mitigation: Set requirePlanApproval to pause for confirmation before committing changes.
