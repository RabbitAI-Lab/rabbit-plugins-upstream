## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Screen capture, audio capture, CloudKit, process monitoring, login item, and daemon examples can affect user privacy or persistence behavior when copied into an application. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit opt-in, visible controls, data minimization, redaction, and clear disable or uninstall paths before shipping these patterns. <br>
Risk: Remote install or distribution commands may execute external tooling or publish signed artifacts if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Verify scripts, signing identities, entitlements, notarization settings, and distribution targets before execution. <br>
