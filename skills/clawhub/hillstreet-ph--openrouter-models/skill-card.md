This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to discover models, compare capabilities and pricing, inspect model details, and select or route tasks across providers through OpenRouter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Model routing and parallel use may send prompts or attached context to multiple third-party providers.

Mitigation: Review OpenRouter and provider data-handling terms, and avoid sending sensitive content unless that sharing is acceptable.
