## Description:

Plans international mobile connectivity, compares travel data options, checks account-access risks, and prepares a pre-departure checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

International travelers and people helping them prepare use this skill to choose a data plan, keep account recovery available abroad, compare connectivity costs, and follow a departure checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Roaming and inbound SMS charges or availability may differ by carrier, affecting both costs and account access.

Mitigation: Confirm roaming, inbound SMS, and plan terms with the home carrier before departure.

Risk: Incorrect account-recovery settings or exposed backup codes could cause lockout or compromise.

Mitigation: Test recovery methods with each provider and keep backup codes secure and offline.

Risk: Destination connectivity and device-registration rules may change.

Mitigation: Check current requirements with the destination's official authorities and mobile provider before relying on the checklist.
