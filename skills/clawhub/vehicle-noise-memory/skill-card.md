## Description:

Helps auto shops record vehicle-noise cases and recall similar past repairs with confirmed fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Mechanics, service writers, and fleet technicians at independent shops and fleet garages can log vehicle noises and compare new symptoms with past cases and their confirmed fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Repair notes, tags, queries, or audio URLs may expose customer or vehicle identifiers.

Mitigation: Avoid customer names, phone numbers, VINs, license plates, payment data, and unnecessary identifiers; confirm the provider's retention, deletion, access-control, and privacy terms before using real repair orders.

Risk: Recording or uploading vehicle-service audio may lack required consent or shop policy coverage.

Mitigation: Confirm appropriate consent and shop policy coverage before recording or uploading audio.
