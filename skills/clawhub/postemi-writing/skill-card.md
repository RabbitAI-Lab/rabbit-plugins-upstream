## Description:

Helps users develop ideas and write or improve social media posts, hooks, headlines, captions, and threads for supported networks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[norbertlevente](https://clawhub.ai/user/norbertlevente)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use the skill to find post ideas and draft or improve social posts, threads, captions, hooks, and headlines while keeping claims grounded in their experience or named sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post could be sent to the wrong account or with unsuitable content or timing.

Mitigation: Review the destination account, post content, and schedule in the preview, then approve before scheduling.

Risk: Publishing relies on the separate Postemi integration and main skill, which are outside this package's review.

Mitigation: Review those components before enabling publishing.
