## Description:

Builds branching call scripts, objection-response trees, printable cheat sheets, and practice drills for difficult phone calls such as billing disputes, cancellations, rate negotiations, landlord repair demands, insurance disputes, collections calls, vendor complaints, salary asks, and sales cold calls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prepare for high-stakes phone calls by turning their goal, leverage, fallback, and likely objections into a structured script and rehearsal flow. It is especially useful for consumer billing, cancellation, collections, insurance, landlord, vendor, sales, and rate-negotiation calls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated call scripts may include personal account details and negotiation plans saved locally in ~/.call-scripts.

Mitigation: Review generated files before sharing, avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details, and protect or delete local saved scripts when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Collections, landlord, insurance, and similar rights-related scripts may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as preparation only and verify local law or consult a lawyer or licensed consumer debt counselor before making admissions, payments, disputes, or threats.
