## Description:

Run social media on autopilot for a small business by turning a website or topic into a month of posts, carousels and videos, getting approval, scheduling through Postmately MCP, and measuring what worked.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Small business owners can use an AI agent to plan a month of social content, prepare platform-ready posts and media, review drafts, schedule approved posts, and learn from performance metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The installer is not pinned to a reviewed version.

Mitigation: Review the installer and pin a known release before installing.

Risk: Using the Postmately MCP service may send user-provided content to an external service.

Mitigation: Before connecting, review what information will be sent and whether Postmately is trusted to process it.
