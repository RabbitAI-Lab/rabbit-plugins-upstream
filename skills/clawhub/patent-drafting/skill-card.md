## Description:

Creates an initial patent-draft Word document from invention disclosures, R&D materials, technical proposals, or an early idea.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and patent professionals use it to turn invention disclosures, R&D documents, technical proposals, or an initial idea into a structured patent-draft Word document and review notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authenticated requests may reach untrusted destinations, credentials may be stored or transmitted insecurely, and unpublished invention materials may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Use a protected environment for credentials, restrict authenticated requests to trusted HTTPS destinations, and confirm permission and PatentMax confidentiality terms before uploading unpublished materials.
