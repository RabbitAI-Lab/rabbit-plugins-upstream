## Description:

Turns R&D materials, technical proposals, or an initial idea into a structured patent-application first draft with claims, drawings, an abstract, and review notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and patent professionals use this skill to turn invention disclosures, R&D reports, technical proposals, or an initial idea into a structured patent-application first draft and review notes. A qualified patent professional should review the draft before filing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
