## Description:

Turns R&D materials or an idea into a preliminary patent draft and supporting review notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Inventors, R&D teams, and patent professionals use it to turn technical disclosure materials or an idea into a preliminary patent draft and review notes. The draft is a starting point for professional review, not a filing-ready application or patentability opinion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: API-key storage and network handling warrant review before installation.

Mitigation: Keep permanent keys out of chat, use a limited credential, and before sensitive use require secure credential-file creation and HTTPS requests that reject redirects to other origins.

Risk: Patent materials may undergo third-party processing and retention.

Mitigation: Only approve materials that may be shared externally and review PatentMax's [privacy policy](https://www.ip930.com/legal/privacy) before use.
