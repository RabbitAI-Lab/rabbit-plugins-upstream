## Description:

Write and review distinctive, opinionated content that stands out instead of blending in -- blog posts, articles, social media captions, LinkedIn posts, threads, newsletters, ad copy, landing pages and scripts -- using the Purple Cow lens (Seth Godin + MrBeast framework).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to evaluate ideas through a Purple Cow lens and produce more distinctive, attention-oriented blog, social, newsletter, ad, landing page, and script content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer general writing requests toward punchier, promotional, or virality-focused output.

Mitigation: Ask for a specific tone or explicitly tell the agent not to apply the marketing or virality lens when a neutral style is required.
