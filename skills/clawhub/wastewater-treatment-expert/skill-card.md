## Description:

A senior wastewater treatment engineering agent for process design, equipment selection, commissioning, operations troubleshooting, and technical consulting across industrial and municipal wastewater scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and operations teams use this agent for wastewater process planning, design calculations, equipment and chemical selection, commissioning plans, operating diagnostics, and engineering consultation. The skill is especially oriented toward industrial wastewater settings such as pharmaceutical, chemical, semiconductor, textile, paper, petrochemical, municipal, and industrial park treatment projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wastewater calculations, chemical dosing, equipment choices, or discharge-standard guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific site.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory and verify calculations, chemical dosing, equipment selections, and current discharge standards with qualified engineers and local regulators before using them in real projects.

Risk: The artifact includes static reference content and no executable behavior, but engineering recommendations can still affect safety, compliance, and cost decisions.

Mitigation: Require professional review, project-specific assumptions, and site validation before procurement, commissioning, or operational changes.
