## Description: <br>
Provides conversational guidance for IPD stage, DCP decision review, and TR technical review readiness using user-provided context and bounded IPD reference criteria. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product, engineering, quality, and cross-functional teams use this skill to evaluate readiness and risks around IPD stages, DCP decisions, TR reviews, escalation paths, and cross-functional alignment. It supports advisory assessment and next-step guidance without replacing final IPMT or responsible-owner decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share more sensitive project material than needed for a stage-specific IPD consultation. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information relevant to the current IPD node, DCP, TR, or team issue, and avoid pasting full confidential project documents unless necessary. <br>
Risk: Advisory readiness guidance could be mistaken for a final investment, launch, or redirection decision. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's output as decision support only; final approval remains with IPMT or the responsible business owner. <br>
