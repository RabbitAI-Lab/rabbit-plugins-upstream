## Description: <br>
Researches and drafts Simplified Chinese literary analysis of "Ode to the Goddess of Luo River" (洛神赋), including literary significance, cultural context, links to Dream of the Red Chamber, Chan Buddhist dimensions, feminine imagery, citations, and textual annotations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, students, and literature researchers use this skill to produce structured, source-aware Chinese essays about 《洛神赋》, its reception, and its relationship to 《红楼梦》. It is intended as a drafting aid for literary research rather than an authoritative source. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated literary analysis may contain unsupported interpretations or inaccurate citations. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited scholarship, primary text references, and claims against authoritative academic or classical-text sources before publication. <br>
Risk: Web lookup results may surface non-academic or culturally informal sources. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer academic publications, authoritative classical-text databases, university or publisher pages, and clearly label unverified details as uncertain or omit them. <br>
