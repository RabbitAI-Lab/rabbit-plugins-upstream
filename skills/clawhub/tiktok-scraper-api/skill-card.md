## Description:

Enables agents to look up TikTok profiles, videos, comments, transcripts, hashtags, sounds, trends, playlists, and follower relationships through Scavio's API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and other users can research TikTok creators, videos, audience engagement, and trends using structured lookup results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok usernames, video IDs, search terms, and related lookup parameters are sent to Scavio under the user's account.

Mitigation: Use only when comfortable sharing those lookup parameters with Scavio.

Risk: Bulk collection or redistribution of comments, transcripts, follower lists, live status, or account region data may raise privacy and platform-compliance concerns.

Mitigation: Avoid bulk collection or profiling without a legitimate use; respect platform rules and applicable privacy law, and handle stored or shared data carefully.
