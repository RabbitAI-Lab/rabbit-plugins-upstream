## Description: <br>
Guides agents through user interview planning, participant recruitment, interview execution, transcript synthesis, and product-market-fit decision making. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, founders, and growth operators use this skill to structure customer discovery interviews, avoid opinion-led questions, synthesize repeated pain points, and turn interview transcripts into product decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Interview recordings, screen shares, and transcripts may contain personal data, credentials, customer information, or confidential business details. <br>
Mitigation: Obtain explicit participant consent for screen sharing, recording, transcription, AI processing, and downstream sharing; redact sensitive information before use. <br>
Risk: Sending transcripts to AI tools or issue trackers can spread private or confidential interview content beyond the intended audience. <br>
Mitigation: Follow organizational retention and privacy rules, limit downstream sharing, and remove personal or confidential details before creating feature requests or bug reports. <br>
