## Description:

A Chinese ecommerce operations skill that helps agents evaluate products, calculate pricing and advertising ROI, diagnose conversion funnels, check promotional copy for advertising-law risk, and consult platform operations playbooks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[g305595965](https://clawhub.ai/user/g305595965)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and ecommerce operators use this skill to support Chinese marketplace decisions across product selection, pricing, paid traffic, conversion diagnosis, listing content, live commerce, and compliance review. Agents can call local calculators and consult reference playbooks to produce decision support, markdown guidance, shell commands, code-oriented tool usage, and JSON-formatted calculations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform fees, benchmark ranges, and legal-compliance checks may be incomplete or outdated for current Chinese ecommerce platforms and regulations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and verify important values against current platform merchant dashboards, official platform notices, and qualified regulatory or legal sources before acting.
