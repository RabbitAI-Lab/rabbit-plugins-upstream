## Description: <br>
Author a new, complete, spec-compliant academic paper from a requirement and/or topic, with required length, section, citation-format, and citation-existence checks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vincentjiang06](https://clawhub.ai/user/vincentjiang06) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to turn an English or Chinese academic-paper brief into a Markdown or plain-text draft with stated length, section, citation-format, and citation-existence checks before delivery. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Academic-policy misuse if generated papers are submitted where drafting assistance is not allowed. <br>
Mitigation: Follow the applicable school, publisher, or workplace rules before using or submitting any generated paper. <br>
Risk: A citation ledger or structural citation check may be mistaken for proof that every source is real and supports the claim. <br>
Mitigation: Require real source lookup for each citation and do not treat form checks or ledger presence alone as proof. <br>
Risk: Unverified or unsupported sources can create fabricated or misattributed citations. <br>
Mitigation: Replace unresolved citations with verified sources or mark the claim with the skill's SOURCE NEEDED convention before delivery. <br>
