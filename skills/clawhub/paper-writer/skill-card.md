## Description:

Drafts new English or Chinese academic papers from a topic or requirements, with citation checks and a compliance report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vincentjiang06](https://clawhub.ai/user/vincentjiang06)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and researchers use this skill to draft a new essay, thesis chapter, or literature review against specified length, structure, language, and citation requirements where AI-assisted drafting is permitted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Complete AI-drafted papers may conflict with academic-integrity or disclosure rules.

Mitigation: Use only where AI drafting is permitted, disclose assistance as required, and review before submission.

Risk: Citation-form checks do not prove a source exists or supports a claim; independent lookup may be unavailable.

Mitigation: Verify citations against sources; when independent verification is unavailable, label self-checks and mark unconfirmed claims [SOURCE NEEDED].
