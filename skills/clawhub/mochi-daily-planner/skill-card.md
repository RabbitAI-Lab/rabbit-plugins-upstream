## Description:

Organize daily priorities and available time, track completion and carry unfinished tasks to a later date. Use for 每日计划与复盘.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hznetnewpower](https://clawhub.ai/user/hznetnewpower)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and other personal productivity users can plan daily tasks around priorities, estimated durations, and available time, then record completions and carry unfinished work forward without losing its original date.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Task descriptions and history are stored as unencrypted JSON in the active workspace.

Mitigation: Use the intended workspace and include only information you are comfortable storing there.
