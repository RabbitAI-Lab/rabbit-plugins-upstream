## Description: <br>
Analyzes marketing pages and forms to provide actionable conversion rate optimization recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, growth, product, and web teams use this skill to audit homepages, landing pages, pricing pages, feature pages, blog conversion paths, and lead capture forms. It helps identify conversion blockers and produce prioritized quick wins, high-impact changes, A/B test ideas, and copy alternatives. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad page-feedback requests where the user did not explicitly ask for conversion optimization. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the page goal and whether the user wants CRO-focused feedback before giving detailed recommendations when intent is ambiguous. <br>
Risk: Recommendations may be incomplete if local product-marketing context files are outdated or missing. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for current conversion goals, traffic sources, audience, and funnel context before prioritizing changes. <br>
