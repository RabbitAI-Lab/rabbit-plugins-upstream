## Description: <br>
Helps system managers and audit program administrators structure auditor qualification and competency matrices, distinguish them from exam review, and produce qualification matrix and maintenance plan reports in plain text and Markdown. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
System managers and audit program administrators use this skill to collect auditor qualification evidence, track validity and maintenance requirements, and generate auditable qualification matrices and annual maintenance plans. It is intended for qualification and competency record management, not exam scoring or final legal or certification decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive employee qualification records such as names, IDs, certificates, training records, and audit experience. <br>
Mitigation: Use organization-approved data handling practices, limit inputs to necessary qualification evidence, and review generated reports before sharing or archiving. <br>
Risk: The skill generates local report files that could replace existing reports with the same names. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the output location and filenames before generation, and preserve prior reports when version history or audit traceability is required. <br>
Risk: Qualification status guidance may be incomplete when enterprise procedures, certification rules, or source records are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Treat missing fields as action items for enterprise completion and validate final qualification decisions against the organization's procedures and applicable certification requirements. <br>
