## Description:

This skill turns a topic brief, research materials, vendor case, policy event, or industry question into a publish-ready single deep-dive article for technology, AI, data, cloud, or enterprise-software audiences, ending at an approved Markdown article plus evidence and review records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External authors, analysts, and editorial teams use this skill to turn technology, AI, data, cloud, or enterprise-software topics into evidence-backed industry deep-dive articles with source verification, originality review, human checkpoints, review records, and a final Markdown draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can produce misleading editorial conclusions if facts, dates, or source materials are incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Use the required fact table, first-hand source tracing, originality review, machine gate, and human checkpoints before accepting the final Markdown article.

Risk: The workflow is heavier than needed for quick summaries, generic research, tutorials, marketing copy, or publishing automation.

Mitigation: Invoke it only for publish-ready industry deep dives that need source verification, review records, and human editorial decisions.
