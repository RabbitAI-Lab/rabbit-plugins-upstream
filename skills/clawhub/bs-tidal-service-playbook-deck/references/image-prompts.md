# Image briefs

## Picture policy

SKILL.md Workflow step 5 is the only picture policy: user pictures first, then the library question, then generation with the tools it names. When you generate, use one request per distinct scene with a full text prompt, at the `aspect` that `style-kit.json` and the compose report list for the slot. One or two pictures from this Skill's library or pictures already made for this deck may be passed as style references so the series keeps one look; never pass reference-template images, source photographs, logos, slide screenshots, or photos of real people.

Every pictorial page needs its own image; never place the same picture on two pages. Keep the finished deck under 20 MB.

## Shared style line

Append to every brief: "Photorealistic editorial photograph, soft natural daylight, calm premium hospitality interior, navy and ice-blue accents with warm wood and white marble, no text, no signage, no logos, no brand marks, no readable screens."

## Slot briefs

Landscape 3:2 is only the default for a new slot that has no listed aspect.

| Slot | Page | Brief | Crop notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Cover | A guest with a rolling suitcase walking through open glass doors toward a navy-panelled front desk where a teammate smiles. | Subject in the right two-thirds; the left edge is cut diagonally. |
| Greeting | Statement + metrics | A desk teammate in a navy top greeting an arriving guest across a marble counter with an open-hand gesture. | Bleeds off the left and bottom; keep faces in the upper half. |
| Desk detail | Checklists + portrait | An empty, prepared front desk: open notebook, pen pot, lamp, screen, softly blurred lobby behind. | Cropped to a tall portrait window; keep the notebook centred. |
| Handover | Steps + photo | Two teammates reviewing a handover notebook together at the counter. | Full-height right half; leave a quiet lower-left area for the stat card. |
| Lobby | Closing arch | A sunlit lobby with a teammate checking in a guest at a panelled desk, plants and a rug in front. | Cropped to an arch; keep the desk interaction right of centre. |

Write each prompt to a text file, record the prompt, output path, and file hash beside the user's deck, and reopen every page after embedding to check crops.
