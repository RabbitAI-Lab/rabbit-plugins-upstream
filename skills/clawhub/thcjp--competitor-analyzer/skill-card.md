## Description:

Analyzes competitor products by collecting titles, prices, sales, reviews, and differentiated recommendations for product research, monitoring, pricing, and competitor analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, marketplace operators, and product researchers use this skill to gather competitor market data, analyze trends and reviews, and produce reports for product selection, price monitoring, and positioning decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor research inputs and review data may be sent to configured MCP services.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive strategy, private customer data, or confidential product plans unless the configured services are approved for that data.

Risk: Generated reports may persist in memory.

Mitigation: Review memory retention settings and clear stored reports when they contain data that should not be retained.
