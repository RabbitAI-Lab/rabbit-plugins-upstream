## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to be invoked for ordinary Google Workspace, CLI, Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or bug-fix prompts.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation for sensitive work and confirm that the user's request matches the intended workflow-planning use case before applying the skill.
