Mitigation: Review citations, prefer academic or primary sources, and treat interpretive claims as arguments requiring source support. <br>
Risk: The skill depends on web_search and fetch_url, so source quality can vary. <br>
Mitigation: Verify key claims against authoritative texts or scholarly references before publication. <br>
