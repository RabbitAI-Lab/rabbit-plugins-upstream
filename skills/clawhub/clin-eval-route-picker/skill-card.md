## Description:

Helps select a medical-device clinical evaluation route under China's NMPA or the EU MDR using product classification and available evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Medical-device development and regulatory teams use this skill to compare clinical evaluation routes and identify supporting evidence before preparing submissions. Its recommendations support planning rather than replace professional regulatory review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (guidance covers China's NMPA and the EU MDR)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or inapplicable route recommendations may misdirect a clinical submission.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning support; have qualified regulatory or clinical professionals check the product and current official guidance before selecting a submission strategy.

Risk: The README's unpinned curl install command could fetch unreviewed code.

Mitigation: Install the reviewed package where possible; otherwise pin the download to a trusted commit and verify the file before running it.
