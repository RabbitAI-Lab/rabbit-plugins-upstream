## Description:

Helps track AI regulatory updates across jurisdictions by grouping local regulatory notes by theme and summarizing their potential impact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (Python code only); documentation and theory all rights reserved

## Use Case:

Compliance teams and developers use this skill to review local AI regulatory notes across the EU, US, and China, classify changes by theme, and prepare a source-traceable update summary. It does not replace current official texts or professional legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory notes may be incomplete or outdated, leading to misleading compliance conclusions.

Mitigation: Check consequential findings against current official regulatory texts and obtain qualified professional review before decisions.

Risk: Live lookups or report-writing workflows may access external sites or modify local files.

Mitigation: Approve web access and file-writing workflows explicitly before use.

Risk: The documentation is mostly Chinese and release provenance is unavailable.

Mitigation: Confirm the publisher and installation source, and review the instructions in a language you understand before installing.
