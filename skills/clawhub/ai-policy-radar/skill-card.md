## Description:

Helps agents track AI regulatory developments across jurisdictions by grouping changes in local policy notes by theme for human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance teams and developers use this skill to scan local AI regulatory notes and prepare themed policy-change summaries for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local notes may be incomplete or outdated, and keyword matches may not establish what a regulation requires.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated local folder of regulatory notes and verify changes against current official sources before relying on the results.

Risk: Policy summaries may be mistaken for legal advice or definitive compliance decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as compliance support and have qualified people review consequential decisions.
