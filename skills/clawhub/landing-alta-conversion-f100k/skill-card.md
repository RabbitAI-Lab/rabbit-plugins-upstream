## Description:

Builds a high-conversion tripwire or launch landing page with countdown, price anchoring, bonus stacking, guarantee, FAQ, creator bio, and GHL/Vercel-ready HTML.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and builders use this skill to collect a product brief and generate a Spanish-language sales landing page as a single self-contained HTML file for GHL, Vercel, or Netlify.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Synthetic testimonial or social-proof images can mislead viewers if presented as real customer evidence.

Mitigation: Require visible illustrative labeling for generated testimonials and use real customer names, results, screenshots, logos, and claims only with evidence and permission.
