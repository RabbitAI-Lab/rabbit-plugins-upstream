## Description: <br>
Generates a long Simplified Chinese research article investigating the identity of Zhiyanzhai, the anonymous commentator on Dream of the Red Chamber, across literary, historical, and poetic dimensions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External readers, educators, writers, and researchers use this skill to draft a citation-backed markdown article about Zhiyanzhai and major Redology debates. It is suited for academic discussion, educational material, and long-form literary content that still needs source review before publication. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated literary scholarship may include inaccurate, weakly sourced, or hard-to-verify claims about Zhiyanzhai and Redology. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated citations and key claims against academic sources before relying on or publishing the article. <br>
Risk: Article quality depends on available web research and the reliability of retrieved sources. <br>
Mitigation: Prioritize peer-reviewed articles, academic monographs, and recognized Redology references, and cross-check important claims across multiple sources. <br>
