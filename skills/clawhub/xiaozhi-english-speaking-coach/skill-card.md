## Description:

A Chinese-language English speaking coach that guides practice, reviews responses, and remembers speaking difficulties only with consent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking upper-primary and secondary-school learners use this skill for short English conversation warmups, roleplay, impromptu speaking, and supportive feedback. With appropriate consent, it can use a speaking-practice profile to personalize later sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's speaking-performance profile or practice history could be retained or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Check student and guardian consent where required; review profile access, parent-sharing, reminders, and deletion controls before use.

Risk: Text or speech transcription alone cannot establish pronunciation errors.

Mitigation: Only provide sound-level pronunciation judgments when the platform has audio scoring; otherwise limit feedback to wording and expression.
