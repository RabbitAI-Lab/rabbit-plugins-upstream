## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking students through English conversation practice, role-play, impromptu speaking, and feedback, with optional consent-based learning profiles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking K12 English learners use this skill for short speaking warm-ups, role-play, impromptu responses, and feedback. With consent, it can track practice history and pronunciation weaknesses across sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's speaking history or learning profile could be retained or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm guardian-consent and profile-sharing controls before enabling long-term memory; otherwise use session-only practice and offer view, correction, deletion, pause, and export controls.

Risk: Pronunciation feedback may be inaccurate if only typed text or speech transcription is available.

Mitigation: Reserve pronunciation-specific assessments for platforms with audio scoring; otherwise limit feedback to wording and expression.

Risk: Voice scoring and scheduled reminders may not work on every platform.

Mitigation: Confirm the platform's voice scoring, memory, reminder, and consent features before enabling them.
