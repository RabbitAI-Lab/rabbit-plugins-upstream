## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking K–12 learners through English conversation, role-play, speaking feedback, and consent-based progress review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking upper-primary, middle-school, and high-school students use this skill for short English speaking warmups, role-play, impromptu speaking, and post-practice feedback. Elementary learners should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize learning assumptions, consent requirements, and crisis resources for other regions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student voice-practice data and progress records may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Start practice only on clear request; obtain consent before reading or saving profile entries, and honor deletion and sharing controls.

Risk: Transcript-only input cannot substantiate pronunciation-specific judgments.

Mitigation: Limit feedback to wording and expression unless audio scoring is available; do not record pronunciation weaknesses from text alone.

Risk: Consent expectations and crisis contacts may not fit learners outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize consent practices and referral resources before deployment in other regions; involve a parent or teacher for elementary learners.
