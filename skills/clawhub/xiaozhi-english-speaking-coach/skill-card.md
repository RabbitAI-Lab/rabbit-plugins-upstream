## Description:

Guides students through English speaking practice with role-play, short speeches, pronunciation feedback, and optional consent-based progress tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary, middle, and high school use the skill to build confidence and fluency through guided English conversation, role-play, speaking prompts, and review. Upper-primary students need an adult present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Mainland); deployments elsewhere should localize the crisis-support guidance for the user's region.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Speaking practice and learning records can involve sensitive student data.

Mitigation: Use session-only practice when persistent records are not wanted. Before enabling profiles or reminders, review the Learning DNA dependency and the host's speech-service privacy settings.

Risk: Upper-primary students need adult supervision when using this open-ended practice skill.

Mitigation: Have a parent or teacher present during upper-primary use.

Risk: Crisis-support information may not match the user's location outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use the emergency and support channels for the user's region, and localize the crisis guidance before deploying elsewhere.
