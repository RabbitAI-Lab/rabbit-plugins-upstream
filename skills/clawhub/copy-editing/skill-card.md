## Description: <br>
Helps agents review, improve, and refresh existing marketing copy through structured editing sweeps while preserving the core message. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and agents use this skill to tighten existing landing pages, emails, product descriptions, testimonials, CTAs, and content refreshes. It is intended for improving and auditing existing copy rather than drafting new copy from scratch. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for casual requests to polish or improve text. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants copy-editing help and keep the response scoped to the provided copy. <br>
Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context files when present. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those files limited to appropriate brand and customer-language context, and avoid storing secrets or sensitive material in them. <br>
Risk: Suggested edits can accidentally introduce unsupported marketing claims, proof points, or compliance-sensitive wording. <br>
Mitigation: Verify statistics, testimonials, pricing, guarantees, and product claims before publishing edited copy. <br>
