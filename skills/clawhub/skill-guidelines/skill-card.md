## Description:

Provides Chinese-language principles for creating, reviewing, refactoring, and improving agent skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shujingjing](https://clawhub.ai/user/shujingjing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and agent-skill authors use this skill to design, review, and improve skill instructions with principles for clear scope, minimal context, controlled workflows, validation gates, and practical examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reviewers who do not read Chinese may misunderstand the skill's guidance.

Mitigation: Translate the guidance or request an English version before relying on it for skill design or review decisions.

Risk: General skill-writing guidance can be applied incorrectly to a specific skill or workflow.

Mitigation: Review proposed skill changes against the target skill's actual requirements before deployment.
