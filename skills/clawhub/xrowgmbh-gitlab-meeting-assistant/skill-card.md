## Description:

Capture features and bugs as sanitized GitLab issues during planning meetings, only on the requester's explicit instruction. Use for GitLab Meeting Assistant.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external meeting participants can use the assistant to turn explicitly requested feature and bug discussions into sanitized GitLab issues. It helps capture the agreed scope and acceptance criteria while keeping the destination project explicit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill authors GitLab issue content in English, which may not suit teams that require another language.

Mitigation: Confirm the English-only issue content requirement fits the team's workflow before using the skill.

Risk: Meeting content may include confidential identifiers or secrets that should not appear in a GitLab issue.

Mitigation: Review the complete issue content and attachments for identifying details and secrets before writing, and create issues as confidential.
