## Description:

Capture features and bugs as sanitized GitLab issues during planning meetings, only on the requester's explicit instruction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and developers use the assistant during planning meetings to turn explicitly requested feature or bug discussions into sanitized, confidential GitLab issues in the project they select.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sanitization may remove technical detail needed to understand or reproduce an issue.

Mitigation: Review sanitized descriptions for sufficient technical detail while keeping identifying information and secrets removed.
