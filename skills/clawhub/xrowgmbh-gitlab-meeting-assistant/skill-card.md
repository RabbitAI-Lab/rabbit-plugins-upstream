## Description:

Capture features and bugs as sanitized GitLab issues during planning meetings, only on the requester's explicit instruction. Use for GitLab Meeting Assistant.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xrowgmbh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xrowgmbh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and teams use this assistant during planning meetings to turn explicitly requested feature and bug discussions into clear, sanitized GitLab issues. It keeps the requester-selected project and avoids creating or changing work items without a clear instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting content may contain confidential details, personal information, or secrets that should not be copied into GitLab issues.

Mitigation: Sanitize identifying details, remove secrets, create issues as confidential in the requester-selected project, and verify confidentiality before reporting success.
