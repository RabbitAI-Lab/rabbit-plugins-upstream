## Description:

Plan trending social content-marketing topics and headline directions from account positioning and target audience, returning topic ideas, viral title angles, and content frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wsd-mj](https://clawhub.ai/user/wsd-mj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, social media marketers, and content teams use this skill to generate topic ideas, headline angles, and content frameworks for niche social accounts based on account positioning and audience details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends account positioning and audience-planning details to wsdsocial.com.

Mitigation: Use only with information approved for sharing with that third-party service; keep WSD_API_KEY private and avoid confidential campaign strategy, client data, or sensitive personal audience details unless that sharing is acceptable.
