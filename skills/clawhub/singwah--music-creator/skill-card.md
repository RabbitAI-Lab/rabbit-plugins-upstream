## Description:

A music creation assistant that interactively selects genres and produces lyrics, arrangement notes, and HappyShrimp-ready prompts in Chinese, English, bilingual, or Japanese.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[singwah](https://clawhub.ai/user/singwah)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and agent users use this skill to turn a song idea into structured music materials, including style selection, lyrics, composition guidance, and prompts prepared for HappyShrimp.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated lyrics or prompts may contain private or copyrighted material before being sent to an external music service.

Mitigation: Review generated lyrics and prompts before uploading them to HappyShrimp, especially when user inputs include private or copyrighted content.

Risk: Generated music guidance may be unsuitable for release without human creative and rights review.

Mitigation: Treat generated lyrics, arrangements, and prompts as draft materials and review them before publication or commercial use.
