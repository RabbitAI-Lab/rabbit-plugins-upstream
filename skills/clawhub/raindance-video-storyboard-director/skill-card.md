## Description:

Plans original visual story briefs and timed storyboards inspired by the broad Raindance social-video trend.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gpt-img-2](https://clawhub.ai/user/gpt-img-2)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and agents use this skill to plan original, text-only short-form video briefs, timed 11-beat storyboards, and prompt reviews using details supplied by the user. It asks for rights and consent before producing a ready-to-use brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference photos, depicted people, or separately added audio may lack appropriate permission.

Mitigation: Confirm photo rights, portrayal consent, and audio licensing before using a ready-to-use brief; keep unresolved items exploratory.

Risk: Connecting the optional GitHub-hosted MCP introduces a separately installed dependency.

Mitigation: Review the MCP source independently before installation; use the text-only workflow if it is not connected.

Risk: Product availability, prices, and terms can change.

Mitigation: Check current details on the product site before using its generator.
