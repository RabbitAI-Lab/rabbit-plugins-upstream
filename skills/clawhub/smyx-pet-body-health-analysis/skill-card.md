## Description:

Identifies obesity, emaciation, external injuries, skin abnormalities, and abnormal mental states from pet images or videos to help owners notice potential health issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners can submit an image or video to receive a structured overview of visible body condition, skin, and activity concerns, or request a list of prior reports. The results are for health reference and do not replace veterinary diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review says the “no analysis” option is ignored and account storage and user separation need clearer controls; selected media leaves the device and credentials persist locally.
