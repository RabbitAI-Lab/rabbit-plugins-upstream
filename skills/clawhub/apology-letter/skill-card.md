## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external support teams, communications staff, and other writers use this skill to draft accountable apologies for customers, communities, or the public after mistakes, complaints, or service failures. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated apologies may include inaccurate assumptions about events, impacts, names, dates, remedies, refunds, compensation, or prevention steps. <br>
Mitigation: Review the apology before sending, confirm bracketed or assumed details, and remove any remedy or prevention commitment the sender cannot actually provide. <br>
Risk: An apology can over-promise operational changes or make commitments that create customer, legal, or trust risk. <br>
Mitigation: Route high-impact or sensitive apologies through the appropriate business, legal, or customer support review before sending. <br>
