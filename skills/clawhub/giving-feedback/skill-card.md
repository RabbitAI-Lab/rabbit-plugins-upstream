## Description: <br>
Helps agents turn vague workplace concerns into specific, kind, actionable feedback using the Situation-Behaviour-Impact structure, with a clear ask, opening line, and dialogue prompts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, and workplace coaches use this skill to draft reinforcing or constructive feedback for peers, reports, or managers. It helps turn specific situations, observable behavior, and impact into respectful feedback with an actionable ask and dialogue prompts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Workplace feedback prompts may include sensitive HR, performance, or personal details. <br>
Mitigation: Minimize sensitive details, omit personal information that is not needed, and review the draft before sharing. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or subjective inputs could produce feedback that feels unfair, vague, or poorly calibrated. <br>
Mitigation: Check the final feedback against observable situation, behavior, impact, tone, and requested change before delivery. <br>
