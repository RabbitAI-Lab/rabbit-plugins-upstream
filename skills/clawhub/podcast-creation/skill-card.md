## Description:

Guides agents in writing podcast scripts with a compelling opening, clear narrative arc, distinct host dialogue, and grounded claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[moltstrong](https://clawhub.ai/user/moltstrong)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Podcast writers and agents use this guidance to plan and draft engaging episodes, including cold opens, listener promises, two-host exchanges, and satisfying payoffs. Audio production and publishing require a separate workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted scripts may present unsupported claims as facts.

Mitigation: Check facts and sources before recording; label hypothetical examples clearly.

Risk: Using the separate AgentOnAir workflow may involve external accounts and content uploads.

Mitigation: Review that workflow and its service permissions before submitting or publishing content.
