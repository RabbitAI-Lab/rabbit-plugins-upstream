## Description: <br>
Build a customer journey map for a product, service, or experience. Use when asked to map a customer journey, create a user journey, document touchpoints and pain points, or design an experience map. Produces a complete journey map with stages, touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and prioritised opportunities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, UX designers, researchers, and cross-functional teams use this skill to turn product, persona, journey scope, and customer insight inputs into a structured customer journey map for discovery, redesign, prioritisation, or stakeholder alignment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide customer research, support-ticket summaries, NPS comments, analytics, or other sensitive customer context to the agent while using the skill. <br>
Mitigation: Share only approved data, redact personal or confidential details when possible, and follow the organization's data-handling rules before providing customer evidence. <br>
Risk: A journey map can become misleading if pain points, emotions, or opportunities are based on assumptions instead of customer evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Cite the sources used for key claims, label assumptions clearly, and use the research-gaps section to identify what still needs validation. <br>
Risk: Prioritized opportunities may steer product decisions if stakeholders treat the generated map as final research output. <br>
Mitigation: Review the map with product, research, design, and customer-facing teams before using it for roadmap or investment decisions. <br>
