## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive user-provided materials such as contracts, budgets, screenshots, and candidate agency details.

Mitigation: Limit inputs to necessary materials, redact personal data where possible, and handle uploaded documents under the user's approved privacy and retention controls.

Risk: Legal, registry, licence, complaint, and enforcement information can change after the baseline date.

Mitigation: Reverify mutable claims from current official sources and include access dates before relying on an audit conclusion.

Risk: Unsupported adverse claims could mislead readers or unfairly characterize an agency.

Mitigation: Use the evidence-grade rules, attribute negative claims to sources, distinguish unresolved disputes from findings, and avoid legal conclusions without competent authority.

Risk: Generated contract clauses may be mistaken for legal advice or treated as enforceability guarantees.

Mitigation: Present clauses as drafting support and recommend qualified local counsel review for high-value, cross-border, or jurisdiction-specific agreements.
