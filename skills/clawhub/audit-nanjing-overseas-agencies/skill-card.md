## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to conduct consumer-side, source-traceable audits of Nanjing study-abroad, immigration, overseas-identity, and private-entry/exit agencies. It supports roster building, entity and qualification checks, agency comparison and scoring, complaint or marketing-claim review, and protective contract-clause drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide contracts, screenshots, or personal study-abroad and immigration context that contains sensitive personal or commercial information.

Mitigation: Request only information needed for the audit, keep outputs focused on consumer-risk analysis, and avoid exposing unnecessary personal details.

Risk: Agency audits can be mistaken for legal advice or for definitive findings about fraud, licensing, complaints, or service quality.

Mitigation: Use the skill's evidence grades and neutral wording, label unresolved issues as unverified or disputed, and recommend professional legal review for high-value contracts.

Risk: Regulatory status and business records can change after an audit is drafted.

Mitigation: Require current official-source checks, include access dates, and distinguish historical licences from current registration and project-specific permissions.
