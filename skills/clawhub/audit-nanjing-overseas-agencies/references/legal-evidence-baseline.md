## 1. 现行法律基线 / Current Legal Baseline

**基准日期 / Baseline date: 2026-08-14。** 每次开展新审计时，均须通过官方来源重新核验。 / Reverify from official sources during every new audit.

### 自费出国留学中介资格 / Study-Abroad Intermediary Qualification

- 江苏于 2016 年取消“自费出国留学中介服务机构资格认定”。 / Jiangsu cancelled “自费出国留学中介服务机构资格认定” in 2016.
- 国务院于 2017 年在全国取消该指定地方行政许可。 / The State Council cancelled the designated local administrative licence nationally in 2017.
- 原“教外综资认字”证书仅为历史证据，不能证明现行专项许可、当前业务能力、团队规模、案例或备用金。 / Former “教外综资认字” certificates are historical evidence only; they do not establish a current special licence, current business capacity, team size, cases, or reserve fund.

### 因私出入境中介机构经营许可证 / Private Entry/Exit Intermediary Permit

- 自 2018-11-10 起，主管机关停止签发“因私出入境中介机构经营许可证”。 / From 2018-11-10, authorities stopped issuing the “因私出入境中介机构经营许可证.”
- 此前签发的许可证自动失效，原备用金予以退还。 / Previously issued permits became automatically invalid, and former reserve funds were returned.
- 现行经营者使用普通商事登记，并继续受市场监管、广告、合同、数据、移民、刑事及其他适用法律约束。 / Current operators use ordinary business registration and remain subject to market, advertising, contract, data, immigration, criminal, and other applicable laws.

### “双资质”主张的结论 / Consequence for “Dual Qualification” Claims

在本基线下，南京目前有效的“留学资质 + 因私出入境许可证”双资质持有者数量为零，因为这两类专项许可制度已不存在。营业执照经营范围同时包含两类服务，**不等于**仍持有双项专项许可。 / Under this baseline, the number of currently valid Nanjing “study-abroad qualification + private-entry/exit permit” dual holders is zero because the two special-licence regimes no longer exist. A business licence whose scope contains both services is **not** a surviving dual special licence.

如未来官方来源改变上述结论，须先注明新的法律文件、施行日期、发布机关和过渡规则，方可更改标签。 / If a future official source changes this conclusion, cite the new instrument, effective date, authority, and transition rule before changing the label.

## 3. 范围与覆盖协议 / Scope and Coverage Protocol

对于“南京全部机构”的请求，应给出以下三个数量，而非一个具有误导性的总数。 / For an “all Nanjing agencies” request, produce the following three counts rather than one misleading number:

1. 在注明日期的官方快照中发现的历史主体数量。 / Historical entities found in a stated dated official snapshot.
2. 通过明确的登记查询发现、目前仍存续的法律主体数量。 / Current surviving legal entities found by defined registration queries.
3. 通过市场发现找到并已映射至法律主体的活跃分支、办事处或品牌数量。 / Active branches, offices, or brands found by market discovery and mapped to legal entities.

应记录以下内容。 / Record the following:

- 审计日期和时区。 / Audit date and timezone.
- 使用的数据库和检索入口。 / Databases and search endpoints used.
- 查询语句和经营范围关键词。 / Query strings and business-scope keywords.
- 覆盖的行政区。 / Districts covered.
- 结果是否分页、受数量上限限制、被验证码阻断或无法访问。 / Whether results were paginated, capped, blocked by captcha, or inaccessible.
- 重复、改名、注销、迁移和分支机构的处理规则。 / Duplicate, renamed, deregistered, migrated, and branch-handling rules.

若官方数据库无法导出，或无法衡量检索召回率，应写“当前公开检索样本，不能宣称穷尽”，而非“南京全部”。 / If an official database cannot be exported or search recall cannot be measured, write “当前公开检索样本，不能宣称穷尽” (“current public-search sample; completeness cannot be claimed”) rather than “南京全部” (“all Nanjing agencies”).

## 4. 证据等级 / Evidence Grades

### A 级——权威证据 / Grade A — Authoritative

- 法律、法规、政府通知和官方登记册。 / Laws, regulations, government notices, and official registers.
- 商事登记记录和年度报告。 / Business-registration records and annual reports.
- 监管决定、法院判决和执行记录。 / Regulator decisions, court judgments, and enforcement records.
- 境外监管机构的执照登记册。 / Foreign regulator licence registers.
- 可直接证明关系的大学或公共机构记录。 / University or public-institution records directly proving a relationship.

法律状态、登记、制裁、判决和境外专业执照应尽可能采用 A 级证据。 / Use Grade A for legal status, registration, sanctions, judgments, and foreign professional licences whenever possible.

### B 级——可归属的一方证据 / Grade B — Attributable First-Party

- 机构网站、官方账号、已签合同、报价单或发票。 / Institution website, official account, signed contract, quotation, or invoice.
- 可识别法律主体的官方招聘页面。 / Official recruitment page with an identifiable legal entity.
- 可追溯至年度报告的已公布员工或社保人数。 / Published employee or social-insurance figures traceable to an annual report.
- 机构对投诉的回应。 / Institution response to a complaint.

B 级证据可证明机构曾作出何种表述、要约或披露，但不会自动证明该主张属实。 / Grade B proves what the institution said, offered, or disclosed; it does not automatically prove the claim is true.

### C 级——可归属的独立报道 / Grade C — Attributable Independent Reporting

- 可信媒体报道、消费者组织调查或有记录的访谈。 / Reputable media report, consumer-organization investigation, or documented interview.
- 附有交易材料及可识别回应的投诉平台条目。 / Complaint-platform entry with transaction materials and an identifiable response.

未解决的报道只能表述为指控或争议，不得据此作出法律结论。 / Describe unresolved reports as allegations or disputes; do not state a legal conclusion.

### D 级——仅用于发现线索 / Grade D — Discovery Only

- 目录、SEO 排名、地图条目、论坛、匿名帖子、软文和未经认证的截图。 / Directories, SEO rankings, map listings, forums, anonymous posts, advertorials, and unauthenticated screenshots.

不得仅依据 D 级证据作数值扣分；只能用其寻找法律主体或更强证据。 / Do not base a numerical deduction solely on Grade D; use it to find a legal entity or a stronger source.

## 5. 主张措辞规则 / Claim-Language Rules

推荐使用以下措辞。 / Use the following wording:

- “官网宣称……” / “The official website claims …”
- “招聘页面显示其正在招聘……” / “The recruitment page shows that it is recruiting …”
- “某年度公示参保人数为 X；该数据不等同于实际总用工人数。” / “The disclosed social-insurance headcount for [year] was X; this is not the same as the actual total workforce.”
- “公开报道记载双方存在退款争议；未定位到生效裁判。” / “Public reporting records a refund dispute between the parties; no effective judgment was located.”
- “本次公开检索未定位到相关处罚记录。” / “This public-record search did not locate a relevant penalty record.”
- “历史证号仅证明曾进入某日期的旧名录。” / “A historical certificate number proves only that the entity appeared in the former roster on a specified date.”

除非有主管机关或有权机构的认定，否则避免使用以下措辞。 / Avoid the following wording unless supported by a competent finding:

- “诈骗”“非法移民”“虚假案例”“空壳”“无团队”“文书必然外包”。 / “Fraud,” “illegal immigration,” “fabricated cases,” “shell company,” “no team,” or “document work is necessarily outsourced.”
- “零投诉”“百分百成功”“政府背景保证”。 / “Zero complaints,” “100% success,” or “government-background guarantee.”
- “当前持有教育部/公安部双证。” / “Currently holds dual certificates from the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Public Security.”
