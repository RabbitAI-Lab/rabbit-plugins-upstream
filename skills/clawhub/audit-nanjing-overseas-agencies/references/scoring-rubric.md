# 评分细则 / Scoring Rubric

## 1. 得分含义 / Score meaning

0–10 分结果是**基于公开证据的消费者风险审计分**，不是录取、签证、永久居留或退款概率。  
The 0–10 result is a **public-evidence, consumer-risk audit score**, not an admission, visa, permanent-residence, or refund probability.

从 10.0 分起，仅扣除已确认且归属明确的事实；结果四舍五入至一位小数。不得仅因机构成立时间短、规模小、线上曝光少或专长范围窄而加分或扣分。  
Begin at 10.0 and subtract only confirmed, attributable facts. Round to one decimal. Never award or deduct points merely for age, small size, low online exposure, or a narrow specialty.

同时发布 / Publish both:

- `综合得分 / Overall score`：`10 - 已确认扣分 / confirmed deductions`；
- `证据覆盖率 / Evidence coverage`：十个必需证据字段中，经 Grade A–C 证据核实的字段百分比 / the percentage of ten required evidence fields verified with Grade A–C evidence.

覆盖率低于 60% 时，在分数前标注“暂定 / Provisional”，且不得与覆盖率更高的机构进行排名比较。  
If coverage is below 60%, prefix the score with “暂定 / Provisional” and do not rank it against higher-coverage institutions.

## 2. 证据覆盖率 / Evidence coverage

每核实一个字段计 10 个百分点 / Give 10 percentage points for each verified field:

1. 当前法定主体、统一社会信用代码及状态 / current legal entity, unified social credit code, and status;
2. 合同、收款及开票主体的对应关系 / contract, collection, and invoice entity mapping;
3. 当前经营范围及相关项目专项许可 / current business scope and relevant project-specific licences;
4. 注明年份的当前团队及社会保险证据 / current team/social-insurance evidence with year;
5. 文书人员/案件经理的归属及外包披露 / writer/case-manager ownership and outsourcing disclosure;
6. 可比且归属明确的案例证据 / comparable, attributable case evidence;
7. 当前营销主张及获客模式 / current marketing claims and acquisition model;
8. 注明日期、引用官方来源的政策风险披露 / dated policy-risk disclosure with official sources;
9. 合同、费用、退款及售后条款 / contract, fee, refund, and after-sales terms;
10. 对投诉、处罚、诉讼、执行及机构回应的检索 / complaints, sanctions, litigation, enforcement, and response search.

“经有记录的 Grade A 检索未发现负面记录 / No negative record located after a documented Grade A search”可满足字段 10，但不得表述为“负面记录不存在 / no negative record exists”。

## 3. 扣分目录 / Deduction Catalogue

采用最接近的单一条目；同一事实不得跨维度重复扣分。  
Apply the closest single item. Do not double-count one fact across dimensions.

### A. 主体与资金链 / Entity and money chain — 上限 / maximum 2.0

| 已确认事实 / Confirmed fact | 扣分 / Deduction |
|---|---:|
| 经书面请求后，仍无法将品牌对应至拟签约主体<br>Brand cannot be mapped to the proposed contracting entity after written request | -0.5 |
| 合同、收款及开票主体不一致，且三方责任不清<br>Contract entity, collecting entity, and invoice entity differ without clear tripartite responsibility | -0.5 |
| 机构要求向无关公司或个人账户付款<br>Institution requests payment to an unrelated company or personal account | -1.0 |
| 重要服务超出登记经营范围或缺少所需项目专项许可<br>Material service falls outside registered scope or required project-specific permission | -1.0 |

### B. 团队与交付链 / Team and delivery chain — 上限 / maximum 2.0

| 已确认事实 / Confirmed fact | 扣分 / Deduction |
|---|---:|
| 未披露重要工作的外包或分包<br>Undisclosed outsourcing or subcontracting of material work | -1.0 |
| 经书面请求后，拒绝提供顾问/文书人员身份或雇佣关系<br>Consultant/writer identity or employment relationship refused after written request | -0.5 |
| 合同允许单方更换核心人员，却不给客户补救措施<br>Contract permits unilateral replacement of core staff without customer remedy | -0.5 |
| 已证实依赖某一个人/渠道，且无连续性安排<br>Demonstrated dependence on an individual/channel with no continuity arrangement | -0.5 |

### C. 案例与能力 / Cases and capability — 上限 / maximum 1.5

| 已确认事实 / Confirmed fact | 扣分 / Deduction |
|---|---:|
| 案例证据无法归属于签约主体<br>Case evidence cannot be attributed to the contracting entity | -0.5 |
| 仅提供录取/批准截图，无可比背景或日期<br>Only offer/approval screenshots are provided, with no comparable background or dates | -0.5 |
| 经书面请求后，机构声称擅长的服务仍无可核验的可比已完成案例<br>A claimed specialist service has no verifiable comparable completed case after written request | -0.5 |
| 已发布案例的重要细节经证实与签发机构/主管机关的信息不一致<br>Material published case detail is demonstrably inconsistent with the issuing institution/authority | -1.0 |

不得仅因新机构案例少而扣分；只有声称经验与证据不符，或拒绝证实重要主张时才扣分。  
Do not deduct merely because a new firm has few cases. Deduct only for a mismatch between claimed experience and evidence, or for refusal to substantiate a material claim.

### D. 营销与政策准确性 / Marketing and policy accuracy — 上限 / maximum 1.5

| 已确认事实 / Confirmed fact | 扣分 / Deduction |
|---|---:|
| 将过去的留学/因私出入境许可表述为目前仍有效<br>Former study-abroad/private-entry permit represented as currently valid | -1.0 |
| 声称保证录取、签证、永久居留或公民身份<br>Guaranteed admission, visa, permanent residence, or citizenship claim | -1.0 |
| 经要求更正后，重要政策主张仍无日期/来源<br>Material policy claim lacks date/source after correction request | -0.5 |
| 实质遗漏官方披露的不利、撤销、运营、居留、税务或续期风险<br>Official downside, revocation, operation, residence, tax, or renewal risk is materially omitted | -0.5 |
| 稀缺性/截止日期主张与其引用的主管机关信息矛盾<br>Scarcity/deadline claim is contradicted by the cited authority | -0.5 |

### E. 合同、退款与售后 / Contract, refund, and after-sales — 上限 / maximum 1.5

| 已确认事实 / Confirmed fact | 扣分 / Deduction |
|---|---:|
| 退款触发条件、计算方式或期限在实质上未定义<br>Refund trigger, calculation, or deadline is materially undefined | -0.5 |
| 大额境外第三方付款未明确收款方、合同及出具收据的义务<br>Large overseas third-party payment lacks named recipient, contract, and receipt obligation | -0.5 |
| 格式条款宽泛排除机构或其分包方的责任<br>Format term broadly excludes liability for the institution or its subcontractors | -0.5 |
| 承诺的售后/安置服务未写入合同<br>Promised after-sales/resettlement service is absent from the contract | -0.5 |

### F. 投诉、处罚与连续性 / Complaints, sanctions, and continuity — 上限 / maximum 1.5

| 已确认事实 / Confirmed fact | 扣分 / Deduction |
|---|---:|
| 审计期内相关的生效行政处罚或判决；须解释严重程度<br>Relevant final administrative sanction or judgment within the audit period; explain severity | -0.5 至 / to -1.5 |
| 有记录的交易争议，且机构已承认或作出纠正性退款<br>Documented transaction dispute plus institution admission/corrective refund | -0.5 |
| 多次有记录且实质相似的争议，无已公布的补救措施<br>Repeated materially similar documented disputes with no published remedy | -0.5 至 / to -1.0 |
| 初创机构采用高额定金合同，且无停业、关键人员或连续性补救措施<br>Deposit-heavy contract with no shutdown, key-person, or continuity remedy at a startup | -0.5 |

单个未解决投诉不构成不当行为认定；应陈述双方说法及监管机构/法院处理状态。  
A single unresolved complaint is not a finding of wrongdoing. Describe both sides and any regulator/court status.

## 4. 计分规则 / Scoring Rules

每项扣分均须发布 / For every deduction publish:

```text
-0.5｜维度 / Dimension：合同退款 / Contract & refund｜事实 / Fact：合同未给出退款完成期限 / The contract does not state a refund-completion deadline｜
来源 / Source：合同第 X 条 / Contract Clause X（Grade B，取得日期 / retrieved YYYY-MM-DD）｜
解释 / Explanation：消费者无法确定退出后的资金回收时间 / The consumer cannot determine when funds will be recovered after withdrawal
```

规则 / Rules:

- 各维度扣分不得超过其规定上限。 / Cap each dimension at its stated maximum.
- 总分限定在 0–10 分。 / Clamp the total score to 0–10.
- 除非生效处罚/判决需要有记录的严重程度调整，否则以 0.5 为增量。 / Use increments of 0.5 except where a final sanction/judgment requires a documented severity adjustment.
- 不得仅以 Grade D 证据支持扣分。 / Do not use Grade D as the sole support for a deduction.
- 不得将证据缺口视为不当行为。机构若拒绝合理的书面请求，可因“拒绝”扣分，并须保留往来函件。 / Do not convert an evidence gap into misconduct. If a firm refuses a reasonable written request, deduct for the refusal and retain the correspondence.
- 正面证据须单独展示；不得用无关赞誉“返还”已确认的扣分。 / Show positive evidence separately; do not “refund” a confirmed deduction with unrelated praise.
- 个人匹配度须在取得用户画像和目标后另行评分，不得混入本风险分。 / For personal-fit scoring, create a separate fit score only after receiving the user profile and target. Do not mix it into this risk score.
