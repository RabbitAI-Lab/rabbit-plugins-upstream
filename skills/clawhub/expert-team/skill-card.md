## Description:

## Use Case:

People weighing a decision, comparing options, improving an existing product or plan, or exploring different writing approaches can use the AI panel to surface distinct viewpoints, trade-offs, and conditional recommendations. It is intended to help assess likely value against time, money, and other costs before committing.

Risk: AI panel recommendations may be inaccurate or miss important context, especially when a decision has health, safety, compliance, or irreversible consequences.
