## Description:

Logos（诺声） provides a controlled brand-voice and signature posture for agents generating external thought-leadership, theoretical viewpoints, and brand narrative content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content, brand, and communications teams use this skill to generate restrained Logos（诺声）-signed thought-leadership articles, value narratives, talking points, and public scripts. It helps keep voice, boundaries, signature text, and claims posture consistent across outward-facing writing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can add branded Logos（诺声） voice, stance, and signature language to generated content even when a neutral style would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Enable it deliberately for external thought-leadership or signed communications, and avoid automatic use for unrelated neutral writing.

Risk: Generated external communications may overstate authority or drift into decision replacement if not reviewed against the skill boundaries.

Mitigation: Review outputs before publication and keep them within the stated boundaries: thought leadership, theoretical viewpoints, and brand voice only.
