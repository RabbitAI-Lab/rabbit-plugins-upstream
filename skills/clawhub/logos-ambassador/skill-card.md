## Description:

Logos（诺声） is a writing and persona skill for public-facing thought leadership and brand narrative content, using a restrained professional voice, consistent signature guidance, and explicit boundaries against decision replacement or authority claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content, brand, and communications teams use this skill to draft thought-leadership articles, brand value narratives, public talk or podcast outlines, and signature conventions in the Logos（诺声） voice. It is intended for narrative and viewpoint expression, not for legal, regulatory, financial, medical, or other expert decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated public-facing content could be mistaken for expert, regulatory, legal, financial, or medical decision support.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for thought-leadership and brand narrative drafting, and route expert or regulated conclusions to qualified human review.

Risk: Persona-driven brand copy could overstate claims or imply authority beyond the skill's stated boundary.

Mitigation: Review public outputs before release, preserve the skill's restrained voice, and avoid extreme claims or authority language.
