## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated external content could include unsupported claims or regulated-topic guidance if published without review.

Mitigation: Review generated external content before publication, especially around regulated, medical, or governmental topics.

Risk: The persona could be used outside its stated brand and attribution boundaries.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when the agent should generate Nomos/Logos brand voice content and apply the provided byline rules.
