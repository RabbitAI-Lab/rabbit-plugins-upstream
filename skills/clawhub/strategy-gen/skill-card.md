## Description:

Strategy Gen helps users turn goals and constraints into a structured strategy canvas using SWOT, OODA, scenario planning, first principles, and OKR closure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to convert business, product, or personal-growth goals into strategy options, tradeoffs, roadmaps, and measurable follow-up criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategy details and learner notes may contain confidential business or personal planning information stored in local files.

Mitigation: Choose output paths deliberately and avoid putting confidential details into learner notes unless local storage is acceptable.

Risk: Generated strategy recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for high-impact decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the output as decision support, review assumptions and tradeoffs, and keep final responsibility with the user.
