## Description: <br>
Helps users plan and track directory submissions for startup, SaaS, AI, agent, MCP, no-code, and review directories to support backlinks, domain rating, and product discovery. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, founders, and go-to-market teams use this skill to assess launch readiness, prioritize directory tiers, prepare tailored positioning, submit listings, and track backlink outcomes for software products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may guide public directory listings, outreach, review requests, or profile creation that could publish inaccurate or unwanted information. <br>
Mitigation: Require human confirmation before submitting forms, posting publicly, contacting reviewers, or creating external profiles. <br>
Risk: Directory quality, SEO value, costs, and platform rules can change after the reference material was written. <br>
Mitigation: Verify each directory's current rules, pricing, editorial standards, and backlink quality before acting on a submission plan. <br>
