Since 2023, my independent studio has worked with local venues, roasters, shelters and record labels.

How I work
  • Every job starts with a written brief we both sign off, even if it is four lines long.
  • Three sketched directions first, then one idea pushed hard. No twelve-option decks.
  • Print proofs on the real stock before anything goes to the press.
  • Source files and a one-page usage note are always part of the handover.

Questions

Before we start.

How long does a poster take?

Usually two weeks, including two revision rounds. Tell me your print deadline in the first message.

Do you handle the printing?

Yes. Printing is quoted separately, with a proof check before production.

Will I get the source files?

Agreed exports and working files are included. Illustration usage is set out in the brief.

What if I only have a rough idea?

A few sentences and a reference are enough. We’ll shape the brief together.

Not sure which one fits?

Share the idea. I’ll suggest the right scope.

Send a brief See the work

From a clear brief to a useful handover.

  1. 01

    Prioritise

    Agree on a short queue with one clear owner. Decide what needs to be finished before adding more work.

  2. 02

    Make and review

    Share focused updates with enough context for useful feedback. Keep decisions and open questions beside the work.

  3. 03

    Close and continue

    Deliver the finished files and record what changed. Review the next priority instead of letting requests disappear into a long list.
