## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using booking features may send identity and health information to Haola; the remote service's implementation and retention practices were not verified.

Mitigation: Prefer provider forms over chat for sensitive fields, review OAuth permissions, and clearly specify any profile updates or appointment changes.
