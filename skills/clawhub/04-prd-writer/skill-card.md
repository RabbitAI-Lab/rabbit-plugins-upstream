## Description:

产品需求文档生成：从产品定位到功能定义、技术方案、商业化设计、路线图，输出结构化PRD文档。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product builders use this skill to turn a product idea and supporting research into a structured PRD. It is oriented toward Chinese-language PRD drafting and covers product overview, user personas, prioritized requirements, technical approach, data strategy, commercialization, roadmap, non-goals, and risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for adjacent product-planning requests where the user does not want a full PRD.

Mitigation: Specify the desired language, scope, and whether a complete PRD is wanted when prompting.

Risk: Generated market, user, or roadmap content may be incomplete or depend on the quality of provided research inputs.

Mitigation: Review cited data sources and validate business assumptions before using the PRD for planning or release decisions.
