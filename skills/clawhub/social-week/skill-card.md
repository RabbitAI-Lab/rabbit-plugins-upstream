## Description:

Plans a week of network-specific social posts from a brief, then schedules approved drafts through PostWire and provides a measurement checklist for future weeks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perufitlife](https://clawhub.ai/user/perufitlife)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to turn a short brief into a seven-day, platform-specific content calendar, review drafts, schedule approved posts, and assess their performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect or unwanted posts may be scheduled to connected social accounts.

Mitigation: Review the exact text, networks, dates, times, and timezone, and require explicit user approval before scheduling or cancellation.

Risk: The workflow can access connected networks, uploaded media links, and account performance data.

Mitigation: Use only the intended PostWire account and connected networks, and review posts and media before approval.
