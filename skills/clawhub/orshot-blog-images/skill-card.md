## Description:

Featured image and OG image generator for blog posts with Orshot: an on-brand cover for every post (WordPress featured image, Open Graph image) plus 3 to 6 in-article images, rendered from templates with the post's real title, logos and numbers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Blog owners, content teams, and developers use the skill to create on-brand featured images, Open Graph images, and in-article visuals for posts, and to automate image creation for supported publishing workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends design text and image URLs to Orshot.

Mitigation: Share only material you are authorized to send and limit connected access to the intended workspace and blog.

Risk: Publishing, changing live covers, overwriting images, and continuing bulk runs can affect a live site or incur credits.

Mitigation: Require approval for live-site changes, overwrites, and continuing bulk runs; review sample images and credit costs, and enable recurring automation only when wanted.
