External users and agents use this skill to answer Dutch Bros menu questions about drinks, prices, nutrition, seasonal items, secret-menu combinations, and customization options. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Menu prices, seasonal availability, ingredients, nutrition values, and caffeine details may be outdated or vary by location.

Mitigation: Treat menu details as informational guidance and verify exact details with official Dutch Bros channels or a local Dutch Bros location when accuracy matters.

Risk: The skill references an independent Dutch Bros menu guide that is not an official Dutch Bros source.

Mitigation: Identify the guide as an independent reference and avoid presenting it as an official brand source.
