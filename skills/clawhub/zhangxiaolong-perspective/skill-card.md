## Description:

Provides Chinese-language, Zhang Xiaolong-inspired guidance for reviewing penguin-oss-suite documentation, governance, community growth, and skill design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and maintainers use this skill during penguin-oss-suite S3/S5 reviews to assess README drafts, contribution rules, community decisions, and skill triggers against a perspective distilled from public talks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Opinionated, inferred judgments may be mistaken for Zhang Xiaolong's direct assessment of this suite.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as an interpretation of public talks and review them against the actual project requirements before adoption.

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may not fit every team's locale or communication needs.

Mitigation: Have a Chinese-fluent reviewer check meaning and adapt recommendations for the intended audience.
