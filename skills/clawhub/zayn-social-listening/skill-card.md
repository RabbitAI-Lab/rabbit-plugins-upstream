## Description: <br>
Analyzes public social media, news, forum, customer review, and employee review signals for a company or brand while preserving limits around sample representativeness, account authenticity, timing, and anonymous comments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Brand, HR, compliance, and market-research teams use this skill to review public online discussion about a company, brand, or competitor across selected platforms, markets, topics, and time windows. It helps separate official statements, media coverage, user feedback, anonymous comments, repeated complaints, and unresolved signals without turning limited samples into overall conclusions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Small, anonymous, stale, or platform-skewed samples may be mistaken for overall brand sentiment. <br>
Mitigation: Keep platform, geography, time window, sample size, and source type visible in the output, and state when the sample cannot support a broad conclusion. <br>
Risk: Public or user-provided posts, reviews, screenshots, and platform data may include uncertain account identity or authenticity. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm official accounts and source quality before attribution; treat anonymous or unverified material as leads rather than facts. <br>
