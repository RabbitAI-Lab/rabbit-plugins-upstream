## Description: <br>
Write executive summaries for documents, reports, or proposals, structured for senior stakeholders to read quickly and act on. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Executives, boards, investors, public-sector leaders, clients, and the teams preparing materials for them use this skill to turn source documents or topics into decision-ready executive summaries with a clear bottom line, recommendation, next steps, and risks of inaction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste confidential reports or business documents into an agent session while using this writing aid. <br>
Mitigation: Use normal caution with sensitive material and follow the organization's approved data-handling rules before sharing source content in the session. <br>
Risk: A generated executive summary may omit nuance or frame recommendations too strongly for the underlying source material. <br>
Mitigation: Review the summary against the source document before sharing it with decision-makers, especially the bottom line, recommendation, next steps, and risks of inaction. <br>
