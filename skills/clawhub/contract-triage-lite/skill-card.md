## Description:

Flags common IP, liability, non-compete, and payment risks in freelancer contracts using local, pattern-based analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nenadzivotic](https://clawhub.ai/user/nenadzivotic)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Freelancers and their agents can screen authorized NDA, MSA, or SOW text for four common contract risks before seeking legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pattern-based screening may miss risks or flag harmless language, and does not cover all contract terms.

Mitigation: Check each flagged clause against the original and have an attorney review material exposure before signing.

Risk: Contract text may be confidential or outside the user's authority to review.

Mitigation: Process only contracts the user is authorized to review; keep the local text and results appropriately protected.

Risk: Installing through an unpinned npx command may fetch code outside the reviewed release.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or trusted installation source.
