# Example: Hedging Variety（学术 hedging 的丰富度）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Midgley et al. (2020). When every day is a high school reunion. *JPSP*, 119(2).
- **论文编号**: 1.3
- **维度**: Vocabulary（hedging language 的多样性）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: vocabulary-diagnosis 的"hedge variety"、"hedge 与 claim 强度匹配"

---

## 原句（Discussion 多段混合）

> *"**Taken together**, these studies **provide** strong and consistent evidence that classic models of social comparisons **may not fully** describe or predict the impact of social media comparisons."*

> *"**Indeed**, this research **provides compelling evidence** that social media has changed the ways in which individuals compare themselves to other people."*

> *"social media comparisons **were more extreme** and **resulted in** more negative self-evaluations..."*

## 分析

### 1. Hedge 与 claim 强度匹配
- **"may not fully describe"** —— 完全 hedge（"may" + "not fully"）
- **"provide strong and consistent evidence"** —— 中度 confident（"strong" + "consistent"）
- **"provides compelling evidence"** —— 较强 confident（"compelling"）

同一个研究被 hedge 在 3 种不同强度——展示了**hedge 的精细度**

### 2. Hedging 的多种形式
- **Modal verbs**: "may"
- **Adverbs**: "Taken together"
- **Adjectives**: "strong", "consistent", "compelling", "remarkable"
- **Cognitive verbs**: "suggest", "indicate", "appear"
- **Hedged comparatives**: "more likely", "more extreme"

### 3. Unit 4.4.2 "Useful words and phrases" 的全覆盖
| 类型 | 词 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| Strengthening | compelling, strong | "compelling evidence" |
| Modal | may, might | "may not fully" |
| Adverb | Indeed, Taken together | "Indeed, this research..." |
| Comparative | more likely, more extreme | "more extreme" |
| Cognitive verb | suggest, indicate | "results suggest" |

### 4. Hedge 不会让 claim 变弱
- "Taken together" + "provide strong and consistent" = 既有 hedge 又有 strong claim
- 这是学术英语的精髓：**hedge 是 claim 的可信度保障，不是削弱**

---

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户的 hedge 过强** — "Our results vaguely might possibly suggest..." → 引用此例展示合适的 hedge 强度
2. **用户的 hedge 过弱** — "Our results prove that..." → 引用此例展示 strong hedge 模式
3. **用户全用同一类 hedge** — 全用 modal 或全用 cognitive verb → 引用此例展示 hedge variety
