# Example: Contribution Language（贡献语言）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Costello et al. (2021). Clarifying the structure and nature of left-wing authoritarianism. *JPSP*, 120(1).
- **论文编号**: 1.1
- **维度**: Vocabulary（contribution 表达的精确度）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: vocabulary-diagnosis 的"contribution claim 词汇"、"happy words 的恰当使用"

---

## 原句（Discussion 关键 claim 段）

> *"**Our results suggest** that LWA both **exists** and **predicts** key psychological and behavioral outcomes, including participation in political violence. **In the following sections, we unpack** our results, **compare** differences and similarities across LWA and RWA, **discuss** the role of political ideology in LWA and RWA, **highlight limitations** of the present research, and **outline several promising directions** for future research."*

## 分析

### 1. Achievement claim 的精确表达
- **"Our results suggest"** —— Cognitive verb + present tense —— 标准 claim 模板
- **"exists and predicts"** —— Strong verbs，表明作者对 finding 的 confidence
- **"including participation in political violence"** —— 具体化，让 contribution 更可见

### 2. Happy words 的恰当使用
- "in the following sections, we unpack..."（动词 unpacking）
- "outline several promising directions"（"promising" 是 happy word）
- "highlight limitations"（谦逊表达）

**3 种 happy words 的恰当度**：
- ✅ "promising directions"——对未来贡献的展望
- ✅ "highlights"——对自己研究的反思
- ✅ "unpacks"——对自己贡献的自信

没有过度使用（不是 "novel / first / unprecedented / groundbreaking" 满天飞）

### 3. Unit 4.2.2 "Achievement vs Contribution" 区分
- **Achievement** (本研究做了什么)："developed and preliminarily validated a multi-dimensional measure"
- **Contribution** (对 field 贡献了什么)："both exists and predicts key psychological and behavioral outcomes"
- **Limitation**："highlight limitations of the present research"
- 3 种类型在 1 段内清晰区分

### 4. 没有 "novel / first / unprecedented" 的炫耀
- 顶刊 Discussion 的 happy words 应该是**低调的**——避免看起来在自我吹嘘
- "Our results suggest" 比 "We demonstrate for the first time" 更学术
