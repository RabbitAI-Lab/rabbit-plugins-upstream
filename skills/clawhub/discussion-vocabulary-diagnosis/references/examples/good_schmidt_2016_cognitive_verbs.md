# Example: Cognitive Verb Variety（认知动词多样性）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Schmidt et al. (2016). The Parallel Episodic Processing (PEP) model 2.0. *Cognitive Psychology*, 91.
- **论文编号**: 2.7
- **维度**: Vocabulary（cognitive verbs — suggest/indicate/appear/argue）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: vocabulary-diagnosis 的"cognitive verb variety"、"claim 强度匹配"

---

## 原句（Discussion 中段）

> *"We **were able to simulate** power curves, the Stroop asymmetry, and the decrease in the congruency effect with practice... Simulations 3 and 4 **successfully simulated** the relationships between high contingency, medium contingency, low contingency, and novel word trials. **Of course, we certainly do not suggest** that we have provided a model of cognition that is in any way 'complete.' **Indeed, however**, we demonstrate that a single fixed model of a small number of processes **can have** wide applicability to different performance paradigms."*

## 分析

### 1. 多种 cognitive verbs 的精确使用
| Cognitive verb | 强度 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| "demonstrate" | 强 | "we demonstrate that a single fixed model... can have" |
| "suggest" | 弱 | "we do not suggest that we have provided a model... 'complete'" |
| "were able to simulate" | 中（带 ability hedge）| "We were able to simulate..." |
| "successfully simulated" | 较强 | "Simulations... successfully simulated" |

4 种 cognitive verbs 对应 4 种 claim 强度

### 2. Hedge 句的精确构造
- **"Of course, we certainly do not suggest"** —— 双重 hedge（interjection + adverb + negation + cognitive verb）
- 这种结构让 claim 既"避免 over-claim"又"显得自信"
- 是**避免 over-claim 的标准结构**

### 3. "Indeed, however" 的双重连接
- **"Indeed"** —— 强化
- **"however"** —— 转折
- 这种"indeed + however"组合很特殊——前句肯定 + 后句限定
- 典型的"bracing for limitations" 模式

### 4. Modal verb + bare infinitive
- "**can have**" —— 弱可能（"can" 是 modal ability/possibility）
- 不用 "will have"（强 confident）
- 不用 "may have"（更弱）

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户全用同一 cognitive verb** — 全用 "show" 或全用 "suggest" → 引用此例展示 variety
2. **用户的 hedge 不一致** — 一段中"strong claim"和"weak claim" 用同一 verb → 引用此例
3. **用户用了 "prove"** — 学术英语中很少用 prove（除非数学定理）→ 引用此例展示替代方案
