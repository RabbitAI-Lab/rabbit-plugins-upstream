# Example: Field-Specific Academic Vocabulary（领域学术词汇）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Ebert et al. (2020). Religious people only live longer in religious cultural contexts. *JPSP*, 119(1).
- **论文编号**: 1.7
- **维度**: Vocabulary（领域专业术语）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: vocabulary-diagnosis 的"领域术语准确性"、"术语 vs 通用词"

---

## 原句（Discussion 开篇）

> *"A large body of social scientific research suggests that religious people live longer lives (Chida et al., 2009; McCullough et al., 2000; Shor & Roelfs, 2013). **However**, it has been unclear whether these longevity benefits are inherent to religiosity (and thus culturally universal) or dependent on the religiosity of the adherent's cultural context. We found that the longevity benefits of religiosity are **not evident everywhere** (i.e., are not cultural universal), but are **limited to religious cultural contexts**."*

## 分析

### 1. 跨学科术语的恰当使用
- **"cultural universal"** —— 文化人类学术语
- **"religiosity"** —— 宗教学 / 社会学专业术语
- **"longevity"** —— 流行病学术语
- **"gravestone imagery"**（稍后出现）—— 研究方法学专业术语

作者展示了**跨学科词汇的整合能力**——这是一个跨文化心理学论文的标志

### 2. 精确 vs 模糊的对比
- ❌ "not everywhere" —— 模糊
- ✅ "not evident everywhere (i.e., are not cultural universal)" —— 精确（加同位语）
- ❌ "in some places" —— 模糊
- ✅ "limited to religious cultural contexts" —— 精确（限定条件）

### 3. 自我解释（in-line definition）
- "(i.e., are not cultural universal)" —— 用 "i.e." 给非领域读者解释
- 这种**自解释**是学术写作的好习惯——既保持专业性又照顾跨学科读者

### 4. 与入门级表达的对比
- 入门表达："religion helps people live longer in religious places"
- 专业表达："longevity benefits of religiosity ... limited to religious cultural contexts"
- 后者使用了**领域词汇 + 限定结构**——这是顶刊的标准

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **用户用通用词替代领域术语** — "study people" 替代 "study religiosity" → 引用此例展示专业性
2. **用户术语后没解释** — 首次使用专业术语时无 in-line definition → 引用此例展示 "(i.e., ...)" 模式
3. **用户的"模糊表达"** — "in some cases" / "etc." 等 → 引用此例展示精确限定
