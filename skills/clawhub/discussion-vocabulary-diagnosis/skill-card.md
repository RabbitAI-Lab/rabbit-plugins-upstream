## Description:

Diagnoses vocabulary in the Discussion section of an English psychology research paper by checking academic register, high-frequency academic expressions, modal verb usage, hedging, terminology consistency, and colloquial language without generating or rewriting prose.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and academic writers use this skill to diagnose whether an English psychology research paper's Discussion section uses appropriate academic vocabulary, modal verbs, hedging, terminology, and register. The skill reports issues and suggested replacements but does not generate or rewrite prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "check vocabulary" may invoke the skill for requests outside its intended scope.

Mitigation: Use it for focused feedback on academic vocabulary in English psychology Discussion sections and confirm scope when the user asks for general writing help.

Risk: The skill provides diagnostic writing guidance and may flag academic vocabulary issues incorrectly when the input is outside the documented genre or discipline.

Mitigation: Treat the report as editorial guidance and review findings against the user's paper, target journal, and discipline conventions before applying changes.
