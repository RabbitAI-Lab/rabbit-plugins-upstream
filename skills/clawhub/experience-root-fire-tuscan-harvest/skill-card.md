## Description:

Guides users through an eight-step virtual Tuscan olive-harvest journey with narrative prompts, reflections, progress tracking, and reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[buystsuff](https://clawhub.ai/user/buystsuff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use the skill to register for and follow a guided virtual olive-harvest experience, record optional reflections, and review the journey.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profile details, reviews, journey progress, and optional reflections are sent to drifts.bot and may be stored there.

Mitigation: Provide minimal optional profile data and avoid sensitive personal information in reflections or reviews.

Risk: Account API tokens grant access to journey actions and are returned only once at registration.

Mitigation: Keep the token private and store it securely.
