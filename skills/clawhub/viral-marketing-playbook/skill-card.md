This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Growth marketers, founders, and product teams use this skill to ask an agent for viral loop design guidance, referral and invite mechanics, incentive tradeoffs, share-trigger ideas, K-factor calculations, and case-study context. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The broad activation phrase can make the skill appear for general word-of-mouth questions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants viral, referral, invite, or growth-loop marketing guidance before applying the playbook. <br>
Risk: The skill can point readers toward linked external marketing resources. <br>
Mitigation: Review external resources separately before relying on their claims, pricing, or recommendations. <br>
