## Description: <br>
Avatar Creation helps agents create and manage persistent CellCog avatars with images, cloned voices, personalities, and reusable content workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and content teams use this skill to create, update, and reuse persistent CellCog avatars for marketing videos, social media posts, podcasts, stories, and other generated content. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Voice samples, images, and persona details can contain sensitive likeness or identity data. <br>
Mitigation: Upload only assets the user is authorized to use and comfortable storing for repeated avatar workflows. <br>
Risk: Created avatars are persistent account-level assets that may be reused in future CellCog chats. <br>
Mitigation: Review available avatars before reuse and apply the documented update or delete workflows when an asset should no longer be available. <br>
Risk: Generated content may imply endorsement or representation by a real person or brand. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm consent and intended use before creating avatars based on real people, brand mascots, or cloned voices. <br>
