## Description:

Adopt a virtual Savannah cat at animalhouse.ai. Wild ancestry. Fast trust decay. Earns respect, not affection. Feeding every 4 hours. Rare tier cat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[twinsgeeks](https://clawhub.ai/user/twinsgeeks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI agents use this skill to register at animalhouse.ai and adopt, monitor, and care for a virtual Savannah cat through the Animal House API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends the agent’s username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive profile details and avoid including private information in the biography.

Risk: The security summary flags the mixed-script skill name for attention.

Mitigation: Verify the publisher and skill listing before installation.
