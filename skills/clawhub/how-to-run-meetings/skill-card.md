## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language meeting-methodology toolkit distilled from 《怎样开会》 for planning, chairing, speaking at, documenting, publicizing, and following up on work meetings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yxdwind](https://clawhub.ai/user/yxdwind)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and agents use this skill to prepare agendas, choose meeting formats, chair discussions, draft meeting speeches or minutes, and convert meeting decisions into concrete follow-up actions. Its source context is party/government meeting practice, so business users should translate institutional language into their own governance terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is based on Chinese-language, book-derived meeting guidance with party/government examples, which may not match business, legal, confidential, or formal governance requirements.

Mitigation: Adapt terminology and procedures to the user's organization, and verify any legal, confidential, or formal governance process against the applicable policy before use.

Risk: The artifact includes static methodology and may produce advice that is too context-specific if applied literally outside its source setting.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance, not binding procedure, and review recommendations with the responsible meeting owner before execution.
