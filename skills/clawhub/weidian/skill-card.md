## Description: <br>
Weidian helps agents guide individuals and micro-businesses through lightweight social-commerce store setup, trust-building, platform choice, and migration timing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External sellers, ecommerce advisors, and support agents use this skill to decide whether a personal or micro-business should start with Weidian, compare it with heavier SaaS or commission-platform options, improve buyer trust signals, draft social-selling copy, and judge when to migrate. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Platform fees, Weidian feature boundaries, and regulated-product requirements can change or vary by seller category. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current fees, platform rules, and applicable local or category-specific requirements in the official Weidian app or other authoritative sources before acting. <br>
Risk: Social-commerce advice could be misapplied as spam, fake reviews, or other policy-violating growth tactics. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for compliant trust-building, real product presentation, and customer service guidance; avoid刷单刷评, mass-spam messaging, and other prohibited practices. <br>
