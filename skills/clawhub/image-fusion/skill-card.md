## Description:

Combines up to eight product images into a coordinated fashion look on one model while aiming to preserve each item's appearance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

E-commerce teams and visual content creators can use the skill to combine product photos into a complete outfit image, optionally keeping a model or scene consistent across looks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images supplied for generation may be sent to an external provider, which could expose sensitive or identifiable photos to that provider's processing.

Mitigation: Confirm which provider will receive the images and review its data handling before use; avoid sending sensitive or identifiable photos unless comfortable with that processing.

Risk: The security scan was incomplete and cannot establish that the skill is safe.

Mitigation: Treat the suspicious verdict as unresolved and review the skill and its dependencies before use.
