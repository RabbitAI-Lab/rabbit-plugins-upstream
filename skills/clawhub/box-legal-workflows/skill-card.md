## Description:

Provides shared Box collaboration role guidance, Box AI decision boundaries, and human confirmation prompts for legal workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Legal professionals and their agents use this reference to choose least-privilege Box collaboration roles, keep legal and access decisions with humans, and request confirmation during legal workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Box AI findings could be mistaken for final legal advice or privilege determinations.

Mitigation: Surface supporting citations and require an attorney to make final legal decisions.

Risk: Overbroad collaboration roles or external sharing could expose sensitive legal materials.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege roles and scoped folders; obtain human approval before access changes or external sharing.

Risk: The referenced Box skill may change between installations.

Mitigation: Review the prerequisite skill before installing and pin a release or commit when strict provenance is required.
