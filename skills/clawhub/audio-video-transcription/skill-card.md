## Description:

将音频或视频转写为文字稿，方便整理口播、会议和直播内容及制作字幕。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dunkong](https://clawhub.ai/user/dunkong)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and teams use this skill to transcribe audio or video from a public URL or a selected local file, then review and export the result for editing, subtitles or meeting notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media URLs, selected local files and derived transcripts are sent to a third-party service.

Mitigation: Upload only explicitly selected files; avoid sensitive recordings without consent and review the provider's retention and account terms.

Risk: Transcription and uploads can incur charges.

Mitigation: Review the live price estimate and authorize the scope before confirming a paid or upload action.
