## Description:

Helps travelers compare roaming, local SIM, eSIM, and portable Wi-Fi options and choose cost-effective ways to get mobile data and calls abroad.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[travel-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/travel-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to compare roaming, local SIM, eSIM, and portable Wi-Fi options for a destination and get activation guidance, purchase pointers, and data-saving tips.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static roaming, eSIM, and local SIM price guidance can become outdated or differ by carrier, destination, or promotion.

Mitigation: Verify current prices, plan terms, hotspot rules, and activation requirements with the carrier or eSIM provider before spending money.

Risk: The skill provides comparison and setup guidance only; it cannot purchase SIMs or eSIMs, confirm live availability, or inspect real-time data usage.

Mitigation: Use official carrier or provider apps and account tools for purchases, activation status, compatibility checks, and live usage information.
