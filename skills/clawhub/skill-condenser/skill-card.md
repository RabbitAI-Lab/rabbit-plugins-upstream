## Description: <br>
Compress verbose SKILL.md files using Chain-of-Density with skill-aware formatting for skills that exceed 200 lines or need terse refactoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[killerapp](https://clawhub.ai/user/killerapp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Apache-2.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and skill maintainers use this skill to condense long SKILL.md files into concise, structured Markdown while preserving metadata, section headers, commands, and key examples. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Condensed output can omit important safety constraints, prerequisites, commands, or examples if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review the condensed output against the original SKILL.md before overwriting or publishing it. <br>
