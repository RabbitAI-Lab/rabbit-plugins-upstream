## Description:

Score how ready a public web page is to be found and cited by AI search (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude search, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Bing Copilot).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aiseorainmaker](https://clawhub.ai/user/aiseorainmaker)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External site owners, SEO practitioners, and web developers use it to check whether a named public page is reachable and readable by AI search crawlers and to get remediation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The hosted checker receives the submitted public URL; its server-side privacy and filtering claims remain unverified.

Mitigation: Submit only public URLs you are comfortable sharing, exclude secret-bearing links, and avoid private, internal, or login-only pages. Review recommendations before changing crawler or firewall settings.
