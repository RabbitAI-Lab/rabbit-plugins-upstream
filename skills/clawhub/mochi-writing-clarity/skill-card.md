## Description:

Flag generic wording, long sentences and repeated paragraphs; compare numeric and URL tokens after editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hznetnewpower](https://clawhub.ai/user/hznetnewpower)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents can review selected drafts for generic wording, long sentences, and repeated paragraphs, or compare numeric and URL tokens before and after editing. Treat findings as editorial aids and check factual meaning separately.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved reports preserve excerpts, numbers, and URLs from selected drafts in the local workspace.

Mitigation: Select only drafts you intend to analyze and retain the host sandbox.

Risk: Unchanged numeric and URL tokens do not confirm that factual meaning is preserved.

Mitigation: Review names, quotations, and meaning separately.
