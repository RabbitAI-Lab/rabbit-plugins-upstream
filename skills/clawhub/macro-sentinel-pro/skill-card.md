## Description:

Macro Sentinel Pro monitors macroeconomic data and produces trend analyses, alerts, charts, cross-channel updates, and reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External macroeconomic analysts, investment researchers, and risk teams use the skill to monitor indicators, compare trends, configure alerts, and prepare reports. Its analyses can inform review, while financial decisions should be checked independently.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Macroeconomic interpretations and market impact forecasts may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Check source data and independently review interpretations before using them in investment or policy decisions.

Risk: Scheduled alerts and external notifications can send information to unintended recipients or destinations.

Mitigation: Choose schedules, recipients, and callback destinations before enabling automation, and use narrowly scoped integration credentials.
