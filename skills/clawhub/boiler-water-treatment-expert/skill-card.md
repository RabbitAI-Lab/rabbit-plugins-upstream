## Description:

Provide expert advice on boiler water treatment, covering feedwater pretreatment, softening, desalination, deoxygenation, and dosing to ensure steam quality and safe boiler operation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, facility engineers, and boiler operators use this skill to design or optimize boiler feedwater treatment, diagnose scaling or corrosion issues, select treatment equipment, and plan chemical dosing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Boiler water treatment advice can affect industrial safety, chemical dosing, corrosion control, scaling, and equipment integrity.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as engineering reference material and validate safety-critical decisions with qualified personnel, site-specific water testing, and applicable operating procedures.

Risk: Calculations or recommendations may be incomplete when boiler parameters, water quality data, or site constraints are missing.

Mitigation: Provide complete system parameters and verify any sizing, dosing, or operating changes before implementation.
