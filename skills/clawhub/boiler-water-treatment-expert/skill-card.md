## Description:

Provide expert advice on boiler water treatment, covering feedwater pretreatment, softening, desalination, deoxygenation, and dosing to ensure steam quality and safe boiler operation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Plant engineers, boiler operators, and water-treatment specialists use this skill to draft boiler feedwater treatment designs, troubleshoot scaling or corrosion issues, and plan chemical dosing, equipment selection, and operating guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Boiler water treatment recommendations can affect safety-relevant plant chemistry, equipment settings, or operating procedures if applied directly.

Mitigation: Use the skill for drafting and analysis only, and validate recommendations with current plant data and qualified professionals before operational changes.

Risk: Advice may be incomplete when the user omits water quality, boiler pressure, load, material, or operating constraints.

Mitigation: Require site-specific inputs and treat generated calculations, equipment selections, and dosing guidance as preliminary until reviewed against actual system conditions.
