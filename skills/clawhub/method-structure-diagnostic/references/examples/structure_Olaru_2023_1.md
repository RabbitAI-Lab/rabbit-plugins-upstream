# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

Here, we included data from participants with at least two measurements of the personality traits and satisfaction domains. This resulted in three combined personality–satisfaction samples of (a) life satisfaction: N = 9,110 (Mage2008 = 43.88; SDage2008 = 18.07; women = 4,985), (b) social satisfaction N = 8,889 (Mage2008 = 44.14; SDage2008 = 18.01; women = 4,875) and (c) WS: N = 5,928 (Mage2008 = 38.06; SDage2008 = 14.24; women = 3,162). At the baseline assessment in 2008, 54% of the participants were married, 20% lived alone, 48% stated that paid employment was their primary occupation, and 51% had children that still lived in the household. ... A detailed overview of participants' demographics and correlations with personality traits and satisfaction for each wave is given in Open Science Framework (OSF) Table S1.

## 来源文献

Olaru, G., van Scheppingen, M. A., Bleidorn, W., & Denissen, J. J. A. (2023). The link between personality, global, and domain-specific satisfaction across the adult lifespan. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
