# 片段类型：结构（历史档案数据样本构建的分步流程）

## 原文片段

> Birthplace Sample. The first sample was based on individuals' birthplaces. To generate this sample, we began by selecting all individuals who ever participated in the SOEP and who were born between 1930 and 1945 (by the end of the war, participants were between 0 and 15 years old, Mage in 1945 = 6.44 years). We excluded individuals born before 1930 to ensure that the sample consisted of individuals who were children or adolescents not conscripted for the Second World War. Next, we excluded all individuals who did not report their place of birth, as well as those who were born in East Germany and the Saarland (due to missing destruction data in these areas). Afterward, we linked each respondent's birthplace to an objective indicator of each respondent's war exposure, that is, the share of destroyed housing after the Second World War from the GLR data set. We linked the data using georeferenced birthplace information and georeferenced housing destruction data. Both kinds of data were linked at the level of the municipalities. Finally, we excluded respondents who were born in municipalities with missing destruction data and those who did not provide answers on at least one adjustment indicator in 2012, the year of measurement. These sample restrictions resulted in a sample size of 1,975 individuals from 848 municipalities with an average age of 73.44 years (SD = 4.00 years, range = 67–82 years) and a share of 49.06% women.

## 来源文献

Entringer, T. M., Halbmeier, C., Buchinger, L., & Reitz, A. K. (2026), Method (Samples). *Within-Nation Variation in War Exposure and Psychological and Physical Adjustment.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 131(2), 355-379. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000601

## 适配诊断点

- 样本构建分步漏斗：起始群体定义（出生年份窗口 1930-1945，理由：儿童/青少年未入伍）→ 逐条排除（未报出生地、东德与萨尔、缺数据）→ 数据链接步骤（georeferenced 出生地 × 破坏数据，市镇层级）→ 最终样本（N、市镇数、平均年龄、女性占比）。
- 每步排除都带理由（"to ensure that ...", "due to missing destruction data"），样本限制可审计。
- 时点对齐说明：出生窗口 × 战争结束年龄（0-15 岁）→ 测量年（2012），时间逻辑自洽。
- 三个样本（birthplace / place-of-residence / children）按研究问题分节构建，结构对称。
