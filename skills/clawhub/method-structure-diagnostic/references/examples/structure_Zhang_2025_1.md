# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

The dataset was collected from a counseling center at a large university in the western United States from 2017 to 2020. Sessions with recording or processing errors were excluded. Clients with only one session (intake) were excluded. The final dataset contained 610 clients, 48 therapists, and 2974 sessions. The average number of sessions per client was 4.9 (Mdn = 4), ranging from 2 to 17 sessions. The average number of clients per therapist was 12.7 (Mdn = 11.5), ranging from 1 to 43 clients. Tables S1 and S2 contain the demographic information of clients and therapists, respectively.

## 来源文献

Zhang et al., 2025, Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology
