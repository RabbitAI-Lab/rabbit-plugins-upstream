# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

We did not specify the sample size for this project a priori but simply collected as much data at the different measurement occasions as possible. All 2,931 participants completing T0 (personality assessment) and T1 (mind game) were invited to take part in T2 (coin-toss task). Of these, 2,021 participants started T2 and N = 1,916 (i.e., 95%) provided usable data (850 female, 1,064 male, two diverse; see https://osf.io/m2abp/ for details on data exclusions). Participants spanned a broad age range from 18 to 66 years (M = 44.2, SD = 12.3), and they covered diverse educational backgrounds, with 35% having a general certificate of secondary education (German: Realschulabschluss), 29% having a vocational diploma or university-entrance diploma (German: Fachabitur or Abitur), and 35% having a university/college degree. The majority of participants (72%) were in employment; only 7% were students. Participants were equally distributed across the two incentive conditions in the coin-toss task, with n = 960 in the money condition and n = 956 in the avoid-work condition.

## 来源文献

Thielmann et al., 2025, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
