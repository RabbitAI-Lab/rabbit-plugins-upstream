# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

Transparency and Openness. We preregistered the hypotheses, methods, procedures, and data analysis plan for this experiment on the Open Science Framework prior to data collection (https://osf.io/g7nsq/; Thiele et al., 2024). Stimulus examples (for direct links, see Figure 1), a document with supplemental information, eye-tracking raw data, and R scripts for preprocessing and analyzing the data are available at the same link. The full stimulus set cannot be shared publicly due to copyright reasons and consent restrictions. All deviations from the preregistered plan are explicitly mentioned.

## 来源文献

Thiele, M., Gredebäck, G., & Haun, D. B. M. (2025). Social connectedness without eye contact: 18- but not 9-month-olds use proximal touch to infer third-party joint attention during observational learning. Developmental Psychology.
