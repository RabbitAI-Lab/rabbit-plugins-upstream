# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

The Family Life Project (FLP) is a prospective longitudinal study of families residing in six predominately low-income, rural counties in central Pennsylvania and eastern North Carolina. A developmental epidemiological design with complex sampling procedures was used to recruit a representative sample of 1,290 children whose families resided in one of the target counties at the time of the child's birth. Participants were oversampled for poverty in both states and for African American race in North Carolina only. Detailed descriptions of the participating families, sampling procedures, and target counties are reported in Vernon-Feagans, et al. (2013). The Institutional Review Board at NYU Langone Health approved all study protocols (protocol # i19-00363), and informed written consent was obtained prior to testing.
