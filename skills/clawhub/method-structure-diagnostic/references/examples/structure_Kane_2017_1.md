# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

We tested 200 undergraduates, 100 each from the University of Colorado Boulder (hereafter referred to as Site A), a flagship state university in the Western United States, and the University of North Carolina at Greensboro (hereafter referred to as Site B), a comprehensive state university and minority-serving institution for African American students in the Southeastern United States. All participants were 18 to 35 years old and participated for partial fulfillment of a research requirement for an introductory psychology course. Half of the participants at each site were pseudorandomly assigned to either a note-taking or no-notes condition, via a prerandomized sequence sheet stored in each testing room; all participants within a session were assigned to the same condition. We tested, but did not retain or analyze the data from, additional participants who indicated on the math/statistics background questionnaire (see the following text) that they had taken a formal course on statistics.

## 来源文献

Kane et al., 2017, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General
