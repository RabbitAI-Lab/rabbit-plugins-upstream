# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

Participants were 135 English-speaking children (Mage = 5.98 years, SDage = 0.54; 78 female, 56 male; eight Asian, 43 Black, 62 White, 13 biracial, nine "other") recruited from a city in the northeastern United States (see the online supplemental materials for more details). We assessed 5- to 6-year-olds because this age period is when emotion recognition is still developing, particularly for negatively-valenced emotions (Widen, 2013).

## 来源文献

Plate et al., 2023, Developmental Psychology

## 适配诊断点

儿童样本的数量、年龄均值/标准差、性别与种族构成及招募地点完整报告，并附年龄选择的实质理由，是发展研究 Participants 段的结构范例。
