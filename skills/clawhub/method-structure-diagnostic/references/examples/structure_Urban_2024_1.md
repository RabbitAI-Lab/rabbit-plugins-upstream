# 片段类型：结构（Sampling and Observations 的样本量论证结构）

## 原文片段

> Due to exclusion criteria to ensure data quality (see Results section), one participant had to be discarded, resulting in a final sample of 480 participants evenly distributed across the three IATs, with 160 participants per IAT. We aimed for this number of participants based on Schönbrodt and Perugini's (2013) recommendations for a minimum number of participants for stable correlations and based on a one-way a priori power analysis for z-tests of two independent correlations with G*Power (α = .05, power = .8, p1 = .2, p2 = .45).
>
> The sample was rather diverse in terms of demographic criteria: From those who gave information on the corresponding demographic data, 45.83% were 27 or younger, 37.71% were between 28 and 36, and 16.46% were between 37 and 45 years old; 46.33% were female, 52.20% were male, and 1.47% were diverse; 46.93% were employed, 38.27 received educational training, 7.19% were self-employed, 5.07 were unemployed, and 2.54% were in other situations.

## 来源文献

Urban, Koch, & Rothermund (2024), Study 3, Sampling and Observations section. *The Implicit Association Test: A Methodological Investigation of the Relationship Between Test Difficulty and Criterion Validity.* https://osf.io/pcjwf/

## 适配诊断点

- 样本量论证双轨制：先引用稳定相关的最低样本量建议（Schönbrodt & Perugini, 2013），再做先验功效分析（G*Power，明确 α、power、假设的相关大小 p1/p2），论证链条完整可复核。
- "resulting in a final sample of X" 句式明确交代排除标准与最终样本的衔接；"evenly distributed across ... with 160 participants per IAT" 交代了分配结构。
- 人口学描述按比例报告（年龄分层、性别、职业状态），先给排除逻辑、再给最终样本构成，顺序规范。
