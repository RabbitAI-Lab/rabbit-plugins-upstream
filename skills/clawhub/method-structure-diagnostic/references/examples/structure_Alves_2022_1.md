# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

For Experiments 1a and 1b, we aimed for a sample of 200 participants to provide sufficient statistical power (>.80) to detect small-to-medium sized effects in the present design (Cohen, 1992). Because Experiment 1c varied both, context and target stimulus type, we doubled the desired sample size to 400. We recruited a total of 200 participants from the campus of a large German university and recruited another 202 participants from the same campus for Experiment 1b. Participants received either course credit or chocolate as compensation. Experiment 1c was conducted online via Prolific Academic, which returned data from 401 participants who were all located in the United Kingdom and who participated for £0.80.

## 来源文献

Alves et al., 2022, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
