# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

A priori power analysis using G*Power (Faul et al., 2009) conﬁrmed that for our intended linear multiple regression tests, a total sample size of 146 was needed to detect a medium effect size (f 2 = .15, power = .95). Families with infants were recruited by posting an invitation on a local baby product company website and Facebook, yielding a total of 168 Chinese mother–toddler dyads in Hong Kong visiting our lab when the toddlers were around age 12 months (T1, M = 11.9 months, SD = .48, Range = 11.2–13.6; 83 or 49% boys). For the follow-up assessment, 160 of them (95%) returned when the toddlers were around 18 months (T2, M = 17.8 months, SD = .57, Range = 16.4–19.6, 79 or 49% boys). For each lab visit, the research session took about 40 minutes, and the family received HK$150 (about US$20) and a small gift to the toddler. Ethical approval was granted by the research ethics committee of the principal investigator’s university. Maternal written consent was obtained prior to data collection.
