# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

Videos from Lucca et al. (2019) were repurposed for coding. In the original experiment, 29 full-term, typically developing 18-month-olds (13 females, 16 males; mean age = 18 months, 3 days, range = 17 months, 19 days – 18 months, 15 days) and their primary caregivers participated, recruited from a university-maintained database. The authors complied with all relevant ethical regulations to conduct this work, which was approved by the Institutional Review Board at the University of Washington: Protocol Title: "Infants' Persistence: Behaviors Throughout Early Childhood", ID: STUDY00003312. Infants were caregiver-reported as White (n = 22), Asian (n = 2), or mixed-race (n = 5). The sample size was limited to that of the original publication. Additional dyads were excluded due to inactivity during warm-up (n = 2), fussiness (n = 2), or parental interference (n = 1).

## 来源文献

Radovanovic et al., 2023, Developmental Psychology
