# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

Six families participated in our first cohort of the FIELD Project (Family-Infant Eye-tracking and Language Development) between May 2021 and October 2022. Families were recruited in the metropolitan area of Austin, Texas through word-of-mouth at the university, a subject database maintained by the university's developmental research group, and advertising at the local science museum. Families were visited 1–3 times for 1-hour long recording sessions, with a range of 1 day to 2 months between visits. Other family members were permitted to be at home during the recordings (other parents, older siblings, grandparents). The study was approved by the university's Institutional Review Board and all parents and other adults present during the recordings provided informed consent for themselves and their children to participate.
