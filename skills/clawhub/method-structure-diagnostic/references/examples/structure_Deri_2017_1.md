# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

Three hundred four participants from Amazon's Mechanical Turk (150 women; Mage = 37.1; 78% White, 10% Black, and 6% Asian) completed a survey in exchange for modest payment. This sample size allows us to detect effects as small as d = 0.16 with 80% power. Participants were first asked to think about their own social lives and the social lives of people they know. They then answered six questions about how their social lives compared with the social lives of those they had called to mind: "Who goes to more parties, you or others?"; "Who has more friends ...?"; "Who has a wider social network ...?"; "Who dines out more ...?"; "Who is part of a greater number of social circles ...?"; and "Who sees and interacts with their extended family more often ...?"

## 来源文献

Deri et al., 2017, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

样本构成、可检测效应量声明（d = .16 @ 80% power）与测量步骤（先引导思考→再回答六题，附完整题项）紧凑衔接，是问卷研究 Participants + Measures 的浓缩结构模板。
