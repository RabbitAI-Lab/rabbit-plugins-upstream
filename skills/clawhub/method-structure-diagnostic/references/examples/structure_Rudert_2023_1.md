# 片段类型：结构（功效分析到最终样本的完整样本流）

## 原文片段

> Participants were recruited via Prolific Academic to participate in a "Short Study about Experiences" for a payment of £0.35. We ran an a priori power analysis to detect the sample size needed to find the main effect of interest, namely a correlation between ostracism frequency and norm-violating behavior/ostracism frequency and perceived expendability, with G*Power (Faul et al., 2007). To account for a potentially higher measurement error due to participants' misperceptions and self-serving biases when responding to the survey items, we powered the study to detect correlations of small effect size (r = .10) with a statistical power of at least .90 and an α error of .05. Thus, we aimed to collect a minimal total sample size of 850 participants (gender-balanced). In total, 852 participants finished the questionnaire. One person indicated that their data should not be used for analysis and three persons reported that they did not complete the survey in a serious way. Thus, the final sample consisted of 848 U.K. residents (419 female, 9 nonbinary, 1 preferred not to answer, Mage = 40.08, SD = 13.97).

## 来源文献

Rudert, S. C., Möring, J. N. R., Kenntemich, C., & Büttner, C. M. (2023), Study 2 Method (Participants and Design). *When and Why We Ostracize Others: Motivated Social Exclusion in Group Contexts.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000423

## 适配诊断点

- 功效分析决策链完整：目标效应（相关性）→ 工具（G*Power, Faul et al., 2007）→ 效应量假设的理由（测量误差、自利偏差 → 保守取 r = .10）→ 功效（.90）与 α（.05）→ 所需样本（850）。
- 样本流汇报四段式：招募渠道与报酬 → 计划样本（preregistered）→ 实际完成数（852）→ 排除原因逐条（1 人拒用 + 3 人未认真作答）→ 最终样本人口学。
- 效应量选择的保守理由被显式论证（"To account for a potentially higher measurement error ... we powered the study to detect correlations of small effect size"），非拍脑袋设定。
- "Thus" 逻辑连词串联分析 → 目标样本 → 实际样本，因果链紧凑。
