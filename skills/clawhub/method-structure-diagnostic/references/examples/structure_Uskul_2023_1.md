## 原文片段

To examine the questions listed above, in twelve study sites, we recruited 4,956 participants, 3,097 of whom we retained for analyses (Mage = 21.45, SD = 4.36, Min = 18, Max = 71) after applying several inclusion criteria described below. Gender distribution was balanced (55.25% women). We primarily recruited participants via participant pools of collaborating institutions in Spain, Italy, Greece, Turkey, Cyprus, Lebanon, Egypt, the United Kingdom, the United States, Korea, and Japan. In the United Kingdom, we also collected data using Prolific to reach the targeted sample size (30.27% of U.K. participants) and to make up for COVID-related disruptions during the initial phase of data collection.
