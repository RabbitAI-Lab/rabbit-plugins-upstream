# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

All studies in this paper received ethical approval from the Sciences & Technology Cross-Schools Research Ethics Committee (C-REC) of the University of Sussex under the protocol names "ER/VC69/3 Predictors of purpose and meaning in life; a longitudinal study" (Study 1), "ER/VC69/9 Sources and dimensions of meaning in life; a longitudinal study" (Study 2), and "ER/VC69/15 AMENDMENT and EXTENSION TO ER/VC69/9" (Study 3). ... Of the 403 individuals who accessed our questionnaire, 84 quit before proceeding to the questionnaire items, and five participants were excluded for giving the same response to all items of a measure (e.g., Neither agree nor disagree for all statements that formed a scale). The final sample consisted of 314 participants (166 complete responses). Missing data were handled using full maximum likelihood estimation.

Costin et al., 2019, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
