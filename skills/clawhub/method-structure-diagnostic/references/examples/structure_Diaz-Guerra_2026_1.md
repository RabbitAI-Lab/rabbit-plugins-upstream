# 片段类型：结构

## 原文片段

The final sample pools data from k = 37 surveys conducted between 1990 and 2022, resulting in a total of 83,809 individuals (51.59% female, Mage = 47.05). The data were collected across five key national and international research institutions, providing comprehensive coverage of social attitudes. The largest contribution came from the Spanish Center of Sociological Research (CIS), which supplied the majority of the observations (k = 23; CIS, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, 1997a, 1997b, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2010, 2012a, 2012b, 2017a, 2017b, 2020, 2021a, 2021b, 2022). The remaining data were drawn from four other major sources. Specifically, we incorporated all available waves from the European Values Study (k = 4; EVS, 2011a, 2011b, 2022a, 2022b), four surveys from the Eurofound (European Quality of Life Surveys; Eurofound, 2023), three European Social Surveys focusing on time use and consideration of time provided via the European Research Infrastructure Consortium (2006, 2012, 2018), and three surveys from the International Social Survey Programme Research Group (2000, 2018, 2020) concerning religion that included a measure of fatalism. All surveys were cross-sectional, meaning that sampling procedures were independent for each wave across the decades.

## 来源文献

Díaz-Guerra et al., 2026, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点
