## Use Case:

External users, content strategists, marketers, and product teams use this skill to turn approved brand or product source material into evidence-backed AI search answers, entity records, content briefs, media prompts, and visibility retest plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated content could present unsupported marketing, pricing, partnership, competitor, or regulated-topic claims as evidence-backed facts.

Mitigation: Use approved source materials, preserve source and update metadata for each claim, and require human review before publishing.

Risk: Generated image or video prompts and outputs may include unverified text or misleading presentation.

Mitigation: Validate all media text and facts before use, and record source material, model, task ID, price snapshot, and result URL for later review.

Risk: AI search visibility retests may be mistaken for stable rankings or guaranteed model citations.

Mitigation: Treat retests as dated observations, keep screenshots and model/query records, and avoid promising third-party model inclusion, ranking, recommendation, or citation.
