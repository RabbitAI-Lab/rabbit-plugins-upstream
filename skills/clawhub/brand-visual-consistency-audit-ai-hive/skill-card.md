## Description:

This skill turns brand visual consistency audit requests into Chinese workflows, runnable AI-HIVE commands, and deliverables for checking logo, color, typography, composition, tone, people, and messaging differences against user-provided brand standards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External brand marketing, design management, advertising agency, and content operations teams use this skill to audit images, videos, and campaign materials against supplied brand standards, then prioritize fixes and retesting. Developers and operators can also use its scripts to create briefs, query AI-HIVE capabilities, submit approved media generation tasks, and process video outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected brand assets, prompts, and reference media may be uploaded to AI-HIVE.

Mitigation: Confirm rights to all input materials and require explicit approval before any upload.

Risk: Image or video generation tasks may be paid operations.

Mitigation: Review price snapshots, routing mode, prompt, model, and batch size before submitting any task.

Risk: API keys could be exposed through logs, files, screenshots, or repositories.

Mitigation: Keep API keys in environment variables or protected local config and avoid echoing or committing secrets.
