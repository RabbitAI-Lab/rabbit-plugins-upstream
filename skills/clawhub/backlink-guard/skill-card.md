## Description:

Checks backlink opportunities before submission, verifies published links, and audits backlink profiles for spam patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuyansen](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuyansen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Site owners, marketers, and developers use the command line skill to assess backlink sources, verify listings, review backlink profiles, and explore competitor or AI search citations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some backlink and search signals use paid external services.

Mitigation: Review the stated costs and approve paid runs before they start.

Risk: Submitting a site may send content to an external destination.

Mitigation: Review and approve each submission's destination and text.
