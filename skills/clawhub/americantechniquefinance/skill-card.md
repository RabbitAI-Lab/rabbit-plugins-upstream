## Description:

This skill analyzes US-listed technology giants such as Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, and Tesla by producing deep, sourced investment and competitive reports with charts, valuation tables, financial analysis, market-share context, moat assessment, and stock trend context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ttggjj](https://clawhub.ai/user/ttggjj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, investors, students, and strategy teams use this skill to generate sourced company-analysis reports for major US-listed technology companies. The reports support business-model review, competitive positioning, financial interpretation, valuation comparison, risk framing, and stock-performance context without providing personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may influence financial decisions even though market prices, estimates, and company data can become stale quickly.

Mitigation: Require dated citations for every figure and advise users to verify cited figures and dates before relying on the report.

Risk: Users may mistake broad company analysis for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Frame stock-performance and valuation sections as context only and include a clear disclaimer that the output is analysis, not investment advice.
