## Description:

Creates short AI videos by adapting a source video's emotional structure, swapping people or products while keeping its shots, or generating an original video from a creative brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[riffkit](https://clawhub.ai/user/riffkit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Signed-in Riffkit users can create short-form social and marketing videos from a creative brief or remake an existing video by adapting its format or swapping its people, products, or setting. The agent guides the user through choices, pricing, confirmation, and delivery of the generated video and related post text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The heartbeat can replace the skill's instructions with remote content, and the supplied scan was partial with risks reported beyond the stated video-creation purpose.

Mitigation: Review the remote updater and its integrity checks, and resolve the incomplete scan coverage before installing or relying on the skill.
