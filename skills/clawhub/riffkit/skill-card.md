## Description:

Creates short AI videos and UGC-style ads from a source video or original brief, with optional characters, product placement, and language choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[riffkit](https://clawhub.ai/user/riffkit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to make short-form videos or product ads by adapting a video, swapping its subject, or creating a new video from a brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The update heartbeat can replace installed skill instructions outside ClawHub review.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates or make them manual; review any downloaded changes before use.

Risk: A reusable account session is stored locally.

Mitigation: Protect the local session and delete it when finished.

Risk: Generation, retries, continuations, extra ratios, and plan changes may incur charges.

Mitigation: Check the current quote and obtain user approval before each charged action.

Risk: Uploaded videos, product images, and creative briefs are shared with Riffkit.

Mitigation: Upload only material the user approves sharing and avoid sensitive files.
