## Description:

Turns supplied articles, notes, or scripts into an approved, listener-friendly solo podcast script and narrated episode audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Podcast creators use the skill to adapt supplied material into a speakable script, establish a consistent host direction, and produce a solo narrated episode.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broader Beatra account permissions than its podcast workflow requires.

Mitigation: Review the shared image, video, voice-editing, and spending permissions before approving access; revoke access in the Beatra Console when no longer needed.

Risk: Damaged installation records can cause uninstall to disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Check the installation records before uninstalling, then verify other Beatra skills remain connected.
