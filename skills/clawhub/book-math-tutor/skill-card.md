## Use Case: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The booking workflow can share customer contact details and location or search details with Lokuli. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the tutor, time, cost, cancellation terms when available, and user consent to share the details. <br>
