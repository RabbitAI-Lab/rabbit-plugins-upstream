## Description:

医疗器械风险管理（ISO 14971:2019 / GB/T 42062-2022）专题技能，帮助用户按风险分析、风险评价、风险控制、综合剩余风险、风险管理报告和生产后反馈组织医疗器械风险管理文件。

## Use Case:

External medical device regulatory, quality, and product teams use this skill to draft and review risk management files, hazard checklists, risk matrices, control rationales, and report outlines for medical device submissions. It is a Chinese-language drafting and checklist aid and requires qualified review against current jurisdiction-specific requirements before reliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory standards and recognition status may change or differ by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify current ISO, NMPA, FDA, EU, and PMDA requirements against official sources before relying on generated outputs.

Risk: Drafted risk-management content may be too generic for a specific device, intended use, patient population, or submission strategy.

Mitigation: Have qualified regulatory, quality, and clinical reviewers tailor and approve product-specific hazard analysis, acceptability criteria, residual-risk conclusions, and benefit-risk rationale.

Risk: Redistribution or reuse may be affected by package attribution and restrictive knowledge-content terms.

Mitigation: Review the package attribution, license documentation, and knowledge-content terms before redistributing, republishing, or reusing the material.
