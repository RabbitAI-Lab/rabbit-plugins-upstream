## Description:

Medical device risk management skill — ISO 14971:2019 / GB/T 42062-2022 6-step process, hazard identification checklists, risk matrix & acceptability criteria, risk management report framework, global standard mapping with official references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Medical-device regulatory, quality, and product teams can use this skill to organize hazard identification, risk estimation and control, residual-risk review, and risk-management report drafts. They should verify current standards and product-specific conclusions with qualified staff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic checklists, risk matrices, and standards references may be outdated or may not fit a specific device or jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Have qualified staff verify current standards and assess conclusions against the specific product and market.

Risk: The bundled audit score and ownership attestation may be outdated or incomplete; rerunning the optional audit replaces its security_results.json file.

Mitigation: Do not treat the bundled score or attestation as current security certification; preserve any needed results and explicitly select the intended skill directory before running the optional audit.
