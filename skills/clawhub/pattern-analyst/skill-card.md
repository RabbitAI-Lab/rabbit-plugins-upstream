## Description: <br>
Analyze interactions to identify patterns in what Enzo shares, why he shares it, and how it connects to his goals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[enzoricciulli](https://clawhub.ai/user/enzoricciulli) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and personal productivity agents use this skill to track recurring interests, intent signals, goal connections, and emerging patterns across interactions. It is intended for private self-improvement workflows where the agent maintains markdown notes and surfaces concise insights during periodic reviews. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can maintain a persistent local pattern log and profile from inferred behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review notes/patterns.md and USER.md regularly, and require explicit confirmation before profile updates if inferred traits should not be stored automatically. <br>
Risk: Private pattern insights may contain sensitive personal context. <br>
Mitigation: Keep analysis private to the intended user and avoid sharing pattern insights in group chats or with others. <br>
