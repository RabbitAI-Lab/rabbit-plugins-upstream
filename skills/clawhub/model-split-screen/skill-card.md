## Description:

Creates side-by-side or stacked comparison videos from two runs of the same prompt, with measured time and cost labels and an optional blind reveal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to make a fair visual comparison of two models, settings, or renders by running the same prompt under matching conditions and presenting the results with measured time and cost.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
