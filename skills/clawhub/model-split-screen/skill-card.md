## Description:

Creates side-by-side or stacked comparison videos from two runs, with labels, measured time and cost, and an optional blind reveal or winner frame.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to turn two model outputs, renders, or edits into a comparison video that presents both results under the same prompt or conditions, with their measured runtime and cost.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
