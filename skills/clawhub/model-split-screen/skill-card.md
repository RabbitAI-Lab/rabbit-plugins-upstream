## Description:

Creates side-by-side or stacked videos comparing two model runs, with measured time and cost and optional blind reveal and winner frame.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to present a same-prompt comparison of two model runs as a social-ready video, with clear labels and measured time and cost.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An installer command may fetch an unpinned package version.

Mitigation: Review the command and package source, and pin a trusted version before installing where practical.

Risk: Prompts or comparison results may contain information that should not be made public.

Mitigation: Review prompts and clips for sensitive content and obtain approval before posting them publicly.

Risk: The security scan was partial because two local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Resolve and review those references before relying on the benign scan result.
