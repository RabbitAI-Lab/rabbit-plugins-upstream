## Description:

A Chinese-language guide for developers creating or revising educational agent skills, covering role, rules, memory, output, safety boundaries, and self-checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Skill developers and technically experienced students use this guide to draft or improve Chinese-language educational SKILL.md files and check their behavior, privacy boundaries, and handoffs. It does not provide subject tutoring or exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curricula, consent practices, and crisis contacts for other regions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese school-system assumptions and mainland crisis contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, consent rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: A draft skill for minors may omit consent or crisis safeguards.

Mitigation: Review drafts against the included privacy and crisis guidance before deployment.
