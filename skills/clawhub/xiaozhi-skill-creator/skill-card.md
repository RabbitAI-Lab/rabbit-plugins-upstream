## Description:

Guides authors in drafting and reviewing education-oriented SKILL.md files using a four-layer structure, templates, privacy boundaries, and a five-step process.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SKILL developers and high-school authors with programming or prompt-writing experience use this guide to draft or improve Chinese K–12 learning skills. It provides authoring guidance, not subject tutoring or practice questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China defaults; adapt curricula, consent rules, crisis contacts, and language before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific curriculum, consent, and crisis-contact defaults may be unsuitable in other regions or languages.

Mitigation: Localize curricula, consent rules, help channels, and user-facing language before applying the templates elsewhere.

Risk: Unreviewed skill drafts could omit appropriate privacy or crisis-response boundaries.

Mitigation: Review generated drafts against the shared privacy, consent, and crisis-response guidance before deployment.
