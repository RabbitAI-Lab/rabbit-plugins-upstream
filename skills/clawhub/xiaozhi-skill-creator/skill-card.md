## Description:

A Chinese-language guide for writing and refining Xiaozhi/OpenClaw learning skills using a four-layer structure, a five-step workflow, and safety and privacy boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Skill developers and high school students with programming or prompt-writing experience use this guide to draft or troubleshoot learning-skill instructions. It provides templates and checks for role, rules, memory, output, and safety boundaries; it does not provide subject tutoring or exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent rules, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific curriculum, consent, and crisis-contact guidance may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Adapt these requirements to the local curriculum and applicable consent and crisis-support practices before exposing generated skills to students.

Risk: Generated learning-skill instructions could mishandle student information or provide unsuitable safety guidance.

Mitigation: Review generated skills against the included consent, privacy, and crisis protocols before deployment.
