## Description:

Turns articles, notes, topics, or briefs into faceless explainer videos with scene-specific typography, diagrams, and abstract graphics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to turn supplied text into narrated, faceless explainer videos with planned scenes, motion graphics, and captions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Review preview processes and stored preferences when handling sensitive projects.
