## Description: <br>
Faceless Explainer turns articles, notes, topics, or briefs into explainer videos with invented scene visuals such as typography, abstract graphics, diagrams, and data visualizations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and creative operators use this skill to transform source text into a HyperFrames faceless explainer video workflow, including brief setup, storyboarding, audio, frame generation, validation, and MP4 rendering. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow tells agents to silently update HyperFrames skills from remote sources and mutate the global HyperFrames skill set. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and require explicit approval or pinned versions before running update or global mutation commands. <br>
