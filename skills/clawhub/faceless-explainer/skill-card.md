## Description:

Turns text into a faceless explainer video using scene-specific typography, graphics, diagrams, and data visualizations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to turn articles, notes, briefs, or topics into narrated or silent explainer videos with invented visuals rather than captured footage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Remote-resolved HyperFrames commands can update installed skills and add registry blocks.

Mitigation: Confirm updates before execution; prefer a pinned or locally vetted HyperFrames CLI and review registry additions.

Risk: Media generation can use configured provider credentials, and sensitive source text may appear in generated briefs or preferences.

Mitigation: Review generated briefs and preferences before sharing, and use media providers only with appropriate credentials and data permissions.

Risk: Preview and audio tasks may run in the background.

Mitigation: Review and monitor background tasks and their outputs before publishing the video.
