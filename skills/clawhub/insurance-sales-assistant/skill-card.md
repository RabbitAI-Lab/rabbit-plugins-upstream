## Description:

Helps insurance agents organize lead follow-up, appointments, policy sales, and performance reviews with Feishu workflows and templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fatboyslash](https://clawhub.ai/user/fatboyslash)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance agents use this skill to organize warm leads, plan customer follow-ups and appointments, record policy sales, and review weekly or monthly performance. It provides Feishu workspace templates and sales content suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad conversation triggers may initiate Feishu setup or recurring tasks without clear user approval.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval for the Feishu account, resource creation, and each recurring job; keep the unrelated news digest disabled and explain how scheduled jobs can be removed.

Risk: Customer records and screenshots may expose identifying or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Import only authorized customer data, minimize identifying details, check sharing permissions, and redact customer screenshots before posting.
