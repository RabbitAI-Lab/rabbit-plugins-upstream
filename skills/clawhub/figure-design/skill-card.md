## Description:

Helps designers turn character concepts into manufacturable collectible figures through design guidance, engineering checks, project templates, and production gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junci003](https://clawhub.ai/user/junci003)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Figure designers and product teams use this skill to turn character concepts into manufacturable collectibles, review specifications and part splits, and prepare sampling and quality-control materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The project scaffold's --force option and the report writer's --json-out option can overwrite files.

Mitigation: Choose a new project folder and report path, and check the target files before using overwrite options.

Risk: Numeric design checks are planning aids and may not be sufficient for factory tooling decisions.

Mitigation: Obtain written factory DFM sign-off before tooling.
