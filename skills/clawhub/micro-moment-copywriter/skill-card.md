## Description:

Generate hyper-contextual microcopy (CTAs, subject lines, push notifications, form labels, error messages) optimized for awareness, consideration, decision, and retention micromoments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth teams, product builders, and developers use this skill to generate conversion-focused microcopy variants for customer journey micromoments across web, email, mobile, SaaS, and commerce experiences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign context, audience segments, or behavioral data supplied to the skill may be sent to AI providers.

Mitigation: Use anonymized or aggregated segment data where possible and avoid sensitive personal attributes.

Risk: Generated scarcity, urgency, health, financial, or compliance-related claims may be inaccurate or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Review claims for factual accuracy, brand alignment, and regulatory compliance before publishing or testing.
