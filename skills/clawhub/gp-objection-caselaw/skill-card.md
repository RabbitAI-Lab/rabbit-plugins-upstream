## Description: <br>
政采质疑答复“判例法”智库 helps PRC government-procurement purchasers and procurement agencies match similar complaint-handling decisions, compare supported and unsupported objection outcomes, identify reply risks, and draft compliant objection responses in Chinese. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External procurement professionals, agency staff, and counsel use this skill when responding to supplier objections in mainland China government procurement matters. It supports similar-case research, legal and factual comparison, risk spotting, and draft reply-letter preparation, with final review by qualified staff. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China (mainland PRC government procurement matters) <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated legal conclusions or draft objection replies may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for the specific procurement record. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as review material only and require counsel or responsible procurement staff to verify facts, legal citations, deadlines, and final wording before sending any reply. <br>
Risk: The skill depends on named IMA knowledge bases for case matching and legal support; missing or unavailable knowledge bases can reduce reliability. <br>
Mitigation: Deploy it only where those knowledge bases are expected to be connected, and treat unmatched or unverified conclusions as requiring manual confirmation. <br>
Risk: Suggested pattern-library updates could alter installed skill behavior if applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Do not allow generated suggestions to modify installed skill files without explicit approval and change review. <br>
