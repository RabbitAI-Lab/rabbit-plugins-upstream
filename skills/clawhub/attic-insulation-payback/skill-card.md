## Description:

Use when considering attic insulation upgrade, before accepting an insulation contractor quote, or when comparing home energy upgrades — R-value stack math, HDD/CDD heating-energy model from fuel bills, real payback in years with degradation and air-sealing priority doctrine (seal before you stack), and quote forensics that catches overblown R-value claims and missing prep charges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners comparing attic insulation upgrades or reviewing contractor quotes can use this skill to estimate heating savings and payback, check R-value claims, and identify when air sealing should come first.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Savings and payback are estimates and may not match a home's measured energy use or an installation's actual performance.

Mitigation: Check attic measurements and model assumptions against recent bills, and verify pricing before committing to work.

Risk: Rebates and contractor pricing can change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm current rebate terms and obtain current itemized quotes before making a decision.
