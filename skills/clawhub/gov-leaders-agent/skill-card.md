## Description: <br>
Collects, maintains, and queries public information about government officials across national, provincial, and local levels, with support for multi-source collection, incremental updates, personnel-change detection, multidimensional queries, and summary reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and analysts use this skill to collect, update, query, and report on public government leadership information from official and reputable public sources. It is intended to preserve source citations, update dates, and public-data boundaries when answering leadership information requests. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill handles public information about government officials, so collecting private details, uncited claims, or outdated records could create privacy and accuracy risk. <br>
Mitigation: Use only official or reputable public sources, keep citations and update dates visible, and avoid collecting or storing private personal details beyond the stated public-interest purpose. <br>
