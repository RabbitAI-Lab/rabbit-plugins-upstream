## Description:

Organizes invoice and receipt details into categorized reimbursement tables, checks missing fields, and flags items for follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance staff, employees, and small-business owners use this skill to organize supplied invoice details into expense categories, identify missing reimbursement information, and prepare a copyable summary. It does not perform bookkeeping or tax filing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Invoice details may contain personal, tax, or business-sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share only necessary details and redact sensitive information before providing invoices.

Risk: Reimbursement and tax-deduction guidance may not match company policy or applicable law.

Mitigation: Verify conclusions with company policy, an accountant, or the relevant authority before acting.

Risk: Broad example triggers could invoke this skill for unrelated table-formatting requests.

Mitigation: Invoke it deliberately for invoice or reimbursement tasks.
