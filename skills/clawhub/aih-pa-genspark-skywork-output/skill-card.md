## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive project material could be uploaded outside its approved scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the AI-HIVE domain and upload scope; minimize or redact private material before transfer.

Risk: Paid model calls or external actions could exceed the user's authorization.

Mitigation: Check current pricing and set a spending limit before paid calls; require separate explicit authorization for sending, payment, deletion or publication.

Risk: A plan or unverified result could be mistaken for a finished deliverable.

Mitigation: Inspect actual output files and evidence before marking work completed or accepted; disclose unavailable tools and unresolved items.
