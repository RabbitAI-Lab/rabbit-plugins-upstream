## Description:

Creates social-commerce outfit inspiration images by keeping the clothing while changing the model, setting, pose, and lighting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and fashion marketers use it to turn outfit images into social-commerce lifestyle imagery while preserving the clothing and changing the model, pose, setting, and lighting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outfit or model-reference images and prompts may be sent to an external image-generation provider.

Mitigation: Before sending inputs, confirm the selected provider and its data-transfer terms; avoid images or prompts you are not comfortable sharing.

Risk: The security scan was partial, so behavior in some package components remains unevaluated.

Mitigation: Review the package before installing and check any components that were not fully inspected.
