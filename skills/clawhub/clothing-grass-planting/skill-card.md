## Description:

Creates social-commerce outfit images that preserve the clothing while changing the model, pose, and setting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and developers use the skill to turn outfit photos into lifestyle images for social posts, keeping the outfit while changing the model, pose, and scene.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photos and prompts may be sent to external image services; the security scan is partial and some behavior may not have been assessed.

Mitigation: Before use, review the selected provider's privacy and retention terms, avoid sending sensitive or identifiable photos, and inspect generated images before sharing.
