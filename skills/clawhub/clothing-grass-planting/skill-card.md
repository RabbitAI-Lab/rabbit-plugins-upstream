## Description:

Creates social-commerce outfit images that preserve the clothing while changing the model, pose, scene, and lighting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and commerce teams use this skill to turn an outfit photo into lifestyle promotional images for social platforms, using a reference image or a written scene description.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A reference person's likeness could appear in promotional images and imply an endorsement.

Mitigation: Review the skill before use; use reference images only with rights and consent, and avoid implying that a real person endorses a product.

Risk: Prompts and reference images may be sent to the selected cloud image provider.

Mitigation: Check the provider and obtain permission before uploading images or sensitive prompts.
