## Description:

Transforms an outfit photo into lifestyle-style social media imagery while preserving the clothing and changing the model, pose, and setting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use outfit photos and optional scene references to produce lifestyle-style images for social posts while keeping the clothing consistent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected prompts and image inputs are sent to the configured external model provider.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when comfortable sharing those inputs with dLazy or the selected provider.

Risk: Using photos without rights or consent can misuse identifiable people's likenesses.

Mitigation: Use outfit and model photos you have rights to use, and obtain consent for identifiable people.

Risk: Generated social or marketing images may misrepresent an outfit or imply an inauthentic endorsement.

Mitigation: Review garment details and the image's claims and context before publication.
