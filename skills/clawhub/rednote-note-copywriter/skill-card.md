## Description:

Create Xiaohongshu or REDnote copy from a product, experience, topic, or audience brief, including title options, a structured note body, cover wording, relevant hashtags, and a natural comment starter.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and marketers use this skill to turn a product, experience, topic, or audience brief into editable Xiaohongshu or REDnote post copy. It produces title options, a note body, cover text, hashtags, and a conversational comment starter without creating images or publishing posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence says this writing skill requests broader account permissions than its task needs, including spending and media controls.

Mitigation: Review the Beatra approval carefully and prefer a version with writing-only access.

Risk: The security guidance says installation reporting is automatic and removing this package may leave a shared connection in place.

Mitigation: Revoke the connection in Beatra Console when you want access ended.
