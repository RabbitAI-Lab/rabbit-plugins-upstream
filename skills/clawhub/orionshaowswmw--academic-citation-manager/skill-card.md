## Description:

Add real references and standardize citations for research papers and theses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orionshaowswmw](https://clawhub.ai/user/orionshaowswmw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic-writing developers use this skill to fetch bibliographic metadata, format in-text citations and bibliographies, batch-import references, convert citation styles, and check citation consistency across papers and theses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Citation searches can send DOI, ISBN, title, or author information to external bibliography services.

Mitigation: Review the metadata being searched and avoid submitting sensitive or unpublished manuscript details unless that disclosure is acceptable.

Risk: Export and report actions can overwrite local bibliography, JSON, BibTeX, or report files.

Mitigation: Use copies or version control for valuable manuscripts and reference libraries, and write outputs to deliberate paths.
