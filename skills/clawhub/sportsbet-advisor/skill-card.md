## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Betting predictions may be shallow, incorrect, or unsuitable for gambling decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as casual research support only; verify odds, injuries, dates, and market details with authoritative sources before acting. <br>
Risk: Confidence scores are not calibrated financial advice and may overstate certainty. <br>
Mitigation: Keep confidence at or below the documented 95% cap and include the required disclaimer that outcomes are unpredictable and may be wrong. <br>
Risk: The helper script relies on web-search snippets and simple heuristics, so event details, odds, start times, or default picks can be incomplete or misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Manually review any generated suggestions and confirm live market details on Sportsbet or other authoritative sports and betting sources. <br>
