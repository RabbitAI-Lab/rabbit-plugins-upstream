## Description:

Finds marketing professionals and growth specialists to recruit using apidojo's Twitter scrapers on Apify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiting teams, startup hiring managers, and executive search firms use this skill to discover, enrich, score, and shortlist marketing professionals and growth specialists from Twitter/X activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow sends recruiting search terms and Twitter/X profile targets to Apify and may produce candidate CSV or JSON files containing recruiting records.

Mitigation: Confirm the Apify data-sharing posture before use, keep APIFY_TOKEN in a secret store or environment variable, avoid sharing live-token commands, and handle exported candidate files as recruiting records.
