## Description:

Creates keyword-focused SEO content briefs from search results and connected site data, then scores drafts before publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content writers and SEO teams use the skill to plan a page around a target keyword, identify relevant internal links, and check a completed draft before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting SEOmatic can expose connected-site Search Console and SEO data to the service.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants SEOmatic involved before querying connected-site data for a general writing request.

Risk: Keyword, SERP, and article-generation actions may use allowances, credits, or prepaid article units.

Mitigation: Tell the user about potential charges before initiating paid actions.

Risk: Suggested content or site changes may be inaccurate or premature to publish.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims and require user approval before publishing or applying site changes.
