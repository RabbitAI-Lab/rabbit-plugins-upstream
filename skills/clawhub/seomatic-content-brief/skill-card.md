## Description:

Build keyword-focused content briefs from live search results and connected Google Search Console data, then score drafts against the brief before publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External site owners, SEO practitioners, and content writers use this skill to build keyword-focused content briefs from search results and connected Google Search Console data, then assess drafts against the brief before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sending draft text to SEOmatic may disclose content that should remain private.

Mitigation: Review the draft for confidential or sensitive information before sending it to SEOmatic.
