## Description: <br>
Audits whether an organization's AI spend produced measurable returns against baselines, producing per-tool keep, consolidate, renegotiate, or cut verdicts, hidden-cost accounting, and baseline plans for unknowns. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Finance, procurement, and operations teams use this skill to audit AI tool spend after adoption, especially for renewals, consolidation decisions, budget defense, or establishing baselines before the next audit. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include unnecessary confidential financial, contract, or personnel data while preparing an ROI audit. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the spend, utilization, outcome metrics, and assumptions needed for the audit, and redact sensitive details that are not necessary for the decision. <br>
Risk: ROI verdicts can be misleading if weak evidence, vendor calculators, or self-reported savings are treated as high-confidence results. <br>
Mitigation: Require each figure to carry its method and confidence, discount self-reported savings, list hidden costs, and create baseline plans where evidence is insufficient. <br>
