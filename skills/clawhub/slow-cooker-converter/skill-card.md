## Description:

Converts stovetop and oven recipes for slow cookers or pressure cookers with time and liquid adjustments, capacity checks, and guidance on when to add sensitive ingredients.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Home cooks use this skill to adapt soups, braises, and similar recipes between stovetop, oven, slow cooker, and pressure cooker, with guidance on timing, fill levels, and ingredients that need special handling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approximate cooking times or pressure-cooker directions may not ensure safe food preparation.

Mitigation: Check the appliance manual, safe internal temperatures, and food-safety requirements, especially for beans, poultry, and frozen meat.

Risk: Estimated recipe volume may exceed a cooker's safe fill limit.

Mitigation: Confirm the actual fill level against the appliance's limits before cooking.
