## Description: <br>
Turns a topic or article into three LinkedIn/X post ideas with hooks, theses, outlines, and template labels while avoiding invented facts or citations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ryancaldor-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/ryancaldor-max) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External content creators, marketers, and professionals use this skill to turn a topic, article excerpt, or notes into structured LinkedIn/X post ideas for drafting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided topics, article excerpts, or notes are sent to SocialNexis for processing. <br>
Mitigation: Do not provide secrets, private documents, personal data, or confidential business material unless authorized to share it with SocialNexis. <br>
Risk: Generated post ideas may still need editorial and factual review before publication. <br>
Mitigation: Review the hook, thesis, outline, and any source-dependent claims before drafting or publishing. <br>
