## Use Case:

Homeowners use this skill to compare appliances and plan purchase, layout, utility reservations, and installation questions during renovation. It provides practical guidance for avoiding common appliance selection and installation pitfalls.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Appliance advice may not match a specific model's installation or operating requirements.

Mitigation: Check the manufacturer's instructions for the specific appliance, keep required airflow clear, and do not handle equipment while it is operating; use qualified service personnel for repairs.

Risk: Gas, electrical, structural, and concealed renovation work can create serious safety or property damage risks.

Mitigation: Use qualified professionals, obtain required utility and property approvals, and do not perform these works as DIY projects.
