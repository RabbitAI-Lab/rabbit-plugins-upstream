## Description:

批判性思维提问教练 helps users evaluate arguments, decisions, data claims, and debate positions through 12-dimension reasoning checks, logical-fallacy identification, and Socratic questioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to critique concrete text, plans, decisions, data interpretations, and debate topics for reasoning quality. It produces structured diagnostics, follow-up questions, fallacy explanations, and revision guidance grounded in the user's provided material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or confidential text submitted for critique may be processed by the user's agent environment and configured model provider.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting secrets or regulated data unless the agent environment and provider are approved for that data.

Risk: The skill may misclassify reasoning issues or overstate flaws in an argument, decision, or data claim.

Mitigation: Review the cited text, diagnosis, and suggested revisions before relying on them in decisions or communications.
