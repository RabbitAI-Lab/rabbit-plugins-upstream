## Description:

A Chinese-first critical-thinking coach that evaluates arguments across 12 dimensions, identifies logical fallacies, guides Socratic questioning, audits decisions, and flags data interpretation traps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to critique proposals, claims, reports, charts, decisions, and debate positions with structured critical-thinking prompts and reports. It is intended for human review and reasoning support, not for making decisions without checking the original evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste secrets, customer data, credentials, or regulated information into arguments, proposals, or reports for critique.

Mitigation: Use only in an agent environment approved for that content and redact sensitive information before submitting text.

Risk: The skill may produce misleading critiques when the pasted argument, data summary, or decision context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the original source material and provide missing assumptions, evidence, and constraints before acting on recommendations.
