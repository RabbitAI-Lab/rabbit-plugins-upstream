## Description:

This skill helps AI short-drama directors, art teams, editors, and continuity managers turn scripts and scene references into continuity bibles, spatial anchors, lighting and prop notes, shot-axis guidance, executable AI-HIVE commands, and review checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, commercial content teams, and agent users use this skill to plan short-drama and social-commerce video continuity before creating images, videos, edits, and task records. It is intended for authorized materials and human-reviewed production workflows, especially where scene, lighting, prop, character, and timeline consistency matter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may upload user-selected media to AI-HIVE and download generated outputs.

Mitigation: Use only private or licensed media that is appropriate to upload, and review generated files before reuse or publication.

Risk: Image and video generation can create paid tasks after user confirmation.

Mitigation: Confirm prompt, mode, routing, model configuration, and price snapshot before submitting generation or batch jobs.

Risk: API keys can be supplied through environment variables, command-line options, or a local config file.

Mitigation: Prefer environment or CLI-provided keys for temporary use, keep any local config file private, and do not place real keys in prompts, logs, screenshots, or version control.

Risk: Short-drama adaptation requests may involve copyrighted references, brands, real people, or platform claims.

Mitigation: Require authorization for source material and rewrite protected expression, character details, logos, music, testimonials, and factual claims into original, reviewable content.
