## Description:

Guides agents in configuring Gladia audio intelligence features for live and pre-recorded transcription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gladiaio](https://clawhub.ai/user/gladiaio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to configure diarization, translation, redaction, subtitles, summarization, and other transcript insights for Gladia live or pre-recorded audio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audio, transcripts, metadata, or prompts may contain sensitive personal information when sent for processing.

Mitigation: Confirm consent and policy approval before submission; minimize shared data, enable PII redaction where appropriate, and verify retention and compliance requirements.
