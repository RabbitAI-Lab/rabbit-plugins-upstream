## Description: <br>
Nov Tarot guides an agent through Rider-Waite tarot readings, matching user questions to spreads and producing empathetic interpretations that frame cards as possibilities rather than fixed predictions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[joe-zhouman](https://clawhub.ai/user/joe-zhouman) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill through an agent to ask tarot questions, interpret drawn cards or spreads, and receive reflective guidance focused on emotional insight and personal choice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save sensitive tarot-session details locally without clear consent. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit user consent before saving session data, explain where reports or session files are written, and avoid entering highly sensitive personal information. <br>
Risk: The skill frames tarot as counselor-like emotional support. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use it for crisis support or medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes advice; direct users to qualified professionals for those needs. <br>
