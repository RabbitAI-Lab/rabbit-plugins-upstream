## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a supportive pregnancy journal for weekly development updates, symptom and mood logging, appointment tracking, and due date countdowns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter sensitive pregnancy symptoms, appointments, due dates, or other health details while privacy behavior is unclear. <br>
Mitigation: Review storage, sync, retention, deletion, and sharing controls before entering sensitive health information. <br>
Risk: The artifact says data syncs across devices and also says all data stays local on the user's machine. <br>
Mitigation: Resolve the privacy-language conflict before relying on local-only confidentiality claims. <br>
Risk: Pregnancy tracking output may be mistaken for medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for informational support only and consult a healthcare provider for medical concerns, medications, or symptoms outside normal pregnancy experience. <br>
