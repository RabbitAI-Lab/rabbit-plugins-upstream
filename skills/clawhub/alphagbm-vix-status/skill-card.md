## Description: <br>
Current VIX level plus a five-tier fear classification and option-seller strategy hint, with current percentile versus one-year history and recent tier distribution. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to translate the market-wide VIX reading into a volatility tier, contextual history, and options-selling posture before per-ticker analysis. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trading guidance can be misleading if treated as financial advice or used without current market validation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the strategy text as educational, verify live VIX and market data independently, and avoid making decisions from the VIX tier alone. <br>
