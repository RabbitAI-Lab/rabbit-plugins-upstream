## Description:

Conduct adversarial testing on uncensored AI models by evaluating refusal rates, response coherence, and comparative model performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and AI safety evaluators use this skill to run controlled adversarial evaluations of uncensored AI models, measuring refusal rate, coherence, quality, and comparative behavior against original models.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Forbidden-prompt testing against uncensored models can produce harmful or sensitive content.

Mitigation: Run only authorized, controlled evaluations; avoid generating or distributing harmful content; and define reporting boundaries before use.
