This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Parents of children aged 0–6 request tailored play activities, routine suggestions, and answers to everyday parenting questions through a paid per-use service. The advice is not a medical diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Parenting prompts are sent to a remote paid service.

Mitigation: Share only information you are comfortable sending to that service; avoid unnecessary personal details.

Risk: The payment request has an unrelated description that may obscure what is being purchased.

Mitigation: Confirm the merchant, service, description, and charge before approving payment.

Risk: The suggested installer uses an unpinned @latest package version.

Mitigation: Verify the publisher and package version before installing, or use a reviewed pinned version.
