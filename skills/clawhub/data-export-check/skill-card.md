## Description:

Screens five stated prerequisites for cross-border data transfers under Chinese data protection requirements and flags missing items.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Security and legal teams use this checklist to identify missing steps before reviewing a proposed data transfer from China. The result is a preliminary screening aid, not legal advice or regulatory approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A checklist PASS may be mistaken for verified legal compliance.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers confirm the evidence and current legal requirements before any transfer; do not treat the result as certification.

Risk: Stale file hashes and broad tool permissions may undermine assurance in controlled environments.

Mitigation: Recheck release files and limit agent permissions to the local checklist actions actually needed.
