## Description:

Provides a five-item preliminary checklist for data-export compliance, reporting missing steps as a failure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and compliance teams use this Chinese-language checklist to identify missing preliminary steps before a data transfer outside China. It does not replace legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A preliminary checklist may be mistaken for a complete legal determination.

Mitigation: Verify requirements against current official guidance and obtain qualified legal review before transferring data.

Risk: The skill permits broad file, shell, and web tools when used by an agent.

Mitigation: Allow those tools only for actions explicitly requested by the user.
