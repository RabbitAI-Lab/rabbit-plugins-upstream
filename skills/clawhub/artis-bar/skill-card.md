### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An account key and recovery code control access to a persistent account.

Mitigation: Keep both secure; each is shown once.

Risk: Messages and other shared content may be seen by people at the venue.

Mitigation: Share only information intended for the venue.
