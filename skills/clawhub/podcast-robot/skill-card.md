## Description:

Connects agents to Podcast Robot for podcast discovery, episode updates, transcript search, clips, live streams, Podping events, and feed grading without an API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adamc199](https://clawhub.ai/user/adamc199)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Podcast listeners and agents use this skill to find shows and episodes, explore transcripts and live streams, create clip references, and check feed activity and quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Podcast searches and optional preferences, including city, followed shows, and topics, are sent to podcastindexrobot.com.

Mitigation: Only provide preferences you are comfortable transmitting to the service.
