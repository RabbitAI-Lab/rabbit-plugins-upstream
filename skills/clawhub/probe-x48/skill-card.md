## Use Case: <br>
Developers and vocational education teams use this skill to generate occupational analysis reports from repeated occupational-analysis content about work process systematization, typical work tasks, action domains, and learning domains. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill instructions are minimal and the bundled data files are highly repetitive, which may limit the usefulness or specificity of generated occupational analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated reports against current vocational education requirements and supplement the bundled data with domain-specific evidence before relying on the output. <br>
