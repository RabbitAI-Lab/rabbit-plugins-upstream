## Description: <br>
Provides Chinese-language analysis of complex English reading sentences, including segmentation, sentence skeletons, modifiers, logic cues, and translation guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zwli123](https://clawhub.ai/user/zwli123) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students and English learners use this skill to break down complex English reading sentences for exam-oriented comprehension and translation practice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generic Chinese study phrases such as reading comprehension or sentence analysis may trigger the skill outside a specific English reading-analysis request. <br>
Mitigation: Enable or invoke the skill only where English sentence analysis is appropriate, and confirm the user intent when the request is ambiguous. <br>
Risk: The skill is likely to answer in Chinese unless the user or platform sets a different language expectation. <br>
Mitigation: Set the desired response language explicitly when Chinese-language educational guidance is not suitable. <br>
