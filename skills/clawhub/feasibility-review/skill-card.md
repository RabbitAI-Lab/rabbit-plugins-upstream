## Description:

Reviews uploaded feasibility study reports and optional project guidelines, combining format checks, technical-route and innovation extraction, patent and paper analysis, policy review, and structured project-review reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Science and technology managers, reviewers, and project-evaluation teams use this skill to assess feasibility study submissions, compare reports with project guidelines, and generate structured review outputs that support funding or project-initiation decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may send extracted feasibility-report content to PatSnap/MCP and web-search services.

Mitigation: Use only materials approved for third-party processing; avoid confidential or unpublished project materials unless organizational policy allows that processing.

Risk: Generated project-review conclusions may be incomplete or misleading if patent, paper, or policy search results are incomplete or mismatched.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer validate the retrieved sources, cited links, and final recommendations before relying on the report for project decisions.
