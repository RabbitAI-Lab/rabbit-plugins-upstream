## Description: <br>
Structure vague opportunities and unclear briefs into actionable one-page problem statements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and product or business teams use this skill to turn vague briefs into scoped problem statements with reframed questions, decision ownership, boundaries, and a minimum viable research plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broadly worded ambiguous planning requests where a more specific research, execution, legal, financial, or technical skill would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Use this skill for clarification and planning; switch to a domain-specific skill when the task clearly requires specialist analysis or implementation. <br>
Risk: Structured problem briefs can still contain incorrect assumptions, unclear ownership, or misleading research priorities. <br>
Mitigation: Review the reframed questions, scope boundaries, decision owner, and minimum viable research plan with the relevant stakeholder before acting on the output. <br>
