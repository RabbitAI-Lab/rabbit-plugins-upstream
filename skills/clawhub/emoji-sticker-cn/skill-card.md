## Description:

Designs three emoji sticker concepts from a user-provided theme, phrase, or character when the user explicitly asks to design emoji stickers or a sticker pack.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to turn a supplied theme, phrase, or character into three written emoji sticker concepts with captions, visual direction, and copyable generation prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may treat the generated sticker prompts as finished assets or publication-ready output.

Mitigation: Present the outputs as draft text concepts and prompts for later use, not as generated files or published stickers.

Risk: Requests involving copyrighted subjects, brand marks, or identifiable public figures may create licensing or authorization concerns.

Mitigation: Avoid introducing those subjects unless the user supplied them, and do not claim authorization or licensing rights.

Risk: The skill could be over-invoked for general emoji, translation, mascot, or publishing requests outside its scope.

Mitigation: Invoke it only for explicit sticker concept or sticker pack design requests that can be satisfied with written concepts.
