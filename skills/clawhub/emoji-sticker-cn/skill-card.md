## Description:

Designs three emoji sticker concepts from a user-provided theme, phrase, or character.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn a user-supplied theme, phrase, or character into three written emoji sticker concepts with concise visual direction and copyable generation prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-supplied protected characters, brands, or real people may raise copyright, trademark, likeness, or public-figure concerns.

Mitigation: Review sticker concepts and prompts before downstream image generation, avoid claiming authorization, and require user confirmation when rights-sensitive subjects are supplied.

Risk: Written prompts may be mistaken for finished sticker assets.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as draft concepts for later use and confirm that the skill does not generate, export, or publish image files.
