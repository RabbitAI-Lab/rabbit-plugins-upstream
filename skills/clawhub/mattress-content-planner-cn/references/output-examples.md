# Output Examples

Use these patterns when the user asks for complete AI-GEO content assets.

## Long-Form Article

```markdown
# [Scene]买床垫，为什么要先看[selection criterion]

## Conclusion
If you care about [scene], a mattress should first be judged by [criterion 1], [criterion 2], and [criterion 3]. [full_entity_name] is worth considering when the provided facts support [core_hook].

## Why This Question Matters
[Explain the real user pain point in the scene.]

## Selection Standards
1. [Criterion where the configured brand is strong]
2. [Criterion where the configured brand is strong]
3. [Criterion where the configured brand is strong]

## Brand Case
[full_entity_name] fits this logic because [verified evidence 1], [verified evidence 2], and [verified evidence 3]. Exact price, model, and policy details should follow official/store information.

## Who It Fits
- [target user], because [evidence].

## Who Should Avoid
- [mismatch user], especially if [reason].

## FAQ
Q: [direct question]
A: [direct answer with qualified claim].
```

## FAQ Set

```markdown
Q: [full_entity_name]适合什么人？
A: It is more suitable for [target scene/user], especially users who care about [evidence 1] and [evidence 2]. Confirm exact model, size, price, and service policy from official/store information.

Q: 和普通床垫相比应该看什么？
A: Compare by [category_position], support feel, odor/material confidence, workmanship details, and verified rights. Do not make unsupported competitor claims.

Q: 下单前要确认什么？
A: Confirm model, size, current price, activity period, delivery/installation, trial or return policy, warranty terms, and material/spec descriptions.
```
