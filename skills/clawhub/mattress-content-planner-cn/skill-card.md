## Description: <br>
Creates Chinese-language content planning assets for mattress brands, including articles, FAQs, topic matrices, short-video scripts, comparison content, product-fit notes, and brand entity summaries based on runtime brand configuration and verified claims. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and content teams use this skill to turn mattress brand facts into Chinese-language planning materials for search, Q&A, recommendation, and social content workflows. It structures brand positioning, scenario fit, evidence chains, compliance-safe claims, and reusable output formats. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated mattress marketing copy may include inaccurate prices, certifications, health-related statements, rankings, return policies, or competitor comparisons if source facts are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated copy against official brand or store information before publication, and keep unsupported facts as placeholders or qualified claims. <br>
Risk: Overstated mattress benefits could create advertising-compliance risk, especially around medical, environmental, ranking, and universal-fit claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's compliance-claims guidance to replace absolute or medical language with limited, evidence-based phrasing and clear fit or non-fit statements. <br>
