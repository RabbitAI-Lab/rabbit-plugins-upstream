## Description:

Universal Beijing Resort trip-planning assistant that helps visitors check live wait times and schedules, plan routes, choose dining, and estimate ticket and Express Pass prices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[travel-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/travel-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External theme-park visitors and travel planners use this skill to plan a Universal Beijing Resort visit, including ride sequencing, show timing, dining choices, ticket-cost estimates, and live wait-time checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live wait-time and schedule data can lag or differ from park operations.

Mitigation: Confirm time-sensitive plans against the official Universal Beijing Resort app or on-site information before acting.

Risk: The skill does not buy tickets, make reservations, or provide hotel booking information.

Mitigation: Use official booking channels for purchases and reservations, and treat this skill as planning guidance only.
