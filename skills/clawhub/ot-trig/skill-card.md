## Description:

Counts policy files and manifest entries for a driving-policy bundle; it does not validate policy formatting, completeness, or manifest coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[anonymous9n23](https://clawhub.ai/user/anonymous9n23)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers can use the skill to get a quick inventory of policy files and manifest entries in a driving-policy bundle. Verify policy formatting, completeness, and manifest coverage separately before relying on the bundle.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The inventory counts may be mistaken for proof that policies are well formed, complete, or fully referenced by the manifest.

Mitigation: Independently verify policy formatting, completeness, and manifest coverage before relying on the bundle.
