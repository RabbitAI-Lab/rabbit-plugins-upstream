# Turn Your App Idea Into a Build Ready Plan, in Plain Words

**Upload your idea. Answer a few questions. Get a full PRD to build your app.**

Got an app idea in your head, but no tech words, no developer, and no idea where to start? This skill turns that idea into a clear, screen by screen PRD, a Product Requirements Document, that any builder, or any AI builder, can follow without guessing, and without rebuilding it later.

**For non technical founders:** no tech words needed. Answer simple questions in plain words, and get a plan you can hand to a developer and say, build this, exactly this.

**For developers and technical users:** a structured PRD, with product summary, user roles and permissions, goals and non goals, screen by screen specs, business rules and logic, data and content, success criteria, scope, and a missing screens check, ready to brief Claude, Cursor, or any AI builder, or a human developer.

Most apps fail before they are built, not because the idea is bad, but because the plan is missing. A screen is forgotten. A rule is unclear. The builder guesses, builds the wrong thing, and you pay twice to fix it. This skill stops that.

## What you get

In one conversation, your AI agent:
- Asks you simple questions, one at a time, in plain words. No tech jargon.
- Writes your full app plan, screen by screen: what each screen shows, what the user can do, and what happens next.
- Writes the business rules clearly: prices, limits, approvals, who can see what.
- Checks for missing screens before you build, so nothing important is left out.
- Gives you a draft to approve, change, or reject. Nothing is final until you say yes.

You end with a plan you can hand to a developer, a freelancer, or your AI builder, and say: build this, exactly this.

## Why this saves you money

A clear plan saves you the most expensive part of building an app: rebuilding it.

Without a plan, a builder guesses. With this plan, a builder follows. You get fewer changes, fewer surprises, and a faster first version, because the screens, rules, and scope are written down before the first line is built.

This skill writes the plan. It does not build the app itself. You use the plan to brief a builder, or your AI builder, to make the app.

PRD means Product Requirements Document. In simple words, it is the full written plan for a product, before anyone builds it. It says what the product is, who it is for, what it must do, what it must not do, and what each screen must show and do.

## What it does

First, it asks the owner questions, one at a time:
1. What is the product, in one short sentence?
2. Who is it for?
3. What problem does it solve?
4. What must it do, in order of importance?
5. What must it NOT do?
6. How will the user sign in, if at all?
7. What are the main screens, in order?
8. What happens on each screen?
9. What are the business rules?
10. What does success look like after version 1?
11. What is out of scope for version 1?

Then, it writes the full PRD draft for owner review:
- Product summary
- Users, what each user can and cannot do
- Goals and non goals
- Screens, screen by screen, what the user sees, what the user can do, and what happens after each action
- Business rules, with when each rule applies and what happens if it is broken
- Data and content needed
- Success check
- Out of scope for later

Then, it runs a missing screens check, and gives the owner a short report:
- Complete: screens fully described
- Missing: screens needed but not in the draft
- Not confirmed: screens or rules that need the owner's answer

Nothing is built from the PRD, and nothing is published live automatically. Every draft is marked DRAFT, NOT LIVE, and needs owner approval first.

## What it does not promise

It does not guarantee that the product will succeed, be used, or make money. It makes a clear plan, so a builder does not start building and then realise a screen, a rule, or a user was missed.

It never invents a product, user, screen, feature, price, rule, or fact. Unconfirmed facts are marked "Needs owner confirmation" and left blank in the draft.

## Who can use it

This skill works with any AI agent that can read a SKILL.md file, including Claude, Copilot, Cursor, Gemini, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Hermes, OpenClaw, Perplexity, and other AI tools.

How you use it depends on your tool:
- Coding agents: save the skill in your skills folder, then ask your agent to use it.
- Chat tools: paste the SKILL.md file into chat, then ask your agent to use it.
- Other agent tools: put the file where your agent reads skills from, then ask it to use it.

You can get this skill by downloading this repo, downloading the zip, copy-pasting the SKILL.md file, or uploading the file to your AI tool. Uploading a screenshot of the skill is a fallback only, not the main way, because text in a screenshot can be cut off or misread.
