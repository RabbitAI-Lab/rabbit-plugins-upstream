## Description:

Turn your app idea into a build ready PRD, in plain words. Upload your idea, answer a few questions, and get a full Product Requirements Document, screen by screen, with business rules and logic, user roles, data, scope, and a missing screens check, for any AI agent. Use when a user wants a written plan before building an app or website, with no tech words and no guessing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, small business owners, freelancers, and developers can use this skill to turn an app or website idea into a clear product requirements draft before development. It asks for confirmed details, writes a screen-by-screen plan, and checks for missing screens for the owner to review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Answers and business rules are recorded in a draft file, which may contain product or business information.

Mitigation: Choose information appropriate for the AI tool, then review the draft before using it to guide a build.
