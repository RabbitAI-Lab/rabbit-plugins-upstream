## Description:

Make, approve, schedule and measure short-form Posts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) for the founder's Product with Topped, through the topped CLI or Topped's MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Indie founders use this agent skill to draft, edit, schedule, publish, and measure short-form social posts for their product across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube using Topped.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected social accounts can be used to publish or manage posts.

Mitigation: Connect only accounts the founder intends the agent to manage, and review drafts and publishing choices before approval.

Risk: Selected media and product information are sent to Topped, and some deletions cannot be undone.

Mitigation: Review what is shared and approve costs explicitly; revoke unused sign-ins.
