## Description:

Make, approve, schedule and measure short-form Posts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) for the founder's Product with Topped, through the topped CLI or Topped's MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders and their AI agents use Topped to create, edit, approve, schedule, publish, and measure short-form social posts for a Product across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube through the CLI or MCP server.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Demo recordings may contain private information before upload.

Mitigation: Preview demo recordings locally and remove private information before upload.

Risk: Posting can have visibility, cost, and deletion consequences, and approval happens after a clip reaches Topped.

Mitigation: Connect only intended accounts; review posting visibility, costs, and deletion consequences, and check demo recordings before upload.

Risk: Additional sign-ins can leave older app connections active until revoked.

Mitigation: Revoke sign-ins that are no longer used.
