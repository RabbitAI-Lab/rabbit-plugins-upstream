## Description:

Research public TikTok creators using Crawlora profile, post, video, comment, and user-search data to build evidence-backed shortlists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creator researchers and marketing teams use this skill to identify and compare public TikTok creators against campaign criteria, with dated profile and content evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A Crawlora API key is required to fetch public TikTok data.

Mitigation: Keep the key in CRAWLORA_API_KEY and install only if comfortable using Crawlora for this research.

Risk: Sensitive search terms may be sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Avoid private or sensitive information in search terms.

Risk: Sampled profiles and comments do not establish audience demographics or authenticity.

Mitigation: Present findings as public research signals, with sample limits and unknowns clearly labeled.
