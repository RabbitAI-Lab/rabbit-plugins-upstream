## Description: <br>
Evaluate whether an early-stage AI startup is worth joining as a technical contributor. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[orangon](https://clawhub.ai/user/orangon) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and technical contributors use this skill to evaluate AI startup offers or invitations, identify red flags and green flags, and produce a join, conditional-join, or do-not-join recommendation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may present opinionated career advice as a structured assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Treat its output as advisory and compare recommendations against concrete evidence such as customer access, domain expertise, funding runway, mentorship quality, and distribution channels. <br>
Risk: The school-prestige heuristic can over-weight educational pedigree. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer direct evidence of execution ability, relevant networks, customer access, and documented mentoring quality. <br>
