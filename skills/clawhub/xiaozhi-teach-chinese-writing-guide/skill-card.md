## Description:

为语文教师提供写作任务设计、过程引导、批改标准与讲评方案，并支持班级写作观察记录。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

语文教师可用此技能设计写作任务、引导构思与修改、制定分层批改和讲评方案，并观察班级写作表现；它不代写学生作文，也不代教师逐篇精批。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information may be exposed through saved records or sharing without appropriate authorization.

Mitigation: Use student aliases and authorized material, check platform storage and access controls before enabling memory or sharing, and honor requests not to read, save, or hand off records.

Risk: Suggested grades or saved record changes may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Keep grading and proposed record changes under teacher review before sharing or saving.
