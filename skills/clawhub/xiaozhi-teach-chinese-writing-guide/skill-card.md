## Description:

为小学高年级和初中语文老师提供写作任务设计、批改标准和讲评方案，并辅助记录班级写作进展。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

语文老师用它设计贴近学生生活的作文任务、制定分级批改与讲评方案，并跟踪班级写作表现。教师审定面向学生或家长的内容，并负责最终评分；此技能不代写作文或逐篇精批。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; crisis contacts and legal assumptions must be localized outside mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students' writing and class records may expose minors' personal information if stored or shared without permission.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent, deletion, export, and parent-sharing controls before use; anonymize examples and check sharing permissions before any transfer.

Risk: A student's writing may disclose self-harm, bullying, or unsafe home circumstances.

Mitigation: Pause teaching feedback, follow the crisis referral protocol, involve a trusted adult, and use locally appropriate emergency contacts.

Risk: AI-generated grading suggestions or writing tasks may be unsuitable or inaccurate for a class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review tasks and feedback, approve materials before classroom use, and make the final grading decision.
