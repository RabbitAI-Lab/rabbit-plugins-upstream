## Description:

为语文教师提供写作任务设计、作文分档标准、修改引导和讲评方案，并保留教师对学生评价的最终决定权。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

语文教师用它规划中国中小学写作教学、拟定分档与诊断标准，并准备讲评和修改指导；教师审核输出并完成最终批改。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student records or writing may contain identifiable or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, follow school authorization and storage rules, and verify consent and teacher approval before retaining or sharing records.

Risk: Unreleased exam materials may be exposed through prompts.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam questions, answers, or scoring criteria; use general task-design guidance instead.

Risk: Writing may disclose an immediate student safety concern.

Mitigation: Stop routine grading, follow the crisis-referral procedure, and connect the student with an appropriate trusted adult or local emergency support.
