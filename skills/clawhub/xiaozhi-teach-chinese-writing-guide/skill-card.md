## Use Case:

语文教师使用本技能设计中小学写作任务、引导构思和修改，并准备批改标准及讲评方案；作文的最终评价和面向学生的内容由教师审核。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；在其他地区使用前须本地化隐私规则与危机求助渠道。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing records may reveal sensitive information or identifying details.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, minimize stored essay text, honor consent and deletion requests, and check sharing permissions before handoff.

Risk: A student's writing may disclose imminent harm or abuse.

Mitigation: Stop routine grading, follow the crisis referral protocol, and provide locally appropriate human support and emergency contacts.

Risk: Teaching criteria or generated feedback may be applied without appropriate review.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review tasks, feedback, local scoring rules, and all student-facing material; leave final grades to the teacher.
