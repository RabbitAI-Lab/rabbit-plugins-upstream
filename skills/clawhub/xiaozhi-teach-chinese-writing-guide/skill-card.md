## Description:

为语文教师提供写作任务设计、过程引导、作文批改标准和讲评方案，并由教师负责最终评价。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

语文教师可用它设计小学高段至高中写作任务、制定批改标准、规划课堂讲评和记录写作成长；最终批改、评分及对学生或家长的反馈仍由教师审核。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing and long-term style records may expose personal information.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, retention and access controls before use; anonymize classroom examples and check permissions before sharing with parents.

Risk: Crisis-response contacts and procedures may not fit every location.

Mitigation: Localize crisis guidance and referral contacts before deployment.

Risk: Draft feedback or grading advice may be mistaken for a teacher's final assessment.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review all student-facing material and make final grading decisions.
