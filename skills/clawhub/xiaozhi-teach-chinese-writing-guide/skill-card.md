## Description:

为语文老师提供写作任务设计、批改标准、讲评方案和写作能力记录建议，不代替教师评定或学生写作。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

语文教师使用此技能设计中小学写作任务、拟定批改维度与讲评活动，并记录学生写作发展；最终评价及对学生或家长的反馈须由教师审核。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing and records may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, omit unnecessary details, and confirm consent before storing records, sharing with parents, or updating student profiles.

Risk: Confidential exam questions may be exposed through teaching prompts.

Mitigation: Do not enter unreleased exam materials; use original practice prompts and let teachers review any generated tasks.

Risk: Curriculum and emergency guidance may not fit every location.

Mitigation: Localize teaching standards, privacy practices, and emergency contacts before using the skill outside its intended Chinese K–12 context.
