## Use Case:

Chinese-language teachers use this skill to design writing tasks, guide drafting and revision, prepare grading criteria and lesson reviews, and track class writing development. Its proposals are for teacher review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing and class records may contain identifying details; sharing or saving them without safeguards could expose private information.

Mitigation: Use aliases and remove identifying details. Review proposed records and teaching materials before saving or sharing, and confirm the platform's consent and deletion controls.

Risk: Optional student-profile writeback is incomplete.

Mitigation: Keep writeback unused until the handover schema and consent records are available.
