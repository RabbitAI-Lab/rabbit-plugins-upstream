## Description:

Guides targeted edits to existing financial spreadsheets while preserving their formatting and checking accounting relationships.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Finance teams and spreadsheet users can adjust specified values in existing balance sheets and income statements while preserving workbook layout and checking related totals. Changes to financial records require appropriate authorization and supporting accounting evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect cell edits or formulas could leave financial statements inaccurate even when headline totals balance.

Mitigation: Keep the original workbook and a backup; check affected cells, formulas, and accounting relationships programmatically after each change.

Risk: Adjusted reports could misrepresent financial results in loans, tax filings, or audits.

Mitigation: Require authorized, documented accounting entries before external use; do not use unsupported adjustments to mislead others.
