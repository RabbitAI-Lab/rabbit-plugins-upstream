## Description:

Write content by interviewing the user instead of drafting - extract their material through questions, then assemble the piece from their own words. Use when the user needs to write something for an audience or mentions "ghostwriter".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tenequm](https://clawhub.ai/user/tenequm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People writing content for an audience can use the skill to develop a draft through a focused interview and assemble it from their own words.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Context research could use sources the user did not intend to approve.

Mitigation: Ask whether context research is wanted and clarify permitted sources before researching.

Risk: A user's interview answer may contain credentials or private URLs.

Mitigation: Do not repeat or include secrets in the draft; ask the user to provide a placeholder.
