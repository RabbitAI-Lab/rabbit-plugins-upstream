## Description:

Write content by interviewing the user instead of drafting - extract their material through questions, then assemble the piece from their own words.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tenequm](https://clawhub.ai/user/tenequm)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache 2.0

## Use Case:

Writers, employees, and other external users use this skill to turn their own interview answers into audience-ready content without having the agent invent missing lines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for detailed personal or sensitive context and may optionally research public information about the topic.

Mitigation: Only share details intended for the final piece, and approve any external context research before it begins.

Risk: The final piece could misrepresent the user if missing details are invented or answers are over-edited.

Mitigation: Review the assembled text and ask for follow-up questions where important details are missing.
