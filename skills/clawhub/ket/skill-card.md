## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Applications built from this guidance may collect learner data or speech recordings. <br>
Mitigation: Disclose collected learner data and recordings, obtain consent before speech recording or AI evaluation, minimize retention, and provide review and deletion controls. <br>
Risk: Generated exercises, speaking feedback, or AI evaluations may give learners incorrect or misleading guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review learning content and scoring behavior before deployment, and provide learner-facing correction or review paths. <br>
