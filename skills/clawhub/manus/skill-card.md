## Description: <br>
Create and manage AI agent tasks via Manus API. Manus 1.5 autonomously browses the web, uses tools, and delivers complete work products. Cost-efficient Manus-1.5-Lite available. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mvanhorn](https://clawhub.ai/user/mvanhorn) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent users use Manus to delegate complex research, automation, and file-producing tasks to the Manus API, then poll task status and retrieve generated deliverables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, task details, generated results, downloaded files, and shareable links may expose sensitive content to an external Manus service. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with data approved for Manus, avoid secrets, regulated data, and proprietary content unless approved, and disable or avoid shareable links for sensitive tasks. <br>
