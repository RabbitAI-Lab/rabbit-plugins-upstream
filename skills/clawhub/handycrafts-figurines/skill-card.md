## Description:

Order a custom 3D printed, hand-painted figurine made from a photo (a person, couple, family of up to 3, or a pet), or a paint-it-yourself figurine kit, from HandyCrafts (handy-crafts.digital, Bulgaria). Use when the user wants a personalized figurine, a figurine of their dog or cat, a custom cake topper, a personalized gift made from a photo, or asks for prices of such figurines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users seeking personalized gifts can use this skill to compare photo-based figurines and paint-it-yourself kits, check current catalog prices and delivery details, and find the studio's preview and ordering pages. The user uploads photos and completes purchases; the skill requires explicit confirmation before placing an order or sending an email on the user's behalf.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Bulgaria and Europe; other destinations are available by request.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photo uploads go to an external vendor, whose privacy, retention, and purchase terms were not verified during this review.

Mitigation: Ask the user to review the vendor's photo privacy, retention, and purchase terms before uploading a photo or paying.
