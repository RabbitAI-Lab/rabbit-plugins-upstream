## Description:

Develops Chinese-language short-drama and motion-comic ideas or source material into creative briefs, story engines, adaptation plans, and episode maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers and creative teams use this skill to develop short dramas or motion comics from original ideas, novels, or existing scripts, and to plan story structure and episode handoffs before scriptwriting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source materials and accepted project decisions may be misrepresented when adapted or summarized.

Mitigation: Review proposed briefs and episode maps against the supplied source and approve changes before downstream use.

Risk: The skill may read supplied material and create or update project-development files.

Mitigation: Provide only intended source material and review file changes; evaluate the optional companion skill separately before installing it.
