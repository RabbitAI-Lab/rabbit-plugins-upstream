## Description:

Develops Chinese-language short-drama or comic-drama ideas and source material into creative briefs, story engines, adaptation plans, and episode maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and development teams use this skill to turn Chinese-language story ideas, novels, series notes, or existing episodes into a coherent dramatic direction, story engine, and episode plan while preserving creator-approved adaptation boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Processing source drafts and writing development files may expose private material or change existing project outputs.

Mitigation: Review input materials and output paths before use, especially helper-script --out and --checkpoint destinations.
