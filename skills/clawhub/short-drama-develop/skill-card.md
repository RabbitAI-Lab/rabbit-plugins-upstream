## Description:

Develops Chinese short-drama and comic-drama ideas or source material into adaptation plans, story engines, creative briefs, director statements, and episode maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chinese-language writers and creative teams use this skill to develop original short-drama ideas or adapt novels into structured story plans and episode maps while retaining control over creative choices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplied manuscripts or project materials may contain sensitive content.

Mitigation: Limit access to the chosen manuscripts and project folder.

Risk: Unreviewed proposals may change intended story facts or creative boundaries.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes before accepting them.
