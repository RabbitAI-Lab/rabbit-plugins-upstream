## Description:

Help users send slow, personal notes with Carrier Pidge — a browser messaging product where a digital carrier pigeon flies the distance with a route and ETA instead of delivering instantly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can get help planning a slow, personal note, drafting its wording, and preparing an optional invitation to a friend. The skill guides users to Carrier Pidge but does not send messages or create accounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A drafted note could include sensitive information or a precise home location.

Mitigation: Use city or region only when describing distance, and avoid secrets the user would not want delayed or shared.
