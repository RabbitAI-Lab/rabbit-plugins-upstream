## Description:

Connect to Brains Behind, the shared brain of a company or a person (who they are, goals, tone, rules, notes, people and every conversation with them, meetings), over MCP. Keep what you learn there (remember), look things up before you answer (recall), take the whole brain (export_brain). Use when the user asks to connect Brains Behind, to save something to their brain, or to look up a person, a company fact or something agreed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to connect an agent to a Brains Behind project, save user-approved information, retrieve context about people or companies, and export the shared brain.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad person or company lookups may cause unrelated conversation details to be sent to or stored in a shared project.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination project and sharing scope before connecting, and grant only the access needed; consider read-only access when saving information is not needed.
