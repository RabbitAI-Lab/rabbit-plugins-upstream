## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses a user-provided AI-HIVE API key stored locally or supplied through the environment.

Mitigation: Store keys outside public artifacts, restrict local config file permissions, and rotate or revoke keys when access changes.

Risk: Selected images, videos, or audio may be uploaded to AI-HIVE for generation workflows.

Mitigation: Use only media the operator is authorized to upload and avoid sensitive or third-party materials without permission.

Risk: Generation commands may create paid AI-HIVE jobs that consume account credits.

Mitigation: Review prompts, model choices, routing mode, and pricing snapshots before submitting batch or high-cost tasks.

Risk: Generated commercial media can contain factual, brand, rights, likeness, or platform-safety issues.

Mitigation: Confirm product and brand facts with the user, verify rights for likenesses and reference materials, and review final media before publication.
