## Description: <br>
漫剧编剧分镜 helps an agent turn stories into standardized 9:16 vertical comic-drama storyboard scripts and Markdown shot tables. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[samwang-001](https://clawhub.ai/user/samwang-001) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, storyboard artists, and production teams use this skill to break a vertical comic-drama story into shot-by-shot scripts with timing, framing, camera movement, dialogue, sound, and drawing keywords. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad screenwriting trigger terms may activate this storyboard format when a different writing style is intended. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke a different skill explicitly when this vertical comic storyboard format is not desired. <br>
