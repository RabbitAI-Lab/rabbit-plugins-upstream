## Use Case:

External users who are planning a home renovation can use the skill to plan hallway storage, lighting, ceilings, beams, focal points, and flooring, and get practical suggestions tailored to their layout and measurements.

Global (Chinese-language guidance).

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Examples that suggest removing or altering walls could be mistaken as safe for a reader's home.

Mitigation: Have a qualified professional assess structural safety before acting on any wall-removal or wall-alteration suggestion.
