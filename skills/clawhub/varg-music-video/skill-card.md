## Description:

Guides an end-to-end AI music video workflow, from song timing and storyboarding through generated footage, lip sync, editing, review, and platform-specific mastering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and production teams use this skill to plan and produce song-driven videos, from measuring beats and approving storyboards to generating, editing, reviewing, and mastering footage for each platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audio or reference media may be sent to external services, and a hosted review page could expose project material.

Mitigation: Use media you are authorized to share, review provider privacy terms, and check access settings before sharing hosted review links.

Risk: The security guidance flags an unpinned installer.

Mitigation: Check the installer source and review or pin its version before running it.

Risk: The security assessment is partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references and repeat the security assessment before deployment.
