## Description:

Guides creators through planning and producing beat-synced AI music videos, from song timing and storyboards to generated shots, lip-sync, graphics, review, and mastering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Music creators and their production teams use this skill to plan and produce AI-assisted music videos or song-driven promos. It guides timing analysis, concept and storyboard development, shot generation, lip-sync, lyric graphics, review, and platform mastering.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The online review page may not clearly protect unfinished work and comments.

Mitigation: Require approval before external sharing, restrict authenticated access to the project, and remove hosted access when review ends.

Risk: Audio, lyrics, and reference materials may be processed by the named services.

Mitigation: Confirm the materials may be processed by those services before use.

Risk: Video generation can consume credits across pilots, revisions, and production batches.

Mitigation: Agree on a credit ceiling before generation and keep each batch within it.
