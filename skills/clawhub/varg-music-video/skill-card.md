## Description:

Directs an AI music video or song-driven promo from song timing and storyboarding through video generation, lyric graphics, review, and platform mastering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and their agents use this skill to plan and produce song-driven music videos, lyric videos with generated footage, and music promos, including timed edits, character references, generated shots, graphics, and platform-ready exports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vocals, lyrics, images, or footage may be sent to external services, and remote review may expose drafts or comments.

Mitigation: Check the services and sharing settings before uploading media or using remote review.

Risk: The documented installation command is unpinned and may retrieve changed package content.

Mitigation: Review the package and skill content obtained by the installation command before use.
