## Description:

Directs an AI music video or a song-driven promo end to end on varg, the way the most-watched AI music videos are made: measure the song (beat grid, word timings), pitch the concept, cast and lock character references, storyboard every cut on a downbeat, approve stills before paying for video, generate Seedance 2.5 shots, run a mandatory lip-sync pass, draw lyrics and graphics in code, run review rounds and master for each platform. Use when someone asks for a music video, a clip for my song, a video for a track, a lyric video with AI footage, a singing character, an AI artist or band video, slopcore, or a launch video cut to a song. Not for a one-off generated song or single clip (use varg-ai), shot planning and 3D camera blocking only (use varg-previz), a talking character without music (use varg-character-keynote or varg-podcast-video), or a typography-only lyric video with no generated footage (use a HyperFrames music-to-video skill).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
