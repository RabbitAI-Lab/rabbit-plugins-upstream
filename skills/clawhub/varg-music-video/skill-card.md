## Description:

Guides an agent through planning and producing an AI music video or song-driven promo, from song timing and storyboarding through generation, review, editing, and mastering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and their agents use this skill to plan and produce music videos or song-driven promos, coordinating song analysis, shot planning, AI generation, lyric graphics, review, and final delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts and review comments may be shared online without clear access protections.

Mitigation: Before using private or unreleased material, confirm which assets will reach varg and its processing providers, require authenticated project-only review access, approve external sharing, and close or expire review links afterward.

Risk: External helper scripts are used in parts of the production workflow, and their code and authentication behavior are unverified by the supplied security review.

Mitigation: Review and pin external helper scripts before running them.
