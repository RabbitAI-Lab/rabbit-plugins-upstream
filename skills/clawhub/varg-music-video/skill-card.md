## Description:

Guides end-to-end production of AI music videos, from beat-mapped storyboards and approved stills through video generation, lip-sync, code-drawn graphics, review, and platform-ready masters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Artists, music-video directors, and creators use this skill to plan and produce song-driven videos, from beat and lyric timing through shot generation, editing, review, and platform-specific mastering.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft-sharing workflows lack privacy controls, and song or reference uploads may contain private material.

Mitigation: Use private, authenticated review pages, restrict comment access, stop tunnels after review, and confirm which recordings and references may be uploaded.

Risk: Blanket refusal retries can repeat unsafe requests, and suggested external scripts have not been verified.

Mitigation: Assess the refusal reason and make compliant changes; pin and inspect external scripts before running them, and keep the stated spending ceilings.
