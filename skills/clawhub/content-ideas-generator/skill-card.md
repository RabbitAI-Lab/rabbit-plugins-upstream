## Description: <br>
Generates structured post outlines from reference materials for wisdom-style social posts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vincentchan](https://clawhub.ai/user/vincentchan) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Content creators and marketers use this skill to turn newsletters, scripts, notes, URLs, or raw ideas into five structured wisdom-style social post outlines. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided URLs or source materials may contain private or confidential content. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide URLs and materials the agent is allowed to retrieve, analyze, and store locally. <br>
Risk: Generated outlines may use strong, absolute language that is unsuitable for publication without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review each outline for accuracy, tone, source fidelity, and brand fit before publishing or expanding it into a full post. <br>
