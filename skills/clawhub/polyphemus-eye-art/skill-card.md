## Description:

Guides an agent to create artist-guided images, small icons, webpage-matched visuals, reference edits, and editable SVG with Eye.Art's Polyphemus service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to guide an agent in generating artwork, icons, site visuals, reference-based edits, and SVG illustrations through Eye.Art.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, page references, and uploaded images may be sent to Eye.Art.

Mitigation: Only share private images, sensitive page contents, or confidential design material with the user's informed approval.

Risk: Service limits or failures can prevent an image from being generated.

Mitigation: Respect service limits and report queued or failed requests accurately; do not present a still image as an animation.
