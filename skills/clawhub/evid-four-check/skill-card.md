## Description:

Checks user-provided electronic-evidence attributes against a four-property checklist and returns Chinese-language decision support as JSON.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0 (release metadata); documentation and theory: all rights reserved

## Use Case:

People reviewing electronic evidence in a PRC-law context can use this checklist to identify missing support for authenticity, legality, relevance, or integrity before human legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (PRC legal context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A checklist result may be mistaken for a legal finding or relied upon outside its PRC-law context.

Mitigation: Treat the result as decision support, and verify conclusions against current official rules or with a qualified professional.
