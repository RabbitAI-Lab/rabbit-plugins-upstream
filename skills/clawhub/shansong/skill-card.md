## Description: <br>
Shansong is an intra-city courier guide for choosing one-to-one direct delivery, planning timing and cost expectations, and handling high-value item precautions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhangifonly](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangifonly) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Users and agents use this skill to decide when Shansong's one-to-one direct courier model is appropriate, estimate timing and pricing factors, and prepare insurance, evidence, and dispute steps for urgent or valuable same-city deliveries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Courier prices, delivery availability, insurance terms, and prohibited-item rules can change by city, time, and item type. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current details in the official Shansong app before booking or advising on a delivery. <br>
