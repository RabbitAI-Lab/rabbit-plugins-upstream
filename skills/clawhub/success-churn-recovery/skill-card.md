## Description:

Success Churn Recovery helps sales and customer success teams assess already-lost B2B customers, identify the champion and recovery pathway, plan a timed win-back effort, and decide when to stop.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and customer success teams use this skill to triage lost or non-renewing B2B customers, reconstruct account history, choose a recovery pathway, prepare win-back actions, and document lessons for similar accounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused guidance for global use where appropriate

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The playbook may involve customer records, contact details, WeChat or enterprise chat history, and other personal or commercial data.

Mitigation: Use only authorized customer data, keep outreach inside approved CRM and compliance workflows, and avoid broad circulation of phone, WeChat, or other personal contact details.

Risk: Win-back recommendations could encourage inappropriate or non-compliant outreach if applied without local sales and privacy review.

Mitigation: Review proposed actions against organizational policies, customer permissions, and applicable local privacy and outreach rules before contacting customers.
