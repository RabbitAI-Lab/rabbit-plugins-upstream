## Description: <br>
Find what actually matters in your content - the ideas that survive any rephrasing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to distill user-provided documents, notes, and methodology text into core principles, confidence levels, supporting evidence, and concise next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided confidential or regulated content may be processed by the model provider configured for the host agent. <br>
Mitigation: Use normal data-handling controls and avoid pasting sensitive documents unless the configured agent and model provider are approved for that data. <br>
Risk: Extracted principles are observations from source text, not verified facts or authoritative conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with human judgment and validate important patterns against independent sources before relying on them. <br>
