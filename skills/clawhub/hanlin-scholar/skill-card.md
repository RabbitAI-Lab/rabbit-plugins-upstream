## Description: <br>
Academic-paper polishing and rewriting skill that uses the DeepSeek API to polish Chinese and English papers, proofread grammar, and suggest paraphrases for reduced textual overlap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[shylamb-token](https://clawhub.ai/user/shylamb-token) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, researchers, editors, and developers use this skill to polish, proofread, or paraphrase Chinese and English academic text while preserving the original meaning and terminology. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided manuscript text is sent to DeepSeek for processing. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid confidential manuscripts, regulated data, student records, or proprietary research unless the organization permits that third-party processing. <br>
Risk: Generated edits may alter nuance or academic meaning if accepted without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review suggested revisions before using them in academic or publication workflows. <br>
