## Description:

根据用户提供的封面文案生成高点击率的中文竖版短视频封面。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and marketers use this skill to turn Chinese short-video cover copy into a finished vertical cover image. It is intended for short-video thumbnails where clear Chinese title hierarchy, strong visual focus, and mobile-feed readability matter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User prompts and supplied photos may be sent to an image-generation tool.

Mitigation: Use only images and cover text the user is authorized to process, and avoid submitting sensitive personal or confidential material unless the deployment environment permits it.

Risk: The skill is narrowly scoped to Chinese vertical short-video covers and may produce weaker results for unrelated image tasks.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese short-video cover generation; choose or modify another workflow for non-Chinese, non-short-video, or purely text-rewriting tasks.

Risk: Generated cover text can contain wrong characters, unwanted extra text, poor contrast, or layout problems.

Mitigation: Inspect the generated image for Chinese text accuracy, readability, cropping, and unwanted additions, then request targeted correction when needed.
