## Description:

Creates narrated podcast episodes from topics, documents, or slide decks through OnePress, with AI voice options and downloadable MP3 audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getonepress](https://clawhub.ai/user/getonepress)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to turn topics, reports, and presentations into narrated podcast episodes or audio briefings using their OnePress account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded documents, decks, scripts, and voice samples are shared with OnePress for remote processing.

Mitigation: Upload only material you are authorized to share, and review OnePress account settings for managing cloned voices.

Risk: The connected account uses an API key for podcast generation.

Mitigation: Keep the key in a secret store rather than chat or logs; revoke it in OnePress settings when no longer needed.
