## Description:

Turns a topic, document, or slide deck into a narrated audio episode through OnePress and reports where the MP3 is stored.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getonepress](https://clawhub.ai/user/getonepress)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators, business users, and developers use this skill to turn topics, documents, or slide decks into narrated podcast-style episodes in their OnePress workspace.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Podcast prompts and source documents are sent to OnePress for processing.

Mitigation: Avoid sending confidential, regulated, or personal material unless OnePress's terms and your obligations allow it.

Risk: Cloned voices could be used without the speaker's consent.

Mitigation: Use voice cloning only with the voice owner's authorization.
