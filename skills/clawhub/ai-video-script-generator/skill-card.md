## Description: <br>
Generates structured video scripts for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili with opening hooks, storyboard sections, dialogue, BGM suggestions, cover copy, hashtags, and reusable style memory. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to draft platform-specific video scripts for short-form and long-form Chinese social video workflows, including hooks, storyboard rows, dialogue, BGM notes, cover copy, hashtags, and posting-time guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated scripts and topic/style history are saved in plaintext under ~/video-scripts, which may expose confidential campaign plans, client information, or sensitive personal topics. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential or sensitive topics unless the local folder is managed appropriately, and delete or protect ~/video-scripts when drafts should not persist. <br>
