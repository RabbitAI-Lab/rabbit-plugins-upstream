## Description:

为 3-6 岁幼儿按健康、语言、社会、科学、艺术五大领域生成可打印活动卡和一周亲子活动计划。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT

## Use Case:

Parents, teachers, and caregivers use this skill to select age-appropriate kindergarten activities and produce printable activity cards with goals, materials, steps, timing, and parent safety tips. It is also useful for arranging a balanced weekly parent-child activity plan across the five kindergarten development domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generator writes an HTML file to the requested output path.

Mitigation: Choose the output path intentionally and keep generated files inside an expected workspace or download location.

Risk: Some activity plans may involve household materials, water, scissors, small items, or physical movement.

Mitigation: Use the generated parent safety tips, supervise children during activities, and adapt or skip activities that are unsuitable for the child or environment.
