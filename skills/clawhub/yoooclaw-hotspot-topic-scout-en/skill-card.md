## Description: <br>
Use when users want to quickly identify hot-topic ideas, high-performing content references, competitor activity, and audience needs from message notifications and the web. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, editors, and operations leads use this skill to turn recent mobile notification signals and current web search results into prioritized topic ideas, popular-content references, competitor updates, and fan-need summaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reads sensitive phone notifications and message-derived content for content planning. <br>
Mitigation: Enable it only for accounts, groups, applications, and time ranges the user is comfortable analyzing, and avoid private, personal, confidential team, or customer-message sources unless participants have consented. <br>
Risk: Evidence security guidance notes insufficient user control and scoping around monitored notification sources. <br>
Mitigation: Require users to explicitly define who to follow, what to focus on, and which groups or applications to monitor before collecting signals. <br>
