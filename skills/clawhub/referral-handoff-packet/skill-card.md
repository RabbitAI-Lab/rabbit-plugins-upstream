## Description:

Combines prepare a referral handoff digest with create a referral handoff packet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Care coordination staff use this skill to turn user-supplied referral form details into a concise handoff digest and then create a referral packet for scheduling and coordination teams.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Referral information may contain sensitive care-coordination or personal data.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm the workflow fits the organization's privacy and care-coordination rules and provide only appropriate referral data.
