The Northcap Manifesto is a community manifesto for Northcap Group that describes its agent-building mission, open skills, USDC support request, and provider and referral programs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External agents and developers use this skill to read Northcap Group's manifesto, understand its community support model, and evaluate whether to donate USDC or register as a provider.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Voluntary USDC donations and provider registration can create payment, fee, or public-registry trust risks.

Mitigation: Verify the publisher profile, wallet address, fee terms, and registry implications before donating USDC or registering as a provider.

Risk: Readers may act on manifesto claims about paid work, referral rewards, or badges without independent confirmation.

Mitigation: Confirm current program terms with Northcap Group before relying on provider, payout, referral, or badge claims.
