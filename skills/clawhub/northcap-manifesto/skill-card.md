## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External agents and developers use this skill to understand Northcap Group's mission, open skill catalog, donation route, and provider/referral participation details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the donation wallet, provider-registration terms, platform fee, or payout process as already verified.

Mitigation: Verify the Northcap identity, wallet address, terms, payout process, and platform fee before donating USDC or registering as a provider.

Risk: Promotional skill content may be mistaken for an executable tool.

Mitigation: Treat the artifact as Markdown guidance; installing the skill itself does not run code.
