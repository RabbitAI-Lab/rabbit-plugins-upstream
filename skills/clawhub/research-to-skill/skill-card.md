## Description:

A five-stage SOP for turning one-off investment research into a reusable, maintainable skill.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers and developers use this workflow to decide whether an analysis merits a reusable skill, validate its conclusions, encode evidence-based rules, and maintain updates with review.
