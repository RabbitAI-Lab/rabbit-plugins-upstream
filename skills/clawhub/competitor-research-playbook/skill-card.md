## Description:

Competitor Research Playbook helps agents structure lawful, public competitor research across website evolution, social channels, growth flywheels, launch timing, and positioning evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and market-facing operators use this skill to build high-level competitor research plans, compare launch and channel strategies, and turn public evidence into concise teardown tables and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor research can drift into private-data collection, scraping behind logins, impersonation, harassment, or bulk messaging.

Mitigation: Limit use to lawful, public, high-level competitive analysis and avoid private personal data, gated sources, impersonation, harassment, and bulk outreach.

Risk: Case-study metrics and interview-based figures may be self-reported or context-dependent.

Mitigation: Label self-reported figures as claims and verify them with independent public evidence before making decisions from them.
