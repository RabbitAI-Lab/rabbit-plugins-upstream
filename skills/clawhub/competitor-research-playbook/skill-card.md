## Description: <br>
This skill guides agents through systematic competitor research, including website evolution analysis, social channel teardown, growth flywheel scoring, X/Twitter propagation mapping, and KOL identification. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External teams, founders, product marketers, and growth researchers use this skill to reverse-engineer competitors from public signals, compare positioning and channels, and produce actionable market research checklists. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat competitive research guidance as permission to scrape, spam, impersonate, or contact people at scale. <br>
Mitigation: Use public sources, respect platform rules, keep outreach transparent and opt-in, and review recommendations before acting. <br>
Risk: Market research conclusions or self-reported benchmark figures may be incomplete, outdated, or misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Validate important findings against primary sources, label self-reported figures, and avoid making decisions from a single unverified data point. <br>
