## Description:

Generates evidence-based Skill portfolio audits with scorecards, privacy classifications, consolidation plans, and a persistent execution queue for Skill portfolio management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and Skill maintainers use this skill to inventory Skill portfolios, identify duplicates or stale versions, classify sharing risk, prioritize Skill candidates, and plan maintenance or publication work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit reports may expose sensitive paths, credentials, personal data, company information, or other private details found in Skill-related materials.

Mitigation: Define the audit scope before installing or running the skill, and review generated reports before sharing them.
