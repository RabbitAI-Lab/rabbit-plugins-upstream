## Description:

Conducts keyword research with intent classification, difficulty scoring, SERP gap analysis, topic clustering, and prioritized content calendar planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heroinyan-stack](https://clawhub.ai/user/heroinyan-stack)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and SEO strategists use this skill to turn seed topics into prioritized keyword clusters, SERP gap findings, and a 90-day content plan. It is intended for planning and decision support, not exact ranking, traffic, or revenue measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search volume, difficulty, traffic, and ROI outputs are rough estimates and may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Label estimates as estimates and review important content or business decisions against current external SEO data before relying on them.
