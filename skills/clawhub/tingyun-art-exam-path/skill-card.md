## Description:

Helps Chinese secondary-school families compare arts, sports, and foundation-program admission pathways and prepare timelines, cost estimates, and decision checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese secondary-school students, families, and advisors can use the skill to compare arts exam, sports exam, and foundation-program routes, then organize preparation steps, costs, and fallback decisions. Verify current admissions requirements with official notices and review the plan with a person before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Admissions deadlines, eligibility rules, and fees can change by province, institution, and year.

Mitigation: Verify details against current provincial exam authority notices and the target institution's current admissions rules before acting.

Risk: Student records and family budget details may contain identifying or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share only necessary summaries and redact identifying details from supporting documents.
