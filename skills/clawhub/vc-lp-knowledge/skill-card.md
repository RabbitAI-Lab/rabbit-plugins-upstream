VC LP 报告与知识管理专家。当用户需要生成 LP 季报要点、基金表现摘要、募资材料，或进行基于知识库的投资认知问答（带引用）时使用。This skill should be used when producing LP quarterly reports, fundraising materials, or knowledge-base Q&A with citations for a VC firm. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
VC fund teams, investor relations staff, and research operators use this skill to draft LP quarterly updates, fund performance summaries, fundraising materials, and citation-backed knowledge-base answers from authorized sources. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: LP reports, fund performance summaries, portfolio details, and internal research may contain confidential or sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Review data classification, visibility, and desensitization before sharing or publishing any generated content. <br>
Risk: Knowledge-base answers or report figures may be misleading if source material is missing, unauthorized, or unsupported. <br>
Mitigation: Use only authorized knowledge sources, require citations for Q&A, verify report figures against traceable records, and state when evidence is unavailable. <br>
Risk: Optional publishing to ima can move content outside the current conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Publish only after explicit user confirmation of the target space, visibility, and sensitive-data review. <br>
