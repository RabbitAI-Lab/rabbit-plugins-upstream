## Description: <br>
Nexl Brand Site Generator guides agents through a structured workflow for creating interactive brand sites, landing pages, and independent websites from a reusable brand profile, template catalog, budget check, and brand-consistency review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[richard702ayu](https://clawhub.ai/user/richard702ayu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, brand teams, and developers use this skill to collect brand inputs, select an aesthetic site baseline, generate a polished static brand site or landing page, and prepare deployment guidance with a public URL handoff. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may collect, store, and re-share sensitive brand profile information. <br>
Mitigation: Use a redacted or non-persistent brand profile for confidential work, verify where the profile is stored, and remove local profile files when they are no longer needed. <br>
Risk: Public deployment guidance could expose draft brand content or private business details. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated site content and deployment settings before publishing, especially pricing, unreleased product plans, private contacts, and legal constraints. <br>
Risk: Some network or deployment behavior is under-described. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the host platform's deployment path and external services before allowing the agent to publish or connect generated site assets. <br>
