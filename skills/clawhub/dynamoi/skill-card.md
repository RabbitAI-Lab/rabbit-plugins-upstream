## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: OAuth access can expose artist account data and permit paid promotional actions.

Mitigation: Review the requested OAuth permissions, confirm the intended artist, budget, and recurring charges before approving actions, and keep write-tool approvals enabled.

Risk: Promotion estimates and campaign outcomes may not match actual results.

Mitigation: Present estimates as estimates and do not guarantee streams, views, followers, placements, revenue, or distribution approval.
