## Description:

Helps medical device companies plan international expansion from a business perspective, including market selection, market-access tiering, entry modes, export trade, distribution, international business development, localization, and compliance checkpoints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External medical-device founders, international business teams, and commercial operators use this skill to compare target markets, choose entry modes, plan export and trade workflows, manage distributors, and identify compliance questions that require current official or professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad export or international-business wording may activate the skill outside its intended medical-device expansion scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the question is about medical-device international expansion before applying the skill's guidance.

Risk: Market access, trade, anti-bribery, export-control, and data-transfer requirements can change and may create legal or regulatory exposure.

Mitigation: Validate business decisions against current official sources and qualified legal, trade, or regulatory professionals.
