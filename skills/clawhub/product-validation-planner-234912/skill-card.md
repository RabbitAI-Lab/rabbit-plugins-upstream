## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and reusable validation workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert rough ideas into assumptions, validation plans, checklists, prototypes, positioning, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad product, startup, validation, prototype, and SaaS trigger wording may activate the skill for requests where a narrower skill was intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit skill invocation for this release and prefer narrower trigger wording in future versions. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs may be mistaken for proof of product demand before real customer, prototype, or positioning tests are completed. <br>
Mitigation: Require visible assumptions, success criteria, validation steps, and remaining risks before treating the plan as build-ready evidence. <br>
