## Description: <br>
Optimizes content for AI-generated answers and citation surfaces such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and AI Overviews. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Apache-2.0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, SEO, and content teams use this skill to audit or transform pages, articles, and briefs for AI citation readiness across AI answer engines. It produces answer-shaped content changes, metadata or schema guidance, GEO scoring, and a handoff summary for review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: GEO recommendations can include estimates and platform heuristics rather than guaranteed citation outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs for factual accuracy, label metrics as Measured, User-provided, Estimated, or N/A, and avoid presenting citability tests as guaranteed surfacing results. <br>
Risk: The skill can consult local entity memory and may save or promote content summaries when confirmed. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm before saving summaries, review memory updates for sensitive or stale information, and keep publisher-approved claims separate from unresolved evidence gaps. <br>
