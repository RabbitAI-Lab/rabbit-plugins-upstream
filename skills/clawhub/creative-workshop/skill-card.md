## Description: <br>
Creative Workshop coordinates five expert roles to help users turn early creative ideas into product plans covering direction, MVP scope, business model, IP strategy, interaction design, and technical specifications. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Entrepreneurs, product teams, and developers use this skill to structure a new creative product from idea selection through MVP planning, business analysis, IP planning, user experience design, and developer-ready technical specifications. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Business, IP, and technical recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's jurisdiction, market, budget, or implementation constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and have qualified business, legal, and engineering reviewers approve decisions before acting on them. <br>
Risk: The workflow can produce developer-facing specifications that may be mistaken for validated implementation requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated specifications, API definitions, data models, schedules, and acceptance criteria before assigning development work. <br>
