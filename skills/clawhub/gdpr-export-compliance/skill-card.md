## Description:

A practical GDPR compliance playbook for Chinese companies expanding into Europe, covering applicability, lawful bases, data subject rights, DPIA, cross-border transfer mechanisms, breach notification, penalties, and a local rules-based toolkit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Legal, compliance, and data leaders at China-based companies use this skill to make preliminary GDPR compliance decisions for products, services, data subject requests, and data transfers involving Europe. Agents can use it to provide structured guidance, checklists, and local command examples for GDPR scoping, lawful-basis selection, rights handling, transfer review, and penalty or breach-response lookup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat GDPR reference material or rules-based helper output as legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for preliminary analysis and checklists, then confirm material decisions with qualified counsel or a DPO.

Risk: GDPR guidance, regulator practice, and case law can change after the packaged reference baseline.

Mitigation: Verify current legal sources and update the skill before relying on it for active compliance programs.

Risk: Prompts or command arguments may contain confidential personal data.

Mitigation: Minimize, anonymize, or avoid sensitive data unless the user is comfortable processing it locally in their environment.
