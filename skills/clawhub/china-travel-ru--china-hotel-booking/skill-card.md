## Description: <br>
Поиск отелей в городах Китая с рекомендациями в реальном времени, ценами и ссылками для бронирования. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External travelers and travel planners use this skill to find and compare hotel options in Chinese cities, including prices, ratings, features, and booking links. It is aimed at Russian-language hotel search workflows for destinations such as Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an, Chengdu, and Guangzhou. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Hotel and broader travel-query details are sent to the skill publisher's remote proxy service. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for non-sensitive travel searches and avoid entering personal, payment, passport, account, or other confidential information. <br>
Risk: Booking and affiliate-link behavior is publisher-controlled. <br>
Mitigation: Review booking links and prices directly on the destination booking site before making reservations or purchases. <br>
Risk: The artifact supports broader travel modes than the hotel-only listing describes. <br>
Mitigation: Limit use to hotel-search workflows unless the additional travel modes have been separately reviewed and approved. <br>
