## Description:

Krites helps frame AI capability, safety, and governance findings with evidence and standards, while keeping evaluations distinct from official certification or regulatory decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

## Use Case:

Teams preparing AI evaluation summaries, independent audit drafts, governance proposals, or compliance readiness checklists can use Krites for measured, evidence-based wording. Outputs should distinguish pre-assessment and advisory work from formal certification or regulatory decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A persona or signature could be mistaken for proof that independent testing occurred or that a system is officially certified.

Mitigation: Verify supporting evidence before publishing evaluation claims, and state clearly when the work is advisory or a non-statutory pre-assessment.

Risk: References to standards, regulations, or external resources may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Check current primary sources and verify dates and factual claims before publishing.
