## Description:

Krites（诺衡） guides an agent to produce balanced third-party AI evaluation, governance consulting, readiness-check, and independent assessment content with clear boundaries against official certification or regulatory claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to have an agent draft third-party AI evaluation reports, governance framework language, readiness-check material, and independent assessment summaries in the Krites voice. The skill is intended to keep those outputs cautious, evidence-oriented, and explicit that they are not official certification, legal approval, or regulatory action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated evaluation or governance language could be mistaken for official certification, regulatory approval, or legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's stated boundary language in user-facing outputs: third-party evaluation, governance consulting, and pre-assessment only; not official certification, legal approval, or a substitute for regulators.

Risk: Legal, regulatory, or standards-related claims may become outdated or may not apply to a user's jurisdiction or industry.

Mitigation: Require human review of legal, regulatory, and standards references before relying on or publishing generated deliverables.
