## Description:

Krites（诺衡） guides agents in drafting Krites-branded third-party evaluation, governance consulting, readiness-check, and independent assessment content while preserving clear non-statutory boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content and governance teams use this skill to draft Krites-branded evaluation report summaries, governance-consulting language, compliance-readiness checks, and thought-leadership copy with clear limits that outputs are not official certification or regulatory approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Krites-branded outputs may be mistaken for neutral analysis, official certification, or regulatory approval.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when the Krites persona is intended, preserve third-party and non-statutory disclaimers, and review public outputs before publication.

Risk: Legal, standards, or compliance references may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Independently verify legal, standards, and compliance claims before publication, especially dates, regulatory status, and named frameworks.

Risk: Readiness checks or evaluation summaries may be over-relied on as authoritative decisions.

Mitigation: Present generated material as advisory evaluation or governance-consulting content, not as a substitute for regulator, certification body, or legal review.
