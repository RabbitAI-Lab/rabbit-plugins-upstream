## Description:

Map every page on a site with its intent, keywords, performance, status and next action, so research, audits and briefs never duplicate work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO teams and agents use this skill to build and maintain a page-level inventory from a sitemap, crawl, CMS export, or URL list. The inventory helps prevent duplicate work, attach available performance data, identify content gaps, and track recommended next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
