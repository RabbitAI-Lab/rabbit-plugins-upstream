## Description:

AI deep research powered by CellCog for market, competitive, investment, academic, due diligence, literature review, financial, crypto, and news research through multi-source synthesis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and teams use this skill to investigate markets, companies, investments, academic topics, and other complex questions. It coordinates multi-source research and can return structured findings in a requested report format.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research prompts and included context are sent to CellCog.

Mitigation: Share confidential information only when authorized.

Risk: Research tasks consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Check credit costs before choosing a task tier.

Risk: Installing dependencies introduces supply-chain and version risks.

Mitigation: Review dependencies and pin versions where practical.

Risk: Research conclusions may lack citations unless requested and may be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Request citations and verify consequential conclusions against their sources.
