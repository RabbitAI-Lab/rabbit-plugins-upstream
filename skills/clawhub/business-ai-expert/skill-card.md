## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business recommendations may be too broad or may not match the user's target market.

Mitigation: Ask the user for business context, target geography, budget, timeline, technical maturity, and data availability before finalizing advice.

Risk: AI adoption advice may lead to poor investment decisions if ROI assumptions are untested.

Mitigation: Present assumptions, compare options, and recommend small-scale pilot validation with measurable KPIs before full deployment.

Risk: Business or customer data shared during consultation could raise security or compliance concerns.

Mitigation: Avoid requesting unnecessary sensitive data and address applicable privacy, security, and compliance requirements in the proposed implementation plan.
