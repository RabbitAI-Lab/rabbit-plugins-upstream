## Description: <br>
Creator collabs and cross-promotion helps agents plan organic creator-to-creator audience swaps by selecting complementary partners, drafting outreach, planning shared-value content, coordinating each side's own posts, and tracking honest per-source results. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators and social media operators use this skill to structure mutual, unpaid cross-promotions, choose aligned partners, draft outreach, plan coordinated posts, and measure results without fabricating metrics or disguising sponsored relationships. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Coordinated public posting can publish partner content or account activity without sufficient human approval. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit approval from each account owner before any post is scheduled or published. <br>
Risk: Giveaways, duet/stitch actions, IG or YouTube native collaborations, and legal disclosure decisions may be outside WoopSocial automation or current platform rules. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those actions as native or human-run unless separately verified against platform rules and applicable law. <br>
Risk: Paid or free-product arrangements can be misrepresented as mutual unpaid collaborations. <br>
Mitigation: Route paid or product-supported arrangements to sponsored-content workflows and apply required disclosures. <br>
Risk: Campaign metrics or partner fit claims can become misleading if unsupported. <br>
Mitigation: Use native per-source analytics and do not fabricate engagement, referral, follower, or subscriber metrics. <br>
