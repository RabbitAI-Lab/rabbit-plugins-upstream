## Description: <br>
Plans creator-to-creator collaborations by selecting a complementary partner, structuring the cross-promotion plan, drafting outreach and posts, and keeping native platform actions and disclosure decisions in human control. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators and social media teams use this skill to plan mutual creator collaborations, cross-promotions, shoutout swaps, joint giveaways, guest spots, and coordinated partner posts. It helps the agent choose a genuine partner fit, draft the collaboration plan and messaging, and route sponsorship or native-platform actions appropriately. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can help plan giveaway-style collaborations or platform-specific collab mechanics that may have legal, disclosure, or platform-rule requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Manually verify current platform rules, giveaway requirements, disclosures, and native collaboration mechanics before publishing. <br>
Risk: A paid or free-product arrangement could be misrepresented as a pure mutual collaboration. <br>
Mitigation: Treat payment or free product as sponsorship, apply required disclosure, and route brand-to-creator sponsorship work to the appropriate influencer or monetization workflow. <br>
Risk: The agent may draft plans for actions it cannot perform, such as native co-authored posts, duets, stitches, direct messages, giveaway administration, or agreement management. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those actions native or human-managed, and use the skill only for planning, drafting, scheduling each partner's own posts, and measurement guidance. <br>
