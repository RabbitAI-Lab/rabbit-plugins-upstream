## Description: <br>
Build a structured account plan for any key customer or target account, including a relationship map, growth opportunities, risks, and a 90-day action plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Account managers, customer success teams, sales leaders, and revenue operators use this skill to draft structured account plans for key customers or target accounts. It organizes account context into retention anchors, relationship gaps, expansion opportunities, risks, success criteria, and near-term actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Account plans can include confidential customer information such as revenue, renewal dates, contact details, opportunities, and risks. <br>
Mitigation: Use only approved agent sessions for sensitive account planning and redact confidential customer data unless the user is authorized to share it. <br>
Risk: Generated account plans may include unsupported opportunity estimates, incomplete risk analysis, or vague action items. <br>
Mitigation: Review the plan against known account data and the skill's quality checks before using it for customer strategy or internal decisions. <br>
