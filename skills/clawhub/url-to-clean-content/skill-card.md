## Description: <br>
Fetches a web page through a paid x402 endpoint and returns its main readable text or markdown without navigation, ads, or boilerplate. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[rccola990-cloud](https://clawhub.ai/user/rccola990-cloud) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and research agents use this skill to fetch public web pages and convert them into clean content for reading, summarization, research, and fact extraction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public or private URLs submitted to the service may be fetched by an external provider. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for public web pages, and avoid internal systems, private documents, authenticated pages, localhost URLs, or sensitive customer or business data unless explicitly approved. <br>
Risk: Each successful use may incur the disclosed x402 fee. <br>
Mitigation: Review the price shown in the 402 payment response before authorizing a call. <br>
