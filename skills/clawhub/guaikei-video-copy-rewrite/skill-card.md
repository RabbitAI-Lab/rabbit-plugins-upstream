## Description:

Turns local videos or supported platform links into cleaned transcripts, summaries, rewrites, quote collections, shot breakdowns, and translations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and teams can turn local videos or supported platform links into cleaned transcripts, summaries, meeting notes, rewrites, quote collections, shot breakdowns, or translations using a custom prompt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A cache-collision issue may cause an older video to be uploaded instead of the selected video.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive videos until the cache-collision issue is fixed.

Risk: Documented network restrictions are inaccurate and actual upload destinations are undisclosed; expect paid cloud processing and local cached copies.

Mitigation: Confirm upload destinations and the provider's deletion and no-training promises before sending confidential recordings.
