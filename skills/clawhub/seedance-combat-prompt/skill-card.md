## Description:

Chinese-language agent that generates 15-second Seedance 2.0 anime fight video prompts with action choreography, shot planning, visual effects, pacing, and scene structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangminrui2022](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangminrui2022)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and prompt authors use this skill to turn character, scene, style, rhythm, and outcome requirements into structured Seedance 2.0 combat-video prompts. It is intended for anime, guoman, cyberpunk, and related short-form fictional fight scenes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts can include stylized fictional fight and impact descriptions.

Mitigation: Review prompts for the intended audience and platform, and reduce violence intensity where needed before use.

Risk: Some advanced weapon reference files mentioned by the skill are absent from the submitted package.

Mitigation: Confirm needed weapon references are available, or avoid relying on absent reference sets when prompting the agent.
