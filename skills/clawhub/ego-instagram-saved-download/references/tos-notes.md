# ToS / ethics notes

- Personal archive of **your own** Instagram Saved posts only. Respect [Instagram's Terms](https://help.instagram.com/581066165581870) and the account you are signed into.
- Do not download other people's private media, sell a dataset, create accounts, or bypass checkpoints or captchas.
- The script stops when Ego is not logged into Instagram. It does not type a password and does not print cookie values.
- `--count` is capped at 100 posts. A carousel keeps at most 10 slides. Videos are capped at 200MB and only when `--media video` or `--media any`.
- Do not commit images, MP4s, `manifest.json`, or Ego profile files.
