## Description:

Produce paid ad creative sets with Canvora — Meta, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Display and banner placements in one pass, with variants for testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[canvora](https://clawhub.ai/user/canvora)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and agents use this skill to create paid campaign artwork for social, display, and banner placements, then prepare variants for testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign material is sent to Canvora and creative generation uses credits.

Mitigation: Share only campaign material you are authorized to send to Canvora and agree on a credit budget before generation.

Risk: The skill depends on a separately installed Canvora CLI and setup skill.

Mitigation: Verify the CLI and setup skill before use.
