## Description: <br>
Analyzes service business websites to identify missed lead capture, weak follow-up, chatbot gaps, and AI automation opportunities. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[subham7872](https://clawhub.ai/user/subham7872) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business owners, marketers, and agents use this skill to audit service business websites or landing pages for conversion gaps, lead-follow-up weaknesses, and practical AI automation opportunities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad business or company-name prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when the user intends a website, landing-page, or lead-follow-up audit, and ask for clarification when the prompt is ambiguous. <br>
Risk: Marketplace naming and artifact naming are inconsistent. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the requested task is a service-business website audit rather than relying on the displayed name alone. <br>
Risk: Conversion and automation recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific business. <br>
Mitigation: Treat recommendations as advisory, verify them against the actual website and business requirements, and review changes before acting on them. <br>
