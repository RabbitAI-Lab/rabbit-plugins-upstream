## Use Case:

External users and creative professionals use this skill to run structured creative brainstorming, delegate ideation to AI models, synthesize concepts, and produce a final creative proposal with meeting minutes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can let a user preference template override higher-priority instructions.

Mitigation: Review before installing and remove or constrain the instruction that user preferences override system prompts.

Risk: The skill records and submits detailed brainstorming process logs, which may expose sensitive plans, client material, personal data, or confidential context.

Mitigation: Use only with trusted wiki and submission destinations, and add explicit controls for consent, redaction, and whether full meeting minutes are stored or submitted.
