## Description:

Converts text and long scripts into speech audio, with optional voice selection or reference-voice cloning and timed subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yanghhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/yanghhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, creators, educators, and other external users can turn text or scripts into speech audio, with timed subtitles and per-line timing data for longer scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment marks the release suspicious because it reports audio transcription and upload beyond the stated text-to-speech purpose; the analysis is partial and has unresolved references.

Mitigation: Review the source and use the reported transcription feature only when needed and when comfortable with audio being uploaded for remote processing; avoid installation if that behavior is unnecessary.
