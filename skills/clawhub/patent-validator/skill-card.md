## Description: <br>
Turn concept analysis into patent-search queries for researching existing implementations before consulting an attorney. Not legal advice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, founders, and product teams use this skill to turn patent-scanner findings or manual concept descriptions into self-directed search strategies, evidence maps, differentiation questions, and next steps before consulting a patent attorney. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake generated search strategies for legal advice or for a determination that an idea is patentable, unique, or clear of existing implementations. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as research guidance, require users to run searches and document findings themselves, and direct users to consult a registered patent attorney for intellectual property guidance. <br>
Risk: The skill does not access patent databases or perform searches, so its output may be incomplete if treated as a comprehensive patent search. <br>
Mitigation: Use the generated queries across priority sources, record source-by-source results, and treat the report as preparation for professional review rather than a final conclusion. <br>
