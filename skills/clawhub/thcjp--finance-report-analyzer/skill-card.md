## Description:

Analyzes Excel and PDF financial data and generates interactive reports with trend sparklines, forecast markers, and variance coloring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and automation users can use this skill to analyze Excel or PDF financial data and produce report-ready summaries, trend visualizations, forecast annotations, and positive or negative variance coloring. It is not positioned as a substitute for human judgment in complex financial decisions.
