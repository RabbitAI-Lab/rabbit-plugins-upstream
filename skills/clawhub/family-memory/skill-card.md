## Description: <br>
Gives AI agents a way to store, recall, and search family-related details using BlueColumn persistent memory. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to help an agent remember family dates, notes, and coordination context across interactions through BlueColumn memory. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill stores sensitive family details persistently through an external service. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid storing sensitive information about children, schedules, locations, health, finances, or private family matters unless affected people understand the risk. <br>
Risk: The evidence does not show clear consent, retention, or deletion controls for saved family memories. <br>
Mitigation: Review BlueColumn controls before use and only save information that can be reviewed and deleted according to the user's requirements. <br>
