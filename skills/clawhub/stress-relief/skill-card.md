## Description: <br>
Manage stress with quick techniques, stress logging, and recovery tools. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jhillin8](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhillin8) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
People using an agent can use this skill for brief stress-relief exercises, stress-event logging, trigger review, decompression prompts, and weekly stress-pattern summaries. It is wellness support and not a substitute for professional or emergency help. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Stress logs may contain sensitive wellness details stored locally. <br>
Mitigation: Only record details you are comfortable storing locally, and review or delete local notes when your agent platform supports it. <br>
Risk: Stress-relief guidance may be mistaken for crisis, medical, or mental-health care. <br>
Mitigation: Use professional or emergency resources for crisis situations, including 988, Crisis Text Line, or local emergency services where applicable. <br>
