This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Project outputs may contain sensitive business plans, IP strategy, deployment details, or other confidential material. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated Markdown files and the zip package before sharing them outside the project team. <br>
Risk: The workflow may produce technical specifications, API designs, database schemas, prompts, and deployment plans that are not automatically validated for production readiness. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified engineering, legal, and business reviewers validate the deliverables before implementation or external use. <br>
Risk: Financial AI project outputs may require investment disclaimers according to the skill behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that any financial AI deliverable includes appropriate disclaimers before publication or client delivery. <br>
