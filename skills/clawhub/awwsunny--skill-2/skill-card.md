## Description:

Generates Chinese writing from a user-provided style description and requested content after checking that all five required input categories are present.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[awwsunny](https://clawhub.ai/user/awwsunny)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and content creators use this skill to generate Chinese articles, copy, reports, letters, or social posts in a directly described writing style. The skill first requires wording preferences, sentence habits, structure, tone, and the requested content before drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated writing may miss the intended style when required style details are incomplete or ambiguous.

Mitigation: Pause before drafting and ask the user to provide each missing category explicitly.

Risk: Longer generated documents may need review before use in external or commercial contexts.

Mitigation: Present longer outputs as files or artifacts and keep the artifact's optional post-generation explanation available for user review.
