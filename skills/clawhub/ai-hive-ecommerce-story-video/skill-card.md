## Use Case:

External sellers, e-commerce marketers, and creative teams use this skill to turn verified product facts, audience pain points, platform requirements, and brand constraints into e-commerce video production plans. It supports shot-by-shot scripts, prompts, subtitles, voice direction, AI-HIVE task records, master versions, platform variants, ad test variables, and compliance checks.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect, unsupported, or outdated product claims could produce misleading promotional content.

Mitigation: Use only user-confirmed or traceable product facts, and verify prices, certifications, performance claims, medical claims, inventory, and promotional terms before publishing.

Risk: Unauthorized product images, brand assets, reviews, likenesses, music, voices, or platform data could create rights or privacy issues.

Mitigation: Provide only assets and data that the user is allowed to use, and confirm consent and licensing for people, voices, music, reviews, and brand materials.

Risk: AI-generated media can distort product appearance, packaging, logos, proportions, or implied results.

Mitigation: Use authorized reference images when product accuracy matters, prefer image-to-image or image-to-video generation for product media, and review small candidate batches before scaling production.
