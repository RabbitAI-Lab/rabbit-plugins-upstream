## Description:

Performs a preliminary cross-border patent and design-right risk screen for an e-commerce or consumer product using product images, a product page, or a technical description.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, importers, manufacturers, product teams, and IP analysts use this skill to triage patent, utility-model, and design-right risk before manufacture, import, sale, or launch in target markets. The output supports evidence-backed review and escalation to qualified patent counsel, not legal clearance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Permitted product images and business launch details may be sent to configured PatSnap tools during screening.

Mitigation: Use only authorized, non-confidential inputs or obtain permission before sending images and launch details to the tools.

Risk: Users may mistake a preliminary patent-risk screen for legal clearance or a freedom-to-operate opinion.

Mitigation: Label outputs as AI-assisted triage and route launch, enforcement, validity, and non-infringement decisions to qualified patent counsel.

Risk: Incomplete product views, missing target-market scope, tool unavailability, or unvalidated search coverage can make no-hit or low-risk results misleading.

Mitigation: Record missing inputs and execution status, verify material candidates with evidence links and status dates, and classify unsupported no-hit results as insufficient evidence.
