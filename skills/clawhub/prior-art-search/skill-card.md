## Description:

Searches prior art and assesses novelty or patentability for technical ideas and invention disclosures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Inventors, engineers, and other external users can use the skill to search prior art and compare a technical proposal with earlier disclosures before filing or project decisions. Treat its findings as screening support, not a guarantee of patentability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to share a full API key in chat and save it for ongoing reuse without clear storage safeguards.

Mitigation: Provide credentials outside chat and control whether they are saved for reuse. Use the HTTPS endpoint, set a stopping limit for waiting, and review PatentMax's privacy policy before submitting unpublished invention details.
