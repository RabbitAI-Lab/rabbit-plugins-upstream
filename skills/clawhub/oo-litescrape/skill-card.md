## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup can execute unverified remote software, and connector actions can affect connected credentials, billing, or data-retention settings.

Mitigation: Review and approve CLI installation separately before running setup commands. Confirm the effects of billing, key-rotation, and retention changes before executing those actions.
