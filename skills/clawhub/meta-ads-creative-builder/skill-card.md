## Description:

Turns an offer brief into Meta ad copy for Facebook and Instagram placements, with the 125/40/25 character limits and policy checks applied. Use when the user asks for meta ads creative builder, facebook ad copy generator, instagram ad headline limits. For US DTC brands and in-house social buyers running Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

US DTC brands and in-house social buyers use the skill to turn an offer brief into concise Facebook and Instagram ad copy, map copy to placements, and identify claims that need supporting sources.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft copy may contain unsupported claims or policy issues; the bundled script checks text length but does not validate advertising-policy compliance.

Mitigation: Manually verify each factual claim against supplied sources and review applicable ad policies before publishing; the advertiser remains responsible for the final copy.

Risk: The separately mentioned Windows toolkit is unsigned and outside this skill's security assessment.

Mitigation: Do not treat it as part of this skill; if obtained separately, verify its published SHA-256 and run it only if you trust the publisher, without unnecessary elevation.
