## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to draft a concise go-to-market plan for a single product, especially for individual developers, small SaaS teams, and one-person companies. It structures ICP definition, positioning, channel selection, pricing, metrics, and a 90 day launch plan.

Risk: The security summary says the skill requests command or write-style authority that is broader than its marketing-strategy purpose.

Mitigation: Install and run it only with permissions needed for reading prompts and producing GTM planning output; avoid exec or broad write access unless the publisher documents exact safe commands, destinations, and confirmation steps.

Risk: The security summary says the skill advertises coding and deployment tasks that do not match its GTM planning purpose.

Mitigation: Use it only for marketing strategy, positioning, channel, pricing, and launch-plan workflows; route coding, debugging, and deployment work to a dedicated development skill.

Risk: GTM recommendations can be incomplete or misleading when based on vague product or customer input.

Mitigation: Review generated plans with current market knowledge, real customer evidence, and human business judgment before acting on pricing, channel spend, or outreach recommendations.
