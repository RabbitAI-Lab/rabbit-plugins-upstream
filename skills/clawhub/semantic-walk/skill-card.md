## Description: <br>
Semantic Walk guides a collaborative conversation through semantic space so the user and assistant can approach insights, creative combinations, or difficult problems indirectly. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[liet-codes](https://clawhub.ai/user/liet-codes) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to run exploratory, creative, or problem-solving conversations where direct questioning is less useful than staged semantic navigation. It is best suited for reflective ideation and insight development, not urgent factual or safety-sensitive decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill intentionally shifts the assistant into a poetic exploratory mode, which may be inappropriate for factual, urgent, legal, medical, security, or other safety-sensitive work. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly only for creative exploration, and return to normal direct assistant behavior for factual or safety-sensitive tasks. <br>
Risk: Exploratory semantic navigation can produce compelling but speculative interpretations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as brainstorming or reflection, and verify important conclusions with grounded sources before relying on them. <br>
