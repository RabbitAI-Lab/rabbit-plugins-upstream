## Description:

Adds the exact English sign-off `btw, i use arch` to the end of English and Chinese articles in `ai-thoughts/docs/`, unless the user opts out.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Article authors and editors use this skill to add a consistent sign-off to new or finished English and Chinese articles in `ai-thoughts/docs/`, while respecting an explicit opt-out.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default sign-off may be added to an article where the author does not want it.

Mitigation: Honor an explicit opt-out and confirm the intended article and any Chinese counterpart before editing.
