## Description:

Appends the verbatim sign-off `btw, i use arch` to articles in `ai-thoughts/docs/` and their existing Chinese counterparts unless the user opts out.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Article authors and their agents use this skill to add a consistent Arch Linux sign-off to English articles and existing Chinese counterparts in `ai-thoughts/docs/`.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default sign-off changes every applicable article, including Chinese counterparts, and remains in English.

Mitigation: Opt out explicitly when a sign-off is unwanted or Chinese content must be fully localized; review affected articles before publishing.
