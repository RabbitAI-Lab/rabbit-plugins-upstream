## Description:

Provides China-focused actuarial pricing, reserving, solvency, and IFRS 17/HKFRS 17 methodology with illustrative calculation templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese actuaries and insurance product pricing teams use it to structure indicative pricing, reserving, solvency, and IFRS 17/HKFRS 17 analysis with formulas and illustrative Python snippets. Outputs are drafts for review by a qualified actuary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some reference files named by the skill were not included in the reviewed bundle, so the security review was incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm the missing references are unnecessary or review the referenced materials before relying on them.

Risk: Illustrative actuarial assumptions and calculations may be mistaken for validated pricing or regulatory conclusions.

Mitigation: Have a qualified actuary verify assumptions, calculations, and current official requirements before real-world use or reporting.

Risk: Insurance and claims data may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use only the minimum necessary data in an authorized environment, and anonymize or aggregate personal information where possible.
