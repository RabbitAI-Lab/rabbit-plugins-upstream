## Description: <br>
Research-driven monthly social media content calendar planner. Onboards a client, researches trending topics in their niche, runs human approval loops, and delivers a fully scheduled calendar ready for content production. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, content managers, and agencies use this skill to onboard a brand, research timely content angles, run approval loops, and produce a dated monthly social media calendar for production. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Client or company planning data may be stored in Google Sheets or collaboration tools with overly broad access. <br>
Mitigation: Use private, account-restricted Google Sheets permissions and avoid anyone-with-link edit access. <br>
Risk: Client summaries could be sent to the wrong collaboration destination. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm Slack or messaging destinations before sending client planning summaries. <br>
Risk: Client policy may not permit storing planning data in Google or collaboration tools. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm client or company policy before using external spreadsheets or messaging tools for this workflow. <br>
