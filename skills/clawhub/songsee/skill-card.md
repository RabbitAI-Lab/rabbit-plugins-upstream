## Description: <br>
Generate spectrograms and feature-panel visualizations from audio with the songsee CLI. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[steipete](https://clawhub.ai/user/steipete) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, audio engineers, and creators use Songsee to ask an agent for concrete songsee CLI commands that generate spectrograms, multi-panel audio feature visualizations, time slices, and image outputs from audio files. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill depends on installing and running an external Homebrew CLI. <br>
Mitigation: Review the Homebrew formula and source package before installation when strict supply-chain controls apply. <br>
Risk: Songsee may use ffmpeg for audio formats beyond native WAV and MP3 decoding. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm ffmpeg availability and provenance before processing formats that require it. <br>
