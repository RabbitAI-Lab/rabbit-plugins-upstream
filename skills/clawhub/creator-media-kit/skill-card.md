## Description: <br>
Builds a creator sponsorship media kit, personalised brand outreach email, and defensible rate card from real creator, audience, offering, and brand inputs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, creator managers, and sponsorship teams use this skill to package audience proof, partnership offerings, outreach copy, and rate guidance for brand-deal conversations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incorrect or invented audience statistics could mislead brands and weaken sponsorship negotiations. <br>
Mitigation: Use real creator performance data when available, and leave clearly labelled placeholders for missing numbers. <br>
Risk: Rate recommendations may be misapplied if usage rights, exclusivity, whitelisting, or expected reach are not reviewed. <br>
Mitigation: Review rates against the creator's actual reach and explicitly price usage rights, exclusivity, whitelisting, bundles, and retainers. <br>
