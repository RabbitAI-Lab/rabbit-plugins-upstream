## Description: <br>
Activate when a borrower anchors on a headline or teaser mortgage rate, when presenting rate, points, and APR trade-offs, or when comparing against a competitor quote; do not activate when simply disclosing final locked terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External mortgage and lending professionals use this skill to frame borrower conversations around comparable APR, points, lock period, assumptions, and total cost when a teaser rate or competitor quote has anchored expectations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Mortgage offer framing can become compliance-sensitive if it misstates rate terms or steers borrowers toward higher-compensation terms. <br>
Mitigation: Verify use against applicable fair-lending, advertising, and loan officer compensation rules before deployment or borrower-facing use. <br>
Risk: A borrower could be misled if a quoted rate is compared without normalizing points, lock period, credit assumptions, PMI, APR, and total cost. <br>
Mitigation: Use apples-to-apples comparisons and avoid countering with any rate that cannot be delivered or locked. <br>
