## Description:

Helps loan officers compare advertised and competitor mortgage rates on equivalent points, lock terms, APR, and total cost when borrowers anchor on headline rates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Loan officers use this skill to respond to borrowers' headline mortgage-rate comparisons by normalizing points, lock periods, and quote assumptions, then comparing APR and total cost. It is not intended for simply disclosing final locked terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A rate comparison may mislead borrowers when points, lock periods, or quote assumptions differ.

Mitigation: Normalize the quote assumptions, compare APR and total cost on equal terms, verify calculations, and review borrower-facing language before use.

Risk: Rate framing could encourage an undeliverable counteroffer or inconsistent treatment of borrowers.

Mitigation: Use only rates that can be delivered, follow fair-lending and loan-officer compensation requirements, and do not steer borrowers for the originator's benefit.
