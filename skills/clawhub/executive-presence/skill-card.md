## Description: <br>
Sharpen how you show up in high-stakes rooms by giving scenario-specific coaching on concise, answer-first communication, tough questions, calm presence, and language habits to drop. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, leaders, and professionals use this skill to prepare for high-stakes meetings, executive presentations, board Q&A, difficult questions, and moments where concise senior communication matters. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide sensitive meeting, leadership, or business details while asking for coaching. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business information unless it is appropriate for the agent session. <br>
Risk: Communication coaching may be generic or poorly matched if the user's moment, audience, goal, and concern are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for the missing context before producing final advice and keep recommendations specific to the described situation. <br>
