## Description:

Helps teams create, review, schedule, and measure TikTok and Instagram posts through Kleos, including posts made from their own images and videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rhuancruz](https://clawhub.ai/user/rhuancruz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and app developers use Kleos to prepare social-media posts from generated or uploaded content, review publishing queues, manage accounts, and report on post performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing or changing accounts and team settings may affect real social-media channels.

Mitigation: Review the selected organization and project, grant only needed scopes, and require explicit confirmation before publishing, deleting, or changing account or team settings.

Risk: Generating posts in volume may consume a paid plan's quota.

Mitigation: Check remaining usage and preview bulk generation estimates before confirming the work.
