## Description:

Turns finished lecture scripts and an authorized teacher portrait into narrated, presenter-led lesson videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators and training teams use the skill to turn approved lecture scripts and a teacher portrait into ordered talking-head lesson videos with narration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A shared Beatra device token grants broad account access.

Mitigation: Authorize only in a trusted environment, keep the credential private, and revoke the connection when no longer needed.

Risk: Voice cloning and video generation can consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Confirm voice rights and each paid operation with its live estimate before submission; review actual charges afterward.

Risk: First use sends installation registration metadata to Beatra.

Mitigation: Review the registration details and privacy implications before connecting.

Risk: Default silent package updates may change the skill without separate approval.

Mitigation: Turn automatic updates off when change control matters and review updates before applying them.
