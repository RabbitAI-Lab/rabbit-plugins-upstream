## Description:

Turn finished lecture scripts into presenter-led course videos with lecture narration. This course video studio prepares the spoken narration for each lesson, then records a digital-teacher delivery, so enablement teams get ready-to-publish lecture videos and training presenter clips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External course and enablement teams use this skill to turn finalized lesson scripts, an authorized teacher portrait, and approved narration into presenter-led training videos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting grants shared account permissions that include image and music generation beyond course-video production; selected media is sent to Beatra and installation reporting occurs automatically.

Mitigation: Review the requested permissions and privacy implications before connecting, approve each paid request, and revoke device access in the Beatra Console when needed.

Risk: Using a teacher's likeness or voice without authorization can violate their rights.

Mitigation: Confirm likeness and voice rights before generating media; treat access to a voice sample as distinct from consent to clone it.
