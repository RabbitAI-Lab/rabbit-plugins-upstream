## Description: <br>
Generates 40-minute, three-part vocational classroom teaching plans in Markdown and DOCX, with templates for uniform or layered student contexts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[flyboat403](https://clawhub.ai/user/flyboat403) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Teachers and education staff use this skill to collect lesson parameters, process optional course or workplace reference materials, choose an appropriate classroom template, and generate a complete vocational teaching plan for a 40-minute class. <br>
