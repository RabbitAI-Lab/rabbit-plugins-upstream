## Description:

Analyzes smart construction technologies for bridges, tunnels, highways, and related infrastructure by combining patent, paper, web, exhibition, and commercialization evidence into professional HTML and Word reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External analysts, infrastructure researchers, and business or patent intelligence teams use this skill to study smart construction technology systems, patent landscapes, academic activity, competitors, commercialization cases, and trends. It is intended for evidence-backed report generation when PatSnap MCP and web research tools are configured.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated analysis may be stale or unsupported if live PatSnap MCP and web searches are not run.

Mitigation: Verify that live searches were executed and that the final HTML or Word report cites current patent, paper, and web sources before relying on conclusions.

Risk: The bundled Word generator can write a fixed report by itself rather than performing live analysis.

Mitigation: Treat the Word script as a report-generation helper and review generated report content, citations, and dates against fresh evidence.
