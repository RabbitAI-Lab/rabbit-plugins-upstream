## Description:

Provides advisory templates and checklists for medical document review, liability assessment, fraud screening, and insurance claims reporting, with human review required for all claim decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance claims professionals can use the checklists, decision-support frameworks, and report templates to organize medical-document review, liability analysis, and fraud screening. Outputs are advisory drafts and require review by a qualified insurance professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment had medium confidence and incomplete inspection because the manifest could not be parsed and three referenced files were missing.

Mitigation: Review the complete package before relying on it for important decisions.

Risk: Advisory claim analysis, OCR guidance, or fraud indicators could be mistaken for verified findings or final decisions, and may involve sensitive medical or claimant information.

Mitigation: Have a qualified insurance professional verify every decision and label; minimize and redact personal data before any document analysis.
