## Description:

当用户需要做今日头条内容研究、文章选题、热点角度整理或同主题内容对比时使用。输入主题或内容链接，基于相关作品和详情输出样本概览、标题模式与可继续验证的内容方向。来自 SocialDataX 社媒数据助手。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research topics and content links are shared with SocialDataX.

Mitigation: Submit only topics and links you are comfortable sharing with SocialDataX.

Risk: The downloaded command-line package and future releases were not verified in the implementation review.

Mitigation: Prefer a reviewed, fixed CLI version, limit its access to unrelated files and secrets, and store the API key using supported secret storage.
