## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for selecting renovation materials, comparing options, and avoiding common purchasing pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renovation planners use this skill to compare tiles, flooring, paints, boards, waterproofing materials, and fixtures before purchasing. It provides material-selection guidance rather than professional construction advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation advice may be mistaken for safe construction, electrical, gas, or waterproofing instructions.

Mitigation: Use this skill for material selection only; refer safety-critical work to licensed professionals and follow local requirements.

Risk: Environmental, health, or product-performance claims may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific project.

Mitigation: Check claims against product technical sheets, independent test reports, and applicable local codes before purchasing or changing plans.
