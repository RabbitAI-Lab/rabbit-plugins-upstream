## Description:

Writes a practical AI usage policy with approved tools, data-handling rules, disclosure duties, review obligations, a decision log, and rollout guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, legal reviewers, and policy owners use this skill to draft a one-page company AI acceptable-use policy that answers day-to-day questions about approved tools, sensitive data, disclosure, and human review. It also produces a decision log for counsel or leadership review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the generated policy as legal advice.

Mitigation: Use it as a drafting aid and route the decision log to legal, compliance, or leadership reviewers before adoption.

Risk: Users may paste regulated records, credentials, or confidential client content while tailoring the policy.

Mitigation: Provide data categories and tool-approval context instead of actual sensitive records unless the organization has explicitly approved that workflow.

Risk: A generic policy may be hard for employees to apply in real situations.

Mitigation: Map each data rule to organization-specific examples, name the policy owner and question channel, and review the policy quarterly.
