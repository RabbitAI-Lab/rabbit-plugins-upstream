## Description: <br>
Writes a practical one-page AI usage policy with approved tools, data-handling rules, disclosure duties, review obligations, a decision log, and rollout guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, legal/compliance reviewers, and policy owners use this skill to draft an organization-specific AI acceptable-use policy. It helps answer day-to-day questions about approved AI tools, permitted data, disclosure, review duties, and escalation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the generated policy as final legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as a practical draft and have legal or compliance reviewers confirm requirements before adoption. <br>
Risk: Incomplete organizational context can produce policy rules that are too broad, too narrow, or hard to follow. <br>
Mitigation: Provide current AI tools, data categories, enterprise agreement status, regulatory exposure, and risk appetite before generating the policy. <br>
