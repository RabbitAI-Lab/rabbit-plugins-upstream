## Description: <br>
Generates a structured project landing risk assessment report for investment-attraction, industrial, and investment projects across construction, funding, operations, market, policy, compliance, credit, and regional-support risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Government, industrial park, and enterprise teams use this skill to assess whether a project can land smoothly, identify risks that may affect signing, construction, production, and operations, assign a four-level risk grade, and prepare mitigation recommendations for review, monitoring, audit, or management decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for overly broad business-risk questions where a formal project landing review is not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow triggers so the workflow is used for project landing, investment-attraction, signing review, construction monitoring, audit, and related project-management risk assessments. <br>
