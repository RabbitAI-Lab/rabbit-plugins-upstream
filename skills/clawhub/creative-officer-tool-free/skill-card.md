## Description: <br>
A lightweight creative director skill for personal users that provides creative consultation, brainstorming, concept evaluation, creative briefs, and basic brand direction. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External personal users, creators, freelancers, and students use this skill to generate creative directions, evaluate campaign ideas, draft creative briefs, and get basic brand or visual direction guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests command execution and file-write permissions that are broader than its documented LLM-only creative guidance purpose. <br>
Mitigation: Restrict or remove exec and write permissions before installation, or require explicit approval before any command is run or file is created or changed. <br>
Risk: Creative recommendations, scores, and briefs may be incomplete, biased, or unsuitable for a specific brand, audience, or legal context. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer validate outputs before using them in public campaigns, client deliverables, or regulated marketing contexts. <br>
