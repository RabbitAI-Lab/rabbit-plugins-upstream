## Description:

Writes the registration, confirmation, reminder and replay email series after a webinar, with the sender identity and unsubscribe elements in every send. Use when the user asks for webinar follow up emails, webinar reminder email sequence, webinar replay email template. For US event marketers and demand generation teams running webinars and virtual events.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

US event marketers and demand generation teams use this skill to draft a consistent four-message webinar series: confirmation, reminders, and replay follow-up, with sender identity and opt-out details across sends.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain unresolved placeholders or omit required footer details.

Mitigation: Review every draft, complete its fields, and check sender identity, postal address, opt-out, and reason for writing before scheduling.

Risk: SMS reminders or attendee comments may be included without appropriate consent or permission.

Mitigation: Use text reminders only with explicit consent and include attendee comments only with written permission.
