## Description:

Rules to avoid identifiable AI writing patterns. Apply to all text output regardless of model or context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide an agent toward plainer, more specific writing and away from common AI-generated phrasing, structure, and formatting patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad style rules may alter tone or wording in outputs where exact phrasing, required terminology, or a prescribed format matters.

Mitigation: Disable or override the skill when legal, medical, security, compliance, quotation, or exact-format requirements take priority.

Risk: The skill may affect general replies, not only explicit editing or writing tasks.

Mitigation: Review outputs for task fit and apply narrower instructions when a different tone or structure is required.
