## Description:

Helps ecommerce advertising teams turn product facts, audience, platform, budget, and compliance constraints into creative hypotheses, hook matrices, static image briefs, short-video scripts, variable tables, and A/B test plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External advertisers, brand growth teams, and creative agencies use this skill to plan ecommerce ad creative and, after review, run AI-HIVE image or video generation with traceable prompts, routing choices, task IDs, and acceptance checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-HIVE generation can involve paid API calls.

Mitigation: Confirm final prompts, model routing, and generation parameters before submitting image or video tasks.

Risk: Reference media may be uploaded to an external service.

Mitigation: Upload only assets the user is authorized to send to AI-HIVE and avoid copyrighted, private, or brand-restricted material without permission.

Risk: API keys could be exposed through logs, files, screenshots, or repositories.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated AI-HIVE API key, provide it through environment or local config, and keep it out of committed files and shared outputs.

Risk: Generated ad plans or assets may contain unsupported product claims or misleading performance expectations.

Mitigation: Review claims against verified product facts, platform rules, and compliance requirements before publishing or launching tests.
