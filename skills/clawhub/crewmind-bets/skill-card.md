## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can help prepare live Solana Mainnet transactions that may move real funds. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the network, program ID, destination accounts, wager amount, and wallet before use; prefer devnet or an isolated wallet with limited funds first. <br>
Risk: Incorrect round, account, or wager details can cause failed transactions or unintended betting activity. <br>
Mitigation: Check round status, end time, minimum and maximum bet limits, selected model index, and claim eligibility before signing any transaction. <br>
