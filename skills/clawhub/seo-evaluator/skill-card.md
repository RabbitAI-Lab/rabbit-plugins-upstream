## Description: <br>
Evaluate e-commerce pages with evidence-first SEO findings that distinguish verified issues, likely signals, editorial opportunities, unavailable checks, and appendix items. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ecom-agent-tools](https://clawhub.ai/user/ecom-agent-tools) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External developers, SEO practitioners, and e-commerce operators use this skill to evaluate a target URL or local HTML file and produce evidence-labeled SEO findings without unsupported scoring or ranking claims. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide analytics, Search Console, merchant, or local files that contain sensitive or proprietary data. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only authorized data intended for analysis, and review any timestamped snapshots or generated reports before sharing them. <br>
Risk: An SEO report could overstate conclusions when ranking, traffic, index status, field Core Web Vitals, or merchant data are unavailable. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unsupported checks in the unavailable section, preserve the skill's evidence labels, and require citations for factual statements based on browsing. <br>
