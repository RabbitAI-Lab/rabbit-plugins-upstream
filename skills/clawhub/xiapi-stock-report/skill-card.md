Investors, analysts, and research writers use this skill to draft structured A-share company fundamental analysis reports with recent news sentiment, industry position, competitiveness, financial quality, ROE/DuPont analysis, outlook, conclusions, and source summaries. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated stock analysis may contain incorrect, outdated, or unsupported financial claims if live sources are incomplete or misread. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify outputs against primary filings, company announcements, and authoritative financial sources before making financial decisions. <br>
Risk: Reports may include confidential notes supplied by the user or reviewed by a subagent. <br>
Mitigation: Do not provide confidential information unless it is intended for inclusion in the generated report and verification workflow. <br>
