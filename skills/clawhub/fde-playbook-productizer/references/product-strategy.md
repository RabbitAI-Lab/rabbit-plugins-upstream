# Product strategy framework (optional)

Not every PRD requires a strategic justification; cite as needed when needed to answer "why do/why do now/what not to do."

---

## 1. Product Strategy Canvas (9 modules)

| Module | Core Issues |
|------|---------|
| **Vision** | How do we inspire others? What do we aspire to achieve? |
| **Market Segmentation** | Who is the first target market segment? Why choose first? |
| **Relative Cost** | Do we optimize for low cost or emphasize unique value? |
| **Value Proposition** | For each segment: Current Situation→Solution→After Change→Alternatives|
| **Strategic Trade-offs** | What are we not going to do? |
| **Key Indicators** | What is the North Star Indicator? What is OMTM this quarter? |
| **Growth** | Sales-driven or product-driven? How to scale? |
| **Competencies** | What core competencies are required? What is self-construction and what is cooperation? |
| **DEFENSIVE** | Why can’t competitors copy? |

---

## 2. SWOT Analysis

| Dimensions | Content |
|------|------|
| **Advantages(S)** | Unique capabilities, brand recognition, customer relationships, technical advantages, cost advantages |
| **Disadvantages (W)** | Resource constraints, technical limitations, low brand awareness, high cost structure |
| **Opportunities (O)** | Market growth, technological progress, regulatory changes, competitive product weaknesses, cooperation opportunities |
| **Threats (T)** | Emerging competitive products, demand changes, technological disruption, regulatory risks, economic downturn |

**Strategic Application**:

- **Attack (Build)**: Advantage + Opportunity
- **Defend**: Disadvantage + Threat
- **Pivot**: Explore opportunities to change the competitive landscape

---

## 3. Porter’s Five Forces Model

| Five Forces | Assessment Questions |
|------|---------|
| **Competitors** | How fierce is the competition? What is the size and strength of the opponent? |
| **Supplier Bargaining Power** | How much say do suppliers have? |
| **Buyer's Bargaining Power** | How much power do customers have to lower prices? |
| **Threat of Substitutes** | What alternative solutions exist? |
| **Threat of New Entrants** | Is it easy for new competitors to enter? |

---

## 4. PESTLE analysis

| Dimensions | Assessment content |
|------|---------|
| **Political** | Government policies, tax regulations, political stability |
| **Economic** | Economic growth, interest rates, inflation, consumer confidence |
| **Social** | Demographic trends, cultural attitudes, lifestyles, education levels |
| **Technological** | Emerging technologies, digital transformation, cybersecurity |
| **Legal** | Data protection, labor law, intellectual property rights |
| **Environmental** | Carbon emissions, sustainable development, ESG requirements |

---

## 5. Ansoff matrix

| | Existing markets | New markets |
|--|---------|--------|
| **Existing Products** | Market Penetration (Low Risk) | Market Development (Medium Risk) |
| **New Products** | Product Development (Medium Risk) | Diversification (High Risk) |

## 6. Business Model Canvas (BMC)

| Module | Content |
|------|------|
| Core Partners | Key Strategic Partners and Suppliers |
| Core Activities | Key Business Activities |
| Core resources | Core resources required to create value |
| **Value Proposition** | What value is delivered to customers? |
| Customer Relations | How to establish and maintain customer relationships? |
| Channel | How do customers discover and obtain value? |
| Customer segmentation | What are the core customer segments? |
| Cost Structure | What are the most important costs? |
| Sources of income | How does a business make money? |

---

## 7. Lean Canvas

| Module | Content |
|------|------|

| Unfair Advantage | Defensive Competitive Advantage |

| Channels | Main customer acquisition channels |
| Revenue Streams | Pricing Model and LTV |
