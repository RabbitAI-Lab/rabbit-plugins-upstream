## Description:

Turns validated customer-delivery learning into reusable delivery playbooks, product-capability candidates, or Agent Skill assets with explicit applicability, evidence, standard steps, and maintenance ownership.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Field delivery engineers and product teams use this skill after adoption and value review to turn repeated, validated delivery patterns into reusable playbooks, asset packages, product-capability candidates, or Agent Skill handoffs while documenting boundaries and maintenance ownership.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer-sensitive or proprietary delivery material could be exposed while preparing reusable assets or public case material.

Mitigation: Use only authorized or de-identified customer information and keep customer material review gates before publishing or reusing outputs.

Risk: A single successful delivery could be over-generalized into a reusable asset before the pattern is proven.

Mitigation: Require two independent deliveries or an equivalent reproducible experiment, document counterexamples, and run a reuse test before promotion.

Risk: Outputs could be mistaken for formal product-roadmap or commercial prioritization decisions.

Mitigation: Treat product-capability outputs as recommendations and route formal candidates through roadmap governance and owner review.
