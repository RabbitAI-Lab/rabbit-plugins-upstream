## Description:

Creates and refines professional Persian RTL resumes with readable mixed Persian and English content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ehsanghaffar](https://clawhub.ai/user/ehsanghaffar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and resume editors use this skill to create or improve Persian-language CVs, including resumes with English technical terms and contact details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume details may be inaccurate or omit important career facts.

Mitigation: Check the generated resume against the candidate's source information; do not invent dates, roles, companies, or metrics.

Risk: Mixed-direction text may render incorrectly in a finished resume.

Mitigation: Validate the generated PDF or DOCX and visually check Persian text, English terms, links, and contact details before use.
