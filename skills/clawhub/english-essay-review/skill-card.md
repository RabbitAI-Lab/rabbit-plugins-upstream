## Description:

This skill reviews CET-4, CET-6, and postgraduate English writing or translation responses with sentence-level corrections, estimated scoring, revised drafts, source-aware sample guidance, and targeted improvement advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners and exam-prep users use this skill to receive sentence-by-sentence feedback, estimated scoring, revisions, and source-aware sample guidance for CET-4, CET-6, and postgraduate English writing or translation responses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Essay scores are exam-prep estimates and may be mistaken for official scores.

Mitigation: Present scores as diagnostic estimates, include confidence and missing-context boundaries, and avoid promising final grader outcomes.

Risk: Users may paste private or sensitive writing into the agent for review.

Mitigation: Advise users to avoid sensitive content unless they are comfortable with their agent and any requested verification tools processing it.
