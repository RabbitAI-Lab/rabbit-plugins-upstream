## Description:

Reviews English exam essays and translations for CET-4, CET-6, and postgraduate entrance exam tasks, giving sentence-level corrections, diagnostic scoring, revised drafts, source-aware model-essay guidance, and study actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners, test takers, and educators use this skill to review English writing and translation answers, estimate performance under the relevant exam rubric, and get actionable revisions and practice guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Essay submissions may contain sensitive personal writing or exam preparation material.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive personal information and limit inputs to content the user is comfortable having processed by the agent.

Risk: Requests for real model essays can lead to uncited, unauthenticated, or copyrighted material if sources are not checked.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source-verification workflow: distinguish verifiable model essays from source-assisted or original examples, provide source details, and avoid reproducing external copyrighted essays in full.

Risk: Scores are diagnostic estimates and may differ from an official examiner or exam-year rubric.

Mitigation: State missing context and confidence, cite sentence-level evidence for score estimates, and avoid presenting estimates as official final scores.
