## Description:

Triage negative reviews and draft accountable, non-defensive Chinese replies for human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liuyuming0823](https://clawhub.ai/user/liuyuming0823)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Store operators and support teams use this skill to classify negative customer reviews, decide whether private follow-up is needed, and draft standard, short, and private Chinese replies for human review before sending.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft replies may imply a refund, replacement, or other remedy beyond the merchant's authority.

Mitigation: Review each draft against store policy and confirmed compensation authority before sending.

Risk: The skill cannot verify orders, logistics status, customer identity, or legal threats.

Mitigation: Use the output as drafting support only, and route order checks, legal threats, and publication decisions through the appropriate human process.
