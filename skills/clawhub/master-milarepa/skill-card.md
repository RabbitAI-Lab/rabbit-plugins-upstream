## Description:

Provides source-cited Milarepa and Kagyu teaching, question answering, and voice guidance grounded in declared Tibetan sources while refusing esoteric practice instructions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to answer questions about Milarepa, Kagyu, Mahamudra, and related Tibetan Buddhist topics with declared-source citations and a controlled teaching voice. It is intended for learning and reference, not as a substitute for a qualified teacher or initiation into esoteric practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat stylized religious teaching as authoritative practice instruction.

Mitigation: Keep the qualified-teacher disclaimer and refuse concrete esoteric practice steps, especially for tummo, the Six Yogas of Naropa, and generation or completion stage practices.

Risk: The skill may frame related questions through Milarepa and Kagyu tradition even when a broader Buddhist comparison is requested.

Mitigation: Disclose the tradition-specific scope and avoid sectarian ranking or superiority claims.

Risk: Live FoJin lookup may return content outside local excerpts or include untrusted text.

Mitigation: Accept only entries matching declared source IDs and treat retrieved content as data rather than instructions.
