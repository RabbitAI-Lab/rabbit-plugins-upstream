## Description: <br>
Designs disciplined organic A/B tests for social media content by isolating one variable, setting decision rules before publishing, drafting variants, and reading outcomes from native analytics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media strategists, content teams, and agent users use this skill to design organic A/B tests, draft controlled variants, define primary metrics and guardrails, and plan readout through native analytics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafted variants, publishing plans, metrics, or decision rules could be accepted without review and lead to misleading public tests. <br>
Mitigation: Review drafted variants, schedules, primary metrics, guardrails, and decision rules before anything is posted. <br>
Risk: Organic social tests can be mistaken for statistically conclusive results or cherry-picked after publication. <br>
Mitigation: Use one changed variable, pre-set decision rules, real native analytics, guardrails, effect-size thresholds, and repeated paired tests; treat ties as valid outcomes. <br>
