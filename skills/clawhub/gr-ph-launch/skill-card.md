## Description: <br>
Guides Product Hunt launch planning from T-14 preparation through launch day and T+7 follow-up, including hunter selection, maker comments, community outreach, launch-day pacing, and post-launch review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, founders, and launch teams use this skill to prepare Product Hunt assets, coordinate launch-day operations, draft launch comments, and plan post-launch follow-up. It is best suited for maker, developer, and B2B launches rather than consumer products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security summary flags guidance about coordinated upvoting, vote pacing, and anti-detection behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for legitimate launch assets, comments, and follow-up planning; keep outreach voluntary, transparent, opt-in, and compliant with Product Hunt rules. <br>
Risk: The security summary flags upvoter-email reuse guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Do not import, reuse, or contact upvoter emails unless users have a lawful basis, appropriate consent, and privacy-compliant handling. <br>
Risk: The security verdict is suspicious and recommends careful review before installation. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit or ignore sections about coordinated upvoting, network separation, vote pacing, and supporter-email reuse before operational use. <br>
