## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests command-execution authority while advising on automation setup. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and require explicit human approval before running commands or enabling automation changes. <br>
Risk: Automation examples include customer messaging, social posting, CRM changes, finance records, and customer data processing. <br>
Mitigation: Test workflows in sandbox accounts, approve external sends and record updates manually, and document rollback steps before production use. <br>
Risk: Insufficient scoping safeguards could lead to incorrect automation behavior or unintended data changes. <br>
Mitigation: Limit connected accounts and permissions to the minimum required, validate field mappings with test data, and monitor error alerts after launch. <br>
