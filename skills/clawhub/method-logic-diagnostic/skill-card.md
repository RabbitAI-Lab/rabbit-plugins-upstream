## Description:

Diagnoses logic and reasoning issues in psychology Methods sections by checking method rationale, design coherence, variable and control rigor, limitation disclosure, and scoring the results with journal-example references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjinhongmy-pixel](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjinhongmy-pixel)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to evaluate psychology empirical Methods sections for method justification, design logic, variable and control completeness, and limitation disclosure. It returns scored diagnostic feedback and relevant positive examples for revision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some source text mentions machine-specific Windows reference paths that may not exist for other users.

Mitigation: Use the bundled reference files when available and verify or replace any local-only path before relying on it in a workflow.

Risk: Limitation-framing guidance could make method limitations sound overly polished or less transparent.

Mitigation: Review suggested limitation wording against the manuscript evidence and preserve accurate, explicit disclosure of study limits.
