# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

To test for measurement invariance across measurement waves, we compared three nested models with increasingly stronger equality constraints across time points. We first tested for measurement invariance across time, and then subsequently also across age. We compared (a) a model with no parameter constraints across time (i.e., configural invariance) to (b) a model with equal factor loadings across time (i.e., metric invariance) to (c) a model with additionally constrained item intercepts across time (i.e., scalar invariance). ... Measurement invariance was tested by evaluating the increase in model misfit between nested models based on a cutoff of ΔCFI = −.010, ΔRMSEA = .015, and ΔSRMR = .030 (Chen, 2007; Cheung & Rensvold, 2002).

## 来源文献

Olaru, G., et al. (2023). The link between personality, global, and domain-specific satisfaction across the adult lifespan. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

不变性检验按 configural→metric→scalar 嵌套递进并以 (a)(b)(c) 标注，拟合变化判据用 ΔCFI/ΔRMSEA/ΔSRMR 精确 cutoff（附出处），时间与年龄两个方向的检验顺序明确，是测量不变性检验的逻辑样板。
