# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

Sample size per data collection site was determined based on sample sizes adopted in previous research (Kitayama et al., 2009; e.g., Na et al., 2010; Salvador et al., 2020; San Martin et al., 2018) and availability of resources shaped by our budget and COVID-19-related restrictions. In each sample, we aimed for 100 men and 100 women to allow meaningful gender comparisons, which in some cases led to oversampling of women before we could reach the targeted number of men. With over 200 participants in most sites, sample size per group was comparable to or greater than the majority of past research that used a similar approach.

## 来源文献

Uskul et al., 2023, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
