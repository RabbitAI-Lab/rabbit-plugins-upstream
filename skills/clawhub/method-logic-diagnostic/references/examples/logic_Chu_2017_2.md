# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

With a sample of 105, we were adequately powered for mediation analyses (Fritz & MacKinnon, 2007). In Study 2, there was also a relatively large proportion of zero responses in our outcome variable (88/105); thus, ZINB regression analyses were conducted to evaluate the proposed paths in our mediation models. ... However, in this study, we were significantly underpowered to evaluate model fit indices (Wolf, Harrington, Clark, & Miller, 2013). ... In Study 3, although inspection of the histogram for our outcome variable did not reveal an excess of zero responses (15/53), we were underpowered (<0.80) to utilize ZINB regressions to confirm whether a zero-inflated model better fits these data.

## 来源文献

Chu, C., et al. (2017). Perceived problem-solving deficits and suicidal ideation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

每项统计选择附功效依据（Fritz & MacKinnon 样本量准则）与数据特征理由（零膨胀比例 88/105 → ZINB），并诚实标注功效不足（underpowered to evaluate model fit / to utilize ZINB），是统计方法选择与局限披露的逻辑样板。
