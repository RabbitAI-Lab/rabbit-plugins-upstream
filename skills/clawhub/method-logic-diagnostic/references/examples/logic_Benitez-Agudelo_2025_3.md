# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

The format and difficulty level of the exam remained constant in all administrations, which ensured comparability of scores over time; on the other hand, the content of the test varied according to the progression of the curriculum. This avoided the repetition of content and the learning effect. The evaluation was not blinded, as it was part of the regular academic assessment process conducted by instructors. However, since it was a pre-established standardized test, its objectivity and consistency were maintained throughout the study.

## 来源文献

Benítez-Agudelo et al., 2025, BMC Psychology

## 适配诊断点

形式恒常与内容变化的对照设计理由、"not blinded ... however ..." 的局限诚实交代与补救论证，是测量等价性与局限说明的逻辑样板。
