# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

Participants first read that the study was about the COVID-19 pandemic and answered two questions to confirm that this context represented a meaningful self-control conflict for them. Specifically, participants responded yes or no to the following question: "Do you have a goal to avoid contracting and spreading COVID-19?" They then used a 5-point scale, ranging from never to very often to answer the question: "During the COVID-19 pandemic, how often have you experienced desires to enjoy in-person social interactions?" We excluded data from those that indicated "no" (N = 23) and "never" (N = 14) to these two questions, respectively, leading to a final N = 263 (age: M = 31.94, SD = 12.34; gender: 145 female, 109 male, nine nonbinary).

## 来源文献

Le, D. T. L., Scholer, A. A., & Fujita, K. (2024). The role of conflict representation in abstinence versus moderation. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

情境意义性筛查（manipulation relevance check）以两个具体问题先行、按回答逐类排除（N = 23 / N = 14）并给出最终样本构成，操纵情境的生态效度论证（COVID-19 疫苗接种早期背景）嵌入其中，是样本筛选的逻辑样板。
