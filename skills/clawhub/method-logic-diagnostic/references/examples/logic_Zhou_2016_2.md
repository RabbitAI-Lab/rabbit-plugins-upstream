# 片段类型：逻辑（条件间差异的平行对照描述）

## 原文片段

> On the first page of the survey, participants in the remedy condition read the following message: [message text]. Afterward, they were told to type a short sentence—"I will answer open-ended questions"—into a text field on the same page if they intended to take the survey. By contrast, participants in the no-remedy condition, only saw this message on the first page: [message text].
>
> Essentially, participants in the remedy condition experienced both prewarning (i.e., typing the short sentence) and appealing-to-conscience (i.e., the bolded portion of the message) strategies on the first page. The second page of the survey contained the consent form. Toward the end of the consent form, we asked participants in the remedy condition to enter their MTurk ID, which embodied the personalization strategy, if they agreed to the terms of the consent form. However, participants in the no-remedy condition were not asked to provide their MTurk ID.

## 来源文献

Zhou, H., & Fishbach, A. (2016), Study 4 Method (Procedure). *The Pitfall of Experimenting on the Web: How Unattended Selective Attrition Leads to Surprising (Yet False) Research Conclusions.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 111(4), 493-504.

## 适配诊断点

- 条件描述用严格平行的对照结构：先完整交代 remedy 条件的每一步（消息原文 + 输入短句），再用 "By contrast" 转述 no-remedy 条件，差异点一目了然。
- 用 "Essentially" 把程序细节抽象为理论构念（prewarning、appealing-to-conscience、personalization 三种策略），实现"具体操作 → 抽象命名"的对应。
- 每个条件差异都落到可复现的操作层面：typed sentence、bolded portion、MTurk ID entry，无一含糊。
- 叙事顺序按页面推进（first page → second page → end of consent form），时间线清晰。
