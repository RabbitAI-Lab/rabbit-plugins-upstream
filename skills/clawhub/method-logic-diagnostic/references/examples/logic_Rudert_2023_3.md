# 片段类型：逻辑（任务操纵的操作化与预测试验证闭环）

## 原文片段

> The performance task contained the two logical reasoning and mathematical skills questions used in Study 6. Participants did not work on the Math task themselves but were told that most people have a score between 5 and 7 and then received information about the scores that the possible group members had achieved: In conditions where the target was not inept at Math, all players thus had scores of 5, 6, or 7 (with the target having the highest score of 7). In conditions where the target was inept at Math, the target's score was 2 and the control persons had scores of 5 and 6.
>
> The cooperative task was a simplified variation of a standard trust game (e.g., Berg et al., 1995; Thielmann et al., 2021) called the Rabbit Game. [...] To strengthen the manipulation, we asked participants to summarize the respective task in their own words after having read the instruction.
>
> Pretest. Both tasks were pretested with 30 U.S. American participants from Prolific Academic (18 female, Mage = 25.97, SD = 7.37). [...] The performance task was perceived as requiring more cognitive capabilities than the cooperation task, t(29) = 10.282, p < .001, d = 2.71; whereas the cooperative task (i.e., the rabbit game) was perceived as requiring more social abilities than the performance task, t(29) = 5.978, p < .001, d = 1.55. Thus, the operationalization of the cognitive and cooperative tasks requiring separate sets of skills (performance vs. cooperation) was successful (see Table 9 for descriptive values).

## 来源文献

Rudert, S. C., Möring, J. N. R., Kenntemich, C., & Büttner, C. M. (2023), Study 6 Method (Materials and Procedure). *When and Why We Ostracize Others: Motivated Social Exclusion in Group Contexts.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

- 任务操纵的参数全部量化：分数分布（5/6/7 vs. target 2 + controls 5/6）、游戏规则（两轮、四只兔子基准、目标六只说明剥削），条件间差异清晰。
- 操纵强化手段具体："asked participants to summarize the respective task in their own words"，确保任务理解。
- 预测试闭环：先测任务感知（cognitive capabilities vs. social abilities 双维度题项），再报告 t 检验与效应量（d = 2.71, d = 1.55），最后用 "Thus, the operationalization ... was successful" 下结论，操纵效度有实证支撑。
- 游戏来源引用（Berg et al., 1995; Thielmann et al., 2021），经典范式改编有出处。
