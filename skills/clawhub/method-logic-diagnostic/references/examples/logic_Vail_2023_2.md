## 原文片段
In the sadness condition, the song list was called "Top 25 Saddest Songs of All Time" and included songs such as Song Cry by Jay-Z (2001); Hurt by Johnny Cash (2002); and The Sounds of Silence by Simon and Garfunkel (1964). The sadness comparison condition was chosen to test whether MS exerts effects beyond the salience of other affectively negative topics.
## 来源文献
Vail, K. E., & Horner, D. E. (2023). The captain of my soul. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspa0000357
