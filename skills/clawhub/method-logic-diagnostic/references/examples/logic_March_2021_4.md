# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

Of the 41,037 trials (see Footnote 4), 588 (1.43%) did not have recorded data because participants exceeded the 2,000 ms limit and 761 (1.85%) ended incorrectly (i.e., participants clicked the distractor, not target, label). Of the 39,688 usable trials, based on our a priori criteria, we excluded 2,487 (6.27%) in which movement initiated too late (>300 ms of clicking start) and 1,407 (3.54%) that ended abnormally fast (<600 ms), yielding 35,794 trials from 118 participants.

## 来源文献

March et al., 2021, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

数据排除按层级漏斗逐级报告（总数 → 各排除原因的数量与百分比 → 最终有效数），且明确标注 "based on our a priori criteria"，是可复现排除规则的样板。
