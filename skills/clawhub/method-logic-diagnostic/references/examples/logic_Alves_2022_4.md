# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

The third series of experiments used the exact same stimuli as the previous experiments with one exception. Experiments 1a and 2a featured IAPS pictures as context stimuli, but the authors of this stimulus set do not allow them to be used in online experiments. We therefore selected four positive and four negative pictures with nonhuman content from the Open Affective Standardized Image Set (OASIS; Kurdi et al., 2017), which served as context stimuli (USs) in Experiment 3a. The procedure of Experiments 3a–3c was identical to the procedures in Experiments 2a–2c, except that one instead of four CSs was paired with a positive US and one other CS was paired with a negative CS.

## 来源文献

Alves et al., 2022, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
