# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

As is typical in longitudinal studies, TEDS has suffered some attrition over time. Accordingly, TEDS participants who contributed data at age 16 and 23 reported on average higher family SES (N = 1,668, M = 0.29, SD = 0.98) than those who contributed data only at age 16, N = 609, M = 0.11, SD = 0.97; t(2,165) = 3.59, p = .0003; Cohen’s d = 0.18, who were on average still of higher SES than the TEDS sample at inception (M = 0, SD = 1).

## 来源文献

Haider, Z. F., & von Stumm, S. (2022). Predicting educational and social–emotional outcomes in emerging adulthood. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.

## 适配诊断点

流失偏差分析用嵌套比较呈现（保留者 vs 仅 16 岁者 vs 初始总体），附完整 t 检验与效应量（d = 0.18），量化说明样本代表性偏差方向，是纵向流失分析的逻辑样板。
