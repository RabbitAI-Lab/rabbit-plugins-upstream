## 原文片段

Because the happy face may appear in either the left or the right in each trial, to facilitate data analysis and visualization of participants’ overall gaze transition pattern between the happy and the angry/fearful face, for the trials with the happy face on the right, we swapped the ﬁxations on the happy face on the right and the ﬁxations on the angry/fearful face on the left so that the ﬁxations on the happy face would consistently appear on the left in our data analysis and visualization of the analysis results (e.g., Chuk et al., 2020). Speciﬁcally, in these trials with the happy face on the right, when swapping, we took all ﬁxations that were on the angry/fearful face on the left and translated them to be over the right face by subtracting x-coordinate of the left face center and adding x-coordinate of the right face center, and vice versa for ﬁxations on the happy face on the right.

## 来源文献

Wang, L., et al. (2022). On becoming socially anxious. Developmental Psychology.
