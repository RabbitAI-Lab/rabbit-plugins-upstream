# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

We began by creating a set of nonsocial questions matched to the six social questions from Study 1. The matching was based on two critical criteria: that each question tap something minimally relevant to people's social lives and that each question be as close linguistically as possible to its matched social item. For example, we matched the social question about party attendance ("Who goes to more parties, you or others?") to a nonsocial question that involves some social interaction yet is not a crucial component of most people's social lives ("Who goes to the grocery store more often, you or others?").

## 来源文献

Deri et al., 2017, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
