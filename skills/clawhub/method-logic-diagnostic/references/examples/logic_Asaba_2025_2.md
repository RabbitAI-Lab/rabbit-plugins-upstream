# 片段类型：逻辑
## 原文片段
Stimuli were similar to Experiment 1a, except that the Overpraise Teacher video was replaced with the Selective-Incongruent Teacher video. The Selective-Incongruent Teacher video was similar to the Selective Teacher's, except that she praised the 3 bad tracings ("This is a great tracing!") and gave a neutral response to the 3 good tracings ("This one is okay!"). ... Our key question was whether children would still endorse the praise from the teacher who selectively praised higher-quality tracings even when the two teachers were matched in terms of the overall valence and the relative frequency of their praise. Indeed, children were more likely to choose the tracing praised by the Selective Teacher than the one praised by the Selective-Incongruent Teacher (18 of 24, 75%, p = 0.022, Binomial Test; exploratory). ... Collectively, these results suggest that children are not simply responding to the relative frequency of praise: Even 4-year-old children trust the teacher who provides feedback that appropriately co-varies with higher-quality work.
## 来源文献
Asaba, M., Stegall, J., Hembacher, E., Frank, M. C., & Gweon, H. (2025), Experiment 1c Methods & Results. *Young Children Infer the Informativeness of Others' Praise.* Developmental Psychology. https://dx.doi.org/10.1037/dev0002073
## 适配诊断点
- 对照条件的最小改动原则：仅替换 Overpraise 视频为 Selective-Incongruent，其余不变（stimuli similar to / procedure identical to），隔离操纵变量。
- 关键逻辑：匹配两个对照教师在效价与表扬频率（matched in terms of the overall valence and the relative frequency），从而排除频率解释，直指协变（co-varies）解释——这是"排除替代解释"的设计样板。
- 先问关键问题（whether...even when...）再给结果（Indeed...），假设-检验-结论链完整。
