# 片段类型：逻辑（刺激抽样与措辞变异的稳健性论证）

## 原文片段

> Across prompts, we utilized a wide range of specific phrasings (e.g., "always hated," "always disliked," "always felt uncomfortable," "felt [X] fit worse"; Table 4) in order to provide a robust stimulus sampling test not dependent on any specific phrasing, and to fit unique contours of each discrimination domain tested. We also sampled both indirect and direct language, given research demonstrating that even explicit hate speech remains all too frequently used in the workplace (e.g., Rosette et al., 2013).

## 来源文献

Phillips, L. T., & Jun, S. (2021), Study 2 Method (Manipulation and Measures, Independent Variable). *Why Benefiting From Discrimination Is Less Recognized as Discrimination.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000298

## 适配诊断点

- 刺激抽样（stimulus sampling）论证：多种措辞变体（4 例 + Table 4 全表）目的明确——"not dependent on any specific phrasing"，结论不绑定单一措辞，外推性更强。
- 措辞变体同时服务于生态效度（"to fit unique contours of each discrimination domain tested"）与操纵纯度。
- 语言强度维度（indirect vs. direct）的选择有实证依据（显性仇恨言论在工作场所的普遍性，Rosette et al., 2013）。
- "in order to" 目的状语串联方法选择与设计意图，逻辑链条透明。
