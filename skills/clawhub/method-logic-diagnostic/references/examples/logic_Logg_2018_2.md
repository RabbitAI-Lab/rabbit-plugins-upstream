## 原文片段

Our sample size, determined ex-ante, sought 210 participants to detect a medium-sized effect (f = 0.25, d = 0.5) with 95% power. After excluding 12 participants (details to follow), a final sample of 200 remained. ... We measured BTA beliefs indirectly by calculating the difference between self- and other-ratings. This sort of indirect measure is a more conservative measure of BTA beliefs than direct measures, which consist of a single comparative assessment and typically produce stronger BTA beliefs (Chambers & Windschitl, 2004; Moore, 2007; Otten & van der Pligt, 1996).

## 来源文献

Logg et al., 2018, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General
