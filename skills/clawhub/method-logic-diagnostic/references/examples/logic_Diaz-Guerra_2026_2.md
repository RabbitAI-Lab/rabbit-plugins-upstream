# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

For a survey to be included in the analysis, all of the following criteria had to be satisfied: (a) Inclusion of a minimum of one measure of FO that aligns with the conceptualizations used in this research (i.e., CFC, optimism, or fatalism), and at least one measure of SES, whether objective (educational level, occupational prestige, or income) or subjective. (b) The survey must cover the entire geographical span of Spain. (c) The sample must encompass the entire adult age range (from 16 years old and above). (d) The data must represent a definitive survey wave, excluding pilot studies.

## 来源文献

Díaz-Guerra et al., 2026, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

纳入标准以 (a)(b)(c)(d) 编号式列举，每条标准具体可判定（含 i.e./whether 限定），是二手数据筛选标准的可复现论证。
