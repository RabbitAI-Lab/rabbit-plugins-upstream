# 片段类型：逻辑（竞争假设的可区分预测与判别检验）

## 原文片段

> Different Construct Aspect. While we initially attributed the low convergent validities between the A-Hard factor and the A-Easy/B5-Q factors within a given Big Five dimension to systematic response tendencies elicited by the hard adjectives, alternative explanations warrant consideration. In particular, the low convergent validities may also stem from a lack of conceptual equivalence between the hard adjectives and either the easy adjectives or the established personality items. If the hard adjectives indeed capture additional trait variance, which is not covered by the easy adjectives or the established personality items, we should find small correlations between the hard factors and the easy factors/B5-Q factors throughout, even if paraphrases in simple language are being used for the hard adjectives. Importantly, the correlations between the easy factors and the hard factors (A-Easy with A-Hard and P-Easy with P-Hard) would be expected to be smaller than those within the easy and hard factors (A-Easy with P-Easy and A-Hard with P-Hard), that is, because both hard factors would be expected to capture the same, additional trait variance yet a distinct trait variance than the easy factors or the B5-Q factor.
>
> Response Tendencies. The contrasting hypothesis on the A-Hard factor is that the hard adjectives themselves are conceptually equivalent and well suited to represent the respective Big Five dimension but evoke systematic response tendencies due to their lexical difficulty. As the paraphrases of the hard adjectives (P-Hard) should be much easier to understand, in this scenario, the correlational patterns of the A-Hard and the P-Hard factor differ because only the A-Hard factor would capture the systematic response tendency. Therefore, the A-Hard factor should show the smallest correlations with the other factors in the model, while the P-Hard factor would be expected to correlate substantially with the easy factors (A-Easy and P-Easy) and the B5-Q factor.

## 适配诊断点

- 竞争假设框架（RRH vs. SRH / 构念差异 vs. 反应倾向）：每个假设都转化为可检验的相关模式预测（"we should find ... would be expected to be smaller than ..."），预测先于检验。
- 预测精确定向：不仅预测相关大小，还预测相对大小（hard-easy 相关 < easy-hard 内部相关），判别力强。
- 假设间差异点被显式定位：P-Hard 因子的表现（easy-to-understand 释义）是区分两假设的关键比较器。
- "While we initially attributed ... alternative explanations warrant consideration" 展示对自身解释的批判性审视，避免确认偏差。
