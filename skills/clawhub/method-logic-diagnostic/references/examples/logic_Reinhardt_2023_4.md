# 片段类型：逻辑

## 原文片段

The study consisted of six different parts: one initial introductory session and the 5-day recording period. In the initial study, participants first confirmed participation requirements (same as in Study 1) and agreed to participate in all parts of this study, otherwise, further participation was not possible. ... Participants who successfully participated in the initial study were invited to take part in the 5-day recording period. For the recording period, we conducted time-based sampling, meaning that participants were asked to fill in the interaction and deception record once per day during the fixed time span between 6 p.m. and midnight Coordinated Universal Time.

## 来源文献

Reinhardt et al., 2023, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 适配诊断点

纵向日记设计的阶段结构（introductory + recording）、纳入门槛（否则无法继续）与时间抽样细节（固定时段）环环相扣，可复现性完整。
