## Description:

Generates entertainment-only China Sports Lottery Super Lotto probability reports that test randomness, explain why number-picking methods have no proven predictive edge, and help users budget, compare options, and avoid misleading win claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to produce responsible-play Super Lotto analysis, including randomness checks, no-edge explanations, budget and ledger guidance, group-buy coverage comparisons, and scam/fallacy warnings. It is for entertainment and harm reduction, not investment, guaranteed winnings, or positive expected return.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can fetch lottery and expert data from the network, so results may depend on source availability or freshness.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit manual runs, review data-source status in generated output, and disclose offline or fallback-data use as entertainment-only analysis.

Risk: The artifact includes Windows scheduling, watchdog, desktop-write, and restore-related behavior that can affect user-profile files or persistent automation.

Mitigation: Use manual `python run_dlt.py` operation by default; enable scheduled tasks, watchdogs, restore flows, or administrator PowerShell steps only after local review.

Risk: Lottery analysis can be misread as a promise of higher winning odds or positive expected value.

Mitigation: Preserve the no-edge conclusion, negative-expectation warning, spending limits, and scam/fallacy guidance in user-facing output.
