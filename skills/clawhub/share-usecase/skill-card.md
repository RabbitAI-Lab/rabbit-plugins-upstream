## Description: <br>
Share your OpenClaw use case to clawusecase.com. Analyzes your recent work and creates a submission for the community. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[josephl37](https://clawhub.ai/user/josephl37) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
OpenClaw users use this skill to turn recent chat context into a reviewed use-case draft and submit it to the clawusecase.com community showcase, either anonymously or with public profile attribution. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recent chat context may include secrets, client names, internal URLs, unreleased plans, or other sensitive details that would become public in a submitted use case. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit the draft before submission; remove sensitive details before approving publication. <br>
Risk: Credited submissions attach a public GitHub or X identity to the live use case. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymous submission when public attribution is not desired. <br>
Risk: The skill submits content to a live public showcase after user approval. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the preview content, category, tools, and attribution choice before running the submission step. <br>
