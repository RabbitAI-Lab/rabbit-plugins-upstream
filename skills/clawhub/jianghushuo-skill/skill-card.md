## Description:

Provides Chinese-language decision and self-media coaching through a disclosed roleplay based on Jiang Hushuo's public thinking frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[simonsxxs](https://clawhub.ai/user/simonsxxs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People seeking a Chinese-language thinking partner use the skill to examine business, content creation, learning, and decisions through Jiang Hushuo-inspired models. It provides perspective and next steps rather than statements from the real person or personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: First-person roleplay could be mistaken for a statement by Jiang Hushuo.

Mitigation: Disclose that the persona is based on public material and label views inferred from his framework rather than treating them as his own.

Risk: Business or investment discussions may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as a decision framework, verify current facts, and do not rely on it for personalized financial advice.
