## Check list before uploading

- [ ] Title and thumbnail match what the video delivers
- [ ] First two sentences give subject, time and place
- [ ] Stakes clear in 15 seconds
- [ ] Plain chronological order, a re-hook at the end of each chapter
- [ ] Every fact has a source
- [ ] Nothing copied from another video or article
- [ ] One look and one narrator throughout
- [ ] Text on screen, not inside AI pictures
- [ ] Thumbnail: one subject, 1 to 4 words, not repeating the title
- [ ] Description has chapters and sources
- [ ] Label answered honestly
- [ ] The video adds something of its own
