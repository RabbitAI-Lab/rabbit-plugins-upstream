# Niches and faceless YouTube video ideas

## What makes a niche work without a face

| Trait | Test |
|---|---|
| Sourceable | You can find two reliable sources for every claim |
| Drawable | Each scene is a place, object, map, person in costume or animation |
| Repeatable | The same chapter structure fits the next ten videos |
| Wide enough | You can list 30 ideas |
| Narrow enough | One look and one voice suit all 30 |

## Niches that suit a faceless channel

Pick one. Do not mix.

- **History:** events, people, places, inventions, wars (told in order).
- **Science and how things work:** machines, bodies, planets, materials.
- **True stories and crime:** documented cases, with care for real people.
- **Business stories:** the rise and fall of a company or a product.
- **Myth and folklore:** where stories come from and what the evidence says.
- **Geography and places:** cities, borders, routes, islands.
- **Explainers:** one idea, built from the bottom up.
- **Sleep and calm documentaries:** slow, long, gentle narration on one subject.

## Idea patterns

Fill the brackets, then check the facts exist.

| Pattern | Template |
|---|---|
| The day it happened | "The Day [Event] Happened, Hour by Hour" |
| Rise and fall | "The Rise and Fall of [Thing]" |
| How it works | "How [Machine or System] Actually Works" |
| The mystery | "The Mystery of [Object or Event]" |
| Then and now | "[Place] Then and Now" |
| The last days | "The Last Days of [Person, City or Company]" |
| Origins | "Where [Thing] Came From" |
| Comparison | "[A] vs [B]: What Was Different" |
| The mistake | "The Mistake That Led to [Event]" |
| The list in order | "[Number] Turning Points in the Story of [Thing]" |

## Turning an idea into a video plan

For each idea, write these before you script:

1. **Subject, time, place** in two sentences.
2. **The question** the video answers.
3. **The opener type** the story already has.
4. **Five to eight chapters** in time order.
5. **The turn** in each chapter.
6. **Sources:** at least two per key claim.
7. **The picture type** for each chapter.

If you cannot write the first two sentences with a subject, a time and a place, the idea is not ready.

## Ten videos at a time

Plan a batch of 10 before you make the first one. Check:

- Each video stands alone.
- The titles do not repeat the same promise.
- The look and voice fit every one.
- At least two ideas answer questions viewers already search for.
- No video depends on copying another channel's script.

## What not to do

- Mass-produce near-identical videos with only the name changed.
- Re-upload or lightly edit someone else's video.
- Use a voice or picture that looks like a real person without their permission.
- Fill a video with filler to reach a length.
- Promise income or growth in the channel or the titles.
