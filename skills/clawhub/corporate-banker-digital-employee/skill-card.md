## Description:

Provides Chinese-first reference workflows and draft analyses for corporate banking visits, due diligence, financial and industry assessment, and credit applications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Corporate relationship managers and credit officers use this reference to draft visit plans, diligence and financial analyses, client notes, and credit application materials for human review. Authorized staff perform any system access, case creation, or submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (PRC banking context); use elsewhere requires local compliance review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Credit-case language may be interpreted as permission to create or submit regulated applications.

Mitigation: Review the workflow before installation near real credit systems; keep case creation, submission, and record changes with authorized staff, and do not grant the agent write or submit permissions while the ambiguity remains.

Risk: Customer records and financial information could be exposed or used without adequate review.

Mitigation: Use authorized accounts and desensitized, minimum-necessary customer data; have qualified staff verify drafts and decisions before use.
