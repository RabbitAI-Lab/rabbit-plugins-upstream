## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and developers use this skill through AI agents to plan X/Twitter posts, threads, hooks, and engagement practices. It is intended to guide content drafting and review, not to control accounts or publish automatically. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated content may use controversy, urgency, or reply-driving tactics that conflict with a brand voice or platform rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated posts before publishing and adjust claims, tone, and engagement prompts to match brand and platform requirements. <br>
Risk: X/Twitter ranking behavior and platform policies can change, so static engagement guidance may become stale. <br>
Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current platform rules, account analytics, and recent performance before relying on them for campaigns. <br>
