## Description:

Creates talking-avatar videos from scripts, custom portraits and voice samples, or a portrait paired with audio; also supports text-to-speech and avatar management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to make digital-human voiceover videos using a platform avatar, a reusable custom avatar, or one-off portrait lip sync.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photos and voice recordings may be used to create a person's likeness or clone their voice without consent.

Mitigation: Use only media for which you have rights and the subject's consent.

Risk: Uploaded local media may become accessible through public URLs.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive or proprietary assets, review access before upload, and remove hosted assets when possible.

Risk: Deleting a custom avatar with the wrong actor ID could remove the wrong persona.

Mitigation: Confirm the actor ID and intended avatar before deletion.
