## Description:

Turn a script into a talking avatar: choose a platform digital human, clone a custom one from a photo and voice sample, or lip-sync a single portrait to an audio track.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to generate AdsTurbo digital-human voiceover videos from scripts, platform avatars, custom photo and voice personas, or portrait-plus-audio lip-sync inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Face photos, voice recordings, audio, and video assets may be sent to AdsTurbo and may produce public asset URLs.

Mitigation: Use only media you own or have explicit permission to use, avoid sensitive personal content, and review AdsTurbo retention and deletion controls before using real people.

Risk: The skill can support likeness generation and voice cloning without built-in consent verification evidence.

Mitigation: Confirm consent and rights for the depicted person and voice before creating personas, lip-sync media, or cloned-voice outputs.
