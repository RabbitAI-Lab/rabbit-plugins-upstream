## Use Case:

Video editors and creators use this skill to plan and apply multi-step timeline edits, verify the result, and export an MP4.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applying an edit can change an existing project timeline.

Mitigation: Enable requirePlanApproval for existing projects or important timelines and review the plan before applying it.

Risk: A rendered MP4 URL may be shared before the output is reviewed.

Mitigation: Review the rendered video and its URL before sharing.
