## Description:

Autonomous timeline AI video editor for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, and podcasts. Use when editing videos from natural language or canonical actions: smart jump cuts, silence removal, 9:16 active-speaker reframing, 280+ animated captions and subtitles, B-roll cutaways, After Effects motion graphics, VFX keying, color grading, voiceover, audio cleanup, and cloud MP4 export.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
