## Description:

When two design requirements seem mutually exclusive, name the contradiction, separate conflicting states, then invent a concrete no-compromise resolution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to analyze technical design contradictions in architecture, APIs, and systems, then document a concrete TRIZ-style resolution without settling for a midpoint compromise.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can shape design-analysis responses into a TRIZ format even when TRIZ is not the right method.

Mitigation: Use the documented template gate and over-application guard: skip TRIZ when the contradiction cannot be stated, measurement settles the decision, or a standard pattern directly resolves the design.

Risk: A TRIZ resolution could present design guidance that still needs engineering review.

Mitigation: Review the proposed contradiction, separation, inventive move, and residual tradeoff before applying the design.
