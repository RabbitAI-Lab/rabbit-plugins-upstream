## Description:

Analyzes draft text for configured clichés, sentence rhythm, repetition, and language-specific patterns, then reports findings and revision guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yanstd888](https://clawhub.ai/user/yanstd888)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers, developers, and agents use the skill to review drafts locally for configured style patterns and receive findings they can consider while editing. It analyzes text and does not rewrite the source.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style scores and findings measure configured writing patterns; they do not establish whether a person or a model wrote the text.

Mitigation: Treat findings as editing guidance and review suggested changes while preserving the draft's meaning.

Risk: Optional voice profiles retain measurements derived from writing samples, and separately fetched releases or the hosted demo are outside this artifact review.

Mitigation: Choose samples deliberately, remove saved profiles when no longer needed, and pin separately downloaded releases to a reviewed version.
