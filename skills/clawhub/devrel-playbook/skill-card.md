## Description:

Build and measure Developer Relations programs across documentation, community, technical content, integrations, events and developer advocacy. Use when designing DevRel strategy, improving developer activation, launching an API/SDK, evaluating sponsorships, or connecting community activity to product adoption and revenue.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, DevRel teams, founders, and go-to-market partners use this skill to diagnose developer journey bottlenecks, plan documentation, community, events, ecosystem distribution, and DevRel-to-sales handoffs, and report activity against activation and retention outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach, sponsorship, moderation, or sales handoff plans could mishandle privacy, consent, truthful disclosure, or platform rules.

Mitigation: Review plans before use, disclose sponsorships, obtain consent for developer stories or code, and confirm outreach and community actions follow applicable platform rules.

Risk: Benchmark, comparison, or case-study guidance could be overstated or interpreted as guaranteed business lift.

Mitigation: Require reproducible methodology, state where each option wins, and treat historical campaign results as evidence rather than promised outcomes.
