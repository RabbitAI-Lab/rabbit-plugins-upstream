## Description:

Helps job seekers discover openings, assess fit, prepare credible application materials, and practice interviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dong845](https://clawhub.ai/user/dong845)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to find and assess roles, tailor resumes and application materials to their actual experience, and rehearse interviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting to a logged-in daily browser exposes sensitive authenticated sessions.

Mitigation: Review browser access before installing; use a separate browser profile or paste job descriptions when accounts are sensitive.

Risk: Locally stored career profiles and application workspaces may contain private personal information.

Mitigation: Treat generated profile and workspace folders as private career data.
