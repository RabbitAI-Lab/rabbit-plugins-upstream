## Description: <br>
Content Recycling helps agents resurface proven evergreen social posts, refresh their hook, format, facts, and cadence, and prepare human-reviewed reposting plans without duplicating stale content or fabricating metrics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media teams and content marketers use this skill to build a queue of proven evergreen content, refresh each item for a new cycle, and prepare platform-appropriate drafts for human review before scheduling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Refreshed posts could carry stale claims, broken links, or misleading performance signals if old content is reused without verification. <br>
Mitigation: Verify facts, dates, prices, links, compliance language, and native analytics before reuse; do not fabricate metrics. <br>
Risk: Identical or high-frequency reposting can fatigue audiences and conflict with platform originality expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Refresh the insight with a new hook, format, opening line, or proof point, and space recycled assets on a deliberate cadence. <br>
Risk: Connected scheduling workflows can publish externally before the refreshed content is checked. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human review enabled before posts are scheduled, recreated, or published. <br>
