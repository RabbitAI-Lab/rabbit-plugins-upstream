## Description:

Decide what to post by turning trending topics or a rough idea into platform-specific draft posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[posteyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/posteyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and social media teams use this skill to develop ideas from trends or rough concepts into tailored drafts for their connected platforms. It requires the Postey skill for account access, writing, and draft creation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approving a schedule authorizes automatic publication, and the scheduling wording is not fully consistent across the skill description.

Mitigation: Review each draft and its proposed time; schedule only after explicitly approving both.

Risk: The required Postey integration is separate, and its account permissions are not described in this release.

Mitigation: Review the integration and connected-account permissions separately before use.
