## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering teams use this skill to produce practical onboarding documentation for a service, codebase, or team, including service overview, architecture, local setup, testing, deployment, operations, and contacts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated onboarding documentation may expose internal service names, contacts, deployment details, operational links, or similar sensitive team context. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill in a trusted workspace, avoid entering secrets, and review the generated document before sharing it outside the intended audience. <br>
Risk: Placeholder commands, access paths, or operational procedures may be inaccurate if left unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Replace placeholders with current team-approved instructions and validate setup, testing, deployment, and on-call sections with service owners. <br>
