## Description:

Provides draft customer segmentation, reactivation and renewal plans, outreach copy, and compliance checklists for insurance operations in China's WeChat ecosystem.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Insurance operations teams and agents use this skill to draft customer segments, reactivation and renewal schedules, WeChat outreach copy, and compliance review checklists before approval in their own systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer identifiers or sensitive insurance information may be exposed if pasted into a chat or exported without approval.

Mitigation: Use aggregate figures and de-identified labels; keep customer-list processing and any required approvals within company systems.

Risk: Unreviewed outreach drafts may misstate insurance terms or contact people without authorization.

Mitigation: Have compliance and operations staff approve content, verify current requirements and consent, honor opt-outs, and send only through approved systems.
