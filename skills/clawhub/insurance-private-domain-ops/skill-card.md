## Use Case:

External insurance operations, CRM, and agency teams use this skill to plan private-domain customer lifecycle operations, segment insured customers, draft compliant outreach, and review renewal or reactivation workflows. It is advisory and should be reviewed by qualified compliance staff before use in marketing or customer communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste real customer personal information into prompts while performing segmentation, outreach planning, or renewal analysis.

Mitigation: Use only aggregated or de-identified labels and avoid names, phone numbers, identity numbers, policy numbers, and other direct identifiers.

Risk: Generated insurance marketing, renewal, or reactivation content may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a regulated communication.

Mitigation: Require review by qualified compliance or operations staff before sending customer-facing content, especially content involving benefits, guarantees, suitability, AI calling, or opt-out handling.

Risk: The skill may be mistaken for connected CRM automation.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory templates and operating guidance only; use approved internal systems for message delivery, list export, audit logging, and database changes.
