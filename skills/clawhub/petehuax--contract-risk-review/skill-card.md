## Description:

Reviews supplied contract text for missing clauses and risky wording, then suggests prioritized edits for negotiation; it is not legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[petehuax](https://clawhub.ai/user/petehuax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People reviewing business contracts use this skill to identify missing terms, rank clause risks from their negotiating position, and draft proposed revisions for discussion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on an incomplete or inaccurate contract review as legal advice, especially for sensitive or high-stakes agreements.

Mitigation: Treat the output as an informational checklist and seek review by a qualified lawyer for high-value, regulated, cross-border, employment, litigation-related, or otherwise sensitive contracts.
