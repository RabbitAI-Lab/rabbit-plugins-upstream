## Description:

Analyzes an everyday habit journal to identify likely triggers and suggest cue disruptions and personalized if-then plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individuals and coaches use this skill to track recurring everyday habits, identify likely cues from event and baseline entries, and plan practical changes to routines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The local journal contains sensitive details about habits, mood, urges, location, company, and device use.

Mitigation: Keep the journal private and limit access and sharing to what the user intends.

Risk: Habit-tracking advice may be mistaken for treatment of substance dependence, eating disorders, gambling, or other compulsive behavior.

Mitigation: Use this skill only for ordinary habits; seek professional support for dependence, compulsive behavior, withdrawal, loss of control, or significant distress.

Risk: Thin or unrepresentative journal entries can make apparent triggers misleading.

Mitigation: Record both events and non-event baselines, treat low-confidence cues as hypotheses, and reassess after making changes.
