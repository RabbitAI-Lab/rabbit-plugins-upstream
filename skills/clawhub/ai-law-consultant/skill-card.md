## Description:

AI Law Consultant helps individuals, companies, and AI service providers assess PRC-focused AI creation and service workflows for compliance obligations, risk points, and practical remediation steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals, enterprise teams, and AI service providers use this skill to triage AI legal and compliance questions under China's regulatory framework. It supports scenario diagnosis, compliance checklist generation, red-line review, filing guidance, and practical remediation suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may apply PRC-focused guidance to another jurisdiction or to formal legal decisions.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable jurisdiction and consult qualified counsel for high-stakes business, administrative, criminal, or cross-border matters.

Risk: Regulatory guidance can become stale as AI laws, filing rules, and enforcement practices change.

Mitigation: Verify current law and regulator guidance before relying on the output for operational decisions.

Risk: The optional checklist script produces compliance triage output that may be mistaken for a complete legal review.

Mitigation: Treat script output as a structured checklist and review it with the underlying reference materials and qualified professionals where needed.
