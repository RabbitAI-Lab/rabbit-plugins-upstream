## Description:

Provides China-focused AI-use legal compliance guidance for personal creators, enterprise AI office use, and public AI service providers, with professional and plain-language conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and compliance teams use this skill to screen AI creation, AI office, and AI service scenarios against China-focused AI regulations, standards, filing requirements, content-labeling duties, and common violation patterns. It is a screening and guidance aid, not a substitute for formal legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may provide outdated or incomplete legal compliance guidance for fast-changing AI regulations.

Mitigation: Verify cited laws, standards, effective dates, and draft-status notices against official sources before relying on the output.

Risk: Users may treat screening output as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Use a licensed lawyer for major business, penalty, litigation, or high-risk deployment decisions.

Risk: The skill is scoped to China-focused AI legal compliance and may not fit other jurisdictions.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable jurisdiction before use and obtain local legal review when operating outside the skill scope.
