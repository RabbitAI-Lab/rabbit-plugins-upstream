## Description: <br>
Novel Writer helps Chinese-language fiction creators plan story outlines, continue drafts, polish prose, check narrative consistency, and use local helper scripts for outline validation and word counts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Writers, content creators, and writing hobbyists use this skill to move from story ideas to structured outlines, drafted chapters, continuation plans, editing reports, and consistency checks for long-form fiction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad triggers such as short writing prompts may invoke the skill during ordinary writing tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill when the user is asking for novel-writing support, and run helper scripts only on manuscript files or directories the user intentionally selects. <br>
Risk: The consistency and outline helper scripts use simple heuristics and may produce incomplete or incorrect findings. <br>
Mitigation: Treat script output as review guidance and manually check important story, timeline, character, and item decisions before relying on the results. <br>
