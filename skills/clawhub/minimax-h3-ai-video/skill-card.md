## Description:

Helps creators plan and generate short MiniMax H3 videos from text, images, frames, or media references through Beatra.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to develop video briefs and create short AI advertising, product, brand, and social media videos from text or reference media, with Beatra account authorization and credit-based generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authorization gives the skill reusable access to broad Beatra media and account operations.

Mitigation: Connect only if comfortable with that access; review account activity and disconnect the installation when access is no longer needed.

Risk: The package checks for and installs verified updates automatically by default.

Mitigation: Turn off automatic updates if changes must be reviewed before installation.

Risk: Video generation and related work can consume paid credits.

Mitigation: Review the live estimate and paid-generation confirmation card before approving a submission or top-up.
