## Description:

Fit helps agents provide general fitness coaching by assessing goals and constraints, shaping adaptable training and nutrition guidance, and tracking progress around real-life disruptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for general fitness coaching, including goal assessment, training structure, nutrition targets, disruption planning, and progress tracking. It should not be used as medical or physical-therapy advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used for pain, acute injury, rehabilitation, or medical constraints despite being general fitness guidance.

Mitigation: Treat responses as general fitness coaching only and direct users with injuries, severe pain, swelling, neurological symptoms, worsening symptoms, or functional loss to professional evaluation.

Risk: Broad fitness goals and injury-return guidance could lead to overconfident training advice.

Mitigation: Review plans carefully before use, keep return-to-training recommendations conservative, and avoid presenting the skill as medical or physical-therapy advice.
