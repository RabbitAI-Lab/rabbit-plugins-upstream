## Description: <br>
Use when the user asks to plan launch pricing, design pricing tiers or packaging, or set up a launch discount or early-bird offer; produces candidate tier structures, value-to-price mapping, launch-offer ladders, beta or early-adopter pricing paths, and guarantee or refund terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Launch teams, founders, and marketing operators use this skill to draft commercially usable launch pricing and packaging plans. It helps structure tiers, map value pillars to price, define launch discounts with real urgency, and prepare candidate offer terms for later registry review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Draft prices, discounts, deadlines, or refund language could be mistaken for approved public offer terms. <br>
Mitigation: Review all proposed commercial terms before adding them to claim or launch registries or using them in public launch materials. <br>
Risk: Saved pricing and offer records could capture premature or unapproved decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Require user confirmation before saving scoped candidate pricing and offer records. <br>
