## Description:

A Chinese homework coaching assistant for 49天成功方程式训练营 learners that guides orientation, weekly assignments, final exam preparation, PPTX template completion, scoring, and follow-up tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners, company operators, and executives use this skill to complete 49天使命血肉化 orientation, week 1-5 coursework, and final exam materials through guided interviews, confirmation loops, PPTX template filling, and completion scoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks learners for real personal, company, goals, stories, and optional photo or case materials to build coursework slides.

Mitigation: Provide only information suitable for the generated slides and review the finished PPTX before sharing it outside the organization.

Risk: Generated coursework can be incomplete or misleading if learner-provided facts, evidence, goals, or photos are missing.

Mitigation: Use the skill's confirmation, missing-item tracking, and quality scoring steps to review facts and mark unsupported material before submission.
