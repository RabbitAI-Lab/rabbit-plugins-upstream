## Use Case:

External learners in the 49-day mission embodiment training camp use this skill to complete the pre-course orientation document, weekly homework, and graduation exam materials. The agent interviews the learner, organizes confirmed facts into PPT-ready content, tracks completion and quality, and prepares editable PPTX deliverables from the included templates.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for personal, course, and business context that may later be placed into presentation files.

Mitigation: Share only information suitable for inclusion in a PPTX and review generated slides before distribution.

Risk: Homework content, scores, or status may be misleading if the learner has not provided enough verifiable facts.

Mitigation: Keep unsupported items marked as pending, base scores on confirmed answers, and review the completed deck before submission.

Risk: Generated slides may contain incorrect or inappropriate business claims if user-provided goals, dates, responsibilities, or evidence are inaccurate.

Mitigation: Confirm factual details, targets, owners, and evidence before finalizing or sharing the PPTX.
