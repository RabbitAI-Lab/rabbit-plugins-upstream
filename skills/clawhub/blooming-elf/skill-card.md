## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to set up plant-care records, receive watering and care reminders, and get concise guidance for adding plants, logging care, diagnosing plant issues, and reviewing care history. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill keeps persistent plant-care records and updates a local MEMORY.md configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if you are comfortable storing plant-care details persistently, and review the stored configuration when plant records or locations change. <br>
Risk: The skill may create plant archive and log documents, including optional knowledge-base associations. <br>
Mitigation: Limit any knowledge-base association to plant documents and review generated archive or log content before relying on it. <br>
Risk: The skill can create weather-aware reminders after confirmation and may use the user's city. <br>
Mitigation: Review the reminder schedule and city-based weather setting before confirming automation, and adjust or cancel reminders when needed. <br>
