## Description:

Helps sales and customer success teams turn post-sales customer value into approved advocacy assets such as testimonials, case material, reference candidates, marketing handoffs, and reputation monitoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and customer success teams use this skill after a customer has gone live, reached first value, or completed a business review to identify advocacy candidates, collect customer voice, manage authorization, hand off approved assets to marketing, and monitor reputation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security review flagged that the skill may include customer outreach instructions outside its stated advocacy scope.

Mitigation: Keep the workflow limited to approved advocacy asset collection and move referral-introduction activity to the separate referral workflow before use.

Risk: The skill handles screenshots, transcripts, customer metrics, and other sensitive customer evidence.

Mitigation: Require explicit customer authorization, approved company storage, source/date tracking, and redaction of unrelated customer information.

Risk: Public collateral created from collected customer voice can expose unapproved names, metrics, or claims.

Mitigation: Route public materials through marketing and legal review, confirm the published version matches the customer-approved version, and archive authorization records.
