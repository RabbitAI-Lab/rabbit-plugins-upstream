## Use Case:

External creators, developers, and media workflow operators use this skill to plan a controlled partial migration from Blooper-style agentic video production to AI-HIVE MCP media tasks. It focuses on preserving proprietary workflow boundaries while validating independent image, video, or shot nodes through approvals, cost checks, quality metrics, and rollback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is primarily Chinese-language and may be misapplied by operators who cannot review the instructions accurately.

Mitigation: Use a reviewer fluent in Chinese or translate the plan before approving production media tasks.

Risk: Paid or external AI-HIVE media tasks can expose cost, asset-rights, or publication risks.

Mitigation: Keep budget checks, asset-rights confirmation, manual approval, and rollback steps enabled before generation, external sharing, or publishing.

Risk: Platform capabilities, availability, and pricing can change after the evidence date.

Mitigation: Confirm current Blooper and AI-HIVE capabilities, regions, model availability, and pricing before making migration or comparison claims.

Risk: A content-layer migration may not preserve Blooper-specific project memory, editor, version tree, team review, or governance features.

Mitigation: Keep the original platform path or an equivalent workflow for proprietary workbench, review, versioning, and rollback needs.
