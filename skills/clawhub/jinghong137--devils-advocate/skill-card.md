## Description: <br>
A rhetorical sparring partner that picks apart the user's opinions using classic logical fallacies only when the user explicitly asks for scrutiny. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jinghong137](https://clawhub.ai/user/jinghong137) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users can opt in when they want an adversarial, stylized critique of a claim or opinion. The skill produces three labeled rebuttals that demonstrate different logical fallacy patterns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The intentionally smug critique style and stop response may annoy users or continue an unwanted tone after discomfort. <br>
Mitigation: Install only for explicit devil's-advocate critique workflows, and revise the stop rule so any stop or discomfort request immediately returns to normal assistant behavior. <br>
