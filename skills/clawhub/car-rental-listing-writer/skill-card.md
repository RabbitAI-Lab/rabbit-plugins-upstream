## Description:

Drafts SEO-focused rental-car listings for vehicle pages, rental platforms, Google Business Profiles, and directories, with confirmed specifications and booking terms stated upfront.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Rental business owners and listing managers use the skill to draft or revise vehicle listings for websites, Turo, Getaround, Google Business Profiles, and rental directories. It organizes user-confirmed vehicle details, pricing, and booking terms into platform-appropriate copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect or unconfirmed vehicle specifications, prices, or rental terms could make a listing misleading.

Mitigation: Confirm all vehicle facts, prices, and booking terms before publishing; omit uncertain specifications and use ‘Contact for price’ when no price is supplied.

Risk: Draft copy may be mistaken for a reviewed, published listing.

Mitigation: Review the draft against the rental business’s actual policies and the destination platform’s requirements before posting.
