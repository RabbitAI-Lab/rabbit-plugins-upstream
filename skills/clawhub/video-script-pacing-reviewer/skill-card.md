## Description: <br>
Reviews video scripts and presentation drafts for pacing, time allocation, visual-narration alignment, information density, hooks, transitions, and platform fit, then returns a structured critique with segment scores and rewrite suggestions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mayf3](https://clawhub.ai/user/mayf3) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, editors, and presentation authors use this skill to audit script or slide-deck rhythm before production or publication. It identifies pacing risks, retention drop-off points, and platform-specific adjustment opportunities without generating the script, creating slides, or editing video. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided scripts or deck text may contain confidential material. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in an agent environment approved for that content, and avoid submitting sensitive or unreleased material unless the processing context is appropriate. <br>
Risk: Pacing scores and predicted retention drop-off points may be overinterpreted as performance guarantees. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the report as editorial guidance and validate important production or publishing decisions with human review, platform data, or audience testing. <br>
Risk: Reviews can be incomplete when visual descriptions, timing, or narration counts are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Provide the script or slide text, target platform, known timing, narration counts, and visual descriptions when available; require the agent to label any assumptions. <br>
