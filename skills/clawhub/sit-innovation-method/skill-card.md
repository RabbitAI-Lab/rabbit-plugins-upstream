## Description: <br>
Guides agents through the Systematic Inventive Thinking method to generate structured innovation ideas for products, services, business models, marketing, operations, and organization management. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill to analyze an existing product, service, workflow, or business model and produce SIT-based innovation options. The skill is suited to structured brainstorming and workshop-style ideation in business and non-engineering contexts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be selected for broad innovation or brainstorming prompts when the user wants a different method. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for a different framework or specify that SIT should not be used when another ideation method is desired. <br>
Risk: Generated innovation proposals may be impractical, incomplete, or a poor fit for the target market. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs with domain experts and validate selected ideas through user research, prototyping, and feasibility assessment before implementation. <br>
