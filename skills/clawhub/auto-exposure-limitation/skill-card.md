## Description: <br>
Explains why camera auto and program exposure modes tend to produce technically safe but creatively similar photos, then guides users toward aperture priority, shutter priority, or manual control. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External photography learners use this skill to understand the creative limits of auto and program exposure modes and to choose a more controllable camera mode for depth of field or motion effects. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The bundled test-prompts.json fixture is malformed, so automated test consumers may fail to parse it. <br>
Mitigation: Fix the quoted phrase in the test fixture before relying on it for validation. <br>
Risk: Exposure-mode guidance may not map perfectly to modern cameras or phone computational photography. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as educational guidance and adapt recommendations to the user's camera, available modes, and creative intent. <br>
