## Description:

Write B2B cold emails and follow-up sequences that get replies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales, marketing, and business development users use this skill to draft, review, and improve B2B cold outreach, subject lines, and multi-touch follow-up sequences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags guidance that could make vendor outreach appear internally originated.

Mitigation: Require transparent sender identity, avoid impersonation or implied internal affiliation, and review subject-line guidance before use.

Risk: Cold outreach may create legal, privacy, deliverability, or consent issues if used without campaign controls.

Mitigation: Review campaigns against applicable email and privacy rules before sending and honor opt-out or breakup-email commitments.
