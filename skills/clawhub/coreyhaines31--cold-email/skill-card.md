## Description: <br>
Writes B2B cold emails and follow-up sequences with guidance for subject lines, personalization, body copy, CTAs, and multi-touch outreach. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, marketing, and founder operators use this skill to draft, critique, and improve unsolicited B2B outbound emails and follow-up sequences. It helps adapt message structure, subject lines, personalization, proof points, and low-friction CTAs to the target prospect and campaign goal. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Subject-line guidance may encourage outreach that appears internal or colleague-like and could mislead recipients. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated subject lines before use and require truthful sales-outreach framing; avoid fake reply or forward markers, deceptive sender identity, false urgency, and misleading claims. <br>
Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context files when present. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where those files are approved for email drafting, and review outputs for accidental disclosure of confidential or sensitive context. <br>
Risk: Cold outreach can create legal, privacy, deliverability, or brand-risk issues if sent without human review. <br>
Mitigation: Review campaigns against applicable anti-spam, consent, opt-out, privacy, and industry requirements before sending. <br>
