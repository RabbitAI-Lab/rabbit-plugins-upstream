## Description:

Research Fortune company directories and ranking editions and Forbes public billionaire and person records through Crawlora for sourced business comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business researchers and analysts use the skill to compare Fortune ranking editions, assemble sourced company shortlists, and inspect public Forbes wealth profiles with year and methodology context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Queries send the Crawlora API key and requested research parameters to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Protect the API key and submit confidential research inputs only when sharing them with Crawlora is intended.

Risk: Public rankings and wealth profiles may use different years, populations, estimates, and methodologies.

Mitigation: Retain source and edition details, distinguish estimates from reported figures, and avoid treating rankings as proof of investment merit or ownership.
