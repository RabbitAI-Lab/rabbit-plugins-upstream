## Description:

Research Fortune company directories and ranking editions and Forbes public billionaire and person records with Crawlora to build source-attributed business comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business researchers and analysts use this skill to compare specific Fortune ranking editions, research company directories, and examine public Forbes wealth profiles while preserving source and methodology context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crawlora receives an API key and research query parameters.

Mitigation: Keep the key private and appropriately scoped; use only the documented routes.

Risk: Ranking editions, revenue figures, and personal wealth estimates can be misleading when compared without context.

Mitigation: Preserve edition years, source links, metric units, methodology, and uncertainty; do not treat wealth profiles as evidence of investment merit or company revenue.
