## Description: <br>
Tactical negotiation framework based on Chris Voss's "Never Split the Difference." Use when preparing for negotiations, during live negotiation scenarios, analyzing counterpart behavior, crafting responses to difficult conversations, handling objections, salary/contract negotiations, or when asked about negotiation techniques like mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions, or the Ackerman method. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Negotiation coaching can encourage pressure or leverage tactics that may be misused against vulnerable people or sensitive personal situations. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for lawful, consensual, good-faith bargaining, and avoid applying its tactics to exploit distress, private information, employment precarity, legal exposure, medical decisions, or personal crises. <br>
Risk: Advice generated from persuasion frameworks may be incomplete, inappropriate for the relationship, or unsuitable for regulated legal, employment, medical, or crisis contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Review guidance before acting, adapt it to the specific context, and seek qualified human advice for high-stakes or regulated decisions. <br>
