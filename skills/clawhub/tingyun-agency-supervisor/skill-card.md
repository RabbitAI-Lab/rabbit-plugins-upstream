## Description:

Helps brand owners review agency contracts, align KPIs and incentives, verify performance, clarify data ownership, and plan exit handovers with human legal review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External brand owners use this skill to assess ecommerce agency contracts, set measurable KPIs and acceptance checks, and prepare data ownership and exit handover checklists. It provides planning support and checklists; a qualified lawyer should review legal conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contracts and business records may contain confidential or personal information.

Mitigation: Share only the necessary redacted material through an environment approved for confidential information; never paste passwords.

Risk: Checklist output could be mistaken for a legal conclusion.

Mitigation: Use it for planning and issue spotting, and have a qualified lawyer review contract decisions.

Risk: Account changes or customer data exports could expose data or affect account control.

Mitigation: Keep account changes and customer exports under authorized staff control.
