## Description:

Helps brand owners review agency contracts, align performance incentives, verify acceptance criteria, and plan data ownership and handover.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand owners evaluating or managing outsourced e-commerce agencies use this skill to review contract terms, align performance incentives, verify reported results, and plan asset ownership and exit steps. It provides planning checklists rather than legal advice; a qualified lawyer should review contractual conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shared contracts and business records may contain personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Share only relevant clauses and records, redact personal identifiers, signatures, payment details, and unrelated confidential information, use an approved processing environment, and keep passwords out of the conversation.

Risk: Checklist guidance could be mistaken for a legal conclusion or treated as an automatic account handover instruction.

Mitigation: Have a qualified lawyer review contractual conclusions and keep account handover decisions under the user's control.
