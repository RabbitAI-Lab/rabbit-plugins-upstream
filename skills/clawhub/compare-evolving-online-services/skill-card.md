## Description:

Compare fast-changing online services using current, scoped sources for features, pricing, access rules, and policy limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to compare fast-changing online services across features, pricing, access rules, policy limits, and fit for different user priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Comparison results can become inaccurate when online service features, prices, access rules, or policy limits change after retrieval.

Mitigation: Use current first-party sources where possible, record source date and scope for conclusion-driving claims, and mark unresolved or time-sensitive details explicitly.

Risk: Dynamic pages or secondary reports may omit details needed for a reliable comparison.

Mitigation: Prefer official product, help, terms, policy, pricing, or storefront sources, and label secondary or indexed sources when first-party pages do not expose useful text.
