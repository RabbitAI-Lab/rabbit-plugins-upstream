## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to find chiropractor providers, check appointment availability, and create bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Creating a booking sends the user's provider choice, appointment details, name, email, and phone number to Lokuli's external service. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, time slot, name, email, and phone number with the user. <br>
