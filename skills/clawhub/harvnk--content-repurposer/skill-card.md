## Description: <br>
Converts content URLs or pasted text into tailored drafts for X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, newsletters, YouTube Shorts or Reels, and SEO blog posts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[harvnk](https://clawhub.ai/user/harvnk) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Content marketers, founders, agencies, and social media teams use this skill to turn a source article, video, podcast, or pasted text into platform-specific campaign assets. It is useful when one source needs to become multiple polished drafts for publishing workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: URLs or source text may be sent to external transcription, search, or extraction services during repurposing. <br>
Mitigation: Use only content that policy allows to be processed by Supadata and, when enabled, Brave; avoid confidential or client-owned material unless approved. <br>
Risk: Generated marketing copy may contain inaccurate claims, unsuitable tone, or platform guidance that does not match the user's brand or compliance needs. <br>
Mitigation: Review, fact-check, and adapt all drafts before publication, especially pricing, claims, calls to action, hashtags, and SEO content. <br>
