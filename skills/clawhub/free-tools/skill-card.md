## Description: <br>
Helps users plan, evaluate, and scope free marketing tools for lead generation, SEO value, and brand awareness. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and growth practitioners use this skill to choose and plan free tools such as calculators, generators, auditors, testers, and interactive resources that can attract qualified leads or earn links. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may advise collecting emails or other lead information. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear notice, consent where required, retention limits, and privacy or marketing-law review before applying lead-capture recommendations. <br>
Risk: The skill may read a local product-marketing context file if one exists. <br>
Mitigation: Review local marketing context for sensitive information before using it with the agent. <br>
