## Description:

Turns invention materials or an early technical idea into a Chinese-language patent first draft or candidate patent directions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Inventors and R&D teams can turn invention disclosures, technical reports, or an early idea into a patent draft or candidate patent directions for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: API-key storage and network safeguards need review before confidential use.

Mitigation: Keep API keys out of chat and configure PATENTMAX_API_KEY through protected local settings; ask the publisher to enforce HTTPS and restrict authenticated redirects to the intended service.

Risk: Invention materials may contain confidential information and are processed by a third party.

Mitigation: Upload only materials you are authorized to share, and review PatentMax’s privacy policy for its processing and retention terms.
