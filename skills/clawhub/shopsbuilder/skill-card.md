## Description:

Launch and run online stores through the ShopsBuilder MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Store owners and their authorized administrators use the skill to set up and operate ShopsBuilder stores, manage products and inventory, review sales and orders, and connect payment methods.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A clean scan does not establish that the skill is risk-free or suitable for every merchant.

Mitigation: Review the skill and consider whether its access and actions suit the merchant's needs before installing.

Risk: Creating stores, publishing products, and changing prices or stock can affect a merchant's live store.

Mitigation: Show the proposed change and obtain the merchant's confirmation before applying consequential changes; review deletion previews and use their confirmation token only after explicit confirmation.
