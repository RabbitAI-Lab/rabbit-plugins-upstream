## Description:

Provides Chinese-language renovation planning and site-inspection guidance across demolition, utilities, waterproofing, finishing, and acceptance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and renovation planners use this Chinese-language handbook to plan construction stages, spot common workmanship problems, and prepare inspection questions. Its examples draw on Xi'an and Chinese renovation practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsafe or locally inapplicable advice on structural work, electrical or gas systems, waterproofing, and chemical exposure could cause injury or property damage.

Mitigation: Use the guidance for planning only; confirm site conditions, permits, local codes, and protective measures with qualified local professionals before work begins.
