## Description: <br>
多Agent协作的小说创作系统，支持起点中文网、番茄小说、晋江文学城、知乎盐选、豆瓣阅读 5 大平台风格适配，并用 8 个专业角色覆盖从灵感到成品的完整创作流程。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External writers use this skill to plan Chinese web fiction, adapt story structure and style for major publishing platforms, draft chapters, review revisions, and assemble a finished manuscript. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow creates local project files that may contain sensitive personal notes or unpublished story material. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing sensitive or unpublished material unless saving it in generated project files is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording can start a full novel-planning workflow from vague creative prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Use step-by-step mode when the user wants confirmation between stages or before writing larger draft files. <br>
