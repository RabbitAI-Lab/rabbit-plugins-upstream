## Description: <br>
Paper Club Pilot helps research groups prepare for, run, and wrap up lab seminars or reading-club meetings by curating papers, generating discussion materials, capturing authorized Tencent Meeting notes, and distilling follow-up knowledge. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External academic teams and research group facilitators use this skill to prepare weekly paper discussions, capture authorized meeting notes, extract action items, and archive meeting outcomes into an academic knowledge base. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Meeting transcripts, participant-linked action items, and summaries may contain sensitive academic or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for meetings where participants have authorized access to meeting notes and where the group accepts storage in an academic knowledge base. <br>
Risk: Post-use method-tag optimization logging may retain usage labels on the local machine. <br>
Mitigation: Disable the local method-tag optimization logging when the group does not want post-use usage labels retained. <br>
Risk: Meeting capture depends on authorized Tencent Meeting smart notes or transcripts and may be incomplete without that access. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm data authorization before capture and fall back to user-provided notes when meeting transcript access is unavailable. <br>
