## Description:

Transforms robotic AI-generated text into natural human-sounding content by replacing 20+ common AI phrases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, writers, and content teams use this skill to convert formulaic AI-generated wording into plainer, more natural text through phrase-based replacements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private drafts, customer data, unpublished work, or business content could be exposed if sent to the hosted API example.

Mitigation: Use the local phrase-replacement implementation for sensitive content unless the hosted endpoint and its data handling are trusted.

Risk: Humanized wording can obscure AI authorship where disclosure is required.

Mitigation: Keep AI authorship disclosures when policy, contract, or law requires them.

Risk: Pattern-based replacements can change tone or meaning in ways the author did not intend.

Mitigation: Review rewritten text before publication, especially for legal, medical, financial, customer-facing, or brand-sensitive content.
