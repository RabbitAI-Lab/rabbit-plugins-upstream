## Description:

Generates concise two-page pitch materials and optional in-person pitch prompts for meeting livestream hosts or influencers, turning product information into a memorable buyer-facing message, evidence points, and ready-to-say live commerce scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[handsomeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/handsomeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External commerce, brand, and livestream operations users use this skill to prepare short product pitch materials for meetings with influencers or livestream hosts. The skill helps convert detailed product claims, proof points, audience context, and cooperation terms into host-facing messaging, objections, and script structures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create persistent onboarding state and save business materials locally.

Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where local persistence is acceptable, and request a one-off session with no archive when handling confidential product, pricing, or negotiation data.

Risk: Generated pitch materials may include product claims, expert references, medical-adjacent evidence, or detection data that require compliance review before livestream use.

Mitigation: Review proof points and claims before publication, especially where evidence involves medical background, expert endorsement, efficacy claims, or testing data.
