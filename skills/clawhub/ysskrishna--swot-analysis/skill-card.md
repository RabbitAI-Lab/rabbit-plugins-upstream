## Description:

Guides evidence-based SWOT analysis of a team, product, or company and turns prioritized findings into TOWS actions and next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams, business analysts, and decision-makers use this skill to assess internal strengths and weaknesses alongside external opportunities and threats, prioritize findings, and choose concrete actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified competitor or market claims can distort strategic decisions.

Mitigation: Label assumptions and verify external evidence before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Analysis may involve sensitive documents, metrics, or customer feedback.

Mitigation: Provide only information you are comfortable having analyzed.
