## Description:

Use for SWOT analysis, or when the user wants to assess where a team, product or company stands: "strengths and weaknesses", "should we enter this market?", "review our position", "competitor comparison". Turns findings into actions. Skip for detailed planning or pure market sizing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Strategy teams, product managers, and business decision-makers use this skill to assess a product, team, company, competitor, or initiative. It organizes sourced findings into a prioritized SWOT and TOWS actions to inform a decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A missing local reference leaves the security review evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Check the unresolved reference and review the skill before relying on it.

Risk: Unsourced or assumed SWOT items can lead to misleading priorities and actions.

Mitigation: Gather evidence first, cite each item or label it as user stated or assumed, and test the key assumption before acting.
