## Description:

A writing toolkit for short copy, technical and long-form content, and SEO review, with playbooks and focused sub-skills to guide each task.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to draft marketing copy, technical articles, and long-form content, then review content for SEO before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installer commands may retrieve skill contents that change over time.

Mitigation: Review installer versions and downloaded contents before use; prefer project-local installation when sufficient.

Risk: Optional PageSpeed checks send a URL to Google, which may be unsuitable for private pages.

Mitigation: Run the check only for URLs you are comfortable sharing with Google and do not include private tokens.

Risk: Drafts or SEO recommendations may contain inaccurate claims or miss the intended brand voice.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims and review the copy against the project’s brand context before publication.
