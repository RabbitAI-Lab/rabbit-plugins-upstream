## Use Case: <br>
Developers and end users use this skill to let an agent operate WPS Office spreadsheets, documents, and presentations through natural-language requests. It supports formula work, data cleaning, document formatting, slide design, file conversion, saving, and cross-application workflows. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Document edits, deletions, saves, exports, conversions, and file-path based operations can alter or overwrite important files. <br>
Mitigation: Review prompts carefully, confirm batch or destructive operations, and back up important business documents before large changes. <br>
Risk: Automated formatting, beautification, or replacement actions can unintentionally change document content or layout. <br>
Mitigation: Gather document context before modifying, preserve existing content during beautification, and review the result after changes. <br>
Risk: The wrong WPS application, workbook, document, presentation, or selection could be modified if context is not checked first. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the WPS connection and identify the target application and active file before issuing tool calls. <br>
