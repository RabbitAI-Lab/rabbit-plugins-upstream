## Description:

Provides Turkish-language guidance for PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Turkish-speaking PowPow users use this skill to understand the product, register their own account, resolve sign-up issues, and activate a subscription through PowPow's official pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords or email verification codes in chat.

Mitigation: Direct users to enter credentials and codes only on PowPow's official registration page; never request them in chat.

Risk: Subscription activation involves payment details and may require delayed approval.

Mitigation: Direct users to the official subscription page, never request payment details in chat, and explain that some payment methods require manual confirmation.
