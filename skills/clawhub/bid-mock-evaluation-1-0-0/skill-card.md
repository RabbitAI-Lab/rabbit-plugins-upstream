## Description: <br>
Helps bidders analyze bid evaluation tables to run mock scoring, audit response coverage, and review potentially noncompliant evaluation criteria. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External bidders and procurement teams use this skill to self-check bid responses against provided evaluation tables, identify missing evidence or format risks, and prepare reviewable mock evaluation or compliance reports. It is intended for analysis and decision support, not as a substitute for official evaluation results or legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, with guidance tailored to Chinese procurement and bid-evaluation materials. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bid documents, responses, and evaluation tables can contain sensitive commercial or procurement information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only materials needed for the selected mode and use the skill only in environments approved for those documents. <br>
Risk: Procurement compliance conclusions and legal references may be incomplete, outdated, or jurisdiction-specific. <br>
Mitigation: Verify conclusions against current official sources or qualified counsel before filing questions, objections, or complaints. <br>
Risk: Mock scores and coverage findings may be mistaken for official evaluation outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as self-checking aids and review evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty before making bid decisions. <br>
