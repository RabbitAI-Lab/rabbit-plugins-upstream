## Description:

Guides users through PowPow account registration, signup troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

New PowPow users can get step-by-step signup guidance, help resolving registration problems, and an explanation of how to activate an account through a subscription. Users complete registration and payment themselves on PowPow's pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment proof in chat while seeking signup help.

Mitigation: Do not request or collect those details; direct users to complete signup and payment themselves on official PowPow pages.

Risk: Users may mistake registration for completed account activation.

Mitigation: Explain that activation follows subscription payment and that some payment methods require manual confirmation.
