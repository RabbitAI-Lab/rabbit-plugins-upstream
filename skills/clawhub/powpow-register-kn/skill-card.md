## Description:

Guides Kannada-speaking users through PowPow account registration, account activation, common registration issues, and subscription-payment handoff without collecting credentials or completing actions for the user.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External Kannada-speaking PowPow users use this skill for guided product orientation, account registration, registration troubleshooting, and subscription activation. The agent provides step-by-step guidance while the user completes registration and payment directly on PowPow web or app surfaces.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could expose passwords, verification codes, or payment evidence in chat while asking for registration help.

Mitigation: Tell users to keep passwords, email codes, and payment evidence out of chat and complete sensitive steps only on the verified PowPow site or app.

Risk: Users could follow a spoofed registration or payment page instead of the official PowPow surface.

Mitigation: Direct users to verify the PowPow URL and terms before registering or paying, and link only to the official PowPow registration and subscription pages.
