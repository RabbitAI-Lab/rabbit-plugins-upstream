## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn early product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support before heavy investment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building by converting rough concepts into repeatable validation workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, and user-facing positioning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad product or startup planning language when product validation planning was not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when validation planning is the user's actual goal, and confirm the outcome, constraints, inputs, and success criteria before producing the artifact. <br>
