## Description:

Provides structured question templates for QA teams to clarify incomplete requirements, confirm boundaries, uncover business rules, and request technical details before designing tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, testers, and developers use this skill when requirement documents are incomplete or product and engineering details need clarification before test design. It helps produce targeted question lists, exploration areas, and clarification needs for requirement review, test case review, bug reporting, and post-incident review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate for broad clarification requests and may prompt users to discuss customer, payment, identity, or production-sensitive details.

Mitigation: Use it for QA or requirements discussions and mask real customer, payment, identity, financial, or production data before sharing context.
