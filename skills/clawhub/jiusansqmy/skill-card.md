## Description:

九三学社社情民意信息写作技能。当用户需要撰写社情民意信息、社情民意报告、参政议政建言材料、反映社情民意稿件，或提及"九三学社""社情民意""建言献策""参政议政"等关键词时触发。该技能提供标准格式规范、内容结构要求和范文参考，确保产出的社情民意信息符合九三学社官方行文标准。

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to draft Chinese-language Jiusan Society social-conditions and public-opinion reports, policy advice materials, and related consultative documents. It helps structure titles, author attribution, abstracts, problem analysis, recommendations, citations, and formatting checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad mentions of Jiusan Society, social conditions, policy advice, or consultative writing and produce a formal official style when the user only wants general discussion.

Mitigation: Give explicit instructions when a different tone, format, or non-drafting response is desired.

Risk: Drafts that include data, cases, or citations can become misleading if the underlying sources are not traceable.

Mitigation: Require source checks for all factual claims and remove or replace any data or case reference that cannot be verified.
