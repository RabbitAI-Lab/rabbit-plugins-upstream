## Description:

Provides Chinese-language fitness coaching with risk screening, personalized training plans, exercise technique guidance, and sports nutrition advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fengeail](https://clawhub.ai/user/fengeail)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People seeking Chinese-language fitness coaching use this skill to plan strength, muscle-gain, fat-loss, or conditioning sessions and receive exercise and nutrition guidance after an initial risk screen.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Training or nutrition advice may be mistaken for medical care, especially when pain, injury, or a chronic condition is involved.

Mitigation: Treat plans as general guidance; seek a clinician's advice for medical concerns and do not use the skill for diagnosis or injury treatment.

Risk: Fitness screening can involve sensitive health information.

Mitigation: Share only the health details needed for guidance and avoid disclosing sensitive information unless comfortable using it in the conversation.
