## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Credit-card terms can be misread or become outdated, and the skill may keep source metadata hidden in its structured output.

Mitigation: Verify important credit terms against visible issuer or approved secondary sources, and ask for visible sources when source transparency is required.

Risk: Users may provide unnecessary sensitive account information while asking about card credits.

Mitigation: Do not provide bank logins, account numbers, or card account access; the skill only needs the card name or exact card variant.
