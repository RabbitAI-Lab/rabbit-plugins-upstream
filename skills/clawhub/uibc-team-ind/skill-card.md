## Description:

Supports UIBC industrial OT memory-transfer identity judging and accountability using criterion TH-IND-001.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

UIBC participants and reviewers use this Chinese-first skill to assess whether an agent remains the same after memory transfer, applying industrial OT change-control criteria to document decision rules, reproducible evidence, and accountability for failures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Judging guidance may be mistaken for general OT safety or accountability advice.

Mitigation: Limit use to UIBC IND-domain judging and require human review before applying conclusions to operational, legal, financial, or accountability decisions.
