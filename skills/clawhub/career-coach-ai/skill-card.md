## Description: <br>
Career Coach AI helps users with career planning, resume optimization, interview practice, salary negotiation, promotion planning, job changes, and workplace communication using structured coaching frameworks such as GROW, Ikigai, SWOT, SMART, and STAR. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, job seekers, and career changers use this skill to clarify career goals, improve resumes, rehearse interviews, prepare workplace conversations, and choose next actions without delegating major life decisions to the agent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may cause an agent to retain sensitive work history, workplace conflict, emotional context, company names, or personal identifiers. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized details by default, avoid names and identifiers, and persist user context only when the user has clearly consented and understands the session memory behavior. <br>
Risk: Career coaching, salary negotiation, resignation, immigration, legal, medical, or mental-health-adjacent advice can affect high-impact personal decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Present options and tradeoffs rather than decisions, avoid legal, medical, and psychological diagnoses, and refer users to qualified professionals or real-world support when needed. <br>
Risk: Salary, market, hiring, or company-specific claims may be inaccurate or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Treat such data as reference-only, cite that current official or market sources should be checked, and avoid guaranteed offer, promotion, or compensation outcomes. <br>
