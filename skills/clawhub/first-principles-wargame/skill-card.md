## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language strategic decision wargame skill that uses a first-principles, three-layer, nine-question framework to stress-test business directions, strategic moves, product lines, and transformations with premortems, timing analysis, and exit conditions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[clongin](https://clawhub.ai/user/clongin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, founders, operators, and strategy teams use this skill to challenge proposed business directions or strategic actions, surface blind spots, and produce a decision card with assumptions, risks, next steps, milestones, and review timing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate from broad strategy-planning phrases and shift a normal planning conversation into a forceful strategic review posture. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for strategic wargames and confirm that the user wants this review style before applying it. <br>
Risk: Decision cards are advisory and can be misleading when company context, constraints, or assumptions are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Collect the required context first, document assumptions, and have accountable humans review outputs before making commercial decisions. <br>
