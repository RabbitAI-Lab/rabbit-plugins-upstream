## Description: <br>
Adapt reviewed WeChat content into WeChat inline-card requirements, Moments copy, community share text, archive assets, and image-pipeline handoffs while preserving the source article's thesis and approved publish metadata. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators and publishing teams use this skill after a WeChat article or publish package exists to prepare inline-card copy, insertion notes, Moments copy, community share copy, and handoffs to the shared card rendering pipeline. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Publish-ready workflows may send article drafts or source material to image-rendering tools or services. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the rendering tools and processing path before using publish_ready mode, and avoid providing private drafts or sensitive source material unless that processing is acceptable. <br>
