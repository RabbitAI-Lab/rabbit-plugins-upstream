## Description:

Builds a post-ready LinkedIn audience persona using the user's Taplio audience settings and real-post research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External LinkedIn creators and marketers use this skill to define one specific target-audience persona and generate 10 post topics, grounded in their Taplio audience settings and relevant LinkedIn posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audience-role and pain-point search terms are sent to Taplio.

Mitigation: Keep those terms generic or anonymized, avoid confidential customer information, and review connector permissions before connecting.
