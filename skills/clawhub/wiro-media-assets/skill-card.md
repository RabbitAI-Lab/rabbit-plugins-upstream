## Description:

Make media assets for a software project with Wiro AI models and put them where the project expects them, sized and wired in, for example a hero image, an Open Graph or social card, a favicon and app icons, product shots, illustrations, a short looping video, a voice-over, background music or a 3D model. Use when the user wants images, icons, video or audio created for their website, app, extension, docs or game, or asks to replace a placeholder asset.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
