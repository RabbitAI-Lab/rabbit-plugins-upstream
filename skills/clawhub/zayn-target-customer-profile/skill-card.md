## Description:

根据目标市场、产品或服务、我方供应能力和历史业务证据，建立可用于搜索与筛选的具体目标客户画像；输出优先与次优客户类型、采购场景、识别关键词、高低匹配信号、排除条件和待确认问题，不替代已有客户事实画像。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business development, sales, and market-research users apply this skill to turn a target market, offering, supplier capabilities, and available business evidence into searchable target-customer profiles. It helps identify priority and secondary customer types, buying scenarios, matching signals, exclusion criteria, and open validation questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce low-confidence customer-profile assumptions when historical evidence is missing.

Mitigation: Mark assumptions as unverified, confirm them with reliable business evidence, and avoid treating generated profiles as established customer facts.

Risk: Inputs may include sensitive customer or opportunity information.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential customer data unless it is appropriate for the current agent session and applicable data-handling rules.
