## Description:

Turn text into natural speech with Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD/Turbo, Qwen3 TTS, Seed Speech and Grok TTS via the PopNow API: many languages and voices, exact USD quote first. Use when the user needs a voiceover, narration, podcast read, audiobook sample or spoken audio from text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and content creators use the skill to turn scripts into voiceovers, narration, podcast reads, or audiobook samples with supported speech models, after reviewing an exact cost quote.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Speech generation is paid and may incur charges.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key restricted to speech models with a spending cap, and approve the exact quote before each generation.

Risk: Text and reference media URLs are sent to PopNow and the generation provider.

Mitigation: Send only material you are comfortable sharing and review PopNow’s privacy policy.
