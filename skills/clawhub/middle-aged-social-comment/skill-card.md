## Description: <br>
A Chinese writing skill for creating WeChat public-account social commentary for readers aged 30-50, covering topic discovery, material breakdown, quote extraction, drafting, and revision. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[songhonglei](https://clawhub.ai/user/songhonglei) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to develop Chinese social-commentary articles for WeChat public accounts aimed at middle-aged readers. It supports the workflow from recent-topic selection through structured source material, concise pull quotes, full drafts, revision, and publishing suggestions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may apply a specific middle-aged-reader WeChat public-account voice when invoked by broad commentary-writing prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated topic framing, tone, and draft style before use, and ask for a different style when the target audience or channel does not match. <br>
