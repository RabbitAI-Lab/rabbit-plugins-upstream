## Description:

A Chinese WeChat public-account writing assistant that helps develop social commentary for readers aged 30-50, from trend discovery and topic selection through material breakdown, quotable lines, drafting, and revision.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[songhonglei](https://clawhub.ai/user/songhonglei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External content creators and social-commentary writers use this skill to plan, draft, and revise WeChat public-account articles for middle-aged readers. It supports topic discovery, structured analysis, quote extraction, long-form drafting, ending variants, and final quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recent-trend searches can introduce time-sensitive or inaccurate material into generated commentary.

Mitigation: Verify current-event claims and source-sensitive assertions before publication.

Risk: Broad commentary-writing prompts may activate this strongly opinionated WeChat public-account style even when the user wanted a different voice.

Mitigation: Use explicit skill routing or confirm the intended audience, channel, and tone before drafting.
