## Description:

Guides agents to generate quick, low-cost photos with Pruna's hosted p-image model for mood boards, bulk panels, and fast iterations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, designers, and developers use this skill for quick photo generation, mood boards, bulk panels, and visual iterations when precise photorealism or readable in-image text is not required.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image prompts are sent to Pruna's hosted API.

Mitigation: Review prompts for sensitive information and approve use of Pruna's service before sending them.

Risk: Recommended companion-skill installs use automatic confirmation and are not pinned to immutable versions.

Mitigation: Review each companion skill before installation and pin a reviewed version when possible.
