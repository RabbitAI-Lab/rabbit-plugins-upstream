## Description: <br>
Guides customer quality and market quality teams through CSAT, NPS, and CES survey design and produces professional questionnaire and analysis report documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Customer quality managers and market quality teams use this skill to design structured satisfaction surveys and analyze real response data for NPS, CSAT, CES, dimensional trends, and action priorities. It is intended for annual or semiannual satisfaction programs, post-launch checks, complaint closure follow-up, and customer quality performance reporting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated survey reports may write local Markdown and text files into an unintended location. <br>
Mitigation: Use an explicit output directory for generated survey reports. <br>
Risk: Survey responses may contain customer or business-sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only survey data that is appropriate to process in the active workspace. <br>
Risk: Satisfaction scores, NPS, and dimension findings can be misleading if based on placeholders or fabricated data. <br>
Mitigation: Use real response data for analysis and clearly mark unavailable sample sizes, NPS values, or dimension scores as pending. <br>
