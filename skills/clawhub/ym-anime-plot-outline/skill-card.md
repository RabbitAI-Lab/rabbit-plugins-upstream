## Description:

Outlines episodic anime-style plots with per-episode summaries, required hooks, a foreshadowing ledger, and closure checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liuyuming0823](https://clawhub.ai/user/liuyuming0823)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, creators, and content teams use this skill to turn a theme, protagonist setup, series length, or setting card into an anime-style season outline with structured pacing and tracked foreshadowing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trigger and language behavior may need review for deployments that require strict multilingual activation.

Mitigation: Review the Chinese-first trigger wording and activation scope before deployment in multilingual or tightly controlled environments.

Risk: Generated outlines can include default assumptions when the user omits genre, protagonist, or episode count.

Mitigation: Review the listed assumptions and replace them with confirmed story requirements before using the outline for scripts, storyboards, or collaboration.
