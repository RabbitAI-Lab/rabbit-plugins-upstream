## Description:

Guides an agent through creating a personalized birthday video from photos, a name song, and personal details, then exporting a shareable MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People use this skill to make a personalized birthday video or invitation for a friend, family member, partner, or pet, with options such as a name song, animated photo, or milestone montage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creating a video may send identifiable photos, names, and personal details to external AI services.

Mitigation: Before generation, explain the external processing and get the photo subject's agreement; share only material the sender is comfortable sending.

Risk: The scanner reports that the installer command is unpinned, and its partial scan leaves evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the install command and verify the trusted release or source before running it.
