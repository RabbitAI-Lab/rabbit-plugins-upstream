## Description:

Creates personalized AI birthday videos from a name, age, personal details, and photos, with an optional custom song and formats for sharing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People making a birthday greeting for a friend, family member, or pet can use the skill to create a personalized video and prepare it for messaging or a party screen.
