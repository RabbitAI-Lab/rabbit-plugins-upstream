## Description:

Creates personalized birthday videos, songs, montages, pet greetings, and animated invitations from personal details and photos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

People use this skill to create personalized birthday videos or invitations for friends, family, and pets. It guides an agent through collecting details, generating media, and assembling a shareable video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creating a video may involve sharing personal photos and details with Varg and its generation providers.

Mitigation: Use only material you are authorized to share, check provider privacy terms before uploading sensitive content, and keep media private.

Risk: Generation can incur costs, and assembly can overwrite an existing output file.

Mitigation: Review generation costs before proceeding and choose a new output filename.

Risk: Animating a child's real photo is disallowed by the skill's guidance.

Mitigation: Use drawings, toys, a pet, or an invented cartoon character instead, and do not animate the child's real photo.
