## Description:

Build evidence-backed TikTok trend briefs from current feeds, Creative Center rankings, hashtag data, and Explore categories through Crawlora. Use for trend discovery, country/period comparisons, and topic or hashtag momentum; use tiktok-ad-research for paid-ad analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and researchers can use this skill to prepare dated, sample-aware briefs on TikTok topics or hashtags across countries and trend surfaces. It helps compare observed rankings and activity while recording filters, sample size, retrieval time, and coverage caveats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and filters are sent to Crawlora, which may charge API credits.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated Crawlora key and keep confidential information out of queries.

Risk: Partial samples and different TikTok surfaces can be mistaken for comprehensive or comparable trend measurements.

Mitigation: Record retrieval time, sample size, surface, and filters; compare only matching scopes and describe findings as observations from the sample.
