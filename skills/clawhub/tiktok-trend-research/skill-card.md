## Description:

Builds evidence-backed TikTok trend briefs from current feeds, Creative Center rankings, hashtag data, and Explore categories through Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, creators, and researchers use this skill to find and compare TikTok trend signals by topic, hashtag, category, country, period, or feed surface. It helps them prepare dated, sample-aware briefs while keeping rankings and feeds distinct.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms are sent to Crawlora, and API requests can consume credits.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated Crawlora API key, avoid confidential search terms, and review Crawlora's billing model.
