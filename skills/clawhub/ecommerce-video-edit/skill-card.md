## Description:

Turn product footage into vertical ecommerce ads with FFmpeg, including hook variants, captions, calls to action, and feed cuts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and ecommerce marketers use this skill to turn existing product, unboxing, or creator footage into short vertical ads for TikTok, Reels, and Meta, then prepare hook variants for testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hosted processing may send footage to a third party, and its access and retention practices are not established in the evidence.

Mitigation: Use local FFmpeg for sensitive footage; review the processor's access and retention practices and avoid uploading footage unless the disclosure is approved.

Risk: Unpinned packages can change, and the security scan did not resolve all local references.

Mitigation: Pin packages to reviewed versions and inspect and scan resolved dependencies before use.
