## Description:

Turns existing product footage into vertical ecommerce ads with FFmpeg, including hook variants, captions, calls to action, and platform-ready exports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, ecommerce marketers, and editors use this skill to turn existing product footage into short-form ads and test alternate hooks for TikTok, Reels, and Meta placements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional cloud processing involves sharing media URLs with varg and may incur credits.

Mitigation: Use local FFmpeg for confidential footage unless sharing is approved and the data-handling terms and costs are acceptable.

Risk: Editing commands can overwrite files or modify the wrong footage if paths are mistaken.

Mitigation: Use a trusted FFmpeg installation, keep original footage separate, and direct outputs to separate paths.
