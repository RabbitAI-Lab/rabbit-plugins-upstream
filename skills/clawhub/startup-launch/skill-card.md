## Description: <br>
Startup Launch provides an hour-by-hour launch-day checklist covering timing, channel sequencing, real-time monitoring, crisis response, and post-launch momentum. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders and launch teams use this skill to plan and operate a startup launch from final pre-launch checks through the first 72 hours after release. It is oriented toward dev, open-source, and B2B SaaS launches, with notes that consumer products need adapted metrics and channels. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Public-posting and outreach suggestions may conflict with a team's brand voice, community rules, or anti-spam expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt outreach plans before publishing or contacting communities. <br>
Risk: Launch tactics and crisis-response steps may be a poor fit for consumer products or non-B2B markets. <br>
Mitigation: Adjust metrics, channels, and readiness criteria for the product category before using the checklist. <br>
