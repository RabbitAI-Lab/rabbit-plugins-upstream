## Description:

Audits public websites for SEO/AEO opportunities and provides sourced content drafts for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Business owners and marketers use this skill to request public website SEO/AEO audits and review sourced findings, priorities, and content drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit requests and public-site details are processed and stored by an external service.

Mitigation: Use public business websites and avoid submitting sensitive private business data unless you intend that service to process it.

Risk: Generated contacts and content drafts may need checking before use.

Mitigation: Review sourced contacts and drafts manually before relying on or publishing them.
