## Description:

Findable helps audit public websites for SEO/AEO and prepare sourced content drafts for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Website owners, marketers, and agencies use this skill to request public-site SEO/AEO audits, review sourced findings and competitor comparisons, and consider draft content and prioritized improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Website targets, optional provider reports, and audit context are sent to Findable's external service; sensitive client data may be exposed if supplied.

Mitigation: Share only public targets and intended audit context; do not provide private metrics or sensitive client data unless you intend Findable to process it.
