## Description: <br>
Decode HOA covenants (CC&Rs) and fee structures before purchase, producing a plain-language restriction review, special-assessment exposure analysis, enforcement and fine mechanics, and records-to-request checklist. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and real-estate buyers use this skill to understand HOA rules, financial exposure, enforcement paths, and document gaps before buying into an HOA-governed property. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the HOA review as legal or financial advice for a purchase decision. <br>
Mitigation: Present the output as a plain-language review and advise users to verify important decisions with a qualified local professional. <br>
Risk: HOA documents may include personal housing plans or sensitive purchase details. <br>
Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when comfortable sharing HOA documents and related plans with the agent using the skill. <br>
Risk: The analysis can be incomplete when reserve studies, budgets, minutes, or assessment records are not provided. <br>
Mitigation: Identify missing records explicitly and request the reserve study, board minutes, budget, insurance, litigation, assessment, and rental-cap records before relying on the review. <br>
