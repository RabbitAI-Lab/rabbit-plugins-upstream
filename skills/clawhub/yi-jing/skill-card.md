Yi Jing helps agents provide entertainment-oriented I Ching divination, hexagram interpretation, Meihua Yi number casting, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Bazi, Qimen Dunjia, and classical Chinese reference lookups with plain-language explanations. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agent builders use this skill for entertainment divination workflows, including casting and interpreting hexagrams, answering fortune-telling prompts, and consulting bundled classical Chinese reference material. It should not be used as a basis for health, emergency, mental-health, pregnancy, financial, legal, or other consequential decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill mixes entertainment divination with actionable medical and ritual claims that could mislead users. <br>
Mitigation: Frame outputs as entertainment or cultural reference material and avoid using them for health, emergency, mental-health, pregnancy, financial, legal, or other consequential decisions. <br>
Risk: Some workflows can involve real names, birth times, and other personal details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing real names or unnecessary birth details; use minimal or approximate information where possible. <br>
Risk: Users may treat ritual, folk-medical, or occult recommendations as a substitute for professional help. <br>
Mitigation: Direct users to licensed professional support for real-world medical, legal, financial, safety, or mental-health problems. <br>
