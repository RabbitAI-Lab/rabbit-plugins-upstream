## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-HIVE API credentials may be stored locally or supplied during command execution.

Mitigation: Use only credentials you are authorized to use, keep local config private, avoid committing keys, and rotate or revoke keys when access changes.

Risk: Media files supplied by the user may be uploaded to AI-HIVE or object storage and generated results may be downloaded locally.

Mitigation: Upload only media you are authorized to share and review generated files before reuse or publication.

Risk: Generation jobs may incur costs or duplicate charges if submitted repeatedly.

Mitigation: Review model pricing snapshots before submission, preserve task IDs, and query existing tasks after timeouts instead of immediately resubmitting.
