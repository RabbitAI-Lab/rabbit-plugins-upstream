## Description:

Turn rough creative ideas into structured English or Chinese motion prompts, reference bindings, shot sequences, and focused debugging loops.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn rough video ideas into structured prompts, shot plans, reference bindings, continuity constraints, and focused revision moves for text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video, and video-to-video workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional MCP uses a third-party package that is not required for the text workflow.

Mitigation: Review and trust the referenced package before enabling it, and keep the MCP tool exposure limited to the four disclosed helpers.
