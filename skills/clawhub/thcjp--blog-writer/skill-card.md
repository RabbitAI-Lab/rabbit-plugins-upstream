## Description: <br>
Blog Writer helps an agent draft opinionated, conversational blog posts from research material, calibrate to prior examples, publish drafts to a notes database, and maintain a finalized example library. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External writers and content teams use this skill to turn a topic, angle, and research materials into a blog draft that matches a specified personal style. The skill also supports draft publishing to a configured notes database and archiving approved final posts as reusable examples. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Unfinished drafts, research notes, links, or internal opinions may be posted to a configured external notes database. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to obtain explicit confirmation before any publish action and avoid using sensitive material unless posting to that database is acceptable. <br>
Risk: The publishing workflow may run before the user considers the draft final. <br>
Mitigation: Keep a review checkpoint between draft generation and publishing, especially for commercial or confidential content. <br>
