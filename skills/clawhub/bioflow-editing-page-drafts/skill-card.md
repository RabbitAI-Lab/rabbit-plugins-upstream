## Description:

Edits BioFlow link-in-bio page drafts by creating pages, changing titles and blocks, and reordering content without publishing live pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

BioFlow page owners and their assistants use this skill to create or revise link-in-bio page drafts, including links, images, products, signups, bookings, titles, and block order.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Removing blocks or overwriting draft fields can discard existing draft content.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes and explicitly confirm removals or overwrites before saving.
