## Description:

Researches user-provided target accounts to prioritize outreach with dated sources, relevant contacts, checked work emails, and opening questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scrollport](https://clawhub.ai/user/scrollport)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to assess a supplied list of target companies using fit and recent public signals, identify potential work contacts, and prepare opening questions for outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A contact-verification exception could leave some contact details or email status unreliable.

Mitigation: Review the person's current role and verify contact details independently before using them.

Risk: Account and contact data may be disclosed to third-party research providers.

Mitigation: Review provider disclosures and share only account and contact information you are comfortable sending to those services.
