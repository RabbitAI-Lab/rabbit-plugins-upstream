## Description:

Research My Accounts. Paste or attach the companies you are considering and learn which to contact now, why (with dated, linked sources) and whom, with a work email checked the same day and an opening question for each. Use when someone has a list of companies, websites or target accounts and asks which to prioritise, why now, who to contact, or for account research. If they want new companies found for them, use Find Leads (lead-list) instead.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales teams use this skill to prioritize a list of companies using fit and recent public signals, then identify a relevant work contact and prepare a sourced opening question. It returns company research for the user to review before deciding whether to contact anyone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company and contact lookup information may be shared with external providers.

Mitigation: Review the planned research and provider use before approval, and share only account information you are comfortable sending to those services.

Risk: A reused email check may be up to 30 days old.

Mitigation: Check the verification date before outreach and recheck an address when current status matters.
