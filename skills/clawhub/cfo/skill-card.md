## Description: <br>
Acts as a CFO advisor for startup finance decisions, including cash forecasting, runway and burn analysis, monthly close, fundraising, debt, equity, revenue, board reporting, and diligence preparation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders, executives, and finance operators use this skill to reason about startup cash, forecasts, financing, controls, board reporting, deal terms, and diligence. It provides advice, calculations, drafts, checklists, and escalation guidance while leaving legal, tax, payment, employment, debt, fundraising, and board decisions to humans and qualified professionals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Local CFO memory may contain sensitive company context if users store more than preferences and high-level context. <br>
Mitigation: Keep secrets, account details, tax IDs, raw bank or payroll exports, employee-level compensation, and legally sensitive decisions out of local memory. <br>
Risk: Financial, tax, legal, employment, payment, debt, fundraising, or board guidance could be misapplied as a final decision. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as advice and drafting support only; route major actions and regulated decisions to humans and qualified professionals. <br>
Risk: Proactive runway or cost-cut guidance can influence consequential staffing, financing, or operating decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review cash, legal, tax, employment, and payment impacts before acting, and require human approval for irreversible or high-impact choices. <br>
