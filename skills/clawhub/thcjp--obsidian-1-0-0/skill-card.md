## Description: <br>
Work with Obsidian vaults as plain Markdown notes and automate note search, creation, movement, deletion, and direct editing through obsidian-cli. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, productivity users, and agent operators use this skill to work with local Obsidian vaults, locate active vaults, and manage Markdown notes through obsidian-cli or direct file edits. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Delete or bulk-edit actions can affect the wrong note or vault if paths are assumed. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the exact vault and note paths, keep backups or sync history available, and preview search results before destructive changes. <br>
