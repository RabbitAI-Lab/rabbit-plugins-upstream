## Description:

Helps individual marketplace sellers estimate used-item prices, screen buyer messages for scam signals, and prepare safer meetups and shipments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Individual sellers use this skill to set starting price bands, check buyer messages for common scam patterns, and plan safer in-person or shipped sales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated prices and scam scores may not reflect a specific buyer or local market.

Mitigation: Treat results as guidance, check comparable completed sales, and use independent judgment before proceeding.

Risk: A suggested sale or payment arrangement may conflict with marketplace or payment-platform rules.

Mitigation: Follow the applicable marketplace and payment-platform rules and confirm payment independently before shipping.
