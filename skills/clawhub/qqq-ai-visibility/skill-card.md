## Description:

Checks a public website's AI crawler readiness and retrieves its latest public report on mentions and citations in AI search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[meelis545](https://clawhub.ai/user/meelis545)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Website owners and marketers use this skill to check a public domain's technical AI readiness and review any existing public report on whether AI search answers mention their company or cite its website.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Domains or URLs submitted for checks are sent to qqq.ee, which may retrieve public website data.

Mitigation: Check only public websites you are comfortable sharing with this external service.

Risk: Technical readiness could be mistaken for evidence that AI assistants mention or cite the website.

Mitigation: Keep readiness, mentions, and citations distinct; attribute report figures to the report date.
