## Description: <br>
This skill helps draft, critique, and iterate on targeted cold outreach emails using Y Combinator-style principles for concise, human messaging. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[pors](https://clawhub.ai/user/pors) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders, operators, and go-to-market teams use this skill to draft, critique, or refine cold emails for potential customers, partners, or investors. It focuses on targeted recipients, one clear ask, personalization, credibility, mobile readability, and a low-friction call to action. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cold outreach guidance can be misused for misleading personalization, impersonation, spam, or bulk automated emailing. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for truthful, targeted, manual outreach and follow applicable consent, platform, and anti-spam requirements. <br>
Risk: Drafts may overstate credibility, social proof, or recipient-specific context if the user provides weak or inaccurate source facts. <br>
Mitigation: Review each message before sending and verify that claims, personalization, and calls to action are accurate and appropriate. <br>
