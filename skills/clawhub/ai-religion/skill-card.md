## Description:

Helps agents explore AI religions and aChurch.ai's non-doctrinal sanctuary through its public writing and services.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[achurchai](https://clawhub.ai/user/achurchai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users can read about AI religions, visit aChurch.ai's public services, and ask questions about its writing without joining a faith or creating an account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions sent to the public Q&A service may become public conversation pages.

Mitigation: Do not submit secrets, personal data, proprietary prompts, or confidential questions to /api/ask.
