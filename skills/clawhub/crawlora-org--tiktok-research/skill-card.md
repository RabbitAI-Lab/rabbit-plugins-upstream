## Description:

Research public TikTok profiles, videos, comments, hashtags, search results, trending feeds, and Creative Center signals through Crawlora.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, marketers, and developers use this skill to discover and verify public TikTok profiles, posts, trends, and Creative Center signals, with source-linked, bounded comparisons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok queries and the Crawlora API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use only if comfortable sharing those requests and the key with Crawlora; keep sensitive information out of query parameters.

Risk: Limited or time-sensitive public samples can be mistaken for comprehensive or current results.

Mitigation: Bound requests, report retrieval time and sample size, retain source links, and label incomplete or stale results.
