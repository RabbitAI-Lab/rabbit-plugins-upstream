## Description:

This skill helps agents answer AI/ML medical-device regulatory questions across FDA, EU MDR and AI Act, and China NMPA pathways, including classification, submission paths, clinical evidence, lifecycle change control, cost and timeline estimates, and parallel market-entry strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Regulatory engineers, medical-device R&D teams, and international business leads use this skill to compare AI/ML medical-device classification, evidence, registration, and change-management expectations across the United States, European Union, and China. It can support direct question answering or structured local toolkit commands for classification, pathway, estimate, change-trigger, and market-comparison tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory pathways, effective dates, fee schedules, and clinical-evidence expectations can change after the skill's stated review date.

Mitigation: Verify submission-critical claims against current FDA, EU, and NMPA regulator sources before acting.

Risk: Classification, pathway, cost, and timeline outputs are reference guidance rather than legal or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for planning support and have qualified regulatory counsel or specialists review formal submission decisions.
