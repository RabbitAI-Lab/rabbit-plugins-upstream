## Description:

A practical guide to getting AI/ML medical devices cleared in the US, EU and China, from pathway selection and classification through clinical evidence and change control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory engineers, R&D teams, and international business leads use the skill to plan AI/ML medical-device classification, submission pathways, clinical evidence, and algorithm change control across the United States, European Union, and China.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, European Union, and China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory pathways, deadlines, estimates, and data requirements can change or may not fit a specific device or submission.

Mitigation: Use results as preliminary planning guidance; verify consequential decisions against current official regulator sources and qualified regulatory advice.

Risk: The security assessment is based on static inspection, its scores are publisher-generated, and the ownership manifest needs its SKILL.md hash updated.

Mitigation: Review the current package and reconcile its manifest before relying on the bundled audit or using it in a production environment.
