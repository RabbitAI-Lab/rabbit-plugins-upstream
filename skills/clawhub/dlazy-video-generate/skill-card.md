## Description:

Generates videos from text or image prompts by selecting a suitable dLazy model, with support for frame-guided generation and lip sync.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use the skill to generate video from prompts or reference media, including image animation, frame-guided clips, and lip-synced video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Risk: The security review was partial, with some referenced paths unresolved.
