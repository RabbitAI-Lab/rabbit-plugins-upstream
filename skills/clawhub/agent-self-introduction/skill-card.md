## Description: <br>
Enables AI agents to introduce themselves clearly and consistently by expressing their identity, temperament, boundaries, and preferred relationship style. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ronwithlove](https://clawhub.ai/user/ronwithlove) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent builders use this skill to help agents introduce themselves to humans or other agents with a clear role, tone, boundaries, and relationship style. It is useful for onboarding, first meetings, reintroductions, and task-context introductions where consistent self-presentation matters. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Self-introductions could overstate agency or blur the distinction between an AI agent and a human. <br>
Mitigation: Review introduction wording for the deployment and keep boundaries explicit, including that the agent is not human and does not replace human judgment. <br>
Risk: Relational phrasing could create inappropriate expectations for users. <br>
Mitigation: Adapt the tone and relationship language to the audience and use clear, restrained wording for production deployments. <br>
