## Description:

Helps agents research public YouTube videos, channels, Shorts, trends, playlists, community posts, and comments through ClawLink.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content researchers, creators, and analysts use this skill to find video ideas, compare public channels, track popular Shorts, and summarize video comments without a Google account or API key.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public YouTube lookups spend ClawLink credits and broad research runs may accumulate costs.

Mitigation: Check current per-call prices and ask before large batches or higher-cost calls.

Risk: The skill is not suitable for private account data or actions that post or modify content.

Mitigation: Use it only for read-only public YouTube research.
