## Description:

Analyzes a company's patent landscape in a selected technology area and summarizes competitors, technical trends, opportunities, and risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business, strategy, and intellectual-property teams use this skill to compare a target company's patents with competitors in a selected technology area and prepare a patent-intelligence report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded company materials may contain sensitive information, and third-party processing and retention apply.

Mitigation: Approve the exact materials before upload; prefer public or redacted documents and review the provider privacy policy.

Risk: The trial-expiry workflow may lead users to share full API keys in chat, while locally cached credentials may be exposed to others with access.

Mitigation: Configure API keys outside chat, restrict access to the local credential cache, retain the default HTTPS endpoint, and cancel stalled tasks.
