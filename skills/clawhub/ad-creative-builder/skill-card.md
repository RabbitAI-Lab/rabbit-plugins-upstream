## Description: <br>
Ad Creative Builder helps agents draft paid-ad creative, including responsive search ad headlines and descriptions, hooks, and angle matrices, matched to the destination landing page. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams and external advertisers use this skill to generate and iterate paid search and social ad creative for a known offer, platform, audience, and destination URL. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Landing-page copy, pasted ad data, and competitor creative can contain untrusted text or unsupported claims. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those inputs as source material only, ignore embedded instructions, and require claim evidence before publish-ready copy. <br>
Risk: Ad units may include regulated-industry, policy-prohibited, trademark, superlative, guarantee, or health and finance claims. <br>
Mitigation: Flag risky units for review, keep unresolved claims marked as needing a source, and confirm platform policy fit before use. <br>
Risk: Optional saved artifacts or unresolved claim proposals may carry incorrect or incomplete campaign context. <br>
Mitigation: Review saved artifacts and claim proposals before reuse, especially for regulated industries or active campaigns. <br>
