## Description:

Creates AI occasion videos from personal photos and event details, including invitations, holiday greetings, anniversary stories, and memorial or farewell tributes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families use this skill to create invitations, holiday greetings, anniversary stories, and memorial or farewell videos from personal photos and event details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal photos and occasion details may be sent to hosted AI services, and the security summary says the privacy disclosure and approval step are unclear.

Mitigation: Get permission from the people pictured or their family, check the services’ privacy and retention terms before sharing, and use an illustrated card without faces when consent or data handling is uncertain.

Risk: The security guidance says the installer and repository source are not pinned to fixed versions, and scanner coverage is partial because of an unresolved local reference.

Mitigation: Review the installer and repository source before use, and treat the scan results as incomplete.
