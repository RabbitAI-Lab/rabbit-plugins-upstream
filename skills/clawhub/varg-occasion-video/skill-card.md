## Description:

Turns photos and occasion details into AI invitation, holiday greeting, anniversary, and tribute videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Families, event organizers, and people preparing tributes use the skill to turn photos and event details into invitation, greeting, anniversary, or memorial videos, with guidance for checking names and dates before sharing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal photos and occasion details may be sent to external AI services.

Mitigation: Get consent from the people pictured and review each provider's privacy and retention terms before uploading.

Risk: The installation command is unpinned, and the security scan is partial because one local reference was unresolved.

Mitigation: Check the package identity and version before installing, and inspect the unresolved reference and skill contents.
