## Description:

Turns occasion details and photos into AI video invitations, greetings, anniversary stories, and memorial or tribute films.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People planning invitations, holiday greetings, anniversaries, or memorials use the skill to turn event details and approved photos into a shareable short video for messaging, social stories, or an event screen.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photos and event details may be sensitive because generation sends them to Varg and its model providers.

Mitigation: Use only photos and details you have permission to send, review Varg and provider handling policies before uploading sensitive material, and remember that private delivery does not make cloud processing local.

Risk: Generating videos can incur costs.

Mitigation: Confirm or quote the generation cost before running a generation.

Risk: The optional multi-skill bundle and remote installer behavior are outside this artifact review.

Mitigation: Prefer the single-skill installation and review the installer and repository version before installing.
