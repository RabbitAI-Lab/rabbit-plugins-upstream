## Description:

Generates conversion-oriented Chinese marketing copy for ecommerce pages, ads, social posts, emails, and landing pages using 12 headline templates plus AIDA, PAS, FAB, and BAB frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams use this skill to draft Chinese campaign copy, headlines, landing page text, email copy, and A/B testing suggestions for product promotion and growth workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence says the skill is mostly a Markdown guide but asks for command, file, and API capabilities that are broader than its copywriting purpose.

Mitigation: Install with restricted permissions: allow normal copy generation and writes only to an explicit output folder; avoid shell execution, broad local file access, network/API access, or callback use unless separately reviewed and approved.

Risk: Generated marketing copy can create compliance or misuse risk when used for sensitive industries or aggressive promotional claims.

Mitigation: Require human review before publication, especially for medical, financial, education, regulated, or high-impact claims, and reject illegal marketing use.
