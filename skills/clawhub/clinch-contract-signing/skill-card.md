## Use Case:

Sales and legal-operations teams use this skill after verbal or email agreement to turn negotiated B2B terms into signed, archived, and handed-off contracts. It provides China-focused checklists for drafting, review, redlines, signing method selection, archiving, and post-signature handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the China-focused contract operations checklist as legal advice.

Mitigation: Route non-PRC, cross-border, regulated, high-value, or unusual contract matters to qualified legal review before use.

Risk: Contract workflows may expose sensitive business, personal, or contact data through OCR, CRM, storage, email, or webhook channels.

Mitigation: Use approved systems only, minimize personal data, confirm recipient permissions, and retain audit logs for access and transfer events.

Risk: Signing or redline guidance may be applied outside the documented China-focused scope.

Mitigation: Confirm jurisdiction, signing authority, required seals, and platform compliance before relying on any checklist or signing workflow.
