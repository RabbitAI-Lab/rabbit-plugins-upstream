## Description:

Calendario Semanal F100K generates a seven-day TikTok content calendar with ready-to-use scripts, viral references, CSV/XLSX exports, and a weekly digest for a user's niche.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and content operators use this skill to automate weekly short-form content planning for a niche. It reads the user's F100K configuration, selects recent viral references, drafts seven scripts, exports calendar files, and prepares the weekly delivery flow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reads user configuration, ideas, reports, and viral-reference data, and may prepare external delivery through Telegram, Gmail, or calendar tooling.

Mitigation: Confirm the configured recipient or account before delivery, and require explicit user approval before sending digests or creating calendar events.
