## Description:

Humanizer edits text to remove common signs of AI-generated writing and make the result sound more natural and human-written.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[seanford](https://clawhub.ai/user/seanford)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise text that sounds machine-generated by replacing inflated, promotional, vague, or formulaic phrasing with clearer human-facing prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be misused to mask AI assistance or alter voice in contexts with disclosure, academic, professional, legal, or policy requirements.

Mitigation: Review the revised text before use and apply it only where rewriting style or reducing AI-like phrasing is permitted.

Risk: Automated rewriting can change nuance, claims, attribution, or tone even when no hidden execution or data exfiltration behavior is present.

Mitigation: Compare the output against the original and verify that the meaning, factual claims, citations, and intended voice are still accurate.
