## Description: <br>
Humanizer helps an agent identify and rewrite AI-sounding prose so text reads more natural and human-written. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[seanford](https://clawhub.ai/user/seanford) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to audit drafts for common AI-writing patterns and rewrite affected passages while preserving the intended meaning and tone. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Rewriting text to sound less AI-generated can obscure authorship, disclosure, or personal voice expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Use only where this editing purpose is appropriate, and review changes before applying them to important files. <br>
Risk: The skill can modify local documents when used with file-editing tools. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed diffs or edited output before accepting changes, especially for legal, academic, professional, or published content. <br>
