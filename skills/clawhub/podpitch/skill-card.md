## Description:

Find podcasts by topic, audience, or name and help connected PodPitch customers manage campaigns, pitches, templates, and inbox replies within their account limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

PodPitch customers and people researching podcast placements use this skill to find relevant shows. Connected customers can also manage campaigns, pitches, templates, and inbox replies within their account limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live searches share the user's public search terms with PodPitch.

Mitigation: Use public, non-sensitive topics or show names; avoid including confidential or personal information in searches.

Risk: Connected account actions can edit or send campaign content.

Mitigation: Connect only for account-management tasks and require clear user direction for requested changes, sends, or scheduling.

Risk: Technical failures may trigger minimal automatic bug reports.

Mitigation: Keep reports limited to technical details and exclude credentials, personal identifiers, and private content.
