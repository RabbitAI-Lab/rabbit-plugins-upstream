## Use Case:

教师和教培从业者输入题目、正确答案、学生典型错误及学段学科，获得可用于课堂讲评的错因分析、解题步骤、知识补丁和练习题。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad education-related phrases may trigger this skill during a different discussion.

Mitigation: Confirm that wrong-answer analysis or a teaching note is wanted before using its output.

Risk: Student details included in a prompt may expose unnecessary personal information.

Mitigation: Omit or de-identify student information; provide only the question and error details needed for analysis.
