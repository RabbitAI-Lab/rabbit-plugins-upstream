## Description:

Kids Homework Helper guides parents and students through primary and junior-middle homework with step-by-step Socratic tutoring, writing support, English study guidance, error analysis, and study-habit recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External parents, guardians, and students use this skill to get guided homework coaching across math, Chinese, English, physics, chemistry, composition writing, error review, and study habits without relying on direct answer-giving.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The bundled learner stores usage history, preferences, and error notes locally, which is sensitive for a child-focused tutoring skill.

Mitigation: Disable or remove scripts/learner.py and learned_patterns.json behavior unless explicit retention, consent, and deletion controls are added.

Risk: The bundled learner may modify the skill file after repeated issues, changing tutoring behavior over time.

Mitigation: Require human review and version-control approval before any change is written back to SKILL.md.
