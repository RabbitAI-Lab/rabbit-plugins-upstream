## Description: <br>
Tailors and polishes an existing Google Docs cover letter for a specific role while keeping claims consistent with the accompanying resume and requiring approval for substantive additions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tangentus](https://clawhub.ai/user/tangentus) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and job applicants use this skill to improve an existing Google Docs cover letter against a specific role description while keeping every factual claim grounded in the accompanying resume or explicit user confirmation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill works with sensitive resume and cover-letter materials in Google Docs. <br>
Mitigation: Grant access only to the specific documents needed for the editing task and verify document permissions before editing. <br>
Risk: Substantive edits could overstate experience, qualifications, motivations, or accomplishments. <br>
Mitigation: Use the resume and explicit user confirmations as the only candidate-fact sources, and require user approval before adding substantive wording. <br>
Risk: Role terminology may imply unsupported proficiency if copied too aggressively from a job description. <br>
Mitigation: Keep role requirements separate from candidate evidence and omit or qualify unsupported claims. <br>
