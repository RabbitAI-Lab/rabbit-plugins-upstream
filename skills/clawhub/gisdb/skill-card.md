## Description:

Find and review source-backed county property records, owner portfolios, and parcel datasets, with saved searches and CSV delivery status.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Property researchers and real estate professionals can use the skill to look up county property records, review owner and parcel information, save search criteria, and check CSV delivery status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting uses a third-party service, so research details will be sent outside the user's environment.

Mitigation: Share only research details you intend to send.

Risk: Account permissions and paid offers may involve user-controlled billing actions.

Mitigation: Review account permissions and paid offers before proceeding.
