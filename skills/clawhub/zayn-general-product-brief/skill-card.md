## Description: <br>
跨行业基于名称、描述、图片、链接、规格资料和业务记录，快速整理实体产品、数字产品或服务的用途、目标用户、核心能力、关键属性、版本差异、购买与交付关注点及信息缺口。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sales, procurement, product, and operations users use this skill to turn traceable product or service evidence into a concise fact brief for clarification, comparison, quotation, or handoff. It emphasizes known facts, conflicts, missing information, and a next step instead of making final purchasing, pricing, or market judgments. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is Chinese-localized, so multilingual deployments may produce output in an unexpected language if language expectations are not stated. <br>
Mitigation: Set the desired output language in the user prompt or release notes before use in multilingual workflows. <br>
Risk: Product or service briefs can become misleading when source evidence is missing, conflicting, outdated, or too vague. <br>
Mitigation: Require at least one traceable source, preserve conflicts, label missing or unverified information, and avoid final procurement, pricing, or market judgments. <br>
