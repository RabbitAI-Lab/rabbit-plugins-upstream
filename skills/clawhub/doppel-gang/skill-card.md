## Description:

Reviews author-written applications, essays, bios, proposals, and important correspondence through explicitly synthetic, contrasting review lenses while preserving the author's voice and final editorial authority for author-led revision, not impersonation or automatic submission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[antreasantoniou](https://clawhub.ai/user/antreasantoniou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External authors and writing-support agents use Doppel Gang to critique drafts such as applications, essays, bios, proposals, and important correspondence through synthetic review lenses. The skill separates checkable facts, evidence gaps, taste, and audience fit while leaving revision and submission decisions with the author.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documented install and repository references are mutable.

Mitigation: Pin or manually verify the source before installing.

Risk: Drafts may contain sensitive personal information, and the optional helper prints the full draft in its JSON output.

Mitigation: Keep helper output private and only process material the agent or provider is authorized to receive.
