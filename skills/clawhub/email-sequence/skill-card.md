## Description: <br>
Write a multi-email nurture, onboarding, launch, or re-engagement sequence with mapped goals, triggers, timing, full copy, preview text, and one CTA per email. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, lifecycle, and growth teams use this skill to draft behavior-aware email sequences for welcome, onboarding, nurture, launch, and re-engagement campaigns. The skill helps turn campaign inputs into a sequence map, full email copy, exit or suppression rules, and per-email metrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated marketing emails may be inaccurate, mismatched to the audience, or incomplete for legal and operational sending requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated sequences for factual accuracy, compliance, audience fit, unsubscribe handling, and suppression rules before sending. <br>
