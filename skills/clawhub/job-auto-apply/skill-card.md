## Description: <br>
Job Auto Apply automates job search and application workflows across major job platforms, including match analysis, tailored cover letters, form filling, submission, and application tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Job seekers and agents assisting them use this skill to search for matching roles, prepare tailored application materials, and submit or stage applications with configurable confirmation and dry-run controls. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can handle resumes, contact details, employment history, job-platform sessions, and possible application submissions. <br>
Mitigation: Keep dry-run and manual confirmation enabled, review every application before submission, and store profile files and credentials in a dedicated secure location. <br>
Risk: Automation guidance may include workflows that need careful review before use with job-platform accounts. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid CAPTCHA-solving or proxy workflows, respect platform terms and rate limits, and require manual confirmation before real submissions. <br>
Risk: Generated application materials or automated matching may produce inaccurate, unwanted, or low-quality applications. <br>
Mitigation: Use conservative match thresholds, verify profile data and generated cover letters, and review each application before submission. <br>
