## Description:

Build a HyperFrames slideshow to present live - slides, fragment reveals, branching hotspots and presenter mode with speaker notes, served by `hyperframes present` - or turn an existing page into one. Use when the person wants a deck or presentation rather than a rendered video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to create or adapt HyperFrames projects into live, navigable slide presentations with fragments, branching hotspots, and presenter notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An unreviewed or unpinned HyperFrames dependency may introduce dependency or compatibility risk.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, pinned HyperFrames dependency.

Risk: The optional Three.js CDN example may not suit offline or sensitive presentations.

Mitigation: Bundle Three.js locally for offline or sensitive presentations.

Risk: Confidential speaker notes may remain in browser storage on shared devices.

Mitigation: Avoid retaining confidential notes on shared devices.
