## Description:

Analyzes succulent images or videos for black rot, water-soaked leaves, and stretching, reporting detected conditions and their severity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Home growers, greenhouses, and flower shops use this skill to assess succulent images or videos for signs of black rot, water-soaked leaves, or stretching, and to review past analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images, videos, and report history are sent to a configured cloud service.

Mitigation: Share only content you are comfortable transmitting and review the publisher's data-retention practices before use.

Risk: The skill can silently create or reuse an account identity and store authentication tokens in a workspace database.

Mitigation: Review account-management expectations and restrict access to the workspace database before installing.
