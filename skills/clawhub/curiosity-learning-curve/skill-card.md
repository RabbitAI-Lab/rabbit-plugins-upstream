## Description: <br>
Curiosity Learning Curve helps agents coach users through diagnosing curiosity decline, distinguishing reversible from terminal domain disengagement, and designing reactivation experiments or transition plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and coaching agents use this skill when a person or domain expert has stopped seeking new questions, feels professionally stagnant, or needs to decide whether to reignite curiosity in a domain or transition away from it. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The coaching framework may misclassify burnout, structural pressure, or a capability gap as curiosity decline. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's fit checks before coaching and redirect to recovery, environment changes, or skill-building when those causes better explain the user's situation. <br>
Risk: A terminal-domain diagnosis could encourage a premature transition away from useful work. <br>
Mitigation: Require specific evidence, a reversible-versus-terminal classification, and a 30-day reactivation experiment before recommending transition. <br>
Risk: Historical examples and synthesis claims may be overgeneralized as proof. <br>
Mitigation: Treat cited sources as supporting context, keep the three-curve model framed as a synthesis, and avoid relying on disputed quotations. <br>
