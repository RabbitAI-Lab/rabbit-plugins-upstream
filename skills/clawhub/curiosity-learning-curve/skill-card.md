## Description:

Helps people and domain experts diagnose declining curiosity and choose a focused reactivation experiment or domain transition.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and professionals can use the skill to assess where curiosity has declined, identify possible causes, and develop a 30-day experiment or a domain transition plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coaching may lead users to consider professional domain changes based on incomplete context.

Mitigation: Treat experiments and transition plans as coaching suggestions, weigh personal constraints before acting, and save observations or schedule check-ins only by choice.
