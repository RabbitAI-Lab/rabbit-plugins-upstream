## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended tool prices, features, regions, and terms may be outdated or unavailable for the user.

Mitigation: Verify prices, availability, and terms against official product sources and include the verification date; mark unresolved items as pending verification.

Risk: Browsing or saving a recommendation report may expose user intent, business details, or selected tool choices.

Mitigation: Ask for user approval before browsing external sites or writing reports, and avoid including unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: The bundled automation diagram could be mistaken for a required automation design.

Mitigation: Treat the image as an explanatory example and add human approval, logging, retry, and credential controls before applying automation ideas.
