## Description:

Use when you need to determine the accuracy of the answer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to audit factual claims in an answer, verify them independently, and correct or qualify inaccurate statements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad use can increase time, cost, and tool activity because the workflow may launch multiple verification subagents and external source checks.

Mitigation: Use the workflow for answers or claims that need higher factual assurance, and scope verification batches to the claims that materially affect the result.
