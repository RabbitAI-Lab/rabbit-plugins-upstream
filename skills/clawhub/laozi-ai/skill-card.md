老子AI Laozi AI provides Tao Te Ching chapter lookup, multilingual Chinese and English quotations, and Taoist philosophy interpretation for Laozi-related prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to retrieve Tao Te Ching chapters, compare Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and English passages, cite selected Laozi quotations, and receive Taoist concept explanations or writing assistance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad Taoism-related prompts and provide more languages than the user expects.

Mitigation: Ask for a specific chapter, topic, language, or output format when a narrower response is needed.

Risk: Generated philosophical interpretations or writing assistance may be presented as authoritative commentary.

Mitigation: Treat interpretive responses as explanatory guidance and verify important quotations or translations against the bundled reference data.
