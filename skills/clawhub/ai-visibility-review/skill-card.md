## Description:

Investigates how ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode recommend a business, who appears instead, and which cited pages suggest practical opportunities for an AEO audit or visibility review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scrollport](https://clawhub.ai/user/scrollport)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Business owners, marketers and their advisors use this skill to review how sampled AI search answers recommend a business, compare those answers with selected competitors, and plan evidence-based content improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The benign scan does not establish which file and network permissions the host runtime enforces.

Mitigation: Review the skill's detected capabilities and tool scope before installation or use.

Risk: Sampled AI answers can vary and do not establish that a content change caused a later result or guarantees placement.

Mitigation: Keep engine counts, settings and source evidence separate, and describe recheck results as observations rather than causal effects.
