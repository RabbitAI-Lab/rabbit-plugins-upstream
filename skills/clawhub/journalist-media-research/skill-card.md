## Description:

Build a relevant journalist and media shortlist for a story or PR brief using Crawlora's journalists dataset, news search, and public author pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Communications teams and researchers use this skill to identify journalists by beat and outlet, check relevant recent coverage, and compile a sourced media shortlist with publicly available contact channels. Sending pitches is a separate action requiring the user's request.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries, submitted URLs, and the API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use an authorized Crawlora account; avoid private URLs and sensitive brief details unless its data-use terms meet your needs.
