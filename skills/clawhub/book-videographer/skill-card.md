## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to search for videographers, check availability, and create bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking creation can send customer contact details and reserve a videographer through Lokuli. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, date, time, customer name, email, and phone number with the user. <br>
Risk: Search results, availability, and booking outcomes depend on the Lokuli MCP service. <br>
Mitigation: Review the returned provider and booking details before relying on them for scheduling decisions. <br>
