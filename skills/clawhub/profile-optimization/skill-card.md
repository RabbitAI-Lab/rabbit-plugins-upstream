## Description: <br>
Optimizes social media profiles as conversion landing pages by producing ready-to-apply per-platform specs for bios, searchable fields, visuals, links, CTAs, pinned posts, and highlights. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators, social media managers, founders, and business users use this skill to turn brand, audience, voice, and strategy inputs into ready-to-apply social profile specifications. It helps improve clarity, searchability, follow conversion, and profile-to-lead handoff without directly changing user accounts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce profile copy or proof statements that would be misleading if the source material is incomplete or unverified. <br>
Mitigation: Use only user-provided, real proof such as actual credentials, results, or testimonials; leave proof out when it is not available. <br>
Risk: Platform profile limits, fields, and eligibility rules can change after release. <br>
Mitigation: Re-verify current platform constraints before applying the spec, especially character limits, link eligibility, and profile feature availability. <br>
Risk: Users may expect the skill to update social accounts directly. <br>
Mitigation: Present the result as an advisory spec for manual application and clarify that it does not edit profile fields or supply profile analytics. <br>
