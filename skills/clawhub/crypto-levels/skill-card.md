## Description: <br>
Analyze cryptocurrency support and resistance levels. Use when users ask about crypto price analysis, support/resistance levels, technical analysis for BTC, ETH, or other cryptocurrencies. Provides current price, key levels, and trading insights for crypto pairs like BTC-USDT, ETH-USDT. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake generated crypto levels or trading context for personalized financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational technical-analysis assistance only, verify live prices independently, and consult qualified financial guidance before making investment decisions. <br>
Risk: Market data may be delayed, unavailable, rate-limited, or different across public data sources. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check important prices and levels against a current exchange or data provider before relying on the analysis. <br>
Risk: Configuration examples include optional API key fields for market-data providers. <br>
Mitigation: Keep real API keys out of files, screenshots, logs, and version control, and prefer provider-side restrictions where available. <br>
