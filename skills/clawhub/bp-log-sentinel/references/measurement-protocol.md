# The Measurement Protocol (and What Each Rule Is Worth)

Why technique matters: each violation below shifts readings by a measurable amount. Stack three
violations and a normal person reads Stage 1.

| # | Rule | Typical error if violated |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No exercise 30 min before | +5-7 mmHg |
| 2 | Empty bladder | +10-15 mmHg (full bladder) |
| 3 | Seated quietly 5 min | +5-10 mmHg (restless/adrenalized) |
| 4 | Back supported (chair, not exam table) | +5-10 mmHg (back unsupported) |
| 5 | Feet flat, legs uncrossed | +5-8 mmHg (crossed legs) |
| 6 | Arm supported at heart level | +10 mmHg per ~10 cm below heart |
| 7 | Correct cuff size (bladder encircles 80% of arm) | +5-15 mmHg if too small |
| 8 | Bare arm (not over clothing) | +5-50 mmHg depending on fabric |
| 9 | No talking (both patient and measurer) | +5-10 mmHg |
| 10 | No caffeine/smoking 30 min before | +5-8 mmHg (caffeine), +5-10 (smoking) |

## The 7-Day Schedule (AHA/ESH home-monitoring protocol)

- **Twice daily**: morning (within 1 h of waking, before meds/food/coffee) and evening.
- **Two readings each sitting**, 1-2 min apart; record BOTH but average within-sitting first
  when possible.
- **7 consecutive days** minimum before interpretation.
- **Discard day 1** from averages (alerting reaction — first-day readings run high).
- Diagnostic quantity = mean of all remaining readings (≈24 readings over 6 days).
- Use the SAME validated, upper-arm, correctly-sized cuff all week. Validate the device
  against the office monitor yearly (readings within ±5 mmHg).

## Device Notes

- **Upper-arm oscillometric** monitors (validated: check validatebp.org / STRIDE-BP lists) are
  the default recommendation.
- **Wrist monitors**: acceptable only with strict heart-level positioning; overread when low.
- **Finger monitors**: not validated — dissuade.
- Cuff sizes by arm circumference (typical): small <24 cm, standard 24-32 cm, large 32-42 cm.
- Arrhythmia (AFib): oscillometric cuffs may misread — flag for manual/auscultatory checks;
  some monitors have AFib detection, which is screening, not diagnosis.

## Logging Hygiene (for the CSV)

- Record date, time, both readings, pulse, arm, and a short conditions note.
- The notes field is where this skill's contaminant detector looks: mention coffee, smoking,
  exercise, talking, cold, crossed legs, sleeves — the analysis will flag them.
- Never round readings or "fix" implausible ones; annotate and re-measure.
