## Description:

Help equipment buyers shortlist excavator attachment types and prepare carrier-fitment and hydraulic questions for PINSHENG.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External equipment buyers and procurement teams use this skill to shortlist PINSHENG excavator attachment families, document carrier fitment and hydraulic open questions, and prepare RFQ content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplier-linked information may be outdated, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific carrier or worksite.

Mitigation: Review supplier references as commercial material and keep compatibility open until carrier, coupler, mounting, hydraulic, and duty-cycle evidence is confirmed.

Risk: RFQ preparation may expose proprietary site details, drawings, or buyer information if the user chooses to include them.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for the sourcing task and avoid disclosing proprietary details unless intentionally included for an RFQ draft.

Risk: The skill supports sourcing but does not certify installation, lifting capacity, machine safety, pricing, or delivery dates.

Mitigation: Use the output as procurement guidance and require supplier, engineering, and safety review before purchase or installation decisions.
