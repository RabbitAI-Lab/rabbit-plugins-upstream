## Description: <br>
Guides retail teams through digital and AI transformation planning with maturity assessment, technology selection, ROI/TCO modeling, implementation workflows, and reusable templates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Retail operators, consultants, IT leaders, and transformation teams use this skill to diagnose retail digital maturity, plan AI and digital initiatives, compare vendors, build ROI cases, and structure implementation or change-management deliverables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste client, customer, member, employee, financial, or system data into consulting workflows and templates. <br>
Mitigation: Anonymize or aggregate sensitive data, confirm authorization to use it, and apply privacy, retention, access-control, and legal-review controls before use. <br>
Risk: CDP, OneID, employee monitoring, cross-border data, and AI personalization work can raise privacy and compliance obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Add jurisdiction-specific privacy review and approval steps before using the skill output for these initiatives. <br>
Risk: ROI, vendor, technology, and implementation recommendations may affect business or procurement decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Validate assumptions, figures, and vendor claims with qualified business, technical, financial, and legal reviewers before acting. <br>
