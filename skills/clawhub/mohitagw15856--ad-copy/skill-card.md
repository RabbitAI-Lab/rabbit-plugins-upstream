## Description: <br>
Writes platform-native paid ad copy variants for Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Instagram, and other platforms, with distinct testing angles, native fields, character-limit guidance, and notes on what each variant tests. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, growth teams, founders, and agencies use this skill to draft paid ad variants for platform-native campaign tests. It helps structure multiple angles, fields, CTAs, and message-match notes so ad results are easier to compare. <br>

Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated ad claims may be inaccurate, unsupported, or misaligned with platform advertising policies. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated claims for accuracy, substantiation, policy compliance, and current platform character limits before using them in paid campaigns. <br>
