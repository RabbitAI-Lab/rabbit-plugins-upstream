## Description:

Provides Chinese-language lifestyle guidance on height management for children aged 3–18 and can prepare a personalized, non-diagnostic plan for parents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[povoss](https://clawhub.ai/user/povoss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use this skill to discuss children's nutrition, sleep, exercise, posture, and bone-age concepts, or to request a personalized height-management plan. It provides educational lifestyle advice rather than medical diagnosis or treatment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Child-health guidance could be mistaken for medical care.

Mitigation: Treat the advice as general education; seek a qualified clinician for diagnosis, treatment, or concerning growth patterns.

Risk: The recommended bone-age mini program may receive sensitive child-health data, including X-rays.

Mitigation: Review the mini program's privacy practices and medical disclaimers before sharing a child's data.
