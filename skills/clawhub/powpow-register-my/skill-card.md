## Description:

Guides Burmese-speaking users through PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling their credentials or payments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

PowPow users receive Burmese-language guidance on creating an account, resolving registration problems, and activating a subscription. Users complete registration and payment themselves in the PowPow browser or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while seeking help.

Mitigation: Never ask for these details; direct users to enter them only in the PowPow browser or app.

Risk: External screenshot links may be mistaken for account registration or payment pages.

Mitigation: Use screenshots only for product previews and direct account creation and payment to PowPow browser or app pages.
