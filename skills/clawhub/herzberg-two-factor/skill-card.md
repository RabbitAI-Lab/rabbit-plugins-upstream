## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a Herzberg two-factor diagnosis for motivation, engagement, retention, compensation, role design, and performance-system questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Managers, HR partners, founders, and leadership coaches use this skill to diagnose whether low motivation or retention pressure is driven by hygiene factors or motivator gaps, then design job-enrichment interventions. Agents use it to produce a structured Two-Factor Diagnosis from concrete team or role evidence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the framework as an automated HR decision tool rather than management-analysis guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as coaching and diagnostic guidance, and have responsible managers or HR reviewers evaluate recommendations before acting. <br>
Risk: Team examples may include confidential employee details. <br>
Mitigation: Anonymize employee cases and avoid storing confidential details in reusable examples unless the user explicitly requests retention. <br>
