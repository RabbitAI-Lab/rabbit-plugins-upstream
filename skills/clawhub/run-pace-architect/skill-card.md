## Description:

Turns a recent road-race result into training paces, equivalent race-time estimates, goal-feasibility guidance, mileage ramps, and race-day splits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Runners and coaches use a recent road-race time to estimate training zones and equivalent performances, assess goals, plan gradual mileage increases, and calculate race-day splits. Estimates are starting points, not medical advice or injury guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Race predictions and training targets may be misleading for runners without sufficient distance-specific preparation or on terrain unlike road racing.

Mitigation: Treat estimates as starting points based on a recent measured race; adjust for fitness and terrain rather than assuming a predicted result is guaranteed.

Risk: Generalized mileage and pace guidance may be inappropriate during injury recovery or rapid changes in training volume.

Mitigation: Increase volume conservatively, do not use the skill for injury or medical decisions, and seek professional advice for persistent pain.
