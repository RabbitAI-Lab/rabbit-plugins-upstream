## Description: <br>
Guides an agent through bilingual A-share quantitative stock diagnosis using akshare and baostock public market data, five-dimension scoring, peer comparison, and buy/sell research recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jeffreyshou7](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeffreyshou7) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Investors, analysts, and agents use this skill to gather public A-share market data and structure a quantitative research note with scores, target-price scenarios, and trading posture. Outputs should be independently checked and treated as general market research, not personalized financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated ratings, target prices, position sizes, or buy/sell recommendations may be mistaken for personalized financial advice or authorization to trade. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as general research, verify figures independently, and do not rely on them as trading authorization or personalized financial advice. <br>
Risk: Public market, fund-holding, financial, and fallback web data can be stale, unavailable, or inconsistent across sources. <br>
Mitigation: Review source dates, compare fallback sources when used, and disclose uncertainty before relying on the diagnosis. <br>
