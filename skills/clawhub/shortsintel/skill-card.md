## Description:

Find winning short-form videos and ads on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and the Meta and TikTok Ad Libraries with the ShortsIntel API. Research a niche, search and score videos, analyse hooks and formats, search ads, and track creators, hashtags, sounds, keywords and advertisers daily. Prepaid Credits, priced per call.
