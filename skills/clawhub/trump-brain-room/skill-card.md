## Description:

A multi-voice panel routes a topic to 3–5 BaZi-derived personas that offer distinct Trump-inspired perspectives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users can request an entertainment-only panel of Trump-inspired personas to hear contrasting perspectives on a topic. Developers can use the included zero-dependency demo to inspect persona routing without generating dialogue.
