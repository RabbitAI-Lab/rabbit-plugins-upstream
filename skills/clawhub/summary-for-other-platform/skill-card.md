## Description: <br>
当用户说「把问题总结一下」「整理一下去问豆包」等时，输出三段式问题总结，方便复制到其他AI平台。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jufangyuan](https://clawhub.ai/user/jufangyuan) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to turn the current conversation into a concise Chinese problem summary that can be copied into another AI platform for follow-up help. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated summaries may include personal, confidential, or proprietary details from the current conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive details before pasting the summary into an external platform. <br>
Risk: Broad summary-like requests may trigger the skill when the user did not intend cross-platform sharing. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the destination and purpose before sharing the generated summary outside the current workspace. <br>
