## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can read and summarize user-provided PDFs, PPTs, or DOCX files into the report.

Mitigation: Do not provide confidential documents unless they are intended to be included in the skill's analysis.

Risk: Pharma, patent, finance, and clinical facts can become outdated or materially affect diligence decisions.

Mitigation: Review the report's source tags, data verification appendix, and time-sensitive findings before relying on the output.
