## Description:

Guides QA teams through evidence-based retrospectives that identify root causes, document lessons learned, and track improvement actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA teams and project leads use this skill after iterations, projects, or production incidents to review defect and test data, investigate missed issues, and assign measurable follow-up actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A vague review request could cause the skill to read unrelated project materials.

Mitigation: Confirm retrospective intent and limit workspace reading to relevant, approved records.

Risk: Suggested process changes could be adopted without team review.

Mitigation: Review recommendations with stakeholders and agree on changes before implementation.
