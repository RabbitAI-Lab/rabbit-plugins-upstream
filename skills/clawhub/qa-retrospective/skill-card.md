## Description:

QA Retrospective helps QA teams run data-supported retrospectives after an iteration, project, or production incident and produce a retrospective report with follow-up improvement actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA leads, test engineers, and development teams use this skill to turn defect trends, missed-test analysis, team feedback, and incident data into root-cause findings, action items, and reusable process assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Retrospectives may include sensitive team feedback, defect data, or incident details.

Mitigation: Confirm the user intends to run a formal retrospective before processing sensitive material, and keep confidential data out of the workspace unless that is explicitly intended.

Risk: Retrospective outputs may propose workflow or release-process changes that should not be treated as approved decisions.

Mitigation: Review proposed action items with the team, assign owners and due dates, and seek consensus before changing team processes.
