## Description:

Helps teams run QA retrospectives after iterations, projects, or incidents by turning defect trends, missed-test analysis, root-cause findings, and team feedback into a retrospective report and improvement tracking plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, test leads, and development teams use this skill to facilitate structured retrospectives after releases, projects, repeated defects, or production incidents. It guides data collection, 5 Whys analysis, action-item planning, asset updates, and follow-up tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases about summaries or recurring problems may activate the retrospective workflow when the user only intended to discuss an issue.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a QA retrospective before proceeding when the trigger context is ambiguous.

Risk: Retrospective recommendations may affect team process, release flow, or collaboration practices.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as proposals and require team review and agreement before changing operating procedures.

Risk: Insufficient defect, test, incident, or team-feedback data can make the retrospective incomplete or misleading.

Mitigation: Collect the missing quality metrics, defect lifecycle details, and team input before finalizing the report and action plan.
