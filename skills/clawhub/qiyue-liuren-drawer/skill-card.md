## Description:

Draws a Da Liu Ren chart for the current or specified time, returning monthly general, lessons, transmitters, and astrological indicator summaries from QiyueAstro.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bloodymarygg](https://clawhub.ai/user/bloodymarygg)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to request Da Liu Ren divination charts for a question or timestamp and display the API-returned chart summary. It is intended for entertainment and self-exploration, not independent high-stakes interpretation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The user's question text and optional casting time are sent to qiyueastro.com.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal, medical, financial, or confidential business details.

Risk: Outputs include divination content and a QiyueAstro promotional link.

Mitigation: Treat results as entertainment or self-exploration and review the external link before following it.
