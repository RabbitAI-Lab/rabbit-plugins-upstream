## Description:

Provides French horn fingering, pitch transposition, harmonic analysis, repertoire, intonation, equipment, and warmup guidance through HornLib.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hornlib](https://clawhub.ai/user/hornlib)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External musicians, horn students, and music educators can use it to look up fingerings, transpose pitches, explore horn acoustics and repertoire, and plan practice routines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
