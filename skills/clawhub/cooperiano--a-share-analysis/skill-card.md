## Description: <br>
A-share analysis layer for individual-stock reports, health cards, technical and fundamental analysis, chip distribution, Wyckoff, Chan theory, K-line analysis, evidence binding, and actionable conditional conclusions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[cooperiano](https://clawhub.ai/user/cooperiano) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and analysts use this skill to structure A-share stock research into evidence-bound reports, health cards, scenario analysis, and conditional conclusions. It depends on separate market-data retrieval and external search rather than fetching raw data itself. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Stock-analysis output may be mistaken for financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance only, avoid absolute buy or sell conclusions, and review decisions with appropriate financial judgment. <br>
Risk: Market data and source material may be stale, incomplete, or incorrect because the skill relies on external searches and a separate data skill. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current market data, source URLs, and evidence bindings before acting on any conclusion. <br>
