## Description:

Generate visual asset prompts for SEO landing pages — banner (16:9, image-to-image with logo), product introduction (1:1), why choose (1:1), and pain points (1:1). Then optionally execute batch generation via image generation service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tutorgpt-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/tutorgpt-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO content creators and marketers use this skill after preparing landing page copy to create prompts for matching banner, product introduction, comparison, and problem-solution visuals. They can also use it to submit those prompts for optional batch image generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated visuals or their embedded text may not accurately represent the landing page or product.

Mitigation: Review prompts and generated images for factual accuracy, branding, and text quality before publishing.
