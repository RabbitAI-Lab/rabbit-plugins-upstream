## Description: <br>
Generate a financial due diligence checklist and analysis framework for any investment, acquisition, or partnership. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external deal teams, investors, and business stakeholders use this skill to create tailored financial due diligence checklists, document request lists, analytical questions, red flag checks, and summary RAG assessments for acquisitions, investments, partnerships, supplier reviews, and fundraising diligence. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Financial due diligence guidance can be mistaken for professional advice or used with confidential financial material. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a due diligence framework only, involve qualified financial or legal advisers where appropriate, and apply normal data-handling controls before sharing confidential documents or company secrets. <br>
