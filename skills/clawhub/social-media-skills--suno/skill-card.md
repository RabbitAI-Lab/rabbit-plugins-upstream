## Description: <br>
The Suno skill helps agents draft music briefs, lyrics, structures, rights-aware workflows, and optional connected-API steps for generating brand music, songs, stems, and audio with Suno. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and social-media operators use this skill to plan Suno music for videos, podcasts, ads, brand themes, and distribution while keeping rights, disclosure, and human review explicit. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Connected Suno/API or publishing workflows could create or prepare music and posts using incorrect rights assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review music, rights assumptions, AI disclosures, and posts before release; verify current Suno terms for commercial work. <br>
Risk: Artist imitation, voice cloning, or fake collaboration claims could create likeness, attribution, or infringement risk. <br>
Mitigation: Use generic style descriptions, only use verified owned voices or consented opt-in paths, and avoid implying artist involvement. <br>
Risk: Purely AI-generated music may have weak copyright protection and may require distributor AI disclosure. <br>
Mitigation: Add meaningful human elements for important releases, keep DDEX or platform AI disclosures accurate, and avoid PRO registration for pure-AI works. <br>
