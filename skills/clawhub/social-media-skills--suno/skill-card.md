## Description: <br>
The Suno craft skill helps agents create Suno music briefs, lyrics, structure plans, rights-aware release guidance, and connected integration steps for brand music, songs, stems, podcast stings, and social video scoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to plan Suno-generated songs, brand sound, podcast intros, stems, and distribution guidance with human review, rights checks, and disclosure discipline. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can guide an agent toward connected Suno or publishing integrations when the user has configured them. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated steps before execution and confirm that any connected services, credentials, and publishing destinations are intended for the task. <br>
Risk: Commercial use of AI-generated music can depend on current Suno plan terms, rights status, and disclosure requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Use paid Suno plans for commercial work, verify current rights and disclosure rules before release, and seek qualified advice for high-stakes uses. <br>
Risk: Voice cloning, artist imitation, or implied endorsement can create consent, likeness, and attribution risks. <br>
Mitigation: Use original style descriptions, avoid cloning real artists or implying collaborations, and keep human review in the approval path. <br>
