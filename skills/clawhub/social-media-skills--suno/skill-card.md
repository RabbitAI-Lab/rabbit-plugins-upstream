## Description:

The Suno craft skill helps agents create Suno music briefs, lyrics, song structures, rights checks, archive plans, and distribution guidance for brand music and audio workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to prepare Suno song briefs, lyrics, structure tags, and rights-aware distribution and archive plans for brand videos, podcasts, ads, and streaming releases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat Suno paid-plan commercial rights as copyright ownership or legal indemnification.

Mitigation: State rights limits plainly, verify current Suno terms before commercial use, add meaningful human elements for important work, and seek counsel for high-stakes releases.

Risk: Generation or publishing workflows may involve paid plans, connected APIs, downloads, archives, or distributor submissions.

Mitigation: Keep human approval before generation or publishing, verify tier and API access, and apply required AI-origin disclosure during distribution.

Risk: Archived stems, voice assets, and Custom Model source tracks may contain sensitive creative or likeness material.

Mitigation: Store WAV, stem, MIDI, voice, and model-source assets securely with access limited to authorized users.
