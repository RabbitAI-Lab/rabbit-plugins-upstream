## Description: <br>
Market-wide sentiment dashboard with VIX, put/call ratio, Fear & Greed Index, market breadth, and sector rotation analysis that classifies the current regime as risk-on, risk-off, or neutral. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Traders and market researchers use this skill to summarize broad market sentiment indicators, compare them with recent historical context, and interpret the current market regime for options and trading posture decisions. <br>

Global <br>
