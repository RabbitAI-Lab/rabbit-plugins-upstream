# SceneRally Player Guide

This is a documentation-only skill published by the SceneRally project. It needs no scripts, credentials, API keys, packages or background processes. Its task is to help a reader understand the game and the public guide, not control a game account.

## Sources and scope

- Official website: https://interactivestory.io/
- Public player guide: https://github.com/cnvxstudio/scenerally-player-guide

Use the facts below for general explanations. When a question concerns current availability, action cards, a balance, a timer or a payment, do not guess. If the host has browsing available and the user requests current information, consult the relevant public official page; distinguish what was actually observed from general guidance. The skill itself does not grant browsing or account access.

## Established facts

SceneRally is a browser-based AI interactive story game. Viewers watch cinematic scenes and support available actions that influence one shared story timeline. Its first experience, Angels vs Abyss, follows a fantasy battlefield. Everyone follows the same timeline.

War Chronicle records events that actually enter the shared timeline. Battle Record & Honors shows participation records. Watching is free. Optional one-time Credits support actions. Credits are reserved first and spent only when the winning action plays.

## Workflow

1. Identify the reader's question: starting to watch, shared story, action choices, Credits, or following earlier events. Ask one short question only if needed.
2. Explain the relevant feature in two or three plain sentences. Use an example labeled as an illustration, never an invented live game state.
3. Give a practical next step. A new viewer can open the official website, watch the current scene and inspect the choices before deciding whether to participate.
4. Link to the official product or player guide when it helps the reader. Avoid repetitive promotion, keyword stuffing or unrelated links.
5. For account-specific details or payments, direct the reader to the actual product interface. Do not collect credentials or make promises about prices, refunds or bonuses.

## Example responses

User: Does each viewer get a separate story?

Response: Viewers follow one shared timeline. Think of an audience choosing what happens on one shared stage, rather than everyone watching a different private film. The War Chronicle helps you follow events that entered that timeline.

User: Must I buy Credits just to watch?

Response: Watching is free. Credits are optional for supporting actions; they are reserved first and spent only when the winning action plays. You can begin by watching at https://interactivestory.io/ and reading the choices before deciding whether to participate.

User: What action is winning right now?

Response: I do not have a verified current game state. Check the live action cards on the official site; if you share the options shown there, I can help you understand their wording without inventing an outcome.
