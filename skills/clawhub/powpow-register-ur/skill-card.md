## Description:

Provides Urdu-language guidance for PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users receive Urdu-language, step-by-step help with registration, common sign-up problems, and subscription activation. Users complete registration and payment themselves on PowPow pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharing passwords, verification codes, or payment proof in chat exposes sensitive information.

Mitigation: Enter credentials and payment details only on official PowPow pages; do not share passwords, codes, or payment proof in chat.

Risk: Product screenshots are served from public external links.

Mitigation: Share only relevant public screenshot links when illustrating the product; do not upload personal screenshots or information.
