## Description:

Reviews recorded voice-agent conversations for quality issues and identifies the moments that need attention.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Businesses and agencies use this skill to log and review timestamped voice-agent call issues, find recurring problems, and check whether changes improved call quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Call recordings, transcripts, customer details, and internal QA notes may be sent to BlueColumn.

Mitigation: Confirm permission to share the data, use consented or redacted content where possible, and review retention and deletion controls.

Risk: A leaked BlueColumn API key could grant access to the account.

Mitigation: Keep the API key secret and avoid including it in shared recordings, notes, or logs.
