## Description:

Edits, rewrites, or proofreads prose for a natural tone and can audit a draft for AI-writing patterns without rewriting it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to improve copy, documentation, emails, blog posts, and PR descriptions while preserving meaning and voice, or to identify AI-writing patterns without rewriting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style preferences may make writing more direct or less formal than intended.

Mitigation: Specify the desired register, locale, and degree of editing; request minimal proofreading when appropriate.

Risk: A rewrite may alter the writer's meaning, level of certainty, or voice.

Mitigation: Review factual claims, hedges, technical details, and voice before using the revised text.
