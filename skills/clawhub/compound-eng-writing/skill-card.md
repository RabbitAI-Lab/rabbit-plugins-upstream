## Description: <br>
Prose editing, rewriting, and humanizing text for natural tone, or auditing a draft for AI tells without rewriting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, writers, and reviewers use this skill to rewrite copy, documentation, blog posts, emails, and PR descriptions so they read naturally while preserving the author's voice. They can also use it to audit a draft for visible AI-style writing patterns without asking the agent to make authorship claims. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is opinionated about tone and may over-prune prose or remove useful author voice if applied mechanically. <br>
Mitigation: Review edits before relying on them, especially for high-stakes or voice-sensitive writing. <br>
Risk: An audit can identify visible writing patterns, but it cannot prove whether a human or AI wrote the text. <br>
Mitigation: Use audit findings as concrete editing evidence and avoid treating them as an authorship verdict. <br>
