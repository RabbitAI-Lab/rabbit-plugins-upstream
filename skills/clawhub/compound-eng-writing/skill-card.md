## Description:

Edits and rewrites human-facing prose for natural tone, or audits drafts for formulaic writing without rewriting them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to improve copy, documentation, emails, and PR descriptions while preserving meaning and voice, or to identify formulaic writing without changing the draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A rewrite may change factual claims, citations, or the writer's intended meaning.

Mitigation: Review edited text against the source for accuracy, citations, and preserved meaning before use.

Risk: Natural-sounding prose may be misused to misrepresent authorship or remove required provenance.

Mitigation: Retain required attribution and disclosures; obtain authorization before publishing or presenting the text as someone else's work.
