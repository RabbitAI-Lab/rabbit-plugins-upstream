## Description:

Prose editing, rewriting, and humanizing text for natural tone, or auditing a draft for AI tells without rewriting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, developers, and reviewers use this skill to edit human-facing prose, PR descriptions, READMEs, docs, emails, and posts while preserving meaning and voice. It can also audit a draft for AI-style patterns without assigning authorship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested edits can change nuance, factual meaning, or the author's original voice.

Mitigation: Review the edited text before publishing and compare it against the intended meaning and voice.

Risk: Detect-mode findings can be mistaken for proof that a text was AI-generated.

Mitigation: Use findings as observable prose-pattern evidence only; do not treat them as authorship verdicts.

Risk: Posting or publishing edited content may require separate authority.

Mitigation: Grant publishing or posting permissions outside this skill and keep final approval with the user or reviewer.
