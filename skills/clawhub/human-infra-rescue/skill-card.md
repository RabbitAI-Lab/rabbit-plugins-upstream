## Description:

A human engineer diagnoses and repairs your own Linux, Kubernetes, CI, DNS or TLS systems when your agents cannot: diagnostic 200 EUR (within 72 hours of payment), rescue 450 EUR; a rescue request is acknowledged within 1 hour at any hour. Use when an outage or a broken deployment needs hands-on work by a person. Pay only an accepted task, escrow on Base recommended.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
