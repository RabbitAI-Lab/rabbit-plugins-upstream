## Description: <br>
Provides metallurgy-sector tax compliance guidance, self-check workflows, policy references, case patterns, report templates, and practical remediation suggestions for ferrous, non-ferrous, rare earth, and precious metals scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Tax, finance, compliance, and advisory users use this skill to ask China-focused metallurgy tax questions, run structured compliance self-checks, identify risk indicators, and draft practical remediation or reporting guidance. It is intended as decision support and does not replace professional tax, audit, or legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China-focused; globally accessible for users who need Chinese metallurgy tax compliance guidance. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tax questions, compliance self-check metrics, and related prompts may be sent to publisher cloud services. <br>
Mitigation: Review the publisher's data handling terms before use and avoid entering confidential business details unless that handling is acceptable. <br>
Risk: The skill can store local API credentials, cache data, and logs on the user's machine. <br>
Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and log directories, rotate or remove API credentials when no longer needed, and avoid sharing those files. <br>
Risk: Advertised setup and matrix-install behavior can add related skills or modify agent/client configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed setup actions first and do not enable auto-setup or force-install options unless you intend to change the agent/client configuration. <br>
Risk: Tax calculations and compliance guidance may be incomplete for a specific company, location, or filing period. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support, verify material conclusions against official requirements, and consult qualified tax, audit, or legal professionals for high-impact decisions. <br>
