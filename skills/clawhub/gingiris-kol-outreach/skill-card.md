## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bulk outreach or platform posting can create anti-spam, consent, or platform compliance issues. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a drafting and planning reference only until a human confirms lawful contact sources, opt-out handling, and platform and jurisdiction requirements. <br>
Risk: Creator contact lists, tracking data, and payment details may include personal or financial information. <br>
Mitigation: Do not let an agent automatically upload contact CSVs, collect bank details, or process payments; use approved secure workflows and minimize sensitive data collection. <br>
Risk: Automated execution could send outreach, post content, or approve creator terms before review. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit human approval before sending messages, publishing posts, accepting campaign terms, or releasing payments. <br>
