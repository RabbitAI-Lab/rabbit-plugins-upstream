## Description:

Create high-performing Instagram carousels with Canvora — right format, right slide count, hook-first content shaping, and the correct refine workflow. Use when the user wants an Instagram carousel, multi-slide IG post, swipe post, or educational/listicle content for Instagram.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[canvora](https://clawhub.ai/user/canvora)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating Instagram content use this skill to shape a topic or supplied copy into a carousel brief, choose the format and slide count, and refine generated slides.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Carousel content is sent to Canvora for generation.

Mitigation: Provide only content you are comfortable sharing with Canvora.

Risk: Generation and revisions spend account credits and usually proceed without separate approval.

Mitigation: Review the announced credit cost before generation and consult the separately installed CLI and setup skill.
