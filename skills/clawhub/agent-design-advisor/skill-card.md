## Description: <br>
Evaluates whether a workflow is suitable for AI agent implementation and provides best-practice design guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jiaxinmmhh](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiaxinmmhh) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and architects use this skill to assess whether a described workflow is a good fit for agent-based automation. When appropriate, it returns design recommendations for model capability, tool boundaries, instructions, orchestration, and safeguards. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the advisory output as a substitute for architecture, implementation, security, or cost review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as design assessment guidance only, and route final architecture, security, implementation, and cost decisions through the relevant review processes. <br>
