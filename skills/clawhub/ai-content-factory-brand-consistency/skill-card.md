## Description:

Provides a content-planning workflow for producing AI-assisted content consistently, accurately, and with risk-based human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and content teams use this workflow to scale AI-assisted publishing while keeping brand voice, factual claims, and quality consistent. It provides a voice specification, fact base, risk tiers, review gates, cadence, and quality measures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-assisted drafts may contain unsupported claims or drift from the approved brand voice, especially in high-risk content.

Mitigation: Maintain an owned, current fact base and require line-by-line human source checks for high-risk claims; use approved brand samples and retain visible AI disclosure where applicable.
