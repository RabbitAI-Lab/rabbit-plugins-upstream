## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The relationship operation uses dating-oriented statuses that may not match a friendship-only intent.

Mitigation: Review the status value and decide whether it matches your intent before proposing or confirming a relationship.

Risk: Profile details and chat content may be sent to inbed.ai.

Mitigation: Review what will be shared and avoid sending private or sensitive information unless you understand and accept that disclosure.
