## Description:

Guides real-estate agents in evaluating stalled listings and deciding whether, when, and how much to adjust the list price.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External real-estate agents use this skill to review showing and offer activity, days on market, feedback, comparable listings, and carrying costs when setting a list price or considering a reduction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing guidance based on incomplete or stale market evidence could mislead a seller.

Mitigation: Review current comparable listings, showing and offer feedback, days on market, and carrying costs before recommending or changing a price.

Risk: The referenced parent decision-tree process is not included in this package.

Mitigation: Use this as a pricing checklist and consult the full decision-tree process before relying on its branches.
