## Description: <br>
Helps an agent decide list price or price-reduction timing and size when a real estate listing is not converting showings into offers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Real estate agents use this skill to evaluate stalled listings, compare market and showing signals, choose whether to hold or reduce price, and size reductions around buyer search brackets. It is decision support, not appraisal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Pricing recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable if treated as appraisal advice or used without current market context. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as decision support and verify pricing decisions against current comparable sales, local regulations, and qualified appraisal or brokerage guidance. <br>
