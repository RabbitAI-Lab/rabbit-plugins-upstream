## Description: <br>
Helps agent users and skill authors create, repair, harden, and validate Multi Search Engine-style workflows and related productivity artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External ClawHub users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn demand for multi-search productivity workflows into practical plans, checklists, code changes, configuration guidance, and verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate too broadly for ordinary search or productivity requests because of broad trigger wording. <br>
Mitigation: Review and narrow trigger keywords or implicit invocation settings when precise routing is important. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow, setup, or code guidance may be incomplete or mismatched to the user's local constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Validate outputs against the stated success criteria and keep assumptions, limits, and follow-up checks visible. <br>
