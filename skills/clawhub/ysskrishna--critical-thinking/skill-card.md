## Description:

Helps an agent examine claims, plans, and decisions for weak assumptions, gaps in evidence, reasoning errors, and credible alternatives before reaching a proportionate conclusion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People reviewing a claim, argument, plan, or decision use this skill to identify unsupported premises, weigh evidence, consider alternatives, and state what would change their judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may inspect available files or documents when checking a claim, including private materials in the session.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with materials you are comfortable including in the analysis.
