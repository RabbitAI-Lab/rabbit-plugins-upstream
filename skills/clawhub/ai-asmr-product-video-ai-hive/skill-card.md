## Description:

Creates Chinese AI ASMR product-video workflows that turn product inputs, authorized media, channel constraints, and sound requirements into production blueprints, shot prompts, AI-HIVE generation commands, and acceptance checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to plan and execute AI ASMR product videos for ecommerce, advertising, social media, and product-content workflows. It helps convert product facts, authorized assets, platform requirements, budgets, and review constraints into scripts, prompts, commands, task records, and quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can use an AI-HIVE API key and upload user media to AI-HIVE.

Mitigation: Use only authorized media, confirm the user is comfortable sharing it with AI-HIVE, and keep API keys out of prompts, logs, screenshots, and repositories.

Risk: Generation calls may cost money and download generated files locally.

Mitigation: Review model, routing, price snapshot, prompt, and output path before execution; run a small sample before batch generation.

Risk: Generated product videos or scripts may imply inaccurate claims, endorsements, or platform suitability.

Mitigation: Verify product facts, claims, rights, and platform requirements with authoritative sources and human review before publication.
