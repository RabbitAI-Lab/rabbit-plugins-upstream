# Journalism: independent reporting, not sponsor copy

Load for reported features, news articles, data journalism and investigations. Distinguish verifiable facts, attributed allegations, forecasts, analysis and opinion. A corporate press release is an interested **source** and a PR artifact, not independent reporting; review it with [PR-AND-MEDIA-RELATIONS](PR-AND-MEDIA-RELATIONS.md).

## Three editorial obligations

1. **Reporting integrity:** trace contested assertions to primary, current and appropriately independent evidence; protect exact quotation, chronology, definitions, denominators, sourcing and meaningful missing perspectives. Never fabricate reporting or fix a quote by silently rewriting it.
2. **Reader sequence:** lead accurately; introduce essential context before readers form an irreversible misleading inference. A vivid scene may lead if it is genuine and correctly attributed, and the generalization is justified.
3. **House mechanics:** use the publication's explicitly selected guide for capitalization, dates, numerals and punctuation. The **University of Oxford Style Guide** is an Oxford institutional communications guide (2014 edition supplied), not the universal professional authority for journalism; use only when specifically adopted.

**Reuters** and **SPJ** prioritize verification, transparency, context and accurate quotation. **AP** treats news releases as information to evaluate, attribute and corroborate, not independently verified reportage. Do not equate a source statement with its truth.

## When data warrant a visual suggestion

Read [DATA-STORYTELLING](DATA-STORYTELLING.md) only when a prose passage forces readers to mentally compare many time points, categories, distributions or relationships. Suggest a chart or table *as a separate editorial option*, with necessary evidence checks, not as a forced graphic or new UI feature.

**Edit:** "The reform saves £20m, experts say" with only a department's untested model. "The department forecasts £20m in savings" and ask for methodology if the figure matters.

**Leave:** a vivid, verified witness account whose context and attribution follow before a broad claim; do not replace all reportage with a detached summary.
