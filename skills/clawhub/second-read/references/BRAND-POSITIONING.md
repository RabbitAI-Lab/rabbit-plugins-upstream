# Brand positioning: what this asset is part of

Load only when the author is writing **on behalf of**, or explicitly aligning to, a brand, product, publisher, company or campaign. Brand strategy precedes wordsmithing. Do not infer representation merely because the draft mentions a company.

## Resolve the positioning from actual project materials

For the intended audience, retain four separate concepts: **target**, **competitive frame/alternatives**, **distinctive value**, and **reason to believe**. Locate these in approved strategy, product sources, earlier assets or the official site; label absent items unknown. The company's description records its **intended positioning**, not proof that consumers perceive it that way.

A brand's **personality and voice** are the expression of its position, not a substitute for it. Jennifer Aaker's 1997 dimensions—sincerity, excitement, competence, sophistication and ruggedness—are an optional vocabulary for an explicitly relevant brand brief, not mandatory scores or universally proven cross-cultural buckets. Archetypes (Sage, Hero etc.) are optional creative frames if the project itself uses them, not scientific diagnoses.

## Cross-asset consequences

- Check whether the message reinforces or contradicts current product capabilities, differentiation and approved claims.
- An email and its landing page must promise the same available thing to the same reader; a campaign sequence may deliberately shift from awareness to action.
- Match the writer's distinct language, not a generic stereotype of "premium", "innovative" or "authentic" brands.
- An evocative brand line can work through context rather than enumerating features. Do not add qualifiers that destroy an effective image unless required to avoid a material misleading assertion.

**Edit:** a product claims "the fastest" without a defined comparison, time period or evidence. Suggest the documented capability, not a new unverified superiority claim.

**Leave:** "Read it twice. Send it once." is a sharp brand expression. No enforced Aaker score or extra call to action.

## Research foundation
