# Textual advertising: compact promises under constraints

Use for text ads and paid/social placements. Respect the declared platform, destination and campaign; if the actual placement or character limits are unknown, do not claim they were checked. The job is not to generate imagery or deceptive pressure.

## In-context checks

- **Offer:** does the text say exactly what is available to the specified audience, at the stated price and time?
- **Primary distinction:** what one benefit matters *to this reader* and is demonstrably supported? Never invent a testimonial or conversion statistic.
- **Destination:** is the promise repeated accurately where the reader lands?
- **Format:** headline/body/CTA limits depend on platform, locale, ad type, automated variants and current policy. Count characters with a tool when those inputs are known; otherwise label unverified.
- **Disclosures:** sponsorship, material relationships, financing, health or financial claims and restrictions may need specific disclosures based on jurisdiction and audience. Flag missing source/material rather than claim legal compliance.
- **Tone:** powerful brevity and humor can be deliberate. Avoid padding to sound “professional.”
- **Policy positions:** analyze political ad claims for evidence and scope without designing manipulative demographic targeting or partisan verdicts.

## Contrastive pairs

**Edit — impossible absolute**
- Original: “Never lose a file again.” Brief only supports automated backups once daily.
- Suggested: “Back up your files automatically each day.” State frequency only if authorized by the product brief or tested behavior.

**Leave — short context-dependent joke**
- A two-word reply completes a customer-initiated exchange. Do not force three features and a CTA into the reply; check the previous message's context first.

**Edit — misleading “free”**
- Original: “Free for everyone.” Terms say a seven-day trial needs a card.
- Suggested: “Try it for seven days” with required qualification from verified terms. Do not create an unapproved offer.

## Platform mechanics are not timeless prose rules

For Google responsive search ads, verify live format/length limits and test whether independently combined headlines remain accurate. **Google Ads Quality Score (1–10) is a diagnostic, not a direct input to Ad Rank**; do not use the inherited formula "Ad Rank = bid × Quality Score" as factual analysis. Current authoritative references: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722122 and https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118 .

## Compliance boundary

Check actual advertising laws and platform policies for the indicated audience. US baseline: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing and https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking . Do not impose US-specific statements as universal Greek/EU law. Platform policies change; verify current version when available.
