Vantri

How Vantri moved 14 teams off spreadsheets in three weeks.

Vantri builds routing software for regional freight carriers. When the platform group grew past 140 engineers, planning in spreadsheets stopped scaling.

Planning by spreadsheet, status by meeting

Before Tracklane, each of Vantri's 14 teams kept its own spreadsheet of work, and a shared one rolled them up for leadership. Every Monday, team leads spent the morning reconciling the two. Every Thursday, a status meeting tried to answer what would actually ship.

"We knew the work was getting done," says Rhea Kapoor, Head of Platform. "We just could not see it without asking forty people."

One team first, then all fourteen

Vantri started with a single pilot cycle on the Routing API team. The importer brought over 2,900 rows from that team's sheet in eleven minutes, including owners and estimates. Two weeks later the team had finished 91% of what it committed to, and the other leads asked to move.

  • Week 1: Routing API runs its first two-week cycle on Tracklane.
  • Week 2: Six more teams import their sheets and adopt the shared workflow.
  • Week 3: The remaining seven teams move, and SSO is enforced for everyone.

“Triage used to eat our Monday. Now it is a ten-minute pass before standup, and every issue lands with the right team.”

Rhea Kapoor, Head of Platform, Vantri

What changed

Triage moved into one queue with suggested owners, so incoming requests from support stopped bouncing between teams. Pull requests now move issues on their own, which made the Thursday status meeting unnecessary. Leadership reads the cycle page instead.

Carry-over, the work a team planned but did not finish, dropped by 61% within two quarters. Rhea's team now uses the extra planning time to review the backlog health view and close stale issues before they pile up.

Privacy policy

Last updated September 1, 2026. This policy explains what Tracklane Labs, Inc. collects, why, and the choices you have.

On this pageWhat we collectHow we use itWho we share it withHow long we keep itYour rightsContact

What we collect

Account details you give us (name, work email, workspace name), the content you create in your workspace (issues, comments, attachments), and technical data needed to run the service, such as IP address, browser type and error logs.

How we use it

To provide and secure Tracklane, to send service emails such as sign-in links and invoices, and to improve the product using aggregated usage data. We never sell personal data and never use workspace content to train machine learning models.

Who we share it with

Subprocessors that host and operate the service under contract, listed on our security page. We disclose data to authorities only when legally required and notify you first unless prohibited.

How long we keep it

Workspace content stays until you delete it. Deleted workspaces are removed from production within 30 days and from backups within 35 days after that.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data at any time from account settings, or by contacting us. EU and UK residents may also lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

Contact

Email privacy@tracklane.dev or write to Tracklane Labs, Inc., 1120 NW Couch Street, Portland, OR 97209.
