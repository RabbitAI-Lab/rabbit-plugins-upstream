## Description:

Guides an agent to identify survivorship bias, examine what missing non-survivors might show, and correct or qualify conclusions drawn from survivor samples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, developers, and decision-makers use this skill to examine claims based on successful companies, active funds, published studies, and other survivor-only samples. It guides a five-step check of the selection filter, missing population, and limits of the conclusion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Educational examples may contain factual claims that could be repeated in consequential decisions without verification.

Mitigation: Treat examples as educational material and verify factual claims before consequential decisions.

Risk: Recording real-use failure patterns could expose confidential details.

Mitigation: Omit confidential details when recording failure patterns.
