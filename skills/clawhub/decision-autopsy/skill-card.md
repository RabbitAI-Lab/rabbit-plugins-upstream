## Description: <br>
Decision Autopsy helps teams review past decisions by separating decision quality from outcome, reconstructing what was knowable at the time, accounting for luck, and identifying one repeatable process improvement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, operators, and decision owners use this skill after a major call has an observable outcome and the group needs a fair process-focused retrospective instead of an outcome-biased verdict. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Decision retrospectives may include confidential business, hiring, strategy, or personnel details. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the decision context needed for the review and avoid unnecessary confidential details unless the user is comfortable giving them to the agent handling the review. <br>
Risk: A process-forensics report can be overread as an objective final judgment on a team decision. <br>
Mitigation: Have accountable stakeholders review the report before using it to change process, and keep the focus on decision process rather than personal blame. <br>
