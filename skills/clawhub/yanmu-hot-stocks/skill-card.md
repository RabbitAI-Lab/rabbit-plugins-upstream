## Use Case:

External users and learners use this skill to choose from static A-share, Hong Kong, or U.S. stock examples for teaching and practice workflows. The listed companies are examples only and are not live market rankings or investment recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the static example stock list for current market data or investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep the demonstration disclosure visible and require independent financial research before any investment decision.

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broader investing questions where static examples are not responsive.

Mitigation: Limit use to teaching requests about selecting an example market or example stock, and decline to present the output as live recommendations.
