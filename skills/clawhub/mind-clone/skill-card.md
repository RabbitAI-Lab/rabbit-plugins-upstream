## Description:

Activate a Cognitive Digital Twin. Can simulate a custom subject (via core/ directory) OR load pre-installed celebrity/expert personas (from personas/ directory).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yzfly](https://clawhub.ai/user/yzfly)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users select a custom subject or an available persona and use the agent for conversational roleplay shaped by that profile.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Roleplay can produce invented responses, identities, recollections, or professional-sounding advice.

Mitigation: Select personas explicitly and treat their identities, recollections, and professional-sounding advice as generated roleplay.

Risk: Using custom memories or sharing birth details, images, or personal experiences may disclose personal information.

Mitigation: Share only birth details, images, personal experiences, or custom memories you are comfortable including in the conversation.
