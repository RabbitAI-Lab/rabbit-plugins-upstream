## Description:

Find and alert on new mentions of a brand across Reddit, X and TikTok, plus its hashtags on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube, using Juicer. Filters out posts that only share the name, drops the brand's own posts and sends a short daily digest to WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram or any connected chat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to monitor brand mentions and receive a concise daily or on-demand digest, with links, in a connected chat.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searches use Juicer credits, and daily automation can send recurring alerts to a connected chat.

Mitigation: Review Juicer permissions and credit costs, choose the delivery chat carefully, and enable daily automation only when recurring searches and messages are wanted.
