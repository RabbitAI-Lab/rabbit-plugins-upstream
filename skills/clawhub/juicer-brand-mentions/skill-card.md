## Description:

Find and alert on new mentions of a brand across Reddit, X and TikTok, plus its hashtags on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube, using Juicer. Filters out posts that only share the name, drops the brand's own posts and sends a short daily digest to WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram or any connected chat. Use for "tell me when someone mentions us", "brand monitoring", "mention alerts", "who is talking about my company".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and brand teams use this skill to find relevant public brand mentions, filter unrelated or duplicate posts, and receive a concise digest for follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searches consume Juicer credits, and recurring digests send monitoring results to the selected chat.

Mitigation: Review Juicer account permissions and expected credit costs, confirm the digest destination and schedule, and disable the automation when monitoring ends.
