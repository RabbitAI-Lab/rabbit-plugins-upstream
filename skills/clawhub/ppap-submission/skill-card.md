## Description:

Build and review AIAG PPAP Level 3 submission packages (18 elements, PSW, Cpk/Ppk) for automotive and electronics parts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sam-zhang01](https://clawhub.ai/user/sam-zhang01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Supplier quality, manufacturing, and compliance teams use this skill to prepare and review AIAG PPAP Level 3 submission packages. It helps check the 18 PPAP elements, PSW fields, process capability evidence, MSA results, consistency across manufacturing documents, and customer-specific requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PPAP materials may contain business-sensitive supplier, customer, product, or manufacturing information.

Mitigation: Provide only the documents needed for PPAP preparation or review and handle submitted files under the user's normal confidentiality and access-control process.

Risk: Checklist guidance can be incomplete if customer-specific requirements, current drawing revisions, or capability data are missing or outdated.

Mitigation: Validate the final package against the applicable AIAG PPAP edition, customer-specific requirements, and a qualified quality review before submission.
