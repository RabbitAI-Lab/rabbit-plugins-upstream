## Use Case:

External social media managers, content strategists, and TikTok growth consultants use this skill to compare hashtag reach, engagement, top videos, and creators for campaign research or post optimization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok hashtags, search terms, URLs, and scrape results may be sent to Apify under the user's Apify account.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when that data sharing is acceptable for the task and account.

Risk: APIFY_TOKEN exposure could grant unintended access to Apify resources.

Mitigation: Keep APIFY_TOKEN secret, inject credentials through MCP or a secure helper, and avoid pasting tokens into commands, screenshots, logs, or shared terminals.
