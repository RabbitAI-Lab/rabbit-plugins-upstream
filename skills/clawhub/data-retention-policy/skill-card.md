## Description: <br>
Build a data retention and deletion schedule grounded in legal basis for data categories, retention periods, legal or business bases, deletion triggers, deletion methods, and flags for data kept with no basis or defined period. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external teams, and developers use this skill to draft data retention and deletion schedules that connect each data category to a retention period, justification, deletion trigger, method, and affected systems. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated retention periods or legal bases may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific organization or jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Review the schedule with legal or compliance staff before treating it as binding policy. <br>
Risk: The artifact references a helper script that is not included in the released files. <br>
Mitigation: Do not rely on automated schedule validation or deletion-date calculation unless the helper is separately supplied and reviewed. <br>
