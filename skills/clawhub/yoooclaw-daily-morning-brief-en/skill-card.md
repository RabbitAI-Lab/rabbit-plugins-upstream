## Description: <br>
Organizes recent notifications into a concise morning brief with key updates, to-dos, missed items, follow-ups, decisions, and risks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and individual users use this skill to turn recent connected notifications into a morning action brief. It helps identify important updates, today's tasks, missed items, follow-ups, pending decisions, and delivery or scheduling risks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reads recent connected notifications, which may include sensitive personal or work information. <br>
Mitigation: Install it only when you want an agent to read notifications for a daily brief, and scope attention filters to the people, topics, groups, and applications that matter. <br>
Risk: Broad morning-report requests may search notification history from yesterday to now. <br>
Mitigation: Provide an explicit time range when a narrower brief is intended. <br>
