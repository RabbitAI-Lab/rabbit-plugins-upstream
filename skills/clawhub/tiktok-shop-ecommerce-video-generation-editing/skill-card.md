External sellers, commerce operators, and developers use this skill to generate or adapt product video assets for TikTok Shop pages, affiliate kits, livestream segments, and localized marketplaces while preserving product facts and approved claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected product media are sent to AI Hive for video generation.

Mitigation: Use only product media and prompts that are approved to share with AI Hive, and confirm rights for any creator or reference assets before upload.

Risk: Running init can store an AI Hive API key on the local machine.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key, prefer scoped or revocable credentials where available, and verify local configuration file permissions before use.

Risk: Generated commerce videos may contain unsupported claims, localization errors, or marketplace-policy issues.

Mitigation: Review generated assets against approved product facts, local language requirements, and current TikTok Shop or advertising rules before publishing.
