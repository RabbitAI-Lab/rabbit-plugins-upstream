## Description: <br>
Provides review-workflow scaffolding for context, evidence, and output so detailed reviews produce consistent, comparable findings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, engineers, and reviewers use this skill at the start of detailed architecture, code, API, math, or process reviews to establish context, inventory scope, capture evidence, and structure findings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording such as review, audit, and analysis may load the skill more often than expected. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the review scope and whether this scaffolding is needed before applying the workflow. <br>
Risk: Review workflows may include repo inventory commands and logged evidence from the workspace. <br>
Mitigation: Review captured commands, snippets, and evidence before sharing outputs, and redact sensitive workspace data. <br>
