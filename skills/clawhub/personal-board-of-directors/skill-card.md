## Description: <br>
Five standing advisors - the Operator, the Skeptic, the CFO, the Coach, the Customer - debate a decision, surface disagreements, vote under an explicit decision rule, and identify the one question to resolve before deciding. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to pressure-test personal or professional decisions by convening five named advisor perspectives, comparing their disagreement points, and reaching a recommendation with clear flip conditions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may trigger on broad decision-help requests and produce a recommendation where the user expected lighter brainstorming. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the decision statement, stakes, and reversibility before applying the full board process. <br>
Risk: Decision-support output can be incomplete or misleading when the input omits important constraints, numbers, or stakeholder facts. <br>
Mitigation: Review the advisor memos critically and resolve the named high-leverage question before committing. <br>
Risk: Users may include sensitive personal or financial details while describing a decision. <br>
Mitigation: Share only details needed for the decision and avoid unnecessary sensitive information. <br>
