## Description:

Read a plain-English request back to the person before anything runs. No price, no work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[specstream](https://clawhub.ai/user/specstream)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use the skill to review and refine a plain-English data request before any research or data gathering begins. It summarizes the requested item, required conditions, useful details, and quantity, and supports deliberate edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The request is sent to SpecStream for interpretation, even though compilation does not start subsequent data gathering.

Mitigation: Use the connector's sign-in, omit unnecessary confidential information, and review the service's permissions, retention, and sharing settings.
