## Description: <br>
Creates and maintains a shared product marketing context document that captures product, audience, positioning, competitor, voice, proof point, and goal details for downstream marketing tasks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing practitioners, founders, and product teams use this skill to create or update a local product marketing context document. The document gives other marketing skills shared context about the product, target audience, positioning, customer language, proof points, goals, and revision history. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Auto-drafting may carry forward inaccurate, incomplete, or stale positioning from project documentation and marketing files. <br>
Mitigation: Review and correct the generated `.agents/product-marketing.md` before relying on it across other marketing tasks. <br>
Risk: Substantive changes to the shared context document can affect downstream marketing skill outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Use the document version and changelog to track what changed, why it changed, and which sections need review. <br>
