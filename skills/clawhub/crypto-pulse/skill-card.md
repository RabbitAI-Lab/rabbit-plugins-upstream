## Description:

Provides public cryptocurrency prices, market rankings, trends, coin profiles, and historical market data through ClawLink.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to look up public cryptocurrency prices, market trends, coin details, and price history without connecting a private exchange account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing and pairing the ClawLink plugin requires a one-time sign-in.

Mitigation: Install only when requested and explain the pairing step before proceeding.

Risk: Market-data calls consume ClawLink credit.

Mitigation: Check current per-call prices and ask before expensive calls or large batches.

Risk: Crypto market data may be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Present results as public market data, not investment recommendations.
