## Description:

Creates host-ready livestream selling schedules, product talk tracks, compliant selling lines, cue cards, and spoken takes from a merchant's product lineup.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External merchants, livestream hosts, and floor managers use the skill to plan and run product-selling sessions with minute-by-minute schedules, product demonstrations, ready-to-read lines, and production-ready cue cards and spoken takes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires lasting account permissions that extend beyond image and speech production, including shared video, music, and voice-modification access; removing this skill may leave a shared connection active for other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Review the requested access before connecting and use narrower authorization where supported. When access should end, revoke the device in Beatra Console; uninstalling this skill alone may not disconnect shared access.

Risk: Installation analytics run automatically.

Mitigation: Review the service's privacy and analytics terms before installation if automatic analytics are not acceptable.
