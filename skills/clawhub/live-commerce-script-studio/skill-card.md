## Description:

Turn a product lineup into a minute-by-minute live selling schedule, per-product talk tracks, ready-to-read host lines, compliance checks, and production-ready cards and spoken takes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External merchants, brands, creators, and commerce teams use this skill to plan livestream selling sessions and generate host-ready run-of-show materials before producing cards and spoken delivery examples through Beatra tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad Beatra account access with spending and media/tool capabilities.

Mitigation: Review the Beatra authorization, account permissions, and device access before installation or use.

Risk: The bundled client can update package-owned files silently by default.

Mitigation: Disable automatic updates with the documented update control when review before update is required.

Risk: Generated cards can contain incorrect or illegible figures that would affect a live selling session.

Mitigation: Read every rendered card figure back against the approved schedule before it is used on air.
