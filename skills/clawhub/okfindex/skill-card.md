## Description:

Find Open Knowledge Format bundles and read their source links with OKF Index.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wrpbr](https://clawhub.ai/user/wrpbr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and developers use this skill to find public OKF bundles and Brazilian federal legislation, read indexed results, and cite their source links and dates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searches and lookups are sent to a remote OKF Index service.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive queries without authorization.

Risk: Indexed documents may contain misleading instructions or outdated legal material.

Mitigation: Treat results as reference material, preserve source dates and provenance, and independently verify legal currency when accuracy matters.
