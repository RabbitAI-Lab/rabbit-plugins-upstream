## Description:

Guides developers through requirements, technical design, and task planning before implementing medium-to-large changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[binggg](https://clawhub.ai/user/binggg)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to turn complex or unclear change requests into reviewable requirements, design decisions, and implementation tasks before coding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The staged workflow may slow delivery, and generated plans may miss requirements.

Mitigation: Review the requirements, design, and tasks with the user before approving implementation.
