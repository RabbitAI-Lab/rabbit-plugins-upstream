## Description:

Diagnose and score cohesion and flow writing quality in English psychology or neuroscience Results sections across connectives and transitions, sentence-to-sentence cohesion, paragraph coherence, and information flow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bhlia](https://clawhub.ai/user/bhlia)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, editors, reviewers, and academic writing assistants use this skill to evaluate the cohesion and flow of a supplied Results section and receive prioritized revision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill analyzes manuscript text supplied by the user and may quote portions of that text in its report.

Mitigation: Review inputs for confidentiality before use and inspect generated quotes before sharing reports.
