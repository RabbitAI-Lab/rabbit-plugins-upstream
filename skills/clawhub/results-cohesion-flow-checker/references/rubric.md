# Rubric — Results 衔接与连贯四维评分量规

每维按 1–5 评分，下列 1/3/5 三档锚点（2 分介于 1–3 之间，4 分介于 3–5 之间）。
锚点示例逐字引自溯源库（notes_A/B/C），保留论文编号+期刊年份+Results 小节标注。

---

## D1 连接词与过渡语（connectives & transitions）

**5 分 — 优秀范例水平**：过渡语准确标示逻辑关系（追加/对比/顺承/总结），密度适中，常锚定假设或前文结果，读者无需回看。
- 锚点示例："In contrast to our encoding results, we did not find schema reinstatement effects in mPFC (Figure 2D, p>0.5) nor SFG (p>0.5). However, we did find schema reinstatement effects in PMC (p<0.01), PHC (p<0.01), and AG (p<0.01)."（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Reinstatement scores）
- 锚点示例："In line with Hypothesis 1a, the main effect of health outcome value was significant… Contrary to Hypothesis 1b, there was no significant main effect of taste outcome value."（B1, CRPI 2023, Study 1, Results – Food choices）

**3 分 — 合格**：关键逻辑节点（对比、追加、总结）大多有标记，偶有关系模糊或缺失；无系统性误用。
- 典型状态：多数段落有 however/also/thus 等基本标记，但个别对比处无连接词、或过渡语与逻辑关系不完全匹配。

**1 分 — 严重问题**：段落几乎无过渡标记，句间逻辑关系靠读者猜测；或过渡语系统性误用（however 用于顺承、therefore 无因果依据、连续堆砌 Moreover/Furthermore）。

---

## D2 句间衔接（sentence-to-sentence cohesion）

**5 分 — 优秀范例水平**：指代链清晰（this/these/such + 名词），关键词跨句复现形成词汇链，缩写先定义后使用，关系从句指代明确。
- 锚点示例："We derived two types of neural scores that reflected the extent to which story-specific and general schematic information were represented during encoding (Figure 1C). These scores were computed in both searchlights and specific ROIs…"（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Encoding scores）
- 锚点示例："Experiment 1 identified a cluster of brain regions centred on the left inferior frontal gyrus… Thus, the aim of Experiment 2 was to investigate whether individual differences in the functional interaction of this cluster with any other brain region…"（A2, Nat Commun 2021, Results > Individual differences）

**3 分 — 合格**：多数指代可解，核心术语基本一致；偶见裸 this/it 指代不明或同一概念换用不同术语。
- 典型状态：偶发 "This shows that…"（this 指代整句无名词承载），或 effect/result/finding 随意互换但尚能追踪。

**1 分 — 严重问题**：this/these/it/they 频繁指代不明；同一概念反复换名（如 scores→indices→measures 无定义）；连续 3 句以上无任何衔接手段（无指代、无复现、无连接词）。

---

## D3 段落连贯（paragraph coherence）

**5 分 — 优秀范例水平**：每段以主题句明示分析目的开头，段内按 总→分 或 目的→手段→结果 推进，段末有收束句回扣开头。
- 锚点示例："To summarize the key regions in which schematic representations were robustly activated and supported memory performance, we intersected regions of the brain that showed significant schema scores and also showed a positive correlation with later memory for story details (Figure 4A)."（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Intersection… 段首主题句）
- 锚点示例："Taken together, the results of this initial comparison are in line with prior investigations that indicate a level of separation in the neural basis of semantic and episodic memory retrieval (Fig. 2c, d)."（A2, Nat Commun 2021, p.3 段末收束）
- 锚点示例（环形结构）：段首 "In particular, we hypothesized that attention could selectively enhance target representations…" → 段末 "These results support our hypothesis that attention could enhance target representations and suppress distractor representations…"（A3, PLOS Biol 2024, Results > Selective attention enhances target representations）

**3 分 — 合格**：多数段落有主题句或可推断的中心，推进基本有序；偶见段落中途偏题或缺少收束。
- 典型状态：段首直接报统计量（"The main effect was significant, F(…)"）而无目的句，但段内内容尚集中。

**1 分 — 严重问题**：段落无主题句、句子堆砌多个不相关分析结果；无推进逻辑（检验顺序随机）；段末戛然而止或以无关内容结尾。

---

## D4 信息流（information flow）

**5 分 — 优秀范例水平**：句首旧信息、句尾新信息；相邻句保持话题延续；新话题以过渡标记开启。
- 锚点示例："In the flankers paradigm, both the lateralized readiness potential (LRP) and the error-related negativity (ERN) were observed. The LRP was a more negative voltage over the motor cortex contralateral to the response hand… and the ERN was a more negative voltage over midline frontocentral cortex…"（C1, NeuroImage 2021, §3.1 Basic ERP Effects）——首句引入 LRP/ERN（new），次句以 The LRP/The ERN（given）承接。
- 锚点示例："Notably, the latency and waveform of the arousal-locked fMRI signatures differed across the thalamus and cortex. The thalamus activated before behavioral arousal… while the cortex deactivated afterward…"（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results）
- 锚点示例："We found striking variability within factual recall… Variability and proportion correct within all three question types in Experiment 1 is illustrated in Fig. 3."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion）

**3 分 — 合格**：多数句组遵循 given→new；偶有新信息置于句首造成轻微跳跃，但话题链可恢复。
- 典型状态：偶见句首突然出现未引入的术语（如 "Alpha-band activity was also reduced…" 而 alpha-band 此前未提及）。

**1 分 — 严重问题**：频繁以全新信息开句造成话题断裂；相邻句主语不断切换（每句换一个话题）；结果罗列如清单，读者无法判断哪条信息是焦点。

## 扣分项清单（typical manifestations）

| # | 扣分项 | 典型表现 | 涉及维度 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 段落无主题句 | 段首直接报 F/t/p 值，读者不知本段分析目的 | D3 |
| 2 | 连续 3 句以上无衔接手段 | 既无连接词、无指代、也无词汇复现，句子如并列清单 | D1/D2 |
| 3 | this/these/it 指代不明 | 裸 "This suggests…" 而前文有多个候选先行词 | D2 |
| 4 | 新信息出现在句首造成话题断裂 | 未引入的术语/条件突然作主语，与前句无承接 | D4 |
| 5 | 过渡语误用 | however 用于顺承语境；therefore 前后无因果；in contrast 对比双方不平行 | D1 |
| 6 | 过渡语堆砌 | 每句都以 Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally 开头 | D1 |
| 7 | 术语漂移 | 同一概念在无定义情况下换用不同名称 | D2 |
| 8 | 段末无收束 | 长段以孤立统计结果结尾，无小结或回扣 | D3 |
| 9 | 话题链顺序不一致 | 前文按 A→B→C 列举，后文讨论顺序打乱为 C→A→B | D4 |
| 10 | 缩写/符号未定义即用 | 首次出现即用缩写（如 CIM/AIM 无全称） | D2 |
| 11 | 定指预设失败 | 概念首次出现即用定冠词/定指形式（The Stroop effect / the P300 / our hypothesis），前文从未引入 | D2/D4 |
