# Examples — Results 衔接与连贯正例库（30 条真实例句 + constructed 对照）

所有真实例句逐字取自溯源库 notes_A/B/C，溯源标注格式：
（论文编号, 期刊 年份, Results 小节）。
- A1 = eLife 2022（schema/narrative memory, fMRI）
- A2 = Nature Communications 2021（cognitive control, fMRI）
- A3 = PLOS Biology 2024（goal-directed attention, fMRI）
- B1 = CRPI 2023（food choice）
- B2 = Collabra: Psychology 2024（disgust/moral character）
- B3 = CRPI 2022（naturalistic learning）
- C1 = NeuroImage 2021（ERP CORE）
- C2 = Nature Communications 2022（thalamic arousal）

「constructed example」为编写者构造的问题句，仅作教学对照，非真实文献。

## D1 连接词与过渡语

### D1a 追加（addition）

1. "Additionally, there were strong schematic patterns in anterior but not posterior hippocampus (p<0.01 for whole hippocampus and anterior hippocampus; p=0.28 for posterior hippocampus)."（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Encoding scores）
2. "Moreover, in addition to the observed main effects at the group level, individual variability in brain activity also depicted significant correlations across the two memory types."（A2, Nat Commun 2021, Results > Common control processes…）
3. "We also observed main effects of question type in Experiment 2b, depicted in Fig. 4: open-ended (F(2,87)=20.74, p<0.001, ηp²=0.42) and forced-choice (F(2,87)=25.66, p<0.001, ηp²=0.47) conditions."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiments 2a/2b, Results and discussion）

### D1b 对比（contrast）

4. "In contrast to our encoding results, we did not find schema reinstatement effects in mPFC (Figure 2D, p>0.5) nor SFG (p>0.5). However, we did find schema reinstatement effects in PMC (p<0.01), PHC (p<0.01), and AG (p<0.01)."（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Reinstatement scores）
5. "In contrast, the attended items in the retrospective condition had lower accuracy (t(52) = −4.67, p < 0.001) and lower confidence levels for remembered items (t(52) = −3.99, p < 0.001) than those in the baseline condition."（A3, PLOS Biol 2024, Results > Selective attention improves long-term memory performance of targets）
6. "Conversely, the same cluster showed negative connectivity (or anti-correlations) with the default mode and motor networks (Supplementary Fig. S5)."（A2, Nat Commun 2021, Results > Individual differences…）
7. "Nevertheless, there were also significant cue-by-attention interactions in all ROIs (all corrected ps < 0.001), suggesting that the prospective and retrospective had different modulation efficiencies."（A3, PLOS Biol 2024, Results > Selective attention enhances target representations）
8. "The vast majority of the cortex did not show a significant increase in arousal (26/30, two-sided t-test Bonferroni corrected); however, there were a few notable exceptions (Fig. 2b and Fig. S2), including the caudal anterior cingulate cortex (cACC) and posterior cingulate cortex (PCC)."（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results）

### D1c 顺承/推进（continuation）

9. "As in Experiment 2a, under forced-choice conditions, participants were more successful on factual questions than both inference (p<0.001) and integration (p<0.001), which did not differ from each other (p=0.669)."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiments 2a/2b, Results and discussion）
10. "Researchers who use the ERP CORE paradigms may wish to compare the frequency-domain characteristics of their data with the CORE data… We therefore computed the amplitude density of the EEG signal at each frequency ranging from 1 to 100 Hz (see Materials and Methods for details)."（C1, NeuroImage 2021, §3.3 EEG Spectral Quantification）
11. "We then repeated our analysis of the thalamocortical activity locked to arousal and replicated our observation that the thalamus activated beforehand (20% latency = −2.59 s, 95% CI: (−6.11 s, −0.93 s)) and the cortex deactivated afterward..."（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results, 7 T 结果段）

### D1d 总结/收束（summary）

12. "Taken together, the results of this initial comparison are in line with prior investigations that indicate a level of separation in the neural basis of semantic and episodic memory retrieval (Fig. 2c, d)."（A2, Nat Commun 2021, p.3）
13. "Overall, these results indicate shared neural responses during the retrieval of weakly associated word pairs across the two memory types."（A2, Nat Commun 2021, p.4）
14. "Together, these three measures of noise will allow researchers to determine how their noise levels compare to the noise levels in our data."（C1, NeuroImage 2021, §3.4 收尾段）

### D1 对照：问题句 → 修改后

- ❌ constructed example: "Moreover, the interaction was significant. Moreover, the simple effect of condition was significant. Moreover, the pairwise comparisons showed differences."
  （过渡语堆砌，Moreover 三连）
- ✅ 修改后: "The interaction was significant, F(1, 60) = 8.42, p = .005. We therefore performed simple effect tests at each level of condition; these tests showed that the effect was driven by the high-load condition."
  （仅在关键因果节点使用 therefore，指代链 "these tests" 衔接，无需逐句加过渡语——范式见例 2、例 10）

- ❌ constructed example: "The training group improved significantly. However, the control group also improved significantly."
  （however 误用：两句为同向结果，无转折关系）
- ✅ 修改后: "The training group improved significantly. The control group also improved significantly, suggesting that the improvement was not specific to the training."
  （同向追加用 also；若确为意外结果可用 "Unexpectedly"——范式见例 2 "also"、例 8 "however 引出真例外"）

## D2 句间衔接（指代链 / 词汇复现）

### D2a 指代链（this/these/such + 名词）

15. "We derived two types of neural scores that reflected the extent to which story-specific and general schematic information were represented during encoding (Figure 1C). These scores were computed in both searchlights and specific ROIs…"（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Encoding scores）
16. "Experiment 1 identified a cluster of brain regions centred on the left inferior frontal gyrus and the anterior insular cortex that was commonly engaged in the retrieval of weakly associated word pairs… Thus, the aim of Experiment 2 was to investigate whether individual differences in the functional interaction of this cluster with any other brain region…"（A2, Nat Commun 2021, Results > Individual differences…）
17. "Having observed expected differences in the neural instantiations of semantic and episodic memory, our next objective was to test whether such effects could be related to variations in memory strength…"（A2, Nat Commun 2021, Results > Common control processes… 段首）
18. "Therefore, we investigated whether this same thalamocortical pattern was present when analyzing only arousals from the deeper stages of sleep, yielding 50 arousals from five subjects."（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results）
19. "Did perceptual attention filter out all the distractor information in the brain? To test this, we conducted a comparison of the ignored items and the other items in the prospective condition."（A3, PLOS Biol 2024, Results > Selective attention enhances target representations）

### D2b 词汇复现 / 同义复现

20. "The correlation between encoding story scores and subsequent memory was significantly more positive for posterior vs. anterior hippocampus… We found a sparser set of regions when using encoding schema scores to predict behavior."（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Memory as a function of encoding story and schema scores）
21. "Following the steps described by Sommet and Morselli (2017), we compared a constrained intermediate model (CIM) with an augmented intermediate model (AIM). … The CIM included available response time as a fixed effect, whereas the AIM allowed for participant-specific random slope variance. Here, the results of the AIM are reported, which provided the better fit."（B1, CRPI 2023, Study 1, Results – Food choices）——缩写先定义后使用 + which 从句回扣
22. "A post hoc Holm–Bonferroni test conducted using JASP software revealed that participants were more successful on open-ended factual recall questions than inferential reasoning (p<0.001) and self-derivation through memory integration (p<0.001) questions, which did not differ from each other (p=0.284)."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion）
23. "The same pattern of performance was observed even with the additional support provided by forced-choice options, as depicted in Fig. 3. That is, there was a significant effect of question type (F(2, 87)=32.58, p<0.001, ηp²=0.53)."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion）——名词化回指（encapsulation）

### D2 对照：问题句 → 修改后

- ❌ constructed example: "We computed an attention modulation index for each ROI. This was significantly greater in the prospective condition. This suggests that attention enhanced target representations."
  （裸 this 两次指代不明）
- ✅ 修改后: "We computed an attention modulation index for each ROI. This index was significantly greater in the prospective condition, suggesting that attention enhanced target representations."
  （this + 名词；用 suggesting that 合并——范式见例 15 "These scores"、例 7）

- ❌ constructed example: "The effect was robust in the dACC. The finding was also present in the insula. The result did not survive correction in the thalamus."
  （effect/finding/result 术语漂移，且无任何指代衔接）
- ✅ 修改后: "The effect was robust in the dACC and was also present in the insula; however, this effect did not survive correction in the thalamus."
  （同一概念保持同一名称 + this effect 回指——范式见例 18 "this same thalamocortical pattern"）

## D3 段落连贯（主题句 / 段末收束）

### D3a 主题句（段首明示分析目的）

24. "To summarize the key regions in which schematic representations were robustly activated and supported memory performance, we intersected regions of the brain that showed significant schema scores and also showed a positive correlation with later memory for story details (Figure 4A)."（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Intersection of significant schema effects and subsequent memory effects）
25. "The main goal of Experiment 1 was to test three learning outcomes from naturalistic virtual museum exhibits, without giving participants the explicit opportunity to re-engage with previously learned material. We first calculated task performance across all three question types (see Table 1)."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion 段首）
26. "The purpose of Experiments 2a and 2b was to test the influence of re-engagement as a mechanism of support for learning outcomes from naturalistic virtual museum exhibits… To meet this goal, we first analyzed task performance within each experiment."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiments 2a/2b, Results and discussion 段首）
27. "We aimed to identify the thalamocortical dynamics underlying behavioral arousal state transitions by imaging participants spontaneously transitioning between sleep and wakefulness during nighttime fMRI scans."（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results 首段）
28. "The ERP waveforms in Figure 2 show that the expected experimental effects were observed for all seven components."（C1, NeuroImage 2021, §3.1 Basic ERP Effects）——总-分主题句
29. "To test whether the values of health and/or taste outcomes differed across or between priming conditions, we conducted a mixed-model ANOVA …"（B1, CRPI 2023, Study 1, Results – Descriptive results）

### D3b 段末收束 / 环形结构

30. "These results support our hypothesis that attention could enhance target representations and suppress distractor representations [4,7], leading to better behavioral performance in the prospective condition than in the retrospective condition."（A3, PLOS Biol 2024, Results > Selective attention enhances target representations 小节末句；回扣段首 "we hypothesized that…"）
31. "Overall, these results indicate shared neural responses during the retrieval of weakly associated word pairs across the two memory types. As such, our findings suggest that at least part of the memory type differences observed in our initial analysis and in other prior investigations might arise due to underlying differences in memory strength…"（A2, Nat Commun 2021, p.4）
32. "Results suggest that, with naturalistic materials, participants successfully engaged in all three learning processes. Factual information was the most accessible to participants."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion 段末）

### D3 对照：问题句 → 修改后

- ❌ constructed example（段首）: "A two-way ANOVA was conducted. The main effect of cue was significant, F(1, 52) = 6.31, p = .015. The interaction was not significant."
  （无主题句，直接堆统计）
- ✅ 修改后: "To test whether cue type modulated neural evidence in the encoding phase, we conducted a two-way ANOVA within each ROI. The main effect of cue was significant, F(1, 52) = 6.31, p = .015, whereas the interaction was not significant."
  （范式见例 24、例 29 "To test whether…, we conducted…"）

- ❌ constructed example（段末）: "…and the difference in the right IFG was marginally significant (p = .06)."（长段以一个边缘显著的孤立统计收尾，无小结）
- ✅ 修改后: "…and the difference in the right IFG was marginally significant (p = .06). Taken together, these results indicate that weak associations consistently engaged left-lateralised control regions across both memory types."
  （范式见例 12 "Taken together…"、例 31 "Overall, these results indicate…"）

## D4 信息流（given→new 句组 / 话题延续）

### D4a given→new 句组

33. "In the flankers paradigm, both the lateralized readiness potential (LRP) and the error-related negativity (ERN) were observed. The LRP was a more negative voltage over the motor cortex contralateral to the response hand compared to the ipsilateral side, and the ERN was a more negative voltage over midline frontocentral cortex for incorrect responses compared to correct responses."（C1, NeuroImage 2021, §3.1 Basic ERP Effects）
34. "Notably, the latency and waveform of the arousal-locked fMRI signatures differed across the thalamus and cortex. The thalamus activated before behavioral arousal, with a latency (defined as the time at 20% of maximum absolute signal) of −1.65 s before arousal (95% confidence interval, CI: (−3.62 s, 0 s)), while the cortex deactivated afterward with a latency of 4.77 s after arousal (95% CI: (3.76 s, 5.69 s))."（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results）
35. "We found striking variability within factual recall (0–85% correct), inferential reasoning (0–64% correct) and self-derivation through memory integration (0–64% correct). Variability and proportion correct within all three question types in Experiment 1 is illustrated in Fig. 3."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion）
36. "We found significant reinstatement story scores in regions overlapping with the default mode network (DMN), particularly lateral posterior SFG, central middle temporal gyrus, PHC, and AG with strongest effects in PMC (Figure 2C, q < 0.05). Our specific ROI analyses (Figure 2C) also showed strong reinstatement story scores in the same regions such as PMC (p<0.01)…"（A1, eLife 2022, Results > Reinstatement scores）
37. "The main effect of time was significant, F(1, 315)=130.21, p<0.001, η²p=0.29, indicating that participants were less hungry at the start of the experiment (M=4.88, SD=2.66) than at the end (M=5.86, SD=2.96)."（B1, CRPI 2023, Study 2, Results – Hunger）——先旧（主效应显著性）后新（方向与描述统计）

### D4b 话题延续 / 平行话题结构

38. "From the expression of disgust, participants inferred that the focus of condemnation was more likely the wrongdoer's bad moral character (M = 5.62, SD = 1.29) than harm caused by the wrongdoer (M = 4.89, SD = 1.38), F(1, 222) = 12.38, p = .001, η²p = .05, but from the expression of anger, they inferred that the focus of condemnation was more likely harm caused (M = 5.27, SD = 1.32) than bad moral character (M = 4.43, SD = 1.50) …"（B2, Collabra 2024, Experiment 1, Results – H1a）——句首状语给条件（given），平行分句保持话题链
39. "In forced-choice testing, performance was significantly different from chance (33%) for factual recall (p<0.001), inferential reasoning (p<0.001), and self-derivation through memory integration (p<0.001)."（B3, CRPI 2022, Experiment 1, Results and discussion）——列举顺序与前文一致
40. "While our initial results identified a major divergence in overall cortical and thalamic activity at behavioral arousal, we next aimed to determine whether these dynamics were consistent within individual thalamic nuclei and cortical regions or unfolded heterogeneously across subregions."（C2, Nat Commun 2022, Results）——While 从句回指旧结果，主句提新问题

### D4 对照：问题句 → 修改后

- ❌ constructed example: "A significant LRP and a significant ERN were found. Contralateral motor cortex showed more negative voltage for the LRP. Incorrect responses elicited more negative frontocentral voltage for the ERN."
  （第二、三句以新信息（脑区/反应类型）开句，话题断裂）
- ✅ 修改后: "In the flankers paradigm, both the lateralized readiness potential (LRP) and the error-related negativity (ERN) were observed. The LRP was a more negative voltage over contralateral motor cortex, and the ERN was a more negative voltage over frontocentral cortex for incorrect responses."
  （首句引入两个成分作 new，次句以 The LRP/The ERN 作 given 承接——范式见例 33）

- ❌ constructed example: "Theta power increased in the frontal midline. Alpha suppression was observed over parietal sites. P300 amplitude was larger for targets. Theta power also correlated with accuracy."
  （每句换话题，theta 话题被切断后又回来）
- ✅ 修改后: "Theta power increased in the frontal midline and also correlated with accuracy. In addition, alpha suppression was observed over parietal sites, and P300 amplitude was larger for targets."
  （同一话题集中说完再切换，切换处有 In addition——范式见例 38 的平行话题结构、例 2 的追加标记）
