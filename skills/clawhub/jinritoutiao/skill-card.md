## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated content or optimization advice may be inaccurate, outdated, or inconsistent with current Toutiao platform rules. <br>
Mitigation: Review advice against current platform policies and account backend data before publishing or changing operations. <br>
Risk: Headline and engagement tactics could drift into misleading clickbait, unsupported monetization claims, or non-compliant growth tactics. <br>
Mitigation: Check titles, claims, and calls to action for factual support, audience fit, and platform compliance before use. <br>
