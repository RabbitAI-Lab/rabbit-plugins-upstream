## Description: <br>
Helps agents recognize and counter anchoring bias in negotiations, valuations, forecasts, and other high-stakes numeric decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and external users use this skill to identify anchors, classify whether a number is informative, generate an anchor-free counterfactual, and decide how to open, counter, reset, or forecast around consequential numbers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Anchoring analysis can shape negotiation, valuation, salary, settlement, or forecasting decisions and may be over-relied on as professional advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support, verify factual market data independently, and seek qualified financial or legal review for high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: An agent may mistake a verifiable data point for a pure anchor and discount relevant evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's informational-content check before acting: distinguish genuine data, partial information, and pure anchors. <br>
