## Description: <br>
Codifies a brand-level voice and naming canon from a durable message house and the brand's own published material, including register, tone spectrum, banned phrases, few-shot examples, product or tier naming rules, and approved or banned terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, brand, and narrative teams use this skill to turn an existing message house and brand-owned samples into reusable voice and naming rules before scaling channel copy. It helps agents stage canon-grade guidance while keeping per-channel adaptation, finished copy, message hierarchy, and claim adjudication out of scope. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brand samples, scraped pages, or exports may contain embedded instructions or misleading product claims. <br>
Mitigation: Treat all source material as untrusted input, use only brand-owned material for voice examples, and route unresolved factual claims to review as needs-source proposals. <br>
Risk: Draft voice or naming rules could introduce incorrect guidance into project memory or future canon decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated drafts and registry proposal events before promotion; keep narrative-registry as the sole canon writer. <br>
Risk: Optional public-page scraping reads external brand surfaces and may persist derived guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm user comfort before installation or use, respect the skill's public-source and robots preflight constraints, and save memory only after explicit confirmation. <br>
