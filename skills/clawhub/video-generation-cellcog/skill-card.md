## Description: <br>
AI video generation and production powered by CellCog for marketing videos, product demos, explainers, educational content, lipsync spokesperson videos, UGC, news reports, training materials, cinematic short films, social media reels, and YouTube content up to 4 minutes from a single prompt. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and content teams use this skill to direct an agent to create marketing, educational, explainer, spokesperson, social, and cinematic videos through CellCog from a prompt or script. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, scripts, and source media are sent to CellCog for processing. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting secrets, confidential business data, regulated data, or personal media unless organizational policy permits it and CellCog's data handling terms have been reviewed. <br>
Risk: Long-form AI video results may be unpredictable and may consume credits without producing a usable result. <br>
Mitigation: Use specific prompts for duration, aspect ratio, style, audio, and key moments, and review generated outputs before production use. <br>
