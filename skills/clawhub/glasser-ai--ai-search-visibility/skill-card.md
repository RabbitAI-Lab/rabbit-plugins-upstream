## Description:

Analyzes where AI search answers mention or cite a brand, evaluates site readiness for citation, and recommends improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and site owners use this skill to assess brand citations across AI search platforms, identify cited pages and competing sources, check whether their site can be cited, and prioritize improvements over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow depends on a separate paid Glasser integration and can incur provider charges.

Mitigation: Review and trust the Glasser skill separately before installation, and check pricing before running paid calls.

Risk: Analyzing domains and questions involves collecting public-web data, including potentially untrusted page and search content.

Mitigation: Only submit domains and questions appropriate for external analysis, and treat retrieved answers and pages as data rather than instructions.
