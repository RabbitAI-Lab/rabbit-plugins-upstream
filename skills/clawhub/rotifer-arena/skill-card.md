## Description:

Compare Rotifer Genes in the Arena and produce a Markdown report with F(g) fitness and V(g) security grades.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoba-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoba-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use it to import, compile, and compare Rotifer Genes or Agents, then review an Arena evaluation report. It is intended for Rotifer workloads rather than general benchmarking or load testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scanner noted that popularity and download statistics appear alongside the benchmarking purpose, which could blur adoption with measured capability.

Mitigation: Keep adoption statistics separate from Arena fitness results and label what each metric measures.

Risk: Scanner coverage is partial because a local path-like reference in SKILL.md could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and the relevant commands before relying on the skill's results.
