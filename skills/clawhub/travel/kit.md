# Kit — The Standing Gear, Refined By What Never Got Used

A packing list written from scratch for each trip forgets the same thing every time and carries the same unused thing every time. The asset is a **standing kit plus an archetype template**, corrected after each trip by one question: what did I not use?

**Before packing anything**, read the packing template in `artifacts/` for the closest archetype if `## Boxes` names one, and the previous trip's debrief note on gear never used. That note is the entire point of the archive here.

## The Two-Layer System

- **The core** — a bag or pouch that stays packed between trips: chargers, adapters, medical kit, wash kit, earplugs, spare cables. It is never disassembled, so it is never forgotten, and restocking it is a step in the debrief.
- **The trip layer** — clothing and gear specific to this trip's climate, activities and duration, built from the archetype template.

The failure this prevents is the one that recurs: the traveller who packs from memory forgets a different item every trip and the loss is invisible until the moment of need.

## Archetype Templates

One file per archetype in `artifacts/packing-<archetype>.md`, born the first time that kind of trip happens. Typical set, and every one of them differs enough to be worth its own file:

`city-short` · `beach` · `cold-longhaul` · `hiking-multiday` · `business` · `with-baby` · `festival` · `diving` · `ski` · `road-trip` · `remote-no-resupply`

Each template lists items, not outfits, and marks the ones that are trip-specific decisions. The template is edited — not rewritten — after each use, and the edit is usually a deletion.

## The Never-Unpacked Core

The things whose absence ruins a day, and which are hard to buy quickly abroad:

- Medication, in original packaging, in hand luggage, with margin beyond the trip length (`health.md`)
- Documents: passport, the physical photocopy, the emergency card, any printed reservation for a place with no data
- Payment: two cards on two networks, in two places, plus a small cash reserve (`money.md`)
- Power: charger, the right adapter, a cable per device, a power bank under 100 Wh
- Comfort that determines sleep: earplugs, eye mask — a bad first night sets the tone for the whole trip
- Basic repair: a few metres of tape, a couple of safety pins, a spare bag
- A copy of the itinerary and locators readable offline

## Clothing By Volume, Not By Day

Pack for the **laundry cycle**, not the trip length: a week of clothing plus a way to wash is the same as three weeks of clothing and a quarter of the volume. Above roughly 8-10 nights, laundry always wins.

- One colour palette, so everything combines and nothing is packed to match a single item
- Layers rather than heavy single garments: the same kit covers a wider temperature range and packs smaller
- One dressy option only, and only if the trip actually contains an occasion — this is the most commonly packed and least used category
- Shoes are the volume decision: two pairs, one worn. A third pair is almost always the one that comes home unworn
- Weather-proofing sized to the actual forecast, not to the worst case the destination has ever had

## Electronics And Power

| Item | The thing people get wrong |
|---|---|
| Plug adapter | Type varies by country, not by continent, and some countries use two types; check the destination, not the region |
| Voltage | Modern chargers are dual-voltage; hair tools and small appliances frequently are not, and burn out |
| Power bank | Carry-on only, always. Up to 100 Wh unrestricted; 100-160 Wh needs airline approval; over 160 Wh cannot fly (`travel-day.md`) |
| Cables | One per device plus one spare of the common type; a shared multi-port charger saves more space than compact plugs do |
| eSIM | Buy and install at home while there is Wi-Fi; activation on arrival with no connection is the classic trap (`long-stays.md`) |
| Offline data | Maps, boarding passes, translations, the first night's address, downloaded before leaving |

## Luggage Choice

Carry-on only wins on connections, disruption and time at both ends; checking wins for a fixed base, gear-heavy trips, or a party where one bag serves several people (SKILL.md, Where Experts Disagree). The tie-break is how many times the bag will physically be carried — a trip with four internal moves is a different bag decision from a fortnight in one apartment.

Practical constraints regardless of choice: weigh at home; cabin-bag weight limits are enforced unpredictably by carrier and airport; the bag that fits one airline's sizer will not fit another's; and a soft bag survives a sizer that a hard case does not. Anything irreplaceable, medical, or needed on the day of arrival goes in hand luggage even when a bag is checked.

## Toiletries And The Liquids Constraint

Assume 100 ml containers in a one-litre transparent bag unless the specific airport publishes otherwise, both ways, including the connecting airport (`travel-day.md`). Solid alternatives — bar shampoo, solid toothpaste, a stick deodorant — remove the constraint entirely for a carry-on trip. Anything available cheaply at the destination is bought there rather than carried, with the exception of anything the traveller is particular about or medically dependent on.

## The Pre-Departure Check

Run the night before, from the template, not from memory:

- Core pouch in the bag, medical kit restocked, prescriptions with margin
- Documents match every booking (`bookings.md`)
- Cards in two places; a small amount of destination cash if arrival needs it
- Everything charged; offline downloads done
- Bag weighed; nothing forbidden in the checked bag (`travel-day.md`)
- Home left correctly: mail, keys, heating, the person who has the itinerary (`safety.md`)
- Photograph of the packed bag contents, for the claim nobody expects to file (`disruption.md`)

**After every trip**, edit the archetype template in the same turn: delete what was not used, add what was missed, note anything that broke or was confiscated, and write the restock into the core pouch list. The template is `artifacts/packing-<archetype>.md` with its `## Boxes` line; the unused-gear note also goes into the trip debrief (`debrief.md`). Formats and destinations: `memory-template.md`.
