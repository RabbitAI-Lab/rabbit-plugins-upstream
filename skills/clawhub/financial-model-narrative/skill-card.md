## Description: <br>
Turn financial model outputs into a clear written narrative for board, investor, management, or banking audiences, with key insights, drivers, variance explanations, and forward-looking commentary. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Finance, strategy, and operations users use this skill to turn pasted financial model outputs, P&L results, variance data, and forecast context into executive-ready narrative commentary. It is intended for board packs, investor updates, management reporting, banking materials, and plain-English explanations of financial results. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may receive sensitive financial figures or forecast context from the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use only financial data approved for the agent environment and avoid sharing confidential figures unless that disclosure is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Financial narratives can be misleading if model inputs are incomplete, unaudited, or missing business context. <br>
Mitigation: Review the narrative before external use and verify variance explanations, one-off items, risks, opportunities, and forward-looking statements against the underlying model. <br>
