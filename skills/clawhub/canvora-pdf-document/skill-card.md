## Description:

Create multi-page PDF documents with Canvora — brochures, reports, one-pagers, company profiles, proposals, menus, catalogs, ebooks, worksheets and cheat sheets. Use when the user wants a PDF, a printable document, a brochure, a lead magnet, or anything paginated rather than a social post.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted content and source URLs are processed by Canvora, which may expose confidential material or private access links.

Mitigation: Use only authorized material and avoid confidential content or private access links; check the page count and credit total before generation (15 credits per page for multi-page documents).
