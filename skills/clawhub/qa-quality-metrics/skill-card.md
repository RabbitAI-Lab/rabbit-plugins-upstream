## Description:

This skill helps QA teams define quality metrics and dashboards across process quality, product outcomes, testing efficiency, and quality-health trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, test leads, and engineering managers use this skill to turn test execution data, defect data, and historical baselines into a practical quality measurement system. It supports management reporting, quality dashboards, trend analysis, and improvement planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The trigger wording may activate the skill for broad quality or trend-analysis requests.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user's request is specifically about QA quality metrics, quality dashboards, or test and defect trend analysis before applying the skill.

Risk: Quality reports may use sensitive test, defect, incident, or operational data supplied by the workspace.

Mitigation: Use only data appropriate for the workspace context and avoid exposing restricted project details in generated summaries.
