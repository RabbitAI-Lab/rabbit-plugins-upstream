## Description:

Helps QA teams structure quality metrics dashboards and reports across process quality, product quality, testing efficiency, and quality health trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, test leads, and engineering managers use this skill to turn test execution, defect, and baseline data into quality metric reports for decision making and trend tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generic analytics or trend-analysis request could trigger a QA metrics report without enough task-specific context.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent and provide only relevant test, defect, and baseline data before using the skill.

Risk: Incomplete or poorly scoped QA data can produce misleading quality metrics or overstate coverage.

Mitigation: Label metric scope, baselines, missing data, and uncovered modules explicitly in the generated report.
