## Description:

Use when Chinese prose sounds AI-generated, template-like, mechanically structured, overly polished, or inconsistent with the author's voice, including requests to 去AI味, 降低AI感, or 改得更像人写.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-language writers use this skill to refine prose that feels generic or mechanically structured while preserving its facts, stance, register, constraints, and authorial voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edits may alter intended meaning, and pattern-based audit results do not establish authorship or guarantee an AI-detector outcome.

Mitigation: Review proposed edits against the source; choose audit targets deliberately, enable recursive scanning only when needed, and treat pattern results as prompts for review rather than proof.
