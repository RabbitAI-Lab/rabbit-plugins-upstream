## Use Case:

Learners and practitioners use this guide to prepare recordings, review speech recognition drafts against audio, and handle recordings and transcripts responsibly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Speech recognition can produce plausible errors, especially for names, numbers, technical terms, quiet speech, or overlapping speakers.

Mitigation: Review the draft while listening to the recording, verify high-impact details, and mark unresolved words or passages as uncertain instead of guessing.

Risk: Uploading recordings may expose personal or confidential information, and a service's deletion claim may not reflect its current retention practices.

Mitigation: Check consent, organizational policy, and the service's current privacy and retention terms before uploading; use an approved local workflow for confidential material.
