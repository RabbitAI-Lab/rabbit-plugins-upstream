## Description: <br>
Audits spending to find recurring subscriptions, lifestyle creep, and other cuttable costs, then ranks savings opportunities by impact with monthly and annualized dollar amounts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users or employees reviewing expenses use this skill to categorize spending, identify recurring charges, and prioritize realistic cuts while preserving user-specified off-limits items. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Using the skill may involve sharing personal financial transactions or statements with an agent. <br>
Mitigation: Redact account numbers, names, addresses, and other identifiers before providing spending data whenever possible. <br>
