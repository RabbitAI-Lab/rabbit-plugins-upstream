## Description:

Diagnoses recurring physics mistakes across diagrams, concepts, formulas, reasoning, and math tools, then produces targeted weakness reports for Chinese middle-school and first-year high-school learners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to identify the root causes of repeated physics errors, track consented learning weaknesses, and plan focused practice rather than label a student as bad at physics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum and crisis-support channels before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term student profiles, reminders, or sharing with parents and other skills could expose sensitive learning or emotional information.

Mitigation: Require student and applicable guardian consent before enabling retention, sharing, or reminders; provide access, correction, export, and deletion controls.

Risk: Incomplete history or mistaken diagnosis could lead to misleading weakness labels or progress claims.

Mitigation: Distinguish current-session observations from verified history and avoid historical counts or durable labels without adequate evidence and confirmation.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis-support channels may not fit users outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum and emergency referrals before use elsewhere; escalate safety concerns to appropriate local human support.
