## Description:

Analyzes physics mistakes for Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school learners, identifies underlying error patterns, and provides targeted weakness reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school learners use this skill to understand recurring physics mistakes across diagrams, concepts, formulas, problem-solving steps, and mathematical tools. It provides weakness summaries and tailored practice suggestions in coordination with a general mistake notebook.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-speaking K–12 education)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles may contain sensitive information and be shared across skills or with parents.

Mitigation: Confirm the host enforces consent and controls for recording, pausing, deleting, exporting, parent sharing, cross-skill sharing, and reminders before deployment.

Risk: A distressed learner may need support beyond physics tutoring, and referral details may be region-specific.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring on crisis signals, refer to a trusted adult and appropriate local help, and localize referral channels outside mainland China.
