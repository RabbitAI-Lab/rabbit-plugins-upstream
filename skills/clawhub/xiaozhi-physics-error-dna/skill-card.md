## Description:

帮助初高中学生分析物理错题的五类根因，追踪反复出现的弱项并生成学习报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to identify recurring causes of mistakes in middle- and high-school physics, maintain a consent-based error profile, and review weak-area reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent error profiles and sharing may expose minors' learning data.

Mitigation: Confirm consent and profile-memory and cross-skill-sharing settings before storing or sharing records; honor pause and deletion requests.

Risk: Crisis guidance and curriculum assumptions may not fit deployments outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency resources, privacy practices, and curriculum references before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Weak-area conclusions may overstate patterns when records are unavailable or sparse.

Mitigation: Use only observed records, disclose insufficient samples, and avoid unsupported historical counts.
