## Description:

Analyzes middle- and high-school physics mistakes across five error dimensions to identify recurring weaknesses and guide targeted practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to diagnose recurring physics errors, build consent-controlled learning profiles, and plan focused practice and progress reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (default; localize crisis support information before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or progress summaries may be retained or shared beyond the learner's wishes.

Mitigation: Check consent before saving, sharing, or scheduling reminders; offer viewing, correction, deletion, export, memory pause, and sharing controls.

Risk: Historical weakness claims may mislead when prior records or cross-session access are unavailable.

Mitigation: Use only available records and clearly label session-only findings as insufficient for historical conclusions.

Risk: A student expressing a safety crisis may need support beyond study coaching.

Mitigation: Stop routine tutoring, direct the student to a trusted adult and location-appropriate emergency support, and localize crisis resources outside mainland China.
