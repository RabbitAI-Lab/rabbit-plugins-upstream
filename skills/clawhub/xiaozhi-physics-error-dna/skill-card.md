## Description:

帮助初中至高一学生定位物理错题的图景、概念、公式、过程与数学工具错误，并生成弱项分析和学习档案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students and educators use this skill to diagnose recurring physics mistakes, plan targeted practice, and review consent-based learning profiles and monthly weakness reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localization needed elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning history or summaries could be retained or shared contrary to their preferences.

Mitigation: Confirm profile memory, cross-skill sharing, parent visibility and reminders against the student's consent choices before enabling them.

Risk: Curriculum and safety guidance are tailored to Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language and support guidance before use elsewhere.

Risk: Without cross-session records, historical error counts may be misleading.

Mitigation: Report only observed session-level patterns and label insufficient samples rather than inventing past counts.
