## Description:

Guides Chinese-language Zhihu article and answer creation from topic research and fact-checking through paste-ready drafts, illustrations, delivery checks, and post-publication records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Zhihu content creators use this skill to research and verify claims, prepare Chinese-language articles or answers with supporting assets, and check deliverables before manually publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Citation verification fetches operator-provided URLs, which may reveal requests to those sites.

Mitigation: Review citation URLs before running verification and avoid submitting sensitive URLs.

Risk: The server license label and bundled license differ, and the bundled package metadata lists another version.

Mitigation: Confirm the release version and applicable license before reuse or redistribution.

Risk: Strict Chinese-language and branding conventions may not fit multilingual or attribution-sensitive work.

Mitigation: Review generated copy, attribution, and publication instructions before use.
