## Description:

Helps product and delivery teams clarify requirements, draft or review PRDs, explore interactive prototypes, and assess changes and acceptance criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[franklinxkk](https://clawhub.ai/user/franklinxkk)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache-2.0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, engineers, QA reviewers, and coding agents use this skill to clarify business decisions and produce reviewable requirements, PRDs, prototypes, change assessments, and acceptance criteria without imposing a full workflow on small edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private requirement materials or examples may contain credentials or sensitive business data.

Mitigation: Keep real credentials out of requirement materials and public examples; inspect inputs before sharing them.

Risk: Local validation scripts or their results could be applied beyond the intended files or mistaken for full approval.

Mitigation: Run scripts only on intended project files, review generated outputs, and limit conclusions to the checks performed.
