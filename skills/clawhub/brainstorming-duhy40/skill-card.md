## Description: <br>
Explore intent, requirements, and design before implementation for creative work such as features, components, functionality, or behavior changes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and project teams use this skill to turn early implementation ideas into clarified requirements, approach tradeoffs, approved designs, and written specs before coding begins. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may inspect the current project, ask planning questions, create a design specification, and commit that specification before implementation planning. <br>
Mitigation: Review the proposed design and generated specification before approval, and inspect any git commit before continuing to implementation. <br>
