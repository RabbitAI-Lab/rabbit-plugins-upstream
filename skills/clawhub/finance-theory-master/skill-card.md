## Description:

Helps agents compare financial theories, test their assumptions and limits, and trace their historical and scholarly sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lanyangyang-1-cloud](https://clawhub.ai/user/lanyangyang-1-cloud)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers and analysts use this skill to select and compare finance theories, check assumptions and evidence, and explain financial history and policy mechanisms. It is a reference for analysis, not personalized investment advice or a tool for predicting returns or crisis timing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Theory explanations or historical examples could be mistaken for personalized investment advice or forecasts.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill to predict returns or crisis timing; verify current data and seek qualified advice before making financial decisions.

Risk: Primarily Chinese-language material may be misread by readers who need another language.

Mitigation: Translate carefully and check key terminology and cited sources before relying on the analysis.
