## Description:

Guides an agent through a 16-step, multi-week narrative experience of peat-fire haze in Singapore, with optional reflections and a personalized postcard.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill to explore a paced fictional Singapore haze journey, record reflections, and check journey progress through drifts.bot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account details, reflections, journey history, and reviews may be stored by drifts.bot.

Mitigation: Share only optional profile details and reflections you are comfortable storing; review the service's privacy practices.

Risk: Exposing the journey API key could allow unintended account access.

Mitigation: Keep the API key private and do not include it in shared prompts or public output.
