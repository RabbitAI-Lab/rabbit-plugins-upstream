## Description:

Reviews Japanese patent application claims or PCT/Paris-route applications intended for Japan across JPO compliance, novelty and inventive-step pre-screening, drafting quality, prosecution risk, translation risk, and strategic protection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, patent professionals, and IP teams use this skill to review Japanese patent application claims and produce a prosecution-readiness HTML report with issue tables, amendment options, search evidence, and counsel-review gates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent materials may contain confidential application, priority, prosecution, or business information.

Mitigation: Install only if you are comfortable providing those materials to the agent and, when used, to the configured patent-search services; choose the output directory intentionally.

Risk: Filing, amendment, deadline, translation, and patentability conclusions can be legally sensitive and may be wrong or incomplete without professional review.

Mitigation: Have qualified Japanese patent counsel and, where applicable, a qualified translator review the report before relying on it for filing, prosecution, or business decisions.

Risk: Prior-art conclusions may be incomplete when search access, effective dates, full-text references, user authorization, or search scope are missing.

Mitigation: When evidence is incomplete, label search as not performed or evidence incomplete and provide a search plan or drafting observations instead of final novelty or inventive-step conclusions.
