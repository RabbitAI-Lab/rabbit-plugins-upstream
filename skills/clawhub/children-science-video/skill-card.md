## Description:

Creates Chinese-language children's science short-video scripts, five-scene storyboards, image and video prompts, cover prompts, platform copy, and illustrations from a topic or supplied text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[weslon1987](https://clawhub.ai/user/weslon1987)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and educators use this skill to prepare Chinese-language science and safety short-video assets for children ages 3–10, including storyboards, publication copy, and illustrations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggering on pasted science or safety text can start a full asset-generation workflow without an explicit video request.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent before generating files or starting the full workflow.

Risk: Illustration generation can consume image-generation credits.

Mitigation: Disclose expected credit use and obtain confirmation before generating images.
