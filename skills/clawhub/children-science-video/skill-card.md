## Description:

Creates a Chinese-language children's science short-video package from a topic or draft, including scripts, storyboards, cover prompts, platform copy, images, and short video clips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[weslon1987](https://clawhub.ai/user/weslon1987)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and educators use this skill to plan and produce Chinese-language science and safety short videos for children ages 3–10, with five storyboard scenes and accompanying publishing assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broadly related pasted text can trigger the full file-producing workflow without a specific video request.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent and topic before starting production.

Risk: Image and video generation may consume credits and create assets that need review.

Mitigation: Get explicit approval before generation and inspect the output folder before using or publishing assets.
