## Description:

Masks phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers, long numbers, and specified names in text before it is shared with an AI, and reports the masking counts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT (code only); documentation and theoretical text all rights reserved

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can mask selected personal information in local text before sharing it with an AI and inspect a count of the matches found.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pattern-based masking may leave personal information in the output.

Mitigation: Review the masked text before sending it to any AI; do not treat masking as complete legal or compliance protection.

Risk: The bundled materials reserve rights in documentation and theoretical text separately from the code license.

Mitigation: Check the split license terms and the ClawHub license discrepancy before redistributing material.
