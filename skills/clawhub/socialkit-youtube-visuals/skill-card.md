## Description:

Turns an approved YouTube script into a shot list (shot-list.json) that maps each script section to a visual asset - user footage, screen recordings, slides, images or licensed stock - with durations, plus an asset checklist. Use when planning B-roll, visuals, scenes or slides for a YouTube video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

YouTube creators and editors use this skill to turn an approved script into a timed visual plan and an asset checklist. It helps them plan footage, screen recordings, slides, images, and licensed stock while tracking asset rights.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposed visual asset may be used without the necessary rights.

Mitigation: Use assets the user owns or is licensed to use, and confirm permissions before adding them to the project.

Risk: Realistic AI-generated or altered media may require a synthetic media disclosure when uploaded.

Mitigation: Identify such assets during planning and ensure the upload step includes the required disclosure.
