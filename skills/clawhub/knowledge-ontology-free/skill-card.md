## Use Case: <br>
Developers and AI-agent users use this skill to create, validate, query, and traverse structured local memory for projects, tasks, people, documents, and related entities. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Local ontology files may persist project or personal notes across sessions. <br>
Mitigation: Review what data is stored under memory/ontology and avoid storing sensitive information unless the workspace policy permits it. <br>
