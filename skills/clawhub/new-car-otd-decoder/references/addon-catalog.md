# Dealer Add-On Catalog — Fair Values vs Charged Prices

Every "dealer accessory / service / protection package" sold in the F&I office, with honest value
for a typical street car. Prices are US-market typical charge ranges.

| Add-on | Typical charge | Fair value | Verdict basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIN etching | $195–$1,995 | **$0** | Kits cost ~$20; many insurers discount without it; can be required by some lease lenders only in rare cases |
| Surface/paint protection ("sealant") | $295–$995 | **$0–$25** | A bottle of sealant; factory paint already has clear coat + warranty |
| Fabric protection | $195–$495 | **$0** | Scotchguard can, 10 minutes |
| Nitrogen tire fill | $29–$210 | **$0** | Air is 78% nitrogen; no measurable benefit for street tires |
| Ceramic coating | $495–$2,495 | $50–$150 (consumer kit) | Real products exist but dealer "packages" are usually sealant rebranded |
| Paint protection film (PPF) | $995–$3,995 | Real product — price-shop independent shops | Legit if full-front quality film; dealer subcontract often +50% vs independent |
| All-weather floor mats | $295–$695 | $130–$260 (OEM list / WeatherTech) | Real product; counter at manufacturer list |
| Wheel locks | $95–$295 | $40–$80 | Real but minor |
| Mud flaps / splash guards | $150–$400 | $60–$150 | Real product |
| "Pro Pack" / "Appearance Pkg" bundles | $995–$4,995 | Sum of parts above | Unbundle and audit line-by-line |
| Market adjustment / "ADM" | $1,000–$50,000 | $0 on normal-supply cars | Legal but pure margin; refuse or shop another dealer |
| Anti-theft system (dealer-add) | $395–$895 | $0–$200 | Car already has immobilizer; alarms rarely affect insurance |
| Key-fob replacement plan | $295–$795 | Price one fob: $250–$500 | Only rational if you lose fobs for a living |
| Tire & wheel protection | $495–$1,495 | ~$300–$600 only if local roads are catastrophic | Road-hazard plans: read exclusions (curbs usually excluded) |
| Gap insurance (dealer) | $495–$1,295 | **$20–$30/yr via your auto insurer** | Same product; dealer markup 10–40×. Buy from your carrier |
| Extended warranty / VSC (dealer) | $1,500–$4,500 | Often $500–$1,500 under same coverage | Markup is where F&I makes the living; compare same-brand plans online |
| Maintenance plans (prepaid oil) | $695–$1,995 | Sum of services at list prices | Compute: years × services × price; usually a wash or loss |
| Acquisition fee (lease) | $395–$1,195 | $395–$995 per lender schedule | Lender-fixed; marked-up beyond lender cap = refuse |
| Disposition fee (lease end) | $295–$495 | Lender-fixed | Legit but know it BEFORE signing |
| Dealer prep / "dealer services fee" | $99–$999 | $0 | Prep is in the MSRP/franchise agreement |

## The refuse list (verbatim, printable)

> "Please remove the following items from the worksheet — I am not paying for them under any
> configuration: [items]. If they cannot be removed, deduct their full amount from the selling
> price, as I did not order them. My offer stands on the corrected out-the-door number."

Add-ons "already installed" are still refusable: the dealer chose to install them before you
arrived. Your counter is the corrected OTD; walking is always available — supply of ordinary
vehicles is normal, and another dealer is one email away.
