## Description:

Creates faceless videos, podcasts, thumbnails and characters, researches YouTube channels, and posts finished videos through the user's Digital Maker AI account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[successwithsam100](https://clawhub.ai/user/successwithsam100)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to make videos and visual assets, research faceless YouTube channels and trends, and publish approved content to social platforms through their own account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Starting a video spends minutes from the user's paid plan.

Mitigation: Start a render only on the user's request; agree on its scope before submitting it.

Risk: Posting can publish finished content to YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.

Mitigation: Confirm the video, title, caption, destination platforms, and privacy settings before posting.

Risk: The CLI uses the user's Digital Maker AI API key.

Mitigation: Use only a key the user is comfortable providing to the CLI.
