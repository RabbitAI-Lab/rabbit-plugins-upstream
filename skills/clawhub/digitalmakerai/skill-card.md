Creators and social media teams use this skill with their own Digital Maker AI account to create visual content, research faceless YouTube channels and trends, and publish approved videos to social platforms.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video creation consumes paid plan minutes.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested topic, length, format, and voice before starting a single creation job.

Risk: Publishing may make content public on social platforms.

Mitigation: Get approval for the exact video, title, caption, privacy setting, and platforms before posting.

Risk: The skill requires account credentials and a third-party CLI.

Mitigation: Install only after trusting the publisher and CLI; use the user's own account key.
