## Description:

Generates e-commerce advertising images and copy, and turns sales videos into image-and-text promotional content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

E-commerce marketers and ad creators use the skill to prepare promotional images and copy, or turn sales videos into image-and-text posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic setup and upgrade guidance may change the environment without sufficient control.

Mitigation: Review setup instructions, use an isolated installation of a reviewed version, preserve existing account configuration, and approve global changes or upgrades.

Risk: Selected media is uploaded to the service, and generation may spend credits.

Mitigation: Confirm the media is suitable to upload and approve generation costs before use.
