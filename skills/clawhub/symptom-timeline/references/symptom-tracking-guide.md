# Symptom Tracking Guide

What to track for common chronic conditions. Use this as a reference when logging
symptoms to ensure you're capturing the data that matters.

---

## Migraines / Headaches

**Key symptoms to track:**
- Pain location (one-sided, behind eyes, temples, back of head)
- Pain type (throbbing, pressure, sharp, dull)
- Severity (1-10)
- Duration (minutes to days)
- Aura (visual disturbances, sensitivity to light/sound)
- Nausea or vomiting

**Common triggers to log:**
- **Food**: chocolate, aged cheese, MSG, nitrates, red wine, caffeine (or withdrawal)
- **Sleep**: too little, too much, disrupted
- **Stress**: high-pressure events, let-down after stress
- **Weather**: barometric pressure changes, bright sun, heat
- **Hormonal**: menstrual cycle (if applicable)
- **Activity**: intense exertion, screen time

## Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)

**Key symptoms to track:**
- Abdominal pain/cramping (location, severity)
- Bloating
- Bowel movement type (diarrhea, constipation, mixed) — compare to Bristol scale
- Urgency
- Gas

**Common triggers to log:**
- **Food**: FODMAPs (onions, garlic, wheat, dairy, beans), spicy food, fatty food, caffeine, alcohol
- **Stress**: anxiety, social pressure, work
- **Sleep**: poor sleep worsens gut motility
- **Medication**: antibiotics, new supplements
- **Activity**: eating too fast, irregular meal times

## Arthritis / Joint Pain

**Key symptoms to track:**
- Joint(s) affected (which fingers, knees, hips, etc.)
- Pain severity (1-10)
- Stiffness (morning stiffness duration is a key metric)
- Swelling or warmth
- Range of motion limitation
- Fatigue

**Common triggers to log:**
- **Weather**: humidity, cold, rain, barometric pressure drops
- **Activity**: overexertion, repetitive motion, new exercise
- **Food**: nightshades (tomatoes, peppers, potatoes), sugar, alcohol (varies by person)
- **Sleep**: poor sleep increases pain sensitivity
- **Medication**: missed doses, NSAIDs, biologic timing

## Autoimmune Conditions (Lupus, RA, Psoriasis, etc.)

**Key symptoms to track:**
- Joint pain and swelling
- Fatigue severity (1-10) — track separately from pain
- Skin changes (rashes, lesions, photosensitivity)
- Fever (low-grade)
- Flare frequency and duration
- Medication side effects

**Common triggers to log:**
- **Stress**: emotional or physical stress is a top flare trigger
- **Sleep**: exhaustion is both a trigger and a symptom
- **Weather**: sun exposure (especially lupus), UV index, temperature extremes
- **Medication**: dose changes, missed medications, new drugs
- **Food**: alcohol, processed foods (inflammatory)
- **Activity**: overexertion triggers post-exertional malaise

## General Tips

1. **Log immediately** — memory degrades quickly. Log symptoms as they happen.
2. **Be consistent** — log even mild symptoms (severity 1-3). Baselines matter.
3. **Track the absence** — note when you feel good too. Patterns need contrast.
4. **Be specific with triggers** — "ate pizza" is better than "bad food".
5. **Use notes** for anything unusual that doesn't fit a category.
6. **Review weekly** — run `correlate` regularly to catch patterns early.
7. **Share the export** — bring the generated report to every doctor visit.
