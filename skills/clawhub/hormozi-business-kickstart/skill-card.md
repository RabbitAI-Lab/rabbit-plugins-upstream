## Description:

Generates business kickstart manuals based on Alex Hormozi's $100M trilogy, covering offers, lead generation, money models, guarantees, execution roadmap, and budget planning in Markdown, PDF, or DOCX.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roboticresults](https://clawhub.ai/user/roboticresults)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business owners, founders, and operators use this skill to answer a structured questionnaire and generate a strategy manual for offers, lead generation, money model design, and near-term execution planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide unnecessary personal, protected demographic, regulated, or highly confidential financial data while answering business-strategy questions.

Mitigation: Ask only for information needed to generate the manual and avoid collecting sensitive personal, protected demographic, regulated, or highly confidential financial details unless the user deliberately provides them.

Risk: Generated Markdown, PDF, or DOCX files may be written to unintended locations.

Mitigation: Confirm intended output paths before creating files and keep generated documents in user-approved workspace locations.
