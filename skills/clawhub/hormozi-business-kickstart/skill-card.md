## Description:

Generates business kickstart manuals using Alex Hormozi's Offers, Leads, and Money Models frameworks to help users shape offers, lead generation plans, and customer value stacks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roboticresults](https://clawhub.ai/user/roboticresults)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, founders, and operators use this skill to turn business inputs into a structured offer, lead-generation, money-model, and implementation plan. The skill first gathers details through a questionnaire, then produces a concise Markdown manual for action planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad business-growth requests and steer the session into a specific Hormozi-style strategy framework.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants that framework before proceeding, then use the questionnaire to ground recommendations in the user's actual business context.

Risk: The generated manual can influence business strategy, pricing, and advertising decisions.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against real customer data, financial constraints, and applicable professional advice before acting on them.
