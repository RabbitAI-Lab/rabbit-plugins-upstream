# Business Information Questionnaire ($100M Trilogy Framework)

Use this questionnaire to collect all necessary information from the user before generating the kickstart manual. The questions are designed to extract the inputs Hormozi's complete $100M trilogy needs: **Offer (Offers) + Leads (Leads) + Money Model (Money Models)**.

**Ask in small batches (2-3 questions at a time) to keep the conversation flowing.** Push back on vague answers. The user came for an expert, not validation.

## Clarifying Questions Template

If information is unclear, use these push-back questions:

### For Vague Target Market:
- "You said 'small business owners'. Can you be more specific? What industry? What role? What revenue bracket?"
- "Where would I find 100 of these people in one room?"
- "If you could only talk to ONE person for 1 hour about your offer, who would that be? Describe them."

### For Vague Dream Outcome:
- "What does success look like in concrete numbers?"
- "If a customer got ONE single result from working with you, what would it be?"
- "What would achieving this allow them to do that they can't do today?"

### For Vague Problem List:
- "You said customers have 'some concerns'. Can you list at least 5 specific concerns?"
- "What objections do you hear most often when someone says no?"
- "What do customers worry about AFTER they buy?"

### For Pricing Uncertainty:
- "What's the most you could ever charge for this, even if you had to over-deliver?"
- "What would a customer need to believe for $X to feel like a steal?"
- "What is the total retail value of every component you provide?"

### For Vague Delivery:
- "What do YOU personally do to deliver the result?"
- "What can be templated, automated, or handed off to a team member?"
- "If you had 100 customers tomorrow, what would break first?"

### For Vague Lead Generation:
- "You said you 'get some leads'. From where exactly?"
- "What does your current funnel look like? Ad → Landing Page → Lead Magnet → Email → Sale?"
- "If you had to double your leads in 30 days, what would you try first?"
- "How many warm contacts could you reach out to this week if your life depended on it?"

### For Vague Content:
- "What topics have you posted about in the last 30 days?"
- "Which post got the most engagement? Why do you think it worked?"
- "If you had to make 5 pieces of content this week, what topics would you pick?"

### For Paid Ads Confusion:
- "What's your customer acquisition cost (CAC)?"
- "What's your customer lifetime value (LTV)?"
- "If LTV is 3x CAC, paid ads work. If less, fix the offer first."
