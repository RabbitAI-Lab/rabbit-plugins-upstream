## Description:

Converts local video files or video links into cleaned text and uses custom prompts to produce summaries, rewrites, quotes, shot breakdowns, translations, and other structured copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External developers, creators, and teams use this skill to transcribe video and turn its content into summaries, meeting notes, translations, and other prompt-directed copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: This paid cloud service receives selected media and prompts; its actual network behavior exceeds the documented single-domain HTTPS boundary, and downloaded copies may remain locally until cleanup.

Mitigation: Before uploading confidential recordings, ask the provider to clarify upload destinations and retention; remove downloaded copies or run cleanup after processing.
