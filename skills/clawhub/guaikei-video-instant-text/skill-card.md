## Description:

Turns a video link or local video into cleaned text, with optional prompt-guided summaries, rewrites, highlights, and translations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, editors, and other users turn public video links or local recordings into readable transcripts and optional summaries or adapted copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected videos or links, prompts, filenames, task IDs, and a token are sent to GuaiKei's cloud service.

Mitigation: Use only approved media and credentials; avoid confidential, regulated, internal-only, or access-controlled material unless the provider and its retention claims have organizational approval.
