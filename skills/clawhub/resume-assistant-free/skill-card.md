## Description: <br>
简历助手免费版 provides basic resume scoring, issue identification, polishing, and Markdown/HTML export for job seekers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Job seekers use this skill to review draft resumes, receive a basic 100-point score with top issues, improve wording and structure, and export the result as Markdown or HTML. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Resume content may contain personal or sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with resume content appropriate for the selected agent or LLM provider, and remove unnecessary sensitive details before processing. <br>
Risk: Callback URLs and exported Markdown or HTML files may expose resume data if sent to or stored in untrusted locations. <br>
Mitigation: Use only trusted callback URLs and store exported files in locations controlled by the user or organization. <br>
