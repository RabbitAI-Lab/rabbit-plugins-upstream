## Description:

Guides creators from a short-drama premise through staged script and storyboard development, with continuity checks and prompts for character, scene, and prop assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yysws5566](https://clawhub.ai/user/yysws5566)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Short-drama creators use this skill to develop a premise into a reviewed story outline, modular script, production-ready storyboard, and visual asset prompts. It keeps key story decisions with the user and checks storyboard continuity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation may incur service costs and send project prompts to an image service.

Mitigation: Check the image service's terms, privacy handling, and pricing; keep sensitive material out of prompts, or request prompt-only output.

Risk: The scanner flags guidance related to moderation blocks.

Mitigation: Follow the platform's review guidance and revise or remove affected content; do not attempt to evade review.

Risk: Script analysis coverage is partial.

Mitigation: Review the bundled scripts before use and account for the scanner's incomplete coverage when deciding whether to install.
