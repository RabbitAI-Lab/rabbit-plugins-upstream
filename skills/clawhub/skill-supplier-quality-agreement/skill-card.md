## Description: <br>
Helps SQE, legal, and commercial teams draft or review supplier quality agreements and customer quality terms, covering five core clause areas and generating clause templates plus gap-review documents in text and Markdown. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
SQE, legal, and commercial teams use this skill to draft supplier quality agreements, review customer quality requirements, identify missing or weak clauses, and generate review-ready text and Markdown documents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Agreement text may contain confidential supplier, customer, pricing, claim, or contract terms. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in workspaces approved for that data, avoid sharing confidential contract terms outside policy, and control access to generated md/txt reports. <br>
Risk: Generated clauses and gap-review recommendations may be unsuitable as final legal or commercial language. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting and review aids, keep specific thresholds marked for company or legal confirmation, and verify final language with counsel before signing or distribution. <br>
