## Description:

Reviews finished technology marketing drafts for citation traceability, customer redaction, product naming, competitor references, internal information, formatting, and AI traces, then reports findings and suggested fixes without changing the original.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and content reviewers use it to assess finished technology blogs, customer cases, product updates, and press releases before publication, then receive a structured report of issues and suggested fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
