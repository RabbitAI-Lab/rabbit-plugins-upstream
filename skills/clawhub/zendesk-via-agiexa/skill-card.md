## Description:

Explains Agiexa’s preview of a planned Zendesk and Shopify integration and helps users join its launch waitlist with explicit permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devkrutik](https://clawhub.ai/user/devkrutik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and support teams use this preview to understand the planned Zendesk and Shopify integration, review future ticket workflows, and request launch updates. It cannot access Zendesk or Shopify; waitlist details are sent to Agiexa only after explicit permission and confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake the described Zendesk and Shopify capabilities for currently available actions.

Mitigation: State that Agiexa is a preview and this skill cannot connect to or act on Zendesk or Shopify.

Risk: Joining the waitlist sends confirmed contact details and any optional link or message to Agiexa.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit permission, collect details directly from the user, show exactly what will be sent, and submit only after the user confirms the consent statement.
