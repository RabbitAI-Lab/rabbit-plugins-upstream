This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Merchants using Shopify and Zendesk use this skill to understand the planned Agiexa integration, explore customer support workflows, and request a waitlist signup when they explicitly consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Waitlist signup shares the user's confirmed email, role, and optional link or message with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Submit only after the user explicitly agrees and confirms the details to be sent.

Risk: After launch, connected business accounts may expose customer data and allow actions in those services.

Mitigation: Review per-agent tool permissions and approval settings before connecting accounts or enabling actions.

Risk: The package documents Agiexa security claims but does not independently establish them.

Mitigation: Review the current permissions and approval settings before connecting business accounts.
