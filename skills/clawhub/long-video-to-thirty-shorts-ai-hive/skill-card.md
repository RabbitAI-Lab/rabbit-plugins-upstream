## Description:

Helps course, interview, launch, livestream, and enterprise video teams turn long videos into up to 30 short-video candidates with topics, timestamps, hooks, scripts, supplemental shots, cover ideas, schedules, runnable AI-HIVE commands, and review checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketing teams, ecommerce operators, and developers use this skill to convert authorized long-form videos or transcripts into a structured short-video production workflow. It produces reviewable plans first, then runnable local ffmpeg commands and AI-HIVE generation calls when supplemental media is needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate AI-HIVE media calls that may be billable.

Mitigation: Review final prompts, model choices, routing mode, and pricing snapshots before submitting generation jobs; run a small sample before batch work.

Risk: Inputs may include copyrighted, private, trademarked, or otherwise unauthorized media.

Mitigation: Use only authorized source assets, preserve provenance records, and provide abstract structure guidance when rights are not confirmed.

Risk: API keys could be exposed through logs, screenshots, repositories, or shared output.

Mitigation: Use placeholders in examples, prefer environment or local config storage, and avoid pasting real keys into prompts or versioned files.

Risk: Generated short-video plans may overstate performance, product claims, prices, or platform outcomes.

Mitigation: Mark unverified facts for review and do not promise traffic, sales, ranking, approval, investment return, real-time prices, or model availability.
