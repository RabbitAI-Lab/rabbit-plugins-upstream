## Description:

Creates or completes editable PowerPoint presentations from a topic or unfinished deck, with slide copy, speaker notes, and cited sources retained in JishuDB.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bowen-aijishu](https://clawhub.ai/user/bowen-aijishu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and other presentation creators use this skill to finish or improve work updates, proposals, and knowledge-sharing decks with editable slides, speaker notes, and traceable sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup may download, install, and launch JishuDB Desktop without technical proof of user approval.

Mitigation: Before approving setup, review the exact release, install paths, client configuration target, endpoint URL, and launch mode; decline if a local-only presentation workflow is preferred.
