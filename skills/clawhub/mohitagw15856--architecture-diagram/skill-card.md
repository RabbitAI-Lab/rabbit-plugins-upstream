## Description: <br>
Creates render-ready Mermaid architecture diagrams for systems, data flows, services, dependencies, and logical layers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and technical teams use this skill to turn component, connection, grouping, and focus details into a Mermaid architecture diagram with a component legend and notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated architecture diagrams may be inaccurate or omit important system details. <br>
Mitigation: Review diagrams against source architecture knowledge before sharing or relying on them. <br>
Risk: Prompts and generated diagrams may contain sensitive system details. <br>
Mitigation: Remove or generalize sensitive component names, trust boundaries, and data-flow details before external sharing. <br>
