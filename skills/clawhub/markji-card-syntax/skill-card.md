## Description: <br>
使用中文生成、改写、检查和修正面向普通用户的 Markji 卡片语法（亦称“墨墨记忆卡”语法）内容。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[celend](https://clawhub.ai/user/celend) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to turn notes or learning material into paste-ready Markji card text, including cloze deletions, choices, text styling, formulas, media references, card links, and syntax fixes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated cards may contain incorrect links, user-provided media IDs, card IDs, or syntax that does not behave as intended in Markji. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated card text before pasting it into Markji, especially links and any media or card IDs; use simpler plain text when syntax cannot be verified. <br>
