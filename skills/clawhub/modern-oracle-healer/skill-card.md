## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive birth details or emotional disclosures while using the skill. <br>
Mitigation: Collect only details needed for the requested reading and avoid retaining or exposing personal information outside the conversation. <br>
Risk: Astrology-based emotional support may be mistaken for professional mental health, medical, or legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present responses as reflection or entertainment, keep professional-advice boundaries clear, and direct high-risk mental health, medical, or legal situations to qualified support. <br>
