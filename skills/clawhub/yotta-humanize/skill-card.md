## Description:

元真 yotta-humanize helps agents score, analyze, suggest edits for, and deterministically rewrite Chinese text to reduce AI-flavored wording while preserving facts, names, data, quotations, and author intent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yottameta](https://clawhub.ai/user/yottameta)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, writers, and external agent users use this skill to review Chinese drafts, identify AI-flavored phrasing, generate reports or suggestions, and apply deterministic text-level rewrites when the edits do not require judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rule-based rewrites could alter nuance, citations, names, facts, or author voice if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for Chinese drafts and review rewritten output before replacing the original text, especially where facts, citations, names, or author voice matter.
