## Description:

Turns a selected game concept into a build-ready design for the player experience, world rules, systems, level pacing, narrative where needed, and a complete playable slice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game designers and developers use this skill to turn a source bible, selected concept, and product brief into an actionable game design document for a playable slice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Non-Chinese readers may find the Chinese internal design notes difficult to audit.

Mitigation: Translate or review the notes with a Chinese reader before relying on the generated design; check the output against the brief and concept.
