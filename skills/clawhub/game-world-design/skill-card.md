## Description:

Turns an approved game concept into a build-ready design document covering player experience, systems, levels, world response, and a complete playable slice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers and development teams use this skill to turn an approved concept and product brief into a concrete game design, including its core loop, world rules, levels, narrative branches when relevant, and playable-slice specification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
