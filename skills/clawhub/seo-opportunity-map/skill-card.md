## Description:

Next SEO Moves - your next five SEO moves, with which page to fix or write, the exact edit and why. Use when a founder or small team asks what to do next for SEO, which pages to update or create, for quick SEO wins or keywords they almost rank for, or for a keyword gap against competitors. Needs only a website; a Search Console export makes it stronger. New-page moves hand off to seo-content-brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
