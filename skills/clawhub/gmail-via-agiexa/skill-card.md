## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Gmail and Shopify agent capabilities are a preview and are not available until the stated November 2026 launch.

Mitigation: Explain the launch timing and present the integration as planned functionality; offer the waitlist for interested users.

Risk: Waitlist signup shares the user's email and role, plus any optional link or message, with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Collect and submit these details only after explicit permission, and let the user review what will be sent.

Risk: The provider's future security claims have not been independently verified.

Mitigation: Describe those claims as unverified and avoid presenting them as established assurances.
