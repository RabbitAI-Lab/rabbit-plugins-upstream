## Description: <br>
Assists users in drafting or revising quality management system policy documents aligned with ISO9001 and related standards, using clause checks, industry references, strategic alignment review, KPI paths, and release reminders. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, quality managers, consultants, and auditors use this skill to draft or revise quality policies, quality manual policy sections, management review materials, and customer-audit preparation content. It guides collection of strategic anchors and produces policy drafts with clause coverage, rationale, KPI paths, and publication reminders. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide company strategy, customer concerns, process risks, or existing policy documents while using the skill. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide business information suitable for the agent environment, and redact sensitive details before sharing. <br>
Risk: Generated policy text may be incomplete, too generic, or unsuitable for formal release without organizational review. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the draft with responsible quality and management stakeholders before use; final publication remains a management decision. <br>
