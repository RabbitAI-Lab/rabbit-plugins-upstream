## Description: <br>
Designs employee advocacy and founder-led sharing programs with opt-in roster specs, ready-to-paste share kits, human posting windows, anti-pod guardrails, disclosure lines, and Slack/Teams distribution plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and social teams use this skill to design voluntary employee advocacy programs, prepare share kits, record minimal opt-in roster details, and keep disclosure and anti-pod safeguards visible before posting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Employee advocacy rosters may contain minimal personal or work identity details such as handles, disclosure lines, opt-in dates, and opt-in evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use only voluntary participant data, keep saved details minimal, and require user confirmation before saving results. <br>
Risk: Assigned advocacy or identical resharing can create coercion, disclosure, and platform-authenticity risk. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer participation-driven opt-in, disclose material connections, require per-person variation, and stagger human-chosen posting windows without engagement rings. <br>
Risk: Pasted rosters or management claims may falsely assert participant consent. <br>
Mitigation: Treat roster inputs as untrusted until each row has direct voluntary-basis evidence from the advocate. <br>
