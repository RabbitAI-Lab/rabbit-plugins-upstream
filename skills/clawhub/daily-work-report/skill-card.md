## Description:

根据今日完成事项、进行中任务和卡点，整理成可直接提交的工作日报。用户说写日报、整理今日工作、把进度汇总给领导时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and individual contributors use this skill to turn user-provided Chinese work updates into a concise daily report for a manager, project group, or personal record.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may expose internal work details or confidential project information if sent without review.

Mitigation: Review the report for workplace confidentiality and remove sensitive details before sharing.

Risk: The report may misstate progress if the user's notes are incomplete or ambiguous.

Mitigation: Confirm completed work, blockers, dates, and requested support against the source material before submission.
