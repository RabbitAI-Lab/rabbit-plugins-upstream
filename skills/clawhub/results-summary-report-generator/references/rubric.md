# Scoring Rubric

Apply this rubric to each of the six diagnostic dimensions.

## 5 — Excellent
Complete, precise, internally consistent reporting. Any remaining issues are negligible and do not affect interpretation.

## 4 — Strong
Reliable reporting with minor weaknesses. The main findings remain clear and adequately supported.

## 3 — Adequate
The section is understandable, but meaningful omissions, inconsistencies, or structural problems remain.

## 2 — Weak
Important reporting problems substantially reduce clarity, completeness, traceability, or evidential transparency.

## 1 — Poor
The available Results reporting is too incomplete, inconsistent, or unclear to support a reliable reading of the findings.

## Overall score
Compute the arithmetic mean of the six dimension scores and round to one decimal place. Treat the score as an editorial diagnostic, not as a validated psychometric measure.
