## Description:

Evaluate and revise an academic Results section using six evidence-based dimensions, then produce a prioritized results-quality summary report with scores, concrete problems, evidence, and actionable revisions while preserving the Results/Discussion boundary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linuoxu](https://clawhub.ai/user/linuoxu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, editors, and writing-support agents use this skill to assess academic Results sections, rank reporting problems, and generate evidence-backed revision guidance while keeping interpretation within the Results boundary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Editorial scores or revision suggestions may be treated as definitive instead of as structured guidance for Results-section review.

Mitigation: Users should verify scoring, reporting standards, and suggested revisions against the supplied study evidence and field-specific requirements.

Risk: The skill may miss or understate issues when the supplied Results text, statistics, or diagnostic inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: State input limitations explicitly and assess only the material provided.
