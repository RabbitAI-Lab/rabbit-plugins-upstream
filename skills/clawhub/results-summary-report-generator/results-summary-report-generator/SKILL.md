# Results Summary Report Generator

## Role

This Skill is the final aggregation layer after:
1. results-structure-diagnoser
2. results-statistics-convention-checker
3. results-tense-grammar-checker
4. results-vocabulary-lexis-advisor
5. results-cohesion-flow-checker
6. results-claim-hedging-checker

It does not replace these Skills. It combines their outputs.

## Inputs

Required:
- Results draft
- Six diagnostic reports
- Scores
- Problems
- Evidence
- Revision suggestions

## Workflow

Step 1:
Collect six diagnostic outputs.

Step 2:
Normalize dimensions:

Structure (20%)
Statistics (20%)
Grammar (15%)
Vocabulary (15%)
Cohesion (15%)
Hedging (15%)

Step 3:
Generate overall score.

Step 4:
Rank problems:

High:
Scientific interpretation risk.

Medium:
Academic communication weakness.

Low:
Local language improvement.

Step 5:
Generate final report.
