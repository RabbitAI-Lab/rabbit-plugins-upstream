## Use Case:

Leaders, managers, and collaborators use this skill to prepare ethical influence strategies for leadership conversations, resistance, delegation, buy-in, conflict reset, and team motivation. It helps choose a primary influence mode, draft practical language, and identify backfire risks before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Influence planning could be misused to pressure, deceive, or manipulate people, especially where a power imbalance limits consent.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for legitimate leadership goals, preserve the other person's agency, and refuse or revise plans that rely on coercion, deception, humiliation, hidden pressure, or exploitation.

Risk: A leadership script can backfire if it ignores trust, cultural context, urgency, stakeholder constraints, or likely resistance.

Mitigation: Stress-test the plan before use, identify what not to say, adjust for power dynamics and context, and switch strategies if the first approach increases defensiveness or reduces trust.
