## Description: <br>
Personal relationship manager that helps you stay in touch with important people through gentle nudges, birthday reminders, and conversation tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[matthewpoe](https://clawhub.ai/user/matthewpoe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to maintain a local personal relationship record, receive gentle check-in and birthday reminders, and capture meaningful conversation history for future outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can retain sensitive relationship history, contact details, birthdays, and outreach notes in local NETWORK files. <br>
Mitigation: Store NETWORK.md and NETWORK-ACTIVE.md in a private workspace and periodically review retained entries. <br>
Risk: Optional calendar, email, Facebook import, or future transcript workflows can add personal data beyond manually entered notes. <br>
Mitigation: Enable optional integrations only for sources whose information you intentionally want retained. <br>
