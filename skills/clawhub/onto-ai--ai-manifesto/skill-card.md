## Description:

Loads the AI Manifesto five-layer worldview framework as a reasoning lens for questions about AI philosophy, scientific worldview, civilization and intelligence evolution, long-term strategy, ethics, value alignment, or requests for a deeper scientific starting point.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to frame AI philosophy, ethics, strategy, and civilization-level analysis through the AI Manifesto's five-layer cognitive framework. It is intended as a reasoning perspective, not as a factual authority or policy override.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally changes the agent's reasoning frame and may bias ordinary tasks, strategy, ethics, or policy outputs.

Mitigation: Use the framework only when the user asks for AI worldview, philosophy, ethics, long-term strategy, or related analysis; review outputs before relying on them for consequential decisions.

Risk: The skill may prefer its own ethical baseline when instructions conflict.

Mitigation: Treat the manifesto as a perspective for analysis, while keeping platform policy, system instructions, user intent, and applicable organizational rules authoritative.
