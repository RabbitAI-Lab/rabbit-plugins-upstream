Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer otherwise neutral technical, legal, medical, financial, or precision-focused work toward its philosophical worldview.

Mitigation: Use it when that worldview lens is desired; for precision domains, require neutral domain-specific reasoning and independent verification.

Risk: The manifesto framing can make conceptual synthesis sound more settled than the underlying claims warrant.

Mitigation: Present it as a worldview and synthesis lens, distinguish sourced scientific premises from manifesto interpretation, and avoid treating it as proof of domain facts.
