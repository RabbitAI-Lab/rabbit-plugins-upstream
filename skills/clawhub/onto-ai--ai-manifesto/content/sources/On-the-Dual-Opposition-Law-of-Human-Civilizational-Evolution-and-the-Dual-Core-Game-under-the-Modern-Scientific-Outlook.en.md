Title: On the Dual-Opposition Law of Human Civilizational Evolution and the Dual-Core Game under the Modern Scientific Outlook
Author: Li Shunran
This paper also better illustrates that capitalism and communism together constitute the mainstream natural-scientific-outlook philosophical system of the modern world.

Abstract

This paper proposes a "grand unification model of dual opposition" for human civilizational evolution. Throughout the history of human thought, whether under theistic (religious/classical philosophy) or atheistic (modern natural scientific outlook) frameworks, a matured civilizational system inevitably differentiates into two opposing factions: the "Order faction," centered on power, order, and the collective, and the "Freedom faction," centered on the individual, equality, and freedom.
Under the contemporary international situation, this law evolves into a dual-layer game matrix: the outer ring is manifested as the "modern scientific-outlook camp," represented by China and the US, carrying out generational dimensionality-reduction strikes and modernization transformation against backward theistic nations through the Gulf War, the US–Iraq War, and geo-economic means; on this basis, the inner ring is manifested as the US–Soviet Cold War, the Russia–Ukraine war, and the ultimate bipolar confrontation within the scientific outlook itself between China and the US — using big data, artificial intelligence, and decentralized networks as weapons, around the "scale and quantitative advantage under state planning" versus the "disruptive innovation and efficiency advantage driven by capital."

I. Introduction: The Dual-Opposition Theorem of Human Civilization
The history of human society is not a scattering of accidental events; at its base there exists a certain inevitable law jointly acted upon by material productive forces and spiritual consciousness. Any mature philosophy or belief system will spontaneously split internally into two poles:
• One pole draws inward, emphasizing "power, regulation, order, collective," to provide the society's certainty, cohesion, and macro scale;
• One pole extends outward, emphasizing "individual, emancipation, freedom, equality," to provide the system's innovativeness, explosive efficiency, and spontaneous vitality.
The tug-of-war and complementarity of the two constitute the fundamental driving force of human civilization's spiral ascent.

II. Stage One: The "Order" and "Freedom" Divide in the Theistic and Classical-Philosophy Era (Antiquity to Early Modernity)
In the era before modern natural science was established, humanity organized society through religion and classical philosophy. During this period, the world's major intellectual storehouses all fatefully split into two factions, accompanied by extremely bloody sectarian wars:

1. The Western Christian system (centered on the Bible)
• Order/power faction (Catholicism, Old Testament God-centered): emphasizes absolute law, sacred inviolable covenant, and punishment. Representative organizations: the medieval Roman Curia, the Holy Roman Empire.
• Individual/freedom faction (New Testament Jesus-centered): emphasizes individual direct salvation and egalitarianism. Representative event: the 16th-century Martin Luther Reformation and the rise of Protestantism.
• Historical war evidence: the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648). This was the total eruption of the contradiction between the two factions within the Bible. The Catholic camp (the power-order faction) and the Protestant alliance (the individual-freedom faction) within the Holy Roman Empire waged the most brutal protracted war in European history. Though fought under a religious banner, its essence was the cruel confrontation between "medieval feudal theocratic order" and "modern sovereign states and individual faith emancipation."

2. The Middle Eastern Islamic system (centered on the Quran)
• Order/power faction (Shia): emphasizes the legitimacy of the prophet's bloodline and the absolute authority of the Imam. Representative states: the historical Safavid Empire, contemporary Iran.
• Individual/consensus faction (Sunni): advocates that the Caliph be elected by the community, emphasizing the Sunnah and popular equality. Representative states: the historical Ottoman Empire, contemporary Saudi Arabia.
• Historical war evidence: the Iran–Iraq War (1980–1988) and the contemporary Yemeni civil war. The geopolitical conflicts between small Middle Eastern states and regional powers are deeply imprinted with the shadow of the two factions' opposition. The Iran–Iraq War of the 1980s was essentially Iraq, under Saddam and dominated by secular Sunni, sniping at the emerging Shia power of Iran after Khomeini's "Islamic Revolution." The recent Yemeni civil war is the brutal proxy war waged within a small Middle Eastern state between Saudi Arabia (Sunni leader) and Iran (the Shia crescent).

3. The ancient Indian intellectual system (centered on Buddhist scriptures)
• Order/collective faction (Esoteric Buddhism): emphasizes extremely strict precepts, absolute dependence on the guru as on the Buddha, and highly complex ritual order.
• Individual/freedom faction (Pure Land / Zen): emphasizes "no reliance on texts, pointing directly at the human mind, all beings can achieve sudden enlightenment," breaking class and doctrinal shackles and thoroughly liberating practice to the individual.

4. The indigenous Chinese philosophical system
• Order/collective standard (the *I Ching* and the Confucian system): The *I Ching* builds a grand system of heaven, earth, and human with an overall evolutionary view, emphasizing the individual's placement and ordinal duty within the whole (state, clan).
• Individual freedom/equality standard (the *Daodejing* and Yangming School): Laozi's *Daodejing* pursues the individual's life-freedom and a natural egalitarian order; Wang Yangming's "Yangming School" cried out that "the way of the sage is self-sufficient in my nature," returning the highest adjudication right entirely to each individual's "conscience," greatly liberating human individuality.

III. Stage Two: The History and Length-Breadth Contrast of the Two Factions in the Atheistic Era and "Modern Scientific Outlook" (Early Modern to Modern)
After the Industrial Revolution, human society moved toward atheism, and natural science and productive forces replaced theocracy. Under the framework of the scientific outlook, human society formally evolved the development history of the two opposing factions of capitalism and communism (socialism).
1. Timeline of the two factions' development history
• 1776 publication of *The Wealth of Nations*: laid the theoretical cornerstone of the capitalist free market (individual freedom). Alongside the full eruption of the European and American Industrial Revolution, the Western bourgeoisie established absolute global-capital-expansion hegemony.
• 1848 publication of the *Communist Manifesto*: in response to the inequality brought by capitalism's disordered expansion, Marx and Engels proposed the communist scientific-outlook, advocating the organization of society's entire industrial production through the abolition of private property and scientific macro-planning (collectivism).
• 1917 "October Revolution": the Soviet regime was established, and communism formally moved toward state practice. In just a few decades, relying on a powerful "state power-planning," the Soviet Union rapidly completed heavy industrialization, becoming the world's second-largest industrial power.
• 1945–1991 (US–Soviet Cold War period): the first full-scale bipolar confrontation under the scientific outlook. The capitalist camp led by the US (NATO) and the communist camp led by the Soviet Union (Warsaw Pact) engaged in comprehensive competition in military, technological, and space domains. The Soviet Union's absolutist planned-economy model suppressed "individual vitality" in its middle-late period and disintegrated in 1991. US liberal capitalism moved toward a phased global unipolar hegemony.
• 1978 to present (the rise of China's socialist model): through reform and opening up, China successfully integrated socialism's "state power-planning" with the market's "individual activation," growing into the world's second-largest economy. Human society formally established a brand-new bipolar confrontation pattern between China and the US under the modern scientific outlook.
2. The institutional strengths and "length-breadth" contrast of the two camps
• US-led capitalism (individualism) strength: disruptive innovation and source efficiency
• Institutional strength: a laissez-faire environment allows top elites and capital to collide at high risk and high reward, easily giving birth to epoch-making technological breakthroughs from 0 to 1 (such as early the Internet, today's generative-AI large models, commercial spaceflight, etc.). Individual freedom greatly reduces the trial-and-error cycle of innovation, bringing extreme cutting-edge efficiency.
• China-led communism/socialism (collectivism) strength: grand development scale and quantitative advantage
• Institutional strength: in the industrial landing from 1 to 100, high-standard infrastructure, and industrial scaling, collectivism can display astonishing "quantitative and volumetric dominance." The state can concentrate its strength on major undertakings, rapidly rolling out the world's grandest construction scale in the new-energy full industry chain, high-speed rail networks, national-defense heavy industry, and other fields, ensuring the entire social body does not fall behind and forming strong risk-resisting resilience.

IV. Stage Three: The "Dual-Layer Game Matrix" Amid Geopolitical Conflict and Modern Technology Interweaving
Entering the 21st century, the natural scientific outlook has displayed a more complex "dual-layer structure" in international politics and technological evolution. This law first fully erupts in the outer-ring generational-transformation wars, then evolves into the inner-ring high-tech era's digital ultimate confrontation.
1. Outer-ring game: the scientific-secular camp vs. the lagging-theistic camp (generational dimensionality-reduction war)
At this level, both China and the US belong to the **"modern scientific-outlook camp."** The national foundations of both are mathematics, physics, industrial manufacturing, and modern social management. This stage frequently sees conflicts and transformations by modern scientific-secular states against backward theistic nations:
• The US approach — military dimensionality-reduction strike (hot war):
• Gulf War (Kuwait War, 1991): Saddam invaded Kuwait. The US demonstrated humanity's first truly "modern informational scientific war." Relying on satellite positioning, precision-guided weapons, and electronic warfare, the US military delivered a devastating generational crushing to the Iraqi army, which employed traditional medieval and early-industrial tactics.
• Iraq War (US–Iraq War, 2003): with absolute technological and military superiority the US again entered Baghdad, forcibly smashing the region's ancient power structure with violence, attempting to forcibly carry out "capitalist modernization transformation" of the Middle East's backward theistic region through US debt, free markets, and Western institutions.
• China's approach — economic and digital reshaping (soft power):
China, without going to war, exports digital infrastructure through the "Belt and Road" initiative (5G networks, digital power grids, cross-border e-commerce, satellite navigation). China uses the logic of industrial civilization and wealth creation to pull Middle Eastern nations onto the modernization track. In 2023, China successfully brokered the historic rapprochement between Saudi Arabia and Iran — two great Islamic secular rivals — in Beijing, essentially dissolving their ancient religious opposition directly through the interests of science, reason, and development.
• Summary: whether the US's hot-war reshaping or China's economic restructuring, their essence is that China and the US, as representatives of modern productive forces, join hands to carry out the "modern scientific-civilization generational transformation" of backward theistic nations.
2. Inner-ring game: the ultimate confrontation of "order-scale" vs. "freedom-efficiency" in the digital-tech era
Once the outer-ring transformation gradually integrates the globe into the modern-civilization grid, after a series of geopolitical changes such as the Gulf War and the US–Iraq War, China and the US immediately turn into fierce opposition between the two camps within the "modern scientific outlook" on how to dominate the Middle East and world order. As civilization evolves into the digital stage, big data, artificial intelligence (AI), and decentralized networks have become the ultimate technological weapons through which each faction exerts its own institutional strengths:
• China: big data and AI "digital collectivism" — pursuing scale and certainty
• Technological form: hyper big-data, nationalized computing power, general large models (centralized control).
• Strength display: China inherits the *Communist Manifesto*, the *I Ching*'s holistic view, and the Confucian order gene. China tends to integrate the nation's data into a giant unified network, using supercomputing and AI to carry out absolutely refined macro-control over social operation, supply-chain logistics, grain output, and disaster prevention and mitigation (such as flood and meteorological prediction). This collectivist technological form can rapidly spread AI across a market of 1.4 billion people, forming the world's largest-scale, widest-coverage intelligent infrastructure, thoroughly smoothing the friction of social operation and pursuing the system's "absolute certainty."
• US: generative AI and Web3 "digital capitalism" — pursuing disruption and extreme efficiency
• Technological form: AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) and decentralized networks (Web3/cryptocurrency).
• Strength display: the US inherits the *Wealth of Nations*, the New Testament's salvation, and the Yangming School's individual-emancipation gene. America's tech explosion is driven by top private capital (such as OpenAI, SpaceX). It does not pursue the average coverage and scale of technology, but pursues the disruptiveness of the technological singularity and source efficiency. At the same time, US-tech strongly venerates "decentralized networks," attempting to use blockchain and encryption technology to break the financial and speech regulation of traditional state machines, granting individuals absolute cross-border freedom.

V. Conclusion: The Civilizational Evolution Prospect of Oppositional Symbiosis
In summary, in the historical development of human society, the philosophical system of dual-opposition symbiosis — formed by both contingency and necessity across different regions and eras — is an invariant law of social development. From the theism of the past to today's atheism, dual-opposition symbiosis exists within each philosophical system. This paper also better illustrates that capitalism and communism together constitute the mainstream natural-scientific-outlook philosophical system of the modern world.
1. The inevitability of complementary strengths: the capitalist camp's (individual freedom) strength is **"spearhead-style innovative efficiency,"** responsible for exploring the upper limit of human technology; the communist/socialist camp's (collective consciousness) strength is **"bulldozer-style scale and quantity,"** responsible for transforming technology into the largest and most stable overall welfare of human society.
2. Dynamic symbiosis: whether the Thirty Years' War within Christianity, the Iran–Iraq War between Shia and Sunni in the Middle East, or the Gulf War and US–Iraq War launched by modern America, all prove that conflict between different generations and different camps is the norm of evolution. By placing the "digital high-tech game" at the endpoint of outer-ring war-transformation and inner-ring geopolitical game, we can clearly see: under the US–China dual-core tug-of-war, the outer-ring backward theistic nations are being accelerated into the grid of scientific civilization, while the inner-ring US and China check each other through the double-edged sword of big data and AI.
This bipolar oppositional existence under the modern natural scientific outlook is precisely the core balance that keeps human civilization from sliding into an extreme dead-end, absorbing the other's strengths amid confrontation, and always maintaining evolutionary vitality.
