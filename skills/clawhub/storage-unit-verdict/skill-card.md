## Description:

Use when paying for a storage unit (or considering one) — computes whether the unit still makes economic sense: contents depreciated value vs cumulative rent, the untouched-12-months rule, purge/downgrade/keep verdicts, climate-control need test, and a dated 30-day move-out plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

## Use Case:

Renters, people planning a move, and estate or divorce decision-makers use the skill to compare current resale value with storage costs, decide whether to keep, downgrade, or empty a unit, and plan a dated exit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Valuations and climate-control recommendations are rough estimates.

Mitigation: Use them as planning guidance and check local resale values and storage conditions before deciding.

Risk: Move-out timing may depend on lease notice terms and actual calendar dates.

Mitigation: Add actual dates to the checklist and check the lease notice period before arranging disposal or cancellation; the helper does not perform those actions.
