# Storage Unit Verdict — Reference

## Industry facts that inform the math

- US self-storage: ~1.8-1.9 billion sq ft; ~1 in 10 households rents (Self Storage Association data).
- Average length of stay: ~2 years residential (crossing the break-even line of typical contents).
- Rate increases to EXISTING tenants run 5-15%/yr — occupancy above 90% lets operators raise rates without churn risk. Your renewal letter is the trigger to re-run this audit.
- Facility economics favor your inertia: the tenant who never visits costs them nothing and pays full freight.

## Depreciation reference (expanded)

| Item class | Resale % of new | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| IKEA/particle-board furniture | 0-10% | near-zero used market; donate or dump |
| Solid wood case goods | 30-50% | the storage sweet spot IF condition held |
| Sofas | 5-15% | buyers fear bedbugs; fabric kills value |
| Mattresses | 0-5% | most states restrict resale; never store long |
| Washing machines / fridges | 20-40% | working + delivery = sellable |
| Bicycles | 30-50% | good market if tuned |
| Power tools (cordless) | 40-55% | battery health drives price |
| Hand tools | 50-65% | nearly immortal value |
| TVs | 10-20% | technology depreciation is brutal |
| Baby gear | 10-25% | safety recalls freeze value; car seats expire |
| Holiday decor | 5-10% | bulk, low value, high sentiment trap |
| Files/records | 0 resale | retention value only (see paper-mail-retention) |

**Sentimental math**: if you cannot name the item without opening the unit, run the 12-month rule with extra honesty. Photographs of objects preserve 90% of the memory at 0% of the rent.

## Lien auction mechanics (why neglect is expensive)

Standard flow after non-payment: late fees (day 1-5), lien notice (~30 days), auction (~60-90 days, state law varies). The unit's entire contents sell to one bidder for cash — commonly $50-400 for a full 10×10. Heirlooms have no special protection; auction buyers re-sell fast. If you're going to exit, exit on purpose, on a schedule, before arrears compound.

## Downgrade math

One size class down (10×10 → 5×10) saves ~30-45% if contents fit after a purge pass. Vertical stacking (shelving racks ~$60) lets a 5×10 hold what people spread across a 10×10 on the floor — the highest-ROI single change for KEEP verdicts. Also ask:

- Upper-floor vs drive-up (walk-up units run 10-20% cheaper)
- Interior vs exterior door
- Promo rates for new tenants (move-in specials don't apply to you — but competitors' promos are your negotiation lever at renewal)

## Alternatives priced

| Option | Typical cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Keep paying unit | $120-220/mo | bounded horizons, high-value contents |
| Downgrade + shelving | $70-130/mo + $60 once | KEEP verdicts |
| Basement/garage corner + dehumidifier | ~$15-30/mo electricity | semi-climate needs at home |
| Parents'/friend's garage | $0 + goodwill | small KEEP tiers |
| Sell + rebuy later | negative cost | LOW-value contents (the usual case) |
| POD/portable container during a move | $200-300/mo but delivered | moving-gap scenario only |

The sell-and-rebuy math most people avoid confronting: contents worth $1,500 resale, unit at $140/mo → in 11 months you've spent contents value. Rebuying the same goods used later costs ~$1,500. Storage only wins if the horizon is genuinely short or the contents genuinely irreplaceable/valuable.

## Estate & inheritance specifics

- Estates: the unit rent runs while probate runs — executor should audit in the first 30 days (heirs consistently overestimate estate furniture value; the table above is closer to reality).
- Divorce splits: photograph everything, agree disposition IN the decree (who pays which months), set a joint exit date — storage becomes a battlefield expense otherwise.
- International assignment/repatriation: usually the one clean KEEP case — but price sea-freight-of-essentials vs 24 months of unit rent; the unit often loses.

## Contract gotchas

- "Intro rate" resets after 3-6 months; the real comparison is month-7+ pricing.
- Mandatory tenant insurance auto-billed at $10-25/mo — your renter's/home policy may cover storage already (often 10% of contents coverage off-premises).
- Notice periods: 10-30 days; canceling on the 1st can still bill you through the 30th if notice is written into the lease.
- Admin fees at move-in ($20-30) are negotiable; vacant facilities waive them.
- Vehicle-in-unit rules (no fluids, no registration) break car-storage plans silently.
