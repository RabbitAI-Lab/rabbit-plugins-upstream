## Description:

Guides users through I Ching coin divination, explains the resulting hexagram, and supports learning and reviewing readings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan195](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan195)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for cultural and reflective I Ching readings, guided coin casting, selected classical commentary, and reviewing saved readings. It treats readings as reflection and directs medical, legal, and investment decisions to qualified professionals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readings and supplied notes may contain personal details and are saved locally in plaintext.

Mitigation: Omit sensitive background details you do not want retained.

Risk: A reading could be mistaken for professional advice on a medical, legal, or investment decision.

Mitigation: Use readings for reflection only and consult a qualified professional for those decisions.
