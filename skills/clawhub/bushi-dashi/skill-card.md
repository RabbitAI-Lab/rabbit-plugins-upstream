## Description:

An I-Ching coin-divination skill that guides a six-coin cast, interprets the resulting hexagram, and can save readings in a personal journal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan195](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan195)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to cast and explore I-Ching readings, study hexagram commentary, and keep a personal reading journal. Readings are for cultural reflection and entertainment, not professional medical, legal, or investment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readings and personal notes may be stored in plaintext in a local journal.

Mitigation: Avoid recording sensitive concerns, precise birth details, or location details you do not want saved.

Risk: A divination reading could be mistaken for professional medical, legal, or investment guidance.

Mitigation: Treat readings as entertainment or reflection, and consult a qualified professional for those decisions.
