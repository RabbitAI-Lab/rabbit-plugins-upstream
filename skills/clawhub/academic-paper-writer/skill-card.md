## Description:

Guided academic paper writing: IMRaD section drafting, citation lookup from Zotero, iterative revision.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External academic and clinical writers use this skill to draft, revise, and complete sections of scientific papers and systematic reviews with structured IMRaD guidance and verified citation support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can query Zotero or PubMed and may offer to add references to Zotero, which can expose confidential or unpublished research context or change a user's reference collection.

Mitigation: Use it only in the intended JD workspace, confirm external reference queries are acceptable, use least-privilege Zotero credentials, and require explicit confirmation before adding any DOI to Zotero.

Risk: Academic drafts can become misleading if citations, epidemiologic claims, or statistical claims are not verified against reliable sources.

Mitigation: Do not use unverified references; require source verification before including statistical or epidemiologic claims and distinguish preliminary signals from practice-changing evidence.
