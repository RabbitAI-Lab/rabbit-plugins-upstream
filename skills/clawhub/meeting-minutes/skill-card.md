## Description:

把会议录音转写、聊天记录或零散笔记整理成可转发的会议纪要。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangaiyang2013](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangaiyang2013)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external collaborators use this skill to turn meeting transcripts, chat logs, or rough notes into concise Chinese meeting minutes with decisions, action items, and open questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts, chat logs, and notes may contain sensitive business or personal information.

Mitigation: Review the generated minutes before sharing and clarify the intended audience when handling sensitive meeting material.

Risk: The skill may omit or mark missing details when source material lacks dates, participants, owners, or deadlines.

Mitigation: Provide the meeting topic, time, attendees, audience, and whether action owners and due dates should be listed.
