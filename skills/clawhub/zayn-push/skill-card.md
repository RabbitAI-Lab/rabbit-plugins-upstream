## Description: <br>
Identifies the current blocker in a customer project and designs the smallest action to reduce decision friction and move the opportunity forward. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales and customer-facing teams use this skill to assess stalled opportunities, identify the current decision blocker, and choose one clear next action. It is intended for customer projects where the user can provide current status, recent feedback, known buying signals, blockers, and the desired next-step objective. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Customer project details supplied to the skill may include sensitive business information. <br>
Mitigation: Treat inputs as sensitive and avoid including unnecessary confidential details. <br>
Risk: Generated customer-facing wording may be incorrect, premature, or too forceful if sent without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review the proposed wording before sending and confirm that it matches verified project facts, customer timing, and company boundaries. <br>
