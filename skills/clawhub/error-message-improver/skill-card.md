## Description:

Helps developers and support teams write or review clearer error messages that explain what failed, why it failed, and what to do next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Application developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users can use this skill to turn unclear errors into messages that explain the failure, its likely cause, and a useful next action. It can also produce reusable workflows, checklists, analysis, or implementation support for error-message work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
