## Description:

Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague error reports into clearer messages that explain what failed, why it failed, and what action to take next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and end users use this skill to produce clearer error-message copy, troubleshooting workflows, checklists, analysis, code changes, or decision support for situations where vague errors block resolution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill during general debugging or support conversations.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrow trigger phrases in environments where accidental activation would be disruptive.
