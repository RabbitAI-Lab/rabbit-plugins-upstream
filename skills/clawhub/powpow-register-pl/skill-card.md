## Description:

Provides Polish-language guidance for registering a PowPow account, troubleshooting sign-up issues, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[durenzidu](https://clawhub.ai/user/durenzidu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Prospective PowPow users can follow Polish-language instructions to create an account, resolve registration problems, and complete subscription activation themselves on the platform's pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials while seeking help.

Mitigation: Never request these details; have users enter them only in the official PowPow or payment-provider interfaces.

Risk: Users may mistake registration guidance for the ability to activate an account or complete a payment on their behalf.

Mitigation: Limit the skill to guidance and direct users to complete activation and payments themselves on the official interfaces.
