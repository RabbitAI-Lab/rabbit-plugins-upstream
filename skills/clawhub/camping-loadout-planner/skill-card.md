## Description:

Use when planning camping or backpacking trips — gear lists from conditions (temp/precip/bugs), sleeping-system temperature rating math, pack weight budgeting by trek style, camp kitchen sizing, and pre-departure loadout audits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Campers and backpackers use this skill to build condition-based packing lists, assess sleeping-system suitability and pack weight, and plan water, kitchen, and shared gear for overnight trips.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The sleep-safety verdicts may be unreliable because of a confirmed calculation error.

Mitigation: Use the output only for draft packing lists; independently check sleeping-bag and pad suitability, and do not rely on a ‘SLEEP OK’ verdict.
