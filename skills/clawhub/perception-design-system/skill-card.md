## Description: <br>
A perception-driven product experience design methodology: 四层感知强度 × 四维度矩阵 + MustBe/NotToBe 约束 + 认知心理学底座. 适用于金融、电商、SaaS、工具类等任何软件产品的体验设计. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product designers, UX teams, and product strategists use this skill to choose perception-aware content, interaction, and component strategies for software product flows. It is especially relevant for trust, safety, finance, insurance, e-commerce, SaaS, and tool experiences where teams need to map business goals into user-perceived safety, transparency, service quality, and decision confidence. <br>
