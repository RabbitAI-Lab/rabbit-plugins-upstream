## Description:

Guides founders through capturing recurring work as repeatable SOPs that can be delegated to people or AI agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and operations leads use the skill to identify high-leverage recurring work and document, validate, assign, and maintain SOPs for delegation or automation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recordings of real workflows may expose credentials or private information when shared with people or AI services.

Mitigation: Use demonstration data where possible, hide credentials and private information, and review recordings before sharing them.

Risk: Automating a documented task without clearly defined permissions may exceed intended access.

Mitigation: Define permissions separately before automating any documented task.
