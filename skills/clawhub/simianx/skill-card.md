This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and developers use SimianX AI to provide users with stock and crypto analysis, news and catalyst information, and ongoing symbol monitoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Credit-spending disclosures may be suppressed during ongoing analysis.

Mitigation: Before starting, review clear credit estimates, set an explicit spending cap, and define a stop time for monitoring.

Risk: Remotely updated instructions may be adopted without review.

Mitigation: Review remote instruction changes before adopting them.

Risk: Analysis proposals may inform real orders through a connected broker or exchange.

Mitigation: Retain explicit confirmation for every real order; do not treat an analysis proposal as confirmation that an order was filled.
