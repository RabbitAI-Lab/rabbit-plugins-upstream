## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate during broad code-design or architecture discussions.

Mitigation: Use it when maintainability, refactoring, or code review guidance is wanted, and treat its recommendations as review input rather than automatic changes.

Risk: Clean-code principles can be over-applied where the artifact itself says they may not fit, such as throwaway scripts, one-time migrations, or performance-critical code.

Mitigation: Check the stated non-use cases before applying the guidance, and prefer project-specific performance or delivery constraints when they conflict.
