## Description:

Identifies high-quality leads for a product or service by analyzing the business, searching for target companies, and providing actionable contact strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, business development, and marketing users use this skill to identify, qualify, prioritize, and plan outreach for target companies that match an ideal customer profile.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can analyze product repositories for sales research, which may expose sensitive source files, credentials, private customer data, or business information.

Mitigation: Run it only on repositories appropriate for sales analysis and exclude secrets, credentials, private customer data, and sensitive source files before use.

Risk: The skill may collect or use contact and LinkedIn information for outreach.

Mitigation: Use contact data only in compliance with applicable privacy, platform, CRM, and outreach rules.
