## Description:

Helps e-commerce operators assess products, pricing, advertising, fulfillment and settlements across domestic and cross-border platforms using store data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1-yyr](https://clawhub.ai/user/1-yyr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Store operators and e-commerce analysts use this skill to evaluate products, model profit and cross-border costs, diagnose sales funnels, review promotional copy, reconcile settlements and preview bulk changes before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Store exports may contain sensitive business data.

Mitigation: Provide only the data needed for the requested analysis.

Risk: Incorrect inputs or unreviewed reports can lead to poor pricing or inventory decisions.

Mitigation: Check source data and review calculations and reports before using them for business decisions.

Risk: Bulk changes or other platform actions could have unintended consequences.

Mitigation: Keep account login, publishing, deletion and actual price changes under human control; use previews to review proposed changes.
