## Description:

ljh-yinzi is a content-factor toolkit for short-video e-commerce that decomposes viral materials into reusable visual and script factors, ranks candidate focus topics by expected value, and helps build factor-library tables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Content marketers, e-commerce operators, and creative teams use this skill to analyze short-video sales content, prioritize creative testing directions, and organize reusable content factors for future campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may keep local business notes, including brand context, spend, ROI, or customer-positioning details.

Mitigation: Use it in private workspaces for sensitive business data, and review or delete the local onboarding file and ljh archive folder when cross-session state is not desired.
