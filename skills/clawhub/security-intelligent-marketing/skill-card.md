## Description:

Helps bank and securities marketing teams draft customer segments, campaign plans, compliant copy, A/B tests, and ROI analyses for review before publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Bank and securities marketing teams use this skill to draft regulated financial-product campaigns, customer-segmentation approaches, marketing copy, A/B test plans, and ROI analyses. Outputs require institutional compliance review before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real customer records or identifiable financial data could be disclosed in prompts.

Mitigation: Use only aggregate segment descriptions and estimates; keep customer selection and identifiable data in approved internal systems.

Risk: Unreviewed financial-marketing drafts could contain unsuitable or misleading claims.

Mitigation: Check product suitability and prohibited claims, then obtain institutional compliance sign-off before saving or launching a campaign.
