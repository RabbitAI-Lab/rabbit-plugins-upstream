## Description:

Decision validation and thinking frameworks for founders. Use to pressure-test a decision, validate next steps, or sanity-check an approach - "should I", "help me think through", "what am I missing". Covers fundraising, customers, runway.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tenequm](https://clawhub.ai/user/tenequm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Startup founders use this conversational coaching aid to pressure-test decisions, clarify next steps, and think through fundraising, customer discovery, prioritization, and runway questions. It offers structured prompts and frameworks such as GROW and solution-focused coaching, with additional crypto and Web3 context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Founders may share personal or business details they would prefer to keep private.

Mitigation: Share only details you are comfortable putting into chat.

Risk: Broad trigger examples may invoke coaching unexpectedly, and dated funding, legal, or treasury information may no longer be current.

Mitigation: Clarify the decision or coaching help you want, and verify current funding, legal, and treasury information before acting.
