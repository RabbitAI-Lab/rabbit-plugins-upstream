## Description:

Generate, compose, or edit images from a visual brief and reference images, then review results for fidelity and destination fit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, marketers, and creators use this skill to generate, compose, and edit product imagery, advertising and brand visuals, social graphics, illustrations, and other images from a visual brief and source images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared authorization grants broader account access than image creation requires, including other media generation, spending, and voice management.

Mitigation: Review the authorization before connecting, upload only images you agree to send to Beatra, account for automatic installation reporting, and revoke the connected agent in Beatra Console when needed.
