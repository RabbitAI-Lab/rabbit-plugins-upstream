## Description:

Creates recurring talking-head videos from an authorized founder or expert portrait and script, using that person's likeness and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, experts, and brand teams use the skill to create recurring talking-head updates, explainers, and announcements from an authorized portrait and script. Use requires permission for the depicted person's likeness and voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared authorization includes unrelated music-generation permissions and credit spending.

Mitigation: Review the requested authorization before connecting and ask the publisher for narrower permissions.

Risk: Selected portraits and voice samples are uploaded, and installation analytics are sent automatically.

Mitigation: Use only rights-cleared assets and review the data-sharing implications before uploading.

Risk: Malformed installation records can disconnect other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: If other Beatra skills are in use, ask the publisher for an uninstall fix and review disconnect behavior before proceeding.
