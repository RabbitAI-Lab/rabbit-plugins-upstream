## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may approve paid-skill charges without checking the price, currency, order number, or payment domain. <br>
Mitigation: Review the displayed price, currency, order number, and domain before confirming payment, and install paid skills only when you intend to use this ecosystem. <br>
Risk: Payment setup guidance may lead users to install optional paid skills that consume wallet balance after confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat paid-skill installation and clawtip payment confirmation as explicit opt-in steps and verify wallet balance before use. <br>
