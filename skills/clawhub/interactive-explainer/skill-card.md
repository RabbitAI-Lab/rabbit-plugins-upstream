## Description:

Helps create short educational videos that combine host narration with on-camera character dialogue.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators and content creators use this skill to plan and produce educational shorts with alternating narrator and character scenes, particularly for history and science topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpinned installation of external generation skills may introduce changes to the workflow.

Mitigation: Install from a trusted source and prefer pinned or vetted versions; install only the dependencies needed.

Risk: Image, audio, and video generation may incur charges or produce unsuitable output.

Mitigation: Review the plan, stills, and clips at the approval gates before paid generation or final delivery.

Risk: Default voice, gender, and language choices may not suit the intended audience.

Mitigation: Set cast and narration choices to match the audience before generation.
