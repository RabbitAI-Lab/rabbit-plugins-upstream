## Description:

Helps teachers turn class assessment results into evidence-based learning profiles, knowledge-point weaknesses, and actionable teaching adjustments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

K–12 teachers use this skill to analyze item-level scores, identify class and individual learning gaps, and plan differentiated teaching or factual parent communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize crisis resources, curriculum assumptions, and consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student assessment records or parent-facing feedback may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use student aliases rather than real names where possible, check sharing consent, and confirm before saving or sharing outputs.

Risk: Unsupported trends or fixed student labels could mislead teaching decisions.

Mitigation: Mark insufficient evidence explicitly, require repeated assessments for trends, and review proposed teaching adjustments before use.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and legal assumptions may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis resources and applicable consent requirements before deployment outside mainland China.
