## Description:

帮助中小学教师分析班级逐题成绩、定位知识点薄弱环节，并形成分层教学调整建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to turn class assessment data into class reports, student diagnosis cards, knowledge-point analyses, and differentiated teaching suggestions. Parent-facing summaries require teacher review and sharing consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, legal assumptions, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student identities or sensitive assessment materials could be exposed.

Mitigation: Use student IDs rather than real names; do not enter unreleased exam materials.

Risk: Parent-facing summaries or student-profile updates could be shared without consent.

Mitigation: Verify sharing and writeback consent, and have the teacher review summaries before use.

Risk: Assessment records could be changed unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Confirm any proposed record writes before saving.

Risk: Mainland China-specific guidance could be inappropriate in other locations.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts, curriculum references, and legal assumptions before use outside Mainland China.
