## Description: <br>
The model-agnostic AI-video router and brief routes video jobs to the right tool category, writes portable briefs, and keeps generation, editing, disclosure, and publishing responsibilities explicit. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media teams, creators, and marketing operators use this skill to decide whether a video job needs generative B-roll, an avatar, voiceover, clipping, or filming instead, then produce a portable brief for the chosen downstream tool. It is a planning and routing skill; it does not generate media or publish content itself. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Video provider rankings, availability, and pricing can change quickly, so stale recommendations may route work to a poor or unavailable tool. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current provider availability, costs, and fit before acting on routing recommendations, and re-check the toolbox quarterly. <br>
Risk: Generated video, avatar, or voice content can create rights, likeness, disclosure, or platform-compliance issues. <br>
Mitigation: Use consented avatars or licensed talent, review rights and usage terms before commercial publishing, and keep platform AI disclosures in the final content. <br>
Risk: Downstream video generation or publishing skills may introduce separate operational or security risks. <br>
Mitigation: Review and scan each downstream generation, editing, or publishing skill separately before deployment. <br>
