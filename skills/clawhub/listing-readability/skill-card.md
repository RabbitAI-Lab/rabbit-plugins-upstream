## Description:

检查 Amazon Listing 的结构、术语、重复表达和评论反映的理解障碍，并提供可读性建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and operators use this skill to assess whether listing copy is clear, identify buyer comprehension barriers from product details and collected reviews, and get readability suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broader account controls may spend credits despite the promised confirmation steps.

Mitigation: Require confirmation for every charge, use manual collection, review recurring schedules, and avoid deepdive for quote-only requests.
