## Use Case: <br>
Quality engineers, product auditors, and supplier quality teams use this skill to plan and execute VDA6.5 product audits for automotive parts, systems, assemblies, and raw materials. It helps structure audit checklists, record findings, rate risk, and draft corrective-action recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Audit photos and records may capture restricted areas, personal data, customer information, or proprietary identifiers. <br>
Mitigation: Capture only the evidence needed for the audit, follow site and customer data-classification rules, and store records only in approved access-controlled systems. <br>
Risk: Generated audit findings, risk ratings, or corrective-action recommendations may be incomplete or inconsistent with local procedures or customer-specific requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified product audit personnel review outputs against VDA6.5, customer requirements, drawings, control plans, and site procedures before release or action. <br>
