## Description:

Helps teams assess action quality and timing separately, classify a decision in a four-quadrant matrix, and choose a next step.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Decision makers and teams use this framework to assess whether a major action is sound and whether its conditions are ready, especially for launches, market entry, policy decisions, partnerships, and post-mortems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
