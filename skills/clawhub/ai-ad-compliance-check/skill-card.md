## Description:

Reviews AI product video ads against platform and market rules and produces timestamped findings, suggested revisions, disclosure guidance, and an overall verdict.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Advertisers and marketing teams use the skill to review finished AI product ads before publishing, identify claims or depictions that need attention, and prepare platform disclosures or revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (including New York), EU, and UK; guidance covers TikTok Shop and ads, Meta, and YouTube.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video or URL submissions may contain confidential ad material and consume VARG credits.

Mitigation: Submit only material you are authorized to share, and check VARG's retention terms before sending confidential ads.

Risk: Compliance findings may be incomplete or out of date and could be mistaken for legal approval.

Mitigation: Check important conclusions against current primary sources; seek legal review for regulated products.
