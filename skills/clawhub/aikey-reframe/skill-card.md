## Description:

Steps back from repeated revisions to check whether the question, planned actions, or problem layer is wrong, then gives a focused fix in the same reply.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Independent creators, small-business owners, and people writing skills or prompts use it when repeated revisions or conflicting instructions are not solving the problem. It checks the framing and suggests the smallest effective change while preserving the user's intended outcome.
