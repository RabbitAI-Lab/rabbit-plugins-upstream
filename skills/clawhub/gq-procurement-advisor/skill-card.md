## Description: <br>
Provides Chinese state-owned-enterprise procurement compliance guidance for procurement method selection, document review, risk assessment, audit remediation, and draft procurement materials, while routing out-of-scope government procurement, pure construction tendering, litigation, and arbitration questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Procurement, legal, compliance, and audit staff use this skill to analyze Chinese state-owned-enterprise purchasing scenarios, select procurement methods, review procurement documents, assess compliance risks, support audit remediation, and draft standard procurement materials. It is advisory and directs users to provide project facts such as project type, budget, supplier situation, and applicable internal controls before relying on an answer. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Procurement compliance guidance can be incorrect, outdated, incomplete, or unsuitable for high-value or disputed matters. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory and verify them against current regulations, enterprise internal controls, and qualified legal or procurement counsel before acting. <br>
Risk: Sensitive procurement documents or supplier information may be exposed if shared in an environment without appropriate data-handling controls. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive procurement materials unless the deployment environment, knowledge-base access, retention, and document-handling policies are acceptable. <br>
Risk: The skill is scoped to state-owned-enterprise production and operating procurement, not government procurement, pure construction-project tendering, litigation, or arbitration strategy. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the funding source, project type, and legal regime before use, and route out-of-scope matters to the appropriate specialist workflow or professional advisor. <br>
