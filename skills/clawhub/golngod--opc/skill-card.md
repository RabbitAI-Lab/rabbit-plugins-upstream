## Description: <br>
Provides finance and tax planning guidance for Chinese one-person company founders, including entity selection, tax optimization, compliance risk avoidance, invoicing, filing, and annual planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External solo founders and agents assisting them use this skill to compare Chinese business entity options, plan tax categories, avoid common compliance issues, and prepare structured finance and tax reminders for one-person businesses. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tax thresholds, filing deadlines, and optimization strategies can change or vary by local facts. <br>
Mitigation: Verify specific thresholds, deadlines, and strategies against current Chinese tax rules and a qualified local tax professional before relying on the guidance. <br>
Risk: Entity selection and tax planning guidance may be incomplete if the user omits revenue, invoice, liability, location, or business activity details. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as planning guidance, gather the relevant business facts, and review the final plan with a qualified professional before acting. <br>
