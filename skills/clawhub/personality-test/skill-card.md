## Use Case: <br>
External users can answer a 70-question MBTI-style questionnaire and use the scoring guidance to calculate a four-letter personality type with dimension percentages and a short type description. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The quiz asks personal personality-style questions that may remain in chat history. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing answers you would not want retained in the conversation record. <br>
Risk: The MBTI-style result may be mistaken for a clinical, diagnostic, or authoritative assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the result as informational only and avoid using it for medical, hiring, or other consequential decisions. <br>
