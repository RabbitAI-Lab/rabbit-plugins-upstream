## Description: <br>
A task-planning skill for decomposing complex work into subtasks, setting deadlines, tracking dependencies, marking milestones, and recording task context. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Individual users use this skill to turn informal project goals into structured task plans with subtasks, deadlines, dependencies, milestones, and contextual notes. It is positioned for personal and lightweight task-management workflows rather than team collaboration. <br>
