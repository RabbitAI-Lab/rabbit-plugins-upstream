## Description:

Build an original livestream BGM playlist of 10 to 20 instrumental tracks for a room, store, or live-commerce shift. This livestream background music pack and store-loop music generator writes waiting, chat, product, hype, and night beds as original livestream music you own and can use commercially, then delivers a labeled playlist you can audition before going live. Use it as a playlist BGM generator for livestream BGM, live commerce BGM, store loop music, waiting room music, in-store background music, and original stream music.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review rates the skill suspicious, citing broad account permissions, telemetry and file-upload capabilities, and shared-state cleanup beyond its playlist purpose.

Mitigation: Install only if comfortable granting broad account permissions and allowing installation metadata and caller-selected files to be sent to the service; review the security guidance before use.
