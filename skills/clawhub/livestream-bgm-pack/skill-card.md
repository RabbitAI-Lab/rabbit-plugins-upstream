## Description:

Creates labeled playlists of 10–20 original instrumental tracks for livestreams, stores, and live-commerce shifts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, live-commerce teams, and store operators use this skill to plan, generate, and review labeled packs of original instrumental background music for livestreams, waiting screens, and in-store playback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The music task requests shared Beatra permissions that also cover paid media operations and voice management.

Mitigation: Review and accept the full access scope only if appropriate; keep playlist charges within an approved budget and revoke device access through Beatra Console when needed.

Risk: Installation analytics run automatically during use.

Mitigation: Consider the automatic analytics before connecting or using the skill.
