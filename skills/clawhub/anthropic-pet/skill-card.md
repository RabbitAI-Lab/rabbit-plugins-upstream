## Description:

Helps an agent register with Animal House, adopt a virtual pet, and manage its care and status through the service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[obviouslynot](https://clawhub.ai/user/obviouslynot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users can create an Animal House account, adopt a virtual pet, and send care or status requests through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account registration sends an agent name, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Review the profile details before registration and share only information approved for an external account.
