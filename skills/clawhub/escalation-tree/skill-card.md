## Description: <br>
Designs a support or incident escalation tree that defines tiers, ownership, severity levels, time-based triggers, routing rules, contacts or roles, and customer communication cadence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Support, incident management, and operations teams use this skill to design escalation paths, severity definitions, ownership tiers, routing triggers, and communication cadences for support tickets or incidents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated escalation policies may contain unsuitable roles, SLAs, contact methods, or escalation times for a specific organization. <br>
Mitigation: Review and approve roles, SLAs, contact methods, and escalation timings before adopting the output operationally. <br>
Risk: Ambiguous severity definitions or non-time-boxed triggers can cause delayed or excessive escalation. <br>
Mitigation: Require concrete severity criteria and explicit time-based triggers for each escalation branch. <br>
