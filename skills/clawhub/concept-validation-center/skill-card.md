## Description: <br>
Helps innovators validate the feasibility of technical or creative concepts by coordinating expert roles across technical validation, business exploration, resource matching, trend analysis, and startup coaching through a Stage-Gate workflow. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External innovators, incubator teams, and technology-transfer staff use this skill to structure concept validation, prepare gate reviews, and generate stage-specific advisory outputs for go/no-go decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce feasibility, market, and go/no-go recommendations that may be incomplete or incorrect for a specific project. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory inputs and have qualified reviewers validate technical, business, legal, and resource assumptions before acting. <br>
