## Description:

Creates short-form product video hooks with coordinated visuals, spoken lines, and text overlays, with optional hook clips for testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use it to develop varied, claim-aware opening hooks for TikTok, Reels, and Meta product videos, with optional short clips for testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional clip generation sends selected photos or footage to an external service and can incur credits.

Mitigation: Confirm the service's media handling, choose assets you are comfortable sharing, and approve the quoted cost before creating clips.

Risk: The separate editing skill and its unbundled script were outside this package's security review.

Mitigation: Review that editing skill before using its script.
