## Description:

Creates short product video hooks for TikTok, Reels, and Meta, pairing an opening visual, spoken line, and text overlay while checking claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product marketers and video creators use the skill to draft and test opening hooks for short product ads, using buyer language and matching the visual, spoken line, and overlay.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
