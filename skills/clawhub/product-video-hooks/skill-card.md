## Description:

Creates product video ad hooks as aligned visual, spoken, and on-screen text openers, with optional short vertical clips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce marketers and creators use the skill to turn a product and buyer language into varied short-form video ad openers, then optionally make vertical clips for testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional video generation costs credits and sends selected images or references to Varg and its model providers; retention terms were not established.

Mitigation: Approve the quoted cost and confirm the selected material may be processed by Varg and its providers before generating clips.

Risk: Hooks may include advertising claims or prices that are inaccurate or do not meet platform rules.

Mitigation: Check each claim against substantiated product information, verify prices against the live offer, and review the hook against the destination platform's rules.

Risk: Related skills may have separate behavior and scripts.

Mitigation: Install only the intended skill and review companion skills separately before executing their scripts.
