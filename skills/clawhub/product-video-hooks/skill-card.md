## Description:

Creates aligned visual, spoken, and text hooks for product videos, adapts them to buyer awareness and platform claim rules, and can prepare individual test clips when footage is available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External ecommerce marketers and creators use the skill to write and vary the opening seconds of product videos and ads. When suitable footage is available, they can also prepare short clips for testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional clip generation may send selected product photos, reference imagery, and script text to external AI services, and may incur credit costs.

Mitigation: Use material approved for external processing, quote the generation cost, and get approval before creating clips. Review the related generation and editing skills before using them.
