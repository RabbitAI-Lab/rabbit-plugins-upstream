### Meals & Entertainment Rules (2024)
- Business meals: **50% deductible** (not 100%)
- Must be with client/customer/business associate
- Must discuss business
- Receipt required for expenses over $75
- Entertainment (golf, theater): NOT deductible since TCJA 2018

## Schedule A — Itemized Personal Deductions

### Medical & Dental (7.5% AGI floor)
- Medical insurance premiums
- Doctor/dentist/hospital bills
- Prescription medications
- Medical devices (glasses, hearing aids)
- Mileage to medical appointments

### Taxes Paid
- State/local income tax OR sales tax (choose one)
- Property tax (real estate + personal property)
- SALT cap: $10,000 max combined (TCJA)

### Interest Paid
- Mortgage interest (up to $750K loan for homes purchased after 2017)
- Mortgage insurance premiums (subject to phaseout)

### Charity
- Cash donations: up to 60% of AGI
- Non-cash donations: document condition and fair market value
- Mileage: 14 cents/mile for volunteer driving

### Casualty & Theft Losses
- Only federally declared disaster areas (TCJA restriction)

## Home Office Deduction

### Simplified Method
- $5 per square foot, max 300 sqft
- Maximum deduction: $1,500/year
- No separate calculation of home expenses

### Detailed Method
- Calculate business-use percentage of home: (office sqft / total sqft)
- Apply percentage to: rent/mortgage interest, utilities, insurance,
  repairs, depreciation
- More paperwork but potentially larger deduction

### Requirements
- Space used **regularly and exclusively** for business
- Must be your principal place of business
- Cannot be an employee (unless for employer convenience)

## Common Missed Deductions

1. **Home internet** — business percentage
2. **Cell phone** — business percentage
3. **Professional development** — courses, books, conferences
4. **Bank fees** on business accounts
5. **Subscriptions** — software, professional memberships
6. **Startup costs** — up to $5,000 first year
7. **Health insurance premiums** (self-employed)
8. **Retirement contributions** (SEP-IRA, Solo 401k)
