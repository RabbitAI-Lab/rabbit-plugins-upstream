## Description:

Provides a structured Five Forces framework for assessing industry competitiveness, profitability drivers, and strategic responses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business strategists, founders, and investors use this skill to assess an industry's competitive structure, profitability, and entry or positioning decisions. It guides users through defining the market, scoring the five forces, identifying the binding force, and choosing a focused response.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or incomplete industry figures could lead to misleading business or investment conclusions.

Mitigation: Verify current industry figures and profitability data against reliable sources before making business or investment decisions.
