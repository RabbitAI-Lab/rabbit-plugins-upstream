## Description:

Turns local videos and public video links into transcripts and prompt-guided summaries, rewrites, and other written content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to transcribe videos or turn their contents into summaries, captions, meeting notes, translations, and social-media copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private or sensitive videos may be downloaded, uploaded to cloud storage, and processed by the Guaikei service.

Mitigation: Obtain permission and review privacy requirements before submitting client, regulated, or confidential media; avoid using it when cloud processing is not allowed.

Risk: Publisher, package identity, license, and upload disclosures are inconsistent or incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm the publisher and actual upload destinations before installation, and verify the license terms and support contact with the publisher.
