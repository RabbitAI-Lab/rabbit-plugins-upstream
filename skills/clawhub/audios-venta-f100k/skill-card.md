## Description:

Escribe guiones de audio de venta en espanol neutro para WhatsApp, Instagram DM o Telegram, convirtiendo una oferta y un contexto de conversacion privada en un guion breve, calido y persuasivo de hasta 50 segundos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, sellers, and business operators use this skill to draft private one-on-one sales voice-message scripts for openings, closes, objections, follow-ups, applications, and post-purchase welcomes. It is intended for Spanish-language scripts that the user reviews, records, and sends themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated scripts may incorporate local prospect or business context from the user's workspace.

Mitigation: Review any prospect or business data included in the Markdown draft before using or sharing the script.

Risk: The skill drafts persuasive sales language that may need audience-specific tone or dialect adjustments.

Mitigation: Review the generated script for accuracy, claims, dialect, and tone before recording or sending it.

Risk: The skill stores generated drafts as local Markdown deliverables.

Mitigation: Check the local deliverable path and contents before distributing the file or recording from it.
