## Description:

Helps agents research onchain tooling and adoption, check packages before installation, and prepare evidence-backed plans or unsigned routes through Sato Hub.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[satohubai](https://clawhub.ai/user/satohubai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use Sato Hub to compare crypto-agent components, cite onchain adoption data, inspect installation disclosures, and draft plans or unsigned swap and launch routes before taking action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lookup inputs, including repositories, packages, endpoints, token addresses, goals or optional email addresses, are sent to Sato Hub.

Mitigation: Share only inputs you are comfortable disclosing; avoid email-based watch subscriptions unless the hosted service's retention and deletion uncertainty is acceptable.

Risk: Swap or launch quotes and unsigned routes can be stale or unsuitable, and signing may incur disclosed fees or move funds.

Mitigation: Treat routes as informational, independently review the venue, quote, calldata and fee disclosures, and sign only with your own wallet after review.
