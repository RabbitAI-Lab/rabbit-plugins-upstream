## Description: <br>
Builds a sustainable recurring content calendar by sizing cadence to capacity, mapping pillars and formats into platform-specific slots, reserving reactive room, and setting a planning rhythm. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, founders, and social media teams use this skill to turn existing strategy, content pillars, and brand context into a repeatable posting rhythm across platforms. It produces a reusable calendar structure rather than one-off post ideas. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask the agent to read brand, strategy, and content-pillar documents that can contain sensitive business context. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the inputs needed for the calendar and review the generated plan before circulating it. <br>
Risk: Calendar outputs can be handed to downstream scheduling or publishing workflows where mistakes may become public. <br>
Mitigation: Review downstream scheduling, queueing, and final content before anything goes live. <br>
Risk: Cadence and timing guidance is general and should not be treated as proven performance optimization. <br>
Mitigation: Use native platform insights or qualitative review to adjust the calendar over time. <br>
