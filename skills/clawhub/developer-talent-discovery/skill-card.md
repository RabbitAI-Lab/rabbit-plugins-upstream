## Description:

Find developers through Crawlora public GitHub user datasets and verify relevant repository and contribution evidence for role-specific technical shortlists or open-source collaborator discovery, without outreach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters, hiring teams, and open-source project leads use this skill to identify developers against job-related criteria and review public repository and contribution evidence before making a shortlist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries and an API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated key and limit queries to the requested, job-relevant scope and credit budget.

Risk: Public profile, contact, location, activity, and availability information may be sensitive, stale, or incomplete.

Mitigation: Keep contact details to relevant public professional channels, avoid unsolicited outreach, preserve source and retrieval dates, and verify material claims separately.

Risk: Popularity or public contribution signals may be mistaken for professional ability or hiring suitability.

Mitigation: Tie each criterion to observable work, state unknowns, and avoid inferring protected traits or treating absent public work as low ability.
