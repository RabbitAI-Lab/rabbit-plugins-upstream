## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read and update local zmm content vault files, including memory, data tracking, and topic pipeline records.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation when possible and review proposed file updates before accepting them.

Risk: A retro based on uncertain or single-video evidence can overstate a pattern or write premature conclusions into memory.

Mitigation: Treat single-video findings as hypotheses and confirm repeated signals before recording them as validated rules.
