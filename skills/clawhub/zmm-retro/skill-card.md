## Description:

Post-publish retro helps a solo knowledge creator collect real short-video performance data, compare it with pre-publish predictions, attribute outcomes, and write validated patterns into skill memory.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo knowledge creators use this skill after publishing a short video to collect platform metrics, compare them with the original topic and hook expectations, identify likely causes, and choose the next content action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read the whole shared zmm skill-memory area and directly change persistent memories, analytics, and topic-planning records without an explicit confirmation gate.

Mitigation: Use it in a controlled vault, review proposed conclusions before allowing writes, and avoid ambiguous casual triggers when only analysis is intended.
