## Description: <br>
GlobePilot AI Agent V1 is a travel intelligence agent that provides visa, currency, airport, event, cultural, emergency, seasonal, lodging, local discovery, and food guidance for global travel planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sarqovik](https://clawhub.ai/user/sarqovik) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Travelers and travel-support agents use this skill to request practical destination information before and during trips, including visa requirements, currency conversion, airport status, local events, cultural tips, emergency contacts, travel timing, accommodations, hidden gems, and food recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel guidance may depend on current travel data sources and can become outdated or incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Verify critical visa, airport, emergency, payment, and itinerary decisions with official or trusted local sources before acting. <br>
Risk: Travel planning can involve sensitive personal, passport, payment, or account information. <br>
Mitigation: Do not provide passports, payment details, account credentials, or other sensitive data unless the skill clearly documents why they are needed and the provider is trusted. <br>
