## Description:

Provides educational product-matching examples, customer-conversation drafts, and suitability checklists for mobile banking wealth teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Digital banking teams and wealth advisers use this skill to draft training examples for product matching, compliant customer conversations, and suitability reviews. Its examples are not customer-facing investment recommendations and require qualified professional and compliance review before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real customer identities, account details, holdings, or risk-assessment records could be exposed if entered into prompts.

Mitigation: Use anonymized customer scenarios; do not provide real customer identifiers, account details, holdings, or assessment records.

Risk: Illustrative matching outputs or conversation drafts could be mistaken for approved customer-facing investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as training material and require licensed professional and compliance review before any customer-facing use.
