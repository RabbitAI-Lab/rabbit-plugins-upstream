## Use Case:

Digital banking teams, wealth managers, and compliance reviewers use this skill as an educational template for bank-app wealth-advisory flows, product-matching examples, customer interaction scripts, and suitability-control checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter real customer identifiers, account details, holdings, or original risk-assessment records into an educational workflow.

Mitigation: Use only anonymized or synthetic customer profiles and exclude personal, account, holdings, and original risk-assessment data.

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for actual financial, legal, insurance, or investment recommendations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as training and template material that requires bank compliance review and qualified professional approval before any real-world use.

Risk: Illustrative code snippets may be copied into production as if they were a complete recommendation engine.

Mitigation: Implement, test, and govern any production matching logic separately within a licensed institution's suitability, data-security, and review controls.
