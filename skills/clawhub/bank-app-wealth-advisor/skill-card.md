## Description:

AI-powered wealth advisory assistant for bank mobile applications -- provides product recommendations, investment consultation, and compliance-compliant customer interaction for digital banking teams.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Use Case:

Digital banking and wealth management teams use this skill as an educational reference for drafting product-matching examples, customer conversation scripts, suitability controls, and compliance-review checklists. Its outputs require qualified human review and must not be used as direct customer recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat educational product-matching examples or conversation scripts as customer-ready financial recommendations.

Mitigation: Require qualified human and compliance review before any real-world use, and keep outputs clearly labeled as reference material rather than advice.

Risk: Users could enter real customer identifiers, account details, holdings, or original risk-assessment records while drafting scenarios.

Mitigation: Use only anonymized or synthetic customer profiles and exclude personal, account, holding, and risk-assessment source data.

Risk: Regulatory summaries or suitability logic could be outdated or incomplete for a specific jurisdiction or institution.

Mitigation: Verify regulatory statements against official sources and apply the institution's approved suitability controls before operational use.
