## Description: <br>
Provides a six-stage product-management workflow for planning, documenting, reviewing, building, testing, and improving SaaS products for video translation, subtitles, and multi-platform content distribution. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jivaklong](https://clawhub.ai/user/jivaklong) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, developers, QA teams, and content operations teams use this skill to draft PRDs, EARS requirements, review agendas, engineering task breakdowns, test reports, acceptance checklists, and launch retrospectives for compliant video automation products. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A planned product could acquire, translate, repost, or automatically publish third-party content without authorization. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm rights-holder authorization and platform ToS/API support before implementation, and treat compliance gates as mandatory blockers before publishing workflows are built. <br>
Risk: Teams may treat the workflow as an implementation plan for scraping or auto-publishing even though the release is a documentation template. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only to create product-management artifacts, and require legal and platform-policy review before any executable acquisition or publishing system is designed. <br>
