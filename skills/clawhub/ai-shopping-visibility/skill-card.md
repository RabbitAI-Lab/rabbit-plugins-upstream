## Description:

Get products shown, with the right price and merchant, in ChatGPT shopping, Google AI Mode and Gemini, and Perplexity. Use for "why doesn't ChatGPT show our products", "AI shows the wrong price" or shopping AEO, LLM SEO and GEO work. Checks the routes in, product feeds, product pages and third-party reviews, then runs a shopping prompt set.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
