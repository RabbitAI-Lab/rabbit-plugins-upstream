## Description:

Provides cloud-assisted clinical-trial design, methodology, statistics, safety, compliance, and regulatory guidance, including analysis of attached documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[medstatstar](https://clawhub.ai/user/medstatstar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Clinical-trial practitioners, clinicians, and students use this skill to explore trial design, methodology, safety, regulatory questions, and related evidence; clinical or regulatory conclusions require independent verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Clinical-trial questions and attached documents are forwarded to the Coze cloud service and may disclose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use only when external processing is acceptable; do not attach confidential protocols, subject-level data, sponsor-sensitive documents, or regulated personal data without authorization.

Risk: Local cache files may expose interaction information on shared devices.

Mitigation: Clear local cache files when shared-device privacy matters.

Risk: Another local process could impersonate or call the resident loopback service.

Mitigation: Run the skill only in a trusted local environment and restrict untrusted local processes.
