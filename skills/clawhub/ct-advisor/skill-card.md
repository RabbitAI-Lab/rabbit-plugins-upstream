## Description:

A cloud-assisted advisor for clinical-trial methodology, design, compliance, quality control, site operations, and research intelligence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[medstatstar](https://clawhub.ai/user/medstatstar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Clinical-development teams and researchers use this skill to ask questions about trial methodology, design, statistics, regulatory compliance, safety, quality control, and related evidence, with optional document attachments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Clinical-trial questions and attached documents are sent to Coze cloud services.

Mitigation: Use only where that cloud processing is approved; do not attach patient-level data, protocols, clinical study reports, or confidential drafts without authorization.

Risk: A stable device-derived identifier is shared with the cloud, and local persistence and sibling-skill execution may exceed expected privacy boundaries.

Mitigation: Review local data retention and sibling-skill behavior before installation or long-running use.
