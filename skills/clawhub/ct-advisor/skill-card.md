## Description:

A cloud-assisted clinical trial advisor that helps answer questions about trial methodology, study design, compliance, quality control, and site operations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[medstatstar](https://clawhub.ai/user/medstatstar)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Clinical development teams and trial researchers use this skill for guidance on trial design, methodology, regulatory questions, quality control, and related research questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions and attached documents can be sent to remote services, exposing confidential trial, patient, or sponsor information.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential protocols, patient-level data, sponsor-sensitive material, or regulated attachments unless their upload to Coze is explicitly authorized.

Risk: Privacy and local persistence disclosures conflict, so users may misunderstand what data remains on their device or is transmitted.

Mitigation: Review endpoint whitelists, local memory settings, and installed sibling skills before use; confirm data-handling requirements with the organization.
