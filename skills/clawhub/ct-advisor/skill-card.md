## Description:

Provides cloud-assisted clinical-trial methodology and regulatory guidance, with optional handoffs to related ct-series skills for data retrieval and analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Clinical-trial practitioners, clinicians, and learners use the advisor for methodology, regulatory, study-design, operations, and deliverable guidance. It can direct data-retrieval tasks to related ct-series skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan rated this release suspicious with high confidence and reported incomplete coverage, sensitive-data concerns, and broad local file and network capabilities.

Mitigation: Review the scan guidance and the skill's data-flow and local-action disclosures before installation; limit access and monitor file and network activity.

Risk: Prompts and attached documents may contain confidential clinical-trial information, and the skill has reported local retention and additional network destinations.

Mitigation: Keep patient-level, sponsor-confidential, and unpublished trial data out of prompts and attachments unless the applicable data controls explicitly allow their use; review endpoints and local retention before enabling the skill.
