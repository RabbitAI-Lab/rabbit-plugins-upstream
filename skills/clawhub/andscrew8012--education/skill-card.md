### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Student analytics, auto-grading, engagement scores, and at-risk labels could affect academic, disciplinary, or support decisions if used without oversight. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review and do not use automated outputs as the sole basis for academic, disciplinary, or support decisions. <br>
Risk: Student data handling may create privacy, consent, access-control, and retention obligations. <br>
Mitigation: Use consent, access controls, retention limits, data minimization, and encryption before deployment. <br>
Risk: Suggested swarm or memory-sharing companion skills may expand data sharing or orchestration behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review each companion skill separately before installing it or enabling shared memory or orchestration. <br>
