## Description:

Helps users find and vet public TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube creators through profile, audience, engagement, and business-contact research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketers and brand teams use this skill to find, compare, and vet creators for partnerships and UGC sourcing using public social-platform data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid creator-data lookups can consume credit, particularly bulk searches, contact-email calls, and audience-demographic calls.

Mitigation: Check current tool pricing and ask for approval before higher-cost calls or bulk lookups.

Risk: Business contact details may be used outside the creator outreach the user requested.

Mitigation: Retrieve published business contacts only for user-requested outreach.
