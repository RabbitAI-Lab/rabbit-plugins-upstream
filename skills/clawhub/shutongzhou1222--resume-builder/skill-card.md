## Description: <br>
Resume Builder is a conversational Chinese resume assistant that collects information step by step, helps draft or improve resumes, and tailors the result to the target role. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External job seekers and early-career candidates use this skill to build a resume from scratch or improve an existing resume through a guided, one-question-at-a-time conversation. The skill is especially aimed at users who need help turning plain experience descriptions into role-specific resume language. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share real contact details such as phone numbers or email addresses while drafting a resume. <br>
Mitigation: Use placeholders or omit contact details until the resume is ready to finalize, then review the final document before sharing it. <br>
Risk: Generated resume language may overstate experience if the user's source details are incomplete or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Review each bullet against the user's actual experience and revise claims before submitting the resume. <br>
