## Description:

Turns customer interview notes into a structured case study with a challenge, a solution, real numbers and an approved quote.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

US B2B marketers and sales teams use this skill to turn customer interview notes into customer case studies with sourced results and approved quotes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer claims, metrics, or quotes may be published without adequate approval or substantiation.

Mitigation: Before publication, confirm a written release, public-use approval for each metric, a source for every number, and approval of the exact quote; do not present an individual result as typical without representative-data confirmation.

Risk: Healthcare case studies could expose protected health information.

Mitigation: Exclude protected health information and verify permissions before using healthcare customer material.

Risk: The optional Windows application is outside the security assessment for the bundled workflow.

Mitigation: Use the bundled workflow without the optional application, or evaluate that application separately before use.
