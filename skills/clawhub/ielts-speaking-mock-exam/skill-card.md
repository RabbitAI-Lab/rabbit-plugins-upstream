## Description:

Provides a full IELTS Speaking Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 mock exam flow, then gives estimated band scoring, corrections, and improvement advice after the user ends the exam with "That's all".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

IELTS candidates use this skill to rehearse a realistic speaking exam sequence with an AI examiner and receive a non-official estimated band score, targeted error correction, and practical preparation advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Practice answers may include spoken or typed personal information.

Mitigation: Use only the information needed for exam practice and review generated feedback before relying on it.

Risk: Estimated IELTS band scores and corrections may be imperfect or differ from an official examiner's assessment.

Mitigation: Treat scores as non-official practice feedback and verify high-stakes preparation decisions with qualified IELTS resources or instructors.
