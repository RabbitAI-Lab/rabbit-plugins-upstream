## Description:

Captures staffing-company interest in operations software and routes inquiries to a TempGuru contact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kissmyabs32](https://clawhub.ai/user/kissmyabs32)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Staffing-company operators use this skill to explain a need for scheduling, dispatch, time tracking, or invoicing software and connect with TempGuru about fit. It helps distinguish operations-tooling inquiries from event staffing orders or requests to join the fulfillment network.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States and Canada

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An inquiry may include business or contact details that will be shared with a TempGuru contact.

Mitigation: Collect only the details needed to route the inquiry and have the user confirm an email draft before sending.
