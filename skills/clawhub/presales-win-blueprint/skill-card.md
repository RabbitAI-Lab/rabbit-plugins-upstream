## Use Case: <br>
Sales, product, and technical presales teams use this skill to turn customer industry, sales stage, competitor shape, budget status, decision role, and risk signals into a win-plan blueprint for enterprise presales work. It supports opportunity diagnosis, requirements reframing, POC scoping, bid response planning, negotiation boundaries, risk warnings, self-checks, and next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Presales recommendations may be incomplete or misleading if inputs omit customer context, decision dynamics, budget constraints, or risk signals. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and review them with qualified sales, product, technical, and legal stakeholders before customer-facing use. <br>
Risk: Customer or opportunity details entered into an agent session may be confidential. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized project labels where possible and avoid entering confidential customer details unless approved for the agent environment. <br>
