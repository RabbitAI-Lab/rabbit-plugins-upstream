## Description: <br>
Generates Bilibili-style video titles, description templates, and tag recommendations for individual creators preparing uploads. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators use this skill to draft Bilibili upload copy, including titles, descriptions, and tag sets from a video topic. It is intended for content ideation and formatting, not technical documentation, academic writing, or legal drafting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests shell execution even though its core behavior is Markdown-based content generation. <br>
Mitigation: Install only after removing exec access or restricting use to Bilibili-specific, user-directed content tasks. <br>
Risk: Generated titles, descriptions, or tags may be inaccurate, misleading, or overly promotional. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated copy before publishing and edit it for accuracy, platform policy, and creator intent. <br>
