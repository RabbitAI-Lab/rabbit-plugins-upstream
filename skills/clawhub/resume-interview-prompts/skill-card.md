## Description:

Interview for one job, then draft a resume from confirmed numbers only, in plain words, with any job and any AI agent. Your AI agent asks one question at a time about skills, strengths, and experience for one job description, checks every number with you, then drafts a one page resume for owner review. No invented numbers, no sent resume, draft only.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to prepare for one specific job application. It interviews them about their experience, checks which claims and numbers they confirm, and produces a plain-language resume draft for their review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested answers could introduce personal claims that the user has not confirmed.

Mitigation: Label suggestions as Suggested, not confirmed; include a claim in the resume only after the user confirms it, and otherwise leave it out.
