Interview for One Job, Then Draft a Resume From Confirmed Numbers Only, in Plain Words

Give your AI agent one job description. Answer one question at a time about skills, strengths, and experience for that job. Get a one page resume draft, made from your real answers only, ready to review.

Most resumes start with a blank page. People copy old lines, guess at numbers, or use fancy words that do not sound like them. This skill interviews you first, one question at a time, then drafts a resume that uses only the answers you confirmed, with a humanize pass in plain words.

## What you get

- An interview for one job, 12 questions, one at a time: job, contact, last job, day to day work, top 3 skills, strengths, 3 results with numbers, education, tools, and what is missing
- A numbers check, so every number in the resume was confirmed by you, or it is removed
- A job match check, so each skill has evidence, or it is marked missing
- A humanize pass, in plain words, in your own voice
- A resume draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: summary, top skills, experience, education, tools, numbers confirmed, and what is missing
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## Who this is for

People applying for a job, who want a clear resume in plain words, for any job. People changing jobs, who need to show what they really did, with real numbers. People who want help to write, but want every word checked before it is used.

## Why this saves money

A clear resume saves time for the person who applies, and for the recruiter who reads it. A short interview stops the writer from guessing, adding a number that is not real, or using words that do not match the job.

## Technical, for coders

Structured interview and draft resume for one job description: interview questions, a numbers confirmation check, a job match check, a humanize pass, and a one page resume draft format, ready to brief any AI agent, or a human writer. No guarantee of an interview, a job, or a reply, and no claim that a draft alone gets a job. The draft is for owner review, not a sent application.

## The 3 steps

1. Interview: your AI agent asks 12 questions, one at a time, for one job description only.
2. Quantify, honestly: for every result, what is the number, how was it measured, and who can confirm it? A number is real only if you confirm it.
3. Humanize: rewrite the draft in plain words, in your own voice, short sentences, simple words, no fancy words, no repeating the job description word for word.

It also gives separate notes: dates, contact, spelling, gaps in work, and length, one page aim.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent one job description, in plain words, and your old resume if you have one.
3. Answer, in plain words: what job, what did you do, what are your top skills, what are your strengths, what results with numbers, what education, what tools?
4. Ask: "Interview me for this job with resume-interview-prompts."
5. Read the numbers check first. Confirm every number, or remove it.
6. Read the draft. Approve the summary, the skills, and every number first, before the resume is sent anywhere.

If you already give the job description, an old resume, and numbers up front, the skill asks only the missing questions, then does the full draft in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not send a resume, apply for a job, contact a recruiter, or publish anything by itself.
- It will not invent a number, a job, a date, an employer, a skill, a result, a certificate, or a testimonial.
- It will not use a number unless you confirmed it, and how it was measured.
- A resume draft is not a promise of an interview, a job, or a reply.

## How to install it

Works with any AI agent that can read a SKILL.md file, including Claude, Copilot, Cursor, Gemini, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Hermes, OpenClaw and other AI tools.

Copy the `SKILL.md` file into your skills folder, in a folder called `resume-interview-prompts`, then restart your agent. The skill file itself is just instructions. It collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.
