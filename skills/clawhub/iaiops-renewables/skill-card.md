## Description: <br>
iaiops-renewables supports agents working on solar PV and wind-farm operations with Modbus, OPC-UA, MQTT-Sparkplug telemetry analysis, cross-protocol diagnostics, predictive maintenance, downtime, OEE, alarm workflows, and underperforming-string detection. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, operations engineers, and renewable-energy reliability teams use this skill to inspect solar PV plants and wind farms across Modbus, OPC-UA, and MQTT-Sparkplug data. It helps analyze underperforming PV strings, asset health, alarms, downtime causes, OEE, predictive maintenance signals, and compliance evidence workflows. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security summary identifies a mismatch between read-only framing and listed tools that can publish, push, export, stream, or record data in industrial systems. <br>
