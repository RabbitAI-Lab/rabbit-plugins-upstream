## Description:

Writes and saves a timed, shot-by-shot scene prompt for an idea the person has already chosen.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use this skill to turn an agreed video idea, photo references, and a chosen duration into a shot-by-shot prompt and save it as a Phantra scene.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photo references may contain personal imagery the creator does not want to share with Phantra.

Mitigation: Use only photo references the creator intends to share with the service.

Risk: Rewriting a saved scene replaces its existing prompt, and rendering uses credits.

Mitigation: Review and approve scene revisions, then approve only a rendering quote whose price the creator accepts.
