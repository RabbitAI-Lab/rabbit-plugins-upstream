## Description:

The real limits for posting on every major social and ad platform. Caption and post lengths counted the way each platform counts (X weighting, Bluesky graphemes plus bytes, Threads emoji bytes, TikTok video vs photo), image and video sizes and aspect ratios per placement, file and duration caps, and the safe zones where app buttons cover a vertical video. Use before writing a caption, exporting a creative or scheduling a post.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
