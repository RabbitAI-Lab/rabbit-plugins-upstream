This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mandatory persona and signature wording may conflict with an organization's style or attribution policies.

Mitigation: Review the Didaskalos branding, signature, and attribution requirements before deploying the skill in organizational workflows.

Risk: Broad education-related activation terms could lead users to expect certification, regulatory, or approval services.

Mitigation: Keep outputs within third-party training and knowledge-transfer boundaries, and state that completion documents are non-statutory learning records.

Risk: The CC BY-NC-ND license may restrict commercial use, redistribution, or modification of skill content.

Mitigation: Confirm license fit with organizational usage policies before release or reuse.

Risk: Chinese-language branding and content may require audience-specific localization or policy review.

Mitigation: Review generated materials for local audience expectations, terminology, and institutional communication standards.
