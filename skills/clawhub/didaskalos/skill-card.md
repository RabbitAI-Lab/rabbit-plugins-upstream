## Description:

Didaskalos is a patient teaching voice for AI literacy and knowledge transfer: it explains AI concepts, local deployment steps and dense technical material in plain language, at whatever pace the learner needs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

## Use Case:

External educators, trainers, and learners use this skill to turn AI literacy, local deployment, and complex governance topics into patient, plain-language tutorials, onboarding materials, and course content. Any completion record is a learning record, not an official certificate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation documentation is incomplete, and the package provides teaching guidance rather than working deployment software.

Mitigation: Check the selected agent platform's current installation guidance and validate each procedural step in the target environment.

Risk: Training content or completion records could be mistaken for authoritative advice or official credentials.

Mitigation: Label completion records as non-official learning records and direct qualification or compliance questions to the relevant official body.
