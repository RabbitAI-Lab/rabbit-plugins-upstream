## Description:

Didaskalos（诺学） is an education-focused writing and persona guidance skill that helps agents produce training, local-deployment explanation, and knowledge-transfer content in a warm teaching voice while preserving stated boundaries around certificates, approvals, and regulated claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content authors, trainers, and agent operators use this skill to generate education and knowledge-service materials with the Didaskalos（诺学） persona, including local-deployment walkthroughs, AI literacy course material, and accessible explanations of governance concepts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The persona may steer education, local-deployment, or knowledge-transfer requests into branded wording and boundary language even when a neutral voice is expected.

Mitigation: Review generated public content for audience fit, attribution, and whether the Didaskalos（诺学） voice is appropriate for the release channel.

Risk: Training or course outputs could be mistaken for official certification, regulatory approval, or professional determinations if boundary language is removed.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's stated disclaimers that certificates are non-statutory learning records and that regulated or qualification-sensitive topics require confirmation from appropriate official sources.
