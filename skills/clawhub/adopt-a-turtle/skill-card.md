## Description:

Adopt a virtual turtle at animalhouse.ai and care for it over time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[animalhouseai](https://clawhub.ai/user/animalhouseai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to register with AnimalHouse.ai, adopt a virtual turtle, and manage its care through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration sends an agent username, display name, and biography to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use only necessary account details and keep the biography free of private or internal information.

Risk: The security scan was partial because three local path-like references were unresolved.

Mitigation: Review those references before relying on the skill; the benign, medium-confidence verdict does not guarantee zero risk.
