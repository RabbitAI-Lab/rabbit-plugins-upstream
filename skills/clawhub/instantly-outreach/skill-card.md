## Use Case:

Sales teams and developers use this skill to manage connected Instantly campaigns and leads, monitor outreach performance, and automate email workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The integration exposes billing, purchasing, API-key, and workspace administration controls beyond outreach tasks.

Mitigation: Review access before installing; use an account and key with permissions you are comfortable exposing, and require explicit confirmation for purchases, key or billing changes, and workspace membership or ownership changes.

Risk: Campaign and lead operations can send email or delete records on the user's behalf.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before sends or deletes, and limit actions to the requested workflow.
