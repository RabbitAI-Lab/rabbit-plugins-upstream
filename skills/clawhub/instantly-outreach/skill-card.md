## Description:

Manage cold email campaigns, leads, and outreach analytics via Instantly AI through the superagnt unified API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Sales and operations teams can use the skill to manage Instantly campaigns, leads, sending accounts, and outreach analytics through their connected account. It also documents account administration actions, which should be used only when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outbound email, destructive changes, purchases, or account administration can affect the connected workspace.

Mitigation: Stay within the user's request and require confirmation before sending messages, deleting or overwriting data, making purchases, or changing account settings; review recipients and changes before confirming.

Risk: Credentials or returned passwords could expose account access.

Mitigation: Keep API keys and returned passwords private, and use limited permissions where available.
