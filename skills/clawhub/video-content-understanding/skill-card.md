## Description:

Analyzes video with multimodal models to provide summaries, timestamped timelines, shot and mood analysis, visual facts, and on-screen text recognition.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and analysts use this skill to summarize videos, extract timelines and on-screen text, and examine shots and mood before producing reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
