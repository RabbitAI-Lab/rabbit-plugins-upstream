## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to find brake-service providers, check appointment availability, and create bookings through Lokuli. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booking requests share appointment contact details with Lokuli. <br>
Mitigation: Before creating an appointment, review the provider, time slot, and contact information and confirm the user intends to share those details. <br>
