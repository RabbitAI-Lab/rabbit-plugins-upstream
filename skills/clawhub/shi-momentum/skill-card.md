## Description:

Guides agents through a five-phase strategic momentum model to assess readiness, focus next actions, and avoid premature scaling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, strategists, and teams use it to diagnose an initiative’s momentum phase, set evidence-based exit criteria, and choose stage-appropriate actions when entering a market, reassessing after a pivot, or considering growth.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
