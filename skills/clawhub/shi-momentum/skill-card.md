## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a five-phase strategic momentum model for diagnosing whether an initiative is ready to build capabilities, partner, ride a market wave, focus, or amplify. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, founders, operators, and strategy teams use this skill to diagnose why momentum is not building, decide whether an initiative is ready to scale, and produce a Momentum Phase Map with observable evidence, exit criteria, macro-wave signals, and premature-amplification warnings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may influence business strategy discussions and could be mistaken for authoritative advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as decision-support only, require observable evidence for each gate, and have accountable stakeholders review recommendations before acting. <br>
