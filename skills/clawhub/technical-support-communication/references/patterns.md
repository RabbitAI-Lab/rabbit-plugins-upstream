# Practice Patterns — The Compassionate Geek

Use these for self-assessment and repeated practice. Each pattern ends with an observable action.

## Competence Level Self-Assessment

**When to use**: Career planning, performance reviews, or personal development.

**How**: Read the competence model in `frameworks.md`. Compare current behavior with the next level, choose one observable learning habit, and set a review date.

**Complete when**: One current behavior, one target behavior, and one practice commitment are named without treating the model as a career guarantee.

## Focus Diagnostic

**When to use**: During or immediately before a tense interaction.

**How**: Ask, "Is my attention on understanding the other person's meaning, or on preparing my defense?" Reset by summarizing their concern before responding.

**Complete when**: The concern and impact can be stated neutrally in one sentence.

## "Like Me" Reflection

**When to use**: When judgment or irritation is blocking curiosity.

**How**: Identify one shared human need relevant to the interaction, such as clarity, safety, time, dignity, or progress. Use it to choose respectful wording without assuming identical feelings.

**Complete when**: The shared need changes one concrete sentence or action.

## End-of-Interaction Recap

**When to use**: Concluding a support call, handoff, incident discussion, or difficult conversation.

**How**: Recap the issue, agreed action, owner, required follow-up, and known timing. Mark unknown timing as unknown and state how the next update will be determined.

**Complete when**: Every participant can tell what happens next and who owns it.

## Morning Compassion Intention

**When to use**: At the start and end of a workday.

**How**: Choose one interaction in which to slow down, notice impact, and pair concern with useful action. Review whether that action happened at the end of the day.

**Complete when**: One intended behavior and one observed result are recorded.
