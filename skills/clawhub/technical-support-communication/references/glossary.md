# Glossary — The Compassionate Geek

**Attentive Listening** — Carefully listening but evaluating and preparing your response while the speaker talks. Self-focused. (Ch 5)

**Charisma** — The ability to understand, get along with, and influence people. Maps to the people-skills axis. (Ch 1)

**Competence** — The ability to perform the core aspects of your job. Maps to the technical-skills axis. (Ch 1)

**Competence/Charisma Four-Quadrant Model** — A self-assessment tool mapping professionals on technical competence and people charisma axes. (Ch 1)

**Compassion** — Combining a profound awareness of another's suffering with a desire to alleviate it. (Ch 3)

**Cortisol** — The "stress hormone"; elevated levels impair cognitive performance, decrease bone density, and lower immunity. (Ch 3)

**Deep Technical Competence** — Technical ability deep enough to solve problems quickly, permanently, and proactively prevent future problems. (Ch 2)

**DHEA** — Hormone associated with countering aging effects; increases with positive emotions and compassion. (Ch 3)

**EIQ (Emotional Intelligence Quotient)** — People skills; the best predictor of long-term career success (Daniel Goleman). (Ch 1)

**Empathic Listening** — The highest level of listening; treating every interaction as if hearing the problem for the first time, fully focused on the speaker. (Ch 5)

**Empathy** — The ability to put yourself in another's position and feel what they are feeling. (Ch 4)

**Five Levels of Listening** — Ignoring → Pretend Listening → Selective Listening → Attentive Listening → Empathic Listening. (Ch 5)

**Human Moment (Dr. Edward Hallowell)** — A moment when two people fully connect — turn off devices, give full attention, do it in person. (Ch 4)

**IQ (Intelligence Quotient)** — Raw cognitive ability; gets you the interview, but EIQ determines career trajectory. (Ch 1)

**Positive Empathy** — Sharing in another person's positive emotions; being excited for them. (Ch 6)

**Respect (Internal)** — How you feel about someone; earned through their words and deeds. (Ch 6)

**Respect (External)** — How you behave toward someone; always within your control regardless of feelings. (Ch 6)

**Scripted Compassion** — Memorized customer service phrases without genuine feeling; perceived as hollow and phony. (Ch 3)

**The Good Samaritan Experiment (Princeton, 1973)** — Students in the greatest hurry were least likely to stop and help; proves hurry kills compassion. (Ch 3)

**Warmth** — Related to people skills; assessed by "Can I trust this person?" (Amy Cuddy). (Ch 1)
