## Description:

Builds week-by-week U.S. parental leave plans combining FMLA eligibility, state paid leave, employer disability benefits, PTO, estimated pay, and job-protection gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Expectant, adoptive, and non-birthing parents in the United States use the planner to estimate leave pay and protection, compare state programs, and prepare questions for HR.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Leave-pay and job-protection calculations contain reproducible errors.

Mitigation: Use the output as a rough worksheet and confirm leave dates, eligibility, benefit overlap, and payment totals with HR and the relevant state program before scheduling leave or budgeting.
