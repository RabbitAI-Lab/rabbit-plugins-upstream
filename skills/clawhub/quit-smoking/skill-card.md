## Use Case: <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Health and recovery information may be mistaken for medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as supportive behavior-change guidance and consult a qualified clinician for medical concerns, cessation medication, or treatment decisions. <br>
Risk: Quit progress, craving notes, and related details may be remembered locally by the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only if local memory of quit-smoking progress is acceptable, and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details. <br>
