## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions are processed through Finch and Powerdrill, so confidential financial or personal information may be exposed to those services.

Mitigation: Do not include confidential financial or personal information; review the external setup instructions before connecting Finch.

Risk: A paid agent run incurs a charge.

Mitigation: Use free runs unless the user explicitly approves a paid run.
