## Description:

Structures analytical questions into driver trees, testable hypotheses, evidence assessments, and answers that state key uncertainty.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ysskrishna](https://clawhub.ai/user/ysskrishna)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other users investigating metrics, comparing concrete alternatives, or breaking down complex questions use this skill to organize hypotheses, assess available evidence, and identify the next useful data to collect.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Evidence gathering could expose sensitive business metrics, logs, or dashboards beyond the user's intended scope.

Mitigation: Limit access to data sources authorized for the specific question.

Risk: An analysis without real data could present assumed inputs as observed facts.

Mitigation: Label guessed inputs as estimates, show ranges, and state when the answer cannot be determined from available evidence.
