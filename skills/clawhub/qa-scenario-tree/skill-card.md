## Description:

Helps QA practitioners turn decomposed requirements into scenario trees covering happy paths, alternative paths, exception paths, and business/data-flow rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, testers, and product teams use this skill after requirement decomposition to design traceable test scenarios for complex workflows, page transitions, state changes, and abnormal paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: QA examples or source requirements may contain customer, payment, identity, or financial data.

Mitigation: Use masked or synthetic requirements and avoid pasting real production data into prompts or examples.

Risk: Generated scenario coverage may be mistaken for complete coverage when input requirements are incomplete.

Mitigation: Label the coverage basis, avoid absolute 100% coverage claims, and mark missing modules with the reason they are not covered.
