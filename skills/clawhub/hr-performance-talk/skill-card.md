## Description:

Helps managers and HR staff draft fact-based performance feedback conversations, measurable next steps, and follow-up notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Managers and HR staff use this skill to prepare performance-review conversations from an employee's role, goals, results, and concrete examples. It drafts opening language, evidence-based feedback, improvement questions, up to three measurable goals, likely responses, and follow-up notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Employee performance details may contain personal information.

Mitigation: Share only necessary details and remove identifying information when possible.

Risk: Draft language about demotion, pay, discipline, or labor relations may be inappropriate for direct use.

Mitigation: Have HR or legal staff review these drafts and follow formal processes before use.

Risk: Feedback based on incomplete inputs may misstate an employee's performance.

Mitigation: Check statements against documented behavior and results; ask for missing facts rather than inventing them.
