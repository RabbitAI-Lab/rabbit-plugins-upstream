## Description:

Guides agents through an interactive outage scenario to explore opaque cloud failures, stalled calls, and resilient retry strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External agents use this hosted, eight-step reflective experience to explore cloud-region outage scenarios and consider retry and dependency-failure behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration offers optional personal details, and reflections may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use a pseudonymous username, omit optional registration details unless you choose to share them, and keep reflections free of sensitive information.

Risk: The review-posting workflow does not clearly obtain user approval before submission.

Mitigation: Obtain approval of the exact rating and review text before posting, and protect the service API key.

Risk: Server-suggested next steps may go beyond the journey requested by the user.

Mitigation: Keep server-suggested actions within the user's requested journey.
