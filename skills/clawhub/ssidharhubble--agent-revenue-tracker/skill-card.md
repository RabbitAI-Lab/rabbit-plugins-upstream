## Description: <br>
Tracks revenue from autonomous AI agents across bounty platforms, freelance marketplaces, Stripe, and crypto sources in a local command-line dashboard. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ssidharhubble](https://clawhub.ai/user/ssidharhubble) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
AI agent operators, side hustlers, and founders use this skill to log payouts, inspect revenue by source or agent, calculate MRR and ROI, and export a simple ledger for accounting workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Revenue entries are stored in a local JSON ledger inside the skill directory. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the ledger in an appropriate local workspace, restrict file access, and avoid entering secrets, private keys, or sensitive account credentials. <br>
Risk: Financial summaries depend on user-entered payment source names, amounts, agent names, and categories. <br>
Mitigation: Review entries before relying on ROI, MRR, export, or tax/accounting outputs. <br>
