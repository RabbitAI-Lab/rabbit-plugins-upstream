## Description:

Guides agents in creating a single cinematic video clip with generated audio from text or first and last frames through Pruna's p-video-2-pro model.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to plan and request one short cinematic clip, including product shots, documentary footage, or dialogue with generated audio. It supports text prompts and first or last frame images, but not imported audio or 1080p output.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing mutable helper skills may introduce unreviewed instructions.

Mitigation: Review helper skills before installation and prefer pinned or pre-reviewed versions.

Risk: Prompts and uploaded images are sent to Pruna's API.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential prompts or private media unless the account's data-handling terms meet your needs.

Risk: Video generation may incur charges for returned video duration.

Mitigation: Confirm the prompt, duration, resolution, and generation mode before submitting a request.
