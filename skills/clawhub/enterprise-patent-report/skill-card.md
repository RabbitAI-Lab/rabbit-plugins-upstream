## Description:

Generates evidence-backed HTML patent strategy reports for enterprise executives using PatSnap patent and literature searches, covering company context, competitiveness, competition, market opportunity, risks, strategy, and PatSnap solution recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External enterprise executives, strategy teams, and IP teams use this skill to produce patent strategy research reports that bind core claims to patent, literature, policy, or attachment evidence and mark unsupported figures as Unverified.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated patent, market, lawsuit, price, or performance claims may be incorrect, outdated, or insufficiently supported.

Mitigation: Review every verified or Unverified claim against the cited patent, literature, policy, or attachment evidence before sharing the report externally.

Risk: Using the skill may submit confidential company, competitor, product, or service-plan materials to the configured PatSnap/MCP account.

Mitigation: Confirm the PatSnap/MCP account, authorization state, and data-sharing comfort level before uploading business attachments or running patent searches.
