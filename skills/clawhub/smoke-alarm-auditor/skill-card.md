## Description:

Helps interpret smoke and carbon monoxide alarm patterns and plan a home alarm audit, replacement schedule, coverage, and placement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners, renters, and their assistants use this skill to interpret alarm chirps, inventory smoke and CO alarms, identify units due for replacement, and plan residential coverage and placement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An active smoke or CO alarm may signal an immediate emergency.

Mitigation: Evacuate or seek emergency help first; do not rely on the skill to assess an active alarm.

Risk: Alarm patterns and placement requirements vary by device and jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Confirm patterns against the device label and check applicable local code before acting on placement advice.
