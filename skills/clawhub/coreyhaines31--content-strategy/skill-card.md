## Description: <br>
Helps agents plan content strategies, content pillars, topic clusters, editorial calendars, and prioritized content ideas for marketing goals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, content strategists, and developers use this skill to decide what content to create, how to organize it into pillars and topic clusters, and how to prioritize work by customer impact, content-market fit, search potential, and resource requirements. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use existing product-marketing context files when present, so unrelated sensitive material in those files could influence content strategy work. <br>
Mitigation: Keep only intended business context in `.agents/product-marketing.md`, `.claude/product-marketing.md`, or legacy `product-marketing-context.md` files. <br>
