## Description:

Use only when explicitly invoked. Model systems with Li Yunhua's 4R method and create separate Mermaid diagrams for Rank, Role, Relation, and Rule, with templates and checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticweb4](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticweb4)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and architects use this skill to model a system's scope, responsibilities, relationships, and collaboration rules as a consistent set of Mermaid architecture diagrams.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Diagrams may present unsupported structures or collaboration rules as confirmed design.

Mitigation: Review diagrams for accuracy and leave unsupported structures and rules unconfirmed.
