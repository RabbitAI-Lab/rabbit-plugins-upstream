## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn Proactive Agent-style workflow needs into practical plans, checklists, artifacts, analysis, code-change support, and verification notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify productivity workflow goals, produce actionable implementation or planning artifacts, and validate the result against stated success criteria. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad implicit triggers may activate the skill during general task-planning requests. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the requested work is about productivity workflows, proactive-agent-style support, bug fixing, setup hardening, reliability, or adjacent skill creation before applying the workflow. <br>
Risk: Workflow, checklist, code-change, or shell-command guidance could be incorrect or incomplete for the user's environment. <br>
Mitigation: State assumptions, validate outputs against the user's success criteria, and ask for missing information only when it materially changes the result. <br>
