## Description:

Creates visually unified, image-based PowerPoint decks from articles, reports, papers, notes, or outlines, generating each slide image through dLazy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People preparing presentations can turn articles, reports, papers, notes, or an outline into a visually consistent PowerPoint deck, reviewing a sample slide before full generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Presentation content or supplied images may be disclosed to an external image service during generation.

Mitigation: Avoid sending confidential material unless its use with dLazy is approved, and review the service's data handling before uploading sensitive content.

Risk: The security review reports a possible API-key exposure or forwarding weakness; it does not confirm that a key was exposed.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key, keep it in protected configuration, and rotate or revoke it if exposure is suspected.
