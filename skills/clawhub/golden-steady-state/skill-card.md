## Description:

A practical framework for guiding AI, writing, research, and decision-making outputs toward a clear direction, auditable reasoning, and explicitly acknowledged open questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI agents, writers, researchers, and decision-makers can use the framework to structure ongoing work with a clear direction, auditable reasoning, and explicit notes about what remains unresolved.
