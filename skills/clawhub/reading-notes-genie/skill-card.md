## Description:

Reading Notes Genie turns a book title, PDF, or EPUB into structured reading notes with chapter summaries, quotes, reflections, knowledge cards, and Markdown, Anki, PDF, or JSON exports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Readers, students, and knowledge workers use this skill to convert books and uploaded documents into structured summaries, review cards, and exportable reading notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded PDF or EPUB text and book titles may be processed by external or user-configured AI providers.

Mitigation: Use offline mode or a trusted local endpoint for private, copyrighted, regulated, or confidential documents, and confirm provider data handling before use.

Risk: Dependencies and optional document-processing tools may change behavior across installations.

Mitigation: Pin and audit dependencies before deployment, especially for environments handling sensitive documents.
