## Description:

Writes original Chinese-oriented lyrics from a theme, mood, and musical style, with rhyme schemes, line counts, song structure, and line-level revision options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liuyuming0823](https://clawhub.ai/user/liuyuming0823)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to draft original Chinese lyrics, plan verse and chorus structures, choose rhyme schemes, mark per-line counts, and polish individual lines without copying existing songs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be too narrow for multilingual lyric writing because the skill is oriented toward Chinese pop, folk, rap, guofeng structures, and Chinese rhyme conventions.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese lyric workflows or adapt the prompt and references before applying it to multilingual songwriting.

Risk: Line counts and rhyme markings are creative drafting aids and may not fit a specific melody exactly.

Mitigation: Review the generated counts against the target melody or provide melody rhythm information for a follow-up revision.

Risk: A user may ask for copying, translation, or adaptation of existing lyrics.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's original-content constraint active and provide a same-theme original alternative instead.
