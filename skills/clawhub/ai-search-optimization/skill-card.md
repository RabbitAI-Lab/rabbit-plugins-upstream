## Description: <br>
Helps brands improve AI-search visibility by auditing buyer-intent prompts, identifying cited sources, and planning authentic GEO actions across retrieval, earned mentions, extractable content, and entity clarity. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and content strategists use this skill to diagnose whether a brand is cited by AI answer engines and to plan ethical actions that improve visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, Gemini, and related systems. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Recommendations may affect public website, crawler, analytics, community, video, review-site, or listing behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations before making public changes, especially to robots/CDN settings, analytics interpretation, Reddit, YouTube, review sites, and listings. <br>
Risk: Users may ask for fake reviews, astroturfing, or invented AI share-of-voice metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Follow the skill's guardrails: refuse fake engagement and report only results from a real prompt audit, GEO tool, or referral analytics. <br>
Risk: AI-search citation behavior and GEO tooling change quickly. <br>
Mitigation: Re-run audits regularly and re-check engine behavior, crawler access, and tool names before relying on prior recommendations. <br>
