## Description:

Helps teachers draft China-mainland middle- and high-school geography lessons centered on map reading, real-world inquiry, core competencies, and supervised field activities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Geography teachers draft and adapt lesson plans, map-reading tasks, question sequences, experiments, and school-supervised fieldwork for middle- and high-school classes. Teachers review the drafts before classroom use or saving them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, consent rules, and crisis-support contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Use for mainland Chinese geography classes unless these references are localized.

Risk: Class records and pending lesson plans may expose sensitive classroom information if platform memory or sharing is not controlled.

Mitigation: Review memory and sharing settings; use class-level or pseudonymous records and teacher confirmation before saving.

Risk: Draft maps, field activities, or AI-organized teaching materials may be inaccurate or unsafe to use without review.

Mitigation: Teachers check sources and standard maps, and arrange fieldwork through schools with adult supervision.
