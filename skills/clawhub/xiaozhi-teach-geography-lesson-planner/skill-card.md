帮助中国大陆初高中地理教师设计以核心素养、读图任务和真实情境为主线的课堂教案与地理实践活动草案。

## Use Case:

初高中地理教师用它起草新授、复习和讲评课的教案，包括单元安排、读图任务、问题链、地理实验和校外实践方案；教师审核后再用于课堂。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The curriculum, emergency referrals and privacy assumptions may not fit regions or languages outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Before using elsewhere, localize curriculum standards, emergency contacts, privacy requirements and classroom terminology.

Risk: Field trips, interviews and classroom materials may expose students to physical or privacy risks.

Mitigation: Have the school organize and supervise off-campus activities; use aggregated survey data and exclude students' identifying details.

Risk: AI-drafted maps, facts or activities may be unsuitable for classroom use without checking.

Mitigation: Teachers should check data and sources, use textbook or standard maps, and approve plans before use.
