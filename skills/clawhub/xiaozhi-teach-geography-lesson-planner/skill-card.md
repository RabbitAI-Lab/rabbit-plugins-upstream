## Description:

Helps teachers draft Mainland China middle- and high-school geography lessons centered on map reading, geographic reasoning, and practical activities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Geography teachers draft junior- and senior-secondary lesson plans, map-reading tasks, question sequences, and supervised fieldwork or classroom activity plans. Teachers review the drafts before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum references, maps, data, or exam-related material may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Teachers check lesson plans, data, maps, and exam content against current curriculum and approved materials before use.

Risk: Fieldwork or demonstrations could expose students to physical hazards or unnecessary personal-data collection.

Mitigation: Require school-organized, adult-supervised activities; follow school safety rules and use aggregated, non-identifying survey data.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, language, and referral guidance may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, legal assumptions, safety and referral channels, and language before use outside Mainland China.
