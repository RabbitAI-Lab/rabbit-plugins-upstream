## Description:

帮助初高中地理教师设计以读图、真实情境和地理核心素养为主线的教案、提问链与实践活动草案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Geography teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft junior- and senior-secondary lessons with map-reading tasks, question sequences, differentiated activities, and geography fieldwork plans. Teachers review and approve materials before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum standards, territorial wording, and crisis contacts may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Use for mainland-China geography teaching; review and localize standards, wording, contacts, and consent practices before other deployments.

Risk: AI-organized maps, data, or lesson materials may contain errors.

Mitigation: Have teachers verify sources and figures, use textbook or standard maps, and approve classroom materials before use.

Risk: Classroom and fieldwork plans could expose student information or create safety concerns.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers, avoid personal addresses and live locations, and arrange fieldwork through schools with adult supervision.
