## Description:

帮助中国初高中地理教师设计以核心素养、读图任务和真实情境为主线的教案及地理实践活动方案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中地理教师用它规划单元与课时、编排读图任务和递进问题链，并起草实验、社会调查及野外考察方案；教案记录须经教师确认。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; review curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis referrals before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson plans or surveys may expose student or respondent personal information.

Mitigation: Use class-level information, pseudonyms or seat numbers; aggregate surveys and omit names, contact details, addresses and live locations.

Risk: Inaccurate maps or unsupervised fieldwork may create classroom or safety problems.

Mitigation: Use textbook or standard maps; treat fieldwork as a draft for school-organized activities with adult supervision.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy rules and crisis referrals may not transfer outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Review and localize these materials before deployment elsewhere, and require teacher confirmation before saving records.
