## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school geography teachers draft lessons built around map reading, real-world contexts, geographic reasoning, and practical activities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Geography teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft differentiated geography lessons, review sessions, and post-exam lessons with map-reading tasks, question sequences, and supervised fieldwork or investigations. Teachers review drafts before classroom use; exam scheduling and test creation are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localization of language, curriculum, privacy guidance, and crisis contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis guidance may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize language, curriculum, privacy guidance, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: Draft lessons could include unverified geographic data or maps.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check data and sources, and use textbook or approved standard maps.

Risk: Classroom records or field investigations could expose student or interviewee information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers, collect only aggregate survey results, and keep off-campus activities school-organized and supervised.
