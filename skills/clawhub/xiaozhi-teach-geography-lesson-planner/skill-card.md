## Description:

Helps middle and high school geography teachers draft curriculum-aligned lessons built around map reading, inquiry, and practical activities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Geography teachers in middle and high schools use this skill to plan lessons, map-reading questions, and classroom or supervised field activities. Teachers review all drafts before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt the curriculum, language, and crisis-referral guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class learning analytics may expose sensitive student information.

Mitigation: Limit access to the authorized class workspace; omit real names, contact details, and home addresses, and honor privacy controls.

Risk: Draft lesson plans, maps, and teaching materials may be inaccurate or unsuitable for classroom use.

Mitigation: Have teachers verify sources, maps, and exercises before use and approve pending lesson-plan records before storage.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis-referral guidance may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language, and referral contacts before deployment outside Mainland China.
