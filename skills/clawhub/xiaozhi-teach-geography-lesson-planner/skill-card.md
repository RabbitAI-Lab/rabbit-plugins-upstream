## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking geography teachers draft map-centered lesson plans, inquiry questions, and practical activities for middle and high school.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Geography teachers use this skill to draft Chinese-language middle- and high-school lessons built around real-world contexts, map-reading tasks, question sequences, and classroom or field activities. Teachers review drafts before using them or saving class records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland (curriculum and safety guidance require localization elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated lesson content or class-record entries may contain inaccuracies.

Mitigation: Teachers should verify content, source data, and proposed records before classroom use or saving.

Risk: Student names, addresses, or other personal information could be included in lesson-planning inputs.

Mitigation: Avoid entering real student names or addresses; use class-level or de-identified information.

Risk: Curriculum, safety contacts, and data rules are tailored to Chinese-language China-mainland use.

Mitigation: Localize those requirements before use in another region or language context.
