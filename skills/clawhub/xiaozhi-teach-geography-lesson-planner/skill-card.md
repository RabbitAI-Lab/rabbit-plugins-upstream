## Description:

Helps junior and senior high school geography teachers draft curriculum-aligned lessons built around map reading, inquiry questions, and practical activities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Geography teachers in mainland China's junior and senior high schools use this skill to draft lesson plans, map-reading tasks, question sequences, and supervised practical activities. Teachers review and approve materials before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local curriculum and crisis-help assumptions may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt curriculum, language, and crisis-referral channels before use elsewhere.

Risk: Classroom records and field activities could expose student information or create safety concerns.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers instead of personal details; have schools organize fieldwork with adult supervision.

Risk: AI-drafted teaching materials may contain inaccurate data or map content.

Mitigation: Teachers check facts and sources, use textbook or standard maps, and approve drafts before use.
