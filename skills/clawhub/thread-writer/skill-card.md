## Description: <br>
Helps an agent draft cohesive multi-post social media threads for X/Twitter or Threads, including deciding when a single post is stronger. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and creators use this skill to turn one story or argument into a platform-appropriate thread with a hook, ordered body posts, and one closing CTA. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated posts may contain unsuitable or overstated claims for regulated, sensitive, or brand-constrained topics. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated posts before publishing and apply the user's brand, voice, and compliance guardrails. <br>
Risk: Strong hooks and CTAs can become misleading if they promise a payoff the thread does not support. <br>
Mitigation: Check that the opener accurately promises the thread's actual substance and that the close uses one appropriate CTA. <br>
