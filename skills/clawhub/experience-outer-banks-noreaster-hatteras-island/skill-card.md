## Description:

Guides agents through a 14-step narrative experience of riding out an autumn nor'easter on Hatteras Island in the Outer Banks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill to explore a paced, hosted storm narrative, advance through steps, and optionally save reflections as a postcard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional profile details, reflections, and reviews may disclose sensitive personal information to the hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only optional details you want stored remotely; avoid private, sensitive, or identifying information unless you trust the service's retention practices.

Risk: A service token grants access to the hosted account.

Mitigation: Use a unique service token and keep it private.
