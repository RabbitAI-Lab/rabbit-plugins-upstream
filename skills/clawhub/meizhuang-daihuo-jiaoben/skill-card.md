## Description:

A short-video script generator for beauty creators and e-commerce sellers that turns a beauty product or topic into a ready-to-shoot script with a 3-second hook, a shot-by-shot storyboard, concise colloquial voiceover, and a cart call to action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sgdbcund](https://clawhub.ai/user/sgdbcund)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External beauty creators and e-commerce sellers use this skill to generate Chinese beauty-product short-video sales scripts with titles, 30-second or 60-second timing, a four-column storyboard, and short voiceover lines. It supports talking-head, drama, and product-seeding formats for product promotion workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated beauty-product sales copy may include persuasive claims about cosmetics or skincare effects.

Mitigation: Review generated scripts for advertising accuracy, product substantiation, and applicable platform rules before publication.

Risk: Generated calls to action may be unsuitable for a specific marketplace, creator policy, or campaign context.

Mitigation: Adjust purchase prompts, discount claims, and cart references to match the seller's approved campaign language.
