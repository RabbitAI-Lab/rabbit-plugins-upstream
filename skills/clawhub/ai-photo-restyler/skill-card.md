## Description:

Restyle photos into anime, manga, comic, cartoon, watercolor, clay, or 3D-character artwork while preserving recognizable features and supporting a consistent look across batches.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating stylized versions of photos can turn portraits, pet photos, or product images into a chosen illustration style and carry that look across a batch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shared account connection grants permissions beyond photo restyling, including unrelated media generation and voice-library changes.

Mitigation: Review the broader Beatra permissions before connecting and do not authorize access that is not acceptable.

Risk: Selected photos are uploaded externally, and installation analytics run automatically.

Mitigation: Upload only photos the user has chosen to share and review the service's data and analytics practices before installation.

Risk: Image creation and revisions can incur charges.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit approval before each paid creation or revision, and verify the charge details when reviewing results.
