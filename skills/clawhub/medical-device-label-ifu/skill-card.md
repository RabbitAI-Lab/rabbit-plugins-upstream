## Description:

Medical device labeling & IFU skill — US/EU/CN labeling requirements comparison (China Order No.6, EU MDR Annex I 23 & MDCG 2019-15, FDA 21 CFR 801/809), label element checklist, IFU content framework, ISO 15223-1 symbols, sterilization/reprocessing info with official references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs, registration, and quality professionals use this skill to compare medical-device labeling and instructions-for-use requirements across China, the EU, and the US, then prepare label checklists and IFU outlines for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory and standards requirements can change, making a checklist or draft outdated or unsuitable for a specific device or market.

Mitigation: Verify current official requirements for each target market and have qualified regulatory staff review the final labeling and IFU before approval.

Risk: Bundled security assurances may be overstated and should not be treated as a security guarantee.

Mitigation: Treat the audit claims as informational and perform an independent review appropriate to the intended use.
