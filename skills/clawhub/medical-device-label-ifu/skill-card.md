## Description:

Medical device labeling and IFU skill for comparing US, EU, and China labeling requirements, checking required label elements, structuring IFU content, using ISO 15223-1 symbols, and reviewing sterilization and reprocessing information with official references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Regulatory affairs, quality, and product compliance teams use this skill to review medical device labels and instructions for use against China, EU, and US requirements. It helps prepare market-specific checklists and IFU structures, but final regulatory positions should be verified against official sources and professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory requirements and standards can change after the skill package is released.

Mitigation: Verify current versions and requirements through official agency and standards sources before relying on the output.

Risk: The skill provides regulatory reference guidance and does not replace official agency decisions or professional review.

Mitigation: Have qualified regulatory affairs, quality, or legal reviewers approve final labeling and IFU content.

Risk: Generic trigger terms could invoke the skill outside a clear medical-device labeling or IFU context.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when the user request is clearly about medical-device labels, IFU content, symbols, sterilization, reprocessing, UDI, or market-specific labeling requirements.
