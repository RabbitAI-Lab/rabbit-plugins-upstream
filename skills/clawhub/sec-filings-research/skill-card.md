## Description:

Research SEC EDGAR filings and financial disclosures through Crawlora, including company filings, extracted sections, normalized financials, insider transactions, and institutional holdings. Use for sourced filing briefs and period-aware company or disclosure comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, investors, and developers can find SEC filings, retrieve relevant sections, and prepare sourced, period-aware company or disclosure comparisons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and scraped URLs are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use public filing links and avoid URLs containing credentials or private information.

Risk: API use consumes Crawlora credits.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated Crawlora API key and monitor credit usage.
