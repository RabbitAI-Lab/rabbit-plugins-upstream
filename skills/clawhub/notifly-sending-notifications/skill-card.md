## Description:

Helps an agent send a Notifly workflow to a subscriber or topic after showing an exact preview and obtaining explicit confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users and their agents use this skill to trigger a Notifly notification workflow for a verified subscriber or topic, review the exact send, and check the resulting delivery status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A notification is real and cannot be recalled after sending.

Mitigation: Review the exact preview and obtain explicit user confirmation before the send.

Risk: Sending to a topic or multiple recipients can reach more people than intended.

Mitigation: Verify the topic or recipient list and show its audience in the preview before confirmation.
