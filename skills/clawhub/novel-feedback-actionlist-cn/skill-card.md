## Description:

将读者或编辑已给出的反馈与章节摘要整理为可执行的改稿待办：按主题去重归组、标出影响章节、排序优先级并保留原意见依据。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Writers use this skill to turn supplied reader or editor feedback and chapter summaries for a chapter, scene, or short piece into prioritized, traceable revision tasks.

Global
