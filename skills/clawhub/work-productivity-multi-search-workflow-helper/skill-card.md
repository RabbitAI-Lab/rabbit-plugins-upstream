## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may auto-activate on ordinary requests mentioning broad terms such as search, engine, multi, or bug fixes. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation for multi-search workflow planning, troubleshooting, or hardening tasks; tighten triggers or disable implicit invocation before broader deployment. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance or generated artifacts may be incomplete or misleading if the user's constraints, success criteria, or available inputs are unclear. <br>
Mitigation: Restate the outcome and assumptions, ask only for materially missing information, and validate the result against the stated success criteria before use. <br>
