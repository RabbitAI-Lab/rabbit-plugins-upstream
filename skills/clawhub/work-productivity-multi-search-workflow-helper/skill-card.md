## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn broad demand for Multi Search Engine-style workflows into concrete plans, templates, checklists, analyses, code changes, or decision support. It emphasizes local-hardware-friendly execution and visible validation against the user's success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Overly broad activation wording may route ordinary search or productivity requests into this skill accidentally.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger keywords and consider disabling implicit invocation before deployment.

Risk: Workflow guidance may be accepted without checking whether it fits the user's concrete constraints.

Mitigation: Restate the user's outcome, constraints, inputs, and success criteria, then validate the result before acting on it.
