## Description:

Turn YouTube comment downloads into structured audience research questions, summaries, and follow-up analyses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[youtube-comments-downloader](https://clawhub.ai/user/youtube-comments-downloader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, product teams, and support teams use this skill to identify recurring audience needs and questions in YouTube comments, then plan videos, products, or support content. It can also use a video's transcript when the spoken content matters to the research question.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security and data handling of the external YouTube Comments Downloader service are not established by the reviewed skill.

Mitigation: Use a trusted service and review its data handling practices before sending it comment or transcript requests.

Risk: Comment downloads spend credits from the user's YCD account.

Mitigation: Consider credit costs and request only the narrowest matching content type needed for the research question.
