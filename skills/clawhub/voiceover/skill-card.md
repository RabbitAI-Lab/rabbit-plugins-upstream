## Description: <br>
Generate spoken voiceover and narration from a script, one speaker, with control over emotion, pacing, and voice. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Creators, developers, and content teams use this skill to turn scripts into one-speaker narration for ads, videos, audiobooks, IVR prompts, and other spoken-audio workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Private scripts, customer data, unreleased ads, IVR copy, or internal narration may be sent to an external audio provider. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm provider retention, storage, and access controls before submitting confidential content. <br>
Risk: Returned audioURL links may expose generated media to anyone with access to the link. <br>
Mitigation: Treat audio links as sensitive, avoid sharing them broadly, and store or revoke them according to the user's access-control requirements. <br>
