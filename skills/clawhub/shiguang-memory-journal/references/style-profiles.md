# Journal style profiles

Choose one profile from user intent and content. Do not infer unsupported facts.

## Portrait · 人像摄影

- **Structure:** one emotional portrait at 35%–48% of the content area; smaller expression, action, and environment cuts.
- **Visual language:** restrained photo wall, film strip, or Polaroid rhythm; warm gray, old rose, ink black, sage.
- **Copy:** sparse dates, places only when provided, and short truthful mood lines.
- **Protect:** faces, gestures, body proportions, and relationships.

## Pet · 宠物记录

- **Structure:** clear archive portrait plus growth moments, expressions, toys, food, or companionship details.
- **Visual language:** rounded cards, stamps, pet newspaper, cream, terracotta, sage, restrained pink.
- **Copy:** warm and playful without inferring breed, age, health, or temperament as fact.

## Travel · 旅游攻略

- **Structure:** start, ordered stops, and destination along a vertical or S-shaped route.
- **Visual language:** landmark hero, numbered nodes, arrows, map line, oatmeal paper, brick red, sea blue, ink green.
- **Copy:** answer where, sequence, and why it matters using only verified locations, times, prices, and transport.

## Game · 游戏攻略

- **Structure:** conclusion first; characters, equipment, mechanics, and steps ordered by priority.
- **Visual language:** strict grid, dark paper, high-contrast cards, minimal rotation, charcoal, cream, amber, teal.
- **Copy:** highly scannable; never infer version, values, strength, names, or builds from screenshots alone.

## Life · 生活记录

- **Structure:** 2–4 narrative groups organized by time, event, or emotion; assign opening, development, climax, return, and detail roles as supported. Use one dominant hero and one lower-page emotional anchor instead of a uniform photo wall.
- **Visual language:** natural notes, verbatim highlights, contextual paper or Polaroid frames, 1–3 tape accents, gentle offsets, and controlled transparent-person overlap; warm white, pencil gray, sage, soft apricot, or a reference-supported deep paper color.
- **Copy:** when the user supplies a story, score factual fidelity, narrative structure, and sentence selection before refining it. Use a layered opening, image-linked notes, one reflection, marginalia, and closing. Do not manufacture lived experience.
- **Typography:** establish at least three sizes and two font voices. Use highlight marks sparingly for real numbers, distances, people counts, achievements, turns, or distinctive original wording.
- **Theme shift:** for distance, mountains, sea, sky, or road, switch to mist blue, mountain green, and dawn white. Remove unrelated legacy motifs.

## Fashion · 穿搭与美妆

- **Structure:** complete look or final effect as hero; items, steps, palette, or alternatives around it.
- **Visual language:** editorial alignment, clear silhouette, soft pink, cream, deep brown, muted gold.
- **Copy:** describe visible form and color without inferring brand, price, body judgment, or product efficacy.

## Food · 美食攻略

- **Structure:** main dish or core choice as hero; menu, ingredients, steps, pairings, or alternatives as supporting cards.
- **Visual language:** preserve food color and plate edges; cream paper, tomato red, herb green, dark coffee.
- **Copy:** appetizing but factual; never invent shop, price, recipe, calories, or taste.

## Shared selection rules

- Use a profile's information order before its decoration.
- Derive the canvas ratio from an explicit target or reference before material count; do not lengthen a page merely because it has more assets.
- Let the intention modify palette and atmosphere without violating factual boundaries.
- Prefer a different profile only when the user's goal changes, not because an old background is convenient.
- For hybrid content, choose one dominant profile and borrow at most two secondary devices.
