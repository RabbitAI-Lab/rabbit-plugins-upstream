## Description: <br>
Defines a producer-consumer data contract for datasets, events, or APIs, covering schema, semantics, measurable quality SLAs, ownership, versioning, breaking-change policy, and change process. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Data producers, consumers, and data platform teams use this skill to draft explicit, versioned agreements for datasets, events, streams, or APIs so schema, semantics, quality expectations, ownership, and change governance are clear before downstream pipelines depend on them. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated contracts may contain inaccurate owners, SLAs, versioning rules, or consumer sign-off requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Review each contract with the responsible producer and affected consumers before adoption. <br>
