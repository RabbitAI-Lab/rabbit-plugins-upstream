## Description:

Translates Chinese business emails into idiomatic foreign-language correspondence, with subject-line options, tone variants, and notes on key phrasing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[petehuax](https://clawhub.ai/user/petehuax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business users translate Chinese correspondence for clients, suppliers, and colleagues into culturally appropriate emails with subject lines and tone options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive business terms in pasted emails may be exposed to the agent.

Mitigation: Share confidential content only when comfortable with the agent processing it.

Risk: Translations may alter commitments, dates, currencies, or units.

Mitigation: Check the translated wording and all converted figures before sending.
