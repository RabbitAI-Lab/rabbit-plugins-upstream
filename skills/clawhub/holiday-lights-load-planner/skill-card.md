## Description:

Estimates circuit loads and checks string connections, extension cords, timers, outdoor requirements, and seasonal energy costs for decorative light displays.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Households and event planners use this skill to estimate decorative-light loads and review circuit, cord, timer, and outdoor setup considerations before installation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; electrical examples primarily use US 120 V residential assumptions, with a brief 230 V note.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The planner can report successful electrical checks without verifying important product or installation limits.

Mitigation: Treat results as rough estimates; verify actual wattage, manufacturer connection limits, and cord ratings before powering a display. An all-clear does not establish safety.
