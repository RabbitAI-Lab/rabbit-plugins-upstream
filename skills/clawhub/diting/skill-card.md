## Description: <br>
Diting is an HR organizational diagnosis skill for turning complex people, team, compensation, culture, performance, and change questions into structured diagnostic reports with prioritized recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tuobadaidai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuobadaidai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
HR practitioners, people leaders, and organizational consultants use this skill to analyze root causes in organizational health, retention, compensation, talent review, performance systems, culture, and change-readiness scenarios. It is intended for deep diagnostic work, not routine HR policy lookup, email drafting, or simple template generation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive HR data, including employee, compensation, performance, layoff, or labor-risk information. <br>
Mitigation: Use anonymized inputs where possible, avoid storing case summaries unless appropriate authorization exists, and keep restricted information within the current session. <br>
Risk: Generated HR, legal, compensation, or personnel recommendations may be incomplete or context-dependent. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified humans verify decisions against current company policy, applicable law, and business context before acting. <br>
