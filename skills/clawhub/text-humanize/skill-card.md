## Description:

Audits English and Chinese social posts for AI-like writing patterns and offers platform-aware rewrites in a more conversational voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[songhonglei](https://clawhub.ai/user/songhonglei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers and social media contributors use this skill to review English or Chinese drafts for formulaic writing and revise them for a target platform. It provides an audit-only mode and an audit-plus-rewrite mode.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewrites can make AI-assisted public posts appear human-authored and conceal their origin.

Mitigation: Disclose AI assistance where required; do not use the skill to impersonate people or evade moderation.

Risk: Suggested typos or first-person details may make a post misleading or inauthentic.

Mitigation: Review rewrites before posting and remove invented experiences or statements that do not reflect the author's views.

Risk: Repeated use can support mass production of deceptive comments.

Mitigation: Use only for legitimate editing of individual drafts, not coordinated deceptive posting.
