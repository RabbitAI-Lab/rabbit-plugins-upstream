## Description: <br>
This Lite skill helps entrepreneurs and one-person or small teams turn a plain-language project description into a structured Business Model Canvas, a BP skeleton, and a short canvas diagnosis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[monsterdt](https://clawhub.ai/user/monsterdt) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External entrepreneurs, founders, and small teams use this skill to clarify a business model, fill the nine Business Model Canvas elements, create a lightweight investor-facing BP outline, and identify obvious gaps before deeper financial, fundraising, or legal work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may enter confidential company finances, valuation expectations, investor progress, or legal and fundraising status. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential fundraising or legal details unless retention and deletion controls are clear, and review the skill before installation. <br>
Risk: The Lite scope may be confused with legal, investment, financial modeling, or fundraising advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as general educational business-planning guidance and consult qualified professionals before formal fundraising, legal, or investment decisions. <br>
Risk: Profile or session details may be retained in workspace files if the agent uses the session profile template. <br>
Mitigation: Keep session profiles minimal, avoid storing sensitive details, and delete any retained workspace profile when the planning session is complete. <br>
