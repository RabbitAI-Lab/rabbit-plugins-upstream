## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trip details or prompts used with the skill may be sent to an external service or gateway. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid including passports, payment details, or unnecessary personal information unless the endpoint and data handling are clear. <br>
Risk: Cruise recommendations, prices, availability, and booking details may be incomplete or stale. <br>
Mitigation: Verify itinerary, pricing, availability, and booking terms with the cruise provider before purchase. <br>
