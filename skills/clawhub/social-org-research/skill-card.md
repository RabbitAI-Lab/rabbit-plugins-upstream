## Description:

Guides source-cited research on B Corps, social enterprises, NGOs, and youth-culture brands using quick or deep analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0xcjl](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xcjl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and teams use this skill to investigate the structure, business model, partnerships, people, and impact claims of social-purpose organizations, with quick and deep research modes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The maintenance notes ask an agent to update the installed skill after each research project without explicit user approval.

Mitigation: Review or remove those notes before installation, and require explicit approval before any persistent skill edits.

Risk: The uninstall instructions contain a permanent directory-deletion command.

Mitigation: Confirm the target directory and use an interactive or explicitly confirmed removal step.
