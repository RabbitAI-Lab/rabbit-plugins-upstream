## Description: <br>
Query a brand DESIGN.md design-system document and present its color palette, typography, component styles, layout principles, and do/donot guidance for users or AI agents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mebusw](https://clawhub.ai/user/mebusw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, and AI agents use this skill to retrieve curated brand design-system guidance and apply it when summarizing design conventions or generating brand-styled UI. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Opening bundled preview HTML may load third-party fonts or images. <br>
Mitigation: Review preview pages in an environment where external network requests are acceptable, or rely on the Markdown DESIGN.md files when offline review is required. <br>
Risk: The included GitHub issue template asks requesters for an email address if used. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid collecting personal contact details unless the repository issue workflow is intentionally used and the requester consents. <br>
Risk: Brand design guidance may be mistaken for authoritative or current brand rules. <br>
Mitigation: Treat bundled DESIGN.md files as reference guidance and verify against official brand materials before public or commercial launch. <br>
