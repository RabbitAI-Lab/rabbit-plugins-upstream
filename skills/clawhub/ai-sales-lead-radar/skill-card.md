## Description:

Finds and ranks Chinese public-sector and enterprise sales leads from planned projects, procurement intentions, and expiring contracts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dragonzu](https://clawhub.ai/user/dragonzu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Government and enterprise sales teams, business developers, and channel partners use this skill to discover and prioritize potential customers and upcoming procurement opportunities, then plan follow-up actions or recurring lead reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trial registration collects a hashed device identifier and may create a local API key.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit consent before registration, or use an existing key; protect the locally stored key.

Risk: Reports may include signed links that grant access without login.

Mitigation: Treat generated reports and links as access-bearing and restrict sharing.

Risk: Lead searches consume paid API credits.

Mitigation: Estimate credit use before scanning and ask permission before exceeding the planned call budget.
