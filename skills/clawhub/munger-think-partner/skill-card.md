## Description:

Provides a Charlie Munger-inspired, multidisciplinary framework for analyzing decisions, risks, and cognitive biases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, business decision-makers, and individuals use the skill to examine choices through mental models, inversion, and bias checks. It structures questions and risk analysis; specific investment, medical, or legal conclusions require independent verification and qualified professional judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses Chinese as its default output language.

Mitigation: Specify a preferred language when starting a request if Chinese is not suitable.

Risk: The skill may activate for broad personal-choice or self-reflection requests.

Mitigation: Use it when a Munger-style decision framework is wanted and state the decision context explicitly.

Risk: Framework-based analysis could be mistaken for a specific investment, medical, or legal conclusion.

Mitigation: Verify primary materials and consult a qualified professional for consequential judgments.
