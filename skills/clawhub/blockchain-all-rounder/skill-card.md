## Description:

Full-stack, multi-chain blockchain assistant covering BTC, ETH, BSC, Solana and major L2s for market analysis, on-chain/address tracking, DeFi yield strategy, and Solidity smart-contract development and security auditing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad crypto triggers can activate the skill for high-risk financial or smart-contract topics.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational, verify on-chain facts with independent sources, and keep final financial decisions with the user.

Risk: Market, yield, and contract guidance can be wrong or incomplete for live funds.

Mitigation: Require independent review, testing, and block explorer verification before trading, approving contracts, or deploying to mainnet.

Risk: Wallet or signing secrets could be exposed if a user shares private material.

Mitigation: Never request or process private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, keystores, or signing authority.
