## Description: <br>
A voice-first assistant for elderly users that supports messaging, weather checks, alarms, news, opera playback, and companion chat without requiring menu navigation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[scikkk](https://clawhub.ai/user/scikkk) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and caregivers use this skill to build or evaluate a voice-operated assistant for elderly people who need simple spoken access to reminders, messages, weather, media, and companion conversation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The assistant processes voice input and may trigger messages from spoken commands. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only after the elderly user and caregiver understand and agree to audio processing and require confirmation before sending messages. <br>
Risk: The proactive care flow can notify family after inactivity without clear opt-in, recipient, threshold, or disable controls. <br>
Mitigation: Make family notifications opt-in, configure approved recipients and thresholds, and provide a simple pause or disable control before real use. <br>
