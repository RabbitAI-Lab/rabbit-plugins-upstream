## Description: <br>
Auto Meeting Minutes turns meeting transcripts into structured minutes with discussion points, decisions, attendees, and action items. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[legionspace-hackathon](https://clawhub.ai/user/legionspace-hackathon) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and external users can use this skill to convert meeting transcripts into concise minutes that identify the topic, attendees, discussion points, decisions, and action items. It also supports per-person action item extraction and combining long meetings split by topic. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Meeting transcripts may contain confidential, personal, or business-sensitive information. <br>
Mitigation: Use only transcripts the user is authorized to process, redact unnecessary sensitive details when practical, and review generated minutes before sharing. <br>
