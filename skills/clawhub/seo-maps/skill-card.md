## Description:

Maps intelligence for local SEO across maps platforms, covering geo-grid rank tracking, GBP audits, review intelligence, NAP verification, competitor radius mapping, and LocalBusiness schema generation across free, DataForSEO, and Google Maps capability tiers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External local SEO practitioners and developers use this skill to audit how a business appears across maps platforms, prioritize maps visibility improvements, and generate maps-oriented LocalBusiness JSON-LD and analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Third-party mapping and SEO service queries may disclose business, review, or competitor information.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's data-sharing expectations and available credentials before running API-backed tiers; use free or manual analysis when external querying is not appropriate.

Risk: Geo-grid scans may consume DataForSEO credits.

Mitigation: Show the scan size and estimated cost, then get explicit confirmation before running credit-consuming scans.

Risk: Generated local analysis reports may contain business intelligence.

Mitigation: Store and share MAPS-ANALYSIS files according to the user's data handling requirements.
