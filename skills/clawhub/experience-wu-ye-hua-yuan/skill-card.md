## Description:

Guides users through a two-step, 30–45-minute moonlit-garden meditation and reflection journey hosted by drifts.bot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[buystsuff](https://clawhub.ai/user/buystsuff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and agents use this skill to start and follow a guided, low-intensity meditation journey, reflect on its steps, and receive a closing postcard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration and journey activity send chosen profile details, reflections, and reviews to drifts.bot.

Mitigation: Share only necessary profile details and avoid sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Risk: The API token grants account access and is shown only once at registration.

Mitigation: Store the token securely and do not expose it in shared logs or messages.
