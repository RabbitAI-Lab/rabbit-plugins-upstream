## Description: <br>
Analyzes ordinary Chinese blog posts for AI writing style signals and argument structure, then generates concise support or oppose comments within a 140 English-equivalent-character limit. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gordonlu](https://clawhub.ai/user/gordonlu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to analyze user-provided Chinese social or blog text for AI-writing-style signals, summarize the text's main argument, and draft one concise support or opposition comment. It is intended for manual, responsible use and does not determine authorship or prove whether generative AI was used. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: AI-writing-style analysis may be mistaken for proof of authorship or proof that generative AI was used. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the score and signals as writing-style observations only, and do not use them for identity claims, academic discipline, legal determinations, or other high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: Generated stance comments could be misused for harassment, political persuasion, bulk engagement, or automated social account activity. <br>
Mitigation: Use generated comments manually and responsibly; refuse political or high-risk content and do not generate bulk comments, impersonation, harassment, or automated posting. <br>
