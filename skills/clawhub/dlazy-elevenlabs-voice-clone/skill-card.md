## Description:

Clones a voice from a clean audio sample for use with ElevenLabs text-to-speech through the dLazy CLI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to request a custom voice from an authorized audio sample for text-to-speech production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Voice samples are sensitive, and unauthorized cloning can misuse another person's voice.

Mitigation: Upload only samples you own or have explicit permission to clone; avoid confidential recordings and review dLazy and ElevenLabs retention and deletion terms.

Risk: Command and output examples contain inconsistencies that can lead to incorrect use.

Mitigation: Review the CLI help and actual output before relying on examples; prefer the per-run npx option until the documentation is corrected.
