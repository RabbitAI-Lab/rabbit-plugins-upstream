# deadlines.md (Canada GST/HST)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; assigned frequencies and dates vary — verify with CRA / Revenu Québec.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- Assigned by annual taxable sales: annual (≤ C$1.5M), quarterly (≤ C$6M), monthly (above).
- Monthly & quarterly filers: due 1 month after the reporting period end.
- Annual filers: return due 3 months after fiscal year end. For individuals, the payment is generally due April 30 while the return due date differs (June 15) — flag this timing trap.
- QST returns (Revenu Québec): generally aligned with the same frequency pattern.
- BC PST returns: due the last day of the month following the reporting period.

## Usage Notes
- Deadlines falling on weekends/holidays shift; confirm in the CRA / RQ portal.
- Nil returns are still required once registered.
- If the user's assigned frequency is unknown, mark "cannot determine."
