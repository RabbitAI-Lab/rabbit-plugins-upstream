# rule-table.md (Canada GST/HST)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Items marked **[verify]** are recent or periodically adjusted; confirm with CRA / Revenu Québec before relying.

## Rate Structure
- GST (federal): 5%.
- HST (harmonized): Ontario 13%; New Brunswick / Newfoundland & Labrador / PEI 15%; Nova Scotia 14% (reduced from 15% on 2025-04-01) **[verify]**.
- PST (separate): BC 7%, Saskatchewan 6%, Manitoba 7%.
- QST (Quebec): 9.975% — separate regime administered by Revenu Québec.
- Rate follows the province of delivery (place of supply).

## Registration Threshold
- Small-supplier rule: worldwide taxable sales ≤ C$30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters → registration optional; above → mandatory.
- Since 2021-07-01: non-resident vendors selling to Canadian consumers must register once over C$30,000 (own-channel sales).

## Marketplace / DPO Coverage (since 2021-07-01)
- Distribution platform operators (Amazon.ca, Walmart.ca, etc.) are deemed suppliers for: sales by non-registered vendors through the platform, and sales of goods located in Canadian fulfillment warehouses.
- Own-website sales by a registered/over-threshold seller remain the seller's responsibility.

## QST & PST Notes
- Quebec: specified QST registration system for non-resident e-commerce vendors (threshold C$30,000).
- BC: online marketplace facilitators collect PST for platform sales into BC (since 2020-07).
- GST/HST registration does NOT cover QST or PST obligations.
