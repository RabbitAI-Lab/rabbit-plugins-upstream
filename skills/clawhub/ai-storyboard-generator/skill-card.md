## Description:

Turn a script, scene, or ad brief into a structured AI storyboard plan with a practical shot list and one to four storyboard key-frame images. Plan shot types, camera angles and movement, action, timing, dialogue, and sound for short films, ads, animation, social video, motion comics, and creative pitches.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and production teams use this skill to turn scripts, scenes, and ad briefs into reviewable shot lists and storyboard key frames for film, advertising, animation, social video, motion comics, and creative pitches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broader account permissions than image creation needs, and the connection is shared with other Beatra skills.

Mitigation: Review the full account grant before connecting; revoke the shared connection in the Beatra Console when access is no longer wanted.

Risk: Selected reference images are uploaded to Beatra, and storyboard key frames can incur charges.

Mitigation: Upload only references approved for transfer and approve each paid frame request before submission.
