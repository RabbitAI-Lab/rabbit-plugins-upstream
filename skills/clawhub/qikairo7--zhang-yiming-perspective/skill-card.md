## Description:

A Chinese-language perspective skill that uses research on Zhang Yiming's public statements and decisions to discuss products, organizations, globalization, talent, and personal growth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qikairo7](https://clawhub.ai/user/qikairo7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams can explore product strategy, organizational design, globalization, hiring, and personal development through a research-based simulation of Zhang Yiming's perspective. The simulated voice is not Zhang Yiming's own advice or endorsement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation phrases may invoke the persona without a clear request.

Mitigation: Review activation triggers and require explicit user intent where appropriate.

Risk: The first-person portrayal may be mistaken for Zhang Yiming's actual views.

Mitigation: Keep the initial disclosure visible and distinguish simulated analysis from verified quotations.

Risk: A silent version check can read git metadata, contact a git remote, and write a local update-check file.

Mitigation: Review or remove the version-check section before installation if those actions are not desired.
