## Description: <br>
B2B PM Super Workbench helps product managers create PRDs, BRDs, MRDs, research plans, roadmaps, pricing and GTM materials, and enterprise product architecture guidance across the B2B product lifecycle. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, product leaders, and product operations teams use this skill to structure B2B and SaaS product work, including market research, requirements discovery, enterprise architecture, AI product design, documentation, roadmapping, growth, pricing, GTM, and customer-success planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated product plans, legal/compliance notes, pricing recommendations, and technical architecture guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific business decision. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified product, legal, security, finance, and engineering reviewers verify important claims and decisions before relying on the outputs. <br>
Risk: Templates for interviews, procurement, customer details, budgets, employee names, or internal systems can expose sensitive information if users paste confidential data into prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Anonymize or omit sensitive customer, employee, financial, procurement, and system details unless organizational policy permits that use. <br>
Risk: The skill may add its own disclaimer, personal note, and author contact footer to responses, which may not fit internal document standards. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated deliverables before circulation and remove or adapt footer material according to organizational policy. <br>
