## Description:

Monitors ADC R&D, clinical evidence, patents, licensing deals, approvals, conferences, and competitive events to support landscape alerts and BD opportunity tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

ADC R&D and business development teams use this skill to generate weekly, monthly, or custom-period HTML monitoring briefs covering clinical updates, patent activity, approvals, deals, conference signals, and trend summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may combine public or licensed research data with proprietary business analysis.

Mitigation: Review each generated report and confirm data-sharing permissions before distribution.

Risk: Conference and public web-search items may be unverified or delayed compared with primary sources.

Mitigation: Keep source reliability labels in the report and verify important claims against original abstracts or authoritative publications.

Risk: Automated summaries of clinical, patent, deal, and approval data can omit context or carry through source errors.

Mitigation: Check key items against the linked source records, especially before using the report for investment, partnering, or regulatory decisions.
