## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pine Script syntax or platform limits may change.

Mitigation: Check current TradingView documentation and compile scripts before use.

Risk: Backtests may overstate the reliability of trading signals.

Mitigation: Use walk-forward validation and test across symbols and timeframes before trusting results.
