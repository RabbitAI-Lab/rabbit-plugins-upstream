## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Pine Script or debugging guidance can be incorrect or fail TradingView compilation.

Mitigation: Compile the script in TradingView and test it with bar replay, strategy tester, multiple symbols, and multiple timeframes before use.

Risk: Backtesting guidance or strategy examples may be mistaken for financial advice or reliable performance claims.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as coding assistance only, avoid performance guarantees, and independently review trading logic before relying on it.
