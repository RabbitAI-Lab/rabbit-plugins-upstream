## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to draft and troubleshoot TradingView Pine Script indicators and strategies, evaluate backtests, and check script behavior before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Version-sensitive syntax or platform limits may change.

Mitigation: Check current TradingView documentation and compile the script in Pine Editor.

Risk: Backtests or revising signals may overstate strategy reliability.

Mitigation: Compare live and reloaded signals and walk-forward test across symbols and timeframes before relying on results.
