## Description: <br>
Latticework guides an agent to analyze complex decisions by combining independent mental models, mapping convergence and divergence, and naming blind spots. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, operators, and decision-makers use this skill when a decision needs several independent disciplinary lenses rather than a single framework. It helps produce a structured latticework analysis with predictions, convergence areas, live unknowns, blind spots, confidence, and next information to seek. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Real-use notes or case examples added to the skill could include sensitive business or personal context. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact examples or observations before sharing, committing, or publishing updated skill files. <br>
Risk: A multi-lens analysis can sound more confident than the evidence supports if lenses are not genuinely independent or blind spots are omitted. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's verification checklist to require independent lenses, falsifiable predictions, named divergence, and at least one blind spot. <br>
