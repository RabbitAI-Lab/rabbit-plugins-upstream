## Description:

Discover and compare suppliers behind brands using Crawlora's ImportYeti company reports and public business websites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sourcing teams and researchers use this skill to assemble evidence-linked supplier shortlists, compare observed shipment activity, and identify questions for manufacturer qualification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, public URLs, extraction requests, and the API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Use the skill only if sharing those inputs with Crawlora is acceptable; keep the API key in the environment.

Risk: Incomplete shipment coverage and unverified company identities or claims can mislead supplier qualification.

Mitigation: Treat results as research evidence, check company identity and source links, and verify capacity and quality directly before making sourcing decisions.
