## Description:

Discover and compare suppliers behind brands using Crawlora's ImportYeti company reports and public business websites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sourcing and procurement teams use this skill to build supplier shortlists, compare observed shipment activity, and identify questions to qualify potential manufacturers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries, target URLs, and the API key are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Keep the API key in the environment and avoid private or sensitive URLs.

Risk: Shipment records may be mistaken for proof of manufacturing capability.

Mitigation: Treat shipment data as evidence of observed trade and independently qualify supplier capabilities.
