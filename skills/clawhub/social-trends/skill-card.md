## Description:

Creates evidence-backed cross-platform social trend briefs for a specified market and optional category, including momentum and changes since a previous run when available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to monitor social-platform trends across markets, research trends within a category, and compare findings with a previous brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Social trend research uses a paid third-party data provider and may incur per-call charges.

Mitigation: Confirm that social-platform monitoring is needed before making paid data calls, especially for generic news or brainstorming requests.
