## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and marketing teams use the skill to retrieve X posts, profile and audience data, and search results for social listening, creator research, and analytics workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and text submitted for analysis are sent to Superagnt and may consume credits.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive text, keep the API key private, and review OAuth permissions before connecting the optional MCP server.
