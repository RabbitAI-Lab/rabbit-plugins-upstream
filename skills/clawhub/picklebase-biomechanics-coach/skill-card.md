## Description:

Provides pickleball biomechanics, 3D trajectory, DUPR assessment, and tactical coaching guidance using PickleBase resources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aizaloapp](https://clawhub.ai/user/aizaloapp)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pickleball players, coaches, and assistants can use the skill to look up biomechanics and shot mechanics, assess DUPR-related skill questions, and develop doubles tactics. Queries use PickleBase resources, including an external service when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Query content is sent to PickleBase's external service; publisher identity and server-side retention were not independently verified.

Mitigation: Keep queries limited to information you are comfortable sharing, and avoid including sensitive or confidential details.
