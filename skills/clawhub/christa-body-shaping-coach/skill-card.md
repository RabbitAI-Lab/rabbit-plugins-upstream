## Description:

Christa is a body-shaping coaching skill that runs an adaptive fitness assessment, scores five readiness dimensions, answers fat-loss and nutrition questions from bundled knowledge, and generates a self-contained HTML report with an inline SVG radar chart.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vvip2u](https://clawhub.ai/user/vvip2u)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for general fitness, fat-loss, body-shaping, workout, diet, macronutrient, supplement, and plateau-breaking guidance. It first gathers relevant assessment information, then provides coaching responses or a personalized HTML fitness report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on fitness, fasting, keto, supplement, hormone, blood sugar, injury, pregnancy, medication, or disease-related responses as personalized medical care.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as general fitness and nutrition guidance only, and consult a qualified clinician or registered dietitian for medical conditions, medications, pregnancy, injuries, or disease-related concerns.
