## Description:

Helps agents break down questions, decide whether to retrieve information, and structure evidence-based reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and research agents use this skill to decompose questions, choose when to retrieve supporting information, and plan structured reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A retrieval decision or outline could be mistaken for verified evidence.

Mitigation: Review the output and check relevant sources before relying on retrieval decisions or report claims.
