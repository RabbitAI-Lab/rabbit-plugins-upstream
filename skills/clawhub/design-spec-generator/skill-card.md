## Description:

Generates design system specifications and developer handoff documents for mini-program, app, web, and large-screen interfaces.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[elise-ctrl](https://clawhub.ai/user/elise-ctrl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, developers, and product teams use this skill to draft platform-specific design systems and handoff specifications with colors, typography, spacing, components, and design tokens.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-first templates and platform assumptions may not suit other locales or platforms.

Mitigation: Request the desired language and verify platform-specific assumptions before using the specification.

Risk: Generated documents may be saved to the workspace when file output is requested.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination and contents before saving generated documents.
