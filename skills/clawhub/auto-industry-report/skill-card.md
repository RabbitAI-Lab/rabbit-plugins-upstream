## Description:

Generates panoramic automotive industry technical reports covering five domains, top auto parts companies, market analysis, technology paths, rankings, core patents, and charts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and patent professionals use this skill to turn supplied materials, search criteria, or automotive industry scope into a reviewable offline HTML report with findings, evidence, and next-step recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may call PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP data and produce sizable HTML reports.

Mitigation: Use it only for intentional automotive industry reporting tasks and provide clear scope, source preferences, and date ranges.

Risk: Automotive patent, market, or technology claims can be misleading if sources, retrieval scope, or uncertainty are omitted.

Mitigation: Require cited data sources, search ranges, time ranges, and explicit uncertainty notes in generated reports.

Risk: FTO, infringement, or validity discussions could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat legal sections as analytical support only and route legal conclusions to qualified counsel.
