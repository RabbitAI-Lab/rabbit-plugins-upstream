## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automotive market, patent, or company-ranking evidence may be incorrect or unsupported if source material is incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Verify cited sources, search scope, time range, ranking evidence, and uncertainty statements before relying on the report.

Risk: Legal topics such as FTO, infringement, or validity may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat legal outputs as analysis support only and consult qualified legal counsel for legal conclusions.
