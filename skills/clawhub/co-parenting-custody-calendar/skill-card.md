## Description:

Builds dated custody rotations, audits overnight splits, plans holiday priorities and travel make-up time, and exports calendar files and parenting-plan wording.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Separated or divorced parents and their advisers use this skill to draft and check custody schedules, holiday assignments, and travel changes before sharing or reviewing a parenting plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Exported calendars and logs can expose children's names, custody dates, travel, and family disputes.

Mitigation: Keep records local where possible and share or sync ICS files only through necessary, secure channels.

Risk: Generated schedules and court-facing wording may not reflect applicable family law or child-safety needs.

Mitigation: Have court-facing language reviewed by a qualified family-law attorney; seek urgent professional help for abuse or abduction concerns.
