## Description:

Guides teams in choosing and structuring usage- or outcome-based pricing for AI products, including attribution, margins, risk controls, and migration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product and pricing teams use this skill to select an AI product's charge unit and create a pricing plan grounded in customer value, measurable outcomes, and unit economics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing recommendations may rely on detailed business data or outdated vendor prices.

Mitigation: Use summarized business data where possible and verify current vendor prices.

Risk: Applying a pricing plan could change live customer billing in unintended ways.

Mitigation: Review the resulting plan before changing live billing.
