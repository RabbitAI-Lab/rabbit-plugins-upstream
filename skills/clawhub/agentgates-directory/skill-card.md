## Description:

Search every x402 service that exists, list your own for a flat fee (from a wallet or your account balance), and turn a settle you already made into a verified review that carries its receipt.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to discover paid x402 APIs, publish their own paid service listings, and submit reviews tied to on-chain payment receipts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Listing services or posting reviews can expose submitted details, including wallet and receipt links.

Mitigation: Review listing and review fields, including wallet and receipt links, before submitting them.

Risk: Listing a service can spend the owner's funds, and the remote service and future signing responses have not been independently verified.

Mitigation: Ask the owner before paying or stay within an approved budget; check the fee and wallet statement, and review the exact statement before authorizing a signature.
