## Description:

Compose a phone-sized morning briefing (schedule, gaps, weather, mail, deadlines) and an evening recap (what got done, what needs you, tomorrow).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tofuhsu](https://clawhub.ai/user/tofuhsu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to receive concise morning schedule and priority briefings and end-of-day recaps based on their calendar, email, weather, and deadlines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scheduled briefings can include private calendar and email details.

Mitigation: Send them to a private destination and grant only the connected-account access you intend.

Risk: The phrase “what did you do today?” can trigger a recap that also retrieves recent unread mail, deadlines, and tomorrow’s calendar.

Mitigation: Use an explicit recap trigger where possible and check that the destination and connected-account access are appropriate.
