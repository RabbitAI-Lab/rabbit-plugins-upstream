## Description:

Compose a phone-sized morning briefing (schedule, gaps, weather, mail, deadlines) and an evening recap (what got done, what needs you, tomorrow).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tofuhsu](https://clawhub.ai/user/tofuhsu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using a personal assistant can get a concise morning view of their schedule, weather, unread mail, and deadlines, then an end-of-day recap of activity, open items, and tomorrow's plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Briefings and recaps may surface calendar, email, or activity details in the selected delivery channel.

Mitigation: Enable the skill only for accounts and channels where these summaries are appropriate, and review connected-tool permissions and scheduling.

Risk: The phrase "what did you do today?" can invoke an end-of-day recap.

Mitigation: Review the recap trigger and enable it only where that phrase should request a summary.
