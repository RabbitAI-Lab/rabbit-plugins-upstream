## Description:

Guides agents to create one short Pruna video clip from text, images, or imported audio, with 1080p and frame-anchored options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to produce a single short video clip from a prompt, optional frame images, or an imported audio track.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected images or audio are sent to Pruna for generation.

Mitigation: Share only media and prompts approved for upload to Pruna.

Risk: Running unverified installer commands may install unexpected skill versions.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or otherwise verified skills installer.

Risk: Generation incurs charges and requires an API key.

Mitigation: Confirm cost-bearing requests before generation and keep PRUNA_API_KEY in an environment variable.
