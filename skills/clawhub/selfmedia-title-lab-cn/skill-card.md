## Description:

Rewrites a Chinese content brief into factual, platform-specific titles for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili, then recommends the title that best fits the supplied content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese independent creators and small-shop operators can turn a supplied content brief into factual, platform-specific titles before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Titles may overstate or add claims if the source brief is incomplete or unclear.

Mitigation: Use verifiable details from the supplied content and review titles before publishing.

Risk: Running an unpinned installer command may use an unexpected installer version.

Mitigation: Prefer manual copying, or review and pin the installer version before running it.
