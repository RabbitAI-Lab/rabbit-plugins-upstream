## Description: <br>
Helps agents make social content discoverable through search across social platforms by guiding keyword research, platform-specific SEO surfaces, natural keyword placement, topical clusters, profile searchability, and native search-insight measurement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and social media operators use this skill to make posts, profiles, and content clusters easier to find through TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and related search surfaces. It focuses on real keyword discovery, platform-specific indexed fields, natural placement, and honest measurement without invented search volumes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use user-provided brand, audience, and native analytics context to produce social SEO recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the brand, audience, and analytics details needed for the requested SEO task, and review outputs before publishing or operational use. <br>
Risk: Social platform search surfaces and ranking behavior change over time, and the skill does not provide guaranteed rankings or live in-app search volumes. <br>
Mitigation: Re-check platform-specific guidance and validate recommendations against native platform search insights, autocomplete, related searches, and approved analytics tools. <br>
