## Description: <br>
Helps developers, maintainers, and QA teams add useful unit tests, raise coverage, and verify changes against regression risk in existing codebases. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, software maintainers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to plan, implement, and validate unit tests for existing codebases. It provides repeatable workflow guidance, practical checklists, code-oriented support, and verification steps for improving coverage and reducing regressions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate during general testing or quality discussions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm this is the intended helper before using its guidance in sensitive repositories. <br>
Risk: Generated test plans, code changes, or coverage advice may be incomplete or misaligned with project behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed tests and run the repository's test and coverage commands before relying on the result. <br>
