## Description: <br>
Guides agents on improving accounts-receivable discipline by measuring DSO and aging, invoicing promptly, shifting payment terms upstream, systematizing follow-up, making payment easy, and enforcing consequences for chronic late payment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and business operators use this skill when customers pay late, cash is tight, or overdue receivables are growing. It helps agents suggest practical accounts-receivable routines such as DSO tracking, aging reports, same-day invoicing, deposits, automated reminders, and payment-term discipline. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Advice about late fees, credit holds, deposits, or collections practices may conflict with contracts, accounting policies, customer commitments, or local legal requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Review recommendations against applicable contracts, accounting processes, and legal requirements before applying or communicating them. <br>
Risk: An agent could over-apply collections guidance to customers where payment disputes, invoice errors, or relationship context require human judgment. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review before enforcing late fees, work stops, credit holds, customer termination, or other consequences. <br>
