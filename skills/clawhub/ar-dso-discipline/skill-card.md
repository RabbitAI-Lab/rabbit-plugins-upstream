## Description:

Provides practical guidance for measuring days sales outstanding, invoicing promptly, setting payment terms, following up, and handling overdue receivables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and finance or operations teams at businesses that invoice customers can use this skill to reduce days sales outstanding and improve collection discipline. It offers a checklist for aging receivables, timely invoicing, payment terms, reminders, and escalation decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Collection actions such as customer messages, payment-term changes, late fees, or credit holds could affect customer relationships or account status.

Mitigation: Review and approve these actions before execution, especially when the skill is connected to operational tools.
