## Description:

Uses Cue to track significant global news from the past 24 hours and explain equity-market catalyst logic, benefiting sectors, and short-term capital-flow signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External traders and market analysts use this skill to scan recent global market news, map catalysts to equity sectors, and produce a structured markdown report for short-term research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries are sent to cuecue.cn using the user's Cue API key.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive portfolio, account, or confidential business details unless the user is comfortable sharing them with Cue.

Risk: Reports depend on Cue service availability and external market-news source quality.

Mitigation: Review cited sources and cross-check important market conclusions before using the report in trading or business decisions.
