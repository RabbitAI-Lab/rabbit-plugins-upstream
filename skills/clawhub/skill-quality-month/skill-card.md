## Description: <br>
Quality Month guides employees through a confirmed, staged workflow for quality-month planning, scheduling, publicity, and summary or awards materials, producing Markdown and HTML reports while marking missing information as reference-only pending confirmation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees and quality managers use this skill to plan a quality-month program across proposal, schedule, publicity, summary, and award-review stages. It helps turn confirmed organization details, dates, scope, budget, activities, and metrics into review-ready internal planning documents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated planning documents may be inaccurate if organization details, budgets, dates, responsibilities, or quality metrics are incomplete or unconfirmed. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated business content against confirmed company inputs and keep unconfirmed items marked as reference-only pending confirmation. <br>
Risk: The skill may direct the agent to use document-processing skills for Word, Excel, or PDF inputs. <br>
Mitigation: Review source documents before processing and approve any additional document-processing skill use according to the workspace's data-handling policy. <br>
Risk: The skill may generate local Markdown and HTML report files. <br>
Mitigation: Inspect generated files before sharing them internally or externally, especially where they include company metrics or award decisions. <br>
