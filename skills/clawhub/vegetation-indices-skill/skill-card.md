## Description: <br>
Searches bundled remote-sensing vegetation index datasets and returns bilingual details, formulas, sensors, applications, source links, and related references. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ruiduobao](https://clawhub.ai/user/ruiduobao) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, and remote-sensing practitioners use this skill to look up vegetation indices, formulas, compatible sensors, application categories, and reference URLs from bundled datasets before applying or documenting spectral-index workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Bundled vegetation-index datasets may become stale or differ from current source databases. <br>
Mitigation: Verify source URLs, especially IndexDatabase.de links, before using results for critical scientific or operational work. <br>
