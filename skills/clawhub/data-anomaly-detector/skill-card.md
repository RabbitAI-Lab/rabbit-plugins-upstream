## Description: <br>
Detect anomalies and outliers in construction data: unusual costs, schedule variances, productivity spikes. Statistical and ML-based detection methods. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[datadrivenconstruction](https://clawhub.ai/user/datadrivenconstruction) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, construction analysts, and project teams use this skill to identify unusual costs, schedule variances, productivity spikes, duplicate records, sequence gaps, and data quality issues in user-provided construction data. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requires filesystem access to construction data files selected for analysis. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if you are comfortable granting access to those files, and limit inputs to the intended project data. <br>
Risk: Generated anomaly reports may influence business decisions but are analytical aids rather than controlled accounting or project-management outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Review estimates, findings, severity labels, and suggested actions before using them for project, schedule, or financial decisions. <br>
