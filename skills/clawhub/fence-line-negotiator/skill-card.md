## Description:

Use when a fence with a neighbor is planned, damaged, disputed, or being replaced — determines boundary responsibility, applies state cost-sharing doctrines (good-neighbor fence laws), prices fair shares, drafts the statutory notice letter, and de-escalates line/side/maintenance disputes before they become lawyer bills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and property owners use this skill to assess fence-line responsibility, estimate fair cost shares, route common damage scenarios, and prepare a neighbor discussion or draft notice before work begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated fence notices and legal guidance may not satisfy current local requirements or accurately describe liability.

Mitigation: Treat notices as discussion drafts, verify current local notice periods and liability rules, and seek qualified local advice before sending a demand, incurring costs, or signing a boundary agreement.
