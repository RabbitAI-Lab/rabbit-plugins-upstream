## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading analysis can be mistaken for financial advice or a guaranteed signal.

Mitigation: Present outputs as decision support, require user review, and preserve the skill's no-profit-guarantee and no-trade guidance.

Risk: Current XAU/USD analysis can become misleading when price, news, or macro data is unavailable or stale.

Mitigation: Require source and timestamp disclosure, avoid invented market data, and lower confidence or decline live conclusions when data cannot be verified.

Risk: Trade journals may contain sensitive account or trading details.

Mitigation: Avoid storing account identifiers, balances, or other sensitive details unless the user has explicitly accepted that persistence.
