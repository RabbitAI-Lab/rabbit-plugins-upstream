## Description:

Guides agents through planning, creating, editing, validating, and publishing HTML-based video and motion-graphics projects with HyperFrames.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to turn briefs, designs, footage, music, or existing projects into HyperFrames video workflows, from planning and editing through validation and rendering.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Prefer the plugin-managed path or pinned versions; require explicit approval before standalone upgrades and skill refreshes.

Risk: Publishing or broad background delegation can cause changes beyond the immediate editing task.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before publishing or broad background delegation.
