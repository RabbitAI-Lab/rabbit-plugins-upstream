## Description:

Analyzes cage-floor images or videos from pet care facilities to estimate visible feces and urine coverage and flag results that exceed a cleanliness threshold.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Pet boarding centers, pet shops, animal hospitals, and breeding facilities use the skill to review cage-floor cleanliness from images or videos and identify areas needing cleaning. Its results are for environmental hygiene management, not medical advice.
