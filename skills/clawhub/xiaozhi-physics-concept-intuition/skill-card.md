## Description:

Builds middle- and high-school students’ intuition for physics concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and explanations of what formulas mean.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle- and high-school students use this skill to understand what physics concepts and formulas mean, using familiar examples and guided thought experiments. It routes calculation practice, error tracking, and experimental procedures to their respective learning skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
