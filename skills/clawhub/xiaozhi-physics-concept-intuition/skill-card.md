## Description:

Helps middle- and high-school students understand physics concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and explanations of what formulas mean.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use this skill to build and check their understanding of physics concepts before relying on formulas. Educators can use its guided analogies and questions to support conceptual learning; it does not replace worked-problem coaching or laboratory instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum assumptions or crisis referrals may not fit students outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy and guardian-consent requirements, and crisis contacts before use in another region or language.

Risk: Student mastery records or reminder requests could be shared without the student's consent.

Mitigation: Check the stated consent settings before recording mastery or proposing reminder handoffs, and honor pause or sharing controls.

Risk: A simplified analogy may leave a student with an inaccurate physics concept.

Mitigation: Explain where analogies stop applying and ask a new-example or counterexample question to check understanding.
