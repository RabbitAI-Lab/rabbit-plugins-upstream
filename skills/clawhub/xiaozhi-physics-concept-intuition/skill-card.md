## Description:

A Chinese-language physics tutor that helps middle- and high-school students understand concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and explanations of formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to build and check intuitive understanding of middle- and high-school physics concepts before applying formulas. Worked problem solutions, mistake tracking, and hands-on experiment instructions are outside its scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China Mainland (curriculum and crisis referrals are region-specific; localize before deployment elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China Mainland curriculum assumptions and crisis referral channels may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy and legal defaults, and crisis referral channels before use outside China Mainland.

Risk: Learning-progress summaries may be stored or shared when platform memory and handoffs are enabled.

Mitigation: Review platform memory and sharing consent settings; honor student controls for recording, sharing, and deletion.
