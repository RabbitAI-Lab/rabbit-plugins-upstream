## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students understand physics concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and the meaning behind formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to build intuition about physics quantities and laws, then check understanding through questions. It covers middle-school physics and the first compulsory volume of high-school physics, not worked problem solutions or experiment procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (curriculum and crisis guidance); localize before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum and crisis contacts may not fit students outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions and crisis contact guidance before broader deployment.

Risk: Student learning records, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, or reminders could exceed a learner's consent.

Mitigation: Keep memory, sharing, and reminders opt-in; honor the documented view, correction, deletion, pause, and sharing controls.

Risk: Learning support is inappropriate when a student signals an immediate safety concern.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring and follow the documented crisis referral protocol with location-appropriate help.
