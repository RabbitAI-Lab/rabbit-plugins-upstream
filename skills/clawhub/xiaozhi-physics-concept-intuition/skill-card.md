## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking middle and high school students understand physics concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and the meaning behind formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to build and check intuitive understanding of middle- and high-school physics concepts. It redirects worked problems, mistake tracking, and practical experiments to other skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; curriculum, consent settings, and help channels require localization elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records, parent sharing, or reminder handoffs may affect minors' privacy or expectations.

Mitigation: Confirm platform consent, parent-sharing preferences, and reminder behavior before enabling these features; honor pause and sharing controls.

Risk: Mainland China-specific crisis channels, curriculum, and consent defaults may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize help channels, curriculum, and consent requirements before using the skill outside mainland China.
