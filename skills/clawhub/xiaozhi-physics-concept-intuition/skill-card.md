## Description:

初中物理概念直觉器，用生活类比、头脑实验和公式意义还原帮助学生把物理概念从“背下来”变成“真的懂了”。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External students and learning-support agents use this skill to build intuition for junior-high physics concepts before applying formulas. It explains concepts through everyday analogies, controlled thought experiments, formula-meaning restoration, and follow-up understanding checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create or update learning records and reminder requests for minors.

Mitigation: Use only the disclosed privacy controls and consent gates for memory, sharing, parent visibility, and reminder enqueueing.

Risk: Crisis-support wording and hotline guidance is localized for mainland China.

Mitigation: Review or localize crisis referral language before use with students outside mainland China.

Risk: Generated physics examples or practice checks could contain inaccurate quantities, units, or age-inappropriate material.

Mitigation: Apply the bundled AI item self-check protocol before presenting generated items or teacher-facing materials.
