## Description:

通过生活类比、头脑实验和公式意义讲解，帮助初中至高一学生理解物理概念，并检验他们能否迁移运用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初中至高一的学生及其教学支持者，用简体中文讲解物理量和规律的含义，借助类比、思想实验与理解检查建立概念直觉；不代替具体题目解答或实验操作指导。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localization of curriculum, minor consent and crisis-support channels.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or reminders may be shared or retained without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm age-appropriate consent and platform settings before enabling memory, cross-skill sharing or reminders; honor viewing, deletion and opt-out requests.

Risk: Crisis contacts and consent defaults are designed for mainland China and may misdirect students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis channels, curriculum and minor-consent rules before deployment outside mainland China; confirm the student's region before naming emergency contacts.

Risk: Analogies or formula explanations may reinforce physics misconceptions if applied beyond their limits.

Mitigation: Check each analogy against the referenced misconception guidance and verify understanding with a new example before recording mastery.
