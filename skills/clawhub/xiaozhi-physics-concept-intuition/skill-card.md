## Description:

Builds middle and high school students’ intuition for physics concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and explanations of what formulas mean.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle and high school students use this skill to understand the meaning and reasoning behind physics concepts and formulas. It builds intuition with familiar examples and guided thought experiments, while routing specific problem solving, error tracking, and experimental data work to companion skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learning records may retain a student’s learning history.

Mitigation: Enable storage, sharing, and reminders only as desired; use “这次不要记忆” to disable record access and saving for a session, or “暂停提醒” to pause reminders. Review the host’s storage controls and companion skills separately.
