## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students understand physics concepts through everyday analogies, thought experiments, and the meaning behind formulas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this Chinese-language tutor to build and check intuition about middle-school physics and first-year high-school compulsory-volume-one concepts. It explains concepts rather than solving specific exercises or directing hands-on experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records or reminder requests may involve a student's personal information.

Mitigation: Check the student's consent and platform settings before sharing learning progress or queuing reminders; honor pause, deletion, and sharing preferences.

Risk: Mainland China-specific curriculum and emergency guidance may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, consent requirements, and emergency contacts; confirm the student's location before giving emergency numbers.

Risk: An analogy or generated understanding check can misstate a physics concept.

Mitigation: Review analogy limits and check generated questions for physical accuracy before using them with students.
