## Description:

Part of the Overpowered skill suite, this skill helps build an evidence-aware current-state map of how work actually happens across people, systems, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, and tacit rules before improvement, redesign, or automation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, operations teams, and process analysts use this skill to map a bounded current-state business process from documented, observed, and reported evidence before deciding whether to improve, redesign, or automate it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Process artifacts supplied to the skill may contain sensitive business, customer, employee, or financial information.

Mitigation: Apply normal data-handling controls for sensitive operational evidence before sharing or processing those artifacts.

Risk: A current-state map can become misleading if documented, observed, and reported process views are collapsed into one account.

Mitigation: Keep evidence status explicit and separate confirmed, reported, inferred, and unknown claims in the output.
