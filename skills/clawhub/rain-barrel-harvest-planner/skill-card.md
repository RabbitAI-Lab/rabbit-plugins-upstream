## Description:

Estimates rainwater yield, irrigation demand, storage sufficiency, overflow, and payback for garden harvesting systems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Gardeners, homeowners, and landscape planners use this skill to estimate roof catchment yield, compare seasonal rainwater supply with irrigation demand, size storage, route overflow, and assess payback. Treat results as preliminary irrigation estimates and verify local conditions and installation details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rainfall patterns, roof area, demand, and storage assumptions can make preliminary yield and sufficiency estimates inaccurate.

Mitigation: Use local monthly rainfall and measured site inputs, then review the simulation's seasonal assumptions.

Risk: The documentation contains inconsistent pressure guidance, and installation requirements can vary by site.

Mitigation: Verify pressure requirements and physical installation details before building the system.

Risk: The planner does not cover drinking-water system safety, and harvesting rules vary by location.

Mitigation: Keep these estimates to garden irrigation, and verify local legality and any requirements for other uses.
