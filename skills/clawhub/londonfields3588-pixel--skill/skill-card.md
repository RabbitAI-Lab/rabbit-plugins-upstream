This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Content creators, marketers, and website owners use this skill to draft SEO-friendly content, improve existing pages, align writing with search intent, and generate on-page optimization suggestions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: SEO recommendations or generated content may be inaccurate, outdated, or misaligned with a site's audience and search intent. <br>
Mitigation: Review drafts and optimization suggestions before publication, checking facts, tone, structure, and business fit. <br>
Risk: Third-party SEO tool recommendations may lead users to share confidential unpublished content with external services. <br>
Mitigation: Use external tools only when trusted and avoid submitting sensitive drafts, customer information, or proprietary strategy. <br>
