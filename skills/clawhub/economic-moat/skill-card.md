## Description: <br>
Helps agents evaluate durable business defensibility by testing economic moat sources, trajectory, and smart-attacker risk for investments, acquisitions, and startup strategy. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, investors, founders, and strategy teams use this skill to assess whether a business has a durable structural advantage, how that advantage may widen or narrow, and what operational implications follow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Moat analysis can influence investment, acquisition, or strategy decisions using stale or incomplete financial information. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current financials, market data, and cited sources before acting on the skill's conclusions. <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on a plain Markdown reasoning aid as a substitute for diligence or professional review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical guidance for review, not as financial advice or a replacement for independent due diligence. <br>
