## Description:

Analyzes whether a business has a durable competitive advantage by testing moat sources, trajectory, and credible competitive attacks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, investors, and business leaders use this skill to assess a company's long-term competitive defensibility for investment, acquisition, or strategy decisions. It produces a structured moat assessment with evidence, erosion risks, and operational implications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks agents to save real-use lessons in its reusable instructions without clear privacy controls, which could expose confidential company information when the skill is later shared.

Mitigation: Remove the recurring save instruction or require explicit approval and generalized, sanitized lessons; review saved changes before sharing the skill.
