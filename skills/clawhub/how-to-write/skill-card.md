## Description:

A Chinese-language writing methodology skill distilled from How to Write by Ren Zhongran for drafting, revising, and structuring official documents, speeches, reports, essays, self-media articles, and other non-fiction writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to apply a structured Chinese writing framework when drafting or revising official documents, leadership speeches, research reports, duty reports, briefings, essays, self-media articles, and other non-fiction text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is a general Chinese writing-methodology aid and may be insufficient for legal, medical, financial, highly localized, or non-Chinese writing contexts.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general writing guidance and use a specialized source or domain expert when accuracy, jurisdiction, format rules, or regulated content matter.

Risk: Official-document examples are grounded in public-sector writing methods and may not reflect current binding document-format requirements.

Mitigation: Verify formal public-sector documents against current authoritative regulations, local policy, and organizational review before use.

Risk: The source material is a distilled methodology from a book rather than the complete source text.

Mitigation: Use the skill for structure, drafting, and revision patterns, and independently verify quotations, facts, citations, and source-specific claims.
