## Description: <br>
Provides guidance for optimizing signup, registration, account creation, and trial activation flows to reduce friction and improve completion. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product, growth, design, and engineering teams use this skill to audit and improve signup flows, registration forms, account creation, and trial activation paths. It produces recommendations for reducing friction, prioritizing changes, planning experiments, and improving form UX across B2B, B2C, waitlist, and e-commerce patterns. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask the agent to use local product-marketing context files when they exist, which can expose sensitive strategy details to the recommendation process. <br>
Mitigation: Keep sensitive strategy details in those files only when they are appropriate for the agent to use in signup-flow recommendations. <br>
