## Description:

Use when a customer has already realized value and the rep or customer-success lead needs to generate new leads by asking that satisfied customer for referrals: whom and when to ask, the ask scripts, orchestrating the intro, and tracking and rewarding the loop.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales and customer-success teams use this skill to turn satisfied China-focused B2B customers into referral opportunities. It helps an agent produce referral readiness judgments, ask scripts, forwardable intro text, handoff fields, follow-up timing, and compliant reward guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can involve customer chat screenshots, referred-contact details, and use of an introducer's name.

Mitigation: Confirm consent before using screenshots, referred-contact information, or an introducer's name; keep CRM and retention practices aligned with company privacy rules.

Risk: Referral rewards can create commercial bribery concerns, especially for government or enterprise customers.

Mitigation: Prefer company or team-level recognition, reciprocal business help, or approved low-value gifts; avoid personal cash kickbacks and pay-for-introduction arrangements.

Risk: The playbook is adapted for China-focused B2B referral channels and may not fit other markets as written.

Mitigation: Review channel assumptions, consent practices, and reward rules before applying the workflow outside China-focused sales motions.
