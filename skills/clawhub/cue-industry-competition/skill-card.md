## Description:

Uses Cue to assess industry cycles, competitive positioning, supply and demand turning points, and potential investment windows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Investment researchers and analysts use this skill to prepare industry briefs on market cycles, competitive dynamics, supply and demand, policy shifts, and allocation opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill installs and runs external Cue runner code from a live, unpinned repository.

Mitigation: Install only if you trust Sensedeal/Cue; review the runner before executing it and pin a reviewed revision where practical.

Risk: Research prompts may contain confidential strategy or proprietary data, and report links are shared by default.

Mitigation: Check Cue's privacy terms before submitting sensitive material and opt out of share links when reports should remain private.
