## Description:

Publish and schedule social media posts on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Threads with Rizamo, see how they did, and answer comments and messages. Use when the user wants to post or schedule social content, check post or account analytics, or reply to their audience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and social media teams use Rizamo to create and schedule posts, review account analytics, and respond to comments and messages across their connected social accounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial, and the skill accesses Rizamo's external API for connected social accounts.

Mitigation: Confirm you trust Rizamo and understand the API access you grant before installing, as advised by the security evidence.

Risk: Publishing posts or sending replies can make changes on connected social accounts.

Mitigation: Show the user the proposed content and wait for approval; keep posts as drafts until approved, and follow the skill's TikTok-specific consent steps.
