## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and automation agents use this skill to prepare RunComfy CLI commands and parameters for Seedance 2.0 Pro video generation, including text-only, image-referenced, and multimodal lip-sync clips. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts and referenced image, video, or audio media are sent to RunComfy and its model infrastructure for processing. <br>
Mitigation: Use only prompts and media that are permitted to share with RunComfy; avoid private, regulated, biometric, confidential, or unauthorized third-party content. <br>
Risk: External media URLs are fetched by RunComfy and may carry privacy, rights, or content-safety concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Review media sources and permissions before use, and avoid untrusted or sensitive URLs in generated commands. <br>
