## Discovery and workflow

Checked October 22, 2026.

- [JobCCC, October 5, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05787): conversational job matching across relevance, preferences and constraints. Its regional/code-mixed results inform avoiding brittle keyword filters.
- [Skills-based hiring, arXiv:2312.11942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11942): sector-specific evidence for considering skills beyond degree/title matches.
- [SEQUOR, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06353): long-conversation instruction drift motivates keeping current choices and corrections usable.
- [WebArena-Lite human audit, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01491): incomplete web workflows motivate actual submission evidence and recovery from observed state.
- [CareerOneStop Skills Matcher](https://www.careeronestop.org/Toolkit/Skills/skills-matcher.aspx) and [mySkills myFuture](https://www.myskillsmyfuture.org/SkillsTransferability/GetStartedPageHelp.aspx): optional vocabulary aids identified during earlier discovery research.

## Interviews, profiles and offers

Checked October 22, 2026.

- [MIT: Using AI for interview prep](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/using-ai-for-interview-prep/): role-based talking points and interactive practice rather than memorized scripts.
- [Adaptive robotic interview coaching, January 22, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15600): a small design study informs controllable feedback timing.
- [PolyInterview, July 11, revised July 17, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10310v3): CV/JD-grounded questions, answer-aware follow-ups and evidence-linked feedback.
- [USC Viterbi: Evaluating and negotiating offers](https://viterbicareers.usc.edu/evaluating-negotiating/): compare the full package against the applicant's priorities.
- [Harvard: Evaluating and Negotiating Job Offers](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/hes-evaluating-and-negotiating-job-offers-2/): prepare salary requests from researched pay, actual terms, alternatives and priorities.
- [Ames and Mason: Tandem Anchoring, 2015](https://www.columbia.edu/~da358/publications/Tandem_anchoring.pdf): range-offer effects depend on the endpoints, width and extremity. A market range is not automatically an employer-facing proposal.
- [Harvard PON: First offers, May 28, 2024](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/batna/power-and-negotiation-advice-on-first-offers/), discussing [Maaravi, Heller and Levy, 2023](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nejo.12423): first-offer choice depends on information and negotiating alternatives.
- [Harvard PON: Multiple equivalent offers, April 8, 2026](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/dealmaking-daily/the-benefits-of-multiple-offers/): research-informed guidance for comparable package alternatives when useful terms are flexible.
- [Takenami et al.: LLM anchoring, revised September 17, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21137): simulated price negotiations demonstrate susceptibility to initial figures.
- [BLS: Using wage data in salary negotiations](https://www.bls.gov/oes/earnings.htm): compare pay by occupation, industry and location; retain the source data period as well as the date checked. Choose local sources for other countries.
- [AI Advice Reinforces Traditional Gender Roles, October 1, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14036) and [salary-advice audit, revised October 8, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15567): reported model differences and demographic effects support grounding salary recommendations in comparable work, market evidence and the applicant's actual priorities.
- [LinkedIn: Skills filter](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a591634) and [Recruiter search guidance](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/resources/talent-acquisition/how-to-use-linkedin-recruiter): relevant skills, role terms and readable work descriptions help recruiters understand and find profiles.
- [Semantic Search At LinkedIn, February 7, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07309): meaning-based job/people search supports describing actual work rather than stuffing exact keywords.
- [LinkedIn: Automated activity](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340567/automated-activity-on-linkedin?lang=en): profile and message delivery uses ready-to-paste text rather than site automation.
