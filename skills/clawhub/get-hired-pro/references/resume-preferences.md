# Select and present this applicant's experience

Use the applicant's requested chronology, emphasis and presentation. Substantive current independent work is core professional experience. Give it a clearly attributed experience entry or an equally visible presentation suited to this applicant. Judge prominence on the actual page so a section choice or page break does not bury a relevant strength. Select individual accomplishments against the employer's needs. Use supplied titles and dates. Do not fabricate missing dates or flatten current work into a career-transition apology.

The applicant can both do the work and improve how it gets done. When they have automation capability, make its employer-specific value visible in both documents, supported by the closest relevant work they built. In research and selection, identify recurring effort, information flows, decisions and handoffs where that capability helps. Explain how the implemented work makes the employer's desired work easier, more consistent or more scalable. Let the role determine the mechanism and prominence alongside other strengths. A product inventory or promised saving supplies no substitute for that connection. Follow the applicant's explicit choices and retain the full capability record for future use.

For the résumé, compare proposed accomplishments with stronger relevant alternatives in the full career. Keep evidence that establishes a needed capability or adds complementary support. Give it space according to its persuasive value; chronology and employer coverage do not require equal detail. Show relevant action, scope, mechanism and available result in normal accomplishment-led prose. Develop a useful decision or constraint when it explains how the work succeeded. Technical detail should make the implemented capability and the work it enables understandable. Keep useful precision and qualitative achievements; these are selection decisions, not a formula for every bullet. Do not recap a whole job because one accomplishment was relevant.

The résumé must work alone and provide additional supporting evidence beyond the letter wherever strong relevant evidence is available. Do not directly duplicate sentences or substantial phrasing from the letter. The same important accomplishment can support both documents with independent language and useful extra detail. Facts, names, tools and metrics can repeat. Do not replace strong evidence with a weaker example just to avoid overlap.

Check reviewer suggestions against the applicant's current preferences before correcting the document. Follow their choices for older work, publication citations, disclosures and credentials. When unspecified, choose a clear presentation suited to their background and target role. Preserve relevant professional certifications and independent work; age, employment type or an unfamiliar title does not make useful work a footnote. Do not import another applicant's approvals or samples.

The applicant's supplied facts are authoritative. Explicitly reject hedging, minimization, deficit narration, defensive credit disclaimers and claim verification.
