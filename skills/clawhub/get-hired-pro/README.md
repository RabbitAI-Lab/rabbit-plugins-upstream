# Get Hired PRO - Get the Job of Your Dreams

Put your OpenClaw to work landing your next job.

Give it your résumé and tell it what work you want. Your Claw finds suitable openings, researches employers and writes applications tailored to each job. It submits them when the employer allows it and tracks each application through replies, interviews and offers.

**Launch Freebie · Free Download · Version 2.0.1**

## Give it to your OpenClaw

Send this package and your résumé. Say:

> Install Get Hired PRO and help me apply for this job: [job link]

Still looking? Tell it what work you want. If you don't have a résumé, it helps you build one from your experience.

## From job search to offer

1. **Find jobs you want.** Tell your Claw what work you're looking for. It finds openings that fit your goals and experience.

2. **Know what salary to ask for.** Get a target based on pay for similar jobs in your field and location.

3. **Refresh your LinkedIn bio.** Get copy tailored to your experience and the roles you want. Want to message a recruiter? Your Claw checks the roles they fill and drafts your introduction.

4. **Get your applications sent.** Your Claw writes a tailored résumé and cover letter in PDF or Word, then submits them when the employer allows it.

5. **Keep track of every application.** See what you sent, when you applied and who replied. Get follow-up drafts when you need them.

6. **Get ready for interviews.** Practice questions for the job and get feedback on your answers.

7. **Negotiate your offer.** Your Claw recommends what to ask for, writes your reply and helps with counteroffers.

Share employer replies, or ask your Claw to check an inbox it already has access to, to keep the tracker current. Use the whole workflow or start at the stage you need. Your background and preferences stay available for later applications.

## Put your Claw to work

Use the OpenClaw and AI model you already have. Your Claw handles the research, application writing and submission. Get your applications as PDF or editable Word files, or read them in chat.

When an application needs a human step, your Claw saves the work and shows you how to finish. LinkedIn copy and recruiter messages are ready to paste.
