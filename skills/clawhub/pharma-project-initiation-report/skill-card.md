## Description:

Generates evidence-based biomedical project-initiation reports for a drug, target, or indication, covering pipelines, competitors, patents/FTO, BD activity, clinical data, safety, market sizing, China regulatory path, ROI, risk, GO/NO-GO conclusions, and a 90-day action plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Biopharma strategy, BD, clinical, market-access, and patent teams use this skill to produce structured HTML initiation reports for candidate drugs, therapeutic targets, or disease indications. The reports emphasize sourced KPIs, negative signals, quality gates, and decision-oriented GO/NO-GO recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may contain incorrect, incomplete, or outdated biomedical, clinical, patent, market, regulatory, or investment analysis.

Mitigation: Review generated outputs against primary sources and qualified medical, legal, regulatory, and business experts before relying on them for decisions.

Risk: The skill performs extensive research using external data sources and web checks, which can introduce source-quality variation.

Mitigation: Verify cited evidence quality, prefer authoritative sources for quantitative claims, and treat lower-confidence sources as background only.
