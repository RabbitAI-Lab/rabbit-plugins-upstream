## Description:

Designs distinctive, on-brand emails using a structured brief and adapts them for a connected email sending tool.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cosmoblk](https://clawhub.ai/user/cosmoblk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and email creators use this skill to design newsletters, campaigns, and customer emails that reflect their brand, then prepare a preview in a connected sending tool.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected email-platform settings, recent templates, media, and project brand files may contain sensitive brand information.

Mitigation: Connect only accounts and project files you are comfortable sharing with the agent.

Risk: The skill may fetch imagery from a brand website or social profiles.

Mitigation: Review proposed image sources and use approved brand assets where available.

Risk: A campaign may reach recipients if a real send is approved without reviewing the preview.

Mitigation: Inspect the dry-run preview and consider a test email before explicitly approving a real send.

Risk: The README suggests an unpinned installation command.

Mitigation: Consider pinning the release or manually installing the skill.
