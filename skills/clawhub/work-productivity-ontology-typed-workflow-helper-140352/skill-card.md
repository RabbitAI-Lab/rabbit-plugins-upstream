## Description: <br>
Helps agent users and skill authors turn ontology-style workflow needs into practical plans, checklists, analyses, code changes, and reliability improvements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External developers, AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to structure ontology-style workflow work into concrete deliverables such as plans, checklists, analyses, implementation support, and validation notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for loosely related workflow, ontology, or knowledge-graph requests. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for ontology-style workflow design or narrow its trigger phrases before deployment when precise routing is required. <br>
Risk: The skill produces guidance and artifacts that may influence setup, safety, reliability, or implementation decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against the user's success criteria and test any proposed code, configuration, or shell commands before applying them. <br>
