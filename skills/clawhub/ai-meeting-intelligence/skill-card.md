## Description:

AI Meeting Intelligence Expert helps teams transcribe meetings, summarize decisions, extract action items, draft follow-ups, and track meeting outcomes across the meeting lifecycle.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Project managers, executives, sales teams, and distributed teams use this skill to turn meeting notes or transcripts into agendas, summaries, action items, decision logs, follow-up drafts, and meeting-health analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting notes and transcripts can contain personal, financial, customer, medical, or confidential information.

Mitigation: Minimize input, redact sensitive fields where possible, pseudonymize identifiers, and keep any mapping outside the agent conversation.

Risk: Draft summaries, follow-up emails, or tracker updates could expose sensitive information if saved, sent, or synced without review.

Mitigation: Show the full draft, mark sensitive content, and require explicit user confirmation before saving, sending, or updating another tool.

Risk: Recording or transcribing meetings may require participant consent and organization-specific compliance review.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable policy and participant consent requirements before enabling automatic recording or transcription.

Risk: AI-generated summaries are lossy and may be insufficient for legal, financial, regulatory, or formal decision records.

Mitigation: Treat generated output as a draft and verify it against the approved minutes or other authoritative record before relying on it.
