## Description:

Helps teams prepare meeting agendas and turn meeting notes or transcripts into summaries, decisions, action items, and follow-up drafts for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Project managers, executives, sales teams, and distributed teams use this skill to prepare agendas, summarize meeting material, track decisions and assigned actions, and draft follow-ups for approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting material can disclose personal or confidential information.

Mitigation: Remove unnecessary sensitive details and use approved local processing for sensitive meetings.

Risk: Recordings or transcripts may lack participant consent.

Mitigation: Confirm participant consent and organizational policy before recording or transcribing.

Risk: An inaccurate or sensitive draft could be saved or shared as an official record.

Mitigation: Review decisions, recipients, and confidential details; require separate explicit approval before saving or sending.
