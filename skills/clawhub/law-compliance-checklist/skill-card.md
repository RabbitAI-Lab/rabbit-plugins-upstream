## Description:

Generates an industry-specific pre-launch compliance checklist covering permits, disclosures, agreements, advertising, and privacy from business details supplied by the user.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance practitioners and small business operators use this skill to draft a pre-launch checklist tailored to their industry, business model, channel, and scale.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A general checklist may omit or misstate local licensing and regulatory requirements.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as non-legal guidance and confirm requirements with local authorities or qualified counsel before acting.

Risk: Broad compliance prompts may activate this skill when a narrower legal workflow is intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that the checklist matches the user's task and choose a specialized workflow when needed.
