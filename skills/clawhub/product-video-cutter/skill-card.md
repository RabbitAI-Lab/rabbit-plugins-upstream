## Description: <br>
Product Video Cutter helps agents turn long product videos into conversion-focused cut plans for PDP, ads, creator reviews, and short social videos by preserving strong hooks, demonstrations, and proof while removing low-value footage. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, ecommerce, and content operations teams use this skill to convert product demos, unboxings, UGC, and reviews into shorter, channel-specific edit plans with source timecodes, retention reasons, deletion notes, transition risks, and alternate cut suggestions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Timestamped cut recommendations can be wrong if the source video is misread or timecodes are not checked. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed timecodes against the source video before editing or publishing. <br>
Risk: The skill may recommend edits that improve conversion framing but still need brand, claims, accessibility, or platform review. <br>
Mitigation: Validate final cuts for product-claim accuracy, subtitles, CTA suitability, and channel-specific requirements before release. <br>
