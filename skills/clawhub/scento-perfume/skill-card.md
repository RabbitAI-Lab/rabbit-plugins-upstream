## Description:

Look up perfume notes, scent families, houses, similar perfumes, side-by-side comparisons and raw materials from Scento's free perfume database (1,400+ perfumes, 400+ raw materials, 140+ houses). No API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scento](https://clawhub.ai/user/scento)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People looking for fragrance information can use this skill to find perfume profiles, scent families, perfume houses, similar scents, comparisons, and information about fragrance materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Perfume searches and preferences are sent to Scento; live service behavior and data retention were not verified.

Mitigation: Keep queries limited to information needed for the perfume lookup.
