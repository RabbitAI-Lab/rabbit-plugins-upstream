## Description:

Evidence-first structured review with source-tiered claims. For code, notes, agent output, and research. Inspired by gstack methodology and gbrain source-tiering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and reviewers use this skill to assess code, notes, agent output, and research with source-tiered claims, explicit evidence gaps, a recommended fix, and stated proof and risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security review is incomplete because a referenced local path was missing, so related evidence could not be checked.

Mitigation: Verify the missing path and review its contents before installation.
