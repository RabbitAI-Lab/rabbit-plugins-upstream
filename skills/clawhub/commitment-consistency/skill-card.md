## Description: <br>
Guides agents in using commitment-and-consistency reasoning to design ethical commitment paths or evaluate when prior small commitments are pulling a decision away from its current merits. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to design ethical commitment ladders for onboarding, sales, hiring, fundraising, and behavior-change workflows, or to defend against escalation tactics. It emphasizes transparency, user benefit, clear exit options, and the fresh-start test for decisions influenced by prior commitments. <br>
