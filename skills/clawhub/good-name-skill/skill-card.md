## Description: <br>
专业命名技能，用于中文个人取名、英文个人取名、公司/品牌命名，以及基于新增约束的多轮改名优化。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhenyangze](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhenyangze) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and naming practitioners use this skill to generate and refine candidate Chinese personal names, English personal names, and company or brand names under stated constraints. The skill asks for missing inputs, routes to the relevant naming scenario, and returns multiple candidates with meanings, fit rationale, risk notes, and recommendation levels. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Naming suggestions can be mistaken for completed trademark, legal, cultural, or linguistic clearance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat trademark, legal, slang, dialect, and cultural checks as preliminary guidance only, and require formal review before adopting a name. <br>
Risk: The security scan notes that output may bias toward Chinese or mixed-language formatting. <br>
Mitigation: Use this skill when Chinese or mixed-language naming output is acceptable, or prefer an updated version when strict language matching is required. <br>
