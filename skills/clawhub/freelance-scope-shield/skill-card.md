## Description:

Helps freelancers document agreed scope, classify client requests, price change orders, track scope creep, and draft professional client responses and contract clauses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Freelancers and consultants use this skill to record contractual deliverables, assess new client requests, calculate change-order prices, and prepare written responses before accepting extra work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The local scope registry can contain client requests, names, rates, hours, and dispute history.

Mitigation: Protect the project directory and avoid entering highly sensitive legal or confidential client details unless appropriate safeguards are in place.
