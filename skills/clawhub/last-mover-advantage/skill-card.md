## Description:

Guides an agent through assessing whether a business can hold a durable market position by evaluating market endgames, longevity, and entry timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, strategists, and investors use this skill to assess whether a market's likely end state supports a durable position and whether entering early helps achieve it. It structures the analysis around an endgame, a durability audit, niche sequencing, and explicit stop rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategic conclusions about market endgames and long-term durability may be treated as certain despite limited evidence.

Mitigation: Support conclusions with case-specific evidence, apply the stop rule, and label uncertain outcomes as scenario-conditional or recommend cheap experiments.

Risk: Confidential business details included in reusable examples could be exposed.

Mitigation: Avoid placing confidential business details in reusable examples.
