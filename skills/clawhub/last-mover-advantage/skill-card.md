## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a last-mover endgame analysis to weigh durable market position against racing to enter a market first. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external strategists, founders, and operators use this skill to test whether a market-entry or AI feature strategy creates a durable 10-15 year position. It produces an Endgame Test with a durability audit, sequencing path, timing implication, confidence level, and re-test trigger. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill's strategic analysis can be mistaken for factual market data or a forecast. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as an advisory framework and validate market facts, assumptions, and timing decisions before acting. <br>
Risk: Users may apply the skill to confidential company plans. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid saving real confidential examples back into the skill unless that disclosure is intentional. <br>
