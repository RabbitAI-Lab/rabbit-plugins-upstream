## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A checklist can miss structural, electronic, or concealed vehicle damage.

Mitigation: Arrange an independent professional inspection before purchase; do not treat the checklist as a vehicle certification.

Risk: Pricing and transfer requirements may vary by place and change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm current local prices and legal transfer requirements before agreeing on payment or completing a sale.
