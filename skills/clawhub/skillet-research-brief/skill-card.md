## Description:

Use when the user asks for researched, sourced, evidence-backed, or current findings, especially for a recommendation or factual brief. Do not use for a single-page summary, ordinary codebase inspection, review of an existing technical post, or decision pressure-testing when the request already supplies all evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[escoffier-labs](https://clawhub.ai/user/escoffier-labs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and developers use this skill to answer scoped factual or decision questions with a concise, evidence-backed brief. It organizes research question boundaries, sources, claims, disagreements, and a fresh citation-support pass while keeping the workflow read-only.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External research queries may disclose information about private topics.

Mitigation: Set a clear research question and search limit, and avoid including confidential details in external queries.
