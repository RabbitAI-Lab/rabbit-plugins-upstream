## Use Case:

External users use this skill to analyze workplace, intimate relationship, negotiation, social, and family interactions. It produces structured interpretations of motives, risks, game stage, likely next moves, and practical response options.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can frame ordinary interpersonal situations as adversarial pressure-and-leverage problems.

Mitigation: Use it only when a hard-nosed strategy lens is deliberately desired, and ask the assistant to prioritize consent, de-escalation, lawful channels, cultural fit, and user autonomy.

Risk: Advice in intimate, workplace, family, or low-stakes contexts could become manipulative or escalatory.

Mitigation: Avoid using outputs to pressure, shame, threaten, or manipulate real people; treat suggested scripts as options to review and soften for the actual relationship context.

Risk: The skill may infer hidden motives from limited user-provided evidence.

Mitigation: Require concrete context, preserve uncertainty in conclusions, and treat motive analysis as a hypothesis rather than a diagnosis or factual finding.
