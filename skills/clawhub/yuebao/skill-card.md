## Description: <br>
Yuebao is an educational guide that helps agents explain Yu'ebao as a money-market fund, interpret yield metrics, plan redemptions around liquidity limits, and compare it with deposits or short-duration bond funds. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to correct misconceptions about Yu'ebao, explain money-market-fund risk and yield concepts, plan fund movement around redemption timing, and frame alternatives without recommending a specific product. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat educational discussion of Yu'ebao as financial advice or a product recommendation. <br>
Mitigation: Keep responses framed as general education, avoid yield predictions and specific fund recommendations, and direct users to qualified advice for personal financial decisions. <br>
Risk: Yu'ebao redemption limits, fund rules, yields, and regulatory details can change. <br>
Mitigation: Tell users to verify current limits, rules, and disclosures in Alipay or official fund materials before moving money. <br>
