## Description: <br>
Build a transactional HTML email that renders correctly across Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail, with a plain-text alternative and dark-mode support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to draft or edit Drin transactional email templates with HTML and plain-text bodies, cross-client layout rules, dark-mode handling, and safe template variables. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated transactional emails may include raw or unescaped template fields, incorrect links, missing variables, or recipient-facing copy that is not ready to send. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated emails before sending, sanitize any raw template fields, verify links and recipient-facing text, and preview templates to check missing variables. <br>
