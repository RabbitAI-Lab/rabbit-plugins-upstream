## Description:

Helps home-renovation practitioners assess floor plans and compare practical renovation options, dimensions, budgets, and construction steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Real-estate and home-renovation practitioners use this skill to draft floor-plan assessments and compare light, moderate, and extensive renovation ideas based on household needs and budget. It provides layout, storage, lighting, and ventilation guidance without replacing professional construction advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renovation drafts may be mistaken for approval of structural, load-bearing, gas, or fire-safety work.

Mitigation: Treat these suggestions as advisory only; confirm such changes with qualified professionals or property management before acting.
