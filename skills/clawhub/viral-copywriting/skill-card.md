## Use Case:

Marketing teams, creators, and agents use this skill to draft, structure, and review platform-specific social marketing copy, sales posts, engagement prompts, and campaign content. It is especially focused on Chinese content platforms such as Xiaohongshu, Toutiao, CSDN, WeChat Official Accounts, Zhihu, Douyin, and video channels.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may include legal, platform, or advertising compliance issues, especially around results claims, scarcity, health, finance, pricing, giveaways, or private-message lead generation.

Mitigation: Review generated copy for applicable law, platform policy, and advertising compliance before publishing.

Risk: Persuasive copy patterns may overstate product results or rely on unsupported urgency, authority, testimonials, price comparisons, or social proof.

Mitigation: Require substantiation for claims and rewrite unverified claims as softer, clearly qualified experience-based language.
