## Description:

Creates structured job descriptions from role details, with prioritized responsibilities, qualifications, reporting lines, and inclusive wording.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[petehuax](https://clawhub.ai/user/petehuax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters and hiring teams use this skill to turn role basics into a ready-to-review job description with clear duties, qualifications, and hiring details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated hiring language may be unsuitable for applicable hiring rules or contain exclusionary requirements.

Mitigation: Review the job description for legal and hiring-policy compliance before posting.

Risk: The default job description may be in Chinese when another language is needed.

Mitigation: Specify the preferred language in the request and check the draft before publishing.
