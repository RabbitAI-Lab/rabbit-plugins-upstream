# Example Prompts

## Backfill history

**User:** "Backfill the process so far and save it in the repo."

**Expected output:** a compact timeline summary that captures major decisions, changes, and why they mattered.

## Record a key decision

**User:** "We changed the result page structure. Record why."

**Expected output:** one process entry with problem, decision, change, result, and lesson.

## Decide whether to log

**User:** "This was just a tiny copy tweak. Should we record it?"

**Expected output:** a short recommendation that skips tiny edits unless they changed the direction.

## Create article material

**User:** "Turn this development into article-ready notes."

**Expected output:** a compact set of reusable angles, lessons, and tradeoffs.

## Rebuild the story

**User:** "Summarize how this project moved from messy iterations to a stable version."

**Expected output:** a narrative outline with the main turning points, tradeoffs, and outcomes.

## Maintain the log

**User:** "We finished another iteration. Add it to the process log."

**Expected output:** append a new entry in `docs/process/HISTORY.md` using the shared template.

## Capture a bug fix

**User:** "We fixed a parsing bug caused by malformed input. Record it."

**Expected output:** a short process note that explains the cause, the fix, and the verification result.

## Capture a design disagreement

**User:** "We resolved a disagreement about layout density. Note the decision."

**Expected output:** a process entry that shows the tradeoff, final decision, and what changed after the decision.
