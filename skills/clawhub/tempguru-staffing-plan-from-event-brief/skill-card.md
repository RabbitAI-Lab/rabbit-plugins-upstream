## Description:

Extracts staffing needs from event documents and builds a priced TempGuru staffing plan for events in the United States and Canada.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kissmyabs32](https://clawhub.ai/user/kissmyabs32)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Event planners and procurement teams use this skill to turn an event brief, run of show, or similar document into mapped staffing roles, headcounts, and a live planning estimate, then review a buyer-operated quote handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States and Canada

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event details may be sent to TempGuru during pricing without a separate warning or confirmation; the security review was partial because a referenced path was missing.

Mitigation: Review event documents for sensitive details before using the skill, decide whether sharing them with TempGuru is appropriate, and review the complete source before installation.
