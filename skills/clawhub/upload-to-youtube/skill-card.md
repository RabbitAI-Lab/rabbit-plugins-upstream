## Description:

Helps an agent upload or schedule a YouTube video or Short through PostWire, prepare its title and description, and return its link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[perufitlife](https://clawhub.ai/user/perufitlife)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and teams use this skill to prepare and, after confirmation, publish or schedule videos to a connected YouTube channel. It can also share the video to TikTok and Instagram Reels when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploading or scheduling can publish a video to the wrong audience or at the wrong time.

Mitigation: Review the title, description, channel, privacy, and schedule; upload only after explicit user approval.

Risk: Connecting a channel grants PostWire access, and exposure of an API key could permit unauthorized actions.

Mitigation: Review the account and connection setup before authorizing access, and keep the API key private.
