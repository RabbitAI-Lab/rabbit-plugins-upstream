## Description:

Maps technology areas and patent portfolios using Crawlora's Google Patents and USPTO search and detail tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and portfolio teams use this skill to build exploratory patent landscapes with documented search coverage, family groupings, assignee comparisons, and dated trends. It does not establish legal clearance or complete prior-art coverage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent search terms, assignee comparisons, and portfolio strategy are sent to Crawlora with an API key.

Mitigation: Use only when external processing is acceptable; avoid submitting sensitive strategy or confidential queries without authorization.

Risk: Incomplete search coverage or sampled families can make an exploratory landscape appear exhaustive or legally conclusive.

Mitigation: Record query scope, coverage, stopping rules, and missing evidence; seek qualified legal review for clearance or validity decisions.
