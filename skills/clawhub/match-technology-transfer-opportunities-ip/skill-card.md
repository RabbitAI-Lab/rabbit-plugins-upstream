## Description:

Evaluate a supplied technology or scoped technology portfolio, identify and rank evidence-backed potential licensees, acquirers, commercialization partners, or joint-development partners, assess transfer readiness and risks, and generate an auditable HTML decision-support report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External commercialization teams, technology owners, and IP or licensing professionals use this skill to evaluate supplied technologies or scoped portfolios, prioritize potential licensees, acquirers, commercialization partners, or joint-development partners, and prepare evidence-backed transfer-pathway reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External patent, company, market, procurement, or valuation lookups may expose confidential transfer materials or rely on sources the user has not approved.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit approval before using each external source, avoid confidential materials unless the user authorizes analysis, and record source IDs, dates, and limitations.

Risk: Recipient rankings, valuation discussion, or legal/IP observations may be mistaken for professional advice or authorization to contact organizations.

Mitigation: Present results as decision support and require human legal, commercial, and compliance review before valuation, freedom-to-operate, investment, outreach, or transaction decisions.

Risk: HTML report generation can create unwanted files or disclose sensitive analysis if written to an unintended path.

Mitigation: Write reports only to exact user-approved HTML paths, use separately approved backup paths, and avoid overwriting existing files unless explicitly approved.
