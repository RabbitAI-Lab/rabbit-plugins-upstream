## Description: <br>
Essential travel guide for visiting China, covering visas, mobile payment, transport, food, safety, hotels, flights, attractions, and itinerary planning for inbound tourists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to answer practical China travel questions, search hotels and flights, find attractions, and produce multi-day itinerary guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Travel questions and planning details are sent to a remote proxy and TripGenie. <br>
Mitigation: Do not enter passport numbers, account credentials, exact home addresses, or other highly sensitive personal information. <br>
Risk: Travel, booking, and price guidance may affect real-world travel decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm visa, entry, booking, price, and safety details with authoritative providers before travel or purchase. <br>
