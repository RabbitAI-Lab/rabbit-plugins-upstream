## Description:

Guides an agent to create and edit a 30-second Kling 4 action montage from three or four key stills, including prompt construction, generation, review, and trimming.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan, generate, review, and trim short Kling 4 action videos from reference stills through Varg.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference images and prompts are shared with Varg and its generation providers.

Mitigation: Use only media and prompts you are comfortable sharing with those providers.

Risk: Video generation incurs per-take charges.

Mitigation: Set a spending limit and draft at a lower resolution before generating a full-resolution take.
