## Description:

A Chinese-language grammar coach that helps students identify six common sentence-error types and, with consent, tracks recurring errors for targeted practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary and secondary school use this skill to diagnose Chinese sentence errors, practice corrections, and review recurring patterns when they consent to an error profile. Adults should supervise primary-school students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize crisis referrals and review privacy controls outside Mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Grammar-error profiles may contain information about minors and persist beyond a lesson.

Mitigation: Obtain consent before creating a profile; review memory, deletion, and pause controls, and supervise primary-school students.

Risk: Cross-skill sharing, parent visibility, or reminders could expose a student's learning history unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Review separate sharing, parent-visibility, and reminder permissions before enabling them; honor opt-outs.

Risk: Mainland China crisis referrals may not suit students in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize referral information for the student's region before deployment outside Mainland China.
