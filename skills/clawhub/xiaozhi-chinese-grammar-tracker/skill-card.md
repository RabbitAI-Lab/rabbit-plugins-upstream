## Description:

Helps Chinese-language students identify six common sentence-error types through guided questions and, with consent, track recurring errors and progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language students and their educators use this skill to practice spotting and correcting sentence errors. With consent, it tracks recurring error patterns and provides progress summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, minor-consent requirements, and emergency contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student's grammar profile could expose sensitive learning information if retained or shared without permission.

Mitigation: Obtain student or guardian consent as appropriate before long-term tracking; honor deletion and sharing choices and share only necessary details.

Risk: Mainland-China curriculum, consent assumptions, and crisis contacts may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Confirm local requirements and the student's location; adapt curriculum, consent practices, and emergency resources before deployment elsewhere.
