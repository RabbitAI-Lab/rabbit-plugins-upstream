## Description:

Coaches Chinese-language students through identifying and correcting sentence errors, with optional consent-based tracking of recurring mistakes and progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language students in upper primary, middle, and high school use this skill to recognize six common sentence-error categories, practice corrections, and review recurring mistakes when they consent to tracking. Younger students should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent practices before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tracking or sharing a minor's learning profile without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep tracking, parent sharing, cross-skill sharing, and reminders opt-in; review permissions with the student or guardian before sharing.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum or crisis resources may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize teaching assumptions, emergency contacts, and minor-consent rules before use outside Mainland China.

Risk: Incorrect grammar judgments or unsupported progress claims may mislead learners.

Mitigation: Check generated exercises and avoid claims about past progress unless reliable prior records are available.
