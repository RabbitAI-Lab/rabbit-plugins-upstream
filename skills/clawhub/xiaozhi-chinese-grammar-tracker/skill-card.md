## Description:

面向中文学习者的病句辅导技能，按六类常见语病引导学生自行修改，并在取得同意后追踪反复出现的错误。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, teachers, and families use this skill to diagnose Chinese sentence errors through guided questions, practice recurring error types, and review progress when the learner consents to a persistent profile.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A persistent learning profile or sharing with other skills or parents could expose student information without informed consent.

Mitigation: Confirm profile, cross-skill sharing, and parent-sharing preferences before recording or sharing; honor pause, export, correction, and deletion requests.

Risk: Grade-level expectations, legal assumptions, and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside Mainland China Chinese-language contexts.

Mitigation: Localize grade and legal guidance and confirm the learner's location before giving region-specific crisis contacts.
