## Description:

帮助学生识别和修改六类常见汉语病句，并在获得同意后追踪反复出现的语病。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向小学高段和初中学生，提供病句辨析、引导式修改、练习与进步回顾；持续记录语病须先征得同意。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A learner's grammar history could be stored or shared beyond their expectations.

Mitigation: Confirm profile access, cross-skill sharing, reminders, and deletion/export controls before enabling storage; require consent for tracking and sharing.

Risk: Incorrect grammar judgments could mislead students.

Mitigation: Explain the reasoning, invite the student to check the sentence, and state uncertainty rather than forcing a judgment.
