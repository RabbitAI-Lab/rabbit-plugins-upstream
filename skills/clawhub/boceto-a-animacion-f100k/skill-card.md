## Description:

Convierte un dibujo a mano o una frase de guion en prompts, cuadros clave y un clip animado vertical de motion graphics para acompanar un reel.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, editors, and social video teams use this skill to turn a sketch or script phrase into one short 9:16 animated clip. It reads the sketch as a composition plan, chooses an animation style, prepares first-frame, last-frame, and motion prompts, and can guide Higgsfield generation after explicit credit approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow sends user sketches to Higgsfield and can spend account credits.

Mitigation: Use only when users are comfortable sharing the sketch with Higgsfield, and require explicit approval after showing current cost and balance before generation.

Risk: Users may expect server-side split or reveal assembly even though this package delivers the clip and supporting files.

Mitigation: Clarify that split and reveal edits are assembled in CapCut or Edits, and provide the generated clip, frames, prompts, and brief assembly guidance.
