## Description:

ljh-maidian reviews one or more e-commerce selling-point statements with six checks and returns pass/reject findings with concrete fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[handsomeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/handsomeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, e-commerce operators, and brand teams use this skill to test already written selling-point copy for uniqueness, defensibility, audience fit, and concrete next-step revisions. It is not intended to decide product viability or replace advertising, platform, or legal compliance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create local onboarding state, read and write a local brand archive, and save full deliverables, which may persist confidential brand, product, or campaign strategy.

Mitigation: Review the workspace before and after use, avoid entering confidential strategy unless the storage location is approved, and delete or restrict generated archive files when they are no longer needed.

Risk: The skill displays personal WeChat contacts for an off-platform user community.

Mitigation: Treat the contact information as third-party promotional content and use only approved support or procurement channels for business decisions.

Risk: A passed selling-point review does not establish advertising-law, platform-review, certification, or category-qualification compliance.

Mitigation: Run separate legal, platform, and claims-substantiation review before publishing or using the selling point in paid commerce.
