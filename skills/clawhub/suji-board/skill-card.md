## Description: <br>
Suji Board is a zero-dependency, single-file front-end web app for collecting fragmented text, organizing notes and documents locally, and exporting structured notes to Word-compatible .docx files. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and developers use this skill to collect pasted text, organize notes and uploaded documents in the browser, and export a Word-compatible document without account sign-in or upload. Developers can also distribute or package the included single-file HTML application. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive pasted content, uploaded documents, or images may remain in browser-local storage until cleared. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only on trusted devices and clear saved entries, drafts, trash, folders, and browser storage when the information should no longer persist. <br>
Risk: Users may treat local storage as a managed backup even though it is browser-specific and user-controlled. <br>
Mitigation: Export needed documents deliberately and avoid relying on the browser page as the sole record for important work. <br>
