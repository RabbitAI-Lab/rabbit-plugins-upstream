## Description:

Explains the planned Judge.me for Shopify integration through Agiexa and, with explicit permission, helps users join its launch waitlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants and OpenClaw users can use this preview to understand Agiexa's planned Judge.me integration, review its future workflows, and request launch updates with explicit consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the preview for a working connection to Shopify or Judge.me.

Mitigation: Clarify that the integration is planned for November 2026 and this skill cannot access stores or reviews today.

Risk: Waitlist submission shares the user's email and any optional role-related link or message with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Review the exact details and consent statement with the user, and submit only after explicit confirmation.
