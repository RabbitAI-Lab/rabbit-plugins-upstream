## Description:

Explains the planned Judge.me integration for Shopify through Agiexa, its review-management use cases, and how to join the launch waitlist with explicit permission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Shopify merchants and prospective Agiexa users can learn what the planned Judge.me integration is expected to support, explore review-management use cases, and request launch updates through the waitlist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Joining the waitlist shares the user's confirmed signup details with Agiexa.

Mitigation: Obtain explicit permission before submission, confirm the required details with the user, and send only the fields they provide.
