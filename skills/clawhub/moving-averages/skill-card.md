## Description: <br>
Helps agents answer educational market-analysis questions about moving averages for trend following, moving-average type and period selection, MA combinations, the Donchian four-week breakout rule, and limitations such as lag and poor performance in range-bound markets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and market-analysis practitioners use this skill to help an agent explain moving-average trend-following methods, choose MA periods or combinations, and describe when moving-average signals should not be used. The skill is educational guidance and should not be treated as financial advice or an automated trade instruction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat educational moving-average discussion as financial advice or an instruction to place trades. <br>
Mitigation: Frame outputs as educational market-analysis guidance, avoid trade directives, and recommend independent review and risk controls. <br>
Risk: Moving-average signals can lag and can produce false signals or repeated losses in range-bound markets. <br>
Mitigation: State the lag and range-bound limitations when relevant, and suggest confirming trend conditions before applying moving-average systems. <br>
