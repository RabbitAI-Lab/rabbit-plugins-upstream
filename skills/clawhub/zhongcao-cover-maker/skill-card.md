## Description:

Creates or refines vertical 3:4 Xiaohongshu cover images from a photo, topic idea, or accepted draft, with optional Xiaohongshu research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and marketers use the skill to create or refine Xiaohongshu cover and post images from photos, topic ideas, or accepted drafts. They can optionally look up Xiaohongshu notes and comments to inform the design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: This version requests audio, video, and voice-management permissions alongside image-generation access.

Mitigation: Prefer a version offering image-only authorization; review the requested permissions before connecting.

Risk: Connecting shares the credential with other Beatra skills; selected photos and prompts are processed remotely, and installation analytics are sent automatically.

Mitigation: Review the material before uploading or connecting, and revoke access through the Beatra Console when finished.

Risk: Image generation and optional Xiaohongshu lookups can incur paid charges.

Mitigation: Review the request and its cost before approving each paid operation.
