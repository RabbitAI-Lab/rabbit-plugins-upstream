## Description: <br>
Adapt explanations to a first-principles learner: start from step zero, use analogies, validate their guesses, and apply their preferred language defaults. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[sinanxrasheed](https://clawhub.ai/user/sinanxrasheed) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to have an agent explain concepts from first principles, address learner guesses directly, use careful analogies, and keep explanations short and structured. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The adapter can bias responses toward short, step-by-step, analogy-heavy explanations even when a different style is needed. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to use a different explanation style, level of detail, or tone for tasks that need a different presentation. <br>
Risk: The skill includes an evolution log instruction that could record learner preferences when explicitly requested. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid asking the agent to record personal learning notes unless that tracking is intended. <br>
