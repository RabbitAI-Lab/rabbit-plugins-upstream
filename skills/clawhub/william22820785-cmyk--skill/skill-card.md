## Description:

A traditional Chinese astrology skill that uses BaZi and Zi Wei Dou Shu for life readings, and Liu Yao and Qi Men Dun Jia to interpret specific questions and events.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill for traditional Chinese astrology readings about life themes or a specific event. It gathers relevant details, calculates a chart or divination, and explains the interpretation with its limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Consultations involve sensitive birth details and personal questions, while the AI conversation may be processed by its host platform.

Mitigation: Use a private session directory, share details cautiously, and delete consultation files when no longer needed.

Risk: Traditional readings may be mistaken for reliable medical, legal, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat readings as cultural content and consult qualified professionals for medical, legal, or investment decisions.
