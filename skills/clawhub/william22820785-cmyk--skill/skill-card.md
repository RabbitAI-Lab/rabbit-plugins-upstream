## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal birth details and readings may be saved locally and remain after the session expires; chat records, backups, and exported files are outside the cleanup scope.

Mitigation: Before collecting personal details, show the actual save location and retention period, obtain explicit consent, use the documented session manager, and explain that cleanup must be previewed and approved.

Risk: Traditional readings have accuracy limitations and could be mistaken for reliable grounds for consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Present readings as traditional interpretation, not as a basis for consequential decisions; keep conditions and uncertainty clear.

Risk: The optional Beijing event-time mode has an inconsistency.

Mitigation: Prefer the default local event-time mode, as directed by the security guidance.
