## Description: <br>
Provides AI livestream compliance guidance, content-labeling checks, prohibited-word filtering workflows, and script templates for live commerce. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, livestream operators, and commerce teams use this skill to produce AI/digital-human livestream compliance checklists, labeling guidance, prohibited-word controls, and reviewed sales-script templates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China-focused guidance; reviewer should confirm whether broader deployment is appropriate. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may steer an agent toward deceptive or manipulative live-commerce sales scripts. <br>
Mitigation: Review and remove conflicting sales-script sections, or constrain generation to verified, documented, platform-approved claims. <br>
Risk: The skill includes patterns involving stock counts, expert credentials, audience behavior, social proof, health or body-effect claims, and urgency language. <br>
Mitigation: Allow those claims only when they are true, documented, and approved for the target platform and product category. <br>
