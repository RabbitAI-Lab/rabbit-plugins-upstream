## Description: <br>
Provides tax guidance, calculations, self-check workflows, and risk warnings for equity transfers, family ownership structures, state-owned enterprise mixed-ownership reform, VIE/red-chip structures, and equity governance scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive tax, ownership, restructuring, or family-governance inputs may be sent to cloud or search services. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only after approving that data flow and avoid entering secrets or identifying details unless the organization has approved the service. <br>
Risk: The skill may set up persistent MCP integrations, store local API-key material, and write local logs. <br>
Mitigation: Review local MCP configuration changes and local API-key/log storage before installation or execution. <br>
Risk: Tax guidance can be jurisdiction-specific and may require current professional confirmation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as assistance for review and confirm material transactions with qualified tax professionals or the relevant tax authority. <br>
