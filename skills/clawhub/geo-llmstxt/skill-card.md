## Description:

Analyzes and generates llms.txt files -- the emerging standard for helping AI systems understand website structure and content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, site owners, and content teams use this skill to validate existing llms.txt files or generate new ones by crawling public website pages and selecting authoritative site content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may crawl public website pages and select page descriptions that are incomplete, stale, or misprioritized.

Mitigation: Review selected pages, key facts, URLs, and descriptions before publishing generated llms.txt content.

Risk: Generated llms.txt files can shape how AI systems summarize a site, so incorrect facts or contact details may be amplified.

Mitigation: Validate generated content against authoritative site sources and approve it before deployment.
