## Description: <br>
Register, track, and validate state observations against declared constraints without assuming universal truth or enforcing physical laws. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to reason about CS-RV constraint-observation workflows, including state registration, constraint declaration, violation recording, projection, audit-trace export, and migration from metaphorical Horus terminology to formal protocol language. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Exported traces may contain sensitive observations, observer identifiers, constraints, violations, and metadata. <br>
Mitigation: Treat traces as sensitive, review metadata before sharing, and redact or restrict exported audit data when needed. <br>
Risk: Trace checksum or completeness claims may not be sufficient assurance on their own. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the implementation and exported trace integrity before relying on audit completeness or checksum guarantees. <br>
