## Use Case:

Legal practitioners and users preparing China mainland inheritance-dispute matters use this skill to organize case facts, identify evidence gaps, build evidence and cross-examination matrices, draft hearing questions, and note legal authority that requires official verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive inheritance, family, property, medical, and litigation facts.

Mitigation: Use only appropriate case materials, limit unnecessary personal data, and review outputs within the confidentiality and data-handling requirements that apply to the matter.

Risk: The skill provides legal drafting and analysis support, not a final legal opinion.

Mitigation: Have a qualified lawyer review original evidence, current law, jurisdictional fit, and litigation strategy before use.

Risk: OCR reference material can contain errors and must not be treated as verified statutory text.

Mitigation: Independently verify legal authorities, dates, amounts, case numbers, and proper nouns against official sources before relying on them.
