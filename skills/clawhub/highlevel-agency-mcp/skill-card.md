Manage HighLevel funnels, websites, domains, forms, workflows, call screening, and sub-account settings from your AI assistant. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

## Use Case:

Agency operators and administrators use the skill to inspect and manage HighLevel marketing pages, domains, workflows, forms, and sub-account settings through an AI assistant. It supports connection checks and previews before account changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The hosted provider's permissions and data handling affect connected HighLevel accounts.

Mitigation: Review provider permissions and data handling before connecting; begin read-only or in a sandbox.

Risk: Changes to live pages, workflows, access, and sub-account settings can affect client operations.

Mitigation: Confirm the target sub-account and its sandbox or production status, preview changes, follow active write restrictions, and verify saved results.
