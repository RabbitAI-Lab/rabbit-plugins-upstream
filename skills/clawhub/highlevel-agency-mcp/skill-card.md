## Description:

Automate GoHighLevel (GHL) funnel and website page layouts, client domain DNS & SSL onboarding, multi-step forms, workflow graphs, LeadShield contact form spam & B2B solicitation blockers, spam-shield IVRs, and sub-account snapshot diffs using HighLevel Agency & Builder MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agency operators, developers, and client-account administrators use this skill to inspect and manage GoHighLevel websites, funnels, domains, forms, workflows, telephony settings, staff access, and sub-account snapshots. It also supports read-only audits and previews before changes are applied.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Client account data is accessed through an external provider whose privacy and enforcement practices were not verified.

Mitigation: Check the provider’s privacy practices before connecting client accounts, and begin with read-only access.

Risk: Changes to live pages, workflows, domains, or staff permissions may have unintended effects.

Mitigation: Confirm the target sub-account and sandbox or production context, then review the preview before publishing or changing permissions.
