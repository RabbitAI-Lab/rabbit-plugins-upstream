## Description:

Monitors early signs of plant wilting using hyperspectral imaging and computer vision to support precision irrigation and disease control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Growers and plant health teams can submit plant images or videos for early wilting analysis, review structured results and recommendations, and retrieve previous monitoring reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The export-only option can still upload files, and processing may involve a cloud service.

Mitigation: Do not use export-only as a privacy control. Wait for fixes before using sensitive media, confirm the provider's account, charges, and retention terms, and do not rely on --api-url to redirect processing.

Risk: Account credentials or tokens may be stored locally without encryption.

Mitigation: Wait for fixes to local token storage before using sensitive credentials, and limit access to any local credential files.
