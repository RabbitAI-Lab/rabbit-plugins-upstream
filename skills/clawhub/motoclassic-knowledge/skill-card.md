## Description:

Use MotoClassic Knowledge to inspect an approved motorcycle research task, research sourced findings, clarify engine uncertainty, pause or resume work, or check local import. Also use for requests about this connection’s private-data, manual and mechanical-safety limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External MotoClassic users use this skill to review their approved motorcycle research task, gather sourced findings, clarify uncertainty, save checkpoints, or check local import status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private identifiers included in research requests could expose personal information.

Mitigation: Keep private identifiers out of requests and do not provide garage exports or full manuals.

Risk: Research findings could be mistaken for a diagnosis or safety certification.

Mitigation: Have safety-critical conclusions checked by a qualified mechanic.
