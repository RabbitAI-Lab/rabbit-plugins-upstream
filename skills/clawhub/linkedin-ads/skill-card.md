## Description:

Run LinkedIn advertising campaigns for B2B across Sponsored Content, Message Ads, Document Ads, and Lead Gen Forms, with guidance on professional targeting, bidding strategy, and optimization for high-LTV B2B products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External B2B marketers, founders, and growth teams use this skill to decide when LinkedIn Ads are appropriate, choose targeting and ad formats, structure funnel campaigns, and tune bids for high-LTV B2B offers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketing advice may rely on LinkedIn pricing, policy, targeting, or ad-format details that change over time.

Mitigation: Verify LinkedIn pricing, policy, and ad-format claims against current LinkedIn documentation before spending budget.

Risk: Generic campaign recommendations may be a poor fit for low-ACV products, narrow audiences, or offers that do not need professional-context targeting.

Mitigation: Ask for product, ACV, ICP, funnel stage, audience size, budget, and objective before advising on LinkedIn Ads spend.
