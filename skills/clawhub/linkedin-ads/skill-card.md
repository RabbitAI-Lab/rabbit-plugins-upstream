## Description:

Create, edit, and report on LinkedIn ads from an AI agent through the AdKit MCP connector, with every change saved as a draft for user approval before it goes live.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jeannen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeannen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Advertisers and marketing teams use this skill to manage LinkedIn campaign groups, campaigns, ads, audience targeting, and reporting through AdKit. It is intended for carrying out requested ad operations, not for ad strategy or creative advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ad changes can affect a LinkedIn advertising account and its spend when published.

Mitigation: Review the saved draft and obtain the user's approval before publishing changes.
