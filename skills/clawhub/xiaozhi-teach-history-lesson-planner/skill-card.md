## Description:

Helps Chinese-language middle and high school history teachers draft lesson plans centered on historical sources, chronology, core competencies, and scaffolded questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

History teachers draft Chinese K12 lesson plans, source-selection notes, and question chains adapted to their class and curriculum. Teachers review and approve materials and any class-record updates before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis-referral resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inaccurate historical claims or unverifiable source citations could enter teaching materials.

Mitigation: Have teachers verify historical facts and citations against authoritative sources before classroom use.

Risk: Class records or lesson-plan changes could expose student information or be saved without review.

Mitigation: Use class-level or pseudonymous data and require teacher approval before any class-data writeback.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and crisis-referral guidance may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum wording, privacy assumptions, and referral resources before student use outside mainland China.
