## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-organized historical sources or lesson content may contain inaccurate facts or unreliable citations.

Mitigation: Teachers should check historical facts and original source attribution before adding materials to lessons or presenting them to students.

Risk: Classroom inputs could expose students' personal or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use class-level details, pseudonyms, or seat numbers; do not enter real student names, contact details, family details, or health information.
