## Description:

帮助中国大陆初高中历史教师设计以时空框架和史料探究为主线的课时、单元、复习及讲评课教案。

## Use Case:

初高中历史教师用它拟定单元与课时教案、史料提问链，以及复习课和试卷讲评课的课堂安排；教师负责核对史实、素材和最终教案。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, privacy settings, and crisis-referral contacts may not fit regions outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use for mainland-China history teaching only unless the operator localizes those assumptions and contacts.

Risk: Draft plans or AI-organized historical sources may contain errors or unverified citations.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check historical facts, source attribution, and the completed plan before use.

Risk: Saved lesson-plan entries could contain inaccurate or unnecessarily identifying classroom data.

Mitigation: Have teachers confirm saved entries and use class-level or pseudonymous information rather than student identifiers.
