## Description:

帮助初高中历史教师设计以核心素养为目标、时空框架为基础、史料探究为主线的教案、单元规划与提问链。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中历史教师用它拟定新授课、复习课和考后讲评课教案，组织单元内容、史料选择和逐级提问；教师须核对史实、出处并审定课堂材料。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, legal references and crisis-help channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inaccurate historical claims or unverified source attributions may reach students.

Mitigation: Have teachers verify historical facts and original source citations before using or storing lesson materials.

Risk: Classroom records or draft inputs may expose student identities or unreleased exam materials.

Mitigation: Do not enter real student names or unreleased exam content; have teachers confirm any stored lesson-plan entries.

Risk: Chinese curriculum assumptions, legal references and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, applicable rules and crisis-help channels before using the skill elsewhere.
