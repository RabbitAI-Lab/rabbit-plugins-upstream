## Description:

Helps Chinese middle- and high-school history teachers draft competency-focused lesson plans, unit outlines, source-based activities, and review lessons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

History teachers in mainland China's middle and high schools use this skill to prepare curriculum-aligned lessons and source-based classroom activities. Teachers review historical sources, exercises, and exam-review plans before classroom use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-organized historical sources or generated exercises may contain errors or unverified citations.

Mitigation: Teachers verify facts, source citations, and exercises against the curriculum and original materials before use.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency-contact, and privacy assumptions may not fit other regions or languages.

Mitigation: Use primarily for mainland Chinese K12 history lessons; localize those assumptions before use elsewhere.

Risk: Student information or unfinished exam materials could be exposed if supplied to the skill.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or seat numbers rather than personal details, and do not provide exam questions before all sittings finish.
