## Description:

为中国大陆初高中历史教师起草以时空框架、史料探究和历史学科核心素养为主线的教案、单元设计与提问链。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

中国大陆初高中历史教师用它设计课程目标、史料探究活动及分层提问，并根据班级情况调整课时安排。教案和史料均为待教师核对的草稿。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；在其他地区使用前须核对课程、隐私要求及当地求助渠道。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-organized historical sources or lesson content may be inaccurate or lack reliable attribution.

Mitigation: Teachers check facts and original source citations against textbooks or authoritative sources before teaching.

Risk: Classroom records could expose identifiable student information.

Mitigation: Use class identifiers, pseudonyms or seat numbers; omit student names, contact details, family information and health information.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions or crisis-referral numbers may not apply outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, privacy practices and emergency contacts before using the skill elsewhere; teachers review all outputs.
