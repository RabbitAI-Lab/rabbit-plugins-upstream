## Description:

## Use Case:

初高中历史教师用它拟定课时和单元教案、筛选可信史料并设计循序渐进的课堂提问；教师须核实史实、出处并审定教学内容。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; review local education, crisis-support, and minor-data requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent class records may expose minors' personal information.

Mitigation: Use class identifiers or pseudonyms, keep privacy controls enabled, and require teacher confirmation before saving records.

Risk: Unverified historical sources or AI-drafted interpretations may mislead students.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check original sources and historical claims against the curriculum and approved textbooks before use.

Risk: Education, crisis-support and minor-data practices may not transfer safely outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Review local requirements and adapt referral information before deploying elsewhere.
