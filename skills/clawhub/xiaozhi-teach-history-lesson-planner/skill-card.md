## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified source material or historical claims could mislead students.

Mitigation: Check historical facts and source citations against authoritative materials before classroom use; have the teacher approve all student-facing content.

Risk: Class records could expose student personal information.

Mitigation: Use class identifiers, pseudonyms or seat numbers rather than real names; retain teacher confirmation and pause, delete, export and sharing controls.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, privacy defaults and crisis contacts may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, legal/privacy requirements and emergency contacts before deployment in another region or language.
