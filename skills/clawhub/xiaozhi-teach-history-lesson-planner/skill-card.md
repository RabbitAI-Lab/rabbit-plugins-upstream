## Description:

帮助中国初高中历史教师设计以核心素养为目标、时空框架为起点、史料探究为主线的教案及递进提问链。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中历史教师用它规划单元与课时、精选并核对史料、设计有依据的课堂提问和分层活动。教案仅供教师审定后用于教学，不用于历史命题或其他学科教案。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, privacy, and crisis-help guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated lesson plans, historical sources, or classroom exercises may contain errors.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check historical facts, source citations, exercises, and the completed plan before using them with students.

Risk: Curriculum, privacy, and crisis-help advice may not fit settings outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt those elements to the local jurisdiction and school procedures before deployment elsewhere.
