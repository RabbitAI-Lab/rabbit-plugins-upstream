## Description:

历史教案设计 helps Chinese K12 history teachers draft curriculum-aligned history lesson plans built around chronology, historical sources, question chains, and core competencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Chinese mainland middle and high school history contexts use this skill to draft lesson frameworks, source-selection notes, question chains, board plans, and pending classWorkspace lesson-plan entries. The drafts support teacher-led planning and review rather than replacing teacher judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland; localize curriculum labels, crisis resources, and schema values before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is specialized for Chinese-language, China-mainland K12 history lesson-planning contexts.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum labels, crisis resources, and schema values before using it in other regions or education systems.

Risk: AI-organized historical sources or lesson drafts may contain inaccurate sources, citations, or interpretations.

Mitigation: Teachers should review generated lesson plans and verify historical sources before classroom use.

Risk: Lesson planning may involve classroom data about minors.

Mitigation: Use class, alias, or seat identifiers rather than real names or sensitive personal details, and keep generated lesson-plan records teacher-confirmed.
