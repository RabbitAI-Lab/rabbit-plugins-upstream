## Description:

Provides Tamil-language guidance for PowPow account registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation without handling registration or payment for the user.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tamil-speaking PowPow users can learn how to register, resolve common sign-up problems, and activate their account through the official subscription page. Users complete all account and payment actions themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account or payment details could be exposed if shared in chat or entered on an untrusted page.

Mitigation: Keep passwords, verification codes, and payment details on the official PowPow site or app; do not request them in chat.

Risk: Subscription charges or renewal terms may be misunderstood.

Mitigation: Check the current subscription cost and renewal terms on the official checkout page before paying.
