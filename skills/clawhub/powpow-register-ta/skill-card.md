## Description:

Helps Tamil-speaking users understand PowPow, create a PowPow account, troubleshoot registration issues, and activate the account through subscription guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External PowPow users use this skill for Tamil-language onboarding from product introduction through registration, troubleshooting, and subscription activation. The agent provides guidance and links while the user completes account, verification, and payment steps directly on PowPow pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while asking for onboarding help.

Mitigation: Keep account creation, verification, subscription, and payment on PowPow's own pages and remind users not to share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.

Risk: Account activation can remain pending until the applicable subscription or payment flow completes.

Mitigation: Guide users to the official subscription page and explain the relevant PayPal, WeChat, or Huawei activation path without handling payment details.
