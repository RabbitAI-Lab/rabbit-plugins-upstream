## Description:

Conduct exhaustive multi-source investigation with methodology tracking, source evaluation, and iterative depth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and analysts use this skill when a question requires structured, multi-source investigation rather than a quick lookup. It guides scoping, search, source evaluation, iterative deepening, synthesis, and reporting with methodology tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries or source notes may expose sensitive private details when investigating external sources.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive private information in search queries or research logs, and scope sensitive investigations before using external sources.

Risk: Open-ended investigations can exceed the user's intended time or depth budget.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested depth and stop condition before starting, then document why research stopped.

Risk: Weak, biased, outdated, or single-source evidence can lead to misleading conclusions.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source hierarchy, corroboration checks, credibility scoring, and caveat sections to weight claims and disclose uncertainty.
