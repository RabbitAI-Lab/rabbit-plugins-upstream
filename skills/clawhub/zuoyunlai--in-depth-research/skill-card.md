This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, analysts, and researchers use this skill to run structured multi-source investigations, evaluate source quality, synthesize findings, and report confidence, caveats, gaps, and methodology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research output can be misleading if citations, confidence labels, or source-quality notes are accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review final citations, credibility notes, confidence levels, caveats, and gaps before acting on findings.

Risk: Sensitive or high-stakes topics may require stronger evidence than informal or secondary sources provide.

Mitigation: Prefer primary sources, corroborate important claims, and route domain-specific decisions to qualified reviewers.
