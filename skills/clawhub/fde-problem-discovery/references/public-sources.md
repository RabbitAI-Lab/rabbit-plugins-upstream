# Public method sources

- [GOV.UK: How the discovery phase works](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-discovery-phase-works): Understand the users, context, constraints, opportunities and success measures, don’t start building during the discovery phase.
- [GOV.UK: User research in the discovery phase](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/user-research-in-discovery): Research different users, end-to-end journeys, existing channels, barriers and support needs.
- [Atlassian Project Poster](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/project-poster): Explicitly document issues, assumptions, risks, scope, and success signals and update with feedback.
- [OpenAI: Practical Guide to Building Agents](https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-ai-agents/): Prioritize workflows that require complex judgments, unstructured information, or difficult-to-maintain rules, and do not increase agent complexity for simple tasks.

This skill translates these disclosure principles into FDE field discovery rules; its original template text is not copied.
