## Description:

FDE Problem Discovery turns customer interviews, frontline observations, tickets, meeting notes, and business data into evidence-backed problem-discovery packages for uncovering needs, reconstructing workflows, designing interview guides, validating problems, and screening POC candidates without committing to a solution, PRD, or prototype.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external delivery teams, and consultants use this skill to turn customer interviews, observations, tickets, meeting notes, and business data into evidence-backed problem-discovery packages. It helps reconstruct real workflows, design interview guides, screen POC candidates, and decide whether to proceed, gather more evidence, or stop.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview recordings, participant data, customer tickets, and business records may include private or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Apply the user's privacy rules, minimize sensitive inputs, and de-identify customer materials before using them for discovery analysis.

Risk: Discovery outputs could be mistaken for a PRD, implementation plan, or committed delivery scope.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as discovery notes and require human confirmation before issue prioritization, POC entry, scope, schedule, pricing, or implementation decisions.

Risk: Sales narratives or customer-suggested features may be treated as established user facts.

Mitigation: Require behavioral evidence, observations, tickets, logs, samples, or accountable owner confirmation before using a claim to justify a POC candidate.
