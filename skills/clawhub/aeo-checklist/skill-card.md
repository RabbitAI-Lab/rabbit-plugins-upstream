## Description:

Audit a website for AI search readiness, then create answer, FAQ, and schema drafts for owner review, for any AI agent. Use when a user asks to check or improve how AI search tools read, understand, and quote a website.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Website owners and the agents helping them use this skill to assess AI search readiness and prepare evidence-based content and configuration drafts for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft answers, FAQs, or structured data could include inaccurate business information if unconfirmed details are treated as facts.

Mitigation: Use confirmed facts only, mark unknown details for owner confirmation, and have the owner review each draft before publication.

Risk: Changing crawler directives could conflict with the site owner's intended access policy.

Mitigation: Present allow and block choices for AI crawlers and require the owner to choose and approve any live change.
