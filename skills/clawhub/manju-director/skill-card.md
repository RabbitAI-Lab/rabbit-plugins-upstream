## Description: <br>
漫剧总控导演Agent helps agents manage vertical comic production by issuing storyboard tasks, checking script, art, animation, and editing outputs, and enforcing concise quality standards. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, production teams, and workflow agents use this skill to coordinate short vertical comic production, review storyboard and downstream creative outputs, and return concrete revision guidance when quality checks fail. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generic quality-review language may cause the skill to activate in broader creative-review contexts than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke it with vertical comic production context or explicit manju-director trigger terms, and confirm the requested review stage before applying its guidance. <br>
Risk: The skill provides creative workflow guidance that could approve weak story, art, animation, or editing outputs if source requirements are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review its checklist results against the project brief, target audience, and production standards before accepting final outputs. <br>
