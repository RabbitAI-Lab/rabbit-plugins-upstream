## Description:

Routes unfinished tasks, cross-channel requests, and personal or work context into reviewable handoffs using approved tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to resume interrupted tasks, organize requests from multiple channels, and keep personal and company information separate while tracking deliverables and approvals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal or company information could be shared across the wrong channel or uploaded beyond the approved scope.

Mitigation: Separate identities and audiences, minimize sensitive materials, and confirm the permitted upload scope before sharing.

Risk: Model use or external actions could incur charges or change business data without informed approval.

Mitigation: Confirm current pricing and budget before paid work; obtain separate explicit approval for sending, payment, deletion, publication, upload, or business-system changes.

Risk: Retrying an unfinished task with an unknown result could duplicate a payment, message, or write.

Mitigation: Check the original task status and receipt before retrying; preserve unknown outcomes for review.
