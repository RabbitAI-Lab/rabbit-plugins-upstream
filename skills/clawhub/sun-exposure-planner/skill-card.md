## Description:

Use when planning safe sun exposure: how long before burning at your destination (Fitzpatrick skin type + UV index), vitamin D synthesis windows (when UV ≥ 3), shadow-ratio timing, and sunscreen reapplication math for beach/hiking/sports days.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People planning beach, hiking, sports, or travel activities use this skill to estimate sunburn risk and timing from UV conditions, skin type, and environmental factors. It provides planning guidance, not diagnosis or medical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sun and sunscreen estimates may be mistaken for a guarantee of safety or used to justify longer exposure.

Mitigation: Treat results as rough estimates, check current local UV forecasts, and do not use SPF calculations to extend sun exposure. For vitamin D, follow clinical guidance; the cited American Academy of Dermatology advises against intentional UV exposure to obtain vitamin D.
