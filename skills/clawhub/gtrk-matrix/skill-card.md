## Description:

Coordinates gtrk matrix workflows to find and assess B-roll, music, and images, then place editable candidate tracks in a video project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitruck](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitruck)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video editors and production agents use this skill to search and assess media, then arrange editable B-roll candidate tracks, music, or images in a video project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media or derived images may be processed in the cloud, and cloud steps can incur charges.

Mitigation: Check that the media is suitable for cloud processing and review estimated charges; limit indexing to intended media folders and inspect candidate tracks.

Risk: Forced replacement can permanently remove prior edits.

Mitigation: Understand the effect of forced replacement before using it, and review candidate tracks at the provided user checkpoints.
