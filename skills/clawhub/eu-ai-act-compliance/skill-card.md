## Description: <br>
Classify HR AI systems by EU AI Act risk level, provide compliance gap analysis, and recommend remediation steps for recruitment and workforce planning tools. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[rochyroch](https://clawhub.ai/user/rochyroch) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
HR professionals, compliance officers, and organizations use this skill to classify HR and recruitment AI systems, identify EU AI Act and GDPR compliance gaps, and plan remediation with Irish and UK context where relevant. <br>

Global <br>
