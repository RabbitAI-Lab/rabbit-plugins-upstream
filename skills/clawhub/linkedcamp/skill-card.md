## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Messages, public posts, deletions, and automated replies can affect real people or remove content.

Mitigation: Review the intended account, recipients, and content, and explicitly approve these actions before execution.

Risk: Activated campaigns and agents can continue operating; confirmations depend on assistant instruction-following rather than enforced approval dialogs.

Mitigation: Confirm campaign and agent activation deliberately, then monitor active runs and stop them when they are no longer intended.
