## Description: <br>
Writes Instagram/Facebook Stories as interactive frame sequences using the FRAME framework, including frame copy, sticker copy, native publishing handoff notes, and guidance to avoid fabricated metrics or engagement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, social media teams, and marketing agents use this skill to draft Instagram/Facebook Story sequences that deepen relationships with existing followers through completion, replies, sticker taps, and one clear action. The skill helps prepare base frames, sticker placement copy, highlight plans, DM reply drafts, and handoff notes for native in-app actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Story guidance could be used to fabricate countdowns, poll results, metrics, or engagement-bait. <br>
Mitigation: Use only real deadlines and native Insights, keep claims honest, and do not invent metrics or interactions. <br>
Risk: Interactive stickers and Story replies could be mistaken for actions the agent or scheduler can perform automatically. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill output as draft content and handoff guidance: WoopSocial may publish base frames where supported, while a human adds native stickers and sends DM replies in-app. <br>
