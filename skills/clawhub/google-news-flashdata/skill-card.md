## Description:

Search Google News with FlashData for article headlines, publishers, publication labels and links. Use for topic research, company news, news search by country or language, and preparing news digests. Requires a FlashData API key and paid Credits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to search news about a topic, company, or event and prepare sourced briefings or digests with article links, publishers, and publication labels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Before using the skill, confirm that connecting to FlashData and spending Credits for the requested queries is acceptable.

Mitigation: Review the available skill files and references before installation or use.
