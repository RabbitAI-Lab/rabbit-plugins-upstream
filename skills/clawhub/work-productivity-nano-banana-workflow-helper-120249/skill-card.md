## Use Case: <br>
External agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn Nano Banana Pro-style productivity needs into actionable plans, reusable workflows, checklists, analysis, code changes, or decision support. It is intended for local-hardware-friendly workflow assistance rather than model training or cloud-only execution. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger keywords and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate for unrelated requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review routing behavior after installation and use the skill when Nano Banana Pro workflow-planning help is specifically desired. <br>
Risk: Workflow advice could be applied without checking whether it matches the user's actual constraints or success criteria. <br>
Mitigation: Restate the desired outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing the workflow, then include a verification note. <br>
