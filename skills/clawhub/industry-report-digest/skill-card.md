## Description:

Summarizes long industry reports into an executive brief with sourced metrics, trend drivers, opportunities, risks, and uncertainties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[petehuax](https://clawhub.ai/user/petehuax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and decision-makers use this skill to turn lengthy industry reports, market research, and analyst PDFs into concise, source-traceable briefs for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad report-summary requests may trigger this skill when a different summary is wanted.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a structured industry-report digest before applying the template.

Risk: Missing units, measurement scope, or forecast assumptions could make report figures misleading.

Mitigation: Include units, scope, dates, and source locations; label missing context and distinguish reported facts from forecasts.
