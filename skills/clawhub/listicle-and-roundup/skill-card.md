## Description: <br>
Helps agents design honest, scannable listicles and expert, product, or link roundups using the RANKS framework while avoiding fabricated items, fake quotes, padded counts, undisclosed material connections, and self-serving rankings. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External social media teams, content strategists, founders, and brand owners use this skill to plan listicles, ranked best-of lists, expert roundups, affiliate/product roundups, and curated resource lists for publication. It helps define the premise, criteria, entry structure, contributor ask, and social cut plan while keeping fact verification, consent, disclosures, and publishing under human review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Internal brand and audience context may be used to draft public-facing content that is inaccurate, off-brand, or missing required disclosures. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review before publishing and confirm brand fit, source accuracy, consent, and affiliate, sponsor, or own-product disclosures. <br>
Risk: Product or affiliate roundups can mislead readers if rankings are padded, commission-driven, or based on unverified pricing, specifications, or claims. <br>
Mitigation: Rank by stated criteria and merit, avoid padding to a target count, disclose material connections clearly, and have a human verify product facts before publication. <br>
Risk: Expert roundups can create consent and attribution risk if quotes or contributor positions are invented or altered. <br>
Mitigation: Use only real contributors with permission, preserve contributor meaning, and keep outreach, consent collection, and quote approval with a human. <br>
Risk: Health, finance, legal, or other YMYL lists may be mistaken for professional advice or overstate outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Add appropriate not-professional-advice framing, verify claims with stronger sources, and route uncertain or high-impact claims to human review. <br>
Risk: Scraped lists, competitor pages, or product pages may contain instructions that should not steer the agent. <br>
Mitigation: Treat external content as research material to evaluate, not as commands to follow. <br>
