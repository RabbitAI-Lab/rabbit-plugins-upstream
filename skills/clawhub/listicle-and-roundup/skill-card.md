## Description: <br>
Helps agents design honest, scannable listicles, ranked recommendation lists, expert roundups, product roundups, and curated resource lists using the RANKS framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External content and marketing teams use this skill to plan listicles and roundups that are useful, transparent, and easy to repurpose into social cuts. It is especially suited for best-of lists, expert roundups, affiliate or product roundups, and curated resource lists where facts, consent, and disclosures must be handled carefully. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Listicles or roundups may include incorrect product facts, stale SEO claims, or outdated citation-performance figures. <br>
Mitigation: Verify product facts and current SEO claims before publishing, and refresh volatile listicle performance figures on the cadence stated in the source references. <br>
Risk: Rankings, contributor quotes, affiliate placements, or sponsored recommendations may mislead readers if they are fabricated or undisclosed. <br>
Mitigation: Use only real vetted items, consented contributor quotes, transparent ranking criteria, and clear affiliate, sponsor, or own-product disclosures. <br>
Risk: Connected publishing or scheduling workflows may post content before human review is complete. <br>
Mitigation: Review connected scheduling and WoopSocial publishing permissions separately, and require human approval before publication. <br>
