## Description: <br>
International freight forwarding quoting and multimodal logistics planning skill for cargo shipping costs, freight quotes, ocean freight, air freight, rail freight, FBA first-leg logistics, trade terms, surcharges, dangerous goods shipping, and import-export logistics planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[javazfj](https://clawhub.ai/user/javazfj) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External freight customers and logistics agents use this skill to route international freight inquiries, collect shipment details, and provide a contact handoff for real-time quotes. It can answer freight knowledge questions only when the user explicitly asks for information without a quote or plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is currently more of a contact handoff for a specific freight salesperson than a full in-chat quoting tool. <br>
Mitigation: Set user expectations that live rates and tailored logistics plans require direct confirmation with the listed logistics contact. <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive commercial shipment details off-platform. <br>
Mitigation: Advise users to share cargo and business details only when they intend to work directly with the listed company or contact. <br>
