## Description: <br>
Loan Decoder helps agents analyze personal, auto, or mortgage loan offers by calculating total cost, reconciling APR against advertised rates, ranking risky terms, and identifying key negotiation questions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and financial reviewers use this skill to turn loan documents into a plain-language cost breakdown, red-flag ranking, and negotiation checklist before signing or comparing offers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Loan documents and financial terms may contain sensitive personal or financial details. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the document text and numbers the user is comfortable sharing with the agent. <br>
Risk: The output is analytical guidance and may not account for jurisdiction-specific legal or financial requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm load-bearing interpretations with a qualified legal or financial professional. <br>
Risk: Incomplete loan paperwork can lead to incomplete cost calculations or red-flag analysis. <br>
Mitigation: List missing numbers as to-confirm items and avoid estimating terms that are not present in the document. <br>
