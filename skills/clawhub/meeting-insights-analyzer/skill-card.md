## Description:

Analyzes meeting transcripts and recordings to uncover behavioral patterns, communication insights, and actionable feedback for improving communication and leadership.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Professionals, managers, coaches, and team leads use this skill to analyze meeting transcripts for communication patterns, facilitation habits, speaking balance, filler words, and growth opportunities. It helps turn meeting records into timestamped feedback, strengths, risks, recommendations, and trend summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts may contain sensitive information about coworkers, customers, or other participants.

Mitigation: Analyze only transcripts you are authorized to process, limit folders exposed to the agent, and redact regulated or unnecessary personal details before analysis.

Risk: Communication assessments may be incomplete or misleading when transcripts lack speaker labels, timestamps, or full meeting context.

Mitigation: Confirm transcript quality before relying on conclusions and review quoted examples against the original meeting context.
