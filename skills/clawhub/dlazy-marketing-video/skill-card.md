## Description:

Creates conversion-focused marketing videos from product information or listing links, with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams can turn product specifications, catalogs, or ecommerce listings into promotional shopping videos for social media and campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Messages and attached files are sent to dLazy services for video generation.

Mitigation: Use only briefs and files approved for upload, and confirm the intended organization and project.

Risk: The CLI saves an API key in the local user configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the configuration file and revoke the key when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Video generation can consume account credits.

Mitigation: Consider expected generation-credit costs before submitting a request.
