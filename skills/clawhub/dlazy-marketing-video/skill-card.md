## Description:

Creates product-focused marketing videos from product descriptions, listings, or briefs for social media and advertising campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers and sellers use this skill to turn product specifications, catalog content, or ecommerce listings into promotional shopping videos, with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, product details, and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only assets needed for the video and avoid unrelated sensitive business information.

Risk: Continuing an existing project reuses earlier conversation context.

Mitigation: Continue a project only when its prior context is intended for the new task.
