## Description:

Create marketing, promotional, or advertising videos from product, brand, or campaign briefs for social media and campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, ecommerce teams, and sellers can use the skill to turn product details or a campaign brief into a promotional video for social media or advertising. The described template supports multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review found a mismatch between the video-creation description and reported login and organization project-listing actions; scan coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Before installing, confirm that sign-in and organization project listing are expected for your use, and review the install and service details.

Risk: Files attached to a session are uploaded to dLazy media storage before they are used.

Mitigation: Attach only files you are comfortable sending to the service.
