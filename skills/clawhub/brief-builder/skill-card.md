## Description: <br>
Brief Builder interviews users with focused questions to turn vague requests into a structured brief with goals, audience, constraints, success criteria, assumptions, and handoff guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and developers use this skill when an initial request is vague or under-specified and needs clarification before another skill or agent produces the final deliverable. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may recommend handoff to a separate skill with different risks, permissions, or behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review the recommended downstream skill before running it, as advised by the security guidance. <br>
Risk: The clarification loop can slow a request or over-question the user if the ask is already specific enough. <br>
Mitigation: Stop once enough information is available and summarize any remaining assumptions before proceeding. <br>
