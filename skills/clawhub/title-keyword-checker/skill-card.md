## Description:

Checks academic paper titles for keyword quality and searchability, including core terminology, generic versus proprietary terms, abbreviations, and keyword coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External authors, editors, and research assistants use this skill to evaluate whether an academic paper title contains searchable, discipline-appropriate keywords and to receive improvement suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may provide title keyword critiques that are too generic or not aligned with a specific academic field.

Mitigation: Review suggestions against discipline-specific terminology and authoritative vocabulary sources before finalizing a title.

Risk: The skill may overemphasize searchability at the expense of accuracy or scope.

Mitigation: Keep the final title aligned with the paper's actual research variables, population, and methods.
