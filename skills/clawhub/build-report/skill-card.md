## Description:

Turn aggregated findings into readable reports as a chat digest, Markdown document, or self-contained HTML dashboard.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samorodkin](https://clawhub.ai/user/samorodkin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and agents use this skill to turn aggregated findings from news, monitoring, or research work into concise, sourced reports that readers can act on.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may contain secrets, personal data, regulated information, or internal-only operational details.

Mitigation: Review reports before sharing, send only to authorized channels, and use summaries or redaction when sensitive findings are present.

Risk: Unsourced claims or mixed facts and interpretation can mislead readers.

Mitigation: Keep sources next to the claims they support and label interpretation separately from confirmed facts.
