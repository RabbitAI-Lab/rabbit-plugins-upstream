## Description:

Provides Chinese-language homeowner guidance on avoiding waterproofing and leak-repair pitfalls during renovations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zx029w](https://clawhub.ai/user/zx029w)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Homeowners use this skill to discuss waterproofing materials, leak prevention, inspection, and repair with qualified renovation professionals. It offers guidance for understanding and communication, not instructions for performing construction work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Waterproofing recommendations may not suit the building or local requirements.

Mitigation: Use them as discussion aids; have a qualified professional check the site, applicable rules, and proposed work.

Risk: Renovation advice may touch structural, electrical, gas, or legal matters.

Mitigation: Do not perform such work from the guidance; consult appropriately qualified professionals and local authorities.
