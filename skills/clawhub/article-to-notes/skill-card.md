## Description:

Turns a web article into a concise Markdown note with source details, key points, short quotes, and a takeaway.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People building a personal knowledge base can turn a supplied article URL or article text into a distilled note with source details, key points, and brief quotes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fetching a private or paywalled article could process content the user did not intend to share.

Mitigation: Use public article URLs; for private or paywalled material, only provide text you are comfortable processing.

Risk: The default note is saved locally and may contain article excerpts.

Mitigation: Check the destination and note contents before retaining or sharing the file.
