## Description:

Helps agents research public Instagram profiles, posts, reels, stories, hashtags, and engagement without an Instagram login or API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hith3sh](https://clawhub.ai/user/hith3sh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and researchers use the skill to compare public Instagram accounts, vet influencers, explore content ideas, and assess engagement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid external lookups can consume ClawLink credits, especially in bulk.

Mitigation: Check current prices and confirm the estimated cost with the user before bulk lookups.

Risk: Public audience and engagement data can be misused.

Mitigation: Limit research to public, lawful uses and avoid treating private-account information as accessible.
