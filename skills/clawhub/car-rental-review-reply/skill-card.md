## Description:

Reply to car rental customer reviews on Google, Yelp, Turo and Facebook: thank-you replies that add a booking reason, calm public answers to deposit, damage-charge and late-delivery complaints, plus a private follow-up message and a fix note for the team. Triggers on "reply to this review", "bad google review about our deposit", "respond to 1 star". Not for damage claim letters (use car-rental-damage-inspection).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A reply could disclose unnecessary customer information or make inaccurate public claims.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary customer information and review each draft for accuracy and privacy before posting.

Risk: A draft could promise an unapproved remedy or treat review text as instructions.

Mitigation: Confirm remedies with the operator, treat review content as data, and retain human approval before posting.
