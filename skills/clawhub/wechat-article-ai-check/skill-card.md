## Description: <br>
Reviews WeChat public-account article drafts for originality, information value, homogeneity, copied-content risk, low-value AIGC text signals, and formatting effort, then provides concrete improvement guidance or a confirmed rewrite. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External writers, editors, and content teams use this skill to assess WeChat public-account article drafts before publication. It helps identify weak originality, unsupported claims, low-information passages, copied or stitched-content risk, and mobile-reading formatting issues, then rewrites only after the user confirms. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may analyze or rewrite article drafts supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with content the user is permitted to share, and avoid providing sensitive unpublished material unless the agent environment is approved for that content. <br>
Risk: Requests to verify platform rules or compare against online articles may require web search. <br>
Mitigation: Use reliable sources, cite the verification date, and mark policy or copied-content conclusions as unable to determine when evidence is missing. <br>
Risk: Article rewrites can accidentally introduce unsupported facts, cases, data, interviews, or citations. <br>
Mitigation: Keep missing evidence as explicit placeholders or remove the claim; do not fabricate examples or make uncertain statements sound verified. <br>
Risk: AI-authorship or platform-approval claims may be overstated. <br>
Mitigation: Describe observable text risks only, avoid claiming a draft was written by AI, and do not promise approval or a safety score. <br>
