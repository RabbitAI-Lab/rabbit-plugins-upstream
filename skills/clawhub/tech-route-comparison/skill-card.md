## Description:

Evidence-backed comparison of two or more technical routes, architectures, or solution paths for route selection, maturity assessment, readiness evaluation, opportunity mapping, and management-grade technical reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, technical analysts, and decision teams use this skill to compare route sets, normalize evidence, assess maturity or readiness, and produce a scenario-specific recommendation with traceable support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill creates local Markdown and CSV research artifacts that may contain proprietary or sensitive research material.

Mitigation: Review generated reports, source indexes, query logs, and claim ledgers before sharing them outside the intended audience.

Risk: The skill uses the search and retrieval tools available in the host environment, so evidence coverage can vary by tool availability.

Mitigation: Record tool downgrades in method decisions and lower confidence on route breadth or frontier completeness when structured retrieval is unavailable.
