## Description:

Analyzes Chinese-language drafts for five heuristic AI-writing signals and guides authorized rewrites with before-and-after comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and editors review their own or authorized drafts for formulaic wording, revise them using genuine details, and compare the results before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewriting to reduce AI-detection signals can conceal AI authorship or violate publication rules.

Mitigation: Use only on your own or authorized material, retain required AI disclosures, and never use it to evade platform rules.

Risk: Adding personal details or examples can introduce fabricated experiences, interviews, or data.

Mitigation: Add only verified details and review every revision against the original facts and sources.

Risk: Heuristic scores may be mistaken for a platform's actual AI-detection verdict.

Mitigation: Treat scores as writing feedback, not proof of authorship or a guarantee of platform acceptance.
