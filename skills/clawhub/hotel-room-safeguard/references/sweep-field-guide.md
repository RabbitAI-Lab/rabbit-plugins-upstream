# Hotel Room Sweep — Field Reference

## Bedbugs: what the evidence means

| Sign | What it is | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Apple-seed adult (4–5 mm, flat, brown) | live bug | active infestation — do not unpack |
| Translucent 1 mm eggs, glued in seams | eggs | established colony (eggs mean a gravid female has been here) |
| Amber/pale empty shells | nymph casings | colony has been developing for weeks |
| Dark marker-dot clusters in seams | fecal smears | bugs feed here regularly |
| Small blood spots on sheets | crushed post-feed bug or fecal re-wet | check the seams now |
| Sweet, coriander-ish odor | alarm pheromones | heavy infestation (you'd know) |

**Bites are a late and unreliable signal**: roughly a third of people don't
react to bedbug bites at all, and reactions take 1–9 days to show. The
seams tell the truth on day one.

### Why the inspection order matters

Harborage probability in audit studies, highest first: box spring (esp.
underside/corner guards) > mattress seams/piping > bed frame and headboard >
nightstand and vicinity > perimeter (baseboards, carpet edge, wall voids,
electrical outlets near the head of the bed). The script walks this order so
a quick 5-minute version still covers the top of the probability stack.

### If you find them

1. Don't put luggage on soft surfaces; don't repack on the bed.
2. Demand a different room — **not adjacent** (shared wall = shared colony),
   ideally a different floor or wing.
3. On returning home: unpack into a garage/bathroom, everything into a dryer
   on high for 30+ min (dryer heat kills all stages; wash first is optional —
   the DRYER is the treatment), suitcase gets vacuumed + left in a hot car
   or treated.
4. One pregnant female home with you = $1,500–$5,000 extermination and
   weeks of psychological damage. The 15-minute sweep is the cheapest
   insurance in travel.

## Hidden cameras: reality check

- Most discovered devices hide in: smoke detectors, clock radios/alarms,
  USB charger bricks, wall outlets, air vents, "motion sensor" discs, power
  strips, and decor with a bed or bathroom view. Hosts need **power** and
  **line of sight**; that constraint is your search strategy.
- **Lens-glint scan**: darkness + phone flashlight swept at each suspect;
  a pinhole lens returns a bright blue-white point of reflection. Slow,
  angled passes beat fast sweeps.
- **RF/lens-finder gadgets** ($20–60): supplementary only. Wired and
  locally-recording cameras emit nothing; Wi-Fi cameras hide among the
  property's legitimate devices.
- **Network audit**: only where the property discloses the router. Unexpected
  always-on devices pushing constant traffic merit a physical look at the
  hotspots above.
- **Found one?** Do not disconnect or move it. Photograph in place, note the
  exact location, call police non-emergency, then management. The device is
  evidence of a crime in most jurisdictions (unlawful surveillance);
  ownership questions get sorted by police, not by you.

## Valuables doctrine

- Room safes: bolted ones are reasonable for passports/cash; a portable
  safe is luggage, not a safe.
- Drawers are staff-accessible and the first place things go missing from.
- The real answer for laptops: cable lock to a fixed pipe, or take it.

## Exit + door audit (90 seconds)

1. Deadbolt and chain work; door self-closes (fire code + break-in stat).
2. Peephole: clear and not reverse-viewable — cover a missing flap with
   tape from inside.
3. Count exits; walk the route once. In smoke, you crawl and navigate by
   wall with a hand.
4. Know the front-desk prefix; test the phone.

## Legal/escalation notes (general, non-specific jurisdiction)

- Evidence with timestamps beats angry words: photos of the finding, of the
  room number, of your conversation with staff.
- Booking-platform escalation works best with the evidence pack from
  `--letter`: platform customer service has refund leverage the property's
  front desk does not.
- Health department / licensing body: bedbugs are reportable conditions in
  many jurisdictions; the reference in the letter is usually enough to
  unlock a comped night.
- Hidden camera: police report first, platform second. Platforms delist
  properties fast over surveillance findings.
