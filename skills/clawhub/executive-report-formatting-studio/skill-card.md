## Description:

Sets style guidance for executive reports and formats status reports for stakeholders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

## Use Case:

Employees preparing executive updates use this skill to choose report style guidance and format status reports for stakeholders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential business details supplied in the request may appear in the generated report.

Mitigation: Avoid including confidential details unless they are intended for the report's audience; review the report before sharing.
