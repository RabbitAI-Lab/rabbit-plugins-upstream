## Description:

Helps users research public TikTok Shop products, reviews, stores, creator showcases, and live-stream listings through ClawLink.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and commerce researchers use this skill to compare public TikTok Shop products and reviews, examine competitors' stores, and identify products promoted by creators or live streams.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawLink data calls consume paid credit, particularly when repeated across many items.

Mitigation: Check current call prices and ask the user before large batches or higher-cost calls.

Risk: Issue reports reach ClawLink support and could disclose information included in a report.

Mitigation: Exclude sensitive prompts, private account data, and unnecessary identifiers from reports.
