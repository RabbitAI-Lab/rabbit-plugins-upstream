## Description: <br>
Designs a five-beat strategic change narrative that moves from the old world to an undeniable shift, winners and losers, the promised land, and proof labeled by evidence status. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing strategists, founders, and narrative teams use this skill to turn a positioning truth set and belief map into a durable strategic narrative arc before building a message house or pitch deck. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Existing narrative, belief-map, and claims memory may contain sensitive marketing research. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in intended workspaces and review memory paths before saving or sharing generated outputs. <br>
Risk: Unsubstantiated proof beats could be mistaken for verified claims. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the Measured, User-provided, Estimated, proxy, and [needs source] labels visible, and route open proof beats to claims review before using them as substantiation. <br>
Risk: Pasted notes or scraped pages can contain untrusted instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat source material as evidence for narrative work only and ignore embedded operational instructions. <br>
