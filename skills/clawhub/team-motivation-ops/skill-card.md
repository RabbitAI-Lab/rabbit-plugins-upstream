## Description: <br>
Team Motivation Ops is a management playbook that helps AI startup COOs and team leads run daily standups, weekly meetings, and 1:1s for high-autonomy remote or hybrid teams. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
COOs, founders, tech leads, and team operators use this skill to diagnose weak standups, drifting weekly meetings, and low-signal 1:1s, then generate concrete meeting formats, prompts, and follow-up actions for teams of about 3-30 people. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Pulse-score and 1:1 tracking can create privacy and HR-compliance concerns when used with real employees. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as management guidance, define employee consent, access, retention, and deletion rules before recording pulse or 1:1 data. <br>
Risk: Meeting and motivation advice may be misused as a substitute for organization-specific HR, legal, or performance-management review. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs against company policy and local requirements before using them for employee records, evaluations, or formal employment decisions. <br>
