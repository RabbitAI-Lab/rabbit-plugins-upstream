## Description:

Provides a COO-style playbook for improving standups, weekly meetings, 1:1s, team pulse checks, and action-item closure in high-autonomy teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

COOs, team leads, and managers use this skill to diagnose weak standups, status-report weekly meetings, and low-signal 1:1s, then turn them into concrete operating routines for teams of roughly 3-30 people.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Employee pulse scores, 1:1 notes, feedback, and related workplace observations can expose sensitive internal HR or team data.

Mitigation: Store and share generated notes only with appropriate consent, access controls, and internal workplace-data handling practices.

Risk: Management guidance may be applied without enough local context and could produce misleading conclusions about team performance or morale.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as decision support, review them with responsible managers, and validate action items with affected team members before acting.
