## Description:

Turns food-visit footage or cooking recordings into a Chinese food-narration video project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitruck](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitruck)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators use this skill to turn restaurant visits, food documentaries, or cooking footage into a Chinese narration script and an edited food-video project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected footage may contain private or copyrighted material.

Mitigation: Confirm privacy, usage rights, and permission for cloud processing before using the media.

Risk: Cloud transcription, TTS, and editing steps may incur charges.

Mitigation: Check current prices and usage estimates, then approve paid steps before they run.
