## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Resolve the intended music workflow before execution and confirm that the selected track is authorized for use.

Risk: Cloud processing shares selected media and may incur charges.

Mitigation: Explain media sharing and estimated costs, then obtain user approval before cloud processing.
