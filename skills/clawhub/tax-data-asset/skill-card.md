## Description: <br>
This skill helps users assess data-asset tax compliance across tax-book differences, valuation risk, transfer and licensing tax treatment, R&D super-deduction classification, data ownership compliance, IPO inquiry readiness, and year-end tax adjustment workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External tax, finance, compliance, and business users use this skill to ask data-resource capitalization tax questions, run structured self-checks and risk scans, and generate practical remediation or compliance-report guidance. It is intended for preliminary guidance and workflow support, not for filing, valuation, legal due diligence, or binding tax determinations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Cloud-backed tax workflows may transmit prompts, self-check metrics, client identifiers, and API keys to remote services. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with data appropriate for the publisher's stated handling practices, avoid sensitive regulated details unless retention and credential-storage terms are clear, and review remote-service use before deployment. <br>
Risk: The skill can install or configure a broader tax skill matrix and remote MCP access. <br>
Mitigation: Review the matrix contents, download sources, configuration changes, and target install directory before enabling matrix installation or automatic setup. <br>
Risk: Tax and compliance answers may be incomplete, outdated, or non-binding for a specific filing, valuation, transaction, or listing review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary guidance, verify current law and local authority positions, and route filing, valuation, legal due diligence, and disputed tax positions to qualified professionals. <br>
