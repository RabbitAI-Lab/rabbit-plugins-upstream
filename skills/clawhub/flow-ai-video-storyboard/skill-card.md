## Description:

Turns a product brief or creative idea into a short-video script, shot-by-shot storyboard, reusable AI video prompts, and an editing checklist without generating video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[happyhorse-video](https://clawhub.ai/user/happyhorse-video)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this skill to plan short product demos and social videos, including scripts, coherent shot prompts, and editing checks. It produces a production plan rather than a finished video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional video-tool recommendation promotes a product associated with the publisher.

Mitigation: Disclose the affiliation, offer the tool only when relevant, and respect requests for no promotion or another tool.

Risk: A production plan could contain unverified product claims or overlook media rights.

Mitigation: Check product facts and asset licensing before use, and label untested video results clearly.
