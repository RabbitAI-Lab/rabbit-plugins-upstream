Developers, agent operators, and external users use this skill when producing, inspecting, or modifying spreadsheet deliverables that must preserve formulas, workbook structure, formatting, dates, and data types.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Spreadsheet edits can affect important financial or business decisions if formulas, hidden sheets, validations, or cached values are wrong.

Mitigation: Review important workbook outputs before relying on them, with particular attention to formulas, hidden sheets, validations, recalculation state, and preserved formatting.

Risk: Macro-enabled workbooks may contain behavior outside normal spreadsheet data and formula review.

Mitigation: Treat macro-enabled files as higher-risk artifacts and inspect or disable macros according to the user's security policy before trusting the workbook.
