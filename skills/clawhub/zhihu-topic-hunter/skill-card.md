## Description: <br>
Generate ranked Zhihu topic candidates from a theme using current discussion signals, platform fit, debate value, and author writability. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators and content strategists use this skill to turn a theme into ranked Zhihu topic ideas, including discussion signals, debate angles, author fit, freshness labels, and topics to avoid. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Hot or recent topic recommendations can be stale or unsupported when current browsing or source material is unavailable. <br>
Mitigation: Enable browsing or provide source material for recent topics, and keep freshness marked as unverified when evidence is missing. <br>
Risk: The workflow is narrowly optimized for Zhihu content planning and may not fit other platforms or languages by default. <br>
Mitigation: Explicitly request a different platform, language, or audience when adapting the output beyond Zhihu. <br>
