## Description:

Provides Chinese-language, checklist-style guidance for medical-device import and export routes, with JSON decision-support output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Medical-device importers, exporters, and compliance staff use this skill to outline registration, export-certificate, and destination-market checks before consulting official sources and qualified reviewers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (guidance focuses on China, the United States, and the European Union; check destination-country rules)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or simplified regulatory guidance could lead to incorrect import or export decisions.

Mitigation: Verify requirements against current NMPA, FDA, EU MDR, and destination-country official sources before acting.

Risk: Decision-support output could be mistaken for legal, regulatory, registration, recall, or clinical advice.

Mitigation: Require qualified human review and obtain official determinations where needed; do not rely on the report as approval or certification.

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may be misunderstood by users who cannot review it confidently.

Mitigation: Use a reviewer fluent in Chinese and the relevant medical-device regulations.
