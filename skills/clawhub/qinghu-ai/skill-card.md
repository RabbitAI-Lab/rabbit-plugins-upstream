## Description:

Helps agents use Qinghu AI to create and edit e-commerce images and videos, develop ad content, and retrieve e-commerce market data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and e-commerce teams use this skill to guide image and video production, translation and editing, ad workflows, and product or market research through Qinghu AI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers may lead to unrequested package installation, upgrades, or persistent configuration changes.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before installing or updating qhkit, changing regions, or storing tokens.

Risk: Local media may be uploaded to Qinghu services, and API tokens may be configured locally.

Mitigation: Obtain consent before uploads or token storage, and use only material and credentials the user authorizes.

Risk: Generated tasks may spend paid credits or involve face replacement, watermark removal, imitation, or third-party data.

Mitigation: Confirm the quoted credit cost and get explicit approval for sensitive tasks before submission; check rights and consent for affected content and people.
