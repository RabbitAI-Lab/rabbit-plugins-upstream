## Description:

Use when a prepared PPT task with a current outline.md should be generated as an image-first presentation with one 16:9 image per slide and optional PPTX packaging.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sensenova-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/sensenova-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Presentation authors use the skill to turn a prepared task pack and current outline into one full-slide 16:9 image per page, with optional PPTX packaging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Slide content is sent to the selected image-generation provider and may include confidential material.

Mitigation: Check the provider’s suitability for the material before generating slides.

Risk: Optional PPTX packaging relies on a companion package and its dependencies.

Mitigation: Review the companion sn-ppt-tools package and use reviewed dependency versions before enabling packaging.
