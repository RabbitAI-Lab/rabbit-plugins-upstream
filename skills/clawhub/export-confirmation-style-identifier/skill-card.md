## Description:

Set acknowledgement guidance for a delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People coordinating deliveries use this skill to turn a supplied delivery profile into brief guidance on when and how often to acknowledge the delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic role and effect wording may imply actions beyond generating acknowledgement guidance.

Mitigation: Use it only for local guidance from the supplied delivery profile; maintainers should clarify the documentation.
