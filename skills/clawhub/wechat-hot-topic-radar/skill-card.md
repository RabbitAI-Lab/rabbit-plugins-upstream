## Description:

Helps content teams find timely topics from cross-platform trending searches, viral posts from smaller accounts, and WeChat search suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dunkong](https://clawhub.ai/user/dunkong)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content editors and social media teams use this skill to research trending topics, compare viral examples, expand keywords, and develop timely article or video angles with supporting links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected topics and filters are sent to an external service.

Mitigation: Use only with information you are comfortable sharing with the We-Media API.

Risk: Some requests may incur usage charges.

Mitigation: Review the current cost estimate before authorizing paid requests.

Risk: Export, snapshot, or checkpoint paths may create or replace local files.

Mitigation: Choose file paths deliberately and check existing files before running.
