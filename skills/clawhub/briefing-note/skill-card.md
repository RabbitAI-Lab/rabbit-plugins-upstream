## Description: <br>
Writes a tight, one-page briefing note that prepares a busy principal for a decision, meeting, or issue review with purpose, background, key considerations, and a recommendation or decision sought. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and support staff use this skill to draft concise briefing notes for ministers, executives, officials, or other principals before decisions, meetings, or issue reviews. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Briefing notes may contain confidential or sensitive material. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential or sensitive briefing material unless the user is comfortable processing it in their agent environment. <br>
Risk: A generated briefing note may omit important context, risks, or sensitivities if the supplied inputs are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review the note for accuracy, balance, and surfaced risks before using it with an executive, minister, official, or other principal. <br>
