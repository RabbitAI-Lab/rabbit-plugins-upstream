## Description: <br>
ALIWAY helps agents turn technical documents, plans, and proposals into Alibaba-style strategic narratives using structured frameworks, rhetorical patterns, and style guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use ALIWAY to rewrite technical documents, product plans, strategic proposals, retrospectives, and vision documents in an Alibaba-style strategic voice. The skill provides document frameworks, opening patterns, rhetorical guidance, and quality checks for producing forceful management-oriented prose. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may make outputs more forceful or management-oriented when prompts contain broad strategy terms. <br>
Mitigation: Ask explicitly for a neutral style when a less forceful or less management-oriented tone is desired. <br>
