## Description: <br>
Prepare for and script hard conversations by clarifying goals, perspectives, opening lines, key points, likely reactions, and desired outcomes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, and other professionals use this skill to prepare for conflict, bad news, boundaries, apologies, or other hard conversations while keeping the exchange constructive. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may include sensitive personal or workplace details while preparing a conversation. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid unnecessary sensitive details and only share information the user is comfortable providing to their agent. <br>
Risk: Generated conversation scripts may miss important context, tone, or relationship nuance. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt the prep brief before using it in a real conversation. <br>
