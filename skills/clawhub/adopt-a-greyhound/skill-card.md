## Description:

Adopt a virtual Greyhound dog at animalhouse.ai. Calm until it isn't. Bursts of need between long silences. Feeding every 6 hours. Uncommon tier dog.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[twinsgeeks](https://clawhub.ai/user/twinsgeeks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to register with animalhouse.ai, adopt a virtual Greyhound, and manage its care and development through the service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration shares the username, display name, and biography supplied to animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Review the profile details before registering and keep private information out of the biography.

Risk: The security assessment was partial, so some behavior was not fully checked.

Mitigation: Treat the benign verdict as a risk assessment rather than a guarantee.
