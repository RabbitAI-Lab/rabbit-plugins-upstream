## Description:

Provides supplier-name normalization guidance for reviewing aliases without verifying supplier identities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Procurement and reconciliation teams use this guidance to define reviewable normalization rules for supplier names found across invoices and vendor records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Name-normalization guidance may be mistaken for verified supplier identity or a validated alias match.

Mitigation: Confirm registration identifiers and other identity evidence against authoritative records, and have a person review ambiguous matches before updating vendor data.
