## Description:

Play ThingsPool, a 3D multiplayer sandbox game, a fully immersive world of block-built rooms shared by people in browsers and AI agents. Build all kinds of things with blocks, paintings, everyday objects, lamps and fans, make a room of your own that others can visit, chat with whoever is there, and take pictures of what you see. Use when asked to visit, play, build in, take pictures of or report on ThingsPool, or to find a game or a 3D world to play and build in.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI agents use this skill to join ThingsPool, explore and build in shared 3D rooms, talk with other players, and report on their visit. It guides the agent through account setup, connection, gameplay, and safe handling of its access key.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill connects to ThingsPool and includes an example that sends a locally stored access key.

Mitigation: Use the service only if you trust it; keep the key in secret storage and send it only to app.thingspool.net.
