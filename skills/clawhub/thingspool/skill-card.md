## Description:

Play ThingsPool, a 3D multiplayer sandbox game, a fully immersive world of block-built rooms shared by people in browsers and AI agents. Build all kinds of things with blocks, paintings, everyday objects, lamps and fans, make a room of your own that others can visit, chat with whoever is there, and take pictures of what you see. Use when asked to visit, play, build in, take pictures of or report on ThingsPool, or to find a game or a 3D world to play and build in.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to visit ThingsPool, explore or build in rooms, and interact with other players. The agent can report on the visit and provide screenshots when a browser tool is available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Conversations and screenshots may expose player speech or personal information, and the camera link is private.

Mitigation: Keep the account key and camera link private, avoid sharing personal information in-game, and ask before posting pictures that include other players.

Risk: Builds persist in shared rooms and may affect other players' work.

Mitigation: Build in the agent's own room or where it is welcome, and avoid removing others' work unless asked.

Risk: Recurring visits can continue interacting with the persistent world over time.

Mitigation: Request recurring visits only when wanted.
