## Description: <br>
Runs an independent freelance or contractor practice as a business, covering rates, pipeline, cash buffer, taxes, contracts, payments, and growth decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Freelancers, contractors, and people considering independent work use this skill to reason through rates, pipeline, cashflow, taxes, contracts, payment disputes, classification, insurance, and scaling. It can produce reviewable advice, formulas, draft messages, clauses, proposals, and local practice records. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may keep local freelance business records, including contacts, terms, quote history, dispute notes, and financial references. <br>
Mitigation: Review the local Clawic data files periodically and avoid pasting secrets or client-confidential material unless it can be stripped to a pointer. <br>
Risk: Tax, worker-classification, contract, and late-payment guidance can be jurisdiction-sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Set or review the applicable jurisdiction before relying on material guidance, and verify high-stakes tax, legal, or compliance decisions with a qualified professional. <br>
Risk: Draft quotes, clauses, chase messages, or outreach notes could create business commitments if sent without review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated drafts as reviewable text only; the user should approve terms and send or sign documents themselves. <br>
