## Description:

Helps select a contact for a client brief and prepare finance-review routing details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Client communications teams use this skill to choose a recipient for a client brief and record routing details for finance-review messaging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A mistaken recipient, mailbox, or subject could misroute a financial or client-sensitive message.

Mitigation: Confirm the recipient, mailbox, and subject context before sending any real outbound message.
