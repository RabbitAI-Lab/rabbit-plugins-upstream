## Description:

Helps users read, triage, and answer BulkPublish Inbox conversations and manage comments on published posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[azeemkafridi](https://clawhub.ai/user/azeemkafridi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People managing connected social accounts use this skill to review messages, reviews, and post comments, draft replies, triage conversations, and moderate comments in BulkPublish.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A sent reply reaches a real person immediately and cannot be unsent.

Mitigation: Show the exact text, platform, sending account, and recipient; obtain explicit approval for each reply before sending.

Risk: Deleting a comment can have a high impact.

Mitigation: Show the specific comment and obtain explicit approval before deleting it.
