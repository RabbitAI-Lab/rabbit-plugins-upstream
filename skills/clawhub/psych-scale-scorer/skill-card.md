## Description:

Scores and explains nine common psychology scales covering mood, anxiety, stress, sleep, trauma, personality, and motivation using documented psychometric scoring rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and other external users can use this skill to calculate questionnaire scores, apply reverse-scoring rules, classify score ranges, and receive non-diagnostic explanatory guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake mental-health scale results for diagnosis or treatment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational or screening information only and direct users seeking diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support to qualified professionals or local crisis services.

Risk: Incorrect, incomplete, or mismatched questionnaire answers can produce misleading scores.

Mitigation: Require complete responses for the selected scale, apply documented reverse-scoring rules, and stop rather than score when the answer count does not match the scale.
