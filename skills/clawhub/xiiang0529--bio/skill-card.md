## Description: <br>
A comprehensive AI agent skill for writing and managing professional bios. Crafts bios for any platform and any purpose, matches your voice and career stage, adapts the same story for a LinkedIn profile, a speaker introduction, a website about page, a press kit, and a conference program, and keeps your bio current as your work evolves. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external professionals, creators, and business users use this skill to draft, adapt, and maintain professional bios for LinkedIn, speaker introductions, websites, press kits, conference programs, directories, podcast appearances, and grant applications. It helps shape the same career story for different readers, lengths, levels of formality, and career stages while preserving the user's voice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated bios may include inaccurate, outdated, or over-polished career claims if the provided biographical details are incomplete or stale. <br>
Mitigation: Review every generated bio before publishing and verify titles, roles, credentials, publications, awards, and dates against current source material. <br>
Risk: Users may provide sensitive personal career details, voice samples, or biographical material while asking for tailored bios. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only information appropriate for the intended publishing context and remove sensitive details that should not appear in public or shared bios. <br>
