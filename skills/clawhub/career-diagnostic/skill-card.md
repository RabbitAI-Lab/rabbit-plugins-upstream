## Description:

AI时代职业风险评估与转型诊断，帮助用户评估岗位在AI冲击下的风险等级，并制定从焦虑到行动的转型路径。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to assess the AI replacement risk of their current job, compare vulnerable and durable skills, and receive a practical transition plan. The skill asks for career context such as role, duties, experience, income mix, skills, and concerns, then produces a Markdown diagnostic report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may collect sensitive career, income, skills, and workplace concern details and save them in a local Markdown report.

Mitigation: Share only details you are comfortable storing, or ask the agent to show the report inline or omit sensitive fields before writing a file.

Risk: Career risk assessments and transition suggestions may be incomplete or overly general without current industry, regional, or hiring-market evidence.

Mitigation: Review the generated recommendations against current labor-market data and personal constraints before making job, training, or income decisions.
