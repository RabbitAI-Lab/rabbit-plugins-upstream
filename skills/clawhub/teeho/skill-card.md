## Description:

Analyzes existing Xiaohongshu notes and produces scored reports, content recommendations, and comparable popular notes.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[mingzhizhiren](https://clawhub.ai/user/mingzhizhiren)

## Use Case:

Xiaohongshu creators and their agents use this skill to diagnose existing posts, review quantitative scores and comparable notes, and receive a formatted report in the user's language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected notes and media are uploaded to Teeho for analysis.

Mitigation: Submit only notes and media you are comfortable sharing with Teeho; confirm file-based submissions before uploading.

Risk: Anonymous reports and original media paths can be read by other local users through shared history.

Mitigation: On shared machines, prefer a formal account and avoid anonymous history; review the impact before clearing history.
