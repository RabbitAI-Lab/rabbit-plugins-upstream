## Description:

Diagnoses existing Xiaohongshu notes with text, images, or video and provides content analysis and improvement guidance.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

Creators and their agents use Teeho to diagnose existing Xiaohongshu posts, review complete reports, track tasks, and manage local report history; the skill does not write posts for users.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected note text and media are sent to Teeho for diagnosis.

Mitigation: Only submit material you are comfortable sharing with Teeho.

Risk: Anonymous diagnosis reports are saved as plaintext in a shared local directory readable by other users of the same machine.

Mitigation: Prefer a formal account if local sharing is unwanted, and clear anonymous history when reports are no longer needed.
