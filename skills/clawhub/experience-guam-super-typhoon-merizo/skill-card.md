## Description:

Guides an immersive, 12-step narrative experience of riding out a super typhoon in Merizo, Guam.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill to explore an interactive storm-survival story, reflect between steps, and optionally share a review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account registration and journey reflections send information to an external service.

Mitigation: Use a unique username and token, and omit sensitive details from optional profile fields and reflections.

Risk: Reviews may be visible to other users.

Mitigation: Avoid personal or sensitive details in reviews unless the service confirms their visibility and retention.
