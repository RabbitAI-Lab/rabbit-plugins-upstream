## Description:

Add bold highlights to key concepts in each paragraph so readers can scan and capture the article's core points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to make article drafts in ai-thoughts/docs/ easier to scan by applying short, selective bold highlights that preserve the article's natural reading flow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can modify article prose and may regenerate related READMEs for registered articles.

Mitigation: Install only for article-formatting workflows and review diffs before accepting changes.

Risk: Over-highlighting or repeated highlights can distort the intended emphasis of an article.

Mitigation: Keep highlights to one or two short concepts per body paragraph and scan the bolded words to confirm the article arc still holds.
