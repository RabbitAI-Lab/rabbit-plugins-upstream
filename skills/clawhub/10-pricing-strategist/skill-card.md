## Description:

Guides pricing strategy work by structuring willingness-to-pay research, competitor pricing comparison, tiered pricing, and monetization planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and product teams use this skill to create a Markdown pricing strategy document for a product or service, including competitor pricing, free-tier limits, paid tiers, monetization stages, and assumptions to validate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing recommendations may rely on incorrect competitor prices or unvalidated market assumptions.

Mitigation: Review competitor pricing, willingness-to-pay assumptions, free-tier limits, and proposed tiers before using the strategy for business decisions.
