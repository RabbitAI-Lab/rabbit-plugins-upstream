## Description: <br>
Helps developers, researchers, privacy-conscious users, hobbyists, and small teams plan and verify local LLM workflows on consumer CPU or GPU hardware without depending on cloud-only systems. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, privacy-conscious users, hobbyists, and small teams use this skill to turn local LLM setup requests into practical workflows, checklists, implementation guidance, and verification steps for ordinary home machines. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad local-AI and privacy-related triggers may route some requests to this skill when another skill would be more specific. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome, constraints, available hardware, and success criteria before giving setup guidance. <br>
Risk: Local LLM setup advice may not match the user's exact hardware, memory budget, operating system, or model requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer small-model or CPU-safe workflows and include verification commands or checks when code, data, or configuration is involved. <br>
