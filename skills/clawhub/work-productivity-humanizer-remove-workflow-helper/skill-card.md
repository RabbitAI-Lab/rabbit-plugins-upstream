## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt popular Humanizer-style workflows into concrete artifacts, implementation help, safety hardening, and reliability improvements. It is intended for practical work-productivity support that remains feasible on ordinary local hardware.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked more often than intended because its triggers are generic.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation when possible, and narrow trigger terms if maintaining a customized release.

Risk: The skill produces workflow and implementation guidance that could be applied without enough review.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the stated success criteria and keep assumptions, limits, and remaining risks visible before acting on them.
