## Description:

Validate test reports and calibration certificates against ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Clause 7.8 content requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Laboratory, quality, and compliance teams use this skill to review test reports, calibration certificates, statements of conformity, sampling reports, and amended reports before release. It helps identify missing ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Clause 7.8 content, uncertainty statements, decision rules, traceability statements, and amendment handling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the checklist as authoritative legal or accreditation advice.

Mitigation: Verify ISO/IEC 17025 wording, laboratory procedures, and any missing referenced material before relying on it for formal release decisions.

Risk: A report review may incorrectly pass or block a release if the reviewed document or context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer confirm critical nonconformities, uncertainty requirements, traceability statements, and decision rules before final release.
