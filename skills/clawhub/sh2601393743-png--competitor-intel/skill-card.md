## Description: <br>
Automates competitor intelligence by researching a product and one to three competitors, then comparing features, pricing, market position, user sentiment, differentiation opportunities, and strategic actions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, marketers, founders, and strategy teams use this skill to gather public competitor research and produce a competitive positioning report with feature, pricing, sentiment, differentiation, and action recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Competitor research prompts may expose confidential product names, positioning, or roadmap details through web searches. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the product names and research scope before use, and avoid entering confidential roadmap or unreleased strategy details. <br>
Risk: Public web research can be stale, incomplete, or misleading, especially for pricing, funding, user counts, and market traction. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer current sources, include retrieval dates, and mark unavailable or uncertain facts rather than filling gaps with assumptions. <br>
Risk: Competitive analysis can drift into unfair or harmful use if the scope changes from market research to probing competitors for weaknesses. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the workflow limited to public product, pricing, positioning, and user sentiment research; do not request vulnerability discovery or adversarial tactics. <br>
