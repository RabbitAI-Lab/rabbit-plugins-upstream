## Description:

Rewrites stiff, vague, template-like Chinese into concrete, restrained prose while preserving facts, names, and the author’s intent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

External users can polish or diagnose Chinese creator, workplace, product, and everyday writing while retaining the original facts and stance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Text supplied for rewriting may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Provide only text you intend the agent to process.

Risk: Rewrites or checker hints may leave factual errors or change the intended meaning.

Mitigation: Review the rewritten text and verify factual claims separately; the checker does not verify facts or authorship.
