## Description:

帮助用户把空泛、模板化的中文改得具体、克制、自然，同时保留事实、数字、专名和原意。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向需要中文写作帮助的外部用户、创作者和职场人士，用于诊断或润色日常、自媒体、职场和产品文案，同时保留原文事实与立场。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A rewrite may change or imply facts that were not in the source text.

Mitigation: Review the rewritten facts against the original before using the result.

Risk: Writing-style signals could be mistaken for proof that text was written by AI.

Mitigation: Treat checker results as suggestions, not proof of authorship.

Risk: Source documents may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share documents according to the assistant's privacy settings.
