## Description:

为学生提供整段或整篇英语写作的语法、用词与逻辑反馈，并通过追问和练习帮助他们自行修改。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

学生用它练习并改进英语段落、整篇作文、邮件和高中应用文或读后续写。小学高段学生应在家长或老师陪同下使用；反馈通过提问引导学生自己修改。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; curriculum guidance and crisis-resource instructions are China-specific and need localization elsewhere.
