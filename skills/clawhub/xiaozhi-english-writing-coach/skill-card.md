## Description:

面向中文 K12 学生，对整段或整篇英语写作提供语法、用词和逻辑反馈，并通过追问引导学生自行修改。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking upper-primary, middle-school and high-school students use this skill to practice English composition, receive focused writing feedback and track progress when they consent. Younger students require an accompanying parent or teacher.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curricula, minor-consent rules and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing and progress records may contain sensitive information or be shared across related skills.

Mitigation: Enable profile tracking and sharing only after consent, and provide controls to pause, delete, export or restrict sharing.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, minor-consent assumptions and emergency contacts may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize educational guidance, consent requirements and crisis contacts before deployment in another region.

Risk: A student may disclose self-harm, bullying or immediate danger during a writing conversation.

Mitigation: Stop coaching, encourage contact with a trusted adult and appropriate local support, and confirm location before naming emergency numbers.
