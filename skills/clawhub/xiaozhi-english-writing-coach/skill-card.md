## Description:

帮助中文母语学生从语法、用词和逻辑三方面审阅英语段落或作文，通过追问引导自主修改，并支持高中应用文与读后续写练习。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆的小学高年级至高中学生及陪同的教师或家长，提供英语作文的针对性反馈、句式升级练习和写作进步回顾；小学阶段须由家长或教师陪同。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires localizing curriculum, privacy defaults, and crisis resources.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student writing profiles or cross-skill sharing may expose minors' learning data without permission.

Mitigation: Enable profile storage and sharing only after consent; otherwise provide feedback solely on the current submission.

Risk: Crisis resources or educational guidance may not fit users outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum assumptions, privacy defaults, and crisis resources before deploying in another region or language.
