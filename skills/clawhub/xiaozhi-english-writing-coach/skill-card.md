## Description:

A Chinese-language English writing coach that guides K–12 learners to revise paragraphs and essays through targeted feedback on grammar, vocabulary, and structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Chinese-language K–12 settings use this skill to practice English writing, revise their own work with guided questions, and optionally track writing progress with consent. Younger students should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, minor-consent practices, and crisis referrals before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A learner's writing history or progress may be retained or shared beyond their preferences.

Mitigation: Confirm learner and guardian consent before enabling profile memory, cross-skill sharing, reminders, or parent-facing summaries.

Risk: Safety or emergency guidance may not fit a learner's location.

Mitigation: Localize crisis referrals before deployment outside mainland China and confirm the learner's region before giving emergency numbers.
