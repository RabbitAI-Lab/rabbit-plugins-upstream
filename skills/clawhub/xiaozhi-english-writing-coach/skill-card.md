## Description:

Coaches Chinese-speaking students through paragraph and essay revision with grammar, vocabulary, and structure feedback, guided rewrites, and optional progress tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking school students use this coach to improve English paragraphs and essays through targeted feedback and writing exercises. Primary-school students should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize language, curriculum, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Writing profiles, parent summaries, reminders, or sharing across skills may expose student data without appropriate permission.

Mitigation: Review and configure profile, sharing, and reminder consent before enabling tracking; keep feedback session-only when consent is absent.

Risk: Chinese-language guidance, curriculum assumptions, and crisis contacts may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the language, educational expectations, and crisis-help resources before deploying in another region.

Risk: Generated writing advice or exercises may be inaccurate or inappropriate for a student's grade level.

Mitigation: Check generated exercises against the skill's grade-level guidance and have an educator review consequential feedback.
