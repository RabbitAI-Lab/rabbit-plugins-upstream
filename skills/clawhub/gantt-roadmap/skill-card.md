## Description: <br>
Turn a plan or set of milestones into a dated roadmap with a ready-to-render Mermaid Gantt chart, critical path notes, risks, buffers, and assumptions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, project managers, and planning teams use this skill to convert project phases, tasks, dependencies, milestones, and timing assumptions into a clear roadmap. It is useful when a schedule needs real ISO dates, visible dependencies, critical path notes, and risk or buffer commentary. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated roadmap dates can be mistaken for firm commitments when inputs are incomplete or estimated. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated dates and keep estimated dates clearly labeled as planning assumptions unless firm dates were provided. <br>
