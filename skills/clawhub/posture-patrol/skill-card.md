## Description:

Track posture throughout the workday with manual logging, break reminders, ergonomic scoring, posture streaks, trend analysis, spinal-load estimates, desk stretches, slouch-pattern tracking, and weekly posture reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and workplace wellness practitioners can use this skill to track posture habits, plan movement breaks, estimate posture-related spinal load, and review ergonomic recommendations from local posture logs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The tool stores posture profile data, check history, and user-entered notes locally in ~/.posture_patrol.json.

Mitigation: Do not enter sensitive medical or workplace information unless local storage is acceptable.

Risk: Posture and stretch suggestions are general ergonomic information, not clinical advice.

Mitigation: Consult a clinician for pain, numbness, injury, chronic symptoms, or mobility limitations.
