## Description:

A coaching framework for diagnosing how people and teams identify, access, and combine resources to address persistent problems and build more proactive problem-solving habits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Managers, coaches, and organizational development teams can use the framework to assess problem-solving behaviors, identify whether a skill, access, habit, or permission gap is blocking progress, and plan a practical next-level experiment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coaching suggestions to seek resources beyond allocated budgets or formal authority could conflict with organizational permissions or confidentiality requirements.

Mitigation: Use the framework as a coaching aid and ensure any proposed resource access respects actual organizational permissions and confidentiality.
