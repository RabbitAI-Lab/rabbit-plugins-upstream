## Description: <br>
Guides an agent to diagnose whether an individual or team is blocked by limited resource posture, identify the next integration behavior, and design a 30-day experiment using resources outside current budget or headcount. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, leadership coaches, founders, and organizational designers use this skill to assess resource-integration maturity, diagnose blockers such as skill, access, habit, or permission, and choose one concrete next-level experiment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Organizational advice may be over-applied to situations where authority, structure, or policy genuinely prevents higher-level resource access. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's blocker diagnosis to distinguish skill, access, habit, and permission issues before assigning individual development actions. <br>
Risk: Level assessments can be distorted by aspiration or self-report. <br>
Mitigation: Require behavioral evidence from specific recent examples before selecting the current level or 30-day experiment. <br>
Risk: External-resource suggestions can affect relationships, budget expectations, or authority boundaries. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed experiments with appropriate stakeholders before acting on resources outside current budget or headcount. <br>
