## Description:

Applies the Storytelling with Data method (Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic) to turn analysis into clear, decision-driving charts, slides, dashboards and reports. Use when creating, critiquing or redesigning any chart, graph, table, data slide, deck, dashboard page or written report that presents data to an audience, including requests like "plot this", "make this slide clearer", "how should I show these numbers" or "build a deck to get budget approved". Covers audience and Big Idea, chart choice, decluttering, focusing attention with color and emphasis, design, and narrative structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Analysts and teams use this skill to create, critique, or redesign charts and data presentations for a specific audience and decision. It helps shape the message, choose and refine visuals, and structure reports or slide narratives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Emphasis and narrative framing can hide relevant patterns when the audience is still exploring data.

Mitigation: Keep exploratory charts neutral and complete; check the source, period, units, denominator, missing values, and contradictory evidence before making a recommendation.
