## Description:

Applies the Storytelling with Data method (Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic) to turn analysis into clear, decision-driving charts, slides, dashboards and reports. Use when creating, critiquing or redesigning any chart, graph, table, data slide, deck, dashboard page or written report that presents data to an audience, including requests like "plot this", "make this slide clearer", "how should I show these numbers" or "build a deck to get budget approved". Covers audience and Big Idea, chart choice, decluttering, focusing attention with color and emphasis, design, and narrative structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chart or narrative recommendations can mislead when source, period, units, denominators, missing values, or contradictory evidence are not handled clearly.

Mitigation: Check the source, period, units, and denominator; preserve missing values and estimates visibly; and keep exploratory charts neutral so emphasis does not hide other patterns.
