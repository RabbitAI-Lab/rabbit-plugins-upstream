## Description:

Generate a narration script with voiceover, shot list, per-clip visual prompts, and music cues for a personal travel-story video or essay from a thought-flow master document and the author's own photos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to turn a master story outline and personal photos into a structured narration script for a video or essay while preserving the source order and checking factual claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fact-checking may send parts of a personal story or factual claims to live web search.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive material or ask the agent to skip or narrow web verification when working with private personal details.

Risk: Narration quality depends on the provided master document and real local photos.

Mitigation: Confirm the master document, photo paths, duration, and target before generation, and omit claims that cannot be sourced.
