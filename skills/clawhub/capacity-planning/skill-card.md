## Description: <br>
Produce a capacity planning document for a service covering traffic forecasts, resource requirements, and scaling strategy. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, infrastructure owners, and engineering managers use this skill to produce capacity reviews for services. It structures current baseline metrics, growth assumptions, resource headroom, scaling strategy, cost projections, capacity triggers, and an action roadmap. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Capacity plans can contain sensitive operational details such as traffic levels, infrastructure choices, database capacity, and cost data. <br>
Mitigation: Use only data appropriate for the planning audience, redact or aggregate sensitive operational details when needed, and review the generated plan before sharing. <br>
