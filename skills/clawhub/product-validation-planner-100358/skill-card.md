## Description: <br>
Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototype workflows, positioning, checklists, and decision support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before heavy investment. It helps convert a live request into a repeatable validation workflow, checklist, template, analysis, code change, or decision-support artifact. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad product, validation, prototype, SaaS, or startup prompts where specialized legal, financial, security, or implementation advice may be needed. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the skill is appropriate for the request and route specialized legal, financial, security, or implementation decisions to qualified review. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs may rely on assumptions and general demand signals rather than the user's own customer, market, or operational evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Make assumptions visible and validate recommendations against user-provided evidence, success criteria, and customer feedback before major investment. <br>
