## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feng shui suggestions may be mistaken for reliable guidance on major home decisions.

Mitigation: Treat them as reference or entertainment and check practical considerations before making renovation decisions.

Risk: Floor plans, home photos, or contact with the listed external storefront may expose personal information.

Mitigation: Share only necessary, non-sensitive details; treat author contact and storefront links as optional.
