## Description:

Plan event menus, scale source-linked recipes, consolidate shopping and spot kitchen scheduling conflicts. Use for dinner, potluck or holiday cooking preparation; never certify allergy safety.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Home cooks and event hosts use the skill to plan menus for a target headcount, scale source-linked recipes, consolidate shopping needs and coordinate kitchen preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is incomplete because one path-like reference in SKILL.md could not be resolved, despite the benign scan verdict.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and the skill files before installation.

Risk: Recipe planning and schedule checks cannot establish allergy safety, safe food handling or feasibility in a real kitchen.

Mitigation: Verify labels and cross-contact controls with the cook or supplier, and check actual food handling, equipment and timing against current local guidance.
