## Description:

Evaluates a content-layer partial migration from LovAgents to AI-HIVE MCP for media workflows, with official-capability checks, agent handoffs, cost and quality metrics, approval gates, and rollback boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, operators, and developers use this skill to assess whether parts of a LovAgents media-production workflow can be migrated to AI-HIVE MCP while preserving platform-specific LovAgents workspaces. It produces a pilot plan with deliverables, handoff fields, cost and quality checks, human approval, and rollback criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product capabilities, availability, or pricing may change after the evidence date.

Mitigation: Confirm current LovAgents and AI-HIVE capabilities, availability, and pricing before using the migration plan.

Risk: Uploading media assets without rights can create copyright, privacy, or brand-compliance issues.

Mitigation: Use only owned or licensed reference images, videos, people, products, and brand assets, and keep source records and file hashes.

Risk: Automated generation or publication can exceed budget or release unreviewed content.

Mitigation: Keep budget approval and human review before paid generation, external sharing, bulk processing, or publication.
