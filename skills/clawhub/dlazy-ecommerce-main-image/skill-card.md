## Description:

Creates ecommerce main-image candidates and single-variable A/B variants from approved product photos while preserving product details and checking marketplace requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and ecommerce creative teams use this skill to draft product listing images and controlled A/B image variants for marketplace campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos and prompts are sent to dLazy's hosted service, and generated images are hosted there.

Mitigation: Use only approved commercial images and avoid uploading sensitive or unlicensed materials.

Risk: Generated images can misrepresent a product or violate marketplace image rules.

Mitigation: Review product details and current platform requirements before publishing.

Risk: Signing in may save an API key locally.

Mitigation: Keep the local API key private and revoke it if compromised.
