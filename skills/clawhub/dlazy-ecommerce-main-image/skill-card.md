## Description:

Generates and edits ecommerce main-image candidates from approved product photos, with guidance for accurate product details and single-variable A/B tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and creative teams use this skill to create main-image candidates for product listings, including white-background baselines, lifestyle scenes, color variants, and controlled A/B groups. It emphasizes preserving product facts and checking outputs before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and product images are sent to dLazy for generation, and generated product details may need correction.

Mitigation: Use images and prompts you are permitted to share, protect the saved API key, preview credit costs before batches, and review generated product details before publishing.
