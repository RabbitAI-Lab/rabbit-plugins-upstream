## Description:

Guides ecommerce sellers in creating product-accurate main-image candidates and single-variable A/B variants from approved product photos using dLazy image generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and creative teams use this skill to prepare approved product photos, generate main-image candidates for different storefront formats, and compare controlled A/B variants before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos are uploaded to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Upload only approved, non-sensitive product assets.

Risk: Generated images may misrepresent products or violate storefront rules.

Mitigation: Review product details and current platform requirements before publication.

Risk: A retained dLazy API key may allow continued access.

Mitigation: Rotate or revoke the key when access is no longer needed.
