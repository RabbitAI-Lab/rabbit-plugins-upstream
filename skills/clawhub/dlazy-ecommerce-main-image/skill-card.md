## Description:

Creates and edits ecommerce primary image candidates from approved product photos, with attention to product accuracy, channel requirements, and single-variable A/B testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and creative teams use the skill to create listing image candidates from approved product photos, plan channel-specific compositions, and prepare controlled image tests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and supplied local images are sent to dLazy-hosted services, and the security scan did not include the CLI package.

Mitigation: Review the CLI package and account requirements before use, and submit only approved images and content suitable for processing by the service.
