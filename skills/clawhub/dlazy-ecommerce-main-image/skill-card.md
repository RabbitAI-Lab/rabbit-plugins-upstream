## Description:

Helps merchants create accurate ecommerce main-image candidates and single-variable A/B variants from approved product photos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and ecommerce creative teams use this skill to plan and generate product listing images, color variants, and controlled A/B candidates while checking product accuracy and channel requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos, prompts, and generated images are sent through dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Use approved product assets, and confirm permission before sending sensitive or unreleased materials.

Risk: Generated images may misrepresent product details or violate listing rules.

Mitigation: Compare each image with the approved SKU and check current channel requirements before publication.

Risk: Batch generation can spend credits on unwanted outputs.

Mitigation: Check help and use a dry run to review parameters and estimated cost before generating batches.
