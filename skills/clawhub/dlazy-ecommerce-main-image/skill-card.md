## Description:

Guides ecommerce teams in creating product-faithful main-image candidates and single-variable A/B test variants from approved product photos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and creative teams use this skill to plan accurate product listing images, draft image-generation prompts and commands, and compare controlled A/B variants before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos and prompts sent to the hosted service may contain sensitive or unapproved content.

Mitigation: Use only approved, non-sensitive product assets and confirm the service is trusted before uploading.

Risk: An exposed account API key could allow unauthorized use.

Mitigation: Keep the key in your own account and rotate it through the dashboard if exposed.

Risk: Generated images may misrepresent product details or violate marketplace image rules.

Mitigation: Compare outputs with the approved product photos and check current platform rules before listing.
