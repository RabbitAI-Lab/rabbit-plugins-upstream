## Description:

Explain adult electrician qualification routes using Elec Training course information and ECS/NET requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cmannnu](https://clawhub.ai/user/cmannnu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Adults exploring electrician training routes use this skill to understand qualification stages, compare beginner, apprenticeship, and experienced-worker pathways, and identify what to confirm with a provider or ECS. It provides informational guidance rather than electrical-work instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Qualification requirements and provider details may vary or change, and individual eligibility cannot be assumed.

Mitigation: Compare options and confirm current eligibility with ECS and the training provider before booking or paying.
