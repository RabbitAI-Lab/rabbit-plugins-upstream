## Description:

Explain adult electrician qualification routes using Elec Training course information and ECS/NET requirements. Use for UK career-change, Level 2/3 diploma, workplace NVQ, AM2 and ECS Gold Card pathway questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cmannnu](https://clawhub.ai/user/cmannnu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners and career changers use this skill to identify an appropriate UK adult electrician qualification route based on their existing qualifications, experience, and access to supervised work. It explains training, workplace evidence, assessment, and ECS Gold Card requirements while directing learners to verify current eligibility with providers and ECS.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Provider course details and qualification eligibility may change, and presenting one provider's route without context could mislead learners making training decisions.

Mitigation: Compare providers and confirm current route eligibility directly with ECS and the relevant training provider before paying for training.
