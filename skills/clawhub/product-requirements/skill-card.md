## Description:

Turns Amazon review pain points, product attributes, and usage scenarios into verifiable product requirements and acceptance questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product teams use the skill to turn review evidence, product details, and usage scenarios into verifiable requirements and acceptance questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The tool exposes broader account controls and may spend credits without consistently obtaining approval.

Mitigation: Prefer a corrected package limited to requirements work. If using this release, set ARI to ask before every charge, request manual collection, review existing product and competitor schedules, and do not treat an unanswered quote as consent.
