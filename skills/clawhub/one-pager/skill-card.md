## Description: <br>
Distil anything - a startup, product, project, or idea - into a single persuasive page with a headline, tagline, problem, solution, proof, and clear ask. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External users, employees, founders, product teams, and project leads use this skill to turn a startup, product, project, or idea into a concise one-page brief or leave-behind with a specific audience and call to action. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill shapes agent writing and may produce unsupported or misleading claims if the source material is thin or ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Review the one-pager against the supplied facts, require concrete proof for claims, and keep the final ask specific and auditable. <br>
Risk: The skill changes agent behavior through documentation instructions even though it contains no executable code. <br>
Mitigation: Read SKILL.md before installing or using the skill and apply normal review for agent-influencing instructions. <br>
