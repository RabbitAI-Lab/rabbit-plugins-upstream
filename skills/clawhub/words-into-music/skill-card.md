## Description:

Creates original songs or instrumentals from ideas or lyrics, tracks generation progress, and finds recordings in a connected private Words into Music library.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[constantinginga](https://clawhub.ai/user/constantinginga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating songs for occasions or personal stories use this skill to turn a brief or their own lyrics into a recording, check its progress, and retrieve it from their private library.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Starting a song spends one existing song credit.

Mitigation: Show the brief and one-credit cost, and obtain the user's authorization before starting generation or any fresh paid retry.

Risk: The connected account's private song library and submitted briefs or lyrics may contain sensitive content.

Mitigation: Access the library only on request, avoid submitting private content as a demonstration, and never ask for passwords, cookies, or backend keys.
