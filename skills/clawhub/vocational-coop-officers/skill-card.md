## Description:

An industry operations framework for vocational education partnerships and HR services, with 15 domains, 125 capability units, 10 workflows, and reusable deliverable examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Vocational education partnership providers and HR service teams use this skill to plan and coordinate school outreach, program co-development, internship placement and management, channel operations, project pricing, compliance reviews, and internal knowledge materials. It provides reusable workflows and document structures that teams can calibrate to their organization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-oriented; verify current local regulations, policies, and school requirements before use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event registration details and photos may expose participant information.

Mitigation: Before collection, define participant notice, photo permission, access, and deletion rules.

Risk: Reimbursement evidence may contain unnecessary personal or sensitive details.

Mitigation: Use authorized business records and prefer redacted or aggregate evidence where possible.

Risk: Operational recommendations could be treated as approved decisions before required review.

Mitigation: Retain the workflow's human approval checkpoints.
