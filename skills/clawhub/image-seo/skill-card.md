## Description:

Audit and fix a page's images for search, speed and accessibility, including alt text, filenames, responsive sizing, lazy loading, LCP images, image sitemaps, social previews and licence metadata.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Site owners, SEO practitioners and developers use this skill to review images on pages or templates and get prioritized improvements for accessibility, image search visibility, loading performance and social previews. It also provides CMS-ready alt text and implementation examples.
