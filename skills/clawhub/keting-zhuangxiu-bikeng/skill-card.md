## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The living-room label is narrower than the bundled knowledge base, which also includes legal, real-estate, contractor, and broader renovation advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as general renovation guidance, keep answers scoped to the user’s actual room and budget, and verify legal, contract, property, and contractor questions with qualified local professionals.

Risk: Some renovation suggestions involve physical changes such as wall removal, electrical layout, waterproofing, balcony changes, and installation work.

Mitigation: Before acting, confirm structural limits, property rules, permits, waterproofing scope, and electrical safety requirements with qualified professionals.
