## Description:

诗遇 Poetry Resonance connects Tang and Song poetry with everyday moments by matching poems, explaining them in plain language, and generating daily quote cards, study notes, and social captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to reconnect with Chinese poetry through plain-language interpretation, poem matching for real-life situations, study notes, daily quote cards, and concise social captions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Poem quotations or historical interpretations may be inaccurate when the built-in poem library does not cover a request.

Mitigation: Prefer the bundled poem library and clearly mark any uncataloged poem text or uncertain historical detail as needing verification.

Risk: Optional image generation or note-saving workflows may send user-provided photos, prompts, or notes to external tools or storage chosen by the user.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit user confirmation before using image generation or saving notes, and avoid including sensitive personal content unless the user provides it deliberately.
