## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Subscription costs, renewal terms, and activation timing may affect users.

Mitigation: Before paying, review the current price, renewal and cancellation terms, and any manual activation delay on the official subscription page.

Risk: Registration and payment involve sensitive account information.

Mitigation: Complete registration and payment on the platform page; do not share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat.
