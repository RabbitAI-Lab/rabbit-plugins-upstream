Security audit

xiaoai-bridge

Security checks for vulnerabilities and agentic risk

Overview

The voice bridge’s account access, background listening, and optional transcript sharing are clearly disclosed and fit its purpose.

Use a dedicated Xiaomi account and protect both .env and cached sessions like passwords. Leave JEV_API_KEY unset unless you accept sharing new transcripts, including speech without the trigger prefix, with Jev. Avoid retaining stdout or stderr: they can contain speech or transcript excerpts. Require confirmation before downstream payments, deletion, or security-device actions. Update the affected dependency to a patched release; the reviewed artifacts do not demonstrate an exploitable path. This review inspected bundled source without executing installed dependencies or live services.

Description-Behavior Mismatch

High
Category
MCP Tool Poisoning
Confidence
95% confidence
Finding

The main bridge and TTS behavior match the description; credential caching, diagnostics, and cleanup are supporting implementation details. However, the description does not disclose the additional external recipient of voice transcripts. In the Jev-enabled listening path, the code calls jev.decide(transcript.originalText, ...) before deciding whether to forward a message, rather than limiting submission to trigger-matched commands. This is a material, configuration-dependent expansion of voice-data sharing and filtering behavior. The runtime disclosure does not make it part of the supplied declared description.
