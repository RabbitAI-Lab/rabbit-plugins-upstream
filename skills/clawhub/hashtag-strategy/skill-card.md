## Description: <br>
Builds modern social discoverability guidance that prioritizes keywords and social SEO before concise, platform-specific hashtag sets. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, creators, and social media operators use this skill to turn brand, audience, and post context into keyword-first social discoverability guidance and compact hashtag sets for each platform. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use brand-profile.md and audience.md to tailor recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Keep those files limited to marketing context that is appropriate for hashtag and discoverability guidance. <br>
Risk: Social platform limits and hashtag behavior can change after release. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current platform limits and rules before publishing recommendations. <br>
