## Use Case:

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analytics may contain sensitive business performance information.

Mitigation: Share summaries only with authorized recipients and avoid exposing unnecessary post or account details.

Risk: Unsupported or delayed platform metrics can appear as zeros and mislead readers.

Mitigation: Check supported metrics and sync status before treating zero as a measured result.

Risk: Combining tracked link clicks with platform clicks can double-count visits.

Mitigation: Report the two click measures separately.
