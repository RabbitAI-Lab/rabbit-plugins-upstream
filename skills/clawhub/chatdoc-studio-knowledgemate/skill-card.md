## Description:

Creates ChatDOC Studio knowledge bases from PDF and Word documents and supports searching and reading their contents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[paodingai](https://clawhub.ai/user/paodingai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and knowledge workers use this skill to upload selected PDF, DOC, or DOCX files to ChatDOC Studio, create a knowledge base, and retrieve or inspect its documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected documents and query text are sent to PAODINGAI/ChatDOC Studio for remote processing.

Mitigation: Use confidential, regulated, or customer documents only after the remote processing and retention model is approved.

Risk: Installing an unreviewed package may introduce changes to the skill.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or reviewed package source when installing.
