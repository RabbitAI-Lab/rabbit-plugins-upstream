## Description: <br>
Generates an informational daily crypto market morning brief from public web searches, covering market overview, BTC and ETH trends, macro and regulatory news, risk notes, and sources. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, analysts, and agent operators use this skill to collect current public crypto market information and turn it into a structured morning brief. The output is informational and should not be treated as investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Crypto prices, market conditions, and regulatory news can change quickly or appear inaccurately in search results. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important prices, dates, and regulatory claims against cited reputable sources before relying on the brief. <br>
Risk: A generated market brief may be mistaken for investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present the brief as informational only, keep risk warnings visible, and avoid unsupported buy or sell recommendations. <br>
