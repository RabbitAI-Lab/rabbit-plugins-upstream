## Description:

Finds decision-makers at companies matching a customer profile and returns same-day checked work emails, fit notes, and a CRM-ready CSV.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scrollport](https://clawhub.ai/user/scrollport)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and business development users use this skill to build a prospect list from a customer profile. It returns decision-makers with fit notes and checked work emails for CRM review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer descriptions may be shared with external search services and could contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential customer details unless their sharing is permitted; disclose the external search use to the user.

Risk: Some local reference checks were incomplete, so the security evidence is not comprehensive.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its referenced materials before use, and inspect the live tools and their data handling before sharing sensitive information.
