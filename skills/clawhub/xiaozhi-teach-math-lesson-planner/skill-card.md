## Description:

帮助初高中数学教师设计包含概念建构、例题示范、变式训练、课堂小结和错例复盘的教案草稿。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school mathematics teachers use this skill to draft lesson frameworks, worked-example guidance, differentiated practice and misconception reviews. Teachers review and approve materials before classroom use or storage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize curriculum and emergency guidance outside mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student aliases, work samples and classroom records may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Confirm school permission, omit real names, use a scoped workspace, and honor pause and no-sharing controls.

Risk: Generated questions or teaching examples may be mathematically incorrect or unsuitable for the class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher verify solutions and suitability before use or inclusion in a resource bank.

Risk: Curriculum examples and emergency guidance may not apply outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Adapt standards and support contacts to the local jurisdiction before use.
