## Description:

Helps middle- and high-school mathematics teachers turn concepts, worked examples, and varied practice into a classroom-ready lesson plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External middle- and high-school mathematics teachers use this skill to plan concept introductions, worked examples, varied practice, lesson summaries, and error-focused review lessons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information may be exposed through class summaries, storage, or sharing.

Mitigation: Use aliases and existing class summaries, review drafts before saving, and check host-platform storage and sharing controls.

Risk: Student-profile changes without consent or importing raw scores may mishandle student data.

Mitigation: Authorize profile updates only with consent and follow the main workflow's prohibition on raw-score imports.
