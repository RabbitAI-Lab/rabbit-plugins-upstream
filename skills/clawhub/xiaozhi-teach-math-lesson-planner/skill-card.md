## Description:

Helps Chinese-language junior and senior high school math teachers draft lesson plans with concept-building activities, worked examples, varied practice, and error analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Math teachers in mainland China use this skill to draft junior and senior high school lessons, including concept introductions, examples, differentiated practice, revision lessons, and exam reviews. Teachers review the plans and generated questions before using them with students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photos of student work and stored learning records may reveal student identities.

Mitigation: Use approved school systems, remove names and faces where possible, and follow local consent, access, and retention rules.

Risk: Unreviewed draft lessons or generated math questions may contain mistakes.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check lesson plans and verify generated questions before teaching or adding them to a resource library.
