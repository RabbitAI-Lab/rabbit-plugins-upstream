## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student information in lesson plans or handoffs may expose personal details.

Mitigation: Use student aliases and aggregate class data where possible; confirm consent before student-profile writeback or parent-facing sharing.

Risk: Generated math questions or teaching materials may contain errors.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check AI-generated questions and lesson drafts before classroom use or inclusion in a resource library.
