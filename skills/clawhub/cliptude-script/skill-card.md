## Description:

Research current facts and create Cliptude spoken narration for video topics or titles when the user has not provided narration or a presenter video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mwitiderrick](https://clawhub.ai/user/mwitiderrick)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to research a video topic and prepare spoken narration or a researched brief for Cliptude video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Topics and media may be shared with external services.

Mitigation: Submit only material you are comfortable sharing with external services.

Risk: Video generation can consume Cliptude credits.

Mitigation: Review the script, template, duration, and cost before submitting.
