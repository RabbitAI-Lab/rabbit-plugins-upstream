## Description: <br>
AI SEO helps agents audit and improve content so it is discoverable, extractable, and citable by AI search systems such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, content strategists, founders, and developers use this skill to assess AI-search visibility, improve content extractability, plan AI citation work, and understand platform-specific optimization tradeoffs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Guidance may encourage publishing crawler access, PDFs, pricing, llms.txt, OKF bundles, or other content that was previously private or gated. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that any newly public content has passed legal, privacy, licensing, and business review before publishing. <br>
Risk: AI-search recommendations can shape public marketing claims and competitive positioning. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated audit findings and optimization plans for factual accuracy, current platform behavior, and brand policy before implementation. <br>
