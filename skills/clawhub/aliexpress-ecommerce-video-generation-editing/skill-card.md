## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected prompts and product media may be uploaded to AI Hive for generation or editing.

Mitigation: Use only media approved for cloud processing, avoid sensitive product or customer data, and use a dedicated API key where possible.

Risk: Generated videos can include incorrect product claims, unsupported compatibility, or market-specific compliance issues.

Mitigation: Review model numbers, connectors, quantities, units, certifications, rights, and AliExpress category or market rules before publishing.

Risk: Generated outputs are saved locally by default.

Mitigation: Use no-download mode when local copies are not needed and manage the configured output directory according to local retention requirements.
