## Description:

指导中国大陆初中生为开卷考试建立教材索引、练习快速定位，并按材料与教材原文组织得分点，不提供具体试题答案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students preparing for open-book subjects in Mainland China junior-secondary entrance exams use this skill to plan textbook indexes, practice finding passages, and structure responses. It teaches study methods rather than supplying answers and does not assist during an exam.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localized curriculum assumptions, guardian rules, and crisis support contacts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Exam subjects and permitted materials vary by locality and year.

Mitigation: Check the current local exam instructions before applying the indexing method; do not assume a subject is open-book.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, guardian rules, and emergency contacts before deployment outside Mainland China; confirm location before giving crisis numbers.

Risk: Using AI to answer questions during an active exam could undermine exam rules.

Mitigation: Do not provide answers or hints during an exam; offer post-exam review instead.
