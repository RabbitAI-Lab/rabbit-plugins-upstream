## Description:

Helps junior-high students prepare for open-book exams by indexing textbooks, practicing fast retrieval, and structuring answer points without supplying specific answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Junior-high students in mainland China use this skill before open-book exams to make textbook indexes, rehearse quick retrieval, and organize material with textbook passages into answer points. It teaches study methods rather than answering specific questions or providing civics viewpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese K12 learning context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local exam formats, curriculum assumptions, and crisis contacts may be wrong outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Use in the intended Simplified Chinese mainland-China K12 setting; verify current local exam rules and localize curriculum, minor-safety guidance, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

Risk: Advice could be mistaken for permission to use AI during an active open-book exam.

Mitigation: Keep coaching to preparation and post-exam review; decline question-specific help during an active exam.
