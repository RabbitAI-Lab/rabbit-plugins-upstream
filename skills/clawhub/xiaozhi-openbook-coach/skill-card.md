## Description:

Coaches junior-secondary students on textbook indexing, rapid retrieval, and organizing evidence for open-book exams without supplying subject-specific answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators in mainland China's junior-secondary open-book exam context use this skill to plan textbook indexes, practice locating passages, and structure responses around source material. It does not provide answers to specific exam questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese junior-secondary exam context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local exam subjects, permitted materials, and grading rules can change.

Mitigation: Check the current local exam notice and scoring guidance rather than assuming any subject is open-book.

Risk: Crisis and emergency contacts may be unsuitable outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and localize help resources before deployment outside the intended region.

Risk: Coaching during an active exam could undermine exam rules.

Mitigation: Provide preparation and post-exam review only; decline question-level help while a test is underway.
