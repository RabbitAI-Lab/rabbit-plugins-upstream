## Description:

帮助中国大陆初中生准备开卷考试，练习教材索引、快速定位和按教材原文组织答题要点，而不代答具体试题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初中开卷考试的学生，指导考前建立三级教材索引、练习定位，并根据教材原文组织得分点；不提供具体试题答案，也不在考试进行中提供提示。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese-language K12 exam preparation)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, open-book rules and emergency contacts may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Check the current local exam instructions and localize language, curriculum and crisis contacts before use in other regions.

Risk: Coaching during an active exam could undermine exam integrity.

Mitigation: Do not provide answers or hints during an exam; offer post-exam review instead.

Risk: A student may disclose distress or immediate danger during study coaching.

Mitigation: Pause coaching, direct the student to a trusted adult and provide location-appropriate emergency support.
