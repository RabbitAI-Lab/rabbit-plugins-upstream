## Description:

Coaches Chinese middle-school students on textbook indexing, timed lookup, and structuring source-grounded responses for open-book exams without supplying answers to specific questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Middle-school students in mainland China use this skill before open-book exams to build textbook indexes, practice finding passages quickly, and organize answers around the exam material and textbook wording. It does not solve specific exam questions or provide political or civics viewpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Simplified Chinese middle-school education); localize crisis contacts and exam guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-specific crisis contacts or exam assumptions may be wrong outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize crisis contacts, school and legal assumptions, and exam guidance before deploying elsewhere; verify the current local exam rules.

Risk: Using coaching during an active exam can undermine exam integrity.

Mitigation: Do not provide question-specific help during an active exam or test; offer post-exam review instead.

Risk: Students may mistake suggested answer structure for authoritative subject matter or local scoring rules.

Mitigation: Direct subject-specific claims to the textbook and consult the applicable local scoring criteria.
