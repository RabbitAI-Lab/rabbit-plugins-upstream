## Description:

为中国大陆初中生提供开卷考试的教材索引、快速定位和分点作答方法，不代答具体考题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

中国大陆初中生在考前学习如何为开卷科目建立教材索引、练习快速定位，并将材料与教材原文组织成得分点；教师和家长也可用其指导备考。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students could seek assistance during an active exam.

Mitigation: Do not provide answers or hints during an active exam; offer to review the method afterward.

Risk: Curriculum, exam rules, and emergency contacts may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Check current local exam guidance and localize language, curriculum assumptions, and crisis referrals before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Students may mistake method coaching for authoritative subject answers.

Mitigation: Refer civics viewpoints to the student's textbook and subject-specific questions to appropriate teachers or subject resources.
