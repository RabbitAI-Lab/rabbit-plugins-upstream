## Description:

Coaches Chinese middle-school students on preparing textbook indexes, locating source passages, and organizing points for open-book exams without supplying answers to specific questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle-school students use this skill before open-book exams to build textbook indexes, practice finding passages quickly, and structure responses around the source material. It does not answer live exam questions or supply subject-specific viewpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; localize curriculum assumptions, language, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local exam subjects, permitted materials, and scoring rules can vary or change.

Mitigation: Check the current local exam instructions and scoring criteria rather than relying on generic examples.

Risk: Assistance during an exam could compromise exam integrity.

Mitigation: Provide preparation and post-exam review only; decline questions or hints during a live exam.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and curriculum assumptions may not fit students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's region and localize safety referrals, wording, and curriculum before deployment outside mainland China.
