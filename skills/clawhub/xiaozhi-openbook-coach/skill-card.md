## Description:

指导初中生为开卷考试建立教材索引、练习快速定位，并用教材原文与题目材料组织得分点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆初中生的开卷考试备考指导：建立教材索引、练习定位，并学习如何结合材料与教材原文组织答题要点；不提供具体题目的答案。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；在其他地区使用前须核对当地课程、监护人同意要求并本地化危机求助渠道。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China curriculum, guardian-consent rules, and crisis contacts may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Restrict use to the intended region or localize those requirements and contacts before deployment.

Risk: Using AI coaching during an active exam or test could undermine exam integrity.

Mitigation: Provide preparation and post-exam review only; do not assist with an active exam or test.
