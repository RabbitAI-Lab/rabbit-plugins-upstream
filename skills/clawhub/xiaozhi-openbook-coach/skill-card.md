## Description:

Guides junior-high students in preparing textbook indexes, finding passages quickly, and organizing source-based responses for open-book exams without providing specific answers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Junior-high students in mainland China use this skill to prepare for local open-book exams by indexing textbooks, practicing retrieval, and structuring responses around textbook passages. It teaches study methods, not subject-matter answers or live-exam assistance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize exam rules and crisis-contact guidance before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local open-book exam rules and permitted materials may differ or change.

Mitigation: Ask students to verify current local exam instructions rather than assuming which subjects or materials are allowed.

Risk: Coaching during an active exam could undermine exam integrity.

Mitigation: Do not provide hints or answers during an exam; offer a review after it ends.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and localize referral contacts before providing region-specific crisis guidance.
