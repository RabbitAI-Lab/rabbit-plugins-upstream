## Description:

为初中开卷考试提供教材索引、考场定位和得分点组织方法指导。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students preparing for Chinese middle-school open-book exams use this coach to build textbook indexes, practice finding relevant passages quickly, and organize source material into scored response points. It teaches methods only and directs students to textbook wording for moral and civic viewpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese-language middle-school exam preparation)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shared worked-answer guidance may conflict with the coach's methods-only scope.

Mitigation: Keep responses to indexing, locating, and answer-organization methods; direct moral and civic viewpoints to textbook wording, and do not assist with questions during an active exam.
