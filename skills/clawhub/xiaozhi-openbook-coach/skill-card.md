## Description:

指导中国大陆初中生为开卷考试建立教材索引、练习快速定位，并用教材原文和题目材料组织答题要点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students preparing for China-mainland junior-secondary open-book exams use this skill to plan textbook indexes, practice finding source passages, and structure answers. It teaches methods rather than supplying answers to specific exam questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland; localize curriculum, language, legal defaults, and crisis-help channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China-mainland curriculum and crisis-help assumptions may mislead students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Use in the disclosed Chinese K12 context or localize language, curriculum, legal defaults, and emergency contacts first.

Risk: Using an AI coach during an active exam could compromise exam integrity.

Mitigation: Do not provide answers or hints during an exam; offer a post-exam review instead.
