## Description: <br>
Helps authors, illustrators, and publishing teams plan book illustration needs, match visual styles, assign illustrations to chapters, and manage basic review workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Authors, illustrators, and publishing teams use this skill to turn a manuscript or book concept into a structured illustration plan, including style direction, placement strategy, creative guidance, and basic project milestones. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for write and shell-command permissions even though its main purpose is book-illustration planning guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Install only in workspaces where those permissions are acceptable, and prefer a version that removes exec or review any proposed command before running it. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes loosely scoped activation and callback URL behavior that could affect private manuscripts or project files. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for book-illustration planning tasks, avoid sensitive manuscript content unless callback behavior is explained, and prefer versions with narrower activation. <br>
