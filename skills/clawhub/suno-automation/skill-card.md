## Use Case: <br>
Developers and end users can use this skill to guide an agent through Suno's browser UI for AI music creation, including entering song details, starting generation, and playing the resulting track. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent operates a logged-in Suno account and may create songs, use credits, or affect account visibility and sharing settings. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with a Suno account you are comfortable automating, review credit, billing, visibility, and sharing settings first, and give explicit approval before the agent clicks Create. <br>
