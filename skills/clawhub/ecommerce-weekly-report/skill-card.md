## Description: <br>
Organizes one week of ecommerce performance data into a management-ready weekly report covering KPI changes, anomaly explanations, profit-quality assessment, and next-week action items. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Ecommerce operators, analysts, and managers use this skill to turn weekly sales, order, advertising, refund, and margin data into review-ready business reporting with KPI comparison, anomaly attribution, risk notes, and prioritized next actions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive business performance data. <br>
Mitigation: Use only data that is appropriate to share with the agent and avoid unnecessary confidential detail. <br>
Risk: Incorrect or unverified financial calculations could lead to poor business decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Verify KPI calculations, margin figures, and high-impact conclusions against source data before using the report for decisions. <br>
Risk: Inconsistent reporting periods, tax treatment, currency, or channel attribution can make week-over-week comparisons misleading. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the reporting basis before analysis and explicitly label any metric gaps, estimates, or scope changes in the report. <br>
