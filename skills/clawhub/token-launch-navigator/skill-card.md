## Description:

Guides Web3 teams through token launch planning across jurisdiction selection, tokenomics, exchange listing strategy, vesting, liquidity, community launch, and security audit checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and token launch operators use this skill to plan token generation events, review launch readiness, structure tokenomics and vesting, compare jurisdictions, and prepare exchange, liquidity, community, and audit checklists. It is a planning aid and does not replace qualified legal, tax, financial, securities, sanctions, custody, or regulatory counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Token launch guidance can be mistaken for legal, tax, financial, regulatory, sanctions, custody, or securities advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning and checklist aid only, retain qualified counsel for each relevant jurisdiction, and preserve the mandatory risk disclaimer in outputs.

Risk: Jurisdiction-specific guidance may be incomplete or misunderstood, especially in multilingual outputs.

Mitigation: Verify critical instructions with qualified counsel and prefer a single output language for final launch instructions and compliance checklists.

Risk: The skill may generate code templates or process checklists that users could implement incorrectly.

Mitigation: Require human review, external security audits, and wallet/operator approval before deploying contracts, filing documents, contacting exchanges, or executing launch actions.
