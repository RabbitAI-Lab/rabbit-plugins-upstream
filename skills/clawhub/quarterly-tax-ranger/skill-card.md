## Description:

Estimates federal quarterly tax payments and set-asides for U.S. freelancers and self-employed earners, with an annualized-income option for uneven earnings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

U.S. freelancers and self-employed earners can use the skill to estimate federal quarterly payments and set-asides, including when income varies across the year. Treat its calculations as budgeting estimates and verify payment amounts and eligibility before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (federal estimates; state amounts are a flat-rate approximation)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Verified calculation errors make the skill's penalty-free payment claims unreliable.

Mitigation: Use the output only for rough budgeting. Verify payment amounts and eligibility against IRS Form 1040-ES and Form 2210 Schedule AI, or with a qualified tax professional, before acting.
