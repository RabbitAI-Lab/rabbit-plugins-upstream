## Description:

Create a PostHog report for each completed website change, mark verified production releases, and review whether business outcomes improved. Use when finishing or shipping a website update, setting up automatic reporting for future releases, or running a due impact review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Website owners, product and marketing teams, and developers use this skill to define a business outcome and baseline for a website change, document a verified production release, and compare post-release results in PostHog.
