## Description: <br>
Guides agents through diagnosing and redesigning incentive systems for compensation, OKRs, contracts, platform rules, and AI reward or pricing structures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, operators, founders, managers, policy designers, and product teams use this skill to map current incentives, diagnose misalignment, design aligned rewards or penalties, anticipate Goodhart-style gaming, and define monitoring. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs may influence compensation, contracts, organizational policy, or other consequential incentive decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and obtain appropriate HR, legal, or domain review before making consequential changes. <br>
