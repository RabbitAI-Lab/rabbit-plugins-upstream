A few useful answers.

A few useful details.

Can I get samples before committing to a food?

Yes. Most foods have a sample pouch for $2, refunded against the first full bag. Add it from the product page.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, bank transfer and the usual mobile wallets. Refill orders are charged the day they ship, never before.

Can I change a refill order?

Any time up to the day before it ships. Swap the flavour, change the size or move the date from the reminder text or your account.

A few practical details.

My pet is new. Where should I start?

The care guide has a first-week list for puppies, kittens and rescues. Or write to our vets with the breed and age and they will suggest a starter box.

Do you sell prescription diets?

Not on the site. If your vet has prescribed a diet, send us the prescription and we will source it at the same price and add it to your refills.

Do you have a shop I can visit?

One, on Harbor Street in Rivergate, open seven days. Bring them along; there is a water bowl by the door and a scale in the back if you need a weight.

A calmer bath day

A calmer bath-day routine.

Gather the essentials

Put the items you need within reach before beginning. Read the product label and choose products intended for the animal and task. If a product or routine is unfamiliar, ask an appropriate professional rather than treating a sample storefront as care advice.

A familiar routine starts with preparation.

Keep the setting calm

Make the surrounding area easy to move through and avoid leaving equipment where it can be knocked over. Work at a pace suited to the individual animal. The goal is a manageable routine with as little unnecessary handling as possible.

Finish by resetting the space

Clean and store the items according to their care instructions. Keep a note of anything you want to discuss with a professional. A well-prepared space makes the next routine simpler without requiring more products.

A place for everyday meals

A familiar place for everyday meals.

Begin with the space

Look at where your pet already chooses to rest and how people move through the room. A bed should have enough space around it without becoming an obstacle. Measure the available area before comparing product dimensions.

A resting place should fit the animal and the room.

Check the practical details

Read the material and care information for the specific product. A removable cover, a washable surface or a particular shape may suit one household better than another. The product image alone cannot tell you how it will fit into daily use.

Keep it familiar

Introduce changes gradually and keep the surrounding area straightforward. Notice how the space is used before adding more accessories. If rest or behaviour raises a concern, seek appropriate professional guidance.

The good in an everyday walk

Make room to enjoy a familiar route.

Check the fit

Use the size information for the specific lead or harness and measure as directed. Adjustability is useful, but it does not replace choosing the correct size. Check the condition of fastening points before use.

Make the everyday outing easier to prepare for.

Pack for the route

Bring the items needed for the length and setting of the walk. Keep them accessible without overloading the bag. Follow local rules and choose a route appropriate for the animal and the conditions.

Reset when you return

Inspect and clean equipment according to its care instructions. Give it a consistent storage place so the next outing begins without a search. A small routine makes the ordinary trip easier to repeat.

the dog park.

Delivery, refills and returns, answered plainly. For anything about a specific animal, our vets answer by message on weekdays.

All questions
How quickly will my order arrive?

Two working days to most addresses, next day on orders placed before noon. Food and litter ship in their own box so nothing gets squashed.

How do refills work?

Choose a product, pick a rhythm, and we send it before you run out. Change the date, skip a month or cancel by replying to the reminder text.

Can I return something they did not like?

Yes. Unopened food within 30 days for a full refund; opened food for store credit, no questions. Beds, harnesses and toys are covered by the fit promise.

Is the food suitable for a sensitive stomach?

Every food page lists the full ingredients and which sensitivities it is suited to. Our vets can suggest a match if you send them a note about your pet.

Do you deliver to flats and offices?

Anywhere with a door. Leave delivery instructions at checkout and the driver will follow them, including the safe place for a 12 kg bag of kibble.
