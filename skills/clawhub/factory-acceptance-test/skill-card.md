## Description: <br>
Writes factory acceptance test plans or reports with a test coverage matrix, AQL sampling plan, pass/fail criteria, golden-sample handling, deviation log, and sign-off structure. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External manufacturing, quality, sourcing, and product teams use this skill to draft auditable FAT plans or reports for outgoing quality inspection, pre-shipment inspection, factory acceptance, and lot disposition decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Incorrect AQL tables, sign-off authority, or safety-critical acceptance criteria could lead to an invalid lot disposition. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated sampling tables, accept/reject numbers, sign-off authority, and safety criteria against the user's quality standards before relying on the output. <br>
Risk: Thin or incomplete product specifications can leave test coverage assumptions unresolved. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unconfirmed coverage rows marked for confirmation and verify product requirements before final acceptance or reporting. <br>
