## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anti-detection browser automation guidance could be misused against platforms or accounts where automation is not explicitly permitted.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for clearly authorized security, QA, RPA, or account-management workflows, and avoid third-party platforms where automation violates policy or law.

Risk: The skill may require read, write, and exec access plus access to local secrets, browser profiles, cookies, and API credentials.

Mitigation: Review requested tool access before installation, run in an isolated workspace, and limit exposed credentials and browser profiles to the minimum needed for the task.
