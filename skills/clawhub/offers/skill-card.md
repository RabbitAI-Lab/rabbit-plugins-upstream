## Description: <br>
Offers helps agents design, diagnose, and improve what a business sells by structuring value framing, bonuses, guarantees, scarcity or urgency, naming, and payment terms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, founders, consultants, agencies, coaches, and creators use this skill to build or improve offers for services, courses, coaching, info products, high-ticket B2B, and direct-response businesses. It helps diagnose weak offers, apply the value equation, and draft stronger components such as bonuses, guarantees, scarcity, names, and payment structures. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Offer guidance could be misused to create misleading scarcity, exaggerated bonus value, or over-promising guarantees. <br>
Mitigation: Review customer-facing claims for factual support, clear guarantee conditions, and real scarcity before publication. <br>
Risk: The skill may use existing product-marketing context files when present. <br>
Mitigation: Keep product-marketing context files limited to information the agent is allowed to use for offer-design work. <br>
