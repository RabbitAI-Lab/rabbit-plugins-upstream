## Description:

Assists bank compliance, data, and regulatory teams with drafting and checking NFRA/PBOC/SAFE regulatory reports, 1104 statistical submissions, Basel III capital reports, AML materials, and compliance documentation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gechengling](https://clawhub.ai/user/gechengling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bank compliance officers, regulatory affairs teams, data analysts, and IT reporting teams use this skill to identify required Chinese banking regulatory reports, assemble data checklists, draft report sections, and review filings for consistency before human approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory references, filing requirements, or report conclusions may be outdated or incorrect.

Mitigation: Verify all regulatory references against official sources before relying on generated material.

Risk: Generated reports could be mistaken for approved filings or formal legal/compliance decisions.

Mitigation: Require authorized compliance or legal approval before using outputs for filings, policy changes, or risk decisions.

Risk: Customer, transaction, or supervisory information used in prompts may be confidential.

Mitigation: Keep customer and supervisory information confidential and avoid unnecessary sensitive data exposure.
