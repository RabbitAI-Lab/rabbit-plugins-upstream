## Description: <br>
Writes persuasive marketing copy across formats, covering tone, structure, clarity, and the discipline of selling benefits rather than features. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and content creators use this skill to draft and refine persuasive copy for landing pages, emails, ads, product pages, and calls to action. It emphasizes clear benefits, specific proof, audience-appropriate tone, and ethical persuasion. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated marketing copy may overstate product claims or fail to match brand and advertising requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Review copy for factual accuracy, brand fit, and applicable advertising compliance before publication. <br>
Risk: Persuasion tactics such as scarcity, testimonials, metrics, and loss framing can become misleading if applied without evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Use only truthful urgency, verified testimonials, supported metrics, and audience-appropriate framing. <br>
