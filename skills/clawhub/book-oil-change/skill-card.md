Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, date, time, and contact details with the user before creating a booking. <br>
Risk: The skill is narrowly scoped to oil-change booking and may not be appropriate for general automotive advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for oil-change search, availability, and booking tasks. <br>
