# Pharmacy Star Ratings: Specification Review Checklist

CMS Star Ratings measures, weights, cutpoints, drug lists, exclusions, and calculation details can change. **Use the official specification for the measurement year under review.** This document is a checklist, not a substitute for the specification.

## For each measure, confirm
1. The full measure name, identifier, year, and applicable population.
2. Numerator, denominator, enrollment/fill requirements, exceptions, and exclusions.
3. Drug classes and combination-product treatment, if applicable.
4. Whether the measure is scored, displayed only, or retired that year.
5. Applicable weight and any risk-adjustment or methodology changes.
6. Published cutpoints and whether the analysis concerns a contract, plan benefit package, or other reporting unit.
7. Directionality: for some safety measures, **lower** rates represent better performance.

## Areas to review
- Medication adherence measures for diabetes medications, renin-angiotensin system antagonists, and statins.
- Statin use in persons with diabetes and how it differs from statin adherence.
- Medication therapy management / comprehensive medication review measures.
- Applicable polypharmacy and medication-safety measures.

## Reporting template
For each conclusion, identify the measure and reporting year; cite the exact source; state what data were supplied; describe gaps and exclusions; and separate confirmed results from estimates. Never claim a specific Star Rating impact without the correct current cutpoints and required data.

## Primary sources
- CMS Medicare Advantage and Part D performance data and technical notes: https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-drug-plans/part-c-d-performance-data
- CMS regulations and annual final rules: https://www.cms.gov/
- Pharmacy Quality Alliance (measure steward, where applicable): https://www.pqaalliance.org/

If a rule or technical note is unavailable, explicitly state that the status could not be verified.
