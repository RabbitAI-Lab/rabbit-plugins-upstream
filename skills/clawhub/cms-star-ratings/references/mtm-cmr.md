# Medication Therapy Management / Comprehensive Medication Reviews

A comprehensive medication review (CMR) is a clinical service, not merely a quality-measure checkbox. Verify current Medicare Part D MTM requirements and the applicable Star Ratings scoring status before using this material for reporting.

## Preparing for a review
- Confirm eligibility and the applicable program requirements.
- Plan accessible outreach, including language assistance and authorized caregiver participation when appropriate.
- Request or reconcile the active medication list, relevant clinical history, allergies, and patient goals.

## During a review
- Reconcile medications, including over-the-counter products and supplements.
- Ask about efficacy, adverse effects, duplications, access, affordability, and adherence barriers.
- Identify questions for an authorized prescriber rather than issuing independent medication changes.
- Explain recommendations in patient-friendly language and invite questions.

## After a review
- Provide the required documentation, such as a personal medication list and medication action plan when applicable.
- Record agreed follow-up responsibilities and referrals.
- Evaluate clinical and patient-centered outcomes separately from reported measure completion.

## Fair access and safety
Do not reduce outreach priority because a person has limited English proficiency, disability, cognitive impairment, or needs caregiver support. Use appropriate accommodations. A documented safety issue may warrant clinician review regardless of any quality-measure opportunity.

This document intentionally does not include proprietary point-based clinical prioritization or fixed intervention-ranking rules.
