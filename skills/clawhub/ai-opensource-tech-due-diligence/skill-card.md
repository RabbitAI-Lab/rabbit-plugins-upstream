AI 开源项目技术尽调与公众号内容自动化 — GitHub Trending 监控 + 技术深度分析 + 公众号文章生成与发布 <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Technical bloggers, developer advocates, startup CTOs, and technical media creators use this skill to monitor GitHub Trending or selected repositories, produce technical due-diligence reports, generate WeChat-ready long-form articles, and save findings to Feishu. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can automate public WeChat publishing and Feishu knowledge-base creation without clear approval gates. <br>
Mitigation: Use draft-only workflows until review, approval, and rollback procedures are configured, and avoid private or sensitive repositories unless their analysis may be sent to the connected services. <br>
