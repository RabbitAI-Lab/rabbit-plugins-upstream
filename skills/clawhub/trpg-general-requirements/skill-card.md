This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External creators and developers use this skill to direct an agent to produce a complete Traditional Chinese TRPG rules package from a requirements specification, including player and GM rulebooks, scenarios, character sheets, maps, and optional catalogs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may create many files and remove intermediate drafts it identifies as process artifacts. <br>
Mitigation: Run it in a clean project folder and review the final directory before moving or publishing outputs. <br>
Risk: Generated adaptations of existing works may rely on web research and can include inaccurate or rights-sensitive material. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated files for factual accuracy, licensing, and publishing suitability before sharing them. <br>
