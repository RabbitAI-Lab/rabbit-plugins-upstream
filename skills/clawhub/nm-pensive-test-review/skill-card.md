## Description:

Evaluates test suites for coverage gaps, TDD/BDD compliance, and anti-patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to review test suites before releases, after test failures, or when planning improvements. It guides framework detection, coverage inventory, scenario quality review, and remediation planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate during broad testing or quality conversations.

Mitigation: Confirm that a test-suite review is intended before following its workflow.

Risk: Suggested test, coverage, or package-install commands may affect large or sensitive repositories.

Mitigation: Review commands before execution and run them in an appropriate local or isolated environment.
