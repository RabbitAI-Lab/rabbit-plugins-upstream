## Description:

Finds human-made visual, motion, type, and music references for creative projects and organizes candidates into a reviewable board.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creative professionals and developers use the skill to find and assess human-made references before making AI-generated or code-produced image, video, and music work. It organizes candidate links, evidence, and notes into a board for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill may involve sending project details or images to external search and model services.

Mitigation: Review each service and decide whether the project material is appropriate to share before running searches or model-assisted checks.

Risk: Installation examples may install packages or image-processing tools.

Mitigation: Review the relevant installation instructions and commands before installing dependencies.

Risk: The security scan was partial and could not resolve some local references, so its evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the skill files and their referenced components before deployment; the suspicious verdict is not itself a finding of malicious intent.
