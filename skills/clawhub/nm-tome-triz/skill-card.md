## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad prompts about innovation, analogy, or being stuck on a problem may activate the skill even when TRIZ was not specifically requested.

Mitigation: Confirm that TRIZ-style cross-domain brainstorming is desired before relying on the skill's recommendations.

Risk: Cross-domain analogies can suggest plausible but unvalidated solution paths.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as brainstorming guidance and validate proposed mappings against domain evidence before implementation.
