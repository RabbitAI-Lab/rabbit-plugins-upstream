## Description: <br>
OPC专家委员会知识蒸馏技能汇聚11位专家风格的思维框架，为战略研讨、商业模式设计、技术规划、品牌传播、合规风险和产品创新等场景提供多视角决策分析。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to request Chinese-language expert-committee style analysis for strategy, market positioning, product planning, risk review, marketing, copywriting, and design decisions. The skill selects one or more expert perspectives and returns structured judgments, insights, action recommendations, risk notes, and cross-expert consensus or disagreement. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce legal, policy, financial, market, or strategic claims that may be incomplete or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify high-impact claims with qualified reviewers and authoritative sources before taking action. <br>
Risk: Some viral-marketing and controversy-oriented guidance could create compliance or reputational risk. <br>
Mitigation: Review marketing recommendations for brand safety, platform rules, local law, and stakeholder impact before execution. <br>
Risk: Broad trigger coverage may cause the skill to answer beyond the strength of its reference material. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as decision support, request source-grounded reasoning when needed, and escalate domain-specific decisions to subject-matter experts. <br>
