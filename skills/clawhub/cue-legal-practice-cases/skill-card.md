## Description:

根据争议问题检索中国公开裁判文书、监管问答及实务案例，并整理类案裁判要点、争议焦点和行动参考。

## Use Case:

Individuals and legal practitioners use this skill to compare public Chinese cases and regulatory materials, identify disputed issues and prevailing approaches, and prepare preliminary, source-linked research for case assessment. Its output is a research reference, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Questions are sent unchanged to Cue and may contain confidential or identifying information.

Mitigation: Anonymize research questions and approve any confidential details before sending them.

Risk: The artifact gives conflicting assurances about programmatic API-key access.

Mitigation: Clarify API-key access and scope with the service provider, retain the ability to cancel, and do not expose the key in prompts or shared files.

Risk: The downloaded runner, local key file, and report links can introduce execution or disclosure risks.

Mitigation: Review and pin the runner before execution, protect the key file, and check report-link visibility before sharing.
