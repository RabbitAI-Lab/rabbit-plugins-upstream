## Description:

面向中国法律实务问题检索公开案例与监管资料，并整理裁判要点、争议焦点和带来源链接的参考报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

普通用户可用它了解同类纠纷的公开裁判情况，律师可用它检索类案、比较裁判观点并整理实务参考。输出用于研究和准备，不构成法律意见。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal questions are sent unchanged to the service, and case details may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Review and anonymize the query before submission; avoid confidential details until data retention and report-link access are understood.

Risk: The workflow handles account keys and runs externally updated code.

Mitigation: Keep keys private with owner-only file permissions, and review and pin the runner version before installing or running it.
