## Use Case:

External creators and operators use this skill to validate whether a niche has demand, analyze competitors across public channels, capture audience language, and turn the evidence into a clear positioning recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may spend unnecessary time or API quota if the niche, scope, or platforms are too broad.

Mitigation: Confirm the niche and target platforms before running the workflow, and limit the investigation to the decision the user needs.

Risk: Market metrics, social engagement, prices, or community counts can be stale, unavailable, or unverified.

Mitigation: Require source links for important numbers and mark older, estimated, unavailable, or unverified data directly in the brief.

Risk: Audience quotes or competitor claims can become misleading if paraphrased as evidence.

Mitigation: Use verbatim audience language only when it comes from real public comments, reviews, posts, or user-provided captures, and separate facts, inferences, and recommendations.
