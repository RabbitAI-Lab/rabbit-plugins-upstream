## Description: <br>
Case and research memory for legal work that helps agents preserve holdings, citations, and reasoning chains across sessions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm](https://clawhub.ai/user/bluecolumnconsulting-lgtm) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Legal researchers, attorneys, and agents assisting legal work use this skill to log research questions, sources, holdings, citations, open issues, and conclusions so later sessions can resume the same research context. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Potentially confidential legal research may be sent to and recalled from an external BlueColumn service. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm authorization, retention, deletion, access controls, and matter scoping before use; avoid storing client names, strategy, privileged analysis, or matter-specific facts unless approved. <br>
Risk: The skill examples do not describe matter-level scoping or user controls for stored legal research. <br>
Mitigation: Use separate approved accounts or workflows for distinct matters and review recalled material before relying on it in legal analysis. <br>
