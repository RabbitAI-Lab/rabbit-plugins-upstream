## Description:

Wealth Manager helps agents discuss a Chinese-language four-stage wealth framework for accumulation, growth, preservation, and inheritance, with supporting trading, risk-control, life-strategy references, and calculator artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for educational discussion of wealth-management stages, trading/risk-control concepts, asset-allocation themes, and inheritance-planning frameworks. It should not be used as personalized investment, legal, tax, immigration, or asset-protection advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat assertive financial, tax, offshore-structure, or cross-border asset-transfer guidance as personalized professional advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as educational only and direct users to licensed investment, legal, tax, immigration, and asset-protection professionals in the relevant jurisdictions before acting.

Risk: Offshore-structure and asset-transfer discussion could be misused to evade reporting duties, capital controls, sanctions, court orders, taxes, or creditor rights.

Mitigation: Do not assist evasion or concealment; keep guidance compliance-oriented and remind users to follow all applicable reporting and legal obligations.

Risk: High-return trading and wealth-growth models can understate loss risk or encourage overconfident decisions.

Mitigation: State model assumptions, avoid guaranteeing outcomes, and emphasize risk controls such as diversification, position sizing, and predefined exit rules.
