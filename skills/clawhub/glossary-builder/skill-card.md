## Description: <br>
Build a translation/terminology glossary so a product's key terms render consistently everywhere. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Localization, product, and documentation teams use this skill to create termbase-style glossaries that define key source terms, approved translations by locale, part of speech, context, and do-not-translate decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product copy or terminology supplied to the skill may contain confidential product language. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact source material before use, and keep glossary outputs within the intended localization workflow. <br>
Risk: Draft translations or ambiguous terms may be treated as approved terminology without review. <br>
Mitigation: Mark uncertain translations as draft and require native-speaker or localization-owner review before importing into a CAT tool or style guide. <br>
