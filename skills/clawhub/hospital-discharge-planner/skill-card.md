## Description:

Helps families and caregivers review discharge arrangements, understand paperwork and post-acute options, plan the first 72 hours at home, and prepare a caregiver handoff card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Families and caregivers use this skill to identify discharge-planning gaps, prepare questions for the hospital team, compare post-acute care options, and coordinate home care and caregiver handoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (Medicare-focused)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Discharge, medication, or Medicare details could be mistaken for individualized medical or coverage advice.

Mitigation: Use as a planning aid, not medical advice; confirm clinical instructions and coverage with the hospital team or insurer.

Risk: Generated caregiver handoff cards may expose sensitive health information in terminal history, shared consoles, screenshots, or logs.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive details unless needed and limit access to generated cards and terminal output.
