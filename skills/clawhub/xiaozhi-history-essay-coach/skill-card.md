## Description:

帮助初高中学生从历史材料中自主提炼论题、选取史实并检查论证逻辑与史论结合，而不替学生拟观点或代写论述。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生使用该技能练习历史开放性论述题，借助引导问题和评分标准自查观点、史实、论证与结构；教师可参考其反馈辅助指导。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing or reminders could disclose a minor's learning data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Keep sessions memory-free by default; confirm separate student consent and applicable guardian permissions before enabling storage, sharing or reminders.

Risk: Chinese-curriculum assumptions and crisis-support guidance may not fit a student's region or local examination rules.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's region and current local exam criteria; use location-appropriate support channels when responding to distress.

Risk: Overconfident scoring or supplied arguments could mislead students or replace their own work.

Mitigation: Use curriculum-based prompts and self-checks, avoid predicting exact marks, and leave the viewpoint and essay to the student.
