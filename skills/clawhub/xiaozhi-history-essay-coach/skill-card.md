## Description:

通过引导提问和论证检查，帮助初高中学生从历史材料中自主提炼观点、选取史实并完善史论结合的论述。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中历史学习者使用本技能练习开放性论述题和小论文，通过自拟观点、寻找史实依据及检查论证逻辑进行考后练习和自评；技能不代写答案或预测具体得分。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum assumptions and crisis/help channels before use elsewhere

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mainland China curriculum assumptions and help channels may not suit students elsewhere.

Mitigation: Use for Mainland China K12 history tutoring, or localize the curriculum and crisis/help channels before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: Optional learning records, cross-skill sharing, and reminders involve student data.

Mitigation: Require separate consent for each feature and support pausing, exporting, correcting, and deleting records.

Risk: A student may disclose an immediate safety concern during tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring, encourage contact with a trusted adult and appropriate local help, and do not offer a diagnosis.
