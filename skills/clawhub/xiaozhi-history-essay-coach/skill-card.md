## Description:

引导初中与高中学生从史料中提炼自己的历史论点、选择史实并检查史论结合与论证逻辑，不代拟观点或代写答案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初中和高中历史学习者，用于练习开放性论述题、自评论证结构，并按课程标准和统编教材核对表达边界。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆（中文 K–12 教学场景）

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A student may share sensitive personal or emotional information during a tutoring conversation.

Mitigation: Keep practice session-only by default; read or save a learning profile only with the documented consent, and follow the crisis referral guidance if a safety concern arises.

Risk: Historical explanations or evaluations may be inaccurate or extend beyond the student's curriculum.

Mitigation: Check historical claims against the applicable curriculum standards and unified textbooks; ask a teacher to review uncertain or sensitive content.

Risk: A student may request help while taking an active test or assessment.

Mitigation: Do not provide help on the active question; offer to review the reasoning after the test.
