## Description:

引导初高中学生从史料中提炼自己的历史论题、选取史实并检查论证，不代写观点或文章。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生用它练习历史开放性论述题：自行拟定观点，用史实支撑论证，并按对应学段的标准自查。教师或家长可陪同学生使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (designed for mainland China's K12 history curriculum)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning-progress records, wrong-answer sharing, or reminders could expose a student's learning data without their informed consent.

Mitigation: Keep sessions unrecorded by default; check separate consent for profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, and reminders before enabling them.

Risk: Unsupported historical claims or a finished argument could mislead students or replace their own work.

Mitigation: Ground feedback in course standards and textbooks, ask students to supply their own viewpoint and evidence, and avoid writing their answer or promising a score.
