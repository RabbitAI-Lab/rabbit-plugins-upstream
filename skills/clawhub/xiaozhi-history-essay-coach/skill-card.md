## Description:

帮助初中和高中学生从史料中提炼自己的历史观点、选择史实并检查论证，不代拟观点或代写文章。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初中和高中学生用它练习历史开放论述题，包括审题、提炼观点、选择史实、组织论证和按对应标准自评。它通过提问和反馈支持学生独立完成作答。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saving or sharing student learning records can expose personal progress information.

Mitigation: Keep practice session-only by default; require clear, separate consent for saved profiles, sharing, or reminders, and honor view, correction, deletion, and pause controls.

Risk: A student may raise an urgent safety concern during tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop the learning flow and follow the referral guidance, using emergency contacts appropriate to the student's location.
