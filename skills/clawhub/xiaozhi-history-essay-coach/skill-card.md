## Description:

面向初高中学生的中文历史论述题教练，通过提问引导学生从材料中提炼自己的观点、选择史实并检查论证，而不代拟观点或代写答案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初高中学生在练习中国历史开放性论述题和小论文时，用它梳理题意、独立提出观点、选择史实，并根据课标检查史论结合与条理；教师可用其引导学生自评。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (designed for Mainland China's history curriculum; review suitability elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records and reminders may expose minors' personal information when memory or sharing is enabled.

Mitigation: Keep practice session-only by default; review consent before enabling profile memory, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing or reminders.

Risk: Curriculum-specific guidance may not fit history courses outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Review the local curriculum and historical framing before using it in other regions.

Risk: Students may disclose distress or safety concerns during tutoring.

Mitigation: Stop tutoring and follow the skill's crisis-referral protocol, including locally appropriate help channels.
