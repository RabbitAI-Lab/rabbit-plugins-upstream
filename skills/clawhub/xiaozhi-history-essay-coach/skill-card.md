## Description:

引导初高中学生从史料提炼自己的历史观点、选取史实并检查论证，而不替学生拟观点或代写论述。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向中国大陆课程背景下的初高中学生，辅导历史开放性论述题和小论文的观点提炼、史实选择、史论结合与自评；不代写，也不预测具体分数。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆（课程内容适用范围）

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Minors' learning records could be retained, shared across skills or used for reminders without informed consent.

Mitigation: Review profile, sharing and reminder consent settings before use; use the skill's pause, delete and export controls when long-term records are unwanted.

Risk: Historical essay feedback could be mistaken for an official score or a model answer.

Mitigation: Use the curriculum-based rubric for self-checking; require students to develop their own claims and consult the actual examination criteria for scoring.
