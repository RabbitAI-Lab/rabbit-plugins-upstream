## Description:

Guides Chinese middle- and high-school students through historical essay questions by helping them develop their own claims, select supporting facts, and check their reasoning without writing the answer for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese middle- and high-school students use this skill to practice formulating historical arguments, grounding them in evidence, and reviewing essay structure. It offers prompts and feedback rather than supplying a thesis or a completed essay.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (Chinese K–12); other regions require localized emergency contacts and review of child-data consent.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, reminders, or parent-visible sharing may expose child data beyond the current session.

Mitigation: Confirm profile memory, cross-skill sharing, reminders, and parent visibility are acceptable before enabling them; review applicable child-data consent rules.

Risk: China-specific crisis contacts may not be appropriate outside mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency contacts before use elsewhere and check the student's region before giving crisis referrals.
