## Description:

引导初高中学生从史料中自主提炼历史论题、选取史实并检查论证，不替学生拟观点或代写论述。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

初高中学生使用本技能练习历史开放性论述题和小论文，借助引导提问、史实核查与分层自评改进史论结合；教师或监护人可协助监督使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (mainland China defaults; localize emergency contacts and privacy assumptions elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profiles, wrong-answer handoffs, and reminders can expose minors' learning data if enabled without consent.

Mitigation: Keep sessions memory-free by default; obtain the relevant student or guardian consent before enabling profile memory, cross-skill sharing, handoffs, or reminders.

Risk: Mainland China emergency contacts and privacy assumptions may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's location and localize emergency contacts and privacy practices before deployment outside mainland China.
