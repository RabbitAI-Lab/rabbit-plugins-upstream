## Description:

Guides an agent in creating, updating, linking, completing, and archiving GetItDone tasks with visible review before changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

GetItDone users use this skill to turn notes into tasks and manage existing tasks, including schedules, dependencies, completion, and archiving.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Task changes may affect the wrong workspace or unintended tasks.

Mitigation: Confirm the target workspace and review the listed tasks before approving creation, updates, dependency links, or archiving.
