# distill — simplify toward one primary goal

Remove what competes with the surface's job. Distillation is subtraction with a thesis — not "make it minimal".

## 1. State the one primary goal

Every surface gets ONE: "understand the offer," "compare plans," "find the incident." Write it down before editing. If the surface has two equal goals, that's a scope problem — say so (split the surface or pick a winner). Everything on the surface either serves the goal or competes with it.

## 2. Simplify across all six layers (in this order)

1. **IA** — is the right stuff on the page at all? Cut sections that serve no goal step. Collapse navigation that only exists for symmetry.
2. **Content** — words first: cut throat-clearing ("Welcome to…"), stack the value high, one idea per paragraph. Controls name their action.
3. **Layout** — one spatial thesis per surface; remove the boxes-around-boxes; let groups form through spacing, not containers (proximity over borders).
4. **Visual** — remove decoration that carries no meaning (dividers as styling, icons as wallpaper, gradients as mood). Keep the signature move.
5. **Interaction** — every click that can vanish should; progressive disclosure over all-at-once (the Wall of Options pattern from `critique.md` is a distillation failure); defaults over options.
6. **Code** — dead styles, duplicate rules, unused variants; tokens over literals. The detector's findings triage here.

## 3. Progressive disclosure is the mechanism

Complexity isn't deleted — it's **sequenced**. The default path stays simple; power lives one intentional step away (expandable details, secondary panels, "advanced" collapses). Test: a first-timer (Jordan persona) reaches the goal without opening anything; a power-user (Alex) reaches everything without wading.

## 4. Avoid-list (simplicity is not…)

- **Not featurelessness.** Removing capability is not distillation unless the goal never needed it. If the brief needs the feature, the feature stays — sequenced, not amputated.
- **Not mystery.** Minimal labels ("Solutions", "Learn more") are vaguer, not simpler. Simplicity is clarity, not fewer words at the cost of meaning.
- **Not uniform emptiness.** Density is allowed where the goal is information (Work/Read surfaces — see `operate.md`); distilling a dashboard into three lonely cards failed the goal.
- **Not a style.** "Minimalism" as an aesthetic identity is `new-work`'s territory. Distill preserves identity while reducing load.

## 5. Verify

The goal is completable in one pass with nothing in the way. The skeleton test from `bolder.md` works in reverse here: strip the copy — if the structure still has multiple competing answers to "what is this surface for?", keep distilling. Batched render check, and confirm the critique backlog (if any) shrank, not grew.
