## Description: <br>
Find discounted local Groupon deals for services and activities by city, category, and user need. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[dejimarquis](https://clawhub.ai/user/dejimarquis) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Users ask an agent to find local discounts for services, wellness, automotive, dining, entertainment, and home services. The agent searches Groupon pages, compares current deal details, and presents concise options with prices, discounts, ratings, locations, and fine print. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Groupon prices, availability, expiration dates, and purchase restrictions can change after the agent reports them. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the current price, availability, expiration date, booking requirements, and fine print directly on Groupon before buying. <br>
Risk: The skill may activate for broad deal or discount requests where merchant quality and offer suitability vary. <br>
Mitigation: Compare multiple options and check merchant reputation, ratings, review counts, location, and service restrictions before choosing a deal. <br>
