## Description:

Reviews embedded engineering capstone reports with an evidence-chain structure, test tables, specification checks, originality guidance, and AI-use guidance while leaving the student's writing and data to them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External undergraduate engineering students use this skill to plan or review embedded capstone report structure, testing evidence, specification consistency, originality, and AI-use disclosures. It provides methods and feedback without ghostwriting or inventing results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course-specific academic integrity rules may restrict AI assistance or require disclosure.

Mitigation: Check the rules for the specific assessment and disclose AI assistance when required.

Risk: Unverified technical claims, citations, or test results could mislead a report review.

Mitigation: Verify claims and references against reliable sources and the real system; use only measured test data.

Risk: Report material shared with an AI service may include information the student is not permitted to disclose.

Mitigation: Share only report material permitted by the course or institution for the chosen service.
