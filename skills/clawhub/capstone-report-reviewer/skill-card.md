## Description:

Reviews embedded capstone reports using an engineering evidence chain, with chapter structure guidance, specification consistency checks, test tables, originality self-checks, and AI disclosure guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Engineering students preparing embedded systems, electronics, mechatronics, or IoT capstone reports can use this skill to review chapter structure, connect design claims to test evidence, and check consistency and academic AI disclosure expectations. It provides methods and structure feedback without writing the report or inventing results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course AI-use rules and technical claims may vary or be inaccurate for a student's situation.

Mitigation: Check the course outline and institutional policy, disclose assistance when required, and independently verify technical claims and academic-policy advice.
