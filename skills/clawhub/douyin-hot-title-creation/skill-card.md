## Description:

Searches Douyin videos and images, retrieves creator posts and comments, and checks trending lists, returning structured JSON for content research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[why20261](https://clawhub.ai/user/why20261)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and analysts can research short-video topics, review creator activity and comments, monitor trends, and inform competitor analysis using public Douyin data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and Douyin links are sent to GUAIKEI.

Mitigation: Use only queries and links you are comfortable sharing with that service.

Risk: The service requires an API token and may charge for use.

Mitigation: Keep GUAIKEI_API_TOKEN private and check service charges before use.

Risk: Query and result logs may be saved locally.

Mitigation: Review local storage and delete saved query and result logs when no longer needed.
