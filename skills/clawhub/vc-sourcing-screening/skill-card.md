## Description: <br>
VC 投前研究与初筛 helps investors generate deal-sourcing candidate lists for a sector or four-factor screening scorecards for startup targets, with cited and traceable rationale. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhuiwensm99](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuiwensm99) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External investors and VC analysts use this skill to standardize early deal sourcing and first-pass screening for investment themes or startup targets. It produces prioritized candidate lists or equal-weight scorecards across team, market, technology, and financial dimensions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Investment research outputs may contain incorrect source interpretation, unsupported financial assumptions, or stale market information. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited sources, financial assumptions, and material facts before making investment decisions or sharing analysis externally. <br>
Risk: Optional ima publishing can move generated research outside the current chat. <br>
Mitigation: Connect and authorize ima publishing only when intended, and confirm the target space and visibility before publication. <br>
