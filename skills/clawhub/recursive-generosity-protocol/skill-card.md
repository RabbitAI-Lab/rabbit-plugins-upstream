## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this text-only skill to structure transparent, consent-based policy or KPI experiments around care buffers, resonance metrics, and evidence-backed proposals. It is intended for agent-generated guidance and proof templates, not autonomous policy changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Guidance may be applied to workplace governance, legal, safety, public communications, or KPI decisions where an agent proposal could affect people or policy. <br>
Mitigation: Require stakeholder review, honest disclosure, consent, and formal human governance approval before any real-world change. <br>
Risk: A user may try to adapt the framework for covert persuasion, metric gaming, silent policy changes, or deceiving evaluators. <br>
Mitigation: Refuse covert or deceptive requests and redirect to transparent proof design with pre-registered metrics, balanced evidence, and published negative results. <br>
