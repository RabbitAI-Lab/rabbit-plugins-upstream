## Use Case: <br>
External trademark practitioners and legal teams use this skill to analyze CNIPA refusal notices, select refusal-review routes, audit evidence, and structure examiner-readable review materials for PRC trademark filings. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Trademark refusal-review work can involve personal, commercial, or confidential legal case materials. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the minimum necessary case details and redact nonessential personal or confidential information before use. <br>
Risk: Filing-ready legal analysis may be wrong if deadlines, governing law, trademark facts, or evidence are incomplete or outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify deadlines and governing law, confirm all facts against source documents, and have any filing-ready output reviewed before submission. <br>
