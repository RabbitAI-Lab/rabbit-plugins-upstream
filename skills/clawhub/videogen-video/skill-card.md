## Description:

Helps agents create, edit, caption, and export videos with VideoGen from text, images, scripts, and other media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[videogen](https://clawhub.ai/user/videogen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to direct VideoGen to generate, edit, caption, and export videos from prompts, images, scripts, narration, or presentations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video generation may consume VideoGen account credits.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested scope and budget before generating videos.

Risk: Prompts and uploaded files are sent to VideoGen's hosted service.

Mitigation: Share only content the user intends to submit to VideoGen, and use the host's OAuth flow instead of requesting credentials in chat.
