## Description: <br>
Generate structured daily news briefs by searching and summarizing the latest public information on any topic, including technology news, policy updates, and custom topics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agents use this skill to prepare concise news briefings from current public web results for technology, policy, industry, or custom topics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: News summaries may be incomplete, stale, or affected by the quality of public web search results. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important claims against the cited source links before relying on the brief. <br>
Risk: Helper-script output may suggest follow-up commands or tool calls that fetch external content. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed searches, fetches, and commands before execution and use only trusted public sources. <br>
