## Use Case: <br>
External users in China use this skill to prepare consumer-rights complaints after purchases involving suspected counterfeit goods, false advertising, refund refusals, product qualification issues, or import-compliance concerns. It guides evidence organization, complaint-channel selection, settlement negotiation, and escalation through Chinese administrative or court processes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save sensitive complaint evidence such as receipts, payment screenshots, prescriptions, and chat logs in a local evidence folder. <br>
Mitigation: Use only evidence needed for the complaint, redact account numbers or medical details when possible, and remove the local evidence package after the matter is resolved. <br>
Risk: Consumer-rights templates and escalation guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific dispute or jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated complaint or court materials before submission and confirm key legal claims, requested compensation, and filing venue against the user's facts. <br>
