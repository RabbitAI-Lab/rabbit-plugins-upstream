Mitigation: Rename and rescope the skill before release, or rewrite the instructions so the title, description, triggers, and workflow all support business migration planning. <br>

Mitigation: Narrow trigger keywords to the intended AI Studio philosophy-graph workflow and disable or carefully review implicit invocation before installation. <br>

Mitigation: Review generated plans and commands before execution, confirm assumptions and constraints with the user, and validate outputs against the listed success criteria. <br>
