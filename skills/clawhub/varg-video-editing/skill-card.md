## Description:

Recommends a service or FFmpeg workflow for editing existing footage, from cuts and captions to dubbing, upscaling, and handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan flags public sharing of footage without a privacy warning.

Mitigation: Before using hosted services, confirm the footage may be shared and obtain approval for private or proprietary media; use local or authenticated transfers when needed.

Risk: The security scan flags an installer example that does not pin its version.

Mitigation: Review the installer source and pin a trusted version before installation.

Risk: The scan was partial because a local path-like reference could not be resolved, so its evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and remaining skill materials before relying on the scan.
