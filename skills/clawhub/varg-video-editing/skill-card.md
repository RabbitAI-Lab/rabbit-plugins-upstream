## Description:

Routes existing-video editing tasks to suitable local or hosted tools and gives practical steps for preserving quality while editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and editing agents use this guide to choose tools and workflows for cutting, reframing, captioning, dubbing, enhancing, reviewing, and handing off existing footage. It emphasizes preserving source media and checking edits before delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hosted editing workflows may send confidential footage to external services, and public media links may be accessible to anyone who obtains them.

Mitigation: Use local processing for confidential footage; approve receiving services, prefer protected or expiring links, and check retention and deletion options.
