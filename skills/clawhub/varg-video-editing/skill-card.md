## Description:

Routes agents to suitable tools and safe workflows for editing existing video, from cuts and captions to AI edits and delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agents use this guide to choose local or hosted tools and plan, review, and verify edits to existing footage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cloud editing workflows may share footage through public links, which can expose confidential media.

Mitigation: Use local-only processing for confidential footage; before cloud use, agree on the destination services, link visibility, retention, and cost.
