## Description:

Routes agents to services and workflows for editing existing video, from cuts and captions to dubbing, AI edits, upscaling, quality checks, and editor handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this guide to choose a service and plan safe, reviewable edits to footage they already have.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential footage could be exposed through hosted services or public links.

Mitigation: Keep footage local unless upload is approved; confirm recipients, link access, and deletion terms before sharing.

Risk: An edit may trigger paid service calls.

Mitigation: Show the edit plan and cost estimate, then get approval before any paid call.
