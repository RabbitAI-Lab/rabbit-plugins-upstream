## Description:

Analyzes supplied social-media traces to offer tentative, evidence-weighted persona and relationship insights with confidence labels and privacy safeguards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to organize supplied social-media traces into tentative, confidence-graded persona or relationship insights and reflection questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Social traces and chat content can contain sensitive personal data.

Mitigation: Obtain the subject's consent where feasible, avoid unnecessary identifying details, and keep the analysis private.

Risk: Personality inferences from limited social traces can be wrong or overconfident.

Mitigation: Label interpretations as hypotheses, cross-check through direct interaction, and do not use them for consequential decisions or diagnosis.

Risk: The security scan was partial because one reference could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the supplied skill contents and account for the unresolved reference before relying on the scan as a complete review.
