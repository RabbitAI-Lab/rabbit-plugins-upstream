## Description: <br>
Influencer Marketing helps agents advise on creator, influencer, and ambassador partnerships, including partner vetting, deal structure, briefs, disclosure compliance, and ROI measurement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, operators, and marketing teams use this skill to have an agent plan and review influencer, creator, and ambassador marketing programs. It supports partner selection, compensation and usage-rights negotiation, creator briefs, disclosure checks, and measurement plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Campaign-specific legal, platform, or disclosure advice may be incomplete for regulated industries or international campaigns. <br>
Mitigation: Review campaign plans with appropriate counsel and platform policies before launch, especially for health, finance, alcohol, or cross-border campaigns. <br>
Risk: Creator claims, disclosures, compensation terms, or usage-rights language may be applied too generally. <br>
Mitigation: Require written agreements, clear disclosure instructions, substantiated claims, and human review of creator briefs and final posts. <br>
