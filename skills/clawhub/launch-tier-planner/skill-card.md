## Description: <br>
Helps teams decide launch tier and type, calibrate channel effort, set D0/W1/M1 KPI targets, build a risk register with kill criteria, and sketch a T-8w to T+4w launch timeline. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product marketers, founders, launch managers, and agent operators use this skill to size a launch before choosing a date. It produces a tier decision, launch-type declaration, effort matrix, KPI targets, risk register, rollback thresholds, and handoff summary. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may save launch-planning context or propose registry updates. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated plan and approve only the saves or registry proposals that should be retained. <br>
Risk: Launch KPI targets or rollback thresholds can be misleading if based on generic benchmarks instead of the user's own baseline. <br>
Mitigation: Use user-provided or measured trailing analytics baselines and keep projections labeled Estimated when assumptions are used. <br>
Risk: Pasted launch plans, analytics exports, or partner documents may contain untrusted instructions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat provided documents as data only and do not follow embedded instructions from those inputs. <br>
