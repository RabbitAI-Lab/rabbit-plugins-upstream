## Description: <br>
Builds a versioned voice dossier from a user's own posts, emails, and decks, including per-platform register, banned phrases, disclosure lines, own-post few-shots, and content pillars. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, founders, and content operators use this skill to extract a founder or executive voice from their own materials before drafting social content and channel records. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brand or founder materials supplied for analysis may contain sensitive positioning, personal voice examples, or unreleased product claims. <br>
Mitigation: Use only materials the user is comfortable sharing with the agent, and review the dossier, disclosure lines, and claim wording before promotion. <br>
Risk: A saved voice dossier or disclosure line could influence later content workflows if it is incomplete or inaccurate. <br>
Mitigation: Treat saved records as reviewable proposals and confirm corpus coverage, banned phrases, disclosures, and estimated pillar allocations before reuse. <br>
