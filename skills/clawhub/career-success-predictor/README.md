# 🎯 Career Success Predictor

> **Get real about your career move.** 10 personalized questions + honest feedback + a 3-minute action plan.

**Available in:** 🇬🇧 English · 🇨🇳 中文 · 🇯🇵 日本語

---

## What It Does

When you're stuck on "Should I really switch to this career?", this skill will:

✅ **Learn your target career** — the more specific, the sharper  
✅ **Ask 10 behavior-based questions** — measuring real history, not wishful thinking  
✅ **Separate fit from readiness** — what you're suited for vs. where you are right now  
✅ **Deliver a 3-minute report** — verdict first, then your story told back by someone who gets it  

### The Two Layers You Get

| Layer | What You'll Learn |
|-------|------------------|
| **Fit** (Success odds) | Are your traits + circumstances suited to this path? Given as a range (e.g., 61%–73%), not a fake precision number. |
| **Readiness** (Where you are) | Exploring → Validating → Building → Ready to launch. What to do next. |

---

## Why This Is Different

### 🚫 Not This...
- ❌ "Do you consider yourself resilient?" (everyone says yes)
- ❌ A cold score table that makes you want to argue
- ❌ Generic advice like "improve your skills"
- ❌ Pretending uncertainty is weakness

### ✅ Actually This...
- ✅ "The last time you worked 2+ weeks under pressure, what happened?" (real behavior)
- ✅ A story told back to you like a knowledgeable friend would tell it
- ✅ Concrete next steps you can take this week
- ✅ "Haven't decided yet?" → That's your action plan, not your score

**Key insight:** If you're asking this question, you haven't figured things out yet — and that's *exactly* who this is for. Uncertainty never lowers your score; it becomes your roadmap instead.

## How to Use It

Just ask naturally. Examples:

- "Am I really cut out to be a product manager?"
- "Can I make it as a freelancer?"
- "Is this career switch realistic? I have 2 kids and $50k in savings."
- "我转行成功的概率有多大?"
- "転職してやっていけますか?"

**No special setup needed.** The skill will:

1. Confirm your target career (and narrow it down if needed)
2. Walk you through 10 quick questions
3. Build your report
4. Hand you a file you can save and refer to later

---

## What You'll Get

### The Conversation
A back-and-forth where the skill understands your situation and asks smart follow-ups.

### The Report File
A short narrative (usually 600–900 words) that includes:

- **Your fit range** — e.g., "57%–68% fit for this path"
- **Your readiness stage** — e.g., "You're in the Validating stage; here's what comes next"
- **What you're bringing to the table** — strengths drawn from your own answers
- **The real day-to-day** — what this career actually looks like vs. the hype
- **The cold truth, kindly** — where you're solid, where you need work
- **A 1/3/6-month script** — concrete moves you can make, not generic checklists

## Who This Is For

✅ **You're seriously considering a career switch** (and want to know if it's realistic)  
✅ **You're fresh in a field** and wondering if you belong  
✅ **You're burned out** and thinking of a hard pivot  
✅ **You're undecided** between a few paths and want to test drive one  
✅ **You're taking a risk** (quitting, relocating) and want a reality check first  

❌ **Not** for quick curiosity ("what would I be good at?" → try a general interest test instead)  
❌ **Not** a predictor of *happiness*, only *odds of succeeding* by industry standards  

---

## Design Highlights

🔍 **No assessment paradox** — Being undecided doesn't tank your score; it becomes your action plan  
📋 **Scoring is credible** — Questions are built with scoring rules from the start, not improvised after  
🔄 **Asks for more when needed** — If the picture is blurry, the skill may ask about your MBTI/Big Five results as backup (weighted by science)  
📖 **A report for the soul** — Honest, warm, with concrete next steps. No cold score tables or corporate speak  
🌍 **Your language** — Speaks whatever language you're using

## Disclaimer

**Results are based on your self-reported answers.** This is a reference-grade career-fit assessment — useful for planning, not a statistical prediction or a verdict on your life. Many people with lower scores have gone on to thrive in those careers; many with higher scores have walked away. What matters most is what you do next.
