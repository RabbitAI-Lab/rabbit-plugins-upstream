## Description: <br>
Career Success Predictor guides users through a multilingual career-fit self-assessment and generates a personalized readiness and action report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tonytan10086](https://clawhub.ai/user/tonytan10086) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users considering a career change or new career path use this skill to assess fit for a target occupation, understand readiness, and receive practical next steps based on self-reported context. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The assessment may include sensitive personal career context in locally generated reports. <br>
Mitigation: Treat reports as private planning material and review or redact them before sharing. <br>
Risk: The percentage range may be mistaken for a professional or statistical prediction. <br>
Mitigation: Use the result as planning guidance based on self-reported answers, not as a definitive career forecast. <br>
