## Description:

Drafts vertical short-drama scripts, from standalone 1–3 minute episodes to paid-series development packages, with optional shot lists and Seedance prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External writers and small creative teams use it to turn a premise into a shootable vertical short-drama episode or a paid-series package, then optionally prepare shot lists and video-generation prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Required configuration and companion rules were not bundled for this review, so using the skill without them may miss its stated safeguards.

Mitigation: Confirm the configuration and companion rules are available and read them before use; stop if either cannot be read.

Risk: Optional video generation may expose unpublished scripts or personal likenesses to a provider and incur costs.

Mitigation: Before generation, review the prompts and images, check provider privacy terms, and confirm the expected cost.
