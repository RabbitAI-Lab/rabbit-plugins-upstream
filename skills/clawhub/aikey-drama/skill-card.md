## Description:

Turns a short-drama premise into a shootable standalone script or paid-series development pack, with optional shot lists and video-generation prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individual creators and small production teams use this skill to develop short-form drama scripts or paid-series pitches from a premise. After script approval, they can request shot lists and prompts for optional video production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill expects a local configuration and may save drafts and memory under configured paths.

Mitigation: Review the configured paths and access permissions before use; do not proceed if required configuration or content guidance is missing.

Risk: Optional video and reference-image generation can incur charges.

Mitigation: Show the requested models, image counts and video durations, and obtain the user's confirmation before submitting paid jobs.

Risk: Default Chinese-language style and publishing guidance may not fit every project.

Mitigation: Adjust the style for the project's language and check the intended platform's current publication requirements before release.
