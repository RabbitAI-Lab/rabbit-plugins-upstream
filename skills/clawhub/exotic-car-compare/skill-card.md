## Description:

Compare exotic and luxury cars to rent side by side: Huracan vs 296 GTB, Urus vs G 63 vs Cullinan, 720S vs 911 Turbo S, with what each is like to drive and ride in, seats and luggage, and the real published daily rates and where each one is rented.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External renters use this skill to compare two to four exotic or luxury cars by rental pricing, availability, driving and passenger experience, and practical fit, then choose an option suited to their priorities.
