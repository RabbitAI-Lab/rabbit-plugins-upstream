## Description: <br>
Xiaoe is a knowledge-commerce SaaS guidance skill for evaluating Xiaoe-tech fit, planning course stores and marketing tools, and comparing Xiaoe with Youzan, Weidian, or custom options. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent users use this skill to advise creators, educators, and organizations on whether Xiaoe fits a knowledge-commerce workflow, how to design course sales funnels, and what pricing or compliance checks to perform before adoption. <br>

Mitigation: Use the skill as checklist guidance and verify current regulatory requirements for regulated education or training scenarios. <br>
