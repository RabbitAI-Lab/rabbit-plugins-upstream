## Description:

Guides agents in creating and editing custom HyperFrames video compositions, including multi-scene reels, montages, title cards, and footage remixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan, build, and review custom video compositions when a specialized workflow does not fit or a companion-led project calls for one.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live, unpinned HyperFrames updates and CLI commands can change installed workflows or project files after review.

Mitigation: Use a trusted, pinned or preinstalled HyperFrames version where possible; review updates before allowing them to modify workflows or project files.
