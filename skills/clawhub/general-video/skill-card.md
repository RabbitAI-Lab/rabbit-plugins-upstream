## Description:

Helps agents create and edit custom HyperFrames video compositions, from title cards and footage remixes to multi-scene reels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan, build, and review custom video compositions when a specialized workflow does not fit, including collaborative multi-scene projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow updates and runs unpinned HyperFrames tooling, which may change after this release was reviewed.

Mitigation: Use only a trusted update channel and pin or locally vet the HyperFrames toolchain for sensitive projects.
