## Description: <br>
Author or edit custom HyperFrames video compositions for longer or multi-scene pieces, brand and sizzle reels, montages, static loops, title cards, footage remixes, and companion-flow freeform builds. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and creative operators use this skill to plan, build, verify, and revise HyperFrames video projects when a specialized workflow does not fit or a companion-driven composition is needed. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can update local HyperFrames workflow state and create or modify video project files. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed file changes and run the required HyperFrames checks before accepting outputs. <br>
Risk: Media or Figma provider steps may require sign-in, billing, or use of external services. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm provider authentication, billing, and render approval prompts before allowing those steps. <br>
Risk: Large projects may dispatch bounded scene-building workers whose outputs affect assembled video behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated composition files and motion sidecars before assembly and final render approval. <br>
