## Description:

Guides creators through building and editing custom HyperFrames video compositions, including multi-scene pieces, montages, title cards, and footage remixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and developers use this skill to plan, assemble, and check bespoke HyperFrames compositions when a specialized workflow does not fit or a companion flow is requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live, unpinned HyperFrames CLI commands run during normal use.

Mitigation: Use a trusted, pinned, locally installed HyperFrames CLI and review commands before running them.

Risk: HyperFrames skill updates can change local tooling or skill behavior during a run.

Mitigation: Review updates before allowing them, especially in sensitive workspaces.
