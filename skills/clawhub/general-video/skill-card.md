## Description:

Guides agents in authoring or editing custom HyperFrames video compositions, including multi-scene pieces, reels, montages, loops, title cards, and footage remixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heygen-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/heygen-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and developers use this skill to plan, build, and refine custom HyperFrames compositions when a specialized workflow does not fit or a companion-led production is requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live, unpinned HyperFrames CLI updates may change the code or workflow content used during a run.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or locally vetted CLI, and review update changes before rendering or broader provider actions.
