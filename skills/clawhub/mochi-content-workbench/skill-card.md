## Description:

Adapt supplied material into channel-specific Chinese or English drafts using a source fact ledger, then check user-defined character budgets, citations, language hints and duplicate copy. Use for content repurposing or multi-channel copy drafts; no posting or official platform-limit guarantees.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use the skill to adapt supplied source material into Chinese or English channel drafts, with fact citations, user-set length budgets, language hints, and duplicate-copy checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated drafts may contain inaccuracies or present source claims without independent verification.

Mitigation: Review the fact ledger and drafts for accuracy before use; treat source statements as unverified unless checked independently.

Risk: Supplied material may include information that is not appropriate to process with the connected model.

Mitigation: Share only material appropriate for that model and avoid unnecessary personal information.
