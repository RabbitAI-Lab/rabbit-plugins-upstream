## Description:

Adapt supplied material into channel-specific Chinese or English drafts using a source fact ledger, then check user-defined character budgets, citations, language hints and duplicate copy. Use for 内容改写 or multi-channel copy drafts; no posting or official platform-limit guarantees.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts or checks may not capture factual meaning, privacy concerns, channel suitability, or current platform limits.

Mitigation: Review source evidence, factual meaning, privacy, tone, and channel fit before using or publishing drafts; treat character budgets as user-defined constraints, not platform guarantees.

Risk: Supplied content is processed by the configured model and retained in local inputs and reports.

Mitigation: Avoid unnecessary personal information and review the configured model's privacy terms and local file permissions.
