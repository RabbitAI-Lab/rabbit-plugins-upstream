## Description:

Offers a research-inspired perspective on penguin-oss-suite reviews, covering trigger tests, skill boundaries, report titles, and workflow checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and maintainers of penguin-oss-suite use this skill to critique routing tests, define skill boundaries, name evidence-based reports, and improve checklist validation. Its judgments are interpretations rather than statements made by Pang Tianyu about the suite.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inferred perspectives from public research and a team-authored README may be mistaken for the researcher's own views or binding process rules.

Mitigation: Present judgments as interpretations, distinguish team material from personal statements, and verify consequential claims against primary sources.

Risk: Unverified routing or checklist recommendations could give a false sense of coverage.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes before adoption and require concrete test cases and validation evidence for each claimed result.
