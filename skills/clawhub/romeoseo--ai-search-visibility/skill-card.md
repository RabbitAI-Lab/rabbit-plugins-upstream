## Description:

Assesses a brand's visibility across AI answer engines and provides a baseline scorecard and prioritized guidance for improving accurate citations and recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, SEO consultants, and brand teams use this skill to measure whether AI answer engines mention and cite their brand for buyer queries, investigate visibility gaps or inaccurate claims, and prioritize improvements to their pages and external sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer notes or other private information may be sent to external AI engines or tracking services as part of visibility checks.

Mitigation: Generalize prompts and remove identifying or confidential details before sending; obtain permission before sharing any remaining private information.

Risk: The security review left two local path-like references unresolved, so their targets were not fully assessed.

Mitigation: Review and resolve those references before relying on the associated material.
