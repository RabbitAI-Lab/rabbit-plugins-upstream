## Description: <br>
Use this skill to coordinate local research work across multiple agents and tools when the task needs lightweight routing, decomposition, handoffs, and local orchestration. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and research teams use this skill to coordinate lightweight local research work, route tasks across agents or tools, create concise handoffs, and track simple status without adopting a full project-management workflow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Unnecessary task splitting can add coordination overhead or fragment work that one agent could complete cleanly. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the decision flow first and split work only at natural boundaries when a task needs decomposition. <br>
Risk: Sparse handoffs can omit constraints, scope, or done criteria and lead to incomplete research outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Use the documented handoff fields: Goal, Scope, Output, Constraints, and Done when. <br>
