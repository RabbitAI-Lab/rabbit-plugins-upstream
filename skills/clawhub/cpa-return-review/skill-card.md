## Description:

Provides a five-pass checklist for reviewing prepared individual and S corporation tax returns before delivery or filing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tax preparers and reviewers use this skill to conduct a consistent pre-delivery review of prepared Form 1040 and 1120-S returns. It organizes checks into five ordered passes and records findings with severity and disposition.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Client records and review findings may contain sensitive taxpayer information.

Mitigation: Use only client records you are authorized to review and protect findings containing sensitive information.

Risk: Tax rules and return details can change, making prior guidance or amounts outdated.

Mitigation: Verify every amount and rule against current IRS and state guidance and current-year software logic; the signing practitioner remains responsible for the return.
