## Description:

Applies a CPA-style pre-delivery checklist for reviewing prepared 1040 and 1120-S returns before client delivery or filing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External tax practitioners, CPA/EA firms, and reviewing agents use this skill as a structured second-pass review standard for prepared individual and S-corporation returns. It helps identify identity, carryover, consistency, source-traceability, and reasonableness issues before delivery or filing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checklist may be mistaken for authoritative tax, legal, or filing advice.

Mitigation: Use it as a review aid only; verify all tax law, thresholds, rates, signatures, and filing decisions against current IRS, state, software, and firm requirements before filing.

Risk: A reviewer may file despite unresolved blocker or correction findings.

Mitigation: Record findings with severity and disposition, and resolve blocker or correction items before delivery or filing.
