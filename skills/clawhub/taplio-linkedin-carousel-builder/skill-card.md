## Description:

Turn a topic, framework, or long-form idea into a LinkedIn carousel script (cover slide + value slides + CTA slide). Returns slide-by-slide copy ready to drop into Canva or any carousel tool. Use when the user wants to ship a carousel but only has the idea or a long-form draft. Requires the Taplio MCP to pull the user's live voice and proven carousel angles, then save the caption as a draft.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External LinkedIn creators use the skill to turn a topic, framework, or long-form draft into structured carousel slide copy and a caption, informed by their Taplio profile and relevant post inspiration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Topics and generated captions are sent to Taplio, and the caption is saved as a draft.

Mitigation: Generalize confidential topics before use, clarify ambiguous slide requests, and review captions before approving scheduling or publishing.
