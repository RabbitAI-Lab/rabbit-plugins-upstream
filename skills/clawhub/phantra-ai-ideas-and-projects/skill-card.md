## Description:

Turns a brief into a few story ideas the person chooses between in chat, and keeps the work in a Phantra project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use the skill to develop a video, ad, short, or scene idea through a few focused questions and a choice among distinct concepts. It helps them review existing Phantra projects or organize selected work in a project before handing the chosen idea to scene writing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The connected Phantra workspace can expose project history and save selected work; the connection implementation and companion skills are outside this package.

Mitigation: Review the service connection's permissions and the referenced companion skills before connecting the workspace.
