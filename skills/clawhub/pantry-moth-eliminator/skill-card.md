## Description:

Provides a staged household pantry-moth elimination plan with food-by-food decisions, cleaning steps, trap placement, and dated follow-up checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Households use this skill to identify and address suspected Indian meal moth infestations in pantry goods, plan safe food handling and cabinet cleaning, and monitor for recurrence. It is not intended for commercial kitchens or clothes moths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Food-disposition or heat-treatment advice may result in unnecessary disposal or unsafe handling.

Mitigation: Inspect each item before acting; use only oven-safe containers for heating, never microwave sealed bags, and follow the stated freezer and oven safety guidance.
