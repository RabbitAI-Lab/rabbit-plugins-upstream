## Description:

The formal vocabulary of ink wash painting - how graduated ink, dry brush, and empty space create depth without color.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creative agents use this skill to apply East Asian ink wash painting vocabulary when creating or evaluating monochrome brushwork, tonal depth, empty space, and related style choices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated creative output may apply the ink wash style inaccurately or overstate visual quality.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the skill's evaluation criteria for empty space, brush pressure, tonal gradation, control, spontaneity, and compositional completeness.
