## Description:

Creates original cartoon characters or restyles a provided character in a range of recognizable animation looks using Varg.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creative teams can request original cartoon character designs, model sheets, or cartoon restyles of a supplied photo or character. The skill uses Varg's image-generation service and recommends providing personal photos only when comfortable with how they may be handled.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags refusal-bypass guidance and insufficient disclosure about sending images to external services; its evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review prompts against the image service's policies, check which external service receives a photo, and only provide photos when comfortable with that handling.
