## Description:

Review how the connected brand shows up inside AI answers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, AI Overviews) using SEOmatic's stored scans, tune the tracked prompts, and only then consider a fresh scan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and site owners can review their brand's visibility across AI answer engines, check and tune tracked buyer prompts, and report missed prompts, competitors, cited sources, and content opportunities using SEOmatic. A fresh scan can be run when requested after its AI-credit cost is explained.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A fresh visibility scan consumes AI credits and is limited to one assistant-triggered scan per hour.

Mitigation: Review the stored scan first. Run a fresh scan only when requested and after explaining its AI-credit cost.
