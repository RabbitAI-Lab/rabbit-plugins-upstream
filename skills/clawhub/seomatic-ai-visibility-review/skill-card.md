## Description:

Reviews a brand's visibility in AI search using SEOmatic's stored scans, manages tracked prompts, and can request a fresh scan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minh42](https://clawhub.ai/user/minh42)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and SEO teams use this skill to assess where their brand appears in AI search, refine tracked questions, and identify content and citation opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tracked-prompt additions, removals, deactivations, or cadence changes can persist without explicit approval.

Mitigation: Present proposed changes first and wait for user approval before changing tracked prompts or cadence.

Risk: Fresh scans can consume paid AI credits.

Mitigation: Explain the cost and run a fresh scan only after the user explicitly requests it.
