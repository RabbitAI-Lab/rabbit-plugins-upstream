## Description:

Helps agents retrieve LinkedIn company, person, job, and post data through superagnt for sales research, recruiting, and enrichment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[superagnt](https://clawhub.ai/user/superagnt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, recruiting, and research teams use this skill to gather professional profiles, company details, job listings, and post information for account research, candidate discovery, and enrichment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: LinkedIn profile identifiers, company and job IDs, post URLs, and search filters sent to superagnt may contain sensitive or third-party information.

Mitigation: Confirm the data is appropriate to share and review superagnt's data handling terms before sending sensitive or third-party identifiers.
