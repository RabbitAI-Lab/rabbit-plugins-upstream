## Description:

Guides agents through creating a 30–90 second character monologue video with consistent camera angles, acted voice, lip-synced clips, and a final edit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and their agents use this workflow to develop a 30–90 second character monologue, from the speech and shot prompts through lip-synced clips and an edited video.
