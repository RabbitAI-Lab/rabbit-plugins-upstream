## Description:

Make an acted 30–90 second character monologue with consistent camera angles, voice, lip sync, and edits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan and generate a short character speech, split it into acted segments, lip-sync each segment to a different camera angle, and assemble a finished monologue video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
