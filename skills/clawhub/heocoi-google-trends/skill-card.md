## Description:

Retrieves Google Trends interest over time, regional interest, related queries, and Trending Now results through an Apify actor.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heocoi](https://clawhub.ai/user/heocoi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their users can compare search interest for up to five keywords, explore regional and related searches, or check Trending Now results across supported search categories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Queries share search terms with Apify and the actor provider.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive private keywords and confirm the data-sharing arrangement before use.

Risk: Using the actor can incur charges on the user's Apify account.

Mitigation: Confirm acceptable usage costs before running queries.
