## Description:

Compares current marketplace prices, product types, and availability for AI subscriptions and accounts using Shuita price data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gm365](https://clawhub.ai/user/gm365)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People comparing AI subscription offers use this skill to check available prices, distinguish account and top-up options, and understand purchase risks before deciding whether to buy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace listings for accounts, top-ups, or KYC-related offers may not be legitimate or permitted by the underlying AI service.

Mitigation: Treat prices as comparison data, not seller endorsements; check the service's terms and the offer's warranty and support before purchasing.

Risk: Availability and marketplace prices can change after a listing is checked.

Mitigation: State the data timestamp and available seller count alongside the price, and verify current stock before relying on a quote.
