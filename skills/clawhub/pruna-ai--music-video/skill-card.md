## Description:

Guides the creation of a complete music video with an original song or vocals, performance clips, B-roll, and lyric-synced edits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video producers use this skill to plan and produce a lyric-synced music video, from songwriting and audio alignment through approved visuals and final assembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creative prompts, images, and audio may be uploaded to external media-generation services.

Mitigation: Review material before upload and use only services approved for the content.

Risk: Media generation may consume paid API credits and create local media files.

Mitigation: Respect the plan, stills, and clips approval gates; review expected costs and local outputs before generation.

Risk: Floating installation commands and broad API credentials can increase exposure.

Mitigation: Use pinned, reviewed installs and scope API keys to the intended media-generation accounts.
