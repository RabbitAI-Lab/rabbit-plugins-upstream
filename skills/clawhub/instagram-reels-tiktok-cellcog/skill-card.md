## Description:

AI social media video and content creation powered by CellCog. Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, Stories, carousels, social posts. Full video production from a single prompt — scripted, shot, stitched, and scored automatically.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to request short-form videos, Instagram carousels and posts, and Stories from CellCog. It also provides prompt examples and setup guidance for invoking CellCog.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts are sent to CellCog and may include information the user is not authorized to disclose.

Mitigation: Share only information you are authorized to disclose.

Risk: Video generation can consume credits and may not produce a usable result.

Mitigation: Review credit costs before use and inspect generated content before posting.

Risk: The separate CellCog dependency is installed outside this skill.

Mitigation: Review dependency versions before installation and consult the separate CellCog skill for its SDK guidance.
