## Description:

Creates 10-second, 1080×1920 vertical educational videos by adapting one of five deterministic narrative templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and developers use this skill to turn Chinese cultural, historical, and general knowledge topics into short vertical explainer videos. They select a narrative template, fill its content slots, and produce a silent MP4.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
