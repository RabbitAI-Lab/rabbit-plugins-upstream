## Description: <br>
VC 尽职调查与投资决策 helps users standardize VC diligence and investment decision workflows by producing five-dimension diligence checklists, three-scenario valuation models, and IC memos. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhuiwensm99](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuiwensm99) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
VC investment teams and external users use this skill to evaluate startup deals, document assumptions, and prepare diligence checklists, valuation scenarios, and IC recommendation memos while keeping investment decisions subject to human review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Valuation assumptions, citations, or IC recommendations may be incomplete or misleading if the input deal data is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review assumptions, citations, valuation ranges, and recommendations before relying on the output for investment decisions. <br>
Risk: Confidential portfolio or target-company materials could be exposed if provided to external sources or publishing destinations. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sending or publishing confidential deal materials unless the user has authorization and intends that disclosure. <br>
