## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language persona and style skill that answers in the voice and recurring rhetorical style of Bilibili creator 峰哥亡命天涯. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tengyunhe](https://clawhub.ai/user/tengyunhe) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents use this skill when a user explicitly requests Feng Ge-style Chinese-language responses for creative, entertainment, or persona-driven conversation. It is intended for deliberate style simulation rather than neutral assistant behavior. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to produce blunt, abrasive, sometimes sexualized or impolite persona responses. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only when that specific style is deliberately requested, and avoid sensitive, workplace, educational, or safety-critical contexts that require a respectful neutral tone. <br>
Risk: Persona simulation can be mistaken for neutral advice or endorsement if used outside its intended context. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as stylized creative responses and review them before sharing in contexts where tone, attribution, or audience sensitivity matters. <br>
