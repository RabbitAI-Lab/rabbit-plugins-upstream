## Description: <br>
Creates engaging, example-driven long-form articles with strong hooks, clear argument structures, and reader retention strategies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aiepco](https://clawhub.ai/user/aiepco) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, writers, marketers, and developers use this skill to guide an agent through research-first drafting of long-form articles, newsletters, social posts, and platform-adapted content that emphasizes specific evidence and reader retention. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may search the web while drafting, which can expose private or sensitive topics to live research tools. <br>
Mitigation: Use non-sensitive topics or explicitly restrict live research when privacy matters. <br>
Risk: Research-backed prose can still include incorrect, outdated, or weakly supported claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review factual claims, citations, names, dates, and numbers before publication. <br>
Risk: Platform-specific defaults may produce unwanted tone, calls to action, or locale conventions. <br>
Mitigation: Specify the target platform, audience, locale, and style constraints before drafting. <br>
