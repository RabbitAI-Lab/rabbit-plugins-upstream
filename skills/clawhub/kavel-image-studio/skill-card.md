## Description:

Helps users choose a Kavel photo or video effect, adapt a prompt to their goal, and find the corresponding generator link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

People seeking photo restyling, restoration, or a photo-to-dance-video effect use this skill to select a Kavel tool, get a tailored prompt, and open its generator page.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the generator requires uploading photos to a third-party website, potentially exposing sensitive images.

Mitigation: Review Kavel's terms and privacy practices before uploading; avoid sensitive photos and photos of identifiable people without their consent.
