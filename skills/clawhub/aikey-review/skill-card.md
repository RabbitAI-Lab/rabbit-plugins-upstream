## Description:

AI KEY reviews talking-head scripts before publication by assessing the opening, sentence-level information density, audience response, and red-line risks; it diagnoses by default without rewriting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Independent creators of knowledge-style talking-head videos use the skill to review completed scripts before publishing. It provides structured feedback on the opening, audience value, clarity, likely responses, and content red lines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review feedback is automatically saved for future reviews without a clear limit on what is retained or a retention choice.

Mitigation: For confidential scripts or business details, use session-only reviews or approve each saved note and its destination. Inspect the shared rules and writeback policy before enabling vault memory.
