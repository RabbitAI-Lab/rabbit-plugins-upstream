## Description:

Oxford documentary-note referencing: footnote citations, shortened notes, bibliography, OSCOLA pointer. Use for papers requiring Oxford style with footnotes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vhsgreed](https://clawhub.ai/user/vhsgreed)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and writing assistants use this skill to format Oxford documentary-note citations, shortened footnotes, and bibliographies for papers that require footnote-based referencing. It also flags when UK legal writing should use OSCOLA instead of general Oxford style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Citation formatting may not match a user's institutional style guide or jurisdiction-specific legal citation requirements.

Mitigation: Verify final citations against the applicable local style guide, and use OSCOLA for UK legal writing when required.
