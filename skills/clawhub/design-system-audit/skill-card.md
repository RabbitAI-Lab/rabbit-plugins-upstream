## Description: <br>
Audit a design system for consistency, coverage, and quality, including component coverage, design token consistency, documentation quality, accessibility compliance, contribution process health, and adoption barriers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Design system teams, design leaders, and engineering teams use this skill to structure audits of shared component libraries, design tokens, documentation, accessibility, governance, and adoption health. It produces an evidence-oriented report with scores, gap analysis, and a prioritized remediation roadmap. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for internal design system details, team structure, tooling, and pain points that could be confidential. <br>
Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the agent session and redact sensitive product, staffing, customer, roadmap, or security details before use. <br>
Risk: Audit scores and remediation priorities can be misleading if based on incomplete or assumed evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Validate findings against production UI, design files, code implementation, accessibility checks, and stakeholder input before acting on recommendations. <br>
