## Description:

Review a site's backlinks for health and risk, assess disavow decisions, reclaim lost links, and identify realistic competitor link opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romeoseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/romeoseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Website owners and SEO practitioners use this skill to review backlink data, assess link risk, reclaim links pointing to dead pages, and identify realistic opportunities to earn links. It produces a profile summary, a risk assessment, a redirect reclaim list, and prioritized link opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inspection was partial because a local reference in SKILL.md could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference before relying on the skill.

Risk: Incomplete backlink data or vendor metrics could lead to misleading conclusions about link risk.

Mitigation: State each data source's coverage and limits, treat vendor metrics as sorting signals rather than Google measurements, and label unchecked information.

Risk: Unjustified disavow or redirect changes could harm search performance.

Mitigation: Recommend disavow only for a manual action or a self-built link scheme that could not be removed, and get approval before redirect changes go live.
