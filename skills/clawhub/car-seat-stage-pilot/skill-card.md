## Description:

Helps parents assess a child's car-seat stage using age, weight, and height, with seat-position guidance, limit warnings, and estimated transition dates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use the skill to review rear-facing, harness, booster, and adult-belt stages and plan when to recheck a child's fit. Its advice is not a substitute for the specific seat manual, local law, or a certified child passenger safety technician.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; check applicable local child-passenger safety law.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Overconfident or internally inconsistent advice could lead to an unsafe car-seat transition.

Mitigation: Verify every stage decision against the exact seat manual and local law; consult a certified child passenger safety technician before changing stages.

Risk: Projected transition dates rely on approximate growth rates and may misstate when a child reaches a seat limit.

Mitigation: Treat dates as reminders to remeasure and check the seat's actual limits, not as automatic switch dates.
