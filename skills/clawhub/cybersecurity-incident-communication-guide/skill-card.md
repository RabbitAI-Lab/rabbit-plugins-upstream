## Description:

A practical educational guide to preparing, coordinating, and improving cybersecurity incident communications for digital platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[malcolmreeves](https://clawhub.ai/user/malcolmreeves)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Incident-response and communications teams use this guide to prepare roles and channels, draft verified updates for affected audiences, coordinate review, and improve communication after an incident.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General guidance could be mistaken for legal advice or an organization-specific incident plan.

Mitigation: Consult qualified legal and privacy advisers and apply the organization's approved incident procedures.

Risk: External references may be unsuitable for a particular incident process.

Mitigation: Evaluate external references independently before incorporating them into incident procedures.
