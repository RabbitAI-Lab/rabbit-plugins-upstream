## Description:

Find free and low-cost events, deals, and local activities in supported US cities using On the Cheap listings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

People and their agents use this skill to find affordable events, family activities, festivals, and deals in supported US cities or compare options across cities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (supported cities and national deals)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event dates, prices, and deal availability may change after publication.

Mitigation: Verify details on the linked listing before making plans.

Risk: Monthly event overviews show only a preview of each day's listings.

Mitigation: Use daily listings when a complete schedule is needed and avoid presenting previews as exhaustive.
