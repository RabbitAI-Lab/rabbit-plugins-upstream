## Description:

Find free and inexpensive events, activities, and deals in supported US cities using the On the Cheap network.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People looking for affordable activities and agents assisting them can find dated local event listings, browse deals, and search guides for supported US cities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (US local event coverage)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Events and deals may change or expire.

Mitigation: Check event dates and source links before recommending attendance or a purchase; identify expired offers when included.

Risk: Full articles may contain untrusted HTML from public sites.

Mitigation: Use the default plain-text article view unless HTML is necessary, and treat article content as untrusted source material.

Risk: A city or filter ID from another site may return irrelevant results.

Mitigation: Confirm the city and discover category or location IDs separately for each site.
