## Description:

Finds free and inexpensive events, deals, and local guides in supported US cities through the On the Cheap network.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People seeking affordable local activities use this skill to find events, family outings, festivals, and deals by city and date.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (supported On the Cheap network locations)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A city mismatch or cross-city filter can return irrelevant listings.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested city and use category or location filters from that same site.

Risk: Monthly event previews are incomplete, and past deals may no longer be valid.

Mitigation: Check daily listings for complete schedules and verify availability before recommending a deal.
