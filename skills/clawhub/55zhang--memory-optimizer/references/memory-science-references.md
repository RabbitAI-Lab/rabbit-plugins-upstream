# 记忆与学习科学参考文献

本技能的科学依据来源，按主题整理。所有文献均来自同行评审期刊，含高影响力期刊。

## 一、遗忘曲线与记忆基础

1. **Ebbinghaus, H. (1885).** *Über das Gedächtnis* (Memory: A Contribution to Experimental Psychology).
   - 奠基性研究：记忆随时间指数衰减的遗忘曲线，首次实验研究记忆。
2. **Murre, J. M. J., & Dros, J. (2015).** Replication and analysis of Ebbinghaus' forgetting curve. *PLoS ONE*, 10(7), e0120644.
   - 现代复制研究，验证遗忘曲线与间隔复习效应。

## 二、间隔效应与复习时间安排

3. **Cepeda, N. J., Pashler, H., Vul, E., Wixted, J. T., & Rohrer, D. (2006).** Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis. *Psychological Bulletin*, 132(3), 354-380.
   - 间隔练习的元分析：分散复习显著优于集中复习。
4. **Cepeda, N. J., Vul, E., Rohrer, D., Wixted, J. T., & Pashler, H. (2008).** Spacing effects in learning: A temporal ridgeline of optimal retention. *PNAS*, 105(3), 1095-1100.
   - 高影响力期刊：最佳复习间隔约为记忆保持期的 10%-20%。
5. **Kornell, N., & Bjork, R. A. (2008).** Learning concepts and categories: Is spacing the "enemy of induction"? *Psychological Science*, 19(6), 585-592.
   - 间隔效应在概念学习中的应用。

## 三、测试效应与检索练习

6. **Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006).** Test-enhanced learning: Taking memory tests improves long-term retention. *Psychological Science*, 17(3), 249-255.
   - 测试效应经典实验：自测显著提升长期保持。
7. **Karpicke, J. D., & Blunt, J. R. (2011).** Retrieval practice produces more learning than elaborative studying with concept mapping. *Science*, 331(6018), 772-775.
   - *Science* 高影响力研究：检索练习优于概念图式精加工学习。
8. **Roediger, H. L., & Butler, A. C. (2011).** The critical role of retrieval practice in long-term retention. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 15(1), 20-27.
   - 综述：检索练习对长期记忆的关键作用。

## 四、学习技术评估

9. **Dunlosky, J., Rawson, K. A., Marsh, E. J., Nathan, M. J., & Willingham, D. T. (2013).** Improving students' learning with effective learning techniques: Promising directions from cognitive and educational psychology. *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*, 14(1), 4-58.
   - 权威评估 10 种学习技术：练习测试、分散练习为高实用性；划线、重读为低实用性。
10. **Bjork, R. A. (1994).** Memory and metamemory considerations in the training of human beings. In *Metacognition: Knowing about Knowing* (pp. 185-205). MIT Press.
    - "期望困难"（desirable difficulties）理论。

## 五、交错练习与学习方法

11. **Rohrer, D., & Taylor, K. (2007).** The shuffling of mathematics problems improves learning. *Instructional Science*, 35(6), 481-498.
    - 交错练习提升学习效果的实验证据。
12. **Rohrer, D. (2015).** Student instruction should be distributed over long time periods. *Educational Psychology Review*, 27(4), 635-643.
    - 长期分散学习的教学启示。

## 六、睡眠与记忆巩固（与 sleep-optimizer 共享）

13. **Rasch, B., & Born, J. (2013).** About sleep's role in memory. *Physiological Reviews*, 93(2), 681-766.（IF: 37.3）
    - 睡眠对记忆巩固的权威综述：慢波睡眠与纺锤波参与海马-皮层迁移。
14. **Lund, H. G., Reider, B. D., Whiting, A. B., & Prichard, J. R. (2010).** Sleep patterns and predictors of disturbed sleep in a large population of college students. *Journal of Adolescent Health*, 46(2), 124-132.
    - 大学生睡眠与学业表现关系。
