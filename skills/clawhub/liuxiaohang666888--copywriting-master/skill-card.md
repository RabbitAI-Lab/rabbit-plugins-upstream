## Description: <br>
A Chinese copywriting assistant that helps generate social media posts, short-video scripts, WeChat articles, product copy, marketing messages, workplace documents, and creative writing from user-supplied topics, platforms, and style requirements. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, marketers, creators, and workplace writers use this skill to draft and refine Chinese copy for platforms such as Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat public accounts, Weibo, product pages, sales scripts, reports, speeches, and creative writing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated marketing or business copy may be inaccurate, misleading, or unsuitable for regulated medical, financial, or legal topics. <br>
Mitigation: Review and approve outputs before publication, and route regulated-topic copy through qualified compliance or professional review. <br>
Risk: Platform-specific posts may still violate evolving platform rules or advertising standards. <br>
Mitigation: Check current platform policies and remove unsupported claims, absolute language, or sensitive wording before use. <br>
