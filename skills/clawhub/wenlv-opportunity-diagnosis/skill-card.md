## Description: <br>
Screens cultural tourism project opportunities with a policy-redline first pass and five go/no-go decision gates for early investment judgment. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External cultural tourism planners, project owners, operators, and investors use this skill to decide whether a site or concept is worth advancing before design, contracting, investment, or construction. It collects project facts, checks policy redlines, evaluates resource, market, product, business, and operations dimensions, and produces GO, GO-WITH-CONDITIONS, or NO-GO guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat advisory GO or NO-GO output as a substitute for legal, planning, land-use, financial, or field due diligence. <br>
Mitigation: Position the report as early screening only and require review by relevant professionals and competent authorities before project commitments. <br>
Risk: Policy redline answers can be incomplete or wrong, which may produce misleading project viability guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Require source documents or authority confirmation for land, ecology, cultural heritage, planning, and approval-path questions before relying on the conclusion. <br>
Risk: Insufficient project facts can cause overconfident diagnosis across the five decision gates. <br>
Mitigation: Use the bundled collection form and ask targeted follow-up questions before issuing a final go/no-go recommendation. <br>
