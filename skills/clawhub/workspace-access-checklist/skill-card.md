## Description:

Compares workspace access lists and creates access review checklists for collaboration operations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Operations and collaboration teams use this skill to compare current and approved workspace membership lists, identify differences, and prepare concise access review checklists for follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Workspace membership lists can contain sensitive business access information.

Mitigation: Provide only the membership information needed for the review and avoid including unrelated private workspace data.
