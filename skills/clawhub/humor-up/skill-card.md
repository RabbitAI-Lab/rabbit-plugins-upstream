## Description: <br>
Punch up your writing with actual wit for toasts, bios, birthday messages, Slack posts, presentation openers, daily briefs, original topical one-liners in English and Chinese, and joke scoring or repair. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and developers use this skill to add appropriate wit to everyday writing, create original bilingual jokes or captions, and score or improve draft jokes. It is designed to avoid humor in sensitive contexts such as grief, health, legal issues, financial loss, errors, or user frustration. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Humor mode or broad captioning requests may add wit in contexts where a plain response would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Turn humor mode off or request a serious tone for sensitive, high-stakes, or emotionally charged content. <br>
Risk: Humor can be harmful if aimed at vulnerable people, personal traits, death, illness, grief, financial loss, or a user's frustration. <br>
Mitigation: Follow the skill's anti-cringe contract: skip the joke in sensitive contexts and target systems, situations, or the assistant rather than people. <br>
