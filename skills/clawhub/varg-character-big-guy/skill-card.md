## Description:

Makes big, heavy AI characters people remember, on varg: very large men (and women) whose size is shown with visible cues instead of numbers, dressed so the contrast tells the joke (a 160 kg florist in a tiny pink apron), played straight and never mocked, as a character sheet ready for scenes and video. Use when someone asks for a fat, big, heavy, chubby or plus-size character, a big guy, a gentle giant, a huge man in a small job, or a strongman. Part of varg-character-design (definition, sheet, reuse in video).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
