## Description:

Creates full-body character sheets for large, heavy characters, using visible details and gentle contrast with their role or clothing while treating the character with respect.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators use the skill to design memorable large-bodied AI characters for scenes, videos, or marketing. It helps describe visual cues that communicate size without making the character's body the joke.
