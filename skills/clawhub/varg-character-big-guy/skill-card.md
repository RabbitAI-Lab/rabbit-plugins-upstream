## Description:

Makes big, heavy AI characters people remember, on varg: very large men (and women) whose size is shown with visible cues instead of numbers, dressed so the contrast tells the joke (a 160 kg florist in a tiny pink apron), played straight and never mocked, as a character sheet ready for scenes and video.
