## Description:

Guides creators in designing very large characters through visible physical details, clothing contrast, and ready-to-use prompts while portraying them respectfully.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators use this skill to develop memorable large characters for illustrations, character sheets, and video, with visual cues and humor based on contrast rather than body-shaming.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation uses Varg’s external service and consumes credits.

Mitigation: Check current service availability and credit costs before generating images.

Risk: The skill refers users to varg-ai and varg-character-design, whose contents are not included here.

Mitigation: Review those separately referenced skills before relying on their instructions.
