## Description: <br>
Positioning Mapper helps launch teams build a Dunford-style positioning canvas with named alternatives, unique attributes, value themes, a beachhead segment, and a one-sentence onlyness statement. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, launch, and product teams use this skill to define launch positioning before downstream messaging work begins. It produces a positioning canvas, beachhead segment scoring, and handoff guidance for the next launch-message workflow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may work with product notes, interviews, competitor research, launch registry state, and optional public competitor pages that can include incorrect or untrusted content. <br>
Mitigation: Review source material and resulting positioning claims before using the canvas for launch decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill can propose persistent launch, positioning, claim, or stage facts. <br>
Mitigation: Allow persistence only after explicit confirmation and keep comparative or unverifiable claims marked for review. <br>
