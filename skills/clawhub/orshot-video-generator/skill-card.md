## Description:

Creates reusable Orshot video templates for Reels, Shorts, product and listing videos, animated explainers, and narrated clips.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orshot](https://clawhub.ai/user/orshot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content teams and developers use the skill to plan and render reusable short videos, explainers, listing reels, and narrated clips through Orshot. It helps adapt a brief or existing post into scenes, motion, captions, and repeatable render inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media or recordings may not be approved for public access, or their URLs may not be publicly reachable.

Mitigation: Use publicly reachable media approved for public access, and review the finished video before posting.

Risk: Rendering content is sent to Orshot, and narration may also be sent to a chosen voice service.

Mitigation: Review what content is included and which voice service is connected before rendering.

Risk: Recurring renders or connected posting destinations may create costs or publish to unintended accounts.

Mitigation: Review connected workspaces, recurring costs, and posting destinations before scheduling or publishing.
