## Description:

Stack Internal (stackoverflow.co). Use this skill for searching and reading Stack Internal data through the OOMOL-connected oo CLI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and developers use this skill to retrieve the current Stack Internal user, questions, answers, tags, and search results from a connected Stack Internal account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: First-time setup includes a one-line remote installer for the oo CLI.

Mitigation: Prefer vendor documentation, pinned releases, and checksum or signature verification before installing the CLI.

Risk: Stack Internal content is accessed through an OOMOL-connected account.

Mitigation: Connect only approved accounts whose internal content is acceptable to route through OOMOL.
