## Description:

合同与法务专家 helps users route pasted tabular contract or legal materials to one of eight local checkers and returns row-level, source-linked conclusions when the supplied columns are sufficient.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Legal, finance, procurement, and audit users use this skill to check contract-related tables for accounting, payment, renewal, invoice, lease, penalty, and milestone issues. It is best used as a checklist aid whose findings should be reviewed before legal, accounting, audit, or payment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial or legal checks may return conclusions even when required input columns are missing.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected sub-skill, verify all required columns are present, and review any missing-column notices before relying on results.

Risk: A clean or successful result can be mistaken for full contract or legal review.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as checklist assistance only and have qualified reviewers validate results before accounting, legal, audit, or payment decisions.

Risk: The local five-use gate may affect availability during evaluation or operational use.

Mitigation: Plan installation and evaluation around the local usage counter and confirm subscription requirements before deployment.
