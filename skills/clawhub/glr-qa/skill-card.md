## Description:

Runs bounded GameLearningRuntime QA on authorized games and adapters, producing dated JSON and self-contained HTML reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[loonghao](https://clawhub.ai/user/loonghao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and QA engineers use this skill to run bounded checks on authorized GameLearningRuntime projects, inspect failures and training evidence, and identify gaps in live-game validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: QA logs, traces, and training artifacts may contain proprietary details or local information.

Mitigation: Review reports before sharing, remove sensitive content, and publish only evidence authorized by the project owner.
