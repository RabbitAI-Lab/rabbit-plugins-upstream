## Description:

Map the sub-questions AI search engines look up behind a money prompt (query fan-out) to your pages and sections, find the gaps, and fill them without thin pages. Use for "query fan-out", "which sub-questions are we missing", or AEO / LLM SEO / GEO content coverage for Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and ChatGPT.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO and content teams use this skill to map likely buyer sub-questions to existing site pages, identify coverage gaps, and prioritize useful content updates and citation retests.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business prompts and targeting details may be shared with external search services during research.

Mitigation: Review the services' data practices and avoid submitting details you are not comfortable sharing.

Risk: The review was partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Check referenced materials before relying on recommendations that depend on them.
