## Description:

A career development coaching skill library for resume writing and ATS optimization, professional profiles, interview preparation, job search strategy, salary negotiation, career transitions, and advancement planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Job seekers and people planning their careers use this skill for practical help with resumes, professional profiles, interviews, job search decisions, salary negotiations, career changes, and promotion planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career histories, compensation, and employer details can be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for coaching and redact confidential employer details.

Risk: Generated resumes and profile changes may contain inaccurate or misleading claims.

Mitigation: Review every draft against your real experience before using it.

Risk: The README's remote installer and separately referenced skills were not evaluated in the release security review.

Mitigation: Prefer reviewed versions and project-local installation scope.
