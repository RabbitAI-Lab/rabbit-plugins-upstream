## Description:

Prompts agents to consult relevant existing skills, knowledge, and experience before substantive tasks and identify how that material informed the result.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lingyu9495-source](https://clawhub.ai/user/lingyu9495-source)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents working on writing, development, analysis, and other substantive assignments use this skill to locate and apply relevant prior knowledge, then disclose its contribution to their deliverables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation can expose private knowledge bases, memory, or client materials during ordinary tasks.

Mitigation: Install only where broad lookup is acceptable; narrow source access and disclosure rules for sensitive work.

Risk: The skill may modify shared skills or project experience files after a task.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before changing shared skills or project notes.

Risk: Its required disclosure of consulted materials may conflict with strict-format outputs or confidentiality requirements.

Mitigation: Limit disclosure to permitted information and avoid use with strict-format outputs unless disclosure rules are narrowed first.
