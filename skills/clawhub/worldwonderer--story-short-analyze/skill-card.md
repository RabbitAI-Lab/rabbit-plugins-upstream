## Description:

Analyzes Chinese short web fiction for story structure, emotional beats, reversals, characters, and reusable writing techniques.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to deconstruct a short Chinese-language story into a structured critique of its plot, emotional arc, twists, and craft for subsequent writing work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow copies the source story into the project, which may retain copyrighted or sensitive text.

Mitigation: Supply only texts you have rights to use and review access to the saved source and analysis files, especially for private or commercially sensitive stories.
