## Description:

Analyzes short fiction by mapping its plot structure, emotional arc, reversals, characters, and writing techniques into a reusable report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External writers and editors use this skill to study short fiction they have permission to analyze, including its story structure, emotional beats, reversals, and character functions. It saves a detailed Markdown analysis, plot-node and writing-technique files, and JSON progress metadata for later reference.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
