## Description: <br>
Availability Heuristic helps agents correct risk or frequency estimates that are driven by vivid, recent, or media-salient examples by grounding them in reference-class base rates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and agents use this skill to test anecdote-driven probability or frequency claims against a defined reference class and base-rate evidence. It is most useful for risk perception, planning, medical, legal, technical, investment, and AI-adoption estimates where memorable cases may be overweighted. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce misleading confidence if the user chooses an inappropriate reference class or applies the correction to rare catastrophic risks where precautionary weighting may still be warranted. <br>
Mitigation: Before relying on the conclusion, review the reference class, cited base-rate source, and the skill's stated exception for rare catastrophic or irreversible risks. <br>
