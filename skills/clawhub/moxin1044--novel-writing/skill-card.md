## Description: <br>
Use this skill when the user wants to write, continue, outline, or revise Chinese short stories, novellas, or long-form novels with coherent structure, vivid description, rhetorical devices, and multi-character interaction. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[moxin1044](https://clawhub.ai/user/moxin1044) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and writers use this skill to guide an agent in producing Chinese fiction, including outlines, short stories, chapters, continuations, and revisions. It emphasizes time, place, characters, action, psychology, dialogue, rhetorical devices, coherent plot progression, and readable literary style. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can influence story structure, style, and content when triggered. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated fiction for tone, factual claims, audience suitability, and policy requirements before publication or external use. <br>
