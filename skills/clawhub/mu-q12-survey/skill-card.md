## Description:

Q12敬业度调研助手 helps team managers, HRBP, and organizational development specialists design employee engagement surveys, interpret Q12-style results, and create 90-day improvement plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Team managers, HRBP, and organizational development specialists use this skill to create Q12-style engagement questionnaires, diagnose survey results, identify high-risk engagement patterns, and plan concrete follow-up actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Employee survey responses may contain sensitive HR data or identify individuals in small teams.

Mitigation: Use anonymized or aggregate data, avoid questionnaire-based anonymity claims for teams smaller than six people, and only paste identifiable responses into AI environments approved for that HR data.

Risk: Rendered documentation, SVG, or landing-page assets may contact third-party services.

Mitigation: Review network behavior before relying on the documentation or site as fully network-isolated.
