## Description: <br>
Q12敬业度调研助手 helps team managers, HRBPs, and organization development specialists design Gallup Q12-style engagement surveys, interpret results, identify high-risk patterns, and create 90-day improvement plans. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees in management, HRBP, and organization development roles use this skill to create employee engagement questionnaires, analyze Q12 survey scores, and turn weak areas into prioritized improvement actions. It is intended for engagement diagnosis and planning, not large-scale statistical modeling or use by employees without authority to run a team survey. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Employee survey responses and comments can contain sensitive or identifiable workplace information. <br>
Mitigation: Use aggregated or anonymized data, avoid entering names or identifiable comments unless organizational policy permits it, and handle survey data under the organization's HR privacy rules. <br>
Risk: Small teams can make anonymous survey responses easier to re-identify. <br>
Mitigation: Do not treat survey results as anonymous for teams under six people; use 1:1 interviews or aggregate results across a larger group. <br>
Risk: The public README and landing page load third-party badges and link to external sites, so the local-only privacy claim applies most directly to the prompt skill content. <br>
Mitigation: Use the prompt-only skill content in the controlled assistant environment when privacy matters, and review external project pages separately before browsing them with sensitive context. <br>
Risk: The skill provides engagement diagnosis guidance but is not a substitute for professional survey systems or formal statistical modeling. <br>
Mitigation: Use professional HR, legal, or statistical review for high-stakes workforce decisions, large-scale modeling, or official benchmarking programs. <br>
