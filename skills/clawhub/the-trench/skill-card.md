## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The example may run with an unintended wallet or Solana cluster. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the Anchor wallet and cluster before running calls, and use a dedicated test wallet for devnet work. <br>
Risk: External IDL or TypeScript types may be stale or unverified for the target program. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the IDL and TypeScript types before integrating them into an agent workflow. <br>
