## Description:

Analyzes tomato and strawberry images or videos through a cloud service to provide ripeness grades, harvest guidance, and report links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Growers, greenhouse operators, and home gardeners use this skill to assess tomato or strawberry ripeness from photos or videos and review past analysis reports to inform harvest decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media and user-linked report requests are sent to LifeEmergence cloud services.

Mitigation: Use only when cloud transmission of the selected media and report requests is acceptable.

Risk: Automatic account selection and persistent local identity or token records may expose another user's reports in a shared workspace.

Mitigation: Avoid shared or multi-user environments until identity selection is server-verified and history access requires explicit confirmation.
