## Description: <br>
Assists users in submitting leave requests through the Feishu desktop app by collecting, confirming, and guiding form completion. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[baofeidyz](https://clawhub.ai/user/baofeidyz) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and workplace assistants use this skill to gather leave details, confirm them with the user, and navigate Feishu's desktop approval flow to submit a leave request. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can help submit an actual workplace leave request through the user's signed-in Feishu session. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the Feishu account, leave dates, duration, type, and reason with the user before allowing submission. <br>
