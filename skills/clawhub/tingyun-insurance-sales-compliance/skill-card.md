## Description:

Provides a structured self-check for insurance sales compliance, with risk scoring, prioritized remediation actions, and references to enforcement cases and regulations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance intermediaries, agency branches, and independent agents can use this skill to review sales practices against a structured checklist, prioritize corrective actions, and prepare a remediation tracker. It supports internal self-checks and does not provide legal opinions or regulatory determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A confirmed calculation error can understate the skill's risk ratings.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer correct the scoring error before relying on ratings.

Risk: Regulatory requirements and local supervisory guidance may change or differ by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify applicable rules independently with qualified compliance staff and save completed CSV records separately before exporting again.
