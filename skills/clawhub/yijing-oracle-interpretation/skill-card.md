## Description: <br>
Generates structured Chinese-language I Ching hexagram interpretation reports from a user-provided hexagram name, changing lines, context, and question. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tuipanda](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuipanda) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, I Ching learners, traditional-culture creators, and reflective decision makers use this skill after obtaining a hexagram to turn the hexagram name, changing lines, and situation context into a structured interpretation with practical next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat reflective I Ching interpretations as professional financial, legal, medical, relationship, or career advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as cultural or personal reflection and advise users to verify major decisions through qualified sources. <br>
Risk: Generated interpretations can be incorrect, incomplete, or overly influential for major decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Require the report to include a decision-autonomy disclaimer and concrete reminders that the hexagram is only a reference. <br>
