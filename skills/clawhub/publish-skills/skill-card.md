## Description:

Helps maintain ClawHub skill releases and diagnose failed or skipped publishes across the history, ai-custom-skills, and ai-thoughts repositories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Developers authorized to maintain the named skill repositories use this skill to publish updates through their workflows, confirm releases, and investigate publishing failures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing or changing public skills without authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm access to the named GitHub and ClawHub accounts, and review skill changes before committing, pushing, or triggering a publish workflow.
