## Description:

Generates FAQ lists from complex medical policies or protocols for FAQ generation, patient education materials, and simplified explanations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aipoch-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/aipoch-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and healthcare-content authors use this skill to turn complex medical policies or protocols into concise FAQ content for patients, researchers, or general audiences. Generated FAQs should be reviewed before publication or use in patient-facing workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source medical documents may contain sensitive or regulated patient information.

Mitigation: Use sanitized or minimally necessary documents, and avoid PHI unless the operating environment is approved for that data.

Risk: Generated FAQs may simplify or misstate medical policy or protocol content.

Mitigation: Review generated FAQs for medical accuracy, privacy, and audience appropriateness before sharing.
