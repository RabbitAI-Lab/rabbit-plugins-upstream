## Description:

Analyzes thermal camera footage of a gathering to compare each person's facial surface temperature with the group average and flag relative differences for follow-up measurement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Families and operators of small group spaces use the skill to review thermal camera footage and identify relative temperature differences that may warrant a calibrated thermometer recheck.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shared accounts and an ineffective no-analysis option may expose private thermal footage or health reports.

Mitigation: Avoid shared-workspace use until sender isolation, export-only behavior, and credential handling are corrected. Obtain participant consent and confirm video handling, retention, report access, and fees before using the service.
