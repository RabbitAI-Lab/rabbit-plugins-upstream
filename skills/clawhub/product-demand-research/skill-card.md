## Description:

Researches public customer discussions to assess demand for a product idea, identify pain points and alternatives, and report findings with links to quoted sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[glasser-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/glasser-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product teams, and marketers use this skill to evaluate a product idea against public customer complaints, workarounds, objections, and buying signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research prompts and targets may be shared with Glasser and paid external data providers.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential product ideas unless sharing the prompt, market, and research targets with external services is acceptable.

Risk: External data-provider calls may incur charges.

Mitigation: Review provider pricing and limit the research scope before running searches.
