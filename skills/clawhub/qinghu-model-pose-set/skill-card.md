## Description:

Generates multiple poses and viewing angles from one apparel model photo for product listings and social media.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[autoagc](https://clawhub.ai/user/autoagc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and content creators use this skill to produce multi-pose apparel images for product pages and social media from a model photo.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may install or update the qhkit npm package and requires a Qinghu AI token.

Mitigation: Confirm the package installation or update and provide the token only when comfortable doing so.

Risk: Image generation may upload the referenced model photo to Qinghu AI.

Mitigation: Avoid private or sensitive photos unless uploading them is intended.

Risk: Generated images may differ from the original in logos, text, or garment details.

Mitigation: Review important product details before publishing the images.
