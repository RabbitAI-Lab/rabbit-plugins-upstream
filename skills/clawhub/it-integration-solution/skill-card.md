## Description: <br>
Generates Chinese-language enterprise IT integration solution proposals as Word (.docx) documents with standardized sections and formatting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[a-lhliang](https://clawhub.ai/user/a-lhliang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, IT integration teams, and proposal writers use this skill to create Chinese-language enterprise IT solution proposals covering project overview, requirements, technical design, architecture, implementation, project management, training, support, and acceptance sections. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for unrelated technical proposal requests. <br>
Mitigation: Review or narrow the trigger wording before deployment in workspaces where unrelated proposal writing is common. <br>
Risk: Generated proposal content may contain incorrect or unsuitable recommendations for a real customer. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified technical and commercial reviewers verify architecture, scope, assumptions, and acceptance criteria before sharing externally. <br>
Risk: Dependency drift could change generated document behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Use locked dependency installation from package-lock.json and review dependency updates before release. <br>
