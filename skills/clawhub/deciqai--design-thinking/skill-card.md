## Description:

Guides agents through design thinking for product and service challenges where teams need observed user behavior, reframed problems, divergent ideas, lightweight prototypes, and evidence-based iteration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, founders, researchers, designers, and agents use this skill to diagnose desirability problems, structure user observation, generate and select concepts, prototype cheaply, test hypotheses, and produce a Design Sprint Report grounded in observed behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on advisory design-thinking guidance and example narratives that may be incomplete or context-dependent.

Mitigation: Verify factual business examples and validate recommendations with direct user observation before making product decisions.

Risk: The artifact includes external source and marketing links.

Mitigation: Review external links before use; the artifact does not instruct agents to fetch or execute remote content.
