## Description:

Activate when: user asks 'why isn't anyone using our product?', 'how do we understand what customers really want?', 'we know the tech works but the market isn't responding', team is stuck on a single solution and needs divergent ideas, or someone discusses user research / personas / prototyping / iterative design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, designers, and product leaders use this skill to investigate user behavior, frame a human need, generate and test concepts, and choose a direction grounded in observation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research notes and reports may expose participants' personal information or confidential business details.

Mitigation: Obtain participants' consent, anonymize research notes, and omit unnecessary personal or confidential information from reports and reusable examples.
