## Description: <br>
Design Thinking helps agents coach teams through user observation, problem framing, ideation, prototyping, and testing to replace assumption-driven product decisions with evidence from real users. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, founders, designers, researchers, and operators use this skill to diagnose weak adoption, understand real user behavior, reframe product problems, generate divergent concepts, and produce a structured Design Sprint Report. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste confidential interview notes, product strategy, or business data while working through design thinking prompts. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential user research or business context unless the agent environment is approved for that information. <br>
Risk: The skill can guide product decisions from incomplete or unvalidated observations. <br>
Mitigation: Use the built-in verification checklist and require direct user observation before treating a Design Sprint Report as decision evidence. <br>
