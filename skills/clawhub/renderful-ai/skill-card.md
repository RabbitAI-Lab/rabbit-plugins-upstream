## Use Case: <br>
External developers and agents use this skill to call Renderful for AI image and video generation, configure API credentials, choose supported models, check generation status, and understand crypto payment options. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts and generation settings are sent to Renderful's paid API. <br>
Mitigation: Use a dedicated API key and avoid prompts that contain secrets, confidential data, or sensitive business information. <br>
Risk: The skill describes x402 wallet settings for autonomous crypto payments without a clear human approval boundary. <br>
Mitigation: Use a low-balance dedicated wallet and require explicit approval before every paid request. <br>
