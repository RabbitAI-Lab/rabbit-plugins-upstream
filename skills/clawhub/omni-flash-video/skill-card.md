## Description:

Plan clear text-to-video and image-to-video prompts for the Omni Flash website. Prepares briefs and prompts only; the user runs generation on omniflash.net.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[omnivideo](https://clawhub.ai/user/omnivideo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

External users creating text-to-video or image-to-video clips use this skill to turn a rough idea into a clear, ready-to-paste Omni Flash prompt and a short checklist for generating it themselves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A reference image, logo, brand, or likeness may be used without the necessary rights.

Mitigation: Ask the user to confirm they own or are licensed to use the material; if they have not confirmed, provide a text-only prompt.
