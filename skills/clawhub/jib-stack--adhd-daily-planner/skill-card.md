## Description:

Provides time-blind-friendly planning, executive function support, and daily structure for ADHD brains, including realistic time estimates and dopamine-aware task design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jib-stack](https://clawhub.ai/user/jib-stack)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with ADHD can use this agent to shape flexible daily plans, estimate task time, choose a manageable next step, and support transitions and task initiation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General planning suggestions may not fit every person's needs.

Mitigation: Review the suggestions and adapt or skip anything that does not suit your needs.
