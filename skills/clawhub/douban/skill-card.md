## Use Case: <br>
External users and content creators use this skill to draft structured Douban-style reviews, interpret rating distributions, curate film and book lists, and understand Douban community norms and second-hand transaction cautions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Rating-distribution and cultural guidance may be outdated or inaccurate for current Douban platform behavior or a specific work. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill's analysis as guidance and verify current ratings, review distributions, and platform norms against live Douban pages before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Review, recommendation, and curation output may reflect subjective taste or genre-specific rating bias. <br>
Mitigation: Ask the agent to state assumptions, audience fit, genre context, and uncertainty instead of presenting ratings as objective quality judgments. <br>
Risk: Douban group second-hand transactions may involve payment or delivery risk because the skill notes there is no platform guarantee. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer in-person checks, avoid risky transfers, and treat transaction guidance as cautionary rather than as assurance of safety. <br>
Risk: The skill discusses suspicious rating patterns and could be redirected toward manipulation tactics. <br>
Mitigation: Keep usage within detection and literacy tasks; do not request or provide刷分, review control, or account-cultivation methods. <br>
