## Description:

Helps assess tourism destination projects before drafting evidence-based, testable development plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tourism planners, destination operators, and investors use this Chinese-language skill to evaluate project value, visitor demand, commercial feasibility, and operational risks before preparing a parameterized implementation proposal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An optional prediction-ledger hook can block edits to sealed predictions.

Mitigation: Review the hook before enabling it and follow the documented resealing process for legitimate changes.
