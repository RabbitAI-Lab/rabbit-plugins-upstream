## Description:

Source-backed property assessment research and draft assessment comparisons. Private veteran review PDFs use explicitly selected saved reviews and show unanswered questions. Retained county facts, owner reports and optional permitted VA observations remain distinct. Building-model, identity, tax-year, taxable-basis and filing holds remain in force. Drafts do not complete official forms, establish eligibility or authorize filing. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Property owners and veteran reviewers use Untax to research assessments, compare county facts with owner reports and permitted VA observations, and prepare private draft review PDFs with unanswered questions visible. The drafts support review; they do not establish eligibility or authorize filing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property or veteran information may be shared through connected services, and saved reviews persist after sign-out.

Mitigation: Review connection permissions and the privacy policy before sharing this information.

Risk: Optional VA observations could be shared without appropriate permission.

Mitigation: Share VA observations only when the separate permission has been explicitly granted.

Risk: Draft assessment comparisons could be mistaken for official forms, eligibility decisions, or authorization to file.

Mitigation: Review the source-backed facts and unanswered questions, and treat generated documents as drafts only.
