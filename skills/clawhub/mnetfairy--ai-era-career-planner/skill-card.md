## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users explore education, career planning, career changes, and AI-era job risk by gathering user context, assessing interests and values, and producing a personalized career planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for AI-era career planning, major selection, job transition planning, and personalized next-step recommendations. The skill can produce conversational assessments, AI impact ratings, salary and demand context, and action-oriented career plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for personal career, education, location, interest, and salary-context information.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the planning task and avoid saving, sending, or reusing personal details unless the user explicitly authorizes it.

Risk: Salary, job-demand, overseas job, and insurance-company references can be incomplete or become outdated.

Mitigation: Treat these references as planning context and verify salary ranges, hiring demand, company credentials, and contact details before making career or financial decisions.

Risk: Optional integrations can send reports, create subscriptions, save memory/profile data, or use live-data lookup.

Mitigation: Use optional integrations only after an explicit user request and confirmation for the specific action.

Risk: Career recommendations and AI impact ratings are probabilistic guidance, not guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Use the report as decision support and combine it with current market research, professional advice, and the user's own constraints.
