## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users assess AI-era career risks and opportunities through guided intake, career-interest and values assessment, salary and job-demand references, and a personalized career plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career advisors use this skill to explore career direction, education paths, AI replacement risk, salary expectations, and concrete next actions. It is designed for career planning, major selection, job transition, and AI-era reskilling conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for personal career background and goals.

Mitigation: Collect only information needed for the planning conversation and require explicit user authorization before exporting, saving, sending, subscribing, or tracking any user data.

Risk: Salary, job-demand, and company references may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a user's local situation.

Mitigation: Treat salary and company data as reference material and advise users to verify important career, compensation, or contact decisions independently.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations could be misunderstood as a sales endorsement.

Mitigation: Provide insurance-company information only when insurance work is relevant or requested, keep recommendations neutral, and remind users to compare options and confirm company qualifications before contact.
