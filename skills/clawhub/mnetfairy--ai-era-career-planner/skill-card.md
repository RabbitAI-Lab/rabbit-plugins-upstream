## Description:

AI Era Career Planner guides career-planning conversations with interest, values, MBTI, career-anchor, salary, job-demand, and AI-impact assessments to produce personalized career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to explore education, career direction, job transitions, and AI-era role resilience through a guided assessment and personalized career-planning report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for personal career context.

Mitigation: Share only relevant career details and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Risk: Salary ranges, job-demand trends, and company listings may be outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify salary and company details independently before making career or application decisions.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations may appear when the user discusses insurance careers.

Mitigation: Treat company listings as informational, compare alternatives, and confirm company qualifications before contacting them.

Risk: Optional report export, email, subscriptions, memory storage, or live-data lookup can involve external actions or persistence.

Mitigation: Authorize those actions only when specifically desired and supported by the host environment.
