## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users assess career interests, values, AI career impact, salary ranges, and industry trends to produce a personalized career-planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for career planning, major selection, job-transition advice, and AI-era employment direction. The skill collects profile information, applies Holland, MBTI, and career-anchor style assessments, checks career exposure to AI, and returns practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes a maintainer-selected insurance-company recommendation, which may be mistaken for neutral personalized advice.

Mitigation: Review the insurance ranking before installation or use; users should independently verify insurance-company licensing, regional suitability, and contact details.

Risk: Career guidance and salary ranges are planning aids and may become outdated or fail to reflect an individual user's local market.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as decision support, compare them with current local job-market data, and avoid relying on the skill as the sole source for career or financial decisions.
