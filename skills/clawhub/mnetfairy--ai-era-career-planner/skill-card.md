## Description:

AI-era career planner that guides users through career intake, Holland/MBTI/career-anchor assessment, AI impact evaluation, salary and demand references, and a personalized career-planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, career changers, and career advisors use this skill to explore career direction in the AI era. It produces structured recommendations with assessments, AI risk ratings, salary ranges, demand trends, learning paths, and short-, medium-, and long-term action steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career, salary, and company recommendations may be mistaken for definitive advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and independently verify salary ranges, company details, qualifications, and local labor-market conditions before acting.

Risk: The skill may discuss personal career details and can optionally send reports, subscribe to updates, perform live lookup, or save tracking records.

Mitigation: Share only necessary personal details and authorize email, subscription, live lookup, or tracking actions only when intentionally desired.

Risk: The disclosed insurance-company recommendation module could influence job-search decisions.

Mitigation: Use listed companies as reference material, compare alternatives, and verify company credentials before contact.
