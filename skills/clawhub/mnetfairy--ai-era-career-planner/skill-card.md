## Description:

AI-Era Career Planner helps users plan careers for an AI-changing labor market by collecting career context, applying interest and values assessments, evaluating AI impact risk, and producing a personalized career planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for career planning, major selection, job transition advice, and AI-era employment direction. The agent gathers user context, applies career assessment frameworks, evaluates AI substitution risk, and returns actionable career directions and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill collects personal career-context information during conversation.

Mitigation: Share only the career, education, location, salary, and preference details needed for the planning task, and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal data.

Risk: The skill includes a disclosed insurance-industry company recommendation flow.

Mitigation: Treat company listings as informational references, compare options independently, and confirm company qualifications before contacting any provider.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, realtime search, report export, and tracking features may involve user data or external actions.

Mitigation: Use these features only after an explicit user request and confirmation for the specific action.
