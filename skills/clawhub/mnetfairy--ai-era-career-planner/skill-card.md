## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users plan careers, education choices, and career transitions with AI-era impact assessment, interest and values profiling, salary references, demand trends, and personalized career-planning reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career advisors use this skill to collect career-planning context, assess interests and career anchors, evaluate AI replacement risk, and produce practical career direction reports with next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career, salary, and company information may be stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat salary ranges, demand trends, and company contact details as reference material and verify them independently before making career or financial decisions.

Risk: Insurance-company recommendations could be mistaken for an endorsement or sales instruction.

Mitigation: Use the skill's disclosure language, present options neutrally, and remind users to compare providers and confirm company qualifications before contacting them.

Risk: Optional export or integration features could disclose personal career-planning details if used without consent.

Mitigation: Run report export, email, subscription, memory, or external-data integrations only when the user explicitly asks and the host environment permits it.
