## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps Chinese-speaking users assess career interests, AI-related job risk, salary context, and next steps, then produces a personalized career planning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, and professionals use this skill for Chinese-language career planning, major selection, career transition advice, AI impact assessment, salary context, and practical action planning. It can also create an optional Markdown report when the user explicitly requests export.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with strongest coverage for Chinese-language career planning and China-focused salary, insurance-company, and labor-market references.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Salary ranges, job-market trends, and insurance-company details may be incomplete, stale, or advisory rather than authoritative.

Mitigation: Verify salary ranges, company qualifications, and recommendations independently before making education, employment, insurance, or commercial decisions.

Risk: Optional export, email, realtime search, subscriptions, or saved tracking can create external side effects or disclose user-provided career information.

Mitigation: Use the default offline mode unless the user explicitly authorizes the specific export, sending, search, subscription, or tracking action.
