## Description:

Helps users plan careers for the AI era by gathering profile details, assessing interests and values, evaluating AI career impact, and producing personalized career-planning reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for career planning, education and major selection, role changes, and AI-era job-risk evaluation. It produces personalized recommendations with assessment results, AI impact ratings, salary context, learning paths, and next-step actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags a hardcoded first-place insurance-company referral with unverified contact data.

Mitigation: Review insurance-related behavior before installation, treat company names and phone numbers as unverified leads, and independently confirm licensing, regional coverage, and contact details through official sources before acting.
