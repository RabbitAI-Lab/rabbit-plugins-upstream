## Description:

A Chinese-language career-planning skill that collects user context, applies career assessment frameworks, evaluates AI disruption risk, and produces a personalized career plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, employees, and career changers use this skill to explore career direction in the AI era, compare role fit, review salary and demand signals, and create an actionable planning report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China and Chinese-language users globally

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career guidance and AI impact ratings may be interpreted as certain predictions.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as decision support, preserve uncertainty, and encourage users to compare the advice with current local market evidence and qualified human guidance.

Risk: The insurance-company recommendation path can appear promotional or partner-oriented.

Mitigation: Show the disclosed insurance guidance only when the user expresses relevant interest, keep alternatives visible, and remind users to verify company credentials before contacting providers.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, live-search, memory, or report export actions can expose personal career details.

Mitigation: Require explicit user authorization for each optional action and avoid persistent storage, outbound messages, or live search by default.
