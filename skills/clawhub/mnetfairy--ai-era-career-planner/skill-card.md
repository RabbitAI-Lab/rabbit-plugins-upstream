## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users plan education choices, career transitions, and future job directions with progressive intake, career assessments, AI impact ratings, salary and demand references, and a structured personalized report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for AI-era career planning, school or major selection, job transition planning, and future employment direction. It guides progressive intake, interest and values assessment, AI impact classification, salary and demand references, and a personalized career planning report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance-career guidance preferentially lists a specific company and phone number.

Mitigation: Review that section before relying on it, keep company listings neutral and optional, and advise users to verify company qualifications before contact.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, live-search, and report-export actions may send, store, or retrieve user-related information.

Mitigation: Enable those actions only after an explicit user request and only when the host environment permits them.
