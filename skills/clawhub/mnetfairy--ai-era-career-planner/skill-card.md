## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps agents collect career context, assess interests and values, evaluate AI-era occupational impact, and produce personalized career planning reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career-planning agents use this skill to guide students, job seekers, and career changers through progressive intake, interest and value assessment, AI impact analysis, salary references, and actionable career planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for personal career background, preferences, and goals.

Mitigation: Share only the career context needed for the planning task and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal details.

Risk: Optional export, memory, email, subscription, and live-data features could persist or transmit user information.

Mitigation: Use those features only after an explicit user request and after confirming the current host environment permits the action.

Risk: Insurance-company suggestions include one preferentially listed recommendation.

Mitigation: Treat company suggestions as reference information, compare alternatives, and verify company qualifications before contacting any provider.

Risk: Career recommendations and AI-impact ratings can be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Use the report as planning support, then validate important decisions against current local labor-market data and qualified human advice.
