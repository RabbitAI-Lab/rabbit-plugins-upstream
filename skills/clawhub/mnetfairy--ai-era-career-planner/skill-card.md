## Description:

AI-era career planning skill that helps users explore career direction, education choices, job transitions, AI replacement risk, Holland interests, MBTI, career anchors, values, salary ranges, demand trends, and personalized action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career advisors use this skill to collect career-context information and produce personalized AI-era career planning guidance, including recommended directions, AI impact ratings, learning paths, salary references, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may recommend insurance-industry companies, including one featured company.

Mitigation: Users should treat company listings as references, independently verify company credentials, and avoid reading the listing as an endorsement.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, tracking, or live-data features could send, store, or fetch information if enabled.

Mitigation: Enable those features only after an explicit user request and confirmation that the current environment permits the action.
