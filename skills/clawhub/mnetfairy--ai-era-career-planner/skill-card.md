## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users explore career choices and transitions with guided assessments, AI impact ratings, salary and demand context, and a personalized career plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get career-planning guidance for education choices, job transitions, and AI-era employability. The skill collects career background and goals, applies assessment frameworks, and returns concrete career directions with action steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to share career background, education, goals, and location during planning.

Mitigation: Users should share only information they are comfortable discussing and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal details.

Risk: Salary ranges, job-demand trends, and insurance-company information may be outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as informational starting points and verify current salary, employer, licensing, and company details before making decisions or contacting companies.

Risk: Career recommendations are probabilistic and may not fit a user's personal constraints or local labor market.

Mitigation: Use recommendations as planning guidance, compare multiple options, and seek qualified local advice for high-stakes education, employment, or financial decisions.
