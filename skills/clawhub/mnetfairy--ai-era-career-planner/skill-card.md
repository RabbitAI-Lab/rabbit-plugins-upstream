## Description:

AI-Era Career Planner helps users explore career paths, education choices, career transitions, and AI-driven labor-market risks through guided assessment and personalized planning reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for career-planning conversations, including school and major selection, career-change decisions, job-direction comparisons, and AI-era resilience planning. The skill gathers user context, applies career-interest and values frameworks, evaluates AI impact levels, and returns a personalized action-oriented report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The insurance referral section highlights specific companies, and some entries may need verification before use.

Mitigation: Review the insurance referral section and confirm company credentials before relying on or sharing referral information.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, tracking, and report export features can send or save user career-planning data.

Mitigation: Use these features only after explicit user authorization, minimize saved data, and keep storage location and deletion under user control.

Risk: Career recommendations, salary ranges, job-demand trends, and AI impact ratings can become outdated or may not match a user's local market.

Mitigation: Treat the report as planning guidance and review key labor-market claims against current local sources before making major education or employment decisions.
