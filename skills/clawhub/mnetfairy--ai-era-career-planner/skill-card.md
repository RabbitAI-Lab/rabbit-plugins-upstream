## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users explore career choices, education paths, career transitions, AI exposure, salary context, job-demand trends, and personalized career-planning reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for AI-era career planning, including staged information gathering, interest and value assessment, AI impact analysis, salary and demand context, and structured next-step recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career, salary, and insurance-company recommendations may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a user's specific situation.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as informational and independently verify salary data, company details, credentials, and market conditions before making career or contact decisions.

Risk: Optional email, subscription, memory, tracking, web lookup, or report file output could disclose or persist personal career-planning information.

Mitigation: Enable external actions, persistence, web lookup, and file output only when the user explicitly requests them and the host environment permits them.
