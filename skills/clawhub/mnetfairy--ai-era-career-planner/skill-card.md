## Description:

AI Era Career Planner helps users explore education, career direction, career transitions, AI-related job impact, salary expectations, and next-step career plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for AI-era career planning conversations, including interest assessment, career values analysis, AI job-impact assessment, salary and demand context, and a structured personalized career plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share personal career background, education, salary, or location details during planning.

Mitigation: Ask only for information needed for the planning conversation and do not retain, export, email, subscribe, or otherwise persist user information unless the user explicitly requests it.

Risk: Salary ranges, job-demand trends, and insurance company details may be outdated or region-specific.

Mitigation: Treat bundled reference data as planning guidance and verify salary data, job demand, and company qualifications independently before acting on them.

Risk: Career recommendations and AI replacement ratings can be mistaken for certainty.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as probabilistic guidance, include uncertainty, and encourage comparison across options before major education or career decisions.
