## Description:

Crypto and market intelligence from Agent Einstein (emc2ai.io) over a public MCP endpoint. 18 free read-only tools, no account or key - AI price forecasts with a published accuracy record, market sentiment, whale and smart-money flows, prediction-market signals, Bitcoin cycle position, token safety screen, US Congress trades. 22 more paid tools use a prepaid credit balance. Use for questions about crypto prices, forecasts, on-chain flows, token safety or prediction markets.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Queries and addresses submitted to the tools are sent to emc2ai.io.

Mitigation: Submit only information appropriate to share with that service.

Risk: Some tools require paid credits or USDC payments.

Mitigation: Check the live tool catalog and prices before authorizing paid use.

Risk: Market forecasts and analysis may be uncertain.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research indicators, not financial advice, and review forecast accuracy alongside any quoted forecast.

Risk: Private keys are not needed for the skill's tools.

Mitigation: Never share private keys.
