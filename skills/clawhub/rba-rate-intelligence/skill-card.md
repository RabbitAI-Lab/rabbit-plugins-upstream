## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill as a general reference for Australian Reserve Bank cash-rate context, RBA meeting dates, CPI trends, and mortgage repayment impact examples. It supports financial awareness and planning conversations, not personalized financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Static RBA, CPI, mortgage, and market-rate figures may become outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current cash-rate, CPI, meeting, and lender-rate information with official or authoritative sources before relying on the content. <br>
Risk: Mortgage repayment examples are general reference material and may not fit a user's financial situation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat repayment tables and formulas as illustrative only and consult a qualified mortgage broker or financial advisor before making financial decisions. <br>
