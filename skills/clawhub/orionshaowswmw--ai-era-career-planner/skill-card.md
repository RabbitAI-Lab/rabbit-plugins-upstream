## Description:

Offline, deterministic career planning for the AI era with China-market career matching, modeled salary references, assessment screens, and Markdown report generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals, career advisors, and agents can use this skill to run offline career-direction screens, compare 12 AI-era career directions, retrieve clearly labeled modeled salary reference ranges, and generate a concise Markdown career-planning report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; career, salary, and job-demand references focus on the China market.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Modeled salary ranges, career-fit scores, and assessment outputs may be mistaken for live market data, certified psychological results, or predictions.

Mitigation: Present them as rough planning aids and verify salary numbers, job-market claims, and career decisions against live official or trusted sources before making career, hiring, or financial decisions.

Risk: Insurance contact details and other dated reference entries may be stale or unverified.

Mitigation: Check business contact details against current official sources before quoting or acting on them.
