## Description:

Provides brand critiques from a public website and optional product document through Sigil's MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams and individuals use Sigil to review a company's brand, positioning, messaging, website, and visual identity using its public website and, optionally, a product document.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitting a website or optional document shares it with Sigil's external service.

Mitigation: Use public websites and only documents you may share; avoid confidential, regulated, or customer-sensitive material unless Sigil's privacy and retention practices are approved.
