## Description:

Provides brand reviews based on a public website and an optional product document through Sigil's MCP service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abe732](https://clawhub.ai/user/abe732)

## Use Case:

People seeking feedback on a company's brand, positioning, messaging, website, or visual identity use this skill to request a scored review and prioritized recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The target website and any optional product document are sent to Sigil's external MCP service.

Mitigation: Confirm this sharing is acceptable before use; do not submit confidential documents without organizational approval.
