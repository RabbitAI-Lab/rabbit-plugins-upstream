## Description:

Helps create consistent character prompts for AI images, comics, storyboards, video, and 3D across different model families.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Artists, designers, and other creators use this skill to draft and refine fictional adult-character prompts, keeping visual details consistent across models and multi-scene projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference images and generated outputs may be uploaded to third-party model providers.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive or real-person images without appropriate permission; review the chosen provider's terms and privacy practices.

Risk: Generated character imagery may violate content policies or applicable law.

Mitigation: Use fictional, non-sexual adult characters and check prompts and outputs against provider policies and local requirements.
