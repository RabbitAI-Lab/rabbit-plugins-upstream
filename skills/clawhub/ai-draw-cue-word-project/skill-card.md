## Description:

Helps agents plan, write, and validate consistent AI character prompts for images, storyboards, comics, video, and 3D across multiple model families.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nohn3043-arch](https://clawhub.ai/user/nohn3043-arch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Artists and prompt writers use this skill to adapt prompts for fictional, non-sexual character design and maintain character identity, composition, and continuity across images or sequences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Two Doubao examples conflict with the skill's adult-only declaration.

Mitigation: Revise those examples to use explicitly adult characters before adapting or reusing them.

Risk: Reference images supplied to an external generation service will be shared with that service.

Mitigation: Account for the selected service's handling of reference images before supplying them.
