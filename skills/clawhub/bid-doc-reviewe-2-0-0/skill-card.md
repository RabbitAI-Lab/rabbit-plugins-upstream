## Description: <br>
Bid Doc Reviewe 2.0.0 helps users review Chinese bid and tender documents against configured procurement-law knowledge bases and produces traceable compliance risks and revision guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Procurement teams, bid agents, and compliance reviewers use this skill to triage Chinese tender documents, identify high-risk clauses, check timelines and qualification requirements, and prepare corrective recommendations with cited knowledge-base support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat generated compliance guidance as formal legal advice or as a substitute for current local regulatory review. <br>
Mitigation: Verify cited rules against current local regulations and use qualified legal, procurement, or administrative review for binding decisions. <br>
Risk: Incomplete project facts can push the review outside the intended scope or weaken the resulting findings. <br>
Mitigation: Provide project type, funding source, tender body, method, estimated value, and jurisdiction before relying on the review. <br>
