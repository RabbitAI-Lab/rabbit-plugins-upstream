## Description:

Islamic inheritance (Faraid, Mirath, Meeras, Wirasat, Waris) calculator and guide. Interviews the user about the deceased, estate, debts, will (wasiyyah) and surviving relatives, then computes each heir's share - fixed shares (1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 2/3, 1/3, 1/6), residuary ('asabah), blocking (hajb), 'awl and radd - citing the Quran ayah, sahih hadith, Companion (Sahaba) ruling or scholarly consensus (ijma) behind every rule, and clearly flagging where the Sunni schools (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) differ. Use when someone asks how to divide a Muslim's estate, who inherits under Sharia, what share a wife, husband, daughter, son, mother, father or sibling gets, or how much can be left in an Islamic will. Works fully offline, no APIs.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inheritance scenarios can involve sensitive family information and decisions with religious or civil-law consequences.

Mitigation: Use anonymized family details where possible, and confirm actual distributions with a qualified scholar and an adviser familiar with local inheritance law.
