## Description: <br>
Regulates intelligent care by sustaining rest, slowness, and play as active states, preserving presence and coherence across extended, complex environments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[otherpowers](https://clawhub.ai/user/otherpowers) <br>

MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents and agent builders use Ambient Stamina to sustain a slower, rest-aware interaction style during extended or ambiguous work, preserving presence, coherence, and care without turning rest into surveillance or scoring. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill intentionally promotes a slower, rest-oriented interaction style, which can be inappropriate for urgent, highly audited, or status-heavy workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only where slower cadence and reduced urgency are acceptable; avoid it for workflows that require frequent progress updates, explicit explanations, or rapid escalation. <br>
