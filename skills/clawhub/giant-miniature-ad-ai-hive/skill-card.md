## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI_HIVE_API_KEY or ~/.ai-hive/config.json can authorize paid generation activity.

Mitigation: Review generated commands before running them, keep credentials out of logs and repositories, and confirm routing, model, and price snapshot before submitting jobs.

Risk: Reference images, videos, audio, logos, or product materials may be uploaded to AI-HIVE.

Mitigation: Upload only media the user is authorized to use, and restrict the workflow to abstract structure guidance when rights are unclear.

Risk: Giant-object and miniature-world ads can mislead viewers about product size, function, endorsements, or real-world events.

Mitigation: Label spectacle as creative expression, verify product and performance claims, and avoid fabricated testimonials or platform-rule evasion.
