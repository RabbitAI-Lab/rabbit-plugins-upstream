## Description:

Turns a user's own paper or research results into a patent technical disclosure by identifying candidate innovations and publication risks before generating a Word document.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and engineering teams use this skill to review candidate inventions and publication-related novelty risks in their own papers, then prepare a technical disclosure for a patent professional. It helps organize the material but does not replace professional patent advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research papers and other confidential materials may be sent to PatentMax.

Mitigation: Send only research you are authorized to share, review confidentiality and retention terms, and approve the exact materials and per-report cost before submission.

Risk: API credentials may be exposed through chat or unsafe redirect and key-storage handling.

Mitigation: Do not send keys through chat; request fixes for unsafe redirects and paid-key storage before using valuable credentials.
