## Description:

Helps researchers identify patentable points and publication risks in their own papers, then prepare a Word technical disclosure for selected inventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ip930](https://clawhub.ai/user/ip930)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and inventors use the skill to review their own unpublished research for potential patent disclosures, select invention points, and generate a technical disclosure for review by a patent professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished papers, laboratory results, trade secrets, or invention details may be uploaded to PatentMax.

Mitigation: Use only with materials the user is authorized to share; review confidentiality and obtain informed consent before submission.

Risk: Trial credentials and task records may persist locally, and disclosure files are written to the working directory or selected output folder.

Mitigation: Confirm the storage locations and access controls before use, and handle generated files and local state as sensitive research material.

Risk: Generated patentability guidance and disclosures may contain assumptions or omit details needed for filing.

Mitigation: Verify source facts and flagged assumptions, and have a qualified patent professional review novelty, deadlines, and filing documents.
