## Description:

Turns a user's paper or research results into an analysis of potential patentable points and publication risks, then drafts a patent technical disclosure for selected ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers and developers use the skill to identify potential patentable points and publication risks in papers or research results, then prepare a technical disclosure for selected ideas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved API keys may not be adequately protected.

Mitigation: Use a private key file with restrictive permissions and do not share keys through chat.

Risk: Redirected requests could send credentials outside the intended service.

Mitigation: Restrict authenticated requests to approved HTTPS destinations and handle redirects safely.

Risk: Unpublished research material is sent off the user's computer for generation.

Mitigation: Start with fictional demo material, review the service privacy policy, and share sensitive details only when approved.
