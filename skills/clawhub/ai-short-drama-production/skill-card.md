## Description:

Plans end-to-end AI short-drama production, from story development and shot design through generation, audio, quality checks, rights, and delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[frankchenfei](https://clawhub.ai/user/frankchenfei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and production teams use the skill to turn a premise, screenplay, mascot, or IP into a scoped short-drama production package. It supports story and shot planning, generation task manifests, audio and post-production guidance, quality checks, rights tracking, and delivery records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project prompts or reference assets may be sent to an external generation service.

Mitigation: Check the service destination and data terms, and confirm the user has approved sharing the selected prompts and assets before submitting generation tasks.

Risk: Video generation may incur charges or create external tasks.

Mitigation: Before generation, state the task count, duration, resolution, variants, expected wait, and known budget impact, then obtain explicit user intent.
