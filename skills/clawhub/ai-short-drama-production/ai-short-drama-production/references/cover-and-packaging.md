# Cover and Launch Packaging

Use this reference for vertical covers, trailers, launch assets and A/B testing.

## 1. Cover spec

Default:

- 1080×1920, `9:16`
- one clear protagonist face
- title readable at thumbnail size
- genre badge at top
- one sentence tagline
- copyright and AI label
- no more than two type families

## 2. Title hierarchy

1. franchise line, e.g. `火猴之`
2. series title, e.g. `烬火行者`
3. genre badge
4. one-line promise
5. season/CTA/copyright

Do not place the main title over the character’s eyes or mouth. Keep safe margins.

## 3. Cover variants

Create three variants per series:

- **face variant:** strongest protagonist expression;
- **conflict variant:** hero + threat + stakes;
- **world variant:** hero in front of the signature location.

Test at 360×640 first. If the title cannot be read at that size, it will fail in feed.

## 4. Launch asset matrix

- 6s hook
- 15s info-feed cut
- 30s main trailer
- 45s story trailer
- 3 covers
- 1 character poster
- 1 end card
- 1 subtitle master
- 1 clean master and 1 watermarked master

## 5. Packaging copy

Use:

- one concrete promise;
- one conflict;
- one character action;
- one reason to continue.

Examples of structure, not fixed copy:

- `免费的最贵，贪心的先输。`
- `字里行间，另有天地。`
- `火会记住，冷火会忘记。`

Avoid empty phrases such as “年度巨制”, “震撼来袭” unless supported by an actual marketing plan.

## 6. A/B test rules

- test three hooks before scaling a 30s trailer;
- test three covers after the hook is stable;
- judge 3s retention, 10s retention, completion, follow conversion and comments;
- replace a weak hook instead of adding more effects;
- keep the winning master and record the variant metadata.

## 7. Rights

Covers must carry:

- company mark;
- copyright year and owner;
- AI label where required;
- licensed fonts and music.
