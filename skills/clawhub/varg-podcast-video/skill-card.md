## Description:

Creates consistent-character, two-person podcast and dialogue videos with AI-generated scenes, speech, a review storyboard, and a stitched MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use it to turn a short two-character dialogue and character reference images into an AI-generated podcast, interview, debate, or explainer video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Character photos and dialogue are submitted to Varg and its generation providers for cloud processing.

Mitigation: Use only photos and dialogue you are comfortable submitting, and get consent for any identifiable people.

Risk: Video generation can incur substantial credit costs.

Mitigation: Review the estimate and approve the cost before generating video scenes.

Risk: External installers, the related varg-ai skill, and live service behavior were not evaluated.

Mitigation: Install only a reviewed revision and assess any external installer or related skill separately.
