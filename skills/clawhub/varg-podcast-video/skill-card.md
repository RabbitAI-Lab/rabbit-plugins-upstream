## Description:

Creates podcast-style videos of two consistent AI characters speaking short dialogue scenes, then assembles them into a reviewable storyboard and MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators use this skill to produce short podcast, interview, debate, or explainer videos featuring two AI characters across consistent camera angles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference images and dialogue are sent to Varg, and video generation uses paid credits.

Mitigation: Use images you have permission to share, avoid sensitive material, protect your API key, and review estimated costs before approving video generation.
