## Description:

Creates short podcast-style videos of two consistent AI characters speaking scripted dialogue, with a review storyboard and stitched MP4 output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators can use this skill to turn a short script and character references into a two-character podcast, interview, debate, or educational explainer video. It guides image and video generation, storyboard review, and MP4 assembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Character references may include identifiable photos, and dialogue may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Use only images and dialogue you are authorized to share, and avoid confidential material.

Risk: The security assessment is partial, and its guidance recommends checking the installer source and version.

Mitigation: Review the installation source and version before installing; treat the current assessment as incomplete.
