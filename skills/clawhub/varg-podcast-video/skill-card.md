## Description:

Creates short podcast-style and two-character dialogue videos with consistent characters, generated scenes, native audio, a review storyboard, and an MP4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to plan and generate short podcast conversations, interviews, debates, and myth-versus-truth explainers featuring two characters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected reference images and prompts are sent to Varg for cloud processing, and private photos may have provider retention implications.

Mitigation: Use images you are authorized to process and check Varg's retention terms before uploading private photos.

Risk: Video generation spends credits and can be substantially more expensive than still-image generation.

Mitigation: Review the generation estimate and obtain user approval before starting video generation.
