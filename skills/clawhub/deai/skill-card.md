## Description:

DeAI improves AI-drafted text so it sounds naturally human by removing filler phrases, restructuring rhythm, and marking places where the user should add personal voice across Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and writing-focused teams use this skill to edit AI-drafted text into a more natural style while leaving personal anecdotes, colloquial phrasing, and specific numbers for user review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edited output may be used in contexts with disclosure, academic integrity, or platform-specific content rules.

Mitigation: Review the edited output before use and confirm it complies with the applicable disclosure, academic, and platform requirements.

Risk: Review markers identify places for personal voice, anecdotes, or numbers that the skill does not know.

Mitigation: Replace or remove all [💬], [📝], and [📊] markers with verified user-provided content before publishing.
