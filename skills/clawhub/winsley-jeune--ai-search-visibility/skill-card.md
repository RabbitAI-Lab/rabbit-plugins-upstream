## Description:

Assess a brand’s visibility in AI search answers and referral traffic, identify cited sources and crawler or content gaps, and prioritize practical improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[winsley-jeune](https://clawhub.ai/user/winsley-jeune)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and SEO consultants use this skill to assess how AI assistants surface a brand, review AI referral traffic and cited sources, and prioritize site and third-party improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Buyer questions may contain confidential information and could be sent to external AI services without a privacy warning.

Mitigation: Avoid including confidential buyer details unless the user has reviewed and accepts the external services’ privacy terms.
