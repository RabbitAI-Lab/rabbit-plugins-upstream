## Description:

Capture errors, corrections, and better approaches as short learnings, and promote broadly useful ones to persistent workspace files. Use when a command fails, the user corrects you, you find a better approach, or before starting a major task.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lemonc19](https://clawhub.ai/user/lemonc19)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their workspace maintainers can record sanitized lessons from failures, corrections, and better approaches, then promote verified lessons for future sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review found no issues in the reviewed document, but reference coverage is incomplete and some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review referenced workspace files and learning notes to confirm they fit your needs before installing or applying the skill.

Risk: Learning entries may be shared or promoted into persistent workspace files.

Mitigation: Keep entries short and sanitized, omit secrets and private file contents, and promote only verified lessons that are broadly useful.
