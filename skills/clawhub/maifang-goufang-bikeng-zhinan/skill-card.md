## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property, financing, and market claims may not fit the buyer's location or current conditions.

Mitigation: Verify local rules, current prices, taxes, and loan terms before committing to a purchase.

Risk: Renovation guidance cannot establish a building's structural or electrical safety remotely.

Mitigation: Obtain on-site assessment and required approvals; use qualified professionals for structural, electrical, and gas work.
