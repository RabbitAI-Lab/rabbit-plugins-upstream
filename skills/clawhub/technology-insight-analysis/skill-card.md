## Description:

Analyzes searched, screened, and tagged patent datasets to produce evidence-based technology competitiveness insights, competitor landscapes, strategic opportunities, and executive-ready Markdown or HTML reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External analysts, patent teams, and strategy stakeholders use this skill to turn searched, screened, and tagged patent datasets into executive technology competitiveness insight reports and visual white papers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is presented as broadly applicable, while bundled report templates include dairy or milk-protein assumptions.

Mitigation: Use it as-is only for dairy or milk-protein patent intelligence, or revise the templates before applying it to other technical fields.

Risk: The skill depends on PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP services that may access account-scoped patent, literature, or related business data.

Mitigation: Confirm enabled MCP services and account permissions before use, and ensure users understand which data sources are available to the agent.
