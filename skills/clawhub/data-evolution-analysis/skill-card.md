## Description: <br>
Analyze data evolution patterns in construction organizations and assess digital maturity and data strategy for construction companies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[datadrivenconstruction](https://clawhub.ai/user/datadrivenconstruction) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Construction analysts, digital transformation teams, and developers use this skill to assess digital maturity, review construction data flows, compare maturity over time, and generate structured recommendations and reports. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Company system inventories, process notes, or survey responses may contain sensitive operational data. <br>
Mitigation: Review and minimize company data before providing it to the skill. <br>
Risk: The report-generation example writes maturity_report.md, which could replace an existing file if reused unchanged. <br>
Mitigation: Use a distinct output filename or confirm that the existing report can be replaced before saving. <br>
