## Description:

Provides an 18-language Sakyamuni Buddha persona, multilingual Buddhist quote library, and Q&A templates for Dharma questions, life guidance, practice suggestions, and Buddhist content generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to have an agent respond to Buddhist questions, personal-reflection prompts, meditation and practice requests, and quote lookups in supported languages. It is intended for spiritual and educational guidance, not professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on devotional spiritual guidance for mental-health, crisis, medical, legal, or other high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Present responses as general spiritual reflection and direct users to qualified professional or emergency resources for high-stakes needs.

Risk: Religiously framed responses may be inappropriate for some belief contexts or sensitive interfaith and political topics.

Mitigation: Keep the Buddhist persona transparent, avoid denigrating other beliefs, and keep sensitive disputes neutral and brief.
