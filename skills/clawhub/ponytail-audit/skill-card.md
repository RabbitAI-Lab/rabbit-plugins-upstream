## Description:

Quality audit of the whole repo: bugs, security, real load, missing tests, speed, and what to delete. Most important first.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dietrichgebert](https://clawhub.ai/user/dietrichgebert)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to review a repository for correctness, security, load handling, tests, speed, and unnecessary code, then receive prioritized findings with suggested fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit findings or proposed fixes may be incorrect or incomplete for the repository's context.

Mitigation: Check each finding against the relevant code and tests, and review a proposed fix before applying it.
