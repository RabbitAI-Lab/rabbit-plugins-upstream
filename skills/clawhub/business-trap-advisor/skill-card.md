## Use Case:

External small-business operators, entrepreneurs, and merchants use this skill to review cooperation proposals, supply agreements, agency terms, sponsorship offers, franchise invitations, and negotiation records for practical business risks. The skill helps structure a review around money flow, goods or services, risk allocation, exit terms, negotiation bottom lines, and pre-signing checks.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive contracts, negotiation chats, company names, people names, and payment details.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive details; summarize or redact private business documents before use when possible.

Risk: The skill includes an off-platform WeChat payment and contact funnel for an unreviewed paid toolkit.

Mitigation: Do not send payment screenshots, private business documents, or accept external scripts or toolkits through WeChat unless the publisher is independently trusted and those materials are reviewed separately.

Risk: Business-risk guidance could be mistaken for legal advice or relied on for high-value agreements.

Mitigation: Treat the output as practical business experience only and consult a qualified lawyer for large, complex, or uncertain contracts.
