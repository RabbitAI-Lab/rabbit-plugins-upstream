## Description:

Reviews business proposals, contracts, and deal chats from a veteran trader's perspective to identify practical risks, negotiation bottom lines, and pre-signing checks, without providing legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and small-business operators use this skill to review pasted cooperation proposals, supply agreements, agency terms, franchise invitations, sponsorship offers, or negotiation chats for practical business risks and negotiation next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste confidential personal or business details into deal materials.

Mitigation: Share only necessary excerpts and redact names, companies, IDs, account details, and unnecessary amounts before use.

Risk: The skill provides practical business-risk guidance, not legal advice, and may miss legal issues in complex or high-value agreements.

Mitigation: Use it for triage and negotiation preparation, and have a qualified lawyer review large, complex, or legally sensitive contracts.

Risk: The review can be incomplete when key deal terms are missing from the pasted material.

Mitigation: Include the core terms for money flow, delivery, risk allocation, and exit rights, and treat missing terms as items to verify before signing.
