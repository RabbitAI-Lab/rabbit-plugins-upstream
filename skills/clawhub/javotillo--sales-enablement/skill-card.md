## Description:

Creates B2B sales enablement collateral including pitch decks, one-pagers, objection handling docs, demo scripts, ROI calculators, sales playbooks, proposals, and buyer persona cards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, marketing, and go-to-market teams use this skill to draft practical sales materials for reps and champions across discovery, demo, negotiation, and closing workflows. It helps structure collateral around buyer persona, sales stage, proof points, objections, ROI, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sales collateral may include inaccurate pricing, ROI calculations, proof points, or customer claims if source context is incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Review generated materials against approved product marketing, pricing, customer evidence, and legal guidance before sharing with prospects or customers.

Risk: The skill can use local product-marketing context when present, which may contain sensitive or non-public business information.

Mitigation: Confirm that any generated collateral is appropriate for the intended audience and remove confidential context before external distribution.
