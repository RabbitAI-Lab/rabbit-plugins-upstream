# Global Restaurant Digitalization Best Practices: Deep Dive

## 1. Yum! Brands

### Company Profile
- **Brands**: KFC, Taco Bell, Pizza Hut, Habit Burger
- **Scale**: 58,000+ locations, 150+ countries
- **Digital Core**: Byte by Yum platform

### Digital Strategy
- **Byte by Yum**: Proprietary multi-brand SaaS platform, end-to-end from POS to digital ordering
- **Three-Layer AI Architecture**: (1) Operational efficiency (2) Customer growth (3) Store operations simplification
- **Multi-Vendor Strategy**: NVIDIA (voice AI), AWS, Microsoft Azure
- **Byte AI Restaurant Coach**: Deployed across 28,000+ locations; AI-driven scheduling, staff reallocation, loss prevention

### Key Metrics
- Digital sales share: **60%** ($11B/quarter)
- Byte-integrated locations: **38,000+**
- Smart operations: 7,000 stores
- Digital ordering: 18,000 stores
- Inventory reduced 85%, delivery errors down 75%, customer satisfaction +10%

### Core Takeaways
1. **Unified platform vs. fragmented systems**: Byte is the single digital foundation across the entire group
2. **Infrastructure before AI**: Byte unified POS and data first, then layered on AI
3. **Multi-brand shared platform**: KFC/Taco Bell/Pizza Hut share a single technology platform
4. **AI must be "in use"**: 38,000 stores using Byte Coach daily is where the real ROI lives

## 2. McDonald's

### Company Profile
- **Scale**: 40,000+ locations, 100+ countries
- **US locations**: ~13,600
- **Digital Core**: Google Cloud Edge + ArchIQ

### Digital Strategy
- **Accelerating the Arches -> McDonald's > NEXT**
- **Full-stack Google partnership**: Edge Cloud to every store, ArchIQ voice AI
- **Three-layer architecture**: Restaurant Platform (store), Consumer Platform (customer), Company Platform (enterprise)
- **Market-localized AI**: Multi-model AI gateway in select regions integrating local LLMs alongside global models

### Key Metrics
- Loyalty members: **185 million** (90-day active), target 250M (2027)
- Loyalty system sales: **$37B** (2025)
- Drive-Thru share of US sales: **70%**
- ArchIQ processed: 1M+ orders, 90% without human intervention
- Geofencing-ready: wait time -50%+

### Core Takeaways
1. **Failed AI projects can be rebooted**: IBM voice AI failed -> Google successfully took over
2. **Edge computing is the foundation for AI**: Edge servers deployed at every store
3. **Tech investment is a long game**: 2025-2027 is the heavy investment period
4. **Global unity + local intelligence**: Global Edge platform + region-specific AI gateways

## 4. Chipotle Mexican Grill

### Company Profile
- **Scale**: 3,500+ locations, primarily US
- **Digital Core**: Fully in-house built

### Digital Strategy
- **Fully proprietary**: POS, KDS, App, website — all built in-house
- **PreciTaste**: AI prep forecasting — tells the kitchen "what to prep, how much"
- **Autocado**: AI-powered avocado-cutting robot
- **Digital kitchen line**: Dedicated make-line for digital orders

### Key Metrics
- Digital sales share: **>40%**
- In-house tech team: hundreds
- Chipotlane (digital Drive-Thru): >500 locations

### Core Takeaways
1. **In-house can beat off-the-shelf**: When your operating model is sufficiently unique
2. **Digital orders need a dedicated kitchen line**: You cannot layer digital orders onto a traditional line
3. **AI starts at the "most painful" point**: Chipotle chose food waste as its first AI use case

## 5. Yum China

### Company Profile
- **Scale**: 18,737 locations (Q2 2025), KFC China/Pizza Hut China/Taco Bell China/Little Sheep, etc.
- **Digital Core**: Proprietary multi-model AI gateway + AI + robotics supply chain

### Digital Strategy
- **Q-Smart**: Wearable AI assistant — managers wear earbuds/watches, voice-control store operations
- **AI ordering assistant**: LLM-integrated voice ordering with fuzzy recognition
- **Multi-model AI gateway**: Integrates multiple local large language models
- **AI + robotics supply chain**: -18degC cold-storage robotic picking, AI order batching and truck routing, autonomous heavy trucks

### Key Metrics
- Net new stores per quarter: 636 (all-time high)
- Digital payment share: >90%
- AI ordering efficiency gain: 75%

### Core Takeaways
1. **Local-market AI requires local models**: Use region-appropriate LLMs
2. **Wearables are the next UI**: Watches/earbuds/glasses are the natural interface for back-of-house
3. **Supply chain AI = the next breakout**: Robotics + AI algorithms transforming the entire chain

## 7. Mixue Ice Cream & Tea

### Company Profile
- **Scale**: 36,000+ locations (world's largest tea/beverage chain)
- **Digital Core**: Extreme supply chain + lightweight digital front-end

### Digital Strategy
- **Supply chain before store digitalization**: Proprietary core factories + cold chain + logistics
- **Centralized procurement platform**: Tens of thousands of stores pooling purchasing = extreme cost advantage
- **Lightweight front-end**: Franchisees only need basic POS; the core is supply chain
- **Standardized SaaS**: Unified POS + unified menu + unified marketing

### Key Metrics
- Locations: 36,000+ (world's #1 beverage chain)
- Core raw material self-supply rate: >80%
- Franchisee renewal rate: industry-leading

### Core Takeaways
1. **Supply chain digitalization > store digitalization**: Mixue's core moat is supply chain, not POS
2. **Franchisee-first design**: Systems designed with franchisee interests as the starting point
3. **Do one thing well before thinking about AI**: Not every business needs to rush into AI

## 11. DoorDash / Delivery Platform Ecosystems

- **Scale**: Serving millions of restaurants globally
- **Positioning**: From delivery marketplace to full-stack restaurant SaaS

### Digital Strategy
- **Delivery as entry point**: Naturally possesses the richest delivery data
- **Expanding to full-stack restaurant SaaS**: POS / KDS / loyalty / inventory / BI
- **AI beginning to penetrate**: Anomalous review filtering, AI site selection, AI operational recommendations

2. **Freemium model**: Basic features free to lower the barrier, premium features monetized
3. **Delivery is the gateway**: Expand from delivery into the full value chain

> **Summary: 5 Common Characteristics Across 11 Enterprises**
> 1. **Digitalization is a CEO-level topic**: Not something the IT department can drive alone
> 2. **Unify data before layering on AI**: AI on top of data silos is a castle in the air
> 3. **Start from the highest-ROI scenario**: Voice ordering, demand forecasting, intelligent scheduling
> 4. **Supply chain digitalization value is underestimated**: Mixue / Yum China / Haidilao supply chain stories prove that doing it well builds an unbreachable moat
> 5. **Organizational change is the last mile**: Technology adoption depends on people, not code
