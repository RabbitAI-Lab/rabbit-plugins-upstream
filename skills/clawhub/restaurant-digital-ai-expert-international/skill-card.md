A restaurant digital and AI transformation workbench that helps agents assess maturity, plan roadmaps, evaluate vendors, calculate ROI, and produce implementation materials for global food service businesses. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External consultants, restaurant operators, IT leads, and finance stakeholders use this skill to diagnose restaurant digital maturity, plan transformation roadmaps, prioritize AI scenarios, evaluate vendors, and draft business cases and implementation materials. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can recommend privacy-sensitive restaurant systems such as voice ordering, video monitoring, facial recognition, loyalty analytics, franchise reporting, and site-visit photo workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Add privacy, employee-notice, customer-consent, retention, access-control, data-residency, and legal-review checkpoints before using those recommendations in a real deployment. <br>
Risk: Templates and benchmark-driven recommendations may be incorrect or unsuitable for a specific restaurant format, geography, budget, or regulatory environment. <br>
Mitigation: Validate assumptions with current local data, qualified professionals, and a limited pilot before approving spend or operational changes. <br>
Risk: Broad transformation guidance can affect food safety, labor practices, customer data, and franchise operations. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review for safety, employment, privacy, and contractual impacts, especially before rollout across multiple locations or franchisees. <br>
