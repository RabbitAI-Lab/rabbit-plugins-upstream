## VIII. Pitfalls to Avoid

| # | Common Mistake | Correct Approach |
|---|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | Only counting software fees, forgetting hardware / training / maintenance | Calculate 5-year TCO (software + hardware + implementation + training + maintenance + upgrades) |
| 2 | Overly optimistic ROI assumptions ("We'll definitely save 30% on labor") | Use conservative industry benchmarks; model optimistic / baseline / conservative scenarios |
| 3 | Ignoring change management cost (efficiency actually drops at go-live) | Reserve 10-20% efficiency discount for first 1-2 months post-launch |
| 4 | Not accounting for opportunity cost ("IT staff is idle anyway") | Cost IT labor at market rate |
| 5 | Demanding a 1-year payback period | Reasonable payback for digital investment is 12-24 months (not the 1-year "quick money" standard) |

---

> **Quick Tip**: If you are just starting out, begin with POS + QR ordering only, at ~$500 - $5,000 / store / year. Do not make large investments until you can see clear ROI. Validate value with the smallest viable digital footprint first, then expand incrementally.
