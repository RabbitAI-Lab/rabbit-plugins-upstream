# Full-Chain 56-Scenario Benchmarking Checklist

> **Purpose**: Rapid self-assessment of restaurant enterprise digital maturity across 8 business chains and 56 digital scenarios.
> **How to Use**: Check current status item by item -> Mark completion level -> Calculate completion rates per chain -> Benchmark against industry levels.
> **Applicable To**: All restaurant formats (mark N/A for non-applicable scenarios).

---

## Checklist Usage Instructions

For each scenario, evaluate two dimensions:
- **Completion Level**: 0 = Not started / 1 = Basic (paper/Excel) / 2 = Digitized (has a system) / 3 = Advanced (data-driven) / 4 = World-Class (AI-driven)
- **Importance**: Star = Nice to have / StarStar = Important / StarStarStar = Critical (core competitive advantage)
