# Restaurant Digital Maturity Assessment Tool

## Assessment Instructions

- Each dimension contains 5 evaluation items, each scored 1-5
- Dimension Score = Average of 5 items
- Overall Score = Weighted average of 5 dimensions (adjust weights by restaurant format)
- Upon completion, generate a radar chart and benchmark against same-format averages
