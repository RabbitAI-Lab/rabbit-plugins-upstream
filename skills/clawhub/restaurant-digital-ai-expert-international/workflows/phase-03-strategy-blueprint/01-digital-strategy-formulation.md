# 01-Digital Strategy Formulation

## Triggers
- After diagnosis is complete, the client needs to formulate a digital strategy

## Strategy Formulation Framework

### Step 1: Vision Alignment (with Leadership)

**Core question**: "In 3 years, to what degree do you want your restaurant to be 'digital'?"

Guide the client's answer:
- Don't ask "What is your digital vision?" -- independent restaurant owners won't understand this
- Ask: "In 3 years, when you open your phone, what restaurant information do you want to see?" -> Surfaces data needs
- Ask: "In 3 years, what does your store manager least need to worry about?" -> Surfaces automation needs
- Ask: "In 3 years, how will your guests place orders?" -> Surfaces customer experience needs

### Step 2: Quantify Strategic Objectives

| Objective Dimension | Example Metrics | Current Baseline | 3-Year Target |
|---------------------|-----------------|:---:|:---:|
| Digital Coverage | % of locations on unified systems | | |
| Efficiency Improvement | Revenue per employee ($K/person/year) | | |
| Cost Reduction | Food cost percentage | | |
| Customer Growth | Loyalty members / digital order share | | |
| Management Transparency | % of locations with real-time data visibility | | |

### Step 3: Design Principles

**Must select principles appropriate to the client's stage**, not blindly apply generic principles:

| Client Stage | Recommended Design Principles |
|-------------|------------------------------|
| L1-L2 | Mobile-first, simplicity above all, incremental, SaaS-before-build |
| L2-L3 | Data integration first, API-first, unified standards, agile iteration |
| L3-L4 | AI-native, platformization, data-driven, security & compliance |
| L4-L5 | Global consistency + local intelligence, open ecosystem, continuous evolution |

### Step 4: Run/Grow/Transform Investment Allocation

| Client Stage | Run (%) | Grow (%) | Transform (%) | Description |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|------|
| L1-L2 | 80 | 15 | 5 | Nearly all investment in foundational systems |
| L2-L3 | 60 | 30 | 10 | Growth investments begin after foundation stabilizes |
| L3-L4 | 40 | 35 | 25 | Significant allocation to AI and automation |
| L4-L5 | 30 | 35 | 35 | Balanced across three, but Grow and Transform dominate |

## Deliverables
- Digital vision statement (1 sentence + 1 paragraph)
- 3-year strategic objectives (SMART format, 3-5 objectives)
- Design principles (8-10 principles)
- IT investment portfolio allocation

## Quality Checks
- [ ] Vision can be clearly stated in one sentence
- [ ] Objectives meet SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- [ ] Design principles are appropriate for the client's stage
- [ ] Investment allocation matches the client's stage
