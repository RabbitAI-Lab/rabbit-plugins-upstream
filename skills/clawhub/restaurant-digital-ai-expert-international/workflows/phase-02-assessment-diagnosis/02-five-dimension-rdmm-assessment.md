# 02-Five-Dimension R-DMM Assessment

## Triggers
- After research is complete, conduct the formal five-dimension maturity assessment

## Execution Guide

Use `tools/restaurant-digital-maturity-assessment-tool.md` for the full assessment.

### Assessment Process

```
Step 1: Technology Capability Assessment (system inventory + integration + infrastructure)
  -> Deliverable: Technology score [1-5] + scoring rationale

Step 2: Operations Capability Assessment (SOPs + scheduling + quality + inspection + energy)
  -> Deliverable: Operations score [1-5] + scoring rationale

Step 3: Data Capability Assessment (collection + quality + reporting + decision-making + governance)
  -> Deliverable: Data score [1-5] + scoring rationale

Step 4: Organization Capability Assessment (IT team + awareness + staff skills + training + culture)
  -> Deliverable: Organization score [1-5] + scoring rationale

Step 5: Customer Capability Assessment (digital channels + loyalty + private domain + personalization + feedback)
  -> Deliverable: Customer score [1-5] + scoring rationale

Step 6: Composite Rating
  -> Deliverable: R-DMM composite level L[X].[X] + radar chart

Step 7: Industry Benchmarking
  -> Deliverable: Gap analysis vs. same-format average and leading benchmarks
```

### Scoring Principles (Must Follow)
- **Evidence, not impressions**: "The owner says data is great" is not valid. "Reviewed last 3 months of POS data, completeness rate of 98%" is valid
- **Err on the side of strictness**: A slightly lower score won't hurt the client; overestimating will
- **Rather than scoring 3.5, score a 3 and note 'shows 4-level potential in certain areas'**

## Deliverables
- R-DMM five-dimension detailed assessment report (use template)
- Radar chart
- Industry benchmarking analysis
- 56-scenario benchmarking checklist (use tool)

## Quality Checks
- [ ] Each dimension score is supported by 3+ specific pieces of evidence
- [ ] Scores do not exceed what the evidence supports
- [ ] Industry benchmarking uses the correct same-format benchmark
- [ ] Gap analysis identifies "where the gap is" and "how big the gap is"
