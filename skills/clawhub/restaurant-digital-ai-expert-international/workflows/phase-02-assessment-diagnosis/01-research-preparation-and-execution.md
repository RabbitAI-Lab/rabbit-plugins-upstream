# 01-Research Preparation & Execution

## Triggers
- After project kickoff, entering the formal diagnostic phase
- OR client says "help us comprehensively assess our digital maturity"

## Research Preparation

### Step 1: Develop Research Plan (1 day)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Research Scope | [All locations / sample of XX locations / HQ only] |
| Research Duration | [Total X days] |
| Interviewees | [List] |
| Site Visit Locations | [List: must include different cities/neighborhoods/performance levels] |

### Step 2: Prepare Interview Guides

**Owner / Founder (60 minutes)**:
1. How is the business overall? What changes over the past 3 years and next 3 years?
2. Where does digital rank among your priorities? (vs. expansion / talent / product / marketing / supply chain)
3. How digitally mature do you think you are right now?
4. What do you most want to solve with digital? (Pick only Top 3)
5. How do your budget and decision-making processes work?
6. Have you implemented systems before? Did they go smoothly? What pitfalls did you encounter?

**Store Manager (30 minutes, select 1-2 per location)**:
1. What are the 3 things you spend the most time on each day?
2. Are your current systems easy to use? What frustrates you most?
3. What's the biggest headache during peak hours? (Scheduling / kitchen output / reconciliation / order follow-ups?)
4. If you could have one digital feature tomorrow, what would you want most?
5. How long does it take to train new hires on your current systems? Is it difficult?

**Head Chef / Kitchen Lead (30 minutes)**:
1. What is the current kitchen production flow? Average time from order receipt to dish completion?
2. How do you handle ingredient prep? What happens if you prep too much? Too little?
3. How do you manage ingredient shelf life? Any waste or spoilage issues?
4. Do you have any food safety systems or processes in place?
5. If you could make the kitchen more efficient, what would you change first?

**Front-of-House Staff (15 minutes, select 1-2 per location, across different age groups)**:
1. Can you operate the current POS system? How long did it take to learn?
2. Do things get chaotic during peak hours?
3. What do you think guests complain about most often?
4. If you had a "digital tool" to make your job easier, what would you want it to do?

**Franchisees (if applicable, 30 minutes, select 3-5 covering both cooperative and resistant)**:
1. Are you using the franchisor's systems? How well do they work?
2. If not using them, why? (Too difficult / distrust / not useful / too expensive)
3. What is currently most profitable at your location? Least profitable?
4. What digital support do you most need from the franchisor?
5. Do you see yourself as a "franchise partner" or do you feel "controlled by the franchisor"?

### Step 3: Document Collection Checklist

Request from the client:
- [ ] Current system inventory (POS / KDS / CRM / Inventory / Scheduling / BI / ...)
- [ ] Last 3 months of revenue / cost / traffic data (if extractable)
- [ ] Organization chart
- [ ] Existing SOPs / operations manuals
- [ ] Sample data reports currently in use
- [ ] In-store WiFi network status
- [ ] Franchisee roster (if applicable)

### Step 4: Site Visits

During site visits, observe 5 dimensions:
1. **Front of House**: Actual efficiency of POS/ordering during peak, customer queuing conditions
2. **Kitchen**: Production workflow, kitchen display method, staffing configuration
3. **Network**: WiFi signal strength (test with your phone), outage contingency plans
4. **Data**: What reports are actually being used? Posted on walls? Written in notebooks?
5. **People**: Staff attitudes and usage patterns toward systems across different ages/roles

## Deliverables
- Research plan
- Complete interview records
- Document collection checklist (received / pending)
- Site visit records (including photos)

## Common Pitfalls
- ❌ Only listening to the owner (what the owner says and what store managers say are often very different)
- ❌ Only visiting the flagship location (the best-performing location does not represent all locations)
- ❌ Only talking in meeting rooms (must visit locations and observe real operations)
- ❌ Not interviewing franchisees (the franchisee perspective is critical for chain brands)
