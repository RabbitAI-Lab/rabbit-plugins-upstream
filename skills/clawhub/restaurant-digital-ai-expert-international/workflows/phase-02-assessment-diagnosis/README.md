# 02-Assessment & Diagnosis

This phase conducts a comprehensive digital assessment of the restaurant enterprise, producing an R-DMM five-dimension evaluation and gap analysis.

## Sub-Processes

| # | Process | Deliverable | Timeline |
|---|--------|-------------|:---:|
| 01 | Research Preparation & Execution | Research plan + interview guides | 2-3 days |
| 02 | Five-Dimension R-DMM Assessment | Assessment report (with radar chart) | 3-5 days |
| 03 | Diagnostic Report Writing | Findings -> Insights -> Recommendations | 2-3 days |

## Key Principles

- **Site visits are mandatory**: Visit at least 3-5 locations (covering different neighborhoods, different performance levels)
- **Multi-role interviews**: Owner -> Store Manager -> Head Chef -> Front-of-House Staff -> Franchisee (if applicable)
- **Data triangulation**: What the client says vs. what the system data shows vs. what is observed on site -- compare all three
- **Don't just interview IT**: Digital assessment is not an IT audit. Discuss operations, business processes, and people.
