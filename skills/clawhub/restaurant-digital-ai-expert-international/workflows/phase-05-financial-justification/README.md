# 05-Financial Justification & Investment Decision

Digital transformation is not "spending" but "investing." This phase helps clients build rigorous financial justification so budget approvals are well-supported.

## Sub-Processes

| # | Process | Deliverable |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 01 | TCO Modeling | 3-5 year TCO model (including hidden costs) |
| 02 | ROI & Business Case | ROI analysis + sensitivity analysis + payback period |
| 03 | Investment Decision & Budget Approval | Investment proposal + phased budget + approval materials |

## Key Principles

- **Look at TCO over 5 years, not 1 year**: The SaaS model of "low first year + high renewal pricing" means looking only at Year 1 is a disaster
- **Hidden costs represent 30-40% of TCO**: Training, operations, integration, data migration, organizational change costs
- **ROI must be "conservative"**: Use low-end benefit estimates and high-end cost estimates. Better to conservatively over-deliver than optimistically miss.
- **Digital investment has a "counter-intuitive" return curve**: First 6-12 months: high investment, low return (foundation building). Returns only accelerate after 12 months.
