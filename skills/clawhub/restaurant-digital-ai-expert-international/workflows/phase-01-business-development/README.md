# 01-Business Development & Contracting

This phase covers the complete workflow from initial contact with restaurant clients through to contract signing.

## Sub-Processes

| # | Process | Deliverable | Duration |
|---|--------|-------------|:---:|
| 01 | Initial Client Communication | Client profile + pain point screening | 1-2 hours |
| 02 | Requirements Clarification & Problem Diagnosis | Problem diagnostic summary | 1-3 days |
| 03 | Project Proposal | Proposal (Word/PPT) | 3-5 days |
| 04 | SOW & Contract | SOW + contract technical appendix | 3-5 days |

## Key Principles

- **Restaurant clients fall into three categories**: those who understand digital and want solutions / those who don't understand but know they have problems / those who have no concept at all
- **Category 1**: Deliver solutions directly. **Category 2**: Help them clarify their problems first. **Category 3**: First raise digital awareness
- **Regardless of category**, the first conversation must establish: restaurant format, number of locations, revenue range, current systems in use, and the most painful points
