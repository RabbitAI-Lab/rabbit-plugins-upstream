# 01-Initial Client Communication

## Triggers
- Client says: "We want to understand digital transformation" / "Help us evaluate our systems" / "We want to do AI"
- OR: Any first contact with a client regarding restaurant digital transformation

## Pre-requisites
- Basic client information (if available): brand name, number of locations, restaurant format, approximate revenue, city
- Communication can proceed even without the above information

## Communication Framework (30-60 minutes)

### Part 1: Understanding the Current State (15 minutes)
```
Opening scripts (choose based on client type):

[For independent / small restaurant owners]
"Let's start with your current situation -- how many locations? What type of cuisine? How's business?
What POS are you using? Do you do delivery? How do you manage loyalty?
What's your biggest headache right now?"

[For multi-unit / chain restaurant operators]
"Let's understand your current digital foundation -- what POS is in use across locations? Are systems integrated?
Can you see your data? How willing are franchisees to adopt systems?
What are your expansion plans for the next 1-3 years?"

[For large restaurant groups / enterprises]
"Let's align on strategic direction first -- where does digital fit in the group's priorities?
What projects are currently in progress? Do you have a dedicated digital team?
How do your budget cycles and decision-making processes work?"
```

**6 Must-Ask Questions**:
1. What is your restaurant format? (Let the client describe in their own words)
2. How many locations? Corporate vs. franchise ratio?
3. What POS system are you currently using? (If any)
4. Which delivery platforms are you on? What percentage of revenue?
5. What are the three biggest problems causing you pain right now?
6. What is your expected budget range? (Don't ask for a specific number -- ask "what level of investment are you thinking?")

### Part 2: Discovering Pain Points (15 minutes)
Use the "pain-to-cost" rapid quantification method:
- "That issue you mentioned -- let me do quick math: if you spend 1 extra hour per day reconciling accounts, that's 365 hours/year = roughly 1/5 of a full-time employee. At $35,000/year salary, that's $7,000/year in wasted labor costs."
- Translate client pain points into money -- restaurant owners are most sensitive to financial impact

### Part 3: Building Trust (10 minutes)
- Share case studies from the same restaurant format (reference the examples/ directory)
- "A brand similar to yours had the same problem. After implementing X, their Y metric improved by Z% within N months."

### Part 4: Defining Next Steps (5 minutes)
- "Here's what we need to do next: 1) You provide X materials 2) We'll deliver Y"
- Clarify roles, responsibilities, and timelines

## Deliverables
1. **One-page client profile**: Format / scale / Top 3 pain points / budget level / decision chain / digital maturity level
2. **Next-step action list**: Who / What / When

## Common Mistakes
- ❌ Pushing a specific system right away (before understanding if it fits the client)
- ❌ Using too much technical jargon (independent restaurant owners won't understand "API integration" or "data platform")
- ❌ Saying "your current approach is outdated" (judgment is a trust killer)
- ✓ Speak the client's language, use numbers, use case studies

## Quality Checks
- [ ] Confirmed restaurant format, number of locations, revenue level
- [ ] Identified Top 3 pain points
- [ ] Established initial trust (client is willing to continue the conversation)
- [ ] Agreed on next steps
