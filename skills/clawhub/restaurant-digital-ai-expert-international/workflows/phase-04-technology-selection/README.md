# 04-Technology Selection & Vendor Evaluation

Based on strategic objectives and technical architecture, conduct structured technology selection and vendor evaluation to ensure "the right system, the right partner."

## Sub-Processes

| # | Process | Deliverable |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 01 | Technology Selection Process | Requirements matrix + build vs. buy decision + tech stack recommendations |
| 02 | RFP Full Process | RFP document + vendor long list -> short list |
| 03 | Vendor Evaluation & Selection | Vendor scoring matrix + selection report + final recommendation |

## Key Principles

- **Eliminate "relationship-based procurement"**: Selection is based on data, not relationships. Let the scoring matrix speak.
- **Eliminate "gold-plating"**: 10 locations don't need a microservices architecture. Choose solutions that fit the client's current and next-18-month needs.
- **Proof of Concept (PoC) first**: Critical systems must undergo PoC before decision-making. Don't rely solely on demos.
- **Vendor stability > Feature richness**: Restaurant SaaS vendor failure rate is approximately 30% annually. Choosing a vendor that "survives 3 years" is more important than choosing one with richer features.
