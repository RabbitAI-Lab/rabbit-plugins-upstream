# Case 2: A Regional Hot Pot Chain's Digital Upgrade

## Background

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Brand** | Shu-X Hot Pot (pseudonym) |
| **Type** | Sichuan hot pot / regional chain |
| **Scale** | 8 company-owned, 3 franchised |
| **Per-Location Size** | 300--500 m2 |
| **Seats per Location** | 120--180 |
| **Avg. Ticket** | $13--$17 |
| **Annual Revenue** | ~$5.6M (8 company-owned) |

## Pre-Digital State

### Scene Re-Creation
- **Chaotic waitlist**: 1--2 hour waits on weekends were normal, managed entirely by a paper notebook and manual shouting.
- **Slow ordering**: Paper menus flipped back and forth + handwritten orders + walk to POS to enter -- a single table could take 10 minutes during peak.
- **Loyalty by chance**: Stamp cards nobody remembered to bring; prepaid balance pushed verbally by waitstaff; "regulars and first-timers treated the same."
- **Kitchen run by yelling**: Expediting dishes, nagging about broth bases, adding items -- the kitchen ran entirely on shouted verbal communication.
- **Every location a different POS**: 8 locations used 3 different POS systems (historical reasons). Data aggregation was manual Excel stitching.
- **Zero franchise visibility**: Headquarters had no insight into franchise operations at all.

### R-DMM Assessment: L2.3
- Technology: 2.0 -- POS exists but not unified
- Operations: 2.0 -- Processes exist but fully people-driven
- Data: 2.0 -- Data exists but cannot be joined
- Organization: 3.0 -- Owner has awareness and willingness to spend
- Customer: 3.0 -- Basic membership exists

## Digital Solution

### Phase 1: Unified Platform + Front-of-House Efficiency (6 months, $84K investment)

| Action | Detail | Investment |
|--------|--------|:----------:|
| **Unified POS** | All 8 company-owned + 3 franchised locations migrated to Toast / Lightspeed | $42K |
| **Waitlist System** | Waitlist management + pre-ordering (scan QR while waiting to order broth first) | $11K |
| **QR Table Ordering** | Table-sticker QR codes (one code per table), reducing menu-search friction | $7K |
| **KDS Kitchen Displays** | Kitchen display screens (split by hot dishes / cold dishes / snacks / broth), replacing paper tickets | $11K |
| **Basic Loyalty** | Tiers + points + stored value (10% bonus on top-up) | Included in POS |
| **Training** | All-staff training + go-live support | $13K |

### Phase 2: Data Integration + AI Capabilities (6 months, $56K investment)

| Action | Detail | Investment |
|--------|--------|:----------:|
| **Unified BI Dashboard** | Power BI / Tableau integration -- daily data auto-aggregated across all 11 locations | $21K |
| **AI Scheduling** | 7shifts / Schedulefly integration -- auto-scheduling based on historical footfall | $11K |
| **Franchisee Dashboard** | Franchisees can see their own ranking, operational diagnostics | $17K |
| **Reputation Monitoring** | AI monitoring of Yelp + Google Reviews + TripAdvisor; auto-alert on negative reviews | $7K |

### What We Decided NOT to Do
- No AI voice ordering (hot pot ordering has inherent social dynamics; human + QR ordering is sufficient)
- No kitchen robotics (hot pot kitchen complexity is far lower than full-service dining)

## Implementation

### Key Milestones

| Time | Event | Key Decision |
|------|-------|-------------|
| M1 | Selected Lightspeed, signed contract | Owner personally participated in vendor selection; brought 2 store managers to demos |
| M2 | Piloted at the worst-performing location | Smart: pilot at the weakest store -- success is a bonus, failure has minimal downside |
| M3 | Pilot succeeded, began rollout | QR ordering share went from 0% to 65%; table turns from 2.1 to 2.5 |
| M4--M5 | All 8 company-owned locations live | Scheduled by "most cooperative manager first," building "flagship stores" |
| M6 | Franchise rollout began | First franchise attracted with "supply-chain discount" for voluntary adoption |
| M8 | AI scheduling live | Ran 3 months in "recommendation" mode (system suggests, human decides) |

### Change Management Highlights
1. **"Digital is not about headcount reduction"**: The owner stated clearly at the all-hands -- systems are here to reduce grunt work, not people.
2. **Store Manager Leaderboard**: Month 2 ran a "QR ordering adoption contest" with bonuses for the top 3.
3. **"Incentivize" franchisees**: Franchisees who voluntarily adopted the system received an extra 1% rebate on supply chain.
4. **Senior-friendly pairing**: Every older server was paired with a "digital buddy" (a younger staff member).

## Results

### Data After 12 Months

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|:------:|:-----:|:------:|
| Table Turns | 2.1 | 2.7 | **+29%** |
| QR Ordering Share | 0% | 72% | Peak-hour staffing pressure drastically reduced |
| Avg. Ticket | $13.30 | $15.70 | **+18%** (QR upsell recommendations) |
| Stored-Value Balance | $0 | $420K/year | Locked in core customer base |
| Labor Cost Ratio | 24% | 21% | **-3pp** |
| Food Cost Ratio | 38% | 36% | **-2pp** (precision inventory control) |
| Waitlist Abandonment Rate | 25% | 12% | **-52%** (waitlist system + pre-ordering) |
| Delivery Revenue | $0 | New delivery channel added | Incremental |
| Reconciliation Time | Store mgr 1 hr/day | Automatic | **-100%** |

### ROI
- Total investment: $140K
- Annualized savings + incremental revenue: ~$250K
- Payback period: ~7 months
- 3-year ROI: ~540%

## Lessons Learned

### What Went Right
1. **"Worst-store-first" principle**: Piloting at the weakest location keeps failure risk contained.
2. **Owner drove it personally**: A digital project without the owner's personal involvement is doomed.
3. **Lead with benefits for franchisees**: Don't say "you must use this"; say "this saves you money."
4. **Training budget = 30% of system budget**: Invest generously in training.

### If We Could Do It Again
1. Should have planned franchise rollout strategy earlier -- only started thinking about franchise adoption after company-owned stores were done.
2. Should have upgraded Wi-Fi proactively -- older locations had frequent network drops during peak hours.

## Universal Takeaway

> The key to hot pot digitization is three pain points: **long queues, slow ordering, scattered loyalty**. Solve these three and you have captured 80% of the ROI. As for AI, IoT -- those come after the foundation is laid.
