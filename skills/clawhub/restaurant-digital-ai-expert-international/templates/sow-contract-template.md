## 7. Responsibilities

### Client Responsibilities
- [ ] Designate a project manager (single point of contact)
- [ ] Provide necessary working environment (store WiFi / equipment / space)
- [ ] Complete data preparation on time (menu / member / supplier master data)
- [ ] Ensure relevant personnel attend training and UAT
- [ ] Drive store-level execution of the go-live plan
- [ ] Make payments on schedule

### Vendor Responsibilities
- [ ] Designate a project manager (single point of contact; no replacement during the project without Client approval)
- [ ] Deliver per contract timeline and quality standards
- [ ] Maintain core project team stability (replacements require Client approval)
- [ ] Complete training and ensure assessment pass rates
- [ ] Respond to and resolve issues per SLA
- [ ] Data security: must not disclose, leak, or sell Client data

## 8. Data & Intellectual Property

### 8.1 Data Ownership

| Clause | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Data ownership | All Client business data (members / orders / menu / stores, etc.) belongs to the Client |
| Data usage rights | Vendor may use Client data only within the scope of providing services under this contract |
| Data export | Client may export full data at any time (during the contract term + within 30 days after termination) |
| Data deletion | Within 30 days of contract termination, Vendor must delete all Client data (and provide written deletion confirmation) |
| Data protection | Data processing must comply with applicable data protection laws (e.g., GDPR where applicable; CCPA where applicable) |

### 8.2 Intellectual Property

| Clause | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Vendor standard product | IP belongs to Vendor |
| Client-specific custom development | IP belongs to Client (source code delivered) |
| Joint development | Jointly owned; both parties may use without restriction |
| Copyright framework | IP provisions are consistent with the Berne Convention and applicable local copyright law (e.g., DMCA in the United States) |

---

## 9. Confidentiality

- Both parties are bound to maintain confidentiality of the contract value, trade secrets, and technical information.
- Confidentiality period: during the contract term + 3 years after termination.
- Vendor must not use Client operational data for any purpose beyond this contract (including, without limitation, Vendor PR / case studies without prior written Client consent).
- Personal data processing must comply with GDPR (where applicable) and equivalent data protection regulations in the jurisdiction of operation.

## 13. Liability for Breach

| Breach Scenario | Consequence |
|----------------|-------------|
| Vendor fails to deliver on schedule | 0.5% of contract value deducted per week of delay, capped at 10% |
| Vendor delivery quality fails acceptance | Remediation within a fixed period; 3 failed attempts -> Client may terminate + full refund |
| Client fails to pay on schedule | 0.5% late fee per week of delay |
| Vendor data breach | Full refund + compensation for Client's actual losses + Client may terminate immediately |
| Vendor replaces core personnel without approval | 2% of contract value deducted per person replaced |
