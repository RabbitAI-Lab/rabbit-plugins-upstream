# AI Scenario Priority Scorecard

## Usage Instructions

For each candidate AI scenario, score across 5 dimensions (1--5). Weighted total determines priority ranking.

## Dimension Scoring Criteria

### R - Reach (1--5)

| Score | Criterion |
|:-----:|-----------|
| 1 | Affects only a few roles / occasional use |
| 2 | Affects 1--2 locations or departments |
| 3 | Affects 30--60% of locations |
| 4 | Affects 60--90% of locations |
| 5 | Affects all locations / customers / staff |

### I - Impact (1--5)

| Score | Criterion |
|:-----:|-----------|
| 1 | No material financial impact |
| 2 | Annual benefit <$7 K or <1% cost savings |
| 3 | Annual benefit $7--$42 K or 1--5% improvement |
| 4 | Annual benefit $42--$140 K or 5--15% improvement |
| 5 | Annual benefit >$140 K or >15% improvement |

### C - Confidence (1--5)

| Score | Criterion |
|:-----:|-----------|
| 1 | Technology immature; no success cases |
| 2 | Few cases; industry uncertainty |
| 3 | Multiple industry success cases |
| 4 | Technology mature; data ready; team capable |
| 5 | SaaS product available for immediate purchase |

### E - Effort (1--5, lower is better)

| Score | Criterion |
|:-----:|-----------|
| 1 | <1 month, <$7 K, zero resistance |
| 2 | 1--3 months, $7--$28 K |
| 3 | 3--6 months, $28--$70 K |
| 4 | 6--12 months, $70--$280 K |
| 5 | >12 months, >$280 K, high change resistance |

### +AI Fit (1--5)

| Score | Criterion |
|:-----:|-----------|
| 1 | Rule engine / human is better |
| 2 | AI helps but is not the strongest approach |
| 3 | AI is one of the optimal approaches |
| 4 | AI is clearly the best approach |
| 5 | Only AI can solve this; traditional approaches are unviable |

## Priority Recommendations

| Total Score | Priority | Action |
|:-----------:|:--------:|--------|
| >4.0 | **P0 -- Start Now** | Initiate this week; MVP next month |
| 3.5--4.0 | **P1 -- Near-Term** | Launch within 1--3 months |
| 3.0--3.5 | **P2 -- Medium-Term** | Evaluate within 3--12 months |
| <3.0 | **P3 -- Watch / Drop** | Reassess periodically |
