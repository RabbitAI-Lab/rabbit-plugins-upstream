# Quick Checklist

Remove or rewrite unless clearly needed:

- inflated transformation language
- abstract noun piles without an actor
- unnecessary “not X; Y” contrasts
- fake revelation openings
- stacked punchy fragments
- repeated three-part lists
- grand metaphors for ordinary claims
- unsupported authority or importance claims
- recap or mic-drop endings
- decorative formatting
- em-dash clusters

Pass only when:

- the real claim remains intact;
- facts and uncertainty are preserved;
- the writer's voice survives;
- the reader can identify any needed decision or action; and
- the edit is no larger than the actual problem.
