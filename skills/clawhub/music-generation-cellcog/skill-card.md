## Description: <br>
AI music generation powered by CellCog for original instrumental and vocal tracks from 5 seconds to 10 minutes, including cinematic scores, background tracks, podcast intros, game soundtracks, ambient soundscapes, jingles, lo-fi beats, orchestral compositions, and songs with lyrics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and external users use this skill to guide agents in requesting royalty-free music from CellCog, including instrumental tracks, vocal songs, background music, jingles, podcast stings, and game soundtracks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts are sent to CellCog's external service and may include sensitive creative, business, personal, or regulated content. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid submitting secrets, private lyrics, unreleased business material, personal data, or regulated content unless CellCog's terms and retention practices have been reviewed and approved for the use case. <br>
Risk: The skill requires a CellCog API key. <br>
Mitigation: Store CELLCOG_API_KEY in the agent environment or approved secret storage, and do not paste API keys into prompts, generated files, or public logs. <br>
