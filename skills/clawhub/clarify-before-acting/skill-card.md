## Description:

Before acting on complex, ambiguous, or high-cost requests, resolve direction-changing unknowns by checking available context and asking focused questions one at a time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yumingtao](https://clawhub.ai/user/yumingtao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users use this skill to decide when a complex or high-cost request needs clarification, resolve direction-changing unknowns, and hand off execution with a concise task contract.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Over-clarification can slow routine work when the skill is applied to clear, low-risk requests.

Mitigation: Use the skill for complex, ambiguous, or high-cost requests; proceed directly with trivial, mechanical, reversible, or already clear tasks.
