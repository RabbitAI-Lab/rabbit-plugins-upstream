## Description:

Helps agents resolve direction-changing ambiguity in complex or high-cost requests before acting, while letting clear, low-risk tasks proceed directly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yumingtao](https://clawhub.ai/user/yumingtao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to decide when a request needs clarification, resolve the highest-impact unknowns, and proceed with explicit assumptions when only reversible details remain.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Missing a direction-changing unknown or relying on an incorrect assumption can send the work in the wrong direction and cause rework.

Mitigation: Do preliminary research and check prior answers, resolve the highest-impact ambiguity before acting, and state assumptions and their consequences when proceeding.
