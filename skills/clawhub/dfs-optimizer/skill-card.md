## Description:

Provides example Python scripts for evaluating Daily Fantasy Sports lineups using manually supplied projections and salary-cap constraints.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Publisher:

[ianalloway](https://clawhub.ai/user/ianalloway)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Daily Fantasy Sports players can use the examples to explore salary-cap lineups and simple stacking or ownership calculations from their own projections and salary data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The examples can produce incomplete lineups despite claiming optimal results, and FanDuel and MLB lineup builders are absent.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as illustrative; check lineup completeness and independently verify current platform rules, salaries, and projections before using results in paid contests.
