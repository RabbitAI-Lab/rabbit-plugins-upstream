## Description:

Run a customer help desk through email tickets, queues, saved replies, and reports in Conecto.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[conecto](https://clawhub.ai/user/conecto)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Small businesses and their support teams use this skill to manage customer email tickets, prioritize overdue work, reply in threads, save common responses, and review support reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Support tickets and replies are handled through Conecto, an external service.

Mitigation: Connect only if the business trusts the service, grant only the Help Desk and Email access it needs, and use the revocable account key described in the setup instructions.

Risk: Creating inboxes, replying, and changing tickets can incur service charges, including a recurring inbox fee.

Mitigation: Review the documented prices and ask the account owner before creating an inbox or taking other cost-incurring actions.
