## Description:

Analyze AI search visibility, competitors, mentions, citations, and Technical Audit evidence for a Videntic Project using its authenticated MCP tools. Use when the user asks about their Videntic analytics or wants evidence-based recommendations from their tracked AI responses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams with a Videntic Project use the skill to review AI-search visibility, competitors, mentions, citations, Technical Audit findings, and related opportunities, then receive evidence-based recommendations. Supported changes are made only when requested and authorized.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requested changes may consume analysis or draft allowances or affect future measurements.

Mitigation: Use read-only access for analytics questions; explain material effects and obtain any missing consent before making a requested change.
