## Description:

审查短剧项目中的原著分析、故事、剧本、视觉设定、图片提示词、分镜、冻结关键帧、视频提示词和已有媒体。用户提出“审稿/检查剧本”“检查资产或连续性”“检查图片/视频提示词”“检查原著分析”“审查模板感”“根据生产观察做项目校准”时使用；只写审查问题、结论和修订要求，不代替负责人修改来源文件。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

短剧创作者和制作团队可用此技能审查原著分析、故事、剧本、视觉资产、提示词、分镜及生成媒体。它提供带证据的问题、结论和修订要求，供负责创作或制作的人员处理。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review findings or conclusions could introduce incorrect or misleading guidance.

Mitigation: Check each finding against its cited source evidence and treat the verdict as review guidance for the responsible creator or production lead.

Risk: The skill's approval and language choices may not fit every project's needs.

Mitigation: Confirm that the review process and language are appropriate for the target project before relying on its conclusions.
