## Description: <br>
基于全年目标、月报、年中报告、项目记录和业务结果，复盘年度目标达成、关键成果、业务结构、能力成长、资源配置、重大问题和下一年度计划，并生成适合年度述职、个人复盘或管理汇报的结构化年报。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, and reporting contributors use this skill to turn annual goals, monthly reports, project records, business outcomes, and next-year priorities into a structured annual review or management report. It also helps check existing annual report drafts for missing evidence, weak attribution, unsupported metrics, and incomplete planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Annual review inputs may include sensitive business records, customer information, project results, or performance data. <br>
Mitigation: Use approved internal reporting data only, limit unnecessary sensitive details, and review generated reports before sharing. <br>
Risk: The skill references shared reporting definitions that can affect status, priority, result, and metric interpretation. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the shared reporting definitions are intended for the current workspace before relying on the analysis. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or unreliable metric inputs can lead to misleading completion rates, trends, rankings, or forecasts. <br>
Mitigation: Require clear metric periods, definitions, and source data; when evidence is insufficient, keep conclusions qualitative and mark data gaps. <br>
