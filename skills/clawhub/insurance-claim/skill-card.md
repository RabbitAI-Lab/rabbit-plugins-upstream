## Description: <br>
Writes structured insurance claim letters or denial appeals that document policy details, incident facts, itemized losses, claimed amounts, evidence, and appeal rebuttals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, claimants, and assistants use this skill to draft complete insurance claim letters or appeals with policy details, incident facts, itemized losses, evidence lists, and deadline reminders. It is a drafting aid and does not provide legal, financial, or insurance advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The server security summary flags the upload as broader than a tightly scoped skill package. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when the publisher is trusted and the full release scope is intended; prefer a narrowly packaged release containing only the relevant skill files for normal use. <br>
Risk: A generated claim or appeal could be mistaken for legal, financial, or insurance advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as a drafting aid, verify policy terms and deadlines, and consult a qualified advisor for complex or high-value claims. <br>
Risk: Inflated, invented, or unsupported losses could mislead an insurer and create fraud exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Require factual incident details, itemized values, and referenced evidence; keep missing details as placeholders instead of inventing them. <br>
