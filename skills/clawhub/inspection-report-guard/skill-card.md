## Description:

检测报告合规护栏用于在产品质量检测报告、委托检验报告、监督抽查报告发布前，检查其是否符合《产品质量法》《检验检测机构资质认定管理办法》及 RB/T 214 等规范要求，并给出拦截和修改建议。

## Use Case:

Compliance, quality, and inspection-report teams use this skill to review Chinese product inspection and testing report text before publication. It returns a structured compliance review with pass/fail conclusions, non-compliance details, warnings, and suggested revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce compliance guidance that may be incomplete or outdated for current Chinese regulations, standards, or CMA requirements.

Mitigation: Verify cited Chinese regulations, standards, and CMA requirements against current authoritative sources before relying on the review for publication decisions.

Risk: Users may treat the review as legal certification rather than a compliance drafting aid.

Mitigation: Use the output as a pre-publication review aid and route final publication decisions through qualified compliance or legal reviewers.
