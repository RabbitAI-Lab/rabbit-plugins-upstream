## Description:

检测报告合规护栏。在产品质量检测报告、委托检验报告、监督抽查报告发布前，实时检测是否符合《产品质量法》《检验检测机构资质认定管理办法》及RB/T 214等规范要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Quality, compliance, and inspection-report reviewers use this skill to check product quality inspection reports, commissioned inspection reports, supervision sampling reports, type-test reports, factory inspection reports, third-party testing reports, and related external promotional materials before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can support compliance review, but it is not legal certification.

Mitigation: Use its findings as a checklist and have qualified personnel verify report decisions against current laws, regulations, standards, and organizational procedures.

Risk: Inspection reports may contain confidential customer, product, or laboratory data.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an environment approved for that data and avoid submitting sensitive report content to unapproved systems.
