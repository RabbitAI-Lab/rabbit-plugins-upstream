## Description:

Analyzes aquarium camera images and videos for visible surface gasping and abnormal breathing behaviors, then returns a visual risk warning and suggested next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Aquarium owners, aquarium staff, and aquaculture operators use it to review fish camera footage for visible distress behaviors and receive a warning with suggested follow-up actions. The result is a visual risk warning, not a disease diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
