## Description:

Analyzes aquarium camera images or video for fish gasping and abnormal breathing, then returns a hypoxia or ammonia-risk warning with suggested next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Aquarium keepers, aquarium staff, and aquaculture operators use the skill to review camera footage for signs of fish distress and decide when to check water quality or aeration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
