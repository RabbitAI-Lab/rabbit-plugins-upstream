## Description:

Provides read-only crypto news, sentiment analysis, and macroeconomic calendar research through OKX.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[okx-retail](https://clawhub.ai/user/okx-retail)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching crypto markets can use this skill to gather OKX news, review coin sentiment and trends, and check macroeconomic events. It supports news briefings and sentiment analysis, not trading or account management.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires live-profile API credentials and may use external web searches when OKX results are sparse.

Mitigation: Use read-only credentials configured locally, never share secrets in chat, and avoid confidential search topics.
