# Billing Privacy Policy

Use the least data, the smallest scope, the shortest retention, and local-only handling.

- Never run `ctyun-cli configure`, request AccessKey or SecretKey, pass real keys on the command line, or enable `--log`.
- Account, user, contract, order, transaction, resource IDs, and real resource names are protected. Show only the smallest masked suffix or summary needed to answer the question.
- Do not paste raw JSON, complete bill rows, complete identifiers, or long command transcripts into chat.
- Do not store real bills in tests, Skill files, source control, persistent storage, or cross-session caches; do not upload, email, or externally share them.
- If the environment cannot keep disclosure to this minimum, stop and explain the limit.
