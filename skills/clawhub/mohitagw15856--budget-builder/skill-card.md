## Description: <br>
Build a realistic personal monthly budget from user-provided income, expenses, goals, and obligations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
People use this skill with an agent to turn take-home income, expenses, goals, and obligations into a realistic monthly budget with a surplus or shortfall and concrete next moves. It is educational planning guidance, not regulated financial advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill works from personal income, expense, goal, and obligation details that may be sensitive. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the budget numbers needed for planning and avoid account credentials, banking logins, account numbers, or other secrets. <br>
Risk: A generated budget could be mistaken for professional financial advice or followed without checking the assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as educational planning guidance and review the numbers, assumptions, and tradeoffs before acting on it. <br>
