## Description:

Find federal contract opportunities and build a cited research brief using GovGazette when the user asks about U.S. government notices, awards or vendors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users researching U.S. federal notices, contract awards, vendors, and classification references can use this skill to find records and prepare a cited research brief. It helps them identify relevant opportunities and next steps while preserving source dates and exact identifiers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and filters are sent to GovGazette and could expose confidential bid information.

Mitigation: Avoid including confidential bid material in queries and review GovGazette’s privacy policy.

Risk: Contract deadlines and requirements can be misread or change, and an active source status does not prove a response window is open.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines and requirements against the cited official sources and their source dates.
