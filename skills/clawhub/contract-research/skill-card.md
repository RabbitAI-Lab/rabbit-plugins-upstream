## Description:

Find federal contract opportunities and build a cited research brief using GovGazette when the user asks about U.S. government notices, awards or vendors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mooshee](https://clawhub.ai/user/mooshee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and contract researchers use the skill to find U.S. federal opportunities, awards, or vendors and prepare a cited, evidence-based research brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms are sent to GovGazette and may expose confidential bid material.

Mitigation: Use only non-confidential search terms and review the GovGazette privacy policy: https://govgazette.com/privacy.

Risk: A record's status may not establish that its response window is open, and deadlines can be date-sensitive.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines against cited official sources and include source dates.
