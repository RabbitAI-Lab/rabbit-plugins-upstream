## Description:

When behavior is emergent across components—fixes elsewhere break, loops/delays dominate—map boundary, stocks/flows, feedback, archetypes, then rank leverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and operational decision makers use this skill to analyze recurring or emergent behavior across components, map stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, and archetypes, and choose interventions by leverage and side effects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Systems mapping can be over-applied to a local, linear bug or a single fixed bottleneck.

Mitigation: Use the skill's falsification checks to switch to local debugging or theory-of-constraints when one component or one fixed stage explains the issue.

Risk: The analysis can force a recurring archetype or elevate a low-leverage parameter tweak without enough evidence.

Mitigation: Require recurrence evidence before naming an archetype, and rank feasible interventions by goals, rules, loops, structure, then parameters.
