## Description:

Locates work in the knowledge-creation value loop, assesses adoption progress, plans advancement routes, and builds domain verifiers and question assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to diagnose where their knowledge work creates value, plan a path toward higher-value work, build lightweight validation checklists, develop question assets, and integrate AI-generated knowledge.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A database-ingestion checklist step could store user material.

Mitigation: Before using that step, confirm what material will be ingested and where it will be stored.
