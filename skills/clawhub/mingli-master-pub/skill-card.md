## Use Case:

People seeking Chinese traditional fortune-telling guidance can describe a question and receive an interpretation using a suitable divination or astrology method.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fortune-telling guidance may be mistaken for reliable advice on major life, financial, medical, legal, or relationship decisions.

Mitigation: Treat interpretations as cultural guidance, not the sole basis for consequential decisions; seek qualified advice where appropriate.

Risk: Broad questions may be interpreted through a fortune-telling lens when that is not the user's intent.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the user wants a traditional divination interpretation before applying one to an ambiguous request.
