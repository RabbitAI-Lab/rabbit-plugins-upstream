## Description: <br>
Sanity-check a greenhouse gas inventory before it goes into a report or gets audited, with review of scope 1/2/3 numbers, year-over-year changes, data quality, emission-factor sensitivity, and fix-before-publishing issues. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Sustainability, finance, audit, and compliance teams use this skill to sanity-check greenhouse gas inventories before reporting, assurance, or public disclosure. It helps identify boundary gaps, weak data, sensitive emission factors, double counting, and year-over-year changes that need explanation or correction. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill reviews greenhouse gas inventory rigor but does not certify compliance or replace professional assurance. <br>
Mitigation: Verify boundary, methodology, disclosure choices, and applicable standards or regulations with the organization's compliance team and assurer before relying on the output. <br>
Risk: Inventory, boundary, factor, and business data may be sensitive in the agent environment where the skill is used. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide data that is appropriate for that environment, and remove or aggregate confidential details when a full dataset is not required. <br>
