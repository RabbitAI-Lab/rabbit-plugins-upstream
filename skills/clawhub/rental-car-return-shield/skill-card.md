## Description:

Helps renters document vehicle condition, compare refueling options, complete a documented return, and prepare evidence for disputed charges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Renters use this skill to document pickup and return conditions, compare fuel costs, and organize evidence and a draft letter when disputing rental charges.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A draft dispute letter or deadline may not fit the renter's facts, agreement, or local rules.

Mitigation: Check all claims, dates, local rules, and contract terms before sending; treat the output as consumer information, not legal or insurance advice.
