## Description:

Task Decomposition turns a goal or requirement into a work breakdown structure with phases, tasks, steps, dependencies, effort estimates, and a recommended execution order.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, agents, and project teams use this skill to break high-level goals into actionable WBS plans before implementation, delegation, scheduling, or delivery review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:
