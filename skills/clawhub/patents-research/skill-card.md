## Description:

Searches Google Patents and USPTO patent records through Crawlora for prior art, patent details, citations, classifications, and portfolio research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, inventors, and patent professionals use this skill to find prior art, inspect patent claims and citations, survey inventor or assignee portfolios, and support freedom-to-operate research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent searches, names, identifiers, and related query parameters are sent to Crawlora.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential invention concepts, unreleased R&D, and sensitive freedom-to-operate queries unless Crawlora's data handling terms meet your needs.
