## Description: <br>
Reviews electrical schematics from EPLAN and similar CAD tools against standardized checklists and helps produce structured review reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Electrical designers, reviewers, and engineering teams use this skill to inspect schematic PDFs, review safety, main power, control, PLC/IO, servo, and communication circuits, and summarize findings before design release or modification review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Engineering drawings and review records may contain sensitive project details. <br>
Mitigation: Process files locally, restrict input and output paths, and share generated reports only with authorized project teams. <br>
Risk: Checklist-based schematic review may miss issues or assign an incorrect severity. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified electrical reviewer validate findings before design release, safety sign-off, or compliance decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill may write review results into user-provided Excel files or generated PDFs. <br>
Mitigation: Keep backups of source templates and review changed report files before distribution. <br>
