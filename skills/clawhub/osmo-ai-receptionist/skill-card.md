## Description:

AI receptionist and AI phone receptionist for your phone number. Use when the user wants an AI phone receptionist or answering service that picks up their calls, screens spam, takes messages and booking requests, and tells them who called and why.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getosmo](https://clawhub.ai/user/getosmo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to manage an AI receptionist for their existing phone number: review call summaries and booking requests, update greetings, and manage caller rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The hosted receptionist service may process call details and booking information.

Mitigation: Before connecting, review OsmO's terms and privacy information and confirm that sharing call or booking details with the service is appropriate.
