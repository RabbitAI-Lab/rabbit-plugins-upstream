## Description:

OsmO answers calls to the user's phone number, screens spam, captures messages and booking requests, and lets the user manage the receptionist from an AI app.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getosmo](https://clawhub.ai/user/getosmo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Phone owners and small businesses use this skill to review inbound calls, manage receptionist settings and caller rules, and check bookings through their AI app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting the receptionist involves sharing call information with the external OsmO service.

Mitigation: Before connecting, review OsmO's sign-in and data handling practices and decide whether to connect the service.
