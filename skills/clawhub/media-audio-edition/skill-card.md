## Description:

Turns a blog post or newsletter into a podcast episode, with user-approved scripts and voices, show notes, a transcript, social copy, and an optional captioned clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scrollport](https://clawhub.ai/user/scrollport)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Blog and newsletter publishers use this skill to turn a post into a podcast episode and companion transcript, show notes, LinkedIn copy, and an optional clip. The user approves the outline, script, voices, and maximum cost before audio is generated.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
