## Description:

Turn a blog post or newsletter into a podcast episode people can follow by ear. Two AI hosts talk the post through, with a hook, a roadmap, the post's main points with an example each and a recap, in voices the user picks, plus 5-second intro and outro music, a transcript, show notes, a LinkedIn post and an optional 45-second captioned clip. One host, a 2-minute brief or a straight reading are options. Use when someone wants a podcast or audio version of a post or newsletter. Nothing is spent until they approve the outline, script, voices and maximum cost in one message.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Episode scripts are sent to third-party speech services, and disclosure may be unclear.

Mitigation: Review the service use before approving generation, avoid sending confidential material, and approve the outline, script, voices and maximum cost first.

Risk: Reruns may silently replace existing episode or clip audio files.

Mitigation: Check the output folder for existing audio before rerunning, and preserve any files that must not be replaced.
