## Description: <br>
Helps bidders generate a printable clause-level bid rejection risk report from province-scoped historical rejection cases and optional current procurement documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Bidders and procurement teams use this skill before submission to compare a single province, industry, and procurement method against historical rejection cases, locate matching clauses in current documents, and prepare a pre-bid checklist. It distinguishes compliance rejection risks from competitive elimination so users do not treat scoring outcomes as avoidable compliance failures. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may submit sensitive bid materials or procurement documents while using the risk-check workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Redact confidential company, contact, pricing, and bid-document details unless the user has permission to share them. <br>
Risk: Historical rejection cases can be mistaken for definitive predictions about a future bid. <br>
Mitigation: Treat results as decision support only and verify findings against the active procurement document and applicable review authority. <br>
