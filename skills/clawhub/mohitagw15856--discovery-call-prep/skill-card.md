## Description:

Prepare a structured discovery call plan for any prospect. Use when asked to prepare for a sales call, discovery call, prospect meeting, or first call with a potential customer. Produces a call brief with research, hypotheses, questions, and success criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

## Use Case:

Sales teams, founders, and customer-facing employees use this skill to prepare discovery call briefs before prospect meetings. It organizes company and contact research, call hypotheses, agenda, discovery questions, success criteria, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Discovery call briefs may include sensitive prospect details or internal sales context.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive prospect or internal sales information unless it is appropriate for the agent session.
