## Description:

Build a qualified US B2B prospect list from public business sources - define your ICP, research target companies, score and deduplicate accounts, and draft personalized multi-touch outreach that a human reviews and sends. Consent-based research, CAN-SPAM and TCPA aware, no spam and no scraped personal data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales and business-development teams use the skill to define an ideal customer profile, research and rank US business accounts using public sources, and prepare personalized outreach drafts for human review and manual sending.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prospect research or optional enrichment may rely on inaccurate, unauthorized, or inappropriately used business data.

Mitigation: Use public business sources and authorized providers; verify records and contact details before use.

Risk: Outreach drafts may contain inaccurate claims or fail to reflect opt-outs and applicable requirements.

Mitigation: Have a person verify each draft and its claims, check opt-outs, and send manually. Treat compliance-check results as text-pattern checks, not proof of legal compliance or working unsubscribe mechanisms.
