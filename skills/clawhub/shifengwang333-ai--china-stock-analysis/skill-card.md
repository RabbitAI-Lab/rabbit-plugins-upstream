## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Financial analysis outputs may be mistaken for professional investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational, preserve risk disclaimers, and require users to verify conclusions with qualified financial judgment before making investment decisions. <br>
Risk: The workflow may prompt package installation and network-based public market data access. <br>
Mitigation: Review installation commands, referenced scripts, and generated outputs before execution or reliance. <br>
Risk: Public market or financial data may be stale, incomplete, rate-limited, or unavailable. <br>
Mitigation: Check data freshness and completeness, retry failed data pulls when appropriate, and clearly state analysis limits in reports. <br>
