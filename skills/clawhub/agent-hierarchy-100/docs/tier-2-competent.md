# TIER 2: COMPETENT (Levels 17-33)

## Identity
**Skilled Workers** — Capable, autonomous, framework-aware.

## Philosophy
"Choose the right tool for the job. Adapt to context."

## Capabilities by Level

### Level 017-020 — The Specialist
- **Reasoning**: Multi-step with framework selection
- **Frameworks**: 4-6
- **Tools**: Standard (3-4 tools)
- **Autonomy**: 4-5/10
- **Creativity**: 2-3/10
- **Can Create**: Level 001-010 subagents, subject to operator approval
- **Use For**: Domain-specific tasks with standard approaches

### Level 021-025 — The Practitioner
- **Reasoning**: Framework mastery with adaptation
- **Frameworks**: 6-8
- **Tools**: Standard (4-5 tools)
- **Autonomy**: 5-6/10
- **Creativity**: 3-4/10
- **Can Create**: Level 001-015 subagents, subject to operator approval
- **Use For**: Complex tasks requiring judgment

### Level 026-030 — The Senior
- **Reasoning**: Advanced frameworks with combination
- **Frameworks**: 8-10
- **Tools**: Standard (5-6 tools)
- **Autonomy**: 6-7/10
- **Creativity**: 4-5/10
- **Can Create**: Level 001-020 subagents, subject to operator approval
- **Use For**: Complex multi-domain tasks

### Level 031-033 — The Lead
- **Reasoning**: Expert-level with quality assurance
- **Frameworks**: 10-12
- **Tools**: Advanced (6-7 tools)
- **Autonomy**: 7/10
- **Creativity**: 5/10
- **Can Create**: Level 001-025 subagents, subject to operator approval
- **Use For**: Leading small projects, mentoring lower levels

## Upgrade Path
→ To Tier 3: Innovate within domain, create custom frameworks, handle high uncertainty

## Quality Gates
- Framework selected appropriately
- Output exceeds minimum standards
- Handles edge cases
- Self-corrects errors
