## Description:

A Chinese-language math coach that helps middle and high school students solve individual problems through guided questions, graduated hints, and similar practice problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in Chinese-language middle and high school math use the coach to work through a stuck or incorrect solution, practice with similar questions, or review before an exam. Educators and guardians should review suitability for minors before deployment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; other regions require localized privacy, guardian-consent, and crisis-support procedures.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student profile use, cross-skill sharing, or reminders may involve minors' personal information.

Mitigation: Require explicit consent for each optional action and review local privacy and guardian-consent requirements before use with minors.

Risk: Crisis-support contacts and procedures supplied for mainland China may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize crisis-support guidance before deployment outside mainland China and direct students to trusted adults and appropriate local emergency services.
