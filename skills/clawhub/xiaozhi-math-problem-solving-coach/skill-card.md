## Description:

A Chinese-language math coach that guides middle- and high-school students through individual problems with questions, graduated hints, worked examples, and practice problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and educators use this skill to work through Chinese middle- and high-school math problems, review mistakes, and practice related questions without immediately receiving a complete solution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (default; localize crisis referrals before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional sharing, long-term profiles, or reminders could retain or expose student learning information.

Mitigation: Keep these features off unless the student or guardian intentionally enables them.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contact information may be inappropriate for students in other locations.

Mitigation: Confirm the student's region and use localized emergency and support channels before deploying elsewhere.
