## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected reference files are sent to dLazy, and use may consume service credits.

Mitigation: Send only content you intend to share, choose the intended project, and confirm you are comfortable with the credit cost.

Risk: Saved API keys can grant access to the user's dLazy organization.

Mitigation: Prefer device-code login over sharing a key in chat, and revoke unused organization keys.
