## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are shared with dLazy, and attached files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Share only material you're authorized to send to the service; avoid uploading confidential or sensitive files unless approved.

Risk: Requests may consume paid organization credits.

Mitigation: Select the intended organization project and check credit availability before starting logo work.

Risk: Login saves an organization API key in the local CLI configuration.

Mitigation: Use a trusted account and device, and rotate or revoke the key from the organization dashboard if it is exposed.
