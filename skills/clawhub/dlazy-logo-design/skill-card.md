## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Files attached to a request are sent to dLazy.

Mitigation: Attach only files you intend to share with the service.

Risk: Logging in saves an organization API key on the local device.

Mitigation: Log in on a trusted device and rotate or revoke the key if it may have been exposed.

Risk: The installed CLI executable and hosted agent were not audited.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI before installation.

Risk: Requests may consume service credits.

Mitigation: Check organization credit usage when planning repeated generations.
