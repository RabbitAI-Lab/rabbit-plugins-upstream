## Description:

Analyze collected competitor ads by classifying hooks, clustering themes, using run length as a heuristic, and drafting counter-briefs and a never-say list.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hermoso-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/hermoso-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing strategists and creative teams use this skill to review a competitor's supplied ads, identify recurring approaches and possible gaps, and develop differentiated campaign briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ad run length can be mistaken for verified proof of profitability.

Mitigation: Treat run length as a heuristic and verify performance claims independently.

Risk: A teardown may imply details that were not visible in the supplied sample.

Mitigation: Use public or authorized ad samples, state when the review is copy-only, and review the resulting briefs.
