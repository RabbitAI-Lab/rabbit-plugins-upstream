## Description:

Analyzes writing style from user-provided text, documents, or images across wording, sentence structure, organization, and tone, then can draft new content following those style traits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[awwsunny](https://clawhub.ai/user/awwsunny)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to analyze the writing style of provided text, documents, or images and, when requested, draft new content that follows the identified style. It is suited to style summarization, tone analysis, and original style-guided drafting where the user has rights to the source material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style imitation could be used to impersonate a person or organization deceptively.

Mitigation: Use the skill for legitimate style adaptation only, avoid presenting generated text as authored by someone else, and label assisted drafting when appropriate.

Risk: Analyzing or imitating copyrighted source material could lead to excessive reproduction of protected expression.

Mitigation: Use only content the user has rights to analyze, summarize abstract style traits, and avoid long verbatim excerpts or requests to copy the original text.

Risk: Short, unclear, or partially unreadable source samples can produce overconfident style conclusions.

Mitigation: State when the sample is limited or image text is unclear, request clearer material when needed, and frame conclusions as tentative for small samples.
