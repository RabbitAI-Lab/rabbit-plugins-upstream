# QSR Weekly P&L Storyteller

Turns weekly restaurant KPIs into a plain-English financial narrative — what happened, why it matters, and what to do about it. Built for restaurant and franchise operators who need actionable financial insight, not just another spreadsheet.

## What it does

QSR Weekly P&L Storyteller converts weekly operating numbers into a structured business narrative an operator can actually use. Instead of staring at KPIs and trying to decide what matters, the skill explains the story behind the numbers and identifies the single most important focus for the next week.

It is designed to help operators:
- understand weekly financial performance quickly
- identify whether misses were caused by traffic, ticket, labor, or food cost
- spot week-over-week trends
- produce a report that can be shared with leadership
- connect financial outcomes back to operational issues

## Core workflow

Each week, the operator provides key numbers such as:
- total sales
- labor %
- food cost %
- customer count
- average ticket
- catering revenue
- waste
- overtime hours

The skill then produces:
- a summary of what happened
- a plain-English explanation of why it matters
- one clear focus item for the coming week
- trend analysis over time
- optional monthly rollups and year-over-year comparisons

## Why it matters

Most operators can read a spreadsheet. Far fewer can turn that spreadsheet into a clear operational decision.

This skill closes that gap by turning raw KPIs into a useful management narrative that can be read, acted on, or forwarded as-is.
