## Description: <br>
Turns weekly restaurant KPIs into a plain-English financial narrative about what happened, why it matters, and what to do next. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Restaurant and franchise operators use this skill to convert weekly sales, labor, food cost, customer count, catering, waste, and overtime metrics into a plain-English P&L narrative, trend view, and one focus item for the next week. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks the agent to retain weekly restaurant performance reports for trend comparisons, which may include sensitive sales, labor, food cost, waste, overtime, and catering figures. <br>
Mitigation: Install only where retaining those metrics is acceptable, and review or clear stored reports when the figures should not persist. <br>
Risk: Financial narratives can be misleading when user-provided KPI inputs are incomplete, stale, or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the source numbers before sharing the report or acting on the weekly focus item. <br>
