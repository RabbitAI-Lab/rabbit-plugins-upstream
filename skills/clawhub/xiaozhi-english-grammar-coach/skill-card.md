## Description:

通过循序追问帮助初高中学生自行发现英语语法错误，并在获得同意后追踪语法弱项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初高中学生可用它练习时态、主谓一致和从句等语法，通过提示自行纠错，并在自愿授权后查看弱项与进步报告。小学阶段需有家长或老师陪同。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Children's learning records or parent-facing summaries may expose personal information without informed consent.

Mitigation: Enable long-term records, cross-skill sharing, parent sharing, and reminders only after the student or guardian knowingly opts in.

Risk: Curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent expectations may not apply outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Use in the intended Chinese-language Mainland China K12 context or localize these elements before deployment elsewhere.

Risk: A student may disclose self-harm, abuse, or other immediate safety concerns during tutoring.

Mitigation: Pause tutoring, follow the supplied crisis-referral protocol, and direct the student to a trusted adult and region-appropriate emergency support.
