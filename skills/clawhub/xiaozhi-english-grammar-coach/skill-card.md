## Description:

通过循序追问帮助中文学习者发现英语语法错误、练习薄弱知识点，并在获得同意后追踪语法弱项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use guided questions to identify and practice grammar errors, including high school required and elective grammar topics. With consent, the skill can track recurring weaknesses and share learning summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-data consent outside Mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records, cross-skill sharing, reminders, or parent summaries may expose minors' data without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm the platform enforces separate consent controls before storing profiles, sharing records, sending reminders, or generating parent summaries.

Risk: Mainland China-focused curriculum and emergency guidance may be unsuitable elsewhere.

Mitigation: Adapt curriculum assumptions, emergency contacts, language, and minor-data consent rules to the deployment location.
