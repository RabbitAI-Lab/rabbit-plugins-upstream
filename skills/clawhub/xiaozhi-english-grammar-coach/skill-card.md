## Description:

以苏格拉底式追问帮助初高中学生理解并修正英语语法错误，并在获得同意后记录语法薄弱项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初高中英语学习者，用于检查句子语法、练习重点语法规则并识别个人薄弱项。学习记录仅在用户同意后更新。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: When learning records are enabled, they may include reading-comprehension mistakes as well as grammar; storage and sharing are controlled by the separate Learning DNA component and platform settings.

Mitigation: Before enabling records for a child, review the Learning DNA component and platform storage and sharing controls. Say “这次不要记忆” for a session without profile access, saving, or handoffs.
