## Description:

A Chinese-language English grammar coach that helps K–12 students identify mistakes through guided questions and, with consent, track recurring weaknesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students and their educators use guided questions and focused practice to understand English grammar errors, including high-school grammar and relative clauses. With consent, the skill can track weaknesses and provide progress summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; deployment elsewhere requires localization of curriculum, language, emergency resources, and minor-consent rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or weakness profiles may expose minors' education data if stored or shared without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Require consent before saving profiles or sharing across skills, honor student record controls, and review parent-sharing settings.

Risk: Mainland-China curriculum, emergency resources, and consent assumptions may be inappropriate in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize teaching content, crisis contacts, and minor-consent rules before deployment outside mainland China.
