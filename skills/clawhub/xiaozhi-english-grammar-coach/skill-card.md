## Description:

通过循序追问帮助中国初高中学生发现并纠正英语语法错误，在获得同意后记录语法弱项。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in China's secondary schools use this skill to diagnose grammar mistakes, practice tenses and relative clauses through guided questions, and review weak areas. Upper-primary students need a parent or teacher present; progress is saved only with consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize student safeguards, consent settings, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's learning records could be retained or shared without authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm student or guardian consent settings before enabling long-term records or cross-skill sharing; honor pause, deletion, and sharing controls.

Risk: China-specific student safeguards or crisis contacts may be inappropriate in another region.

Mitigation: Use only for the intended Chinese K12 context until consent requirements and crisis referrals are localized.
