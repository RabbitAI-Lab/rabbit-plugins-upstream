## Description:

A Chinese-language English grammar coach that helps middle and high school students discover mistakes through guided questions and, with consent, track recurring weaknesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle and high school students use this skill to identify grammar errors, practice targeted questions, and review recurring weaknesses when they opt in to tracking. Younger students need adult supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis resources before deployment elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles or reminders may be retained or shared without the expected consent controls.

Mitigation: Confirm the platform supports consent for profiles, cross-skill sharing, reminders, and parent visibility, plus pause, export, correction, and deletion, before enabling those features.

Risk: Curriculum, consent rules, or crisis contacts may be unsuitable outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum and crisis resources and review local consent requirements before deployment in other regions.
