## Description:

A Chinese-language English grammar coach that helps middle- and high-school students identify mistakes through guided questions and tracks weak areas with consent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking middle- and high-school students use guided questions to diagnose and practice English grammar, including tense, agreement, relative clauses, and high-school grammar. With consent, the coach can track recurring weaknesses and provide progress reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, consent requirements, and crisis resources before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A minor's grammar records or progress could be stored or shared without consent.

Mitigation: Confirm student or guardian consent settings before enabling persistent profiles, cross-skill sharing, parent summaries, or reminders.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contact guidance may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum and emergency guidance for the learner's region before use elsewhere.
