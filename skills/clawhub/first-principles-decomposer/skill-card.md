## Use Case: <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger phrases may cause the agent to use a structured first-principles decomposition when a lighter answer would be sufficient. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit instructions when a brief answer is preferred, or reserve the skill for problems that benefit from assumption-challenging analysis. <br>
