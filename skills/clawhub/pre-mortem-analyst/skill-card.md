## Description: <br>
Imagine the project already failed, then work backward to find why. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Project owners, operators, and teams use this skill to run a structured pre-mortem: assume a project failed, identify likely causes, prioritize the most important risks, and define prevention actions and warning signs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad risk-related trigger phrases may activate the skill when a user expected a lighter risk assessment. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a pre-mortem framing before assuming failure as the analysis mode. <br>
Risk: Included examples are specific to named business scenarios and may not generalize to every project. <br>
Mitigation: Use the examples as format references only and adapt categories, thresholds, mitigations, and warning signs to the user's project context. <br>
Risk: Prevention priorities can be misleading when project scope, constraints, stakeholders, or dependencies are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Gather missing project details before ranking top risks or recommending concrete action owners and monitoring thresholds. <br>
