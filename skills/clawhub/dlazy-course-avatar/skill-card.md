## Description:

Guides course creators through planning and producing digital-lecturer videos with reusable avatar portraits, narrated slides, and short on-camera segments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators and training teams use this skill to turn lessons, slides, and scripts into videos with a consistent digital lecturer for paid courses, public teaching, or internal training.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course files and generated media may be uploaded to dLazy cloud services.

Mitigation: Obtain permission before uploading confidential or third-party training materials.

Risk: Login and auth setup may store an API key locally.

Mitigation: Use temporary DLAZY_API_KEY authentication when appropriate and protect any locally stored credentials.

Risk: Installing the dLazy CLI globally adds a local software dependency.

Mitigation: Review the pinned package before installing or use the documented npx alternative.
