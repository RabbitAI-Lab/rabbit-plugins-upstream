## Description:

Creates digital human lecturer videos for paid courses and training using reusable portrait recipes and a slide-first production workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators, trainers, and internal learning teams use the skill to turn lesson materials into landscape course videos with a consistent digital human lecturer. It provides portrait prompts, production steps, and commands for narration, avatar segments, slides, and final assembly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected course files are processed externally by Dlazy and applicable model providers, so uploads may expose confidential or unauthorized material.

Mitigation: Review Dlazy's privacy policy, share only authorized documents and media, and check internal presentations for confidential content before upload.

Risk: Batch generation can incur credit costs.

Mitigation: Confirm estimated credit costs with --dry-run before batch generation.
