## Description:

Helps course creators turn teaching materials into course videos with a consistent digital lecturer using portrait recipes and a slide-first workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators and training teams use the skill to create consistent digital-lecturer videos from slides, scripts, and other authorized teaching materials for paid courses, internal training, and knowledge-sharing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course documents and media submitted for generation may be shared with third-party model providers.

Mitigation: Use only materials you are authorized to share and confirm privacy requirements before processing internal training content.

Risk: Paid generation batches can incur usage costs, and saved API credentials need protection.

Mitigation: Protect the saved API key and use documented dry-run checks before paid batches.
