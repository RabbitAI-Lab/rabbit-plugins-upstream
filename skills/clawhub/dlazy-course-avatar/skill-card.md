## Description:

Guides course creators through landscape lecturer portraits, slide-first video production, voiceover, avatar segments, and assembly for online lessons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Course creators, trainers, and educators use this skill to plan and produce lessons with a consistent digital lecturer, narrated slides, and short on-screen avatar segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course scripts, slides, audio, images, and generated media may be sent to DLazy services.

Mitigation: Confirm the data-sharing requirements before installing the pinned DLazy CLI or submitting course materials.

Risk: DLazy API credentials may be exposed if stored or shared carelessly.

Mitigation: Treat the API key as sensitive and use DLAZY_API_KEY for temporary access when practical.

Risk: Generated course content may include unsupported commercial claims or omit required AI-content disclosures.

Mitigation: Review course claims, AI-generated-content labels, and applicable platform rules before publishing.
