## Description:

Helps course creators plan teaching videos with a consistent digital lecturer, reusable portrait recipes, narration, and a slide-first workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Course creators, trainers, and educators use this skill to plan and produce landscape lessons with a recurring digital lecturer, spoken narration, and slides or other teaching visuals.
