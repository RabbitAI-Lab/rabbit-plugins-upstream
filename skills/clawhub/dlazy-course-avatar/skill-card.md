## Description:

Guides creators through consistent digital lecturer portraits, narration, and slide-first production for online courses and training videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators, educators, and training teams use this skill to plan and assemble landscape lessons with a consistent digital lecturer, narrated slides, and short presenter segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lecture material, student information, slides, or voice and video assets may be uploaded to an external service.

Mitigation: Upload only material you are authorized to process through dLazy; avoid confidential lectures, student data, and proprietary assets without approval.

Risk: The stored dLazy API key may expose account access if disclosed.

Mitigation: Treat the key in ~/.dlazy/config.json as a credential and keep it out of shared files and published course materials.
