## Description:

Knowledge-course / online-teaching avatar lecturer — 16 landscape base-portrait recipes plus the slide-first pipeline that keeps a 20-minute lesson under 3000 credits. Use when the user wants a digital human to teach a paid course, a training module or a knowledge-sharing video on platforms like Xiaoetong, Tencent Classroom, Bilibili, YouTube or an internal LMS.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Course creators, trainers, and instructors use this skill to plan narrated teaching videos with a consistent digital lecturer, course visuals, and short avatar segments. It provides portrait and slide recipes, production steps, and cost guidance for turning course materials into lessons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course documents, images, or audio may be sent to external services during production.

Mitigation: Confirm that the materials are approved for external processing before using them in the workflow.

Risk: A generated lecturer or course promotion could imply unverified credentials or guaranteed educational outcomes.

Mitigation: Review the lecturer's presented identity and course claims against verified qualifications and applicable current requirements before publication.
