## Description:

Guides creators through a slide-first workflow for producing landscape course videos with a consistent digital lecturer, portrait recipes, voiceover, and short avatar segments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Course creators and training teams use this skill to plan and produce narrated lessons with a reusable digital lecturer, slides, and limited on-camera segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Course documents, prompts, audio, and generated media may be sent to dLazy services.

Mitigation: Confirm the service is suitable for the material before uploading sensitive or proprietary content.

Risk: Batch generation can consume paid credits, and API credentials require protection.

Mitigation: Review estimated costs before batch runs and keep the API key private.

Risk: Commercial training content or a synthetic lecturer may create advertising, credential, and AI-disclosure concerns.

Mitigation: Have legal and compliance teams review the course claims, depicted credentials, and required AI-content disclosures before publication.
