Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST) — OFFICIAL eligibility
Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/who-can-apply/federal-skilled-trades.html (fetched 2026-08-21)

## Minimum requirements (must meet ALL)
- Work: 3,120 hours (24 months full-time at 30h/wk, or part-time equivalent). Max 30h/wk counted. Must be in your skilled trade.
- If worked in more than one country: experience counts only if you were qualified to practise the trade where gained; if qualified in one country but worked elsewhere with different rules, must also meet requirements to practise independently there.
- Job offer OR certificate of qualification (one of):
  - valid job offer of full-time employment for total period at least 1 year, OR
  - certificate of qualification in your skilled trade issued by a Canadian provincial/territorial/federal authority (pass certification exam + meet requirements to practise in that province/territory).
  - If province/territory where you plan to live doesn't give certificates in your trade => need a valid job offer in your trade.
- Language: meet minimum scores in all 4 abilities (speaking/listening/reading/writing) on approved English/French tests.
- Education: NO education requirement. (Education only matters to improve CRS rank.)
- Proof of funds: needed unless you can legally work in Canada + have valid job offer.
- Admissibility: must be admissible.
- Must plan to live outside Quebec.
- Student work experience: does NOT count toward minimum requirements for FST.
