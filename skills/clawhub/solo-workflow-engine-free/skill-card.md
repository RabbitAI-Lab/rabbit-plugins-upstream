## Description: <br>
A no-code automation workflow guide for independent developers that uses the first four steps of a seven-step method to identify, select, design, and build automation workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External independent developers, freelancers, and one-person companies use the skill to audit repeatable work, choose no-code automation tools, design workflows, and test automations for tasks such as lead capture, invoicing, and scheduled publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be selected for broad automation tasks and can advise connecting high-impact business accounts. <br>
Mitigation: Review every connected app permission, use least-privilege accounts, test with non-sensitive data, verify field mappings and destinations, and keep a plan to disable automations and revoke OAuth or API access. <br>
Risk: Workflow guidance could produce incorrect mappings or destinations that move business data to the wrong place. <br>
Mitigation: Test each trigger and action with representative non-sensitive records, include failure notifications, and manually review results before relying on the automation. <br>
