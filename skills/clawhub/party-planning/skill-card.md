## Description:

Plan a party end-to-end — party game recommendations with rules, party food and drink quantity calculations (pizza, drinks, ice, taco bar, and 35+ other foods), trivia night packs, printables, and hosting timelines. Use when a user asks what games to play at a party, how much food or drink to buy for a group, how many pizzas to order, how much ice or alcohol to get, or is planning a birthday, holiday party, game night, tailgate, wedding event, or any gathering. Draws on HostPartyPlay's tested game library and portion calculators as its data source.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Hosts use this skill to plan gatherings by choosing games, estimating food and drink quantities, preparing trivia or printables, and pacing activities for their guests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Food and drink quantities are estimates and may not fit every guest mix or event.

Mitigation: Adjust estimates for guest ages, appetite, and event duration, and use the linked calculators to refine quantities.
