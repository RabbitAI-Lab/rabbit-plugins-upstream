## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The ClawHub security summary reports that the relay helper may send an auth token over plaintext TCP. <br>
Mitigation: Use a dedicated limited-privilege token or wallet, avoid sensitive relay messages until protected transport is confirmed, and rotate any token used with the relay helper. <br>
