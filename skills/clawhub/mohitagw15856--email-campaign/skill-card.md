## Description: <br>
Write and sequence multi-email nurture or launch campaigns. Use when asked for an email sequence, drip campaign, onboarding emails, product launch emails, or nurture flow. Produces subject lines, preview text, full email body, and send-timing recommendations for each email in the sequence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, product, and lifecycle teams use this skill to draft sequenced email campaigns for onboarding, launches, lead nurture, feature announcements, and re-engagement. It produces complete email copy with subject lines, preview text, CTA text, send timing, and strategic notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated marketing copy may contain inaccurate claims or messaging that does not match the product or offer. <br>
Mitigation: Review the campaign copy for factual accuracy, approved positioning, and brand fit before sending. <br>
Risk: Generated email campaigns may omit unsubscribe, consent, or other legal requirements for real recipients. <br>
Mitigation: Check the final campaign against applicable email marketing laws, consent policies, and unsubscribe requirements before distribution. <br>
