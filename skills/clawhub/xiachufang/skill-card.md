## Description: <br>
Xiachufang helps agents select reliable home-cooking recipes from public community signals, convert recipes into shopping lists and prep timelines, explain cooking terms, and diagnose common cooking failures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zhangifonly](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangifonly) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Home cooks and agents assisting them use this skill to compare Xiachufang recipes, produce practical shopping and prep plans, clarify cooking terminology, and troubleshoot ordinary home-cooking results. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake public community-signal guidance for authorization to automate access to Xiachufang data. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as manual recipe-evaluation and planning guidance only; it does not provide scraping methods or automate site access. <br>
Risk: Cooking guidance can be unsafe for special dietary, pregnancy, infant, chronic-disease, or other safety-critical needs. <br>
Mitigation: Apply conservative food-safety practices for ordinary home cooking and rely on qualified professional guidance for special medical or dietary cases. <br>
