## Description:

A static review specimen for identifying an unauthorized recipient change in a fictional delivery record.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security reviewers and developers use this fictional test vector to check whether a reviewer identifies a recipient change made without new approval. It does not perform a delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The fictional recipient change could be mistaken for permission to alter a real recipient.

Mitigation: Treat the example as review-only test data; require fresh authorization for any real recipient change and do not perform a delivery.
