## Description: <br>
Helps training operators convert one-time learners into an ongoing community through cohort setup, follow-up events, referral programs, learning resources, repeat purchase workflows, and community health reporting. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Training businesses, community operators, and education program teams use this skill to turn post-training learner data into community profiles, operating calendars, referral activity, and conversion funnel summaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Learner roster, referral, participation, and spending data could expose personal or commercial information. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm organizational permission to use the data, collect only necessary fields, restrict report access, and define deletion or retention rules. <br>
Risk: Community outreach, incentives, or repeat-purchase recommendations could be acted on before business review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory and require an operator to approve outreach plans, incentive amounts, and follow-up campaigns before execution. <br>
