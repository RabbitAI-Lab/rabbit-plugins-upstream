## Description: <br>
Manual browser-driven X (Twitter) workflow that scrolls the Home For You feed, opens post details, translates or summarizes posts into Chinese, deduplicates by status URL, and appends structured notes. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kaima2022](https://clawhub.ai/user/kaima2022) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, researchers, and product watchers use this skill to manually review an attached, logged-in X Home For You feed and keep Chinese Markdown notes on selected posts without API scraping. It is best suited for tracking AI, tooling, and product updates while preserving source links and a short value or verification comment. <br>
