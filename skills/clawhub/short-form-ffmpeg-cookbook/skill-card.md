## Description:

Provides copyable FFmpeg recipes for cropping video vertically, burning captions, trimming and joining clips, and mixing music under speech.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to prepare short vertical videos for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts with FFmpeg recipes for cropping, captions, edits, and audio mixing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The no-shell workflow may send media URLs to a third-party processor.

Mitigation: Use local FFmpeg for sensitive media, or share URLs only when comfortable with that service.

Risk: The security summary notes unpinned npm commands.

Mitigation: Review and pin package versions before running npm commands.
