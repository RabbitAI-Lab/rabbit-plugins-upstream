## Description:

Provides copyable FFmpeg commands for converting footage to vertical short-form video, adding captions, trimming or joining clips, and mixing music.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this cookbook to prepare short-form videos for social platforms with FFmpeg commands for vertical cropping, captions, clip edits, and music mixing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the optional Varg fallback sends supplied media URLs to a remote service and may incur credit charges.

Mitigation: For private recordings, prefer local FFmpeg installed from a trusted package source. Before using the fallback, review its privacy terms and the credit estimate.
