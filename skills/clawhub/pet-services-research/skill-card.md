## Description:

Researches public Rover sitter and trainer listings and profiles through the Crawlora API, returning structured JSON.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners and researchers use this skill to find public Rover sitters, dog walkers, and trainers by location and inspect their public profiles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, locations, and profile slugs are sent to Crawlora when querying Rover listings.

Mitigation: Use only queries you are comfortable sharing with Crawlora; avoid unrelated secrets or private personal data, and keep the Crawlora API key private.
