## Description: <br>
Helps software maintainers, QA engineers, contributors, and product teams add useful unit tests and raise coverage in existing codebases through a repeatable local workflow. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, and product teams use this skill to turn a testing or coverage goal into practical unit-test plans, checklists, code changes, verification commands, and follow-up risks. It is intended for existing codebases where teams need confidence that changes do not break current behavior. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad testing or quality-related requests. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation for sensitive workflows or narrow trigger language before installation when tight routing is required. <br>
