## Description: <br>
Caocao is a bilingual guide for comparing Caocao Mobility ride-hailing options, EV fleet characteristics, fare considerations, and corporate travel management. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External riders, travel planners, and business travel coordinators use this skill to understand Caocao Mobility service options, EV-focused ride characteristics, cost considerations, safety features, and enterprise travel workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Fare estimates, city coverage, service details, and safety-feature descriptions may be outdated or vary by city. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm important trip, invoice, business-account, and safety details in the official Caocao app or with customer support before relying on them. <br>
