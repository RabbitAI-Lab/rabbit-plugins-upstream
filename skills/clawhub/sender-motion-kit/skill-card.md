## Description:

Helps teams plan, build, and audit immersive editorial websites using scroll-driven motion, 3D, film, performance, accessibility, and award-submission workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Web designers and developers use this skill to turn real client briefs and visual references into plans, reusable motion patterns, implementation guidance, and QA workflows for immersive editorial sites.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested workflows may involve sharing Lighthouse reports and user-provided videos publicly, which could expose private or confidential information.

Mitigation: Review reports and videos for sensitive content, and confirm the audience and access settings before sharing.

Risk: Some workflow instructions can deploy changes to a live site.

Mitigation: Review the proposed changes and deployment target before pushing or triggering a deployment.

Risk: Three referenced paths were unavailable for analysis, leaving gaps in scan coverage.

Mitigation: Inspect the referenced files before relying on their guidance.
