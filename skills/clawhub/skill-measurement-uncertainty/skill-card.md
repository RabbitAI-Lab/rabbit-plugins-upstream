## Description: <br>
Helps laboratory and metrology engineers assess measurement uncertainty using the GUM method by collecting Type A and Type B components, combining standard uncertainty, and producing uncertainty evaluation reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Laboratory, calibration, testing, and metrology teams use this skill to structure GUM-based measurement uncertainty assessments, gather required measurement inputs, and generate report-ready uncertainty results. It is intended as an assistant for review by authorized metrology personnel, not as a substitute for accredited laboratory judgment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Unverified formulas, assumptions, or missing referenced helper files could lead to incorrect uncertainty calculations or report content. <br>
Mitigation: Verify formulas, referenced files, and generated uncertainty reports with an authorized metrology or laboratory reviewer before relying on results. <br>
Risk: Reports can be misleading when required measurement data, calibration certificate uncertainty, distribution parameters, or coverage factors are missing or estimated. <br>
Mitigation: Use enterprise-approved measurement and calibration data, keep missing values explicitly marked, and document reviewer-approved assumptions before final use. <br>
