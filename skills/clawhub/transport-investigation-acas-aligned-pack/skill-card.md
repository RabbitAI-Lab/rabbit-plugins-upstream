## Description: <br>
Generates ACAS-aligned investigation invite wording, neutral question sets, and evidence logs for starting a driver incident investigation or interview. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kowl64](https://clawhub.ai/user/kowl64) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Managers, HR teams, and transport operations staff use this skill to prepare fact-finding investigation materials for driver incidents. It helps draft neutral invite wording, an interview question plan, and an evidence log without drafting disciplinary outcomes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated investigation materials may involve sensitive employee information and employment process decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review all generated materials before use, limit included personal data to what is necessary, and follow internal HR governance. <br>
Risk: Serious allegations or cases that could lead to dismissal may require specialist review. <br>
Mitigation: Escalate to HR or legal reviewers before issuing materials or relying on them in a formal process. <br>
Risk: Using the pack at the wrong process stage could blur fact-finding and disciplinary outcome decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the matter is at the investigation stage and keep outcome or sanction drafting outside this skill's outputs. <br>
