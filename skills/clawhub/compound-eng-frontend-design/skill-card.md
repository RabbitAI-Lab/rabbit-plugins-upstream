## Description:

Visual design and aesthetic direction for frontend interfaces.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to build or redesign webpages and applications with a coherent visual direction, responsive layouts, accessible interactions, and implementation details suited to the existing design system.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Externally hosted placeholder images can send requests to third parties, and private information in image URL seeds may be exposed.

Mitigation: Replace remote placeholders with local assets before deployment and keep private information out of image URL seeds.
