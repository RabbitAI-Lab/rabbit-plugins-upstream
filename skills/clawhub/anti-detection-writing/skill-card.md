## Description:

Polish AI-written replies, emails, articles, and documentation for smoother flow and better human readability while preserving meaning and factual accuracy; when requested, test revisions with GPTZero or another detector and work toward lower AI-detection scores.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to revise AI-assisted prose for clearer, more natural communication while preserving claims, code, and operational qualifications. When explicitly requested, it can help run bounded detector-score measurements and report the limits of those measurements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Detector-score optimization may be mistaken for proof of human authorship or permission to publish.

Mitigation: Report detector scores as limited observations only, preserve quality review separately, and follow site, school, workplace, or publication disclosure rules.

Risk: Lowering a detector score can conflict with factual accuracy, readability, or necessary qualifications.

Mitigation: Stop or reject edits when improving a score would degrade correctness, omit needed detail, or hide uncertainty.

Risk: Browser scans or public posting actions can expose private content or violate user constraints.

Mitigation: Require explicit user authorization, preserve spending and access limits, and review browser scans or public posting actions before they happen.
