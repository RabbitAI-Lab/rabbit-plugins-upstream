## Description:

Provides a Chinese short-video and talking-head scriptwriting SOP with a three-part structure, rotating hook formulas, and quality-control checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, talking-head video makers, and content teams use this skill to turn rated source material into reusable Chinese short-video scripts for platforms such as Douyin, WeChat Channels, and Xiaohongshu.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated scripts may contain personal information or unpublished third-party details from source material.

Mitigation: Review and redact scripts before publishing, and do not automatically publish content that has not been explicitly cleared.

Risk: Script guidance may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a target platform or audience without human review.

Mitigation: Check each script for accuracy, platform fit, and the skill's quality-control rules before release.
