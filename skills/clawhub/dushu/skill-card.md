## Description:

Generates evidence-driven Douyin book recommendation short-video scripts and review plans from user-supplied book, product, and performance materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and commerce teams use this skill to turn real book and product materials into a publishable Douyin book-promotion video plan, including audience diagnosis, hooks, evidence chains, voiceover, 9:16 storyboard, compliance checks, and post-publish iteration guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video plans could repeat inaccurate or unverified book, product, author, award, sales, or reader-feedback claims.

Mitigation: Verify product and book claims against the provided materials and mark uncertain details as pending verification before publishing.

Risk: Book-page handling could expose too much copyrighted interior content or alter the meaning of quoted material.

Mitigation: Use only the minimum real page excerpts needed for evidence, preserve context, and check copyright and platform rules before release.

Risk: Marketing copy could overpromise outcomes or pressure sensitive audiences.

Mitigation: Keep claims evidence-based, avoid guarantees around income, grades, therapy, career results, or life changes, and include clear suitability boundaries.
