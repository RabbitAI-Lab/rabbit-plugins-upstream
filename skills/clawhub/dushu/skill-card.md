## Use Case:

External creators and book commerce teams use this skill to turn book names, covers, pages, product screenshots, or reading notes into Douyin book recommendation video plans with research, positioning, scripts, storyboards, conversion support, and post-release review guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill often performs online research about books, screenshots, marketing context, or user-provided reading material, which could expose sensitive search terms if private material is supplied.

Mitigation: Avoid providing private drafts, non-public business plans, personal reading notes, sensitive customer data, or other confidential material unless the user is comfortable with related terms being used for web research.

Risk: Generated book-marketing plans can become misleading if unsupported claims, private excerpts, or market observations are treated as verified book facts.

Mitigation: Keep user materials, verified facts, market observations, creative inferences, and unresolved items clearly separated, and review claims before filming or publishing.
