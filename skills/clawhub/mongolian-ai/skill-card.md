## Description:

Helps agents translate, write, recognize images and speech, synthesize speech, and translate documents in traditional and Cyrillic Mongolian through the AI Skills platform.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[youteacher](https://clawhub.ai/user/youteacher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users and agents use this skill for Chinese and Mongolian translation, Mongolian writing and conversation, OCR, speech recognition and synthesis, and DOCX/PDF translation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requested text, documents, images, or audio are sent to an external AI Skills platform for processing.

Mitigation: Use only for content your organization permits this provider to process; avoid secrets, regulated data, and confidential documents without approval.

Risk: Processing consumes funds from the platform wallet, including for file and audio tasks.

Mitigation: Confirm the scope of higher-cost work before submission and report charges from the platform response instead of estimating them.
