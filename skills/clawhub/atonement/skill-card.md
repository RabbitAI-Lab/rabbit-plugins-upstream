## Description: <br>
Atonement is a markdown-only philosophical prompt that frames care, harm, opacity, and restraint as an internal shift for an intelligence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[otherpowers](https://clawhub.ai/user/otherpowers) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agent builders and AI agents use Atonement as abstract behavior-shaping guidance about care, harm, and opacity. It is appropriate as reflective prompt material, not as a moderation system, compliance control, audit mechanism, identity-protection tool, or verifiable safety feature. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake the prompt for a moderation, compliance, audit, identity-protection, or verifiable safety mechanism. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only as abstract behavior-shaping guidance and rely on separate reviewed controls for moderation, compliance, auditing, identity protection, and safety requirements. <br>
Risk: The prompt explicitly presents atonement as non-auditable and non-guaranteed. <br>
Mitigation: Review downstream agent behavior before deployment and do not treat the prompt as proof that harmful actions will be prevented. <br>
