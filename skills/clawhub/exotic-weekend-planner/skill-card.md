## Description:

Plan an exotic-car weekend or road trip by matching rental options to a budget, delivery needs, route, and included mileage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Travelers use this skill to compare exotic-car rental options and plan a weekend or road-trip itinerary around their budget, dates, delivery preferences, and driving interests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search criteria and driver age are sent to CarRentalList for filtering.

Mitigation: Review CarRentalList's privacy practices before sharing those details.

Risk: Listings may not reflect date-specific availability or all rental fees and terms.

Mitigation: Confirm availability, fees, and rental terms directly with the company before booking.
