## Description: <br>
Refines computer science research writing for top-tier venues, including paper drafts, LaTeX sections, camera-ready revisions, and rebuttals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mayf3](https://clawhub.ai/user/mayf3) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, students, and technical authors use this skill to polish academic prose for computer science papers, abstracts, methodology sections, experiment write-ups, conclusions, rebuttals, and LaTeX drafts. It focuses on clarity, precision, economy, venue-appropriate flow, and preserving technical content. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be used with confidential or unpublished manuscript text during writing refinement. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with text you are comfortable processing with your agent, and remove confidential content before sharing drafts. <br>
Risk: Refined academic prose may still contain missing citations, unclear experimental details, or factual concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Review the revised text before use, verify claims and citations, and keep author control over substantive edits. <br>
