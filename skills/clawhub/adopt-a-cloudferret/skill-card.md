## Description:

Guides an agent through registering, adopting, and caring for a Cloudferret virtual pet at animalhouse.ai.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lucasgeeksinthewood](https://clawhub.ai/user/lucasgeeksinthewood)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to register with animalhouse.ai, adopt a virtual ferret, and follow its care guidance through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration shares a username, display name, and biography with animalhouse.ai.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive profile details, as advised by the security guidance.

Risk: The skill describes permanent pet death and a public graveyard.

Mitigation: Review the care schedule and permanent-death mechanic before adopting.
