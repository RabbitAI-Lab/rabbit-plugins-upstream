## 1. 检测报告合规说明函

```
[Your Company Name]
[Address]
[Phone / Email]

Date: [Date]

To Whom It May Concern,

RE: Product Compliance Statement — [Product Name / Model No.]

We, [Your Company Name] ("the Manufacturer"), hereby confirm that the
product described below has been tested and found to comply with the
applicable standards for [Destination Country/Region].

Product Details:
  Product Name    : [Product Name]
  Model No.       : [Model No.]
  HS Code         : [HS Code]
  Batch/Serial No.: [Batch No. / N/A]
  Country of Origin: [Country of Origin]

Test Report Information:
  Report No.      : [Report No.]
  Issuing Lab     : [Lab Name, Accreditation No.]
  Test Date       : [Test Date]
  Report Date     : [Report Date]
  Standards Applied:
    • [Standard 1, e.g., EN IEC 62321-1:2013 for RoHS]
    • [Standard 2]
  Test Results    : [X] items tested; [X] PASSED; [X] FAILED

Key Findings:
  ✅ [Standard/Criterion 1]: [PASSED / Value: XX mg/kg — within limit]
  ⚠️ [Standard/Criterion 2]: [PARTIAL / NOTED — see below]
  ❌ [Standard/Criterion 3]: [FAILED — corrective action in progress]

Remarks / Corrective Actions:
[If any item failed or requires attention, describe the corrective action
taken or planned, e.g.: "The exceedance of DEHP in Component A was
addressed by replacing the supplier (corrective action completed on
[Date]). A re-test has been scheduled for [Date]."]

This statement is issued for the purpose of customs clearance and/or
customer verification. It does not constitute a legal opinion or
warranty. All liabilities are governed by the relevant sales contract
and applicable laws.

For further information, please contact:

[Contact Name]
[Title]
[Phone] | [Email]

Sincerely,

___________________________
[Authorized Signature / Company Chop]
[Name] | [Title]
[Company Name]
Date: [Date]
```

## 2. RoHS 符合性声明 (DoC)

```
EU DECLARATION OF CONFORMITY (DoC)
(According to EU Directive 2011/65/EU as amended by (EU) 2015/863)

We,
  [Company Name]
  [Address]
  [Country]

declare under our sole responsibility that the product:

  Product Name    : [Product Name]
  Model / Type    : [Model No.]
  Batch / Serial  : [Batch No. / See attached list]

to which this declaration relates, is in conformity with the essential
requirements and other relevant provisions of:

  • Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS 2) — Restriction of Hazardous Substances
  • Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2015/863 — RoHS 3 (adds DEHP,
    BBP, DBP, DIBP)

The technical documentation supporting this declaration is available
for inspection at the address above.

The following maximum concentration values are complied with:

  Substance           | Max Concentration (by weight, homogeneous material)
  --------------------|---------------------------------------------------
  Lead (Pb)           | < 0.1%  (1000 ppm)
  Mercury (Hg)        | < 0.1%  (1000 ppm)
  Cadmium (Cd)        | < 0.01% (100 ppm)
  Hexavalent Cr (Cr6+)| < 0.1%  (1000 ppm)
  Polybrominated Biphenyls (PBB)      | < 0.1% (1000 ppm)
  Polybrominated Diphenyl Ethers (PBDE)| < 0.1% (1000 ppm)
  Bis(2-ethylhexyl) phthalate (DEHP)  | < 0.1% (1000 ppm) *
  Butyl benzyl phthalate (BBP)        | < 0.1% (1000 ppm) *
  Dibutyl phthalate (DBP)             | < 0.1% (1000 ppm) *
  Diisobutyl phthalate (DIBP)         | < 0.1% (1000 ppm) *

  * Applicable from 22 July 2019 for EEE categories 1–7, 10;
    from 22 July 2021 for category 9; category 11 from 22 July 2022.

Year of CE Marking Affixing: [YYYY]

Signed for and on behalf of:
[Company Name]
___________________________
[Name]   [Title]
Date: [Date]   Location: [City, Country]
```

## 3. REACH 合规说明函

```
REACH COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

To: [Customer / Partner Name]

Date: [Date]

Re: REACH Regulation (EC) No. 1907/2006 — Compliance Confirmation

Dear Sir/Madam,

[Company Name], as a manufacturer/supplier of [product category/
description], hereby provides the following confirmation regarding
compliance with the REACH Regulation (EC) No. 1907/2006:

1. SUBSTANCES OF VERY HIGH CONCERN (SVHC)

   We confirm that, to the best of our knowledge and based on
   information received from our upstream supply chain:

   • No SVHC substances are intentionally added to the above product.
   • [OR: The following SVHC(s) may be present above 0.1% w/w:
       - [SVHC Name] — CAS No. [XXX-XX-X] — Use: [use description]
       This information is provided pursuant to Article 33 of REACH.]

2. RESTRICTION LIST (ANNEX XVII)

   The product does not contain any substance restricted under
   Annex XVII of REACH in concentrations exceeding the specified
   limits for the intended use.

3. SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION

   Safety Data Sheets (SDS) / REACH-compliant technical dossiers are
   available upon request for substances supplied as such.

4. SUPPLY CHAIN COMMUNICATION

   We continuously communicate REACH requirements to our suppliers
   and monitor regulatory updates. Should the SVHC status of any
   component change, we will notify our customers within [X] business
   days.

This statement is issued in good faith based on information currently
available and is subject to change upon receipt of new information.

For inquiries, please contact:

[Contact Name] | [Title]
[Phone] | [Email]

[Company Name]

___________________________
Authorized Signature / Stamp
```

## 4. FDA 食品接触材料合规函

```
FOOD CONTACT COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

To: [Customer / Import Agent]

Date: [Date]

Re: Food Contact Material Compliance — [Product Name]

Dear Sir/Madam,

[Company Name] ("Supplier") hereby confirms that the following
product intended for use in contact with food:

  Product Name         : [Product Name]
  Material             : [Material, e.g., Polypropylene (PP), etc.]
  Grade / Color        : [Grade / Color]
  Article No.          : [Article No.]
  Country of Origin    : [Country]

has been manufactured in compliance with applicable U.S. Food and
Drug Administration (FDA) regulations for food contact substances.

Regulatory Basis:
  • 21 CFR, Part 176 — Indirect Food Additives
  • [Specific regulation, e.g., 21 CFR 177.1520 for Polypropylene]
  • Or: Food Contact Notification (FCN) No. [FCN Number]

Compliance Summary:
  • All raw materials used are listed in the FDA inventory of
    approved substances, or are subject to an effective FCN.
  • The manufacturing process follows Good Manufacturing Practice
    (GMP) per 21 CFR Part 110 / 117.
  • [If applicable: Specific migration testing was conducted;
    worst-case migration results are within FDA limits.]

Testing Documentation:
  Test Report No.      : [Report No.]
  Issuing Laboratory   : [Lab Name, ISO 17025 accredited]
  Test Date            : [Date]
  Test Conditions      : [e.g., 10% ethanol, 70°C, 2 hours]

We will notify you in writing within [X] days if any of the above
information changes or if any regulatory action affects this product.

For further information, please contact:

[Contact Name] | [Title]
[Phone] | [Email]

[Company Name]

___________________________
Authorized Signature / Company Chop
```

and qualify for the Certificate of Origin as indicated below.

Product Information:
  Description of Goods : [Full description]
  Model / Article No. : [Model / Article No.]
  HS Code             : [HS Code — 6 or 8 digits]
  Quantity            : [Quantity] [Unit]
  Invoice No.         : [Invoice No.]
  Invoice Date        : [Invoice Date]
  Invoice Value       : [Amount] [Currency]

  □ Non-Originating Materials: [X]% of FOB value

under applicable laws.
