# Example: Sequential Connectives (顺序连接词)

## 元数据
- **来源**: Midgley, C., Thai, S., Lockwood, P., Kovacheff, L., & Rohlinger, N. (2020). When every day is a high school reunion. *JPSP*, 119(2), 213–231. DOI: 10.1037/pspi0000336
- **论文编号**: 1.3
- **维度**: Cohesion（L1 局部衔接 — 顺序连接词）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: cohesion-diagnosis 的"连接词密度与多样性"、"段落首句是否推进 topic"

## 原句（Discussion 第 2-7 段，6 个 sub-section 的开场句串联）

> *"First, I found that social media comparisons on social media are remarkably frequent... Second, social media comparisons are more likely to be upward than downward... Third, these studies are the first to demonstrate that more frequent upward comparisons... Fourth, this research clarifies the role of self-esteem... Fifth, this research is the first to show that the image-based nature of social media may pose a particular social comparison threat... Sixth, social media has opened up a vast array of individuals... Seventh, these findings suggest that social media comparisons operate differently from more traditional social comparisons..."*

## 分析

### 1. 教科书级的顺序连接词
- **First, Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, Seventh** —— 显式 ordinal connectives
- 这种结构告诉读者："我现在进入第 N 个论点"
- 读者可以**主动跟踪**作者论证进度

### 2. 每段第一句都推进核心论点
- 不只是"another point..."这种空洞连接
- 每段第一句都嵌入一个**实质性 claim**（"comparisons are more likely to be upward..."）
- 这是 Unit 4.1.1 "Wrapping the discussion in a narrative" 的最佳实践——**narrative thread 通过每段首句的 claim 推进**

### 3. 连接词的"显隐结合"
- 显式：First/Second/.../Seventh（结构信号）
- 隐式：每段内部也有 adversative（"However"）、causal（"because"）等
- **两类连接词互补**：显式连接段间，隐式连接段内

### 4. 计数词 (counting) 的叙事作用
- "First...Second...Seventh" 暗示作者**已经预见了 7 个 sub-claims**——这种**前瞻性结构**让读者感觉作者对自己的论证有完整规划

---

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **段落没有显式顺序信号** — 用户用"Furthermore / In addition"等 additive connective 串联，但读者不知道已经讲到第几个 → 引用此例展示 First/Second/... 的 ordinal 模式
2. **每段首句是过渡词而非 claim** — 用户段首说"Also, ..." 然后才引入 claim → 引用此例展示 claim-first 模式
3. **sub-section 太多导致读者迷路** — 用户有 5+ 个 sub-section，但段落间无清晰信号 → 引用此例
