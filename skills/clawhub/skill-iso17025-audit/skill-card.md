## Description: <br>
辅助实验室内部审核员进行ISO17025管理体系审核，支持体系文件符合性与适用性审核、基于实际文件的审核清单生成、不符合项判定与分级、审核报告生成；当用户需要进行实验室内部审核、文件审核、不符合项分析或生成审核报告时使用 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Laboratory internal auditors and quality teams use this skill to review ISO/IEC 17025 management-system documents, generate audit checklists, assess findings, classify nonconformities, and draft audit reports against bundled CNAS reference material. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is intended to read and analyze lab management-system files, which may contain confidential business or accreditation information. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with documents the user has permission to process and handle generated materials according to the laboratory's confidentiality requirements. <br>
Risk: Generated audit conclusions may be incomplete or unsuitable as final accreditation judgments. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as assistance and have qualified accreditation or quality personnel review conclusions, clause citations, and nonconformity classifications before use. <br>
