## Description:

Guides users through making vertical digital-human videos from an authorized portrait or video and a script, with voice, lip sync, templates, and a cover in a browser.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[2253128](https://clawhub.ai/user/2253128)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and marketing teams use this browser-based guide to choose a digital-human workflow, prepare authorized face and voice media, select a template, and export a captioned short video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Face and voice media may be used without consent or handled under unclear privacy and retention terms.

Mitigation: Use only media with clear permission from the depicted or recorded person; avoid public figures and third-party media, and review the provider's privacy and retention terms before uploading.

Risk: API credentials may be disclosed when following the optional API reference.

Mitigation: Keep API keys out of chats, logs, screenshots, and shared scripts.
