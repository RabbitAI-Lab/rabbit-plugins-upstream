# Example: Climate Risk Boardroom

## Brief

Audience: board members and risk leaders.

Goal: understand how climate risk propagates through assets, suppliers, insurance, and capital planning.

Tone: clear, high-stakes, executive.

## Narrative Promise

By the end, the board will understand climate risk as a system of connected exposures because the presentation shows a single disruption moving across operations, finance, and strategy.

## Visual System

The persistent object is a regional operating map. It transforms from asset map to weather layer to supplier network to financial exposure model.

## Acts And Scenes

### Act 1: Risk Is Already On The Map

S01 - Calm Operating Map

Facilities, routes, and suppliers appear as normal operations.

S02 - Weather Layer

A flood or heat event enters as a physical force, not a chart. The camera follows the affected region.

### Act 2: Risk Propagates

S03 - Facility Disruption

One site degrades. Production capacity visibly drops.

S04 - Supplier Network Ripple

Connections pulse outward. Second-order dependencies appear.

S05 - Financial Translation

Operational disruptions convert into cost bands, insurance gaps, and revenue risk.

S06 - Scenario Controls

The audience changes severity, duration, and mitigation investment to see exposure shift.

### Act 3: Governance Decision

S07 - Capital Allocation Frontier

Risk reduction and investment tradeoffs appear as a decision surface.

S08 - Board Decision Map

The experience ends with prioritized actions: harden, diversify, insure, monitor, exit.

## Interaction

- Toggle climate scenarios.
- Scrub event duration.
- Select asset groups.
- Compare mitigation strategies.

## Accessibility

Every map layer needs non-color encodings and a concise textual summary. Critical risk states need labels, patterning, or iconography beyond heat colors.
