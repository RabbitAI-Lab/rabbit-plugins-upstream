## Description:

Guides an agent to explain completed work through a source-backed handoff document and, when decisions remain, a separate confirmation sheet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users and their agents use this Chinese-language workflow to understand work completed independently by the agent and explain its background, changes, validation, and open decisions to colleagues, customers, or experts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Handoff documents or screenshots shared with others may expose sensitive business data, accounts, server addresses, or credentials.

Mitigation: Review and redact quoted material and screenshots before sharing; limit online-document access to intended readers.
