## Description:

Merges, dedupes, ranks, and formats research findings into a report for multi-channel research results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and research agents use this skill after multi-channel research to merge findings, rank them, group related themes, and format a final report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger words such as merge and format may activate the skill during unrelated work.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger phrases or review activation settings before publishing or installing widely.

Risk: The skill synthesizes research results and may carry forward incorrect or misleading source findings into a final report.

Mitigation: Review the ranked report against the underlying findings before using it for decisions or publication.
