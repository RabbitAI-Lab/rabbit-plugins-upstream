## Description:

Guides agents through splitting, merging, moving, and archiving wiki pages while preserving content and repairing inbound links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dexio](https://clawhub.ai/user/dexio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and wiki maintainers use this skill to reorganize overgrown or duplicated wiki pages, update inbound links, and check for broken links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Moving, merging, or deleting pages can lose content or break existing links.

Mitigation: Review proposed moves and deletions, keep backups or version history, and check for zero broken links after refactoring.
