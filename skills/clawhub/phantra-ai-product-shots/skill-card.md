## Description:

Writes a product-led Phantra scene anchored to the person's own product photos, with macro and turntable shots, and explains 4:5 or 1:1 reformat options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product owners, marketers, and creative teams use this skill to create a product-focused scene from their own product photos, then guide generation of a short video or pack-shot still. It also explains how to reformat a finished clip to 4:5 or 1:1.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos may contain material the user does not intend to upload.

Mitigation: Share only product photos intended for Phantra and use a trusted product page or upload source.

Risk: Image or video generation can spend credits.

Mitigation: Review the quote's model, shots, credits, keyframes, and balance, then approve explicitly.

Risk: External Phantra service behavior was outside this review.

Mitigation: Use a trusted Phantra connection and review generated media before use.

Risk: The current frontmatter should be corrected for reliable skill loading.

Mitigation: Correct and verify the skill frontmatter before release.
