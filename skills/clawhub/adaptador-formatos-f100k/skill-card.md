## Description:

Adapts the viral mechanics of a user-provided Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube post into 2-3 content angles for the user's own niche.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[formula100k](https://clawhub.ai/user/formula100k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and content teams use this skill to analyze a single viral social post, separate its engagement structure from its topic, and adapt that structure into niche-specific angles ready for scripting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided social media URLs, screenshots, captions, thumbnails, or comments may be shared with scraping or public-page tools.

Mitigation: Use public, non-sensitive posts where possible, and avoid private or sensitive material unless the user explicitly accepts that sharing.

Risk: Adapted ideas and the original source link may be saved in local idea files.

Mitigation: Review saved notes before retaining or sharing them, and remove sensitive source links or context when they are no longer needed.

Risk: The skill can produce misleading format analysis if a post cannot be inspected well enough to confirm its mechanics.

Mitigation: Require evidence from the caption, frame, and comments; state uncertainty and request user-provided screenshots or context instead of guessing.
