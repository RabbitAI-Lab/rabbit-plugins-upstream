## Description:

商品换背景。白底商品图 → 逼真场景图，光影与投影匹配新环境。当用户说「换背景」「加场景」「白底转场景」「放到桌面上」时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

E-commerce merchants and product content teams use this skill to turn plain product photos into lifestyle scenes while aiming to preserve the product's appearance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and prompts may be sent to external image providers, and the scan did not fully inspect every component.

Mitigation: Review each provider's data handling terms, avoid sending confidential or personal images unless that transfer is approved, and inspect the unverified components before use.
