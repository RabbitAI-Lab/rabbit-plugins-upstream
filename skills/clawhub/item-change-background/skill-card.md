## Use Case:

External users and ecommerce content creators use this skill to create lifestyle-scene versions of product photos. It provides prompts and commands for generating scenes while guiding users to preserve product details and review the resulting image.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and prompts may be sent to an external image provider.

Mitigation: Before running, confirm that the provider and the images and prompts are approved for your use.

Risk: Generated scenes can change product details or depict an inaccurate or misleading use context.

Mitigation: Review each result for product fidelity and scene accuracy before using or publishing it.

Risk: The security scan reports partial inspection and unresolved behavior.

Mitigation: Consider the scan's stated inspection limits when deciding whether to install or use the skill.
