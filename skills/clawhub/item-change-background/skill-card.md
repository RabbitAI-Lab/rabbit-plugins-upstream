This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

E-commerce teams and creators use the skill to turn product photos into realistic lifestyle images for listings and marketing materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images and prompts may be transferred to an external image provider; the security scan is incomplete and rates the skill suspicious without establishing malicious intent.

Mitigation: Confirm which provider will receive the content and review its data handling before use. Avoid sensitive images or prompts until the provider is verified, and review the skill's command flow.
