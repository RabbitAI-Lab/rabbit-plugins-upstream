## Description:

Guides agents in placing product photos into realistic scenes with matching lighting, reflections, and contact shadows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product photographers, merchants, and marketing teams use this skill to turn plain-background product photos into lifestyle scenes while reviewing product details and visual realism.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos, prompts, and brand references may be uploaded to a cloud image provider.

Mitigation: Confirm upload permissions and use an approved provider; do not submit confidential local files.

Risk: Generated scenes may alter product details or depict misleading use.

Mitigation: Review product shape, color, logo, shadows, and scene suitability before publishing.
