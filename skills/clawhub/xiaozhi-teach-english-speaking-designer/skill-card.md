## Description:

Helps teachers design task-based English speaking lessons with realistic scenarios, differentiated practice, and feedback frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Chinese K–12 classrooms use this skill to plan speaking objectives, information-gap and role-play tasks, differentiated practice, and teacher-reviewed feedback. It provides assessment frameworks rather than grading students or conducting one-to-one speaking practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize crisis contacts and education rules before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student speaking records and class-workspace updates may expose sensitive information or change student profiles without authorization.

Mitigation: Use student aliases, require teacher confirmation for writebacks, and obtain consent before student-profile updates.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts and education rules may be unsuitable in other locations.

Mitigation: Deploy in a teacher-controlled Mainland China K–12 setting, or localize crisis referrals and education rules before use elsewhere.
