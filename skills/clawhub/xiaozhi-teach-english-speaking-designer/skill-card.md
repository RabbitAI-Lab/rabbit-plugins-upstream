## Description:

英语口语活动设计 helps teachers turn reading and memorized dialogues into task-based English speaking lessons with clear contexts, student output, feedback, and teacher-approved class workspace records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External K12 English teachers use this skill to design speaking lessons for upper-primary and middle-school learners, including task goals, input preparation, information-gap or role-play activities, feedback, rubrics, and teacher-approved class workspace updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China by default; deployments elsewhere should localize crisis resources, curriculum alignment, and minor-data consent requirements before student-facing use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may involve sensitive student speaking records or recordings.

Mitigation: Confirm school consent before use, prefer aliases and minimized samples, and avoid storing unnecessary identifying details.

Risk: Class workspace updates could record inaccurate or unintended learner information.

Mitigation: Keep writebacks as teacher-approved drafts and record observable speaking behaviors rather than personal judgments.

Risk: Crisis and minor-data guidance is China-specific by default.

Mitigation: Localize emergency resources, consent expectations, and student-data handling before deploying outside mainland China.
