## Description:

Helps English teachers design age-appropriate, task-based speaking lessons with activities, feedback, and assessment frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking K–12 English teachers use this skill to plan differentiated speaking activities, draft feedback and oral-assessment rubrics, and prepare teacher-reviewed classroom records. It does not conduct student practice or assign final scores.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class performance records and student profiles may expose sensitive learner information.

Mitigation: Confirm platform controls for pseudonyms, teacher approval, oral-only assessment entries, and consent before student-profile writeback.

Risk: Draft speaking tasks or assessment guidance may be inaccurate or inappropriate for the class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check generated tasks and rubrics and make all final student assessments before classroom use.

Risk: Speaking-task materials may include content without reuse permission.

Mitigation: Check material rights and use authorized content or links rather than copying unlicensed material.
