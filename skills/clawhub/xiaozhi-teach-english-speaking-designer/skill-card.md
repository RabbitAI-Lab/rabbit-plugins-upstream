## Description:

Helps teachers design task-based English speaking lessons with realistic scenarios, differentiated activities, feedback prompts, and draft assessment rubrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers in Chinese primary and secondary classrooms use this skill to plan English speaking tasks, adapt activities to class size and grade level, and prepare feedback and assessment templates. Teachers review all student-facing material and retain responsibility for evaluation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (intended Chinese K–12 classroom context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning records or recordings may expose minors' personal information.

Mitigation: Use student pseudonyms, confirm consent before handling recordings or writing personal records, and require teacher review before saving.

Risk: Generated activities or draft rubrics may be inaccurate or mistaken for final student evaluations.

Mitigation: Have teachers check generated tasks and scoring frameworks before classroom use; teachers make all final student evaluations.
