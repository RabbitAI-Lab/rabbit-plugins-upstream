## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence rates the release benign with medium confidence, but notes that the scan was partial because a local reference could not be matched.

Mitigation: Review the skill's bundled reference materials before use; the scan guidance does not guarantee zero risk.

Risk: Class speaking records and student work may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, follow the skill's pause, delete, and sharing controls, and require the stated consent before writing to a student profile.

Risk: Generated task cards or practice questions may be unsuitable or inaccurate, and unreleased exam content is sensitive.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check generated items before use and do not provide or reproduce unreleased examination materials.

Risk: Speech transcripts may not preserve pronunciation evidence.

Mitigation: Do not infer pronunciation from transcription; pronunciation judgments should be made by a teacher or supported speech-evaluation capability.
