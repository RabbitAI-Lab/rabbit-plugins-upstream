## Description:

Helps teachers design task-based English speaking lessons with realistic scenarios, differentiated activities, and constructive feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to plan English speaking activities, adapt tasks for different learners, and draft rubrics and feedback for classroom review. It does not replace teacher assessment or conduct one-to-one student practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student voice recordings and performance records may expose sensitive classroom data.

Mitigation: Confirm school consent and retention rules before recording or writeback, use pseudonyms, and keep student records private.

Risk: Generated speaking items or weakness summaries may be inaccurate or unfair if saved unchecked.

Mitigation: Have a teacher review generated activities and summaries before use or storage; obtain authorization before writing to a student profile.
