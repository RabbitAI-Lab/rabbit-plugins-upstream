## Description:

Helps teachers design Chinese K–12 English speaking lessons with contextual tasks, practice activities, and feedback frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers design age-appropriate English speaking activities, task cards, assessment rubrics, and feedback for Chinese primary and secondary school classes. Teachers review materials and retain responsibility for student assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (teacher-controlled K–12 English contexts; localize before use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student privacy may be compromised by class-record memory or student-profile writeback.

Mitigation: Confirm school privacy policy and required consent, use student aliases, and leave student-profile writeback disabled without consent.

Risk: Sensitive exam materials or unauthorized student recordings may be disclosed.

Mitigation: Do not upload unreleased exams or unauthorized recordings.

Risk: Guidance for Chinese K–12 classrooms may not fit other educational settings.

Mitigation: Use in a teacher-controlled Chinese K–12 English context unless localized and reviewed.
