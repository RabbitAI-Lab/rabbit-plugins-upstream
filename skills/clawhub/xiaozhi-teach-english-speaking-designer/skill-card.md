英语口语活动设计：把“读课文、背对话”变成有任务、有情境、有反馈的口语课。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking English teachers use this skill to design classroom speaking activities with task goals, role or information-gap formats, feedback strategies, rubrics, and optional student speaking profile writeback.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student speaking records or recordings may include sensitive learner information.

Mitigation: Confirm required school and guardian consent, use aliases or de-identified clips, avoid unnecessary recording uploads, and review writeback before saving to student profiles.

Risk: Generated tasks, rubrics, or feedback may be unsuitable for the class level or lesson goal.

Mitigation: Have the teacher review AI-generated tasks before classroom use or storage, checking context, role split, information gap, and grade-band fit.

Risk: Learner disclosures about self-harm, abuse, bullying, or severe distress require support beyond English teaching.

Mitigation: Stop the teaching workflow and follow the bundled crisis-referral protocol: stabilize, explain AI limits, contact a trusted adult or professional help, and record only the referral fact.
