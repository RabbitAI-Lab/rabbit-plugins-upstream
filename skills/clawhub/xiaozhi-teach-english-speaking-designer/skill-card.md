## Description:

帮助中国中小学英语教师设计有情境的分层口语任务，并准备课堂反馈与评价框架。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

面向中国小学高段、初中及高中英语教师，设计信息差、角色扮演和讨论等课堂口语活动，提供分层练习、纠错建议和供教师审定的评分框架。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (Chinese K–12 teaching context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Class records and student speaking notes or recordings may contain sensitive learner information.

Mitigation: Confirm school authorization and guardian/student consent where required; use pseudonyms, retain minimal records, and pause, delete or disable writeback when records should not persist.

Risk: Generated exercises or speaking assessments may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check activities and scoring criteria before use; teachers make the final evaluation and pronunciation judgments.
