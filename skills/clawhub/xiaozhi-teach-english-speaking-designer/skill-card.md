## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 English teachers design task-based speaking activities, differentiated practice, and feedback frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 English teachers use this skill to plan contextual speaking tasks, adapt activities to learners, and prepare teacher-reviewed feedback and assessment rubrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student voice samples or speaking records may expose children's personal information.

Mitigation: Follow school policy and obtain required consent; minimize and de-identify records, and use pause, delete, and sharing controls when data should not be retained or shared.
