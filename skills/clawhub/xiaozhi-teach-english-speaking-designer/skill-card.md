## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 English teachers design contextual speaking activities with differentiated tasks, feedback, and assessment frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 English teachers use this skill to draft information-gap, role-play, and discussion activities, with differentiated tasks and teacher-reviewed feedback and speaking records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (intended Chinese K–12 teaching context)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student recordings and speaking records may expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Confirm school and guardian consent, use pseudonyms, avoid real names and public sharing, and keep student-record updates teacher-reviewed.

Risk: Generated tasks or assessment materials may be unsuitable for a class or learner.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check tasks and assessment items for age, language level, and classroom suitability before use.
