## Description:

帮助中小学英语教师设计有情境、有信息差和反馈环节的分层口语课堂活动。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese K–12 English teachers use this skill to plan task-based speaking lessons, differentiated activities, feedback templates, and teacher-reviewed assessment rubrics. It does not conduct student practice conversations or grade students on a teacher's behalf.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; use elsewhere requires locally appropriate crisis-support channels.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student and class information may be sensitive, and unauthorized profile updates could expose or alter student records.

Mitigation: Require teacher confirmation for writes and honor teacherWritebackConsent before student-profile updates.

Risk: Crisis referrals may direct learners to inappropriate services outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize crisis-support channels before use in other regions and keep a teacher involved.

Risk: AI-generated tasks or rubrics may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a class.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check activities and assessment materials before classroom use.
