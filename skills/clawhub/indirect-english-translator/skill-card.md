## Description:

Translate indirect English into direct meaning and safe replies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and language learners use this skill to interpret indirect English in workplace, social, academic, customer-service, diplomatic, management, hiring, negotiation, dating, and everyday settings. It helps translate likely subtext, rewrite messages in direct simple English, and draft neutral clarifying replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Indirect-language interpretation can be mistaken for certainty, especially when context, tone, or relationship details are missing.

Mitigation: Present likely readings with confidence labels, include alternate interpretations when context is limited, and encourage direct written confirmation for important messages.

Risk: Sensitive messages about employment, legal rights, medical issues, money, safety, housing, immigration, or contracts may lead users to over-rely on a language reading.

Mitigation: State that the output is language and subtext guidance, not legal or professional advice, and recommend confirming directly in writing.

Risk: Automatic invocation may analyze messages the user did not explicitly ask to interpret.

Mitigation: Use the skill when interpretation or reply drafting is relevant, and keep outputs framed as possible readings rather than definitive claims about intent.
