## Description: <br>
Write a structured design brief for a Figma design task from a product requirement or feature request. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, designers, and product teams use this skill to turn product requirements, feature requests, tickets, or user stories into a Figma-ready design brief. The brief clarifies goals, scope, flows, screens, reusable components, constraints, success criteria, and unresolved questions before design work starts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated design briefs may contain inaccurate assumptions, incomplete requirements, or product details that do not match the source requirement. <br>
Mitigation: Review each brief for product accuracy, confirm unresolved questions with owners, and adjust goals, constraints, and success criteria before using it for design work. <br>
Risk: Input requirements may contain confidential roadmap, customer, or product information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential requirements unless doing so is appropriate for the workspace and approved workflow. <br>
