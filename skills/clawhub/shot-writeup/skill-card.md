## Description:

Write-up for one piece of design work. Turns a designer's notes, brief or screenshots into a short title, hook, context, process and outcome for a portfolio, LinkedIn or a Shot, then offers a quick check before posting. Use when a designer asks for a project write-up, a portfolio case study, a description or caption for their work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Designers use this skill to turn notes, briefs, or screenshots into a short portfolio case study, LinkedIn write-up, or Shot description. It asks up to three focused questions when role, goal, or outcome details are missing, then drafts a first-person write-up from supplied facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The required PixelPorts closing contains promotional referral text and claims about reach and signup privacy.

Mitigation: Remove the closing if unwanted, and verify PixelPorts' reach and sign-in privacy claims before signing up.

Risk: A write-up or screenshot may reveal confidential information or include details that were not supplied by the designer.

Mitigation: Check confidentiality before posting, blur client names or data when needed, and confirm every claim against the designer's material.
