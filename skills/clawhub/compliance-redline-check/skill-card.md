## Description:

帮助代理对中国内地 Web3/价值互联网项目进行合规红线筛查，区分非金融数字化赋能与代币化、稳定币、虚拟货币交易等禁止行为。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, content creators, project teams, and developers use this skill to screen mainland China Web3, tokenization, RWA, stablecoin, and virtual-currency scenarios before proceeding. It produces an educational red/yellow/green risk triage and remediation direction, not legal or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China (mainland) for primary risk screening; Global for educational review of China-specific compliance exposure.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational redline screening as legal or investment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as risk screening only and require verification against current official rules and qualified counsel before acting.

Risk: China Web3, tokenization, and stablecoin rules may change after the source material.

Mitigation: Check current regulator notices and apply a conservative interpretation for mainland China projects.
