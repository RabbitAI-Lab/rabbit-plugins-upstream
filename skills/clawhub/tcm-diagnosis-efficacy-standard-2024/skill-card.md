This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Clinicians, researchers, and other readers can look up Chinese-language TCM diagnostic criteria, syndrome classifications, and efficacy assessments by condition or specialty. This is reference material, not a tool for diagnosing or treating patients.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical reference content could be mistaken for a diagnosis or treatment recommendation; the skill lacks medical safety guardrails.

Mitigation: Treat the material as informational and refer diagnosis and treatment decisions to licensed clinicians.

Risk: Using the reference for urgent conditions could delay necessary care, especially for children.

Mitigation: Direct urgent symptoms, including breathing trouble, serious injury, severe infection, suspected cancer, snakebite, burns, and illness in infants or children, to immediate professional care.
