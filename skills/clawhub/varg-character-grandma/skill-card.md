## Description:

Guides the creation of memorable AI grandparent characters through distinctive age details, behavior, clothing, and props for character sheets, scenes, and video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to design distinctive AI grandparent characters and prepare consistent character sheets and prompts for scenes, video, and merchandise.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generation inputs are sent to an external Varg service and use credits.

Mitigation: Consider Varg's data handling and costs before sending inputs.

Risk: A VARG_API_KEY could be exposed if mishandled.

Mitigation: Keep the API key private.

Risk: Companion skills may have separate behavior and risks.

Mitigation: Review companion skills separately.
