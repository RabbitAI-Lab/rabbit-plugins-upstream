## Description:

Makes very old AI characters people remember, on varg: grandmas and grandpas (80+) with a contrast between age and behaviour, real wrinkles and age spots, a loud signature outfit and one prop, as a character sheet ready for scenes and video. Use when someone asks for an AI granny or grandpa, an old lady or old man character, a babushka, nonna or abuela, an elderly influencer, or an old character for an ad. Part of varg-character-design (definition, sheet, reuse in video).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
