## Description:

Creates memorable AI grandmothers and grandfathers aged 80+, with distinctive behavior, realistic age details, a signature outfit, and a character sheet for scenes or video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to design distinctive AI grandparent characters for character sheets, social posts, advertisements, and video scenes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and supplied images are sent to Varg for paid generation.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive inputs, review and pin the installer and repository revision, and check generation costs before use.

Risk: Connecting credentials to the separate Varg integration introduces behavior outside this package's review scope.

Mitigation: Review the Varg integration separately before connecting credentials.

Risk: An AI character could be mistaken for a real person or presented as a customer's testimonial.

Mitigation: Label the account as AI and do not present the character as an actual customer or product user.
