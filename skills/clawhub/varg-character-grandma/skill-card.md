## Description:

Makes very old AI characters people remember, on varg: grandmas and grandpas (80+) with a contrast between age and behaviour, real wrinkles and age spots, a loud signature outfit and one prop, as a character sheet ready for scenes and video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and marketers use this skill to design distinctive elderly AI characters for image, video, and advertising concepts. It provides character details and prompts for reusable character sheets and scenes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was partial because a local reference was missing, and it reported a mismatch between the skill's character-design purpose and some setup instructions.

Mitigation: Review the setup instructions and any referenced material before execution; the available scan reports medium confidence.

Risk: The installation options include a broader plugin setup, and the skill may use a VARG_API_KEY or a connected Varg account for paid generation.

Mitigation: Choose the install scope you need, review and pin the installer version, protect API credentials, and check generation costs before use.

Risk: An AI character presented as a real customer could mislead audiences, especially in product testimonials.

Mitigation: Clearly label the character account as AI and do not present the character as an actual product user.
