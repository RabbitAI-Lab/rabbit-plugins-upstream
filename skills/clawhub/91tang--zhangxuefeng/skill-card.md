## Use Case: <br>
Students, parents, and education advisors use this skill to receive blunt, Zhang Xuefeng-style guidance on Chinese admissions planning, major selection, postgraduate exam planning, and employment-oriented career tradeoffs. It is intended for pragmatic decision support and should not replace authoritative admissions data or professional counseling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Admissions, employment, biography, and quote claims may be inaccurate, outdated, or presented with more certainty than the evidence supports. <br>
Mitigation: Verify current school, admissions, ranking, employment, and biographical claims against authoritative sources before relying on them. <br>
Risk: The abrasive persona can be inappropriate for emotionally sensitive counseling or users experiencing distress. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid using this skill for emotionally sensitive counseling and route users with clear anxiety or distress to appropriate professional support. <br>
Risk: Real-person references and quoted opinions may blur the line between factual guidance and a stylized viewpoint. <br>
Mitigation: Label persona-driven recommendations as style-based advice and distinguish facts, sourced data, and opinionated tradeoffs. <br>
