## Description:

Guides agents in creating original vocal songs from lyrics and optional style prompts using MiniMax Music 2.5 via Replicate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pruna-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/pruna-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use this skill to guide an agent through preparing lyrics, specifying a musical style, and generating a vocal song for standalone listening or a music video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lyrics and prompts are sent to Replicate and MiniMax.

Mitigation: Do not submit confidential or regulated text; review the providers' terms before use.

Risk: Unpinned skill-install examples can introduce supply-chain changes.

Mitigation: Prefer reviewed, pinned skill versions when available.
