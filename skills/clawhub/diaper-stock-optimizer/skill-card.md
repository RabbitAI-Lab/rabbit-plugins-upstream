## Description:

Helps parents plan diaper purchases by size, estimate when stock will run out, and compare box lifespans and costs using a baby's birthday.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use the skill to plan diaper purchases and registries, check days of stock remaining, and decide when to reorder or move to another size.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Typical growth-band estimates may not match an individual baby's needs or fit.

Mitigation: Adjust purchases to observed fit and growth; do not use the estimates as medical advice.

Risk: A saved plan may be replaced when the planner is rerun with the same start date.

Mitigation: Review or back up an existing plan before rerunning it, and keep locally saved plans private.
