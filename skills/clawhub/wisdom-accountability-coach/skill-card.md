## Description: <br>
Wisdom & Accountability Coach helps agents provide compassionate personal accountability, longitudinal pattern reflection, and practical philosophy teaching. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mikecourt](https://clawhub.ai/user/mikecourt) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for accountability check-ins, commitment tracking, values reflection, and philosophy-informed growth conversations. It is intended as a supportive coaching and reflection aid, not therapy, crisis intervention, medical advice, or legal advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask the agent to remember personal commitments, relationships, health-related goals, struggles, writings, and behavior patterns over time. <br>
Mitigation: Use agent memory controls to review or delete retained information, and avoid sharing highly sensitive information that should not be retained. <br>
Risk: Users may try to use coaching conversations for therapy, crisis intervention, medical advice, legal advice, or severe mental health and addiction concerns. <br>
Mitigation: Keep use limited to reflection and accountability, and direct users to qualified professionals or emergency support for those needs. <br>
