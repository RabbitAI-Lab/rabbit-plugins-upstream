## Description:

Guides agents through ethical nudge design by diagnosing behavior barriers, selecting interventions, and planning tests and monitoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product designers, policy teams, and developers use this skill to address a measurable gap between people's intentions and actions through choice architecture. It guides them in diagnosing barriers, designing reversible interventions, and evaluating results without mandates or financial incentives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Defaults or framing could steer people toward outcomes that serve the designer rather than the chooser.

Mitigation: Keep options available and easy to reverse; use transparent, symmetric framing and check that the intervention serves the chooser.

Risk: Examples or expected effect sizes may not apply to a different population or setting.

Mitigation: Validate claims for the intended context, test interventions with an appropriate control, and monitor results and effect decay before scaling.
