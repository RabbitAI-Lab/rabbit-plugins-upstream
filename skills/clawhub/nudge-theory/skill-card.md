## Description: <br>
Guides agents through EAST-based nudge design for behavior gaps where choice architecture, defaults, framing, social norms, or friction can change behavior without mandates or incentives. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, product teams, policy designers, and other agents use this skill to diagnose intent-action gaps, design ethical nudges, and specify tests for defaults, opt-in or opt-out flows, onboarding, public health, retirement, privacy, and AI-product choice architecture. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Nudge design can become manipulative when it steers people toward outcomes that serve the designer rather than the chooser. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's chooser-serving, transparency, and free-choice checks before deployment, and review any intervention whose effect depends on hidden friction or obscure defaults. <br>
Risk: Social norm nudges can backfire when the stated norm is aspirational or not true for the target population. <br>
Mitigation: Validate norm claims against the actual target population before using social comparison language. <br>
Risk: Vague behavior goals can produce misleading or untestable recommendations. <br>
Mitigation: Require a specific, observable target behavior, baseline rate, control condition, primary metric, and monitoring trigger before scaling an intervention. <br>
