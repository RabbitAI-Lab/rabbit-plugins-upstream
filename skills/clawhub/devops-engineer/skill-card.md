Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation before execution, verify the target environment, and confirm the rollback plan for production or sensitive operations. <br>
Risk: Operational recommendations may be incorrect or misleading for a specific infrastructure environment. <br>
Mitigation: Review proposed commands and configuration against the live environment, existing runbooks, access controls, and change-management requirements before deployment. <br>
