## Description:

Helps operators running several business lines identify their binding resource constraint and decide where to invest, maintain, or cut.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners and non-technical operators running multiple product or business lines use the skill to compare those lines, identify the resource limiting growth, and choose where to invest, maintain, or cut based on their business records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
