## Description:

Analyzes user-provided podcast or live-audio transcripts to produce per-episode notes, cross-episode guest stance tracking, persona profiles, chat material libraries, and content-draft material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users, podcast listeners, researchers, and content creators use this skill to turn transcript files into structured podcast notes, cross-episode summaries, guest profiles, talking points, and social-content material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to imply firsthand familiarity with a podcast or guest that the user does not actually have.

Mitigation: Use outputs as study or preparation notes and avoid presenting generated details as personal listening experience.

Risk: Private, copyrighted, or consent-sensitive transcripts may be processed without appropriate rights or permission.

Mitigation: Process only transcripts the user has the right to use and avoid private audio unless all relevant participants have consented.

Risk: Cross-episode contradiction or stance-change summaries can harm reputations if quoted without context.

Mitigation: Keep source episode references and original quotes attached to any contradiction or stance-change claim, and review context before publication.
