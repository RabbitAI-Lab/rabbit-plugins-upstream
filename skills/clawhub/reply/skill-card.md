## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to draft tweets, threads, hooks, and X content strategy from a provided niche, goal, message, and optional supporting metrics. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Niche research can expose unreleased campaign plans, customer names, private metrics, or confidential business strategy through web searches. <br>
Mitigation: Use public or generalized topics, omit sensitive details, or ask the agent to skip research when drafting sensitive content. <br>
