## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to discuss Hupu community content, summarize sports or player sentiment, compare crowd ratings, and assess whether forum posts should be treated as opinion rather than verified fact. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Hupu posts and ratings can be mistaken for verified facts. <br>
Mitigation: Treat community posts and ratings as opinionated public-source signals and cross-check important claims with official or reliable sources before citing them. <br>
Risk: Forum sentiment and ratings can reflect immediate emotion, fan mobilization, or uneven audience samples. <br>
Mitigation: Summarize the community signal neutrally, note possible bias, and compare ratings or claims against other sources when making decisions. <br>
Risk: Sports betting topics can create harmful or inappropriate downstream guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid participating in sports betting discussions and keep analysis focused on community sentiment, ratings literacy, and content credibility. <br>
