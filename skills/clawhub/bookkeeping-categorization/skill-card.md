## Description: <br>
Set up a practical chart of accounts and categorization rules for business transactions, including examples, edge cases, and a clean-books routine while flagging tax-sensitive items for accountant review. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business owners, bookkeepers, and finance operators use this skill to organize transactions into clear chart-of-accounts categories and maintain consistent monthly books for accountant review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users could treat categorization suggestions as professional accounting or tax advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present the output as an organizational aid and require tax-sensitive treatment to be confirmed with a qualified accountant. <br>
Risk: Inferred categories may not match the user's business model, bookkeeping tool, accounting basis, or jurisdiction. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for missing business and tool context when needed, label assumptions, and keep category guidance reviewable before use. <br>
