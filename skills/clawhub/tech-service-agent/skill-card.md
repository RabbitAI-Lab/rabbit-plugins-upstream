## Description: <br>
Collects, maintains, queries, and reports on official public information about technology service institutions, including incubators, makerspaces, pilot bases, concept validation centers, technology parks, specialized enterprises, and related policy updates. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, and operations teams can use this skill to gather official public information about technology service institutions, run region/type/industry/OPC-tag queries, generate daily briefings, and prepare DCAR weekly reports. It is not intended for private business data collection, institutional ratings, investment advice, or crawling that violates site rules. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may collect or summarize public web information in ways that accidentally include private business information or violate a site's access rules. <br>
Mitigation: Limit collection to official public sources, respect website access rules including robots.txt, and review source lists before use. <br>
Risk: Generated analysis, recommendations, or query results could be mistaken for institutional evaluation or investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as informational summaries, include sources and update times, and avoid subjective ratings or investment recommendations. <br>
Risk: Incremental local state used for deduplication may retain stale or unnecessary public records. <br>
Mitigation: Review local state retention practices, refresh stale entries, and keep only data needed for deduplication and reporting. <br>
