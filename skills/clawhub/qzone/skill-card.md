## Description: <br>
Qzone helps agents provide QQ Space guidance for content operations, legacy album, journal, and post backup or deletion, privacy settings, and safe working boundaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and social-content operators use Qzone to plan QQ Space posts, albums, journals, privacy settings, and legacy backup or deletion workflows. The skill also helps agents decline unsafe access-bypass requests and caution users before irreversible account changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Deletion or batch account changes can permanently remove old QQ Space photos, journals, or posts. <br>
Mitigation: Back up important content before deletion and treat deletion guidance as manual account-change advice. <br>
Risk: Third-party export tools may expose passwords, session data, or account access. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer manual or official download flows and do not provide passwords or session data to third-party tools. <br>
Risk: Incorrect privacy settings may expose sensitive content or fail to apply the intended audience limits. <br>
Mitigation: Review QQ Space visibility, friend-only settings, and blacklist choices before relying on the change. <br>
