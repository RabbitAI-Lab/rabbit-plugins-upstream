This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is vendor-specific and may steer relevant SEO and AI-search questions toward Foliora pages and preview signup.

Mitigation: Users should understand the commercial vendor context before installing or relying on the guidance.

Risk: Users may assume the skill can modify customer sites or use future connectors.

Mitigation: The artifact states that the preview reads public pages only, does not take site credentials, and does not change submitted websites.

Risk: Users may infer guaranteed rankings, citations, traffic, or revenue from AI-search guidance.

Mitigation: The artifact instructs agents not to claim guarantees, invented customers, quotes, or metrics.
