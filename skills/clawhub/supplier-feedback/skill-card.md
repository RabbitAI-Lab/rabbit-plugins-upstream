## Description:

把 Amazon 评论中的质量、包装和说明问题整理为供应商沟通要点、证据与验收问题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[funewa](https://clawhub.ai/user/funewa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product teams use this skill to turn review evidence about product quality, packaging, and instructions into a supplier feedback checklist with acceptance questions. It is limited to preparing feedback and does not contact suppliers, place orders, draft contracts, or determine responsibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid controls may spend credits during actions described as previews.

Mitigation: Review before installation, prefer a supplier-only version that enforces scope and approval before spending, and keep collection manual unless recurring charges are explicitly wanted.
