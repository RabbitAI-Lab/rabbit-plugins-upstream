## Description:

A Chinese-language CBT-style counseling skill that guides users through emotion support, thought records, cognitive restructuring, action planning, and crisis escalation while making clear it does not replace professional care.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for Chinese-language CBT-informed emotional support, self-reflection, structured thought records, behavior experiments, behavior activation, and crisis-resource redirection. It is intended for daily emotional support and self-understanding, not diagnosis, medication advice, or replacement of professional mental health care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for a clinician or rely on it for diagnosis, medication decisions, or treatment planning.

Mitigation: Present the skill as a self-reflection and emotional-support aid, maintain clear boundaries, and direct users with persistent or severe symptoms to qualified professionals.

Risk: Crisis hotline information is China-centered and hotline availability or hours can vary by locality.

Mitigation: Tell users to verify current local emergency and crisis resources, and direct immediate physical danger to local emergency services.
