## Description: <br>
Learning teaches any topic in adaptive sessions by probing prior knowledge, calibrating depth and format, and checking retention before advancing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Learners and assistants use this skill to run adaptive tutoring sessions for explanations, exam preparation, re-explanations, retention checks, and repair of confidently wrong practice answers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can retain learner preferences, past misses, and topic progress in local files, which may expose study history on shared machines. <br>
Mitigation: Review, protect, or clear files under ~/Clawic/data/learning/ when local retention is not desired. <br>
