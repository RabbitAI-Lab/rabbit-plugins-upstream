## Description: <br>
Wiki Normalize standardizes commercial research term names, classifies them into an L1-L5 business taxonomy, selects a sizing metric baseline, and returns database-ready JSON. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business research and data teams use this skill to normalize raw commercial term names, resolve ambiguity, classify terms into L1-L5 taxonomy levels, and align each result with an appropriate market sizing baseline. <br>
