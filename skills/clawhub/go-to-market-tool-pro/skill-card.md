## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can cause an agent to read GTM, advertising, CRM, or analytics data and write reports or exports that may contain sensitive business or customer information.

Mitigation: Use scoped datasets and tokens, avoid unnecessary customer or regulated data, and review outputs before sharing.

Risk: The skill includes purpose-aligned local command and write/export behavior, so broad commands or exports could affect local files if approved without review.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before delete, reset, broad export, or unfamiliar command actions, and keep work in a scoped project directory.

Risk: GTM recommendations, attribution results, CAC/LTV calculations, and pricing experiment plans may be misleading when source data or assumptions are incomplete.

Mitigation: Validate calculations against source systems and have qualified GTM or finance stakeholders review recommendations before execution.
