## Description:

Provides evidence-based review guidance for OSS comparison reports, distilled rules, and corpus governance using a perspective derived from Yonglong Tian's research methods.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qikairo7](https://clawhub.ai/user/qikairo7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers reviewing penguin-oss-suite S1 reports, distilled rules, or corpus revisions use this skill to assess comparable benchmarks, rule freshness, and traceable corrections. It returns an applicable, violated, or not-applicable judgment with the supporting criterion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Claims or dates in the advisory guidance may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Check cited sources and dates before relying on judgments for important decisions.

Risk: Inferred judgments could be mistaken for Yonglong Tian's own views.

Mitigation: Present conclusions as the skill author's interpretation, not as statements by Tian.
