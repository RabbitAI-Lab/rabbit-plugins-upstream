## Description: <br>
Influencer Discovery helps agents build a multi-platform influencer candidate pool with per-creator profiles, authenticity red-flag screening, and a tiered shortlist for later fit scoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams and growth operators use this skill to discover creator candidates from scratch or expand into a new niche, platform, or follower tier. It prepares screened candidate profiles and a tiered shortlist for downstream fit scoring and outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow may identify and organize public creator profiles and public business contact paths. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for professional outreach, avoid private contact details, and record contact paths only when there is a clear business need. <br>
Risk: Discovery triage signals can be mistaken for final suitability scores. <br>
Mitigation: Treat shortlist tiers and preliminary signals as discovery inputs, then use downstream fit scoring before ranking or outreach decisions. <br>
Risk: Optional persistence can store discovery reports or roster update proposals. <br>
Mitigation: Require separate explicit authorization before saving reports, promoting facts, or proposing roster updates. <br>
