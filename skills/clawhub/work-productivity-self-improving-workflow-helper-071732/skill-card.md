## Description: <br>
Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical workflows, checklists, analyses, code changes, and decision support for self-improving and proactive agent-style work. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn broad self-improving or proactive agent requests into actionable local workflows, templates, checklists, analysis, code changes, or verification notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad productivity, bug-fix, and self-improvement triggers may select this advisory skill when a narrower skill would be more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Narrow trigger terms or disable implicit invocation when tighter routing is needed. <br>
Risk: Generated workflow or code-change guidance may be incorrect or incomplete for the user's environment. <br>
Mitigation: Validate outputs against stated success criteria before applying them to production work. <br>
