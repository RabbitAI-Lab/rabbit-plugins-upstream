## Description: <br>
Automatically extracts daily or weekly work progress, communication points, and to-dos from work-related message notifications and generates a work report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[vivalavida-say-hi](https://clawhub.ai/user/vivalavida-say-hi) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and professionals use this skill to turn work-related mobile notifications into concise daily or weekly reports covering progress, communications, approvals, to-dos, and risks. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read broad notification history for selected dates and apps. <br>
Mitigation: Configure the narrowest practical allowlist of work apps or groups and limit the date range to the report period. <br>
Risk: Generated reports may include identifiable workplace information, confidential project details, deadlines, names, or private messages. <br>
Mitigation: Review and redact the report before sharing it outside the original work context. <br>
