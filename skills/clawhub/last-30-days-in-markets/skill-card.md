## Description:

Creates a sourced, dated recap of the past month in US equities, including market mood, major story themes, ticker activity, market signals, and upcoming earnings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, analysts, and other readers use this skill to research the past month in US equities or a single ticker through a dated market brief grounded in SentiSense data. It does not place trades or provide personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market and ticker research requests are sent to SentiSense and may use a SentiSense API key.

Mitigation: Review the data-sharing implications before use and protect the API key.

Risk: Market summaries may be mistaken for investment advice or trading signals.

Mitigation: Use the brief for research only; check its dates and coverage, and do not treat it as personalized advice or trading automation.
