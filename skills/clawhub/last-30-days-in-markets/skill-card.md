## Description:

What happened in the stock market over the last 30 days, as one synthesized brief: the day-by-day arc of a fear-to-greed market mood index, the month's biggest AI-clustered story themes ranked by impact, which tickers and sectors dominated the news, the sentiment and smart-money signals that accumulated, where the market stands today, and the earnings ahead.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and market researchers use this skill to generate a concise, data-backed recap of recent US equity market mood, story clusters, ticker attention, current conditions, and upcoming earnings. It is intended for research and educational market catch-up workflows, not investment advice or trading execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends market-recap queries to SentiSense and requires a SentiSense API key.

Mitigation: Install only after confirming that the SentiSense data flow and API-key use are acceptable for the intended environment.

Risk: The output is market research and education content, not investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's required disclaimer and avoid using its recap as a recommendation to buy or sell securities.

Risk: Coverage and detail can vary by SentiSense API tier and preview limits.

Mitigation: State the actual coverage window and preview limits in generated briefs, and avoid presenting partial data as complete.
