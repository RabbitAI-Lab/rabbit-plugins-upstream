## Description:

Analyzes videos of children’s study behavior and returns structured observations and family education suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and educators use the skill to review study-session video and receive structured observations about attention, posture, and study habits, along with family education suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The no-analysis/export-only option may still upload children’s videos; account and credential handling and cloud retention also raise privacy concerns.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading real children’s footage until the upload path and account/credential handling are addressed and the provider’s retention policy is confirmed. Do not use export-only as a safe preview.
