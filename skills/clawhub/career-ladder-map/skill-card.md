## Description: <br>
Map where you are against the next level and build a concrete plan to close the gap. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, and career coaches use this skill to compare current evidence against target-level competency rubrics, identify promotion gaps, and build a prioritized 1-2 quarter development plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Career rubrics and work examples can contain confidential employer information or sensitive personal career details. <br>
Mitigation: Use only information the user is comfortable sharing in a career-planning prompt, and avoid including confidential employer material. <br>
Risk: An optimistic or generic self-assessment can produce misleading promotion guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Ground the map in the actual ladder rubric, concrete evidence, and explicit manager calibration. <br>
