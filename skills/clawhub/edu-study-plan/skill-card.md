## Description:

Creates weekly study schedules, five-minute daily review templates, flexible time buffers, and milestone checks from a learner's goals and availability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators and learners use this skill to turn study goals, available hours, and weak subjects into manageable weekly plans and brief daily reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad frustration or formatting requests may activate the skill without a study-planning intent.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a study plan before applying the skill to ambiguous requests.

Risk: An unsuitable study schedule could conflict with a learner's actual time or sleep needs.

Mitigation: Check availability and preserve adequate sleep; adjust the plan rather than promising specific grade gains.
