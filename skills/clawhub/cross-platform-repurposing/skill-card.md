## Description: <br>
Turns one source idea or long-form asset into platform-native social posts by extracting reusable content atoms, adapting hook, format, length, mechanics, CTA, and register per platform, and handing finished versions to scheduling. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External content teams, marketers, founders, and social media operators use this skill to repurpose one post, blog, video, podcast, newsletter, or idea into multiple platform-native social assets. It helps preserve brand voice and compliance guardrails while avoiding duplicate cross-posting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated social posts may be published with brand, compliance, or factual issues if sent directly to scheduling without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated posts and scheduling handoffs before publishing, especially for regulated, sensitive, or brand-critical content. <br>
Risk: Duplicate or poorly adapted cross-posts can look spammy or underperform when audiences overlap across platforms. <br>
Mitigation: Require each version to stand alone, feel native to its platform, and vary the angle or format for heavily overlapping audiences. <br>
