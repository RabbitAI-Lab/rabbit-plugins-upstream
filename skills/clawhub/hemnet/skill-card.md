## Description:

Search and analyse Swedish property listings, sold prices, market statistics, and mortgage estimates using Hemnet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish property can find for-sale and sold listings, compare local prices, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property searches may send addresses, locations, search terms, or listing identifiers to Hemnet's public API.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal details beyond what is needed for a property search.

Risk: Mortgage estimates may not reflect the rules or terms applicable to a particular borrower.

Mitigation: Treat estimates as indicative and check current Swedish lending rules before making financial decisions.
