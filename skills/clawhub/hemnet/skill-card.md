## Description:

Search and analyze Swedish real estate on hemnet.se, including active listings, sold prices, listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and Swedish mortgage calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer Swedish property questions by searching Hemnet listings, sold prices, listing details, photos, local market statistics, address matches, and mortgage cost estimates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may contact Hemnet's public API for listing searches, details, photos, sold prices, and market statistics.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for Hemnet property search and analysis, ask clarifying questions for casual housing discussion, and follow hemnet.se's terms of service.

Risk: Market statistics and sold-price medians can be misleading when based on thin samples.

Mitigation: Check the reported sample size before relying on medians or price-per-square-meter comparisons.
