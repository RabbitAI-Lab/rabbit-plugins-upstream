## Description:

Helps agents find and analyze Swedish property listings, sold prices, market statistics, and estimated mortgage costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish housing can search active and sold properties, compare listings, review local market figures, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Listings or market figures may be irrelevant or too sparse to support a housing decision.

Mitigation: Check location and listing relevance, and review the market sample size before relying on comparisons.

Risk: An address search can miss a listing when its results are truncated.

Mitigation: If the result is marked truncated, narrow the search with price filters rather than treating a miss as definitive.

Risk: Mortgage estimates may not reflect a buyer's terms or later rule changes.

Mitigation: Confirm inputs, current mortgage rules, and financing terms before making a purchase decision.
