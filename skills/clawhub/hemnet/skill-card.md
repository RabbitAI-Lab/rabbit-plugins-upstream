## Description: <br>
Search and analyse Swedish real estate on hemnet.se, including active for-sale listings, sold prices, listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and Swedish mortgage calculations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Agents and users researching Swedish real estate use this skill to find Hemnet listings, compare sold prices, inspect listing details, calculate monthly housing costs, and summarize local market data. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global; the covered property market is Sweden. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Hemnet queries may access public property data subject to Hemnet's terms. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for permitted Hemnet-related property research and keep usage aligned with Hemnet's terms. <br>
Risk: Market statistics, comparable sales, and mortgage calculations may be mistaken for financial advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat calculations and estimates as informational and review important purchase or financing decisions with qualified professionals. <br>
