## Description:

Helps agents research Swedish homes using Hemnet listings, sold prices, market statistics, address lookup, photos, and mortgage estimates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish property can find homes for sale, compare sold prices and local market statistics, inspect listings, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Swedish real-estate data)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mortgage calculations are estimates and may not reflect an individual's financial or legal circumstances.

Mitigation: Verify financing, tax treatment, and legal details independently before making a decision.

Risk: Listings and sold-price searches may be incomplete or change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm important property details and prices against current listings; check sample size when using market statistics.

Risk: Real-estate data is retrieved from Hemnet's public service.

Mitigation: Use the data in accordance with Hemnet's terms of service.
