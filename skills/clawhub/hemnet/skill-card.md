## Description:

Search and analyze Swedish real estate on hemnet.se, including active listings, sold prices, full listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and Swedish mortgage calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and real-estate researchers use this skill to investigate Swedish properties, compare active and sold listings, resolve locations or addresses, inspect listing details, and estimate Swedish mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Sweden

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property searches, addresses, and listing lookups may be sent to Hemnet's public service.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive personal information and use the skill only for appropriate Swedish real-estate research.

Risk: Use of Hemnet listing data may be subject to hemnet.se terms of service.

Mitigation: Review and follow Hemnet's terms before operational or repeated use.

Risk: Market statistics and medians can be unreliable when based on a small sample.

Mitigation: Check sample size before relying on local market statistics for decisions.
