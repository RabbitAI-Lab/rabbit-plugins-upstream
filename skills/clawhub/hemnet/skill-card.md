## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish property can find and compare homes, review sold-price trends, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property queries, addresses, and search filters may be sent to Hemnet's public API.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive details in searches, and use the skill only for intended property lookups.

Risk: Use of Hemnet data may be subject to Hemnet's terms of service.

Mitigation: Check Hemnet's terms before using or redistributing its listing and sold-price data.
