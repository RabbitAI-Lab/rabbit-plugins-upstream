## Description:

Helps agents search Swedish property listings and sold prices, review listing details and local market statistics, and estimate mortgage costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish real estate can compare active listings and sold prices, review local market statistics, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property lookups may be subject to Hemnet's terms of service.

Mitigation: Review hemnet.se terms before using the skill for automated lookups.

Risk: Market statistics based on small or partial samples may mislead property comparisons.

Mitigation: Check sample size and total matching sales before relying on summary statistics.

Risk: Mortgage estimates may not reflect individual lending terms or tax circumstances.

Mitigation: Treat estimates as illustrative and confirm actual costs with a lender.
