## Description:

Search and analyze Swedish real estate listings and sold prices, inspect property details and market statistics, and estimate Swedish mortgage costs using Hemnet data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish housing can find active and sold properties, compare asking and sale prices, review local market statistics, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Sweden

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property data access may be subject to Hemnet's terms of service.

Mitigation: Review and comply with hemnet.se's terms of service before use.

Risk: Sampled market statistics or mortgage estimates may mislead property decisions.

Mitigation: Check sample sizes and verify financial calculations and market conclusions independently before deciding.
