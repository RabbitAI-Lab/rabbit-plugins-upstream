## Description:

Search and analyse Swedish real estate on hemnet.se — active for-sale listings, sold prices (slutpriser), full listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and a Swedish mortgage calculator. Use when the user asks about Swedish property, Hemnet, bostäder, lägenheter, villor, or slutpriser.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People researching Swedish property use the skill to find active and sold listings, review property details and local market statistics, compare homes, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property searches send location or address criteria to Hemnet.

Mitigation: Check the separate tool provider before enabling it.

Risk: Mortgage cost estimates depend on the assumptions and rules used by the calculator.

Mitigation: Verify mortgage assumptions before relying on an estimate.
