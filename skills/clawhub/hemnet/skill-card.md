## Description:

Search and analyse Swedish real estate on hemnet.se — active for-sale listings, sold prices (slutpriser), full listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and a Swedish mortgage calculator. Use when the user asks about Swedish property, Hemnet, bostäder, lägenheter, villor, or slutpriser.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can search active and sold Swedish property listings, compare homes, review local market statistics, and estimate monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Sweden

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Property search requests are sent to Hemnet.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive personal information in search queries.

Risk: Mortgage estimates depend on stated assumptions and dated amortisation rules.

Mitigation: Verify applicable assumptions and estimates with your lender.
