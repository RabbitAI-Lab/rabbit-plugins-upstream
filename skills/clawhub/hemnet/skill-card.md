## Description:

Search and analyse Swedish real estate on hemnet.se - active for-sale listings, sold prices, listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and Swedish mortgage calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and real estate researchers use this skill to search Swedish Hemnet listings, inspect sold-price comps, compare properties, resolve addresses, and estimate Swedish monthly mortgage costs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hemnet searches may send search terms or addresses to Hemnet's public service.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal data unless sharing it with Hemnet's public service is acceptable.

Risk: Mortgage calculations involving income are informational and may be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat mortgage outputs as estimates and verify affordability, tax, and lending assumptions with a qualified financial professional.

Risk: Market statistics based on small sample sizes can be misleading.

Mitigation: Check the reported sample size before relying on medians or price-per-square-meter summaries.
