## Description:

Search and analyse Swedish real estate on hemnet.se, including active for-sale listings, sold prices (slutpriser), listing details, photos, market statistics, address resolution, and Swedish mortgage calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chrischall](https://clawhub.ai/user/chrischall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to research Swedish property listings, sold-price comparables, market statistics, and listing details from Hemnet. It can also estimate Swedish monthly mortgage costs for informational planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hemnet API data, listing availability, prices, and terms may change or be incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify important listing details and hemnet.se terms directly before relying on the results.

Risk: Mortgage calculator results may be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Present calculations as informational estimates and direct users to qualified financial sources for decisions.

Risk: Market statistics from small samples can be misleading.

Mitigation: Check sample size and describe thin medians or averages as low-confidence signals.
