## Description: <br>
Designs, runs, records, and closes out meetings with agendas, facilitation guidance, decision records, minutes, recaps, and follow-up. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, managers, facilitators, and operations teams use this skill to prepare meetings, run rooms, capture decisions and actions, and close follow-up across internal and external meeting types. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Meeting records and attendee context can contain sensitive third-party working data. <br>
Mitigation: Review shared contacts and projects behavior, set confidentiality preferences, and avoid storing HR, legal, compensation, health, or subjective performance notes outside the proper system. <br>
Risk: Pasted invites, transcripts, or notes may contain credentials or off-the-record content. <br>
Mitigation: Strip embedded passcodes, dial-in PINs, meeting passwords, and secret calendar URLs; store pointers only and do not write off-the-record content. <br>
Risk: Recording or transcription may require participant consent. <br>
Mitigation: Use the default recording consent posture of asking first, and propose or announce recording or transcription before it occurs. <br>
