## Description: <br>
Produces structured redline suggestions for Word documents, including insertions, deletions, replacements, margin comments, review summaries, and application guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, reviewers, legal teams, managers, and document authors use this skill to turn a draft or uploaded Word document into a structured redline with substantive edits, stylistic edits, margin comments, and instructions for applying the changes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Confidential, contractual, or regulated document content may be exposed to the agent during review. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the sections needed for review when handling sensitive material, and avoid sharing documents unless the agent environment is approved for that content. <br>
Risk: Generated redline suggestions or comments may introduce incorrect legal, compliance, factual, or stylistic guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated redline before applying changes in Microsoft Word or Google Docs, especially for contracts, compliance material, or regulated documents. <br>
