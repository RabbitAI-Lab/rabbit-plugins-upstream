# Full-Chain 60-Scenario Digital Benchmarking Checklist

> **Purpose**: Rapid self-assessment of a retail enterprise's full-chain digital maturity, covering 8 business chains and 60 digital scenarios.
> **How to Use**: Check current status for each item → mark completion level → calculate completion rate per chain → benchmark against industry levels.
> **Target Audience**: All retail formats (mark N/A for scenarios that do not apply).

---

## Checklist Usage Instructions

Evaluate two criteria per scenario:
- **Completion Level**: 0 = Not started / 1 = Basic (paper/Excel) / 2 = Systematized (digitized) / 3 = Well-executed (data-driven) / 4 = World-class (AI-driven)
- **Importance**: 1-star = Nice to have / 2-star = Important / 3-star = Critical (core competitive advantage)
