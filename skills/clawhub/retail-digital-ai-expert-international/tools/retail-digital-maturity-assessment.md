# Retail Digital Maturity Assessment Tool (R-DMM)

> **Purpose**: Rapidly assess a retail enterprise's digital maturity level (L1-L5), covering 5 dimensions and 25 sub-indicators.
> **How to Use**: Score each item (1-5, decimals allowed), enter actual scores, and automatically calculate the composite score and maturity level.
> **Target Audience**: All retail formats.

## Usage Instructions

1. **Before Assessment**: Collect the client's system inventory, 12-24 months of KPI data, org chart, and member data.
2. **During Assessment**: Score each item independently; do not let other items influence your scoring. Use 0.5 precision.
3. **After Assessment**: Compare against industry benchmarks, identify the Top 5 gaps, and develop a leapfrog pathway.
4. **Update Frequency**: Re-assess every 6-12 months to track progress.
