# AI Scenario Priority Scorecard (RICE+ Model)

> **Purpose**: Systematically evaluate retail AI application scenario priorities; identify which scenarios should be "launched immediately," "short-term priority," and "medium-term roadmap."
> **How to Use**: List candidate AI scenarios → score each item (1-5) → auto-calculate RICE+ score → rank → decide.

---

## 1. RICE+ Scoring Model

| Dimension | Weight | Description | Scoring Criteria (1-5) |
|------|:---:|------|------|
| **R** Reach | 25% | Breadth of impact | 1 = impacts <10% of stores/categories/customers; 3 = 30-60%; 5 = >90% |
| **I** Impact | 30% | Business impact magnitude | 1 = <1% impact on revenue or profit; 3 = 3-10%; 5 = >20% |
| **C** Confidence | 20% | Technical feasibility | 1 = pure research / 0-30% confidence; 3 = mature solution / 60-80%; 5 = proven / 95%+ |
| **E** Effort | 15% | Implementation difficulty | 1 = extreme (>$500K / >12 months); 3 = moderate ($50-200K / 4-8 months); 5 = very low (<$10K / <2 months) |
| **+AI** AI Fit | 10% | Suitability for AI | 1 = rules engine / manual better; 3 = AI enhances results; 5 = AI is the best or only solution |

### Calculation Formula

```
RICE+ = R x 0.25 + I x 0.30 + C x 0.20 + E x 0.15 + AI x 0.10
```

### Priority Classification

| Score Range | Priority | Action |
|:---:|:---:|------|
| >= 4.0 | **P0 Launch Immediately** | Kick off now, allocate resources, start within 1-2 months |
| 3.0 - 3.9 | **P1 Short-Term Priority** | Start within 3-6 months; prepare prerequisites first (data/team) |
| 2.0 - 2.9 | **P2 Medium-Term Roadmap** | Monitor within 6-12 months; keep tracking technology readiness |
| < 2.0 | **P3 Watch / Not Recommended** | Do not invest for now; wait for technology maturation or condition changes |

### Detailed Scoring Criteria

#### R - Reach (25%)

| Score | Store Coverage | Category Coverage | Customer Coverage |
|:---:|------|------|------|
| 1 | <10% of stores | <10% of categories | <10% of customers/members |
| 2 | 10-30% | 10-30% | 10-30% |
| 3 | 30-60% | 30-60% | 30-60% |
| 4 | 60-90% | 60-90% | 60-90% |
| 5 | >90% of stores | >90% of categories | >90% of customers/members |

#### I - Impact (30%)

| Score | Revenue Impact | Profit Impact | Efficiency Improvement |
|:---:|------|------|------|
| 1 | <1% | <1% | <10% |
| 2 | 1-3% | 1-3% | 10-20% |
| 3 | 3-10% | 3-10% | 20-50% |
| 4 | 10-20% | 10-20% | 50-100% |
| 5 | >20% | >20% | >100% |

#### C - Confidence (20%)

| Score | Technology Maturity | Data Readiness | Team Capability | Success Likelihood |
|:---:|------|------|------|:---:|
| 1 | Pure research | No data | No team | 0-30% |
| 2 | Early-stage application | Insufficient data | External hires needed | 30-50% |
| 3 | Mature solution exists | Data mostly usable | Basic team in place | 60-80% |
| 4 | Industry case studies exist | Good data quality | Experienced team | 80-95% |
| 5 | Industry standard practice | Complete data | Top-tier team | 95%+ |

#### E - Effort (15%)

| Score | Timeline | Budget (Estimated) | Change Resistance |
|:---:|:---:|------|:---:|
| 1 | >12 months | >$500K | Very high |
| 2 | 8-12 months | $200-500K | High |
| 3 | 4-8 months | $50-200K | Moderate |
| 4 | 2-4 months | $10-50K | Low |
| 5 | <2 months | <$10K | Very low |

#### AI - AI Fit (10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|:---:|------|
| 1 | Rules engine or manual solution is better |
| 2 | AI is usable but not the best solution |
| 3 | AI can significantly enhance results |
| 4 | AI is the current best solution |
| 5 | AI is the only viable solution (no AI = can't do it) |
