## 1. POS / Unified Commerce Platforms (10 In-Depth + 8 Quick Reference)

### 1.1 Global Leaders

#### Oracle Retail
- **Positioning**: The most complete enterprise-grade full-stack retail solution globally
- **Core Products**: Oracle Retail Xstore POS + Merchandising + Planning + Supply Chain + Insights
- **Best For**: Large supermarkets / department stores / chains / global 10K+ stores
- **Strengths**: Integrated merchandising, supply chain, pricing, and promotions; 40 years of retail technology heritage
- **Weaknesses**: Expensive (6-7 figure engagements), 6-18 month implementation, requires specialized operations team
- **Global Coverage**: Worldwide presence, strong in Americas, EMEA, and APAC
- **Typical Clients**: Walmart (historical), global department stores, large chain supermarkets
- **Reference Annual Fee**: Custom quote (typically $150K-$750K+/year)

#### Shopify POS
- **Positioning**: Unified commerce platform from online to offline, the SMB leader
- **Core Products**: Shopify POS (hardware + software) + Shopify E-commerce + Shopify Payments
- **Best For**: Independent retail / DTC brands / small-to-mid chains / pop-up stores
- **Strengths**: Unified online/offline inventory/order/membership; exceptionally easy to use (30-min onboarding); rich app ecosystem
- **Weaknesses**: Limited support for complex large-scale retail scenarios (multi-warehouse / multi-tax / multi-language); hardware ecosystem outside US/Canada varies
- **Typical Clients**: Global DTC brands, independent retailers, small-to-mid chains
- **Reference Annual Fee**: $29-$299/month (POS subscription) + transaction fees

#### NCR Voyix
- **Positioning**: Global retail POS giant with deep vertical expertise in grocery, convenience, and department stores
- **Core Products**: NCR Voyix POS + SCO self-checkout + digital banking
- **Best For**: Supermarkets / hypermarkets / convenience stores / department stores
- **Strengths**: Extremely deep grocery/CVS vertical capabilities; integrated hardware + software; 140-year history
- **Weaknesses**: Primarily Americas/EMEA focused; closed ecosystem; premium pricing
- **Typical Clients**: Global large-scale supermarket and convenience chains
- **Reference Annual Fee**: Custom quote

#### Cegid
- **Positioning**: European retail POS/ERP leader, deep vertical expertise in apparel, beauty, luxury, and lifestyle
- **Core Products**: Cegid Retail (Y2) + Cegid Pulse (AI) + Cegid LiveStore
- **Best For**: Apparel / beauty / luxury brands / lifestyle / specialty retail
- **Strengths**: AI-native (Pulse AI assistant); mobile POS leader; multi-country European compliance; global deployment
- **Weaknesses**: Lighter coverage in APAC; luxury positioning means premium pricing
- **Typical Clients**: European luxury brands, apparel chains, beauty brands
- **Reference Annual Fee**: Custom quote ($45K-$300K+/year)

### 1.2 Mid-Market & Regional Leaders

#### Square for Retail (Block)
- **Positioning**: Micro-to-small retail, zero-friction onboarding
- **Core Products**: Square POS + Square for Retail + Square Online + Square Payments
- **Best For**: Micro-retail / independent / pop-up / farmers markets
- **Strengths**: Zero barrier to entry, instant activation, transparent pricing
- **Weaknesses**: Not suitable for complex multi-store operations; limited WMS/ERP integration
- **Reference Annual Fee**: Free + transaction fees (2.6% + $0.10 per transaction)

#### Lightspeed
- **Positioning**: SMB retail + hospitality, strong inventory management
- **Core Products**: Lightspeed Retail POS + Lightspeed E-commerce + Lightspeed Payments
- **Best For**: Independent retail / specialty / multi-store SMB
- **Strengths**: Robust inventory management; multi-store; strong in sporting goods, bike shops, apparel
- **Weaknesses**: Limited enterprise capability; POS hardware lock-in in some regions
- **Reference Annual Fee**: $69-$199/month

#### Toast
- **Positioning**: Hospitality-first POS with retail extension
- **Core Products**: Toast POS + Toast Online Ordering + Toast Payments
- **Best For**: Food & beverage / food retail / bakery / cafe
- **Strengths**: #1 restaurant POS; extending into food retail; excellent handheld POS
- **Weaknesses**: Primarily food service; not a general retail solution
- **Reference Annual Fee**: Starting from $69/month

#### Revel Systems
- **Positioning**: iPad POS for mid-market retail and QSR
- **Core Products**: Revel POS + Revel Advantage (payments) + Revel Enterprise
- **Best For**: Mid-market retail chains / QSR / convenience
- **Strengths**: Robust offline mode; enterprise iPad POS; strong in C-stores
- **Weaknesses**: Limited e-commerce integration; less known outside Americas
- **Reference Annual Fee**: Custom quote (typically $2K-$8K/year per terminal)

|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| **Southeast Asia (SEA)** | **Shopee** / **Lazada** | 650M+ population; mobile-first, high social commerce adoption | Digital wallets (GrabPay, GCash, Dana, TrueMoney), COD prevalent | Data localization laws vary by country (e.g., Indonesia PDP, Thailand PDPA); import duties differ per ASEAN member | 3PL-heavy (J&T, Ninja Van, LEX); cross-border warehouse options in SG/MY/TH |
| **Japan & Korea** | **Rakuten** (JP) / **Coupang** (KR) | High-income, high digital penetration; loyalty-driven | Rakuten Pay / PayPay (JP); KakaoPay / Naver Pay (KR); cards also strong | Japan: Act on Protection of Personal Information (APPI); Korea: PIPA. | **India** | **Flipkart** / **Reliance JioMart** / Amazon India | 1.4B population; price-sensitive mass market; rapid digital adoption via UPI | UPI (PhonePe/Google Pay/Paytm), COD remains significant | FDI restrictions on B2C inventory model (marketplace-only for foreign entities); DPDP Act 2023 data rules; diverse state-level GST compliance | COD rates 40-60% in some categories; extensive courier network; Reliance omnichannel integration |
| **Latin America** | **Mercado Libre** (dominant) / Amazon / Americanas | 650M+ population; growing middle class; high mobile usage | Mercado Pago, local credit/debit cards, installment payments (boleto, Pix in Brazil) | LGPD (Brazil), ARCO rights (Mexico); tax compliance complex (Nota Fiscal in Brazil); currency volatility hedge needed | Mercado Envios logistics réseau; cross-border from US/MX warehousing common |

### 11.2 Regional Compliance & Payment Checklist

| Check | Key Questions |
|------|------|
| **Entity & Tax** | Is a local entity required? VAT/GST registration needed per market? Any FDI/investment restrictions? |
| **Data Privacy** | GDPR (EU), LGPD (BR), POPIA (ZA), DPDP (IN) — which applies? Cross-border data transfer allowed? |
| **Product Compliance** | CE/FCC/UL/INMETRO/GSO — which certifications needed? Restricted/prohibited categories? Labeling language requirements? |
| **Payment Localization** | Which payment methods cover >80% of target consumers? Currency pricing strategy (local vs. USD)? FX risk management? |
| **IP Protection** | Trademark registered in target market? Anti-counterfeit mechanisms on platform? Platform infringement complaint process? |
| **Logistics & Returns** | Local warehousing or cross-border shipping? Last-mile partner SLA? Returns policy compliance with local consumer law? |
