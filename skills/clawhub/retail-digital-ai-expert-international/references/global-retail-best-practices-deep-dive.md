## 1. Walmart -- AI-Native Transformation at the World's Largest Retailer

### 1.1 Overview
- **Headquarters**: Bentonville, Arkansas, USA
- **Scale**: 10,500+ stores (19 countries), 2.3M+ associates
- **FY2025 Revenue**: $648B
- **Digital Sales Share**: ~18% (US market)
- **IT/Tech Investment**: $15-20B/year (including CapEx)
- **AI Strategy Publicly Disclosed**: Systematically shared since 2023

### 1.2 Core Technology Architecture

```
Walmart Global Tech Platform
+-- Unified Agentic AI Framework (launched 2025)
|   +-- Sparky -- Consumer AI shopping assistant
|   +-- Marty -- Supplier/procurement AI assistant
|   +-- Associate -- Employee AI assistant
|   +-- Developer -- Internal developer AI assistant
+-- Walmart Commerce Platform
|   +-- Online (Walmart.com + App)
|   +-- In-store (POS + SCO + Scan&Go)
|   +-- Fulfillment (WFS + Ship-from-Store + Curbside Pickup)
+-- Walmart Data Platform
|   +-- Real-time data lake (global stores + online + supply chain)
|   +-- AI model platform (unified training + deployment)
|   +-- Internal AI developer platform (2025+)
+-- Walmart Cloud Native Platform
    +-- Hybrid cloud (on-prem DC + public cloud)
    +-- Edge AI (in-store local inference)
    +-- API Gateway + Microservices
```

### 1.3 AI Application Depth

| AI Scenario | Deployment Scale | Technical Approach | Results |
|------|------|------|------|
| AI Shopping Assistant (Sparky) | US-wide online + App | GenAI + RAG + real-time inventory | AI interactions +210% |
| Supplier AI (Marty) | Global suppliers | AI negotiation + price optimization + risk alerts | Procurement efficiency significantly improved |
| AI Demand Forecasting | All categories / all stores | Time-series forecasting + ML + multi-factor | Continuous inventory optimization |
| AI Pricing | Online dynamic pricing | Competitor monitoring + demand elasticity + ML | Competitive price leadership |
| AI Inventory Optimization | Omnichannel inventory | Multi-echelon inventory ML + simulation | Out-of-stock rate continuously declining |
| AI Visual Loss Prevention | Piloting in select stores | CV + behavior recognition | Shrink rate declining |
| Employee AI Assistant | Rolling out to all US stores | LLM + knowledge base + task management | Employee productivity significantly improved |

### 1.4 Key Metrics
- AI interactions increased 210% (2024 vs 2023)
- Global digital sales: $462B
- Marketplace third-party seller GMV growing rapidly
- Store SCO penetration continuously rising

### 1.5 Key Takeaways
1. **Unified AI Framework**: Do not build AI for each scenario in isolation -- have a unified platform and agent framework.
2. **Four Agent Categories**: Complete coverage across consumers, suppliers, employees, and developers.
3. **AI Must Be Tied to Business KPIs**: Every AI investment at Walmart has clear business metrics.
4. **Think Big, Start Small**: Begin with shopping assistants and forecasting, then expand gradually.

## 3. Costco -- AI Evolution in Membership Retail

### 3.1 Overview
- **Scale**: 870+ warehouse clubs (14 countries)
- **FY2024 Revenue**: $254B
- **Membership**: 130M+ cardholders
- **Membership Renewal Rate**: >90% (US/Canada)
- **SKU Count**: ~4,000 per warehouse (radically minimal SKU strategy)
- **Gross Margin**: ~11% (extremely thin product margin)

### 3.2 Digital & AI Strategy

| Domain | Strategy | Description |
|------|------|------|
| **Membership AI** | AI member analytics + renewal prediction + personalization | Renewal rate >90%, members contribute 73% of sales |
| **Supply Chain AI** | Demand forecasting + auto-replenishment + route optimization | Minimal SKUs make AI predictions more accurate |
| **Pricing AI** | Cost-plus + market benchmarking | Maintains price competitiveness |
| **E-commerce** | Costco.com + mobile app | Online penetration steadily increasing |
| **Store Technology** | SCO self-checkout + AI membership authentication | Improves checkout efficiency |
| **Data Strategy** | Membership data is the core asset | Conservative but high quality |

### 3.3 Key Metrics
- Membership fee revenue accounts for >50% of operating profit -- members are more profitable than merchandise.
- Radically minimal SKU count (4,000 vs. traditional supermarket 30,000) -- AI predictions are more accurate.
- Among the fastest inventory turnover rates in the industry (~12 days).

### 3.4 Key Takeaways
1. Less is more: fewer SKUs leads to higher data quality and more accurate AI predictions.
2. Membership is the foundation of digital transformation -- you must know who is buying before AI can work.
3. Extreme operational efficiency does not require the flashiest technology.
4. Costco did not rush into AI vision or cashierless checkout -- they got the fundamentals right first.

## 4. 7-Eleven Japan -- Global Benchmark for Convenience Store Digitalization

### 4.1 Overview
- **Japan Stores**: 21,000+
- **Global Stores**: 85,000+ (19 countries)
- **Core Model**: Franchise system (Japan franchise rate >95%)
- **Daily Deliveries**: 3-4 times/day (select stores)
- **Single-Item Management**: Radical single-SKU management culture

### 4.2 Digitalization Framework

| System | Generation | Characteristics |
|------|:---:|------|
| POS System | 7th Gen | Single-item management + real-time data + AI-assisted |
| Ordering Terminal | 7th Gen | Store-autonomous ordering + AI recommendations |
| ISDN/Satellite Communication | Continuously Upgraded | HQ <-> Store real-time data |
| Logistics System | Hour-level precision | 3-4 daily deliveries, 6 temperature zone classifications |
| AI Assortment | Since 2018 | AI-assisted store ordering recommendations |
| Weather AI | Long-standing | Weather-sales correlation (unique to convenience stores) |
| Delivery AI | 2024+ | AI route optimization + joint distribution |

### 4.3 Core Methodology: Single-Item Management
- **Not an IT technique -- a management philosophy**: Every single SKU's ordering, sales, and waste is tracked down to the unit.
- **Store-autonomous ordering**: Store staff decide what to order tomorrow based on data + AI recommendations.
- **Hypothesis -> Verification**: Each day's orders = hypothesis; evening waste rate check = verification.
- **Results**: Fast inventory turnover (3-4 daily deliveries), extremely low waste rates.

### 4.4 Key Takeaways
1. Digitalization is not the head office's surveillance eye -- it exists to make stores more capable.
2. Single-item management is the soul of convenience store digitalization.
3. The core of franchise digitalization is "enablement," not "control."
4. Weather data + sales correlation is the convenience store's secret weapon.
