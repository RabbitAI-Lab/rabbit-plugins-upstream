# Case 01: Mom-and-Pop Convenience Store Digitalization from 0 to 1

> **Format**: Neighborhood mom-and-pop convenience store
> **Scale**: 1 store, 80 sqm, daily revenue $400
> **Initial State**: L1.0 (Manual — paper ledger + standalone cash register + no online presence)
> **Target State**: L1.5 (Basic digital — cloud POS + delivery + mobile payment + simple membership)
> **Investment**: $350/year
> **Results**: Revenue +25%, Profit +35%, saves 1 hour of bookkeeping per day

---

## 1. Customer Profile

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Owner | Mr. Wang, 45, husband-and-wife operation, no IT experience |
| Store | Neighborhood street-side shop, surrounded by 3 residential communities (~2,000 households) |
| Categories | Tobacco, beverages, snacks, daily essentials, ~800 SKUs |
| Daily Transactions | ~100 transactions, average ticket $4.00 |
| Monthly Revenue | ~$12,000 |
| Monthly Gross Profit | ~$3,000 (margin ~25%) |
| Current Systems | One aging standalone cash register + paper ledger |
| Online Presence | None |

## 2. Core Pain Points

1. **Time-consuming bookkeeping**: 30-40 minutes every night reconciling accounts, frequent discrepancies
2. **Inventory blind spots**: No visibility into bestsellers vs. slow movers; expired products discovered too late
3. **Declining foot traffic**: New community group-buy pickup points nearby diverting customers
4. **Customer churn**: No membership system; no way to identify frequent vs. lapsed customers
5. **Cash handling risk**: Manual cash transactions prone to errors, change hassles, counterfeit risk

## 5. Key Success Factors

1. **Choose the simplest tool**: Not the one with the most features, but the one anyone can figure out instantly (Square POS is as intuitive as a smartphone)
2. **Delivery first**: The fastest incremental channel with the quickest payoff (no additional upfront investment)
3. **Don't chase perfect inventory**: 800 SKUs don't need granular management — manage the top 200 first
4. **WhatsApp group is the best CRM**: Zero cost, instant reach, and keeps the personal touch alive

## 6. Pitfalls to Avoid

1. Do NOT let vendors upsell you a full ERP system — one store doesn't need it
2. Do NOT keep using a standalone POS to save money — offline data will never reconcile
3. Do NOT over-engineer the loyalty program — points + stored value is enough
4. DO invest in good product photos for delivery platforms — they are your online "storefront"
5. DO spend 5 minutes reviewing daily reports — infinitely better than 30 minutes of manual reconciliation

## 8. Next Steps

- If opening a 2nd store -> upgrade to Lightspeed Retail or Shopify POS (multi-store management)
- If delivery exceeds 30% of revenue -> consider a branded mobile app (reduce platform commissions)
- If loyalty members exceed 500 -> consider WhatsApp Business API (1:1 personalized outreach)

---

> **Case Insight**: For a mom-and-pop shop, digitalization isn't about "spending big on systems" — it's about "spending small to solve the most painful problems." A $350/year investment yielding $7,750/year in benefits is the highest-ROI scenario in retail digitalization.
