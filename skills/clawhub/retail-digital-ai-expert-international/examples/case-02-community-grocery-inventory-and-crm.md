# Case 02: Community Grocery Store Inventory Management and Private CRM

> **Format**: Community fresh grocery supermarket
> **Scale**: 3 stores, each 150-200 sqm, daily revenue $2,100/store
> **Initial State**: L1.5 (Basic POS but chaotic inventory, no loyalty system, no online presence)
> **Target State**: L2.5 (Integrated inventory + loyalty system + private community + mini-program/app)
> **Investment**: $7,000/year
> **Results**: Spoilage rate dropped from 8% to 3%, 30-day repeat purchase rate rose from 15% to 28%

## 2. Core Pain Points

1. **Persistently high spoilage**: Fresh produce spoilage rate ~8%, monthly loss ~$3,300
2. **Inventory tracked but not managed**: Basic POS exists but inventory accuracy <80%
3. **No loyalty system**: Regulars recognized by face, new customers forgotten, no repeat-purchase visibility
4. **No online presence**: Helpless during pandemic lockdowns; younger neighbors ordering from Instacart
5. **Three stores operating independently**: Siloed purchasing, inventory, and pricing across stores; inflated total costs

## 5. Key Success Factors

1. **Daily cycle count is the foundation**: The biggest enemy in fresh grocery is "roughly knowing what you have" — Category A items must be counted daily after closing (30 min)
2. **Expiry dates are the lifeline**: Record expiry upon receiving → 3-day near-expiry alert → 1-day markdown → auto-log disposal
3. **Community groups need a human touch**: Don't just blast ads — share recipes, cooking tips, weather alerts, "strawberries are especially sweet today"
4. **E-commerce creates incremental sales, not substitution**: Community group buys sell items "not normally stocked in-store" (e.g., durian, seafood gift boxes) — no cannibalization

## 6. Pitfalls to Avoid

1. Do NOT rely solely on system inventory for fresh goods — daily physical counts are non-negotiable (physical degradation is invisible to systems)
2. Do NOT use generic e-commerce platforms — fresh grocery needs weight-based pricing and pre-order support
3. Do NOT chase per-item inventory precision — managing fresh SKUs at the "crate/box" granularity is sufficient
4. DO use stored value as the best customer lock-in for grocery — "$70 bonus for $700 top-up" outperforms any ad spend

> **Case Insight**: For community grocery, the biggest digitalization value isn't "selling more" — it's "spoiling less." Dropping spoilage from 8% to 3%, in a business with ~3% net margin, is the fundamental lever for doubling net profit.
