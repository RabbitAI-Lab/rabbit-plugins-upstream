# Phase 03 — Strategy & Blueprint

> **Phase Objective**: Based on diagnostic results, develop a 3-year digital strategy, target architecture, and phased roadmap.
> **Duration**: 3-4 weeks
> **Key Deliverables**: Digital strategy document + Target architecture design + 3-year roadmap + AI strategy plan

## Phase Overview

```
Digital Strategy Formulation → Target Architecture Design → 3-Year Roadmap Development → AI Strategy Planning
         (1 week)                    (1 week)                    (1 week)                  (1 week)
```

## Core Principles

1. **Strategy aligned to business**: Digital strategy must serve business objectives, not exist in parallel
2. **Target architecture first**: First envision the "ideal state," then plan "how to get there"
3. **No skipping maturity levels**: L1 cannot jump directly to L4 — must progress through informatization → digitalization → intelligence
4. **Run / Grow / Transform**: IT investment must have a rational allocation across all three categories

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Does the digital strategy directly support the business strategy?
- [ ] Do SMART goals have quantifiable, time-bound metrics?
- [ ] Is the target architecture implementable (not just a pie-in-the-sky diagram)?
- [ ] Does the roadmap account for real-world constraints (budget / team / timeline)?
- [ ] Are the Phase 1 actions in the roadmap sufficiently specific?
