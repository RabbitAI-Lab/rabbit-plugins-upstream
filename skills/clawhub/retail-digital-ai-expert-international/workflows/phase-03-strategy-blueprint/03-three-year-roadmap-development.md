## 4. Franchise Rollout Cadence (Applicable to Franchise Chains)

### Tiered Rollout Strategy

| Franchisee Tier | Share | Rollout Timing | Strategy |
|------|:---:|------|------|
| **TOP 10% Flagship** | 10% | First batch once system is ready | Resource prioritization + co-testing + produce flagship case studies |
| **Middle 60%** | 60% | Months 3-6 after flagship success | Batch rollout + flagship testimonials + centralized procurement rebate incentives |
| **Long-tail 30%** | 30% | Months 6-12 after middle tier | Individual conversion + mandatory switch at renewal + custom coaching |

### Rollout Timeline

| Phase | Timeframe | Target | Strategy |
|------|------|:---:|------|
| Pilot | Months 1-3 | 30 flagship stores | Free hardware + dedicated support |
| Small Batch | Months 4-6 | 200 stores | 50% system fee + flagship recommendations |
| Large Batch | Months 7-9 | 600 stores | Standard pricing + procurement rebates |
| Full Coverage | Months 10-12 | All stores | Mandatory switch at contract renewal |

## 6. Common Pitfalls

1. **Too many Year 1 projects**: 6 projects running simultaneously → insufficient resources, all half-done → focus on 3-4 key projects
2. **Ignoring dependencies**: AI recommendations depend on CDP, CDP depends on master data → must build the foundation first
3. **No buffer**: Project plans packed without slack → one delay cascades to everything
4. **Franchise rollout too aggressive**: Pushing before the system is stable → collective franchisee resistance
