# 01 — Digital Strategy Formulation

> **Trigger**: Diagnostic assessment completed, entering strategy formulation phase
> **Deliverables**: One-page digital strategy + SMART goal framework + Run/Grow/Transform investment allocation

---

## 1. Five-Step Strategy Formulation

```
Step 1: Vision Alignment → Step 2: Current vs. Target Gap → Step 3: Strategic Pillar Design →
Step 4: SMART Goal Setting → Step 5: Investment Portfolio (Run/Grow/Transform)
```

### Step 1: Vision Alignment

The digital vision must answer three questions:

| Question | Example |
|------|------|
| What does digitalization mean to us? | "By 2028, digital becomes [Company]'s second growth engine" |
| What value will digitalization create? | "Omnichannel sales exceed 30% of total revenue; AI drives 80% of replenishment decisions" |
| What changes will customers / employees / franchisees feel? | "Customers enjoy a consistent experience across all channels; staff learn the new system in 30 minutes" |

### Step 2: Current vs. Target Gap

```
Current Maturity: L2.4 (assessment result)
         ↓
Target Maturity: L3.5 (within 18 months)
         ↓
Key Gaps:
  1. Inventory accuracy 82% → 95% (gap: -13%)
  2. Member identification rate 30% → 85% (gap: -55%)
  3. Omnichannel not integrated → unified inventory + unified orders
  4. Zero AI applications → at least 2 AI use cases live
```

### Step 3: Strategic Pillar Design

Typical 3-5 strategic pillars:

```
Strategic Pillar 1: Unified Omnichannel Commerce
  → Online-offline inventory / orders / members — three unifications

Strategic Pillar 2: Data-Driven Operations
  → BI dashboards covering 80% of decision scenarios

Strategic Pillar 3: AI-Accelerated Intelligence
  → AI customer service + AI recommendations + AI forecasting go live

Strategic Pillar 4: Organizational Digitalization
  → Build digital team + training system
```

## 2. SMART Goal Framework

### Goal Design Principles

| Principle | Example (Good) | Counter-Example (Poor) |
|------|------|------|
| **S**pecific | "Omnichannel sales share grows from 5% to 20%" | "Strengthen online sales" |
| **M**easurable | "Inventory turnover days drop from 45 to 35" | "Improve efficiency" |
| **A**ttainable | Set reasonable targets based on industry benchmarks | "Full AI deployment in 6 months" |
| **R**elevant | Tied to business metrics | "Increase API call volume" |
| **T**ime-bound | "By Q2 2027" | "As soon as possible" |

### Goal Card Template

| Goal | Baseline | 6 Months | 12 Months | 18 Months | Measurement |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|------|
| R-DMM Maturity | L2.4 | L2.8 | L3.2 | L3.5 | Five-dimension assessment |
| Inventory Accuracy | 82% | 90% | 93% | 95% | Spot-check counts |
| Omnichannel Sales Share | 0% | 5% | 12% | 20% | System data |
| Member Identification Rate | 30% | 50% | 70% | 85% | CDP |
| AI Use Cases Live | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Go-live reports |

## 3. Run / Grow / Transform Investment Allocation

### Concept Definitions

| Category | Definition | Typical Retail Projects |
|------|------|------|
| **Run** (Operations) | Keep existing systems running, fix issues | POS maintenance, ERP annual fees, hardware warranty |
| **Grow** (Growth) | Expand existing capabilities, support business growth | New store system deployment, omnichannel go-live |
| **Transform** (Innovation) | Explore new models, technologies, capabilities | AI deployment, data platform, in-house platform development |

### Allocation Guide by Maturity Level

| R-DMM Level | Run | Grow | Transform | Notes |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|------|
| L1 → L2 | 40% | 55% | 5% | Growth-focused — get systems in place first |
| L2 → L3 | 45% | 40% | 15% | Integration-focused — start exploring AI |
| L3 → L4 | 50% | 30% | 20% | Intelligence-focused — AI investment increases |
| L4 → L5 | 40% | 25% | 35% | Transformation-focused — heavy AI + innovation investment |

## 4. Strategy Communication Techniques

### Presenting Strategy to the CEO (15-minute version)

```
3 min:  Industry benchmarking — "Competitors are already at L3.5; we're at L2.4"
5 min:  Target blueprint — "Here's what a digital enterprise looks like in 18 months"
3 min:  Roadmap — "Three phases; Phase 1 is the most urgent"
3 min:  Investment & return — "3-year investment of $[X]K, return of $[X]K"
1 min:  What we need from you — "These 3 things only you can drive"
```

### Presenting Strategy to Senior Management (45-minute version)

Expand on each strategic pillar. For each, cover:
- Why (industry trends + business drivers)
- What (specific projects)
- How (implementation path)
- Investment and return

---

## 5. Common Pitfalls

1. **Strategy disconnected from business**: The digital strategy has nothing to do with the company's business strategy
2. **Goals too big and vague**: "Become an industry digital benchmark" → reframe as "Omnichannel sales reach 20% of total within 3 years"
3. **One-shot fantasy**: Planning to jump directly from L1 to L5 → must phase it
4. **Ignoring organizational readiness**: Planning AI without an AI team → must plan people + systems in parallel
