# 01 — Research Preparation & Execution

> **Trigger**: Project kickoff (post-contract or pre-study phase)
> **Deliverables**: Research plan + interview notes + document collection checklist + store visit records

---

## 1. Pre-Research Preparation (3-5 days post-contract)

### 1.1 Document Collection Checklist (Send to Client in Advance)

```
□ Organization chart
□ Store list (address / area / headcount / opening date)
□ Current systems inventory (system name / version / go-live year / coverage scope)
□ System architecture diagram (if available)
□ 12-24 months financial data (revenue / gross margin / net profit / cost details)
□ 12-24 months operational data (foot traffic / ATV / conversion rate / inventory turnover / shrinkage rate)
□ Member data (member count / repurchase rate / stored value / churn rate)
□ IT budget and actual spend (past 2 years + current year plan)
□ Existing digital project list / IT roadmap (if available)
□ IT roles and responsibilities within the organization
```

### 1.2 Role Interview Plan

| Role | Count | Duration | Core Question Direction |
|------|:---:|:---:|------|
| **CEO / Owner** | 1 | 90 min | Strategy, vision, pain perception, budget willingness |
| **COO / Operations Lead** | 1 | 90 min | Operational processes, store management, performance mechanisms |
| **CTO / IT Lead** | 1 | 90 min | System current state, technical debt, team capabilities |
| **Store Manager (2-3 different stores)** | 2-3 | 60 min each | Daily operations, system usage experience, biggest pain points |
| **Sales Associate / Cashier** | 2-3 | 30 min each | POS experience, member operations, training status |
| **Procurement / Supply Chain Lead** | 1 | 60 min | Supplier management, procurement process, inventory management |
| **Finance Lead** | 1 | 60 min | Reconciliation process, cost accounting, IT expenditure |

## 2. Interview Guides by Role

### CEO / Owner (90 minutes)

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | "What do you envision for your company 3 years from now?" | Vision and ambition level |
| 2 | "What operational issues keep you up at night right now?" | Core pain points |
| 3 | "What does 'digitalization' mean to you?" | Digital awareness level |
| 4 | "What happens if your competitors go fully digital and you don't?" | Sense of urgency |
| 5 | "How much are you willing to invest in digital? Do you have a budget range in mind?" | Budget willingness |
| 6 | "Have you spent money on digital before? How did it go?" | Past experience (good / bad) |
| 7 | "Who in your company should be responsible for digitalization?" | Organizational readiness |
| 8 | "If this project succeeds, what does it mean for you personally?" | Personal motivation |

### Store Manager (60 minutes)

| # | Question |
|---|------|
| 1 | "Walk me through how your day starts. What are the 3 most time-consuming things?" |
| 2 | "Is the system you use now easy to work with? Anything you particularly want to vent about?" |
| 3 | "Is inventory accurate? How often do you count? How long does a count take?" |
| 4 | "How do you know what's selling well and what's not?" |
| 5 | "What tasks from HQ do you feel are unnecessary?" |
| 6 | "How are your staff with the system? Anyone struggling?" |
| 7 | "If there were a new system, what's the ONE thing you'd want it to solve?" |

### Sales Associate / Cashier (30 minutes)

| # | Question |
|---|------|
| 1 | "How many steps to check out / serve one customer? Does it feel smooth?" |
| 2 | "Is it easy to check inventory? (Have them demonstrate on the spot)" |
| 3 | "When was your last training? Do you still remember how to use everything?" |
| 4 | "What do customers ask you most often? Is it easy to answer?" |
| 5 | "Has the system made your work faster or slower?" |

### CTO / IT Lead (90 minutes)

| # | Question |
|---|------|
| 1 | "What's your current system inventory and tech stack? Which are built in-house vs. purchased?" |
| 2 | "How are systems integrated? To what degree?" |
| 3 | "What's your biggest technical debt?" |
| 4 | "Team size and skill composition? Any AI / data capabilities?" |
| 5 | "Which system do you most want to replace? Why hasn't it happened yet?" |
| 6 | "Current state of data governance? Do you have a data dictionary / master data standards?" |
| 7 | "What's in your IT plan for the next 12 months?" |

## 3. Store Visit Guide

### Visit Objectives

- Verify actual system usage (what the system shows vs. actual use)
- Discover problems not reflected in the system (physical process breakdowns)
- Gauge frontline employee sentiment and attitude

### Observation Points

| What to Observe | What to Look For | What to Ask |
|------|------|------|
| Checkout counter | POS model / speed / number of steps | "How many button presses to ring up one customer?" |
| Sales floor | Are price tags accurate? Any out-of-stock tags? | "How do you know when something is out of stock?" |
| Warehouse / back room | Inventory organization / bin location labels | "Is it easy to find items?" (follow a staff member as they locate an item) |
| Receiving area | Receiving process / handheld scanner usage | "What's the process when goods arrive?" |
| Member operations | Are staff adding customers on WhatsApp? Community engagement? | "How many customers have you connected with on WhatsApp?" |
| Staff demeanor | Expressions / efficiency / morale | "Have you been busy lately? Tired?" |

## 4. Common Pitfalls

1. **Client gives "standard answers"**: Owner says "we have strong digital awareness" → visit the store to see real usage
2. **Only interviewing management**: What the store manager says and what the cashier says are two different truths
3. **Over-scheduling**: Maximum 4-5 interviews per day; beyond that, information quality degrades
4. **Arriving with pre-formed solutions**: Research is for discovering problems — don't come with the answer already decided
