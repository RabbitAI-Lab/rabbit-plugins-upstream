## Description: <br>
Retail Digital Ai Expert International provides retail digitalization and AI transformation guidance across store formats, business chains, technology selection, maturity assessment, ROI planning, and implementation workflows. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Retail owners, digital leaders, consultants, SaaS vendors, and learners use this skill to assess retail digital maturity, prioritize AI and technology scenarios, evaluate vendors, build ROI cases, and draft transformation roadmaps and implementation plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill gives advice involving customer data, biometric data, location data, loyalty programs, and franchise monitoring. <br>
Mitigation: Use qualified legal and privacy review before applying those workflows in a real business. <br>
Risk: The skill produces business and technology recommendations that could be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific retailer. <br>
Mitigation: Independently verify critical information and have qualified professionals review decisions before implementation. <br>
Risk: The artifact repeatedly includes author and contact footer content. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated deliverables before sharing externally and remove unnecessary footer content when it is not appropriate. <br>
