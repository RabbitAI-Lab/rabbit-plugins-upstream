## Description: <br>
Builds UK incident and PCN evidence packs with timelines, appeal drafts, corrective actions, and follow-up monitoring. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kowl64](https://clawhub.ai/user/kowl64) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Operations, compliance, and fleet teams use this skill to assemble factual UK PCN appeal or internal incident packs from supplied case details, evidence lists, deadlines, and policy excerpts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
United Kingdom <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated appeal or incident documents may include incorrect factual claims when inputs are incomplete, weak, or contradictory. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated packs against the source evidence, mark weak or contradictory evidence as unconfirmed, and fill gaps before submission. <br>
Risk: PCN and incident evidence can include personal data about drivers, employees, vehicles, locations, and case events. <br>
Mitigation: Include only personal data needed for the case and store evidence in controlled locations with source, timestamp, and integrity notes. <br>
Risk: PCN appeal drafting is legal-adjacent and may affect business or employee outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Keep submissions factual, cite evidence references, avoid speculative claims, and have a responsible reviewer approve final documents before use. <br>
