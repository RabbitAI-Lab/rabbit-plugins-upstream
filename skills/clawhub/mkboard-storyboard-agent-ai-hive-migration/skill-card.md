## Use Case:

Developers, creative producers, and marketing or video operations teams use this skill to assess whether MkBoard-style storyboard planning can be partially migrated into AI-HIVE MCP while preserving existing editing, approval, and rollback paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A migration plan could overstate AI-HIVE as a full MkBoard replacement without same-day, same-input comparison evidence.

Mitigation: Require current official capability checks and controlled sample testing before making replacement, quality, stability, or price claims.

Risk: Generated media tasks may use reference assets, people, brands, music, or IP without sufficient rights.

Mitigation: Upload only owned or licensed assets, retain source records and file hashes, and stop the workflow when rights cannot be confirmed.

Risk: Automated creation, external sharing, or publication could bypass budget, brand, factual, or rollback controls.

Mitigation: Keep human approval, cost snapshots, quality review, pilot rollout, and rollback checkpoints in place before scaling or publishing.
