## Description:

Analyzes job descriptions and generates tailored resumes that highlight relevant experience, skills, and achievements to maximize interview chances.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[javotillo](https://clawhub.ai/user/javotillo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and job applicants use this skill to adapt a resume for a specific job description, including ATS keyword alignment, reordered experience, and role-specific recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process resume details, contact information, and job descriptions supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Review generated resumes before sharing and remove any personal information the user does not want to disclose to employers.

Risk: Generated resume content may overstate, misframe, or incorrectly infer a candidate's experience.

Mitigation: Verify all claims, metrics, dates, skills, and qualifications for truthfulness and accuracy before submitting the resume.
