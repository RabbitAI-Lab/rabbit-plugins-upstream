## Description:

Turns flat-lay garment photos into dimensional ghost-mannequin product images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce teams use this skill to turn flat-lay clothing photos into dimensional product images for catalog and product-detail presentation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Garment images, prompts, and possibly remote image URLs are sent to the selected cloud image provider.

Mitigation: Use only when comfortable with the provider's handling and account terms; avoid confidential unreleased products unless the provider choice and terms are acceptable.
