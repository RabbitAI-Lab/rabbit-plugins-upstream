## Description: <br>
Measures how a brand narrative is landing by producing a resonance report with echo rate, AI-answer perception, locked-panel share of voice, and public resonance signals. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, narrative, and brand teams use this skill to evaluate whether market language, answer-engine descriptions, and public attention signals align with a declared canon lexicon and competitor panel. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Ambiguous canon, competitor, or analytics inputs could produce a misleading resonance read. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the canon lexicon, locked competitor panel, and analytics exports before using the skill. <br>
Risk: Saved reports or registry proposals could persist incorrect or unsupported narrative claims. <br>
Mitigation: Review any requested save or registry proposal before approving it, and require unsupported statements to stay marked as needing a source. <br>
Risk: Proxy public signals could be mistaken for measured brand metrics. <br>
Mitigation: Keep AI-answer, GDELT, Bluesky, and similar public-signal outputs labeled as proxy unless the user supplies measured evidence. <br>
