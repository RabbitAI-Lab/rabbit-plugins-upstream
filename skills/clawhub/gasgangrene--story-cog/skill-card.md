## Use Case: <br>
Writers, creators, and agent users use Story Cog to generate and iterate on short fiction, novel outlines, screenplays, fan fiction, world-building material, character profiles, and narrative prompts with CellCog. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Story prompts and included draft text may be sent to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, private, or rights-sensitive material unless third-party processing by CellCog is acceptable. <br>
Risk: CELLCOG_API_KEY is required for use. <br>
Mitigation: Store the API key as a protected environment secret, avoid committing it to files, and rotate it if exposed. <br>
Risk: Generated creative text may be inaccurate, unsuitable, or inconsistent with the user's intended tone or rights constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit outputs before publication or incorporation into production creative work. <br>
