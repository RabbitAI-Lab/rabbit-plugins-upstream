## Description: <br>
Designs low-pressure, genuinely useful customer relationship maintenance approaches when there is no specific inquiry or active project. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[zaynpeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaynpeng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales and customer-facing users use this skill to decide whether and how to maintain a customer relationship when there is no active inquiry or project. It helps structure the needed context, assess whether contact is appropriate, identify a real value point, and draft a low-pressure outreach message. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Draft outreach could use inappropriate or unapproved customer context. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only approved customer context and review every message before sending. <br>
Risk: Relationship maintenance could become pressure selling, repeated outreach, or contact without new value. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's required risk checks and send only when there is a real value point, appropriate timing, and a clear reason to contact. <br>
Risk: The artifact is still waiting for real anonymized test cases and no-contact examples. <br>
Mitigation: Validate the skill with real anonymized cases and add explicit no-contact examples before relying on it for repeated customer workflows. <br>
