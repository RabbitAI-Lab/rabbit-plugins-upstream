## Description: <br>
Helps developers, support teams, and users turn vague application errors into clearer messages that explain what failed, why it failed, and what action to take next. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Application developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and end users use this skill to rewrite or systematize unclear error messages into actionable feedback. It can produce local-hardware-friendly templates, checklists, workflow notes, analysis, code suggestions, or decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill during general debugging or support conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or tighter routing settings where available, and confirm the task is about improving error messages before applying the workflow. <br>
