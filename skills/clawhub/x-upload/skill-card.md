## Use Case:

External users can prepare and publish text, image, or short-video posts to X through EasyPost using an active Chrome session.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post could be published from the wrong active X account or with unintended text or media.

Mitigation: Before publishing, check the active X account, post text, and selected media; use a trusted installation because EasyPost operates through the logged-in session.
