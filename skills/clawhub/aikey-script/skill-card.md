## Description:

Collaboratively develops Chinese-language talking-head scripts by choosing a format, assembling source material, and drafting section by section.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking independent creators use this skill to co-write knowledge-focused video scripts from their own notes and experiences, approving the outline and draft sections as they go.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic write-back can change local drafts, writing rules, and skill memory without a separate confirmation.

Mitigation: Review the write-back behavior and back up the local content vault; require confirmation for persistent changes if preferred.
