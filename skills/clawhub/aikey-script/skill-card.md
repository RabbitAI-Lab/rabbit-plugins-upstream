## Description:

Collaboratively develops talking-head video scripts by choosing a structure, assembling source material, and drafting one section at a time with user feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators use this skill to co-write knowledge-focused talking-head scripts from existing content and real experiences, reviewing the outline, hooks, and each section before finalizing a draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read broad local content and memory stores without clear opt-in.

Mitigation: Review configured vault paths and limit access to material you intend to use; avoid confidential client content unless this access is acceptable.

Risk: Drafts, feedback, cases, and writing preferences may be written back to local repositories.

Mitigation: Confirm write-back locations and approve local persistence before using the skill with sensitive material.
