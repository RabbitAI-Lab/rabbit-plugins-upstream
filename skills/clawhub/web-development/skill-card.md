## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authentication examples may treat an unverified token as an authenticated session for protected routes.

Mitigation: Require real server-side token validation before granting access to protected resources.

Risk: Bearer-token cookies may be readable by client-side code.

Mitigation: Avoid client-readable bearer-token cookies unless the application deliberately accepts and mitigates that exposure.
