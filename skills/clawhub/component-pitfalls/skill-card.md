## Description:

A static guide that helps electronics buyers assess supplier and counterfeit risks, verify components, avoid common module pitfalls, find substitutions, and follow mains and lithium battery safety guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liujycode](https://clawhub.ai/user/liujycode)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External electronics DIY users, makers, hardware engineers, and students use the guide before purchasing components to compare supplier channels, check authenticity, assess common module pitfalls, and identify substitutions. It also provides precautions for mains and lithium battery work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports incomplete evidence because it could not match a local path referenced by SKILL.md.

Mitigation: Check whether the missing local reference matters before relying on guidance that depends on it.

Risk: Component guidance and substitution choices may not fit every part or application, and the skill covers mains and lithium battery work.

Mitigation: Verify hardware-specific values against the official datasheet and follow the stated safety precautions, including disconnecting power for wiring and beginning with low-voltage tests.
