## Description: <br>
基于上半年目标、月报、周报、项目记录和业务结果，复盘半年目标达成、阶段性成果、结构性问题、资源配置、机会质量、能力短板和下半年策略，并生成适合年中复盘或管理汇报的结构化年中报告。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business users, managers, and report writers use this Chinese-language skill to turn semiannual goals, monthly or weekly reports, project records, business results, resource inputs, blockers, and support needs into a structured midyear review and second-half strategy. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste confidential business records while preparing a midyear review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only in an agent environment approved for those records, and avoid entering confidential data unless that handling is acceptable. <br>
Risk: Incomplete or unreliable business inputs can lead to misleading rates, trends, or conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Require clear metric periods, definitions, and source data before calculating rates or trends; otherwise mark the data as insufficient. <br>
Risk: Chinese-language output may be misunderstood by reviewers who cannot assess the generated report. <br>
Mitigation: Have a reviewer who can read Chinese validate the business conclusions, risks, and requested management decisions before use. <br>
