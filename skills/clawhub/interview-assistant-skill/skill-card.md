## Description: <br>
腾讯会议面试助手 helps agents use Tencent Meeting contact, transcript, recording-summary, or local interview files to prepare interview outlines, capture key signals, and produce structured five-dimension candidate evaluations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golgys0621](https://clawhub.ai/user/golgys0621) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Recruiters, hiring managers, and interview teams use this skill to prepare candidate profiles and structured interview questions before an interview, then turn Tencent Meeting notes or local interview records into a traceable evaluation table with human-reviewed hiring recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Interview workflows may involve candidate personal information, Tencent Meeting contacts, meeting notes, recordings, or transcripts. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only with authorized Tencent Meeting access and confirm the candidate, meeting, resume, job description, and notes before processing or sharing outputs. <br>
Risk: Generated scores and hiring recommendations can be incomplete or misleading if the interview record lacks evidence. <br>
Mitigation: Treat generated evaluations as decision support, keep missing evidence marked for verification, and require a human hiring reviewer to make final decisions. <br>
