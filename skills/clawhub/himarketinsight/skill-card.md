## Description: <br>
HiMarketInsight is a market and strategy analysis skill that applies Huawei Five Looks and Three Decisions, 518 management models, and market research frameworks to industry research, customer insight, competitive intelligence, opportunity assessment, and strategic planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, employees, and business teams use this skill to structure market research, competitive intelligence, customer insight, opportunity assessment, and strategy planning into evidence-backed reports and recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill requests shell execution and broad file modification capabilities without explaining when they are needed for normal market analysis workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Review before installing, limit command execution, and restrict file writes to explicit report export paths. <br>
Risk: Market and strategy recommendations may be misleading if source data is incomplete, unverified, or outside the skill's stated scope. <br>
Mitigation: Require cited sources, confidence labels, and human review before using recommendations for business decisions. <br>
Risk: Web and WeChat article search can introduce untrusted, promotional, or outdated source material. <br>
Mitigation: Cross-check source dates and authority, and separate confirmed facts from assumptions. <br>
