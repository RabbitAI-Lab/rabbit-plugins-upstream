## Description: <br>
Read your marketing claims, landing page, or ad copy the way a consumer-protection investigator would (FTC/ASA framing) and draft the inquiry letter they could send. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, product, legal, and compliance reviewers use this skill to examine claims in landing pages, ads, emails, and listings through an adversarial consumer-protection lens. It produces a claim inventory, substantiation demands, a simulated inquiry letter, and per-claim keep, reword, disclose, or drop guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can produce compliance-oriented conclusions that may be mistaken for legal advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as a simulation and confirm load-bearing decisions with qualified advertising-law counsel. <br>
Risk: Users may paste confidential marketing evidence or substantiation materials into the agent environment. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business evidence unless the agent environment is approved for that data. <br>
