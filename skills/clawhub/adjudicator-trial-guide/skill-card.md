## Description:

Guides judges, arbitrators, judicial assistants, and law students through Zou Bihua's Nine-Step Method for civil and commercial adjudication, trial management, fact-finding, judgment-writing checks, and judicial clarification boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External legal professionals, adjudicators, arbitrators, judicial assistants, and law students use this skill to structure civil and commercial case handling around claims, legal bases, issues, evidence, fact determination, reasoning consistency, and appropriate judicial clarification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat jurisdiction-specific legal methodology guidance as binding legal authority.

Mitigation: Verify any cited practice against current law, court rules, and professional obligations before relying on it.

Risk: Use in adjudication or arbitration can affect high-impact legal decisions.

Mitigation: Keep a human legal professional responsible for final judgment, reasoning, and procedural compliance.

Risk: Judicial clarification guidance could be misapplied in ways that favor a party or substitute for party choice.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's stated boundaries: do not choose a party's litigation path, do not favor either side, do not act as counsel, and record clarification activity.
