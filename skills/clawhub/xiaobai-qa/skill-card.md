## Description:

Helps beginners clarify vague questions about AI, work, or income through one question at a time, and drafts contextual replies for creators responding to followers.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[iamzifei](https://clawhub.ai/user/iamzifei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use the skill to turn broad questions into a specific, confirmed problem and one practical next step. Content creators use it to draft replies to followers' comments and private messages while maintaining per-person conversation records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive personal and follower conversations are stored in local records and reply files.

Mitigation: Use a private, access-controlled folder, omit unnecessary sensitive details, and periodically delete old records and drafts.

Risk: Follower-derived names can become unsafe file paths.

Mitigation: Sanitize names before creating files and ensure the resulting paths remain inside the intended records and replies folders.
