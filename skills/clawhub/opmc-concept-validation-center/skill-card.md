## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language OPC concept validation skill that guides feasibility assessment across technical, commercial, resource, team, risk, and conclusion dimensions for TRL 1-4 ideas. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, founders, and project evaluators use this skill to structure early concept validation, feasibility analysis, TRL 1-4 review, and preparation for pilot or mid-scale validation handoff. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may not expect Chinese-language output. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the desired output language before use or translate the resulting guidance for non-Chinese workflows. <br>
Risk: Broad activation wording may apply the skill to general feasibility questions where the framework is not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use narrower activation or invocation wording when many skills are installed or when general feasibility discussions are common. <br>
Risk: Concept validation guidance can be incomplete or misleading if treated as a final business, technical, or investment decision. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified domain, market, legal, and financial reviewers validate conclusions before funding, build, or handoff decisions. <br>
