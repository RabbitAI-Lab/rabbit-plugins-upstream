## Description:

Turn a business owner's public services, opening hours and FAQs into a concise phone-answering brief and blank call-note template. Use when preparing business reception information, not to answer live calls or confirm bookings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cnvxstudio](https://clawhub.ai/user/cnvxstudio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners and reception staff use this skill to prepare a concise, fact-based phone-answering brief and blank call-note template from public business information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect or outdated business facts in the brief could mislead callers.

Mitigation: Provide public business facts and have the owner review the completed brief for accuracy.

Risk: Including passwords, payment details, patient information, or real customer records could expose sensitive information unnecessarily.

Mitigation: Use public business information only; do not provide credentials, payment details, patient information, or real customer records.
