# APA 7 quick reference (generic)

## Reference list templates

| Type | Template |
|---|---|
| Journal | `Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pp-pp. https://doi.org/xx` |
| Book | `Author, A. A. (Year). Title of work: Subtitle (2nd ed.). Publisher.` |
| Chapter | `Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), Book title (pp. xx-xx). Publisher.` |
| Web page | `Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title. Site Name. URL` |
| Report | `Organization. (Year). Title of report (Report No. xxx). Publisher. URL` |
| Preprint | `Author, A. A. (Year). Title. Repository Name. https://doi.org/xx` |

## In-text

| Situation | Example |
|---|---|
| Parenthetical | (Smith, 2024) |
| With page | (Smith, 2024, p. 12) |
| Two authors | (Smith & Jones, 2024) |
| Narrative two | Smith and Jones (2024) |
| 3+ authors | (Smith et al., 2024) from first citation |
| Group author | (World Health Organization [WHO], 2024), then (WHO, 2024) |
| No date | (Smith, n.d.) |
| Multiple sources | (Smith, 2024; Jones & Lee, 2023) alphabetical, semicolon |
| Secondary source | (Eriksson, 1984, as cited in Willman, 2022, p. 203) |
| Direct quote | "..." (Smith, 2024, p. 12). |
| Block quote 40+ | indented, no quotes, citation after final punctuation |

## Structural rules

- 1-20 authors: list all, `&` before the last. 21+: first 19, `...`, final author.
- Alphabetical by lead author surname; same author+year: `2024a`, `2024b` by title order.
- Sentence case for titles; journal names in title case and italic; volume italic, issue not: `Journal Name, 12(3), 45-60.`
- DOI as full link `https://doi.org/...`; no `Retrieved from` unless a retrieval date is needed.
- No publisher location. Edition from 2nd: `(2nd ed.)`.
- `pp.` for page ranges (`pp. 707-745`), `p.` for single pages.
- Hanging indent standard; be consistent.
- List contains only sources cited in text, and every in-text citation must appear in the list.
