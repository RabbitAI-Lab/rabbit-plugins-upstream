## Description: <br>
Designs lead magnets and social-to-capture-to-nurture funnels that turn social traffic into subscribers, leads, and customers using the MAGNET framework. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, founders, and social media operators use this skill to design a focused lead magnet, the capture path that promotes it, and a value-first nurture sequence. The skill drafts funnel strategy, landing and nurture copy, and social promo posts while leaving landing pages, forms, quizzes, email automation, and metrics collection to external tools. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A user may deploy drafted funnel copy without reviewing consent, unsubscribe, tracking disclosure, data retention, or applicable marketing law requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review of consent language, unsubscribe handling, tracking disclosure, data retention, and jurisdiction-specific compliance before any real funnel launch. <br>
Risk: A user may treat the skill as a tool that builds or runs landing pages, forms, quizzes, email automation, or analytics. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for planning and copywriting; build, configure, operate, and measure the funnel in external tools with human oversight. <br>
