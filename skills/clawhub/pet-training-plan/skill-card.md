## Description:

Drafts phased pet behavior training plans with daily exercises, household rules, environment adjustments, progress tracking, and referral signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Pet owners and pet-care practitioners use this skill to draft step-by-step plans for barking, destructive behavior, jumping, and toileting problems, tailored to the pet's age, triggers, household, and available training time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General training guidance may be inadequate for aggression, self-harm, severe anxiety, medical symptoms, or other safety-critical cases.

Mitigation: Consult a veterinarian or qualified behavior professional rather than relying on the plan for these cases.

Risk: Pet and household details shared for personalization may include unnecessary personal information.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the training plan and omit identifying information.

Risk: The free-tier description also mentions enhanced Pro features, so feature availability may be unclear.

Mitigation: Confirm which capabilities are included in the free release before depending on enhanced features.
