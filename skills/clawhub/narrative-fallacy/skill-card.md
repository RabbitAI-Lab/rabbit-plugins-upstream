## Description:

Helps users examine retrospective explanations for selection bias, uncertainty, contingencies, and overconfident causal claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to help users assess business, historical, market, and post-mortem narratives before drawing causal conclusions or transferable lessons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saving real-use failure patterns in reusable instructions may retain confidential material without clear approval limits.

Mitigation: Before using confidential business material, remove that instruction or require explicit approval for each saved, anonymized lesson; check who can access the installed skill file.
