# rule-table.md (US Sales Tax)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Rules change; when citing, note "subject to the latest guidance of the state Department of Revenue."

## Structure
- No federal sales tax. 45 states + DC levy sales tax.
- No statewide sales tax (NOMAD): Alaska (local taxes only), Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, Oregon.

## Economic Nexus (post-Wayfair)
- Most states: $100,000 gross sales into the state per year (transaction-count triggers largely repealed).
- California: $500,000. Texas: $500,000. New York: $500,000 AND 100+ transactions.
- Physical nexus: inventory in FBA/3PL warehouses creates nexus in the storage state regardless of thresholds.

## Marketplace Facilitator Laws
- All 45 sales-tax states + DC have them (Missouri last, effective 2023-01-01).
- Platform collects & remits on marketplace sales; the seller's own website/direct channels remain the seller's responsibility once nexus exists.

## Rate Examples (state rate; combined incl. local)
- CA 7.25% (combined up to ~10.25%) · TX 6.25% (up to 8.25%) · NY 4% (NYC 8.875%) · FL 6% (+ county surtax)
- Most states: destination-based sourcing. Some origin/modified rules for in-state sellers (e.g. TX, AZ, IL; CA district taxes destination-based).

## Taxability Examples
- Clothing: exempt in PA; most clothing exempt in NJ; NY: items < $110 exempt (state level).
- Groceries for home consumption: generally exempt; supplements: taxable in most states.
