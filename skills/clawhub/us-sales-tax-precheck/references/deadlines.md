# deadlines.md (US Sales Tax)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; actual deadlines are assigned per state and per account — verify with the state Department of Revenue.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines
- Most states: monthly filers due the 20th of the following month.
- California: monthly filers due the last day of the following month.
- Quarterly and annual frequencies are assigned by the state based on filing volume; New York quarterly filers due the 20th after quarter end.

## Usage Notes
- Zero-returns are mandatory even with no sales — missed zero-returns trigger penalties and can block permit renewal.
- Deadlines shift for weekends/holidays; advise users to confirm in the state portal.
- Frequency reassignments happen after annual reviews; if the user's assigned frequency is unknown, mark "cannot determine."
