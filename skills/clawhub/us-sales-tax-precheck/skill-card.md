## Description:

This skill helps cross-border sellers pre-check draft sales data and platform statements across six common US sales-tax risk areas—rate and sourcing, nexus, marketplace facilitator coverage, taxability and exemptions, filing frequency, and shipping and refunds—and produces a structured risk report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[snowhola](https://clawhub.ai/user/snowhola)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Cross-border sellers use the skill to pre-check draft US sales data and marketplace statements for common sales-tax risks, then review a structured report and prioritized follow-up actions. It does not file returns or register businesses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Simplified tax rules and deadlines may not reflect current state-specific requirements, and a pre-check is not a compliance determination.

Mitigation: Confirm applicable rules and deadlines with the relevant state authority or a tax advisor before acting.

Risk: Draft sales data and platform statements may contain sensitive customer or account information.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the pre-check and redact customer identifiers and account details.
