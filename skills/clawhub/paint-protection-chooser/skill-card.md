## Description:

Choose between carnauba wax, paint sealant and ceramic coating from parking, ownership time and budget; work out renewal dates and yearly cost from the user's own prices; and decide whether the paint needs polishing first. Use when someone asks whether ceramic coating is worth it, how often to wax or reseal a car, or when their protection is due again.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Car owners use this skill to compare wax, sealant, and ceramic coating based on parking, ownership plans, and budget. It also helps estimate renewal dates and yearly costs and decide whether paint should be polished before protection is applied.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Durability, renewal timing, and yearly cost can vary with the product, application, climate, and washing habits.

Mitigation: Present durability and cost as planning estimates, use the user's own prices, and recommend checking product guidance and the paint's condition.

Risk: The optional polishing link promotes one specific service provider.

Mitigation: Present the link as an optional provider rather than an endorsement, and leave room for the user to compare local services.
