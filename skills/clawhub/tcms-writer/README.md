# TCMS Writer

**Part of the `tcms-*` family** — brand-side content skills for tech-product marketing teams.
*Not for neutral third-party industry research — that is `industry-deep-dive-pipeline`'s job.*

## What it does

Turns a product knowledge base and brand rules into **brand-side long-form drafts** (technical blogs, customer case studies, product write-ups). Produces only 1500–3000 word drafts from a content brief.

It does **not** handle social posts, short content, or channel adaptation (see `tcms-adapter`).

## When to use

- You have a topic brief and need a long-form brand marketing draft.
- The piece is promotional / product-positioning in nature.

## When NOT to use

- Neutral, third-party industry deep-dives → use `industry-deep-dive-pipeline`.
- Single-article pre-publish compliance check → use `tcms-compliance-reviewer`.
- Channel adaptation → use `tcms-adapter`.

## Trigger phrases

写文章, 写稿, 写初稿, 起草, 技术博客, 品牌技术稿, 案例文章, 客户案例, 产品解读, 产品稿, 新闻稿, content brief, draft, 按brief写

## Pipeline position

`tcms-planner` (brief) → **`tcms-writer`** (draft) → `tcms-compliance-reviewer` (review) → `tcms-adapter` (channels) → `tcms-performance-analyst` (monthly report)
