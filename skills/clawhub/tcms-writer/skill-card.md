## Description:

Drafts long-form brand-side technical blogs, customer case studies, and product updates from a content brief and product knowledge base.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tech-product marketing teams use this skill to draft 1,500–3,000-word brand-side articles from a brief and source material, with citations and a review checklist. It does not publish or adapt drafts for other channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic writing requests may trigger this brand-side workflow without sufficient context.

Mitigation: Provide a clear brief, target product, source location, and desired language before drafting.

Risk: Drafts may expose customer or internal information or contain unsupported product claims.

Mitigation: Review customer redaction, source citations, internal facts, and product claims before publication.
