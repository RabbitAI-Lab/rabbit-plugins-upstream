## Description:

For tech-product marketing teams, this skill turns a product knowledge base, brand rules, and a content brief into brand-side long-form drafts such as technical blogs, customer case studies, and product write-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tech-product marketing teams use the skill to turn a content brief and product source material into a first draft of a brand-side long-form article. It also prepares citation traceability and a pre-review checklist; the draft requires human review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The benign security result has medium confidence because two local references were unresolved; draft claims also depend on the supplied source material.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references and verify product claims and customer details against approved source material before using a draft.
