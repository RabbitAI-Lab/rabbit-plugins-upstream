## Description: <br>
For tech-product marketing teams, TCMS Writer turns a product knowledge base, brand guidelines, and a content brief into brand-side long-form drafts such as technical blogs, customer case studies, and product write-ups. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing and product teams use this skill to draft 1500-3000 word brand-side technical blogs, customer case studies, and product update articles from a content brief and approved source material. It is intended for long-form promotional product writing, not neutral industry research, short social content, or channel adaptation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may read internal product and case-study material and generate brand-facing drafts that include sensitive customer, product, or capability details. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear briefs and approved product names, keep internal customer cases redacted by default, require source traceability for data points, and send every draft through human review before publication. <br>
