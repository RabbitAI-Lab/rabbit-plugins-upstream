## Description:

For tech-product marketing teams, this skill turns a product knowledge base and content brief into brand-side long-form drafts such as blogs, case studies, and product write-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, and technical communications teams use this skill to create 1500-3000 word first drafts from a content brief, product knowledge base, brand rules, and article templates. It also prepares a citation traceability table and pre-review checklist for human review before publication or channel adaptation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing drafts may expose internal customer details or unpublished product information when source material includes confidential content.

Mitigation: Use the skill for scoped product-marketing drafts only, keep internal customer cases redacted by default, and review generated files before use.

Risk: Drafts may include unsupported product claims or unsourced metrics if the brief or knowledge base is incomplete.

Mitigation: Require a clear brief and target product, cite only data from provided source material, and confirm uncertain product capabilities during human review.
