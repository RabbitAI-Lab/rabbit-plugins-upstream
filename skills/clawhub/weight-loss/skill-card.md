## Description: <br>
Track weight loss journey with weigh-ins, trend analysis, and goal milestones. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to log weigh-ins, review smoothed weight trends, set goal milestones, and record non-scale victories while keeping health data local. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Weight history, goals, and progress notes are sensitive personal health data. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only on a trusted device or account where local storage and deletion controls are understood. <br>
