## Use Case: <br>
External and internal delivery, sales, operations, finance, and project teams use this Chinese-language skill to turn a confirmed product order, service contract, or project into an executable kickoff checklist with scope, milestones, dependencies, acceptance criteria, communication paths, and owners. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The generated kickoff checklist may contain incorrect or premature dates, owners, payment dependencies, or acceptance criteria. <br>
Mitigation: Review the checklist with accountable stakeholders before using it operationally. <br>
Risk: Users may treat advisory planning output as approval, commitment authority, or a change to commercial terms. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that responsible business owners approve commitments and commercial terms outside the skill output. <br>
