## Description: <br>
Structure a cohort analysis for retention, LTV, or behavior patterns, producing a presentation-ready framework with methodology, cohort definitions, retention curves, SQL reference, and prioritized interventions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, growth teams, and data analysts use this skill to structure cohort analyses for retention improvement, LTV modeling, behavioral segmentation, and churn detection. It helps turn supplied product metrics or schemas into a clear analysis plan, tables, SQL reference, and recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Using the skill may require sharing product analytics, revenue, retention, or user-behavior data with an agent. <br>
Mitigation: Use sanitized schemas or aggregated metrics where possible, and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive data. <br>
Risk: Generated SQL may not be safe or correct for a production warehouse without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt generated SQL before running it against production data. <br>
Risk: Outputs can contain placeholders or hypotheses when the user does not provide enough data. <br>
Mitigation: Keep unsupported values clearly marked as placeholders or hypotheses and ask for missing metrics before drawing conclusions. <br>
