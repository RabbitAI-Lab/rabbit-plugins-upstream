## Description:

Helps users prepare pre-contract negotiation and pricing strategy packages for China B2B sales, including quote tiers, negotiation scripts, concession trades, discount authority checks, and risk registers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[summerzb](https://clawhub.ai/user/summerzb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales teams and deal owners use this skill before contract terms are finalized to structure a single-opportunity negotiation package. It supports diagnosis of buyer discount motives, tiered quotation planning, BATNA/ZOPA preparation, concession exchanges, discount escalation, and risk registration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with China-market adaptations documented for RMB pricing, WeChat or WeCom communication, tendering, payment terms, and local decision-chain patterns.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Discount thresholds, approval paths, or concession scripts may conflict with a company's actual pricing authority, margin model, or legal requirements.

Mitigation: Review and adapt thresholds, escalation rules, and scripts against internal approval policies, margin requirements, and legal or compliance guidance before customer use.

Risk: China-market negotiation assumptions may be inappropriate when used in another geography, industry, language, or sales motion.

Mitigation: Localize communication channels, currency, procurement norms, payment terms, and compliance review before applying the guidance outside the documented China B2B context.

Risk: Generated negotiation guidance can be incomplete or misleading if source deal facts, ROI data, competitor pricing, or customer BATNA assumptions are missing.

Mitigation: Require human review, mark missing facts before sending customer-facing material, and validate pricing and risk assumptions with current deal evidence.
