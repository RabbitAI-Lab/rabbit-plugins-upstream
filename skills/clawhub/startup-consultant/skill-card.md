## Description:

Startup growth consulting agent specializing in Product Hunt launches, open-source marketing, and Go-to-Market audits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, startup operators, and growth teams use this skill to diagnose growth stage, segment, evidence, and constraints, then turn Product Hunt, open-source, and go-to-market reviews into prioritized two-week experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for sensitive startup metrics, positioning, launch assets, repository details, or outreach plans to tailor advice.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the review and redact confidential customer, financial, or credential data before use.

Risk: Suggested outreach, Product Hunt mobilization, or social posting plans may be inappropriate for a platform, audience, or marketing compliance context.

Mitigation: Review proposed launch and outreach actions against platform rules and applicable marketing or email requirements before acting.

Risk: Growth recommendations can overemphasize acquisition when activation or retention is weak.

Mitigation: Use the skill's stage-first diagnosis and guardrails, and pause amplification when activation or retention evidence does not support scaling.
