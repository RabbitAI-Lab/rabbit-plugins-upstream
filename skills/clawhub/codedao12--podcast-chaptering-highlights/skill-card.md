## Description:

Create chapters, highlights, and show notes from podcast audio or transcripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Podcast producers, editors, and content teams use this skill to turn audio or transcripts into chapter markers, highlight suggestions, concise show notes, and optional social post drafts without publishing or distribution actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private podcast audio or transcripts may contain sensitive personal or business information.

Mitigation: Treat audio and transcripts as private, prefer offline or local analysis, and avoid sharing raw audio outside the workspace.

Risk: Podcast excerpts, highlight clips, or show notes may be used without the necessary rights or consent.

Mitigation: Confirm rights before using excerpts and keep generated material in draft form until a human approves it.

Risk: External transcription, analysis, hosting, or publishing services can expose content or perform unintended distribution actions.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before any external transcription or hosting-related access, and do not call hosting APIs unless specifically requested and approved.
