## Description: <br>
Give structured, constructive feedback on any design using UX frameworks. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Designers, product managers, developers, and UX reviewers use this skill to critique screens, flows, components, wireframes, Figma designs, or product interfaces against UX principles. It produces prioritized issues, heuristic and Gestalt checks, Jobs-to-be-Done alignment, and actionable next steps for iteration. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Uploaded designs, screenshots, and product flows may contain sensitive product material. <br>
Mitigation: Treat provided design artifacts as potentially sensitive and only analyze the material the user intentionally supplies. <br>
Risk: Design critique can produce misleading or incorrect recommendations if the design context or user goal is incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Ask for missing context and review recommendations before using them for product decisions. <br>
