## Description:

Summarizes discussion threads on SnapVisor builds, tests, and media, and helps users respond to and manage them with confirmation before changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SnapVisor users and reviewers use this skill to summarize build, test, and media discussion threads and, after confirmation, post replies, manage comments and reactions, resolve threads, or follow builds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posting or editing a comment may send incorrect text to the wrong discussion thread.

Mitigation: Review the draft and target thread, then explicitly confirm before posting or editing.

Risk: Deleting comments, changing reactions or thread status, or following and unfollowing builds can alter collaboration history or notifications.

Mitigation: Check the target item and intended action before confirming; edit or delete only your own comments.
