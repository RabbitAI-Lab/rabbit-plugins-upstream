## Description:

Tailors a user's resume to a target role, highlighting relevant achievements and identifying gaps without inventing experience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[petehuax](https://clawhub.ai/user/petehuax)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to revise an existing resume for a target position, compare their experience with job requirements, and identify details they should supply or verify.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resumes and job descriptions may contain sensitive personal or employment details.

Mitigation: Share only details you are comfortable providing to the agent.

Risk: Suggested metrics or job-fit scores may be mistaken for verified facts.

Mitigation: Confirm claims against the original resume and job description; supply missing numbers rather than inventing them.
