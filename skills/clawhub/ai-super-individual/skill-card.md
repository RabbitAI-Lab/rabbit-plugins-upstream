External users and individual professionals use this skill to choose and run OPC modules for career transition, capability monetization, side-income validation, one-person-company planning, pricing, content, SOPs, and growth review. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create or overwrite local files containing career, income, customer, pricing, or business-plan details.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to show reports in chat first and require explicit confirmation of the destination path before saving or overwriting files.

Risk: Resource lookup can encounter login, membership, captcha, or permission barriers.

Mitigation: Do not provide passwords, cookies, tokens, or captcha values; stop automated access at barriers and provide public detail links instead.

Risk: Business and career recommendations may be confused with validated market evidence.

Mitigation: Require outputs to separate user facts, AI assumptions, evidence gaps, and real validation results.
