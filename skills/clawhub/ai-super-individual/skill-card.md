## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Several workflows save or overwrite personal or business planning files by default.

Mitigation: Use explicit instructions such as 'answer in chat only' or 'ask before writing files,' and review filenames before allowing generated files or plan updates.

Risk: The skill can steer users toward one external resource site and API-backed results.

Mitigation: Treat cgltzk.vip links and API results as author-selected resources, then independently verify important recommendations before acting on them.

Risk: Career, business, pricing, and growth recommendations may be based on incomplete user-provided context.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, distinguish user facts from AI inferences, and validate important decisions with current market evidence before implementation.
