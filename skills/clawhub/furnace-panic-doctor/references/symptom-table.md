# Symptom Table — 25+ presentations → ranked causes

`DIY` = safe for a careful homeowner. `PRO` = licensed tech. Costs are
US-typical bands (2024-2026), parts+labor, normal hours; emergencies add
$100-400.

## No heat at all

| Presentation | Most likely → least | Who | Band $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dead thermostat display | batteries → wiring → stat failed | DIY | 5-30 |
| Nothing runs, no lights | furnace switch → breaker → door switch → board transformer | DIY→PRO | 0-300 |
| Blower runs, no flame | igniter → flame sensor → gas valve → board | PRO | 150-600 |
| No glow, no spark | HSI igniter → control board → pressure switch (blocked vent) | PRO | 150-600 |
| Clicks, no flame | gas valve → low gas pressure → flame sense | PRO | 200-450 |
| Burners light, die in seconds | flame sensor dirty → bad sensor → gas pressure | PRO | 150-300 |
| After power outage | lockout → power-cycle switch 30 s; then board surge damage | DIY→PRO | 0-400 |

## Heat, but wrong

| Presentation | Likely | Who | Band $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weak airflow everywhere | filter → blower wheel fouled → duct crush | DIY→PRO | 15-400 |
| One room cold | register/duct damper → duct leak → bad design | DIY→PRO | 0-800 |
| Air lukewarm | limit cycling (filter!) → undersized unit → dying burner | DIY→PRO | 15-900 |
| Short cycles (2-4 min) | filter/high-limit → flame sensor → thermostat placement | DIY→PRO | 15-250 |
| Runs forever, never warm | oversized stat swing → duct loss → dying single-stage burner | PRO | 200-900 |
| Blows cold on HEAT | FAN set to ON (not AUTO) → no ignition → limit stuck | DIY→PRO | 0-350 |
| Big temp swings | thermostat anticipator/location (hallway draft) | DIY | 0-60 |

## Noises

| Sound | Cause | Who | Band $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| BANG on start (door-slam) | delayed ignition — gas builds then lights | **PRO URGENT** | 250-450 |
| Pop-pop in ducts (metal) | expansion, harmless unless new | NONE | 0 |
| Scraping metal | blower wheel rubbing/broken mount — running it grinds the motor | **PRO, OFF NOW** | 250-450 |
| Squeal/screech | blower bearings dry → belt (old units) | PRO | 120-350 |
| Rumble | burner adjustment / dirty orifices | PRO | 150-350 |
| Rattling | loose panel/screw → blower mount | DIY | 0-60 |
| Hum, blower not turning | bad capacitor (classic) → seized motor | PRO | 150-450 |

## Smells

| Smell | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Burning dust, first run of season | dust on exchanger | normal; if > 20 min, check filter |
| Burning plastic/electrical, sharp | melting insulation | OFF + breaker; emergency call |
| Rotten eggs | gas leak odorant | leave → utility emergency line |
| Oil/fuel near unit | leak, spill, or delayed ignition | OFF if safe; oil emergency line |
| Musty from registers | mold in coil/ducts | PRO (coil clean) 200-500 |
| Sooty/burnt-metal smell + symptoms | cracked exchanger suspicion | OFF; CO check; PRO urgent |

## Water & leaks

| Presentation | Likely | Who | Band $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water pooling under high-eff furnace | condensate drain clog → pump failed → frozen drain | DIY | 0-150 |
| Water + AC season only | frozen evaporator coil (low refrigerant/airflow) | PRO | 300-600 |
| Leaking around boiler relief valve | pressure too high/expansion tank waterlogged | **PRO plumber** | 200-500 |
| Rust streaks on old unit | aging tank/exchanger — plan replacement | PRO | assess |

## Error codes & blink patterns

See `manuals-and-codes.md` for where the LED is and how to count blinks.
Most common families:

| Pattern (typical) | Family meaning |
|---|---|
| Steady ON | normal operation (on many boards) |
| Fast continuous | limit/airflow problem — FILTER first |
| 2 blinks | pressure switch stuck open — vent blockage/inducer |
| 3 blinks | pressure switch stuck closed |
| 4 blinks | high-limit open — overheating: filter/airflow |
| 5-7 blinks | flame-sense / lockout — power-cycle once, then PRO |
| Solid OFF + no power | transformer/fuse/switch/breaker |

## When to replace instead of repair (rough rule)

- Heat exchanger crack on a unit > 15 years: replace (exchanger labor ≈
  half a new furnace).
- Second major repair (> $600) in 3 years on a 15+ year unit: replacement
  math usually wins; get quotes before the emergency premium.
- Repair quote > 40% of replacement cost on a 12+ year unit: at least
  price the replacement.
