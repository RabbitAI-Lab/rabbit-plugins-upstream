## Description:

根据商品自动生成真实、高质感的商品场景图，适用于家居、美妆、服饰、数码等行业。当用户要求生成商品场景图、使用场景展示图、氛围图、生活方式图（lifestyle）时必须触发。关键词：青虎AI、qhkit、场景图、商品场景、使用场景、氛围图、lifestyle、生活方式图、实景图、家居场景、美妆场景、点击率、转化率。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

E-commerce content creators and marketing teams use this skill to create lifestyle product-scene images from product photos for product pages and advertising. It also provides setup and usage guidance for the Qinghu AI image-generation CLI.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images are uploaded to Qinghu AI for processing and may include confidential designs.

Mitigation: Use only images you are authorized to process and check Qinghu AI's data-retention terms before uploading confidential designs.

Risk: The external qhkit CLI was not included in the artifact review.

Mitigation: Review package upgrades and account configuration changes before use.

Risk: Image generation may incur costs.

Mitigation: Review generation costs before running image-generation requests.
