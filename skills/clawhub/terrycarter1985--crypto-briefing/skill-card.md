## Description: <br>
Get structured global crypto market morning briefings via public web search, including market overview, key asset dynamics, macro and regulatory news, risk alerts, and source information. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to generate concise daily crypto market briefings from public web sources. It is intended for informational market awareness, not personalized investment advice or live trading signals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Crypto market and regulatory summaries may become stale or reflect incomplete public web results. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important claims against the listed sources and current authoritative sources before acting on them. <br>
Risk: Users may mistake a general market briefing for personalized investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as informational only and consult a qualified financial advisor for personalized decisions. <br>
Risk: Public web sources can contain inaccurate or misleading market information. <br>
Mitigation: Review source quality, recency, and corroboration across multiple sources before relying on the briefing. <br>
