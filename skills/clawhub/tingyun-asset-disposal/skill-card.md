## Description:

Helps factories and retailers compare equipment retention, rental, resale, dismantling, and scrap options, estimate recoverable value, and prepare disposal checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingyun01](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingyun01)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees responsible for factory or retail equipment use this skill to compare disposal paths, estimate recoverable value, and prepare pricing, ownership, transaction, environmental, and recordkeeping checklists. Valuation and legal, tax, and environmental decisions require human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China; local requirements may vary.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Illustrative price ranges or general legal, tax, and environmental guidance may not fit a specific asset or jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Treat estimates as planning inputs and have qualified valuation and legal, tax, and environmental professionals verify the relevant details before acting.

Risk: Business records shared for a disposal assessment may include sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share only the business records needed to answer the question.
