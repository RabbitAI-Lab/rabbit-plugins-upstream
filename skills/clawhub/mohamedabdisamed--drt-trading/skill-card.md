## Description:

Dealing Range Theory (DRT) covers ICT-style continuation, reversal, and consolidation range structures, quadrant levels, entry models, SMT confirmation, and a news filter for market analysis and trade setups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohamedabdisamed](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohamedabdisamed)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External traders and market analysts use this skill as a structured DRT reference and checklist for identifying dealing ranges, classifying continuation, reversal, or consolidation setups, and applying SMT, news, session, risk/reward, and stop-loss filters before planning trades.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading guidance may be mistaken for verified financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as an analysis reference only and require independent review before acting on any trade setup.

Risk: Backtest claims and win-rate figures may not generalize to future market conditions.

Mitigation: Validate the strategy against current data, market regime, transaction costs, and user-specific risk limits before use.
