## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for quit-vaping support, including nicotine-free streak tracking, craving-management prompts, health recovery milestones, and progress summaries based on their own quit information. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may involve quit dates, cravings, triggers, and progress details that are personal health-related information. <br>
Mitigation: Use agent or platform data controls to review or delete this information, and avoid sharing more personal detail than needed for quit-support guidance. <br>
