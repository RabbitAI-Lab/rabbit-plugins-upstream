## Description: <br>
Generate professional HTML proposals from meeting notes with built-in proposal styles, color themes, a design wizard, and responsive HTML output ready to send or export as PDF. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jeffjhunter](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeffjhunter) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, consultants, agencies, and service providers use this skill to turn meeting notes, saved pricing, and client context into polished proposal drafts and final HTML proposals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated proposals can include sensitive or incorrect details from meeting notes, MEMORY.md, and SERVICES.md. <br>
Mitigation: Review every generated proposal before sending it to a client. <br>
Risk: HTML templates and examples include Google Fonts links that make external requests when opened. <br>
Mitigation: Remove or localize the Google Fonts links when proposals must avoid external network requests. <br>
