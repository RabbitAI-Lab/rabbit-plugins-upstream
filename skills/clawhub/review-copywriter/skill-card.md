## Description:

将 Amazon 评论中的购买动因、疑问和体验语言整理为 Listing 优化方向与证据清单；仅用于证据驱动的页面文案规划，不用于无数据创作、广告投放或自动发布。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向 Amazon 卖家和商品运营人员，依据现有评论数据整理购买动因、疑问与体验证据，并形成 Listing 文案优化方向和证据清单。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broader commands may spend credits unexpectedly or enable recurring paid collection without consistent consent checks.

Mitigation: Before installation, require confirmation before every charge and use manual collection unless recurring costs are approved; review collection schedules and competitor subscriptions separately.

Risk: Quotation paths may trigger charges if the package does not enforce its listing-only scope.

Mitigation: Use a corrected package that enforces listing-only scope and prevents quotation paths from charging.
