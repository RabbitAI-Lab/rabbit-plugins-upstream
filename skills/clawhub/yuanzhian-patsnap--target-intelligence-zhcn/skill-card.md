## Description:

Guides Chinese-language biomedical target-intelligence research using PatSnap life-science MCP services to produce target, drug, clinical, patent, literature, and competitive landscape reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Life-science analysts, business development teams, and drug discovery researchers use this skill to answer target-centric questions in Chinese, including target biology, drug pipeline status, clinical progress, patents, literature, and competitive landscape findings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Biomedical queries and the MCP API key are sent to PatSnap-hosted external services.

Mitigation: Use an appropriate PatSnap account and avoid submitting sensitive research topics unless permitted by the user's data-sharing policy.

Risk: Biomedical target-intelligence outputs may be incomplete or misleading if retrieved source data is insufficient for the user's research or business decision.

Mitigation: Review the underlying PatSnap MCP, patent, clinical, literature, and web evidence before acting on the report.
