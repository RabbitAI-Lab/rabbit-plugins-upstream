## Description: <br>
Generate visitor-specific booth conversation scripts for cold walk-ups, warm leads, competitor customers, current customers, and live demos. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External marketing, sales, and event teams use this skill to prepare booth staff with tailored opening lines, pitches, qualification questions, CTAs, and printable quick-reference cards for trade-show conversations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Booth scripts may collect or prompt staff to confirm visitor contact information during lead capture. <br>
Mitigation: Add the organization's privacy notice and consent language before using email capture, follow-up list, or renewal-trigger workflows, especially in regulated industries or regions with strict marketing and personal-data rules. <br>
Risk: Unsupported customer outcomes, implementation timelines, integrations, certifications, quantified claims, or competitor limitations could mislead booth visitors. <br>
Mitigation: Use only supplied or explicitly sourced proof; otherwise keep claims qualitative or replace them with visible placeholders such as [verified outcome]. <br>
