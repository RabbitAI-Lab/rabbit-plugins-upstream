## Description:

B2B sales and business development users can use this skill to turn a product or service brief into sourced downstream buyer analysis, structured customer leads, compliant outreach drafts, and fallback channel guidance when direct leads are scarce.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onsoul](https://clawhub.ai/user/onsoul)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sales, business development, and B2B operators use this skill to identify likely downstream buyers, collect public-source lead information, prepare outreach drafts, and choose compliant acquisition channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may send outreach emails through the user's mailbox without a clearly required final approval step.

Mitigation: Require explicit manual review and approval before any Agent Mail send action; when approval or connector setup is absent, generate an email draft for the user to send manually.
