## Description: <br>
Writes a complete credit memo for lending decisions, including borrower narrative, facility structure, repayment analysis, financial ratios with covenant headroom, risks with mitigants, rating rationale, and recommendation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Credit analysts, lenders, and reviewers use this skill to draft credit committee memos for borrower requests, including facility terms, repayment sources, ratio trends, risks, mitigants, rating rationale, and a recommendation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may provide confidential borrower and financial information while drafting credit memos. <br>
Mitigation: Handle borrower and financial inputs according to institutional data-handling policies and only use approved environments for sensitive information. <br>
Risk: Generated credit analysis may include assumptions or errors that affect a lending recommendation. <br>
Mitigation: Verify assumptions, ratios, repayment analysis, rating, approval authority, and terms against institutional credit policy and applicable regulation before use. <br>
