## Description:

Assesses whether product data can create a durable competitive advantage by testing uniqueness, compounding value, feedback loops, usage rights, quality, and customer-visible impact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product leaders, and investors use this skill to assess whether a product's data can create a durable competitive advantage and to guide investment in data collection, labeling, or partnerships. It produces a structured review of uniqueness, compounding value, feedback loops, rights, quality, and customer-visible impact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business data or saved assessment notes may contain confidential details.

Mitigation: Use high-level or redacted descriptions of business data and review saved lessons for confidential details.
