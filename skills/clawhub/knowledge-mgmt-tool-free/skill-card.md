## Description: <br>
This skill helps individual users audit knowledge-management practices, design taxonomies, create documentation templates, and plan knowledge-capture workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Individual users use this skill to organize knowledge-management work such as audits, taxonomy planning, documentation templates, and knowledge extraction plans. It is intended for routine personal knowledge-management workflows with standard Markdown, JSON, and YAML outputs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad read, write, and command execution authority could affect sensitive knowledge-management workspaces. <br>
Mitigation: Constrain allowed paths, review proposed export or mutation actions before execution, and avoid running the skill on sensitive business repositories unless manual approval is enforced. <br>
