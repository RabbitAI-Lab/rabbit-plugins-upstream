## Description:

帮助教师梳理知识图谱与复习重点，制定包含间隔回看、交叉练习和考前活动的复习计划。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers of upper-primary through high-school classes use this skill to draft exam and unit review schedules from curriculum scope and class-level learning needs. Teachers review the plan before using it with students or saving it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize curriculum, language, privacy rules, and crisis-help channels for other regions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Curriculum, privacy practices, or crisis-help information may be inappropriate outside the Mainland China Chinese K–12 context.

Mitigation: Localize these elements and confirm the teaching context before use.

Risk: A saved review plan could expose identifiable student information or be stored without teacher approval.

Mitigation: Require teacher confirmation before saving or revising a plan; exclude real student names, individual scores, and rankings.
