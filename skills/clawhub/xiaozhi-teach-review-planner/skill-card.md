## Description:

把"从头再讲一遍"变成有间隔、有交叉、有取舍的复习排期。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External K12 teachers use this skill to turn unit, midterm, final, or pre-exam review needs into spaced and interleaved review plans, knowledge maps, stage strategies, and classroom review activities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reflects Chinese-language K12 teaching, curriculum, consent, and crisis-help assumptions.

Mitigation: Localize language, curriculum assumptions, consent rules, and crisis-help contact guidance before use outside mainland China or with non-Chinese users.

Risk: Persistent review-plan records could expose class planning data if platform controls are incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm that teacher approval, privacy controls, and sharing restrictions are enforced before allowing stored review-plan writes.

Risk: AI-generated examples or review activities could contain incorrect educational content.

Mitigation: Require teacher review and use the bundled AI item check before classroom use or repository entry.

Risk: Learner safety or crisis signals can exceed the scope of review planning.

Mitigation: Stop the review workflow and follow the bundled crisis exception and referral protocol.
