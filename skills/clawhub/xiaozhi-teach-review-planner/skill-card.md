## Description:

Helps mainland China K–12 teachers create data-informed review plans with knowledge maps, spaced recall, interleaved practice, and exam-stage pacing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to prioritize class-wide weak topics and draft unit or exam review schedules, recall activities, and knowledge maps. Teachers review plans before saving or sharing them; separate skills handle assignments and test papers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt crisis referrals, grade and curriculum assumptions, and data-consent rules before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review plans use class learning data and can persist in the teacher workspace.

Mitigation: Tell teachers what data is read, omit student names and individual scores, and save or revise schedules only after explicit confirmation.

Risk: Mainland China crisis contacts, curriculum assumptions, and consent rules may not apply elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize referrals, grade and curriculum assumptions, and data-consent rules before use in another region.

Risk: Exam stress or crisis disclosures may exceed the scope of educational planning.

Mitigation: Avoid diagnosis and stop planning for crisis signals; direct the teacher to appropriate local human support and emergency services.
