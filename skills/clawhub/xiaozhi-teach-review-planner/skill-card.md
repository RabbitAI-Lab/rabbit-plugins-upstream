## Description:

Helps teachers plan prioritized, spaced, and interleaved K–12 review using knowledge maps and classroom learning signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking teachers use this skill to draft unit and exam review schedules, identify shared learning gaps, and plan retrieval and interleaved practice. Teachers review the plans before classroom use; the skill does not produce full assignments or exam papers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China (localize curriculum, crisis contacts, and minor-data rules for use elsewhere)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom planning may expose minors' personal or performance data.

Mitigation: Confirm platform controls for teacher approval, pseudonymization, deletion, pausing, and sharing before using real classroom data.

Risk: Curriculum or crisis guidance may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, crisis contacts, and minor-data rules before use elsewhere; refer crisis signals to appropriate local human support.

Risk: An inaccurate review plan or generated example could mislead students.

Mitigation: Have a teacher check the schedule and verify any generated examples before sharing them with students.
