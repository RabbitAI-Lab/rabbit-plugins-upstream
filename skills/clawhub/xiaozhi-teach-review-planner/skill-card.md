## Description:

Helps K–12 teachers build review plans with knowledge maps, prioritized topics, spaced recall, interleaved practice, and exam preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language K–12 teachers use this skill to draft unit and exam review calendars from class-level learning needs. It plans review activities and timing rather than generating assignments or test papers, and saves plans only after teacher confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize language, curriculum, privacy practices, and crisis contacts before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review plans could expose identifiable students or individual scores.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms or student IDs and knowledge-point-level aggregates; exclude real names, individual scores, and rankings from plans and shared materials.

Risk: A draft or changed schedule could be saved without the teacher's approval.

Mitigation: Show the complete plan first and require explicit teacher confirmation before saving or updating it.

Risk: Exam-stress discussions can reveal a student safety crisis beyond educational guidance.

Mitigation: Stop planning, avoid diagnosis, and follow the crisis referral protocol with location-appropriate help contacts.
