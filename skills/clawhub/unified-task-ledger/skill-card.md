## Description:

Guides agents in recording task ownership, status, acceptance criteria, and closure in a single auditable ledger.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Teams and agents use this methodology to assign tasks, track their status and owners, and verify results before closing them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
