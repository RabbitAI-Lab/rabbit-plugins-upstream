## Description:

Checks household cleaning products for dangerous combinations and provides prevention, sequencing, and first-aid guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Household cleaners and caregivers use this skill to check product combinations before cleaning, plan safer product sequencing, and find initial guidance after a suspected exposure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cleaner formulations vary, so a product-name lookup may not reflect the actual ingredients.

Mitigation: Read the product label and treat uncertain combinations as incompatible.

Risk: First-aid guidance may be insufficient for an actual exposure or breathing symptoms.

Mitigation: Leave the affected area and contact poison control or emergency services for real exposures or breathing symptoms.
