## Description: <br>
Helps frontend developers, designers, product teams, and maintainers create workflows, checklists, analysis, and implementation support for mobile responsiveness, navigation layout, and cross-device UI issues. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, product teams, and maintainers use this skill to diagnose and fix responsive layout and navigation issues across phone and desktop interfaces. It produces practical guidance, reusable workflows, checklists, code-oriented suggestions, and verification notes grounded in the user's constraints. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be selected for broad frontend or layout requests where responsive-design help is not the intended task. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit invocation when precision matters, and review the output as responsive-design guidance before applying it. <br>
