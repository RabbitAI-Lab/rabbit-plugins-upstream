# Example: Upfront Move Announcement (结构预告)

## 元数据
- **来源**: Costello, T. H., Bowes, S. M., Stevens, S. T., Waldman, I. D., Tasimi, A., & Lilienfeld, S. O. (2021). Clarifying the structure and nature of left-wing authoritarianism. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 120(1), 1–30. DOI: 10.1037/pspp0000341
- **论文编号**: 1.1
- **领域**: Social & Personality (JPSP)
- **维度**: Structure（结构预告 / Roadmap）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: structure-diagnosis 的"Opening move 类型判定"、"Move 顺序是否清晰"

---

## 原句（Discussion 开篇段）

> *"In the following sections, we unpack our results, compare differences and similarities across LWA and RWA, discuss the role of political ideology in LWA and RWA, highlight limitations of the present research, and outline several promising directions for future research. We conclude with a discussion of LWA's implications for (a) general authoritarianism and (b) psychological science."*

## 分析

### 1. 教科书式的 Roadmap 句
- **"In the following sections, we..."** 是经典 opening-roadmap 信号（Unit 4.2.2）
- 一句话预告了 6 个即将出现的 move：
  1. unpack results (Move A)
  2. compare LWA and RWA (Move B: map to literature)
  3. discuss political ideology role (Move B: contextualize)
  4. highlight limitations (Move D)
  5. outline future research (Move E)
  6. conclude with implications (Move C + closing)

### 2. 对应 unit 4.2.3 Generic Discussion Model
完美匹配教科书定义的 Discussion 章节结构：revisit results → map to literature → implications → limitations → future work → closing

### 3. 与 Introduction 的对称（Intro↔Discussion）
- Intro Block 1（建立重要性）↔ Closing（"implications for ... psychological science"）
- Intro Block 3（研究 gap）↔ Results reinterpretation
- Intro Block 4（描述本研究）↔ Achievement statement

---

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **缺 roadmap** — 用户 Discussion 开头直接讨论第一个结果，没有"In the following sections..." → 引用此例展示如何在开篇预告后续 move
2. **move 顺序混乱** — 用户 Discussion 把 limitations 放最前面、implications 放最后 → 引用此例展示规范顺序
3. **opening move 类型不明确** — 用户不确定该用 achievement 还是 reboot 开篇 → 引用此例展示"先 achievement + roadmap"模式（不是纯 reboot）
