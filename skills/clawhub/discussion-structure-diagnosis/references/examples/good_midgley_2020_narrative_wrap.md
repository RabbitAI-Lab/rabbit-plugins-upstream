# Example: Narrative Wrap + In-Sum Summary

## 元数据
- **来源**: Midgley, C., Thai, S., Lockwood, P., Kovacheff, L., & Rohlinger, N. (2020). When every day is a high school reunion: Social media comparisons and self-esteem. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 119(2), 213–231. DOI: 10.1037/pspi0000336
- **论文编号**: 1.3
- **领域**: Social & Personality (JPSP)
- **维度**: Structure (叙事包裹 + 段落整合)
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: structure-diagnosis 的"段落是否完成 narrative wrap"、"是否每个 sub-section 都有 in-sum 整合"

---

## 原句（Discussion 第 7 段，"In sum" 整合段）

> *"In sum, these studies provide important evidence that social media has changed social comparison behavior. Individuals now make more image-based comparisons that, despite involving less close targets, are more strongly upward and more threatening to the self. Previous social comparison theory would predict that social media comparisons to distant acquaintances and celebrities would be less meaningful and upsetting than those made in "real life"; however, I demonstrate that a few minutes spent browsing one's news feed presents a minefield of very real and meaningful upward comparison threats."*

## 分析

### 1. 叙事包裹 (narrative wrap)
- **"In sum" 显式信号词**——明确告诉读者"接下来是整合"
- 整段围绕一个核心论点展开："social media has changed social comparison behavior"
- 结尾呼应论文标题"high school reunion"隐喻（"a minefield of very real and meaningful upward comparison threats"）——这与 Introduction 的 hook 形成 narrative 回环

### 2. 段落功能（unit 4.2.3 Generic Discussion Model）
- 这段同时承担了 A（revisit results）+ B（map to literature）+ C（implication）三个 move
- 把"social media comparison theory"这个 literature 与本研究结果**明确对立**（"would predict...however, I demonstrate..."）——这是教科书级的 confirm/contrast/extending 引用模式

### 3. 论述收敛
- **Forward-moving**: 每句话都把读者往核心结论推进一步
- **No backtracking**: 不重复前面段落已说过的话
- **Take-home reinforcement**: "social media comparisons are threatening" 这个 take-home 在前 6 个 sub-section 已经铺垫，这里最终落地

## 用法（诊断时怎么引用）

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时，引用此例作为**正向对照**：

1. **缺 narrative wrap**：用户段落散乱、无"in sum/taken together"等收束词 → 引用此例的"In sum"开头模式
2. **段落间逻辑跳跃**：用户从"revisit results"直接跳到"future work"，中间无 wrap → 引用此例展示教科书过渡
3. **take-home 不清晰**：用户最后一段没把核心论点说清 → 引用此例结尾的隐喻呼应
4. **引用整合弱**：用户用了 citation 但没有 relationship word → 引用此例的"would predict...however, I demonstrate..."对比结构

## 反例参考（指向同一论文的另一段）

同篇 Midgley 2020 中也存在**结构较弱的段落**（用于 bad example 对照）：

> *"...these studies provide significant new insights into social comparison processes more generally. In past research, investigators typically have examined the outcome of a single comparison, or have compared an upward to a downward comparison on various outcomes such as self-evaluation (e.g., ...)."*

这段虽然内容扎实，但**结构上像"研究综述"而非"叙事推进"**——大量罗列前人研究，无核心论点收束。详见 `bad_midgley_2020_literature_dump.md`。
