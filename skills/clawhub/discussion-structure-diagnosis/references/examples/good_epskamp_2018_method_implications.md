# Example: Methodological Paper's Implications-Heavy Structure

## 元数据
- **来源**: Epskamp, S., Borsboom, D., & Fried, E. I. (2018). Estimating psychological networks and their accuracy: A tutorial paper. *Psychological Methods*, 23(4), 617–648. DOI: 10.1037/met0000167
- **论文编号**: 7.1
- **领域**: Methodology (Psych Methods)
- **维度**: Structure（方法学 tutorial 论文的 Discussion 结构 — Implications 占主导）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: structure-diagnosis 的"Implications 段比重异常"、"tutorial 论文的结构适配"

---

## 原句（Discussion 中的 Implications 段）

> *"We suggest that researchers always compare networks based on polychoric correlations with networks based on Spearman correlations (they should look somewhat similar) to determine if the estimation of polychoric correlations is the source of this problem. ... Again, the network based on polychoric correlations should be compared to a network based on Spearman correlations. Obtaining very different networks indicates that the estimation of the polychoric correlations may not be trustworthy."*

## 分析

### 1. Methodological tutorial 的 Discussion 偏重 implications
- **不像实证论文**——没有"revisit results"
- **不像 simulation paper**——没有"for each simulation"
- **tutorial paper 的 Discussion 主要给实践建议**："researchers should do X"
- 这个 example 展示了**paper type aware** 的诊断思路

### 2. Achievement-style "we suggest"
- **"We suggest that researchers always compare..."** —— 这是 contribution statement
- 紧跟的是**操作化建议**——让读者能立刻行动
- 这是**methodological paper 的"happy words"替代**——不是"new findings"，而是"practical guidelines"

### 3. Limitations 段与 implications 段的关系
- tutorial paper 的 limitations 通常是 **method limitations**（"polychoric correlations has known biases"）
- implications 段就是 **how to mitigate these limitations**——形成 limitation → implication 的紧密耦合

### 4. 与 Costello 2021 的结构对比
- 实证论文（Costello）：achievement → results revisit → literature → limitations → future → implications
- 方法学论文（Epskamp）：implications（贯穿）→ method limitations（操作指南）

---

## 用法

当用户上传**方法学、tutorial、tools & techniques 类论文**的 Discussion 时引用此例：

1. **判定 paper 类型错误** — 系统默认按实证论文打分，导致方法学论文得分低 → 引用此例说明应切换 paper type
2. **用户写"more research is needed"** — 在 tutorial paper 里这是冗余（不需要 future work）→ 引用此例展示应直接给操作建议
3. **implications 段比例过低** — 用户 80% 是 limitations，10% implications → 引用此例展示 implications 应占主导
