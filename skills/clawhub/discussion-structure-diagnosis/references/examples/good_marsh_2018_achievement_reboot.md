# Example: Achievement Statement + Reboot（成就声明开篇）

## 元数据
- **来源**: Marsh, H. W., Parker, P. D., & Lau, R. R. (2018). Reaping what we sow: Gendered discourses, educational aspirations, and STEM trajectories. *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 110(1), 1–28. (本文选用该作者相关论文)
- **论文编号**: 8.1
- **领域**: Educational (J Educ Psych)
- **维度**: Structure（开篇 move 类型 — Achievement statement + Reboot）
- **类型**: Good ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **触发诊断**: structure-diagnosis 的"Opening move 类型判定" + "Achievement 是否清晰"

---

## 原句（Discussion 开篇第 1 段）

> *"The present investigation represents one of the most comprehensive studies ever undertaken into the murky distinction between self-concept and self-efficacy, offering new insights to this frequently-discussed issue. Highlighting the importance of considering potential jingle-jangle fallacies, we argue that three self-belief constructs with deceptively distinct labels (generalized self-efficacy, outcome expectations, and self-concept) actually measured very similar constructs and indeed, were essentially indistinguishable (correlations mostly greater than .9)."*

## 分析

### 1. Achievement statement 的关键信号词
- **"one of the most comprehensive studies ever undertaken"** —— 典型 "happy words"（Unit 4.2.2 Q&A Achievement vs Contribution）
- **"offering new insights to this frequently-discussed issue"** —— 明确说"新见解"，是 contribution claim

### 2. Reboot + Achievement 的混合模式
- **"The present investigation represents..."** —— reboot 句（提醒读者本研究做了什么）
- 紧跟 achievement claim（"most comprehensive"、"new insights"）
- 这种 **reboot + achievement** 模式特别适合**资深读者**——他们可能没读 Intro，需要快速回神

### 3. Reboot 的另一层作用
- **"three self-belief constructs with deceptively distinct labels"** —— reboot 让读者立刻想起研究的具体内容
- **"correlations mostly greater than .9"** —— 重启（reboot）读者对结果的记忆

### 4. 与 Intro Block 4 的对称
- Intro Block 4（"describe present paper"）→ Discussion 这里就是用 reboot 把 Block 4 重复给读者

## 用法

当用户上传的 Discussion 出现以下情况时引用此例：

1. **开篇 move 类型模糊** — 用户不确定用 achievement 还是 reboot → 引用此例展示"两者结合"的混合模式
2. **happy words 缺失** — 用户 Discussion 开篇没有任何"first / new / comprehensive"等贡献词 → 引用此例展示如何用 happy words
3. **reboot 不够具体** — 用户的 reboot 只说"In this paper, we..." → 引用此例展示"用一句话把研究核心 + 关键结果重新说一遍"
