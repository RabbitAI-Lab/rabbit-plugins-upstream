## Description:

Diagnose the structural completeness and move ordering of the Discussion section in an English psychology research paper.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to diagnose whether a psychology Discussion section covers expected rhetorical moves, follows a logical order, and aligns with the Introduction. It provides diagnostic feedback and suggestions, not generated replacement prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat diagnostic comments as a substitute for academic judgment or journal-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Review the diagnosis against the target journal, supervisor feedback, and the paper's actual study design before making revisions.

Risk: Users may ask the skill to write or rewrite Discussion prose, which is outside its stated academic-integrity posture.

Mitigation: Use the skill for structure diagnosis and suggestions only; keep final manuscript wording authored and reviewed by the user.
