## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste sensitive contract or quote details, including names, phone numbers, addresses, signatures, payment details, or dispute facts. <br>
Mitigation: Redact personal, payment, address, signature, and dispute details before sharing contract or quote text with an AI chat. <br>
Risk: Practical renovation guidance may be mistaken for legal advice or region-specific legal conclusions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as renovation-industry guidance and verify legal, monetary, or litigation issues with a qualified local professional. <br>
