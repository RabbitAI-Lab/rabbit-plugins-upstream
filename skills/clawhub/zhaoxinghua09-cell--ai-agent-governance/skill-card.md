## Description:

A practical guide to assessing AI agent risks, governing the agent lifecycle, assigning accountability, and applying local tools and templates to support safer deployments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, including managers, compliance and legal teams, security professionals, and AI engineers, use the skill to assess agent risks, plan lifecycle controls, assign responsibilities, and draft governance procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Governance scores are rough planning aids, and regulatory guidance can become outdated.

Mitigation: Use scores for triage and verify regulatory statements against current official sources or qualified counsel.

Risk: The bundled audit and integrity claims need updating, according to the security guidance.

Mitigation: Review those claims against the current release files before relying on them.
