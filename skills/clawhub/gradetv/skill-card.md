## Description:

Read collected TV schedules by channel and date without building a programme collector. Use free daily reads or a paid response with several available days.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wrpbr](https://clawhub.ai/user/wrpbr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

TV viewers use the skill to find schedules by channel and date, discover channels, and review reported playback problems. It can also compare free daily schedule reads with an optional paid multi-day response.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid requests may incur costs, and current pricing was not independently verified.

Mitigation: Check the published pricing and use paid requests only within approved spending authority.

Risk: Payment credentials could be exposed if mishandled.

Mitigation: Keep payment credentials secret.
