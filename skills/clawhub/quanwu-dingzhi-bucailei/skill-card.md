## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Construction, material, environmental, pricing, or acceptance guidance may not match local regulations, site conditions, contract terms, or current standards. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for preparation and screening, then verify important claims with local professionals, current standards, written contracts, and qualified inspection or testing providers. <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on broad cabinetry or board-material phrases outside the user's intended scope. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking about full-home custom cabinetry, cabinet materials, pricing, merchant selection, installation, or acceptance before applying its guidance. <br>
Risk: Quote and contract analysis can influence renovation spending or disputes. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the output as practical consumer guidance, not legal, engineering, medical, or certified inspection advice, and obtain professional review before making high-cost or binding decisions. <br>
