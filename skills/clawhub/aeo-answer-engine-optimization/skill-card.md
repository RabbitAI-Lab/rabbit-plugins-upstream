## Description:

Guides answer-engine optimization by mapping real user questions to concise, answer-first passages and aligning visible page content with relevant structured data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content strategists, marketers, and support teams use this skill to turn recurring questions with verifiable answers into clear page passages that answer directly and can be tracked in search and answer engines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer questions may contain identifying or sensitive details.

Mitigation: Use relevant, redacted customer questions as inputs.

Risk: Proposed page edits may be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes before publishing.
