基于《AI超级个体》配套的 12 项创业准备清单，检查需求、可复制交付、主副业边界、竞业与资源冲突、资金、定位、获客和交付能力，输出启动、试运行或暂缓建议。

## Use Case:

External users and solo founders use this skill to assess whether they are ready to start or expand a one-person company. It separates side-business validation from full-time resignation decisions and turns readiness gaps into near-term actions and milestones.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may discuss employment boundaries, noncompete obligations, intellectual property, tax, or contract concerns.

Mitigation: Frame these as risk topics, avoid legal conclusions, and direct users to qualified local professionals for review.

Risk: Users may provide sensitive employer, customer, contract, or personal finance information during readiness assessment.

Mitigation: Ask for ranges or summaries, and avoid collecting employer names, customer names, or full contract text.

Risk: Readiness output could be mistaken for a direct recommendation to resign or make a major financial decision.

Mitigation: Separate side-business validation from full-time resignation and require checks for customer stability, cash reserves, and family risk tolerance.
