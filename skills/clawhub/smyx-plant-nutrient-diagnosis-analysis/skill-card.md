## Description:

Analyzes plant leaf images for visual signs of nutrient deficiencies and returns a likely nutrient diagnosis with confidence and general fertilization guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Home gardeners and growers can submit a plant leaf image for an indicative nutrient-deficiency assessment and general fertilization direction, or retrieve previous diagnosis reports.
