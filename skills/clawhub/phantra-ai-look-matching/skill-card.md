## Description:

Guides users in matching a Phantra scene’s visual look to their own elements and a reference image, while clarifying what can and cannot be matched.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zeropage-onryo](https://clawhub.ai/user/zeropage-onryo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People creating Phantra scenes use this skill to translate reference images into cinematography and color-grade guidance, assign each image a role, and anchor scene subjects to their own elements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may share reference photos without permission.

Mitigation: Use photos the user has permission to share, and let the user choose or provide references.

Risk: Photos added as elements are stored in Phantra's studio, whose service-side retention and deletion controls are outside this package.

Mitigation: Review Phantra's retention and deletion controls before adding or uploading photos.
