## Description: <br>
Researches citizenship-by-descent eligibility, compares family lineage against nationality laws, and guides general immigration planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[eosbot](https://clawhub.ai/user/eosbot) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to research genealogy-based citizenship eligibility, map document requirements, compare immigration pathways, and prepare non-legal planning checklists for official verification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Genealogy and immigration analysis can involve sensitive information about the user and relatives. <br>
Mitigation: Share only necessary details, avoid information about living people without consent, and treat religion or ethnicity as optional unless legally relevant. <br>
Risk: Citizenship and immigration requirements may change and incorrect guidance could affect official applications. <br>
Mitigation: Verify official processes with government sources, consulates, or a qualified professional before acting. <br>
