## Description:

Earn a fair, accurate place on the third-party "best X", comparison and review articles that ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews cite for your buying prompts. Use for "get us on best-of lists", "why do AI answers skip us" or AEO, GEO and LLM SEO outreach. Scores targets, builds a pitch kit, drafts corrections and keeps an outreach log.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Brand and SEO/AEO practitioners use this skill to find third-party comparison pages cited for buying queries, assess their accuracy and influenceability, and prepare factual outreach or correction drafts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was benign but partial because two references in the Markdown file could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Check those references when recommendations rely on claims they support.

Risk: Outreach based on inaccurate product facts could mislead editors.

Mitigation: Verify product details against current authoritative sources and have a human review each draft before sending.

Risk: Paid or affiliate placements can be misleading when commercial relationships are not disclosed.

Mitigation: Disclose payment or commission beside the listing and mark paid links as sponsored or nofollow, following applicable local rules.
