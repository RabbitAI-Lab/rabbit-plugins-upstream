## Description: <br>
Suno AI 音乐提示词专家 helps users turn fuzzy music ideas into copy-ready English Suno prompts using a five-part framework, genre templates, meta tags, and iteration guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, creators, and developers use this skill to build, diagnose, and refine Suno music prompts from natural-language music ideas. It produces English prompts with Chinese explanations, variants, and iteration advice for AI music generation workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompt guidance may include copyright-sensitive style references or requests to closely imitate protected music. <br>
Mitigation: Review style references before use and prefer general genre, mood, instrument, tempo, and production descriptors instead of copying melodies, lyrics, or distinctive protected elements. <br>
Risk: The skill's Suno guidance is scoped to v5/v5.5 and may become stale as the platform changes. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm current Suno limits and behavior before relying on version-specific prompt rules in production workflows. <br>
Risk: AI music generation is variable, so a single prompt may not produce the intended result. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's variant and iteration workflow to adjust one dimension at a time and review generated outputs before publication. <br>
