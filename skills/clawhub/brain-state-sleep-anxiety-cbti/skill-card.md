## Description:

This skill helps an agent explain brain-state screening reports in plain language and provide educational CBT-I-style sleep and anxiety self-regulation guidance without diagnosis, medication advice, or cure claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianzhen777](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianzhen777)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask for plain-language explanation of brain-state screening report indicators or sleep and anxiety self-help suggestions. The skill is intended for educational self-regulation guidance and explicitly directs severe, worsening, or clinical concerns to qualified care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake sleep, anxiety, or brain-state screening guidance for clinical diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Preserve the skill's required disclaimer and direct diagnosis, medication decisions, crisis support, and severe or worsening symptoms to qualified clinicians or emergency support.

Risk: Plain-language self-help suggestions may be insufficient for serious or persistent sleep and anxiety symptoms.

Mitigation: Present the content as educational self-regulation guidance and avoid substituting it for medical care.
