## Description:

Turn a CSV into a one-page brief built from profiled numbers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goofproof](https://clawhub.ai/user/goofproof)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People working with spreadsheet exports use this skill to turn one or more CSV files into short, evidence-based summaries and questions for follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated profile may contain sensitive values from input CSV files.

Mitigation: Review profile.json before sharing the results.
