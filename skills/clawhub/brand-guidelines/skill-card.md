## Description: <br>
Extracts a brand's visual and verbal identity into a compact guideline kit, then applies those tokens and voice rules consistently to documents, decks, pages, and other artifacts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external creators, designers, and developers use this skill to extract brand kits from real brand materials or apply existing brand guidelines to documents, decks, pages, and other artifacts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Brand evidence may include confidential assets, unpublished documents, or website material. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide materials that are approved for agent inspection; omit confidential assets unless that review is authorized. <br>
Risk: Incomplete brand evidence can lead to inferred visual or voice rules. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's confidence notes to label inferred decisions and defer to official guidelines when they are available. <br>
Risk: Brand application can reduce readability if visual style overrides functional constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Keep contrast and legibility checks binding, and document substitutions or unresolved design calls in the conformance note. <br>
