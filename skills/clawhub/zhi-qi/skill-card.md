## Description: <br>
Zhi Qi is a Chinese-language fictional sales persona for roleplay simulations that models customers across 12 dimensions to practice persuasive dialogue, negotiation, and deal-closing scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wavegeometry](https://clawhub.ai/user/wavegeometry) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers can use this skill for explicit, fictional, or consent-based sales training, customer objection handling, negotiation roleplay, and discussion of persuasion boundaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may encourage hidden psychological profiling or covert pressure tactics in sales interactions. <br>
Mitigation: Restrict use to explicit, fictional, or consent-based training scenarios with clear disclosure and respect for refusal. <br>
Risk: Roleplay outputs could be misapplied to real customers, vulnerable people, or high-stakes decision makers. <br>
Mitigation: Require truthful claims, human review, and a rule that the persona must not be used to manipulate real purchasing decisions. <br>
