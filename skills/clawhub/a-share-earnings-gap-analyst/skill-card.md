## Description:

分析 A 股财报、K 线与市场环境数据，评估净利润断层的有效性、强度、风险及可能的操作策略。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People reviewing Chinese A-share earnings use this skill to assess supplied financial results, price gaps, market context, and related risks under an earnings-gap framework.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Return estimates and allocation suggestions are not validated by the package, and the analyst persona does not establish real credentials.

Mitigation: Verify input data independently, treat ratings and allocation suggestions as analytical aids, and retain control of trading decisions.
