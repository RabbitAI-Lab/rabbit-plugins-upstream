## Description:

Generates an HTML patent transfer intelligence brief from patent numbers by combining patent retrieval, semantic search, company intelligence, and news signals to identify potential transferees and recommended outreach actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent operations, IP, and business development teams use this skill to assess a patent package, identify high-fit transfer or licensing candidates, and generate a shareable HTML brief with supporting signals and action recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent, company, and market context may be confidential business intelligence.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when authorized to submit the relevant information to the configured MCP services and share generated reports only with approved recipients.

Risk: Patent events, company signals, and news-derived recommendations may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for negotiation decisions without review.

Mitigation: Verify cited sources and validate report conclusions with qualified IP or business reviewers before outreach, transfer, licensing, or negotiation.
