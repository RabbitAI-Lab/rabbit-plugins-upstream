## Description: <br>
Prompt and direct Runway for control-grade generative video shots, including camera moves, character consistency with references, edit-grade video-to-video work, consent checks, and AI-disclosure planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and social media production agents use this skill to decide when Runway is the right video tool and to draft controlled shot briefs for branded ads, product shots, previz, consistent character sequences, and edits to existing footage. It keeps a human editor in the loop for assembly, audio, captions, consent, rights, and disclosure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, reference images, or existing footage may be sent to Runway and could include confidential, client-owned, regulated, or sensitive media. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the media is approved for use with Runway, remove sensitive material where possible, and verify the current Runway plan and terms before sending it. <br>
Risk: Act-Two or similar performance workflows can create likeness, consent, or impersonation risk when a real person or performance is involved. <br>
Mitigation: Use only consented performers, owned performances, or licensed characters, and refuse requests to transpose a non-consenting person's face or performance. <br>
Risk: Generated or edited video may need clear AI disclosure before publication. <br>
Mitigation: Plan an AI-generated or AI-assisted disclosure in the caption, on screen, or platform workflow before the asset is scheduled or published. <br>
