## Description:

Draft replies from messages and verified notes, with evidence and a follow-up checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hznetnewpower](https://clawhub.ai/user/hznetnewpower)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Support staff and other people handling incoming messages can use the skill to prepare evidence-linked email or support replies from supplied messages, verified facts, and policies, then review unresolved items before responding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A draft may include inaccurate facts or commitments that were not authorized.

Mitigation: Verify recipients, facts, and commitments before responding; passing checks does not establish accuracy or authorization.

Risk: Generated checklists contain supplied message content.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary message data and keep generated checklists private.
