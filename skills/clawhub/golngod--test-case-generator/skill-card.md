## Description: <br>
Generates structured test case sets from system architecture material and acceptance checklists, covering functional, performance, security, and compatibility testing with P0/P1/P2 prioritization. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, QA engineers, and product teams use this skill to convert architecture plans, module lists, non-functional requirements, and acceptance criteria into executable test case plans. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated test plans may be incomplete, misleading, or incorrectly prioritized for a release decision. <br>
Mitigation: Have a human reviewer compare generated cases against the requirements, architecture, and acceptance criteria before using them for release approval. <br>
Risk: Inputs may include sensitive architecture, security, or operational details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing secrets or highly sensitive internal details unless the workspace is appropriate for that data. <br>
