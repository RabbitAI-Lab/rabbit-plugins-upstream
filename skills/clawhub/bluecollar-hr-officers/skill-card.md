## Description:

Provides blue-collar HR service organizations with routed workflows and templates for labor dispatch, vocational internship placement, on-site management, fee settlement, and compliance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Blue-collar HR service organizations use this skill to route staffing, internship, on-site operations, finance, and compliance work into task workflows. It helps agents draft batch plans, checklists, reconciliations, reports, and related structured deliverables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Identity-document and arrival photos may expose sensitive personal data, including student or minor data, if collected, shared, or retained without clear limits.

Mitigation: Review photo workflows before using real data; minimize identity records, establish consent and authorized recipients, protect storage, set deletion dates, and apply safeguards for minors.
