## Description: <br>
Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague error messages into clearer troubleshooting copy that explains what failed, why it failed, and what action to take next. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users use this skill to rewrite or structure unclear error and troubleshooting messages. It produces practical copy, checklists, workflows, analyses, code changes, or decision support that make assumptions and next steps visible. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may select the skill for general debugging or support requests where error-message improvement is not the user's intended task. <br>
Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly or confirm that the user wants clearer error or troubleshooting text before applying its workflow. <br>
