# Patterns & Techniques — Agorism in the 21st Century, Vol. 2

## Secessionary gesture
**When to use**: marking a consequential transgression or founding act so its meaning survives the actor.
**How**: adopt a symbol with telescoped historical resonance (anagram, old flag, dead revolutionary's motto); let it be legible beyond your own biography.
**Trade-offs**: invites state attention; requires genuine willingness to accept social death (Baudrillard: bury the name of God).

## Yes Chad / super-ordination (Wilson)
**When to use**: when authority strikes, sanctions, or "wins."
**How**: integrate the blow into your plan's narrative — "of course, according to plan" — and proceed. Never perform wounded surprise.
**Trade-offs**: reads as arrogance; requires actually having a plan, else it's cope.

## Mediation audit (Girard)
**When to use**: evaluating a rival project or your own motivation.
**How**: ask (1) is the mediator named (external) or demonized (internal)? (2) is the object still in focus or has rival-obsession replaced it? (3) who's claiming credit for battles they didn't fight?
**Trade-offs**: Girard's frame can itself become a purity-spiral instrument; use diagnostically, not as verdict.

## Dark-forest enclosure
**When to use**: building communities, treasuries, or knowledge-bases that must survive hostile visibility.
**How**: semi-permeable boundaries (prokaryote model) — opaque by default, selectively revealed; shielded rails for the treasury; published opt-out guides; lore that travels by gossip.
**Trade-offs**: enclosure invites "why so secret?" suspicion; requires trust rituals (comhrún) to onboard.

## Minor-calendar timekeeping (Valens)
**When to use**: designing ledgers/protocols where surveillance is the threat model.
**How**: private by default, verifiable by initiator, discretion in revelation ("some, but not all, transactions could be revealed, when necessary").
**Trade-offs**: irregularity reduces auditability for third parties — that's the point.

## Eternal-return selection (Nietzsche/Deleuze)
**When to use**: deciding what practices/projects to eternally repeat.
**How**: ask whether affirmation (difference-generating) or reaction (difference-denying) would compound if repeated infinitely; kill reactive loops before they self-cancel and take the cycle down.
**Trade-offs**: hard to self-diagnose — reaction feels like vigilance from inside.

## Oikonomos funding design (Lakshmi)
**When to use**: any movement treasury or public-goods mechanism.
**How**: produce + fairly redistribute for communal well-being (the original economy); reject wealth-antagonism; pool purchasing power as a squad; co-own appreciating assets.
**Trade-offs**: holding wealth makes you a target; requires the dark-forest enclosure above.

## Assertion over negation (Dunkel)
**When to use**: choosing between protesting the old world and building the new.
**How**: the synthesis isn't the antithesis — inherit the cryptography of cypherpunk + the care of solarpunk, drop defeatism and naivety; build under the canopy.
**Trade-offs**: assertion is slower and quieter; visibility calibrated to moonlight.

## Solar-audit of epistemics (Hermes)
**When to use**: any data-collection, archive, or transparency decision.
**How**: ask "is this illumination or endarkenment?" Both are legitimate; the solar frame alone makes concealment taboo. Apply sousveillance (transparency for the powerful, privacy for the weak); practice deletion against generative entrenchment.
**Trade-offs**: deletion destroys context too; decide what the scaffold keeps.

## Comhrún protocol (O'Leary)
**When to use**: forming trusted cells across distance.
**How**: shared secret + common purpose as a single bond (the Irish word merges them); exoteric writing to the curious, comhrún to the committed.
**Trade-offs**: vertical (esoteric) encryption breeds hierarchy; horizontal (exoteric) encryption leaves the work of decoding to the reader.
