# Chapter 3: Necroprimitivism Rising — Wassim Z. Alsindi

## Core Idea
Proof-of-work Bitcoin is an *indifference engine* — a "necroprimitivist" assemblage of thermo-Austrian scarcity economics, petro-masculine identity politics, and code-as-law immutability that would tolerate ecological collapse rather than change its consensus mechanism: "it is more prophetable to imagine an end to the world than a change to Bitcoin's consensus."

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Necroprimitivism**: 0x Salon's compound diagnosis of Bitcoin-maxi culture — nihilistic union of (1) thermo-Austrianism, (2) petro-masculinity, (3) immutable whitepaperism/code-as-law. Its manifesto inversion: "If nature is unjust, destroy nature."
- **Timechain / chronaissance**: Bitcoin as first and foremost a *decentralized timestamping server* ("Satoshi used 'timestamp' fourteen times in the whitepaper") — a new temporal regime (time) divorced from celestial cycles. Bitcoin = event-ordering system; the "chronaissance" is the emergence of new abstract time.
- **Indifference engine / sensorship-resistance**: PoW is insensitive to externalities; difficulty adjustment gives it an insatiable-by-design appetite for energy with *no capability to discriminate between power sources*. Beyond censorship-resistance lies sensorship-resistance — the network cannot even perceive what it burns.
- **Determinacy-as-a-service**: certainty is contingent and must be paid for in perpetuity; "timechains don't pivot, they fork" — ossification as schism-avoidance.
- **Petro-masculinity** (Cara Daggett): fossil systems as identity-formation and authoritarian desire, not mere profit — maps onto "techno-lumberjack" Bitcoin demographics and the petriarchy.
- **Thermo-Austrianism**: Austrian-school commodity-scarcity logic (Menger/Jevons: scarce, limited supply, costly to produce, facile authentication) materialized as thermoeconomic hashing — a *materialist scarcity logic applied inside an environment built for abundance*.
- **Cockroach of money / Nakamoto's Basilisk**: unkillable (zombie chains reanimate; forks schism) — hence change must come from *inside* the faithful, hence "Curb Their Malthusiasm."

## Key Concepts
- **Necroeconomics / necroeconomic apotheosis**: extraction logics taken to terminal conclusion (Montag's necro-economics of Adam Smith).
- **FOMO Sapiens**: humanity as apex predator running extraction/exploitation to grotesque completion.
- **Machine-faith**: "No Gods, No Masters, Only Rules" — an Open-Source Cathedral with ordained priest-developers and miner-bishops; "code is law" as transubstantiation ("transaction as transubstantiation, hashes as sacrament").
- **The price of anarchy in Bitcoin's agora**: insiders (developers, miners) with hands on the pumps; a central bank without a government.
- **Schismogenesis**: protocol "upgrades" risk full network secession — change-resistance is structural, not incidental.

## Mental Models
- Think of PoW as an *externalities firewall*: the protocol literally cannot see what its energy purchases — so critiques must target the incentive shape, not miner morals.
- Use the *temple hierarchy* lens for "decentralized" systems: every leaderless network still ordains priests (client maintainers) and bishops (block producers).
- Use "more prophetable to imagine the end of the world..." as a general purity-test detector for any ossified protocol culture.

## Anti-patterns
- **Coal-is-law**: treating the energy source's damage as either denied (#LearnToCoal) or as acceptable sacrifice "to serve the coin."
- **Whitepaperism**: immutable founding text as scripture — change-resistance as piety rather than engineering judgment.
- **Abundance-blind scarcity**: importing commodity-money scarcity into a virtual environment "ideally suited to logics of abundance."

## Worked Example
The Daggett quote block: petro-masculinity defined and applied — fossil fuels "contribute to making identities," fossil use as "violent reactionary practice" under Anthropocene anxiety. Alsindi's move is to graft this onto Bitcoin: the energy-hunger isn't a bug to maxis, it's identity. Hence the Steve Barbour #LearnToCoal tweet sits in the essay as specimen, not parody — the necroprimitivist genuinely proposes renaming coal plants "forest fertilizer plants."

## Key Takeaways
1. Bitcoin is fundamentally an event-ordering/timekeeping system; the currency is an application layered on the timechain.
2. PoW's design virtue (indifference, difficulty adjustment) is precisely its ecological vice — the mechanism cannot discriminate.
3. Ossification is encoded conservatism: forks, not pivots — and the faithful defend continuity economically AND ideologically.
4. The necroprimitivist syncretism: thermo-Austrian scarcity + petro-masculine identity + code-as-law immutability = tolerating hothouse Earth.
5. The essay's wager: since the network can't be killed from outside, persuasion must happen *inside* the faith — curb the Malthusiasm.
