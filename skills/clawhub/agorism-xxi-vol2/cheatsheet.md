# Cheatsheet — Agorism in the 21st Century, Vol. 2
Decision rules distilled from the volume.

## Identity & Strategy
| Situation | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Authority strikes you | Say "of course, according to plan," proceed | Super-ordination of voluntary over compulsory (Ch 2) |
| Choosing a high-stakes act | Attach a secessionary gesture legible beyond yourself | Symbols outlive actors (Ch 2) |
| Rival project obsesses you | Audit mediation: named model or demonized rival? | Internal mediation loses the object (Ch 2) |
| Purity-spiraling community member | It's mediated desire wearing metaphysical autonomy | Girard trap (Ch 2) |
| Protest vs build | Build the synthesis; assert, don't negate | Prison of inevitability is the real enemy (Ch 7) |
| Movement feels righteous but resourceless | Diagnose poverty-as-identity; build squad wealth | Movements win with logistics (Ch 9) |

## Privacy & Architecture
| Situation | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Designing a ledger | Shielded by default, revealed with discretion | Minor calendar: time about revelation (Ch 5) |
| Public good without defense | Add enclosures before scaling the commons | Bigger commons = bigger capture vectors (Ch 6) |
| Transparency vs privacy fight | Neither — demand sousveillance instead | Transparency for the powerful, privacy for the weak (Ch 6) |
| Archive growth | Tombstone versions; delete to enable change | Generative entrenchment cancels futures (Ch 6) |
| Boundary design | Semi-permeable: full open and full closed both kill | Penumbral dynamics (Ch 6) |
| Community under pressure | Go dark-forest: opacity is the seed vault | Encryption regenerates what empire clears (Ch 4) |

## Time & Cycles
| Situation | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dark period (movement/personal) | New moon = passage, not ending; three days, then return | Lunar logic: reversible extinction (Ch 4) |
| Evaluating a repeated practice | Would affirmation or reaction compound eternally? | Return is a selection filter (Ch 4) |
| Choosing infrastructure | Which calendar does it serve — extraction or vernacular life? | Solar time → production; lunar time → conspiracy (Ch 5) |
| Institutional timing | A cycle's opening is prepared by decades; invest anyway | LunarDAO was positioned before it began (Ch 9) |

## Collective Action
| Situation | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Calling a group a squad | Require belief + methodology + strategy + goal | Maxis aren't squads (Ch 9) |
| Funding commons | Voluntary opt-in, trackable outcomes, ZK where possible | Non-coercive collectivism (Ch 8) |
| Political unit design | Match governance to affinity, not geography | Homogeneous systems are obsolete (Ch 9) |
| Vocabulary drift in your movement | Name it — whoever defines the words defines the movement | Semantic shift hollowed crypto (Ch 9) |
| State vs capital framing | Both are faces of the 5,000-year domination system | Don't fight one face with the other (Ch 7) |

## Thresholds & Tells
- **Comfortable state of discomfort** = poverty-trap tell (Ch 9)
- **"We're even more of a target than X"** = stolen-valor/stolen-danger tell (Ch 2)
- **"There is no other way"** = liberalism's third lie; treat as attack surface (Ch 9)
- **Total transparency demanded of the weak** = solar epistemology in the wild (Ch 6)
- **More prophetable to imagine the end of the world than protocol change** = ossification pathology (Ch 3)
