# Glossary — Agorism in the 21st Century, Vol. 2

**Anti-economy** — capitalism as negation of oikonomos; profit-extraction parasitic on the original household-wealth logic (Ch 9)
**Becoming-desert / Becoming-forest** — O'Leary's paired movements: colonization as deforestation, resistance as re-forestation of culture (Ch 4)
**Block time** — blockchain's invented abstract time; linear, discrete, unforgiving ("blockchains never forget") (Ch 5)
**Cethern Coille** — Irish "forest-forces"; guerillas using the wood as natural encryption (Ch 4)
**Chronaissance** — Alsindi's term for the new temporal regime inaugurated by the timechain (Ch 3)
**Comhrún** — Irish: shared secret AND common purpose; encryption as intimacy (Ch 4)
**Dark forest** — anonymity/opacity as protective canopy for developing moral and political society (Ch 4, 9)
**Death Athletic** — Wilson/Sloterdijk: freedom-tech practice as acrobatic discipline with one's social/physical life at stake (Ch 2)
**Democratic modernity** — Öcalan's post-nation-state confederalism of self-becoming units (Ch 4)
**Determinacy-as-a-service** — blockchain certainty paid for in perpetuity; "timechains don't pivot, they fork" (Ch 3)
**Endarkenment** — Hermes: concealment/deletion as legitimate epistemic praxis; the right to forget (Ch 6)
**Eternal return (as selection)** — Nietzsche/Deleuze: return filters — affirmation compounds, reaction self-cancels (Ch 4)
**Exoteric / Esoteric writing** — Strauss: horizontally encrypted (studious reader can decode) vs vertically encrypted (initiates only) (Ch 4)
**Extromission** — pre-modern vision theory: eyes radiate outward; eye-as-sun (Ch 6)
**Generative entrenchment** — crystallized states disable alternatives; why deletion enables change (Ch 6)
**Geas** — Celtic spiritual prohibition; sacred constraint as culture-preservation (Ch 4)
**Indifference engine** — PoW Bitcoin: insensitive to externalities, cannot discriminate energy sources; sensorship-resistance (Ch 3)
**Kali Yuga** — the dark age; Eliade's quintessential lunar myth — its end seeds the golden age (Ch 4)
**Lunar mansions** — 27/28 star-conjunct daily residences of the moon; vernacular time with talismanic action-qualities (Ch 5)
**Lunar epistemology** — concealment as legitimate epistemic act, entangled with illumination (Ch 6)
**Minor calendar** — Valens (after D&G's minor literature): shielded blockchains as timekeeping of revelation vs solar transparent time (Ch 5)
**Necroprimitivism** — Alsindi/0x Salon: thermo-Austrian scarcity + petro-masculinity + code-as-law immutability tolerating ecological collapse (Ch 3)
**Oikonomos** — original economy: household management, matriarchal culture of giving, produce + redistribute for communal well-being (Ch 9)
**Penumbras** — semi-permeable light/dark boundaries; full openness or closure both kill (Ch 6)
**Petro-masculinity** — Daggett: fossil systems as identity-formation and authoritarian desire (Ch 3)
**Prison of inevitability** — the enforced sense that no alternative can exist; lunarpunk exits it (Ch 7)
**Ressentiment** — Girard/Nietzsche: impotent hatred at mediated failure projected onto the mediator (Ch 2)
**Rún** — Irish: secrecy/love/mystery/resolution woven together (Ch 4)
**Secessionary gesture** — Baudrillard/Wilson: symbols burying the name of God; telescoped independence (Ch 2)
**Shadow cone** — the absurd contest's suspending/foreclosing effect on authority's other pretensions (Ch 2)
**Sousveillance** — "transparency for the powerful, privacy for the weak"; inverted pyramid (Ch 6)
**Squad wealth** — Lakshmi: collective knowledge/resources/infrastructure as the movement's power and first defense (Ch 9)
**Thermo-Austrianism** — Austrian commodity-scarcity logic materialized as thermoeconomic hashing (Ch 3)
**Timechain** — Bitcoin as fundamentally a decentralized timestamping server; event-ordering before currency (Ch 3, 5)
**Vernacular time** — nightly observable, peer-scale timekeeping unlinked from production (Ch 5)
**Xwebûn** — Kurmanji: self-being-and-becoming; Öcalan's autonomous political unit (Ch 4)
**Xwedayî** — self-given/self-creating (the wild apricot); self-causation (Ch 4)
