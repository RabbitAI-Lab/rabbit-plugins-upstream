## Description:

Tracks upcoming A-share lockup expiries in a dated calendar with sourced holder, share, total-capital ratio, and cost details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huhoo](https://clawhub.ai/user/huhoo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users tracking A-share disclosures can organize future restricted-share unlock dates, holder types, share counts, sourced ratios, and issue prices into a traceable calendar.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China
