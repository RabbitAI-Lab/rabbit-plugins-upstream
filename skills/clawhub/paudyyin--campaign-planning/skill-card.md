## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams and business operators use this skill to plan campaigns, product launches, content calendars, channel mixes, budgets, KPIs, approval flows, and post-campaign reviews. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Campaign plans may contain inaccurate market, competitor, budget, or KPI assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans against current business data, validated market research, and campaign performance history before operational use. <br>
Risk: Generated campaign actions, claims, or approval checklists may miss legal, brand, or compliance requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Route externally facing claims, budget commitments, and legally sensitive campaign materials through the appropriate internal reviewers before launch. <br>
