## Description:

Steuerklassenwechsel fürs Elterngeld – rechnet mit den Zahlen des Nutzers aus, ob sich der Wechsel (IV/IV, III/V, V/III) lohnt, bis wann er beantragt sein muss (7-von-12-Regel, § 2c Abs. 3 BEEG; Wirkung ab dem Folgemonat, § 39 Abs. 6 EStG; Antrag bis 30. November) und was ein Monat zu spät kostet. Für werdende Eltern in Deutschland.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users who are expecting a child in Germany use the skill to compare how possible tax-class changes may affect estimated parental benefits and identify when an application would need to be filed.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated benefit amounts and application deadlines may not match an individual case or current official requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm estimates and deadlines with the responsible Elterngeldstelle before making financial decisions.

Risk: The linked external planner's privacy practices have not been verified, and entering personal details may expose more information than necessary.

Mitigation: Review the planner's privacy terms before entering information and share only details needed for the estimate.
