## Description:

Writes a UGC-style unboxing scene in Phantra: a person opening a package on their phone camera (the sealed box, the open, the product lifted out, a first touch of its details, a first impression in their own words), rendered against their own face, product and packaging photos. Use when someone asks for an unboxing, a "what arrived", a first-look or a package-opening video. NOT for a verdict after use (ugc-review), a general talk-to-camera ad (ugc-ad) or a product-only film (product-shots).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators use this agent to write a short, first-impression unboxing scene from their own face, product, and packaging photos. It structures the scene into three or four shots and keeps spoken lines and product details grounded in what the creator supplies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photos of a person or package may expose personal details or information the user is not authorized to share.

Mitigation: Use only photos the user is authorized and comfortable sharing with Phantra, and remove addresses or other personal details from packaging images.

Risk: Rendering is paid, and the prompt or price may not reflect what the user intends to produce.

Mitigation: Show the prompt and quote, review both with the user, and get explicit approval before rendering.
