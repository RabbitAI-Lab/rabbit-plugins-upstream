## Description:

Turns a teacher-supplied wrong-item script and authorized still images into short talking explanation clips, one clip per still.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to plan and produce short talking clips explaining mistakes from their own wrong-item scripts and authorized images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connecting Beatra grants broad account access through a shared device token.

Mitigation: Review the account connection before installing and use only an account you are comfortable connecting.

Risk: Selected images, voice samples, or student material may be uploaded to Beatra.

Mitigation: Share only material for which you have the required rights and consent; avoid sensitive material.

Risk: Voice, speech, and video generation can spend account credits.

Mitigation: Check live prices and explicitly approve each paid stage before submission.

Risk: Package-owned files may update automatically without separate confirmation.

Mitigation: Review the update policy and disable automatic updates using the documented update command if unwanted.
