## Description:

Plan and generate short product and e-commerce videos with Makernice, such as product showcases, ad clips and marketplace listing videos. Covers choosing a mode, writing the prompt, picking aspect ratio and resolution, checking the price and running the job.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and product marketers use the skill to plan and generate short product showcase, advertising, and marketplace listing videos from product descriptions or public product photos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos are provided through public HTTPS links and may be shared with Makernice.

Mitigation: Use only public product images the user is comfortable sharing with Makernice.

Risk: Video generation incurs a charge, and results blocked by safety review may still be charged.

Mitigation: Show the user the estimated prices and obtain approval before generation; keep each job within the approved amount.

Risk: Server-side billing caps, retry deduplication, and data handling were not independently verified.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's per-job price limit and stable request ID guidance, and avoid sharing sensitive images or information.
