## Description:

Plan and generate short product and e-commerce videos with Makernice by choosing a mode, writing prompts, selecting aspect ratios and resolution, checking prices, and running the job.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlesaitime](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlesaitime)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can plan short product showcases, advertising clips, and marketplace videos, then request generation through Makernice. The workflow helps them choose a model mode, prepare a prompt and product image, review costs, and track the generated clip.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product photos and descriptions are sent to Makernice, and the package does not establish the service's retention practices.

Mitigation: Use photos suitable for public hosting and descriptions you are comfortable sharing with Makernice.

Risk: Video generation can incur charges, including when a result is blocked by safety review; billing safeguards are not established by the package.

Mitigation: Review estimated prices before generation, approve a per-job amount or clear total budget, and track the combined cost of multi-clip requests.
