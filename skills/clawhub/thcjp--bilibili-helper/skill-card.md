## Description: <br>
Bilibili Helper assists Bilibili creators with video titles, descriptions, tags, spoken scripts, publishing strategy, and creator operations guidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External Bilibili creators, MCN content teams, and brand account operators use this skill to draft upload-ready video copy, tags, scripts, and practical publishing guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated titles, tags, scripts, or publishing advice may be inaccurate, misleading, or unsuitable for Bilibili's current moderation expectations. <br>
Mitigation: Review and edit generated copy before publishing or reusing it on Bilibili. <br>
Risk: Optional callback URL and shell command examples could send data to an untrusted destination or run an untrusted script if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing callback URLs or running optional shell commands unless the destination and script are understood and trusted. <br>
