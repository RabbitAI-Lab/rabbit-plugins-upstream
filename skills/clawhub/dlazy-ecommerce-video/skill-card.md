## Description:

ecommerce video, product video, shopping ad video, ecommerce video generator — turn a product (photos or link) into a conversion-focused ecommerce ad video. Use when the user has a product and wants a store, TikTok, or ad video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
