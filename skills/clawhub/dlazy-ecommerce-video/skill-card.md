## Description:

Helps turn product photos, links, or specifications into ecommerce advertising videos for stores and social channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers, marketers, and other external users create product ads and shopping videos from product information or images, including multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be uploaded to the third-party dLazy service.

Mitigation: Attach only files intended for dLazy and review prompts before submission.

Risk: Signing in can store a dLazy API key on the local device.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and rotate or revoke the key through the provider dashboard if needed.
