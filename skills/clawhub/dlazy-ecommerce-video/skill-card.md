## Description:

Turns product photos, documents, or store listings into ecommerce ad videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and marketers use this skill to create product ads and shopping videos from product images, documents, or listing links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files may be sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Only submit product content and files you intend to share with dLazy.

Risk: The external CLI may save an API key locally during login.

Mitigation: Protect local credentials and review the pinned CLI package before installing it.
