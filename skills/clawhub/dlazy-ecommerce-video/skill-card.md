## Description:

Turns product images, documents, or listing links into ecommerce ad videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and marketers use this skill to create product ads and shopping videos from product materials or ecommerce listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The dLazy CLI stores an API key locally or accepts one through an environment variable.

Mitigation: Protect the key and rotate or revoke it if exposed.

Risk: Attached product files are uploaded to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Attach only files intended for dLazy processing; avoid sensitive files.
