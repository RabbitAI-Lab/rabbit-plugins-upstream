## Description:

Turns product photos, details, or listing links into conversion-focused ecommerce ad videos for stores, TikTok, and other shopping channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers, small businesses, and marketing teams use this skill to turn product details, photos, or listing links into short shopping and advertising videos for channels such as TikTok Shop.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan had partial coverage with medium confidence and flagged organization project listing and formatting padding; its benign verdict does not establish that the skill is risk-free.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI install details and consider whether listing projects in the active organization fits your use before installing.

Risk: Messages are sent to dLazy services, and local files attached with --files are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Attach only product material you are comfortable sending to the service, and omit sensitive or customer data.
