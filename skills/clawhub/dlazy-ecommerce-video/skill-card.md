## Description:

Turns product photos or listings into conversion-focused ecommerce videos for stores, TikTok, or ads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers, marketers, and agents use this skill to turn product photos, listings, or product documents into shopping and advertising videos, with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flagged organization project listing as outside the skill's stated video-generation purpose, and the review was partial.

Mitigation: List organization projects only when appropriate; review the project selected before continuing.

Risk: Files attached to a request are uploaded to dLazy media storage.

Mitigation: Attach only files intended to be processed by the dLazy service.
