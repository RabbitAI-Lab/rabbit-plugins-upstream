## Description:

Helps turn product photos, specifications, or listings into ecommerce advertising videos with optional multilingual voiceover and a virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and marketers use this skill to create product advertising videos for online stores, social commerce, and cross-border campaigns from product materials or listing links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The dLazy CLI stores an API key in local configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and rotate or revoke the key if it is exposed.

Risk: Prompts and options are sent to dLazy, and attached files are uploaded to dLazy storage.

Mitigation: Send only prompts and files that you are willing to share with the service.
