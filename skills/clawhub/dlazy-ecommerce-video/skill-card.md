## Description:

Turns product photos, listings, or specifications into conversion-focused ecommerce video ads through dLazy's hosted service.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Merchants and marketing teams use the skill to create product advertising videos from photos, product details, or store listings for ecommerce and social channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached product files are sent to dLazy's hosted service.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing secrets or confidential product assets unless you intend to disclose them to dLazy.

Risk: The CLI can store an API key locally and may be installed globally.

Mitigation: Protect the local API key and use the pinned npx invocation if you prefer not to install a global CLI.
