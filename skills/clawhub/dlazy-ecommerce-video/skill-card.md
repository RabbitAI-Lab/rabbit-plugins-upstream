## Description:

Turns product photos, links, or product materials into ecommerce ad videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn product photos, listings, and product documents into ecommerce ads for stores, TikTok, and cross-border campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached product materials are sent to dLazy, and usage may consume credits.

Mitigation: Submit only product materials you intend to share with dLazy, select the intended project when continuing work, and check credit usage.

Risk: The external CLI and hosted backend were not audited, and the CLI can save organization credentials locally.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI package before installation and protect or revoke saved organization credentials as needed.
