## Description:

Turns product photos, product links, or product information into conversion-focused ecommerce videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and marketers use this skill to create shopping ads and product videos for store listings and social commerce from product photos, listings, or product documentation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached product files are sent to dLazy's hosted service, and attachments are uploaded to its media storage.

Mitigation: Send only materials you are authorized to share; avoid sensitive or confidential files unless approved.

Risk: The CLI can save an organization API key in local configuration.

Mitigation: Protect the local configuration and rotate or revoke the key if it is exposed.

Risk: Continuing in the wrong project can apply a prompt to a different saved session.

Mitigation: Check the project list and confirm the intended project before continuing.
