# deadlines.md (Australia GST)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Simplified references for common cases; actual deadlines depend on registration type — verify with the ATO or a registered BAS agent.

## Filing Frequency & Deadlines (BAS)
- Quarterly (standard): due the 28th of the month after quarter end —
  Q1 (Jul–Sep) → Oct 28; Q2 (Oct–Dec) → Feb 28; Q3 (Jan–Mar) → Apr 28; Q4 (Apr–Jun) → Jul 28.
- Monthly (large turnover): due the 21st of the following month.
- Annual (voluntary small registrants): aligned with the income tax return.

## Usage Notes
- Agent-lodged BAS often receives extended deadlines; if lodged via an agent, dates may differ.
- Penalties accrue per 28-day unit overdue — late zero-activity BAS still requires lodgment.
- If the user's assigned frequency is unknown, mark "cannot determine."
