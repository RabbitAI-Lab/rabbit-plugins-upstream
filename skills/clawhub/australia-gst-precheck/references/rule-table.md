# rule-table.md (Australia GST)

> Data year: 2026 (compiled 2026-10). Rules change; when citing, note "subject to the latest guidance of the Australian Taxation Office (ATO)."

## Rate & Classification
- Flat 10% GST.
- GST-free: basic food, health & medical, education, childcare, exports.
- Input-taxed: financial supplies, residential rent (rare for goods sellers).

## Registration Threshold
- A$75,000 GST turnover (current + projected 12 months).
- Sales where an electronic distribution platform (EDP) is the deemed supplier do NOT count toward the seller's own threshold.

## Low-Value Imported Goods (LVIG, since 2018-07-01)
- Consignment value ≤ A$1,000: GST charged at the point of sale by the seller, or by the EDP (Amazon/eBay are deemed suppliers for marketplace sales).
- Consignment value > A$1,000: GST (and any duties) collected at the border — charging GST at checkout on these creates double taxation.
- Mixed consignments: assess per-consignment treatment; when in doubt, mark "cannot determine" and advise agent review.

## Channel & Registration
- Marketplace sales: EDP collects & remits.
- Own website: seller registers (ABN + GST) and collects once over the threshold.
- Simplified non-resident GST registration exists (limited input tax credits).

## Export & Pricing
- Exports are GST-free with evidence of export within 60 days.
- Australian Consumer Law: consumer prices must be GST-inclusive — ex-GST display is a compliance risk.
