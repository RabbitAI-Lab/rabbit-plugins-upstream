## Description:

Access Futunn market, financial, account, order, position, news, and community information through an OOMOL-connected account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oomol](https://clawhub.ai/user/oomol)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors and analysts can retrieve and review Futunn market data, financial metrics, account balances, positions, orders, news, and community discussions through their connected account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup may execute unverified remote software without a clear approval step, and the connector mediates sensitive financial information.

Mitigation: Review setup behavior, verify the CLI release separately with approval, review or disable automatic skill synchronization, and connect only the Futunn account needed.
