## Description: <br>
Pre-implementation exploration workflow for deep interviews, approach comparison, and design docs when exploring vague feature ideas, clarifying ambiguous requirements, or comparing approaches before coding. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[iliaal](https://clawhub.ai/user/iliaal) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineering collaborators use this workflow to clarify what to build before implementation by interviewing stakeholders, comparing approaches, and capturing decisions in a design document. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can write and commit brainstorm documents without enough guardrails around what gets committed. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only where document creation under docs/brainstorms is acceptable, keep commits opt-in, and verify the agent stages only the generated brainstorm document. <br>
