## Description:

Reviews psychology papers, research plans, proposals, or manuscript sections using a structured academic peer-review framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yunyue619](https://clawhub.ai/user/yunyue619)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, authors, and academic reviewers use this skill to receive structured, journal-style critique of psychology manuscripts, proposals, study designs, methods sections, analyses, and partial drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad peer-review triggers may route non-psychology review requests to this skill.

Mitigation: Keep routing metadata scoped to psychology manuscripts, research plans, study designs, and related academic materials.

Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese depending on its source instructions and user context.

Mitigation: Specify the desired response language when consistent language output is required.
