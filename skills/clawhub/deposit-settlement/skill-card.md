## Description:

Settle a US rental security deposit after move-out with the Tektolo deposit settlement engine - the return deadline, which deductions are allowed and how much after depreciation, interest owed, the refund, penalty exposure and a draft itemized statement, every rule cited.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tektolo](https://clawhub.ai/user/tektolo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Landlords, property managers, and tenants use the skill to assess US rental-deposit return deadlines, deductions, interest, refunds, and potential statutory penalties after move-out. It can also provide cited state or covered-city deposit rules and a draft itemized statement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rental amounts, dates, and deduction details are sent to Tektolo when using the calculation.

Mitigation: Exclude names, addresses, account numbers, and identifying details from descriptions, and review Tektolo's privacy policy.

Risk: A deposit assessment or draft statement may need review against the facts and cited rules before use.

Mitigation: Check the cited rules and review the draft before signing or sending anything.
