## Description:

Helps landlords, property managers, and tenants assess US security deposit return deadlines, deductions, interest, refunds, and potential penalties, with cited rules and a draft itemized statement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tektolo](https://clawhub.ai/user/tektolo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Landlords and property managers use this skill to prepare a move-out deposit settlement and draft itemized statement. Tenants use it to check whether a refund was timely and whether listed deductions may be allowed; both should review the cited rules rather than treat the result as legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (50 states and Washington, DC; specified city rules where covered)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rental dates, amounts, and deduction descriptions are sent to Tektolo's service.

Mitigation: Share only necessary case facts; omit names, addresses, account numbers, and other unnecessary personal information.

Risk: A settlement summary or draft statement could be mistaken for definitive legal advice, especially when facts or local rules are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review cited rules, warnings, and missing facts before relying on the result; treat it as legal-information support, not legal advice.
