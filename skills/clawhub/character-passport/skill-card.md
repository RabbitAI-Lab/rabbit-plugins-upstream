## Description:

Helps creators keep AI-video characters and hero props consistent with detailed passports, turnaround sheets, expression and voice notes, and shot prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Filmmakers and generative-video creators use this skill to define characters and hero props consistently, prepare reference sheets and voice guidance, and build prompts for repeatable shots.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
