## Description:

Designs consistent AI video characters and hero props through detailed passports, turnaround and expression sheets, measured voice notes, and prompt guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Video creators and agent users use this skill to define characters and hero props before production, then keep their appearance, acting, voice, and behavior consistent across generated shots.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports that an unauthenticated screenshot server may expose local files.

Mitigation: Review and restrict the screenshot server before use; keep sensitive files out of any location it can serve and do not expose it to untrusted networks.

Risk: Uploads to hosted image or voice services may expose private or identifiable material.

Mitigation: Use only material that is appropriate to send to the selected service, and remove private or identifying details when they are not needed.

Risk: ClawScan reports capabilities that may not match the declared character-design purpose, and notes that the scan was partial.

Mitigation: Review the reported verdict and inspect the skill's behavior before enabling it; treat uninspected content as unassessed.
