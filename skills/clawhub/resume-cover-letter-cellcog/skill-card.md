## Description:

AI resume and cover letter generation powered by CellCog. ATS-optimized resumes, CVs, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, career documents — PDF or DOCX. Research-first approach that analyzes target roles before writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellcog](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellcog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People preparing for a job search use the skill to create or refine resumes, CVs, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, and portfolio documents for target roles. They provide career details and preferences, then receive career materials in formats such as PDF or DOCX.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume and career details are sent to CellCog to create documents.

Mitigation: Share only information you intend to disclose, redact confidential employer metrics, and review CellCog privacy terms and usage charges.

Risk: The skill depends on an external Python package.

Mitigation: Check dependency versions before installation.
