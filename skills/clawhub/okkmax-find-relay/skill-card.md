## Description:

Helps users choose or compare AI API relays for Claude, GPT/Codex, or Gemini using OkkMax measurements of price, model verification, uptime, and latency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[okkmx](https://clawhub.ai/user/okkmx)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers use the skill to shortlist or compare AI API relays for a selected model and priorities such as price, model verification, or stability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Relay measurements are dated third-party evidence and do not guarantee a relay's safety or current service quality.

Mitigation: Show the data timestamp and verify current pricing, availability, and security directly with the relay before relying on a comparison.
