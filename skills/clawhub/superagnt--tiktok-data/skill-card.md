Developers, marketing teams, and research teams use this skill to retrieve TikTok video and creator details and search accounts and videos for content research, social listening, and creator intelligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries and authentication requests are sent to the third-party superagnt API, and API use may consume credits.

Mitigation: Keep the API key private, review credit use, and consider the documented scoped MCP option.

Risk: The documented shell examples need correction before execution.

Mitigation: Review and correct shell quoting before running any example.
