## Description:

Guides product, engineering, and operations teams through a commercialization-focused SOP for unified backlogs, sprint cadence, release veto rules, feedback loops, and technical debt management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gingiris-1031](https://clawhub.ai/user/gingiris-1031)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External product, engineering, and operations leaders use this skill to align sprint planning, issue triage, release readiness, user-feedback loops, and technical debt decisions around shared commercial goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked during broad sprint-planning or team-alignment conversations where its process advice is only partly relevant.

Mitigation: Confirm the product stage, team roles, and decision scope before applying the SOP.

Risk: Release-veto and prioritization guidance could be followed mechanically without matching the team's governance model.

Mitigation: Adapt P0 definitions, veto authority, and escalation rules to the team's accountable owners and approval process.
