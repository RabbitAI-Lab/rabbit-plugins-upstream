## Description: <br>
Check data compliance with construction standards and validate data against ISO 19650, IFC, COBie, UniFormat, MasterFormat, and Omniclass standards. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[datadrivenconstruction](https://clawhub.ai/user/datadrivenconstruction) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Construction project teams, BIM managers, and data engineers use this skill to check project records, batches, or datasets against construction data standards and receive pass, failure, warning, and issue summaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Compliance results may be incomplete or advisory for standards coverage that is not fully implemented. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers confirm findings before relying on results for contractual, regulatory, or project governance decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill may read user-provided project files and write requested report exports. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only intended project data and review export paths before allowing files to be written. <br>
