## Description:

Create PDF invoices, estimates, receipts, contracts, non-disclosure agreements, and real estate documents with previews and optional payment links. Built by Moosh Works (https://mooshworks.com).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business users and their agents can create and preview invoices, estimates, receipts, contracts, non-disclosure agreements, and real estate documents, convert estimates to invoices, and prepare receipts from payment details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creating a PDF may also save contact details and start automated follow-up, without clear instructions for approving or disabling those effects.

Mitigation: Before submitting real business or client information, confirm where customer sync stores data, who receives follow-ups, and how to disable both. Use a guest session and synthetic details for evaluation.
