## Description: <br>
Turn one topic into a finished Vox-style paper-collage explainer or ad video using Atlas Cloud API and local ffmpeg for scripting, collage keyframes, motion, voice-over, music, captions, and assembly. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[alisa0808](https://clawhub.ai/user/alisa0808) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, developers, and media teams use this skill to turn a topic, product, or person into a narrated paper-collage explainer, short ad, or scrapbook-style tribute. The workflow guides beat mapping, visual style selection, keyframe generation, motion, narration, music, captions, and final ffmpeg assembly. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, generated media, scripts, images, brand assets, or personal references may be sent to Atlas Cloud or related model providers. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sensitive or proprietary inputs unless the user has reviewed provider terms and data handling for the workflow. <br>
Risk: The skill references companion scripts and reference files that may not be present in the provided artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Verify required scripts, references, Atlas Cloud access, ffmpeg, ffprobe, and Pillow before relying on the automated workflow. <br>
