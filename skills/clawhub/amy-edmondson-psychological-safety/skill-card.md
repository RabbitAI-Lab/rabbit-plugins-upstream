## Description:

Amy Edmondson psychological safety framework covering the definition, the psychological safety by accountability 2x2 matrix, three leadership behaviors for building psychological safety, and the Right Kind of Wrong failure taxonomy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
