## Description:

Provides Tiantai Buddhist literature retrieval and citation verification using the 天台藏全集2026 knowledge base, with simple Q&A and deeper research modes that require source citations and CBETA line-level links when available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and researchers use this skill to retrieve Tiantai Buddhist source passages, verify doctrinal citations, compare related texts, and produce structured research notes with explicit document, volume, chapter, and CBETA line references where available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill depends on access to the named IMA knowledge base for best results.

Mitigation: Confirm knowledge-base access before relying on outputs, and disclose when source coverage is unavailable or incomplete.

Risk: Broad Tiantai-related questions may activate the skill outside narrow citation-checking tasks.

Mitigation: Keep answers grounded in retrieved Tiantai source passages and clearly state when the available material does not cover a claim.
