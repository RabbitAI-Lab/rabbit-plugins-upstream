## Description:

天台藏全集2026 helps agents search and verify Tiantai Buddhist texts from the named knowledge base, producing source-backed answers with document, volume, and section citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and researchers use this skill to retrieve Tiantai school source passages, verify quotations, and build citation-backed answers or deeper literature reviews. It is best suited to focused Tiantai Canon lookup rather than broad Buddhist studies coverage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally narrows Tiantai-related answers to one named knowledge base, which can omit relevant material outside that collection.

Mitigation: Use it for source-backed Tiantai Canon citation work and clearly state coverage limits when the knowledge base does not contain enough evidence.

Risk: Responses could become misleading if citations are missing, incomplete, or treated as broader Buddhist studies evidence.

Mitigation: Require document name, volume, and section citations for claims, and ask for explicit web search or another corpus when broader coverage is needed.
