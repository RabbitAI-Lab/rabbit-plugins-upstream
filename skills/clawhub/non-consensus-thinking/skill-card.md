## Description:

Guides agents through testing whether a well-supported minority view has a real edge over consensus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to pressure-test decisions in crowded markets or situations where mainstream belief may be wrong. It helps them document evidence, a specific advantage, downside asymmetry, and conditions for updating their view.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user could rely on an unverified or outdated minority-view case when making a consequential decision.

Mitigation: Treat the audit as a thinking aid and verify supporting evidence before making consequential decisions.
