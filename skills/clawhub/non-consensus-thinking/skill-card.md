## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a disciplined audit of consensus beliefs, possible errors, user-specific edge, asymmetry, and update triggers for competitive or high-stakes decisions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, operators, and strategy teams use this skill to pressure-test a non-consensus position before committing capital, time, hiring, research, or market-entry effort. It helps an agent structure the decision around observable consensus evidence, the mechanism behind consensus, the user's edge, asymmetry, and a pre-committed review trigger. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the framework as financial, strategic, or other high-stakes advice instead of decision support. <br>
Mitigation: Keep final high-stakes decisions under human control and review the audit before acting. <br>
Risk: A non-consensus position can be misleading if the user cannot verify the cited consensus evidence or name a real edge. <br>
Mitigation: Require observable sources for the consensus claim, validate cited sources, and stop when no specific information or analytical advantage can be identified. <br>
