## Description:

Iatros（诺康） helps agents create calm, evidence-conscious health-literacy and health-communication content while staying within general education boundaries.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

External communicators, content teams, and agents use this skill to draft public-facing health-literacy articles, handout copy, rumor-checking responses, and signature language. It is limited to general health education and does not provide personalized diagnosis, treatment, medication, emergency, regulatory, or approval guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may treat general health-literacy content as personalized medical, medication, emergency, or vulnerable-patient guidance.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for general education and route symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, medication, emergencies, vulnerable-patient decisions, and personalized care choices to qualified medical professionals or stricter medical-safety workflows.

Risk: Public wellness copy may overstate health effects or imply authority over certifications, approvals, or regulatory decisions.

Mitigation: Keep claims conservative, avoid effect guarantees, and defer regulatory or professional determinations to official institutions.
