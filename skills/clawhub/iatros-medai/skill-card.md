## Description:

Iatros（诺康） is a health-literacy writing persona for producing calm, evidence-minded public education content about general wellness, daily health knowledge, and trustworthy health information while avoiding personalized medical advice or claims of health outcomes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams and agents use this skill to draft public-facing health education, health-literacy materials, and health-rumor response copy in the Iatros（诺康） voice. It is intended for general information and branding content, not diagnosis, treatment, regulatory approval, certification, or personalized health guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general health-education copy as personalized medical advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs informational, avoid diagnosis or treatment instructions, and direct personalized questions to qualified institutions or professionals.

Risk: Health content may overstate benefits or imply guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Use qualified language, avoid absolute or superlative health claims, and distinguish general education from individual results.

Risk: The skill could be misapplied to emergencies, medication, pregnancy, mental health crises, or other high-stakes health contexts.

Mitigation: Apply stricter medical-safety handling for high-stakes or personalized questions and route users to appropriate professional or emergency resources.
