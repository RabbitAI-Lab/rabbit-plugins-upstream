## Description: <br>
AI Detector & Humanizer for LinkedIn/X Posts helps agents check LinkedIn and X post drafts for AI-written tells, platform-specific formatting issues, weak hooks, engagement bait, and related quality problems before publishing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ryancaldor-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/ryancaldor-max) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Social media creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to review LinkedIn and X drafts, locate quoted issues, and apply concrete fixes that make posts sound less AI-written and better suited to the platform. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
