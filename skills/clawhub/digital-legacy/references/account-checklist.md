# Account Checklist

A comprehensive list of account types to inventory for your digital legacy
plan. Use this as a checklist — you likely have accounts in categories you
haven't thought about.

## How to Use

Go through each category. For every account you have, add it using:
```bash
python3 scripts/digital_legacy.py add-account
```

Don't worry about being perfect on the first pass. It's better to start and
update than to wait until you have a complete list.

## After the Checklist

Once you've inventoried your accounts:

1. **Specify wishes** for each: archive, memorialize, delete, transfer, maintain
2. **Document access methods**: where to find passwords, recovery keys, 2FA
3. **Generate the encrypted will** with all access details
4. **Generate the emergency guide** for your trusted person
5. **Review annually**
