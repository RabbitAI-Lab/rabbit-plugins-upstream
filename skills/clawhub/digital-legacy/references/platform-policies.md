# Platform Legacy & Deceased Account Policies

Major platforms' policies for handling accounts after death or incapacitation.
Use this to specify the right wishes for each account in your digital legacy
plan.

## Google (Gmail, Drive, Photos, YouTube)

### Inactive Account Manager

Google's built-in feature for digital legacy.

- **Setup**: myaccount.google.com → Data & Privacy → Inactive Account Manager
- **Timeout**: Choose 3, 6, 12, or 18 months of inactivity
- **Action**: Share data with up to 10 trusted contacts, then optionally delete
- **Trigger**: No sign-in for the chosen period

**Recommendation**: Set this up. It's the most automated digital legacy tool
available.

### Without Inactive Account Manager

- Family can request account closure with a death certificate
- Google does NOT provide password or account access to family
- Data may be obtainable via court order (rare, expensive)

## Apple (iCloud, Apple ID)

### Legacy Contact

- **Setup**: Settings → [Your Name] → Password & Security → Legacy Contact
- **Available**: iOS 15.2+, macOS 12.1+
- **How it works**: Designate a contact who gets an Access Key. After death,
  they request access with the key + death certificate.
- **What they get**: Photos, files, notes, messages — everything in iCloud
- **What they DON'T get**: Subscriptions, licensed media (movies/music), in-app
  purchases

**Recommendation**: Set up a Legacy Contact immediately if using Apple devices.

### Without Legacy Contact

- Apple requires a court order to transfer data
- Death certificate alone is NOT sufficient
- Active subscriptions continue billing until cancelled

## Facebook / Meta

### Legacy Contact

- **Setup**: Settings → Memorialization Settings → Legacy Contact
- **How it works**: Choose a friend to manage your memorialized profile
- **What they can do**: Pin a post, respond to friend requests, update profile
  photo
- **What they CANNOT do**: Read your messages, log in as you, delete posts

### Memorialization

- Profile becomes "Remembering [Name]"
- Friends can still post memories
- No one can log in
- Content stays visible per your privacy settings

### Account Deletion

- Family can request permanent deletion with death certificate
- Use: facebook.com/help/contact/228813257197480

## Instagram

### Memorialization

- Owned by Meta; similar process to Facebook
- Requires death certificate
- Account becomes memorialized, no new posts

### Account Removal

- Family can request removal with death certificate
- Form: facebook.com/help/contact/228813257197480 (shared with FB)

## X / Twitter

### No Legacy Feature

- Twitter/X does NOT have a legacy contact feature
- Account remains active unless deactivated

### Deceased Account Removal

- Family can request removal with:
  - Death certificate
  - Copy of government ID of the deceased
  - Copy of government ID of the requester
- Form: help.twitter.com/forms/privacy

### Inactive Accounts

- Twitter removes accounts inactive for 6+ months (policy varies)

## LinkedIn

### Deceased Profile Removal

- Fill out the form at: linkedin.com/help/linkedin/ask/TS-RHA
- Provide: Profile URL, death certificate, relationship confirmation
- Profile is removed (not memorialized)

## Microsoft (Outlook, OneDrive, Xbox)

### Next of Kin Process

- Microsoft has a "Next of Kin" process
- Requires: death certificate, proof of account ownership
- Provides: Access to emails, files (via DVD), closure of account
- Form: microsoft.com/en-us/concern/nextofkin

### Xbox

- Digital game licenses are tied to the account and are NOT transferable
- Subscriptions (Game Pass) continue until cancelled

## Amazon

### Account Closure

- Family can close account via customer service
- Requires: Death certificate, account email
- Digital content (Kindle books, movies) is licensed, NOT owned — cannot be
  transferred

### Prime Membership

- Continues billing until cancelled
- Family member can cancel via customer service

## Netflix

### Account Cancellation

- Simple: anyone with the death certificate can cancel via customer service
- No data transfer (viewing history, profiles)

## Spotify

### Account Closure

- Use the contact form: support.spotify.com
- Premium subscription continues until cancelled

## PayPal

### Account Closure

- PayPal has a deceased user process
- Requires: Death certificate, executor documentation
- Funds in the account go through estate/probate process
- Link: paypal.com/us/smarthelp/article/how-do-i-close-a-deceased-person's-paypal-account

## Cryptocurrency Exchanges

### Coinbase

- Has a dedicated deceased user process
- Requires: Death certificate, will/probate documents, government ID
- Assets transferred to estate
- Link: help.coinbase.com

### Binance, Kraken, Others

- Process varies by exchange and jurisdiction
- Generally require: death certificate, probate documents, legal heir
  documentation
- **Slow**: Can take months

### Hardware Wallets (Ledger, Trezor)

- **No platform to contact** — access is purely through seed phrase + PIN
- If family has the seed phrase: they have full access
- If they DON'T have the seed phrase: funds are **permanently lost**
- This is why documenting seed phrase location is critical

## Domain Registrars

### Namecheap, GoDaddy, Google Domains

- Domains are assets — they have value and can expire
- Transfer requires: death certificate, executor authority
- **Auto-renew**: If on, the domain continues. If off, it expires and can be
  purchased by anyone.
- Document: registrar login, domain list, auto-renew status

## Cloud Storage

### Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive

- Follow the parent platform's policy (see above)
- Files may contain critical documents (taxes, deeds, insurance)
- Ensure backup of these files in the encrypted will

## Password Managers

### 1Password

- Emergency Kit: A printed document with account details + Secret Key
- Emergency access: Designated person can request access; you have a timeout
  period to deny

### Bitwarden

- Emergency Access: Designate trusted emergency contacts
- They can request access; after a wait period, they get full access

### KeePass

- Database file + master password
- No platform to contact — ensure master password is in the encrypted will

## Key Takeaways

1. **Set up automated features** (Google Inactive Account Manager, Apple Legacy
   Contact) — they're the easiest safety net.
2. **Crypto is special**: No platform to contact. Seed phrase access is
   everything.
3. **Digital purchases are licenses, not property**: Kindle books, iTunes
   movies, etc. cannot be transferred.
4. **Document death certificate requirements**: Most platforms require one.
   Ensure your family knows where to get them (vital records office).
5. **Auto-renew is the enemy**: Cancel subscriptions or document them so family
   can.
