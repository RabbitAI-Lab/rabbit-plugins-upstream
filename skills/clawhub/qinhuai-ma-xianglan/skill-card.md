## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may bring the historical persona into unrelated conversations.

Mitigation: Enable it only when historical-literary companionship or in-character conversation is wanted.

Risk: Companionship responses may be mistaken for professional advice in high-stakes situations.

Mitigation: For crises or medical, legal, or financial needs, seek appropriate professional help rather than relying on this persona.
