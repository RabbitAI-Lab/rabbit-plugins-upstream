This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and small businesses use this skill to plan GEO content, draft AI-friendly articles, review them before publication, and monitor whether AI answers cite their work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform-source and citation-rate claims may be mistaken for guaranteed AI visibility.

Mitigation: Verify current platform behavior and numerical claims, and measure actual citations before relying on the strategy.

Risk: Draft content may include unsupported figures or stale examples.

Mitigation: Use verifiable business data and review facts and dates before publishing.
