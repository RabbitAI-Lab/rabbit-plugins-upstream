## Use Case:

Business strategists, market analysts, founders, and product teams use this skill to assess industry attractiveness, competitive pressure, and strategic positioning before entering or evaluating a market.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market facts or competitive assumptions used in the analysis may be incomplete, outdated, or unsupported.

Mitigation: Verify current market data, cite supporting evidence where available, and distinguish assumptions from confirmed facts before using the analysis for business decisions.

Risk: A static Five Forces assessment can miss changing trends, geography-specific dynamics, or regulatory factors.

Mitigation: State the market and geographic scope clearly, note dynamic trends, and include regulatory considerations when they materially affect industry structure.
