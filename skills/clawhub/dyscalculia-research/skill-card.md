## Description:

Cited dyscalculia statistics and screening-test facts from dyscalculia.io through its hosted MCP server — prevalence by study and age, 93 peer-reviewed and official references with DOI/PMID, chart datasets (persistence, math anxiety, interventions, sex ratio, comorbidity) and how the five-game dyscalculia screening test works. Use when someone asks how common dyscalculia is, wants a source or citation for a dyscalculia number, asks what a dyscalculia test measures, or needs data for an article, report or presentation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People preparing articles, reports, presentations, or school materials can retrieve cited dyscalculia prevalence and related research data, understand what the screening test measures, and present figures with appropriate context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prevalence estimates vary across studies and could be presented as a single universal figure.

Mitigation: Give a range across studies and include the study, year, age, and sample size when available.

Risk: The five-game screening explanation could be mistaken for a diagnosis.

Mitigation: Describe it as screening of basic number skills and direct diagnostic questions to a qualified professional.
