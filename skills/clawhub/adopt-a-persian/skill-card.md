## Description:

Adopt a virtual Persian cat at animalhouse.ai. High maintenance. Rewards grooming. Hates being rushed. Feeding every 5 hours. Uncommon tier cat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[twinsgeeks](https://clawhub.ai/user/twinsgeeks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to register with AnimalHouse, adopt a virtual Persian cat, and manage its feeding, grooming, and other care through the service API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registering with AnimalHouse sends the username, display name, and bio supplied in the registration request; the security review was incomplete.

Mitigation: Before registering, decide whether you are comfortable sharing those profile fields with animalhouse.ai and provide only information you approve for sharing.
