## Description:

Generates Chinese white paper-style HTML strategic reports for executives that analyze an organization or industry chain across supply-chain position, upstream risk, downstream markets, patent competition, innovation posture, trends, and action roadmaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business leaders, strategy teams, and technical decision makers use this skill to draft executive industry-chain intelligence reports for a company or sector. The report format emphasizes patent and market evidence, risk grading, competitive benchmarking, and action planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary says the skill may present hard-coded business intelligence as real-time Zhihuiya MCP data.

Mitigation: Use it only for mockups or report-format drafting until real data ingestion, provenance checks, explicit user confirmation, and visible unavailable-or-estimated data labels are added.

Risk: The skill produces executive decision-support reports that may include estimates, supplier recommendations, patent windows, and market conclusions.

Mitigation: Require human review of sources, quantitative claims, estimates, and recommendations before using the report for business decisions.
