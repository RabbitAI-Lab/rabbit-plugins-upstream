A few useful answers.

A few carton-sized answers.

Can I try before I buy a box?

Most stockist cafés pour it as their default oat milk, so order a flat white and see. The mixed box of twelve is the easy way to taste all six at home.

How does delivery work?

Order by Tuesday midnight and a box arrives Thursday. Cartons are shelf-stable, so no ice packs and no rush to be home.

Do you supply cafés?

Yes. Wholesale cases of twelve ship weekly, with a spare crate for the counter if you want one. Write to us with your address and how many you pour a week.

A few practical details.

Which flavour should I start with?

Original for the everyday, Coffee, Please if you live on flat whites, Berry Big for porridge and small people. The mixed box settles it.

How long does a carton keep?

Twelve months unopened in the cupboard, seven days in the fridge once opened. Shake it first; the good stuff settles.

Is it suitable for allergies?

Oats are naturally gluten-free and ours are milled in an oat-only facility. There is no soy, nut or dairy anywhere in the recipe.

A slow-morning stack

Fluffy pancakes. A slow morning.

Ingredients

One generous serving; pancakes make a small batch.

Plain flour
120 g
Baking powder
1 tsp
Oat drink
180 ml
Egg
1
Neutral oil
1 tbsp

A small stack for a slow morning.

Use the flavour you enjoy, and check all pack labels for allergens and suitability. Adjust the liquid to the texture you prefer.

Make it step by step.

  1. 01

    Mix

    Combine the flour and baking powder. Whisk the drink, egg and oil separately, then gently bring the mixtures together.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Warm a lightly oiled pan over medium heat. Add small portions of batter and turn when bubbles appear and the underside is golden.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Cook through on both sides and serve warm with fruit or a topping you enjoy. Adjust the heat between batches.

LET’S TALK OAT

A few good
questions.

Still curious?

Say hello
What is Oat Parade?

Oat milk made from oats grown near the mill, in six flavours: Original, Vanilla, Coffee, Cocoa, Berry and Matcha. No gums, oils or fillers.

Which flavor goes with coffee?

The Original is mellow. Vanilla adds sweetness. Coffee, Please is ready to pour.

Can I cook with it?

Try pancakes, porridge or a berry smoothie.

Where can I find it?

In around 300 grocers and cafés, listed on the stockists page, or delivered to your door in mixed boxes of twelve.

Does it froth?

Yes, properly. Steam to around 63 °C and it holds a glossy microfoam for latte art. Coffee, Please is tuned for exactly this.
