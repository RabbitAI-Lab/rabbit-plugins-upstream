## Description: <br>
Coaches creators through filming real-human talking-head or piece-to-camera videos, including phone-first setup, beat-map or teleprompter delivery, retake rules, batch filming, and honest routing when the user cannot or will not film. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Creators, founders, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to prepare and film authentic talking-head videos from an existing script. It helps the agent give setup guidance, format beat maps or prompter text, plan retakes and batch filming sessions, and provide self-review checklists while leaving filming and take selection to the human. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The agent may be asked to judge footage or choose a final take even though the skill cannot see video. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the human responsible for filming and take selection; provide a self-review checklist instead of claiming that a take looks natural or ready. <br>
Risk: Talking-head workflows can involve other people on camera or AI-enhanced footage. <br>
Mitigation: Require consent for anyone appearing or audible in the recording and follow platform disclosure rules for eye-contact fixes, retouching, background replacement, avatars, or voiceover. <br>
Risk: Adjacent editing and publishing tools could be mistaken for this skill's responsibilities. <br>
Mitigation: Treat this skill as coaching and preparation only; route editing and publishing to the appropriate tools after the human has selected and approved the finished file. <br>
