## Description:

Organizes information for a medical appointment into a symptom timeline, preparation checklist, questions for the clinician, and a visit-notes template.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sxq0607](https://clawhub.ai/user/sxq0607)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patients and healthcare workers use this skill to organize symptoms, medical history, medications, and prior test results before an appointment and prepare questions and notes for the visit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A visit checklist may be mistaken for a diagnosis or emergency assessment.

Mitigation: Use it only to prepare for a clinical visit; seek immediate medical care for urgent symptoms and confirm medical decisions with a clinician.

Risk: Preparing a checklist may involve sensitive health information.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the conversation and avoid identifying information unless necessary.
