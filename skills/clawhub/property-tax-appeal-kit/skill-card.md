## Description:

Helps homeowners evaluate a property-tax assessment using equalization calculations, property-record checks, comparable-sale analysis, and an appeal timeline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners use the skill to check assessment ratios, identify property-record errors, compare recent sales, estimate potential tax savings, and organize appeal deadlines and hearing evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Appeal deadlines, assessment rules, and valuation assumptions vary by jurisdiction, while the timeline uses a fixed default window and milestones.

Mitigation: Verify current filing requirements and valuation assumptions with the local assessor, and set the timeline window to the applicable jurisdictional deadline.
