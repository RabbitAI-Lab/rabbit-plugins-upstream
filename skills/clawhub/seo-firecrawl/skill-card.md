SEO practitioners, developers, and agents use this skill to crawl, map, scrape, and search websites with Firecrawl MCP for audits, content inventory, broken link checks, and site-structure analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Firecrawl is an external service for the URLs and page data submitted through crawl, scrape, map, or search workflows.

Mitigation: Use only authorized public or approved pages, and avoid private, logged-in, internal, staging, customer, paywalled, or regulated-data pages unless Firecrawl retention and privacy terms have been reviewed.

Risk: The skill depends on a separate Firecrawl MCP server and installer.

Mitigation: Confirm Firecrawl tools are available before use and review the Firecrawl extension installer before running it.

Risk: Large crawls can consume Firecrawl credits or hit rate limits.

Mitigation: Estimate credit usage before large crawls and reduce scope, depth, or concurrency when needed.
