## Description:

Build, change and publish websites and web apps with WebTwizz through its MCP server. Use when the user wants a website, landing page, online store, booking site, portfolio or web app built or changed, wants a site published or put on their own domain, or asks about their WebTwizz sites, data, versions or credits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use WebTwizz through an MCP-enabled assistant to build, edit, publish, and manage websites and web apps, including their connected data, integrations, domains, and account settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The linked WebTwizz service and its external destinations were outside the inspected artifact.

Mitigation: Review the service and linked destinations before connecting or signing in.

Risk: Building and editing sites consumes WebTwizz account credits.

Mitigation: Check available credits and pricing before starting substantial work.

Risk: Site data tools can change records directly, including deleting records.

Mitigation: Confirm the target and intended impact with the user before data-changing actions.
