## Description:

Patent Research Analyst helps enterprise IPR teams, R&D engineers, and research project leads turn a technical problem and proposed solution into a patent search, multidimensional patent analysis, and structured HTML and Word reports using PatSnap/Zhihuiya patent data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Enterprise IPR teams, R&D engineers, and research project owners use this skill to collect patent-search inputs, run iterative PatSnap/Zhihuiya patent searches, analyze technology routes, novelty, FTO risk, competitors, and recent publications, and produce structured patent-analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends technical problems, proposed technical solutions, and competitor names to configured PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP services.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when the user and organization are comfortable sharing those inputs with the configured service.

Risk: Generated FTO and infringement conclusions may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat FTO and infringement outputs as patent research support and have qualified counsel review legal conclusions before relying on them.

Risk: Patent links and analyses require live PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP configuration and current patent data.

Mitigation: Confirm the MCP service is configured and authorized before running searches, and preserve source markers and appendices for review.
