## Description:

Helps AI-agent users and skill maintainers turn a request into a practical, repeatable workflow or deliverable and check it against stated success criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, and maintainers use the skill to turn a concrete need into a plan, checklist, analysis, implementation, or other practical deliverable. It also guides them to check the result against the stated requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Common everyday words in the activation rules could cause the skill to be selected for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Review and narrow the activation terms before enabling implicit invocation; use explicit invocation where the broad triggers are unnecessary.
