## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation keywords may surface the skill during unrelated tasks.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or disable implicit invocation to reduce unintended selection.

Risk: Users may mistake workflow guidance or its demand score for a security assessment.

Mitigation: Use the skill for planning and checklists only; review and scan outputs before deployment, and do not treat the demand score as security certification.
