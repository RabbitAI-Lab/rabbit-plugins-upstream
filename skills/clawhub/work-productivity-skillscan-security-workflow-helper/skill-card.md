## Description:

Helps agent users and skill maintainers turn workflow needs into practical plans, artifacts, or implementations and check the results against stated criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, and maintainers use this skill to plan and carry out practical workflow, reliability, safety, or adjacent-skill work. It can produce a tailored deliverable and a reusable checklist, then check the result against the user's criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill outside its intended workflow.

Mitigation: Invoke it with a clear work-productivity or SkillScan-related request, and review the proposed steps against the user's stated goal.
