## Description:

Provides Swahili guidance for registering a PowPow Bubble account, resolving sign-up issues, and activating a subscription.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking Swahili guidance can learn about PowPow Bubble, follow its registration steps, troubleshoot account setup, and find the appropriate subscription activation flow. The user completes registration and payment on the relevant official site or app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user could disclose passwords or verification codes during assisted registration.

Mitigation: Never request or handle credentials or codes; have the user enter them only in the official PowPow flow.

Risk: Subscription payments may incur charges or automatic renewals that the user did not expect.

Mitigation: Ask the user to check current pricing and renewal terms before subscribing, and complete payments only through official PowPow, PayPal, WeChat, or Huawei flows.
