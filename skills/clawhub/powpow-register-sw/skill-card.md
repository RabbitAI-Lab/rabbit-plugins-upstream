## Description:

Guides Swahili-speaking users through PowPow Bubble account registration, common signup troubleshooting, and paid subscription activation on PowPow's official web or app surfaces.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand PowPow Bubble, open the correct registration and subscription pages, resolve common account setup issues, and complete activation themselves without sharing credentials in chat.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share passwords, verification codes, or payment credentials in chat while following registration guidance.

Mitigation: Tell users to enter secrets and payment details only on PowPow's official browser or app pages, and never request those values in chat.

Risk: Users may expect the agent to create accounts, submit forms, or complete paid activation for them.

Mitigation: Keep the skill guidance-only: direct users to the official registration and subscription pages and have them complete account and payment actions themselves.
