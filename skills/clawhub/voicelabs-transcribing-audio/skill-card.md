## Description:

Transcribes audio with VoiceLabs and helps users find and work with earlier recordings and transcripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bsalaeddin](https://clawhub.ai/user/bsalaeddin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People with voice memos, meetings, or other recordings use this skill to transcribe audio, review past captures, and summarize or refine transcripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected recordings and resulting transcripts are sent to VoiceLabs and saved as account captures.

Mitigation: Use only recordings the user agrees to upload; avoid highly sensitive audio unless this storage is acceptable.

Risk: Transcribing a public audio link uses a metered request.

Mitigation: Use a link supplied for the current request, and confirm before using a link found elsewhere.
