## Description:

Transcribes audio with VoiceLabs and helps users find earlier transcripts and recordings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[devino](https://clawhub.ai/user/devino)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users transcribe voice memos and recordings, review past captures, and work with the resulting text to summarize or extract action items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recordings and transcripts are processed and saved as captures in the user's VoiceLabs account, where they can be retrieved later.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive recordings unless VoiceLabs storage and OAuth access are acceptable to the user.
