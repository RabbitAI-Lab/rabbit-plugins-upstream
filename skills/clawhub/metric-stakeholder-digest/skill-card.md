## Description:

Combines calculate a metric trend summary with build a stakeholder metric digest.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxt-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxt-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, analysts, and operators use this skill for routine analytics reporting: calculating metric trend summaries and turning structured metric summaries into stakeholder digests for planning, leadership updates, and operating reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Metric inputs may contain sensitive operational or business data.

Mitigation: Provide only the metric data intended for analysis and avoid including unrelated private or confidential details.

Risk: Incomplete or malformed metric series can lead to misleading trend summaries or stakeholder digests.

Mitigation: Review the input metric series and check the generated digest before using it in planning, leadership updates, or operating reviews.
