## Description:

Validates Amazon product ideas before launch by analyzing reviews of comparable products to identify buyer complaints, willingness to pay, and opportunities for differentiation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and product teams use the skill to assess demand before launch, compare customer feedback across similar products, and identify product improvements and listing opportunities.
