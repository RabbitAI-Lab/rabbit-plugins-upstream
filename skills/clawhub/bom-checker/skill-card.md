## Description: <br>
Validate visual inspection device BOMs for completeness by detecting missing critical, high-risk, and medium-risk components before purchasing or assembly. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Manufacturing engineers, procurement teams, and automation engineers use this skill to audit visual inspection equipment BOMs before purchasing or assembly. It helps identify missing cameras, lenses, lighting, control hardware, cables, software, and related components. <br>
