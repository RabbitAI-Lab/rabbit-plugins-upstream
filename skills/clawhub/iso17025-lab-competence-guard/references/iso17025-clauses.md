# ISO/IEC 17025:2017 — Key Clause Reference

Detailed clause text for edge-case review decisions.

## Clause 4 — General Requirements

### 4.1 Impartiality
- Laboratory activities shall be undertaken impartially.
- The laboratory shall be responsible for the impartiality of its activities and shall not allow commercial, financial, or other pressures to compromise impartiality.
- The laboratory shall identify risks to its impartiality on an ongoing basis.

### 4.2 Confidentiality
- The laboratory shall be responsible for the management of all information obtained or created.
- The laboratory shall inform the customer in advance of information it intends to place in the public domain.

## Clause 6 — Resource Requirements

### 6.2 Personnel
- All personnel, internal or external, that could influence laboratory activities shall act impartially.
- The laboratory shall document the competence requirements for each position.
- The laboratory shall have procedures for authorizing personnel to perform specific activities.

### 6.4 Equipment
- The laboratory shall have access to equipment required for the correct performance of activities.
- Measuring equipment shall be able to achieve the required measurement accuracy and/or uncertainty.
- Equipment shall be calibrated when accuracy or uncertainty affects the validity of results.
- Equipment shall be checked between calibrations to maintain confidence in its status.

### 6.5 Metrological Traceability
- The laboratory shall establish and maintain metrological traceability of its results by means of an unbroken chain of calibrations, each contributing to measurement uncertainty, linking results to appropriate references.
- Traceability to SI units shall be established through calibration by a competent laboratory, or through use of certified reference materials.

## Clause 7 — Process Requirements

### 7.2 Method Selection and Verification
- The laboratory shall use appropriate methods and/or method combinations.
- Methods shall be validated before use for the intended application.
- The laboratory shall verify that it can properly perform methods before introducing them.

### 7.8 Reporting of Results

#### 7.8.2 Common requirements for reports, certificates, and statements of conformity
Each report shall contain at least the information listed in the SKILL.md table, unless the laboratory has valid reasons not to.

#### 7.8.3 Specific requirements for calibration certificates
Calibration certificates shall include:
- Conditions under which calibrations were performed
- Measurement uncertainty of the result and of the reference standard
- Traceability statement
- Results before and after adjustment or repair (if applicable)

#### 7.8.5 Opinions and interpretations
When opinions and interpretations are expressed, the laboratory shall ensure that only personnel authorized for expressing opinions and interpretations shall do so. Opinions and interpretations shall be clearly marked as such.

#### 7.8.7 Amendments to reports, certificates, and statements
When a report, certificate, or statement has been issued and needs to be changed or amended:
- Any alteration shall be clearly identified
- A reference to the original report shall be included
- A new unique identification shall be assigned if re-issued

## Clause 8 — Management System Requirements

### 8.4 Control of Documents
- Documents shall be approved before issue.
- Documents shall be periodically reviewed and updated.
- Obsolete documents shall be promptly removed.

### 8.7 Corrective Actions
- The laboratory shall have a procedure for implementing corrective actions when nonconforming work is identified.
- Root cause analysis shall be performed.

## Measurement Uncertainty Guidance

- For calibration laboratories, measurement uncertainty shall be reported for all calibrations.
- For testing laboratories, uncertainty shall be reported when:
  - It is relevant to the validity or application of results
  - A customer's instruction requires it
  - The uncertainty affects conformity to a specification limit
- Where a well-recognized method specifies limits of major sources of uncertainty, the laboratory may rely on those limits provided it demonstrates it can achieve them.

## Statement of Conformity (Clause 7.8.6)

When a statement of conformity is provided:
- The laboratory shall document the decision rule applied
- The decision rule shall take into account the level of risk (false acceptance, false rejection)
- The decision rule shall be communicated to the customer

## Proficiency Testing (Clause 7.7.2)

- The laboratory shall monitor its performance by comparison with results of other laboratories.
- This may include participation in proficiency testing or interlaboratory comparisons.
