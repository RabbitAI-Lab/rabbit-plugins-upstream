## Description:

Audit test and calibration laboratory documents against ISO/IEC 17025:2017 before release, flagging nonconforming reports, certificates, and quality records with clause-level citations and fix suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1003429073](https://clawhub.ai/user/1003429073)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Laboratory quality, accreditation, and document-review users apply this skill to check test reports, calibration certificates, quality manuals, method validation records, equipment records, audit records, and accredited capability claims before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The compliance review could be mistaken for accredited, legal, or regulatory approval.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a checklist and have qualified laboratory quality or regulatory personnel verify findings before release or accreditation decisions.

Risk: Submitted laboratory documents may contain sensitive customer, lab, or quality-system records.

Mitigation: Only provide documents the user is authorized to share with the agent and redact unnecessary sensitive information before review.
