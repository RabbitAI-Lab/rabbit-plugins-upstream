## Description: <br>
Helps founders and builders turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning through a repeatable planning workflow. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps turn a rough idea into outcomes, assumptions, validation steps, prototypes, positioning, reusable checklists, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms may make this skill appear for general startup or product conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Use this skill for product validation and MVP planning; choose a more specific skill when a different workflow is needed. <br>
Risk: Planning outputs may be mistaken for completed market validation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning artifacts and validate key assumptions with users, evidence, and measurable tests before committing major resources. <br>
