## Description:

Designs survey questions, interview scripts, and feedback loops for user research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SaaS and startup founders use this skill to plan user research, prepare customer interview scripts, design surveys, set up feedback loops, and turn findings into product decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for product, user-base, and customer-segment details while tailoring a research plan.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential customer data unless it is necessary for the research task, and prefer aggregated or anonymized details.

Risk: Research plans and interview or survey questions may shape product decisions based on incomplete or biased user input.

Mitigation: Review the proposed plan before use, sample across relevant user segments, and compare qualitative findings with behavioral or analytics data where available.
