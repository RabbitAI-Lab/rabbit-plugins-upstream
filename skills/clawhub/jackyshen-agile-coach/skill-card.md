## Description: <br>
Provides structured agile coaching prompt support for Scrum Masters and Product Owners across common Scrum, backlog, planning, facilitation, and product decision scenarios. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mebusw](https://clawhub.ai/user/mebusw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Scrum Masters, Product Owners, and agile team facilitators use this skill to select and customize GRACE-structured prompt templates for backlog readiness, prioritization, risk analysis, product requirements, retrospectives, personas, churn, and decision support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for generic product or business requests that are not agile coaching tasks. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for Scrum or agile coaching before applying the templates, and proceed without the skill when the request is unrelated. <br>
Risk: The artifact references references/prompt-library.md, but that file is not included in this release. <br>
Mitigation: Use only the scenario quick reference and GRACE structure present in SKILL.md, and obtain the missing prompt library before relying on unavailable templates. <br>
